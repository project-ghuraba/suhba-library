Audhu Billahi min al shaytan al rajim, Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Assalatu wa s-salamu ala Rasulina Muhammad Sayyidi l Awwalin wa l-Akhirin. Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fil jamia' Our way is through association & blessings are in the gathering. May we be a blessed jamaat insha Allah. Madad ya Rasul Allah, Madad ya S. Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, madad ya S.Muhammad Nazim al Haqqani, dastur! Madad.

The people of this time want easy things- they want ease and convenience. Allah made everything easy. But the people of this time, themselves are making everything a total mess. They are the ones who put themselves into hardship. Allah (awj) says, "fa innama.."- "fa" is the letter for oath. "Fa'inna Ma`a Al-`Usri Yusraan, 'Inna Ma`a Al-`Usri Yusraan" (94:5-6). Says twice; "So verily, with hardship, there is relief". After every hardship comes a relief. Therefore, really a relief comes following every hardship. But mankind wants continuous relief, doesn't want any hardship at all.

In Dunya, for sure there is a hardship & will be a relief after it. There is no relief coming directly.. But for a person who utterly surrendered to Allah, because everything comes from Allah, he welcomes everything with relief; illness, poverty, all... But a normal person makes even the smallest thing a problem. As an example to this, Arabs have a saying: "carrying the ladder sideways". Now, take a 4 m ladder & walk in the street, how will you walk with it? You will walk by hitting people. But if you hold it straight, you can walk comfortably without hitting anyone.

Like this, girls have candidates for marriage but want educated man. The man wants to study but doesn't believe he can do it. So he comes, "pray for me that I can study, my understanding opens". The girls say for their candidates, "this is young, old, handsome, ugly..." You found a husband, what else do you want? If his character is good,take him. They don't like the job offers, saying "I studied so many years.." The most important thing is, Allah created us as Muslims, we should be very happy with this. There is Verse for this: "Qul Bifadli Al-Lahi Wa Birahmatihi Fabidhalika Falyafrahu" (10:58). Allah created us as Muslims with His Bounty & is treating us with His Mercy. It says, rejoice over this. Doesn't say, if you are to rejoice over something, rejoice over this. It is an order-rejoice over it, be happy with it.

Today we speak about people. Allah give everything for people but people they are complaining. Every time they are complaining; "we want this, we want this"... And Allah (awj) in Ayah said "Fa'inna Ma`a Al-`Usri Yusraan, 'Inna Ma`a Al-`Usri Yusraan" (94:5-6). First become little bit difficult. And this "fa", "fa inna"- this is Allah giving oath. He said, first must be difficult. After difficult, become easy. Without difficult, you cannot find easy.

But people, all people they like to be everything easy- this nowadays people. There is people, everything is easy for them. But they are very rare people. Who is this people? Who they accept everything from Allah; Good, bad, illness, poorness, not respect..All they accept they coming from Allah, they are all time happy. But this is people very rare & rare like...no. Very very rare people this. Allah give everything for people. But people not happy, all time complaining.

We want to marry but who looking, coming man - "no, this poor man, this not educated man. This is not..And after this, before this also,they coming "we want to study but we are not so clever, you must read for us to be successful". You are not, so you don't read, don't do this. The most important thing, Allah give for people to born Muslim, or to become Muslim. This is Allah, in Ayah saying: "Qul Bifadli Al-Lahi Wa Birahmatihi Fabidhalika Falyafrahu".

Allah said, the Mercy of Allah & His Generosity, He give you this. This, you must be happy with this. This is order to be happy. Don't look "we are ill, we are poor, we are not handsome, not beautiful, not.." All this nonsense thing. Allah order to be happy with His Mercy & His Generosity. Everything you must be happy. He give this, from Allah. When you be happy like this, you will be like this rare people who happy for everything. But people still complaining. Don't complain. This is order of Allah & order of mashayikh, Prophet (sas). Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, Al Fatiha.

There is one point I forget. There is Arab people, they said "you carrying ladder sideway". If you have 4 m ladder, how you can go in street like this? You will hit cars, you will hit people, everything. But if you do it, carry it straight like this, if it is ten meter also not difficult to carry it. The most people also they do this;they only want what they have in their mind they must do it.Not listening for Allah, not listening for Prophet, not listening for mashayikh. And Allah show happiness, how to be.

Allah make all of us happy insha Allah, here & hereafter also, more important. Al Fatiha.
