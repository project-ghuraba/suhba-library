As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
فَتَبَارَكَ اللَّهُ أَحْسَنُ الْخَالِقِينَ
<br>“Fa tabārakAllahu aḥsanu l-khāliqīn.”<br>
“So blessed is Allah, the Best of creators.” (Qurʼān 23:14).

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says that He is the Best of Creators. He exalts His Name. There is no flaw in anything Allah has created; He has created everything beautifully and in its proper place. He created all things in the most beautiful and perfect manner. Allah created the earth. 

We will repeat what we said,<br>
فَتَبَارَكَ اللَّهُ أَحْسَنُ الْخَالِقِينَ
<br>“Fa tabārakAllahu aḥsanu l-khāliqīn.”<br>
“So blessed is Allah, the Best of creators.” (Qurʼān 23:14).

He is, indeed, the Best of Creators. Yet, He exalts His Name so that people may come to their senses.

Allah created this world so that humanity might dwell and settle upon it. The entire Earth originates from a single source. However, there are certain things that Allah subsequently sent down to earth. The most famous of these is the Black Stone, Ḥajar al-Aswad, which came from Paradise.

There are others, though they are not widely known or famous; they exist, but the primary one is this blessed, sacred stone. Apart from that, Allah created the earth to serve humanity, providing a place for them to inhabit.

In Qur’ān ʿAẓīmu sh-Sha’n it is stated,<br>
مِنْهَا خَلَقْنَاكُمْ وَفِيهَا نُعِيدُكُمْ وَمِنْهَا نُخْرِجُكُمْ تَارَةً أُخْرَىٰ
<br>“Minhā khalaqnākum wa-fīhā nuʿīdukum wa-minhā nukhrijukum tāratan ukhrā”<br>
“From the earth We created you, and into it We will return you, and from it We will extract you another time.” (Qurʼān 20:55). 

“We created [you] from it, We return [you] to it, and from it, you shall rise again on the Day of Judgment.”

No matter how much people seek to benefit from the world, they do so only for a time; then, they too pass on. No one can take the world with them. Therefore, the one deceived by the world is in a state of loss. The world remains for no one.

Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam states that regarding the Day of Judgment: Whoever believed in Allah but never prayed, and only committed wrong and sinned—he will enter the Hellfire. Whoever has acted like this goes into the Fire. But at least he believed in Allah. For the one who denies Him completely, things are entirely different. This believer will enter the Fire, but in the end, he will be redeemed. Whether he has to atone there for hundreds of thousands or millions of years—eventually, he will come out of hell.

And the very last believer to leave Hell... When he enters Paradise, he thinks, “Surely nothing is left for me. Billions of souls are already there before me—what could possibly be left for me? Is there even any room left? Not even a spot to lay my head!” he believes. Yet even to him, Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla grants a realm many times greater than this entire Earth. And that is not just for a few years or a human lifetime, but for all eternity.

Therefore, those who chase after this world—forgetting Allah, rebelling against Him, or disbelieving in Him—are in a state of loss; they lack true understanding. For this world is finite and limited.

One cannot obtain anything from elsewhere; dunya is simply dunya. It is a passageway for everyone. No one can remain here; there is no immortality here.

You know, there is all this talk nowadays about “Artificial Intelligence” yet even that has its limits. People imagine that through these things, they can deny Allah even more, yet those technologies have their own boundaries. People with weak faith—or those with no faith at all—become deeply confused, wondering what will happen or what the outcome will be... [But the truth remains:] Allah exists.

Nothing moves, nothing happens, and nothing comes into existence without the will of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. 

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says in Qur’ān ʿAẓīmu sh-Sha’n,<br>
سَخَّرَ لَكُم مَّا فِي الْأَرْضِ وَالْفُلْكَ تَجْرِي فِي الْبَحْرِ بِأَمْرِهِ
<br>“Sakhkhara lakum mā fī l-arḍi wa-l-fulka tajrī fī l-baḥri bi-amrih”<br>
“See you not that Allah has subjected to you (mankind) all that is on the earth, and the ships that sail through the sea by His Command?” (Qurʼān 22:65).

He has made everything on land and at sea subservient to your service. Muslims must realize that these things come from Allah—by His will—and that nothing happens without His will.

Muslims have become quite bewildered lately; their minds are thoroughly confused. Without faith, there is no peace. 

It is stated in Qur’ān ʿAẓīmu sh-Sha’n,<br>
يَحْسَبُونَ كُلَّ صَيْحَةٍ عَلَيْهِمْ
<br>“Yaḥsabūna kulla ṣayḥatin ʿalayhim”<br>
“They think that every cry is against them.” (Qur’an 63:04).

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla declares in the Qur’ān that they perceive everything as being against them—those are the ones without faith.

As for the believers, they have submitted and placed their trust in Allah, believing that “Everything comes from Allah, both good and evil” and they carry on with their lives. Whatever provision is destined for us will certainly come to us; we must understand this. As we said, they should attribute everything to Allah and not overtax their minds with worldly affairs or let themselves become confused.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says, “You may consider something to be good, yet it turns out to be evil; and you may consider something to be evil, yet it turns out to be good.” Therefore, worldly affairs belong to the people of this world. Let them worry and be confused; let them deal with these matters, while you must remain at ease.

There is so much attachment to worldly things here. Almost everywhere - it is much the same everywhere, but here— and in certain places—people are more preoccupied with worldly distractions. This is because they seek to satisfy their ego and desires to a greater extent.

Therefore, whoever controls their ego stands to gain in every respect; otherwise, nothing will ever satisfy them. You only destroy yourself—and drag others down with you as well. The best remedy for this, inshā’Allāh, is to seek good friends and keep the company of good people—and, of course, to offer one another counsel.

Our Holy Prophet stated,<br>
يَدُ اللَّهِ مَعَ الْجَمَاعَةِ
<br>“Yadullah maʿa l-jamāʿah”<br>
“The hand of Allah is with the community (the congregation).” [al-Tirmidhī, al-Nasā'ī]

Allah’s Hand of Power is upon the Jama’ah and the community. When one is alone, one easily goes astray. In this day and age, a bad companion can be worse than shayṭān; they can easily do what shayṭān himself cannot.

Therefore, one should stay away from such people, inshā’Allāh. May Allah help you all—and us, too—wherever you are in the world. While this place may have a bit more of that, nowhere is truly safe anymore.

May Allah send the savior, inshā’Allāh. That savior is Mahdi ʿalayhi s-salām. We will carry on until he arrives, inshā’Allāh. When he comes, all this evil will be swept away and cleansed. May he come soon, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
