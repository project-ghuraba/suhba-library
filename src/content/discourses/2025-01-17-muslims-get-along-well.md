As-Salāmu ‘Alaykum wa RaḥmatuLlāhi wa Barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu BiLlāhi Minash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. BismiLlāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm.

Wa ṣ-Salātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Akhirīn.

Madad yā RasūlAllāh, Madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi RasūliLlāh, Madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

Dastūr Mawlana Sheikh Abdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistāni, Sheikh Muḥammad

Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad.Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-Suḥbah wa l-Khayru fi l-Jam‘iyyah.

Our Holy Prophet is saying,

aw kama qal. A Mu’min and Muslim person is the one who gets along well. People get along well with him, and he gets along well with them. He is an accepted Muslim. Muslim is the one who doesn’t harm people and gets along well with them.

There is no contrariness between Muslims. There is nothing like harming people. The attribute of Allah ‘Azza wa-Jalla is mercy. The attribute of Mu’mins must be from the attributes of Allah ‘Azza wa-Jalla. They must be merciful and generous. They must help people. This is what being Muslim is. Islam is not what they show as bad.

Hypocrites and non-Muslims are the ones without mercy. They seem merciful from one side, but from another side they do all kinds of badness to people without pity. Therefore, Muslims must not be similar to them. Muslims must be similar to our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, Sahabah, ‘Alims and Awliya’. They must follow their way and be like them to bring benefit to people. If they are like Kafirs without mercy, will there be any benefit? There is no benefit.

It is important that our Holy Prophet is the one who shows us the way. Heis the most beautiful person. Our Holy Prophet is the most honorable one among all creatures. We must follow and imitate him as much as we can. Therefore, people should get along well with others, with their parents, children and families. They should treat all of them well and live in goodness. This is the order of our Holy Prophet. It is a beautiful order. Instead of living in contrariness all the time, live nicely and have good relations with people. Your life will be good as well. This is Islam. Islam does not order anything apart from goodness and beauty. May Allah not let us obey our egos. May we be like this on the way ordered by Islam and our Holy Prophet insha’Allah. May it be perpetual insha’Allah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.

There are recited Qur’an Khatms, Surahs, Yasin, Tabarakah, Tasbihat, Tahlilat, Dala’il al-Khayrat, Salawat, and whatever goodness there is, may Allāh accept them all. We gift them to our Holy Prophet, his Ahlu l-Bayt and Sahabah, souls of all Anbiya, Awliya, Asfiya and Mashayikh, to the souls of all Muslims and Mu’mins, may they reach them. May the good goals of those who recited be reached. To us all insha’Allah may there be happiness here and hereafter.

Al-Fatiha.
