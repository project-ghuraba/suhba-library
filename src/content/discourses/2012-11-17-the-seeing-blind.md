Allahumma Salli wa Sallim ala Nabiyina Muhammad alayhi salam. Salatan tadumu wa tughda ilayh ma marra l-layali wa tula dawam. Allahumma Salli wa Sallim ala Nabiyina Muhammad alayhi salam. Salatan tadumu wa tughda ilayh ma marra l-layali wa tula dawam. Ya Salam! As-Salamu Alaikum for all of you.

Today insha Allah will speak in two languages, following S. Mehmet's sunnah. Insha Allah today I will speak in two languages, one English & one Turkish upon the Sunnah al Saniyya of S. Mehmet Effendi.

Today it is very happy day, Alhamdulillah. Today is one of the most precious days that Allah Almighty, He grant us. Everyday it is more precious. Everyday it is more valuable. Why? Because Allah Almighty, He grant us this day. Alhamdulillah today is a very beautiful day. Today is also a very precious day, a very valuable day. Why? Everybody is asking "why today is so precious?" Because Allah granted us life, granted us breath & granted us to live this day. Therefore today is a very precious day. So many people, they are all the time complaining about the day they are in. Say "Ooo this day is very terrible, this day is very hard, this day is..." Yahu you have to think! You been granted the most valuable things & you still complaining. And this is, we call it "the seeing blind" people. They look at you, they see everything but they are blind. These people say always "today is a very bad day, today is..." Yahu Allah granted you the most precious thing; can't be anything more precious, more beneficial than this. If there is no life & no breath what is the benefit of everything? They don't understand this & these people are "the seeing blinds". He looks at you but at the same time he is blind, doesn't see anything. May Allah not make us from them. And we are asking Allah Almighty to not make us "the seeing blind". They look & they are blind. Subhan Allah.

It is how Allah Almighty give, give, give.. & you still blind, cannot see. Cannot see, still blind. Allah Dhu-l Jalali wa Taqaddas Hz give, give, give all the time. Still people are blind & don't see the blessings - blind & ungrateful. Look, it fits in Turkish- blind & ungrateful, doesn't fit in English. Because people are blind & ungrateful. Allah doesn't love the ungrateful ones. That Allah Almighty doesn't like the one who doesn't appreciate, He hates the one who doesn't appreciate. That is why so many people, they have been given a lot of things and after while they lose everything. And when they lose everything "Ooo what we done?" Yahu, you been not appreciating. You been not having shukur. You been not giving anything to Allah Almighty, even one sajda.So if Allah Almighty take this one don't blame Allah Almighty. Allah grants people so much & mankind returns it in unbelief, returns in ungratefullness. And who Allah doesn't love is the ungrateful servant. He takes everything from him, all the blessings- his health, his wealth, his children...In the end of the day he asks "what have I done?" Yahu you're ungrateful- didn't make shukur, didn't even make one sajda for Allah! Why do you start fighting with Allah when everything is taken? You already had your fight with Allah from the beginning. You were ungrateful from the beginning. Allah grants you everything, does not ask you.

There is one very nice story about Mulla Nasruddin. He was going to the market & he bought one donkey. And he was so happy. With his son, buying brand new donkey. And he was looking for the donkey from up to down, very nice donkey, brand new! How can be brand, donkey, I don't know but.. That is a brand new for Mulla Nasruddin because he bought it! So Mulla Nasruddin tell his son "O my son, let's ride on the donkey & we go for our village". He & his son, they ride on the donkey & the donkey start to walk... By going, they see some people. They said "What a terrible people, what a terrible man this man- He & his son, he doesn't have any mercy for this poor donkey". And Mulla Nasruddin hear this one & say "O my son, I think you have to go down&I will be on the donkey". His donkey, he paid for it! So the boy went down & he pulled the donkey. And Mulla Nasruddin proud about his new vessel.

After a while, there are other people. They say "Look for this man. No mercy for his son. Small child is pulling the donkey & this man, he has full power & riding on a donkey. And how come? No mercy for his son". And Mullla Nasruddin listen to this one. "My son, you come on the donkey & I will pull the donkey". So he take the rope & start to pull the donkey and the boy is on the donkey. Another people say "Look for this boy, look for this child! He doesn't have any respect for his elderly father. Old man walking & he is on the donkey". Also Mulla Nasruddin "Yes, they are right. Ok let's do the other way." He said "My son, come. We walk & let the donkey free".

And after a while they see another people who said "What a stupid people; they have donkey & they are walking". He said "My son, I decided to ride on the donkey- you & me, and we go". And the son was telling him "Why, why?" He said "if we are listening for people, everyone have his own thought & he has own idea.Allah Almighty gave us this one so we have to use it in proper way."So this is the wisdom about people. When Mawlana Shaykh says something, this is the right. It is not the thinking of people or the comment of people or whatever the people say, the right is one. So this, it was a story about when Allah Almighty give you something you have to digest it first & you have to accept it & you say "This is my style. This is what Allah Almighty give me & I have to be from the shakur- the one who make shukur."

Now I want to tell a story of Nasreddin Hoja. One day Mulla Nasruddin went to the market with his son & bought a donkey. He was so happy with this donkey & it was a very beautiful donkey, how they say today "brand new, never used, first hand". So they bought it & with this joy they both mounted on it & started traveling to their village. And while going, they passed through some people who said "How merciless is this Hoja Effendi, this man" since they don't know him, "He is crushing this poor animal with his son". Nasreddin Hoja said "O son, you get down. You walk & at least I enjoy the ride on the donkey" since he paid for it. So while his son pulled the donkey, Hoja Effendi sitting on the top they passed through some other people who said "Yahu what a cruel man is this. He makes the small boy pull the donkey & he, himself enjoy sitting upstairs". He said "O son, come. You ride the donkey & I pull it". So Hoja was pulling the donkey..

They passed through other people, they said "Yahu what a disrespectful child is this. His father, the old man, is pulling the donkey & he is sitting on the top". Finally he said "O son, you get down from the donkey & we walk together letting the donkey free."While they were walking like this, they passed through some others who said "What foolish people these are, can't be more stupid than this; they let the donkey free & walk on foot!" He said "Son, let's ride on this donkey since we can't end this with other peoples' opinions. So they both rode on the donkey & went.

Now, what is the wisdom in this story? The wisdom is this; Allah Almighty granted His servant a qismat/destiny, a halal (spouse), a life.. If that person accepts this qismat, this life inside himself, he is comfortable with everything & makes shukur. But if he says "That one has a better car, this one lives a better life..." he goes towards the ungratefulness & stops making shukur. Therefore shukur- a person being thankful for his situation & feeling that what is granted to him is what belongs to him, is the best feeling, it is the best wasila/means for shukur. That is why we are saying that when Allah Almighty give things for His servant, must His servant see this one & say "shukur ya Rabbi, shukur Alhamdulillah" & when he make mistake like me, he says "Tawba ya Rabbi, tawba Astaghfirullah".

So with this one we say, Wa min Allah at-tawfiq. Bi hurmat al Habib, bi hurmat al Fatiha.
