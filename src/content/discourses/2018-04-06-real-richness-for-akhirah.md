As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa raḥmatu Llāhi wa barakātuh. A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīn wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

In the mosque of our ProphetNone, al-Masjid an-Nabawī, there were poor ṣaḥābah. They used to live there to obtain knowledge. They were poor and had no jobs. Their biggest duty was that they were useful to our ProphetNone. They sometimes could hardly stand on their feet because of hunger. Yet they didn't leave our ProphetNone even in that condition. They were together with himNone, in hisNone assembly. Our ProphetNone asked them one day – of course, everything is to teach people – "Is this assembly better for you or later when AllāhNone will conquer all the countries and will give you favours? Is this or that better for you?" And for people to be a lesson, they said, "Of course, the latter one." Favours and barakah will come, there will be conquests. They said, "Big favours will come to us from AllāhNone, that is better." Our ProphetNone swore that the first is better for them. It is so.

You don't see the favours and mercy of AllāhNone, HisNone watching you and the reward HeNone is giving. You only see the apparent favours and think they are good. Our ProphetNone swore that the first is better. And the richness we mentioned is not the material richness. Our ProphetNone mentioned in his ḥadīth, we recited in the khuṭbah, "Laysa l-ghiná ‘an kathrati l-‘araḍi." The richness... HeNone didn't say goods but ‘araḍ. That means all kinds of wealth that is temporary. Even if you have so much business, so many shops, so many positions and goods and money, all of them is ‘araḍ – meaning temporary. You can't be rich with them because even if you give the whole world to a man, he asks for more. His ego is not rich but poor. The ego is poor. Even if the whole world belongs to him, he won't be rich. "Wa lākinna l-ghiná ghina n-nafsi." This richness is in your soul. If you don't give value to this, even if you don't have anything, you are rich, you are not needy, you are not down. The rest are down, they need dunyā.

Our ProphetNone is teaching this by every means, through the words of ṣaḥābah. HeNone tested them with their words. And people are same until Qiyāmah. They are giving value to useless temporary things. Because when a person closes his eyes, he has nothing. They don't send anything with him. Nothing goes down to their graves. Nothing goes down but the shroud. Even if the shroud is made of gold, they find it out and take it up. Therefore, you don't take anything with you to the hereafter. You should pay attention to the real richness, says our ProphetNone. Ask for it. If you want to gather the goods of dunyā, you are poor. But if you use it for ākhirah, then you'll have benefit. May AllāhNone give all of us this understanding. If a person thinks and understands... It is important to think and contemplate: "What is this? How far will we go? What will happen to this?" Then a man does good things. May AllāhNone make all of us capable of doing those good things, in shā’a Llāh. May HeNone let us be able to do goodness.

ProphetNone teaching us with his companions also sometimes. Many times heNone speaking, giving us what to do, what we must do. Once heNone was sitting with ṣaḥābah – Aṣḥābu ṣ-Ṣuffah they call this ṣaḥābah. They are maybe hundred or two hundred one they was living in Masjid an-Nabawī where ProphetNone build this mosque. And this people poor or not poor they are coming there just to listening to ProphetNone to not lose any minute away from himNone. They left work or anything, only asking for knowledge and to be company ProphetNone. Quickly learning what everything heNone saying, catching like record and for new Qur’ān verses coming they was also sitting there waiting. There when something coming to ProphetNone or heNone has or some people giving food or anything for them, clothes, there was enough for them. It is normally not enough. Maybe nowadays one man maybe eats more than hundred of this people. This time like this but that days they was only not caring about this.

Once ProphetNone... And they have also clothes, very little clothes – only one maybe half, up is open only they have down. So they are. Once ProphetNone saying for them "Look" or "My ṣaḥābah, my companion, you are here like this." Sometimes from hunger they cannot, couldn't stand up. Sometimes when praying some of them fall down from weakness of hunger. And ProphetNone he call them and tell them, "Look you are like this but AllāhNone good tiding for you. You will be, after me, all this Islām it will be spread all around countries and it will be come from there many thing. You will be rich. You will be find many thing to eat. Not only you are now – if we find one bread you are happy. Everything it will come for you. Which one it is better?" heNone ask them, "Now or that time." And because they was very poor and they said, "O ProphetNone we cannot lie – that it will be better one we think," they said for ProphetNone. "Everything AllāhNone give in that time, all this provision, provision coming from all around our place and it will be very good." He said, ProphetNone swore, "No" said "this time it is better. This time it is better. This time is better."

Because they saw only what provision who appear for like food, like drink, like clothes. This is what they was thinking but what they don't seeing what ProphetNone seeing, what AllāhNone send them from HisNone bless and HisNone reward. It is of course as ProphetNone swore hundred thousand time or million time better than what they was thinking. This is because it is not real one. Real one for ākhirah. And ProphetNone saying, in khuṭbah we said ḥadīth. It is: "Laysa l-ghiná ‘an kathrati l-‘araḍ wa lākinna l-ghiná ghina n-nafsi." This Prophet'sNone ḥadīth meaning – don't think you are rich when you have many things – but ProphetNone he is very, his language it is heavenly language and giving exactly meaning for this. HeNone didn't say for if you have money, too many money you have, you will be rich. He said ‘araḍ. ‘Araḍ meaning who we have everything in this, it is money, it is gold, it is shops, it is cars it is women, it is lands – everything including this world. But ‘araḍ meaning it is like coming, going – not stable. With this you cannot be rich. Only you will be rich if you are, you have, you don't look for this. This is because if you look for this, you are not rich. Even they give you whole world it is not enough for you you will say if any more. You are poor man.

The rich one who was not giving any value for this. Maybe they he can have it, but not giving value to keep it for himself. He can make charity, he can make good thing for Ummah. But other one only for himself and it will be going from his hand. When he is going, passing away, he cannot take anything with him. In graveyard only they put one material coffin and they put in there. If they put coffin from gold, thiefs it will be come and take this also. So only he can take with him only this material and it will be rotten after a while. So real richness who [it is] can take with you for next life. Next world with you only you can take what ProphetNone showing people, his companion, "not this what you say it will be better. The better what we, you cannot, you couldn't see it."

"We are doing, we are doing, we are doing," some people saying. "We are doing but we not feeling anything." Of course if you feel you will be no meaning for this to feel. You must believe without questioning this order coming from our ProphetNone, AllāhNone send order to ProphetNone and ProphetNone saying to us. So you must do this until end of your life, not asking "I feeling, I not feeling." And it is big favour to be in this way, but of course, many people they are not happy for you to be in this way. They are jealous. Especially shayṭān and his followers they are very jealous from you. When you are in this good way and you must be happy and thankful for AllāhNone – not asking "I feeling, I am not feeling." This is people they thought they will be like saints from beginning. Some saint they passed away without feeling anything also. This is also from AllāhNone. What HeNone want to give you HeNone can give you. What you are, you are obligatory to do it, not to ask "what is this? "What is, why this? Why that?" Especially for ṭarīqah people. Only you obey and continue. And without feeling you will be in Day of Resurrection you see how it is you are in highest place with ProphetNone and with companions, in shā’a Llāh.

AllāhNone make us, all of us, to be with them, in shā’a Llāh; to be away from shayṭān and his followers and waswās, in shā’a Llāh. Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
