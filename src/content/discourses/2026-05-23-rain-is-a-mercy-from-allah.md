As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِى يُنَزِّلُ ٱلْغَيْثَ مِنۢ بَعْدِ مَا قَنَطُوا۟ وَيَنشُرُ رَحْمَتَهُۥ ۚ وَهُوَ ٱلْوَلِىُّ ٱلْحَمِيدُ
<br>“Wa huwa l-ladhī yunazzilu l-ghaytha min baʿdi mā qanaṭū wa yanshuru raḥmatahu wa Huwa l-Walīyu l-Ḥamīd”<br>
“And He it is Who sends down the rain after they have despaired, and spreads abroad His Mercy. And He is the Walī (Helper, Supporter, Protector), Worthy of all Praise.” (Qurʼān 42:28). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

It is Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla who sends the rain. Rain is a mercy. Sometimes it is mercy, sometimes it is wrath. But shukr to Allah , this year mercy came. It hasn’t been like this for years. Sixty years ago, it was raining like this every year, it was beautiful. There was rain everywhere. Sixty years ago, the state of the world became so bad, there was rebellion against Allah, kufr against Allah, not recognizing Allah. Because of this, Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla withdrew His mercy from people.

That’s why they’ve been complaining, “There’s drought, there’s such and such.” After that, shukr to Allah. But people haven’t turned to Allah completely. Very few people turned to Him, but people’s opposition to Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla disappeared. They forgot about it, they became engrossed in other things. They indulged in their own desires and lusts, but of course, they are still rebelling against Allah and leading everyone astray. The situation was much intense at that time.

The 1960s, the sixties of the last century, were the time when disbelief was at its peak. It was a time when opposition to religion was very strong. Because of this, Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla withdrew His mercy, causing drought and other hardships for people. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla may not send a drop of rain if He wishes. But He still sends it for the honor of believers, for the honor of the poor and needy. These rains fall for honor of the Awliyā’, shukr to Allah.

As we said, in that time of the last century, all kinds of evil reached their peak. The greatest injustice is rebellion against Allah. 

إِنَّ ٱلشِّرْكَ لَظُلْمٌ عَظِيمٌ
<br>“Inna sh-shirka laẓulmun ʿaẓīm”<br>
“Verily! Joining others in worship with Allah is a great Zulm (wrong) indeed.” (Qurʼān 31:13). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

Associating partners with Allah is a great injustice, and it is an injustice done to Allah. They didn’t make shirk; they did not recognize Allah at that time.

Therefore, nothing is without wisdom. Shukr to Allah, people are witnessing this again this year. Inshā’Allāh, may it be good tidings. It means that the coming of the sāhib of this religion is near, and these blessings and mercy have begun to spread. Inshā’Allāh, this is good tidings.

Of course, only Allah knows when it will happen. Sometimes they say, “The Shaykh said he will appear this year.” We wait. We wait every minute. The promise of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla is true. Our belief that he will come is certain. We accept every good thing as good tidings, inshā’Allāh. May Allah send the sāhib soon.

Because in his time, there will be much barakah. Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim used to say, it will rain at night, and the sun will shine during the day. There will be such beautiful days. There will be two harvests a year. Animals will give birth twice. He says everything will be prosperous, and so it will be.

Therefore, these things are good tidings, inshā’Allāh. It means it is approaching. May Allah let us meet him. There has been too much oppression, too much everywhere.

Therefore, we await him as good tidings, and may the savior of mankind come, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
