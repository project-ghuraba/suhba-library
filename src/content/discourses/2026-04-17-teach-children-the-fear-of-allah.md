As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Sayyidinā ʿAli karrama Allahu wajhahu says,<br>
رَأْسُ الْحِكْمَةِ مَخَافَةُ الله
<br>“Ra's ul-hikmah makhafat Allah.”<br>
“The head of wisdom is fear of Allah.”

The pinnacle of everything is fear of Allah. The head of every good deed, every intelligent act, is the fear of Allah. He says this is the head of all good things.

What is wisdom? It is the highest thing in Islām, in humanity. Wisdom means being intelligent, using one’s mind for good. When this is not taught, mankind loses its humanity and becomes worse than animals. Shayṭān makes them forget it, he doesn’t want it. Shayṭān teaches them, “Don’t do that. Don’t fear Allah. Don’t be ashamed before people.” Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla, through His Prophets, taught us to be good-natured and good people.

Fearing Allah is the highest goodness. One who fears Allah will not harm or torment anyone. They will only do good. But those who do not fear Allah: we see their situation clearly in front of us. People are devouring each other. This is being done everywhere.

Here, they say it’s forbidden to fear Allah. They say, “Don’t fear Allah.” That’s what they teach. That’s the new learning system. Therefore, the question of why children are like this is clear. There’s no need to say, “He did this, he did that,” etc. You’re not teaching them to fear Allah. You’re saying, “Disobey Allah.” You’re saying, “Don’t do what Allah says.” And then you expect good. Don’t expect it. The solution is to pay attention to what you teach these children.

Teach them the fear of Allah. You teach them from morning till night. What do you teach? Useless things, bad things. “Do whatever Europe says. Don’t believe in Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. Don’t believe in the Prophet at all. Don’t respect the Awliyā’. Don’t look at good people as good.” That’s what they teach.

There are so many beautiful things to teach. Allah has entrusted them to you; teach them well so that it will be beneficial. It will benefit you, them, and humanity. That is what it means to fear Allah. May Allah protect us. May Allah reform them and us all, inshā’Allah.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq

Al-Fātiḥa.

There are Qur’ān Khatms, Yasin, ayāt, surahs, tasbihāt, tahlilāt, salawāt, khatms of Dalail al-Khayrāt, and good deeds done; we gift them all to the soul of our Prophet ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, his family and companions, all the prophets, saints, pious people, and mashāyikh; for the souls of believing men and women, Muslim men and women; for the arrival of good and the warding off of evil; to be a means for goodness; for happiness here and hereafter inshā’Allah.

Lillāhi Taʿāla

Al-Fātiḥah.
