As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh. A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Qul a-ra’aytum in ja‘ala Llāhu ‘alaykumu l-layla sarmadan ilá yawmi l-qiyāmati man ilāhun ghayru Llāhi ya’tīkum bi-ḍiyā’in, a-fa-lā tasma‘ūn” (28:71). Aw kamā qāl. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla created everything beautifully. And He created this worldly life also beautifully. Everything in this life has its counterpart. The day has the night. There is black and white. Oppression has justice against it. Everything has its opposite or counterpart in this world. Why is this so? Because it is a world of tests. As it is a world of tests, there are shayṭān and angels. There are believers and kāfirs. Everything has its opposite. Nothing exists alone. Nothing is singular. The only One is Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. He is the possessor of everything. He has no equal or similar. Ḥāshā. All the others are human, and each certainly has its opposite. Those who are alike stay together. Those who are opposite stay with opposites. Believers are with the Prophets, with our Holy Prophet. They are with him. Who is his opposite? It is shayṭān. The devils are with shayṭān. Those who follow his path are with him. This is the will of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla in this world. That is why some so-called “clever ones” say things like, “If I were in charge...” Many speak without thinking. “I wouldn’t leave anyone poor, or allow any disbelievers, or permit this or that.” Sometimes people speak such nonsense that they unknowingly oppose Allāh. Ḥāshā. It is Allāh who has taught you what you know. Does Allāh not know how to make them all believers? To create them all as Muslims? To make them all good? Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla’s wisdom is such that He created everything as a test. That’s why, while in this world, mankind always says this. We express it in different ways, but the important thing is contentment. It is important to be content with what you experience and with the days you live. Allāh has given every blessing. We must give shukr to Allāh and be content. We must obey Allāh and give shukr for the blessings Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla has given. We must not object to anything. There is wisdom in everything. People are oppressed, people suffer. But they have their Lord, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. He will reward them if they are patient. If they are not patient, they suffer in vain. Therefore, as we said, everything in this world has its opposite. Be on the good side. Be on the bright side. Don’t be on the dark side. Don’t be with the oppressors. Don’t be with kāfirs. Be with people of nūr, with good people. Stay away from the bad. That’s it. So, this seems bad – but without night, one cannot know the day. Of course, in Paradise there is no heat, no daylight, nothing like that. Paradise is different. But dunyā is different. This is how dunyā is. We must understand this. “If I were in charge, I would do this or that.” The best was Merkez Efendi Hazretleri. His shaykh, because he loved him, said, “Let me test the murīds.” He told everyone to take a piece of paper and write what they would do if the world were in their hands. Each wrote: “I wouldn’t leave any oppressors, any disease, any evil,” and so on. They wrote many things: “I would do this and that.” They asked Merkez Efendi what he would do. Merkez Efendi said, “I would leave everything in its centre.” That’s why his name became Merkez (centre) Efendi. He meant: “I would leave everything as it is. Allāh ’s decree is best; He has done what is best. I have no objection to anything of His.” This is how true believers are – those with contented hearts. May Allāh make us among those who are content with Allāh. And may Allāh be pleased with us. Allāh is pleased, but many people are not content with Allāh. Ḥāshā. They complain about this and that. Those who remain discontent as much as they want have lost themselves, because they have no balance, no mind. Those who oppose Allāh may claim to be educated and cultured. But it’s all just chaos and noise. Their culture is nothing but noise. That is what those who oppose Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla are like. Therefore, we must pity them. They think highly of themselves and speak arrogantly. Many stronger than them also opposed Allāh. They will suffer eternal loss. They will be in ruin and loss. May Allāh protect us.

We are saying, Allāh in Holy Qur’ān, He mentioning everything, and one of verses saying, “If we not doing night, who will be, how you can be rest? If no day how? You will be in darkness whole time.” So Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, He create everything, like twin, or same [in pairs]. There is two kinds. There is darkness, there is light. There is good, there is bad. There is black, there is white. There is non-believer, there is believer. There is oppressor, and there is justice. There is prophets, there is shayṭān. Everything Allāh create two kinds, not only one kind. So only one, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. No anything like Him. Nothing compare. Nothing at all. Beyond our mind, beyond every thinking. He create this. Even from this, people they must look and accept His greatness. His greatness, to make people with small thing, can, who thinking, to accept Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. So, He make this world two kinds. There is opportunity to choose, people, if they like to be believer or non-believer. To be happy, to be unhappy. So, this is our situation for whole creature: this world there is two kinds – night and day. And blessed one, cursed one. Blessed one, against. Two kinds – only blessed and only cursed one also. So who are with white, light, with prophets. With darkness, with shayṭān. Shayṭān, who are with shayṭān, they are the loser. They are not have any chance for them to be happy, to be satisfied. So, in shā’a Llāh... Many people, they are, without thinking, they are speaking, “If I have authority, I can change this poorness, to not make people to be poor. I will change people to not be ill, and [I will] change people to not with be oppressor.” Change this, change that – this is what they are thinking. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla... They are not thinking, “We are thinking not good, which Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, He create us, and He know best what He done, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.” You are in this, your small mind, you thinking like this. You must be repent and ask for forgiveness from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, to not, for this what you did. Believer must be sensitive for this matter. Many people, they are not happy with what Allāh give them. Allāh give you this, to be in side, of better side, not worse side. There is also better side, worse side. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are all in the better, best side, al-ḥamdu liLlāh. With īmān, Islām, we are in our best side. And it is very precious, cannot be value... cannot give value for what you have. For this, there is one in Istanbul, big awliyā’, they calling him Merkez Efendi, because he was murīd for one shaykh. So around him, Shaykh, he was loving him too much. And the other murīd, they was little bit jealous for him. So his shaykh said to murīd, “I will make one test for you. In shā’a Llāh, we look who win, we can make him khalīfah for me, or to be wakīl for me.” So they was, [to] everyone [it was] said, “You write what you will do if you ruling this world. If Allāh give you all full authority to do anything for this world, for this humanity, for human being.” And everyone they was writing, writing, writing. And they bring the paper for Shaykh. And this shaykh, Merkez Efendi, he bring his paper, it was nothing, white, no one point. Look for other, he said, other say, speaking, “I will do this, I will that.” And at end, when he come to him, he said, “What you will do? You didn’t write anything.” He said, “What I want, to be nothing changing, exact like this. But Allāh happy with this, I accept. Only like this, it must be.” Centre, merkez meaning centre, meaning the point. “This is what we want,” he said. And this is, believer must be accept their situation, not to be sad, not to be worry, not to be afraid. Just accept, and Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, He is with you, in shā’a Llāh. Allāh make us to be satisfied with Him, to make Him happy with us. He is happy with us but we are not happy with Him. Most of people they are not happy with Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, subḥāna Llāh. Not accept what He said. All time complaining, complaining, complaining. Allāh give hidāyah for all of us, in shā’a Llāh. Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
