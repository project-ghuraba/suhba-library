We are knowing nothing. We are asking to learn as much as possible and we are saying, Assalam alaykum our attenders. Not really our attenders, we are weak servants but for the honour of the Seal of Prophets, the Lord of Heavens is sending riyah. Riyah, that means giving life to people and those winds coming from heavens, reaching to people because those heavenly winds give us real life attention. Real life power is coming through those winds [just] as winds through spring time giving life to nature and an opening coming.

In such a way, heavenly winds reaching from heavens to some on this dunya and those who are prepared for spring winds, spring winds reaching from heavens and giving life to nature. And now we are asking heavenly winds to reach ourselves to makes ourselves to be blessed ones. Those winds, heavenly winds giving ourselves real life's power. Therefore those who are asking to be in a good position through this life and after it they must give their attentiveness for accepting those heavenly winds, windy airs, to reach our physical being, and then physical being taking its share.

Our spirituality is our real being - no one knows the beginning of our spirituality. We may know our tazahur, our appearance, physically here we know it, but for our heavenly appearance from which time beginning because time, no need of time there, no time. Through the line of pre-Eternal to Eternal, no time on it. And anytime, we may say that appearances from Creation Oceans reaching and coming in existence. Therefore now, we are on this planet and we are needy of windy airs to come and to give us something from heavenly grants of our Lord. Therefore, those who are asking to live hayatun tayyibah, a good life through pleasure and through enjoyful life they must look to reach heavenly winds, these we may [call] ‘windy airs from heavens’.

We must be ready or we must be prepared, for this to come to ourselves. When we are saying this and asking from heavenly winds, through every association, heavenly winds giving our physical being pleasure as well as giving for our spirituality endless pleasure and enjoyment. Therefore I am people, O our attenders, try to be present for such meetings that are supported through heavens. Run and take that heavenly pleasure and enjoyment or you should be on this planet through darkness. Who is entering darkness, fear is covering him and also, no looking [sight] for him, for them. He can't see, he can't hear, he can't taste, he can't be joyful. But people running after material enjoyments. They are zero! Material enjoyment never giving anything to mankind. O our attenders, leave everything for a short time and ask heavenly pleasure through heavenly, windy waves that are reaching to earth to accept that one, and to reach real life here, so that you are not going to be dust through your graves, your physical being should also be just protected through earth. It is not so important but your spirituality [spiritual being] is needy of reaching heavenly enjoyment and needy of heavenly pleasure and they are in need, they're needy for heavenly satisfaction so that they may reach also heavenly Beauty Oceans. You can't enter that ocean with your material being, no!

Thanks! Countless thanks and praisings and glorifying to our most Mighty and most Majestic Lord, Lord of All Creation. And we are saying audhu Billahi min ash-shaytani r-rajim. That is something, a grant from heavens, you must run after it to take even one precious stone, even one diamond, even one ruby or emerald. Reach and take, even one! That is a chance we have been granted. Why you are being so heedless? And in front of you are heaps of jewels, diamonds, and pearls, and rubies, and emeralds. Why you are not opening your eyes to take as much as possible?

Assalamu alaykum our attenders! And say: audhu Billahi min ash-shaytani r-rajim. That is a preface, what we are speaking now, [what they are] making me to speak. They preface a beginning for a precious meeting that is making or preparing people to reach real pearl fields, or to reach real diamond fields, or for reaching real ruby fields. They're not small ones but heaps of them. And if a man is going to be happy with a small ruby or a small diamond, what about if he is seeing a heap of diamonds that are shining. How he should be if it had been said to him, ‘that is for you’?

But shaytan, - O our Lord, make that enemy of mankind to be far away from ourselves, - coming from so many manfaz, finding so many ways to reach people to make them heedless about huge jewel mounds. And this dunya you may be enjoying, but your life is just an endless grant from Allah Almighty and our souls are never going to die. Our physical being after a while is going to finish, to fall dead. But, what is enjoying with man is an understanding that everything, that the most valuable jewels belong to our spirituality.

Assalamu alaykum once again to you, O heedless ones. All of us heedless, running after dunya and leaving real, valuable grants of our Lord. That is the last limit of being heedless. But shaytan's trips making people fall in it. Therefore Allah Almighty saying “O My beloved one”,

wa dhakkir fa inna adh-dhikra tanfa'u al muminina (51:55). And remind, for indeed the reminder benefits the believers.

“Oh My most beloved one, say to My servants that in front of them are so many heavenly grants that never eyes saw it, or ears never heard it, or our mentality never reaching for an understanding of what I've prepared for My servants”. Assalam Alaykum O Salafi ulamas. Wahabi people are not understanding. Their understanding - they're imprisoning themselves among four walls. Imprisoned, they are not able to destroy those walls and to look what is in existence and making for people prisons so that they can't move in any direction and people are thinking that Allah Almighty sending Islam for imprisoning His servants. No, but they are saying, yes! Wahabi people are saying that Allah sending heavenly orders for imprisoning His servants. Therefore we are also imprisoning people through kufr, shirk, haram and bida, so that they aren’t able to move in any direction. No, Allah Almighty sending His heavenly orders not to imprison His servants, no!

Allah Almighty only sending His heavenly orders to make shaytan and its followers [to be] defeated by the Children of Adam. Allah Almighty created Adam and Eve and put them in Paradise and Paradise was not prison. Tafham, understand! O Wahabi people and Salafi ulamas you are making people to be in prison, no, Allah Almighty just created Adam wa askanahum (and housed them). He made their living place to be Paradise, because Allah Almighty created men to grant to them from His endless Grant Oceans, not to make mankind in prison. But shaytan making his followers to make this life as a prison for mankind. No, no.

And the first lifestyle Allah Almighty was showing to His servants [was that] “I have created you for Paradise, listen to Me, follow Me, and obey Me [and] you will be here forever. But there will be a most terrible enemy for you, that is shaytan as he never accepts to bow to your father, Adam. And beware, that one is asking to take you away from Paradise. And beyond Paradise, only punishment, only miseries, only sufferings, only troubles, only bad things, only darkness. Nothing else!

I've created eight paradises for you to give to you from My endless blessings, ni’mat, endless grants. But that one, shaytan, asking to put you in a square but that is a place without any possibility to get out. Shaytan asking you to be in such a place that you never going to be happy there. That is shaytan's target, but I have created you and put you in Paradise. I like that you may be there forever. But shaytan, it is most terrible enemy for you, asking to be always in prison”.

Therefore, awliyas never like to make for mankind, prisons. But shaytan and its followers asking to make people through prison always, not to look and to see, not to hear and understand, not to eat, no, but no taste. Everything shaytan asking for men to be mahrum (deprived of). Yes! Therefore Allah Almighty ordering His most honored one, "Say to your nation, for their mu’amalat, dealing with each other, they must use My Command and My Wish, to be followers of My Holy Command through yourselves I am sending My Holy Demand, “yassiru wala tu'assiru bashshiru wala tunaffiru” (make things easy, not difficult, spread good news, not bad). O Marhaban! Marhaban ya, Salafi ulamas, you are accepting? For every step you are putting something, preventing people to move, and you are doing as much as possible to make hard, their lives and imprisoning them, never giving My heavenly pleasures to them. Saying, no! Haram, bida, shirk, kufr. How should people move? Say, O People! Your Lord created you in Paradise and asking to be there up to Eternity. Preparing Eternity for you.

Therefore, it is a very hard time now that we are in because so many people who are claiming they know something, and guiding people, saying they are on truth, no. Your guidance never showing the ways of Paradise. Always you are cutting the truth to make people to fall into fire. May Allah forgive us, O People! Listen, and obey now and be happy. Leave [shaytan] and you should be protected, for the honor of the Seal of Prophets, Sayyidina Muhammad sallallahu alayhi wa sallam. Fatiha.
