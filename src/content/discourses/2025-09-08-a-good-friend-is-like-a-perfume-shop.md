A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah. Our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says, being with good people and good friends is beneficial for a person. Being with bad friends is not good, says our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam likened it to this: A good friend is like a shop that sells pleasant perfume. You go in there, and if you are going to buy something, you buy it. If you don’t buy, you still enjoy the pleasant scent. At least, you feel refreshed and leave. This is like a good friend. Because if a good friend has a good nature, he will not hurt you, he will not harm you. He will not do you bad. His behaviour is good, his nature is good, you can get along with him. Our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam likened a good friend to a perfume shop. Every kind of comfort comes to a person when he has a good friend. He commands what is good, leads to what is beautiful, and shows the way of Allāh. He becomes a good person in this world as well. To talk with him, to be with him, does not cause you harm. He does not say bad things. Being with that person is a good thing. Our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says to be with him. Stay away from bad people. Our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam also likened it to a blacksmith’s shop. Now, of course, that practice is mostly gone. There used to be blacksmith’s shops in the past. They would light a fire. They would use bellows to make the fire stronger. While doing that, smoke would come out. Besides the smoke, some other materials would also be used in the process so that the iron became stronger and better. But standing there beside it, standing in a blacksmith’s shop, was not pleasant for people. Either they were disturbed by the smoke, or while he was working, sparks would fly out and burn your clothes. So, that is like a bad friend. If you stay next to a bad friend, surely his evil will affect you. Therefore, from those bad people, stay away, says our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Be with good people. As for bad people, they will either harm you with words or speech, or in some other way. Any kind of evil can come from them. Therefore do not approach them, says our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. You look at a person once, twice, or three times or so, and you see no good in him. Each time you go there, he treats you worse. So, it is better not to approach him. This is the saying of our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. May that person also be good, so that he is like a perfume shop, like good people, in shā’a Llāh. In this life this is what a person needs for his comfort, and to live a good life. With goodness and good friends, a person does good to others. People must get along by doing good to each other. Otherwise, if people see someone and feel like running away, may Allāh protect us. May we not be with them, in shā’a Llāh. May Allāh grant goodness to us all, in shā’a Llāh.

Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam was saying, for ḥadīth – it is Arabic ḥadīth, we must say it, but we must say it correctly. Once you say ḥadīth [in Arabic], you must say it as Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying. So we are, we are translate English or Turkish. This ḥadīth saying, to be with good people, it is like good friend or good people. They are like good, selling nice perfume, very nice perfume shop. When you go inside, enter inside shop, if you buy or not buying, you are be happy with smelling nice smell, good thing, and you, nothing wrong for you, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying. This is, he make example for good people. They are like this. If you are with them, they are, you will be not hearing any bad thing about you, or not angry for you, or making you unhappy, all this. They are helpful, they are giving good advice, giving good smile, and they are helpful for everything. This is the good friend example, for Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, he give, for Muslim to be like this, and to be with people like this. They are make you happy, whole your life and ākhirah also, you be happy. Because they are following Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, his life like this, no any bad thing. He is most perfect example for us. And he give example for bad friend, bad people, who are, if you go like smith, iron smith, black smith who making iron shop. Old time, they was. Now no, you cannot, very rare place you can find this shop. They are working with fire, and making iron, taking from fire to melt or to be soft. And they heat it, and then, they doing this. But when they doing this, there is smell of some kind of material they put with iron, or smoke, or another thing. When they put in this water also, they have some bad smelling. They must put, sometimes they put urine of horse or something, to make it more strong, this iron. And so it is not nice to be there. Even, you hear bad smelling, or smoke with smell, or some little fire can jump on your clothes, burn something from you. So when you are there, you are whole time in not good mood, or to not good place to be there. And this is like bad friend. You say, “I’m following this. He is just a friend. I be with him.” No. Even like this blacksmith’s place, you will be not happy. Something will happen for you. So when you are following bad friend, it is, it will must be something going bad for you. Sometimes very small, sometimes bigger, sometimes it destroying your whole your life. So must be following order of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, and to be with good people. Maybe you said, “I, my friend, maybe he can be coming take naṣīḥah from me and he can be again better.” You can try sometimes. If no benefit, you must know this: he try to make you like him. Because, this people they are very jealous. If you are in good life, they are not happy. They like to, like you to be like them, to destroy your life like what they destroy their life. But they don’t know – if they are following you, they will be safe. But these people, most of them, not doing this. Allāh keep us safe from this people. Bad friend, this is worse than shayṭān. Shayṭān he is poor one, comparing to bad friend. Allāh keep them away from ourself and our children, our people, in shā’a Llāh. Allāh save us from their bad thing, in shā’a Llāh. Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
