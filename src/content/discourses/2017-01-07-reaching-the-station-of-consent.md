Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

The grandness of Allah Azza wa Jalla cannot be thought of or imagined. Not that it fits but it does not even pass by the border of intelligence and imagination. Moreover, people go ahead and do not recognize Allah. Allah Azza wa Jalla sent to these people every morsel of food. Eeach drop of water descends with rain together with an angel. The angel makes that rain reach the ground. They talk about nature and what not, but not even a drop would reach the ground if Allah Azza wa Jalla did not order it.

Allah Azza wa Jalla knows the number of angels. Nobody can think of or imagine how many angles Allah Azza wa Jalla created. Each angel brings each drop down to the ground. This is its job. Then it ascends back. When it snows, angels also descend with snow and stay on earth. This is why it is more blessed (more baraka). Allah Azza wa Jalla is being more merciful when it snows. We need to be thankful for this.

People have no gratitude. They look for an excuse to rebel against Allah every minute. They complain, “It is hot. It is very hot. O it is too hot! It is cold. It is so cold! It snowed and we could not get out of our house. What a horrible thing!” So they object to everything without thinking, and they are opposing Allah. Those with faith, meaning those who are Muslim, also do this. Those who are not Muslim are one thing, but there are also people with faith who speak like this without thinking. We need to think, “I wonder if I am opposing Allah?”

Being content with everything and rising to the level and station of consent is a good thing. People in the past would suffer torture, suffer torment, for being on the way of Allah, but they were still content for being on Allah’s path. Now they forget about Allah in the smallest thing. They start with cussing at the municipality, then at the government, then at the state, and so on.

This is something taught by Shaitan in the last 100 years. Because the whole world has leaned towards getting educated. The instructions in schools come from one place. They teach them all almost the same thing. “You will rebel against everything. You will object!” they say.

This rebellion started with Shaitan. Shaitan is the first rebel. Therefore, rebellion is not a very good thing. It has its own place. Nobody rebels when it is in place. They only rebel for their ego. May Allah make us amongst His servants who are content, who are content with everything, and who think of the wisdom behind everything.

Good is being done and people still see the good as bad. Allah Azza wa Jalla is showing us so much mercy: He gives us rain, He gives us snow, and even that is seen as bad. You can imagine the rest. We say “May Allah give us common sense and intelligence”, because people have become extremely foolish lately.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq. Al-Fatiha.
