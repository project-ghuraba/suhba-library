As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

1 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

اعْزِلِ الْأَذَى عَنْ طَرِيقِ الْمُسْلِمِينَ

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Remove from the path of Muslims anything that would cause them harm.” Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam commanded that whatever is on the path, whether it be stones, rubble, or other harmful things, be removed.

2 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

أَمِطِ الْأَذَى عَنِ الطَّرِيقِ، فَإِنَّهُ لَكَ صَدَقَةٌ

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Remove harmful things and bad things from the road, for this is charity for you.” Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam gives this order to all of us so that it will benefit us; it is a charity. Allah also gives reward for charity. Now some people say, “It’s on the road, what does it have to do with me!” They pass by it, and don’t touch it. However, if you remove it, you will attain the reward of charity and you will also gain thawāb.

3 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

غَفَرَ اللَّهُ عَزَّ وَجَلَّ لِرَجُلٍ أَمَاطَ غُصْنَ شَوْكٍ عَنِ الطَّرِيقِ، مَا تَقَدَّمَ مِنْ ذَنْبِهِ وَمَا تَأَخَّرَ

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla forgave the past and future sins of a man who removed the thorny branch on the road.” This is a big thing. His sins for the past are forgiven. This is our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam’s gift to us. It will also be a means for the forgiveness of the future sins; sins that will happen. Because our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam told about the forgiveness of future sins only for the Companions of Badr. But this [act] will be barakah for us and our future sins will be forgiven, inshā’Allāh. 

Now, the people of this time consider themselves clean, they do cleaning things, tygiene, mygiene, [hygiene]. But they are disgusted with food. Two days ago, we went to a beautiful place given by Allah. The entire side of the road and the mountain were stones, the dirt, and much food and drink they had eaten and thrown away. They ate and drank and threw away. What you’ve done is harmful to you! It is a burden upon you. I don’t know if it is a sin, but it is a burden. 

Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam told about this. This is the beauty of Islām. People show Islām as bad. Islām is the most beautiful, the best thing. Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam says, all the Ḥadīths are like this: be clean, do not be dirty, do not harm, do not throw dirt. Now you throw everything you eat and drink there. However, you can pick it up and put it in a bag, the municipality has put huge garbage bins, so you throw it there and they will take care of it. 

Masha'Allah, Muslims and non-Muslims, those who pray and those who do not: act the same. As they say, wherever there are many people, there is dirt. That’s exactly what’s happening. People are acting like that. Our Holy Prophet talks about goodness in all his Ḥadīths from beginning to end. Even the one who says he is a Muslim does not act upon this!

4 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

كَانَ عَلَى الطَّرِيقِ غُصْنُ شَجَرَةٍ يُؤْذِي النَّاسَ، فَأَمَاطَهَا رَجُلٌ، فَأُدْخِلَ الْجَنَّةَ

Our Holy Prophet says, “There was a branch on the road that was causing harm to humanity. A man lifted it and was admitted to heaven because of this deed.” What a great benefit it is for a person, for his hereafter, that he protects people from evil and harm. He becomes from the people of paradise, says our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. Just because he moved a branch out of the way.

5 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

لَقَدْ رَأَيْتُ رَجُلًا يَتَقَلَّبُ فِي الْجَنَّةِ؛ فِي شَجَرَةٍ قَطَعَهَا مِنْ ظَهْرِ الطَّرِيقِ، كَانَتْ تُؤْذِي النَّاسَ

Our Holy Prophet says, “I saw a person who cut down a tree on the road that disturbed people, swimming among the blessings in heaven,” says our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. His deed is that much [precious] that our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam saw that person; because he sees everything of the hereafter. Just because he cut down the tree hanging over the road so that people could pass by comfortably, he reached the gardens of Paradise, said our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam.

6 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

مَرَّ رَجُلٌ بِغُصْنِ شَجَرَةٍ عَلَى ظَهْرِ طَرِيقٍ، فَقَالَ: وَاللَّهِ لَأُنَحِّيَنَّ هَذَا عَنِ الْمُسْلِمِينَ، لَا يُؤْذِيهِمْ، فَأُدْخِلَ الْجَنَّةَ

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “One person saw a tree on the road that was disturbing people.” So, while he was walking on a path, he saw a tree that was harmful on the road. People have a hard time passing through there. 

He said, “Wallah, I swear to God, I will move this tree to one side of the road so that it will not cause any more harm to Muslims.” So he removed it from the road. 

For this reason, our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam says, “For this reason, he will be admitted to heaven, he has become from the people of Paradise.”

7 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

مَنْ أَخْرَجَ مِنْ طَرِيقِ الْمُسْلِمِينَ شَيْئًا يُؤْذِيهِمْ، كَتَبَ اللَّهُ لَهُ بِهِ حَسَنَةً، وَمَنْ كَتَبَ لَهُ عِنْدَهُ حَسَنَةً، أَدْخَلَهُ اللَّهُ بِهَا الْجَنَّةَ

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Whoever removes something that causes harm from the path of Muslims, Allah writes a reward for him. Whoever Allah writes a reward for, he will enter him into Heaven with that reward.” Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla gives people opportunities so that they can enter Heaven. It also becomes a service to others, a service to people.

8 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

مَنْ أَمَاطَ أَذًى عَنْ طَرِيقِ الْمُسْلِمِينَ كُتِبَ لَهُ حَسَنَةٌ، وَمَنْ تَقُبِّلَتْ مِنْهُ حَسَنَةٌ دَخَلَ الْجَنَّةَ

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Whoever removes something that harms Muslims from their path, a reward will be recorded for him.” As we said, whether this thing was a thorn, a stone, an iron - anything that causes harm to a person is written as a reward for it. 

“Whoever has a reward or good deed accepted, that person will enter heaven.” So everything a person does isn’t definitely going to be accepted. But this will definitely be accepted that the person will receive the reward of that good deed and will go to heaven because of that good deed.

9 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

مَنْ رَفَعَ حَجَرًا مِنَ الطَّرِيقِ كُتِبَتْ لَهُ حَسَنَةٌ، وَمَنْ كَانَتْ لَهُ حَسَنَةٌ دَخَلَ الْجَنَّةَ

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Whoever removes a stone that causes harm on the road will receive a reward, and whoever has a reward will enter heaven.”

10 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

نَحِّ الْأَذَى عَنْ طَرِيقِ الْمُسْلِمِينَ

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Remove harmful things from the path of Muslims.” So this is an order. The one who obeys the order of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, will definitely gain reward, gain the pleasure of Allah, become a beloved servant of Allah and enter heaven.

Unfortunately, this issue is more prevalent in the countries of Muslims. The country where it’s most found - there are many, māshā'Allah. We have been to that country a lot. It’s called Pakistan: Pak means pure, clean country. But unfortunately, everything is worse than here. There is not a single square inch of it without nylon, garbage, dirt and pollution. 

So they left India because they are Muslims and wanted to live as Muslims. This is what Islām demonstrated by our Prophet is: it is cleanliness. So we say that one thing. Everywhere in our country is like that, every Muslim country is like that. In Europe, they force them to be like that. If they do not clean or be clean, there is a penalty for that. But still better than the Muslims. By Allah’s wisdom, the kāfir, infidel in this time is better than the Muslim. One of their shortcomings is they haven’t said the Shahadah. Our people, may Allah protect us, the Shahadah keeps us safe. Otherwise, we will be worse than them. May Allah protect us.

The Messenger of Allah says the truth in what he said or as he said.

Ziyadatan ila sharafi’ ‘n-Nabi, sallaLlahu `alayhi wa sallam wa Alihi wa Asahabihi l-kiram, wa ila arwahi jamee’i al Anbiya’i wa l-Mursalin wa Khudama’i sharai’ihim wa ila arwahi al ‘Aimmati al arba’a, wa ila arwahi Mashayyikhina fi t-Tariqati n-Naqshbandiyyati l-Aliyyah, khassatan Imamu t-ṭarīqah wa Ghawthu l-Khaliqah Khwaja Bahauddin Muḥammad al-Uwaisiyi l-Bukhari, Sayyidina Abdul Khaliq al Ghujduwani, Mawlana Shaykh Sharafuddin al Daghestani, Mawlana Shaykh Abdullah al Faiz al Daghestani, Mawlana Shaykh Muḥammad Nazim Adil al Haqqani, wa sai’ri Sadatina wa s-Siddiqiyun, wa man nahnu fi hadratihim wa jiwarihim. To the souls of all our ancestors. To the souls of all Shahīds. For goodness to come and evil to be warded off. For people to come to their senses. Li-Llahi Ta’ala, Al-Fātiḥah.

To the souls of all our ancestors. To the souls of all Shahīds. For goodness to come and evil to be warded off. To Allah Almighty. Al Fātiḥah.]
