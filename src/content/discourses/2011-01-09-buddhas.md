Audhu Billahi min al shaytan al rajim, Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim.

Welcome to you, O servants of Allah. What is our identity? They don't know about their identities now. They throw what is old, bring something false & made up. Now, this is an example: The furniture of a palace, if they throw it outside saying "it is old" & bring cubical things. Do they have any value? The people are such foolish ones now. This is the doing of those who came after the Sultanate. Throw away the old, bring new. Can the value of the old & new be the same? No, can't be. But there is no sense left anymore. Unreasonable things at full speed now! Who are you saying it to? You say & you listen to it yourself again. Allah bless you, you bring these young men here to me. What can we do? To bring one of these young ones closer to Allah is better than gaining whole world, says our Prophet (sas)- Sultan of Prophets, Beloved of Rahman (jwa). The value of man, Shakir. Hj Shakir?

G. Not yet, insha Allah soon.

M. Our Shakir Effendi son, the value of man with who he is committed to. Now a person who commits himself to a priest, his value as his value. Who commits himself to Jews, to their belief, as their value. Who is committed to dunya, as valuable as dunya. Who is committed to akhirat, is with akhirat's value. To whom we are committed, that when we mention his honourable name, we stand up. We say our Prophet, Sayyidina Muhammad Mustafa (sas). Who is committed to him, takes from his value. We should be careful about this. Who is committed to a coffee shop, his value as the coffee shop. Who goes to jeweler... Hj Shakir! Jeweler. Is the value of who works in coffee shop the same with who works in jeweler? Who lives for dunya can be same with who lives for akhirat? Here, dunya is going (towards it's end). Dunya is going.

You know these places for planes..? The man coming out from planes? Why do I use tayara (old Turkish word)? Tayyara is a high-spirited word, not a made up one. They say "ucak". Why should we use that word? What is wrong with tayara? For what? You have nothing else to do, other than making up things? For 90 years, since Sultanate is gone, what did you you achieve in this 90 years? Making up things! Invent, make up as much as you can. "kul/school", why you invent new words? What was wrong with "maktab"? Why are you enemies to peoples' speech? Why do you corrupt their language? Is it not so? What we are doing for 90 years is making up things. And the made up building has come to this situation,its foundation collapsed. Who did not make up, did steady work, are our ancestors. For 700 years they raised the flag of Ottomans, the flag of Islam. What did you raise? If you say these things, the Beys.. "Don't say Bey Sheikh Effendi, say Bay". (New word for Bey/Sir). Now they don't like it & returned to Bey again. Ladies & Gentlemen (in new turkish). May they all faint (word play) May they faint? What was wrong with "Bey & Hanim"? Why did they change it? Why? Why did you attack peoples' language, beliefs? Why you attacked peoples' beliefs, to remove them & bring out made-up beliefs?

Why do you put up statues in front of people who know their Lord? What is a statue? Doesn't talk. You put it up there & it sits. Egypt is full of statues. Rome is full of statues. Greece full of statues. Do they speak? Do they speak? What can they speak? Here, this is nonsense. Alhamdulillah, we will keep our way. Why to go there all the time.. We are Cypriots, so we may say it with Cyprus phrase; why should we go in front of the statue all the time? Doesn't see us. Doesn't hear us. Doesn't speak to us.

I went to..what is it's name? Singapore. Allah Almighty granted us to go there, made us go from East to West. When we went to Singapore.. Are you sleeping Shakir? Open your eyes! The first time we went there, there was a religious conference there. They also invited us to speak there, so we went. Of course, do we have money to buy tickets to go there? It needs a fortune to go to Singapore from here, to stay there & return. Anyways, in short we went there. We were staying there for a few days. We may say about this place, Singapore, that they may be ashamed. Singapore is maybe about one-tenth of this Cyprus, maybe even smaller. But it is one of the busiest, most productive cities in the world. Astonishing. We may say, it is a place as wide as the area within the city walls of Nicosia, or twice as wide. Full of buildings, bazaars, markets, businesses.. Everybody is on such a run, such an effort, such work.. And we say here.. Why should we not do the same? Why our side here, the Turkish side in Cyprus.. that if you put 20 Singapores together it can't reach the Turkish side in size.

They are such that...Shakir! If you look into the sea, you may think that it is a forest because of the ships, carrying goods. They are not passenger ships, all cargo. And when you go to the airport, planes constantly going back & forth, 3 land in one minute, they said. Plane..When you say plane, not simple old ones like ours, latest technology. And they don't stop & ask you "how much money did you bring?" in customs. They don't ask "what are you bringing, what are you taking" either. "Is there anything?" "No" "Pass" They don't search or anything. Free sea port, free airports. The people visiting enter easily & exit easily. It is not pain & suffering on top of each other, like ours: "Write this, sign this, bring this, take this, pay this..." Not like what we have... Is it civilization, what we have? What did we say now, when talking about Singapore... In Singapore, these people.. There are Muslims among them, there are Christians & Buddhists who worship Buddha. Hasha, Astaghfirullah. They worship him, it is their belief. The person who was accompanying us, you say guide, who show us around, that guide person said, "Shaykh Effendi, here Buddhists are crazy about Buddha's statue. But it is not an interest like we have. They are committed to that person with a strong belief". You understand? Meaning, they worship him. They worship Buddha. But they are not under any official pressure, from government or anything. No one forces them to go there. Who likes to go, go & who doesn't want, doesn't go. The one who was guiding us said "Here, they have their temple which has the largest Buddha statue in the world. I may take you there if you like & you see it". I said "No problem. We go & have a look". And so our guide took us & we went. It is an astonishing structure. Meaning, like the Chinese structures, a strange structure. We went inside. There is a building, a huge hall. Imagine it, like a theatre hall. It is such a big hall. When we went in, I looked maybe 40 meters above us there is a huge Buddha, his legs like this (cross-legged). He never opens his eyes anyways. Never opens, whichever statue of his you look at, in all of them he sits like this & his eyes closed. Never looks at you, that one they worship.

The servant of the temple immediately ran & came to us. She said, "Welcome sir". I said "We came well, thank you". She said, "it is a custom here to light incense & candles. If you will do this, I may give you some". I said, We don't believe in this that you worship that we light candles or incense. We only came here for a visit, our guide offered to show us this one called Buddha, which is the reason for your temple. She got a bit upset but I then continued: "How do you see this person?" According to our belief, this person is our God who we worship". I said "Hasha, thumma kalla", stamped my foot on the ground. "If this is who you worship, does this person see that your coming or us coming here?" "No, he doesn't". "If we talk, does he hear?" "No" "If we spit on him, does he raise his hand?" "No, he doesn't". "If we break one part of him, does he say 'oh, I got broken?'" "How many years that it is here?" "It is more than 2000 years". "For 2000 years, this one that you worship still does not open his eyes? He always sits like this. Does he not get tired inside this thing?" She said "it is our belief". I said, "Does he never lay down? He sits likes this, doesn't get tired?" She said to me, "you are the Shaykh of Muslims. I will show you that this, that we worship, sleeps too". She took me to the back. I saw that there is another one laying down. She said "Here he sleeps & here he sits".

I asked "How many Buddhas are there?" She said "Countless Buddhas. There are countless Buddhas here". "All like this?" "All we show respect like this. It is our religion". And this belief of ours shows us the Glorious Way, our spirituality grows stronger". "What spirituality do you have?" I asked. "If you have spirituality from Buddha, show me. Look, I will show you my hand. Pay attention". And at that time, the power got dressed on me. I said, "Come this way & this way". It came this way & that way! "Bow your head!" It bowed it's head. We saw that the servant lady fell down. I said "Let's go before we get into trouble. They will say 'this one destroyed Buddha'". For the ones who worship the new statue, if that one gives one sign, which he already did, not one can remain up in their places till morning. Its time will come. Its time will come...May Allah forgive us. It is the respect of these people for their own religion. The country which has the most statues in the world is first Singapore, second is Turkish Republic. We also filled (with statues). It is filled! It is said when something becomes plenty it's value goes down. It becomes cheap. It became very cheap. Before we, when archbishop passed away.. Are you sleeping, O Shakir?

G. No Mawlana, we are in a state of ecstasy.

M. Who brings these young men here.. I say in Turkish that they understand. If it is lie, they can go & ask that one (in Singapore).

G. Hasha!

M. What were we saying?

G. You said archbishop.

M. When archbishop pass away, they make him sit in his throne, chair. You understand? They dress him, put his crown on his head. They dress him with his clothes, etc and take him to a place. He stands there, all who pass by.. One person holds his hand like this. This one is from Cyprus? He knows such things. What was his name?

G. Husein.

M. Husein! Long live Husein. You are a clever one. One person holds the archbishop behind, holds his hand that people kiss his hand. And another person holds his head that it doesn't drop! They would take him like this to the cemetery, with parade. And they wouldn't make him lay in the cemetery either. They would lower him to the grave in his chair sitting like this, and then cover with soil. Now ours as well, in new fashion way after the Buddhist countries, second country with most statues has become Turkey. They may count (the statues) if they like. But they wouldn't be able to count (so many).

We may illustrate the statue with the example of plastic fruits. You may offer plastic fruits to your guest: "Here you go". He picks a banana & looks, it is plastic. Puts it back. Takes a grape & looks,it is plastic too. Takes an apple, it is as well plastic. Orange..He says "It is not possible to eat this. Why did you put this for us?" "We are treating you". "From nylon?" Is it nylon? Plastic. Can someone get full with plastic? Bring something else, at least you could bring yellow roasted chickpeas with dried grapes (famous Turkish snack mix). That it would be something when we put it in our mouth. But you put in front of us plastic things for 90 years; from bronze, from marble, from iron..And you say "Believe in this. Keep his word, don't you depart from it's rule". We didn't depart till now but now we did! Before they didn't depart but the new generation say "what is this giving us, what can it give us? If it gives us something, we may walk in his way. But if it can't give something, we are starving".

Meaning, our hunger is a spiritual hunger, not physical. We have so much to eat for physical hunger, but this can't satisfy our spirituality. Since it can't satisfy, then don't put this in front of us each time! Now the youth got hungry. They say "What is it giving us? If it is guiding you, then why don't you follow his way but instead you fight? Why are you fighting each other? That one you put up his statues, he didn't show you a way? If he did, why don't you follow it? Now you become 70 different parties. You speak ill of each other. You fight, debate each other, what is this? Why don't you follow his way? When I say this, they say "you don't know". No, I know what your father & also grandfather did not know. There is nothing you know. If there is a man who can come out & say what we speak of, he may say it.

G. There is no such one even in whole world other than you.

M. May Allah forgive us. Ok, this is enough for you now. There are so much to speak of, wouldn't finish even till morning. But I think this has been almost half an hour.

M. This is for the barakah of Husein, and you, Mehmet, and you also. Allah bless you. May Allah forgive me. What can we do,should we hide the Truth? Who doesn't speak the Truth is silent shaytan. Why should we not say? Why are you deceiving people? "Who deceives us is not one of us" said the Sultan of the Prophets (sas). Why are you deceiving the people? Here, whole Islam world is falling over & collapsing because they deceived. Who forgets Allah, who doesn't know the Prophet (sas) has so much to come on them.
