Waṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-F’āiz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-

A’ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi ’Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm.

The Holy Prophet (SAW) says, “Give glad tiding. Do not make them afraid.” Insha’Allah, glad tidings to all the servants of Allah, those who submit Allah: Everything happens with the will of Allah. Everything we do must be done for His sake, and then a man is at benefit, for otherwise no matter how much he does, or how great what he does seems in the estimation of people, if it is not for Allah’a people, there is no benefit coming from his deed.

This world is a place of test. There is no one who will not be tested. Those who have understood that this is a test from Allah are relaxed, but it is difficult for those who have not come to this realization.

In the Holy Quran, bismi ’Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm, Allah ‘azza wa jalla says, “Those who know and those who do not know are not equal.” No, they are not equal. Allah has told us everything in the Quran, from Adam’s (AS) time until the time of the Holy Prophet (SAW). Those who have faith should believe Him. Those can be relaxed who believe and act in submission with faith. If not, and if one follows unbelievers and does what they say, then one can never relax, and his household and children will not be relaxed [either]. We accept that all is from Allah. We are to accept everything that is ordained by Allah. We will fulfill Allah’s orders, and then we will trust Allah with regard to everything else.

Satan and his pipe-blowers are [now] leading people in whichever direction they wish and are governing them. In the midst of this wave, Muslims also submit to this and disturb themselves, too. In fact, Allah ‘azza wa jalla promised that after difficulty there is ease. “Inna ma‘a ’l-‘usri yusr, inna ma‘a ’l-‘usri yusra” (Qur’ān 94:5-6). Twice Allah says it. After any difficulty there is easiness. Certainly there will be ease; beautiful things will happen. Let this be a glad tiding to Muslims and to those with faith.

Do not be afraid. Allah is with us: you also be with Him, so that you may find comfort. May Allah grant us real iman. In this world, the most beautiful thing is iman. As long as you have iman, there is no need for anything. May Allah make our faith strong. Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-F ti ah
