As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad.

Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah. Our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says, “Al-Mu’minu mirātu l-Mu’min.” The mu’min (believer) is the mirror of his mu’min brother, says our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. A believer is different from a Muslim. A Muslim might not be the mirror. But a believer knows the person in front of him, the believer, as himself. He wants for him what he wants for himself. He wants the same for him. He does not want less for him. Just as he wishes every good for himself, he wishes the same for his mu’min brother as well. He knows him as himself. He treats him as himself. This is the believer with īmān. The true believers are those whom our ProphetNone loves, those who follow the way of our ProphetNone. They are those upon ṭarīqah and sharī‘ah.

Because the way is shown with sharī‘ah and ṭarīqah. Those who follow them are believers. And a believer: whatever good there is, it is in the believer. No harm comes from a believer. Our ProphetNone has ordered not to harm. The status of a believer is beautiful. A believer is a person who wants to benefit people and everyone. He wants people to enter the way, to enter paradise. That is why, most mashāyikh show the shaykh who will come after him as his mirror. They show what he did.

One of the most famous of the great awliyā’ is Mawlānā Jalālu d-Dīn Rūmī. He says that Shams-i Tabrīzī is his mirror. He looked at this mirror and took from his blessings. They had ṣuḥbahs together. They taught knowledge and the truth. Shaykh Tabrīzī became a mirror only for Mawlānā. He did not become a mirror for others. Because only Mawlānā Jalālu d-Dīn Rūmī could bear his state and station. He did not become a mirror to anyone else, and they did not understand him. Since he did not have duties that anyone could understand, he became a mirror only to Mawlānā. Those truths passed from his heart to his heart. They passed back and forth. And they became the means for great guidance.

Until now, millions of people have come to īmān and found the truth through their means. This is what we call a mirror. As our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam said, he knows whomever the person in front of him is as himself. There are some proverbs on that. But of course, most people are not like him. A non-believer is not like him. You treat him thinking he is like you. That person whose heart is not pure thinks how he will benefit from you, how he will get ahead of you. Sometimes a person gets disappointed. That is not important. What is important is to find a believer and to mirror each other and look at your shortcomings and what needs to be done and to do like him. It would be a beautiful thing, in shā’a Llāh. The order of our ProphetNone will also be fulfilled. A person will also benefit from the blessed words heNone said.

Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying: “Al-Mu’minu mirātu l-Mu’min.” Mu’min, which the real believer. There is – every time we say this and we must repeat – Muslim and mu’min. Everybody Muslim who said, “Lā ilāha illā Llāh Muḥammadur Rasūlu Llāh ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam.” They are Muslim, all of them Muslim. You cannot say, “They are kāfir. They are mushrik.” This, AllāhNone, HeNone will ask for them. Whole people Muslim who said this, but there is more, higher rank, mu’min, for this.

Qur'ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying, for A‘rāb, Bedouin: “Don't say, “We are mu’min.” Say, “We are Muslim; we become Muslim.” Because belief – mu’min meaning the real belief – it is from heart, coming to heart. And they following Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam with what hisNone way, way of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam: sharī‘ah and ṭarīqah. Sharī‘ah, rule of Islām, this is way of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam.

Ṭarīqah, also same, teaching. For this, saying mu’min, who is more higher than Muslim, he is mirror of his brother mu’min. What he look for him, he see good, every good thing, from his brother. And he is wish[ing] every good thing for him. No jealousy, no cheating, no anything. Just love for AllāhNone and for Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. And he want every, what he want for himself, everybody, mu’min he want to his brother, mu’min brother, for same. For this, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying this.

So, look for yourself. If you are real believer or not real believer, how you can know this? When you not envy, not enemy for mu’min. Everything what mu’min has good thing, you must be happy, much happy, not be jealous. “Oh, I don't, oh he has this, I must have this.” He can have it, but no [need] to be jealous. AllāhNone, say: AllāhNone give for all of us this good life, good belief, good worshipping, to be every good thing for sake of AllāhNone. You must be happy with this.

For this, mashāyikh they was have, for murīd, they are also mirror for murīd. And many of them, of course before, also they have. Some of them different, maybe only one mirror like Sayyidunā Jalālu d-Dīn Rūmī Hazretleri and Shams-i Tabrīzī. Shams-i Tabrīzī he was mirror for Mawlānā Jalālu d-Dīn Rūmī, and, but only for him, because people, they was not happy with Shams-i Tabrīzī, because he not care about people. He only his mirror. He was big mirror, real mirror for him, Jalālu d-Dīn Rūmī Hazretleri. Even, he know he will be martyred for him, he gave his self to be killed. But he know, this just for Jalālu d-Dīn Rūmī, and it was, this mirror, real big feyz, gift, for human being for Muslim, for many millions of people. They become, for sake of AllāhNone, for in this way, they reach the highest station, Divine Presence of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. And so, this is one example, but everybody can be like this. Not be... Who is Muslim must be, try to be more, to be Mu’min, to look for his, what he is seeing from his brother. If good, he must be happy. If not good, tell him or he... Other, if he tell him, “You have this not good or this is good,” you must be happy, not be angry. Many people, if you say anything, “You are wrong” or “You have this,” become not happy, even sometimes not speaking anymore for people. No, this is not from mu’min attribute. Mu’min attribute: accept the truth and every advice for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. He must be happy and thank for his brother, what he said to him.

AllāhNone make us to accept and to be mirror for each other, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
