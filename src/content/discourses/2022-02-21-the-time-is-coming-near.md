As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Iqtarabati s-sā‘atu wa-nshaqqa l-qamar. Wa-in yaraw āyatan yu‘riḍū wa-yaqūlū siḥrun mustamirr" (54:1- 2). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘aẓīm. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says in Qur’ān, "Qiyāmah is near. The time is near." Qiyāmah has many names. One of them is the Time, the Hour. The Time is near.

There are many signs. In the time of our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam the moon was split. The moon split into two halves by the power of AllāhNone, with the miracle of our Holy ProphetNone. This is from the signs of Qiyāmah. People saw it and still didn't believe. But now we are approaching the end of times. The world is boiling everywhere. They ask, "What will happen?" What will happen is that everything AllāhNone has assigned has its hour and that hour is near, says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. We are waiting for it. What should we do? What we should do is to turn more towards AllāhNone, to beg AllāhNone, to believe in AllāhNone. This is it.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is showing the sign but people keep calling it magic or a spell and they don't believe in them anymore. They say, "It will pass and be over. So what if there is a war?" If there is a war, by the will of AllāhNone, Qiyāmah will be even closer. What have you done for Qiyāmah? Have you prepared yourself? This is important. There will be Qiyāmah. No matter if you want it or not, it will happen. This is something AllāhNone knows and decides. But for you to prepare, for your benefit, there are signs, there are āyāt – āyāt means signs – for mankind to believe, to be ready. Rather than thinking about running here or there, run to AllāhNone, turn to AllāhNone. Ask for help from AllāhNone. Don't be afraid of the condition of this world, wars or anything else. Be afraid of AllāhNone. AllāhNone is giving these as signs for you so that you turn towards HimNone.

Instead of being scared, you should be happy for having signs. For the signs are coming and the Time is near. The time to meet AllāhNone is near. So therefore, we should carry out Allāh'sNone orders. When people are in comfort, they don't think of it. They don't think of ākhirah, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, our Holy ProphetNone, or anything, when they are at ease. We have reached this age. We have seen so many things and wars, been through such times. During wartime, people turned to AllāhNone more, they believed in AllāhNone more. But afterwards, they followed their egos and forgot everything. They didn't remember any of this. When something happens, they scream "AllāhNone". When it doesn't happen, they don't remember. We should always remember AllāhNone. The world's condition is a mess now. What will happen? It will be good. It will be whatever AllāhNone said will happen. Don't be afraid.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying in Qur’ān, Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Iqtarabati s-sā‘atu wa-nshaqqa l-qamar. Wa-in yaraw āyatan yu‘riḍū wa-yaqūlū siḥrun mustamirr" (54:1-2). AllāhNone saying in this: Time coming near. Near and near and this moon cut by half. This miracle of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam happen when heNone was in Makkah before Madīnah. This mushrik people every time they trying to make difficult thing for ProphetNone, to make himNone, to make people not believe himNone. So one of this time; they said, "Oh, we will believe, look this full moon, youNone cut this moon in half and we will believe." And AllāhNone give permission for Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam to make this miracle by hisNone stick. Like when heNone do this – it was two part with half, one this part, one this. And this people, they saw, "This is cannot be like this. HeNone make magic, magic. HeNone is magician, make magic make this." But after many people coming from away, from other place. They said, "We see very strange thing. This moon it was two part. How it happen this?" So if magic, this – only this people [in Makkah could see]. But it was real and now if this people can see it, it was this miracle of ProphetNone for sign of Time of Resurrection – Qiyāmah, Yawmu l-Qiyāmah near, coming near and near.

This one of sign – many sign for Last Day. And this Day, every time coming more and more and more. We are going near end of time – we are living in the end of time. And we are, "What we will do?" many peoples asking. "There is war here, there is – the people fearing from big war." Eh, it will be happen because this also all sign coming to for believer. They must be happy what, when they see this it is become near and near. And we are looking for this because end of time, all tyrant it will be in this world and oppressor and we see – even the most you see, you think they are peaceful people [but] they are oppressing other people for their religion, for their colour, for everything. So, this meaning it must be something happen to finish this all. And this is what we are waiting and it is...

Don't fear from this because every time this war or something happen. I was living until this time, many time we from war, we are living in Middle East. All time war here, there and inside, outside. You feel, when there is war or there is fear, people they are remembering ākhirah, they are remembering Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, ProphetNone, religion. They, "O my God – Yā AllāhNone help us." They pray, they make everything. But when nothing, they are peace and they are happy and everything there, they forget everything. Only they running after their ego, their pleasure. They are not praying, not doing anything. But when coming this, they coming to remember. For this, don't be afraid from what happen. Everything by will of AllāhNone and HisNone will, you cannot do anything.

Don't go against AllāhNone will. You go with will, HisNone will, you be happy. You be safe, no stress, no fear – nothing. This is AllāhNone will, we are happy. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are going with HimNone. All time we must be with HimNone to be safe and to be winner. "Ulā’ika humu l-mufliḥūn" (9:88). For other – winner, for other they are, they are loser "Ulā’ika humu l-khāsirūn" (8:37). Khāsirūn mean loser – who are against will of AllāhNone. Now we are living Time. Don't worry, don't afraid from anything. Be happy. If you are believe, mu’min, believer, you must be happy. It will be for us – good for us, al-ḥamdu liLlāh. This Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying: everything happen good for believer – for mu’min. AllāhNone give us more īmān, more happiness, in shā’a Llāh, more power.

Fātiḥah.
