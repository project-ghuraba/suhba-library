Because the spiritual side it is tell you to go for mountains & make worship/ibada and worship to Allah Almighty, because it is belonging to that station. But the ego is belonging to this world. Al Hamdulillah! So Allah Almighty as He (swt) give us what we have, He (swt) Says `` use it! And thank Me (swt) for what I give you.`` Al Hamdulillah! So that was one of the nice experiences that I wanted also to share it with you. Because Allah Almighty He (swt) doesn`t like the extremists. By saying, you have to have your balance, you have to have your wisdom to what to use & what not to use. And where to use & where not to use. You cannot recite Quran in the WC. So that`s why the balance is very important. Those days so many people they go for extreme. No it is not the extreme. Allah Almighty never asked from anybody the extreme.

And this is also very nice story from Hj Anne. She wants us to remember her on this holy day. And she has very nice story. We are coming from donkeys to be parallel. They couldn't handle it, they couldn't stand it! "How can be these people with such a stupid mind, they're still making money and not money....

He say, "tell me, maybe be can do it together." Say "no, no I give you the idea." Clever merchant telling the stupid merchant. Say "what to do?" "Whatever you have you buy dates & go to Baghdad to sell dates." And Baghdad is known as they have 80% dates of the world production. And this stupid merchant he ask his brother, "lets go." He was the chief of the family, say "we sell everything & we invest in dates." & they make a very big caravan of dates & they slowly slowly going to Baghdad. And then when they arrive at Baghdad market all the people they make feast - say "Al Hamdulillah, Ya Rabbi Shukur! We have tree here no dates.
