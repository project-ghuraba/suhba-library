Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. The three holy months. Its 24 hours, with day and night, 24 hours of every day is blessed. The mercy is coming down. Allāh Almighty said "The month of Rajab is MineNone" and assigned it for HimselfNone. It is the month of AllāhNone. Sha‘bān is the month of our Prophet ‘alayhi ṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salām. Ramaḍān is the month of ummah. It's appointed so. Allāh Almighty granted us these holy months in order for them to be an honor for the servants of ummati MuḥammadNone.

HeNone has sent down mercies which are not seen in other months. And who respects these months, shows respect and is busy with worshipping, he will progress immediately, he will easily reach the pleasure of Allāh AlmightyNone. Every hour of each day and night of these months are holy and blessed. Rajab came as the first month. It is half over. It is close to be over, this holy month. Then comes the month of our ProphetNone Sha‘bān Mu‘aẓẓam. And then just as we reach Laylatu l-Barā’at, the first of Ramaḍān will come. And we won't understand how holy Ramaḍān came, that it will already be gone.

Therefore, who is busy with worshipping during these holy months, he'll be in benefit. Because it's easy and it gives spiritual joy. It gives relief. It removes worries and problems. Who stays away from sin will feel the taste of īmān in his heart. And his work will go well. Who deals with ḥarām, his work will go bad. Who eats ḥarām, their goods will become rotten. It will be seen for a short moment, then will disappear and break into pieces. So Allāh Almighty who let us reach these holy months granted us this chance once again.

Don't leave your nights free. Pray. Don't leave your days free. At least fast on Mondays and Thursdays with the intention of covering your missed days. If not, when you make intention to cover the missed days of fast of your deceased mother or father or siblings. Our ProphetNone says there is a spring called Rayyān in paradise; this spring of Rayyān, only its' name is similar to the springs in this world. In fact, there is no such spring in the world. It comes gurgling from the rivers of power. No one knows where it starts and where it ends.

It starts from the power oceans of AllāhNone. Whoever drinks from it will say he has never tasted water sweeter and brighter than it. Allāh Jalla wa ‘Alá will let the one who fasts even a day for the honor of HisNone month, for the honor of Rajab drink from the spring of Rayyān. When we say spring, it's not one or two springs. It's like an ocean. It is according to Allāh'sNone mercy. May AllāhNone let us drink from that holy spring too. May HeNone make us from those who respect and observe this month. This is so.

Recite

100-1000 Lā ilāha illā Llāh, 100-1000 Lā ilāha illā Llāh Muḥammadu Rasūlu Llāh, 100-1000 Ṣalawāt Sharīf, 100-1000 Ikhlāṣu sh-Sharīf.

Don't stay jobless. This awrād sharīf is in all 41 ṭarīqahs. Pay attention to it. May Allāh make it successful and increase our love. May we not be weak.

Biḥurmati l-Ḥabīb, biḥurmati l-Fātiḥah.
