As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa raḥmatu Llāhi wa barakātuh. A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīn wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

Mankind sometimes thinks, "What are we doing here? What were we created for?" Yet you have come to this existence, AllāhNone created you. Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla created us – all of mankind to be more accurate – to worship AllāhNone, to know AllāhNone and to obey HimNone. This is the reason of our creation, like it or not. Because sometimes people ask why it is so. Some even don't ask and live their lives just like that. We should be on the way and religion that obeys AllāhNone and was ordered by HimNone. The religion in front of AllāhNone is Islām. "Inna d-dīna ‘inda Llāhi l-Islām" (3:19). In Islām: "Wa man yabtaghi ghayra l-Islāmi dīnan falan yuqbala minhu" (3:85). Whoever looks for a religion apart from Islām is not accepted by AllāhNone.

And nowadays they look for something new. They say, "I found a new religion." Impossible. The main religion since Ādam ‘alayhi s-salām is Islām. All Prophets have followed it. Going through changes, at last it was completed with our ProphetNone as AllāhNone wanted and ordered it to be. Slowly, slowly it became Islām. Shukr to AllāhNone, we became Muslim. What is our duty next? Next comes sincerity and closeness in the presence of AllāhNone, to be close to HimNone. This is the biggest purpose.

As we said, AllāhNone created people in different colors. There are different people and different nations. Black, white, yellow, all types of people. Which type of people is more accepted in the presence of AllāhNone? White? Because people think they are more accepted when people are white. Or black people? Or yellow, or red? No need to think about it. Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla said, "INone created all of you in different types and appearances. The most accepted among you, whether you like it or not, the one accepted by AllāhNone is the person who fears AllāhNone." "Inna akramakum ‘inda Llāhi atqākum" (49:13). The one who has taqwá. What we mean by taqwá is the fear of AllāhNone, the fear of feeling shame in the presence of AllāhNone. People with taqwá fear so. They are not afraid of AllāhNone. AllāhNone is the most merciful. AllāhNone is Arḥamu r-Rāḥimīn. The most beneficent is AllāhNone. No one is more beneficent than AllāhNone. Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla is Ar-Raḥman and Ar-Raḥīm, HeNone is extremely merciful. Therefore, who fears and feels shy of AllāhNone won't do any evil. He won't oppress anyone. He won't harm anyone. He doesn't have enmity against anyone. He doesn't do anything that may harm someone's property, children, or anything else. Because he knows that when he enters the presence of AllāhNone, he'll be asked about it. He fears to be asked about it and doesn't do these wrong things. He does goodness. He does all kinds of goodness to be accepted by AllāhNone. That is it.

The purpose AllāhNone created people is to worship AllāhNone. The purpose of Muslims and those who are on the right way is to fear Allāh and be close to HimNone, to be more careful and to be closer. That is our purpose. There are many people who wander without purpose. The truth is in front of them but they don't see. This is the truth. This is the purpose. This is the purpose of our creation. The purpose of our creation is not to eat and fill the toilets like Mawlānā used to say. The purpose is to work for ākhirah, to be an accepted person in the presence of AllāhNone. This is the purpose.

Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla make, create this people, human being, other creature also but human being it is the highest between this creature. But the people, human being, they are sometimes asking – some of them, not all of them – many of them they are living without knowing why they are living, how they are living, for what they are living. Some of them asking about purpose of being in this existence. Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla answering, "Wa-mā khalaqtu l-jinna wa l-insa illā liya‘budūn" (51:56). AllāhNone answering HeNone said, "INone create whole creature especially human being and jinn." Jinn this also they are created, created for, created as we are created – We are created from earth, they're created from fire. We can to see them, rare to see them. If they want you can see them if they can. If they they don't want we cannot see them. But it is not our subject.

Our subject – AllāhNone create human being and jinn to worshipping, worshipping HimNone not[hing] else. Like human being looking to worship what they are like, what their imagination like. But AllāhNone He said, "Only one religion. INone have Islām." "Inna d-dīna ‘inda Llāhi l-Islām" (3:19). AllāhNone from Adam ‘alayhi s-salām until now HeNone make religion and coming and end to ProphetNone. And before heNone is passing to next world, heNone said, for four month, heNone said, "Today I was complete your religion." So whole human being AllāhNone make them, create them to obey HimNone and worship HimNone and to follow what HeNone is saying. But people they are not seeing this and going like crazy chicken from where to where, looking here, here. "Where it is?" and it is in front of them like sun but they are not seeing this. Still looking for something, to find something. AllāhNone give for everybody mind and remembering, to see and to be accept reality. But people they are put dark in their eyes and not seeing anything and after they said we don't see sun.

This is for human being. To purpose of creation to worship. Not to our creation to looking after ourself, to feed ourself like cows and becoming fat and full of toilet and other thing. No. Not our, this not purpose. This for human being but for who accept also religion of AllāhNone and accept this way, also there is another thing for them to look for this. AllāhNone create all races, language, different people, tribe – all create from AllāhNone. There is black people. There white, there is yellow, there is red. AllāhNone create them like this. Which one the best in Divinely Presence of AllāhNone? Human being, the racist they said the white man he is the best. They are not accepting anybody else. They treat people as they are less than animal. But in Divinely Presence of AllāhNone it is not like this.

In Divine Presence of AllāhNone who having "inna akramakum ‘inda Llāhi atqākum" (49:13) – who fear from AllāhNone he is the best one, near one to Allāh ‘Azza wa Jallah. How to fear? Some people they are not accepting to fear from AllāhNone. Of course there is many kind of fearing of AllāhNone but this people fearing to be ashamed in front of HimNone – if they do bad thing, cheating people, harming people, stealing, or doing bad thing. This is what they are afraid to be in this position in front of Divinely Presence of AllāhNone and they are not doing this. This is people black or red or... yellow or white who is the most careful to be not to be in this position, he is the nearest one for Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla. AllāhNone not looking for colour, not looking for language, not looking... but only looking for who obey HimNone and doing what AllāhNone like us to do.

AllāhNone He didn't like us to kill people, to destroy, to cheat people, to harm people. HeNone wants us to do only good things. And when you are doing this – this is our aim and our purpose also. Not looking for good wearing or good having, food, other thing. No. It is most important to be obey, obey for AllāhNone and be mercy for people. And AllāhNone will be mercy with us and HeNone will be happy with us. We are asking His bless and His mercy for us, in shā’a Llāh. This is for believer people. Next purpose, next aim for after, when we people asking what we are for here. When you are Muslim also, you are accepting, you must also try to be more nearer for your Lord and your ProphetNone. This people they are near with Prophets and near for beloved one for AllāhNone and they are beloved one for AllāhNone. This is our aim and the teaching of ProphetNone. Who doing against this, they are not with ProphetNone or near for AllāhNone. They are away from this position. They are must be regret and ask forgiveness from AllāhNone and try to be near for HimNone.

It is our aim to be near and near for our Lord, in shā’a Llāh. Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
