As-Salāmu ‘Alaykum wa Raḥmatullāhi wa Barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu Billāhi Minash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm.

Wa ṣ-Salātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Akhirīn.

Madad yā RasūlAllāh, Madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, Madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

Dastūr Mawlana Shaykh Abdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistāni, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-

Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-Suḥbah wa l-Khayru fi l-Jam‘iyyah.

Our Holy Prophet says for whoever is Muslim that Allah rewards him for his every step and forgives his sins. He raises him, says our Holy Prophet. There is big benefit for Muslims to visit each other. Of course, it is up to their capacity.

People have come here for the pleasure of Allah; may Allah be pleased with them and give them their rewards. They did so with big difficulties, but for the pleasure of Allah. May Allah accept it. May Allah be pleased with them. As they came for the pleasure of Allah, may Allah make them means for guidance there.

By the wisdom of Allah, with the Barakah of our Shaykh, many Muslims, many people are accepting this way, Shukr to Allah. Allah is favoring them that through our brothers, through the followers of our Shaykh, local people in those countries are becoming Muslim. They are becoming the means, and they are increasing day by day. They are more and more day by day.

Of course, there is a lot of Kufr. There are many Kafirs all over the world. May Allah protect Muslims and those who are going to become Muslim. May Allah help them. Certainly, their rewards are like the rewards of the Sahabah, because it is not easy for them. People think that it is easy. It is very difficult. But as long as Allah wants it, everything is possible. Nothing is difficult for the will of Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla. Everything is easy with the will of Allah insha’Allah. May Allah give guidance to all of us here and protect us from the evil of shaytan. May He make us steadfast and firm on this way of truth insha’Allah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
