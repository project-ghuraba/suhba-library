These are End Times, now is the time of disorder and discord (fitna). Our Holy Prophet (SAW) says about these times, “Fitna is all over the air.” To be protected from it they ask, “What should we do? What will happen?” What Allah wills will happen.

What we have to do is trust in Allah and follow His orders. When our Holy Prophet (SAW) conquered (fatih) Mecca he said, “Those who stay home are in safety.” For people to be in safety, they need to sit at home and not interfere much in this and that. They say to “keep one’s nose clean”.

People need to get used to not interfering much in everything. It is not good to meddle much in everything. Now they say, “Democracy,” and, “Get involved in everything.” You get involved in everything but are unable to achieve anything! On top of it all, it causes you trouble.

For Allah to protect people, we need to not interfere, argue, and struggle much, and go along while getting along with people. Otherwise, people cause trouble for themselves out of nothing.

People have become such that even from little things, they get up in anger and destroy their surroundings. They are sorry five minutes later, but it is too late. That is why we need to avoid coming face to face with these kinds of people. Shaykh Mawlana says it is best not to go out much when you have no business outside. Be busy with your own work.

May Allah help us all Inshallah. May Allah protect us. In these times of fitna, those who stay home are again in good condition. When the environment is bad, he says not to go out looking and thinking, “What happened? What remains?”

Allah made us reach these days as well. The best time for commerce are these End Times. It is priceless to trust in Allah and follow Allah’s orders in times of fitna. The reward of 100 martyrs is awarded for each sunna, so how much will be awarded for wajib and fard now Allah knows. May Allah protect us all Inshallah.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq.

Al-Fatiha.
