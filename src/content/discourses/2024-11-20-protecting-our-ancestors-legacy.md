A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Our ṭarīqah is founded on ṣuḥbah. Its aim is to form good communities; this is its purpose. The aim is to seek the pleasure of AllāhNone. In shā’a Llāh, this gathering is one of the gatherings loved by AllāhNone, in shā’a Llāh. This is certainly true, because we have gathered here for the pleasure of AllāhNone. And you have also come here with this love. May it be everlasting, in shā’a Llāh.

You gather once a week anyway. Be careful not to miss these gatherings at all. If you don't go one week, go on the second week. If you don’t go the second week, make sure to attend on the third week. Benefit from its thawāb (rewards). These gatherings are jewels, among the most precious things a person can have in life. They are so valuable that nothing else can compare to them. That is why, at least once a week... Tomorrow evening on Thursday, there will be dhikr and Khatmu l-Khwajagān. As we said, if not every week, be sure to attend at least once every two weeks. Even once in three weeks brings benefit. And if a person doesn't maintain this, after missing for three weeks, he may lose the desire to come. They won't feel drawn anymore. So, to subdue your ego, make sure to participate in these ṣuḥbat and dhikr.

Thanks to AllāhNone, this is a Muslim land. Muslims are everywhere here. This is the land of our forefathers, not of Christians. Cyprus is Ottoman land. There is no North or South – it is all the same. All of it is Islām's soil. Thus, we must be mindful of this. We must be careful with the inheritance left by our ancestors. We must not leave the inheritance like a prodigal. A prodigal is someone who inherits from their father, but wastes it within five days, because they did not suffer, they did not endure hardship. Our ancestors shed their blood to acquire these lands. They conquered them while hungry and thirsty, enduring all kinds of suffering, for the pleasure of AllāhNone.

The holy ones came here and built tombs, dergâhs, mosques, and various other structures. They established waqfs (trusts). A waqf includes many different types. Whatever is written in the waqf deed must be done. For example, this place was made a waqf: "This place belongs to me. I dedicate it for the pleasure of AllāhNone to be used as a masjid, mosque, dergâh, food kitchen, madrasah, and a place to help people." They documented everything precisely: this much land, this many yards, this many meters. People of old times were careful, unlike today. They said, "We donate these places for the pleasure of AllāhNone until qiyāmah." And they added, "May the curse of AllāhNone be upon those who oppose this." This applies to most waqfs. However, there are other types of waqfs that can be sold or exchanged according to their specific intentions. That is different.

But for waqfs with clear written deeds, such as Ayasofya in Istanbul, Ḥaḍrat Fatih Sultan Mehmet himself wrote its deed and paid for it. Those who turned it into a museum for years are cursed. Oftentimes, people ask, "Where do these troubles come from?" Troubles come from the curse. A cursed person is the worst of all. The curse means badness. The only way to get rid of it is by abandoning what you have done. But once you have done it, you can't just stop. If you can't stop, the curse will be on you. And others will be cursed too.

May AllāhNone protect us. This is an important matter. People don't give it the importance it deserves. "It's waqf," they say. "Let's benefit from it. Why should the poor have it? We deserve to eat more than they do. Let them starve and die. We will eat instead." Shukr to AllāhNone, the money they earn is enough for all. It is abundant enough. The poor ones have nothing. They take away their rights. So, they are sick from the inside, and they bring harm to people around them. When the curse comes down, it is scattered, just like mercy comes down. It touches everyone around, friends and acquaintances. It touches everyone.

This place where we are sitting now is not even one tenth of the place the holy one donated. They've done everything. They opened their ancestors’ graves. They did what the disbelievers (kāfirs) wouldn’t do, and threw them away. Forty to fifty years ago, they were playing with their skulls like balls here. Since they didn't give importance to such things, there is no barakah in anything. But thanks to AllāhNone, this place belongs to Muslims. This is the important thing. Their rights will be returned in the future, with the permission of AllāhNone. When Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām comes, he will bring justice to those whose rights were taken. All these wrongdoings will be corrected.

Shukr to AllāhNone, the time is near. This world has reached the peak of oppression. When the world reaches its peak and perfection, its destruction will come. It will end. It is almost here. In shā’a Llāh, oppression will be removed from this world soon. The religion of AllāhNone will be manifest. Justice, goodness, and beauty will prevail everywhere, in shā’a Llāh. May AllāhNone let us reach those days. May HeNone increase the mosques and believers. May HeNone give guidance and understanding to people.

Those who take away rights are foolish. Those who oppress are foolish, because they will be held accountable. If you thought about the judgment, you wouldn't do what you are doing. You wouldn't oppress or take rights. You wouldn't harm anyone. You wouldn't do bad things to anyone. You wouldn't do any evil to anyone. Those who do evil are foolish. May AllāhNone give understanding to us, in shā’a Llāh. May AllāhNone not let us get confused in our minds. People get confused sometimes. Something happens and they cheat others. And the biggest cheater is shayṭān. He deceives and destroys people, and leaves them. May AllāhNone protect us from his evil. And may HeNone protect us from taking others' rights, in shā’a Llāh, knowingly or unknowingly. May AllāhNone forgive us all.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
