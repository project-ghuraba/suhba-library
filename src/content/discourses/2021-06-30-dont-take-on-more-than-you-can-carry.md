As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla doesn't give a person more than he can carry. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Lā yukallifu Llāhu nafsan illā wus‘ahā" (2:286). AllāhNone gives as much as people can handle. It can be different for everyone or same for all but they are things that people can carry. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla doesn't make them responsible for whatever they cannot carry.

Everyone can follow the conditions of Islām. It is the same for everyone. And there are sunnah and nafl. Whoever is able to perform them should do as much as he can. The more good deeds people have, the more profit they get. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla doesn't need them. HeNone doesn't need our good deeds or charity. However, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla loves them. HeNone is happy with them. When HisNone servants obey HimNone and do good things, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is pleased. HeNone wishes them good. And when they don't do anything at all, there is no harm to Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. But mankind should do as much as he can.

They shouldn't take on more of a burden than they can do. Even HeNone, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, is not giving extra burden to people. But sometimes people make a promise to do this or that. And when they cannot do those things, they break their promise. It is not a good thing then. It is okay not to do something, if you haven't promised to do it. But when you promise that you will do something, no matter what, and give hope to people, sometimes you cheat them and sometimes you cannot fulfill your promise. You take those people's rights then. You are responsible for them. It is clear that it is not a good thing to promise something and not fulfill it. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla orders to keep your promise. If you can't fulfill it, don't promise. Promise as much as you can do.

If someone promises to give 1,000 lira, it would have been better to promise one lira and give it. rather than to promise and not give at all. One lira would be counted as 1000 lira. Otherwise, when you promise and give hope, you break your promise and upset people. It won't turn out well for you. As we said, don't overload yourself. Don't make a burden on yourself. Even if someone forces you to make a promise, say, "I will do as much I can, in shā’a Llāh. I don't promise you, as I can't do so much. I will help you as much as I can." You are free to do as much as you like then. But when you say, "Don't worry. Go and rest. I will take care of everything," and when they come to realize you couldn't do it, they will say, "If only you told us in the beginning, we wouldn't be in such a state." You can also be cursed by them.

Therefore, people, and especially Muslims, should measure their every action in order not to take anyone's right and not to give [false] hope. When we give hope and don't deliver, it is not good for us. As Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla doesn't give anyone a burden he cannot carry. May AllāhNone protect and make us from those who keep their promise and service, in shā’a Llāh.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone give every person, every human being, all – everyone what they can carry. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone say: "Lā yukallifu Llāhu nafsan illā wus‘ahā" (2:286). This two āyah from end of Sūratu l-Baqarah. One of them this. These āyah, they was gift for Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, when heNone go through mi‘rāj. What meaning? Night journey. Night journey for Divine Presence of AllāhNone without Jibrīl ‘alayhi s-salām. This is was gift for Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. AllāhNone HeNone give himNone this two āyāt.

One of them said, "AllāhNone not giving any order who you cannot carry. What you can carry, AllāhNone give this order." This of course order of Islām. Everybody, every human being can do it. It is not difficult. For this, we must not give ourself under something we cannot do. For example, best example for us, what Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, even HeNone said, "WeNone are not giving, putting..." So you must look for yourself and not put yourself under something you cannot carry. From anything, from worship, maybe you say, "I will worship whole night, day." This is not good because you cannot do this. You can do a little bit. After, you leave everything.

Or [for example] you want to make charity for people and you promise people. After, this people – you cannot do anything. But you must know you are, you can – what you can help. AllāhNone not order you to put whole your thing and, finish, you stay poor. No. You can give what enough for ṣadaqah or for help. Some people they said, "I will give," but normally they will not give. And this make people to disappoint. No. When some people ask you, no need to give thousand or say thousand and after you, you not giving anything. This people become disappoint. So you can say, "I give you one." Give one. He will be happy. But if you say, "I will give you thousand," and after, you cannot give him this, you will be make him disappoint. And you are also, in front of Divine Presence, you are liar. You promise and you don't make your, what you promise.

When you promise, you must do. For this, when if you promise something, ask or something, want something, somebody ask something, tell him, "I can what AllāhNone make me can me – make me to help you. I will help you. I don't know how much but in shā’a Llāh, we help you." You give one, this okay. Give two, okay. Five, okay. No need to give thousands of from money. It is enough what... Don't you put yourself under burden, burden of promise, promising people. After you are not giving, you are making yourself in front of Divine Presence also not good and in front of people also disappoint. Because maybe some, many people we meet, many people, they ask them something, "Okay you go and take everything, I will manage this." He going. After, when coming to this, they are disappear. You cannot find them. This what is happen. But no need to do this because this, you are not...

In trading with people, you can cheat people, you can do this, you can do that. You are with – even with trading also, you must be true. What you can buy, what you can sell, you must make calculation, make what benefit for you, what not benefit for you. Also you must look for this.

Before ten days, somebody sent message, said: "We are going with partners work for some project and we have four partner. They left me and I am now alone. And this project, it is not profiting." First of this, this who are partner, they run away. They also not reliable people. They run away. And what this man also he said, "Project it will be lose. I lose too much money from this project." I said, "Of course but first you must look for project, before your partner, and calculate and if it is suitable or not suitable. After you make, give your project for government." But without anything, they give. After, when they saw it is not good, they run away.

This also lesson for people for this in dunyā. For Ākhirah also, who promise and not giving and they will be lose too much. So only this man, if he not do anything, he will be happy and his money was in his pocket. Nobody tell him, come put your money here. No, he promised by himself and he lose his money. And this charity case, also same thing. Nobody force you to say to people, "Come I will give you," and after you run away. You think you will be you save your money. You save your money but you will be asked for this in Ākhirah. So don't do. Everything, it's very important for us to not put burden on ourself without any benefit. If you – you must be calculate, you must be think too much – if you can do, if you not can do. After, you can say. Not for fun to make people, laugh for people. They'll be in bad condition and you are be happy how look this people, "I was promising them and they are cannot do anything."

AllāhNone give us good understanding. Because it is not joke this. It is important thing.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
