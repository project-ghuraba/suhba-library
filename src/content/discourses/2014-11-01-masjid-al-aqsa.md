Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Our Holy Prophet (SAW) states, “Those who see true things and do not say it are tongueless devils.” The world of unbelief are continuous enemies of Islam. It is like this in every era. In the past they could do it openly. Now, under various pretexts, again they are continuing their animosity. And Muslims are falling for their tricks. Because they are upset with them and they are protesting, they are burning down their own countries.

Al-Masjid al-Aqsa is the third mosque. Visiting them is important. Of course, there is no possibility to go at the moment. There are issues relating to that place. Allah knows what else they will do to make Muslims angry. What is the saying? They “show right but hit left”. Again they have a dissension in mind. As Shaykh Mawlana said: “What Muslims need to do is gather in mosques and make dua to Allah there as, ‘Subdue and desolate them!’” Muslims gather in mosques and make dua. There is no need to go out on the streets, burn, and destroy. You could yell and scream as much as you want. That time you would be committing a sin and it is no use. What the men want is happenening. You are doing exactly what the enemy wants you to do. How convenient! You are falling in the trap like a rabbit.

In fact, if you supplicate to Allah (JJ), Allah would take care of them and He would subdue and desolate them. We are not content with the things that are happeneing. May Allah keep this holy mosque safe from the bad ones. Inshallah, may we also have the kismet to visit that place. We went to Mecca and Medina. Inshallah we get the opportunity to visit that place too. In the past, it was controlled by the king of Jordan, King Hussein. At that time, you could go and visit. Apparently we went when I was two years old. Of course I do not remember. Inshallah we get the opportunity to visit again later. Let the tyranny there end already. Blatantly, there is tyranny in front of the eyes of the world. All of them are saying something just for the sake of conversation.

We are supplicating to Allah: may Allah protect it. May Allah help Muslims, genuine Muslims, Inshallah. The other Muslims, those who are not Ahl-e Sunnat wal Jamaat, are those people’s own making anyway. They made groups outside of Ahl-e Sunnat wal Jamaat to destroy Islam. They did many things. Yet again, religion is Allah’s. Allah keeps it safe.

1. Fitna 2. Pray

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq.

Al-Fatiha.
