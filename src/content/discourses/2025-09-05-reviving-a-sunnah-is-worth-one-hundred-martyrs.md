A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah. Allāh says, “I sent My Prophet to you from among yourselves.” Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “La-qad jā’akum rasūlun min anfusikum ‘azīzun ‘alayhi mā ‘anittum ḥarīṣun ‘alaykum bi-l-mu’minīna ra’ūfun raḥīm” (9:128). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘Aẓīm. “I sent him from among you,” says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla about our Holy Prophet. Not from other beings, but He sent him from mankind like them. Yet our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam is different. Many ignorant people say, “He is a man, and we are men.” The one they are speaking of is our Holy Prophet, the true man. He is not a man who utters empty words. He is something else. Because people who do not appreciate value cannot attain value. Those who appreciate value become valuable themselves. Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam is valuable. His value is that he is merciful to his ummah, showed them the way, and warned them of hellfire. Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam begged Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla so that they would not be destroyed. Whatever happened, he always invited them to the way of truth and to Paradise. That was all our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam desired. At his birth, when he came into this world, he was in sajdah saying, “Ummatī, ummatī.” Babies cry, but our Holy Prophet said, “Ummatī.” When Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla created him, as soon as he entered this world and drew breath, he thought of his ummah. Until his last breath, he kept showing the way to his ummah and interceding for them. He will continue to do so forever. On the Day of Judgment, he will beg Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla to allow him to intercede for them. Certainly, our Holy Prophet wants his ummah to benefit, to be among those whom Allāh loves. That is his wish and aim: to bring humanity to the right way, so that Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla bestows upon them from His favours and rewards. Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam is a nabiyy – nubuwwah means one who informs of what is to come. All Prophets are anbiyā’, meaning those who inform of what will happen later. In this ḥadīth sharīf, he said, “My ummah will be corrupted. My ummah will go astray.” In order not to go astray, people must hold firmly to the sunnah. The more they hold fast to the sunnah of our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, the stronger their īmān will become. Those who neglect the sunnah will have no īmān left. It may be that Islām is only on their tongue. But as our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam said, it does not go below their throat – it remains only on their tongue. Therefore, our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says in this ḥadīth sharīf, “When my ummah is corrupted, whoever revives one of my sunnah,” that is, by reviving a sunnah that our Holy Prophet practiced, any one of his sunnahs, whoever does it, “earns the reward of a hundred martyrs.” It is not easy to be a martyr. And earning that reward is not easy either. But now is exactly the time when his ummah has gone most astray, when they have become most distant from Ḥaqq. Therefore, each sunnah a person does earns the reward of a hundred martyrs in the sight of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. What is this sunnah? The sunnahs of ablution, the sunnahs of actions – however our Holy Prophet moved and acted. There are thousands of sunnahs. Do as many as you can, as many as come to your mind. Most of them are not difficult at all. Practicing the sunnah is very easy. Whoever does them, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla grants him not just the reward of a hundred martyrs in a day, but if you do a thousand sunnahs, for each one you earn the reward of a hundred martyrs. The treasures of Allāh are endless. Allāh is generous, He gives. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla does not break His promise like people nowadays. People say, “I will give,” but when you go to them they deny saying so. There is no doubt with Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. The whole universe is in His hands. He will give you. Don’t be afraid, with the permission of Allāh. That is why this sunnah is very important. As we said, the intercession of our Holy Prophet is very important. Whoever practices sunnah strengthens his īmān. Without īmān, then corruption – may Allāh protect – and the end not being good are very likely. Because of this, shayṭān does everything he can, by every means, to stop people from practicing sunnah. May Allāh protect us from his evil. May we, in these blessed days, attain the shafā‘ah of our Prophet, in shā’a Llāh. For his sake, in shā’a Llāh. And this du‘ā’ for rain is also his sunnah. It is the sunnah of our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, and we performed it too. May Allāh accept it, in shā’a Llāh. May He grant us good and beneficial rain, in shā’a Llāh.

Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam he is, Allāh describe him as: he is from, one from you. And he is very careful to make people to come to way, safe way. Safe way: to be with prophets, to be with Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Other way, it is not safe at all. This is what, in āyah, “La-qad jā’akum rasūlun min anfusikum ‘azīzun ‘alayhi mā ‘anittum ḥarīṣun ‘alaykum bi-l-mu’minīna ra’ūfun raḥīm” (9:128). He is very mercy for you, very careful for you, to show you the way, the right way to make you safe in next life. And this life also. But who follow Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, he will be safe, and be happy, and be blessed one in Divine Presence of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. And Allāh happy with, what Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam showing, people following this. For this, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam – also in this ḥadīth we read – he said prophecy, you know, meaning to saying about future. All prophets, they are telling about future: what will happen, what will be. It is not like these other people. They [prophets] are saying the right one, right thing. And Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, “Man aḥyā sunnatī ‘inda faṣadi ummatī falahu ajra mi’ata shahīd.” Meaning, who make my sunnah – sunnah of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam – to do what Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam doing or what he saying, how he acting, to act one of this. There is of course thousands of sunnah, not only one. But Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying this is prophecy to, meaning, ummah they will be not in same like Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. It will be corrupt and it will be many people, cheating people, taking them out of Islām, showing as [their way] is the right way. And Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, who make one sunnah from this, he has hundred shahīd (martyr) reward. One shahīd, it is very precious in Divine Presence Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. And for only one sunnah, it is hundred shahīd. If you do two sunnah, two hundred. If you do ten, ten hundred. Like this, it is Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, He give without fearing it will be finished, what He has. You can do thousand, ten thousand sunnah, also Allāh reward you. No doubt about this. For this is very important, very good, for who can do as much they can in every day. And sunnah, many of them not so difficult. Even people, maybe they thought some of sunnah difficult. You can do what it is easy for you, no need to make... But also, there is one point, the most important sunnah to follow – the praying of daily praying, especially for five times praying. Don’t lose any sunnah from five time praying. And extra this, also, you can make nāfilah, praying for this. And from everything in your life, even when you drink water or what you doing, everything also this you can intention for sunnah of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Putting ring, or makes miswāk, or wearing hat. All this, sunnah of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Life, our life, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam was also, he is human being and he was doing what we are doing. So, not difficult to follow what he was doing. For this, we must know the sunnah. It is a very precious thing. Don’t be saying it’s not important. Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, he is happy for this, to give ummah from reward of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. For this, I am saying, mentioning ḥadīth, this ḥadīth for people, to encourage them to follow sunnah, in shā’a Llāh. Allāh make us with him, and Allāh bless us, in shā’a Llāh, all of us, in shā’a Llāh. Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
