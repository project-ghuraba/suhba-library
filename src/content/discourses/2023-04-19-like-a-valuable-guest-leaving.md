As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh. A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm.

"Shahru Ramaḍāna lladhī unzila fīhi l-Qur’ānu hudan li-n-nāsi wa-bayyinātin mina l-hudá wa-l-furqān" (2:185). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘aẓīm.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is honouring the holy month of Ramaḍān. The Qur’ān was sent in this month as a means of guidance for people. There are many miracles in it. It shows what is good and orders goodness. It has great value. But certainly, among mankind, most people don't know its value. When they don't appreciate it they are at a loss. It is important, shukr to AllāhNone that we spent and are spending this Ramaḍān all together. The days are passing quickly. A month is already gone.

People of old times used to do something in the mosques; in some mosques they still do it today. In the old Ottoman mosques, they begin a farewell. In between tarāwīḥ prayers, as a consolation for people, they recite beautiful poems and qaṣīdah, as the month of Ramaḍān is leaving like a guest. It is a good thing to honour it. There is no reason not to do good things. On the contrary, it is beneficial in that it encourages people, and they increase in love. AllāhNone already made them love. Whoever does this in Ramaḍān, this holy month, there is barakah for all of them. We say, "Ramaḍān is over. Another year is gone from our lives." Thanks to AllāhNone, our destination is a beautiful place with the bountiful gifts of AllāhNone.

Therefore, life is passing but what is important is that it isn't wasted, and to spend it how AllāhNone likes. Because the guest who visits wants to see in you the favours given to you by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla in this world. As HeNone has given them to you, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is happy when you take from them. Therefore, life should not be wasted. Make intention for the pleasure of AllāhNone. As long as you pray and fast, what else can life be about? These people who don't understand this waste their lives in pubs, coffee shops, here and there, or just sitting around. There are many people who are too lazy to get up from where they sit and pray. May AllāhNone not make us from them. May HeNone guide them, in shā’a Llāh.

May AllāhNone bless our Ramaḍān. May AllāhNone accept your fasting, your zakāh and ṣadaqah. May AllāhNone accept your goodness, in shā’a Llāh. May AllāhNone make them remain forever, and their blessings remain forever. We must make shukr to AllāhNone. We are making shukr to AllāhNone, shukran liLlāh. When we are grateful, HeNone gives more, and doesn't decrease. We make shukr to AllāhNone so that our shukr increases our īmān and goodness, in shā’a Llāh. Ash-shukru liLlāh wa-l-ḥamdu liLlāh.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying: "Shahru Ramaḍāna lladhī unzila fīhi l-Qur’ānu hudan li-n-nāsi wa-bayyinātin mina l-hudá wa-l-furqān" (2:185).

HeNone praising month of Ramaḍān which it is, whole Qur’ān sent in this month to Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, to hisNone ummah, to hisNone nation. We are Ummatu l-Qur’ān, nation of Qur’ān. And it was in this month AllāhNone send this. It is saying for goodness of people, and there is bayyināt, miracle. There is miracle until Qiyāmah, until forever miracle in Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n, which it is word of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, Allāh Almighty. And this is one month, nearly finish.

We are, quickly [Ramaḍān] went and it was, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, very blessed for mu’min, for believer, and you feel sometimes sadness. For this, in Turkey or Ottoman time, there was in mosques after twenty of Ramaḍān, they make beginning to make poem between tarawīh, each rak‘ah, saying, "Al-waḍa‘ yā shahru Ramaḍān, al-waḍa‘ yā shahru ṣ-ṣiyām." So, saying, it is greeting: "You are going, we are sad. You leave us, as you are guest for us and you leave, valuable guest." When some people, guest, valuable guest they have, and he leave to his place, they are getting sad. We also, believer, they become sad at the end of month of Ramaḍān. We used to it, and because not only used to it, because the bless from AllāhNone, mercy from AllāhNone, showering us in this month, so you feel sadness when it is finished. Because many months until coming next one. You don't know if you will reach or not reach.

But you must be happy, because your life, one year also going from your life, but your life, your destination, it is good destination, which – paradise, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone promise us for paradise. And don't be sad, "My one year went." Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, one year went with good thing, with way of AllāhNone, way of ProphetNone intention to be good and to do good thing. This is going with happiness towards our LordNone, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone bless you and, in shā’a Llāh, protect you.

And give the people who are not understanding value of this month to understand this, and give them hidayāh also, in shā’a Llāh. We are just – AllāhNone, mercy – we are mercy for people who are not knowing the value, not knowing this sweet taste of belief, īmān. AllāhNone keep HisNone, this bless for us.

And we thank AllāhNone for what HeNone gave us, because when you thank HimNone, HeNone give more. And we thanking HimNone for what HeNone gave us, and to put us in this way, way of ProphetNone and HisNone way. We are thanking billions time for HimNone for this, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone make you happy here and hereafter, in shā’a Llāh. In shā’a Llāh, we reach next Ramaḍān. In shā’a Llāh, we hope, with Sayyidunā Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
