Waṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-F’āiz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-

Mankind usually is in hopelessness, [yet] those who are hopeless of Allah’s mercy are those who have no faith. Allah ‘azza wa jalla says, “Do not cut your hopes in Allah.” Look at everything with hope. There are many who are now dragging people towards hopelessness. They plan things based on their own thoughts and say, “It will be this or that way…” They do not include Allah in any affair because they have no faith. Allah is all powerful in every affair! He is the one who does it all, so mankind should not become hopeless. Allah opens a door, and so however this thing came, it will also leave. So do not cut your hopes in Allah’s mercy and in what He will do.

They are dragging people towards hopelessness, while their intention is something else. Their intention is evil. May Allah protect us. Insha’Allah, last night was the Night of Miraj and many prayers were made. Allah will open the door of Rahmah so that this calamity will be removed from upon us. Insha’Allah, they will learn their lesson from it. Don’t let this [experience] go to waste, but let’s learn our lesson and turn to Allah, ask from Allah. It does not help asking non-Muslims, but [it helps to ask] Allah. So that this suffering and the sadness that has been felt shall not be wasted, we will ask Allah. If you leave it to Allah, then He rewards you. If not, if you do not let this intention come to your mind, then all is wasted and useless. All suffering is rewarded. All is calculated in front of Allah. We believe in Allah and ask from Allah. May Allah protect the world of the Muslims. Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-F ti ah
