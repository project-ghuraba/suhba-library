Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Today is the holy day of Friday. Friday is a holy day presented by Allah (JJ) to Muslims and our Holy Prophet (SAW). Friday is not only holy in the world but also in the hereafter. There is death and there is eternal life in the hereafter. In the hereafter, Allah Azza wa Jalla will show His beauty as a gift to Muslims every Friday. No matter how many beautiful things there are in paradise, the beauty of Allah will appear more beautiful for the people of paradise.

We do not know how Allah will manifest Himself. Nobody has the ability to see Allah Azza wa Jalla. You cannot see Him. He shows His manifestations as He likes. He shows His beauty, not His essense. His attributes and His beauty are separate things. Allah has hidden Himself because nobody can withstand seeing Him. Nobody can withstand it! Everything has a limit. The limit is different for little people as it is different for great people. Our Holy Prophet (SAW) was able to reach a limit the most. Even our Holy Prophet (SAW) had no permission beyond that limit. Allah is such a being that He tells us not to think about Himself. Think about His attributes. It is enough to look at what He does and the delight in the things He created.

Because of the illness of the ego - there are many of course - some people, due to their mind’s shortcoming, to attract people to themselves or for some benefit, make judgments about Allah Azza wa Jalla. They say that He is like this and like that about Allah. These are all things that happen because of going too far about Allah Azza wa Jalla and because of the illnesses of the ego.

Allah has given us what is enough for us to do. We are not able to do them anyway. Let us not go ahead and cross the limit. Those who cross the limit, who cross the boundary get punishment. Allah has given the greatest blessing. Let us thank and glorify Him. It was just read, it is in both Yasin Sharif and Sura Sajdah: “Allah guides whom He wills. This is a great blessing.” This is not vouchsafed for everybody. Do not go after these people who fly and run. We do not need them. The ego wants such things and Shaitan incites it.

Let us go on the right way, on the way we know, and after things Allah has shown loud and clear. Not after doubt and suspicion. They make people lose their faith with suspicion. Allah forbid. May Allah grant us true faith, strong faith Inshallah. May He keep us safe from the evils of Shaitan and especially from these human shaitans Inshllah. May Allah keep us under His protection for the sake of this Friday Inshallah. May there be plenty of blessings.

Al-Fatiha.
