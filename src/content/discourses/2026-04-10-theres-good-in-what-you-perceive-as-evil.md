As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says in Qur’ān ʿAẓīmu sh-Sha’n: “There is good in some things you dislike, and there is no good in some things you like.” That is, don’t think of evil negatively. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla will create good in it. Good will come from what you perceive as evil.

Now, the state of this world is already clear. There is good in everything. For a Muslim, for someone who is in Islām, it is good. Nothing is evil, by the permission of Allah. Everything is written in our book of deeds. In return, Allah will bestow favors upon them and upon us in the Hereafter, inshā’Allah.

Therefore, most things that appear in this world are not actually as they seem. They are not at all as they seem. Shayṭān deceives people. Evil is inherently evil, but in this world, there are many evil things that appear good. There are shayṭāns in dunyā, even human shayṭāns who are worse than shayṭān. They take on all sorts of forms. They can deceive all kinds of people.

So, Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla makes the state of this world as a means to test people. So, we will regard everything that happens as the decree of Allah. Let’s see what the outcome will be; as they say, “Whatever Allah does, He does beautifully.”

Therefore, some people rebel against the decree of Allah; Ḥāshā (far from it). They rebel knowingly or unknowingly. They say, “How is this happening?” Ḥāshā! “How does Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla allow this?” If He didn’t allow it, nothing would happen without His permission. Let these people repent and seek forgiveness for what they see.

Allah knows what is happening. Ḥāshā, are you going to teach Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla! Repent and seek forgiveness. Repent and seek forgiveness. Some people not only think this in their hearts but also say it directly with their mouths; there are many fools and mindless people who do so.

They say, “We are rebelling.” To whom are you going to rebel? Do you have anywhere to escape from the command and presence of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla? Do you think you will be saved by rebelling? Ḥāshā! Repent and seek forgiveness. Allah does everything in the best way. Don’t be sad, don’t worry. Don’t listen to those who talk too much. See the truth. The truth is what Allah wants. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla is the one who does everything, and He does everything in the best way. There is no questioning of what He does. He cannot be questioned!

This morning is a Friday morning. Let us all repent and seek forgiveness. Whatever has crossed our hearts – our Prophet ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam says, “Do not rebel.” Everything has a purpose. For those who are upon Islām, the outcome of everything is good.

Therefore, may Allah protect us. May Allah protect us from the evil of our egos, from the evil of shayṭāns, and from the evil of human shayṭāns. May Allah protect us from the evil of those who make evil appear good and lead us into sin.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq

Al-Fātiḥa.

There are Qur’ān Khatms, ayāt, surahs, tasbihāt, tahlilāt, salawāt, khatms of Dalail al-Khayrāt, and good deeds done; may Allah accept them all. For the soul of our Prophet ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, his family and companions, all the prophets, saints, pious people, and mashāyikh; for the souls of all our ancestors; for the souls of believing men and women, Muslim men and women; for the arrival of good and the warding off of evil; for the strength of our faith; for happiness here and hereafter.

Lillāhi Ta’āla

Al-Fātiḥah.
