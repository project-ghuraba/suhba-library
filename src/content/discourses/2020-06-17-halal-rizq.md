Aū‘dhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-Shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Waṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-F’āiz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-

A’ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. …wa Allāhu yarzuqu man yashā’u bi-ghayri ḥisāb Qur’ān 2:12 and 24:38) (“Allah provides rizq to whomever He will without any accounting.”) Allah Almighty says in the holy ayah that He gives rizq to whomever He wills without having to answer to anyone. So we should not be worried about our provision. Everybody has been assigned however much to earn and consume and breathe. It is all written. It is not going to be more or less than that.

It is assigned by Allah, and therefore we need to search out our provision in permissible, halal ways. Halal is the most important thing [in this matter], the greatest. It is not only for you, but for your children and family, for your wife. It provides benefit to the whole family. On the other hand, whoever receives it in forbidden haram ways will find it of no use and a harm to people.

In the minds of today’s people this is not something real. They do not pay attention to this matter at all. As long as money comes in, it does not matter [to them how]. Of course, what is haram money is clearly known, just as halal is. When there is something doubtful, you should stay away from what is doubtful.

May Allah open the eye of the heart so that we are able to distinguish haram from halal. May Allah help us know, insha’Allah. May Allah give us what is halal. May we stay away from what is haram. May we be provided and fed with halal, and may we feed our families with halal. May Allah help us all and help the Muslims. May He strengthen our faith. Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-F ti ah
