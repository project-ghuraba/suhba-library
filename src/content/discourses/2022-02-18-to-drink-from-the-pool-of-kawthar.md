A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

May AllāhNone always bless this day of ours. May Jum‘ah, our holy day, be blessed, in shā’a Llāh. On this holy day, may AllāhNone make HisNone generosity and blessings last. In the time we are living, all kinds of needs are made easier for people from one perspective. Until recently, people did not have such ease due to tools and machines. But there is also much hardship that comes with this. People are spoiled now. When they devoted themselves to work, there was not much opportunity for their egos. But now everything is done by others.

When mankind has nothing to do, what will happen eventually? They will still pass on to ākhirah. There is no comfort in dunyā. Make as many tools as you want or have nothing from what you want. This world is still not paradise for people. There is no life without troubles and sufferings in this world. Everyone has something, big or small. There are many things that will disturb or annoy people. Therefore, believers, those who believe in AllāhNone, are relaxed because they know our life is not everlasting. Everlasting life is in ākhirah. There is no trouble, no sadness, nothing in paradise. There is no envy, stinginess, or treachery. When you enter paradise and drink from the pool of Kawthar, all these attributes will not enter paradise with you, by the order of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. All of them will be stripped off then. People will wash themselves and enter paradise without them.

Some people here imagine paradise based on their worldly minds and say, "It will be like this and like that there. How will it be so?" Don't ask how. There are incomprehensible and unimaginable things in paradise. First, as we said, bad manners and egoistic useless things, things that bring sadness, will be cleaned off before people enter paradise. This is how people will enter paradise. And those who will enter hell, may AllāhNone protect us, they will enter together with all their bad attributes and remain there. May AllāhNone protect us.

Therefore, we are asking for the present time, may Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla strengthen our īmān. When we have īmān, all the hardships of this world are nothing. The more hardships you go through, the more rewards you are given. You will see it in ākhirah. But people without īmān will go to ākhirah together with their sufferings, without having any benefit or reward for them. May AllāhNone give īmān to all of us. May AllāhNone give guidance to those who don't have īmān, to all humanity. People are creations of AllāhNone. We should ask for goodness for them as well. The greatest, the best goodness for people is guidance, to be on the way of AllāhNone. May AllāhNone forgive us all.

We are, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are today, Jum‘ah. It is our blessed day for Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam and hisNone ummah – day of Jum‘ah, Friday. The beginning for week we call Friday. For Muslim ummah, it is blessed. It was, some place they make holiday, they close Friday. In time of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, it was blessed day but they can work, they can do everything but only time of praying it is forbidden to sell or to buy anything. You must go to half an hour, fifteen minute, to mosque, pray Jum‘ah. After this, you are free whole [day]. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla only want from believer to put this time out of material thing – only spiritual thing Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla order for people. And it is not so big thing.

But people they are not listening what... Who not listening he is unfortunate, unlucky. But, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are happy with this day and we are living time of very, for some, something, sometime very difficult – for some people very difficult. For some people, not for human being difficult it was not difficult. Because now everything they try to do – whole world for people, to serve people, human being. They have machine, washing machine, many thing of machine for ladies, for housewives. Before, it was must burn fire with wood to cook and to put for also for washing. It was so difficult. Now, very easy for them. For men also, same. Even there is machine for everything. In factory also machine. No need for people to be tired. Cars, motors, airplane – everything, very easy for human being now.

Ah yes, this very easy but what happen? People they are happy? No. Make them more sad, more unhappy. Even they have everything but they are unhappy with this. They must be happy because everything make easy not like old time. To go from here to Lefkosa, Nicosia, three days you want to go walking there. Now, half an hour you be there. But also that people they was more easy for them because people, they have now lot of time. With this time they are not thinking to do good thing. They are doing what their ego like. For this, every time they are follow ego, they are become more sad and more unhappy. Before, old time, there was no time to enjoy yourself. Only whole time you must you work by your hand, by your walking, coming, going, cutting. Factory also you must work in by your hand. So, no time for people to be, have time, enough time to be stressed and to be unhappy, to become mad, to become crazy. There was only, they concentrate to finish this work. This finished, tomorrow same. Again, you must run or to do this again and again. No time for people to be spoiled.

But now people, they are all not happy. But people, they are wanting more and more, more comfortable. They say, "This is not enough, not enough for us. We must, we need more and more." But people who are nonbeliever, they will be never happy. Why? Because they don't have any hope for real life. Real life... What we are living in world – the most richest one or most poorest one, all they are, they have some problem with themself. This problem why? Because you are in place [to] prepare for next life. Next life it will be happy and peaceful for who are believe, believer. Who not believer, they're trying in this life and they're saying, "No, nothing after this." But for believer, they are know if they are patient they will be have, from their Lord, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, for every difficult[y] – reward. And when, "wa-d-dāru l-ākhiratu khayrun li-lladhīna yattaqūn" (7:169). Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying. "The next life, it is the better life for believer, who afraid from AllāhNone."

And it will be real when, for paradise no stressful, nothing from this, any unhappiness who in this world. Especially, in the door of paradise, this is, there is Ḥawḍu l-Kawthar – Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, hisNone pool this. Water from Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, heNone is owner this. HeNone will, everyone coming, before going to inside paradise, they will drink from this water. And this water's speciality, it is like to clean everyone going inside. From what? From ego desire, ego attribute, and what is this: jealousy, enemy [enmity], retreat [hatred]. Every bad thing you imagine in this world, it will be finish. You'll be like angel, no anything against anybody. Everybody they be happy with each other, with other. Not, no any jealousy there. No any hatred. And this, all this what we have in this world from bad character, from bad attribute, it will be finish. Cannot be enter paradise.

So, in paradise – the place of happiness. For this, we are not unhappy here also because we are knowing this it will not go for nothing. All this AllāhNone will reward us for this. For disbeliever, no not believer, they are unlucky. But we are making we pray for them to AllāhNone give them hidāyah, to show them right way because human being AllāhNone create them and we are, we must try to help them. To help them – show them right way. This is our help for them and pray for them to be in right way, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
