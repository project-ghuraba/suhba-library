As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh. A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam is saying, "Lā yu’minu aḥadukum ḥattá yakūn." Aw kamā qāl.

The Turkish translation of this ḥadīth... When reading ḥadīth in Arabic, people must read each word correctly [and perfectly]. Therefore, let's say it in Turkish now. A person's belief is not complete until his every action is according to the pleasure of AllāhNone. He must strive for it, to ensure that AllāhNone is happy with him, that he is leading his life according to the pleasure of AllāhNone. Of course, people make mistakes. And AllāhNone forgives their mistakes. What AllāhNone wants and what AllāhNone loves, is to forgive us. This is it.

The blessed words of our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam inform us that we can't do everything. We have done wrong and made mistakes. We must ask for forgiveness for AllāhNone to be pleased. That is something AllāhNone loves. Other than that, there is not much we can do. We cannot do things that AllāhNone orders one hundred percent. We can hardly do even one percent. Therefore, the important point here is to love asking AllāhNone for forgiveness. AllāhNone says HeNone forgives people, and HeNone forgives believers.

There is a ḥadīth sharīf, another ḥadīth sharīf. If you didn't commit sins and make mistakes, AllāhNone would create other people who would make mistakes. They would ask for forgiveness and HeNone will forgive them. Therefore, this is good tidings for us: that Allāh'sNone happiness with us increases with each action we repent of, and each time we ask forgiveness for what we did so that HeNone forgives our mistakes. That is easy.

Some people force others to do good deeds. Certainly, everyone does good deeds, shukr to AllāhNone. There are many people who do good deeds. But what we do has no value. We must know this too. Compared to the value of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla's forgiveness, what we do is nothing. It is not even a thing. If you value it and assume you are doing something praiseworthy, you are mistaken. There are mistakes in everything we do. We need forgiveness. We need the forgiveness of AllāhNone. We must know this, because then we can attain the pleasure of AllāhNone. May AllāhNone be happy with all of us, in shā’a Llāh. May we achieve HisNone pleasure. Ḥadīth saying – Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying – you will not be real believer until everything you do, it is accepted with Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla in HisNone way. This is to be real believer, real mu’min, you must be like this. Because time of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, whole ṣaḥābah they was doing with Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, same, this hundred percent. They doing everything and AllāhNone accept it, happy with them. They get Riḍā Allāh, Riḍā. But ourself, we cannot do hundred percent. Maybe, hardly, I don't know if we can, also, one percent we can do, what AllāhNone HeNone ordered, and or HeNone is happy with us.

So, how we will be get Riḍā Allāh, happiness of AllāhNone, blessed of AllāhNone for us? There is easy way for us, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, this ummah. To be ask for forgiveness from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Everyday you must ask forgiveness. Because we do wrong thing, we do sin, we do everything. Even our worshipping, it is not proper. If you think you are doing good and it is acceptable, it is – you are wrong. At what we praying, we fasting – all this, I don’t think we are doing proper as much as can be – but, we are... and you must know this.

But al-ḥamdu liLlāh, there is thing for us. It’s very easy. To ask forgiveness. Because Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying for – in ḥadīth, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone saying if this ummah not doing wrong, not doing sin, not doing thing – so all they are pure, no sin for them – INone will create some people and they will do sin, they will do wrong and they will ask for forgiveness. INone will forgive them.

So, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are already, we doing everything. So, only what you must do, to be, to make AllāhNone happy with us: to ask forgiveness. HeNone said, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, AllāhNone, the most happy thing for HimNone, to hear HisNone servant, asking forgiveness from HimNone. And He'sNone generous. He'sNone happy, HeNone forgives us. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, and accept what we do. And we get HisNone Riḍā Allāh. Riḍā, meaning HisNone pleasure, HisNone happiness from us. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are asking for forgiveness all time, in shā’a Llāh.

Don’t think the wrong thing. For if anybody think what he do it is valuable, it is no – not valuable at all. Compared [to] what AllāhNone HeNone gave us, we are doing nothing from to thank for HimNone, from worshipping, ‘ibādah or other thing. Only we ask forgiveness from HimNone. And for what do [this]? To accept what we are doing from all this worshipping, ‘ibādah or good thing, to accept this, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone forgive us and give us this, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
