Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Today is the last day of the holy Ramadan. Our Holy Prophet gave good tidings that the beginning of this holy month is mercy, the middle is forgiveness and the end is exemption from hell. This is the greatest favor. Allah starts this beautiful month with mercy for the Ummah of our Holy Prophet, grants forgiveness in the middle and liberation in the end. We have these good tidings while in this world, thank Allah.

We’ve had a good month. The last day is when prayers are accepted, especially after Asr. You can do goodness and ask forgiveness throughout the day as well. All good deeds and prayers will be accepted. Tonight is the eve of Eid and it is a holy night. There are several holy nights in a year and one of them is the eve of Eid. It is also a favor from Allah. Allah is Al-Karim, the Generous. He creates opportunities for people for them to take from His generosity. However, people are not aware. If they are aware, they don’t want it sometimes because of laziness, sometimes because of stubbornness and sometimes because of misfortune. They are in loss themselves. Those who do not obey the orders of Allah are in loss. They lose great and valuable things. They run after the garbage of this world and leave the jewels. And these days are jewel days, thank Allah.

The holy Ramadan is gone. It is gone as a good visitor. We are sad, but certainly Allah is giving its rewards. May Allah help us reach more years and more Ramadans with better conditions for Islam and better conditions for Muslims. May He send us the Sahib. May this oppression come to an end. And may all this world brighten with the light of Islam insha’Allah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
