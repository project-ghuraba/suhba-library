As-Salāmu ‘Alaykum wa RaḥmatuLlāhi wa Barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu BiLlāhi Minash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. BismiLlāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm.

Wa ṣ-Salātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Akhirīn.

Madad yā RasūlAllāh, Madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi RasūliLlāh, Madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

Dastūr Mawlana Shaykh Abdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistāni, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-

Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-Suḥbah wa l-Khayru fi l-Jam‘iyyah.

Insha’Allah with the permission of Allah ﷻ, we are going on a trip today for the pleasure of Allah ﷻ [to Europe]. Insha’Allah it will be blessed and good, because traveling is the Sunnah of Prophets. And by following their Sunnah insha’Allah, we made an intention to be the means for guidance with their Barakah and Himmah/support. Insha’Allah there will be guidance both for us and for others.

We will reach out to the good servants of Allah ﷻ and spend time with them. If we bring happiness and peace to them, it will be enough, Shukr to Allah ﷻ. And according to the Sunnah of our Holy Prophet ﷺ, if a believer visits another believer for the pleasure of Allah ﷻ, Thawab will be written and a sin will be forgiven for every step of his. And his rank will be raised. It is enough for us Shukr to Allah ﷻ, and it is enough for them.

Therefore, it is something done for the pleasure of Allah ﷻ. It is not for Dunya. No matter where it is, when it is for the pleasure of Allah ﷻ, everything has a great benefit and Thawab. The most important thing is the pleasure of Allah ﷻ. Allah ﷻ will be pleased. Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla will say, “These people are going on My way. They are going in order to spread My orders. I am pleased with them.”

Therefore, may everything we do be purely for the pleasure of Allah ﷻ. We are trying to imitate as much as we can. May it be accepted insha’Allah and blessed. May Allah ﷻ be pleased with those who came here. May they have great rewards insha’Allah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
