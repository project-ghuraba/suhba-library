This is we don't know. This shows how we are ignorant. This shows how ignorant we are, this shows how we don't know anything. Alhamdulillah, Allah Almighty accept all His servants. Why? Because He is creating them. He is the One designing them. He is the one "I want this shape for My servant". Allah Almighty, Himself, designing His servant. Very important. This is how much importance Allah Dhul Jalal wa Taqaddas Hz gives to His servant & how He created His servant with His Own Hands & Allah Dhul Jalal wa Taqaddas Hz Himself decided how his form will be, how he will look like & He, Himself shaped with His Hand of Power. This is so precious. Noah (as) invited people to Allah, to His servanthood for 80 years. But each one of them is occupied with his own doing, with his own fun.. Finally by the end of 800 years, he prayed "O Lord, these people will not be corrected. It is time that You may bring the flood & finish them." And prophet's prayers are accepted. So the flood arrived... This is the known story. This remind me with Sayyidina Noah. He was 800 years asking people "O my people, follow. Be.. Follow me & be good servant for Allah Almighty. I invite you to be servant for Allah Almighty". And everyone was in his own doing his things... At the end of the day he said.. 800 years- it is not 1 year, 2 year. He was fed up & said "Ya Rabbi, I am cursing them & I am asking from You to bring the tufan, the flood." And you know the story of the flood.

After this flood finish & he was safe with his people and animals, Allah Almighty ordered Noah (as). He said "I want you to build for Me 10 very nice pots from mud, clay". And he make very nice pot from the clay & make very nice flowers, colors & he was really working to make it nice as much as he can. And it was like, each one was like piece of art- 10 pieces. And he put them & he said "Ya Rabbi, I finished my pottery." Noah (as)..The flood is finished. He safely landed off from the ship with his animals. Allah DhulJalal wa Taqaddas Hz said "O Noah, I want from you to make 10 pots. You may do them as you like but they should be beautiful." And Noah (as) really made 10 pots. He put flowers, designs on them, made them in colors..spent so much time making them, such an elaborate work for the pots. And he placed these 10 pots & said "O Lord, I made these pots & present them to You. I finished my work". The order descended from Allah. He said "O Noah, take these pots & start breaking them!" Noah (as) looked at the pots, looked at his work. He said "Yahu how will I break these? I spent all this time for them". But the order came from the Lord of Heavens, there is no return anymore. He took his staff & broke all of them. He smashed all of them into pieces & made like this with his feet- all totally turned into dust. Noah (as), when he put his potteries & said "Ya Rabbi, I am proud that I make this pottery for You & I am presenting this pottery for You". He said "Yes, we accept your pottery. And now the 2nd order is to break all these potteries". "Break? What? I was so excited to do these potteries & now You are ordering me to break it?" And he was like, "there is no choice, Allah Almighty order, finish!" And he start breaking.. But when he was crushing, it was like he crush himself also. And finally he was also (stepping) with his feet, to make it really dust. Do you think I was happy to finish them? You only make 10 pots & you are so desperate & you are so sad inside. And you've been not happy with this order. I am accepting your dua as the order, that I am giving you". Then Noah (as) realised that the curse was not good sign or not good things that he done.

That is why when you see Mawlana Shaykh, he is the most merciful & the most tolerant, it is coming from Sayyidina Muhammad (sas). Because he is the only Prophet who didn't make any curse for his nation. And this, when he was going & they throw stones & break his teeth, and Gabriel (as) was next to him. Whoever hears this, knows this, will know how great & wide the Mercy of Allah is, how open the door of tawba is. And if he knows, everyone will find faith. Insha Allah Allah grants us this faith that we may also benefit from this Mercy of Allah.

There is a story that happened in old times. Most people take it as a tale, or as an example, or as a lesson for people. There were passengers on a ship. And the ship got caught in a storm & it sank. Only a pregnant woman survived the ship & she was able to reach an island. That woman delivered & that kid survived by the power of Allah. His mother died but he survived. He spent his entire life on that island. He was inspired & it became a means for him to worship & he became a good servant to Allah. He spent his life alone on that island with worship & in obedience to Allah. And after that, his soul was taken. Of course man starts, lives & dies. It came to the Day of Judgement & on the Judgement Day he was questioned. He was asked "You never sinned, never committed any haram, never did anything other than worship in your whole life. Would you like to enter paradise with Allah's Mercy or with your deeds?" And he said "Of course I would like to enter with my good deeds since I spent an innocent life." They said "Ok, bring all his good deeds & we put them in the scale. We may also put on the scale the eye, the blessing of seeing Allah granted". The scale turned upside down (since eye outweighed). He said "Aman ya Rabbi with Your Mercy, I want to enter paradise with Your Mercy". Therefore Allah's Mercy is always ahead of everything else.

Don't do a good deed or a goodness & be proud of it afterwards. Don't do a nice thing & be proud of it. Say "This is a goodness Allah granted me. It is a good deed Allah granted me" because it is a grant. It is not by your effort or trying- Allah granted & you did it. If you take it this way, you receive very high maqams. But if you are proud with your deed, then it is considered as egoistic & Allah doesn't like ego at all.

And this is another story about somebody- it has become as a fairy tale or it has become as a lesson or as an example to be known. Once upon a time there was one boat in the ocean. Even in dunya no action, in akhirah also no action. Said "Ya Rabbi, tawba ya Rabbi! I want to be with Your Mercy". And this shows Allah Almighty's Greatness.
