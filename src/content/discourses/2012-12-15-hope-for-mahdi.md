Allahumma salli wa sallim 'ala Nabiyina Muhammad 'alayhi salam. Salatan tadumu wa tughda ilayh mamarra layali wa tuula dawam. Allahumma salli wa sallim 'ala Nabiyina Muhammad 'alayhi salam. Salatan tadumu wa tughda ilayh mamarra layali wa tuula dawam. Allahumma salli wa sallim 'ala Nabiyina Muhammad 'alayhi salam. Salatan tadumu wa tughda ilayh mamarra layali wa tuula dawam.

Alhamdulillah, Alhamdulillah that Allah Almighty make me to sit here & speak to you. (laughter) Shukur Allah that He made me sit here & granted me to speak to you. How is this? (As a shukur). Everyone wishing to speak to people but my wish come true. (laughter). Everyone wants to address to people, desires it all the time & Allah granted me this. We are in the time that the people been very confused. And we don't know who is telling the truth, or who is telling the lie, or imagination, or or or.. We've reached such a time that people are speaking & we don't know if they say the truth, or false, or imaginary things. We can't know who is doing what. We are at such a time now- all in a twilight zone. It's between darkness & light. Nobody knows what is going on.

Now we have very famous question in these days, very famous question: The Mayan calendar. Nowadays there is a very important question in this twilight zone. People are asking "what about this Mayan calendar & the doom?" "Yahu!" I am saying, Mawlana Shaykh saying, "You're not believing in Allah Almighty. You're not believing in Prophet. You're not believing in the prophecy, awliya.. And now all the world, all the world say "what will happen, what will happen?" Nothing will happen, Mawlana S. says. Whole people now..No one believes in Prophet, nor do they believe in Allah. They don't believe in the foundation of Prophets, awliyas..don't believe in anyone & now everyone talks about this Mayan calandar & doom & want to believe in it. They say"Yahu what will happen, what will happen?" Mawlana Shaykh, very clear answer: "Nothing will happen". "Why?" Everyone saying "Why?" Why? It is very simple because the pure knowledge & the pure information from the purest spring is from Mawlana Shaykh in this century. Alhamdulillah. Now everyone is asking Mawlana S. "What will happen?" And Mawlana says "Nothing will happen". It is very definite.

They ask "Why?" Because what comes out of the mouth of Mawlana is the clearest, purest & healthiest information of this century. There can't be an information that is more pure or correct than this. And Mawlana Shaykh saying "according to our belief & according to our information, it says we have to wait for the Sahib al-Zaman. So we have process to go." It will not be like this (all of a sudden). Even when the Sahaba asked the Prophet (sas), "you say you are coming & the Qiyamat is like sun rising" Then this Ayat coming "'Innaka Mayyitun Wa 'Innahum Mayyituna" (39:30).This is "you will pass away and they also will pass away." And the Sahaba say "It will be after you, O Rasulullah?" And Rasulullah explained. Everyone knows this story, I don't want to go there but that means there is a process to go on. The Companions asked our Prophet (sas) "O Rasulullah, you told us that "I have come & the Qiyamat is coming immediately". Then the Holy verse came, "Verily, you will die & verily, they (too) will die". They asked "O Rasulullah, is the Qiyamat right after this?" He(s) said "No. Caliphs will come, Princes will come, Sultans will come, tyrants will come.." Meaning there are certain time periods that we need to go through.

Because of this I am saying to the people: "Look at what is yours, what belongs to you a little bit. Whatever it is that belongs to you, look at it, believe in it, enjoy it. "There is this in that place, there is that in that place.." People rush to other places. Yahu look at yours, your table is rich also, even richer. They have nothing, it is all empty". They say "Let us have a look & come back". I am saying for the people, my humble advice-I am humble person anyhow- is to look after what you got. You don't have to look for others, what is going on. Ok, be informed about what is going on in the world. Don't be blind & don't be like ostrich-putting your head in the sand & say "Hah, this is me & I am happy" No! You look around but you have to know that you have the richest information & richest knowledge & the richest that can give for; not for this world only but for other world also. This is the barakah of Rasulullah (sas).

And I am saying, the people have very simple things to make their life more nice & more easy. Very simple but they never use it. It is the hope. I am saying that I have a simple advice for the people, that it is a very simple matter & most of the people are not using this, which is "the hope". "The hope". Everyone is hopeless, everyone is hopeless, hopeless saying "I have no hope, I have no hope".. Everyone is hopeless. Yahu, hope. Everyone knows Magellan, the adventurer. When he was passing from Pacific Ocean to Indian Ocean he say "must be something downstairs, must be some passage, must be.." And when he got for hope point, he said "Alhamdulillah, we are safe". So this is the hope. Imagine, if somebody doesn't have hope like Magellan, that he is not a believer for.. but he was believer in what he was doing & he was hoping about what he will gain. Finally he find the hope point & say "this is the hope point, I am now in the safe side". See, the hope, how it brings any person from bad place to the good place-hope. Everyone think "it is hopeless" Don't say hopeless. Say yes "I hope, it is my hope this I catch". Alhamdulillah we have the solid hope that Allah Almighty give in our hand. Now people say "it is a hopeless situation, there is no hope". Don't be like this. Allah doesn't accept it, The Prophet doesn't accept either. He says "You should definitely have a hope. You should definitely have something to hold on to. Even if it is far, doesn't matter".

The famous sailor Magellan, he was going down down down when passing from the Pacific to Indian Ocean, looked like it will never finish. When he finally discovered the Cape of Good hope, he said to the sailors "we are saved". Yes, like this he said there "we are saved". So it was named Cape of Good Hope. So if that man didn't have that hope, that faith but gave up, he could be destroyed. But he has his name written in history. Everybody knows; when you say "Magellan" everyone knows him. Therefore the real hope came to us-Allah gave it in our hands through Prophet(s). This is not a small matter to be underestimated. Then they didn't leave it after him, the awliyaullah took over & they are carrying it on. Our hope is Mahdi (as). There is no harm in waiting for him- waiting is ibadah, a very big ibadah. That is why our hope, with all what is going on & with all what is happening is Mahdi (as). This is our real hope & light. Even you cannot catch that time, your waiting is more than ibadah. Your waiting & hoping is more effective in this time because now is the trade. Now you trade, in Mahdi (as) there is no more trade, finish. Finish!You are in the safe area. Or going with the boat (fighting with huge waves) & finally you come for the hope point. Then no more pain, Alhamdulillah, safe.

But to continue, that is the trade. Now we are going through the waves, it is big waves, (hitting) bom bom bom.. And Alhamdulillah, we are trying to keep up with our hope. And to cooperate with our hope because sometimes coming cloud in front of the sun & they say "oh, no more sun!"but actually the sun is there. Only the cloud is closing the sun. So don't lose your hope, by waiting Sayyidina Mahdi (as) & don't lose your hope, by asking for Mahdi (as). Make it more fire-put more wood in your fire. Put petrol in your fire. Make it hot, don't make it cold. This is very important also, to burn your hope- make it more flame, more flame, more flame.. And this, it shows that your love for Allah Almighty, for the Prophet, for the awliya & for Mahdi (as) is flaming all the time. This is very important. It is in your hand; either you put some dust & earth on it & finish or even you feed the fire of the hope & make it more flame. Now this hope matter of ours is a very important matter. Our people always say "there is no hope" & case is closed. This hope is like a fire. And you need to feed this fire all the time- by finding some wood, or gasoline or petrol. Whatever it takes you need to keep it going & burning.

When people fall into despair, they say "just extinguish & finish it". When you do that, everything is finished. Because of this, mankind needs to keep his hope live & burning all the time. The Companions conquered the world- in 25 years they conquered the world. The made the huge Byzantine Empire fall down. What was it? With the smallest hope Prophet (sas) gave them, it opened. They fed it, fed it constantly & made the world come to faith, come to Islam. Ok, we are not the Companions, we are ordinary people but they give us also the flame of hope. We are not short of, or deprived of it. That is why we say, the Sahaba conquered the world in 25 years. So little people, how? They flamed their love, hope for Allah Almighty & they distributed it for everyone. We are not Sahaba. We are ordinary people, maybe less than ordinary people. But Allah Almighty also grant us this flame of hope. So our duty & job is to keep this flame alive & make it bigger & make it bigger. As much as you make it bigger, as much as you can get the people in it.

See Mawlana Shaykh? All the world now know him. Why? He is ordinary person. Very ordinary person. He doesn't have army, he doesn't have money, he doesn't have any political power.. But because of his flame that Allah Almighty give him, he can rule the world. It is not difficult because have the sincere of the hope. And with this one we say, wa min Allahu tawfiq, bi hurmat al Habib, bi hurmat al Fatiha. It is more than enough.
