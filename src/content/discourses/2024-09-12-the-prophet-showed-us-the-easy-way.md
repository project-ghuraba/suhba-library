As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam tells us to be on the middle way. HeNone wants the ummah to be on the middle way. "When you do something, do it without going to extremes," suggests our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Everything should be easy. The religion of Islām is not difficult. It is an easy religion. There is ease in this religion. What should we do?

All the worship we must do is difficult for our egos. It seems difficult for the ego, but actually it is a very easy thing for people. But some people are taking more burdens on themselves. And some are not doing anything at all. Therefore, to be in the middle and to be consistent is the advice of our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam.

Three people once came to Masjidu n-Nabawī of our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. One of them said, "I won't sleep at all. I will worship continuously." Another one said, "I will fast. I won't do without fasting, worshipping and fasting." The third one said, "I won't ever get married in order not to miss my worship. I will only worship." When our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam heard this, heNone said, "INone sleep and wake up. INone worship. INone fast and sometimes INone don't. INone don't always fast. And INone get married. Not getting married is not good," said our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. This is a lesson for us.

Those ṣaḥābah thought according to their own minds, but in reality, our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, who was far superior to them, did not see the need to do what they suggested. No need, because when your worship is accepted by AllāhNone, you are together with AllāhNone. You attain the pleasure of AllāhNone. Things you do within a day, time you spend with your family, working and earning your provision, are counted as worship by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Therefore, you shouldn't pull yourself away from this world like a monk and do nothing except worship, says our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. You attain rewards and thawāb through those things you do. This is easiness. Otherwise, if HeNone told you to do these things, most of those who devote themselves to worship, people on the way of AllāhNone, would leave everything and do only that – staying awake, fasting, staying single; people have different capacity. Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam is teaching us the best. HeNone is advising the best for us. Following hisNone way is the biggest gift for believers. If AllāhNone makes you successful in going on that way, that is the biggest and best thing you can ask from AllāhNone. We should make du‘ā’: May AllāhNone not separate us from the right way. May the love of our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam always be in our hearts. This is the most important thing. May the love of our Holy ProphetNone be above everything. There is nothing above it. There is no worldly thing above it. Because people have been living and leaving for thousands of years. They live and leave. What states have been, what kings have lived, what sulṭāns have come. None of them have remained. Those who had love, who loved for the pleasure of AllāhNone are in profit. But when they say, "I got so much and so much profit," no benefit comes from things that are not for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.

Others' benefits are for themselves. We should always have the love of our Holy ProphetNone in our hearts more than anything. Sometimes people say they love this and that without knowing. Therefore, we should ask for forgiveness every day. There cannot be any love above the love of our Holy ProphetNone. Nothing. Neither children nor parents, nor the love of your homeland can be above the love of our Holy ProphetNone. Therefore, people must be careful when they speak. If they always keep this in their minds, they will attain a great reward for their intention in the presence of AllāhNone. And the biggest reward is the intercession of our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. That is what we all need the most. Without it, you may have all the knowledge in the world, you may perform the worship of all servants but unless you have love for our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, none of that has any benefit.

May AllāhNone make all of us steadfast, in shā‘a Llāh.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are having this blessed days of our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. HeNone praise us with HisNone blessed, to companion to be from hisNone nation. It is big thing for us. We must think about only this, for Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, because heNone make everything [easy] – [heNone] ask from AllāhNone, to make it easy for us, for believer, for follower of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam: for hisNone nation, to make it easy, not to make it difficult.

Because, time of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, there was ṣaḥābah, three ṣaḥābah, speaking in mosque, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, masjid. And they were, one of them tell other, "I am, I will to be not sleeping at all. I will worship whole twenty-four hour." Other one, he said, "I will be fasting whole time and also worshipping whole time. No, for no need to eat. No. To make, get more reward, I will be fast all time, all time." Other, third one, he said, "I will be not married. I will not married, because I want, I don't want to waste time with family or another. Only, I will worship. Only, I will do this." So Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, when heNone see, heNone see, hear this, heNone said, "INone am sleeping and waking up, praying, worshipping. INone am fasting and sometime not fasting. Also, it is for me must be like this." And [for the] third one, heNone said, for said, "INone am... INone am married. INone am not be unmarried. This is my way."

So Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, showing us the easy way for Islām. Islām not difficult. Not difficult at all but difficult for our ego. Maybe, we can do many thing, many heavy thing during day but for praying coming, become very heavy for our ego. But normally, it is very easy to be a Muslim, to be a follower of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. And heNone is showing us this way. You can... Because when you are working, you are sleeping, you are waking up, you are going, coming, married – all this, when you doing by your intention for to be follow Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, all what you do, it is worshipping. AllāhNone accept as HisNone worship. So no need to be like monk in, in monastery or to be in far away from people. No. It is when you are go around and you are, in your heart you have love of AllāhNone, love of ProphetNone, all this AllāhNone accept as worship.

So no need to be make difficult for people. Maybe, people, they thought if you are Muslim, you must be twenty-four hour in mosque, not sleeping, not eating, only fasting, not married. This, no. All of they, you can do and you reward for what you do also. This is what Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, telling people, calling people to be in Islām, to be happy, because this giving happiness here, before ākhirah. This happiness...

People, they lost this happiness. Especially non-believer, they don't have any happiness. Even they are trying, trying hard to get happiness but, each time they make something to make be happiness, it become more dark, more worse. So Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, saying for people, to calling them to come to House of Peace. Peace of heart, it is with AllāhNone, with Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. For this Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, "You must love meNone more than everything, anything." Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, heNone don't need, heNone is not need to be to love himNone, because AllāhNone love himNone. So, what our love? But from hisNone mercy for us, heNone is saying this to be, to be make us happy, to make our heart in peace, to get reward from AllāhNone. This is very important – to love Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, more than anything in this world, from this life.

It is very important. It must be intention, this. Even you cannot do but you must say this: "We love Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, more from anything from mother, father, from children, wife, from land, from anything." No thing before Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. First for love – Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. This is give you reward. For each breath you take, for anything you do, AllāhNone reward you. AllāhNone make us in HisNone way. To be, to be with HimNone, in shā’a Llāh. Because this is love of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, the most precious thing. It is save you in Day of Judgment from bad end. Because if you have love, sure Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, heNone will save you. But if you don't respect, you don't have anything, if you have worshipping whole, like whole world but you don't have love for ProphetNone, it is dangerous for you. Maybe, you will not be safe in ākhirah. AllāhNone save us, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone bless you. Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
