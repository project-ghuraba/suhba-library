Astaghfiru Llāh, astaghfiru Llāh, astaghfiru Llāh min kulli dhanbin wa ma‘ṣiyah. Min kulli mā yukhālifu d-dīn al-islāmi ya arḥama r-rāḥimīn.

"Inna Llāha yuḥibbu t-tawwābīna wa yuḥibbu l-mutaṭahhirīn." (2:222) Allāh Almighty (jj) loves clean servants. He (jj) says no doubt He (jj) loves clean servants. Allāh Almighty (jj) loves clean servants, who are excellent in cleanliness. Mutaṭahhirīn, are people, who are extremely clean. Let's see what it means. In shā’a Llāhu r-raḥmān, we understand who is clean, who is dirty. Don't be dirty, troublesome. Be clean. You can be physically clean by washing your body. But the real cleanliness and purity is purity of the heart, is the pleasure of the heart.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. What does this mean? It means -keep me away from all kinds of evil things. A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm - when you say this, it means "I take refugee in Allāh from the rejected shayṭān, O Lord keep me away from all the things that shayṭān teaches people; keep me away from them, O Lord! Shayṭān teaches dirtiness to people. It is dirt. If you step inside, fall into it, you can't be clean. When you say A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm, it means "I reject all kinds of dirty actions... I don't accept any of them. Protect me from falling into them O Lord."

What are dirty actions, dirty deeds? That's why the Prophet (saw) said: "an-naẓāfatu mina l-īmān" - cleanliness is from belief. What has a clean person got to do with dirty deeds? A clean person has nothing to do with dirty deeds. It shouldn't be. There are different deeds in the world, clean and dirty. Mankind has honor. He should have clean deeds to keep his honor. When he has dirty deeds, he loses his honor. All right. There are two types of cleanliness and two types of dirty deeds that throw people into dirtiness. Two types of cleanliness: One type of cleanliness belongs to our physical body. The second is a spiritual cleanliness, the purity of our heart. The unclean, dirty things there are physical ones and there is also spiritual dirt, dirt of the ego. It is necessary to keep away from both of them. It is better not to be involved.

Our grand Shaykh, Sulṭānu l-Awliyā’, he used to relate from ‘Abdu l-Khāliq al-Ghujdawānī. Ghujdawānī is a big awliyā’, whom Allāh appointed as an imām. We are reciting these khatms, doing dhikr on dhikr night according to his instructions, according to his method - it is Allāh Almighty's instruction to him. Ghujdawānī is among the big awliyā’ who is recognized in the presence of Allāh. That's why at the end of each khatim we present it to al-Ghujdawānī Hz. Our Shaykh related what he said: "It has been 37 years since I stepped into a dirty place. I didn't jump over dirty places. I neither stepped in dirt nor did I jump over it." There is a huge wisdom, lesson for us in his words. You have to open your eyes when you walk, because there isn't a clean place left for us to prostrate, especially in our time now. The whole world is full of corruption now from East to West, from North to South. Inside there is the dirt of corruption and oppression. Even if there is no clean place left, if people still prostrate and pray, they are excused. Now our situation is like this. The world is drowning in such a corruption and oppression.

When a believer prostrates, he smells a beautiful smell as a reward. It is a reward for him when he prostrates. A beautiful smell comes to him. When he makes a real prostration he both smells a beautiful smell and also a veil is lifted from the eyes of someone, who really prostrates. If he makes a real sajdah once, a veil is lifted from his eyes. Heaviness goes away from his ears. He both hears and sees. He both watches and hears the unseen world. Our proof is the Prophet, ‘alayhi ṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salām, our confirmation is Qur’ān-i karīm.

When pharaoh sent the magicians to Moses ‘alayhi s-salām, pharaoh thought, Prophet Moses was an ordinary magician. Far from it. Because in his time, Egypt was full of thousands of magicians. When Prophet Moses threw his stick and it turned into a dragon, pharaoh thought he was tricking him by doing magic. He underestimated Prophet Moses. He said "I have thousands of magicians, who could do what you did. Let me arrange a competition of them against you. With this trick you are inviting me to serve your God, by showing proof. However I'm a god myself. I look right and left, I can't see any god except myself." The Egyptians at that time worshipped thousands of idols, statues, animals. Pharaoh said: "No! All you worship is below me. Nobody is superior to me." "Rabbukumu l-a‘lá" (79:24) - I'm your Lord most high. You are worshipping thousands of idols but I'm superior to all of them. I'm your greatest Lord. How can you tell me to come and be a servant to your Lord?" He wanted confirmation and proof from Prophet Moses. When Prophet Moses threw his stick it turned into a dragon. He said; "All of my magicians can make this trick. Well then we will set a date. All the best magicians in this country must come together and compete."

Then one day thousands of magicians were gathered. Prophet Moses was standing alone. They were thousands and each of them wanted to appear fearful physically. It is the magicians' job. They came in strange outfits, in fearful shapes. Whoever saw them was afraid. If you are empty inside, you fear. If you are solid, you don't fear. So the people of Egypt were scared of magicians when they saw them. Moses had the majesty of a prophet. He came alone and stood. Among thousands of magicians the head of them, their leader looked at Prophet Moses. He looked. It came to his heart and he said "If he is like us, it will be impossible for him to stand against us. We know the science of magic. We know each other. So if he is like us he can't stand against us. How will he compete us? We have the same knowledge as he has. It will be unwise for him to stand against us. If he is one of us how can he deal with all of us? If he is standing against thousands of magicians then perhaps he is appointed from the heavens." This came to his heart. His heart told him that he [Musa] is appointed, he isn't afraid of us, his order is connected to the heavens.

So he came closer to Prophet Moses. And he said: "O Moses, shall we start first or you'll start this competition?". He showed good manners. He showed good manners to Moses ‘alayhi s-salām. "Will you start or shall we start?" He was actually going to show his magic with all the crowd with him without asking or caring. Instead he kept good manners to Moses and asked him "Will you throw first?". Because of that act of good manners, their hearts were turned. Allāh Almighty turned the hearts of thousands of magicians because they showed that much respect to the Prophet. They were ready to believe. Moses said "You start".

Then they threw the sticks in their hands, they put the threads in their bellies. They all started to run as dragons. The huge space was full of dragons. Prophet Moses was addressed: "O Moses don't fear or hesitate, leave the stick in your hand. Their job is delusion, false. It isn't real. Throw the real stick, Let reality swallow the false." He threw it. When he did, his dragon followed them and swallowed them all. He swallowed but the stick didn't get fat. That dragon didn't get bigger. Why? Because false has no substance. There is nothing in falsehood. It swallowed them because false is actually nothing. Ḥaqq swept them all. After it swept all the dragons it turned into a yellow dragon. It opened its mouth - his jaw was on the ground and the top jaw was one mile up. It attacked pharaoh's palace, it took his palace in his mouth also. Pharaoh fell down from his throne and ran away. Where? To the toilet. Why did pharaoh say "I'm the Lord"? He used to go to the toilet every 40 days. He said he was the lord because he went to the toilet every 40 days. He started going 40 times a day out of fear. Allāh Almighty trained him. He ran inside. He dirtied himself. Allah disgraced pharaoh.

"O Moses, hold this dragon" he said, "I will believe." Moses (as) held him. But let's come back to the magicians, they immediately prostrated. They prostrated saying, "We believe in the Lord of Moses and Aaron, the Lord of heavens and earths.” When they prostrated the veil on their hearts' eyes lifted. Because of their good manners, they were made ready to believe. When they prostrated they were dressed in real belief. Allāh Almighty dressed them with real belief. They started seeing their stations in heaven. When they prostrated, the veils lifted from their hearts' eyes. They saw, they prostrated directly to Allāh Almighty. In one prostration they reached, they reached to their final station. They saw. They raised their heads from sajdah with shahādah. To whom were they bearing witness? They said "Lā ilāha illā Llāh Muḥammadun Rasūlu Llāh". All right. The prophecy of all prophets is dependent upon our Prophet (saw).

Pharaoh turned into a real pharaoh/tyrant. He told them that they [magicians] had made an agreement with him [Moses] to take pharaoh's wealth from him. "You conspired" he said, so I will cut your hands and arms on either side." He threatened them by saying "I will hang you to tree trunks." His threat didn't work. They told him: "You can reach our bodies, but you can't reach our souls. Our souls found what they were looking for. They took what they needed. They know what they need. So whatever you do to our bodies, do what you can but our souls belong to Allāh's hand of power. We surrendered to Him." In one prostration, at once. So it is a proof to what we said. When you truly prostrate, wholly prostrate, when your heart prostrates with your body, you'll see the unseen world. Your ears will open, your eyes will see, your soul will fly there. Prostration is a big service, it is the biggest worship. There is no servanthood without prostration. Anyone, who claims he is a servant without prostrating, is lying. You can't be a servant without prostration. If you say I'm a servant of Allāh then who will you prostrate to? If you are a servant of Allāh why don't you prostrate? For whom do you keep the prostration? That prostration is a real prostration. That threat of pharaoh didn't work. Pharaoh martyred them [magicians]. He cut and hung them. But they all reached their stations in heavens. They found. They took what they should. Yes.

When you prostrate purely of course you can watch the angels. At the moment of prostration when we sit for taḥiyyāt, we say: "As-salāmu ‘alayka ayyuhā n-nabīyyu wa raḥmatu Llāhi wa barakātuh" - we salute the Prophet (saw). When we read Taḥiyyāt, it is a salām to the Prophet. When we directly address and salute the Prophet (saw) our ears should hear the Prophet saying wa ‘alayk as-salām. Because when we salute the Prophet (saw), the Prophet (saw) isn't deaf. He (saw) isn't dead in the sense we know. He is alive. "Whoever salutes me I hear, they make me hear it. The angels offer it to my presence. This salām came from this servant. I give salāms in return." If you don't hear, you are deaf.

If you truly prostrate your eyes will open. The heart's eye will open, the heart's ear will hear. What makes the heart blind and deaf is the spiritual dirt. Someone's ear might be dirty, it is clogged, he can't hear. He has it washed then after that it is open. The heart's ear is clogged. It doesn't hear. We should hear. We should see. But even if you have eyes you can't see in darkness. So there are things that keep the inside of the heart dark. You have eyes, but without light your eyes have no use. It should either be daytime or light at nighttime so you can see. We have an eye inside the heart but if it is dark inside you can't see, can't see and can't hear. Even if you pour down the seas of the whole world, maybe you can be physically clean, but you can't be purified from the dirt of the heart.

Pureness of the heart is according to his asceticism. Without asceticism [zahid] your heart can't be clean. What is asceticism? Asceticism is the service to push yourself to accept the divine will. Now our ego rejects the divine will. It asserts its own will. By asceticism's power you push your ego to accept Allāh's will. This is what Allāh wants, you must accept it. You take that power from asceticism. The veils, dark veils are lifted from the heart of the one, who has asceticism. Darkness ends. After the veils are lifted, light comes in. When you are veiled, darkness comes. The ego has 70 thousand veils. What will you see behind those veils? Will it show you sunlight? Our assembly here is counted as asceticism. Divine support is sent down here to tear down the veils over your hearts. Whatever we say, how long we sit here, what we say is valid. Divine support is sent down to tear down the veils one by one from the hearts of the people present here. This is asceticism. Why? Coming to the mosque is of course a matter of will. A weak willed man can't go into the mosque. You come to the mosque by will. You can't go to the cafes, bars, theatres, cinemas, night clubs by will. Your ego puts a chain around your neck, pulls you and takes you there. The devil ties you, pulls you there. No need to have will. When you tie the donkey and pull does the donkey come with its will? Does the donkey have will? No. It follows you. The lion has will. You can't tie and pull the lion. You tie and pull the ox, the donkey, the camel. Don't you? So, no need for will. Entering the mosque requires will.

I say it in Turkish to be understood. This is an address to all, to people inside and outside the mosque, to you, to me, to them, me, you, he, she, us, you, them. I shall teach them Turkish grammar. It should be understood. You need will to come here. We are present here to pray, you need will to stay here after praying. Because when praying is finished, you can leave. Now you are pushing your animal; you are forcing your ego's animal with your will. "Sit down. Submit to divine will. Sit and listen" you say. That's why it is asceticism. You sat here half an hour, one hour, and you do asceticism. There are angels, who work on your hearts, in between your weekly visits here. They don't leave that. This is a big matter, good news. From one week to the next, they don't let the veils come down, the veils of darkness to come down a second time. The angels keep an eye on them. The meaning of asceticism is to push your ego to submit to divine will. You force it. This is asceticism.

Why are they against the ṭarīqah? They don't have will. They follow other people. They don't want the ṭarīqah or sharī‘ah. Ok. Without ṭarīqah or sharī‘ah, go wherever you want. Let the ones, who have will, come. Let them enjoy this path. Let them be reckless. Yes. The heart is purified with will, with asceticism. Understand the meaning. Then "an-naẓāfatu mina l-īmān" - Belief brings purity. Your belief makes you do that asceticism, pushes the ego, keeps it clean.

May Allāh keep us with the clean servants, whom He (jj) loves. Biḥurmati l-Ḥabib, biḥurmati l-Fātiḥah.
