We are believers to Allah Almighty. What is the sign of you or what is the proof that you are believers? And they said, "We are thanking to our Lord's favours and we are patient for his... for [with] his heavy burdens that putting on us. And also, we are satisfied or we are putting our wills under His Wills. As He likes, we like. That is, Prophet saying, "That is alright. You are believers, you have proof."

If a person [is] not giving his thanks, shukr, and praisings to his Lord when He is giving to you from His favours, he is not a real believer. And who is not giving his patience when some burden things coming on him, we are saying "ibtila'a, balā'" suffering, sufferings coming on him from poverty, or illness, or death, or any damage. Suffering, if comes on him, not going to be patient - he is not real servant also, or a real believer. [A] believer, for his Lords' favours, thanking and for His Lords' heavy burdens when He is putting on him - carrying, not complaining. He is patient for sufferings. And thirdly, he is putting his desires under His Will, divinely Will. What He likes, he likes. That is the third one, is too important and too difficult. That is the real servant-ship, servanthood: to put his desires under His Lord's Will. If He likes to be something for him, he says 'Yes, I agree with my Lord." If giving, he says, "I am agreeing for His grant." If He taking, he says "I am agree. He gave, and He takes. He gives and He takes. I am agree with my Lord for giving, for taking, for everything that He likes for me. I am agree and I am like, I like Him." That is true love for Allah Almighty.

Therefore, complaining is out of [not in] the dictionary of Prophets, yes? When a person complaining, his belief coming down. If you believing in your Lord Almighty that you are going to be as He likes for you to be - [is there] anything may be better than this? Who can imagine for himself - best form only Allah Almighty - which He likes for you, it is best for you, from everything. Therefore, our Lord's 'taqdīr', we are saying 'Taqdīr', [destiny] the divinely writings for everyone. Just He wrote for everyone in His divinely book. As He wrote, it is best for you. That writings going to appear in any moment, any second. When it is going to appear, don't be angry, don't fight. When you are angry, complaining. When you are fighting, you are complaining.

Something may be in our hands, outward looking. Something, without our wills going on. If you are seeing something going on without reaching our willpowers, to that point - leave it to go on. Perhaps, you are feeling troubles and sufferings from that being, but leave it, go on. That is best for you, He knows.

Once a grandshaykh was, he was ordered one murīd for seclusion and saying to him that if you are looking, you are seeing anything during seclusion, inform me. One day that murīd looking, Allah Almighty opening his heart's eyes and looking on table, preserved tablet, preserved tablet tablet, preserved tablet. Seeing, Allah Almighty showing him that his shaykh going to be from hell people. He was very worried, crying "How my shaykh going to hell? And he giving us light to find Allah Almighty to be His servants? Let him go to Paradise and make me on his place to be in hell." Making sajda and praying. Then divinely addressing coming to him, "Look now." He looked and saw that, that writing on his shaykh changed and just written that he is from the people of Paradise. And he coming to his shaykh and saying "O my Grandshaykh, al-hamdulillah, I am thanking to my Lord that this night just I saw your name among those whom they are going to hell. Then I made du'a strongly asking from my Lord. Then He changed your name and putting among people they're going in Paradise."

This is a tale, a real tale, to make us to understand how we may be in divine presence, how we must be - our rabita - connection with our Lord. We are servants, He is Lord. How it should be, our connection. Then his shaykh saying - going to be too angry - saying, "Look, O my son, you saw it yesterday. I am looking, my Lord showing to me since 37 years. I am looking and seeing but I never making such a du'a for changing my name because He is my Lord, and I am His servant, very humble servant. He may do everything or He may judge for me as He likes. And I am agree with my Lord. If putting me in hell, I agree with Him. If putting me in Paradise"- everyone who is put in Paradise agrees with his Lord but no one going to be agree if He putting him in hell. He is asking to change from hells to paradise but that grandshaykh, strong one, real servant who serving to his Lord for His sake, divinely sake, not for anything. He asking only his Lord. No asking anything else. Therefore if ordering him to Paradise or ordering to hells - equal.

My grandshaykh, Allah blessing him, was saying to me a tale from Aba Yazid, Allah blessing him, that Allah Almighty just ordered to angels to prepare two paradise maidens, girls, maidens to make them like bright, making everything zeenat [beauties] Zeenat nadir? Ornament. And ordering when Aba Yazid coming to his tomb let them enter, entering on him. And when Sultanu l-'Arifīn, Aba Yazīd died - and his death not like our death, going from here up to there - putting him in his tomb and covering his tomb. Then Allah Almighty ordering two maidens from Paradise entering, one from one side, one from the other. If anyone looks from people to them, no more, no more life for him finish. Going to die everyone if looking them. Allah Almighty giving from His endless beauty oceans. Allah Almighty just gave to Eve, Eve, Eve, grandmother of sons of Adam, beauty as much as her girls up to end of this world has been given from beauty. Take all of them, she had been given. Our mother, therefore we are all beautiful people, nice, beautiful ladies and handsome sons. Yes. She was, her beauty more than maidens of Paradise more beautiful. Then, two of them coming.

But Aba Yazīd, his eyes like fires going through towards Throne of his Lord Almighty. These eyes and these eyes, heart's eyes and head eyes, going to be together, looking. And he shouting. They are coming to be with him up to Day of Resurrection but he was shouting, "O My Lord, I was serving to You. I was Your servant only, I never asked from You such a things. I never prayed or worshipped for Paradise or anything in it. But I was asking [for] the Creator of Paradise. Without You, paradise is nothing. I am asking [for] You, from the Day of Promises up to end. I never asked anything except You, O My Lord." He says “Don't leave me to be against, with such a things. They are only little spots, with them, with two maidens - very little spots from beauty oceans. Endless beauty oceans with You! Let me getting. Very spot beauty, with this, with that. I am asking [for] You Allah Almighty."

Looking and Allah Almighty ordering to two maidens, "Return, you may return to his paradise. And I am giving you, if he is going to look you, more than this I am giving you now - familiarity." And they returning. And his eyes, yet in his tomb looking like fire to, towards his Lord's Throne. Looking to His endless beauty oceans. And any saint or wali, awliya, has been ordered a heavy order and he cannot be able to do, going to the tomb of Aba Yazīd and looking to his eyes. This power coming to his eyes, they are taking and going away. Like a station, spiritual power station. Divinely power stations, all awliyas but he is famous one. They are coming to his tomb, his tomb in Damascus, Damascus. Every time his tomb engaged by awliyas. You may see or you can't see them. His tomb at the, near to Damascus. Just I went there several times. And now, I am feeling that from his heart coming to my heart a wave from his divinely powers so that may go through your hearts. He is a grand wali, king size wali, Aba Yazīd, who asking His Lord's pleasure only, nothing else.

And that Grandshaykh who was angry with his murīd because he changed his name; worshipping to Him, because He is my Lord. He is the Lord of creatures, the Lord of Universes. And, we must be His servants because He is Creator and we are His slaves. He brought us from nothing into existence and we must worship to Him. He may judge for us anything that He likes. As He likes, I like. That is the top point to be His real servant. Everything may be considered but you must be pleased with your Lord. As Moses asking; "How you are going to be pleased with me O my Lord?" And He answering and saying, "When you are going to be pleased with Me, I am pleased with you." You understanding? "First, you must be pleased with Me, then I am pleased with you. But difficult for you to be pleased with Me" He says.

Every worshipping going to teach us how we should be pleased with our Lord. The main goal from worshipping, to make us to be pleased with our Lord. And, more than this, important - important thing, more than worshipping, this worshipping that we are doing is very simple but there is another worshipping. That is important worshipping - to be with your Lord, as He willing for you. Or to be pleased with Him as He willing something for you. That is important worshipping, more than our prayings, more than our fastings. Perhaps a person may pray, may fast but not every time going to be pleased with his Lord. When we are going to complain from anything, then it means we are not pleased with our Lord. Everything may be for us, or for others. You never going to be above His Will. His Will going on, on you and on others.

And the highest point of worshipping to leave to say 'Why this?' or 'How this? or 'How that?' 'Why that happened?' That is not worshipping. Real worshipper or real servant, never saying 'Why this happened?' You don't know that, without His Will, nothing going to happen? Do you not know that nothing happens without His knowledge, without He knowing, or without He willing? Do you not know? When anything happening, going to happen, He knowing and willing. When you are saying 'Why that happened?' your will coming against His Will. That makes our face down. How His Will running, run with it. That is more important than our prayings and our fastings, than our hijāj. Yes. That is true.

Then, that Grandshaykh was very angry with his murīd. Saying - "Why you are going through my Lord and His servant? You haven't this, this is not for you or for me. We are servants and He is our Lord. He judging for us, anything as He likes and we are under His order." That is real worshipping, real servanthood. Now we must bring our attributes from Prophet (saws) because Prophet's attributes just same as his Lord's attributes. And we are trying to listen something from that attributes. This is one, an important attribute - to be agree with our Lord's Will. Agreement - we must side with our Lord. "We are agree with You, O our Lord, for everything."

Not fighting, leave fighting, do agreement. Then you are happy, here and hereafter. Perhaps, at the beginning may be bitter for you but finally coming to be sweet-full, sweet. Yes.
