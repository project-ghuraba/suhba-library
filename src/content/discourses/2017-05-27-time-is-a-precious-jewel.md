A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad.

May your Ramaḍān be blessed. May it be good, in shā’a Llāh. May it be blessed, in shā’a Llāh. We reached the beautiful month again by the kindness of AllāhNone. In shā'a Llāh we will reach more Ramaḍāns. Countable days are passing. Until this time, up until today we saw more than 60 Ramaḍāns shukr to AllāhNone. With the virtuous kindness of AllāhNone. It is a virtue. We spent these virtuous months together with Mawlānā and other people. Those were very good days. How do these beautiful days become beautiful? If you spend them by obeying AllāhNone, they'll be good. You will have valued them. You won't be in loss.

"Wa-in tuṭiʿ akthara man fī l-arḍi yuḍillūka ʿan sabīli Llāh." (6:116) says Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla. If you follow people, they will take you from the right way to depravity. They will mislead you from your way. They think they came to this world for eating, drinking and playing. They think the more they do so, the more benefit they will get but they are mistaken. They are out of the right way and are depraved, says Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla. You should follow the way shown by AllāhNone so that your time is not wasted. Time is the most precious thing that AllāhNone gave us. It is a jewel. Don't waste it away. If you waste it, you will regret it. It won't come back.

We had more than 60 Ramaḍāns. Some had 30, some had 40, some may have 80. It means this month will have passed in the blink of an eye. Then you look, and Ramaḍān has come again. Time will have passed again. Did you spend your time in the right way? Did you make that time live or die? You should know.

People talk. They may do empty talk but they talk. Who talks without knowing the meaning talks emptiness and uselessness. He talks with harm. They say "We are killing time. We are bored." We don't have enough time to be bored. Our time is precious. Value it and don't kill. Spend it with the worship and orders of AllāhNone. As we said, when you fast in Ramaḍān, you are knowing its value. You won't have wasted it. Because you will be held to account for it. "You are given this much time. You are given these many chances. And you didn't do it. Come here." Time is the creature of AllāhNone. Instead of one, you will spend a million years there. Know its value, know the virtue of this precious time. Such events happen to give an example. People are not even aware. Ten years ago the world was much easier. Before that, it was even easier. Now it's worse. It's becoming worse day by day. People think they rule over this world. AllāhNone is ruling over this world. HeNone is making it do whatever HeNone wants. Therefore, don't trust in this world too much. Try to value your time wherever you are. Value your every moment. May AllāhNone help us understand the value of these holy moments, in shā’a Llāh.

Ramaḍān mubārak for all of you, in shā’a Llāh. It is nice month. AllāhNone make it barakah for us and happiness and to know the value of this month because it is time quickly passing. Now I'm calculate for myself more than 60 Ramaḍāns. I was, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, from childhood sometimes without knowing but since maybe 6th year we remember. Sometime with grandfather, sometime with father, sometime without, we are we have been making Ramaḍān. And al-ḥamdu liLlāh it is, it was whole time nice and blessed time.

Time it is very important. People they are not knowing value of time. How they are not knowing? They thought they are only they created to eat and drink and playing. No, it is not only for this. So there are when they getting old they becoming miserable, they become more worried. And they are hopeless. But who are believer they are every time, they are more happy, they are more believer and they are more knowing value of time. Time it is very precious jewel AllāhNone gift us, HeNone give us as gift. We are not knowing the value it is the most valuable thing in our life. Every second Allāh HeNone give us it is favour, very precious thing. Because Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla for each person he give him life maybe it is very short life, maybe long life but it is the time it is for all of them it must be very valuable thing. Not to as a ignorant people they are saying ooh I'm very bored, today boring. Bored I bored today too much. What I do? It is no time to bore. Not time to be bored. It is very short our life. Every minute, every second you must be happy, must be thankful for AllāhNone.

Many thing you can do for yourself for to be benefit for you. When you say this it is also you are not happy with Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla. Allāh HeNone give this very valuable thing and many ignorant I'm killing time they said. Don't kill time. No we must make your time to be alive. Make it live not to kill it. Kill not good at all. Anything you don't kill, especially for what anybody give you. Jewel you throw it? No you keep it. This is more important. More valuable than jewel, more important than gold. This is our time. One second what Allāh HeNone give to you can take breath inside and take it out it is, you not know how it is precious. But people looking for each other and they are thinking this is what life? No. Life, what AllāhNone HeNone is telling us what to do, how it is precious. But Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla said "Wa-in tuṭiʿ akthara man fī l-arḍi yuḍillūka ʿan sabīli Llāh." (6:116) If you follow people the majority people they are making you to go wrong way, not in right way. Only don't look for what people doing, only look for what AllāhNone ordering because very few people they are understanding and they are precious that time what AllāhNone give us and following and they will be winner.

Now we are whole time going. You lose your time but this also you will be asked in Day of Resurrection. You will be for each minute maybe you will be punished thousand year. So it is important and we are in Ramaḍān al-ḥamdu liLlāh. Who are fasting they are giving value of this time. So al-ḥamdu liLlāh this Ramaḍān remember us [remind us] value of time and value of worshipping. Our life when you are worshipping you are following order of AllāhNone it is full, not empty. But if you kill your time it is empty and you will be asked for what you done for this, what you done for that time. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh AllāhNone make us in this way and not follow majority. Minority they are more important.

Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq al-Fātiḥah.
