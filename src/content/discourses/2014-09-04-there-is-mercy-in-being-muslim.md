Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Allah (JJ) shows mercy to those who also show mercy on Earth. Islam is the religion of mercy. There is no mercy in people who are outside of Islam. People who say they are Muslim and do not have mercy are also like them. Their mercy is only for show. Essentially they have no mercy. People would show mercy to everything in the past. They would say about people of old times: “He does not even crush an ant!”

First of all, non-Muslims do not consider Muslims to be human. When something befalls Muslims they say, “Ah!” and want it to get worse. A Muslim always shows mercy to his brother Muslim. When a disaster, war, or the like strikes, he feels sorry because of his mercy and immediately runs for his help or sends help. And this is how it is supposed to be. People who are outside of Islam keep distance between themselves because they are far from the commands of Islam. This is why these things befall them. These things being done are things that should not be done with being Muslim. There is mercy in being Muslim.

A Muslim is the brother of a Muslim. Let alone Muslims, non-Muslims also have rights. They cannot be treated that way either. They are also to be treated with mercy. You can defend yourself against a person who does you evil. Everything has a measure, a limit. Islam shows where it should stop. Islam is the religion of mercy. Those who deserve it get their punishment. That punishment is not given to those who do not deserve it. These things are not according to a person’s mind. Everything has its methods and manners. As we said, Islam is the religion of mercy.

The events of recent times are events that are caused by those amongst Muslims who want to lay the blame on Muslims, but that have nothing to do with being Muslim or with Islam. We all find it strange and do not approve of it. We are innocent of it. We need to at least say it with the tongue. We need to warn people. As we always say, the one who has no mercy cannot be a Muslim. It says in the hadith sharif: “You become from those you emulate.” May Allah fix people who pass themselves off as Muslim. It is an important thing to show mercy to Muslims. Do not deviate from it Inshallah.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq. Al-Fatiha.
