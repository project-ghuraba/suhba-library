As-Salāmu ‘Alaykum wa RaḥmatuLlāhi wa Barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu BiLlāhi Minash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. BismiLlāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm.

Wa ṣ-Salātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Akhirīn.

Madad yā RasūlAllāh, Madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi RasūliLlāh, Madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

Dastūr Mawlana Sheikh Abdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistāni, Sheikh Muḥammad

Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad.Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-Suḥbah wa l-Khayru fi l-Jam‘iyyah.

“Allahumma Barik lana fi Rajab wa Sha’ban wa ballighna Ramadan”, “O Allah, bless us in Rajab and Sha’ban, and let us reach Ramadan.” Amin. May it be blessed upon you. The Three holy months have started today, Shukr to Allah. Allah ‘Azza wa-Jalla says, “The month of Rajab is Mine. Sha’ban is of our Holy Prophet. And Ramadan is the month of Ummah.”

These are the holy months. Shukr to Allah, we have reached them safely with Iman. Thousands of Shukr to Allah for letting us have this nice condition. What He wants happens, and what He doesn’t want doesn’t happen. We could have been like the people last night. Shukr to Allah, we must make Shukr for that. May Allah forgive them. May Allah forgive us all, because we should repent and ask for forgiveness wherever there is a sin. Even if others commit it, we all need forgiveness. We are not happy with the sins committed by us or by others. May Allah forgive us. May Allah show mercy to all of us. May He grant guidance. Guidance is the biggest favor. Being straight on the way of Allah is the biggest favor. And may Allah grant guidance to those who are not on the way.

We must be sorry for them, because Allah didn’t mean it for them. We could have been like them. But with the generosity of Allah, insha’Allah we’ll be steadfast and be on His way until we leave this world. We won’t obey shaytan. We won’t be cheated by shaytan’s tricks which show the bad as good. And we won’t cheat insha’Allah. We make such a Du’a on the 1st of Rajab, on the first day of the three holy months. These are the important things.

The things others do are for them. If they were guided, if Allah meant it for them, they wouldn’t do that. But we can’t interfere in the will of Allah. We can’t interfere in the wisdom of Allah. Allah knows what He does. Ḥāshā (far from it)! We can’t interfere in His business. Sometimes people say, “What would I do if I were..? I would guide all people.” If you wish so, Allah ‘Azza wa-Jalla has wished otherwise. We can’t question the wisdom of Allah ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Allah doesn’t oppress anyone. He doesn’t mistreat anyone.

It is the month of Allah ‘Azza wa-Jalla. We must believe in Allah ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Don’t say this and that about His business. Be on the way of Allah, be the way Allah has ordered. Always make Shukr to Allah. Make Shukr and ask to make us steadfast on this way. May He not let us lose our footing. May Allahprotect us insha’Allah.

May this month be blessed. May it be good for the Ummah insha’Allah. We wait for the Sahib. The condition of the world is bad. The Sahib is the one who will improve it. It is Mahdi ‘alayhi s-salam. Insha’Allah we ask from Allah that he comes. May Allah send him. The Ummah is without Sahib. May Allah bless these days. May they be full of Barakah. May they be good and be the means for goodness insha’Allah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
