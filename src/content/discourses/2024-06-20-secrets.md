As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Inna Llāha ‘ālimu ghaybi (35:38) – wa-sh-shahādah” (13:9). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘aẓīm. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla knows everything: that which is hidden, that which is present and what we see. Things not seen to us, things that are hidden are in the knowledge of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. What we know is nothing. In the presence of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, next to HisNone knowledge, it is nothing from nothing. It is nothing at all.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla knows everything Himself. HeNone created it in the best way. Some people say, “I studied this. I studied that. I know some secrets. No one knows these secrets. I know them.” Many people make this claim. Real scholars, real saints do not do this. But AllāhNone reveals a little so that people benefit from their knowledge. Even that is nothing. That is also like nothing next to the knowledge of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla created everything with HisNone wisdom. All secrets are with HimNone. Some of those secrets will be revealed to people on Judgement Day. As we said, some of them from the ocean of HisNone knowledge... We cannot say ocean. The knowledge of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is unlimited. It is wrong to say it has a limit. HisNone knowledge is something without the end.

There are secrets that we don’t understand. They will be revealed to people by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla on Judgement Day. There are some secrets... There are also secrets that people hide. They will also be revealed on Judgement Day: “Lā takhfá minkum khāfiyah” (69:18). You cannot hide anything. All secrets will be evident. Everything will show up then: what you did, whom you harmed, whom you helped. These days, no one understands what is what. Everything is mixed up. Shayṭān is playing with people as he likes. And they go around happily, thinking no one knows their secrets. All of those secrets will be evident on Judgement Day. And everyone will pay accordingly. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla has other, good secrets. People will understand them also in that time. Certainly, as we said, those secrets are not even an atom of the knowledge of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.

Therefore, some people get very excited when you tell them a secret, wondering what and how it is. And many people run after those people who claim to know secrets. Then, they are often lost. We are not ordered to go after those secrets. People who use those secrets, what they call a secret is showing the way of our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam differently. “It is not like this. The secret is like this. You must go together with the possessor of this secret.” There were many groups who mislead people by saying so.

Therefore, that is not our business. If there are secrets, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla will bring them up on Judgement Day, and we will see them. Otherwise, we must keep on the way ordered by our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. We must keep to the prayer, the fast, ḥajj, zakāh, doing goodness, avoiding badness, and avoiding all kinds of sins. We must follow all of these the way they were ordered. But there are many people who say, “The secret is like this. You must not do so.” People who listen to them will perish along with that secret. Those secrets will be shown on Judgement Day. And they will see that the things they did brought no benefit then.

May AllāhNone protect us. Mankind is curious to learn hidden things, to know what to do. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla has given this. This is in the nature of people. And we must be careful to make it right. No need to destroy yourself for useless things. May AllāhNone protect us. May AllāhNone protect us from the evil of evil people.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Inna Llāha ‘ālimu ghaybi (35:38) – wa-sh-shahādah” (13:9). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘aẓīm. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone know the hiding thing and shahādah which it’s appear, appearance and exist. AllāhNone know everything. “Wa-Llāhu bi-kulli shay’in ‘alīm” (4:176). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘aẓīm. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone know everything. What it is secret, what it is apparent. Nothing will hide from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.

What we know, AllāhNone give knowledge for human being through prophets, through HisNone beloved oneNone. Many thing, but with this many thing, you cannot say, compare, you cannot say anything. Sometimes we speak, AllāhNone forgive us. We speak without knowing, just because we used to say something, compare to knowledge of AllāhNone. How we can compare ourselves with knowledge of AllāhNone? No compare. But whole human knowledge it is not one, not drop, one atom from AllāhNone what HeNone gave from HisNone knowledge for us, for whole universe. Nothing.

But also, people they are still like to know what, what Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla want, what HeNone is, what HisNone knowledge, and they are trying to look after secret from what Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gave from knowledge for human being. All this, don’t be too much tire yourself. There is many secret in life. Personal secret, for government secret, for human being secret, for many thing secret. And all this secret, for part of humanity, for our world, for universe, it will appear in Judgment Day, Yawmu l-Qiyāmah. One part, special for human being – what they done in their life, and they was hiding their secret from other people – it will appear that time. If they do good or bad, it will be appear. If they do good, AllāhNone appear and the people, they will be happy. If they do bad and they hide this secret, also it will be appear also. But what if somebody do bad thing, and he not appear for [show it to] people in his life, and they ask for forgiveness from AllāhNone? Only this, can be secret. AllāhNone, because, HeNone said this: “My servant he didn’t appear [show] this, what he done wrong, and he regret and ask for forgiveness. INone will be, keep him not to be appear and [so that he won’t have] to be ashamed between these people.” But if other, they not ashamed, as we see these days – they do very bad thing and they are happy, showing people, “I am doing this, I doing that.” So this, they will be punished. And this secret it will be, they will regret and will be ashamed in that Divine Presence, in Yawmu l-Qiyāmah.

This, for secret of people, all will appear. Everybody they will see. What also, nowadays we don’t know what is right, which one helping, which one enemy, which friend, who is not in, like whole world. You don’t know who is friend, who is not friend, because many of them they appear as a friend, but really they are enemy. That time also, all this also will appear. All, every people, everyone they will know who killed, who not killed. Now we see many thing, they said, make a movie, this man killed by that, but by this, but you don’t know all exactly. But at that time, Day of Judgement, all this, it will appear. Nothing will be hiding. Other thing, for what people claiming they are doing good and they are following, but reality, they are not. For this we are only for... also for secret of some thing from earth, from sky, from heaven, from all this, it will appear.

But also, there is secret which Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, most of, whole of secret, AllāhNone, HeNone know. We cannot looking what HeNone want. We are happy with what HeNone gave us and everything. Everybody he will be know what they was doing – which one wrong, which one right. All this it will appear, in shā’a Llāh.

And we are, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, following Prophet’s ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam order. What Prophet’sNone order? Order to pray, to fast, to give zakāh, to go ḥajj, and to do good thing. This is order: to not do bad thing, not drink, not steal, not kill, not many thing, also. All this we are following. We must follow this. But there is people, kind of people, because human being they like to look after secret thing. So many people running after people who they are saying, “We have special secret. We have this, that. So if you come, we will show you this secret and you will be, no need for you to follow what majority of people, the idiot people, they are following. We are more clever. We have this secret. And no need for you to pray, no need to pay. No need to ḥajj, nothing. All this no need, because secret only with us. With this secret, you can do anything and you will be happy.”

Many people, since Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, after Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, this coming. Every time you find this thing. This, they call themselves also: “hiding people”. This is what they call themselves, because they don’t, they have special name, special thing to say: “We have secret. We have secret, we have secret. This is secret. You will learn after sometime, after this, after that. You will see our secret, and after this you will be whole life with us, and you will be special one.” This people, they don’t have this secret. They are doing something, no benefit for them. And this is not, we are not ordered for this. This not our order from ProphetNone, from awliyā’u Llāh, from ṣaḥābah, from Ahlu l-Bayt. No one saying secret. No one say, “Secret, for this.” Only secret, for this people who are making friends with shayṭān. AllāhNone keep us safe from them, because they are taking many millions of people to bad end. AllāhNone keep us from their bad end, in shā’a Llāh.

And it is, whole time until Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām, it will be like this. So be careful not follow who are saying, “I have secret. Don’t do this. Don’t follow ṣaḥābah, don’t follow this.” Don’t be with this people. Be careful. This is very important. It is most important thing for you, because if your feet slip, you will be going from high to crush yourself, to finish yourself. AllāhNone keep us from them.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
