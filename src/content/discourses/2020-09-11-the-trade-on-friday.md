Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Today is Friday; it is a holy day. It is our holiday insha’Allah. In the time of our Holy Prophet, there was no holiday like now; Friday is a holiday, Saturday is a holiday, Sunday is a holiday. There was no such thing as a holiday. Allah made all the day on Friday beautiful and blessed, but there is a time on Friday when trade is forbidden.

In honor of the day of Friday, there is a verse that says that buying and selling is a sin at the time of prayer. It is forbidden, do not do it. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm: ‘ اَي مْ ُتنكُ نإِ مْ كُ َّل رٌ يْ خَ مْ كُ لَِذ ٰ ۚ عَ يْ بَ لْا اورُ َذوَ ِاللََّّ رِ كْ ذِ ىٰ لَِإ اوْ َعسْ اَف ةِ َعمُ جُ لْا مِوْ َي نمِ ةِ لََ صَّ للِ يَ دِ وُن اَذِإ اوُنمَ آ نَ يذِ َّلا اهَ ُّيَأ نَ ومُ َلعْ َت’, ‘O you who have believed, when [the adhan] is called for the prayer on the day of Jumah [Friday], then proceed to the remembrance of Allah and leave trade. That is better for you, if you only knew.’ (Qur’ān 62:09). “When the adhan is called on Friday, go to Friday Prayer and stop trade. This is better for you”, says Allah Azza wa Jalla.

Just because he will earn two cents, he rejects all the blessings and becomes a rebel against Allah. That’s why, you can work all day on Friday, but you have to be patient for ten minutes or fifteen minutes regarding trade. The person who owns the shop should close it and write [a sign] that says “I am going to Friday Prayer”. There are many of such people. But those who do not know and say: “What is the use of this”, will be deprived of that thing and its blessings will go away.

Therefore, Friday has many virtues. There are many things to do on Friday. And this is one of them such that you should not be trading during the time of prayer. Friday Prayer is not Fard (obligatory) for women, but this trade order is also valid for them. It is for all Muslims, not only for men but also for women. Women should not go shopping during Friday Prayer. They should wait and go after that.

There is a blessing on Friday. There is the hour of response. There is the hour of response on Friday when Allah accepts the prayers. It is not clear when in 24 hours. Therefore, it is necessary to constantly make Dua on Friday, especially those who have a need; we all have a need anyways. May it coincide with the hour of response. May Allah give strength to our faith and blessings to our days. May Allah protect us, and guide Muslims insha’Allah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
