As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa raḥmatu Llāhi wa barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīn wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

When taking ablution, we should say Bismi Llāh. You make intention and say Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. When a person forgets it – for example he makes intention, doesn't say Bismi Llāh and takes ablution. Then he remembers while washing his feet that he didn't say Bismi Llāh. Our ProphetNone says, "Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm awwalahu wa-ākhirahu." No need to take ablution all over again. "Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm awwalahu wa-ākhirahu." "Bismi Llāh from the beginning till the end." Why do we say it? Because our life passes in heedlessness. We are in heedlessness most of the time. Mankind is heedless. They forget that they come to this world for AllāhNone. They forget that dunyā is the place of tests and it is necessary to always remember AllāhNone. They commit sins and make mistakes. They are in heedlessness. They do things which are not good. People are 30 years old, 40 years old, even 70 or 80 years old, and they live all their lives with mistakes. That's like in ablution – if you repent, all of it can be forgiven. AllāhNone writes thawāb (reward) instead of it.

When repentance is pure, Allāh'sNone kindness is great. Don't fall into hopelessness. Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla waits for HisNone servant to repent so that HeNone accepts it and turns sins into thawāb. Therefore, like Bismi Llāhi awwalahu wa-ākhirahu, even if we make sins throughout our lives and then repent, the doors of repentance are open. It will be accepted. And then: "Yubaddilu Llāhu sayyi’ātihim ḥasanāt" (25:70). Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla will turn your sins into thawāb. Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla gives such good tidings. And mankind is lazy to do even that. They don't accept it. Most people pass away without repenting. May AllāhNone protect us. The door of repentance is open until the sun rises from the West. Therefore, don't lose hope of Allāh'sNone mercy. May we always make tawbah. We are making sins every day anyway. And AllāhNone is changing them into thawāb.

We should make shukr to AllāhNone. We should praise AllāhNone. We should always make sajdah and thank AllāhNone for these favours. Because most people are heedless. They are not aware of so many favours. They don't think a bit about it. And they don't accept that they make mistakes. They think they are right. That is the biggest heedlessness. We are making mistakes and sinning continuously. May AllāhNone forgive us, in shā’a Llāh. Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa sallam saying when we make wuḍū’, we must make intention – "nawaytu l-wuḍū’" or "we intend to make ablution" and saying Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm first. But people sometimes forget to say Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. In our, maybe, I don't know other but Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa sallam saying also for this Ḥanafī Madhhab, if you forget to say Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm, maybe you wash your hand, mouth, face, hands, you still forget until you are coming to end of maybe feet. You wash your feet, you remember but said, you say, "Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm awwalahu wa-ākhirahu." This Prophet'sNone ḥadīth also meaning "Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm from beginning to end." So it is acceptable, no need to make ablution from beginning. You finish. Before finish, you say, "Bismi Llāh awwalahu wa-ākhirahu." Your ablution it is okay, no problem.

Why we saying this? For because this one example for how our life it is easy when you are following AllāhNone order. Because people, whole life maybe they forget to do good thing. Maybe they are not obeying order of AllāhNone. Maybe twenty years they are like this. Maybe thirty years, maybe forty years, maybe sixty years, or more they forget. They not doing anything good. But when they asking forgiveness from AllāhNone, all this years it will be, AllāhNone forgive what you done – like "Bismi Llāh awwalahu wa-ākhirahu" from as you are not doing anything. Even Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa sallam saying, "Yubaddilu Llāhu sayyi’ātihim ḥasanāt" (25:70) – He make, changing bad deeds to good deeds to reward you.

This is Allāh'sNone favour for people. HeNone just want people to be ask for forgiveness, to forgive them. HeNone want something to forgive them. Even whole life doing bad thing but the end they ask for forgiveness, HeNone will forgive and changing the sins to reward. This is from HisNone generous. We are, how this human being lucky. But even for this people they are not, they in ignorance not accepting, not knowing this, that not asking for this. Even only they are running after their desire and running after their ego not – the most of people they are going without asking forgiveness from Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla. And Allāh HeNone is asking for people, waiting for people to ask forgiveness, to forgive them. HeNone is the most Merciful, Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla and waiting for people to forgive them even what they do in their life bad thing to changing to good thing.

We must be thankful for HimNone because we are not saying we are angel. We are also every day doing wrong thing, doing sins, sin and we are asking for forgiveness from AllāhNone. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh we know HeNone is changing this to reward, good deed for us and we are thanking HimNone. And as much we can thank HimNone it is not enough for HimNone but we are only small things for... We cannot say compare. No comparison, astaghfiru Llāh. Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla, nothing compare to HimNone. HeNone is more Generous and only you asking. We are less than ants. We are only we thanking for what HeNone give us, thank for AllāhNone.

Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
