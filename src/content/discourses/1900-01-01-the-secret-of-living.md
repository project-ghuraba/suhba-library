Audhu Billahi min as shaytani r-rajim Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim La hawla wa la quwwata illa Billahi-l Alliyi-l Azim

Should the mind’s capacity measure a mere atom a person would understand that this world has a Keeper. It would be another matter if that mind was less than an atom. A person without a mind cannot understand. However, somebody who assumes to be clever, should know that there is a Keeper of this world. You walk around Famagusta, stay somewhere, then say ‘This place belongs to me’. How many people go there? Somebody may come and ask, ‘How do you own this place? Where are the deeds?’ Somebody may claim ‘This place is mine’, whether it is a mountain, a stone, a tree a house or a shop, everyone has an owner.

What an individual doesn’t own, is owned by the government. There is nothing, nowhere that doesn’t belong to somebody. Think! Who is the registered owner of this planet’s title deeds? Whose world is this? Of course, we ask. The unbelievers question everything. We ask also: to whom does this world belong? Many claimed, ‘This world is mine’, but every claimant got tossed down a pit. The pit dug for him wasn’t huge. Its size could accommodate any one of us. He was settled in. If it were huge, it would still need filling, leveling up. Whoever says that we own this world all together, their lies have been exposed. They cannot ever own it.

How could they own the world? Can they possess themselves? Show me someone who can, we can’t even own ourselves! If that was possible we would be in command. We would choose never to get old because old age is not desirable. Everybody wants to stay fresh and young. Nobody would want to age if it were possible to stay fresh and young. So why are you getting older? Keep your youth, stay young. Why can’t you stay young? Stay young. No, you can’t keep yourself young. Yahu! Finally, you decay. Don’t die. There it is, defy death, remain without dying! Really, we cannot even have ownership of ourselves. So how can we own this world?

If you own this world, put it in your pocket and go, you may put it in your bag and go, no one can have it. Finish. Take your capital and go. Now, what do we have? We must find somewhere else. Isn’t it so? Such a work! Who are they, this world’s owners, if not English, Greek, Russian or American? And if so, let them take it and go. All countries say ‘ours’ but they can’t cut it in pieces and put it in their pockets. No one can do that and go. Therefore, Mankind has an Owner Who controls with His Will. Look, we put this instrument here. It has an owner. This instrument doesn’t function by itself, you must push its button then it works. Do you function by yourself? Do you rush around by yourself? Do you eat and drink by yourself? No! Therefore it has an Operator who pushes the button to operate it, pushes the button to stop. Whoever our Operator is, He must be outside. We can say, ‘Oh, his battery is finished and he died’. All of our batteries will finish. Can he say his battery won’t finish? Let him show it, we can check him. ‘Oh, change this ones battery’. ‘Our batteries don’t change’. Batteries don’t change, they exhaust more and more, then we go to zero.

What mindless people we are. We don’t think yahu! We have an Owner. Where is our Owner? On the ground? In the skies? In the east? In the west? Inside us? Outside us? His, Subhanahu wa Ta’ala’s reach extends through His Domains because He (swt) is the Owner of the Domains. The Creator is where His Creation is. He (swt) is with His Creation. If He (swt) was not with us continually, would we be here? Your existence is from Him (swt), no doubt you live through Him (swt). Without His existence man cannot exist. Without His existence there can be no world, without Him (swt), the universe cannot be. Who is there without Him (swt)? Who can exist? Existence is through Him. How can you say He (swt) is absent?

Drunks are of two types: the first, drinks to get drunk and becomes oblivious because of it. He is someone who loses his mind when he drinks. He becomes mindless. The other one is drunk with heedlessness. Being drunk, he is unmindful. Drunken in unmindfulness. Why did He (swt) give you a mind? For thinking/reflecting. If crippled in his ability to think, can there be a difference between a man and a four-footed animal? Four-footed ones are unable to think. Have you seen any that can? No. An unthinking man resembles one who has lost his sense of reality.

Yes, everything has a beginning and an end. This worldly life of ours has a beginning and an end. How will our end be? Can anyone control his own fate? ‘I choose my end to be like this’, can anyone say that? Is there any human who can choose his own death and say ‘I want my end to be like this’? Human beings are weak. They can do nothing. They can do everything! Now, people have a mastery over many things but concerning their destiny, they have no control. Gigantic ships, massive instruments, huge factories are brainy inventions but they are powerless in choosing what their end will be. Impossible. No choice. They can’t even know how their end will be? They haven’t any option regarding the end of their life. Neither have they an option nor can they alter what is decreed for them.

None can choose the end they want, which means that human beings are created weak, yet think themselves to be strong. ‘There are umpteen thousand canons and rifles, countless thousands sabres and bombs, zillions of aircraft’. In himself Man feels mighty and powerful. If you are so powerful and, with the command you have, can you design a death of your choosing? You cannot! Bygone tyrants would say, should the celestial spheres collapse we have the power to prop them up with our soldiers’ swords. Proud and pompous. What did Janab Allah do? Reduced them to rubble. A celestial gyrating drum, running over, crushed everything. Now and again on the highway a car hits perhaps a cat or rabbit. Soon follow trucks and other cars plastering them flat on the asphalt. Janabul Haqq flattens such tyrants and despots, these mindless paraders, with that Divine gyrating, drum-roller. Will He or not? Flat! Like cigarette paper, even thinner than that. Yes, man is misled by his pride. He has no right to show off. We haven’t any right to flaunt ourselves. If Janabul Haqq chooses He (swt) may create us with our heads soaring skyward. Just as with the ancient tribes, the people of Hud (as) and those of Ad. The shortest among them was seven times taller than a minaret! Tall & majestic. If Allah (swt) chose to make us like them, He could.

We even measure our height in centimetres, not even in meters. We are against our Creator! If He (swt) were to give us such height, what would we do? We are only centimetres in height and we can’t even lift 1 lb. of weight for 5 minutes because our arm gets tired. And, Janabul Haqq imprisons us between two breaths. If we don’t inhale for a minute we start feeling ill, if we don’t exhale, we suffocate. Should our lungs not fill up with fresh air we would die. If the stale air remained inside, it would poison and suffocate us! Mere mortals, centimetres high and, contending with the Creator of the Universe! Man is insolent and arrogant!

Brandishing a sword, he thinks himself mighty, there aren’t any swords now, but flaunting a gun. Bravo! How is it? Wearing a holster/scabbard, walking about flashing his weapon, masha’Allah! Boasting about. He (swt) is Greater! Owners of guns and cannon think their massive weapons will protect them! All strut and show! This pride is smothering them. Pride slaughters man, stabs him and throws him away. Pride bears no fruit. What has Mankind achieved? Making cars, making a ferry?, Making planes, guns, cannon? Houses, mansions, palaces? Factories? Nothing.

What is Mankind’s moral obligation? It is his servitude to Allah! Except for servitude, everything is futile. His Pride hinders him. It prevents him from serving Allah. We say we are busy with other things. What else do you need to do? Build a house? Build mansions? Build cars, planes? No! Worldly commodity misleads. All material things are distracting. Mankind can see it and is still being misled, failing to mind its real duty. Our days are passing, running out for all of us. It is uncertain who will leave this life or when.

Janabul Haqq does not reveal it. Allah hides it. If He (swt) disclosed to us when we would die we would become useless. Janab Allah kept it secret. Whether one is 90, or 100 years old still man should not rush. Perhaps one who is 100 years old may say, ‘I am 100 years old but there is one 110 year old, I am not in a hurry’. What is that? He says, let’s get to 110 years, let’s see what will happen then. The one who is 110 years old says, ‘There are some living 120 years we’re not in hurry. I have enough time before I pass away’. A 120 year old one waits to be 130, 130 to be 140, 140 waits to be 150. I met someone 150 years old in Hijaz. Twice his hair turned black, twice he grew new teeth! He came to visit our Grandsheikh, Sultan al Awliya and said, ‘Sheikh Effendi, my hair turned black, a second time, now its pitch black. Not even a single white hair!’ He also showed his teeth, he was not toothless like me. I saw that his teeth were grown, ‘these are the second set, before the second ones, they grew and fell out then grew once more’. At the end, in his mid nineties! And also the third set are coming. And I looked, there was an old man with him, with a white beard, bent double. ‘This is my younger son’ he said. ‘He’s become very old’ he said to Sheikh Effendi. ‘This one is ninety years old’, he said. That faqir (holy man) was like that. Allah’s mightiness, how amazing!

He told Sheik Effendi that coming to Hijaz, this year while getting his passport the officer asked: ‘ ‘How many times have you been there Haji?’ ‘Well, I’ve gone so often, I don’t know how many’ he replied. ‘You’ve gone so often it’s unnecessary for you’, he said. ‘Just give me my passport, don’t ask how many times I’ve been’. ‘No, the government refuses you permission’. ‘Well, then I will complain to the Prophet (sws) about you’, and I left. Before reaching the door, something happened to him. ‘Oh my!’ he started to cry. ‘Oh my! Call that old man back, whether or not he has gone forty times let him take his passport and go. Oh my!’ he said, ‘I feel gutted by the pain’. ‘I was helped by...’ he’s saying, so that I could come to Hijaz. Here is my little old son, his beard is pure white. He looks like an old man, the other one doesn’t. He looks like our Hajiwat Abdul Kahhar’.

Yes, we are saying 150 years old. These Daghistanis live very long and their secret which they never talk about, is what they did, as we aged and can’t do anymore. How they used to work at a young age, so that they became prosperous, with a strong faith. They never said, ‘We’re old, we aren’t able to do’. Grandsheikh Effendi is saying that: ‘My grandmother’s brother lived 140 years.’ A General from Russia had come to the little town where the old man was staying, when Daghestan was occupied.

The Russian general came with a translator. The translator said to the Russian General, ‘This old man is 140 years old’. That General said, ‘Insha Allah you live ten years more’. He retorted, ‘Why do you say 10 years more? Shall I eat halvah, I’m not in a hurry to pass away so quickly, ten years! Imagine! That unbeliever’. He told that unbeliever off. ‘Why hurry. Is there halvah? Why send me off so quickly, only ten years? Go away’. The general asked, ‘What’s he saying?’ ‘He wants you to bring halvah’, Had he told the truth, the Russian general would have been offended. The general said: ‘Ok! Bring halvah for the old man.’

These are smart people and because they are smart Janabul Haqq purifies their hearts, they never feel suffocated, they find contentment, their minds don’t weaken. If a person’s faith is strong, his mind won’t be weak. As long as man’s faith is strong his mind will be sharp. His mind stays radiant. So he lives in contentment with faith. The faithless one is discontent, fading away day by day, his face getting darker. Darkness covers him, suffocation overcomes him. ‘Where can I escape or how can I get away? What is going on? I am bound from above and below. I’m finished, what am I going to do now?’ He says. The unbeliever’s face and heart are dark. His body wears out and stops working. Every part of it experiences a different pain and discomfort.

Therefore, accept the Owner of the Domain. Prosrate to Allah (swt), be a servant to Allah (swt,) then live beautifully, and die beautifully Live clean, die clean. Re-join the pure ones, hereafter. May Janabul Haqq give us awareness May Allah give us a good ending in honour of the Beloved One, in honour of Al - Fatiha.
