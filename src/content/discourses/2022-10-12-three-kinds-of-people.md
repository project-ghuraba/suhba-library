As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Often the khuṭbah (sermon) of Jum‘ah starts with, “ ‘Ibādu Llāh ittaqu Llāh ūṣīkum wa-iyyāya bi-taqwá Llāh.” “O people, O community of believers, be afraid of AllāhNone, respect and love AllāhNone. I give this advice to you and to my own ego. We are preaching this.” The khuṭbah starts like this. So not only the listeners, but also the one who speaks gets benefit from ṣuḥbah (association), khuṭbah and advice. There are three kinds of people, says our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. One kind is someone who knows, learns and speaks. He does what he says, and he tells people what he knows. Another kind is someone who tells what he knows but doesn't do it. And then there is the kind which only listens. And that is the jamā‘ah (congregation). The worst of these is the one who tells but doesn't do it, says our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam.

Therefore, when there is ṣuḥbah or advice, the one who gives it must apply it too. It is not only about speaking. And afterwards, giving advice and speaking of things that Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla and our Holy ProphetNone want, will lead Muslims to do them. They will do as much as they can. Because people cannot do it all, attending that gathering brings them barakah and a great reward. As long as good things are told, as long as knowledge is shared, it is wrong to think, “Should we say this? Will this man be upset, angry or disappointed?” You must tell what you know as true. Who accepts will accept it. Who won't, won't; and they know it. But if you are scared to say something to people, you won't be able to say a thing, because you can't make everyone happy. Who are happy, fine. Who are not, it's up to them.

But your duty is to speak the truth, to give advice to people. It is the order of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla and our Holy ProphetNone. The duty of hodjas, scholars, and shaykhs is to give advice, because, “Ad-dīnu n-naṣīḥah,” (religion is advice). Their duty is to make ṣuḥbah and to show people the way. What would be the benefit if they don't do so? It would be useless. We must say what is true.

And people who don't like it must think about it. Some people nowadays object to it directly and don't like it, saying, “It is not true. I don't think so.” Well, your opinion is not reliable. You must look into what you don't like. Is it coming from your ego? If not, is there a mistake in the advice? You must look into it. If it comes from your ego, you must improve it. Otherwise, you won't get any benefit from it. And instead of attaining a reward, it will be like a sin for you. Because who doesn't accept the advice will regret it. Advice is for AllāhNone, for the ProphetNone and for the believers.

Therefore, all of these talks and advice are the gatherings of knowledge. These are the gatherings loved by AllāhNone. Certainly, not here, but some people listen to someone and object. Whereas there is nothing to object to. It is ṣuḥbah for the good of the people. We are speaking so that people benefit from it. May AllāhNone make us from those who listen and benefit from it. It may have been Sayyidunā ‘Umar who said, “Isma‘ū wa‘ū, wa-idha wa-‘aytum fantafi‘ū.” “Listen and understand. When you understand, use it.” May all ṣuḥbah be useful to all of us, in shā’a Llāh.

In khuṭbah of Jum‘ah, many times they said, “ ‘Ibādu Llāh ittaqu Llāh ūṣīkum wa-iyyāya bi-taqwá Llāh,” saying, “Oh servant of AllāhNone, be aware, be afraid from AllāhNone. I am advise you first to, and myself, to be afraid from AllāhNone.” This is very important thing to say, not only for who are listening. Who speak, he said, “I am also, I make myself to be accept this, to be afraid from AllāhNone, not to do bad thing.” This is what all khuṭbah of Jum‘ah, many place, they saying this to make people listen. Only for who listening? No. First, what we make ṣuḥbah, make speech, for AllāhNone – this making to benefit people and to benefit ourself. Not only for people. Because, many people they thought, only they make advice for [other] people. This what Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying: “There are three kinds of people. Some of them they have knowledge, and speak and they have, also they accept and doing what they speak. Another kind of people, they have knowledge, they have very big knowledge, but only making speech, advise people to do this, to do that, but they are not doing. And, there is people, only for listening. They don't know, they coming to listening. And this first and third one is good. But, second one who only speak and not doing, this is not good.

For this, we are saying for ourself to be benefit from what we speak. This is very important. This, for sake of AllāhNone, we doing this. And people must be benefit from knowledge of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla and Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Many people, they are maybe not happy with some speech, but we are not looking for who will be happy, who will be not happy. What we know is right, we must tell people about this, to make people to benefit from this. So, people who don't know and quickly they don't happy with this, don't like this.

You must, if you don't like, even you must first look if it is right, if not right. If this what AllāhNone like, ProphetNone like, or you like. There is two kinds. [If] they are like and you don't like, you must repent and accept what AllāhNone like. Because, not for everybody, make, to make them happy. You can [not] give them what they like. It is impossible. Said Arab, “Lā yarḍi l-‘ibād illā Rabbi l-‘ibād,” and only Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla can make people to be happy, all of people. You, never be, you cannot give them happiness. Only you must tell and if they like to do, they can do. If they don't like, you make your job to tell people about good thing, about reality, about what human beings must do to be happy, here and hereafter. This is our mission and we are doing this. We are not looking for who say, “This is bid‘ah, this is shirk, this is not from humanity.” No, just we say, and what we are not listening for...

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, AllāhNone not make us anything coming to our ear or to see something. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, this is barakah of Mawlānā, nobody make this and coming to us. AllāhNone protect us, and they away from us to not see and not to hear anything from this bad thing. Just, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we try to give advice for AllāhNone, for ProphetNone, which Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, “Ad-dīnu n-naṣīḥah.” Dīn, religion, advice – to give advice for people. Advice, meaning good thing. There is no advice to tell people bad thing; it is not advice. AllāhNone protect us and to give us good understanding, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
