Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi Rasulillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

People show some things different from what they actually are. For example, they show Hadrat Isa (Jesus Christ), saving his presence, as Allah’s son and as god. Whereas many prophets came to Bani Israil (Children of Israil) and none of them were called god. Only for Hadrat Uzayr did they say the same, knowing it could not be so. Thousands of prophets came. Prophets suffered a lot of pain. They showed the prophets differently. Those who came later did this.

7000 to 8000 thousand years have passed since the time of Adam Alayhis Salam. Some histories are written. Most are not written. Written histories are not even 1%. So many nations came and so many people passed. Allah sent a prophet to all of them. Meaning He notified them all. Nobody who was not notified was left. Allah Azza wa Jalla says in the Koran: “I sent a prophet to every nation.”

Our Prophet, Hadrat Muhammad Alayhis Salam, came in the very end. He is the king of prophets. He is the one with the highest station in the presence of Allah Azza wa Jalla. He is the most perfect. Of course following him is an obligation (fard). Those who do not accept him can do as much good as they wish, and their works will have been for nothing.

According to other nations, the nation of our Holy Prophet will be the most on Judgment Day. The Nation of Muhammad (Ummat Muhammad) will be the most crowded. What a joy for us! Even prophets wanted to be an individual from the Nation of our Prophet (SAW) and continuously supplicated. They would pray, “Let us also be a part of Ummat Muhammad.” A couple of them will make it. Hadrat Isa Alayhis Salam will descend from the sky and he will also be a part of the Nation of Muhammad. The same with Ilyas (Elijah) Alayhis Salam and Khidr (Saint George) Alayhis Salam. They will also be a part of the Nation of our Holy Prophet. What a virtue it is that even prophets wished to be a part of our Prophet’s Nation!

However, people are not aware. Most people are not aware. They are not aware how big of a blessing this is. The regret in those who do not believe in him will be as big as this blessing. May Allah not misguide us from the right path, and may He make us from those who love our Prophet (SAW).

Loving him saves us. Nothing else saves. Shaitan tries from every side so that our Holy Prophet (SAW) is not respected. He tries thinking, “OK he became Muslim, but at least let him not respect the Prophet so that he is regretful in the hereafter.” May Allah not let anybody be fooled by Shaitan’s evils and tricks. May Allah (JJ) guide Muslims who have gone off this road, off the love of our Holy Prophet (SAW) Inshallah.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq.

Al-Fatiha. Hadrat Shaykh Muhammad Mehmet Adil 23 May 2015, Akbaba Dargah, Sabah Namaz
