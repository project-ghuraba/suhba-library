As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says that one of the signs of the end of times is that there will be “harj wa-marj”. Everything our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam said heNone used words of the highest language. Sometimes even those who knew Arabic did not understand what it meant. Many times, they would ask our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. They asked, “What is harj wa-marj?” It means killing and murders. "Killing people will increase," said our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. It is from the signs of the end of times.

Therefore, this is what we see happening in the world, and there are many more that we do not see. The unseen ones are much more than the seen ones. Murders, killing people, harassment of people, and the displacement of people from their homes and homelands are many. What is seen is only few. This is from the signs of this time. Dunyā in the end of times is over now. There is nothing left. There is no humanity left. There are no manners left. There is no goodness left. All the evil is happening in this time. Which means that the time our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam was talking about is the time we are living in.

Of course, it happened also in the past. But at that time there was an Islamic state. There were people ruling us. The Muslims had a Khalīfah, the Khalīfah of our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. His command was done. His will was done. And now, by the wisdom of AllāhNone, in order for these things to appear, they [Khulafā’] no longer exist. That is why people suffer hardship, and Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla will eventually send a savior. Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam gave good tidings about this. With hisNone good tidings, we all wait for that person.

Because there is nothing anyone of us can do about what is happening. Only when he comes, when that person comes the whole world will reach safety, in shā’a Llāh; Muslims and non-Muslims also. He will be a means of guidance for them too. But these difficult days are the days we are living. It becomes easier when a person knows what is happening. He will have both reward and benefit. Because the person who does not know thinks about what is happening and what will remain. “We will be harmed.” Everything whether harm or goodness, whatever there is, has reward and benefit for the Muslim, the believer. Everything is beneficial for the person who believes in AllāhNone and follows the way of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla and the way of our Holy ProphetNone. Nothing is in vain. But for the kāfir (unbeliever), everything is a loss. Even if he thinks he has won, he has lost. If he doesn't win, he has also lost.

May AllāhNone strengthen our īmān. May HeNone send the Ṣāḥib to Muslims, to believers. May HeNone save the oppressed ones soon, in shā’a Llāh. May AllāhNone make us reach those days soon, in shā’a Llāh.

Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam say from hisNone good word, "At the end of time it will be" in Arabic theyNone said, "There will be harju marj." Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, heNone was speaking the highest Arabic. When heNone speak sometimes even the ṣaḥābah they don't know, they don't know about what heNone said and asking, "What is this what you say? Harju marj?" TheyNone said, "This is killing people. People killing each other, oppressing each other. There will be crime. There will be people killing other people. It will be too many, in end of time. And who kill, he don't know why he kill. When the one he killed, he don't know why they kill him." This is we are living in this time and this meaning we are what Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying about this time, we are now in exactly in this time.

Because what we see it is very few we know about what happen where they kill. It is more and more than one you hear, one you see. This is very few they show you, but the reality – everywhere in the world they killing people, torturing people and they are not seeing, not showing. But we hear very little because this is shayṭān controlling and whole world now under shayṭān control. Because we are living time must be like this. To approach the Qiyāmah it must be like this. And we are living in this time and many people they don't know what is happening.

But we are, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, with Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, we know. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying, "Hal yastawī lladhīna ya‘lamūna wa-lladhīna lā ya‘lamūn" (39:9). Any equal between who know and he not knowing? No but he, you know what is happening, what will be, you will be most, better than who don't know. Because he thought it will be this forever. Nothing forever here. There is time and, after this time, it will be again – this people they will be different, throwing out with what Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam good tiding us. It will be guidance. Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām, coming and throwing all this people. They will be not find anyone from them. This oppression-people, who not following order of AllāhNone, just following order of shayṭān – all of them it will be finish.

But we are, what happen also for believer, for mu’min, for Muslim, it is reward for them. Nothing going for empty. Empty for who are, they are thought they are have power, they have everything. But reality they are loser, this people. The winner who is with Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Because the end it will be with Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. AllāhNone send this Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām, in shā’a Llāh, very soon to finish this suffering for from people, in shā’a Llāh. Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
