Audhu Billahi min al shaytan al rajim, Bismillahi r-Rahman r-Rahim. Assalatu wa Al Salam ‘ala Rasulina Muhammad Sayidi al Awwalin wal Akhirin. Madad Ya Shaykh Muhammad Nazim al Haqqani, dastur!

Tarikatuna al sohbah wa al khayr fi al jamiah- Our way is through association & goodness is in the assembly. We are making a short sohbat today after dhuhr, with Mawlana's permission. We are trying to advice here about the small things that Mawlana does not look to. As for the manners of the Tariqat follower who just started or have been on this path (for a while), 3 things are very important in this way: To eat less, sleep less, and speak less.

Today we may speak only about eating less, others we speak later on. This matter of "eating" is important, something Mawlana also always warns about: People at old times would eat one kind of meal only. There was not much but they were healthier, stronger. Now, in this time there are all kinds of foods available. People eat too much. What they eat now...The orders of Tariqat, Subhana Allah, are for the goodness of people, not only for the murids but for whole mankind. When people start a meal, they eat everything without looking what is in it. There are many kinds of poisonous foods, without checking what is inside, if it is halal or haram.

Especially eating outside, in these restaurants is even more unsuitable. Meaning, it is very dangerous to eat outside at places where you are not 100 % sure (of what you eat). Because if you eat unknowingly, there is no haram but once that poison is in the food, it harms you. It harms your spirituality as well as harming your physical being, your body. Therefore being shameless & eating too much.. When you eat something too much, you already got fed up with it. Before people would eat meat every once in a while. They would appreciate it. Now they eat everyday, they are saying meat has become expensive. However it has become too cheap, everybody is able to eat everyday! One must be careful about this point as well! But when you come to dargah, it is shifa-cure, with Allah's permission- you can eat as much as you like. We are not saying this, but in your own house, in your own place, there is no need to make your children eat too much. Once your child ate & got full, it is ok, enough. When you start with Bismillah, and finish with Fatiha it becomes a shifa-healing insha Allah. When we say "eat less";you can still eat in normal amount, don't eat excessively. There is permission to eat within limits but be careful about what you eat. This is the first matter, others we say later insha Allah. The other two matters, we speak in other sohbats, insha Allah. Today's sohbat is, for murids there are 3 subjects they must take care of this. Who following Tariqat & murid, for human beings also this is very good. What Tariqa orders it is for people, all people also. Good for all people. But then people cannot do, so never mind. We don't tell for our people also. First is to not eat too much. Second one, don't sleep too much. Third one is, don't speak too much. Today only for eating, we speak. Because eating, all illness coming from the stomach. So when you eat little bit, it will be.. you're safe from illness first. And this nowadays, food it is... you don't know what is inside. Especially for restaurants, or what you eat outside it is too dangerous to eat there. Even many, they mix with this bad meat you don't know. If you eat like this it is ok, you are not haramed. If you do this without knowing. But it is harming you, for your spiritual & physical, this meat. For this, if you are not sure 100% from the restaurant, don't go there. And, we say this for children also. Many families say, "my children is not eating. Eat, eat, eat.." How much he eats, it is enough for him. Don't force him to eat. But this is for common people we are saying. In your houses do this. But when you come to derga, Alhamdulillah here this is shifa, and the clean food here, and baraka. You can eat here. We are not saying this for here. Alhamdulillah, Baraka of Mawlana this is derga food, all shifa, pure. We welcome other subjects, after insha Allah this.

Wa min Allah al tawfiq, Al Fatiha.
