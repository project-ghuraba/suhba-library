Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Authu Billahi Minash-shaitanir Rajim. Bismillahir Rahmanir Rahim.

“Yaa ayuhallathena amanut taqullaha wa kunu ma’assadiqeen.” (Sura Tawba:119) “O you who have believed,” says Allah Exalted and Glorious be He, “fear Allah and be with those who are true, those who are good.” You see hundreds of people every day in this life and they are not all on the right path. You already know that they are not on the right path. However, what is more dangerous is the one who says, “I am a shaykh,” or, “I am a hodja,” and he is not a true one, meaning not on the right path, whose inside is different than his outside or who shows the true as false and the false as true. May Allah keep us safe from them. They should not be followed.

Their situation is not good. They are not true ones but liars. Those who follow liars are ruined. It says in the holy hadith, “Al halalu bayinun wal haramu bayinun.” “The haram (forbidden) is clear and the halal (permissible) things are clear. If a person makes the halal haram and the haram halal, Allah gave people a mind so then they should not follow him. They need to stay away from him because servitude is only towards Allah (JJ).

It is possible a person might have took you to the right path, to Allah’s path, but you do not owe your whole life to him. Allah created you and you can follow him as long as he follows Allah’s words. Move away from him if you witness behavior that is not good, actions against Allah, or hear words against Allah. Move away so you may save yourself. Especially stay away from people who say, “I repented on my own. I received inspiration!”

Do not follow persons who do not reach our Holy Prophet (SAW) and who are not on the line that is connected to him. They are people who are not in tariqa. The man has no knowledge and he stirs himself saying, “I reached guidance and I will guide people!” He could guide a little but then Shaitan can play as he likes with that person. Therefore, be careful and be with those who are not liars. Liars are the enemies of Allah (JJ) and the enemies of the Prophet (SAW). May Allah keep us all safe Inshallah.

Wa Minallah at-tawfeeq. Al-Fatiha.
