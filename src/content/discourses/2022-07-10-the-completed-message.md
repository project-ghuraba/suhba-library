As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Yā-ayyuhā r-rasūlu balligh mā unzila ilayka min rabbika wa-in lam taf‘al fa-mā ballaghta risālatah” (5:67). This is what Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says to our Holy ProphetNone: “Tell the people about all of Allāh’sNone orders. Tell them about the way Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla ordered us to follow, that shows the good and the bad,” HeNone says to ProphetNone. That is why our Holy ProphetNone said all of it. If heNone didn’t say all of it, heNone would not have delivered the trust given to himNone by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. So our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, thanks to AllāhNone, conveyed everything to Muslims and to all mankind. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gave HisNone commands in order for everyone know the right way, and the truth. This Ḥajj was Ḥajju l-Akbar (The Greatest Ḥajj). Our ProphetNone, four months before leaving this temporary world for the eternal world, said during a sermon, “Today, INone completed your religion. INone completed all of it. HeNone told us to keep to that way.

The way of our Holy ProphetNone is open to everyone. Everyone can take benefit from it. Whoever takes benefit, is benefitting. Whoever isn’t taking benefit thinks he knows better. “Fa-man shā’a fa-l-yu’min wa-man shā’a fa-l-yakfur” (18:29). Whoever wants should have faith. Who does not want can remain in unbelief without faith. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says that people decide for themselves. Who wants to be in loss will remain in unbelief. Who wants to take profit will have faith. Faithful people are always in goodness, always profiting. They are never in loss. Whether they are poor or rich, sick or well, the people of faith are rewarded for everything in the presence of AllāhNone. But without faith, without belief in AllāhNone, nothing has any benefit. One can suffer as much as he wants, seek riches or power as much as he wants – it won’t benefit him. The welfare of all humanity is in what our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam told us. Not only to Muslims, but to all humanity. This beautiful way is for people’s happiness, for their joy and goodness.

AllāhNone wishes us goodness. And shayṭān wishes badness and embarrassment. He wishes poverty and all kinds of bad things on mankind. Because they are his enemies. “Inna sh-shayṭāna li-l-insāni ‘aduwwun mubīn” (12:5). Your biggest enemy is shayṭān. Don’t be cheated by him. But people are cheated. They do everything he tells and wants. And then they suffer in this world. And there is worse suffering in the hereafter. May AllāhNone protect us.

Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam showed us this beautiful way. HeNone told us everything about it. Certainly, we can’t do everything. We should do as much as we can. And AllāhNone will forgive the rest. We will attain the intercession of our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, in shā’a Llāh.
