Subhanim Allah Sultanim Allah Nabim Muhammad alayhi's-Salam Subhanim Allah Sultanim Allah Nabim Muhammad alayhi's-Salam Subhanim Allah Sultanim Allah Nabim Muhammad alayhi's-Salam

Salamu alaykum all my brother and sister.

Alhamdulillah, I'm still continuing to give you the good news and we are hoping the good tiding from Allah Almighty that, to make us happy with our Shaykh.

Today we have some kind of the treatment it was not really good but Alhamdulillah, with the baraka of your du'a and with the baraka of Mawlana Shaykh it's been come back again. We are hoping that Allah Almighty He is accepting our du'a and He accepting that our Shaykh will remain with us.

It is very difficult time, I must be honest with you and I must be clear with you because you have the right to know everything about Mawlana Shaykh because he is not only my father, he is our father. So I feel it is duty for us to explain for my brother and sisters what's going on and what's happening and what the treatment is.

Normally Mawlana Shaykh his everything according to his age it is below average. This is been known, but Alhamdulillah with the miracle of Mawlana Shaykh everything is working like perfect. Even the doctors they are looking and they are seeing the miracle of Mawlana Shaykh, the miracle of Awliya and the miracle of what's going on.

Alhamdulillah, we are happy to be as follower for such a person and to be his children and to be the people that we love him and he love us.

Alhamdulillah, it was, this morning it was some kind of news that we didn't like normally, but afternoon everything been changed and the doctors they been shocked again and they say 'how can be something like this happening and how can be things as this happening'.

We are as a family of Mawlana Shaykh, we say among ourself, myself, Shaykh Mehmet, and my sister Haji Ruqaiya and my sister Haji Naziha, we say we are surrendering for Allah Almighty Will and we are here in Allah Almighty’s Hand, and we are hoping Allah Almighty will give the curing at the hand of the doctors and with this place and we call not the intensive care, the dergah, 'intensive dergah'. It is that become here as a dergah. All the murids here, masha Allah they are giving their blood to Mawlana Shaykh, the people they have the same type of blood with Mawlana Shaykh so Mawlana Shaykh become blood brother or blood father for so many murid Alhamdulillah here, and this is one of the biggest opportunity and this is one of bigger honour for us to be from his blood or to give our blood to him or he accepting our blood.

Alhamdulillah, I can say that he hope still there, I can say that the light still there and I can say that we can expect that Mawlana Shaykh will remain with us and he come back to us and this is our hope, and this is our sun, and this is our light.

So we will never lose hope, we will never lose the sun or we never lose the light because as much as we wishing to see Mawlana Shaykh breathing we are asking from Allah Almighty give him more life, give him good health, and let him to remain with us. So I'm asking from all my sisters and my brothers to keep du'a for Mawlana Shaykh and to keep hold the light of hope in their hand, in their heart and in their eyes.

This is what we are doing and this is really, really, really make us very comfortable and this is teach us how to be surrendering for the Lord Almighty Allah that He create everything and we know that we are under heavy test and we know that we are under very big pressure and we know we don't have any choice than to surrender to Allah Almighty. That's why I'm giving my humble advice to be surrendering and to be with Allah Almighty and to be relaxed in your heart and keep praying for Allah Almighty to give Mawlana Shaykh long and healthy life.

Wa min Allahi tawfiq bi hurmati l-Habib bi hurmati l-Fatiha
