A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Lāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

“Wa-tawāṣaw biṣ-ṣabri wa-tawāṣaw bil-marḥamah, ulā’ika aṣḥābu l-maymanah” (90:17-18). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l- ‘Aẓīm. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gives good tidings to the patient and merciful believers. They are in the highest ranks, on the right side and in the beautiful places of paradise. They attain the mercy of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.

Patience and mercy are the basis of Islām. It is the way our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam taught us, recommended to us and showed us. Muslims must be merciful and patient. Impatient and merciless ones must reform themselves. Kāfirs have no mercy, no patience and no goodness. They are the customers of hell. They are people who deserve hell. A person with no patience and mercy, AllāhNone will not have mercy on him either. He who does not show mercy will not find mercy.

That’s why the believer is always in goodness. He wants good for everyone. Not just for people, but for everything – animals, trees – he has mercy for everything. Therefore, cutting down trees should not be done unnecessarily. It is wrong to burn greenery in Islām. It is makrūh (disliked). There is no permission for that. Mankind does so. They do all kinds of things. As we said, mercy is not only towards humans, but to the tree, to the bird, to the animal. Mercy towards everything is the way shown by Islām.

Hypocrites now show themselves as merciful. They show mercy to people. Whereas, they have no mercy, they have nothing. They do the opposite of the laws and lifestyles placed by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. It is also oppression towards people. Because everything is made according to a balance and scale. If you change or interfere with something, you are oppressing everyone. People of this time do the same thing. There is no mercy left. There is no goodness left. And on top of that, they say, “We are good. We are merciful.” It is Islām that’s showing all the most beautiful things, showing the best way. It is what humanity needs. Everything other than Islām is badness to humanity. It is faulty. It is the means to all kinds of harm. Therefore, the way of Islām is the salvation of humanity.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says in Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n “Wa-law a‘jabaka kathratu l-khabīth” (5:100). Which means these dirty things, bad things, “Everyone’s doing them; I’ll do them too.” Beware. Don’t try to do them, don’t like them – HeNone says, “Law a‘jabaka” means do not like it. “All people are doing this, let me do the same.” They are at a loss, you too will throw yourself into danger, you will throw yourself into the dirt. Never even try it, says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Therefore, even if those who follow Islām are few in this world, stick with it and follow it so that you will be saved, and also be a means of salvation for others, in shā’a Llāh.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying: “Wa-tawāṣaw biṣ-ṣabri wa-tawāṣaw bil-marḥamah, ulā’ika aṣḥābu l-maymanah” (90:17-18). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l- ‘Aẓīm. Who they are, believer, mu’min, who this - they are patient and they are merciful. They are praised one in Divine Presence of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, in the good place. “Maymanah,” meaning good place for next life. It is for believer, for mu’min, for who has mercy and have patient. This is what Islām teach people, what Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam show us this way. HeNone was the most merciful one for human being. HeNone was from hisNone birth, heNone asking from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, O, ummatī, ummatī, my nation, my nation, AllāhNone save them. HeNone ask from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, from HisNone mercy, to not make anybody to go to hell. HeNone try and ask people to come to heaven. This is attribute of believer which Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam show us this.

Who is believer, he is merciful. Mercy and patient. Who is not believer, they don't have this. They are not patient, no mercy. Non-believer, who not believe in anything, you cannot find any mercy in their heart. Only they imitate and cheating people. Saying, “we are looking for human being, we are want to make them better but they making them worse.” They are interfere in what Allah ‘Azza wa-Jalla show human being to be in way of mercy, way of patient. They are seeing this but they are not doing. Just imitate, pretend, and people they believe them. They are leaving way of AllāhNone, which it is real mercy for human being. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying, don't believe for this people, even they are majority.

Now of course in world majority they are non-believer. They are running away from religion. Now running from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla only for their desire, after their ego. Because ego they don't like to be tied, or to be make them to not do bad thing. This is what ego. Ego like to do bad thing and Islām prohibited this. For this they are enemy for Islām. But Islām it is real thing for human being, for this world which it is need for. Because their mercy not only for human being, for animal, for grass, for trees, for anything, all of this AllāhNone show us with Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam how to deal with all this. There is teaching how to eat, how to drink, how to go, how to come. All this, who follow this, it will be world, excellent world. But they are, they left this, and they are running after their ego. And ego only they want for themself, not thinking what will happen after me. It is not important, because non believer they thought just they will die and nothing happen after this. But they're wrong, they'll be regret in Ākhirah.

AllāhNone keep us in this way which it is best way for us, in shā’a Llāh, for whole of human being. AllāhNone give hidāyah for all of them, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
