As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam’s words are like jewels; very beautiful. What he says is light for us, for our way, for our hearts, for everything. Allah rewards you when you hear it also.

One of his sayings,<br>
كُنْ فِي الدُّنْيَا كَأَنَّكَ غَرِيبٌ أَوْ عَابِرُ سَبِيلٍ
<br>“Kun fī d-dunyā ka'annaka gharībun aw ʿābiru sabīl.”<br>
“Be in this world as though you were a stranger or a wayfarer (traveler).” [Bukhārī]

Be in this world as a traveler going from place to place. What does this mean? Don’t think you’ll be here forever. Here is as if you’re going very fast through a place to another place.

So don’t put your effort into making something thinking you’ll stay here. Your only effort must be to take with you as much as you can in this way for the next life. Do goodness, obey Allah, love Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, love Ahlu l-Bayt, love the Ṣaḥābah, mashāyikh, mu’minīn and mu’mināt. This is what you must look for. As much as you can do, that’s what you’ll take with you. 

Of course, you can also work for a living, but what’s important is for ākhirah. Don’t think you’re here forever and that you won’t die. Everybody is coming, one step after another, going to reach his place. Nobody stays here.

For this, Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam shows in this small ḥadīth the way of believers to make them wake up. Because they are like sleeping. Sayyidinā ʿAlī karramAllahu wajhah says, “People are asleep. When they die, they wake up.” But that time it’s too late. Inshā’Allāh, may Allah keep us and open our hearts for this ḥadīth. 

May Allah reward us, inshā’Allāh, in this blessed day of Jumʿah. Mubarak, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
