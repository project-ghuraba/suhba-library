As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Shukr to Allah, these are blessed days. These ten days of Dhul-Hijjah are among the blessed days of the year. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla has also mentioned them in Qur’an Azimu sh-shan. Their Barakah and benefit are for Ummati Muḥammad. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla gave it especially for the Ummah of our Holy Prophet.

Therefore, these holy days are important for both those in Ḥajj and those not in Ḥajj also. These are days with many rewards and many gifts. Benefit as much as you can from these days, says Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla.

The treasures of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla are endless. No matter how rich the people in this world are, their generosity is to some extent. But in the Presence of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla, there is endless generosity. That generosity is given to people, but people do not benefit from it. They run away. And after that, they keep wondering, “Why is there sadness? Why is there narrowness?” Whereas AllahʿAzza wa Jalla says, “I give breadth to the one who follows my way, I give him comfort. Whereas to the other one, I give distress in dunya and punishment in akhirah.”

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla is Arhamu r-Rahimin. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla is the One who has Abundant Mercy. He gives everyone from that mercy. It is enough for everyone; it increases. There is never any deficiency. People are ungrateful; or rather mindless.

Those who are not on the way of Allah, their thoughts are not good. Their thoughts are wrong. Those who are with Allah are always the winners. They are the ones who always have a good end.

May Allah make these days blessed for all of us. May He give their rewards for those who cannot go to Ḥajj and for all of us. Because many people could not go. May Allah bless those who went. May Allah complete it for them with gifts, with spiritual gifts. May they return with both apparent and esoteric gifts inshā’Allāh. May they return with His Barakah inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
