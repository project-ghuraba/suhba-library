As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Wa-jā’at sakratu l-mawti bi-l-ḥaqqi dhālika mā kunta minhu taḥīd” (50:19). Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says in Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n, “When the stupor of death comes to a person, he gets confused.” This is what he has been avoiding. There is nowhere to escape. It is something that will happen to everyone in the end. It is the truth. The biggest truth is death. One has to prepare for it. The biggest preparation is not to leave your prayers and worship for ākhirah. Settling your account in ākhirah is difficult. According to this world’s calculations, for each prayer you will pay for it for eighty years. Therefore, before this comes, Muslims and all human beings should not neglect it. They must be careful. If there is something a person missed, he should try to complete whatever prayers and fasts he has. Of course, one cannot do them all. He will do as much as he can. AllāhNone gives the rest according to the person's intention, in shā’a Llāh.

Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says, “When a person borrows money from someone, AllāhNone makes it easy if his intention is to repay it.” That debt is paid. But if he decides to take the debt, like most people do today, and says that either he will pay or not pay, then it will not be paid. And it is the same for prayer. If you say, “I will perform it and make up for it,” with the permission of AllāhNone, you can do it and finish it. If you are a true, sincere person and you try to do it, but you pass on to ākhirah without being able to do it, then AllāhNone will forgive you according to your intention, in shā’a Llāh. Death is something that everyone will taste. Arabs have a saying about prayer, to make them be in fear if one did something: “Jā’aka l-mawt yā tāriqa ṣ-ṣalāh.” “O who has abandoned prayer, death has come to you.” It is such a thing that the person who has no compensation for it is destroyed. They use that phrase if someone is caught red-handed or something happens.

So, they give it a different meaning without knowing its greatness; but the main meaning is that we should not leave with a debt of prayers May AllāhNone protect us. It is the most dangerous thing. May AllāhNone protect us. May AllahNone guide people. May they know the valuable things, and not look at worthless things. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying: “Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Wa-jā’at sakratu l-mawti bi-l-ḥaqqi dhālika mā kunta minhu taḥīd.” (50:19). Ṣadaqa Llāhu -l ‘aẓīm. “The drunkenness of death coming,” Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying for, because when somebody leaving this life, this difficult – slowly, slowly become like drunk. Sometimes seeing something, people they not seeing, the…Other not seeing, he speak and people not understanding, look like drunk. This is time of death. That you, whole life you are not thinking about this. You are thinking it is, it will not be for you. It is for everybody, not for only one. For whole, everybody who born, he will be die. This is what Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, hearing from one poem in place like people was gathering. Everybody saying something, and there was one big, wise one, he saying, “Everyone, he born, he will die.” And what it is: “Kullu ātin qarīb.” “Everything coming, it is near, not far.” And he is, many thing he said, but what the important for us here: everybody who born, nothing, cannot do anything.

So, we must be, to save ourself from this bad position, to be obeyed for AllāhNone and to not leave anything for ākhirah, what AllāhNone order us. First thing: praying ṣalāt, five time a day. We must, as much you can be in jamā‘ah, better. If not, with yourself any time, quickly in any place, you must find something you can do. Many people they said, “We are in work and we cannot do.” You can. It is five minute. For drinking coffee, you need half an hour Five minute you can pray, you can finish this. But when, for if you leave anything for ākhirah, it will be difficult. The most difficult thing in ākhirah, in dunyā, maybe take you five minute, ten minute or…But for each praying in ākhirah, there, you will pray eighty years. Eighty years, your whole life you are not praying, so you can count. But when, if you miss many of praying, you must try to pray it again, what you didn't pray. And you must be sincere for this.

If you are sincere, as much you do, AllāhNone help you to finish it. Because Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying for, if anybody, anyone, take loan from people, and he is intention to give it back for this people, real sincere intention, AllāhNone make it easy for him. He can pay back this loan for other people. But as this time, with, we are living in time, people they like to take loan and to not give back.

So, if their intention like this, they will be never be pay anymore [anyone] for this, when you are, you have loan for AllāhNone for praying five time, if your intention to finish it, it will be easy for you and you will finish. Intention very important. If you couldn't finish, that time, AllāhNone also, in shā’a Llāh, forgive for your good intention. HeNone is knowing our inside and outside, that you cannot cheat Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.

So, must be finish this, and other also. This is very important time for people. There is also this time, time of living this life, it is very bad for this people. Arab saying, saying: “Jā’aka l-mawt yā tāriqa ṣ-ṣalāh – Death come for you, O who not praying.” So that time, because he know everything finish, then he will be very afraid. Very horrible time for this. For this – but of course the Arab, they use it for other thing. As they, somebody catch somebody in wrong thing or doing or, government catch him or other… So, they saying it's very terrible moment. They said for this but reality, the horrible time for not praying, not doing, listening to what Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying. That moment, very terrible moment for them. AllāhNone keep us safe from this moment, in shā’a Llāh. To be, in shā’a Llāh, good moment for all of us, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
