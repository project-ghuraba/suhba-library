Sheikh Omar asking some questions, deep questions. He was waiting one year to ask, what is that?

Q: So should we in reading Holy Quran, or studying Holy Quran, is it fard to have wudhu?

What is the most respectful action for Allah? Yes, that is [an] important question. Just I heard about that point from my Grandshaykh, that [is] Abdul Khaliq al Ghujdawani, Allah blessing him. He was one of king-size wali. Allah Almighty giving him to be deputy, from Allah. Almighty granted to him to be khalifah for Khatm-e-Khwajagan, for dhikr in Tariqat Naqshibandiyyati l-Aliyya. It was held [the] first time in the cave of Thawr, on the way to Madinah, the first building of tariqah. Allah Almighty giving talqeen (initiation) to His Holy Prophet and saying:

Allah Hu Allah Hu Allah Hu Haqq Allah Hu Allah Hu Allah Hu Haqq Allah Hu Allah Hu Allah Hu Haqq

Allah Almighty, this [is] His own dhikr. He make dhikr for Himself by Himself. This to Holy Prophet giving. Holy Prophet, Beloved Muhammed (pbuh), giving to Siddique. Siddique giving to seven thousand and seven Naqshibandi Grandshaykhs. My Grandshaykh giving to me and I am giving to you. Alhamdulillah. The first Khatm Dhikr in that holy place, Allah Almighty ordering to Abdul Khaliq al-Ghujdawani to make this first Khatm. He was doing. Allah giving khilafah to Abdul Khaliq al-Ghujdawani.

I heard about him from my Grandshaykh that Kaaba, Holy Kaaba was coming and making tawaaf around him. People running to make tawaaf around Holy Kaaba, Holy Kaaba coming and making tawaaf around him. He just built a private place, dome. He spent seven hundred coins. One coin, that time you may do one house. Seven hundred, making. Everything is alright, muzayyan, muattar, ornament[ed] and perfumed. For what he putting such a thing? High, high table, 'rahla' we are saying, for Holy Qur'an. Putting Holy Qur'an on it.

That house for that Qur'an-e Kareem, only for Glorious Qur'an. When he was asking to go and to recite Holy Qur'an, was taking bath, whole bath and wearing white or green clothes, everything. Never using that clothes in anywhere else. Wearing and walking as [if] he was going in front of a grand emperor, in a sultan's presence. Going like this and asking for reading. First he (Grandshaikh) was listening [heard], "Oh, don't touch my book till you should be finally clean" and he was saying "Oh, my Lord. I am making whole bath and coming, but you must do tajdeed iman (renew faith), three times 'shahada' then 100 times 'astaghfirullah', this, as asking permission to recite my, my book". As much as you are respecting - this signing those people, how they were respecting Holy Book, Holy Quran.

And in our time, what they are saying now, people? Asking to recite without wudhu also. If no water or if no possibility for wudhu, you are making 'tayammum', then coming and reciting. For the 'azimat', for the firm orders, firm will, determination orders we have been [given]. No permission. Even [if] we are stepping without 'tahara' (and) the most simple, simple tahara [is] by 'tayammum'. Just I heard it and showed me also, Grandshaykh.

And Prophet (pbuh) going for out and returning to come and to make wudhu. He was living there, coming [to] a clean place, making tayammum then coming reaching to the water and making wudhu. Till from there, [the] toilet, coming to water for making wudhu, he was not going without tayammum. Must be, because servants must be always clean. With 'ghusul' or wudhu or tayammum. Tayammum is the most [easy], or easiest 'tahara'.

If you are, no possibility [and] you can't take wudhu quickly make tayammum. Then recite Holy Quran. Say "Oh my Lord, I am asking forgiveness, I am reciting." Yes, that is true. You see important thing that Sheikh Omar asked about it and coming a good lesson for everyone. As much as we are respecting Allah Almighty, makes us respectful in front of whole creatures. That is important. It is enough.
