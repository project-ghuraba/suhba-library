As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. ABismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Fa-man aẓlamu mimmani ftará ‘alá Llāhi kadhiban liyuḍilla n-nāsa bighayri ‘ilmin, inna Llāha lā yahdi l-qawma ẓ-ẓālimīn” (6:144). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l- ‘Azim. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says: “Then who is more of a tyrant than the one who invents lies, saying what Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla did not say, and presents it to people as the word of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.” The person who shows it to them is a tyrant. AllāhNone does not guide the tyrants.

The way they show is not a way. Everyone shows their own way anyway. The unbeliever shows his disbelief. The deviant shows his deviance. They are already clear. But the person who says he is a Muslim and presents to people, saying, “This is the word of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, the word of our Holy ProphetNone,” is the worst of them. AllāhNone does not guide them. Their end will be bad.

There are many people now who claim to be Muslims and are trying to destroy Islām. Some are doing this knowingly, and some are doing it unknowingly. Those doing it knowingly are worse. Those doing it unknowingly are also bad, because, since you say you are guiding people, you must have some knowledge. The most important knowledge you can have is to know what Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla did not say, and not to present those things as the way of AllāhNone to people.

This is the greatest oppression, because you are playing with people's faith, their lives, and their eternal life. If they go astray, most of them will be ruined and lost. Few people come to their senses and repent. Therefore, this is a big oppression. Leaving people to eternal suffering, eternal bad life, and leading them astray is the worst thing. There are many of these people in this time. People follow them as well. But you are not following the right person; you are going down the wrong path. There is no excuse for this. A person might leave the right way and sometimes confuse ways, but he can still return and go back on the right way.

Yesterday, for example, we were going to Hala Sultan. We lost our way. We wandered around and then returned again to the same way. But if you go on the wrong way, if you follow that way, you will never reach. You will have gone out of the way. If you do not return to the right way, you will never reach anywhere. You will have gone astray. You will have gone down crooked paths – paths that will lead to your ruin. Therefore, we need to pay attention to this. There is no excuse. People say, “I unknowingly followed this man or this woman. They said this and that, and they spoke well, so I followed them.” But this is not an excuse. Look into it, ask questions, and investigate what the truth is. If it’s true, follow it. If it’s not, turning back immediately from wherever you are will be a gain for you. May AllāhNone protect us.

There are many antichrists and many liars. False prophets appeared after our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. After them, there appeared false scholars and bad scholars. Millions of people who followed them were destroyed. May AllāhNone protect us. We must be careful about that. This way of ours is the right way. It is clear and obvious for everyone. Whoever leaves it and follows the wrong path bears their own fault. May AllāhNone protect us. May AllāhNone preserve us, in shā’a Llāh.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla say in Qur’ān: Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Mimmani ftará ‘alá Llāhi kadhiban liyuḍilla n-nāsa bighayri ‘ilmin, inna Llāha lā yahdi l-qawma ẓ-ẓālimīn” (6:144). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l- ‘Azim. In Qur’ān, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying that there is – who they are – most oppressor people, who showing people way and they said, this is what Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla order and make people to confuse and go out from right way. From right religion, right belief, and make them to run away from this, showing wrong thing for them. This is the most oppressor one, because who make this, maybe this, who cheat them, they are doing good, doing many good thing. But this who show them – “This is wrong, come follow me” – they will follow him, and they will be, in end, very bad end, going out of way, way of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, way of AllāhNone. This is make them to destroy their belief, destroy them, to take him at the end to Hell. This is, there cannot be more oppression from this, who doing this. Because they are in way of AllāhNone, or even not way of AllāhNone, maybe.

Sometimes they will be in way, but this man or woman or anybody, show them: “Come, I am, have real belief. Don't believe in this people. It is not right what they say. No need for praying, no need for fasting. But by intention, by dream, by sleeping, by waking up. You can do.” They have thousand of way to cheat people and when they doing this, they be destroying this people, make them to be, very bad end for them. This is since Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, many of liar coming, antichrist, dajjāl, hypocrite people. They take out millions of people from way of ProphetNone and they make them bad end. Take them from good religion to following shayṭān and shayṭān he said, “I want to take whole people to Hell." They take them to Hell. Straight way, it is open.

AllāhNone give everybody good thinking, give them brain to know what is right, what is wrong and it is clear. So, some people, they can be cheated, but it is not good thing to follow everybody; your ego like what he said. You must see if it is right, or it is wrong. Because the end it is your life, you're responsible for this and even sometimes, can be going out of way. But if you know it is wrong, come back quickly to right way. Like yesterday, when we are going to ziyārah (visit) Hala Sultan, we missed road and we look where go out right way. Quickly we turn, maybe ten minute going here, there. But we find right way, and we, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we make ziyārah. If we insist to wrong way, we will not, never be visit, make ziyārah, for Hala Sultan. It is like this: life.

Maybe many time making wrong thing but you quickly, you must repent. You must ask for forgiveness for AllāhNone. Come back to way. AllāhNone happy with you. HeNone reward you because you ask for forgiveness and ask for to be in HisNone way. This is everybody in this time. There is more people everywhere making, destroying life of human being, Muslim especially, to with this. They have many. Before it was maybe somebody saying something very far. Nobody hear about him, nobody listen to him. But now, in every, in your hand, every minute this shayṭān cheating people, destroying people. They give fatwá in this. They give idea, they give advice as they said. But advice, not advice, good thing; advice to do bad thing.

So, you must be very careful. Don't listen to this who are not real ‘ālim, not real scholar. Because many scholar they are following their ego, what they thinking, they saying for people, “This is what AllāhNone order. And this is not what AllāhNone order.” AllāhNone order it's clear and openly for everybody.

AllāhNone save us from this people, keep us in way of AllāhNone, way of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, right way until of the end, in shā’a Llāh, end of life.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
