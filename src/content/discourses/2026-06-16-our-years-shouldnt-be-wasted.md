As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

May the first day of Muḥarram be blessed and auspicious, inshā’Allāh. This year will be better, inshā’Allāh. We make duʿā that this will be the year of the appearance, the good tidings foretold by our ‎Prophet, inshā’Allāh.

Qiyāmah is near. People are heedless, they don’t talk about qiyāmah at all. They think it’s far away. But it is near; days and years pass by in an instant. The Hereafter is drawing near. Of course, everyone’s own qiyāmah is the moment they close their eyes; because when a person opens their eyes, they find themselves on the Day of Resurrection. Therefore, this is very important; those who know its value will have prepared for the Hereafter.

Today is the first of Muḥarram. Inshā’Allāh, if we live to see it, by this time next year, a year will have passed in the blink of an eye. Last year we were on Ḥajj, and this year another Ḥajj season has passed; the days really do pass in an instant.

Every moment that passes while on the way of Allah is beneficial; it becomes provision for the Hereafter, they say. The journey to the Hereafter is long.

Therefore, these years, these days, should not be wasted; they should all be spent in the way of Allah. It is necessary to remember Allah; because mentioning (making dhikr of) Allah means remembering Allah. Of course, merely pronouncing Allah’s name with the tongue is also a form of dhikr, but dhikr is also remembering Him and keeping Him constantly in the heart. You must constantly remember Allah so that your hereafter may be prosperous, so that days, hours, and seconds do not pass in vain.

To attain the intercession of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, it is absolutely necessary to remember this. Every minute, every second, we should think, “Allah is with us, Allah sees us, Allah is a witness to our actions.” A person who acts accordingly will not have wasted their days.

You say, “The year passed in an instant,” yes, it passed, but it was a full and meaningful year; not empty, not filled with sin. Of course, there are sins; everyone has sins. However, after repenting and seeking forgiveness, they too will be erased with the permission of Allah.

May Allah bless our year; may it be a blessed year, the year we hope for, inshā’Allāh. May it be the year in which Islām finds victory, and the year in which Mahdi ʿalayhi s-salām and ʿIsā ʿalayhi s-salām, as given good tidings by our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, will come, inshā’Allāh. This is what we desire. If we attain this, we will have attained Allah’s promise, inshā’Allāh.

Because in the current state of the world, there seems to be no salvation; it is sinking deeper day by day. Only when the Mahdi ʿalayhi s-salām comes, inshā’Allāh, this will improve. There is no other way for it to improve. May Allah protect us, and may our new year be blessed and auspicious, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
