Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi Rasulillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Sohba is the order of tariqa. Inshallah, let us say a couple of wrods. You are also travelling at night, are you not? You came all this way for the sake of Allah. You came from Ankara, some came from Holland, some from Adapazari (a city near Istanbul), and some from Germany. Mashallah. May Allah send his friends. May Allah’s friends come. Other people would not come anyway. They do not like gatherings such as these. You came all the way here from Ankara. May Allah be pleased with you. Some came from further away than you. Some came from Germany, there are those who came from Holland, and there are those who came from further away. They came for Allah. At least you are able to come once in a while. Thank Allah. Why did we offer thanks? Because Allah granted us these gatherings.

There are some people who go somewhere every week. They put forth their money, they put forth their honor, do everything bad, and repeat it every week. They go without getting bothered, tired or bored. We thank Allah that He did not make us one of them. May Allah correct their way as well, and may they depart from that bad path. We call this place far sitting here, but they never call it far. They do not care whether it is night or day and go to every place on earth. Not just to our nearby Cyprus. They go wherever this illness of gambling is. The poor go to Cyprus and the little richer go towards Europe. The “more affluent” go to America, to Las Vegas. They say they have gambled in whichever famous casino. May Allah fix their situation. Because there are people who know Allah amongst these people. There are people who saw Mawlana Shaykh too. But they still fall to their ego and go to such gatherings.

There, your being here and coming for Allah is a great favor. You have come for Allah, so know its value. We are here today, gone tomorrow. Allah is always existent. The people who know this do not lean their heart to bad places and to bad things. They are disgusted from such things. And they feel sorry for those who do. So many people came today.

They are also trying to ruin this country. They are trying to take people off the path with all kinds of mischief and discrod. Make dua (pray) to Allah Azza wa Jalla. He accepts your dua Inshallah. We are praying too. May Allah guide however many who have departed from the path. May Allah protect those who have not been misguided as well. Because we are living in very tough times. Shaitan is scanning everywhere, bombarding so nobody would escape. Just as there are merciless people now, do not think Shaitan has mercy. He has none. He would not feel sorrow if he took all people to hellfire. It says both have gone to hell in the Koran. In the end, he says, “I fear Allah.” If you do not fear, you go to hell too.

That is why, let us be careful and not follow our ego. Do not even go behind your ego for one step. Do not show an excuse. While thinking, “Let me just guide this one to the right path, I will also go after this one and save one afterwards,” both of you would fall of the cliff and perish. Be very careful. We are in dangerous days! We are living in a dangerous time. If Believers (Mumins) protect themselves, those around would also be protected. The others would be guided through them. If you are a good example and if you hold your ego, Allah directs his servants to you. There is no need to go after them and jump in danger. Continue with goodness and go as ordered. It becomes blessings (baraka) for you, and guidance and light for people. Little by little, Allah willing they increase in number everywhere. People are living in emptiness. There actually is a foundation. Our ancestors gave their blood and lives in the way of Islam. Through their duas and their blessing, it is a great favor and a great gift to be protected in the middle of so much discrod.

May Allah really help us. Those whom Allah helps have no fear. Our backs are strong and the place we lean on is strong. Everything changes, even mountains change. Allah Azza wa Jalla is as He is. The true being that never changes is Allah Azza wa Jalla. We need to believe this and always think like this. Let us exalt His most beloved servant, our Holy Prophet (SAW). We are protected with his blessing and his intercession (shafaat). Because even in the world, if you are going somewhere, you are more esteemed going with the greetings of someone or with someone. If you try and enter spontaneously, they would kick you out asking you who you are. Allah Azza wa Jalla opens the doors if you say for the sake of our Holy Prophet (SAW).

Do not believe these new comer people who have gone wild and spread everywhere like cancer in the last fifty to sixty years. They say the Koran. How are you going to understand the Koran? You could not understand it without our Holy Prophet (SAW). This is all you understand. We wee what you understand. You have ruined everywhere and made it wretched. Those mindless of us here are the same way. They have learned two words. They say the Koran. Oh, how can you understand the Koran? Nobody would understand the Koran if not for our Holy Prophet (SAW). May Allah give a mind. Truely, those people are absolutely mindless, out of reason people. They have no such thing as logic. What can you say?

May Allah protect us all. From both the evils of those people and the evils of Shaitan. May Allah be pleased with you all. You came for Allah. Whatever wishes and wants you may have, may they all be gained for the best. May it be happy for all of us in the world and in the hereafter. Just like we are together in the world, may we also be together in paradise. May we be housed in our Holy Prophet’s (SAW) place, and be with our Shaykh Inshallah. There is separation now, but there is no separation there Inshallah.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq.

Al-Fatiha.
