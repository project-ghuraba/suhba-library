As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Wa-Llāhu ‘alá kulli shay’in qadīr” (3:189). Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is powerful over everything. Everything is in HisNone power. Nothing is difficult for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. AllāhNone does everything. AllāhNone created everything. Our grandfathers say the mind cannot comprehend the work of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Out of their adab (good manners), they say they cannot comprehend what Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla does. And they would never even approach HisNone essence. Not out of of adab, but because of danger. Old time people...

People of the present time think people of old times were stupid and mindless. There was nothing then, no universities, no schools. They think those people didn’t know anything. And they claim they know now. There are universities. They say, “I graduated from three to five universities.” And it is obligatory to send children to school. It wasn’t so in old times. Who could, studied. Who couldn’t, it was okay. But now it is mandatory. Some people say, in some countries, you can go to jail for it. In others, you must pay a fine. And are they learning anything? No. What they teach is ignorance. Nothing else. People of old times were a thousand times cleverer than them, a thousand times more knowledgeable.

Knowledge is to obey the order of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla; to know your limits. If everyone knows their limits, there will not be any problems. But everyone has exceeded their limits. They have crossed a line with other people. They have meddled in the affairs of others. Whereas people of old times questioned themselves. They thought about the good and the bad, and lived with respect and honour. They lived with love towards each other. But people of the present time don’t have this at all. The more they claim to learn something, the more they fall into ingnorance and unawareness. Learning is not knowledge. Living is knowledge. Knowledge is to live like a human. People of times past lived like humans. What people of the present time have is enmity, jealousy and competition between each other. This is what they are taught.

Therefore, as we said, they don’t look at the power of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. They look to see what is and what is not. Whereas if they look into the universe created by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla – insects, animals, people and jinn – they cannot comprehend how these work. How can you understand things planned by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla? Therefore, we can learn only a part of it through the prophets. And they teach as much as AllāhNone allows them to.

Therefore, old time people, and people who walk on their ways, know something. They believe in something. It is qiyāmah, ākhirah, the end of times. Shukr to AllāhNone, we are living in the end of times. It is near. There will be salvation for humanity after this. People have no other solution. They have tried everything. But none of it has been useful to them. They must turn to AllāhNone in order to see any benefit. They must obey the will of AllāhNone.

You may deny it as much as you want. Truth won’t change by your reluctance to accept it. Therefore, who is clever obeys Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Many people don’t obey Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. They obey shayṭān. And they are devastated, in a devastating situation. May AllāhNone protect all of us.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Wa-huwa ‘alá kulli shay’in qadīr” (5:120). This is āyah from Qur’ān verses. Saying, everything... meaning AllāhNone able to do everything. This is, I don’t know, it’s maybe not perfect translation. But AllāhNone, HeNone make everything. Everything. So, no, nothing difficult for AllāhNone. Nothing. You cannot say this. [It] is from HisNone attribute to be Qudrat, Qadīr. From HisNone attribute, HeNone can do everything. Nothing impossible for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.

So, we are living in a time, this people, they thought they are clever, they are more clever than before generation, or fifty year generation or hundred year generation before. They thought they are more clever, more intelligent, more knowledgeable because they have school, they have high school, university... Even, if you don’t send your children to school, they punish you. Even, they put in jail or give penalty. And they thought they are better than this old time people; they are ignorant, they are not clever. Now, thousands of years people living in this earth, and when only since maybe fifty or sixty years, people know to learn to live. How they was without school, without university, without anything? They was living and they was happy, and they was doing every good thing. And they have good knowledge, everything. But you said, “No they don’t, they are not clever people. Now, we make better. We have everything free for people. These people, they were living in ignorance. They were miserable, they were unhappy.” No, they were better than us. They were living better life because they have knowledge.

Because these people, who were not happy with old people, [the] more they open university, [the] more they open school, make people to study, to force people to do this – they become more ignorant. Not become more clever, more intelligent. They become more ignorant. What is this ignorance? Ignorance: to deny the CreatorNone, and not do what HeNone create human being, with their character, whether for men how to be, for woman how to be. Everything, HeNone create attribute. And everything with body, HeNone create also soul, and what can, must do. But, this people, they are ignorant, they are not accept this and they destroy human being. As they destroy with nature, they destroy everything in this world. Now also, they try to destroy human being. To bring another kind of thing. For this we saying, old people they was more knowledgeable. Because, they have, they know everything from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. HeNone create everything and HeNone able to do everything. But this people, they not accept this. But, no problem. They cannot – If they accept, not accept, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla not changing. Not... HeNone is still as HeNone was. For this, old people, they was not thinking about Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HisNone exact. But, only thinking about HisNone attribute, and what HeNone create, what HeNone done. From whole this universe, HeNone created. Not coming to this: “Who is? What is? How this?” This is – They know, because they are clever, this is very important: don’t come to this area, you will be finished. Very dangerous area, this. These people, they know this. They was quickly, if anything coming to themself, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, “Say, lā ilāha illā Llāh Muḥammadur Rasūla Llāh, ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam.” So, this people, they was doing this. And they was safe, their belief, their īmān.

But now, this shayṭān people – All this teaching about to deny, not to accept. AllāhNone destroy them, this shayṭān army, dajjāl army. Antichrist army, these people. In shā’a Llāh, we believe with everything. AllāhNone HeNone know what HeNone done, and we are only astonishing for what will happen. But Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone give news for people through his messengers, the prophets. And Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying about this time. And after this time, it will be time of happiness. Whole world it will be believer. Not one non-believer it will be. After this also, the Day of Resurrection, and, in shā’a Llāh, jannah. For paradise, or for hell. In shā’a Llāh, AllāhNone make us from paradise people. AllāhNone keep us, keep whole human being to be accept this and to save themselves, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
