As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Shukr to AllāhNone, we were able to witness this month, Ramaḍān. It is passing well, with barakah and goodness. AllāhNone has gifted this month to us, to the Ummah. It has all kinds of beauty in it. And the Night of Power is usually in the month of Ramaḍān. It can be any day of the year. That is a different matter. But usually, it is in the month of Ramaḍān. From the first day of the month until the end, there is a good chance of it. It is not officially only on the twenty-seventh. Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says to look for it after the twentieth. It mostly happens then.

It can be on any date, odd or even. But there is a higher chance of an odd date, says our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. One of the ṣaḥābah asked himNone, "Can you tell me the day of Laylatu l-Qadr?" He insisted that our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam tell him. HeNone replied, "If people know it, it will be too easy for them. They will leave praying at other times. They will pray only on that night, and leave the rest of the prayers." That is why our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam didn't reveal it.

And of course, it changes every year. Still, our Holy ProphetNone could have told, but there was wisdom in hiding it. Because it is better than a thousand months. A thousand months is eighty years. And that is more than the average age of a person. Many people pass away before reaching that age. And if you count the years of youth and childhood, a person will be ninety years old for him to have worshipped for eighty years. So, one can do more good deeds in one night than throughout his entire life. That is easy for people.

People may put off good deeds, and be deprived of the goodness of the present. That is the wisdom in it. Also, it leads to worshipping each day and every night. Turkish people have a good saying: "Know every night as Qadr and every person as Khiḍr." Show importance to every night. When you perform worship, show importance to it, as it is a holy night. As that night is a great goodness for people, they should treat every night as if it is that night. But of course, people have come to such a state now that they say, "Let's worship until the morning just on the twenty-seventh." And they ask how to worship on Laylatu l-Qadr. It is unknown when Laylatu l-Qadr is. Therefore, perform your worship every night. You will spend all year as if it is Laylatu l-Qadr with its barakah. And know everyone as Khiḍr. Sometimes you may not like some people. But don't ignore them. When you least expect it, they may bring you goodness without you knowing it. A person you don't pay attention to may be Khiḍr. And that is a great goodness. Khiḍr ‘alayhi s-salām is a prophet. But of course, there is no prophecy for him in this time. It is a great chance to meet Khiḍr ‘alayhi s-salām, to receive his du‘ā’. It is a great thing to see him.

You see that many people don't show importance to a stranger, sometimes. Sometimes they even swear at a very important person who is venerable and respected. And then they feel ashamed when they find out the truth. For this reason, it is from Islamic etiquette to show importance to everyone. It is a means of getting along well with everyone.

This night has a lot of barakah. As we said, it is like praying for eighty years. It is better than eighty years, says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Because Sayyidunā Yūsha‘ ‘alayhi s-salām, as our Holy ProphetNone related, fought for eighty years. Eighty years is not easy. All his life was spent in jihād. And to make jihād is a great virtue. When our Holy ProphetNone heard this, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla said, "I grant you the goodness of eighty years in one night." Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gifted it to our Holy ProphetNone. Therefore, it is a great favour to us from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.

As we said, it is good for us to know every night as Laylatu l-Qadr and to continue praying and worshipping in the same way. And what we do is not difficult. You pray two raka‘āt, go to bed with ablution, and wake up for tahajjud. Even getting up a half an hour or fifteen minutes before fajr prayer to pray two raka‘āt is tahajjud. It will be as if you were awake the whole night. Pray at least two raka‘āt. You can pray more, pray Tasbīḥ or Najāt. You can do everything, but always pray two raka‘āt. Not everyone can do the other things all the time. Make sure you get up before fajr and pray tahajjud. You will have revived that night. It will be counted as Laylatu l-Qadr for you, in shā’a Llāh.

May AllāhNone give it to all of us, in shā’a Llāh.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla give us this blessed month, holy month, blessed month, month of Ramaḍān. Many thing, good thing AllāhNone give for ummah of Sayyidunā Muḥammad ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Which it is from beginning to end, it is blessed. Every night, every day and night, we are worshipping twenty-four hour, al-ḥamdu liLlāh this. There is tarāwīḥ, there is zakāh, there is Zakātu l-Fiṭr, i‘tikāf. Every good thing. And especially we have Laylatu l-Qadr. This night it is, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla say, "Better than thousand months." Thousand months become equal to eighty year. Eighty year, life of any one of human being. Between many people, they cannot reach even eighty year. They pass away before. This so, there it is more blessed and holy than thousand, whole your life. And it is hidden. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla with HisNone wisdom, make it – hide it. It is whole year you can find Laylatu l-Qadr, not only in Ramaḍān. But in Ramaḍān – most of Laylatu l-Qadr coming in Ramaḍān. Why Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla make this? To make people to look for this. And Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam was... Ṣaḥābah asking for himNone, "For which night this?" HeNone... heNone didn't tell exactly. One of ṣaḥābah, he insist too much. HeNone said, "If I tell you about this night, this people, they will leave praying another time. Only they pray in this night, and they will be, no need for other thing." For this wisdom, AllāhNone hide it, and Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam can tell, but heNone know this attribute of people. They will become lazy. They only choosing one night, and not making anything other time. Because, we are, have weakness of our ego, following ego. So, it will be not benefit. It will be bad thing for people. AllāhNone know best, and HeNone hide it to make people to search for this night.

And as we said, it is... whole year can be. And most in Ramaḍān. Ramaḍān also, from beginning to end, can be. And also, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, most of this, after twenty of Ramaḍān, any night can be this. And especially in, not, single night. Like 21, 23, 25, 27, 29, like this. And people they are only knowing twenty-seven, it is night of Qadr, Laylatu l-Qadr, twenty-seven. Can be twenty-seven, but not every year. People now, every year, they know twenty-seven, they will be... But now, they celebrate, they celebrate for barakah.

To remember Laylatu l-Qadr in Ramaḍān. This is also special thing for Ramaḍān, ya‘nī, most of people knowing. And they celebrate, AllāhNone also reward them because they make more ‘ibādah, more khayr. And they are – it will be also good for them. But what, there is Turkish they saying... Mawlānā, he said for this. First, when he met his, went to his Shaykh, he didn't know, he went. It was in Istanbul and it was forbidden to do any worshipping outside of mosque. And not wearing sunnah, not wearing turban, and no nothing else. It was – but secretly they bring him, Mawlānā to his Shaykh in Fatih. First he said, when he enter there, the Shaykh tell people, "You must think every night Laylatu l-Qadr." This meaning, every night, you must be ready for this night.

How to be ready? To sleep with wuḍū’, and to pray two raka‘āt, and wake up for tahajjud. Tahajjud, before Fajr you can maybe... fifteen minute, half an hour, one hour, as people they can do this. Maybe they cannot do all of this. Very short, two raka‘āt or four raka‘āt or eight raka‘āt or other worshipping also. What important, to continue this whole year. To think maybe this, it will be Laylatu l-Qadr and AllāhNone bless you. This is very important, this wisdom of hiding Laylatu l-Qadr.

Second thing this Shaykh said: "And you must think everyone as Khiḍr ‘alayhi s-salām." Who is also... he is not appear. He change his appearance. Can look like child, like old man, like beggar, like crazy, like... anything can do, can be seen. People, many time they're not happy with him, and they not caring about him. And they lose big thing – to be, to get his bless, to get his praying. It is very important, like treasure, to, for Sayyidunā Khiḍr. So, for this also, hidden between people. And who...

This was also in this Shaykh when he said this, he see Mawlānā first time and he saw his, of course, his secret. And they said this people. They didn't understand. Because it is Turkish saying, "Her gördüğünü Hızır, her geceyi Kadir bil." You must, meaning, this is saying between people. But people when he said this, Shaykh, they they thought it is only normal speech. But it was sign for Mawlānā when his secret known by this Shaykh, and he was make tawajjuh for him. And from that time Mawlānā reach the highest station al-ḥamdu liLlāh. AllāhNone bless them and AllāhNone make us to be able to do this. To make, maybe, to make every night like tahajjud like this, it is easier than to accept everyone.

AllāhNone make us to not be, make wrong for people. Because, many time, this also happen, people they don't know people, and they make not respect them. After, even sometime they shout on them or make them to be not happy with them. After, when they know this man he is very respectable, and so they become very ashamed. We must be careful also, to good behave for everybody. To ask and understand who is, what he want, what they want. And be good, polite with people. To not be, after you be ashamed and very bad situation with, in front of these people. AllāhNone not make us to ashamed in front of HimNone also, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
