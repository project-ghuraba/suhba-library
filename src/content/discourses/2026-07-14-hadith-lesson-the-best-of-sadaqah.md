As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllah, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

1 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “The most excellent dinar,” dinar is the highest money in value, as gold, “is one that a person spends on his family.” This money spent is the most virtuous, and most rewarded. 

“And the dinar which he spends on his animal in Allah’s path,” So if one is going to go to war, taking care of his horse, his donkey, grooming him and strengthening him - this comes second, and this is one of the best of money spent. 

“And the dinar he spends on his companions in Allah’s path.” That is, the third is money spent on helping people on the path of Allah, says our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam.

2 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

A man asked the Prophet, “O Allah’s Messenger! What kind of charity is the best?” He replied, “To give in charity when you are healthy and greedy hoping to be wealthy and afraid of becoming poor. Don’t delay giving in charity till the time when you are on the death bed when you say, ‘Give so much to so and-so and so much to so-and so,’ and at that time the property is not yours but it belongs to so-and-so (i.e. your inheritors).” So, a person who is healthy, strong, young, and has money. The charity given by someone who is very eager for that money, who desires it, is the most acceptable charity. 

A young person has all kinds of opportunities. They want to be rich, they fear poverty, but if they give charity, that charity is the most acceptable, the one with the greatest thawab and reward, says our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. 

Another is a person is living their last days. They’ve reached 80-90 years old. They haven’t given any charity. Then, when they come to their senses and wonder what to do with the money, they say, “Let this charity belong to so-and-so, let it belong to such-and-such.” Be careful, don’t delay. This ṣadaqah is already theirs. It’s no longer yours. Therefore, give charity at the time when it is most acceptable. Don’t leave it until your last days, says our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam.

3 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

Our Holy Prophet is saying “The most excellent ṣadaqah is what a man with little property can afford to give; and begin with those for whom you are responsible.” So, the most acceptable ṣadaqah is the one who gives that ṣadaqah even though what he has is a few. But still, our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam says that this acceptable ṣadaqah should be spent starting first with your family and your closest ones. So that’s also considered ṣadaqah. Taking care of your home and family is ṣadaqah.

4 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

Our Holy Prophet is saying “The best kind of charity is that which is given when you are rich, and the upper hand is better than the lower hand, and start with those for whom you are responsible.” So, the giving hand is better than the taking hand. And giving charity to your family first by providing them with their sustenance is also charity. Start with them, says our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. Do not leave them and give others; but start with yourself, then your relatives, neighbors, and the poor if there’s money left.

5 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

Our Holy Prophet says, “The most virtuous form of charity is providing drinking water.” The most virtuous charity is providing water.

6 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “The best of charity is when a Muslim man gains knowledge,” so knowledge is also ṣadaqah, “then he teaches it to his Muslim brother.” This charity, the charity of knowledge, is a great thing. Charity is not just with money. There is also charity through knowledge. Shukr to Allah, when we were studying in the madrasah, our teachers were great scholars, they would tell us so. At that time, they both looked after the madrasah and paid salaries. May Allah be pleased with them, may their ranks be high. They would tell us, “Our right upon you is for you to teach this knowledge to others. We do not want anything else from you.” That’s why the ṣadaqah of knowledge is also a great ṣadaqah.

7 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “The most virtuous charity is the one given to a relative who thinks evil.” For a long time, relatives have sometimes been very evil to each other. Even if it is that, the charity you give to that relative who treated you badly is the most virtuous charity; it is very virtuous in the sight of Allah.

8 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “The most virtuous charity is the one you give to a slave in the presence of a bad head of state or administrator.” So, it’s to the man works for a bad person and suffers all kinds of torture from him. The ṣadaqah given to him is virtuous. Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam says that it is one of the most virtuous ṣadaqah.

9 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “The most virtuous charity is the one given in the month of Ramaḍān.” Because its reward is very great. Those holy months, especially the month of Ramaḍān. That’s why, zakāt and ṣadaqah are more virtuous during Ramaḍān. Ṣadaqah can always be given, but it would be better if you give your zakāt in Ramaḍān.

The Messenger of Allah says the truth in what he said or as he said.
