Shaykh Mohammad Nazim Al-Hakkani An-Naqshbandi, Sohbat of the 28 of November, 2012.

Shaykh Mohammad: It says "Sultanu Dhillullah".

Mawlana: Dhillullah, there is a sign in Akbaba Sultan (tomb in Istanbul), you may see it- Sultan Dhillullah fi 'l-ard (The Sultan is the Shadow of Allah on His Earth). Subhan Allah. When he is not present, it is impossible to resolve. It needs Sultan, the rest are all garbage. We need a Sultan to rule us. Not by selecting, or electing- this is not something to be elected from the market or bazaar, no. This is the reality, Sultan Dhillullah fi 'l-ard. We should respect him like this, that he is confirmed & supported from Heavens and he is perfect. And he is majestic also at the same time. People should ask this, not by electing. Is this Bandabulya (bazaar) that you say “I don't like this, go to another. I don't like this also, another”... No.

So this appearance/inspiration came to me that I found relief. People should come to their senses & bring back their Sultan that their matters may be resolved, or else there is no way that things can be straightened. May Allah make us be together with the Sultan. Amin Ya Rabbi.

O Our Lord, May You send us a Sultan. Amin, Amin.. Salam on all the Prophets, especially on the Master of the Prophet(s). And safety for all who are present & not present & all the believers, and praise be for the Lord of Heavens, al Fatiha.

If there is no Sultan, things are left to the hands of the mob. Everyone has a Sultan- Egypt has a Sultan, Sham has a Sultan, Baghdad has a Sultan, Ottomans has a Sultan, Hijaz has a Sultan. Astonishing, astonishing..Look, even in the apparent; in ceremonies the emperor is in the front, after him comes the king and then comes these presidents. Therefore the Queen of England has an authority wherever she goes. What authority do we have? We collected men from the markets, bazaars & formed a government? Masha Allah! Who gave them the permission to do this? Shaytan! May Allah protect us from the evil of shaytan. Read for me too. Fatiha.

Affarin, Affarin. It is approaching, it is approaching. Let's wait & see, many things are coming now. It starts in Muharram al-Haram also and in the month of Safar, most of them will be moved. The Sultans of each nations will come. We may see & serve them insha Allah. Is it not so, o son in-law?

Hj Feridun: Yes.

www.saltanat.org Page 1 Mawlana: Who has the majesty of Sultan? They say "that one is president". Where did he come from? From a village. All fighting. What is their value? Paper money! What is the value of the Sultans? Gold. The people will come to their senses or they will get beaten until they do so. Aman ya Rabbi, tawba ya Rabbi. We ask Sultan from Allah Almighty.

Declare this, have no fear. Fatiha. There is Umm Haram Sultan here. Insha Allah the Sultan will come.

Sometimes such astonishing sunuhat/ inspirations come to me & I find comfort, Allah shukur. May I see you also together with the Sultans. Amin Amin.

La ilaha illa Allah Sayyidina Muhammad Rasul Allah. Fatiha.

Ok son, you may go. Step strongly, step with firm steps.
