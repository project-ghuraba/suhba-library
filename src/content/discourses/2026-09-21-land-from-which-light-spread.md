As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Alḥamdulillāh this journey of ours [to Central Asia] went beautifully; shukr to Allah. We visited the blessed ones; ten from among our mashāyikh. Apart from them, there are many other scholars and blessed ones in that region. It is Allah’s wisdom that people beneficial to the world have emerged from that area. They have taught and instructed people, in a beautiful manner, in both worldly knowledge, spiritual knowledge and that of the Hereafter, including the religion, sharīʿah and ṭarīqah.

At one time, a period of communism swept through those lands; they thought they had completely eradicated everything. Yet, māshā'Allah—much like lush green grass sprouting anew from parched, mown earth—we thank Allah that wonderful people, scholars, hāfiz, and men of learning are once again being raised; sharīʿah and ṭarīqah and worldly knowledge continue to be taught just as before. This is Allah’s wisdom. Allah declares that His Light continues. No one can extinguish or sever the Light of Allah.

Therefore,māshā'Allah, this visit of ours was truly beautiful. The state of our blessed brothers and the people there is beautiful. Even if they do not—or even if they are ordinary people—they hold immense respect for the religion and for the people of the ṭarīqah.

Of course, shayṭān never rests, he always seeks to stir up trouble, but inshā’Allāh, his influence will no longer prevail. Inshā’Allāh, from now on, the Truth (Haqq) shall endure; for barakah, goodness, and benefit lie with the Truth. To walk the way of Allah is to walk a beautiful way. The journey along this way is beautiful, and its end is beautiful, too.

Shukr to Allah, with every visit, we witness the service expanding further and see people turning toward the religion and holding fast to the commands of Allah. Māshā'Allah, mosques are overflowing with congregations. New mosques are being built.

Shukr to Allah, all that we see here is truly something that makes one happy. What brings true joy to a person is the way of Allah; it is the blessings of īmān and Islām that He has bestowed.

A person who turns away from the Hereafter to pursue the world—valuing only worldly things—finds neither joy nor peace of mind; they live in a state of constant anxiety and distress. Yet, Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla has granted you the greatest blessing: He has given you īmān and shown you the true way.

Remain steadfast on this way so that you may attain happiness. May Allah never let any of us stray from that way, inshā’Allāh.

It is the way of the blessed ones. Although, from the outside, one might see only the maqāms of three or four hundred blessed ones, in reality, hundreds of thousands—even millions—of blessed ones have emerged from those lands. Naturally, since they did everything solely for the sake of Allah, they sought neither fame nor any other worldly gain.

Consequently, their end was one of goodness and barakah. Barakah overflow from those lands, spreading in every direction. May Allah make these barakāt everlasting. May our visit be accepted, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
