As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, madad ya Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Wa-khalaqa kulla shay’in fa-qaddarahū taqdīra” (25:2). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘aẓīm. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla created everything. And Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla created everything with wisdom. There is nothing without wisdom. Among creatures, there are good ones, bad ones, clean ones, dirty ones. There are all kinds of things. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla created all of them with wisdom. HisNone wisdom is with HimNone. HeNone also created night and day. HeNone created summer and winter. HeNone created heat and cold. The Creator is Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.

As we said, in everything there is wisdom and benefit for humans. For people who seek that wisdom, there will be both wisdom and benefit. Now it is hot. It is hot during summer. This is normal. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla created some countries with permanent summer. In other places, there are seasons. In countries with fewer seasons, by Allāh'sNone wisdom, they are given their livelihood, their food and drinks accordingly. For those in other places, it is hot in the summer and cold in the winter. Mankind complains without understanding the wisdom. They say it is too hot, it is too cold. However, what happens in this season, at its time is beautiful. What happens out of time[season] is not good. If it is cold in summer, it is not considered good. It should be hot in summer. Even if a person feels uncomfortable, it is something that a person needs. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gives the heat of summer according to that, and those who are patient endure it. Who is not patient do not benefit from it. They must be patient and give thanks to AllāhNone.

Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says, “When it is hot,” in Qur’ān ‘Aẓīm, “Qul nāru jahannama ashaddu ḥarra" (9:81). “The heat of hell is much more intense.” Therefore, in this world, the one who is patient will be protected from hell in the hereafter. Again, these are beneficial for humans. Sometimes the human body needs heat. Then, in the cold, it also needs the cold. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla created everything with HisNone wisdom in the most beautiful way. We must give thanks for these things which HeNone has given. We must be patient with which is not in its time. Last winter, it was not cold. When it is not cold, everything is somewhat different. We also say that this is Allāh'sNone wisdom. We cannot interfere with that. The whole world has advanced technologically and everything. Can they do anything about it? No, they can't. They cannot go against Allāh'sNone will and the order HeNone established. Therefore, everything happens according to Allāh'sNone will. As we said, when it happens at the right time, it is beautiful. When it happens on time, it is beautiful. Things one does when he is young are different. Things one does later are also different. Everything is according to its time, and one must act according to the time.

May AllāhNone help us. May AllāhNone bring about good things. May everything be a means for good. May HeNone give us the reward of patience, in shā’a Llāh.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying, Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Wa-khalaqa kulla shay’in fa-qaddarahū taqdīra” (25:2). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘aẓīm. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone create everything and HeNone put perfect management for everything. But HeNone, in this exist [wisdom], from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Maybe there is good, there is bad, there is dirty, there is clean, there is dark, there is white. All this Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla create and special[ly] for our life, to live in this earth. For, HeNone give us everything, to be serve Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, to know this. But unfortunately, people they don't know. They don't even thinking about this thing. They thought it is by itself coming like this. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla create day and night, day and months. Create this weather, hot or cold or what we are living in this earth. There is many seasons for human to live.

And every place there is people living in this place, they have specialty to be easy for them to live in this area. Like there is one area, whole year –whole year it is summer. No winter, no spring, no autumn. Only summer. Twelve months, summer. But in this area people, AllāhNone create them different from other people. They can easily live in this area. No need for them to [do] anything else. But other place, like our place, little bit far from … here is seasons. There is four seasons. Some of them, some place, this [season] is very short. Some of them in some place longer, very long. But normally they have four seasons. And four seasons [this is] also wisdom from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Now we are living [in] summertime. In our area, maybe in other place it is winter, different from here. But north of world, whole summer time and people, they are sweating. “Very …” they said, “Very hot. What is this?” As [if] they are first time seeing summer, they are complaining every time. So … But it is wisdom from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Because our body, because we are living four seasons, one – each season our body – AllāhNone give what [is] in need in this time.

Everything in time, it is good, perfect. In summer hot, it's perfect. In winter cold, it's perfect. Maybe if in summer cold, it's not … This is weird. Winter like this, last winter we have, it was real weird. No cold, no rain, nothing. And – but it was not so good. And of course, from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla everything good but for normally, it is not normal for what AllāhNone, HeNone give us. In winter, cold and this cold also good for your body. Good for your spiritually, for mind, for everything it is good. Summer hot also it is same.

So we must know this from AllāhNone and HeNone – HeNone give us what we are in need. We must be thankful for HimNone. And of course they have technology, everything but they cannot change this what AllāhNone give from seasons or other thing. They cannot do anything. But they also trying to go to another planet to live there. There, even they cannot live in desert and they are … But it is cheating people just to make them forget Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone said, “Minhā khalaqnākum wa-fīhā nu‘īdukum wa-minhā nukhrijukum tāratan ukhrá” (20:55). “WeNone create you here and you go out and be in life after, you will be here also.” Not in moon or not in Mars or not in Pluto. I don't know. This is, Muslim they must know this. Don't say, “Which century we are in? What this ignorant Shaykh, saying about this.” It is impossible because order of AllāhNone for us to be here. For this you must be accept what AllāhNone give us. Don't be worry if it is hot or it is cold or it is … Just thank for AllāhNone. HeNone give us this belief, īmān. it is most precious thing. Because Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, people, some of them saying, “Very hot.” HeNone said, “Nāru jahannama ashaddu ḥarra” (9:81). Meaning, “Fire of hell, it is more burning.” So don't be complain about this. Just be quiet and AllāhNone reward you for everything.

Even Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, saying, if small thorn coming on you, AllāhNone, HeNone reward for you. For hot or for cold, also, in shā’a Llāh, AllāhNone reward us. And make us to be happy with HimNone. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are happy with YouNone. AllāhNone give – bless us, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
