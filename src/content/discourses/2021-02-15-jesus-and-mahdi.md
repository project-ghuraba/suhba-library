Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Humankind was created by Allah ﷻ and Allah ﷻ created the Nafs (ego) with him. What always gives trouble to a person is his ego. Those who do not follow their ego win insha’Allah. At the time of Nuh/ Noah (as), in the same manner, he was inviting people to the way of Allah ﷻ but they found their idols to be more worthy of worship. They were worshipping those idols and in the end a cleansing occurred. Allah ﷻ cleansed the whole world from nonbelief and from infidels with that flood, all of them perished. Even though humanity was on the right path, after a while people followed shaytan and their ego and everywhere is filled with nonbelief and tyranny again.

In these times also, nonbelief is at its peak and it keeps increasing. Just as you think that it has reached the peak and it can’t go higher, and that you can’t have more evil, rebellion and sinning, you look around and see that it increases more. Things that you couldn’t even imagine are happening. Everything has a maximum peak. Then, for sure Allah ﷻ will hit them and they will be finished. This tyranny, rebellion and sinning filth will end. Now you think it has reached the peak, but we don’t know. Allah ﷻ knows if it has reached the peak or not. Yet when it does, this means that their end is coming; because Nuh/ Noah (as) prayed to Allah ﷻ not to leave even one nonbeliever on this world, so Allah ﷻ perished and finished them all. Allah’s ﷻ promise is the truth. In the same way, not one nonbeliever will be left in this world and Allah ﷻ will perish them all. The Sahib (owner) will come and he will cleanse everybody insha’Allah, do not forget this, Allah’s ﷻ promise is the truth. When Isa/ Jesus Christ (as) and Mahdi (as) come, they will finish all this nonbelief and tyranny. May Allah ﷻ make us reach those beautiful days and make them closer insha’Allah.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq. Al-Fatiha.
