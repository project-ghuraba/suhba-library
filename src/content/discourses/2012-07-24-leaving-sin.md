As-Salamu Alaikum wa Rahmatullahi wa Barakatuhu. Tariqatuna sohbah wal-khayri fil jamia' Madad ya Rasulullah, madad ya S. Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, madad ya S. Muhammad Nazƒ±m al Haqqani, dastur!

We said in yesterday's sohbat that Ramadan Sharif is the month of tawba. Now, it is repenting of their sins. And besides this there is sin & also things with harm in them. When they say "I am repenting of such sins for the honour of this month" Allah helps them & they are saved from that illness also.

What kind of things are they? A lot of people say "we can't quit smoking. What should we do? How do we do it?”. The easiest & most suitable way is to do it in Ramadan. Because you can't smoke in Ramadan, it also breaks the fast. And Ramadan has coincided with the summer. Already days are long. Even countries that have shorter days it is still not less than 16 hours. In Europe daytime can go up to 20 hours. He has only not to poison himself in that 4 hours it would be better & he could also manage not smoking. Smoking is haram because it is also harming the body. They say makruh but when you repeat makruh so many times it is haram.

Of course many people smoke now. Who prays smokes. Who leads the prayer smokes. Hajjis also smoke, saying "it is makruh". But because it is harmful for you, it is a sin. Meaning Allah doesn't say to people "you can smoke poison". If you smoke of course it is sin & harm. You are killing yourself with it, you are falling into sin.

There is this & also there is something else, may Allah protect all of us from it: they get addicted to drinking, become alcoholic. Drinking is also one of the sins, one of the big sins. And before in old times, those who are addicted to alcohol would be ashamed & stop drinking during the 3 holy months, for their honour. Now leave aside the 3 months, they don't even think of not drinking during Ramadan. And for it too, if he quits by repenting of it for the honour of this Ramadan Allah makes it easier for him to quit.

And now there is what is worse than all this. They are after the young ones now. We hear in many places in Europe people use drugs. They either roll it & smoke or by injection. That is also from big sins. Also it is very hard to be saved from it but if he repents starting from Ramadan, he can also be saved from it with Allah's permission. Just like how a person can endure fasting in this heat, with facing difficulties, it means Allah granted man willpower & also Allah is helping us. Now people say, "how to fast in this heat?" Our summer is going easier than for those who are not fasting. Who break their fast, meaning who break their fast with no excuse- he is as healthy as a horse, has no illness no nothing, he breaks his fast, saying "I can't fast". He can drink as much as he wants all day but it won't release the heat nor will it satisfy his thirst. This is also by the Wisdom of Allah. We may be careful about this, making tawba from such harmful things so that we can be saved from it with the barakah of Ramadan.

Today we speak about, yesterday repentance. For what? There is many things for sins you can do, between you & Allah. You can, you repent it for this. Allah forgive you. And there is also between you & Allah but it is harmful for you first. What is this? First of this, cigarette. Cigarette, it is very bad habit. Our mashayekh, they add it is like haram. But common people, they said makruh, meaning less than haram, common people. And all, many people smoke. Hajji smoking, imam smoking, normal who praying smoking.. But it is normally haram because it is poison. And poison when give harm you, this is meaning haram for you. But normal people, they say makruh. Makruh also, when you do it everytime it become like haram.

In this Ramadan month, it is more easy to leave it. Because specially now in summer time, days are very long. Even in this country, 16 hour day. In Europe, 20 or 19 hour day. Only 4 hour. So when you are fasting you cannot smoke. If you smoke, it means you not fasting. So 20 hour you not smoking, only you can eat & sleep little bit & make suhur. So no need for smoking. It is more easy for you to leave this bad habit. And this is barakah of Ramadan. When you repent it in the 1st of Ramadan, "I will not do this again," Allah help you. It will be more easy for you to leave it.

And second, also big sin, to drink alcohol. Old times, and old time what we say before maybe 40 years or 50 years from now and before, when 3 months Rajab, Shaban, Ramadan coming, those who they are alcoholic they not... they leave it 3 months, respecting for these months. Now for Ramadan even, many people they not remember this. This is, when they respect Ramadan and repent "we are not, we will not have it anymore" Allah will help them also. And they leave this bad habit, and bad illness also.

Third one, now everywhere, it is very bad for, especially for young people, they used to have drug. They was asking for Mawlana how to leave it. Mawlana said, making repent and for 40 days not taking, Alhamdulillah not anymore, they not taking anymore this poison. And this is for beginning of Ramadan until 10 of Shawwal. Insha Allah they get rid from this very bad and, very bad and it is not suitable for Muslim people. Because Muslim people, they believe in Allah & believe in akhirah. So they know, Allah will ask them about what they do for this. Why they do this & this is not good for Muslim & making everybody miserable. And Islam, you are from family & from jamaat- jamaat/community. Everybody they look to each other. But in Europe or other they don't have this.

I see it many times. They have given freedom. This is in Islam, or in Muslim community, not totally freedom. There is limit for freedom also. You cannot do anything, thing harm family & harm community. So for this, insha Allah this talimat/instruction from Mawlana, to how to leave this bad illness. Everybody they were asking before when I was in Europe. Many people they ask how they will leave this illness. So insha Allah from now & until 40 days if they do this, insha Allah never again they will have this poison.

For Allah and for Sayyidina Muhammad (sas) & for Mawlana S. Abdullah F. Daghestani, M. S. Muhammad Nazim Haqqani, for awliya Allah make them away from this illness.

Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, Bi Hurmat Al Fatiha.
