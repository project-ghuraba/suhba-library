As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh. A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says, “Yurīdu Llāhu an yukhaffifa ‘ankum, wa-khuliqa l-insānu ḍa‘īfa” (4:28). Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says that HeNone created mankind weak. HeNone desires to lighten mankind’s burden. With the commands given by AllāhNone, the burden becomes lighter. That is how a person strengthens their weakness. When they are with Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, their weakness turns into strength. But when they oppose Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, their strength becomes weakness. They become weak and of no benefit. Human beings are the weakest of all creatures in terms of physical body. Any animal, insect, or other creature can do far more than a person can. For example, even if you think about a simple ant, it can probably carry something ten times heavier than itself. If a person were to carry even half of their own weight, they could not walk five hundred metres. That ant, with its body, compared to a human, would be as if it walked a hundred kilometres a day. AllāhNone gave it such strength. Yet HeNone created mankind weak. If you look at other animals, it’s the same with them. Whether running, jumping, or leaping – in all such things, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gave them strength. But HeNone created mankind weak. Yet, though created weak, they still ruin everything. HisNone wisdom cannot be questioned. If mankind had been created like them, with such strength given, no one would remain. No humans, no jinn, no animals would remain on this earth. That is why Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla created mankind weak – but gave them intellect instead. If they use that weakness wisely, it benefits them. But most people use their intellect not for good, but for evil and harm. And so the world has reached its end. Unless someone saves them, people will devour one another. But thanks to AllāhNone – Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla – to the person who is Muslim: “Lā taqnaṭū min raḥmati Llāh” (39:53). “Do not despair of Allāh’s mercy.” So, thanks be to AllāhNone, our hope lies in the true word of our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam: “In the end times, one from my lineage will emerge and cleanse the whole world.” He will establish justice. All this filth and corruption will be purified. No more problems. No more wars or battles. No more evil, no more perversion. That time is surely near, in shā’a Llāh. For our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam had the power to foresee the future and share its news. HeNone gave us this news. Most has come true. Only a little remains. In shā’a Llāh, we will reach that time. It won’t be long. For though we say tyranny has reached its peak, it keeps rising higher and higher. But no matter how high it rises, every rise has its fall. And that will come, in shā’a Llāh. May AllāhNone grant us those blessed days soon, in shā’a Llāh.
