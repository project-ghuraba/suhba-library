As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Quli Llāhu thumma dharhum fī khawḍihim yal‘abūn" (6:91). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘aẓīm. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says in Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n: "Say: AllāhNone. The rest may go on with their games. Let them play their games." It is important not to obey them, not to be with those who don't say AllāhNone. Because this life is a game. No matter how seriously they take what they do, or think highly of it, it is nothing but a game. People who spend their lives in games and entertainment, but don't remember AllāhNone, are useless. They don't do anything useful. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla makes us do what HeNone wants. No one can do anything on their own. Unless Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla wills it, it is impossible.

As the time we are living in is the end of times, there are all kinds of new knowledge. And people act arrogantly, saying they invented this and discovered that. But they forget Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. They don't make dhikr of AllāhNone. "Say: AllāhNone," says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Who says AllāhNone is in profit. You cannot attain anything with these games and entertainment, with the goods of this world, whatever they are. You can't reach anything. And nothing brings benefit. As long as you don't say AllāhNone, this world is empty. When a believer says AllāhNone, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla shows mercy to him without needing a reason. HeNone favours him and makes his ākhirah prosperous. And his dunyā is in comfort and peace, in shā’a Llāh.

It is an important thing. It is the order of AllāhNone. Say: AllāhNone. "Quli Llāh." It is the order. We must always say AllāhNone. May we not take the dhikr of AllāhNone out of our hearts. And may we always remember Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla with our tongues, in shā’a Llāh.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying in Qur’ān, "Quli Llāhu thumma dharhum fī khawḍihim yal‘abūn" (6:91). Order from AllāhNone, "Say: AllāhNone." Say AllāhNone whole time. Say AllāhNone. And let this people with enjoying, playing, in this life, they are forgetting the most important thing [which they] must not forget. For this, if you forget Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, whole your life playing, enjoying... not serious thing. What you [do] in this life, like playing. Some, many people, they take it very serious. And they thought they invent invention, something. But all this Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, without HisNone will, nothing can be appear. And since thousands of year, AllāhNone give slow, little bit, little knowledge for people. But now we are living end of time. The knowledge and many thing making people to be proud, to be not accept Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. "We do this. We are doing this." But when, if they are looking seriously, they will find who give this for them: Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. No, not from their mind. Many thing you cannot imagine to be, by human being, to find this. If AllāhNone not give them idea and to do this... Even they can have idea, but if no will of AllāhNone, it will not work.

For this, who are clever, saying whole time, AllāhNone. Not with, for, playing. This the most serious thing. It is not a play, to be with AllāhNone. Whole time to remember HimNone. Everything you do, if you remember HimNone, you will be happy here and hereafter. This is the most important thing. And people, the most of people, they are not thinking at all about this. They thought – They are very astonished with new thing. Every time, every day, they bring for market new car, new clothes, new computer or something. They are not thinking, this, from where coming? Or only, they so happy with new thing, and they thought it is good to have this.

But the good thing: to remember Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. If you remember, you will be not in, like place for playing. You will be serious and you will be forever happy. But if only for playing here and not thinking, it is, will be no benefit at all. AllāhNone keep us with HimNone and make HisNone... We mentioning HimNone whole time, to be in our heart, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
