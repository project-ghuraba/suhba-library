Madad Ya Rasulullah, Mada Ya Ashab-e Rasulullah, Madad Ya Mashayikihina. Dastur. ------------------------------------------------------------------

Holy Prophet (saw) is saying, “There will be people from my ummah who will deny the destiny”. People who do not believe in destiny will be within ummah.

Shaytan is bothering everyone. He can mislead anyone. Therefore, we must believe in destiny. ------------------------------------------------------------------

Holy Prophet (saw) is saying, “When the day of resurrection comes, a caller will address the people with a loud voice and say, “Let the enemies of Allah rise up. They are qadariyyah”.” There is a group called qadariyyah. They are the people who left the right way of Islam. They are the enemies of Allah. There are many groups that lost their ways. Qadariyyah is one of them. ------------------------------------------------------------------

Holy Prophet (saw) is saying, “There are two groups in my ummah. They are not from Islam”. They are from ummah but they are out of Islam.

“One is murji’ah and another is qadariyyah”. They explain everything as they like for their own benefit. But they have no benefit, only harm. ------------------------------------------------------------------

Holy Prophet (saw) is saying, “There are two groups in my ummah. My intercession will not cover them on the day of resurrection”. Muslims who believe in Holy Prophet (saw) have the right for his intercession. And these people are out of Islam. Therefore, there is no intercession for these two groups.

“They are murji’ah and qadariyyah”. ------------------------------------------------------------------

Holy Prophet (saw) is saying, “There are two groups in my ummah. They will not reach my pool and will not enter paradise. They are murji’ah and qadariyyah”. Murji’ah people claim they do things because they have to. They do all kinds of badness and do not take the blame for it. They say Allah made them do so. Together with qadariyyah, they will not enter paradise. They are always around even in the present time. There are many kinds of them; some are aware of themselves and some are not. May Allah protect everyone. ------------------------------------------------------------------

Holy Prophet (saw) is saying, “Qadariyyah people are Zoroastrians of my ummah”. Zoroastrians mean who are not from people of the Book, they are worse than unbelievers.

“Do not visit them when they are sick. Do not attend their funerals when they die.” ------------------------------------------------------------------

Holy Prophet (saw) is saying, “Qadariyyah people are cursed by seventy Prophets”. They had existed before Holy Prophet (saw) and it is such a bad thing. They are soldiers of shaytan. Seventy Prophets cursed them. ------------------------------------------------------------------

Holy Prophet (saw) is saying, “Every nation has a Zoroastrian group. Zoroastrians of my ummah are those who claim there is no destiny”. According to the description of Holy Prophet (saw), Zoroastrians are the worst people in a nation because they do not have conscience.

“Do not visit them when they are sick. Do not attend their funerals when they die”. No need to communicate with them at all. May Allah protect, these people do all kinds of dangerous things in order to obtain their interest. They interfere in useless things. People can claim such and worse things in order to gather more people. May Allah protect everyone. ------------------------------------------------------------------

Holy Prophet (saw) is saying, “Do not sit together with qadariyyah people and do not open this matter with them”. Do not be with qadariyyah group. The matter of destiny is as forbidden as to ask about the souls, how it is and what it is. If you sit with them, they will pass sickness into your heart. You will be harmed. That is why do not ever sit together with them. Trading with them is different. But do not ever sit and talk; do not make companionship with them.

Sadaqa RasuluLlah fima qal aw kama qal.
