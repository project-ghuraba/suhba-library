Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

A’ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm:

(Qur’ān 06:132). Allah says: ‘For all there will be ranks from what they did.’ Allah Azza wa Jalla created each person in a different rank from the other. Some people are higher by the favor of Allah. These are the prophets. The highest is our Holy Prophet. Then rank after rank, comes the companions, scholars, Mashayikh and true believers. They are higher than other people. With what are they higher? With money or authority? No, by faith and by closeness to Allah. That is the real ascension, otherwise there is no benefit.

The state of this world is a state of tests. Some will be high, some will be low and some will be totally low. But the important thing is to be with the great ones. To follow them is Falāḥ. What is meant by Falāḥ is salvation, goodness. Thank Allah we are with Mawlana Sheikh Nazim. We will be reunited with him, and we will be together in heaven insha’Allah. It’s been six years. The years go by, and this world gets mixed up. Our final destination is clear. The safe harbor is where we’re going. That’s why, there is no fear. This world can shake as much as it wants, where we will go is clear. When we are with Allah, with the prophets and with the Awliya, we have no fear and no need to worry.

The things that our Holy Prophet said and what the Mashayikh told will happen. The program is given, but it does not happen on a day-to-day basis. However, it will eventually happen as Allah wills. The things that our Holy Prophet said will appear, and the Qiyāmah will come. This world is not forever. This is a timed place. That’s why, a believer or a Muslim should not worry, and should keep his faith strong. May Allah protect us, and give victory to Islam insha’Allah. May the unbelievers be defeated and the oppressors be defeated insha’Allah. This is a blessed month, a beautiful month, may we all have strong faith for its honor insha’Allah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
