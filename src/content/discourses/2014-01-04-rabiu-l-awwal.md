Who are we? We are zero people. Janab-ul Haqq is sending. May our Lord, our Subhan, our Sultan give you strength. May He dress you in majesty, in the bliss of love, my son. May Allah bring you to a good end. May you carry the flag of the Prophet. May you live in health, you and your father. MashaAllah, may your descendants to seven generations be loaded with the bliss of love. Fatiha.

We ask for the love of heavens. Don't look at the one who gives, look at the One who lets give. Give. The rest will follow. May we walk not for dunya but for Allah. Read on me a little so that I can behave next to the lions. Live long, live long, live long. Ar-Rahim and Ar-Rahman is Janab-ul Mawla. Who are we? We are nothing. He is letting us give for our own grace. Give. Give. Even if there's no one looking, when they look into your face, give. Our Allah, they are Your treasures. How beautiful! How beautiful! How beautiful! All ugliness will be turned into beauty.

Don't forget, read Qur'an-i Kareem. Pay attention to Qur'an-i Kareem. Your reading it is good. I love him, I love all of them, I love all of them. But he looks a little like my father Mustafa. I love him too much. May it open. With the honor of this holy month, may it open for you, my son. May it open for the nation of our Master. May his enemies be cursed.

The month of Rabbi-ul Awwal arrived with honor. It arrived with a power, which can destroy all darkness for the sake of the Glorious Prophet. It will touch us too, it will be enough, inshaAllah.

We hope that cure comes to the sick ones. We hope that hope comes to the hopeless ones. We hope that the pleasure of servanthood to their Lord comes to those who can't find pleasure in anything, to all of us. May we be like lions. May we be servants to our Lord, may we be servants to His Beloved. Allah is the One giving beauty to our faces. Subhan Allah, Sultan Allah. Rabi-ul Awwal, a holy month, a very beautiful month has started. Power is coming now, it will destroy. A power which will destroy the sultanate of shaitan is descending.

O Shah Mardan, your lovers adore you. MashaAllahu kana wa ma lam yasha lam yakun. Al-Fatiha from the seven heavens.

Let's say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. As-salamu alaikum wa rahmatullahi wa barakatuh. May Allah be pleased. All right, my son. As-salamu alaikum. See, it's opening slowly, slowly. Rabi-ul Awwal, a holy month, will come slowly, slowly now. O Sham...
