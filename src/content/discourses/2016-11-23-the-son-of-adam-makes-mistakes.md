Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Our Holy Prophet (SAW) says: “Kullu’bni adama khatta’un.”

That is to say all people, meaning sons of Adam, can make mistakes. People should repent if they unknowingly or knowingly make a mistake. It continues: “wakhairul khatta’een at-tawaboon.”

“The best of those who make mistakes are the repenters.” Allah accepts the repentance of those who know their mistake and repent. Some people unknowingly say wrong things. It is alright if they repent from these. Then there are people, non-Arabs, who when reading the Koran cannot pronounce the words exactly or it comes out differently. Allah Azza wa Jalla made it easy for them too. There are angels whom Allah Azza wa Jalla assigned to them. The angels correct them if they pronounce incorrectly. This way it rises to the fold of Allah correctly.

Some nations cannot pronounce certain letters and others say it differently. There are some foolish people who make everything difficult. They insist, “You must say it like this.” But they cannot say it. Even Hazrat Bilal Habashi (RA) could not pronounce some words and our Holy Prophet (SAW) would not say a thing. He could not pronounce a letter having come from Habashistan. To make it easier on the Ummat and as an example to others our Holy Prophet (SAW) did not say a thing to him. The angels would correct him.

So as long as a person’s intentions are sincere, Allah (JJ) provides all kinds of help to the person. He helps with angels too and makes everything be utilized for them. The beloved servants of Allah are always with Allah. Their intentions are pure, meaning their mouths talk beautiful words and their inside is not dirty. People whose mouth talks beautiful words and their heart is full of all fitna are useless. What is important is for our insides, our hearts, to be clean and to be connected to Allah. The rest is not important. May Allah make everything be utilized, and accept our worships and actions Inshallah.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq. Al-Fatiha.
