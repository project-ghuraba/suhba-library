Sheikh Muhammad Nazim Al-Haqqani An-Naqshibandi, Sohbat of the 1st of June, 1984.

Tariqat means to be a person awakened every moment so that devils cannot cheat him. Tariqat giving a person yakaza. Yakaza - awakened. Who making dhikr always going to be awakened. Who forgetting dhikr, who forgetting his Lord, he may be cheated, may be cheated by devils quickly. Therefore, who asking tariqat, asking to be awakened always with dhikr, with his Lord Almighty. When his tongue is stopping on dhikr, his heart going on dhikr. When heart is going with dhikr, every organ and every cell that blood runs in it, blood making dhikr and that cell making dhikr. Therefore, this going to make dhikr, that is Sultan-udh-dhikr. Sultan-udh-dhikr. You may listen whole your body making dhikr. Who may listen his body's dhikr and his heart's dhikr, listening whole universe's dhikr. He is going in dhikr. That is Sultan-udh dhikr.

Therefore, who understanding that wisdom, that reality from dhikr, going on. Who they are not understanding, they are fighting (those) who making dhikr. Who fights mureed, who fighting tariqats, they're ignorant people never knowing what is the purpose from dhikr. Therefore, you asking now for dhikr, for tariqat, and tariqat coming through Prophet, peace be upon him, to Siddiq and to Sayiddina Ali also. Naqshbandi tariqat taking dhikr from Abu Bakr as-Siddiq (ra), Allah blesses him. And other 40 tariqats taking from Sayiddina Ali, karamAllahu wajha wa radiAllahu anhu. Allah blesses him. Now we are saying for Naqshbandi:

Audhu billahi min-ash shaitani r-rajim. Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. ina lladhina yubay'unaka innama yubay'una Allah yadu Llahi fawqa aydihim faman nakatha fa innama yankuthu ala nafsah faman awfa bima ahad alayh Allaha sayu'tiah ajran adhima sadaqa Llah Rabbuna 'ala radhina biLlahi Rabban wa bil Islami dinan wa bi-sayyidina Muhammad sallAllahu alayhi was sallam rasulan wa nabiyan wa radhina bisayyidina Shaykh Abdullah ad- Daghestani shaykhan lana wa murshidan wa imaman wa qidwatan ila Llahi ta'ala wa Llaha 'ala ma naqulu wakil hasbuna Allah wa ni'ma wakil ila sharifi n-Nabiyi sallAllahu alayhi wa sallam wa alihi wa ashbihi l-kiram ikhwanihi┬ámin al-anbiya'i wal mursalina wa khudamayi sharayihim ila arwahi a'imatil arbaa wa ila arwahi mashaikhina fit-tariqatin-Naqshbandiyatil-aliya khasatan mina r-ruh imamati t-tariqa wa ghawth il-khaliqa Khwaja Bahauddin Naqshband Muhammad al-Uways al-Bukhari wa hadratil ustadhina sultanul awliya Abdullahi Daghestani ustadh ustadhina Abdul Khaliq al- Gujdawani was-sidiqiun al-Fatiha

And there is our brother, as we met him on the Day of Promises now we are meeting with him. As we promised to him for tariqat, now it is going on also. And we are saying for him all of us, ash'hadu 'an la ilaha illa Allah, wa sh'hadu anna Muhammadan abduhu wa rasuluh (saw). awdana hadhihi-l kalimataini shahidatain 'indaka ya RasuluLlah wa hiyalana wadiatun yawm ul qiyamat ya man arsalahu Llah bita'ala rahmatan lil alamin amin bi hurmati l-Fatiha - BismiLlahir Rahmanir Rahim...

And he has 7 names in the Day of Promises. One of them is Abdul Malik. Good name, Allah Almighty giving him Abdul Malik. Alhamdulillah. Allah granting us from His endless favours during this Ramadan. We hope to be during Ramadan opening in our hearts, heavenly openings and heavenly wisdoms go through our hearts which makes us to approach Divine Presence. Holy month that we are asking from our Lord to make us to approach nearby from His Divine Presence. Make your heart with dhikr to approach to his Lord. When you are approaching, He coming on you, Allah Almighty. No need to come, to go but He making for us for understanding. When we are going to Him, He is coming to us, Allah Almighty. Every dhikr, every holy word, that Holy Name that we are speaking making us to approach, to come nearer. Allah Almighty making so many ways for His servant to make it easy for approaching to His Divine Presence. It is not only one way. So many ways you may approach, you may go in Divine Presence. Every word or every breath you are repeating making you to approach from Divine Presence. If anyone getting in, he is not asking to come out. Finished.

Entering, for example, who coming this world born, looking this, asking to enter in, say, mother's womb? Never. No one can say this in this big universe. And I'm telling him - womb, that he come out? Never. Who coming in Divine Presence, if you giving your all treasures, even whole universe, He is not leaving and coming out. Not this world, even whole paradise He gives to be in Divine Presence. Therefore, Friday - Juma, He is yawm-ul mazid. Yawm-ul mazid every Friday in paradise. Allah Almighty inviting all people in paradise to come and to look Divinely Face. When His glory curtains opening and people looking, they are going with themself. They are forgetting whole paradise, never asking anything except mushahada to look, gone away. Then comes this huzub-ul Izza wal Azama, then people coming back to themselves. They are going with their Lord. Then Allah Almighty leaving them to come back to their, back to their bodies for enjoying with paradise. When they are looking, they are forgetting everything. No value for anything when you are looking your Lord's face, Divinely face.

We are trying little by little to approach to His Divinely entrance. We're asking when we are going to find it opened for us, not closing and saying "Go away. Whole life you are not asking us, go away." But who asking his Lord, that door's opening. And say "Welcome, My servant. Come to Me." And we are going. Never you can find yourself - there, finish.
