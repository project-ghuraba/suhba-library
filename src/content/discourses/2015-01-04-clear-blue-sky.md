It is important things to understand. Everything it comes from understanding, everything. Whatever you have it's coming from understanding. If you don't have understanding, you don't have nothing. It is so simple. If you understand you have so many things. But if you don't understand, nothing. Some child they go for school. The first year they say "Yeah he is lazy, we push him for the second class." And then in the 2nd class they say "O maybe he has to repeat the 2nd class." So he repeats the 2nd class But the teacher will be fed up with him and will push him for the 3rd class. Just to not make him embarrassed against the other children, all the young ones are coming. Because he cannot understand. You cannot do anything against. If he understands he will be going in the 10th class. But because he doesn't understand he is stuck in the 5th class. And they send him home, saying "This, nothing here, no understanding."

Ya Rabbi, we want to be from the ones that understand. We want to understand the love of Sheikh, first. We want to understand the love of RasulAllah, second. We want to understand for the love of Allah Almighty, and the most important. But we have to climb the stairs step by step, step by step. Nobody can be in the 10th step in one go. Even the tallest man, even the strongest man, he has to go step by step. This is nice teaching. We are looking for the smooth and nice teaching. Mawlana Sheikh, he was huge ocean. He was a huge ocean of knowledge. But he never put this ocean in one go on us. He was not grounding us by his knowledge. You know sometime you go in front of knowledgeable people or clever people, or smart people and they make you to feel really stupid. They make you feel you are really stupid. And you'll be very angry! "What he is saying? I am a smart one. And I am a stupid one?" Make you very down, when they see you are stupid, they ground you more, and more and more..."Come on I'm a small boy! What you are doing to me?" No, they don't have this mercy because it's coming as very big ego - "My ego is bigger than you, so I have to ground you."

Mawlana Sheikh, Subhana Allah, he was saying "Poor children let me feed them slowly, slowly. Let me give them slowly. Let me make them to understand. Even they don't understand, let me make them to understand very patient, very patient." And that's why everyone of us thinks and feels that Mawlana Sheikh he is only his Sheikh; that Mawlana S. only looking for him. That Mawlana S. only caring about him. That Mawlana S. he is only seeing his murid. And this is good feeling for us as a murid. Say "Mawlana Sheikh is my Sheikh, Alhamdulillah!" It's a good feeling. It's not a bad feeling but don't take it too much for your egoistic side. Say, "I am the one!" Also dangerous. Also dangerous. Mawlana Sheikh he was equal to everybody and he was giving slowly slowly, with the patience, with love, with care. You know when you have small child in your house you run after him to feed him. He doesn't care. You care to feed him. You want to feed him otherwise he will stay small, he will stay not grown up. And you know you're aware about this. And Mawlana Sheikh he was in this situation exactly. He was running after us, to feed us, to put in our mouth and to look after us and to spoil us.

We've been spoiled badly. Dr. we've been spoiled badly, you know that! Mawlana Sheikh he was a great personality that he was able to look after us as spoiled children. Some of us they still continue where they are, as they've been spoiled by Sheikh. No, don't be spoiled, come back to reality. This is the reality, now we're grown up. When Mawlana Sheikh he is in his maqam, we grown up. Now we have to act as grown up child. There is now responsibility. When your child become 9, 10, you don't run after him to feed him anymore. He says "I'm hungry, I'm hungry, I'm hungry..." you come. Now we are in this age, we are 10, 11, not more. We cannot be more than this. It is realistic, we must understand. This is why I say if you understand you'll be happy. If you don't understand you will not be happy. There's some child he goes in front of mirror and he takes his father shave and he starts to shave and all the blood coming from here. Say "What you're doing?" Say "I shave like my father." This is not the way, you have to wait for your time, to beard coming out, then you start to shave it or trim it or to.. to be a big boy, or young boy, or young guy.

This is must be understood - Where are you, who are you. What are you. Where are you. Today is the day of "who are you? How are you?" Questioning day. We are at the stage where we start to have responsibility. This is for all the murids, it is not for me or for one in particular, no. We have to understand that every one of us is a flag of Mawlana Sheikh, that's very important. Every one of us is a flag of Mawlana Sheikh, we are around the world. Alhamdulillah we are distributing the love of Mawlana Sheikh. We are distributing the humanity of Mawlana Sh. If we can catch one, Mawlana Sheikh will be very proud of us because we are giving the true love, we want to give the true love. We want to give the good things for humanity because Mawlana Sheikh he was all his life trying to give this, and he give it! And he distribute it and he was so generous to give this. This is how Mawlana Sheikh he was doing it. And this is how Mawlana Sheikh he was really not playing with this mission. It was not a joke for him, it was a mission.

And now when I look around the world so many people. I see Mawlana Sheikh in each of you, every one is Mawlana Sheikh. Everybody is shining as Mawlana Sheikh. This is coming from Rasulallah (saws). It's coming bom, bom, bom...and it ends up in everybody, in everybody. Mawlana S. he never made discrimination, never! He was against it, he was hating it. And he was saying "All of you my jewelry." This all the time saying it. "My jewelry, my weath." Mawlana Sheikh he never keep any dunyaness, never! And he didn't like it and he didn't want it and whatever come to him, he was immediately distributing. Immediately distributing. Immediately distributing, saying "O this is burden!" It is burden, my real weath it is my murids. This is my real wealth, this is I'm proud that I collect this to put in front of Rasulallah (saws)." Say "Ya Rasulallah all my wealth is for you!" This is how Mawlana Sheikh was, and he is, and still going on. "This is my wealth, but it's for you Ya Rasulallah!" Never say, claim "mine" no. "I'm collecting this for Rasulallah. I'm collecting this for Rasulallah."

This reminds me with olden days when Mawlana Sheikh he was going around the countries and then he come to Damascus to Grand Sheikh and when he was going, he was having some gift, sadaqa, zakat, hadiya. Things they were giving him. And Mawlana Sh because he was coming from wealthy family he didn't take he was saying "no, no I don't." But Grand Sheikh ordering him, "No, your hand must be like this. One for taking and one for giving, no for keeping." No for keeping. Mawlana Sheikh was like this. And then, he was coming to Grand Sheikh and he was like having maybe 20 pockets, minimum. And he was taking from this pocket, putting. Take from this pocket, putting. And he was putting everything, finish. Nothing left. And saying "Ya Sayyidi, this I brought for you. I'm working for you. I am for you Ya Sayyidi! I don't work for myself." Put 70% on this side. 30% for the Sheikh. Or 90% for this side and 10% for the Sheikh. Everything he was. And he was taking the pocket outside to see if anything.. because those days poor people they give also pennies and he was looking if there's any pennies left or any coins left. Even those he was putting there. Ya'ni tamamin, absolute to be empty. Absolute to be for Sheikh.

And then the Sheikh say "Masha'Allah, Alhamdulillah. Nazim Effendi!" He take some of this money and say "Take this one for your house." Did you see Mawlana Sheikh, whatever he give, he doesn't care. It's Mawlana Sheikh, so huge personality. Once, coming and Alhamdulillah we've been honored to be with him. We've been honored to look to his face, we've been honored to kiss his hands and feet. And to be with him, now it's the time that Mawlana Sheikh it is in you. It is in everybody of you. It is he is you now. This is Mawlana Sheikh and all of us we are flag. Now that's why I'm saying the responsibility starts. It is our responsibility to make Mawlana Sheikh flag higher and higher and higher. He make the foundation. He put the foundation for us. Now, we are only making the decoration. We don't have anything to do because the foundation is strong. When you have strong foundation anything you can build. Anything! They say, some people they find foundation and they build paper house. No, we don't want to build paper house. We want to build solid house.

So many people they try to put few things to change the mind of people, to make the mind of people like this, like that according to their knowledge or according to their thoughts or according to their benefit. We are here, not looking for benefit. We don't care about benefit. Specially dunya benefit, it is not our things. We are caring about reality, we are caring about the realistic mission of Mawlana Sheikh. If, I'm asking from you "bring me money." Then I'm looking for my own benefit. If I'm asking to get help from you, and also I'm looking for my own benefit. No, the Sheikh duty is to help. The Sheikh duty is to look after his murid. The Sheikh duty is to run for the murid's problem, for the murids suffering, or for the murid happiness. Have to run, have to be with the murid, in good day and in bad day. He has to be with them, this is his family, this is him.

That's why when Mawlana Sheikh he put Sheikh Muhammad, Sheikh Mehmet, to be our Sheikh, he know that this Sheikh he doesn't have any benefit among (to get from) the murid. He knows that he is the one running for murid. He is the one who will continue as Mawlana Sheikh, giving. That's important, important for us because so many people they say "confusing." No it's not confusing. No confusion, sach masapa. Confusion it is for people they have some kind of benefit. They make it dusty and they hunt. Mawlana Sheikh never make it dusty. Mawlana Sheikh make it clear - like blue sky. When you look for the sky, blue. There is no dust, fantastic! This is Mawlana Sheikh.

Mawlana Sheikh never throw anybody from Tariqa. Never threatened anybody "I will throw you from Tariqa." If you don't do this, you are out of Tariqa. Never.
