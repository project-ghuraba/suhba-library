As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh we have reached to this day, shukr to AllāhNone. Tonight is the holy night, in shā’a Llāh. It is the night of miracles of our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. It is the night which is worth thousands of years. Events that took place within two hours of worldly time would require thousands of years for us, to travel to and see those places. Certainly, it happened especially for our Holy ProphetNone. But its barakah came to his ummah as well. On this night our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam travelled from Makkah to Jerusalem and visited the other Prophets there. HeNone visited the holy places, and then ascended to the heavens.

There, at last, heNone went to the presence of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, in a way known to Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. It was a gathering, or an association, that our minds cannot comprehend. There is always an association. It is impossible without an association. A favour was granted to our Holy ProphetNone and as a present to hisNone ummah as well. This favour is an important thing: the order for the ummah to pray. They used to pray before as well. But it was not five times a day. It was made farḍ to the ummah on that night, as a pillar of Islām. Fasting was also practiced, but that was made farḍ in Madīnah. Same with ḥajj. There was no zakāh then. They were making shahādah as farḍ. They used to pray. But the prayer was ordered and made farḍ for the ummah on Laylatu l-Isrā’ wa-l-Mi‘rāj, as a present for them.

So, praying is the most valuable pillar of the religion. And praying was ordered to our Holy ProphetNone by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, directly without any intermediary. Jibrīl ‘alayhi s-salām used to go between them. It was ordered directly to pray fifty times a day. Fifty times would be worshipping without stopping; it means mankind was created to worship. We would pray fifty times a day. But as the ummah of Sayyidunā Mūsá ‘alayhi s-salām couldn’t carry it, through Sayyidunā Mūsá ‘alayhi s-salām, the mercy of AllāhNone came. Our Holy ProphetNone couldn’t say it was too little or too much in front of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, because of hisNone adab. So when Sayyidunā Mūsá said it was too much and the ummah could not carry it...

Certainly, many of us know this story of our Holy ProphetNone. I should tell it, nevertheless. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla reduced the prayers to forty-five times. Later, when told that was too much, it became forty. Sayyidunā Mūsá ‘alayhi s-salām told our Holy ProphetNone thirty-five, thirty, twenty-five, twenty, fifteen and then even five was too much. It was reduced to five. Even for five times, Sayyidunā Mūsá ‘alayhi s-salām told our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam it was too much. Our Holy ProphetNone said, “No. If we ask to reduce the five times, there will be nothing. There will be no barakah and no beauty, no pillar of the religion then.” Sayyidunā Mūsá ‘alayhi s-salām knew that everyone now would see praying as difficult. If they pray two raka‘āt, even that seems hard. We sometimes tell people to pray two raka‘āt. They say, “We pray two raka‘āt once during Jum‘ah.”

Therefore, these five times a day are a gift from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla to people. It is the greatest favour, the greatest goodness to people from all aspects, spiritually and physically. Its every movement, every part of it, is thawāb, barakah, and goodness for people. Those who skip praying take on a big responsibility. They deprive themselves of great benefit. They are in loss. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is the one who created everything. Does HeNone need your imperfect prayer? No. Even if you pray imperfectly, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla accepts it. But HeNone doesn’t need you to pray. HeNone has ordered it for your own good. And the prayer was ordered from the highest, the most exalted place to our Holy ProphetNone, directly without intermediary by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. That’s why praying is a very important thing.

And then, in addition to this, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla granted many valuable gifts to the ummah. There were also the last two āyāt of Sūratu l-Baqarah, Āmana r-Rasūlu. They were not sent through Jibrīl ‘alayhi s-salām but came directly from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. In one of these āyāt, it is said, “Lā yukallifu Llāhu nafsan illā wus‘ahā” (2:286). AllāhNone didn’t give any commands that people cannot carry. People can do all of them. People can easily do what Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla ordered. And if it was fifty prayers a day, people would still be able to do it. There is no obstacle to it. But out of mercy, it was reduced to five times. If AllāhNone orders something, HeNone orders things we can carry. HeNone does not order what we cannot do.

Therefore, may this night be blessed for all of us. In shā’a Llāh, may we pay attention to our praying. It is a gift from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla to our Holy ProphetNone and to hisNone ummah. There cannot be a greater gift. Praying is a pillar of the religion. It is important. How lucky are those who do it. And those who miss it, if they make it up later they are saved. But its virtue is not even a thousandth part of what it would have been. If they pray all their lives to make up for that missed prayer, they won’t reach the same reward, says our Holy ProphetNone. That is why we have to be careful. We have to pay attention to our prayers. Ḥajj, zakāh and other things became farḍ afterwards. The first farḍ after bearing witness that Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is one, is the prayer.

Praying is very important. Be careful. Don’t miss it. Do it on time. Unless you have to... That is, AllāhNone forgives when you have to skip it, or when you imitate other madhhab. But only when it can’t be avoided. Imitating other madhhab does not mean choosing the easy way and skipping the prayers. When it is necessary while you are travelling, when you have no other option, it is fine to imitate. In shā’a Llāh, AllāhNone will forgive. But otherwise, it is not right. May AllāhNone make this night full of barakah for all of us. May it be blessed and good, in shā’a Llāh.
