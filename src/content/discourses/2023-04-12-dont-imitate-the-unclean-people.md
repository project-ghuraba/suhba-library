As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh. A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Ẓahara l-fasādu fī l-barri wa-l-baḥri bi-mā kasabat aydī n-nās" (30:41). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘aẓīm.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is saying both on land and in the sea there is corruption. It is spoiled. Because of the badness and sins of people, there is no goodness on land or in the sea. Everything is ruined. They have destroyed it. Mankind has destroyed this world. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says they did it with their hands. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla granted this world to us clean and beautiful, but mankind has ruined it by doing badness continuously. As they have ruined it, of course, the world will have its end. It will be cleaned with Qiyāmah.

Certainly, first of all, Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām will come and clean it. However, mankind is used to comitting sins. In the end times of Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām and ‘Īsá ‘alayhi s-salām, people will start doing evil again. The world will once again become dirty and then only with Qiyāmah will it be cleaned. It will be completely clean. Whoever is meant to go to paradise, will go to paradise and who is meant for hell, will go to hell. Therefore, cleanliness is among the conditions of īmān, faith. Without cleanliness, there is no īmān. None of your deeds will be accepted. A prayer is not valid without ablution and it must be the best of cleanliness in everything. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gave you good things. Ask for good things and do good things. Even if others don't do so, it is still useful for you to do it, and those who don't do it are in loss. They will carry its punishment themselves. Let them think about their own ending.

Everyone is responsible for their own condition and situation. Everyone says, “This person is not doing it, so I won't do it either." Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says in Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n, "Wa-lā taziru wāziratun wizra ukhrá" (17:15). No one is responsible for others. No one should think that because someone else is not doing something, that he can get away with not doing it as well. You will be saved if you do it. If you don't do it, you will not be saved, just like him. May AllāhNone protect us from the evil of our egos, from the evil of people. Because dirtiness is not just in one thing, it is everywhere, even in matters of morality.

Mankind has reached the peak of dirtiness. In relations, in shopping – everywhere. People have neither conscience nor faith anymore. Therefore, don't do something just because others do it. Those who were aboard the ark of Nūḥ ‘alayhi s-salām were saved. Even if they were few, they were saved. The majority were unbelievers, they were bad people. Those who were with them were destroyed too, and only a few people were saved. Therefore, don't think you will be saved by doing what the majority does. Be on the way of AllāhNone. Be with AllāhNone, and you will be saved. May AllāhNone protect us.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says in the Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n, "Ẓahara l-fasādu fī l-barri wa-l-baḥri bi-mā kasabat aydī n-nās" (30:41).

AllāhNone saying, there in land and sea, everywhere is spoiled. The human beings, they destroy and spoil land and sea – water. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is saying this, for AllāhNone is Qadīm (Eternal). Qur’ān, it is not new, but when coming to Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, since one thousand four hundred years ago, saying about these days which we are living now. Everywhere, dirty. Fasād meaning dirty; spoiled meaning dirty. And Islām, religion of clean, pureness. Not accept dirty. If without cleanness, you cannot be doing anything from what AllāhNone order us. So, it is: "An-nazāfah niṣfu l-īmān, sharṭu l-īmān." Cleanness, it is half of īmān, belief. Without this, you cannot do anything. For this, Muslim people must be looking for themselves, not imitate people who are not clean. Without clean, they destroy whole world. And there is people, they are claiming they want to clean earth from pollution, from dirtiness. But this, all their people, they are hypocrites because they have more dirtiness than dirty what they, in earth. They are very hypocrite people. Don't believe them because they are against Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Just they thought they're doing good, but really they are not doing anything good. All what sin, make this world more dirty and more dirty and more dirty. Not clean place anymore.

From what human being done, said Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, become dirty this world. Because, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone gave us this world very clean, very nice. Everything you can drink and eat you can... Now, you cannot drink from any river from earth. All river, pollution. Sea even, the whole oceans, seas, also dirty. All this coming from dirtiness of kufr, non-believer. AllāhNone can make it clean, but HeNone said from this, what done people, become dirty. And don't imitate them. You must be clean. Don't say, “This not doing, I will not do." Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla not will forgive you. "I was imitating these people, they are..." No, you are not responsible for these people. You are responsible for yourself. AllāhNone ‘Azza wa-Jalla say, "Wa-lā taziru wāziratun wizra ukhrá" (39:7). Nobody responsible for another people. Everybody, they will ask, "What they done? Why they done?" "I done this because this majority people most of people, whole people, they are doing this." "You don't know this not good?" "I know but they are doing so I thought it is good to do. No need to make myself tired and doing what it is difficult for me to do, for my ego to do." For ego, of course every good thing, it's difficult to do. "And I doing this." You will not be safe from hell, that time.

You, even you are very few people, you must be with good people, with what Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla ordering, you must be with them. Because, when Sayyidunā Nūḥ make this ark and he call people but people, most of people, they was laughing, nobody believe. Only forty or fifty people from whole world they were safe in his ship. But others, they finish. Why they finish? Because they are looking for each other and they not believe. AllāhNone make us to be believer and accept what HeNone is ordering us. To not follow the majority, who majority they are running after their desire, after their ego, after the bad thing doing. Don't be with them, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone save us from them.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
