As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa-l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

Our ProphetNone is saying, "Yā, yassir... anta taj‘alu... "[Allāhumma] lā sahla illa mā ja‘altahu sahlan, wa-anta taj‘alu l-ḥazna idhā shi’ta sahlan." Our ProphetNone says this. It is a du‘ā’ and ḥadīth sharīf. Only Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla makes every task easy. HeNone makes it easy. Even sadness and sorrow, HeNone makes them easy. For everything to be easy, if you want your work to be easy, you should beg AllāhNone, as ease is from AllāhNone. If HeNone wants it to be easy, even the hardest, the most difficult work will be easy. And when you think it is so easy and you can do it, if AllāhNone doesn't want, HeNone can make the easiest very hard and nothing works. These people keep forgetting AllāhNone. They say they can't succeed in easy things. AllāhNone doesn't want it.

You should pray to AllāhNone from the start, asking, "Make our work easy. Make the difficulty easy. May our everything be easy. May our dunyā work be easy and may the work of Ākhirah be easy." "Rabbi yassir wa-lā tu‘assir." "O AllāhNone, make it easy, don't make it difficult." "Rabbi tammim bi-l-khayr." "May it end with goodness." It is our Prophet'sNone ḥadīth and du‘ā’.

May AllāhNone make it easy by virtue of this Ramaḍān and these last ten holy days, in shā’a Llāh. Nothing works out by our ability or skill. No matter how much you say you know something, if AllāhNone doesn't want it nothing works out. You should believe this and not credit your ego all the time. Know that everything comes from AllāhNone. Favours are from AllāhNone and the biggest favour is to give ease.

May AllāhNone let us do the right things easily. May HeNone make wrong things difficult so that we don't do them. If it's wrong, may we not do it.

Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam heNone was saying this du‘ā’ "Allāhumma lā sahla illa mā ja‘altahu sahlan, wa-anta taj‘alu l-ḥazna idhā shi’ta sahlan." Meaning – "O our LordNone, nothing easy except what YouNone make it easy for us. Even the sadness become easy if YouNone want." To be make everything easy is Will of our Lord Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. If HeNone want, make very difficult thing you can make it easily but if HeNone don't want, very easy thing you thought I can do it in one minute, you cannot do at all, become very difficult. Everything with AllāhNone Will. With HisNone hand, of AllāhNone hand, HeNone can make easy, make difficult. Even sadness, the very hard sadness, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying even this can be also easy. It is not easy to be sad for something, for somebody, for... but when you, AllāhNone want, HeNone make this even easy.

For this, we are everything, we must make remember AllāhNone, to ask from AllāhNone to make it easy, to make good thing easy for us to do. And it is big favour. And to make, if it is not good, to make it difficult, to not make any bad thing for our life. When it is not good – maybe sometimes people not happy but there is also wisdom. But the most of time Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla HeNone like to make easy for people who remember HimNone. But if they are not remember, HeNone put every difficult for them and they are struggle and they are trying to do without remembering AllāhNone.

AllāhNone make everything easy for us, in shā’a Llāh. We are weak, weak people. We are not in need for test. We are... AllāhNone make with barakah of this month, and special this last ten days of Ramaḍān, and Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam and Mawlānā to make everything easy for us, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
