As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Mubarak, the day of the birth of our Prophet ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. Fifteen hundred years ago, our Prophet ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam came into this world. From this time, and indeed all the people until today are from the Ummah of Muḥammad.

Allah sent our Prophet ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam as a gift. To be part of his Ummah is a great blessing. To reject that would be considered foolishness.

Shukr to Allah we are gathered here today, for the sake of Allah and the love of the Prophet. The atmosphere today, māshā'Allah, is very similar to the atmosphere of Madīnah Munawwarah. It’s a little warmer, but beautiful. There is divine manifestation. Everything has a good side. Shukr to Allah, for this good side. Everything comes to us with a good side.

Whatever happens, whatever we do, as long as it is for the sake of Allah, it is the good side. No matter how much trouble there is from others, inshā’Allah, there is no trouble for those who are on the way of our Prophet. There is only the love of Allah for the sake of Allah.

Once that love is present, nothing else has an effect. The greatest gift from Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla is that He places the love of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla and the love of our Prophet in our hearts; after that, nothing else matters. That is the most important thing. That is the most precious thing. If Allah grants us that precious thing with His permission, inshā’Allah, it will remain in our hearts forever, it will never leave our hearts, inshā’Allah.

Because this is our greatest duʿā. More than eating, drinking, or any kind of trouble, the greatest duʿā we should make is for our hearts to be filled with that love, to increase and continue without ever diminishing. This is the greatest virtue.

Once you are on the way of Allah, you will follow his way and honor him. You will give your life for our holy Prophet, as he wants. You mustn’t deviate from his way. You mustn’t be a bad example to others. To say, “Look, this man is on the way of the Prophet, on this way, and look what he’s doing,” is the greatest evil. May Allah protect us from this.

May this way protect us. Let us not stray from the way. Those who stray from the way will perish.

Therefore, whatever worldly things they show you, do not accept them. Do not compromise on them. There is no compromise on your love for Allah and the Prophet. Once a person gets used to compromising, a little bit here and there, neither faith nor Islām remains in him.

Therefore, the most important thing is the way of the Prophet, the way of ṭarīqah, the way of madhhab, the way of Islām. There is nothing more precious than that. Even if they offered you the whole world, “Deviate from this way, don’t believe in this, don’t do this” – or even if they offered you a small thing and said that the world will be yours, don’t accept it. Don’t compromise with it.

The more you compromise, the more you will be destroyed. The greatest destruction is losing your Hereafter. Don’t lose your Hereafter for worldly gain. Worldly gain is temporary. The Hereafter is eternal. If you give up your Hereafter for worldly gain, how will you look your Shaykh in the face in the Hereafter, how will you look our Prophet ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam in the face? You won’t be able to do so.

Therefore, one must be careful. Many people have given their lives, everything, for the sake of religion, so that they wouldn’t abandon their faith. Therefore, one must be careful about this. One must stay away from such things, one must not follow his ego.

May Allah not separate us from this way. May we not be disgraced us in the presence of our Prophet ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. May we not be disgraced in the presence of the Mashāyikh. Do not give up your hereafter and being on this way for worldly gain.
