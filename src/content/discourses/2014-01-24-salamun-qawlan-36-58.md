Shaykh Muhammad Nazim Al-Haqqani An-Naqshibandi, Sohbat of the 24th of January, 2014. Shukr to Allah. Ya Rabbi, Your week servants prayed so much. May His mercies come down. May no unbelievers remain. May Sham be opened. May the Sultan come. May we be worthy of paradise. Fatiha. Fatiha. Say that the angels are embracing everyone. HR: The angels are hugging you. Angels are hugging everyone present here. – It's because of you. It's your karama. Pray for us. And we are praying for you. – May Allah give you long life, goodness, health. They will walk under the flag of our mother Fatima-z Zahra. All of you are under the flag of Fatima-z Zahra. With her intercession, we hope to enter paradise too. – InshaAllah. May they not look at the decoration of dunya. May they look for the decoration of Akhirah. HR: Don't look for dunya, look for Akhirah. SN: This is a child whom Sheikh Effendi (Sh.Abdullah ad-Daghistani) took on his lap. She has light. - MashaAllah. May all of you enter paradise under the flag of Fatima-z Zahra. - Amin. All together, inshaAllah. – SN: Where is your father? – G: In England, in Wales, in the mountains. SN: He must be in the maqam of Sayyidina Musa. Mahdi coming from that side. Allahu Akbar. InshaAllah. Fatiha. May Allah give you health. May I be given health too. InshaAllah we will meet Mahdi alaihi salam. Are you happy? Is anyone hungry? HR: Are you hungry? - All of them have eaten, mashaAllah.
