As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

We are thanking Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla that we meet again. All the time, we must thank Allah because He gives us the opportunity to be with his good servants, with good people. In every minute of our lives, we must be thankful for Allah.

Mawlānā Shaykh used to say, one breath while alive, saying “Lā ilāha illa Allah,” being in a place of dhikr and ṣuḥbah, is better than a thousand years under earth.

Many people wish - they are not happy with life. Some of these crazy people kill themselves or wish they would die. This is not good. Because everything for the mu’min is good; nothing is bad for the mu’min. When Allah gives you a niʿmah, you must be happy. And when you thank Allah for it, He will reward you and be happy with you.

If you have balā (affliction) or any difficulty and you say “Alḥamdulillāh,” Allah will also reward you. Any difficulty or any bad thing happening in life doesn’t go without reward. For this, Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam was saying, the situation of the Mu’min is ʿajīb, strange: he’s rewarded when both the good and bad befalls him. Nothing, not any one state passes without blessings from Allah. You must know this.

[Sound disturbance]. Mawlānā used to get very angry when somebody comes and goes here and there [moves during the ṣuḥbah], so no need to do so. It finishes the atmosphere (manifestation) of mercy. Don’t speak. What comes from the mercy of Allah to our gathering is enough. No need to make this. Our gathering is for the sake of Allah. If you understand or not understand, it’s okay. Alḥamdulillāh, many people are understanding also, all over the world. Alḥamdulillāh, they’re coming from everywhere. They are happy, inshā’Allāh.

Our gathering is very important because we are continuing the way of Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, Ṣaḥābah, Awliyā’ullāh and Ahlu l-Bayt. All of our way is the same way. Alḥamdulillāh many of you are coming from a very long distance: China, India, Turkmenistan. They’re coming from everywhere for the sake of Allah, Allah will reward them.

A great example of this is Sayyidinā ʿAbdul Qādir Al-Gaylāni, aʿlallāhu darajātuhu dā'ima (may Allah always elevate his ranks). He is the Bāz, Hadri Bāz. He is the greatest awliyā’ullāh, who said “My foot is on the neck of every Wali”. Maybe people will think, “How could he say this! How can his foot be on the neck of our mashāyikh!” The mashāyikh have no problem. If they are not happy with this, they are not mashāyikh. For this, many people sometimes don’t understand the wisdom of what Awliyā’ullāh say. You should say, “Yes. He is a great Awliyā’ullāh. We don’t object to that.”

Sayyidinā ʿAbdul Qādir Al-Gaylāni was born in Gilan, north of present-day Iran. Allah gave him love for Awliyā’ullāh and learning. His father had passed away; he was yatīm (paternal orphan). He only had his mother and brother. He told his mother, “I want to go and be with Awliyā’ullāh, to learn from them, to be closer to Allah.” His mother was worried a bit, saying, “How will you go..” and so. But he insisted. She said, “Okay. Your father inherited 80 gold coins. I will give you 40 and your brother 40.” She put them in his jacket and sewed it to not be stolen or fall down. He went in a caravan to Baghdad. Baghdad at that time was a city of knowledge that had many Awliyā’ullāh.

So on the road, there were thieves. They were sixty or sixty-six thieves. They attacked the caravan and stole everything from the people. One thief came to him and just asked him, “What do you have?” He said, “I have gold inside my jacket.” The thief looked at him: he looks like a small boy who’s not rich. So he thought he was joking. He left him but the other thieves also came to him. They asked him the same question. And the same happened. So they told their chief, the master of thieves, “This boy said so. He might be joking with us!” He said, “Open his pocket.” They opened it and found 40 golden coins in his pocket. They asked, “Why did you tell us! You could’ve kept them with you.” He said, “I promised my mother to say the truth. If anything happened, I shall say the truth and nothing but the truth without hiding anything.” When they heard this, the chief of thieves said, “Oh, what is this! This boy is afraid from Allah and from his mother. He’s not lying. I repent and ask forgiveness from Allah. I will give back what I stole from these people.” And the other thieves also said, “Our chief repented. He was our master in stealing from people. Now he is our master towards Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla.” All the sixty, by the barakah of Sayyidinā ʿAbdul Qādir Al-Gaylāni became good people, and Allah accepted them.

This is the way of Awliyā’: to teach people goodness; not to steal from people, or cheat people, or harm people. Alḥamdulillāh he is the biggest Awliyā’ullāh and at the same time he was learning under Sayyidinā Yūsuf Al-Hamadānī. Hamadan is near Gilan also. But now it’s of course a little bit far. It’s a different city. Yūsuf al-Hamadānī was the Shaykh of Aḥmad Yasawī. Maybe you haven’t heard much about Sayyidinā Aḥmad Yasawī here; but he is a great man. He has a hundred thousand murīds. And he lived for 126 years. After 63 years, he dug under his house and stayed down under earth. He said, “Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam wasn’t upon earth (living) after 63 years. So, I cannot walk on earth.” For 63 years, he was living under earth, making a zāwiyah there, not going up at all.

He has, as we said, a hundred thousand murīds. They were going east and west. Most of them were going west. Of course, I think they must’ve reached this area also. Because everywhere they were going, with the barakah of these mashāyikh, they were spreading Islām. He and Sayyidinā ʿAbdul Qādir Al-Gaylāni’s murīds were opening the hearts of people. When the Islāmic army came, everybody was waiting for them to finish the oppression they had upon them. And people were happy, not running away from the country. Muslims weren’t throwing them out of the country. Islām is mercy. And ṭarīqah, especially, is teaching people the most to be merciful towards people. Mercy for people is by giving them a good example and making them be in Jannah. This is the biggest mercy they’re giving to people.

Many people came to hidāyah through them. The army of those not in ṭarīqah or who don’t respect our Prophet, don’t respect Awliyā’ullāh, Ṣaḥābah and Ahlu l-Bayt are not making any good for Islām. They are, every time, making trouble for Islām and making people run away from Islām. For this, ṭarīqah is very important. And you see how the great Awliyā’ullāh, the great people serve Islām, serve from east to west, make millions and billions of people come to paradise, not run away from Islām. They bring people to Islām.

Those against ṭarīqah and against sharīʿah cannot benefit at all. Many people are cheated by them. Why? Because they are cheaters and liars. In our way - there cannot be liars in Islām. Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam says, a Muslim can steal, can do this or that but cannot be a liar. This is a very bad thing.

The worst thing for any human being is to be a liar; and especially for a Muslim; he cannot be. These people are a hundred percent liars – those against ṭarīqah, against madhhab, against sharīʿah, against ʿaqīdah. They cannot take people to the right way. They make them run away from the way, and go the opposite way. Not go to Jannah but to Jahannam. This is their way, way of shayṭān.

In Qur’ān also, it is mentioned many times that shayṭān takes people who follow him straight to Jahannam.

May Allah keep the us and the ummah safe from these people. May Allah make people not be cheated by these people who are really terrible ones. May Allah help us, inshā’Allāh. May Allah send the one, whom Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam promised about, Sayyidinā Al-Mahdī ʿalayhi s-salām, to clean the world.

Now the world is dirty spiritually and - everything is dirty. Nothing you find is good. Everything is making people feel bad. Because this dirtiness can be cleansed only with Sayyidinā Al-Mahdī. How will it be clean? Allah promised Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam that the whole world will become Muslim. And Muslims are clean. It will all be clean. And inshā’Allāh, we are not far from this time. Because everything is reaching its end.

May Allah help us. Inshā’Allāh we reach this time; all of us, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
