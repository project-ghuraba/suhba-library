As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa raḥmatu Llāhi wa barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīn wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, Madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

"Wa man yuridu Llāhi bi-khayrihi yufaqqahu fī d-dīn," it says in the Ḥadīth sharīf. If Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla wills goodness for a person, HeNone guides him. You see that people you would never expect have been guided to ṣalāh. They are people who have come to the way of AllāhNone. AllāhNone has willed good for those people. If HeNone had not willed good for them, they would be like other people running with worldly preoccupations, running after this world. The world is deceptive. That is why when Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla wills good, it is a great favour from AllāhNone. We need to thank AllāhNone. There cannot be a greater good.

Being on the path of AllāhNone and among those whom AllāhNone loves is the greatest of good and the greatest of favours. Money, wealth, power, and beauty, all of them are the bounty of AllāhNone, but they are all useless things if not used on the way of AllāhNone. It would be striving in vain. They are things that have no benefit. What is beneficial, as we said, the most beautiful thing is being on the path of AllāhNone and on the path of guidance. People, even believers, do not understand this. Sometimes they tell their worries like worldly people. They tell of restricting things when they should be happy they are on the path of AllāhNone. We should not pay much attention to other things. Of course people can be troubled but you should not give it importance. You should not give importance to anything – it is all from AllāhNone. Since AllāhNone has shown us the beautiful way the rest is small details. What is essential is goodness, and what is left are small, little things.

However, it is normal to be in this world and suffer distress. So even if you come to Ṭarīqah, there can be little stuff. If you think, "I have entered Ṭarīqah, or this good way. All troubles will be gone," well it could be. But if the way you think makes a big deal of little things... Do not forget that you are in the world. There are troubles in the world. So there is no person without troubles. Therefore, do not give importance to little things. AllāhNone will give the rewards for it if you are patient. "Innamā yuwaffā ṣ-ṣābirūna ajrahum bighayri ḥisāb" (39:10). For the patient ones, rewards will be given, uncountable in the sight of AllāhNone. Therefore, let us always remember this generous gift. Guidance, being on the right path, is a great gift. The rest are easy matters. No matter how big they may all seem, do not give importance to them. Only give importance to AllāhNone and to our Holy ProphetNone. The rest is unimportant. This is a gift for you from AllāhNone. Your station rises and your rewards increase with them.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Wa man yuridu Llāhi bi-khayrihi yufaqqahu fī d-dīn." Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla saying: who AllāhNone He love him and HeNone like him, HeNone make him to be love religion, to love way of ProphetNone, way of Anbiyā’, Awliyā’, this way. If you are in this way you must be thankful for AllāhNone because not many people in this way. This is special people in this way. There is many people even they thought they are religion but they are making thing against what AllāhNone like, what ProphetNone like. When you are in right way, you every day thank AllāhNone: "YouNone make us to be realise, to notice what we are, what we are doing here. Thank YouNone for giving us light to see reality, to see benefit thing – not to be whole life running after nothing, after shadows, after sound, after something not real."

And you – we are happy? No. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh you thank HimNone you are in this life following HimNone, you accept HimNone, you know HimNone. The most important to know your Lord, your ProphetNone. This is very important. Nothing more important than this. Other thing it is only decoration for this life. Sometimes you are ill. Sometime you are poor. Sometime you are not happy. Sometimes it is – this is life. When you are in this earth, this life, you must have like whole human being. What they have, you have. But they have without knowing the reason so they are loser. They don't have anything to be reward. But you are rewarding by AllāhNone everything. You are not happy or difficult for you – you are rewarded.

So, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we must be thankful for this. The most important thing we have – we got bless from ProphetNone and knowing Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla, mercy of HimNone. This is chance for luck for people who are in right way. Other people they are think they are happy. They are proud for their life but they are reality they are poor people, real poor because real richness in next life, not this life. AllāhNone help us to be with ProphetNone, with Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla whole our life, in shā’a Llāh, and give hidāyah for other also, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
