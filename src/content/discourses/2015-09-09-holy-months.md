Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi Rasulillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

These are months known as “Ash-hur al-Hurum”: Zilqada, Zilhijja, Muharram, and separately the month of Rajab, another holy month Allah gave to Muslims and humans. These are virtuous months that need to be be shown respect.

Rajab is the month of Allah. These are months people go to Hajj to obey the orders of Allah. For the sake of Hajis, these months are full of blessings and virtues for all Muslims.

As we said, Allah Azza wa Jalla made a means for people, for Muslims, so they may take from His virtuous favors in every opportunity. These are sacred months. May Allah give people guidance. May the virtue of these months be a reason to guidance for those who are not on the right path Inshallah.

Al-Fatiha.
