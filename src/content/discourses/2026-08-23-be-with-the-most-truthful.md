As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا اتَّقُوا اللَّهَ وَكُونُوا مَعَ الصَّادِقِينَ
<br>“Yā ayyuhā lladhīna āmanū t-taqūllāha wa kūnū maʿa ṣ-ṣādiqīn.”<br>
“O you who have believed, fear Allāh and be with the truthful.” (Qurʼān 9:119). 

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla commands us to be with the truthful ones; to those who believe. The most truthful is our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam,

لا اله الا الله محمد رسول الله صَادِقُ الوَعْدِ الأَمِينِ
<br>“Lā ilāha illā Allah Muḥammad rasūl Allāh, ṣādiq al-waʿd al-amīn.”<br>
“There is no deity worthy of worship except Allah, Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah, truthful to his promise, the trustworthy.”

The foremost truthful one, the one who is truthful, is our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. The command is to be with him, to be with those who are on his way. Stay away from those who are far from him. Do not approach them. No benefit will ever come from them. Because they are only concerned with their own egos, they inflate their egos, and they do not allow anyone to speak to their egos. They are people who have absolutely nothing to do with truthfulness. They have nothing to do with trustworthy, trust, or loyalty.

That’s why, Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says to be with the good, the truthful. Truthful means people who don’t lie, who are honest; continue to be with them. Stay away from the others. The further you stay away, the better. Because their nature is greed, envy, and wickedness; nothing else. They never want the good of others or yours. They may appear as friends, but they are actually enemies.

Those who are friends are those who are on the way of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla, on the way of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla and our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam; those are the good people.

وَحَسُنَ أُولَٰئِكَ رَفِيقًا
<br>“Wa ḥasuna ulā'ika rafīqā.”<br>
“And how excellent these companions are!” (Qurʼān 4:69).

What excellent companions they are! They are good people who are truthful to Allah, and Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla accepts them as truthful. Allah describes the qualities of those on the way of Allah, they are good companions, good friends, Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says to be with them.

So, you should continue to do everything for the sake of Allah, as Allah desires, remembering Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla every minute, and thanking Allah for being with these good people, checking whether you are on His way or not. Of course, many states come upon a person. Good things will come, and trials will come.

Therefore, if you have good friends by your side, they will give you advice, tell you what to do, and help you. They will help you to walk the right way, the way of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. But of course, humankind thinks, “If I had done this, it would have been different.” No, it is Allah’s decree. One should say, “It is good as Allah decrees,” but your main duty is not to object.

Your duty is to accept what Allah has decreed. We are doing our best. If things turn out well, we will give thanks and praise to Allah. If there are trials, we will say, “That too is from Allah,” and we will not rebel.

Now, there are some new Muslims but many Muslims who say so, “Why is this happening to us? Why is this situation befalling us?” It’s not in your hands. It’s Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla’s decree. Whatever happens to a believer, a Muslim, has a reward. Nothing is in vain. Nothing goes in vain. You must know that. Of course, there are difficult times, very difficult times. It’s hard to be patient then. The more difficult it is, the greater the reward. To ensure that reward isn’t lost, you must be patient and rely upon Allah. The things Allah gives, the blessings – or however long He grants you life, you will live that long. After that, it will be your reward. Then you will see the reward.

May Allah not give us tests we cannot bear. For the sake of this blessed month, and for the sake of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam teaches us all good things. He says, “Don’t ask for patience.” Patience is difficult. Ask for Allah’s beneficence. Never say, “Let us be tested, let us be patient,” says our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. He says, “Don’t ask for that.” May Allah protect us. It is a difficult thing.

Therefore, may He always treat us with His beneficence and not test us. We are not servants of testing, but servants of His beneficence. We are beggars at His door, seeking His beneficence. In this blessed and beautiful month, may Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla, for the sake of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, not test us all. May He always bestow upon us beauty and goodness, inshā’Allāh.
