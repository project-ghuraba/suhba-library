As-Salamu Alaikum. Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fil jamia' May it be a beneficial sohbat Insha Allah. This one is always considered to be the father of Prophets, Ibrahim (as). This barakah starts from him. The holy one is a Prophet with great barakah. He is the father of Hz Ismael & the Prophet (saw). The Prophets..

Allah (awj) created man in different classes. Of course all of them look the same but in reality it is like a line. Really people are all in different lines. Some of them He (swt) made the Prophet family, these Prophets all come from the same family. Some, He (swt) made Kings. They also come from the same family. Of course people cannot see this but Allah (awj) created this. And this is also a great matter. It is something people cannot see but no one has the right to say "this is like this, like that" for what Allah created. They have no proof, no evidence. Therefore it is big adab/good manner for everyone to be content with their own condition. Great ihsan/grants come from Allah (awj) because of this adab. Such a person gains thawab & he, himself, is at peace. Otherwise if he struggles, tries "I will be like this, like that".. Of course people may try. Maybe something had come to him but if he says "I will definitely do this" for something no one accepts on his own, he suffers the misery & pain. He suffers the misery in vain.

These are not very important but we were speaking about Ibrahim (as). The Prophet who is mentioned most in Quran is Prophet Ibrahim (as). It says "Khalil al-Rahman" (friend to Rahman). He has barakah. He is the one who built Kaaba for all Muslims. He is the father of all Muslims. Till now Wahabi didn't leave anything around Kaaba, only that maqam Ibrahim. That stone was there, it served as a scaffold when building Kaaba. It would go up & down, & he would go up & down on it. Hz Ibrahim (as) built the stones this way. They couldn't remove it. If they could, they would have removed it also, but it can't be removed. Even if whole world comes together it doesn't move from its place.

So Prophet Ibrahim like this: At night time he wouldn't sit in the table without a guest. He wants to have a guest no matter what, so that he can host him. Allah dressed him from these beautiful attributes, from His Generosity attribute. Even when angels came to him, he slaughtered a calf & offered them but they didn't eat. When they didn't eat, he hesitated..And at another time, he didn't have a guest for a week. He looked here there..Ibrahim (as) would wait for a guest to come to his house. If no guest comes, he would go out & look, would look in far places to find a guest & host him. 1 week passed by like this. Finally he found an old man. The man's hair was all white. His back was hunched. The wind could almost blow him away. When he saw him, he got very happy. He said "Please come & eat with us. Let me host you". And when the man comes, he said to his servants "prepare a perfect table. Put the best of food. We found a guest after one week". They sat at the table & were starting to eat. Of course all who were with him said Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. This old man didn't say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Hz Ibrahim said "How come? With all these blessings we have, how can you not say Basmala? You don't thank Allah? You don't start with Basmala?" The man said "My master didn't teach me like this. We don't have Basmala".

Hz Ibrahim (as) then understood that he is majusi/worshipping fire. When he is majusi, Hz Ibrahim got angry. He kicked the man out, "with all these blessings from Allah, you are not saying Basmala". So the man left. Immediately after him Jibril (as) descended to him. He said, "This man reached 100 years old. All these years Allah fed him. He didn't eat & drink from someone else's blessings. How is it that you kick him off your table because he is not Muslim? Allah grants him his rizq/provision for 100 years although he worships fire, although he doesn't know Him (swt). Quickly catch that men. He may come back & eat his food" he said. When he heard these words, of course Ibrahim (as) regretted what he did. He ran after the man, apologized to him. He said, "Please be so kind & come again, eat with us". The man got surprised. He came but he was surprised.

He said: "Why did you kick me out & invite again?" He replied "It happened such that Allah (awj) sent Jibril (as), to say that it is necessary to host this man, this guest". When the man heard this he said "Allah (awj) sends His angel, His revelation to His Beloved for a weak and poor person like me? He values me this much? Then I am also becoming Muslim". He became a Muslim at that time and Hz Ibrahim became a means for him to be a Muslim with his invitation. And it has been a hikmat/wisdom that after all these years still people hear this story & learn a lesson from it. Meaning, when you do something, do it for Allah. Give/infaq for Allah, not for your own ego.

Today we speak about Ibrahim (as). He is one of big Prophet, Ulu 'l-'Azm. He is father of Prophets. Allah (awj), He created all mankind and when you look for them, they are all same. Every people they have ego, they have good manner, good.. They look like same, all of them. But even between them, Allah (awj) put some people, they are coming line from Adam (as), Noah (as), Prophet line this is. This line, you cannot find Prophet out of this line. There are other lines also, for like Kings or Sultans, Kings and.. they have also line. Because not everybody can make to be president or king or Sultan and take people behind him, no. It is different line.

But now they make Republic & this and that. It is even, some of them also coming from this line but most of them they are not coming from this line. And they make chaos in world. They not know to rule people and they make bad thing when they thought they will be like good thing. And Sayyidina Ibrahim (as), he was the beloved for Allah (awj), between Prophet also. And he was very generous. He did, in Kaaba even now when he built Kaaba there was no scale (ladder) to build Kaaba. And there was one stone - now this Maqam Ibrahim in front of Kaaba. This stone coming to him, he step on stone & stone going up & down like a crane. Everywhere moving. This is miracle for him also. Still now in Kaaba, this same place. They took everything from around Kaaba this people but they couldn't take this. Even all world coming to take it, they cannot move it from there. Many miracle.

He was also father of barakah. Barakah meaning to, if you have a small thing when you have barakah it is enough for everybody, for family, for..Maybe when no barakah you have billions, even not enough for him. But with barakah it is enough, small thing it is enough for people. Once we were with Mawlana Shaykh in Nicosia and there was one man there. His family, good family. He is selling something in his shop & he put alcohol also there. My father he told him, this is you can win too much money to sell alcohol. Maybe you have big money but no barakah for this. Don't sell this. Really it was like this, after his shop it was gone after 2 years. This is secret, many people not knowing. Only they saw front of them what they have, but not seeing what is behind.

And there was one story happened with Prophet Ibrahim (as). He was very generous. Every night, when he sit to eat he must find somebody, for guest to eat with him. If he not find, going around near his house. Not finding, going more far. It was like this. Once, one week he couldn't find anybody. After one week he find one from little far. And he went to him. Look, he was very old man, with long hair, with long beard, white, very white. And he is like this, his back- like hunchback. He bring him, "come". Invite him to eat. This man, he coming. He come & S. Ibrahim (as) he was very happy. He order his servants & people, "Cook, "make good food because long time we didn't gave guest". So they make very good food & they served them. And they will begin to eat. Making when to begin eat, he must say Bismillahir-Rahmanir-Rahim. They saying. And this man, he was not saying this.

S. Ibrahim (as), he was calm, haleem, gentle. But when he not hearing that he is not giving, he asking "why you not saying "Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim"? He said: "My master, he didn't teach me this. He said forbidden to me to say this word". Even S. Ibrahim was calm & gentle, he got angry. He is sending him away, kicking out. "Go away" he said. And this man, he slowly slowly going. That time, Allah (awj) sending Jibril (as) to S. Ibrahim (as). And he was little bit, make not, he said "how you... I feed this man, he is 100 year old. 100 year I feed him & I not ask him if he worship for Me or he was worshipping for fire. And you only invite him & when you didn't hear this you send him away? Quickly go & bring him back to eat with you".

S. Ibrahim (as), he went quickly behind him & he apologized from him. He said I am sorry & come back to eat. This man also, he was happy to come back. He didn't say anything. But he said "Why you send me & you bring me back?" He said, tell him "Allah (awj) sent Jibril (as) to, and he was tell him this story". This man he was shocked and he said: "Allah (awj), He send for

His beloved man the biggest angel only for this poor man and to come back? And I am 100 year not worshipping Him? I am now become Muslim." And he become Muslim this time. And coming eating with S. Ibrahim (as).

This is wisdom for us also. You must.. you don't know. You see something and the wisdom behind it, it is bigger. This is, Mawlana Shaykh, many time he make us to mutajjub/suprise. Every scholar, only they saying what they saw behind him. But he is seeing what behind this, the secret behind it. Alhamdulillah. We are with him, Alhamdulillah.

Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, Al Fatiha.
