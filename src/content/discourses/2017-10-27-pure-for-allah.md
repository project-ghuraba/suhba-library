A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistāni Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla saying, "Alā liLlāhi d-dīnu l-khāliṣ," (39:3) in Qur’ān āyah. Your belief, it must be pure, pure for AllāhNone, not for anything else. This is order of Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla for mu’min, for believers. They must try to do this. And this cannot be without help from somebody. And this who helping, he must have connection from Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa sallam, not from book. Book not enough. If only you are following book, maybe your end it will be bad end. Bad end what meaning? When somebody coming to die, shayṭān coming to him and ask him, "Believe in me. You're not in right way. Change your belief." This is what happen at the end of life. Many people they can, if they don't have real belief and help, going with bad end.

All awliyā’u Llāh, they are teaching people, leading people to this way, to pureness of their heart, to pureness of their belief. Slowly slowly they become pure. Many awliyā’u Llāh, many from each ṭarīqah, from tābi‘īn, tābi‘ī l-tābi‘īn, all they are showing this way. One of them, he was named Sufyān ath-Thawrī. He is from tābi‘īn. He was big ‘ālim also, scholar. And AllāhNone give him memory. He said, "My memory never leave me at all." It meaning what he was seeing not forget whole life. And he was also madhhab imām. His madhhab was named Ath-Thawrī. Because tābi‘īn time was maybe more than hundred, maybe thousand madhhab also. But slowly slowly who not practicing, they forgetting. And this one – one of them. He was with Junayd al-Baghdādī. He was from his madhhab. And he said, "I was" – because he is awliyā’u Llāh – "Three big scholar ‘ālim I was studying. They teach me this from book knowledge." They were real like this time professor. "And what I saw, their end was not in right way." So he is making, trying to teach people to be pure for AllāhNone.

Once one of his murīd, he passed away and his son, he sent him two coins of gold. He said, "This is my inheritance. Inheritance from my father. And my father he was loving you too much. And he was from your student, from your murīd. And it is ḥalāl. Because it is coming from him. And it is ḥalāl. So I give this hadiyyah (present) for you." He sent him this. When he take it, he told his son, "O my son, take this and bring back for this man. Tell him we were loving him for AllāhNone, not for any benefit." His son take it and give it back to this man. And when he come to his father, Sayyidinā Sufyān ath-Thawrī, he said to him, "O my father, we are very in need for money. Why you sent it back?" Sufyān ath-Thawrī told his son for teaching him and teaching us, "I don't want to sell love of AllāhNone for two dinar coins. If I do this Qiyāmah it will be my wages finish with these two. I am only loving him for Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla, not for dunyā." So he was teaching us.

And he was all time running away from – not duty, to be in – he don't like high ranks. He was time of khalīfah Manṣūr ‘Abbāsī. Khalīfah he want make them judge, he and Sayyidinā Abū Ḥanīfah also same time, they were at same place and two more also big scholar. Khalīfah Manṣūr he want to force them to be one of them judge, qāḍī. And they sent them come order. When they are going, they were very sad. They don't want because it is responsibility. And ProphetNone saying who is judge, he is... they cutting him, they kill him without knife. So difficult this situation.

So he is, when they are going, Sayyidinā Abū Ḥanīfah said, "Look what we can do: I can manage myself, you must run away." And the third one he imitate like crazy. Only one, he like this. It will be for him. And there was... When Sufyān ath-Thawrī he ran by boat, ran away, Sayyidinā. So the judge coming for this man, fourth one. There were three of them, they don't like. This is what only for AllāhNone. They not like at all dunyā. Even Sayyidinā Abū Ḥanīfah at the end of his life also, because he didn't accept to be judge, he passed away in prison. They were putting him in prison and he passed away there.

And once from karāmāt of Sufyān ath-Thawrī, he has thousand of karāmāt. He was with khalīfah in mosque and khalīfah was playing with his... in praying like what we see in television, many not stopping his head, everybody making his scarf and other... So normally three, if you make three movement in your praying in Ḥanafī madhhab your prayer is finished. So khalīfah was all time playing, doing something. After praying he told him "O Khalīfah, you cannot do this in praying. So if you do this, AllāhNone in Judgment Day HeNone will throw this your praying in your face as dirty clothes." And khalīfah he was angry for him. He said, "Why you said this?" He said, "I must say because I'm near you. If I don't say, AllāhNone He will ask me this." And Khalīfah was very angry for him. In nighttime they were sleeping with his friend. This khalīfah very angry. He said, "I must punish him to not make people to say anything about me. It must be a lesson for others." And he was sleeping and other two friends, they were speaking. They said this what will happen. And he hear this. He said, "What?" They said, "This is... They will hang you this morning." He said, "I am not happy to live. But I have many things to do for khidmah for ummah. So he was crying and he make du‘ā’. And in this du‘ā’ he said, "O AllāhNone, save me from this oppressor." And this, there was dome at palace of his name Ja‘far. His dome coming down, killing him and all his these who were with him. And other two people, they said, "This is what we du‘ā’... The first mustajāb du‘ā’ he was."

Many karāmāt like this for him. Awliyā’u Llāh when they are depending on Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla, AllāhNone help them. Once also... Nice story for him also. He's really big from awliyā’u Llāh. Mawlānā many times he was speaking, told us about him. One of ḥajjī or pilgrimage or who going to ‘umrah, he was in Ka‘bah. Old time not like this twenty four hour people, million people, in Ka‘bah. Nighttime very few people. One of them he was praying or sitting for tahajjud. And he said, "I was near well of zamzam. Old time also they coming with taking with rope and putting something, bringing water up. I saw one man coming and he was his face covered and he put this rope and bring down and take water from this and drink and he left. After him I thought it was still water, I can drink also." He said, "I drink. It was almond with like juice making with sugar. I never have such a nice drink. After some time also again this man in same time he was there. And he coming, also he bring water, zamzam water and he drink. And he left this." Said this man, "I also drink from this. It was", he said, "honey syrup with water. It was also, I never drink something like this. After third time also happened this. And it was... I ask him quickly, 'Who are you?' He said, 'If you promise to not say anything until after my death, I will tell you. I am Sufyān ath-Thawrī', he said." This is karāmah. And he drink this, he said, "It was like milk. Very nice, also never I drink."

This is what awliyā’u Llāh, why they are not looking for this dunyā or benefit from dunyā. All they are leaving this for ākhirah. Who want ākhirah, he must not looking after his ego, to feed ego, no. Ego you cannot satisfy your ego. What you doing, it is not happy and not satisfied, still be hungry. It is hungry – give more, give more, give more. But awliyā’u Llāh they are thousand, millions since Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa sallam. Each one has karāmāt. Their story, their good knowledge coming from them. And when we are mentioning them, AllāhNone give from their knowledge for us and from barakah and blessing us, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone keep us in this way because these days people they are only looking what you study, what you are doing. This is looking for something not important, only for book. We must look for inside, your soul, not your ego. Because who studying without connection from Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa sallam their ego going bigger and bigger and bigger.

And today we was going around this area. Mā shā’a Llāh, here jungle. We ask, Mehmet Nazim Efendi he ask, "Any big snake here?" They said yes. There is this python. And what they said when it getting bigger, they becoming more egoistic. So their ego becoming bigger and bigger. And this for ‘ulamā’ who are they have, they getting more knowledge, their ego coming bigger and bigger. And they are doing something, AllāhNone will ask them. They are giving fatwá, giving judgment. AllāhNone He will ask them for this what they are doing. Especially for who pure mu’min they are making fatwá. This is they are not in right way. They are in bad way. AllāhNone He will know. AllāhNone He is "Aḥkamu l-Ḥākimīn" (11:45). HisNone is the best judgment.

But what has happened from ProphetNone time only good ‘ālim, good awliyā’u Llāh, they are mentioning. And AllāhNone give them barakah until Yawmu l-Qiyāmah. AllāhNone make us from them, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone keep us in right way even without knowing if we do something wrong, we are regretting, we are asking for tawbah astaghfiru Llāh. Astagfhiru Llāh ‘aẓīm lī wa lakum wa li-sā’iri l-mu’minīn Wal-ḥamdu liLlāhi Rabbi l-‘ālamīn, al-Fātiḥah.
