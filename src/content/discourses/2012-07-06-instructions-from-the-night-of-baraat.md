These are the instructions from last night- Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim, after the night of Baraat. They don't want anyone to come here from Europe. And those who are here should return to their places. These are the instructions- Everybody will do what he has to do in his home, in his own place. There is no permission anymore to visit here.

It is not certain what may happen till Ramadan. Later they may get stuck here & their families are there. They can't handle it. Therefore whoever is here from the Europeans. Who wants to come here from Europeans.. Until a 2. instruction, no one may come. No one may leave their homes, their children. They may not come here with their children. Now there is no necessity, no necessity anymore to come here for visiting. Until Eid al-Adha/Festival of Sacrifice, 3 months. 3 months- whatever happens, happens & whoever remains, remains. Therefore whoever is here, coming from Europe, they may make their preparations this weekend immediately and go back to their families.

No one will come here from there anymore, they can't handle! It is not possible to handle! There is such an instruction. The people of Turkey also, they should keep to their own places. They may not crowd in here. No need. They have appointed Shaykhs there, they will look after them. Everybody who finds a chance comes here. No, it is closed now. You understand? They will have both sides emptied because it will be a burden on them. It won't be a burden on us, but on them. Everybody back to their homes.

There can be a war, battle & they can't find each other, can't find their ways. They can't find a place to go. And they would be left here bewildered. No need. Such an instruction came to me in the night of.. This is the instruction of the night of Baraat. He said "Don't let anyone stay here, other than it's own local people. Whoever had come, may return to their own place, until the end of the Eid. At the end of the eid, if there is nothing they may come for eid visit. If not, until the end of Ramadan no one should move from his place. Then they may be here & their children there. A state of misery comes on them. Thanks to Allah, there is no heaviness to us. But there is heaviness to them.

There is no heaviness for me if all people in Turkey came here. They will eat what they find. But the people of Europe will remain where they are. Inform & warn people about this. That Europeans, starting from Monday, or tomorrow, they may start preparations to return. No one now should come here from there. Here is a dangerous zone. They can't find their way out later. They may remain in their homes, with their families, if possible in the countryside, should look to countryside. They should not live in the cities. The instruction is this.

Cypriots here are different, we don't interfere with them. It is not our job. Cypriots, it is their home. They may do whatever they like. But for the visitors from outside, the doors will be closed, he says. Everybody may do his wird, his mission, in his own place. No need to come & sit here anymore. It is enough! No one from Europeans may come! Turks may sit in their own places. They have their own dargahs & takkas. If Sham opens, new instruction comes. Even if Sham opens, it still has time. No need to come here, but to Sham! They may go to Sham.

The ones in Sham may remain steady in their places, may they have no fear. The instruction has been received. Lock your doors & remain in your house. It is the instruction of our Prophet (sas): "Who enters his home & locks his door is in safety & security". May the ones in Sham have no fear! Remain in your home, spread your prayer rug & continue with your tasbeeh, no one can touch you. If they do this, there are many awliya there who take the responsibility. This instruction is for them (Sham).

The ones in Egypt are different.The ones in Egypt may not go in the streets! They may not go out except for mosques. They may not go out, men & women. If women go out, they are responsible for what will come on them. Women should cover themselves, conceal themselves; they may not be seen! They may lock their doors & remain inside. Aman Ya Rabbi.

This instruction has come, especially for the Europeans. Europeans, Indian, Iranian, whoever is here there is an order now for all of them to go back to their places. Now the dargah only welcomes the ones belonging here, does not welcome visitors from outside anymore. Whoever has something, may go to Hala Sultan Hz. Here can't hold it anymore. They can't take responsibility, the ones here now. They may leave them & they learn their lesson, the people of Lefke. All of them will leave, this is the order: They go back to their places! There is a separate instruction for the other Cypriots. We seek refuge in Allah.

What will come after this is not known. People may go to the countryside. People should go to countryside now. They won't be able to be in the cities, will run away from the cities. This instruction is especially for those who stay in the dargahs & come to visit from outside. All of them may get ready to move starting from tomorrow. Men, women, children..No need,not wanted anymore. Dargahs are opened for them in their own places. No need for them to be here anymore. We have the power to feed even the whole world. It is not that but they can't tolerate it. They can't tolerate it.

The people of Egypt may not move from their places. They may stay in their houses, or they may move to the countryside. They may go to countryside. There is danger, there is danger for Egypt. Sham has already received it's danger. There is also danger for Turkey. They may not move from their places. Who are in Istanbul, may move out from Istanbul back to their towns. If not, they go to their own ruin. This is the instruction from the awliyas who carry the spiritual burden of that place (Istanbul). "If the people in Istanbul remain here, 95% of them will be ruined", he said. There is no need. If they don't listen, they will be ruined. Our situation is like this now. From Europe, America, wherever they came from, everyone turn back to his place! There is no permission to stay here,they don't want anyone to come either. They may stay in their places & carry out the Naqshi way. There is no need for them here anymore.

This is an instruction which came to my heart in the morning of the Baraat night. We have never become needy, helpless in all these years, thanks to Allah. We managed with the blessings & grants of Allah Almighty but there is an instruction,there is an instruction that "they should go back to their places". They may raise the flags of the Naqshi way & may they not fear. And the Egyptians may not go out from their homes. They may not wander out in the streets. Especially at nights, may not go out. They may sit, their doors & windows closed and their lights switched off. The house many not be without a man. To solitary places, the people in Istanbul may go back to solitary places, to their towns. If they don't, there is ruin for them. We don't interfere. There is no danger for the old inhabitants like you, but who came later on, there is danger for all of them.

The time is near. The Master of the Time is coming. What will happen, will happen. There will be war, as well as damage. They informed me about this important point- American; European or whosoever no need for them to be here. No one may come to visit either. It's (closed), holiday until the Eid al-Adha. They may keep some supplies in their homes. They may not show outside if there is a girl,or boy in their homes. They may close, and lock their doors from outside and they may not move from there. They may make a circle around it with 7 Ayat al Kursi, no one can touch. Allahumma salli ala...Ya Rabbi, You are Great. Ya Rabbi, You are Great.

This is important- the foreigners will move out now immediately! They may prepare & move out starting from tomorrow. Turks also, they may move out and go back to their places. No one should stay here. I will close it. The instruction is like this. I can't close but the instruction is such that, close the thing in here & leave. The people who live here may come & go. Who came from outside, no need. There is no permission. Women without men come here. No need. Women, even in their places, they may not go out from their homes, may not be seen! This martial law is coming from the Heavens, Hj Feridun. May Allah protect.

Other than who is with us, no one can stay. You understand? Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, Al Fatiha.

Inform about the instruction. Starting from tomorrow, all Europeans here will return to their places. May they be in safety. May we be in safety also. "Out! Out of Istanbul!" Don't stay in Istanbul!"- The order. It will happen within 6 months. Therefore, my great grandfather was given the news. He said "I don't want Istanbul nor do I want Turkey. I may travel to Cyprus, and be in the open". They are strong, their understanding is strong, they know.

Allahu Akbarul Akbar! Allahu Akbarul Akbar! O Our Lord, we are weak. We are weak, O Lord. Fatiha.

May Allah protect you. They have reached the end of the road in Sham to hit, the others are finished. They can't hit from here to there. No, no. It is closed now. They can come till Jordan but can't go beyond it. If they do, I will take care of them! I may not be able to walk but I can reach them! Aman ya Rabbi!

Until now, to whomever we gave permission to visit & stay here, after the Night of Baraat, the permissions are taken away, until the situation in the Middle East is resolved. They may keep to their homes, themselves & Allah keeps them. Ok son, go on with your work.

G. Thank you! Allah bless you. I am happy with this.
