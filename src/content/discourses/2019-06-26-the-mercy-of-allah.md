Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

We can’t thank Allah ﷻ enough. Allah’s ﷻ mercy is more than enough for everything. Allah Azza wa Jalla says, ‘ ء يْ شَ لَّ كُ تْ َعسِ وَ يِتمَ حْ رَ وَ ’, ‘And My Mercy encompasses all things.’ (Qur’ān 07:156). His mercy has encompassed everything. Allah Azza wa Jalla wants all people to be on the right way and eventually go to paradise. But people talk as if Allah ﷻ is in need of them; they say “I do not accept.” The mercy of Allah Azza wa Jalla is great. They may be granted paradise because of their good deeds.

It is also stated in a Hadith Sharif that after the reckoning is over, Allah Azza wa Jalla calls the people of hell, and there may be hope for them to enter paradise just because they made someone happy or helped someone. Our Holy Prophet ﷺ said that someone there says, “O friend! Intercede for me, do you recognize me?”, “I do not recognize you.” “I offered you water.” For the sake of that water, that person will intercede for him. Allah ﷻ inspires his heart. He is saved from hell because of what he did.

There is nothing of reason or logic in those who do not worship Allah ﷻ Who is so merciful, so forgiving and so generous. No one can reach the mercy of Allah Azza wa Jalla. They rebel, and He grants them again. This world has no value at all. That’s why He grants them more so that they finally come to faith and reach pardon and forgiveness.

Now we see, people are getting very rebellious. But insha’Allah they will regret it later, repent and attain Allah’s ﷻ mercy. May Allah ﷻ improve them. People think denial and rebellion are cleverness. Rebellion never did any good. Wherever there was rebellion, there was terrible evil. Harm is done first to the rebels. May Allah ﷻ protect us.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
