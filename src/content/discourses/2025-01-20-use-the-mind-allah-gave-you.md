As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīmBismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says in several places in the Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n, “A-fa-lā ta‘qilūn” (2:44), (37:138). HeNone says, “Do you not have a mind? Do you not use your mind? Be mindful.” Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gave mind to people so that they see the bad and the good, to see the useful and the useless, to see the harmful and the harmless. HeNone has given mind. Humans and minds. Animals do not have minds. However, they do not approach some harmful things. They run away from danger. They avoid it. AllāhNone has given them a brain to manage. Thus, they get by with it. But humans must understand what is good and what is bad. Because when animals die, they will turn into dust and disappear. They will not go to hell or paradise. Only a few insects and animals will go to paradise. The rest, if they have caused harm to others, will get their punishment on Judgment Day. On Judgment Day, if one animal struck another, that animal will strike back. If one is bitten, it will bite back. Then, they will turn to dust.

But mankind is different. Mankind will be punished for their deeds forever in ākhirah. Dust is too much for them. The faithless kāfirs will wish, “If only I had turned to dust. If only I could have been punished for my mistakes and turned to dust.” But there is no such thing. Why? Because AllāhNone gave them minds so they can understand what happens with those minds, to recognise danger and avoid it, to see the good, and to worship. Worship is difficult. They must work and earn their livelihood. The mind is given for that. They are given minds.

Not everything is easy. Those who work only for the world endure hardship. They must work, earn money, do this and that. The same must apply to the ākhirah. By the wisdom of AllāhNone, AllāhNone does whatever HeNone wills. We are in the coldest time of winter, the darkest time of winter. But right now, it is sunny and warm. Foolish people say, “How nice. It is so sunny. We did not feel cold this winter, we did not suffer. There was no darkness, and it did not rain on us. We are not covered in mud. We had such a nice winter.” And then what will happen in the summer? But in winter, the wise people pray to AllāhNone: “AllāhNone, send rain. Let there be mud. Let it rain. Let there be snow and cold. We have our crops to tend.” Everything depends on water. AllāhNone created everything from water. If there is no water, nothing can exist. The wise ones are content with sunny days, recognising it as the wisdom of AllāhNone. However, unlike the others, they don’t see it as something normal. Many people say, “What a wonderful day. We are going out to enjoy ourselves.” The same applies to the ākhirah: many people wander around, thinking everything is sunny and bright, neither worshiping, doing good deeds, performing charity, nor obeying. They live for their own pleasure. But the pleasure of this winter will end with summer. It will bring drought, no water will be found, and they will start struggling, wondering what to do. The ākhirah is far worse.

If you did nothing in this world, lived only for your pleasure, indulged yourself, drank this and that, and did who knows what else, and then expect something in the ākhirah, you will suffer the consequences there. For this reason, everything has its adab and proper method. You will seek it. You will perform your worship in this world. You will find its result in the ākhirah. That is why Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says, “Use your intellect.” Know the good, know the bad. Things that are done at the right time are good. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla has given us this mind so that we do not miss the opportunity and cannot find it again, once the time has passed.

May AllāhNone enable all of us to use our minds. May HeNone grant us minds and the ability to use them. Because, as we said, there are no intelligent people left anywhere in the world. Give, give, give. If HeNone gives, you will give. What happens next? Nothing. As you give, they take from the other side. You ask, and it goes from the other side. May AllāhNone send Ṣāḥib. May he make things right, for people have completely lost their minds. They no longer value money, their wealth, their health, or their lives. They no longer appreciate the blessings of AllāhNone.

May AllāhNone make us among those who appreciate value, in shā’a Llāh. May AllāhNone protect us. May HeNone send us beneficial rain, in shā’a Llāh. May the sunny days wait till summer. Now, may it rain, may there be storms, may there be snow, in shā’a Llāh. May AllāhNone give barakah, in shā’a Llāh.
