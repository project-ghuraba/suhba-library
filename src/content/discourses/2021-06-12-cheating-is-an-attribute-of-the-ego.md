As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Wa-makarū wa-makara Llāhu wa-Llāhu khayru l-mākirīn" (3:54). says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Friends of shayṭān are trying to cheat people. Mostly they are successful. They cheat people. To cheat people is actually to make them do wrong to each other. When a person cheats someone, he does not do good, but only bad. This habit of cheating is from the ego, it is from the badness of ego.

Most people cheat each other. They cheat in different ways. Ignorant people are cheated one way, educated people are cheated another way. Children are cheated differently, adults are cheated differently. And they are upset for being cheated. They say that their property is taken from their hands by cheating and get sad. This is how the world is. Those who are not in a religion or those who are religious but following their egos, are also like this. They can cheat people. They cheat people, but the greatest cheating is done by shayṭān. His goal is to cheat people and keep them away from truth, to not accept the truth.

Because the rest are the things of this world, they remain in this world. You can be not eating meat every day. You may be left without money and eating only bread. Even with just bread you can still live. You can live as long as you are meant to. But when someone takes your faith away, leaves you faithless, they do the greatest evil to you. Because as we said, one can live in poverty. Eventually, if he believes in AllāhNone, he will be saved. He will not go to hell in Ākhirah. But a person who did not save his faith, falls into the biggest trouble then. He will regret being cheated in Ākhirah. Regret in Ākhirah is not like regret in this world. It cannot be changed there if you say, "I regret it. I wish I did not do it. I was cheated." You shouldn't have been cheated. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla sent the best people, the most steadfast, the most reliable, and the greatest people as prophets. You did not accept them and saw them as enemies by following the words of worthless people who obeyed their egos. You were an enemy to AllāhNone and now you regret it. Regret is useless now. It is over.

There are many universities. There are many people who claim to be professors and who cheat others. Many of them do not believe in AllāhNone and are enemies of Islām. They think they are important people. And those, who don't accept our true way but follow the evil ways, think they are clever. They are the ones who should regret. AllāhNone gave them mind and understanding. If they use their minds, they can expose these cheaters. Most of the time they show up in our dergah as well for cheating people. Our people realize it after being cheated, after they are gone. It happens to our people a lot. They see them as naive and deceivable dervishes and cheat them. But they are exposed immediately and leave. If people think about their cheating they can understand how fake they are.

And they are educated people. Because it is not an easy thing to reach university. It is difficult, as it requires intelligence. But unfortunately, mostly they are the ones who are cheated. And those who claim to be clever and intelligent, those who don't like us, are cheated by crooked people who teach ignorance as knowledge and they run after them for years. May AllāhNone protect us. Some of them find their way out. And some go to an eternal bad place in Ākhirah without finding the way out. May AllāhNone protect us.

As we said, be careful. We should try not to be cheated in this world. But what can we do? The things of this world are not important. They can be replaced. If not, let there be faith which should be enough. May AllāhNone protect us. May we not be cheated by shayṭān. May we not be cheated either by our ego, nor by shayṭān, in shā’a Llāh.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying in Qur’ān, "Wa-makarū wa-makara Llāhu wa-Llāhu khayru l-mākirīn" (3:54). Human being the most of them they have this ego, many type of ego - desire. One of them, for this we see everywhere in the world this, to cheat people. Everything they do to cheat people, to show them bad [is] good; or, even it is good, but also they cheat them to say this is what. But most of them normally not good.

Even the most example for this, this politics. Every time they cheat people, especially in election time. Many poor people they said, "This people they promised us like this." Like country, not like Europe or other country, poor country, they said, "They promise but they didn't do this. They promised this they didn't do." You are real poor people, you are not understanding. They can cheat you. But this people they cheat even the most rich country who they thought they are ruling world, also same. Cheating people. This is ego attribute. And Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying, "They are cheating people but AllāhNone HeNone is the best of, HeNone know everything. They cannot cheat Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla."

Why we said this? Because people not accepting truth, they like to be cheated. When you say... There is example from Turkish: They said, "If you say truth, they kick you out from nine villages because you say truth. But if you are liar and cheat people, they are happy with you, they accept you." So don't be, go for this. Be honest, be honest, because honest it's attribute of prophets and good people. The cheater, they are follower of shayṭān. Every time they cheat people, some of them when sell something, one of them when ask for something. And many people they are doing this, and other people they not realise this – they be cheated. But it is for this material world. It can be everybody can be cheated but at the end, who is believe, it is not cheated by people to be nonbeliever. It is ok, maybe he cannot eat meat because this people they cheat him and take his money. He can eat even dry bread, he survive until his death. And when he die, if he his in belief, he will be survive.

But the worse thing [is] to cheat people and, this is what happened in whole world, to be nonbeliever, not accept the truth. Ḥaqīqah, ḥaqq – this is truth, to accept Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, to accept our Lord, accept HisNone messengers. But this people they are cheating people. They say, "No. Don't believe this. We are not believe on this, you also don't believe." "How you are not believe?" "Because we are, we study in university. The most high education we have. We are professors, we are doctors, we are this, we are that. So, you are... you must believe in what we said." And unfortunately the most people cheated by this people they are educated people who study in university or higher school. They believe in this people. "Oh look this he is professor and he is saying this, we are coming from ape, we are coming from monkeys, we are know nothing. We create by ourself, no thing to create us." But this people they are said, "We are clever. We not believe in you, you are poor people, you are uneducated people, you don't know anything about creation. We are believe in this people they have university, they have researching. Whole time they are researching and doing this, doing that. They write books for to make us to benefit from this." This is what [is] bad cheat, cheated by this people.

But what it is important, for this people to be research, to sit and think about this matter, not only reading. You read, research. What they said it is not true. Because If you look and real researching, everything going to make you believe, to be believer. Because attribute of cheater – to cheat and run away. This is what happen every time. And this people they write book and just putting so people not, only like reading, not thinking. He must make his book and run away. Like cheater everywhere they are like this. Even in our dergah some, many time, every time, maybe every week, every month, they are coming. They look like dervish like this, and cheat people. And they after, our people they are poor people, they are dervish, good heart, not thinking about this. If they think what is this man coming, what he is saying? Real quickly you can find he is crook, he is not trustful.

So this is attribute of cheater. Even small or weak, the biggest one cheater people, they are this professors who denying our Lord Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. The most dangerous one, because other one they take maybe money, they take car, they take anything from you and run away. No problem. But with this it is real problem because if you lose your belief, you be unhappy here and worst thing for next life. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla HeNone not forgive this people who he is... Forgive everything but not forgive to make for HimNone partner or to deny HimNone. So in Ākhirah nobody can save them. So be careful for dunyā and for Ākhirah to not be cheated. In shā’a Llāh, AllāhNone keep us safe from this people.
