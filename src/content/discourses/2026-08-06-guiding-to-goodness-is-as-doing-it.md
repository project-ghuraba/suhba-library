As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam says,<br>
الدالُّ على الخيرِ كفاعلِه
<br>“Ad-dāl ʿalā l-khayr ka-fāʿilih.”<br>
“The one who guides to good is like the one who performs it.” [Jāmiʿ at-Tirmidhī]

A person who strives to do good or shows others the way to do good is considered as if they have done the good deed themselves. The reward for a person who displays all kinds of goodness, facilitates it among people, helps people, or enables others to help even if they cannot help themselves, is the same as the reward for the person who actually does the deed, says our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam.

Because the main goal of a Muslim is to benefit people. The main goal of Islām is to do good and benevolent for all of humanity.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla sent Islām and commanded it to humanity not only to benefit people, but also animals, the environment, and everything else. There is no evil in Islām; there is only beauty. Therefore, in Islām, our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam said that the one who guides others receives as much reward as the one who performs the good deed. His words are blessed.

Of course, while some people want to do these good deeds, shayṭān does not remain idle; it does not suit him; he does not want good to be shown or done. The shayṭān always wants evil, conflict, and chaos, and no peace. Therefore, he torments and attacks those who do good.

But a person who is on the way of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla does not pay attention to this and continues on his way. He does not ask for thanks from anyone. He does not ask for words of praise from anyone. He does everything only for the sake of Allah. He says, “We do it for the sake of Allah; whoever wants to like it, like it, and whoever doesn't want to like it, dislike it.” As long as Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla is pleased, the rest is not important.

May the pleasure of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla always be upon us. May we always be a means of goodness, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
