As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Our ṭarīqah is people who follow the Sunnah of our Prophet ṣallā Llāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, who give importance and try to do every kind of Sunnah. Of course, we can't do all of it. We do as much as we can. And we can't deny what we can't do and say it's unnecessary. Sunnah is love of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam says, "Who doesn't follow and care about my Sunnah is not from me."

There are some people now who say, "We are Muslim. We take knowledge from Qur'ān. We follow Qur'ān and don't know anything else." Some say Qur'ān and ḥadīth. They accept ḥadīth. And some don't even accept it. We reached such a time now that they look critically at others and by saying to them, "you are not a proper Muslim," they insult people and look down on them. A Muslim is a humble person. He cares and values people and other Muslims. Therefore, we should follow all the Sunnah of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. Especially people related to religion, like muftis, hojas and Ḥajjīs, should pay more attention to it.

We see in many places now, when prayer time comes, we prepare for Sunnah and, if we let them, people stand up to make the iqāmah. They skip the Sunnah of Dhuhr prayer. Even Asr prayer there is a Sunnah and it is Sunnah muʾakkadah (emphasised Sunnah). In some places they don't pray the Sunnah of Asr prayer. So there is Sunnah in everything. But the Sunnah of prayers are a very big responsibility. You are a scholar, hoja, in front of people — you shouldn't leave it. They come for Jumuʿah prayer, you pray 10 rakʿāt on Jumuʿah prayer. They say "2 rakʿāt is enough. The rest is..." It's not enough. They confuse people. Such people don't serve Allah but serve Shayṭān, with or without knowing, because they put people in doubt about things they know and deprive them of thawāb (reward). It is still ok if it is only about thawāb and doubt. Some people...

When you don't do Sunnah, you can't have love of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam — then harm comes to you. It's different to do it with love and belief. But when they say it's not important and no need to do it, then they are harmed for not respecting the most Beloved one of Allah ʿAzza wa-Jalla, our beloved Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, for whom He created the worlds. The Ummah gets harmed.

Ṭarīqah teaches this adab. What we call adab is first of all to respect our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, to follow his Sunnah. Especially, don't leave the Sunnah of prayers. They say you can do makeup prayers using your Sunnah — leave Sunnah and make up farḍ. Make up farḍ and Sunnah also. Is it so difficult? You can make up a prayer for every time. Pray Sunnah as well. If your life ends, you will have earned those Sunnah and done your qaḍāʾ. If your life doesn't end, Allah ʿAzza wa-Jalla on Judgement Day deducts Sunnah from your farḍ even if you pray a lot. Therefore, leaving Sunnah cannot be tolerated or encouraged. Make sure you pray Sunnah. There are nafl prayers. You can pray them if you want or not. But always pay attention and keep Sunnah of these 5-time prayers.

Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam has Sunnah. So what does Sunnah mean? What he did. Because we are loving him, we are doing this Sunnah from his acting, from his action — how he was wearing, or saying, or doing. We are following him. This is what Sharīʿah teaches. Ṭarīqah also, because ṭarīqah and Sharīʿah are the same. No difference between them. It is like meat and bone in the body. So it is not — you cannot say any different from this.

But there are people nowadays who are saying, "We are following only Qur'ān, no need for ḥadīth, no need for Sunnah, no need." Only what they are doing, they are doing what they understand. But they don't understand anything even. If they understand, they must follow Prophet, they must follow Sunnah of Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, they must follow murshid. But these people, they are not knowing anything — just saying, some of them they said, "We are taking Qur'ān and ḥadīth," but now there is more extreme. They are saying, "Only from Qur'ān we are taking." So these people, they don't have any adab, they don't have anything.

Because adab — ṭarīqah teaches adab, and first adab is to follow and love Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. "Who is not following my Sunnah, he is not from me." He said, Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. Al-ḥamdu li-Llāh, these people are not from Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. Al-ḥamdu li-Llāh, we are with the Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. We can — there are thousands, tens of thousands of Sunnah that the Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam was doing. We are trying to do as much as we can. If we cannot do, we respect Sunnah of Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. But we are poor people — only we can do what Allah makes us. But for other Sunnah, may Allah forgive us. Because we are respecting and we wish to do it, but we cannot do all.

But most important is to do Sunnah of praying. Especially for the five times of day. There are many people we see — they are not praying Sunnah of Dhuhr even. They quickly come to make iqāmah for ṣalāh. We stop them: No. We must pray. There is Sunnah before this. For Asr prayer it is not as strong a Sunnah as Dhuhr. Even for this, we must pray — we cannot leave it. Sunnah — there is a Ḥadīth Qudsī: Allah ʿAzza wa-Jalla saying "wa mā yazālu ʿabdī yataqarrabu ilayya." With nawāfil, with Sunnah — this is a Sunnah — to come closer to Allah ʿAzza wa-Jalla. When you do this, you are coming closer. Only when you just do four rakʿāt farḍ and leave, it is like eating straw. No benefit for this praying, because you don't have adab. Only just a little bit — who is hungry can eat a little bit and just enough to not die. But otherwise Allah gives us everything with Sunnah.

Many people also say, "We are praying — maybe we didn't pray before farḍ — so we make it instead of Sunnah, we can make this farḍ." No, you can do Sunnah and you can do farḍ every time for what you didn't pray before. You pray one farḍ and you pray Sunnah of this prayer. If you have life, you can do both of them. But if you pass away before finishing, Allah also accepts Sunnah instead of farḍ. So no excuse for people to say, "We are doing this and leaving this." No, you must do and you must respect — give importance to Sunnah.

There are many ʿulamāʾ, many imāms, many people who are doing these things in front of people and people are following them. So they will be asked for these people also, because they made them go astray. For Jumuʿah we pray 10 rakʿāt, we pray before and after. No, they say: "Jumuʿah is only two rakʿāt, you pray and go." Of course, for new people who don't know anything — ok, you pray two rakʿāt and go. But people who are praying ten rakʿāt — they come and tell them, "It's wrong. It is bidʿah. Only pray two rakʿāt and go." They are confusing people.

That is the important thing — to follow Sunnah of Prophet, because our life is only play. The real thing is what we do: we are worshipping Allah as much as we can do and following Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. This is our benefit. No benefit — there is no other benefit. So we — ṭarīqah teaches this. Also, I say again, the benefit of ṭarīqah: ṭarīqah teaches the good way and to be closer to Allah, to be closer to Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
