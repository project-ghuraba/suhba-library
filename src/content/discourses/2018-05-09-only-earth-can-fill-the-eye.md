A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīn wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

"Wa sakhkharunā lakum mā fi l-barri wa l-baḥr." Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla says, "INone made everything for you." Everything on earth and in water is created for mankind. They are the favours of AllāhNone for us. Living and non-living, HeNone showed them not for other creatures but for us. HeNone showed them to us for our use. Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla gave them for us to use. Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla gave it, and what is mankind doing? Mankind is greedy. Whatever they get, they're not satisfied and ask for more. People are serving them and they don't like it, complaining at them. Whereas, so much is done, so much benefit is for people.

It is not said for politics. We are saying it to show the ego of mankind. Even if a politician or an ordinary man gives, people are greedy. People are ungrateful. Their egos are created ungrateful. They need to be educated. People don't look around and say this is less, this is more. They don't make shukr nor do they value anything that is done. We have to value it. When we value, we realize how good things are, what good things have been done. People get appreciated, people are put in the places due to them. And instead of being grateful, everywhere in the world they say this must be like this and like that, and don't value any work. They always ask for more. You give and still it's not enough. They ask for other things then. You give that, still not enough.

Therefore, ḥadīth sharīf of our ProphetNone shows what mankind is like, what the ego is like. Ḥadīṭh sharīf says if you give someone a handful of gold, he'll be happy a little, then ask for more. If you give him not a houseful, but a valley full of gold, and that gold is enough for hundred thousand, even million years, a man will ask if there is more. Therefore, people who try to satisfy others shouldn't waste their time. They can only be full with their eyes. Even two valleys won't make their eyes full. Only the earth can fill those eyes. When you throw the earth on their head in their grave, only then it stops. He becomes full. As long as he is breathing, a man is not going to be full.

May AllāhNone make us from those who know value. Who know value, are the valuable ones. In the eyes of AllāhNone and the ProphetNone, valuable people are those who can value. Mawlānā used to value even the tiniest thing. He wouldn't forget goodness done to him for years. He'd say, "This man did this. That man did that," and taught us that way. And people of dunyā say, "Come and vote for me, I'll give you this." You give and they ask for more. Therefore, no need to cheat people of dunyā with goods because they won't be full. You waste your time. Teach them adab. Teach them the way of AllāhNone, the way of the ProphetNone so that they know the value.

Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla saying in Qur’ān: "Wa sakhkharunā lakum mā fi l-barri wa l-baḥr." This āyah verse is from Qur’ān. Whole this world, from water oceans and lands, it is for benefit of human being. All this AllāhNone give us for to be thankful and to be satisfied, to be happy with what HeNone gave us. But for human being they are happy? No. It is complaining. Everything AllāhNone make this world, from material from lands, from wood, from water, from everything, even animal, all animal for us to eat and to wear from their wool or everything, AllāhNone give this for us. We are not appreciate this, human being what they give them. They need more. They – no they not need more – they want more. For needing is enough for them. AllāhNone make enough for everybody but people they are not thinking. They thought, "it is not enough for us this."

There is what they politician they give them for election, or before election also, many government people. Government they doing good thing – road, water, electricity. Everything they give people, they are not happy with. They want more and more and more. There is never ending asking. They are not satisfied with anything and not appreciate anything what they done this people for them. They want more and more and more. But if they give them, they will be satisfied? Never. This our ego, never satisfied, never be enough for them. And the people trying to make people happy. And when election coming, they are make them, give them word also – "We will to this for you, will do that for you." They are doing this okay but this people it will be good for them? No also never happy. How they can be happy? ProphetNone he describe very nice ḥadīth from himNone and it is enough for everybody for what this psychologic people they are trying making, how this our life they looking, researching about life of human being – how they will be happy.

ProphetNone saying for human being they give describing who if you give him one full of gold he will be happy maybe. After he will need, want more. If he give him one full house gold, he want more and ProphetNone saying if you give him one valley full of gold, and it is enough for him maybe for millions of years, he will ask for another one. His eyes are hungry. Not his eyes – and his ego hungry, not satisfied with anything. And ProphetNone saying, "If you give him one valley of full of gold, they will ask if there is another one." This enough for this whole politician to know and to not be tired themself trying to satisfy people. They must teach them. Because ProphetNone saying this will not fill their eyes – all this two valleys of gold. Only what will fill their eyes – earth. When they throwing in this hole and throw on his head earth, after he will be satisfied. No need for any gold anymore.

This is, this is advice for politician, for people, to teach people to not follow their ego. If they are following ego, they are never be sincere with anybody. Only their sincerity for themself. If anybody give them more, they will go to other. So they must teach this people not to make them what they are like. They must teach them the proper way, way of ProphetNone and way of AllāhNone. This nothing. Don't be cheated by this and you leaving what it is real. You must learn and you must be people, sincere people, and acceptable people. Who accept this, AllāhNone accept him. He then who are not acceptable for this, AllāhNone never like him, ProphetNone not like him. Some crazy people saying AllāhNone if HeNone like us? If AllāhNone like, of course, the people who are leaving their ego and going for HimNone. If he go, they like their ego, why? AllāhNone not obligatory to love you or to like you. Because HeNone is showing good way and this is good for you first not for HimNone.

So in shā’a Llāh, AllāhNone make us to acceptable, be acceptable people in His divinely presence, not following our ego. And ego never satisfied and will be never be good people if you are follow this. AllāhNone help us, in shā’a Llāh. Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
