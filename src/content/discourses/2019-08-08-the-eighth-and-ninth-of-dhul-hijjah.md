As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

We have these blessed days now. And tomorrow is the day of Tarwiyah. The day afterwards is ‘Arafah. Yawmu t-Tarwiyah is the 8th of Dhū l-Ḥijjah, a day before ‘Arafah. Both are holy days. Showing respect to them and making worship brings big profit. People in this world want to become rich immediately. So showing respect to these days and praying during them is so profitable that it remains permanently. No matter how much you earn in dunyā, it is not permanent, it goes away. Even if you have it until you die, after you die you have nothing. And the real profit is the profit of Ākhirah. And huge reward is given by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Who fasts on the day of ‘Arafah, his sins are forgiven a year ahead and behind. For Yawmu t-Tarwiyah, your sins of the last year are forgiven.

Sins are a burden, trouble, and disaster. In order to get rid of the sins of this world, our ProphetNone always used to say make tawbah so that AllāhNone forgives you. People sin throughout the day. We are sinning, knowing or not knowing. Therefore, this is the chance for those sins to be forgiven. In shā’a Llāh, who can fast can do so tomorrow, on the 8th and the 9th. Both days are within the ten days of Dhū l-Ḥijjah fasting. Fasting before that is also virtuous, but the most virtuous are Yawmu t-Tarwiyah and ‘Arafah, tomorrow and the day after. And especially on ‘Arafah, we should recite one thousand Ikhlāṣ Sharīf. People give ṣadaqah. Sometimes some people make qurbān. Before cutting on ‘Īdu l-Aḍḥá, ‘Arafah is also a holy day. If other qurbān animals are sacrificed on ‘Arafah, it is also lots of rewards. May AllāhNone bless it.

In shā’a Llāh. One thousand Ikhlāṣ on ‘Arafah. One thousand lā ilāha illā Llāh Muḥammadur Rasūlu Llāh, one thousand ṣalawāt, one thousand ṣalawāt sharīfah. If you can do more, you may do, in shā’a Llāh. May AllāhNone bless it. May we all have it again, in shā’a Llāh. May the darkness of this world disappear with the appearance of Islām and the Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām. Because Mawlānā says, it is worship to wait for the Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām too. Therefore, don't forget. We don't forget, in shā’a Llāh. We hope every time that next year we will be with Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām. We will always hope, in shā’a Llāh, because the condition of dunyā doesn't look good. As the days are passing, it is becoming worse. It is not getting better. And the result of its worsening, with Allāh'sNone permission, is that events will occur that are promised by AllāhNone and our ProphetNone. Qiyāmah is near. In shā’a Llāh, it is close. May AllāhNone let us reach those good days with Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām and with ‘Īsá ‘alayhi s-salām, in shā’a Llāh.
