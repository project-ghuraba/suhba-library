As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

If they ask what is happiness in this world, it is to be in peace. A person at peace has happiness. Wherever he is, he is happy. And this is granted only to those who are together with pious ones on the way of our Holy ProphetNone and the Awliyā'. This peace and happiness we are talking about are only present for those who are on the way of AllāhNone. Those who have love for AllāhNone and love for our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam in their hearts and for those who are ṣaḥābah, Ahlu l-Bayt, mashāyikh; all of them are on this way. Whoever is on this way has a peaceful life and a prosperous ākhirah. Their ākhirah cannot be prosperous otherwise.

People who say, "I will do it myself. I will find a way by myself," can never find a way. When there is a guide, people don't get lost. What we mean by a guide is that there is a murshid or a guide showing the way in front of them, that way leads to safety. Otherwise, people who don't look for such way will be lost. Going here and there, they will lose their way. They will wander around but won't reach anywhere. If you say you can find the way...

In the time of Mūsá ‘alayhi s-salām when he ran away from Egypt, he came to the desert of Sinai. They divided the sea and passed to another side. Pharaoh and his soldiers were destroyed. This is one of the biggest miracles that people can see. They have seen many miracles there, and this one was the greatest miracle in the world. As soon as they passed through the sea, the people rebelled immediately. But the wrath of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla came upon them. An order came for them to kill themselves. Even after everything was done, they did not obey. They said, "We will find a way." A great Prophet, ūlū l-‘Azm, Sayyidunā Mūsá ‘alayhi s-salām was guiding them in what to do. Orders were coming directly from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla: "Come here and you will reach safety. You will be in comfort and find peace." But they said, "No. We don't want it. Go away." So when the wrath of AllāhNone came upon them, HeNone said "Go away and be lost in this desert." "How will we get lost? The desert is here, the path is here. We can go through and out."

They went out in the morning but were in same place in the evening. Morning out, evening same place. A month, a year, forty years they remained there until everyone died. They couldn't find a way out. This is because when someone doesn't follow a perfect person but follows his ego, he gets lost. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is showing us this as an example so that we certainly go on the right way with the right people. If you are with bad people, you will either not reach your destination, or get lost.

Shukr to AllāhNone, AllāhNone put us on the way of Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim. His way is the way of our Holy ProphetNone. The way goes on. We will keep on this way, in shā’a Llāh. We will reach safety, in shā’a Llāh. It is the way shown by AllāhNone. No one can walk on this way as he likes. May AllāhNone not let us go astray from this way, in shā’a Llāh.

To be happy in this life, to be satisfied, this is the most thing human being they are looking for this. Each everywhere from East, North, South, everywhere people they are trying to be happy, to be satisfied, to be nothing make them anxious. What is the way for this? Only there is one way for this – to be your heart and you with good people, with following way of good people, which they are believer, their heart connected with Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, with Prophet sallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. This make them happy, make them satisfied. Nothing else.

No drug, no alcohol, no woman, no man, no gold, nothing can make you happy. You get this of somebody – "I want more." You get this. "I want more." But where is happiness? No, still nothing. If all this world for you, also nothing. This, the way of happiness, to follow good people, to be with good people, who are they connected with ProphetNone, with Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. The way, there must be somebody showing you way. Without showing, you will lose. With somebody taking you, who taking you straight way, it is important. But if you are by yourself, quickly you will be out of way and you finish or even you lose your way or you find yourself in dangerous.

Because this is what happen for Mūsá ‘alayhi s-salām nation, where they see many miracle. The miracle what they see, maybe no any nation they saw this miracle. Many miracle but the biggest one to be make sea way for them, open sea and they pass safely in peace. And when they go to other side, the Pharaoh and his army all died in, sink in this water and they was in second place and quickly they forget this. They ask many thing for Sayyidunā Mūsá. Even in that place also many miracles happen, many thing happen. Even this Mount of Sinai, AllāhNone was very angry for them and they come on top of them and it will be broken them down. But AllāhNone save them and they order them to kill themselves.

Many miracle happen. After this, whole this miracle, when they are, Prophet Mūsá ‘alayhi s-salām saying, order from AllāhNone to "Go to this city, go and go inside, enter in peace. Don't worry." They are, even for this order, they are not believe and they was objecting. And after this, AllāhNone say to them, "Get lost there in this desert." They said, "No, that way, here desert, here other place. Quickly until nighttime we will be reach our place so no need to worry." But they went from morning to evening. They come same place. From morning to evening, next day, next week, next month, next year. Still they are going, going every day. Nothing happen. Coming to same place. Forty year and all of them die and they finish and their children they went out from this. So, and children they go out with because Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla when HeNone order and they not listening.

HeNone order to Sayyidunā Mūsá, Sayyidunā Hārūn ‘alayhi s-salām to be away from them, not go with them. So they have, without murshid, without anybody to show them way, until the whole of them die. After their children coming and Sayyidunā Yūsha‘ ‘alayhi s-salām, prophet Yūsha‘ ‘alayhi s-salām he take them and he enter to this city with also, with somebody to show them.

So we are, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we must be happy. Our guide, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, he is spiritually and physically from Prophet sallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. He put us in way and he guide us, al-ḥamdu liLlāh. This is a big favour, big happiness for us, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone make us, this way, strongly hold this way, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone raise their maqām and make barakah whole time. Sure they are, with their karāmah, with miracle, many, more than half of new people coming through him, through his coming, appear them for dream or another type, he send, call them here. We are happy with this. This is also miracle of awliyā’, karāmatu l-awliyā’. It is true and acceptable every Muslim believer, mu’min, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
