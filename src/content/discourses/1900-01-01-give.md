On Judgement Day it is granted to the martyrs of this world, says Mawlana Sheikh, that he will stand before Allah Almighty and be given 1000 people to his right, 1000 to his left, 1000 before him, 1000 behind him, and 1000 above him. It is granted to him to intercede for 7000 people. There will be madad according to what the servant requests. And the issue is not by numbers but by madad (support). The one who seeks madad attains and gains blessings eternally.

The one who asks for quantity, the amount is given to him until he leaves this world, then along with this world he becomes nothing. The Prophets (peace be upon them) lived for Allah's sake and in the way of Allah. Their lives were for Allah and in the way of Allah. Their deaths were also in the way of Allah; they were most fortunate. Because they gave everything they had, they got everything. If you give a drop, do not expect to be granted an ocean. No. What is this? Is it according to their whim? Bad manners.

You give a thousand liras and want to buy a car? And now people want to buy a car for 1000 lira bills. You give 1000 and want to get the whole car for 1000 lira bills? No. Bring a hundred thousand and take it all. What you give, you will be granted. However, our Lord Almighty multiplies. He gives to you manifold. He also multiplies it for the one who gives it all. The one for whom it is multiplied is the one who gains.

Those who give all that is precious and of value are the ones who gain. That servant is granted on Judgement Day. Then the angels are ordered to bring before the servant what he spent for the sake of Allah, and what he spent for his own sake. What he gives for Allah: worn shoes, clothes full of holes, and food that is stale. Of his cars, Hajji Effendi rides a Mercedes. Give a car to Jamaluddin! Quickly an old car is brought. He presents this old car and worn clothes for Zakat. He gives the oldest one that no longer works.

In brief, what is of no value for him and he no longer cares about, that he gives for Allah's sake. For his own enjoyment, “o-o-ohh, o-o-ohh”. For whom is this? This is for Allah? This is for himself? How can this be for Allah when it is rubbish? Everything fine he takes for himself. The most precious should be for Allah. May Allah keep me from selfishness, I am a weak servant. If a person presents me something, he is pleased to give me what may please me and make me happy. They say: Let's give the Sheikh what makes him happy. So what about Allah?

The most precious for Him! "laqad ja'akum rasulun min anfasikum" (9:128) There is one reading "min anfusikum" and another "min anfasikum". The preferred is "anfasikum", meaning, from the most precious among you, whereas "min anfusikum" means from your own people, from amongst you. No one can reach to the value of the Prophet, sallAllahu alayhi wa sallam, not any single created being, of course. However, since he is from the people, it became "anfasikum", the best of you. The preferred reading is "anfas", the best, "ahsan" the most precious who was sent to us. He must be regarded with the highest and utmost appreciation.

The duty of the servant when he gives for Allah's sake is that it should be the best, finest, dearest, and most valuable of what he has. On that day, the servant will be ashamed when it is said to him “this is for yourself, and this is for your Lord? Are you not ashamed, O servant? Are you not embarrased? What good is this to you now? Why did you not present this to your Lord, and that to yourself?”

This is a lesson for those who wish to progress and draw close to Allah. This is a right. Allah's right. He is to be given the best. In return for what you give to Him He awards you without limit and beyond anything you can imagine - if you present to Him what is most precious. An instruction for us: spend your effort for your Lord! This is the best teaching. Spend you effort for your Lord, and He will reward you with the best, with what you cannot possibly imagine.

A man went out of his house. He left in the morning and took with him what he needed for work - a spade, a pickax and a saw. He went and sat on the pavement by Sinan Pasha. He waited and waited, but nobody came to him to offer him work. He spent the time from noon to sunset, sitting there. He prayed the five prayers, and sat making dhikr and giving thanks. Afterwards, he went home. His wife asked, "O husband, what have you brought us today?" He said, "O my wife, I worked today, but the One I worked for did not give me my due." And he said, "Tomorrow He will give me. Let us be patient." He looked about and found no food.

The second day was like the first day. He went out, sat waiting for someone and nobody asked to employ him. He thought, "Let me make ablution and go in." At maghrib, he went home. His wife asked him, "O my husband, how was it today?" He said to her, "Today also I worked for the same employer, but he delayed paying me, and it is up to Allah Almighty to judge." The third day was the same, he went out seeking work, but it did not go well. Again, he entered the mosque and stayed till maghrib.

When he returned home, he hung his bag behind the door. His wife came rushing to him with good news as he came in. He found his home filled with all sorts of foods. She said to him, "O my husband, Subhanallah, what is this work that you have done? You were hired for two days, and the third day He sent your wages. Today I was home awaiting your return, and somebody knocked on the door. I looked, it was a servant, he looked like a servant. He handed me a bag in which there was a plate. When I took it from him, it felt heavy. I took it, opened it up, and, behold, it was full of gold. He said to me, ‘The One who employed your husband for two days has sent this to him’. I quickly took one gold coin and rushed to the market”. And they loaded up two mules to carry home all the various types of food. A single gold coin, they said this gold was not like ordinary gold, it was 24 carat gold. That is why they filled two mule loads and I brought them back home. Praise be to Allah, the Almighty, the Sublime. The man was ashamed. He went and took the bag from the door which he had filled with sand, and secretly wanted to throw it out. When he extended his hand to lift it, he could not move it from its place - it had become so heavy. He opened it, and found that all the sand had been turned to gold by Allah. He was ashamed. He was trying to throw it away, Subhanallah Almighty.

His wife asked, "Who is your employer, O my husband?" He said "This is my story. It is Allah. He tries and tests His servant, then He grants him." Now, you, as I was told, every day you must give from your wealth, or from the wealth of other people. Over there at the Sinaniyye (mosque). I used to give after zuhr to some people sitting there without work. When you see those lowly ones, give them each in secret, at least fifty Syrian lira. You understand? Ask them secretly, "You found work today?" If he did not, say to him: "Take this, and tomorrow I will employ you. Take it and go. Tomorrow, insha'Allah I will employ you." So that he is not embarrassed.

They would sit there, would not eat, and would sit until asr time. My heart would ache. They would sit there. I would go after asr. Their women sitting near them. Then their leader came up from behind me, of the Abdal of Sham. He enjoined this upon me and said he wished to tell me this story. This is now a responsibility upon you, you are now appointed. Then starting from tomorrow, mercy will descend upon Sham. You must search in the alleys for those who are oppressed, who are deprived of their rights. Do you understand? You too, every day you must give some amount to every one that comes to you.

If they are inspired in their hearts, they may give to you and you may distribute it. Especially those poor ones sitting there from morning to sunset. Perhaps they do not even have a penny in their pockets to buy a loaf of bread. They want to return home, they are in need, poor. This will be a cause for the doors of mercy to open upon the people of Sham.
