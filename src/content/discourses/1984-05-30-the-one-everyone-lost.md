Sheikh Muhammad Nazim Al-Haqqani An-Naqshibandi, sohbat of the 30th of May, 1984.

By the name of Allah Almighty, All Merciful, Most Beneficent, and Most Munificent. Audhu billahi min ash-shaytan ir-rajim Bismillah ir-Rahman ir-Rahim La hawla wa la quwwata illa biLlahi l-'ali al-'adhim.

O our Lord, we are asking guidance from Him, to Him. Why? We are coming from far countries, from America to London, or from Germany to London. Our brothers, our sisters coming from far distances. It is important to know for what we are coming in a city, 50 million people living in it. And in a humble place gathering - 15 people or more. And they are sitting as little boys to listen or to learn. What they are asking? What is their aims? What is their purposes? It is important to know that point.

It is something belonging to our souls not to our bodies. What is pleasures? You may find everywhere, as much as you ask - plenty. But soul's pleasures, it is very rare and it is so difficult to find it. And we are asking for soul's pleasures. That is our aim - to come from far distances and to meet here with each other. And Allah Almighty sending me from Middle East, sending you from far west. His Divinely Wisdom - bringing one from far east, bringing another from far west and making London a place of meeting. Subhan Allah - Glory to Allah Almighty. We are asking, all of us, to find someone that we lost him. Not only we lost him, everyone lost him. But they are not asking, they're not feeling that they lost him. But in our selves, our feelings, signing that - O servant of your Lord, you lost someone. Look after him! We find main point now. For what we are running from east to west, from west to east.

Once, Mawlana Jalaluddin Rumi, Allah blessing him, was sitting with his people making an association. That is main pillar of tariqah or for tariqahs, particularly our tariqah - Naqshbandi. Main pillar - the association with Sheikh. Taking the mureed from earth to heavens. Therefore - Tariqatuna s-sohba. Shah Naqshband, the imam of Naqshbandiya, most distinguished Naqshbandi order, Sheikh, Ghawth, Shah Naqshband, Allah blessing him, was saying - our tariqah established on association with Sheikh. Because the Sheikh taking mureed, taking his follower, from his down, low station, taking in his (sheikh's) station by association. This wird makes mureed to be clean. But association with Sheikh taking him from low station to high station.

Mawlana Jalaluddin Rumi, now people making him whirling dervish. They're doing such a folklore for such a ballet troop - for people looking. He was not doing only this; not whirling only, but he was making his followers ready for getting up from earth to heavens. He is signing by whirling. When he was whirling, he was getting up from earth to up, showing the way to his followers, to whole believers, to whole nations. And glory to Allah Almighty that He made him acceptable through every nation. Mawlana Jalaluddin Rumi is accepted by every nation. Every nation, or every religion, is accepting his whirling because signing how man may be able to take wings and getting from this dirty life to heavenly life. Even, not this pope, but before, he was praising, he was praising Mawlana at the ceremony of seven hundred years giving a message about him.

He is a person, Mawlana, who signing the top point of humanity, according to our understanding. Common people, they may look in Mawlana Jalaluddin Rumi and may see in him the horizon of humanity. But he is not seeing that the top point of humanity or the top point of humanity horizons, he was looking in holy Prophet Muhammad peace be upon him, that he is the top of humanity, the last level of horizon. He is sitting there but common people are not looking. Now there is telescopes. You may look by your eyes. There are electronic telescopes, you cannot see with your eyes. Radio-electronic. Radio-telescopes, looking and taking gigantic galaxies. Their eyes such eyes. Mawlana Jaluluddin Rumi's eyes, they are like radio-telescopes looking and seeing who is at the last point of humanity horizons, who is there. He is seeing Prophet Muhammad peace be upon him. But people saying - O Mawlana Jalaludin Rumi. Even pope praising him. But pope not praising Muhammad peace be upon him. Why? Because he can't see. He never seeing. But Mawlana Jalaluddin was seeing and saying - I am putting my head who Muhammad peace be upon him putting his feet. Who says this? (He) Who knows Muhammad, who he is, peace be upon him. Therefore everyone going to deal according to their understanding. Allah Almighty has balance, balancing everyone according to their understandings. And everyone carrying responsibility according to their understanding also. And everyone going to be rewarded according to their intelligence.

Mawlana Jalaluddin Rumi not only teaching people how to whirl, or he is not going to teach them how they may whirl. Without any desire, he was doing this. His purpose is not whirling but his purpose to show people how you are getting up, you are leaving this physical body and taking your soul up to heavens. But now we are doing that his way is only whirling. We must know how we can get out from the rule of satan. And we are in need to learn how we can get out and we can be free from satan. Mawlana Jalaluddin Rumi always speaking, making association (with) everyone, then at the end, coming these holy winds through attenders and coming and whirling. Every time he is going up and everyone who was with him and leaving his physical body. And enough power, spiritual power with him, taking him up. And this... music and coming down.

Now we are speaking about an association for him. He was sitting and making association with his attenders, with his people, with his mureeds. From every kind of people attending. Then, one person just running in and crying and shouting and falling on his feet. Mawlana Jalaluddin Rumi asking - what is the reason that you are crying and shouting? What's the matter? And he is crying and saying - O my master, my son, I lost my son. Three days I am asking everywhere and never finding him. Everything that I can do, I just did, but I'm never finding him. Last I am coming to you. I finished everything. My hope with you. I am believing in you that you can find him, not anyone else. I am believing and trusting in you. You can find, not anyone else.

Then Mawlana Jalaluddin Rumi saying - SubhanAllah, glory to Allah Almighty. This person...this person, O people, look, (he is) coming and crying and he lost everything. He never going to taste from his life anything. No eating, no drinking, no sleeping, no resting, no running here or there, no going there asking from him from that. Because of a boy, 7 years, 10 years or more. But I am looking that everyone, not only you, O my attenders, whole world, whole people they lost most precious one. But they are not asking, not crying, not shouting. I am looking that everyone lost their Lord, Allah Almighty. But no one asking where He is, who He is, how can I find Him. No one crying, no one asking, no one shouting.

Look at that person. For a little boy, he's crying and he lost everything that he may taste from this life. Finished. No tasting from food, from drinking, from sleeping, from ladies, from cars, from horses, from grass. Nothing. But you lost your Lord, most precious One, most valuable. Nothing can be like Him. But all of you eating, drinking, making pleasure with ladies, with horses, with grass, with gardens, going, coming, working, sleeping. Everything is all right for you. That is true. We are ghafil/heedless people. We are asking from nothing. We are running after nothing, and we are leaving He who is Eternal, from pre-eternal up to post-eternal. Allah Almighty. If you are not finding Him, then what we are doing here? What is the purpose of this life? If you are not finding Him, what we did here? Nothing.

Therefore, you are coming from far west, and I am coming from far east to meet here and to discuss about the lost One, and where we can find Him. One Jewish coming and asking Imam Ali, Allah blessing him, Sayyidina Ali, makes Him pleased with him. Where is your Lord? And he is saying - where He is not? Who can say such an answer, except Sayyidina Ali. Where He is not that you are asking where He is, O foolish person! Tell me smallest thing that it is in existence without Him Almighty. One angel meeting with second one through heavens, asking one of them - from where you are coming? I am coming from Divine Presence above Throne, holy Throne. And you from where coming? I am coming from my Lord's Divinely Presence under - taht ath-thara - the last point of creatures, the opposite side, the lowest point. One coming saying I am coming from Divine Presence, second also from highest point coming and saying from Divine Presence. Everywhere that you may come, you may come from Divine Presence. Therefore Allah Almighty saying, everywhere you may turn your heart's eyes, you may find Him, His Divinely Presence. You may find Divinely Qiblah there. But for our eyes, we are looking towards Mecca. Allah makes him honorable more.

Allah Almighty. We are asking Him and you may find Him through those people that they find Him and they are with Him, not with any other thing. Whom they are with their Lord, you may find Him through their hearts. If you are finding anyone who his heart going without his Lord, you can't find Him. You must find someone who he is always with his Lord. Who find Him, or it is impossible to find the One you lost. Therefore Allah Almighty just sent His prophets to His people. And the last one, He sent him for whole nations. Because he was with his Lord always. And who reached him and find with him his Lord also. If you are finding such a person, you are finding Him.

We are asking now Who sent us from east to west and Who sent you from west to east. We hope to find Him during holy month, at least to smell from holy smell from His Divinely Presence. That is our purpose. We are not claiming anything. But asking, asking to find His Divinely Presence. That is our purpose, our aim. And as we are going to be a little group, doesn't matter. We are asking to be Jama'ah, even a little group. May be 2 persons, or 3 persons, doesn't matter. We are asking to find Him. And we hope to give us a sign, and to make us to smell from His Divinely Presence good smells. This is the day that we are approaching to the holiest month of Ramadan. We hope to continue by the blessings of our Lord Almighty and by the blessings of our holy Prophet peace be upon him and by the blessings of our Grandsheikhs, the awliyas, to continue 30 days to our associations here and we asking from their favors to make our tongues to speak and our ears to listen. Amin.

Wa min Allah it-tawfiq. Asking from our Lord to make us successful for saying and listening and acting. Bi hurmati l-Habib, bi hurmati l-Fatiha.
