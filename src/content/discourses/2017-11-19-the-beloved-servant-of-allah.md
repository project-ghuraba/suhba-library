Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Today, thank Allah is the last day of the month of Safar. It is called “Safar ul-Khayr”, and it passed well thank Allah. It was good for the Islamic world and for Muslims. May Allah give the unbelievers (kafirs) what they deserve, and He did so Allah willing. Tomorrow is the month of our Holy Prophet (SAW), Rabi’ul Awwal.

Rabi’ul Awwal, meaning the First Spring. It is the month of spring and beauty. Spring months are nice. That is why even if this Rabi’ul the sacred birth of our Holy Prophet (SAW) is in the middle of winter, it is still beautiful. The beauty of spring is in the month of our Holy Prophet (SAW) and we need to honor it beautifully. You can read a Mawlid everyday if possible. Of course, not everybody can read the Mawlid.

We are to recite 1000 salawat a day for the whole month in honor of our Holy Prophet (SAW). When we do it for the honor of our Holy Prophet (SAW), Allah Azza wa Jalla would look at you with a good gaze, your business would go well, and whatever troubles you have would be taken away. We need to recite it with this intention. Do good deeds on the occasion of our Holy Prophet’s (SAW) mawlid (birth) month and say, “We are doing this for the honor of our Holy Prophet (SAW).”

Its rewards would be multiplied. It would be not ten times but 1000 times more, because he is the beloved servant of Allah (JJ). Our Master (SAW) was sent as a mercy for the worlds. Our Holy Prophet (SAW) is the highest of people and of the Universe. We need to be grateful that we are his Nation (Ummat). So many prophets said they wished they would be one of his Nation and not be a prophet. They would look at this Nation with admiration. Of course, people do not know its worth and disregard it. And some swear violently at our Holy Prophet (SAW). They will answer to Allah for it.

Our Holy Prophet (SAW) was sent as a mercy for humanity, because there was no such thing as Muslim when he was sent. In this case, he was sent as a guide to all of humanity and not just to Muslims. However, Shaitan does not sit idle. Shaitan’s greatest enemy is our Holy Prophet (SAW). Allah’s most beloved servant is our Holy Prophet (SAW). Look at it this way.

Honoring our Holy Prophet (SAW) is the greatest worship because nothing would have existed if he had not existed. Our Holy Prophet is a great blessing (nima) for us and we need to recognize the value of this blessing. Inshallah this month passes in constant relief and affection. Inshallah we get to honor our Holy Prophet (SAW) as much as we can. We need to tell this to people too. Shaitan has entered even amongst Muslims, and they are trying to show our Holy Prophet (SAW) as a regular person. Whereas nobody can reach and get to the level of our Holy Prophet (SAW).

Even though our Holy Prophet (SAW) had this high value, he would beg Allah Azza wa Jalla saying, “My Nation,” and wanted to become an intercessor. May we be granted his intercession (shafaat) Inshallah and Inshallah we will. How a person thinks and hopes is how it turns out. We will be saved through the shafaat of our Holy Prophet (SAW) Inshallah. We will not be saved through our own actions and works. Things that we give in honor of our Holy Prophet (SAW) will be valuable. He will intercede as much as we honor him Allah willing.

May Allah (JJ) grant his affection to increase. May our faith strengthen for his honor and for his blessing. May the faith in our hearts increase Inshallah. May Allah make us reach so many Mawlids like this Inshallah. We hope Islam will be victorious with Mahdi Alayhis Salam, because what our Master (SAW) gives glad tidings of is the truth and will come Inshallah.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq. Al-Fatiha.
