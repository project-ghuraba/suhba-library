As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllah, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

1 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “When one of you comes on Friday while the imam is making Khutbah (preaching), he should pray two rak'as and make them short.”

2 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “When one performs Friday prayer, he mustn’t perform another prayer after it till he has engaged in conversation or gone out.”

3 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “When any of you prays the Friday prayer, he should pray four rak’as after it.” So these are sunnah. Of course, what we said is according to the madhhabs. We will follow accordingly. Madhhabs have more explanation on which and how we will perform it. These are like the sunnahs and ādāb (principles) of Friday prayer.

4 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Indeed, Allah and His angels send prayers upon those wearing turbans on Friday.” So, on Friday, Allah and His angels send prayers upon those who wear turbans. Them wearing a turban means that they have attained the prayers of Allah and His angels.

5 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “There is nothing wrong with any one of you, if he can afford it, buying two garments for Friday, other than his daily work clothes.” Friday is a virtuous day. So, wear new clothes on that day. It is sunnah to wear new, clean clothes. Therefore, the work clothes – in old times, finding clothes wasn’t as easy as it is in this time. So if you can afford it, says our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, have two clean garments for Friday apart from the work garment.

6 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “If anyone recites the surah that mentioned Āli ‘Imrān on a Friday, Allah and His angels will invoke blessings on him till the sun sets.” The virtues of Friday are many.

7 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Whoever reads Sūrat al-Kahf on the day of Jumu’ah, a light will shine for him between the two Fridays.” Between two Jumʿahs, light will shine upon whomever recites Sūrat al-Kahf.

8 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Whoever reads Sūrat al-Kahf on the day of Jumu’ah, a light will shine for him which extends from him to the Ancient House (i.e., the Kaʿbah).” So he will be given light which extends between him and the Kaʿbah. So wherever you read it, you will have light from your place till the Kaʿbah. That light will reach you; and that is for whoever reads Sūrat al-Kahf.

9 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Whoever recites after the Friday prayer Sūrat Al-Fātiḥah, Al-Ikhlās, Al-Falaq and Al-Nās seven times, Allah will protect him from evils until the next Friday.” And that will be his protection.

10 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Whoever recites, after the imam gives salāms on Friday, before he gets up from his place, Sūrat Al-Fātiḥah, Al-Ikhlās, Al-Falaq and Al-Nās seven times, his past and future sins will be all forgiven.” This is one of the virtuous deeds of Friday.

11 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Be present for Friday prayer and stay close to the imām; for if anyone always keeps far away from the imām the result will be that he will be put in a back place in paradise, supposing he enters it.” The virtue of Friday prayer is great. Leaving it without an excuse is a great loss for a person; may Allah protect us.

12 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “When it is Friday, the angels stand at every door of the mosque and record the people in the order of their arrival; the first, the second, etc.” So on every door, there are angels. “Who came? This one came first, Aḥmad. The second one who came is Muḥammad. The third one who came is Hassan.” They write it down as such. They write them all in order. 

“And when the Imām sits (on the pulpit for delivering the sermon) they fold up their sheets and go inside to listen to the Khutbah.” They go inside from the doors and don’t write anything anymore. Those who come after that enter without their names being written. 

“And he who comes first,” the one who is written down as the first, “attains the reward as the one who offers a she-camel as a sacrifice, the next like one who offers a cow, the next a ram, the next a hen, the next an egg.” The firsts to arrive will be attain the most. Those who come after attain lesser rewards. The first to enter is the winner. The greatest reward is his.

13 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

Our Holy Prophet is saying here, “On the Day of Resurrection people will gather near Allah according to how early they came to Friday (prayer), the first, second, third and fourth.” So the earlier one goes to Friday, the closer he is to the mercy and the presence of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla on the Day of Judgement.

14 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

Our Holy Prophet is saying here, “The angels sit at the doors of the mosques on Friday, writing down the first, second, and third, until when the imām comes out, the scrolls are raised.” They write it down until the imām arrives and goes on the pulpit to begin the Khutbah: first, second, third, fifth, tenth, hundredth. Until the imam ascends the pulpit, they close it, and they go inside and listen to the Khutbah.

The Messenger of Allah says the truth in what he said or as he said.

Ziyadatan ila sharafi’ ‘n-Nabi, sallaLlahu `alayhi wa sallam wa Alihi wa Asahabihi l-kiram, wa ila arwahi jamee’i al Anbiya’i wa l-Mursalin wa Khudama’i sharai’ihim wa ila arwahi al ‘Aimmati al arba’a, wa ila arwahi Mashayyikhina fi t-Tariqati n-Naqshbandiyyati l-Aliyyah, khassatan Imamu t-ṭarīqah wa Ghawthu l-Khaliqah Khwaja Bahauddin Muḥammad al-Uwaisiyi l-Bukhari, Sayyidina Abdul Khaliq al Ghujduwani, Mawlana Shaykh Sharafuddin al Daghestani, Mawlana Shaykh Abdullah al Faiz al Daghestani, Mawlana Shaykh Muḥammad Nazim Adil al Haqqani, wa sai’ri Sadatina wa s-Siddiqiyun, wa man nahnu fi hadratihim wa jiwarihim, for the souls of all our past ones, for the souls of all Shaheeds. Li-Llahi Ta’ala, Al-Fātiḥah.

[(Translation) To the honor of the Prophet, Prayers and Peace be upon Him, and his family, and his companions, and the souls of all the prophets as well as of His messengers and of those who served their Sharia, and to the souls of the four Imams. And to the souls of our mashāyikh of the Most Distinguished Naqshbandi Tariqa, in particular to the soul of the Imam of the Tariqa Ghawth al-Khaliqa (the Help of Creation), Khwaja Bahauddin Muḥammad al-Uwaisiyi l-Bukhari, Sayyidina Abdul Khaliq al Ghujduwani, Mawlana Shaykh Sharafuddin al Daghestani, Mawlana Shaykh Abdullah al Faiz al Daghestani, Mawlana Shaykh Muḥammad Nazim Adil al Haqqani, and the rest of our masters and Siddiqiyun, and to whom we are in their presence and near them, for the souls of all our past ones, for the souls of all Shaheeds. To Allah Almighty. Al Fātiḥah.]
