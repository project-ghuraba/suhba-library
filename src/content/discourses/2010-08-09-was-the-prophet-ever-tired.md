Madad Yā Sulṭān Allāh Allāh, Allāh Allāh, Allāh Allāh, ‘Azīz Allāh, Allāh Allāh, Allāh Allāh, Allāh Allāh, Karīm Allāh, Allāh Allāh, Allāh Allāh, Allāh Allāh, Subḥān Allāh, Allāh Allāh, Allāh Allāh, Allāh Allāh, Sulṭān Allāh. Zid Yā Rabbanā, sa’īri l-anbiyā’ wa l-awliyā’ wa man tabi‘ahum bi iḥsān ilá Yawma l-qiyāmah. Irḥam ḍa‘fanā irḥam dhullanā, Yā Rabba l-‘izzati wal-‘aẓamat. A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Allāhumma qawwī ḍa‘fanā fī riḍāk Yā Allāh. Thumma s-salām ‘alayka yā ṣāḥiba l-waqt yā quṭbu l-mutaṣarrif. A‘inūnā yā Rijālu Llāh bi ‘awni Llāh.

We are, weak servants. O holy ones give us heavenly support. And help us O holy ones to be on the right way, to keep holy orders of our Lord, our Creator.

Change your direction O believers through this holy month. Not only, but holiest one through 12 months. Don't do this holy month as other holy months, or ordinary months. It has a speciality, holy Ramaḍān. That, heavenly beings, Angels they are getting happy, happier and more happier for this holy month. That what is that reason to be holiest month? O salafī ‘ulamā’, say! Say! What is reason that shahru Ramaḍān, holy month Ramaḍān going to have a special status, status among other months? Make it clear for the servants of our Lord. All of us servants - That is our highest, highest honour that we have been granted from our Lord Almighty Allāh, to be His servants.

Servanthood is the most high respected level for mankind, for whole nations, for whole mankind beginning from Ādam up to the Seal of Prophets, Sayyidinā Muḥammad (saws). Who is working for Allāh never getting to be tired. O people! You are tiring daily. Why you are tiring? You know it? Why not saying to people, O salafī ‘ulamā’? Why people tiring? Answer, I'm asking. What is the answer for that question? For what mankind tiring? That is important point. Why tiring? Say! Clearly to your Mulūk (kings) and Shaykhs, and rich ones and those people whom they are tiring. Why you are tiring? For what tiring? Malik, tiring? Yes sir, His Majesty also tiring. Even tiring Sulṭān of Brunei? Yes sir, tiring. Why? Why tiring? Which thing making Sulṭāns to be tired? Say! ‘Ulamā’ in Brunei, that they are running to be wahhābī ones, on wrong way.

Before his Majesty Sulṭān, he was taking much care and was getting to be more pleased, by the celebration of the birthday of the Seal of Prophets, Sayydinā Muḥammad (saws). But those around his Majesty coming so many Wahhābī people they are saying that is "O Sulṭān, that is bid‘ah. Don't do this, leave it! Leave it." That is their advice. And his Majesty also making that celebration slowly, slowly making it not to be seen, not to be heard. Making the day of birthday of the Seal of Prophets as one as ordinary day. No! You know ‘ulamā’, O that you are saying we are, salafī ‘ulamā’. Yes, you are advising to his Majesty not to do birthday celebration for the Seal of Prophets. But, you are saying nothing if his Majesty making celebration for his honoured princes and princesses. No one saying "that is bid‘ah, it is not true." But to make a celebration for the Seal of Prophets they're saying "no, it is not true."

Allāh should send on your head a headache that no any physician may find a [cure] cure, curing you. Coming to you. Coming to your heads, that you should drink full of...hand tablets. But not stopping. Because you are such a people saying, "No for the most glorified one in Divinely Presence Sayyidinā Muḥammad (saws), for his birthday you are saying "that is bid‘ah." It is not true. But you are not saying if his Majesty Sulṭān making a ceremony for his crown prince you are running for that celebration and saying "O congratulation and long life, long, long happiness to his, prince, his highness prince or princess." Allāh Almighty should send on you O salafī ‘ulamā’ in Brunei, on your head that no one should find a way to stop it, that paining through your heads. And you are - it is suitable for you. Yes. You should understand what is true way and wrong way. You should understand, that you must give your most high honour and glorifying for the Seal of Prophets Sayyida l-awwalīn wal-ākhirīn. Yes.

What we are saying? We are saying, "why tiring?" Why tiring? Say! To your attenders. As-salāmu ‘alaykum our attenders. Beware and hear, listen and keep heavenly advising. Which thing, making a person to be tired? Say to people. You can't say, we may say bi-idhni Llāh. Because - you are doing everything not for your Lord's pleasure. You are following your egos, to make your egos happy. If anyone running to make his ego happy that effort, that works making you to be tired. Everything that it is not for the sake of the Lord of Heavens, it is a heavy burden on the shoulders of Kings and Queens, and prince and princess, and governors and and... presidents and so on. O people! You must know why you are getting tired. Why people tiring, because they are not working for Allāh Jalla Jalālahu subḥānahu wa-ta‘ālá ‘ammā yushrikūn.

Which thing making people to be tired. O people! You must understand and you must ask to aẓhari sh-sharīf ‘ulamā’, and Dr. of Sharī‘ah, and salafī ‘ulamā’ - why man tiring? And usually never going to be tired mankind. O salafī ‘ulamā’ say, if any time any day, any hour, the Seal of Prophets he said "I am tired". Say! Never said. Any, Ḥadīth books writing that Prophet said "I am tired."? He never tired! But, we are tiring including myself also. Yes, and I am asking forgiveness when I am tiring. Anyone who works for Allāh, never tiring. Whole Muslim world and humanity must know that if they are working for Allāh, they never going to be tired. Always they are like giants. Giants powerful, powerful, their hands powerful, their palms powerful, their bodies powerful, their brain, their heads powerful. And every organ powerful. I must say this because, because Kings and Princes umarā’, wa l-mulūk and... and Shaykhs and, rich ones, they are tiring. If a man tiring, that means every organ from him, tiring. Never making shahādah, always down! Good understanding, Shaykhs. And Mulūk, because you are not doing for Allāh, therefore like this - your organs downstairs. If you are working for Allāh, you never getting to be tired! Like the Seal of Prophet - always like this! But you are tense, pole just broken, down. You may run to East to West to find a cure, you can't find. Because if you are tiring, every organ tired, finish.

When you are tiring you must understand, where are you? Salafī ‘ulamā’, say to people in Arabic in Turkish, in Persian, in Urdu. People tiring because they are not living for Allāh, because they are not working for Allāh, because, they are agents of shayṭān, because they are working for shayṭān, and they are going to be tired here and hereafter. May Allāh forgives us. That is an important point. Allāh Allāh, Allāh Allāh. Therefore, I am making du‘ā’, who coming against me to be tired! To be tired, if from East to West doctors they can't find any, any cure for them. Don't assault on me, I am sometimes easy one, sometimes I am very hard one, difficult one.

That is, that is... a celebration khuṭbah (association) What association! Khuṭbah, declaration may be for the honor of the holy month Ramaḍān. Mubārak Ramaḍān Karīm. If you are not listening, you're eating, you're tiring. Half way, you are going to be tired. Take care O Mulūk, Shaykhs, rich ones, Umarā’ and others. If you are asking to be always in act, and in power, try to do everything for Allāh. You never getting to be tired, therefore they are making me to say to you to ask if the Seal of Prophet he said "Oh I am tired."? Which Ḥadīth? No. Beware an heavenly cursing, heavenly punishment. Do everything for Allāh and you are never getting to be tired. You may be 90 years, 100 years, but you are not tired. You may be 18, 20, 25, but you are not working for Allāh, you are going to be tired. Your physical being becoming to erimek melting, melting... That is the reason that countless hospitals, and at the entrance of hospitals countless Shaykhs, Umarā’, Mulūk, presidents coming for what? For which thing? Because they are feeling we are tiring. "O my doctor, you can't find any new medicine takes from me that ‘adamu iqtidār, inability? Please sir. Please sir. You must look for me." Finish! You're tired because you lived for shayṭān not for Raḥmān, you are going to be melted, to be finished.

May Allāh forgives us. Amān Yā Rabbī. That is this tonight is first night of holy Ramaḍān. Try to do everything for Allāh, then you are not going to be hungry or angry or thirsty, or tired. Give and take. You're not giving for Allāh, you can't take anything back. May Allāh forgives me and forgives you also.
