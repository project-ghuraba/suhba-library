As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Everything happens by the will of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. We read this ayatu l-karīmah in the khuṭbah: "Wa-in yamsaska Llāhu bi-ḍurrin fa-lā kāshifa lahu illā Hū" (6:17). When AllāhNone wants something to harm you, no one can remove it except HeNone. And when HeNone wants something good to happen to someone, it will certainly reach him. Some people ask why something happened to them. It happened because it was the wish of AllāhNone. It happened by the will of AllāhNone. If they believe in this and keep patience, they will earn their reward – not only reward, but also they will have peace. They should believe that these things happened because HeNone wanted them to happen. They should believe that if AllāhNone wants, HeNone can take it away and give them relief by belief in AllāhNone. They should believe in this.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla said so in Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n, HisNone Eternal Word, to the Holy ProphetNone and to us. It is the beautiful Word of AllāhNone. If everyone believes and accepts it, all kinds of goodness will happen for them. And if they complain about things that happened to them, they will be devastated trying to figure out what is the reason, if they fell down or ate something wrong, or they drank something. It came from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. HeNone wanted this. Just like HeNone wanted these hardships, only Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla can remove them and give you relief. And when HeNone wishes people good, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gives goodness. No one can prevent HisNone wish. No one can prevent HimNone. Even if the whole world is against, it cannot prevent the will of AllāhNone. And when AllāhNone gives from HisNone favours, even people, who think they are something important, resist with all their weapons and bombs, those favours will reach people.

This is our condition now. The poor condition of this world is because of faithlessness. This illness has come. While people are guessing if it started in China or America, in one place or another, from such and such animal, they are forgetting the main source. And because they forget, they cannot reach a conclusion. As a result, it happened by the will of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. It may have occurred in a factory or in a laboratory, in a farm or in an animal market. If it wasn't the will of AllāhNone, it wouldn't have occurred. And as it is by the will of AllāhNone, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla will remove it as well. This is the way it is. And there is a ḥadīth of our Holy ProphetNone: a bedouin asked him, when he heard this āyah, "Should we get treatment when we get sick?" "Of course be treated," said our Holy ProphetNone. But know that cure comes with the belief in AllāhNone.

The cure is from AllāhNone. You must accept this in order to be cured. If you say that a doctor cures you, some people believe in AllāhNone but still they think cure is coming from the doctor, then it has no benefit for them. Eventually, mankind will pass away anyway. But when they say that by the favour of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, this medicine was effective and the cure came with this doctor by the order of AllāhNone, then they will receive its reward. They will be rewarded for sickness and make shukr to AllāhNone for recovery. They will have not forgotten AllāhNone.

People of the end of times swear at diseases and say various things. They blame this and that. No need to blame anyone. Everything is possible in this world. Nothing is impossible in this world. Prepare yourself accordingly. As we always say, it is dunyā here, not a Paradise. When you go to Paradise, everything will be nice. And anything can happen any moment here – anything – good and bad. But that is not important. It is important to guard our faith. You are being tested. And a test can be very big, may AllāhNone protect us. You can both fail the test and commit a sin. Therefore, we should know that everything is by the kindness and favour of AllāhNone.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone said, "Everything by HisNone will." It's the āyah we read. "Wa-in yamsaska Llāhu bi-ḍurrin fa-lā kāshifa lahu illā Hū" (6:17). This what AllāhNone wants. If HeNone wants something difficult for you, it must be. Because it is HisNone will. HeNone can... Nobody can stop in front of HisNone will. And it is, can be this from AllāhNone but you must be believe for this. What happen, it's coming from AllāhNone. If it is bad – also from AllāhNone. But who he has belief, he must believe also AllāhNone, HeNone will take this from on him. If any illness or other thing happen, AllāhNone, when HeNone want – you pray for, ask HimNone for to take this bad thing on, from you – HeNone can do. Nobody else can do. And when HeNone want something for people, for good thing for human or for men or for women, nobody can prevent this from to be happen. Even whole countries coming against this, nothing can be happened against this. Because AllāhNone will stronger from anything.

And it is good tiding for us, for people who are nowadays, they are hopeless, they are anxious, they have every "Why this happen?" Because they don't have belief. If they believe for this, they will be rest and happy because they know this is the will of AllāhNone and it will be happen. If you are happy or not happy, it happen. But if you are happy and believe what happen, AllāhNone will reward you and maybe take this from you, make you safe. But if not, what happen now, if you see whole world like they said, world war. No, no any country they safe from this. Everybody, they are anxious and most of people, they have problem, psychology problem from this. Little or more, small or big, they have this. Why, for what this? Because they are only believe everything happen, happen by themself, by other people, by maybe other country. Now they are living in this situation of illness. They are accusing from maybe China, they accusing America, they accusing Europe, they accusing anything else. But all this what happen by will of AllāhNone. it's coming from laboratory or factory or coming from farm or coming from grocery or other thing – all this, it is by will of AllāhNone. Nothing can be without of HisNone will. Everything by HisNone will and you must believe for this.

There is, they trying hard to take this belief from people and they are nearly succeed. Most of people they don't have belief. Even Muslim, they make them to not understanding spirituality. They understand only material. This is, this it must be like this but this is normal calculator for dunyā. But Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone has endless thing. You cannot imagine what HeNone has. So, it is important to be believer. And when you are believer, you are rest and happy. Of course for...

Also Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, one bedouin coming for himNone and asking, "If we are ill, we must take medicine?" Because this he, they know Arabic well and when they hear in Qur’ān like this maybe he said, "We mustn't take. Because everything from AllāhNone, we wait to AllāhNone make us [well]." But ProphetNone said, "Of course, you must do this." Because life, human being life, it is everything it must be inside: growing and looking after themself, to teaching, doctor or teacher or master – it must be. So Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam say, "Yes, you must treat yourself." And the... but what you, when do this don't say, "This medicine make me healthy." This medicine, by will of AllāhNone. "AllāhNone give shifā’ for this medicine and we are thanking AllāhNone for this." This is what we must do. We must thank AllāhNone for good doctor, HeNone also give shifā’ by his hand. Because many doctors, they can do anything but when AllāhNone not, HeNone don't want shifā’ for, cannot even the most richest one, they will not find cure. So for this also we must look for treatment but it is also not forget Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, WhoNone put secret in this treatment. Sometimes must be good, sometimes not good. So, we must also looking after treatment, in shā’a Llāh.

AllāhNone keep us safe and strong īmān – this is the most important thing. Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
