As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

What Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla loves is that people, when they do something, do it well – both materially and spiritually. But it should not be done for this world. It should be for ākhirah – for the pleasure of AllāhNone. We should try our best to do good and beautiful things. Especially in our worship; Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla loves people who make ablution and pray perfectly. HeNone loves those who do their worship well.

People should pray on time and not leave it to be made up later. That is most acceptable. A missed prayer is like stale, sour food. Because praying is the most important worship. To do it perfectly is to make a good ablution and to pray well, and not to miss its time. You should do it on time and not delay making it up. When it is late, its reward is not even one thousandth part of a prayer made on time. But still your farḍ (obligatory prayer) is considered as done. When you don't perform your farḍ, you make more work for yourself. You have to make it up. And if you don't make it up in this life, you will when you go to ākhirah. If you have a trustworthy family, they will make kaffārah (atonement) for you. You are redeemed a little then. If you don't do it now, then you will do it in ākhirah. For each prayer, you will pray 80 years in hell, AllāhNone forbid.

People underestimate the importance of prayer. The worship of AllāhNone is not something to be underestimated. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gave it to you as a beautiful gift. And when you turn away from it, you will regret it, in a place where regret is not useful. Therefore, Muslims should make up their missed prayers; for sometimes that can happen. If you make one of the missed prayers, together with each prayer and the sunnah and nafl prayers, with the permission of AllāhNone, AllāhNone will accept it. And you will be released from the liability of that farḍ. As we said, even if they don't... if AllāhNone has given good children to some people, they will make kaffārah for what their families didn't do. That is possible.

Also, in every prayer someone performs, as Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim used to do, they can make intention to do it on behalf of their parents as well. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla accepts their prayers, and rewards them and their parents both, without dividing it. Meaning, the reward won't be divided between them and their parents. Equal reward will go to all of them. Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says in ḥadīth sharīf, to do your good deeds with this intention. That is the benefit of a good child.

People don't want good children now, they want educated children. And then they see that there is no good in these children and feel regret. "What should we do? We have suffered so much for our child. And now he is causing more suffering. He is not doing anything right." This is how people are. May AllāhNone, in shā’a Llāh, let people teach goodness to their children. May AllāhNone give good Muslim children to all of us. May AllāhNone protect all of them from the evil of shayṭān.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone love who people making thing what they done to do it with perfect. Good, not without looking for it is okay or not okay, just to do it. Because most of this people time, they are doing job or anything, without looking carefully for this. Everything, this for this world, working or job or other thing – and especially, for worshipping also, for spiritual side of this life. And especially for praying. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone order us to pray and this order, everybody can do easily, especially for looking for your wuḍū’ and after, praying, praying in time. This is the perfect, for people to do it. Who do this, he is perfect, no problem.

Some say you must say, "Like this, you must say like that, you must be very slow, you must be make takbīr ten times." No, no need for this. Quickly, you make your wuḍū’ and pray. If you have jamā‘ah, it is better. If no jamā‘ah – by yourself, by time. Don't let your praying to another time for qaḍā’. Qaḍā’, who time between each time, from morning Fajr until Ẓuhr, from Ẓuhr until ‘Aṣr, from ‘Aṣr until Maghrib, from Maghrib until ‘Ishā’ and from ‘Ishā’ to... This is time for, until Fajr. So we, we don't miss it. This is perfect, what AllāhNone like. But people maybe they missed and they pray later. This is like an old meal you eat; cold, or even become little bit sour. So, like this. If you leave it more for later, maybe you not pray at all. After some years, or some month you pray. This become also, more not good. But also acceptable, because they make in their life. But the reward for this, it is less – more than thousand or hundred thousand time – from praying in time of ṣalāh. And maybe not praying at all, after they're...

Some people they have children, they was, even their father mother not looking for religion, They realise this, and they make for them charity. For each praying, there is, they must pay something. And they pay, and AllāhNone accept also this. But who they don't do anything for themself, it is problem for them for ākhirah. Each time, each praying, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, eighty year they will pray this ma‘ādha Llāh in jahannam, in hell. Until finish, and then they go to paradise. Eighty year, many people, they cannot reach eighty year here whole life. For each praying, eighty year.

So it is, when we are doing this in dunyā, it is big favour for us and AllāhNone happy with us. Because HisNone order this. HeNone done this because HeNone give you very good thing, very valuable thing, and you make by your hand like this; after, nobody help you from your relative or son or children. So you will be like this. Because you don't, you didn't teach your children also to do something for you. Because many people they have good children. They teach their children good, and the children – even their father and mother they wasn't pray – they, after their death, they make this paying for them and to charity, and save them. And many of them also, they pray – Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying: when you make charity, or you pray, or you do anything good; who say, "This also for my mother, for my father, also same."

This is ḥadīth from Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. AllāhNone accept this, and reward all of them. Each one same – not taking from who child or son who is doing this, half or third – no. Each one, the complete thing AllāhNone reward them. For this, it is very important to teach children, your grandchildren also, to be good people. It is very important to teach them goodness. Nowadays people, they are not teaching children goodness, only they are teaching something which in school and university. And this no any goodness – they not teaching any goodness. Only they teach, maybe science – more than science, they teach to be against everything. This is what they are teaching, and they thought it is good. And after this, they are when finish, and their children running away from them and coming against them, "What is this, why our children like this? They are not doing anything good. Even job, their job also not good. Only looking for themself." This is punishment for this people, in dunyā before ākhirah. Because in ākhirah, if they was good, all they will be good and happy, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone save whole these, youngster and old people, from this bad teaching, bad thinking. To guide them to good thing, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
