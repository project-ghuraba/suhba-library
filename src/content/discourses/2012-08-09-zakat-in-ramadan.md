As-Salamu Alaikum wa Rahmatullahu wa Barakatuhu. Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fil jamia'

Today also, another worship of Ramadan, of course it can be performed outside of Ramadan as well but because it is more virtuous to give it in Ramadan most people give their zakat in Ramadan. Zakat is a very important thing. People can't fully understand this. There is an amount that deserves to give zakat. It is an amount as 100 grams of gold. And that amount of 100 grams of gold should be with you for one year, one year has to pass. After one year passes, you give 2.5% of it as zakat.

It is not like you give that 2.5% as a charity or good deed but it already does not belong to you anymore. Because giving it is fardh. What happens when it is fardh? You fall into big sin, very big sin when you don't do it. And when you fulfill it, performing one fardh is more virtuous than performing 1 million sunnah because it is the basic. And when you are performing it..

It doesn't belong to you anymore. Even if you didn't give it, it is that you don't fulfill the fardh & also that 2,5% mixes with your wealth, as haram. It is haram in every way- you are wrongfully seizing it & you also are stealing it like a thief, unfair to other's right, Allah's right. So when you don't do it, you are losing in every way.

Hadith Sharif says "Zakat increases wealth". So because you give 2.5% don't say "I lost 2.5%". Allah gives at least 10% more, not 10% more but 10x times more gives Allah (awj) for it- gives thawab & also replaces it, by Allah's permission. Everything the Prophet (sas) says is true. It increases wealth.

So when people give it in Ramadan it is beautiful but different beautiful- it is a custom of our ancestors. Otherwise zakat is given annually, if you like you can give it in other months also. You can give it in any month. But when it is in Ramadan you receive 2x more thawab & you also remember it. If it is other months, mostly people forget about it.

Therefore this is an important matter, this zakat matter. You should be happy when giving it. Don't make it like you are doing a favor for them by giving zakat. This is not a good thing at all. Today we speak about worshipping in Ramadan and this is worshipping, outside of Ramadan also can be, zakat. What is zakat? This is order from Allah, to give when you have around 100

gram of gold, you must give after one year 2.5% zakat. You can give any month, but in Ramadan it is more thawab. Allah giving more reward to you.

And in Ramadan, you remember this. You remember this for fasting month, and many coming from, custom from old salih/good people, they was making in Ramadan. But this, everytime you can give. But in Ramadan you remember this is month you must give zakat also.

And zakat, one of five obligatory in Islam. When you do this, you must do it. For if you don't do, you are in big sin. Why? Because first of all, this is order & obligatory. It is, you can make million not obligatory things, like sunnah or nafila you cannot reach one obligatory from Islam because it is the main from Islam. So, this is first.

Second one, this 2.5% from money which you have, it is not yours anymore. It is for Allah. When you give this, you are in big reward from Allah. If not give, big sin you have. Another thing, this is not yours anymore. 2.5%, it is not your money. And this money, you must take it out. If you not take out, you are like thief, thief also.

Many thing in this, wisdom from zakat, you must give it. You are obligatory to give this. If you not give, this money still in your.. in your account or you have it with you this money, it is not clean anymore. And it is you put in your money, all money become dirty. Because coming dirty money, this is dirty, no barakah for this.

And Prophet (sas) saying "zakat making money, making goods to be more & more." Because Allah also (awj) saying, "when you give one, Allah give you ten." Many wisdom from zakat.

And when you give zakat, you must be happy to give it. Not to make people as you are giving, you are proud when you give this. No, you must be happy, Allah making you to give this zakat.

And many people, when they become more rich, become more greedy. Because maybe if he have 1 million, must be give 25 000. If he have 10 million, 250 000 he must give. And when he see, looking, "this is very big money. I can buy 4-5 car for this money. Why I will give this?" This is what happen, ego make people to be greedy. Because amount getting big & big. And greedy people, they even not giving 5 pound. So when it is like this and shaytan more easy for him to make them to not give.

This is very important. Everything you give first you must say for zakat, our money. If it is more, you give more than zakat so this also coming like sadaqa or.. You are not losing anything. Allah, insha Allah not leave us for our ego or for our shaytan. Because really, Hadith Sharif saying "the greedy is obeying- "shuhhan muta'an/greediness obeyed" This is Hadith Sharif, you obey the greediness. Many, most of people like this. Not many, most of people. This is very important point- zakat, it is not yours anymore. Don't put in this money. If you put in water dirty thing, all water coming.. You cannot drink anything. But if you put from water to making bigger,you put for another thing. This is very important.

Insha Allah Allah make us all to give without regret. Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, Al Fatiha.
