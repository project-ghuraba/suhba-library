Dastur ya Sayyidi La ilaha illa Allah La ilaha illa Allah La ilaha illa Allah Sayyidina wa Nabiyuna Muhammadin Abduhu wa Habibuhu wa Rasuluhu zidhu Ya Rabbi izzan wa sharafa nuran wa surura ridwanan wa sultana wa man tabi`ahu bi ihsan ila Yawm al Deen.

Assalamu alaykum Ibadallah, servants of Allah Almighty. And, salamu alayka Ya Sayyidina Qutb al Arifin, Qutb al Mutasarrif who is looking after everything through this Creation on Earth. He is responsible. One is enough for being responsible for every creature that is living on this planet. He is responsible. And his power may reach everything in existence on this planet through dunya. That is heavenly nizam, order. Heavenly order.

Assalamu alaykum attenders from Mankind. Attending and asking to hear and to learn and to understand and to be more close to heavenly stations. And we are saying: Audhu Billahi min as shaytan al rajim. O our Lord! We are humbly asking on behalf of the Lord of Heavens that the one who is responsible for this planet makes us understand something. And like the Seal of Prophets, he is advising all nations. What he is advising? His advising is so perfect. His advising is saving people from darkness and from badness, from evil and from devils. Showing a way. And we are asking humbly because we don't know.

Allah Almighty is saying:

Fas'al Bihi Khabeeran (25:59) Ask the one who knows.

O Salafi ulamas! What you are understanding when Allah Almighty is saying: Fas'al Bihi Khabeeran. Hmm? Yes, that is an ocean. Therefore they are making me address Salafi ulamas. Because they are so proud and are saying, "We are Salafi ulamas". Ehhh, what does it mean: Fas'al Bihi Khabeeran? It is a command, an order and also heavenly t`alimaat, instruction. Everyone must ask that: Fas'al Bihi Khabeeran. Three words may change the face of this planet and may change the minds of all people living on this planet. That is a heavenly address, a declaration and an order. And it is for those who have a responsibility for being servants of their Lord, Almighty Allah. He means to say it to his most glorified servant, the most honoured one in His Divine Presence, Sayyidina Muhammad (sas). First He is addressing him. And then the addressing is through that most glorified, most honoured and most beloved one’s understanding. First responsibility is for the Seal of Prophets (sas). The order, khitab, addressing is to the Seal of Prophets (sas), His most beloved, most glorified and majestic one in Divine Presence: Fas'al Bihi Khabeeran. The Seal of Prophets, Sayyidina Rasulullah (sas) is: La Tukallafu Illa Nafsaka (4:84)

Mukallaf, responsibility is only for the Seal of Prophets (sas). His most majestic one, most glorified one. “You must ask Me. And, all prophets must ask (you) anything for learning, understanding, looking and seeing, hearing and obeying. You are My representative. O My most beloved one. They can't be able to ask Me. Impossible. They will be burned. They can't address Me but, from among the prophets they may ask to you. Prophets ask the Seal of Prophets (sas) and he is that one who is giving what they are asking. Fas'al Bihi Khabeeran. So many things everyone may ask.

All prophets also are asking. They are asking but there is no way for any one of the prophets directly to the Divine Presence, to the Lord of Divinity. Uluhiyyah. Divinity, yes. First, prophets are responsible to ask as they are in need of an honoured one who has connections with divinely levels. And divine levels are over heavenly levels. No one can pass from heavenly levels, up. Even Archangel Gabriel. When the Seal of Prophets (sas) was saying, "Come with me", he was saying, "I can't, that is only for one, only for you O Habibullah, Ya Muhammad (sas). Only for you to pass from here, up!” Where are the Wahabiyin? Raghman ala anfun, Inspite of their foolishness.

They are seeing too much. If a person coming in his holy tomb and making their hands like this, they are saying 'shirk, shirk'. No-mind people. Allah Almighty is welcoming him. Only him. No one can pass up even a hair (breadth). Archangel Gabriel is saying, "If I am coming to pass even a little bit, like a hair, that Divine Light or Lahut, Divinity, would burn me and I would never be able to come back as I am now. For you only. No one else passing from this up, only you”. That is Allah Almighty's Divine Order to all prophets, saints and messengers. For all of them this t`aleem, Instructions.

All prophets can't ask anything directly. Must ask the one who is Khabira. He is Khabira, Sayyidina Muhammad (sas). Not anyone else is going to be Khabira. You must learn Salafi ulamas! Don't say "We are following that imam, that imam, that imam”. No, they are knowing nothing. Allah Almighty is first addressing to anbiya, to all prophets, if they are not understanding something, you must ask that one who is My representative from pre-Eternal up to Eternal. He is representing Me. No one can be representative for Me. I am only making that one.

So that asking was beginning from Adam (as). He was asking from him because no one can address their Lord without a wasilah, a means. Yes, Adam (as) can't speak. He may speak to the Seal of Prophets (sas). Abraham (as) is not able to address. For understanding something he must ask the Seal of Prophets (sas). He is Khabira. He knows about Creation, not anyone else. Noah (pbuh) can't reach the Divine Presence of the Lord of Divine Levels. He was asking from the Seal of Prophets, Sayyidina Rasulullah (sas). Moses (pbuh) can't ask if on Tur Sina, there wasn’t one Reality of the Seal of Prophets (sas). His Reality is like endless oceans. Therefore, they are making me say ‘one’. One of his heavenly powers. Sayyidina Musa (as) was speaking and he never would be able to come to be with the Lord of Heavens. He needed there to be between his Lord and him a mediator, a wasilah. That is an endless ocean. So that: Fas'al Bihi Khabiran.

O Mankind! A Heavenly Order. You must ask because you are knowing nothing! Yes, you may say, "We are knowing so many things through technology”. Maybe, but what is beyond technology? Which one is giving that authority for technology to be under your command. Who is that one? And Mankind now is not thinking. They are thinking that we are reaching everything through our mentalities. Maybe your mentalities are helping you, but your mentalities are an inner attribute of you. You are the deep-being of a creature. You are in need to come in touch with your Creator and no one is able to reach that point to ask from their Creator. Allah Almighty is making this mediator for all angels and prophets. They must ask through the Seal of Prophets (sas). The Lord of Heavens through His Divinity is teaching His most beloved representative without saying Fas'al Bihi Khabiran.

Where is the Seal of Prophets (sas) going to ask? He knows what he is in need of. What should Sayyidina Rasulullah ask something for? He is in Divine Presence through his divinity. But He is making the Seal of Prophets (sas), the last one as the Lord's mutlaq, Absolute Representative. So ask from him. And, all prophets were asking to learn something from his spirituality oceans. And that is in the Holy Quran. The Lord of Heavens is addressing the Seal of Prophets (sas) that were he not to be in existence, no one would be in existence.

The Lord of Heavens is ordering all nations, "If you are not understanding, you must ask Khabir, the expert who is knowing what you are in need of. If I am giving to him he is knowing. But not what belongs to Me, no one is knowing that ocean's muntaha, end and depth. And all prophets were saying that they are asking understanding from the Seal of Prophets, Sayyid Rusul Kiram, Sayyidina Muhammad (sas). And, we must ask because we have been created to know something.

Therefore when Last Day coming and angels are questioning common people, then, every nation will be questioned by their prophet. And all prophets will be questioned by the Seal of Prophets (sas). As well he is going to be questioned by his Lord, Almighty Allah, to show people that he is responsible also and, “I am asking him”.

You must have someone who knows something more than you, you must ask and you must learn. And Allah Almighty is ordering the Nation of the Seal of Prophets (sas) if you are not knowing or understanding you must ask who knows about that one. Therefore I am asking Salafi ulamas who is that one that you are asking? They may say that "We are asking from our ulamas". Ulamas, from whom are they asking? Asking, asking, and reaching the Seal of Prophets (sas). So you must ask, you must try to find someone who is carrying you to the holy presence of the Seal of Prophets (sas) and then ask. On the Day of Resurrection, Judgement Day don't think that Allah Almighty is making Judgement for everyone, no. But there are so many representatives on behalf of the Lord of Heavens, they will be questioning their nations.

And angels in the graveyard are asking:

“Who is your Lord? Man Rabbuk?” “Rabbi Allah, my Lord is Allah Almighty”, you may say. “From whom you learned? Yes, from whom you learned?” “We learned from the Seal of Prophets (sas) most glorified one”. “Yes, okay”. “Who was means between you and that one?” He will ask. "That one. This one. That one." “Do you think that they were Khabir? Expert for everything? We are looking for the teacher that taught you to say, 'Who is your Lord?' ‘My Lord is Allah’. We are going to look who is that one and from whom did he learn this answer. He asked or without asking he was knowing? No one can learn something without asking”.

Therefore, I am asking now Salafi ulamas ‘Who is that one?’ From whom you are learning your knowledge to where you are? On which base you are building your knowledge? On a rock? On a tree? On a throne? On Earth, on heavens? Where you learned? Who is that one for whom in Holy Quran the Lord of Heavens is saying: Fas'al Bihi Khabiran. If you are asking to learn something you must find someone who is authorised to answer what you are asking. Unauthorised peoples' answers are of no value, ‘go away’.

Therefore O People, now very, very, very few people are asking something about Real Being of this life. Very, very, very few ones are asking their personality. Who am I? Yes, he may look in mirror and say, "I think that is mine, I am that one”. But how am I learning that that I am that one? I am looking and seeing but I must have two witnesses to say, "Yes we are witnesses that you are the one you are seeing through that mirror”. There is certainty, yaqeen, in Islam. Certainty must be through Reality. If no certainty it is not Reality. Can't be, can't be. You must ask this, "Do you think that I am that one?" "Yes", he may say, "Yes, you are this one". Without asking you may look but you can't say, ‘I am this one’.

Downstairs open, Wahabi people. Up. Down. Up and down. We must look, measuring everything. "I am that one, this one". You are in need witness. You must have a witness for your personality and for your existence through this life. If not, never accepted your shahadah, witnessing.

O People, there are countless doors of knowledge. Neverending oceans. Give daily even 5 minutes for understanding. You can't understand if there is not someone there who is making you to understand. Therefore all prophets are that ones giving understanding to people, giving their real identity for them. Believer or non-believer. And that is an endless ocean.

O People come and listen, not to be regretting one day. This is free teaching and this teaching is heavenly teaching. This is not from my knowledge that I learned, no. That is something else. It is obligatory for everyone to ask such a question and to learn what it means. If you are not doing you are going to be taken from Humanity and sent to the level of non-understanding creatures. As you like. May Allah forgive me.

Allah Allah...
