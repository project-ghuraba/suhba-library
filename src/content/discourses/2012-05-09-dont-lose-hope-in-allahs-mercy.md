Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Assalatu wa s-salamu ala Rasulina Muhammad Sayyidi l Awwalin wa l-Akhirin. Madad ya Rasul Allah, madad ya S. Abdullah Faiz al Daghestani, madad ya S. Nazim al Haqqani, dastur. Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fil jamia' With the order of Mawlana, we may say a few words insha Allah.

Allah (awj) says, "Don't lose hope in Allah". Allah is the Forgiver of sins. Some people look & say, "we have so much sin, we are already in fire so we may as well continue like this." There is no such thing. "Allah forgives sins. Don't lose hope in Allah" He says. No matter how many sins you have, still Allah is the Forgiver. "Ghafur" means most Forgiver, "Ghaffar". "My Mercy precedes My Wrath" says Allah (awj). Then it says in Hadith "If you don't commit sins, Allah takes you away & in your stead He brings people who sin. And when they repent & ask forgiveness from Allah, He forgives them". Therefore Allah (awj) for sure forgives no matter how many your sins are.

But man should also be aware of his sins. He can't do as he likes, either. The most important thing.. Ok you do by mistake but..Now most people, especially in this country say I don't pray,don't fast. I drink but have a clean heart". It can't be like this. Cleanliness of the heart is different, being aware of your sins is different. When you say "we are drowning in our sins for all these years. They went over our head &we sink under them, but may You forgive us", Allah (awj) forgives. In fact if it is true repentance, because you repented your sins He turns them into sawab-good deeds. Allah (awj) is that Merciful & that Generous.

Therefore when you see a person who commit sins, no need to get angry & curse at him but instead, by giving advice & telling of the good ways that person, insha Allah, will come to these good ways. And if that person repents through you (by you being a wasila) Allah (awj) says "this is better for you than this world". And in Hadith also says "Guiding a person is better for you than all that is on the earth".

We speak today about, many people if they are making too much sins they become hopeless. They say "We are sinking in sins, so no need to make tawba. Allah cannot forgive us". They want to continue like this. No. Allah (awj), how much it is too much you have sin, also better forgive these people. In Quran also says "Don't be hopeless from Allah. Allah, He is the most Merciful for people" (39:53). Even Hadith saying, "if you not to sin, I make you... for want forgive from Allah, Allah take you & bring people, they make sin & they want forgivness from Allah,Allah forgive them".

So don't be hopeless from Allah. If it is, you never make any good thing, from now on you can change. Allah change this sins to good things. It is like this. But people, this is what people they know they have, they do this sins & they apologize from Allah & want to forgive them. But there is people especially in this area, they said "we are never praying, never fasting. We drink alcohol, we do everything. But our heart, it is clean". This is not true. Not true. You must be humble. You must be, accept your sin. And when you accept your sin, Allah change this.

This is very good attribute from Allah. Allah everytime give people, human being, something to come to, to make them to forgive them. Especially somebody saying "this people in Europe or another place and they are well educated but they don't know Allah. How this?" If they were educated, they must be knowing. They read everything. They read everything, even for you never hear such religions, they know about this. But for Islam they don't know anything & they hit Islam also. But Allah, even for this people, when they coming to Islam Allah forgive them. And Islam, it is not different from Judaism, from Christianity. Real Judaism & real Christianity same line, coming from Adam (as), all Prophets coming, last Prophet our Prophet Muhammad (sas).

So we invite from here, this people to research for Islam better. Not from one side, from other side they must look. And of course Muslim different, Islam different. Muslim people they are, most of them they not following Islam, real Islam. For this, they think "this is Islam". And they are wrong. But if they look for Islam & by clean heart, Allah, they will be.. Allah, He will be give this light for them, heart & Allah make them Muslim insha Allah.

Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, Al Fatiha.
