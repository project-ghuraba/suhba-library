Sheikh Bahauddin Adil Al-Haqqani sohbat of the 12th of December, 2014.

Alhamdulillah that we are the nation of Sayyidina Muhammad (saws). We are Ummata Muhammadin (saws). And we have to love Sayyidina Muhammad, at least as he loves us. Sayyidina Muhammad he was crying for his ummah. He was crying, saying - "Ummati, ummati!" Alhamdulillah we've been honored to be his ummah. And we must love Sayyidina Muhammad (saws).

Alhamdulillah today is a Friday. Do you think why this Friday is holy day? Because of Sayyidina Muhammad (saws). It's been granted to him and he gave it as a gift to his ummah. It is every week we have a feast, we have eid because of Friday. Friday it is the most important day. Salatul Eid is not as important as Salatul Juma. Salatul Juma it is must have, must do, must come, because the manifestation that is coming in Salatul Juma it does't have in any prayer.

Masha'Allah I was so happy to see this crowd, to see these people, to see these young ones coming for Salatul Juma; to get the manifestation of Salatul Juma. It is important for us to understand things that are as a key point in our life. Now the youngsters they need key points. Masha'Allah our Imam - I listen to his sohba when I'm coming - it was fantastic sohba. It was very good sohba. it was a touching sohba. It was teaching how to love Rasulullah (saws). And this is fantastic things to understand how to love RasulAllah (saws). We are the lover of Rasul. We are, we want to be, more in love to Rasulullah (saws).

This dunya, whatever you love in this dunya, you will end up like the marhum, like we just prayed janaza. Maybe he's the poorest man or he's the richest man, it doesn't make any difference. Maybe he is the one having the most happy life, or maybe he is having the most miserable life, also it doesn't matter any more. Finish! Time up! And Alhamdulillah he is lucky that he find jama'at to pray for him and to praise him and to make dua for him. Also this is important in our life.

Alhamdulillah we are believers. Alhamdulillah we are the one who want to continue as believers. Don't make anybody to spoil your mind by saying that Rasulullah is ordinary person; RasulAllah only he do his duty and he go; RasulAllah he is not the one. No! RasulAllah is different! Everything has been made because of RasulAllah. Why? Very simple, he is Habibullah! Allah Almighty He loves him, He praise him. He said "he is My Habibi. He is My beloved one!" How we cannot love him? If Allah Almighty love him, of course we have to love him more and more and more. There is no shirk by loving RasulAllah (saws). Not at all. Our life, our love, for him. We are weak servants. We are very weak. We cannot imagine how we are weak. But with RasulAllah's (saws) love we become stronger, we become more nice. We have the life in our life because of his love. This is RasulAllah. This is how important is RasulAllah in our life.

This is specially for the young. The young now in those days they go right and left and then they don't realize the love of RasulAllah (saws). We want the young to realize how RasulAllah is so nice; how he is full of love, and how he is caring about his ummah. This is important things in our life to know how RasulAllah was caring about his ummah. And he was saying to Sayyidina Omar "They are my beloved ones." Sayyidina Omar was saying "What about us?" He said "You are as'habi, you are my companions but these days people, they are my beloved ones. I love them so much. And I put them in the title that, they are my beloved."

Get this in your heart and make it grow up, grow up, and don't be jealous for RasulAllah (saws). Just be under his wing, under his eyes, under his vision. Try to be, to show yourself in his love. It is important for us to have such a things in our life because our life it will be only, sleep, wake up, go to work, come back, www.saltanat.org Page 1 sleep, work, get up, go work, come back. It is monotone. It is not excitement. When you have the love of RasulAllah, your life will be having a lot of excitement. It will make you feel alive. This is, maybe you pray, maybe you make ibada, but without saying "Ya RasulAllah, Sall Allah Alayk, Ya RasulAllah" it will not have the taste of ibada. Because everything is because of him. "Lawlaka lawlaka la ma khalaqtul aflaq." Allah says "If I'm not creating you I'm not bothered to create all this! Because of you I create everything." This is RasulAllah, so precious. So precious RasulAllah, so precious! Nobody can measure how he is precious for Allah Almighty. Yes! This is RasulAllah.

I wanted just to put this matter in the hand of you, in the heads of you, and finally end up in your heart. You have to make the biggest part in your heart to RasulAllah. Whatever coming in these dunya things, it is passing. Only true love never passes. This is the love of RasulAllah. Sayyidina Jalaluddin Rumi, he was famous by the loving and distributing love. And why he distributing love? From where he got this love? Of course he got it from RasulAllah and all the world knowing him. And what he said? He said "Lawla RasulAllah I cannot give one drop of love." He said "if there is no RasulAllah, I will not be able to give any small drop of love. I just catch the ocean of love of RasulAllah. And myself I will be enough for world to give this love." What about RasulAllah? Imagine how huge, how huge and you are part of his love. And you are one of his beloved ones. Imagine. This is important!

Important things in our love to understand RasulAllah (saws). Without understanding you cannot convey your love. You think he is different. You think, no he is with us, he is inside us. Many of you are sayyids. Many of you carrying his blood. Many of you, especially in Pakistan, so many masha'Allah - half of Pakistan, maybe of 3/4 of Pakistan, they are Sayyids. That's why I feel the love there is boiling, masha'Allah. Never come less, it is going up, and up up and up, rising for RasulAllah (saws).

May Allah forgives us, and may Allah make us in Divine Presence with RasulAllah and he will receive us saying "this people they've been loving me Ya Rabbi. I want them and I give them my shafa'a." May Allah give us his shafa'a. May Allah gives us to be a neighbour to him in his paradise. And to be with him, because we love him without seeing him. Imagine if we see him how we will be, SubhanAllah! May Allah forgive us and forgive me. And insha'Allah we meet up again and again... and insha'Allah this mosque will be forever shouting of the love of RasulAllah.

Wa min Allahi t-tawfiq, bi hurmati l-Habib, bi hurmati l-Fatiha.
