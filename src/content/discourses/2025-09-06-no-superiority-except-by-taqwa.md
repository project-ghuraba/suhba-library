As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh. A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Yā-ayyuhā n-nāsu innā khalaqnākum min dhakarin wa-unthá wa-ja‘alnākum shu‘ūban wa-qabā’ila li-ta‘ārafū inna akramakum ‘inda Llāhi atqākum” (49:13). Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla created us. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla created people as various tribes and communities. He created them in different colours. He settled them in the world. All of it happened by the command and will of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. But what people must understand here is that they should use their minds. They should use their minds and know why they were created. They must know what is good for them, so that they may know that among all people, the best is the most pious, who fears Allāh, and fulfils the commands of Allāh. This is the most virtuous and the best person; none else. “I am white, I am yellow, I am red, I am black” – there is no place for pride in that. There is no place to boast. No benefit. Because there is no benefit in colours and such things, in what nation you are, or what you belong to. When you close your eyes, after a month you too will become dust. The black is the same, the white the same, the red the same, the yellow the same. No benefit remains from it. These are passing things. The one who has taqwá, who fulfils the commands of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla and attains Allāh’s pleasure – this is the greatest benefit for a person. That is the gain. Nothing else has value. Therefore, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla has given people a mind and has also given them the will to use that mind. Is it partial or is it complete? Scholars say this and that. What matters is that Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla has given a mind. If one uses that mind, it will be good. Whoever does not use the mind has no benefit. “I used my mind, I became a doctor. I used my mind, I became an engineer. I used my mind, I became a bank manager. I became this, I became that.” Those are things that will bring no benefit. If you use these for Allāh’s pleasure, that is good. But if you use them for your nafs (ego), they have no benefit. The human mind must submit to Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla and obey His commands – nothing else. The mind shows this. Whoever does not use it does not have a complete mind. The mind reveals what is best. If it does not, it is not a true mind. Let no one count himself as intelligent. Whoever is on Allāh’s path should offer shukr to Allāh. Whether they have a mind or not is irrelevant; it means they do have one. Whoever fulfils the orders of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, no matter what, even if people dislike him, call him majdhūb or foolish, or do things against him – he is on the path, on the path of reason. But the others – the whole world, as we see, the whole world is under their command – but do they have a mind? You tell me. They have no mind. Without fear of Allāh, there is no benefit. And their end will be bad. May Allāh protect us. May Allāh not deprive any of us of mind. Mind is a blessing, an adornment, an ornament, a person’s adornment. If he does not use that adornment rightly, it means he cannot use it properly. May Allāh protect us.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, saying in these holy verses of Qur’ān: “We create you, all human being, from one man and woman, and we make you different tribe, different nations, different colour, different language.” You are all human being created from man and woman. Don’t listen to this people who are saying you are created from ape or from, I don’t know, from worm or from... Don’t listen for this. The most important, this: whole human being they are created from Ādam, ‘alayhi s-salām, and Eve, mother Eve, ‘alayhi s-salām, also. So, many tribe, many kinds of people – white, black, yellow, red, dark, light – all this, no any matter, no any... who proud with his colour or his nation, it is not good. Because Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying, “All this, between this, who is the best? White? Black?” He said, He not looking for colour or for language or for nations. He said “atqākum”: Who are, he is aware of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, to be afraid from, to do anything to make Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla not happy with him. The most thing, one beloved for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, this one. Even he is, anything from this nations, no anyone higher than other. No one higher than other. Only higher, one who Allāh love him and He happy with him. And this, He saying, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, describing the most fearful from to make Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla not happy with him. This is the most important and most benefit for human being. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla give us brain to think, and this brain, it is only for human being, they have. Other creature, they don’t have brain. They don’t have something to think. And they are not responsible. Responsibility all for mankind only, because Allāh give them this to use it, to know reality. Why? What is the benefit of brain? To know, benefit to know your Creator, and to follow His order. Many people, they think they are clever. They are studying, they are doing something, inventing... many thing they have. And they are proud for this. This is, no any proudness for, with this, in your this life. Nobody must be, if he has, use his brain, he must know this is not something to be proud. Because this, as much you are in this life clever, rich, or beautiful, or handsome, or powerful – all this only little period. After this, when you die, in after one month, two months, even this who are they are thinking they are most clever in the world, they, after, ten days or fifteen days because they first not burying the body, this clever European people, they are now new fashion, they burn it. But also, they still keep it out, maybe ten days, twenty days, maybe one month. After this, they put in oven and after five minutes, giving. “Take your father ashes, your mother ashes, your lover ashes and do what you like.” This is, this is what it will be for anybody of human being even, he become earth or become ashes. This is what happen. You are white, you are clever, you are smart, you are handsome, you are rich, you are powerful – nothing. Only what benefit it will be, for people who Allāh love them. This is what He say: “Akramakum ‘inda Llāhi atqākum” (49:13). The most precious one, good one, in Divine Presence, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, who is, he is following His order. Allāh make us to not lose our mind, to be thankful for Allāh, that He give us this, and in His way, in shā’a Llāh. Allāh give hidāyah for other also, in shā’a Llāh. Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
