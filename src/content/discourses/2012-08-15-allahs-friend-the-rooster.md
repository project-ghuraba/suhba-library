As-Salamu Alaikum. Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fil jamia' Today's sohbat: Everything in Islam is an opportunity to get reward. Allah has Karam/Generosity. For people to do more good deeds & gain more rewards there is a khayr, virtues in everything, even the things you least expect.

There are things which are already well known: A prayer performed in jama'at is 27 times more virtuous than a prayer performed alone. Even if you can't go to mosque, if you pray with your family, kids at home it is also considered as jama'at. And also, to be in the first row/saf behind the Imam. There it is the most virtuous row of the jama'at. The first row, it is the most virtuous row. Try, run to attain this more in the mosques. The one in here, here is dargah. Order of Mawlana for the first row, to encourage people he says, the ones who have beard & turban should be in the first row. Of course in other mosques most people don't even have a mustache. Therefore when you go to the big mosques try to be in the first row, it is virtuous. These are the known ones.

There are also some unknown ones. They are also very beautiful, very interesting things. For example, raising a rooster is also a virtuous thing. Prophet (sas) says "Don't curse the rooster. He is my friend- my friend, Allah's friend & also an enemy for my enemy" And who is enemy to the Prophet (sas)? Shaytan. Shaytan doesn't love roosters. Allah's wisdom. Why not love? Because it wakes people up at night for prayer. When two thirds of the night passes, you see that roosters start crowing everywhere.

Of course shaytan doesn't like it waking up the believers, for this he is enemy. Really when there is a rooster in the cities, especially in the nonMuslim countries, they impose a big fine, the municipals, governments. If someone complains one can end up paying 3-5 thousand fine because it is disturbing people. They have everything there but this they can't stand because it is a friend to Allah & Prophet. Shaytan even finds something for it, makes a reason to prevent it. And shaytan's friend is the peacock. It has an ugly voice, an arrogant thing. But if you look, really it is no good for anything. That one is the friend of shaytan. And shaytan was considered to be the peacock of angels before, show off.

And it is necessary to pay attention to such things. Allah wants to grant something to people and especially the Muslims in everything. He wants to reward people with His Generosity. Insha Allah if you say, when you wake up in the morning, "I do everything to please Allah" all you do knowingly & unknowingly is recorded as reward with Allah's permission. After you perform all these prayers, good deeds for Allah, for pleasing Him, Allah does not sit & bargain with people. Since they do it for Me, all their actions, everything is a virtue for Allah, I record it as a reward, He says.

Today we speak about, many thing Allah make ways to people to take benefit from His Mercy, from His Gift- endless Gift for Muslim people especially. Because they are saying "You are our God, no God but You". For this, He is happy with Muslim people & everything make occasion to give from His Gift, endless Gift. There is many thing we know about them - Like to pray in mosque with jama'at it is 27x better than to pray alone in your house. Jama'at is very, very important. Even if you cannot come to mosque, in your house also you can make jama'at with your family, with children, wife. Also Allah accept as jama'at

And when you are in mosque, the first line it is the most important, most get Mercy of Allah. Many Hadith telling about this subject. Here, dargah. For dargah Mawlana Shaykh, he give order: Who they are in first line must be with turban & with beard. So here, it is ok if you cannot go. Who not have this thing, they mustn't come here. But in normal mosque you must be hurry to be in first line. Normal mosque, people even they have no mustache, some of them. No, they have nothing. So when you pray in normal mosque try to be in first line. Because it is most benefit & most gift for Allah, for this line. After, coming like this. And this is adab from... And Sunnah & adab, Prophet, in first better, first line better.

After, good. The last one, you must put small children. Not putting big. But sometimes, when you put last line children they making naughty & they laughing.. So if it is like this, you can put them between you. If not, normally you must put them like this. And after that, ladies. Because, they cannot be in front of men. This is what we, most of people they know this.

But there is many thing, even you cannot imagine it is good. There is Hadith of Prophet (sas), "I like cock, rooster. And don't swear on him. Don't say bad thing, don't shout for this bird. He is my friend and friend of Allah, friend of good people. And he is enemy for my enemy." Who is Prophet's (sas) enemy? Shaytan.

Shaytan, he not love rooster. Shaytan love peacock. Because he is like him, bad voice and only very proud. Useless, not like rooster. Rooster, why he is Prophet (sas) like? Because he wake up people for praying. Each time of praying, he is shouting. In night time after, for tahajjud- this is after two third of night become tahajjud.

Many people now, they are not noticing for they have alarm & other thing. But you see, you hear he becoming to shout. And because he is like this, many people they become angry for him, especially in nonMuslim country. Before I come, in London or in Germany they have big punishment, big penalty for, if somebody telling government or (municipal) they coming & make penalty for you because this shouting. But nobody can hear it, when that time all sleeping like there. But if he doing like this.. Specially from shaytan this, law from shaytan.

And this is, Allah give for Muslim people many thing they know or they don't know. Allah give them, rewarding them. For this when you make, you praying & worshipping- in the morning when you going from house or when you wake up, say "We are waking up, insha Allah today everything we do for Allah." So Allah not bargaining with you "you do this, you do that." Your intention to, "all this to be for Allah", He will reward you all day like this. You do something with knowing, you do something good or you don't know, Allah rewarding you. Allah is most Generous, Alhamdulillah. "Bushra lana ya ma`ashar al-Islam. Inna lana ruknun ghayr munhadimi." This is poem of Busayri (Qasida Burdah). There is big gift from Allah. And Allah, He is looking after Muslim, if they know or they not knowing. It is good, good tiding for them, Alhamdulillah.

Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, Al Fatiha.
