Shaykh Muhammad Nazim Al-Haqqani An-Naqshibandi, Sohbat of the 24th of January, 2014.

Allah Allah, Allah Allah. Allahu akbar, Allahu akbar, Allahu akbar wa lillahi l-hamd. O our Lord, may this weakness go from us. May we get relieved. May the servants of the Nation of Muhammad get relieved. Marhaba. It is like that, it is like that. There is not any limit to our Lord's grants. There is not any limit to our Lord's gifts. Let us say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. All doors will open. Do not be afraid. Say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim and move on. Don't be afraid.

O Allah, O our Lord we are weak. Qawwi dha'fana fi ridak. (Strengthen us to Your pleasure). For the honor of Ummuhan Sultan, May He give us strength. May He give us the strength to destroy disbelief. I would like to have the strength to defeat satan. I would like to have the strength to give to the people. How beautiful. It is so beautiful to give to Allah's servants. Janab al-Haqq gives to his servants. You shall also give. O our Lord, our Subhan, For the honor of Your glorious Habib may the heaviness go from us. May the world get spacious, may the world get spacious. Let us say "Allah" Let us do Zikr for our Mawla. May He give us strength. He (swt) shall not give us weakness. His grants are uncountable. His gifts are endless. Ask, ask. Our Mawla's kindness is unending.

O our Lord, we have become weak. At your door is no weakness. Whoever knows You, finds You. The ones who love You won't be despised, they won't be deprived. May our children also be given strength for carrying on Your way O Lord! O Allah. Allahu, Allahu. La ilaha illa Hu. Fatiha.

For the honor of the Holy Sahaba (companions) may lights come over us. I'm asking for light. I am weak, we are weak. We are weak in our condition but for the honor of the most famous Prophet our weakness will go and we will expand. Do not struggle for this world. Do for Allah. Do everything for Allah. Do not be afraid. His grants that He gave His Habib have no limits. For the honor of the Holy Sahaba (companions) may light come over us. I am asking for light. I am weak, we are weak. We are weak in our condition but, for the honor of the most famous Prophet, our weakness will go and we will expand.

Do not struggle for this world. Do for Allah. Do everything for Allah. Do not be afraid. His grants that he gave His Habib have no limits. May this boredom go from us. May we get relieved. May all the people get serious minded. May they know their humanity - Who is Man? Who is Man? Allahu Akbar. Allahu Akbar. Who is Man? Allahu Akbar. Who is Man? If Man's magnificence and beauty would be known then Man would not touch another Man even with a feather. He would not hurt, he would honor.

O our Lord, for the honor of the glorious Habib give us strength, stamina for honoring You, for doing servanthood for You, our Lord. May all my young children turn to Your way, O my Lord, to Your way, my Lord. May we not look for another way. May we not become enemies of each other. Peace is from the heavens. Enmity is from the earth. Allah Allah. Peace is from the heavens, enmity is on earth. Look at the sky. Look at the sky to get relieved and happy. Don't look to this world for benefit. Keep this world under your feet. Look after your Lord's, His Habib's way. O the most Beloved (sas), the most Beloved of our Janabul Mawla, for his glory, shall we live. We shall serve for him, O our Lord. Fatiha.

May our ranks get higher. May we get relieved. May this day be more joyful. May we be stronger. I am not asking for the strength of animals. I am asking for the strength of the holy angels. Allahu akbar wa lillahi l-hamd. Fatiha.

Did you pray on me? Pray Mehmed. May Allah be pleased, may Allah be pleased. May wherever your hand touches be healed. Is the money enough for everybody? Yes it is, then give. Give as it is a holy month. Give. Don't be afraid that it will run out. Give, don't be afraid. He (swt) can pile up gold under your feet. May Janab-i Mawla open for your father, for your uncle and for your ancestors. Allah is the most Glorious. Fatiha.

Don't refuse anybody who asks. Give. Let the world be under your feet - For serving the servants of Allah. O Allah, O Allah, O Allah. Live with the love of the Habib (saw). Ummuhan Sultan (Hala Sultan) protects us, supports us. When I was this small she supported me. O Ummuhan Sultan - I could not look inside. I couldn't look, I never looked to her blessed tomb. Fatiha.

Give. I have no energy. I have energy. There it is. It is like that. He gives us strength according to our condition. Those who have ruined Man, they will get ruined, they will get ruined. Aman Ya Rabbi. Don't make us from that group. Be of the ones who are honoring man. Janab Allah honored Adam. He honored the Prophet Adam. Janabul Mawla honored him. How is it that you don't honor him? How can you kill Adam? Don't kill.

O our Lord, You know. The Awliya who will save us from our evil egos will come. They are coming now. Damascus will open. Damascus will open. Damascus will open. We shall also go to sacred Damascus. Fatiha.

Mehmed, Mehmed Effendi, O faqir, ascetic one, May Allah be pleased. May Allah give you also physical strength and also strength of heart. Go with safety. Pray for me.
