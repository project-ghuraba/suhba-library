The taste of this life and its pleasure depends on the level of īmān (faith) that one has. And our levels of īmān depend on our mind, because the mind that is complete makes it possible to restrain the nafs. He who has a weak mind, cannot overrule his nafs. And fasting grants us the power to restrain our nafs. This is why from the nation of Ādam, alayhi ṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-salām, to every ummah since, Allāh Almighty has made it farḍ (required). Fasting reinforces will. For that reason it has been ordered. He who says they cannot stand to fast has zero self control and they can be dragged into every sharr (evil). They have no endurance.

Now we have a few days before the coming of Holy Ramaḍān Sharīf. In shā’a Llāh, from now, for holy Ramaḍān Sharīf at the command of Allāh Almighty, HisNone offer for us to fast and, ‘alayhi ṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-salām, hisNone information about prayers that are sunnah, the nāfilah prayers, tarāwīḥ prayers – we can make intention ahead of time to perform them. Of course a person's intention has more goodness then his actions. If we stay on earth forever, we intend to obey Allāh Almighty forever. We don't know how many days, or how many months or how many years of life we have, yet our intention is to remain servants of Allāh Almighty, even if HeNone makes us live on earth forever. We have intention for everlasting servanthood to Allāh Almighty. That is the niyyah that gives us strength. And the one who makes niyyah (intention) for the sake of Allāh Almighty, the moment they make niyyah for that matter, Allāh Almighty gives them such patience and strength and ability that even if shayṭāns come to them as many as there are people on earth, they will fail to turn them from the niyyah they have made.

Look at the power of niyyah. I intend, for the sake of AllāhNone, to do this, to keep this fast, do this prayer, take this wuḍū’ (ablution), take this ghusl (full ablution), do that. For the sake of Allāh Almighty – when the servant opens their mouth to make niyyah, bind themselves by a niyyah, surely from Allāh Almighty assistance and blessings will come to them. Even if shayṭāns come as many as there are humans on earth and try to take back the step he took for the sake of AllāhNone, they will not find the chance. Niyyah, intention, pay attention to it.

Yes, our makeup is weak, "wa-khuliqa l-insānu ḍa‘īfā" (4:28). Humans may be the weakest creatures of all created creatures. Humans are created weak. That is why Allāh Almighty made it farḍ (obligatory) to make niyyah before every action. For every duty, niyyah is farḍ. To make niyyah from the heart it is farḍ. What we say is sunnah, our tongues making niyyah. Saying: "I intend for the sake of AllāhNone to take wuḍū’, to fast." This is sunnah. However, the niyyah being bound to and remaining in the heart is farḍ. Action without niyyah is not correct nor accepted. That is so, O believers. There is an easier way for everything and because we are weak Allāh Almighty opened for us such a wide door and ordered that to get the power you need, "INone am ordering you to make niyyah for MeNone, to do work on behalf of MeNone. Finish. Since you are living on behalf of MeNone, they say living life, you are maintaining your life, then to help you, since your life is for MeNone, inna ṣalātī wa-nusukī wa-maḥyāya wa-mamātī liLlāhi rabbi l-‘ālamīna lā sharīka lah (6:162-163)."

Allāh Almighty is teaching us from HisNone Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n, from Prophet Ibrāhīm's (as) tongue, that from ṣalāt to ḥajj, all services, in a word every service is ‘ibādah to be done for Allāh Almighty. "For Thee, for YouNone. "wa-ḥayātī wa-mamātī" – my life, my living is for YouNone. I am not living for my nafs." What to say about those who are living for food and drink? I am not living for the pleasure of my nafs. "Wa-maḥyāya wa-mamātī." My living, my death is for YouNone. I am for YouNone Yā Rabbi. That is highest point of perfection of īmān. Who are you living for? "We live to gather the world" – this person is of no use. "Who did you live for, O MyNone servant?" AllāhNone will ask tomorrow at Qiyāmah. "We lived Yā Rabbi, we planted once, planted twice, planted three times, did this, did that," you will answer Allāh Almighty. We are people who are ordered to live for AllāhNone. From Prophet Ibrāhīm's (as) tongue AllāhNone is teaching the servants of Ummati MuḥammadNone. "Servants of Ummati MuḥammadNone should live for MeNone. The moment they live for MeNone – INone made the fire a rose garden for MyNone Ibrāhīm." If the world is on fire, a cloud is coming from there. People get anxious. Even if every cloud disappears, it [the fire] wont touch the man who is for AllāhNone. That believer will blow out the fire of the seven hells. What is their fire? While a believer is passing above hell, hell calls out to the believer: "Pass by quickly O believer. The light of your īmān is putting out my fire." Therefore if a single mu’min, single believer, stays in hell – that is why they can't because then hell will be extinguished. How can it touch the believer? Their atomic or poisonous clouds are coming, not coming.

He, whose heart feels fright from these things, reveals a weakness in his īmān, a sickness. These people are sick people. Is your duty with them or with AllāhNone? "Since MyNone servant is living for MeNone, now INone will give him what is necessary both in ẓāhir, the seen, and bāṭin, in the unseen." Live for AllāhNone, because it is worth it. Living for AllāhNone is worth it. Living for anything else is not worth it. Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq.

May AllāhNone make us from the ones who live for HimNone, give their selves for HisNone way. Biḥurmati l-Ḥabīb wa-biḥurmati l-Fātiḥah.
