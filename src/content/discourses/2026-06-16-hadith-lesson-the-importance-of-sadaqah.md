As-Salāmu ‘Alaykum wa RaḥmatuLlāhi wa Barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu BiLlāhi Minash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. BismiLlāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm.

Wa ṣ-Salātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Akhirīn.

Madad yā RasūlAllāh, Madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi RasūliLlāh, Madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

Dastūr Mawlana Sheikh Abdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistāni, Sheikh Muḥammad

Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad.

 ِ ٍ Our Holy Prophet is saying, “I swear by Allah for three (qualities).” Our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam swore by these three things as truth. “Firstly, no wealth is diminished by Sadaqah (charity).” That is, whoever says that their wealth or money has decreased because of sadaqah is lying. Because our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam is truthful and says nothing but the truth. He says, “Give sadaqah.” “If a servant forgives an injustice done to him, Allāh will surely augment his honor.” They have wronged him, he forgives, he leaves it to Allāh, and Allāh increases his honor. “Forgive so that Allāh may honor you.” That is, if you forgive, you will honor others. If you do forgive, even if it does not benefit you, the person’s wrongdoing remains with him. “If someone opens the door of begging for himself, Allāh will surely open the door of poverty for him.” So if someone begs for money or does other things like that, it will also cause poverty.

ة ﻛ ﷲا هداز ﻻإ ﱃﺎ ﷲ Our Holy Prophet is saying “I know these three things are true. The first is, if the person forgives an injustice done to him, Allāh will definitely increase his honor for this.” So, if he is right, if someone else oppresses him and if he does not do anything to them, he will definitely become honored, he will rise above them, his degree will increase. “If a person opens a door to begging with the intention of increasing his wealth, Allāh will definitely increase his poverty.” So, if he does not have money, he begs and asks for money to get more money, or he receives money but cannot give it, this causes poverty, not profit. “If a person opens a door for himself to give sadaqah, seeking the pleasure of Allāh, Allāh will surely increase his wealth.” So, as much as one has to give sadaqah, that wealth does not decrease, it increases with sadaqah. That’s why, sadaqah is very important. A person must definitely give sadaqah every day; sadaqah important for his own needs, for his own benefit, so that his reward increases for the sake of Allāh, and for all kinds of good things.

3- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet is saying “One in three people had ten dinars.” Dinar can be gold or something else, or ten pieces. He donated one dinar. So this man’s entire property was ten dinars. He donated one of them. So he gave one-tenth. “Someone else had ten Ooqiyyahs and gave as charity one Ooqiyyah.” Ooqiyyah is probably two hundred grams. That’s one kilo for five kilos. He also donated one-tenth of his wealth. “One of them had a hundred Ooqiyyahs and he donated ten of them.” They all gave a tenth. Since they all donated one-tenth of their wealth, they are equal in reward. So, one gave too much of his wealth, and the other gave a little. But all of them gave their one-tenth so they received the same reward.

4- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet is saying “The best person is the poor believer who gives as much as he can.” He is poor but he will still give sadaqah, his sadaqah is according to his situation and that is the best. The charity he gives is the best charity. Because he is poor, and he definitely gives sadaqah.

5- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet says, “The best door of goodness is charity.” There are many good deeds, there are many good things to do. But charity is the best, says our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

6- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “The best of you is the one with the longest hand, i.e. who gives sadaqah.” Our Prophet said, “Khayrukunna,” which means he is addressing the ladies. It’s addressed to women, but it also applies to everyone. The one with the longest hand means the one who gives more charity.

7- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

لﺎﻣ  رو ،ﺎﳘﺄﺑ قﺪ نﺎﳘرد  ن.رد ﻒﻟأ ﺔﺋﺎﻣ رد ﻖ

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “A Dirham surpassed a hundred thousand Dirhams.” The one dirham sadaqah received more rewards than the hundred thousand sadaqah, says our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. “A man had two Dirhams and gave one in charity,” that is, he gave half of his property to charity. “And another man went part of his wealth and took out a hundred thousand Dirhams and gave them in charity.” He gave a hundred thousand but he has more than a hundred thousand millions more. That’s why, this one dirham gives more reward to a person than a hundred thousand dirhams.

8- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Sadaqa given in secret extinguishes the wrath of the Lord.” So it is better to give it secretly. One should give it without offending people, and without it being seen as charity.

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Sadaqah given by a Muslim prolongs life and averts an evil death.” Charity prolongs life. It also protects from evil death, may Allāh protect us. There are very strange things in bad deaths. And what is even more important is, “Allāh removes the feeling of boasting and arrogance from a person with sadaqah.” So, one does not act arrogant or boastful. They are bad traits, traits that Allāh dislikes. Allāh will not make him do this or that.

10- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Sadaqah closes seventy doors from evil.” If there is any kind of evil or bad things, those doors will be closed and the believer will be protected.

11- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Sadaqa guards against an evil type of death.” May Allāh protect us from bad death.

12- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

ُ َ ُ ُ ْ ً َ ُ ُ َ

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Charity closes the doors of seventy troubles, the smallest of which is leprosy and leucoderma.” So the benefit of charity is very great. People don’t care that much, they spend money on unnecessary things. If they are going to give a penny for a good thing, they think, “Why did I pay so much!” May Allāh protect us. May Allāh make us among those who give sadaqah insha’Allah.

The Messenger of Allāh says the truth in what he said or as he said.
