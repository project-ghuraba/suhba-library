As-Salāmu ‘Alaykum wa RaḥmatuLlāhi wa Barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu BiLlāhi Minash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. BismiLlāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm.

Wa ṣ-Salātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Akhirīn.

Madad yā RasūlAllāh, Madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi RasūliLlāh, Madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

Dastūr Mawlana Shaykh Abdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistāni, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-

Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-Suḥbah wa l-Khayru fi l-Jam‘iyyah.

BismiLlahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim:

(Qur’ān 22:40). Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla says, ‘Wa layanṣurannā Llāhu man yanṣuruh’, ‘And Allah will surely help those who help Him.’ Whoever is with Allah, Allah certainly helps him. He always makes him victorious. It is the promise of Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla.

Therefore, who is with Allah should not be afraid of the state of this world, the state of this time and Fitnah. As long as he is with Allah, Allah will certainly protect him. And the victory will be his with the permission of Allah. Even if the whole world goes against him, no one can overcome the one who is with Allah.

It is the promise, the word of Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla. But people should understand and believe in it. If they understand and believe in it, they will be at peace and at rest. Otherwise, there will be no rest. Even if the whole world is with them, they will still be afraid and worried. No one has lost by being with Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla. ‘ُنَ يقِ َّتُم لْلُِ ُةَبقِ اَعلْاوَ ’, ‘Wa l-Ā’qibatu Lil Muttaqīn’, ‘And the [best] end is for the righteous.’ (Qur’ān 28:83). Certainly, the end is for the righteous ones who fear Allah. Their end is always victorious and good. This is what is important. If you are always good but your end is bad, it has no benefit.

Therefore, Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla gave these good tidings to believers and Muslims to be with Allah. Always be with Allah. Our intention is to be with Allah and to carry out the beautiful orders of Allah to us. The orders are beautiful. If they are difficult, it is the cheating of shaytan, the deceit of shaytan to people. The orders of Allah are never difficult. They are difficult for the ego and difficult for shaytan.

Therefore, may Allah protect us. May we be with Allah so that we find peace and so that there’s beauty insha’Allah. May Allah help us. May we be with the Haqq. And may the love of Dunya be away from us.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
