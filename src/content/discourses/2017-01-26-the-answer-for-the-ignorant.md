As-salāmu ‘alaykum wa raḥmatu Llāhi wa barakātuh. A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Wa-idhā khāṭabahumu l-jāhilūna qālū salāman" (25:63) Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla says in Qur’ān; "When the ignorant address them, good people answer 'salām'". Good people don't answer ignorant people, they say 'salām'. There is no need to argue with an ignorant person. Because in his head he has a fixed idea, he does not want to understand. He shouts and says nothing other than that idea. He accepts that. Nothing else.

No matter how much you try to tell this is right or this is wrong. You can't get anywhere by shouting and arguing. He is ignorant. You look at him. If you are smart, you will understand him after he speaks a few words. So you try to tell him if he has the ability to understand you can tell him more. Otherwise if he is coming to you just to argue and teach you what he knows don't bother. Keep silent. Don't bother says Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla.

Now there are more of these kinds of people in the world. There are many people, who don't accept other than what they know. Even among Muslims. Thanks to AllāhNone, we are living in a Muslim country. We have followers all over the world. They may attack stronger in non Muslim countries. They may be dangerous. So, no need to risk yourself because of these ignorant people. The best answer to an ignorant is to keep silent.

Let him shout. Let him shout as if he is barking. When a dog barks will you also bark at him? You can't bark. He'll bark. Then he shuts up. No need to argue with him or try to tell him. Because your voice won't be as loud as a dog. The more you shout his voice will always be stronger. He shouts more, he silences you. So, don't put any effort in.

Don't be with the ignorant people. Don't get close to ignorant people. Don't waste your time for people, who won't understand You will be sorry. That is why thanks to Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla HeNone didn't order us to tell them. HeNone tells us to pass them by. Pass them by, saying "salām". Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla tells us not to say anything. Thanks to AllāhNone if HeNone told us to shout and argue we would be finished. It is easier to keep silent and pass them by. Keep this in mind that these are ignorant people. AllāhNone describes them. No need to listen or talk to them.

Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla says A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Wa-idhā khāṭabahumu l-jāhilūna qālū salāman" (25:63) AllāhNone, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, HeNone said for us to not be with ignorant people. Don't try to teach them anything. Because there is a kind of people that are ignorant, really ignorant. Which people are these? They have ideas. And this idea, they thought it is the real one, no any idea better than this idea. These people, these kind of people...

And it is they are not few people. The majority of people are like this. Don't argue with them. This is an order from AllāhNone, from ProphetNone. He said "jawābu l-jāhili s-sukūt". The answer for the ignorant is to be quiet. Don't argue. Don't try to say to them the right thing. Because you must - You have also a brain. You have something, you know people.

If you see these people who are not accepting anything, don't try to teach them or to tell them the truth. Only keep quiet and be away from these people. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are in a Muslim country. Our country it is also, the majority also people they have ideas. They are not accept anything. But now also in non Muslim countries. Who are living... Many of our murīds, brothers, sisters; they are living in non Muslim countries, this is more dangerous for them to argue with these people, non-believer people. If they are swearing on you or swear on our faith, our ProphetNone, don't argue with them. Because this is an order from AllāhNone.

Only keep quiet and go peacefully near them. Because when they are arguing, they are like this animal, dog. When he is barking, you cannot go and bark like him. If you shout, he will more shout. If you – You cannot be your voice louder than a dogs voice. So no need to be like a dog, like him. Only keep quiet and just what you... looking for at this animal, you can look for these people. Only this is what you can do. And leave this for AllāhNone. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, they not give us order to argue with them. He said "Don't argue." Don't speak with these people. Just keep away from people like this.

It is now shayṭān and his followers attacking every believer, every people. So no need to make for yourself to be in danger or to be sad for this. No, don't be sad. If animal barking for you, you will be sad? No. He is animal only. You must be - It is normal. So for these people also who attacking people because they have a religion, only what you can do just keep quiet and be happy. Because you are in right way.

These people attacking you because they are following shayṭān. Don't be sad. You will be rewarded for this even, for what you are suffering and what you are not saying only for sake of AllāhNone. All this coming for you. So you must be happy, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone keep us safe from this shayṭān and his followers, in shā’a Llāh. Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
