As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

People in old times, before our Holy ProphetNone came, were searching for the truth, of course. A long time passed between Sayyidunā ‘Īsá ‘alayhi s-salām and our Holy ProphetNone. There were no prophets in that time. However, there were wise people. AllāhNone inspired them to learn this way, to get to know AllāhNone, to know Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. They were the light for other people. Even without anything, wise people know what to do. They know about the existence of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. They knew that idols and statues didn't work. Therefore, they used to speak wise words and do wise things. And they were the means of guidance for people. They used to give advice and tell others to follow the ProphetNone when heNone comes. They were looking for the way of truth when there was nothing they were telling about.

That was the first time of ignorance, Jāhiliyyatu l-Ūlá. That's when our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam was born. The whole world was in ignorance and darkness. It was the time absolutely opposite to Islām, humanity, goodness and everything. That was the time our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam was sent. Many people reached guidance then. And those who didn't reach it corrected themselves a little, because they saw that humanity is not like that. So they improved.

The second time of ignorance is now. This time is worse. People know everything. Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam came. The whole world has heard about himNone. And people know that heNone is the last ProphetNone. Yet they are ignorant. And it is worse. It is worse than the first ignorance. There was nothing back then. People didn't know what humanity, religion, or faith are. As they didn't know anything, it was the first ignorance. It was lighter. The present ignorance is harder. Ignorance means not to know anything. This is what ignorance is. You can swallow all books in the world but when you don't know Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla you are ignorant, you are nothing. Ignorant is a man who was given mind but who doesn't use it.

We are living in such a time now. Many people have become slaves and servants of their egos, desires, and wishes. They are doing whatever their egos want. They don't want the truth. They say they are happy with it as it is and continue to live. Their only aim is dunyā. They don't want ākhirah. They don't think about and don't accept it. They say they are fine. And they want to encourage others to join their way. They tell others to do all the evil possible. They say if a person wants something, he can do everything. A man didn't create himself to be able to do everything he wants. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla created him. HeNone taught him about things that he must do. And HeNone forbade things that are not appropriate for his nature and character.

It is the second ignorance now. Not only individuals, but even governments and universities do everything they can in order to control people so that they become completely ignorant. If you go against them, you get fined in some places. In some places, they are not happy with you. But we are right in the middle of this era of ignorance. Afterwards, when it reaches its completion, it must finish, it will be over. Everything has an end. The first time of ignorance is over. This second time of ignorance will be over too by permission of AllāhNone. Therefore, people, Muslims should not lose hope. This whole existence is something given by AllāhNone. The whole world, universe, everything around, are things created by AllāhNone. Others cannot use them as they like. They cannot do as they want. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gives some time, and then it will be gone. As we said, wise people of old times and our Holy ProphetNone say the same thing, "Kullu ātin qarīb." Everything that's coming is near. What's not coming is far, says our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Old wise people had said the same thing. And our Holy ProphetNone heard it. "Kullu ātin qarīb."

One wise man made a long khuṭbah on what was coming. There was wisdom in it. It is just a part of that. The future mentioned in the ḥadīth of our Holy ProphetNone and by that wise man is near. Even if in 100 years, what's coming is still near. What's not coming is far. What's not coming will not happen. Therefore, everything has an end which is coming for these things. It is near, in shā’a Llāh. It will change to the better, in shā’a Llāh.

Old time people, they was knowledgeable people before Islām. They was looking for knowledge, for wisdom, for good thing. They was waiting something to happen because they wasn't happy with situation. Which situation it was, after Sayyidunā ‘Īsá ‘alayhi s-salām, long time no prophet coming. So, this time it was the ignorance time. After Sayyidunā ‘Īsá until Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, they was jāhiliyyah – ignorance, ignorance time. It was maybe six hundred, seven hundred years. People they not knowing anything and the world there was darkness of ignorance, no religion. Or if there is religion, they was few people they know reality of Christian, prophet ‘Īsá ‘alayhi s-salām what he brought. Other they change. Other place, they don't know anything. Other place, also there is no any right for human, for woman, for captured people – nothing it was. Only like darkness of no right, nothing it was.

This period it was real... and Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam call it ignorance time, first ignorance time – "Jāhiliyyah Ūlá." Meaning, it will be second one. This first one... but also there was many knowledgeable people, they was looking for knowledge and they know the CreatorNone it must be not this idol or statue or thing. There must be something else. They was searching and looking and telling people, it must be something else. So, after this when Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam coming, many of them easily they become Muslim. And they was become this period finish darkness, coming light and coming knowledge for whole world. Even the who are not under Muslim control, they was... because they are in touch with them, they be make a treat people better than before. Before they was treat them worse than animal. When they see this and they must make it little bit better to for people to not be against them. So, it was for whole world this time light for... and knowledge time. This is first one.

Now we are living real second one – second ignorance time. It is even worse than first one. Because first one, even they don't have knowledge but they have maybe some custom. People, everybody know himself – he is man or he is woman, like this. But this time, this is the worst because you, when you are not knowing your CreatorNone, this is real ignorance. Even you are know hundred university knowledge and you not accept, you are ignorance – ignorant one. Because ‘ilmu l-yaqīn, ḥaqqu l-yaqīn – meaning, ya‘nī, to believe this is real knowledge. To believe for your CreatorNone, this is the real one. When you don't know this, you what will happen? This time what will happen here, in this time since it's become everyday worse and worse; slowly, slowly like darkness coming slowly, slowly. We are now in middle of darkness.

The most dark time we are living. Because they are this people, they are only they have, they said – human right, personal right. But this is... But really it's ego right. "You can do everything. No don't ashame, you are..." We ashamed to say what they do. They are not ashamed. They said, "This is human right. This men right, this women right, this right." It is no. This not right, their ego right. Ego they want this, shayṭān they want this. This shayṭān and ego rights. Now we are living in this time. If you say anything against them, many country they punish you, many country not happy with you. Leave this. Not only their country, they are forcing now Muslim country to accept this also by law. Even not only Muslim, all this whole world they make pressing people, countries, goverments to accept this, this ego law, ego right, ego – shayṭān rights. And they are saying, this is right of human being. No, this is not right of human being.

Human being, AllāhNone create them and AllāhNone give them everybody character, different character and this character different between everybody. Not by what you impose in movies, telephone, internet. Everywhere they make people to accept this. Slowly, slowly, slowly. Like poison giving small, small poison. At the end they will be finish. But this is their plan and AllāhNone now give chance for them to do this. But real OwnerNone of this universe – not only you, your... our world – whole universe HeNone create and HeNone know and, when time coming, this ignorance time also will finish. Everything, they have end. And Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying ḥadīth also, "Kullu ātin qarīb, wa-kullu mā lam ya‘tī ba‘īd." Ya‘nī, meaning, everything coming... coming, it will be soon. But who, what will not be, it is far.

So, this also in this world, AllāhNone make for end for everything. And it will be end for this also. And, in shā’a Llāh, AllāhNone keep us safe until that time. Keep our countries' youngs and old peoples to be safe because everybody have ego, can be danger for him. This people encouraging, encouraging for people to do bad things and not to do good things. If you do good thing, they prevent you. For bad thing, you can, you are free to do. You are welcome to do, they said. AllāhNone keep them, keep away from us, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
