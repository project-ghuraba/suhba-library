Mawlana Shaykh Mohamad Nazim al Haqqani an Nakshibendi

11 Jumādā ath-Thāniya 1432 A.H. Friday, May 13, 2011 Sohbet in English: http://saltanat.org/

Audhu billahi minash shaytanir rajim, Bismillahir Rahmanir Rahim

Don't be a servant for shaytan. Meded ya Rijal'Allah

We are asking from Allah Almighty to make ourselves to understand. Oh our attenders from east to west and north to south. [Arabic] You must try to be Allah Almighty's servants. If you are mankind, you must try to learn what is your mission - if you are from mankind … If you are from the animals' world, if you are donkeys, you must try to learn the mission of donkeys. A donkey knows its mission. You - mankind -you are not knowing your mission. Donkeys know. Dogs know. Foxen know. Wolves know. You mankind, you are not knowing your mission. [Spit on] whole ones who they are not knowing their missions. Animals knowing, mankind now never knowing.

Biggest shame to mankind. They are thinking that their mission is to kill each other. [Spit] on that missionary. Man now 21st century people, they are saying or believing that their mission now is to kill each other, to do their worst for each other. It is no honor to kill each other! Animals - they are not doing what mankind is doing now in our days - the 21st century. No honor for them.

But [Arabic]...

But you must know that Divinely might can take them away in two seconds. Look what is happening now. I am sorry to say that in the Muslim world now, the heads who are saying, "We are ruling" are [also] killing. Rulers of Islamic world - Most of them are fighting their citizens. And citizens are weak people with nothing through their hands. They are coming and asking something when the Lord of Heavens is giving a chance [for them to speak to] those tyrants. "Give us a little bit." They [tyrants] are saying, "No!".

"Give to us some honor." They [tyrants] are saying, "No. Honor is only for us. You must be under our feet. You must be our servants. As Pharaoh was saying, we are saying to you " Oh Egyptians, I am the most high lord. You must be under my feet. If I am giving to you, you may eat. If I am giving to you, you may drink. If I am giving you chance to live, or I am killing you, I am the first [top]. All power is in my hands."

Now I am sorry to say that in the Islamic world, so many tyrants were raised like Pharaoh [as if] they are not Muslims. They are refusing any word except [from] themselves. But now in our days, Libyan ones, Egyptian leader, Iraqian leader,... leader, Yemen leader - they are saying, "No, I should be here up to death."

Allah should send to take their souls to fire!

[Arabic, salawat]

Say to those people who are killing, murdering, and killing people. It is a warning, but... "Give them tidings that who is killing, they should be killed." Oh whole tyrants, who are killing their citizens who haven't anything to fight back with... They are like sheep or goats and you are bringing heavy weapons and then killing everyone: small ones, women, old ones, and without [them] doing anything - youngsters. And you are saying "You must be here and your family must be here." No!

Bad tidings, not good tidings for you. Once upon a time there was a tyrant living in Baghdad. He was saying, "I am the only one here [at the top]". He killed hundreds and thousands of people. And he was saying, "I am here [the top]." [Arabic] Take your lesson from what happened to that one, who was saying, "I am here. I am a king. I may do everything." What happened? The last part of his life- how did it end? Allah Almighty making him to be caught and and to be hanged on a tree.

Tyrants who are fighting against their people, take your lesson. If you are not taking that lesson, you will be regretting. Because Holy Prophet (sas) not speaking by himself. But his holy words are from heavens. [Arabic]

Should be their ends to be hanged or to be shot down. No more hope for them. No more the Lord of Heavens going to give them a chance to be a president or to be a statesman on their people. [Arabic] The Lord Allah Almighty was looking at what you were doing. He gave to you as a grant to be the head of your nation, and how you are going to deal with your people. Are you using justice or are you using oppression? But they were following shaytan and shaytan's way.

[They are] following and shaytan is going to be happy to make them to fall in a trap. [Arabic] Oh whole muluk of Arab, oh whole kings through east and west, oh all tyrants from east and west. You must fear one day coming to you. When the Lord of Heavens' justice - absolute justice – [comes] no one can escape. You will be trapped in a trap [when] Allah Almighty is doing his heavenly judgment. You should not...

Tyrants, don't say that when we are killed, we are going to finish. I shall say to all Muluk kings of Arabs and Islam, tyrants of Arabs and Islam one tale that was written in old books about Hajaj the tyrant. Hajaj you know. Oh Arabs, you know Hajaj was a tyrant. He was an example for tyrants. The top point. When he passed away, he was seen in a dream by holy people - awliya. He was seen through a dream by a holy one who was asking "Oh Hajaj, how was your Lord's dealing with you?" and Hajaj was saying, "Oh, good servant, just how many heads I cut, how many heads I cut, for example 1000 heads I cut? Now 100,000 times they are cutting my head. For every mazlum - innocent one that I killed, my head is cut once. Only for [Arabic]... for him they cut my head 70 times.

Oh Arab kings, you have a chance for one year more, ten years. But you are going to die. And what we are saying now, it is Divinely Justice to give to you as a punishment up to the day of Resurrection.

How many people, oh Bashar, are you ordering to be killed? You are not going to run away. One day you will be caught. And Divinely Justice is going to order to angels for you to be cut and to be shot, for you once, once again, once again, once again... Therefore, oh tyrants, oh Saleh, oh Husni, oh Gaddafi, oh [everyone] who are doing their worst for the servants of the Lord, they are approaching you and you cannot escape. This is a heavenly warning from heavens to all of you.

I am a weak servant. I am not able to speak. But they are making me to make advising to you. Quickly stop killing people! If you do not stop, it means that angels will never stop killing you up to the day of resurrection.

[Extensive Arabic]

Taube ya Rabbi, Therefore, the seal of Prophets was saying... [extensive Arabic]

They are not keeping their force, their mightiness from weak defenseless people. Leave them [alone]. If not you will be taken [in the] worst [way].

[Arabic dua and an aside]

Oh, people through east and west, from north to south, particularly those people who are governing and they are commanders of millions of people. Saleh of Yemen - That one is crazy. If they take him to a doctor, the doctor will say that this person is 100% a no-mind person. “Take him to a mental house. No mind at all.”

This is heavenly advice for you. If you are happy to be in the Divinely Presence, to come innocent, and your face is shining, you must follow what heavenly advice is coming to you. Accept it. If not - you know. If heavenly punishment is not coming today, tomorrow coming. If not coming in this month, second or next month. If not coming this year - next year. You will be taken. You cannot escape. Until the last one – his soul - who should be taken will be Iblis - shaytan. When whole mankind is taken, [when they all]. No one is going to be alive - just shaytan. And Heavenly order coming, "Oh Azrail, go and take shaytan's soul". [Azrail] coming to him, for example, from east - shaytan running to west. Finding there Azrail – [shaytan] running to north. Finding there Azrail, [shaytan] running to south. Finding there Azrail - final.

Oh people, you cannot escape. You will find Azrail (as) one day in front of you. And he is going to take your soul. No escaping. No way out. No way. [Arabic. Ayatul Kerim] Finish.

And to Arab [Arabic]: Without a reason you are making trouble to reach from east to west, from north to south. And it is shame to you and blame to you.

The non-Muslim world - they are laughing, And they are saying, We are happy. If their religion was a real religion, those people cannot do [these things]. That means they are doing their worst. Look, oh people, at what Muslims are doing to each other.”

And that is big blame to them [tyrants] for Islam. Astaghfirullah. Taube ya Rabbi

[Arabic dua] On behalf of people cheated by shaytan, Oh our Lord. Let him not to do more than this injustice that the biggest sin is to kill each other without a reason. May Allah forgive us.

Taube ya Rabbi, astaghfirullah Taube ya Rabbi, astaghfirullah [Dua in Arabic] Taube ya Rabbi, astaghfirullah Astaghfirullah, astaghfirullah, astaghfirullah [Dua in Arabic] Taube ya Rabbi Fatiha
