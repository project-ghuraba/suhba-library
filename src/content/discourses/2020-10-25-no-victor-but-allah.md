Aū‘dhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-Shay"āni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Ra!māni r-Ra!īm. Wa#-#alātu wa s-salāmu ‘alā Rasūlinā Mu!ammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti A#!ābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-F’āiz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Mu!ammad Nā$im al-Bismi Llāhi r-Ra!māni r-Ra!īm: “Inna lladhīna yu!āddūna Llāha wa-rasūlahu ūlā’ika fī l-ādhallīna kataba Llāhu la’aghlibanna anā wa-rusulī inna Llāha qawiyyun ‘azīz” (Qur’ān 58:20-21). Allah ‘azza wa jalla says in the Holy Quran, “As for those who defy Allah and His Messenger, they will definitely be among the most debased. Allah has decreed, “I and My messengers will certainly prevail.” Surely Allah is All-Powerful, Almighty.” These people have no value whatsoever. Allah ‘azza wa jalla says, “I will be victorious. My messengers will be victorious.”

Ever since the beginning of the world, there has been a war between, a battle between, truth and falsehood, between good and evil. Now it is continuing the same way. Sometimes it is through heavy weapons, sometimes through swords, and sometimes when there is peace and what not. They say so, but there is no peace. There is always a continuous battle between truth and falsehood. There is a war going on. So let us be with Allah, let us be with the Holy Prophet (SAW), so we may be the victors. If you are with Shaytan, you will not be victorious. You cannot be.

Now they say they want to upset people, and they make people mad by doing a few speeches or caricatures or something against the Holy Prophet (SAW), against Islam. They think they are going to win against Islam this way. They are trash anyway. As regards darkness, there are none worse than those who do this. They are worse than sewer rats. There is no need to deal with them. Allah will give them what they deserve, and [He will] give them their troubles and punishments. We should say,“ %asbunā Llāhi wa ni‘ma l-wakīl.” They don't know what they're doing. They have no idea what they're doing. They think they do this to boil people up all over the world. Once in awhile, they come out like a sewer rat comes out and throws off its filth. That is what they are doing.

So Allah will be victorious over them. Allah’s word is reality, the truth, and the others are not. The greatest, most beautiful book is the Holy Quran, glorious. It is the book of Allah. The rest are just lowly base ones. Nothing else. So there is no need to be upset. Indeed, there is no need to be sad. We are with Allah, and the things that they do against the Holy Prophet (SAW) don't affect the Holy Prophet. Allah is the victorious one. and they are the losers, all the time. Whatever they do, they fight, they envy, and they hold grudges, [and still] they cannot get rid of it. They think day and night, and then they try and struggle day and night to finish it off. But they cannot finish it, insha’Allah. They [themselves] will finish and go away. In the Hereafter, they will go to Hellfire forever. They will stay there. Then they will realize that what they did was not useful, but rather was harmful for themselves.

So Muslims need to awaken and see the greatness of Islam. Let them see the greatness of Islam. Allah, exalted and glorious be He, is with truth and reality. Falsehood cannot stay, insha’Allah. Ever since the time of Adam, the truth has been continuous and everlasting, and it will [continue to] be everlasting, insha’Allah. May Allah help Islam. May Allah help Muslims. May Allah protect us, and strengthen our faith, insha’Allah. Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-F ti ah
