As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm.Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh,madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh,madad yā Mashāyikhanā,dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāhal-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah,wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Wa-la-qad naṣarakumu Llāhu bibadrin wa-antum adhillah” (3:123). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l- ‘Azim. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla made our Prophet ṣalláLlāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallām victorious in the Battle of Badr. Even though you were few, AllāhNone granted you victory. Victory comes from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Even if a person has nothing, huge armies can be defeated. When AllāhNone wills it, it happens. The victory given to our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam in the Battle of Badr, is shown by AllāhNone as a lesson for the believers. The believers should not say, “We cannot do it.”

Whoever is with AllāhNone wins. Whoever is an enemy to AllāhNone is always the loser. Some ask why some people could not win … It is the will of AllāhNone.Victory and loss are both from AllāhNone.But a believer, whether he wins or loses, as long as he is with AllāhNone,he is always in profit. He will not lose. He walks on the path of AllāhNone. He does everything for the sake of AllāhNone. His reward and merit are with Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. This blessed day is tomorrow.

It falls on the seventeenth. But from today, our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu‘alayhi wa-sallam started preparing. It is a campaign. To set out on that campaign requires a lot of preparation, not simply leaving everything behind. Our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhiwa-sallam told the ṣaḥābah, “Here, there is a battle now. You are few and they are many. According to this, if you have something to say, tell me.” Two ṣaḥābah came: Miqdād ibn Aswad and another blessed ṣaḥābīy. They said, “We do not say like Banī Isrā’il.” They said [to Sayyidunā Mūsá ‘alayhi s-salām], “Go, you and your Lord, and fight. Then, we will come.” That is what they had said. If AllāhNone wills, of course one person can defeat them all, but that is not the way for people. War and jihād are also virtues. Therefore, those ṣaḥābah said, “We do not say like what Banī Isrā’il said, ‘’Go and fight with your Lord, then we will come and sit here. “We are with you until the last breath. Until the last drop of our blood, we are on the path of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.” Our ProphetNone knows. And heNone rejoiced at the words of these blessed ones. HeNone was very pleased. And that is how it should be. You should be with the Truth. You will be with the Truth and thus you will win. The dunyā passes, but the gain remains. The gain is the gain of the ākhirah. The names of those blessed ṣaḥābah are among the greatest people in Islām. Their names are mentioned. Barakah is taken from them. When they are mentioned, there is virtue, there is barakah and there is goodness. May AllāhNone elevate their stations, in shā’a Llāh. May their barakah be upon us.

Aṣḥābu l-Badr, they are renowned. This is the day our Holy ProphetNone started the preparations. The war began like tomorrow, the second day, on the seventeenth. Like tomorrow the polytheists were defeated. Tomorrow, in shā’a Llāh, we will explain this matter more, in shā’a Llāh.

Today, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla in Qur’ān‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n saying, Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Wa-la-qad naṣarakumu Llāhu bibadrinwa-antum adhillah” (3:123). Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla give, help you in Day of Badr. Badr is where there was fighting between, big fighting, Ghazwat Badr al-Kubrá. The big Badr, because there is a small fighting. But this was big, for Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, AllāhNone praise them, and showing believer to be not be hopeless. AllāhNone said, “You was very few people and you don't have anything. Even weapon you don't have, knife maybe you don't have.” Only maybe stone, maybe have wood to fight. No... Other side, they was full armed –with this knife, with sword, with spear, with arrow, with everything. But this side – nothing, only very few. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, when HeNone want something happen, even one was enough to finish this other army. But to give this people honour forever, HeNone was, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla give them this chance for them to be blessed one. It was first fighting, big fighting with mushrik, with non-believer, who they was coming to fight with Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam.

So, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, with, who is with Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, he will be winner, all time. Who fight against Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, he will be loser every time. Even they thought, “We are win,” they are not winning. They are losing, because real win for forever, not for only this life. This life finish. Other life, either in Hell or in Paradise. So, who is winner, he who is in Hell, or in Paradise? Of course, the winner in Paradise. The loser in Hell. This is very simple. They must understand this. Don't think, “We are killing his innocent believer and we will be praised, we be in Paradise.” – No. Because this, for [people to understand], AllāhNone, with all prophets, and especially with our Sayyidunā Muḥammad ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, giving very good example. Because heNone was making people, believer, to encourage them to be more believer. Not only for more, to be fighter, or to be this – to be more believer.

And when you are believer, you will be doing everything for your benefit, for benefit of ummah. This is what happen with this Badr. And because, before also, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, said to ṣaḥābah, “Look, you, we are facing big army. If anybody want to say anything, tell, tell me.” And two ṣaḥābah – one, Miqdād ibn al-Aswad and other, I don't remember what his name – they come to Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhiwa-sallam, saying,“O ProphetNone, beloved one for us, for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, we are not like Banī Isrā’il, who they said to Sayyidunā Mūsá,‘alayhi s-salām, when he tell them, “Go, enter this city, and you will be safe.” But they were afraid. They said, “No. There is … we are afraid from this people. Go you and your Lord fight with them. When you finish, we can come and settle there.” So they said for Prophet ṣalláLlāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, “We are not like this people. We are will fight with you until last man from us last breath, last blood from us. We will not go back. YouNone go in front of us, order us, and you will be, we will obey it.” This is how must be believer, when Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying something, to be real hisNone companion. And heNone was very happy, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, and heNone know this ṣaḥābah, hisNone follower, companion, they will be light for ummah forever.

And this is special gathering, this 313 ṣaḥābah, companion. They was in this battle. and they were, they are, the highest ranking between ummah they have. And when you mention their name,or you put their name in your house, you are be safe, and there is barakah for you and for your family. AllāhNone make them barakah for us, in shā’a Llāh. We are asking from AllāhNone this,in shā’a Llāh.

So today... today Prophet ṣallá Llāhu‘alayhi wa-sallam, they prepare. And today, sixteen of Ramaḍān, tomorrow seventeen. Seventeen of Ramaḍān this battle happened and Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam won on this mushrik. In shā’a Llāh, maybe tomorrow we can make more explain for this, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq,al-Fātiḥah.
