Sheikh Mehmet Adil Al-Haqqani sohbat of the 18th of November, 2014.

Madad ya RasulAllah, madad ya Sadati Ashabi Rasulillah, madad ya Mashayikhina, madad ya Abdullah Faizi d-Daghistani, madad ya Mawlana Sheikh Muhammad Nazim al-Haqqani, Dastur. Tariqatuna s-sohbah, wa l-khayru fi jam'iyyah.

The foundation of our tariqah is sohbah/association. Sohbah is when friends converse with one another, or ask one another how they are doing, or talk about nice things, or give advice about religious matters. All these are considered as sohbah. Blessings come with gatherings, when people gather together. Gather at least once a week, or even once every 2 weeks for dhikr. Don't let 3 weeks pass without gathering. Dhikr, Khatim al-Khwajaqan, must be done at least once every 3 weeks. Who doesn't do this, should be ashamed to say, "I am in tariqah".

Shukr to Allah that our forefathers built these places. This holy one (buried here) came from Bursa. He came from Bursa. He is the sheikh of Hz. Ismail Haqqi. He is Qutub Osman Effendi, a great scholar. Allah sent him to Cyprus. That means they have the title deeds for Cyprus. Without them, there is no way to show that Turks or Muslims live here. They all came for the sake of Allah and they built these places. They made a waqf (a charitable foundation protected by a trust). If someone makes a waqf, they can do whatever they want. They can give a little or they can give a lot. But it is by the wisdom of Allah that all these years have passed by, and even if one gives a small piece of land and then another adds to it, that land becomes bigger and bigger until this day. Mashallah. It has benefit for people; it gives benefit for the one who made the waqf, and it benefits all the other people.

We are now on the island of Cyprus. There was a war about 40 years ago. So many people became shahid/martyrs. About 40% of the island is ours. They (Greeks) say - "If you give us 30% we will make peace." However, the waqf land that belongs to the Muslims is more than half the island. The reality is that we have the title deed to all these places. Half the island belongs to Muslims. But forget about the Greeks, even our own people were heedless and they stole and took advantage of waqf property.

Waqf is such a great thing but it is also very dangerous. Who takes advantage of waqf property will never succeed. Those people don't believe in akhira. It doesn't matter, they will be punished in akhira. But also in dunya/this world, they will regret it. If it is waqf property, stay away from it. Don't take even a small amount from it (unlawfully). Even if there is doubt, stay away, don't take. We had an uncle who always told us a story: Prophet Suleyman (as) could speak the language of all animals. There was a sparrow that he liked, but he couldn't find it. He looked around and he found it. He got upset and said - "Where have you been?" And the sparrow said roughly - "What are you saying?" It said to Sulayman (as) - "I can destroy your kingdom." He said - "Who are you to destroy my kingdom? You are such a small thing. Are you crazy, what are you saying?" It said - "I will take some soil from waqf land and throw it on your palace and all your kingdom will be destroyed."

That is how important and valuable waqf property is. We must manage it very carefully. Waqf property is not booty. But waqf property can be rented - You can rent it and take the rent money. The person who works at the Waqf foundation has a right also. They can take their salary. He can take his right, there is no problem in that. It is not wrong or bringing guilt. There is not objection to that. It also says in Qur'an "wal 'amilina 'alayha" (9:60) Those working for waqf can take their salary. They can take their wages. It is mubah/permissible for them.

However, these holy people made a waqf, as in donating a piece of land for the purpose of providing financial support for the mosque, for its imam, for its mu'adhin, and for its restoration. But if you sell that waqf land or use its money for some other purpose, especially for a bad purpose, then you will never succeed.

The waqf foundation is very important and a wonderful institution in Islam. They can build houses. Just because it is waqf property, that doesn't mean you can't do anything with it. You can build something on waqf land. Whatever the rent is, they can pay it regularly. It will be like taxes. It might not be very expensive, but that place will belong to the waqf foundation. Allah be praised, because of waqf we have mosques, farms, turkish baths, rest houses (inns). Without waqf, these beautiful structures would not have lasted to this day. Whatever they made hundreds of years ago, it is still continuing to this day. People are worshiping here. They built these places so it would be sadaqa jariya (continuing charity) until judgment day.

Our Prophet said that when a person dies, 3 things will continue to add to his rewards; his book of good deeds won't be closed. First is to leave behind a righteous child. Second is to teach knowledge; to be useful to people, as are sheikhs. If someone teaches knowledge or writes a book about Islam, and guides people, then until Judgment Day, they will receive rewards whenever people read it. Third is sadaqa jariya, like waqf; to leave a piece of land for Allah's sake. Until judgment day the book of his deeds will be filled with good deeds and rewards. And these people have done that. But if other people come and take over the waqf land, and use it for their own advantage, Allah will still reward the original givers according to their intention. They made their good intention and the reward will surely be written for them until Judgment.

But those who stole the waqf property, they will be cursed until Judgment Day. Because most people have cursed those who use their waqf for a purpose other than it was intended. Like Sultan Mehmed Fatih - his waqf document is very famous. "You must do it like this." He bought it with his own money. For example in Istanbul, there is a place called Okmaydan. Nothing should be built there - it is a waqf established for learning archery. A huge piece of land. What a nice waqf. Even the disbelievers are making their cities to have green areas. What would they lose from making Okmaydan a grassy field? Would they lose anything? The great Sultan made that waqf and cursed those who build anything there or use it for another purpose. And you see, it is the worst place in Istanbul now. Allah is showing people but they are stubborn and not accepting truth. Allah says "Don't do this", and they do it. Then they ask, "Why did this calamity come on me? Why are these things happening to me?" Everyone gets his just reward.

We must learn to do good, beneficial things. We must respect holy things. These waqfs/trusts are holy places. Who tries to steal them will be harmed. We must teach and warn our children not to be greedy. No matter how nice it is, not matter how valuable, even if it is free, do not take! And now people are trying to take waqf property by bribe and other bad ways. May Allah correct their ways. In our tariqa, in our way, we must learn adab/best manners. Adab is to teach good things, to show respect; show respect for the things that were left behind for us by our people, by our elders, by our saints, and by our Prophet. Allah did not give this beautiful thing to Arabs. I will speak openly. They have no respect. If they did, then they would respect the Companions that are there. If they could remove our Prophet's tomb, they would destroy that too. They have no respect for anything; no respect for waqf or anything else.

Praise be to Allah that Adab and respect were given to our people. And it was given to us through our Prophet's way. Each Sheikh carried that love and respect for everything. The holy beard and holy jubba of the Prophet are being visited by thousands, hundreds of thousands of people. This respect is a beautiful thing. This is what we teach in this tariqa. May Allah pull our young ones into this way, Inshallah. It will be good for them and it will be beneficial for our country and our people. Praise be to Allah that in our country so many waqf relics have survived to this day. And we hope to protect the ones that are remaining. But we must use the waqf only for the purpose for which it was intended, or for something that will support that purpose. Sometimes they claim that something is unnecessary. But you must support it and in the future there will be a need for it. With Allah's permission, the people gathered here, these children will all come to the right path, and the mosques will not be enough to accommodate them all.

May Allah be pleased with you. May our gatherings last forever. In the past we used to come to Famagusta with Shiekh Effendi. This is the first time we have come for many years. His spiritual support is present with us. He is with us. We believe in that. Inshallah he is pleased with us. Inshallah the holy ones are pleased with us.

Wa min Allahi t-tawfiq al-Fatiha.
