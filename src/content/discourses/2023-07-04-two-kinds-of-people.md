As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Wa-yā-qawmi mā lī ad‘ūkum ilá n-najāti wa-tad‘ūnanī ilá n-nār, tad‘ūnanī li-akfura bi-Llāh" (40:41-42). Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is saying this. When someone, who believed Mūsá ‘alayhi s-salām from the people of Pharaoh, came to Mūsá ‘alayhi s-salām and invited people to īmān and Islām, they didn't accept it. When someone close from that community saw the truth and started believing, they oppressed him. They said, "What are you doing? Why are you doing such a thing? You are out of the religion. Don't do this." But he said to them, "This is the true religion. Follow it. It is the religion of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. It is the religion of the prophet. Follow it." They still said, "Leave it." So he said then – it is said in Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n – "O my people, I invite you to salvation from hell. I invite you to salvation from a bad ending. I invite you to goodness. But you are doing completely the opposite. Where you invite me is to hell and the fire, evil, ugliness, and all kinds of mischief. How are you even inviting? You think you are doing goodness to people, whereas you are not doing any good. You are calling me in order for me to be destroyed like you, in order for me to be with you," the holy man said. He was saved, of course.

This event is an event that took place thousands years ago. But it goes on the same every day. They look badly at people who believe. People who don't love Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, don't love these people too. But believers continue to invite them so that they are saved. They beg them so that their end is not fire and hell. They struggle in order to save them. But those people struggle to destroy them. This matter... How many thousands, millions and billions of people have passed in the same way. Their destination is the same. Their destination is ākhirah, death, reaching the presence of Ḥaqq. Therefore, people must be careful.

Not everyone who invites can be followed. Don't go out of the right way. The right way is the way of the ProphetNone, Awliyā’, Ahlu l-Bayt, Ṣaḥābah and the way of Ṭarīqah. The way is Ṭarīqah. Guard it so that you reach salvation. Otherwise, there are thousands, not only thousands but hundreds of thousands of fake opinions and people. They strive to make you similar to them. May AllāhNone protect us from their evil. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying in Qur’ān, "Wa-yā-qawmi mā lī ad‘ūkum ilá n-najāti wa-tad‘ūnanī ilá n-nār, tad‘ūnanī li-akfura bi-Llāh" (40:41-42). This sūrah of Mu’min. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla describing one believer, he was in Pharaoh's palace when Sayyidunā Mūsá ‘alayhi s-salām, he come and invite Pharaoh to be accept to be believer, to accept Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, follow prophets. Prophet Mūsá ‘alayhi s-salām is great prophet. So this one from his follower of Pharaoh and his companion, he accept and he find it is, this is the truth and he accept Islām and following Mūsá ‘alayhi s-salām. And advising this Pharaoh and his follower – "O Pharaoh, this man is, he is prophet, he is..." He not saying, "prophet". He said, "He is saying truth. Everything he say, truth. We must follow him. We must go and accept him."

But of course, there is another people around Pharoah they're... Once Pharaoh sometimes he like to accept truth and ask for forgiveness. Quickly they are protesting, not accepting. Saying, "Don't... this. You are, you are god. You are, you are our... Don't. How you can accept this?" Especially there was one named Hāmān. This was the worst friend for Pharaoh. Every time he giving bad advice, not good advice.

So this man, he was saying this, and Pharaoh nearly he was accept. But quickly this people, not make Pharaoh to accept. And they was torturing this man. When he is... they saying this. He saying, "O my nation, I am calling you for to be safe, to survive from bad end, from fire, from hell. And you are invite me to hell, invite me to not accept AllāhNone, to unbeliever. Who? Why? How can I follow you? You must follow me, I like good for you. You like bad for me, for yourself even." And this, of course, they not accept. They... only he was survive. Other, they was following same way and going to hell.

And this happened before thousands of years. Thousands of years and until that time and now same thing happening. This million, billion people since that time coming, going. All time, some people they accept good, truth and following. Some people not accepting. They are enemy for believer. Not accept them. They make all time to make them to come back from their belief, belief of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. And other, whole time they want good for this people. They are trying hardly [hard] to bring them to way of AllāhNone, to save them from hell, to save them from fire. But other, they thought they are good, doing good, not accepting this. They are saying, "You must leave this. You must come here." And there was, many time, they was killing them, they was burning them, they were doing... torturing them very badly to come back from their belief. But the way of AllāhNone, it is for whole people, whole, for whole human being. And AllāhNone send people to warning people from bad thing, Show them good thing.

There is two kind of people. Some of them accepting and they survive. Some of them not accepting and they are... difficult for them for to be in for end, difficult end for them. But important for people to not be cheated with people they show them, "This is good. You are in wrong. You are... you have bad imagination. We are right. Don't believe for this. Enjoy your life, and after – nothing." But if you are enjoy – everybody, believer and not believer, AllāhNone give them same enjoyment. So better to be enjoy yourself and to be believer without enjoying self and you be unbeliever. This is also very important thing. Who has [a] little intelligence, he must accept this and follow to be save himself from something, forever it will be horrible for him. AllāhNone save us from bad end, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
