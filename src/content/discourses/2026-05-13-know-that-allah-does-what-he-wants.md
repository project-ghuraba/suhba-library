As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
فَعَّالٌ لِّمَا يُرِيدُ
<br>“Faʿʿālun li-mā yurīd.”<br>
“Effecter of what He intends.”(Qur’ān 85:16). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

“He does what He wants,” Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says in the āyah karīmah. He doesn’t ask anyone, “What should I do?” He does whatever He wants. Therefore, asking “Why did this happen? Why is this happening?” is of no benefit to anyone.

However, if one’s asking for good [intention], then one can ask, “What’s its wisdom?” But asking “Why?” as an objection or so brings no benefit. It brings nothing but harm. Harm, because it becomes a habit, objecting to everything. Not accepting everything. When one doesn’t accept, it destroys and ends their īmān. May Allah protect us.

Allah has made everything beautiful, created everything beautifully. Of course, since dunyā is a place of test, some things are good for the believer. But for the non-believer, it is not good. Even if it’s the best thing, when it’s for the non-believer, it’s not for their own good, it’s not for their benefit. It’s something given by Allah so that they become more wicked, they do more evil, so that,

وَنَمُدُّ لَهُۥ مِنَ ٱلْعَذَابِ مَدًّا
<br>“Wa-namuddu lahu mina l-ʿadhābi maddā.”<br>
“And extend for him from the punishment extensively.” (Qur’ān 19:79). 

So that they are in more suffering. So that they attain more punishment. Sometimes, for the unbeliever, it is not a blessing but an evil. Blessing is for the believer. Everything, what’s good or what seems as bad, is good for the believer. For the unbeliever, even if it’s the best thing, it is of no benefit to him; Allah opens the way for them to become more wicked, to do more evil.

Just as the current state of the world, people might think that everything is in the hands of the wicked. But everything is in the Hand of Allah. Allah opens the way for them to suffer more severely for what they have done. Do more evil, commit more injustice, so that you will receive even more punishment. May Allah protect us.

Therefore, as we said, do not be someone who constantly objects and complains, is always contrary and opposed. What Allah says will happen: it is necessary to know that. A person with īmān must know that.

May Allah grant us all true īmān, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
