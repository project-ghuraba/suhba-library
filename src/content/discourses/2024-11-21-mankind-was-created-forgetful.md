As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

The word Insān comes from the word nisyān, which means forgetfulness. This means mankind is forgetful. It is also one of the attributes of mankind. Forgetfulness – people sometimes forget things. Everything has a wisdom. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is the One who created everything. HeNone knows the wisdom behind everything and its purpose.

Therefore, if human beings did not forget everything, life would become unbearable. Their pain, memories, all kinds of things; there is wisdom in everything. There is wisdom in forgetting also. Because if a person remembers everything, he wouldn’t need anyone. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla removes things that should be forgotten and pain from one’s heart and mind by making him forget them. As days pass, a person’s sad conditions or the difficulties and hardships he faces are forgotten. His life passes by.

That’s why in forgetfulness – but there are some things that are difficult to forget. Even for these, certainly – for example, if you have memorised the Qur’ān, you need to read it constantly in order not to forget it. That is, you need to take steps not to forget the parts you have memorised. And for forgetfulness, if you recite Ṣalawāt Sharīfah upon our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, the things you have forgotten will come to your mind with the permission of AllāhNone.

Regarding lessons and this and that, sometimes people get sad and say, “I am forgetting.” Forgetfulness is something AllāhNone gave to mankind. Because it comes from AllāhNone, you must accept it. But in order not to forget when important things happen, you should repeat them in your mind or write them down somewhere: what you will do, “I will go there, I will go here.”

The most important thing is not to forget the principles of Islām. You should not forget prayer. You should not forget fasting. As for Ḥajj, you should put it in your mind, “I will perform ḥajj also, in shā’a Llāh.” You must not forget to give zakāh. These are important things. In order not to forget them, you should take note and pay attention. May AllāhNone help us. May AllāhNone protect us. In order not to forget what we know, especially what we have memorised of Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n and the beautiful words of our Holy ProphetNone, recite ṣalawāt, and in shā’a Llāh, we leave this world in our right minds. Now there is also a disease of forgetfulness. It is much worse and more severe. They do not recognise anyone. These new diseases have emerged. May AllāhNone protect us all from them, in shā’a Llāh.

Name of human being in Arabic, insān. Insān, meaning coming from nisyān. Nisyān – to forget. So, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone make, create us, and one of our attribute – to forget. Many people complaining, “We are forget, we are... Why we are forget? Why we like this?” But this also some kind of favour for human being. If anybody not forget anything from his beginning of life and for end of life, many thing, it will be difficult for him. Many thing, he has bad experience or not good experience or something happen for him, for his life, for his anybody with him... So, it will be whole life miserable.

It will be not... Without forgetting, it will be like hell for this people. But with forgetting, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla make people day by day, day by day, going this bad experience or bad memory or anything happen. With this forgetting, it will be days sweeping the whole this sadness or any bad thing in life, covering from another from our... But if whole life not forgetting like first day, it will be – cannot be anybody carry it. Very difficult, it will be difficult. But, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone make this attribute for human being.

So, but there is also something you mustn't forget, you must be careful. If you learn something, you must look for to not forget it. Especially for who remember Qur’ān. Reading Qur’ān by heart or little by from Qur’ān, or more and whole Qur’ān. They must continue. To not forget, it is to repeat. This is very important to solution for not forgetting. You must repeat it.

If you have one sūrah, one sūrah repeat. Five sūrah, five sūrah. Ten also, if you have juz’, you must repeat. Don't say, I will not forget. This was Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, saying, to forget Qur’ān, it is – if you leave camel without tidying, you cannot find him anymore, quickly going away. So remembering Qur’ān, also same thing. You must be tied every time to repeat and to not forget. And also many thing to not forget, Make ṣalawāt, if you forget something: “Allāhumma ṣalli ‘alá Sayyidinā Muḥammad.” So, AllāhNone send this what you forget, you remember, in shā’a Llāh.

Many thing to forget its also to not forget, there is very important thing. Don't forget praying. Don't forget fasting. Don't forget giving zakāh. Don't forget ḥajj. You must not say, “I am later when I old.” No, but you make from beginning, you put every day collect money for yourself, even small amount. So you will be remember and you will be reward because your intention there to make ḥajj. Many thing, good thing to remember, to visit family, to visit brothers, to visit mashāyikh, to visit maqām and visit, of course, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. All this you must remember. When you remember, you, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, reward you. AllāhNone keep us safe. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, in shā’a Llāh, until end of life to be with our remembrance, to be with us, in shā’a Llāh. Because, now also, there is many illness. They make people to forget everything, to not remember anything. AllāhNone keep us also safe from this. To be all time to do our duty for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
