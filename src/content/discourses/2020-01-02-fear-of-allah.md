Waṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-F’āiz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-

The Prophet (SAW) always gives us advice that beautifies our lives. I think that in one of his hadiths to Anas (RA) he says, “Wherever you may be, always remember Allah, and fear Him. If you do so, Allah remains in your heart at all times, you will not do evil.”

A man does something wrong. Let’s say you commit a sin; you wronged [someone]; you went against Allah’s order. Now against this wrong, do something good, and this good will cancel out the wrong, and that means that you are saved against the previous sin. Then get along well with people. After getting along well with people, once you keep your adab and stay with them, no one can harm you. Harm does not touch you.

This advice is beautiful for both this world’s affairs and for Next Life. Whoever follows this advice remains in comfort. This hadith of the Prophet (SAW) is short, but very strong. All the hadith are important, but if one were to do this right, there would not be much else to do. Everything is included in this. May Allah help us to carry out the Prophet’s (SAW) advice. May He make us successful, insha’Allah. Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-F ti ah
