As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says, “Obey Allah and obey the Prophet, so that He may treat you with mercy and compassion.” The second part of the Khutbah is this Ḥadīth Sharīf: which says that hearts become rusty, just as iron rusts. 

When iron rusts, it needs to be cleaned. If the rust is removed, then it can be used. Rust causes it to slowly decay. The heart shouldn’t become rusty. The rust refers to darkness, disbelief, faithlessness, and all kinds of wicked things; it darkens the heart, rendering it unusable. Qur’ān ʿAẓīmu sh-Sha’n cleanses it, polishes it, and increases faith. 

Qur’ān ʿAẓīmu sh-Sha’n was sent to our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam through his holy tongue, and the word of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla reached us through him. Now, some people say, “We don’t need the Qur’ān, we don’t need the Prophet’s ḥadīths.” 

However, Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla said it specifically:<br>
 أَطِيعُوا۟ ٱللَّهَ وَأَطِيعُوا۟ ٱلرَّسُولَ وَأُو۟لِى ٱلْأَمْرِ مِنكُمْ
<br>“Aṭīʿūllāha wa-aṭīʿū r-rasūla”<br>
“Obey Allah and obey the Messenger (Muhammad SAW)” (Qurʼān 04:59). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

Aṭiū’ Llāha wa aṭiū’ r-Rasūla’, ‘Obey Allah and obey the Messenger.”(Qur’ān 04:59). 

Obey Allah, and obey the Prophet. That sign is to understand that: the beautiful words spoken by our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam are the essence of Islām, everything is there. Without the ḥadīths, nothing would be understood. And Allah does what He wills anyway. Allah has commanded this. You must obey it. 

Anyone who says, “I don’t need the Prophet, I can figure it out myself,” has already knocked themselves out from the start. They’ve thrown themselves off the right way, it’s a disaster. Because Qur’ān ʿAẓīmu sh-Sha’n was explained, read, and written through our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. Everything is through our Holy Prophet. How can you deny that? He is the foundation of the religion, the foundation of Allah’s way: our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. A person who lacks respect for him has nothing of īmān. They may only say they are “Muslim” with their tongues; may Allah protect us from that. And they might go without īmān because of that.

Therefore, this month is the month of Mawlid, the month of the birth of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. We send salawāt, takbir, and tahlil upon him, asking Allah to accept our good deeds and acts of worship through him. We make him our intercessor, saying, “There is no good in the prayers we perform, no good in anything we do. We only present it to Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla for the sake of our Prophet.” When we do this, Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla will not reject it. Otherwise, there is no good in what we do, neither in what we recite, nor in anything else with our ego. We present it as sinful. 

Some people think of themselves as something important, saying, “The Prophet’s intercession is not necessary, I do it myself.” But what you do is like a rag; it’s worthless. Because you have mixed good with bad deeds. You have defiled, polluted, and made impure something pure. You do that and then you stand before Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. They will throw it back in your face. If you present your worship in such a disrespectful and impolite way, they will say, “Take this and get out!” If you bring your deeds and show disrespect to our Prophet in the presence of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla, thinking it’s something important, that will not be accepted at all.

Therefore, we present everything we do with the barakah and intercession of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. 

May Allah guide people. The shayṭān is playing with people. Especially now, during this month of Mawlid Sharīf, shayṭān and those who are with him are even more enraged. Who are those who are with shayṭān? Those who do not respect our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. Those who do not know his worth and value are the playthings of shayṭān. 

Therefore, may Allah bring them to their senses, may they be guided, and may they come to the right way, inshā’Allāh. Our intention is what our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam taught: mercy. We will be merciful, and we hope that they will be guided to the right way, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
