As-Salamu Alaikum. Audhu Billahi min al shaytan al rajim, Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Assalatu wa s-salamu ala Rasulina Muhammad Sayyidi l Awwalin wa l-Akhirin. Madad Ya Rasul Allah, madad Ya Mashayikhina Tariqatuna Naqshbandi Aliya Madad Ya Shaykh Abdullah Fayiz al Daghestani, madad ya S.Muhammad Nazim al Haqqani, dastur! Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fil jamia' We may say a few words as for sohbat, insha Allah.

Allah has made ablution as a nur/ heavenly light for people. There is a Tradition of the Prophet(sas) if a person had a river running in front of his house, how clean he would be if he bathed in it 5 times a day... Allah (awj) made five times prayer obligatory & this, with ablution. When a person makes ablution, his face, hands, feet will come as nur on the Day of Judgment. Our Prophet (sas).. Now, certain things were fardh/obligatory on him, but mustahab or sunnah/recommended for us.

When a person greets/gives salam it is wajib (obligatory) to reply to it. Giving salam is sunnah, replying to it is wajib. It is ok if you don't give but not ok if you don't reply to it, it will be sin. When the Prophet (sas) was making water, someone gave him salam. Of course he was not in front of people but when he was in that designated area. He didn't reply to his salam. Then when he got out, made tayammum and said "wa alaikum salam" to that person. He (sas) then said, "it is not ok for us to say even one word without ablution". Because this, it is obligatory for him. Awliyas also, they are always in ablution. And they always recommend it to all mureeds: it would be helpful in order to stay away from sins if you are always in the state of ablution.

Even at night, who makes ablution & prays 2 rakats is considered to be in ablution. Although in sleep ablution breaks, but a person who makes ablution & prays 2 rakats before he goes to bed is considered to be in ablution all night long. This is advised to the people of Tariqa- to be in ablution all the time. Before some people would ask Mawlana, "what should we do?" He said "be in ablution", even to a person who doesn't pray. This way, that person started praying.

This one is a big sunnah- to be in ablution all the time. It is not easy but people may do as much as they can. Even if it is not all the time but most of the time he is in ablution, it is still better than not being in ablution at all.

Today we speak about ablution. This is big favour from Allah. Prophet (sas), he said in one Hadith; "what you think about who has river in front of his door & 5 times he is washing himself. You can see him, any dirt on him?" They said, no. This is for praying 5 times. Praying & taking ablution. Ablution, it is nur/light on the Day of Qiyamat/Judgment Day. All what your face, your hand, your legs; all will be light. And there is some Sunnah - Sunnah meaning what Prophet (sas) did, you make same thing like him. For people sunnah, for Prophet (sas) fardh.

And Prophet (sas) never been without ablution. Even once somebody make salamu alaikum, say greeting. When you say "salamu alaikum", this is sunnah. But to give answer, it is wajib-obligatory. If you don't do this, you will be in sin. But maybe somebody coming, saying "salamu alaikum"; only one man say "wa alaikum salam", enough for whole jamaat. So Prophet (sas) not giving answer. After quickly coming & making tayammum to speak to him. Said, "wa alaikum salam". And he said "I cannot speak any word without ablution". For this, for Prophet (sas) obligatory, for us sunnah.

And all awliya, whole life with wudhu. Everytime, you cannot find one minute without wudhu. And they tell people to be with wudhu. Because when you are in wudhu you cannot do sin. You all time, you remember you are in wudhu, you cannot do. And Allah keep you away from sin. And when you sleep, you make wudhu & make 2 rakats. You sleep, all night you will be like you have ablution. Because when you sleep, normally wudhu finish. But when you do this, Allah accept as you are in wudhu until morning.

For this, insha Allah..Mawlana Shaykh saw once, he tell somebody who asked him some advice. He said to him "you will be in ablution all time". This man, he wasn't praying even. He said, with this word of Mawlana Shaykh, begin to pray 5 times a day. So as some, now maybe you cannot do all time. But you must be most of time with wudhu insha Allah, until some days all time will be with wudhu. This is very big hadiya/grant from Allah. This you cannot - it is grant for Ummatul Muhammad this.

Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, Al Fatiha.
