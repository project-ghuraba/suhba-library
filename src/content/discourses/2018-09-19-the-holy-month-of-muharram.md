As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa raḥmatu Llāhi wa barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīn wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

This month of Muḥarram is the last of the holy months, Dhū l-Qa‘dah, Dhū l-Ḥijjah, Muḥarram. These are sacred months. Normally, these are months when there is no war, it is forbidden. When they attack you, then you can attack them back but otherwise it is forbidden. No war is allowed during these months. These months are respected. Because people go on ḥajj. Allāh 'Azza wa Jalla made an exception for them.

Everything has a rank in front of AllāhNone. Some people have higher ranks, some have lower, some have a middle rank. Everything is like this, same with the months. And it is forbidden to make war in these months. Forbidden. But when they attack you, you are free from sin. The sin is for the one who attacked. It has such respect. Especially the day of ‘Āshūrā’, the 10th day of Muḥarram is blessed. Our ProphetNone used to fast in Muḥarram before there was Ramaḍān. When it was changed to Ramaḍān, when it became farḍ in Ramaḍān, no farḍ remained in Muḥarram.

Who wants, can do as sunnah. But on the 10th Muḥarram, our ProphetNone said, "I will fast." Jews and the nations before used to fast because it was a holy day. It is a day of survival. On this day the repentance of Ādam ‘alayhi s-salām was accepted, he met our mother Ḥawwā’. Nūḥ 'alayhi s-salām was saved from the flood. Idrīs 'alayhi s-salām ascended to heaven. Ibrāhīm 'alayhi s-salām and Ismā‘īl 'alayhi s-salām reached their safety in Muḥarram. ‘Īsá 'alayhi s-salām, Mūsá 'alayhi s-salām, for each this is a special day, so it's respected. Fasting on this day gives much thawāb. But you can't fast one day. Either 9th and 10th or 10th and 11th. You can fast on the 9th, 10th and 11th. Because Jews fast only on ‘Āshūrā’, this is in order not to be like them. It is better to fast a day before or after. Who can fast then shouldn't be lazy. They shouldn't say it's difficult. Because our ProphetNone said, "If I am alive next year, I'll fast on the 9th and 10th." But heNone knew he'd pass away. Therefore, as an instruction for this nation, so that they would do it, he said this ḥadīth sharīf. So the ummah is doing it.

It is a holy day. It is a blessed day near to AllāhNone. On that day who gives presents to his family and children, gives ṣadaqah to the poor, will have much provision. Who does shopping for his household on that day will be in comfort with Allāh'sNone permission. Who makes ghusl on that day will be healthy throughout the year. Who applies kohl won't have eye diseases. This day is a holy day. AllāhNone has given it to us as a favour. AllāhNone has favoured it and we get the benefit of the fast, in shā'a Llāh. Also you do other dhikr and prayers and get their blessings. You do shopping and give ṣadaqah and your charity grows. Your barakah increases. May AllāhNone accept it.

On this day the beloved grandson of our ProphetNone, Sayyidinā Ḥusayn (ra) and seventy of his ṣaḥābah became shaḥīd. As we said earlier, no war is allowed in this month, not only against enemies, but also against unbelievers. But shayṭān is making people do wrong things that they think are right. May AllāhNone protect us from the evil of our ego and from the evil of shayṭān.

Today we are al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are in ninth of Muḥarram, month of last month of Ashhuru l-Ḥurum, meaning month of respected months. Because this month it is three months Dhū l-Qa‘dah, Dhū l-Ḥijjah, Muḥarram, coming all together and there is one, Rajab, also from Ashhuru l-Ḥurum but it is... There is four months between this, but for this three months – Sha‘bān, Ramaḍān, Shawwāl, three months between them, but these three months they are respected and there is, it was between they known to not fight in this month. Nobody fight in this month and it is forbidden for Muslim to fight in this month unless somebody attack you or preparing for attack you. So you can defend for yourself but it is normally no fight in this month, no anything between people. To respect this pilgrimage and to make peace in this month.

And it is very holy month because we are for calendar of Islāmic calendar, beginning of Muḥarram first of our holy year – moon – we are for Islāmic calendar. It is holy and it is beginning of year, of our years. Everything we are doing for our worshipping according to this year – to this month for ḥajj, for zakāh, for fasting. For everything – for ‘Īd praying we are following this month. So they are holy months and we are respect for this and especially for ten of Muḥarram. It is very holy day. Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa sallam, before Ramaḍān fasting, they was fasting in Muḥarram. But when AllāhNone say in Qur'ān you must fast in Shahru Ramaḍān, obligatory it was in Ramaḍān.

But for Muḥaram also there is very respect and sunnah to fast in Muḥarram, especially in tenth of Muḥarram because Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa sallam saying when coming for tenth of Muḥarram there was Jewish in Madīnah they are fasting one day. For this Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa sallam saying, "If I will be in this life for next year, I will fast ninth and tenth of Muḥarram, not one day so for this." He was knowing he will change his world but for us, to say for his nation, to fast this day, to not be lazy, to not be afraid from fasting. Fasting it is the most perfect worshipping and AllāhNone reward from HisNone endless treasure, from HisNone endless mercy. HeNone know how will do what HeNone will give us – ten times, thousand times, ten thousand times. HeNone know. HeNone said, "I'm only knowing and I will give. Nobody responsible for this. Only I will. No angel, no any other people." So it is a very holy day because it was day of surviving, day of getting free, and getting happy from every badness. For Nūḥ ‘alayhi s-salām, [Ādam] AllāhNone accept his regret and make him to find Eve, our mother Eve, and for Idrīs ‘alayhi s-salām to take him heaven, to Nūḥ ‘alayhi s-salām to finish from this flood, and for Ibrāhīm ‘alayhi s-salām, for Mūsá ‘alayhi s-salām, for ‘Īsá ‘alayhi s-salām – all this days it was day of surviving to day of feast. Finish their bad time, coming happy time for them. For this it was day very important for whole nations and it is also for ummah also. Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa sallam saying who make ṣadaqah in this day and give his family also, make them happy, and to buy thing for house, for his family. Also make barakah to wash himself, to take shower, make it healthy for whole year. And to put in eye kohl make his eye whole year to be safe and healthy also. So it is very good day and very, it is like gift from AllāhNone for ummah.

And of course there was something happen in this day for grandson of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa sallam, Sayyidinā ‘Abdu Llāh Ḥusayn (ra). His, he and his companions this day they become shahīd and this, as we said before, day this Muḥarram even it is not allowed to fight enemy, to fight non-believer but this when shayṭān and nafs coming for some people they make everything okay for themself, not looking even for grandson of ProphetNone they can kill him. AllāhNone save us from our bad ego and from shayṭān because shayṭān can play with us, with people who are not following, not making most important thing – to love Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa sallam.

If when you are love Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa sallam, shayṭān cannot be coming to you. Don't say, "I'm, I can following Qur'ān and I take everything from Qur'ān." If you take from Qur'ān you do like this people. Astaghfiru Llāha l-‘Aẓīm, AllāhNone save us from this our bad ego not make play with us like toy and to take us to Jahannam. AllāhNone, ma‘ādha Llāh.

Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
