Bismillah Shariff & 40 Salat Munajiyya, 100x Hasbun Allah wa Ni'mal Wakil 10x La Hawla Wa La Quwwata illa Billahi l Aliy' Al Azim 100x Ya Wadud.

You may continue with these. don't stay idle. This is the basic wird. 100x La Ilaha illa Allah, 100x Hasbuna Allah wa Ni'mal Wakil 100x Allah Allah Allah Allah... 100x Ya Wadud. When they are free they may recite these..

Whoever has time & knows how to read Holy Quran, may recite Quran. If there are people here asking to learn to recite Quran,teach them Rukiya. There are people who already know. If people are asking to learn, you may teach them for an hour daily.

"Recite 1000 Tawhid, Ahmad". He would come & look from the door: "I have finished, O Master". "Have you finished the 1000?" "Yes, I have" "Read 2000, Ahmad & come back" He says Ok & leaves. "La Ilaha illa Allah La Ilaha illa Allah La Ilaha illa Allah" It doesn't even take 5 minutes & Ahmad comes back. May Allah make his abode paradise.

Again, he comes to the door, so that he can talk to and listen to Grandshaykh (Shrafuddin Daghestani). "O Master, I have finished" "Have you finished the 2000?" He says "I have finished 2000 and did even more". "Ok Ahmad, I have more things to write. You complete 5000 and come back" says Grandshaykh to keep him away for a while. Ahmad goes & comes back finishing in less than 5 minutes. "I have finished, O Master" "How many?" "It has been 2500.. or it can go up to 3000" Allah Allah.. "Then, complete it to 5000 and come back. I have some work, writing to do now." "Ok, O Master as you order". Ahmad leaves goes up to 50 000 until he finishes his letter. Again, comes back to his door. And, Sh Abdullah Effandi said: "I wanted to have a look, have a look at him, how he does it" The way he did it was "La Ilaha illa Allah la Ilaha illa Allah" He was doing like this and coming back to him. He said "Ahmad's wird is like this". May Allah make their abodes paradise. Allah Allah...

May Allah make us be with good servants in dunya & under the flag of the Prophet (sas) in the akhirat, in Jannat ul Firdaus. Jannatu Firdaus.. Whichever jannah Allah puts us.. Allah Zul Jalali wal Kamal.. If we can step into one of them, no need to ask "which jannat is it?" He says "Put him into paradise" and finish! Are we like those people who like their comfort "first class, second class..." we don't distinguish. He may put us wherever He wants. Even if He puts us on the doorstep, it is still fine. May Allah treat us with His Mercy & endless Grants. If He treats us with His Justice, no one will be saved. But for the honour of our Prophet (sas) He may let all of us in: "You may get in" through the door of the paradise. Even if millions, billions, trillions, quadrillions people await hoping, hoping from the Mercy of Allah Almighty, and waiting by the doors.. The doors open and they are all let in. Even if we are from the ones who enter last, how good is it.. how great is He, Aliy Al Ala! May Allah not make us fall into hell.

People cannot tolerate even the simplest disease in the dunya. I am thinking, how is the situation of the previous nations? How did the torment reach to them? It is not easy to stay in that pain & torment. But, what can we do? Aman Ya Rabbi, Aman Ya Rabbi... We can not tolerate even the lightest pain. And it is a strong, strong pain... Very hard to stay in it constantly.. Aman ya Rabbi, Tawba ya Rabbi.. Ya Arhamu r-Rahimin.. You know, O our Lord, You know.. You know, You are Arhamu r-Rahimin

Arhamna Wa Arhamna Bi Jahi Nabiyyi Rahma.. Allah Allah.. Allah Allah.. Aman ya Rabbi Tawba ya Rabbi.. Tawba ya Rabbi.. Tawba ya Rabbi.. Tawba ya Rabbi.. Tawba Astaghfirullah, Fatiha.

May Allah accept our Tawba.. Ya Rabbi shukur...
