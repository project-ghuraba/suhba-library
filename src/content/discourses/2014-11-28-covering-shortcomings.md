Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Our tariqa is based on Tasawwuf. And Tasawwuf is subject to Sharia. Nothing should happen outside of Sharia. Also, the real meaning of Tasawwuf is ‘At-Tariqatu tahammul al-adab’. The essence of Tasawwuf is bearing contrary things and covering up shortcomings. It is easy to talk about this but difficult to apply. A person needs to do it slowly, gradually and as much as one can. A person’s progress increases level by level. It is not good for a person to enter tariqa and continue their old ways and old habits.

When people enter tariqa, they get under training next to a guide. Training is done slowly as much as they can do. Allah (JJ) would forgive what they cannot do now. There is a hardship in the way of tariqa of course, it is hard on the ego. The soul is happy with these things (worship, guidance, etc.). A person’s soul is happy with good things. The ego is not happy and it is hard on it.

What kind of shortcomings are there? Everybody has shortcomings. There is no need to go and announce that shortcoming in front of everybody. There is no need for backbiting. But some things that might harm people can be said. These things need to be told so people are careful and do not get harmed. Because it would not be good for the brothers’, people’s good intentions to be abused. When there is danger, it needs to be let known. There is no obstacle in letting know what that person is going to do.

However, there is no need to go and say that everywhere in normal times. Everybody has flaws and faults. Do not mention the flaws, but say the good things. You find goodness if you say the good things. Change to better. This way your ego will have been trained. May Allah (JJ) never leave anybody to their ego Inshallah.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq.

Al-Fatiha.
