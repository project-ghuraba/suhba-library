Shaykh Muhammad Nazim Al-Haqqani An-Naqshibandi, Sohbat of the 21 of February, 2014.

Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim Mankind who burns & is burned for a nothingness. Keep this in mind-Mankind who burns and is burned for a nothingness. It is not worth it but, people are running after the nothingness. They are running after the non-existent. What can come out from the non-existent? There is a saying that "No existence can come out from the non-existent".

Let us say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Say it. Say it. Say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim extinguishes any fire. What comes out from the nothingness? Only nothingness comes out. Our Allah. Our Subhan, our Sultan. We woke up in goodness and may we find goodness, son. That is how they said- O Shahi Mardan- it is annihilating the world for a nothingness. For something which is not existent it made the world upside down- for the non-existent, not for the existent. Don't run after the non-existent, the nothingness. O our Lord, we are weak. What should we go after? Go after the existent. Why do you run after the non-existent?

Shaytan has made it all upside down for nothingness. It made people fall into troubles- may it be away from us. Seek the existent. Don't run after the non-existent. Humanity today are after the non-existent. They are running to collect what is non-existent. It doesn't even exist! What can you collect from non-existence? It doesn't exist that you can collect it! Why don't you go after the existent?

Go ahead O Shahi Mardan. Your yaran admire you. Our Umm Hiram Sultan (Hala Sultan) supports us so we can stand on our feet O great Sultan- she has been in that maqam for 1500 years. She is keeping her maqam for the good of the nation of the Muhammad (sas). If it wasn't for the good of the nation of Muhammad (sas), what is the need for that mosque, for that turba, for that visit? Aman Ya Rabbi. It is the great Sultan who lies there, her support reaches us. Her support reaches us- to our people, to our children.

O Allah. People are after the non-existent. They want to collect the non-existent. It doesn't even exist! Why do you run after the non-existent? What can you take from the non-existent? The old people would say "No existence can come out from the non-existent". All people are after the non-existent now. And for this they have no peace, no rest anymore.

O our Lord! May He forgive us for the honour of His Habib. We should know what to seek. What should be sought is what exists. Don't seek what doesn't exist! It tires you, crushes you, consumes you. Don't go after what doesn't exist. Look to the existent. Allahu Akbar Allahu Akbar Allahu Akbar wa Lillahi l-Hamd. How beautiful.

It destroyed all these people- to run after the non-existent. O my son Mehmet Effendi, they are running after the non-existent. They are tiring and can't gain anything either. Then at least say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Recite, recite it 7 times and you may open up. O our Lord, we are weak servants. You sent us the Sultan who will give us strength- Your Habib- what a beautiful Prophet, what a glorious Prophet. What a beautiful Prophet is he, O glorious Prophet. May you send us a Sultan. Fatiha.

Allah Allah. Let us say Allah Allah. Let us remember His Great Prophet- the Master of Creation. Let us walk on his way. The holy ones may have us walk but what can we do? We are weak. May He take away our weakness. May we keep the way of Allah. May we be filled with the love of the glorious Prophet, who is

Fatiha.
