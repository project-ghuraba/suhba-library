As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam says, “Do not interfere in everything.” He said, “Do not interfere in things that are not your business and do not concern you.” A person who possesses the beautiful morals of Islam does not interfere in such things.

مِنْ حُسْنِ إسْلَامِ الْمَرْءِ تَرْكُهُ مَا لَا يَعْنِيهِ
<br>“Min ḥusni Islāmi al-mar'i tarkuhu mā lā yaʿnīh”<br>
“Part of the perfection of one’s Islām is his leaving that which does not concern him.” [Ibn Mājah, Tirmidhī]

He should not interfere in things that are not necessary for him. It is acceptable if he is attending to his own affairs and helping his family and friends. But he should stay away from things that are completely irrelevant, things that wouldn’t benefit him whether he interfered or not.

One already has many things to do; as long as he’s on the way of Allah, there’s no need to look at anything else. Every person has a different duty. To help him fulfill his duty, you should say, “May Allah help him.” If you think you can achieve something by interfering in his work and giving him advice, things will get worse if he listens to you; it’s better if he doesn’t.

Because Allah has given everyone a duty to perform; everyone has their own work and responsibilities. One person can’t do what another can; one person can’t study what another can. Everyone has their own separate tasks.

Everyone must know their limits. Say, “My limit is here, I don’t know what goes beyond this.” There are experts who know what’s beyond this. Because nowadays, people all think of themselves as all-knowing. They interfere with everyone, saying, “You will do this, you will do that.” Or, they tell others, “I heard this, I heard that.” What you heard is different, what you understood is different, what you saw is different. Those are things you don’t know; don’t interfere in them. If you don’t interfere, you will be at ease, and you will make others at ease too.

Therefore, this is a beautiful saying of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. For the good of mankind, he says, don’t interfere in things you don’t know. That is important, very important. Control yourself, your ego. Know your limits, don’t go beyond them. If you know things, you can tell people. There is no need to tell others about things you don’t know, just because you heard them by word of mouth.

May Allah protect us. This is already a strange time. Therefore, if you don’t want to cause yourself a headache and stress, don’t interfere; don’t interfere in matters that don’t concern you.

May Allah protect us all from fitnah, there are many fitnahs. May Allah protect us. Allah rewards people according to their intentions. So many shukr to Allah.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
