Allahumma salli wa sallim 'ala Nabiyina Muhammad 'alayhi salam. Salatan tadumu wa tughda ilayh mamarra layali wa tuula dawam. Allahumma salli wa sallim 'ala Nabiyina Muhammad 'alayhi salam. Wa Salli Ya Rabbi wa sallim 'ala jami'il anbiya' wal mursalin, wa ali kullin ajma'in, wal Hamdu Lillahi Rabbil 'alamin. As-Salamu Alaikum.

Sit comfortably, yes. Sit comfortably that the gathering finds comfort. Don't sit on pins & needles. It makes everyone uncomfortable if you do so. Therefore when you sit in a gathering for Allah, you should sit comfortably. Even if you miss your plane, or ship, let it go. I will pay for your ticket. Now I will again insha Allah do my brother's sunnah & talk in two languages but with this different attitude or different style. S. Mehmet Effendi is doing sohbats both in English & Turkish. I was doing sohbats only in English but now following his sunnah al saniyya I will do Turkish for our Turkish brothers & English for English. So we try to divide it somehow.

Now the important things in life, so many people don't realise & they don't understand. When you are doing something for Allah Almighty, it will come like you've been spending ages & like you've been having a lot of time & very heavy burden; like you carrying one ton, like really feeling the time. But what you are doing for other things, let's say you go for cinema or you go for theatre or you go for other things the time will pass like very quickly because it is empty. You don't feel the time. When people reserve a time for Allah they feel the weight of this time & they feel how slowly this time passes. Because Allah Dhu-l Jalal wa Taqaddas Hz charges the time which belongs to Himself with fayz/spiritual energy even if it is only a minute & for this shaytan doesn't let man - "let's go, let's leave, come on" Why? Because it is for Allah. But Allah also charges it. If we sit here for 10 minutes it feels like 10 hours. But if man goes to a movie theatre or a shaytan thing, it finishes in 5 mins. You say "Allah, how fast it finished." It is shaytan’s work, it is empty inside, nothing. Therefore when something is for Allah Almighty, try to hold yourself, be patient because you gain more rewards. Why? You stay there for Allah & also you don't obey shaytan. This is, it is doubling. You can do the math. This is like this.

Now people are coming to Mawlana Shaykh & they say "we can't see Mawlana". You should know that Mawlana being upstairs & breathing is enough. Who comes here does not leave empty- handed. Who comes here leaves definitely taking whatever was in his heart. So this door is not an empty door, Alhamdulillah. And for other things that when people coming for this door they have to know, even they can't see Mawlana Shaykh, even they are missing Mawlana Shaykh, & even Mawlana Shaykh is not present with us in our sitting, in our sohbat but by his breathing upstairs we know that Alhamdulillah everything in place & whatever we ask been accepted. Because this door is not empty door. This door is full of Power of Allah Almighty, Rasulullah & Awliya. Because you are coming for the presenting of them. And that is why however, whoever coming here with any intention in his heart, he will be given. That is because He know that he is coming for this door. And even by coming by coincidence. Like last night there was some children coming. And Alhamdulillah with the barakah of Mawlana, been given for them. They have been coming for other intention & they got the pure intention.

Alhamdulillah that shows that Mawlana Shaykh still existing, still in life & still in care about us. Alhamdulillah. That is why awliyaullah never die, never die.. Therefore Awliyaullah never die. They are always alive. They still have power & shafa'ah. He says "what things do you gain by following an awliyaullah".? It makes you gain so many things, in dunya & also in akhirah. So when you see the door of an awliya, try to hold on to it so tight. And what is the benefit to be knowing waliullah? It is that you have benefit in dunya & you have benefit in akhirah. That is why, my humble advice, when you see waliullah, catch him! Catch him very strongly & don't leave him.

And this reminds me with very funny story about one man. He was taking wudhu in the mosque & somebody entered the bathroom. And he went out with different shape. He was "Huh, what?" He catch him. This reminded me of a story. It is a bit of a funny story. Once upon a time while a man was making ablution, he saw that another man came in for ablution also & left as a different man! He said "You leave as a different man than the one entered. There is a secret in you". "No there is nothing, I am a normal man". He said "No, there is a secret in you. You came out as a different man. This is not possible. There is a saintliness in you" "No, I have no such a thing. Leave me". He said no & held on to him. That man say "You have something in you. And you must be waliullah. You entered inside different personality you coming outside different one. How come? You are waliullah. I don't leave you". He hug him & catch him & say "I will never leave you". Whatever that man said, he didn't leave him. So finally he said "Ok, come with me. You caught me". He took him & together they went here, there.. but he is holding his hand so tight, never letting him go.

Finally after going to many many places they came to a strange neighborhood, maybe it was the gypsy's neighborhood, something strange. So they went in it. He looked, there is a gathering & there are a lot of men like him. They told him to sit. That man was stick on the other man & he was going all around with him. And after that, they come to a very humble place & a very humble house. And when they enter he find a few people like him. And they've been sitting & there was one small pot of water. And there was boats in that water. And one of them start to make wave. He make wave, he make wave & become as a storm. And one of the boat start to shake and then start to sink. And when that boat sunk, there is some people. And other one, the other one he made the boat to sink. He put his finger & he make it to sink. And the other one, there is some people he start to make "this one must go, this one must leave". So he was pointing. And that man come "save them, save them!" They ask their friend, he say "who is this one, From us or not?" He say "No, he got me. That is why I brought him here."

He say "Ok. Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim", he make for his face like this & he find himself taking wudhu. But the secret in this story is you cannot carry the burden of the awliya & what they are doing. This is, they've been Ahl al Nawba, this is they are responsible for the moving things in the world. So when they say "this man must sink" must sink. This boat must sink, must sink. This boat must be saved, will be saved. The storm must come, everything in control.

They took that one among themselves when he came in their place. He saw that there is a large can of water there. There were a few people & one of them said "Let's shake this a little bit". And there are small boats in it. So one of them started to make waves. The water started to get really rough and there were some boats there. One of them said "this boat must sink". So he pushed it with his finger&sink it. And people started to come out from that boat & jumping into the water. And the other one said "This one must go, this one must stay, this one must be saved & these ships may save, etc" And the man who came with him said "Yahu, why are you killing these men? Save them, save these boats!" So he started to make noise like this. And they asked him, the one who brought him "Yahu, is he from us or not?" "No he is not, he found out about me so I brought him". They said "Ok then.

They made like this to his face "Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim" & the man found himself making wudhu. He said "Where are these people, where did they go?" But that is not the secret of this. The secret is that Allah appointed such servants that everything is under control. The waves, the ships, the planes... everything is in the control of Allah's power.

So you should not underestimate awliyaullah. Each one of them is appointed with a mission. Therefore we should pray so that Allah show every one of us the right way, the right person, the right awliya. Alhamdulillah we believe that we saw/ found the right person, the right awliya & he found us, he saw us & he invited us. We are weak people, there is nothing in us, no secret. But they have a secret that they say "Come, you have a right/entrust in me. I need to give it to you". That is the power of awliya. Awliya doesn't ask money or people to be behind him, following him. He doesn't care. Awliyaullah is with Allah. Allah only, nothing else. Now waliullah, he doesn't need to come to him. He is saying to you "come to me". Making your heart to move & you come for awliyaAllah. And he say "you have some right with me, so I have to give it to you, I have to deliver it to you". So that is why you are coming.

Don't think that "Eh I am coming to Shaykh & I support him by being his man". No. he doesn't need. He doesn't need people, he doesn't need money, he doesn't need power because he have Allah Almighty. Allah is for him. If Allah for that waliullah, what else can ask? Allah is everything. Allah is everything. So that is why we should be from shakirin, like my brother & we should be from tawba, like me, saying "tawba ya Rabbi Astaghfirullah". I am the one, it is must be in tawba & all the time telling my bad ego "you must be in tawba all the time. Don't be proud by sitting in this chair. Don't be proud by saying "I am talking to these people" & don't say "these people are respecting me because maybe I make them to laugh with coffee talk" & etc etc etc..
