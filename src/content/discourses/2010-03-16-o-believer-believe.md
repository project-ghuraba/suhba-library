Madad. Dastur Ya Sayyidi

La ilaha illa Allah La ilaha illa Allah La ilaha illa Allah Sayyidina Muhammadun Rasulullah alayhi salatullah wa salamuhu.

Assalamu alaykum, Attenders, Listeners for a humble association. May Allah grant to you a good understanding, also to me and to make people to understand. Allahu Qadirun, Allahu Azizun, Allahu Karimun, Allahu Lateefun, Dhi'l Arshi wa'l Karam, Rabbuna wa Rabbu 'l Alameen. He is Creator of this World and everything on it. For the honour of the Seal of Prophets, Sayyidina Muhammad (sas). O Habibullah, I am a weak servant to you and I am asking support. Support from you, O most beloved one in Divine Presence, through Divinity. I am ashamed asking from you, but at least the weakest creature may support me,

Wama Dhalika `Ala Allahi Bi`Aziz And that is not difficult for Allah (14:20).

O Salafi ulamas! What do you think? If Allah Almighty asking to give to me a support through one small ant, you can accept it or not? Yes, why not? And Allah Almighty saying: And that is not difficult for Allah. So easy. Try to believe. Try to believe, all people. To understand is so difficult, but to believe is easy. You may believe and for believing there is no limit. But understanding has limits. You can't understand everything endlessly, but you can believe endlessly. That is a speciality granted to Mankind.

And say: Audhu Billahi min as-shaytan ar-rajim, Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Rahim. When you are believing in the Holy Names of Allah Almighty, B’ismAllah ar-Rahman ar-Rahim, you may reach distances and territories and oceans. You may reach. Beliefs are taking you there but your material being can't carry you to those distances. Therefore, understanding is in limits but beliefs have no limits. And as much as you are believing, beyond your beliefs are unlimited belief-fields. Not fields, oceans. Oceans of creatures that He created.

And we are saying: Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Rahim by the honour of the Seal of Prophets, Sayyidina Muhammad (sas). Support us, O the Seal of Prophets (sas). That also is weak asking. The Lord of Heavens may support you through an ant. Why you are asking for the Seal of Prophets (sas)? The representative of the Lord of All Creation in Divine Presence. The Seal of Prophets (sas) is the only one through divinity who from pre-Eternal up to Eternal is representing his Lord's Divinity. That is a depthless ocean. Allahu Akbar! Allahu Akbar!

And we are asking a support from the one who is supporting the whole world that we are on. One person supporting everything on this planet, on dunya. How many dunya you are knowing O Salafi ulamas? You are knowing only one. You may know, but if all peoples' power coming to be in one person it would not be enough for surrounding this world, to know the purpose of everyone's creation; what they have been created for and which holy name they are using to be in existence. No one knows. No one knows. The Qutb is holy one on this planet, holiest one. Even by himself, his heavenly power can't reach an understanding for everything. Generally he knows, but personally if he is not granted a private or special ability, he can't approach an understanding of that creature. Heavenly permission must be given for understanding that.

You are looking at an ant running. You as a man here, as representative for the heavenly being, with your power can't understand if an ant is running like this or like that. What is its destination? Where it is going? Which way it is running on? You can't know. Millions, trillions, quadrillions, pentillion ants running. If the authority to know is not granted to you -where it is running or what is its purpose or what is its last point going to be, you cannot understand. But the Creator is knowing. This is like a lightning on our mentalities; thunder or lightning. If you are asking, you may understand. There must come a lightning from heavens so that you may understand about that. Allah Akbar! Allahu Akbar wa Lillahi'l hamd.

You know O our Lord, You are Creator. We are weak servants and according to Your most beloved ones will, this humble association, meeting is going on here. And people may think that this knowledge is going to finish one day. Not in one day, one year, one hundred years, thousand years, never finishing. Never finished. Don't think that only what you are learning is knowledge and beyond it is nothing. No! Salafi ulamas are thinking that knowledge is only what they are knowing and beyond it no knowledge or reality. No. You have wrong way and wrong understanding. Allahu Akbar wa Lillahi'l hamd.

Therefore, for understanding we are beginning for example with an ant. So many people are asking, "Who is Allah?" And I am saying, before asking, ‘Who is Allah’, ask an ant, "Who are you?" "From where are you coming?" "To where are you going?" "What are you doing?" "Why are you in existence?" Ask him or ask that one, and speak to ant. Don't think that it is deaf. No! Hearing. Listening. Don't think that it is not speaking; dumb. No. It may speak and it may understand and it is able to answer you. Perhaps you are not knowing about you what an ant is knowing about itself.

Therefore try to know, to learn and to believe. Knowledge is behind and belief in front. Therefore, Allah Almighty ordering:

Ya Ayyuha'lladhina Amanu Aminu O Believers, believe (4:136) What does it mean, O Salafi ulamas? What does it mean if Allah Almighty (astaidh Billah) is saying: O you who believe, believe in Allah and His Messenger (4:136) Which Iman?! You are saying, "We are believers", but Allah Almighty is calling His people and saying, "O Believers, believe in Allah”, ya ayyuha alladhina amanu, aminu Billahi wa Rasulihi. That means your beliefs for Allah are very weak because beyond it endlessly, there is knowledge. Oceans of knowledge, oceans of wisdom. How you are saying, "We are believers"? To where you are believing? Is your belief in a limit? After that limit there are unlimited oceans.

Therefore Allah Almighty saying: ya ayyuha alladhina amanu, aminu Billah. That means O Salafi ulamas, O People, it is not enough for you to say: amantu Billahi wa mala'ikatihi wa Kutubihi wa Rusulihi wal Yawmil Akhiri wa bil Qadr khayrihi wa sharrihi min Allahi T `aala. To say this is most simple way for entering through unseen territories of Creation. Now we are curtained, we are veiled. Not yet an opening coming to look behind that veil, what it is. And don't think that after you are passing that, it is the first or the last. No. You may find countlessly firsts and countlessly lasts, continuously.

O People therefore, try for zero or even less than zero because belief makes you move forward. Look right hand side and left hand side to see that you can understand at that time the endless power, endless greatness, and endless full Kamal, perfection countless perfections of your Lord Almighty Allah. "Move! Don't stay". That means, ya ayyuha alladhina amanu, aminu Billah. That is one limit that you are reaching, don't stop there, move further you will find another horizon and veil. Don't stop there when it is opening to you, ask beyond that horizon, what there is. That is pleasure, that is honour, that is Real Life. And that is real grant from Allah Almighty to you, O Mankind. You have been created for that, You are not created for this dirty life, Clean yourself and come to Me! No one approaching My Divine Presence if not reaching heavenly presence".

Heavenly presence, heavenly stations and levels are impossible to reach also if you are not moving, if you are carrying your donkey on your back. Donkey? What is our donkey? Our Egos. If you are coming with your donkey instead of riding on it, it is riding on you you are never going to be accepted. Thrown away. "Go! You are coming here with your donkey? Putting on your shoulders and bringing it? Go away!" And angels kicking down that person. Therefore be clean! Be clean! Cleanliness is most important aim of heavenly messages through heavenly messengers. To make people clean, from what? From the dirtiness of this life. And the dirtiness of this life is our material beings' dirties.

O People, Alhamdulillah, we are trying to believe. As much as you are believing you will approach. Latarkabunna Tabaqan An Tabaq You will progress from stage to stage (84:19)

Step by step yes, latarkabanna is addressing to Rasul (sas) and latarkabunna, collectively to all of you. You must ask for reaching countless levels. And when you are reaching, you are not going to be fully satisfied. Yes, every level gives a satisfaction and perfection. If you are happy with that station you may be there, doesn't matter. But the Lord of Heavens is not happy with His servants to be on one level. He is asking, "Come and take. Come to Me and reach much more".

O our Lord, we knowing nothing. And therefore we are living in last days of life on this planet. And specialised people have been granted special knowledge. If you can, ask for such people. They may show you some ways for a take off; to leave the position you are in, to get up. The first level of mankind on this planet is the level of animals. Try to make a difference between you and animals. Animals are always looking down, never looking up. First-level people are always occupying themselves with this dirty life. Never looking up to see what is above their heads. That is the foundation of troubles and miseries and sufferings, battles and wars. All because we are not looking up, always looking down. And down below is level of animals.

If a person is like an animal there is much quarreling with each other. You ever heard of angels fighting each other? And prophets had been sent to make people like angels. To save them from animals' position. To carry people from a bad position, a position of animals, to take them up. Make them clean and to take them up. But now, la hawla wa la quwwata, it is getting so difficult for people, because they are running to be slaves of dunya, slaves and servants of their ego-levels and shaytan. They are going to be fixed on this Earth, never tasting any good taste through this life for the Afterlife.

May Allah forgive us and give us something for understanding. O People say Astaghfirullah, Astaghfirullah, Astaghfirullah. For the honour of the Seal of Prophets Sayyidina Muhammad (sas). Ya Rabbi zidhu izzan wa sharafa nuran wa surura ridwanan wa sultana.

Fatiha

People may ask, "What is there from pleasure?" One after one, coming much more pleasure. With heavenly singings, heavenly music of angels. You can't imagine. Think on it, on what has been said from heavenly revelation, from prophets, so that you should reach endless pleasure here and Hereafter.

For the honour of most honoured one, Sayyidina Muhammad, Allahumma salli ala Sayyidina Muhammad.
