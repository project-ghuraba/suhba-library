From a Lion (74:50-51) - (ar) Mawlana Shaykh Mohamad Nazim al Haqqani an Nakshibendi 10 Shaʿbān, 1432 A.H. Tuesday, July 11, 2011 Translated from Arabic by: http://saltanat.org/ BismillahirRahmanirRahim Anta Rabbuna, Anta Hasbuna, Anta Khaliquna Anta Raziquna Anta 'Adina, Ya Rabbana! Ya Rabbana! Ultuf bina, ultuf bina... Anta Al Qadir Al Muqtadir! You are our Lord. You are enough for us. You grant us our provision. You created us. You are our Lord. Bestow your mercy on us. You are Almighty, All Powerful. Subhansin, Sultansin. Oh our Lord, Glory to You, all Mightiness to You. And we are your servants. Grant us to be on the right path. Improve our situations and keep us away from the deception of this dunya and the punishment of Akhirat, for the sake of Your chosen Prophet (saws) of Mercy. Subhana Allah Al Aliy Al Azim! Today, it is probably the 10th of the glorious month of Sha'ban, the glorious Sha'ban. Its glory has been given by Allah. To glorify what ALLAH Almighty has glorified is a duty on us for our faith (iman). Oh servants of the Lord, glorifying the Orders from Heavens is a proof of true faith. Therefore, the whole Islamic world should glorify for the sake of the order of Heavens. This is our first obligatory task. This is the first duty of the Islamic world. Those who surrendered and became Muslims and who said, “We have submitted to You, oh Allah Almighty, the Lord of Heavens”, say, "We have submitted to Islam". First you must accept to be Muslim. Later you become mumin (believer). First islam (submission), then comes iman (faith). Subhana Allah!

Qul Lam Tu'uminu Wa Lakin Qulu Aslamna (49:14)

Say (unto them, oh Muhammad): Ye believe not, but rather say "We submit," This is a Holy Verse and you may look it up and read it. Whoever says Aslamna (we have surrendered to Islam) gives his word to his Lord (jwa) that he will be an obedient servant to the Divine Order. He submits to the orders of Allah (jwa) and follows and submits to the sunnah of His Beloved (saws). Allah Almighty said (Astaizzu Billah...),

Wa Ma Atakumu Ar-Rasulu Fakhudhuhu Wa Ma Nahakum Anhu Fantahu (59:7)

And whatsoever the messenger giveth you, take it. And whatsoever he forbiddeth, abstain

(from it). (Picthal) Is it not like this? Now people are ignorant in the mosques and in the churches. On the mimbar (pulpit) they are moving their mouth with a speech in which they claim to be believers, that they believe what was brought in the Holy Qur'an, and [they declare],“We do not accept anything else." This is the deepest level of ignorance. Blind ignorance. And the Lord (jwa) ordered, "And whatsoever the Prophet (saws) gives you, take it; and whatsoever he forbids you, abstain from it". Allahu Akbar! Whoever rejects even one letter of the Holy Qur'an is kafir (unbeliever). The word 'amanna' (we believed) indicates that we believe in whatever is necessary to be of good faith – everything that is required to be a muslim, and later to be a mumin. For everyone it is the same. We have left all the forbidden actions in obedience to the order of the Prophet of the end of times (sas), the one who was given the Magnificent Qur'an with Allah's (swt) words. We are asking support from the men of Allah. La Ilaha illa Allah. Listen and understand, take wisdom. If you understand, you will benefit. Nowadays, people are wild. An example of them is,

Ka'annahum Humurun Mustanfirahun Farrat Min Qaswarahin (74:50-51)

As if they were frightened asses, fleeing from a lion. (Picthal) The knowledge of the people of old times was knowledge from the Heavenly Realm (Malakut). This verse describes people nowadays, as it once described the liars, hypocrites, and unbelievers in the time of Holy Prophet (sas). They used to run away and a description came on them in a Holy Verse, "As if they were frightened donkeys". They were described as the lowest, most foolish of animals, the donkeys (jack-asses), "Ka'annahum Humurun” (resembling frightened asses). This is a simple description. So easy. He (swt) said, "They are like frightened donkeys, frightened..." Qaswarah is one of the names of the lion. Humurun is the donkey. Mustanfar is running and rushing away. Istinfar mixes the feelings of fear and confusion. Istinfar is fear together with astonishment or shock. The donkeys are running away in a state of shock from the lion. "Ka'annahum Humurun” (as if they were frightened donkeys). Allah did not say 'himir' (donkeys), but 'humurun' (also meaning donkeys). The Arabic language is extremely eloquent and articulate. The Arabs at the time of the Prophet reached the peak of eloquence and articulation in their speech. Therefore, the Qur'an used the word 'humurun' as the plural for donkey, not the word 'himir', which also means donkeys. There is a distinction between himir and humurun. Humurun was used, and not himir, because the tazim ('un' at the end of the word) is used to indicate greatness. But there are two types of greatness. One type is honoring, the absolute honor The second type is emphasizing the absolute belittlement. Greatness is for the Heavenly realms. But when the 'un' is used with the word hamir (as in 'humurun'), it is to indicate the lowest, most foolish animal level, the asses that are only able to carry burdens. They have nothing except the ability to transport burdens or whatever human beings need to transport. That's it. This is their only mission. "Ka'annahum Humurun Mustanfirahun Farrat Min Qaswarahin.” 'Qaswarah' is one of the names for 'lion'. And for the lion, there are 100 names. The large number of names indicates the level of honor attributed to the one who carries them. How many names are there for Prophet (saws)? Say! We know 313 or more names for the Prophet (sas) that are mentioned in the books. These are attributes of the Prophet (saws); but this is only according to our capacity to understand. A Divine favor was granted to us to know him and to know the attributes given to the Beloved, Al Mustafa. This is for the level of the servants, the majority of the people. But

Wa Fawqa Kulli Dhi Ilmin Alimun (12:76)

… and over every lord of knowledge there is one more knowing. (Picthal) There are different and various levels of understanding. You cannot reach the horizon of the various stations of understanding. Subhana Allah!

Wa Lillahi Al Asma'u Al Husná (7:180)

The most beautiful names belong to Allah. (Yusufali) Asma'u Al Husná (Divine Names), how many are they? There are not just 99. This is what was given to us at our level. But for Allah there are so many names, they cannot be counted. From pre-Eternal to post-Eternity, appearing, appearing in a new manifestation that represents the Divine Name that is appearing in the Heavenly domain. Allahu Akbar! In spite of the fact that people say they are Muslims, Muslims have actually been given that honor Allah has honored them by granting them Islam. He (swt) honored us. And for all Muslims their honor is coming in part from having any of the names of the Prophet (saws). Any blessed name they have from him (saws) gives them honor Subhana Allah! And we are talking about the situations that are taking place and are happening everyday from the al khabir al yakin – the certain news. (That us, the news from indisputable sources about the present and what is about to happen, the manifestations [from the heavenly realm].) We are living in a time, where dunya (the world) is all that is important for the people. They are not interested in Akhirat (hereafter); and this indicates that people now have reached the lowest level, extreme humiliation. They have no honor left to understand things that are from the Heavenly Realms. And Allah (swt) sent selected servants to spread His (swt) Divine Message, to give His (swt) servants honor according to their capacity. The honor of a nation is according to their connection to their Prophet (saws). It's incredible! And people now look strange, with an astonishing form. They don't have a sound understanding. No! They don't have a way to comprehend. They don't have understanding. They have lost their logic. They have no justice nor balance; and Allah (swt) says,

Wa Wada`a Al-Mizana (55:7)

… and He has set up the Balance. For whom is the balance? For Him (swt)? Hasha! He (swt) set the balance for His (swt) believing servants according to their capacity to understand, and the ability of their minds, and their efforts to expand [their understanding]. The Balance is placed according to their capacity to understand, and not higher. It is not possible for it to be above their capacity. 'Al-Mizana' is the singular form of balance. In the Holy Qur'an, it is mentioned also,

Al-Mawazina Al-Qista (21:47)

We shall set up just balance-scales. (Asad) This is the plural form of balance – balances (scales) - not one balance (scale). They will be placed on the Day of Judgment. Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar! Allahu Akbar! And we... There is a phrase in Arabic that the high level people use when referring to a test. They say that when a person enters a test, that person is either granted honor or is humiliated. Now all of us have entered into the test. Whoever is saved is honored. Whoever fails is humiliated. Too bad for him. And people nowadays do not think about the news brought by the Prophet (saws) that people will be either honored or humiliated. At the test, a person will be honored or humiliated. Now people are lazy. It is impossible for even one of them to advance. They stopped; and when they stop, they fall. Whoever falls is a humiliated person. People in the whole Islamic world all came to the lowest level of humiliation. All the people! In the test, they have reached the absolute level of degradation. They are not raised. No, they are down to the lowest level. I regret that my ego is also counted with them. I don't claim to be free from this. All of us we are on the same path, fashioned in the same model. We are all on the same way. The arrogant one is proud of himself, proud of his knowledge, proud of his understanding and his actions. He is proud of his attributes and his movements. He is proud of his understanding. He does not lower himself to anyone. He says, "I am me! I am me!". And I am sorry to say these words. I am not claiming innocence myself, because it is my ego also. It is fashioned on the same model. This is an important matter. Except for a few who put their egos under their feet, the nation of the Beloved (sas) are mocked by shaytan. Instead of following the Prophet of the end of times (sas), they are rushing after shaytans. Now they have no honor nor understanding and they have lost the sweetness in their lives, here and in the hereafter. La hawla wa la quwwata illa Billah..Allah Ya Allah. Allahumma Anta Allah.
