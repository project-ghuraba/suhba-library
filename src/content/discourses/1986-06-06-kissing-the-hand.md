Kissing the hand? To kiss the hand of a wali or a scholar or a respected ruler, a sultan - this is the adab of Islam. Now if we found ourselves in the presence of the Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam would we shake hands with the Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam? Strange. Wouldn't you kneel down and kiss him? The Jews came and converted to Islam. They kissed his hand, prostrated at his feet, kissed his feet. The Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam didn't say, “Don't kiss.”

How do these devils invent this? Really, they don't want any person in the society to be shown respect. If there is a respected person, he gathers people. They don't want anyone to gather people around him. They don't want anyone to gather the hearts together.

You come and kiss my hand out of love. Do I say, "Come kiss my hand?" Now in the books of our Ahle s-Sunnah, it is written in books in Arabic, the last one in the Big Islamic Ilmihal, written by Omar Nasuhi Effendi Hazret (a book of religious instruction) it also says that you should kiss the hand of a wali, a scholar. You should kiss the hand of a just Sultan, a just Ruler. You kiss your parents' hands, respected and honoured people's hands. I mean it is all right to kiss the hand. It is a way to show respect; it is a sign of respect. We inherited this from our ancestors, it is one of our nice adabs. They want to destroy this also, the Wahhabis.

You are you, I am me. This leads to Communism. There is the tradition of kissing the hand of a wali, of a scholar. There is no rule that says only men can kiss. Everyone is allowed to kiss. Ignorance makes people terrible. May Allah correct them.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
