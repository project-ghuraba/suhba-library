As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Happy ‘Īd, ‘Īdu l-Aḍḥá, the feast of ḥajj. They used to call it the feast of the ḥajjīs in Cyprus. May it be blessed. May it be good, in shā’a Llāh. May we reach the years when Islām shows itself all over the world, in shā’a Llāh. We are approaching the time of Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām. We are waiting for him, in shā’a Llāh. Because Islām is something good for all people. It is a favour given by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla to people. In order for all people to attain it, in shā’a Llāh, Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām will come and make all the world Muslim with permission of AllāhNone. The promise of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is true. The promise of our Holy ProphetNone, everything heNone said is true. Everything will happen, in shā’a Llāh.

‘Īd, these feasts, shukr to AllāhNone are the good days Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla granted us. And we have ‘Īd treats. Just like children wait for their treats, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gives us too. We are Allāh'sNone servants. In order to celebrate ‘Īd, the sulṭāns used to present treats to the guests who visited them. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is the Sulṭān of all sulṭāns. Certainly, HeNone has treats for us from among these beauties, both spiritual and physical. Spiritual ones are better and more beautiful. We must make shukr to AllāhNone for reaching these blessed days. When people know this and rejoice, they receive from the beneficence of AllāhNone. AllāhNone likes it when we know this, when we understand these beautiful favours of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla and are thankful for them.

If we say, "What is this feast?" it becomes useless. Or when they say, "I don't want ‘Īd. I am performing my worship anyway." No. There must be joy on these feasts. Because we are lucky servants of AllāhNone to have deserved this. It is the duty of Muslims to be thankful for favours given. And it is from adab to be thankful and to praise. The biggest adab we must show is to be thankful for things given by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, for our every breath and our every minute, for being on the right way.

May AllāhNone bless these days, in shā’a Llāh. Those who are on ḥajj completed their ḥajj today, shukr to AllāhNone. And for those who are not on ḥajj, it brings great benefit to make qurbān, slaughter, as it is wājib for Ḥanafī. Other madhhabs do it as sunnah mu’akkadah, something our Holy ProphetNone did. It is because, as it is told in ḥadīth, our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam told Sayyidatunā Fāṭimah to make qurbān. For every drop of blood that flows out, all your sins are forgiven. And for each of its hairs you attain a reward. There is a lot of virtue in it. AllāhNone has given this as a reward to people and to those who are in need. However, shayṭān doesn't sit still. He says, "You are slaughtering animals. You are killing so many animals." We are slaughtering them. But what we slaughter as Muslims in a year, they slaughter as much in a day. They have become meat eaters themselves, but when they see Muslims do it they are offended. They are swollen from eating meat themselves. Poor people eat from what we slaughter. And they either eat meat once a year, or not at all. Therefore, shayṭan doesn't sit still. He wants all kinds of badness. He doesn't want any help to come to poor people. Therefore, some Muslim people fall under his influence and question this. Questioning this is a sin.

It is the barakah of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Animals increase when they are slaughtered. And it is a good thing for the animal which is slaughtered. That animal wants this. When animals are slaughtered as wājib on this ‘Īd, they all feel great joy. Don't say animals don't feel joy. They do rejoice. It is great luck for them to be slaughtered for the pleasure of AllāhNone. Therefore, some people go against Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla without realizing it. Those who don't accept this have doubt in their belief, may AllāhNone protect us. May AllāhNone protect Muslims from the evil and whisperings of shayṭān.

Every order, everything created is by the order of AllāhNone. Do it. Don't doubt at all, so that AllāhNone gives you thawāb and accepts your effort, in shā’a Llāh. May AllāhNone let us reach many more years. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are thanking AllāhNone for this day, for ‘Īdu l-Aḍḥá. There is two feasts. ‘Īdu l-Fiṭr, after Ramaḍān, and now, the ‘Īdu l-Aḍḥá. "Aḍḥá" meaning: to feast of qurbān. Qurbān, to slaughter sheep, or cow or camel. What they are, subḥāna Llāh, AllāhNone give this three: goat, sheep and camel, and buffalo and cow, to slaughter this kind of thing. For other thing, there is maybe not only this, I think. Other, maybe some things you can eat like rabbit, like deer, like other thing. But for qurbān, only this five kind of animal you can slaughter.

And this is day which feast for human being. AllāhNone give this feast for human being who accept HisNone way. And feast meaning, to be happy. You must be happy because this gift from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. And HisNone gift for believer, it is important. You must be happy because HeNone is generous and HeNone gave us from HisNone present. As children, in ‘Īd, normally big, elder people giving children money or sweet or other present, to be happy. We are also waiting, and AllāhNone giving. HeNone is more, the most generous. We are... If you give, AllāhNone, HeNone will not give? HeNone will give us also. For these days, we are happy from HisNone present, giving us bless[ings], showering us from bless[ings].

This day, which ḥajjī already become accept[ed], they finish their, what they must do in ḥajj. And yesterday, it was ‘Arafāt, and who was in ‘Arafāt he become ḥajjī. And today still something to do to finish their... to complete their ḥajj. In shā’a Llāh, this also bless[ings] for ummah also. And for us also, we are slaughtering. For qurbān, for order of AllāhNone, Ḥanafī must making like wājib, Ḥanafī madhhab. Other, sunnah mu’akkadah, what Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam heNone was done everytime, because they must follow this. And slaughter and distribute to poor people, and for themself, and for anybody. It is nice worshipping. AllāhNone reward us for...

Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying for Sayyidatunā Fāṭimah, from first drop of blood from qurbān, AllāhNone forgive you all your sin. And other also, give for each hair of qurbān reward you one reward. It is millions of rewards in this one. And it is very important to know, because the hypocrite people, which they are eating whole year meat – this meat not coming from water, not coming from air. They slaughter... we slaughter in whole year, in one day, they cutting, killing. We are slaughtering – they are killing animal. As we, in one year we slaughter, they, in one day, they are killing and to eat. They only eat meat, meat, meat.

After they said, "Oh Islām, they are savage people. They are wild people. They are killing this poor animal." This "poor animal", they are happy to be slaughtered for qurbān. The most happy animal, who slaughtered for qurbān. Others, they wish to be in this day. They be happy. Who say, animal can be happy? Yes, they can be happy. AllāhNone give happiness for anything. For, especially for this good, pure animal. They are happy to be slaughtered for sake of AllāhNone.

And this, giving poor people. Once a year they eat meat. In one year, what we slaughter, they are killing in one day, and for rich people, or... not for poor people. We are slaughtering for poor people, most of them. But this people, the hypocrite people, and they are saying this: "Islām not good. They killing animal." They are killing, real... They don't have any... ḥaqq... they just lying, every time. They are not sincere. They don't have sincerity. Only they have hypocrisy. Seeing one point, and they are forgetting whole world. Don't listen to this, because many idiot of, some Muslim also, they are believing this. They reading this. They read everywhere, and after, they say – "Oh, why we are slaughtering?" Because Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla create human being. What they in need, HeNone give them. What we are not in need, it's not good for us – forbidden for us. To eat pork, to drink alcohol, to smoke marijuana, to smoke... take injection of heroine, meroine... What it's not good, AllāhNone saying, don't do this. But you are coming and saying: don't eat meat. What is this idiot? Allāhu akbar.

AllāhNone make our idiot people to not listen to this people. It is a real pity. We are seeing, many people from Muslims, they believe them. And they do everything, but when coming to do something AllāhNone ordered, they are saying, "This is good or not good?" AllāhNone help us. Yā AllāhNone, send us Sayyidunā l-Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām to save us from this bad people, bad character, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
