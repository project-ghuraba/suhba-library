As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
وَلَا تَهِنُوا وَلَا تَحْزَنُوا وَأَنتُمُ الْأَعْلَوْنَ إِن كُنتُم مُّؤْمِنِينَ
<br>“Wa-lā tahinū wa-lā taḥzanū wa-antumū l-aʿlawna in kuntum mu'minīn”<br>
“So do not become weak, nor be sad, and you will be superior if you are indeed believers.” (Qurʼān 3:139). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says, “Do not consider yourselves inferior to others, to the disbelievers.” Do not look at disbelievers and feel disheartened; if you are a believer, if you are a Muslim, your rank is higher. In the sight of Allah, a person’s worth is determined solely by their īmān.

A disbeliever holds no value whatsoever, even if they were to possess the entire world. In the sight of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla the whole world is not worth even the wing of a mosquito. It is essential to understand this well.

People today—even Muslims—look at worldly people with full admiration. They think that disbelievers are in a better position or possess more things. Yet, those people hold no value in the sight of Allah; one must recognize the true worth of īmān. The one who is precious in the sight of Allah is the one who possesses īmān. Therefore, a believer should not grieve, worry, or ever belittle themselves.

A believer’s value in the sight of Allah is immense. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla has bestowed value upon you—that is, the gift of īmān. Your worth is great. Let the faithless possess as much wealth, property, and power as they wish; they hold no true value. This worldly life is fleeting. Let them boast and amass as much wealth as they desire in this world; the world is short-lived.

The true, eternal existence is the Hereafter. Without securing the Hereafter, this worldly life passes by like a dream. Life slips away like a dream. What endures is the Hereafter; that is the eternal life. Whoever fails to realize this is in a state of loss; nothing they have amassed will benefit them in the Hereafter.

As our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam stated: The world is worth no more than the wing of a mosquito. What significance would even the mosquito itself hold? It has no value whatsoever. Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam offers this beautiful example to illustrate the worthlessness of the world.

Had the world possessed even the value of a mosquito’s wing in the sight of Allah, Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla would not have granted a disbeliever even a single sip of water. Because the world is of no value, Allah has given everything in this world to the disbelievers. Believing themselves to possess everything, they oppress others and do no good.

Therefore, a believing Muslim must be content with what Allah has bestowed upon them. Allah has granted them the greatest blessing—the blessing of īmān. Nothing else matters.

Whether there is work or not makes no difference; it is Allah who provides a person's sustenance. Allah grants provision when He wills. Of course, you still seek your livelihood, but the outcome rests upon Allah’s decree. Things are chaotic these days; people are constantly distressed over whether, “There’s work. There’s no work.” Yet, Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says, “Do not grieve!” If you are a believer, you must not grieve.

May Allah strengthen our īmān.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
