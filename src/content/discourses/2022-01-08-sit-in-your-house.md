As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Wa-tilka l-ayyāmu nudāwiluhā bayna n-nās" (3:140).

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is saying, this world passes from people from one to another. Wealth passes from one to another too. Ruling and everything else as well. Nothing remains for anyone in this world. Apart from the goodness a person does, nothing remains with him. "I am Sulṭān. I am Pādishāh. I am a King." No one can take anything with him by becoming like this. Everyone has left everything. This is the law of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. This is what applies to this world.

Therefore, goodness is something that everyone can take with him to the other side. They shouldn't be greedy: "I will take this world. I will become this. I will become that." They shouldn't run after dunyā by saying this and forget Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. AllāhNone has given favours. These favours will be gone as well. People will close their eyes. Everyone will close his eyes one day. Some will go and what remains will stay for inheritors. If they are good how lucky you are. If they are not, you have wasted yourself on them. And if they are good, they will do something for you. You will have thawāb. Otherwise, if you bring them up badly, you will have a share from their sins and mistakes. May AllāhNone protect us.

The world is all mixed up now. There is no need to make too much noise and fight with anyone at this time. Various events are occurring. May AllāhNone protect us, there is a lot of fitnah. Don't get involved in this fitnah. This is from the last advice of Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim: "When there is fitnah, do not go out and do not be involved in it. Don't even leave home. As long as you do so, nothing will happen to you by the permission of AllāhNone. If you go out, you are responsible then. You will carry your responsibility then." Because you will do it for dunyā. The actions of most people are for self-interest, dunyā, position, presidency, this and that. They claim to be so and make fitnah. Whereas, if they sit still, no one will be harmed.

Therefore, our advice to Muslims, especially to brothers, don't go out to the streets to join protests and this and that. Don't get involved in it. Don't be from them. You will be in peace from where you are. If you don't listen and join them, you will be responsible for it. AllāhNone will ask you why you did so, why you put yourself in danger. "Al-muslimu man salima l-muslimūn min lisānihi wa-yadih." A Muslim is a person from whom people are safe from his hand, from his tongue, from everything. A person who harms cannot be Muslim. Muslims don't harm, by permission of AllāhNone.

May AllāhNone protect us. May no one be harmed, in shā’a Llāh. May everyone be safe. We have brothers and Muslims everywhere. All of them are on the way of AllāhNone. They are with AllāhNone. There will be barakah in their countries, in shā’a Llāh.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Wa-tilka l-ayyāmu nudāwiluhā bayna n-nās" (3:140).

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla in Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n, HeNone said, "This life days living in earth, there is all time new day coming – new day coming, new week coming, new month, new year, new century. This each one it is for new people. Cannot be same people whole forever here. All time changing people." Before in this Island, there was seven kingdoms. Now only two parts and they are not happy. But they was million year before – thousands year and now – now our turn. After us, AllāhNone know what will happen. But this earth, since Ādam ‘alayhi s-salām, many people coming, many kingdoms, many emperors, many parties it was here. But AllāhNone every time changing from one to one, from one to one. And people must take lesson from this. It's not forever.

They can, if what they left, only good thing. But what they do – they collect gold, jewellery, silver, house, and they left. And they, other people coming. After that people, also other people coming. Only if they are... they have good deed – this what they take with them. Not taking anything, carrying house or treasure or anything – only good deeds. And now also we are living in time the – I think it is most difficult time in humanity time because it is end of time. Every day become more trouble, more fitnah to make people to not accept this and they said, "We will do better" and, they are trying to do better, but every time they try to do this, becoming worse. For this Mawlānā, he was saying all time, "Don't interfere with this. If anything happen, demonstration... don't at all – don't go for demonstration – for protest. Don't go with this people. Sit in your house, you will be safe." This Mawlānā's order for us, and good tiding for us.

If you sit in your house, you will be safe. If you go out and interfere with this, this is in your responsibility. What happen for you, we are not responsible for it. AllāhNone will ask you, why you go and you put yourself in this dangerous? And people not listening. They try to do better but they make it worse. AllāhNone, HeNone save us from this trouble and only make us to take with us good thing, good deed – helping for people, and obeying for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla and Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, in shā’a Llāh.

AllāhNone save Muslims because the most fitnah between them to destroy Islām and Muslim. They thought they will do better but they – every time they make something, they become worse for them and for other country – Muslim country also. AllāhNone make people to awake, to not be cheated with this people and destroy themself. AllāhNone save us, in shā’a Llāh. Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
