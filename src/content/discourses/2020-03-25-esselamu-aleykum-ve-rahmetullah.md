Vesselatu Vesselamu ala Resulina Muhammedin Seyyidul evvelin vel ahirin.

Medet Ya Resulullah, Medet ya Sadat-ı Ashab-ı Resulullah, Medet ya Meşayihina,

Şeyh Abdullah Dağıstani, Şeyh Muhammed Nazım el Hakkani, destur.

Bismillahirrahmanirrahim

Thanks be to Allah that we have reached the month of Shaban. May it be blessed. It is the month of the Holy Prophet. Rajab is the month of Allah, Shaban is the month of the Holy Prophet and Ramadan is the month of ummah insha’Allah. May it blessed for the ummah for the sake of Holy Prophet. May this month goes easy insha’Allah, lighter so that Allah forgives us for the mercy of Allah and for the sake of the holy prophet. These difficulties, suffering perishes. At night once again Salawats are recited from the minarets for the sake of the Holy Prophet saw insha’Allah for his sake this month will be easier, insha’Allah this difficulty will be perished from upon this ummah, Muslims. Those who were not on the path insha’Allah have repented and learnt a lesson. This happened to be a lesson not to forget for Muslims. Human beings were far too spoiled that Allah does not cause calamity for no reason, He causes such things because of His mercy, so that those would come back to the path again and remember Allah, believe in Allah. Therefore insha’Allah this month will be good for mumin for the sake of blessings of the Holy Prophet saw while those others who are against Allah receive penalty. If not here in next world. Therefore a lot more salawat to be recited during this month. Amongst all other wazifa let’s daily recite 1000 salawat and send it to the Holy Prophet as a gift. It is a gift for us too all what we do is welcomed multiple times by Allah Azza wa Jalla. During calamities Allah’s mercy is showering upon us. Insha’Allah this month difficulties are removed from upon this ummah. They become a little more relaxed but this situation have become something that cannot be forgotten. Since world had come to existence nothing like this happened before, yes somethings did happen similarly but not in times where population of human being was huge. Because These people claim they are strong, nothing can harm them then Allah Azza wa Jallah devastated them all. Kaffir keep saying “We did it, we succeed...” No, without Allah’s allowance you may not succeed or cause anything. Because of the prayers of servants of Allah is the salvation of humanity not because of benefits of technology or weapons, there is no benefit in them. This is the manifestation of Allah. As it has come upon us it will leave us in this month insha’Allah. May Allah protect us and increase our faith. Ve Min Allahu Tevfik El Fatiha
