As-Salamu Alaikum. Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fil jamia' Today we say, first saying for our own ego, in this Haqq way, in the way of Islam, in the way of Tariqat, there is no taking offence or resenting.

Once it is Haqq way you shouldn't resent, saying "this one said like this, this one like that". You shouldn't be offended by anyone. There is a wisdom in everything. Don't make haste on certain things and, how do they say, don't accuse anyone wrongly. First (investigate) very well.. By people speaking "this one said like this, that one like that" falling into sin. When you fall into sin, of course Allah forgives the sins but until He forgives, it becomes a very big sadness for you. Your stations go down instead of going up. Your levels go down instead of going up. Meaning you become like the ordinary people when you say "this one said like this, that one like that". And you also happen to have an ill opinion of people.

As it is said; the tongue has no bones, it speaks as it likes. If there is no proof, don't believe in rumors at all. If something happens, if one man says something, there is Holy Verse "if there is something among you, don't you believe anyone without having something written on paper, or without witness. Don't falsely accuse anyone. This is a very important thing.

And other than that, also in Tariqat, now because Shaykh Effendi is not seeing people lately.. Our Cypriot brothers are already.. Cypriots as a nation, one of their attributes, characteristic is that they get offended very much. They are strange people, this is their habit. But when you enter Tariqat you should cut this habit a little bit. They say "We can't see Mawlana, why should we come?" They say this but there are many people from our mureeds who are not coming for years, neither on eid nor on holidays. Leave aside the manners of Tariqat, this does not suit any kind of manners He is the greatest person in Cyprus & oldest one by age. Even if you can't see him, still if you come say 'here we have come'. Mawlana already has the news of your coming for sure.

So this is because of the evil of ego, people can't understand the miracles of Mawlana either. They think that Mawlana can't walk. He is sitting here not able to do anything. But it is Mawlana who controls the whole world, with Allah's permission! This is from the evil of ego. Don't follow your ego. If you follow your ego it takes you to evil, to your self destruction. Even if it is not destruction in the end, you get disappointed. You lose- This Great person, the Qutb al-Aqtab who is controlling the whole world, the inheritor of the Prophet (sas) is alive & you didn't visit him. You are lazy to come from such a short distance. Whereas people, our mureeds, are coming all the way from the other end of the world. And they keep it quiet, don't say any word like saying "I want to see him, meet him". They wait with adab. And when Mawlana comes out, they see him. And that is only from a distance. They don't even talk.

But this ego..This ego doesn't take man to anything other than his destruction. If you ride your ego, you rise. If your ego rides you, you go down. So there is nothing like "I have come but didn't see him". Come, kiss the threshold of his door & leave. It is enough for you. You reach the highest station with this. With this, you receive the heavenly support, dua of the shaykh. But we are not able to teach this to people. Allah bless them, there are some who come, they say "I saw Mawlana 10 years ago, in such place. Mawlana told me to visit him, I remembered it now". Mawlana of course remembers it but also Mawlana's current state is..

There is nothing remaining unchanged other than Allah. The only One unchanged is Allah. Allah gives for everyone something. When one grows old, all the time some things happen. And Mawlana's current state is like this. Before his door was open 24 hours. He was seeing everyone but now, even his breathing is through oxygen mask. We may say this openly. Therefore coming here and... He is already making dua for all who come. Allah blesses him, he asks for who is coming. Even if he doesn't ask, he knows. For this "this one said like this, that one like that", this doesn't matter. No one matters. You come with adab.

If you want to see him, his schedule is known- after asr time he goes out for a bit & comes back. That is if you want to see him. If you don't have this much time either come & kiss his threshold. It is also the same thing. Don't get offended. Don't follow your ego. Of course no one is obliged to..But it is our duty to say this here so that no one is deprived from such a great blessing. We say "we have already warned here". Then they may not say "no one has told us like this". Because who knows but doesn't speak, they are called "deaf shaytan". They will say on the Day of Judgement "Well, no one told us like this, no one told us to come. We thought Mawlana is sick so we better not come" No. Come, take his dua & go. It is also the same thing. So we have also declared this point here.

Today we speak about Islam & Tariqat, you must not be upset with something in Islam or in Tariqat. There are many things you cannot do in Islam. If somebody told you about this, you'd be upset about this. This is not good, from your ego. You must- if you can do, you can do. If you cannot do, don't be upset. Only repent to Allah, "we cannot do this, May Allah forgive" In Tariqat also, many thing. In Tariqat, in Islam also, there is many thing you hear by your ear, you hear it. Somebody, many people speaking.

The most of people, when they speak too much of course, one hearing from one, maybe he add little bit more. To other one, one more, one more, one more..It become very big untruth. But when there is certain subject true & it is serious one it must be written. And must be with witness. If you hear, you see this & say "this is true", Ok. But when you hear little bit, little bit & you think other thinking, other thinking adding, it become big lie. So don't listen for this. This is very important thing.

Other thing for Tariqat, especially for I say our brother in Cyprus, they have a habit. They become, not angry, not so happy.. When you see, they hear anything or they not happy with man, they are not coming not looking at all. This is our Cypriot people habit. Maybe Middle East also like this, but Cypriot I see this more. Even once... No, no need to speak for this. When some.. Now many years you cannot find Cypriot brother coming to visit Mawlana. Why? They said, "No, they were saying this for me. I become angry. I am not going that". That man said this, I am not going there because he is, I am angry with him". This is from ego. Ego making your good intention to be bad. Because from your ego, you lose the biggest favor you had between you. He is the inheritor of Prophet (sas) in this (time). And he is the most powerful spiritual in this world also, now. They said, "we are coming we are not seeing him so no need to come". This is also wrong because he, certainly he knows who come, who coming who is going. So this is also from your, their ego, it is not good. I said there is many from our brother here maybe more than 10 year they are not coming even for Eid, for praying, for dhikr, for sohbat they are not coming. They are angry for something, we don't know for what.

But there is people, when you come by adab, he is accepting your visit. And they are Sultan, they not let you go without present. I say "Even there is masha Allah coming from Argentina & they are coming from long distance, from other side, long distance and they are happy to, only to see him from away. But who here they are between him, them they are not knowing this. Many people coming from Turkey, from other side sometimes. They are also insist to speak to Mawlana when he is before noon or when he resting most of time. Because now changing his.. not like before. In this world everything changing except Allah. Everything is changing, nothing still on same.

So now like this, before it was 24 hour he is with people. He is running for everywhere. Even he was going to this coffee shop also. All this island, by walking, he went from village to village. From coffee shop to coffee shop. And this people, they are the worst. I never been inside, maybe once or twice by for something for coffee shop. It is not good. People also not educated, and they are tough people. They speak what they, without thinking. Even for this, he was happy to go with them.

So he is not by his happy to be away from you. But he cannot anymore to be with people. So it is now different. Allah give you from different side. You, Alhamdulillah, you are lucky to be, to come to him. Not he is going to you. And when you come to him, he give more barakah & more present for you. You not going empty, all going full insha Allah. This is very important. Don't be angry with people around. No, you only, you come you look for him. A rose also, they have too much thorn. The most beautiful smelling rose, too much more have this thorn. So don't look, you only when you look for Mawlana, everything is easy for you. This is very important. Don't follow your ego. Come to Mawlana insha Allah.

Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, Al Fatiha.
