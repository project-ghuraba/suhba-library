Mawlana Shaykh Mohamad Nazim al Haqqani an Nakshibendi 3 Jumādā ath-Thāniya 1432 A.H. Thursday, May 5, 2011 Translation by: http://saltanat.org/

BismillahirRahmanirRahim [Mawlana is speaking to two guests.] Each time you eat or drink, say Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Rahim. Don't forget. Also, when you have a chance, say everyday, “Rabbuna Allah Hasbuna Allah. Rabbuna Allah Hasbuna Allah. Rabbuna Allah Hasbuna Allah.” This is very strong. One piece of advice I will give you: Keep company with good people. If you are together with a good person, they say you are also a good one. If you go with a bad one, they will say: this one is also bad. Now, there are no good people left, very few of them left. All of them are running after this world. We were not created for this world. We were created for the service of Allah Almighty; but everyone is rushing and running after this world. Nobody is being a servant of Allah Almighty. The reason why Divine anger and punishment falls upon whole world nowadays is because they forgot Allah. Don't be afraid! Don't let a day pass by without making sajda. When you wake up in the morning, pray at least 2 rakats, then go out. It is dangerous if you leave the house without doing that. What is his name? Guest: Mohammad. And yours? Guest: Emir Husseyin. My grandson is also called Emir Husseyin. You should say everyday,”Rabbuna Allah Hasbuna Allah”, at least 100 times. Do not go out at night. After praying Isha, it is forbidden to go out. There are so many very bad people outside at night. Stay at home in the evening (maghrib prayer) and after Isha. Don't smoke tobacco. If a person smokes, he may get used to the other one as well: hashish (marijuana). The one who smokes tobacco may also be smoking hashish without realizing it. That is why I am saying to be with good people. Hold my hand! [Mawlana makes dua for the two visitors.] Oh, Allah Almighty, may You fix them firm on the true way. Fatiha. If you have any heaviness or you feel any heaviness, call my name. Come, Husseyin [Mawlana's assistant]. [Mawlana hands a folded tawhiz to each of the two guests.] They may fix this at home. Husseyin: [giving instructions for what to do with the tawhiz] Wrap it up and cover it with gelatine [or plastic wrap] to wear around your neck, so you may take a bath and sleep with it. Fix it and wear it. Keep my tawhiz on you. Is he married [indicating Mohammad]? Emir Husseyin: Yes, he is married. [Mawlana looking at one of the guests – Emir Husseyin] If someone grabs you by your leg [to go out], you should escape. There are so many dirty people, you understand? You have light on your face. You are from Ahlul Bayt [family of the Prophet sas]! You are Sayyid. Emir Husseyin: My mother is Sayyid. It is showing through your eyes. [Mawlana to the other guest] Come here. [Mawlana blesses him by twisting his ear.] Don't go around too much. Make dua for us. Emir Husseyin: Don't forget us, because my name is like your grandson. Now, I will go out for a little bit. [Mawlana's afternoon excursion]
