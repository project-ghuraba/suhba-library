Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Thank Allah ﷻ, we have reached the end of the Holy month of Ramadan. Laylatu l-Qadr, the night of power is usually revived on the twenty-seventh. May Allah ﷻ accept the worship of those who perform it on Laylatu l-Qadr insha’Allah. Anyone who performs a Sunnah in Ramadan receives the reward of a Fard, and even more on this night insha’Allah. May Allah ﷻ make us reach many more years.

Ramadan has passed, but it will come. Ramadan will come again. Ramadan will always come, but will we be there? Therefore, we should take it as an opportunity so that the more goodness we do and the more worship we do, the better for us. These opportunities do not always show up. People do not recognize the opportunity, they do not know about it. Opportunities pass and they miss them. They do not realize what precious things are going away. They are not sad now, but they will be sad in the hereafter. They will say, “When there were so many opportunities, I passed away without taking any advantage, without taking a speck.” Whereas, if his eyes were open, he would see how many valuable things Allah Azza wa Jalla gave to people. People are heedless, they don’t know anything. Whether you say or not - they say “No, it doesn’t exist”, but the truth exists. Indeed, we are incapable of being grateful for the blessings and bounties of Allah Azza wa Jalla.

Therefore, thank Allah ﷻ He has made us reach these day. We are doing as much as we can do. And for what we cannot do, our intention to do more. That’s all we can do. Insha’Allah, may Allah ﷻ grant us to do more. May He reward us as if we are doing more insha’Allah. May Allah ﷻ make it blessed for us.

Insha’Allah, for all the recitations of the Qur’an, Tasbihat, Tahlil and verses, may Allah ﷻ accept them all. May Allah ﷻ have mercy on their ancestors and bless them as well insha’Allah. May they be protected, with the intention of strengthening our faith insha’Allah. Amin.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
