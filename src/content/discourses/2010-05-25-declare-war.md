Dastur Ya Sayyidi, Ya Mawla. Allahu Akbar Kabira wa l-hamdu li'Llahi kathira wa Subhana'Llahi bukratan wa asila La ilaha illa Allah La ilaha illa Allah La ilaha illa Allah Sayyidina wa Mawlana Muhammadun Rasulullah Habibullah nur arshillah Allahumma zidhu izzan wa sharafa nuran wa surura, ridwana wa sultana wa man tabi'ahu min ibadika min al awwalin wa l-akhirin Ya Rabb. Assalamu alaykum ya ibadallahi s-salihin Assalamu alayka Ya Sahib al-Waqt Assalamu alaykum Ya Budala, Nujaba, Nuqaba, Aqtab, Akhyar, ma bayna l-mashariq wa l-magharib.

Assalamu alaykum our attenders from mankind and from jinn. Because there are some who are appointed for protecting mankind, from bad sounds from jinns and from shaytans. Therefore from mankind and from jinn, some people are appointed to look after all mankind so that shaytan can't touch bad souls and injure them, because shaytan and its armies have arrows. The arrows are poisoned, not ordinary ones.

O People, say audhu Billahi min as shaytani r-rajim Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Run to Allah, that means, run to those ones that are representing holy ones through heavens. Must be on this planet. This planet can't be without guardians from jinn and men also. Huuu. O People, Attenders, say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim, this is your protection. When you are saying audhu Billahi min as shaytani r-Rajim say, Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim, it is your protected area. Let shaytan not be able to step on it.

O our attenders, during childhood, when there were days of festival, so many people coming and enjoying with so many things - they are selling, buying, and for children, to make them happy, so many (amusements), like horses for riding on and going around, such things. I was perhaps 9 or 10 years old, I was going also, for Eid. The festival place was nearby, from here up to where that statue is.

I was going and once I met a person sitting, his tarboosh, fez, like this old Ottoman one, was old style, custom. Sitting. I am looking - so many children around that one. I am coming and looking at what they are doing. That person was saying, 'O People, O my attenders, look, I am giving 1/2 piastre and you take 3 piastres'. It was the time of Queen Victoria, silver coins, yes. 'Put here half piastre and you take this 3 piastre coin. Then people coming. Coming so many children and they are putting there 1/2 piastre. Then this one old person. It was like a small box, I am looking, there is water in it. Water in it and silver coin in it. One putting and he was asking to take he was making wrrrng, wrrrng, wrrrng, wrrrng and that boy making like this. Take! Why not taking - wrrrng, wrrrng, wrrrng, take! If you put 1/2 piastre take, making like this, making like that. We are not knowing what was happening, so many children coming and trying. He gave 1/2 piastre, it is for me, this is for you, take it and go away, I will bring another one.'

Like this shaytan is trying. When we are saying: audhu Billahi min as shaytani r-rajim and (shaytan) is asking to approach us and angels - wrrrng, wrrrng, wrrrng, coming and it can't do. Escaping. Shaytan thinking that it is easy, that mankind is just left hiyallah (haphazard). American not knowing English. Haphazard. I know, shaytan thinking that it is easy to touch man. And so many trying and not giving this and taking them away. But if a person saying audhu Billahi min as shaytani r-rajim, just trying to touch and running away.

Therefore say, Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim, don't worry, if asking to touch you, may escape. Shaytan is through east and west, from north to south, full, more than mankind, but they can't do anything if that person is taking his guard. Don't forget to say: Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. That is important. Every time shaytan is trying to touch people or to send its arrows and at the edge of arrows there is poison.

Once I was going to market, I am asking Grandshaykh 'Anything to say O my Master.’ 'Nazim Efendi,' he was saying, 'Yes sir,' 'Salman git, salman gel,' in Turkish language. 'Go, as you are going, safely go and safely come'. ‘What does it mean?' He was asking when I am coming back. 'Nazim Efendi, what happened?' 'As you said, I did,' 'You understand what I meant to say?' ‘As you are saying it, I understood, or is it difficult for understanding what you are saying, go safely and come safely?' 'O Nazim Efendi,' he is saying, 'shaytan is everywhere. If a man is coming out out of his house, 7 shaytans around that one, running to make that one do something so that the Lord of Heavens is going to be unhappy with him.

If for a man, seven, what about for women? Seventy shaytans waiting for them to go out and arrows on them. Therefore when they are going out and coming back half of their minds have just been left through the market and half a mind is coming inside. And the Lord completing their minds until they go out. That means don't leave shaytan to throw its arrows on you. Some arrows are ordinary arrows, some of them poisoned. Those poisoned arrows making a person to be ill. And the illness may be through their physical being or may be through their hearts, or may be through their minds, or may be through their understanding. That is dunya.

Therefore, for guarding people Allah Almighty ordering, say: audhu billahi min as shaytani r-rajim and then say: Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim, you will be protected. And so many poisoned arrows filling everywhere. Now, full, 100 % full with poisoned arrows. People are thinking their illnesses are only from their physical beings and some wrong things through their physical being, no. It is something, poisoned arrows touching people and making them ill. And, no medicine can stop it and no doctor may treat them. Doctors finally are saying, 'We must look what is happening up to tomorrow, if up to tomorrow that patient is okay, means we have a hope he may come to be healthy.'

Yes, therefore O People, our attenders beware! You are writing 'Beware of Dog'. Leave writing 'Beware of Dog', write 'Beware of shaytan'. I am saying first to Salaf us-Saleh ulama and second, to Azhar Sharif ulama. And then to doctors of shariat I am saying, are you going to say 'People, beware from anything?' They will say, 'What do you mean to say?' If you are not understanding, how are you saying 'I am doctor of shariah'? How are you claiming you are doctors, hmm? La hawla wa la quwwata illa billahi l-aliyyi l-azim.

Allah ordering His servants that you must have someone who is warning you against the most dangerous things. And the most dangerous thing for mankind physically and spiritually, the main reason is shaytan, to follow shaytan. That is most difficult and most dangerous thing for mankind. Nothing else. Therefore, Allah Almighty saying, a`alanu l-harb. Yes, Allah Almighty ordering through His Holy Book, Quran-i Karim that you must declare harb, war between you and your dangerous enemy, a`alanu l-harb. Allah Almighty is ordering the nation of Sayyidina Muhammad, peace be upon him.

Standing up is good sometimes because most people going to sleep. When standing up, sleep running away. Very good. Therefore I am making wasiyah, advice to Salafi ulama, scholars, and doctors of shariah, time after time you must stand up, so that people may (ask) 'What's happening?' Happening something by standing up? Yes. Yes.

yā ayyuha alladhīna āmanu ittaqu allāha wakūnū ma alssadiqīna (9:119)

We are standing up, yet I am not understanding. We may consider what doctors of shariah are saying, if he is standing up, we are standing up also'. Mostly this is good style for addressing people, particularly for khutabah, preachers, because they are making such long bayan (sermons). Long and long and people are sleeping even, they are dreaming also.

'O my brother, one snake just appeared asking to get out, therefore our speaker warning us to look out for it.' 'Where is it?' 'Just passed, you must take much more care.' 'Hanh'. Awakening and listening to them. Allah Almighty saying 'O My servants, keep My Orders with your most high respect. I am the Creator and you must try to be as I like. If you are not going to be as I like I am sending you away, throwing you out.'

Beware O ulama! You must declare to people to be careful not to be thrown away from Divine Presence. If anyone thrown away from Divine Presence, finished. That is important - to be with your Lord Allah Almighty Subhanahu wa Ta`ala. Some people, perhaps our attenders, so many of them sleeping, therefore I'm making them stand up. “O my brother, I was just dreaming and Shaykh saying ‘stand up’, I lost what is coming after this’ ”. 'Better.'

O People, give your high respect and take high respect from heavens. Don't run after such imitated respects on Earth. That is nonsense. Try to take real respect, heavenly respect, so that when the Lord of Heavens, the Creator, when He is going to be happy with you, granting to you a respect from heavenly ones. Try to take that respect, not ask respect from common people. No, no value. Respect from one to another one in this life gives nothing to you. No. But if you are giving your high respect for the Lord of Heavens, you will take respect, real respect. Allah Allah, Subhan Allah, Sultan Allah.

It is such a thing, people are not interested in it because they are not taught or have not been taught such things. Holy Quran is giving life to people, their spirituality and their physical being. Those who follow heavenly t`alimat never saying, 'O my head, O my stomach O my feet'. Good life, good end. Good life through this world and excellent life, next life, eternal life. O People! Try to prepare yourself for excellent life, most sweet, most joyful, pleased and satisfied life. Ask for that life and try to reach it before you leave this dirty life.

Our physical being always becoming dirty. Outside dirtiness and also inside. Beware not to become dirty. At-tahhir thiyabak, what does it mean, O Salafi ulama?

wa thiyabaka fatahhir (74:4)

Thiyabak - you are understanding what thiyab is? That is we have a dress on our spiritual being, which the physical being is covering and those are our clothes. Keep that physical being clean. Keep it, you will be happy here and hereafter. In the Hereafter it is going to be taken away because you are going to be dressed in another dress that belongs to eternity. Don't waste your time through this very short life but through that short time try to reach eternal life with clean clothes that you will be dressed in. If you are not cleaning your dress, you will be cleaned when you are dying, when you are going to be in the graveyard, when you are going to be, the Day of Resurrection, if not enough, when you are going on the bridge of hells. If not enough, falling down to be cleaned. It is so, so simple to clean ourselves through this life. Not to leave it for eternal life.

May Allah forgive us. O People, try to be clean ones. What is cleanliness, O our ulama? What you are understanding? Tathir - to be clean, what is its meaning? You are saying we are doctors say! What is cleanliness of ourselves? We are not making our souls one hand and our material being on other hand. But both of them you must mutl`a, observe. What does it mean, tathir, tahhir? What is that? Washing with so many things? No. Wa thiyabaka fa tahhir, make your material being so clean so that you are not going to be taken away.

You may be in a clean working place. Clean ones are going to clean place and dirty ones going to a dirty place. Keep your way. Dunya making people to become dirty. Try to make it down, don't make your last target to reach huge numbers of money, or to have treasures. They are not giving you any cleanliness. What is through your hands, throw it away and come to Allah Almighty. In His Divine Presence He may grant to you that which no one can think about, no one has seen, or none among people may understand.

That opening will come on the Day of Resurrection, yawmu l-qiyamah, yawma tashshaqqaqu l-ardi, yes. All heavens opening and Earth just opened also and giving up what is in it. That means you, yourself. Everyone has a special world. Your dunya belongs to you, his highness' dunya is something else, his dunya something else. Some music making you dirty, some music that belongs to heavens, giving to you cleanliness and mussarrat, happiness and enjoyment.
