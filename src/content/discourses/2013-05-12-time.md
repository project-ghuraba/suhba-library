Allahumma salli wa sallim ala Nabina Muhammad alayhi salam Salatan tadumu wa tughda ilayh Mamarra layali wa tul ad dawam Allahumma salli wa sallim ala Nabina Muhammad alayhi salam Salatan tadumu wa tughda ilayh Mammarra layali wa tul ad dawam Allahumma salii wa sallim ala Nabina Muhammad alayhi salam Salatan tadumu wa tughda ilayh Mammarra layali wa tul ad dawam

Masha Allah welcome, welcome to Haqqani. Welcome, welcome to Rabbani. Welcome, welcome to the beauty of the soul of Islam. Masha Allah, if you are coming here, you must come for something very delicious. If you are coming here you are coming for something that should be worth it. If you are coming here, you have to know for what you are coming. Important because, there is replacement for everything, except for time there is no replacement. Every minute that is taken from you it will be taking from your life. So Allah Almighty makes time the most precious thing in our life. Because when we are born the countdown will start. You have 100 years, now you have 99 years, 99.9999. The second minute you have 99.9998. Then.9997, then will be 90, then will be 80, then it will be 20, then it will be 10, then it will be 1, and then it will finish. No replacement.

Maybe Allah Almighty will give you 1000 years also. Will go, will finish. Sometimes you are losing money then something else replaces your money. Sometime you are losing ‘I don't know what’, you will have replacement but time is never replaced. That's why I am saying to all of you, including myself, Alhamdulillah Allah Almighty put us in such a place we are dealing with our time in the right way. To deal with your time in the right way is very important. So many people think that to deal with their time is to build up their future. There is no future. If there's an end, that means there is no future. So many people say, ok, we build up for our children. You never know what time limit Allah Almighty gives your children. Nobody knows.

This is also another thing that we have to think about. We have to think how? How we can be able to put the time that Allah Almighty gave us in good place, important place, in valuable place. That's why so many people when they pray, say, 'this prayer will never finish'. Normal timing you know is only few minutes, but when you are praying you feel it is like hours and hours and never finishes. Then you understand this is the valuable one. They call it 'time inside the time'. Allah Almighty makes the precious time inside the time. The time is passing but when it is precious, it comes slowly, slowly, slowly, slowly. That's why your ego will be very bored and say 'Why we are praying? Why to pray? Why to be good person? Why to be with Allah Almighty? Why to be with Shaykh? We have other places to spend our time, not only to be with Allah Almighty, to be here. Boring talking. Thinking, when this man will finish his talking and we'll be free. Why he is keeping us here so long? It is only 10 minutes yahu, no. I cannot speak more than 10 minutes even. Still too much. Maybe if we watch TV it will be better, very quick. Not boring. This is shaytan. All the time fighting against Allah Almighty's time. Actually he fights against you, he fights to not make you get this time to be valuable, he wants to waste your time.

So many people say, 'Why you waste my time? I wait for you to come 11 o'clock, you come 12 o'clock, Why you waste my time?' Very angry. We are never angry at shaytan to say, 'Ay shaytan why you are wasting our time, not to let us be with Allah Almighty?' Aw, very, very relaxed with shaytan. Easy going. But if somebody tell you something, 'Oh, why you waste my time? You make me work like this and then you say, 'I don't like it'. But Allah Almighty like it, whatever you do for Him, He likes it. He love it and He say to His angel, 'O My Angel look for My servant, how he is doing for me?' He is proud. And imagine, one of the governors in the world, king or queen or president or maybe minister should say to all his people 'Hey look for this man, I am proud of him'. How he feels, that man, how he would act?

And Allah Almighty is the Absolute Governor for everything. He says to his angel, 'Look at My servant, he does for Me, I am proud of him'. Very simple but very important. That's why anything for Allah Almighty is precious. Anything for Allah Almighty makes you higher and higher and higher. And it makes you be in Divine Presence of Allah Almighty one of the stars. Anybody want to be star. They say ‘He is rock star’. All the children run after him, rock star, why he sing? People happy, ‘He looked at us’. He is nothing, he's rubbish. But they say he is rock star. Yahu, to be a rock star is not a big thing. Maybe, anyone from this crowd could be more rock star than this rock star. But everything is coming upside down.

Insha Allah we will be rock star for Allah Almighty's eyes. Because this is the real star as when Prophet (sas) says about the Sahaba, all my Sahaba are stars. Whichever one you look at and follow you will find a way because they are very sincere to Allah Almighty, to Prophet (sas). Very sincere with what they begin and these are the people who have used the time right that Allah Almighty gave them. Maybe they have short life, long life, whatever life, doesn't matter. But they fulfilled their time. And insha Allah we will be the people that will fulfil our time to Allah Almighty.

Our intention is to be like this. We want to be like this. If you say Ya Rabbi, my time is for You, then you will be with your intention in this role that you fulfilled your time to Allah Almighty.

wa min Allahi tawfiq bi hurmat al Habib bi hurmat al Fatiha. Bismillahir Rahmanir Rahim.

If we are not a teacher then we don't know anything. And that is why our time here is important. Everything can come back, except time. If Allah (swt) gives someone a life of 100 years, after a minute it will be 99 years, 99.99. It's continuously decreasing. One year will finish and it will be 99 years, then 88 years, 20, then 1 year, then finish. It can't come back, whatever the time is, that's why who uses this time for Allah (swt) he is using his time most wisely.

So, why did you say - Ya Rabbi, we will use this time for your sake, for Your love, for the love of Prophet (sws). We have set aside this time for the love of our Sheikhs.
