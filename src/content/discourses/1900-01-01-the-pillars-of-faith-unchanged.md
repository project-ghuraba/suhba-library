In our times people are getting to be more positive, science, positive knowledge. They are asking everything to compare with mentalities, with mind, to make everything to be understood by mind. Therefore, even spiritual or religious beliefs they are asking to bring in life through mindly exercises. And therefore, Allāh Almighty He sent His last message and basing it on mind so that out of beliefs. And for beliefs also you may find a limit that you can understand, by your mind.

And for others, or after beliefs, Islām brought beliefs, but beliefs they are not new beliefs. Perhaps it is from the beginning as declared first Prophet, the last Prophet just mentioning, declaring and saying same pillars of beliefs. Six pillars, six bases of beliefs. It is never changing from the time of Ādam. He was saying: you must believe in God Almighty Allāh, He is eternal God; and unity of God; and to believe in angels, to believe in holy books, holy commands, heavenly commands; and to believe in Prophets, first Prophet, last Prophet and whole Prophets; and to believe in last day, Judgement Day; to believe in eternal life in paradise, or in hells; and to believe that everything in universe is not accidently, but prepared and arranged, everything must appear in its time,punctuality.
