As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

"Wa-qihimu s-sayyi’āti wa-man taqi s-sayyi’āti yawma’idhin fa-qad raḥimtah" (40:9), says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla in Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n. Protect us from sins. When we are protected from sins, we are covered in the mercy of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. We don't commit sins – or rather, we don't increase our sins then. Because people are sinners. AllāhNone created them to commit sins. But some of them think they were created only to commit sins. They try to do all kinds of evil and sins. Their egos want that. So they run after whatever their egos want.

There are many people who run after sins without getting tired. This is the majority. Not a minority, but a majority of people are like this. This is not good. This is bad for people. Therefore, people commit sins. They repent and ask forgiveness. When they know their sins, AllāhNone forgives and erases those sins. But if people persist in sinning, they should not think they are benefitting from it. It brings nothing but harm. "I don't pray. I don't know AllāhNone. I don't love the ProphetNone. I drink alcohol. I run after women and commit adultery. I do all kinds of wrong things. I am taking everything from life." They shouldn't think so. There is no benefit to themselves and no benefit to others. It is nothing but sin.

For every sin someone commits, a burden falls on him and darkness covers him. He alienates himself from others. And even if people seem to like him, they actually hate him. And he will find all kinds of troubles in Ākhirah as well. He will regret what he has done there, but it will be too late. Therefore, through the mercy of AllāhNone those who don't commit sins, as well as those who do but repent are protected by AllāhNone. This is a great favour. To prefer and to imitate the sinners is not a clever thing. Ego makes people, believers and Muslims, see these things as something important. Their egos are drawn to that way and wish they could do those things as well. When they can't, it is from the protection of AllāhNone. You should make shukr to AllāhNone that you have not run after those ways and didn't become one of them. It is a great favour.

There was one brother who is gone now. He used to say, "If we try to steal something, the sun will rise in the middle of the night." It means some obstacle will occur. That obstacle is a good thing. When you want to do something bad, something will prevent you. When you don't do it, you are saved. That is the mercy of AllāhNone. May AllāhNone protect us. May AllāhNone keep all of us from sins. Sinning is the biggest harm. The biggest evil people can cause themselves is to commit sins. We certainly do, but may we repent and ask forgiveness, in shā’a Llāh.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying in Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n, "Wa-qihimu s-sayyi’āti wa-man taqi s-sayyi’āti yawma’idhin fa-qad raḥimtah" (40:9). O, our LordNone, save us from to do bad thing, to do sin. Who YouNone save him from this, YouNone be mercy for him. This biggest mercy for us, for AllāhNone to save us, to not make us to do wrong. Wrong, you can do; but to make sin, to make bad thing.

This is in Qur’ān, some verses – it's very important. Because people – most of people, they are doing – they living for to do sin; to do wrong – bad; to follow what they like. The ego, what they like? Ego like everything bad. Ego like, maybe to drink, to steal, to to every bad thing. People they – most of people, they are doing drinking, going for making bad thing. Every bad thing, most of people they are doing this. We are – we must be happy we cannot do this. Something prevent us. Even you go to do, and it is something happen, you cannot go to that place to do this. Maybe you are: "Why this happen?" But it is happen from AllāhNone, HeNone prevent you, protect you to do bad thing.

To do bad thing, it is first – thing, not benefit for you. Maybe people, whole life they are running after this thing to do, to collect, like horse – not tiring, not fed up. All time running, running to do this without any complaining. But it is – they running for bad thing for themself, not to enjoy themself. Every time they thought they enjoy themself, they coming on them more punishment, more heaviness, more darkness. And maybe they thought people they like them, but people also they hate them. Because when Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla not love someone, HeNone order everything to not love him. You can have billions but you cannot – nobody love you. You – all people hate you. This is not benefit. And in dunyā this – in Ākhirah it's worse. It will be very bad for them.

Because this who had protect and prevent to do this thing, it is mercy from AllāhNone; and he is – he must be happy he couldn't do this. Because sometimes, as we said, our ego they like to do something it is not allowed. And when something prevent them, they become angry: "Why this happen?" Eh, this happen from AllāhNone. AllāhNone prevent – protect you. You must be thankful for AllāhNone you are not doing bad thing. Don't think who doing bad thing and they are happy in this life – no. They are not happy at all, and they will not be happy until they are asking for forgiveness from AllāhNone. And AllāhNone, HeNone is opening HisNone forgiveness door. It is opening. If they ask for forgiveness, they can have. And they be, become happy. If not, they will be no happiness here and no happiness in next life. AllāhNone protect us. And we are thanking AllāhNone to protect – prevent this thing from away – to be away from us, in shā’a Llāh.
