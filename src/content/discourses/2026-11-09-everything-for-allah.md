As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Shukr to Allah always. May His blessings upon us be everlasting, inshā’Allāh. The greatest blessing is the blessing of īmān. May Allah grant īmān to everyone. As we have said, we are grateful to Allah. This was a long journey; shukr to Allah, we went and came back safely.

The brothers there were able to see us. This travel had been quite long this time, as it had been two years since we last visited them. They were in great need of this; they had missed us, and we had missed them. May Allah be pleased with them—they were so hospitable. They showed us every form of affection and generosity, doing it all solely for the sake of Allah. May Allah accept all our deeds—sincerely performed for His sake, inshā’Allāh.

One must do everything for the sake of Allah. You should give for the sake of Allah, receive for the sake of Allah, and walk for the sake of Allah... In every endeavor, the intention must be to seek Allah’s pleasure, so that living in this world may have meaning and purpose.

If one travels merely for the sake of wandering, the journey remains nothing more than a trip of wandering. Yet, it is possible to travel while simultaneously serving others for the sake of Allah. Many people embark on such journeys for the sake of Allah, setting out to help others; their visits are truly accepted.

On the other hand, you see those who live solely for worldly pursuits and the desires of their own egos—they wander about to no avail. Moreover, if they commit sins, this becomes an even worse punishment for them. Even if they do nothing else, they have simply squandered their lives.

Therefore, ensure that everything you do is for the sake of Allah; as long as it is within the bounds of what is ḥalāl, you may engage in any pursuit. As we have said, we must live every step, every minute, and every second for the sake of Allah, inshā’Allāh.

May Allah accept it. May Allah also be pleased with our brothers there; they were very hospitable and truly delighted. May Allah bring joy to us all, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
