Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Faith is believing in Allah and believing in our Holy Prophet. Religion is the religion of Allah Azza wa Jalla. The religions in the heavenly books are the religions that Allah conveyed to people through His prophets. Religion is one. ‘َّم لَ َّسإ لِْ إاََِّّاللّ ََّّد نعِ ََّّن يد ِ لاََّّن ِإ’, ‘Indeed, the religion in the sight of Allah is Islam.’ (Qur’ān 03:19).

All belong to Islam. Religions other than the heavenly ones are not considered religions. They call it philosophy, not religion. But they still accept it as a religion, worship and do unnecessary things. They are trying in vain. All of them, even their ancestors have not found the way. But the issue now is that they want to lead Muslims astray, without stopping. This enmity continues. Why does it continue? What is the benefit of it? It is the enmity of shaytan. The shaytan has deceived them. They are the associates of shaytan. They are the followers of shaytan.

Right and wrong are in constant war. Evil and good are in constant war. They are the evil. Goodness is in Islam. That is why they tirelessly attack in order to destroy Islam. They make a lot of excuses, but they are all lies and unfounded things. They even do it themselves, throwing it at Muslims. As if it was not enough for the Muslims; they say, “Just believe in Allah, do not believe in anything else”. This is not religion.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
