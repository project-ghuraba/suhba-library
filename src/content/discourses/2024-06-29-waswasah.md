As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Min sharri l-waswāsi l-khannās” (114:4). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l- ‘aẓīm. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says, “We seek refuge in AllāhNone from what causes waswasah (whispering): shayṭāns and jinn.” So waswasah is the shayṭān’s job. What does waswasah mean? You are doing a deed, any deed. The most important of them is worship. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla has made it easy for you. “Make it easy,” heNone says. Everyone can do it. This is shayṭān’s trick, he makes waswasah for him showing what he did as supposedly not accepted. He falls into distress. He falls into sadness. He destroys himself. Then, he won’t gain anything. Because waswasah is from shayṭān. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla told everyone what they could do. “Yassirū wa-lā tu‘assirū,” heNone says. “Yassirū wa-lā tu‘assirū. – Make it easy, don’t make it difficult." If you make it difficult, people will make it to a limit. After that, they won’t perform the worship they did because they think what they have done is not accepted. They give up on it. Then, what shayṭān wants happens.

That’s why, some of these groups of shayṭān – we will call them as such from now on – are operating under the guise of Muslims. Some turn people’s lives upside down saying, “We are doing them a favor.” They ruin them. They have neither friends nor relatives left. They all become enemies to each other. Why is that? It is because of things that do not exist in Islām. They present these things as if they exist in Islām, to those who are sincere Muslims. This confuses them, turning them against others, and making them their own enemy. That person will be ruined. This waswasah is not good at all.

As we said, some people have waswasah disease. They make it worse. They don’t like Muslims. They do not like what Muslims do. However, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla accepts everything. Ḥāshā, they put themselves in the place of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla and make judgments. Those who follow them will definitely suffer from those bad things. They fall into bad things. Their life is also ruined. As we said, they cause enmity among Muslims. May AllāhNone protect us from their evil. Shayṭān has many evils and many tricks. He won’t come to you clearly like an unbeliever and tell you. He will tell you what seems as a good deed, but inside that good deed is poison. He kills you with that poison. Your whole life will be ruined. You gain nothing from the harm you inflict. On the contrary, it becomes a burden on you. The time you spend, the effort you make, the water you use – all of it is written in the Presence of AllāhNone. They are written as sins. If you perform regular ablution, perform ghusl, and engage in worship, AllāhNone will reward you for every drop of it. Here, for every drop, it becomes a sin because you wasted water.

May AllāhNone protect us. May AllāhNone protect us from the evil of these people. Religion is easiness. The religion of Islām is ease. Don’t listen to anyone who says it is difficult or this and that. Do as much as you can. Perform as much as you can. If they say they don’t like it, say to them that you don’t like it either. If they tell you, “Your prayer is not accepted. I didn't like it. I didn't like it either,” You should say, “May AllāhNone like it.” AllāhNone likes it. AllāhNone does not look at small, insignificant things. AllāhNone gives according to a person’s intention. According to that intention, we consider the goodness of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. People troubled by waswasah think, “AllāhNone will be angry with us. AllāhNone will punish us. AllāhNone will not accept the deeds we have done, so we must do them again.”

Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu 'alayhi wa-sallam says, “How a person thinks about Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, what he thinks will be.” We think that AllāhNone will forgive us. HeNone accepts our deeds that we have done with all their flaws. Therefore, there is no need to worry. There is no need to follow the path that shayṭān wants. May AllāhNone protect us all.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla say in Qur’ān ‘Aẓīm, “Min sharri l-waswāsi l-khannās” (114:4). Who is this to whispering for people and make them to be confused? This is shayṭān. Shayṭān but he is hiding after many thing he not appear many time he without hiding appear many time, every time. But for believer, for mu’min, he must be hide himself to make people to catch them, to hunt them. He must be wait in some place, which it is people that it is safe and they going there and suddenly they be hunted. What is this?

There is many group of Muslim. Most of them I think they are with shayṭān. Even you see them praying five time, going ḥajj, doing this, doing that. But normally, they make people to confuse, and to not knowing what they are doing. They doing everything different from other. They making wuḍū’, maybe in one, to make three times one, they make twenty times. “It is good, not good. Good, not good.” When they are praying also, “You must say like this, you must be pronounce this word like this, you must do this.” And after, this people, they become very confused and try to follow this. Forget to ask happiness of AllāhNone, pleasure of AllāhNone. Only they are thinking what they are doing. If it is good or not good, not looking for sunnah, not looking for anything else. Only to listening to this people who are real confusing people and putting enemies between people, between Muslim. Because they said, “You are, you don't know how to pray, how to worship.” And people they become enemy for each other – family, husband for wife, mother for daughter, for son or other. All this becoming whole, this in family but outside also. They are making people to be difficult life.

But Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla and Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam heNone is saying, “Yassirū wa-lā tu‘assirū – Make it easy, don't make it difficult.” We will] listen for you or listen for AllāhNone and Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. You make it difficult; you make it impossible for whole people to do this. Especially doing this to make people run away from Islām, from worshipping. “I cannot do this so I will leave. What happened, happened,” they said after this. But if you with good intention and say for people, “Can you do what – can you do and AllāhNone, HeNone is not looking for our worshipping, not. HeNone is the Greatest. Just you pray intention good, you will be reward, in shā’a Llāh.” This is what Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla what you think about HimNone, HeNone is like this. If you think HeNone is, HeNone will be mercy for you and HeNone forgive you for everything what you done, AllāhNone will not be make you make not true, what you think. What you think, AllāhNone will be this. If you think AllāhNone HeNone will punish you and HeNone will not accept your ‘ibādah, also because you are thinking bad for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla HeNone may give you this. But if you think good, we are weak servants and we can do little bit. It is whole what we done.

We also not happy with what we are doing from ‘ibādah, from praying, fasting or – this is not complete, only as much we can do this, we can do. Many people sometimes in mosque also – you saying, “What you are doing? How is your praying? You praying this.” Many time we ask them, “You are not happy with this? No. Also I am not happy with this but I think AllāhNone, HeNone is happy with this.” This important not you, not me. AllāhNone important, in shā’a Llāh.

Our thinking for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone will be forgive us and put us in HisNone Paradise. Never going to jahannam, in shā’a Llāh. Who think he goes to jahannam – we are not. He free with his thinking. But, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are thinking this – good thing for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. HeNone is Greatest, HeNone is Generous. HeNone is forgive everything. Ghaffār, Ghaffāru dh-Dhunūb. We are happy with this, al-ḥamdu liLlāh. AllāhNone make us whole to be in Jannah (Paradise) altogether, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
