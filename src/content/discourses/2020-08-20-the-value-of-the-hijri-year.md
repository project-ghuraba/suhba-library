Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Thank Allah, Allah Almighty created us as Muslims and made us one of those who recognize this new year. This is a favor of Allah, it is not meant for everyone. Most people follow the solar year. They follow the Gregorian year which they made according to the birth of Sayyidina Isa. Of course, it works for their daily lives, but this year is important for the Islamic life and worship.

This year is a blessed year. Its months and days are made for the sake of Allah. So whatever worship or anything similar is done, it’s for the sake of Allah. Therefore, to honor this year is a favor of Allah to people. It is a great favor for those who know it. When the other year comes, people seem to be happy at first, then they realize they are one year older. They have aged, but most people grow old not by worship, but by evil. And they enter the new year with drinking and drunkenness.

This year, the Hijri year is more beautiful. It cannot be compared because we enter it with worship and Dua for it to be a good year. Then there is no such thing as being a year older. Although we are getting old, we are getting closer to Allah. Since the year is spent in worship, there is no sadness. On the contrary, people will be happy insha’Allah because another year has passed with worship and obedience, and it is reserved for the hereafter. Allah does not waste our worship. May Allah forgive us if we have made any mistakes or sins.

This year is also beautiful. Everything is beautiful in Islam. Allah Azza wa Jalla has made a perfect order for people. Whoever follows Islam receives all the beauties. Whoever does not follow it, or the less he follows, the more trouble he has. The more he follows Islam, believes in Allah and believes in our Holy Prophet, the more happiness he will live both in this world and the hereafter. May Allah make it blessed. May this year be a good one for all Muslims insha’Allah.

May Mahdi ‘alayhi s-salām come too. We must always wait for Mahdi ‘alayhi s-salām. We are always saying: “He will come now. He will come now.” When Allah wills it, he can come any time. There are signs to expect, but as our Holy Prophet said: “There are big signs of the coming of Isa ‘alayhi s-salām and Mahdi ‘alayhi s-salām that must happen before the Day of Judgement.” They will certainly happen. As our Holy Prophet said, all of them will definitely happen even if it is in one day. Allah will certainly make them happen. He can come in Muharram or in Rajab. There are signs, but that is also known in the sight of Allah; it is a thing that He knows. He comes when Allah wants.

Therefore, our constant Dua is to reach him, and to reach those beautiful days insha’Allah. May Allah bless this year for Muslims and protect them from this disease. We seek refuge in Allah from it. And we seek refuge in Allah from the evil of our ego. May it be means for goodness insha’Allah. May we be on the right way. May Allah guide the Ummah of Muhammad as well insha’Allah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
