As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
وَمَنْ يَتَوَكَّلْ عَلَى اللَّهِ فَهُوَ حَسْبُهُ إِنَّ اللَّهَ بَالِغُ أَمْرِهِ قَدْ جَعَلَ اللَّهُ لِكُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدْرًا
<br>“Wa-man yatawakkal ʿala Llāhi fa-huwa ḥasbuhū. InnAllāha bālighu amrihi. Qad jaʿala Llāhu li-kulli shay'in qadran.”<br>
“And whosoever puts his trust in Allah, then He will suffice him. Verily, Allah will accomplish his purpose. Indeed Allah has set a measure for all things.” (Qurʼān 65:3). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla teaches people what they should do. The instructions are in Qur’ān ʿAẓīmu sh-Sha’n and the beautiful words of our Holy Prophet. People today, all of them, that is ninety-nine percent; it would rather be accurate to say nine hundred and ninety-nine per thousand, are confused about what to do. It was like this before, but now there is more of that confusion. “What will I do? How shall I act? What will happen? How will I get by? Will I get sick? Will I live?” These all exist in the ego and essence of mankind.

The cure for that is: “And whoever relies upon Allah - then He is sufficient for him,” says Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. By relying on Allah, you will know that what Allah wants will happen. “Inna Allaha bālighu amrihi” (Qur’an 65:03). What Allah wills will happen. Everything has its time, or its happening. Therefore, if a person wants to be comfortable and live without worry, he must rely on Allah. He must submit to the will and destiny of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. Destiny is the secret of Allah. He must surrender to it.

Of course, he must do what he does and what he can do. After that, he should rely on Allah and wish for what Allah wants. Whatever Allah wishes, may it be good. May it be as Allah wants. It is necessary to make du‘ā’ that may what He does not want or like not happen. Because you will both rely upon Him and follow the path that Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla tells you. You should look at everything. You should follow Allah’s work with the specialties that He has given you. That is relying on Allah. It is not: relying upon Him and sitting around doing nothing. You must do. Then, you must be pleased with what happens. You must accept what happens next.

There is a famous hadith of Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam that says, “اعْقِلْ وَتَوَكَّلْ”, “Aʿqil wa Tawakkal”, “Tie it, then rely upon Allah.” One of the companions left the camel [without tying it] in front of Masjid an-Nabawi of our Holy Prophet , saying that he had relied upon Allah. So it was gone. After that, he came to our Holy Prophet and said, “My camel is gone.” “What have you done?” “I left it here because this is a Masjid. I relied upon Allah as I will enter to prayer and listen to the good words. I looked and it has run away,” he said. That is a famous holy hadith. “Aʿqil” means tie it, then rely upon Allah. So there is a principle for everything. After following that principle, inshā’Allah it becomes a peaceful life and your hereafter will be prosperous inshā’Allah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq

Al-Fātiḥah
