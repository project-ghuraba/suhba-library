As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
وَهُوَ الَّذِي يُنَزِّلُ الْغَيْثَ مِن بَعْدِ مَا قَنَطُوا وَيَنشُرُ رَحْمَتَهُ ۚ وَهُوَ الْوَلِيُّ الْحَمِيدُ
<br>“Wa Huwa lladhī yunazzilu l-ghaytha mim baʿdi mā qanaṭū wa yanshuru raḥmatahū wa Huwa l-Waliyyu l-Ḥamīd.”<br>
“And it is He who sends down the rain after they had despaired and spreads His mercy. And He is the Protector, the Praiseworthy.” (Qurʼān 42:28). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

Māshā'Allah, regarding this rainfall—since everything is in the Hand of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla, the rain, and indeed everything else, belongs to Him. All of these are manifestations of the great power of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. Rain. Māshā'Allah, the rain has been pouring down heavily since morning.

If those who do not believe seek a miracle, this is the greatest miracle of all. Vast bodies of water are floating in the air. Whenever and wherever He wills, He sends down mercy from them. He sends down either mercy or wrath. May Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla treat us with His mercy; may these rains be a source of mercy, not a calamity. May He send them down with barakah.

Yet, one must also recognize the power of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla and stand in awe of that power. He does whatever He wills and enacts whatever He desires. Clouds drift about. There are millions of seas in the air; vast waters are floating above. Allah sends the rain down where He commands, “Descend!” and passes over the places where He commands. “Do not send it down!” That very same cloud may pass by without shedding a single drop.

Everything concerning Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla is a miracle. What is termed a “miracle” is the accomplishment of something deemed impossible; yet, within the power of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla there is no such thing as the impossible.

Therefore, let people take heed and give thanks to Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. Let them not lose hope in Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. In the verse,<br>
وَهُوَ الَّذِي يُنَزِّلُ الْغَيْثَ مِن بَعْدِ مَا قَنَطُوا
<br>“Wa Huwa lladhī yunazzilu l-ghaytha mim baʿdi mā qanaṭū” (Qurʼān 42:28).

The word “qanaṭū” signifies the loss of hope. No matter the circumstances, one must never lose hope in Allah.

Regardless of one’s situation, one must have faith, believing that Allah’s help will arrive. A person who loses hope in Allah has weak faith or lacks faith altogether. Indeed, those without faith fear everything, for they have nothing to lean upon. A believer, however, relies on Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. Knowing that everything is in His Hand, the believer takes refuge in His mercy and walks His path. By entering into that mercy, one is protected by Allah.

May Allah protect us all. May these rains bring barakah. May they be beneficial to the entire Ummah, inshā’Allāh. May faith and barakah descend along with this mercy, inshā’Allāh. May Allah be pleased with you all.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
