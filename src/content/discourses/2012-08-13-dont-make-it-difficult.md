As-Salamu Alaikum. Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fil jamia' Our sohbat today is "Yassiru wa la tuassiru".

Our Prophet (sas) is ordering, upon the Order of Allah Almighty: "Make things easy, don't make them difficult". So the religion of Islam is easy. The ones outside of Islam, they are facing difficulties. Now they are saying "we pray 5x a day, we fast, we do this, we do that, how can it be easy?. Of course it is easy because Allah helps us. Allah's help & glance is upon you. Because of this it is the easiest thing. As long as Allah helps, there can be no difficulty. Of course there is ego, there is this & that but still, once man obeys Allah's order, none of them can do anything, with Allah's permission.

Prophet (sas) says "Don't make the religion difficult. Perform your worship as Allah ordered." If you put on yourselves a heavy burden, no matter how much you do, how much you put burden on yourself, saying "I do this much worship, that much" you still can't catch up with it. Do as much as you like, still the religion beats you" says Prophet (sas). So he says, take the middle way. Perform your worship without pushing too much, easily & smoothly. So it means Islam is an easy religion as Prophet (sas) says, as Allah says.

But of course ego & shaytan show it differently. Some people, to lose weight or to be healthy, they workout from the morning till evening, they exercise etc.. They get tired & they get tired in vain. And of course how long can you do it? You can do 1 year, 2 years, 5 years. And you have to stop eventually. Recently we met a lady we haven't seen for a long time. 30 years ago she was skin &bones. She was a gym teacher. We saw her last year, she became such that.. Masha Allah she got 3x fatter than that time. Of course she can't keep it up.

But if you perform the worship Allah ordered- if you pray, if you fast, you can go the same way without getting tired until you die. The other way, as we said, there are very few people who can keep up the workout he did when he was 20 also at 80. For sure he will stop it after 3-4 years, 10 years or 20 years. What he did won't be a benefit for him. On the contrary it will be even harm for his body. Stopping all of a sudden because he is tired, can't do anymore but the body is used to it, so it will come back to him as a harm.

So as we said, as the Prophet (sas) said: "khayr al-umur al-wasat". The best is to go the middle way. Do as much as you can. But of course there are some seasons, you can do more worship in them- the holy nights, Ramadan, etc.. There is no obstacle for this. But if you want to do it all the time, or all year long, then it is difficult for your body. It is not good if you can't keep up with it. Therefore don't make it difficult. Allah forgives, Allah accepts.

At some time 3 men came to our Prophet (sas) & asked him. They heard about the Prophet (sas) & because he is Prophet.. And they also do worship. One of them would fast whole year, one would pray all night, & one didn't marry so that he can do worship only. And that day, they traveled from a far place hearing of Islam, they thought it is also like this. They asked when they arrived, how the Prophet(sas) does. And when they heard, they got surprised, & they had a bad feeling inside.

When the Prophet (sas) heard about them, he said "I sleep, and wake up and pray. I fast and break fast. I marry women. Therefore whoever does as I do he follows my Sunnah. Whoever does not follow my Sunnah is not from me". So he said this Hadith.

Today we speak about Islam, Prophet (sas) and in Quran also saying, "Don't make it difficult, make it easy" Islam, it is easy religion, the most easy religion. Why it is easy? Because Allah helping. Without helping from Allah, nothing can be easy. The easiest thing if Allah not help, you can't do it, it will be the most difficult thing. And Allah (awj), He is happy with who following Islam, helping him. They say "how it is easy?" Difficult for shaytan and ego and other thing, making people to see Islam, it is very difficult. No, it is not like that. But just imagination from shaytan & ego & other bad character from people, making like it is difficult. No, it is because when you do this Allah help you, you are happy.

When you do your worship, you become more happy when you finish this worshipping. When you pray, after praying you feel very happy. When you are fasting, when time of break fast, nobody from nonMuslim they have this happiness. They cannot imagine this happiness. It is signal from Allah to show people how it easy and it is good to be worshipping.

And Allah, when ordering for worshipping He said "don't be very tired, Allah give you certain worshipping. If you do this it is ok, enough for you. But if you do more, it is ok also. But don't put burden on you you can't carry it. Only what you can carry, you can continue with this." Because you cannot win worshipping. As much as you can do worshipping, there is more also you can do. But when not, we are not obligated and Allah (awj), Prophet (sas) saying "do it as much you can do. Don't be very hard on yourself."

There was 3 Muslim, they didn't see Prophet (sas). They come to Madinah. And one of them, he was fasting whole time, other praying whole night, 3rd one not marrying. When coming to Madinah & asking about Prophet & they know he is not doing what they do, little bit their ego, they have something about Prophet. Prophet (sas) he know what they have. He told them "I am sleeping & waking up praying. And fasting sometime, sometime not fasting. And I am marrying also. Who not do this, not marrying, it is from my sunnah he is not from me. Who can marry, he must marry also. Other thing, he must do as they can do." And he said "I am the best of you."

This is also, he said for them. Because he is Prophet (sas). And he do not like what they do. Because if he do like them, all ummah, they must do like him. So he is merciful & it is enough for people to do this.

Because when you do worshipping- you pray, you make wudhu, you wash yourself, you pray, whole life you do this. Until 90, sometimes if somebody live until 100 year, also he can do praying & doing worshipping, same. But who do not for Allah, not doing this. Many people now, it is fashion to make gym. Sometimes some of them doing 1 hour. Some of them we hear, maybe for 4 or 5 hour they do this & it is very hard. When they do this, maybe they can do 1 year, some of them, more patient they can do 5 year, some of them become like craziness 20 year, 30 year. But after it is so difficult to find people continue until 70, 80 year, it is very rare. So when they do this & stopping, this is giving harm, harming themselves.

But when you do worshipping, every time you go making worshipping you be more happy. When you finish praying, after praying you feel yourself happiness. Specially when you are fasting & break your fast, this happiness nobody can find who is not fasting from from nonMuslim. Even there is many Muslim also they are not fasting this days. This happiness, they cannot find at all in this world. Allah giving this happiness for Muslim to show them they are in right way. Other, when they doing worshipping, they cannot find anything, just empty. No spirit from..

Alhamdulillah we are Muslim & everything Allah make it easy for us. Don't make hard for yourself. What you can do, do. But don't leave your worshipping first of all. This is very important to pray, to fast & to make.. if you have, you can go to Hajj. You can do, if you have money, must pay zakat. This is the most important. After this, what you can do, you can do.

Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, Al Fatiha.
