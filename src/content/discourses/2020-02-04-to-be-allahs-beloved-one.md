As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh. A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Inna walīyiya Llāhu wa-huwa yatawallá ṣ-ṣāliḥīn" (7:196). It is an āyah. It shows how nice it is to be a friend of AllāhNone. The biggest Friend is AllāhNone. If you are Allāh'sNone friend, you are among the winners, among the pious ones, among the good ones. It is a big favour. To be Allāh'sNone friend is not given to everyone. Because people do not know their friends. However, AllāhNone is the best of Friends. "INone am the best Friend," says AllāhNone. "No one can be better than MeNone." Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is saying this to everyone. And people are asking neither for HisNone friendship, nor for the friendship of the ProphetNone; neither for that of the Awliyā', nor of the believers. Wherever there is a useless one, they want to be his friend. And then they look for peace and happiness. Of course, they cannot find it.

Allāh'sNone Mercy is great. All beauties are at Allāh's, ‘Azza wa-Jalla, door, in HisNone presence, all kinds of goodness and blessings. Who is HisNone friend wins in dunyā too. And the main thing is friendship in Ākhirah. To be with HimNone every moment is a great gain for people, to be together with AllāhNone. You should be with AllāhNone. How can you do this? AllāhNone should be in your heart. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says, "Neither heaven nor earth can contain MeNone but the heart of a believer can." AllāhNone is near to you, near to everyone. But you should recognise that nearness. You should appreciate it. Then you will be HisNone friend. May AllāhNone let all of us reach this level. The highest level is to be a friend of AllāhNone.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying in Qur’ān: "Inna walīyiya Llāhu wa-huwa yatawallá ṣ-ṣāliḥīn" (7:196). My Beloved One is Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla and HeNone is love good people, ṣāliḥ people. HeNone is with them. This is the biggest, highest rank for human being, to be Allāh'sNone beloved one, friend. "Dost" for AllāhNone, to be this it is our aim. Who has this, he is the most happy one, the most benefit one. AllāhNone His door open for everybody. "Come be beloved one for MeNone," HeNone is saying. But nobody, very few people they are listening for this. They are not listening. They thought only few people they can be beloved one for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla whole universe can be beloved for HimNone. But they are, no any, HeNone will be no less, no more. AllāhNone can, nothing you cannot say, "HeNone cannot do any of this." Every good thing, everything AllāhNone able to do. For this to be, you must be, try to be HisNone beloved one. This is the most benefit for human being, for everybody. Must be with HimNone. How we can be with HimNone? Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says in Ḥadīth Qudsī, "HeNone is greatest. No heaven, no sky, no earth can host MeNone. Only INone am in MyNone beloved, believer servant's heart." HeNone can be in heart but other thing cannot be. We cannot imagine but Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying. This is meaning, your heart it must be full with HisNone love, remembering HimNone whole time, you will be beloved one for HimNone. It is the most thing we must look for, because people in dunyā they are not looking for this highest love. They are looking for nothing, for other thing. But for mu’min, can be in his heart and to be beloved one for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, not difficult.

All time remember AllāhNone, HeNone is in your heart. Help, HeNone is help you, giving you all happiness. The happiness to be with AllāhNone. If you are away from AllāhNone, you're not happy. But if HeNone is in your heart all time, you'll be happy, you be satisfied. You know AllāhNone with you, nothing can harm you, nothing can make any bad thing for you. All this thing in the world not important, all this. Only for Ākhirah. So when you are in dunya and put your... Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla in your heart, you'll be reach the highest station, in shā’a Llāh, in Ākhirah. This our aim for all people. For poor or for rich, everybody must be this they be happy, no different. AllāhNone create everybody same. Everybody he can reach the highest station, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone help us to be all time with HimNone, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
