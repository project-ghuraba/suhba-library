Waṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-F’āiz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-

Allah ‘azza wa jalla says that when you do good, it is good for you. These deeds that are done have benefits here and in Next World. The doer is protected here, and in Next World he receives a reward and his station is raised. Now around the world there is this situation, and [to imagine] such a state would not have come into people’s minds. Allah 'azza wa jalla has given this situation. There happened to be a lot of nations, countries, that had reached the summits of oppression. This is not a reward, but a punishment.

But thanks be to Allah that our country has done good deeds, has taken care of people and has helped them around the world. Now wherever they are, they are mentioned as being good. Because they are mentioned as good, the reward of this insha’Allah, will be that this curse will pass by this place [Turkey] lightly or without any effect. This is in the hands of Allah ‘azza wa jalla, in His seven powers. Allah causes its adjustment as He wishes. It might be light or strong. That which happens strong is against non-believers and oppressors. Insha’Allah, we will not become oppressors. Our forefathers also were merciful. They always followed the path of the holy Prophet (SAW). They were on the right path, and that is the beautiful path. They were merciful towards all, and helpful. Today, insha’Allah, this service continues.

These kinds of trials will pass without any effect in this country, insha’Allah, so that it will be a lesson for others to learn from. With the permission of Allah, there is no need to be afraid or to panic. Charity is important; helping is important. Our government is doing this and our people, too. Non-believers are merciless. They are hypocrites (munāfiq), who do all kinds of things, but do not accept [even] a little wrong from others. But Allah is there, and He knows! He is with the innocent.

May Allah help us. May this go by lightly and with no effect, insha’Allah, for all of the Muslim world. This [country, i.e, Turkey] is the head of Islam, exemplary with its help to the world. Insha’Allah, we will pass through this situation in haqq (truth, reality, rightfulness) as this event is from Al-Haqq (The True, The Real). May this curse go away, insha’Allah. May Allah be our helper. May He protect us all. Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-F ti ah
