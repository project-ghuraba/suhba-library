Shaykh Muhammad Nazim Al-Haqqani An-Naqshibandi, Sohbat of the 23rd of January, 2014.

How are you? Thanks to Allah Almighty. May Allah not deprive us of what we see. Let's say - Bismillahi-r Rahmani r-Rahim. Don't be afraid. Those who say "Bismillahi-r Rahmani r-Rahim" are not afraid. Those who say, "Allah", achieve all their desires. Allah. Allah. Those who say, "Allah", reach all desires.

Ya Rabbi, we are weak. We couldn't walk toward Your servanthood. We couldn't keep the sunnah/traditions of Your Beloved Habib. I am living by the blessings of Ummuhan Sultan. May she give us strength. If they don't give us strength, then we are useless. Allahu Akbar Allahu Akbar Allahu Akbar wa Lillahi l-hamd. We have become weak servants. Ya Rabbi, give us from the blessings of Basmala Sharifa that are coming from heavens, so the weakness will leave. May they dress us with the mightiness of the Basmala Sharifa. Allahu Allah Allahu Allah Allahu Rabbi ma li siwahu. Allah, may weakness be taken away from us. Allahu Akbar. Allah Allah.

Let's thank and praise Allah, and our day will be good. Those who are with their Lord will have a good day. Those who are with satan will have a terrible day. Be with Allah. Kun ma'Allah wa la tubali. Allah. Let's be with Allah. Let's not fear. O Allah, ya Rabbi. Ya Rabbi, You know our condition. You created us. You created us pure. Protect us from every fear, ya Rabbi, in this world and the next. Your mother, father, siblings, and our brothers, may we all be inside the mercy oceans of Allah. What else can we do?

Marhaba/welcome. Allah Almighty Jalla wa A'la didn't create us for dunya/this world. He created us for His Pleasure. Allahu Akbar. O our Lord, joy comes from Your Habib's way. May joy come to us. May our burdens leave. Let's run on the way to gain our Lord's Pleasure. O our Lord, You are Subhan, You are Sultan. Seek Allah. Let's ask for Allah. We will find strength with Allah and love & longing. Let's not be weak. Those with Allah are never helpless. They are lions. O our lord, Our condition...

For the sake of the Beloved Habib, give us strength, ya Rabbi, so we won't be helpess. Let's be lions. Let's be lions. Let's be lions in our Lord's Presence. Our desire is to make You pleased with us. If we cannot succeed, You are Forgiving, forgive us Allah. Allah Allah Allah Allah. Thanks to Allah. Thanks to our Mawla who created us. Be thankful. Say Shukr/thanks to Allah Almighty and we won't be in difficulty. Allah Almighty doesn't give troubles to His servants. O our Allah, You loved us and let Your Habib love us. And we also want relief & joy, ya Rabbi. We want relief, we don't want anything else. We want the relief of our Lord. Let's be content and happy. It is an important matter - To be happy with our Lord.

O our Lord, we became weak. We are weak. O our Lord, those who are with You are never weak. Those with Allah are never weak. Those who are with Allah are never hopeless. They are joyful. O our Lord, our Subhan. Fatiha.

Let's say - Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Then no distress will come. No distress comes to those who say "Allah". No troubles, except for those who don't say. Fatiha.

O Allah, our Subhan, our Allah. No troubles remain. Allah Almighty doesn't give troubles to His servants. Never, Never. Allah Almighty gives relief & joy. You are Subhan, You are Sultan, ya Rabb. We love You. You created us, ya Rabb. Let's be happy that we are our Lord's servants. Let's be joyful. Fatiha.

Let's be from the people of bliss. Not the people who are cut off. The people of bliss (Ahlis Safa). Don't be from people who are cut off (Ahlil Jafa). Allah doesn't give His servants suffering. Aman ya Rabbi. He gives www.saltanat.org Page 1 His servants bliss. He says, "My servant shouldn't suffer, he should be in bliss". O our Lord, O our Lord, our Subhan, our Sultan. Generosity and grace is from You, ya Rabb. Let's say "Allah". Let's say "Allah". Let's say "Allah". Let's say "Allah" and not be afraid. Those who say "Allah" will have no suffering. Those who say "Allah" will be in bliss. Those who don't say will suffer. Aman ya Rabbi, tawba ya Rabbi. Tawba Astaghfirullah.

I want to be in bliss with our Lord. Bliss. O our Lord, there is no suffering for us. We have no suffering if we are with You. We are in bliss. I am in bliss. We should know that we are in bliss. There is no suffering, only bliss. O Owner of heavens and earth, there is no suffering for us since we are from Your Beloved Habib's nation. There is bliss. There is no hell, there is paradise. Fatiha.

This is a very important matter. We should pay attention. What can we do, what can we do? O great and glorious Prophet, our Master, Master of creation. If only we could be in the place of the sand that he steps on. If only we could behold your magnificent face, O Rasul Allah. That is paradise. To not see him is hell. O Allah, O Allah. Fatiha.

Mankind doesn't know where bliss and suffering are. They should know. O our Lord, Your glorious Prophet informed us where bliss is and where suffering is. O Allah, our Subhan, our Sultan. Fatiha. Endless oceans, endless oceans. Allah is Subhan. Allah is Sultan. Those who are with the Subhan, with the Sultan have no worries or grief, no concerns. May Allah forgive us. Tawba ya Rabbi. Tawba Astaghfirullah.

Fatiha.
