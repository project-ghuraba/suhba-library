Those who are not Muslims start worshipping things they find; what they do is in vain. What they do is rebelling against Allah. Allah ‘azza wa jalla created everything; there is no other creator. Worship is only for Him. Now people reject Him. Already those who reject Him are unbelievers (those who cover the truth, k ir). They are against Him. Going against Him is an ill manner, because there is nowhere to run other than to Him. They think themselves clever, and all their lifelong, they claim that they are researching. Research is not necessary—Allah shows His signs clearly, every sign indicates Him and nothing else!

There is only one God. Everything in this world, in the universe, is pointing towards God. Everything shows Allah’s reality; everything worships [and serves, ibadet] Allah. Except for corrupted people, everything else such as stones, earth, trees, insects, animals, every other thing, though they have no mind, they have captured this beauty, but people who claim to have intelligence rebel against Allah. However, they will suffer. Those who think they are elevated, who say this is who I am, those who do not accept Allah, will be punished.

One who has a mind, one who uses his mind, who acknowledges the existence of Allah, knows how to submit to Allah, is saved. The end of others is unfortunate; their end is hell. There is nothing else, no where else to run either. The One who created all is Allah. Man has no home, not even a hole, to run to. The only return is to Allah. He says run to Allah, go towards Him. May Allah grant guidance to all, insha’Allah, otherwise the rest is in vain. The blind became able to see; they should [now] have their eyes open so that they worship Him at all times, and keep His orders, insha’Allah. Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah
