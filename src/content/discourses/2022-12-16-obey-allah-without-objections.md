As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Aṭī‘ū Llāha wa-aṭī‘ū r-Rasūla wa-ūlī l-amri minkum” (4:59). The duty of people, first of all, is to obey Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, and to obey our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. And then you should obey Ūlū l-Amr, those in authority where you live. Ūlū l-Amr means the government. If the government doesn’t prevent you from praying, you should obey it. You should follow the law, whatever it is. This is something Muslims everywhere should do. But, as we said, first you must carry out the order of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. HisNone orders are beneficial for humanity. They are goodness for people. AllāhNone shows that way to HisNone beloved servants. HeNone makes them love it, so they follow that way. And those whom HeNone doesn’t love or doesn’t want go the other way round. They don’t come to this way. They go against Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. They go against our Holy ProphetNone. They don’t keep their orders. And they are unfortunate people. They are unlucky people. And that is the will of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. HeNone does whatever HeNone wants. No one can interfere.

Those who are not on this way don’t have understanding, no matter how clever they are in worldly matters. They write books and build various things – machines, buildings and other things. Their minds are limited to this world. They have knowledge as mathematicians of this world, or whatever aspect of the world they can see. And people believe them. They are modifying the religion accordingly as well. And their religious leaders are misleading them from the right way. They are showing the way to these people, not as Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla ordered it, but as they like. Therefore, they are rejecting Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla most of the time. And those who don’t reject outright, are protesting against Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. They say, “Why did YouNone do this? Why are there so many wars, so many diseases and so many deaths? Why is AllāhNone doing this?” Ḥāshā.

Shukr to AllāhNone, there is no such thing in Islām. It is not from Islām. There is no such issue at all. Because we know, “Khayruhu wa-sharruhu mina Llāha ta‘ālá” – Good and evil are in HisNone hands. We cannot interfere. But as I said, they think of it as a matter of this world. Ḥāshā. They think Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is not able to make everyone happy; that everyone must be happy, everyone must be healthy, and nothing must be bad. They think HeNone cannot do it. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla has power over everything. HeNone says, “I gave you this world as a test.” Many people fail HisNone test, never to pass it. They rot and wither away. But for those who believe in and accept Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, everything is good. Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says both the good, and that which seems bad, will be good. If they are thankful for the good things, AllāhNone will reward them. And the patient ones will be rewarded even more.

Therefore, this is dunyā, as we said. People who are Muslim, who believe in AllāhNone, have been blessed with the favour of AllāhNone. They have been granted the mercy of AllāhNone. All of them are under the mercy of AllāhNone, both here and hereafter. Don’t covet others, those who have left this way for dunyā. So much wealth, so much I don’t know what. People are leaving their countries for the world of unbelief and vanishing along the way. All kinds of disgrace happen to them. They go there, though AllāhNone would have given their sustenance anywhere. They are the cheated people. It means Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla wanted them to be like that, and they have sufferings to live through. They shouldn’t forget their religion when they go there. They shouldn’t forget Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla and our Holy ProphetNone, in shā’a Llāh. Because people are showing bad things as good everywhere now. And they show the good as bad, whereas AllāhNone gave us the greatest blessings. Muslims have the greatest blessings both physically and spiritually, in Islām. We should be thankful for that. AllāhNone will increase HisNone blessings, with permission of AllāhNone.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Aṭī‘ū Llāha wa-aṭī‘ū r-Rasūla wa-ūlī l-amri minkum” (4:59). Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla ordering, Muslim, mu’min, to be obey. First, for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla and Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, and “ūlī l-amr”, meaning, the people who are ruling. But first, you must follow order of AllāhNone. Order of AllāhNone to pray, to fast, and to make Ḥajj and zakāt. This is order of AllāhNone. You must believe in Allāh and accept no one, only AllāhNone, no god, only one; HeNone is Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. This is first order for human being, to accept this. And Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, also, you must accept himNone as last Prophet, seal of prophets of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. This is, for believer, this. And third, to obey the, where, which country you are living – you must obey their laws, as much it is not make you against Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. You must obey them for everything. For... even when going shopping, going for travelling, or coming, going. All this, no problem to obey them. If you are not happy, you can leave this country. Not fighting with them. No order to fight, only you must obey. And Muslim, he obey completely with surrounded [surrender]. No any objection for what Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying.

But, in non-Muslim – now, the, most world, it is non-Muslim, non-believer – This people, they don’t understand this, to obey something they not seeing, they not... only with spiritual not physical. And everything they have in their mind, in physical, you must touch, you must do this, you must do that. For this, they have problem in their life. Their life, miserable. Because, they try to fix life. But, when they like to fix their life, they are taking, they thought, “This people, other people, they are taking our things. So, we must take from them. But if we give, it will be finished from us.” Whole time like this, their life. From ancient times, they’re attacking, killing people, taking from them. For this, their thinking is very different from Muslim, believer thinking. And they are also, most of them, they are not believe in God, they’re denying.

Even who believe, they are also, they have more problem than, maybe, than non-believer. Because they are thinking, about material things, as God like them. So, He must fix this world. They ask many times, we see in every place: in book, movie, and if we speak With people first thing they asking, “Why God not prevent this virus? Why this happen? Why this drought? Why this hunger? Why this people killing others and God not doing anything?” This is their thinking, because since centuries, they are learning like this. Not from... like Muslim people for centuries they have believed in spirituality, and they have this like... very known thing. Very well people knowing this. But, this people, they are maybe good for machine, for mechanics, for other thing they are clever, but they are... I don’t want to say... They are idiot from spiritual side, because they thought AllāhNone, He isNone like their labourer; astaghfiru Llāha l-‘Aẓīm. HeNone must fix this. HeNone must not do anything in this world. Must be everything perfect. This is idiot thinking. Not acceptable to... who has, clever man he has good thinking, to think like this.

Because, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone not one second, maybe one millionth from second, HeNone can make everything like paradise here. But here not paradise. AllāhNone sending this for people to test them. And, every... What this people also, what they’re not understanding – everything people having from illness, from misery, from very, fighting or other thing, all this people, if they are believer, they will be rewarded from AllāhNone. If not believer, they are also, it is here better than jahannam, from hell – even for this people. Because, it is very... But for believer, it is everything Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, for believer... good or not good, everything happen for them good. If they are good, they are asking, thanking AllāhNone – AllāhNone reward them also. If not good, they are asking for mercy from AllāhNone – also AllāhNone reward them.

This is, we are for this. Who is believer, he has big ni‘mah, big favour from AllāhNone for him. Even in this life, he is happy. Anything happen to him after said, when he become calm or something, later he said, “This is from AllāhNone. AllāhNone, coming from AllāhNone. AllāhNone, HeNone will reward us for this.” He is happy. But other, they are not, they don’t have anything. Even they have millions, billions money, but, no happiness. No any hope for them. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, who is believer, he is happy. For this, AllāhNone, HeNone calling people for House of Peace. “Wa-Llāhu yad‘ū ilá dāri s-salām” (10:25). Peace, happiness. All people coming. Don’t run away, come. Don’t – Here nobody asking for you any money, not asking for you anything. Just come and ask for forgiveness and you will be happy. This is the Greatness, the Most Merciful, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla calling for people. AllāhNone make people to understand this and to come to HisNone way, in shā’a Llāh. Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
