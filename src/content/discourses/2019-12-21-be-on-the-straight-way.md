Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Our ProphetNone said, "Sūratu l-Hūd turned my hair white" – "Shayyabatnī Hūd." There is one āyah in it: Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Fa-staqim kamā umirta wa-man tāba ma‘ak" (11:112). "Be on the straight path. Those who repent with you, the believers, be on the straight path." It is the order of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.

What is the straight way? It is not easy. Even our ProphetNone said: "It was so hard for me. I got scared. It made my hair turn white." Our ProphetNone had just few white hairs. Therefore, it is a sign that the straight way is not easy. In order to mislead you from the right way, there are so many issues, so much waswasah during the day that your ego bothers you, shayṭān bothers you, dunyā bothers you. To be on the straight way is a big test. Who succeeds has won.

We should walk on the way of our ProphetNone. You should try as much as you can, but you must walk on this way. Of course, sometimes you are side tracked. However, you must not leave the straight way. The main issue, the issue of creed, worship and following a murshid on the path – these are signs of the straight way. They help you be on the straight way. If you are on your own, if you do not follow a murshid, you will certainly be misled from the path. And the person you follow must be connected too so that you are also on the solid way. Sturdy. If the man you follow acts as he likes based on his own fancy and whims, he has left the way anyway and he will mislead you too. Therefore, may AllāhNone help us keep on the straight way, in shā’a Llāh.

Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, "There is one Sūrah, Sūrat Hūd, make my hair to become some of them, some my hair, white." What, "Shayyabatnī Hūd, Sūrat Hūd." Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, his hair it was black and some, only maybe 20, of hair was white. This coming from fearing from Sūrat Hūd. There is one verse: Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Fa-staqim kamā umirta wa-man tāba ma‘ak" (11:112). Be in istiqāmah. Istiqāmah meaning to be straight, to [be] in right way, youNone and who are following youNone.

This is, people say, "What is, it is easy to go straight way." No, it is not easy. Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, heNone was very heavy for himNone because every day who hundred things happen, to make you, if you are not strong believe, to follow your ego, to follow shayṭān, to follow other thing in this world. From morning to evening there is many things make you to go out of right way. So, to be in right way it is real good thing, but it is difficult. To be right thing, you must follow Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, you must follow murshid to keep you in right way. And who follow murshid, murshid also must be connected to be with Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, to make him safe.

If you not connect even he is putting thing on his own idea and this is not correct. Correct... Of course in, when you go way, sometimes you are tired, sometimes you are lazy, sometimes... but if you are following you will be safe. If you not following you will be find yourself out of way and make people also going out of way. So it is important to be connected and to be with honest one. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīmi. "Yā ayyuhā lladhīna āmanū t-taqū Llāha wa kūnū ma‘a ṣ-ṣādiqīn" (9:119). This also order, "O believer, be aware from AllāhNone and be with honest people." The most important thing to be honest, because the most Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, was hate from attribute of people – liar, lying.

To be, if you some, Mashāyikh never, they never lie, never saying anything not truth. All they say truth. So you must be with them, you will be safe, in shā’a Llāh, you will be in straight way. What is, big favour to be with following Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim and whole Mashāyikh. Whole of them strong holding for straight way. AllāhNone keep us with them in shā’a Llāh, not to follow our ego or to go out of istiqāmah.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
