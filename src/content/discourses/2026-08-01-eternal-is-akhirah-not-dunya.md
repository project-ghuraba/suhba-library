As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
فَأَمَّا مَن طَغَىٰ . وَآثَرَ الْحَيَاةَ الدُّنْيَا . فَإِنَّ الْجَحِيمَ هِيَ الْمَأْوَىٰ
<br>“Fa-ammā man ṭaghā. Wa āthara l-ḥayāta d-dunyā. Fa-inna l-jaḥīma hiya l-ma'wā”<br>
“So as for he who transgressed. And preferred the life of the world. Verily, his abode will be Hell-fire.” (Qur’ān 79:37-39). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says: “Whoever prefers this world to the Hereafter, his end will be Hell.” This world is temporary, while the Hereafter is eternal, everlasting; it has no end.

Therefore, whoever prefers this world to the Hereafter follows his ego. Ego destroys people. Because no matter how much suffering a person endures in this world, everything in this world has an end. The Hereafter, however, has no end.

Those who enter Paradise will remain there forever. Those who enter Hell, may Allah protect us, will also remain there forever.

The life of the Hereafter is eternal. Some people do not accept this. According to their own minds, they say, “There is no eternity,” and call other things in this world forever. They do not accept the truth that Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla has shown and told.

There is eternity. This is important. What is important is not this world, but the Hereafter.

Therefore, our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam said, “This world is like the blink of an eye.” And indeed, it is so. Years pass, and people wonder, “Have I only lived this long?” They say, “How did so much time pass so quickly? How fast it went by!”

That’s why, our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam said, “The world is not even the blink of an eye.” So, compared to the Hereafter, the world is nothing. Whatever happens, it comes and goes. What’s important is the Hereafter.

Of course, there are many ignorant people. Some think, “Life passes by anyway, let’s not waste it in vain; let’s inflate our egos as much as we can, let’s do everything our ego desires.” They think, “Life passes by in an instant. Therefore, the more fun we have, the more we inflate our egos, the more we serve it, the better it will be for us, it won't have been wasted.”

However, by doing this, they live very empty lives; or rather, they fill their lives not with emptiness, but with bad things. It would be good if they went to the Hereafter empty-handed, but they won’t go there empty-handed either. Because they will be held accountable in the Hereafter for the sins and ḥarām they have committed.

If they depart from this world in a state of disbelief, may Allah protect us, there will be an eternal punishment for this. If they are not in a state of disbelief, they will suffer their punishment and eventually enter Paradise; however, a short time here is equivalent to a lifetime there. A wasted time here is like losing an entire lifetime compared to the Hereafter.

Therefore, you must be very careful and do what is difficult for the ego. You mustn’t follow the ego. You must turn back from the wrong way. You must stay away from evil. People today use bad things and associate with bad people just to please and satisfy their egos.

They do evil solely to satisfy their egos. This has no benefit whatsoever. On the contrary, it is harm upon harm.

May Allah protect us; may Allah protect us from following our egos and doing evil. May Allah protect us all, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
