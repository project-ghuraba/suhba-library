Shukur Allah, shukur Allah Almighty. Let us have a nice sohbat, let us have a nice talk. As-Salam Alaikum ayyuhal hadhirun- we may greet those who are present, send them salams. Salam is the sign of Islam, giving salam is the indication of a Muslim. We say Salam Alaikum, Arabs say As-Salamu Alaikum. We say "Salam Alaikum, how are you Yahu?" Salam, how beautiful.

Dastur Ya Sultan al Awliya, madad our Umm Hiram Sultan. We may say a few words with your permission. We are in an astonishing time- A person speaks in a small place & the whole dunya from east to west hears it. Astonishing. There are a lot of astonishing things. As-Salamu Alaikum ayyuhal hadhirun, those who are present. Who wants to listen to a few words, who wants to find a spiritual relief, who wants his doings to succeed, who wants a wasila for all this, should be present in a sohbat assembly. There he can hear even if it is only few words.

According to the first order, "may we remember the name of Allah first. It is obligatory for a servant in his every act". Now they throw Mawlid al-Sharif aside, saying "how can we read it, it is so many volumes of books". We want to walk in the Nur/light of the Orders that descended from Heavens. We don't want the imitated ones. The words spoken descend from Heavens with Nur. Otherwise, it has no value. It is only a piece of wood given the shape of a cucumber. They painted it green & put it on the table saying "Go ahead, the cucumbers are fresh from the garden". That man took it in his hand & looked, "This is not a cucumber, this is.. This is wood! From where did you find this?" "Yahu, I saw it in the market. They were advertising it as..."

Where is that place in Istanbul that is famous for its cucumbers? Yes, Cengelkoy. Famous. Sometimes they put wooden ones also among the real ones & shout "Come to the cucumbers of Cengelkoy, O people of Istanbul & you may see the cucumbers of Cengelkoy! They are very delicious" One man took one, tasted & said "Yahu this breaks our teeth. What is this?" "They are the newly invented cucumbers of Cengelkoy sir. We threw away the old ones & now there is a new style factory. That factory brings us many cucumbers these days. We mix them with the other cucumbers & say "Come, cucumber of Cengelkoy". "May you find your punishment from Allah. How can you cheat people like this?" "What can we do? The time is like this now- half of it real & half of it imitated".

"Yahu, in this time there is nothing left whose half is original, all of them are imitated!" "Buy if you like & leave if you don't like sir! These are the cucumbers that the people of Cengelkoy eat. Who has a good taste may come & buy from them". "Ok then, but our teeth will get broken. How come this is the cucumber of Cengelkoy?" "This is the new invention. We brought innovation to our people. Because we brought innovation, now we give it the name of "hiyar" for our cucumbers (slang word used both as cucumber & stupid man) We removed its original name cucumber & say "hiyar". And we brought a new innovation to our country because our people are in love with innovation". Now we may stop here.

We greeted with Salam, Salam is the greeting of our ancestors. It is the order of Allah for the people to greet each other with Salam. When you see each other, greet with Salam. Call the one who greets with Salam a Muslim. Don't call who doesn't greet with Salam a Muslim. A Muslim is the one who has Salam on his lips. Who doesn't have Salam on his lips is not a Muslim. Allah Almighty does not accept him.

Nevertheless, let this be the entrance to our sohbat. We spoke a few words from the door of the entrance. We may greet all of you with Salam, that you are listening to these words- As-Salamu Alaikum wa Rahmatullahi wa Barakatuhu O believers who know the Salam, O believers who remember the Name of Allah in the first place, that it gives you Nur. It becomes a Nur for your faces & a joy for your hearts. For this we greeted with Salam "O believers, Salam on you". We greeted with Salam & made an entrance about Salam also. And as a permission to say a few words we say Bismillahi Rahmani Rahim. After we say Bismillahir Rahmanir Rahim there is no end for the words, knowledge & good manners to be spoken till the Day of Judgement.

Bismillahir Rahmanir Rahim is the axis of man's life. Mihwar/axis, the earth spins around its own axis. It is the axis which it turns around. If it didn't exist, the earth would have no night or day. Because it spins around its axis it receives Nur & gives Nur. And the Nur of mankind's life is in Bismillahir Rahmanir Rahim. If we speak this word till the Day of Judgement- The Bismillah, its beauty.. Nothing can give the spiritual power & the happiness it gives. So say the Bismillah. Forgetting the Bismillah means that he forgot himself too. Let us say, O believers! O Muslims! O who did not change their names! Let us say Bismillahir Rahmanir Rahim. The 3 Holy Names of the Almighty Lord- with His Ism-i Jalal (Allah), 2 of His Holy Names are mentioned. Bi Ismillah, Allah, Allah, Allah! Allah Who is Ar-Rahman & Ar-Rahim, Allah, Allah...Say!

And you will know Hanya (in Crete) & Konya (city where Rumi's turba is). Now they sell you Hanya saying it is Konya & Konya as Hanya. Meaning they change things. Hanya & Konya ryhme but where is Konya? "In the middle of the Mediteranean". "Where is Hanya?" "In the middle of Anatolia". "You are making it the exact opposite way, what is this?" "But it is like this, Shaykh Effendi, now everything is renewed. We will make everything upside down & down side up. We will invent a new life system & we will keep a way as we like". "Is this way going to take you anywhere? Do you have a map or compass?" Yahu Shaykhh Effendi, what map? What compass? Are you talking about the old tales?" Everyone says "I have my own opinion" & goes according to his opinion. They don't use any map or compass. "We go, we know, we invent! We can cheat everyone with a different trick. We don't say the Bismillah" they say. If you don't say, your heads are empty. Your hearts are like flea markets. Flea market. Your market, the goods you bring are all rubbish things. Who says Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim, each time it comes to you with a new heavenly wisdom, with a new heavenly gift, with a new heavenly beauty. Each time you say the Bismillah, each time you say Bismillahir-Rahmani r-Rahim you are dressed with the dress of faith. When you say Bismillahir-Rahmani r-Rahim you get all the world's.. What do they call these beauty things? Each time they put makeup, they look like monkeys. But they look & say "it looks very beautiful. This is a new thing, we should use this". O mindless, empty-headed people! A person can't be beautiful with creams or makeup. Beauty comes down on people from the Heavens. With what? It comes down to who says Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. It is our true makeup. With every Bismillah comes Nur on our faces, comes joy to our hearts. Your work goes right.

"No, this is an old style thing". Since shaytan got on their necks & made them shaytan's animals, he says to them "Don't believe it. This is innovation. It is innovation- use this cream, use this makeup & your face, your skin will shine". The people of this time are the people of 2nd era of ignorance. "Wa La Tabarrajna Tabarruja Al-Jahiliyati Al-'Ula" (33:33) O people, Allah Almighty says: Don't do like the people of that first time of ignorance. Tabarruj, don't go out dressing up. Don't present yourselves to the sight of strangers. Because each sight of a stranger takes from you, takes from your beauty- the beauty Allah granted. It won't come back with creams or makeup. Say O mankind, Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. How beautiful. How nice. If he says Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim that person can lift a mountain! Who says Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim can carry a mountain. Now, man cannot even carry a rubbish.

They made all kinds of machines. Was it not snowing in the old times? It was. "With what would they clean the snow?" "With shovels Shaykh Effendi. It was very easy- people had shovels & would throw the snow with their shovels". "And now?" "What shovel now Shaykh Effendi? Now there are vehicles which are maybe 100 tons to clean the snow. Yet they can't succeed, they can't manage". Why? They don't say the Bismillah. Because they don't say it, shaytan piles up the snow on top of them. They say "We can't manage it". How could you manage it? It is the snow coming down from the skies..But before it could be removed with shovels. Because men at that time were believers. When he said Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim & threw, it would open & because he recited the Bismillah, it would not pile up there again. This is the miracle of the Bismillah. No one has this in mind now & huge machines that are 10 meters, 15 meters come but can't remove the snow. Of course you can't remove it! Because you have no faith, no spirituality, no Bismillah. You are without the Bismillah. You are afraid of getting stuck under that snow along with that machine that removes it.

Snow falls from mountains. I heard that sometimes rocks fall too. It makes rocks move too. But you can't remove the rocks with machines. Remembering the Lord Almighty Who keeps the rock still in its place, say "Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Remain in your place, don't harm us. We are believers". And they remain in their places. Why? Because of Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Special angels come down to protect them. But you don't believe. Ok, if you don't believe, make machines & manage it if you can. The machines as well as who are in them will be lost. These people should say Allah or all of them will get stuck under the snow. Allah Almighty leaves them there. They say "It is 10 degrees below zero". Really? "In Erzurum, it is -40 degrees". If Allah Almighty likes, He can make it 50 degrees below zero in Istanbul. What can you do against it? With what heater can you get warm? In which oven can you enter? Nothing! Say "Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. O our Lord, we remembered Your Holy Name. Make us secure from these snows O Lord. Send us the angels who will secure us, O Lord". "What angel Yahu? In which time are we living?" he says in reply, donkey man. "In which time does he live?" Ask your self, how come you became tailless? You are tailless. The word you spoke is like the word of 4-legged ones. What value does it have?

Why don't you say the right words, "Allah sends this snow" like in the old times they would say about the snow? Year of snow is the year of abundance. People would make shukur for snow. There would be no frost. Snow would come down & melt. It wouldn't pile up on earth. So the year of snow was the year of abundance. Now because of these ones' wildness, the year of snow turned into the year of disasters on them. Mountains collapsed, avalanches came. Houses couldn't take the snow & collapsed. And Allah knows what happened to those inside them because they couldn't say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Say this & shield your house.

Put a sign inside your home- Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. What avalanche can fall on your home to crush the Bismillah? Hasha! O imitators, O who are animals to shaytan! He will make you regret your life, your work, whatever it is you are doing. He will make it all corrupt. You won't be able to cope with it. They have no work, no jobs left anymore, it is all closed. They can't do anything. Why? "Because the snow reached 1 m" they say. "0.5 meter". It made the houses collapse & Allah knows about the ones in them. Why don't you say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim? O our Lord, stop this calamity & make it far from us. Our homes are full with those who make sajda to our Lord, who recite the Holy Quran. Don't crush us!" Angels protect them. Those huge machines won't be able to save these ignorant people. Because what has our time become?

The old ones would say like this: "Ignorance covered the world. If you ask them, they don't know what faith is" People who have faith don't put the Bismillah inside their homes? "We put a portrait &show respect for the portrait".Do you have respect for Allah? What is this? "We also make statues". Then? "We also bow before it". "What?" "This is the new life, Shaykh Effendi. Don't you know?" "I know son, I know but it weighs it out for you so that you see it & use your mind, that you know the power of Bismillah. Even if all the skies came down (with all the disasters they bear) no harm will come to your house, family, to your children or crop. Never! Therefore say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. It is the black winter. They even suffer the worst of the black winter. They say "below zero". It can be even 100 below zero. Angels warm up where he is, and the house remains protected in it (in the snow).

Ya Hafiz Ya Allah. "O Allah Who protects everything Protect us, we make sajda for You. We remember Your Holy Names O our Lord" They don't say this, they don't say.. With the shovel in their hand, they don't say the Bismillah. There are pipes in their hands, they can't drain away the flood water. They don't learn the lesson, they don't use their minds. Allah Almighty's big grant is Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. "Hawalayna wa la 'alayna" Not on us should Your Wrath come, O our Lord but let it go on to the mountains. "We have faith O our Lord. Let it not come on us but go to the mountains, rocks around us. Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim". Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Even if we don't know anything else it is the greatness of the Bismillah. Say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim.

They have so many schools & they teach the children all kinds of games. Everything they have is artifical & they don't make the children say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. If you don't make them say the Bismillah you can't educate decent men. You can't educate humans. You make them as wild herds that kill, destroy, burn each other. They invent & commit whatever evil, bad actions there are. O our Lord, we have faith in You. We have faith in Your Beloved. We are Muslims O Lord. You are our Lord. Your Beloved is our Beloved Prophet. For his honour may You make these disasters far away from us. May You protect us from floods, snow, avalanches O Lord.

Adhilna fi Rahmatika, hide us in Your Mercy O Lord. They don't say this. Let them say & they will be saved. They have become like rocks, their consciousness is darkened, they don't say Allah. They say other things. Mountains will fall on them. Rivers will overflow. Floods will take away the cities. This is nothing yet. There is more to come. While it snows on one side, fire comes from another. If this is not enough it starts quaking. You can't stop it. Say "O Lord, for the honour of Your Beloved, may You lift these calamities off us O Lord. We are believers O Lord". They don't say this. They don't teach our children anything other than games & silly talks. They don't make them say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Teach it. What will save us is Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Nothing can harm You.

O Lord, forgive us. Tawba Ya Rabbi, tawba Ya Rabbi, tawba. Shukur Ya Rabbi, shukur.. O Allah, may You grant us to use our minds. We are not using our minds any more.
