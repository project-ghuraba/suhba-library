As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh. A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah. “Kullu mri’in bi-mā kasaba rahīn” (52:21). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘Aẓīm. Every person is responsible and accountable for himself. Whatever he has earned, he will be treated accordingly. In the presence of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, whatever he has done, he is responsible for it himself. A person will see what he himself has done from acts of worship, obedience, and whatever else there is. Nothing will be lost. The deeds done in dunyā are never lost. What is lost? If you have committed sins and you repent and seek forgiveness for them, Allāh causes them to disappear. He erases them. But if you persist, thinking highly of yourself, continuing to do the same thing every day and directing others to that way, then you will find your punishment. You will give account for what you have earned. Then you will see what benefit it brought you. That is why, while in dunyā, they say one breath in dunyā is better than a thousand years beneath the earth. Because as long as you are breathing, Allāh forgives you, and your sins are forgiven. Otherwise, it would already be in the past. When a person closes his eyes, his book is closed. His good deeds come to a halt, except for three things, says our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. The first is a righteous child who does goodness and charity after you. It comes to you as goodness, and he will benefit from it too. The second is beneficial knowledge that you leave for people to benefit from. And the third is doing a good deed for the benefit of the ummah: a mosque, a madrasah, a hospital, an orphanage, or whatever else there is, even a fountain. Things that people benefit from. The du‘ā’s of those who benefit from them will be accepted and will reach you. Otherwise, you have done nothing. And on top of that, you’ve committed a lot of sins. And that was not enough. You have also been stubborn and defied Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Then your situation will be bad – not just difficult, but very bad. May Allāh protect us. So, a person will receive in the ākhirah according to all that he has done. “Kullu mri’in bi-mā kasaba rahīn” (52:21). He is held, as if seized, like a hostage. If there is something against him, he will pay it and be freed. “No, I have nothing.” “Then come here, to this side, the left side.” If there are some deeds there, you may be released later. But if there is nothing, you will stay there forever. May Allāh protect us. May Allāh not let us follow our egos, in shā’a Llāh. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, saying: Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Kullu mri’in bi-mā kasaba rahīn” (52:21). Everyone he is, he is will be asked for what he done, and he will be like hostage. “Rahīn”, meaning hostage. If he has everything, no, not hostage. He will be free quickly. But if he not have any good thing, it will be problem for him. Because in life, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, He record everything. Every movement for everybody. There is two angels, from right and left. They are writing what he is doing, and for, until for Qiyāmah they will be waiting. When he pass away, he close eyes, finish, no breath, die, no more. Cannot be do anything. But as much he is in life, he can save himself. He can be safe. What you done, you ask for forgiveness, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, can give forgiveness. Even, right side angel writing rewards. Left side angel writing sin. But when somebody doing good thing, this right one, immediately he write if he done this good thing. He make ṣadaqah, he make praying, he make good thing. But other side, who writing sin, waiting. He ask, because his boss one right side, “Oh, can I write this? He make this sin, I will write.” He say, “No. Stop, wait. Wait, maybe he will be ask for forgiveness.” He waiting one hour, two hour, five hour, and asking again. Everytime asking, he said, “Wait,” until eight hour. After, he said, if not asking for forgiveness, said, “Okay, write this one.” So he will write sin. But even for this, if anybody can ask for forgiveness, also can Allāh forgive him. But if he is not asking for forgiveness, and he is stubborn, and he coming against, he is happy with what he done, and make people to follow him with bad thing he done, it will be no forgiveness for him after dying. Before dying, if he ask Allāh for forgiveness, He forgive, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. But when you dying and go to other world, that time, no anything can save him. Only, because when somebody dying, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, no good deed coming for him. Finish. No more obligatory for praying, no more for ḥajj, for or fasting. So after him, who is looking after him, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, three thing only can be, help him. One, the good children after him. They are praying, they asking for forgiveness for their parents. It will be good. Second, good knowledge he give for people and people benefit from this. Also coming, every time, reward for him. Third, to be make charity for human being. Anything like mosque or dergah or hospital or water or building for poor people, anything good for... This all coming up behind him. But for, another thing, who not done this, it will be nothing coming for him. Only he will be regret and suffering for, some of them forever, some of them for some time. So everybody must be ready, for to be everything okay for him, before going to another side, to be prepare good thing for himself, in shā’a Llāh. Allāh help us, and Allāh make us to look for help from by ourself, in shā’a Llāh. And, in shā’a Llāh, give us good thing for all of us, in shā’a Llāh. Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
