Tariqatuna as-sohba wal khayru fil jam'iya.

Yassiru wa la tu'assiru. Prophet salallahu alaihi wa sallam saying: make it easy, don't make difficult. Don't make people to run away from Islam. You must be soft, you must be gentle. You must be polite for people. This is teaching of Prophet salallahu alaihi wa sallam. If you worshipping whole night and day, and you are making people afraid from you, make people unhappy with you, it is not good. Especially for who not like tariqah, showing tariqa like bad thing. This is from teaching of satan.

There is one story, it is real story. In old time, before Prophet salallahu alihi wa sallam, there's people we hear about worshippers, they are going away from people and only worshipping Allah, not anything else. Prophet salallahu alihi wa sallam said, in my ummah, my nation, no place for these people. We are praying, doing our business, we can do anything, we must be around people also. Before Prophet salallahu alihi wa sallam, it was okay, they were doing this.

There was one of these people, his name Barcisan. He was only in his house or his cottage, day and night worshipping, not doing anything else. Satan is not happy with people who are with Allah. It tried everything to make him out of this way, to make him bad man, to not worship Allah, to not do what order of Allah. But every time it tried, it couldn't do anything to him. But it is satan, it is clever. It is doing everything to cheat people. How it can cheat worshipping man, who is whole life doing worship?

It said, you must do this. "No I cannot do this I am afraid from Allah." Do this. "No I cannot do this. Also, you are order me bad thing, go away from me." And then satan make trip to cheat him. What it did? It making its beard white and put clothes like worshipper people on itself and it came to his door and knock his door. When he opened, he said, “You’re one like a saint. O brother, what you want?" Satan tell him - "I am worshipping Allah all the time, I am looking for friend to worship together.

I am looking around, all this country, someone told me, here there is one good worshipper, so I come for you if you accept, we can sit together and we pray, worship Allah, not doing anything else". So of course, "welcome, welcome" he said and he take him into his cottage. After, satan beginning day and night praying. Not sleeping at night, not eating anything, only worshipping. Many months like this. And this man was very happy with him, but he said, "How you can do this? I cannot do without sleeping, without eating, I'm not all time praying. How you do this?" It said, "also you can do this". He said, "How?" It said, "You can make wrong thing and after big wrong thing, Allah, if you regret, repent to Allah, Allah will accept your repentance and you be more afraid from Allah because you do wrong thing and then you will be like me". He said, "How I can do this? What we can do?" He asked him. It said, "You can commit adultery". He said, "No I cannot do this". "Okay, leave this. You can kill somebody." "Also I cannot do this", he said. It said, "Of course, never mind. Drink little bit alcohol, and it will be big sin for you, after you repent and Allah He forgive you and you continue and you'll be like me". He said, "This is easy, it's only alcohol".

So he went to bar. And who was serving there? A woman. He said, "I want to drink". Drink, after when he became drunk, he beginning to think to be with this woman. And after he did this bad thing, he was awake, "What I done? Now people will hear and I will be in very bad situation. So what I can do? I can kill this woman". And he killed her and put her behind house. Digging and putting on her. But people knew him and they catch him. Bringing to court, and after court, of course they will give him death penalty. And they bring him to hang him there. And he was in very bad situation.

Even in that situation satan not leaving him. It saw him, "O brother what happened?" "This is what happened. Please save me", he said to it. It said, "Of course I can save you”. He said, "What, how save me?" It said, "You must make sajdah for me". Even in this situation, not finished from all it done to him. "Come make sajdah for me". "I cannot make sajdah, I am tied". It said, "Also, only by your eyes, you can do this for me". And when he doing this by eyes, they opened under him and he was hanged. And in dunya, in akhira, he was finished. This is what Salafi, Wahabi, doing to people.

Our people, since thousand years in this area. We visit masha'Allah awliya. How Islam coming in this area? By awliyaullah. Not by Salafi and not by Wahabi. No, no Wahabi in that time. They come and teach people with politeness, with goodness, with every good manner. And people were happy with them, and they become Muslim here and continue in real Islam. Of course satan try to make something, but it couldn't be successful like what happened at the end. It bring Qadiani, it bring Shi'a, it bring many thing, but people, when they saw this in front like mirror, "of course we don't want this," they said.

Little, little, people go behind them. But it is like this, satan, what it's showing people. “We are real Islam, we are the best in Islam. We are doing this. You are in wrong way, you must come with us”. So they are taking these poor people, mindless people. They make them to destroy all what they have, what they got and not even here, in akhira also. Not let them to see Prophet salallahu alaihi wa sallam, because if they see Prophet salallahu alaihi wa sallam, there is hadith, they'll be ashamed from what they are doing against ahl as-sunnah wal jama'a and not respecting Prophet and awliya, beloved to Allah and Prophet salallahu alaihi wa sallam. Their face meat will become like water. No meat after in their face. This is very sad because people they are really not thinking. Prophet salallahu alaihi wa sallam saying: to think 1 hour better than 100 year ibadah (worship). This man was worshipping, making ibadah, but he not thinking. These our people also, who are following these ahl al-bid'a. They are bid'a, they are bid'a sayyi'a, these Wahabi people, Salafi people because they are taking people to danger. Even in dunya, they making adawat, enmity between muslims. If you're not happy, ok, don't come, but don't make enmity. Everyone is responsible for himself. You are not responsible for him.

If you are responsible in what you do, you are doing same what satan doing. You are also coming interfering with people, forcing them, telling them this and that, and cheating them, and after this they are thinking that he is worshipping day and night and not doing any wrong. He must be right one. But they don't know the real, inside him. For this Prophet salallahu alaihi wa sallam saying: I am eating and fasting, sleeping and waking up, I am married and I have children also. We can do everything that Allah make halal. No need to make it haram for us.

To visit awliyaullah and to visit saliheen also is good. Many hadith from Prophet salallahu alaihi wa sallam. He is saying you can visit, and especially for father and mother, for Friday, it is the best to visit in graveyard. And everything they are showing people as wrong, it is halal and, even sunnat of Rasulullah, salallahu alaihi wa sallam. But it will be bad for them and Insh'Allah they will not be long term. Insha'Allah Mahdi (as) coming soon. He will be Mujtahid Mutlaq.

When Mahdi (as) come, no need for mazhab, no need for other thing. All mazhab one, tariqa also one in this time because he is Mujtahid Mutlaq, like absolute, like what Prophet showing. We must follow him. Who objects, he will send him to other place, other dunya. No, only one religion in all world, Islam only at that time. No Wahabi, no Salafi after. And it is promise and ahl as-sunnah wal jama'a believe he will come. And it looks like it is very near, insha'Allah. May Allah make us to be in those happy days. The 'asru-s sa'ada ath-thaniya, the happy century after Prophet salallahu alaihi wa sallam, in time of Mahdi (as). May Allah make him come quickly Insha'Allah. And Insha'Allah we will be with him.

Wa min Allah at-tawfiq,m al-Fatiha.
