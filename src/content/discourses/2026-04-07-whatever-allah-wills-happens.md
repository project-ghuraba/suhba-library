As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Our Prophet ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam says, “If Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla wants someone to do something unusual, He takes away their mind and makes them do it. Then the person wonders, ‘How did I do this? How did I make this mistake, how did I do this action?’” Everything happens by the will of Allah, of course, but there are things that go normally in life, and things that don’t.

If a person, while going straight on the right path, suddenly does something not good at all, something that wouldn’t even cross one’s mind, this is also a manifestation of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. This applies to everyone; young or old, president or prime minister - Whoever it may be, Allah’s will is happening.

Since this world is not paradise, there will certainly be setbacks, good things, and bad things. These will certainly happen, as the Divine will manifests, and the days advance to the Day of Judgment.

The last of the names, attributes of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla that we have read is “As-Sabūr”, meaning “The All-Patient”. We are now in that manifestation. Therefore, if these events had occurred in the time of ancient peoples, Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla would have sent punishment upon them and razed them all to the ground. However, now, due to the manifestation of this last name, Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla is patient, for everything has its time. Everything happens by His will.

Therefore, may Allah protect us. May Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla not allow us to do inappropriate things. May He not make us do anything we regret, inshā’Allah. May we be steadfast in good deeds, inshā’Allah. May He keep us away from evil deeds by His will, keep us steadfast on the way of truth. Because the world is temporary, and its end has come.

May Allah protect the Muslims and Islām, and send the Sāhib. As you see, the state of the world is such that its end has come. Inshā’Allah, the Sāhib comes and save us all. All of humanity, not just Muslims, will be saved when Mahdi ʿalayhi s-salām appears, with the permission of Allah. May Allah send him soon, inshā’Allah.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
