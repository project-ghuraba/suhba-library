As-salāmu ‘alaykum wa raḥmatu Llāhi wa barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

May AllāhNone bless it, in shā’a Llāh. ‘Īd is coming. Today is ‘arafah, a holy day. Today is the last day of Ramaḍān. AllāhNone has a nice promise. When Ramaḍān is over, all our sins will be forgiven, in shā’a Llāh. It is beautiful good tidings. May we spend it with īmān and barakah. It is a month in which AllāhNone shows us grace and love because it is spent with fasting during the day and worship during the night. One more year is gone from our lifetime. People are sad on the New Year that they are one year older. And as we become closer to AllāhNone by our īmān in Ramaḍān, in shā’a Llāh, we should be happier.

Our ProphetNone is saying to live with good manners in this world. HeNone says to stay away from bad characteristics. Our ProphetNone says there are three characteristics that destroy people. The first one is arrogance. Because of arrogance, shayṭān didn't make sajdah to Ādam (as). He was thrown away from paradise, from the presence of AllāhNone. The second is greed. Ādam ‘alayhi s-salām ate from the forbidden fruit because of his greed. Instead of more and better things... There was everything but out of greed, Ādam ‘alayhi s-salām ate it too. So he was taken out of paradise. And the third is envy. And that's the worst. Two sons of Ādam, one killed the other because of envy. We have to stay away from these bad characteristics. Our ProphetNone is warning us against them. Never approach them.

Arrogance. A person with arrogance is an idiot because AllāhNone created all of us. To be proud is for AllāhNone only. Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla is the one who created everything. We are creatures. AllāhNone is the Creator. Therefore, a creature can't be arrogant to another creature. Greed is not good either. You should be patient. And it is not good to want something too much. You won't feel at peace. And envy is a totally bad thing. A person who has it lives his whole life as in hell. He envies everyone and feels jealous that others have and he doesn't. You can't have everything. AllāhNone created and AllāhNone gave. HeNone gave to whom HeNone wanted. Therefore we should stay away from envy.

May AllāhNone bless this night. The night of ‘Īd is a holy night. There are several nights in a year. Two of them are the night of ‘Īdu l-Fiṭr and the night of ‘Īdu l-Aḍḥá. And there are 5-6 holy nights, like Laylatu l-Qadr, Raghā’ib, Mi‘rāj, Mawlid. Among these nights, these two are holy nights. We should pay attention to them. May our prayers and ṣalāh be good, in shā’a Llāh. May AllāhNone let us reach more years.

Today, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are reach the end of the holy month. ProphetNone saying when last day you are breaking fast AllāhNone also forgive you for everything. Whole Ramaḍān you, you are fasting. Especially for the end of this month special present from AllāhNone to forgive you and bless you. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are safely we reach this end and it was very nice month Ramaḍān because daytime fasting, nighttime worshipping, praying. Everything was so beautiful in this month. And in the end AllāhNone also HeNone give us present for mu’min, mu’minah, and Muslim believer - for whole believer. HeNone forgive them and HeNone said "I'm only knowing what INone give. Nobody know what INone give for this." People they are who are they are fasting in Ramaḍān "ana ujzi bihi" I'm only and not tell anybody what INone will give and HeNone is the most generous. So you cannot imagine what HeNone will give. Because we have limit and we only maybe say ten times, twelve times, hundred times, thousand times, but Allāh HeNone is generous, you cannot imagine what HeNone give.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, it is nice time, nice day and it is blessed night also this night. In ‘Īd night... There is few holy night in year, maybe five or seven. Two of them, one this night and one in ‘Īdu l-Aḍḥá ninth of Dhū l-Ḥijjah. It is also very holy night. And AllāhNone accept your du‘ā’, accept your charity, your worshipping. so, in shā’a Llāh, we can with our intention to ask all good thing for Muslim and to be safe and to be in right way, in shā’a Llāh. And ProphetNone he said for warning ummah to not follow their some bad desires, some bad attributes they have. It must be attribute nice good behaviour. But there is three of them ProphetNone was saying.

First of them to be proud. Proudness make iblīs satan to not accept Ādam ‘alayhi s-salām, not obey order of AllāhNone to make sajdah for Ādam ‘alayhi s-salām. And he was kicked out from his everything he has but he from his proudness he didn't make it. So it is very bad attribute this.

Second one to be greedy. Greediness it is also bad because Sayyidinā Ādam ‘alayhi s-salām, ProphetNone saying he was asking more in Jannah. Everything he has but only one forbidden and he from his greediness he take it and he eat it. And also he was thrown out from paradise.

And third one, envious. It is the worst because son of Ādam ‘alayhi s-salām he killed his brother from what he was have envy. He said: "He has better than me, I must kill him."

So it was these three things, if you keep away from these ProphetNone saying you will be restful and you will be not doing any mistake anything. So, in shā’a Llāh, we try, in shā’a Llāh, to be away. But especially envious it is the worst. All of them worst, bad, but the envious because who... it is like illness. If somebody have it, difficult to get rid of it. And it make whole his life like hell. Even he has whole world but he has this envious he will not be restful, he will be whole time in bad misery and not happy. And it is... ProphetNone order we must be away from this, in shā’a Llāh. This is also naṣīḥah (advice) for end of Ramaḍān. AllāhNone make this attribute away from us, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq al-Fātiḥah.
