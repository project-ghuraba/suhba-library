As salamu alaykum wa rahmatullahi wa barakatuhu.

Our voice is gone anyway. Therefore honorable Haji Kadir Misiroglu completed our words in full. Nobody says he's haji but in fact, he is. Maybe you don't know, he is 12-times haji, Haji Misiroglu. The message he wants to transmit has reached its perfection from pre-eternal up to eternal, from beginning up to end, and mashaAllah together in an understandable way. First with success from JanabAllah, then by the compassion and intercession of Rasulullah, sallAllahu alaihi wa sallam, with support of Ahlullah [people of Allah]. He was in complete submission to the ones who empowered him with spirituality and were assigned to be witnesses during these nights, Sultans of the Hereafter, who lie in Konya, this holy city of Konya, and especially Sultan of the Hereafter, Sultan Mawlana Jalaluddin Rumi. This is a divine favour.

I have always heard a little bit from the honorable Haji Kadir Bey's sohbats. However, I heard it in full tonight. MashaAllah, his understanding is very broad. This is a divine favour to him. And it is a big sign and good tidings that our nation is a great nation. And the ability to educate such a knowledgeable historian as himself, not just an historian, but someone who can tell history in its details and with its wisdoms, reasons and consequences, is truly an appropriate amazement, an appropriate appreciation. And I can't accept that it is from the education of the present regime of the last 70 years. Probably he achieved it because of his piety and untiring aspirations, as well as by a healthy faith in JanabAllah. His love for RasulAllah, care and belief in Ahlullah, and at the same time, his respect and observance of them have brought him to this point.

At the same time, we are Ottomans. If we deny what we are, we won't gain any wisdom. However, unfortunately, we are under such conditions now that when we say we are Ottomans, there is such an atmosphere that makes us seem criminal - as if we are Russians or Germans, or Armenians; or we are considered to belong to some other nation which is an enemy to the Turks. Whereas, if we don't love our ancestors, who built the Ottoman Empire, the greatest government in the world, if we don't appreciate them, if we don't keep respect for them, what value will we have? Are we a mushroom-like nation? I was calling too. He was born in 1933. In 1933, I was 11 years old. We came out proud from every battle in 10 years and brought up 15 million youngsters from every age. It was a march of the 10th year. Of course, I couldn't understand it then. But now some part of our brain works. How is it possible? Did we grow up like mushrooms [ie from nothing], 15 million in 10 years? What are we? Ottomans planted sycamores. Did these plant mushrooms? How is it possible?

In summary the reason of our pride, the reason of our existence on earth, the reason of our existence in Anatolia, the reason of our existence in history, is because of our ancestors, who walked ahead destroying unbelief with their swords, removing kufr in front of Islam. If I open up a little more, some people will leave. That's why, I leave it. You must have become tired. MashaAllah 3 hours is not an easy matter. I am ashamed of sitting and talking here. This son of ours talked standing for 3 hours. If we can't stand for 3 minutes, how can we be Ottoman? We are an old one full of energy, hale and hearty alhamdulillah. And inshaAllahi r-Rahman, Sultan of this place Mawlana Jalaluddin Rumi, his spirituality is upon me since the time I entered here. He is giving support with that spirituality. I can hear his good tidings. If he is wild criminal, Mawlana will have been criminal too. He can't be. Because if the truth is criminal of course, it won't be written as a good note for our nation.

And our nation always defended the truth and showed the highest tolerance. It has carried the flag of Islam for 700 years, has built the greatest government and been praised. We are a nation praised by RasulAllah. What can we ask beyond it? However, our duty hasn't finished yet. We are in a break now. When the break is over, mehter band will say "Stand at attention! Go! Ya Allah!" and we will go. Until where? Until the red apple. Where is the red apple? The heart of Rome - the conquest of Europe.

Wa min Allahi t-tawfeeq.
