Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

A’ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm:

(Qur’ān 07:170). Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla says, ‘Inna la nudee’u ajra-l musliheen’, ‘Certainly, We shall never waste the reward of those who do righteous deeds.’ Allah ‘Azza wa-Jalla says that the things that good people do are not wasted. As this world is the world of tests, all kinds of situations occur. And these situations are always beneficial and always rewarding for Muslims and pious people. Allah ‘Azza wa-Jalla doesn’t lose that Thawab and reward. It is kept in the presence of Allah ‘Azza wa-Jalla.

There are so many troubles that people have all over the world. These troubles go uselessly and are harmful for many people. People who are with Allah ‘Azza wa-Jalla take benefit from these troubles. Troubles are beneficial for them, goodness is beneficial; everything is beneficial for those who are Muslim. The rest are like pouring water into a bucket without a bottom; it doesn’t get full.

Everything is saved for Muslims. Even for the tiniest problem, reward is written in the presence of Allah. People keep forgetting this. Muslims must be reminded that these troubles will turn out as a great reward, a great Thawab and benefit in Akhirah. Your life is passing without you noticing it. But whoever is with Allah, his life is full. It’s full with goodness and Thawab. Otherwise, there is no benefit.

There are many sinful people, because they are away from Allah. They live as they like. They act as they like. They think that they are doing goodness. They say, “I am successful. I am ruling over the world. I did this, I did that”. But as they don’t believe in Allah, all of them are empty. All of them are meaningless and useless here and hereafter. They are full of sins and full of evil. When they look at their punishment, they will be astonished. They will think that they did goodness, but there is nothing good, nothing but evil.

Therefore, who are with Allah shouldn’t get upset during these difficult days. They have a great benefit for Akhirah. May Allah not separate us from this way. And may He not deprive us from these rewards insha’Allah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
