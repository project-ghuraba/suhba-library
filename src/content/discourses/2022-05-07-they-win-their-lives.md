As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Alā inna awliyā’a Llāhi lā khawfun ‘alayhim wa-lā hum yaḥzanūn" (10:62). says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Awliyā’u Llāh, the beloved servants of AllāhNone have no fear at all, no sadness, no sorrow, no worry. They are people loved by AllāhNone. Their ranks are very high.

Today we are gathering for the memory of our beautiful Shaykh. May AllāhNone increase his maqām. We love him. "Al-mar’u ma‘a man aḥabb." A servant is with whom he loves, says our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Therefore, for your love and for coming here in order to have his blessings and love, AllāhNone will reward you, by permission of AllāhNone. We will be together in paradise, in shā’a Llāh. That is the biggest reward.

People of the present time think that death is the end. There is a new trend now; people don't say someone passed away or someone died. They made up something more polite. They say someone "lost his life". Many people lose their lives. Awliyā’ and believers are those who don't lose their lives. They have won Ḥaqqu l-Ḥayāt (real life). They are those who attained eternal life. Those who lost their lives, wasted themselves for nothing, their end is disappointment. They are those in loss. In profit are the friends of AllāhNone, Awliyā’u Llāh, beloved people of AllāhNone. They don't lose their lives. They have won their lives. AllāhNone created people in this world not for losing their lives but for winning their lives. Therefore, those who are together with Awliyā’ profit, by permission of AllāhNone.

Eight years have passed since his passing away. Days are passing instantly. Life is passing. That is why don't say, "I am young. I am old." Everyone's breath is counted. No matter how long you live, your breath will finish eventually. Therefore, be careful. Always do things that AllāhNone likes. Be with people whom AllāhNone loves. Don't do things and don't be with people Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla doesn't love. "Al-ḥubbu fī Llāh wa-l-bughḍu fī Llāh." Love for AllāhNone and hate for AllāhNone. If you dislike someone, of course you can't like the one who goes against AllāhNone. You must love the one who loves AllāhNone. So today is the blessed day when our Shaykh departed to the presence of ḤaqqNone. Therefore, in shā’a Llāh, your service will be accepted. We should walk on his way. We shouldn't separate from his way. Mawlānā says the way of ṭarīqah is the way of striving. It is not the way of laziness. Mashāyikh say: "Those who get tired are not from us, those who stop are not from us." That is why... It is okay when you just join ṭarīqah, you don't know anything. You must learn something slowly every day. If you do more, it is good. If you don't, you will still learn something. You must learn what is good and bad, what is ḥalāl and ḥarām. Because the worship you perform goes to your Shaykh as well, by permission of AllāhNone. Because those who reach guidance through him... Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says through our Holy ProphetNone that whoever becomes a means of guidance for someone, he takes from his reward. Nothing is taken from a person who provided guidance.

Therefore, shukr to AllāhNone this way is everlasting. It is alive. For the barakah of the shaykhs, for the barakah of our Shaykh this way will give guidance and goodness to those who come here until qiyāmah, in shā’a Llāh. There will be guidance and the way, in shā’a Llāh. May AllāhNone increase his maqām. We will be together in paradise, in shā’a Llāh.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Alā inna awliyā’a Llāhi lā khawfun ‘alayhim wa-lā hum yaḥzanūn" (10:62). Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying in Holy Qur’ān: beloved ones for AllāhNone they don't have any sadness, any fear, any thing bad for them. Nothing affect them. This is [how] AllāhNone describes HisNone beloved ones, beloved people, beloved men, beloved women. For AllāhNone this like this. They are with AllāhNone and AllāhNone bless them.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh we are gathering here today for seventh of May, for Mawlid, ‘Urs of Mawlānā. All of you coming here for his love and this love, in shā’a Llāh, with saying of ProphetNone, who is the most truth one in universe, "Al-mar’u ma‘a man aḥabb." Mawlānā Shaykh, in shā’a Llāh, we will be with him, in shā’a Llāh, from time of closing our eyes and, in shā’a Llāh, we be with him. This is very important time because now from his eight year quickly pass away. And, but his way, not his way, way of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam continue and who think it finish with Mawlānā Shaykh, they are not believe for Mawlānā. For this we are, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are, what we said we are happy to be with him because nobody live forever in this life even Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam when last days of hisNone life, in this bodily life in here, it was so difficult for whole Ṣaḥābah, for whole family, for hisNone daughter, for hisNone grandson, and but at the end ProphetNone saying "This is life and you will be after me. In shā’a Llāh, we also we love Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, and Sayyidunā Mawlānā Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim and, in shā’a Llāh, we meet also in Ḥawḍu l-Kawthar all together.

AllāhNone accept your coming and reward you and make more barakah for you, for your family, for all. Keep you safe from every badness and give us strong īmān, in shā’a Llāh, to be in this way, serving this way for whole life. Because Mawlānā many times he said, "Mu’min must be hard worker." He said, "Who stop, not walking in this way, he is not from us. Who is tired also, he is not from our way." So every day you must learn little bit, little bit, little bit – more and more and more, in shā’a Llāh. And many people coming, they don't know anything, also every day even one thing, small thing, this is still you are going. So, in shā’a Llāh, this way, light way, nūr, way of nūr, in shā’a Llāh, for paradise. AllāhNone keep us and help us, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
