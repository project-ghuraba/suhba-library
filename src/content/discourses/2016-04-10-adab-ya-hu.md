MashaAllah. Allah, He is the one giving us the permission to go on. Today if you are sitting here in the masjid of Allah Almighty, in His house, this is by His permission. Don't think that you are coming by yourself. Don't think that you have iman [faith] by yourself. Don't think that you are making worship by yourself. All being granted by permission from the Lord Allah Almighty. This is important to understand. So many people, they have misunderstanding. And what they are misunderstanding? They say "We are the one having iman. We are the one praying to Allah Almighty. We are the one in service of Allah Almighty." Don't misunderstand and don't make credit for yourself.

We are alhamdulillah been named as Muslim. What's meaning Muslim? Meaning the one he surrender to his Lord - that his Lord will do anything to him and [he will] say "Ya Rabbi, I am for You. Whatever Your command, whatever Your order, I will be following." We are people that we are first surrendering and then we will come for the stage of belief that's called 'iman'. Allah Almighty, He will never be growing up or coming more magnificent by your prayer and never going to be more than He is with your prayer. Your prayer only make you higher in your level. And with this dhikr and prayer, you are coming nearer to Allah Almighty that it makes you higher and higher and higher. It is important for us to understand what we are doing. It's important for us what is our size, to know our size.

In those days, we are suffering from the new generation Muslim - either ISIS, either wahhabi, either salafi, either other trends that it is getting the people to be proud about what they are doing. They don't pray much. They pray only fard and they say Allah Almighty He only order fard. And with this 2-rakat or 4- rakat fard, when they say "Allahu Akbar", they say "Oh we are the one creating this planet" immediately. And they say "your prayer is not good, your dhikr is bidah, your this whatever. Don't go there, don't come there." And they start to make fatwa and make so many kind of things that Allah Almighty never agree.

The Ottomans, when they begin with their tradition and when they became as empire, they never left the sufism, the spirituality. Every corner was zawiya, was dergah. And written in the head of zawiya or dergah, it was "Adab ya Hu". That means you have to behave your good manner before entering here. We will never accept you in bad manner. Before you're entering dergah, first you have to say "I have to behave as good mannered one and I have to be the last one coming here. I am not the first one". They say "Allahu Akbar. I am the first one". No. Say you have to be the last one, you have to start with the cleaning the toilet to make your ego down. This is how it was and how this nation become great nation. Because they've been behaving as adab. And they say you have to look for yourself and to teach yourself how to be good one. Not before you're teaching yourself to be, how to be good one, you teach other how to be good one. This is never accepted, never been allowed.

And this they say "No, no, no. If you read Quran and hadith, you will be professor". Ya Hu, if you are studying in university for example and you are student of university and you go outside say "I am the one professor of this" - everybody will laugh on you, say "You have long way to go." What? This is not even... www.saltanat.org Page 1 You are not beginning from the first class and you become professor? This is how they make our religion to be like easy-going or make so many fatwa, make so many things. It is not logical, it is not the things that people, they cannot balance it. If there is no balance, it will be no religion. This is what they are thinking.

Because the enemy of Islam, the enemy of RasulAllah (saw), the enemy of Allah Almighty, they have been since Sayyidina Muhammad (saw) born. And till today they are trying to finish this religion. And they will never, they will never have chance to do it because Allah Almighty, He is the one protecting this religion. May Allah forgive us and make us in this straight way and make us as much as we can with RasulAllah (saw), with Allah Almighty and with our religion and with our murshid.

Wa min Allahi t-tawfiq. Bi hurmati l-Habib, bi hurmati l-Fatiha.
