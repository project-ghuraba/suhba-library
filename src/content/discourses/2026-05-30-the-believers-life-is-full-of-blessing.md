As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
وَمَا الْحَيَاةُ الدُّنْيَا إِلَّا لَعِبٌ وَلَهْوٌ ۖ وَلَلدَّارُ الْآخِرَةُ خَيْرٌ لِّلَّذِينَ يَتَّقُونَ
<br>“Wa mā l-ḥayātu d-dunyā illā laʿibun wa lahwun. Wa la-d-dāru l-ākhiratu khayrun li-lladhīna yattaqūna. A fa-lā taʿqilūn.”<br>
“And the life of this world is nothing but play and amusement. But far better is the house in the Hereafter for those who are Al-Muttaqun (the pious).” (Qurʼān 6:32). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says, “This world is a game, an amusement, and the Hereafter is better for those who fear Allah.” Shukr to Allah, with Allah’s beneficence, during these beautiful and blessed days, we were together with believers and Muslims on the way of Allah. There were visitors, and we visited also, shukr to Allah.

Sacrifices (qurbān) were slaughtered. Ḥajjīs performed their Ḥajj. This is the highest degree of happiness. It cannot be attained with the amusements of this world. A person can still be happy. When they do what they want, as long as it is ḥalāl, those deeds become rewards for them. As long as they are on the way of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla, whatever good things there are, whatever pleases a person, as long as it is ḥalāl, Allah both records them as rewards for the person and bestows gifts upon them. They are grateful for the favors He has given, and their life is not wasted. It becomes full for the Hereafter. All these things become a source of joy for the Hereafter, accumulating for the Hereafter.

Therefore, these blessed days have passed yet again. Days pass quickly. But shukr to Allah, may they not pass in vain, inshā’Allāh. May they not pass in vain. What we mean by “full,” it means that every breath is beneficial when always remembering Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. It is not wasted, it is not empty. When you do not remember Allah, when He does not come to your mind, everything you do is wasted. Even if the whole world belongs to you, even if all people revere you and love you, if you are not on the way of Allah, it has no importance, no benefit whatsoever.

Therefore, this way is the beautiful way of Allah, the blessed way of Islām. On that way, the way of our Prophet, the more you revere and exalt him, the more you will be exalted in the sight of Allah. His way is beautiful, all beauty is in him. Generosity is in him, caring for the weak is in him, he cares for the poor, for his ummah. He will be an intercessor in the hereafter. He will always intercede for his ummah in front of Allah.

Therefore, his honor is immeasurable. We cannot give our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam his due. The more you honor him, the more you will be honored, the higher you will rise, the more you will be exalted. If you do not honor our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, your worth and value will decrease. You will have no value left. 

Shayṭān also deceives Muslims. Even if he doesn’t lead them astray from the religion, he at least strives to destroy what those with religion have gained, saying, “Beware! Do not honor him. The Prophet is like you.” It is detrimental for people to not love our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. It is harmful, detrimental, and nothing else.

Therefore, showing respect to our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam is beneficial and profitable. May Allah grant us continued success. May we attain his intercession, inshā’Allāh. Without his intercession, our situation is difficult. Whoever attains his intercession is saved.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
