As-Salāmu ‘Alaykum wa RaḥmatuLlāhi wa Barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu BiLlāhi Minash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. BismiLlāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm.

Wa ṣ-Salātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Akhirīn.

Madad yā RasūlAllāh, Madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi RasūliLlāh, Madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

Dastūr Mawlana Shaykh Abdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistāni, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-

Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-Suḥbah wa l-Khayru fi l-Jam‘iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm:

(Qur’ān 06:21). ‘Wa man azlamu mim’man iftara a’la Allāhi kadhibāan aw kadhdhaba bi-āyātihi’, ‘And who is more unjust than one who invents about Allah a lie or denies His verses?’

Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla says that the biggest oppression is to talk in the name of Allah and lie. Allah doesn’t like those people, the oppressors. Why? Because what they show to people as true is false and useless. There are all kinds of badness in it. Because what Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla says doesn’t correspond with what people say. People must obey what Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla says. Those who make something up and say, “Let’s do this. It is from Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla” and cheat people are oppressors. No one can be more oppressive than them.

Some things have no importance for the people of Dunya. Where does that come from? Because of the lack of Iman, they don’t give importance to important things. They think, “We are saying it anyway. No one can do anything to us.” They don’t know that Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla controls people every instant, every minute, every second. Everything they say is written down. There’s no need to write it down. There are angels on the right and on the left. There’s no need at all, but Allah knows. And that will be proof for those people. When they say “I didn’t do so”, everything they did will be written there. You told a lie and did some things according to your mind in the name of Allah. You didn’t do what Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla wants. You did the opposite of what He says, and you pretended that Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla had said that. Every minute, every second there are angels that write down the things you did. You can deny as much as you want. Certainly, that will be put in front of you.

Therefore, a person who speaks on behalf of others and lies by saying “He said this and he said that” has no salvation. Unless he repents and asks forgiveness, he will be punished. There is punishment for such oppression in this world and in Akhirah as well. Don’t think that you will tell lies and be saved. It is possible that you escape once. But at last, if you don’t repent and keep doing so, you will be punished.

May Allah protect us. May we walk on His way, on the way that Allah likes. May He help us in carrying out His orders on the beautiful way ordered by Him. May we not obey our egos. May we use our minds. A person who does evil is stupid, he doesn’t use his mind. A person who uses his mind does nothing but goodness. May Allah make us all successful in things like this, because success is from Allah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
