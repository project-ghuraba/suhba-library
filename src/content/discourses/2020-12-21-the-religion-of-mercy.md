Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

The basis of Islam is mercy. Mercy is the attribute of Islam, it’s the attribute of Allah. People should be merciful. It is an order for Muslims anyway. Our Holy Prophet says: “ءامسلا يف نم مكمحري ضرلأا يف نْ مَ اومحرْ ا”, “Have mercy on those on earth so that He Who is in heaven will have mercy on you.”

Islam is the religion of mercy, compassion and help to people. Those that are outside of it should be like that too but they’re hypocrites, liars, traitors, and they do not have mercy. They act with hypocrisy. They show Islam as a bad and oppressing religion. Whereas Islam is a religion of mercy. They know everything clearly, but since they are the soldiers of shaytan they show evil as good. They say that they’re doing good and they are not oppressing. The evil they do will not remain. But they show Islam as bad so that people stay away from it.

In the end, Allah will show the truth. It is the end of times now, oppression has increased. The Sahib will come and everything will be clear. The truth will win. We are sad and people are sad when the truth is shown as bad. But the truth is the truth. The false will never overcome the truth. Allah willing, this truth will come out soon insha’Allah and all the oppressors will be erased from this world. It is the promise of Allah to our Holy Prophet. Everywhere will be full of light, it will be illuminated with the light of Islam insha’Allah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
