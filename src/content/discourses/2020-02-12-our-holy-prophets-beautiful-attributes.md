Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

We were reading the beautiful attributes of our Holy Prophet ﷺ yesterday. We should try to imitate those attributes. We should try to do like him. Of course we can’t do all of them, but we should do as much as we can. One of the attributes of our Holy Prophet ﷺ is patience. And another one is to always be smiling, to smile and put people at ease. When you smile, people relax and an interaction happens. A frowning, terrible face makes people run away, it demoralizes people.

Therefore, it would be beautiful to have the attributes of our Holy Prophet ﷺ. Your works will be more at ease and more beautiful among family, neighbors and people; you will help people. That’s why imitating the attributes of our Holy Prophet ﷺ is a great favor, a great honor and a benefit to us. Imitating him and performing his Sunnah are rewards. Even smiling indicates that you follow the Sunnah. When it is done with that intention, rewards are written for every Sunnah. There is the reward of a hundred martyrs for some forgotten Sunnahs. Therefore, we should intend to do all the Sunnahs of our Holy Prophet ﷺ so that Azza wa Jalla gives us according to the intention.

Our deeds are of no use. If you do not honor and respect our Holy Prophet ﷺ, if you do not love our Holy Prophet ﷺ, it is of no use to you. Therefore, good morals, goodness and beauty, what we call Sunnahs, are all in our Holy Prophet ﷺ. Both ugliness and evil are in the shaytan. That’s why we follow our Holy Prophet ﷺ and imitate him insha’Allah. May Allah ﷻ help us all and accept the Sunnahs that we do insha’Allah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
