Shaykh Bahauddin sohbat of the 25th of May, 2014.

Masha'Allah! Alhamdulillah! Shukr Ya Rabbi Shukr Alhamdulillah. Tawba Ya Rabbi, tawba astaghfirullah.

It is our tawba day. If we are lucky we can say "tawba Ya Rabbi." If we are unlucky we don't have this chance to say "tawba Ya Rabbi." And if we are more lucky we say"Shukr Ya Rabbi, Shukr Ya Rabbi Alhamdulillah!" If we don't have more luck we cannot say Shukr Ya Rabbi, Shukr. It is important to remember the tawba (repentence) and remember the shukr (thanks). It is very important for us to know what we've got and to know what Allah Almighty He presents to us. It is very important things to know.

Sometime they give you a form and they write so many things, but they put important notes - they write it in red color - important notes. Now what I'm doing I am not bringing all the books for you. I am giving you the important notes. Because we are such a people maybe we read much but we cannot understand much. But when you put "important notes" could be more effective than the book. It is very important to know that the key point is very important in our life. So Mawlana Shaykh used to teach us the key point that makes us to switch on and shape up! This is, maybe some people..

It reminds me of a very nice story of Imam Ghazali. Imam Ghazali is a huge Imam and full of knowledge. And one day he was writing, writing, writing and in that night one fly comes and drops in his ink. And when he sees that fly and he remembered that was the birthday of Rasulullah (saws), Mawlid day. And he said "for the Prophet's sake I'm saving this fly"and he put it aside. From that secret, that he gave respect to Rasulullah by not killing that fly, Allah Almighty He opened up for him the spirituality.

Don't underestimate anything. This is key point. This is how they make the forbidden knowledge to be available. Or the difficult knowledge to be available. Or the spiritual knowledge to be available. So they put the secret in very small details that if you catch it, you will be ok. And Mawlana Shaykh he was presenting this key for us, on golden table, on golden plate. It is a secret. They call it secret. Everybody thinks "what is secret?" It 2 words between you and me? It is not. The secret is the power that Allah Almighty He gave it for certain servant to be present for others. And that secret it will be as a power approaching that person. And from that person it will be seen. It is not - it is called secret, only named as secret. It is not secret actually. But it is the hidden power that Allah Almighty He give for that person.

And so many people they say "how to understand such a thing?" If you are following Tariqa, if you're following Shaykh, then you understand. how to get this secret. It is like you are going for the market and you are looking. You see tomato, you say, "I need tomato? No we have at home. You look. We need cucumber? No we have at home. We need meat? No it's at home. Then, while walking you see a closed shop and say "You need shirt? No I have at home. No, it is not this one!" So you go for such a shop and you see something that you are really in need, and "yes, I find it!" Then you take it. So you've been searching and searching... until you find the right things that you need.

But if you have a Shaykh he will tell you "you go for that shop. You go for that area, place and you get your things from there immediately without wasting any time. This is the difference about by searching by yourself and by having Shaykh to give you the full service. And telling you this is the secret that you need. And you put it in your heart and be showing for all over the world to say "yes it is our secret! Alhamdulillah! This secret is belonging to me."

www.saltanat.org Page 1 Because every person has his own secret belonging to him. It is available in the shop! It is available in the shop! But, it is our life - will it be enough to go for that shop and to find that secret, the one that belongs to us? This is the question matter. Big question mark, our life to go all this way in this market and to find the right shop. The right area, the right place to get our right secret. It could be a coincidently happening - not on purpose. But if you have a Shaykh, you go, you take it and you enjoy it all your life. Alhamdulillah like Mawlana Shaykh he give us this enjoyment.

Very important to know. This is key point! It is important to know that when you have a Shaykh you will be delivered your secret. You don't have to go for your secret because he is keeping your secret with him and he gives it to you. And he makes it to be in safinat al najat, that's the safe boat that he is providing also. And you will be, here and hereafter, very relaxed, because here maybe we have some difficulties but we know that we are going for better address. We know that we are going for love of Allah Almighty, for Rasulullah. And this is rubbish life - it is whatever you do, it will have ending. Even you are in good condition or in bad condition, it doesn't matter.

This is the good things here when you have your secret with you. If you've been in very good condition you'd be stable. If you're in very bad condition also you'd be stable. Because the secret is, putting you in the safe boat. And we are asking from Allah Almighty to give us the blessing of Mawlana and to give us the beauty of Mawlana and to give us the stable stability that Mawlana he put in our heart. And make us sincere for him and make us sincere to Sh. Mehmet that he's been appointed by Mawlana Shaykh and we are believer and we are sincere followers insha'Allah.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfiq, bi hurmati l-Habib bi hurmati l-Fatiha.
