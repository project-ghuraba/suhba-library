Shaykh Mehmet: Audhu Billahi min al shaytan al rajim, Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Assalatu wa s-salamu ala Rasulina Muhammad Sayyidi l Awwalin wa l-Akhirin. Madad Ya Rasulullah, Madad Ya Ashabu Rasulullah. Madad Ya S. Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, madad Ya S. Nazim al Haqqani, dastur! Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fil jamia' We make a short sohbat insha Allah.

When we went to Shaykh Effendi in the morning, he said "May your hand always be in khayr-goodness. As long as you are in this world, do khayr/charity works. Our mureeds may always stay away from the profits of dunya & look to akhirat. The works of dunya, unless they are in the way of Allah, are loss, 100% loss, have no gain.

We saw this in Turkey; people who had not millions but billions dollars passed away. But there is nothing with them that they did for Allah. These peoples' akhirat, instead of being so prosperous, in fact there is nothing because they didn't give their zakat or didn't go to Hajj, although they had all that money. Allah knows, we don't know if they fasted or not.. He was the richest one in dunya, but is considered to be the most terrible one in akhirat. All this money, wealth, all is a burden on him, crushes him in akhirat.

Mawlana says "dunya is a field for booty". When there was war in Cyprus, everybody attacked all places for war booty. They collected it - meaning all the shameless ones, all collected it. But that booty is nothing, useless. The booty for akhirat is the eternal one. Therefore like our Prophet (sas), Mawlana & all awliyas said; "dunya is a place of trade, but for those who think about Akhirat. For those who never think about akhirat the more money they collect, the more loss it is for them. Always make yourself love akhirat, disincline to dunya, says the Prophet.

In dunya, it is possible that one can be rich. As long as he uses it for Allah he maybe as rich as he likes more booty for him. The more booty he has, the more sawab-good deeds he will get. But dunya should only be used as a wasila-means. Our target is not to win the dunya but only akhirat. And it is only a wasila to make our akhirat prosperous, with sadaqa, zakat, charity... A person who thinks about this & understands that this dunya is temporary & who has faith in akhirat does not care at all about the hardships of dunya, they come easy to him. No matter how painful & difficult is the dunya life, it comes easy to him. Therefore Mawlana always says in his sohbats "remind about akhirat. Make people love akhirat & the works done for akhirat". This should always be like this in people's head- "dunya is a field for ghanimat/booty. Do everything for Allah. Do not take Allah out of your mind even for a second".

Today when I was speaking, Shaykh Effendi said to remind people for last days. You must love not dunya, you must love last days. This dunya, it is like trade center for people. You must do everything for akhirat. Who only doing for dunya, he is loser- loser trader.

We have in Turkey before 2 years, before 4 or 5 years, there was very very rich people. They lived & they died. And there was not millions dollars, billion dollars they have. But they are loser. Why? Because even they not give zakat for their money. Not making any charity. Not making even one mosque, one looking for poor people, nothing at all. Just what they do, they bring from Europe or from other country; allegro, sinfonigro..something like this, we hear for this. This is loser. Because Allah give them all this. And when they will be in Judgment Day they will be asked for all of this, it will be like mountain on them, very heavy for them. In dunya also, they are not, I don't think they were happy.

For this Allah & Prophet, Awliya, all.. Allah (awj) told Prophet & awliya & good people to look for akhirat, for last day. In Quran also, there are many ayah. They said, "Akhirat better than dunya". But in dunya, it is like when going to war. In war, after war people taking this from enemy, it is halal but they take as much as they can take. This dunya like this for mu'min, who remember akhirat. Mawlana Shaykh said, "it is ghanimat". When you say ghanimat, it is people very happy to take it. When you are in dunya you must make charity, you must make all good thing to be in akhirat very rich man. Who is in this dunya rich without giving charity or giving... We don't need...They, people even...Allah... First, before to give charity, you must give your zakat. Zakat for billion dollars, it must be millions dollars, like this. But people, when become more rich, they become more stingy. And when you, even our people..

There is one important point also here. When giving charity, first you must say "this is zakat". Because zakat, it is obligatory. Charity, it is good but for to keep you, your children & other protected. But zakat, it is obligatory. You will be asked for this. You must do this. But many people, when they looking "this is too much, why will we do this". It is very important point, first to collect your zakat. If you don't know, most of them you say for zakat if you give for poor people. It is zakat ok. Finish your zakat, it will be charity. This is Mawlana Shaykh saying to tell people, "everytime to love akhirat, last day more than dunya". All sohbats tell this for people. And when people, they have very big wisdom- Mawlana & awliya.

When be, you are believe in akhirat & doing this, every difficulty - you cannot feel it in dunya. Just for Allah & life is very short. Quickly, quickly you not feel any difficulty. Even from illness, from poorness, or from... You cannot feel anything when you believe in akhirat & doing for this. This is big wisdom from Mawlana today.

Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, Al Fatiha.
