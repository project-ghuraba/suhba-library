Shaykh Mehmet: As-Salamu Alaikum. Audhu Billahi min al shaytan al rajim, Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Madad ya Rasul Allah, Madad ya S Abdullah F. Daghestani, madad ya S. Muhammad Nazim al Haqqani, dastur! Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fil jamia' Upon the sohbat order of our Tariqat we may say a few words with the intention of sohbat insha Allah.

"Khayr al-Umur Awsatuha" said the Prophet (sas) in his tradition. And the Holy Verse says: "Ja`alnakum 'Ummatan Wasataan" (2:143). What does this mean? It says "find the middle way in everything". It says, "don't go too extreme". Now some people who enter Tariqat, or who start working on something they put all their efforts all at once saying "I will do all of it, I will reach the highest point". And when they do like this, man's power is up to a point. When they spend it all at once, then at this time they can't find the middle way and totally quit it. Therefore don't go too extreme in anything, says the Prophet (sas).

It may be normal, average. And for worshipping/ibadah, to be continous. "Ajalla al-karamat dawam at-tawfiq" says the Hadith. If there are worships/ibadah you do consistently it is the biggest karamat/miracle for mu'min. Even if it is little, it will continue the same way. Doing it excessively & then quit, or not doing it at all, it is not good either. You will go the middle way, meaning you won't get too tired.

And there is also total laziness, always laying around lazily. It is no good either, being lazy is not good. If you become lazy, you have no value. Dunya work is also the same way. In dunya if you can work, you will do. Shouldn't sit lazily when there is work to do, saying "I don't like this work". And when you are out of money, to say "I will go & get it from someone else, I may pay him back later". This is no good either. Laziness is also no good. Mashayikh don't like laziness either. Laziness brings all kind of musibah/ afflictions on people. A lazy person borrows from everybody, as we said. He borrows 1 time, 2. time, 3. time.. You are healthy & capable of work. You have a good head too.

Either work & make money, & spend according to what you can make. Or since you have a good head, do your own business. Or work for someone, again to ensure your family's livelihood. Don't borrow from this that & be disgraced. Some say halal when you can't pay back, but some say haram. At that time, what you eat & spend with that money become haram for you. Therefore one should go the middle way. It is not good to show to disfavor certain jobs.When you earn halal money you can be a shepherd or a garbage man. You can do anything as long as it is halal. That halal money becomes barakah/blessing for you.

And the show off..Even if you are rich, don't go extreme in show off this time. When you show off, big bala/cursings come on people. Because as Mawlana says, peoples' eyes are like poison. When showing off for this that, later that thing become like a poison for him. It becomes useless. On the contrary it brings harm, not benefit.

If people followed the best things that Islam teaches- Prophet's (sas) order, Islam's orders, they would live in peace. But everybody lives according to their own customs. Every country has a custom. One country, it is luxurious. Other, doesn't do any work.. All different, in different positions. But the best way to describe Islam in brief "the best of all affairs is the middle path".

Furthermore the Prophet (sas) says, "When you love a person don't love too much. It can be that he becomes your enemy one day. If you love him too much & tell him all your secrets if he becomes your enemy one day, then he knows all your secrets. And for your enemy, don't hate him too much it can be that one day he becomes your friend. Meaning, Prophet (sas) says "even for friendship/enmity, keep the middle way". This is a very big advice. If people think on this, it is enough for them.

Today we speak about, to how to live. Prophet (sas) says: "Be in middle". Don't go exteme. There is Ayat also. First Hadith, "Khayr al-Umur Awsatuha" Ayat, ""Ja`alnakum 'Ummatan Wasataan" (2:143). We are ummah, middle. Not extreme, not very this side or this side. Only middle you must be. This is for everything, even for ibadah also. Because when people coming to Islam first, or to come to Tariqat sometimes they try to do everything as awliya, or mashayikh doing. They want to do this. But you cannot do this. If you try to do this,you maybe try 1 month, 2 months. After you say "it's too much".They leave everything. Not even do one small thing. So you can do only what you can do,

it is biggest karamah, fadhila like virtue for mureed, to be in same level. It is the biggest virtue. But there is also another side of this. They are very lazy. We not doing this. No, you must do. One level, but not so high not so low. You must be on one level. But even the lowest level, to pray 5 times prayer- this is lowest for mureed.

And can do little bit dhikr, tasbeeh daily. This is for Tariqat or for..Because some people I hear, they say.. They looking, they saying "we cannot be so high like this. It is if they asking, what is the matter. We are wrong for this. We love Prophet, we love Mashayikh Mawlana Shaykh, but we see some people, they are very love, but this is wrong for us?" No, this is normal. Because first when you come to Tariqat, mashayekh they giving jazbah/attraction. It is, for everybody this happen. First 1 month, 2 month, maybe 1 year too much they going. But after, the mashayekh they take this. It is enough for people. So will be normal. So some people wondering if it is normal or not. Yes, it is normal. Don't worry for this. This is normal-wasat/middle for everybody.

For dunya also, when you.. you must do some work to live. If you have good job, ok. If no, don't be lazy. You can do any work for halal work. Not haram like gambling or other, this bad thing. For halal, even you will be shepherd, or working to clean road. Everything it is better than to ask from people. Because when you are lazy, what you do? You have children, you have family. You must bring money. Some people, they are taking loan from people. And this loan, you are lazy. Of course if you have money, you don't need loan. But you are lazy, you take loan. So you cannot..already you are not working. So how you will..if you work, you don't need for loan. You take loan, so you take this for just to big money. You not ask for if it is halal or haram. Some people, if you take loan they make charity it is ok. So even not nice, but it is ok. But some of them they are not making halal, this. They say "I want my money, I want my money" You don't give. This is bad for you & for your family. For children also. Even for, when you feed children or family haram, it becomes when they growing you cannot control them after this. So for this, middle. You must work also. Who...

But if you are, no need to be working for be richest, to be millionaire or something. No, just for living. Allah give barakah for this. And there is rich people also, they like to make to show off. People say "we are rich, we are.." So, this is also not good. Because there is eye, poisoning eye, Mawlana Shaykh said. When they see this and somebody looking and this posion coming, they cannot be anymore happy. This is Islam, there is rule in Islam. If you following this rule, you will be happy here & hereafter. And this is rule, one of rule to be in middle. This is also very important.

Don't be proud, don't be lazy. Don't be dirty..something like this. You must be in middle. Some people, they are very extremely clean. This is also not good. So maybe 10 times make wudhu. But if we make wudhu, we must make also by normal. You wash your first feet also. Many people, they not washing feet. This is very good, for everything this.

When you be in middle, you don't have burden in you. You don't take burden. If you want to be very high, you take more burden. So it is, Alhamdulillah Prophet also ordering this. We follow his order & sunnah. Allah help us insha Allah.

Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, Al Fatiha.
