The most important thing in life is for the Lebanese people to feel that AllāhNone has granted them – that Lebanon is beautiful. They love everything to be the best in dunyā and in spirituality also. But sometimes dunyā seems better so they forget the hereafter. For that we should remind them now that one has to sacrifice his comfort, sacrifice the pleasures of the dunyā. When he does that, he will save himself, his people, and the country, AllāhNone will guide him to goodness. But when he follows [his self], he forgets AllāhNone and the hereafter, so he becomes a slave to dunyā. The dunyā runs away from him and he becomes a despicable slave. But when he is a servant for AllāhNone, he is accepted.

By permission of AllāhNone, there are many young people here. It isn't the most important thing to them, nor is their purpose dunyā. Dunyā is a way to live in this life. If dunyā is not his aim, whatever he receives from it will be a blessing and a benefit for him; he will benefit from dunyā and not be in need of anything. While if his concern is the dunyā, he loses both dunyā and the hereafter. AllāhNone make us from those for whom dunyā is their last concern. AllāhNone grant us the dunyā – not in our hearts but to be our way to AllāhNone – goodness, worship, and the hereafter, in shā’a Llāh. This is required, in shā’a Llāh.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, the people of Lebanon have great willingness as I said. They love everything beautiful and nice, they meet for it and love doing it. But there are many people who show things that are not beautiful. They add decoration and makeup, so people think the ugly is beautiful and run after it. When one does that, he has no taste. Taste has to make known the natural beauty and run after it. Allāh Almighty is the beautiful and so is the ProphetNone. AllāhNone make us from the followers.

You people, AllāhNone save you, and the Muslim children in this country. Of course, Islām is an honour, glory and the greatest blessing for us, in shā’a Llāh. Nothing is greater than that. AllāhNone protect them – they are protected by permission of AllāhNone, and guide those who have lost the road and save them from the shayāṭīn of men and jinn and every disliked thing, in shā'a Llāh.

Wa min Allāhi ṭ-ṭawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
