Sheikh Muhammad Nazim Al-Haqqani An-Naqshibandi, Sohbat of the 28th of June, 1994.

... If you know these measures, you did wrong because you went out of Islam. When you're in Islam, you won't be mistaken, you'll be alert. "Min bayni yadayhi wa min khalfihi yahfadhunahu" (13:11) When you enter the circle of Islam, Allah Almighty appoints protecting angels. They protect you from what comes from the front and from what comes from the back. Your guards cannot protect you. Do you have guards? You say it is here, here, there, everywhere. Even if you go out armed, if the protecting angel opens the way, they will blow you up. Finished.

When you are inside the circle of Islam, that is a guarding circle, you'll be guarded. Allah Almighty assigned angels to: "Guard My servant from what comes from the front and from the back." If anything happens, say; "Tawba ya Rabbi, astaghfirullah. Ash'hadu an la ilaha illAllah. Ya Rabbi, I entered the circle" immediately. Be within the circle immediately. It hits you because you went out. You won't be hurt unless you are heedless. When you're heedless, you lose your humanity. Being heedless doesn't suit Muslims. When you're heedless, shaitan catches you with a hook. You're hooked when you smoke also.

How many Muslims there are... all day, all night long taking from that smoke of shaitan. A small thing, he got his hook in you and is pulling to make you fall. I will fight against smoking. You mustn't smoke. If something happens to you, know that you are out of the circle of Islam... There is neither beer, nor cigarettes in the circle of Islam. People normalized both of them. Even hodjas smoke. Hodjas will go to hell too. Not every hodja goes to paradise. There are many hodjas, who'll go to hell. Awliya won't go to hell. Is it open? If He doesn't treat us with gentleness, the one, who obeys his ego, will surely be slapped both in dunya and in akhira.

And stand with Islam. Guard the honor of Islam. Guard it when you sit also. Keep Islam in all circumstances. Don't leave Islam. This is what Allah wants. Whoever goes out of Islam, becomes a target for Allah's punishment. It's a short talk but it's enough for all nations, for the nation of Muhammad (sas). This is an advice for everyone. The circle of Islam is drawn by Allah. It's protection for individuals, for jamaats and communities, for nations and for governments. Governments went out of Islam too.

When a government goes out of Islam, it is finished. Finished. As long as they are out of it, they will never get Allah's approval. Whoever is out of Islam, is an enemy of Allah, of His Prophet (saw), of His book, of His religion and of His faith. Can he get any benefit? When he goes out of the circle of mercy and gentleness, divine punishment comes on him constantly. "So and so person is accursed." Is there anyone, who isn't accursed once he is out of Islam? "If that person comes, troubles come constantly. How many thousands of people are standing outside. What happens when millions are staying out of the circle of Islam and not entering?

If we make shukr, not raising our heads from sajda it is still not enough. Allah Almighty is still treating us with His gentleness. If Allah Almighty doesn't treat us with His gentleness, we would disappear from the face of the earth. For the honor of the pious servants, for the honor of the children, the (?) for the honor of the elderly ones, ya Rabbi keep us in the circle of Islam. Don't throw us out of the circle of gentleness ya Rabbi. Open Your doors of mercy ya Rabbi. Open Your doors of paradise ya Rabbi. Open Your doors of victory ya Rabbi. Open Your doors of guidance ya Rabbi. Open Your doors of grace ya Rabbi. Send Your support to all anbiyas and awliyas and mu'mins in this holy month ya Rabbi.

Bi hurmati l-Habib, bi hurmati l-Fatiha.
