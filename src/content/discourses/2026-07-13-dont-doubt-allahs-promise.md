As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
وَعْدُ اللَّهِ ۚ إِنَّ اللَّهَ لَا يُخْلِفُ الْمِيعَادَ
<br>“Waʿdullāh, innAllāha lā yukhlifu l-mīʿād.”<br>
“The Promise of Allah. Verily, Allah never breaks His Promise.” (Qurʼān 13:31). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

Everything Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla has promised will certainly come to pass.

Therefore, Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says, “Let there be no doubt or suspicion.” The promises and pledges of Allah will certainly be fulfilled. That’s why He warns people to be careful; He advises that those who have gone astray should return to the right way.

He sent the prophets for this reason. After them, the companions, scholars, and saints; they all came to save people. Because the way people were following was not the right way, He sent the prophets, saints, and scholars—all for the benefit of humanity—to save them. So that people would follow Allah’s way and not stray from His way. Because salvation is there. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla has promised Paradise and Hell.

Therefore, Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla invites us to Paradise. “Come,” says Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla, “Come to Paradise. Enter, enter into its beauty. This is a beautiful way. Stay away from evil, do not ruin yourselves, do not make yourselves miserable. Save yourselves.” His promise is true.

Now everyone lives according to their own minds. Shayṭān and his followers are not striving to save people, but to destroy them. They lead people astray by saying, “This is the way; there is nothing but this world. You came into existence on your own; there is no one who brought you into existence or can take you away from existence.”

However, it is impossible to be created without a creator. And it is Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla who created us. He is the All-Knowing; what is and what will be, everything is within Allah’s knowledge. He invites people to that way, the way of Allah. “My promise is true, be sure to pay attention to it. Follow the right way, the beautiful way. Stay away from the evil way, stay away from the dangerous way. Paradise awaits you.” What Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla has promised us is Paradise. He says, “Enter Paradise.”

May Allah never keep any of us away from this way. May we remain steadfast on this way. May we not stray from it because of the words of this and that, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
