As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Our Holy Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam says,<br>
خَيْرُكُمْ مَنْ تَعَلَّمَ القُرْآنَ وَعَلَّمَهُ
<br>“Khayrukum man taʿallama l-Qur’ān wa ʿallamah”<br>
“The best among you is he who learns and teaches the Qur’ān.” Ṣadaqa Rasulullah. 

The best among you is the one who learns and teaches the Qur’ān. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla has given each person a different ability, but He has also bestowed upon them all the ability to learn the Qur’ān.

As we have said, Qur’ān ʿAẓīmu sh-Sha’n is the greatest of the miracles of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. Because it is the blessed book of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla, which will remain forever, until the Day of Judgment, and which explains everything.

Therefore, learning and reading it is a great blessing for people. Not only Muslims, but often non-Muslims also read and study it out of curiosity. Many even become Muslim while studying it.

Of course, when learning the Qur’ān, some people may not be able to pronounce the letters correctly. If they can, then they have; even if they cannot, Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla will still accept it.

يَسِّرُوا ولا تُعَسِّرُوا
<br>“Yassirū wa lā tu'assiru”<br>
“Make things easy and do not make them difficult”. [Hadith]. 

“Yassirū” means to make it easy; grant ease, and let it be easy.

Sometimes people struggle and give up reading altogether, just trying to pronounce a letter correctly. They say, “I can’t pronounce this letter.” However, our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam said that no matter how well you pronounce the letter, the angels will correct those letters and present your recitation beautifully. Thus, it will be fully recorded in your book of deeds.

Therefore, our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam said, “Read and learn Qur’ān ʿAẓīmu sh-Sha’n; the best among you are those who learn it and teach it.”

Therefore, shukr to Allah, this summer, children are learning the Qur’ān. It would be very good if those who don’t know how to read the Qur’ān could at least learn along with them. Summer vacation is an opportunity.

Instead of going here and there – of course, they can go wherever they want and have fun – they should take advantage of this opportunity and learn the Qur’ān. Slowly, as they read as much as they can, they will progress and learn more each time.

Qur’ān ʿAẓīmu sh-Sha’n has a very miraculous aspect. People who don’t know any Arabic can memorize it even if they don’t understand its meaning. This is truly unique and unseen in anything else.

Of course, there are people who learn a foreign language and memorize other poems, or who read and learn books written by others even if they aren’t in their native language. But there is no other book in the world that can be memorized from beginning to end without knowing or understanding its meaning. There is no other book like this in the world; this is one of the miracles of Qur’ān ʿAẓīmu sh-Sha’n.

So, the fact that it can be memorized without knowing the language or understanding its meaning is actually very interesting to us.

Shukr to Allah, since we were born in Damascus, we know a little bit. However, I am also very surprised at how a Turk, a Persian, or someone from another nation who doesn’t know any Arabic can memorize it perfectly. But this is a miracle of the Qur’ān, that Allah makes it easy to memorize.

Thus, people memorize it perfectly even if they don’t know its meaning. May Allah fill our hearts with the light of Qur’ān ʿAẓīmu sh-Sha’n. May He keep it in the minds of the children who memorize it, so that they never forget it, inshā’Allāh; may we all not forget it, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
