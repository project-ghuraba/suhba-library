As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says in Qur’ān ʿAzimu sh-shān,<br>
قُلْ إِن كُنتُمْ تُحِبُّونَ اللَّهَ فَاتَّبِعُونِي يُحْبِبْكُمُ اللَّهُ وَيَغْفِرْ لَكُمْ ذُنُوبَكُمْ
<br>“Qul in kuntum tuḥibbūna Llāha fa-ttabiʿūnī yuḥbibkumullāh, wa-yaghfir lakum dhunūbakum”<br>
“Say [O Muhammad]: "If you love Allah then follow me, Allah will love you and forgive you of your sins."” (Qurʼān 3:31). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

It is said by our Holy Prophet: “If you love Allah, follow me. And Allah will love you.” The love of Allah is the most important thing. How will Allah love you? If you follow our Holy Prophet, He will love you. If you go on his way, He will love you. If you love him, He will love you.

Our Holy Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam was sent to everyone, to all humanity and the universe. People and jinns, everyone, even angels; our Holy Prophet is the Prophet for all of them. Who asks for the love and pleasure of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla, he should follow our Holy Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam.

They say, “We love humanity.” What do you love of humanity? Is there humanity left? There is no humanity anymore.

وَإِذَا الْوُحُوشُ حُشِرَتْ
<br>“Wa idha l-wuḥūshu ḥushirat”<br>
“And when the wild beasts are gathered.” (Qur’ān 81:05).

When the wild and savage appears in the universe, it shows the end of times. There is no humanity. Everyone is ready to harm others like wild creatures. And the greatest harm is to take people out of the right way, not to show them the right way. Accusing those on the right way of falsehood and cheating many people preventing them from this great blessing is the normal state of the present time.

Shayṭān is making up new tricks and fitnah so that Muslims don’t honor and respect our Holy Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. In order not to be close to our Holy Prophet shayṭān has cheated them. Every day he is cheating people more and more in many ways. Kāfirs (disbelievers) don’t have any belief anyway. All of them think we appeared like mushrooms. They don’t know about souls, ākhirah or dunyā. They are cheated even more. They’ve fallen in the traps of shayṭān.

Therefore, the love of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla is the most important thing for humanity and for people. For evey individual and for all humanity, honoring and believing in Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla is the sign of humanity. Without Him there is no humanity. 

Because there is nothing to be afraid of. There is nothing they hope for. They neither hope for paradise, nor fear hell. They say there is not such thing. But they will regret it later.

Certainly, mankind did not come to this world all of a sudden. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla created the body. He made it grow slowly in the mother’s womb. Then the soul entered that person’s body. With that soul, he becomes human. He becomes different from other creatures. Others are not like mankind. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla gave them different features.

Therefore, if you are a person, you must believe in Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla to be human, to know humanity. You must honor our Holy Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam to be saved. That is your salvation. Humanity has no other salvation. People have tried everything they could. Nothing brought any benefit. They are left with nothing. 

And the biggest sign of kufr is stubbornness. They can go till the end with their stubbornness. Yet they won’t believe. They will regret it then, but it won’t be of use.

Therefore, ask for the love of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. May we attain it, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
