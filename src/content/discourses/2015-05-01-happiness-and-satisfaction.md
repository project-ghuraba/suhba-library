Salamu alaikum.

Alhamdulillah. Alhamdulillah, al-hamd al-kamileen as-salatu wa s-salamu 'ala Sayyidina Muhammad, Sayyidi l-awwalina wa l-akhirin.

Today is Juma and today is the day of happiness. Today has been given to RasulAllah (sas). It is the most precious day for Allah Almighty. And Allah Almighty, He give it to RasulAllah (sas). The first one to say "I want to be the second day for me", it was Sayyidina Musa and he been taking Saturday. Until the other Prophet woke up, Sayyidina Isa, he said "I want the second day of Juma" and it was Sunday.

We are doing the dhikr after Juma and we want to catch the happiness of Juma. The real happiness of Juma, it's coming in Juma prayer time. Allah Almighty, He open up, open up and give, give, give. If the people they realize what Allah Almighty, He give, they will leave everything and wait for that moment that Allah Almighty, He give. We are heedless, we don't know. If we know what Allah Almighty, He give, we will be much different, we will be more elite, we will be more higher.

Allah Almighty, He is giving and He give you and He make you to be full, more than full. To not be shrewd to think what your brother take and what your neighbor take and what who takes. Allah Almighty, when He give, He make you to feel that you get more than everything. And this, it makes you more high in spiritual way, it makes you more high in your journey. And they say the danger will come from the hungry man, never coming from full-up man.

That's why they say the hungry one has a smell, bad smell in his mouth but actually it is not his mouth smell, this is his ego smell because the ego when it is hungry, it's coming so terrible, so bad, can do anything. That's why, my mother she used to tell us nice story about one kingdom they been fighting with other kingdom, but before they fight, they sent some questions. And I remember one of these questions that's saying they sent an eye, a human eye.

And they say make this human eye to put it in the balance, put it in the scale and put in front of it as much as you have from the wealth. So if you are able to make this scale to be in the wealth side, then "we will not fight with you". And the king, he says "Small, it is 50 gram. 50 gram. Cannot be more than 50 gram. What a simple question for us. So we win the war without fight". They brought the eye and put in the scale. And they brought all the wealth of king and they put in the scale. And the eye didn't move. It didn't make any small things. And the king - he was shocked, said "This eye is 50 gram, all my wealth, it didn't make it move. Bring the people wealth, bring everything." And they brought everything and didn't move. And there was one wise man. He said "I know the answer". "What you know? Look, this wealth and this small eye is never been come together".

He said, "O my king, I know the answer". "You crazy man, how you know the answer? Look at this wealth, look at this gold, look at everything, we couldn't make it move". He said, "If you give me permission, I will give the answer". And he was saying, "We don't have any choice, do what you like". And he went to the ground and he take little bit of earth and he put on the scale and the scale gone. He said "only the earth can make this hunger to be finished".

Beware, whatever you do, you have to look for the earth. Look for the earth and then try to do something, try to be something, and try to make something. It's very important. My mother, she was telling us this fairy tale but look for the wisdom. This, it is very important thing for moms that they are producing grown up children, to make them to understand to not be hungry, to be having the wisdom to be full. Maybe today they cannot get it but tomorrow they will. If they don't get it, they will get it the day after.

It is very important to teach the people to not be hungry because to brought up hungry, after that nothing will be satisfying. And this is the illness of this century. Nothing satisfies us. Allah Almighty, He gives everything. In one day you'll be in the farthest point of this earth. Maybe olden days, it would take them ages - one life to go for one day travelling. One age, one life can be lost for one travelling. Today Allah Almighty, He tells you in one day you go wherever you like. This, I'm travelling today, that's why I'm saying.

Today’s people, the worst ones in this time. He is better than one man before 100 years. He didn't have anything as this the worst [one] having. See how Allah Almighty, He is so Generous for us and He is giving us. So it's important to continue your 2-rakat shukr praying. You pray 2- rakat shukr and feel satisfied, feel happy, because we want everybody to be happy. The tariqa is the way to make you satisfied. That's why the tariqa is very important. That's why the tariqa is teaching you how to be satisfied and how to be good one.

When you are having the satisfaction, you will be definitely good one. This is the rule. If somebody having everything and he claim everything but he doesn't have satisfaction that means he is not full, he is not satisfied, he is nothing. That's why when you look for Mawlana Sheikh, you see how he was satisfied and he distribute the satisfaction. This is important. If you are sitting with Mawlana Sheikh you know he is distributing the satisfaction, that he is having more than enough. This is criteria to understand the level of somebody claiming that he is sheikh or he is something. Is he satisfied and distributing the satisfaction or he is all the time having ear for gossip what they are saying and he is not satisfied himself and you are also as his mureed not satisfied. This is criteria you must understand. May Allah forgive us and make us happy here and hereafter and make us to be with the true one. Mawlana Sheikh, even [despite] he was the true one he was saying, "Ya Rabbi, I am asking to keep with the true one".

This is, what Mawlana Sheikh was saying, "We want to be with the true one", he was claiming the Grandsheikh and claiming the Grandsheikhs and claiming RasulAllah and then Allah Almighty. And he was so humble, never claiming that "I am this or I am that". This is important - kibr. Allah Almighty, He hate the kibr, He hate the proudness. He hate it. If you are mutakkabir, if you are proud, Allah Almighty will hate you. Try to be humble for Allah Almighty, that Allah Almighty, He love you. Try to get the love of Allah Almighty. Don't try to be above the people. Try to be under the people.

Mawlana Sheikh with his greatness, he was below the people. Anybody coming to him, he was so humble to receive and he was so humble to send. And if necessary, he was making the shoes also for his guests. We see so many times. This is Mawlana Sheikh. But Allah Almighty He make him one of the greatest. This is important - to be humble, because your humblness, it will be to Allah Almighty, You're showing your humblness to His servant.

Wa min Allahi tawfeeq bi hurmati-l Habib, bi hurmati-l Fatiha
