Mankind is filled with secrets, my son. Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. The secret is inside the Basmala Sharif. May we pay attention to Basmala Sharif. There is a power in it, a secret in it. Much shukr to Allah. Marhaba! Ey yaran Shah-i Mardan! Allah Jalla wa Ala is sending the spiritualities that He loves from the Heavens to Earth. Happiness to those people who can receive them. Let's say, Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Allah is giving health and wellness to His Habib's ummah for the sake of His Habib. May we be granted our share. This has nothing to do with doctors, this has nothing to do with medicine. This is a Divine secret, it is a Divine favour.

Let's say, Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. The secret is inside this Basmala Sharif. Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Bismillahi alladhi la yadurru ma`a ismihi shay'un fil ardi wa la fi s-samaa wa Huwa as-Sami`u l-Alim. O our Lord! For the sake of Al-Habibu l-Kibriya, give us strength so that we can keep Your Command. Why do we want strength? For moving a car, for pulling a car? For keeping His command, His Holy command, Janab Mawla's honourable command. We ask strength in order to keep His command, not to be able to move a car; a carriage can be moved by animals.

The Commands of the Heavens are kept by humankind whom Janabu l-Haqq appointed and gave honour and strength. Where are we, where is that command, Sheikh Mehmet Efendi? Marhaba. May we understand, inshaAllah. Let's say, Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. To mention our Lord's Jalali name is of biggest concern. We are doing it according to our state. My son, Sheikh Mehmet Effendi, we do it according to our state. We can't reach beyond that.

Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Allahu Akbar wa Lillahi l-hamd. Much shukr. May we reach the light descending from the Heavens, by the blessings of our Prophet and, by the blessings of the awliya. Light descends every day, Mehmet. Light descends everyday, may we reach that light also. According to our state. The favours we are asking from our Lord are to be able to keep Janabu l-Mawla's Holy Command and to be able to carry that honour. Say, Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar wa Lillahi l-hamd.

All the angels tremble, when Allahu Akbar is recited. The angels of the Seven Heavens tremble. O we are weak servants, weak servants. Our Mawla, Al-Qadir, Muqtadir, we ask His generous favours. May we be dressed with that generous favour, Haji Mehmet. Ask for that. There are Divine favours coming from the Presence of Janabu l-Haqq. I am saying that you ask to be dressed that robe of generous favours. Don't look at other things. There are robes of honour descending from the Heavens. Aman ya Rabb, aman ya Rabbi, aman ya Rabbi. I am saying, may we strive to be dressed with that robe of honour. Let's say, Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Hayy. What a power comes to one! What a power comes, Sheikh Mehmet Efendi. Let's say, Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim, Ey yaran Shah-i Mardan. Come to say, let's say, Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. May strength come to our bodies, may our spiritualities strengthen. This is coming from the Heavens. This is not something acquired on Earth, Haji Mehmet. Descending from the Heavens, coming to holy servants by the generous grant of Allah. It is said to look for the holy servants, Sheikh Mehmet Efendi.

The holy addressing coming from the Heavens: Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim, it gives life, it gives blood, it gives faith, it gives Islam, it gives honour. It opens the doors of treasures. Let's say, Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar wa Lillahi l-hamd. The holy angels tremble every second while reciting His name. When saying, Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. May Allah not deprive us of that honour, for the sake of His Habibu l-Kibriya.

Say, and be beautiful. Say, Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim, be beautiful. Say, Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim and be acceptable in the Presence of Rabbu l-Izzat. He dressed the most blessed, honoured robe on our Prophet, the Master of the Universe. May we ask to be dressed also. O our Lord, we are Your weak servants, although by the blessings of Your Habib, we are given that beautiful rank, we are given that honour, ey Haji Mehmet. Ey Haji Mehmet, be ambitious for wearing and make people wear this honourable robe. Strive for this.

We are charged with wearing it and to make people wear it. This is the duty of prophets. They are responsible for wearing and make people wear that robe of honour. Ya Rabbi tawba, tawba astaghfirullah. Let's say, Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. May every problem/worry and sadness leave us. May all the honourable doors here and hereafter be opened for us. Fatiha.

Pray on me Mehmet. It will be written on your heart. Don't fear. You are like a dragon. May Allah be pleased with you. May Allah be pleased with you. This one is a lion. He is lion. The other one will be a lion too. You will be too. Long live! Long live! Pray on me, Mehmet. Allahu Akbar. MashaAllah. May we destroy the fortresses of satan. May we be dressed with strength, may we be dressed with honour, my son. Be in safety!
