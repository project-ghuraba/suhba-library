As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Inna lladhīna āmanū wa- ‘amilū ṣ-ṣāliḥāti... wa-lā khawfun ‘alayhim wa-lā hum yaḥzanūn” (2:277). Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says that for whoever has īmān and believes in AllāhNone there is no fear, no sorrow, and no sadness. One must be on the right path. The right path is the way of our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. From the time of our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam until now there is only one path. HisNone path is the everlasting one. Many invented things have emerged that oppose himNone or do not conform fully to hisNone path. They have all been exhausted and vanished. Indeed, those that remain have no effect – they are names without substance. There are those like that, but indeed, shayṭān never tires. Constantly, new things are brought forth as the way.

However, the path of our ProphetNone is firm and solid; it is hisNone alone. That path endures. Our Prophet’sNone path continues until Qiyāmah. The one who enters that path will find safety. The one who strays from the path will be destroyed if he does not return. His life, his existence has no value at all. There is nothing but harm. On the Day of Judgment, they will say, “Yā laytanī kuntu turāban” (78:40). I wish I were dust so that I would not have done what I did. But turning to dust happens only once. After that, it is an eternal loss.

Therefore, hold firmly to the path of our ProphetNone. “Wa- ‘taṣimū bi-ḥabli Llāhi jamī‘an wa-lātafarraqū” (3:103). This is the command of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. HeNone says, hold firmly to this rope of salvation so that the waters do not sweep you away, so that the floods do not carry you away. Hold firmly to it, and you will be saved. If you do not hold firm, your ego and desires, shayṭān, and this dunyā will lead you astray. You will be destroyed. May AllāhNone protect us.

These paths are the way of the mashāyikh, the awliyā’, the ṣaḥābah, Ahlu l-Bayt, and our Holy ProphetNone. None of them has ever strayed from this path. They have walked upon this path. Those who have left for the other path have gone to ruin. Their path has ended and disappeared. Therefore, AllāhNone has granted us a great blessing by guiding us to this path. May AllāhNone make it everlasting by HisNone will. Our path, in shā’a Llāh, is a beautiful path. A path of light. The path of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla and our Holy ProphetNone. It is the path of the awliyā’. In shā’a Llāh, may we remain steadfast. May Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām appear so that we may reach him, in shā’a Llāh. We make du‘ā’ to HimNone. This is the belief of our path.

Ahlu s-Sunnah wa-l-Jamā‘ah, those who follow the path of our Holy ProphetNone are the people who believe in himNone. Those who do not accept himNone have gone astray. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, saying, who are the believer, and following AllāhNone, there is no sadness, no any difficulty for them. Don't be sorry. This is because in life everybody, they have sadness, they have something to think about this. But the important thing for believer who following Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, there no sadness for them, no anxious. They are, just keep themselves with Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, and hisNone way, which it is, hisNone way, straight way, coming through himNone since more than one thousand four hundred years. Same way, which it is, no another way was like this. Because, this right way all continue until Qiyāmah. Other, of course, since time of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, many way, they are coming, they claiming they are right, they are... you are wrong. But all of them was finish, disappear. Only right way, way of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, continue with whole ummah, giving light for ummah, giving happiness for people.

Other way, they are not giving any happiness. Giving only misery, giving trouble for people, harming people. Only way of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam giving happiness, not harming people, helping people. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are this way of Ahlu s-Sunnah wa-l-Jamā‘ah, which it is ṭarīqah people. Sharī‘ah and ṭarīqah, together continuing in this way. And it is blessed way, benefit way for who following this.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, with barakah of mashāyikh, awliyā’, people they are like to find this way. Many of them, AllāhNone, for their intention, they give them, keep them away from bad way. But, other people, it will be not good for them, to follow way of harming people, giving trouble for people, taking what they like, left what they not like No. It’s important to follow what Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying. And follow easy way. There is two way in Islām. There is, it can be more difficult for people, maybe. But Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, all time Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam was saying, “Yassirū wa-lā tu‘assirū” – be easy, not make it difficult. For this, Islām, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, the way of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, whole giving good and happiness, in shā’a Llāh.

Who find making people to run away from you, this is not good way. The way of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, for whole human being, to be, show them the right way. Even the non-Muslim, if asking something, don't say wrong thing. You must say what is true, what is not true. This is also from nice side of – all on nice, Islām, but also for people, they cheated. They, maybe they, when see some people, they claiming Islām, and doing something not in Islām, people thought Islām like this. And for this, you must be show the good thing every time from Islām, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone bless you, in shā’a Llāh. We are, also our way going until Sayyidunā Mahdī, ‘alayhi s-salām. When he coming, we, it is from our belief also. Ahlu s-Sunnah wa-l-Jamā‘ah, ṭarīqah and sharī‘ah people, they are waiting Sayyidunā l-Mahdī, ‘alayhi s-salām. Who not waiting, this also, he has trouble.

AllāhNone give hidāyah for all of us, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
