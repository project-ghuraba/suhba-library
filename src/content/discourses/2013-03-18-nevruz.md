Sheikh Muhammad Nazim Al-Haqqani An-Naqshibandi, Sohbet of the 18th of March, 2013.

Marhaba to all, who are present. O lovers of Shah Mardan. Masha'Allah. There is nothing that doesn't have Bismi llahi r-rahmani r-rahim written on it. The Basmala Sharif is the seal of the universe. Allahu Akbar Allahu Akbar Allahu Akbar wa li-llahi l-hamd. Ya Sayyidi Ya RasulAllah Ya HabibAllah Ya Muhammad Mustafa. Nuru l-'ayn, the light of the universe. O our most beloved, most respected Master. The honored Rasul, whom we treat with honor. His uncle's son is Shah Mardan. Chivalry comes from him. He is the lion of Allah.

Today they say it is Nawruz, in old traditions. Isn't it? It means a new beginning. Marhaba O lovers, O friends of Shah Mardan. Thank Allah, be grateful that you have heard about Shah Mardan. But you didn't see. You heard but you didn't understand. Shah Mardan, the owner of the arena, Haydar-i Karrar, is it? Stand like a lion with our closeness to him. New day, new provision. They don't serve yesterday's food at the Sultan's table. They don't. The Sultan doesn't bring yesterday's food to his people. The Sultan has majesty. So they finish all of yesterday's food yesterday. They give, because they know today 'new day, new provision' comes. New day comes with new provision from the Sultan's door. The Sultan's beloved Haydar-i Karrar, lion, is Shah Mardan. So his table is not the same everyday.

Marhaba O friends of Shah Mardan. How sweet, how fresh it is coming. Madad ya Sultan. Astaghfiru llah. We aren't worthy of mentioning Allah's lion's name but we are full of his love. Endless glory to Allah Jalla Jalalahu. Haydar-u Karrar is he? He gave him to this nation. The holy Haydar-i Karrar is Shah Mardan who guards His palace, who is the guard and the uncle's son of the Beloved, for whose sake Allah created the universe. Say it and refresh! Marhaba O lovers of Shah Mardan. Marhaba to you, no distress. O friends, fellows of Shah Mardan, the ones, who love him. A person takes something from his beloved one. Haydar-u Karrar. In the old times, his everlasting legend was told in assemblies, saying - "lovers, friends of Shah Mardan". The Muslims were filled with love and himmet (striving). There is nobody left to be loved. Rubbish is left. What to love about rubbish? They run after a carcass saying "this is mine". What can they give people? They will eat the carcass themselves. They will throw bones to people, whom they betray, as if they are dogs. They have nothing else.

Shah Mardan's legend, his value in the presence of Allah and Rasulullah, his service, his legacy, his power and ability, the majesty of the dhulfiqar [his sword] in his hand, gives us a spirit, a life to our spirits, love and himmet. He doesn't make you sleep and get up like a donkey. Hasha! If you are Shah Mardan's servant, he attires you with a dress of honor. He attires the ones, who love him with a beautiful dress. He is the Sultan, Shah Mardan. O Shah Mardan we are weak servants. We love you, we love the ones, you love. We love the ones, who love you. How beautiful, how majestic. What honor, what majesty for the friends, lovers, fellows of Shah Mardan. The ones, who rejoice with him, who take power from him. Take power. What power? The power he gives, isn't horsepower. No. The power we take from him leads us to the field of contest. Brave men can go onto that field. "If you won't sacrifice yourself or your head, stay back, don't go onto the field." Say, "we sacrifice ourselves in the way of Shah Mardan". What is his way? Sultanu l-Anbiya Habibu l-kibriya's way. He takes us to the presence of Allah. If you won't sacrifice yourself or your head, stay back, don't go on the field. Give everything for Shah Mardan's love, respect and service. Give! So that you'll be a lion, a lion.

Shame on today's people, thay can't appreciate Shah Mardan. They can't say, you can't say enough about the good manners of that Sultan, that lion. "Be lovers, lovers." He says like this: "If you won't sacrifice yourself, your head, stay back, don't go on the field." It is a field of contest: the assembly of Shah Mardan and his field, is a field of contest. Cats and dogs can't go on his field. Those lions they sacrifice, give their lives, for Shah Mardan. If you want that field, leave your fake form. Let them dress you with a beautiful dress of real lions. They are superior in the divine presence Shah Mardan and his followers. They celebrate Nawruz and they fight. People, who don't know Shah Mardan, they make new fashion Nawruz, a new day. They do all kinds of infamy. Then they say: "We are in Shah Mardan's way". Hasha. Hasha thumma kalla. His way is the way of lions. Lions don't run in the streets, they are the kings of the jungle. They roar and roam. Where is your roaring? There are lots of liars, who are claimants. They are worthless. We are the ones, searching for Shah Mardan's assembly, for the lovers', friends' assembly so that we refresh. Let our mortal bodies perish and they will dress us with the dress of real bodies. A drop from the sea, that is presented to the big prophets, from the attribute of Al-Hayy (The Living) Shah Mardan offers to people, who keep his way, who love him. No illness, no trouble, no distress, no worries for them. Their hearts are always open. Why? They are the lovers of Shah Mardan.

The friends, lovers of Shah Mardan start the day with what? Bismi llahi r-rahmani r-rahim. He draws the sword 'Bismillah Ya Allah.' O useless claimants! say 'Bismillah O Shah Mardan' before every difficult action. Masha'Allah O Shah Mardan. Say Bismillah Ya Allah. He won't leave either China, nor the USA. We have power but nobody knows. They take refuge in China, Russia, USA. We have Shah Mardan. He immediately finishes 10 Americas. You idiots! We have the power of Shah Mardan. Say "Ya Allah. Ya Habib Allah, Ya Shah Mardan, Bismillah, Allahu Akbar" go onto the field. See what happens. The field of brave men is Shah Mardan's field. We are weak. We want to listen. Let's listen to Shah Mardan. What does he do? What he did, we can say like a drop from the sea. Beyond that, the fields that they fought in, the field of contest, doesn't accept this world. They don't fit in this world. If he kicks the world, he will throw the world out of its orbit. He is Shah Mardan for the honor of the Master of the Universe. What are these? These crows are running after carcass, non-believers, dirty ones. What is their value? Nothing.

Say 'Bismi llahi r-rahmani r-rahim'. Draw your Dhulfiqar O Shah Mardan. We may walk behind you. How beautiful O Shah Mardan! We refresh. Sadness, gloom leaves our hearts. We may say "Ya Allah, Ya Muhammad Mustafa, Intercessor on the Day of Judgement. O Habib! Your lion is Shah Mardan. Your love is above his love O Beloved. Your uncle's son Shah Mardan is wearing your love." He wears the Beloved's dress of love. He wears the Beloved's power dress. They wear the Beloved's majesty dress. Why are you afraid? Go out on the field. "If you won't surrender yourself or your head, stay back, don't go on the field." Many heads are cut in this field nobody asks. When he goes onto the field saying 'Ya Allah' - That is Shah Mardan. Search. Don't play on the streets with women, girls. Don't say: "We are Alawi, we are this, that." It is worthless. Was Hz. Ali, Shah Mardan, out on the field with women and girls? Shah Mardan looked for the field of brave men. You play. You obey your ego, you drink, eat, scream, shout and dance. Your way is the devil's way. It isn't Shah Mardan's way. Shah Mardan's way is an honorable way. If dishonorable people go onto his field, they will be beheaded. O those, who have useless claims, don't make Shah Mardan angry or else you'll all be lost.

Marhaba, O lovers of Shah Mardan. Learn this. Say this when you say Marhaba. Marhaba friends of Shah Mardan, marhaba. How nice, how beautiful, he dresses such majesty and power. Don't go out with women and girls. Something may come over you. Either a stone falls, or snow falls or a storm comes. He sweeps either with earthquake, thunder or fuss. Keep the straight path of Shah Mardan and say 'Bismi llahi r-rahmani r-rahim'. O Shah Mardan! They closed all the associations that could show the way for people. The ones, who closed and don't open, may their graves be opened and in 40 days not even one be left. We need a Sultan. The Sultan, is the one, whom Shah Mardan sends. The Sultan is ─āl-i Osman. Sultanate is ─āl-i Osman. If you don't give back his right, his honor, you can't change your ways. You'll be a hyena running after carcass. A hyena. Which ones are shouting badly? You'll be a jackal. You can't be a lion, you can be a jackal.

O Muslims! If you don't know Shah Mardan, there is no power in your hands. Don't say "USA gave this, England will give money". We have. Shah Mardan has it, there are also soldiers. They have both gold and jewellery. Who do you think Shah Mardan is? You don't mention his name. You mention the names of people, you never should. May not even one of you remain within 40 days. If you don't write Hz. Ali's name and hang it high you'll be in trouble. Not even one will remain in 40 days. They will throw you in the graveyard. Useless titles won't save you. Say 'Bismi llahi r-rahmani r-rahim, dastur Ya Shah Mardan. O the Beloved's beloved uncle's son Ya Shah Mardan. Keep an eye on us. Expel these insects.' If he shouts, neither their bombs not other weapons can make such a noise. This is an important matter. Say! Be an unsheathed sword. Be an unsheathed sword. If you hold a sword in your hand say 'O Shah Mardan come.' He turns all this corrupted world upside down. Believe. Straighten your path, if you say "I'm Alawi". Surrender to Shah Mardan. Shah Mardan didn't dance with girls and women. Hasha. Or else his sword will cut your necks. Don't slander. Keep the manners of Shah Mardan or else you'll be finished. O our Lord, may He forgive us. Bismi llahi r-rahmani r-rahim opens all the treasures. People, who dance, does it ever occur to them to say 'Bismi llahi r-rahmani r-rahim'? Does it ever occur to them to say "O Shah Mardan? Where are you, Owner of the field? Where are you O beloved lion of Allah's Beloved?" Say. Everything gets better. But they call the devil. They don't call him. They'll perish, become extinct.

Tawba Ya Rabbi, tawba astaghfirullah. We may crown our assembly with Basmala Sharif: Bismi llahi r-rahmani r-rahim. It opens the door of every treasure. The world also. The world has no value. Bismi llahi r-rahmani r-rahim opens eight gates of paradise. It is written while entering the Presence, angels tell you to say Bismi llahi r-rahmani r-rahim and go into the Presence. The presence that Shah Mardan will show is different. The station he will take you to is different. Come and obey Shah Mardan, O lying claimants! Or else a storm will take you, won't be anything left of you within 40 days. This is a good tiding for the believers, for people, who keep his way. Fear for the others. What happened? Planes crashed. Cars crashed. What happened? An earthquake hit. What happened? A plane crashed. Finished. His book is closed. Where are the titles, were they useful? No. The ones, who loved people disliked by Allah, were hit, died in a plane crash like this. None of them survived. Fear Allah! Keep the manners of Shah Mardan, fear Allah.

Take majesty from Allah's lion O people of Anatolia! Leave the Turkish-Kurdish problem. We are the soldiers, servants of Shah Mardan. We are full of his love and striving. If you say 'O Shah Mardan reach to us' if he can't reach, who owns the field of contest? Field of contest. MashaAllahu kan. May our day be good. These words are for awakening our hearts after the morning prayer. We don't claim this or that but our claim is to be loyal to Shah Mardan. We are trying. May we be from his lovers, from the ones keeping his path. Al-hamdu li-llah our lineage reaches to Shah Mardan. There are many, who have pure lineage but they are tempted by corrupted ones. A punishment, a trouble, a torment comes on their head in 40 days. I don't know that's why they say Rajabun Ajaibun. Rajab comes in one month. Rajab Shahrullah is coming in 40 days. It is the month Shah Mardan draws his sword. They should beware. Shah Mardan isn't afraid of Russia, USA or Europe. Islam has a flag, Shah Mardan carries it. It has a sword, Shah Mardan carries it. Those people are revolting. What is the value of people running after carcass? What is their majesty? They have nothing. They are crowded, doesn't matter. The jungle is full of animals but none has a voice like a lion. The roaring of the lion makes all animals in the forest tremble.

Be like that O clean people, who love Shah Mardan! It won't work if you say we are Alawi, etc. Surrender to Shah Mardan, keep his way. Give your respect, sacrifice for him. You may take from the honor of that field. O Lord don't make us from the cast- off ones. Give health to our bodies. O Lord You created us for Your servanthood, not for this world. Shame on people, who live for this world and who forget their Lord. They won't win. That's why we need a Sultan. We need sultans coming from Shah Mardan's lineage. We exiled those Sultans, we are disgraced. We won't be disgraced but they made us. Act, O lovers of Shah Mardan! It is our pride. Our wish, prayer from Allah Almighty is that we'll be Shah Mardan's soldiers. We'll keep his way. We'll be the soldiers of the field of contest. We won't be from the ones licking bones. Children of lions are lions. Be from them. Don't be a bone licker. They are liars, they'll disappear. O our glorious Shah Mardan give us at least one drop from your love. Liars drink alcohol, their assemblies are in dirt now. They are useless claimants. None will be left within 40 days. Or else they'll be the servants of Shah Mardan's field. They'll open the field for Shah Mardan. Don't smoke, you'll burn. Don't drink, you'll burn at the bottom of hell. Don't fornicate, you'll be a humiliated crazy of the devils both here and hereafter. Sultan accept us." May he put on us a dress of majesty. Bait-ullah (house of Allah) is full. Whoever wants a man's dress should go to Bait-ullah. If you can't go, ask from afar. Bait-ullah isn't an empty house. Whatever they want, they may find. We also want. They put on us the dress of servanthood to our Lord. One drop from the Beloved's crown may be put on our heads too. We shall want this. Don't make tawwaf aimlessly. Don't make tawwaf around Bait-ullah aimlessly. Know what to ask. This house isn't empty. There is everything for lions. Whatever the weak ones need, they may also find. He puts on majesty. They take away the dress of abasement. Go to Bait-ullah that way. It isn't empty.

O Lord dress us with power, from the trusts that descend to the Ka'ba that you ordered us to glorify. Dress us with beauty and majesty, with light, with honor and belief, with health. Give us lion-hood O Lord. Know what to ask for O Hajjis. O ruler! Gold and petrol bewitched you. Allah sent the glorious Quran, my son Sheikh Mehmet Effendi. O Lord, Tawba Ya Rabbi. Tubna wa raja'na ilayk. O Shah Mardan forgive us. Accept it. Let's say Bismi llahi r-rahmani r-rahim. The doors of every goodness open. May today be holy.
