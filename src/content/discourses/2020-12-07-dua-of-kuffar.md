Aū‘dhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-Shay"āni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Ra!māni r-Ra!īm.

Wa#-#alātu wa s-salāmu ‘alā Rasūlinā Mu!ammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādati A#!ābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā

Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-F’āiz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Mu!ammad Nā$im al-%aqqānī. Madad. Allah ‘azza wa jalla says, (A’ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shay"āni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Ra!māni r-Ra!īm): “Wa-mā du‘āu l-kāfirīna illā fī 'alāl” (Qur’ān 13:14), #adaqa ‘Llahu l-‘A$īm. “ And the dua of kāfirīn [i.e., those who cover the truth] is nothing but an error.“ Dua, supplication, is a blessing given to us by Allah. It is special for Muslims. Prayers of the kuffār have no effect. It does not matter whether they make dua or they don’t, because they do not accept the command of Allah. They can say as much as they want making dua supplicating or praying, but it has no value, it has no benefit.

Allah gave this to Muslims. Allah gave this as a weapon. They are making so many weapons, but their weapons have no use in front of [a believer’s] dua. This weapon of dua is to protect ourselves, and for victory. We should always make good duas. “Well, I made dua, but it was not accepted.” Allah answers the dua of those who believe, the dua of Muslims, but not all of them are answered here. Some of them are left to be answered in the Next Life. These days the most important thing, and what we need the most, is dua. We need to make dua for each other, and to ask for others’ dua. This will have a great benefit for people.

Dua is the key to everything. Medicines too have benefit, but with dua. Most people take medicine, but then say that it did not work. That is why we need to make dua, and to say that the medicine is from Allah, that you (He) gave it as a means, and so we find health through it. It is a means to help, and so that the means may have its [good] effect, we make dua. So that this sickness will go away. We need to make dua, so that evil and bad things will go away and stay away from us. That’s how we should make dua for everything. It is beneficial for all kinds of things.

Allah 'azza wa jalla gave this blessing to people. That’s why dua of kuffār is not answered. No matter how much they make dua. First of all they need to believe in Allah. They need to accept the commands and orders of Allah, and then their dua will be answered. This is the secret of this matter. That’s why sometimes those who are non-Muslims used to come and ask for dua. Why? Because theirs were of no benefit, and they heard that Muslims’ prayers are answered. We have seen many such people, have heard of many such people. They always came and asked for dua.

May Allah give them guidance so that their prayers can also be answered. We want good for people. And the greatest good that we can ask for people is to recognize Allah and accept Allah, to come to faith. May Allah give strength to our faith, insha'Allah. Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-F ti ah
