As-Salamu Alaikum. Audhu Billahi min al shaytan al rajim, Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fil jamia'

The religion of Islam is the religion of Mercy towards the creatures of Allah. Compassion, there is compassion in Islam. The real believer has compassion for everyone in his heart. For them it is said "They don't even step on an ant". Because that heart is soft. It is not hard & tough. Hard & tough, the unbeliever's heart is hard & tough, has no compassion. When he becomes Muslim, with the Heavenly Glance of Allah & with the love of Allah, that hardening disappears. When fulfilling the orders of Allah, by obeying Allah man can do everything. But they are done as the order of Allah and that is done for the benefit of people.

"How can you call it the religion of compassion & then you make war, you fight, you do this, you do that..." They make war either to bring those bad hearted people to the right way or they remove them so that they don't harm the people anymore. It says "every harmful thing should be killed". That is what his punishment is. That is what Allah says. Whatever it is that Allah orders, it is fulfilled. Arham ar Rahimin is Allah. You can't be more Merciful, more Compassionate than He.

We see in the poorest Islamic countries... We visited many times with Mawlana, may Allah give him long life. We reach Mahdi (as) with him insha Allah. We went on many journeys with him. We visited the poor countries like Pakistan, India..No we didn't go to India but Sri Lanka. There is no one dying from hunger there, yet it is poor. Everybody helps & shows compassion to one another, feeds people.. Millions, tens of millions of people continue their lives as poor. But who we call the richest country, they call "homeless people", there are millions of homeless people. Our foolish people here think that when they go to US they enter paradise, that they have everything there. However we went to the US once, Allah granted to go there also & it was for a lesson. And we have no desire to go for a 2nd time, no desire even a bit. Hundreds of homeless people. Wherever we went, homeless people. Wherever we went, they go around with push-carts collecting from here there... It is full with homeless people. Nobody helps them, not the government either. They find & eat from trash. Meaning no one shows them compassion. They themselves also, because they are not on the way of Islam they don't show compassion to themselves either. But when it is an Islamic country people help the poor, show compassion to them. When they need something, it is provided for them. If their children are to get married, people help them too. So Muslims are like this, compassionate. Others say "humanity" but there is nothing like humanity at all. What they call humanity, it is of no use when it is merciless. When the Prophet (sas) was kissing his grandson, some Bedouin Arabs saw him. They looked as if they disapproved, saying "how can you act like this? I have 10 kids & never kissed one of them". Then the Prophet (sav) said: "We love, we are human. We show compassion and it becomes mercy for us. Whoever shows mercy will be shown mercy".

Compassion is one of the best virtues of Islam. And what teaches these good virtues are Shari'ah & also Tariqa. We see a lot of those who don't follow them, who do as they like saying "I don't follow Shaykh, I follow Quran". There is no mercy in their hearts. They also lost their way. Ahlu Sunnah wal Jama'ah are, Masha Allah as the Prophet (sas) said, soft, quiet people like sheep.

And the others are like wolves. When they attack a herd, destroy them all. Without even taking one, they kill all. In the end, maybe they eat one or leave without eating. This is no good. What Tariqa does to the herd is if there is a sick one among them they take him, then the herd is safe and healthy. But others want to destroy everything without looking what there is in it. They call everyone unbeliever, everyone mushrik. When you do this you do the biggest oppression to people by accusing them of being both unbelievers and mushriks. You didn't leave people any door of paradise and they are all Muslims. These are the merciless ones.

It even says in the Holy Quran that "if your heart was hard & tough, no one would come to you." This is also a big sign. And they say "We look from Quran, we know from Hadith". How all the main, big Ayahs are in front of them & Allah blinded them because of their oppression, because of their mercilessness. Although these people are Muslims since they don't follow a Shaykh, a Tariqa, or a holy person, they follow their egos. They oppress everyone. Their hearts are in a hardness which is not in Islam and their reckoning is with Allah.

Today we speak about Mercy. The biggest teaching in Islam is to be merciful with people, with mankind, even with animal, with insect even. Some of them said they not passing on an ant even. Islam is like this, Mercy religion. Because Allah is the most Merciful. Prophet (sas) also, he is the most merciful. Even sometimes he was kissing his grandson, Sayyidina Hassan or Hussain. And one from Bedouin astonished. "What are you doing O Prophet (sas)? I have 10 children, I never kiss my children. And Prophet was little bit not happy with him & saying to him "I love & I have mercy. You must be merciful. Who get mercy for people, Allah will be merciful with him."

So we see some of fundamentalist in Islam. You see, they are tough. Even with Muslim people they are very tough. We hear from everywhere especially country like Daghestan or other..They cheat them.
