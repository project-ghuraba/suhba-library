Assalam alaykum

Allahumma salli wa sallim ala Nabina Muhammad alayhi salam Salatan tadumu wa tughda ilayh Mammarra layali wa tul ad dawam Allahumma salli wa sallim ala Nabina Muhammad alayhi salam Salatan tadumu wa tughda ilayh Mammarra layali wa tul ad dawam Allahumma salli wa sallim ala Nabina Muhammad alayhi salam Salatan tadumu wa tughda ilayh Mammarra layali wa tul ad dawam

Masha Allah, masha Allah, tabarak Allah. Today we are in the nisfu Sh`aban, the middle of shahru Sh`aban. Tonight is Laylaytu'l Bara`a. We are thanking Allah Almighty that He allow us to reach this night. Alhamdulillah. If you are thinking for small, small, small happening you can understand it is actually very big happening. And it is the reason to make you thankful for Allah Almighty. He gives you small reason for big things, for big happenings.

Our fault as Bani Adam is that we don't understand. We don't understand. It is our fault. If we can analyse and understand how you should behave for Allah Almighty we will be all of us awliya, first me. I will be the first awliya and then you follow me. Hopeless case. But if you are in the step of awliya that means Allah Almighty will give us from His Generosity to be awliya. Don't be hopeless. Be hoping all the time. Mawlana Shaykh is all the time advising, say, 'If you been asking for something in the good way, you will have it as you ask. But if you ask it in negative way, you will get the negative answer. If you go by positive asking you will get the positive answer.

If you go for anybody by angry face, the one in front of you will answer you in angry face. If you go for somebody with smiling face, for example you go to the professor asking to make your note better, 'You professor, you make my note very bad', what's his response to you? The bad will be worse. Yallah! But if you go 'Please I will try my best to improve myself and if he is in a good mood and the wife doesn't give him hard time, you will have your note. But if he is comme-ci comme-ca, he will look for your behaviour, if you are smiling, you give him some chocolate as a present, or you give him very sweet talk and make him to accept your requesting, then you will have what you ask. Allah Almighty, Alhamdulillah, Alhamdulillah, Alhamdulillah, shukr Ya Rabbi. He never reply back for us as we behave. He is all the time making the door of His generosity open and He is making the asking easy. We are the arrogant ones, we don't understand. We are the ones all the time trying to get things, not understanding. We try to make the formula cannot be accepted. If you make formula in physics, they say, 'No, no, no, unacceptable, there is nothing, no such things. From where you bring this?' I think about it and I write, XYZ, ZFB, whatever, and it is formula and the professor sees it is not. How can be?

This is how we Muslim are thinking today about the formula of how to behave and how to treat Allah Almighty, His Prophet and His beloved servants. There is no formula. The real formula is there but today's people don't believe in that formula, say, 'No, we are renewing the formula, we are doing this formula’. You cannot renew because it is all the time updated because it is for Humanity. It is like formula for medicine, for example, the root of this curing is there, you cannot change it. Maybe you can modify according to the living but, totally to be changed, it's never happened. So that means you are changing everything and Sayyidina Muhammad (sas) say 'I came and I complete. When I give you the formula it was complete'

But what we do? According to the date we play with it but we never play with the main formula. Today, if you are drinking cola, the formula is coming from the factory. Even if you have factory in Russia, China, even I don't know, the moon but the recipe is coming from one place. Say this is secret, we cannot give it to anybody, very secret. Secret recipe. But Alhamdulillah! In our religion there's no secret recipe, it is open recipe for everybody. And that's why tonight is very important. Tonight is the night that it will be telling, it will be written what will happen until the next night. That means tonight one wrong and coming for tonight again, so with this one year period Allah Almighty give all the happening, who who will die, who, who will live who will come, who will go, what to eat, what not to eat,

All the details, you cannot imagine the details being given by tonight. Allah Almighty make it like so small, small, small piece of details. This is Allah. Nobody can compete with Allah Almighty, this is Allah Almighty, He is giving everything because He say 'I will not have any excuse from anybody saying 'Ya Rabbi why you didn't do this corner?' Ya Rabbi why you didn't explain this sign, everything get full details. This is Allah. Nobody can compete with Allah Almighty. Don't play with Allah Almighty, play with me it's ok. But Allah, don't dare to play with Him. He is Allah Jalla Jalaluhu. But in our level, ok, no problem. Maybe we can be ignorant, we can behave badly, but for Allah, try to keep your respect, your good behaviour and try to keep adab with Allah Almighty.

Allah Almighty is different. He is the Lord, He is everything. That's why the respect in Islam is very important. Some people are mixing between the question and the respect. You can ask for knowledge, you don't ask for harming. It is not allowed to ask as ignorant asking. If there is benefit for asking, ask but if there is ignorant asking, don't ask. If you know that's ignorant, don't ask. It's coming from shaytan, it's coming from ego. But to know, it is conditional to ask. We want to know, we are hungry for knowledge. We are all the time hungry to know. If you are hungry to know, Allah Almighty will give you, give you, give you. Will never stop giving you. He has oceans of giving.

And there is saying, what is the knowledge of all the creatures amongst Allah Almighty's knowledge? Imagine you see an ocean as Pacific ocean and you take one nail and put it in ocean and what there is as drop of water, not even that dot of water, will be ocean, Pacific ocean, and from there you put another nail and take, again that drop left will be another ocean and then you put your nail and say maybe you make it billion times. This is Allah almighty. Allah Almighty is not a joke. Allah Almighty, you cannot frame Him, no frame for Allah. If he have a frame, he's not Allah. Endless. That's why they say don't think about Allah Almighty's size, think what Allah Almighty created. He teach you from the Creation.

That's why the detail is very important here. We look for the details, 'Ahh.. Allah, Allah, Allah'. They are discovering the gene, they say, 'Ahh.. Big discovery'. But it is nothing against so many things, Subhanallah. It is nothing, but it's important. Allah Almighty makes you to know nothing but at the same time it is very important. This is Allah Almighty. Alhamdulillah, we are at the right door and with the right person to teach us the pure, Allah Almighty knowledge. The pure one and as we start to say Alhamdulillah we want to be waliullah.

Our intention, to be waliullah. But with His generosity, with His giving, not with our ignorance and ego and bad behaviour. He is Generous and we all have to smile, 'Ya Rabbi, please we want to be'. Don't be ignorant and say 'I want to do it by myself'. 'I want to make seclusion, I want to make prayer. Do this one, I'm not saying don't do, but don't depend on your doing'. Depend on Allah Almighty's Generosity. He is the Generous and we are waiting for the Generous to give us. If Allah Almighty accepts to give us and we are ready to take Alhamdulillah, be the humble one accepting everything.

Be the one who is accepting from Allah Almighty; whatever He give him, he takes. Sometime you take bitter things. Also ok, because you know this is coming from Allah Almighty. That's why if you can be from the shakur, the shakireen, all the time thankful, maybe if you get one bitter, you get 10 sweet ones. But if you are the one arguing, maybe you have one sweet and 10 bitter ones. Very important to understand this point also.

With this one we say Ya Rabbi, we're asking for the sweet one to come all the time and make our face, happy face and smiling and our days, happy days and happy laughing and insha Allah from now on until next year to Laylatu'l Bara`a we have happy days, healthy days, wealthy days, and we don't need anybody except Allah Almighty to look after us and to make us from His awliyullah, that we support Allah Almighty and meaning to support Allah Almighty and to support Sayyidina Muhammad (sas) to support Awliullah, Mawlana Shaykh and Masha'ikh al Izam. They don't actually need our support but we're volunteering to be their followers and we are asking from Allah Almighty to accept us as their followers.
