As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa raḥmatu Llāhi wa barakātuhu

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh madad yā Mashāyikhinā dastūr Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistāni, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we have been here since twenty two days, ma shā’a Llāh. This time was, every time, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, making tour with people who are from Ahlu dh-Dhikr. "Fa-s’alū ahla dh-dhikri in kuntum lā ta‘lamūn." (16:43) Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are, in shā’a Llāh, from dhikr meaning to mention, to remember AllāhNone and to make people to remember AllāhNone. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are trying to do this and we are going where is people asking. In shā’a Llāh, we have been there many thousand people they have been seen and they see us, we are see them. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, to be for each people each one AllāhNone reward us and reward them because they are coming for AllāhNone and we are going also. And every time, in shā’a Llāh, getting more and more.

It is way of AllāhNone, it is blessed way. Not like tourism for dunyā, running after dunyā. No. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we try to connect our heart with Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla. To try also to connect people's heart with Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla, with ProphetNone. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, everywhere we have zāwiyah, dergah, and mosque and people they are gathering to every place when, because we are not whole time with them, but our heart, their heart can be make rābiṭah. Wherever are you in this country or other country, important thing to be your connection with your heart with your Shaykh, with wakīl, other. It is important. To gathering also important.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are try this and, in shā’a Llāh, today we are going back. But when we are going back, in shā’a Llāh, people must also gathering. If anything they want to ask, they can ask the deputy where they are. Every place we have, al-ḥamdu liLlāh. And here it is headquarter for Mawlānā also this. St Ann's priory, Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim Sufi Center. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, there is servant here - Shaykh Zumar and Shaykh Sa‘īd. It is open for whole people who coming for AllāhNone. It is open for them. They are coming for asking other people something also. For whole people it is open. It is order of AllāhNone to accept everybody, to answer them for what they want. We have here Mawlānā place, we have in Peckham also. In Manchester, Sheffield, Bury and Nelson, Birmingham - everywhere, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we have all for serving people. Servant of AllāhNone, we serve them where they are, what they want. There is place to go, to go and to be satisfied, in shā’a Llāh. We are thanking you, all of you, for you are listening for this ṣuḥbah. Every day you are coming and making also live somebody they can see. We are thanking AllāhNone for this opportunity. HeNone give us and asking help from HimNone. AllāhNone help us and help you, in shā’a Llāh, to be in right way ṣirāṭu l-mustaqīm, to be helpful for people also, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone make our heart wide to accept everybody, in shā’a Llāh. Thank you for you, in shā’a Llāh, see you again.

As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa raḥmatu Llāhi wa barakātuh. Al-Fātiḥah.
