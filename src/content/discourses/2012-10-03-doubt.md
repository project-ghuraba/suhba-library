As-Salamu Alaikum. Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fil jamia' "Min Sharri Al-Waswassi Al-Khannas" (114:4)

Waswas (doubts) is big now, everyone has some kind of doubts inside. Some have less, some more. And that is, Allah doesn't oppress anyone. How doesn't He oppress? He doesn't give him a burden that he can't carry. Therefore you may have doubts but as long as they remain in your heart or thoughts and don't go beyond, it is nothing. Meaning, as long as these doubts don't result in evil or bad actions, it is nothing. Everybody has that.

And there is another kind of doubt, that is bad. "Did I make ablution correctly or not?" Many people have this. For this too, Allah doesn't ask anyone to make ablution for 3 hours. He (swt) says "Do ablution once, it is enough". If you have too much doubt then you are excused. Make ablution for the excused. Excused meaning, for some the urine comes continuously, he makes ablution for each prayer time. And some can't hold off the gases, for that one too when he makes ablution for dhuhr, it is valid till asr time.

And those who have waswas/doubts, they are mentally excused. So they make one ablution for one time of prayer & 2nd for the other time saying "I am excused". No need to make 10 times, Allah made it easy. Son don't do "is it correct or not, is it ok or not.." Make it one time, say "I am excused". 2nd time, after the prayer time is over. You make once for asr, then you make 2nd time for maghreb, then one for isha, so you manage it this way. This way is easy for you. The other way is from shaytan because it is waswas. Waswas & shaytan don't let go of man. Don't say "I made ablution 10 times, he will leave me now". No, he doesn't. Then he puts inside you other things, other things then finally that person cannot even get out of his room. Saying "this is, that is" he doesn't meet with anyone either. Shaytan doesn't find comfort even with this. Until he takes man out of iman, out of religion, the more he tortures mankind, mu'min people as he likes the more happy he gets. Therefore it says in the Ayah that waswas is from shaytan, from bad jinns & we just said the easy way for it now. Be careful about it.

There are also other kinds of waswas, especially like "they did magic, did sihr on me.." There is no such thing! Mawlana says: "If everybody was able to make magic nobody could survive". There is almost no one available now who is able to do it in this time. Allah knows better but such things are not within the reach of just anyone. It is also a big waswas, saying "there is sihr on me" & immediately go to... Don't you believe those who claim to have contact with jinns at all! No matter what they say..Especially ladies should never go to them alone, nor listen to their words. With Allah's permission and dua of Mawlana,you don't need anything else. "I went here, there..This one said you are possessed by jinn. That one said you have magic on you.." Don't deal with such people at all. This is also a big waswas, this 2nd waswas.

There are many kinds of waswas, all are from shaytan. Don't you pay any attention to them. And where does it all come from? Coming from weakness of iman. All of us have weak iman. It is hard to find real iman in these days. There are very few who say "all good and evil is from Allah" and are content with it and beg Allah for cure. As we said, there are many kinds of waswas but all are from shaytan so don't pay attention to them. "This one did that, to you; your aunt did magic on you; we went to this hodja that has jinn. He was able to know his name. This that.." He can say as much as he likes. All of them are frauds. If you search there is definitely nothing behind it. Don't believe in them. Depend on Allah. What Allah orders, happens. May Allah not separate us from iman, this is what is really important. "We are dying because of sihr, etc.." Dying is not important but to die with iman is. If you believe in people who do magic, then you are out of iman. Therefore don't go to people who do magic at all.

Today we speak about doubt or some illness. We make wudhu. It is right or not? Making 10 times. Doubt or whispering doubt. This is big illness. "Min Sharri Al-Waswassi Al-Khannas" (114:4). From where coming this? From shaytan. Because shaytan want people... Allah not put burden on people they cannot carry. He (swt) is not oppressor. He is saying everything for goodness of people & easy for people. And when they do, they be more healthy & more happy. Not giving something to do & they become miserable. So this doubt, everybody has doubt inside themselves. And it is not harmful. When it becomes harmful? If you do this what you do taking, saying for people or you do by your hand. So after this. But when it is inside, nothing.

And for this doubt of cleaning, or for praying- you can pray right or not, you make ablution right. It is, there is certain people, they (have) illness. They have maybe urinating small small, they cannot hold it. This is, they have special permission. They make ablution every time of praying. Example, from when making adhan for dhuhr until asr, it is ok. Even the urine little bit coming from them. There are some of them, they have gas they cannot hold it. Also one time. Every time (prayer time) they must renew their wudhu. So who has doubt and they cannot make it, also they have illness of their mind. So it is ok, take only one until next time (next prayer, then) you can take one more. Every prayer time; like dhuhr, asr, maghreb, isha, every time they must take one ablution. It is ok. No need to make it "it is right, not right, not right.." This is easiness for them. Allah gives permission for that who are worse than them. So it is, of course it is more easy for them, only once enough. When they do more than once this is not from Allah, from shaytan.

So they must think about this, not doing again. Because shaytan not leaving man. Even he make 3 times, 5 times, 10 times... Even not leaving them like this. They make more & more doubt. In the end maybe they not coming to sit with people. They close themselves in their room. This is coming from this illness. So, cure like this. They must listen for nasiha/advice. Don't listen for shaytan.

Many doubt they have, other people also. There is also another doubt. They say "we have magic, black magic". Mawlana Shaykh said "this is, to make this, it is now nearly impossible to find these 2 angels, Harut Marut and they must go there & they find them. Only from them they can take. And maybe you cannot find 5 people in this world like this-they have this magic. Every people say magic. It is not true, this is also doubt. Especially don't go to people who make.. They say about themselves "we make with jinn, we speak with jinn, we make this." Especially for ladies, it is very dangerous for them-these people. They don't have any haya', no shame. Don't go, ladies.I say from here, I know very well what they do, these people. And don't go to who say "we can make magic". Because you are.. if you go there, you lose your faith/iman. And if you die, you may go without faith to Allah. What you do? Only pray, dua of Mawlana Shaykh, put on yourself. And tawakkal ala Allah/depend on Allah.

What Allah said is the best for us. If we will die, we die. Nevermind.. But we die with faith. Without faith, it is not good. Living wothout faith also not good but when you die without faith,it is never mind. If you die early, or old, it is not necessary. Everybody will die but don't make your life miserable with this nonsense thing. Especially if you are following this way, Naqshbandi Way & following Sultan al Awliya Shaykh Muhammad Nazim al-Haqqani don't worry. And don't think. When you think about this thing, you become miserable. Do not be miserable. We said we are only following him, and insha Allah we will be happy.

Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, Al Fatiha.
