Sheikh Mehmet Adil Al-Haqqani sohbat of the 17th of November, 2014.

As-salamu alaikum wa rahmatullahi wa barakatuh. Audhu billahi min ash-shaytani r-rajim. Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. As-salatu wa s-salamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidi l-awwalina wa l-akhirin. Madad ya RasulAllah, madad ya Sadati Ashabi RasulAllah, madad ya Mashaykhina, madad ya Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, madad ya Sheikh Muhammad Nazim al-Haqqani, dastur. Tariqatuna s-sohbah, wa l-khayru fi jam'iyyah.

Allah Azza wa Jalla is telling in Qur'an Azimu Shan, how He describes our life. He both describes it and tells what we should do with it. Our life is like a game: enjoyment, gathering wealth, increasing earnings, showing off to each other, saying "I have more than you. I have more children than you. I am stronger than you". What does Allah, the Creator say? "You are like this". It is like rain: life is like this to unbelievers. When it rains everywhere becomes green with grass, they assume themselves to be something and then they dry up and vanish. Allah Azza wa Jalla says that our life is like this too. Life in this world is not everlasting. And things He said are about people also.

Life is like a game. Life is just an enjoyment. No matter how serious people assume it to be, it's just an amusement. What is its end? There is a heavy punishment after this. There is hellfire at the end of it. What else? There is Allah's forgiveness and paradise. That's why, Allah is ordering "Run and struggle, don't be late. Ask for forgiveness from Allah". Ask for forgiveness from Allah so that He puts you in His paradise. He has paradises. It's larger than the space between earth and sky. Paradise is not a small place. Everyone can fit in. Paradise is very wide. Allah has been given wideness. In this world, even if people live in spacious places, they say they don't fit. Most people say, "I feel bored." And there is spaciousness in paradise.

This is a sign for you to come quickly. Don't leave it. Hurry up. Don't make your repentance late. Don't make your repentance late. Let Allah forgive you and put you in His paradise. Many people ask "Why am I, why were we created? What's the reason for our existence?" Many people ask and make philosophy. And many philosophers think about it. Allah explained it clearly. Who understands, does well. Indeed, when you look at it, if you want to see it clearly, life is a game, an amusement. Play and enjoy, it doesn't matter so long as you do what Allah orders. Don't go for what Allah has forbidden. Do what He ordered and you'll be happy and live in prosperity. Otherwise, you will go to hell.

So Allah's order is telling you to go to paradise. But if you want to go elsewhere, it is your fault. Keep Allah's order, you'll be in comfort both here and hereafter.

Allah Azza wa Jalla described human being life. He is the Creator. He created everything. He is the only one God. Never can be like the philosophers say, for God many gods. One place, it is said, they have 30 million gods. This is nonsense. God - only one God, and He is the Creator. He created all the universe. And He described our life and the meaning of our life.

Many people, they make philosophy. They say "Why were we created? Why are we here? What are we doing here?" So Allah created us and He is showing us what to do, why we are here and how we are, how is our life. He said in Qur'an "Your life in this world is playing, enjoying and gathering money and children and property." It is like this. The shortest way, it is playing. Allah sent us here just to make us to play. But from after how, what will be? It is also like rain coming to a dry place - after weeds coming up very green and very nice. And people, they are like this. "Oh, it is very nice". They thought it will be forever like this.

www.saltanat.org Page 1 But weed, it is only 3 months, 4 months. After when come summer, it will be dry and it will be cut and thrown. Nothing anymore. Our life, it is like this also.

After this, there is punishment and azab. Punishment, torture and what else? There is (asking) forgiveness and there is accepting from Allah. This is the end of our playing. So believer or not believer, all we are doing same things. But if you believe in Allah, you'll get forgiveness from Him. But if you are not believing and doing what is wrong, not doing His order, you're doing wrong, you'll be punished. And Allah Azza wa Jalla ordering "Come quickly, very quickly to take forgiveness, to receive forgiveness from Allah." And Allah, He will give you paradise. Paradise, it is so wide. He explained also - It's like between earth and sky. And it will be enough for everybody. So He is inviting you - "Come quickly. Don't be lazy. Don't do what I am not ordering. I am ordering you to come and take forgiveness and to be in paradise".

This is Allah's order. Not order "Do bad things and I will put you in hell". No, He is not saying this. If you want to go to hell, Allah not ordering you to go to hell, but you yourself are doing this for yourself. So you must be obeying Allah and going to good place, the best place. This is what Allah promises and He is Creator, and it will be like this. This is belief. Who he said, somebody said "You see this?" Of course, because Prophet (saw), he is telling and Allah Azza wa Jalla, who is Creator, He's saying this, we believe in this. This is our belief, and we are hoping inshaAllah, all together we'll be there. And we are inviting according to invitation of Allah Azza wa Jalla - People, come to paradise, don't go to hell.

Wa min Allahi t-tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
