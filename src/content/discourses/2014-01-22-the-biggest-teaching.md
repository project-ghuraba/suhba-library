Shaykh Muhammad Nazim Al-Haqqani An-Naqshibandi, Sohbat of the 22nd of January, 2014.

May we be clean ones. Let's say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. This is it. Bismillah Sharif, Bismillah Sharif. Honored Bismillah. May Allah not let us forget Bismillah Sharif. Let us say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. What shall we do? This is the greatest favour to say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. JanabAllah made us say it. What a great favour it is - Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim.

JanabAllah gives strength to those who recite Bismillah Sharif. There won't be weakness, there won't be sickness, there won't be troubles, there won't be ugliness, there won't be disasters. There won't be ugliness. The one who recites Bismillah Sharif is beautiful. Our Allah has granted such a great favor to His Beloved. "Say, O My Beloved, Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim." Allah Allah. Allah Allah. As long as Bismillah Sharif is recited, strength comes - strength, both evident and hidden. Our disasters will be gone.

Let's say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Allahu Allah, Allahu Rabbi, Allahu Hasbi ma li siwahu. Ya Rabbi, may our burdens be gone. May the heaviness on us be gone. May we become light. May we be clean, may we be beautiful. May we be like lions by saying Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. The one who recites Bismillah Sharif is like a lion. Who doesn't recite is rubbish. Who doesn't recite is not worth a penny. Aman ya Rabbi. May He make us among those who know the value of Bismillah Sharif. Let's say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Don't let us forget, O my Lord. Who doesn't recite Bismillah is a disaster. He is a disaster. Don't be a disaster.

Recite Bismillah Sharif, you'll be light. Bismillah Sharif dresses a man in light, Mehmed. It dresses with light. O our Lord, don't deprive us of that light. The one who recites Bismillah is firm and upright. He is honest and filled with the bliss of love. He says Allah with the bliss of love. O our Lord, it's not our right, however, You have favoured us. Let us recite Bismillah Sharif. How beautiful, how beautiful. Let us be beautiful. Say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim, you'll be beautiful. Say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim, you'll be virtuous. Say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim, you'll possess virtue in the presence of Allah. Say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim so that you are healthy. Tawba ya Rabbi, tawba ya Rabbi, tawba astaghfirullah.

Who recites Bismillah Sharif won't crawl. He won't crawl, he'll go up high. He will go up high. Let's say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. All doors will open. All troubles will go away. O our Lord, you showed kindness to us. Let's say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. O man, say this, be rescued. Be rescued from all troubles. Be rescued from ugliness. Be rescued from problems. Say "My Lord", Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. You won't have troubles, you won't be in hardship, you won't be ugly. Whatever you ask, your Lord will grant. Say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Fatiha.

The feet of the one who says Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim step firmly, sheikh Mehmed Effendi. Say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. You will step on the ground firmly. You won't have troubles, you won't have hardship. You won't be bored and depressed. Your Lord will love you, you will love your Lord. Say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Fatiha.

Ya Rabbi, You granted us the Bismillah Sharif. May we not forget. We won't, inshaAllah. Fatiha.

Don't forget Bismillah. The biggest lesson, the biggest adab is to say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Don't forget. The biggest strength is Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Our weakness will be gone, our ugliness will be gone, our fears will be gone, our hearts will open. We'll be happy. Fatiha. The one who says Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim is beautiful. The one who says Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim is pure, very pure and clean. O our Lord, You created us beautiful. You made us pure with Bismillah Sharif, ya Rabbi. O our Lord, forgive us. The greatest of Your favours to us is to say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Allahu Akbar. Fatiha.

Sicknesses are gone with Bismillah Sharif; worries are gone, fears are gone, troubles are gone, sadness is gone. Allahu Akbar. Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Bismillahi lladhi la yadurru ma'a 'ismihi shay'un fi l-'ardi wa la fi s-sama'i wa Huwa s-Sami'u l-'Alim. You are Subhan, ya Rabbi. Our Allah, our Subhan, our Sultan.

The one who recites Bismillah won't have troubles, he won't be in despair. O our Lord, "Rabbi Ishrahli sadri wa yassirli amri." (20:25-6) (Expand my breast & ease my task). "Rabbi Ishrahli sadri" give comfort to our souls. With what? Say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. May your soul open. Our Allah. On any way say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim.

Read on me. May heaviness on me be gone. There won't be disasters for people. There won't be ugliness. May Allah be pleased. Long live, Mehmed. This is my haji Mehmed. Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. "Salamun qawlan min Rabbin Rahim"(36:58) Your here and hereafter will be prosperous. Be a lion. Say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Say salam to your father, grandfather, to all who ask. Stand like a lion.

Salamun alaikum. -Alaikum as-salam. Long live, Mehmed.
