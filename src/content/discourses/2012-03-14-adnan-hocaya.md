Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. G. Do you have a message for Adnan Hoja? Can we record it?

M. Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Before you start anything, say "Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim". Our spiritual son Adnan Bey, Salam Allahu Alaikum. And salams to all who follow you. Also salams to all the people of Islam. May Allah Almighty reward him & reward you also for thinking of us & making this service for us. My strength is not too much. The matters that need to be spoken are given to his heart, he may go on and wait. Those who are with him, they may wait also. The time is near. Everybody's reality will be revealed. He may look like a civilian but only upstairs they know what he really is. There are very few downstairs (in dunya) who know. Our Prophet (sas) said: "Wa Astaqim" (42:15). Be steadfast, on the right path. Do not be misguided. Who goes on the straight path, is honoured in dunya & in akhirat; is gathered with the prophets & saints. What we want is Istaqim-to be on the right path, as much as possible. Man's ego sometimes wants to set forth his own desire but one who knows himself can distinguish between the inspiration which is heavenly & shaytan's whisperings-waswasa. When he distinguishes, he is saved. If he follows (the waswasa), he is gone. Therefore, if a person does not have someone who will hold his hand he can't know where to go. Alhamdulillah, there is one who will hold his hand. He is not alone, my son Adnan Bey.. He looks like an ordinary person among the people but his reality is different. Insha Allah, upon this reality there will be appearances in Mahdi's (as) time & he will serve him as well. We hope that he, and all of you reach Isa (as) as well.

H.R. Along with you.

M. Our message is this: Be steadfast. Be steadfast. An Arab came into the presence of the Prophet (sas). He said: "O Prophet of Allah, awseeni. Give me an advice". Prophet (sas) said to him: "La taghdab- Don't fall into a rage. Don't get angry". "Advise me again O Prophet" he said. He repeated: "La taghdab". He asked 3 times, and Prophet(sas) told him 3 times:"Don't fall into a rage". It means, "Don't get angry. Don't get furious. Your ego condemns you". Therefore, sometimes negative things come to people. No need to get angry. "Al-hilmu sayyidul akhlaq". Being gentle & good tempered is the best of good character. Who is fiery, burns himself as well as the ones opposing him. He knows. He knows. He sends to his heart. May Allah protect him & protect you. It was kind of you to come all the way here. You made us very happy & pleased with this. May Allah Almighty make you reach the happiness & safety of dunya & akhirat. May your dunya & akhirat be prosperous. May your offspring be from the good ones as well. May the saints be your companions. Insha Allah.

G. Is it needed to look for Mahdi (as)?

M. He himself will come. For what should we look for him? He will look for us. G. Insha Allah.

M. It is like this. He keeps us with the power in his hands. No matter how much we look for him, he has not yet gotten the order to appear, but he will appear after this battle. He will appear in Sham. There is Qadem (in Sham). It is a district that it is on the way to Hijaz. Is it not Qadem? He says Takbeer in Qadem & appears. The moment he says Takbeer, all the firearms will stop operating.All finish! He draws his sword. He has a red horse, a white horse & an all black horse. When he says "Allahu Akbar", it comes up like this. When he says "Wa Lillah al Hamd", comes down. We want Allah to show us this,for this I am preparing myself to go to Sham. I may go to Sham. Sham may open, we are not to leave them there. To Sham- "Alayka bi al-Sham -you must go to Sham" said the Prophet (sas). I don't know what kind of a state it is that I have now. We don't know why they have put us into this situation. There is a wisdom in it. You may see that he sets foot on here & be present there. Many people with such powers now. But all of them, the stirrups of the horses they are riding are held. (When released) He goes like this. What was the name of their plane? Those jets that they are talking about, compared to them (their horses) can't even be like the speed of donkey. Their power is astonishing. It is an astonishing power. Starting from this Muharram, the time that marvels will appear has began. We are in it now. We are walking in it. That power is opened to the saints on the 12th night of Rabiul Awwal. Everything is marvellous-miraculous. They are waiting for the order now. They will finish them within a moment. Aman Ya Rabbi. We entrust you to Allah. In dunya, may we meet under the flag of Mahdi (as) & in akhirat under the Prophet's flag of "Hamd", insha Allah. If they give me a crippled donkey, I may ride on it & follow them.

G. Insha Allah, Mahdi & Isa (as) both will kiss your hands.

M. You may kiss (his hand). My hope is to be able to reach Mahdi (as). I can't rule what is beyond it. But insha Allah you can be under the flag of Isa (as). (Dua) Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Keep us blessed and don't leave anybody in this meeting deprived of Your blessings. Bless us, accept us & forgive us- all those who are attending this meeting and correct our situation. For this, send us the Sultan of both ins & jinn, Sayyidina Mahdi (as). May Allah make him appear. And grant him, bless him. And may Allah accept our prayer. And may Allah support him. Bi jahi Nabi (sas), may we reach it all of us insha Allah. Fatiha. Send our salams to Adnan Hoja, our son. We wish for their good, all of them. May we see those happy days.

G. Insha Allah. Salamu alaikum.

M. Bosnian people, the real of Ottomans will come to Istanbul. Have no fear. Allah bless you. You came all the way.

G. It is an honour for us.

M. May Allah Almighty reward you. I am happy but it has been difficult for you. May Allah make your honour higher.

G. Insha Allah. Allah bless you.
