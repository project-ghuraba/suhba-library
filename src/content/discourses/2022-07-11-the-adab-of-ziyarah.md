Here is Sayyidatinā Hala Sultan’s maqām, palace and beautiful garden. We came to visit her. She accepted us. We are thankful to AllāhNone, we thank her. We weren't going to make a ṣuḥbah in this maqām but this is not a ṣuḥbah but rather we’re saying something to be done. You should know the value of this place.

This is the place of the Sulṭān, of the Hereafter. Whoever enters here should enter with adab. Let him remove worldly preoccupations from his mind. If they have requests, let them tell Sayyidatinā. Make du‘ā’. Now, in shā’a Llāh, Yā-Sīn is to be read, and du‘ā’s are to be made. Whoever wants can make du‘ā’ there and present it to her. A person should enter with adab and leave with adab. You must not leave anything else in your heart.

Don't say this is like this and that is like that to anyone. You should not think about anything else. This is the Sulṭān's place. The Sulṭān herself – the management and everything belonging to this place is all in her hands. We should not interfere with that. We should thank her for accepting us as guests, and wish for our du‘ā’s to be accepted. We should enter and leave with this adab. This is it in short.

We are, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, today, in Hala Sultan palace, garden of Paradise here. She is Sulṭān, her – this area for her. We are guest for her. And guest must be polite, good behaved one, not coming with, to fighting with, or to make... Must come with adab, good behave, polite, with happy to be accepted here and go like this also. This is the adab of ziyārah. If you do this, you will be accepted for her. She will be happy with her, when she is happy with her, with us, we, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam also happy and Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla happy.

But, if you make thing – when water is clear, if you throw some dust or earth, it will be not clear anymore. And water not be clear, it is not nice. So, you come here like clean water. Don't say, "I am right, I am don't, I have this, I have that." No. We are only coming here for sake of AllāhNone, in shā’a Llāh, one, two hour. Make your heart clean, not thinking for anything bad. Don't think you are, you have this, you have that, this do this for me, that done that for you. Don't think for this. We are not coming for anybody. We are coming for Sulṭān. She accept us and we are happy. AllāhNone accept your ziyārah.
