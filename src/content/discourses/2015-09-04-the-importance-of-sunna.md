Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi Rasulillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

The good is in the gathering Inshallah. May there be gatherings. Becasue today is Friday (Juma), it is of great reward to read Sura Sajda and Sura Insan, and it is sunna (the tradition of the Prophet). Why are we saying this? To show how much Shaykh Mawlana used to pay attention to sunna.

He would for sure follow every sunna. Just as he would do the obligatory (fard), he would like to do the sunna the same way. He would not show any slackness or give unimportance. Because the way shown by our Holy Prophet (SAW) is the most important and most suitable way, he would try to exactly do the things he did.

It’s human nature, one Friday because he was not feeling too well, he forgot to read these suras. Shaykh Mawlana started crying after prayer. He said, “I did not once leave reading these suras for 70 years.” He lamented much and was very sad. Then we decided, “Let us pray again.” He was a little relaxed once we prayed like that. So he gave such importance to going on the path of our Holy Prophet (SAW).

The saints (awliya) are like this. People come out and talk much nowadays. There is a saying “All mouth and no trousers”, that is how they make things up. When it comes to prayer, they do not even pray the sunna. They do not even pray the normal proven sunnas (muakkada). These types of people are out all over now. Those who follow them go rotten too. Then others snipe words at Islam. Islam has no blameworthy side. Islam is the most perfect religion. We should follow it. We need to not go astray from the path shown by our Holy Prophet (SAW). It is all shown: the sunna, the fard, the necessary (wajib), and the (mustahab), meaning it is written in books. But if you are not connected to a Shaykh, to a true guide (murshid), you would not even here about it.

These Wahhabis said, “He is out of sharia like this and like that...” The Wahhabis in Malaysia wanted the Shaykh of the Azhar Sharif to give a relgious edict (fatwa) that Shaykh Mawlana is supposedly not on the right path. That holy one said, “There was a conference in America. I stayed with Shaykh Mawlana for about 15 days. He does all the sunnas. In fact, he even does the forgotten sunnas, those that we do not remember. What are you talking about?”

During Morning (Fajr) Prayer, our Holy Prophet (SAW) would lie down like this inbetween the sunna and the fard. That is also a contemplation. That position is made to remember mawt, meaning death. He lies facing the Qibla on his right side then gets up. He told them, “You cannot say anything about this Shaykh.” The highest authority for Muslims in this world and for people of religious law (sharia) says so. The whole Islamic world accepts that place.

Such a person was our Shaykh Mawlana. That is why he was the true guide. Those who follow him have won, thank Allah. Inshallah it will still increase. This Wahhabi crew is a lie. Just like Shaitan is fooling people, it is exactly like that, nothing else. May Allah protect us from their harms.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq.

Al-Fatiha.
