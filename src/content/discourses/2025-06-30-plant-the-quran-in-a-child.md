As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh. A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Inna hādhā l-qur’āna yahdī li-llatī hiya aqwam” (17:9). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘Aẓīm. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says, Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n shows the straightest way. It is a means of guidance. Our Prophet says: “Recite the Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n.” Draw benefit from it. The Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n is the only book in this world that is the true word of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, exactly as He revealed it. There are many books. They speak of the Torah, the Gospel, and others, but they have corrupted them all. They changed them a lot, according to their own thinking and as they wanted. The only book, the heavenly book – the book that descended from the presence of Allāh – is the Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n. “Recite it,” says our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. In it there is healing, there is goodness. Every kind of goodness is found in the Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n. Of course, memorising the Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n is not something everyone can do. Most children are sent to madrasahs to memorise it, and they do. May Allāh be pleased with them. May Allāh protect them. Now, thanks to Allāh, it is summer time. When it’s summer time – it’s found more in Turkey; we don’t see it much in other places. When it’s summer time and schools are on holiday, shukr to Allāh, teachers give lessons in mosques. For a month, a month and a half, or about forty days, children go there every day for one or two hours. They learn Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n, starting from the Arabic alphabet. They learn to read it in its original script. This is a great blessing. May Allāh be pleased with those who do it. Most people also send their children. It is done here in Cyprus as well, but our former waqf (endowment) president, may Allāh be pleased with him, he does all kinds of good deeds. To make the children happier, he would also give gifts to the children who attended the course. Now he is no longer around. But still, they give small gifts to the children, so they will feel interested. Qur’ān is the most necessary thing for a person. Learning Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n is more important than eating, drinking and breathing. The teachers already have beautiful methods. If a child pays attention, they can read in a week. But if they don’t pay much attention, then maybe in fifteen days or at most one month, they will start reading Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n. Within forty days, they can read comfortably. That much is the teacher’s duty. After that, in order not to forget it, we say the children and their families should sacrifice some time, but it is not sacrifice. To gain that time, sit with the child for five minutes. Let the child read that one page so that he continues and does not forget. That one page the child reads, or what he learns of the Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n, improves his entire character, develops his intelligence, and beautifies his morality. It benefits him in all kinds of goodness: helping him to respect his family and to become a beneficial person to his country and to humanity. A small daily act, yet it gives strength to his soul and fortifies his īmān. Therefore, most people do not give it importance. But teaching, learning, and continuing the Qur’ān is the greatest, most important thing. It is a great gain. It is the greatest gain. Those who do not do it live in vain. People today are confused about what to do with their children: “What should we give them?” People today have become slaves to their children. Instead of the children serving them, families serve their children. And on top of that, they do not like it. Reading Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n and continuing it also beautifies one’s morality. He respects his family, he respects people. He becomes a beautiful servant of Allāh. May Allāh grant us all to be good servants, in shā’a Llāh. This is a seed for the child. It is a seed planted inside him that will grow into a mighty tree in the future, a great tree, a barakah, in shā’a Llāh.
