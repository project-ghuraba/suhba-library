As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

When waking up to begin the day, say - it’s most probably a ḥadīth - when asked how’s your day beginning, “Kayfa asbaḥtum?” You say, “Asbaḥnā wa asbaḥa l-Mulku lillāh.” How are you beginning your day, how was this night? When you are beginning a new day, say, “Alḥamdulillah, we are beginning our day with Allah, everything is for Allah.”

Al-Mulku lillah, everything is for Allah. And the victorious is who says, “Aflaḥa man qāl “Lā ilāha illa Allah Muḥammadun Rasulullah ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam.” The winner all the time is who says, “Lā ilāha illAllah Muḥammadun Rasulullah.”

This is what human beings, especially Muslims, must know: everything is for Allah. Since He created this world and created Sayyidinā Ādam ʿalayhi s-salām, every day has been the same; nothing is getting less from Allah’s Mulk (dominion), everything is the same. Don’t worry about life, about this and that. Only be sure you will gain, you will be a winner when you say, “Lā ilāha illa Allah,” you will be an obedient one for Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla.

Now, and in every time, this world has had many issues, many things. People worry about this. But of course, in our days it’s more than other days. But also, nothing has changed for Allah, not for us.

So alḥamdulillah we’ll be happy, and we’ll be happy for those who are saying this, “Lā ilāha illa Allah Muḥammadun Rasulullah.”

May Allah bless you.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
