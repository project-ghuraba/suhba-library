As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam says,<br>
الدالُّ على الخيرِ كفاعلِه
<br>“Ad-dāl ʿala l-khayr ka-fāʿilih”<br>
“The one who guides to good is like the one who performs it.” [Tirmidhi]

The one who shows good deeds, even if he doesn’t do them himself, earns thawāb as if he has done them. Good deeds are a benefit for humanity. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla wants benefit for humanity. Therefore, He sent Prophets for that.

Wherever there is goodness and deeds to be done, Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla sent Prophets there for them to show the ways of goodness. And then there are ṣaḥābahs, awliyā, ʿālims and ʿulamā, hojjas.

If everyone, even normal people strive to do good deeds and make charity, or if they point at them and say, “I couldn’t do this. Can you help them?” they will earn thawāb as if they did it.

Our religion of Islām is for goodness. Whatever goodness there is, doing it is advised.

And we should prevent bad things and say, “This is bad. Don’t do so.” Many people nowadays still don’t know what is good and what is bad in this time. They accept everything. People see the worst things as normal now. Even if you just say this is not right, it will have benefit, because that is something Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla prohibited, that is something against people’s nature. So you will have shown that. And thawāb will be written for you. It will be counted as if you did goodness. And if you say "Alright, do it" about badness, it will be harmful for you. There will be no benefit.

Our Holy Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam always shows the highest levels of goodness and good deeds to people. To all people, not only Muslims. If all people followed the way shown by our Holy Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam the world would be peaceful, full of barakah and beauty. As they have gone out of it, as they have chosen bad ways, there is no benefit to anyone. They get nothing but harm.

Therefore, wherever there is goodness, do it as much as you can.<br>
وَافْعَلُوا الْخَيْر
<br>“Wa fʿalū l-khayra”<br>
“And do goodness.” (Qur’ān 22:77).

This is said in Qur’ān ʿAẓīmu sh-Sha’n. It tells to do goodness as an order. If you can but don’t do it, at least show it to someone else. That is good. If he does it, Allah will grant you from its thawāb. May Allah let us all do goodness.

You don’t have to become hojja or a scholar to do goodness. Everyone can do goodness. Everyone can certainly do it. Therefore, Allah has ordered to do goodness. Stay away from evil.

May Allah let all of us always do goodness, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
