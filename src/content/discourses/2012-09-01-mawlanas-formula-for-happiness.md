As-Salamu Alaikum. Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fil jamia' What Mawlana just said a little while ago is a very important thing for man. Asking "for what does man live?"

Mawlana says a person who lives without an aim cannot be considered a man. Who lives without an aim is from the 4 legged class. There are also 2 legged class-like chicken, chicks. These are animals classes. They are born & they have no aim. Their aim is to eat & drink, grow & reproduce. Even this is not their aim really but Allah has shown them such a way & they are going like this. But mankind- Allah granted man intellect, thinking, mind so that he knows for what he is living. He should have an aim, a purpose. When he does so, he also finds peace.

What is the purpose? To study? To graduate college? After this, to have a job. After finding a job, what is the purpose? Do they think that it is spending the life & then disappearing? This is wrong. The aim, what Mawlana calls as purpose, is pleasing Allah. And how do you do this? It is by serving the nation of Muhammad (sas). Our whole aim, purpose is serving Islam. If we could put this aim in front of our eyes man would find inner peace. Because most people say "I feel an emptiness inside. What is this? Why did I come, why am I going.. I don't know". This is. Now, they call it "material world". It is materialistic. There is no aim, no purpose other than collecting money, playing & having fun. When he does this he thinks that he reaches his goal but he sees that when he collects money, there is still an emptiness in front of him.

Even if he has fun, he plays, goes here there, still he can't save himself from that emptiness. Because he has a real aim, a real purpose that Allah Almighty has sent. And Allah (awj) put it right in front of him, no need to search for it much. And when they ignore it, they carry a very big emptiness along with them. They keep questioning "What is this, what is it not?" Without feeling any peace, rest or contentment. Having troubles & being stressed they try to satisfy themselves with other things. But it is of no use.

Therefore Mawlana says the difference between man & animal is the aim, purpose. Yet animals know their Lord & glorify Him although they will become dust on the Day of Judgement. So they don't care as much. But it is man who has the life of akhirah. The real aim is akhirah. Don't you forget this, it is Mawlana's advice. And you should explain it to the children from an early age that our real aim, goal is pleasing Allah. Being on the right path, goodness- this is the purpose. Pleasing Allah-Ilahi Anta maqsudi wa ridhaka matlubi. What we are aiming for is Allah & we want to please Him. Our aim, our purpose is this. If you always keep this in mind you will be in peace, with Allah's permission. Today Mawlana Shaykh, he was saying for what, why are you living? Most people don't know for what. What purpose & what is the aim, target? What you want to have? It is difference between animal & human being, this is- to know, to have target; to go to where you want to go. Animals, Allah created them. They don't know anything. They are born, eating, drinking & getting big & getting more & more. But they don't have thinking "why you are coming? Where you are going?" They don't have any responsibility. And they are, when they have no responsibility, no need to think for anything. Just eating, drinking and even they are happy. They know the Creator better, more than many people. But Allah, He created them like this. And no need for target for them. Because on the Day of Resurrection they will be also dust.

But for people, there is aim. Without aim, you are like animal. What is aim? To be in good, right way- Allah, what He order & Prophet (sas) what he order. This is our aim, to be good people & to serve Islam & to look for around you to be good Muslim or to help them. This is aim. But for what? For Allah rida/pleasure of Allah- this is our aim. When you do good thing, it is Allah, He is pleased with you. And this is the biggest benefit. Because many people, they have..

First of all, they coming. What is the first aim? To read. After to be in, to take degree from university. After? To have work. After, what? To go here & there. But everytime they do this, who is not with Allah or not thinking about what we say about aim they have big emptiness. Every time they do many thing they feel they are empty. They are in stress. Every time they thought "we are doing good thing". But after, we said, they feel, they have big emptiness. This is real big emptiness because Allah- the aim, you forget the main purpose for you, for human being. This is what Mawlana Shaykh, everybody he must put this aim in his eyes.

For children also you must teach "why you are coming to this life? Where you are going?" The first thing when you teach them this, they will be every time looking what we do good, bad? And they will be satisfied because they have this aim. Many of them, maybe they are not successful in their life. Maybe they don't have job. Many of them, they cannot understand to read or to write. Many of them poor. If their aim to be rich, it is not good. Not everybody can be rich. But this aim- to have pleasure of Allah Jalla Jalaluhu, everybody can do it. Everybody can be happy with this & he will be satisfied. This is formula from Mawlana for happiness of people. Every word he speak have thousand of wisdoms. And today it was very nice from him to hear this also.

Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, Al Fatiha.
