As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
وَقِهِمُ السَّيِّئَاتِ ۚ وَمَن تَقِ السَّيِّئَاتِ يَوْمَئِذٍ فَقَدْ رَحِمْتَهُ
<br>“Wa qihimu s-sayyi'āt wa man taqi s-sayyi'āt yawmaʾidhin fa-qad raḥimtah”<br>
“And save them from the sins, and whomsoever You save from the sins that Day, him verily, You have taken into mercy.” (Qurʼān 40:9). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says in Qur’ān ʿAẓīmu sh-Sha’n, “Protect us from sins. The one whom You protect from sins is the one who has attained Your mercy.” He is within the mercy of Allah. Because sin is something everyone can commit. Some are minor sins, some are major sins. 

Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam says,<br>
كُلُّ ابْنِ آدَمَ خَطَّاءٌ
<br>“Kullu ibn Ādam khaṭṭa'un.”<br>
“Every child of Ādam is a sinner.”

That is, every human being has sins and mistakes. But the best of them are those who repent and seek forgiveness.

You go here and there, there are people who commit sins. Do not consider yourself superior to them. You are not an angel. If Allah wills, He will protect you. So we always make duʿā to Allah to be under His protection. May Allah forgive us, for we too are sinners. May He make us among those who know their sins, and repent and seek forgiveness. Do not look at what others do and say, “I am better than them.” May Allah protect us.

Many states happen to a person throughout their life. Our ancestors used to say, “Do not speak too boastfully, saying ‘I am this, I am that’.” A person who speaks boastfully, when a calamity befalls them, cannot know where it came from and goes on in the state of heedlessness. May Allah protect us, may we not fall into heedlessness. May we know our own state so that we are not defeated by our ego.

Do not say, “I pray a lot, I get up at night for worship; look, these people are always playing and having fun, I am better than them.” Beware! Because this is a favor that Allah has bestowed upon you. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla may grant that favor to them as well. He may change your state with theirs. May Allah protect us.

Therefore, everyone should constantly repent and seek forgiveness for their mistakes. They should look at their own state and not concern themselves too much with the state of others. May Allah forgive us all.

Do not be arrogant towards anyone and do not consider yourselves superior to anyone. May Allah protect us. May Allah protect us from sins. Those who are protected from sins are saved. May Allah help the Ummah of Muḥammad. May He send us the sāhib so that we may be saved from these states, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
