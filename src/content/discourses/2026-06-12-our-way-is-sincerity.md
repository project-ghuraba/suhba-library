As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

The Naqshbandi ṭarīqah, shukr to Allah, is a way that gathers good people so that, inshā’Allāh, people’s morals may become better. It is the way Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla and our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam love. It is the essence of sharīʿah. It does not compromise sharīʿah; it is a ṭarīq, a way. Those who enter this way, those who are blessed by it, are among the beloved servants of Allah, inshā’Allāh.

Therefore, the mashāyikh have followed this way since the time of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam for the service of people and for the pleasure of Allah. May Allah elevate their ranks. May their himmah be present. Their himmah is constant. Without this himmah, the world would not stand.

Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam said, “It is for the sake of those blessed people that rain and barakah come to you, and through them you achieve victory.” All kinds of goodness is for their sake. Because their essence is sincerity. Sincerity is not for personal gain. It is not for anything at all. 

Every day they say,<br>
إِلَهِي أَنْتَ مَقْصُوْدِي وَرِضَاكَ مَطْلُوْبِي
<br>“Ilāhī anta maqṣūdī wa-riḍāka maṭlūbī”<br>
“O, my Lord! You are my objective and Your Pleasure is my request.”

My aim is You, O Allah. What we desire is Your pleasure. Nothing else. People find it difficult to do this; it is hard for them to do it purely for the sake of Allah. Most of the time they do it for personal gain.

Therefore, without their himmah, people cannot be saved. From our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam’s mercy he always said “My Ummah” and asked Allah for intercession, which is a great benefit for this ummah. Without his himmah (support) and intercession, there is no salvation. The way of the one who lacks sincerity, goodness, and beauty is not the right way.

The true way, inshā’Allāh, is the way of those who are with our Holy Prophet. Those who follow him have attained happiness. Their end is beautiful, their lives are beautiful, and their companionship with people is beautiful. They are barakah, they spread barakah around them, and they live with blessings. Through this barakah, inshā’Allāh, their himmah will reach people as well, shukr to Allah.

May Allah keep us on the right way. May He make us a means of guidance for people, inshā’Allāh. What’s important is sincerity. Being sincere for the sake of Allah is the most important thing. 

Associating partners with Allah is called shirk. However, if there is no sincerity, if worship is not pure, then it becomes shirk (polytheism). If you want to harm others for personal gain in your heart – and you mix this into sincerity – that is shirk.

May Allah protect us. May Allah keep us all steadfast on this way, inshā’Allāh. May guidance be granted to all people, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfīq

Al-Fātiḥah.

There are complete recitations of the Qur’ān, surahs, verses, Yasin, tasbihs, tahlilāt, salawāt, recitations of Dalail Al-Khayrāt, and all kinds of good deeds, for the sake of Allah.

For the sake of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, for the souls of his Ahlu l-Bayt and ṣaḥābah. For the souls of all prophets, Awliyā’, asfiya, mashāyikh. For the souls of our ancestors. For the souls of believing men and women, Muslim men and women. For goodness to come and evil to be gone. For the strength of our īmān.

For happiness here and hereafter.

Lillāhi Taʿālā, Al-Fātiḥah.
