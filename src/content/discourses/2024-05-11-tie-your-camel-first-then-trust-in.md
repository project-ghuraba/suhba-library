AllāhNone

As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Wa-man yatawakkal ‘alá Llāhi fa-huwa ḥasbuh, inna Llāha bālighu amrih, qad ja‘ala Llāhu li-kulli shay’in qadra" (65:3). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘aẓīm. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla teaches people what they should do. The instructions are in the Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n and the beautiful words of our Holy ProphetNone. People today, all of them, that is ninety-nine percent; it would be more accurate to say nine hundred and ninety-nine per thousand, are confused about what to do. It was like this before, but now there is more confusion. “What will I do? How shall I act? What will happen? How will I get by? Will I get sick? Will I live?” These all exist in the ego and essence of mankind. The cure for that is: “And whoever relies upon AllāhNone – then HeNone is sufficient for him,” says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.

By relying on AllāhNone, you will know that what AllāhNone wants will happen. “Inna Llāha bālighu amrih” (65:3). What AllāhNone wills will happen. Everything has its time, or its happening. Therefore, if a person wants to be comfortable and live without worry, he must rely on AllāhNone. He must submit to the will and destiny of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Destiny is the secret of AllāhNone. A person must surrender to it. Of course, he must do what he does and what he can do. Then after that, he should rely on AllāhNone and wish for what AllāhNone wants: "Whatever AllāhNone wishes, may we have good wishes. May it be as AllāhNone wants." It is necessary to make du‘ā’ that what AllāhNone doesn’t want and dislikes does not happen. Because you should both rely upon HimNone and follow the path that Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla tells you. You should look at everything. You should work for AllāhNone with the specialties that HeNone has given you. That is what it means to rely on AllāhNone.

To rely on HimNone does not mean to sit around doing nothing. You must do. Then, you must be pleased with what happens. You must accept what happens next. There is a famous ḥadīth of the Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam that says, “A‘qil wa-tawakkal – Tie, then rely upon AllāhNone.” One of the companions left a camel [without tying it] in front of Masjid an-Nabawī of our Holy ProphetNone, saying that he had relied upon AllāhNone. So it was gone. After that, he came to our Holy ProphetNone and said, “My camel is gone.” “What have you done?” “I left it here because this is a Masjid. I relied upon AllāhNone as I will enter to prayer and listen to the good words. I looked and it has run away,” he said. That is a famous holy ḥadīth. “A‘qil” means to tie. Then rely upon AllāhNone. So there is a principle for everything. After following that principle, in shā’a Llāh, it becomes a peaceful life and your hereafter will be prosperous, in shā’a Llāh.

In our days, nowadays, people they don't know what they will do. They are very confused, very stressed, unhappy. Why? Because they are not following what Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone create us and in Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n say, Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Wa-man yatawakkal ‘alá Llāhi fa-huwa ḥasbuh, inna Llāha bālighu amrih, qad ja‘ala Llāhu li-kulli shay’in qadra" (65:3). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘Aẓīm. Meaning, who want to be rest, he must be depend on AllāhNone because everything what AllāhNone HeNone want, HeNone give you. Don't be stressed, don't be anxious what will happen, what it will be. Everything Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, create and HeNone is, in this Holy Book, instruction for everybody what they must do to be happy in life and safe for next life. And this one it's very important, to be accept what Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla want.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gave everyone some specialty, some kind of what they can do. This is not everybody can do same thing, not everybody thinking same. Everybody different business, different job, different thing they can do. And for this, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying, you must do what you can do, what able to do. And after this, to ask from AllāhNone to be accept what you are doing for your life, for next life. Because in this life people they are not happy, "I doing this it is not good, doing that not good." And this is also from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. So you must, if you don't [be] happy with this, you can change until you find what AllāhNone give you specialty, you can do – not to be not happy because... And depend on AllāhNone meaning – you must not sitting and saying, "What AllāhNone He will, HeNone gave me. I will, I am waiting for AllāhNone to give me." No.

Everybody have some work, something to live. And this is meaning which Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam teaching people, teaching ṣaḥābah. From ṣaḥābah we can learn. There is very famous ḥadīth from Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam – "A‘qil wa-tawakkal," meaning... This two word very important because meaning. A‘qil meaning tied. Tawakkal and to depend on AllāhNone after this.

One of ṣaḥābah he came to Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam mosque, Masjidu n-Nabawī, for praying and listening for Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. And by camel he come and he just left his camel front of mosque and he go inside. After coming out, no camel. Looking here, looking there. "Where is my camel?" After, coming to Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying for him: "You tied him?" "No, I just come here. It is holy place so it must be not running away." But it is run away. Even in mosque of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, cannot be without making. First you must do something. After you depend on AllāhNone. For this, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, heNone told him, "You must first tied your camel and after depend on AllāhNone." If you tied and after [it] run away, that time it will be you must be not... But now you do this, you will, you, you tired until you find your camel. For this, everybody must do what they can do and after ask from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla to make succeed and give good thing for them. AllāhNone help us to...

This is the most important for nowadays people – to not forget AllāhNone. They are doing everything but not... They are forgetting. And when something happen, they thought it is end of world, nothing can be good. But everything good still. You are in life, you are praying, you are fasting, you are going, coming. You are have breathe – it is most important thing. Other not important. If your provision finish, you will die. If not finish, you will still in life. And this is from one secret of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla to be happy, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone help us, all of you, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
