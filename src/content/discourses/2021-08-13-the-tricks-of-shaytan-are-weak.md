Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

A’ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm:

(Qur’ān 04:76). Allah ‘Azza wa-Jalla says in Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Shān: ‘Indeed, the plot of Satan has ever been weak.’ The tricks of shaytan are weak. Shaytan struggles and tries, and thinks that he made people go to hell. People do all kinds of evil. But when they repent and ask for forgiveness, all the tricks of shaytan become useless. They become worthless. The sins are erased and Thawab is written. This is good tidings from our Holy Prophet ﷺ to us. Shaytan has no power with permission of Allah ﷻ. Therefore, mankind should make Shukr to Allah ﷻ, for He did not make the tricks of shaytan powerful.

The whole world is in Fitnah and badness now. They are powerless in front of the will of Allah ﷻ. Certainly, they will be gone. Those who are with Allah ﷻ will win. And those against them with shaytan will be swept away. Don’t be with shaytan. Be with Allah ﷻ. Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla gave you all kinds of favors, and there will be all kinds of beauties in ākhirah. Don’t be cheated by the tricks of shaytan. His tricks are worthless and useless. They are harmful. In order not to be harmed, stay away from shaytan. Don’t expect help from him.

When people turn away from Allah ﷻ, they think that shaytan is powerful. They fear him and try to be together with him. Whereas, he has no power. He will be swept away. Those who are with him will be in loss and swept away too. May we be with Allah ﷻ insha’Allah. We should perform more worship and make more Shukr to Allah ﷻ during these holy days. May Allah ﷻ bless them.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
