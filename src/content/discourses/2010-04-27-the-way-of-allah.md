Madad ya Sayyidi.

La ilaha illa Allah La ilaha illa Allah La ilaha illa Allah Muhammad Rasulullah Alihi salatullah wa salamu min al azali ila'l abad, from pre-Eternal up to Eternal. Madad Ya Rijal Allah, amidduna, reach Your weak servant O my Lord. And we are saying audhu Billahi min as shaytani'r rajim Bismillahi'r Rahmani'r Rahim. That gives us honour and it is stamp of King on His seal. Nahnu abidukum, we are your weak servants O our Lord. Strengthen us O our Lord, support your weak servants. You have endless dominions. You have endless oceans through each dominion, You are the Lord of Creation. We are nothing but You granted us such an honour and You said O our Lord, Bismillahi'r Rahmanir'r Rahim

wa idh qala rabbuka li'l mala'kati inni ja'ilun fi'l ardi khalifah (2:30). [Prophet], when your Lord told the angels ‘I am putting a deputy on Earth’.

O our listeners through east and west, O our attenders, Assalamu alaykum. That heavenly salute is for reaching servants and bringing to them heavenly blessings. Therefore it is something urged by the Seal of Prophets Sayyidina Muhammad (sas) to say: Assalamu alaykum. Madad, O our Guide who is responsible for everything happening on this planet. Send O People, to that one your salutes. Assalamu alaykum. Assalamu alaykum Ya Sahib al Imdad. One name of director who is directing everything on this planet, everywhere, because if heavenly support, heavenly blessings, if not reaching someone, it is impossible to be in existence. This is an important point O ulamas, Muslim ulamas from Ahl as-Sunnah, Ahl as-Shia and Salafi ulamas. It is such an important point.

Everything on this planet from biggest one up to smallest can’t be in existence unless it reaches direct support from heavens. Reaching that one, then it is going to appear in existence. We may say whole world in need with its greatness, taking its share but on it and in it, countless beings each one is taking their ranks, tarteeb. The Lord of Heavens, Azamatu Lillah, Glorious Greatness for Allah Almighty. Don't think, O People living through 21st Century, you must try to know something that is real knowledge from heavens; heavenly knowledge is through heavenly books. Yes, you are saying we found elements. Yes. You must think. Because Allah Almighty saying always la'allahum yatafakkarun. Allah Almighty asking from chosen creatures as they have been honoured to be deputies of the Lord of Heavens. Where they are deputies? You must think on it. To whom we have been chosen to be deputies? Don't be heedless. Wake up and look beyond that limit there are unlimited horizons, as you reached only a material horizon and you are getting so proud and claiming that 'we reached top point'. May be top point for material horizon but, material horizon, from where coming? For whom coming? Yes, don't say 'Big Bang', that is foolishness. That is from no-mind people. Not anyone can accept this. That is 100% foolishness because they are drunk people and making such nonsense claims. For what? Because their teacher shaytan saying, 'People will ask you about this world, - beginning of this world or an ending for this world. Now you must take your most attention to occupy minds of people, khalt, (mix). No, more than mix to make their minds in a disabled position so that people may occupy with something useless and not what is reality. Confuse, yes. Because people now are saying 'we reached top of civilisation and technology'.

And people going to be asked, 'What about beyond this? Because 100 years ago, no one knew such a technology now it is coming in existence, you can say for us that the horizon of technology now is last horizon. Can't be beyond that horizon any other horizon’. They ask. Shaytan is teaching them, ‘Therefore you use now useless theories that never mind accepting. But the real maqsad, aim to occupy people on that point they are saying it was only Big Bang, one atom and then exploding and going to be through time, through one billionth of second, coming this, going that. I am sorry, I am sorry. Therefore Allah Almighty punishing Islamic World also. Mostly the crises are through Islamic territories because they left true way and they are munhani, diverting to wrong ways. Astaidh Billah the Lord of Heavens saying:

anna hadha sirati mustaqiman faittabi`uhu (6:153) This is My path, leading straight, so follow it.

The Lord of Heavens saying. Why you are not saying O ulamas such things? Inna hadha siraati mustaqiman. Which siraat? I am asking all Ahl as -Sunnah Ahl as-Shi'a ulama and Salafi ulama, what is that mustaqima? What is that siraat? From where coming, to where going, that siraat? You can say? You don't know? Siratee - My Way, for whoever is asking to reach to Me, to learn, to know, to be, to understand. That is The Way.

La `iwaja lah (20:108) No crookedness (can they show) him. -also ayatu'l karima? Yes, you know. M`awaj tareeq -crooked way. Why you are not saying? If no teacher, no one learning anything. Prophet says that: innama al ilm bi-tta`allum. Ilm, knowledge, particularly real knowledge belongs to heavenly stations that is siraat, direct way to heavens. Why you are not using.. Why you are not giving a meaning about that knowledge? Why you are not asking who is knowing such a knowledge? Why you are being always in hasad, envy? No. What is dunya? For you dunya is so big? And Allah Almighty saying it is only less than a wing of a mosquito. O People, wake up! Don't be enemies to such a person who never is claiming anything for himself only asking to be accepted as a servant from the Lord of Heavens, Allah Almighty. Allah Allah, Allah Allah Allah Allah, Aziz Allah Allah Allah, Allah Allah Allah Allah, Subhan Allah Allah Allah, Allah Allah Allah Allah, Sultan Allah.

National singing, national anthem because they are trying to teach youngsters I am also saying to all Kings, Queens, Sultans, they are teaching only national anthem you are so, so careful and in our countries Turkish people also they have a law, that if their national anthem is playing if sitting, not standing up, they are taking that man to prison. O our Lord forgive us, forgive us Ya Rabb. We are so-wrong-people, wrong and useless servants. He grants us countless, countless favours and we are not taking any care to give at least an honour for honouring Azan-e-Muhammadi (sas). Stand up, even one moment is enough.

You will make your Lord happy with you and He grants to you, dresses you in honour. Dresses on you heavenly lights and He is saying ‘My servant, giving His full respect for My holy name’. O our Lord forgive us. O our Lord, forgive ourselves. We are sinners, we are sinners. Send us someone to teach us the way of heavens. Siraat, Siraatullah. May Allah forgive us. Tawba Ya Rabbi, tawba Ya Rabbi, tawba Astaghfirullah Fatiha.

Only heavenly lights, O our Lord, to make You happy with us, that is our real aim. To make You Almighty pleased with us. Try O People, to make your Lord pleased with you. May Allah forgive me and you. Fatiha
