As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Fa-inna ma‘a l-‘usri yusra. Inna ma‘a l-‘usri yusra" (94:5- 6). Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is saying that for Muslims, there is always relief after hardship. A difficulty goes away, and there is ease. On the other hand, when those who don't have īmān (belief) experience troubles, they lose hope. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla promised those with īmān that there is ease and relief after difficulty. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla said it is so. For two years we haven't been able to come here during Ramaḍān. There were problems because of Covid. The whole world went through great hardship. Everyone was confused and lost. Those who believe in Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, those with true īmān, didn't have hardship. Because they know that every single thing, every hardship, ends with relief. Suffering isn't in vain. Certainly, AllāhNone rewards for it. It isn't wasted. It has great benefit for ākhirah. It brings great goodness.

Now, there are troubles everywhere in the world again. But the promise of AllāhNone won't be broken. The promise of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla doesn't change. After every difficulty, there is ease and relief. Therefore, believers should not forget this. Whenever they are troubled, they should remember this. They should trust in AllāhNone. "Ḥasbuna Llāhu wa-ni‘ma l-wakīl. Lā ḥawla wa-lā quwwata illā biLlāhi l-‘aliyyi l-‘aẓīm." This is the treasure of AllāhNone. They should recite this so it becomes easier, in shā’a Llāh.

Mā shā’a Llāh, a lot of our brothers have been able to come here after a very long time. In shā’a Llāh may they not have any difficulties. May they always be at ease. May we always have strong īmān, in shā’a Llāh.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying in Qur’ān, Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Fa-inna ma‘a l-‘usri yusra. Inna ma‘a l-‘usri yusra" (94:5-6). Two time HeNone make this to be, for people to be sure: after difficult time, it will be best – better time. This is for believer. For mu’min who believe, it will be like this. Nothing going forever. It will be change. And HeNone is promise – Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. And we are – Two years now, it was big difficult for whole world, for whole human being. Two year of closing, pandemic, other thing, many thing, we don't know what happen. But it was not for one country, two country – it was for whole world country. Two hundred country. Two hundred, they are have problem, even the smallest one, even biggest one. They was suffering from this. And many people, they are become... illness from this. But al-ḥamdu liLlāh, now it open.

After two years we are here. Two year we haven't been in Ramaḍān in this place – this blessed, holy place, with Mawlānā, to celebrate Ramaḍān, to practice Ramaḍān. For tarāwīḥ, fasting, praying and in shā’a Llāh, after two days it will be i‘tikāf also. Many of our people, they will be for i‘tikāf here also. So al-ḥamdu liLlāh, this finish. But there is of course, another problem in world. People not rest, not happy. It is normal for end of time. But also, for believer, this difficult they will get paid. They will get reward for difficult they have in their life. If you are not believer, nothing. Only you suffering without any benefit. But if you are a believer, if you suffer you will be reward. AllāhNone reward you for this. But we must be believer.

If you are believer, you are happy, you are rest, you know this coming from AllāhNone. Not from that or from this or from there, from here. This coming – AllāhNone make them, this people or countries, to do this with HisNone will. Without HisNone will, nothing can be. So, we are accepting and al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we ask Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla to help us, to not make it difficult for us. Because "yaj‘ala l-ṣa‘bi hayyinan." AllāhNone can make difficult easy, by HisNone hand to make everything easy. And we ask for us, after all this difficult, to be after this easy for us. Many of our people also, they are coming after two years. Many of them, they couldn't go out from house, from their country, from their village. Many, two years they are suffering; but al-ḥamdu liLlāh, now opening, and we are thanking AllāhNone for this opening.

AllāhNone help us, AllāhNone give us strong īmān, in shā’a Llāh. Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
