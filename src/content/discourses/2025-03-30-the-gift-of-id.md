As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

In shā’a Llāh, may this ‘Īd be blessed. May it be a means of goodness. May AllāhNone accept your acts of worship and obedience. We rejoice on these days, for the sake of AllāhNone, as thankfulness for these favours that AllāhNone has given us. May AllāhNone help us.

No matter how much difficulty Muslims face everywhere, this ‘Īd is a barakah for them. It is a grace from AllāhNone. AllāhNone looks at everyone with HisNone gaze. HeNone looks at them with HisNone gaze of grace. HeNone gives everyone their reward. Of course, the rewards of those who are patient despite their hardships are much greater. Now people are oppressed and troubled in many places. They have no home, no land. But they are happy with this favour given by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. No matter how bad their situation is, they are still thankful for the grace of AllāhNone and the favours HeNone has given them.

The greatest favour is the favour of īmān. Even if the world is destroyed, it is not important as long as you have īmān. Even if the whole world is yours, it has no value as long as you do not have īmān. Therefore, may this ‘Īd be blessed for everyone, in shā’a Llāh. May AllāhNone increase goodness, may it be the means for goodness. May HeNone send the Ṣāḥib. May it be a greater ‘Īd, in shā’a Llāh.

The old mu’adhdhins would make du‘ā’ and ṣalawāt for ‘Īd and so. They would also say beautiful things. They have qaṣīdahs. “Laysa l-‘Īd li-man labisa l-jadīd, innamā l-‘Īd li-man khāfa l-wa‘īd,” they would say. It means ‘Īd is not merely for the one who wears new clothes. ‘Īd is for those who fear AllāhNone. If a person fears AllāhNone, ‘Īd is for him. Because that is the real ‘Īd. AllāhNone looks at him with a gaze of grace. HeNone looks at him with a gaze of mercy. HeNone looks at those people with a gaze of consent.

Therefore – in old times, new clothes were worn on ‘Īds. Now, every day: children don’t like one dress and want another. In old times, clothes, shoes, and such were bought from one ‘Īd to another. They were worn until they were full of holes. That’s why when ‘Īd came, people would be happy with these new clothes. We are saying it from that perspective. But that is not important. What is important is to fear AllāhNone and to be on the way of AllāhNone. ‘Īd is for that. ‘Īds are spiritual occasions. It is a gift from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Therefore, during these ‘Īds, one should be visiting relatives and visiting the graves of the deceased and reading for them. On the morning of the day of ‘Īd, by the wisdom of AllāhNone there is the ‘Īd prayer. There are many who do not pray at all. At least, they pray a prayer from ‘Īd to ‘Īd. That is also a benefit. Therefore, ‘Īd is pure goodness and pure beauty for Muslims in every respect. Everything is in this ‘Īd.

Of course, children in old times had places to go to for ‘Īd. They would go in ‘Īd. They would play there and do all kinds of fun things for children. Now there are not that many anymore. Because a thousand things were made for them to play every day. Even if the ‘Īds no longer have the joy they had in old times, they still have joy in being near Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Even if there weren’t such toys, AllāhNone gives that beauty to the inside of a person on ‘Īds. You won’t find this in any other holiday – not some random holiday, not a holiday of wood, a holiday of logs, or a holiday of fire; none of them matter. These beautiful ‘Īds of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla give people physical and spiritual happiness and goodness. May AllāhNone bless them. May they be a means of guidance for people, in shā’a Llāh.

First of all, we are, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, thanking AllāhNone and we are happy with this ‘Īd. We wish best thing for for our believer, for Muslim, for mu’min, for all of them. We wish best thing, because it is time which AllāhNone make it for happiness, ‘Īd. For everybody, AllāhNone send this. Who happy with this, AllāhNone happy with him. Of course, everybody happy. Whole Muslim world happy, which it is, many country they celebrate today. Some of them, maybe tomorrow they will celebrate. And it's okay, because there is...You must see moon, you must see... But some place there is cloud, which is Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying if you cannot see with cloud, you make it later. So many country, they make it tomorrow. Many of them, is today. So, it's okay. It's not important. The important thing, to be AllāhNone happy with us.

Many people they are living in very tough time and not, they don't have anything. They have no house, no, all destroyed. But still, if you see them, the children and other, they are happy for ‘Īd, for this ‘Īd. And this is from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Because HeNone gave this belief for them, they are not worried about anything. AllāhNone reward them more than which people they are living in peace, and they are not happy. So AllāhNone make this people more than happy in ākhirah, because they suffer, and they are patient, and they accept from AllāhNone.

For us also it is some gift from AllāhNone for whole Muslim. These days not look like another days, because even once a year or twice a year, some people only praying this Ṣalātu l-‘Īd. Which it is, beginning from morning until evening, whole day blessed, very blessed day. Even from last night, the most blessed night and day in the year one, of them this ‘Īd night and ‘Īd day. So, AllāhNone, HeNone become happy and give happiness for you, if you are happy, and ask what you want from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.

Our, the best thing you wish: to give you īmān, strong īmān. You will happy if whole world, not... Whole world, nothing in world, you don't have anything, you are happy with īmān. This is the biggest gift from AllāhNone. And other, if they have whole world, no īmān, no benefit, nothing at all. For this, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are, with this ‘Īd, we celebrate, real celebrate. Even, anything in this world happen, all this gift from AllāhNone for people, which they will be reward in ākhirah. They will be happy, not regret. Regret people, who are not appreciate what AllāhNone gave them, and they are looking for only dunyā. AllāhNone give hidāyah (guidance) for all of us with this day of ‘Īd, and give us from HisNone endless mercy, endless treasure, in shā’a Llāh, strong īmān, in shā’a Llāh.

AllāhNone bless you, and make, in shā’a Llāh, we hope to meet Sayyidunā l-Mahdī soon, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
