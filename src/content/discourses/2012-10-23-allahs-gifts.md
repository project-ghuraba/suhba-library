As-Salamu Alaikum. Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fil jamia'. Insha Allah we may say a few words with the intention of an association.

Allah Shukur, it is Eid after 2 days. Eid- eid means relief, happiness. In Turkish when a person is very happy they say "it is eid/feast for him". Allah (awj) established 2 Eids, Eid al-Fitr & Eid al-Adha. People made a lot of other special days & feasts but neither of them has a happiness in it, a joy in it.. They do as they like & seem to be happy but in reality there is no such thing.

When Allah establishes Eid He (swt) also gives people joy & happiness. What is the reason for this happiness? Allah's Mercy comes upon people, comes upon the believers. He (swt) forgives the sins of who has an atom's weight of iman in his heart & respects this Eid. He (swt) already starts to give His gifts even before the Eid comes, to those who fast before this Eid al-Adha comes. It is a fast of 10 days-the last day of Dhul Qidah & 9 days from Dhul Hijjah, it completes 10 days. There is Hadith Sharif - for each day is granted the blessings of 1000 years. And for Arafat, for tomorrow, he is granted the blessings of 10 000 years. For those who fast, there are such grants. All their sins are forgiven. Of course when this happens - when such a burden is lifted and such grants come that is how one makes a feast; finds joy & happiness. At that time he reaches the true meaning of Eid.

People establish some other special days, only.. Now every month, in every 15 days there are either the special days people established, or the feasts they call "this day, that day" & all of them are money pits. "Did you get me a gift or not?" "Gift for your mother, father, teacher or valentine.." They invented nonsense days & occasions but all of them are money pits. They have no meaning nor do they have any benefit. On the contrary, they established these days only for people to make money through one another- The Jews, money traders established especially for this. But when Allah (awj) establishes..

Allah has 2 Eids only, no other. And He (awj) is the Lord Who gives without expecting anything in return. His Prophets & His awliyas, His beloved ones are the same way. All others establish feasts for a personal gain. Many times it is said "He doesn't even greet a person if he has no self-interest". So their special days are like this. May Allah make us reach many Eids insha Allah.

Today is the day of tarwiyah, it is also a very holy day. Who fasts today takes the reward of Ayyub (as), said the Prophet (sas). And who fasts tomorrow, the day of Arafat, takes the reward of Isa (as). Alhamdulillah today, after 2 days there is Eid, feast. Allah (awj) give for Ummat al-Muhammad (Sas) two Eid, two feasts. One for Ramadan, one for Qurban (sacrifice)- this Hajj Eid. Of course people make many days. They say everyday "this feast, for this feast, for other thing.." It is no. Feast, it must be.. With feast, happiness and pleasure. You cannot find in another day this two thing, or many thing also in Eid. Eid meaning, the biggest feast for human being even. Why? Because when somebody he has something very happy "it is feast for me" he says, "very happy day for me. Very good day for me" because he has something he like too much.

Allah (awj), when He make feast for all people He is giving full of gifts for people. Especially forgiveness, He give forgive people who believe in Him & make tawba. Everything He forgive for them from Eid. Even who is fasting 10 days for 1 year, all one year before from Eid whole life, Allah forgive him for what he do. And who, even another gift, who fasting 10 days before Eid, for each day Allah give him for each day thousand year blessing. For Arafat day, for tomorrow, 10 000 year blessing giving for him. This is gift & taking this what you do from sin, from bad thing He forgive you. It is on you like mountain. So you are not you cannot feel good when all this on you. For this Allah, when you believe & you happy & you happy for this-you have belief in your heart. Allah take all this from you & give you this gift, it is real feast for this.

Mawlana was saying "I was whole my life fasting many times 10 days. But last years he was fasting 8th & 9th. 8th Yawm al-Tarwiyah, 9th Yawm al-Arafah. Who fasting, Hadith saying, today he is getting blessing like Ayyub, Prophet Ayyub (as). For tomorrow, who fasting tomorrow- yawm al-Arafah, blessing like Isa (as) Allah give.

It is like this, when real real happiness this. Not for like other people they making feast for everything every month, every 15 days, you said "this is day for lover, this is day for teacher, this is day for boy, this is day for girl.." This is only trap for to take money from people, to just take their money. For this, people even when this day is coming near they be feeling more pressure on them, more stress on them. "What I will do now, I must give this gift for this. But no money, I must take credit or something like this". And he looking, "maybe this, they will not be happy with this. What I will do, what I will do.." Like this making because this is shaytan days, not real days. So they feel bad from beginning. And when it is finished, they may be fighting with each other. So it will be, they said "if maybe, if not happen these days, it is better". This is shaytan & his follower days.

But real Eid, ours. Allah (awj) gift from His Self. And give barakah & pleasure & health also in this, & wealth in this Eid. When people following Him & doing what He orders all this blessings coming on them & they will be happy. Insha Allah many years with this happiness with our Mawlana Shaykh Nazim Haqqani insha Allah & with Mahdi (as) also insha Allah.
