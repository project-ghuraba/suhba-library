As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn.

Today is a blessed day, the most blessed day. In the presence of AllāhNone, the most blessed day of the year is Yawmu ‘Arafah. It comes once a year during ḥajj. When you stand on ‘Arafāt at this time, your ḥajj is accepted with the permission of AllāhNone. It cannot fall on another day. Why? Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla arranged it this way and wants it this way. HisNone wisdom cannot be questioned. You should be happy that we were given these days. These days are precious days. These are the days when our prayers are accepted.

The pilgrims are standing on the plain of ‘Arafāt now. Those who were meant to, have gone for waqfah (standing at ‘Arafāt). Those who were not, didn’t. Whether due to an obstacle, or something else, we should know it comes from AllāhNone. Even if there are reasons, it is not our business. We should accept our fate. If AllāhNone wills, even if the whole world is against you, it will happen. If HeNone doesn’t, even if it is easy, it won’t happen. People of ṭarīqah should have trust. They should rely on AllāhNone and be pleased with AllāhNone. Today, this blessed day has coincided with Friday, and it is Ḥajju l-Akbar. May AllāhNone let all of us attain its reward, in shā’a Llāh.

Because when people ask sincerely from their hearts, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gives. The treasures of AllāhNone are endless. They have no limit, no amount. The treasures of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla are unlimited. HisNone favours and gifts are endless. Therefore, ask as much as you like. It is especially accepted when you make du‘ā’ (supplication) today. This time is the most acceptable. Don’t wait around until the evening. Make du‘ā’ continuously. Make ṣalawāt and tasbīḥāt (glorifying). There are special recitations for today. Work on completing them until evening. And ask that the angels complete what you cannot. We should make intention for all of this. With such an intention, we will gain these precious virtues. These valuable virtues are not like the garbage and dirt of this world; you don’t see them. And since you can’t see them people don’t value them. But afterwards, when they see, they will regret it. Shukr to AllāhNone, our intention is to do these things and reach these valuable gifts. Even if we cannot do all of it, AllāhNone will give. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gives according to intentions. And that is good tidings for us. It is something special from AllāhNone for the nation of MuḥammadNone. The other nations had to complete everything. It is the holy word of our Holy ProphetNone: “Innamā l-a‘mālu bi n-niyyāt.” Actions are according to intentions. If you made an intention but could not do it, it will be written for you. As we said, today, Yawmu ‘Arafah, is a blessed day. Tonight is from the most blessed nights of the year. And tomorrow, in shā’a Llāh, it is ‘Īdu l-Aḍḥá. That day also is a big gift for us from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says that for each hair on each sacrificial animal, Allah ‘Azza wa-Jalla forgives ten sins and writes ten rewards. HeNone raises him ten ranks higher. There are probably millions of hairs on one animal. Calculate yourself how much that would be. Leaving that aside, it is more accepted if you keep one third of it for yourself, give one third to friends, and one third to the poor. If you are needy, you can keep all of it for yourself. You will still earn that reward. You won’t be deprived of that reward if you make the sacrifice and don’t give any away. On the Day of Judgment, the sacrifice you made will take you out of your grave and give you a ride to paradise, to the pool of Kawthar, with Allāh’sNone permission.

This feast is the real feast. They say there is rabbit’s feast, there is father’s feast, and this and that. And they neglect this ‘Īd. Those are not holidays. This is a holiday. What is a holiday for us, and what brings good tidings for us, are the everlasting gifts that AllahNone gives. May AllāhNone make it blessed. May AllāhNone accept it.

Today, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, the day of ‘Arafāt. This meaning they [will] – this until Maghrib – these pilgrimage [pilgrims], they must stay in this certain place, on certain date. Only one day in the year, this. Ninth of Dhū l-Ḥijjah, you must be in this ‘Arafāt. There is a border, there is a limit of ‘Arafāt. You must be in this limit. When you are in this limit, you will be – until Maghrib, you will be Ḥajj. Accept you... you done the obligatory... fifth of orders which we must – if they have money and they have health and they can go – this order for every Muslim and Muslimah, they can do this. But it is only this day until Maghrib, not before... Until Maghrib you will be complete your religion, you will complete your duty for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. And this AllāhNone will give you. What HeNone will give you? HeNone will make you pure from sins, like a baby come from mother, newborn baby. Nothing at all. But this, you cannot say like other people they say: “We are going pilgrimage.” We see they are going many countries, they are coming here, they are coming there. And they say this is pilgrimage. It is not pilgrimage. They imitate and they are not real.

Real [is] only this, which Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla put. HeNone put a value for everything. So we must respect this value, and not say this. Because many shayṭān people, they make people to be waswasah (whispers of shayṭān). “Shirk” they said. If its shirk, how it is AllāhNone give? For some place, for some people, for some date, special – speciality. It is from AllāhNone, this gift from AllāhNone. So you must praise our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, Ṣaḥābah, Ahlu l-Bayt, Awliyā’u Llāh; all these praised from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, as we said for this day of ‘Arafāt and place of ‘Arafāt. This day is once a year. It is the most valuable day whole year. Because other time, many time. Thirty days you can fast, Ramaḍān. Praying – everyday praying. But this only – for ‘Umrah also, you can go every time – but for Ḥajj, only this day and this place. So, it is a very special day, very valuable day, very important day. For this we are also, here or another place of the world, it is blessing for this day and this place. Going through east and west for people, and AllāhNone accept du‘ā’.

And we can also intend to from AllāhNone – asking from AllāhNone to accept, give us reward of this people who are in ‘Arafāt. In this time we cannot go, but our intention to go. But intention not every time becoming true. But AllāhNone accept for your intention. Because now, this year Ḥajju l-Akbar, al-ḥamdu liLlāh. And normally when Ḥajju l-Akbar coming it is maybe ten million people. Now only... one million people. And people they are angry. They said, “This not allowed us, that not allowed us.” This is also wrong. Because when AllāhNone want something for you to be in this time, this place to be ḥajjī, you will be – nobody can prevent you. And if HeNone not – HisNone will not for you to be there, all world if they coming, you cannot go reach that place. This is also from AllāhNone, and this is a teaching of ṭarīqah also; to not say, “This, that, if that was.” This is program from AllāhNone, you will be in ḥajj this time and you will be that time.

Because HeNone, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla... When Sayyidunā Ibrāhīm ‘alayhi s-salām built Ka‘bah, nobody was there. Only he and Ismā‘īl ‘alayhi s-salām, his son. When finished this building, this building Ibrāhīm ‘alayhi s-salām he built. How this huge building can be built by one? Because with mu‘jizah – miracle. This maqām Ibrāhīm was like forklift – ya‘nī, no tashbīh (comparison), tashbīh it is wrong. But like, if he take this, and this coming up until up, coming, going and coming this maqām where he step. So he finished building and Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla calls, tells him: “Call people for ḥajj.” Ibrāhīm ‘alayhi s-salām, he was calling; nobody around, it is only desert. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying, call people, and with order of AllāhNone. He make adhān and calls people for Ḥajj. And this wisdom, after Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying: “You will say this and until Qiyāmah, who has naṣīb (destiny) will hear this. For who you call him, he will come. Who he didn’t hear, he will not come this, for Ḥajj.” For this, don’t be sorry: “We couldn’t go. They not let us. This is very expensive, not cheap.” This is will of AllāhNone. We accept.

But the best thing, if you couldn’t go, to make intention and Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla HeNone accept your Ḥajj, and reward you as [if] there, in shā’a Llāh. This is for today, we must, in shā’a Llāh, from here until Maghrib, you try to make your wird (recitations), especially a thousand times of Ikhlāṣu sh-Sharīf. Ṣalawāt, others you can do also, but the important one, to make a thousand times Sūrah: “Qul huwa Llāhu Aḥad” (112:1) – Ikhlāṣu sh-Sharīf, in shā’a Llāh. And this night also very important, very blessed. AllāhNone gives HisNone gift. HeNone gives like HeNone not fear to finish. Because HisNone what HeNone owns, it is no end for this. For this, if somebody is a very rich man, he will give, he will think maybe it will finish. But Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla not finish.

For this, this night also very important, very blessed night, and tomorrow, in shā’a Llāh ‘Īd. It is also for us, also very precious and very important day – day of ‘Īd, which it is for Aḍḥá: to slaughter sheep or camel or cow. This is also Wājib for – more strong than sunnah, less than farḍ. Wājib. It is for Ḥanafī. But for others, Sunnah Mu’akkadah; they must do also. But for this also for us benefit, for who cut this. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying, for each hair of sheep or goat or camel or cow, AllāhNone forgive ten sins from us, and reward us ten rewards, and make us ten degrees higher. So, you can imagine one sheep, maybe more than a million hair they have. And AllāhNone, HeNone is not afraid to give us all this. But HeNone make it for us present for ‘Īd, for feast, day of Aḍḥá. This is real ‘Īd, real feast. Not like what they are inventing every time, to spend money for nothing, for nonsense thing. This is, when you spend this, you can eat third of it, third for friend or relative, third for poor people. This is for normal, you can do this. But if you are not rich, you are poor, even you cut and you can – without giving anybody, you can take for yourself. But what happens here – also same. For each hair, same ten rewards, ten forgiveness, ten degrees. Not becoming less, also same. So, this is Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla what HeNone gives us, it is barakah and shifā’ and happiness, and every good thing in it.

Don’t listen to crazy people who are drinking the whole day, and giving money for nothing. And after, they are eating whole day meat, meat, meat, not nothing. But when you cut and give for people who are – maybe in one year they can eat once or twice meat, they become angry. “You are killing animal, you are doing this, you are doing that.” And this is most ridiculous this. In Austuralia they killing camels. In America they, one man what eating in year, maybe one tribe in Africa cannot eat. But when they coming for Islām to attack Islām, they doing this for people to think Islām, it is savage and not good. Islām, it is perfect for every human being, for everything in this world. If you keep it, whole world it will be safe. When you are not keeping, it is like this: mess, and it will finish with nothing. AllāhNone help us. AllāhNone give Muslim people also, to not be cheated with shayṭān and his followers, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
