As-salāmu ‘alaykum wa raḥmatu Llāhi wa barakātuhu.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Wa ṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīn wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh madad yā Mashāyikhinā dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistāni, Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī dastūr yā waliyu Llāh madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh we are gathering here today first of Ṣafar, Ṣafaru l-Khayr. And they said beginning of building this mosque it was one Ṣafar, also first of Ṣafar. AllāhNone create everything and for benefit of believer, for human being, but you must be believer. For this - day, night, and sun, earth, sea, wind - everything created by AllāhNone and when, AllāhNone want good for believer, all it will be for them, in shā’a Llāh, helping them. This month of Ṣafar it is heavy but ProphetNone saying for this month - Ṣafaru l-Khayr, meaning good Ṣafar. Mawlānā Shaykh also saying, Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim qaddas Allāhu sirrahu, he was saying, good for us, bad for non-believer.

And there is of course something also to do to more protected - to say three kalimatu sh-shahādah every day, three hundred astaghfiru Llāh, and to give charity. Charity is very important. It is taking away balā’, every bad thing, keep and make our life longer. This is ḥadīth of ProphetNone: "Aṣ-ṣadaqatu tarfa‘u l-balā’ wa tazidu l-‘umur." ProphetNone saying: "ṣadaqah keep balā’ away and make your life longer." So this month it is good for believer to remember AllāhNone, to ask for protection from AllāhNone, and make tawbah astaghfiru Llāh, and make charity. Of course there is not million [but] billion of wisdom from AllāhNone and Prophet'sNone saying but this is one of them. Because as we said in beginning, everything AllāhNone create and they are under order of Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla. When believer they are real believer, it is becoming help for them not desire.

Once one from awliyā’u Llāh, they are fighting because maybe before eight hundred years near Damascus or Palestine, fighting against crusader and there was wind. There was nearly wind coming against this Muslim army and facing them, making trouble for them. And there was this awliyā’u Llāh, his name ‘Izzu d-dīn, he order to wind: "O wind you are coming wrong side. We are in trouble. You must go other side on them." This wind coming on them, they destroy them. Many of them was killed or captured and they left. They run away. So it is like this. This everything AllāhNone create for mu’min, to serve mu’min. Serve - musakhkhar, musakhkhar meaning it is for them; they can use, they can take help from them, they must help them. So al-ḥamdu liLlāh we are in, we are thanking AllāhNone and asking khayr of Ṣafar to be khayr for us, the bad for oppressor people and against mufsidīn, munāfiqīn. In shā’a Llāh, AllāhNone keep Muslim safe from every badness. Because for barakah of... we see now we are near great waliyu Llāh, ‘ālim, strong believer. He and his friend or his friend or his brothers, they are all this big island. AllāhNone make them easy for them to give hidāyah for this people. And they are until Qiyāmah also hidāyah (guidance) for this island and protection also. They was wise also because, without any fighting, they make this million of people to come to Islām.

AllāhNone give us from their barakah, from their blessed to bless us for them, sake of them. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh we are, in shā’a Llāh, now we visit them now.

Thank you for you. Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq al-Fātiḥah.
