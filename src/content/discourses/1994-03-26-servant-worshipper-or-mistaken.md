We are claiming that we are servants. Really, we are servants, we can't be anything else because after servanthood coming Lordship of Allah Almighty. La yumkin, it is impossible to be a second lord. Lordship only for one, from pre-eternal up to eternal. Everyone in pre-eternal, they are servants up to eternal. They are servants, no one going to be lord. With our services we are not going to be lord. No!

That must be well-understood because some foolish ones - there are some foolish ones that they are saying, "we are not in need for praying, just we finished". It is big mistake because if servanthood finishing, going to be lordship and no room for any other lord to be given lordship through existence. And the existence of one lordship must be. Can't be together two lords.

As you can't find even in a small office to be two directors. You can't find through a country two presidents. You can't find in a territory two kings or two emperors on same empire. Can't be. How a person can say or can imagine to be through this universe a second lord? And no one can be able to give his servanthood to his lord in much more perfection except Sayyidina Rasulullah sal Allahu alaihi wa sallam and he is saying "I am His servant". Never asking to reach to that point to say. It is impossible. It is impossible.

Abd servant, servant forever and Lord is Lord forever. Now we are saying two kinds of servanthood. One servants, they are good servants. And some others, bad servants. One servants they are obedient servants. Another group, disobedient servants. One group best servants and another group cursed servants, but yet they are servants. And you as a servant you must know in which way you are going to be blessed servant. And whom they are going to be cursed servants. What making them to be cursed and what making them to be blessed? Everyone must know such a things. They're establishing hundreds of universities but they're only ignorance centres. Why not teaching people how you should be blessed servant? Even Islamic countries know that. Islamic countries also they're not teaching how a person going to be blessed servant and how a person going to be cursed servants. There is such a lesson through your madrasah, through your schools, through your high schools? What is high schools? High School.

What does it mean 'High School? Hmm? For what becoming High School? It is on moon? Or on Saturn, or on Neptune, or on Mars? Where High School? Not on Earth? Where it is? Why High School? Putting on stick, to be there? Or on Post Office top? Therefore it is High School? What is that, High School! What High School? And not teaching who is blessed servant, who is cursed servant.

How we can reach His blessings, Almighty Allah's blessings. How we can reach In Divinely Presence high positions and stations? And how a person going to be cursed. High School, Academy, what it's teaching? And then good servants also, they are two kinds. Al abdu wa l-abidu. Fi abd, fi abid. One servant, good servant that we are saying abid, means worshipper. Worshipper. And another servant, good servant, they are servants also worshippers and they have another characteristic or speciality that we are saying that worshipper that is servant.

Worshipper, he may do his service as he has been ordered and also he is obedient servant but he has something, yet his ego not going to be surrendered. Yet he is pressing his ego, forcing, pressing and ego is obedient by force. If you are leaving, getting back as it was before. Mutiya' - he is obedient but by force worshipping. Yet he has something through himself that he may say "I like this, I don't like that". Rubama (maybe), when he is saying "I like this", Allah Almighty does not like that one. But yet he is forcing himself and sometimes he is saying, his ego saying, "I don't like this". But worshipper forcing himself not to say "I don't like that".

Once Vizier was sitting in front of Amir ul-Muminin in front of sultan. Not Vizier, was sat in front of Vizier and Sultan and was present Imam Abu Yusuf. It was said that Prophet, Rasulullah sal Allahu alaihi wa sallam was too much liking pumpkin and that Vizier suddenly he said that "but I never like it", and quickly Imam Abu Yusuf saying, "Take your word and say, 'astaghfirullah', or I am giving fatwa that you should be killed. Saying, "Yes must be killed that person" because just he said opposite what Prophet was liking, he said "I don't like".

Such a things, because our ego yet not prepared to accept what Allah Almighty likes or don't like. Perhaps we are worshippers but yet our egos saying something, "I like this. Allah don't like". Saying "I don't like this, Allah Almighty likes it". But under pressure not saying. That is worshipper and his name written good servant but yet inside he is not in perfection. Second ones they are abd. Abd means real servant, he is going to be after what Allah Almighty likes and Rasul, Prophet likes, to make himself to like it or not to like. They are on perfection. They are 'abd', real ones. No more ego there. May be worshipper, but ego yet commanding. If finding a chance they escape but the second ones their egos just surrendered, and their egos saying to them, "as You like, no, O my Lord, as You like, O my Rasul as you like." "You like this?" "If You like". "You don't like it?" "If You don't like it". That is highest position.

Then the horizon for servanthood, and their paradise not same paradise that worshippers getting in. They can't see. What is in it, they are not knowing. And everyone happy with their paradise. Man should be anything through stars but we are not getting interested.

You are happy or not? No one asking to go to moon. Anyone asking to go to moon, to live there? No, we are happy here. Therefore everyone that getting into their paradise should be happy but those people whom they are with Prophet sal Allahu alaihi wa sallam, they should be given.

If one ray of lights coming from there, whole paradise disappears. So powerful. Always from divinely lights running on them. That coming on paradise from Arsh. Lights of Arsh coming on paradise. But that paradises that is special only for private, special people. They are whom? They are those people whom with their Lord. Always they are saying, "as You like". Difficult conditions never making them to say to their Lord, "O my Lord, I don't like". They are happy with every condition that their Lord may be put them in it. And most difficult conditions just put on the Seal of Prophets, haithu yaqul (where he says): “ma udhiya nabi'un mithla ma udhitu” (Hadith). He was saying that no any other prophet suffered as I have been suffered. But he always saying “O my Lord as You like: kama tuhibb wa tarda”.

The last word from Rasul sal Allahu alaihi wa sallam to say “O my Lord as You like, as You please. What You like, I like it, what makes You pleased, makes me pleased also”. The horizon of servanthood. And then Allah Almighty opening for those people through His Divinely Presence. It is impossible for others to be there. Therefore paradise people they're abid, worshippers, but those people more than that. They are servants. Illnesses as Ayub alaihi salam, fire for Ibrahim alaihi salam, flood for Nuh alaihi salam, and for Moses, Pharaoh and Jesus Christ, Banu Isra'il, the Children of Israil and for the Seal of Prophets, beloved and praised servant Sayyidina Rasulullah, his nation.
