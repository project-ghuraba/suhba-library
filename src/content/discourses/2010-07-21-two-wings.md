Dastūr yā Sayyidī Madad Allāh Allāh Allāh Allāh Allāh Allāh 'Azīz Allāh Allāh Allāh Allāh Nūran wa sururan wa ridhwanu Sultana (and light and gladness and gratification and majesty) fi mithli hadha l-shahri l-sharif (in such a holy month) Sha'bani Mu'azzam (the great Sha'ban) thumma s-salāmu 'alāyk (and then salam to you) Yā Sāhib l-Waqt (O Owner of Time) Yā Qutbul Mutasarrif Fi dunyā (in Dunya) from holy ones we are needy [for] your support for addressing to people through East and West.

And we are saying a‘ūdhu bi-llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm Bismillāhi r-Rahmāni r-Rahīm. O our Lord We are weak ones. Give us from heavenly support to take away everything that belongs to shayṭān. Allāh Allāh As-Salām Alaykum our attenders from ins (men) and jinn from mankind and from jinnis.

Salāmu llāhi 'alaykum wa 'alaynā wa 'alā 'aman it-taba'a l-hudā (and on whoever follows guidance). O People and in pleasure and to play to make their egos happy. No. Madad yā Sultān al-awliyā’. We must know what is right and what is wrong. Originally man never knowing what is right and what is wrong and for that reason Allāh Almighty sending His prophets to teach people what is true and what is wrong. Nothing else. Nothing else. If you are not trying to know what is true one and what is wrong one, we never [can] be able to fulfill our oath in divinely presence [on] the day of oaths. And the Lord of Heavens [is] always sending His prophets; that they are special beings. They are not ordinary ones. Ordinary ones, not knowing. But special ones they have been authorized to show what is good and what is bad. What is true [and] what is wrong. What is honour for mankind and what is taking honour from mankind.

Madad yā Sultān al-awliyā. That is important point. O our attenders! Try to hear and I am trying also myself to understand what is true and what is wrong. Yes. The Lord of Heavens He ordered and calling His servants to learn. and the Seal of Prophets was saying that "ṭalabu l-'ilmi" to seek knowledge "farīdatun 'alā kulli" (is obligatory) mu'minīn wa mu'mināt" (Al-Tirmidhi, 74) (on every believer) or 'muslimīn wa muslimā' (muslim men and women). Yes. O our salafi ulamas marhaban (welcome). You know that hadīth ash-sharīf or you are sleeping? Say! about that holy hadīth.

Everything that the Seal of Prophets is speaking it is holy. It is not ordinary words. "in huwa illā wahyun yuhā!" (53:4) (It is not but a revelation revealed) You are making holy speeches of the Seal of Prophets as [if it were] your speeching or speaking. No! He is specialized one in divinely presence, and [he is] just granted such a position that no one can reach that position. Only that specialized or chosen one. Sayyidinā Rasūlullāh (saws) What saying? "ṭalabu l-'ilmi" (to seek knowledge) farīdatun 'alā kulli mu'minīn muslimīn wa muslimā (is obligatory for all Muslim men and women) Not saying "mu'min wa mu'mina" [There] Must be a difference, using the Seal of Prophets "muslim wa muslima" Because Islam just coming on [the] base [of] īmān. If [there is] no faith; īmān, [there is] no Islam. You must believe that you can stand up on the base of Islām, īmān. And, therefore saying " 'alā kulli muslim wa muslima" For what? "talabu l-'ilm" (to seek knowledge) Make it clear to your people. "talabu l-'ilm" to ask such a knowledge that it is Heavenly Knowledge! Never going to be what you are knowing. Islām.

Yes. "Muslim wa Muslima" Allāh gives us through His most Holy servant for an understanding between īmān and Islām. " talabu l-'ilm' " If a person is Muslim or Muslima What they are asking to learn? You must 'mulāhaza' you must try to understand why the Seal of Prophets saying "Muslim wa Muslima" Not saying 'mu'min wa mu'mina' Open it! If you are ulamas. "qūlu aslamna" (49:14) (say: we have become Muslims). Allāh Almighty saying. lil-A'rāb (for Arabs) and what they were, when they were claiming we are, "amanna" (49:14) (we have believed) but saying you may say "aslamna" (49:14) (we have surrendered/become muslim) That means the level of īmān [is] most high than the level of Islām. But, as a first step you must come to Islām to say, I am "aslamtu" (3:20) (I have surrendered) "Aslamtu" (3:20) Just I am surrendered to my Lords' orders. But, when you are reaching that, that level you must ask [for] more knowledge that [is] taking you from the level of Islām to [the] level of īmān.

īmān [is the] most important thing. Therefore, we must ask 'How we can be able to reach the level of īmān?' Who knows this? [The] prophets [do]. [The] Prophets coming [and] calling people to Islām When they are saying "aslamna" (49:14) Then, teaching them how they should be mu'mins, believers because, their Islām [is] only through their tongues. Saying 'Lā ilāha illa 'Allāh' 'Lā ilāha illa 'Allāh' 'Sayyidunā Muhammadu r-Rasūlullāh (saws)' [It is] ok but, that is Islām. What is īmān? [If] you are saying Kalīmatu sh-shahādatayn you are going to be Muslim. But, you need to be mu'min. Mu'min that [is] he [who] surrendered and he ask, he is asking to work on it for reaching the level of īmān. Anything wrong?

O our salafi ulamas? You are saying this hadīth ash-sharīf? Opening your knowledge too much. You must open it more and more and more. To say kalīmatu sh-shahāda is [the] first step [of] what Allāh Almighty calling his people; to say this kalīmatu sh-shahāda. And then, what saying the Seal of Prophets? What [is he] saying? [He is] saying that "al īmānu bid'un wa sab'unā shu'batan" (Belief is seventy and some branches) Allahā kalīmatu sh-shahāda adhnāha imātatu l-adhā an l-tariq (Its lowest branch is the removal of harm from the road while its highest is to say: There is no god but Allah).

We are beginning to come into īmān; bidāyatu 'an-taqūl (its beginning is to say). You are coming in Islām by saying 'Lā ilāhā illā Allāh Muhammadu r-Rasūlullāh (saws)' wa sharrafa wa karram!' (and honoured and dignified) Allāh Allāh Yā Sayyidi l-awwalīn wa l-akhirīn. 'Rūhi fadāk' (may my soul be sacrificed for you) Yā Sayyidi l-awwalīn wa l-akhirīn. That hadīth ash-sharīf O salafi ulamas [is] carrying people from 'adhnā' to from [the] lowest step of Islām to a'alā [the] highest. Therefore, "talab il-'ilmi" To ask to learn it is an obligatory from Allāh Almighty to every man and woman whom they are saying 'We are Muslims'. Yes

You are teaching this? Or speaking so many things that can be like 'mā'ala yāni' (nonsense). It is not. Why you are speaking from academic knowledge to a standard level of schools. What is that? Yes, it is [a] higher level. [The] lowest level they have been offered to reach a'alā (the highest) [the] top point of being. Muslim and mu'min. One wing [is] Islām, one wing [is] īmān. You must use it to go up [to] high levels of heavens. Islām [is] 'yukhāṭib' addressing to our physical being. But, īmān addressing to our spiritual being. You can't reach with your with your material being or physical being. You can't be able to, to get up. [It is] heavy. Material things is heavy things. You can't use it for going up!

But when you are going to be [a] believer, mu'min, we are taking heavenly power through our spiritual being and we may go up and up and up and up! Endlessly horizons or endlessly levels of īmān. Therefore Allāh Almighty saying, Subhāna wa ta'āla, "walikullin darajātun mimmā ʿamilū" (6:132) (And for all [will be] degrees for what they did). Darrajāt. Allāh Almighty saying 'For everyone, in front of him, endless horizons!' That endless aims, morād, targets? more than targets! Try to reach one after one. [There is ] no any hindrance if you are asking. Therefore saying 'for everyone there is a level that he may reach that level and another one may reach another level'.

"Wa likullin" (6:132) for everyone darrajāt for everyone that can't be your level same to another one! Abadan Never! You have a special level for yourself. But his level [is] never going to be [the] same level! Allāh hu Akbar! "walikullin darrajāt" [6:132] That means the Lord of Heavens [is] granting to everyone as He likes. Endlessly oceans! Endlessly levels that 'ma fi dhihām' no crowding there. Everyone, Ya Allāh, going up, going up. No one coming and preventing and when it is going to be? O salafi ulamas when you have been granted darrajāt? You know or not? As long as people [are] following their rights, desires, no any level for them. Their level always going to be levels of animals. When leaving that [an] opening [is] coming to him and his opening it is only for himself. [It is] not for anyone else! You don't know what is his degree, level, and he not knowing what his level and degree.

Therefore, for the paradise people it is only for himself, for herself. No one knows what you have through your paradise. And you never knowing what is granted to anyone else. No! Allāhu Akbar! Allāhu Akbar! Therefore, O our salafi ulamas you must be teachers, teaching people about īmān [and] about Islām. To make their feet fixed here, and hereafter. If you are not making people to be their feet on a right station, they can't be able to get up. Then you should be like an airport; some airplanes going up, some of them waiting. For what waiting? Because [there is] no 'benzin yok' - No petrol! No petrol, [just] sitting there.

Therefore, we have been ordered to ask more heavenly powers to move from this airport up to our real targets, levels. Or you should be here. Never getting up. Whole orders and whole commands, heavenly commands and orders [are] putting a petrol power to work - our spirituality to go up. If people [are] not using [it] they are going to be always on airport. Going like this, coming like that. But, others whom they are taking from every order and command from the Seal of Prophets they are getting up and going [in] special directions that it is heavenly granted for them from the Lord of Heavens. And no one knowing where that going. No one knowing what [is] in that in that level, what is in it. You can't look there. If you are looking, you can't see. You can't see. You can see only what it is belonging to you. Not, no one can look. Even if looking inside; it is just [a] veil. Your paradise [is] veil[ed], he can't look in it. And for his paradise, you can't look [at] his paradise, because it is veiled also. [It] belongs only to him.
