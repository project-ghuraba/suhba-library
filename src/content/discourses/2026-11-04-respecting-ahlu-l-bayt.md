As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Alḥamdulillāh, we are thanking Allah for this meeting with Ahl ul-Bayt. They are barakah for every place they are in. And, alḥamdulillāh, even though it is not a Muslim country, they are letting Muslims do their Islāmic practices very easily here. Especially this: Naqabat al-Ashraf, meaning those who register Ahl ul-Bayt. Even in many Muslim countries, they don’t have this, or many of them cancelled this. You cannot find such an association. It is very important. 

In the last state of Islām, the Ottomans, it was very important for them: this Naqabat al-Ashraf. The head of it is called Naqīb al-Ashraf, the head of Ashraf, who are Ahl ul-Bayt. They had a special situation in this Islāmic State for the sake of Prophet, for the love of Prophet, to praise Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. There was respect; they were very respectful of Ahl ul-Bayt. Because of this, Allah was helping them all the time. Allah is happy with them, and Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam is happy with them, because they are respecting them for the sake of Prophet. 

Allah says about Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam,
وَمَآ أَرْسَلْنَٰكَ إِلَّا رَحْمَةًۭ لِّلْعَٰلَمِينَ
“Wa mā arsalnaka illā raḥmatan lil-ʿālamīn”
“And We have not sent you, [O Muḥammad], except as a mercy to all the worlds.” (Qur’ān 21:107). 

Allah sent Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam to save people, as a mercy for people. So whoever has good knowledge and good understanding respects anyone who belongs to Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. 

For this, the Ottomans were the last state of Islām. After them, there is no Islāmic state. Now none of them are. We have cut it off and said there is no state of Islām. It was only at that time, more than a hundred years ago. After this, nobody is showing respect. They are not even respecting Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. Because of this, in the time of the Ottomans, they had a special status. They even had their own court. They had their own jail; they didn’t interfere with Ahl ul-Bayt because they would do among themselves what was necessary if any wrong happened. So their judge was from among them. And they judged and did what they had to do. Not with ordinary people; they were separate from ordinary people. This was out of respect for Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam.

The Ottomans were the supporters and defenders of Islām, for Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, rāyatu l-Islām (flag of Islām). What is the true Islām? To follow Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, respect Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, respect his four Khalifahs, his Ṣaḥābah and Ahl ul-Bayt. 

Of course, the ṣaḥābah and the Ahl ul-Bayt... The ṣaḥābah also have many different levels: first is ʿAshara al-Mubasharah (the Ten Promised Paradise), and the As'hāb ul-Badr, and As'hab ul-Uhud. All of these, their rank is higher than others. And the highest after them is Ahl ul-Bayt. They are the highest rank. Because of this, they were looking carefully at who was coming from Ahl ul-Bayt when a new baby was born. They were registering them so they would not be mixed with others. Because many people are claiming to be Ahl ul-Bayt. They are cursed if they don’t have proof. Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam says, “Whoever says, “I am from this line and from that line,” and they are not truthful, they are cursed ones.” For this, they were carefully registered and known. But now, in Allah’s wisdom, it is like this.

When Mahdi ʿalayhi s-salām comes, many things will appear: what is right and what is wrong. Many things people thought were right are wrong. Many things are wrong that people say are right. This is for religion or even for common things as well. People don’t know many things about what happened before in history, which at that time will appear from Sayyidina Ādam ʿalayhi s-salām until now: how many nations came, how many prophets came, how many different people were here and there, everywhere; which prophet, which Awliyā’ullāh. In the time of Mahdi ʿalayhi s-salām, everything will be clear.

For this, Mawlānā Shaykh is saying, when Mahdi ʿalayhi s-salām comes, he is Mujtahid Muṭlaq. Only his ijtihād will appear. So we will follow him, all of us. At that time, you can no longer say, “I am Hanafi, I am Shafiʿi...” What Mahdi ʿalayhi s-salām says, you will follow him. Ṭarīqahs will also follow him, all of them. All will unite under one man, Mahdi ʿalayhi s-salām, as in the time of Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. 

In the time of Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, everyone followed Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. It will be like this: everything clear, nothing else. Not any other kind of thing in religion: it will be only Islām, only Mahdi ʿalayhi s-salām. For this, inshā’Allāh, at that time the real and the false ones will appear. May Allah keep us with the real ones, inshā’Allāh. 

May Allah bless you. May Allah bless Ahl ul-Bayt here and everywhere, inshā’Allāh, and keep them safe. Many Ahl ul-Bayt from the beginning are Ahlu l-Ibtilā (people of trials): they have suffered, to raise their station higher. Even Sayyidina Ḥasan and Ḥusayn became shahīd. Since that time, they have been suffering. So may Allah keep us safe, and keep them safe inshā’Allāh, to reach Sayyidina Mahdi ʿalayhi s-salām.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
