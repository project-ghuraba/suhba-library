Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Our Holy Prophet ﷺ states in the Hadith Sharif:

“Blessings and bounties aren’t continuous, they don’t go on forever so get used to hardship too”, says the Holy Prophet ﷺ. It’s not going to be easy all the time. Therefore, during easy and good times, prepare and get yourself used to hard times. Get used to it so that it doesn’t affect you and then you’ll be at ease. But when those who get used to ease and comfort all the time face the smallest hardship, they get very sad and their morale goes down, their health decreases and they get upset about everything. We are not created for this world; we are created for the hereafter. This world is sometimes good and sometimes bad, it’s sometimes beautiful and sometimes ugly, it differs all the time. There’s no such thing as: ‘it’s going to be easy all the time.’ The state of affairs changes in this world. In order for it not to change for us, make yourselves used to hardship too, just as our Holy Prophet ﷺ said. When this is the case, a person wouldn’t get saddened or upset, he wouldn’t complain and wouldn’t mind losing things.

Our Holy Prophet ﷺ has informed us everything, such as how we’re supposed to live and how we should spend our days in this temporary world. The Prophet ﷺ is a guide for us. In order for us to reach salvation and safety, he ﷺ has given us hadiths, advices and instructions that will be enough for us until the day of judgment. Those who follow him will not be sad in this world and will be in felicity in the next world. They’ll be in felicity and happiness both in this world and in the hereafter, but the hereafter is what’s important.

There are some people who say that they’re living in happiness in this world, they’re lying, they have no peace or tranquility. But those who believe in Allah ﷻ and have faith (iman) will have no worries in this world and will reach felicity in the hereafter, even if he was the poorest person. This is what the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ and all the Prophets taught. They taught people goodness and forbid them from evil. But son of man isn’t listening to them, he’s going after shaytan instead. People are doing things that aren’t useful for them but will rather harm and sadden them. They run after such things and then say: “We are ruling this world”, they’re not ruling it, everything is in the hands of Allah ﷻ. May Allah ﷻ give more strength to our faith and may He help muslims. Allah ﷻ can make the hard easy, there’s nothing hard in the sight of Allah ﷻ. It’s the prayer of the Prophet ﷺ: “Make what is hard easy for us”. May Allah ﷻ help us all. May Allah ﷻ send the Sahib (owner) insha’Allah.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq. Al-Fatiha.
