A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

The prayers says, "Allāhumma alhimnā rushdanā." Inspire us to do what we should do. Help us to do good and to do what is right, O AllāhNone. This is what this du‘ā’ asks. It is an important prayer, because most people think that they are doing good when in fact it is not good but bad. Therefore, every minute, every second, we should ask for help from AllāhNone so that HeNone shows us what is right and permits us to do right. Because everything is in Allāh'sNone hands. HeNone makes people do what HeNone wants. If a man does something from his ego without thinking, most of the time he makes a mistake and then he is in loss. Therefore, this is important. Our hearts should always be with AllāhNone so that everything we do is with HisNone help, HisNone mercy and barakah. May HeNone not let us do wrong things.

Because most people, when they are to do something, they don't think to ask for Allāh'sNone help or to say, "I am a weak servant." Therefore, most of the time they don't succeed. Because what you know is little. You know if Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla lets you know. We are nothing but weak servants. May AllāhNone help us, in shā’a Llāh. May HeNone show the right way and let us do the right things.

There is a praying, "Allāhumma alhimnā rushdanā." Meaning, "O our Lord, show us the right thing." For everything we in need for this. Because people, without help from AllāhNone, most of time they are doing wrong thing. They thought they are doing, they try their best to do good thing, but at the end it is nothing happen, or happened, opposite happen, bad thing. Why? Because they are not thinking for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla HeNone is doing everything and without HisNone help nothing can be.

But what you ask for HisNone help, HeNone is Merciful, HeNone guide us, because we are weak people, weak servants. We are not knowing, only saying, "We are doing this, even we are not doing, YouNone give us inspiration, give us to understand what to do, what not to do." Because this is whole human being they are trying, trying and they thought they are doing good thing, but the end nothing happened. In shā’a Llāh, AllāhNone helps us all time to do benefit thing for dunyā, for Ākhirah, in shā’a Llāh. And to not make... Every time we must think about our Lord Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. You think what you doing, "O our Lord we trying our hard, trying, trying YouNone help us to succeed, in everything, in shā’a Llāh." AllāhNone help us.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
