As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Shukr to AllāhNone, we have good tidings. Islām is good tidings for Muslims. We reached these beautiful days, of the birth of our Holy ProphetNone. We attained their virtue, shukr to AllāhNone. Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam and Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla are giving good tidings to people. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Wa-abshirū bi-l-jannati llatī kuntum tū‘adūn. Naḥnu awliyā’ukum fī l-ḥayāti d-dunyā wa-fī l-ākhirah" (41:30-31). May you have the good tidings, that AllāhNone promised you Paradise. You will be in Paradise. This is the best news. No need to fear, for believers and people who are Muslim. Those who are together with AllāhNone don't need to be afraid, with permission of AllāhNone. The thought of going to hell makes them feel like crying. "Will we go to hell or Paradise?" Don't be afraid. AllāhNone gave you good tidings. The Word of AllāhNone is true. You have been promised Paradise. You are going to Paradise. These are great tidings for people in Islām, because when people make mistakes in this life and don't correct them, it will be difficult. But whether they correct them or not, their life continues. However, the life of ākhirah is not like this. If they slip, may AllāhNone protect us, they cannot ever be saved.

One of the great awliyā’, Ḥaḍrat al-Būṣīrī, says in Qaṣīdah al-Burdah: "Bushrá lanā ma‘shara l-Islām inna lanā mina l-‘ināyati ruknan ghayra munhadimi." He wrote these beautiful words. And people always sing them. They used to sing this. Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh ad-Dāghistānī always used to repeat this verse. We heard it from his holy mouth many times. This verse says, "May good tidings be upon Muslims. We have a base like a firm castle. What we are based on will never collapse. Don't be afraid. May you have good tidings." This way is a beautiful way. Therefore, being steadfast on this way is beneficial, first to people themselves. And it is beneficial to their families, relatives and other people. To benefit from Islām is something everyone can do. But there are enemies, like the ego, desire and shayṭān, which prevent it. There are four enemies. What do these enemies do? They don't wish you goodness. They wish you badness. They want to destroy you. Don't listen to them. They are... "Wa-khālifu n-nafsa" – oppose your ego and oppose the rest. Oppose shayṭān so that you have good tidings, and become a beloved servant of AllāhNone, so that you live in their Paradise forever, in shā’a Llāh. May AllāhNone let us attain these good tidings for the honor of holy Mawlid, in shā’a Llāh. To be in, Muslim, and Islām, it is very nice, very blessed thing for human being. Because Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla and Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, and many time they are giving good tiding for believer, for Muslim. And especially this āyah we read: Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Wa-abshirū bi-l-jannati llatī kuntum tū‘adūn" (41:30). Be, good tide, happy with this. What? With Paradise. Which Paradise? Which Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla promised you to be there. AllāhNone promised us to be in Paradise, in Jannah. And we are not listening for this. Many people, they are not doing what AllāhNone saying. AllāhNone... or some people, they are hopeless. We see sometimes people, they crying, "Oh, I not doing well. Am I be in Jannah, in Paradise?" Yes, you will be. It's Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone promised you. When you are in HisNone way, you will be in Jannah. Don't afraid. Don't make shayṭān to make you not believe for this. It is, it must... You must believe also, this. Because everything AllāhNone saying, we must believe. No, you don't have any choice, any chance to say "no, maybe" or "not". No. Must, must believe. AllāhNone says for us: "Be happy, you will be in Jannah." AllāhNone promise us.

So for this, we are lucky. Everything Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, heNone said for ummah and heNone said in ḥadīth, "Ādam and below, he is under myNone flag." So we are happy, we are lucky with this, to be in hisNone nation. Because whole prophets, they are asking bless from himNone, asking help from Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are reach this happy day, and we celebrate for Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Many of you, they are coming special to greet Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam in this occasion. and of course, heNone will be happy. HeNone will be, give you reward. And you will get this good tiding: to be in Paradise with believer. To be in with Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam Paradise, also. This is good tiding.

And all awliyā’u Llāh, they are happy to be from nation of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Especially, there was Imām al-Būṣīrī who write Qaṣīdatu l-Burdah. One poem, one chapter in this: "Bushrá lanā ma‘shara l-Islām inna lanā mina l-‘ināyati ruknan ghayra munhadimi." This poem when I was young with, I was hearing this everyday, I think maybe. Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh ad-Dāghistānī, he was saying this. Sometimes we go to mosque, we hearing from his window, it was up. And he was saying this. He was very happy with this chapter. What meaning? Good tiding for Muslim. They have strong thing to hold them. It will be not, cannot broke. This, for Muslim, nothing can broke this strong thing holding you. This is for whole Muslim. You must be hold in some strong thing, not weak thing. You will be crush, and you go and within to fall down. No. You must be happy, and ask to be hold this, in shā’a Llāh.

Whole awliyā’u Llāh, they are giving good tiding. Don't be listening for people who making everything, saying, "You will go to jahannam, you will go… You are not doing this. You go, you do this wrong. You, something, you are mushrik. You are… You will be in hell." Don't listen to him. We listening for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla and Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Don't listen to this, who ignorant, not understanding what AllāhNone saying. AllāhNone saying, "INone promise you good tiding. You will be in Jannah." Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, this enough for us. No need to listen for other one, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone make us, in shā’a Llāh, whole in Jannah.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
