Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

There is a saying:

“Imitating good people leads to salvation.” Trying to be like them in every way is good. The most important thing is to be like them in their character and in what they do. It is good to imitate them. Dressing like them is also good.

Of course, some people can’t do everything. They dress like them, then they stay as they were before. That is not good, not good at all. Rather than dressing like them, it is much more acceptable if he imitates the good ones. Because he is deceiving others; he is both making the people he is trying to imitate be worthless and he is deceiving people. People may be trusting him and think that this person with that title is good. But since the inside has not changed, he thinks that he deceived people, got what he wanted and won. Whereas, he has lost.

Therefore, may Allah ﷻ protect us. May we imitate the good character of the great and the good ones. The important thing is to imitate the inside, the essence, not the shell. The outside is not important at all. The important thing is that may Allah ﷻ grant this to all of us, so that we can be in good character. May Allah ﷻ help us because these are not easy to do, but everything becomes easy when Allah ﷻ wants.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
