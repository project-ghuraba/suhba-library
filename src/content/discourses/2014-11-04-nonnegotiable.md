Sheikh Bahauddin Adil Al-Haqqani sohbat of the 4th of November, 2014.

Ya Salam! Alhamdulillah that Allah Almighty, He returned us safely to Mawlana Sheikh. It was a long journey, it was a holy journey and it was a very good journey Alhamdulillah.

This is it shows that Mawlana Sheikh he chose the right person. It shows because they say, to know the person even you travel with him, or you give him money, or you make business with him. So you understand the person that you are dealing is right or wrong. This is very comment. You don't have to discover South Africa again. Allah (jj) He doesn't do things coincidentally. He does thing properly. He doesn't say "O maybe, it can be ok, or maybe not." The great Awliya' also they never do things coincidentally. Everything been proper. Maybe you see so much mess around, but everything is very proper. These are things we have to know. If we don't know, we have to learn. If we don't learn we have to think. This is important for us to understand.

What I am talking here, I am talking personally. What does it mean personally? It concerns the person. If you do it, you win. If you don't do it you lose. This is nothing to do with other factors. It is concerning you personally. This is for everybody including me. If I am believer, I will be winning. If I am not believer I will be a loser. And it doesn't make difference to other believers that you don't believe. Or it doesn't concern others if you believe and others are not believing. Now, it is your boat that you have to save. Don't, don't look for others. Don't look for the other's advertisement. No need to look for other flashy things. Don't think about it. What is Mawlana Sheikh telling you, follow. You are saving your boat. It is important to understand this point. It is not so difficult to understand this point. Even a small child, he looks at me and tries to understand. Yes! It is true what I am saying, it is very true.

Alhamdulillah it is sign of Mawlana Sheikh, that it gives everybody the true safe way. Mawlana Sheikh he is not playing all these years. He was not joking all these years, he was truly giving knowledge. He was truly giving the heavenly knowledge. The heavy knowledge not the small knowledge. Everybody can read Quran, everybody can learn Hadith, everybody can learn Fiqh. Everybody can learn - it is open. But the real one it is not open, it has been given for some people and that's been given for other people. But Mawlana Sheikh he was so generous that he distributed for all. He didn't keep it for himself. So many Awliya' they keep this knowledge for themselves. Mawlana Sheikh he was opposite even to Awliya' he was sharing with the world. Sharing with the world. He was not greedy. He was the most generous, because he was acting with the generosity of Rasulullah (saws) when he says "wa ma 'arsalnaka 'illa rahmatan lil 'alamina" (21:107) That I sent you for whole nations. And you can see how Mawlana Sheikh he talked for whole nations.

Nobody can escape from Mawlana Sheikh because he is in the rank of "wa ma 'arsalnaka 'illa rahmatan lil 'alamina"(21:107). This is Mawlana Shaykh. And this is how Mawlana Sheikh he put his successor and he become Sheikh now. Now our.. Not orphan, you have to know that we are not orphan. This travelling it was giving for us a lot of experience, to know how the secret of Mawlana Sheikh is going through Sheikh Muhammad. It must be known. It must be known, and why? I know it. It is ok for me, I am happy. But I must say it for everybody to be known. They say there is other things, there is other...No! It's only one! We are looking for one. We cannot look for 2, we cannot look for 3, we cannot look for 4. We cannot look for 5. We have to be under one. Then the one if he is still opening the secret for you, you can speak. He is Alhamdulillah he is still opening the secret for me, so I can speak. Otherwise I cannot speak.

We cannot ask, he sends. We cannot say we are spiritually connected to Mawlana. He is the one who will connect to you. We don't have such a level, we don't have, none! None. I can say it very easily, nobody. But Mawlana Shaykh if he wants, he can reach. Imagine the king is sitting in his throne and the king orders "I want, any name. And he says I want this person to be in my presence in 24 hrs. Or maybe he is far away - in 1 week time. What you think, that person will come to the presence of the king or he cannot come? But if that person if he wants to be in the king's place he has to go through so many formalities, that it is not allowing him to be in the king's place. This is how Mawlana Sheikh he is.

This is only a small example to understand where we are stepping and who we are. Who we are? Because if we are claiming, we have to prove. If we are with Mawlana Sheikh we have to prove. We are saying we are weak people. We are so weak people to be with Mawlana Sheikh. Mawlana Sheikh he is the one collecting us. This has to be known. Nobody can say to Mawlana Sheikh "Ya Mawlana if I am not around, you will not be Mawlana Sheikh." Shut up you! Nobody can say this for Mawlana Sheikh. No tongue can dare to say such a thing, it's a big responsibility. What you are thinking? How you are thinking and how you're acting? You have to look for yourself first and then to understand who is Mawlana Sheikh and what is Mawlana Sheikh. If there is no Mawlana Sheikh, there would never be us. Maybe we don't know where we'd been. Everybody where he will be. Or we've been having the safe side of our ending, do you think? Do you think so? Even now we are thinking.

But Alhamdulillah we are happy that Mawlana Sheikh kept us with him. And Mawlana Sheikh he put somebody on our head to say "this is the one that you have to follow. This is the one that he is with you. And this is the one that I left for you." Then be happy for what you got. Don't be confused, don't be stupid. Don't be stupid! Stupid, to see the reality and deny the reality. Don't be like Abu Jahl. Abu Jahl he was seeing Rasulullah, for years, for years and still denying Rasulullah. He cannot see the sun of Rasulullah, he cannot see the power of Rasulullah. He cannot and say, "the orphan son. I am more powerful than him, I am more like this, I am Quraish, I am this, I am that." He was jealous of Rasulullah. Don't be jealous from your Sheikh. Don't be jealous from your Sheikh. Be under your Sheikh, try to. This is the big test for our ego to not be jealous from our Sheikh.

Mawlana Sheikh he put me under Sheikh Muhammad, like this, under him. And Mawlana Sheikh he tested me with this point. I am under the test, Alhamdulillah I say "whatever Mawlana Sheikh wants it is done for me." I will never say and if I say, I prefer to die - to say "I am better than Sheikh Mehmet, I am better than Mawlana Sheikh." I will die, I prefer to die to not say such a small word, I even not put it in my ear, or to pass through my mind. It is important, very sensitive place, very sensitive. You have to understand the tuning there is very sensitive. There is, they say, electronic tuning, the hand cannot touch it. If the hand touch, it will destroy it. So it is more than that sensitive. You are dealing with high level, it is not easy. When something breaks in high level it will be earthquake in all the world. It will be earthquake in all the world. It is not easy to get something like this done. This is how Mawlana Sheikh in tuning he was making very fine tuning. We know the story we know everything Alhamdulillah & we are acting according to that story. If Mawlana Sheikh he put me here he knows he put me here. If you put Mawlana S. Muhammad into his maqam, you know why he put him in his maqam? He doesn't have to ask you "are you happy with him or not? There is nothing, no such thing. There is no democracy in Tariqa. There is no "I like the Sheikh, or I don't like the Sheikh. He can satisfy me or he cannot satisfy me." This you don't have anything like this.

I remember in 1994 we went to Hajj with my mother, my wife, Sheikh Mehmet, his wife, and we've been there and I was saying, "what is this about manasik Hajj?" He was telling me one thing, very clearly. He say "Nobody can interfere with manasik Hajj. Why it is like this and what it is like that. You have to do it to be Hajji, to get that maqam/station." He said, "Manasik Hajj it is nonnegotiable. And there is nobody www.saltanat.org Page 2 he can put his ideas or to say, why it is like this, and why it is like that." You have to do it, if you don't do it, it is not. Tariqa also same, what Mawlana Sheikh he was saying to be done, we have to follow. No, negotiable. It's nonnegotiable and nobody asking you if you are happy or you are not happy. You have to be happy because the Sheikh he is the one putting this for us.

You have to be relaxed. And I saying this for senior murid and junior murid, for all including me. I don't know where I am, but I am accepting to be with Mawlana Sheikh in his order. And whatever is his order, it is order for us and we are trying to follow as much as we can. And we want to follow and we are making our intention to follow Mawlana Sheikh.

Wa min Allahi t-tawfiq. bi hurmati l-Habib, bi hurmati l-Fatiha.
