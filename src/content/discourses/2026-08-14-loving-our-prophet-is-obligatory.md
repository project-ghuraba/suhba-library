As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Shukr to Allah, this is the blessed month of Rabīʿ ul-Awwal, the month of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. Our beautiful Prophet was created with beauty. Everything about our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam is beautiful. Therefore, this month, Rabīʿ means the month of spring. Whenever it comes, it is the month of spring. It brings the spring, that beauty, to people. This is a great blessing from Allah to mankind. 

It is a great honor to be from the Ummah of Allah’s beloved servant, our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. Those who know this value will win. Those who do not know will not benefit from anything; they will have harm, not benefit. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla created our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam from His own light, and He created all the universes from his light. 

Therefore, respecting our Holy Prophet is obligatory. It is the very first obligation. There are some foolish people—those who are not Muslim become enemies of Allah and the Prophet—but those who claim to be Muslim but do not instill love and respect for our Holy Prophet in Muslims are another affliction for humanity and the Ummah. Because they deprive the Ummah of the beauty, goodness, reward, and all kinds of blessings that they could gain. They give them nothing but evil. They sow doubt (whisperings) and instill all kinds of wickedness. 

Therefore, the love for our Holy Prophet is obligatory. It is obligatory for all Muslims to honor him. Honoring him is not shirk. In fact, engaging with them is not reasonable in the first place. Engaging with them yields no benefit. Because talking to people who lack mind and logic brings no benefit. But those who want to learn, those whom Allah wishes to guide, will come to the right way. Through mind, reason, logic, reflection, observation, and research, one can certainly understand that honoring our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam is obligatory. 

The greatest evidence is in Qur’ān ʿAẓīmu sh-Sha’n,<br>
إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ وَمَلَٰٓئِكَتَهُۥ يُصَلُّونَ عَلَى ٱلنَّبِىِّ ۚ يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ صَلُّوا۟ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلِّمُوا۟ تَسْلِيمًا
<br>“InnAllāha wa malā'ikatahu yuṣallūna ʿala n-nabīyī, Yā ayyuhā l-ladhīna āmanū ṣallū ʿalayhi wa sallimū taslīmā”<br>
“Indeed, Allah and His angels send blessings on the Prophet: O you who have believed! Ask blessings on him, and salute him with all respect.” (Qurʼān 33:56). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

There is a command: “Allah and His angels send blessings upon our Holy Prophet, so send blessings upon him.” What could be greater than that? “I know the Qur’ān, I’ve memorized it, I’ve memorized the ḥadīths,” says someone, but then they don’t know the greatness and honor of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. Where is their mind, their logic, their humanity?! May Allah protect us. 

Most of them go down that wrong way, but some return. Anyone who thinks with their intellect will surely come to the right way. We give this advice so that people don’t get cheated by them. 

Shukr to Allah, in this month of Mawlid, sending more salawāt upon our Holy Prophet every day, following his Sunnah, and walking in his way is a great gain for us. May Allah protect us, let us not squander that gain. It’s not difficult. What Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla wants is to have love and respect for our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. That’s what He wants; He doesn’t tell you to “Carry stones on your back!” Is it really that difficult to honor and respect our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam?!

There is no creature, no human being, no being in the world that deserves more respect and honoring than our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. Shukr to Allah, we are from his ummah. May we be on his way until our last breath, forever, inshā’Allāh. May Allah’s barakah be upon us. May our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam’s himmah be with us, inshā’Allāh. Mubarak this beautiful month, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
