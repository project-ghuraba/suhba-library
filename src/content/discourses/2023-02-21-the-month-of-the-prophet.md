As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Shukr to AllāhNone, today is the second of the three holy months. Rajab is over. We have entered the holy month of Sha‘bān, shukr to AllāhNone. This month is the month of our Holy ProphetNone. It has great blessing. Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam used to fast most in this month, after Ramaḍān. Sha‘bānu l-Mu‘aẓẓam Mukarram (Sha'bān, the Exalted and Honoured) is a month which contains great bounty. AllāhNone grants different favours each and every day. The grants of this month are great. Their greatness is for the honour of our Holy ProphetNone. Worship performed in this month is preparation for Ramaḍān. The worship of Ramaḍān becomes complete.

And people will profit from the bounty of the three holy months. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla will grant people who do this the greatest of grants. If HeNone has written it for you, it is a great honour. There are holy nights in this month, especially the night of the fifteenth. On this night, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla assigns who will remain, and who will go, and what the portion of rizq will be for the coming year. Who is to stay will stay. Who is to go will go. Therefore, making du‘ā’ on this night brings the favours of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. HeNone will bestow HisNone favours. Who doesn't forget AllāhNone, AllāhNone won't forget him. HeNone shows mercy to whomever wants it. Who doesn't want it knows for himself. Because who asks, wants goodness. Who doesn't ask, doesn't want anything. Therefore, his condition is difficult. To do good, which is like this... Every day is precious in people's lives. They should do goodness every day. They should obey AllāhNone. They should make du‘ā’ that HeNone increases their love to our Holy ProphetNone.

The three months are for giving bounteous grants. The holy nights in these three months are even greater gifts from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla to humanity, to people. But people don't appreciate them. They appreciate useless things. They don't appreciate precious things. May AllāhNone let all of us know these good things. And may HeNone let us know things that are not good so that we do not approach them. May we do the good things. May we not waste our lives and days.

There are duties in this month, of course. The greatest duty we must do is to make more ṣalawāt to our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Tasbīḥāt, Ikhlāṣ, Āyatu l-Kursī, lā ilāha illā Llāh, reading Qur’ān and Dalā’il al-Khayrāt. Don't forget them. Many people cannot do it in other months. They should do at least during these months: Rajab, Sha‘bān, and Ramaḍān. It is the season of the holy months. It is a beautiful season. May AllāhNone bless it for all of us.

May HeNone protect us from disasters. As we are living in the end of times, it is unclear what will happen. As no one is secure, our security is to seek refuge in AllāhNone. There is no other security. As Nūḥ ‘alayhi s-salām told his son when he said, "I will hide on top of the mountain," even on top of the mountain your are not protected, if AllāhNone doesn't protect you. Therefore, we seek refuge in AllāhNone. May AllāhNone help us. May AllāhNone protect people and our īmān. Our īmān is more important than our life. May AllāhNone protect it, in shā’a Llāh. Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq.

We are al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we come to, AllāhNone make us to reach, second holy month. First, Rajab, al-ḥamdu liLlāh finished. Today, for us, first of Sha‘bān. Sha‘bānu l-Mu‘aẓẓam Mukarram, month of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Second one from holy blessed months, three months: Rajab, Sha‘bān, Ramaḍān. One month also passed from our life. Good month, in shā’a Llāh. This month, AllāhNone accept what we done, and AllāhNone bless people who are... Many people this month also, there was earthquake and they lost, many people lost, their life, or their, many things. It was big, but AllāhNone will reward who believe HimNone and was – he not lose anything. Who lose his life? Who not following AllāhNone. Other people, they are not losing anything. They are win[ning] everything, in shā’a Llāh.

This is month we said, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, blessed holy month. Each day to pray more, to keep your praying more, to fasting, also. Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, heNone was fasting in Sha‘bān. After Ramaḍān, the most month heNone was fasting – this month. HeNone not fasting all, because, if heNone fast, it will be for us also farḍ. For this, heNone was fasting, and breaking. But the most after Ramaḍān, this month heNone was [fasting]. HeNone sign for us to make this, from which AllāhNone give from HisNone reward, more than any time else.

So, this we are trying to follow himNone, in shā’a Llāh, and to prepare ourselves for Ramaḍān. Old people, old time people – This time people they are not knowing anything. Only few people they are taking care about these months. Even, who will claim they are Muslim, they make Muslim to be [in] doubt for this. "This is, you do wrong. You are fasting? You are mushrik, you are ahlu l-bid‘ah. You praying? You are ahlu l-bid‘ah, you are mushrik also." But, when you worship AllāhNone, you are, they said, doing good. They not interfere with other people. All they are make – shayṭān make them to make people not to make any worshipping for AllāhNone, to come near more for AllāhNone, near for Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam – They don't want this. Only they want to be accept them, and do what they are doing, which [is] taking īmān, belief from your heart, and throwing away.

But this month make people following Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam making sunnah, praying, fasting, preparing for Ramaḍān, to prepare with their Zakāt, and their business, to be ready for Ramaḍān. It will be, AllāhNone accept this, and reward them, and give them barakah and keep them safe. Because, in this month also, many good nights, blessed nights, especially the night of fifteen, Laylatu l-Barā’ah, which it is everything from year to year, AllāhNone write what will happen for people, for rizq, for drought, for rain, for everything in this night also writing. For this we must be ask for from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla to give us the best thing. To give us health, and wealth, and strong īmān, in shā’a Llāh – this is: " ‘Afwa wa-l-‘āfiyata wa-l-mu‘āfāta l-dā’imah", in shā’a Llāh.

This is very important month and there is many thing – waẓīfah. Especially, you must read – many people they cannot read other times – Qur’ān, every day one juz’. From Dalā’il al-Khayrāt also, and as much you can do from ṣalawāt for Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. You pray also Sūrah Ikhlāṣ, Āyatu l-Kursī, lā ilāha illā Llāh. As much you can do, you can do. Very few if you do, even it is – you win. Don't make your life going without tak[ing] from this jewel for your last, next life, you take. Take as much you can.

AllāhNone make it a blessed month and, in shā’a Llāh, be protected from every bad thing, from fitnah, from balā’. This world we are living in time, end of time. Everything which Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, heNone was saying in this time, many thing, balā’ coming: like many earthquake, many flood, many war, killing. We are living in this time, but we protect ourselves with du‘ā’ and ṣadaqah and, in shā’a Llāh, to tawakkal ‘alá Llāh (trust in AllāhNone), in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone protect you and make us to be safe, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
