Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

When our Holy Prophet and Hadrat Abu Bakr left Mecca for Medina and were stuck in that cave, the polytheists (mushriks) reached them. Hadrat Abu Bakr got very sad and very upset. The mushriks are about to capture them and are standing two feet in front of the cave. Our Holy Prophet says, “Do not fear, Allah is with us.” Allah is with His Prophet.

What is our Holy Prophet saying? Do not be sad, do not be flurried. Allah is with us. As long as Allah is with us, I do not want anything else. If all unbelievers (kafirs), the Kufr world, and the whole world stand against us they can have no effect. Let Muslims not get flurried and not fear. Allah is with Mu’mins. Allah (JJ) never said He was with kafirs. Allah is with Muslims.

Muslims are naive people. There is nothing tricky about them. However many lies, however many tricks, however many dirt there is, it is in the Kufr world. They would never leave anything unused. If it were under normal circumstances, not one Muslim should be left since the time of our Holy Prophet till today. But Allah is with us and Allah is helping.

You can be inside the fire, as long as Allah wants, like He did with Hadrat Ibrahim He can turn fire into a rose garden. So there is no need to get sad. Let us make dua. May Allah not leave us to the hands of tyrants. Let us stay under the rule of good people like these. This is a favor (lutuf) of Allah. Even though people do not deserve it, with the favor and kindness (karam) of Allah again, thank Allah good people are ruling over us.

That is why, do not go ahead and oppress anybody. What needs to be done is to make dua. We need to read the Koran and read mawlids for the martyrs (shahids), and pray for their stations (maqams) to get higher. May Allah give patience to their families Inshallah. This is what we are to do. Becasue what Allah says is happening. Allah gave everybody a lifetime.

There are no weapons, no bombs, no cannons, and no rifles inside Mecca. A little while ago, a crane fell and they said 15 people have passed away. Allah knows, but it is more than 15. Allah is capable of everything. It is hard but we wish everybody had true faith (iman). May Allah help Muslims. Do not fear: Allah is with us.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq.

Al-Fatiha. Hadrat Shaykh Muhammad Mehmet Adil 11 September 2015, Akbaba Dargah, Post-Hadra Sohba
