As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
وَقُلِ ٱعْمَلُوا۟ فَسَيَرَى ٱللَّهُ عَمَلَكُمْ
<br>“Wa quliʿmalū fa-sa-yarAllahu ʿamalakum”<br>
“And say, 'Do, for Allah will see your deeds.’” (Qur’ān 09:105). ṢadaqAllahu l-ʿAẓīm.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla will see everything you do. He will see; do whatever you do. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla, everyone, the prophets and all people, will see it. It has consequences in the hereafter and in this world.

We are always under the power of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. There is nothing He does not know. People are ignorant. How are they ignorant? A person who doesn’t even know their Creator is ignorant. A person who doesn’t know the majesty, power, and attributes of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla is also ignorant. They think, “I did something, and no one knew, no one is aware.” Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla is aware of everything. He is our Creator, He is the One who sees us, He is the One who directs all our actions.

Therefore, you must be careful. A person who is careful is a wise person. A wise person is the one who thinks about the outcome of their actions. A person who doesn’t do this, who doesn’t think about the hereafter, is foolish. A person must constantly strive and work hard so that his end will be good. Otherwise, if one does what their ego desires, then they will have lost everything. One must consider this.

The motto of our ṭarīqah is:<br>
‎الله حاضري، الله ناظري، الله شاھدي
<br>“Allah hādiri, Allah nādhiri, Allah shāhidi.”<br>
“Allah is present with me, Allah sees what I do, Allah is my Witness.”

This is what it means to be in the remembrance of Allah every minute; it means to remember Allah, to be with Allah. May Allah never remove this from our hearts, inshā’Allah.

Being with Him every moment is the most beautiful thing; remembering Allah. Everything you do will be good then. Of course, a person cannot do this every minute. He should keep Him in his mind as much as he can. We should think, “Allah sees us. I should not do evil. If we do good, Allah will be pleased with us,” and act accordingly. That is what a beautiful life is. That is what a good life is. That is what the most beneficial life is. Without it, it is incomplete. If we never remember Him it is a loss.

May Allah protect us. May Allah always keep Him in our minds, hearts, and thoughts, inshā’Allah.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
