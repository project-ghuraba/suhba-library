As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

‘Īd Mubārak. May it be good, in shā’a Llāh. It is a feast granted by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla to the ummah. When heNone came to Madīnatu l-Munawwarah, our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam said, "You had two feasts. One is Nowruz and another is Mihrajan." They used to entertain themselves and celebrate on these days. These days, these holidays, were not given by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, but were something they made up themselves. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla has given you something that is better, heNone said. Something given by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla has goodness in it indeed. The first is ‘Īdu l-Fiṭr. And the second is ‘Īdu l-Aḍḥá. These two days are given to you by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. They are blessed days. Both their days and nights are holy. There is no fasting on these days. These days were given in order for people to celebrate, so they can eat, drink, and be happy; as blessed and good days for this world, to eat and drink as they like, to visit places and people. In return, AllāhNone rewards to people who do this.

Shukr to AllāhNone, there are two of these blessed days, these ‘Īds. But there are many other days that other people call holidays. They have the New Year, the pumpkin feast or the aubergine feast, or I don't know what feast. They are not holidays. The real holidays that Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla granted to people, to the ummah of our Holy ProphetNone, to our Holy ProphetNone, are the two ‘Īds. To show respect to them is to show respect to Islām.

There are many wisdoms in it, in the presence of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. In order to show mercy to people, in order for the door of mercy to be open, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gave all kinds of opportunities. All Muslims are celebrating these feasts now and congratulating each other on it. Not all Muslims actually fast, to say nothing of praying. Most don't pray. But they celebrate ‘Īd. Even those who don't pray ‘Īd prayer and don't fast, and who do all kinds of other things, celebrate these feast days. That is also one of the favours of AllāhNone to people. For when they remember and celebrate the feast given by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, it brings benefit to them.

Certainly, praying and other deeds have their account. But this has a different account. To celebrate and show respect to it, to honour it and not to forget it, does not go to waste in the presence of AllāhNone. It is one of the wisdoms of celebrating ‘Īd. As we said, even those who do not do any good deeds, do this. Observing the day made holy by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla strengthens the faith in their hearts, in shā’a Llāh. And it becomes a means for their salvation. This is also the wisdom of ‘Īd. Therefore, these days are blessed days.

We are saying it once again, there are ‘Īdu l-Fiṭr and ‘Īdu l-Aḍḥá. The rest are not holidays. They are just occasions. There are religious and non-religious occasions – they are useless. They are completely useless. They are useful neither here, nor hereafter. Therefore, show respect to these days. Honour them. They have great benefit, both physically and spiritually. May AllāhNone bless them. May they be a means of guidance for people, in shā’a Llāh.

We are celebrating ‘Īd, feast of Ramaḍān. We are, AllāhNone make it happy and blessed for all of you. Which it is, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla give these days for us. ‘Īdu l-Fiṭr for Ramaḍān, and ‘Īdu l-Aḍḥá for Ḥajj and sacrifice sheep and anything. These two feasts, given by AllāhNone for Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam and his nation Ummatu Muḥammad ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Because Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam when heNone arrive to Madīnah Munawwarah, they was celebrating two days in that time. Which, one called Nowruz, second was named Mihrajan. This normally maybe from celebrated like Eastern, from Iran and other country, Asian country. They was celebrating this, and this because Madīnah people going and coming, they also was celebrating this.

Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, "What is these days?" They said, "This we celebrate and we be making feast and playing and eating, and we celebrating like this." And Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, "AllāhNone, HeNone give you two ‘Īds, feasts, to celebrate, better than this. One, after Ramaḍān, first of Shawwāl, and second, tenth of Dhū l-Ḥijjah. These two, you can make celebration, and eat, drink, and go around, play, in these two days. AllāhNone, HeNone will be happy with you.

So, this is what Muslim celebrating. Only two these feasts, we accept this. And when somebody accepts and celebrates, AllāhNone be happy with him and ProphetNone be happy with him also. Because, it is a gift from AllāhNone and HeNone happy to make people to be happy with this. What HeNone gives is very valuable, days and nights. Very holy and very blessed nights this, and days. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, Muslim world, whole they are celebrating this. From whole, all around world, Muslims, they have this since that time, they celebrate and they happy with this. Maybe some country they are more celebrate so you can feel more. But, everywhere where Muslim, they celebrate, and they make for each other to be happy, be peace, and to be happy days this.

And there is, we say, many wisdoms from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. For people, excuse, to make excuse for people. To forgive them. And make them to be, they have blessed and mercy of AllāhNone for this. We are – In Muslim world, most of people they are not practicing Islām. Not practicing even praying. Most of them, they are not practicing this. For to fast – fasting, many of them not fasting. But when they come to celebrate ‘Īd, all of them, they celebrate. Who was not fasting, not praying, doing bad things, doing sins – all of them, they celebrate this. And there is wisdom for this. Who celebrates and accepts ‘Īd, he accepts what AllāhNone gives for Muslims, and he celebrates this. Maybe this make them to be give belief and īmān in their hearts. And AllāhNone forgives them.

For this, it is also some beauty for whole human being, for whole Muslim, to be gift from AllāhNone, this. This big gift from AllāhNone, for people to put something, aḍ‘afi l-īmān, the weakest belief we call this. Aḍ‘afi l-īmān, it also you have something in your heart, meaning. It is not completely empty. They have something. We, in our country also, it is very weak for practicing of Islām, but all they celebrate, and going, visiting each other, asking for celebrate together and asking good feast for everybody. They make clean their dress, or a new dress. You see people come from visiting relatives, visiting neighbours, asking for good wishes for this ‘Īd. This is also even for to be eating and for playing, AllāhNone make this for people, maybe to make them accept in shā’a Llāh, and give belief on their heart. And to – from little thing can be big thing, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone give us barakah and blessing of these days, and make our belief, īmān, more and more, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone bless you and bless us, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
