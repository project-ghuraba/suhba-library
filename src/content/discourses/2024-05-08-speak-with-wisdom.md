As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

There is a saying; "Li-kulli maqāmin maqāl." There are words and speech for every rank, for every assembly. The same thing cannot be said everywhere. In some places you may speak about knowledge. In other places you must speak in a language people there understand. You may speak about agriculture or something else. It must be something you can give that is suited to the people. Whatever people need or ask for, you speak about that. And by that means, it is important to turn people's hearts towards Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. You may speak about anything, just don't let those people go.

People like good people. They like good speeches. They like what they love. And the love of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla can be put inside it. In some matters, some people impose their thoughts on others through persuasive speech even if there is no need to say anything at all. However, as they talk, they can make them leave. Because when speech is without wisdom, it has no benefit. When there is wisdom, people benefit from that wisdom. Otherwise, it is useless. No need to tell some things, not everything. "This is right. I must say it." It's not the time. It's not the place. If you say it, there may be more harm than benefit both to yourself and to others.

Therefore, you must know the people you talk to well and where you talk. You must understand so that you act accordingly. You don't go straight to the topic. Sometimes you must speak about this matter, sometimes about another matter. People will benefit from it. And you will have benefit. Otherwise, it is useless. Even if there is no harm, there is no benefit. It will be "mā lā ya‘nī". It will be useless. "Mā lā ya‘nī" means something useless. May AllāhNone protect us.

Who used to speak the best way, who used to tell the most beautiful things we have ever heard so far, things people benefit from – that was Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim. Every word he said was a jewel. He spoke according to every person. And everyone understood as much as they were able. But whoever listened to him benefited for sure. Therefore, we must follow his way and imitate him as much as we can, in shā’a Llāh. May AllāhNone help all of us, in shā’a Llāh. There is saying in Arabic, "Li-kulli maqāmin maqāl" – for each place, each gathering or for thing, there is speech. You cannot speak everywhere same thing. Some people understanding. Some people, they not understanding. For this, you must be have the wisdom, you must have understanding about situation of place and people and time also. Because many people now they are saying, "In time of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam like this they was doing this, so we must do this." Okay if you can do, do. But not everybody can do. And it is also AllāhNone and Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, HeNone, they are merciful. They said... When ṣaḥābah must do ninety-nine percent from what Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, still he is be ask, "Why you didn't do hundred percent?" But for us, only if we do one percent, it is even good – Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, saying in ḥadīth.

So for this you cannot, when you speak, you must speak about your time, your place, situation. Not what you think it is. This must be, everyone must do this – it is not possible. And Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, for merciful, for this HeNone said, "You must teach people slowly, slowly of what they like, what they are happy." You can speak about culture, about agriculture, about anything, art or thing what they, people like. And through this you can tell them about some truth, so they will be happy with you and be continue with you. But if you only like coming from desert and saying, "You must follow me," not possible for this people. If they do, they cannot, they do wrong thing even.

Even they cannot do like ṣaḥābah or like tābi‘īn because they have wisdom, this. AllāhNone bless them. We don't have wisdom. Without wisdom we, what you do, not be getting benefit, getting harm for you and for other people. For this we are, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we have most perfect example for speech for people, for addressing for people. Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim, al-ḥamdu liLlāh. He was and still people getting benefit from his speech, from his teaching. And they said, "Oh, Mawlānā was saying this and he is right. Even before ten years, or before twenty years ago, he say this – and now we are seeing this." So many things Mawlānā was saying, not only for speech, for benefit of people.

This is very important – to say something good. If you say something, even it is good but sometimes not necessary to say this. Sometimes if you say this, you can harm people even. For this, you must be careful. You must be, have wisdom, not only speech. When you speak without wisdom it is nonsense – "mā lā ya‘nī" meaning in Islām. Mā lā ya‘nī – nonsense it is not acceptable in Islām.

AllāhNone give us wisdom, in shā’a Llāh, to imitate Mawlānā Shaykh, imitate Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
