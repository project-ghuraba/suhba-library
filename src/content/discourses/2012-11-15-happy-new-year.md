All brothers are wishing you "a Happy New Year."

May they also be in goodness. Insha'Allah we will see goodness in this Blessed month, the month of Muharram Al Haram. We ask our Lord that it may be an opening for the believers & that the heads of the Muslims may be raised, and that Allah (swt) may destroy the enemies of Islam. May Allah grant you all goodness. Happy New Year for Muslims, for mankind living on earth. We are asking from the Lord of Heavens to give us more power for Islam, to keep His (swt) worshipping. We are happy that we reached this New Year, new month, that it will be a new opening for all of mankind.
