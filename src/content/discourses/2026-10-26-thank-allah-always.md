As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam says,<br>
وَبِالشُّكْرِ تَدُوم النَّعَم
<br>“Wa bi sh-shukri tadūmu n-niʿam.”<br>
“With thankfulness do blessings perpetuate.”

When you are making shukr, the niʿam, favors of Allah perpetuate. When people have something, they don’t know its value. So we must always thank Allah for what He gave us. Unfortunately, we are not taking care of this [valuing thankfulness].

When you’re living a normal life, you think it will be like that forever. No. Sometimes it changes. But if you thank Allah, He gives you more, and whatever you have of goodness continues; isn’t cut. There’s an old Turkish saying—people now, everywhere are not happy and are complaining about everything, before, old people used to say, “O Allah, don’t take from us what we’re used to!” This means they are happy with what Allah has given them and want it to continue and not be cut off.

This is good adab, good teaching from Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, and an order from Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. May Allah make us from those people who are happy with His favors.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
