Madad La ilaha ill'Allah La ilaha ill'Allah La ilaha ill'Allah Muhammadun Rasulullah alayhi salatullah thumma Assalamu alaykum Ya Ibadallahi s-Salihin ammidduna bi madadikum Assalamu alaykum wa rahmatullahi wa barakatuh Audhu Billahi min ash-shaytani r-rajim Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim.

Say and don't fear! O our Master who is controlling this planet, please we are asking a heavenly support for understanding and then, to follow what we understand. We have been created for understanding. He who understands must follow what he understood. O our attenders from human nature, men on this planet are of two different kinds, one of them human nature and second animal nature. Yes. He who is belonging to human nature, that means he is riding on his ride or on his donkey, or on his horse. And second ones, as they are animal nature, their animal nature is getting in front of them and they are controlling that person.

As a mum with her small child, when entering a supermarket and looking and seeing so many things that are occupying their minds. Mum is carrying and [child] crying 'come and buy for me, then buy that for me’, and mum running like this, like that. Mum is under command of the small one. Just in such a way so many people are not able to control their ego and their egos are like that small child carrying on that way, this way. They are animal in nature. Their levels going to be animal’s level. But, human nature, that is belonging to their heavenly identities and their heavenly beings carry them through a good way to follow the ways of holy people, the ways of the blessed ones, the holy ways of prophets, peace be upon them.

They are following that way. They are good ones. Therefore, they are those people that ride on their rides and send their rides according to a heavenly direction. But it is a pity, now in our time people are not able to control their physical beings. They are not able to control their rides and are following their rides according to the demands of their rides. Now through east and west, millions of people, all are going to be into two parts. One of them of human nature, another, animal nature. And mostly people, their efforts are for their ride's demands. They are not living for anything else but they are living only to make their rides happy. Never thinking about their spiritual being, that human appearance.

All prophets have appeared through this world with their human figure. No one appeared as an angel appears. No. Allah Almighty sent his representatives as human nature, human appearance. But those people who are following their rides are always objecting. They are saying, 'If you are real one that was sent as a different being and you are saying you are prophets coming from heavens, we are not accepting you until your appearance is the appearance of an angel. We are not accepting you as a man because we never understood a heavenly representative to be like us. Your appearance must be as an angel's.

That is the first point of their objection, never accepting you and me, we are same. We are not accepting you. We are accepting someone whose levels are over our levels, therefore the last prophet's nation, were mostly saying, 'Why coming an orphan as an extra[ordinary] being or [why not] a special being granted to him [rather than being] an orphan. Why was that special appearance not granted to that big city's leader or that country's king?' That was their objection. That means their levels were from their [out]look. Always to be a different status - to be a different being.

They never accepted an ordinary person or they couldn't think that an ordinary person could be dressed in a heavenly dress. Therefore they were always objecting [against] prophets. Because prophets were coming and attacking their bad characteristics, attacking their cruelty, attacking their bad attributes. Therefore they were attacking prophets saying 'you are not prophets, you have not been sent from heavens. If you are real ones bring some treasures or you must come with angels in front of you and behind, and be surrounded by angels.

They never understood that a heavenly person is warning them or could warn them to come on the right path, and they continued. Now in our days, the same position. They are like "ula'ika kal an'am", O Salafi ulama, are you understanding Holy Quran? Quran, azimu sh-shan. O Muluk, kings and sultans are you understanding what it means? When Allah Almighty is saying, astaidhu Billah:

ulaika kal an`ami bal hum adallu (7:179)

Subhanallah, Subhanallah, Sultan Allah, ulaika hadir, the present ones kal an'am, are like animals, and coming after that, you must understand astaidhu Billah, ulaika kal an`am. Their understanding is only at the level of animals, bal hum adall.

O Salafi ulama, what is the difference in understanding what Allah Almighty is saying, ula'ika kal an`am? Ula'ika, are those people, O My most glorified and most beloved one in My Divine Presence, those people who are objecting [against] your prophecy are like animals because they are under the hegemony of animalness, animal nature. But what do you think O Salafi ulama, addressing to you bal hum ula'ika yushirr ala l-hadireen, ula'ika, you know that they were objecting in front of the Seal of Prophets about his prophecy. O My chosen one, O My most glorified one, don't worry, in front of you they are like animals, don't worry. Leave them.

Ulaika kal an`am bal hum adall. What He knows, but what we can understand is something just granted, bal hum, it is not hadir people, present ones. Present ones, saying for them, 'ula'ika', ‘O My most beloved one, those are animals, they are never understanding because their animal nature is passing over their human nature. Leave them’, 'bal hum adall', Allah Almighty yushir, points out that there are some people yet to come, in future, that will also be on their level but in their foolishness they are more idiotic than those people.

Those people were not reading, learning anything and saying this. But in future those people who will come, some other ones on the same route but worse than those people. Those were ummiy, not knowing anything and were objecting on your prophecy, but in future, coming from mankind, millions and billions of people who will also be objecting on your prophecy. Ula'ika - bal hum, coming nations who are going to claim, 'We are knowing everything, we reached the top of civilization, we have such and such technology-power', bal hum adall, their levels are under the levels of those people before you because they don’t know anything and are objecting.

But coming ones, you must understand from Holy Quran, O Azhar ulama, and it is your responsibility to make more clear that ayatu l-karima, this holy verse to all people. Now from east to west, from north to south, all nations are throwing away the understanding of prophecy. And they are saying it is not true for something to come from the heavens and we are on this world, we are knowing, we are reaching, we are understanding everything and we are not in need, therefore they are preventing holy books to be through their universities, academies, high schools.

Bal hum adall, is signifying, yushir, signifies that nowadays all doctors and professors or academics or technicians are claiming that, ‘We know everything and we are not in need of something coming from heavens to make our ways’ that we are asking to be on, and the Lord of Heavens is saying about their levels that when the end of the world comes, their levels are going to be under the levles of the ummiy, illiterate nations, illiterate tribes. And now, we are in it as everyone is claiming ‘We are not in need of holy books particularly, we are not accepting the last book they are saying is coming from the heavens. We are not accepting it, astaghfirullah.

And Allah Almighty is giving their levels, their levels of understanding are under the understanding level of animals. That is the spring, the source of all problems. The source of everything now through this world, everywhere, everything is wrong because their source is not a clean source. Their sources are so dirty, like sewage channels. That is it. Now they are in it like rats and they are not understanding, and I am sorry to say, those who are claiming ‘We are Salafi ulama’, why are they not making this clear for all nations? And I am sorry to say that hundreds and thousands of doctors of shariah, why they are not making clear to people such a subject. To make people wake up and we are in need of such people that may wake up all nations.

We are hoping from the Lord of Heavens to send us real ones, not imitated ulama imitated learned people, no. They are alimu bi l-lisan, jahilu bi'l qalb. Those people are knowing only to speak something from books that they are writing according to their understanding. They are not coming through the level of their hearts, as the hearts are the centre of heavenly lights and heavenly lights show to mankind what is black what is white, what is darkness, what is light. And you will be asked. Therefore O People, don't claim to say ‘We are Salafi ulama followers’ or doctors, or we are this or that ones.

Keep for understanding from Holy Quran and Holy Quran, Fihi ma fihi. One of the great ones of Prophet's inheritors was saying, he was writing a book and saying Fihi ma fihi. Are you understanding that everything you are asking is in it. And endless Knowledge Oceans, nothing can be closed or out of that one, of that Holy Book. Fihi ma fihi - everything, you can find in it. And that is a heavenly `ata, heavenly grant. Try to reach that heavenly grant, O People. Particularly ulama and doctors. Keep it and no one can learn without a teacher.

The Seal of Prophets, peace be upon him was the teacher of everyone in existence, including heavenly beings. Here and hereafter, he is the only one that may take real Reality from Divine Presence that is suitable for mankind to know, to keep and to do and to reach an honour such, that no one can reach. Allahu Akbar wa Lillahi'l hamd. What we are saying is that mankind some of them are going to be of human nature and some others are of animal nature.

Enough, O People to follow animals' ways. Come and listen, come and obey come and act according to what the Lord of Heavens granted to Real Ones of mankind, as their levels are levels of humanity, humanity and human nature. Come and hear, come and learn, leave level of animals and [what is] under animal's level also.

May Allah forgive us for the honour of the most honoured one, Sayyidina Muhammad, sallAllahu alayhi wa sallam. O our Lord make us understand and send us those who may teach us and grant us some understanding - for Muslim World and for non-Muslim world. It is for our fa'idah, benefit. If not, no good or no bright future. The trouble we are in now. its real sources are those people who are insisting not to leave the level of animals. And they are quarrelling, fighting and destroying everything. Those who destroy humanity, no more honour for them, they will be taken away. May Allah grant us from His heavenly sheltering. Fatiha.
