As-Salamu Alaikum wa Rahmatullahu wa Barakatuhu. Madad ya Rasul Allah, Madad ya Shaykh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, madad ya Shaykh Muhammad Nazim al Haqqani, dastur! Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fil jamia'

Don't make su'u dhan (holding negative thoughts, ill-thinking)- Don't have bad opinion for Allah (awj). Some people have bad opinions by saying "why this happened like this? why that is like that...?". Of course it can be unintentionally, but be careful for this. Allah (awj) does not resemble anything. He has no equal. He has no partner either. There is also nothing that can stand against Allah (awj). Allah (awj), along with His Mightiness, is Merciful- does not punish the wrongdoers immediately. If it was for ordinary people, when someone does something bad they for sure want to give his punishment as soon as possible. Allah (awj) gives these people time so that they may repent if they can.

Allah does not need the worship of anyone - all people, jinn, insects, bugs, animals, all that exist. He is not in need, hasha! Their worship is only for themselves. No tyrant/oppressor can get away with his tyrannical & cruel actions. He definitely finds his punishment. Allah (awj) is the Owner of Adalat/Justice. Therefore you should always have husn al dhan/good opinion for Allah (awj). Allah is Great.

One shouldn't lose hope in Allah's Mercy. If he does, it is ill thinking. No matter what difficulties or hardship you are going through, the biggest blessing is that Prophets & Awliyas made us know Allah. Of course there are levels of knowing Allah. They make us know Allah (awj) & His faith, according to our understanding. Prophets, and after them awliyas & ulamas show this, for their belief is strong. And of course, they also have different levels. Some dive into this sea more than others.

And this sea, is not an easy sea. Most captains have sunk their ships in that sea & got destroyed. It has no shore, nor does it have an end. When a ship sinks, it comes ashore but it doesn't have this either. Therefore don't dive into these seas on your own. You can go up to the point your Shaykh shows you, where he shows as your rushd/maturity. Don't go further than that. If you do you get destroyed. There are dangerous places in some seas. If you go 100 m from the shore.. It says "don't go" from the shore, forbidden. If you go, you get drowned no matter how young or what a good swimmer you are. Doesn't matter. Don't endanger yourself. Allah (awj) is Merciful. Know this. It is enough. Don't be disobedient to Allah. Be content with everything, the station of rida/contentment is also good. And Tasawwuf (Sufism) is only for the discipline of man's ego. That we are already seeing it in other societies as well. Whoever says "I know Quran better, I understand better" we heard the worst of unbelief in these places, in these Arabic speaking countries. We don't call them "Arab" because they are not the real Arabs. Real Arab is the Prophet (sas). But the things they do in these Arabic speaking countries, you get goose bumps when you see them, their kufr in Allah. Because they don't have the level of Tariqat. When they say "we know everything" on their own this is what happens.

Therefore as we said, don't have bad opinion for Allah (awj). Everything is in Allah's hand, you can't do anything. Surrender to it & be content. The sohbat for this Ramadan day is that much, insha Allah.

Today we speak about, to think good for Allah (awj). Allah (awj), He is only God. No God, no any God except Him, He is God. If you say, no partner for Him, no another one can stop in front of Him. If you think other way, you think wrong for Allah. And this is forbidden. Allah (awj), He is only One. Nothing you can compare or make example for Him. He has everything, He not need for anything. He not need to make advertisement for Him. All mankind, jinni, angel, creature, animal- what you see, what you don't see in this universe, if they are, Allah (awj) not in need for any worship from them. What they do worshipping, it is not necessary for Him. Nothing even. No, you cannot say even necessary. It is ridiculous word for this.

Because there is people, they think when they making worshipping or they teach them; you make good for God. No, this is not. If you worshipping, only for you it is good. And when you be worshipping, Allah happy with you because you do good for yourself. Not good for Himself. When He see people making wrong, He is not happy with them. Why? Because this is harming them, not harming Him. Nothing affect Him. Any part, even the smallest part from this world affect Him, or the biggest one affect Him, it is impossible. You cannot say this, at all.

You cannot imagine Allah's Mightiness. Allah giving everything for they are, who worshipping or who they are not worshipping. If somebody here in world doing something wrong, quickly they will punish him. Allah (awj) not punishing people until maybe they repent it. And give for every people food & drink and everything for people, who they are believer or not believer. Waiting because He is Allah, not hurry like people. His attributes they are diferent. Many of them you cannot find in mankind or in creature. Only it is special for Him, like Creating & Highness.. Many of them I cannot speak in English name. And He is not forgetting anything & calculating what people doing.

The oppressors, maybe they die without somebody punish them here, in dunya. But this is not won (a win) for them because they will be judged for this, what they do. Even in dunya also, Allah not leave this oppressor people without punishment but people maybe they, he is 30-40 year or 60 year live, after die without anything happen to him. But Allah of course in dunya also He punish from inside, without people knowing what happen for him. Allah, He is the most Just. This is very important. For this, be good thinking for Allah. "Why Allah making this people dying from hunger? Why this people going to die like this, that?".. Don't ask for this. And don't..There is only limit for people to think about Allah (awj). This limit, Alhamdulillah Prophet & awliya & scholars they teach them until one point.

The good scholars, there is many scholars. Even in Islam also there is scholars following money. What they give them money & tell this, they tell people. For this, you must follow scholars who they are good. Only for Allah they believing, following awliyullah, saints.

Because when you are going to this very big sea- Allah (awj), to research about Him, many captains they sink their ship in this sea & they die. And this sea, there is no shore for this sea. And no limit for this. In dunya, when ship coming sinking, coming to shore. But in that, not going at all. You cannot find anything. For this, be careful. Don't be looking for this philosopher people. And you will be danger, halaq/destroy yourself completely.

Be careful for this. And you must be with awliyullah & the good scholars who following awliyullah. Don't follow new fashion this people. They are looking for to be famous. This is very bad illness, also from illness of ego. They destroy their last days for this temporary life. Be careful from this people.

Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, Al Fatiha.
