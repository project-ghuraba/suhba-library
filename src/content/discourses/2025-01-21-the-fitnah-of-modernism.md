A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

“In hādhā illā asāṭīru l-awwalīn” (8:31). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l- ‘Aẓīm. It is said in the Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n, “They say, “These are just old stories” When prophets said something, they objected, saying, “These are old stories.” Many times, Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim used to laugh a lot at those people, at what they did, what they said, and what they wrote. In his eyes, everything they did was ridiculous. They called it çağdaş (modernity). Mawlānā used to call it çaydaş (wordplay: tea-stone). He would joke about it, implying it's just a meaningless term, a playful twist on “modern”. It means being modern. That is nothing new. It has existed since old times. They would say to the prophets, “You’re telling old stories. You’re saying old things.” They would object. Unbelievers and kāfirs didn’t approve of what they did. But unfortunately, nowadays – AllāhNone created all people the same. For two hundred years, what shayṭān has embedded inside the Islāmic world is: “You are stuck behind. Your minds are backward. Look at the kāfirs, they’ve surpassed you. They are modern. They surpassed you. Don’t keep those old principles in your religion. Renew your religion. Be innovators. New things. Don’t accept the old ones. Look how the Europeans live. Look at what they do.”

There was admiration. When you mentioned Europe, the admiration for kāfirs was at its peak. Whatever they did was very beautiful in people’s eyes. They’ve done more and more, until we reached this day. Eventually, they saw that there was no difference. But still, people go directly to be with them, to follow their ideas, because they say, “There is money.” But in reality, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla has created mankind, and wherever they are, their sustenance will come. No one is superior to anyone else. There is no need to be subject to anyone. They subject themselves to them. They lost their dignity. They lost their personalities. They lost their honour. They fell into pieces. They play with them as they want.

So, modernity is not a skill. It’s not modernity – If you are not smart, even if you are a thousand modernists it is of no use. There is harm, not benefit. They robbed you by calling it modernity. They took everything, leaving you nothing. They took their hometowns. They killed, cut, and reaped. But still, you follow them in the name of modernity. That’s not important. People are ordinary, but what’s more dangerous is that those who pretend to be scholars, those who pretend to be ‘ulamā’, do not love or approve of the mashāyikh. They say, “They are reactionary, backward. They are not intellectuals like us. We look at the orientalists in Europe. Look how beautifully they talk and what good ideas they have.” Their ideas are to make people without īmān, to take them away from Islām and make them enemies of our Holy ProphetNone and the companions; nothing else. These are the modernists. It is more dangerous than the admiration for the life of Europeans. It is more dangerous because they are striving directly with the intention of disrupting society; nothing else. They have nothing. Those who follow them are either fools or traitors, nothing else. One of the two; there is no third. Therefore, watch out for that.

Our way, the way of the mashāyikh, is the right way. Do not deviate from it. Hold on to it tightly so that they don’t make you a toy and play with you in their hands. May AllāhNone protect us from their evil.

Mawlānā Shaykh was, many time he is making joke about people In our country, and the people in whole Islamic states. All, since two hundred years and when after – until one hundred years before, was after this become more – but since two hundred years. Mawlānā was hate this French Revolution, because bad idea all coming through this revolution, shayṭān revolution. And this revolution still continue until now. Maybe until Sayyidunā Mahdī, he will clean all this. But he was, Mawlānā Shaykh, whole time he is… Mawlānā Shaykh he was whole time laughing, making joke about them. Give them funny names, funny thing, because people they thought they must follow the Europeans, they must leave Islām. Islām make them to be backward.

To be modernist, you must leave Islām or you follow what the idea of European. And people, they was real...like their mind went out, and only thinking about this. What, how this people they are doing this? Through bad scholar, through idea of this revolution. Every time pumping, pumping in this Islamic world. Why? To get rid of the real people who defend land and defend for religion. Not leave them. To be easy to take them. But this, they make this people who defend Islām as they are backward people.

And modernist people, they are other people, who, with Europe, going, studying in Europe and coming to back. And many of them they are send them, even Sulṭān ‘Abdu l-Ḥamīd, raḥmatu Llāhi ‘alayh, he send thousand of this people to make, they say modernist, to make people country with machine and new things to see. But what happen? Whole this when they went to Europe, they give them women, they give them drink, and only teach them bad idea, nothing about technology, nothing else. And they come destroy whole this empire. For this…But now, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, people they know there is no difference between European or other, any people. Maybe other people they are more clever now. Many thing they do more better than European. But they finish, and this what happen.

So, people, all this coming through centuries when prophets coming to their nation. They said, “Oh, this is old stories you told us.” Now also same. They say, when saying mashāyikh or people who following Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam way, Sufi way, they said, “You are backward people. We are not follow you. You must be modern. Modern like other people, like this.” And still this people, until now in university everywhere, they – especially in Islamic university, especially in Türkiye – there are many of them. Many of them not accept – even not mashāyikh, not accept ḥadīth, not accept madhhab, not accept anything. So AllāhNone keep us safe from this people. This is balā’ (calamity) for end of time, this fitnah. But, in shā’a Llāh, who be with mashāyikh, with ‘ulamā’ ṣāliḥīn, he will be safe, in shā’a Llāh. Allāh save us from them. Keep us safe until Sayyidunā Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām come to clean all of this. Because Mawlānā Shaykh was saying, Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh, many time we hear: when Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām coming, he will clean all this bad idea, bad people, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
