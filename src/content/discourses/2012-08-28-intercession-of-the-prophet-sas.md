As-Salamu Alaikum. Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fil jamia' Thanks to Allah that we are from the people of Tariqat.

To appreciate, realize the worth of the Prophet (sas).. No one other than Allah Almighty can appreciate the true worth of the Prophet (sas) but a little bit respect.. Showing respect for the Prophet (sas), keeping his honour high is first to the benefit of people, themselves. Therefore the new generations coming, don't you believe in these scholars..Not even scholars but some little educated men. Because if you don't show respect for the Prophet (sas), if you don't ask from him his intercession, your end will not be good. And your end, we don't mean the end here but what is important is akhirah.

The Hadith of the Prophet (sas) says, he compares himself to the one by the fire. Before they would make fire or light candles for light and insects would be attracted to that light; bugs, flies, crickets would come to fire, or moths. They would get burned & destroyed. And our Prophet (sas) compares himself to this; his nation is coming to the fire for it's light & he is expelling them so that they won't get burned, says the Prophet (sas). Or else, people will be in eternal fire. But of course, no matter how much he does, still most people fall into fire. And which also shows that who don't follow the Prophet (sas), they are going to their destruction. Their end will not be good.

There is Nur there. You should come to the Nur. If you don't show respect to the owner of Nur & you rush to the fire, you get burned & become ashes. If you insist on your own reasoning, for sure you get destroyed. And the Prophet (sas) is saying this to his Companions. Where are the Companions & where are we compared to them? So it means, this is not only for them but until the Day of Judgement Prophet (sas) is protecting us. He will protect also on the D. of J. insha Allah. Who asks for his intercession wins both in dunya & in akhirah. This is a very important thing. Such people who are not aware of anything. They put forth a logic that even kids laugh at these things.

This time period is over now. After the Ottomans, a punishment came on these people, especially on Turks. Just like the events taking place today are a punishment, as Mawlana says. They went against the Ottomans. First the Turks suffered this punishment. They lived an era of ignorance where such crazy things happened that one cannot even imagine. Now this time is over, the saying of "this was like this, this was like that" No, everything is clear. No need to misinterpret the Hadiths of the Prophet, or saying "there is no such a thing as Laylat al Qadr, or this or that".. The blame in on the people who listen to their words. Don't even speak of such things. The Prophet (sas) until the Day of Judgement, more than you or we live, meaning he is increasing the good life span of man.. We can't see with our eyes but with the eyes that see; awliyas, the good ones, afdals, abdals, they are all meeting with the Prophet (sas) every night.

We are the nation of such a Prophet. All the Prophets wanted to give up their Prophecy & prayed only to be an ordinary man from his nation. This is a very important thing. Always ask intercession from the Prophet (sas). And also advise the new generations to do so, so that they won't take the wrong ways. Because the ways of shaytan are plenty. Before he was directing people to communism, left-wing, right-wing.. Now he changed it & now people don't want tariqats, awliyas & the Prophet (sas). They say "we don't accept, there is no such a thing". Meaning, shaytan sneaks inside people in thousand different shapes to misguide them. Therefore if you go in the way of the Prophet (sas), you go to Nur. You don't go to fire, you don't get burned in fire. Our Prophet (sas) leaves you in Nur. But if you want to pass it & go to the other side then you go to the depths of hell. This is the summary of it.

Today we speak about, shafa'ah of Prophet (sas). Because he said in Hadith, "I am like the man.." Before in old time there was no electricity, light only by fire or by candle. So insect coming to this candle. "I am that man, taking this insect away from fire, to not going to fire". What, they are listening to me, not going to fire, only to be in light. But who is not listening, going fire & they finish, destroy themselves. And this is for, especially for who is not agree with shafa'ah.

Shafa'ah meaning intercession. Without.. because he was saying this for Sahaba. And Sahaba, all they were with him, Companion of him. And they are doing 100 % instruction of Islam. After Sahaba, there is billions of Muslim coming. So we are more in need for his intercession. So it is proof, because he is all the time with us. Here & in Qiyamah also. Without him, no, direct you go to hell for fire. But with him, you will be in light. It is easy to be, in light.. Not easy to be in light, easy to go to hell, to fire more easy than stay in light.

So for this, most of people they are going to fire. With their desire, with their bad action, with not listening, agree with awliya or for Tariqah. They are saying "this is not good way. Our way better" But with your way, nobody can step between you & fire. This is the biggest proof, it is Hadith from Prophet (sas), "I am preventing you from fire." It is very easy to go to fire. You are in fire. You can be in light only with Prophet (sas). If you are not with him, directly fire. This is shaytan, he know this. For this he want.. he don't want to be, saying "Prophet, he is finished, he died so you can only yourself, you can survive" No you cannot survive. This is the proof of this-Hadith of Prophet of (sas), "I am preventing you from fire." This is enough.
