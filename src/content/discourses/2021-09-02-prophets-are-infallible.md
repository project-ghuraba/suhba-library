Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla sent Prophets in order to show guidance to people. Prophets are superior among other people. They are beloved servants of Allah ﷻ. Their attributes are the most perfect. Allah ﷻ created them in such way so that people cannot find a flaw in them. Allah ﷻ created them in the best way so that people follow them and take example from them. They are infallible. They never commit sins. Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla does not let Prophets commit sins. They are infallible which means they are free from sins. Everyone has big or little flaws, mistakes and sins except for Prophets. And in the sight of Allah ﷻ, Prophets are on the highest level.

Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla sent Prophets to every nation. Anywhere in the world wherever there were people, He sent a Prophet there. Certainly, there were those who believed and those who did not believe. Prophets carried out their duties. And this duty was given to our Holy Prophet ﷺ, the last Prophet. There is no Prophet after him. Whoever said he was a Prophet was punished as a false Prophet. They were eradicated and everyone following them perished.

As we said, Prophets were from every nation. But most of them were from Bani Isra’il. Most Prophets mentioned in the Qur’an are from them. However, people did not understand their value. They were either killed, or exiled, or denied. It is important that the Prophets appeared from their nation. But as they didn’t show importance to it, Allah ﷻ doesn’t show importance to them. Many Prophets, even Zakariya ‘alayhi s-salam and Yahya ‘alayhi s-salam were martyred.

There were some times when several Prophets lived at the same time. As that didn’t happen with our Holy Prophet, people get surprised how that’s possible. There was even an incident in which the Jews killed a thousand Prophets in one day. Therefore, they shouldn’t boast. They did too much oppression. Even though Allah ﷻ had favored them, they are the nation who didn’t appreciate this favor, who killed and cheated, and who didn’t believe in Prophets. What can you do? We believe in all Prophets because all of them are Muslim. Religion in the sight of Allah ﷻ is Islam. Everything taught to them is also Islam. There is no other religion. Since ‘Adam ‘alayhi s-salam until the time of our Holy Prophet ﷺ and until Qiyamah the religion is Islam. There is no such thing as another religion.

Therefore, our Holy Prophet ﷺ is the last Prophet and the most perfect Prophet, the highest of all Prophets. As we said, the superior among people are Prophets. And the superior among Prophets, the most beloved servant of Allah ﷻ is our Holy Prophet ﷺ. It is an honor for us to be proud of him and to obey him. It is the best thing for humanity and Muslims to be from the Ummah of our Holy Prophet ﷺ. To respect him is something that Allah ﷻ loves. He ﷻ let us have the best thing. We should appreciate it. We should always make Shukr. No need to look at the condition of this world and complain. We should make Shukr that Allah ﷻ is giving us our Rizq with the Barakah of our Holy Prophet ﷺ. May Allah ﷻ make it last. Millions of Shukr to Allah ﷻ.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
