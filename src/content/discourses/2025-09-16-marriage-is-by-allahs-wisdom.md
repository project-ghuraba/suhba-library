As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
يَا أَيُّهَا النَّاسُ إِنَّا خَلَقْنَاكُم مِّن ذَكَرٍ وَأُنثَىٰ وَجَعَلْنَاكُمْ شُعُوبًا وَقَبَائِلَ لِتَعَارَفُوا ۚ إِنَّ أَكْرَمَكُمْ عِندَ اللَّهِ أَتْقَاكُمْ
<br>“Yā ayyuhā n-nāsu innā khalaqnākum min dhakarin wa unthā wa ja’alnākum shu’ūbāan wa qabā’ila lita’ārafū inna akramakum ‘inda Allahi atqākum”<br>
“O mankind, indeed, We have created you from male and female and made you peoples and tribes that you may know one another. Indeed, the most noble of you in the sight of Allah is the most righteous of you.” (Qur’ān 49:13). SadaqAllahu l-‘Aẓīm.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says in this ayah karīmah, “We created you from a man and a woman, and multiplied you.” It is a wisdom of Allah for people to meet. When Allah decrees it, He brings you together in unexpected places.

They build a home, a ḥalāl marriage, and a beautiful life. What matters is that they are on the way of Allah, that they be good servants of Allah, and that they have a rank in the sight of Allah. This is what’s important when living in dunyā.

This entire Qur’an Karīm, each sacred word of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla contains millions of wisdoms, billions of wisdoms – Qur’ān ʿAẓīmu sh-Sha’n is filled with infinite wisdom. One of them is people’s marriages, who will they marry, how will it be, who will they have, and how will their children be.

By Allah’s wisdom, He brings people together. They build a home. They must build that home for the pleasure of Allah. It becomes beautiful only if it pleases Allah, Allah willing. That is the purpose of living in this world.

Shayṭān enters the minds of some people. They say, “I curse and disrespect my parents. Why did you bring me to this world! If you weren’t here, I wouldn’t have come to this world. I don’t want this!” That is the very essence of foolishness. Because that happened by the will of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla.

Who will be born, who will die, who will marry; all of this happened by the will of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. Following that will, thanking Him, and following His way and commands both brings peace to people and becomes a means to raising good people.

It’s not good to blame others or to make yourself seem like the victim. Allah created you. Allah has shown you the beautiful way. Go on that way and keep on it.

This is the meaning of life, after all. Life is short. If you appreciate it and make it beautiful, you will be the winner in the end. You will be at peace forever. Otherwise, you will suffer a great deal.

May Allah protect you. May Allah grant those who want to marry good marriages, may they have good families, and may you raise good generations, inshāAllah.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
