Shaykh Muhammad Nazim Al-Haqqani An-Naqshibandi, Sohbat of the 10th of April, 2004.

That tajalli is here. His ancestors were gentle also. Light, their associations were also light. He's Sheikh Mahmud (al-Faghnawi), a great awliya in the Golden Chain. Even today if any of his children sit in an association, then that association laughs the whole time. Therefore, I said it's strange how this state came over me - laughing at a maqam, in front of a great awliya. I never laughed at a maqam before.

We visited Ubaydullah al-Ahrar Hz., visited Sheikh al-Gujdawani Hz, also Shah Naqshband Hz. High up, in Bukhara we visited the Prophet's, alaihi s-salam, uncle's son - Our Prophet's uncle Hz. Abbas's youngest son. They made me go up the stairs, maybe 70 flights. We stayed -like this there- I said let me have a look how I might climb up and two people held me from each side, -don't know if our mureeds or others- like a wind I was at the top. Hz Hasan is up there. What a flight! They said, the wind cannot catch up with you. I went up, my feet flying. What a flight. This is the maqam of the Prophet's uncle's son, Hasan Effendi's maqam. When the Russians came to destroy this maqam dragons came out of his maqam and ate all of them up. Russian soldiers came to destroy this maqam. Snakes came out of this grave and killed them, finished them. The Russians did not come back again. Hazrati Hasan.

There, we visited their maqams. There is majesty. With this majesty a state of laughing came over me! I said; Oh yahu how is this? I have seen so many maqams - Sultans lying at the front and their deputies buried at the feet of their Sheikh. No sultan ordered for a maqam or dome to be built. Sheikh Effendi lies up there, his deputies at his feet. He lies at the front. So many of them. They are all maqams with haybat (majesty). Also Sheikh Ali Ramitani Hz. - He is also full of majesty. Dua came. We made a very powerful dua there. He pointed out and we said it.

But Mahmud al-Faghnawi Hz., a state of laughing came over us... We weren't in this state when we came here. We sat and then laughing came over us. I didn't know what du’a to make! He said, "Don't worry. He gave permission to laugh as much as you like. Everybody else laughs, he said.

The tree trunks are like this - as if jasmine comes out of them or vine leaves come out or figs. There are so many fig trees there. That maqam is full of fig trees. It wasn't the season for figs so they were not ripe. In short, that was it. Wa min Allahi t-tawfiq. May Allah grant peace to you all.

They opened up Sheikh Ubaydullah al-Ahrar Hazrat's grave and found him as he died. Hisham Effendi said to me he is in the same state as he died. He said "Sheikh Effendi, he looks like Hazrat (Grand Sheikh). They took a photo - he said he'll send it. Ulamas there didn't like it like this bare head and placed a turban on his head and took a photo. They will send it. I will show you tomorrow. He is exactly like Sheikh Effendi (Sh Abdullah ad-Daghistani Hz). None of his features have changed, he is lying there. Ubaydullah al-Ahrar Hazrat - May Allah bless him and his secrets.
