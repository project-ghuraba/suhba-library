"Ilm (knowledge) is gained by studying & hilm (forbearance) by trying to be tolerant" (Hadith). Is it not true? We have lost the old things & can't find any taste in the new ones. People cannot find pleasure in the new. The old ones had taste. Who planted them, would plant with pleasure & joy. Now they plant acres of cucumber (with machines), acres of lettuce, etc.. but they have no taste. Inside Lefkosa (Nicosia), there were one, two, three, four gardens within the city walls - they would come to the bazaar last. The village people would bring first, for example the fava beans,or cucumbers. But theirs would arrive last & they were expensive - the ones growing inside Lefkosa.

There were horse drawn water wheels, although now there are many two-legged ones, a poor four-legged animal would keep revolving to pull water from the well with a water wheel. We even had one of them at our house - at the house that I grew up. It is an antique well. It's wheel was so high that we would get scared when we look from it's top - we would rush & run to the upper side. Even when it got dark, we wouldn't be able to go down. People have no fear anymore. These are old matters. Around it, there was a wheel drawn by animals that it would bring up water. It's water never decreased, even in a drought. Astaghfirullah, tawba ya Rabbi, tawba ya Rabbi...
