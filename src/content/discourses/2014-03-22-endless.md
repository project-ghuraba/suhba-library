Shaykh Muhammad Nazim Al-Haqqani An-Naqshibandi, Sohbat of the 22nd of March, 2014.

O our Lord. Your gifts are without limit. O lovers of Shah Mardan O lovers of Shah Mardan. Our Allah, You granted us, O Lord. You granted O Lord. There is no beginning to Your grants and no ending, my son. We should ask from our Mawla. We should ask from our Mawla. We should wish from our Lord. He is the Giver, He is the Giver. We are poor, weak. We are at Your door O my Lord. We are beggars at Your door.

O lovers of Shah Mardan. There is no ending to Your grants O our Lord. There is no ending to Your grants O our Lord. You created this universe. You granted. You made it to exist. Let us say, my son, our children - Let us say O lovers of Shah Mardan. May Allah Almighty grant His favors and kindness to your ancestors. May we open. May we watch and see in our Lord's dominion. May we watch and see O our Lord. May our hearts open, be content. Let us say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. How beautiful, How beautiful. How beautiful it is to say our Lord's holy Words, my son.

May we expand. May we get majestic. May we become beautiful. May we do dhikr, thank, praise our Lord. Let us recite Your Almighty Name, O our Lord. May the doors of heavens open. Our Allah, our Subhan. May the heavenly doors open for you my son. We shall not stay in constricted places We don't want narrow places. I am asking for a spacious place from our Lord. Then let us say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. How beautiful O lovers of Shah Mardan. How beautiful are Shah Mardan's words, how beautiful, how sweet. Our Allah bestowed on us. Our Allah won't cut His grants, our Allah won't cut. His door is open. He is Subhan, He is Sultan, He is Subhan, He is Sultan. O our Lord. Allah Allah, Allah Allah. Allah Allah, Subhan Allah. Allah Allah, Allah Allah Subhan Allah.

Let us say Bismillah, Bismillah, Bismillah, Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Your bestowal is endless. There is no boundary to what our Mawla will give, has given. Allahu Allah, Allahu Allah. Allahu Rabbi ma li siwahu. We shall wish everything from our Lord. We shall ask everything from our Lord. He gives. He gives eternally. Our Lord gives endlessly. His granting is endless, limitless.

Let us say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Say Bismillah. Say Bismillah so that you open. Open my son, Haji Mehmed our son. He (swt) won't leave us in difficulty. Our Lord did not leave any of His servants to be in difficulty. Our Lord did not leave any of His servants in poverty. He gave, gave, gave. O lovers of Shah Mardan. Listen, ask. Say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. How beautiful. Our Lord gives, gives, gives. Our Allah gives from His unending, limitless kindness and favors. You are Subhan O Lord. You are our Lord with endless favors and grants.

O lovers of Shah Mardan. He (swt) says ask, I will give. O our Lord, O our Lord. Wherever I look, I see our Lord's beauties. Wherever I look, I see our Lord's beauties. We get content and we say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Our Lord is the possessor of unlimited grants. He (swt) also grants us, He(swt) gives us endlessly. Fatiha.

Say O servants Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Let us say O servants Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Our Allah is so beautiful. May You give us strength our Mawla. May You give us strength, steadiness. May You give us beauty, honor. May You give us honor, O our Lord. Our Allah, may our sons also be given strength. Your servants O Lord. Your grants are endless, limitless. O our Lord.

Fatiha.
