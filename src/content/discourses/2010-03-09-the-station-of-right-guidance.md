La ilaha illa Allah, La ilaha illa Allah, La ilaha illa Allah Muhammadun Rasulullah alayhi salatullah wa salamauhu. Ya al Azali wa'l Abad.

O Attenders, assalamu alaykum. Give a little bit care when you are looking and asking to hear something. Give a little bit more care. Cut yourself from everything that belongs to this world, and come empty. If anyone coming empty, may fill it with heavenly powers. If you are coming full, what may they give to you? Because now we are in contact with heavenly beings. Heavenly beings. And we are asking for heavenly support for all of us, not only for a handful people here. We, all of us are in need and we are asking for that heavenly support to give much more power, a growing heavenly power for ourselves.

This association is granting attenders heavenly support, giving much more power and making them more close to heavenly stations. Yes, and as much as you are giving care, you are taking much more. If you are occupying yourself with something through your life, you are going to be supported as you are asking. If you are giving all of your care, you are taking full support. If occupying yourself then you may take a half support or you may take a tenth support. If never interested, you are taking nothing! Therefore as much as you are giving your care and attention taking more, taking more.

If a person is going to be in a place where there is a heap of gold, heap of silver, and the holder is saying, "Take as much as you like", then as much as you are taking, it is for you. It is free if you can carry a whole heap of gold. It is okay, another is coming. Endlessly. Allah Almighty is giving and granting endlessly. He is saying:

Lahum Ma Yashauna Feeha Waladayna Mazeedun (50:35)

O Salafi ulamas, marhaban! How are you this evening? Something that you are saying, you may understand, I think so. Therefore, O Salafi ulamas or Hadari ulamas say to your attenders, say to your followers, say to your listeners, "O People, give more and take more. Give one and take ten. Give ten and take hundred Give hundred and take thousand, Give 1000 and take 100,000. Give 100,000... 'Anfiq bila la', give million and look for ten million coming to you". Why are you going so 'khasis', mean? Or 'bakhil', miserly? Why? Do you think that when you are giving, getting (less) your Lord's grant to you? No! Give at least one/tenth. Give 10, take 100. Why are you not urging? Why you are not urging people to give? "O People, give more and take muda'fu muda'f, double on double". Why! Why!? If you are Believers, why you are saving through banks? Saving your treasure? Why? For what are you fearing? And people are in need. Needy people are countless. Why not giving? Give! You are not trusting what Allah Almighty saying to you? You are not trusting Allah? At least give one/tenth? Why?

I am shouting at Salafi ulamas. Why are you not saying? Tell the muluk, kings presidents and rich ones. Say, "Don't save your monies through bank vaults". Why you are not urging rich ones? So many, hundreds and thousands of rich ones in Saudi Arabia, in Arab peninsula. In Kuwait, Qatar and Dubai - all people have billions on billions through banks, why!?

Believe in your Lord's declaration. Believe what He is promising to you. He is saying: Wa Ladayna Mazeed. 'Mazeed' never comes down. "Never going to be less My treasures", saying the Lord of Heavens. Why are you saving, saving, saving? Millions, 10 million, 100 million. People can't find something to eat, but you may eat lamb. Delicious food you are eating, why you are not sending? Send!

Once upon a time, and we must say: Audhu Billahi min as shaytan al rajim, Bismillah al Rahman al Rahim. Biggest treasure for us is to say: Bismillah al Rahman al Rahim. Take Bismillah al Rahman al Rahim from your saved money and give Bismillah al Rahman al Rahim. Don't say my treasure is going to be less. Yes, maybe less here, but growing through Akhirat, in heavens. Yes, why are you not saying? That is such an association to make frozen hearts to be 'hayyin layyin', soft and tender. Leave them to give. Give! Don't worry, don't worry! Don't say 'coming less', no. Coming more.

Man zaada li Allah, zadahu Allah. One who gives for Allah more and more, Allah’s grant gets more, and more, and more for him. When you are lying in your coffin, finish. They are even taking your ring. Taking your earring. Taking your very, very expensive watch, taking from your hand. Taking everything from you. They are not saying, "That was a prince, we may leave this on him". No, no, no, no. They are taking everything and you are going naked. Your naked body just is covered with 10 yards of cloth.

O People, before the coming of Judgement Day if you have a chance to give for Allah, give! Give! Give! As coming through heavenly knowledge: anfiq bilalan wa la takhaf man dhi'l arshi ikhlala. You must say Salafi ulamas, to all Wahabi people, all of them have treasures, to write in golden letters, to keep in front of their sitting place, ‘anfiq bilalan wa la takhaf man dhi'l arshi ikhlala’. Don't say to me it is not obligatory. Yes, now it is obligatory.

You must give, you must give! You can't save billions or trillions. It is haram. Al halau bayyinu wa al haramu bayyin. At least, why are you not saying it is bid'a or it is against Shariatallah? Don't say, 'we are giving every year our zakat'. What are you giving from your zakat? From 40 you are giving one. One for Allah? And 39 for yourself, not ashamed? Not ashamed to give for Allah from 40, one, and 39 for yourself? Masha'Allah. What is their mentality? That is their knowledge? Make it the opposite. Give for yourself one, 39 for Allah. Al abd wa ma yamlikuhu li Sayyidihi That is Holy Shariat saying. Abd, the servant, everything that he has, that belongs to him, that he is carrying, is for his Owner or for his Master. Why you are not doing this? You are Salafi ulamas, you don't know this? The 'hukm', that rule, that judgement you are not knowing? You are knowing it.

Say to people, "O People, give from 40, 39 for Allah and one for you. What you are going to say? "Bid'a". Bid'a? Not bid'a but real Shariah, real adab from servant for his Sayyid, for his Sultan. Allah Almighty granting to you, so give! Give! Without fear of any decrease from the Owner of the Throne. That means if you are giving, Allah Almighty not making it less. And Allah Almighty is saying on behalf of Himself:

They will have all they wish for there and We have more for them (50:35)

"When you are giving, getting less My treasures? No! I am the Lord of Heavens, I am the Lord of All-Creation, I am That One Who gives to everyone what they are in need of. More and more. And my treasures are never getting less even than the measure of an atom". Not getting less than an atom. Because, if getting lesser than an atom, they would finish, but what is in Allah Almighty's treasures is never finishing. That is the reason He is saying: And We have more for them (50:35)

Why you are not giving commentary for that Ayat-ul-Karima? About, ‘Wa Ladayna Mazeed’. When the servant asked, 'Hal min mazeed' (is there no more)? Servants are asking when they have been granted from endless treasures of Heavens, of Paradise. They are saying, "Hal min mazeed - No more after this, O our Lord?" And the Lord of Heavens is saying: And We have more for them. Never-ending treasures. It is for Me. No one knows, I only am knowing. I am the Lord of Everything. I am the Creator and I have endless oceans (of everything) that are never-ending.

Wa Ma Indallahi Baaqin (16:59)

What Allah Almighty has is forever. From pre-Eternal up to Eternal. Never-ending, never-ending. And the servant is asking, "O our Lord, is there no more?" The 'awsaf' description, more than description. Dunya and Akhira. What is on power through this life is not going to be in act in Akhirah, in Eternal Life. This is a temporary life, may be billion years may be trillion years, may be more and more millions, but for all of them is written a limit. But for Allah, ‘And there is more for you’. Endless.

You are knowing nothing! You are knowing according to your capacity, ability and your understanding. O People, don't lose your chance Give more for Allah. Keep for yourself a little bit, but all of it, say "O my Lord everything You granted to me from Your grants, I give everything for You. Who am I to say I am giving?" Allah Almighty saying, Hal ata'una "That is Our grant to you. What you have is from My grant to you. O People, if My grant is never-ending, never-ending, never-ending”. Allahu Akbar wa Lillahi'l hamd. Subhanallah, Subhanallah, Subhanallah.

Stinginess. The worst characteristic of our egos is to be greedy. Greedy, greedy. For what are you greedy? If your Creator is saying, is'al tuta, "Ask! I am giving". "Ask as much as you can ask and I am giving it to you". Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar.

Waidha Sa'alaka Ibadi 'Anni Fa'inni Qaribun Ujeebu Da'wata Alda'i Idha Da'ani Falyastajibu Li Walyu'minu Bi La'allahum Yarshuduna (2:186)

A person never gets the honour to be Rashid. Rashid is highest of high levels for Man. Highest level.

Alaysa Minkum Rajalun Rashidun (11:78)

Al Rushd is highest level of our good characteristics. Rashid is he, who understands something of what Allah Almighty is saying. [Prophet], if My servants ask you about Me, I am near. (2:186) You are Arabs. You know. “If asking, I am hearing”. Falyastajibu Li “O My servants, if you are asking something from Me, I am asking also from you that you must run (to do) what I am ordering you, to do.” So let them respond to Me, and believe in Me, so that they may be guided. (2:186)

Iman is to believe in Allah Almighty and our belief level, lowest level. So that they may be rightly guided; rightly guided means, to reach the level of Rashid. Allah Almighty is asking servants to reach the level of Rashid. As the Holy Quran is declaring, ‘Is there not a single right-minded man among you’? Rashid is the highest level that a person may reach. That is name also, the highest level, Rushd. Allah... We are such useless ones. And I am only asking from Allah Almighty that, at least we may reach someone who is on the level of right-guidance, Rushd. Rushd means every side of that one is one hundred percent, in ‘kamal’, in perfection.

Is there not a single right-minded man among you? You are understanding such things? I am asking Salafi ulamas. Have you found someone as Allah Almighty asks people, all people, in the Holy Quran also, saying, Alaysa Minkum Rajulun Rashidun (11:78) It is not only that one who was in Pharaoh's presence, but he was a believer in Deen al Hanif, Real Belief. But he was hiding and was saying, "O People, is there not a right-minded man among you?" (Is there) no one whose mind can understand what the Lord of Heavens is sending? That Station of Right-Guidance is the highest level that servants may reach.

Rushd, you must know what it means. If you are not knowing, look in the dictionary. If you are not finding it, look at the Tafasir, commentaries of Holy Quran. Don't say this is bid'a, this is shirk, this is kufr. No. All ulama, of whom there are hundreds and thousands bringing pearls and diamonds from Holy Quran and its endless treasures. Why are you saying it is not a real diamond, it is plastic? No, you can't say. If you are claiming this we may ask you: Qul, Hatoo Burhanakum, bring your proof that makes my heart to be in rest, in satisfaction. If not, you will be responsible in Divine Presence. And Divine Presence never accepts you to be in Divine Presence. Sending you to angels.

May Allah forgive us. O People, come and hear, come and listen. There are endless oceans through Holy Quran. That which is always giving people a refreshment and giving a 'young-power' to them for understanding, to be in pleasure and to be pleased with such hidden treasures from Holy Quran. May Allah forgive me and forgive all listeners, and forgive those Salafi ulamas also. And give us hidden treasures of holy verses. Endless hidden treasures through holy verses.

O People, take your adab with Holy Quran and take your adab with such people who are bringing such strange meanings full of power and lights. Accept it for darkness to be taken away from your faces and given to you Heavenly Lights.

O Allah forgive me, forgive Salafi ulamas, forgive Ummat Muhammadi (sas).

Ya Sayyidi, Ya Rasulullah, ishfa'lana.
