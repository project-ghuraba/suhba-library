As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh. A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Yā-ayyuhā n-nāsu innā khalaqnākum min dhakarin wa-unthá wa-ja‘alnākum shu‘ūban wa-qabā’ila li-ta‘ārafū, inna akramakum ‘inda Llāhi atqākum" (49:13).

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says in Qur’ān: "We created you as different races and different nations." Your colours are different. Your languages are different. Your looks are different. Those differences are not important. In the presence of AllāhNone, none of these is superior to the others. When it comes to comparing people, everyone is the same in the presence of AllāhNone. As Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is not in need of anything, there is nothing like, "I should make this one higher," or "I should make this one more valuable," in the presence of AllāhNone. Who is precious in the presence of AllāhNone? Pious ones. People who fear AllāhNone and obey AllāhNone. That is what is acceptable. They can be white or black. They can be whatever they are, Arabs or Persian. People who do good service to AllāhNone are the accepted and valued people in the presence of AllāhNone. Certainly, among mankind, there are various kinds of people. They have different looks. But as to what they do...

There are some people who have the characteristics of animals. What is the quality of animals? Some of them are dangerous. Some are slow and others are not. The characteristics of people are like this. Sayyidunā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh ad-Dāghistānī one day told Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim, "Let us go for a ride, you and I together." His house was in the mountains, on Mount Qāsiyūn. Damascus is a plain. And there is a mountain behind it. Our dergah, our house, the dergah of Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim and Sayyidunā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh was there. His house was also close to there. When it was necessary, they came down off the mountain. That day, the wisdom of coming down was to show him something. He said, "I will take you for a ride around Damascus." They went to Damascus. When they got down, he said, "Shaykh Nāẓim, look at them, all of those people in the crowd. Look now." He looked. There were no people. Each of them was a different animal. Some were wolves, some were dogs, some were donkeys, some were snakes, some were scorpions, some were other things. He asked, "How did this happen? Where did the people go?" He said, "These are the people. I showed you their characters. If their characters are not trained, they are like animals." The character of people's egos is like that of animals. Until they reach their potential as humans, the ego must be trained. People must correct themselves so that they have human characteristics. A human characteristic is the perfect characteristic in the presence of AllāhNone. It is the best. They have high ranks. "Wa-la-qad karramnā banī Ādam" (17:70). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘aẓīm. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is saying, "We have raised mankind to the highest rank." But if they give themselves the characteristics of animals, they lose their humanity then. They belittle themselves. They put themselves down. People lose that honour then.

Therefore, as Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla said in Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n, the most virtuous among you is not the richest, the most beautiful, or the most powerful, but the most pious one who obeys Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. He is the valuable one. He is the true human. May AllāhNone increase all of us to that rank, in shā’a Llāh, together with our shaykhs. Because whoever is with them must be a human, since they train him and raise him along with them, with permission of AllāhNone.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying in Qur’ān: A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Yā-ayyuhā n-nāsu innā... ja‘alnākum shu‘ūban wa-qabā’ila li-ta‘ārafū, inna akramakum ‘inda Llāhi atqākum" (49:13).

Saying in Qur’ān: "We create you from man and woman, from Sayyidunā Ādam and Sayyidatunā Ḥawwā’." All human being they are coming from two people – from man and woman. AllāhNone create them not like what these people they are saying, they saying... they are not believer, they are believer or not believer. AllāhNone said we are coming from two person, man and woman, Ādam and Ḥawwā’ ‘alayhumā s-salām. And from this they make... people, different people, different nations, different colour, different tongue, different speech – thousands of kind of people. Subḥāna Llāh, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, for each climate or for each country, they have giving different shape, different language, understanding.

But it is... This not important in front of AllāhNone. Which is more precious for AllāhNone between this people? The strong one or the beautiful one or the most rich one? This is not important at all in Divine Presence of AllāhNone. AllāhNone not in need to anything from what human being they are like or they in need. What HeNone is saying to not make, from his justice... not saying this it will be, when he white it will be more precious, from black one, or from yellow one, or from other this thing. No, AllāhNone only said, precious one who is more close to AllāhNone, more obey to AllāhNone, more afraid from AllāhNone, and keeping HisNone teaching, teaching of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, order of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. This one more precious.

We are hearing many time about story from ṣaḥābah, or from other people, which they was poor or not beautiful. They are, they was precious in divine presence of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. One of them... one ṣaḥābīy, he was very ugly and very poor. Nothing at all. He was, nobody – he was crying, nobody want to marry him. Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam was happy with him, and asking why crying. He said, "Nobody want..." ProphetNone said, "Okay, you come with me," and [heNone] asked one of ṣaḥābah, "Anyone want to take... marry this for his daughter?" Nobody want him. One ṣaḥābah, tell him, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, "I want to marry... I want to your daughter to marry [him]." And ṣaḥābah thought, for Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam he said, "Okay," heNone said, "INone will marry... INone want to marry her to this ṣaḥābah." "You go, and ask her." And this ṣaḥābah he... from order of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam not saying anything, but he was not so happy. And he went to his house. Mother of daughter, she was, "No, no, we don’t want. He is ugly, and he don’t – he is poor, nothing with him. What we will do with this? No need for him." That time, this daughter she was there, ya‘nī, was hearing what happening, said, "If order of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, I will marry him." So, she was marry him.

And after there was – I think in one of ghazwat, I don’t know Badr or Uḥud, he was fighting this. And he asking for shahādah. And after they were finished, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam asking about him, because heNone know he become shahīd. Where he is? HeNone was in – looking, looking, they find him – [he] killed six of mushrik until they kill him. They was there. And Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, "He is now, he is with ḥūru l-‘ayn." And his wife, she is also from highest station.

This is... No, AllāhNone not looking for ugly. Only looking for what, how he is obeying or not obeying, he believer or not believer. The believer it is more valuable. Other, not, until they become believer. This is the shape of this people, showing what they are. Because people, as they are colour, they have colour, they have also shape of, or inside how acting, like animal. Like animal or any other creature. Not only from outside. From inside, also.

Because once, one time with Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh ad-Dāghistānī and Mawlānā Shaykh, they was, you know – many of you, you don’t know – where we was in Damascus. Where is dergah, where is house of Mawlānā Shaykh Nāzim or Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh. Damascus it is a... field, but behind, where dergah and house of Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh, Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh's dergah in Qāsiyūn, mountain of Qāsiyūn. And there was – sometimes going to Damascus downtown to see what buying, or sometimes just going for ziyārah (visit). And this day – Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh tell him, to Mawlānā, "Come, we will go for looking, for making ziyārah and looking around." And he went with him walking. It was many crowd, Damascus, this bazār or other place was. "Look," tell, Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh, tell Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim, "What you see?" "I see crowd, people, going around." He said to him, "Look again," and he was looking again. This time, he didn’t see any human being. Just seeing, in shape, inside clothes, animal: dogs or fox or snake or donkey or other, rat or... many thing, but no human being. He said, "Where they went?" He said, "No, they are same, but this is the shape of their ego."

Because shape of a human being, AllāhNone create human being and honoured him. The most honoured one, human being. Animal not honoured like... they are not honoured – just, for serving for human being. And when they are living, human being must be obey for AllāhNone, for Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, they will be good. But when they are not obeying, they become like animal or worse than animal. This honour what AllāhNone give, it will be go away from them. Only honoured one, human being. For this Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying in Qur’ān: Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥim. ‘‘Wa-la-qad karramnā banī Ādam" (17:70). We honoured human being. Other, they are not honoured. Only for some of them for service, some of them for other kind, AllāhNone create them, there is wisdom we don’t know.

For this, the important thing, to be human being. To human being and obey for AllāhNone, to be valuable in divine presence of AllāhNone. Nothing else. AllāhNone help us to be like this, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
