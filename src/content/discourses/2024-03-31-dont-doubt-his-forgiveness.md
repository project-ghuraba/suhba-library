As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Inna Llāha yaghfiru dh-dhunūba jamī‘an innahū huwa l-ghafūru r-raḥīm" (39:53). Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is the one who forgives all sins. Whatever sins you commit, as long as you repent, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla will change them to reward. Your sins will be erased, and reward will be written instead. This generosity of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is to save people. In order to save them, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla created various means. There are many people who do evil throughout their lives, or who never do any goodness. If they repent before they die, they will profit. The doors of repentance are open. The door of repentance only closes before qiyāmah. When it is closed, repentance is no longer accepted.

There are some people with waswasah (devil's whispering), doubtful people who think, "Will Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla ever forgive our sins?" What are you, O man or woman? Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is saying "INone am forgiving" at all times and at every chance in Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n and in the ḥadīth sharīf of our Holy ProphetNone. HeNone gives good tidings by saying, "Repent, and INone will forgive all your sins." Yet, some people question, "Will HeNone forgive me?" Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla forgives. You should not have a doubt. There cannot be a doubt. The word of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is true. You must hear what HeNone says.

If you obey your ego and say, "Will HeNone forgive me or not?" you will have heard and listened to your ego. You will have not accepted the word of AllāhNone. That is the bigger danger. Therefore, don't ever say, "Will HeNone...?" in anything you do. Certainly, AllāhNone will forgive us. You must know that HeNone accepts all goodness we do. You must believe in it. Who believes in it will be saved. Otherwise, if people think they will be saved with their actions, they will not be saved but mistaken. May AllāhNone protect them.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, saying in Qur’ān, "Inna Llāha yaghfiru dh-dhunūba jamī‘a" (39:53) – "AllāhNone forgive every sins you... you do." AllāhNone forgive this. No doubt. Because HeNone is the most merciful. HeNone give us chance to save ourself, to save from bad end and to, from HisNone generous, to change what you do from sin. If you ask for forgiveness, HeNone forgive you and HeNone put... again... not... for reward instead of this sin what you done. HeNone forgive sin and put reward in your, your book. This is what we are, we must believe. Because people, sometimes they are coming asking, "If AllāhNone, He can forgive me of I am doing, praying like this, doing this? HeNone accept from me?" Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying, "INone am the most forgiving." So when you said, you put doubt on yourself. It is, you put doubt on Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, what HeNone said. So you following your ego. Ego even here they make trick for you to not accept what Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying, "INone am forgiving", and you saying "HeNone, I have, maybe HeNone cannot, HeNone not..." Cannot say "cannot". "HeNone not forgive me." AllāhNone, HeNone said, "INone forgive everything." So don't be doubt what you been doing – worshipping, doing anything from order of Islām.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying, "Make it, INone make it easy for everyone, no difficult for this." Also this, don't make it difficult for yourself. When you make it difficult for yourself you come again... against Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. We are saying, "Yassirū wa-lā tu‘assirū" – "Make it easy, don't make it difficult." And when you make it difficult, you, that time you are harming yourself. You make yourself to be punished. So don't make it difficult.

Don't think, "AllāhNone, HisNone oppression," or "HeNone not forgive." This is bad thought for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. For this, everybody must be thankful for AllāhNone, for this good tidings HeNone gave us. AllāhNone forgive us all of us, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
