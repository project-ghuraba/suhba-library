Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Our Holy Prophet (SAW) state:

“Allahumma arinal haqqa haqqan warzuqnat tiba’ahu, wa’arinal batila batilan warzuqnaj tinabah.” “O Allah! Show us the truth as truth and grant us to follow it. And show us the false as false and make us succeed in avoiding it.”

Our Holy Prophet (SAW) says may Allah show us true things as truth. Because people talk about bad things as truth, as good, and they do whatever their egos want and continue. In other words, most of the time these people commit sins as if normal life was like this and their conscience is not bothered. Therefore, our Holy Prophet (SAW) says, “May Allah show us the bad as bad, let us hate it, and let us stay away from it.”

This is a beautiful dua of our Holy Prophet (SAW). It is both a dua and an advice for people. Do not see the bad as good. Let us see the good as good. The opposite occurs now: people see the good as bad and they see the bad as good. May Allah (JJ) help us to see. May we see what is bad and what is good. May Allah keep us away from the bad. Let us lean towards the good, let us love the good, let us do good works, and let us do good things.

Those who do this, people who are successful in this, become Allah’s beloved servants and also become someone people love. People who do an evil, even if they do not know they are doing evil, are known by others as doing evil. When they do evil towards people, people hate them and do not like them. It is not a good thing for a person not to be liked. But Shaitan does not make them feel it.

People do something bad without being intuitive of why they are doing it. May Allah protect most people from this and may He Inshallah protect us too. May we see the good as good. May we also see the bad. May we stay away from the bad and do the good Inshallah.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq. Al-Fatiha.
