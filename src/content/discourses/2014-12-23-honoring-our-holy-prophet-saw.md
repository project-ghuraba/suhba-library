Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Today, thank Allah, we have entered the month of Rabi’ul Awwal. Rabiul Awwal is the month our Holy Prophet (SAW) was born. It is one of the most beautiful nights. Hadrat Sulayman Chalabi says of the 12th night that it is equivalent to Laylatul Qadr. It is truely so. Allah created the universe for our Holy Prophet (SAW). He loved him, made him His beloved and habib. Allah loves those who love him. Allah respects those who respect him. Allah gives everything beautiful for his sake. It is Allah’s promise and Allah’s word.

They can keep worshipping and keep trying as much as they want as long as there is no respect for our Holy Prophet (SAW). Meaning it is not one in a million as virtuous. Certainly, respecting and honoring our Holy Prophet (SAW) is a necessity. In fact it is obligatory. It is the order of Allah. It is mentioned in so many places in the Koran: “Honor the Prophet (SAW),” and, “Make salawat on the Prophet (SAW).” Allah (JJ) says, “Angels make salat and salam, so you do it too.” Can there be a greater order than this anymore? It is a direct order. Just as there is an order to pray, there is an order to honor our Holy Prophet (SAW).

Thank Allah our nation does not commit that disrespect. Most Muslims do not. But new groups have come out that confuse people by saying this is alright and that is not. The most important order is to honor the Prophet (SAW), to love the Prophet (SAW). It is a great disrespect and bad manner to say about our Master (SAW): “He died and left. He did his duty and left.” He did his duty till now and he is doing it. Our Holy Prophet (SAW) says, “I am ready at once for those who make salat and salam on me.” That is why it is superior to make salat and salam during this month of Rabiul Awwal. We need to do more. Do as much as you can. May Mawlids be done Inshallah. Do good deeds to the poor and needy for the sake of our Prophet (SAW). Let them be happy and ask, “Why was this done to me?” This is the month of our Holy Prophet (SAW). When giving anything, say you are giving it because it is the Mawlid of our Holy Prophet (SAW) and let them know.

The foreign giaour made a fake birthday that is not upto much for Isa Alayhis Salam. It is not even his birthday. He was born on another date, but because it was winter time, there was no work to be done, they made it a pastime during this season. They say it is the birthday of Isa Alayhis Salam and prepare for it months ahead of time. In their cities, it says 90 days are left. Subtracting every day 89, 88 until the day they call his birthday arrives.

We need to remind and introduce the month of Rabiul Awwal to everybody. What is meant by Rabiul Awwal is spring in Arabic. Afterall, our Holy Prophet (SAW) was born in spring, on 21 April. However, our Islamic festivals are not done according to the Gregorian calendar but according to the Islamic calendar. Essentially now, we need to show more importance. Now we need to warn and awaken people more. It has become a fashion to celebrate the Sacred Birth Week in April. We are not opposing it because it is done for our Holy Prophet (SAW), but the real blessed and good deed is the month of Rabiul Awwal. It is better and more blessed to do more good deeds, to recite mawlids, and make salawat. May Allah (JJ) engrave the love of our Holy Prophet (SAW) on our hearts. May we do all our thinking according to him Inshallah. May his gaze be upon us. May his blessings be upon us. Becasue every goodness is with our Holy Prophet (SAW). May Allah be pleased with you all.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq.

Al-Fatiha.
