Sheikh Muhammad Nazim Al-Haqqani An-Naqshibandi, Sohbat from 1992.

Al-hamdu liLlahi Rabbi l-'alamin wa s-salatu wa s-salamun 'ala Sayyidina Muhammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalin wa l-Akhirin wa 'ala alihi wa sahbihi ajma'in.

We will keep it short since you have little time. You are students. Perhaps you are busy with your lessons. Is there a monthly gathering here?

G: Once in every month.

SN: Every month?

G: Yes.

SN: May Allah be pleased, there are blessings in heavenly gatherings. In these gatherings Muslims come closer to each other. This gathering is most probably for Allah. Young honourable Muslim students are here who are studying in all departments and preparing themselves to carry the responsibilities of the future. So you are like this! My wish from Janabul Haqq is that He may keep you steadfast on this way! May you keep on this way in which you believe until the end. The world is changing; many changes are coming but you shouldn't change your personality/identity. You will not change your attributes. You will preserve your faith most carefully. This is the aim.

This is a small gathering here. To expand this gathering to reach all students who are studying must be the chosen target because a generation whose characteristic qualities are not perfect will not be able to carry the load of the future. This quality of character can only be perfected by strong faith. Character/Human nature is supported by a strong faith. A strong faith depends on the faith we have in Allah and His Prophet and also on the firmness and determination with which we perform our prayers/ ways of worship. By supporting our characters with faith and prayer we can carry the responsibility of the future.

Because a new year, every new year, is coming more heavy compared to the previous one. This new year has brought more heavy conditions than last year. Even though it is the Gregorian calendar's new year. And the conditions compared to the conditions of last year - the conditions of this year 1992 are heavier. Don't think that '93 will be easier! The way we are going, our direction, is not a safe path, so the year '93 will not bring easier conditions. Not until we change our direction and when sanity determines our direction then conditions will begin to be relieved. Otherwise Janabul Haqq will make it more difficult until we return back (to the right path)!

Asta'idhu biLlah "Wa lanudhiqannahum mina l-adhabi l-adna duna l-adhabi l-akbari la'allahum yarji'una" (32:21) "And we will surely let them taste the nearer punishment short of the greater punishment that perhaps they will repent." Maybe they will come to their senses, sanity will prevail and they will turn back. (from their wrong doings). And they will realize that everything is going wrong. What is wrong? "Surely this way we are on is fruitless." Let's change direction, there is something wrong. "la'allahum yarji'una" (32:21) It is hoped that they return back. Till we are made to return back (from wrong doing) we will be whipped. And we need clever/awake Muslims - they say conscious - conscious Muslims. It means one who bases his faith on knowledge, one who rejects imitation. Such faithful believers are required.

www.saltanat.org Page 1 I hope that your faith is founded on knowledge; that it is not just an empty belief inherited from your family. Because you are studying knowledge and every aspect of knowledge points to a clear reality. Every student of knowledge is guided to evident reality. Every branch of knowledge leads to Allah. Pay attention! Whichever knowledge is concerned it certainly enables us to reach Allah.

Although you are studying in an atheist system, despite this you will destroy atheism. You should destroy it! Atheism is a problem for this nation. It is atheism which brings calamities to this state and nation. You, young Muslim students, you will destroy atheism, leaving no trace of it. Wherever they run you will follow them; "Come here! Why do you say such words about Allah, about Prophets, about Holy Books, about our holy things? Why do you say, how dare you claim just the opposite? How dare you defend atheism? How do you suggest materialistic opinions? Are you using your mind?" There is no man who uses his mind and becomes a materialist! A materialist man has no mind, he is in the class of animals. That is why this is my hope for these gentlemen, they are our young Muslim people coming from Anatolia. They are surely the ones who will support their faith with knowledge. Each one of them will be a fortress of Islam. Watch over them, look after them.

And mashaAllah as I can see from here you are all enlightened young students (with nur). My hope for you is that you will be patient and determined on the way of knowledge to destroy disbelief. Remove atheism by the way of knowledge. Our jihad now is not with a sword, no! Jihad with a sword is when Mahdi alaihi s-salam will come. Jihad can't exist without a Sultan! Otherwise it is useless. Neither you nor I can be the Sultan! He is Sultan to order for jihad. Therefore when they call for jihad now it is only fitna/mischief. Don't look at them! You look at whether your jihad now destroys atheism or not. Can you destroy the negative movements that came with atheism or not? This is our jihad. To destroy those ignorant ones by the way of knowledge. You must do this! Because, what is the aim of every knowledge? It is to bring you to Allah Dhul Jalal Who is eternal clear truth. If it doesn't bring you there then it is not real knowledge.

Therefore, the service given to you inshaAllahu r-Rahman is proper. It is a donation on Allah's way. And this donation inshaAllah can be increased and expanded by our wealthy Muslim brothers. And let atheists lose their hope in this country. You are Muslim students. There used to be Madrasas in the old times. The scholars studying in madrasas, were mostly practicing zuhd/asceticism in their lives. They were content with little things -food enough to keep them alive. Because a full stomach decreases the mind. It gives darkness to the heart. Nevertheless hunger must not be to a degree that ruins your bodies. Little eating makes heart and soul milder. This is why one who is fasting, his heart and his head work better. This is the virtue of fasting.

A full stomach lessens intelligence. And most of our elder scholars used to be content to dip bread into vinegar. They carried on with kifaf an-nafs (to go with the little). This is our Prophet's, alaihi s-salatu wa s-salam, eminent sunnah. He went somewhere and they served him dry bread and vinegar. "Ni'mal idham al-khal" he said. "What a beautiful food is vinegar" he said and dipped dry bread into vinegar. Therefore it is an eminent sunnah. In the Fatih Madrasas, where they trained the most famous scholars of Islamic World, the students of knowledge, although they had more opportunities, didn't use them but they lived their lives as ascetics and the most famous scholars of the world were raised like this, to enlighten the Islamic world.

Whatever is given to you, no matter if it is little, there is baraka in it. Janabul Haqq will increase it. You don't have needless expenses. In fact who finds abundant circumstances can't be trained well. Be content and give thanks to Janabul Haqq for the conditions you have. Pay attention to your lessons as much as possible. Don't be deceived by people around you who maybe have more pocket money for a day than you have for a month. Don't try to be like them! Be content and pay attention to your lessons! And Janabul Mawla will endow you with the enlightenment of real knowledge. You must learn knowledge in order to remove ignorance. www.saltanat.org Page 2 This country belongs to Muslims. Don't forget this! And be careful - non-Muslims also have claims of ownership on this country. Either you or them. The conclusion will reach that point. Either Muslims will own/rule this country or it will be in the hands of non-Muslims till yawmu l-qiyamat. People will be without value, condemned. However this country has been conquered by the sword of your Muslim ancestors and these ancestors will not give you their blessings if ever you leave this land to the enemies of Islam! This must be your target. This country belongs to Muslims. It will always belong to Muslims. Strive for this! Wherever you are you must defend this cause(your rights). Be defenders for this cause! It is a made-up word - 'savunucu'. A defender or a shooter?

G: Defender.

All right then, Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim.
