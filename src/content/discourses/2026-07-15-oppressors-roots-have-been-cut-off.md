As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
فَقُطِعَ دَابِرُ الْقَوْمِ الَّذِينَ ظَلَمُوا ۚ وَالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ
<br>“Fa-quṭiʿa dābiru l-qawmi llaḏīna ẓalamū wa l-ḥamdu lillāhi Rabbi l-ʿālamīn”<br>
“So the roots of the people who did wrong were cut off. And all praise and thanks be to Allah, the Lord of the worlds.” (Qurʼān 6:45). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

Shukr to Allah, for Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla has repelled the tyrannical people. Thanks and praise be to Him. Thousands of thanks and praise be to Allah.

The disbeliever is always attacking the Muslims; the greatest enemy is shayṭān. Their enmity is constant along with shayṭān’s, but Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla is victorious. He cut off their roots, says Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. They have no power whatsoever. Let them strive as much as they want, may Allah protect us from their evil.

Of course, shayṭān considers all kinds of deceit and evil his right. He doesn’t care about others; as long as his own kingdom is established. So, everything is permissible for this purpose.

Therefore, may Allah protect us from their evil. A person on this way needs to have a strong door, a strong īmān and belief so that Allah may protect him from the evil of shayṭān and the wicked.

Shukr to Allah, many years ago, a drastic event befell our entire nation. Shukr to Allah, Allah prevailed. Allah is always victorious.

لا غالب إلا الله
<br>“Lā ghāliba illā Allāh”<br>
“There is no Victor but Allah.”

Those who oppose Him will eventually receive their punishment. Many people have been deceived by them to this day. May Allah grant them wisdom and understanding; may they repent and not engage in evil deeds. Because when you engage in evil deeds, you may be able to hide and conceal yourself in this world, but in the Hereafter, you will truly see how evil those deeds were and how much harm they caused.

Until this day, many people have deceived others saying, “It was like this, it was like that.” When everything is so clearly evident, why are you still being stubborn? Stubbornness is the greatest characteristic of disbelief. The disbeliever is stubborn and obstinate.

Even Abu Jahl, while dying and the Companions were about to cut off his head, said, “I know the truth, but because of my pride and stubbornness, I will not follow you and Muḥammad.” This stubbornness is a characteristic of disbelief.

Therefore, come to the right way, turn to Allah, seek Him, and repent. Thus, Allah will forgive your sins, you will save your hereafter, and you will be saved from being with shayṭān. Because the way of Allah is clear; there is no secret or hidden aspect to this way. You should be with the truth; there is no need for anything beyond that. May Allah protect us, but if you spread this evil to others, their sins will also be written upon you.

May we always be among those who are with the truth, inshā’Allāh. May Allah protect us from evil; from the evil of shayṭān, from the evil of our egos, and from the evil of the disbelievers. Our ancestors said of the disbelievers, “Water sleeps, but the enemy does not.” Therefore, may Allah protect and preserve us.

May He send the sāhib so that the truth may be fully revealed, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
