Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

In front of the greatness of Allah Azza wa Jalla, human beings are insignificant, helpless and have no attributes. But because of the mercy of Allah Azza wa Jalla, He valued human beings. Those who know that value become valuable. Those who do not know have no value. BismiLlahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim: “ ر َِبلْا يِف مْ هُ اَنلْمَ حَ وَ مَ دَ آ يِنَب اَنمْ رَّ كَ دْ َقَلوَ رِحْ َبلْاوَ ”, “And We have certainly honored the children of Adam and carried them on the land and sea.” (Qur’ān 17:70). “We valued human beings; We helped them at sea, on land and in the air.” Allah says. We have provided them with all kinds of things so that they can go wherever they go, so that they can use whatever they want, and come and go with the permission of Allah.

Of course, without the permission of Allah, people who brag and say: “We are this, we are that” are worth nothing. Without Allah’s permission, nothing moves, nothing happens. Neither the engine works, nor the machine works, nor the slightest thing works. Everything is by Allah’s permission, everything happens by the will of Allah Jalla Jalāluhu. Whether it is electricity, atoms, gasoline or diesel, none of them would work without Allah’s permission.

Allah has given so many blessings, and yet human beings think that they are something and go against Allah. We should be grateful for the blessings of Allah Azza wa Jalla so that we become valuable. If you are valuable in the sight of Allah, then you are valuable. If you are not valuable in His sight, you are worthless; you are useless even when you die. When animals die, they are eaten or used. But the most stinking thing is the dead human.

Therefore, in order to be valuable, we should be as Allah wants. Now people are doing a hundred somersaults for others to give them value; they keep wondering what to do. You should do it for Allah. We have to obey Allah and do what Allah says insha’Allah. May we be amongst those whom Allah gives value to insha’Allah. Because a person can have the whole world in his hands, but it is of no use as long as he has no value in the sight of Allah. May Allah give us all value and not separate us from the right way insha’Allah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
