As-salāmu ‘alaykum wa raḥmatu Llāhi wa barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

People, everybody is interested in becoming awliyā’. They struggle to become awliyā’. Our ProphetNone says for awliyā’, "Man kāna fī thalātha hisāl kāna Waliyu Llāhi ḥaqqan aṣ-ṣalātu wa ṣ-ṣiyām wa l-ghusl min al-janābah. Wa man ijtanabahunna kāna ‘aduwwu Llāhi ḥaqqan." Awliyā’u Llāh means a beloved servant of AllāhNone. Our ProphetNone is naming three things for you to do to be a beloved servant of AllāhNone, awliyā’u Llāh. You should pray, fast and make full ablution (ghusl). You should always be clean, as no worship is accepted unless you are clean. The one who keeps these three things is beloved by AllāhNone. A walī is the beloved servant of AllāhNone.

People now want to become awliyā’ in order to show miracles. And when it is so, a person's intention is not for AllāhNone but for some benefit, for some personal interest, to have miracles. And that is impossible. It doesn't work. If you do it for your interest and not for AllāhNone, AllāhNone won't give you miracles. You'll be just someone who sees the surface. Therefore, in order to be from the beloved servants of AllāhNone, keep your praying and fasting, pay attention to your cleanliness and shower. Who doesn't do that, our ProphetNone says he becomes Allāh'sNone enemy. They are disliked servants of AllāhNone.

There are people AllāhNone loves and people He doesn't love. To be from the beloved people of AllāhNone is a big favour. To be HisNone enemy is the worst thing for a person. This life of a person is just one part. It is not long. We should be careful to be from the beloved servants of AllāhNone. It is not actually hard. To be a beloved servant of AllāhNone, do just three things: pray, fast and keep your shower. Don't worry about the rest. If AllāhNone opens it, HeNone will open it for you. If HeNone gives you miracles, fine. If not, our purpose is not miracles. Our purpose is to be from the beloved servants and beloved people of AllāhNone.

May AllāhNone make all of us from the beloved servants.

ProphetNone saying most of people they like to be waliyu Llāh, awliyā’, in English they say, to be saint. Saint what mean in Islām? Walī - who one who AllāhNone love him. Who AllāhNone love him, he is waliyu Llāh, beloved for AllāhNone meaning. But people they are mixing [up]. They want to be saint or to be awliyā’u Llāh. For what? For make miracle, to be everybody looking for them, asking from them miracle. When your intention like this from beginning you are in wrong, you will never have any miracle. Even maybe AllāhNone not love you because you are not sincere for your love for AllāhNone.

And ProphetNone said: to be beloved for AllāhNone, there is three things - who do it, he will be beloved for AllāhNone. First to ṣalāh, praying five times. Must praying and fasting and to clean yourself, to make shower. From to be clean, you must take shower. If you don't take this you will not [be] acceptable for your any worshipping. If you are dirty - meaning what junub? - impure, you must make shower. This is three thing, if you done you are one who AllāhNone love you. It is the most important for us to be loved from Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla. And who not done this, he is enemy for AllāhNone. AllāhNone choose him as His enemy.

There is two kind of creature or human being. One of them with AllāhNone, AllāhNone love them. And [other] they are against AllāhNone, they are enemy of AllāhNone and HeNone not love them. HeNone curse them and HeNone is not happy with them. And to do, to be beloved for AllāhNone is not difficult - only praying and fasting and wash yourself to clean yourself. But people they are not listening, not caring for this. The most important thing in our life, nothing more important from AllāhNone happiness for us, and to be loved from AllāhNone. It is the most important thing and easy to do it. And to be enemy for AllāhNone it is the most horrible thing, the most worst thing can anybody do. Because if you have enemy like you, you can beat him or your follower, your friend they can beat him but for AllāhNone whole universe they cannot do anything.

So who are have little bit good thinking, little bit mind, he mustn't be enemy for AllāhNone. You must be friend with HimNone. AllāhNone make us from His friends and from beloved servants for HimNone in shā’a Llāh. And be for ProphetNone also, in shā’a Llāh, beloved for them.

Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq al-Fātiḥah.
