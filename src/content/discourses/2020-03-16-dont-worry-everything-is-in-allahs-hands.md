As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Mawlānā used to sing one qaṣīdah a lot of the time. Probably a majdhūb would sing it. "Lā tukthir li-hammik, mā quddir yakūn, fa-Llāhu l-Muqaddir wa-l-‘ālam shu’ūn." Don't worry too much, AllāhNone wrote everything down. What has been destined will happen. AllāhNone is the one who does it. People and creatures belong to HimNone. They are HisNone work. HeNone does whatever HeNone wants. HeNone makes someone sick and someone healthy as HeNone likes. HeNone raises someone and lowers another one. Therefore, don't be upset on account of this dunyā. Turn to AllāhNone. Be with AllāhNone. That is the most important thing. The rest is not important. It is miscellaneous stuff.

So Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla causes something to come up even though people claim to rule over everything. HeNone shows their weakness. HeNone shows that they are weak. Everything is in Allāh'sNone hands. Therefore, who is with AllāhNone, how lucky he is. He can be Turkish or Arab, he can be black or white. How lucky is the one who is with AllāhNone. It is not for one race or heritage, happiness is to be with AllāhNone. We live here in this world as long as we are meant to. Age, illness or something else becomes the means. We don't live forever. One can live 100, 120, at most 140 years. He can't live longer. Therefore, a person who is with AllāhNone is at peace. Who submits himself to AllāhNone is at peace.

May AllāhNone give all of us that īmān. When he has īmān, a man doesn't worry about anything. When īmān weakens, of course a man gets confused about what to do. Important is to be with AllāhNone as we said. We should be friends with AllāhNone in order for our end to be good. May we not pass in sadness but in happiness, in shā’a Llāh.

Mawlānā was saying every time one qaṣīdah in Arabic. Mā shā’a Llāh he is with, making with voice, nice voice, but I don't have this voice, so just said: "Lā tukthir li-hammik, mā quddir yakūn, fa-Llāhu l-Muqaddir wa-l-‘ālam shu’ūn." And there is little bit more but what I was important this, it is this part: Don't worry, don't be thinking too much. Don't be sad, AllāhNone He is Creator and HeNone is making everything. AllāhNone He is... What will be, it will be. AllāhNone make it, it must be. And HeNone, everything in HisNone hand. HeNone is doing what HeNone want.

This all world, all universe, is from HisNone, what doing, from His CreatorNone. Everything is CreatorNone. HeNone is making some people higher, some people stronger, some people... Sometimes people they're saying, "We are controlling everything." Sending one thing – people they are become crazy, they don't know what they do. This is from HisNone Creator, and HeNone is doing everything. We cannot say, "What is this? What is that?" But, most important thing – to be friend with AllāhNone, to be happy with HimNone. If you are happy, everything done, at the end you will be happy. If you are not with HimNone, you are be in life also miserable, in end is bad also.

Most important here, to be strong īmān. This is very important. If you have a strong īmān nothing at all can make you sad, make you worry, make you not happy. All this with weak īmān. If you have strong belief, īmān in AllāhNone everything it is very easy. Because they was throwing people, making fire and throwing to fire, they are not coming back from īmān. This is strong īmān. But if other people, they maybe say said, "Oh okay, we are not believer." But when you have strong īmān even fire cannot be worry about this, about death, about other. Only you make your īmān stronger and it is the most important thing. AllāhNone give us strong īmān, in shā’a Llāh, to not be worry for this small thing, is small thing, not important, al-ḥamdu liLlāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
