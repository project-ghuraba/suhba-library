As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa raḥmatu Llāhi wa barakātuhu.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīn wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh. Madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr. Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistāni, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

Our Ṭarīqah – "Aṭ-ṭarīqatu taḥammulu l-aḍdād," meaning ṭarīqah too, we must be able to carry what you don't like. Your opposite – meaning opposite what you are like. There is opposite thing – many thing more what you like there is what you not like. So ṭarīqah teaching to be patient and to can carry this order, what it is. If you are not happy with this, maybe you are not happy with your house, maybe you are one not happy with wife, maybe you don't happy with neighbour. Everything of course you have your ego, they are wild, they are not good. By teaching of ṭarīqah, little by little you are can be strong and nothing affect you. If you are not strong you are getting afraid from everything, you can, you are getting angry for everything. But when you are strong and you can face difficulty, nothing affect you. Ṭarīqah teaching this.

You must be accept what AllāhNone give you in this life. And what you can meet, what you are, what you can find whole your life, you must know this is from AllāhNone. And if I accept this and I can manage with this without complaining it will be good for you, it will be more easy your life. If you get angry for everything or you can, you like to fight for everything, so you are following your ego. You are not patient. You are miserable life for you. You have miserable life for you. You must "taḥammul" – you must carry this and it will be at the end you are winner, you are stronger, in shā’a Llāh.

AllāhNone help us, because without helping it is difficult. But also you must train yourself. Yesterday we are coming big place, now fashion they are making gym. People they are only training their body. You must train your... to more be more stronger character; you must train also your ego. They are this for body, hours they are doing something with making body to come stronger. But after, if you are leave it, your body quickly coming worse than before. But for your self, who training your ego, you, it is, must continue and it is not you used to it. After, not coming like before. But for your body, it will quickly what you done going useless. With teaching yourself to be carry and to be patient it is for whole life going.

AllāhNone help us, in shā’a Llāh, to be in this good adab and accepting ṭarīqah order and it is order of ProphetNone. Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq al-Fātiḥah.
