Allah Almighty addressing all mankind, because He granted us some special being whose real position only He knows. That is ego. Ego, as much as you are giving is asking more.

And our position to be deputies is so low level for us. Lowest level. And then we are asking more and more, hal min mazeed.

Catching ourself, what is that rein? It is death. Death finally making our egos to stop. At least to come a little bit down, not to ask for something from ma siwa, that means beyond the Lord of Heavens, ma siwa Hu. Allah Almighty is granting such a position or such a talab, (request).
