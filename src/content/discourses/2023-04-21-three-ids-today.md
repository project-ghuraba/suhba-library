As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh. A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

May AllāhNone bless our feast. May it be accepted in the sight of AllāhNone. On this ‘Īd, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla shows the angels what Muslims have done, and is happy. Therefore, may it be blessed. This is from the real feasts. There are many things that are considered holidays now. They have neither virtue nor barakah. The things made precious by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla are valuable. The rest has no value. Even if it seems valuable at first, it later becomes clear that it is worthless. People waste their time. They waste their lives.

Shukr to AllāhNone, today is ‘Īd. And shukr to AllāhNone, they say there are two ‘Īds: ‘Īdu l-Fiṭr and Jum‘ah. Jum‘ah is a feast as well. As it is a feast, some people who know only by hearsay claim there is no wedding between two ‘Īds. They say a marriage is not made then. That is not between ‘Īdu l-Fiṭr and ‘Īdu l-Aḍḥá, but between 'Īd and Jum‘ah. As Jum‘ah is also a Muslim holiday, there is no chance, no time in between. It is okay, it is accepted. But there is not enough time. People mistakenly can call permissible things sinful or call accepted things unacceptable. That is not important. Shukr to AllāhNone, what is important is that we have two feasts today.

And the third one is that it is the birthday of Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim, according to Gregorian calendar. The twenty-first of April is the blessed day when he came to this world. That is the means of joy and relief for us. Because he is example of the āyah we recited in the khuṭbah a while ago. HisNone awliyā’u Llāh, "Alā inna awliyā’a Llāhi lā khawfun ‘alayhim wa-lā hum yaḥzanūn" (10:62). Awliyā’ of AllāhNone have no fear. They have no sadness. They fear and respect AllāhNone. They are the people who believe in and fear AllāhNone. Good tidings for them, both here and hereafter. This is a big thing. Because people dive into this world and think they can find everything in it. They forget about the next world. Everything is good for awliyā’u Llāh, even in this world, as well. AllāhNone helps them. And HeNone gave them good tidings about the next world. "Lā tabdīla li-kalimāti Llāh" (10:64). The word of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla cannot be changed. The promise of AllāhNone is true. Therefore, as the third reason for joy, we are celebrating and making shukr to AllāhNone for sitting at his feet. There are hundreds of thousands, millions of people all over the world who find salvation at his feet. We make shukr to AllāhNone for being with him. It is said in ḥadīth sharīf who are the most beloved people of AllāhNone, the best loved people of AllāhNone. People are servants, created by AllāhNone. And the most beloved people are "anfa‘uhum li‘ayālih"; in the presence of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, the most beloved people are the people who benefit HisNone servants. Awliyā’ means walī, the beloved, a person who is loved. And the most important thing for us is not to be loved by this and that one, but to be loved by AllāhNone. As long as AllāhNone loves you, everyone will love you. If AllāhNone doesn't love you, do whatever you want, no one will care about or love you. AllāhNone makes others love those whom HeNone loves.

Of course it is important to be loved by good people. It is better not to be loved by people who are not good. It is a good thing, too, so that good ones are with good ones. And bad ones will be good if AllāhNone gives them guidance. The door of goodness and the door of forgiveness are not closed. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is the one who forgives mistakes. You make one mistake in dunyā, and even though you repent and ask for forgiveness, they will throw you in jail or you will carry its punishment whatever it is. There is no other option. But Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is forgiving.

Therefore, join the way of AllāhNone so that you become beloved of AllāhNone and attain those good tidings. and you have goodness here and hereafter. This is the promise of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.

We are, al-ḥamdu liLlāh... Happy ‘Īd, in shā’a Llāh. This ‘Īdu l-Fiṭr, feast of fasting, after Ramaḍān finish. The real feast, it is this one. And there is only two feasts for believer, for Muslim. We celebrate and – small, young, old – everyone celebrate. Even they are not practicing, but still, they are celebrating this feast. They are going, visiting each other. And children they are playing, running, they have some... Old time there was more enjoyment for small children. But now it's okay. Even now they are feeling for this feast, ‘Īd, and they will become happy. This happiness, from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, which it is given by HimNone, and, in shā’a Llāh, who happy with this, in shā’a Llāh, give him even little bit in his heart belief, īmān. It will grow, in shā’a Llāh. For this, we are happy with this ‘Īd.

And today, Jum‘ah, Friday, also feast for believer, for Muslim. AllāhNone give this day for Muslim. Every week we celebrate, but, of course, the ‘Īd not like other ‘Īd we celebrate. Just, we celebrate. So, today, there are two ‘Īd, two feasts. We are happy with this. But, I don't know if anyone from Turkish people, they saying: "You cannot marry between two ‘Īds, two feasts, between ‘Īdu l-Aḍḥá and [Fiṭr]," but it is really, reality, if coming to Friday, so Friday also ‘Īd. So, between this, it is difficult to make marriage, to make nikāḥ in this two hours, three hours. For this, they said. But people, they are mixing between what is, can be, or cannot be, or between to be ḥalāl or ḥarām. So, but normally, no time to make this. And al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are happy from morning. Becoming more happy with Jum‘ah also.

And al-ḥamdu liLlāh, today also, there is third ‘Īd for us. For who following ṭarīqah, who following sharī‘ah – especially for Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim follower. It is day – birthday of Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim. Twenty-one April in 1922, he honoured, light – AllāhNone give light with him to this earth, which it was the real, the most darkest time. In time of Islām which, Khalīf – they was throwing out Emperor of Islām, destroying with this non-believer, and they thought they finished Islām. Because, when khilāfah (caliphate) finish, nothing. All they exile the Khalīfah and other, whole salṭanat people, and they thought they finished Islām.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh that time, AllāhNone send Mawlānā Shaykh. It was his birth time. It was very terrible time for whole planet, for whole Earth. Everywhere mess, illness, hunger. No hope at all. But, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, from no hope, AllāhNone bring light, bring hope for this. And this, there was one of our old murīd, who is relative to my auntie. He was murīd of Shaykh Sharafu d-Dīn ad-Dāghistānī. And that time, he was saying to Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh ad-Dāghistānī, "Today, one of our child, they coming to this dunyā, to Earth." And even he describe him. He said that he will be opening – many non-Muslims become Muslim on his hand. Even he gave his number of shoes, his feet [shoe size]. He was saying, giving tiding to Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh ad-Dāghistānī, and it was real good tiding.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are following him. We are happy AllāhNone make us to follow him. He give light for whole world. Everywhere coming, even until now, many, still more and more people coming through him, al-ḥamdu liLlāh. He is – in āyat we read in khuṭbah: Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Alā inna awliyā’a Llāhi lā khawfun ‘alayhim wa-lā hum yaḥzanūn. Alladhīna āmanū wa-kānū yattaqūn" (10:62-64). "Beloved one for AllāhNone, there are no fear for them, and no sadness." And, because they are believer and they are afraid from AllāhNone to do anything wrong, they have: "Lahumu l-bushrá fī l-ḥayāti d-dunyā wa-fī l-ākhirati lā tabdīla li-kalimāti Llāh" (10:64). AllāhNone help them in this world and ākhirah. Nothing can affect them. This, good tidings they said, and no changing for word of AllāhNone. Allāh'sNone word, not like our word. This is for human being to see how good to be with AllāhNone, not to be away from HimNone. Because, in dunyā and in ākhirah, in this life and other life, they will be winner. Nothing affect them. Nothing make them sad or fear for them. Because many, maybe, people they are working in dunyā. They succeed in dunyā, but for ākhirah, they don't have anything.

But awliyā’u Llāh, they are for dunyā and for ākhirah. And they are bringing people also with them. They are benefit for human being. Ḥadīth of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam we said, "Aḥabbuhum ilá Llāhi anfa‘uhum li‘ayālih." "People, they are servant for AllāhNone, and the most beloved one for HimNone, who help HisNone servants." Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, Mawlānā Shaykh, whole life he was helping in everything, for spiritual, and also material, also. All time, he is looking to help people, from his young time until end of his life. AllāhNone bless him. We are thanking him, because until now, with his barakah, you are coming, because his barakah not cutting. It is still running like big river, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone make us with him, in shā’a Llāh, forever.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
