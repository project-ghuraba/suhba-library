As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Shukr to AllāhNone we are in these ten blessed days. For every good deed done in these days, AllāhNone gives countless thawāb (reward). What kinds of good deeds? All kinds. Our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam said if you remove a stone or any harmful thing from the way, even that is a good deed and will bring you thawāb. We don't put value on such little things but Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla values even the things that we don't value.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla looks for every opportunity to show kindness to us and give from HisNone generosity. Mankind is the opposite. He thinks how to avoid giving to people or how to give less. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla does the opposite. AllāhNone loves giving. Giving thawāb to people and rewarding them are the favourite things of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. This is what is said in the ḥadīth of our ProphetNone. If Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla had not loved us, HeNone would have given us one reward for each good deed but HeNone gives at least ten for one. This is the least and then it increases until ten thousand, hundred thousand. And AllāhNone Himself knows the limit. That is what our ProphetNone says regarding thawāb.

Therefore, we should try to do all kinds of good deeds. Being kind to your family, relatives, and parents, children, wife, or husband, that is a good deed too. It has big thawāb too. Because life in this world is very short, people don't know its value. But when they lose their chance, they regret it. May AllāhNone not let us regret.

May AllāhNone help us to do good deeds. Because mankind obeys his ego if HeNone does not help. And the ego does not like any goodness and help. It doesn't like any good thing. Whatever is bad, it follows and obeys. May AllāhNone help us, in shā’a Llāh. These days are very blessed. May we do more good deeds, in shā’a Llāh.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh we are living in ten, ten days of, the blessed days Dhū l-Ḥijjah which AllāhNone reward us without – countless. HeNone knows what HeNone will do. Everything we do HeNone happy to see something to give us, to reward us. Even very small thing, you are thinking it is not, cannot be reward for what we do, but Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla HeNone is happy and not making... very small, small thing, HeNone reward for this. So, we must be happy with Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla because HeNone is happy to give, HeNone is generous.

You find generous people... very few people in dunyā, generous, they like to give. But Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla HeNone is not like them. Even generous people maybe they thought, "Maybe it will be finished, maybe when finish what I will give?" But still they can give but Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla – no fear. Everything HeNone has. Whole universe for HimNone so HeNone happy, most happy for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla to reward people. This is what Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying. Attribute of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, to be generous. Generous it is most, because without generosity HeNone will be, we will be, cannot get anything. But Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla even for non-believer HeNone give generosity in this dunyā. Maybe they find and accept AllāhNone.

But Ākhirah also generosity for believer. For non-believer they will be regret and they will be very regret that time. So, in shā’a Llāh, we are living in time it is important, especially we are living the end of time, as most people they can say, this physician, there is billion years to finish this earth. But it is for Banī Ādam, sons of Ādam, all nearly their time finish. So this is what it will be, so we must in this time, we are more reward from another, any time before. So, we are asking AllāhNone to help us to do good thing. Even you take small stone from street, to clean street, to make good for people, for animal, for anything – AllāhNone reward for this.

So AllāhNone make us to do good thing, what HeNone is happy with us to do. We need help. Without help, if we are for our ego, our ego they don't like help, they don't like good thing, nothing good, any good deed. Ego, if you leave for your ego, never it will be giving good thing. Ego, you must control. And we are asking Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla to help us to control our ego and to do good thing, in shā’a Llāh. Especially these days which it is more reward for us, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
