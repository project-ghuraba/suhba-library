As-Salāmu ‘Alaykum wa RaḥmatuLlāhi wa Barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu BiLlāhi Minash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. BismiLlāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm.

Wa ṣ-Salātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Akhirīn.

Madad yā RasūlAllāh, Madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi RasūliLlāh, Madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

Dastūr Mawlana Sheikh Abdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistāni, Sheikh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-

Ḥaqqānī. Madad.Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-Suḥbah wa l-Khayru fi l-Jam‘iyyah.

We are thanking Allāh. AlhamduliLlah wa ShukruliLlah. We meet again. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla helps us in everything. Every breath, every second, every minute, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is with us. We must know this and we must thank Him for this.

Many people, ignorant people are not knowing what is good, what is bad. Normally, who doesn’t know this has no mind; is not responsible. And these people are in a mental house. But most people are like this. These are not like those in the real mental house, those not responsible. They are responsible because they know everything. They are going to school, they are teaching, they are working, making business, everything. They know this. But when it comes to thanking Him, they are not knowing this. This is not good for who had done this or who is doing this. It means life is without any purpose, life like animals.

Even animals know their Creator and make tasbīh for Him. Everything, “ ءٍ ﻲْ ﺷَ ﻦﻣِّ نِإوَ هِ ﺪِ ﻤْ ﺤَ ِﺑ ﺢُ ِّﺒﺴَ ُﯾ ﻻﱠ ِإ”, “Wa 'In Min Shay'in 'Illā Yusabbiĥu Biĥamdihi”, “And there is not a thing except that it exalts [Allah] by His praise.” (Qur’an 17:44). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘Aẓīm. Allāh is saying, everything is mentioning Allāh and knowing Him, asking and thanking Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Only these people who are not knowing that are even worse than this creature. This creature, Allāh created is and it is; everything, even stone, even water, tree - everything is praising Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Allāh put this knowledge for them to know their Creator. When you are not knowing your Creator, they are better than you then.

So be careful! In everything, you mustn’t object or be unhappy. You must be happy because Allāh has given this for you, given you this knowledge to know Him. Many people are doing something not looking for what will happen. May Allāh make us be knowledgeable people, to know Him, to accept what He gave us and to thank Him for all of this.

Wa min Allāhi t-Tawfīq. Al-Fātiḥa.
