That ego which fighting to the Lord of Universes and claiming that he is the lord for everyone. He is the Lord. He is The First and He is The Last. He is the first from everything. And He is the last after everything but it is so hard thing to make our egos to accept that to say "You are my Lord".

Allah Almighty, the Creator He created nafs/ego. He created. Then saying, "Come", and going, saying, "Go" and coming. Aks, (opposite, always doing the opposite). Yes. He thinks that he is something. If you are saying to me "Come", I am going, if you are saying "Go", I am coming. As I like, I am doing. Then asking "Who are you, who am I?" Saying "I am myself, you are also yourself", "man ana man anta ana ana anta anta" Ordering one thousand years in fire-hell in prison, nafs. One thousand years. Then calling him out saying, "Who I am?" "Who am I and who are you?" After one thousand years burning in fire coming and saying same story. "I am I, myself, you are you". Not saying, "You are Lord and I am servant".

Ordering one thousand years in freezing hell. He was sleeping one thousand years more in freezing hell. Deep freezing. Bringing out, calling, saying, "Who am I and who are you?" That saying "I am I, you are you". Not changing, nafs. Ego. Too hard person. Then Allah ordering to imprison in hunger valley, wad al ju`u. Saying, "ju`u fa innaha bi'sa d-daje`u". (Hunger is a terrible way to go to bed at night). Yes. So hunger making nafs to say, "Yes, You are my Lord". Nothing frightened him except hunger.

Allah Almighty lightening that command and giving us permission from fajr, from dawn, up to sunset only, and after sunset we may eat. Such a difficult fastings, Allah Almighty ordering people. For what? It is not a cruelty. But cruelty comes to sons of Adam from their egos. To cut egos down he was ordering. The easiest one for our nation.

Or if a person sleeping after maghrib no eating and awaking, no more eating also. Too late finish. Then Allah Almighty lightening and giving fursa, a chance for us from sunset up to dawn.
