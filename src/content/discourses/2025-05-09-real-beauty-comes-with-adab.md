As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh. A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah. Ṭarīqah – let us say this again – is a beautiful way founded upon adab. It teaches adab, and adab raises people’s ranks. When people have adab, their value increases. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla created people in the most perfect state in every aspect. There is a saying of Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim about this – when a person looks in the mirror, Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim used to say it. It is probably a ḥadīth sharīf. “Allāhumma kamā aḥsanta khalqī fa-ḥassin khuluqī.” “Just as YouNone created me in beautiful form, make my manners beautiful too.” Saying this when looking in the mirror is probably sunnah – or something similar. It should be said. There is wisdom and beauty in every word of Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim. He shows the way to people. AllāhNone created people in the most beautiful and perfect form. Accordingly, their manners, training, and adab should also be beautiful. And a person must cultivate that themselves. So, when a person asks for help from AllāhNone, when they seek HisNone assistance and walk upon HisNone way, their character becomes beautiful. Everyone appreciates them. No one can say anything bad about them. But someone who does all kinds of wrong and then says, “I am a human being” – that person has much to learn and a long way to go. A Muslim must be even more careful. As for others, it is said: “Laysa ba‘da l-kufri dhanb.” When someone is a kāfir, they have already committed the greatest sin. Whatever they do afterwards – whether they drink, destroy, or do anything else – it no longer matters. But a Muslim without adab – such a person is very incomplete and lacking. Adab is important. Now, Muslims are imitating others in everything. Whatever others do, they do the same. They shout – these shout. They jump – these jump. They run – these run. They copy everything exactly. They are inserting things into Islām, saying this too is part of it – but Islām is dignity. It is seriousness. Of course, there is beauty in it too. You can joke, you can laugh, have fun – but not the way that person does it. You can do it within adab, and it becomes very beautiful. People of the past did so. The greatest example is our Shaykh, Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim. How beautiful his jokes were, how beautiful his laughter was, how beautiful his humour was. May AllāhNone be pleased with him – for ninety-two years, from his earliest years onward, shukr to AllāhNone, we heard and learned so much about what he did, how he lived. He knows the way. We must walk that way. It is the way of our Holy ProphetNone. Just as our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam walked that way, so did the caliphs after himNone, those who followed himNone, ṣaḥābah, Ahlu l-Bayt, mashāyikh, the shaykhs – all walked the same way. Whoever leaves that way – it means they have taken a different path. The way of our Holy ProphetNone is clear. HisNone way is the ḥadīth sharīf and āyatu l-karīmah. AllāhNone is always watching over you. This is a ḥadīth sharīf of our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam: “Worship Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla as if you see HimNone.” If you cannot see HimNone – and you cannot, says our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam – then worship as if HeNone sees you. That is certain. Because you cannot see Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, but Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is with you every moment, every second. So, within the bounds of ḥalāl, within the bounds of adab, every kind of beauty is possible. It is not beauty if it involves filth, evil, or disgusting things. That is why adab is very beautiful. It is important for the human being. One must observe it. One must not harm others. One must not violate others’ rights. Whoever pays attention to this and walks this path, with the permission of AllāhNone, reaches the highest stations. There is the Insānu l-Kāmil – the perfect human. There are humans, and there is the perfected human. And there are other creatures in human form, like vermin. May AllāhNone protect us from the evil of our egos. May AllāhNone protect us. May we remain on the right path, in shā’a Llāh. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are thanking AllāhNone also. We are again with Mawlānā way, with his blessing, with his teaching. We are going and travelling through days, weeks, months, years. We are travelling, but many of us not feeling they are travelling, but very fast, very speedy we are travelling. And our way, it must be way of light, not to... not dangerous way. This way, way of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, which heNone is the example for us. Because Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone create all human being, and with HisNone creation, HeNone – in best shape. Mawlānā sometimes saying if we, if maybe if we have three eyes or four legs, for this it will be? No. AllāhNone create us the best way, shape. But, for inside, you must be careful. You must be, you make yourself better – which Mawlānā Shaykh many times he say when somebody looking, he also was looking for mirror. This is maybe ḥadīth or I don’t know exactly. He said, “Allāhuma aḥsin khuluqī kamā aḥsanta khalqī.” “O our LordNone, make my inside attribute nice, as YouNone create me in good shape, nice shape.” So AllāhNone create you the best shape. HeNone create whole Muslim, kāfir, or every believer, non-believer in best shape. But HeNone want you, for this shape, to put inside yourself best thing, to be perfect. That which they say, “Insānu l-Kāmil”. Insānu l-Kāmil meaning perfect human. Other one, maybe less perfect, maybe no perfect at all. Maybe it is only shape of human being. It is like animal, or like other creature. So this is way of ProphetNone to human being, whole human being: to be good people, to be good attribute, which it is adab, meaning. “Addabanī Rabbī fa-aḥsana ta’dībī.” “AllāhNone made meNone behave the best.” This from Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. We must follow himNone. We must trying to be, as much we can do, as Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. This give you good situation, your degree in Divine Presence of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla coming higher and higher. And when this making, you will be respected in between people also. Even you are not rich, you are not, don’t have any follower or something, but still you, AllāhNone give this in your side. So people they cannot do any bad thing for you. They must be respect, and they make away from you. If they don’t want you, they can be away from you, not harm you. This is what Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam showing us: the way of life, way of life for to be human being. But if you not, it’s your fault. After, you will be regret for this. And this, what we said, it is especially human being. There are many kind of human being, but two kind it is important: believer, non-believer. If you are believer, you must follow what Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla want, what HeNone like. If you are not believer, no blame at all for you, because they say, there is rule: “Laysa ba‘da l-kufri dhanb.” After non-believer, no sin for you. It is the biggest sin you are not believe in Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. For this, we are saying this for, especially for Muslim, which they don’t have good attribute. They don’t have, nowadays, there is no good manner, no good adab, no good behave. This is for Muslim. They must, who say, I am Muslim, he must be first to learn good behave, to be good people. And in this situation, not imitate non-believer, which nowadays it is the most common in between Muslim, to imitate non-Muslim, how they are laughing, how they are shouting, how they are speaking, how they are dressing or everything. They want to imitate them. It is not good for Muslim. Muslim, they have also, they can enjoy themselves with everything AllāhNone make it ḥalāl. Many thing, they can enjoy themselves. And they will be more happier than other people. They [other people] enjoy themselves with harming their body, harming their soul, harming their situation and the society, all this harming. Now we see, whole world, they no manner, nothing. They destroy their happiness, they destroy good manner. Who have good manner, maybe people they looking for him as he is an idiot. This is what happened. They becoming upside down. This is not from Islām. This against Islām, and Islām want everything to be perfect, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone help us, to send us Sayyidunā l-Mahdī to make it. Only he can do it, in shā’a Llāh. Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
