Aū‘dhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-Shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Waṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-F’āiz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-

There are beloved servants of Allah towards whom we should show respect, whom we should appreciate, because they are rare people. Most of the time people think of them as ordinary and talk ill about them knowingly or unknowingly. Those uttered words harm people, because they are Satanic slanderings. When those who follow him accept these words and spread them around, such becomes a cause of fitna. On top of that, when a beloved servant of Allah is mistreated, certainly Allah punishes those people.

This has been the same since the time of our holy Prophet (SAW), as also since the time of Adam (AS). Satan bothers people. He does not like good people; he wishes to do evil against good people. So this is a serious matter about which we should take care. This is why Allah says in a hadith qudsi, “Against whomever starts a war against my beloved servant, I myself start a war and fight.” No one can defeat Allah; Allah is forever victories.

So may Allah help us recognize His beloved servants, so that we do not fall in error, and do not afflict them but rather respect them. Those who respect them, respect Allah, as they are the honorable ones of Allah. May Allah protect us from doing evil, insha’Allah. Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-F ti ah
