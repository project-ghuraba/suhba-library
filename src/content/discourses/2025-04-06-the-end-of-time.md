As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhiwa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīnawa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāhal-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh MuḥammadNāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah,wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

The time we are living in is the end of times. Shukr to AllāhNone, HeNone created us in this time. Whatever AllāhNone wants happens. Nothing is the way you want it. Therefore, you must submit yourself to the will of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Nothing happens when you say “If we were in this time or that time.” You live in this time. So, manifestation of this time is coming to you. That manifestation of the end of times is somewhat disordered. It is the manifestation of the time of fitnah.

Humankind introduces new things daily, from every direction. So, people don’t know what to do. What should you do? You must surrender to HisNone will. You must fulfil the orders of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. And you must not interfere in the rest. This happened, that happened. What Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla wants is happening. Nothing else is happening.

Therefore, Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim used to say it is better not to go out of your house in the end of times. Because when you go out, some fitnah appears, something happens, there is danger. Therefore, stay at home. It’s fine to go out if you have a need or if it’s for work. But don’t look outside, wondering what’s happened. Mashāyikh used to say that. Whoever looks outside wondering what’s there is in danger. In the time of Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim and Sayyidunā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh Dāghistānī, things weren’t like they are now. It is much more intense now. There is a lot of fitnah and confusion. Therefore, people possess wisdom; they must think about the result when they do something.

Those lacking wisdom act heedlessly. But they will regret it then. And something they will regret the most is going against Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. You cannot compensate for that regret later [ākhirah]. You may compensate while you’re alive. Whatever you do in this life, there is a solution for that near Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. You can repent. If you took someone’s right, you can pay it back. You can make peace with someone you fought with. You can ask for forgiveness from someone you oppressed. Everything can be fixed. But once it’s in the ākhirah, things will become difficult. Therefore, this time is the time when shayṭāns are walking around. They turn people against one another over the slightest matter and cause their ruin. There is a story. Shayṭān was sitting somewhere. He said, “We don’t have much left to do here.” He was with his son. “Let’s leave here, go somewhere else, and there, turn people against each other and cause fitnah.” His son said, “Okay. There’s something over there that’ll just take a minute or two. I’ll do it and come right back.” A woman was milking a cow. He pulled something on the cow –probably the tail. So, the cow kicked while being milked, and the milk spilled on the ground. Her husband came and beat her for spilling the milk. The woman went to her tribe. Her tribe attacked the man. They killed him because he had beaten her. After that, two brothers began fighting each other. As the dead piled up, they kept fighting. The whole place turned into chaos. Father shayṭān asked, “What did you do? I didn’t do anything. I just made a small move. I didn’t interfere with anything. They did it themselves.” That’s how things happen.

Therefore, we must be careful. People must pay close attention to everything they do, every action they take, so that no misfortune or calamity arises. People on the path of AllāhNone don’t involve themselves in worldly matters. They’ve left the affairs of the world to those who pursue them. And they made everything chaotic. The whole world is upside down now. They don’t know what to do. Just sit and watch. There’s no need for you to do anything. You don’t need to act. Watch what AllāhNone will do. You already know the ending.

The ending, shukr to AllāhNone... For whom is the end of the world? “Wa-l- ‘āqibatu lil-muttaqīn” (28:83). The ending is for the pious. They will be the winners. Therefore, don’t be upset about anything. As long as you are with Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, don’t chase after anyone, don’t struggle with anyone. Because there is a time to remain silent, and a time to speak. That’s why it’s best to be silent now.

A thousand years ago, a holy man said: “Hādhā zamānu s-sukūtiwa-mulāzamatu l-buyūt.” This is the time to remain silent, the time to stay at home. It was said a thousand years ago. So, according to that, now you should not go outside at all. Therefore, for Muslims who follow this path, the most they should do is this: when they sense something evil with their hearts and know it is disliked by AllāhNone, they should leave it to HimNone. AllāhNone is Aḥkamu l-Ḥākimīn. HeNone is the Most Just. HisNone ruling is eternal. Shukr to AllāhNone. We are living in time which it is, “end of time”, we say No more...

They said, there is for Earth, billion years, trillion years, to finish, to whole this system. They make calculation. They are saying very, very known thing, about scenario of how it will be finish, this world. They thought it is, still there is million years to finish, or more – billions. But Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying it is near for this. We are living in time, end of time. And, subḥāna Llāh, their description, what Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying in Qur’ān, about when Qiyāmah coming, they said, this people. AllāhNone give them little bit knowledge, to show how it will be. They say... “... fa-kānat wardatan ka-d-dihān” (55:37). It will be, they said, it will be big, when this is, after million or billions years. Sun, it will blow, and it all, it will be big, orange or rose. All this, coming this. AllāhNone describe this in Qur’ān. So, it is not like, what they said, “It is long time.” It will be after... We are living in end of time, and when Qiyāmah coming, all this will be quickly, all together. So, we are living in time, it is difficult time. But we cannot do anything, because it's will of AllāhNone. HeNone create us in this time. Every time has special thing. Old people time, it was difficult to go from place to place, or to make thing, very slow. Now, everything very fast and very, people, they are not tired too much. Machine, they are doing this other thing. But this time, AllāhNone give another difficulty for us. It is most difficult time, since before.

So, we are living in this time, and Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, heNone praise who live in this time and he continue in way of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. It's too difficult, but it is also reward for us. So, we are accept, and we are happy with what AllāhNone gave us. But we are, also, must be careful for living in this time. No need to put yourself in dangers, or to make fitnah, or to make bad thing for people. No. You must just be quiet. This is what one of big “ulamā” or tābi‘īn, he was saying, “Hādhā zamānu s-sukūtiwa-mulāzamatu l-buyūt” "This time it's time of to be quiet and to be in house.” This, before more than thousand year, he was saying this.

Now it's worse and worse. It is, no need to follow people, any people. They are, you maybe think it is right, and you must go and to make something with them. No, this is not good at all. Only make du‘ā’ for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, and Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla [make you] to win, to keep you safe, keep Muslim safe. This is what we can do, because now time of shayṭān.

Shayṭān, they are controlling everywhere. This is what Muslim; they are not real understanding. You can do something which it is, can be, but what it is not can be, no need to open fitnah or something. Because, shayṭān waiting. What...

There is one story about shayṭān. He was living in one tribe, in desert. And they are, he and his son. He said for his son, “Oh, my son, here people they are living in peace, and not doing anything. We must go and look for another place to make fitnah, to make people to be not happy,” he said. His son said, “Okay, two minute. I am coming.” And there was one woman, she is milking cow. And so, he just move something from tent. And this, coming afraid, and they, it kick the milk bowl, this cup. And milk fall down in earth. And husband coming. He see this milk in the earth. He becoming angry and beat up his wife. And she run away to her family, and family coming angry, attacking this man and killing him. After him, this whole tribe, to another tribe, they was fighting. Many people died; many people destroyed. Son coming, and father shayṭān was, “What you done?” he said, “For...” He said, “No just I move something, and it, what happen after this, it was like this. I didn't do anything.” And from small thing, coming very bad thing.

So, for this, Mu’min, ṭarīqah people, they must be have wisdom, to –with anybody, with wife, with family, with another people –to be careful what they are doing, what they are saying. To not make fitnah, and something you cannot go back to what it was before. And you will regret and you said, “Oh, I wish I didn't say this. I wish I didn't make this.” This is for, we are living in this time, real tough time. Not only in local. Whole world is boiling, with economy, with fighting, with no respect, no good education, no belief. Everything it is finish. Must be, in shā’a Llāh, someone, who we are, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are knowing, and we are happy for end of this chaos. AllāhNone will send somebody, Sayyidunā Mahdī, and he will be, make everything again the best, best of best, in shā’a Llāh.

So, believer, they must be happy, no problem. “Lā khawfun ‘alayhim wa-lā hum yaḥzanūn”(10:62). We are not have fear. We are not sad. We are know, what coming. But other people, they don't know what there coming. For this, if anything happen, going street, fighting, swearing, making problem, because they don't have any hope what will happen. They thought, by themselves, It will be good what they done. But, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, Mu’min, Muslim, he must know: “Wa-l- ‘āqibatu lil-muttaqīn” (28:83). The end [is] for who, who they are believer, and they are care for their religion. AllāhNone keep us safe, in shā’a Llāh, in this time. But is this time also, again we saying, it is time for big trading, trader. Now they this crazy thing, they say “crypto money”. They giving one, you [collect]ten thousand. This false. But what Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, for our time for reward, it will be like this. So, it’s really thousand time more than before time.

AllāhNone keep us in HisNone way, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq,al-Fātiḥah.
