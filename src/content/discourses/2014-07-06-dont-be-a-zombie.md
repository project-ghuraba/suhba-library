Sheikh Bahauddin Adil al-Haqqani sohbat of the 6th of July, 2014.

Dawam! Masha'Allah! Alhamdulillah that Allah Almighty He gave us the opportunity to say salat and salam. It is...so many people they cannot pronounce this. And so many people they've not been permitted to say salat and salam. You have to know that everything is according to permission, Heavenly permission. If the Heavenly permission is given, then you can do. If the Heavenly permission is not given, then you cannot do. We are happy that Allah Almighty He give us this Heavenly permission, that we are able to pray, we are able to fast and we are able to say salat and salam.

The zikr, it is the most important thing in our heart. And the most important thing in our body, is the heart. And if there is no heart there is no life. So, all it's coming step by step, subhanAllah. So measure how the zikr is important for us. Now, they have so many TV serials about zombies. And everybody watching! What is this zombie? But actually they put the realistic being of the human today, that they are all like zombies, because they don't have zikr and they don't have heart to work. And they don't have this light of life that Allah Almighty He put in the secret of zikr.

If they make up, they are wearing very nice dress and they are having nice life, as seen from outside. But in reality all of them are zombies because there is no zikr. The zikr is the most important thing for the heart. Allah, Allah, Allah, Allah, Allah... you see the beating of the heart also dum, dum, dum, dum.... Allah, Allah Allah, Allah, Allah.... it's going all the time. This is the zikr of the heart, otherwise the heart will not be alive. Will be alive because have to function as an organ but the real one functions as the Nur of Allah.

This is ajayib/strange. It is a very deep sohbah, sheikh! It is very deep sohbah I didn't realize that we'll go so deep. It is very strange manifestation coming, because this is baraka of Ramadan. This is strange manifestation coming because of the gift of Ramadan. This is special manifestation coming for the honour of Ramadan.

Alhamdulillah that we are happy to be fasting and, even our ego not happy that we are fasting, but we are happy. We don't like that our ego to say we are not happy. Yes! We are happy to be fasting. It is, when you are used to something it is very difficult to change. It very painful to change. And we've been having this with Mawlana Sheikh, that he had, the last 2 years he was not able to fast Ramadan. But it was the most painful Ramadan for him. And this year Mawlana Sheikh he makes Ramadan in his Maqam. Alhamdulillah that he made us to love Ramadan, he taught us to love Ramadan. And he taught us how to appreciate the month of Ramadan.

It is important to know about Ramadan - Ramadan is the month of zikr, month of Quran, month of worship to Allah Almighty, and it is the month that is carrying the whole year's ibada in it. Because outside Ramadan, you are not bothering to fast. Outside Ramadan you are maybe not bothering to read Quran. Outside Ramadan you are very lazy to pray night prayer. Outside Ramadan you are making thousands of excuses like me. But in Ramadan there is no excuse. You're trapped! Ramadan is a trap. And Allah Almighty He knows how He makes this trap. Alhamdulillah Shukr. Because He knows His servants.

We are saying that we are loving shahr/month Ramadan so much because shahr Ramadan is taking us to worship more for Allah, and to make ibada and to be nearer to Him and to feel the heat of Allah Almighty. The hot love of Allah Almighty that you are directly in touch with Allah Almighty because you are sacrificing - we think that we are sacrificing - but we are sacrificing from so many things in this month. And to say "Ya Rabbi we are for You." And we are coming to be good servant for You - even temporarily, like one month. Even those You are accepting us as we are full time servants for You and to be with You all the time with our heart, with our feelings and our prayers.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfiq, bi hurmati l-Habib bi hurmati l-Fatiha.
