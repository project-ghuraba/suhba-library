As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh. A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah. AllāhNone is Aḥsanu l-Khāliqīn. the most perfect Creator, is Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. HeNone created everything in the most perfect way. Therefore, a believer must not object. Whatever Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gives you, what HeNone has destined for you, or everything that happens by the will of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, there is no need to panic. A true believer is like this. Certainly, Muslims have weak īmān. As the īmān of many people is weak, they become anxious when something happens. That stems from a lack of faith. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla created this world. Before mankind, there were countless other insects, animals, and all kinds of creatures HeNone created. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla knows them. Everything has its own lifetime. When it ends, it passes. This world also has a lifetime. Everything mortal will do something, and then vanish. The same with this world. Therefore, what AllāhNone has promised is not dunyā but ākhirah. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla did not say you would come to this world to be comfortable and stay forever. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla has declared that dunyā is a temporary place. But mankind thinks they will never die. They think they will always remain like this, that everything will be like a rose garden, that everyone will do whatever they want, and it will stay that way forever. There is no such thing. Grandshaykh ‘Abdu Llāh ad-Dāghistānī used to say jokingly, “Wa-khuliqa l-insānu majnūna.” He would say that mankind was created mindless and mad. Meaning, when a person does not use their mind, when they do not accept what is plainly visible, then they are not thinking – they are acting irrationally. That is why in this world there is war here, I don’t know what there; there is goodness in one place, evil in another. There is no clean drinking water left. There is no space left for people. They have multiplied. Everyone is running from one place to another. This stems from the weakness of their īmān. If everyone were content with what Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gives, everyone would live at ease. There would be no trouble. There would be no fighting or strife. But mankind is not content with their fate. They bring hardship upon themselves. May AllāhNone protect us. May we not object to what Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gives, in shā’a Llāh. This happens there, that happens there – May AllāhNone help them. May AllāhNone protect those who suffer. The faithful will receive their reward and merit. Otherwise, they will suffer in vain and be ruined. May AllāhNone grant understanding to people, in shā’a Llāh. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone is the best, “Aḥsanu l-Khāliqīn”, best Creator. No one creator, only AllāhNone Creator. But HeNone is praising HimselfNone. HeNone said, “The best Creator.” Create whole, not one universe, billions of universes HeNone create. And everything, HeNone put law and calculation for everything, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Calculation is very important also. This calculation, many secrets, they can appear; but of course what AllāhNone, HeNone allowed for its secret. But the important thing, AllāhNone, HeNone create this earth also. And since billions, millions year they are many creature, many thing was in this earth. Everything, they have time. They have limited time, so they come and go. Now, our turn, which it is from Ādam ‘alayhi s-salām, we are continue. Of course, this also will finish. This age of earth, it will be finish. With, when Qiyāmah going, it will be different from now. So AllāhNone create everything and HeNone also tell us, in HisNone Holy Book, the only holy book here in this earth, Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n, HeNone said, “You must accept, you are living here not for forever. It is only time for you. As for time for you, for earth also, time.” So people who are believer, real believer, they are not anxious at all from what happening. This happening war, this happening fire, this happening corruption, this happening fire, killing people each other. This, for believer it is normal, not abnormal. It is normal for human being to do this. It’s not abnormal. It is normal, very normal for, because they are following, and they are living in this earth, so it must be like this. This is not Paradise. And Paradise, you go in Paradise you will be live forever. No war, no anything disturb you. For this, believer they know this, and they accept and they just want pleasure of AllāhNone, to be AllāhNone happy with them. This is what... this is real believer, and they are very few. There is millions, billions of Muslim and billions of people, they are not thinking for this at all. Only thinking, “Oh, what happened today? This attacking that. That attacking this. What will happen?” And what will happen? AllāhNone, what HeNone want, it will be happen. Nothing else. So for this, we must be surround [surrender] ourself for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, and looking for HisNone pleasure. Hunger or war or anything, it is not important. It is just, continue to worship Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, you will be more reward. For this, one who has good thinking, he know this: this is earth not like forever. So you must be look for real one, which, Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh ad-Dāghistānī, many time he was laughing. Sometimes, laughing very nice. He saying, “Wa-khuliqa l-insānu majnūna.” Meaning, AllāhNone create human being crazy, no mind. This is what, because ninety-nine percent, they are like this. If one percent good, it is okay. All not losing, for this. Who not use his mind, he is mad. So for this one, Mawlānā was saying this. And so who thinking about this, so he must be accept, and he be restful. AllāhNone keep us in this thinking. Why? If you think like this, you are rest, and happy for every time, dunyā and ākhirah. But if not this, maybe in dunyā not happy, but in ākhirah, also you will be happy, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone keep us, give us good thinking, in shā’a Llāh. Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
