Shaykh Mehmet: Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Madad Ya Rasul Allah.. Madad Ya S. Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, madad Ya S. Nazim al Haqqani, dastur! Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fil jamia'

The sohbat of today insha Allah, first let us say the Verse we recited in the Khutbah: "Wa Ma Tuqaddimu Li'nfusikum Min Khayrin Tajiduhu `Inda Al-Lahi Huwa Khayraan Wa 'A`zama 'Ajraan" (73:20). You do khayr-good works, definitely Allah keeps it, it won't be lost with Allah. Now, as we said before, dunya is a trade center. And Allah... Not like the employers here.. Maybe it is not nice to say this, sometimes people are getting offended. But who is offended more are actually the ones who do it. They hire a person for some work but don't pay his salary afterwards. He tries many methods to not give him his salary. He thinks it is a gain. It is like this for those who work for such people.

But who works for Allah, when you do a good deed Allah (awj) gives you.. Normally it is 1 sawab-reward but Allah already records it as 10 sawab. It can go from 10 to 700 sawabs & some even higher, only Allah knows how many. Just so the servant does it, His Generosity is endless. There is no limit with Allah (awj). Man's understanding can't reach to such things. Therefore Mawlana also says "may your hand always be in khayr-good deeds". Do whatever good deed/khayr there is for you, in terms of material and/or spiritual charity. Not everything is with money, a person who doesn't have money can still do charity. He can do all kinds of good deeds, can do ibadah. All is being saved in Akhirat. All you do in terms of good deeds are mahfuz/safely kept with Allah.

In Akhirat, you will look & see it such that you will say "did we do this much? Where did all this khayr/hasanat come from?" You will get surprised like this. "Tajiduhu" means "you find it". But now mankind, with the little iman/faith he has, he already thinks that he will never die when he is in this life. And he doesn't tend much to khayr as a result. He tries to enjoy whatever he has.

In the Hadith, it says Hell.. We continued with this after Ayat. "Hell is veiled with egoistic desires/shahawat". Shahawat/egoistic desires means all kind of it- looking at haram, lying, ghiybat/backbiting, seizing someone else's money.. all possible bad actions are shahawat & hell is veiled with them. Therefore so many people ask - When one wants to make khayr, it is difficult. And the Hadith continues with this, Paradise is veiled with hardships. Meaning when a person sins, he can do it easily. But when it comes to doing khayr, doing ibadah, it is difficult. And the Paradise is veiled with them.

Meaning, if the person can see the hell behind that desire, he won't do it. But he can't see. That desire veils it, veils it like a curtain. And Paradise is veiled with hardships. It's curtain is hardships. As one does these hard things, one by one the curtains go up. That one becomes satisfied & at that time he can do easily. This of course is a battle with ego. It is a bit difficult to do on his own. If he is not together with a jamaat, with a blessed/good jamaat... Because this is mostly achieved with Tariqat. There is a Shaykh leading you. And people follow that Shaykh & are saved from the evil of their egos. And these curtains go up & at that time he can see the hell behind that desire, and the hardships..No matter what kind of hardships there are; like poverty, illness, fights..Behind all, when you endure hardships there is paradise. If he could see the paradise, these hardships are already nothing to him. And if he can see the hell, he can't even lift his head for the desires because of the greatness of hell. But he just can't see because there are all curtains in front of him.

Today we speak about the Khutba, what was said. Allah (awj), He said: "Wa Ma Tuqaddimu Li'nfusikum Min Khayrin Tajiduhu Huwa Khayraan Wa 'A`zama 'Ajraan" (73:20). What you do good thing, Allah keeps this thing for you. Because people in life, you find very few people who can find some businessmen, or trader or work who people work for him they keep their promise, or they give the right of.. Not right, right of course. But what they say for who work for them "we will give this for you, we will give that for you". But if they find way to not give, quickly not giving. Even small one, they don't want to give.

But Allah (awj), He said "You do & Allah, He keep for you in Akhirat". Even you do one good thing, Allah not give you only one hasanat but He (awj) give ten, at least ten. After until 700, and there is more than 700 also. Allah knows how He will give this. And when you do this, when you go in day of Akhirat you will find this."Tajiduhu", meaning- you find. You will be surprised: "How? I didn't do this much of good things". So Allah, when you do one may be give more than 10 000 even, if you continue like this. And to do good thing, it is not easy.

Hadith Sharif after, Khutba- Prophet (sas) said "Hell, there is curtain in front of hell. People can't see this, what behind this. Only this curtain, your bad desire". All bad desire, behind they cutting. You cannot see hell. Because there is all bad things, for killing people, and drink or using drugs, or you making bad thing, shahwat/egoistic desire. All this cutting, you cannot see what is behind this curtain. You see good thing behind it, it is easy. And many people, they said "it is easy to do bad thing". Very easy, ego like. Can go without tiring. Second part of Hadith he said, there is curtain for paradise. What is this curtain? It is to do good things. It is hard to do good things. This is curtain too. You cannot see paradise behind this curtain. Because if people, they can see what behind this bad desire, egoistic desire hell, they not go to do this. But it is closing this. For paradise also, to do good thing, it is difficult. Many people saying "we cannot pray 5 time. We can not even, "you said one time 2 rakats, even for this also we cannot do" they say. It is too difficult, too heavy. You feel tired.

Everything coming on you, to not do this good thing. Praying or fasting or giving sadaqa or giving zakat or going to Hajj. You cannot do. Very few people, they do this. Even in Muslim country, maybe 2 billion you cannot find 200 million doing this. Very few, maybe 10%, something like this only. So if they say how to finish from this, it is to be with mashayikh. To be with good people, you practice. You take your ego under your feet. Slowly slowly, you see & you believe to.. This is not seen also from weak belief. And when you are alone, not easy to get strong belief/iman. But in mashayikh, especially murshid/guide, Tariqat, especially these days very few you can find this. And he take you from down to highest place, insha Allah.

Alhamdulillah, you all find this. Alhamdulillah we all find this, Mawlana Shaykh. Alhamdulillah we are very lucky. Very lucky, and insha Allah, he getting insha Allah better. Insha Allah soon. Now he is coming to garden. Insha Allah, he will be coming to Juma also. He like to come insha Allah. Allah give him long life, insha Allah. Allah keep us with him here & hereafter.

Bi Hurmatil Surat al Fatiha.
