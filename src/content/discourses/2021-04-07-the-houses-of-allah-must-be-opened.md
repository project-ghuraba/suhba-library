Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

A’ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm:

(Qur’ān 72:18). Allah ﷻ states in the Holy Quran: ‘And [He revealed] that the masjids are for Allah, so do not invoke with Allah anyone.’ Mosques and Masjids are the houses of Allah ﷻ. The ruling of Allah ﷻ applies there. Allah ﷻ let us know this through our Holy Prophet ﷺ. No harm comes from mosques; no harm comes from the houses of Allah ﷻ. Only benefit comes from the houses of Allah ﷻ. If there is a trouble, it will be gone not by closing down the houses of Allah ﷻ but by opening them.

People can’t understand this and they are doing the complete opposite. When there is a problem, they go firstly against the houses of Allah ﷻ and they close them. What is there? Are there microbes? They are clean. People come in after cleaning themselves, to the Presence of Allah ﷻ. They don’t go to do something bad. They go in order to do something good, to beg Allah ﷻ. A prayer made in a mosque, a prayer made in Jama’ah (congregation) is twenty-seven times superior to a normal prayer [offered alone]. It is counted not only as 100% but as 2,700%; it isn’t an easy/little thing [in value]. However, there is no Iman (faith) now. There is Islam but no Iman. Everyone is a so-called Muslim but when it comes to reality, they cannot pass the test. They are failing it. Starting from the biggest to the smallest [test], we are failing them all. They are professors and all, studying and learning about Iman yet when it comes to applying it, they cannot do anything and they fail.

This trouble is all over the world now. This trouble will only go by begging Allah ﷻ, by repenting and asking for forgiveness from Allah ﷻ. Allah ﷻ is showing the weakness of people. They do one thing, it doesn’t work. They do another thing, it doesn’t work either. Only by relying on Allah ﷻ and asking for help from Allah ﷻ that this trouble can be lifted. Otherwise, it will continue. Shaytan is making up excuses in order to close down mosques. Who will benefit from closing the mosques? Shaytan will. There is no other benefit of it. If there is anyone who says that there is benefit in closing the mosques, let him come out against Allah ﷻ and he will see how Allah ﷻ will treat him then. May Allah ﷻprotect us. This world goes on with Duas (prayers), with the Duas of some holy people. Our Prophet ﷺ says: “If there hadn’t been these holy people, neither would it rain, nor would you have Rizq”, “نورطمت مهبو نوقزرت مهب”. For the honor of these holy ones, Allah ﷻ gives Rizq and makes it rain. And with their Barakah (blessings), these troubles will go.

Therefore, some people are doing these things easily and without [giving it] importance. Mosques are being closed. They say, “Let them close. We can pray at home”. Your praying at home is different. This has another big meaning. May Allah ﷻ protect us. You are showing arrogance and coming against Allah ﷻ. You have badness at home, you have sickness and microbes at home. Cure is from Allah ﷻ. Every good thing is from Allah ﷻ. There cannot be such lack of Adab. May Allah ﷻprotect us. We are asking for forgiveness and repentance. Astaghfirullah. People are doing it without knowing. May Allah ﷻ give them understanding. May Allah ﷻ give Iman to all of us insha’Allah.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq. Al-Fatiha.
