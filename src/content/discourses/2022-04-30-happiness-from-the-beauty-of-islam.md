As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

It is good to give happiness to the hearts of believers: "Idkhāli s-surūr fi l-qalbi l-mu’min," says our Holy ProphetNone. To give beauty and happiness to believers' hearts is great goodness. Goodness is written in the presence of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.

There is every kind of beauty in Islām. When good things are done, they are accepted in Islām. To do good things, to help and make all kinds of charity between Muslims is goodness, and it is beautiful. But evil is ugly. Therefore, more goodness and charity are made in the month of Ramaḍān. With invitations and ifṭār, it passes beautifully. We have come to its end now. Apparently, they still do it in some mosques – after the 15th or the 20th, they sing qaṣīdah bidding farewell to it [Ramaḍān]. They say goodbye to this holy month, this beautiful month. Of course, it is a bit difficult for some people. But even if it is difficult, its beauty is appreciated even more. By the favour of AllāhNone and the blessing of AllāhNone, the more it is difficult for people, the more virtues and happiness are given to them. Therefore, all kinds of goodness and charity made in this month are met with great reward in the presence of AllāhNone. And HeNone has rewarded us, in shā’a Llāh.

There is the last tarāwīḥ prayer tonight, and tomorrow is the last day of fasting. And afterwards, after ‘Īd, people go back to their old conditions. There is no suffering in hell in this month. Shayṭāns are locked up. We cannot count all the virtues of this month. These are very virtuous months, especially this third month of Ramaḍān. It was granted as the most virtuous to Ummatu Muḥammad by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Therefore, may AllāhNone bless it.

We bid farewell, as people never know if they will reach it next year or not. That's why they say goodbye to this month. May AllāhNone give it again. May it be even more beautiful with these beautiful things, in shā’a Llāh, with the light of Islām, with appearance of Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām, for this world is getting worse day by day. They say it is sinking. It is sinking badly. People have no hope. Only Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām will change everything. It is impossible otherwise. It won't work by putting a patch here and a stitch there. All of this system of shayṭān must be changed to the system of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla for people to be relieved. May AllāhNone show those days, in shā’a Llāh. We hope that next year Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām comes, in shā’a Llāh. This is our du‘ā’. Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim used to say to make du‘ā’ that Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām comes, that he appears, in shā’a Llāh.

To, "Idkhāli s-surūr fi l-qalbi l-mu’min," Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam – to put happiness in believer's heart, it is good deed for mu’min for Muslim. He must make people, believer or not believer also if it is not, if it is not touching our belief, we can make everybody happy. But for believer, this is Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, there big reward for this. But for everybody, as we said, not touching our belief we are not angry with anybody. But if they are touching our belief, we are not accept, we are not happy with them. This is another subject.

But our subject – to show Islām it is every nice, every beautiful thing, every good thing in Islām and Islām accept khayr, good deed, beautiful, nice, benefit for people. Sharr, who it is bad thing, bad deed – it is ugly, not good, not benefit, harming people.

For this, you put happiness in heart of people from beauty of Islām for Muslim to be happy, to make people happy also, in shā’a Llāh. This is main thing for Islām – to give light, to give happiness for people, to call them, "Wa-Llāhu yad‘ū ilá dāri s-salām" (10:25). AllāhNone calling people for "House of peace". Peace, which we cannot find now in world. But al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are Muslim. This month of Ramaḍān it is make people to be happy anywhere, any condition, they are happy because this is some miracle from AllāhNone for people to see this happiness for Muslim people even in this very bad situation, very bad time. But still Ramaḍān, which AllāhNone help people, it is giving happiness for people.

For this, in, there is custom, when make tarāwīḥ, they make qaṣīdah, singing, to be say goodbye for Ramaḍān. Every night they saying this, "Oh we will miss you, you are going". It is like good friend. He guest for us and he is leaving us. For this, every night we saying, it was singing. But now many, very few places they make this custom. But old time, they was making every Ramaḍān, after twenty of Ramaḍān, saying this. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh this is, we are finished nearly this blessed month which it is full of reward and barakah. For this we are hoping, we say goodbye [because] maybe next year some people cannot be in this life.

But we hope, in shā’a Llāh, we be to next year also to be with Ramaḍān better than this condition. Because world it is sinking, sinking down and down and down. No hope, no finish. Everywhere, they're hopeless, people. This because this system of this hypocrite shayṭān people making people to be unhappy. Only system of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla can make people happy. Under this system, in shā’a Llāh, with Sayyidunā Mahdī coming. We are hope, in shā’a Llāh, to next year to be with him. We must pray every time for this, Mawlānā Shaykh was saying all time. Other thing cannot anyone make peace or make good thing in this world because system they're like octopus. Everywhere you cannot make you to take a breath. What they want, they do and they do only bad thing. For this, in shā’a Llāh, we are not hopeless. We are... because AllāhNone promise us, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam tell us about this Sayyidunā l-Mahdī and ‘Īsá ‘alayhi s-salām. They will make this world again without problem, without any fitnah, any fasād, in shā’a Llāh. We hope, in shā’a Llāh, next Ramaḍān to be with him, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
