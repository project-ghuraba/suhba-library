Tuesday, May 3, 2011 Translation by: http://saltanat.org/ BismillahirRahmanirRahim The people have started moving. They are on the move; and they said, "Enough … Your term is finished. Your job is done, and your term is finished". So, if the tyrants react, even if they have countries, it is similar to a slaughtered animal: It is finished. Even if it is a slaughtered lion, as long as it is slaughtered, if it is still kicking its leg, it will not get anywhere because it is finished. Similarly, this world will be cleaned from oppressors. Those who cause injustice among people, will not live anymore. Even if there are billions, they will all leave. Because when the people are saying, "Leave!", they don't mean that only crazy Saleh should "Leave! Leave!" No! Saleh, the president of Yemen, is just one man among all the tyrants that belong to the age of tyrants. And when the people are screaming and calling and saying, "Leave! Leave!" in the streets, it is not only addressed to a particular individual. No! This is addressed to that one who is a representative of the age of tyrants. And people from east to west are asking and calling. When they scream, "Leave! Leave!", they mean for the age of tyrants and tyranny to leave. Allah Almighty made their tongues to scream and to yell in the streets, addressing the age of tyrants and the age of tyranny, not only presidents. No! The people mean, "We are fed up! Fed up!”. They are fed up with the time and the age of tyranny and tyrants. ”Leave us, oh age of tyrants!” - not only particular individuals. People are not screaming for only Khadaffi to leave, or for only the Tunisian president to leave, or for Husni Mubarek individually to leave, or for Saleh to leave, or for Bashar al-Assad individually to leave. The people do not want only individuals to leave, but they are asking for the whole age of tyranny and tyrants to leave them. Finished!

Anyone who was killed without having any means to defend himself is included in the meaning of this verse.
