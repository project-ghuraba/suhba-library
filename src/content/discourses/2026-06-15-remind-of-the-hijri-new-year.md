As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Today, shukr to Allah, is the last day of the year. It is the last day of the valid year, the Hijrī year, the Islamic year. This evening, with the adhān of Maghrib, the year begins. Everything is different in the Islamic year. All the acts of worship are performed according to this calendar. Therefore, may it be blessed and auspicious, inshā’Allāh. May Allah send a head (ruler) to the Islamic world. May he bring peace to the whole world. There is no other way to achieve peace.

These are blessed days. As we said, the month of Muḥarram begins this evening. It is the beginning of the year. It is called the year of the hijrah because of the migration of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. It is blessed; Allah has given it specifically to him. Everything, every day has its own manifestation, its own beneficence. They used to fast in Muḥarram before Ramaḍān. Ramaḍān (Fasting) used to be observed in this month; from the first to the tenth. Now, those who fast those days will attain great rewards. One will attain Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla’s favors. But the most important thing to fast is the 9th and 10th, or 10th and 11th. One can fast for as much as possible, but it’s crucial not to miss the day of ʿĀshūra.

People should take advantage of these blessed days and remind each other. When it’s New Year’s Day, according to the Gregorian calendar, they exchange congratulations and discuss what to do. They often forget the Hijrī Year. They don’t give it much importance, but shukr to Allah, the day of ʿĀshūra exists, and most Muslims remember it. It’s an important day. These days are days that our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam valued and recommended to us so that we may fast on that day. Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, in opposition to the Jews, said it should be two days, not just one; either the 9th and 10th or the 10th and 11th.

May Allah bless it, may it be of barakah, inshā’Allāh, may it be a means of goodness. May it be the cause of salvation for all humanity, inshā’Allāh. Today, these days are the days when the blessed Mahdi ʿalayhi s-salām is approaching. May Allah grant that his rule and the world of Islām’s salvation soon prevail, inshā’Allāh. May Allah bless it, and may this barakah be upon us again and again

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
