Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Allah ﷻ does not like liars and does not like those who slander. There is hell for them. We live now in the end of times, anyone can say whatever they want. Those who believe everything they hear are called idiots. If they hear a good thing, they do not believe it. If they hear a bad thing, then they believe it. This will cause Fitnah. It is called slander and defamation. If you backbite, you are saying what the man did. When it comes to saying things he didn’t do, it is slander, it is worse. Even though backbiting is a sin, Allah Azza wa Jalla says how much worse it is to slander and lie.

Therefore, a person who believes everything he hears without research commits sin. He will sin for no reason, for nothing, and he will be with the shaytan. In the end of times, they attack good people, but Allah ﷻ is with the good ones. Therefore, you should not be against Allah ﷻ, but be with Allah ﷻ.

Don’t be a partner in someone’s sin by believing everyone or looking at their sins. Let his sin be upon himself, and let him suffer for the evil he has done. He will surely suffer for it. Those who do these things have no faith, no religion. They want to deprive others of their faith and religion. We need to be careful. We live in the end of times, we need to look at everything carefully. We need to be careful. We need to protect ourselves and our faith insha’Allah. May Allah ﷻ protect us. May we be with Allah ﷻ insha’Allah. May Allah ﷻ make us among those whom he loves, and may He keep us away from conditions that he does not like.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
