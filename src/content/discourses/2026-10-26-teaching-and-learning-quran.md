As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam told about whom the best people are,<br>
خَيْرُكُمْ مَنْ تَعَلَّمَ القُرْآنَ وَعَلَّمَهُ
<br>“Khayrukum man taʿallama l-Qur’ān wa ʿallamah.”<br>
“The best among you is he who learns and teaches the Qur’ān.” [Bukhārī]

Those who learn the Qur’ān and teach the Qur’ān. Alḥamdulillāh, we see here a madrasah of the Qur’ān and many children attending. Childhood is the best time for learning the Qur’ān.

Alḥamdulillāh, you are here in a very good place with very good people. Alḥamdulillāh, they are following the sayings of Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, and they are teaching children from the beginning to read the Qur’ān. 

There is a saying—I don’t think it is a ḥadīth, maybe it is, I don’t know,<br>
العِلْمُ فِي الصِّغَرِ كَالنَّقْشِ عَلَى الحَجَرِ، وَالعِلْمُ فِي الكِبَرِ كَالنَّقْشِ عَلَى المَاءِ
<br>“Al-ʿilmu fī ṣ-ṣighari ka-n-naqshi ʿalā l-ḥajari, wa-l-ʿilmu fī l-kibari ka-n-naqshi ʿalā l-mā'i”<br>
“Learning when young is like carving in rock; and learning when old is like carving in water.” [al-Ṭabarānī]

It is said that knowledge in childhood is like carving into stone. Just as when we write something in stone using a hammer or a tool, when a small girl or boy learns at a young age, it is strong, and they do not forget it anymore.

Today, alḥamdulillāh, we visited many places. The first place was the Sultan’s place. There is a stone there that they wrote on, and after 500 years, it is still there and can be read. These people were sincere people. From their barakah your country became like this; keeping Islām better than most Islāmic countries. This is a favor from Allah. He sent these people and made hidāyah through them. And this hidāyah continues one after another. It continues, alḥamdulillāh; this is what we see.

This is important for all human beings. The Qur’ān gives every good thing because it is the word of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. When you read it, your heart opens, it is shifa for you, and there are countless benefits for Qur’ān ʿAẓīmu sh-Sha’n. For this, we are happy. We stayed the whole day. We have been listening, seeing new places, and everything is not stopping.

Alḥamdulillāh, they are making an effort to help more and more children learn the Qur’ān and teach people. Alḥamdulillāh, this is very good because in two years, it has become maybe three times bigger. Because Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam said that he whose two days are equal is a loser, and he whose today is worse than his yesterday is ruined. Because the next day, the second day, must be better than the first day.

For this, we are happy with the people who are following the saying of Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam and the word of Allah to serve the students of the Qur’ān and the students of knowledge. This favor is from Allah. Those who have this favor are lucky to do what Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla ordered. This is great luck for people: the luck to do good work for Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla.

Because nowadays, people don’t take the time to do what the people of the past did. When nighttime came, they would spend five or ten minutes thinking, “What have I done today? Was it good or not good?” Because many things happen during the daytime. So these people used to think about what good they did, what wrong they did, and what sins they committed. You must think about this and ask for forgiveness from Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. 

If they did anything wrong, they ask for forgiveness from Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla because, alḥamdulillāh Allah orders the angel on the left side, who writes down wrongdoings, to wait until the evening to see what this person does. If he asks for forgiveness, Allah will forgive it and not write it down as a bad deed. And of course, since we might forget, it is better before sleeping to make wudu’, pray two rakʿahs, say Astaghfirullah, and ask for forgiveness from Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla.

Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam says that if you pray these two rakʿahs, recite Astaghfirullah, and wake up before Fajr to pray at least two rakʿahs for taḥajjud, it will be as if you spent the whole night in worship.

Alḥamdulillāh, we are thankful to Allah for creating us in the nation, Ummah of our Prophet Sayyidina Muḥammad ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. We are very lucky. We are very lucky for this. We are lucky because before the Ummah of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, for Allah to forgive them, those people were ordered to do many difficult things.

When Sayyidina Mūsā ʿalayhi s-salām went to Tur Sina and came back, his nation was worshipping the golden cow. Allah cursed them, and when they asked for forgiveness, Allah said: “You must kill yourselves.” And they started killing themselves - killing, killing, killing - until 70,000 were killed. This is one example of how difficult it was to ask for forgiveness from Allah before Sayyidina Muḥammad ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam.

We just say Astaghfirullah, and Allah forgives us; very easy. This is very, very, very easy. And when you do this at nighttime: reciting Astaghfirullah and praying two rakʿahs - all your sins will be forgiven, and you will rest and sleep peacefully.

Alḥamdulillāh, we are thanking Allah for this. Inshā’Allāh, may Allah forgive all of us, and bless you and us, inshā’Allāh, for this meeting. Thank you for you. You are coming here for the sake of Allah. May Allah bless you and give you barakah, inshā’Allāh. Inshā’Allāh, may we meet again and again in good health and with more happiness, inshā’Allāh. Inshā’Allāh, you must think about the ākhirah.

Dunyā is not important; what’s important is the ākhirah. Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam says that whoever has his daily food, a house, and his ʿibādah is among the happiest people. May Allah, inshā’Allāh, give all of us the most precious treasure: Qanāʿah, to be happy with what you have. 

It is said,<br>
القناعة كنز لا يفنى
<br>“Al-qanāʿatu kanzun lā yafnā.”<br>
“Contentment is an inexhaustible treasure.” 

Meaning to be happy with what you have is an endless treasure. Thank you for you, and may Allah bless you.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
