As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm, alḥamdulillah,<br>
وَلِلَّهِ عَلَى النَّاسِ حِجُّ الْبَيْتِ مَنِ اسْتَطَاعَ إِلَيْهِ سَبِيلًا
<br>“Wa lillāhi ʿala n-nāsi hijju l-bayti man i-statā’a ilayhi sabīlā”<br>
“And [due] to Allah from the people is a pilgrimage to the House (Ka’bah) - those who can afford the journey.” (Qur’ān 3:97). SadaqAllahu l-‘Aẓīm.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla ordered Ḥajj for people. Those who can go, whomever Allah gives the opportunity, they go. It also happens that someone wants to go but is obstructed. But shukr to Allah, who is destined goes.

Therefore, thanks be to Allah, endless thanks be to Him. Shukr to Allah, we were destined again to go this year. We went and arrived in the evening. May Allah accept it. May Allah be pleased with us all. May those who haven’t gone go, inshāAllah.

Many went. Their intention was to fulfil the order of Allah. Their Ḥajj is accepted, inshāAllah. Because there were some who faced an obstacle on the way and weren’t able to go. Their reward is also great. They followed the way of our Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam.

Our Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam intended to perform Ḥajj and then turned back when they could not enter. Then, Allah granted our Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, and his Ḥajj was Ḥajj ul-Akbar. It is the Great Ḥajj when ‘Arafat comes on a Friday.

Therefore, for those who cannot go, Allah will surely give them according to their intention. We were granted it. It is Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla’s will. We had no intention. But even though we had no intention, we were destined later on and we went, shukr to Allah. Ours is now considered a nāfilah Ḥajj. When it is destined, you go. When it is not destined, you can strive and work as much as you want, you won’t go.

A year before our first Ḥajj, we ran here and there trying to go to Ḥajj, but it did not happen that year. We went the next year. InshāAllah may those who intended be granted to go. May Allah make it destined. May Allah complete it, inshāAllah.

Now many went but couldn’t perform Ḥajj and were turned back. The Ḥajjīs who were turned back were more than those who became Ḥajjis. Allah is Al-Karīm. His Generosity is endless. Their Ḥajj was accepted, inshāAllah.

May Allah perpetuate His blessings. Shukr to Allah. We must make shukr. It is a great blessing, it is a great virtue to go to that place. Visiting the house of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla and the surroundings of our Holy Prophet, and returning is one of the greatest rewards in dunyā, one of the greatest blessings. We must know its value. And of course, going and returning without appreciating it is not good either.

May Allah make us among those who know their value. Shukr to Allah for with shukr blessings increase.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
