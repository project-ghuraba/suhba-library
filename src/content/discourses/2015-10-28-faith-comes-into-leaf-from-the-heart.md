Allah Azza wa Jalla gave everyone a heart. Faith (iman) comes into leaf from the heart. For faith to leaf out, continuous worship and following Allah's orders is required. If these are done, faith strengthens and the heart is lit (with nur).

Faith exists in everybody. Some grow it with watering. Others leave their faith and that seed of faith in a bone-dry place, it turns into something of no use, and stays like that. We read the Hadith of our Holy Prophet (SAW) yesteray, “The heart is like the moon. It is dark when clouds are in front of it. If they go away it turns bright, it turns into light.”

People do not need anything else if there is light (nur) in the heart. Everything becomes easy. But when it is a dark and black heart, all kinds of evil can be found on that person. He is of no use to himself or others. If he is of no use to himself, he is of no use to anybody. That person becomes bad for everybody.

Faith is goodness, beauty, and light. Let us increase the faith in the heart with worship Inshallah. Faith comes into leaf even if a person proclaims the Kalima Shahadat. As we said, everybody has faith, but when they do not declare the Kalima Shahadat it stays like that, dries out, and is of no use. Faith comes into leaf and lights up with the Kalima Shahadat and with worships, Allah willing.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq.

Al-Fatiha. Hadrat Shaykh Muhammad Mehmet Adil (QS) 28 October 2015, Akbaba Dargah, Sabah Namaz
