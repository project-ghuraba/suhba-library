Waṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-F’āiz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-

Thanks be to Allah that today is another blessed Friday, a feast day for Muslims. Its virtues are so great that Allah 'azza wa jalla allocated this day to the Holy Prophet (SAW), and has given it as a gift to his umma. No one other than Allah knows the worthiness of this day. It greatly benefits us. There is a destined time to join when all prayers are accepted. Within the twenty-four hours, this destined time is an hour long. [Exactly] when or what time it is is uncertain, but dua should continue all day. If the dua happens to be at that appointed time, acceptance occurs.

Generally, Allah ‘azza wa jalla says, “Make dua; I accept dua.” Some say their dua are not accepted. That may well be, but it means that their dua will be answered in the Next Life instead. When they are left to Next Life there is such benefit in it that a man later says,“ I wish all my prayers had not been granted to me in life but Here.” Allah’s favor and grace, however, are great. He is generous so that we ask from Him for here and for the Next Life, insha’Allah.

These are days of hardship. Let’s pray that Allah helps us. May Allah protect. May Allah have the world of Islam go through this in peace and protection, Muslims and those who are close to Allah. Those who go against Allah certainly receive their penalty unless they repent. Instead of repenting, though, there are people who go even further [astray] in their action. This is because of the stubbornness of denial (kufr). Someone who is kafir is stubborn and does not accept the truth. A Muslim is the one who accepts the truth. Those who do not are not Muslims.

You need to accept the truth so that Allah will help you. May Allah give strength and protect us. We do accept the truth, and we accept that all is from Allah. May Allah favor us so that we come out of this in safety. What is important is to protect [our] faith. We all have to pass the test. Stay stable. Seek refuge in Allah, as we always say, by means of prayers, recitation, dhikr. This is a beautiful month, the month of the Holy Prophet (SAW). Insha’Allah, for the Holy Prophet’s sake, the umma will come out of this in safety. This Tuesday is the 15th of Sha’ban. That night whatever will occur [in the coming year] is written. May good be written down. Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-F ti ah
