As-Salāmu ‘Alaykum wa RaḥmatuLlāhi wa Barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu BiLlāhi Minash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. BismiLlāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm.

Wa ṣ-Salātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Akhirīn.

Madad yā RasūlAllāh, Madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi RasūliLlāh, Madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

Dastūr Mawlana Sheikh Abdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistāni, Sheikh Muḥammad

Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad.

1- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Make your last prayer at night the Witr prayer,” he says. Of course, the last prayer is the Witr prayer.

2- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “I was commanded to perform the Witr and Duha prayers, but they were not made obligatory upon me.” This means that whoever performs them attains the same reward as our Prophet, following his example. The Witr prayer is already wajib, but the Duha prayer is a Sunnah. Performing it is like giving sadaqah of the whole day.

3- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “I was commanded to perform the Witr prayer and the two rak’ahs of the Duha prayer, but it was not made obligatory for you.” These are not obligatory for you; they are for our Prophet. The command is for him. It is not obligatory for us. The Witr prayer is wajib for us, and the Duha prayer is Sunnah. One can perform it from two to twelve rak’ahs.

4- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “The Witr prayer is performed at night.” So, that three-rak’ah prayer is for the night. The night prayer is the prayer performed after the ‘Isha prayer, not during the day.

5- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Indeed, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla has added a prayer to your five daily prayers. This prayer is better for you than red camels.” At that time, red camels were the most valuable. So, our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says that this prayer is better than them; it is valuable. “This is the Witr prayer, which Allah has placed for you between the ‘Isha and Fajr prayers.” That is, you can pray it after ‘Isha until Fajr, before the Fajr prayer. But it’s better to do it before going to bed. If you say you’ll pray when you wake up after you sleep, you might be overcome by sleep and not catch the time. Therefore, let the last prayer be the Witr prayer.

6- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Indeed, Allāh is One, and He loves oneness.” That is, Allāh is One. Oneness is also the odd numbers: one, three, five, seven, nine, and so on. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla loves them.

7- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

نآﻟا ﻞھأ ﺎﻳ ،اوﺮﺗوﺄﻓ ،ﺮﺗا ﺐﺤﻳ ﺮﺗو ﱃﺎ ﷲا نإ

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Indeed, Allāh is One, and He loves oneness. So perform the Witr prayer, O people of the Qur’an.” All prayers are performed in pairs; two rak’ahs, two rak’ahs, four rak’ahs, two rak’ahs. The Witr prayer is the last prayer performed, and it consists of three rak’ahs, as Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla loves it. The Shafi’is pray it differently; they perform the Witr prayer as two rak’ahs, then give salams and pray one more rak’ah completing it. That is according to the Shafi’i Madhhab. As for the Hanafi Madhhab, we pray all three rak’ahs together.

8- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet is saying “The Witr prayer is only performed at night.” That is, it is never performed during the day; it is performed at night.

9- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet is saying “Perform your Witr prayer before the morning.” So, perform the Witr prayer before Fajr prayer is called. The best is to perform it before going to bed, not leaving it for the morning. If not, perform it directly after the ‘Isha prayer.

10- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet is saying “Hasten to perform the Witr prayer before the Fajr prayer.” So, if Fajr prayer is called, the reward of the Witr prayer is already over.

11- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet is saying “There are three things which are obligatory for me,” there are three prayers which are Fard for our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. “And Nafilah for you.” Nafilah means Sunnah. It is either Sunnah or Wajib. “The first is Witr prayer.” It is obligatory for our Prophet. According to the Hanafi Madhhab, it is Wajib. In some Madhhabs, it is Sunnah because it is not Wajib, but it is a Sunnah Mu’akkadah according to them. “The two rak’ahs of Duha prayer,” that is also a Nafilah prayer. “And the two Sunnah rak’ahs of Fajr prayer,” the Fajr Sunnah is also a Sunnah Mu’akkadah. It must be performed. It is close to Wajib, but it is a Sunnah Mu’akkadah, so it should not be missed.

12- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

ةاو ﺔﻌﻛر ﲆﺻ ﺢﺒا أ ﴙﺧ اذﺈﻓ ﲎ ﲎ رﺎﻟاو ﻞا ةﻼﺻ

Our Holy Prophet is saying “The night prayer is two rak’ahs at a time.” It is prayed two rak’ahs, two rak’ahs. “If one of you fears that the time for the Fajr prayer will pass, he should pray one rak’ah at the end. This will count as one rak'ah for all the rak’ahs he has already prayed.” Of course, as we said, when we read a Ḥadīth Sharif, it’s not like we can just say, “I read this, and I’m going to do it this way.” You have to understand its meaning; how the imams, the Maddhab imams, the scholars have explained and shown it. This one rak’ah is for the Shafi’i Madhhab. For the Hanafi Madhhab, as we said, the Witr prayer is performed as three rak’ahs all together.

The Messenger of Allāh says the truth in what he said or as he said.
