As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam is saying, "Do not think about the essence of AllāhNone. No one can comprehend the essence of AllāhNone." If you look at HisNone creatures, it is enough to see the greatness of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Creatures we see in this world, no one knows their number, how they live, what they do. Various creatures, things AllāhNone created for this world are different. And there are other worlds and universes. There are different people and different creatures there. If they are human or something else, only Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla knows. No one know except AllāhNone.

Angels and Prophets (as) show the Greatness of AllāhNone. And Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla showed each universe to our Holy ProphetNone. HeNone showed himNone HisNone Greatness on the Night Journey. Due to that Greatness, there is nothing difficult for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. "Wa-mā dhālika ‘alá Llāhi bi-‘azīz" (14:20). Nothing is difficult in front of the Power of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. There is nothing. "Kun fa-yakūn" (36:82). When HeNone says, "Be", it becomes. Without it nothing will remain. If HeNone says, "Don't be", everything will finish.

The Power of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is enormous. Mankind comes against it and tries to challenge it. People should know their weakness. We are weak. All of us servants must confess and acknowledge our weakness against the Greatness of AllāhNone. Who doesn't confess and doesn't acknowledge it, will be punished. It's okay. No one will care. Does a mosquito have any value? No. People who are against AllāhNone have no value either. They have as little value as the wing of a mosquito, says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.

It is farḍ, it is wājib for us, for all mankind, to be thankful for the favours that Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gives us. HeNone brought us to existence out of nothing. HeNone favoured us. Those who don't accept HimNone and go against HimNone lose everything. We read in a newspaper the other day that someone passed away. They wrote, "He lost his life." Who loses his life? A person who doesn't know AllāhNone and His ProphetNone loses his life. Who knows, doesn't lose it, he finds real life. May AllāhNone make all of us know HimNone in shā’a Llāh. May HeNone make us from those who know our own weakness and insignificance.

Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, heNone show us our, how we are very weak in front of Mightiness of AllāhNone. For this, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, don't think about Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla – how HeNone is, where HeNone is, what HeNone is. In Islām it is forbidden to think about this thing. Other religion, they are, they make by themself statue, they make picture, they make something like this. They say, this is God. No, God cannot be in your [or] anybody's imagination. Cannot be.

Real religion, from Ādam ‘alayhi s-salām to Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, whole Islām, they not accept to show God as staying in one place or in shape. This is impossible. For this, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam say, don't think how HeNone is. You cannot imagine this. Nobody can reach this. Even Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, the most highest, nearest for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, also heNone is not describe or not seeing Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Just heNone is different level but nobody can.

Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam said, "Don't think about this. Only think about HisNone, what HeNone create." In this world even, there is millions of creature we don't know – which one, what is this, what is that, how they live, how they're growing. In this small our planet, in this space, not even like one atom compare this. And there, there is millions of planets like our planet, AllāhNone create. HeNone is Creator, whole time create. Because if there is value, even like not mosquito [but] mosquito's wing, AllāhNone will not give them one drop of water – this Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. There is no value for them, only that just they think they are something.

No we are must know our weakness and be thankful, thankful for AllāhNone [that] HeNone create us and HeNone honour us. In Qur’ān say: Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm.
