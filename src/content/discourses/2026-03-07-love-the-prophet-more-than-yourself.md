As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
تِلْكَ ٱلرُّسُلُ فَضَّلْنَا بَعْضَهُمْ عَلَىٰ بَعْضٍۢ
<br>"Tilka r-Rusulu faḍḍalnā baʿḍahum ʿalā baʿḍin"<br>
"Of those Messengers, some of whom We have caused to excel others" (Qur’ān 2:253). ṢadaqAllahu l-ʿAẓīm.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says in everything He created there is the most virtuous. It means people are in ranks. Prophets and prophethood – there is Nabī and there is Rasūl. They are on the highest rank. And they have ranks between each other. The highest rank belongs to our Holy Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Among all creatures that Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla created; among angels, jinns and humans, our Holy Prophet is on the highest rank. He is the most beloved servant of Allah. Allah has wished so. He created our Holy Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam from His light so that his light becomes the means of guidance for people.

Barakah and all goodness comes for the honor of our Holy Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam to people. Therefore, honoring and glorifying him is our greatest worship. It has the greatest benefit. It is something that saves us. The love of our Holy Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, following his way, being with him, loving our Holy Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam.

Our Holy Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says, "You should love me more than your parents, your children and yourself." Our Holy Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says so. Does the Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam need your love? No. In order to have goodness for us from His compassion, to be closer to Allah He orders us to love him. Otherwise, our Holy Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam was given everything by Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. He doesn't need anything.

But as he said, "My ummah, my ummah," and thought about his ummah from birth till his death, it is to save them our Holy Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam asks for that love. People will be saved with it. They will be saved with his shafā‘ah. Who say they don't want shafā‘ah will perish. "I am ḥāfiẓ, I am ‘ālim. I am this and that." Don't mix these things. Those who say, "You love the Prophet too much, you are making shirk," and prohibit it will perish. They have no salvation. Because people can't reach anywhere with their actions. They can't move from here to there.

May Allah give understanding to people. May they attain the favours of our Holy Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. May they have his shafā‘ah. Otherwise, they can't be saved.

Being from ummah of our Holy Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam is the greatest honor. All Prophets wished if only they had been from his ummah. And only from few of them, Allah accepted their du‘ā’. They are Sayyidinā ‘Īsā, Khiḍr ʿalayhi s-salām, Ilyās ʿalayhi s-salām, and Idrīs ʿalayhi s-salām. They are the lucky Prophets as they will be in ummah of our Holy Prophet because they are alive. And they will reach this blessing.

Prophets have great ranks, as we said. People are in ranks. And the highest rank is our Holy Prophet's ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. And then come Rasūl and Nabī. Prophets have prophethood, and Allah sent them His books. They invited people to the way. And then there were 124,000 Prophets. Then come ṣaḥābahs. Ṣaḥābahs have ranks too. Their ranks are known. And accordingly, ranks go up. There are ranks above and below.

Therefore, we must know the value of everything. We must appreciate everything we are given. They are the favour from Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. All of them are a favour. Loving them, being with them, visiting them; all of that is for our benefit. We will attain high ranks. It will lead to thawāb for us inshā’Allah. May Allah let us reach their shafā‘ah inshā’Allah. May He make us from those who appreciate. May He not make us from cheated ones.

People are cheated a lot. Kāfirs are cheated from the beginning. Shayṭān is happy with them. And he is cheating Muslims now. Because the greatest enemy of our Holy Prophet is shayṭān. He dislikes him the most. Whoever loves him he strives to cheat him to remove this love. "No one is doing what you are doing. No one is worshipping like you. You are ḥāfiẓ. You are ‘ālim. Beware of making such shirk." He cheats people by saying so and leads them to heresy.

May Allah save us from his evil. ’Āmīn.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
