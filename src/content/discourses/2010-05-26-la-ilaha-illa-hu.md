Mawlana Shaykh Muhammad Nazim al-Haqqani al-Qubrusi, sohbat May 26, 2010.

Madad Ya Sultan. Allah Allah, Allah Allah Allah Allah, Aziz Allah Allah Allah, Allah Allah Allah Allah, Subhan Allah Allah Allah, Allah Allah Allah Allah, Sultan Allah Ya Rabbi zid Habibaka Sayyidina Muahmmad Sayyid al awwalin wa l-akhirin izzan wa sharafa, nuran wa surura, ridwana wa sultana aqbal ma'adratana Ya Rabbana

Thumm As-salamu alaykum ya Ibadallahi s-Salihin, those who have been granted holiness from the Lord of Heavens. We are in need of your support. And Assalamu alaykum Ya Qutb uz-Zaman. Assalamu alaykum Ya Rijal Allah, those who are giving everything for the sake of the Lord of Heavens. Through their good wills, they are saying, al abdu wa ma yamlikuhu li sayyidihi, aja`ib.

Audhu Billahi min as-shaytani r-rajim, Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim Ya Rabba l-ibad irham da'afana, irham dullana. We are weak servants of You, O our Lord. Weakest. If all creation is going to be in one atom, can it be? What is your opinion, O People? We are leaving common people, we are asking from those people who are claiming 'We are knowing Allah'. That is a question for all holy ones or those who are claiming that they are learned people and knowing everything.

Madad, Ya Sahib al Imdad - to make clear something that our Creator, He likes it to be known. Yes. Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. We are saying, O Salafi ulama, O Doctors of shariat, O so many - strange, they have such brightened titles about something that belongs to Islam and Islam is universal. And you are saying that Islam is a universal mua'ssassa, reality. Islam, that is reaching everything. Islam is reaching every atom in existence. May be material aspects - the smallest part of the material world, they are saying 'atoms' and then they are saying that atoms are such fawqu l-`adah, khaliqu 'l-`adah, incredible, Subhanallah.

Every atom is representing its Creator. When an atom is representing, that means man's understanding can't reach the knowledge that belongs to atoms. Each one must give a complete understanding according to its understanding level about its creator. Everything must be, O ulama, O learned people from Book-given People, you also, you must listen, you must try to understand. Don't argue. What we are saying is not for argument. It is only to say, 'Yes, that is true'. No argument on ilāhiyāt, divinity. Don't argue. If the huge appearance of this world could be divided smaller and smaller and smaller, you would reach a point that is impossible for it to be divided. Finish. Incredible, what is that? (Incredible, wondrous). Much more than - what you said? (Impossible to be divided). Hanh, that point. What does it mean? What's remaining? If something was there, it could be divided. That means the whole of creation is coming to the point of zero. A huge space. What we were saying – universe. You may divide, divide, divide, divide, and then coming to the point that you can't divide it. That means the whole of creation is coming to zero. La mawjuda ill'Allah! Understanding Salafi ulama? They are making me to speak on that line because you are claiming that you know something.

There is a brilliant ifadah, brilliant 'speak' or declaration, expression. That is in Arabic words, you must understand, what has been said, ism`a, O ulama listen, fafī kulli shay’in lahu ayāh. This is an ocean - each thing, even to last point, where it is impossible to divide it - fa fī kulli shay’in lahu ayāh tadullu annahu wāhidun! Subhanahu wa Ta'ala. That is an ocean, Subhanallah, tadullu annahu wāhidun, that means everything is witnessing that it has been ordered to do what is its mission.

Every (thing) unable to be divided, impossible to be divided, every one must do its mission. What is their mission? Saying: He only is in existence! Allah Almighty only, and nothing else. That is the meaning of: la mawjuda ill'Allah. Everything is giving its witnessing and disappearing, la mawjuda ill'Allah. Can't be in existence ghayrAllah, that is impossible to be divided. But everything in existence is reaching the last point and at that last point they are giving their declaration that only He is in existence, nothing else, and finishing.

Where are they going? Whoever is making that declaration is going to divinity - wahdāniyat, Unicity Oceans. Disappearing. As a drop coming from clouds is reaching the ocean, its identity just disappears - identity only of the ocean. You must try to know something they are saying. O ulama, Azhari ulama also and Christian learned people, Jewish holy people, what you are saying? No comment on it. Finish. We are coming and going from Unicity Oceans, appearing and giving our declaration, annahu huwa wāhidun and coming, running into Divinity Oceans.

Therefore such knowledge is giving to servants an honour. And we are not in need to come for a second time to be tried or to be asked to declare annahu wāhidun - enough. That is a simple explanation. Therefore the Seal of Prophets, Sayyid al Awwalin wa l-Akhirin, Sayyidina Muhammad sallAllahu alayhi wa sallam, when he was asked about afdal ul-`amal, you understand Arabic or not? Leave to be Wahabi or Salafi no, come and try to be muwahhid! Muwahhid - who says: la ilaha illa Hu. Come to that point, hanh. Accept this.

When the Seal of Prophets was asked what is the best work or best action for a person? Afdal ul-`amal, the highest point of effort - what is that? The Seal of Prophets was saying: al `ilmu bi'Llah. People were becoming surprised. For what they were becoming surprised? Because they were asking for something that servants should do, and his answer was that it never belongs to the efforts of our physical being, that it is beyond it. Beyond, because they were asking for something that belongs to our physical being and its efforts. The answer of the Seal of Prophets is so high because what we are doing physically from ibādāt, worshipping it is so – majāl, - y`ani maydan hal li yajul alayh - a horse when it is going to run it has an area, that is the area, its range. Everything that belongs to our physical being and its efforts that is a kind of area that we are in but this station that the Seal of Prophet's was talking about, that is beyond our, beyond our efforts. Yes, the drop coming and it has a majāl, it has a being through its creation. When moving from its appointed point, leaving and coming and reaching an ocean and its identity is disappearing. Only remaining the identity of the Lord of Creation. Clear, you understand, American?

That is important and that hadith nabawi sharif is such an ocean saying, afdal ul-`āmāl. What can you do that is a more valuable action through your whole life say? Azhari ulama, what is that? Ulama Azhar Sharif or learned people, east and west? What is to ride on horse, or to run, or use that horse to reach the Divine Presence? This horse – it is not important for it to be horse, to be donkey or to walk, or to run, but for what you are using that one? For what are you praying, for what are you fasting? For what are you doing so many majabihin nabi, that which prophets were bringing and teaching?

Is that the real target for us? No! On the Seal of Prophet's Night Journey, he had a ride from Makkah mukarrama up to Quds sharif, yes. What do you think was the real target? Was it to ride on Buraq? Then after Buraq going up, what is that, merdiven? Stairs. Jibril bringing and saying with this you may go up, one after one, one after one, but it's not the real target. Until reaching Divine Presence, when Jibril saying, 'I can't move even one hair's (breadth) from here up to there, (if I do) I am finished. But you O Muhammad, sallAllahu alayhi wa sallam you can pass. You are matlūb. You are the only one that is invited to that Divine Presence, not anyone else. That Ocean is only for you, not anyone else.

Therefore what we are doing is not our target but the real target is to reach those Infinity Oceans in the Divine Presence. There you are going to say: La ilaha ill'Allah, La ilaha ill'Allah. La mawjuda ill'Allah. La mawjuda illa Ant. We must learn, we must try to learn, it is not such a simple/ordinary thing we have been granted. We have been granted such a big grant that only He, Who granted it to us knows. That is the beginning for understanding, astaidhu billah:

wa laqad karramna bani adam (17:70).

What is real takrīm? What is real honour that is granted to Man? We are knowing nothing! O Allah, forgive us. We are running through earthly pleasures and running to reach some aspects from material aspects. Forgive us and open for us something so that we may understand something. When we are understanding something, only then we are going to be through the Divine Oceans of wahdāniyat. May Allah forgive us. Assalamu alaikum attenders, where are you? Work, work, for dunya. You are working, you are amusing yourselves. As animals are amusing, you are amusing yourselves. And you are so proud, you are saying 'We are Mankind'. For what? Because we are amused with dunya, with material aspects. Just as the animals' world is amusing itself with that. It is blame to all mankind, if they are not understanding their real aspects. May Allah forgive us.
