As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

May it be of goodness and barakah, inshā’Allāh. As we always say, dergahs are like oases in the desert. Everywhere has become like a desert. What is a desert like? They may speak of “greenery” and the like, but in terms of religion and spirituality, the land has become barren—a desert. There is nothing there. People’s souls are parched; there is no one to offer that water of Allah’s mercy—except for these dergahs.

This is a land of disbelief; yet even in places that are not lands of disbelief, spirituality has vanished. But these places here are in even greater need; they are oases.

Whoever comes to these places benefits, because they were established solely for the sake of Allah. They were built not for personal gain, but for the pleasure of Allah. They were created so that the faith of all Muslims—young and old, men and women—might be strengthened. This is the fundamental purpose and aim of the dergahs: to teach the religion and show the true path.

Especially the Naqshbandī dergahs.

Of course, other dergahs are the same, but no one can reproach the sincere Naqshbandis with even a single word. Shukr to Allah, the Naqshbandi ṭarīqah is the very essence of the sharīʿah. It encompasses all sharīʿah, Sunnah, wājib, fard, and mustahabb, as well as the duʿās, awrad, adhkar, and salawat exactly as our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam demonstrated. That is why this path and these dergahs are beneficial; the greatest benefit lies within them.

Of course, you should also go to the mosques. However, there are many places where there is no mosque. In these dergahs, one can both perform prayers and acquire knowledge. Of course, there are separate days for women and for men, and there are days for children as well.

May Allah be pleased with you. Let them continue on this path shown by the mashāyikh and the instructions of Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim. May Allah be pleased with you.

As we have said, whoever comes will benefit. As for those who do not come—if they wish to go elsewhere, let them go; yet, the importance of dergahs is immense. Therefore, wherever there is a dergah or a mosque, it is essential to go there—not even missing a single week. They must go, for in doing so, they bring barakah and benefit to themselves, to their families, and to those around them. May Allah be pleased with all those who contribute to this. May Allah increase their numbers.

Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim, as we constantly say, we heard that and everyone also knows—he intended to make forty thousand Zāwiyah, Dergah and told to do so. […]

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
