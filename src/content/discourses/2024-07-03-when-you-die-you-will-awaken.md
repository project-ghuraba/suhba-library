As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

“Wa-hum fī ghaflatin mu‘riḍūn” (21:1). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l- ‘aẓīm. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says, people are in heedlessness. Because of heedlessness, they are turning away from goodness. They don’t want to get close to it. When in heedlessness, they think their lives will never end. And there is nothing else. But when they reach ākhirah, they will be surprised, says Sayyidunā ‘Alī. Sayyidunā ‘Alī, karrama Llāhu wajhah, says, “People are asleep.” Life passes like a dream for people. When do they wake up? “People wake up when they die.” People wake up every day in the morning, but that is different. The real sleep and heedlessness is in dunyā. People say they will do this later or there is no such thing, and go through their lives in heedlessness, deceiving themselves.

Dunyā does not stop. Whether you are heedless or aware, you only gain by avoiding heedlessness. If you are heedless, you may open your eyes and see that qiyāmah is happening, or you may close your eyes and wonder what has happened. Then these things will have come true. Everything will become clear. People will feel regretful and remorseful because they can’t do anything anymore. But that will be of no benefit anymore.

Those who did something will say, “I wish I had done more.” Those who did nothing will say, “I wish I had done something.” Non-believers will acknowledge that what these people were saying is indeed true. But if they say, “Let’s do something,” they can't. Because that opportunity is now over, it’s gone. Life has passed. Life doesn’t stop.

What is beneficial in life, what is valuable in a person's life, are the things he has done for ākhirah. For the things he has not done, he must seek forgiveness, mercy, and beneficence from AllāhNone. But people, especially the people of this time, never approach these things because they are arrogant. They have become more arrogant than shayṭān. At least, shayṭān acknowledges AllāhNone. These people do not acknowledge AllāhNone. They do not accept HimNone either. As we said, they live as heedless and deceived. When they close their eyes, they will see everything clearly. By then, it will be too late. Nothing will be of benefit.

Therefore, may AllāhNone protect us. May we not be heedless. The heedless person is at a loss. They say that a Muslim must be alert. Our Turks say that a Muslim must be alert, but in terms of which alertness – if he obeys AllahNone, then he is alert. Otherwise, alertness for the sake of dunyā will not be of much use. There are many foxes and many cunning ones. If you are alert but go to bed, they will strip you bare. That is not important. What is essentially important is that they don’t steal your ākhirah. May your ākhirah be secure. When you close your eyes, it is to attain Allāh’sNone mercy, Allāh’sNone consent.

May AllāhNone not make us all among the heedless ones. May our hearts not be heedless of AllāhNone and the Prophets of AllāhNone, in shā’a Llāh.

There is āyah. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Wa-hum fī ghaflatin mu‘riḍūn” (21:1). Saying in Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n. Meaning: the people, they are not awake. They are busy with thing. It is make you away from reality. Away from what you must do. You very far. You are far as much you can be far, and you don't want to be near this, to push this reality. “Ghaflatin mu‘riḍūn,” meaning: you are in away from reality and don't – you don't want to listen or to ask or to anything with – nothing to do with this day, you say. This people, they are saying, “This time, we are living in this time.” This meaning, they are in ignorance and running away from good thing. AllāhNone call them for good thing but they are, they don't want. And they push this, “We don't want this.” This is our whole human being like this. But this time, it is more difficult. More people, they are doing this. And what – but when they will be awake from this ignorance, there is time – everyone, they will know the reality, the truth.

Sayyidunā ‘Alī, karrama Llāhu wajhah, he said, “An-nāsu niyāmun, fa-idhā mātū intabahū.” Meaning – whole human being they are in sleeping, they are … Our life like sleep. Sleeping, whole people, they are sleeping like a dream. When in a dream, when you will awake? When you close your eyes. Your death coming for you, you close your eyes. That time, it will; you will open, you will see. Without opening your eyes, you will see. That time you will awake, but your body, everything, your eyes, closed. But that time, you awake and seeing, “Oh this was – this is the reality, this is the truth. This is what we are, we – they are saying about, telling us about this thing and we are not listening. We – that time we are sleeping. Now we are awake.” You are awake but no benefit for you from anything because your body not moving, your tongue not moving, your hands, your eyes, nothing moving. No any action for you to do anything. That time you awake, but awake with only for be regret or to be happy. That time, the reality, it will appear, and many people, they be regret but no benefit for regret. Crying or doing sadness, nothing you – benefit for you. You must be awake in dunyā. Even you are not awake, only to be obey for AllāhNone, to be not be regret in that time when you awake and see reality.

There is now, we are living real tough time for believer. Whole world become non-believer. Even who calling themselves Muslim, most of them also, non-believer. Only by doing something as shape, but in reality, most of them, they don't have this real belief what Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, saying. Because, many people, they make them to doubt about ProphetNone, about Ṣaḥābah, about Ahlu l-Bayt. And with this, they are not believer. But when they be awake from this “ghaflah”, they will be seeing whole truth. There is time - Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, by HisNone wisdom, HeNone make this for people, but what meaning to be believer? To believe in what you not seeing. But there is time for not what we didn't see, to see it. But that time [is] not when you are here. When you are – second when you close your eyes, you will see and you will be regret. AllāhNone keep us safe from this shayṭān and his follower. To be awake, in shā’a Llāh, or at least to be with good people, to be seeing when you close your eyes what they, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, promise, what Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, what mashāyikh, they promise for you from good thing.

AllāhNone make this true for us and keep us away from this non-believer people, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
