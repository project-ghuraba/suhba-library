Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

A’ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm:

كَ سَ مْ َتسْ ا دِ َقَف ِللََّّ اِب نمِ ؤْ ُيو

(Qur’ān 02:256). Allah states in the Holy Quran: ‘The right course has become clear from the wrong. So whoever disbelieves in Taghut and believes in Allah has grasped the most trustworthy handhold with no break in it. And Allah is Hearing and Knowing.’ Truth is clear and falsehood is clear. Those who believe in Allah and are connected to Him win. ‘Taghut’ is unbelief, it means the oppressors. Those who do not submit to them and do not believe them but are with Allah are the winners. Their rope is strong, so hold on to that rope so that you’ll also win in the end. Those who are with the oppressors are always in harm. They harm themselves and others because they no longer have conscience, Iman, or anything left anymore. They don’t care about anything, even if all the people are gone so that they achieve their purpose. They don’t think about anything but their egos. What they do is also wrong. Those who believe them and are with them are hypocrites.

Hypocrites existed during the time of the Holy Prophet too. They’re in the lowest pits of hell because you can [clearly] know who the Kafir is; he is in front of you. But you can’t know the hypocrite. They go and tell the enemy, the Kafirs, what you’re doing. Their harm is much greater. That’s why, even though our Holy Prophet knew who they were most of the time, Allah didn’t want to reveal them. He wanted them to repent themselves.

Even since that time, they were amongst our Holy Prophet. Now they are a hundred thousand times, a million times more. They think they will gain something, but they are losing. They say that they are Muslims but they are hypocrites. The hypocrite is the one who harms Muslims. They can’t harm Islam but they’re harming Muslims. Yet that harm is actually to themselves. The torment they inflict on Muslims is written as a reward for Muslims but as a sin for them. Don’t be fooled by others and become hypocrites. Don’t let your religion go in vain in the end. Because in the end it’s going to be a heavy burden. We should be careful of this.

The most important thing is to follow the path of our Holy Prophet, and oppose those who are against Him and not to follow them. Unfortunately, people are being deceived. And when they are deceived they think that they’re right. What is right is clear. Allah states in the Holy Quran: "ِ ي َغلْا نَ مِ ُدشْ رُّ لا نَ َّيَبَّت دَق", ‘The right course has become clear from the wrong.’ (Qur’ān 02:256). The truth is clear and the false is clear. Apart from this, the person who sees the truth as false is foolish. And the one who sees the false as the truth is also mindless. The truth is like the sun. Thank Allah, He has shown it to all humanity. But do not follow those who close their eyes, are blind, and say that there’s no sun. May Allah protect us. May Allah help us and may He strengthen our Iman.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
