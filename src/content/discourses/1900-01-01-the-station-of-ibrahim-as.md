Maybe 100 years old or 80 years old or 70 years old. Whole life passing through kufr. But it was enough to come to RasulAllah (saw) and to sit, to be present with him. Even one moment looking to him, accepting him, carrying everything from him. Therefore, in most distinguished Naqshbandi order meeting and to be present with Sheikh is so important. Daily is excellent. If not, weekly. If not, monthly. If not, even annually must be. Must be.

Hajj pilgrimage once in a life, visiting Baytullah, the house of Lord Almighty Allah. Who representing Allah Almighty as His khalif, as His Almighty's deputy, is not less than visiting the House of Lord. Therefore, everyone must look after that point. Allah Almighty inviting people to visiting His house, House of Lord. And He is saying ordering also Asta'idh billah:" ittakhidhu min maqami Ibrahimi muṣalla" (2:125) (and take Ibrahim's station for a place of prayer). Ordering to look maqam Ibrahim, the station of Ibrahim, and to make rakatain, 2 rakats there. And He knows what He means. It is not only that we are saying to look after maqam Ibrahim, that place for making 2 rakats. But important who is going to be on same station of Ibrahim, peace be upon him, and to pray with him even 2 rakats. Urging His servants to look after that person who he is on same station, on same step of Ibrahim, alaihi salam, peace be upon him.

But people running to Mecca Mukarrama to look after House of Lord. But mostly they are going to look after that building. What is the meaning, if nothing in it from that building? You are going to a castle, to a palace to look after it, or who is going to be in it? You are asking? Everyone looking that palace, going coming. But very few people meeting with the owner of that palace. And they are lucky people whom reaching to the owner that palace. Or everyone looking out, everyone coming to Ka'ba, looking that building. But very few people is going to meet with the Lord of that house. That is important.

Hasan al-Basri just came to Baytullah, the House of Lord, and looking, no one there. Haqiqat-ul Ka'ba (the reality of the Ka'ba) is not there, wasn't there. Haqiqat-ul Ka'ba who he is appointed by Divinely order to welcome the Lord's servants. When he came, looking, no one there. And he asking where is, where he is. Then hatif (invisible caller) calling to him and saying just now [he] left for to make welcome Rabiat-ul Adawiya. Rabiat-ul Adawiya, that waliya (woman saint) coming, therefore, he went to make welcome to her.

Only very few people, they are meeting with that one who representing haqq through Ka'ba. And mostly people going to look that building and making tawaf and returning back. But real purpose for calling people there, invitation, the wisdom or secret of invitation to come and to find that one who representing the Lord Almighty Allah in Ka'ba.

Or we are going, coming. So many camels going and coming. Don't be like camels. Perhaps camels may understand more than ourselves. Perhaps camels they have more feelings about that Divinely appearances in Ka'ba l-Musharrafa. Yes. Therefore, we must look after someone whom representing the Lord Almighty Allah for our destination, for our health, for our advancing and to be able to reach our destinations in Divine presence.

www.saltanat.org Page 1 Therefore, association with Sheikh [is] one of the most important pillars of tariqat-ul Aliya. Because 'bidunu' without that, no tariqat. May be short one, may be tall one but baraka comes same. Allah gives us from the endless mercy oceans, from His endless baraka and blessings.

Bi hurmati-l Habib, bi hurmati-l Fatiha.
