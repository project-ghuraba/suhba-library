Look the bird came.... poor bird came.

1944 they migrated from Russia to Damascus. (Hj Anne's family) 75 years. 75 years...Yes Sayidi. La Ilaha illa Allah When they arrived I was in the city, Hums they came to Damascus, I was there & Allah (swt) made Hj Anne to be my wife. G: Sayidi, Mawlana Shaykh Abdullah engaged you with Hj Emine?

M: Of course. Shaykh Abdullah Daghastani, sent a message from Sham while I was in Cyprus saying, "I have prepared for you a righteous wife so you must come to us with a dowry... with her dowry. I prepared it & I went to Sham. In Sham, his(the great nephew's) grandmother Karima Hanim, the sister of Hj Anne. The household they said... "for Hj Emine" Mawlana Shaykh pointed for her. My household said, "but her sister's age is more suitable for you." They said this to Mawlana, & Mawlana Daghestani he got very angry and said "I am looking at the Preserved Tablets & it is showing that she is your wife! My son Shaykh Nazim, his picture and Hj Emine's picture together & that's why I am talking. You understand! Idiot one." Idiot one...

May Allah (swt) forgive us.... Hj Anne served me in a way that no one could ever be like her for me. May Allah (swt) bless her soul & raise her station. And I remained here. O poor one... Fatiha.
