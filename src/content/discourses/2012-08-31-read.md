As-Salamu Alaikum wa Rahmatullahu wa Barakatuhu. Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fil jamia' Whatever is destined by Allah is happening. It is the Divine Will that put us here to speak to you a few words. Insha Allah it will be Khayr/good for you & for us.

Speaking like this to people is not easy for us, it is rather difficult but the other day we read something somewhere & said "Ok good. Insha Allah we are like this". Hz Uthman Thun-Nurayn, the 3rd Caliph after the Prophet (sas), 3rd of the Rightly Guided Caliphs, his public speaking skill also was not very good. And he had haya/shyness. When one has haya, he is not very comfortable in front of people, even forgets what he was going to say most of the time. But he said a beautiful speach. He said "May Allah make us a just imam, make us rule with justice. Not make us a qail/lecturer imam". Qail means talkative, lecturer. Much talking & words, but no action.

Hz Uthman, may Allah be pleased with him, he was Dhun-Nurayn, married to the two Nurs, two daughters of the Prophet (sas), the two apples of his eye. He was such a holy person, he said like this. What is important is instead of speaking much which has no use, better to speak less but wisely.

Today we speak about this, which is a big deal in these months. It is the whole talk of most people in the world, before there were no such things. Everybody is thinking now, about how to educate their children; "what can I do, which school can I put him into?" Of course in Islam the first order is "Read", knowledge is the highest level- The best of you is the one who learns Quran and teaches it to people. The biggest virtue, thawab is this knowledge. Our Prophet (sas) said this from the beginning to teach about the knowledges of Quran. Even in the first battle, in Badr, when they took captives, some didn't have money but they were literate. So he (sas) told them: "teach 10 kids & you will be free". So it was more important than money.

Islam gave such an importance to knowledge. Islam is the knowledge. It is the real knowledge, Islam. There is no knowledge in anything else. Now most people look, and start putting their kids into school from kindergarden. And the kindergarden, most of it- 95% of it is public school & 5% just started recently are good. They teach Quran & some Arabic, Arabic alphabet. They teach Holy Verses. So they are good but the rest, 95% starting from this early age, just like they vaccinate trees & they become something else when they grow, they instill dirty things in children this way. Therefore 95% of kids going to these kindergardens their parents are not doing them good. They are harming them. Now it is obligatory to send children to school but before that (1st grade) don't send at all. Don't send them in vain.

But what happens now, people become lazy & get used to it. They send their children to Hoja in summer & Masha Allah in 1 month they learn how to read Quran. After they learn Quran, the schools starts & all this is gone. The Hoja taught well but at home his parents don't sit next to them- even if they don't know themselves, only be with him & say "read 2 lines, read half page, read 1 page". If they continued it, he wouldn't need to go next year, also wouldn't forget.

But people are lazy. And this kindergarden is popular- ladies send their children because they are lazy. When they send to kindergarden, they are comfortable at home, they go around. They don't have a headache from the children's noises, chaos. Masha Allah there are very good, pure teachers, Hojas who teach in schools. Most of them teach as a service to the nation of Muhammad (sas) rather than doing it for money. Meaning "these kids learn & in akhirah their dua helps us". There are many teachers like this, many from mureeds also, such teachers, in school. There are many like this. Even the ones who taught our kids were like this. They advise people about their kids. Meaning "How this kid doesn't become lazy. How he doesn't fail at the end of the year. How he can understand what he reads better.." And they say "I am teaching this kid very well in school. When he comes home from school, stay 5 minutes with him & he tells you what he has learned that day. If he repeats once at home he doesn't fail & won't have to repeat. No need at all".

Why are we saying this? Not to lecture you but people in the end of times because there is a big emptiness. They are used to being lazy. Before women, there was no electricity, or even when there was electricity, there were gas ovens. They would cook in wood fire. There were no washing machines so they would wash by hand. So cooking, washing..everything would take hours but still they were more hardworking than these people. Now they have everything in front of them but can't even look after their children. They say "We will get a private tutor". A kid who is smart & will study doesn't need private tutor. What they say is wrong from beginning. As we say, shaytan has built a system & put all people in the machine, all of them the same- just like meat grinder, all come out the same. They have nothing else to think about. Their kid is studying, gets to his 20s-30s, he is yet to go to college. "What university should we choose? This one requires that much payment, that one that much.."

A person who goes to college has no use anyways! Even who is smart enough, it is still of no use. Meaning, these are useless knowledges. If you for sure have to send for school, then he may learn the Word of Allah, Word of the Prophet (sas). He looks for this. He looks for Hadith. He studies things that will have a use. No matter how hard you try, a person who won't study, won't study. Therefore don't try in vain. Who has an inclination to work, may work. If he has no inclination to work & is very smart, then you can send for college. And that is without him depending on you, Like hiring private tutors, sending to special courses. Then his parents are doing everything. And with the amount of money they spent that kid could have great capital in his hand. Saving that money, he can open a shop or start a business for himself, more than enough. But for his studying expenses, parents sell their shops, lands, houses.."What can we do, we spent on the kid's education expenses".

And masha Allah their kid, a young man, as it is said "crazy blood", crazy. He is mingling in all kinds of places. If his parents saw the things he did they would regret it all. So what we are trying to say is there is only one knowledge in Islam & it is the one taking you to Allah, to the Prophet (sas). The rest are useless things. If it is for earning livelihood, who can study, may study. For who have no such talent, Allah granted rizq/provision for everybody, and different abilities for everyone, not like these people do- making everybody the same as if they come out of a grinder.

Most of the time such great awliyas, such scholars came from the most remote town, most remote village. The teachers, hojas teaching in that village looked & said "really, this is a special kid". And the scholars, meaning the sincere scholars, not like the scholars of this time who showed up recently & misguide people, but the sincere scholars who have taqwa, fear of Allah, are gathered with Prophets (as). so be careful about this.
