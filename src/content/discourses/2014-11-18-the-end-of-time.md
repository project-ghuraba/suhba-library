As-salāmu ‘alaykum wa rahmatullāhi wa barakātuh. A‘ūdhu billāhi min ash-shaytāni r-rajīm Bismillāhi r-Rahmāni r-Rahīm As-salātu wa s-salāmu ‘ala Rasūlinā Muhammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad ya RasūlAllāh, madad ya Sādāti Ashābi Rasūlillāh, madad ya Mashaykhinā, madad ya Shaykh Abdullah Fāizi d-Daghistāni, madad ya Shaykh Muhammad Nāzim al-Haqqāni, dastur. Tariqatuna as-sohba, wa l-khayru fi jam'iyya.

The times in which we are living are the last times. The Last Day has drawn near. People say: there is still plenty of time. In the Holy Qur'ān Allah, ‘azza wa jalla, says: Iqtarabati s-sā‘at (54:1), the Last Day is approaching, says Allah, may His Glory be exalted. We cannot say that it is far off. It is near, by permission of Allah. All signs are pointing to this. Resurrection, that too is a condition of faith. After death there is resurrection. Whoever does not believe this dies without faith.

Therefore, forever - this world will not go on forever. It has its time, all things have their time. As they say: kullu ātin qarīb, As far away as it may seem, as long as it might take, what is coming is near. Because it is coming. Years are passing. One year, two years, ten years, 100 years have passed. Hundreds of years have passed. From the time of the Holy Prophet to this very day. The Resurrection is near, he said.

Our Holy Prophet said: "The best of all ages is my age," says our Holy Prophet. They call it the blessed Age of Bliss. Together with our Holy Prophet there are four khalifs: Hz.Abu Bakr, Hz. ‘Umar, Hz.‘Uthman and Hz. ‘Ali, they make up thirty years. These 30 years form the Age of Bliss. After that, "The age that comes after mine is the best." That was the time of the Sahāba, and the Tabi‘īn, also. After that, the age after that, he says, those first times, the first ages were the best. After that people began to multiply more and more and corruption spreads. People are becoming corrupted, it is getting worse and worse. Yesterday was better than today, today is better than tomorrow, they said. Shaykh Efendi used to say this.

Now, what can we do? Our Holy Prophet has given us good tidings: "Whoever keeps my sunna alive, whoever follows Allah's command, will receive the reward of 100 martyrs." This means, in these difficult times, a person who holds on to his religion, will receive great rewards. He will be granted great blessings. There are difficulties, but the reward for these difficulties will be a very great one. It is a bargain. Very great saints have hoped for this: "If only we could be alive in those times." At that time it was not difficult, the Holy Prophet said to his Companions: "If you leave out 1 of 100 things, you will be blamed. But if the last of the Umma do only 1 of 100 things, they will receive the total reward for all the 100 things."

These are the times we are living in. Allah has given us to live at this time. Because we are on this path, on Allah's way, in tariqa, because of our love for our Holy Prophet, we must give thanks. It is a great thing, also to wait for salvation. To wait for the glad tidings the Prophet brought us, that carries another reward. As he says: Intizar infaraja ‘ibadati. Our salvation is Mahdi, ‘aleyhi s-salam. When he appears it will again be like the Age of Bliss. The whole world will find peace and justice. The whole world will worship Allah. There will not be a single one of His servants denying Allah. Of course this too will only last a certain time.

This too will pass. After that things will again go wrong, and the Last Day will overcome the disbelievers. But as we have said, the important point we wish to make is that in our times there are so many difficulties. So many kinds of troubles. If you have patience with these and do not separate from Allah's way, then you may reach the rank that the Sahāba were hoping to attain. You will receive a very great reward and divine favours.

May Allah grant all of us, our families and children, the gift of faith, and may this faith grow stronger, insha'Allah.

This time it is time of end, near Qiyama, Judgment Day. It is calling this "iqtarabati s-sa'at" (54:1) - Allah in Quran He is saying, Judgment Day it is approaching. It is very near. Many people they are saying, "No there is many time." Even Muslim people they are saying this. Who not believer they say maybe we have a billion years to finish this, finish the world. No! Everything Allah put it, He put time for everything. And what has limit in time, it is near. kullu atin qarib (everything that is coming is near) they say in Arabic. What it is coming, it is near because days, months, years, 10 years, 100 years, quickly going. But now it is really the end of time, very near for Judgment Day. And it is difficult time, for everything it is difficult.

Time of Prophet (saws) it was the best time. Prophet (saws) he said "The best time - my century." And his century it is until 4 Khalifa after him - Sayyidina Abu Bakr Omar, Uthman, Ali - 30 years. It is one century with, they accept like this. After 2nd century it is also good. 3rd century good, after becomes bad. Everything going worse and worse and worse because people getting away from Sahaba, from Tabi'in/followers, from these people. Everyday Mawlana also he was saying, "Yesterday was better than today, today better than tomorrow." So it is like this. We are coming the time every bad thing is happening. We hear and now they are showing also what happened never happening before. The bad thing that happened now, they say, from everything. What we will do?

It is time what Prophet (saws) said "when people they are getting very bad and Muslim ones doing my sunnah, Allah rewards them as 100 shahid, martyrs." And it is this time because it is a very difficult time. And Prophet (saws) he was telling his Companions "If you do from 100 things, 99 and you didn't do 1, you will be blamed because you didn't do 1. But these people (of the last days) if they are doing 1 from 100, they will be rewarded like you." They asked why. He said because it is a very difficult time and they are doing - Allah accepts from them what they do. If doing very few even, and they believe and they are doing this for love of Allah and Prophet (saws), Allah accepts from them like 100. And really every shaytan, every bad thing - fighting, attacking people to do bad thing. Even who is coming for mosque or coming for Sheikh, also 100 shaytan attacking him to not come to this good place. But it is now trade, trading time. Especially to wait for faraj, opening. It is worshipping what is opening, but Prophet (saws) he gave good tiding of Mahdi (as). Because when Mahdi (as) comes also it will be like his (Prophet's) century. Everything it will be peace and justice and baraka. And to wait for this, we are waiting, every second you get reward, every minute you get reward for this. Because we are believing what Prophet (saws) promised and we are waiting. And he will come insha'Allah because every coming it is near.

So we are waiting insha'Allah to meet him. Because it is the end of time and it will be the century of happiness like Prophet (saws), until some years - maybe 10, 20 years. After also beginning to people to get bad and Qiyama they will...Judgment Day it will blow on them. But for Muslims it is good. For non-believers it will be bad. And Allah keeps us in this way, good way. Because if you don't have any hope it is. you are not believer. But Alhamdulillah Allah gave us belief and He gave us hope, good hope, to be happy even in worse conditions. We are happy because we have hope and we believe in Allah and what will be it will be good for us.

Wa min Allahi t-tawfiq, Al Fatiha.
