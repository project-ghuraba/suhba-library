Audhu Billahi min al shaytan al rajim, Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. May Allah protect us from the evil of shaytan, from the evil of enemies & evil of the government. You came with goodness, may you return with goodness as well.

Today the holy month of Mawlid al-Nabi, Rabi'ul Awwal has started. Alhamdulillah, we reached the end of the month of Safar safely. So many people within the month of Safar, facing musibat (affliction) left dunya when the calamities of Safar descended on them. Alhamdulillah that this heavy month has passed by. Now our Prophet's (sas)... Zid'hu Ya Rabbi izzan wa sharafan nuran wa sururan. After the name of the Prophet (sas) is mentioned even if we stand up for 100x, it is right & well deserved.

G: This one came with us and asked to see you as well.

M.The boy? Masha Allah, Masha Allah. Visiting the salihin (good ones), one mu'min visiting another pleases Allah Almighty. Before there were Shaykh Effendis, Masters, Awliyas, Ulamas (scholars) who were worthy of visiting, kissing his hand & asking for his dua. Now they are finished. A person from Eskisehir had come to visit me. That person told me "I came to visit you, Shaykh Effendi". I said to him "I may ask you something now. Eskisehir- I passed through Eskisehir many times, would take half an hour to drive from one edge of the city to the other at that time. Now maybe even 1 hour is not enough. The city has grown. That was an old Effendi.

I said to him, "Look, you are a man who has seen so much in his life. I may ask you something. In this city of Eskisehir is there anyone whose hand is to be kissed, who is to be visited&asked for his dua? Is there such a person, whose door & table is open for everyone and who looks after the poor? Is there such a person?" He said to me" Shaykh Effendi, no such person is left. None left. Leave aside Eskisehir, today even in Istanbul there is no such one left- whose table is open to everyone & who is to be visited. There are some people in some places, and they say "some guests, some good ones may come to visit us, that they leave us some pocket money". They wait but no one comes. Their tables are not spread. Their doors are closed, tables are covered. We are in such a situation" he said. I said "What can we do then? You couldn't find there (such people in your land).

Whoever told you, told you & you came to this deserted place to visit me". Compared to such people, we had become zero. We are a person who has become zero. But you came all the way from Islambol. Allah bless you, and your good thought about us- May Allah Almighty verify the husnu dhan (good opinion) you have about us. And to where are we sending the ones coming to us, O heavenly ones? "Go and find them in the graveyard. All who you look for lie in graveyards, in turbas. Go to them, why do you come to me? I have nothing. I am a man who has become zero. Why do you come to me?"

But Allah Almighty is sending them- Friends of the Prophet (sas) are glorifying You. Go to him, there is such a person in such a place who works for Us. But beware, he also gives anyone who visits what he deserves". Ok. When they come, I swear at some. Is this good? You give permission?

We got permission to swear from the Heavens. I said before already, "The Pen is lifted from me. The angel on this side, this one, does not record anymore. This one records the good things but this one does not, no matter how much I curse. It is like this. Welcome to you, you came with goodness. "Wa innama li-kulli imri` ma nawa- Each man is [rewarded] only according to that which he intends". (Hadith) "We are going to visit a Holy one. We are going to visit Shaykh Effendi, to kiss the hand of an awliya.." One comes with such an intention- according to his intention, he may look to me, Allah gives his reward".

Welcome O ladies, how are you? Insha Allah you are good. G. Alhamdulillah, how are you? M. Alhamdulillah, we may be thankful for our situation because the time now is the end of times. All that appears is from it's signs.

The last day is not far. Now the Sahibul Waqt Mahdi (as) will come. After him, Prophet Isa (as) will come. G: These are my neighbors. M. Welcome. Affarin to you, you did well. After that, a big war will start. Mahdi (as) stops it. Prophet Isa descends. He rules for 40 years. After this, his soul is taken too. Qiyamat-Last day will fall on the unbelievers. Aman Ya Rabbi. May You not make us from them. We say "we are Your weak servants".

Therefore now your awrad, meaning your practise is: Don't let a day pass by without prayer-salat! Something may come to you. We don't interfere. If something comes on a person who doesn't pray even 7 headed awliya (strongest) do not interfere. "Who does not make sajda-prostrate, even 2 rakats is not My servant" says Allah Almighty. Aman Ya Rabbi. Aman Ya Rabbi.

"Those who dress inappropriately (exposing their bodies) are not from my Nation" says the Prophet (sas), for women. La hawla wa la quwwata illa billahil 'Aliyyil 'Azim. What should we do? We will say now what we can do, to be saved? "Rabbuna Allah Hasbuna Allah". People may continue with this. It is enough for them, along with sajda-prostration. "Whoever does not pray even 2 rakats is not My servant, not from the ummah". For ladies "Who dress exposing their bodies are not from my ummah". "To whom do they belong, O Prophet?" "To shaytan. They are from shaytan's group. They have their name written in shaytan's book-

Her hair, head uncovered. Exposing her body, marketing herself. These women are not from me & I not from them" saya Fatima Al- Zahra (as). If something happens to such ladies, even all the doctors gather around her they will say "we can't do anything for her. No. There is no cure for this one." "What should we do then?" "Take her home. Her time is up. Make her place ready in the graveyard". This, their inappropriate dressing and exposing of their body brings so much punishment upon people.

Don't go out unless necessary. Islamic women, Muslim ladies- don't go out, to markets, etc.. Astaizu Billah "Wa Qarna Fi Buyutikunna" (33:33) Gather in secret corners of your home & recite Quran there. Listen to the word of the Prophet (sas). Keep your homes, your family & children and Allah keeps you. If not there is a battle now which will wipe out,not millions but billions of people.

We seek refuge in Allah. Fatiha. Amin. May your end be in a good way. Go in safety. Be in safety.
