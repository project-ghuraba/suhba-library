As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh. A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh wa-shukru liLlāh. No matter how much we offer thanks to Allāh and praise Him, it is not enough. Our shukr must be endless for being created as Ummatu Muḥammad, as ummah of our Holy Prophet. We must offer shukr with every breath and praise Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Our shukr is endless for being created from this beautiful ummah and for being given this favour. Mu’mins must know this. Those in ṭarīqah, especially, must be the first to know it, that the greatest favour is to be from the ummah of our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, to be on the way of Allāh, to follow the beloved servants of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Indeed, if you ask people about the state of the world today, they all say they have never seen anything worse. Regarding this world, Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim used to say, every coming day is worse than today. This means the state of the world is not good, not beautiful in worldly terms. Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam said about this time, “It is the time of fitnah.” This is the time of fitnah. But for believers, it is the best time. How can it be good among so much oppression, among so many troubles? Because Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla has willed it so. We cannot interfere in Allāh’s work. His wisdom cannot be questioned. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla cannot be questioned: “Why did You do this?” There are many who think themselves very clever. They ask, “So many people die here, so much oppression happens there. Why does Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla do this?” The very words you say – may Allāh protect us – are the greatest kufr according to the normal ruling of Islām. Because, astaghfiru Llāh, you speak of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla as if He were a normal sulṭān, a president, or a government minister, oppressing and doing such things. Everything happens by Allāh’s will. Nothing happens outside His will. Therefore, as we said, a Muslim must not object. They must offer shukr to Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. They must not interfere in His work. It is also not proper to say, “He did this, so this is his punishment. He did that, so this is his reward.” You must not interfere in the work of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. You must look to your own state and your own nafs. Do not concern yourself with anything else. Other matters are by the will of Allāh. We live in the end of times. This is the manifestation of the end of times. You must believe in this. You will be at peace, and Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla will be pleased with you. Certainly, we don’t approve oppression. We can’t approve any oppression that is done. But we can’t interfere in Allāh’s will. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla does what He wants, and doesn’t do what He doesn’t want. No one can interfere in His will. No one can change His will. Therefore, mu’mins must know this. They must be grateful for their state so that Allāh makes them firm. That is, with this matter, a person – with thoughts, or – even if his thought is not good, but a thought is nothing. If something passes through a person’s heart, if he has waswasah or thinks of something, unless he says out loud, “If only it were like this or that,” then it is not important. Shayṭān’s work remains inside then. It has no ruling. But if they come out and confuse people’s minds, saying, “Why is it like this and not like that? Why is there oppression?” everything has its time. “Wa-l-‘āqibatu lil-muttaqīn” (28:83). The outcome is for the pious. We must know it. We must wait for it. Nothing goes without account. Everything will be accounted for, down to the smallest particle. “Fa-man ya‘mal mithqāla dharratin khayran yarah, wa-man ya‘mal mithqāla dharratin sharran yarah” (99:7-8). If one does an atom’s weight of good, he will see its reward. If one does an atom’s weight of evil or oppression, unless he repents, he will find its punishment. Therefore, mu’mins must follow the way of our Holy Prophet. They must know his value. Those who know his value will gain. Those who don’t will come like grass and go like grass. He will lose his life without attaining anything. They say, “He lost his life.” It is a newly popular phrase. It fits some people exactly, but it does not fit others. The one who lives an empty life in this world, with play, amusement, companions and friendships, and useless actions and useless deeds, thinking he gains much, will lose all his life. May Allāh protect us. May we know the value of our lives, in shā’a Llāh.
