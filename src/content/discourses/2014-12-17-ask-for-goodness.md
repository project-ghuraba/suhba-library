Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Our Holy Prophet (SAW) states: “Interpret everything as good and you will find good.” Allah Azza wa Jalla created every good and bad. He also gives goodness to those who obey Him. He gives evil to those who do not obey and go astray from the path.

Today is the last Wednesday of the month of Safar. The month of Safar is a heavy month. Our Holy Prophet says about this month Safarul Khayr so it becomes good. Allah willing, things done with the instructions of Shaykh Mawlana (QS) turn good. As long as you recite three Kalima Shahadat, 300 Astaghfirullah, seven Ayatal Kursi, seven Surat Feel, and give sadaqa throughout the month of Safar, Shaykh Mawlana used to say, “May its goodness be for us and its evil be on them.”

Evil comes to those who rebel against Allah, torment people, and do evil. Goodness comes to us. Do not forget, ask for goodness in everything and expect good. Hope for Allah to give its good to us. May its evil be upon those who rebel against Him and oppose Him. May it be on those who are anti-Islam. There is not a minute Shaitan is asleep. Our ancestors said, “Water sleeps but the enemy never sleeps.” In the same manner, those with Shaitan think day and night, “How are we to cause confusion in Islam and harm it?” And we have, thank Allah, seeked refuge in Allah. We wish for Allah’s help and compassion. He does not turn back. We want goodness Inshallah. May goodness come to us continuously. If there is good, may it happen, and if there is evil, may it be cleared away. Let us be under self-protection.

We have purely seeked refuge in Allah. Allah would protect us. This is our belief and our creed. They are trying with things that they can do, but everything is in Allah’s hands. May Allah protect from their evils. Whatever there may be, may it be protected. May the good of today be for us and its evil be for them too.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq.

Al-Fatiha.
