Assalamu alaykum.

Allahumma salli wa sallim ala Nabina Muhammad alayhi salam Salatan tadumu wa tughda ilayh Mammarra layali wa tul ad dawam Allahumma salli wa sallim ala Nabina Muhammad alayhi salam Salatan tadumu wa tughda ilayh Mammarra layali wa tul ad dawam Allahumma salli wa sallim ala Nabina Muhammad alayhi salam Salatan tadumu wa tughda ilayh Mammarra layali wa tul ad dawam

Wa tul ad dawam, that means forever. As long as we are breathing, let's say, Ya Rasulullah we are greeting you and our salatu-salam to you. Let's have this intention. It's very important to make your intention, it's more important than your doing, that means amal, because, to put it in your heart, it is like the architect who makes the project of the building, the drawing of the building and this is his intention. Sometime this drawing will come alive, but sometime the owner of this project doesn't like it, says, 'Different one', but still the drawing is there.

So that means the intention is the project of what you want to do. And it is all the time accepted by Allah Almighty. Even you didn't make it as a physical being but Allah Almighty accept it as your 'done'. He says 'It is done'. In your book, this is in our book, it is done. This is important. To happen is very important, and specially for lazy people like us the intention is very important, so that's why all the time, making big intentions and I believe that Allah Almighty is accepting it and we say 'Ya Rabbi, You are the Generous One, You never think like us, You never treat us like us, You are Allah Almighty. You are the Most Magnificent, You are the Most, Most, Most Generous. You are never calculating what You are giving.

We are the only ones calculating. If we make today $10, or €10, it will not be enough for living it will be going, half will go for telephone bill, half will go for electric, half will go for one sandwich, so will be shortage, so will be €12 or $12 or 12 whatever. This is small calculation. Will never end up with the big huge things. That's why they say 'You have to think big', whatever. Must be big, must be important thinking. Allah Almigthy never ask you to think about 'how to spend my day', where $10, or L10, or 10 cents. Walk and see what Allah Almighty will give you from His generosity. This reminds me of the story of Grandshaykh when he immigrated to Damascus, he have in his pocket 35 cents. 35 cents of Syrian lira, in those days, qirsh. Even not one. And he went. In those days, hotel, it was inn. So he went and he rent one room. He entered the room. He was with his wife and Abu Bakr, his servant. Called ‘servant’ but he is not, actually he is mureed, khadim. And he entered and check his pockets, he have a.. with the zip in those days, zzzipp.. open it, he calculate coming, 35 cent. 'Abu Bakr come, this 20 cent you buy tea and with the rest you buy sugar'. He spent all. Nothing for the hotel room. This is all his money. He say, ‘Abu Bakr, he take’. His servant doesn't care what the boss is saying and they don't know the language. They are in Arab country they don't know even what is 'water' in Arabic. They went for the grocery shop. They give him, he say, 'chai' and 'shuker, shuker'. Arabs say 'suker'. He give him.

And there is one man looking at Abu Bakr and his heart is melt to Abu Bakr. Looking, 'white beard, turban, I never see such a person in Damascus, in Sham Sharif'. Looking and he tell him, try to speak to him. He cannot speak and finally, he say.. Abu Bakr say, 'Not me, not me, I have my Shaykh, I have my Master'. It's not me'. That man was more melting. Say, 'If the servant is like this, what about the Shaykh?' He say, 'Okay, where are you? You know, this is tarzan language. Everyone know this language. 'Follow me', he say in tarzan language. He follow him and they knock the door of Grandshaykh. 'Ya Sayyidi, there is somebody wants to know you'. He say, 'Bring him, we are waiting for him, Ya Abu Bakr, this is the one who was supposed to look after us'. We finish everything. Must be someone to look after us.

I am working for Allah Almighty, it is not easy job. If you are working for Allah Almighty, don't worry about your income, don't worry about your living. If He is the One to give you the living purpose, He will give you the reason to live. He gives you the reason to live, means He will give you the income. Maybe one day He will tell you, 'You must be fasting this day'. For Allah Almighty this is, you never know and maybe He tell you, put a lot of things in your store you never know from where it's coming and this is the tawakkul for Allah Almighty. He is all the times saying, 'You, make your things first'. He doesn't deny you from working, he doesn't deny you from selling and buying but, He say 'Do your thing and then make tawakkul'. That means, 'Depend on Me'. 'Depend on Me'. It is very important.

So many people say, 'Ah, we do this, this, this, but it fails'. 'We do that thing but it fail'. Maybe it's not yours, not belonging to you. Then the Grandshaykh accepted that man, wearing the traditional wear with the Fez and Arab traditional. And he was one of the most respectable persons in Sham Sharif and he was one of the quiet, wealthy ones in Sham Sharif and he immediately come and take bay`a from Grandshaykh and was looking after Grandshaykh for a long time. Subhanallah. See how Allah Almighty brings His servants to look after other servants belonging to Him. That's why we are asking from Allah Almighty, 'Ya Rabbi, make us belong to You so we don't have any worries about our living'. Maybe we have some difficulty in our life-journey, maybe we have good things in our life-journey, maybe we have things not going well in our life-journey. Its name is 'journey' and when you are in travelling, if you are in car, maybe sometimes the car will go to final destination without any problem. Sometime the car might be broken, sometime people have accidents, sometime people have to rest somewhere, sometime people robbed. It is a journey, you never know what is coming. That's why we say we have to depend on Allah Almighty and say, 'Ya Rabbi, we are belonging to You, so make our life journey easier'. Because the life-journey is very hard.

Now I speak like this because I will become 50 in a few days time. So now I'm old man. Before we been speaking more easy because we have the flame of young things in our blood, but now we start, 'Oh, oh, it's not like we are thinking, it's changed, it's start to change'. That's why I'm not saying we are older, I'm saying we are wiser. Good one. When I'm looking at my beard, it's coming whiter, but now saying, 'wiser'. It is the life-journey. Life-journey is all the time being difficult.

But to be honest, these days, as a comfort, the possibilities that are being given to us, it is becoming easier. We can go anywhere, we can come anywhere. We can eat everything, very important. Go back 30 years, not much, 30 years. Before 30 years time maybe the chicken was some kind of imagination. We remember that when very important guest coming they been slaughtering the chicken for the guest. Important. Today if we tell the children, 'Today we are eating chicken', what they will say to you? 'Again, chicken?' Again chicken? Yahu, chicken for the honourable guest. Chicken again! What is this?

Now this is one small example. Alhamdulillah, we say Allah Almighty He give everything but take the barakah. There is no barakah. That's why they say, when Sayyidina Muhammad (sas) asked 'Ya Jibreel, after I pass away are you coming to this planet again? Say, 'Yes, I will come 4 times'. Say, 'What's your reason for coming, there will be no prophet?’ Say, 'I will come and I will take the barakah. And then I will come again and I will take the Justice. And then I will come again and I will take the Mercy. People don't have any mercy anymore. And then I will come the fourth time and I will take the haya, Shame. I will take the shame from people, they will not be shy from anything. And you can see.

But as you see there is too much of everything. They say we have economic crisis. In this time in the world, never happened to have gold as today. All the centuries. In this time, never happened you have money as today. In this time never happened to have building as today. You never have dressing as today, you never have shoes as today. We remember in our time we have shoes 2 times a year, for Eid-ul-Fitr and for Eid-ul-Adha only. And if we have bonus around that one it will be 'Wahh..' So many people say 'We sleep with our shoes when we have new shoes'. Today, everyone including me have 10 shoes at home. At least. I have my friend who was having one full.. with shoes. What you are doing with those shoes? 'I love shoes'. I love shoes. This is somebody loves who loves something, you never know what they love. Maybe when he was young he didn't have any shoes so.. he made one.. of shoes. This is his love -the shoes.

But we say 'Ya Rabbi, let us love you. Either we have all these things around us, either we have this happening around us and Alhamdulillah I am saying if you are listening for coffee talk like this, I am sure you are loving Allah Almighty too much to carry me talking to you, nonsense, but I say this is because of your love to Mawlana Shaykh and this because your love to Rasulullah (sas), and this is because of your love to Allah Almighty, so you can listen to me. Otherwise you would run away, even me, I would run away.

And with this one I say, wa min Allahi tawfiq bi hurmati'l Habib, bi hurmati'l Fatiha.
