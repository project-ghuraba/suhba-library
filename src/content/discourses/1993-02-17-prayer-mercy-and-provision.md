It is a government under the rule of the Anglican church. And the queen is the head of the Anglican English church. It is a queen now. The queen is the head. When there is a king, he is the head. The state is built on religion. It is an empire. Is it clear? It's not secular, that means they believe in God. The queen has made a decision for people of different backgrounds, groups, gender, every religion and faith that everybody should pray. "My citizens should pray. Either in church, or synagogue, or mosque, or temple; wherever they want, they should pray." We should ask mercy from Allāh Almighty. She said "I order you". We were there during this month of Sha'bān.

I said "We also must pray as Ahlu l-Islām, as Ahlu s-Sunnah wa l-Jamā‘ah. Certainly our prayers will be accepted. Ramaḍān has come. You will be rescued." Because it will be mercy, it is awwaluhū Raḥmatun, wa awsaṭuhū maghfiratun, wa ākhiruhū ‘itqun min an-nār. Mercy will come down. It's unknown whose prayers Allāh Almighty accepts. But we will pray as Ahlu l-Islām and Ahlu s-Sunnah wa l-Jamā‘ah. We went out and made a prayer in that big square. Allāh Almighty requested it.

Mercy has started coming down since the beginning of Ramaḍān. Consequently, hundreds of people are honored with Islām when they see that mercy. I said "It is Ramaḍān. As there is such salvation, we'll pray. Apart from the benefit, with Ramaḍān, we'll be praying" Allāh Almighty accepted the prayers. If we don't work for it, how will you sustain? How and where will you live? If Allāh doesn't send your provision from above, what will your money give to you?

They found some treasure, Ḥajjī Efendi. No one could estimate its quantity. When they opened it, it was full of jewels. And on top of it there was a cover from a doe skin with a writing on it "O man who holds this treasure, listen to my story. I was a king who owned this treasure. Allāh Almighty tested us with hunger. He punished us with hunger so that we searched for a bite to eat. And I, the owner of this treasure, filled a basket with gold and gave it to my soldiers.

I told them to travel around the country and give from this gold to anyone who has something to eat and to bring that food. My soldiers travelled. No one was able to give anything. Nothing could be found instead of bread too. Nothing that could relieve my hunger was ever found. They returned and said no one gave anything. I told them to empty it each of you fill jewels of the same value as a basket of gold and leave. I filled each basket with jewels worth a whole treasure from my treasures and sent them again to find something to eat and to bring if anyone has it. They travelled again and returned. They said no one gave anything because they don't have. While being an owner of this treasure, I couldn't find a bite to eat. I ordered to crush those jewels and turned it into dust. I swallowed that dust and drank dirty water that I found. That filled me and I died. I am the owner of this. I wrote it as a lesson here."

Is it clear? You are people living in abundance. But you don't value food. You don't keep your food. Take a lesson from that. ‘Alayhi ṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salām found a piece of dry bread from barley in the corner of his holy room. He took it up from there, blowed on it and ate it. Then he said "O Fāṭimah, or ‘Ā’ishah" whoever was there. He told one of them to look well after food. Because if food gets upset and leaves, it's hard to make peace with it again.

Where is the way and adab of ‘Alayhi ṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salām and where is our attitude towards food? How they throw away that food with tissue papers at once. They put tissue papers on that food so that no one can eat it. These people are get used to such bad character. Allāh Jalla wa ‘alá doesn't make jokes. We have to respect Allāh Almighty. To look well after food is to respect Allāh who is Mun‘im. You must keep even a bit. They would use an apron in old time not to drop anything. And if something fell down, they gave it to birds. It wasn't like yours down the drain. All your leftovers go down the drain.

Rizq. They impute blame on Allāh Almighty regarding rizq. They say "If we didn't have salaries, we'd die". Where is Allāh? Where is Razzāq? Doubt in provision creates doubt in the Provider. Provider is Allāh. And because there is doubt in Allāh, so many people we see, they talk about this matter of provision. What is the attribute of the believer? Our Shaykh Sulṭānu l-Awliyā’ used to say so. If the sky is covered with steel and if the earth becomes all rocks, the one who worries about his provision has no faith.

Allāh is saying "I will give. Let the sky and earth be closed, why do you care? Kun fayakūn. I will create." That's why, don't run after salaries. Try to increase your knowledge for Allāh. And try to transfer it to those who don't know. "Takaffala Llāhu rizqa ṭālibu l-‘ilm." Don't forget this ḥadith sharīf. Allāh Almighty has a guarantee of provision to all people. He says "I am the Guarantor to all creatures. Especially, I guarantee the provision for those who ask for knowledge. Khālis, khālisan li wajhi Llāh." Do they give you Ta‘lim al-Muta‘allim? Did you read it? Did they make you read? If your teachers make you read it, it will be so good. It is a very nice book. of Imām Ghazālī, it awakens. They are great teachers. Who asks for knowledge, Allāh Almighty guarantees their provision.

Since the beginning of it al-ḥamdu liLlāh we weren't given what others have. And we haven't repeated what others do since 50 years. That is the way of big mashāyikh too. They are connected, He make them to be served. If you are in service of Allāh, He make people run towards you. They say "Take. It's yours". Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq. It's enough. May Allāh forgive us.
