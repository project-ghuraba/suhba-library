As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
وَإِنَّكَ لَعَلَىٰ خُلُقٍ عَظِيمٍ
<br>“Wa-innaka la-ʿalā khuluqin ʿaẓīm”<br>
“And verily, you (O Muḥammad) are on an exalted standard of character.” (Qur’ān 68:04). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla praises our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, saying, “The best attributes are in you.”

In Qur’ān ʿAẓīmu sh-Sha’n, Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says about our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, “The most beautiful character, the most sublime character is in you.” Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam is the best example for us. He is the example for all of humanity. How a person should become a perfect human being and how they should live—in short, humanity itself—is found in our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam.

Those who do not follow him possess neither humanity, nor goodness, nor any other beauty. All kinds of evil reside within them. Because the way they follow is the wrong way. They are on a way that opposes Allah; not the way of Allah.

Therefore, the way that will save and benefit humanity is the way of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. That way never changes. It is the right, straight way. Nothing is beneficial if one is not on the right way. It drags a person to a place with no end.

The way of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam leads directly to Paradise. Never deviate from that way. Follow that way so that you may win. Gaining the Hereafter is far more important than gaining this world. Whoever sacrifices the Hereafter for this world is foolish.

Therefore, as Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla has said, our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam is the person who guides people to the highest degree. We learn goodness, adab, manners, and all kinds of beauty through our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam.

Shukr to Allah that we are from his Ummah. This is the greatest blessing Allah has bestowed upon us. To be both a believer and from the Ummah of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. Endless thanks be to Allah. May Allah keep us steadfast on this way.

May He grant guidance to those who are not on this way, and may those who have strayed find the right way, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
