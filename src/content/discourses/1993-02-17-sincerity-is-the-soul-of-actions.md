Their love is abundant during their time too. I said "We couldn't arrange for you... I didn't know you were coming here. You showed up suddenly." Grand Shaykh Efendi, may Allāh sanctify his secret, spent seven years in khalwah and riyāḍah in dargāh of Shaykh Ḥasan al-Jibāwī in Shām Sharīf. We were in Homs then. And it was the last years of the war. When people became scared from the French and wanted to get rid of them, The French started bombing. They targeted at Homs and they targeted at Damascus. I was a neighbor to Khālid ibnu l-Walīd (ra) then.

I was making seclusion and riyāḍah for a year in Homs. That great ṣaḥābah had accepted us. May Allāh be pleased with them, especially with him, Khālid ibnu l-Walīd, Sayfu-Llāh. I used to make morning adhān there. Sulṭān Ḥamīd, may paradise be his abode, made an exceptionally beautiful mosque there, big like Istanbul Mosque with domes and two minarets from the other side which look like the ones on Valide Sultan in Aksaray or Laleli. I used to walk up the stairs to the minaret for morning adhān. I would make adhān Muḥammadī. There was a lot of virtue.

They bombed it too. Then they bombed Damascus. When a bomb was coming towards the dome of Yaḥyá ‘alayhi s-salām, one holy man from Ahlu Llāh saw a hand reaching out from dome. A big bomb was coming towards his holy dome. They saw him. He caught that bomb, threw it on another side and disappeared. Then people left the center of Damascus and moved back.

There was a police station named Shaykh Ḥasan. The staff of police station also moved back. Then Grand Shaykh Efendi sent families. Grand Shaykh Efendi up there and I am sitting and looking from here at the East towards the covered market. There is a graveyard of Bahlūl Dānā. There are other saints. They call it Bābu ṣ-Ṣaghīr. There are many ṣaḥābah from the pure wives, Ahlu l-Bayt of Rasūla Llāh, from all Awliyā’u Llāh including Bilāl Ḥabashī. Most of them are there.

I was looking so up until there, he said. Armoured parties of France reached it. And the top of the center is covered. They came till its exit. And I was watching like this, he said. They came there. And their look made them stop. They returned from there. I was looking from the chalet from above. And from downstairs I heard voices and the sounds of the horseshoes. I thought how come there are horseshoe sounds downstairs? That Shaykh Ḥasan al-Jibāwī. When Sulṭān Salīm, may paradise be his abode, was on his way to Egypt, he passed opposite him. He is a holy one replying to everyone, Whoever asks for anything for his honor, Allāh Almighty grants it for his honor. When he was told so, he stopped, dismounted his horse and visited him. He prayed two raka‘āt, made his du‘ā’ and continued on his journey. It is such a holy place. I took bay‘ah for the first time from Grand Shaykh Efendi on that maqām.

Shortly, Grand Shaykh Efendi is upstairs and how did these horseshoe and crowd noises suddenly appear? And there is no one in the country. When I went down to look, the head of awliyā’, ‘Abdu r-Ra’ūf al-Yamanī was also there. There are 40 saints with him. ‘Abdu Llāh Efendi said "Yā akhī, these French people here; their days are over. We came here to expel them". They said they will make ablution, pray, do this service and turn towards Quddūs Sharīf. Then they will go to their maqāms. Indeed, they did so and went out of the gates towards that direction. They became invisible and disappeared. And sounds of bombs and guns of the French disappeared.

Immediately, the British landed their forces in Latakia from Aleppo. An army came full of tanks from here all the way till that road. I went to watch in Homs. There was a road passing in front of Khālid ibnu l-Walīd. The road of Aleppo goes straigh downwards. 1942-1943. I looked one by one. Allāh Almighty brought the British that were assigned to expel them. What do poor Arabs have? They can do nothing. He made British to expel them. They didn't leave anything from the French. The meaning of their intervention now...

Grand Shaykh Efendi went down after he suddenly heard the noise. He saw that crowd. And I saw it while going to the guestroom from the ladies side. Such a crowd. I was surprised too; from where they appeared. Whether they came from heavens or elsewhere, these people; I suddenly found them. This addressing is not an ordinary addressing. As they came from their maqām.

My duty is to interpret. Nothing else. I have to inform you. And you came here to listen to it, not for something else. There is no chance to offer you tea now. Because each of you are on fire. There is no better assembly for you. But you say "We'll go here and there at night." You could take permission from your wives only so. by saying "I'll bring this and that for you." And they send you out for that.

Shortly, what you need and every Muslim needs is according to the order of Allāh Almighty "Alā liLlāhi d-dīnu l-khāliṣ." (39:3) Keeping a religion sincerely and without tricks. There is no value in actions without sincerity. You must pay attention to it. There can't be any value. Sincerity is the soul of actions. Allāh Almighty wants a religion with sincerity. Without sincerity, it is without soul, without value. You can throw it away.

Unfortunately, the bad situation of all Islamic world today is the result of losing sincerity. If we had sincerity, there would be no one looking at us so. Shortly, whole Islamic world needs it. When we have sincerity, Divine support is close. Very close. It's closer than our jugular vein. Allāh is close to us. When He's closer than our jugular vein, His blessing and support is close too. However, because we are weak in sincerity, we can't have that power transmitted to us. May Allāh give us sincerity. Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh ḥamdan yuwāfi ni‘mahi wa yukāfi mazīdah. Kamā yanbaghī li jalāli wajhika ‘azīmi Sulṭānika yā Allāh. Ni‘mati Jalīlu Llāh, barakati khalīlu Llāh, shafā‘at yā Rasūla Llāh. Allāhumma irḥam wa akrim wa an‘im sāhiba hadha t-ta‘ami wa ‘akilīn. Allāhumma arzuqnā ṭayyiban wasta‘milnā min aṣ-ṣālihīn. Allāhumma zid wa bārik wa lā tuqallil. Al-Fātiḥah.
