Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

A’ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm:

(Qur’ān 06:64). Allah Azza wa Jalla says, ‘Say, “It is Allah who saves you from it and from every distress; then you [still] associate others with Him.”.’ When a person is in trouble, he remembers Allah and begs Allah, and when he is saved from it he forgets, gets busy with his work, commits Kufr, commits polytheism, and nothing good remains.

Therefore, there is always something going on in this world, because those people do not make Shukr, because they do not recognize Allah. These disasters, diseases, new things come out every time. Of course it does. The place where people live is not easy. Without the grace of Allah, people cannot live for a minute, and everything ends in a moment. This universe exists by the grace of Allah. Every atom stands with the power of Allah, it does not happen otherwise. Mankind forgets this and says that it happened by itself. Such a thing cannot happen. They say, “This did it or that did it”. Let them look at the One who made it, it is Allah who made it. We have to turn to Allah and we have to beg Allah, then we will be in peace.

Thank Allah, Muslims have a place to take refuge in. The unbeliever does not have, and then he does not know what to do. But thank Allah we have nothing to fear. Muslims should turn to Allah and pray to Allah, Allah will help them. ‘ يِنوعُ دْ ا مْ كُ َل بْ جِ َتسْ َأ’, ‘Call upon Me; I will respond to you.’ (Qur’ān 40:60). Allah Azza wa Jalla says, “Make Dua to Me, I will respond to you.” Do not forget what Allah has done after He responds. We should be in constant gratitude so that we win both this world and the hereafter. May Allah protect us from evil. May Allah always be in our hearts, we should not forget. We should not forget for a minute or for a moment that those who are with Allah are saved, and those who are not are not saved, may Allah protect us.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
