As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

A‘ūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
وَٱعْتَصِمُوا۟ بِحَبْلِ ٱللَّهِ جَمِيعًۭا وَلَا تَفَرَّقُوا۟
<br>‘Wa-‘taṣimū bi-ḥablillāhi jamī‘an wa lā tafarraqū’<br>
‘And hold firmly to the rope of Allah all together and do not become divided.’ (Qur’ān 3:103). ṢadaqAllahu l-‘Aẓīm.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says, “Hold fast to the rope of Allah and do not be divided among yourselves.” This is the command of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. Those who follow ṭarīqah should do this most of all, for ṭarīqah is the heart and essence of sharīʿah.

Some ṭarīqah people are now very fragmented, opposing each other. Whereas they should be opposing other things, they must be opposing disbelief in order to attain the pleasure of Allah. Now, by making division, they are going against the command of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. Therefore, what is happening lately is happening outside of Allah’s command. It is happening at the command of shayṭān. Shayṭān rejoices. It pleases shayṭān. It saddens our Prophet and the Awliyā’.

Our Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says,<br>
المُؤْمِنُ مِرْآةُ المُؤْمِنِ
<br>“Al-mu’minu mir’ātu l-mu’min”<br>
“A believer is a mirror for his fellow believer.”

Therefore, if you criticize him or act against him, it means you are the same, there is no difference between you.

Therefore, you must conceal the faults of a believer. You mustn’t claim he has a fault when he doesn’t and then come out and gather a jamā’ah against another jamā’ah. It is not good. It is not in line with the adab and morals of ṭarīqah. You must know what you are saying, weigh your words carefully. There is no need for unnecessary things. You should do every good thing so that there is unity. Unity against shayṭān. That is what we need most these days. Don’t please him. Stay away from him. Do not submit to his commands. Sad things are happening. Unity is what Allah loves, what Allah ‘Azza wa-Jalla loves. It’s what our Prophet loves.

Therefore, following shayṭān is not befitting for us. We will never approve of this. You must know what you are saying. Just because you wear a jubbah and a turban, and grow a beard, doesn’t make you a scholar. A scholar is someone who possesses wisdom. Someone without wisdom is useless. Therefore, be careful. Pay attention to what you say. There is no such thing as “I am good, you are bad.”

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla has given everyone a unique quality. As Mawlānā Shaykh said, a Muslim should follow wherever his heart is inclined. Don’t think you can win hearts by talking. The heart is in the Hand of Allah. Therefore, let a person go wherever their heart has gone. Don’t force him to come. May Allah show everyone the right way.

Once you’ve followed, it’s not important which... No matter which ṭarīqah you follow, it’s the way of Allah. It’s the way of our Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Don’t fight for that. Don’t fight amongst yourselves, and don’t fight with others. May Allah protect us. This is an advice. inshā’Allah, we won’t hear such news again.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
