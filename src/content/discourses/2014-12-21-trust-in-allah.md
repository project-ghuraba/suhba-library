Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

A Muslim is the person who trusts in Allah. A Muslim expects everything from Allah. This is tawakkul. But you cannot trust and be lazy. You will try and you will trust. It is the order of our Holy Prophet (SAW). First work then have trust.

A man left his camel and went off. Then he came searching for the camel. He said, “I trusted in Allah: I let it go in the grass so Allah may protect it.” The camel naturally ran off. Our Holy Prophet (SAW) said, “If you are to trust in Allah, you have to abide by its rules to avoid complete laziness.” We need to act logically then leave the rest, and this is how we need to believe in Allah.

We need to trust in Allah in everything. Complaining or asking for everything from others is not right. If you say you trust in Allah, you will work then trust as such. If your situation is hard nevertheless, may Allah have pity on you. Do not make other people pity you. You will not leave it off completely and ask for everything from people while complaining.

There were great scholars or dervishes in the past. They were full of Allah’s love and left everything. They had trust and went off. Even though they had a single attire to wear, they used to carry a needle set. When asked why they would say, “If these clothes are torn, my private areas would show and my prayers would not be accepted.” That is also a good thing but again, we need to work. We need to work then trust in Allah.

Allah helps those who work. It is better this way too. It is best to say, “I worked and my Allah helped me.” May Allah grant us true trust, without expecting help from anybody.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq.

Al-Fatiha.
