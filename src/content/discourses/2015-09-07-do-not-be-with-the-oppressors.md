Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi Rasulillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

“Wala tarkanu ilallazina zalamu fatamassakum annar.” (Hood:113)

Do not be with the oppressors. The fire would touch you too and you would regret it.

The person who is against Islam and against Allah is an oppressor. Those who are with Allah are merciful people. Do not be with people who are against Allah’s orders. At least, do not be with them in your heart. Sometimes you are unable to talk. If you open your mouth, many people try and shut you up. They yell and do not let you speak. Even if you cannot do that, do not be with them in your heart so the fire does not touch you.

Allah is with the good. May Allah give us what is good and beneficial for us Inshallah. May Allah (JJ) not grant us to be with those kinds of people. The world is full of deceit and lies. Shaitan is showing black as white and white as black. And the people believe him. May Allah Azza wa Jalla make us see him with the light of faith. May we see the truth Inshallah. May we not be with the oppressors.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq.

Al-Fatiha.
