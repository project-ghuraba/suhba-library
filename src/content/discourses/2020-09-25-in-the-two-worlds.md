Aū‘dhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-Shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Waṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-F’āiz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-

Today is blessed Friday. It is the blessed day that Allah ‘azza wa jalla gave to our Prophet (SAW). Today is a holiday both in this world and in the Hereafter. It is a feast day for Muslims and believers, because Allah ‘azza wa jalla manifests Himself on Friday in Paradise. That day will be a feast day. The manifestation of Allah ‘azza wa jalla amongst so many blessings and beauties is more beautiful than all of them.

Those in heaven wait for that day so that they can attain that manifestation and see Him. Heaven is such that there are things there that have never come to mind before. This is very different from our life here. There are some idiots [who say], "There will be believers and Muslims in Paradise, so choose Hell! It will be better!" they say. They are supposedly smart. All the famous people are saying that the people whom they think are beautiful will be in Hell, so “Let's go there, what is in paradise? There is nothing,” they say. There are unimaginable beauties in Paradise! Whatever beautiful things there are in the world, compared to the blessings in heaven, these are zero, nothing.

Nevertheless, the greatest wish of the people of heaven is to see and watch the manifestation of Allah ‘azza wa jalla on Friday. How will that manifestation be? Allah knows best that there are special things in Paradise. Few things have been said about Paradise, but there are beauties there that our Prophet (SAW) said cannot even come to one's mind here. There is also a blessed feast day there on Friday.

We don't know how the days and months are there, but it is “Friday”. There is no night, there is no sun, but with the power, grandeur, and knowledge of Allah ‘azza wa jalla, there is Friday. May Allah make us live those days, and make us happy in this world and in the Hereafter, insha’Allah. Allah bless us all. May Allah guide those who are not on the right path so that insha’Allah they will see that beauty. Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-F ti ah
