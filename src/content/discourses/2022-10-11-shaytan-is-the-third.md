As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says in Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n, when women interact with men, they should not act with too much familiarity. When it is necessary, they should speak to the point. When they are not relatives, they should not talk too much to unrelated men. Talking with them is wrong. It is not good for an unrelated man and woman to stand together. Therefore, when they go somewhere, there must definitely be someone else with them. Otherwise, the third one will be shayṭān, says our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam.

This matter has become even worse at the present time just like everything has become worse. There are so many different shayṭāns that have entered people's lives. One of those shayṭāns is that they a man and a woman) speak together without anyone else present. They pretend to ask something or tell something. This is not accepted. To interact with a non-relative, whatever happens, to talk to him is a complete work of shayṭān. This has become something by means of which shayṭāns destroy people's homes and dignity, and make them commit sins. Therefore, "I want to ask this. I want to ask that" – as long as you are not relatives or unless it is something urgent, it is not good to chat like, "How are you? I am fine," every day and every night. It is unnecessary. It is dangerous. How many times have we said this? But we are repeating it, for AllāhNone says "wa-dhakkir" (51:55) – repeat and remind so that people do not do this thing. It is not accepted. Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says the third will be shayṭān.

There are different sites like Facebook and I don't know what. There are many different things. People go there and say, "I will ask this and that. I am just curious." They send friend requests and I don't know what. And then you see that married men have gone bad ways. They do bad things. They commit sins. Therefore, this is an important thing. It is not accepted for women to interact with unrelated men without necessity. Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says, stay at home and don't interact with anyone. It is mentioned in several places in Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n. Therefore, it is not good to look for pretexts and to use them. If there is some issue, then a person must be asked but without additional interaction. Otherwise, if it is not necessary, even if they are a murīd, it is wrong. Murīds are also strangers. What we mean by strangers is, those who are not relatives, are strangers. Therefore, we should pay attention to this. We are warning. They should be careful.

As we said, shukr to AllāhNone, we haven't heard about big things from our people. But what we have heard from others – women leaving their homes and children and running away. There are many stories. What we hear seems like a lot but normally, what we hear is just little. Because AllāhNone knows what happens in that moment. May AllāhNone protect us. This is the game of the ego, the game of shayṭān. If you follow it, it will put you in danger and you will end up in regret. May AllāhNone protect us from this new fitnah of shayṭān.

In Qur’ān and Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, there is many verses, many ḥadīth, they are saying about for woman and man who are not relative, not married, to be away from each other, not to come and speak and make friendship. You cannot make this. AllāhNone and Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam not happy with this thing. Many ḥadīth Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying if to one woman and one man in same place, third must be shayṭān. Without this, you cannot say, don't say it, you cannot nothing. For this, if anybody – must be third one [in order] to not be only two people together. If [there] is two people, third one shayṭān. This is general but [in] this time it is more worse.

We live, as we say, every day, every time, we saying this – our time, it is giving everything, everybody for freedom of to do bad thing. Old time, we see many families, many people they was living together, even they not meet until wedding day. Their family going, find girl for their son and they are married and they are not looking because nothing, no more thing for people to... Now we going out, you see people, every kind of people, so they are, who married he will not be happy with his wife; looking here, there, and looking for internet for this, making friendship they said. This is very bad thing. We have many difficult for control ourself, our ego, these days. For this, and even shayṭān make it more and more, encourage people to do this.

This is also big fitnah through this internet. And they said many names for this – Facebook, I don't know, other many thing they said. Only what I have from Shaykh Bahauddin. He say every time "Facebook koru". This only I have in my mind... But this making people they said, they offer friendship for who has this thing, program. "I want to be friendship." Okay, other one give, okay. But this, after this many thing happen, many bad thing happen. if you want this, only if anything important you can look only for one person who expert for what you want. Not to make friendship and to make this. This is order of Qur’ān saying, "Qulna qawlan ma‘rūfa" (33:32). Say direct what you want and this, don't make long chat, long speech with people, even in the door or in anything. So, these people they make it whole day, whole night and they speaking this nonsense thing and after, at the end, some, many people they make sin, big sin and make ḥarām. This is not from... Many time I speak about this to, it is – to not do this. Still people doing and many crazy people they, as they are fighter, they will fight in internet they said. And they are angry for each other and they are very sad somebody saying something for them. All this [is] nonsense. But the most harming thing, we hear about many families they destroyed with this kind of thing. Woman run away from house. Man he left his wife, going for others. This is not good. This is... Because Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, when heNone said something, heNone said this for our benefit. So we must listen for this. Don't make even "ikhwān" [for] murīd or murīdah – this is also nonsense. Shayṭān make it more easy for him to make this excuse for you to speak with women or to speak with men. This is not from ṭarīqah, not from Islām. Our ṭarīqah following sharī‘ah and sharī‘ah, ṭarīqah – once – one – not others [different]. Don't say this is, "We are ṣūfī, no need for sharī‘ah." Without sharī‘ah you cannot be ṣūfī. You must be another kind which it is not in Islām. You must follow this.

AllāhNone help us. We are living in a real bad time and AllāhNone keep shayṭān away from us, in shā’a Llāh. Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
