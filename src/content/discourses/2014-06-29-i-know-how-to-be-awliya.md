Sheikh Bahauddin Adil al-Haqqani sohbat of the 29th of June, 2014.

Masha'Allah this is the second day of Ramadan. Alhamdulillah! Alhamdulillah. All ok. We are trying our best Ya Rabbi. We are weak servants Ya Rabbi. We are not able to do anything. We are only in need of Allah Almighty's power. If we say huuuuuh! And we don't take our breath, we die. If we make ehhhh! And we don't let out the breath we die. So what you think you are powerful?

They say, if somebody he will go for arena to fight with somebody, first of all he must estimate the one in front of him. Is he taller than him? Is he more strong than him, or more fat than him? Or more wide than him? Or more experienced than him? First they make searching, they say, for the one he fight. This man his kilo is, 80 kg. This muscle is like this powerful. This man you cannot knock him out in one go. This man...So they give him all the reports, and then they make him to prepare to fight. Now we are as humble servants, we are looking to fight or to be ready for fight. We don't have power. We don't have talk, we don't have experience we don't have nothing and we are coming for arena and we say "Huh! I can beat you. I will do this!" Yahu! First you have to look for yourself, what you can do and what you cannot do. It's important.

This is how is the tasawwuf is giving you the good way to Allah Almighty; to prepare yourself and to be one of the best of Allah Almighty's servants. It is known by tasawwuf to make people, some of the people very important people in Allah Almighty's and the Divine Presence of Rasulullah. It is important to understand this one. And it is very simple to understand this one and it is very important to make this child to shut up! Motherhood is not easy, but ok. It is to know that way is open in tasawwuf and in the spirituality that make everybody (to be able to) can be Awliya'. Everybody will be Awliya', not because when you are.

It is like - when you register yourself, let's say police department. Everybody will become police. They will not say, "this police is just new one or this is old police or this is high rank police." No! Or if you register yourself in the army everybody will be army - even the smallest one and the biggest one. If you register yourself as officer in any department you'll be officer. They will not say "you are small" if you say Senior officer or big officer. Everybody is officer. So when you are in spirituality and your target is to make the satisfaction to Allah Almighty and to satisfy Allah Almighty - so all of us will become Awliya'.

Alhamdulillah good tiding. Good tiding doctor we become awliya'. We are officer for Allah Almighty, we are awliya'. Alhamdulillah it's Haqq. It's not job this is Haqq, Haqqul Haqq. Now we understand that Alhamdulillah we are in the level of Awliya'. Allah Almighty He grant us and He registered us as Awliya'. Alhamdulillah we are very happy. Masha'Allah! Awliya' Dr. Munir. Awliya' Sheikh Ahmad. Alhamdulillah. I am junior Awliya'. But! This is good tiding from Allah Almighty to give us this level, but to be Awliya' it is not enough. This we have to understand. We have to work for it. We have to go and we have to work. Because the road is open and wide to give you better maqam, better situation and better behavior and better to be nearer to Allah Almighty. Nearer to Rasulullah and near to big Awliya'.

Alhamdulillah this is must work for it. We must get good things for this one. Don't waste your time for nothing. Don't waste your time for sitting. Don't waste your time for sleeping. You have to work for it. This is what Mawlana Sheikh he wants. What Rasulullah he wants and what Allah Almighty wants. This is an important announcement for us how to be Awliya' and how to be registered as Awliya'. Its registration is not enough. After a while they say "this police is not good police. Send him away! He is not police anymore." We don't want to be sent away.

Alhamdulillah! We are happy to be with you Ya Mawlana Sheikh and we are trying our best to do better and much better and to go further in the satisfaction that Allah Almighty He is asking from us, to satisfy ourself first and to have satisfaction from Allah Almighty to say "Radhiya Llahu 'anhum wa radhu 'anhu" (58:22). It is important, this is a very important maqam. That you are happy with what you got, and you are happy with what Allah Almighty He gave you and you are happy with Allah Almighty how He gives you and how He is generous to you. He was generous for you. This is, you have to know that Mawlana Sheikh he prepared so many good maqams for his murids, for his beloved ones. And he made them very nicely designed. Very nicely waiting for us! And only we have to do, to have to continue, we have to continue with Mawlana Sheikh way and it's important registration here is the baya.

When you are taking the baya from the Sheikh this is that you are surrendering for Sh. And you are giving your will for Sh. And the Sh will open the way for you and make you to go for your maqam that he prepared for you. And this is good tiding again, as Ramadan entered and now the gifts are starting to be given. Start to be given and we are asking from Allah Almighty to give us the satisfaction "Radhiya Llahu anhum wa radhu anhu"(58:22). This is a very important maqam & we have to work and think on that maqam and we're hoping that we're reaching that maqam with the baraka of Mawlana Sh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfiq Bi hurmati l-Habib. Bi hurmati l-Fatiha.
