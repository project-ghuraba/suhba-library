We are in the divine presence of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. We are his guests. The guest must respect the host. He is hosting us alhamduliLlah. This is the biggest favor for people, for human beings. AlhamduliLlah, we are here. So don’t disturb Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. If anything happens, don’t say this and that, and object. Don’t be unhappy. You must be happy for everything. Even if anybody hurts you or does anything, don’t follow shayṭān. Don’t be an enemy towards this one. Everyone here is a brother. And they are invited by Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. So, you must respect to receive the love of Prophet, the happiness of Prophet, for him to be happy with you, in shā’a Llāh.
