Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

A’ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm:

‘And I will command them so they will change the creation of Allah.’ (Qur’ān 04:119). Allah Azza wa Jalla says in this Ayah in the Holy Qur’an, “[Shaytan says] I will command them to change what Allah has created. I will command all kinds of evil, and they will do it.” This is how the world is now. They are trying to change the things that Allah has created. And when they do, they cause troubles to themselves. They think that they will do good, but they do evil.

Allah created this world and gave it to people for their benefit. It will be like this until Qiyāmah. Hand it over as you received it, nothing more. How can we do this? If you use it, there is no other [than it]. This is the place reserved for you in this world, and heaven in the hereafter is boundless. “The least, weakest person has as much place in heaven as this world. But that’s it for this world, so use it well”, says Allah Azza wa Jalla. If you make changes, you will get a job by yourself and it will not be good for you, it will be bad.

They ruined this world, finished it. Dirt is everywhere, filth is everywhere. Neither the sea nor the land remained clean; filth is everywhere. They will deal with it. After that, they still do not repent. They obey the shaytan and continue with whatever their minds command, and try to change what Allah has created. They think that they will multiply what Allah has given, but everything is measured. ‘ رٍادَ قْ مِ ِب هُ دَ نعِ ءٍ يْ شَ لُّ كُ وَ ’, ‘And everything with Him is by due measure.’ (Qur’ān 13:08), says Allah Azza wa Jalla in the Holy Qur’an.

Everything is measured in this world. Therefore, Muslims and non-Muslims, all are included in this. It is a trust, you have to take good care of it. If you go to a Muslim country, you can’t pass because of the garbage everywhere. Streams and rivers are contaminated. When polluting them, a curse happens. Our Holy Prophet cursed those who pollute the seas and waters. This is the shaytan’s order. He has dirtied the people, dirtied the world and dirtied everything, and he likes it more. Therefore, you should be careful.

People ask, “What can I do?” You do what you can do. If you do that, it will benefit you too. You will have done the cleaning that our Holy Prophet said. When you do a good deed, it is not in vain, you will get the reward and it will be beneficial. Badness does not bring benefit, it does harm. Goodness brings benefit. Whether you do it on your own, it will benefit you, and you will have earned rewards. May Allah give people consciousness. Be careful. Don’t say, “I’ll do it, I’ll throw away this much”, don’t act like that. Follow the way of our Holy Prophet. Do good and you will find good.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
