Audhu billah mina sh-shaytani r-rajim May Allah make shaitan far away from our assembly. Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. We mention His Majestic Name may its blessings come upon us. Today is Shaba'n-i Muazzam, 1431 Islamic calendar, Shaba'n-i Muazzam, second of the holy 3 months. We are having this meeting on such a beautiful day. We hope that Allah Almighty will shower upon us the blessings of this month and keep us firm on the way with which He is pleased, make us persist on the way of Haqq.

Welcome. You came with goodness. Now what is the name of the television? - Happiness platform. Mankind, since mankind stepped on this world until the book of this world is closed, a speech is required for everyone. And like we said it is impossible to address each individual separately with this speech. Whoever can hear, may hear it. And you did not force us to make this speech and we are not willing to force anyone to listen to it. No. The whole world is speaking, I'm looking - everybody is speaking from morning till evening. From evening till morning everybody is speaking. What do they say? To whom do they talk? Why do they talk? What outcomes from their talk? Nobody knows. People show their character when they talk. There are useless people, when they open their mouth, it is useless, rubbish. But they speak in front of that machine from morning till evening. Who takes rubbish? "Whoever wants takes it, I will speak" he says.

There are teachers at the university. Not everyone comes and listens to them. A limited number of students listen. Some understand, some don't. But the teacher gives his lecture anyway. Who understands understands. Who doesn't, what can they do? We call it coffee talk in our culture. Everywhere - your name is Fatma?

G: Fatma. My name is Guzin but you always call me Fatma.

Sh: Eh my daughter Fatma, now we say coffee talk everywhere. I mean like a straw without grain. To whom do they give the straw? They give it to animals. If you have grain with you, give them grain. They are human beings. Why do you keep pouring straw in front of people? The man with the highest rank - of course he did not take the highest rank from the Malakut or from heavens, he took it from the people of this world - What is his job? If he took his rank from this world what does he pour in front of people? He pours straw. The Prophets, who are authorized by the heavens they pour jewels in front of people. They pour jewels. The heavenly ones, they pour jewels. But now, if you put 2 bags in front of a donkey, one full of jewels, one full of straw. Which one does the donkey choose?

G: Straw.

Sh: Straw. Where is the level of humanity now? It is inside the straw bag. They don't appreciate the jewels. They don't respect what comes from heavens. They put human beings in place of animals and give them straw. So people cannot be human beings by eating straw. Whoever eats straw is an animal. Humans don't eat straw. Who appreciates jewels is a human being. Animals can't understand jewels. They appreciate straw. In this age all nations, all humanity, put their heads inside the straw bag, nobody asks for the jewellery bag. That is why they are suffering, they will suffer. They will suffer

more, be devastated, burned. They will cry, complain, get sick and die in grief and leave, but they will never succeed.

O people! Your Creator sent you jewels, jewellery from heavens. You do not look. You invented people to worship according to your minds. You go and you bow down in front of him. You stand up. What does he give you? What? Does he speak? He does not speak. Does he give inspiration? What inspiration? He doesn't have any. Inspiration does not come out of a stone. Inspiration comes from something alive. People of today! where do you get that inspiration? I ask them. Sheikh Effendi, we do not take inspiration from heavens, we take inspiration from the ones we love and respect and in front of whom we stand. Eh when it is like this everybody speaks. Eh everybody takes inspiration from shaitan. What they call inspiration is the whispering of shaitan. Or it is Divine, sent by Allah Almighty, inspirations coming down from heavens? The other one is the waste, rubbish of shaitan. He shows people that way.

All right now this was the introduction. Some may laugh, some may get angry. And I say whoever gets angry, let them. We want people to laugh. We speak so people can forget about their grief. What do you do? Can you make people laugh? From morning till evening, from evening till morning, you shout like this on the podium, you debate. What is this show in shaitan's closet? You make people grieve more, sadden more. No more increase in prices, the employees are on strike, there were clashes in this place, a cave collapsed in that place, some soldiers were martyred in the war, some were buried. You frustrate people with such news. At least I'm making them laugh. Let them forget. Let them forget.

Allah Almighty loves His laughing servant. Whoever can make these people laugh, he is acceptable in Allah's presence. If he can't make them laugh - the ones, who make people cry Allah throws them in hell. If you wish go and ask from the source that you get inspirations. They will tell you. The ones who make people cry, where do they belong? The ones who put people in difficulty, where do they belong? Ask and they shall tell you.

G: How can people be happy in this age?

Sh: We already said it - Straw, who puts his head in a straw bag, who gets inspiration when he drinks, can he be happy? No, he will be putlu. Can't be. We should be reasonable. So we should use our minds. Now we put people in such an order they can not speak unless they drink. What they say is drunk talk. Whose translator is the drunk? The drunk is the translator of shaitan. Shaitan tells him, he translates to whoever is listening. How can we be happy? No money, no home, no place, no peace, no comfort. Only lots of hunger, unemployment, sickness. We have built such a community that we do not have any undamaged side. What shall people do? I'm not only mentioning 70 million or 80 million people. The whole world from East to West take inspiration by drinking. They say we have inspiration accordingly. Yes.

If it was only our country, we wouldn't complain. But whole people take inspiration from shaitan after they drink. Shaitan doesn't show mercy for human beings. That's it. But the drunk ones misunderstand us. What can we do? The Owner knows. May Allah keep us firm on the right path, good path, honored path. This is what we want. We do not want to see people this way. We do not also claim that we are purified ones. But we are appointed to tell the facts. We don't have power in our hands. Nobody listens to what we say. They say they know everything. Like what? Let me tell you a story and we finish it.

One man, O people of Anatolia listen and laugh a little. What shall we do? A man, after his wife passed away, realized man is not able to do house work. Allah Almighty appointed women to do house work. For this reason he said "I cannot cope. I work outside, I cannot manage myself and the house. I will marry someone halal/ permissible." He married her, he married a lady. That lady was a young one. She did not know how to treat a man, how to manage her house, but she was graduated from university. Because now all the girls run to university. "Where are you going?" "I go to the university." For what? High, high. What high? You are wearing high heels? "No, for higher education Sheikh Effendi, to get more knowledge." "What knowledge? Can you differentiate Alif and Ayn?" "What is that Sheikh Effendi? How can you ask such a question?" "You did not study at school?" "I studied and I taught. I forgot at the end."

I said, this lady was also probably a graduate of a university, she did not know housework. Her husband understood she knows nothing. He had an old lady neighbour. He called out to her "Fatma Granny." "What is it son?" "You know we married last week, she is inexperienced. Can you make some time for her and give her a recipe?" "What sort of girl is she?" "She is a graduate of University. She has a high education, but she does not know how to serve a man, how to organize her house, how to mother the children." "Ok son, I shall teach her." That man left. That old lady came and knocked at the door. The young lady opened the door. She had this on her head, what do you call that? Scarf. "So you are a Muslim girl?" "Yes granny." "How, how you are a Muslim girl?" "Look I have scarf on my head, it is obvious."

"Good girl. Ok, have you ever cooked?" "No granny. We know how to eat, don't know how to cook. We know how to wash but we make others wash because we are graduates of university. That's why we train our men, so that they go in the kitchen. They wash the dishes. We give them aprons so they don't spill food on themselves. With his apron, our men go in the kitchen. While he washes the dishes, he breaks couple of plates, couple of glasses, spills the food in the pans, breaks couple of oil and syrup glasses, and then he - ooo - he swears a lot saying 'we studied all this time for this?' That's how we were brought up."

"All right since you know this, come and I shall give you the recipe. What did he bring?" "Today my husband brought potatoes, meat, and rice. He told me to get his dinner ready for when he comes home. That what I brought. Now I get ready and go in the kitchen, never done it before. Because we are used to working in the office wearing the latest fashion clothes. Even the directors bow in front of us. We are not used to cooking in the kitchen. I put on an apron, looked in the mirror, did not like it, I took it off and threw it away. While I was throwing it the pan spilled all over me whatever was inside spilled on me." "My daughter I explain how to do it. Now If he brought potatoes and meat it means he wants potato kabab." I don't know how many types of potato dishes there are. Ok you say it. "My daughter you peel the potatoes like this." "I know granny, I know." "Ok. Then you cut them like this." "I know granny, I know." "Then you cut the tomatoes like this." "I know that also." "Then you put oil inside the pan, you put it on the fire and you put the lid and it is cooked. Then you eat it." Whatever the granny said, the new bride said "I know granny, I know, I know, I know." "If you know then you cook it and eat it." And she left.

Her husband came in the evening and asked "What did you cook?" "I started cooking for you, our old neighbour came. She gave me the recipe. I cooked it like she told me." He opened the lid, it was burnt. No potato, no onion, no tomato. Everything stuck at the bottom. The man ran to his old neighbour and he said: "Granny I told you she is inexperienced, You explain to her. How did you do the recipe?" "My son I went like you said. I explained to her how to peel the potato. She said 'I know it granny.' You peel the tomato like this. 'I know Granny.' You peel the onion like this. "I know Granny." "You chop the meat like this." "I know Granny." "You put the oil like this. Enjoy your food. When she told me I know after I said to her "do this, do that" I thought if she knows all of these she should know that she has to put water also. I didn't tell her to put water in the pan. If she knows everything, she should know to put water."

If she did not put water, she knew everything, she did not put water. Now all the governments, communities Turkish, Kurdish, Arab, British whole world they know everything but they do not know how to put water. That's why whatever they do, they burn it. You cannot eat or swallow it. They know everything now, they talk all the time. What do they say? What is this? Don't talk from morning till evening. Do something. The Ottoman World Empire was ruled by 45 people in 25 million square kilometers over 3 continents - 45 people! They can look at the history books. They filled everywhere with civil servants. Can you pay their salaries? I read in the newspaper, there are 15 million civil servants in the Turkish Republic. It means the government works for them not for the public.

May Allah forgive us. We know everything we don't know to add water. We destroyed that huge Dawlat-i Aliyya (Ottoman Empire). We buried it in history, The new-comer bride does not know how to put water and everything is burnt.

May Allah forgive us.
