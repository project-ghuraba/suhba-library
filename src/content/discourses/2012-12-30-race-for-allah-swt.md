Alhamdulillah that I have some listeners. Alhamdulillah that I have this chair. Alhamdulillah that we try to be sincere. Today I have coming to my heart two things. One, the sincerity and the second one is discrimination. These 2 subjects it is actually very, very, very, twin brothers or sisters. Because sisters are also objecting, ‘Only brothers?’ No, there are sisters also'. So that's why it is coming to my heart how we can be sincere to Allah Almighty. We cannot. Sorry to say, we cannot. You want the realistic one or the brother? 'No we want the realistic'. Ok, I'm telling you the realistic because to tell for yourself a lie is very easy.

If you want to make yourself be happy with you, whatever, your ego is saying, 'Yes, good, yes, good'. But there's other part that say, 'This is realistic of everything'. Haqq, Ya Haqq. Haqq. The Haqq is realistic so what is the realistic now? We say, the sincerity. Can we be sincere to Allah Almighty? Even the prophets say, "Ya Rabbi we are not able to be sincere for you". Imagine so high level. We are not even, we're just micro organic things in front of the prophets. They say "Ya Rabbi we are begging You, to be sincere to You with Your Prophet Muhammad (sas).

Sayyidina Muhammad, he is the one getting our pure sincerity, he is the door to Allah Almighty. So high level, I don't know how I speak like this. I'm catching up with Shaykh Adnan now, so high level sohbah today. It doesn't seem like coffee talk, it is so high. But luckily nobody sleeping. Now we go one level down. We see the Grandshaykhs also, they are carrying the sincerity of the murid. All the Grandshaykhs know their murid are not really in sincerity level. So what they do? They make-up them, they put them nice where they say, "Ya Rabbi these are sincere murids, sincere people for You". And this we call the lucky people because you never know what your ego will tell you. You never know where your ego will lead you and you will approve for you to say 'yes, yes, true, true, you are sincere person'. You have to have a real master to guide you and put you in the right line for the sincerity.

This we put aside now, we go for discrimination between the brothers or the sisters. Now everyone is doing his own thing by worshipping Allah Almighty, by trying to do for Allah Almighty. Some people can be rolling their wish, some people have very nice voice, some people they use very nice instrument, some people like me can speak very nice. I must put myself everywhere like sugar. It is a matter of discrimination. Don't say here discrimination is happening. Don't say 'This one is making the rolling dervish as artistic'. Now I am here sitting, artistic talking for you. Don't say I am doing artistic talk, I am doing very important job here. Very important. Yes it is so important to understand why this dervish is rolling. Can you be rolling like him for one hour? No way, I'll be faint. I can speak here for one hour. And I beg you that you will not sleep I will not let you sleep because if I see somebody sleeping I will be attacking him. I will not make my reputation bad. 'This man make the people to sleep'. It is very bad reputation, very bad.

All the people sleeping and you are sleeping alone, why? Only Dr. Munir can sleep because he is open heart, masha Allah listening for every word even if he is sleeping. And if somebody doing mawlud, playing flute, playing drum, but can you make drum Nabil? No way. But you can draw very nice. With your drawing you can show you have sincerity to Allah Almighty. By your drumming you can show you have sincerity to Allah Almighty. By your rolling.. Whirling. By your whirling you can show that you are sincere to Allah Almighty. By your driving you can also show that. Insha Allah. Asking little money, not too much money, ‘it's ok, it's ok’.

When you start to object for somebody, what he's doing, how he is catching the people or how he is hunting the heart of the people, then your discrimination starts to be affected. Why? Because the jealousy is coming. You have to be jealous in this point, “Why my brother here, he hunt 5 heart of people and make them in good way, in good track?” Don't say “How we do this?” No, you try to get six, from a different way. This is open field. Everyone he can, what he will do? (Hunt) No, rolling. Whirling! As he like. Big field, nobody hit each other, only here. All the dervish hitting each other, but in the reality that is big field.

If you can bring one, Mawlana Shaykh, he said "Nabil, if somebody become Muslim and he been granted iman, if this world belonged to me I'd give it without looking in it, for one to say, Ashhaduan lailaha illaAllah wa ashhaduanna Muhammadan Rasulullah, I will give everything without looking, I will be walking away”. This is so important and you have 7 billion people. So for what you are fighting? Everyone can be star, everyone can be a sun. Everyone can be whatever he wants. As long as you are servicing for Allah Almighty, as long as you are sincere to Allah Almighty. We have to learn how to be sincere where to go for the sincere part.

Second one, how not to be jealous. Of course we have to be jealous. 'You are riding better car than my car, I'm jealous'. But, try to get better car. Don't tell him your car is better than my car, ok better but I'll go and get better one, for example. This is for me. But in the way don't say, 'What you do is bad, what you do is wrong, what you do is this, no'. Try to get better things, try to improve yourself. This is the jealousy that Allah Almighty is allowing us to do. Why when Prophet (sas) say, "There is no difference between Arab and `ajam, only with taqwa, that means you race only in the iman field and belief-field. Say, you race. They know the pronunciation, it's not perfect but they know. They know the real sincerity so big lift. It is not dunya lift. It is heavenly lift. But if you can get the right track. Important. Yeah I declare everyone is wali. Alhamdulillah. Why? Because everyone have mission, everyone have to make the other to be enlightened, to be light. I have my profession, I can speak. He can sing, Osman can drive. Mission! Doesn't matter. That's why I'm asking from Allah Almighty to keep our sincerity for him, and to keep our sincerity for Sayyidina Muhammad (sas) for the Grandshaykhs and for Mawlana Shaykh. Whatever Mawlana Shaykh is telling us we're accepting, we are believing and we want to follow the sincere track. Very important.
