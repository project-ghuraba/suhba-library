Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh, Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem, Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin, Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina, Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur. Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya. Today is the last Wednesday of the month of Safar. Of course, there are duties to be done today:  3 Kalima Shahadat  70 Astaghfirullah  7 Sura Fil It would be more virtuous to pray two rakats after Dhuhr prayer. Al-Fatiha.
