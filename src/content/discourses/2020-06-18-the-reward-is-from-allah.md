Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

There is a Hadith of our Holy Prophet ﷺ, “When a person dies, his book of deeds is closed, but three things will keep going. One is Sadaqah Jariyah, ongoing charity. One is a righteous child. And one is beneficial knowledge.” Everyone can do good deeds. Good deeds are done for the sake of Allah ﷻ. It is rewarded by Allah ﷻ. Allah ﷻ gives them their reward.

If something is done, such as mosques, fountains and Madrasas, there is no need to mention the name of that person because it is written in the sight of Allah ﷻ; Allah ﷻ knows. It happens whether the servant knows it or not. No matter how much it goes on in that land, Allah ﷻ writes that reward to that person; the name is not required. He has already done it for the sake of Allah ﷻ, whether you say his name or not. No matter how many people come and worship and benefit from it, he will definitely get a reward.

There are some people who say, “No name was mentioned, there was no name.” It is not necessary. The person who did that good deed thought better about this matter than the person who said like that. He knew what to do because he did it for the sake of Allah ﷻ, not so that his name would be mentioned and not so that his name would be remembered. He did it for the sake of Allah ﷻ, and left it that way. Thousands and millions of such good deeds have been done. They were made hundreds of years ago. They say that neither his name nor his body remains, but in the sight of Allah ﷻ, nothing is lost. May Allah ﷻ accept those who have done it. May their good deeds continue insha’Allah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
