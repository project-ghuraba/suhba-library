Sheikh Bahauddin Adil Al-Haqqani sohbat of the 8th of December, 2014.

Salam alaikum wa rahmatullahi wa barakatuh. With the permission of my Sheikh, Sheikh Muhammad, I'd like to give small talk. First for the boys and then for middle age and then for the young age like me.

Alhamdulillah, we are happy that Allah Almighty He created us a human. And we are happy that He honored us with Islam. And we are happy that He raised us up with iman. Alhamdulillah. If there is no iman, there is nobody here. Islam is everywhere, but iman it's leading you to be here. This is how we are believers to Mawlana Sheikh Nazim that he showed us the successor, and he show us the second stage of tariqa through Mawlana Sheikh Muhammad. And this is important for us to understand.

It is personal things. It's concerning you, only you. It doesn't concern to follow this or that or whatever. This is for your oxygen, for your breathing. That's why, Mawlana Sheikh said "I give you the oxygen. I give you the life. And I give you Sheikh Muhammad to follow." There is nothing except him. The rest we are respecting, we are loving, but the Sheikh is only one. The king must be one. Wazeer can be 1,000, no problem but the King is one. The Sultan is one. The Sheikh is one. We are believing in oneness. We are not believing in tri... Triness... No. We believe in one. We are saying la ilaha illAllah, Muhammadan RasulAllah. Cannot be 2 Muhammadan RasulAllah. The one who occupies the maqam of RasulAllah must be one. Cannot be 2, cannot be 3, cannot be 5. We are not Christian, alhamdulillah.

We are Muslim and we are believers. We believe in One. "Ahadun Ahad." They die for this one. Every day sahabas, they'd been said "No, you say..." "No, I will not say. I will say only one Allah - One." And Allah, He is. When the Sheikh he leave, he leave 1, he doesn't leave 2, he doesn't leave 3, he doesn't leave 4. The King is one. The Sultan is one. The Sheikh is one. You have to understand this. This it is for your own safety. Now the Queen Elizabeth, may Allah give her long life, do you think she cares if you say "I don't know you or I know you"? Nothing. Because she is a queen. She absolute ruling. The Sheikh also here, mashaAllah. He's been taking, he's been given his maqam. Now it's our role to follow him because we need to be in same side.

This is what I'm trying to say since Mawlana Sheikh passed away. When Mawlana Sheikh he come to his maqam, it was my... coming to my heart as an order from Mawlana Sheikh - He is the one connecting to me. We don't have power to connect to Sheikh. No. Nobody has power to reach the Sheikh. The Sheikh is reaching you. And we try to be ready for reaching the Sheikh. This is important to understand how to reach the Sheikh. When the Sheikh reaching you, you must open your heart and say "Reach me, my Sheikh". Because we don't have the ability to reach the Sheikh. No. Cannot be. Cannot be. It's impossible. The Sheikh, he is the one reaching you. This is Awliya'ullah. This is how they are treating us and this is how they are. And he was reaching and saying "You must be following Sheikh Muhammad. Follow him blindly." This is - you must follow him. This is not a joke. This is not a game. This is not democracy.

In tariqa, there is no democracy. There is order and following order for your safety. What you do for your child? 5 year old child, you leave him in the street because he want to be in the street? He cries, shouts, because you keep him in home. Cars going up and down. They say there is democracy, leave him free. If you leave him free, he will be hit by car, he will be injured, somebody will kidnap him. So you have to put him in order, put him at home in security, in safe. This is what Mawlana Sheikh, he sent to my heart to save you because Mawlana Sheikh he loves you so much and he cares about you so much. This is Mawlana Sheikh, how he is willing to give you the love, the true love that he is promising to you.

www.saltanat.org Page 1 He was saying, Mawlana Sheikh, "Here you don't see nothing. In this world you don't see nothing. When you come there, you see the majesty of Allah Almighty, how He give for His servants." And He's been giving for His servants through His servants that they are nearby to Him. They are Awliya'ullah, and especially Sultan ul-Awliya, Mawlana Sheikh Muhammad Nazim al-Haqqani. Alhamdulillah, we are proud that we know him. We are proud that our eyes see him, our lips kissing him, and we've been with him, eating with him, praying with him, and making tasbih with him. Alhamdulillah.

May Allah bless you and make you stronger, and make you holding tight for what Mawlana Sheikh, he order you to hold tight. May Allah bless you and make you powerful here and hereafter.

Wa min Allahi t-tawfiq. bi hurmati-l Habib, bi hurmati-l Fatiha.
