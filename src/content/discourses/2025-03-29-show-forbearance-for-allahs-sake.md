As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Kullu mri’in bi-mā kasaba rahīn” (52:21). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l- ‘Aẓīm. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla said this āyah, everyone is responsible for his deeds. Therefore, Islām is the religion of forbearance. Muslims show forbearance. They strive to carry out Allāh'sNone orders. They are responsible for their own egos, not for others. They must be on the right path. And they advise others. Who wants to accept may accept. Who doesn’t will not. As we said, this religion of Islām is the religion of forbearance. Muslims don’t harm anyone. They endure things that are done and seen as part of this world. If it is good, they give thanks to AllāhNone. If it is not good, they meet it with forbearance too.

It is not right to force people to do things. If you force things according to your will, there will be conflict. You will create bigger problems. Therefore, whatever happens, you should remember their understanding only goes so far. This man did this. He understands only this much from it. Since he doesn’t understand more, nothing can be done. There is nothing we can do about it. We have advised him. If he wants to learn something, it is fine to teach him. If he doesn’t want to, that’s fine too. Don’t dwell on it or trouble yourself. He is on his own, you are on your own. Move on and don’t linger.

There are already enough problems, enough responsibilities, and enough difficulties in this world. Don’t add yet another burden to your mind. Whoever does something, this one or that one, don’t give it importance. Meet it with forbearance. They are like this. AllāhNone has given them this much. If they don’t act on it, don’t be as ignorant as they are. Don’t think as they think. Don’t understand as they understand. What you understand should be what Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla has explained, and what our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam has conveyed. All 124,000 prophets were sent to explain these matters. Don’t abandon them and fixate on the actions of ignorant people. Stay away from them. And if you see them, show forbearance. Don’t give it any importance at all. Don’t turn it into a problem. These are insignificant matters. Your preoccupation should be with Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. They are there to distract you. If you don’t give them importance, you will draw closer to AllāhNone. Of course, it’s not easy to do this. But you must begin to endure little by little, more each time. People learn. And through training, with experience, you will endure more and more. You will handle what you see with greater ease. Therefore, it doesn’t happen all at once. It comes gradually, in shā’a Llāh.

We must be mindful of this. Believers must pay even more attention to this. By believers, we mean the people of ṭarīqah, those who have entered ṭarīqah to discipline their egos. Disciplining the ego is also about forbearance. It is showing forbearance to people. You must address everyone according to their understanding and their love. May AllāhNone make it easy, in shā’a Llāh. May HeNone place forbearance within us.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying, Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm, “Kullu mri’in bi-mā kasaba rahīn” (52:21). Everyone, he is responsible for what he get in life. It is tiding for him. Tiding, man or woman or anybody, what he done, this tiding, him. So, everybody he is responsible about himself. He must be look for himself. And Islām religion, it is religion of tasāmaḥ, forgiveness, or to be not angry with, to be easy with people. Because everyone, he is responsible about himself. You are not responsible about him so he will be, you can give him advice, you can give naṣīḥah, do this, do that. If you like, if you don't like, no problem. He is free and AllāhNone give everybody life and he can do what he can do. But you, only you can tell him. If he like, he can do. So we are Islām like this, not forcing anybody, not forcing anybody for to be in way, straight way.

Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying if you see somebody, you can tell him. If he accept, accept. If not accept, you be not saying anything. Maybe you can make a problem for and fighting with him. No need for this. Just in your heart say, “It is not good what he done.” But we cannot do anything, because order to not make problem with people. And to, when you are seeing something, don't be angry with people, fighting with people. Because everyone, they have idea, they have mind and they, level, and understanding level also. So not everybody what you think about them, as you are thinking about yourself. No.

Everybody, you cannot control them. Just what you want, you must do. You must be able to live like this, without angry. Without angry, you must be calm. Just, for sake of AllāhNone, be not fight with people, not do problem with people. Because with problem, nothing can solve. With calmness, with goodness, you will be better, because problem still problem everywhere. So, you must teach yourself to be more soft, more calm, not to try to fix everything, which you cannot. Nobody can fix everything.

You must be calm and soft. This is main thing for ṭarīqah people. Because ṭarīqah people, they come to train their ego, themself, to be in way of AllāhNone. So if you are angry for everything, and you will do this, you will do that – of course you can be not happy with, if there is something against Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla or Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-salam but you cannot do anything. But normal problem, also you must be not angry. Maybe some people, you are not happy with them. They are not good adab, they don't have good manner, they don't good system. So, what you will do? You are living in this life. You must be, you know, to try, train yourself to be more calm. You cannot [at] once, to do this. Slowly, slowly, everything. When you see somebody or something not good, you remember, “I must be... Today, I was angry. Okay, not too much.” Tomorrow, less. After tomorrow, less, until you go through this life in safe and peaceful, in shā’a Llāh.

As was in Qur’ān, “Alladhīna yamshūna ‘alá l-arḍi hawnan” (25:63). Who going, walking in this earth in peacefully, happily. And if any, “wa-idhā khāṭabahumu l-jāhilūna qālū salāma” (25:63). If this ignorant people, they saying something against them, not good thing, they said, “Oh, peace.” Salāman, it is peace. So, they go. Other people, they cannot do anything for them. Even they become maybe more angry, but they will be blow in themselves, not to do anything for you. For this, every time, slowly, slowly, you become more spiritual, more higher from this people, seeing everything coming from AllāhNone.

What Mawlānā Jalālu d-Dīn Rūmī saying, to be see, to accept what this people doing, for sake of AllāhNone. For sake of AllāhNone, you accept all this creature, they are servant of AllāhNone. And so you know this: nothing can be done without will of AllāhNone. So you accept this, and you go through this life, in shā’a Llāh, better and better, and higher and higher, in shā’a Llāh.

AllāhNone give us this, for this day, the day of, last day of Ramaḍān. Give this barakah for all of us, in shā’a Llāh, strong īmān, happiness, to be no ignorant, or to be away from ignorance, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah. Even this [fly] coming and teasing.
