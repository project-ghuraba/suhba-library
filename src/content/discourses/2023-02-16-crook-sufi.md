As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Wa-ta‘āwanū ‘alá l-birri wa-t-taqwá wa-lā ta‘āwanū ‘alá l-ithmi wa-l-‘udwān" (5:2). Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is saying, "Help each other." Support doing goodness, support each other, help other people. And most importantly, help those closest to you – that is the most important. The closest ones are your relatives, children, and siblings. Whatever relatives you have, you should help. And married couples should help each other.

Of course, sometimes a man can be poor. His wife may support him. Certainly, it is the duty of a man to look after the household, his children, and his wife. But sometimes it happens in such way that we hear a lot about. A man is poor and gets married. His wife has to work and take care of him. And if that is not enough, she has to take out a bank loan in his name. It is unclear what he will do with it. And then he says to his wife, "Are you not a dervish? You have to take care of me. You have to help me for AllāhNone." This is wrong. That is not help. That is direct crookedness. It is exploitation. It is wrong.

In Islām men must work. But as we said, if a woman is capable, she may take care of the household and provide food. But buying a car or a house, or even taking a bank loan – that is both foolish and crooked. It is not correct. We must pay attention to this. "You must obey me. You must help me." They cheat women by using the name of Islām, ṭarīqah and sufism and then run away. Men and women must be careful about this. We always get complaints about people like this. There is no obligation for a woman to give money to her husband, to take loans. But as we said, if she is rich, she can feed him and not demand anything. But giving money to him on top of that, for going around, is completely wrong. AllāhNone is not happy then and our Holy ProphetNone is not happy. Awliyā’ are not happy as well.

May AllāhNone protect us from such people, from their evil. They say such people can squeeze water out of a stone. May AllāhNone give understanding to these people as they think they profit by doing this. This is not profit. It pulls your rank down. It decreases your value. Your rank and worth go down in the presence of AllāhNone. And your worth goes down among people. AllāhNone loves hardworking ones. HeNone loves content people. Content people are good. But as we said, those who take more than they should, who make it a habit, are not good. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying, Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Wa-ta‘āwanū ‘alá l-birri wa-t-taqwá wa-lā ta‘āwanū ‘alá l-ithmi wa-l-‘udwān" (5:2). AllāhNone saying, "Help each other, to what they want, help them with everything." For people Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying, "Don't help for, to make fitnah, to make hatred between people." And to first thing you help, you must help around yourself – your family, your relative, your neighbours, or your city. This what we must help. When something happen, also we can help even you are far. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh for this earthquake, many people from everywhere, they sending help to ṣadaqah, to make zakāt, for help these people. This is what AllāhNone love. ProphetNone like this thing, action, what you do.

There is also, of course, sometimes between husband and wife in marriage. All marriage people, they must help each other. Sometimes poor man he can marry rich woman. She can help. She can give him food and where staying. No problem. This is also good for, AllahNone reward her. But sometimes what happens, this kind of people, some of this people, they're greedy. They when see, they don't want work, they don't want to do anything. Just he want his wife, not only look after him, he also take money from her to travel, to spend his, to enjoy himself. And even they make sometimes, if lady – we hear many, I hear complaining from many people, from many ladies – he is not working and he ask money from her. Even he make her to bring loan from bank to give him. He want to buy car or to buy flat and he cheat her and she do this.

This is not acceptable for Muslim one. And who, there is some of, who claim they are in ṣūfī people, also they are saying this, when she is not doing this, he say to her, "AllāhNone not happy with you, mashāyikh not happy with you because you are not looking after me." This is, we have this, because many ladies they are pure, when they are hear. "He is my husband and he is from ṭarīqah, and he Muslim and doing this." But every time when this happen, she is working like day and night to give back loan for bank and he is enjoying himself and after he said like this. This is not honourable for man who claim he is in ṭarīqah, he is Muslim. AllāhNone not happy with him, ProphetNone not happy with him, Awliyā’, Mashāyikh all not happy with him.

So, we are seeing, you must be careful what you are doing, not be greedy to want everything. Who ṣūfī he must be not greedy. He must leave dunyā, not looking wife looking after him and after he not enough take her money and make her to work like a slave, to bring money from, give loan of bank. This is only crook ṣūfī. New type of ṣūfī. Mawlānā Jalāluddīn Rūmī he was saying, from even his time, there were many crook ṣūfī. He said, "From thousand of ṣūfī, only you can find one real one." So this kind, this our time is more professional ṣūfī crook ṣūfi. Many of this, every time we hear this complain, for lady also we are saying – if you are rich, only give you look after house, no need to give any penny for your husband's pocket. Food, everything staying in house, and eating, drinking, everything – but for his pocket, not one penny. He must look for himself. This is adab of ṭarīqah, adab of Islām, in shā’a Llāh. Because normally they are not asking, must not ask for this. But if ask – only for house, not for outside. AllāhNone give our people good understanding for Islām, good understanding for life. Who must be he has some attribute of ProphetNone, attribute of Ṣaḥābah, attribute of Awliyā’u Llāh – this is not attribute of crook people, AllāhNone save us from our bad ego's desire, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
