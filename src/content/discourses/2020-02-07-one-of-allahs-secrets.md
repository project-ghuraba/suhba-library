As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh. A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Sincerity is an important thing. In religion, you should act with sincerity. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Mukhliṣīna lahu d-dīna ḥunafā‘ " (98:5), says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. For believers, sincere people, they are very few. We recited a Ḥadīth Qudsī in the khuṭbah – "sincerity is one of Allāh'sNone secrets." Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gives it to HisNone beloved people. Therefore, people should pray to AllāhNone, when they don't know what to do, asking to be given sincerity. It is a big thing.

There are things we do not know, they are secrets of AllāhNone. What are they for? Why is it this way? When it is a secret, there is no need to question. Some people ask a lot: "What is the secret of the universe? We will search for the answer. We will study it." No matter how much you learn, what you learn will be as an atom next to the knowledge of AllāhNone. Next to the power of AllāhNone, no knowledge can be bigger than an atom. What we mean is that the whole universe, this world, is less than an atom. But the main thing we are saying is that the highest knowledge, the best research is smaller than an atom next to the knowledge of AllāhNone. It is better to say it is zero because it cannot be even as an atom. If it is like an atom, it is still big. But it is nothing.

AllāhNone gives sincerity to some people as a favour. So those people are the lucky ones. Who does not have it, should pray to AllāhNone so that he is given it. May we all have it, in shā’a Llāh. Sincerity means when you worship, you do it without mixing it with your ego and desires – "When I worship, my rank will increase, or my provision will be more." This will happen but we should not make it our goal. The goal is obedience to AllāhNone. Sincerity is to get Allāh'sNone pleasure. The rest will be given in abundance through Allāh'sNone kindness. Don't worship for that. Let your worship be with sincerity for AllāhNone. This is important.

Most people now have forgotten sincerity. They pray in order to get this and that. And there is one group which prevents all Muslims from being sincere. They eliminated sincerity. They made all Muslims run after their self interests and not make their worship with sincerity. And then they say to sincere people that they make shirk. However, a sincere man does everything for AllāhNone. But they are leaving sincerity, which has been ordered and explained by AllāhNone. AllāhNone has given mind to people in order for them to think. HeNone sent Qur’ān Karīm that clearly explains it. People still do not use their minds and do not accept what they see.

We need a Murshid who can show the way. It will not work by thinking and seeing only. When you have a Murshid, you will get sincerity, in shā’a Llāh, and your actions will be sincere. How can we learn to be sincere? In shā’a Llāh, when you follow a Murshid, a true Murshid will lead you to sincere worship and sincere servanthood to AllāhNone, in shā’a Llāh. May AllāhNone let us reach that station. May HeNone make us beloved servants, in shā’a Llāh. Because Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla said in a Ḥadīth Qudsī, "INone will give this secret only to whom INone love". May AllāhNone make us from those servants, in shā’a Llāh.

To be sincere it is very important. There is āyah: "Mukhliṣīna lahu d-dīna ḥunafā‘ " (98:5). Pure for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, be sincere for AllāhNone. To be sincere it is very important in Islām. But it is not for everybody because Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying in Ḥadīth Qudsī we read in khuṭbah: sincerity it is one secret from Allāh's, ‘Azza wa-Jalla, secret. HeNone give this sincerity for beloved one. HeNone love... Who beloved one for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, he get this sincerity, purity. To be pure for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, not mixing anything with HisNone order, what you worship, only be sincere for HimNone. This is gift from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.

Because many people worshipping but they want job, they want money, they want this, they want that. And they make this intention: "When we do this, it will be happen for this." Normally it happen. But even you are sincere, no need to mix this. Only make your worship pure for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, for HisNone happy, AllāhNone to be happy with you. This is aim. Aim of to be sincere, to be pure, it is very high rank for human being. Secret... AllāhNone He has secrets, countless. One of this secret is sincerity. HeNone give for anyone HeNone love. So he has sincerity, purity he is lucky. Only for HisNone riḍā’, HisNone pleasure. For Allāh's, ‘Azza wa-Jalla, pleasure everything we do. But HeNone is generous, HeNone give us what we are in need.

But when you mix with, you make this condition: "I will pray and AllāhNone will give me this, give me that, I will be here, there." This is not sincerity, not pureness. For pureness only for AllāhNone, for HisNone pleasure. Others not important thing, not precious thing. The most precious – Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. HeNone must be for us, HisNone pleasure the most important thing. When you do this you are lucky one. But these days many people they are not giving any attention for this. Especially this new kind people, they are not looking for sincerity, only looking for outside of people. Especially they are looking for people to correct them by their idea, what they have bad idea, who it is not true. Every time, not looking to be sincere or to be pure, only to look for other people. This people they are making normal people to be away from pureness, from sincerity.

Sincerity you must, you can find, if you are lucky, with murshid. True murshid, he can give you this. But people they are running with this, cheated by these people and they are thinking this is not good. So al-ḥamdu liLlāh who is with murshid, he is lucky because he has sincerity and he has pureness, in shā’a Llāh. And slowly, slowly who coming, AllāhNone give him, open for him his heart, give light for his heart and this make you pure, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone give us this good attribute, to be beloved for HimNone, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
