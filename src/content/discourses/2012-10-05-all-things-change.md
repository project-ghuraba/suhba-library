As-Salamu Alaikum. Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fil jamia'

Mawlana just said in his dua "May Allah Who corrects the good ones also correct us." This is a big blessing. To be in the rank of salihin/good ones is a big blessing. What they call "rich" in dunya, maybe 5 or 10% of of people are rich. And they are rich by the Will of Allah. It is not like he himself does business and gets rich. If Allah doesn't want it then thousands of obstacles appear in his way & he can't succeed. This, Allah making people to be from His good servants, is also a big blessing. For this Mawlana says "Correct us, make us from the good ones" - the dua he just made after Juma now & it is also a beautiful dua he always says.

To be salihin means to be a good servant who is beloved by Allah. And Allah's beloved servant is the one who is content with everything Allah gives, or actually the one who is on maqam al-rida (level of contentment). And Tariqa is the way which disciplines peoples' egos & helps them, disciplines them, shows them the way so that they become content and be on the level of contentment.

They may say "we are content" but when a small thing happens immediately they rebel. Even in the holiest places you see that people fight- in Makkah, in Madina & right next to the Kaaba, still they are not showing contentment. And after them, in holy places like this- like dargahs etc shaytan provokes people more. The people you least expect get angry & follow their egos such that they don't think about the others anymore. Therefore the people going for Hajj would ask Mawlana "advise us" & he would say "don't get angry". This is a big, important thing. They say "Ok, I won't get angry" but you see how he gets angry- how shaytan mounts him and he loses his temper, he is defeated.

And also, man is used to something & he thinks that it will go forever the same way without changing. He can't be content with the new things. For example, we were saying this, all the time Mawlana 10 years ago everybody could reach him. 10 years before that, it was even easier- he was going to all the places and people could speak to him easily. And before that it was very very comfortable & easy. When people understand its value, they started coming more. And now when they can't see Mawlana all of a sudden, people are rebelling. Rebellion means they don't show contentment. However we should thank Allah in either case, that Mawlana is with us, above us. May Allah grant him long life. So everything is changing except Allah. It is Allah's attribute. People don't have it. Not any creature has it because He is the Creator. Everything that is created changes. Therefore we should also be content and pleased with this in any case. What Allah orders takes place, you should be content with it. Because Mawlana himself would also like of course, to have strength & to go out, to talk to people. But be thankful for this much also, thank Allah & Allah give him strength, health & long life insha Allah. We may reach Mahdi (as) together with him as he says insha Allah. The people who are coming here, as we say each time, it is big thawab. There is Hadith that says for each step he takes when going to visit his Muslim brother, a sin is removed & one reward is written in its stead. Now you are going to visit the beloved servant of Allah. You are going to the Wali of Allah. No one knows other than Allah what thawabs you will get, how many of your sins will be lifted from you. But we should show contentment for this.

Of course there are so many people who love him, may Allah bless them, and they all want to meet him. And some can't believe that Mawlana doesn't want to meet. It is not that he doesn't want to meet but he can't meet. And this, because of their extreme love they can't accept this condition of Mawlana, that he got weak. Of course in appearance he is weak, but because he is the beloved servant of Allah, as we say each time, he has more spiritual power. Therefore all who come definitely return from here with his gift. This you can't buy with money in any place, in the other places of the world. You are coming to the inheritor of Muhammad (sas), this means his glance, his help reaches you. Be sure of this! shukur Allah. Here you see his holy face, his breath is upon you, it is enough. Allah Shukur.

He is making dua for all who come to visit him. He is reaching to who asks madad, with Allah's permission. He is from the ahlu Tariqa. Allah (awj) says: "Wa Man Yu`azzim Sha`a'ira Al-Lahi Fa'innaha Min Taqwa Al-Qulubi" (22:32) Wahabis say "you make shirk" but it is not shirk. He is Allah's beloved servant. As you see Mawlana is the Shaykh of Tariqa, beloved servant to Allah, he does make shirk. But "there are certain things that you glorify them, show respect for them" says Allah Almighty in Quran also. It is the highest of taqwa. Why do you love Mawlana? You love him for Allah, for what else should you love him? He is granted the love of Allah & love of Prophet therefore everyone comes to him. But there are some who lost their way, may Allah grant them guidance also & grant them understanding. They should think some more so that they find reality. They should not listen to the empty words of people. There are a lot of people like this, they speak from morning till evening. They are of no use other than taking a person out of iman & belief. You build a huge building but if it has no foundation, it totally collapses from the smallest thing. Then no faith or belief remains after that.

So this, for the honour of awliya, for the honour of the Prophet (sas), because we follow them, they tie us; prevent us from escaping to here, there bi'ithnillah. We pass insha Allah with them also in the last breath, with iman bi'ithnillah. Today we speak about Mawlana. He said in his dua, "Allah make us with good people". Because good people are very few. Maybe 5%, 10%. Allah (awj) Will this. Because Allah (awj) even for richness also maybe 5%, 10% of people are rich, others not rich. This richness also from Allah coming. What you do, nobody can by himself be rich. If Allah not Will, he cannot be rich. Many things coming front of him & making his business all wrong. But if Allah Will He opens ways one by one, one by one, you reach to richness. To be from good people, from salih people is very big honour & very big favour from Allah. And to be in good worship & doing every goodness is not easy. Many, many things prohibited you, some thing coming in front of you. And especially in holy place like Makkah & Madina, after in these zawiyas, shaytan coming more. Fighting, coming to fight against people in this area. Especially some people were asking Mawlana when they were going to Hajj, "What you tell us to do there?" He said "Only one thing you do, don't be angry". Many people the first time they're going "why will we be angry? We are not here even angry. But when they are going there really become different. We don't know from where coming this anger.

So the good people teaching to be satisfied with what Allah gives them, what Allah put them in position. They must be satisfied for everything because this is coming from Allah. And when you do this, you be rest, not angry, not sad, no pressure on you. It is good for you first. Tariqa teaching this. And many people they are happy with this. But now for Mawlana Shaykh, they are not used to see him like this. Because 10 years ago, he was more stronger & he was coming. Whole day nearly he was here. Giving sohbat 2 times, 3 times maybe in a day. And seeing, meeting every people. Before 10 years, before 20 years he was more & more stronger. Maybe from 5 times praying he was here. Before also he was more & more, maybe it was very few people. It was very easy to meet him, to sit hours with him, you enjoy yourself even by spiritual & physical- you eat with him, whole day he was with you. So it was very good but now..

Everything in this world changing except Allah (awj), the Creator. What He create-everything changing. Only Creator not changing. So it is normal to be like this. And even you must be satisfied for this also. You must be happy also. Of course everybody miss that days. We also miss that days but what will we do? Even for this, Alhamdulillah. Alhamdulillah, Allah make him long life. Insha Allah we meet Mahdi (as) with him. But many people, from their love, they don't know what to say. Somebody saying something but nevermind, it is Ok because from their love coming. They said "we miss, we like to sit". Even many people not believe Mawlana is weak & cannot stay with people. Of course this is the material. But in his spiritual more, more & 100% more stronger. And he is knowing what people coming, who is coming to him.

And Prophet (sas) said "when some mu'min coming to see his mu'min brother, to going to visit him, every step Allah take one badness from him and good action (hasanah) for him. He takes one badness & put one reward for him". How for Mawlana? He is beloved servant of Allah (awj). Allah know how many, counting this, only Allah know. Because we are coming only for Allah now & for love of Allah. And Allah not leaving you without rewarding. And you see, even when you see him by face once it is enough for you coming from thousand kms away. And Alhamdulillah, for this still people coming. Not saying "we are not seeing him, we cannot come". Every people like to come but many times coming order from Mawlana "don't come, come". So Alhamdulillah, also even now it is full here. They cannot help without coming. They are coming all, Alhamdulillah.

So don't be sad, only be satisfied from this situation. Allah give him more health insha Allah and strong insha Allah. His dua like this, insha Allah Allah let him all time to be strong enough to walk, not being in bed insha Allah. Make dua for him insha Allah.

Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, Al Fatiha.
