As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh. A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah. Shukr to Allāh for these beautiful occasions. He is bringing us to them. May Allāh grant those who have come their rewards and recompense. Because they have come for the pleasure of Allāh, and for the love of our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. We have gathered. May Allāh be pleased with all of you. This Mawlid, the birthday of our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, is a light for humanity. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla indeed created humanity from the light of our Prophet. Therefore, to show reverence to our Prophet and to love our Prophet brings us the pleasure of Allāh. That is to say, they ask what is most beneficial for a person. Money and such things have no benefit at all. What is beneficial is the love of our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, respect for him, to respect him, to follow his path. To love him is the greatest gain for a Muslim. For non-Muslims too, all things and blessings were created by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla through him, for the honour of our Prophet. Whoever benefits from them is in fact benefiting from the light of our Prophet. Whatever exists in the world and the universe, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla created for the honour of our Prophet. He dedicated it to his love. His love is farḍ for Muslims. Whoever does not love our Prophet – shayṭān is the one who most does not love him. And those who follow him also do not love him. And Muslims are also deceived; he is able to deceive them. They say, “If you love our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam too much, you are associating partners with Allāh, committing shirk.” Shayṭān has devised such a trick. Those with little understanding are deceived by him and become enemies to Muslims. They oppress Muslims. They strive, as much as they can, to prevent this. This has been so since the time of our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. But sometimes it becomes very evident. As soon as there is a chance, they immediately come out and cause disorder. They lead people astray. They do everything they can so that those on the way of our Prophet go astray. They appear to be the truth, yet they command falsehood. They say, “Shirk is ḥarām.” Then they forbid the love and reverence for our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. They deprive people of it. People are deprived of the shafā‘ah of our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam and of the honours that Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla granted to him. Because the ḥadīths of our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam – they too know them. This group we call Muslim, the group deceived by shayṭān – they know the most but do not understand what they read. If they understood, they wouldn’t do such things. Our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says, “Whoever sends ṣalāt wa-salām upon me, I return it.” Our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says, “I accept it.” Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gives its recompense. He gives it tenfold. Therefore, it is against shayṭān’s interests. He doesn’t want Muslims to earn such thawāb and to be close to Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. He wants to take everyone to hell with himself. Therefore, this Mawlid occasion is a beautiful occasion. It would be nice if it were celebrated everywhere. But unfortunately, some places don’t allow it. They don’t want it. And people are deprived of this benefit. They too are deprived of the greatest gain of their lives. They say, “We will not do it, we will not perform it.” If you don’t do it, then don’t. But if you prevent others, you will also bear the sin of that. May Allāh protect us from the evil of shayṭān. May we be on the way of our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, in shā’a Llāh. May we be among His beloved servants, in shā’a Llāh. May Allāh bless these days, in shā’a Llāh.
