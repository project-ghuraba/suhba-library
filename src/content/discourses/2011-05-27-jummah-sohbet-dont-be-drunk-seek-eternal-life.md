Mawlana Shaykh Mohamad Nazim al Haqqani an Nakshibendi

24 Jumādā ath-Thāniya 1432 A.H. Friday, May 27, 2011 Sohbet in English at: http://saltanat.org/

BismillahirRahmanirRahim Allah Hu Akbar, Allah Hu Akbar, La ilaha Il Allah, Allah Hu Akbar, Allah Hu Akbar, wa illa Il Hamd Selatul wa selam aleykum, ya Anbiya Allah

A'udhu billahi minash shaytanir rajim BismillahirRahmanirRahim

Meded, ya Rijal'Allah, meded, meded. Oh people, oh our attenders, Selamun Aleykum. That is heavenly blessings for whole people living on earth, when you are saying, “As-salam aleykum”. [Heavenly blessings] just coming on those people who they are using, “As-salam aleykum” and saying “Aleykum selam, wa rahmatullah wa berakatuhu”.

Oh people, ask Allah Almighty from His endless Mercy Oceans to forgive us and to bless His weak servants. Oh people, enough! Up to today you are drinking and getting drunk. Oh people, leave to be drunk ones. You are not coming here to be drunk ones. But you are coming here to understand something that...

Meded, Sultan Awliya, Meded ya Rijal'Allah.

You are sent here just to know and to understand and to do what you are understanding. But people when they are getting drunk, they are not understanding and they are not knowing anything. And they should be sent away from this life to another place, to another level of life, because life is a grant from our Lord, Allah Almighty swt, Who created us. [Mawlana stands up.] If we are standing up from the beginning until the end of our lives, it is nothing. Oh, people, keep full respect and glorify our Lord Who created us.

Oh people, listen! and learn! Try to follow the way that [is] taking you to heavenly ways. Heavenly ways [are] carrying you to such territories that are [n]everending territories, [n]everending dominions of Allah Almighty. Oh people, do not lose the chance that has been granted to you by our Lord Allah Almighty swt. Try to learn and try to do something for our Creator, for the Lord of Heavens, and for the Lord of Creation from pre-eternal up to eternal.

Don't think that the life of mankind is only between 1 and 100 [years]. No! No! No! No! Allah swt Sultansin, ya Rabbi. Sultansin, Ya Rabbi. He is most Kerim - Generous. That word is not enough to explain Allah Almighty’s grants; and we are most lucky creatures that Allah Almighty granted us such an honor that was given to us. Even angels were asking to have that honor for themselves. But Allah Almighty saying, "I am bringing them from endless territories and endless dominions. I am bringing new ones to be on earth as my deputies”. And Allah Almighty created man [with] that endless grant from our Creator Allah Almighty swt. Ya, Rabbi – [Arabic] forever! not only here. Up to eternal, we like only for your glorifying. We are asking that only.

We are not running for our bodies to eat and drink and enjoy. That is for our physical being that it is going to be changed in paradise - to enjoy. But our real enjoyment and real honor is to be in the Divinely Presence forever... forever! Up to eternal. We are asking eternity... eternity. We are asking to be with Allah Almighty and his glorifying. People are not understanding such things and they are bringing some understandings that only belong to our physical being.

Physical being is like our donkeys. The horse is not important; but the horseman is. [The one] who is riding on the horse is granted honor to be king, to be a sultan. You are coming to the palace of a majestic king on your horse; but when you are coming to the glorious entrance of that king's palace, you are not going in on your horse. You must get down and you must enter. Therefore, when Abu Yazzid (Allah blessing him) reached the highest level of obedience and the last point of servanthood, he reached the Divinely most honored most glorified entrance of the 'super palace' of the Lord of Heavens. He was asking to come in. He was asking and saying, "Oh my Lord, let me to be in. How can I be in from the heavenly announcement that is representing the holy invitation of the Lord of Heavens?” [The Lord of Heavens was] saying to him, “Oh, Abu Yazzid, leave your ego. Leave your donkey and come in. I am not accepting [you] to come in with your donkey. Oh angels, take from him his donkey and let him come.” And our donkey is taken away. At that time we are getting honor to be with our Lord Almighty's invitation. You can enter.

But we are looking and seeing all of us that we are trying to give our horses everything that it likes. We are trying here always to give rights to it for drink and dressing and decoration. Those people now are doing this for their physical being or for their rights or for their donkeys or for their horses. No one can get in on his horse or on his donkey to the Divinely Presence. But people now - all of them now - are trying to get in to enjoy with eating and drinking and sexuality. Nothing else. And they should be going to such a new territory [for those] who are asking to enjoy material aspects and [who] have been sent to that area, to that territory. [Not] until they are leaving their donkeys may they be taken to the Divinely Presence. They cannot be, because of their donkeys. Then they can be. And those people are in such a position that they are only running after physical enjoyment like eating, drinking, and sexuality.

It is not true subject, or it is not a real aspect for our being on earth. But we have been sent to learn what are our rights and to know who we are and for what we are in existence, and finally to where we have been created to go. But people, all of us are now running after material aspects and enjoyment. And the summary of our education, our last limit that we are understanding from this life, is to make our beings enjoyful here. And it is a wrong, wrong, wrong understanding because that wrong understanding is making people to fight each other, to run after material aspects and to fight and to kill each other. But they are never working and asking Divinely aspects that have been created in the Divinely Presence of the Lord of Heavens.

Oh, people. Come and listen. Learn and understand what was whole prophets' prophecy... prophecy. Prophets never calling people to dunya for a temporary [life]; but prophets coming just to teach people the ways of eternity. Oh, people, come and use your mentality and your whole energy. Look to understand something and to do something for eternal life and for eternity. I am sorry to say that people are drunks and that they are not understanding [these] aspects. They are following shaytan and shaytanic ways, May Allah forgive us.

Oh people, say Astaghfirullah, 70x at least, daily.

[Say:] “Oh our Lord, we are not from those people, that they are understanding temporary here on this world, and that they are trying to make it for everyone and they are wrong ones and wrong ways.”

While you are living wrong ways, you cannot reach happiness and real enjoyment and real protection and real of what Allah Almighty is preparing for ourselves. Oh people, listen and obey and try to do something for your eternal life. You should be happy forever. May Allah forgive us.

Allahumma sali ala sayidinna muhammadan nabi ummi wa ala ali wa sabihi wa salam...

Fatiha
