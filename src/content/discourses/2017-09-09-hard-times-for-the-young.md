Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Our Holy Prophet (SAW) says, “In End Tımes, raise your children well and do not be happy because you have many children!” Sometimes it is better to own a dog than to have a child. It is truly so. It is better for a person to take care of an animal than to have a bad child. Because it carries responsibility. The children need to pray after you and send good deeds after you. Raising children is important. They could send all sorts of curses after you. Instead of this, it is better not to have a child at all. Training is important.

Feeding your children from the permissible (halal) is very important. If you feed them from the halal, that child will never come out bad, Allah willing. People need to look for the fault in themselves. Adding the slightest morsel of the prohibited (haram) is like adding poison. It would be a reason to get bad, oppose you, oppose the family, or oppose Allah while growing up, in later years, or ever since a young age till now. That is why our Holy Prophet (SAW) mentioned this matter. Most people in End Times say, “It does not matter how it comes as long as money comes.” It means they want money to come without regard for whether it is good or bad money. Whereas what is more important for his own children is eating and drinking halal. The halal morsel is important.

It is alright once you trade with sweat (make an effort). However, if he deceives people from here and there, or does things to the detriment of people, then the money that comes will be no good. Allah gives people according to their intention. Whatever their intention that is what will be gained. If a person does something unknowingly, it is alright and he is not responsible. However, it does not work if he says everything is alright and takes it. This subject is a very important subject.

You need to pay attention to your child’s prayers and fasting from a young age. You also need to pay attention to the child’s surrounding friends. Even if a good child befriends a bad friend, he would slowly turn the friend good. That child also becomes a good child. May Allah keep all our children safe. End Times are hard times for the young. People you would never think of end in troubles.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq. Al-Fatiha.
