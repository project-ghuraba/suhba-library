As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
أَلَا إِنَّ أَوْلِيَاءَ اللَّهِ لَا خَوْفٌ عَلَيْهِمْ وَلَا هُمْ يَحْزَنُونَ
<br>“Alā inna awliyā'Allahi lā khawfun ʿalayhim wa-lā hum yaḥzanūn”<br>
“Verily on the friends of Allah there is no fear, nor shall they grieve.”(Qur’ān 10:62). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says, “There is neither fear nor sorrow nor grief for the beloved servants of Allah.” Because they know that everything comes from Allah, they do not grieve or worry. They rely on Allah. They are the servants whom Allah loves; to be an awliyā means to be a servant whom Allah loves.

Everyone can be an awliyā. Sometimes people ask, “How do we become awliyā?” If you are content with Allah’s decree and what He bestows upon you, if you submit and accept that everything comes from Allah, Allah will make you one of His beloved servants.

To be a wali means to be His beloved one. That is, being an awliyā does not necessarily mean performing miracles; it is not solely about that. As we said, if a person is obedient and content with what Allah gives them, and gives thanks to Him, they become one of the people whom Allah loves, one of His beloved servants.

If they constantly complain and are dissatisfied with everything, then they know for themselves. Such a person is constantly filled with anxiety and sorrow and can never be in a good state. The hardships endured will be in vain, and the things asked of Allah will not be fulfilled.

You should be grateful for what Allah has given. The greatest blessing is īmān. If you have īmān, the rest is easy.

Of course, minor things happen; it’s human state; one can feel upset or sad. But instead of complaining about these things, knowing that they come from Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla brings comfort. He will have made dhikr of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla – make dhikr means remembering Allah.

Dhikr can be done with the tongue, but it can also be done through one’s state. Dhikr of state means remembering and mentioning Allah every second, every minute, knowing that everything comes from Allah.

Therefore, may Allah make us among His beloved servants. Let this always be in our minds: everything people do happens by Allah’s will. Nothing done outside of that is accepted. Everything that Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla gives is a gift and beneficence from Him.

May Allah treat us with His beneficence and not test us. This is very important; never ask for tests. Sometimes people think tests are easy and ask for them to gain a higher rank; never ask for that. We are servants of His beneficence. Tests are difficult for us. Do not ask for them, may Allah protect us, inshā’Allāh.

May Allah always give us from His beneficence. May He make us all among His beloved servants, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
