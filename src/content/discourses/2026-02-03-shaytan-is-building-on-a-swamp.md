As-Salāmu ‘Alaykum wa RaḥmatuLlāhi wa Barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu BiLlāhi Minash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. BismiLlāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm.

Wa ṣ-Salātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Akhirīn.

Madad yā RasūlAllāh, Madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi RasūliLlāh, Madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

Dastūr Mawlana Sheikh Abdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistāni, Sheikh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-

Ḥaqqānī. Madad.Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-Suḥbah wa l-Khayru fi l-Jam‘iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm, “ﻢٍﯿﻜِ ﺣَ ﺮٍﻣْ َأ ﻞﱡ ﻛُ قُ ﺮَ ﻔْ ُﯾ ﺎﮭَ ﯿِﻓ”, “Fīhā Yufraqu Kullu ‘Amrin Ĥakīm”, “On that night is made distinct every precise matter.” (Qur’an 44:04). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘Aẓīm. Laylah Mubārakah! Last night, in shā’a Llāh, will be an opening for Islām for khayr, for good, for peace. In shā’a Llāh, this night is a powerful night.

Now there are two things: kufr and īmān, belief and unbelief in the world. Now it is like this. But, ‘ﺎًﻔﯿﻌِ ﺿَ نَ ﺎﻛَ نِ ﺎﻄَ ﯿْ ﺸﱠ ﻟا ﺪَ ﯿْ ﻛَ نﱠ ِإ’, ‘Inna kayda l-shayṭāni kana dha‘īfā’, ‘Indeed, the plot of Satan has ever been weak.’ (Qur’ān 04:76). Now shayṭān and his army, and the head of shayṭān’s army dajjāl, the antichrist are controlling everywhere in the world. But no fear AlhamduliLlah, because Allāh said shayṭān and his followers are not powerful. Powerful is Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. They thought they will be controlling everything. In one second, they will collapse. Because when you build a building on a swamp or so; even if you make a big big building, it will quickly collapse. As much as you think it is powerful, you maybe think that nothing will happen to it, but it is from the beginning a bad place, a bad thing, everything bad. It won’t need to [be destroyed] from the outside, it will be eaten from the inside, it will become rotten, it will become weaker, it will become of no power and it will collapse on them. No need for you to worry.

Of course, Muslims now also have no power and are under the control of shayṭān and his followers. Now, the whole world is like this, but no fear, in shā’a Llāh, in one second, everything can be as Allāh wants when the time comes. In shā’a Llāh, time is near for the antichrist and their army to be finished, in shā’a Llāh. May Allāh send the savior to save us. And we believe that Sayyidina Muhammad ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam said, Mahdi ‘alayhi s-salām will come and all this what they think they’re controlling, they will see that there’s no control and they will be finished without doing anything, in shā’a Llāh.

May Allāh keep us safe to meet Sayyidina Al-Mahdi ‘alayhi s-salām. Mawlānā Shaykh was every time saying to make du’ā, pray for Mahdi ‘alayhi s-salām to appear. Because even though it’s a benefit: as much people are suffering, as much they’ll have more reward from Allāh - but it has become too much for people. We want, in shā’a Llāh, Sayyidina Al-Mahdi to come and save all humanity, in shā’a Llāh. Amin.

Wa min Allāhi t-Tawfīq. Al-Fātiḥa.
