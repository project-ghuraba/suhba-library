Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm:

اومُ لِ سَ و

َ

(Qur’ān 33:56). ‘Inna Llāha wa mala‘īkatahu yuṣallūna ’ala n-nabiyyi ya‘ ayyuhā alladhīna ā‘manū ṣallū ’alayhi wa sallimū taslīma, ‘Indeed, Allah and His angels send blessings on the Prophet: O you who have believed! Ask blessings on him, and salute him with all respect.’ Allah ‘Azza wa-Jalla shows so much respect to our Holy Prophet ﷺ. He raises our Holy Prophet ﷺ so much. And there is an order to make Salawat and Salam on him ﷺ. Just like there is the order to pray and to fast, there is the order to show respect to our Holy Prophet ﷺ.

Who doesn’t love our Holy Prophet ﷺ? Shaytan. Apart from him, everyone who is not shaytan loves him ﷺ. Those who are with shaytan don’t love him ﷺ and don’t show respect to him ﷺ. Whoever says that he is Muslim and doesn’t show respect, he is also shaytan.

Since the last century, even before as well, there had been enemies of religion, enemies of Allah ﷻ and the Prophet. But during the last hundred years, they have been showing their enmity more and more. They are misleading people from the right way. They are trying to make people leave their ways. People are lost in Dunya anyway. They are paying attention to the words of useless and worthless people. And then they are destroyed. A person who doesn’t have the love of our Holy Prophet ﷺ in his heart will be destroyed. There is no other way. Nothing can save him.

Therefore, our Holy Prophet ﷺ is for our goodness. Every minute and every second our Holy Prophet ﷺ thought about his Ummah. He wanted higher ranks for them in the presence of Allah ﷻ. However, people don’t understand. One of the beautiful Hadiths of our Holy Prophet ﷺ says, ‘هي َل إِِتَ ن سَ ح َأِِن مَ ِِرَّ شَ ِِق َّتا’. Beware of the evil of someone you do goodness to. Our Holy Prophet ﷺ wanted goodness for people, but many people want badness to our Holy Prophet ﷺ. Whereas if they wish well.. he ﷺ is not asking them anything for his ﷺ benefit anyway. It is for the pleasure of Allah ﷻ, for your own benefit. To always remember him ﷺ is for your benefit and for the benefit of mankind.

What can you do? The shaytan, the enemy has promised to cheat and mislead people until Qiyamah. He is doing it without getting tired or bored. May Allah ﷻ increase the love of our Holy Prophet ﷺ. We have to think about it. We cannot do without it. If there is no love of our Holy Prophet ﷺ, you won’t be at peace in Dunya and your condition will be terrible in Akhirah. May Allah ﷻ protect us. May Allah ﷻ make us steadfast and increase our love to our Holy Prophet ﷺ.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
