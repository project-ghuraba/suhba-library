Forgotten Them

As-Salam Alaikum, ayyuhal hadirun. Those who are attending our assembly, may Salam be upon you. Dastur Ya Rijal Allah, with your dastur (heavenly permission) we ask something from you for these weak nations. You holy ones are full; O awliya, wali servants of Allah! O dear nations of the Beloved! O lions who watch the way of Allah! Grant us, look upon us with your heavenly glance. O our Umm Hiram Sultan, look upon us with your heavenly glance so that we won't be broken, won't be pressed, crushed and also we won't crush others. May they not separate us from the rank of servanthood. The best rank for mankind is servanthood. Shukur that there is permission to say this; they make us say this. O Holy ones! O beloved servants of Allah. We ask for your heavenly support. We don't know anything. You are the ones who know. May you teach us. May you whip who doesn't learn or who doesn't want to learn. May Allah not make us from those who are whipped or who deserve to be whipped. Shukur Allah, Al-Ghani Lord.

O our Lord! O our Lord, for the honour of Your Beloved... To show respect for Your Glory O Lord, we show high respect for Your Beloved. Man 'azza 'uzza. A person who values what Allah has created has value also. Allah Almighty created them. He (swt) created the servants through His Mightiness. Show high respect for whom Allah created. Who values is valued. If you keep them dear and honoured, you are kept dear and honoured too. If you don't keep them honoured and glorified, you also become as trash. Be the jewel. Be mighty. Be valuable. When do you become valuable? When you value who Allah created, when you make them honored and strong your value and honour also increase.

May Allah dress on us the honour and glory of faith. May He (swt) dress on us the glory and honour which comes through the honour of faith. Ask this. There can't be any honour greater than this. No honour can be greater than this; no honour can be greater than dressing in the glory and honour dress of Allah Almighty, He (swt) dresses you with it. He (swt) orders His Angels: "Dress on this servant of Mine the dress of honour and glory so that he becomes azeez (honored). Because he valued My servants, kept them valued and honored. He didn't throw them away like trash, didn't crush them. And whosoever stands against the ones I created and I kept alive, the slap of My Wrath and Curse comes on him! Let alone the earths but even the whole creation cannot stand the slap of My Wrath. Aman Ya Rabbi! He (swt) says "Don't even crush an ant. I created it! Whatever I created, I created it to be a glory, an honour for the mankind. So that it gives them a benefit. I didn't create them in vain. O My servants! Be valued ones! When do you become valued? Whenever you keep the ones I created honoured, you also became honoured in My Presence. If you don't value and honour the ones I created, if you crush them, I will not leave you. Especially I created the children of Adam and made them glorified ones. I made them honourable ones. The honour I granted them, I granted to no other creation. And who despises mankind, who doesn't look after the glory and honour of mankind is My foe! I created the children of Adam and granted them. The grant I bestowed on them, I didn't bestow on any other creation. Allahu Akbar! You are Subhan O Lord! Let us say then: Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. O our Lord, shukur to You. O our Lord, we glorify You. We glorify Your Beloved and all that You created, for whichever purpose You created them. We try to appreciate their value and honour.

May You make us azeez, valued ones O Lord. May You make us honoured and glorified also O Lord. "If you keep the ones I created dear and honoured as I appreciate their value and honour, I will glorify you also. You will have a different station. If You don't honour, don't value what I created, what I made; who doesn't value My creation shows that they don't respect Me! I didn't create anything in vain! "Wa 'In Min Shay'in 'Illa Yusabbihu Bihamdihi" (17:44). At the very least, that creature glorifies Me! It says "Allahu Akbar, Subhan Allah, Sultan Allah". And where are you in this, O people? "Wa 'In Min Shay'in 'Illa Yusabbihu Bihamdihi" There is not a thing that does not recite His Praises. There is not a thing that does not say about His Magnificence: Subhan Allah, Sultan Allah, Rabbuna Llah Hasbuna Llah. No, every one of them says this. There is My tamjid: "'Innahu Hamidun Majidun" (11:73).

Allahu Dhul Jalal says "I am Who is Hamid, I am Who is Majid. Know Me, glorify Me. And I will make you attain to My Paradise.I put you in My Paradises because you glorified Me and valued, honoured the ones I created. There is no creature that does not say "Subhan Allah!". "'Innahu Hamidun Majidun". There is none which doesn't say "Ya Majid Ya Hamid. Ya Majid Ya Hamid. Ya Subhan Ya Sultan". Don't be heedless! Don't be heedless. Be aware of how high and great what I granted you is. If not I take it away from you. You will be humiliated in dunya and in akhirah no one will look at your face. The nar al-jaheem, hell fire will be your destination, they will throw you there.

"Nasa Llaha Fanasiyahum" (9:67). You forget Allah, Allah forgets you too. Where is the place of the forgotten ones? The nar al-jaheem, the fires of hell. O Lord, may You forgive us. "Then O My servant, glorify Me! The door of glorifying Me is Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Who wants to praise Me, who wants to glorify Me should say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. It is enough". Aman Ya Rabbi, shukur Ya Rabbi, shukur Allah. And for this,we always open our assembly with Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. This is an ocean. It is an ocean. Whosoever says Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim opens the door of that ocean. His dunya as well as his akhirah will be prosperous. He will be from those who respect Me and who are respected in My Presence. The dunya life of whom I respect... Dunya has no value but they are respected ones in dunya also. And on the day of Judgement, they will be from the respected ones". This is a new thing: On the day of Judgement, on the foreheads of the people who are respected in the sight of Allah is written "Bismillah". It will shine so brightly: Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Nur, more light than the light of sun. "This servant of Mine said Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. He glorified Me. He should be distinguished", so on their foreheads is written like this: Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. The beauty of each one is more than the other. The elegance, the beauty, the majesty of each one is different from the other. Why? "I don't make copies". Hasha. I am Al-Mubdi'. I don't create in duplicate. I dress each of My servants with a different manifestation of My Holy Names so that when this one looks at him, he says "How beautiful". When he looks at this one, he says "How elegant". There are no end or limit to the manifestations of the Bismillah. Every day a person says it, he is dressed with a new dress of Nur. He is dressed with a new elegance, a new beauty.

Don't forget, say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Tyrants don't say it. Pharaohs don't say it. Who always says "Me" doesn't say it. But they are humiliated in dunya and they are humiliated in akhirah also. Say Allah, say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim, so that Allah Almighty says "My servant remembered My Ism Al-Jalal. Dress on him a new dress". Our Lord Almighty dresses us with a new beauty, a new Nur each time we say it. The people of Paradise will come with Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim written on their foreheads; they come like this, the Bismillah like a crescent on their foreheads and they enter Paradise without reckoning. Their dunya as well as their akhirah is prosperous. You forgot? Then you are forgetten.

How can Allah be forgotten? Aman Ya Rabbi. May You not make us from who forget, O Lord. Don't make us from the forgotten ones either, O Lord. "If you don't want to be forgotten, then don't forget Me and you won't be forgotten. If you forget Me, you will be forgotten also. You are trash. You are of no use. You will be expelled from the door". Who comes with the Bismillah on their foreheads in the shape of a crescent, their Mizan (balance) will be heavy and they enter paradise without reckoning. They will pass over the Sirat like lightening. Angels will welcome them: Salamun Alaykum Bima Sabartum Fadkhuluha Khalidina". How beautiful. Angels will welcome them: "O valued servants! Salam be upon you. The doors of Paradise are open, enter through whichever door you like. "Salamun Alaykum Bima Sabartum" (13:24). You didn't follow the wildness of your egos; you struggled against it, you were patient. For this "Fadkhuluha Khalidina" (39:73). Enter the Paradises of our Lord forever. There is no departure from it.

The Paradises are eternal, there is no eternal death but there is eternal life. What is its key? Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. For this, the angels welcome. "Fadkhuluha Khalidina". O servants, beautiful servants who glorify our Lord. "Fadkhuluha Khalidina". Enter in it eternally. "Fadkhuluha". Here you are, enter. Bima Sabartum. You persevered in patience. You didn't listen to your dirty ego. Here you are, enter O dear servants of Allah, fadkhuluha. Enter My Paradises. Bima sabartum. Be patient ones. Say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim, angels welcome you and you enter Paradise. Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim is an ocean. Angels welcome them in the door: "O dear servants of Allah, Fadkhuluha Khalidina". The doors of Paradise open for them. Angels greet them with Salam and say: "Come on in, eternally. There is no going out ever again. These Paradises are for you. Ilal abad, abadiyyu-l abad". Allah! Shukur our Lord who granted us to say the Bismillah.

Who wants to do goodness may say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. The life of who doesn't want is ruined and he is also humiliated in akhirah. Don't forget the Bismillah! Let us say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Bismillahi lladhi la yadurru ma`a Ismihi shay'un fi l-ardi wa la fi s-sama'i wa Huwa s-Sami`u l-`Alim. Recite the Bismillah and blow it on yourself, nothing can harm you. You don't need any doctor or judge. "Fadkhuluha Khalidina". Angels welcome them. "Come on in, come on in to stay forever. Our Lord is pleased with you, come on in. His Paradises are for you eternally, come on in O Allah's pure servants, O believers". Dive into the Mercies of the Bismillah.

What should be the first thing you make children accustomed to? Make them accustomed to say Bismillahir-Rahmanir-Rahim and these children will be under guarantee. They won't get sick or won't be disobedient ones. They won't turn into ugly or tasteless creatures. Whoever sees them feels relieved and says "what a beautiful child". Caress your children with the Bismillah, say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim and Nur comes down on them. Now dunya has become without the Bismillah and they are fighting each other, all of them are without the Bismillah. This world will not be corrected until these people say Bismillahir r-Rahmani r-Rahim. If they don't say, they will continue fighting each other. There will be no goodness in their dunya or akhirah.

Ya Rabbi Tubna wa Raja'na ilayh. Tawba Ya Rabbi, tawba Ya Rabbi, tawba Astaghfirullah. May You not make us from the heedless ones O Lord. And He (swt) doesn't but it is us who fall into heedlessness. May Allah protect us for the honour of His Beloved. Who will be able to stay under the banner of the Prophet are who say Bismillahir-Rahmani r-Rahim. Who will drink from the pool of kawthar are who say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. The doors of eight Paradises will open to who say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. O people! Arabs and Turks, Muslims, hear and listen. The prosperity of dunya life is nothing, but listen so that you won't be deprived of the beauty of the eternal akhirah.

Fa la tahrimna Ya Rabbi, don't make us from the deprived ones O Lord. Don't make us from the heedless ones. Dress on us value, dress on us from the Nur of the Bismillah. Say everyday at least seven times Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim and wipe it over your face. Your face enlightens, your body revives. Your name is written with the names of beloved servants in the Divine Presence of Allah. Let us say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Ya Rahman Ya Rahim. Ya Rahman Ya Rahim. Say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim and then go out into the streets. And we see how the situation of dunya will be. Now they scream in the streets like the animals that kick and stamp. Why do you scream? Why do you shout? Why don't you call to Who created you? Who do you call to in the streets? Be Man, don't be animal. Protect yourself, say "Aman Ya Rabbi". We should say "Allah Hu, Allah Haqq, Allah Hu Allah Haqq, Allah Hu Allah Haqq". Say this and march in the streets! What is it that you seek - the goods of this three-day dunya life? Shame on you! Shame on them that they leave the jewel and seek the filth! "Dunya is a carcass" said the Prophet (sas), not something to be loved. Allah Almighty made ready for you what should be loved: Seek your Lord and say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Make us not forget O Lord. He (swt) doesn't make us to forget. Angels also: "Salamun `Alaykum Bima Sabartum", you were patient in dunya. You make service for the servanthood of your Lord and Allah Almighty granted you to enter Paradise through His Generosity". "Fadkhuluha Khalidina". Enter eternally for there is no return from Paradise. There is no death in Paradise.

The paradises are for the handsome ones. You will be dressed with Nur, everyday you will dress new Nurs. Say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim.O Lord, make us not to forget the Bismillah. Hasha, He (swt) doesn't make us forget but it is our dirty ego which does. Allah doesn't make you to forget. Tawba Ya Rabbi. Say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Bi smika llahumma Ya Rabbi, wa bi Hamdika Ya Rabbi. We praise You with the Bismillah. We glorify You and show high respect for Your Order. We are Your weak servants. May You send us Your Divine Grants O Lord. May we not forget the Bismillah, remember Your Ism Al-Jalil and walk. Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Bismillahi lladhi layadurru ma`a Ismihi shay'un fi l-ardi wa la fi s-sama'i. There is nothing that can harm you in the Heavens or in earths after you say this Bismillah.

O Allah, may You lift this tiredness off us with the barakah of Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. You are the Lord Al-Jalil. The servants of my Jalil Lord can't be wretched ones. The servants of our Jalil Lord can't be disgraced or humiliated, hasha! O Lord! O Lord, O Lord... In this three day dunya life, forgetting You is the biggest fault. To forget Allah for the dirty life of three days and fight after dunya is not the glory of Man. Say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim and all doors open for you in dunya and in akhirah. If not, suffer. And they will suffer. Whips on top of whips will come. Astaghfirullah. Astaghfirullah Tubna wa Raja'na Ilayk Ya Rabbi.

We should not forget or we will be forgotten. Our Lord is Allah Almighty. There is no end to His Grants and Generosity. O Lord, I seek refuge in You from being wretched. Hasha, servants of Rabbu-l Jalil can't be wretched. Can't be. O our Lord, Tubna wa Raja'na Ilayk. We are asking for all the people O Lord, for all the people. The Prophet of the End of Times came for all people. He (sas) came to save all the people, to protect us from the evil of shaytan and he came to save us from the fire of hell. Respect him. Love him and keep his way. Your dunya and akhirah will be prosperous.

Ya Rabbi Tubna wa Raja'na Ilayh. Ya Rabbi Tubna wa Raja'na Ilayh. We repent O Lord. O Allah, our Subhan, our Sultan, send us a Master for the honour of Your Beloved. O our Umm Hiram Sultan, may you look upon us with your heavenly glance. I have become weak. May we get strong. May we be strengthened in this life and run in the way of our Lord's servanthood Send us divine support. Amin wa Salamu 'ala l Mursalin. O scholars! Allah Almighty says "Wa Dhakkirhum". "Wa Dhakkirhum Bi'ayyami Llahi" (14:5). Remind these people of the good days and grants of Allah Almighty. Is there one person who comes out and says "This thing you do is wrong" You don't deserve to scream in the streets. Don't you have mosques? Why do you forget the invitation of Who created you? Is there no Creator for you? There is. If there is, turn to Him and ask from Him.

Ya Rabbi, may You forgive us. May You send us the Master, the Sultan who will show us the right ways O Allah. That Master will not need any weapons. He says Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim and his work succeeds. The work of who doesn't say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim never, ever succeeds. Aman Ya Rabbi.

We seek refuge in You. May You not deprive us of remembering Your Ism Al-Jalil. He (swt) doesn't. We may say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Wa bihi Nasta'in. We seek refuge in Allah. We may ask what we want for the honour of Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim, for the honour of His Beloved and our Lord will grant it. He is the Most Generous of the Generous.

Ya Rabbi tawba Ya Rabbi. May this tiredness be lifted off us. May we stand like lions in Your way, against shaytan and shaytan's armies. We may say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim.

Fatiha.
