The news that will make us happy will come. 1000 Salawat, 1000 la ilaha illa Llah for the dervishes. 100 Istighfar, 100 Surat al Ikhlas.

G.S. Mawlana says the people should do the qiyam al-layl/night prayer even if it is at least for 2 rakats. What dhikrs should they do?

M. 1000 Salawat, 1000 Shahada, 100 Ikhlas al-Sharif.

G.S. And you also said 100 Istighfar?

M. Yes, 100 Istighfar. 100x Ya Latif. 100x Ya Jabbar Ya Ghaffar. Ya Mu'in al-ajeezin (O Helper of the helpless), 100x. I congratulate all of them, let all of them be straight/true! Let them be with the true ones or they will get beaten by the stick! No one should go to the wrong ways, even if it is his grandfather, he will still get the stick! They should take the straight way. As long as they continue on the wrong way, the stick will always be on their back. They will either be men or become a donkey for shaytan. May Allah protect us from this.

This month has started with great power. The 10th of Muharram comes after this. Insha Allah the Sultan of this time comes and after him will come Mahdi (as). Ya Rabbi may You make us reach it also. Be true & get saved! If you are untrue you will be beaten with the stick, in dunya & in akhirah. Aman ya Rabbi, tawba ya Rabbi.

Continue with the Istighfar. 100 Astaghfirullah. Be careful with your prayers. Prayers are important. There is so much that will befall those who don't pray. They can't be saved, can't be saved.. The broom is coming to dunya, the big broom is coming from upstairs. It will sweep. May Allah not make us from the rubbish it will sweep. May we go with the clean servants.

O Lord, You know. Tawba Ya Rabbi, tawba.. O Umm Hiram Sultan, may you look upon us with your glance of mercy, may you send us a power. These people are left to be ignorant ones. They are left as ignorant ones, no one is left to teach. O Allah, may You send us from Your servants one who will teach us.

I am shy to ask from Allah Almighty, shy to ask from the Prophet (sas) also but I am asking from the awliyas. There is the Qutb al-Mutasarrif who is watching over us. He can reach from East to West. May he send us a power. Fatiha.
