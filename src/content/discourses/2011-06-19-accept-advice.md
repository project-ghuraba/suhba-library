Sunday, June 19, 2011 Translated from Arabic by: http://saltanat.org/ BismillahirRahmanirRahim Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar, La Ilaha illa Allah, wa Allahu Akbar. Allahu Akbar wa Lillahi Al Hamd. I am sorry. I am sorry for the nation, for those of the nation who are going out into the streets and demonstrating. I never heard from anyone that the demonstrators are making Takbir or glorifying their Lord. No! Instead, they are like beasts, spreading in the streets and alleys. And there is no haiba / heybet (grandeur, prestige) on the ummah, it has been lifted. Thumma Assalat wa al Salam àla Sayidil awwalin wa al akhirin. Praises on the Master of the begin-ning and the end. Ya Habib, Habib Allah, Oh our beloved, beloved of Allah intercede for us. Intercede for us. All of us now became ignorant ones. The kings,their kings, their leaders, the majority of people - all of them are now ignorant, ignorant, ignorant ones. I am sorry. And the ummah is drunk, as if they have gone astray. I am not going to say like animals; no, they are like 'ad-dawab' (crawling creatures) - they don't know their right side from their left side, or where they stand and where to sit down. They don't even know what they were told and what they have been giv-en as a responsibility. They don't know. Their men look like ladies. Young men are trying to resemble women; and their ladies want to look like men, like their men. Yesterday I heard that in Saudi Arabia, the women protested against the government. Is this the order of Allah? - or what did our Lord (jwa) say?

Astaghfirullah, astaghfirullah, astaghfirullah. 50 years or 60 years ago, I was in Madina al Munawwara àla saqinha afdalu salat wa tamu al taslim Hazreti Muhammad (sas). At that time in Rajab the holy month of Allah there was a lunar eclipse, Subhana-Allah! And this year also (1432) a lunar eclipse took place in Rajab Al Murajjab, Allah Akbar! I was in seclusion in Madina Al Munawwara àla saqinha afdalu salat wa afdalu al taslim. I could hear from the minarets the mua'zins were asking forgiveness from Allah Almighty. They were not using these speakers, electric microphones, or recordings like nowadays. This was after the Kusuf prayer in the Min-arets until the eclipse would be clear from the moon, from the full moon. Continuously until the eclipse cleared, the Mua'zzinun was asking forgiveness from Allah. This was ordered by the Efendi or Mufti at that time. "Astaghfirullah, astaghfirullah, astaghfirullah," because this was a sign. It was not good tid-ings. The lunar eclipse is not good news. It is a sign of the Lord's Anger. This moon eclipse happened to wake you up. Therefore ask forgiveness from Allah! This is an order from Allah. So ask forgiveness from Allah, Astaghfirullah! Astaghfirullah! “Oh My servants, ask forgive-ness from Me (astaghfiruni)”. And what happened last Friday? Last Friday there was a [full moon] lunar eclipse.
