Shaykh Mehmet: As-Salamu Alaikum. Audhu Billahi min al shaytan al rajim, Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Assalatu wa s-salamu ala Rasulina Muhammad Sayyidi l Awwalin wa l-Akhirin. Madad ya Rasul Allah, Madad ya Ashab al Rasulullah, madad S Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, madad ya S.Muhammad Nazim al Haqqani, dastur!

Sadaqa/charity..There are all kinds of charity. And one kind of charity is to smile at your Muslim brother, look at him with a beautiful face. Looking at him smiling, not in an angry way is sadaqa, says the Hadith. Mashayikhs, all their faces are enlightened, like nur, as if they are smiling already. And some of them make many jokes, this is very nice as well.

Of course joking is not by lying, as the Prophet (sas) said. Mashayikhs, they make nice jokes. And Mawlana also, everyone knows, he always makes nice jokes to people. And he gives a lesson with that joke. He narrates many times from the old stories, jokes as well.

In the Golden Chain.. We once went to a visit - ee went to make ziyarah for Mahmud Injir al-Faghnawi Hz, he was also a very humorous person. Whoever came to him would not leave without laughing. Really, when we were next to his grave, it was a nice incident, laughing at Mawlana's jokes. It was very nice.

Mawlana (Rumi) Hz also..Now people, they read Mathnawi & see him sitting like this. They think that he is a person who never laughed in his life. But he has many jokes. And there is one of his jokes that comes to our mind which exactly suits our followers...Our followers, other followers all same.. It is a joke that suits them. Today people may laugh a little. When he was sitting with Shams al-Tabriz in his dargah, some people came from Aksehir side (district of Konya). And they said, "Send us one or two Shaykhs, that our people will have a renewal/ihya too." Mawlana Rumi said, "Go out & look around in the courtyard. Whoever you find, take them with you". They left happily taking 2 people, they took like picking sheep. Then Mawlana Rumi said to Shamsi Tabrizi, "thanks to Allah they didn't ask for mureeds. When they asked for Shaykh, all of them are Shaykhs anyways. There is no mureed" He made a joke like this.

So it is like this; smiling face, jokes. But not rough jokes, should not be taunts or pranks. It is a nice thing among the mureeds, among the people. Even when a person walking in the street see you with a smiling face like this, feel symphathy, become "uns"/familiar. Don't sulk, making people run away. This is also very important. It is considered as sadaqa & also uns/ familiarity. When it is friendly, there won't be hatred. When a person hates you you feel a sorrow inside you, or feel strange. This is also very important.

Our Prophet (sas) is already always in a smiling state. Rarely, even when he got angry only would turn his face, would not shout at anyone. They may be a model, example for us. We must take them as role models & imitate. It gives men all kind of benefits.

Today we speak about sadaqa. There are many kinds of sadaqa. To give money, to give food, to give help, to take- clean road. One kind of sadaqa also, to be smiling for your Muslim brothers. Smiling, it is very nice. And Allah give reward for this as sadaqa. And all awliya & mashayikh, they have nice face. Without smiling even. But most of them, all of them, they were smiling. Because Prophet (sas) also ordering for smiling and he never look like upset..When he look like upset he put his eyes down & not looking at people. Just like this, not shouting or making.. And you cannot look angry for his face (sas). And Mawlana Shaykh also, all time he is liking to make joke and to make... smiling for people and making people happy.

Once we were in, before 11 years, maqam- qabir Mahmood Injir al-Faghnawi. He also was like Mawlana, like to make joke too much. And people was laughing too much. When we were in his grave, Mawlana start speaking, all people was laughing, laughing.. 1 hour was laughing, like this. And even Mawlana Jalaluddin Rumi, they make picture for him sitting like this. It is you see, all time in his life never laughing you thought. But he was also making too much joke and good stories for people to be happy, to laugh also. One of his stories, and it was real, happened. But he make joke for this. When he was in his dargah with Shamsi Tabrizi some people coming from another town. It is Aksehir, near Konya, maybe 1-2 hour by caravan, 3-4 days by walking from Konya. They said " we don't have any Shaykh in our village. Can you send us 1 or 2 Shaykhs?" Mawlana was saying to them: "Go outside the dargah, in the garden. Take any Shaykh you want. You can take & go". And they take 2 & they were happy, going and Mawlana Jalaluddin Rumi was happy, and laughing for, saying to Shamsi Tabrizi, "Alhamdulillah, they come wanting a Shaykh, mashayikh they don't want mureed. So, mashayikh we have too much mashayikh. But mureed, no.. Many stories like this. It was nice to smile, even when you are going for your brother in Tariqat or other, in road also, when go shopping. And when you smiling, people will be happy, more happy. And to be happy, it affect on your soul and you get benefit. Because you make sadaqa, and you are rewarded for this.

Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, Al Fatiha.
