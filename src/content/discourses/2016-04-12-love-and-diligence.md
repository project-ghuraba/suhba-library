We have our promising for you, ya RasulAllah, to keep our salatu s-salam as long as Allah Almighty, He give us counting of our breathing. Make your intention for RasulAllah (saw), to keep him in your heart, to keep him in deep of your heart, to not be able to take him out of your heart. You make your promising to RasulAllah (saw), and RasulAllah (saw) will answer you - "Yes, I'm accepting your promising". It's very important. They say the promising, it is the most valuable things in the human nature. But after that, they make paper, they make notarize, they make steal, they make seal, they make so many things that that people keep their promising. But Allah Almighty, He is only recognizing the mouth promising. He will never ask a writing from you. He say "Your promising to Me is by your word." And the first promising is Ash'hadu an la ilaha illAllah wa sh'hadu anna Muhammadan abduhu wa Rasuluh. This is the biggest promising. You cannot find bigger than this promising. Did Allah Almighty ask you to sign a paper and to put your seal under it and to say "Yes, I promise"? No. He said "Only say Ash'hadu an la ilaha illAllah wa sh'hadu anna Muhammadan abduhu wa Rasuluh. This is My enough promising. I'm not asking more than this."

Why we are saying this? Because today situation is requiring this. Now we have so many people acting in the name of Islam. And they think they are the most higher level of Islamic authority and they are free to do everything for anybody in front of them. And they make so many fatwas that Islam never agree. Islam never agree with such, Allah never agree with such thing, RasulAllah (saw) never agree with such thing. That's why, Allah Almighty He ask from His servant to declare that he believe in Him and to declare that he believe in His RasulAllah (saw). This is the only thing. After that - making prayer, not making prayer, doing the duty, not doing the duty, it is the second level.

This is very famous story about one bedouin. He come in Friday prayer and RasulAllah (saw), he was in mihrab giving the khutba of Friday. And the bedouin was so huge that he put his hand like this and the other hand like this in the door of Masjid an-Nabawi and he closed the door. So huge person. And he's saying "Ya RasulAllah! Ya RasulAllah, how can I be with you? If I love you, it will be enough for me?" And RasulAllah (saw) "Yes, it will be enough for you." Say "Ya RasulAllah, if I love you, it will be enough for me?" He said "Yes, it will be enough for you." And then he said again "Ya RasulAllah, if I love you, it will be enough for me?" He said "Yes, it will be enough for you." Everybody think that he will come and pray Juma with them but he release his hand from the door and he walk away. And RasulAllah (saw) said "Yuhsharu l-mar'u ma'a man ahab" That means the one, he will be together the one he love, the one he is with. Very important.

If you love RasulAllah (saw), you will be with RasulAllah (saw), you will be in his company. This is how it is fantastic the meaning of love for somebody. And especially the love for RasulAllah (saw). This is our main target in our life - to understand how we can be in certain level or how we can be in certain areas. Now there is another thing in our way to Allah and RasulAllah (saw), called ijtihad. The ijtihad, it means your effort. This man, he became sahabi to RasulAllah (saw), that bedouin. Even he didn't make any practicing. But his love to RasulAllah (saw) and his looking to RasulAllah (saw), he registered as sahaba to RasulAllah (saw). But there is other sahaba that'd been with RasulAllah (saw), they had kind of effort. The effort is very important in our religion.

When you make effort to make this service to be further, Allah Almighty, He give you. That's why, in the religion also there is levels. In the religion there is also ranks. In the religion, there is also noble people and there is ordinary people. Why we are saying like this? We are not communist. We are not communist. The communist, they say everybody same. One man says "I'm professor." Ya Hu - same. The one he cleans the road same as professor. It is not different. Then he say "No. Allah Almighty, He make levels." These levels, it is important for us to get on. Because whatever we are having with these levels then we understand are we appreciated by Allah Almighty or not. Because we have the important things in our life to be also appreciated by your effort. Everybody has some kind of work.

Somebody has a grocery shop. He says "My grocery shop is enough for me, my living. It's more than enough". But some people, they say "I want to make mega supermarket." You cannot stop him because his vision and view, it's mega view. This is also in the spiritual way also. If you are going in the spiritual way, you can be happy to have a grocery shop or you can be having your mega dreams to be there. Allah Almighty, He make it open for everybody. This is important for us to understand the meaning of ijtihad. So many people they say "What kind of ijtihad? What is this ijtihad? Why this ijtihad?" Yes. Ijtihad [diligence], it is there to be more perfect, to be more nearer to RasulAllah (saw), nearer to Allah Almighty.

There is things called fard and things called nawafil. Now we speak again for salafi, wahhabi, ISIS people - say you only pray fard, it's enough. Ya Hu, this fard, it's named fard. This you have to do it. This is you cannot run away. You have hajj. This also fard. You have to do it. You have fasting. This is also fard. You have to do it. You have to give zakat. This is also fard. You have to give it. You have to be believer as - ash'hadu an la ilaha illAllah, ash'hadu anna Muhammadan abduhu wa rasuluh. This also fard. You have to do it. This is you cannot run away from. But the nawafil that Allah Almighty He love to see His servant making effort to grow up his wealth. This is the spiritual wealth. Why you are not happy to grow up your spiritual wealth? Why you are not allowing the others to grow their spiritual wealth? Because you are lazy, because you are making the fard more than enough.

Ya Hu, you make your business. Anybody will be happy with his business to have the salary of worker? Never. You'll say "No, I cannot earn as my worker. I must own 100x of my worker, 200x of my worker, 1,000x of my worker. I have to be more rich. I have to be more ahead. I have to be more famous. I have to be more." You cannot see in these dunya people somebody say "I want to be humble". No. Look for the artist. Look for the rich people. Look for the political people. Look for presidents. Look for ministers. If somebody, he is parliament member, he says "My dream to be minister." If he is minister, "My dream to be prime minister". If he is prime minister, "My dream to be president." Ruling. Nobody saying "I have to be like ordinary citizen." No.

Everybody have dream. Why you are not using this in Allah Almighty field and say "Fard is more than enough? Don't do more than this. No need, no need, no need. Allah no need. Ya Hu, this is for me." Allah Almighty, if you pray for Him or you don't pray for Him, it is say, if you make ijtihad or you don't make ijtihad Allah Almighty - Allah is different. But it is for you, for your own purpose. Today they put very nice table or very nice food. And you are very hungry and say "I'm humble man. I can go and eat some bread and salt and water. It's more than enough for me"? Do you think it will be like this? He will jump in the food table and say "This is my table. This is not for you. I have to eat it alone. You eat too much. I'm hungry one. You cannot eat." Why you don't be hungry for spirituality?

We must be hungry for spirituality. This is advise for all salafi, all wahhabi to make them understand the spirituality, how value it is. Because only the spirituality, it is remaining. The rest, it will be in the toilet, like eating food, it will be in the toilet. Whatever you do, it will be in the toilet. Because we are living in the toilet. Dunya is a big toilet by the way. Yes. That's why Allah Almighty, He put you in toilet to make your spirituality and spiritual higher and higher to not touch in this dirty dunya.

May Allah forgive us and make us in the right way with spirituality and make us with the good one, to make us with the true one, to make us in the true way. That's why we have to love RasulAllah (saw) because he is the most true one. RasulAllah, he is the most precious one. He is the most of the most. Nothing above him. Nothing. That's why whoever tell you this is bid’ah, this is that, don't listen to him. Just focus in RasulAllah (saw) because he is the purest, the purest, the purest, is shining like the sun. It is important for us to understand this point. And after that nothing will take us away from the good way.
