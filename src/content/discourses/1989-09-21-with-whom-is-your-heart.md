And we are asking from Allah Almighty His blessings. Through His blessings coming something on ourselves also. And only I am asking from my Grandsheikh to grant ourselves something to be short but sharp - to remain through our hearts. If we are leaving to our minds, minds after some while it is going to forget, but hearts keeping going. Mind working by ‘pil’ (Turkish for battery). This instrument if you are using batteries after a while coming weak. Minds such a worker. But hearts, that belongs to Allah Almighty from you.

Allah Almighty says, "whole your body for you, O Bani Adam, O the children of Adam, only your heart for Me". "The hearts of believers, they are My thrones. Thrones, royal thrones, and I am there. Keep that throne for Me, alone. Don't leave anyone comes and sit on it. No, I am there only. And I am very jealous to look anyone else to be on My throne." Yes, no one going to be happy to find a person in his home. Or I don't think that any king or sultan or agong (title for Malaysian king) to be happy to find one person sitting on his throne. Yes, you know this. How you are not looking after your throne through your hearts? To whom you are renting, if you are not selling, to whom you are renting your heart? You must look after it.

Yes, you are professors, you must know. You rented it or sold it? What you are doing? How is your heart? With whom your heart? And He every time, looking after our hearts, not looking our faces. Our forms, it is not important. Important for the Lord, our hearts. He is looking and asking "My servant with whom?" You must say, you must ask yourself at least seven times daily. You must ask, "My heart with whom?" "My heart with whom?" If you can't asking 7 times, at least because 24,000 times Allah Almighty looking. "My servant with whom? With whom?" And for you at least 7 times, if you are not looking after your heart at least 7 times, it is difficult for you to save real faith when you are dying. It means at least 7 times you must be with your Lord, through your heart. If more, it is more honour to you.

And every time when you are meeting with your Lord through your heart, dressing you. It is not balash (for free). What is balash? When you are meeting with Lord Allah Almighty, through your heart, He is going to pay you, dressing you - Nur (light). A dress from Nur. Blessing and making you closer to His Divinely Presence. Look after yourself. Time going on. Yesterday we were small ones, today, yes, we are adults. Tomorrow we are going. Yes, quickly, time running. Particularly as Sayyidina Rasulullah (sas) informed that when last days approaching time going to run very quickly. And we are living in the time of 'quick-time'. Everything fast. You must run. We are running. We are like very speed-ful. And therefore our lives speedfully running to finish. You can't reduce it, it is not car, to make reduce your speed. Written there, "reduce now". Reduce speed, we can't do it. All of us, same speed running.

Therefore, as much as you can be able to approach, to be closer from Divine Presence, do it. Alhamdulillah, we are happy finding some people that they are asking their Lord's pleasur and asking to be more closer to their Lord. Educated people, understanding people. And we hope that it should be day by day spreading through yourselves, around yourselves, to awake people - To awake them to know about their lives, to know about dunya, to know about Mawla. And before leaving this life to make Mawla pleased with us. That is main target. Who making his Lord pleased with him, just he did what expected from him through this life. Expected from everyone to make his Lord, Almighty Allah, pleased with him.

Anything else? I don't know if anything more important than this point. Yes. And we are asking blessings from Allah Almighty, and to make ourselves successful on that way, before leaving this life. Leaving, and our Lord pleased with us, with ourselves. May Allah grants us from His endless blessings and favours, to be His sincere servants, and to follow His Beloved Muhammad, peace be upon him, step by step. And we are asking tawfiq (success) for that purpose from Allah Almighty's endless favours.

Bi hurmati l-Habib, bi hurmati l-Fatiha, it is enough.
