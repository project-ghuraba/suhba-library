As-Salamu Alaikum wa Rahmatullahu wa Barakatuhu. Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fil jamia' In today's sohbat, we will talk about trade.

When it is the trade of dunya, there is gaining as well as losing in trade. As Allah wills, no matter how smart a person is, the trader can lose, or he can gain & make profit as well. And we can see this in dunya now that some men, in one day they can say "whole world is mine"- houses, yachts, cars, banks, everything in their hand. And 2-3 days later you see that he went bankrupt & fled the country. If you ask "how can we do a trade which will not lose like this?" There is a trade that never loses, it is the trade of akhirah.

Once a mu'min, believes in Allah & does all as He orders, he is always making profit. If he is rich, he is making profit. If he is poor, still if he is patient- patient with Allah's order, tawakkul, he again makes profit, even more profit. Such a person never loses. The trade of akhirah is like this. It is a trade that never loses. But those people who have no faith, those without iman, they are always losing. No matter how rich they are, no matter how well is their health & wealth, they are losing. Because they are temporary benefits/ blessings. Allah granted them so that you do trade with them & your akhirah be happy.

As we said, if you are poor, you have nothing to trade. If you be patient with it, you can be happy with that also. If illness comes on you, you be patient with it and you make profit again on Allah's side with this also. There is a profit in everything but a believer should think about it. "I will do everything to please Allah" he should say.

In our Prophet's time (sas), there was a man from the big Sahaba, Abu Talha Hz. He had a son who got ill, he got very ill. He got so upset, seeing his own son dying before his very eyes. He couldn't take it anymore so he went out for a while to get some fresh air. It is said that right after he left home, his son passed away. He came home at night, asked his wife, "How is he? What has happened to him?" She said "He found the perfect rest, such that he can never be more restful than this". And he didn't understand exactly what she meant. Of course, the ladies of Sahaba never lie. Really, that misery left him. So that night she cooked dinner, made a feast so that he won't get upset. They also went to zifaf/slept together. Later she explained to him & he got angry. He went to the Prophet (sas) explaining, "It happened like this, how can she do this?". The Peophet (sas) made dua for them for a good offspring. And really she got pregnant that very night. When she gave birth to the baby, he said "Don't suckle the boy before I show him to the Prophet (sas)." He took the boy to the Prophet (sas), he also brought with him some dates. He said to the Prophet, this is the boy. The Prophet (sas) asked,what do you have with you? Of course he knows what he has..He took one from the dates, chewed it & put it into the baby's mouth. And 9 scholars came from that boy- Hafiz, Hafiz for Qur'an, the ones who had knowledge of Quran.

So it was like this. Before the people would say for everything "All comes from Allah" but of course it is not an easy thing that a child dies. But the Sahaba lady knows how much they gain with it. Of course we can't be like this now. People's faith is very weak. They can't do that much but the little troubles, calamities..Whether they are small or big if you can be patient with it saying "All comes from Allah" for sure there is a reward for it. It is written all in your trade account. Your account is going up. You get rich in akhirah insha Allah. Of course for who has money, it is not patience but if he makes shukur for it, that is also a benefit for him, his profit is going up & up.

Today we speak about trading. Must be, make trading today. When you make trading in this dunya, in this world or in this country where you are living many people, they make trade. In trading, you are winning & you are losing also. Sometimes you bring something, nobody heard or little bit. Or many people they like to buy this thing. You win too much. Sometimes they bring something, maybe it is nobody like it. You are losing. In dunya, in this world, like this everywhere. Here in another country, every country, trader, some of them win, earn big money, make money. Some of them losing money. But there is one trade, not you not lose anything - All time you win. You get benefit, you make benefit every time. What is this? This is for last day, who become mu'min, believe in Allah, in Prophet (sas) & making worshipping. This is who never lose. Because if you lose money in this way and you be patient, Allah give more for akhirah.

If you are making charity, also. You are rich & making charity, you also, you win much & much. And maybe you are poor. You don't have anything. You be patient for this & say "this coming from Allah" Allah make this for you, for akhirah- your account going big in akhirah. Maybe sometimes you have bad thing happen to you, or to family or your health or another thing happened. Or in country some difficulty, no job, you are poor.. But when you are be patient & waiting from Allah, Allah give for you. It is not bad. Because you have belief in last day. Last day all this difficulty, you are like trading, putting putting- slowly slowly going, you are rich in akhirah. If you are in dunya not rich also, but in akhirah rich. This is for mu'min, the believer for Allah (awj). And there is loser. The loser, who not believe in Allah. Even he is the most rich in world but not believe in Allah it is not richness. He is, this is real poorness for him. There, they are loser. Because dunya quickly finish. And when you coming to real world you find you don't have anything. You are minus.

And this is what Sahaba they were knowing this very well. There was one story about one Sahaba. His name was Abu Talha. He had one very ill boy, child. He was in his house & looking looking & he was getting very sad. And he said "I must go out & look around & after I come". When he went out, this boy passed away. And his mother, she hide him & waiting for husband to

come in night time. Preparing food & everything. When he come, he asked about him: "How is the child?" She said, he is very rest, he never be rest like this". Because finish this harm- they are not lying, Sahaba. After they eat & sleep together. She told him & he come upset. And going for Rasulullah (sas), telling "this what happened". Rasulullah (sas), he make dua for this night: "Insha Allah you have baby insha Allah from this night". After when it happened & delievered, also boy coming. And Sahaba Abu Talha (ra), he said "I will never give milk for this boy before Rasulullah (sas) see him". And he took him & bring some dates with him, gift for Prophet (sas). Show him this boy. Prophet (sas) asked "what you bring?" He said date. Take one date in his mouth&chewing date. And from chewing, putting in his mouth, this boy's mouth & called him Abdullah. And this, from this boy coming 9 boy, he had children. So Abu Talha, 9 but all of them they was knowing Qur'an by heart. They were big scholars from Islam.

And this is how Sahaba, they believed. And Allah give them in dunya, in akhirah also. So insha Allah these days, last days of dunya. It is very difficult for everybody. Everybody asking "we have no job, we are ill, we are problem with job, or coming with wife problem, with family"...So you be patient, Allah reward for you for akhirah. And you will be happy because you have this world problem. Or if you don't have problem, also the same. You are trader, and you are never losing.

This is Alhamdulillah, Allah make us from Ummat Muhammad (sas). This is very good tiding for us. This is Imam al-Busiri saying: Good tiding for Muslim because Allah all time looking for them & never they are never losing.

Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, Al Fatiha.
