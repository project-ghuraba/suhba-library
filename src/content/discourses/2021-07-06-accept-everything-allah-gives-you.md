As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Wa-mā qadarū Llāha ḥaqqa qadrihi" (39:67), says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. They don't know AllāhNone. As much as they don't know, they don't value HimNone. In fact, "Wa-s-samāwātu maṭwiyyātun bi-yamīnihī" (39:67). Heavens and earth are too small, they are nothing next to the greatness of AllāhNone. One of our brothers asked a question that came to his heart yesterday. Most people might have asked this question in their childhood. It is not a question to be asked in adulthood. But most of the world believes like this.

So what is this question? "Why did Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla create this world with so much suffering, poverty, and illnesses, with people killing each other? Would it not be possible for them to be in paradise without all of this?" This question cannot be asked by those of us who are Muslim. A Muslim, who thinks and believes, cannot ask such questions. Non-Muslims usually ask this question, either Christians, or Jews. The rest don't care about anything anyway or they don't accept anything. Therefore, most of the world does not know the power, greatness, and grandness of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla didn't ask, while creating you, if you have any wishes or how you want it to be, if you want to suffer in this world and choose to go either to paradise or hell. HeNone doesn't ask such things. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is the Creator. HeNone created us. We don't have any right. We don't have any power. We don't have any chance to ask Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, "Why did YouNone do this? What did YouNone do it for?" A person with sound mind doesn't even think like this. Because if he thinks a little, he knows Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla does what HeNone wants. "Lā yus’alu ‘ammā yaf‘al" (21:23). No one can question HisNone actions. And no one can question HisNone wisdom. Old people say you can't question HisNone wisdom. It is not a joke.

Christians and Jews misbehaved as much as they could against Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. They say, "Ādam ‘alayhi s-salām was in paradise. He made a mistake and committed a sin. Because of his sin, all people are sinful because of him. If he didn't eat the fruit, we would be in paradise." They don't say, "AllāhNone wanted, so it happened." Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla sent Ādam ‘alayhi s-salām to earth for test. We, as Banī Ādam, as children of Ādam ‘alayhi s-salām, are tested in this world. Whoever passes will go to a place he deserves. Whoever fails will go where he deserves too. You cannot question why AllāhNone created hell and paradise. Our power is useless. Not only the whole world, but even if the whole universe was against AllāhNone, they wouldn't be able to do anything. We should know this. We should understand this. Muslims are not allowed to ask such questions. It is wrong.

Others, especially Christians have been doing so since the beginning. Even the apostles of ‘Īsā ‘alayhi s-salām, those who were the closest to him, asked him such a weird question. They asked, "Can Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla send down a table with food?" The question itself is wrong. Is there anything AllāhNone cannot do? Where did you get all of your food and drink from? Therefore, it is normal for people who follow this completely broken religion to say such things.

But people who are Muslim should not imitate them and ask such questions. The biggest thing is, "Fa‘‘ālun li-mā yurīd" (85:16). Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is the Absolutely Powerful, who can do anything and everything HeNone wants. That is why you should banish those things, which don't suit the essence of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, out of your mind. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla can do everything that comes to your mind or not. There is nothing Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla can't do.

May AllāhNone make us firm on this way. It is HisNone way. We ask for this. Everything HeNone does for us is a great blessing. And the greatest blessing is for us to follow this way firmly, in shā’a Llāh.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Wa-mā qadarū Llāha ḥaqqa qadrihi" (39:67). Meaning, the human being now, of course other maybe they are better, like jinn or other. HeNone can, HeNone do everything.

Why we saying this? Because many times people, they asking question. There is, when you are in childhood, you can say this. One brother, he ask something many people they ask him. This [if] children – okay. It is we not blame them, they don't have real knowledge. But when you are getting older, Muslim people, they are respecting. They are – this question, not asking too much. War and killing each other, doing very bad thing. "Why AllāhNone not make... why HeNone make this, all this for people? and not put them in paradise without this. It is difficult for HimNone?"

This is very wrong question for Muslim. For non-Muslim, they are all time they doing this. Not one second they not blame Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla or other, anybody, prophets, anybody for this. This mentality of Western people. But for Muslim, this is – you cannot say this. Because you are not by yourself. "O, my LordNone, I am – I will born but I want to be rich and happy and this. If YouNone don't make, give me this, I will not come to this world." Can anybody can say this? Nobody. So we have been here by HisNone will, AllāhNone will. Nobody can blame anybody. Because he is here.

Like one, call Jāhiz, he was very ugly, poem from time of, I think Abbasid time or something like this. He was also not Ahlu s-Sunnah. He was very ugly. He said, in Arabic, "Hādhā mā janāhu abī ‘alayya wa-mā janaytu alá aḥad." "This is what my father done for me." Because he was very ugly and everybody run away from him. But he said, "He done this for me but I didn't do anything for anybody." He didn't marry, no children for him. So he thought by himself he is coming and his father – blame his father to for his coming for dunyā. Nobody can blame his father, nobody blame his family – "You bring me here." Family also they must be... Many people they blame themselves. "Uff, we... We married and we get children and this children become misery or sad or something like this."

All from AllāhNone. If AllāhNone not want, nobody can come to this world. This is our belief, Muslim belief, real belief. And it is who has mind, accept this. Because no anything – you cannot run away from AllāhNone. To come against AllāhNone, to ask, "Why YouNone do this?" Nobody can ask. If you ask, you will be cursed. You are not in democracy, or somebody want vote from you, to give you what you want. But Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, if you obey and accept, you will be winner. If no, you will be whole time loser. AllāhNone can be do anything. Nothing cannot do. This is our belief and the not like Christian or Jewish. They all time, they are coming against. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Every time they are with prophets ṣallá Llāhu – their prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, AllāhNone sent them something and replied for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, "We want this. So this no good behave, no adab, this. Islām, it is a real adab. All prophets they are Islām but their nations they following their ego.
