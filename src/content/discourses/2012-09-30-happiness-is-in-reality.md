Assalamu alaykum

...New version. Everyday same thing you be fed up. This is the character of the human. If you give him everyday kebab, he'll say, "You don't have anything else?" Yahu, kebab is the best. So everyday same tune also it will be… Except the Azan. Subhanallah, since 1500 years same words, almost same tune, almost the same song, but everyone says, "Masha Allah what a beautiful Azan". Yahu, yesterday I done the same one. "Today masha Allah very nice, because it is going deep."

Yesterday I see something and the topic it was, it is very difficult to make people laugh. One of the most difficult things is to make somebody to laugh. I say but drama, it is very easy. We are very ready to cry, just make like this and 'wuuhh', crying. But to make to laugh is very difficult. Actually, to make to laugh is also not difficult, it is easy. But the difficult thing is to put happiness in the heart of people. This is the real difficult thing. That is why we are here to get this happiness from Mawlana Shaykh. To put this real happiness in our heart, because it is really thirsty.

We are the thirsty people Ya Sayyidi! We are here to get some happiness in our heart. The life is difficult, the breathing is difficult, to deal with people is difficult, to deal with family is difficult, to deal with parents is difficult, to deal with children is difficult. It is like pushing, pushing, pushing. And at the end of the day... Even when we coming to this dunya say, "Push!" So starts from there, they start to push you, "Out". Then you deal going on and on and on until the end. And in the end also, "Huuuh", you push also. Release it!

In this period we have to search for the real happiness. We have to search for the real address. And I say Alhamdulillah we are in the real address. "Why?" You say "Why, why your real address?" Asking. Yeah, because I am happy. They say that you are the son of the Shaykh that is why you might be happy. No, it is not the case. It is that if you can see it, you can get it, doesn't matter who you are. But if you cannot see it and you cannot get it, maybe you are in the higher rank for dunya things. Or claiming in the spiritual world that you are something but still, your inside is boiling and not getting the real happiness. You are not looking for the real triangle although Allah Almighty, He give you this opportunity, to go and look, because if somebody is hunting, he is just looking and gets the right aim. Allah Almighty, He gives it for everyone. It doesn't matter who you are, it doesn't matter what you are, it doesn't matter where you are. Maybe you have millions of followers, billions in money, maybe you have everything but you didn't have this aim. So what! So what? You didn't get the real treasure. Real treasure is to get the real aim and then to see the target. Slowly, slowly, maybe. Not all in one, this is not Nescafe. Three-in-one. One-one. Even if they give you one big lamb to eat, you cannot eat it in one, you need to cut it in pieces, you need to cook it and you need to put it in the plate and then to take it piece by piece, see! One, one, one, one. But you have to slaughter the goat first, also.

So, Allah Almighty, He gives us such a fantastic system. He shows you everything but, He give you one by one. In everything maybe you can be ruling, 2 billion people as the Chinese president. He is ruling for 2 billion people but, he have to speak for one. To get his order for 10 people and maybe those 10 people will go forth and go. He cannot rule the 2 billion. He may be shown as the ruler for the 2 billion but at the end of the day he is ruling only one person that takes the order from him. See Allah Almighty, how He is great. Allah Almighty, how He is great. Everything is for you! But no, it is only One. You have Nabil for example, 10 billion. How much you have in your pocket? 10 lira. Yahu, this man is billionaire, doesn't matter, but he is only ruling the money in his pocket.

I remember when we were passing in London with Mawlana Shaykh in Bank Street. This is very famous, 'Bank Street'. All the banks in London. "All this money is belonging to me". Mawlana Shaykh says. Ya Mawlana, how come? "Ehh, these people that think this money belongs to them, they give them only one paper that says 'you have this amount of money in your account', but, physically it is not his. If this man dies, they will say, even if the family coming, ‘Stop, stop, stop, stop, we have so many formalities to fulfill. First, you are family? Are you sure that you are family? Sure this money belongs to your father? Bring the documents". If they succeed & bring the documents, "OK, hmm, maybe this money is belonging to you but the government has to take 20% of the money. We are also having share".

So imagine nothing is belonging for the person. Allah Almighty He make everything look it is yours, but at the end it is not yours. This is Allah Almighty how He is giving you 2 side of looking. One side is the Reality and one side is the imagination. Or we call it today as 'Cinema'. It is big screen. We see everything as exciting. You are looking but you are sitting in a chair and you are not doing anything actually. And this is how Allah Almighty, He put it in our physical being. So that is why there is nothing important here than to get the real happiness that we have been talking about in the beginning. I say, "Why I am coming here? And how I can catch it back". Happiness, that is the Reality, the realistic part in our life because everything is unrealistic. When somebody says, "Huuh!" And he cannot take it, he dies. "Euefff", and he don't release it, he die. So where is the realistic part of our life? In this spiritual being, in the spiritual gaining. Alhamdulillah we are at the real address that Mawlana Shaykh, he grant us to be in his presence. We don't say, ‘we come’. He grants us. This is the other realistic part of our life. Alhamdulillah, it is good tiding to know this, and I'm sure that everyone knows this but Mawlana Shaykh he just put me here to remind me and you, to say, "Shukr Ya Rabbi, shukr Alhamdulillah, like my brother, & tawbah Ya Rabbi, tawbah Astaghfirullah, like me, because I am the one in need to be in tawbah all the time. But my brother he is in the 'shukr' side. This is Alhamdulillah, my brother, he is our Shaykh, he is our beloved one. Even he has put us in the straight way. This is to make us to understand that we have a serious part in our life, and we have the mercy side in our life.

So Mawlana Shaykh put two wings that present him. In the way that Mawlana Shaykh was in two forms. He is the complete shape. But with two sons he separated his shape because I cannot carry the serious side of it and my brother cannot carry the funny side of it. So Mawlana Shayk just made it equal. So my advice is to get two sides to be seen very well and to study it very well, because the two sides are the right side, inshaAllah. With this one I say, wa min Allahi tawfiq, bi hurmat al Habib, bi hurmat al Fatiha.
