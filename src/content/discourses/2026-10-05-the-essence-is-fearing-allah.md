As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam said, “I am the city of knowledge,” and declared regarding Sayyidinā ʿAli, “And ʿAli is its gate.”

This means that all forms of knowledge resided in Sayyidinā ʿAli. And he summarized all knowledge in just a few words,<br>
رَأْسُ الْحِكْمَةِ مَخَافَةُ الله
<br>“Ra'su l-ḥikma makhāfatu Allāh”<br>
“The head of wisdom is fear of Allah.”

The head of all knowledge, goodness, and counsel is the fear of Allah. When there is no fear of Allah, anything can happen; such is the state of the world. Let alone fearing Allah, they do not even believe in Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. That is the root cause of it all; the reason for such chaos, such evil, such oppression, and every manner of wrongdoing is the lack of fear of Allah.

A person who fears Allah does not commit evil. A man who fears Allah does not oppress others. A man who fears Allah does not steal. A man who fears Allah does not drink alcohol, does not commit adultery, and abstains from all kinds of evil deeds.

Now, as we said, these people have lost their belief in Allah—let alone fearing Him. Yet, after failing to fear Him, they ask, “Why is our situation like this?” It is the truth.

Sayyidinā ʿAli spoke these beautiful words fifteen hundred years ago. Without the fear of Allah, no act of worship or obedience... Just the other day, one of our brothers asked, “How does one become awliyā?” It seems some people are actually holding courses in certain places titled “How to Make Saints.” A wali is a friend of Allah; if you fear Allah, Allah will love you. Awliyā —a waliyyullah—is a beloved servant of Allah. It is not a matter of performing karāmah; a wali is a person whom Allah loves, and who fears and feels a sense of reverent shame before Him.

To fear Allah means to feel this reverent shame—to think, “Let me not fall into such a disgraceful state in His presence.” That is the essence of the matter. After all, Allah is The All-Forgiving. What we call “fearing Allah” is essentially this sense of reverent shame. It is the oppressors who truly ought to fear; let them be afraid. Let those who commit evil fear Allah. Yet, the door to forgiveness and pardon remains open.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla is Merciful. No matter how much evil a person has committed, still, He forgives the one who repents.

Therefore, one must fear Allah and turn to Him so that things may go well. Nothing else will avail; you cannot achieve anything simply by saying, “We shall do this, we shall do that.” Without the fear of Allah, nothing comes to fruition.

May Allah help mankind. May Allah grant guidance to us all.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
