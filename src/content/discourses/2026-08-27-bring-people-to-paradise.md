As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
يَا أَيُّهَا الرَّسُولُ بَلِّغْ مَا أُنزِلَ إِلَيْكَ مِن رَّبِّكَ
<br>“Yā ayyuhā r-Rasūlu balligh mā unzila ilayka min Rabbika”<br>
“O Messenger, announce that which has been revealed to you from your Lord.” (Qurʼān 5:67). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla commanded our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam to convey what He sent to humanity, to inform them of His message. And he conveyed it to the people. Many were guided. People emerged from darkness and gloom into the light. The whole world was illuminated by Islām, by the light of Islām, shukr to Allah. The world was illuminated everywhere by the light of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, the light of Islām.

The greatest light of his is the light that comes from the light of Allah, that is, the light of Islām. Therefore, it is a means of guidance, it is goodness for people. If people followed this way, there would be no wars, no quarrels, no problems. But because it is dunyā, shayṭān turns people against each other and leads them astray.

What is important is to be on the right way. Others ask these questions, “How will we do so? What shall we do?" and so on. This is the way shown by Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. Come, follow that way. Let’s all go to Paradise. Allah invites us. 

وَاللَّهُ يَدْعُو إِلَىٰ دَارِ السَّلَامِ
<br>“Wa-Llāhu yadʿū ilā Dāri s-Salām”<br>
“Allah invites to the Home of Peace (i.e. Paradise)” (Qurʼān 10:25).

Allah invites everyone. The place where we are invited is a beautiful place. There are two places. Either you will go without an invitation, may Allah protect us, to the bad place, to Hell. Or there is Paradise where everyone is invited. They say, “We don’t want this. We want to go where there is no invitation. It’s easier there, less hardship, we enjoy laziness. We prefer that.” They know for themselves.

But it’s good to show the right way to those who desire it, because Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla and our Prophet say, “If a person is guided by your means, it is better than the whole world.”

Shukr to Allah, our brothers are everywhere, people are coming and seeing. May it be a good invitation and a good example for them, inshā’Allāh. They are doing everything they can to include others in this good way.

Shukr to Allah, we are going on a journey. There’s Shāmil Efendi from South Africa, his father, and the brothers. They, shukr to Allah, have been the means for hundreds of thousands of people to embrace Islām. It was granted to them. They became the means of their guidance. 

People who go out of the way, those who have gone astray, all of them, by the will of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla and by Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim’s barakah are entering Islām. Hundreds of thousands are entering the very beautiful way. Because for years they were not treated like human beings by these disbelievers. They were oppressed, they were subjected to various things. When they see this beautiful way, the way of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla, they are pleased, their souls are satisfied. So, they go on that way.

Shukr to Allah, this is a great service. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla does not look at a person’s color, language, or race; whoever enters that way attains happiness. They become a beloved servant of Allah, a servant as Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla desires.

Therefore, race, language, and religion are not important at all. What is important is closeness to Allah, fear of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla, and walking on the way of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. It is about loving our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. That is very important.

On this way, they both come to Islām and learn true Islām. The Islām that comes from the way of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam is pure Islām. Others do things according to their own minds. They do things that are not in the religion, in Islām, and present this as Islām. Shukr to Allah, may Allah be pleased with them, because they both become Muslims and follow the true way of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, the way of Islām.

Ṭarīqah is the essence of sharīʿah. Those who enter it will not go astray, with the permission of Allah. 

May Allah be pleased with all of you, inshā’Allāh.
