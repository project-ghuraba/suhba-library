"Khairu'l-birri 'ajiluhu." The best of the good deeds is the one, you make immediately. Unfortunately I have seen few people, who take care of practicing this rule. It is better I do something, as soon as I say it, than to do 80 percent of it one month later. Maybe at that moment it is 3-5 percent. That ratio is very good.

"Who knows? Who can we tell? We're not prepared". Be content with whoever is present. 3 people is enough. One man came and believed in our Prophet (sas) Sayyidina Abu Bakr (ra). "Let's wait for the people to be ready, then we'll do it". No. Always hurry to do good deeds because shaitan runs after them. If you leave it to tomorrow, it will cool down, next day more so. Next day it will cool down and freeze. No one will come. This is a very strong rule in our religion Mr. Huseyin - "Khairu'l-birri 'ajiluhu." The best of the good deeds, is the one you do immediately. Don't wait.

"Let's get ready and then call people" - No. Serve bread and cheese for the guests. If you can't find a building, do it in a tent. If you can't find a car, hop on a tractor. Go on foot. Walk. Move. Run after a good deed when you see it. Don't look for this, that occasion, because shaitan comes quickly saying: "Conditions aren't suitable for this." Luckily my son doesn't make me say a second time. He takes care immediately. I hope that you carry on these assemblies - once a week. This is for Allah.

This mosque livens up one night, with this community. Inform each other, so that we prove we have a presence here. It is an Islamic region. It is the heaviest region of Lefkosa. It is the place where Muslims stay with power. This mosque is surrounded by a cemetery. Because the Ottomans entered this mosque, knee deep in blood - our ancestors' armies. For this reason this mosque is a holy and sacred mosque. So it is good to organize an assembly every week and this mosque should be like a showcase, showcase for ideas.

There are many educated people around us. Invite them. They may come and we may make a debate or a dialogue or a translation. There can be two groups. One group defends one case, the other defends another case. The judge, who comes to watch the debate, will decide between them - someone, who is able to say "This group is right, this group is wrong." So this should be organized. Rational people in the community, whose words can influence people because people now are restless. They are searching. They fell down to the sea, to the stream, to the river, looking for something to hold on to. Because socially we are on zero level and this is not a good sign.

Of course it is not only economical development because everybody is economically well. Everybody is a millionaire. Children have 1 million Turkish lira. So what is really important is the social life that keeps them going. It should be put in order and should keep standing. A body without bones is a mass of flesh. There is no other expression. Bones are the spiritual elements that hold the society up. If you ask; "What are the elements, that hold up the society?" The ruler doesn't know either. What will he say? Is it money that keeps the society going? No. Money doesn't keep the society going. There are many societies with money but they don't hold up. What are the elements that hold up the society, the community? It should be known, there should be debates. There are hundreds, thousands of educated people in Cyprus. They may come and talk. Not only on TV, they may also talk on TV, they may make a debate. Because people need it, they will watch it.

We made a humble start in this direction but this won't last forever behind closed doors. It should be made public. It should be explained by questions and answers. People should know which way to go. Dark, light, dead end? They should know what it is. So this is a good start Mr. Huseyin. Each subject can fill one book. A subject was opened lately, he summarized it in half an hour. But like we said - each subject should be explained in volumes of books.
