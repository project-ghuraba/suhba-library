As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

This month of Dhū l-Ḥijjah is a holy month. There is a lot of goodness and worship to be performed. One of them is the prayer of ‘Īd. ‘Īd prayer is wājib for us. It is wājib in the Ḥanafī madhhab. It is not wājib in other madhāhib, it is sunnah mu’akkadah for them. Also there is a matter of qurbān (sacrifice). It is the sunnah of our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. According to other madhāhib... Among all four madhāhib, only in the Ḥanafī madhhab is it wājib. In the others, it is sunnah mu’akkadah. It is a sunnah of our Holy ProphetNone. Worship that is called wājib lies between farḍ and sunnah mu’akkadah. It is something greater than sunnah and is very virtuous. Those who follow the Ḥanafī madhhab shouldn't leave it. As it is sunnah for other madhāhib, they may do it or not. It is not obligatory for them but it is better to do it.

According to the Ḥanafī madhhab, whoever has got money equivalent to 100 grams of gold, whoever has got this amount for a year, it is wājib for him to make qurbān that year. Making qurbān brings blessings and he performs wājib worship. Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says that Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gives a palace in Paradise for each hair of the animal sacrificed. It is so precious. And it is the same for every drop of its blood. Its rewards are uncountable. As much as you can count, there is so much thawāb. Apart from this, when a person makes qurbān, normally he can divide it into three parts. He keeps one third of it for himself, then gives one third to friends and one third to poor people. If he is poor himself, he can keep it all. If he is in too much need... In old times, they used to dry it and make pastirma from it, or used to dry it directly. They would use it during the whole year. It can be done like this by the poor. They can keep it in a freezer and consume it later. They will earn as many virtues. And their earnings remain with themselves. If they distribute to other poor people, their thawāb increases. They will have their du‘ā’.

These qurbān... shayṭān is interfering in everything now. He wants to prevent everything. He says it is a pity for animals. They are killed and slaughtered. What a pity. Especially non-Muslim people interfere in it a lot. Whereas in the first place, those animals want to be slaughtered on such holy days for the pleasure of AllāhNone. Certainly, these people don't understand it. People without faith, kuffār don't understand this. Hypocrites don't understand at all. Hypocrites say, "Don't torture them. Don't kill the animals." O kāfir, O hypocrites, O faithless ones, you eat half a kilogram meat every day. Where does it come from? Do you pick it from the tree? The amount of animals slaughtered on ‘Īd in all Muslim countries, among all the Muslims, kuffār kill double of it in two days or in a week. They eat double of it. Muslims distribute it to poor ones in Africa, India or Pakistan. Some of the poor people who receive it, eat meat only once a year, not even once a year. We have heard that in some places there are people who ate meat for the first time at the age of 30 or 40.

People, who believe in whatever shayṭān says, absolutely rebel against AllāhNone. People have started a war against AllāhNone. Never ever does anyone or anything under the order of AllāhNone get harmed. It brings benefit. Therefore, whoever can afford it, as we said, it is wājib for Ḥanafī madhhab. For the rest, it is sunnah mu’akkadah.

Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam used to always slaughter two big rams with his own holy hands. The name of this ‘Īd is ‘Īdu l-Aḍḥá, the Feast of Sacrifice. Qurbān in Turkish and Aḍḥá in Arabic. Aḍḥá means sacrifice. From the beginning till the end, with the barakah of this day, all of these animals wait to be sacrificed on such days. May their throats not be touched by the knife of kuffār without Bismi Llāh. These holy animals wish like this. Therefore, it is a blessing. These animals never decrease. They lamb once a year and deliver one lamb. And they feed millions of people. It is a sign of barakah, the barakah of Khalīl Ibrāhīm ‘alayhi s-salām. He is their shepherd. Their blessings came with him.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are in Dhū l-Ḥijjah month, month of Dhū l-Ḥijjah. There is many blessed days in this Dhū l-Ḥijjah month, month of Ḥajj, month of sacrifice. It is blessed. And there is ten days, ten nights – very blessed in this month and there is of course, many thing. One of them also, ‘Īd, ‘Īdu l-Aḍḥá. "Feast of Aḍḥá" – we call. It is...

First of this day, we pray, prayer of ‘Īd. It is for, they have four madhhab, Ahlu s-Sunnah wa-l-Jamā‘ah. First of them Ḥanafī madhhab. According to this madhhab, this is wājib. "Wājib" meaning, it is after farḍ. Wājib, sunnah – we have three kind of worshipping: Farḍ, you must do, you cannot leave it at all. You will [be] ask[ed] about it. Wājib, it is between sunnah and between farḍ. It is more higher than sunnah, it is less than farḍ worshipping. But this only in Ḥanafī madhhab we have. Other three madhhab, they don't have this. Only they have sunnah mu’akkadah or sunnah, normal sunnah. Sunnah mu’akkadah – meaning, strong sunnah. We must do it because Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam was doing every time. One of them – this ‘Īd praying – for us wājib, for other sunnah mu’akkadah.

But after this, also there is one more wājib worshipping for Ḥanafī, and sunnah mu’akkadah for not Ḥanafī people – to slaughter, to slaughter sheep, or goat, or cow, or camel. This four thing only, you can slaughter. Oh, buffalo also same like a cow it is. But for other like kangaroo or llama - this is, I don't think it is acceptable – or deer or other. This you can eat but not for ‘Īdu l-Aḍḥá. Other thing you can eat but not for ‘Īdu l-Aḍḥá. For ‘Īdu l-Aḍḥá, only this [four] animal. And this animal, who has enough money, which it is around hundred gram of gold – around hundred gram, maybe ninety, maybe more than this – around hundred gram, he has it in for one year – he is for him, for Ḥanafī madhhab, wājib to slaughter.

When you slaughter, this can be make it three part: one part for yourself, one part for your friend or anyone you like to give, and third part for poor people. This is sunnah, you can do this. But if you are poor, you don't have every time to you can find meat – you, all of this, what you slaughter, can be for you. No problem. And this is very good because Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla reward for each hair of this [animal] palace of Jannah. And for each drop of blood, also like this – countless bless[ings], countless reward, countless – in Jannah palaces and everything. This is what AllāhNone reward you and beside this, you can eat your what you slaughter – for whole year. This is good. People must be happy for this because this is ta‘ẓīm AllāhNone – AllāhNone, HeNone is happy with you. And...

But there is many hypocrite, non-believer, enemy of AllāhNone, ṣhayṭān, they are not happy with this what AllāhNone give us. And you can look, you can have everything and without... HeNone give too much for this. But they are not happy, they said, "Oh, you are killing animal, you are doing this, you are not, you are violence." But they don't know and they don't believe – this animal they are very, very, very happy – all of this animal who slaughter in this blessed day. The whole animal they like to [be] slaughter in this day with "Bismi Llāh, Allāhu Akbar" – the name of AllāhNone. The other animal who slaughter without this, they are unhappy. But one this slaughter with this, they are happy and they are wishing. And when they are happy, your – what you eat from their meat, it will be good for you, for bless. But other, it is, it will be illness and misery for people who eat without making bless.

And this hypocrite people, this crook people, this shayṭān people, they are saying, "This day millions of sheep, animal, they are killing this and they are slaughter this." But they don't know, this people, when we slaughter this, we distribute to people – poor people. Even in our country even some of them – in one year, maybe once they eat meat. Maybe through... many times – not so much, maybe ten times or five times a year. There is many people they don't have meat. There another place like Africa, like India, like Pakistan or other poor country, who, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, now people they send to slaughter there...

[It is] sunnah to slaughter by yourself but when you find people they are more in need from you to this meat, you can send and slaughter there. AllāhNone will be reward you also. This one now important point here: this all what we slaughter and this billion – more than one billion people eating this in ‘Īdu l-Aḍḥá. This hypocrite people [eating] every day. This poor people maybe not eating meat but not eating one kg or two kg – coming maybe five hundred gram, two hundred gram for them – small, small. But this hypocrite people, every day, they eat at least half kg from meat. From where they find this? They collect from tree? This really hypocrite. What they eat from meat in two days, maybe this whole ‘Īdu l-Aḍḥá, it is not same – equal for this people. They killing, killing, killing and after they said, "You are killer." They are doing this for people, they do it for everything. After they are make – to make people to be away from AllāhNone, to away from doing good thing. But al-ḥamdu liLlāh, some crazy people from who like to imitate Western people, in our country also they are saying this, but they also they eat meat. They are not going away from meat.

So don't listen to this. This is feast and AllāhNone, HeNone give us this gift for whole they who want this – Ummat Muḥammad ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. And HeNone give barakah for this animal, who they are only once a year they give one baby but still billions of people they eating and they not finish. This is barakah for this animal and for the shepherd of this animal – Sayyidunā Ibrāhīm ‘alayhi s-salām. He has barakah – barakat Khalīlu Llāh. We said HeNone is give – AllāhNone give barakah for him (as) and for this animal. AllāhNone accept what we will do, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone make it health and from this illness also for people also, for all Muslim.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
