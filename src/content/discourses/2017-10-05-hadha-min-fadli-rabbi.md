As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa raḥmatu Llāhi wa barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-Ṣalātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīn wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

"Hādhā min faḍli Rabbī" (27:40). These words must be our motto. It is a kind favour from AllāhNone to us. All these favours are from the kindness of AllāhNone. Don't think you have something from yourself. If you feel proud by thinking you did this and that, you are an idiot then. Everything is from AllāhNone. AllāhNone raises some people, puts some other people down; makes some people taller and some shorter; some people beautiful and some ugly, some people smart and some stupid. Everything is from AllāhNone. Nothing is from a man himself. A man has nothing in his hands. Therefore, whatever a person sees: "Hādhā min faḍli Rabbī" (27:40). It is from Allāh'sNone kindness. You should say shukr to AllāhNone. You should praise AllāhNone.

Who is Muslim, who is a believer, says these words. Others keep boasting – "I did this. I did that. I earned this. I won that. I am strong. I am this and that." They have no value. Because AllāhNone will question them. Even if everyone lives like they are young, they will still become old. The strength will leave them. Most of the time money leaves too. People who boast about their richness, people who think they made a miracle, their end will be sad. A person who knows that everything is from AllāhNone, that is Allāh'sNone kindness too. You should say, "We declare and we believe in this – we have no power, no strength. Power and strength are from AllāhNone. AllāhNone has given them to us. It is HisNone kindness." You should say shukr to HimNone, all praisings to HimNone.

To... we must say and they – "Hādhā min faḍli Rabbī" (27:40). This is from our Lord Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla. This gift from HimNone, generous from HimNone. What this everything we get from health, from wealth, from hidāyah, from beautiness, beauty, from handsome, from children, from land, from everything – we must say and accept this is from Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla. HeNone is, everything from HimNone and we accept this and we thank HimNone. Nothing we, even one stone we cannot make without HisNone will. So HeNone is everything HeNone give us. We are must accept everything from HimNone. Very important this because believer only, they are accepting this. Allāh HeNone give everything. HeNone give you life. HeNone give you belief. HeNone give you wife. HeNone give you children. Give you good health. Maybe HeNone create you tall, create you short, create you clever, create you idiot, create you beautiful, create you maybe ugly. Everything from HimNone. This is from HimNone and we accept and we are not object for this.

If we have something we not happy with this, we also this saying from AllāhNone we accept. If we have good thing also we say this is from HimNone not from our. We read, we study in university, famous university and become famous and there is hundred thousand people they are studying there and they are making not better. They finish their families' fortune even who study in university. Not important to study to be the most important thing. If Allāh HeNone will HeNone can make you this. This is belief of believer.

Who are not believer they said, "I done this. I was, I get this. I become rich like this. I become handsome like this. I become succeed like this." Everything they thought they are from themself. This is not good thinking. This is foolish thinking. Because AllāhNone create millions same and HeNone some of them for showing people and to make them test – who accept and who not accept. So we must be thankful for HimNone. Everything saying, "This is from Your Kindness, from Your Generous. YouNone give us all this good thing and we are thank YouNone for YouNone." We mustn't think anything from ourself. And really sometimes you thought, "oh I done this" – after quickly AllāhNone make you some slap. You said, "What happen?" So everything be careful. Thank for AllāhNone, "this is from Your Generous and make it, in shā’a Llāh, all time for us, in shā’a Llāh."

Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
