As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
ادْعُونِي أَسْتَجِبْ لَكُمْ
<br>“Udʿūnī astajib lakum.”<br>
“Call upon Me; I will respond to you.” (Qurʼān 40:60). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla is saying, “Make Dua, and I will accept it. I will respond to you.” Whoever makes Salawat to our Holy Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam in the beginning and makes Salawat in the end while making Dua, that Dua will be accepted. Some Dua are accepted and some are not. But when you make Salawat for the honor of our Holy Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, those Duas will certainly be accepted.

Dua is our weapon. The weapon of believers is Dua. The effect that Dua makes cannot be made with weapons. Therefore, making Dua is always necessary. It is a must. It should not be underestimated. When you get up in the morning and until the evening, it is good to make any Dua all day. 

Our Holy Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam has a lot of Duas, the Sahabah have Duas, mashāyikh have Duas. You can say them all. You should say whatever comes to your mind in order to be protected. As we are living in Dunya, some people have evil eyes, some have envious evil looks. There is intolerance. So there are all kinds of things. Therefore, when you get up in the morning and until the evening, as you step out of the house, as you ride the car, [make Dua] in all kinds of actions, Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla will protect you, you will gain reward and you will also remember Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla.

Therefore, these things are important. It is much more important than other things; the useless things people give importance to. They shout from morning to evening over unnecessary things, and tell them to each other. They give importance to those things. Even if sins weren’t written, it is still of no benefit to you. Support this team for a thousand years, support the other team for a thousand years, you will have nothing; nothing but harm. That’s it.

Therefore, beg to Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla and make Dua to Him without forgetting Him. Also, make Dua for useful things. Don’t make Dua for useless things, for Dunya or for things that have nothing to do with you. “May this win, may that win.” It is a very useless thing. People think there will be some benefit. Benefit is in the works that are on the way of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla, that are for akhirah. Remembering and making Dua is also Dhikr. 

Therefore, Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says,<br>
فَاذْكُرُونِي أَذْكُرْكُمْ
<br>“Fā-dhkurūnī adhkurkum”<br>
“So remember Me; I will remember you.” (Qur’ān 02:152). 

“Remember Me; I will remember you. I will give you Thawab and rewards,” says Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla.

Therefore, these Duas are for protection and a means for the increase in the rank of rewards. May Allah protect us all from the evil of shaytan and the evil of the ego. May Allah protect us. May Allah show us the right way and let us do the right things inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
