Sheikh Mehmet Adil Al-Haqqani sohbat of the 9th of December, 2014.

Salam alaikum wa Rahmatullah wa barakatu Audhu Billah mina-shaytan ir-rajim Bismillahi r-Rahman r-Rahim Madad ya rasulAllahSalat wa salam 'ala Rasulina,Sayyidi-l awwalin wal akhirin madad ya Mashayikhina madad Ya Sh Abdullah ad-Daghestani madad, dastur.

Welcome, masha'Allah. We are coming here for Allah for Beloved of Allah, Prophet, Sayyidina Muhammad (saws). We are praising Nabi'ina Muhammad (saws). This what is acceptable present of Allah. Divine present of Allah this event; it is holy and blessed event. Alhamdulillah we are doing this and only for Allah. Nobody else coming for any wrong idea or wrong thinking, only for Allah and we are happy to be like this.

Masha'Allah this Qawwali they are, I think, Sufi music. Sufi music and coming from more than a 1000 year, alhamdulillah, Qawwali and the other. And as you know Mawlana Jalaluddin Rumi also he was making, blowing ney/flute. They said blowing, not playing. And the verse of Mathnawi Sharif was for this ney - Yes, explain how it is. It is coming through - he said - bamboo field. But all bamboo nothing, not field of bamboo, no you cannot say good for.... only maybe you can take, make house, make anything. But when special - they must cut him and after they must make hole in it by knife. And after they must put in fire until giving this nice voice.

So, human being also must be like this. To make himself better and better he must suffer, he must be tired. He must be effort to make the best. Alhamdulillah this is for our first teaching from Mawlana, you know Mathnawi is very huge, spiritual book. Muslims or non-Muslims all they mu'ajibin - impressed with this book. And he is showing to people the way of spiritual. Even by making poems. Making blowing in ney, or by drum or by anything, they can encourage people to be away from dunya, only to look for God.

There is two kinds of music. One music for dunya and one for spiritual. Everybody knows this. So Alhamdulillah this, tonight for spiritual one. They're people affect in their soul this thing, and music. Even in Prophet's time they were making by.... When he come to Madina, they welcoming him by singing, Tala' al badru 'alayna and with daff. He was happy. So, we must make difference between normal music and this Sufi music. Sufi music it is continue 100 years, 1000 years, but other - one day, next day you forget. Alhamdulillah, we are happy. And many they will say Qasida Burda also. Qasida Burda is for Prophet (saws). They were saying poem for Prophet. Prophet (saws) he give him his jubba. The meaning of 'burda', meaning 'jubba'. For this whole poem calling by "Burda".

Tonight Alhamdulillah we are here, we are happy with you. Thank you for your coming, and we continue with our brothers' song. Thank you.
