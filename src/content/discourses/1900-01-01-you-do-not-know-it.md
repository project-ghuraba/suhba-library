Shaykh Muhammad Nazim Al-Haqqani An-Naqshibandi, Sohbat.

Allahu Akbar Allahu Akbar wa Lillahi l-hamd. Allahu Akbar Allahu Akbar La ilaha illa Allahu Allahu Akbar Allahu Akbar wa lillahi l-hamd. Allahumma salli ala Sayyidina Muhammad. Wa ala aliha wa sahbihi wa sallam. Sallu ala Rasulina Muhammad. Sallu ala Habibina Muhammad. Salli ala shafi'i dhunubina Muhammad. Allahumma Salli ala Sayyidina Muhammadin Wal fatihi lima ughlik. Wa khatimi lima sabaq. Nasir-al haqqi bil haqq Wa l-hadi lis-siratikal mustaqim. Wa ala alihi haqqa Qadrihi miqdarihil Adheem.

Our master alaihi s-salatu wa s-salam, said with happiness: "whoever leaves out the salatu salam for me, who forgets to say salatu salam for me, surely forgets the way to Paradise." Salatu salam, to our Master expresses our respect for him, as well as our love. If someone does not show respect and love to our master no doubt his/her faith is weak and that faith cannot save him/her at the last breath. Therefore, salatu salam for our prophet opens for us the way to Paradise. Say "alaihi s-salatu wa s-salam" to our master for every occasion. Just as prayer can't be without salatu salam, azan can't be without salatu salam. But now there is a new fashion, they don't call for salatu salam after the azan. While the world is full of hundreds of thousands of unbelievers, our religious authorities don't care about them but they worry about salatu salam after the Azani Muhammadi and say let's not do this in case we make a mistake & it's a sin!

They are in an unnecessary panic. They wonder if the Prophet will not be happy with them if they say salatu salam to him? They fall into such a mistake. Say Salatu salam with courage! Since the time of the Prophet alaihi s-salatu wa s-salam all the nations, and the last and the biggest of all our states (Ottoman) ever to exist until the end of the world, led Muslims for 600 years. They showed their respect and love to the Prophet for 600 years with salatu salam from the minarets and Janabi Mawla honoured them with blessings. In the last 5 or 10 years something strange has happened. They stopped calling the salatu salam after the azan in the whole country. Whereas, here (in Cyprus) I remember when they outlawed the Azan-i Muhammadi (in arabic). They would read "God is Great, God is Great" (in Turkish) but they would still call salatu salam after the azan. They forced the Azan to be read in Turkish but kept the salatu salam after the azan in the original.

When Azani Muhammadi was restored in 1950 this tradition carried on but the students going and studying in the Hijaz come back to their homeland and started practising what they learned there and, strangely enough, they object to saying salatu salam to our Prophet alaihi s-salatu wa s-salam. This is not right because the Prophet says forgetting salatu salam is the same as forgetting the way to the Paradise. There is no specific time for Salatu salam, no specific place. In every place and time you can say it because Janabi Mawla's angels in the seven Heavens call for salatu salam on His beloved one from "minal azal ilal abad" (from pre-eternity to eternity). For any time, it is timeless. This is an order. No one is going to question us why you say salatu salam after the azan.

Recently I was in Europe, Azani Muhammadi was called in a mosque. After the azan the Imam Effendi read, Allahumma Rabba hadhihi d-dawati n-nafia from the mihrab. As a respect to our master there is a dua, wasila dua - he read that one. I said, before this dua the Hadith asks you to say salatu salam. Why do you skip the salatu salam and read this dua? The order is, Sallu alaih thumma Falillahi l wasilata wa l-fadilah. The Hadith Sharif is this. It says after the azani Muhammedi say salatu salam to me. Then say Allahumma Rabba hadhihi d-dawata n-nafia wa salati l-qa'ima. Read this one.

Now in the all mosques, this is the new fashion, straight after the azan they read loudly "Allahumma Rabba hadhihi n-nafia" whether it is the imam or mu'azzin (azan reader) yahu Hadith there is also salatu salam in that hadith. Why do you skip that? I said. I said you are an educated one so you have more responsibility. "E, we only read this, Sheikh Effendi". No, open that book and read properly, read what is first. "Let's do that" he said. Fortunately, he was a fair person so he accepted our proposal and did not argue further. I told you this (incident) as a note. If you want you accept it, if not then it's up to you. No obligation! But it is so sad this disrespect to saying salatu selam to the Prophet. It's not a good sign, the whole nation suffers for this.

The Religious Affairs Ministry, instead of paying attention to this point, trying to dispel the darkness descending on the whole country because of omitting salatu salam, they are doing the opposite. They think they are the most knowledgeable ones but there are more knowledgeable ones outside the ministry. There are people who know more than they. In this country, officially, they have the highest authority, but outside the Religious Affairs Ministry there are also alims (knowledgeable ones) who have no official title. They should also consult them because pride doesn't suit those with knowledge. Arrogance is a sign of shaytan and to seek knowledge is an order of the Prophet, order of the "alaihi s-salatu wa s-salam". Salatu salam. If a nation leaves a sunnah of the Prophet consistently then according to our shariah it is permissible to bring down that nation. What about a whole nation rejecting to say salatu salams to the Prophet!

Our madhab is Ahlis sunnah wal jamaa. We haven't stopped being Ahlis sunnah wal jamaat. Therefore, respect to the Prophet is a sign of iman (faith) for us. You must show this respect. We must present our respect to the Prophet. One day when our Prophet was in a gathering he said: "Ya Rabbi, Grant blessings to our Damascus and our Yemen". Allahumma barik lana fi Shamina wa fi Yamanina - said the Hadith Sharif. Ya Rabbi, to our Damascus and our Yemen, Damascus was not conquered yet. Shami Sharif (Damascus) and land of Sham was not conquered at that time. Same with Yemen! However, the Prophet said our Sham and our Yemen. He said our Sham, our Yemen because they were sealed with the seal of Islam and presented him. Ya Shami Sharif. Honourable Sham. He said "Mubarak (to you) ". May it be full of blessings for us. There was someone within this gathering who said "Wa fi najdina". (and to our Najd). The Prophet paid no attention to this remark. He repeated: "Allahumma barik lana fi Shamina wa fi Yamanina". and repeated. He said, Ya Rabbi to our Sham and our Yemen grant blessings.

This is the miracle of the Prophet alaihis-salatu wa s-salam. He was giving the good news that his ummah will conquer these lands. Perhaps Ahlul Islam at that time, disbelievers and infidels were surrounding these places but the Prophet was telling the good news as a miracle. That person said again "wa fi najdina." "Pray for najid also!" He (the Prophet) said "Hunak al fitani wa zalazil Wa minha yakhruju qarna sh-shaytan." He said there will be discord there. There will be many traumas, disorders, shake-ups there and shaytan's horn will emerge from there. The main fitna (disorder) that will split inside of Islam will emerge from there, he said. He ended it. Now, the rulers in Hijaz are descendants from Najd, they were opposing the Prophet even then. In appearance they have iman but in reality they don't.

In 1928 they ruled over there. They ruled in 1928. The rule of Ottomans finished there (in 1928). Straight away salatu salam was banned. Straight away they showed the dislike and hate they held inside them. Until Yavuz Sultan Salim -may he rest in Jannah- became Khadimul Haramayni Sharifayn the Khalifas before him also prolonged and enlightened the Holy Hijaz with salatu salam. In 1928 with the treachery of disbelievers they surfaced. While there was a Sharif following Ahli sunnah wal jamaat ways they tricked him and rioted against the Ottomans. Then they did not keep Sharif there either, they deported him. Wahabis drove them out. You go out they said. This nation is unaware. They don't know history or geography. When they first came to power, about 200 hundred years ago, they attacked Taif, and to all the Muslims in Taif they said you are idolaters - you worship dead ones (graves). You are not Muslims and they slaughtered all the men, and took the women and children as slaves, destroyed their homes. Then, the Ottoman Sultan ordered The Khedive in Egypt, ─Ibrahim Pasha, charged his army over them and cleared the area, killed them all. They had used Harami Sharif as stables. same with the Rawda Sharif, as stables.

This is their mazhab (school of thought) - mazhab. And according to their tradition, they don't say salatu salam to the Prophet. They don't wear the turban. They get scared if they see people with turbans. They bother them. They only wrap a scarf around the head. Did the Prophet not wear a turban? If they see a person with turban they call it a false mazhab. Those who study there they still teach these ways. They make them practice their (wahabi) ways. "Don't say salatu salam to the Prophet." "Don't read/sing Mawlud." "Don't read Delail Sharif." "Don't read!" Respect to the Prophet, is the core of iman (faith). We leave them to Janabul Haqq to deal with. It cannot carry on like this. This false way cannot go forever.

It will change but what I am sad about is that they don't know about all these facts and also our Ministry of Religious Affairs in the whole country, sneakily, whispers into the ears of mu'azzins (azan callers) these ways. They say - Don't call for salwat after the azan. So what happens when you say salatu salam after the azan? You become unbeliever? Is it haram? Is there an ayat or hadith prohibiting it? I seek Allah's help for all of them on this holy day. A'udhu Billahi Min ash-shaitan ir-Rajim. Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. La hawla wa la quwwata illa billahil aliyul azeem.

Today is a holy day. It is Eid ul Adha day. Then once they have the authority in Hijaz, they brought in so many obstacles for visiting there. They ask for a passport... They ask for visas... They have quotas.... They have tax, airport tax.... So many different obstacles they put in front of the Ummah of Muhammad. In reality, this holy land should be open for anyone to visit freely. Janab-i Haq, opened Ka'aba Mu'azzama with no buildings, to all. When our ancestors built the arched sections there was no door to the Haram Sharif in Mecca Mukarrama. Doorless! You could enter from any direction. Wherever you come you could enter. Now it is like a castle, surrounded by concrete, then so many doors that close, closed up with high doors. Doors! No door! It is open! What is this a sign of? From East to west, whoever has the inspiration and love in their hearts to visit, they can walk if they want all the way. All the Muslim countries on the way, also have to open the way. These people are "Wafdullah". They are guests of Allah. With bare feet, poor clothes, walking, make way for them to go to the Ka'aba Mu'azzam. Help them. You need to organize the whole desert so they can walk freely....

Now it is castled in. The Islamic World is also asleep. Keeps sleeping. No one says anything: Who does the Ka'aba belong to? to Saudis? Have they inherited it from their fathers? Why no one asks what is a viza for? No one opens up their mouth to say something. All of the Islamic World, one and half billion people! This is our holy land. We will visit with no obstacles. Like the unbelievers obstructed the Prophet. They stood in front of Sahaba Kiram and said, you can't enter. Why? We need a passport? Visa? They say you can't visit this year. Huuu. Has this happened before in the whole of the history of Islam? Any one went with visa or passport during the time of the Prophet? What a shame! They say the quota is full, then the rest can't go. MashaAllah. Will they not fit in? Would Janabi Haqq invite people if they could not fit in? If you have an invitation, you would know how many people you can care for. Yes, I can care for up to 40 people, so you invite 40 people. This is Allah yahu. This is Allah's House. Even if the whole world went, would He be Allah if he couldn't fit them in? How can He be the owner of Baytullah? What silliness! They say we will widen it, open up. Hajis don't fit in. Are they visiting you so you can't fit them in? When I first went to Hijaz in 1947. After the second world war, it was Hajjul Akbar. Allah granted that I visited. It was still in its old state, with its buildings. 2 million hajis.
