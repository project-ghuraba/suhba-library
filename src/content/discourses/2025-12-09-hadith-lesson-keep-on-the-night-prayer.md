As-Salāmu ‘Alaykum wa RaḥmatuLlāhi wa Barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu BiLlāhi Minash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. BismiLlāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm.

Wa ṣ-Salātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Akhirīn.

Madad yā RasūlAllāh, Madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi RasūliLlāh, Madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

Dastūr Mawlana Sheikh Abdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistāni, Sheikh Muḥammad

Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad.

1- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

َ < ﱠ َ < ُ ﱠ َ َ ﱡ

مِﻮا ةﻛ نﺈﻓ ﻞ مﻮا ﻜﺗ ﻻ ﲎﺑ ﺎﻳ ،نﲈ دواد  نﲈﺳ مأ 

Our Holy Prophet is saying, The mother of Prophet Suleiman son of Daud, ‘alayhi s-salam, said to him, “O my son, do not sleep too much at night. For sleeping too much at night leaves one poor on the Day of Judgment.” So since the night worship is acceptable, Prophet Suleiman ‘alayhi s-salām’s mother told him not to spend all his time sleeping.

2- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says: K K ﻛ ﻢو ،ناﺮ لآ و ةا ةرﻮﺳ ﺢﺘ ﻞا فﻮﺟ ﰱ م اﺪ ﱃﺎ ﷲا ﺧ ﺎﻣ

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla will not let the efforts of a person who gets up in the middle of the night and prays reading Surat Al-Baqarah and Surat Ali Imran go to waste. The best treasure for a person is Surat Al-Baqarah and Surat Ali Imran.” So, if he recites the beginnings of Surat Al-Baqarah and Surat Ali Imran, it will be the same. The greatest treasure is Surat Al-Baqarah and Surat Ali Imran.

Now here, may Allāh be pleased with them, many people want their children to become Hafiz, memorize the Qur’an. They have them memorize it for a year or two to make them Hafiz. They memorize it, but then forget it. Therefore, our advice is to teach them few Juz’s; for example, Surat Al-Baqarah, Surat Ali Imran, Surat Al-An’am, Yasin, Al-Mulk and all Surahs left until the end. If they memorize those Juz by heart, it is much better than memorizing all the Qur’an and then forgetting it. Therefore, they should pay attention to Surat Al-Baqarah and Surat Ali Imran as they are treasures.

3- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

نو ﻪ ِ ﺗ ِ ﻼﺻ ﺮﺟأ  ﷲا ﺐﺘﻛ ﻻإ م ﺎ ﻪ ِ ﻐﻳ ﻞﺑ ةﻼﺻ  نﻮﻜﺗ ئ ٍ ا  ﺎﻣ

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “There is no one who intends to perform the night prayer but sleep overcomes him, lest Allāh writes down the reward for his prayer. His sleep becomes charity for him.” So if one prays Tahajjud prayer every night, but falls asleep while praying once. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gives that servant the reward for that prayer. His sleep becomes charity for him.

4- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “One of you should pray when he is vigorous, and when he is tired or slack, he should sit.” This happens more during night prayers; if one is tired, he sits. He can pray while sitting.

5- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “The face of a person who prays frequently at night becomes beautiful during the day.” So the faces of most mu’min people, shukr to Allāh, are beautiful. The faces of those who are rebellious and far from religion are full of darkness and ugliness. May Allāh protect us. Whoever desires beauty should pray at night, in shā’a Llāh.

6- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

َ ﱠ ُ ُ َ K K ﱠ َ ﱢ َ K َ َ َ َ

ﺢﺒﺻأ ﱴﺣ هﺎﻨ ﻪﺘ ، ﻞا  ﲆﺼﻳ مﻳ نأ يﻮﻳ و ﻪﺷاﺮﻓ ﰏأ 

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “If someone goes to bed intending to wake up for night prayers, but was overcome by sleep and didn’t wake up until morning, the reward for his intention is written down.” He goes to bed at night thinking, “I will get up and pray tahajjud prayer, I will pray najāt prayer, I will pray shukr prayer,” then he finds himself awake in the morning having missed it. But the reward of all is written by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla according to his intention; the same reward. “His sleep is granted by Allāh to him as charity.”

7- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Whoever recites one hundred ayah in one night will have the reward of having spent that night in worship.”

8- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet is saying “Whoever recites a hundred verses at night.” That is, if he prays ten rakats and recites ten verses in each rakat, it will be one hundred verses. If he prayed twelve – it means that one hundred ayah can be easily recited. For example, Surah Ikhlas has four verses. Each one is a verse, meaning it’s not a hundred surahs but verses, says our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. “Whoever recites a hundred verses at night will not be recorded among the heedless.” Heedless people means those who are heedless of Allāh. So heedlessness is not a good thing.

9- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet is saying “Do not abandon the Night Prayer, even if the prayer you offer is the same length in time as milking a sheep.” So, even if it is only two rakats, as milking a sheep does not take more than five minutes.

Therefore, it is absolutely necessary to keep on the Night Prayer. There are great rewards, ranks, and beauties that our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam gave good tidings about. May Allāh make it perpetual for us, and we have made the intention to do it until the end of our lives. May Allāh grant us according to our intention, in shā’a Llāh.

The Messenger of Allāh says the truth in what he said or as he said.
