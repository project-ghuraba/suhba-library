As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Wa-lā yakhāfūna lawmata lā’im” (5:54). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l- ‘Aẓīm. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says in Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n, this is for those who dislike them. They say bad things to those who are Muslims. But they are not afraid that bad words will be said to them, says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. They are the believers. Because their way is the righteous path; they are the ones in the right. Those who are not righteous get angry at them, reproach them, fight, curse, and insult them. They don’t care about them at all, says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.

So, if you’re on the righteous path, let them say whatever they want to say. Don’t be ignorant with the ignorant people. Let them say whatever they want. If there’s someone who will understand, if there’s someone who will grasp your intent, you can talk to them. If they ask you what you don’t like or what you didn’t do, you can answer them and talk to them. Otherwise, when people come at you just to cause harm, don’t care about them. Don’t be afraid of them. What they say has no value. No harm will come to you. Harm will come to them. Therefore, when you are on the righteous path, give thanks to AllāhNone. “I am on this path. Shayṭāns are attacking me. I am facing the assaults of the shayṭāns. They hold grudges, hatred, and aversion toward those on the path of AllāhNone.” Therefore, you should rejoice that you are on the path of AllāhNone. That is also something that will not go unrewarded for you. AllāhNone will grant you its reward. People attacked you, harmed you, spoke harsh words to you, and you remained patient – Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla will grant you the reward for that patience.

This blessing – every blessing has its reward. At the very least, sometimes people look at you strangely, even if they don’t say anything. Even that look benefits you. It’s a reward and merit for you. Therefore, those on the path of AllāhNone should give thanks. You should give thanks for the grace and favor of AllāhNone, that you are not among those who oppose HimNone and speak harsh words to those who are with HimNone. You should pray for them. Because when most people are on the wrong path, later, many turn to the righteous path, and they regret it. Therefore, this path is a beautiful path. We should pray for guidance for them too. Of course, there are many ṣaḥābah, great ṣaḥābah, who were once the chief enemies of Islām. Then, they became its greatest defenders. Those people became the leaders of the believers. At first, they waged wars against Islām. Many fought against our Holy ProphetNone. Yet they too were guided and reached the highest ranks among the ṣaḥābah. Therefore, may AllāhNone grant them guidance.

May AllāhNone guide those who oppose Islām, those who dislike Islām, those who, out of ignorance, attack Islām.

As we said, don’t be saddened that they disliked you and spoke these words. They will either regret it or be guided, in shā’a Llāh. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying, “Wa-lā yakhāfūna lawmata lā’im” (5:54). Meaning, this āyah, verse of Qur’ān: “They are not afraid from people who was, who they are not happy with you.” But they are not happy, because you are with Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. You following HisNone order. Many people they are attacking people by making them, looking to them as they are not good people. Or they are shouting to them, or they are swearing to them, or sometimes they are fighting with them. “Don't be afraid from them,” HeNone said, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Because you are in way of AllāhNone, the most real way for human being AllāhNone showing. You are in this way. So other people, they are ignorant. They don't know this way and they, so, see you like following this way, they are not happy. Because, shayṭān make them to be angry with you, because they are out of this way. They are following their ego. They are running after their desire, after bad thing.

So, when somebody [is] not like them, they will be become enemy for him. For this, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying, they are not afraid from people or from sayings [of] people or idea of these people, or what they are thinking about you. All this they are not, not afraid and they are not care also. Why not care? Because you know reality and you know these people, they are not in way which true way. And so, for this, if some crazy people saying something for you, you become angry? I don't think [so]. Who has good mind people, just laughing, because he says, “This is crazy.” He is not stable. He is not knowing what he is saying. Maybe he shout to you or he swearing on you, also you not care for this. For this, for this people also same, because they don't have... they are ignorant. Same like crazy people. They are not knowing what they are saying, what they are doing. So, for this, no fear. No any care about them. But what we do?

We thank Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, we not following this way of them. If they are not happy, we are not leave our way and running after them. No. Just praying for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla to give hidāyah for these people. Because, many people, sometime, they, AllāhNone give hidāyah, and when they are become into way of AllāhNone they was very regret. “What – how we was ignorant, we doing this, we saying this for these people and they doing...” And now, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are in this way. This is, from time of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, many of big non-believer, mushrik. Fighting, even they was fighting against Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, they become Muslim and they become leader from Muslim, many of them. And giving much benefit for Islām. For this, AllāhNone if HeNone give hidāyah, they become the best people. If not come... give hidāyah, they become worst people. For this, also we are asking AllāhNone to give hidāyah for them, to see this good, nice way, beautiful way, way of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.

AllāhNone, from this blessed days, accepts our du‘ā’ and gives hidāyah for this people, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
