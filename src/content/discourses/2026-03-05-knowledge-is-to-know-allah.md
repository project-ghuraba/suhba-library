As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
هَلْ يَسْتَوِى ٱلَّذِينَ يَعْلَمُونَ وَٱلَّذِينَ لَا يَعْلَمُونَ
<br>“Hal yastawī lladhīna yaʿlamūna wa-lladhīna lā yaʿlamūn”<br>
“Are those who know equal to those who know not?” (Qur’ān 39:9). ṢadaqAllahu l-ʿAẓīm.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says, "Are those who know same with those who don't know?" They are not same. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla brought about this meaning. Knowledge is to know Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. Everything else is not knowledge. Actually, everything people learn is the knowledge of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla from start to end. That knowledge is beneficial if they know Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. If they don't know, it has no benefit – knowledge turns into ignorance. It turns into unawareness. It becomes something useless.

There are people who feel proud of themselves for learning a lot, for becoming professors and graduating from several universities. But they don't accept Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. They don't accept things given by Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. They say it happened by itself. It means they are ignorant. They are right in the middle of ignorance.

Certainly, there is no end to knowledge. Our Holy Prophet says it is farḍ (obligatory) to ask for knowledge. It is farḍ for everyone. Everyone must learn from the time of birth till they die. How should they learn? They must intend to be on the way of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla and ask for knowledge step by step. With such intention, they will both become accepted people near Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla and angels will spread their wings beneath their feet, says Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. Ignorance is not good.

As we said, knowledge has its levels. Allah has given a rank to everyone. You must know this. Normal Muslims listen to lectures, take advice from each other. That means attaining knowledge. That means gaining knowledge. As we said, even when you claim to know something, every day something occurs that you don't know. You learn new things every day. And you must say that for the pleasure of Allah. You must say, "I accept what I learn, this knowledge as the order from Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla". You must say so.

Of course, knowledge teaches beauty and goodness. It teaches whatever good there is. What teaches badness is not knowledge. That is the things to destroy and finish people. If you say, "I learnt so much to cheat people, to make money from people, to be crafty," that is not knowledge. If you study and don't accept the existence of Allah that is bad knowledge as well.

There are good ‘alīms. ‘Alīms are on the highest level near Allah. They are the good and pious ‘alīms. And ‘alīms who obey their egos are bad ‘alīms. If a normal person has one sin, they (‘alīms) are written a double sin. Because they don't do on purpose, or they do the opposite. Their sin is larger.

May Allah protect us. May Allah open our minds. May we have good knowledge inshā’Allah.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
