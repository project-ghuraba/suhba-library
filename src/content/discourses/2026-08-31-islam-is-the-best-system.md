As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
وَتَعَاوَنُوا۟ عَلَى ٱلْبِرِّ وَٱلتَّقْوَىٰ ۖ وَلَا تَعَاوَنُوا۟ عَلَى ٱلْإِثْمِ وَٱلْعُدْوَٰنِ
<br>“Wa taʿāwanū ʿalā l-birri wa t-taqwā wa lā taʿāwanū ʿalā l-ithmi wa l-ʿudwān”<br>
“And help each other in goodness and piety, but do not cooperate in sin and aggression.” (Qurʼān 5:2). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla commands you to help one another in doing good. He commands you to do good. He commands you not to help one another in doing evil. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says, “Do not help one another in doing evil.” Be together in doing good. If someone is going to do evil, prevent them, warn them, and command them, saying, “This is evil, this is wrong, it cannot be done.” 

This is the religion of Islām. The religion of Islām is the mother of all systems. In this world, they create systems to serve people. That service to people is not a service. Because it is a form, a system created by people. Because it lacks the spiritual support, because it is not the command of Allah, it will not work. It will never work. 

They say communist. They said communist system means everyone should be together, help each other. But you are not helping in that way. You take a person’s possessions, their labor, their house, their land, their factory. You say they are helping, but where is the person’s right and justice in that? You are committing injustice. It doesn’t constitute doing good; it doesn’t become a good thing. 

Islām is not like that. Islām says help each other. The rich should help the poor. Whether it’s charity, giving zakāt, lending, or a benevolent loan (qard hasan). That’s how help is. If you come and take someone’s property, then that’s inhumane, and it’s completely incompatible with religion. That’s why this system was garbage. 

Then they made the socialist system. In that system, everyone would work, do something, and earn. But they made it worse than communism. A man earns five cents, and they say, “You’ll pay ten cents in taxes.” “How can that be? I earned five cents, and you’re taking hundreds, five hundred percent, three hundred percent, sixty percent, eighty percent in taxes? How? I have to work, what will I do, what will I achieve?” This is also useless. 

This is the state of the world now. If all these Western countries worked on that system, people are now searching for communism. Because in communism, they were all the same. They were just managing and getting by. The state provides assistance. No one can do whatever they want, but it’s not like that here. This is worse. They both oppress people and don’t give them money. And then they say, “I’m taking care of you, I’m doing this and that.” That’s unacceptable. 

In Islām, those who earn, earn, and they pay their zakāt. Those who receive zakāt do so from halāl sources, and those who give earn thawāb. So, all these systems have come to power over these people. Socialist, communist, fascist, whatever, this and that. They’re all worse than each other. None of them succeeded. They were all ruined. And this one is ruined, the world is going ruined now, in the same way. Nothing is beneficial. 

Therefore, the system, the system established by Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla, is that you pay two and a half percent of your wealth annually as tax. Generally, it’s two and a half percent, but now Allah knows how much a person pays daily, five hundred percent a year, or a thousand percent. And then they say, “The economy will be good, this and that will happen,” but that won’t happen. It won’t happen with this system of human oppression.

The system of Allah is the acceptable one. The system that will bring blessings and comfort to people is the system of Islām, the system established by Allah, and that is the successful one. Everything else is a failure, all are futile endeavors. None of them are successful; they are all unsuccessful systems. In the end, they consume themselves. Like communism. Communism didn’t get destroyed from the outside; it was garbage from within and it was destroyed.

Allah knows when but it will happen. People will see this too. Then, Allah will, inshā’Allāh, establish His system with Mahdi ʿalayhi s-salām, inshā’Allāh.
