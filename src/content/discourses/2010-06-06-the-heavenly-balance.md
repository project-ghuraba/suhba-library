O People you have been granted heavenly support and heavenly powers. One is enough or one may be more than enough for such a planet 100 times over because that is a heavenly grant from Allah Almighty's Heavenly Presence, granted to His most beloved one in His Divine Presence, Sayyidina Muhammad, zidhu Ya Rabbi izzan wa sharafa nuran wa surura, ridwana wa sultana.

Give respect and take respect. Give goodness and take back heavenly goodness. You may give something from this life and everything that you are giving must be for your Lord, Almighty Allah! You may give the minutest, smallest, and He grants to you something that you are not going to imagine. Your imagination is never reaching there.

When Allah Almighty is granting even if, so small but from Him that small thing is going to be doubled and doubled and doubled. Such a creation. He is the Creator and His grants are going to be according to His Majesty. He gives something according to His Mighty Kingdom. His Mighty Kingdom, not only of your imagination, but if all creatures were going to be on one point, their imagination couldn't reach even the smallest grant from our Lord's Mighty Kingdom. May Allah forgive us.

Allah Almighty's smallest divine grant may be [worth] countless times such a dunya and its treasures. For what are you running? "Because we are no-mind ones."

Calling people, "Don't look there, come, follow and take from these ones, it is better suited to you".

And they are leaving eternal tasting. In the time it takes one of them to finish a cigarette, another one during that short time may reach up to the heavens, may reach eternity. O Eternity. Eternity gives such freshness to our hearts and to our minds.

O People, so many people are complaining about their hearts, so many people are complaining about their minds and they are asking to find a way to make their physical being restful, in a happy way, but it is impossible because they are using something that is carrying them from one level to another level, that is under it, and under it, and under it. As much as they are smoking, each time they are smoking, they fall into another dark place and falling into a second, another black area, coming down and coming down and never tasting anything but troubles over troubles come on them, miseries over miseries, suffering over suffering.

You are claiming you are ulama and followers of Salaf us-Saleh. What does it mean when the Lord of Heavens says in the Holy Quran, astadh Billah: wawada`a almeezana And imposed the balance (55:7).

Verse from the Holy Quran or am I saying it on behalf of myself? Yes, we are saying from the Holy Quran, wawada`a almeezan. He is putting a Balance. For whom? For animals, or for angels or for jinn, or for mankind. For whom is the Lord of Heavens making that Balance? Wawada`a almeezan and also saying:

alla tatghaw fi almeezani That you not transgress within the balance (55:8)

Yes? Surat ar-Rahman you may recite it. Alla tatghaw fi'lmeezan. He orders that you must keep the Balance for everything and you must be very careful not to lose the Real Balance that the Lord of Heavens granted to you - wawada`a almeezan. Putting balance for what, O Ulama? For what? For weighing barley or wheat. Is Allah Almighty sending that Balance from heavens and giving it to His beloved ones and ordering them to say, "People use that Balance"? For what? For barley or for wheat, or rice? For what? Say?

You are saying 'we are ulama', why you are not saying to people why Allah Almighty wawada`a almeezan? Where did He put that Balance? Outside? What you are using for weighing pilgrim's boxes, bags. Allah is sending that? You are those people who are not fearing Allah and saying "we must weigh this bag, that bag, that box". That Mizaan, that Balance is for this? Allah Almighty is lending that Balance, wawada`a almeezan. What is Mizaan? Say! Say!

That Mizaan, that Balance is for mankind for balancing their actions, their demands. What do you think about wawada`a almeezan? This Mizaan, is it for balancing this world's diamonds or valued things for which you must pay this and that? Now you are using electronic balances everywhere and you are looking and saying, "You must give hundred rial", another, "You must put 1000 rial". For what? "Because your balance is showing you must pay this". For this? Is Allah a merchant? You are thinking the whole Muslim World is running after a foolish system, dirty system, leaving Shariat-Allah, leaving Allah Almighty's Mizaan and running after democracies? Collecting people and saying, "We must bring new balances for our people". Who is giving to you that authority? Say! And which law or rule can be like Heavenly Rules? Why you are not speaking - all learned people, why you are not saying? O Salafi ulama of all Islamic territories why are you not saying, "Use this Balance, cancel those parliaments". Parliaments make such foolish rules putting people under their oppression. Why are you not saying? You are fearing men, not fearing Allah Almighty? O People wawada`a almeezan, for everything, for every action you must use that Balance. For everything that we are speaking or doing, or thinking, or saying you must use that Balance. For your efforts, your actions, for everything that you are making, use that Balance. What saying, is it true or not? From where are you bringing parliament? Are you ever putting parliament on Heavenly Mizaan? What is its value? O those people who are not fearing Allah, why bring parliament?

wa ma khalaqta jinna wal ins illa liy`abudoon And I did not create the jinn and mankind except to worship Me (51:56)
