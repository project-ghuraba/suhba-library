As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Wa-kullun fī falakin yasbaḥūn" (36:40). A visitor came a little while ago. He asked where this world is going. Where is this world going? We replied, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla sent this world on a journey. So it is going on that journey. Everything has a place to go, a destination point. And the destination of the world? Shukr to AllāhNone, the prophets, the scholars, the awliyā’, the ṣāliḥ ones and the mashāyikh, all of them have said where it is going. The world is going to ākhirah. In order to inform people about it, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla sent the prophets. They explained everything to people. Yet mankind is still asking where we are going, where the world is going.

Ignorant people keep asking, assuming it will not come to an end, and that it will continue moving on like this. No, everything has an end. And the final destination is qiyāmah. Qiyāmah is the point where people will reach real life once again. And this is the journey we are going on. It is the real journey. By the wisdom of AllāhNone, the world does not stand still. It is moving at a very high speed. With the power of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, the universe and everything in it is in motion. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gives this motion. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gives this power and speed. Even if people don't feel it, and claim it is standing still, we are moving.

Once, we were on a journey. And one majdhūb... We prayed two raka‘āt of the traveller's prayer, and told the others to finish the four raka‘āt. But he didn't continue. "You are a local." He said, "No, I am a traveller too." We are all travellers. The world is moving and so are we. We are not permanent residents here. Our journey continues. It is going towards ākhirah. People who know this and prepare themselves, attain the generous kindness of AllāhNone. They attain the mercy and guidance of AllāhNone, for they are the lucky ones. They are not in the majority in this world. The majority is unaware of anything. They think nothing will happen and say, "We are on our own here. Life is short. Let us enjoy it as much as we can. Let's cheer ourselves up and have fun. Let's do everything. There is nothing after this." No, there is.

There is a destination point. You have your destination point too. And it is clear where the world is going. Those who know, and accept it are lucky people. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla sent the prophets to inform everyone about it. And then HeNone sent the scholars. Books were sent down to all of them from the presence of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. You have been informed that you will go here or there. You say, "No, I had no idea." You knew, but you didn't accept. You say this is nothing. But in the end, they will be disappointed and lost, says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. When the mushrik, those without belief, and those who go against AllāhNone go to ākhirah, they will feel strange. They will be shocked. They will say, "AllāhNone AllāhNone. We didn't anticipate this." You must. You were told about it. You must know. AllāhNone doesn't oppress anyone. People oppress themselves.

May AllāhNone protect us. In shā’a Llāh, our final destination is good, both here and hereafter are good. Because when people start off on a journey, when they go somewhere unknown, they don't feel relaxed. When they know their destination, they go by familiar ways, and arrive safely. May AllāhNone take all of us to safety, in shā’a Llāh.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Wa-kullun fī falakin yasbaḥūn" (36:40). Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying, "For this universe, for our world, our planet, all, they are going." And today, one of our brother, visitor, he came. He looking for, he asking, "Where this world going? Because everywhere trouble, everywhere bad thing happening." I said, "It is question, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla HeNone answer for this. HeNone send HisNone MessengerNone, HisNone heavenly Book, and HisNone scholar, ‘ulamā’, awliyā’. All, they are telling people where they are going. But people not listening. They thought they are staying here, not going anywhere.

Even our – everything going. Nothing stopping. They are going toward where? Toward – everything they have end, and our end, for Qiyāmah, Day of Resurrection. For Day of Judgement. This what we are going. Dunyā world going to this. This in spiritual, but... for people who know spiritual, you are going to Qiyāmah. But for other people, they don't know this. Even, our Earth, planet, also going very speedy, toward where? To, AllāhNone know where it going. But people, they thought they are sitting, not moving. We are here, nothing moving. Everything moving. Every atom moving. And this movement, from where coming from, this energy? From AllāhNone. Where HeNone is order to be, and they be. This is what people not accept.

One of our majdhūb brother, we was visiting some place, and we pray safarī, "musāfir"; meaning, when you go somewhere, you make pray after, not four raka‘āt, two raka‘āt. We prayed and told people to continue praying four raka‘āt. They continued, but this man, he didn't continue. We said, "You are here, living here." "No," he said, "I am musāfir also. I'm going toward Ākhirah, so making two raka‘āt only." This is, must know. We all, we are not origin. We are, not our home here. We are all travellers. We are travelling toward Ākhirah. To – not end – we are travelling to beginning, our real life. But the people, they thought they are travelling to end. End, for them, finish everything. No. They will be shocked when they will be find what AllāhNone promised them, this Day of Judgement. They will be, "Oh. What is this? Why we didn't know this?" You must know because, you know everything, and AllāhNone not making oppressing people, HeNone tell people. HeNone send HisNone Messenger, HisNone Books, to show people what will happen, to give them instruction. But this people, they don't – They throw this instruction. They not listening to anything, and they go without anything. At the end, they will be loser. This is what Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla telling us. Every time, every minute, everywhere, there is signs and there is – AllāhNone show us this. We are not in forever here, and everything changing. Who not changing? Only Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. We, whole changing. Going from childhood, old, and after old we go to be dust, to be earth. And after we'll be, in shā’a Llāh, again for our beginning. That time, beginning. When we are dying, our beginning it is that time, in shā’a Llāh. Real life.

AllāhNone keep us in HisNone blessing and mercy. We are lucky because we have, we know where we are going, where we are coming. But who not... If you give people, throw them some place, and they don't have any instruction to go from here or to there, they will be very unhappy. But who know, quickly go to safe place. AllāhNone keep us safe, in shā’a Llāh, here and hereafter. And keep us happy here and hereafter, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
