Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

May the month of Safar be good Inshallah. May it pass in goodness and end in goodness. Every month has an attribute and a speacilaty that Allah (JJ) gave. The month of Safar is a little heavier. However, our Holy Prophet (SAW) says, “Give good tidings of goodness, expect goodness, and you shall find it Inshallah.” That is why he said ‘Safarul Khayr’.

When the month of Safar enters, let them read three Kalima Shahadat, 300 Astaghfirullah, seven Ayatul Kursi, and seven Sura Feel every day, and give charity. Inshallah it will pass in ease. Every month has an attribute. They all exist so that people remember Allah (JJ), remember the hereafter, and see. Some call it a superstition and what not. These are not superstitions.

Our Holy Prophet says, “Who will give good tidings to me for the emergence of the month of Safar?” So it is like giving him a reward. There is something, and so that is why he is saying this. There are things to be done to be protected from it. If you do them, Allah willing it will pass comfortably. May its good be for us and its evil be for the enemies of Allah (JJ).

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq.

Al-Fatiha.
