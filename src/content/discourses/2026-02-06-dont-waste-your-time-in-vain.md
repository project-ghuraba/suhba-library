As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

A‘ūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
وَقُلِ ٱعْمَلُوا۟ فَسَيَرَى ٱللَّهُ عَمَلَكُمْ وَرَسُولُهُۥ
<br>“Wa-quli ʿmalū fa-sa-yarAllahu ʿamalakum wa rasūlih”<br>
“And say, Do, for Allah will see your deeds, and [so, will] His Messenger.” (Qurʼān 9:105). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says, “Do!” for what you are going to do, everything you do in this world is seen by Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla, and He will continue to see it. Everything you do will be seen in the Presence of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. If you do good, Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla sees it and rewards you accordingly. If you do evil, there is certainly a punishment for it if you do not repent.

These worldly preoccupations, Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says this world is just games and amusements. Whatever you do should be done in the way of Allah. There is no need to give so much importance to unnecessary things. The effort given, the money spent, if it goes to unnecessary things, it is of no benefit. It doesn’t even benefit yourself. Some things they pursue might be very bad. Some of them, although not too bad, are still a waste of time and effort and become in vain.

SubhānAllah, two days ago, when we were arriving at the airport, we saw a huge crowd. They said, “There’s a kid who plays football, I think, people are coming to greet him.” But with what enthusiasm they come! No one worships with that much enthusiasm. The eagerness with which they rush around – I mean, people go to Ḥajj, they come to listen to Shaykhs and scholars, but I’ve never seen such eagerness.

To rush around so much for worldly things, for unnecessary things: if we had even one percent of that, we would all be Awliyā’, we would all be flying high. SubhānAllah, shayṭān gives people such encouragement that if you think about it, you realise you’re not gaining anything, you’re losing something, you’re at least losing your time and your effort. If it were on the way of Allah, it wouldn’t be like that.

Therefore, we need to be careful. Sitting in front of this machine, talking to people, to those in front of them - I don’t understand what they’re talking for hours. They don’t sleep until morning.

These people need to think. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla sees what we are doing. He will hold us accountable for it. People will regret it later, thinking, “I wasted so much time. If I had said “Allah,” all of this would have been profit. Otherwise, there’s no profit, only harm.”

Therefore, one must be careful. Let it be for the sake of Allah, let it be as Allah wants; at least don’t forget Allah. Wherever you go, even if you go there, don’t forget Allah, say, “May Allah forgive us. We are not satisfied with our current state.” May we pray to Allah for our state to improve, inshā’Allah. May Allah not let us occupy ourselves with useless things. May we have things that benefit us. May Allah help us.

People don’t think about anything other than pleasure and frivolity these days. May Allah improve us all.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.

There are Qur’ān recitations. Li-Llahi Ta’ala, Al-Fātiḥah.
