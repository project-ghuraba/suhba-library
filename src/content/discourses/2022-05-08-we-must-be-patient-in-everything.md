As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

"Wa-la-qad kudhdhibat rusulun min qablika fa-ṣabarū ‘alá mā kudhdhibū wa-ūdhū ḥattá atāhum naṣrunā" (6:34). Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is saying prophets are the most patient people. All of them were patient with their people. They brought goodness to them. People caused them suffering. They called them liars and despised them. But Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla helped these prophets in the end. HeNone was with them. And those who weren't with them were destroyed. Therefore, prophets are the most patient people.

People of the present time don't advise each other patience. They say you must always fight and get your right. People don't tolerate each other. And then their problems become worse. When there was one problem, it becomes ten or a hundred problems. If they had shown patience, everything would have been solved. It will be solved before bigger problems occur. Therefore Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla...

There are many āyāt on patience in Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n. "Wa-laman ṣabara ẓafar." This is ḥadīth. "Whoever is patient comes out a winner in the end." "Wa-ṣbir wa-mā ṣabruka illā bi-Llāhi" (16:127). Be patient, for AllāhNone is with you. All good things are with the patient ones. It wasn't said that impatience is good, that impatient ones profit. There is no such āyatu l-karīmah. Therefore, patience is hard. It is bitter. They say patience is bitter but its fruit is sweet. Therefore, mankind should be patient in this time. If they get angry at everything, they will destroy themselves and harm themselves. That is why the greatest testament and the biggest advice is to be patient.

Especially in this time, by listening to those shayāṭīn they say you must be angry at everyone, be angry at your leader, be angry at the government. They have made it a habit to demand your right. There is a strange saying, "I will get my right by force." You can't get anything. And you won't gain anything. AllāhNone will not... If you try hard yourself, if you show patience to Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla and trust in AllāhNone, you will gain then. Because patience is a good thing both for dunyā and ākhirah. Patience is not easy. Patience is the most difficult thing. It is easy to say, but being patient is the most difficult thing. May AllāhNone protect. May AllāhNone help us, in shā’a Llāh.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying in Qur’ān. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥim. "Wa-la-qad kudhdhibat rusulun min qablika fa-ṣabarū ‘alá mā kudhdhibū wa-ūdhū ḥattá atāhum naṣrunā" (6:34). Meaning, whole prophets they was patient because their nation they, every time they make them, treat them badly and they say for them, "You are liar, you are crazy." And they was make them also, treat them badly – sometimes kill them, sometimes beat them, sometimes they make many, many bad things for prophets. But prophets they was most patient ones because Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying this prophets they was patient for what their nation done. But AllāhNone they give them at the end victory on these people and they was reach to help of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. For this patient, it is most patient people the prophets. After them, companions and their followers, little by little.

But the most people who was patient, they have ṣabr, patience – prophets. Because they are taking order from AllāhNone and with this order they are continue. Not looking for easy thing. If there is easiness, okay, [but if] not, they must continue. They must doing their teaching people, to make people happy here and hereafter. This is what their mission. But they was all time persecuted by people and not good treat them. For this, we are also, we must be patient for everything.

But this century, last century also, all they was against to be patient. "Don't be patient," they said. "You must be fight against these people. You must do, take your right. You must demonstrate, you must shout, you must kill, you must destroy everything for your right." But what they done? If you are, you are not patient, if you will take something bad for you, it comes ten times back worse than you. Maybe ten times, maybe hundred times bad. Become worse and worse and worse. When every time you are not patient, you will not be better. You be worse. But these people they like to make people angry and to be people not happy and to be give miserable for people. This is what shayṭān promise human being. But Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla promise you good thing. Be patient and you will win. Even in for this world, in this life with people, be patient, less problem for you. If not patient, more problem – double, triple, ten times, hundred times, thousand times. From angry and not patient many people they was in graveyard or in hospital or in jail or mental house from not patient. Nothing can be with not patient good. With patient good.

But all in Qur’ān many verses about patient "Wa-ṣbir wa-mā ṣabruka illā bi-Llāhi" (16:127). "Who patient AllāhNone with him." "Innamā yuwaffā ṣ-ṣābirūna ajrahum bi-ghayri ḥisāb" (39:10). "AllāhNone give the patient people reward without count, countless reward." We are saying this but, of course, to say patient it is very easy by tongue but when you like to do it, it is real not easy – difficult, very difficult. But AllāhNone give us example for prophets, for especially for our Prophet, Sayyidunā Muḥammad ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. HeNone said, "Mā ūdhiya nabīyun mithla mā ūdhīt." HeNone said heNone there was the most of make, treat badly from hisNone nation. between, among [compared to] other prophets. But heNone was patient, patient until AllāhNone order himNone to make different thing. But heNone is most perfect example for us how can people they can be patient for the end of the line. HeNone was patient – nobody can be patient like himNone. We must try to be patient, not be not patient for anything. AllāhNone help us to do this. It is difficult, But what AllāhNone HeNone want make difficult – easy, it is easy.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
