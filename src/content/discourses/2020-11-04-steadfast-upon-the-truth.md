Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Our Holy Prophet ﷺ states:

“There will never cease to be a group from my Ummah manifest upon the truth.” Our Holy Prophet ﷺ states that a group from his nation will be steadfast upon the truth till the Day of Judgment. They are upon the right path. Some people think that the whole of humanity has been corrupted, but there are those amongst them that are on the way of truth. There are such people; it is not like they don’t exist. So people should not fall into hopelessness and despair. The believer should not do that. Those on the right path will always exist. Allah ﷻ will protect them, and through their Barakah and blessing the Ummah will stand.

Thank Allah ﷻ. Sometimes you see things happening and signs that show that this nation is not without a Sahib (owner). Allah ﷻ is the Owner and Allah ﷻ helps this group. He gives protection. Because if these had not existed, then shaytan and his followers, the nonbelievers would try and hope to get rid of all of them, all of us. But even though they try so hard and they attack again, thank Allah ﷻ that through His help the Ummah is standing.

Those who are with Allah ﷻ do not lose hope. Hopelessness should not exist. Hopelessness is bad Adab towards Allah ﷻ. To ask for help from Allah ﷻ is the greatest virtue for us, so we should not fall into despair. If that does happen, it means that your faith is weak. So everyone should believe this: Allah ﷻ is with us. Allah ﷻ is not with the unbelievers. He gives them an opportunity, but He is not with them. It is to test us. Therefore, thank Allah ﷻ that the blessed teachings of our Holy Prophet ﷺ are keeping us on our feet. No matter how much they try and come against these people, this nation and Islam. Thank Allah ﷻ that even though many people are silent, but they are on the right way.

As we have seen, during this earthquake they were showing that they had rescued people. Perhaps they may not pay attention to something small, but they found a wallet. Who knows whether its owner passed away or not. We do not know, but inside it was a list. May Allah ﷻ raise that person’s station. Apparently, he had borrowed some money, and had put a list inside his wallet so that if something happened to him others would know that he owed people such and such amount of money. These are signs that people are not corrupt. Even though shaytan has tried so hard to corrupt these people, they’re not all corrupt. And there are millions of such silent people. Allah ﷻ helps us through them and helps the Ummah.

We hear that some person was such a scoundrel and fooled some people. This makes people fall into despair and hopelessness, and they feel that they can’t find anyone to trust. Who is one to trust? You would think that everyone is a thief, everyone is shameful and that everyone is looking for an opportunity to rob you. Well, such people might be more evident, but the silent ones are more in number. The silent majority believes in Allah ﷻ. They are the ones who uphold the truth. And for their honor, as we said, these people will exist on the way until the Day of Judgment, as our Holy Prophet ﷺ said. Those who uphold the truth and righteousness are the beloved servants of Allah ﷻ. May Allah ﷻ protect us from the evil of our ego. May we be like these people. May Allah ﷻ give strength to our faith. May Allah ﷻ help this nation and Islam insha’Allah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
