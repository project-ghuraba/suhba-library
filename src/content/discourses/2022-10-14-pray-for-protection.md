As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Our Holy ProphetNone is saying that in the end of times, because there is fitnah and people are spoiled, everything will be spoiled. Rain will fall as a disaster. Children will bring not goodness, but evil to people, says our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. We are living in such a time. Everywhere, whenever you see the news, things that were normally sent as favours are being sent as punishments by AllāhNone. You want rain? Have some rain. HeNone drowns people in rain. You want children? Have a child. And that child drives the family crazy. These are the problems of the end of times.

Our Holy ProphetNone always told us what to do in order to be protected from this. We should know there is a solution for everything in this life, and be with Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. We should beg AllāhNone and ask for protection so that AllāhNone protects people. Everything has its du‘ā’. Everyone can do that. What we just read in the khuṭbah... Sometimes lightning may strike a person. Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam recited that āyah and made du‘ā’ then. When this is done, there is protection with permission of AllāhNone. It is the same with rainfall. If the rain is severe, we should repent and ask forgiveness in order to be protected. Nothing else. Sometimes there are warnings in the news to be careful about approaching storms. People should be careful, of course. People should be careful and seek refuge in AllāhNone. When they trust in AllāhNone, they will certainly be protected, in shā’a Llāh.

Also, do not put yourself in danger, says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. If you build a house or a building in inappropriate places, you will have put yourself in danger. You will be responsible. Therefore, people must be careful in everything, because our Holy ProphetNone ordered people to be careful. “A‘qilhā wa-tawakkal.” Look first, think and contemplate. And then submit. It is not a smart thing to throw yourself in the flood and say you submit. A clever thing is to pay attention to these things and to stay away from dangerous places and things. As we said, this time is a dangerous time. We need to be very careful. We must be careful.

May AllāhNone protect us. May AllāhNone help people. People are doing things however they like, and then they are suffering. It is not only physical, worldly things. They are doing useless things for ākhirah as well. They are losing their ākhirah. It is okay to lose in this world. But it is not good to lose in ākhirah. May AllāhNone protect us. We are living in time which Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, saying it is end of time. He said, ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, it will be rain. Rain, normally, it is mercy from AllāhNone, bless from AllāhNone. When raining, everything become better, become nicer. But, in the end of time, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, this rain, it will be curse for people, disaster for people, bad for people. Because –“You want rain?” Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla say, “Take this rain.” HeNone send in once and make a flood. Make for people to be not good. And to be also, “Yakūnu l-maṭaru qayẓan, yakūna l-waladu ghayẓan.” Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. And children, sons also, they will be against their family, against their father and mother. So they will be, not happiness, making bad for people.

This is the time of end of time, it is like this. From where this coming? From, coming from AllāhNone. Punishment for people, who, they forget AllāhNone, forget to pray. Many thing can protect from this thing, but people, they not doing. Just listening for news, listening for what will happen. They said, “It will be heavy rain, be careful.” And what heavy rain, many people also, they make – not thinking for any bad thing – they put their place, their house, in a dangerous place. Which, you must be careful. You must think what you do. Because, to put yourself in danger, it is not order from AllāhNone. It is order to keep yourself, your family from danger. Don’t throw yourself in a dangerous place.

And many thing happening because people they are forgetting the prayer to protect which Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, even in khuṭbah, heNone said for thunders. When thunders coming, killing people, there is this praying, you say it, and it will protect you. This is what, who saying this ḥadīth, Sayyidunā Abū Hurayrah, and who said this and anything happen to him from thunder, “I will pay for his fee, because it is insurance.” This one of this, there are many thing to be insurance for people, which can protect them.

Everywhere, who do this for Allāh’sNone sake, for sake of AllāhNone, AllāhNone protect. AllāhNone make us... In this time, difficult time, but it is more reward for everybody who thinking for this. But, who not thinking, it only become harm, and no benefit. Benefit thing, everything in this dunya happening, you must know it is from AllāhNone, and you must – Don’t make bad behave with AllāhNone. Even, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam was saying, “Don’t curse wind or a storm. This is all from AllāhNone.” Forbidden to curse even this.

So, everything, every minute, every second, you must think, “This, what happened, for our benefit.” We are in place, we are collecting precious thing for ākhirah. So, AllāhNone protect us and give us to be good thinking about what happens everywhere, and make praying, “astaghfiru Llāh”, ask for forgiveness, for repent from AllāhNone. AllāhNone protect us, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
