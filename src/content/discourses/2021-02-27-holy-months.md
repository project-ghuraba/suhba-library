Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Thank Allah ﷻ [for] these Three Holy Months, may they come with their own blessings. Today is the 15th of Rajab. With the Barakah (blessing) of this Holy Month, Allah ﷻ gives strength to Muslims because we are living in end times and Kufr (nonbelief) has reached its peak. Our Holy Prophet ﷺsays: “In the past it was ‘ركنملا نع يهنلاو فورعملاب رملأا’, ‘enjoining M'aruf (good) and forbidding Munkar (evil)’, but in end times it will be ‘فورعملا نع يهنلاو ركنملاب رملأا’, ‘enjoining Munkar (evil) and forbidding M'aruf (good).” So in end times, not commanding but forbidding goodness and ordering evil will occur; they will order evil and forbid good. We’re living in those exact times.

When we were younger, we would think and wonder how such a thing will happen, but this exact thing is happening and people are astonished. So much evil is going on and a person with intellect whether Muslim or not, [people normally] wouldn’t accept such a thing. Commanding evil and forbidding goodness is nothing to be accepted by anyone who has a mind, but now that is what’s happening. Whatever bad and evil there is, that’s what they’re ordering. Whoever bad person there is, that’s whom they’re telling us to be with.

We are astonished and amazed. Our Holy Prophet ﷺ says: “اًنْ ارَ يْحَ مَ يلِحَ لا ل َعجْ َت”, it makes the clever one confused and astonished, and it leaves them in that state. Every day we see new things that we’re amazed by and our mouths just keep opening from surprise. “Could it be this much?”, we ask ourselves. More and more people are believing and following these things. May Allah ﷻ protect us, for the honor of these months, may Allah ﷻ protect the Mu’mins (believers) and Muslims. May He keep us away from such evil and from such evil people. These are soldiers of shaytan, it is those who are with shaytan. We are asking: “Who are these people? Where are they coming from? Where are they coming out from?”, they’re coming out from shaytan and dajjal, they’re coming out from his soldiers. Otherwise, nothing but goodness comes out from Islam. Islam prohibits evil and orders goodness; we need to pay attention to this. May Allah ﷻ protect us.

There are plenty of Fitnahs (dissensions/ discords). Fitnah is deceiving people, people are seeing good as evil and evil as good. Allah ﷻ is with Haqq (truth) and with Mu’mins (believers). Be with Allah ﷻ and don’t be with those filthy/dirty people, be with clean/pure people insha’Allah. May Allah ﷻ strengthen our faith. May Allah ﷻ protect us and protect the children and the young ones/youth of the Ummah (nation) of Muhammad ﷺ. Our ancestors said it right, they’re young blood, they have a craziness to them and they’re not full of intellect. May Allah ﷻ help us.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq. Al-Fatiha.
