As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
وَهُوَ الَّذِي سَخَّرَ الْبَحْرَ لِتَأْكُلُوا مِنْهُ لَحْمًا طَرِيًّا وَتَسْتَخْرِجُوا مِنْهُ حِلْيَةً تَلْبَسُونَهَا
<br>“Wa Huwa l-ladhī sakhkhara l-baḥra li-ta'kulū minhu laḥman ṭariyyā wa tastakhrijū minhu ḥilyatan talbasūnahā”<br>
“And He it is Who has subjected the sea (to you), that you eat thereof fresh tender meat (i.e. fish), and that you bring forth out of it ornaments to wear.” (Qurʼān 16:14). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says: “He created this sea for mankind, for their benefit. From it you can both eat food and extract pearls and other ornaments.” Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla has made everything subservient to us.

What does subservient mean? It means He has made it a means for us to benefit and to serve mankind; Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla created it that way.

The earth, the sky, the mountains, and the stones created by Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla all make dhikr (mention) Allah. Therefore, a person who makes dhikr of Allah along with them also benefits from them. Those who do not glorify Allah, who never think of Him, and who think of other things, are at a loss. No matter how much they do, they say, “I know, I do.” They show and explain, “This happened, that happened,” but they do not mention the Creator. They do not mention Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla.

Everything clearly shows the existence of Allah. Yet they deny it. People strive tirelessly and relentlessly to keep others away from Allah. But their efforts are futile; in the end, they will gain nothing. It will only cause harm; nothing beneficial.

Knowledge is the knowledge given by Allah and that which makes us know Allah. Anything else is nothing but ignorance. They keep boasting, “We have built so many universities, we have educated so many students,” but that knowledge is useless; may Allah protect us from it.

Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam said, “May Allah protect us from useless knowledge.”<br>
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ عِلْمٍ لاَ يَنْفَعُ وَقَلْبٍ لاَ يَخْشَعُ
<br>“Allahumma inni aʿudhu bika min ʿilmin lā yanfaʿu, wa qalbin lā yakhshā.”<br>
“O Allah, I seek refuge with You from knowledge that is of no benefit, a heart that is does not fear You.” [Ḥadīth].

“May Allah protect us from a heart that does not fear Allah, and may Allah protect us from useless knowledge.” This is the duʿā of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam.

May Allah grant us all the fulfillment of that duʿā, inshā’Allāh.

The sea is, of course, everywhere in the world; they say that most of the world is sea. But we are in the end of times, and Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla states in Qur’ān ʿAẓīmu sh-Sha’n:<br>
ظَهَرَ الْفَسَادُ فِي الْبَرِّ وَالْبَحْرِ
<br>“Ẓahara l-fasādu fī l-barri wa l-baḥr”<br>
“Corruption has appeared throughout the land and sea.” (Qur’ān 30:41).

“Everything has become corrupted on land and sea.” Because mankind has gone astray, they have corrupted that vast sea and everything in it. The command of Islām, the command of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla, is this: Keep everything clean. On the contrary, they strive to defile everything. What they eat is filth, what they drink is filth. May Allah protect us.

When everything is done without Basmala no matter how clean they claim it is, it’s useless; they don’t really know much about cleanliness anyway. And not saying Basmala makes everything even dirtier.

May Allah guide them and send the sāhib, inshā’Allāh. Let there be cleanliness. When he comes, everything will be perfectly clean, inshā’Allāh. May Allah unite us with him soon, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
