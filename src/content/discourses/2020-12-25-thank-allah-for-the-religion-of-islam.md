Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Thank Allah ﷻ for the religion of Islam because the true religion now is Islam. There is no other religion than Islam. They have corrupted all of the heavenly religions. No true ones are left, they are all fake. Today is supposedly the birthday of Sayyidina Isa ‘alayhi s-salam. They are doing an anniversary and a yearly calendar according to it but that’s not true either. They are saying this with their own mouths. It is not what we say, it is what the Christians say. The birth of Sayyidina Isa ‘alayhi s-salam is two months later from now. Today is something they made up from a pagan religion. So since the beginning, what we mean by the beginning is fifty to a hundred years after Sayyidina Isa ‘alayhi s-salam, they came up with fake apostles and saints. They corrupted the religion from the beginning. It is the work of shaytan. The shaytan does not want the truth. Billions of people are following something fake called a religion.

Talking about the Jews, they’re not [corrupted] as much, but most of them are also corrupted. They are hiding the truth. They have the truth but they hide it because of their benefit. They do not say the truth. Our Holy Prophet ﷺ states in a Hadith Sharif: “If ten of the Jews became Muslims, all Jews would become Muslims.” Because amongst their scholars, many became Muslims but they weren’t ten. They were most probably two or three who became Muslims. They were the highest in status, but they still refused. This means that it is the wisdom of Allah ﷻ that it should be this way as a test for people until the Day of Judgment.

Those who find the truth find happiness. Those who do not find it; they say, “We created the world. It is in our hands. We do as we want. We cut, we kill, we fight.” They think that they are ruling the world. Let them rule, this is not important. What’s important to us, thank Allah ﷻ, is being on the right religion. Our religion is perfect. ‘ م لا سإ لِْ إا ِ اللّ دنعِ ن ي دِ لا ن ِإ’, ‘Indeed, the religion in the sight of Allah is Islam.’ (Qur’ān 03:19). It is the religion that Allah ﷻ wants. He completed it with our Holy Prophet ﷺ. Those who are happy have been following it. Those who are not happy have delved into this world. They don’t recognize anything but this world. They live with all kinds of difficulties because they don’t recognize anything but it. Let them claim that everything is theirs as much as they want, yet nothing is theirs. They will leave it and go away. This is important.

There are foolish Muslims who are running after this world. And those who run after them, they follow them thinking that there’s something special, their end will not be good either. Those who are attacking the Quran, the Hadith, the Prophet ﷺ and the Awliya think that they are doing something. We say, may Allah ﷻ guide them. The religion is the religion of Allah ﷻ. Allah ﷻ will protect it. They can attack it as much as they want but they’re only harming themselves and their end will not be good. May Allah ﷻ protect us. Thank Allah ﷻ, He has granted us this religion. May Allah ﷻ keep us steadfast on this path and strengthen our Iman insha’Allah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
