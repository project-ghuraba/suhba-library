Asbahna wa asbahal mulku Lillah wa nahnu abidan Lillah. As-Salamu Alaikum ayyuhal hadhirun.

What can we do? We got up with khair/goodness insha Allah. We got up with khair... Who gets up with khair? Who gets up with the consciousness of worshipping His Lord gets up with khair. What is the place of who has no consciousness for worshipping His Lord? It is below the animals. Animals know what they should do. Men who don't know what they should do are below the level of animals. As-Salamu Alaikum. Alhamdulillah. We should say the Bismillah. Our strength is with the Bismillah. Say the Bismillah & you are strong. Say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim, you are strong. No one can stand against you. Even if the whole world was shaytan it can't stand against Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. It will be crushed, melted & gone. Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Is it not so? It walks over everything. The power in the Bismillah can crush & walk over everything. Who believes in this can defeat the whole world.

Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. O our Lord, I got up for Your worship. You are my Lord. I am in existence to worship You. Accept us, support us O Lord. May You accept our worship O Lord. We are helpless. We are weak. May You strengthen us. Our ego is pushing us. With what? With shaytan. Shaytan says "Come & be with me. Come & be with me". When shaytan says this, what is its meaning? "Come & work for me. Come & be my animal" He doesn't say this but says "Come & work for me. I will pay you". What do you pay? All the trouble & ugliness. What is it that shaytan can pay to the people? He ties you with troubles. When you try to solve one of them, he ties another knot. While you try to solve this, he ties another knot..This is his job! He multiplies one trouble into thousands & makes people fall in them. For this, he approaches & says "Come to me. Is it the adhan? Let it be recited, you continue to sleep. Sleep, there is plenty of time". He makes you sleep. And on top of you he puts.. Shaytan has a lousy, dirty quilt. He throws it on you & says "Here it is, sleep, be comfortable. Don't get up. Sleep, sleep, there is plenty of time". And the one who doesn't use his head, who doesn't know his honour immediately says "Of course, let me sleep a little bit more". He sleeps & forgets his worship, his servanthood. No worship left for him anymore.

Who wakes up & says "Audhu Billahi min al shaytan al rajim. O Lord, I seek refuge with You from shaytan & his invitation. Protect me O Lord". "I protect you O My servant! Say Audhu Billahi min al shaytan al rajim & Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim"."Fasta`idh Bil-Lahi Mina Ash-Shaytani Ar-Rajim- Seek refuge with Me" He (swt) says. I am the One Who created you. I am the One Who is the Lord of the Earths & the Heavens. I am Who created them. What do you think? That they came out of nowhere? This world and all these different kinds of creatures on it, made themselves? Thu/spit on what you study as well as what you teach. "It is nature Shaykh Effendi". Ok, nature puts you through the grinder, crushes you, makes you fertilizer & throws you away! You believe in the nature but don't believe in Allah. They say "doga" now, different new names & expressions. Nature. Following this, mankind has become humiliated & lost their honour. They forgot the One Who brought them into existence from the nonexistence. YaHu did you come to existence by yourself? "My mother gave birth to us so we exist". Really? So is it your mother who created you? Then Who created your mother? Why don't you say?

They can't say "there is Allah, there is the Master of the Creation". When you go up to a mountain, or go to a place & see a building, you ask "Whose is it?" "It belongs to this person". "Who does this farm belong to?" "This is Agha's". "Who does this factory belong to?" "To this man". Then beyond this, who does this man belong to? Who does this man belong to? Ok, we understand that the factory belongs to this man. But then who does this man belong to? For everything, there is someone who owns it. "Who is its owner?" "The owner of this house is this person" "What about the owner of this farm?" "That one". "The owner of this house?" This person. Ok, we understand this. But Who is your Owner? Who is your Owner? Shaytan says "I have no owner. I exist on my own".

The cursed ones- shaytan & who believe him also, they are teaching what shaytan says. Under which title? They call it "the national education". They say "National education". What is the source of this national education? From where did you learn this national education? Who taught you this national education? There is no answer. The one who teaches the national education is shaytan. Why don't you call it instead "the religious education, the education of religion, it teaches me the religion?" "No, it doesn't suit us. It should be national but should not be religious".

Since life on earth started & Islam formed, there has been the Sultanate. Allah appointed the Sultan over you with all his majesty. He(swt) dressed this majesty on you from the majesty of the Heavenly Sultanate. You throw this away & collect people from the markets, bazaars? Shame on you. Say, may your tongue be tied. Say! Say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Why do you speak without the Bismillah? Are you ashamed to say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim? So you are ashamed, aren't you? Angels say "May none of your work succeed. May you never find any goodness, may you never find any comfort! May you not be saved from troubles, sufferings, calamities". All angels curse those people who don't say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Even if you form a 100 parliaments & put all these hundreds of deputies, ministers, it still won't succeed,O son! A cursed act can't succeed. You collect some people to follow you & then say "here, our work succeeds". Ok, go ahead & make it succeed if you can. Make it succeed & we see it. To call it "religious" is a shame but to call it "national education is accepted. What education was there before the national education? There was the religious education, the religious tadrisat. Religion is the basis- religion is the basis of the knowledge of man. The tadrisat, which they call education now, which is not based on religion, is zero. If you drive a stake into the straw, can you make a building on it? shaytan's teaching, which is the main trouble of the whole world today, says: "Don't construct the building here. Come & I show you an easier place. Come" & he brings them into a hayloft. He makes them drive a stake into the hay & says "Here, this is a very strong building. It is new. It is new, this is the innovation. You should follow the innovation. Leave the old castles, leave the old palaces. I will make you construct a building on top of the straw,it is very easy. You can carry it anywhere you like & when you get hungry, you can also eat from that hay! And that time you will become the man exactly in the shape I like. Meaning, you become an animal for me". Shaytan says to the people "Once you become animals for me, I have plenty of hay. I have so much hay, I can give you as much as you like".

In the time of the Sultan there was gold in the market- Gold & silver. They removed it in the First World War, they also call it the harb al-umumi. Instead of gold they gave paper. They said "Spend this paper. Spend with this paper". "How will I spend with it?" "Go & buy whatever you want from the market. We will put a seal on it. We will put a seal & it will be valid everywhere. Well, a paper will be worth the amount written on it. 1 TL; another one says 5 TL. 10 TL, 20 TL, 50 TL, 100 TL. Finally they wrote 200 & felt ashamed. They wrote 200. They say "there is no higher number yet but we want to print 1000 TL banknotes also". So this, to make it 4 digits now, to write 1000 on it & print such a banknote it can make the people totally corrupt & crazy. It will put them into such a situation. Therefore now, in these peoples' iqtisad, which they say 'economy' now, all the nations went bankrupt. All of the nation went bankrupt. Because they are playing with paper money. What value does the paper money have? We may not go there now.

So it means that they are not giving us what is valuable. Because it is the teaching of shaytan. In the time of Sultanate, everyone had gold & silver in their pockets. After this, there were reforms in the world & in us also. We said "We should follow the world also. Why should we keep the gold & silver. They are printing paper. They have dollar, sterling, drahma, they have lira... We may also do TL. It belongs to us. And we may also make a dollar, as the whole world follows it. But our dollar will be stronger than all others". "Where is its equivalence?" "It is in the vault". Why do you put it in the vault? Why don't you give it to the people? Why are you hiding the gold & silver? For whom are you keeping it? If it is for the people, let them have it. If not, why do you hide it? Why do you cheat the people? You show paper & say "its equivalent is in the vault". You lie! There is nothing in the vault. But they say like this, "its equivalent is in the Central Bank, it is this bank, in that bank.. It is stored there". Why should you store it? Take it out & give to the people. Give the gold & silver to the people & release them to the streets. You can't even find a man who will sell his gold. No, he will keep it! But for paper money, he doesn't care & spends it recklessly. So this is their reform, the traps of the devil. The shaytani/evil traps. Why did you refuse Allah's teaching for you? "Well, that one is religious, this one is national". It is a lie! One is Heavenly, the other is shaytani. There are 2 sects now in the world. One is the system which is Heavenly. And the other is that which is shaytani. Shaytan makes it foam & foam & foam.. But it has no result. Foaming & foaming, it spilled over like the boiling milk & finished. Only its watery part is left. Even that water is not left in those people now. This is the trap that is set for whole world now: "You will hide the gold & the silver & give paper into the hands of people". No, you don't have the right for this! A person's service cannot be paid with paper. This is the wrong way. This is the way shaytan teaches the people & deceives them. Therefore they don't find goodness: The Egyptians, Turks, Arabs, Americans, none of them. Finished. This is the trap of the shaytan. The Heavenly value is gold & silver. They hide it..No, you can't hide. Gold & silver is the right of the people, you can't hide it. It should be in their hands, not in your vaults. Gold & silver should be in the hands, in the pockets of the people. Don't say "They are in the vault of the bank". Don't lie!

This world went bankrupt because of lying. The people of this time fell into every trouble because of lies. Therefore O man of reason, O man who can think! They asked a philosopher: "Describe man". That philosopher said "Man is a creature that can think". The animal that can think, in other words. Leave aside the animals, in our time there is no man left who thinks. Straighten O people! Come to the right way. Don't fear to say Haqq! Don't fear. What can they do? They can only do what Allah wants. As long as Allah watches a person no one can touch even his hair. Some people say "How is this man?" "His back is strong" "How come?" "This president backs him up. That millionaire backs him up". Leave the people! Say, Allah (jj) backs up this man! Meaning Allah Dhul Jalal is his guarantor. Fear such a person, fear! If he touches you, not even nine doctors can find a cure for you. Don't think that it is an easy thing, to touch the man of Allah is an easy thing. Not even 99 doctors can find a cure for you. These people should know Allah! They should expel shaytan so that they become decent. They find comfort. Their dunya as well as their akhirah be prosperous.

Let us say then, Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. O our Lord, may You accept us to Your servanthood. Although we have many defects, many sins, Your Forgiveness is plenty O Lord! You made us, all these people, nations to Your Beloved (s). And these people, the people of this world are all running away from it! They will never find goodness. They will fight each other. They will crush each other. They will give each other's punishment with their own hands. This is not with money. Be a servant to Allah & be saved- in dunya, in the grave & in akhirah. If not, you will suffer. Aman Ya Rabbi, Tawba Ya Rabbi. Tawba Ya Rabbi.

May You send us the ones who will teach us. We may say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. O our Lord, may You not expel us from Your servanthood. We are Your servants. May You forgive us O Allah. They didn't teach us. May those who didn't teach suffer the punishment wherever they are buried. Those who corrupted the people are all in the torment, in the fire. May Allah make us away from them. And we should appreciate the fact that we are the nation of His Beloved & say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. La ilaha illa Allah, Sayyidina Muhammad Rasulullah (sas). Fatiha.

Even if it is little, I want to do what I can. Once my Lord helps me, a person can correct even 9 worlds. Allah, Tawba Ya Rabbi.
