After Islam opened up this place it became an important center. From the knowledge of Reality given to us, our Master, alaihi s-salatu wa s-salam, Constantinople-The city Constantine built-which was Islambol, when the good news of its future conquest was heard by the blessed Sahaba, they, wishing to obtain the honour, all followed this path (to Islambul). It became a path they took regularly. It was crossroads (to Islambul). They were passing by Kayseri. Therefore, this place, since the Prophet's sahaba/companions stepped on it and honoured it with their light, is a place that has a holy identity.

The manifestation on this mountain - You have the Mount Erciyes - to this mountain also light from the eyes of the Sahaba has reached. Janab-i Haqq has dressed this mountain in haybat/majesty. It has height on one side. From the other it looks like it has its hand open for prayer. That's how this mountain looks. Janab-i Haqq dressed it with haybat from His Mightiness. It's also teaching us that, no matter how far up we rise, in the Presence of Janab-i Haqq don't forget your servanthood. Servanthood gives you honour.

This is the second time we are coming here under the same conditions. We hope that as this place has been one of the oldest paths among the lands, just so may Janab-i Mawla grant those living here iman/faith and the haqq/reality of Islam.

G: Amin.

ShE: and may Allah make them leaders of the other lands in Anotolia with zikr and thinking and give them success in seeking the pleasure of Janab-i Haqq.

G:Amin.

ShE: Yes. Talabana wajadana. If a person asks for something truthfully according to his level of truthfulness/ sidq, he will get what he has asked. When our sidq is at a high level whatever we are seeking, whether it is the qutb, then you find the qutb, asking for Allah's awliyas, then you find them. If you are asking for the grawth (high awliya), then you find the grawth, if asking for Khidir alaihi s-salam, then you'll find him. Looking for Isa, Musa, alaihum us- salam, then you'll find them. Looking for Sultan of Anbiya, you'll find him too. Yahu if looking for Allah, then you'll find Him.

Allah is not far away. We make Him far away. Can Allah be far away? We think Allah is in the highest heaven or we will find Him in Akhirat. Why assume He is not in this world? Everyone behaves freely. Is there anyone closer to men than Allah? Let them say it. You are the one closer to us then ourselves. You are the ONE closer to me than myself. Allah Jalla wa 'Ala. That's for everything.

G: Ya Rabbi.

ShE: Mountains, rocks know this but man is ignorant. He thinks He is far away. Be with Me says Janabi Haqq, Be with Me. What is better than "being with Him". One who is away from Me falls into the flames of separation, left alone. If man is not with Allah he is forced to loneliness.

Now there is an illness. Like this cloud covering up the sky, this illness covers up this world. This illness is not spreading with a germ. Doctors say nothing is wrong with you to those having this illness. Nothing is wrong but I am sick. This is a new fashion illness. Astaghfirullah. G: My sultan you are not blessing us with himmat, then Allah is not granting rahmat either. They are buried in Rahmat

G: They don't realize it. They don't realize it my sultan.

ShE: Hell is wrapped in Rahmat. If it wasn't wrapped, it would burn everywhere. It is loneliness, loneliness depresses people. When someone says that they are depressed, what does that mean? I am alone and I am bored. When you have people to talk with then you won't be depressed, bored. Depressed for what? When left alone, they get depressed. They say: "Let me go out and find a friend so this depression goes away." OK Ana jalisu man dhakarani.(Hadith Qudsi) "I am with the one who remembers Me." Janab Allah jalla wa 'ala mashaAllah. Whoever says Allah, I am with this person, says Allah. I am with that person. When he forgets Me, trouble rains down on him. When forgetting Janab-i Haqq, darkness covers, man falls into darkness. He gets worried then; "Quickly, look... what happened, turn the lights on" a light in his heart, light in his heart. It is the Noor of Allah. This Noor, when it is extinguished, he remains in darkness. When he is in darkness, he is scared. What is coming? What will that do? Fear comes from this. When you are with Allah why fear anything?

Many awliyas go up to the mountains. They go to the deserts. When they are doing zikrullah, wild animals, snakes, lions from those mountains come and sit there. They set up an assembly and sit with the awliyas, and do zikr. They don't hurt him, how can they hurt him? There is a higher one above him. A cat when likes somebody comes and he rubs against you. If you show him a stick, he won't come near you. When you are loving even animals come and come close, enjoy it. They feel friendship from those people, no harm will come from them. All creatures, recognize those people who are friendly with Janabi Haqq. They come and sit there at ease. Because they know, he will not hurt them. Wild animals attack because they fear they will be hurt. They fear he will attack me, kill me. So they attack the sons of Adam. If not, if they know he will not hurt, they don't touch. Many awliyas, while praying, birds land on them. They land and awliyas don't touch so they feel safe. Therefore, now the land of Kayseri has 70 great awliya and sahabas' secret maqams are here. It is a land of barakat. Barakat comes down with their tajalli. This land has never had hard times because of their barakat. Because of this wisdom, Janab Allah did not let them enter here. I think unbelievers didn't enter here.

G:They did not.

ShE:They didn't enter. There is a strange secret in this place. People from here, their personality is different. Many of them have ancestors reaching to holy people, as we said. Therefore, there is nazar (watching out) to here and we hope, this place will be a key to the spiritual conquest of Anatolia.

G: InshaAllah.

ShE: We hope it will be an opening. Evet.

G: My sultan, if your himmat can take away their love of money then this will happen. My sultan, they love money so much!

ShE:No one left now that doesn't like money so much. They all became "Kayserili".

G: Those from Kayseri made everybody else like them. Now, my Sultan, Kayseri has only one illness, one fault and that is they love money. Otherwise they pray a lot, they have good hearts.

Sh.E: Allah Allah.

G: They have no other fault except this one.

ShE: Let's find a land where they don't love money and go there! We look but can't a place where the people are not after money.

G: You have found such a place alhamdulillah.

ShE: Have I?

G: This is the land of Muhammad.

ShE: SallAllahu alaihi wa sallam. May Allah give barakat to those people of Kayseri; take the love of the dunya from their hearts and give barakat to them.

G:Amin. ShE: May love of Akhirat and Mawla come to their hearts. May they never be lacking provision. That's what is important. This is a good dua for you.
