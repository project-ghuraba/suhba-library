Salām Aleikum Allāhu Allāhu Allāhu Allāh, Allāhu Allāhu Allāhu Allāh, Allāhu Allāhu Allāhu Allāh, Allāhu Allāhu Allāhu Allāh, Allāhu Allāhu Allāhu Allāh, Allāhu Allāhu Allāhu Allāh, Allāhumma ṣalli wa sallim ‘alá nabiyyinā Muḥammad ‘alayhi s-salām ṣalātan tadūmu wa tuhdá ’ilayh, māmarra llayāli wa ṭūla d-dawām. Allāhumma ṣalli wa sallim ‘alá nabiyyinā Muḥammad ‘alayhi s-salām ṣalātan tadūmu wa tuhdá ’ilayh, māmarra llayāli wa ṭūla d-dawām.

Ya salām! Now we are going in the new year. Year after year. What you realise was year after year. You realise your eyes become weaker. And then you realise that your hair becomes whiter and wiser. And you realise that your joints are starting having pain. It is not only number of the passing years. And then you realise that you are more - when you are young your head is more for sky, and now your head more for earth - looking for destiny. Why we are saying like this? No idea! If I have idea I would not speak like this, but I don't have any idea. This is all coming from Mawlānā Shaykh. Because if we are claiming something we have to prove it and we don't have the proof and we are not claiming. We're saying everything from our master.

You have to believe! This is important, if you are believer, you are the one going ahead. Today we don't believe in anything. Once upon a time some people they said "we believe we can fly" and today the humanity is flying. If that person he says "I don't believe in flying" we would never end up as today. This is, everything is coming from believing of something. That's why the belief in our life is like the soul in the body. Without the soul the body is rubbish - even nobody buy it! "I have body for sale, body for sale!" "It has soul?" "No." "Not interested." But body with soul, so many people will be interested. This is Allāh Almighty's wisdom. You have to look for the smaller things in your eyes and to get the biggest wisdom out of it. This is how the spirituality teaching you. Because everybody say "what you are teaching here?" We say "we don't know anything.” We are ignorant people but al-ḥamdu liLlāh we know everything. Lets say we know so many things. Let's say we know the key things. Let's say we know the things that make our life easy. Yes, because something you know sometimes makes your life hell. And sometimes something you don't know makes your life so sweet.

The other day, Mawlānā Shaykh Muḥammad (qas) he said a very nice story and this is.. that give some wisdom for us. And the story was about - hunger came for the country. No food, no nothing and one man he was having a donkey and the donkey become like bag of bones. So the man he said "I don't find something to feed myself, how can I feed you?" He says "I release you free. Maybe you can find something to eat around and this will be for you." And the donkey he went, going around to find some grass to find some dry bread, dry things to eat and to continue his life.

In the other side there was a lion and his personal assistant the fox. Both of them they're very hungry. Days and days they didn't eat nothing and suffering, and the fox he was looking and he sees the donkey from far away. He say "O your Majesty, I think I find our dinner." He says "where? Where?" "There is a donkey, he is walking maybe I can convince him to come to your side and when he is here you jump and we eat him." Says "good idea, go and convince him."

And the fox he went. Coming slowly, slowly. Donkey says "what, what you want?" Says "O nothing, nothing. I am travelling for very nice place." Says "where are you travelling?" He says "I'm travelling for hidden paradise." He says "what they have?" "They have a lot of greenery, here they have a lot of vegetable, a lot of water, a lot of grass. You can eat as you like." And he make it like fantastic picture and the donkey looking like me. Wow! His mouth is - "O so many food to eat." I say, Ok. "Can you take me with you?" He says "yes of course." It is plenty of food for everybody we can go and we enjoy ourselves. Say "ok, follow me." And then the donkey very happy following him. Slowly, slowly going and they come for the point where the lion has to attack for this donkey. And the lion, he was so exited, then he jump before the time. And the donkey he sees the lion jumping on him and runs away. He runs so fast, so fast!

And the lion because he was low energy he couldn't follow him, he fell. And the fox he was so angry with him. He is saying "your Majesty! You come so early you have to wait for me to give you the signal and then to jump on him. He says, "now the donkey ran." "Ohh. Please go and bring our dinner." He says "how can I convince him?" "No you will manage, you are so clever, you are this, you are that..." The lion still begging him and like kissing his hand just to bring the donkey back. The fox says "Ok, ok. I'm going to bring him." Then, he went, looking, looking. He finds him also and says, "you take me for lion to eat me?" He say "Nooo! No it's not like this!" "So how?" "The donkey [lion] was almost killing me," he says "even me, he want to eat me and I ran away. You ran this way and I ran that way." He says "but unfortunately that road it was taking us for paradise and the lion was making trap for us." So he talk, talk.... and then the donkey he says "ok we go together again," because he sell him also the new vision, the new paradise, and the donkey he couldn't manage. He said "ok let's go!" They walk, and they come for the point where the lion he was also hidden and, when he come for the target, the fox says (signal). Ok, the lion jump on the donkey and he kills the donkey.

But the lion he was so out of energy he fell asleep. The fox, he get the option to eat the heart of the donkey. He says the heart of the animal is the best place so... The lion he like to eat the heart first. Then when the lion he wake up and get his power back and he went and he looked for the heart of that donkey. He couldn't find it. He says "where is his heart?" And the fox says, "your Majesty this donkey doesn't have heart." He says "why?" He says "because he believed in me. If he had a heart and he was listening to his heart, he would never follow me again." This is how shayṭān and ego trying all the time to win your heart. Putting new things in your life, putting new visions, new dreams, new so many things, and then you will end up without heart.

This is important story in our life, that if Allāh Almighty He give us the belief and He make us to be in that belief and then we have to act according to that belief. We have to stick to that belief to not run after another vision or another dream, or another nice story. This is Mawlānā Shaykh he was trying to explain - when you are qadami thabit, solid feet. If your feet is solid, nobody can take you right or left and you cannot be like that poor donkey, he was believing in that fox, foxy fox, and end up without heart. This is important for us to understand how to act and how to believe in our way that we are going. And I think, for us, the belief it is the biggest gift from Allāh Almighty. Because Allāh Almighty when He grant the belief He said "maybe you are Muslim but the belief, you have to know that it is a grant from Me to you."

Iman, this is very important. Very important because when you have the pure iman, pure belief in your way, then you can continue without fear. No need to fear, no need to worry. Today people's illness is the worries. Today people's illness it is not having the satisfaction. Today people's illness, to be worried all the time. Today people they are scared from everything. You give your hand as a friendly hand, they say "O nobody give me friendly hand. Why you are giving me friendly hand?" "I give you this for Allāh." "No, no, no, must be something under it" "No, there's nothing under it. It's for Allāh." And if you are in the outside world, everybody will tell you "O nobody give nobody without expectation." So, if you give me sugar, you'll be expecting honey from me. If you give me honey, you'll be expecting gold from me. If you give me this, you'll be expecting and whatever you give it should be smaller than your expectation.

And this is very bad for today people, because al-ḥamdu liLlāh still there is saint around the world, still there is Mashāyikh 'idham around the world. Still some people working for Allāh and we still proud that we are continuing the journey of Mawlānā Shaykh that he spent all his life for it. And we're still proud that he give us this amanat/trust that to continue his mission, and this is being given to Mawlānā Shaykh Muḥammad that he is carrying the flag and we are all of us, in shā’a Llāh, helping him to continue his mission as Mawlānā Shaykh Nazim has done, as the previous Mashāyikh 'idham done, as this forty of Golden Chain that Mawlānā Shaykh Muḥammad is the forty-one - that continue forever, in shā’a Llāh, until Mahdi (as) coming.

May Allāh forgive us and make us from the loyal servants to Mawlānā Shaykh Nazim, loyal servants to Mawlānā Shaykh Muhammad and to make us straight in our duty because the shayṭān still not leaving us free. Beware of shayṭān, beware of your ego. Mawlānā Shaykh used to say "Beware of shayṭān". Now I understand. Beware of your ego, now I understand. Before I was not understanding, but now al-ḥamdu liLlāh I am understanding and I am trying to make you to understand also. May Allāh forgive us and make us happy here and hereafter.

Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, biḥurmati l-Ḥabīb biḥurmati l-Fātiḥah
