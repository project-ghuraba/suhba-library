There are two ways to discipline the people that get wild and to discipline the people that cause fitna/trouble. Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Rahim. One is.. in London.. for example, in London which one is the largest square? (H.F.) Trafalgar is square? (M.) It is where Nelson is located. The first thing to do is to put a gallows there, in a high place. A man should stand right next to it dressed like an executioner of people whom the kings before asked to execute. And the ax is also there. Whoever is caught, his punishment is here. They may go on, whoever we catch we will cut his head with this. And, they may do a few of them... They may cut his head off when it was on it's side. We will see if even one person is left in the square. The second one... The second one is, they will catch such people. Whoever they catch, they may not be taken to... (H.F.) To the prison? ( M.) Yes. To where? Where will they take them to?

How many cemeteries are there in London?( H.F.) They may take them to the late-Ramazan Bey, it is very big. Woking (in UK)! Who they catch, they will not take inside (prison). They will take them to the cemetery. And in the cemetery, to darkness. They will put them inside and they will return after leaving them there. (H.F.) It is perfect. How many more cemeteries there are in London. Cemetery prison! It is open & free, there is also water available. (M.) They may not be given water. (H.F.) They may do as they like. (M.) He may urinate & drink (joke).

They will leave them there for 1 night. The next day, they will put on their necks a sign, saying: "these are the bandits who ran away from the cemetery. He stayed 1 night in the cemetery prison!" They can't find a single man in the crowd! If there is someone who liked it, we will take him there again. Will take him from London to Woking. Woking...Our Haji Kemal said to me: "O Shaykh Effandi, I went to Woking for a visit. It was after Asr. When it started to get dark, I completed my visits & decided to return. I saw that downstairs, in an empty area there are a lot of kids ages 7-8. They were playing. I asked: "Where are their mothers & fathers? It is sunset & the sky is getting dark"... These kids are running, playing, wandering around there... Neither their mothers nor their fathers were around. I was surprised. After that surprise, it came to my heart: "These are are from the other group".. There are so many jinns there. These jinns have a headquarter in Woking. We used to call it "ghonjoloz" (ghost) before. Our son-in-law... there are things that we call ghonjoloz-ghost. The jinns can have many different appearances. After what I saw, it came to my heart. I realised at that time, that these are not the children of humans. They are from jinns.. I ran so fast that, I don't know if I got out from the door or from the window.
