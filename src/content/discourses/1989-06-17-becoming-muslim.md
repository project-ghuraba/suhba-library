A'udhu Billahi min ash-shaytan-i'r rajim Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim.

A young lady here has made an intention to be honored with Islam. Is anyone forcing you? She is of sound mind and full age and she has accepted Islam as a true religion with her beautiful pleasure. And with your witnessing she will make Shahada, so help. As much as she needs to make Shahada, the more we need to renew our faith. That's why:

ash'hadu an la ilaha illa'llah wa Allah ash'hadu anna Muhammadan abduhu wa rasuluhu awdana hataini kalimataini shahadataini indaka ya Rasulullah wahiya lana wadiyatun yaum al-qiyamati ya man arsalahullahu ta'ala rahmatan li l-alamin Rabbana amanna bima anzalta wattaba'na ar-Rasul fa aktubna ma ash-shahideen bi hurmati l-Habib, bi hurmati l-Fatiha.

Do you have a name you chose for yourself? Elif? Alright, do you like Elif? Elif. Elif hanim. Insha'Allah she'll have a direction like alif (upright/straight) and have faith. And here and hereafter will be prosperous for her. Congratulations! May it be blessed! Go ahead, kiss Hodja Efendi's hand, let him make a good prayer for you. Madad ya Sultanu l-Anbiya, madad ya Sultanu l-Awliya. Sultan Aziz Mahmud Hudai, his blessings have attracted this young lady who just became Muslim here. He manifested his faith by being a present witness.

Ya Rabbana laka l-hamd hamdan daiman ma khuludik. We praise Janab Allah from pre-eternity to post-eternity. He has given us iman and Islam. Janab Allah is the Possessor of Absolute Justice and His great favours and generosity can't fit within a boundary. The benevolence and kindness of Janab Allah are endless. Therefore, it is a manifestation of His great favours. Janabu l-Haqq doesn't give His favour only to some and deprive others of it. That's why, faith is an unconditional kindness of Janab Allah to His servants. Janabu l-Mawla doesn't give anything conditionally.
