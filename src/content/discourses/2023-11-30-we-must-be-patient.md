As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

The orders of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla are very important for Muslims. There are five conditions and orders of Islām. Kalimatu sh-shahādah, praying five times a day, fasting, giving zakāh, and ḥajj are the things ordered for Muslims by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. They must do these things. Of course, praying and fasting must be done by everyone. They are obligatory for everyone. It is a command. There is punishment in ākhirah for those who don't do them. In order to make up for them, if they don't do them in this world, they will be forced to do in ākhirah. These are the normal duties for every Muslim.

Certainly, zakāh and ḥajj are different issues. They are for those who can afford them. For those who can't afford them [zakāh and ḥajj] kalimatu sh-shahādah, praying and fasting are the conditions of Islām. They can give zakāh as well when they have money. And when they have more money, they must go to ḥajj. As we said, these are the conditions of Islām. There are other orders as well. These orders have their conditions. If you want to do them, you must follow their conditions. There are some things that you cannot do on your own. You cannot deal with them alone. Because if you try to do them alone, you will harm yourself and other Muslims will be harmed as well. As we are living in the end of times now, this matter is troubling people's minds.

There is oppression everywhere. There are attacks on Islām everywhere. What shall we do? They are saying, "Make jihād." Certainly, jihād is an order. But as we said, this order has its conditions. If you decide to make jihād on your own, before you even start jihād, your Muslim government will either remove you, or destroy you. And there will be harm to many others. You think you will gain, but there will be loss. Jihād is from the most beautiful orders of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. But as we said, you cannot do it by yourself. Ūlū l-Amr (those in authority) must do it. You must make jihād by his order. It cannot be done otherwise, because there will be no benefit.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says not to put yourself in danger. If you put yourself in danger, you will have gone against Allāh'sNone order. The order of AllāhNone is different. You must make jihād, jihādu l-akbar, fight your ego. Oppose your ego. Train your ego. That is also jihād. Otherwise, we can help with du‘ā’. Du‘ā’ is the weapon of believers. With permission of AllāhNone that du‘ā’ will do things that bombs and guns cannot do. Du‘ā’ will overcome their bombs and weapons. It will make them harmless. Therefore...

AllāhNone made this religion of Islām in a perfect way. It is the religion made in the most perfect way. If Muslims follow this religion truly and sincerely, nothing will happen to them. They will be at ease and others will be at ease. But because Muslims are not following the orders of the religion, all kinds of bad things are happening to them. Badness and bad people are oppressing them. What are the orders of the religion? The order of the religion is to walk on the way of our Holy ProphetNone. People who walk on hisNone way reach safety. Otherwise, it is impossible.

Shukr to AllāhNone, there are good tidings on the way of our Holy ProphetNone. Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām will come in the end of times, ‘Īsá ‘alayhi s-salām will descend, and the whole world, which is filled with oppression and injustice, will regain justice and eliminate oppression. We see clearly now that there is nothing like justice anymore. The lies and deceit in the world, the dishonesty of people have come to light. No one can be trusted. "Such and such country is so fair. Such and such country is so democratic; everyone's wish is realized, they don't oppress anyone." All of this is known to be a lie now. Oppression has reached its peak. Certainly, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says when it reaches its peak, there will be a decline. They say there is death after completion. Death means it is finished. After this peak, it will be finished, in shā’a Llāh.

Therefore, people must be patient and make du‘ā’. Believers must make du‘ā’ so that AllāhNone sends Ūlū l-Amr as soon as possible, so that HeNone sends Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām to clean out this hypocrisy, kufr, atheism and all kinds of such bad ideas, bad regimes. He will clean and wipe out all of them. It will be completely clean, in shā’a Llāh. Other ways are impossible. Other things have no benefit.

May AllāhNone help the Muslims. May AllāhNone send us the Ṣāḥib as soon as possible, in shā’a Llāh.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla give for whole human being to be in peace, to follow order of AllāhNone. What is order of AllāhNone? There is five order. Whole human being must follow this. Obligatory for every human being to follow this. "I am," they said maybe, "I am Christian, I am Buddhist, I am Atheist." This is not... but this order for whole people. No any differentiation between human being. What is this order? First to be believe and say shahādah with no god, only AllāhNone, one God. This is first obligatory for whole human being to say it. After this, praying five times and fasting in Ramaḍān. This is three for whole human being.

After this also zakāh for some people who has enough money to give zakāh. After this ḥajj also. Ḥajj also for who is got money and his health is enough for him to go and come to ḥajj because it's difficult for ill people, sick people. It will be difficult or they cannot do this. This is order for Muslim. We are not say only Muslim because first must believe in God – for whole human being. When you do this, you will be safe. You will be, you save yourself from bad end, from hell, from, forever you will be in safe. Not only in this dunyā, in this world. Whole for whole life, next life also, forever you will be safe. This is order we must follow. If you not follow, you will be punished. Even you are Muslim, you are not praying, you not fasting, also AllāhNone they said HeNone will punish. Or you can ask for forgiveness. AllāhNone [does] what HeNone want to do. But Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying, who not doing this, there is punishment for this.

This five thing for whole human being. But there is also some order, order which it is, they have thing you cannot do by yourself. This, what is this order? Order of jihād. Jihād meaning to fight for sake of AllāhNone, people. But this, this is have condition. You cannot say, "AllāhNone order me to make jihād and I am taking my sword, I am taking my weapon and attacking people." This cannot be. This is no permission for this. There must be, you follow your guide, you follow your country, you defend for your country and under order of your commander and commander is under another commander, another commander and it must be. This is, you can be like this. But for yourself, you are going and making... You said, "I will attack people."

First thing what they will do? Your country, government, policemen or others, they will shoot you and you will be died for nothing and making problem for yourself, for your family, for country, making chaos for this. And you thought, "I done very good thing, I now become martyr because I am following order of Allāh, ‘Azza wa-Jalla, jihād. I make jihād and I was died but I am al-ḥamdu liLlāh, happy." No, this is not. You are making problem and Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla say, "Don't throw yourself in danger. Wa-lā tulqū bi-aydīkum ilá t-tahlukah" (2:195). This order of AllāhNone. How you can follow this order? This is, you come against order of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. First you are not following anybody, by yourself. It is not, cannot be like this. In Muslim armies since Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Every time they are going for jihād, even they maybe send ten people, fifteen people, forty people to make jihād, this with order. "And you do, go do this and come back. Don't do anything we are not order you." And this is, must people understanding this. This is many people not understanding and they are making for themself and for other also, problem.

But, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, who follow Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, sometimes, we must be patient. Patience also good attribute, but we are not happy with what happen, what are happening in the world, because we are living in time, end of time. With everything, the mask they are fall down from people. This people who claiming they have justice, they have democracy, they have anything. But the whole this, it was lie and lie and lie – big balloon of lie and blow up now. Nobody believe for this. But, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are following still. We are not cheating people. We are not following way of hypocrite people who claiming they are with Muslim and their reality with non-Muslim or against Muslim.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are not, because we are in way of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam and we have good tidings from himNone. HeNone said, when the world [is] full of injustice, full of ẓulm, oppression, when it's full, it will be now in top. After top, coming and taking out. And it will be, in shā’a Llāh, with Sayyidunā l-Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām. Look like it is very near, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone send him. We are really whole world, not only Muslim, other world also they are not happy with this situation. Which it is only chaos and unhappiness and oppression. Every bad thing in this time. So, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are have this good tiding. When it is coming in top, it will be time to go out, go from, fall down, finish, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone make this days to come and to make us happy, in shā’a Llāh. Happy forever this, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
