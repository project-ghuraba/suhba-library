As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
إنما بعثتُ لأتمّم مكارم الأخلاق
<br>“Innamā buʿith-tu li-utammima makārima al-akhlāq”<br>
“I was sent to perfect good character.” [Muwaṭṭa Imām Mālik] Ṣadaqa Rasulullah fi-ma qāl aw kama qāl.

Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam said, “I was sent to perfect good character.” Most importantly, I was sent to perfect adab, good manners, morals, and states in people. Because people learn gradually. From Ādam ʿalayhi s-salām to the present day, every prophet taught something .

Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam is the last [prophet] and most perfect human being. Every beautiful virtue, attribute, quality, and skill is embodied in our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. He is a human being, he looks like humans, but in every respect our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam was specially created by Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla.

This month is the month of his birth. Even when he was born, he was born while prostrating. The developmental stages of babies passed very quickly for him; that is, after one, two, or three months, things progressed. His movements and speech were very early compared to others, because he was far superior to humans in terms of the capacity for endurance given to him by Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. His strength was both spiritual and physical. Because his physical strength was immense. He already had spiritual strength.

No human body could withstand the word of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. A normal human body could not withstand the word of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. No one could face our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. In terms of apparent strength, his power is such that there’s a ḥadīth that two, three or even five people were not as strong as the Prophet. Sayyidinā ʿUmar said this ḥadīth.

Even if there were a thousand, even ten thousand, they still wouldn’t match our Prophet’s strength. Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam is stronger than a million people, in that apparent sense.

His physical strength is such that Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla created him different so that he could withstand spiritual strength. Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam was 63 years old – and even then, his physical appearance was like that of a 30-year-old; his beard was black, maybe five or ten white strands, or even less.

At 60, we all turn completely white. Of course, our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam’s essence, his uniqueness, is one of a kind. Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam is worth the whole world. Therefore, Allah created him as such to carry all these states.

Shukr to Allah, we are from his ummah. Shukr to Allah. Some people—we will say that again—if non-Muslims say that, we might understand, but for a Muslim to say, “He’s a man just like you and me,” is completely illogical and unreasonable.

Every aspect and state of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam’s life was a miracle. Miracles (muʿjizā) are attributed to them, and karamāt are for awliyā. So, for those who came after him it’s not called a miracle. Miracles cannot be done by anyone else. Those are only for prophets. They exist in all of them. Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam had them.

There were countless miracles in the life of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. Some are well-known, some are very well-known. Some that people believed and some they didn’t believe, but of course, all true ones became well-known. He split the moon in two. Water flowed from his hand. He gave water to all the thirsty companions. A gazelle came and spoke to him. Insects and animals spoke in front of our Holy Prophet. There are many miracles in the life of the Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam.

Therefore, as we said, some who claim to be Muslims but deny the miracles have zero faith. They may be a Muslim; it’s just a word uttered from the mouth.

Everyone who says “Lā ilāha illa Allah” is a Muslim, but they are not necessarily people of faith. People of faith are those who follow the way of our Prophet, believe in him, respect him, honor him, and glorify him. No one has been created greater than him.

Regardless of anything else, our Prophet is above all religions in every respect. He does not discriminate against people based on race. Everyone is part of the Prophet’s ummah. All people are from his ummah. Even the jinn are from his ummah.

Those who accept our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam are part of his ummah. Those who don’t - that’s their business. Let them do whatever they want later on. They will regret it. Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam opened his way to everyone. Come and benefit from this blessing. If you say you don’t want it, then it’s up to you.

May Allah protect us. May people not be mislead, especially Muslims. May they not harm those who follow the way of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, and may they not look down upon those who show him reverence.

Because, unfortunately, shayṭān has deceived people these days. There are many who claim to be Muslim but do not show respect to our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam.

May Allah improve them. May Allah grant them guidance, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
