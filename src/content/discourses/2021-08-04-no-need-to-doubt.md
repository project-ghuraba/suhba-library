As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Fa-man yuridi Llāhu an yahdiyahū yashraḥ ṣadrahū li-l-islām, wa-man yurid an yuḍillahū yaj‘al ṣadrahū ḍayyiqan ḥarajan ka-annamā yaṣṣa‘‘adu fī s-samā’ " (6:125). Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is saying this: if AllāhNone wants to give guidance to people, HeNone opens their hearts to Islām. HeNone gives that beauty. And a person who receives that beauty doesn't get pleasure from anything but Islām. It is a kind favour from AllāhNone. When Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla wishes goodness for a person, something beautiful happens and everything appears beautiful to him.

Sometimes, in the beginning, some brothers say, "When we entered this ṭarīqah we had more pleasure. We used to perform more worship and received more benefit. It is not like that anymore." That's because when Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gives this beauty for the first time, a person leaves his old state. Later, he gets used to it, he doesn't need it. It is not given all the time because this is a world of tests. If AllāhNone gave you guidance and you got that beautiful feeling in the beginning, you must be content and be thankful. You must continue your worship. You must not leave your worship. Otherwise, if it lasted forever... Not everyone receives it. It is possible that there is something missing when such a question is asked. You have as much as you get. If you don't ask, there will be more such gifts. But when you ask, virtues decrease.

You are on the way already. You are following it. Keep firmly on it and don't look back. Thank AllāhNone. Don't say this worship, that worship. Just don't do less than you can. Don't do less than you are doing. Continue with what you were doing. That is a favour and beauty of AllāhNone for you. And when AllāhNone doesn't wish goodness for someone, HeNone puts their heart under pressure in such assemblies so that they don't feel like coming. They want to run away. And that is also a manifestation of AllāhNone. May AllāhNone protect us.

If you are on this way and your heart is open to it, be thankful for this and continue without asking questions. You have found the right way. Always continue with obedience and love in order to reach happiness on it. The end will be good. AllāhNone wished you goodness that you're put on this way. But shayṭān wants to mislead you and destroy your heart. May AllāhNone protect us.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying: "Fa-man yuridi Llāhu an yahdiyahū yashraḥ ṣadrahū li-l-islām, wa-man yurid an yuḍillahū yaj‘al ṣadrahū ḍayyiqan ḥarajan ka-annamā yaṣṣa‘‘adu fī s-samā’ " (6:125). This is āyah from Qur’ān. It said, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla who want for one to be hidāyah (guidance), HeNone open his heart for Islām. He be, his heart will be very happy in to find this way. To this, to be your heart very happy, it is not many time happen this – only when AllāhNone want. And this the best thing when AllāhNone, HeNone want you to be, give you hidāyah, HeNone open your heart for Islām and you'll be happy with Islām. Your heart will be like fresh and very happy. You can't, you cannot find this happiness in another place. But for mu’min, it is gift from AllāhNone and he, AllāhNone, HeNone want him to be in this way.

Many people, they are happy with this and many of them, they said, "We are very happy, we find our way." Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone put them in the way, so they must be happy. They must continue in this way, not saying this and that. Many people we hear, from ṭarīqah people also, they said, "First, when we come to ṭarīqah we was very happy. Day and night we try to do worshipping, to do everything. Everything we happy. Our heart it's like bird coming like this, very happy. But now we don't have this." This is because first, begining of this, AllāhNone when HeNone give this, you feel very strong this. But it is [continues] all time there so, no need to make yourself in doubt. There is... But who feel this all time – awliyā’u Llāh because they are not asking this question: "How it is like this or like that?" No. Because they know they are from chosen ones. AllāhNone choose them and give them this, in their heart and they are happy all time.

For this, we must be not listening to shayṭān. Because if AllāhNone want for people to be in not this way, they will be very bad in their heart – squeezing them, making them or thinking for very dark, not happy, unhappy, sad. Nothing can make them happy. They use everything to be happy but they cannot do. Because Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla... They are going for dalālah, for kufr, for to be unbeliever. When you be like this, never you be happy. Your heart not open at all, close and close and close. So, we are... AllāhNone give us this, we must be thankful and happy. It's to go ahead in this way, not look here, there. "How I was like before? How when first I come here how I am?" No. You only you look ahead, front and it is take from what AllāhNone give you. Because our life, you cannot go back, only go ahead. What you done before, AllāhNone forgive you and so don't be worry. Make for... Ask for forgiveness and continue. Not say, "If I do this, it was like this or like that." No. You are in way, you go full speed, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
