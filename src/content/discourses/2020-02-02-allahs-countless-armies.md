As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatuLlāhi wa-barakātuh. A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is saying, "Wa-liLlāhi junūdu s-samāwāti wa-l-arḍ" (48:7). The armies of Heavens and Earths are under the command of AllāhNone. What kind of armies are they? Are there tanks, guns, rockets and planes? Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla has all kinds of armies. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla has undefeatable armies. Who fights against AllāhNone is foolish and crazy. Mankind thinks he rules over the world now. While they say, "We have the biggest army in the world. No one can touch us. We can defeat anyone," AllāhNone made one of HisNone very small armies to destroy them. The whole world is alarmed. HeNone frightened the people with the biggest army and the whole country became frozen. AllāhNone is Great. Who wants to fight against AllāhNone is foolish.

It happened like this once before during Mawlānā's time. He was saying that was Allāh'sNone army, an invisible army. No need for a big army. If one angel is sent, for him they will not be even as big as a chickpea. No need for that. An invisible army was sent, and they are destroyed. Why? Because they went beyond oppression. They tore and burned the Qur’ān. Mankind went further, they reached the last level of unbelief. So they got a slap. And this slap came to the unbelievers. If Muslims want to be protected, they should return to AllāhNone. They should repent and ask forgiveness. Nothing will happen to them with Allāh'sNone permission. But those who are with those others, who see them and think they are doing right and try to act in same way, they will be punished.

Brothers keep asking, "What's this? What shall we do?" Pay your ṣadaqah. It will protect you from accidents, sicknesses, and everything. And then repent. "We are begging you, O AllāhNone. We are not with those people. We are not against YouNone. We are happy with YourNone judgment. We obey YouNone." Say this so that you do not get punished.

It is a big miracle in fact. People think it is a disaster but it is a clear miracle of AllāhNone. A person who claims that he is the bravest in the whole world, cannot open his mouth against this oppression. Because these people have big armies, he said, "We can't deal with them. We would rather stay silent." You keep silent. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla won't remain silent. When it comes against the work of AllāhNone, AllāhNone doesn't fear anyone and won't look at consequences. People in this world think how it will turn out when they are thinking to do something. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla doesn't worry about that. HeNone doesn't worry about the end. "Wa-l-‘āqibatu lil-muttaqīn" (28:83). A good end is for those who are God fearing. They will have a good ending. And a bad end is for those who go against AllāhNone and do not accept AllāhNone. There is nothing to be afraid of. Fear AllāhNone and you won't need to fear anything else. This is the summary of this issue.

This world is not where we will live forever. In this world, we have things to do, duties to keep for Ākhirah. We should do those things. The biggest thing is to fear AllāhNone, to be a friend of AllāhNone. Don't oppose AllāhNone.
