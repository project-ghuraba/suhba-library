As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa raḥmatu Llāhi wa barakātuh. A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīn wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

Our ProphetNone is saying, "Khayrukum man ṭāla ‘umruhu wa ḥasana ‘amaluhu". What is our purpose in this world? HeNone says, "Who has long life and spends his life by doing good deeds and follows the way of our ProphetNone, he is the best among you." A good person is not the one who earns the most money or who leads the most people. Apart from the orders of AllāhNone, there is no benefit in anything. No matter how much knowledge, education, or rank someone has, he has no value in the eyes of AllāhNone. The worthy is the one who follows Allāh'sNone orders and conducts his life on that way.

You don't have to be imām or ‘ālim to be a good person, says our ProphetNone. Any Muslim who carries out Allāh'sNone orders and avoids forbidden things all his life is the best, the best among the Ummah. Our purpose is to be accepted by AllāhNone. To be accepted by the people in this world is sometimes not acceptable in the presence of AllāhNone. Most people who are accepted by AllāhNone are not given value and care here. But the value given by AllāhNone is accepted. The rest is nothing.

Therefore, may AllāhNone give us long life. May HeNone make it easy to be on the way of AllāhNone. That is a favour. That is a kind grant AllāhNone gave us from HisNone generosity for us to be on the right way. If it's for the ego, most people will follow other ways. But AllāhNone wished goodness for us. HeNone made it easy for us to keep on this way. Therefore, don't give much value to this world. You have joined the way and follow a Shaykh, that is a big favour. Know the value of that favour. Don't say, "I did this. I did that." All of our egos can take us from the right way. However, if a person controls his ego, AllāhNone helps him too.

May AllāhNone not separate us from this way, in shā’a Llāh. May we be on the right way both while young and old. Some people sometimes say when they are young: "I am too young. Let me have fun now. I will come to Allāh'sNone way afterwards." That is a wrong thing. Because near AllāhNone young pure people are among the most beloved. They receive the most blessings. Their stations become high. After doing things, certainly there are virtues for those who repent. But the young pure people are gathered together with the Prophets. A young pure person is the person who respects AllāhNone and fears the forbidden things. Therefore, they should come to this way while young as well. May we be on this way till the end of our lives and until our last breath, in shā’a Llāh.

ProphetNone saying, "Khayrukum man ṭāla ‘umruhu wa ḥasana ‘amaluhu." HeNone is saying to us what it is the best thing for believer, for whole human being of course: "Who he becoming old and his good deeds, he make good thing." Good thing, what is good thing? To build long building, to have big money? To have to be president? To be this? No this not good thing in Divinely Presence of Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla. This is not any no any value for this. The value – you obey His order. His order, HeNone is our Creator, His order good for whole human being. To do this is good for you, for believer, for non-believer, for whole creature. This is Allāh'sNone order, to be good for all of this to obey and to make what HeNone is order. First to worship HimNone, to know HimNone and to be mercy for creature and to good to do good thing for people, to not harm people. This is good thing. And who he is – AllāhNone give him long life, he is the best one in Divinely Presence of Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla. This is ProphetNone and of course in ProphetNone also heNone is also happy, loving this people – not to be coming old and every people they waiting to what this man doing. No. It is the best thing for us, for all of believer, to be in this order of ProphetNone and which is to be good for people. And when you are good for people first you be in this become good for you before others also.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh we are in way of ProphetNone, way of mashāyikh and who coming to this way they must accept to not look. Some of them they saying, "We do this we do that." No. This is, when you say this you finish your value what you done. Even you done, you do this for sake of AllāhNone not for sake and for anybody – first for AllāhNone and HeNone is reward you. HeNone is happy with you from young until the end of your life. People also, some people saying, "We can be good later." No. From beginning if you be good Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla HeNone like more young people who are following HimNone. They are the His lover and they are in stage like Prophets, very, very high stage they have.

So, in shā’a Llāh, we try to be whole life from childhood until end of life, our aim to accept, be acceptable people in Divinely Presence of Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla. This is our aim; not to for dunyā. Only for Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla and ProphetNone. AllāhNone help us because this also from His favour for us. We cannot do without His favour, His big favour to get us to be in good way and acceptable people. AllāhNone help us, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
