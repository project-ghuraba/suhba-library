Maulana: Bismillahi r Rahmani r Rahim. Now, Shaykh Yahya may ask what he is going to ask.

Sh. Yahya: Yes. In the morning you said there is no use of my going to India, this you would be explaining.

Maulana: For what you are going to India?

Sh. Yahya: Now, I think I've already found some tariqa, some spiritual path I'm much more familiar to Hindu philosophy than Islamic philosophy and I think it will be also possible for me to find a spiritual teacher in India as well as here but you think this will not be possible.

Maulana: Now, this instrument, some people are working hard and they are making this instrument. What are the principles of these instruments? They found them and they are arranging and making this tape-recorder. Is there any necessity for us to return and search what those principles are so that we may rediscover and make this again?

Sh. Yahya: There's no necessity, we have a tape-recorder.

Maulana: Yes! Here.

Sh. Yahya: This accessory is for 200 Syrian Lira, as you know.

Maulana: Yes. Now, searching about Hindu philosophy or Chinese philosophy or Persian philosophy, or others' philosophies. Now, we must look at what is the most important and most improved tariqat. Now, in the world, which one is that? There are forty or fifty philosophy ways. What is among all these tariqats or philosophic ways or philosophic schools? What is the famous one now? You can't find one that is more improved than Sufism in Islam.

All of them, when we are making qiyas - measuring them with Islamic Sufism, will be reduced to nothing. Or, everything that every philosophical school is carrying, you may find in Islamic Sufism, most completely. They (other schools) may signify something but, a complete existence for that thing you may find in Islamic Sufism. Therefore, it is better to understand firstly, what philosophers are meaning by making philosophical schools? Or in other words, 'What is philosophy for?' This question, if we can give an answer for philosophy, then all of the answers must meet on that point, must meet all of them. If they are not meeting, it is not philosophy. If the meaning of philosophy is changing according to the person or schools, it is not philosophy. Or it means philosophy has not any base, a strong base to build any building on it. If philosophy keeps changing in every mind, in every century, in every nation, that means philosophy has not any mustaqirr, hanh, firm base, any foundation. You can't find any foundation for philosophy. Therefore, it is only wasting our precious lives for nothing. And it is such (?) we are saying.

Philosophy has so many meanings in every mind, in every century, in every country. Therefore, no, you can't find at any time all philosophers to agree on a point. You can find every philosophy from the centre, going out, as his mind is ordering him or indicating to him. They are not coming from the outer to the centre, perhaps they are moving from the centre and going in different directions. Isn't it so? We are asking what is the centre of philosophy? What is the main goal of all philosophers. You can't find the mushtarak, the common goal of all philosophers, because they are doing, just as I am saying now, they are going from the centre, out.

Sh. Yahya: So how can you find the centre Maulana?

Maulana: Since you can't find the centre, therefore, there is no philosophy in Islam, because philosophy is not based on a firm foundation. Bring to me two philosophers meeting on any point with their ideas. Philosophers, leave them with one another, and they're going to disagree amongst themselves also.

A philosopher, when he is hungry, says something. When he is full-up, another mind emerges. When he thinks at nighttime, it is another, daytime, sun-shining time, yet another thinking replaces it. When it is wintertime, his thoughts are in one way, and in summertime, his thinking is in another way. When he is young, his mind is another, when he is an old person, it is changing and he is fighting his thoughts of when he was young. When can you find those two persons, two philosophers to meet on the same goal?

Therefore, philosophy has no base for building on. Therefore you may see all philosophers making private écoles, schools for themselves. Therefore, in Islam, no philosophy. Some ignorant people are saying 'Islamic philosophy', but in Islam there is Reality, no philosophy. No! La ilaha illa’Llah Muhammadun Rasulullah. You understand now?

Sh. Yahya: Yes, yes

Maulana: It is clear?

Sh. Yahya: Hmm Maulana: I can discuss with everyone, this point. This is Reality. No imagination in Islam, that is the way of philosophers. Therefore, Shaykh Yahya may go and seek Indian philosophers, find what they are saying among themselves, because they are only using their minds. Philosophers are imprisoned in their..

Sh. Yahya: Philosophical edifice

Maulana: Hanh, philosophical? What's edifice?

Sh. Yahya: That's a building, like a limit compartment.

Maulana: Yes, they can't come out. And if a man cuts out the wall that his mind has put in front of him he still can't reach Reality. They are always going into the prison of their minds.

Sh. Yahya: Are you saying that in order to reach Reality we have to silence our own minds, or get beyond our own minds?

Maulana: Yes, because in ourselves, as Allah Almighty is saying, in ourselves and in our outer life, there are so many things that it is impossible for our minds to solve them. We can’t find any solution with our minds for those happenings, in ourselves or in outer life, in the universe.

Sh. Yahya: Not through experimental and empirical knowledge also?

Maulana: No! Because so many things, you can’t try them, you can't see them, you can't imagine them. Therefore we must believe that for understanding some realities our mind is not enough. We must use our spiritual powers for understanding. For example in ourselves we have a spiritual life, we have spirits, we have souls but, we may say we are alive, we have life, but we can't explain what is the secret of life and what is the secret of death, but it is an event you can't deny. You can't deny it. And you can't explain what is the secret of life, even in a cell. You may put everything there from materials that a cell contains.

Sh. Yahya: It still wouldn't have life.

Maulana: What is that secret? You can explain with your mind? It is impossible. But there is a Reality. There is life and so many (other) things. Now men are proud with their knowledge but their knowledge is only like a drop from an ocean in front of unknown knowledge. But philosophers are using only their minds. Not accepting that after mind there are some other powers man has been given. If they are accepting this, they must accept prophets. Not a single philosopher is listening to even one prophet. They are such foolish people, all philosophers. They are not accepting this instrument, saying 'No we are not accepting this. We must think, and we must find the principles and we must do this'. And if I say, what are you saying to me? (They say) ‘You're foolish, you're mad!’

Sh. Yahya: Maulana there are people, for example, in Hindu tradition who aren't philosophers, say they're mystics and also follow revelation.

Maulana: What does it mean 'mystic'? Mystics khurafah, (sheep) also, what does mystics mean?

Sh. Yahya: To be in communication, to turn to Allah Almighty, to have m`arifah. They are also claiming that they believe in certain revelations and prophets but the different traditions and they claim building upon this.

Maulana: What is meaning of mystics in Arabic?

Listeners: irfan / al arif, / arifin.

Maulana: Hanh 'arifin, mystics. If it is tasawwuf, yes we are saying also the same thing because everything in our life is going towards improvement, or everything is improving, or developing. Therefore, we are not going to study using the first machines, now that there are electronic machines, yet, we are asking what is the principle of the first machine? How is water boiling and going around this, that? For what, this? You have too much time to waste? Yes. For what Indian mystics? For what Indian mystics for us? Has no development come to people since 4000 years? No development coming? All European people are running after Indian philosophers, Indian mystics? And in front of them the most complete and developed mysticism, tasawwuf is ready.

Sh. Yahya: We've been Influenced by Indian mysticism before we came to Islam. We all came to Islam partly through Indian mysticism, we began studying Sufism through that.

Maulana: Western people always interested in oriental countries, oriental thoughts, oriental things, just as oriental people are always interested in western countries. This is it only. And as much as you are full-up with Christian or Jewish priests, or religious people who do not look to Islamic principles, your heart is boiling and you are finding a way to Indian mystics. Then little by little, from it, development is coming. This is the point.

Wa min Allahi tawfiq bi hurmati l-Fatiha.
