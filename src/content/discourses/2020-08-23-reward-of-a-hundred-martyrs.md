Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Our Holy Prophet states in Hadith Sharif:

“So hold fast to my Sunnah and the path of the Rightly-Guided Caliphs who will come after me.” Our Holy Prophet says, “Follow the way I showed you and what I did, and follow my Sunnah.” The Sunnah is all the things our Holy Prophet did. Whoever does them will receive the reward of a hundred martyrs in the end of times.

Four caliphs came after our Holy Prophet. And there are some things that the Companions also did. Our Holy Prophet says, follow them too. Do what they do, hold on tight even with your teeth so that they are not forgotten. These are gifts to the Ummah, a gift from Allah. For every Sunnah a person or a Muslim does, he has the reward of a hundred martyrs in the end of times. Allah knows how many rewards even one martyr has.

Following even the smallest Sunnah of our Holy Prophet and doing it is a great favor from Allah Azza wa Jalla. The companions carefully followed all the actions and deeds of our Holy Prophet. They were with our Holy Prophet 24 hours, and his wives were also with him. They told us how to do everything and what to do, Allah bless them. These have been known for 1400 years. They were written in books and volumes of books. Insha’Allah none of the Sunnahs is lost. By the will of Allah Azza wa Jalla, none of them is lost. Therefore, these Sunnahs should not be taken lightly.

Let alone the small Sunnahs, they are even making people leave the big Sunnahs as in prayer. And the one who does this is supposed to be a scholar, a teacher or a student. It means they didn’t understand anything. Those teachers or those who look after the religious affairs should read this Hadith Sharif first. It is advised that they do that as much as they can because the nation follows them. When they follow their ego, they are deprived of that great virtue and they are deprived of rewards. Since they are guiding people, people think of them as something [important] and they are harmed as well. May Allah protect us. May Allah help Muslims. May we be successful in doing the Sunnahs of our Holy Prophet insha’Allah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
