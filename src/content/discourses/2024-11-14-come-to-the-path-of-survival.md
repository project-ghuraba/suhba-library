A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Wa-yā-qawmi mā lī ad‘ūkum ilá n-najāti wa-tad‘ūnanī ilá n-nār" (40:41). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘Aẓīm. In Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n, Mūsá ‘alayhi s-salām says to his nation: "I am calling you to salvation. I am calling you to the good path. But you are not accepting it. You are calling me to be destroyed. You are calling me to Hell." Since the time of Mūsá ‘alayhi s-salām until now, most of the time people were like this. Prophets, awliyā’, ‘ālims and ‘ulamā’ have been calling people to salvation. But they don't want it. "Come with us. Be destroyed, too." They call to Hell. They call to make shirk to AllāhNone, to make kufr to AllāhNone. Someone wishes goodness to people. But they wish the worst on the other side.

A clever person wants to go to a good place. He wants to be with those who wish goodness. He doesn't want to be with those who do badness. He wishes to stay away from them. Both before and after the time of our Holy ProphetNone, the nature of mankind has always been to obey the ego, to listen to the ego. They always stay away from the truth and serve badness. Wherever there is badness, they are there. And in the end, they perish. But not only them; they don't want to keep this for themselves. They want everyone to go to Hell with them. There are foolish people sometimes who say, "There are many people in Hell. There are artists there, such and such people there..." They joke about that. The end of this joke is not good. It is a bad joke.

May AllāhNone give guidance to everyone, both to artists and non-artists. May they be with ḥaqq, truth. May they be on the way of salvation. May they come to salvation. The way of salvation is open. The way to Paradise is open. It is enough for everyone. All can fit in it.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, in Qur’ān saying: This āyah from Sayyidunā Mūsá, ‘alayhi s-salām, saying: "Wa-yā-qawmi mā lī ad‘ūkum ilá n-najāti wa-tad‘ūnanī ilá n-nār, tad‘ūnanī li-akfura bi-Llāhi wa-ushrika bihī" (40:41-42). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘Aẓīm. Meaning: "I am calling you to survive from... to good thing, to be safe from bad thing. And you are, what you doing? You calling me to... "No, you come. Don't do this. Don't follow this way. Follow us. We take you to bad thing, bad, every bad thing. We are calling you there to be non-believer, to be against Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, to be against whole religion. And you will be with us in Hell." "I call you for Paradise; you call me for Hell." This is different. There is two way for human being. So there is one, want good for human being, for whole human being. "Come to peace, come to Paradise, come to beauty of life and beauty of next life also." But when you are calling me: "Come to Hell, come to us. You will be bad like us. Don't follow your order of AllāhNone. Follow us, follow order of shayṭān. You will be with us in ākhirah." Because there is, sometimes they are making joke: "In Hell there is beautiful people, or artists or actors or singers or this. But in Jannah, what is this? You, only, you have dervish, you have people they are not wearing, not enjoying their life." And, but they are, this is bad joke.

We are also, want this people also to come to right way, to ask for forgiveness from AllāhNone. AllāhNone forgive them, and they will be in Paradise also. Until end of life, still there is a chance for people to come back for AllāhNone. AllāhNone forgive them. But when they are, they finish their breath, their life finish, no more chance for them. For this, in this verse of Qur’ān: "I am calling you to survive, to survive forever." Not when only here. Forever, you will be survive. You will be in Paradise, in shā’a Llāh.

AllāhNone give hidāyah for all of the people. We, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are with people who want good for human being. Not with people who want bad for human being. We, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we want for them happiness. We want peace. We want for them good life, here and hereafter. AllāhNone help us. AllāhNone help people to come to listen to advice. This is advice, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
