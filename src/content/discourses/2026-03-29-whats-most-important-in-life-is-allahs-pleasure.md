As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Shukr to Allah, we have returned from this journey. It was a blessed journey for the sake of Allah, inshā’Allah. As the ḥadīth sharīf states, whoever loves for the sake of Allah, whoever hates for the sake of Allah, whoever gives and receives for the sake of Allah, if everything one does is for the sake of Allah, then that person has īmān. May everything be for the sake of Allah.

When our journeys, our stays, our comings and goings are all for the sake of Allah then we will have earned Allah’s pleasure. That is the most important thing in this life.

As we see, people have become enemies of one another, they cannot stand each other. That is not a good thing. Why? Because they have forgotten the pleasure of Allah they have become engrossed in the world. They love what they love for their own ego. They hate what they hate for their own ego. They do not love what Allah loves, and they love what Allah does not love.

Therefore, everyone is not at rest. They are only thinking of their egos, wondering what will happen and what will remain. They harm themselves. And they don’t bring peace to others, nor to their families.

Therefore, a person who seeks Allah's pleasure for the sake of Allah, Allah will be pleased with them. Their life will be good. They will accept everything. They will know that everything that happens comes from Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla.

Therefore, they will neither love this nor quarrel with that. Life is already passing by. Ramaḍān has passed, Eid has passed. In almost a couple of months, Ḥajj will come, the new year, the new Hijrī year will come. It’s all passing by.

Therefore, let’s live life beautifully. Let’s live life as Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla wants. Let’s live as Allah says. Ignorant people say, “We only live once, let’s have fun,” but having fun doesn’t benefit them. If your enjoyment isn’t in accordance with Allah’s pleasure, you can’t have fun, you can’t have peace and you can’t be happy. Those who are with Allah are happy. Otherwise, you’ll just keep sinking deeper. Those who seek Allah’s Allah pleasure will rise higher and higher, by Allah’s permission. That is what’s important.

Therefore, may Allah grant us all to attain His pleasure, inshā’Allah. May Allah bless our days, months, and our every hour, inshā’Allah. To be blessed means to attain Allah’s pleasure. May Allah be pleased with us.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
