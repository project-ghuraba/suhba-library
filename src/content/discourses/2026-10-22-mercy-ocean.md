As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا تُوبُوا إِلَى اللَّهِ تَوْبَةً نَّصُوحًا
<br>“Yā-ayyuhā lladhīna āmanū tūbū ila Llāhi tawbatan naṣūḥan.”<br>
“O you who have believed, repent to Allah with sincere repentance.” (Qur’ān 66:08). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says to believers to repent to Allah and ask Him forgiveness. Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam says, every human being makes wrong things all the time. For this, Allah addresses the believers also. Don’t think believers don’t do wrong. Every human being is a sinner. We sometimes do something wrong, sometimes sin. But Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla is merciful and He forgives us, yet we must ask forgiveness. When you do something wrong, Allah is happy for you to ask forgiveness.

Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam says that Allah says, “If a nation of human beings don’t sin, I will create other people to sin and I forgive them.” This is Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla’s mercy. For this, we are lucky, alḥamdulillāh, He forgives us.

In everything you do wrong, you must ask for forgiveness and intend not to do it again. But also if you do a wrong and ask for forgiveness, Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam says, Allah forgives you. They asked Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, “Even if we do it again?” Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam said, “Even if done again.” They asked him, “And again?” He said, “Even if again. Even if a hundred times, Allah will forgive you a hundred times.” If you do sin and ask for forgiveness, but after that do it again and again: in every time, Allah forgives you.

This is the ocean of mercy of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. Only ask for forgiveness. Don’t be hopeless. Because there are people who invent some things from their heads and show it to people as if it is religion, Islām. This is the saying of Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, ḥadīth. Allah says in ḥadīth Qudsī also that He is happy with the one who asks forgiveness.

So we are, alḥamdulillāh, lucky with this mercy, mercy ocean, because we are from the nation of Sayyidinā Muḥammad ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, Ummat Muḥammad. Before him, to ask forgiveness was more difficult, not easy like this. But for the sake of Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla opened this mercy door, inshā’Allāh.

May Allah make us to be in this mercy, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
