As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Yā-ayyuhā lladhīna ’āmanū rka‘ū wa-sjudū wa-‘budū rabbakum wa-f‘alū l-khayra la‘allakum tufliḥūn" (22:77). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘aẓīm. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is saying in Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n, "Perform worship, make sajdah and do goodness."

Goodness is helping people in all kinds of ways. Showing the way is goodness. All kinds of good deeds done for people are commanded, meaning you should do them. Prayer and prostration are obligatory. The others are also commanded. Those who do them reach salvation. What is salvation? It is when people take their last breath on the way of AllāhNone, when the last breath leaves the body with the pleasure of AllāhNone. That is the goal. You should do all types of goodness. And eventually, you will reach salvation.

Because this world is like a prison for the soul. Turkish people say the soul is a bird. That bird is in a cage. And the cage is our body. It wants to escape from that cage. That escape must be good. Either way, it is not happy with the cage, whether it is good or bad. If you put the bird in a golden cage, it still won't be happy. If you open the door, it will run away. The soul is the same. It waits for its time in this body. As soon as the door opens, it goes immediately. But as we said, does it go for salvation, or take a turn for the worse?

Those who carry out the order of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, according to this āyah certainly go for the better. They escape from the cage and go to a nice place. Otherwise, people who do badness and don't carry out the orders of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, those who harm everyone, their souls go to the worse place. Those who will go to a good place are the people who do what Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says. And the way is shown.

Doing goodness is good for everyone. People who do goodness are loved by others. And those who do badness are not loved. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla doesn't give them love. Because when Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla loves someone, HeNone tells Jibrīl ‘alayhi s-salām, "INone admire this servant. Tell everyone to admire him." Jibrīl ‘alayhi s-salām informs everyone. And people love him then. Creatures also love him. Otherwise, when someone is bad, "INone don't like him. Don't let anyone like him," says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Even though they pretend to love him in this world, not only do people not love him, but they look for some benefit. It is a strange situation which is not love but badness.

May AllāhNone protect us. May AllāhNone help us to do goodness and good deeds. May we be allowed to go on HisNone way, in shā’a Llāh. May HeNone give permission for this. Shukr to AllāhNone, people present here, believing people, are striving to use various means and tools in order to do goodness and to attain the pleasure of AllāhNone. They are both performing worship and making qurbān. They ask what to do with it. Helping poor people, helping people in need, helping neighbors – these are all acts of goodness. May AllāhNone not deprive us from that goodness.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, saying in Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n, "O believer, you pray and make sajdah and do good thing, good deed, khayr." Good – be help for people, for yourself, do good thing every time. This is order, "La‘allakum tufliḥūn" (8:45). You be succeed, you will be happy at the end. To be win – falāḥ. Because this is way to happiness. Because we are living for ākhirah, not for dunyā. And Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone gave us what we must do to follow one step, second step, until the end. You will be happy at the end. This is for believer, and HeNone said, "Yā-ayyuhā l-ladhīna ‘āmanū." "O believer, who believe in this, you must, don't follow other way." Follow this way. At the end you will be winner.

Because everybody in this life they have like soul, and soul it is in our body. Because our body, without soul, nothing. It will be rotten quickly with[out] this soul. But it is like cage for our soul, this body. And our soul waiting to run away from cage. They not happy in cage, so we must be careful for this. Because this cage must be good. But for cage, it's for believer or for non-believer, same cage. It is not... soul cannot be here. When they find place to run away, quickly run away. Even cage from gold or from diamond, also it will not stay there. So when cage open, this, our soul, going.

Where it's going, it's important. If going to Paradise, this is winner. This is way of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. What HeNone show us, you do this, quickly your soul, from opening door, flying quickly to Paradise. But for other, also – even this cage not [as] bad as other side – still not staying there. Soul running, flying, but for, flying for fire, for jahannam, for hell. This is two way.

But Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone is showing and giving direction for people to follow this direction. But people not following direction of AllāhNone. Following direction of shayṭān. And shayṭān take them to hell. We are living really in a time which people they are not care, and they make joke about this or, even they don't know anything and not believe. But it is not important. Important thing, if you are in right way, thank AllāhNone for what HeNone gave you from this good luck. This is luck. In Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n also, "ḥaẓẓin ‘aẓīm" – who has this, he good, big luck. He is very lucky who find this, because he is lucky. In dunyā maybe you can be lucky sometimes, sometimes not. But there is an option to be continue. But for the last minute, it is no any more luck. So if you are in this way, continue, and thank AllāhNone for this. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are seeing people they are liking to do good thing. To who make good thing, people also love him, like him, even if he is not very, they not... Not bad people, they like good people who doing good thing. Because why, this? Because Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla love him. When AllāhNone love somebody, HeNone order Sayyidunā Jibrīl ‘alayhi s-salām, "O Jibrīl, INone love this man or this woman or... So call people to love him." And people they love him. But when HeNone not love him, HeNone said, "O Jibrīl, call this people. INone don't love this one." So people also will not love him. They said, there is a secret. Even maybe you can see some bad people, you thought that people love him. Never. It is just from outside. No anybody can love him from his heart. But for believer who good, doing good thing, AllāhNone give love for, from heart, for these people. AllāhNone make us from them. AllāhNone keep safe from every badness, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
