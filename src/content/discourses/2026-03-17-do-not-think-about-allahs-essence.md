As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
وَلَقَدْ خَلَقْنَا ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلْأَرْضَ
<br>“Wa-la-qad khalaqnā s-samāwāti wa-l-arḍa”<br>
“And indeed We created the heavens and the earth” (Qur’ān 50:38)

وَقَدَّرَ فِيهَآ أَقْوَٰتَهَا
<br>“Wa-qaddara fīhā aqwātahā”<br>
“and measured therein its sustenance” (Qur’ān 41:10). ṢadaqAllahu l-ʿAẓīm.

We must think about the power of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla, says our Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. But don't think about the essence of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla like how, where and what He is. Think about His creatures, and take lesson from them. Know the greatness of Allah through them, says our Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla who created this world, earth and sky did everything with such measurement that some people can comprehend them. They are only few. Most people cannot comprehend them. If people interfere in things they can't understand, they may lose their mind. Either mind, or īmān may be gone. Therefore, our Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says not to think about the essence of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. Stop thinking about it.

Some people go too far. They worship useless things as idols. And there are those who've gone even further. Hāshā Allah, as if they go around people in different bodies. These are so nonsense things, things that have nothing to do with logic. You are human. If Allah gave you mind, your mind has its limit as well. Some things are limited. Don't go close to them. If you do, you will perish. If you approach them, you will be ruined. Therefore, you must know those limits.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla created earth and sky. He created them in six days according to human calculation. Allah knows how many millions and billions of those six days. You don't know it. But people are still striving to know something. They are striving to find out how big this universe is. People can't find its edges. And even this is showing the greatness of Allah. How did He create? What is the measurement? Everything is done. There are billions and trillions of stars existing without striking each other. Just like everything, they have a lifespan. When their life is over, something else will come.

Allah knows Himself what will happen. And now it is our turn. When our time comes, we will have to meet Allah. Think about that. We should carry out His orders and perform this beautiful worship. And then we should believe in Allah and wait for the day we'll meet Him, for that is the most important day. Passing with īmān, going to ākhirah by saving our īmān is what's important.

May Allah accept all our worship. Our worship is deficient, of course. There are many people who interfere in everyone's business. "Your worship is good. You prayed well. You didn't pray." There are many people who say so. We know that our worship is full of flaws. Allah will accept its flaws.

May Allah forgive and accept inshā’Allah. May He lead us to paradise inshā’Allah, all of us inshā’Allah.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
