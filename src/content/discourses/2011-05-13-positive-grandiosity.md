swt) Mawlana Shaykh Mohamad Nazim al Haqqani an Nakshibendi 11 Jumādā ath-Thāniya 1432 A.H. Friday, May 13, 2011 Translated from Arabic by: http://saltanat.org/ BismillahirRahmanirRahim What has reached from the World of Dominion to the servants of Allah Almighty is for whoever exerts effort until he reaches that spring, or to the source of that pure spring. But the one who uses his personal mind, this is only temporary. You may use your mind, but it may become empty later. Glory to Allah Almighty! A scholar (aalim) whose knowledge is from the Malakut - from the World of Dominion - that knowledge doesn't "increase". It comes full and remains full. But, those who did not reach to the Spring that is based in the World of Dominion - the Malakut - they are cut off. For those who have reached, their hearts are connected to the Malakut - the World of Dominion. They never finish. They may speak on various topics, and, it may never stop or finish. It always comes, continuously coming. As an example, the Water of Zam Zam. It is in the middle of the desert. First of all, the Archangel Jibril (as), touched the ground with his wing and that spring started giving water. But if you had dug down from that spot a thousand meters you would not have found water. It was not possible to find water in that area. It only happened by the Divine Order, Glory to Allah Almighty! And, it will never finish or run out of water. It is always increasing, never decreasing. If there are a million visitors, it is enough for them. if a billion visitors, it is enough. Trillion..it is also enough for them. It is continuously increasing. Therefore, Heavenly Knowledge is similar.

"Wa 'Allamnahu Min Ladunna 'ilman" (18:65)

"We have taught him from the Heavenly Knowledge". Glory to Allah Almighty! And, that is still within a dot, Ilman Ladun - The Heavenly Knowledge. We are not relating this to or talking about the Head of all Prophets, Sayyidina Muhammad (sas). No! Although there is no way to express or describe the Sahib ul Risalat, we say that he is the one with the Absolute Message. And he is also, "la sharika lahu". He (sas) also has no partner. Allah Almighty made him 'one'. And the ocean that was granted to the Prophet with the Absolute Message (sas), no one else was granted. Glory to Allah Almighty. The Prophet Moses (as), was proud of the knowledge that was granted to him. He was carrying [the Torah]; and the Tablets that were granted to Prophet Moses (as) were carried by 40 camels. And still, his knowledge [that of Holy Prophet Muhammad (sas)] can never be matched, or there is nothing like it. In the holy verses from Surat al Kahf (18:62-65), we read:

62. Falamma jawaza qala lifatahu atina ghadaana laqad laqeena min safarina hatha

nasaban

So when they had passed further on, Moses said to his boy-servant:

Bring us our morning meal; truly, we have suffered much fatigue in this, our

journey.

63. Qala araayta ith awayna ila alssakhrati fa-innee naseetu alhoota wama

ansaneehu illa alshshaytanu an athkurahu waittakhatha sabeelahu fee albahri

AAajaban

He said: Do you remember when we betook ourselves to the rock? I indeed forgot the

fish, and none but sheytan made me forget to remember it. It took its course into

the sea in a strange (way)!

64. Qala thalika ma kunna nabghi fairtadda AAala atharihima qasasan

[Moses] said: That is what we have been seeking. So they went back retracing

their footsteps.

65. Fawajada AAabdan min AAibadina ataynahu rahmatan min AAindina waAAallamnahu

min ladunna Aailman

Then they found one of Our servants, unto whom We had bestowed mercy from Us,

and whom We had taught knowledge from Us. Prophet Moses (as) when they reached that shore and, when the fish went into the sea and became alive again, he understood that he had found the person who had been taught the Heavenly Knowledge. A servant – abdan – indicated by the arabic letter (nun) at the end of abd … The word "abdan" is to give honor and glory to that servant. They found a servant from among Allah's servants, who had been granted "Ilmal Ladunna", the Heavenly Knowledge. Allah Almighty says in the Holy Quran, "We taught him from our Heavenly Knowledge", "We granted him Heavenly Knowledge". Rahmatan - Mercy, "And, We have sent you as a Mercy to all creation". That is for the Prophet (sas). He was sent as a Mercy for the whole creation "Wa Ataynahu Min Rahmatan Min AAindina" (18:65) And We Have Granted Him a Mercy from Us. It is impossible to enter into that ocean. The one who is granted a drop from this, that drop is itself like an ocean. Even if you are granted a drop from that ocean, it will be like a huge ocean for you. And Allah Almighty grants oceans, oceans... And He grants, and gives.. But, the capacity (or opportunity) of the ant is something; and, the capacity (or opportunity) of the elephant is something else. The capacity (or opportunity) of a small fish is something and that of the whale is something else. And, why does the fish open it's mouth in the water? It means, it wishes to swallow the whole ocean. That is why it is opening its mouth. That fish has this eagerness, or Absolute Aspiration, harisun Aleikum. These are oceans. Sayyidina Al Khidir, had been granted something. Not one creature can be in direct connection to be receiving from Allah Almighty directly. No! There must be a means. There is a means between us and the Divine. But for the Ilmil Ladunna, the Heavenly Knowledge, you must seek. "Wattahu ilayhul wasila". You must seek a "means". It was not mentioned in the Holy Quran, the word "wasita" "intermediary". But, it was mentioned, "wattahul ilayhul wasila". Seek, or find a means to be able to connect. The pronoun "ilayhi" meaning "to him", to whom is it signing or pointing? Find a way to reach and connect to whom? "Ilayhi". This "ilayhi" refers to whom? Does it refer to Allah Almighty? No! It refers to Prophet Mohammad (sas), "Wattahul ilayhi al wasila". "Try to find a means to reach, or to connect to him". And, if you wish or ask nearness to him "wattahul wasila", then you must find the means. Without the means, it is not possible to reach or to connect. A servant is asking to fly. If that servant does not use a plane, there is no way to reach, or to fly. "Wattahul ilayhul wasila." Find the means to be able to reach to him, or to connect to him. Allah Almighty says: "Wattahul ilayhi..." Find the ways to reach to him, or to connect to him. That pronoun points to whom? (This is addressed to all the servants.) "Ilayhi" - "to My Prophet, to My beloved Messenger, the one who represents Me". This is most important! Where are you? If you don't know that one, how can you know Allah? Fatiha! This is a deep ocean. Fatiha. But, the human being is arrogant, and proud. He never submits. And he never humbles himself. And his aim or target is [egoistic] pride. The Prophet (sas) is granted the Secret of the Ocean of Grandeur. All human beings are asking Grandeur – 'Kibriya' from our Creator. Glory to Allah Almighty. They like and desire this Grandeur. We are asking for grandeur to glorify our Lord Allah Almighty. Originally, it was not for our ego or for ourselves that we are asking Grandeur, no! We are asking it, to be able to reach to our Lord, Allah Almighty. For example, a rubbish person is not granted an audience, nor is he accepted at the Royal Court, no! He must seek more grandeur to be able to enter into the Royal Court. This is why we are asking for Grandeur. There is nothing wrong in the creation. Glory to Allah Almighty. A person likes and desires Grandeur, for what? SH: This is a new interpretation, and it is very important A person likes Grandeur; but when our ego seeks or desires grandiosity, that is a disliked character. But if you are using Grandeur for your Lord, that is permitted, and allowed, and even preferred. "Kibriya" and all Greatness is for Allah Almighty in the Heavens. Therefore, when we desire or ask Grandeur for ourselves, it is for Him, for His sake, because then, we can be in the Divine Presence, and in His Greatness and Grandeur. Like, when a person dresses in the best possible clothing to be able to attend the Royal Court. This Grandeur is a very subtle, fine point. There is no interpretation for it. With what intention are we asking for this noble pride or Grandeur? - For Him, because He made this secret in ourselves. The secret is to desire greatness, or Grandeur, for His Sake. Glory to Allah Almighty. La hawla wa la quwwata... SH: You have corrected the understandings, and you have changed the way to look at this understanding from beginning to end. It was one of the disliked manners. Now it became from the preferred manners. It is from Hakkikat. Subhuanallah! There must be a secret, that made the human being desire and ask. He made this desire in our heart! We did not buy this desire for Greatness from the market. It is from Him. And there is nothing wrong in the Creation. And He likes that his servant be dressed from the ocean of Grandeur, even a tiny bit. And how can we be in the Divine Presence without noble pride or greatness? Are we going to be like a slave? No! He, Allah Almighty likes that all His servants are kings. And He is the King of Kings. How many kings are there? - 5? 6? [Mawlana waves his hand, indicating countless kings.] Allahu Akbar, Hisham Efendi. Your pump pulls, doesn't stop. We became hungry. SH: No, leave us in the Ocean of Grandeur. This new interpretation went beyond any books. Nobody wrote about this point. I don't know, this just came now. SH: Fresh And, it is a very important point.
