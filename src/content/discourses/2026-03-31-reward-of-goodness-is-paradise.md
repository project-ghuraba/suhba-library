As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَـٰتِ أُو۟لَـٰٓئِكَ أَصْحَـٰبُ ٱلْجَنَّةِ ۖ هُمْ فِيهَا خَـٰلِدُونَ
<br>“Walladhīna āmanū wa ʿamilu ṣ-ṣāliḥāti ulāʾika aṣḥābu l-jannati hum fīhā khālidūn”<br>
“And those who believe and do righteous good deeds, they are dwellers of Paradise, they will dwell therein forever.” (Qur’ān 02:82). ṢadaqAllahu l-ʿAẓīm.

Those who believe and do good, Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says in this blessed āyah, are the dwellers of Paradise. They will be there forever, immortal. This is a great gain. People forget this, and work for dunyā; they work for dunyā without believing. But the essential thing is to believe and do righteous deeds, to do good works, to do goodness.

Islām is goodness, Allah’s commands are goodness. There is no evil in them. Evil is with shayṭān. And those who are his followers help him. Those who do evil will be in the opposite of that: they will be in Hell. So, there is punishment and reward. Who will attain reward are those who are on the right way, who believe in Allah, who fulfill His commands, and who do good to people in every way; they will remain in Paradise forever.

Paradise is a place that not everyone can enter. But if they want, everyone can easily enter. He who does not follow his ego, who overcomes and destroys his ego, who does not obey shayṭān, and who obeys Allah will be in Paradise forever.

Paradise is nothing like this world. There are some gatherings mentioned by our Prophet ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, gatherings of Paradise, his holy tomb, Paradises of dunyā. They are beautiful examples of the Hereafter. Those who pass by and stand there find peace and experience the beautiful state of Paradise.

Therefore, goodness brings goodness. We should not think of anything other than goodness. A person who thinks evil torments himself and harms himself. May Allah protect us.

May we always be in goodness, inshā’Allah.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
