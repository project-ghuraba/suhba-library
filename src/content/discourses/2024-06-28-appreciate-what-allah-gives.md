A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

By the wisdom of AllāhNone, mankind's ego is ungrateful. It doesn't appreciate anything and thinks everything happens by itself. It is ungrateful. It does not know how to economise. By nature, mankind is raw. If Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla guides a person, and he trains himself and becomes good, there will be benefit to him, both here and hereafter. The benefit will be for himself.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is the one who gives everything. Who gives, doesn't need anyone. The one who gives is Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. HeNone has given it to you. "Wa-mā khalaqtu l-jinna wa-l-insa illā liya‘budūn. Mā urīdu minhum min rizqin wa-mā urīdu an yuṭ‘imūn" (51:56-57). HeNone says, "INone created humans and jinn in order to worship and gain rewards. I ask neither provision, nor food from them." Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is not in need. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is not in need of anything. HisNone attribute is to have no needs. Therefore, these people must be grateful.

In order for them to appreciate their blessings, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla has given examples in Qur’ān, in many places. There is a place called Saba’ in Yemen. Everything was abundant there: food, drinks, fruit and vegetables. But they went against AllāhNone. They disbelieved and became kāfirs. When that happened, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla became angry with them. HeNone didn't like them. Their beautiful lives were turned into fear, famine and misery, because they did not appreciate their ni‘mah (blessings). They didn't respect their ni‘mah. They thought it would be like that all the time. But when you don't appreciate ni‘mah, that ni‘mah goes away, says our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam in ḥadīth sharīf.

Our Holy ProphetNone saw a small piece of bread in the house of our mother, Sayyidatunā ‘Ā’ishah. Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam took it from the floor, blew the dust off, put in hisNone holy mouth and ate it. That is a lesson for us. Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu alayhi wa sallam shows us what to do and how. And heNone explained its wisdom. HeNone says, "Be careful. Appreciate your blessings." Once a blessing is gone, it is hard to find it again. Very rarely does it come back, wealth and other things. Once you lose it, it is hard to get it back.

Therefore, when you appreciate ni‘mah, that ni‘mah stays with you. If you don't appreciate, you may run after it all your life. There are thousands of rich people. All of them wasted their money thinking their richness would last. They didn't appreciate ni‘mah. They didn't show respect to it. They didn't treat it as it deserved. So they lost it. You look at them and think, "They were once rich. Now they are not. What are they doing?" – "I am dealing with some work. If it works out, I will earn a lot of money." Wish you well. What are you earning now? AllāhNone opened it once. But you didn't appreciate it. Once it slips out of your hand, it is hard to get it back.

Therefore, this is a big key to success, a secret to success in life, explained to us by Our Holy ProphetNone. Pay attention to ni‘mah. Don't lose it. Our ancestors used to say: when the well is open, don't wait for it to close. Fill your bucket from it. Fill it from that well. Don't just wait. Don't say you will fill it later. The more you fill, the more water will keep coming. If you don't fill it, it will go dry. Therefore, appreciating ni‘mah is important. We see the condition of this world. There was so much abundance and barakah (grace). As no one appreciated it, everyone is complaining now about their livelihood and other things. Why is that happening? As our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam said, it is because we lost these things, we lost ni‘mah, that these things are happening.

May AllāhNone make us from those who appreciate. Because who appreciates knows the value of the OneNone who gives ni‘mah. Ni‘mah is valuable. Who created it is valuable. HeNone is the most valuable. Therefore, we must give value. May AllāhNone make all of us from those who appreciate, in shā’a Llāh.

We are living in the place and time which it is important for people to know value, value of everything what AllāhNone gave us. HeNone give us, HeNone gave many thing, without asking. HeNone don't want anything from you. HeNone not in need for anything from you. Only for your benefit, HeNone give everything. For AllāhNone, if HeNone in... This is attribute: to be not in need. Because, HeNone create everything and give everything. Nobody can give anything for HimNone. But for HisNone, sake of HisNone, for sake of AllāhNone, you can help, you can give, you can ask from HimNone to help you.

HeNone said in Qur’ān, "Only INone create human and jinn, to worship MeNone. INone don't want any provision. INone don't want any food." HeNone is not in need for this. And for worshipping, also HeNone not in need, but from HisNone mercy, HeNone gave us this opportunity to get reward, get good thing from HimNone. HeNone [is] generous. Generous people, they are happy when somebody happy with them. So, this is Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla attribute.

And HeNone give everything, but people they not appreciate what HeNone gave, what HeNone give also. Many example from this. This one, we read in khuṭbah – "Qaryatan kānat ’āminatan muṭma’innah" (16:112). Meaning, there was one city, one country. This country, now, to these days they call Yemen. In the old time, they called Saba’. This is, it was, everything, too many thing. You go from place to place, whole way, full of fruits, coming on you shadow. Water, gold, everything they have, they had, this people. But after they said, they forget AllāhNone, and he become against HimNone. And not appreciate this, what AllāhNone gave them from HisNone gift. And AllāhNone take all this from them. And after this, they cannot find anything to eat or to drink, and they become... They was, before, they have safety, and after this, they had all only fear and hunger. All this, said Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, from what they done. "Bi-mā kānū yaṣna‘ūn" (16:112). They done this: not appreciate, not give value for this what AllāhNone, and not praise Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. And all this gone from their hand. They become miserable. And this is very important thing.

Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, heNone, we said in khuṭbah – also ḥadīth. Once Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, heNone went to room of our mother, Sayyidatunā ‘Ā’ishah. HisNone wife, blessed wife. And heNone – raḍiya Llāhu ‘anhā – heNone find, little piece of bread under some corner, or some, down. And heNone looked, and heNone took this, and heNone blow – it was dust on it – blow, and clean it, and heNone eat it. HeNone is showing us what we can, we must do. When you find something from AllāhNone gift, don't say, "This is dirty. This is bad, throw away." You must respect. You must be... even you don't eat, you can keep for animal, for other thing, but not throwing there. And heNone said, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, very... Everything heNone said, wisdom.

But this also for us, very important for people. Whole world now, this day, forget this. HeNone said, for, "Yā ‘Ā’ishah be careful for this provision, rizq, or what AllāhNone gave you. Be respectable for this. Don't see it is small and no important for this. You must look for everything."

Because when this provision gone from you, it is so difficult to come back. It is not easy to come back. Maybe, people they thought, "Oh, later I can build myself, I can become... " We see thousands of people who was rich and after they bankrupt and they struggle, today, tomorrow. "We have new project, we have a new business, we can do. You must look after this." But when you find, very rare, people, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, can they take it again. Meaning, maybe, one from thousand people only can take this.

For this, when you have good thing, you have ni‘mah from AllāhNone, you must keep, you must protect, you must look after this. Don't say, "I will make better and I will, I will throw this." Because we see many people, they left their job there, and they said, "We was looking for better but now we are jobless. We don't have anything." And so you must be careful. When something AllāhNone gave you, you must be keep it, until maybe, make it better. But to not appreciate, and say...

But nowadays people, their attribute, whole, like this. And they are, if you gave them... We see many poor people, you gave them sometimes, something, they throw this in way. Some of them throw in your face also. This is... this type of people in these days, they are living. They are not appreciate at all. And the whole world now in crisis, in economic crisis and I don't know what crisis. They have this everywhere, from themself. From what AllāhNone say, "Bi-mā kānū yaṣna‘ūn" (16:112). They bring this for themself. So AllāhNone keep us to appreciate HisNone, what HeNone gift for us and to thank for HimNone. When you thank, it will become, give you more, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone give us this good attribute, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
