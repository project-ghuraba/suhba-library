As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

The ḥadīths of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam from yesterday are beautiful, truly beautiful. The words of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam are priceless treasures. Whoever obeys him, follows his example, and heeds his advice and beautiful words will be happy and from people of joy both in this world and the hereafter.

This beautiful way of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam is a way devoid of any evil.

Yesterday we read, our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam said, “No wealth diminishes from charity.” Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam said this as an oath; anyone who says that their wealth decreases from giving charity is lying. Charity increases wealth, wards off trouble and calamity, and leads to all kinds of goodness and the pleasure of Allah.

Therefore, this ḥadīth we read yesterday is a very beautiful saying of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. There is no doubt about this, as our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam swore an oath; no one should think, “If I give charity, my wealth will decrease.” By giving sadaqah, a person protects himself, becomes a means of attaining the pleasure of Allah, and fulfills our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam’s word.

Secondly, even if you are in the right, if you forgive someone who has harmed you, you will become ʿAzīz (honored) in the Presence of Allah, you will be a person of dignity and you will be an accepted person.

Otherwise, if you succumb to your ego and quarrel or argue with that person, that virtue will be gone. This is also important, because it is not easy for a person to control his own ego. To relinquish falsehood and injustice for the sake of Allah, even when you are in the right – people may sometimes object by saying, “This is unacceptable,” but if you relinquish it for the sake of Allah, Allah will make you honored and exalted in His sight. This is also very important.

Thirdly, asking for money from people, begging—our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam said that this is not a good behavior at all. It leads to poverty. If it is given to you without you asking, if Allah sends it, you receive it; but if you are constantly saying, “Give me,” then that leads to poverty. May Allah protect us.

These three teachings are very beautiful pieces of advice from our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. They are a means to a person’s lifelong goodness, beauty, happiness, and bliss, both in this world and the hereafter.

May Allah make us among those who follow his way, inshā’Allāh. May Allah grant us all the ability to accept this beautiful ḥadīth and act upon it without following our own egos, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
