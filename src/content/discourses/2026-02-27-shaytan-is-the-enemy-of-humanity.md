As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Shayṭān, “علیھ ما یستحق”, ‘alayhi ma yastahiq [upon him what he deserves], never stops. He is constantly striving. He tirelessly and relentlessly pursues mankind to drag them into Hell. Most people are already subject to him, together with him. He shows no tolerance for those who are not with him; he wants to send them all to Hell. May Allah protect us.
Therefore, being with Allah saves us. Nothing else benefits humankind. Because it is Allah’s dominion, Allah’s will. To oppose Him is foolishness. If Allah has given something, one must obey Him. One must follow the good way He has given. Do not be with His enemy.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says,<br>
إِنَّ الشَّيْطَانَ لَكُمْ عَدُوٌّ فَاتَّخِذُوهُ عَدُوًّا
<br>“Inna sh-shayṭāna lakum ‘adūwwun fa-ttakhidhūhu ‘adūwwā”<br>
“Indeed, shayṭān is an enemy to you; so take him as an enemy.” (Qur’an 35:06). ṢadaqAllahu l-ʿAẓīm.

He says that shayṭān is the greatest enemy. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla commands, do not take him as a friend. Consider him an enemy. An enemy can never become a friend; that is, regarding shayṭān. How can an enemy [from mankind] become a friend? Only if he comes to the way of Allah he then becomes a friend. You cannot befriend those who are not on the way of Allah, those who are against Allah, you cannot befriend shayṭān. He does not wish you well. He constantly desires evil. He constantly seeks all kinds of trouble and suffering for you. He strives day and night to eliminate even the slightest goodness he sees in people.

Therefore, be careful. Do not befriend shayṭān. Do not follow his way. Stay away from him. Stay away from his soldiers. He is only concerned with how much he can corrupt societies and how much evil he can inflict. He never thinks of anything else. He is an enemy of humanity, let alone Muslims. He wants people to do even more evil.

May Allah protect us from their evil. Be with those who are on the way of Allah. Be with those who are on the way of our Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Hold on to both, not just one, because if you don’t, shayṭān will snatch you. They ask, “What is ṭarīqah?” and say, “There’s no need for ṭarīqah.” Those who say that have no benefit in their knowledge. No matter how many thousands of books they write, no matter how many students they teach, if they lack faith, they are all in the hands of shayṭān.

May Allah protect us from their evil. May He not separate us from the right way, inshā’Allah.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq

Al-Fātiḥa

There are recited Qur’ān Khatms, ayāt, surahs, salawat, tasbihat, good deeds done. We gift them to the soul of our Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, for the souls of his Ahlu l-Bayt and ṣaḥābah. For the souls of all prophets, Awliyā’, asfiya, mashāyikh. For the souls of our ancestors. For the souls of believing men and women, Muslim men and women. For goodness to come and evil to be gone. For the strength of our īmān.

Lillahi Ta’ala

Al-Fātiḥah.
