Waṣ-ṣalātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīn wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā

Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad.

Live on earth, but there is a set time appoionted by Allah for age and life. There is Next Life for all. Sometimes a person is sick, [and] thinks he will die. In fact, [though] sometimes he continues to live, but a healthy person might die. There is an appointed time but by Allah. Death will find us all. May Allah have us pass this world with faith, insha’Allah, because there is nowhere else to return. In the Quran, it is said that

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm.

Everyone will die, no one should think they have any guarantee. Everyone will die, and everyone will go to the Next Life, [but] before reaching there [and] while still in this world, one should [really] live—that would be a gain. There is no benefit in pondering whether one lived longer than another. A person might live a short time, but have done better than you, [and] earned his next life.

An unbeliever might live for 100 years, or 120 years, might try to live for 200 years, but no matter what he too dies. Therefore, let us make our Next Life thriving. We should not bother ourselves with thinking how long others live. Only ponder on yourself. This is an opportunity, being still alive. As long as we are alive we must ponder on the Next Life. The world is temporary; it will slip out of our hands.

May Allah give us true faith. May He let us contemplate it everyday of our lives. May He let us think about it with the image of the Next Life in front of our eyes. There is nowhere else to turn. Think whatever, be wherever, do whatever you do, but have love of Allah and His prophet in your hearts. They will save us, insha’Allah. Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah
