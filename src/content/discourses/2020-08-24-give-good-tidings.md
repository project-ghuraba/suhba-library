Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

A’ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm:

(Qur’ān 39:47). Allah Azza wa Jalla mentioned in many places in the Holy Qur’an: ‘And give good tidings to the believers.’ Give good tidings to believers. Allah gives good tidings of paradise and beauty to people who have faith. The good tidings start from this world until the hereafter. Because whoever has faith has been given good tidings. Allah has given them a great blessing. When this blessing is given, it means it is good tidings. The one who doesn’t win that, even if the whole world is his, it doesn’t matter and it’s of no use. The good tidings is for the hereafter. The one who has a good end has attained goodness. The good tidings is to attain goodness. You can’t give good tidings with bad news, but with good news.

Therefore, the pleasures of this world are temporary. We live in this world for the sake of Allah. We eat and drink from His favors. We need to know its value. If we do this, we will live happily and well in this world. In these difficult days, some people are given benefit from the sufferings they go through. Who are they? They are the ones who are given good tidings. Allah rewards them with every second of their suffering.

The other class who have forgotten Allah, they have all kinds of bad deeds. They give good tidings to themselves, but they have no good tidings. They suffer in vain and they live in vain. They have a life of no use. They are not given good tidings. May Allah make us one of those who are given good tidings. May our end be good insha’Allah. May Allah guide those who do not know about this. And may Allah guide those who have deviated from the way as well insha’Allah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
