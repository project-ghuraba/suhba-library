As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

We are thankful to Allah, shukran lillah. We are happy to be with people chosen to be beloved for Allah. Those in this majlis, in this gathering are beloved ones to Allah. Maybe about ten days ago, they asked us this question - which people always ask. A boy who’s a university student asked, “How can I be Waliyullah?” Because there are some people who make a course on how to make people Waliyullah. There’s a three-month course they write on the internet on how to make you Waliyullah. So, how can one be? You must pay maybe a thousand dollars, and after three months you become Waliyullah! There’s no need for this! If you’re praying five times, fasting, paying zakāt, performed Ḥajj, you’re a beloved one for Allah. Waliyullah means a beloved one for Allah. If you’re looking to become a Waliyullah, beloved for Allah, to make miracles, to make karāmāh, this means you’re not doing it for Allah, you’re not a beloved one for Allah. You’re doing this to get benefit. Waliyullah knows, he doesn’t need to make a karāmāh for himself.

So, alḥamdulillāh most of you here are doing what Allah orders, and Allah loves you. If He doesn’t love you, He wouldn’t have put you to be here. Because millions of people are going after their desires, going for shopping and coffee shops. They have no interest in religion or asking for the sake of Allah. Who asks for Allah, Allah will never make him go empty handed. Allah rewards him.

For this, alḥamdulillāh our ṭarīqah, from beginning to end, aims for this matter: the matter of how to make you beloved for Allah (Waliyullah). When you are beloved for Allah, you will be beloved for people also. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says, “When I love someone, I order all the creatures to love him.” But whom Allah doesn’t love - even if people maybe follow him it’s for money, not because they love him. It is only because they love his money or whatever he will give them; not for his personality. Only the one who loves Allah and Allah loves him, people will also love him.

For this, in our ṭarīqah, the way of Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam taught us every good thing. It is benefit for all humanity, from beginning to end. Not as they say it was a thousand year, a hundred thousand or five hundred thousand years ago - this is the biggest lie of humanity. Without ṭarīqah, there’s no life, no spiritual life or spirituality. Every goodness came with it. Even people who are not following and are cursing Islām, harming Islām, and Muslims who are also harming Islām - still Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says, for the karāmāh of these people, Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam says,<br>
بِهِمْ تُمْطَرُونَ، بِهِمْ تُرْزَقُونَ، بِهِمْ تُنْصَرُونَ
<br>“Bihim tumṭarūn, bihim turzaqūn, bihim tunṣarūn.”<br>
“Rain falls upon you by their barakah. Rizq (provision) comes to you by their means. You reach victory by their means.” 

Through these people, you get rizq, victory and rain.

For this, it is important for all human beings to follow ṭarīqah. But this is also - as we said in the beginning - you must be chosen by Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla for that. He says who will be following ṭarīqah and not following shayṭān. Ṭarīqah is the heart of sharīʿah. Ṭarīqah is what Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam taught us and is still teaching us for the whole Ummah. The first thing is to love; for the Muslim to love his Muslim brother. And if you love somebody you cannot behave badly towards him or others. One will be all the time in adab and good behavior. Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam taught us every good manner. Alḥamdulillāh here you have good manners and good adab. And inshā’Allāh, all will be beloved ones for Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. Allah protects you from every bad thing.

Before, they thought that by destroying the Islāmic state, the Ottoman Empire, they would finish Islām. But Islām is safe with good behaved people, good mannered people, ṭarīqah people and Awliyā’ullāh. They attacked Islām in every way. And if they had actually succeeded, you wouldn’t have found anyone here, not one Muslim on earth. 

And what are we doing [to face them]? We don’t have weapons, we don’t have missiles, we are not fighting, we are not making secret plans to destroy others. But they are doing all of this. All these kuffar, unbelievers, anti-Islām groups are doing all this to finish Islām. But Allah protects it. We are relaxed, we are happy. We have nothing to worry about. Why? Because Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam said,<br>
لَا تَحْزَنْ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ مَعَنَا
<br>“Lā taḥzan, innAllāha maʿanā”<br> 
“Do not grieve; indeed Allah is with us” (Qur’ān 09:40). 

Even when they were in the most difficult time, Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam and Sayyidinā Abu Bakr, he said, don’t worry, don’t be sad, Allah is with us. The Creator of all of the universe is with us. So don’t worry about these weapons, about these people. Just be in this way. Keep your heart with Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. And the end will be good for us. 

As they say, I don’t know is it an English saying or what - “He who laughs last, laughs best.” But when you laugh before, thinking you are finishing some people, in the end there won’t be any victory for you. It is said in Sūrat al-Muṭaffifīn,<br>
هَلْ ثُوِّبَ ٱلْكُفَّارُ مَا كَانُوا۟ يَفْعَلُونَ
<br>“Hal thūwwibal l-kuffāru mā kānū yafʿalūn”<br>
“Are not the disbelievers paid (fully) for what they used to do?” (Qur’ān 83:36). 

Mu’mins will be seeing these people in hell. And Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla is asking them in this ayah what they’d done, what did they get for what they’d done. Allah is asking in the end of this Sūrah. 

So we should be in the way of Allah, and not to worry about what’s happening here and there. Just be sincere with Allah, love Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam and love Mu’mins and Muslims, inshā’Allāh. 

May Allah bless you. May Allah keep us and make you more and more, to be hidayah for others, to be all blessed ones, inshā’Allāh. May Allah make you all to be Awliyā’ullāh, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
