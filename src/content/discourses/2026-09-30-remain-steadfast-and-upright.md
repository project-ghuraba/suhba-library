As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
فَاسْتَقِمْ كَمَا أُمِرْتَ وَمَن تَابَ مَعَكَ
<br>“Fa-staqim kamā umirta wa-man tāba maʿaka”<br>
“So remain on a right course as you have been commanded, [you] and those who have turned back with you [to Allah].” (Qurʼān 11:112). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

Regarding this āyah, our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam said, “It caused gray hairs to appear in my beard.” Naturally, the blessed body of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam is in human form—he is a human being—yet he possesses immense strength to bear the Word of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. There are indeed a few scattered white, graying strands in his beard—just a handful—and this is due to the gravity of this āyah. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says: “Remain steadfast; You and those with you as well.”

Steadfastness is crucial. He says, “Walk truly upon the right path.” Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says, “Do not swerve here or there; do not stray from the path.” Because this is such a weighty command, even with all his immense strength, our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam remarked, “It caused hairs in my beard to turn white—meaning, it turned parts of my beard white.”

Of course, it was like that even then. And remaining steadfast in this time is extremely difficult; yet, it is a command. Obedience to the command brings gain to a person. Whoever obeys the command becomes a person accepted in the sight of Allah; a person beloved in the presence of Allah.

Nowadays, the world employs every manner of trickery, hardship, and evil to try to lead people astray and cause them to lose their steadfastness.

Straying from the path means ruin. It means losing everything one has gained. That is why—so that we do not lose sight of the path of uprightness—our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam uttered these blessed words, “Remain steadfast and upright.” Steadfastness and uprightness are a great blessing and a great gain.

A person bears no such obligation unless they are a Muslim; once one becomes a Muslim, however, this obligation applies. Otherwise, those outside of Islām do as they please. But if you are a Muslim, you are bound to obey this command.

May Allah grant us all ease. May we remain steadfast and upright, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
