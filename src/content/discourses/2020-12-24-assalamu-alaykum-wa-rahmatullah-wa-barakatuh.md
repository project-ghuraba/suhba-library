Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Our Holy Prophet states

“Let him speak good or remain silent.” Speaking should be for good. A person shouldn’t speak something that is not useful because it harms people and it also harms the person himself. Therefore, it is better to keep quiet and stay at home in the end of times. This time is the end of times so we should speak good. Everything causes Fitnah, and Fitnah causes harm. At first it harms the person himself. He thinks that he is gaining some benefit. There is no benefit but harm for both himself and others. Now they came out with these things where everybody follows someone and believes them too. Allah gave everybody a mind to think. They accept the bad as good and the good as bad. Therefore, this Hadith of our Holy Prophet is important. All of his Hadiths are important.

In our time, these people are saying speak out. But what should we speak about if it’s not going to be good? It is better to keep quiet than to speak something that’s not going to be for benefit. When you hear a news, Allah says in the Holy Qur’an: ‘اوُنَّيَبَتَف’, ‘Investigate.’ (Qur’ān 49:06). Look back at its origin to see if it is true or not. If it is good, accept it. If it is not, then do not be upset for nothing and do not worry. After all, most of the news we hear, even the most reliable ones, can distract people of today and make them do as they please. Thousands of news come out, a thousand articles are published, speeches, etc. But all are unnecessary. They’re not for good but for evil. May Allah protect us. May Allah always show us the truth. May we always follow the truth insha’Allah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
