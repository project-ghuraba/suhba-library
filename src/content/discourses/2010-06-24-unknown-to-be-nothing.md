Dastur Ya Sayyidi Allah Allah, Allah Allah Allah Allah, Aziz Allah Allah Allah, Allah Allah Allah Allah, Karim Allah Allah Allah, Allah Allah Allah Allah, Subhan Allah Allah Allah, Allah Allah Allah Allah, Sultan Allah

O our Lord, give Your most high praisings and glorifying to Your divinely deputy from pre-Eternal up to Eternal and grant us from his honour to be his real followers. May Allah forgive us. And we are saying our salute to holiest one on this planet, Qutb ul-Mutasarrif, Sahib az-Zaman, to look after [this] humble meeting. And asking from his holiness, heavenly support. May be that support, weakest support, but taking ourselves to heavenly centre. Therefore we are needy to reach such a one who is responsible and muqtadir, able to take ourselves up!

O People, ask to reach upper levels and stations. That is our good effort, or best effort that we may use, to climb from our level to another level. And we are saying Assalamu alaikum our attenders, who are asking more unchangeable and never-ending honour, and pleasure, and satisfaction, and praisings from the Lord of Heavens. Give an attention, and say: audhu Billahi min ash-shaytani r-rajim. You must be awakened - a wakened one, awakened, awake. Don't be heedless, instead you must try to be an awakened one. Yes.

Assalamu alaykum to those also, whom, they are interested to hear something. Everyone interested [in] something. For your interest to be for something never-ending you must be interested in Eternity. If you are not interested in Eternity you are going to be dust. Nasyan mansiyya - Salafi ulamas, welcome to you, Ahlan wa Sahlan. Say! Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. For the Honour of the Lord of Heavens, stand up. You are claiming that you are ulamas, and your level, you are thinking is over the level of [everyone] now living through ummah, yes?

We reached two words from Holy Quran. That is mentioned through Holy Quran for Sayyidatina Maryam. She was saying:

wa kuntu nasyan mansiyya And that I were not in existence, unknown (19:23).

What does it mean, "nasyan mansiyya"? Write a book on it. Not to look at other books and making copy [of] something, no. From yourself - what you are understanding, "wa kuntu nasyan mansiyya". It is mentioned for Jesus Christ's mum, Sayyidatina Maryam. She was saying, "I wish to be nasyan mansiyya (non-existent, unknown)". That is an ocean, Subhanallah. Shaykh Hisham Effendi. For what she said that? "That I were not in existence, unknown". That is her wish, tamanni. Another word there is? Or deeply [yearning], she was saying, "I wish not to be in existence, [I wish] to be unknown.

To be known through our real being, that belongs to our spirituality. Second, is to be known from material being. She was asking to be nasyan mansiyya. She was asking not to be known through Creation. She was asking to be in heavenly oceans, to be in those oceans, [where] no one except her Creator, the Lord of Heavens, knowing her. And she was asking not to be anything for herself, nothing! "I wish to be nothing!"

But shaytan trying to make people to wish to be something. And she was asking to be nothing! Mansiyya, mansiyyan. Unknown. Unknown. "I never like [it] to be known. I wish to be through Unity Oceans of the Lord of Heavens, [and] not to be known". Not to be known as a creature, no. "I wish to be through heavenly oceans that no one knowing [me], I wish to be unknown". And that is highest adab for a servant. Servant never likes to prove, to show himself or herself in Divinity.

There are, O Salafi ulamas, two kind of oceans - bahru l-wahdaniyyah and second, uqiyanus muhitatun ahadiyya. Oceans that belong to wahdaniyyah, Oneness. Oneness, that everyone appearing there, appearing. Ithbat al-wujud, everyone asking to be through that ocean with his personality. But she was saying "I don't like it. I don't like it, I like to be second oceans of wahdaniyyah". Oneness, Absolute Oneness. Not to be [seen] in my Lord's Divine Presence, not to be seen. I don't like [it].

That is maqam (station) that Salafi ulamas always objecting on it, fana-u fi'Llah, fana-u fi Rasul, fana-u fil masha'ikh. Always they are objecting on that point because they like to be seen as a creature in that ocean. They don't like to be 'nothing', but they like to be 'something'. And, Sayyidatina Maryam, Allah blessed her with such a blessing, that no one reached to that blessing. She is happy. But our ulamas always they are asking ithbat al-wujud. They like to bring themselves in existence, to be seen. But the Lord of Heavens, He never likes that. He is asking "La ilaha illa Ana". "La mawjuda illa Ana".

Therefore even an atom to be in existence, He never likes. Therefore that is a big teaching for those [among] mankind always asking to be seen in existence, to be honoured, and to be presented, or to be just rewarded. So many things they are asking to be for them. They never ask to be nothing, nasyan mansiyya. That is Kalamullah. Sayyidatina Maryam is teaching to all people [that] you must ask in Divine Presence of your Lord, not to be 'shayan madhkura' (a thing remembered). Ayatu l-karima.

That is highest grant from Lord to His creatures, “nasyan mansiyya”. Finished. A drop coming from up, reaching to ocean and becoming nasyan mansiyya. Highest adab. You are saying 'la sharika lah', O Salafi ulamas, la sharika lah, but our egos never accepting that and saying 'no', we also are here in existence. Claiming to be in existence by themselves. They are not asking to be nasyan mansiyya. And that is biggest trouble for mankind not to be in peace through their lives, here and hereafter.

Till you are going to sacrifice yourself. Sacrificing, then you are reaching real Tawhid, Oneness. We were saying, they are making me to speak to you, to our people, but this a little bit higher level but people mostly, common people like ourselves, asking to [have] proof - ithbat al-wujud. Yes, they like to show their existence, "I am here also. If you are there, I am here also". That is the source of trouble on this planet.

And the Seal of Prophets, peace be upon him therefore was saying, man istawa yawmahu fa huwa maghbun (he, whose two days are the same, is in loss). This, a big lesson for people, man istawa yawmahu fa huwa maghbun. But people, they are not looking, they are not asking what is maqsudun asli, the main target. They are thinking that if we reached today £100, tomorrow must be 200. No. Material things, it is dirtiness and most expensive is our spirituality.

And now people, all of them running after materiality, therefore their troubles never-ending here, or after this jumping-place. We are jumping. Everyone reaching, and jumping to another level. Some of them may not [be able to jump] up. May jump up, some of them [but] because they are carrying heavy burden and heavy burden is this world's aspect, and our egoistic desires, making people to fall down. Yes, a hen can't fly, falling down. But an eagle, may be its weight more than this and flying, flying, flying up.

Prepare yourself for uluwu l-manazil; try to reach higher stations higher levels - highest, highest, highest level. It is open, no [bar on it], not closed, it is open for mankind. As much as they can ask, they may get up. But mostly people, they are like a good hen. Hen, even can't walk, [let alone] to fly, can't walk. But a sparrow, phurr-rr, phurr-rr, going. Why you are carrying so much heaviness with you? What is heaviness, O Salafi ulamas? Dunya! Why carrying dunya, why you are not leaving dunya? For Mawla, leave dunya for Mawla. You may prefer either Mawla or dunya. But people now, they are preferring dunya.

Ad dunya jeefatun (this world is a carcass). They are running after it because shaytan, it is dirty and it calls people to dirtiness. Calling people, ad-dunya jeefah, carcass. Shaytan calling people to carcass. Why you are keeping dunya O muluk ul Arab? Muluk ul Islam? O Muslims why you are keeping that dirtiness? Why you are not giving that [away] for the sake of your Lord, Almighty Allah? Think on it, think on it. Before last day and last hour and last moment reaching to you. May Allah forgive us for the honour of the most honoured one, Sayyidina Muhammad, sallallahu alayhi wa sallam. Fatiha.
