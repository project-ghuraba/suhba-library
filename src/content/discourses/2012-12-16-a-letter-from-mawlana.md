Allahumma salli wa sallim 'ala Nabiyina Muhammad 'alayhi salam. Salatan tadumu wa tughda ilayh mamarra layali wa tuula dawam. Allahumma salli wa sallim 'ala Nabiyina Muhammad 'alayhi salam. Salatan tadumu wa tughda ilayh mamarra layali wa tuula dawam. Allahumma salli wa sallim 'ala Nabiyina Muhammad 'alayhi salam. Salatan tadumu wa tughda ilayh mamarra layali wa tuula dawam.

Alhamdulillah wa Shukur, that the Prophet allowing us to make salatu salam for him & to greet him. This is because His allowance making us to do this. Shukur to Allah Almighty that He granted us to get our tongues around for making salatu salam on the Prophet (sas). He granted us this breath to glorify him. There can't be anything more beautiful than this.

I am following my brother's sunnah again because we have a lot of Turkish guest & attenders that they want to understand also what we are talking. Even we are talking from, not really deep talking but it is market talking. Now again we speak Turkish & English following the sunnah of Shaykh Muhammad Effendi Hz.

Our speech is not really significant but we are just passing time here. I asked Mawlana Shaykh, I told him that we will pray dhuhr. He said "you lead the prayer". We are only leading by his order. Otherwise we have no capacity or qualification to sit in this maqam. But if there is an order, even if a rock sits here, it can do. Now I've just spoken with Mawlana Shaykh & he was telling me "Ok, go & lead the prayer & talk for the people". I say "I am not the one to lead the prayer & I am not the one to talk for people". But the order, when it is coming, the stone can make you to pray & the stone, like me, can address you. But nice stone. (laughter) Now I said to Mawlana "What should I speak to these people? I have no knowledge or irfan/awareness. He said "In this flea market, one can definitely find a jewel, or an antique somewhere. So you find something from there & speak to the people". I was telling him "Ya Sayyidi, I don't know what to speak for these people". But, he was saying, the flea market all the time have some jewelleries, have some antiques, have something. Cannot be going empty. If somebody enter the flea market, he must find something to buy.

So I am now, as a flea market, trying to say something to you. And I am apologizing if I make something messy or I make something that not really giving what you want. Now I am asking for your forgiveness through this sohbat again that sometimes I may speak nonsense things or speak things that people don't like but we may ask for forgiveness in advance just in case. They asked a man "Why did you beat this man?" He said "Yahu I beat this man but I apologized to him in advance before I beat him" So now we are going towards this direction also.

Yesterday we had a very important subject that it is called "the hope". The people, when they are losing their hope that means they are losing their strength & they are losing their, the beauty of the living. And plus they are losing the enjoyment of what Allah give them. Yesterday we had a subject, which was "the hope". I wanted to expand on it again, that when people lose the hope, they forget the beauty of this life, they forget this beauty that Allah granted. They forget the breath, this beautiful grant of Allah Almighty. And they start to not take joy from anything which is something that Allah doesn't like at all. It is for this reason that I am speaking of this hope subject again today. Because most of the people, the people of this time have everything. If they need to go somewhere they can travel a distance of 1 day in an hour, or in 10 minutes- the distance of 1 day. They travel the distance of 6 months in 2 hours. Food is more than what they expected- all kinds of food. How much do you think the people in old times used to eat or how many blessings they had? How many clothes, dresses do you think the people in old times had? How many pair of shoes do you think they had?

In the simplest example everyone today have a mobile phone. They can speak to a person in the States, or in China. Is this not a blessing? Is this not a grant of Allah Almighty? We remember that Mawlana would come to Cyprus & we were in Sham. We would receive a letter from Mawlana in every 6 months. I would go to that barbershop everyday to receive this letter. We had a barber from Urfa, I would go to him everyday because our address was his shop so I would go to him every day to see if there is mail from Mawlana. In every 6 months a letter would come & we would be so happy. And this is recent time, 30 years ago. Not so long ago, 40 years ago actually. Now we are saying about today people- what they got from Allah Almighty's gift. They have vessel, it makes them to travel one day, old days travel, in 1 hour or 10 minutes. They can travel 6 months distance with 2 hour travelling.

And old days I don't think the people have been having such an option of food or something to eat. Now everything available, everything available. You have some fruit in China, you can have it in Cyprus, you can have it in any table. China, Cyprus. You need 10 hour to travel, you have the Chinese fruit in your table. Or the American, I don't know what kind of technology, 2nd day it is in your hand or it is in your child's hand. And still the people don't appreciate what Allah Almighty give them in this time. That reminds me with very simple things that everyone have telephone in his hand & he can dial America, he can dial Russia, he can dial anywhere in the world & he can speak.

When we been kids, when our father was away on his mission, he was sending for us a letter & it was coming in 6 months. And we had barbershop & I was everyday going for that barbershop & saying "There is any letter from my father? Any letter?" They say "No, not today." The second day going, "No, letter didn't come". The second day, again.. Week after week & one day he say "Here is your letter from your father" & I feel the world is mine! I am not talking ancient history it is only maybe 35 years ago or 40 years ago. Now I become 50, that is why half century now. But it is not so far distant because now the technology is really fast.

But with this one, with this speed, we are losing the hope. We are losing the hope. That is, if anybody from the past will be living here, he will become mad from whatever Allah Almighty give & he will object in Judgement Day. He say "Ya Rabbi, You give them & they've been never thankful. And look what You give us & we been thankful". Definitely, he will be "I want my right" & make demonstration. For this reason I am saying that if a man came from the past & saw how much blessings & opportunities we are granted, he would say "O Lord, I am objecting. You grant them too much & they are not thankful. But we have not been granted much yet we were thankful. I am objecting on this point, I will take this to court". We are granted to so many blessings & opportunities. For this, the Doomsday didn't come on their heads but probably will come on ours. I think the Armageddon will not be on their head- the past peoples' head, it will be on our head because we are really, really not appreciating what Allah Almighty give us.

And this is the scary part of our life. Scary part, I am saying the scary part because Allah Almighty is As-Sabur/The Patient. He is patient, patient, patient.. But they say the patient sometimes have one moment, it was not the patient. This reminded me with very nice story. There was one man who was famous with his power & he is the real powerful man. That if he hit somebody, in one hit he kill him. And that man was having one of the people, he was teasing him all the time. Teasing him, teasing him, teasing him everyday- morning, evening, noon time, before sleep.. He was teasing him, you know, really teasing him. And it was this going for years. One day that man was sitting & that guy came &he started to tease him again. One hit! Dead. All the people "What happened, why you do this for him? He was teasing you since years & you didn't beat him & you didn't mind". "Today I was not in the mood".

What is it that I am scared of now at this point? I say "O Lord, You have the Great Name As-Sabur, You are patient with us no matter how much tasteless, bad mannered, terrible things we have, which reminds me with a story. Once upon a time there was a very tough guy in one country. He can kill a man with one Ottoman slap, such a strong guy. And there is a wicked man, always hurting him- Morning & evening, wherever he sees him, in front of the people always hurting, humiliating..So for years he continued to disturb him. One day after long years, this man was sitting somewhere. He came & started to insult him, disgrace him again in front of the people. He didn't say anything- just slapped him & he did 3 turns in the air & died. Everyone was like "Yahu, what happened, what did you do to this man? You were patient with this man for all these years!" He said, "I am not in the mood today".

We are also praying "O Lord! May You always be in the mood so that You never slap us like this!" That is why we are asking "O my Lord, O Allah! Please be in Your good tiding & be in good, the Sabr shape all the time that we will not be punished. It is very important Yahu, very important. That is why Mawlana Shaykh is advising for this Safar- the month of Safar, to say 3 times "Ashadu an la ilaha illa Allah wa ashadu anna Muhammad Rasulullah" & to make a minimum 70 Astaghfirullah al-Azim, 70 times that it will be take the bad, the cursing of this month. This is the heaviest month in the Arabic calendar. It is the heaviest month. So that is why we are asking from Allah Almighty to forgive us & if we are able to put small sadaqa just to protect us from this month & insha Allah it will be passing very smoothly & it will be passing in the good. And we are trying Ya Rabbi to say, we are trying to behave as good servant for You that You will accept us in Your Divine Presence & grant us the paradise. For this Mawlana says for this month to recite 3x Shahada & at least 70x Astaghfirullah al-Azim & to give at least a small sadaqa that they may save us from the troubles & evils of this month & make the Mercy of Allah be upon us & He accepts us in His paradise.

Wa min Allahu tawfiq, bi hurmat al Habib, bi hurmat al Fatiha.
