Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Days pass by. These days are valuable things given by Allah to people. They are the most valuable things. Because if it is gold, money or something else, you can replace it, but you cannot replace the days and time. The time that has passed has passed, it does not come back whether you give a million, a billion or a trillion. Therefore, this is a very valuable thing, but people waste and kill time. Ignorant people see it as something useless or worthless.

As we said, you cannot replace the time that has passed, but everything can be done in the sight of Allah. For the things you can’t do, you can start by making an intention for it. Then if you do it, Allah will write you the reward according to your intention, and all your time will be valuable. A person who is with Allah and follows the path of Allah Jalla Jalāluhu and our Holy Prophet, appreciates the value of his time. Otherwise, whatever you do is worthless. The two of them must be together. The love of Allah Azza wa Jalla must be in the heart.

If you have a work to be done in this world, when it is done for the sake of Allah, the time becomes valuable. Otherwise, let them sit and cry. “We didn’t do anything. We couldn’t do them”, there are many people who say so. They cry because they couldn’t do something. But they are sad and they cry for what they couldn’t do for this world. The older they get, the more their morale deteriorates and the worse they become. But as a Muslim gets older, he is more grateful to Allah. And Allah gives more mercy to those people. They say that when one reaches the age of eighty, he becomes “نمحرلا قيتع”, Allah frees him. A person who spends his whole life in the obedience of Allah, Allah sets him free. Allah Azza wa Jalla says to the angels, “Do not write down his sins.” Therefore, his time becomes valuable, not wasted.

May Allah make us give value to things that have real value. And may we not give value to these useless things such as useless tin, glass and jewelry insha’Allah. May Allah protect us. May Allah give us a good life and good days with the obedience of Allah, His love, and the love of our Holy Prophet insha’Allah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
