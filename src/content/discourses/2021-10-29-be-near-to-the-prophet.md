As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

People live in this world as if they won't die. They don't think there is another life [ākhirah]; that they will be questioned. In reality, this life is short. And afterwards, everyone will be questioned. A mindful person makes his preparations in this world. He doesn't follow the opinions of others, but follows the way of truth. If you run after everyone's opinion, you will be in loss in the next life. They will not take responsibility for you, and in the end there is nothing they can do. And they will end up in hell along with you. Whoever does kindly works in this world; who follows the right path; who holds steadfastly to the truth, will certainly have a prosperous hereafter. In this life, how many millions and billions of people have passed away and been buried? Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla appointed an end for everything in this world. And at the end of this world, all of these people come to their real lives. People can think as much as they like and say, "It will happen like this or like that."

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla created everything by HimselfNone. And HeNone put order and balance in everything. There is wisdom in everything; beauty in everything. When someone finds and understands that beauty and wisdom, he finds comfort. And if he alienates himself from it by following his ego, then he won't find any goodness. It won't be good for him. He will be at a loss. They say someone lost his life [when he dies]. That is true for many people who indeed have lost their lives. They lost their ākhirah. They lost their dunyā and they lost their ākhirah. Muslims do pass away. By passing away, they reach AllāhNone. But the rest truly lose their lives. They are lost in dunyā by losing their way and going astray. And they have lost everything in ākhirah as well.

We should be on the way of AllāhNone. We should be on the way of our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam is inviting us to goodness and showing us what to do. HeNone tells us to send ṣalawāt: when you pray, in order to draw closer to AllāhNone, make ṣalawāt on our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Especially on Fridays. It is said in the ḥadīth sharīf we recited at Jum‘ah: "All ṣalawāt are offered to meNone on Friday. I see them. Whoever among you makes more ṣalawāt, he is closer to meNone and closer to AllāhNone. His station is higher." Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam was sent as mercy to everyone. HeNone shows us every good thing by which we can profit. HeNone shows goodness. HeNone shows benefit. Shayṭān, on the other hand, shows evil, poverty and rebellion. Who follows him has lost this life and the next. Ṣalawāt on our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam is better than anything. It is useful for everything. Some people say they keep forgetting things. Make ṣalawāt; it will remove forgetfulness. Make ṣalawāt and your rizq will increase. Shifā’ and barakah will come. All kinds of good things, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla will give you with the barakah of ṣalawāt on our Holy ProphetNone. And HeNone will give whatever you ask, with permission of AllāhNone.

May AllāhNone not decrease our ṣalawāt to our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, in shā’a Llāh. People will improve with his barakah, in shā’a Llāh. Who improves will improve. Who doesn't improve, this whole earth belongs to AllāhNone. The land belongs to AllāhNone. The seas belong to AllāhNone. Everything belongs to Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. You can rebel as much as you want, eventually, everyone will be swept away. The world will become flat. There will be no mountains, no hills. And then Qiyāmah will take place. No sticks, no stones, nothing will be found. It will be flat. And on the day of judgment, people will see what they did. May AllāhNone protect us. With the barakah of ṣalawāt, in shā’a Llāh, we will be under Liwā’u l-Ḥamd. Because there will be neither shade, nor tree on that day. Completely flat. Shade will be only under Liwā’u l-Ḥamd, wherever our Holy ProphetNone is. And that is for people who make ṣalawāt, who respect and love our Holy ProphetNone. May AllāhNone make us from them. Jum‘ah mubārak! We are reaching the last days of Rabī‘u l-Awwal.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone create this human being and give them the most precious thing. HeNone honour them and give them honour to be worshipping HimNone – reward them. Everything in the world worshipping Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. But other thing[s], they will... they will not be in paradise or hell. Only this honour, AllāhNone give the human being. So, human being, it is honourable thing in between creatures. We are honourable, we must be – know this honour. And this honour give us by... to be for Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. HeNone create us from hisNone light, and who want to be more light for him, he must accept and follow Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam way, which it is honourable way for everybody. Especially for nation of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. They... Many prophets, they wish to be from hisNone Ummah. And we are from hisNone Ummah, but we are not appreciate this good thing.

Many people, they are going without any thing to do for ākhirah, only for their life. And they are thinking it is good thing to do this. And to make themself to be happy, enjoyful and to enjoy themself. But this is not what Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla ordering us. Yes, you can be happy and enjoy yourself. But you must follow also Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla order [and] Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. To follow himNone, this you will be – find good thing. If you not follow this, you will be disappointed at the end. But people these days, they are not believe in anything for after this life, or not believe even for creator. They are... they think they're clever. But the clever one, who is following order of AllāhNone and Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, and love Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. This, even you – maybe they not finish university, not finish school. You can, maybe these people they look them and they say this is idiot people; they don't know – ignorant people. But this people, they are more clever than who saying this without believing. Because they will lose their life.

There is a saying – I don't know if in English if there is. "He lose his life and died." In Turkish, they're using this: "He lose his life." This is real for people who, whole life, they was not thinking for ākhirah. Only thinking for dunyā, for enjoying themself. And they are, they will lose everything here and hereafter. Who will win – who we [will] be with AllāhNone and accept and doing good thing. And it is not difficult. But shayṭān making difficult for people, to accept this. They do everything but not accepting to say two word to save themself from hell.

And it is also, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, heNone advise us very good thing; to get more and more blessed and reward. And heNone said, especial[ly] for Friday, to be more saying ṣalāt wa-salām for himNone. HeNone said, "Who saying, you..." – ordering Ummah, "O my Ummah, nation. In Jum‘ah day make more ṣalāt wa-salām for me. Because, Jum‘ah day, Friday, their – your deed, good deed, and ṣalāt wa-salām – AllāhNone show meNone which one making more. And who make more, he will be more near for meNone ". And to be near for... from Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, it is the most acceptable, most benefit thing. Not benefit from money, not from beauty, power, anything. Not benefit. All this will go. Only what will [be] left: if you are near more to Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. To be, to make shafā'ah for you, and to be under hisNone flag Yawmu l-Qiyāmah – Day of Resurrection.

Because, this other thing, before Day of Judgement – this world, everything, there is end for everything. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone create, and HisNone order like this. In Qur’ān saying, it will be whole world, no mountain, no hill, nothing – straight [flat]. And everybody will be there for judgement. For asking for what they do. Only shadow that day, Liwā’u l-Ḥamd and throne of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Who... Which it will be for mu’min and for believer; especially who are more close for, from Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. This is very important thing, this. No anything more important than this. Because other people, no shadow, nothing at all. No tree, nothing. And it will be like burning people from heat. Only people who not feeling this, who are, they're closer to Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. And even who not respect Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam from Muslim also – they say "this is not good", "shirk" or "bid‘ah". They will be also away from Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, they will be with this people also. AllāhNone make us with Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. More closer for, from himNone, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
