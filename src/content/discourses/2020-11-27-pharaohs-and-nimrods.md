Aū‘dhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-Shay"āni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Ra!māni r-Ra!īm.

Wa#-#alātu wa s-salāmu ‘alā Rasūlinā Mu!ammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādati A#!ābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā

Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-F’āiz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Mu!ammad Nā$im al-%aqqānī. Madad. Ever since this world was founded and established there have been various types of people. Sometimes they are good people, but most of the time people are bad. Because when people follow their ego, the ego tries to takeover Allah 'azza wa jalla’s place and thinks of itself as Allah. Many Nimruds and Pharaohs have come, and many more people like them have also come and gone. Now we're in the End Times, and the worst times are coming. Those of the past tyrannized their own people, their hands reached only so far, but now they have the power to tyrannize the whole world. So they continue to tyrannize the world.

ﻣﻻﺎ[ “%attā idhā akhadhati l-ar'u zukhrufahā wa-zzayyanat wa-$anna ahluhā annahum qādirūn ‘alayhā ātāhā amrunā laylan aw nahāran fa-ja‘alnāhā!a#īdan ka-ān lam taghna bi-l-ams” (Qur’ān 10:24) “Then just as the earth looks its best, perfectly beautified, and its people think they have full control over it, there comes to it Our command by night or by day, and We mow it down as if it had never flourished yesterday!”

In fact this verse in the Holy Quran gives time to people, since they are saying, we are ruling everything, we are in control of everything. Now the pharaohs and nimrods of today have control of the whole world, and they think they can do anything they want, and they can do whatever they want. They are changing many things, and are trying to change Allah 'azza wa jalla creatures. They are trying to change the world, and they think of themselves as something special. Yet none of them has any value in the sight of Allah 'azza wa jalla. In one night, Allah 'azza wa jalla can finish all that they do, like burnt crops, as if it never existed.

The latest pharaohs and nimrods oppose the power of Allah 'azza wa jalla. Nimrod is worst than pharaoh, and his lineage still continues today. These are the people of his offspring, his kindred who still give him credit. Pharaoh is gone; they do not respect him. Those of Nimrod—these are from his progeny and lineage—but Allah 'azza wa jalla will get rid of them too, and whatever they have done will be as if it wasn’t done at all. It will remain like burnt crops. The tyranny does not continue; there is an end to the tyranny. When this latest tyranny reaches its peak, it is bound to end and disappear.

Allah 'azza wa jalla will send a holy one, the one who is mentioned by the Holy Prophet (SAW), Imam Mahdi (AS). Only then will they all be finished and cleaned. Until then, of course they try to say that we have done this and we are trying to do that, that he did this and that. This will not end; their fitna will continue. When Allah 'azza wa jalla sends Imam Mahdi (AS), he is going finish them and cleanse them away until no unbelievers will be left in this world. May Allah 'azza wa jalla make us reach those days in safety, insha'Allah. It is close now, insha'Allah. Those with patience will be in safety, and their rewards will be great, insha'Allah. May Allah strengthen our faith, and keep us safe, insha'Allah. Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-F ti ah
