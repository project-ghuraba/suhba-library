Alhamdulillah that Allah Almighty, He grant us another day. This day is wa ashraqatul ardu bi nuri Rabbiha (39:69) This planet been lightened by Allah Almighty's Nur.

There is also a Turkish group but I may say English first, then will say in Turkish insha Allah.

Today that Allah Almighty... We have to be thankful that He grant us a beautiful day. It is a day that is giving us a chance to worship Allah Almighty and it is to gain more of Allah Almighty's benefit. When you are having another day, you have to think that you get benefit from this day. That is why Rasulullah (sas) was saying, (If) everyday is equal you are losing. You have to add something in your day. And you have to give in your day. And you have to make it more for Allah Almighty's mercy - to get more from Allah Almighty's mercy and lights. Ashraqatul ardu bi nuri Rabbiha

We are happy to have this light from Allah Almighty and to have this light of Mawlana Shaykh on our head and we are asking Allah Almighty to give him longer life, and more healthy life, and Alhamdulillah, He start to give lecture. He start to give sohbah. He start to continue his duty, his wazifa. I know that everyone of us has a duty; every one of us must have his wazifa. Now we are coming to the age that we have grown up, Alhamdulillah. Grown up in the way that we have to do something. We will be responsible. We will be responsible insha'Allah for what Allah Almighty gave us and we have to act as our wazifa.

Wazifa is important for all of us. If we are murids of Mawlana Shaykh that means we start having responsibility. Start to be in our circle, everyone has his own circle. First coming his family, his friends, his community. So this circle will be bigger, and bigger, and bigger. And may Allah give us the power to make this wazifa happen. It is very important for all of us to act as Mawlana Sahykh; to act on behalf of Mawlana Shaykh and to act for the benefit of Mawlana Shaykh. This we have to understand.

And when we are under his umbrella, all the ways will open, everything will be easy-going, because, masha'Allah Mawlana Shaykh, he make everything ready for us during his 90 years. Now we don't have to do so many things. We just have to plug in because he make the building, he make the furnishing, he put the people and everything is ready. You have only to make your wazifa, so easy, so simple. He get the hard part. We are getting the easy part, he make the difficult part. Alhamdulillah that Allah Almighty granted us such a personality that he give us the good signing and the good way to behave as murid even if we don't deserve to be murid, we are playing. Mawlana Shaykh masha'Allah, he do his best for us.

That's for our English-understanding people. We have our Turkish people here visiting Mawlana, so I would like to tell them few words by your permission.ë Insha'Allah salamu alaykum. As-Salamu Alaikum. We have some English visitors here so I wanted to speak to them.

But we also have alot of Turkish visitors, murids. I am first telling them "Welcome" on behalf of Mawlana Shaykh. I know that all of you have come to visit Mawlana Shaykh. But we also are trying to fill in his place a little bit. I am not saying "we are filling" but, "we are trying to fill in his place".

Mawlana Shaykh, Alhamdulillah, served these people for 90 years. He has seen people one by one, individually in this 90 years. He has been together with people. He cared about people. Whosoever came here, saw Mawlana Shaykh no matter what. Mawlana Shaykh has seen every single person, without saying "you are small, you are big, you are important, you are not important"..And he listened to everybody; listened to peoples'troubles-material or spiritual. And he did his best for his guests, visitors, murids- supporting them materially or spiritually & taking care of them individually.

Now I am sitting here, let's not say "representing him" but I am trying to carry out some part of his mission & seeing really what kind of a heavy mission it is. And Mawlana carried it out for all these years. He put emphasis on this matter for all these years. He never said "ouf", never said "I have a headache" all these years. He worked day and night & said "I live for mankind & for serving mankind". He was never involved in any kind of trade but his trade is with Allah. Because he is trading with Allah, he never got into dunya trading because there are lies & tricks in dunya trade. He said "I am in service for Allah, for people & my life is for this". And Alhamdulillah he has been our source of pride. And Allah has granted him such a high maqam in dunya & in akhirah, Alhamdulillah.

All kinds of people have come to Mawlana, from kings to gypsies. Alhamdulillah, this means he addressed to the whole spectrum. And why is this? Because it is for Allah. For whom is Allah? For all mankind. And Mawlana said, "if I am going to work for Allah then I will work for whole mankind". He never said, "this one is like this, that one like that". No, all kinds of people have come & all have seen Mawlana.

But because today Mawlana has a different tajalli/manifestation & passed on to a different maqam, this mission somehow came down to us. And I say "welcome" to all of you, respresenting him, or on behalf of him. And all of you are free here. You may eat. You may pray. You may read Quran. You may sleep here.. All of you are Mawlana's guests. Bi hurmatil Habib, bi hurmatil Fatiha.
