As-Salāmu ‘Alaykum wa RaḥmatuLlāhi wa Barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu BiLlāhi Minash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. BismiLlāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm.

Wa ṣ-Salātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Akhirīn.

Madad yā RasūlAllāh, Madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi RasūliLlāh, Madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

Dastūr Mawlana Sheikh Abdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistāni, Sheikh Muḥammad

Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad.

1- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

ﻦﻣ ﻦﻧﻮﻜﯿﻟ ﻢﱠ ﺛ ﻢﮭﺑِ ﻮﻠﻗ ﻰﻠﻋ ﻦﱠ ﻤَﺘﺨُﯿﻟ وأ تِ ﺎﻌﻤُ ﺠُ ﻟا ﻢﮭﻋِ دو ﻦﻋ مٌ اﻮﻗأ ﻦﱠ ﯿﮭﺘﻨﯿﻟ

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “People should stop neglecting Friday prayers, or their hearts will be sealed and they will be among the heedless.”

2- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

[…] He says, at least give him a gold coin. Dinar is a little less than a whole gold coin. But our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says let him give it as a dinar of gold. Let it be his sadaqah. If he doesn’t have that, then half a gold coin. So at least – in terms of Turkish Lira: fifteen thousand. That is the dinar our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam told to give. Half a dinar is seven thousand or so. Therefore, the one who leaves Friday prayer without an excuse should definitely give that as sadaqah.

3- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

عﺎﺻ وأ ،ﻢھرد ﻒﺼﻧ وأ ﻢھرﺪﺑ قﺪﺼﺘﯿﻠﻓ ،رﺬﻋ ﺮﯿﻏ ﻦﻣ ﺔﻌﻤﺠﻟا كﺮﺗ ﻦﻣ

Our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam even made it lighter. Our Holy Prophet is saying, “The one who leaves Friday prayer without excuse should give one dirham as sadaqah.” Dirham is less than a dinar. “Or half a dirham.” The dinar is the highest. After it comes the dirham. But a dirham is definitely less than a dinar. “Or he should give half a dinar or sa’ (a measure) of wheat, in alms.” So a person who leaves Friday without an excuse should definitely and at least give sadaqah. If he has an excuse, then that’s something else. Most people don’t leave work. That is considered an excuse, but one should not leave every Friday.

4- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Whoever neglects the Friday prayer three times (in a row) without an excuse, then Allāh sets a seal upon his heart.” Some don’t give importance to Friday prayer, “I don’t go to Friday prayer.” And there are some foolish people [in non-Muslim countries] that although there are Muslims everywhere, the adhan is called for the five prayer times and everywhere is full of Muslims – There are those who say, “This is a place where non-Muslims rule,” and they don’t pray Friday prayers. They are a different kind of fools. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla will not accept their [worship]. Their hearts are sealed. May Allāh protect us.

This is not a joke. Religion is not a joke. Our Prophet says, “Go to Friday prayer. Don’t leave Friday prayers.” Even if you’re in a foreign country, there is a mosque, they pray Jum’ah. Who can reach it must pray. “This is not a Muslim country, the ruling is like this and that.” It’s not your ruling nor your business! You pray five times a day, give your zakat, go to ḥajj and do everything; then, you say there mustn’t be a Friday prayer because it is a kāfir country. Even in a kāfir country, there is Friday prayer. Allah Allah!

5- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

ﻦﯿﻘﻓﺎﻨﻤﻟا ﻦَ ﻣ ﺐَ ﺘﻛ رٍ ﺬﻋ ﺮِ ﯿﻏ ﻦﻣ تٍ ﺎﻌﻤﺟ ثَ ﻼﺛ كَ ﺮﺗ ﻦﻣ

This is a very beautiful ḥadīth of our Prophet. Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Whoever leaves three Fridays without a valid excuse is written down as one of the hypocrites.” Take this!

The Messenger of Allāh says the truth in what he said or as he said.
