As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllah, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

1 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “People should stop neglecting Friday prayers, or their hearts will be sealed and they will be among the heedless.”

2 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

[…] He says, at least give him a gold coin. Dinar is a little less than a whole gold coin. But our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam says let him give it as a dinar of gold. Let it be his sadaqah. If he doesn’t have that, then half a gold coin. So at least – in terms of Turkish Lira: fifteen thousand. That is the dinar our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam told to give. Half a dinar is seven thousand or so. Therefore, the one who leaves Friday prayer without an excuse should definitely give that as sadaqah.

3 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam even made it lighter. Our Holy Prophet is saying, “The one who leaves Friday prayer without excuse should give one dirham as sadaqah.” Dirham is less than a dinar. 

“Or half a dirham.” The dinar is the highest. After it comes the dirham. But a dirham is definitely less than a dinar. 

“Or he should give half a dinar or sa’ (a measure) of wheat, in alms.” So a person who leaves Friday without an excuse should definitely and at least give sadaqah. If he has an excuse, then that’s something else. Most people don’t leave work. That is considered an excuse, but one should not leave every Friday.

4 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Whoever neglects the Friday prayer three times (in a row) without an excuse, then Allah sets a seal upon his heart.” Some don’t give importance to Friday prayer, “I don’t go to Friday prayer.” And there are some foolish people that although there are Muslims everywhere, the adhan is called for the five prayer times and everywhere is full of Muslims – There are those who say, “This is a place where non-Muslims rule,” and they don’t pray Friday prayers. They are a different kind of fool. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla will not accept their [worship]. Their hearts are sealed. May Allah protect us.

This is not a joke. Religion is not a joke. Our Prophet says, “Go to Friday prayer. Don’t leave Friday prayers.” Even if you’re in a foreign country, there is a mosque, they pray Jumʿah. Who can reach it must pray. “This is not a Muslim country, the ruling is like this and that.” It’s not your ruling nor your business! You pray five times a day, give your zakat, go to Ḥajj and do everything; then, you say there mustn’t be a Friday prayer because it is a kāfir country. Even in a kāfir country, there is Friday prayer. Allah Allah!

5 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

This is a very beautiful ḥadīth of our Prophet. Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Whoever leaves three Fridays without a valid excuse is written down as one of the hypocrites.” Take this!

The Messenger of Allah says the truth in what he said or as he said.

Ziyadatan ila sharafi’ ‘n-Nabi, sallaLlahu `alayhi wa sallam wa Alihi wa Asahabihi l-kiram, wa ila arwahi jamee’i al Anbiya’i wa l-Mursalin wa Khudama’i sharai’ihim wa ila arwahi al ‘Aimmati al arba’a, wa ila arwahi Mashayyikhina fi t-Tariqati n-Naqshbandiyyati l-Aliyyah, khassatan Imamu t-ṭarīqah wa Ghawthu l-Khaliqah Khwaja Bahauddin Muḥammad al-Uwaisiyi l-Bukhari, Sayyidina Abdul Khaliq al Ghujduwani, Mawlana Shaykh Sharafuddin al Daghestani, Mawlana Shaykh Abdullah al Faiz al Daghestani, Mawlana Shaykh Muḥammad Nazim Adil al Haqqani, wa sai’ri Sadatina wa s-Siddiqiyun, wa man nahnu fi hadratihim wa jiwarihim, for the souls of all our past ones, for the souls of all Shaheeds. For the souls of true Muslims. May Allah guide people. This is an important lesson. Many people are confused. Shayṭān shows the falsehood as the truth. May Allah grant us to follow the truth. May we not obey the hypocrites, inshā’Allāh. Al-Fātiḥah.
