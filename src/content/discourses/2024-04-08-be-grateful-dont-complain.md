As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Wa-in ta‘uddū ni‘mata Llāhi lā tuḥṣūhā" (14:34). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘aẓīm. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says to people with these beautiful words that no matter how hard you try to count these blessings, you cannot. They used to count with paper in the old days. Nowadays, it is with a computer. Even that cannot count the blessings given by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla to people and creatures. It cannot count them at all. And they cannot pay Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla back. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is not asking for anything anyway. HeNone gave these blessings so that they stay with you. Whoever rejects these blessings is in loss himself. He will have caused himself suffering. If he doesn't accept the goodness he has been given, either goodness, or badness is the result. But bad things happen to him afterwards.

The blessings of AllāhNone, even every breath, are great. Every sip of water, every movement is a great blessing. People don't appreciate them at all. They are not grateful. They don't give thanks (make shukr). Set aside making shukr – they are rebellious and disobedient. They are challenging Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Mankind thinks a lot of themselves. They give great value to themselves and show arrogance. Arrogance is the worst characteristic. There is nothing to be arrogant about. Being arrogant in front of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is the worst thing for a person. Because people are arrogant to others, and then they are arrogant to Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. They don't appreciate HisNone blessings. Who doesn't appreciate a blessing loses that blessing.

May AllāhNone protect all of us. May we be grateful for the blessings given by AllāhNone. With shukr, the blessings HeNone gives increase. People nowadays are get used to complaining. They got used to not noticing goodness. They got used to not seeing blessings. They only think about their profits. When we say it is a blessing, they say, "No. What is it? Is it anything at all? It is nothing." They insult the blessing, and then they expect more. If you don't appreciate what is given to you, you won't be given anything. May AllāhNone protect us. They have taught the same thing, both to believers and non-believers, to Muslims and kāfirs. They are not content with anything. They don't like anything. We must like what AllāhNone gives. No matter what, even if it's a little thing, we must be grateful, as that is a big blessing. We must say we make shukr to AllāhNone. May AllāhNone make us from the grateful ones, and from those who know the value of things, in shā’a Llāh. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying in Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n: Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Wa-in ta‘uddū ni‘mata Llāhi lā tuḥṣūhā" (14:34). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘aẓīm. Meaning, what AllāhNone give you from good thing, you cannot count this. What HeNone give from HisNone treasure, from HisNone generosity, you cannot count. Before, it was counting by hand, by writing. Now, even by computer, they, you cannot count that AllāhNone give us, gave for whole creature. They are, they cannot count very precious thing, what AllāhNone gave us. We must think for this, not thinking for other thing. We are in life, we take one breath – this is big favour from AllāhNone. You take, you drink one water – also big favour from AllāhNone. You are walking, coming, going – all this HisNone favour, HisNone generosity for us. But our time people, they are not appreciate at all what AllāhNone gave. And they are not happy with what they have. Everybody complaining, not happy, and not thinking this is good thing. They thought it is normal thing. It is not normal. If AllāhNone not give you, it will be not – you cannot get anything.

They teaching people to not be happy. When, if you are accept this present from AllāhNone and thanking for HimNone, you will be happy. Even anything – small, big, little, lot – everything it will be good for you. But nowadays people, after Ottoman, they finish the good thinking, politeness. And every good thing, they are changing. And not all, it, before it was some place. Now they said it, global. For this, everywhere you go, you see people complaining, not happy, not appreciate what AllāhNone gave them. And AllāhNone saying, this is, you cannot count what HeNone gave you from good thing. And you are not thinking about this? Only thinking sometimes, little things. You swap the good thing, all what AllāhNone gave you, and just you complaining and angry with this thing.

This is not from good behave of human being. Good behave what Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam showing us. And this is... Must be for Muslim to [be] happy with what AllāhNone give him from... First thing: you are human being and you are believer, you accept AllāhNone. This is countless happiness for anyone. If they have this, no more anxious, no more unhappiness. This is one of these. The biggest one.

But AllāhNone make us to not follow our ego. Because our ego they are not satisfied, not happy with anything. And nowadays, people, they all looking for their ego, and listening, and speaking like that. But they... this is not good way for good life, happy life. More you complain, your whole life will be complain, not happy. And the end, you cannot find even good thing in your good deeds, because all this you are complaining. You are coming against what AllāhNone want, what AllāhNone happy to give you – and you are not happy. How you can be happy at the end? This is very, very bad for human being these days. AllāhNone keep us away from this habit, and reward us, and give us more from HisNone gifts, in shā’a Llāh. Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
