As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
لَا تَحْزَنْ إِنَّ اللَّهَ مَعَنَا
<br>“Lā taḥzan innAllaha maʿanā”<br>
“Do not be sad (or afraid), indeed Allah is with us.” (Qur’ān 9:40). ṢadaqAllahu l-ʿAẓīm.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla tells us in Qur’ān ʿAẓīmu sh-Sha’n about our Holy Prophet and Sayyidinā Abu Bakr. Sayyidinā Abu Bakr was in distress during the migration. When they were in the cave, and they seemed to be caught, Sayyidinā Abu Bakr became worried. Not for himself, of course, but for our Prophet.

He said, “Do not grieve, do not worry. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla is with us.” Once Allah is with a person, once the believer knows this, sadness, grief, and gloom will not affect him. Of course, these things may come, but a person should immediately turn to Allah.

This beautiful āyah and everything in Qur’ān ʿAẓīmu sh-Sha’n, from beginning to end, shows everything, good and bad. The way of our Prophet, his beautiful way, is happiness and goodness for us.

Now, because we live in the end times, there are all kinds of oppression and evil everywhere. It can affect anyone. Therefore, we need to think about this. Allah is with us. If Allah is with you, nothing can affect you.

Sayyidinā Bilal al-Habashi, even with the scorching sun, with the hot things placed on his back and without being given anything, would say, “Allah Ahad. Allah is one, Allah is one.” What he endured was nothing to him. It had no effect on him because he was with Allah.

What affected him greatly was the separation from our Holy Prophet. Because Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla granted him to be in the presence of His beloved servant. Therefore, after the passing of our Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, he could not stay in Madinah and went to Damascus. And he passed away in Damascus.

So, the things that will affect a believer in this world are the matters of faith. What remains after that is not important. May Allah protect us. We should protect our īmān, inshā’Allah.

Now, there are all kinds of oppression. If you talk about Muslims, they are oppressing people worse than the unbelievers. What unbelievers do is already obvious. What should we do? What we should do is seek refuge in Allah, be with Allah, Allah is victorious.

وَاللَّهُ غَالِبٌ عَلَىٰ أَمْرِهِ
<br>“WAllahu ghālibun ʿalā amrih”<br>
“And Allah has full power and control over His Affairs.” (Qur’ān 12:21).

Lā Ghāliba illAllah (There is no victor but Allah ). After being with Allah no one can touch you or harm you with the permission of Allah.

وَأُفَوِّضُ أَمْرِي إِلَى اللَّهِ ۚ إِنَّ اللَّهَ بَصِيرٌ بِالْعِبَادِ
<br>“Wa ufawwiḍu amrī ilAllah, innAllaha baṣīrun bi l-ʿibād”<br>
“And I entrust my affair to Allah. Indeed, Allah is Seeing of [His] servants.” (Qur’ān 40:44). 

This is what Sayyidinā Yaqūb ʿalayhi s-salām said.

Therefore, after being with Allah during these difficult times, and remembering the companions and prophets, our situation is nothing. Our Holy Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam said, “I am the Prophet who suffered the most.” He suffered for the sake of Allah and for his Ummah. Therefore, after becoming one of his Ummah, you have attained the greatest blessing.

May Allah protect us. May He not test us. Tests are not easy. Some people unknowingly desire or seek tests. “Beware. Do not ask for it,” says our Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. The mashāyikh also say so. The test is difficult. The test is not easy.

Therefore, we cannot endure the test. Ask for beneficence. There are two kinds of grace of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. There is beneficence, and there is a test. Therefore, ask for more of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla’s beneficence. May Allah grant us all from His beneficence, inshā’Allah.

For the honor of this blessed month of Ramaḍān, may Allah keep us away from evil people. May we not be tested by them. May their evil turn back on them, inshā’Allah. Because there are many evil people, many who want to torment others. Since that is the case, may their own torment turn back on them. May it reverse upon on them.

We don’t ask for anything. We ask Allah for mercy and compassion. We ask Allah for His barakah, inshā’Allah.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
