Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

A’ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm:

(Qur’ān 71:14). Allah states in the Holy Quran: ‘While He has created you in stages?’ Everything Allah has created is a witness to His Greatness and Majesty. Allah Glorious and Exalted Be He created everything and He ordered them. Ever since the beginning He created everything in stages and in different types. He created them from a dot (Nutfah), then it grows a little (clot), then it becomes a lump [of flesh], then bones and in the end it becomes a human being. Allah taught this to people by the means of our Holy Prophet and the Prophets around 1000 to 1500 years ago when nobody knew about this; nobody had an idea about how humans are created.

The whole creation of human beings shows The Greatness and Majesty of Allah. Nobody can deny this however there are plenty who do. Allah created this universe to show us His Magnificence and Greatness. We cannot question why He created it. With Allah’s Lutf (grace), He created son of man and made him honored and revered. He created everything for Mu’mins (believers) and Muslims; He actually created them for everybody but those who deny it lose. Those who see The Greatness of Allah reach felicity. Humans can think of themselves as something [important] whereas they’re not even a dot. Yes, they were created from a single dot but they’re not even a dot [in importance]. Those who don’t know their limit rebel and oppose Allah. They have no saying and no value. There are some people who go out in the streets and say: “I’m this and that”, they can be called crazies, we can’t say anything else about them.

Those who oppose Allah, no matter how much they think of themselves as something [important], sometimes people tell them: “Who are you? Who do you think you are?” Some people say: “Well I can do this and I can do that”, then someone stronger than them comes, lifts them and takes them away saying: “Who are you?” and runs off. Those who come against Allah and oppose Him are mindless and lack intellect. Those with minds and intellect recognize Allah, obey Allah and be with Allah. Those who are not with Allah are mindless and foolish. They have no mind or intellect because this universe and everything including those who rebel and oppose Allah are creatures of Allah.

People of today claim to be clever yet deny the Religion, Sharia (sacred law), Quran and Hadith brought about by our Holy Prophet while thinking that they’re clever. The smart people are astonished. Why are you doing such things? What use or benefit do you get from this? Allah is the One who gives you the sustenance, Allah is the One who created you and Allah is the One who gave you everything and you’re rebelling against Him and then claiming to be clever. There are so many Ulama (scholars) and Prophets who came before and accepted. Yet now in these end times, these mindless people are coming out and deceiving people. Those who are deceived by them are also foolish ones. The clever person is not deceived. The smart person knows the truth. Those who do not know the truth are mindless ones. May Allah protect us. We say may Allah give them intellect and common sense, we’re not saying anything else. May Allah strengthen our faith.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq. Al-Fatiha.
