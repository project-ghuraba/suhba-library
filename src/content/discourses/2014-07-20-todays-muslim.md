Shaykh Bahauddin Adil Al-Haqqani sohbat of the 20th of July, 2014.

Audhu biLlahi min as-shaytani r-rajim Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim "Fa'inna Ma'a l-'Usri Yusran." (94:5) (Verily with hardship comes ease). This is the tajalli of today. This is the manifestation of today. Subhana Allah.

It is sad to see people suffering and it is sad to see children dying. It is sad to see the drama going on all over the Arab countries or, the Muslim countries. And Allah Almighty He is saying, "Fa'inna Ma'a l-'Usri Yusran." (94:5) We hope that these days will be passing quickly, and we hope that Allah Almighty He will be generously rewarding these people that are being killed, or that are becoming shahid or that are suffering.

It is now almost end of Ramadan. And now we've been having 3 weeks of Ramadan passed. That means one quarter left, and we are happy to get into eid/feast insha'Allah. And we are hoping that with this eid Allah Almighty will take this heavy burden from the shoulder of the Muslim nations. Now, I would like to say a few things that are important. When you see somebody punishing somebody you have to think twice why? There must be a reason. There must be a reason for what is happening. This means that Allah Almighty is not happy with His Muslim servants. This is a very simple explanation. If you see the results you will say, why? There must be something wrong in us.

First we have to look for ourselves. Are we good Muslims? Are we good believers? Are we good lovers to Rasulullah? Or we are proud people, we say we are Muslims and everything is for us? We have to look it is so simple. There is a story, it is full of wisdom. One man... Some people they've been passing and they see one man tied on a big rock. And one young man he was hitting him with a stick. And the people they say "what are you doing, you're hitting this old man with a stick?" And the old man shouting to these people interfering. He said, "leave him because I was hitting my father in the same place in his age. And Allah Almighty He is punishing me with my son because I hit my father with the stick. Now, we have to look for the wisdom. We are here, a small amount of people, we are asking Allah Almighty that to love Rasulullah to obey Rasulullah, to respect Rasulullah and to be in the road of Rasulullah. And to sacrifice ourselves for Rasulullah. And to melt with our love for Rasulullah. What is happening now?

They have a new generation of religion, they have a new ideas of Islamic religion. They say: "Rasulullah he passed. Finished. With due respect we have our own rules. We modified the rules and we are now either wahabi, or salafi or whatever." I don't know what to name it. So many waves of new generations of religions. And now you see the result where we've been and where we are coming. If you are far away from Rasulullah, you are far away from Allah. This is very simple. Very, very, very simple. Because when you look for the history - it is important to know the history - when the Arab people they ran away from Islam they become communist. The Arab world. What I am talking about the 50s, 60s, 70s, 80s, you have to see until the 80s, it was totally communist ruling for Arab countries. Some of them they've been...The rich ones they were following the Americans. The poor ones they were following the Russians in those days, it was fashion.

Now everything has been changed. Rules have changed. Everything changed, it's upside down. They say: "We are Muslims." Alhamdulillah that's good. But what kind of Muslim are you? You are weird. You are weird. You can judge to kill anybody just because he is I don't know what and what he is thinking. Yahoo! If it is so simple, so all of us, we should be dead. We don't have any right to live. And see he is saying "La Ilaha illa Allah." The other one is saying "La Ilaha illa Allah." He is saying "Allahu Akbar!" The other one is saying "Allahu Akbar" and bombing each other! Yahoo! This one he is saying "La Ilaha illa Allah" he is same as you. This one is saying "Allahu Akbar!" He believes as you. It is egoistic desires and egoistic ruling! Nothing else.

If somebody is doing this that's why Mawlana Sheikh, he was advising - "Today it's fitna day. You have to keep yourself in your house. Keep yourself with your family. Keep yourself with the people that you love. No need to interfere with anything. It is fitna time." This is very simple advise for today manifestation. And worse days are coming. And you have to know this. It will not go for better, it will go for worse. So many changes, so many changes. Allah Almighty He said "don't throw yourself in danger." If you see danger coming on you, and you can solve it with talking, with politics, with things that...you have to solve it. It is not right to be ignorant and to sacrifice children, to sacrifice I don't know what. To sacrifice your house, your building, your life, other people's life. It's not only you. And Allah Almighty "if you are in danger, you have to think wisely and you have to get on wisely." If you are powerful it is different. If you are not powerful it is different thinking.

This is Allah Almighty He made everything clear, very clear. Everything very clear. It is not the matter to take the knife and fight with a tank. Who will win? Tell me who will win? Knife and tank? I go further. Knife and pistol. Who will win? This is...so you put all the nation in danger for stupid desires. May Allah help them. May Allah make them winning. We don't say that we are happy with what's happening. Feeling sorry for what is happening. We have to comment because if we close our mouths, Allah Almighty also will say why you didn't speak? Why you didn't give advice? Why you didn't say something? If they listen, they are welcome. If they don't listen it's up to them. We just give our comment and we say what is necessary to be said.

Now it is very heavy days. And our duty in those days, is to be in our houses. Don't stay late outside your house. If you finish, you just come home. And you say "Hasbun Allah wa ni'mal wakil" especially in the hot areas. In hot places it is, to say "Hasbun Allah wa ni'mal wakil" this is one of the best zikrs that Allah Almighty He gave for His servants. And this insha'Allah protects you from anything and you make rabita with our Sheikh, you make rabita with Rasulullah, you make rabita with Allah Almighty. And you make dua "Ya Rabbi take all this burden from us and make the beloved one S. Mahdi (as) to approach and to finish all this tyrants and to finish all this nonsense. And to finish all this bad, bad, bad days insha'Allah."

May Allah forgive us and may Allah forgive you. And may Allah make it easy for these people who are suffering. It is not easy in this Ramadan. It is Ramadan itself it's heavy. Plus with such burden coming on them. May Allah make it easy and may Allah reward them and reward us.

Bi hurmati l-Habib, bi hurmati l-Fatiha.
