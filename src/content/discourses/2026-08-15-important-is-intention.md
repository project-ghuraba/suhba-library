As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
إِنَّمَا هَٰذِهِ الْحَيَاةُ الدُّنْيَا مَتَاعٌ
<br>“Innamā hādhihī l-ḥayātu d-dunyā matāʿun”<br>
“Truly, this life of the world is nothing but a (quick passing) enjoyment.” (Qurʼān 40:39). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says, dunyā is nothing; it’s useless. If you misuse this useless thing, it won’t benefit you. However, if you use it for the sake of Allah, then it attains value. It goes from being worthless to being valuable.

Therefore, everything must be done for Allah’s pleasure. Intention is very important. The first ḥadīth we learned of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam is,<br>
إِنَّمَا الأَعْمَالُ بِالنِّيَّاتِ وَإِنَّمَا لِكُلِّ امْرِئٍ مَا نَوَى
<br>“Innamā al-aʿmālu bi-al-niyyāti wa-innamā likulli imri'in mā nawā”<br>
“Actions are but by intentions, and each person will have but that which he intended.”

All of a person’s actions depend on their intentions. If the intention is good, the outcome will be good. If the intention is bad, then it will be bad from the beginning.

Therefore, whatever you do—when you set out on a journey, study, or travel—let your intention always be for the pleasure of Allah. If you do this, you will gain both this world and the hereafter. Otherwise, if your intention is evil and you think, “How can I deceive people, how can I gain advantage for myself?” then don’t strive in vain. Abandon that endeavor from the start. Because neither you nor anyone else will benefit from it.

Therefore, turning worldly life into good is in one’s own hands. If a person wants to, he can choose not to do evil, or he can do good. Therefore, let our intention always be for the sake of Allah and to do good. Let our intention be, inshā’Allāh, to be a servant as Allah desires and commands. That intention will benefit you in this world, hereafter and forever. Otherwise, you will see no benefit.

There are those who think they are very clever; they think, “I will do this, I will do that, I will deceive people.” But then they themselves suffer the greatest deception.

May Allah protect us. May Allah protect us all from the evil of our egos. Those who do not do their work for the sake of Allah are always in loss. Shukr to Allah, our intention is to please Allah, alḥamdulillāh. This is the intention of the people of ṭarīqah. This is what we have been taught. The people of ṭarīqah follow the way of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam.

Others, however, abandon Allah and seek benefit and advantage from people; this is of no use. May Allah protect us. May Allah keep us steadfast on the right way, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
