As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
وَإِذَا الْبِحَارُ فُجِّرَتْ
<br>“Wa idhā l-biḥāru fujjirat”<br>
“And when the seas are burst forth.” (Qurʼān 82:3). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla is telling us about the Day of Judgment. People think that everything will continue forever, that nothing will happen. But the Day of Judgment is coming; Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says that the sky will split open, the stars will scatter, and the seas will burst and overflow.

Years ago, we went to Italy, where a volcano erupted. The huge mountain was practically destroyed. They say, “It’s nothing. The real issue is this sea.” The scientists are also saying so. There is a huge volcano inside it. If it erupts, the world will be destroyed; nothing will remain. They are afraid of these things. There is nothing to fear. Everything is waiting for Allah’s command; nothing will happen until Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla’s command comes. It will happen when the time comes.

Qur’ān ʿAẓīmu sh-Sha’n also states this: the seas will burst open. The Day of Judgment will come, nothing will remain. There will be no mountaintop left; everything will be completely flat.These are things that will happen.

Although Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla informed about these things, and although they know this, only some come to īmān; most don’t care. They dismiss it, saying, “This is a natural event, we cannot do anything about it.” They have no fear of Allah; they only say things such as, “A rock from the sky will strike earth.”

Some Muslims who believe this also have weak īmān. Īmān is believing that these things will happen when the time comes, and that whatever Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla has said will be fulfilled.

Therefore, look at your own situation. Without looking at others, without fearing anything; what Allah says will happen, there’s no need for anything. What difference does it make whether you fear or not? If you are going to fear, fear Allah, and nothing else. May Allah protect us and our īmān. That is what is important, nothing else.

Whether a meteorite strikes, a volcano erupts, or an earthquake occurs - all of these happen only by the command of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. This is what needs to be known. This is what īmān is.

Īmān is believing that everything is under the command of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. Otherwise, they will play with you like a toy. A report will come out saying, “This happened, this will happen,” and people will immediately believe it, but they will not believe in the blessed words of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla.

May Allah improve them and grant us all īmān, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
