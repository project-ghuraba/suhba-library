As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa raḥmatu Llāhi wa barakātuhu.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Wa ṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīn wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistāni, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī, madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

ProphetNone saying: believer, mu’mīn, they are like one body. When one part of body have problem, have pain, whole body they are same feeling pain. We are as much you can – maybe you are not so tall small you have pain you feel in your whole body. But if you are also big tall and wide this also if you have pain, anybody from your anywhere in your body you feel pain. Mu’mīn, Muslim they are like this. When something happen somewhere or for some mu’mīn, they must be feeling with them. And when somebody want to make good thing for mu’mīn for Muslim, for our brothers, we are happy with him same.

Because this time shayṭān he is controlling everything. Muslim or non-Muslim, believer or non-believer, all controlled by some shayṭān same iblīs. So they make very bad thing for human being. When you something wrong we must, especially what harming human being, we must as much we can to prevent this. To where it is or for whom they make, we are must doing this because you cannot say this is not my son, this is not my daughter, this is from other people – no need to interfere. If you can interfere, you must. But if you cannot [then] by your tongue you must say, "Look, don't do this." But even you are afraid by your action – by your heart you must not accept this. Everything harming human being it is ḥarām.

Nothing no excuse for you – "I'm not feeling well, I can do this." No you cannot do this. Especially too, if you have something like this and you are, you cannot do anything – you must be hiding this. Don't say, "I can make it here openly," because when you... And more than this, if you have bad habit and you are teaching to other people, this is the worst. Or you can, you think you will get benefit from this thing, it is poison for you. This is ḥarām money and ḥarām money everything it is poison – for your body, for your soul, for your children, for your ancestor. Don't say, "This is everybody doing this."

AllāhNone said, "Wa idhā..." ProphetNone, "Wa-idha btulītum bil-ma‘āṣī fāstatirū." If you make bad thing and you cannot manage, you hide it. Don't show people, don't make it for other people to be look like normal. It is not normal. We not accept anything doing against order of AllāhNone and order of ProphetNone. Who come to ṭarīqah must be you try to clear themself from bad habit, from sins. But if you cannot, slowly slowly they can but not openly and try to make for other people to teach them bad habit. It is, we cannot accept this. You mustn't accept also. And say ego ego. We must not follow ego, we must make ego to follow us.

AllāhNone save human being from this people. Especially youngster because they are easily they can follow this. After they cannot get rid of it. And who be teaching this, they will be AllāhNone punishment them for each one same. Not only one. If he make ten people to go bad way he will punish for ten people, hundred people he will [be] punish [for] hundred people. AllāhNone save us from this people, keep them away from us, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone send good people and save this people in īmān and happiness, in shā’a Llāh. Because happiness with ḥalāl not with poison. This poison it is no benefit at all.

Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
