As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Wa-mā taf‘alū min khayrin fa-inna Llāha bihī ‘alīm" (2:215). Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is saying, whatever goodness you do AllāhNone is aware of it. It is important that AllāhNone knows, and it is not important if other people know it or not. But in order to show off, mankind does many things for the sake of appearances. Many people’s intention is to do something so people will know who did it. They build schools and other things and give their names to them. It is not for the pleasure of AllāhNone then. Their intention is for people to know them. When they do something for their names to live on, then their intention is not for a good purpose, but for their egos. They do it for themselves and their pride.

Because when something is done for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, whether it is destroyed or not in the end, AllāhNone accepts that goodness until qiyāmah. Because the intention of that person is for people to use those good things and to take a share of the pleasure of AllāhNone through other people using it. Muslims used to do like this in old times. But nowadays, most do it in order to avoid paying taxes. They avoid paying taxes and give their names to what they do, as if they are the owners of a charity. It is not charity. They do it in order to satisfy their egos as if they are doing something good. AllāhNone doesn’t wish goodness to those people. The people HeNone wishes goodness to are those who give charity for the pleasure of AllāhNone, whose intention is for AllāhNone.

Such people can do whatever they want. Even if they plant one tree and people or animals eat from it or rest under its shade, benefitting from it in some way, it will be good for them. Their intention is good then. AllāhNone gives them their reward when it is not done for showing off, or for bragging about who did it. Certainly, if someone gives his name or the name of his parents to some goodness he has done, yet his intention is the pleasure of AllāhNone and for people to pray for them, it is accepted. It is okay. But things done without remembering Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla at all are useless. They are done for nothing.

May AllāhNone let people benefit from the goodness they do. Because as we said, they may do it, but it has greater benefits, spiritual benefits in the presence of AllāhNone which are much more numerous. May people think about this, in shā’a Llāh. May things not be done in vain, in shā’a Llāh.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying: Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Wa-mā taf‘alū min khayrin fa-inna Llāha bihī ‘alīm” (2:215). Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying, what you done from good thing, AllāhNone, HeNone is knowing. AllāhNone knowing this. This is, people, they must know this. Not forget Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Doing something, you thought... Many people, they thought there is nothing, only themselves. [E]very, even one drop water you give, AllāhNone know what you do. Big, small, everything, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla knowing and giving, rewarding, according to HisNone mercy and to HisNone generous. But, many people doing things, and they thought just for this benefit of themselves to be shown, to be known, “This one, he make this thing.” His name – they put their name, school or other thing. Many thing can do and they can put their name. This is, many people, they do this only for show, to be known, “This man, he done this.” He thought it will be forever. But forever, it is, which – It can be with remembering of AllāhNone and intention to have his reward from AllāhNone.

[When] you do thing, don’t say, “I will show this for people.” Even one tree if you plant, and animal or people eating from this, or, even without fruit, they can sit under with the shade, and also AllāhNone reward for this. No need to say, “I am this, I am that.” And this, you make charity, you make mosque, you make house for people, for ill people, for hospital or other thing, or water – for all this, AllāhNone reward you. And what you intention: “This for AllāhNone, I make this for AllāhNone for until qiyāmah,” it is, in shā’a Llāh, for this. And even this not going so long, but AllāhNone still rewarding you. Not cutting HisNone reward from you because this finish. Because, your intention, make this for until qiyāmah, for bless and for pleasure of AllāhNone and help for people. This, AllāhNone reward for this.

But other people, they only... Many people we know, especially, everywhere, they say, they show they were very proud. “I make this hospital, I make this university, I make this, that.” And they put their name only for to be shown, not remembering AllāhNone. All this, no benefit for them. Even in dunyā, no benefit for them, because it will be useless. They will be – they will die, and after this, no. But, if they want forever, they must make intention for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, for sake of AllāhNone and for his reward from AllāhNone. Not from people. AllāhNone knows everything. “Wa-mā taf‘alū min khayrin fa-inna Llāha bihī ‘alīm” (2:215). HeNone know your intention for yourself or only for showing. For [according to] this, HeNone can reward you. So, AllāhNone make us, our intention whole time, everything for HisNone sake, for HisNone riḍā (pleasure), in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
