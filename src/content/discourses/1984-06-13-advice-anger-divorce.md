Yes, was saying Grandshaykh that everyday during 24 hours, must look a grandshaykh to his mureeds, even east or west they may be. Three times, look like this, 'nazar' looking. Looking heart-eyes, nazar looking. And he was saying "Oh, Nazim Effendi! When Grandshaykh looks to his mureeds he is not going to give them candy" but coming to him, on that mureed, coming a unliked thing. Standing on him. And even it is going to be in touch with unliked thing, how he is? Firm? Or jumping on it or escaping? Or firmly keeping his position. When this arriving, is he leaving his faith or keeping firmly?

Therefore, when coming something during our life something, unliked things, to try our faith. Trustworthy or not yet? Understanding? For that reason when you are seeing a person, coming on you - you don't like that person; his speaking, his actions towards you. And like such a stomach if no good food coming in it, asking to get it out. And you also, when you are looking at something that is no good for you you are going to be angry and getting it out. From stomach, you are taking away the food that is in it, but when you are angry, you are taking away your faith. It is not like food in your stomach therefore it is dangerous.

When you are coming in contact with a unliked thing you must be firm. Not leaving to get out your faith by angering, by anger. You must keep. You must keep. A person comes to you and makes you, gives you trouble, gives you suffering. You don't like and you are getting to be angry. Don't get angry, keep yourself. Keep anger prisoned in yourself.

They are looking at that time. Looks Grandshaykh. He Looks and say his report to Prophet, "He is alright". If in 40 days, every day’s report going to Prophet: "He is alright," then, they are wearing to you a new dressing and calling you to Assembly of Awliyas. Were saying Grandshaykhs, "You may surrender, you may say 'O my Lord, I am weak, You know my weakness' ". Then Allah Almighty 'yatawalla as-saliheen' (is an Ally to the righteous).

On behalf of you, comes Divinely defeating. What saying Allah? Astaidhu billah:

inna l-laha yudāfiʿuʿani alladhīna āmanū Truly, Allah defends those who have believed (22:38).

Allah Almighty defending His believers. Anger changing in hearts, worst, worst illness. Therefore it is prohibited. But, you may shout at children for 'tarbiyah,' training but don't leave anger always through your heart. That is damaging your heart and destroying your faith. From here you may say for children, for praying. Doesn't matter. Marriage must be an agreement from both sides. When I am giving my hand and he gives his hand that is going to be marriage, full. When he says "I am taking my hand away" and my hand is alone, there is no marriage, finished! If a lady, if a wife saying to her husband "I am asking for divorce because I am not agree with you," that, in Shari'a, finished! He must give divorce to her.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq bi hurmat Al Fatiha
