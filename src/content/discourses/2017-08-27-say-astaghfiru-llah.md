As-salāmu ‘alaykum wa raḥmatu Llāhi wa barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīn wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Faqultu staghfirū Rabbakum innahu kāna ghaffāran." (71:10) "Yursili s-samā’a ‘alaykum midrāra." (71:11) Ask forgiveness from AllāhNone, says Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla in Qur’ān. If you are in trouble, it will remove your trouble. It will open the blessings of heavens. It will make the earth blessed for you. Repentance is a big blessing. And AllāhNone is showing that way.

People always say they have troubles. A person who is always in trouble should say astaghfiru Llāh 70 times, 100 times a day or even more. Our ProphetNone used to make istighfār 70 times a day. The ṣaḥābah said, "You don't have sins!" "I want to be a servant grateful to AllāhNone," said our ProphetNone. Be there problems or not, to make istighfār is a big blessing. It releases all types of troubles from people. For the sick, it will be a cure. If someone is in need, he will be increased. If no rizq, rizq will open. And the sins you carry will be forgiven. Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla is saying, "INone will forgive the one who repents." Who commits a sin then repents is loved by Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla. Who doesn't persist but repents and asks forgiveness, Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla will be happy, says our ProphetNone. Just like someone is happy when he loses something precious then finds it, Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla is happy with HisNone servant when he repents. It is a big blessing. It shouldn't be taken lightly.

Every day on our tongues, tawbah astaghfiru Llāh, astaghfiru Llāh, astaghfiru Llāh; 70 times, 100 times every time. If you recite 100 times, the more you recite, the more benefit it is. May AllāhNone accept our repentance, in shā’a Llāh. May HeNone forgive us.

Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla saying in Qur’ān many places, but one place, "Istaghfirū Rabbakum innahu kāna ghaffārā. Yursili s-samā’a ‘alaykum midrārā. Wa-yumdidkum bi-amwālin wa-banīna wa-yaj‘al lakum jannāti wa-yaj‘al lakum anhārā." (71:10-12) To make, ask for forgiveness. Forgiveness, astaghfiru Llāh in Arabic. Say astaghfiru Llāh, astaghfiru Llāh. You may say it 70 times at least because ProphetNone he was saying, "I'm saying 70 times astaghfiru Llāh a day." Ṣaḥābah was astonishing, "Why you are? You don't have any sin, you don't have any wrong thing. Why you are saying this?" They said for ProphetNone. HeNone said, "I like to be thankful servant for my Lord, Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla. Even I don't have sin, this is also like thanking for AllāhNone to say astaghfiru Llāh."

And it is what Allāh HeNone say in Qur’ān. If you have any problem, make, ask for forgiveness, say astaghfiru Llāh. When you ask this HeNone said, HeNone send for you rain, HeNone send for you barakah, HeNone send for you children, HeNone send for you money, treasure, every good thing. It is door for, key for opening door of every good thing, to ask forgiveness, to say astaghfiru Llāh. Now you may say it 70 times, hundred times, more. When you are in difficulty say astaghfiru Llāh, astaghfiru Llāhi l-‘aẓīm.

And it is for Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla to be happy with us, saying astaghfiru Llāh. Allāh HeNone is happy with his servant who doing sin and after asking forgiveness. HeNone forgiving and HeNone is very happy. As somebody lose something very precious and when he find how he will be very happy - Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla every time when HisNone servant asking forgiveness from HimNone HeNone is happy like this. So it is very small word but it is treasure, precious thing. Don't think it is nothing. When you are believe in your Lord HeNone will not even one atom not leave without reward for you. So it is big favour for who are believer. Everything they do they will be reward, in shā’a Llāh. And is open also difficulty in your life. When you say, some people they say magic, they thought magic. It's not magic. It is true.

Say astaghfiru Llāh, astaghfiru Llāh, astaghfiru Llāh, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, in shā’a Llāh, it will be open what you have difficulty and you will be happy, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone forgive us, all of us, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq al-Fātiḥah.
