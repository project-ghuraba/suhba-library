A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Alā inna awliyā’a Llāhi lā khawfun ‘alayhim wa-lā hum yaḥzanūn" (10:62). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘Aẓīm. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says: The awliyā’ of AllāhNone, the beloved servants of AllāhNone, have no fear, no sadness. They are with Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Those who are with AllāhNone have no worries about anything. We must come to know Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. How can we know HimNone?

After creating us and bringing us into being from nothing, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla showed this kindness to us, letting us know HimNone and allowing us to walk HisNone path. This is the best thing. There is nothing more precious than this. This is the most precious thing. A person walking HisNone path, who is with HimNone, who is with HisNone beloved ones, attains all kinds of goodness. Because thousands, millions, and billions of people have come and gone. You came here, and you too will go. Who should you be with?

Be with HisNone awliyā’, with HisNone beloved ones. There is no sadness, no fear for them, says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. So, you come to visit Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim and then leave. You can also perform ziyārah from a distance. When you read and recite for all awliyā’, prophets, and ṣaḥābah, you become spiritually united with them. It is as if you visit them at their maqāms. Some of their maqāms are known. And there are many awliyā’u Llāh, beloved servants of AllāhNone, whose maqāms are unknown. Their himmah (spiritual energy) will be with you.

Therefore, this is the best thing for people. Wealth, property, houses, parks, and material possessions are of no benefit to people if they are useless for the ākhirah. But if they are for the ākhirah, all kinds of goodness will be attained. All kinds of beautiful things will be attained. We must know their value. We must give thanks (shukr) to AllāhNone so that HeNone grants more, for the blessing of knowing and being with them. AllāhNone honours those who recognize the awliyā’, who truly know the awliyā’, and who are with them.

Those who are blessed with this goodness, who are blessed with this beauty, are fortunate people. Because they have many enemies. When you go to a good servant, or a good person, a thousand shayṭāns will show up and say, "What is the need for this? What are you doing? Why are you doing it?" This is for Muslims. And for non-Muslims, there is no end to it. You must protect yourself. Don’t listen to anyone. Don’t let anyone lead you off the path. This path is the beautiful path. This path is the right path. As we said, there aren’t just a thousand, but a hundred thousand shayṭāns in this world. They may immediately trip you up, and even if you don’t go to hell with them, you will end up far away. You will be far from the awliyā’ and the beloved servants of AllāhNone. Being with them is salvation. Being with them is salvation and goodness. Being with them in ākhirah is the greatest wish for mankind and believers. May AllāhNone not separate us from them. May we always attain their himmah, in shā’a Llāh.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Alā inna awliyā’a Llāhi lā khawfun ‘alayhim wa-lā hum yaḥzanūn" (10:62). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘Aẓīm. AllāhNone saying, "The beloved one for AllāhNone, there is no fear for them, no sadness." This is what Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone said, promised us. We are in HisNone promise. Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
