It will be an opening, we ask Allah (swt) that this evening is the beginning of Rajab Al Murajjab which is known to be the Month of Allah (swt). "Shaaban is my (saws) month and Ramadan is the month of my (sas) nation." We ask from Allah (swt) that the beginning of this month is dressed with glory, so the hearts of the tyrants may be shaken and that they may be defeated - they may be defeated with no chance to escape.

O Your Highness Prince, may Allah (swt) support you. Welcome to you... We ask Allah (swt) that our days may be happy & our end may be good and all the royal family insha'Allah may be in service to your Great Grandfather, the Master of the beginning & the end (sas).

May Allah (swt) support His Royal Highness the King. We ask Allah (swt) that he may carry the regiment of Sayyidina Muhammad (saws), the regiment of Mahdi (as) & the regiment of his Sultanate, and he shall rule insha'Allah!

My heart is happy, with Allah's (swt) permission. Welcome to you. From the Beloved of Allah (saws), may your light and joy increase here & in the Hereafter. May your honour & glory increase here & in the Hereafter. That you may be happy with your children.

Bi Hurmat Al Habib Bi Hurmat Al Fatiha. Madad Ya Sultan Al Awliya' Madad Ya Sultan Al Awliya'... Let them be a sword for the Prophet (saws)... he & his brother, His Royal Highness the King.

Fatiha.
