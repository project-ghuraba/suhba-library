As-Salāmu ‘Alaykum wa RaḥmatuLlāhi wa Barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu BiLlāhi Minash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. BismiLlāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm.

Wa ṣ-Salātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Akhirīn.

Madad yā RasūlAllāh, Madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi RasūliLlāh, Madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

Dastūr Mawlana Sheikh Abdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistāni, Sheikh Muḥammad

Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad.

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Indeed, there is a door in Paradise called Duha.” The door of Duha refers to the Duha prayer. So this door is called Duha for the prayer. “On the Day of Judgment, a caller will proclaim, ‘Where are those who regularly performed the Duha prayer? This is your gate; enter through it with the mercy of Allāh.” The Duha prayer, which can be performed as two, four, six, eight, or twelve rak’ahs. But even two rak’ahs is sufficient even for entering through this gate.

2- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

َ ْ َ َ ﱠ َ ﱠ ُ َ ْ ُ َ ٌ ﱠ َ ﱣ َ ْ ْ َ َ ُ 6 ْ َ َ ْ َ َ

ﲔ ﻘ ة

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Two rakat prayers performed in the time of Duha has the reward of two Hajj and one Umrah in sight of Allah.” The Duha prayer is performed as we said, at least two rak’ats; and more can be prayed.

3- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

ءﺎﺷ  ،ﺔﻜﺋﻼا ةﻼﺻ ﺗ: لﺎ ،ا ﺔﺤ ﱴﻣأ  ﺐﺘﻜﻳ نأ ﰉر ﺄﺳ

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “I asked my Lord to ordain the Duha prayer for my Ummah.” Our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam wanted it to be obligatory. “My Lord Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla said, “This is the prayer of the angels. Whoever wishes to pray it may do so, and whoever wishes to omit it may do so.” So it is not obligatory, but rather the prayer of the angels. Whoever wants to pray it may do so, and whoever doesn’t want to may do so. “Whoever prays it should not pray it before the sun rises high.” The Duha time, which is about an hour or an hour and a half after Ishrāq (sunrise), until the sun has risen high. The Ishrāq prayer is 20 minutes to half an hour after sunrise. Duha is prayed after Ishrāq time.

4- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

او ﺎھﺿو ﺲﻤاو ﲈﺗرﻮ ا ﱴﻌﻛر اﺻ

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Pray the two rak’ahs of the Duha prayer with their respective surahs, namely Surat Ash-Shams and Ad-Duha.” “Wa-sh-shamsu wa-duha’ha.” “Wa shams” means the time when the sun rises. Their names are mentioned. That is what Duha means.

5- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Awwābīn” those who pray the Sunnah of Awwābīn. “Meaning those who turn to Allāh, the Duha prayer is when the feet of baby camels burn from the heat.” So, Awwābīn prayer are the same. Awwābīn means those who turn to Allāh.

6- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “The Duha prayer is Awwābīn prayer, those who turn to Allāh.” That is, it is the prayer of those who are true to Allāh in everything.

7- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

َ ﱡ 6 َ َ َ َ ُ ْ ُ َ ٌ َ َ ﱢ

ا ﺎﺘﻌﻛر ﻛ ذ ِ ئﺰﺠﻳو ﺔﻗﺪﺻ مﻮﻳ ﰱ مدآ ا  ﱉﻼﺳ 

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Every day, the son of Adam has a charity to give for each of his joints.” Humans have about 360 joints, and a Muslim should give a charity for each of them. If he can give, he gives; if he cannot, “Two rak’ahs of Duha prayer will suffice for all of them.” Two rak’ahs will replace all the charity he would give. Because it doesn’t occur to a person to give charity one by one every day, but the Duha prayer will suffice for all of them.

8- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet is saying “Perform and keep on the two rak’ahs of Duha prayer, for there is much reward in them.” That is, there is reward and Allāh accepts one’s wishes.

9- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

ُ َ ْ ْ َ َ

هﺮﺧآ ﻚﻔﻛأ رﺎﻟا لوأ  تﺎﻌﻛر ﻊﺑرأ ﻦﻋ ﺰﺠ ﻻ مدآ ا ﺎﻳ

Our Holy Prophet is saying “Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says: O son of Adam, do not neglect four rak’ahs at the beginning of the day, for I will suffice you at the end of it.” This means, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says, “I will protect you from afflictions.” Therefore, both the Fajr prayer and the Duha prayer protect people. They become under the protection of Allāh.

10- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

ﺎﲠ اوﺆﺗ و ا ةﻼ تأو ﲂﯿ ﺐﺘﻜﻳ و ﺿﻷا  ﺐﺘﻛ

Our Holy Prophet is saying “Sacrificing an animal was made obligatory for me,” meaning it is not obligatory for us, but rather a wājib. Some things were obligatory for our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam and become wajib or a Sunnah Mu’akkadah for us. “But He did not make it obligatory for you. I was commanded to perform the Duha prayer, but you were not commanded to perform it.” So it is like an obligation for the Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. For us, whoever can pray it, whoever can not pray it. But sacrifice is wājib for the one who affords it.

11- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

ر ﺎا  ةءاﺮﺑ  ﷲا ﺐﺘﻛ ﺎﻣﺮ ﻻﻮﺣ ا ﺔﺤ ﺢ 

Our Holy Prophet is saying “Whoever performs the Duha prayer continuously on time for a year, Allāh will grant him salvation from Hellfire.” He will write for him salvation from Hellfire. This applies to the person who performs it for a year.

12- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet is saying “Whoever performs the two rak’ahs of the Duha prayer, his sins will be forgiven, even if they are as numerous as the foam of the sea.” So, the sea is not much more than foam; even if a person’s sins were so much, Allāh would still forgive them.

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

ﱠَ ْ ٌ ْ َ َ ُ َ َ ُ ً َ ْ َ َ ُ ْ َ ْ َ َ ً َ ْ َ َ ﱡ 6 ﱠ َ ْ َ

Our Holy Prophet is saying “Whoever performs the Duha prayer as four rak’ahs and four rak’ahs before Dhuhr prayer, a house is built for him in Paradise.” Duha is at least two rak’ahs, but this applies if one performs four, and also the Sunnah before Dhuhr prayer which is definitely a Sunnah Mu’akkadah, those four rak’ahs. Some of these new type of people don’t perform Sunnah prayers, but even though they’ve heard and seen how virtuous these Sunnah prayers are, there are some who claim to be Muslims say, “Don’t pray them.”

14- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet is saying “Whoever performs the Duha prayer as 12 rakats, Allāh will build him a house of gold in Paradise.”

15- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

ﲔﺑاوﻷا ةﻼﺻ ﱓ و ، باوأ ﻻإ ا ةِﻼﺻ  

Our Holy Prophet is saying “Only the Awwāb, the one who turns to Allāh keeps on the Duha prayer.” So it is the prayer of the one who turns to Allāh. “For Duha prayer is the prayer of those who turn to Allāh.”

The Messenger of Allāh says the truth in what he said or as he said.
