As-salāmu ‘alaykum wa raḥmatu Llāhi wa barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīn wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

Mankind, AllāhNone has shown the right way to mankind. And mankind is turning to different directions. The truth is AllāhNone. Other than HimNone is false, fake and imagination. Shayṭān is hiding the truth from people. He is showing the false as true. And they believe him. They waste their lives for nothing. They run after false imagination. At the end, they find sorrow. It has been so since Ādam ‘alayhi s-salām. Most people are far from truth. They are far from reality. They always assume false imagination is truth and suffer for no reason. They expect some benefit. There is no benefit, no benefit for them. They are worshipping idols. They are worshipping pictures. They follow this and that. None of it is useful.

As Sayyidinā Ibrāhīm ‘alayhi s-salām said... He destroyed all the idols and when asked who did that, he said, "He did it". He had left one untouched. He had put the axe in its hand and said: "He did it". Then they opened their eyes but objected. He said, "This big one did it." They said, "How can he do it? He's just an idol. He can't do anything." Then Sayyiduna Ibrāhīm said, "You said it yourself. It has no use. No use for itself as well. Therefore, open your eyes. Come to the truth." Even in that situation, they didn't accept the truth. They decided to throw Sayyidinā Ibrāhīm into the fire. They did so. And AllāhNone turned that fire into paradise. It became a cool place. Who follows the truth, even if he suffers, that suffering is useful for him. Otherwise, without truth, a person not worshipping the Truth, finds suffering and harm.

May AllāhNone protect. May AllāhNone show guidance to people and keep us steadfast, in shā’a Llāh.

Human beings they are since Ādam ‘alayhi s-salām there is AllāhNone show them send them guide to guide them to right way. ProphetNone and Prophet'sNone disciples and mu’min believer, to show them right way. Right way is true ḥaqq. Ḥaqq - way of reality, true and real thing, Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla and what HeNone is showing people. But people, majority of people they not like this. They going after the imagination, following many things, many ways following. Especially they are cheated by shayṭān. And they are spending whole life after nothing. They are struggling, they are fighting, they are tired, they are doing everything, their effort for what? For nothing.

AllāhNone is only True. And nothing anything else True except AllāhNone and His order. Don't think this people who ordering something you see, you thought it is good, maybe they put poison in very nice food. The food we eat without thinking, they are people eating. "As-sammu fi d-dasam," they said Arab. Poison in very delicious butter. You cannot... You say this is the best thing - we must take from this. But when you take it is poison, finish you. And the majority of people they are following their ego, their what they are think it is reality. Nothing reality except AllāhNone. Nothing, you cannot get any benefit from anything except AllāhNone. If you worship idol, if you worship animal, if you worship planet, if you worship...nothing benefit for you.

And people sometimes even they knowing this they still continue. Like Ibrāhīm ‘alayhi s-salām he... when he was young, he go to factory of idol, and he... factory or... temple. When they was in feast, they had feast there was outside of city. Ibrāhīm ‘alayhi s-salām go inside. He went inside and he take axe and he break all of this idol. Only he left one, the biggest one, and he put his axe in his hand and he went. When they come they see this, they shocked. "Who done this for our poor idols? We must find him and punish him," they said. "We hear one called Ibrāhīm, he is against this, maybe he done this. Bring him quickly." And he come He said, "Why you ask me? Look the biggest one you worship for him you ask for him and everything he get angry and break all this." He said, "What you are joking? It is cannot do anything this man, this idol. He is idol, he cannot do anything." He said, "Yes, you say by your tongue he couldn't do anything and you are still worshipping him. He is not benefit for himself even."

This people, our day's people also they are knowing nothing except Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla can help and they are worshipping everything, forgetting reality, closing, making dark for good thing and showing bad thing as it is good. So it is favour from AllāhNone to know reality. It is big ni‘mah, favour to know reality and accept reality. Maybe you know not accept also not good but you must accept and you will be winner. Even you suffer in your life, all this suffering AllāhNone reward you for this. But if you are knowing and not accepting this, whole your life you can maybe suffer for do everything - AllāhNone, AllāhNone not accept. If you not accept HimNone HeNone not accept you. Even you say, "Oh look this man he is helping humanity and doing this and doing that." It not important. Because humanity AllāhNone create and HeNone know what HeNone create and why HeNone create and this is test. And even they give whole thing and not accept AllāhNone it is not acceptable and not reward. Only for nothing because not accepting reality only accepting imagination and so will be empty.

AllāhNone make us from people. AllāhNone give hidāyah for this people also and accept true. Accept reality to be benefit here and hereafter, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq al-Fātiḥah.
