A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Innā ‘araḍnā l-amānata ‘alá s-samāwāti wa-l-arḍi wa-l-jibāli fa-abayna an yaḥmilnahā wa-ashfaqna minhā wa-ḥamalahā l-insān, innahū kāna ẓalūman jahūla” (33:72). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘Aẓīm. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says, this trust – that command for people to serve Allāh – even the mountains say, “I will not accept.” Mountains and rocks; nothing could carry this trust, saying it is a great burden, and they could not bear it. Mankind said, “I can do it.” He can do it, but Allāh says he is ignorant. He is unjust and ignorant. Because this trust – mankind – not everyone can carry it. Only the Prophets can carry it, and through the Prophets it is made lighter for people, so that mankind can endure it. Most do not even do that. Mankind does whatever is comfortable, whatever pleases him, whatever suits him. To be on the way of Allāh and to do what Allāh commands is difficult for people. Most people make various excuses to escape from it.

Qur’ān, in Qur’ān you can make mistake, no problem, because it is maḥfūẓ. It is preserved. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, when you do – maybe you read wrong or you forget – it is no problem. But for ḥadīth, you must say correct. For this, we read this āyah, meaning, Allāh when He create, there is some order, some secret. Allāh say, for mountain, for sky, earth, everything to carry this. They don’t, they didn’t, they not accept. Because this, “We cannot carry this. It is too heavy. It is, we cannot carry this.” So only human being they accept. Why? Because they are ignorant and oppressor. This is attribute of human being. Of course, there is exception for prophets, for good people, for beloved one for Allāh. But the majority, like this. And they are accept, and they are not doing what this order must done. When they accept and say “Yes”... Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla create souls, and they [were] asked, “Who am I? Who are you?” They said – some of them not accept. At the end, they are accept, all of them. And this time Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, who, He gave this order, and all of them accept. But later, most of them they change. They are not following what they promise. For this, our life, it is important to be whole life in this way. What Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla – His order about us – to follow this order. If you not follow, you will be outside. You break your promise, and you be not acceptable one, not good one for Divine Presence of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. As Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, only love the people who are following His order. Sometimes people saying, “Allāh not love us.” Allāh love, but what you are... You don’t love yourself. What you are all you done, this for yourself. Allāh show you, give you everything, every good thing, and you are going against this. What you must do? It is your fault. You must punish, you punish yourself. Okay. This is one of attribute of human being: to not stay in way and what they promise. Many time, since centuries, whole history of human being full of this people. And they are... Nobody live until now. Just short life, and they finish. Short and then they see reality. Reality of life, and what Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla show them, and they will be regret for this they didn’t do. This for common people. Muslim, non-Muslim, like this. But also there is some people, they are making proudness for they are over than other. So, maybe this believer people, Muslim people, they said, “I am like this, I am this. I am Shaykh, I am like this.” So Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, He said, “Only you must do what you can do. Don’t put on yourself heaviness. Don’t ask for something become for you difficult.” So, many people, they are not happy with their situation. They are like to be bigger or higher or more famous. This is, special this time of people, they like to be famous everywhere. They do anything good, bad, only to be famous. For this, no need to make what you cannot do. And people trying to push each other to be higher and higher. But this is no benefit for you. If you do this, only you do for your ego. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla not happy with this. Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam not happy with this. Only for your ego you like to be higher. Maybe for common people, maybe they want to be like MP or president or other thing. For ṭarīqah people, they want also to be... Many of people asking, “How I can be shaykh, how can be awliyā’?” This is very easy to be. Only you follow what Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying, and don’t think about other thing. If Allāh give you your provision, your rizq, and you are happy with family, you doing your ‘ibādah, this is the biggest favour from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. To continue this until your last breath, it is the most reward for you. This is... If you try, you must be try like this. Not to look for up, down, anything. Only you look for yourself, for your brothers or family. Everything must be perfect for you. No need to jump, to try to be higher and higher. Only if you are in this way, this is, Prophet saying: “Ajallu l-karāmāt dawāmu t-tawfīq.” The biggest miracle to be in way, same way, without make it less. No need for make it also high. Only this, enough for you, until you end. If you do this, you are winner. Allāh when want you to be higher, He open. If not, also you are beloved one for Allāh, if you continue whole life like this. Allāh keep us in this way, not to be, follow our ego. Because there is also, only who... Many people, they are praising themselves: “I am like this, I am like that. I am awliyā’, I am quṭb, I am shaykh, I am wakīl.” This is also not good thing. Only who praise himself – Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, He is, can, He every time praise himself. Even Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, when he said, “I am the highest one for human being, and no any proudness.” “Lā fakhr,” he said. And he must say this, but he is saying, “lā fakhr” – and no proudness for me. Only Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, He, “Wa-lahu l-kibriyā’u fī s-samāwāti wa-l-arḍ” (45:37). Every proudness for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, for heavens and Earth and everywhere. For this, who praise himself, it is not acceptable one. Anywhere – in ṭarīqah or not ṭarīqah. Nobody like who praise himself. Allāh keep us from, away from this attribute, in shā’a Llāh. Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
