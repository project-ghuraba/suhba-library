Education - (ar) Mawlana Shaykh Mohamad Nazim al Haqqani an Nakshibendi 21 Jumādā al-Ūlā, 1432 A.H. Monday, April 25, 2011 Translated from Arabic by: http://saltanat.org/ Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Rahim How many years have you been in the university? Guest: Two years. Can he give me an answer for the question I'll ask him? The question is: How can we get out of this economic crisis? Or what are the means to bring the world out of this economic crisis? This is the important point. Knowledge is an ocean. You many not be able to reach its bottom and you may not reach its shore. There are many oceans. Ships are only using one way to navigate in the ocean. It's a wide ocean but the ships move only in one direction to reach their target. And knowledge is an ocean. What is needed for us, we must learn it, get to know it, or teach it. This is important. But the teaching methods and the learning methods used in the universities ask [too much] from the students. What is required from the young people is over their ability. And Allah Almighty says,

La Yukallifu Allahu Nafsan Illa Wus`aha (2:286)

On no soul doth Allah place a burden greater than it can bear. This is a condition. Oh, this is a young man and he is a student in the faculty of economics in the American University in Cairo. I asked him a question, he may go on his way, and find a good solution. Or by finding an answer to this question, he may find his target and reach his safe heaven. We said knowledge is an ocean. For example, say we [want to] teach you swimming, or if we are about to teach you how to steer a ship. At that time what is important is for you to learn and find out the way that will take you to your target, to accomplish your mission. For example, say his target is to reach New York. They should teach him a way to steer the ship from London to New York. This is the wise way; but in the universities they are teaching him how to navigate the ship to various harbors, different harbors. Why? For what is this? This would be fine if he wants to be the captain of the ship, and if we gave him the responsibility to control this ship, and he is going to take this vessel here and there. This is overburdening the student. Teach him one way to his target, enough! That line should make you reach New York. But they are teaching him to steer the ship to many harbors. This does not give good results. Therefore I asked him one question and I am asking him to answer me. He is a young man, a student in the American University in Cairo. How many years is required for you to study and get a degree? He studied 2 years already and he has 2 years more. What did you learn? Economics. OK, tell me the rules of economics. I am asking how to arrange the economy. Tell me how it would be possible for the [economists to manage] the trust (economy) for the people. He is saying,

“I am the captain, I am going to go to this harbor and that harbor”.

“No! I am saying I want to go directly to New York.”

“No! You have to be with us. Come with us to at least 10 harbors Then after 10 harbors we will

reach the harbor of New York.”

“Why? Why do you take me to 10 harbors before I reach New York? This effort is wasted. Why

did I tolerate such a trip that is not needed?” Economics is the main pillar of human life. It is the main pillar. It is not possible for anyone not to know something about the knowledge of economics. It is a must to know something about economics for every individual, even for a small servant who is working in a small shop. Even such a person must learn something from economics. He is two years already in university and and he still has two years more. What did he learn? How is it possible for the community, or for each individual, to save himself or themselves from sinking into the ocean of danger, of economic crisis? How many books did you study? How many books did you read? Guest: Three, three books. In two years? Guest: That's what they gave us. But you may use the book for a long period. What are the titles of the books? [He cannot say.] No, the human brain does not concentrate on an important point. There is no focus. What is required for the student to learn? And now we are living in the time of economic crisis, an extreme economic crisis. Even countries nowadays are bankrupt. How could that be, for a country to be bankrupt? It is not possible! How, how could this happen? For what reason did countries reach bankruptcy? In spite of the fact that they are printing money, printing more money, and no one is asking about the amount that they have printed. And they cannot give the wages to the workers. They are cutting, cutting, cutting from his wages, and they are giving the workers the minimum wage. How is this? They are ignorant! They are ignorant to the reality of the knowledge of economics. Economy. 'Economia' is a word for which its roots are found in the Greek language. Economia, do you know its origin, do you know its roots, from where? Economia is, to keep or preserve what one has, so it is not wasted. You did not learn such things. They did not actually learn anything. Two years! What does it mean, economy? He doesn't know. Economia is, to preserve, what you got, or what you have. Did he learn? At the university, did he learn? No, nothing. And they are all like him. They don´t know. And if someone doesn't know … Even the teachers don't know; so how can they teach? And their life is going to waste. And their energy is wasted. His personal energy or effort is gone to waste. Effort is wasted; and it reaches to the point of zero, instead of coming up from zero. All the effort of the student is at zero level. We are already at zero level. Why then are we going to university? For what? For what reason? We are asking to come up from zero - higher! This is our aim; and this is the mission of the university, the college of economics - to bring the level of the servants to something higher than zero. Luxury. How can we reach that? Through the college of economics? Oh, they´re not teaching them anything in the college of economics. And Islam came with one word that is required, and actually it is fit to write it in letters of gold. Prophet (sas) said, “Ma al maniqta sad”. It means, “The one who saves and economizes can never go wrong”. Related to this tradition, there is another saying: “No one that overspends can be successful”. No one, or no nation, that is wasting their wealth can ever be successful. Write these two sayings from the tradition of the Prophet (sas) in gold. It is enough. You may study three, four, five years and you still won´t have an answer. This is enough for you. The one who saves, or economizes, cannot go wrong. We have another famous saying, “Don´t stretch your legs or your feet beyond the limit of your blanket.” Don´t stretch your feet beyond the limit of your blanket. That´s it, for what do we need the university? Teach people such things. shaytan makes brotherhood with those who are spending wastefully. Everyone now does 'tabsir', which is the arabic word for extreme overspending or wasteful spending. And in our economy now, the principle is to fill the whole world with things - different, various kind of things. Present things to the market for people to consume. But acquiring things, the abundance of things, is overspending or wasteful spending. And they can never be successful in such a way. Even worse than wasteful spending is tabsir, which is extremely wasteful spending. Then they become brothers of shaytan - the devil. Go, learn, oh people! The economic crisis will never leave you, until you stop spending wastefully in your lifestyle, in your homes, in your work. That´s it. Finished! We ask forgiveness. We repent to Allah Almighty. Fatiha. How many minutes now? 25 minutes. It is enough. It is enough. Isn't it true? For what then is all this studying? Oh our Lord. What is justice? Define justice. What is the definition of justice? This is an ocean, we must speak a few words to complete this association. Define justice for me. It is placing everything in its right place. Putting everything in its right order. Give me an example of this. Apply this definition; put it in a scenario. I am going to give you an example to show you: Is there justice nowadays? No, it is lost. Why? They are never putting anything in its place. For example, in the 21st century, shaytans are among humans and jinn. They pulled the ladies out of their homes [to work]. This is not justice. Because they, the ladies, were not created for what the men can tolerate and carry. They were not created for such work. No, it is not for women. They cannot carry and tolerate what men can carry and tolerate. The ladies, they are delicate. Delicate. They are not fit to work outside their homes. Until the women return back to their homes, the economy will never be corrected in the whole world. Nowadays they are saying that men and ladies are equal. This is wrong. Why are they saying such things? Taking the ladies out of their homes, and leaving no women in the homes, destroys the home. Allah Almighty is saying in the holy verse,

Waqarna fee buyootikunna wala tabarrajna tabarruja aljahiliyyati aloola (33:33)

And stay quietly in your houses, and make not a dazzling display, like that of the former Times

of Ignorance. And if you do not listen to Divine verses, you will be punished. You deserve at that time a punishment that will make it impossible for the two sides of your shirt to meet. Return the ladies to their homes. Keep them in their homes. Men may work outside, and the ladies remain in their homes. Oh Allah Almighty, make us learn and understand. Oh our Prophet (sas). La hawla wa la quwwata ila billahi aliyul adhim. Fatiha. To place everything in its place, this is a principle or a rule. Injustice fills this whole world. The whole world will be filled with injustice, until “a man from my household will appear”. Now, injustice and wasteful spending fill the whole world. May Imam Mahdi (as) may appear and correct everything and place everything in its order. Fatiha.
