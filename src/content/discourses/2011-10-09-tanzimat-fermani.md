Sultan Al Awliya Mawlana Shaykh Nazim al Haqqani 9th of October 2011

Who understands it? No one understands! All the magazines that are published they all pass above people's heads. They don't reach their hands. There is no one that can understand them. Especially this new... new made up language. They made it totally messed up. It is not understood at all. There is no hoja that can understand the language they use to write. The language spoken can't be really considered as a language. They made a total mess, ruined everything around. And the poor Hojas can't do anything about it. The people don't come to an understanding with this now. They don't come to understand... If there was no one that has spiritual power who will make them understand. These ones don't understand anything..Who else is there? Yes, also...Adnan hoja. What is he writing? Do you go there?

(S.M.) No. I can't go to anyone. There is no time. There is no time left for the people to visit. They are writing about him. This man sent me all these files. Mr. Misiroglu. All kind of things in these files. These things... Leave these things Do an autopsy of this. What we mean with autopsy - diagnosis. Is it not? (S.M.) Yes. Diagnose it. How did this nation fall into this situation? How were they dragged into such a situation? Write these things.. It is not true that the scholars go against each other through-using individuals. Misiroglu also writes so many things, in many books...

I say, I want a diagnosis, an autopsy until Hatt-i Sharif - Tanzimat Ottoman Imperial Edict of Reorganization. How did it happen? What was it that they called "Reorganization"? Tanzimat was an edict that directly targeted Shariat, by the pressuring of unbelievers in the Europe. Because the unbelievers knew that, as longs as Muslims kept their religion & Shariat it would not be possible to defeat them- have victory over them. - Our aim is to kick these Muslims out from Europe -(they said) Europe is a Christian union. They are heedless here. We may throw them from here. And, we may conquer them from within. We can't do it by attacking them from outside. We may use some people from among themselves & make them destroy themselves. It is a big mistake, The Hatt-i Sharif! What "reorganizing"? Can there be a "reorganizing" of Shariat? They did not fully apply it - therefore things got out of order. What "reorganization" is this? Greeks & Islam to be together? Can't be! It can't be!

There is Holy Verse: Yu`tu Al-Jizyata `An Yadin Wa Hum Saghiruna (9:29)? "until they pay the Jizyah with willing submission & feel themselves subdued" (M) "Saghiruna"? (S.M.) Yes. Meaning, they will pay "Jizyah - tax collected from non-Muslims", and meaning they will be on the level of shame. There is no other level for them. There can be no other level, other than Islam! Allah's order is that they will pay Jizyah and they will be in a... they may remain in a "lowly, humiliated" situation. There is no honour for them! These Christian nations, the ones other than Muslims, they are Dhimmi -non-Muslims who live in Muslim lands through an agreement. The order to the Dhimmis is for them to pay Jizyah-tax and while paying it... it will be like an insult, like a shame for them. Not paying proudly, but they will pay as a humbled one, not like they are doing a favor to the state. Shariat is like this.

But they brought this "Reorganization-Tanzimat" which says "Armenians are equal to Muslims,Jews equal to Muslims... Christians equal to Muslims". How can this be? What does this mean? What does it mean? The breakdown started from there. They started declining - all Muslim states... They are Muslim in name but when they see the unbeliever, they stand up. Standing up for an unbeliever is kufr-unbelief. They only do it to show-off for the people they respect, as a hypocritical friendship. They relate, that in Baf-Cyprus lived Ibrahim Sidqi Hadrat, a Naqshi Shaykh. Maybe you remember. He had a turba inside the mosque. You were not born yet. That Holy one, once when he was sitting in a majlis the people said that: "The Mayor is coming, the mayor is coming!" When they said the mayor is coming, the people in the majlis-gathering stood up for the mayor. They should not stand. Their gathering is not accepted. Subhan Allah..He was such a person that he would walk on the ocean, Ibrahim Sidqi Hadrat. May Allah reveal his secret & his support be present here. Fatiha.

He was sitting on the mat, that Holy one when people said "the mayor is coming". He disappeared. The people welcomed the mayor. The mayor sat and gave his sohbat. When he (Hadrat) came in the mayor stood up for him! There is such a respect in Islam. These people kiss unbeliever's bottom! What kind of Muslims are they? They are people with no honour. They are people with no honour! They are trying like crazy to become like them! They try then, they become uglier, dirtier, more unclean. When they become like unbelievers... "Innama Al-Mushrikuna Najasun" (9:28) They become mushriq-pagans! They have forgotten Allah, & they invented "God" in His place. They filled everywhere with them, saying "You will worship this. You will not go to the mosque. It is forbidden!" You were given a chance, but when that chance is taken from you, you will see! You will see what kind of an end you will reach with that one you worship! The Holy Verse says, you will know who is high & who is low. Allah.. Allah... It started from there. It started from Tanzimat-Reorganization. They made us fall into such a situation. They are still trying to become like them.

Tawba ya Rabbi, tawba astaghfirullah, O our Lord, send us a guide who will guide us to the Right Path. Send us an Imam, send us a Sultan O our Lord, who will raise Your Flag, the Flag of Islam.

Fatiha.
