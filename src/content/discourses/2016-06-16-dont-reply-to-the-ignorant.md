As-salāmu alaykum wa raḥmatu Llāhi wa barakātuh. A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alá Rasūlina Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūl Allāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh ad-Dāghistāni, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqāni, madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

Imām Shāfi‘ī Hz. says "I give you an advice my son. If you want to be peaceful, if you want to be safe if you want people not to attack you - keep your tongue." Because you have a tongue and like you, everybody has a tongue. If you say something negative to someone there will definitely come someone saying something bad to you. So if you don't say anything negative to anyone you'll be peaceful. You won't think "I told them this now what will they say about me?" Even if you don't say it, once they say something definitely something will come, you can't be peaceful.

ḥifẓu l-lisān salāmatu l-insān. Keeping your tongue keeps you safe and is a means for salvation, is a means for being at peace. The more you say unnecessary words the more you are not at peace. Even if you hear it, pretend not to hear it. They say "jawābu l-jāhili s-sukūt" The answer to ignorant people is to keep silent. Answering them is to give them value. No need to. What they say or do is unnecessary. It means they have no value. It is better not to answer them. This is the holy people's attitude, the attitude of the prophets, saints, and the companions. There is no need to answer someone, who isn't on your level. If you do, you give them value. So for your peace of mind, keep your tongue. You'll be peaceful. You'll have peace of mind, you'll be at peace.

Imām Shāfi‘ī Hz, he was saying, giving advice. If you like to be rest and happy, keep your tongue because this makes you safe from others. Others also they have tongue. As you have tongue other people also they have. If you are saying and speaking nonsense for people they will speak 10 times more and you will be not happy. You will be sad you will be miserable you will see every bad thing. Because when speaking, people also will speak on you. So the best thing, as big Imām madhhab, Imām Shāfi‘ī he said, "O son keep your tongue, because you will be rest if you're not keeping other people they also have tongue, they will make you unhappy."

The most thing coming for people that make them to in dunyā and in ākhirah bad place, bad situation - from tongue. So you must be every time keep your tongue, to know what you are saying. Don't speak without understanding. Without looking for what will happen after. "ḥifẓu l-lisān, salāmatu l-insān" saying. To keep your tongue it is from the safety of the person. And people they can speak, the Prophet, ṣaḥābah, Awliyā’u Llāh, all people were speaking against them but they're not replying. If you reply for these people, you make for them, value. These people who are speaking nonsense they don't have value. So, don't be angry.

Jawābu l-jāhili s-sukūt, this also answer of ignorant person - to be quiet. Don't come on his level, stay in high level. If you are coming to speak to him, you're coming quickly down and you will be nearly like him. But if you are not saying anything, he will be more angry because he said "they are not giving me any respect they are not giving me any value." Because what is their speaking people, the value for people is what they are speaking. You must be careful to not give answer for any ignorant people, be quiet only. Mawlānā Shaykh whole his life, was suffering from where, everywhere, but he's not taking personally for himself. If anything wrong he was saying in suḥbah, but not giving name for person or for what they are doing, only he was angry for... angry for situation not for person. For situation and he was replying but for common, not for one by one. And it is in Qur’ān and Ḥadīth also same like this. They cannot say make for ignorant people to speak to them. Allāh saves us from bad word, in shā’ Allāh, and to be happy life, in shā’ Allāh, happy end also.

al-Fātiḥa.
