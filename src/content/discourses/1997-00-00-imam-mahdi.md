...they don't have the courage to conquer. In order to save themselves, in order to run away, they become so helpless. At that time they gradually walk from Damascus in 7 stops: 1. Homs, 2. Tripoli, 3. Konya, 4. Bursa, 5. Istanbul, 6. Aleppo, 7. Hama. He comes in 7 stops, he prays, takes bay‘ah from the people. When he comes to Istanbul in seven stops, he takes the banner of Islām. He takes all of the holy relics. The holy relics will be in the same state as in our Prophet's (saw) time. Their old appearance will disappear. They will appear as brand new. This is a miracle. Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām walks with miracles.

He (as) stops one by one and comes to Istanbul in 7 stops.. When he (as) gives bay‘ah in Istanbul again (?) says a voice comes from skies, "O people, O believers! Allāh's enemy, the enemy of Muslims, the dajjāl has appeared. Whoever wants to save himself from his harm should come to Damascus. He (dajjāl) can't enter Mecca, Madina or Damascus. He will go everywhere but he can't enter Damascus - Jerusalam is also included as part of Damascus. He can't enter Mecca or Madina also. Until ‘Īsā (as) comes they'll be kept in Damascus.

When Allāh (jj) sends ‘Īsā (as) ‘Īsā (as) descends from the heavens for fajr prayer. When Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām is entering the miḥrāb, he (as) will see ‘Īsā (as) coming, the majestic prophet. He (as) withdraws from the miḥrāb and points to the miḥrāb and invites ‘Īsā (as) to approach. ‘Īsā (as) says: "For whomever the iqāmah was called, he is the imām". Iqāmah was called for you. Then our Prophet (saw) said: "kayfa antum idhā nazala ibnu Maryama fīkum wa imāmukum minkum." This is a ḥadīth from Bukhārī. "O my nations, when ‘Īsā (as) descends from heavens you'll be so happy, so much relieved, you'll be happy. Your imām will be from you on that day." The Prophet (saw) said that ḥadīth. ‘Īsā (as) says "When I descend, the imām will be from you. Iqāmah is called for you" He (as) prays behind Mahdī (as) to prove that he (as) is from the nation of Muḥammad.

These events are approaching now. It will be more frequent towards 2000's. It's full of signs. It isn't without a reason they are boiling Turkey. They are boiling the pan. But their plans will backfire or their weapons will backfire. Things won't go as they planned. Those days are near. We are waiting for this victory next year or the following year.

May Allāh (jj) make us reach those days. Allāh Allāh.
