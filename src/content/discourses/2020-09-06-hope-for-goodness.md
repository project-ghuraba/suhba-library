Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Our Holy Prophet says:

“Hope for good, you will find good.” Hope for goodness and you will find it. There is goodness behind everything. Everything you do should be looked at with goodness. If you hope for goodness, Allah will turn even the badness into goodness through His will. But if you look at everything in a bad way, it will be bad as well.

Therefore, it is necessary to constantly think about goodness in every work; in every work, whether it is or it is not [good]. When that happens, a person will be comfortable and at peace. “This is Allah’s will. Allah has certainly willed us good. This job will turn out good. What we do will be good. Where we go is good. Where we come from is good. There is goodness in everything.” And so people will find goodness.

Our Holy Prophet is ‘ني ق د ا ص لا ق دص أ’, ‘The most truthful among the truthful’. Everything he says is the truth. We should say: “ا نق دص و ا نم آ”, “We believe and we confirm”, and do what he says. Subhan’Allah, Allah Azza wa Jalla sent our Holy Prophet as a mercy to people. If people want to live comfortably in this world and in the hereafter, they must follow our Holy Prophet’s way and do what he showed.

If they do this, they will be comfortable in this world and happy in the hereafter. What is important is the hereafter. The real comfort and beauty are there. May Allah grant us all goodness insha’Allah. May Allah grant us to follow the way of our Holy Prophet firmly so that goodness comes to us insha’Allah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
