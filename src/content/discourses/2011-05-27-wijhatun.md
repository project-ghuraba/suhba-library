(2:148) - (ar) Mawlana Shaykh Mohamad Nazim al Haqqani an Nakshibendi 24 Jumādā ath-Thāniya, 1432 A.H. Friday, May 27, 2011 - after Fajr Translated from Arabic by: http://saltanat.org/ Our way is in association with the Shaykh and goodness is in the gatherings. Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Rahim. Janaba Haqq...Allah Almighty created human beings and made them in different tribes and various nations, right? In the same time He granted every nation and every tribe different identities, so they can be distinguished from each other. This is very important. But Subhuanallah … Miza (meaning particularity, uniqueness, feature, distinction) - right? The advantage that Allah Almighty granted to every tribe is in the uniqueness that gives them their Qadr/power. For example, gold has a quality that makes it unique among the rest of the minerals or metals, like iron. Every element has its own distinct identity. Another example: sodium would not lose its uniqueness and become chlorine. Same for chlorine: it does not lose its distinct identity to become another element, even if the chlorine is in the form of gas. The uniqueness of everything is a witness to the perfection of the Creator (swt). Everything! The same applies for all types of human beings who are from different nations and tribes. He (swt) granted each one its own distinct identity. They all have unique qualities. So there would not be among them any possibility to say "I am better", or "You are better". No! For each one there is a unique direction toward which he faces (wijha). [Mawlana is referring to 'wijha' as in Surah al Bakarah, ayah 148:

… Walikullin wijhatun huwa muwalleeha... (2:148)

… To each is a goal to which Allah turns him (Yusuf Ali)

… And every one has a direction to which he should turn (M.S. Shakir)

… every community faces a direction of its own, of which He is the focal point (Asad)] This means that for the direction in which each [tribe, nation, or individual] faces, there is a uniqueness (miza) about that direction (wijha). What do we understand? What do we understand? For example, an Iranian restaurant has its unique specialty, while another restaurant has another unique specialty. Similarly each human being has a unique distinction. [This is] so there should not be any person feeling heartbroken, or any tribe feeling belittled, because they are not special (without distinction); and so that any nation may not be arrogant toward the other nations. "They don't have … We have it." No! Allah Almighty granted every nation and every tribe it's own particularity so that each one can be proud with their own uniqueness. Allah Almighty gave distinction to His servants, subhuanallah! - from His Qudra (Divine Power), so that each one of them can see the Greatness of Al Haqq from their own side in a way that the other ones cannot see. Uniqueness is given from Allah Almighty to His servants, even to ants, even to atoms. Each atom has its own distinction and its own grant, particular to that atom, not similar to the other atoms. Each atom has its own particular personality and identity, not similar to the other atoms. Even minute atoms, like the hydrogen atom, each one of them has its own distinction. Allahu Akbar! And each one recognizes and realizes its own glorification to the Lord. This is particular to itself, and not to anything else. And this unique glorification becomes the gate or the way to their Creator. This is a very important matter. Glory to ALLAH Almighty! [Mawlana refers to people in the room.] You are not similar to her. Her mother is not like her. Her grandmother is not like her. Her brother is different from another brother. Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar! Each one witnesses to the Oneness of Al Haqq. Allahu Akbar! La ilaha il Allah, Sayidinna Muhammad Rasulullah (saws)! Each tribe has a distinction that surrounds that tribe totally. The Druze (of Lebanon) have a distinct identity that differentiates them from the rest. It gives them their own particular personality. How many other nations and tribes are there [on earth]? But that distinction is granted to the Druze in Lebanon and gives them their own particular identity. Through that distinction, the Druze reach to their Creator; and their uniqueness gives them their own glorification, their own understanding, and their own principles - particular only to them. When we were speaking about the Druze nation, they have their own principles and their own distinctiveness that differentiates them from the rest. And the important point is that they are keeping their unique identity. They are not letting it go, not taking [other people's unique features] from here and there, no! They keep and preserve their own distinction. They put all their efforts into maintaining their own personality and character that were a grant to them from Allah Almighty. The Arabs also have their own distinct attributes, but they are not holding on to their distinctions and principles. They are letting go of their principles. Once they drop their uniqueness, their value goes down. They lose the Qadr/power that was granted to them. Our Druze brothers are holding on to their principles very strongly and firmly, keeping the traditions that distinguish them from the rest. And we - the Arabs, Turks, Iranians, Jews - all of them became 'cosmopolitan'. This means that they adopt everything [from other nations] to themselves. They put everything in the pot. Mixed up from all international [ways], it becomes without taste. If they put vinegar with sugar in the pot, it doesn't taste good. If you put oil with pumpkin, it doesn't taste good. Everything, every item must be used according to the specialty that was granted to it. For example, if a nation has a particular attribute, they must preserve it and use it as it was granted to them. But this 'cosmopolitan' attitude, corrupting the world, makes every nation want to leave its own tribal or national specialty. [It means] to leave the uniqueness that differentiates them from the rest. They leave it and are mixed up with everyone else. They mixed oil with sugar, with vinegar, and with pickles. They mixed it all together. This is what brought the whole world down from a high station to a low level, zero level. And this holy verse …

Wa Makaru Wa Makara Allahu (Al Imran 3:54)

And they plotted and planned, and Allah too planned. (Yusuf Ali) Makara (plotted): shaytan plots. This is from the tricks of shaytan. shaytan used his tricks and traps when he advised the whole world to be 'cosmopolitan' - to be 'one'. It cannot be! It cannot be! It cannot be 'cosmopolitan' - all mixed up, confused. You must throw it away, that's it! The world now is like this. Not one nation or tribe knows, or recognizes, or preserves its own principles or distinct identity. They join the rest of the nations in this global attitude that is making them lose their distinction. They confuse everything until it is not possible to distinguish who are Arabs, who are Turks, or who are Kurds. Each nation must keep and preserve its own specialty. According to the holy verse:

… Wa Ja`alnakum Shu`uban Wa Qaba'ila Lita`arafu... (Al-Hujurat 49:13)

… We made you into nations and tribes, that you may know each another … (Yusuf Ali) To know each other, to get to know each other... And when you get to know each other, you may take from each other's specialty or something that identifies them, and you may use it. Each tribe has their own education, training and knowledge and basic principle. Now they have lost everything. And Prophet (saws) came to all the people, for everyone. He came to everyone and he gathered all the people, regardless of their differences, their customs, and their principles. He gathered them as a jeweler collects the various colors of jewels and precious stones for the crown of the king. For the Crown of the King! He does not throw even one jewel away. Human beings and all the tribes and all the nations each have their own jewel. The clever one is that one who recognizes the jewel from that tribe and who places it in their crown. Wa min Allah Al Tawfeeq, Fatiha. It is forbidden/haram among human beings and among nations to have hatred. Hated and aggressiveness are from the tricks of shaytan, from the plotting of shaytans. But now we are in a period of time that we drop or throw away the jewels that belong to each nation. And we have come to the lowest level in this time of the 21st century. May Allah send us a King to arrange our situation and to restore justice. What does justice mean? - to put everything in its right place. Islam – Masha'Allah! Islam made everything clear. May Allah forgives us Tub aleyna Ya Rabb. Fatiha. This is a grant from Allah (jwa). Subhuanahu wa Taala Subhansin Ya Rabb! Teach us Oh our Lord what is beneficial for us. This is enough now. You may take your rest. Al Fatiha. Link to video: http://saltanat.org/Blog/tabid/271/PostID/182/-For-Each-Is-a-Direction-toward-Which-It-Faces-2-148-ar.aspx
