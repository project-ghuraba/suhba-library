S. Abdullah Daghestani Effendi Hz was the deputy of S. Sharafuddin Hz. Assalamu Alaikum o servants of Allah. This is for Orhan Effendi. Orhan Effendi is the grandson of our Shaykh. We love him & also respect him. Because - leave Orhan Effendi - loving even your Shaykh's cat is from our adab-good manners. Is he the grandson of the Shaykh? Since he is his grandson, then we have love & respect for him, whether he likes us or not - It is another thing.

But we are not sitting here out of our own desire. If he has such a spiritual power to meet & speak with his grandfather, he may ask him: "Am I an authorized person to sit here or not"? What they told me is: "Sultans are inside the palace. (Our master) here is Shaykh Abdullah Daghestani Hz, that there is no one who is on his level. There is no such person. Once, a person from Daghestan came. This sohbat is what S. Abdullah Daghestani told us, I have it written. They would call this person - coming from Daghestan, "the green shaykh". I think he was on Qadiri tariqa-way. He had a mureed-follower in Rashadiyya (the town of S. Sharafuddin's maqam). He came to visit him. The owner of that house also invited S. Sharafuddin Hz. S. Sharafuddin Hz would not let anyone to come with him, only S. Abdullah Hz. He told him, "Let's go together". So they went.

He sat in the majlis - gathering. Now that Shaykh Effendi was "green shaykh". Everything he had was green- his staff, his turban, his shoes, his shirt... During the sohbat, in the beginning S. Sharafuddin Effendi Hz told him: "There may be a sohbat in our gathering, so that we can benefit from it. Would you like to give the sohbat, or should we do it?" He said to S. Sharafuddin Hz, "O S. Effandi Hz, in your presence we cannot say anything. We may listen to you". He said "Ok. What you said- "Listening", is it an easy thing? You go ahead. Do you listen to your wife?" "S. Effendi Hz, we can't hide anything in your presence. Sometimes I listen to her". He said, "Then you can't listen to me". The green Shaykh does not say anything. S. Sharafuddin Hz says: "Why did we say this? Because when he listened to his wife, prophet Adam was thrown from the paradise. He listened to her once, & had to leave paradise. If you sometimes listen to her there is nothing you can listen to, from us. S. Abdullah, you may speak".

S. Abdullah Effendi Hz started talking from such a spring that, the green shaykh got surprised, astonished! He gave such a high sohbat, commentary- well, it was not that high. Finally, the green shaykh asked, S. Sharafuddin Hz: "Who is this one, who came with you? What is his level? What is his station?" he asked. S. Sharafuddin said: "Ok, may I tell you his station in dunya, or in akhirat?" He said, "We may leave his station in akhirat to there but his station in dunya". He said: "Now, there is no one who can reach S. Abdullah Effendi's station, including myself!" Orhan Effendi may accept this or may not accept all his life, as he likes. This is his grandfather's statement. "La`nata Al-Lahi `Ala Al-Kadhibina - the Curse of Allah upon those who lie" (3:61). We don't take from this curse.

The ijazat-permission has come to me this way (through S. Abdullah). Or else, it is not that I know anything. Or, when he was about to leave this world..What did S. Sharafuddin say: "We can't say about (his station) in akhirat". When S. Abdullah Effendi Hz was about to leave dunya, he said: "Nazim Effendi, I have given you 7000 Sohbats!" He has 7000 Sohbats with me. If one is man enough to come & say: "He has that many sohbats given to me". S. Abdullah Hz is S. Sharafuddin Hz's absolute deputy & as he stated - I can't say more than this: "There is no one who can reach his station, including myself!" If he is a shaykh, he may ask his grandfather: "Am I lying or saying the truth? It is written in my book- he has given me 7000 Sohbats in 40 years!".

I am a man of no use but Allah has appointed us like this. I have come from Istanbul & committed to here. My commitment was not so easy. I have come upon an order. Now, one day.. I would go to S. Abdullah Effendi Hz everyday & ask: "I am going down, O Master, if there is anything you like, I may buy & bring". He said to me: "Go safe & come back safe". Then he told me: "This is also a Sohbat along with those 7000 Sohbats - 'Go & come back safe'. Meaning, shaytan attacks you when you go down. May you never become a target for shaytans! Go in safety. May all your organs go in safety & may you come back in safety" This is also a Sohbat. This word is enough to discipline the mureeds. Marhaban Orhan Effendi. Come here if you can. If you can't come, leave! Fatiha.

I was not present there when S. Effendi Hz was giving us ijazat-permission. He had the ijazat written to S. Adnan Effendi. In it, he said that "Nazim Effendi is as close to me as Sayyidina Abu Baqr Siddiq was close to the Prophet (sas). I have completed 6 riyadat an-nafs - disciplining the soul & khalwat-seclusion. The permission was given by the Prophet (sas). Dastur ya Sayyidi ya Rasul Allah. He (S. Abdullah Hz) counted it to be 6 times, with the order of the Prophet (sas). One khalwat & riyadat in Amman, one khalwat & riyadat in Baghdad, one khalwat & riyadat in Madinah al Munawwara, two times & later on 6 months of khalwat & riyadat in Madinah al Munawwara were also counted. He had it written & said: "Because he completed these, he is given ijazat". He may ask his grandfather about this ijazat.

This is an important point. One can not be a shaykh without completing khalwat! He can't even be accepted as a mureed in the Naqshi Order. It is not an easy thing! Now we have become of trash level here. Allah Allah. Aman ya Rabbi. We got hungry too (joke). You understand? They would not even accept as a mureed. A person who has not completed even one Arba`een (40 days) in his life may not call himself "Naqshi", it is said. Now shaykhs go around following their own ideas & call themselves shaykh! How shaykh? If you are a shaykh, tell me who is the Qutub of this time! G. You are!

M. We are of trash level. Our duty is, when Sultan ul Awliya was leaving.. Here is the palace of the saints. The awliyas are inside, in the palace. He told me: "Put here a.. What do they call these that they put fruits in it - crate. "Put a crate there, sit on it & keep people busy". We don't let them in. There are Sultans inside. We are only the doorkeeper. We don't have a claim. I am not saying anything else, only what he has told me: "Here, in front of the door, on a crate..". Turks call it 'crate'? He said: "Take a crate, turn it upside down & sit in front of the door. Don't let people in. You can handle them", he said. We don't claim anything, by our own desire.

Allah Allah, Allah Allah, Allah Allah, Aziz Allah. Allah Allah, Allah Allah, Allah Allah, Karim Allah. Allah Allah, Allah Allah, Allah Allah, Subhan Allah. Allah Allah, Allah Allah, Allah Allah, Sultan Allah. Marhaban, marhaban Orhan Effandi.

What have we got accustomed to in the presence of Grandshaykh: to speak to the common people, to eat hinkal (Daghestani ravioli) & to drink tea! My situation is this. I got used to eating hinkal & drinking tea. I leave what is beyond this. Orhan Effendi may take it. He may come to me, if he can find a man from east to west in that station - S. Abdullah's Effendi Hz's station. May Allah send us the Sahib al Waqt, that he shows who everyone is. Aman ya Rabbi, we didn't ask to be anything. We don't ask anything other than being a servant to Allah, hasha! "Abduhu wa Rasuluhu - servant & prophet of Allah". This is the attribute of the Prophet (sas). If one stands up on his feet to glorify the Prophet (sas) till the Day of Judgement, it is nothing!
