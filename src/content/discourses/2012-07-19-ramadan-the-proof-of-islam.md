As-Salamu Alaikum. Madad ya Rasul Allah, Madad ya Mashaykh Izam, Madad ya S Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, madad ya S. Nazim al Haqqani, dastur! Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fil jamia'

Insha'Allah today's Sohbat is for the occasion of the Holy Month Ramadan which starts tonight. Tonight we will pray tarawih insha Allah & tomorrow we will start fasting. Fasting is one of the five pillars of Islam and it is fardh/obligatory. First it was not obligatory. People would fast the first 3 days of each month in the time of Prophet (sas). Later on the fast of Ramadan began.

Who couldn't fast - Some found it difficult to fast, really fasting is a difficult worship - the wealthy ones from the Sahaba would feed 60 poor ones for each day. Then when the Holy Verse descended upon them, saying "it is obligatory for you", who does not have an excuse must fast, as it is obligatory now. If he doesn't fast, it is considered as sin. Of course, there is the kaffarah/expiation for each day. One who didn't fast for years should fast kaffarah once-60 days continuously. Of course not for each day he didn't fast, 60 days but once, as kaffarah. Then, calculating how many days he didn't fast & slowly fasting the missing days.. It is better for him if he fasts when in dunya.

Ramadan is a holy month. Like we said although there is this fasting. They call it "the Sultan of the 12 months". Really, in Ramadan Sharif there is a different happiness & relief. No matter where you are the barakah of Ramadan gives people happiness, by the wisdom of Allah. It means, the more a person worships the happier he gets. Leave single people aside, it is a happiness & relief for the whole country. Because when fasting, people are thirsty all day when it is summer. When it is winter time people get hungry. It feels long. And the nights have tarawih. All of this feels so good in this month. It is the wisdom of Allah.

Whether you are in Makkah, or in Madina Al Munawwara, or in Cyprus, or in Europe, or in Istanbul, the happiness & relief of this month reflects on people no matter what. And people feel it very well. There can't be a sign greater than this for people. Allah shows that the True/Haqq religion is Islam. Whoever wants, can reach this beauty. Whoever doesn't want, up to him.

It says in the Holy Verse, "Don't you grieve at all..." "Fala Yahzunka Kufruhu.." (31:23) The end of that Holy Verse is,"Don't you grieve..To Us is where they will return" There is no other place to go. They will see what they have done & at that time, they are the ones who will grieve. Allah does not need anything from anyone. He created everything. Everything is in need of Allah, everyone is in need of Allah.

Therefore may this holy month of Ramadan be mubarak for all you. Insha Allah we spend many years with Mawlana.

Today we speak about Ramadan. Tonight insha Allah we will begin tarawih. Tomorrow, Friday, 1st of Ramadan. Alhamdulillah, Ramadan - it looks like difficult worship. It is one of five orders of Islam- to be fasting in Ramadan. And fasting, it was the most difficult for ego. When Allah created ego, He (swt) said "who are you?". He said "I am me. You are You". And He (swt) give him, to make him many years of suffering ego. Second time asking, he said the same. After 3 months in fasting, not giving anything, he was saying "I am Your servant". Because this is fasting, it is not easy. But it is, even if in the first of Islam it was not obligatory. Every month they were fasting 3 days. After the order coming "In Ramadan you fast!"

But if you not fast, you give food for 60 poor people. Many people from Sahaba, because difficult to fast, they were doing this. After coming Ayah, "Kutiba `Alaykumu" (2:183). This is obligatory, you must fast. If you are in good health, you must fast. You can't be without fasting. Only for ill or who travelling, he can fast later. Or if he cannot- his illness not cure, he can pay for this.

And Subhana Allah this is, Ramadan everywhere. If you are in Makka Al Mukarrama, or in Madina Al Munawwara, or in Cyprus, or in Turkey,or in Europe..Ramadan it is in 12 months', it is Sultan of these months. You feel happiness. Wherever, you have been in everywhere world you feel this happiness. Even it is so hard to fast & night time you pray, you wake for sahur..But still when Ramadan going, feeling sorry for this. And this is biggest secret and the proof for Islam- It's real, Allah's religion. For Prophet (sas) said, "Truly, the religion with Allah is Islam" (3:19) And one of this, everything He (swt) has wisdom.

And this is if people thinking for this, even everyone, all world, they must be Muslim. Because proof - Allah put this secret in this month: you happy & around you also happy, even they are not fasting but they are also taking barakah from this month. Alhamdulillah this is very good tidings. Even for atheist, because they said this is material. But if material, this month from where this happiness coming to this people? Poor people, rich people, everyone they are happy in this month.

Alhamdulillah. Insha Allah we reach many Ramadans with Mawlana, with all in health insha Allah. With barakah of this month. Insha Allah Allah..Allah reward for fasting by Himself. Not saying "how many reward", so He is Generous, Who created generous. So you can't imagine how He will give for these people. And don't be "we cannot fast, it is so long, it is hot, it's like this like that". Because small children, 8 year 9 year, also fasting in this, we see everywhere. Don't be, say "we cannot, I am ill". Even who is ill & he want to take many..he can when he not fasting, there is adab for not fasting people: not eat in front of people. Old time, even this can't be. There was non Muslim, they had adab. When they are not fasting, they not eating in front of people. It was like this, but now all people no adab, nothing, no good behavior at all. And this is what important. If he is so ill, he can fast in winter- Who can't, his health is not good and he takes medicine. But as we said, you can eat in secret. In short days, you fast for this. If you can't fast for this, you must pay for each day, sadaqa fitrah, kaffarah.

Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, Al Fatiha.
