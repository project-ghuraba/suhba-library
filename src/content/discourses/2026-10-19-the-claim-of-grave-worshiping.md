As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

[…] Better than everything anybody has in this dunyā. This is the best favor from Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. Because this favor of being beloved for Him is a favor forever; not only for a short time in this dunyā. Many people have something in dunyā; maybe they are very rich, they’re very powerful, very effective, but they don’t have anything for ākhirah. These people are poor people. Because richness is not to be so for a little time and after that nothing. Alḥamdulillāh, you have, and we also have inshā’Allāh the niʿmah, favor, richness forever.

Alḥamdulillāh we are gathering here for the sake of Allah and near one of Awliyā’ullāh who came here for serving, for khidmah for Allah, serving people to guide them to also be rich forever; not only for a short time. And they have done very much for this country, for this land, for Islām. For each one they brought to guidance - Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam says, if any one attains hidāyah through you, it is more valuable than this earth and what’s inside it. Not only a small place, a small country or continent; but the whole world is nothing compared to what Allah will give.

We are coming to visit these people upon whom Allah showers His mercy. This mercy alḥamdulillāh also comes upon those who visit them and ask Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla through them to accept their duʿā, through Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. These people are all acceptable people in the Divine Presence of Allah. For this, we are not asking from them. We are asking through them. This is how many people are cheating other people saying, “These people are worshipping graves. They are not worshipping Allah.” Ḥāsha! (far from that).

Alḥamdulillāh, all these people are coming through these blessed people to Islām; not by themselves. And these blessed ones are not saying, “Come worship me.” They’re only saying, “Worship Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. We guide you to Allah, to paradise. Follow Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam and what he  said, you will be in paradise.” Nobody is saying “Worship the Prophet.” Nobody is saying “Worship me, worship the graveyard.” No.

All this is pure Islām and they are the highest, the real believers, they are the real Mu’mins. Because there is Mu’min and there is Muslim. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says,<br>
قَالَتِ الْأَعْرَابُ آمَنَّا قُل لَّمْ تُؤْمِنُوا وَلَٰكِن قُولُوا أَسْلَمْنَا وَلَمَّا يَدْخُلِ الْإِيمَانُ فِي قُلُوبِكُم
<br>“Qālati l-Aʿrābu āmannā qul lam tu'minū wa-lākin qūlū aslamnā wa-lammā yadkhuli l-īmānu fī qulūbikum”<br>
“The bedouins say, “We have believed.” Say, “You have not [yet] believed; but say [instead], “We have submitted,’ for faith has not yet entered your hearts.” (Qurʼān 49:14). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

The Aʿrāb, bedouins, said we have believed. Allah says, “Don’t say you have īmān, only say you are Muslim.” Īmān is (more) higher. And īmān comes through these people. They put īmān through the hearts of people and make them higher.

Whoever has īmān, real īmān, nothing affects him. Even if they throw him in fire, nothing affects him. Even if he doesn’t find anything to eat, also nothing affects him. Even if he’s about to die from thirst, also nothing affects him. Because with īmān, he knows Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla will give him. 

And what’s important in īmān is that you must know:<br>
آمنت بالله وملائكته وكتبه ورسله واليوم الآخر والقدر خيره وشره من الله
<br>“Āmantu bi-llāhi wa-malāʾikatihi wa-kutubihi wa-rusulihi wa-l-yawmi al-ākhiri wa-l-qadari khayrihi wa-sharrihi min Allāh”<br>
“I believe in Allah, His angels, His books, His messengers, the Last Day, and in destiny, its good and its evil, being from Allah.”

Everything is from Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. This is īmān. Every morning we recite this. The rule of īmān is to have full belief in Allah that when anything happens: not to be sad, not to be anxious, not to say “Why?”

Sometimes we see many people who say, “Why me?” This is why. Because you are not Mu’min, you are only a Muslim, so this is happening. And you will suffer, you will be sad, you will be unhappy, you will be asking, making bad adab towards Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla, asking “Why me?” Who are you to ask Allah “why me”! This is the most craziness. The most mindless ones are those who ask “Why me?” Because Allah. You cannot say this for Allah.

You cannot run from Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. In dunyā, in ākhirah, and everywhere: you are in the Hand of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. There isn’t any place to run away. For this, may Allah forgive us.

May Allah make us to not be in bad adab or badly behaved towards Him and what He gave us. May Allah bless you. May Allah keep us. May Allah increase you more and more, believers, mu’mins, inshā’Allāh gathering here, gathering in all Malaysia, inshā’Allāh, in the whole world, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
