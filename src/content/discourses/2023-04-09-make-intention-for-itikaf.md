A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Ramaḍān is almost over now. More than half of it is over. It has its forms of worship, one of which is i‘tikāf. I‘tikāf means spending the last ten days of Ramaḍān in a mosque or a place where they perform the prayer five times a day. It is the sunnah of our Holy ProphetNone. It must be made by at least one person in each country so that barakah comes, in shā’a Llāh. Our Holy ProphetNone never skipped it. In fact, once heNone was occupied with something, I don't remember what now, so heNone did it twice that year. HeNone did his missed ten days first, and then heNone did it normally after the twentieth day.

Certainly, i‘tikāf must be made for ten days. However, people may do it less. When entering a mosque or a masjid, they write, "Nawaytu sunnata l-i‘tikāf," above the door, which means, "I make intention to make i‘tikāf." You attain its thawāb (reward) then. I‘tikāf can be made any time of the year. But the best is to follow the sunnah of our Holy ProphetNone and make it in the last ten days, for those who can do so. For men, it is made in a mosque, in a masjid or in a dergâh where they pray five times. They must not talk about worldly things, but perform worship and eat, eat normally. It is not like the khalwah (seclusion). They can make saḥūr and eat in the evening. And there is permission to eat anything they like. There is no limitation. Its virtue is great in the eyes of AllāhNone. There is so much blessing for those who can do it.

Shukr to AllāhNone, they do it all over the world. They do it in Masjidu l-Ḥarām, Masjidu l-Aqṣá, Masjidu n-Nabawī, the mosque of our Holy ProphetNone – in all of these. I don't know how it is now, in Masjidu l-Aqṣá. They used to make it there. In shā’a Llāh, may AllāhNone give power to Islām with their barakah. May HeNone protect us from fitnah and evil. Because of one person, there is barakah and mercy for other people as well. It strengthens their faith, in shā’a Llāh. May it be blessed. May AllāhNone reward those who perform this beautiful worship. May AllāhNone be pleased with them.
