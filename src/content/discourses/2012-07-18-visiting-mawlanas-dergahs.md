As-Salamu Alaikum. Madad ya Rasul Allah, Madad ya S Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, madad ya S.Muhammad Nazim al Haqqani, dastur! Audhu Billahi min al shaytan al rajim, Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fil jamia' We may say a few words insha Allah, that it becomes a benefit insha Allah, upon the order of Mashayekh.

Shukur to Allah, with the himmat & barakah of Mawlana we have been travelling for a month. We went to the places that once Mawlana had passed through. Mawlana has not been going to these places for about 15 years. MashaAllah the seeds Mawlana had planted are turning green, becoming plenty everywhere because Mawlana's (mission) is Haqq. Because he is the inheritor of the Prophet-warith Muhammadi (sas) wherever he passes through, he is collecting the people who have isti'dad/capacity & they are following Haqq way. They also are bringing others. Who has qismat/is destined to come, is coming. Of course, the ones who are not on the Haqq way are the majority. The people who follow their egos out number the others. But for those who have isti'dad & the intention to save themselves from the evil of their egos, Mawlana had traveled for years & planted the seeds. And they turned green after him.

Just like Ahmad Yasawi Hz, how he sent out dervishes to Anatolia hundreds of years ago and first they made a spiritual opening. The people in Europe were very much in need of this. But our people who went there before.. Many Muslims went there but they didn't know their language, nor did they go for this purpose. They went there only for money. First they went to make the money & return to their household back in their countries. But of course, once they went there.. The concept was different there. Their morals are different, people are different. Many people were condemned by their egos, wasted themselves as well. How can one who wastes himself guide the people there? Couldn't do that either.

Later on, they tried to put themselves together and entered the way of Islam but still it was different. Without Tariqat, to address the people there, to reach the good manners of Islam, to guide others is not possible. Therefore, thanks to Allah, Mawlana's dargahs & mureeds.. Thanks to Allah, all of them are with Mawlana's himmat, with his Heavenly glance upon them.

So Alhamdulillah, we went for a month. Even we heard that one mureed told another, "Hj Mehmet is giving sohbat. Come & listen". That man left his work & came. He said "What kind of a thing is this, 5 minutes of sohbat!". He thought we will make sohbat for hours. That is what we are saying, we are nothing! But Mawlana's himmat is on us. When we went there, it felt as if he was there! It was because we went every step with Mawlana's himmat & dua. Otherwise we have nothing. We have no knowledge, no speech, no nothing. Even if you are a world wide person, Allah Almighty can take that knowledge from you in one minute.

All these people gathering and feeling happy, even all of them say "it felt as if Mawlana was here". It was because, no doubt that his barakah & himmat was upon us. And this, I am not saying from myself- I am saying openly: I can barely put two words together & speak here! Meaning, we have no claim for our ego. But when mashayekhs want you and love you, there is nothing to question anymore. Like we said, here even if you say "ha ha ha" people will still gather. Whatever you say, people will still come. So this is with himmat, barakah. And this is because of the greatness, power of the mashayekh. We have nothing.

May Allah not leave us without them insha Allah. Thanks to Allah we have witnessed their himmat & miracles. Thousands of people gather because of his love for Allah & Prophet (sas). It was very good, Shukur Allah. May Allah make Mawlana's himmat, and himself as well, last until Mahdi (as) that we will be with him insha Allah.

Alhamdulillah today, we speak about the journey of one month traveling many places in Europe. Alhamdulillah, it was good. Very good, Alhamdulillah. It is, we see our dargahs, mureeds of Mawlana. They are like small tree, become more & bigger. And this is like, 400 years ago there was Shaykh Ahmad Yasawi from Turkestan. He sent all dervishs to Anatolia before, opening by army. There was, all people like Islam because of this people, good people. They coming & they teach good behavior and Islam, exact Islam & Tariqat, and the Mercy, love for people, respect for people. They were happy. Before they come they didn't see something like this. And now Alhamdulillah, our Mawlana Shaykh's dargahs like this in Europe.

More and more people coming. And who is coming? Because most of people.. You can find only few people, they can be good people. And this good people must reach them. Most of people.. Even in Quran also, it says "you cannot.." Allah speaking to Prophet (sas), "if you like all people to be in good way, you cannot give this for people. Only few people they can do". And this good people, you must reach them. Alhamdulillah Mawlana, he is putting dargah and mureed. They catch these people around Europe.

Before there was many Muslims from Turkey, from Algeria, from Morocco. They are all coming to Europe. But they going, not for to give teaching Islam or to give.. Only they go to, for earn money. They are poor people in their counrty, & this opportunity it was big for them. They were thinking "maybe we go, in four years we can take money and come back to our country. And make good life for children". But it was like trap, all they bring their children also, their wife, people. And Europe culture is different from Islamic culture, these people where they come. Most of these people they was under their ego. From everywhere, they were not to.. they can't bring people to Islam. Even they are, most of them, maybe most of them left Islam, or something, left behaving like Islam. And this is for, because there is millions of people. If this people was good behaving, it was even for them it will be more respectful people in Europe. They are complaining too much, and I saw, it is difficult for people but it was their fault before. Because they not behave like good people. And when this people seeing this people, "this is Islam not good like this people". But after, because they don't have Tariqat, they don't have practising Islam, after 10-20 years, they becoming to think better. But also they are on, inside themselves. They cannot bring another from European people to Islam.

Only Shaykh Effendi, by Tariqat and giving good behave, adab of Tariqat, his goodness and beauty of Islam by Tariqat. For this many Europeans coming by this way. Without, otherway, they run away, they can't give them. Alhamdulillah this is karamah, big karamah for Mawlana Shaykh. Because I also went there but by his dua & praying & his protecting thousands of people they come & they were happy.

I say, I cannot speak well. It is real. But even, but when Awliya looking, protecting you and they are happy with you, people also all happy. But you can say only "ha ha" or something like this even this is enough for them. Yes, Alhamdulillah. And they were really very happy, Al hamdulillah. This is karamah of Mawlana. I am not claiming I could speak. Even when I speak here, difficult to speak. But Alhamdulillah, this is barakah of Mawlana. He protects & he is Alhamdulillah giving support.

This is, last night also sohbat, he was saying "if Allah likes His servant, He said be Rabbani. For belong to Allah all people they like you & they protect you.InshaAllah all of us we will be like this. Because when you are like this you will see many of people, they were not speaking many years to each other, all fighting together. Alhamdulillah all coming together. Barakah of Mawlana.

Allah keep him with us all time insha Allah, until Mahdi (as), Isa (as). Bi hurmatil Fatiha.
