Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Our Holy Prophet (SAW) says, “Whoever hears my name and does not send salawat on me, may their nose sweep the floor.” Honoring our Holy Prophet (SAW) is Allah’s command. It is an obligation on every Muslim. So it is not a sunna or nafila (supererogatory) but fard.

Because our works are useless without the love and affection of our Holy Prophet (SAW). Our works are half measure anyway. What makes them acceptable is honoring our Holy Prophet (SAW), loving him, and making him be loved. This is the essence of being Muslim since nothing we do is of use without our Holy Prophet (SAW).

Now there are many factions confusing the minds of people. They confuse people’s minds and make them perplexed saying, “You would be committing shirk against Allah (associating partners) if you honor the Prophet (SAW).” They misguide people from the right path, take them far away from the right path, and push them to a bad end, because those who do not respect the Prophet (SAW) are with Shaitan!

When our Holy Prophet (SAW) entered next to Shaitan he asked him plenty of questions. One of them was the question “Who is the one you hate most?” He is not lying either and says, “You are,” to our Holy Prophet (SAW). When this is the case, people who do not honor and love our Holy Prophet (SAW) are certainly with Shaitan.

Shaitan has many ways to fool. The worst is using religion, Islam, to deviate from the path. They are saying, “Honoring the Prophet (SAW) is shirk and sin.” That is how mindless these people are. The Koran is full of honoring our Holy Prophet (SAW) from start to finish. Allah (JJ) is honoring him. They are fooling people. There are lots of mindless people who are fooled by them.

May Allah keep us safe, and may our love for Allah and the Prophet be plenty Inshallah. May Allah not misguide us from the right way. May we all be granted the intercession (shafaat) of our Holy Prophet (SAW) Inshallah. And may we be housed near him in paradise Inshallah.

Wa Minallah at-tawfeeq. Al-Fatiha.
