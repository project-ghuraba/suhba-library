As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīn wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa-l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

Our ProphetNone is saying when AllāhNone wills something to happen, even if a person says he is clever, he will lose his mind and that thing will take place. And then his sanity will return and he will wail about what he did. This means that when AllāhNone wants something, it will happen. And a person with a mind can’t make that mistake or do that thing which needs to be done. Certainly he will lose his mind first, and then wail in regret. This can happen to everyone in this world. Sometimes big mistakes are made, sometimes small ones and even those bother people.

Mankind thinks this world is going to be their place forever. However, there is not long to live. There are people who claim they rule over the world. They believe it is as they like and according to their order. But when the Will of AllāhNone is going to appear, they will be surprised too. They won't know what to do, and it will be as AllāhNone says. This world is not eternal. It is only until some time. Then it will change, then change again. It is the End of Times and then Qiyāmah will happen. Certainly for everything to happen, there must be some sign or action. And that is the regret of people for not knowing what to do after realizing the Order of AllāhNone. But everything is over then. There is no return from that. Therefore, we are living in the End of Times, and its signs are slowly showing up. Therefore, no need to get upset for dunyā. Be with AllāhNone, and AllāhNone will protect you both here and Hereafter.

May AllāhNone not let us lose the way. May AllāhNone help us use our minds, in shā’a Llāh.

Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, when AllāhNone want something to happen, HeNone take the thinking, good thinking, mind from people. After they done something wrong or who has mind, he has, he is clever, cannot do this. After this they say, "What we done?" with, they are regret. They are cannot... They are blaming themself – "How we done this? We are people, clever people, we are thinking for everything but what we done it is wrong." This is what AllāhNone, HeNone want for whole human being like this. For sometimes even they make small mistake also. You, when you see it, it is funny, cannot anybody do it. "But why I done this?" he said. This is from AllāhNone. So this, for small one, for bigger one, for more powerful one, for every one AllāhNone can make this to make HisNone Will with the program to be done.

Because now people in this world they are saying, "We are make law, we make our program. We are controlling whole this world. Nothing can do. We make plan before hundred year and this coming like this. We are not thinking to about anything, no mercy, nothing. Our plan must go like this because when our program like this world it will be good for us." So they are continuing this. But AllāhNone for this people also, HeNone can take their mind and doing wrong thing. After, they cannot continue what they are doing and they are blaming themself – "How we done this mistake?" But it is normal because nothing be forever in this world and there is now time, End of Time, who thinking theirself they are powerful, they are good, they are can controlling whole world – they are forget Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. HeNone has [His] program and it's [a] program slowly slowly going to Judgement Day which it must be something happen. And at the end, all people they will be – who some of them happy, some of them not happy. They are some of them regret but time, that time they cannot do anything.

So AllāhNone save us from bad people and keep our mind to not be do wrong thing; to be in this right way, in shā’a Llāh, not going out this way. As much we are asking help from AllāhNone because HeNone done, HeNone can do anything. But when you are asking, praying for this, HeNone is Merciful. HeNone will be, in shā’a Llāh, keep us in this way, way of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
