Sheikh Bahauddin Adil Al-Haqqani sohbat of the 4th of October, 2014.

Ya salam, opera!

Alhamdulillah that Allah Almighty He makes us to reach Eid ul Adha al mubarak. Kurban bayram. The bayram of Hajj, the eid of Hajj. This is the most holy event that Allah Almighty He presented for His servants. And this is like a newborn because it is required once in the life. It is not tourist place to go everyday. This is once in the life, it's enough. Mawlana Sheikh he used to say "when you do your duty, it's done." You don't have to pressure yourself, put so many expenses to go again and again and again. Unless you are having duty, unless you have something it is important to do, unless you have to be for other one because the Hajjis have own conditions.

Today, the subject to go for Hajj place and go for sacrificing because in this day we have to sacrifice for Allah Almighty with some animals. And they say the animals that are being sacrificed for this 4 days they are the most lucky animals among the other animals. This is Allah Almighty He honoured them by doing this duty in such holy days. Allah Almighty He is asking, but normally He doesn't eat it and He doesn't receive it. It is for poor people, it is for aid, it is for so many purposes that remind us there are poor people. It reminds us to aid. We have to do our duty as humans. So many people say "why you are killing this animal?" We are not killing this animal. This animal they have been created for such an occasion. This animal Allah Almighty put them in order of Bani Adam. He put them in order of humanity because there is heavenly balance.

Now we sit on the table we have olives, we have cheese, we have some meat, we have some bread, we have some rice, we have some vegetables. We have a mixture of food. Yahoo! When you sit on the table you don't calculate which is this, what will this do, what will this do. Normally there are specialist people they say "if you eat meat you will cover your proteins. If you eat vegetables you will cover your vitamins. If you drink water you will cover the transport inside your body." And this goes on and on. But what we are doing as normal people we sit, eat, drink and we go. Never have calculation to say "how many calories we take, what is the necessary to take." In that moment Allah Almighty He gives us the feeling that the body will say "today I am not good to eat meat." For example, so you go for vegetables. Some days you say " I am not good to eat vegetables, I need meat." So the body is telling you actually, what you want. So this is being designed by Allah Almighty, and it is known by everybody.

But now, people they became more clever and they say "why you are slaughtering sheep on this day? Why you are doing this in that day? Why it is like this? Why it is like this?" Everything has an explanation. Nothing is without explanation. But the important thing here how you are asking. Are you asking to have an explanation or are you asking to make an argument? It is very important. If you are asking for an explanation, you will have your answer. But if you are asking for arguing, you will never have your answer. That's why it is important to understand why you are asking. This is Allah Almighty's all the time He put everything in front of His servant. Everything, because Allah Almighty He is Justice that He will never... He said "I will never bring a hole of a needle hole to be an excuse on Me for you make this hole as empty place for your justice." This is how Allah Almighty He is so careful about His servants. And we are trust in Allah Almighty.

Because here the big issue with us and we call it Iman, the belief. If you believe, the first thing that you do, you trust. When you believe and when you trust, you will be relaxed. Alhamdulillah, I don't have anything to ask & I don't have anything to explain. Whatever is coming in front of you, because you are believing and because you are trusting, it's coming easy on you. Very important point in our life is who to trust. It is the biggest question. First we have to trust that we are human, we are not animals. And the second thing we have to trust that Allah Almighty He gave us a mind to think. And then we have to trust our eyes, what we are seeing. And the most important to complete all this, to ask your heart because Iman and the full trust is there. So it is like one package. It is one package.

Because some people they say, "the mind it is not enough." Yes, we know the mind has borders. And one hit the barriers will turn back and say "no!" And then it put the eyes to control that barrier. If it is ok or no. And the eyes sometime say "yes, it is possible." Sometimes it says "yes, no it is not possible." Then with the feeling you have to go back to your heart and your heart will say, "O mind, you are so limited, Allah Almighty He gave you so much power, but at the same time you are so limited. You cannot go over that barrier. You are stuck in that barrier and you will be going round and round without going ahead." Then the Iman, the belief, is coming from the heart and saying "Yes! Now, I am taking over, whatever you see, whatever you think, I am handling the situation and now I am shifting you up, to go for space."

If you say to people 100 years ago "we will be in space and we can walk and make spacewalk. In those days the mind, and eyes will tell you "no! Impossible! How you can climb such a high place?" We know the world is round we know the world is having ozone. Before 100 years they were knowing all this. But if you tell any ordinary person, he would say "No! No way, impossible!" But today if you ask your small child, 10 years old, "Are they going for space?" "Yes they are going for space." No problem. It is very ordinary. But that belief the one make... it was in some people, it was not in all people. Say "yes, one day these humans they can make spacewalk." And it happened. So this is how you have to look at it. Don't look at it as, minor looking and don't stick with your mind saying, "no, impossible. Or don't believe in your eyes and say, no impossible." You have to have the belief it's in your heart. So that's why it is one package.

If you can put it together. It will take you for the safe area, it will take you further. It will take you for the beauty, it will take you for the nicest view. They say the best view you can ever have it is to look to earth from space. It is the best view. Cannot be better than this view. This means as much as you are higher, as much as your view is much nicer. It is not, if you are sitting here there is no view. We are sitting 4 walls, no view. But if we go on the roof, wow we have different view. If we go higher we have better view. And they say the best view it is to look from space to planet Earth. But what made people to look from there, is the belief. Even it is not necessary, but it is effective, but it is valuable.

Now all the electronics are being commanded from space. They throw one satellite and they say "now we have HD channels. Now we have HD sound." 100 years ago tell him "what is HD?" What they will tell you? "Hmmm? You are crazy go!" This is, how the belief will take you to HD belief. Now we are in HD belief. We are HD belief. Don't stick in the past, just feel to, put yourself in the future. Because Allah Almighty He tell you "don't forget the past, live the moment and hope for the future." It is very important. Because so many people they say "past, is past!" No past is not past. Past is to learn. Now - it is to live and the future - to hope. Because without hope you are useless.

There is very nice things. One man he was saying to me "you know my father is old man and he is feeling very bored, very bored. He doesn't know what to do." He said, "I am scared to tell him, dad will you pray 5 times a day? I am scared to tell him. "And he said "if I tell him he might be angry with me. But this is the only thing to make him go out of his boredom." And say "I think, why you say like this?" He said "Very simple. You know if you have 5 appointments a day. When you finish 1 appointment you start to wait for the second one. And when you are having that deadline for the 2nd appointment, then you have to concentrate that you are busy to wait for that appointment. And when you are waiting for that appointment, you feel that your life is being completed and you feel your life has a value."

Because old people they've been working so hard doing so many things and are tired. They say "we are retired." This they call it retired, because they're tired. So, when they are tired, they become retired. But they forgot something - that you cannot rest. When you rest your psychologic point will come and say "Hey you! You're useless man! You worked all your life to sit like this?" Then, he will be having fight with himself. He will say "O my God, what have I done? What have I done? It is wrong to be retired." We already retired. If he wants to go back for his job, say "No, no you are so old. Thank you very much. Alhamdulillah we are finished from him", they say." The office of the work area they say "Oh, oh, oh we have been waiting so long to make you retired. We cannot take him back." Say, "leave a chance for the youngsters." But they want to get rid of him. I don't know when they will get rid of me, but one day it will happen.

Then, he will sit at home and say "yes, I like TV." He watch TV. TV makes him more.... taking all his oxygen. And then he says, "Ok I go for garden." He goes for garden also hoping to get some oxygen. The sun will hit him, and says "O makes me more tired." And then nothing to do. So he will go walking, walking, walking and no appointment. No appointment, nobody calling him, nobody asking him nothing. Must have some appointment from Allah Almighty, this 5 times is very important. Everyone finishing prayer, and then he looks for the watch, "O we have like 3hrs to go, 4 hrs to go, 5 hrs to go." So he counts and this will fill his life and make his life colourful in the psychological side of his life, and there is also the spiritual side of his life that makes his life richer and makes him to love everything. Because he knows that the One He is loving him, calling him to meet with Him. This is how Allah Almighty He makes perfect design. This is how it is HD Iman.

We have to talk according to today. We cannot talk according to yesterday. Yesterday they have good things about yesterday to teach us. But we have to live today & we have to have the hope for the future. The future is very important for us, we've been having our future with Mawlana Sheikh. Mawlana S. he is our future, but now Mawlana S. he said now "I am retired. Now I am retired." Now it is our job to give future for others. That's why we sit here to get this future light, lightening again. Not dying. Mawlana Sheikh never dies, and Mawlana S. never asks us to die. He asked us to continue the duty, but what he said - "if you want to continue this duty, don't be a proud one." This is his condition. Mawlana Sheikh's condition - "don't be proud. If I am giving you a duty, don't be proud, kibr."

Allah Almighty He hates the kibr, He hates the proud ones and He specially hates - Allah Almighty in His hate list, it is the poor proud servant. The poor and proud servant, Allah Almighty doesn't like him. He says "poor and proud." This is the worse combination. Worse combination ever it is poor and proud. Say, "if you are poor be humble. At least the people will say "O poor man, we help him." He will say "No, I don't need your help, I don't need this, I don't that." Hungry? Nooo. No place to sleep, no wife, no children, nobody. No reputation and still proud "Nobody can help me! No I don't accept anything." "Yahoo! I am just trying to.." "No!" Imagine such a person, Allah Almighty also doesn't like him. This is how Allah Almighty He put categories. Astaghfirullah Al Azim.

So, when we are in duty like this, we have to be humble until the ground. This is Mawlana Sheikh condition. You have to be with people, you have to be inside the people. You have to be deep with people. You cannot be saying "I am the son of the Sheikh, oh I cannot shake hands with you. Oh maybe I don't know what you are carrying. O what is this?" No! Even it is so dangerous, you don't have a choice. You don't have a choice. I remember Mawlana Sheikh we went with him to Karachi, Friday prayer. I don't know how many thousand people jumped on Mawlana Sheikh. And Mawlana S. shaking hand, shaking hands and the people jumping jumping... And Mawlana S. going like this, going like that, and he said "where are my shoes?" And the people around him, "Ya Sheikh forget about your shoes, let's go out of here!" They went in the car and the car cannot move because of the love of the people. This is nothing bad, but it is dangerous. Even those, Mawlana S. was so tolerant.

This is how Mawlana S. is teaching us, this is how Mawlana Sheikh is telling us to be with people. You cannot be out of people. If somebody wants something, you give him. If somebody give you something you take. Don't say it's too much, don't say it's too little. Don't say that you ask too much or you ask too little. It is up to the manifestation. Whatever's coming it's ok. Whatever going it's ok. This is how Mawlana Sheikh treated us. And this is how Mawlana S. asking all murids to treat others. He want you to be humble. When we've been in Mawlana Sheikh he was all the time saying "kiss his hand, kiss his hand..." I'm saying when you look at it, why I kiss his hand? You ask why I kiss his hand? This is to make you feel, for you to be humble for him, to be humble for your Lord.

Because your Lord He loves His servants except this the poor proud one! This is Allah Almighty He is so Generous and He likes the generous ones. Allah Almighty He is so humble to us, that He still with all our mistakes He still gives us oxygen, He still gives us water, He still gives us food. He still gives us the Mercy of Him. He makes us to feel, and some people they don't feel. Even those He continues to give. He doesn't say cut, cut, cut. Everything is in His hand. This is today's gift from Allah Almighty that at least we try to be poor. Maybe we try to be rich and humble is much better. So everybody must be rich and humble. Amin. For this occasion of Eid, and today it is all the requests almost 90% being accepted.

And insha'Allah because today's manifestation is so high, yesterday and today manifestation it is Heavenly manifestation and all the dua and requests we are hoping that it will not be refused. And we are asking from Allah Almighty to make the happiness for the heart of Ummata Muhammad and make the satisfaction for Ummata Muhammad. Cut off this silly wars that is going around the world. Because they say, you are slaughtering sheeps and animals, but nobody is saying, look they are slaughtering people. And nobody cares about slaughtering people and they are caring about some animals that have been created for this duty. And the human was not created to be slaughtered.

And, we are also reminding that Allah Almighty He Is Merciful, and we are asking for the people to be merciful for each other. We don't have any power, we don't have anything to say except, Ya Rabbi You are the Powerful One that You can teach humanity, and insha'Allah with Your blessings that Sayyidina Al Mahdi will come and save humanity. This is our only hope, and our only dua for this day insha'Allah.

May Allah bless you, and bless your families, and may Allah give mercy for your ancestors. And may Allah give mercy for us to be good people, to be good servants and to deserve to look for Rasulullah's face. Deserve to look at Sayyidina... to the face of Mawlana Sheikh, and deserve to be with them. We've been here and we are hoping to be after insha'Allah with them. May Allah make this Eid Al Adha mubarak for all. We start from our families and then we go for our big family, the murids, and then we go for the nations, and then we go for all the world to say that we are Alhamdulillah Muslims. We are congratulating everybody for this occasion and may Allah make this occasion to give inner peace for our hearts.

Wa min Allahi t-tawfiq Bi hurmati l-Habib, bi hurmati l-Fatiha.
