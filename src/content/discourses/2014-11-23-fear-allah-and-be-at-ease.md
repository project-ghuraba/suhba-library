Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Those who do not fear Allah (JJ) are faithless. Is Allah (JJ) to be feared? He is not to be feared. He is Arhamur Rahimin, but we need to frighten our ego. Our ego is so wild that if we do not frighten it, it does everything. It would do anything. Our ego is not ashamed, it does not become well-behaved, and it is never enough for it. That is why it needs to be frightened.

Allah (JJ) needs to be feared because everything is in His hands. There is no reason whatsoever to be frightened if you do what He says. If you do not, Allah is still Arhamur Rahimin, but you get your punishment. You would be contemptible in the world and suffer torment in the hereafter. Because Allah (JJ) rules with justice. If you bother someone out of the blue, Allah (JJ) would give that servant their servant’s right. And that servant would surely get their right. They would be contemptible, as we said, both in the world and they are due to suffer in the hereafter. If people think about this, there is no need for anything else. Fear Allah and be at ease. This is the highest wisdom.

There are many scholars who have read libraries full of books. There was a place we went to the other day. There were almost as many books as this wall. May Allah be content with him, he is a good scholar. What I am saying is not to make him look bad. I asked him, “Did you read these books.” “I did,” he said. He has read them all, Mashallah. He is a fine scholar. They put him in a good position too so he may issue fatwas and work in scholarship. But there are some who have read twice as much as him and who have no wisdom. Instead of benefiting, they do harm. May Allah (JJ) not make us from those people. If you did not learn anything, Allah (JJ) showed a couple of words: “Fear Allah, and be at ease.” You will surely be at ease. You would be at ease in the world and at ease in the hereafter.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq.

Al-Fatiha.
