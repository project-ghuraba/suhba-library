Mawlana Shaykh Mohamad Nazim al Haqqani an Nakshibendi 3 Rajab, 1432 A.H. Saturday, June 4, 2011 Translated from Arabic by: http://saltanat.org/ BismillahirRahmanirRahim Allahu Akbar Allahu Akbar, La ilaha illa Allah, Allahu Akbar Allahu Akbar, wa li’Llahi ‘l hamd. Thousands and thousands of praisings for the Master of Beginning and the End, the beloved Master of creation, Prophet of Allah Almighty, Sayyidina Muhammad (sas). May Allah increase his honor and lights and his happiness with his nation. Oh Allah Almighty bring clear his Shariah from east to west; and send us a righteous king so that we can be under his banner. Audhu Billahi min as Shaitani ‘r rajim, Bismillah ‘r Rahmani ‘r Rahim. The source of all goodness, Sayyid-al-bashar, Sayyidina Muhammad (sas) is the spring of all goodness. The source of all badness is shaitan. An honorable gentleman came to visit us from the high-level people of Kuwait, Mr. Yusuf. We are honored by his visit; but he is in a hurry to return even though the right of a guest is three days. There is a Turkish saying that a guest is never a burden on the host. At the same time also, the soul is not in the physical body of a human being forever. Therefore, the [complete] saying is that the guest is never a burden and the soul is not for the physical body forever. So when the guest arrives, do not be burdened because Allah Almighty sends provision for the guest 40 days before that guest arrives. Don’t be burdened, masha Allah. We have approximately 300 people eating and drinking here twice a day and it is never a burden. Why should it be, as the guest arrives with his provision? He is not taking what is provided for me, and I am not responsible to provide for him. Allah (jwa) sends their provision with them. Allah Almighty is responsible to send the provision for both of His servants (the host and the guest). Therefore, if Sayyid-Mister arrives, he has three days’ right over us to host him, at least three days; and he may stay as long as he likes. May Allah teach us good manners. Allah Almighty taught His beloved Prophet (sas); and Sayyidina Muhammad (sas) said, “Allah taught me the best of manners.” Manners come before knowledge. shaitan had knowledge but no manners. It became proud and arrogant with its knowledge and disobeyed its Lord. He granted you such knowledge and you tell him, “No, I won’t make sajdah to You.’? With all the knowledge you acquired, Oh rejected one, you never learned that Allah Almighty can never be disobeyed? shaitan committed a mistake, sinned with his bad manners and was thrown out. Prophet Adam (pbuh) also disobeyed; but he asked forgiveness, saying, “Oh our Lord, we have committed a sin.” But Iblis said, “You have deceived me, Oh Allah Almighty; and with that deception I disobeyed you.” Adam (pbuh) also disobeyed; but he said, “Oh our Lord I committed a sin. The mistake is on me”. But the devil put the responsibility of its sin on its Lord. Therefore, good manners comes before knowledge. Now the general public have no manners. They study and study and learn everything; but they never study and learn good manners. No one has good manners, no adab! Students have no manners. When Allah Almighty created the ego, He said to it, “Come!”, and the self went away. When Allah Almighty commanded it to ‘Go!,’ the ego came forward. It always opposed it’s Lord. When Allah Almighty asked, “Who are you?”, it replied, “I am me; You are You.” This the biggest curse of shaitan and those who follow the way of shaitan. The way of Truth is in opposition to the way of the shaitan. All the prophets are on the way of Truth, the ways of Truth; but shaitan is on the way of falsehood. We must learn. If we do not learn, our life takes on some of of hellfire. Even if we possess all of the world's treasures we have no spiritual chance. We are always in difficulty and heaviness because we are trapped. However, the person does not know he has trapped himself in the prison of the self or the prison of the devil. And people generally, nowadays, have trapped themselves in the prison of shaitan or the prison of the ego/self. Finished! This is the current situation from the east to west, north to south, desert or seas. All people are imprisoned; and they don’t know the way out. They say, ‘democracy.’ Democracy is the prison of the devil. There is no way out. They are changing one president for another. If one president leaves, say Mubarak, another one comes. If Ghaddafi leaves, another replaces him. Exert all your effort to free yourself from the devil’s prison. The ego is rushing, running to be imprisoned by the enemy of Allah Almighty. People, rich or poor, men or women, learned or uneducated, black or white, Europeans or Orientals, are all on the way to a blocked end, dead-end – all of them. And Allah Almighty is saying,

inna Hadha Siratun Mustaqimun. Siratun `Alayya Mustaqimun (15:41)

This is indeed the Way which will lead straight to Me. “This is My right way.” The straight way is one, and the Prophet (sas) pointed with a stick in his hand making a straight line saying, “This is the straight way”. However, on both sides are many intersecting lines; and at every intersection a shaitan is waiting, and calling, “Come to me. Come to me.” The claim of the Lebanese people is something, and that of the Syrians or Kuwaitis is something else. The Emiratis, Saudis, Yemenis each claim something different. Ghaddafi also has a different claim. Two of them can never agree on one line.

Jaza'an Wifaqan (78:26)

Reward proportioned (to their evil deeds). (Pickthall) Therefore, the punishment is according to their deeds, Mr. Yusuf. The one who wants to be in safety and security here and hereafter, and who wants to come to Judgement Day with an enlightened face must keep the way of Truth, the way that was sent by Allah Almighty and shown by his beloved Prophet (sas). Now we have everything; and what are we are asking? Our yearning and desire should be for our Lord to be happy with us, to accept us. If He is happy with us, all of creation will be happy and accepting of us. If Allah Almighty is not happy with us, everything may give harm to that servant; and no remedy or medicine may give benefit, outwardly or inwardly. Sayyid Yusuf came to us and gave us honor with his visit. Alhamduli’Llah, we are Muslims. We hope to be under the Islamic banner here and herafter, liwa’al hamd, the one that Almighty promised to be granted to the Prophet (sas) Alhamduli’Llah. Say shukruli’Llah (thanks to ALLAH Almighty). What is Allah Almighty saying?

wa qaleelum min 'ibaadiyash shakoor (34:13)

... few of My servants are thankful. Why? Why should we not be one of the thankful servants? Only a few servants are thankful ones. Why? We have been created as the most honored creation. What else do you need or ask? Do you wish to be from donkeys, or bears, or dogs? Or jackal? What do you wish or desire to be? Allah Almighty granted you an appearance that was not granted to any other creature. He dressed you with an honor that was not even granted to the angels. Why are you not thanking your lord? Why are you complaining? And, He is saying, “Very few of My servants are thankful”. Oh servants of the Lord, these words are under the heading, Religion is Based on Advice. This gentleman is asking about the situation of the world, of the people in Kuwait, and the rest of the Arab world. All, or the majority of them, are competing to raise high buildings. Competing in construction of high buildings is to claim, “I am richer. He is less wealthy than me. My building is higher”. This is one of the signs of the last hour, when nations will compete to build higher and more luxurious buildings. What is the need to build higher, richer buildings? The government in Hijaz or wealthy families in Hijaz say, “We want to construct a building 1500 meters high”. What madness! Why are they going to construct such a building? The Prophet (s) said, “The sign of the end of times, of Judgement Day, is to construct high buildings”. When a servant wants to build higher than two stories, the angels address him saying, “Oh soldier of Nimrod, to where do you want to go? Is the earth not enough for you?” Two floors are enough. Are you competing with Nimrod? Why are you going beyond two floors? Then what about this 1500 meter building? The people became crazy. Oh Allah Almighty, do not make us from such people. If the person who owns such a building dies, what will they do with his body? They will wrap him in a sheet and a straw mat, and two or three servants may carry him down. They are afraid to carry him in a lift because people may find it a bad omen that there was a dead person in that lift. Therefore the servants take him down the fire escape stairs in the dark of night. What is your value now, oh man? That high building was yours. How did they take you down? - secretly down the fire escape wrapped in a straw mat? And, then they secretly rush you to the morgue. Oh people with sound hearts, take your wisdom. Our brother came to visit us. We were extremely happy. He came from a far distance, by plane it is about four hours to reach us. Maybe there is wisdom. He came to hear such words because the people in Kuwait are very eager to collect more money and they are never spending in the way of Allah Almighty. Only (what do you say in English...?) - they have a target, an aim.Their aim is to collect and collect and save and save and that’s it. They are famous for their extreme wealth but they only collect and no one takes benefit from that wealth, not them, not others. If these words reach them, Allah Almighty said,

wa- anfiqo fe sabel 'allaah (2:195)

... spend your wealth for the cause of Allah. Allah Almighty said, “Spend in the way of Allah”. Sadaq Allahul Adhim. The Prophet (s) said, “Spend without saying ‘no’, and don’t fear from the Lord of the Throne that your wealth will decrease or become less.” The Prophet (sas) said, “Start with sadaqa (charity) because hardship cannot cross over charity”. What is the smallest coin in Kuwaiti currency? Fils. The dinar is 1000 fils. So even one fils in charity protects you from hardship. So at the time you are leaving your house, rush to give at least one fils. Even if you can’t find someone to give that charity to, put a box in your house and [put some sadaqa into it] before you come out of the door. Give at least one fils, because hardship and suffering can never approach you or cross over that charity. This is it. Do you understand? Many troubles may come on their heads. For what? The Prophet (sas) said, “Spend! Spend! Don’t say ‘no’ to the person asking you for charity”. Bila ‘la,’ meaning no, or Bilalah, the companion of the Prophet (s.) “And do not fear from the Lord of the Throne to take from your wealth or for your wealth to become less.” And if you put one fils in the box, don’t think your wealth has become less or decreased. No! Allah may replace that charity and his Divine Order is: “Spend in the way of Allah Almighty”. This is enough for us. Mr. Yusuf: My sister was asking that Maulana may make a prayer for the people of Kuwait to find guidance, to be well guided. This association came exactly for the same purpose. All support is from Allah Almighty. May He open our hearts and keep us safe here and hereafter, for the sake of the one on whom He sent, Surat-al-Fatiha. People here, maybe 500, eating and drinking three times a day. He is the One Who is making their provision. Anyone arrives here they find their food and drink. There is one oven we built over there we have a sack of 10-20 kilos of flour everyday, Glory to Allah Almighty. Isn’t it true? Yes! But people never realize this fact and think it is a burden to host a guest. May Allah widen our chests, open our brains, open our hands in charity, until we increase to be called ‘big spenders,’ to be counted among the charitable. Don’t fear from the Lord of the Throne that your wealth may decrease. This lack of charity from the leaders of Egypt, Tunis, Libya, Yemen, Baghdad, Turkey and others, became the biggest curse for them. Ya Hu, give! Allah will give. May Allah forgive us. Welcome to you, what can we do? Arrange for him a box of oranges and lemons - maybe a little, little one so that he can carry it. Maybe someone else can carry it. It is better, because he is the government. [Mevlana acting:] “Why are you asking shiekh? I am a government.” Don’t open your mouth! Allah may grant you health, you are welcome to come any time you like. Insha Allah he is never seeing any sadness or sorrow. Our greeting and salam to your sister Doctor Maryam, your brothers, your mother and your children and everyone. I may stand up. Welcome to you. Take him together.
