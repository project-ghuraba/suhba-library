Dastur Ya Sayyidi, madad Ya Rijal Allah La ilaha illa Allah La ilaha illa Allah La ilaha illa Allah Sayyidina Muhammadun Rasulullah (sas) Allahumma salli wa sallim wa barik alayh da'iman abadan. Shafa`at Ya Rasulullah (sas) Madad, Ya Rijal Allah. Madad Ya Ashab an-Nawbah, madad Hu. Ya Qutub az-Zaman. Madad!

Assalamu alaykum our Listeners. We are seeing humble people and whoever is attending is hearing some heavenly news or heavenly declaration or what Allah Almighty is sending to His Prophets (as). Humble ones, humble ones. Absolute greatness is for our Creator for Allah Almighty. You are Ya Rabb, Allah. You are Akbar, Ya Rabbuna, You are Akbar al Akbar and we are weakest servants. We are asking your forgiveness.

O our Lord, grant us much more understanding. The Lord of Creation is only Lord, no one can be Lord. Everyone is His servant, His creature. How they are asking Lordship? Lordship is only for the One Who is creating and granting endlessly from His endless Grant Oceans. If the Lord of Creation asks an atom to give for everything in existence what they may need from those Grant Oceans, His Order only is to say, "Do this", and that smallest atom in Creation may do. Allahu al Qadir, al Muqtadir, Allahu Akbar.

O our Lord, grant us an understanding. If You Almighty order it to be granted, everything is coming into existence. O our Lord, forgive us and give much more from Your Divinity and Your Divine Glory, for Your most exceptional, most precious one, Sayyidina Muhammad (sas). That is an atom from Divinity, from Divine Oceans covering all Creation. Subhanallah, Sultan Allah. O people, don't be heedless. The Lord of Creation never likes heedless ones. Heedless ones are those that are losing their ways, or they are losing hikmat al-Ijaad, real wisdom for Creation. No one can know it from the beginning up to the end.

O People, I am a weak servant and I think you also are weak ones. Whoever is claiming, 'I am powerful one', is not true. Yes? Subhanallah, Glory, Absolute Glory for our Lord Allah Almighty. And we are saying, assalamu alaykum Attenders. Give your attention. You must give attention for understanding something from Divinity. Divinity -no one can be there. Divinity is for the Lord of Creation, for the Lord of Creatures. No one can be there. That belongs to the Lord of Creation from the Beginning up to the End. One Lord. And we are saying to you O Attenders, may heavenly protection be on you. Anyone escaping and not attending and making some excuse, that one is losing. There can't be anything more valuable for a person than sitting and looking at what heavenly breathing is coming on earth from heavens and he should find refreshment through himself. That refreshment is making a person to be in Happiness Oceans, Enjoyment Oceans and Pleasure Oceans. To be, to be, to be... So that our words can't reach to say what is coming from heavenly beings on ourselves. Blame on Mankind that they are losing that chance for understanding through heavenly beings. No one knows about heavens, where they are beginning, where they are ending.

Assalamu alaykum to those coming and sitting and hearing and trying to understand and give the most high respect even for one word that belongs to the Lord of Heavens. Tooba lahum -Good tidings endlessly. O People we may say: Audhu Billahi min as-shaytan ar-rajim. Only shaytan is making people not to come, not to attend, not to listen, not to understand.

Inna alshaytana lakum aduwwun No doubt, Satan is your enemy (35:6).

O our Salafi ulamas marhaban! Why you are not giving a daily declaration. You have so many stations for addressing people. You are saying, 'We are Salafi ulamas'. Yes, okay you may be, but what you are bringing to us from Salafi ulamas? Which words are you bringing? Were Salafi ulamas not saying, astaidh Billah, Inna alshaytana lakum aduwwun. Saying or not? Why you are not giving daily warning to all nations and Muslims, Muslim World? Why you are not saying to them, "O People O Muslim World, the Lord of Heavens saying:

W`atasimu bihabli Allahi jami`an And hold fast together, all of you to Allah’s rope (3:103).

Where are your representatives here in Cyprus, Turkey, Persia, or in the east or west to declare, O People: Inna alshaytana lakum aduwwun? No doubt, Shaytan is the most terrible and the most dangerous enemy for Mankind. You must put a station through east and west twentyfour hours to say to all nations, not only addressing to me, if not that person is going to be m`atuh (idiot). Idiot is one who is not declaring. I am not idiot. M`atuh is if a person gets older and begins to lose his mind and his thinking coming from up, down. But that is only for people who are following shaytan.

Those who follow the Lord of Heavens, Allah Almighty! Stand up! Even Malik! Even kings, even emperors must stand up when I am saying, "Allah!" If we never sit all our lives and stand for our Creator's, our Lord's holiest name, it is nothing. Don't (even) say once that that person stands up when saying 'Allah'. For whom am I going to stand up? Khalifa from Sham, Damascus was standing. Perhaps you are reading that in books but if we are repeating something, it makes it firm. For everything that we are saying our heavenly connection is giving to my heart and making me speak. Otherwise, I don't know. I don't know but some signs come.

I am asking Salafi ulamas, is something coming to you from heavens? Look up! "Where?" You are seeing? Why you are not seeing? So many, so much unexpected news with unexpected angels, like rain raining on people but they are closing their understanding. They are never giving even one moment to think, "Who am I?" And for what I am, or for whom I am? Who is bringing me into existence? And who is taking me from existence to non-existence. Yes?

Salafi ulamas, what you are saying? Say! You have so many stations, and satellites. Why not send these waves to all people. O People, ask your selves, "Who am I?" Or who is bringing me on this planet? Who is making this? Why you are not trying to wake up all nations? This is a question the Lord of Creation granted to all Prophets and finally the Seal of Prophets, Sayyidina Muhammad (sas). Every time you are standing up you are taking much more power. What is he saying about that question the Seal of Prophets, Sayyidina Muhammad (sas)? What he is saying? Ask Him! Salafi ulamas, ask. "To whom?" Don't ask me, ask your Prophet (sas). Who is your Prophet? That Prophet is at the same time my Prophet (sas). And he is a Prophet for all nations. Ask him, because he is as yet hearing, and seeing, and speaking.

Salafi ulamas, give your understanding from Holy Quran! And Allah Almighty is saying for Shuhuda, Martyrs, that:

Wala tahsabanna alladhina qutilu fee sabeeli Allahi amwatan bal ahyaun `inda rabbihim [Prophet] do not think of those who have been killed in the way of Allah as dead. (3:169)

Allah Almighty is saying for Shuhuda that they are really living, they are in real life and this life is imitated life. The Holy Quran says this for those who are Martyrs in battles, who raise the word of Allah and the flag of Tawhid. Real life has no beginning and no ending. Another question coming to our Salafi ulama brothers. You are accepting 'brothers'? I am saying you are my brothers, only you are taking money from government, I am not taking. Allah sending to me sadaqah, I am eating sadaqah, you are eating government's money. Doesn't matter. You are happy. My clothes, I am not changing everyday. But you are everyday dressing very good abaya. Putting on everyday this ghata and making...

Allahu Akbar, everyday I am looking to find my eyeglasses, but I can't find, only this I am looking through. I am looking at Salafi ulamas from a far distance. Do you believe that a person can look from east to west or from west to east? Are you believing or not? What are you saying Salafu's-Saleh? They were looking from east up to west, from west up to east. If you are saying 'No', it is not true for you. Salaf as-Saleh, their hearts were pure. Pure... Not corroded. There is something here you can't see. You must clean it. Polished. American, he forgets English also.

O Salafi ulamas, my respect to you, you can look from east and see up to west? Or from west up to east, what do you say? Hmm? Batil or Haqq? You can't say it is false because Salaf as-Saleh, Sayyidina Umar (ra) was on minbar and cut the khutbah and called, “Ya Sariyat -al jabal, Ya Sariyat al Jabal! He was seeing (Sariyah) and his sound reaching him.

You have such a power? Why you haven't? You must have it if you are claiming we are followers of Salaf as-Saleh. Bring your proof. I am not claiming that I am something. I am saying I am nothing, but you are saying "We are something". If you are something, say it, if there is a chance to speak about it. I don't know but they know and my soul is in connection with their souls.

Therefore what is coming from that endlessly is coming, to say. Don't think that I know something, no! But there is a way that is making my heart to wake up and is ordering my tongue to address you because you are putting yourselves in front of the Muslim World as well as the non-Muslim World. You must try to correct your understanding and to learn. Say, bring your proof, if you are saying 'We are followers of Salaf as-Saleh'.

Now who are Salaf as-Saleh? You are going to their tombs? No, you are not accepting to go to their tombs and to speak to them. How are you getting in connection with them? You have such a power through your hearts? Then, min al qalbi alal qalbi sabila, amanna wa saddaqna, from heart to heart, are some invisible ways that this from here without wires sending through, that knowledge. Why you are not reaching Salaf as-Saleh through your hearts? Hearts have written on them, 'Made by the Lord of Heavens'. Therefore O People always making me to speak, to address Salafi ulamas because they are claiming so that I have a right to ask from them to make clear what I am saying.

O People, leave this khalifat al-Muslimin, khalifat-al-Mu'minin and Amir al Mu'minin from Damascus, Sham. From Sham sending to people in Iraq. Always making this 'ghalabah', making these troubles, never-ending troubles from the times of Babylon Ashuriyeen, Qaldaniyeen, always troubles. La hawla wa la quwwata illa Billah, making trouble and Amir sending to them, to make order there. Sending Hajjaj Zalim. He went there alone saying, "Amir al-Mu'minin is appointing me governor on Iraq, I am here and saying, I like to address people here, in Basra's Jami al-Kabir, and he is standing on minbar of Rasul salawatullah wa salam alayh and using veil and sitting, saying to the servant/scribe, "Ya Ghulam, stand up and read the decree of Amir al Mu'minin". Get up and read that Amir al Mu'minin sent me here to be governor".

The scribe was standing up and saying, "Salams to the people of Basra from Amir al-Mu'mineen. Greetings to you, my salam to the people of Iraq". And not even one replied to his salam. Al Hajjaj was on minbar, he was saying, "Stop, Ya Ghulam, stop your reading to people Amir al Mu'minin's order. O people of Iraq you have such bad adab with you, worst manners with you. Amir al Mu'minin saying to you 'Assalamu alaykum' and no one standing up and saying 'Wa alaykum salam'? I am coming to teach you best manners. Amir al Mu'minin put in front of himself his arrows and was looking which one is bitter and looking, finding and sending me to you. I am going to train you best adab. Repeat it, Ya Ghulam". Ghulam, second time, not even a fly sitting there, everyone standing up! Standing up like this. That is it. I am asking to Salaf as-Saleh ulamas, do you think it was bad manners when the governor was ordering and people were standing up? Was it wrong? Munkar? And you are saying no one is giving respect for another one. Why? Allah (swt) saying, astaidh Billah,

wala tansawu alfadla baynakum And do not forget to be generous towards one another (2:237).

Ahl al-Fadl, are granted from Allah Almighty some grants and others must repect those ones. (Ahl al-Fadl) are granted from their Lord a good manner. When Jalalatul Malik in Hijaz enters, are you sitting or standing up? Standing up, because Allah dressed him in that honour. You are not honouring malik but Malik al Muluk. Yes sir! Understand. I am nothing, I am last one, deepest one, don't say this person is speaking, no. Who is making this weak and old person to speak, to address all people? And this addressing is from east to west. May Allah forgive us.

O people, always they are making me address them, because they are proud ones. They wonder at amazement. Sometimes I know Arabic also, doesn't matter. I know Arabic. Don't worry. May Allah forgive me and forgive you. The Malik should appreciate that the Lord of Heavens is giving that honor to him, Haramayn Sharifayn, free for all Muslims. I don't like also to be put there ta'shir-ma'shir (visa) -No!

Wa min Allahi tawfiq.

Assalamu alaykum. Fatiha.
