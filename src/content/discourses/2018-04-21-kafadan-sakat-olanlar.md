Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Eûzübillâhimine’ş-şeytâni’r-racim, Bismillâhirrahmânirrahim;

“Wa man yabtaghi ghayr al-islâmi dînan, fa lan yuqbala minhu” (Surat Aali ‘İmran – 85)

The ayah says, “And whosoever desires other than the submission (Islam) as a religion, it will never be accepted from him.”

The religion of Islam is the last religion sent down by Allah. All the good actions of those who follow it are acceptable to Allah. Such a one’s deeds cause his station to increase, and they are accepted by Allah.

Nowadays, it is a fashion to say, “I do not believe.” A so-called smart man says something, then many of them, children or adults, say, “I do not believe.” Later, they state that they do this or that. Why are you doing it? Can a good deed be done in vain? The deed will return to you as harm in the end. You harm others so that they do not have faith in Allah, so that they do not submit to a religion, or accept religion. You are no use to anyone. You are no use even to yourself, so how could you help anyone else? He himself is nothing but a harmer. What he eats is a harm; what he drinks is a harm; his walk is a harm; everything is a harm. However, a little deed of a believer receives a reward, and his deeds are acceptable.

Because it is End Days, the world has closed itself to people. Others do not let the people open their eyes. When their eyes are open, their eyes only open to wickedness or disbelief. Those people who think smart of themselves are either embarrassed, or do not accept at all. Then what happens? As we said earlier, harm is caused. All he might have read, searched, ate, drank--none give him any benefit but only harm.

May Allah give intellect to people. The intelligent one accepts the truth. The one who lacks intellect does not accept. Sometimes a crazy man shows a short temper--useless to talk to a such man all day. One knows that there is no need to talk or argue with such man who is in a temper--he knows nothing or accepts. He has no mind. Those who do not accept Allah are the same. Do not sit with them. No need to talk with such people, because they have no mind. The one who refuses the religion of Allah is an insane man.

May Allah protect us all--the children, Islam, Muslim children. May Allah protect the memorizers (huffaz of Quran). May our faith stay intact, insha’Allah. Wa min Allahi at-tawfiq Al-Fatiha
