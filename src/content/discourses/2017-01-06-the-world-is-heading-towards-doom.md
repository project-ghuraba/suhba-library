Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Those who do not believe in Allah are infidels. Allah Azza wa Jalla, He created everything. He created the day and He created the night. He also created the moon and He also created the year. He created the Universe. Allah’s creatures are plenty in the universe. Do not think Allah only created us and did not create anything afterwards. Hasha (far from it)! One of Allah’s attributes is Khallaq. It means the Creator. Allah (JJ) is continuously creating.

There is no time Allah is not creating. This is Allah’s attribute. For Him, let alone humans, the whole world is not even a speck of dust in the Universe. We said those who do not believe in Him become kafir. Allah Azza wa Jalla, there we see it, makes it rain whenever He wants and does not make it rain whenever He wants. When He does not make it rain, people can try for it to rain as much as they like. They can throw bombs and what not. They can do whatever they like and it will not rain.

Everything happens when Allah wills it. We see now, thank Allah it is also snowing. These clouds above us look like cotton, they look lighter than cotton. Not just streams but sees overflow through Allah’s power. You would think it weighs nothing but sees are overflowing in that sky.

We need to have faith in Allah’s ability. Who can do it but Allah? They can try as much as they like. When Allah Azza wa Jalla wants, He brings out such a wind for people to see they are helpless, and it mostly hits over there, to the state that says it is the strongest state in the world. These winds are messing up. Still they do not believe and while saying, “It was this and it was that,” they go [to the other side] without faith.

Allah Azza wa Jalla created everything beautifully, but everything is ruined now because it is End Times. And some of our scholars believe the nation that does not beleive in Allah saying, “There is still a long time till Doomsday.” They say, “It is not mentioned in the Koran. There is still a long time left till Doomsday.” Allah Azza wa Jalla says in the Koran: “Doomsday is near.” We cannot understand how they are saying there is still time left.

Bismillahir Rahmanir Rahim.

The holy verse says, “Thaharal fasadu filbarri walbahri bima kasabat aydin naas.” (Sura Rum:41) Sadaqallahul Athim. “Corruption has appeared throughout the land and sea because of what people themselves have done.” Everything is corrupt in the sea, on land and in the air. How did this happen? Humankind has ruined everything due to the mistakes they made themselves. They cannot breathe in this world. There are millions and billions of people. Who is the cause of this? Who did this and where did it come from? Allah Azza wa Jalla created it immaculately. He created air, land, water, and sea immacutaletly and people have ruined it. This is a punishment of Allah, but they will also suffer the punishment for what they did themselves.

Allah Azza wa Jalla is still being merciful on us. He makes it rain and He makes it snow. We need to not be grateful but thankful for this. When should we be grateful? If he does not make it rain, we are grateful to Allah in anycase. When He sends blessings and goodness we need to be thankful so that He may give more and His blessings on us are continuous.

We are thankful millions of times to Allah that while there is so much evil, so much tyranny, and so much perversion, He is still granting us these blessings. We thank him millions of times. Because people have truely become such that they are at the peak of rebellion against Allah. They have reached the peak, and leave accepting advice aside, you become guilty if you give advice. They can say whatever they want – not guilty – they can say every evil – not guilty – but if you command good you become guilty.

As we said, the end of the world is approaching, Doomsday is approaching. As we said in the beginning of the sohba, the end of Adam Alayhis Salam’s age is near. There are always those being created. Just as humans come to this world, it is not hard for Allah Azza wa Jalla to created new worlds and new humans. It is continuous. It is His own attribute.

Doomsday is a strange thing. They have poisoned the minds of people who do not believe in Doomsday anyway such that they do not believe in anything. They do not believe in the hereafter either. It has been thousands of years since Adam Alayhis Salam came to this world. How much longer will it be? Just as for a ship to sink it takes in water from all sides and it sinks, the world is the same now. They have neither left a clean place, nor have they left clean air, nor have they left drinkable water.

Everywhere, everyday they are saying, “This posion comes out of this material, that comes out of that, and people cannot find jobs.” They ask, “Where is the world heading?” The world is heading to Doom. People of old times would say, “We are riding on a sign, we are heading towards doom.” Olden people were smarter than us. Doomsday was always in their minds.

Allah has a promise: the whole world will become Muslim before Doomsday. First, people who think they are Muslim and everybody else are kafir, perverted factions, will be cleaned up. Afterwards the whole world will become Muslim. Afterwards Mahdi Alayhis Salam will come and Isa (Jesus) Alayhis Salam will also rule for forty years.

Isa Alayhis Salam will descend from the sky. Isa Alayhis Salam will himself clean off the Christians, those who do not believe. Because even when Isa Alayhis Salam arrives they will say, “No, it is not you!” When this is the case they will have no excuse left and Isa Alayhis Salam cleans them off. Isa will say, “Religion is Islam.”

Therefore, the whole world of unbelief, all perverted people who have left religion and gone astray are attacking Islam. They are all Shaitan’s soldiers and think they will finish it. No, it is near Allah willing. Mahdi Alayhis Salam will arrive and clean them all. Allah’s promise will be realized. For once, the whole world will become Muslim.

Again, humankind are creatures prone to corruption. Even after seeing so many karamat and miracles people will turn to disbelief again. Doomsday will erupt on them. It does not erupt on Muslims. Allah (JJ) will send Muslims a pleasent smell. He brings out something like a smoke, they hand over their souls, and then Doomsday erupts on the kafirs. When Israfil Alayhis Salam blows on the trumpet nothing is left alive in this world. When the time is right afterwards, in places related to this world, the second trumpet is blown and everyone gets up and goes to the place of the last judgment (mahshar).

May Allah resurrect us with our Holy Prophet (SAW) since that is what is important. These are tough days now. Really, it is not easy as our Holy Prophet (SAW) said but its rewards are that much more. So those living in these times and are on the right path are gaining so much rewards that even the companions were enviuous of them. They were envious saying, “They are gaining so many rewards.” The more the torment the more the rewards you gain.

May Allah protect us and Inshallah may we see the beautiful days with Mahdi Alayhis Salam. May we be with him, with his sohba, with Isa Alayhis Salam’s sohba, and be together Inshallah. May we serve Islam in safety with our children, all of us, in this country. Because truely this is Anatolia, full of awliya.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq. Al-Fatiha.
