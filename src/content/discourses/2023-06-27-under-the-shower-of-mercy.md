Labbayka Llāhumma labbayk. Labbayka lā sharīka laka labbayk. Inna l-ḥamda, wa-n-niʻmata, laka wa-l-mulk, lā sharīka lak. Allāhu akbar, Allāhu akbar, lā ilāha illā Llāhu, wa-Llāhu akbar. Allāhu akbar, wa-liLlāhi l-ḥamd.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, this Day of ‘Arafah is a blessed day. In shā’a Llāh, may its reward be written for you and for us, in shā’a Llāh. This beautiful and blessed time now is the time when Ḥajjīs stand at ‘Arafāt. Ḥajj makes people virtuous and is a means to all kinds of goodness. Therefore, to perform Ḥajj is farḍ (obligatory). Farḍ are things that are of great benefit to people. They are kinds of worship that are beneficial in a person’s life. Among the kinds of worship, the farḍ are works that have great benefits.

The works of dunyā are without value. The works of ākhirah are beneficial and true works. How lucky are those who can do them. It is a pity for those who cannot do them, and those who have the opportunity and do not do them. What a pity. It is a pity for the person who does not do them, meaning for those who neglect them and do not do them even though they could. But of course, the situation of the world now does not allow it. In old times, going to Ḥajj was a great burden. Few people could go. They were also of benefit for themselves – and others also. In the present time it is easier, but this time there are other obstacles.

So the worship of Ḥajj is determined by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, "Wa-liLlāhi ‘alá n-nāsi ḥijju l-bayti mani-staṭā‘a ilayhi sabīla" (3:97). Ḥajj is obligatory for everyone who is able to find a way, has money and health. Shukr to AllāhNone, HeNone didn’t say that you must go in all cases. When you have the opportunity, when the ways are opened, when you have a visa – now this issue has come up, and it has become an obstacle. Some people in old times used to be able to go after five or six years. Even some could go after ten years, they say. Now there are some people who have been waiting for 15 years. They are waiting because the permit hasn't been issued yet, the visa has not been issued. This is another obstacle. This is also by Allāh’sNone wisdom. Therefore, when someone has the opportunity, he should perform this worship. He should certainly do it, for the greatest benefit is that he will be cleansed of all his sins. Whatever burdens he has and sins he has done, he gets rid of them. It is like being born again by his mother. He comes without any sin, by Allāh’sNone permission. This is an important thing.

Of course, if there is a servant’s right [that he has transgressed] that is another matter. He needs to be forgiven [by them], just as Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla forgives and cleans all the rights that belong to Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Our Prophet ṣallá Llahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says that whoever is not on Ḥajj but fasts today, the Day of ‘Arafah, the sins of the past year and the year ahead are forgiven; which means two years. The rights of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla from worship and other things, all of the sins done, are forgiven. And that is not including the servants’ rights. This is also a great mercy from AllāhNone and a grace from AllāhNone to people. If it is according to mankind’s own mind, people will not forgive the things done. Which means they do not forgive the things done to them. But the word of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is true and the word of our Holy ProphetNone is true. All sins are forgiven.

But, in shā’a Llāh, we must also repent and not return to those sins. Sins are not a good thing. They are a bad thing. Even if forgiven, not doing them again is a great goodness for people. Committing sins is bad. People do goodness to themselves, and people do badness to themselves. The biggest enemy for mankind is his own ego. The badness done by their egos is forgiven, in shā’a Llāh, for those who have fasted and those who went to Ḥajj. But they shouldn't do it a second time. He must control his ego and not do all of them at least. People forbid themselves from some of them, they control their egos. Sin is something that has no goodness at all. It has a lot of evil, and no goodness at all. But still, this opportunity exists because, by Allāh's, ‘Azza wa-Jalla, mercy, the Door of Repentance is not closed. The meaning of the āyah: O people who do badness, O people who commit sins, O people who do all kinds of inappropriate things, do not despair of Allāh’sNone mercy. AllāhNone forgives all sins. Therefore, the person who is a Muslim and does not leave faith, does not leave Islām and does not go into kufr – AllāhNone forgives him for all kinds of sins he has done. And he will take care of the servant’s right by himself.

So today is a blessed day. May AllāhNone grant us all a good life with īmān and Islām, in shā’a Llāh, with barakah and all kinds of goodness. May HeNone make easy what is good for us. May we make our lives according to Allāh’sNone pleasure, in shā’a Llāh. Today, these hours until the evening are the most blessed time. It is one of the most blessed days of the year. At least recite a thousand Ikhlāṣ, ṣalawāt, tahlīl, takbīr, Qur’ān, whatever you can do. Make du‘ā’ also. AllāhNone accepts them, in shā’a Llāh. May AllāhNone bless you. May AllāhNone make us reach better years. May we be together with Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām, Ṣāḥibu z-Zamān, in shā’a Llāh.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are now in very blessed time and day of ‘Arafah. And now they have Ḥajjī pilgrimage. They are in Jabal ‘Arafāt which it is one day in the year – one day and very special day; not one day any day from year. This time which Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla accept the, the from people their obligatory fifth of Islām pillar – Ḥajj. Ḥajj, it is very good, very nice worship, very benefit for people who are going there. Because this make them under shower of mercy, shower of bless, shower of every good thing from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. In this area, this time, and this place, special place which it is limited and there is sign from where begin from it to where end. They must be, people who going there to be Ḥajjī, in this day to be there until maghrib.

In this time, AllāhNone bless them and AllāhNone forgive them from everything they done in their life according for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla right[s]. But for human being['s] right[s], of course, there is also maybe AllāhNone forgive them. But it is very important for believer to get this benefit, which it is real benefit – not like benefit of world, material world – for it is forever. Because who can do this and he done it, he chosen one from time of Ibrāhīm ‘alayhi s-salām when he call for people for Ḥajj. And you must, it is not easy. You can say like, "We go to Ḥajj." But from even old time there was difficulty to go to Ḥajj because to go there, need many thing special. You must be – have enough money. You have to be maybe healthy. You must be afford your family after you to come, and to be way also safe. Because old time also many time they attacking this caravan of Ḥajjī and they killing them before they arrive to Ḥajj.

So it was difficult that time, so not everybody can go to Ḥajj. For this AllāhNone, for mercy of AllāhNone, HeNone said, "Wa-liLlāhi ‘alá n-nāsi ḥijju l-bayti mani-staṭā‘a ilayhi sabīla" (3:97). Ḥajj obligatory for who can afford this and to can go there. Who can afford and he not going, pity for him because he lose what thing he cannot imagine, what he lose from barakah, bless, and reward from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Every obligatory AllāhNone gave us, for Muslim, for believer, it is, they have the most extremely benefit for you. Obligatory, farḍ – for praying, for fasting, for zakāh, for Ḥajj also. All this benefit coming for you, more than other. AllāhNone not in need for your anything from this. They, AllāhNone from HisNone mercy, HeNone make this obligatory for you to give, get benefit here and hereafter. But people, they're not giving, many people not giving important for this and they say, "Later, later." But later you can't, you don't know what will happen – like these days.

Before thirty year, everybody can go to Ḥajj. No problem. You pay money, you can go and not so expensive. Many people they didn't go. After they make too many people. They make lottery to take little, some people, not all of people. Now, I hear there are people, they are waiting since thirteen or fifteen year for Ḥajj, but not cannot go. Even they still not, lottery not come for them. For this, AllāhNone from HisNone bless, HeNone said, "Who can afford this." But there is something you cannot afford when not giving visa, not giving way for Ḥajj. For this, you must make it intention every time so AllāhNone will reward you as you are Ḥajj every year, in shā’a Llāh, because you cannot – you waiting for this but you cannot go. But AllāhNone also, not leaving this without reward – reward and accept your obligatory thing, Ḥajj you've done. This is very good.

And today also, ‘Arafāt for who are not in Ḥajj, who is fasting, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, HeNone, AllāhNone will forgive him for one, this year and another year also. So, HeNone forgive him for two years for everything he done according to law of AllāhNone, right of AllāhNone, HeNone forgive him. For people, right of people, must ask from people to forgive him, to make what their problem with them – to finish this problem. Everything, AllāhNone saying, Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥim. "Qul yā-‘ibādiya lladhīna asrafū ‘alá anfusihim lā taqnaṭū min raḥmati Llāh" (39:53). Meaning: Who done very bad thing, very bad sin, don't... When you are in Islām – you not going to kufr, not saying, "We are not accept this religion and this," not going outside of Islām – AllāhNone forgive everything. For this what you done in this year, you must ask for forgiveness and AllāhNone, HeNone forgive you. Don't think you will be asked for this. This is word of AllāhNone and Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, HeNone promised for this.

But of course, to make sin is not good. Not good at all. To ask forgiveness, it is good for you. So you take all this what you done from dirty thing, from everything, smoking, drinking, other thing what they do you do, when you ask for... and AllāhNone forgive you. Don't think it is not. Because today I hear something, from people, they was doing whole... Every time I was very angry for this people. They are doing bad thing, they doing sin. But today someone saying, this people, today they are fasting. So I was very happy to hear this. Because, in shā’a Llāh, not coming back to jumping on this dirtiness again. Now become clean. AllāhNone make us, all of them, clean from our sins, the dirty of sin and to be all time clean, in shā’a Llāh. Because sin, to make sin, it is heavy. To be clean, it is light, give you happiness, give you every good thing. For this, we are asking for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, to make us to control our ego, not running after this dirty thing, in shā’a Llāh.

From this holy day, and asking for forgiveness from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, to give us strong īmān, to give us love for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, for ProphetNone, for Awliyā’u Llāh, for Ṣaḥābah, for Ahlu l-Bayt, for all believer, for all mu’min, to make us to be hidāyah (guidance) for other also – to give us from HisNone endless treasure, to not to be in need to anybody, rizq ḥalāl (clean livelihood) and long life with happiness and to reach Sayyidunā l-Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām to find this nice, very good days, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
