Assalat wa al salamu ala Rasulina Muhammad Sayyidi l Awwalin wa l-Akhirin. Dastur ya Sayyidi, dastur ya Shaykh Muhammad Nazim al Haqqani. Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fi jamia' Our way is through association & blessing is in the gathering.

This is method of the Tariqat - to bring together in jamaat, a good jamaat: To save people from the hands of shaytan and for them to follow the jamaat- to follow the Shaykh, the guide. Mawlana Bahauddin Naqshband Hz would say this in every sohbat of his. This is because, who doesn't follow a jamaat, a Shaykh, or doesn't follow a jamaat in his location, hangs out with bad friends. And bad friends are worse than shaytan. One can handle shaytan & his waswas in some way but this bad friend sticks to him, doesn't let go. And leads you to destruction, in dunya & in akhirat.

Our Prophet (sas) said: "You will be divided into many sects. Follow the greater majority". Of course, the majority is Ahlu Sunnah Wa al Jamaat. Follow them, they are steady,with Allah's permission. The evils of bad friends/company... It causes problems-mischief among the family-he doesn't listen to his mother&father. If he is married & ends up with bad company, it leads him to all kind of disgrace. He separates from his family & his children become miserable.

If he is a small boy, he falls into bad ways- ways that Allah doesn't want. Therefore, the benefit of Tariqat is to bring people together like this. Mankind, can fall into emptiness - he for sure, needs a person to be with him. If it is a good person, he is a lucky one. If he ends up in bad company.. God forbid.

Today we speak about, in Tariqat first thing. Shaykh Bahauddin Naqshbandi saying to sohbat first: Tariqatuna as sohbah- sohbat meaning giving lecture, lessons. Wa l-khayri fi jamia'- Goodness in, to be together.

This is big thought when you think about it. Because when you are not coming together, so bad friends, they can take you away from right way. And when you go away from right way, you're going to finish, you disappear and you lose everything. Especially for akhirat, you lose everything. In dunya also, if some bad friend with you they can make you separate from your family, your father, mother.. Or you separate from wife if you have bad friend. They take you every bad way. And bad way, their ego likes. Because they think it is, look like very nice. But when you finish, it will be shaytan be happy. And this bad friend, he is worse than shaytan. Shaytan, you can't see him. But this bad friend, they stuck on you. They not leave you anywhere. We see many times, many people run from one country to another country, also not finish from this problem. So, to finish from this problem you must come to tariqat. Because they keep you away from shaytan and from bad friends. Allah help us & keep us away from this bad ego, bad friend, shaytan and Insha Allah friend,our children, our family, all of them insha Allah (too). Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, Al Fatiha.
