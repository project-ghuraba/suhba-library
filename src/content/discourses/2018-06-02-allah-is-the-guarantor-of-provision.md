As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa raḥmatu Llāhi wa barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīn wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

AllāhNone created us so we may be HisNone servants as a duty. HeNone created us as servants. HeNone is the guarantor of our sustenance: "Your sustenance is with MeNone. You do your servanthood. Obey MeNone. Turn to MeNone. Let your hearts be with MeNone," says Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla. AllāhNone is the guarantor of your sustenance and livelihood. HeNone will send it to you. Some of us become farmers, some become shepherds, some become doctors, and some become drivers. AllāhNone has shown everyone a way for their livelihood. We should not forget AllāhNone for that. Sustenance comes from AllāhNone. HeNone sends you your sustenance. HeNone makes you work and HeNone gave you a specialty accordingly. Your sustenance will come like that.

You should not run after sustenance and work and forget AllāhNone: "We are working. We cannot pray. Because we are working we are getting tired." It cannot be. That work of yours is to strengthen your body so you may worship AllāhNone anyway. Not for work. Work is not the goal. It is the means. The real goal is Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla. "We cannot fast. We are working." That is not all right either. Your fasting is worth your whole lifetime. The lifetime that passes without ever fasting is not worth five pennies. The lifetime that passes without prayer is also not worth a penny. What is important, as we said, is that AllāhNone grants the work for you. The man might be a professor, but he is unable to do the work you do. AllāhNone gave that specialty to you. You will do that job and earn money from it. HeNone gave the other one another talent. He will do that. He will also earn his money and sustenance.

Sustenance can be permissible or forbidden. The permissible is of course the one that is for AllāhNone, and the forbidden is the one that does not have Allāh'sNone consent. Therefore, we need to be careful. We did not come to the world for dunyā. We came for AllāhNone. That is our real goal. We should not forget AllāhNone for dunyā. We need to always remember AllāhNone and make dhikr of HimNone. HeNone should never leave our heart. It might not be through our tongue, but HeNone should be in our heart. HisNone being in our heart occurs with the worship we do with our limbs. The one who has nothing in his heart has no worship and no thought. He considers himself from the four-legged class and roams around. As we said, he dies with an empty life, a useless life, and a worthless life. Whereas, if he had a little bit of love for AllāhNone, it would add value to his life and it would be worthy. You are worthy with AllāhNone.

When you are not with AllāhNone, your position can rise as much as you like, and you might think you are something, but you have no value. Your value in the end is you will be thrown in a hole and you will rot there and be gone. That is your value and there is nothing else. However, those who are with AllāhNone are worthy. They will be treated with respect and honor in the sight of AllāhNone. Due to the good they have done, AllāhNone grants them paradise, and grants them the highest stations. However, as we said, this life might change in an instant if you repent and ask for forgiveness. AllāhNone accepts your repentance. HeNone replaces the sins with good deeds. So we should not despair from AllāhNone.

Therefore, let us not forget AllāhNone, in shā’a Llāh. May AllāhNone always be in our heart.

We are, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are living in this life and this life it is, AllāhNone give us for remembering HimNone, to worshipping HimNone, to do what HeNone like to do. Our main aim - pleasure of AllāhNone, to HeNone be pleasure with us. This is our first thing. But we are in this life. AllāhNone create us to worship HimNone and HeNone is guarantor for our provision, rizq, provision. Our provision from AllāhNone so we must not be running away from AllāhNone after provision.

With provision AllāhNone He guarantee for us. Everybody, everyone [HeNone] give him special, speciality to get his provision. Some of them farmer, some of them doctor, some of them driver, some of them engineer, some of them cleaner. There is thousands of jobs and AllāhNone give this people from this job their provision. Who like follow and asking from AllāhNone. AllāhNone He guarantor with provision. HeNone give him from His ḥalāl clean money to continue his life and to continue worshipping for AllāhNone - fasting, doing everything good from AllāhNone be happy with this. But if other people they are saying, "Oh you pray?" "No I have work I cannot pray because I'm working." "Do you fast?" "No I cannot fast because I also I have work. How can I fast in this hot weather? I cannot because I'm working." But here the main aim not work. This people they are not thinking.

They are thought they are coming for this life for working or for eating, drinking, like animal. Animal also only they are whole time doing same thing - not praying, not fasting. All time they are, AllāhNone create them like this, for this purpose. But our human being AllāhNone create them for purpose of to be worshipping HimNone. This is main aim and people not caring about this, only they are tiring themself and make difficulty from, for themself running after dunyā, after provision. And they are, they are not looking for provision it is clean or not clean. Ḥalāl, ḥarām they not looking at all. Just they want money they can, how they can bring, and this is not good and AllāhNone not happy with this. AllāhNone happy with people who depend on HimNone and saying, "We everything coming from AllāhNone. We want from AllāhNone." And AllāhNone He will be happy with them.

Other people difficult for them but also AllāhNone accept to from people to be ask forgiveness from HimNone. If they asking and they whole life they are doing bad thing, AllāhNone accept and change this to good - rewarding them. AllāhNone He is the most Merciful. HeNone is giving for provision for everybody - for believer and nonbeliever because this dunyā, this world or this universe it is not, don't have any value in divinely presence of AllāhNone. For this HeNone is giving for believer and nonbeliever and this is also we must try to imitate our Lord to not give any value for dunyā. Only for Ākhirah it is important because Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla saying, "If this world, universe, world it has any value like mosquito wing, I will never give one drop water for nonbeliever. But it is no, not impo... not... No value for this. So for this I'm giving this people provision and giving everything."

So be careful. Don't be give value for this dunyā. Give value for our LordNone, for HisNone happiness. If HeNone is happy with us, we are happy, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone give, make Him, AllāhNone all time, in shā’a Llāh, HeNone be happy with us, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
