Sheikh Bahauddin Adil al-Haqqani sobhat of the 30th of July, 2014.

MashaAllah again we are eating our time. I think everything must have beginning, and everything must have an ending. If you don't know your beginning and you cannot reach your ending, you will be lost. We know that Allah Almighty He put for everything a beginning and He put for everything an ending. Ya'ni! They say this planet has the age of millions of years. Nobody knows but, they are searching figuring out and calculating and they put.

They say "this planet has 7.829.000 and 2 days of age. Do you think you will count all this? Any number you put, it's ok! Because you have the title of professor, Doctor and specialist with the age of earth. So this is proof that you can say this planet has the age of 7.829.000 and several days. Alhamdulillah! We're accepting, ok no problem. We are not arguing for the age of the earth. But we say "there is a beginning?" They say "Yes! "There is a beginning." So this is a very important thing to understand. There is a beginning. Nobody denied the beginning. They say "yes, beginning. "So if you have a beginning you must have an end. "No, no, no! This world will go another 7.000.000." Yahu! Maybe it's going 70 million, or 100 million it doesn't matter, but what is your beginning and what is your end, this is important.

If you can put yourself in this huge beginning and ending that you have a small beginning and ending, then you are in the safe side. Don't look for the big picture. You never know if after 1 hour you are here or not. You never know. After 1 minute, you never know. After 100 years, you never know. But this, you have to know that in this big beginning and ending, you have a small share that you have a beginning and ending. So, when you have this beginning or ending, you have to concentrate in one thing: to put you in the safe area. Because when you look at the picture, the big scale - sometime you go for a movie and you see the future in movies and you are in an age, let's say my age 50s and the movie will be after 2250. And you say, where am I there? You'll be existing there? You are giving me imagination for future. Or maybe you go for a very historical movie and you say "If I would have been there could I survive here also?"

Allah Almighty He put us in the exact place, in exact moment, and exact share of our life time. You believe in beginning and you believe in ending. That's why we have to believe in our beginning and we have to believe in our ending. Now, we must be in the safe side. We have to enjoy our moment in this share time. How? So many people they like to get drama in their life. Yahoo! You have to realize it is a very short time, there is no time for drama! You have to realize it is a very short time, there is no place for drama. Because if you put yourself in drama, and then you end in a big drama. You say "Ya Rabbi I didn't do anything I was busy with drama!" Also, dangerous! And then don't be so happy, also you forget so many duties in your life, serious duties. Don't take it as a joke or don't take it as - how to say? - do not hesitate or do not to do things. Also it will be ending and say "Allah, Allah! The time run, I didn't do anything."

Allah Almighty He is telling you very simple "make the balance in your life." How to make the balance? We are living in this world, of course we have drama. We have happy moments. We have joking moments. We have serious moments. We've resting moment. Even the resting they say, don't rest too much, you'll be lazy." It is our time share, it should be valuable time share. Allah Almighty He gives you of course all the feeling. So He tells you to enjoy all your feeling. How to enjoy all you're feeling? It is so simple, when you have too much drama you have to close the door of drama and you go for happy side of your life. And then when you so many happy things in your life, you have to go for neutral side of your life. And then you have neutral side of your life you go for worshipping to Allah Almighty for your life. So when you make combination with everything, you will have a very nice flavour of your life, of your time share. And when we end up with our time we say "Ya Rabbi, we used everything in our life."None It is important to use everything in your life. Allah Almighty why did He provide you all this? To use everything in your life. Because if you don't use everything in your life, you will be missing something in your life. My mother she was telling me, we went to Hajj with her and she was saying, "don't waste your time with waiting somebody to come with you or go with you. You just memorize the Kaaba and you go and do your duty by yourself, don't wait for anybody." Our problem is, we are waiting for some- body. And at the end of the day when we are finding that somebody he says "I'm already done." "I was waiting for you!" "How come?" "I was waiting" No, this is free to run! To go ahead, don't wait. Just this is your life.

The life has been given to individual person, the life wasn't given... we are public. We are together, but we have individual life. We are making community together, but at the end of time we have individual lives. We have individual things. Allah Almighty He proved His Oneness by making everybody individual. Maybe we look like each other, but we are individual. And this is the sign of Allah Almighty's Oneness. One! Ahad! That's why we have to use our life for very useful and maximize our usefulness to our life and that will make Allah Almighty happy with us. If you are making trade, make it the best. If you are teaching make it the best. If you are student make it the best. If you are sportsman, make it the best. If you are professional doctor, make it the best. Because Allah Almighty He likes to see His servants shining.

He doesn't like servants to be in dark city. That's why in many communities they don't like the alcoholic ones. They don't like the drug addicted ones. The community doesn't like them. They want to help them, but they don't like them because their behaviour is not good. It is not shining. It is dark, it is disgusting. It is not good, even the people. So what you imagine Allah Almighty He likes His servants to be shining in any place, any place. Allah Almighty He created us as His servants and He wants us to be shining - Shining here and hereafter. And we are hoping with the teaching of Mawlana S. with the guiding of Malwana S. we will be shining here and hereafter.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfiq and Eid Mubarak for all of you. And may Allah makes this Eid coming again, with good health, more successful in our life, in our family, in our children, and in our community.

Wa min Allahi t-tawfiq, bi hurmati l-Habib, bi hurmati l-Fatiha.
