As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

May this month of Dhū l-Qaʿdah be blessed. Today is the first day of Dhū l-Qaʿdah. Shukr to Allah, those sacred months have begun. They are three months. These months are even more virtuous for the blessed Ḥajj season. The divine decree and wisdom of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla are boundless. Shukr to Allah, may those months be blessed and of barakah for us.

Our Prophet ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam had already performed Ḥajj once, and that was his last Ḥajj. He showed and taught us everything that should be done during Ḥajj. The way of our Prophet ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam is the Qur’ān, the way of the Qur’ān. No one can explain the Qur’ān better than him.

Now people have come out saying, “We will only read the Qur’ān and follow it.” You wouldn’t even understand what’s going on if you read a newspaper, yet you’re saying you don’t want our Prophet ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. There are many such foolish people. May Allah protect us from these foolish, unwise people who lead others astray.

Therefore, respecting these blessed months is to respect our Prophet ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam and to love him. Those who are not true, sincere people are striving to lead the nation astray. Shukr to Allah, we love and respect everything our Prophet ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam did and everyone he loved. This is our way. This is true īmān, true Islām.

Those outside of this, may Allah protect us, are captivated by shayṭān. Therefore, a murshid (guide) is needed, a way is needed; we must cling onto them. May Allah not separate us from this way.

Inshā’Allah, today we are going to visit our brothers; our brothers in those regions (Europe) we haven’t visited for a long time are waiting for us. May Allah help them too. Because there, by Allah’s wisdom, are some who claim to be “Muslims” but have gone astray. Those there have become a group that does not respect our Prophet ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. For some reason, shayṭān can catch them more easily. Shayṭān’s tricks are many; may Allah protect us.

Therefore, may our brothers there rejoice, and may their numbers increase, inshā’Allah. May they not stray from the right way, and may they not fall prey to any of shayṭān’s tricks. May Allah protect us. May Allah help us. May our journey be blessed, inshā’Allah.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
