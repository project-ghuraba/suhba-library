Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Alhamdulillah, Allah ﷻ has granted us to go on this journey again. We went to Spain, or rather Andalusia, we visited the brothers there. Masha’Allah, the brothers there are getting along well. Our Holy Prophet ﷺ has a saying, “ ام مهِ ِِّيح و ،مهِ رِاد يف ت مدُ ام مْ هِ رِاد مْ هِ ِِّيح يف ت مدُ ”, “Please them as long as you are in their place, and greet them as long as you are in their land.”.

Getting along with people is from cleverness. It’s not from cleverness to fight with everyone. It’s good to get along with people because that’s the land of unbelief. There are people there who are against Islam. From ancient times, from ancient histories, it was a place that was against Islam, and now it is in the same way. Christians have holidays, they say, “Feast”. There, on the Andalusian side, they dress in strange clothes and walk around; there’s no such thing on the other side. This was an Islamic state, and the opposition to Islam still continues.

But again, what Allah ﷻ says happens. So many people took Shahada and became Muslim after all these things. As we said, you have to manage and live there. They should be more careful to preserve the children. There is no safety anywhere in the world now. As Mawlana Shaykh Nazim said: “Don’t interfere in everything.” You will manage, because Allah Azza wa Jalla has charged man to do as much as he can. He ﷻ says, “اه عسْ وُ لَ ِإ اسً فْ ن ُ اللَّ فُ ِّلِك ُي ل ”, ‘Allah does not charge a soul except [with that within] its capacity.’ (Qur’ān 02:286). There is no imposition for what you cannot do. You take care of yourself, take care of your family, there is no need to look at anyone else. Do not throw yourselves into destruction. Allah Azza wa Jalla says, ‘ ةِ ك ُ لهْ َتلا ى لإِ مْ كُ يدِ يْ أبِ اوُقلُْت ل و ’, ‘And do not throw [yourselves] with your [own] hands into destruction’, (Qur’ān 02:195).

Thank Allah ﷻ, our brothers and the Muslims there get along well in all this. Because no one can say anything to a good person. The shaytan tries to make them not go well, and to get rid of them. May Allah ﷻ help Muslims all over the world. The misfortune of that place is by Allah’s ﷻ will. There has been a lot of oppression in those places. One of the greatest oppressions in the history of the world is in its history.

As we said, nothing good comes from oppression. While it is the richest state in the world, it still cannot stand straight. They are also surprised, wondering why. There is no blessing in what comes with oppression. The place is beautiful Masha’Allah, the country is rich, there is wealth that Allah ﷻ has given, but Allah ﷻ has taken the blessings because most of the people have made oppression. Even if that oppressor leaves, blessings will not be left behind him. Therefore, wherever we are, we must stay away from oppression, and we must rule with justice. May Allah ﷻ help us all, may Allah ﷻ protect Muslims as well.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
