As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla is saying in Qur’an ‘Azimu sh-Shan,<br>
وَيْلٌ لِلْمُطَفِّفِينَ
<br>“Waylun li-l-muṭaffifīn”<br>
“Woe to those that deal in fraud.” (Qurʼān 83:1). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

“Woe to people who scam in trade and play with scales.” In our present time, this is everywhere, not only in scales. Scales are given as an example. Apart from the scales, people are doing 3-5 times more, not just several grams. They are scamming and harming poor people. Their goal is to carry out the order of Dajjal, of course. Everywhere, here and in other places.

But Allah will bring them to account. “They don’t believe. Do they not know they will be punished on the big day?” No matter if they know or not, people who do badness will get into big trouble in Akhirah. Just like they caused trouble to people in this world, they will get into trouble in Akhirah. The troubles caused in this end of times won’t go without questioning. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla will certainly bring them to account. What was it done for? In order to make money? In order to cause trouble to people? This badness they do won’t be unpunished. They will regret doing it.

Such condition was rarely seen. These conditions are not good. It is because people forget Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. It is a punishment for them. It is a punishment for everyone. Those who rely on Allah, who don’t forget Allah will get out of that trouble safely. But those who forgot Allah, who follow their egos and shaytan will certainly regret it. They will regret doing so on the day when their regret will be useless. May Allah protect us. May Allah not let us obey our egos.

What can we do? We are living in the end of times. There is no conscience anymore, there is no mercy. May Allah not take mercy out of our hearts inshā’Allāh. May Allah protect Ummati Muḥammad ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. And may they come to the right way. May there be Barakah inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
