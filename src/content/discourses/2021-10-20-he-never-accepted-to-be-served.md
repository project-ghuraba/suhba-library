As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam had every good characteristic. Even though heNone had a servant, heNone wanted to do hisNone work by himself. HeNone liked to do so. HeNone never wanted to trouble anyone. Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam never wanted anyone to be bothered by hisNone own material concerns. HeNone wanted them to serve on the way of AllāhNone, for the pleasure of AllāhNone; to serve on the way of Islām. This is what our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam liked.

There are many examples. Our Holy Prophet ‘alayhi s-salām used to patch hisNone torn clothes by himself. And when Masjidu n-Nabawī was being built, heNone used to carry rocks and cut mud bricks. Ṣaḥābah would beg our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam to have rest, telling him they would do everything. But our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam never accepted. “I am just like you. Just like you are striving, I will do the same, for the pleasure of AllāhNone,” he said. In the case of Sayyidunā Salmān al-Fārisī, his owner had asked for gold in return for his freedom. He had asked for a lot of gold, and for dates to be planted. He said, “Wait until it gives fruit. I will set him free after that.” Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam told him to accept it. As it was Salmān al-Fārisī, the man accepted it. And since it was a binding agreement, neither one could break it. The man thought it would take years to give dates. Usually here as well, a palm tree won't give dates before 10-15 years. Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam dug holes for the date palms with his holy hands, together with ṣaḥābah. HeNone planted the trees with his holy hands. That same year, as a miracle of our Holy ProphetNone, it gave fruit. And the man had to acquiesce because of the agreement. He set Salmān al-Fārisī free. Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam is an example to us. It is good for us to do our own work by ourselves. It shouldn't be a burden to others. It will bring benefit to you. Something done with your own hands brings more barakah.

Therefore, when you have work to do, strive to do it not for this world, but in order to follow the way of our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, and do it by yourself. Certainly, that has benefit for people. Above all, out of respect for the sunnah of our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, don't ask favours from anyone. Don't trouble anyone. It is much better when you do whatever you can by yourself. It is better both physically and spiritually. May AllāhNone help us and not give us hardhip. AllāhNone gave us legs and arms. HeNone gave us strength, ears and eyes. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gave us everything. We should be grateful for them. And when we use them, we make shukr for them as well. Turkish people have a nice saying; “May AllāhNone not let hand or foot to drop down.” By which they mean, may you always have healthy arms and legs.

Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, heNone was very good example for – not only for Muslim, for whole human being. The perfect one, how he must be – this – believer, non-believer, they must see and study what Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam doing. Everything heNone do, heNone do the best for people to follow this. HeNone is doing first, not like to put burden on people for himselfNone. HeNone never accept somebody to serve himNone for himselfNone. HeNone was – even heNone make hisNone clothes, if it is broken, by himselfNone heNone was making. But for AllāhNone, must be. Help for people for AllāhNone, serve for AllāhNone, make go to help people for AllāhNone, for jihād for AllāhNone; all this for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.

HeNone is example: “La-qad kāna lakum fī Rasūli Llāhi uswatun ḥasanatun” (33:21). HeNone was good example. This is in Qur’ān, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying for people. For this, heNone was doing everything by himself. Not asking help from anybody. Even when Salmān al-Fārisī, he was... That day... with contract, or with saying they can – when they say something, cannot be not doing this. Everything Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam teach us, hundred percent benefit for spiritual and physical for us. Because this two both it must be.
