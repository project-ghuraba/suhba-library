Audhu Billahi min al shaytan al rajim, Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Assalatu wa s-salamu ala Rasulina Muhammad Sayyidi l Awwalin wa l-Akhirin. Madad O Rasul Allah, Madad O great saints of Naqshbandi Way, Madad ya Mawlana Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Madad ya Shaykh Muhammad Nazim al Haqqani, dastur! Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fil jamia' Our way is through association & blessings are in the gathering. Insha'Allah it may become a good jamaat & we make a sohbat.

Today's sohbat insha'Allah, is about not looking at haram. When Allah prohibits something, He prohibits also for the good of people. If you look at haram its zulmat (darkness) comes on you. When zulmat comes, then man says "where is this heaviness coming from?" Meaning a state of anxiety, depression comes to people. With zulmat comes anxiety, heaviness. Therefore, looking at haram is one of these prohibited things. Looking at haram means..

Sayyidina Ali asked the Prophet (sas), "When we are walking outside & see a woman, how should it be?" He says, "The first glance is.." Of course now everybody, when they are walking outside..They can't sit at home all the time concealed Even back then it was like this, now this time totally.. When you are walking outside you look, don't continue looking at it. Try to think about something else, try to look somewhere else. When you stare, at that time it is like you look at what is a sin, you sin. One should pay attention to this.

Now this time is winter time. People are not yet too much uncovered. When it is summer, they totally open up. It looks like a normal thing to the people- people of this century see being naked or exposing body as a normal thing. It is not normal. In Islam, looking at them is prohibited. Why is it prohibited? Mawlana Shaykh says. This, what we heard from Mawlana, we haven't heard from anyone else. When a married man looks at haram, the more he looks, the more Allah shows his wife as bad & ugly in his eyes. Therefore he tries to look for more haram outside, goes to other bad ways.

All of you may try this for yourselves: when you don't look at haram outside, how beautiful, how good your wife will look to you. And you will be content with her. Otherwise, as you pour gasoline on fire, does it extinguish? No, it does not. It increases & gets more. No matter what you do,you can't stop it. So the Wisdom of Allah prohibiting haram, it has benefits also. The biggest benefit is that one's marriage doesn't fall apart, his kids doesn't become all miserable, he don't lose his love. Because the most important thing, if you live peacefully with your family it is like you are in heaven in dunya. Don't look for more. But if your wife nags & bothers you, if all day you fight, you are like in hell. Even if you are the richest man in the world, still it is like you live in a hell. Because you have no peace. It has no benefit for you. You can go for halal as much as you want, it has no effect on you.

Our Prophet (sas) also said, "Take care of ladies well" in many of his Hadith because they are weak. If you see something bad in her, for sure she also has something good. People can't be all bad, can't be all good either. All have imperfections. Don't look to her bad side, look to her good side, then you can get along well. There are many things to be said on this subject but it is enough for now. We may keep this much in our head. The most important thing as we said, that Mawlana said beautifully: "the more you look at women outside, the more your wife at home looks bad, ugly to you. You have no peace left. Don't look - then the most beautiful woman in the world becomes your wife.

Today we speak about a forbidden thing to do. Why Allah forbid something to do? This is, Allah knows everything and He never does bad thing. All good thing for people, what He orders only for good of people. When Allah (awj) forbids something, there are many benefits for people. One of the forbidden thing is to look for lady, who is not your wife or your halal. Especially uncovered; covered no problem because you cannot see. And this century, it is most of opening. Everywhere you can find nearly naked ladies, all.

So Hz Ali (ra) asked Prophet (sas): "Sometimes when I am walking, looking at ladies. I am going in haram. How is this?" Prophet (sas) said: "One looking, first one- because suddenly you make this- It is ok, but don't continue to look. Second one (look) is haram". For this, this century very bad. Getting worse and worse. And the people, they think it is normal. Before it was European or Western people. In their culture it is like this. From before- BC or AC, all like this. You look, picture or statue, all naked. But in our Muslim country, it was not like this. But now Muslim also become opening. So when you are going around, if you see something like this-everywhere like this, don't look. Just it is ok.

And most benefit, Mawlana Shaykh Nazim said, nobody else (said), I didn't hear anybody else of this. When you look for lady who is not your wife, & you look like this your wife, she becomes in your eyes very ugly. You want to run away from your wife. Each one you look for another lady, become more, and more and more, until become fighting. And you be miserable, your family miserable, your children miserable. Because if you don't do this and you not looking, every time your halal, your wife become more good for yourself- more beautiful, more beautiful. Allah make you satisfied with your wife, with your family. But otherwise it will be very bad. Because Allah even in this world, they said they can give paradise when your wife & your children are obedient & happy. You are like in paradise in dunya. But if you have disobedient&unhappy wife & children, even you are the richest of the world- he is sitting in most expensive palace, everywhere cars, lands, rich you like you are in hell in dunya with all this what you have. Because you don't have this happiness. And the poorest man & he has this happiness with family, he is in paradise. This is very important. We didn't hear this from another book or scholar. This is very, mashaAllah he give very important point, Shaykh Effendi Mawlana.

Many people coming; wife not good. Wife, she complain from husband. Because when you put petrol on fire, not coming down. Everytime going more & more & more. The world, a woman for man, not enough - not finish this. Only to be without haram and taking care to not look for haram.

Because now summer coming, it is more and more worse. Become worse. Now it's winter time, little bit, you can't find people like this. But in summer, it is very bad. And people they are really, sometimes you don't know what to do. You get sick, you can vomit from some you see - ladies maybe 50-60-70 years old taking clothes and it is very terrible, very terrible. Allah make people with clothes better. Without clothes, it is not good. Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, Al Fatiha.
