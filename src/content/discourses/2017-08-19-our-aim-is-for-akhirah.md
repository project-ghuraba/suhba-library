As-salāmu ‘alaykum wa raḥmatu Llāhi wa barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīn wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla is giving us chances. There are some occasions, Muslims gather and it becomes a good cause to earn thawāb. Everything has its ḥalāl side and ḥarām side. AllāhNone has shown both sides. For instance, may AllāhNone bless it, we had a good occasion yesterday. There was the wedding of our niece. For the wedding, all beloved people of Mawlānā came from far and near. It was a good occasion. May AllāhNone give them rewards. May their rewards increase. May it be a good, blessed, and beautiful marriage. We want a marriage for good people to be together. Good generations will come from them. Shukr to AllāhNone, with qaṣīdahs and nashīds, all covered, it was a very beautiful wedding. Otherwise, if it was different, the opposite of it...

People are making a start. If they make a good start, they should do as AllāhNone ordered, as our ProphetNone said. However, unfortunately, most people make this start with alcohol, all uncovered - and are trying to build a family. Then they think why they couldn't get along, why it didn't work out well? Of course, it won't. AllāhNone is not pleased with such things. What AllāhNone is pleased with, what is done for Allāh'sNone pleasure is blessed. Shukr to AllāhNone, we thank those who came here. May AllāhNone be pleased with them. May they have many rewards. Whatever good purposes they have, may AllāhNone let them meet good people.

Dunyā is not a purpose. Dunyā is a means. Dunyā is not a goal. Dunyā is to use to make our ākhirah prosper. Therefore, dunyā... A man was sitting at breakfast this morning: "How many hundred houses this man has. How many thousand apartments that man has." And does he say AllāhNone? No. No he doesn't say AllāhNone. Apartments, property, wealth - nothing else he says. It has no benefit neither for him, nor for others. But who has that wealth, if he gives away to a good cause from that wealth, it has benefit both for him and for others.

Therefore, as we said, AllāhNone has shown two ways. HeNone has shown the permissible, beautiful way, the way of thawāb and He has shown the useless forbidden way which puts a man in danger. And if a man on the ḥarām way has all dunyā, he will still ask for more. It won't be enough. And he doesn't use it. And a man earning ḥalāl, he sees its benefit. As he doesn't worship it and worships AllāhNone, he spends it for good causes and it will be useful in ākhirah too.

Some people say "Our work is right. But we are fed up with working. Let us leave it and turn to worshipping without asking anything from dunyā." That is not quite good either. Because rather than making worship and not being useful to people, you are better when you are in dunyā - rich and being useful to people. You are more accepted by AllāhNone. AllāhNone has given a share to everyone and opened a door. If that door is open, use it for a good cause. There are ḥadīth sharīf: If a servant prays for 100 years, one day of an ‘ālim is better than that. Making worship alone without being useful to others... If you are darwīsh and cannot do anything, sit and pray. But when there are people beneath you taking their rizq, you serve thousands people. If you leave them and decide to pray only, you make a mistake then.

May AllāhNone make us from people in service who become means for good causes, in shā'a Llāh.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, AllāhNone give us good occasion, many occasion. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, last night also one of these occasions, wedding of Shaykh Bahā’uddin's daughter and Shaykh Abdul Jalīl's son. One of this occasion, nice occasion. This AllāhNone create ḥalāl and ḥarām. Every time they are putting in ḥalāl food. I told one - make one ḥarām food also, put for ḥarām. So everything AllāhNone make ḥalāl and ḥarām. No anything else between - either ḥarām or ḥalāl. So for wedding also, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, it was with qaṣīdah, with Qur’ān, with, and people they was good, happy. No alcohol, no anything against sharī‘ah or...

And this is beginning of life. The beginning of life it must be what Allāh HeNone like. You must be try to make AllāhNone happy with you. But we see many people when they making wedding, they are making everything against AllāhNone. From alcohol, from dancing, from every what they do very bad thing and they are after they are wishing to be happy marriage. It couldn't be, it cannot be. It cannot be this because they are beginning from bad. Who make with ḥalāl beginning from good and, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, it was very nice and all people they are, either they are coming here or they are watching from now internet or this thing, they are happy and they are making good temenni, good wishes for this. And AllāhNone accept because they are good people. And we are looking everybody look to have good people to be with them, not to be with bad people.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, they are who coming from Mawlānā time and now they are coming. They are also respecting and they are happy and we wish them, all of them happy days like this. And because this time it is difficult time, we must find good people to be with good people, in shā’a Llāh. And it is thing AllāhNone give His favour for people they can do good thing, who are working for AllāhNone. It is never mind for them because this is not our aim for dunyā. Our aim for ākhirah. So we can use dunyā, - money, everything without to be only for collecting. Our aim for this - to use to be like - not aim, but for under... to help us, to make us more higher in divine presence of AllāhNone. This morning we in breakfast there was speaking business man and they are too much speak. They said he has hundred of house this, have thousand of house. But when you see, we know this people they are not in right way. This is not benefit for them. No any benefit for them because only if they have... he was before ten years have ten house, now he has fifty house. After ten years he want to be five hundred house. And if he live he want to make it five thousand house. This is his aim. He is not looking to give any penny for charity, to make any charity for people, or to for ākhirah to give his zakāh, to give his ṣadaqah - nothing.

But other side, who have, AllāhNone open for them and they are spending for charity, they are good. They have good luck because AllāhNone make this opportunity to them to make them to give. Because many people, rich people, they said: "we cannot give. we don't know how it is your hand open not tiding [tight]. No we cannot take, open our hands" they said. No we see and many people they know this. But for khayr, for charity you can have whole world not - no harm for you. Opposite - AllāhNone open for you. You must use this, you must be thankful. You must be happy because AllāhNone create millions, billions people some of them they give them, each one AllāhNone give them special speciality and some of them open for them. If He open they must know this is from AllāhNone and they must accept and they do best. Because some of them saying we don't...

Sometime people coming for themself - "oh we are doing this, looking after dunyā so we must be look for ākhirah. We must leave this all of this and we must worship." But if you are for worship, AllāhNone create billions they can worship, but also who help people he get more than people who are worshipping only and not doing anything for people. Because there is ḥadīth from ProphetNone if one worshipper, some time they was worshipping hundred time, two hundred, two hundred year. Hundred year of one worshipper who is ‘ālim or who is a scholar or... one day from him it is better from that hundred year of worshipper. So everything we have we must ask, we must follow, not by yourself - because this is door not every time AllāhNone open. When HeNone open you must be thankful, you must be continue.

AllāhNone open for all of us what for we are suitable for this and make us to help and to help Islām and Muslim, in shā’a Llāh, and human being, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq al-Fātiḥah.
