As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Wa-‘taṣimū bi-ḥabli Llāhi jamī‘an wa-lā tafarraqū" (3:103). It is the order of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Be together and do not separate, says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.

Everyone knows that strength is in unity. This is why the enemy always wants to divide people, to make them argue and fight with each other so that Muslims become powerless. Although this is widely known, people never unite, and always separate because they follow their egos. They say, "You are like this. You are like that," and using it as a pretext, they forget the order of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Both what they are like and what they did is for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla to decide.

You should be together with the Muslims. You should be with the Muslims on the right way, so that the grace of AllāhNone is upon you and HisNone blessings come to you. You will see all kinds of goodness. For people who carry out Allāh'sNone orders and obey HimNone, the grace of AllāhNone will reach them. And the support of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla will be with them. After our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, they immediately started dividing the Muslims. There are many groups who lost their way. Shayṭān is the one who made them to weaken Islām, finish Islām. But Islām continues, by the grace of AllāhNone.

However, since people are often in this situation, they don't have the grace of AllāhNone. Because they came against the order, and they didn't preserve the order. Certainly, some people have good intentions and some have bad intentions. Those who have good intentions don't go against their brothers based on others' words.

After the collapse of the Caliphate in the last century, what was one state became fifty or sixty states. Each one is an enemy to the others. As if that isn't enough, they dislike each other between madhhabs. And most importantly, people of ṭarīqah say, "My ṭarīqah is better than yours. Yours is not good." They are similar. Even those who are in same ṭarīqah cannot tolerate each other. However, you have joined this way through the favour of AllāhNone. It is the true way. Shukr to AllāhNone, all ṭarīqahs are the way, the path that leads to our Holy ProphetNone. All forty-one ṭarīqahs are on the way. Their saints are our saints, and our benefactors, because they are the beloved servants of AllāhNone. They are not dead. Their grace and support are always there. Therefore, no need to obey shayṭān and say, "Your shaykh is like this. My shaykh is like that." Stay focused on your own duty. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla has favoured you and showed you this way. And HeNone showed others another way. But the point where they eventually meet is the presence of our Holy ProphetNone. Therefore, we should pay attention to this.

It is different with common people. Common people are normal people; they don't understand. "You support this football team. I support that team." They fight over this with each other, and even smash each other’s heads. Sometimes they even kill each other. Ṭarīqahs are not like this. Ṭarīqah means adab. It is obedience to Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Whatever AllāhNone meant for you, as Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim used to say, your heart inclines towards it. It means your fate is there. Keep on your way and make shukr to AllāhNone for being on this way. Millions of people have no idea about ṭarīqah, about the path, about religion and īmān. Make shukr and praise for joining this way. And when you see other people who are in ṭarīqah, show them love. Don't hurt them and don't feel enmity towards them.

Believers are brothers. Mu’mins are brothers, says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla: "Innama l-mu’minūna ikhwatun" (49:10). They are your brothers, so make peace. Don't make fitnah among them. May AllāhNone make all of us steadfast and firm on this way. May HeNone show us the beauty of this way, in shā’a Llāh.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Wa-‘taṣimū bi-ḥabli Llāhi jamī‘an wa-lā tafarraqū" (3:103). Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HisNone order to us, to be together. Not everyone by himself. "Don't be separate," HeNone said. Come together, you know everybody knows this. Muslim, non-Muslim, believer, non-believer. They know the power with unity. So, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla ordering mu’min to be unity. United, not separate. Because separate making weak the people. And AllāhNone, HeNone like to be mu’min, believer, strong, to people not harm them.

Because now, what happened, since Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, shayṭān and his followers, they know this – better than me and you – and beginning from that time to make separate. Make new way and make new idea, so some people they are separate from Islām, or from gathering of ṣaḥābah, and they make fitnah. Since that time, this going on. But we are obligatory to be follow order of AllāhNone. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla ordered us to be united. If you go, leave gathering, you are not obeying AllāhNone. You are coming against AllāhNone. This also very bad for who do this. If you follow order of AllāhNone, AllāhNone will be with you, and help you and help your gathering, give barakah, and give support.

This happened, the shayṭān and his followers, they know this, and they – after khilāfah, Khalīfah of Rasūla Llāh ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam they sending away, making one country, maybe fifty country. Fifty country, and each country, they are enemy to other country. Even they are same race and same language, but still they are enemy for each other. They put border, they put many things, And why this? Because this people following shayṭān, not following AllāhNone. They not like to be united. Sometimes they make little bit to be together; quickly, shayṭān and his followers putting fitnah and make it again enemy for each other. And cannot be any peace between them. And this is not good for ahlu d-dunyā also. And this not only for this, they put separation for everything. for your race, for colour, for language, for... many thing. They, even they are same race, but they said, "You are from this tribe, I am from this tribe. I am better than you and you are not good like me, and our ancestor was like this." But normally when you see, they must be only one country, but make it small, small country. And each other, enemy to other. And even in this small country, people also enemy to each other. This for dunyā and for...

Most important also, for spiritual, for ṭarīqah, for taṣawwuf people who following ṭarīqah. Also they make separate each other. And they said, "Your shaykh, our shaykh, my shaykh better than your shaykh, you are not good. Your ṭarīqah not good, you are..." And this also, this is not acceptable at all. Because, you must be more obeying order of AllāhNone and Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. If you're saying, you are in ṭarīqah, you are in taṣawwuf, way of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, ṭarīqah meaning way and these forty-one ṭarīqah all going at the end to Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. So you cannot say, "You are good, I am bad. You are bad, I am good." This also not from adab of ṭarīqah. Because, Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim, our father, he was saying, everybody he has his naṣīb, his shaykh, and HeNone, AllāhNone put in his heart this. When you go someplace, some shaykh, if your heart it open, you are happy with there, Mawlānā was saying you can, you must stay there. This is your shaykh, and you follow. And if not, don't follow.

For this, we see millions of people of ṭarīqah, they have… Each one have his shaykh, his ṭarīqah. And they are all in right path because ṭarīqah people, they are Ahlu s-Sunnah wa-l-Jamā‘ah, following order of AllāhNone and teaching of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam; all of them. But they forget to obey for to be love each other. "Innama l-mu’minūna ikhwatun" (49:10). "Mu’min, believer, they are brothers," says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam also saying, "You must love to your brother as you love to yourself." This is order, and so we must be not angry for people who are from another ṭarīqah, from another way, another shaykh. No, you must be happy.

Because nowadays who following ṭarīqah, who following way of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, they are not so much. They coming less and less, because people they cheated by this shayṭān and follower saying, "This not good, not way of Islām." But it is heart of Islām, it is ṭarīqah with sharī‘ah. You cannot say, "Ṭarīqah without sharī‘ah", it is not good. Sharī‘ah also without ṭarīqah, also not good. They are – it is same one. You cannot separate. If you separate, you make half of your religion. Because, many things not teaching in sharī‘ah, because only by book and no example for you. In ṭarīqah, they were from same way to Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, way of ṣaḥābah, way of ṣāliḥīn. AllāhNone keep us in this good way and make us to not follow our ego, making like if we are in ṭarīqah, like we are in dunyā. They have this now, people crazy with football, and they have club. Each club they hate other club. Even [if] they are his brother or his father or... they are not accept. Only my club it is best. They fighting they... even they sometimes, they [are] killing each other for this. So, this for dunyā. Don't make ṭarīqah like football club and be angry for other. No. You must be happy. This is way of beauty and way of blessings, and every good thing, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
