Waṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-F’āiz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-

Astaghfiru-’Llāh. Astaghfiru-’Llāh. Astaghfiru-’Llāh.

“Allahumma, lā tu’ākhidhnā bimā fa’ala -s-sufahā’u minnā.” We ask forgiveness. We do not agree with the sins that are taking place in this country and around the world. May Allah protect us all. May Allah ‘azza wa jalla protect us from the sins of these dissolute people. Because people have turned this night into a night when they rebel. We repent for them. May Allah have mercy on us all! May He guide us! May Allah protect us from obeying our egos.

Everyone has an ego. Whoever Allah has granted [though] is able to restrain it. For those who are not granted, Allah puts their ego on their head, and on their backs, and [their] ego has them do whatever it desires. For this reason, may no punishment descend because of the sins committed by them. When punishment descends, it strikes everyone. People are not aware of this. Those who are aware should repent and ask for Allah’s forgiveness so that they [i.e., these sinners] are guided. Cursing does not suit a believer; supplication is necessary. May Allah protect Muslim children from the evil of the End Days, the evil brought about by Satan and by bad people, insha’Allah. May Allah show us the right path, because people are trying not to get wet though it is as if they had fallen into water. This is how it is. At the time we are in it, is not easy to save oneself, but we do not cease to hope.

“Lā taqnaṭū min raḥmati ’Llāh” (Qur’ān 39:53) “Do not despair of receiving the mercy of Allah.” Insha’Allah, may Allah send someone who will take responsible leadership [lit., “a possessor, owner”] over this umma. The umma has no responsible leader. They [i. e., the umma] do everything, thinking as if evil is good, and then they question and ask why this [i.e., a particular result] happened so. What to do is to ask forgiveness! May Allah forgive us. May Allah guide us. May Allah impart intelligence, insha’Allah. Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-F ti ah
