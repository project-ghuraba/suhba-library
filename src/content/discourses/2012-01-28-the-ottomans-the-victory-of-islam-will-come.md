Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. The time now is the End times. And since the appearance of Islam, all the way to year 1923 there was Ulul-Amr-Sultans of the Islamic World, those who kept Shariat. And, who was the strongest of Ahlu Sunnah wal Jamaah was the Ottoman Dynasty, may paradise be their abode. There was none among them on corrupt ways, all on Ahlu Sunnah wal Jamaah. Now it has been 90 years that the Caliphate was abolished. The era of tyrants began. The Sultans were deprived of their Sultanate. In their stead, a lot of things of mushroom type started appearing.

Our Prophet's (sas) Hadith... Ya Sayyidi ya Rasul Allah, We ask for your affection & intercession. I ask for your Heavenly Support to say these few words, O Sultan al Awliya.What should we do now? The Islamic world is in a miserable condition.Muslims are in a miserable condition. As our Prophet (sas) informed us from the unseen: "Wa min ba`d al-muluk - After the kings will come tyrants". He stated clearly in his Hadith, with the power of prophethood, that after Sultans will come tyrants - who have no religion, faith, good manners, honour, virtue but instead have all kind of dirtyness - such a tribe will come. These will try & work to destroy Islam. Yes, this type of man came to all Muslim countries.

But the Holy Companions (sas) asked: "O Prophet of Allah, will they hold the power over the Ummah until the Day of Judgement? "No, thumma lil-tarakhi". What does 'tarakhi' mean? Then, after some time. "Yakhruju rajulun min Ahli bayti - Then will come a man from my family". Tanween (n sound in the end) is for tazeem-praising: "Rajulu-n". It shows his magnificience - A man from the pure line of the Prophet (sas) will come to this world to defeat these tyrants, a powerful, high one, who is Sahib ul-Diyanah - the Owner of Piety, Sahib ul-`Adalah - the Owner of Justice, Sahib ul-Karamah -the Owner of Miracles, such a person will come, it is said.

"Kulli amrin fi waqtihi marhuun - There is an appointed time for everything. And now.. what did they do since 1923? The tyrant appeared (in Turkey). After him in Sham, after that in Baghdad, after that in Egypt, after that in Libya, after that in Balkans, after that in Iran, after that in Afghan, after that in India, China.. These have appeared. "Kulli amrin fi waqtihi marhuun-Everything appears in its appointed time". Now the time of tyrants is over. Now these unbelivers & oppressors who are called tyrants will be defeated. Thay have filled the whole earth with oppression. After them, a person- "He will come from my family, from my Sulala at-Tahira - pure Generation. And he will fill the whole world with justice & mercy". It has been 90 years from 1923 to now. This continues for 90 years. There is a time appointed for everything. Now, who is expected is Hz Mahdi (as). But before the appearance of Hz Mahdi (as) the order to the saints now: Give the true owners, their rights. Now, the Ottoman Sultans who had been the protectors of Ahlu Sunnah wal Jamaah & who carried the flag of Islam for 700 years, they will take their rights now. Allah Almighty will grant them. But of course, it is not something that these traitors can accept & digest easily. What is it that they are trying to do, that they even expelled the nursing baby from their land (Istanbul)?

O you low ones! The Conqueror, Mehmed II, took over this land with his sword. How can you insult his grandsons this way? Of course it is not an easy matter to find the heir to this Sultanate. You know - O our Lord, with Your Order.. Surely such a zuhurat-appearance may take place this year. Investigate on this, invastigate on this person too. Because there is no crown prince left in Turkey from the male-line. Sultan Selim received the Holy Relics from Egypt. Sultan Selim again will present them to Mahdi (as). He will come to Istanbul, he will welcome him. It will be again Sultan Selim, it says. May Allah show us these days, too. We can be saved from these musty, dirty, communists and faithless, atheist democracies like this, by him. The time has come, it will be saved. The Divine Help will reach you. If our way is Haqq, Help reaches us. Is it not so, Hj Mehmed? Alfu alfu salat alfu alfu salam ala Sayyid al Awwalin wal Akhirin. Sayyidina Muhammad wa Ahlihi wa Sahbihi ajmaeen.

Fatiha. I am tired. I got tired but He allowed me to say this, Alhamdulillah. This will happen. Umm Haram Sultan Hz can destroy whole world. Masha Allah, still.. Fatiha. Madad. One can't come close. There are so many jinns there, they can hit! If one of them comes close there and enters at night, I may show them! If I appear to them, it will be very different (strong). How is this?
