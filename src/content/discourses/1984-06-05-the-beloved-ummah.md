Sheikh Muhammad Nazim Al-Haqqani An-Naqshibandi, Sohbat of the 5th of June, 1984.

What you are saying, we are listening. Even for a few minutes, it is honor to be in a holy place. (May) Allah Almighty makes us, with our hearts, to be always in His Divine Presence. However, our bodies are going around, but our hearts, we hope to be with our Lord always.

Now, most important benefit from fasting, it is to learn how you can carry the burden of this life. That is a training. Fasting is a training for us - how we can carry people also, like Sheikh Isa. That we are hoping to come quickly to save ummah/nation from every devil. Now, we are in a time that it is most difficult time for ummah. And who can carry in this time that heavy burden, he is going to be with Prophet peace be upon him. Prophet saying for them - my friends, my beloved ummah, beloved nation. Ashab/companions asking to be that beloved ummah. Prophet saying - you are ashab, but they are my beloved ummah. When the time approaching, and people going after their ego's desires, and going to be very few people who following the way of Prophet peace be upon him, they are beloved ummah.

But sahabah, they are followers. Allah Almighty chose them to be His Prophet's followers and friends in his life. But after 14 centuries, coming the ummah. Among them there is a few people whom they are keeping the way of Prophet with his love. Then Prophet saying - they are my beloved ummah. And we're trying to be followers for those people whom they are beloved by Muhammad peace be upon him. And we are trying to keep his light in our hearts and his shari'ah/divine law in our hands. But to keep his way in our hands, it is more difficult than carrying fire in our hands. Because at the time of beloved Muhammad peace be upon him, whole people were helping each other for keeping that shari'ah. In our time, people fighting to those whom they are keeping shari'ah to make them to leave shari'ah, to leave the way of Prophet, to leave the ways of prophets, from beginning up to end. Therefore it is so difficult in our time.

And ashraf al ummah, the more honored people from ummah, also most or best ummah. They are as the cream of milk. If whole ummah as milk, in our time, there is ummah, they are as the cream of milk. They are few, not like milk. But they are few in our time. Now we are in holy month, and expecting from Allah Almighty to show us from some of them. And it is not a difficult thing for our Lord to show us from them. If we are looking them, we are looking as we are looking to Prophet peace be upon him. Because who saw Muhammad peace be upon him, and we are looking to him, his lights coming to your heart also. And it is honor to meet such a people. Prophet's lights coming through them, up to end of this world, up to us.

Thanks to Allah Almighty that giving us pleasure in our hearts. So many people, in their hearts, they have been caught. Always coming darkness and suffering. Even they are eating, drinking, dressing, going around, but yet their hearts such a prison. But Allah Almighty's favors giving us through this holy month, pleasure in our hearts. Who feeling a pleasure for this month, Allah Almighty taking away from him the whole year's darkness and suffering from his heart. And making him away from fire, from hells. So many people they are living in this world, but fire without flames, so many people they are burning with fire but we are not seeing. Everyone...he knows that. This from hell coming to them.

Only Allah Almighty making from this ummah a group of people, never getting in their hearts any darkness. They are always, their hearts always shining. No darkness, no night for them. Day by day going to be more brighter their hearts, lightening. And particularly in our time, they are going to be a light for whole ummah, for whole nation. Allah Almighty saying for them - they should be neighbors for Prophet peace be upon him and they should be neighbors for Allah Almighty in heavens, in paradise. It is speciality

for them. Their stations should be seen from ahli janna, from paradise people as we are looking stars. And it is impossible to reach stars. Their stations should be so higher from common people in paradise.

There is a good tidings from Prophet peace be upon him - "al mar'u ma'a man ahab" A person should be with that person who he likes him. That is a big, good tidings. You, who likes him, you should be with him forever. Love makes us to be with prophets, to be with awliyas/saints, to be with sahabahs/companions. We like them. Nothing for us, except love to Prophet and prophets peace be upon them. And love his sahabahs and love his awliyas. That is our way.
