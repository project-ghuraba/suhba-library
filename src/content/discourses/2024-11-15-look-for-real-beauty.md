A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

The most beautiful attributes are the attributes of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. HisNone names, the beautiful names of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla: “Wa-liLlāhi l-asmā’u l-ḥusná fa-d‘ūhu bihā” (7:180). HeNone says, “Make du‘ā’ by the means of the beautiful names of AllāhNone.” Du‘ā’ is accepted. The one who begs AllāhNone, who makes dhikr of AllāhNone, finds it as a means to all kinds of goodness. He will attain it.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla has the most beautiful names. Beauty belongs especially to Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. A beautiful person is the one who is with our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam and with AllāhNone. They are the beautiful ones. The other, which is outwardly, is not beautiful. Therefore, the names of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla are beautiful. “Remember them,” says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam also says, as we read the ḥadīth sharīf in the khuṭbah, there are ninety-nine names of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. They were given to the ummah of our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Whoever reads them will definitely go to Paradise, heNone says. Of course, when one recites the beautiful names of AllahNone, through their means, through their barakah, he will definitely enter Paradise.

These beautiful names are great gifts and rewards to those who are part of the ummah of our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Their value is priceless. Their value increases more and more every day, forever. May AllāhNone grant us these manifestations and beauties. They are available to everyone. So, just because one person reads them, or ten people, or a million people, it doesn’t mean they will become fewer. No matter how many read them, even if a million or a trillion people read them, they will all still be rewarded, for Allāh'sNone treasures are endless. May AllāhNone be pleased with all of you.
