The benefit for the believers from the Prophet (saws)... Eat & drink... As much as you eat and drink the Prophet (saws) is happy. The Master of Prophets (saws) As much as you eat... And it is a training for the nation... The Prophet (saws) used to say "I sit down like the servant sits, and I eat like the servant eats." This is a teaching. The Prophet (saws), it is his teaching, the teaching for servanthood. To teach servanthood to human beings, to all of them, so they may not be proud, or arrogant or oppressors. Suhbana Allah! Our Sayyid knows such things but for our gathering to be blessed (I'm saying), for your blessings.

The Prophet (saws) said, "my Lord well mannered me." He (saws) did not say "my Lord taught me". And therefore to learn good manners is better than to learn knowledge. "My Lord well mannered me!" (Instilled good manners in me) There are no more good mannered people from East to West. Between the believer, the Muslims, we became " There will come a time where nothing will be left of Islam except Its name and of religion except for its symbol" (Hadith) Subhana Allah! There are no good manners left. The foundation of our Religion, the Prophet (saws) describes it "I sit as the servant sits". This it the teaching from Al Haqq, to him (saws). What can we do..But good manners... His Lord well mannered him (saws), did not order him, he did not say "my Lord ordered me." He said "my Lord well mannered me". An order is an obligation. An order is something and good manners is something else. He (saws) did not say "my Lord ordered me". But "He well mannered me". Allah Allah... Allah (jwa) likes from His servants servanthood... servanthood, Allahu Akbar! Allahu Akbar! Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar!

The whole nation of the Beloved lost good manners, all of them! From their kings, their leaders and their scholars. The Shaykhs nobody is asking about them. Who asks the Shaykhs? Their scholars... lost good manners. It is the attribute of the scholar, I am ashamed to speak my Arabic is little, but our Sayyid the Shaykh did not speak. The attributes of the scholars, it is mentioned in the Quran astaizzu Billah "It is only those who have knowledge among His servants that fear Allah" (35:28) Where are these scholars? They don't know their right hand from their left. All their aspiration is to fill their stomach.

There was one from the scholars of Damascus he was sahib ahwal/spiritual states and he was an Imam in the mosque in a place called Marji, and devilish people they destoyed it. The Imam was a very nice person. He had a light spirit. He was a member of the Awqaf. His name was Abdul Wahhab al Salahi do you know him? You know him masha'Allah! He had spiritual states, he had glory, and his voice Masha'Allah! May Allah raise his power & station. Sometimes when a person dies after 40 days they make... what do they call it some baked bread.. Sometimes that Imam is looking for me to tell me there is a meeting of the Awqaf. I am looking then, to see what he is signing to. What he was pointing to, if he was pointing like this... (a sign, meaning lets go) "Salam aleikum there is a gathering more important than this one, I want to go to there!" No body else in the gathering knew this sign. May Allah (swt) bless his soul. He was very nice & had good manners. Subhana Allah Al 'Aliy Al Azim. There is nothing left except for dry wood! There are no more.... Allah Aman! It is as if they are dry wood.

May Allah forgive us and send us from the land of Damascus. It was filled with Shaykhs the glory of Shaykh Abdul Wahhab Al Salahi his voice was so loud! Subhana Allah! Sometimes I was coming down to the market, from Jabal Qasiun to buy what was needed for my house. And when it was time to pray Dhuhr, I used to go to his mosque & Subhana Allah not one time..if it was Dhuhr or Asr prayer, he never led the prayer but was appointing me to be Imam and I would say "no you" but he would insist, "no you It must be you!" And after the prayer he used to take me from my hand and there used to be a room and that room was connected to the Mosque so the visitors may come inside. At that time people were busy, they did not come therefore he used to make a meal called chun-chun, when you cook tomatoes & onions and eggs together, you fry them. He himself used to fry them and he used to put 2 loafs of bread & use to bring it and call me saying "Shaykh Nazim, come!" He used to eat with me & whatever was needed financially he used to give tome. Because I was not employed,I had no real work so he used to give me something. Allah Bless his soul. Allah Bless his soul. Several times he used to come.

One day he came and we were according to our situation, we were not buying from the market or from the shops. There used to be there a piece of fabric they use to weight it. One time I bought by weight and I paid 20 Siryan & Allah bless the soul of the mother of my children, she used to be an excellent tailor. And she made a Jubba for me from the fabric I bought. & Shaykh Abdul Wahhab asked me "who made this jubba for you, Shaykh Nazim?" I told him "your servant, the mother of my children." Astonishing! He liked it. He also bought a piece of fabric, the pieces are already pre-cut and brought it & asked several tailors and they all told him "it is not enough fabric to make a jubba for you." Then he called "O mother of Muhammad" mother of my children, "yes, my master" she said. "Allah bless you, please look this piece of fabric I bought it & all the tailors told me it is not enough fabric to make me a jubba. But you can fit it. "as you like." She said to him, Shaykh Abdul Wahhab. And she measured him & tailored a jubba that was perfect for him. And he used to say the most famous tailor is Umm Mohammad! The mother of my son Mohammad. May Allah bless her soul.

What can we do O Shaykh! O our Shaykh, may Allah bless you. May Allah forgive us and correct our situation. You know Shaykh Yahya Al Sabbagh, from Sham? You did not reach his time. After Isha prayer he used to go out of the Mosque, between Maghrib & Isha, he used to go to his home. Till he reaches his home he used to look behind him, and there was a long line of dogs following him in his footsteps. And he used to call his wife the mother of his children. "Yes my master!" "What you may find from food, my group is there behind me." And he used to give food to the dogs. "Whatever you may find, bring it we give it to my group." And he used to put for them food & the dogs would eat. He used to have a long line of dogs following him, he was also a person with spiritual states. He used to explain the words of Shaykh Muhiuddin Ibn Arabi. May Allah bless his soul, he was illiterate.

You know the mosque of Sidi Muhiuddin? When you enter, the Maqam is on the left side. For the Friday prayer I used to go there with my Master Shaykh Abdullah, I used to carry his shoes and after prayer, Shaykh Yahya Al Sabbagh was sitting on the left side near the Maqam of Sidi Muhiuddin Ibn Arabi. He was not going down, he was getting up. When Shaykh Abdullah use to pass in front of him, he greeted him. Not one time did I see that he replied to the greetings of Shaykh Abdullah, while he was sitting. He used to call those beside him "quickly come assist me to stand up." And Shaykh Abdullah Daghastani, used to greet him standing, never while sitting. Whenever he saw Shaykh Abdullah, he stood up for him.

One time there was an invitation, in one of our brothers's homes for someone who passed away. I was a soldier/shawush for the dead ones. He took me Shaykh Abdul Wahhab Al Salahi Hz. May Allah raise his station I entered and there used to be there, sitting Shaykh Yahya Al Sabbagh. Once he saw me, "O young man, who is your Shaykh?" He asked me. I replied to him "my Master is Shaykh Abdullah Al Daghastani." He said "keep him! Because he is the only one who meets with the Prophet (saws) awake not in his sleep, dreams. No! Keep him!" Allah raise his station and may give us benefit from him. When you mention the righteous ones mercy comes on the meeting. Is it not true? Allah Allah..Allah Allah..Allah Allah...

O my Lord, forgive us. And grant us repentance. And remove every burden from our body. These are your servants, they came all the way to here to grant us honour. They gave us honour. Your coming here has dressed us with honour. And we can never give you, your due right. They told me that the Shaykh is coming from Bahrain, I said he has been ordered... he has been ordered therefore he is coming. Welcome to him. You honoured us. If there is dessert we may eat now. If they bring dessert we may eat it now. Welcome to the Shaykh, masha'Allah! He is like my son as a value he is more, but as in age my days are more than his, therefore he is like my son. Look there - yes he is sleeping.

Yes, now I will kiss this all the time. This I must kiss from now on. The Imam Al Rifayi, used to say "If I was far I used to send my soul to kiss & this the world of souls arrived. And so give your right Imam Al Rifayi said "The scent of the Prophet (saws) like musk on my hand for 40 years and whoever likes to kiss the Prophet (saws) may kiss my hand." And who wants to kiss the Shaykh may kiss my hand, because his smell will remain on my hand. Bismillah ar-Rahmani ar-Rahim, we are asking your dua.
