As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

“Ṭūlu l-amal,” our Holy ProphetNone says. Oftentimes the hopes of people prevent them from doing what they have to do. Therefore, many people place too much hope on doing this or that in the future, but cannot do it most of the time. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says to do good things in a hurry, while most people delay things, saying, “I will do it later, or some other time. I will do it in the future.” It is unclear if you will reach that time or not. Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam showed this by drawing on the ground. HeNone drew three lines – one going ahead, and the rest behind it. HeNone said, “The line ahead of the others is the line of death. Those behind it are hope.” Hope is always behind.

Yet Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla created it for people to comfort themselves. It has benefit, and it has harm. Therefore, people must use it correctly. There is an Arab saying: “Mā aḍīqa l-‘aysha law lā fusḥatu l-amal.” Meaning, this life is so difficult that without hope and dreams, it is too restrained. People feel imprisoned. But with hope, all the world belongs to them. This is so regarding everything. Worldly matters are so too. We have this worldly game of elections everywhere here now. There are many people who won't get even two votes, but feel as if they are going to be elected president. They hope for it. They comfort themselves. These things...

As we said, sometimes many people waste their time and money when it comes to hope. But when following the way of AllāhNone, you can hope for everything from AllāhNone. Every goodness, every barakah – everything is given by AllāhNone. AllāhNone gives with HisNone permission to whomever HeNone wants. Their hope is not useless. If they are not pleased here, they will be in the hereafter. But people who follow this material life, who only think about and hope for that, they will have a hard time. May AllāhNone protect all of us. May we hope for good things, in shā’a Llāh. May we be on the way of AllāhNone.

To asking... People hoping, “I hope this, I hope that,” they said. This is life of human being. AllāhNone gave them this thinking for to do business, to live in this earth. AllāhNone also, HeNone put this hope. “I will do this, I will do that,” but it is, without this, sometimes good, sometimes not good. They said, “So I will do this after ten years, after five years or later I will do.” But you cannot know if you will reach that. Which, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, heNone was showing ṣaḥābah. HeNone drew in sand a line, one long line, and after coming many lines behind. HeNone said, “This is line, first line: ajal, meaning death. Your death, your destiny it is this, but your hope coming everytime behind. Not in front. Many times, can not reach this hope.”

For this, you must hope good thing. You must hope what AllāhNone likes, for if you reach it, okay. You also here happy. If not, AllāhNone reward you. But when people they hope something not good, not thinking for ākhirah nothing, only thinking for themselves – they will be not having anything. Only air, hawā’ they will take – no thing.

AllāhNone create this hope in every human being. They hope, and they are imagining or dreaming they will do this, they will do that, but many time, not happening. This is what happen for now. I said... playing democracy, they make election. Now we have election here in Cyprus also. Maybe one of them cannot take five votes. He thought he will be the president for all of the world, not only for Cyprus. This is their hope. This is nonsense hope. Many people they have this, and they are dreaming and not doing anything for themselves. But it is also, AllāhNone give this – but the Arab word saying: “Mā aḍīqa l-‘aysha law lā fusḥatu l-amal.” The life it is very narrow, very tidy [tight], very boring, if you don't have this imagination, you don't have hope. With hope, everything people they dream, and their life going easier. AllāhNone give us good hope. Good hope, good intentions, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
