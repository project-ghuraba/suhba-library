Aū‘dhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-Shay"āni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Ra!māni r-Ra!īm.

Wa#-#alātu wa s-salāmu ‘alā Rasūlinā Mu!ammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādati A#!ābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā

Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-F’āiz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Mu!ammad Nā$im al-%aqqānī. Madad. The Holy Prophet (SAW) says,“ WhoM does Allah help? He helps the mu’mins and the Muslims. The one who helps and keeps helping his brother in Islam, Allah helps that person. Islam is the religion of goodness, and help should be done for the sake of Allah, not for one’s own benefit. This is what Islam teaches: not for one’s own benefit. When it is done for one’s own benefit, it becomes riyā', something for show and not for the sake of Allah. Allah’s help does not come [in such cases].

Thank Allah that our ancestors [even] up until now have been dedicated to helping everyone else and Islam. That’s why Allah has been still helping them. Help comes from Allah, and when it does come, everything becomes good. If you seek help and ask it from someone else, then there is no benefit. You just get embarrassed and nothing else. You should not ask help from the non-believers but from Allah, and Allah will help you. He gives when you serve Him. Thank Allah that coming from our ancestors today also they are helping Muslims and others. Individually, as a government, and as a nation. Wherever people are in need of help, they help them. That’s why Allah’s help is with us. As long as He helps us, we need nothing else, no one else. Neither from other countries nor from anyone else. Allah will make them all come to your feet and serve you. As long as you start with serving others, also that help will reach you.

This is why a Muslim, a mu’min, should take our Holy Prophet’s (SAW) words as glad tidings. One must believe it. There might come hardships, however, with the help of Allah, everything can be overcome, and hardships can go away easily. It is said that these are hard times, yet with the help of Allah nothing is difficult. Trust in Allah and help others for the sake of Allah. So as to receive the help of Allah, let’s help everyone, insha’Allah. May Allah be pleased with all of us. Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-F ti ah
