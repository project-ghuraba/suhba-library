Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi Rasulillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Our tariqa stand with association (sohba), and the good is in congregation. Allah willing, these are continuous. They say, “Lean (your back) on a strong place.” Allah willing, we have leaned our back on Allah and not on such and such. We have leaned on the awliya of Allah and they are strong. Just as the mountain is strong, they are stronger than a mountain. We have no fear, Allah willing.

What causes fear? It occurs due to a person not having trust. A person says, “I will do,” and “I will accomplish.” A normal person may not be able to hold one’s promise. However, the awliya certainly keep their promise. Believe in the words of the awliya. May we belong to them Inshallah. May Allah make us meet dutiful people. The awliya are veracious. The worst thing for a person is for something you trust in to be taken away. This way the person is left without a chance to do something. That is why, find the strong place.

When you are with Allah, let nothing be of concern to you. Never fear! He is the First, He is the Last; the place where we came from and are going to is His. Allah is Qadeem (very old). What is meant by Qadeem is always in existence, and the only one existing. We are transient. That is why you should be careful who you are with.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq.

Al-Fatiha.
