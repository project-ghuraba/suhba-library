As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam described the end times. He said there will be such fitnah that it will be,<br> 
كَقِطَعِ اللَّيْلِ الْمُظْلِمِ
<br>“ka-qiṭaʿi al-layli al-muẓlimi,” meaning “Like a part of the dark night”.

Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam said there will be many parts of fitnahs, like a dark night. One asks himself, wondering, “How can such things happen!” Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam expressed this with his blessed words.

Prophethood means foretelling what will happen in the future. Everything our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam said has come to pass and appeared. Lastly, he described the time we are living in.

These fitnahs, this turmoil, the state of the world, the state of people—it truly amazes one. There is fitnah everywhere. There is no place without fitnah. This fitnah exists in every country, whether Muslim or not.

Therefore, we must be vigilant, saying, “This is the end of times.” Our blessed ones said, more than a thousand years ago,<br>
هَذَا زَمَنُ السُّكُوتِ وَمُلازَمَةُالبُيُوت
<br>“Hādhā zamanu al-sukūti wa-mulāzamatu al-buyūti.”<br>
“This is the time of silence and staying at home,” meaning that only at home can you be saved from fitnah. Therefore, we must be very careful about people and our surroundings.

Something can happen at any unexpected moment. This is the fitnah of the end of times. Seek refuge in Allah and be saved from its evil, inshā’Allāh. Surely, whoever seeks refuge in Allah will receive help; Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla will help them.

Therefore, may Allah protect us from the fitnahs and evils of our time. May Allah grant people mind and understanding. May they control their egos. May they not follow shayṭān. May they not believe everything they hear. The way to believe is the way of sharīʿah, the way of ṭarīqah. May they not give credence to anything outside of these, so that they do not fall into fitnah. May Allah protect us.

Everyone, young and old, adult and children – you cannot say, “I am an adult, I am safe from fitnah, shayṭān cannot deceive me.” The shayṭān deceives everyone. Therefore, you must seek refuge in Allah. Whoever seeks refuge in Allah will be saved, by the permission of Allah.

May Allah protect us all. May Allah protect us from these fitnahs and evils.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
