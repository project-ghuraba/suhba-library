May our day be good. May our day be good. Salamun qawlam mir Rabbir Raheem. "The word from the Merciful Lord is – Peace." Salamun qawlam mir Rabbir Raheem. (36:58) May we receive healing for the honor of our Ummuhan Sultan. (Hala Sultan) May we receive strength. May we receive health. Everything is in the hands of Janab-ul Mawla. May He accept our servanthood, my son. May the flags of Islam be opened. May our day be a day. May our honor be vast. Ya Rabbi, salamun qawlam mir Rabbir Raheem.(36:58) Salamun qawlam mir Rabbir Raheem. Salamun qawlam mir Rabbir Raheem. Fatiha. May our day be blessed. May Bayram(Eid) be blessed. Isn't it the second day? May it be open, my son. May Islam be open, may the flags of the Sultans be opened. Our Lord. Recite Ya Fattah. Recite Ya Fattah. 70 times every day. Start. Ya Fattah, Ya Fattah, Ya Fattah, "Inna fatahna laka fat'han mubeena" (48:1) "Verily We have given you a clear opening/victory" "Inna fatahna laka fat'han mubeena" (48:1) InshaAllah, may we see those days. "Inna fatahna laka fat'han mubeena" Isn't Ayat-ul Karima like this? May we get strength, my son. Fatiha. "Inna fatahna laka fat'han mubeena" (48:1) Ya Fattah, Ya Fattah, Ya Fattah. For everyone - Recite "Inna Fatahna laka". You will open 70 times a day. Well, son. May Allah be pleased. May our day be a day. May your strength and power reach from earth to heavens. Salamun alaikum.
