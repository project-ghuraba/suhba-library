Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

A’ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm:

(Qur’ān 14:15). Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla says, ‘Wa Khāba Kullu Jabbārin `Anīd’, ‘And disappointed, [therefore], was every obstinate tyrant.’ We are living in the age of tyranny. Oppression has reached its highest point. Oppressors are ruling the world. There are very few people who are going against them, but they are unable to do much. They cannot do anything because the current manifestation shows that it is their time now. And as everything has its end, certainly, this will not last forever and will finish. The age of happiness will come then insha’Allah. The second age of happiness, after our Holy Prophet’s ﷺ, will be during the time of Mahdi ‘alayhi s-salam.

Up until the end of the last Ottoman Khalifah, Islam had the most glorious days. After that, tyranny started and humanity was oppressed like never before in history. And it is still going on. Those who are with oppressors are saying that they will save the oppressed. And then they torture and kill people. They show themselves as angels of goodness, and show the oppressed ones as oppressors. Their biggest act in order to show example to people, is that they put a white man in a pot to eat in Africa. However, they took away their goodness and drunk their blood. They made all kinds of oppression and cut their legs and arms. Poor people are making livelihood through oppression in order to survive.

There is oppression everywhere now, in Burma and everywhere. They talk about Palestine. But as Palestine is always watched, not much is going on. And in other places, they are making oppression freely. No one is saying anything or able to do something. As we said, it will have its end. We don’t approve of this, but we cannot do anything. This is oppression. And we are not with oppressors. Allah ﷻ will punish them insha’Allah. Oppressed ones will certainly be given their rights. There is Allah ﷻ. As long as we are with Allah ﷻ, the end of the oppressed is good and the end of the oppressors is not good. They will go to hell as a punishment. And they won’t have peace or comfort in this world with the permission of Allah ﷻ. There is a big reward for the oppressed ones in the sight of Allah ﷻ. May Allah ﷻ help us. May Allah ﷻ send the Sahib as soon as possible insha’Allah. May the age of the oppressors end insha’Allah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
