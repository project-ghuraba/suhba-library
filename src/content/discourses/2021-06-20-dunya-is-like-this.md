As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

"Allāhumma-rzuqnā fahman wa-‘ilman." I think it is a du‘ā’ of our Holy ProphetNone. "May AllāhNone let us understand." When people understand the wisdom of AllāhNone, when they understand what happens and why, there is no problem anymore. And when people don't understand, they all get into a fight. They call it a misunderstanding now. They don't understand, so they misunderstand each other. Because they don't know their rights, they cannot get along. All problems are because of this. When someone says something and another one does not understand it, he says something back. They keep saying things back and forth and a fight bursts out. People's lives are like this.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla sends people tests. And they spend all their lives with thesetests. Ḥājjah Anne used to say that Ādam ‘alayhi s-salām cried for forty years and laughed for one year. There are much fewer comforts in this world than there are problems. But even that is enough for people. When people know its value, it is more than enough and even it increases. This is seen more in people of the end of times because people in old times used to do everything under harder conditions. Their cleaning, food, work, and transportation – everything was difficult. And now everything is easy for people. In old times, if a farmer had land, it took at least one to two months, according to its size, to plant it. They used bulls and horses. And when it came to harvest, they cut with their hands, lined it up and dried it. They didn't have machines, so they carried it on horses. To collect the harvest and fill the storehouse, it took more than a month. And how does it work now? This work of a month is done, not in a day but just half a day with machinery. And then they are free. They go to coffeeshops and spend time with people.

When people are jobless, they interact with each other and create misunderstanding. There is a problem in everything. People of old times got along well but their problem was to work too much under the sun to hardly earn a living. The wasting of time of people now causes them other problems. The life of this world is not the life of Paradise. People can live without a problem only in Paradise. And they should be patient in this world. Everyone has their own problems. No one is in comfort. Poor and rich, everyone is given a test by AllāhNone. Whoever passes that test, understands it. A person who understands the wisdom of AllāhNone lives with understanding. Who does not understand it will always be sad.

May AllāhNone protect us. May AllāhNone let all of us understand. May we understand the wisdom of everything. May AllāhNone let all of us understand the kindness and generosity of AllāhNone, in shā’a Llāh.

Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam was saying, "Allāhumma-rzuqnā fahman wa-‘ilman." This is hisNone du‘ā’, it was "fahim" meaning "to understand". What understand? Everything. Because all problems in this world is misunderstanding. People when they misunderstand, they thought they are right and they thought this what other [person] meaning or saying it is like this. But normally, maybe this man or one, he said something, you misunderstand him and after, become problem more and more. This is example but meaning, when you understand the wisdom, everything it is easy for you. If you not understand, whole time problem, whole time sadness, anxious, everything, it will be with you.

This is nature of human being. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla created like this. If they understand each other – no problem. But this is what AllāhNone also make people to try to understand each other. If not understanding, become like this their life terrible life. If they understand and they accept other's rights, their right what it is, so it will be nice but unfortunately this life like this. But this is also from wisdom of AllāhNone – our life in this world, this life. Next life it will be either no problem or to [be] problem forever. So in this life we must be patient and to know what we are doing, and do this for the sake of AllāhNone.

HeNone put us in this position to, "li-yabluwakum ayyukum aḥsanu" (67:2), to understand which you will be better. It is for whole world, whole people, who rich or poor or middle, or the poorest one. Each one, they have problem for themselves. Don't think it is, it was no problem before. Before of course it was problem to work, to lifestyle, it was difficult for people. They was using their body energy to work. Men he work in field or in other, factory or other. No car for him like now. They go from early morning by waiting for bus 6 o'clock to come nighttime. Woman in house, no electricity. They wash by hand, cook by fire. Make them tired and they was tired with this. To farmer it was also for him to grow his field [he] need maybe one or two months. And to take harvest also need one or two months, because by hand, by cow, or by horse. They hand working. But now what they do in two months maybe half a day or one day it will be finish. To harvest also same. So it will be...

Don't think, now they're no tired. Now tired more because they have time and this time using for themselves, for their ego. So also problem. Don't think when you are poor, no problem, or you... old time people also they don't have problem. Each one AllāhNone make them test for them. And for Ākhirah, HeNone will reward for this. This is what meaning people not understanding, not listening. Don't think in this life like Paradise. Paradise in next life. This life must be problem for each one, for believer, not believer, rich, white, dark, black, yellow. Everyone they have their problem. Some of them you see, you see these people suffering, you say, "Oh, al-ḥamdu liLlāh." But after you forget this also. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh we are, with ni‘mah of AllāhNone, we're happy. We must be thankful and understanding we are in, in dunyā, in world. This life it is like this. This is the most important thing to understand. AllāhNone give us, help us in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
