As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Our Prophet ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam shows us the way. He says that the one who loves for the sake of Allah, hates for the sake of Allah, gives for the sake of Allah, takes for the sake of Allah, and marries for the sake of Allah has indeed perfected his īmān.

What does that mean? For the pleasure of Allah. Allah has given every good thing. You should love that beauty; you should love goodness. You must not stay away from it. This is for your own good. When īmān is perfect, all kinds of troubles disappear for a person. A person with īmān has no troubles. He has no worries about this world, or rather, no worries about the troubles. He won’t worry about anything.

Therefore, you should love for the sake of Allah. Why do you love this person for the sake of Allah? Because they work for Allah, they love Allah. We should love them too. They will bring you good, no harm will come from them. And you should hate for the sake of Allah.

People nowadays want to be humanists. It means you must love all people. You can love them, but you cannot love someone who doesn’t love Allah. Because that person is nothing but harm. They cause harm. They harm you and they have already harmed themselves from the start. Therefore, hating means staying away. Not talking or showing care to them, not sitting with them, not listening to them. Not getting much close to them. That’s what hating means.

And you will give for Allah. You will receive for Allah. That’s it. When you give, you give for the sake of Allah. And if you receive for the sake of Allah, it will benefit you. So, when all trade, buying and selling, is for the sake of Allah, no rights will be violated then. No one can infringe on anyone’s rights. You give what is due; you make it ḥalāl. That too becomes for the sake of Allah. And you marry for the sake of Allah. That is, when you marry as ḥalāl, you marry and have a ḥalāl marriage.

The person who does that, says our Prophet ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, is someone who has īmān. A person who possesses īmān is someone who knows that everything comes from Allah and relies upon Allah in everything. When that happens, mankind finds peace. Otherwise, they are constantly worried about what will happen, etc.

Allah knows what will happen. Your duty is to focus on your own affairs, continue steadfast on the right way, give and receive with goodness, love with goodness, and hate with goodness. That’s your duty. There is no need to worry about other things. Allah created you. He gives you your rizq (sustenance), and gives you all your needs. He knows when and what will happen.

Therefore, a person with īmān seeks refuge in Allah. A person with īmān believes in Allah, has īmān, and does all kinds of goodness. As we said, a person with īmān is the best of people, the most comfortable. He has no worry. A true believer wouldn’t care even if the world collapsed, because it is Allah’s decree. A person with īmān doesn’t worry but says, “Allah wanted it this way, He willed it this way, it happened this way.” That’s what a true believer is like.

As we said, a person who does all his affairs for the sake of Allah finds peace. May Allah grant us all that īmān, inshā’Allah.

Because these days everyone is under influence. Everywhere is full of people with no īmān, no religion and no morals. They influence people, making them forget Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. They instill such fear in people that you might think fear has some benefit. Just fear Allah, it’s enough.

May Allah help us all, inshā’Allah. May He grant us true īmān. May these troubles be lifted from within us, inshā’Allah.
