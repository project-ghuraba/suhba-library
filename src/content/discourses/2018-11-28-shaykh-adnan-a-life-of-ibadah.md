As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa raḥmatu Llāhi wa barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīn wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Innā liLlāhi wa-innā ilayhi rāji‘ūn" (2:156). This is always said when someone passes away. But it is the summary of all our life. We came from AllāhNone, AllāhNone created us, and to HimNone we will return. There is nothing to be surprised at. The destination is known. However, people think they will live forever even though they see every day that a grandfather is gone, a father is gone. The turn is coming slowly to them. This is the will of AllāhNone. Next to the will of AllāhNone and HisNone order, no one can do anything. We should obey HimNone, accept it and get prepared. It is important to get ready for this journey. What we mean by getting ready is to walk on the path of AllāhNone, to follow Allāh'sNone orders.

So the other day Shaykh ‘Adnān passed away. Mā shā’a Llāh, probably no one can reach his worship. He used to love worshipping very much. He would worship day and night. Praying and making tasbīḥ most of his day, he wouldn't sit idle. Wherever he was, he would always pray. Fasting, nafl fasting, seclusions, mā shā’a Llāh he made everything very well. And he was sick at the end but he was patient with it. He didn't object and question why it happened. He obeyed the order of AllāhNone and passed to Ākhirah nicely. Because to pass away in this world, for a believer it is like passing from one room to another. Death is not hard for a believer. It is easy for a believer. It is hard for non-believers. And we saw his condition. After his soul left him, he didn't have the ordinary appearance of death on him. It was as if he was sleeping, his color didn't change. We saw such a condition on Ḥājjah Anne before.

Pay attention, see how Awliyā’ are. They are alive. Their souls go but their bodies don't change. As we said, we went from here. Separation is hard for people. Separation is not easy. But shukr to AllāhNone, it is not a separation forever. It is for a short time. They will be together on Qiyāmah, in Jannah. May AllāhNone not give eternal separation. It is for people who are not on this way who were friends or just acquaintances who left the way or made kufr. Their way is separate forever. You will see them neither here, nor hereafter. May AllāhNone protect us. These separations are hard. But there is consolation in that we will reunite with Allāh'sNone permission. Because they loved, we loved and our ProphetNone loves. Who love will always be together in Ākhirah. He served a lot. May AllāhNone raise his maqām, in shā’a Llāh.

As we said, we made our farewell with sorrow. There were his family and brothers, many people who knew Shaykh ‘Adnān Efendi. All of them were sad, but sad from here. There are those who greeted him. Mashāyikh and Shuyūkh greeted him. And they greeted him with joy. It is so. In this world too, when there is a journey, it is this way. You send someone somewhere and become sad. But the people who meet him do so with joy. Going to Ākhirah is the same. A person who is loved like this will certainly be met by those who love him. Mashāyikh meet him. Grandshaykh ‘Abdu Llāh ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Baba Shaykh Nāẓim, other brothers and mū’min people he knew are meeting him in Ākhirah. They met him well.

May AllāhNone give us such meeting so that when we pass away from dunyā, we don't hope to stay in this dunyā. But it is important to depart ready for Ākhirah. Whether you fear from death or not, it will happen. They say fear doesn't help with death. Therefore, if you are going to fear, fear that your end will be bad. Prepare for your end. Prepare for it well, to depart easily. Like Mawlānā Rūmī, he said it is like a wedding to die, Shab-i ‘Arūs. A wedding. He asked for a celebration. Same with Shaykh ‘Adnān Efendi. We sent him to his maqām with drum beats like for a wedding. May AllāhNone send us HisNone blessings, in shā’a Llāh. May we all be given that spiritual power. May we not fear death. May our end be good, in shā’a Llāh. This is important. People get sad of course. But that finishes too. What can you do? It is the will of AllāhNone. Mawlānā said no one has stayed in this world.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Innā liLlāhi wa-innā ilayhi rāji‘ūn" (2:156). We say this every time when people passing away. This not only for this occasion. We use this so it is normally, it is summary of life. Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla put it in here because HeNone create us, send us here. So we are coming from AllāhNone. "Wa-innā ilayhi rāji‘ūn" (2:156) and after you will come back to Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla. This is our life. They, people they are make it so big, so fashion. They put everything they thought. They forget this. There very important this it is to be ready for to go back for Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla. And when you go back, you must go back with happiness. Many people they are going without doing anything, even they make wrong thing - their end it is not good.

Mā shā’a Llāh, Shaykh ‘Adnān he, we was in his funeral janāzah. Pass away Sunday and he buried in Monday like Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa sallam. Good day and it is Mawlid, month of Mawlid. Real from his love for ProphetNone, AllāhNone make him to pass away through this month. And we know him from long time. Our childhood also he know us but he was, because elder than us. Mawlānā he was knowing him since his childhood. From childhood he was like to be worshipping. Whole his life, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, it was full of worship and doing ‘ibādah - praying, fasting, making khalwah, real ya‘nī. Maybe very few people you can find they are doing what he was doing. More than half his day with worshipping, Qur’ān reading, awrād, ṣalawāt. Everything nāfilah he was looking very well for this. He is doing real job for AllāhNone and when we arrive there he was real like sleeping when we see him. This is from ‘ibādah, from worship. AllāhNone give him this light, not look like dead man. And it was also same like Ḥājjah Anne she was when she pass away. You never say, tell. Because I see many dead people, their color changing, their face changing but this Ḥājjah Anne and Shaykh ‘Adnān was real different. They was like only like sleeping. Very normal people from feet you see very yellow, other people that. He was this nothing at all.

This is for us it must be example to be ready for Ākhirah. And he was ready. He was not knowing. Everybody must know this but people they are not realising - they are thinking they will be forever. No it is for forever. After this, in shā’a Llāh, and to, of course when we was there, there is to separate from people to say wadā‘ - goodbye. For it is look like normal - when we sending somebody for traveling you are sad because you are leaving him and it is of course... Many people, all people they know him. All around the world they was loving him because he was special. They was sad but this is AllāhNone order for everybody. Nobody will be forever here in this world.

We are who sending him was sad but who receiving him from other side, they was happy with him, they welcoming him. Who is this people? The first - Awliyā’u Llāh, especially Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh ad-Dāghistānī because he was really lover for him and Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim and other murīd, other his relative who they are in same place. They are happy but who are sending him his, they are sad. But this sadness he not forever because also we are going to same place and no separate, in shā’a Llāh. Too we will be in with him with Mashāyikh in Jannah, in shā’a Llāh. Who can be sad? Who separate forever, who are not believer, they are be separate and you will not see them at all even they are your friend or your knowing people. If in this dunyā you can be together but if they pass away, without [being] believer you will never see them. So for this for some, AllāhNone give sadness but after they take this sadness from you because you know you will meet them again, in shā’a Llāh.

AllāhNone give us this belief and to be ready for that day, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone make us with Awliyā’u Llāh forever, to be happy here and hereafter. AllāhNone make, give ṣabr for his family and for our murīd, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone grant him highest maqām, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
