As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa raḥmatu Llāhi wa barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-Ṣalātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīn wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

"Alā liLlāhi d-dīnu l-khāliṣ" (39:3). The religion must be with sincerity for AllāhNone. Who is for AllāhNone, wins. How can we do it? With sincerity. You shouldn't mix anything. You shouldn't mix Allāh'sNone love with the love of dunyā. You should do everything for the sake of AllāhNone. And it's difficult to do it alone. Therefore, there are ṭuruq. And there is the true one among ṭuruq. Those which are not true, they've mixed dunyā with ṭarīqah and religion. They are useful up to a point. After that, they either don't go further, or go backwards – people become worse than they were.

Ṭuruq and dargāhs should be clean. They should be for AllāhNone. They can't ask for things in return. Benefit comes from AllāhNone, it doesn't come from people. People of ṭarīqah know this. This is the way of the dargāh. They say, "Leave dunyā outside the dargāh door." You should come like this. Shukr to AllāhNone, Mawlānā's dargāh is based on this. Not just now but since long time. Since long time is since our ProphetNone. Mashāyikh are one. The way is one. Who follow that way go on the same way. One goes, another comes. One goes, another comes. That is the way of our ProphetNone.

HeNone didn't expect anything from people who came to Masjid an-Nabawī. Those who came there expected something. They were hungry. Most of the time, our ProphetNone let darwīsh and murīds, poor ṣaḥābah, eat before himNone. HeNone would eat after them. HeNone fed them first, then ate himselfNone. This is our way. No one is for dunyā. Shukr to AllāhNone, whoever comes here, looks and sees nothing from dunyā here. Some want to follow and keep dunyā. Who look for ṭuruq with dunyā, which are good with government, politicians, and leaders, they see we have nothing. They say, "They have nothing," and decide to leave. That's ok. Bye-bye. We can't change this way for anyone. It is purely for AllāhNone. Who asks for dunyā shouldn't come. Who comes for AllāhNone, AllāhNone will give him in dunyā. Who comes for dunyā will leave without taking anything. This is our way.

Most of the time, as we said, things like this happen. Sometimes with the wisdom of AllāhNone people come for dunyā. Then they see our main goal is AllāhNone, not dunyā. They leave dunyā and slowly direct towards AllāhNone. May AllāhNone make us sincere. This is the biggest richness, to be pure for AllāhNone.

Our way as AllāhNone saying "Alā liLlāhi d-dīnu l-khāliṣ" (39:3). "To be pure for Allāh" ‘Azza wa Jalla for our Lord, this is our way, the way of ProphetNone. When you are in this dunyā, you must try to make it, to follow this way of why we are here – nothing important than our Lord. You must be all time clean yourself from material thing. Don't look after you praying, praying with sincerity. You fasting with sincerity. You giving something also. This is what teaching of ṭarīqah and this ṭarīqah. There is only in ṭarīqah they are teaching this. Who are not following ṭarīqah, they are every time looking for some benefit. Even when they are praying, they want salary for this. Many imāms they are, they have one holiday in their every week. Even their house near mosque, they are not coming for mosque for praying. This is difference between ṭarīqah and who are not following ṭarīqah.

And there is ṭarīqah also – many ṭarīqah, most of them not connecting and this they are also not sincere, not pure. They are looking for who coming to take something from him, to get benefit from, benefit from him. But our ṭarīqah, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, from Mawlānā's time very pure. He not asking anything from anybody and this is not new. It is very old. It is coming from ProphetNone. Our ṭarīqah from Prophet time – same. No one from mashāyikh, our mashāyikh, asking something. All time they are giving. Who coming – they are giving [to them]. They [mashāyikh] are asking for if he has he eat or he not has anything? Help him. This what ProphetNone was doing with his ṣaḥābah especially this people they have whole time sitting in mosque. They don't have anything, even only one clothes, nothing to eat. Many time they was hungry but heNone wasn't eat before they eating. HeNone was giving, send them food and after, heNone was eating. This is teaching of ProphetNone.

Whole our way they are like this. They are not looking for any benefit. And many people they are thinking now ṭarīqah he has power for government, from government, from rich people, from other. But when they are coming they seeing nothing. So many of them who are looking for dunyā for benefit, many of them they run away. "This what is this ṭarīqah – they don't have anything to help us to to get rich, to get more in maybe for election they can help us, maybe for they have something..." But, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we don't have anything from this. AllāhNone not allowed for us to do any connection, real connection because real connection you must give something which we cannot give. You must put order of AllāhNone down and looking for them. We cannot do this. So they are not ok. They are sending salām but when you coming for reality – nothing, al-ḥamdu liLlāh. This is also very good for us. We are happy with this. So only sincere people coming for our dergah. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, this is what we need.

We are happy with people they are coming from everywhere. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we have very precious people, very, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, beloved of AllāhNone, awliyā’u Llāh, al-ḥamdu liLlāh. AllāhNone make this people who are coming even for asking for dunyā we are to change them and to come to be sincere, to find the real satisfaction in this way this... our dergahs. And it is most precious thing. Many people looking for precious thing they not find but, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, it is simple when AllāhNone want. AllāhNone help us, in shā’a Llāh

Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
