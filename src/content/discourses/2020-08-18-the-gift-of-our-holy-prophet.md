Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Thank Allah for the blessings He has given us. It is His gift. And there are days for Him too. We are now approaching the end of the Hijri year. So this Wednesday, not this Tuesday, counts as the last day of the year. Thursday is the first day. May it be means for goodness insha’Allah.

Most people are not even aware of it. People are not aware of valuable things. They give importance to invaluable things. The Hijri year is a valuable thing. The Hijri year is the gift of our Holy Prophet to us. He has appointed the time of his Hijrah (migration) as the Islamic date. Thank Allah, 1442 years have passed since that date was appointed by him. Since then, millions and billions of people have converted to Islam.

Of course, in the meantime, shaytan did not stay idle. He was constantly causing Fitnah and tried to cause harm. Those who are harmed are those who follow him. Those who do not follow him will not be harmed and will attain happiness. Therefore, this is important. There are some days, some places and some people that Allah has given a special feature; to honor them is to honor Allah insha’Allah.

May this new year be a good year for us insha’Allah. Whoever wants can also fast on Wednesday and Thursday. Or they can fast on Thursday which is the first day of Muharram until Ashura Day, these ten days. Before Ramadan, the obligatory fast used to be at that time. Then when it became obligatory in Ramadan, it became a Sunnah to fast in Muharram. It is the most virtuous fasting after the obligatory. Whoever wants can fast them for the sake of Allah. May it be blessed insha’Allah. We are saying that from today so no one would miss it tomorrow. We are telling you from today because it is Imsak time. May Allah make it blessed. May it be means for goodness insha’Allah. May this year be a good year for Muslims. The coming of Mahdi ‘alayhi s-salām is near, may he come insha’Allah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
