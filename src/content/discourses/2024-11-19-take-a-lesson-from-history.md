As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says, “Wa-kam ahlaknā qablahum min qarnin hum ashaddu minhum baṭshan fanaqqabū fī l-bilādi hal min maḥīṣ” (50:36). It is written in the Qur’ān, as Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla has said. This is the interpretation of the āyah. There have been many nations that came before, nations that lived and experienced life just like these people. They were all stronger, more majestic, and had more wealth and property than these. They all passed away and are gone. Most of them were destroyed. Those who were not destroyed are the ones who are on the path of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.

So, for this world, people walk around saying, “This world is mine.” Whereas, it is nothing. Wealth, the world, everything, and the Ākhirah belong to Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Everything has been created so that humans may take a lesson. Those who learn from it will be saved. He who is on the way of AllāhNone is saved. The others, as much as they may think they are something in the world, saying, “We are like this, we are like that. We are from here, we are from there. We are stronger, we are better,” speak in vain. Because strength, wealth, and property – everything has an end. That too will pass.

That’s why we must look at the things that will not pass, at what is eternal. Do not focus on the pleasures and joys of the world, or the life of this world. Live well in this world, but do not forget Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla has made everything that is ḥalāl for you. You can benefit from all that is ḥalāl and good. If you thank AllāhNone, everything will be of benefit to you. If you don’t thank HimNone, if you act boastfully here and there, if you write, “This place is a tavern,” then AllāhNone will give you no benefit. You too will perish, just like the nations before who were destroyed.

A person who defies Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is foolish. Defiance is not an act of intelligence. You may defy your equals, but it is not good at all to act this way towards AllāhNone. Your strength is not enough to face a man. How can you do such a thing towards Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, the Creator of the universe? How dare you! What a foolish person is he who goes against HimNone, doing all kinds of disgrace. And he also thinks he’s something. He wants to be praised. Thanks to AllāhNone, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla has given us everything. HeNone gave everything to Muslims. Shayṭān also wants to take these things from Muslims. Unfortunately, most of them are deceived by shayṭān. They become disgraced in this world and gain no benefit. If they have anything in their hands, they lose that too. They lose their body, their health, their honour and dignity; nothing remains. He becomes like a rag; even a rag is better than him. He passes through this world and leaves.

Therefore, do not deviate from the way of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Take a lesson. Dunyā has been around for thousands of years. How many people like you, stronger than you, healthier than you, more beautiful than you, smarter than you, have come and gone? None of them remain. For thousands of years, this world has been a graveyard for all of them. It will also be a graveyard for us. May the grave be blessed, in shā’a Llāh. May the ākhirah be ours.

Therefore, this world has no value. The world is a graveyard; nothing else. May AllāhNone protect us from evil, from the evil of shayṭān. Shayṭān is attacking people greatly these days. When they commit evil, when they sin, they are proud of it. After doing things that should not be done, the most shameful things, they walk around with great pride and without any shame.

May AllāhNone protect us from their evil. May AllāhNone grant them wisdom and understanding. If HeNone does not grant them, they will still give their account in front of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, saying in verse of Qur’ān: “WeNone – there was many nations coming through this world. They were more powerful, more strong, more wild, violent, more wild people. All of this, they destroyed. They finish.” Don't for yourself, don't be thought you are more stronger than this people, you are more clever, more rich, more powerful. These people they was more than you powerful, more than you strong, and more rich. They have more intelligent. But all of them they finish.

This we must be take lessons, lesson from this people, from history which we are living in Earth. Everywhere before us, thousand of year, people was living in world. And they was nobody left. What left? Only, little bit. From like palace or anything. All, everything for destroyed, finish. So, don't be think you are powerful, you will be against AllāhNone, and you will win. No, don't think this. This is because nowadays people they are very proud, very making... coming against AllāhNone. Everything AllāhNone forbidding, they doing. Do, they like to do.

But when you do this, you are fight with AllāhNone, and you must be no-mind one, because no chance for you to fight with Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Maybe, whole, most of world, they are against AllāhNone, but Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone is, nothing affect HimNone. What you do, you do for yourself. For yourself, if you do good, you will be reward. You do bad, you be punished. And this punish not from AllāhNone, from yourself. This is AllāhNone – not in need for you to punish you or to reward you. Everything AllāhNone give for you to not be oppressed. To say, “Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla oppress me.” You oppress yourself. Even in when you are in this world doing bad thing, you harm yourself. You harm your family, you harm your country, you harm everybody who against this, who with AllāhNone. You harm them.

So, it is bad thing to not take lesson from old time people. Thousands of year, billions of people, they are coming through this world and going to Ākhirah. And this world only like graveyard, nothing else. You are coming here for to die and to make a new life in Ākhirah. This is this dunyā – world mission. Not to enjoy. You can enjoy, but AllāhNone giving you also, to you can enjoy very well with what AllāhNone allowed you. You will be happy here and hereafter also if you enjoy.

But when you are not doing this, coming against AllāhNone and doing every bad thing and you are happy, you not ashamed, no nothing. Every time you do very ugly thing, very bad thing, you are proud and try to make people to do like you. This is not good for you, not for other also. But today, we are living in time people all never been like this time before. To make something, people before, if they done something like this, they were ashamed, and they try to hide themselves. Now they are not hiding. They are coming openly. And here also we have, they are proud to say, “This is for place for drink. We are opening this for you – new place.” They thought they are doing something good. But AllāhNone will ask what they done, and they will be regret. But no any chance to come back.

From now, we must ask for forgiveness to not be to be punished in Ākhirah. AllāhNone keep us safe from this people and not to be cheated, because most of Muslim world, they are cheated by this people. They are going out from Islām, going out from their religion, their custom, their good behave. They are running after shayṭān and his follower. AllāhNone keep us safe from them, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
