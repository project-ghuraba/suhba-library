Waṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-F’āiz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-

Another beautiful advice from the Prophet (SAW) is not to go to fortunetellers, because what Allah has destined will happen. Even if you know it, there is no benefit to knowledge. You cannot escape from destiny. You cannot escape from what Allah has ordained. So those people are uselessly spending their time and confusing themselves, not know what to do. They are committing sin both by spending a lot of money for these things, and also by doing what the Prophet (SAW) banned.

There is a good example of this. This man called Stalin was a tyrant, and killed millions of people. He caused the deaths of 40-50 million people, most of whom were Muslims. During the war someone approached him, and was waiting outside. A man told Stalin, “There is someone here who claims that he can tell the future, a fortuneteller.” Stalin instantly asked him to be shot. Others asked, “He could have been a good benefit for us. Why did we kill him?” Upon hearing this question, Stalin said, “If he was beneficial, he would have known that he was going to be killed here, and so would not have come. This is the proof that he has no benefit even for himself, so how could he benefit others?”

Fortunetellers are empty people. They are empty and sinners. People think they know things, but Allah has banned them, and the Prophet (SAW) has banned them. To resort to them is not a good act. As we said, there is a lot of harm in this. May Allah protect us. May Allah not divert us from the right path. Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-F ti ah
