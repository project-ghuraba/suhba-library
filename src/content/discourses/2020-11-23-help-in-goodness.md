Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

A’ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm:

ن ِ او َ دْ ُعل ْ او َ مِ ْ ثلْ ِ ْ ا ىَلعَ اوُنو َ اَعَت لََ و َ ۖ ىٰ و َ قْ َّتلاو َ ر ِ ِبل ْ ا ىَلعَ اوُنو َ اَعَتو َ

(Qur’ān 05:02). Allah states in the Holy Quran: ‘And cooperate in righteousness and piety, but do not cooperate in sin and aggression.’ The orders of Islam are beautiful orders. They are the orders of Allah. Help one another in goodness. Help each other, Allah says. Muslims should rush to do goodness wherever there is. Help each other because if a person does it alone, it doesn’t seem much. But when it is done in congregation, something effective comes out.

Therefore, help by doing goodness and giving advice to one another, says Allah Azza wa Jalla. ‘ نِ اوَ دإ ُعلإاوَ مِ إثلِْ إا ىَلعَ اوُنوَ اَعَت لََ وَ ’, ‘But do not cooperate in sin and aggression.’ (Qur’ān 05:02). Do not cooperate in committing sin, becoming enemies toward each other and oppressing each other, says Allah. Do not help to sin. Stay away from sin, and never help those who seek help in this. Stay away from people who do evil, sin, oppress and do whatever is not good. Do not let them deceive you by saying, “Come help us, we will win, we will do something...”. There will be no benefit from that. Nothing but harm will come out of that. Allah Azza wa Jalla teaches us, by giving us Islam, to do good and help each other. It is a great blessing. To be with good people and to help each other, strength comes out of it. Seeing each other makes people more and more comfortable. It is difficult when they are alone.

So Allah tells us to help each other in goodness. Help each other in doing good. Avoid badness, do not even approach it. May Allah protect us. Now there are many bad people and many people who follow their egos. They are greedy, and then they regret it. May Allah not make us from the regretful ones, neither in this world nor in the hereafter. May Allah protect us.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
