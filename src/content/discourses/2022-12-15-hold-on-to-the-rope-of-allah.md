As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Madad.

There is a saying: "Who plants wind harvests a storm." What does it mean? It is about evil. If you plant something good, you will harvest goodness. If you don't plant a good thing, it won't result in goodness. It will result in badness. It won't be useful. It will be something that harms people. Good things were given by the prophets ‘alayhimu s-salām to people. They showed good ways to people. They showed every kind of beauty. And most importantly, they told about their CreatorNone. Good people resulted from the prophets and awliyā’.

And in opposition to them, shayṭān has cheated and raised up people like himself. They plant badness. They harvest evil. Goodness cannot come out of badness. Certainly, it was in the times of all the prophets, but especially after our Holy ProphetNone, people went astray and got cheated by shayṭān. People started following shayṭān. No goodness came from them. Only badness came from them. They planted it, so they harvested it. Then they disappeared. Neither they, nor anything from what they planted and harvested has remained. As their way was not right, they are extinct. They are gone. The people who replaced them were foolish and similar to them so they did the same. They did badness to people, not goodness. They are present in all times even though they are not the same people. As their ways are not right, it finishes with one and starts again with another. Another way of fitnah and evil occurs. But that finishes too and another comes in its place.

Right is the way of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, the way of all prophets. This continues without change. Whoever follows this way, a person who walks on this way, is brought to a firm place. He is bound to a firm place that doesn't change or finish. It is there forever. People nowadays say, "We will follow the way of such and such a man forever. It will last forever." What will last forever in this world is the way of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. The rest is false. They are ways which will end. Therefore, even if they last for 10, 100, or 200 years, they don't have a good ending. They will finish. They are things that have no use, they cause nothing but harm. Therefore, connect to this firm way. "Wa-‘taṣimū bi-ḥabli Llāh" (3:103). "Hold firmly to the rope of AllāhNone." Hold firmly, says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Who holds on to that way will be saved. Who holds on to that rope will be saved. The rest will perish. May AllāhNone protect all of us. There is a saying, they said, "If you plant wind, you harvest storm." You harvest a storm. This is, maybe many people they know about, they hear about this, and this is for people who making something not useful, not good. And what they will get from this what they done? It is bad thing, nothing good, no benefit – harming people. This is what they will get. They will not get good thing. If you plant bad thing, you must harvest bad thing or worse, more than bad.

This other side, they plant good thing, you harvest good thing. Who [did] this? All prophets since Ādam ‘alayhi s-salām to our ProphetNone. Until Yawmu l-Qiyāmah this good thing giving for people, for their this life, and for other life also. Not only for this life, for other life also, it will benefit. But other people, their whole time they're not accepting and they try to do another thing they thought it will be better for themself but this, who do bad thing, it is not, can't be forever. Maybe some times and after this they will be finish and what they teach people also finish. This is what happened especially after Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Thousands and thousands like these people, they plant bad thing and they harm people. And who follow them also they was harming them and after a while they destroy and they finish, nobody follow these people. But unfortunately, another coming. Another one coming. Also, sometimes also they destroy and finish. After – again. This is because they are not in right way, way of AllāhNone they will be finished here and in ākhirah, in next life they will be punished.

But who follow way of AllāhNone, this is forever. Not only for here, forever it is good way, blessed way and saving people from every badness. But the other side, they're saying "forever". Nothing forever in this life, nothing at all. They say, "We will follow this man forever, love forever." This is all lie and nothing. Forever, only one thing forever, way of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, way of prophets, which you must hold this way strongly, to be saved. But AllāhNone order, Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Wa-‘taṣimū bi-ḥabli Llāhi jamī‘an wa-lā tafarraqu" (3:103). "Hold this rope of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla." Who fall in sea, to save themself they catch rope. So this is also for our, to save ourself you must not leave this rope. Strongly hold this. Don't go this way, that way. Nothing can save you from bad thing, only this way. Don't be cheated with this people, who they are saying something, cheating people. As we said, millions of like this people they was coming and passing. Nobody remember them. AllāhNone save us from these people and keep us in HisNone way, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
