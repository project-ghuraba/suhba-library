Aū‘dhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-Shay"āni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Ra!māni r-Ra!īm.

Wa#-#alātu wa s-salāmu ‘alā Rasūlinā Mu!ammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādati A#!ābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā

Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-F’āiz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Mu!ammad Nā$im al-%aqqānī. Madad. Our father Sheikh, Sheikh Nazim (QS) often said, “Do not worry too much for this world, for what is happening, all things are willed by Allah.” Whatever Allah wills and has written, happens and will happen. Allah is the creator and these people are just a means.

Now they say this one did this, this one did that. There is no need to worry about other people. The real creator and the one who does things and causes things to happen is Allah. So there is no need to be upset about certain things that happen. As long as you are with Allah, in the end you are the victorious one; you will win. If not, if you are not with Allah, then you can be as anxious and worried as much as you want and like, but it is not going to benefit you at all. Now the state of the world is this as we see now: the whole world is worried. Worried about what they should be doing, where we’re coming from, where we’re going, with “this one did that” and “that one did this.” Yes, people do things, but without the will of Allah nothing would happen. Allah gives the person that permission to do things, whether it’s good or it’s bad. Allah is granting to people that they are able to do things.

There are people who follow their ego and those who do not, but it's all from Allah. It is Allah’s predestination, something He willed to happen. Something could be this way now, and it could be different from now on in the future. Yet whatever be the case, it is what Allah allows, and Allah wants us to submit to Him. “Don't be sad,” He says, “Don't be worried or anxious.” As long as you are with Allah, then you will win. Those who are not with Allah, those who are away from Allah and with Shaytan are the losers. Even if the whole world is within their hands and under their order, under their command, they will [only] win for a short period of time, [only] temporarily, and then forever, for eternity, they are at loss. So people are anxious, but there is no need to be anxious. They are both losing their health, and some of them are losing their minds. Be with Allah to stay strong; everything is from Allah. We will return back to him. The things that He does are beautiful and good. There is the saying, “Let’s see what our Lord does, and whatever He does it will be good for good.” This is what the lovers and awliya of Allah say, and in fact it is like that. May Allah strengthen our faith, and the rest will be easy, insha'Allah. Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-F ti ah
