Sheikh Bahauddin Adil, Sohbat of the 10th of May, 2016.

Allahu Allahu Allahu Allah. Allahumma salli wa sallim 'ala Nabiyina Muhammad alaihi salam. Salatan tadumu wa tuhda ilaih mammara layali wa tula dawam. Allahumma salli wa sallim 'ala Nabiyina Muhammad alaihi salam. Salatan tadumu wa tuhda ilaih mammara layali wa tula dawam. Allahumma salli wa sallim 'ala Nabiyina Muhammad alaihi salam. Salatan tadumu wa tuhda ilaih mammara layali wa tula dawam.

MashaAllah. We are trying to be, as much we can, strong in our iman. Today shaitan he is focusing in one thing only, how to break our iman. And his mission is like this. Whatever you do, it is out of iman. Whatever you act, it's out of belief. Whatever you're trying to do, it is out, out, out, out. Now imagine that you will build a house. We go in history for the first house in history. They say the first house in history it was the cave. Today we are in 2016, which one of us will leave the city and go and live in a cave? It's impossible. But during the history everything been built up.

This is not history lessons or not history teaching but we have to understand, even in religion, point where we are coming. Because so many people, they say mainly of our things is bid'a and mainly of our things it's shirk. Ya Hu, we say as people living in 2016. This is we cannot deny 2016. We cannot say we are in the bronze century or ancient centuries. No, we are very modern at the moment. Even though people they say you are mushrik, they are using modern weapon. Why you are not fighting with the sword and riding the horse? All of you, you are getting the tanks and getting the weapons and getting the explosion. In sahaba time and RasulAllah (saw), there was no such kind of things. There is no C4 or this kind of explosions. Why? That's why, they say for them it's ok and for you it's not ok. This is how they are making the mentality to put nifaq and shiqaq. They say to put the discrimination and to put the fitna among your heart to let you be all the time having suspicions about your thinking or about your doing.

It is very clever idea of shaitan. Yes. Shaitan he is the one putting clever ideas how to take your iman or how to make you not sure by your prayer, by your wudu, by your acting. "Qul audhu bi Rabbi l-falaq min sharri ma khalaq wa min sharri ghasiqin idha waqab wa min sharri n-naffathati fi l-'uqad wa min sharri hasidin idha hasad" (113) Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. "Qul audhu bi Rabbi n-nas, maliki n-nas, ilahi n-nas min sharri l-waswasi l-khannas" (114:1-4) Allah Almighty saying "min sharri l-waswasi khannas". This waswas, that means he put so many idea in your things. You make wudu, "My wudu is not correct. Maybe I miss one point here, didn't put water. Put water, water... Now it's ok. No, no, no. Still water..." This is waswas. This is waswas and khannas. This makes you to not be sure about what you are doing.

Allah Almighty said when you say La ilaha illAllah Muhammadun RasulAllah even by joke, it'd been accepted as true. If somebody coming to you, say "I want to be Muslim" but he doesn't have any intention, just to make fool of you and say Ash-hadu 'an la ilaha illAllah wa ash-hadu anna Muhammadan abduhu wa Rasuluh tell him it's done. I don't have any issue. Finished. You've been sealed, you will have the stamp. You go for immigration and say "I want to enter this country". "No, no, no. I changed my mind". You already entered. Finished. Your passport's been... This is how Allah Almighty make it so simple and make it so easy and make it available for everybody. This is how you think the water is available for everybody. Yes, it's available for everybody. Allah Almighty doesn't say - "This is My enemy. I don't give him water. This is My enemy. I don't give him oxygen. This is My enemy. I don't give him rizq. This is My enemy. I don't make him rich. This is My enemy. I don't make him powerful." Everything is available. This Allah Almighty. He put everything available for everybody. This is Allah Almighty, never think like you. He

make available. What you think about Islam and iman? He doesn't make available as water and oxygen or rizq? Hasha li-Llah that to not make it available.

That's why, when you have this kind of discussion, it's coming in this way. I say this is, you touch this one haram. You touch this one I don't know what happens, you look like this it's haram.. So what to do? We have to go for the cave. Anybody want to go to cave? Nobody. Nobody. They say this comfort at least we are sitting here. We have something to sit on. We have carpet. And under the carpet we have the ceramic. Under the ceramic we have good place. And the worst place it is better than cave because the cave you never know when the scorpion will come or the spider will come down or the snake will be going around and say, this is my house what you are doing here? And if you are lucky, you will have neighbour as a bear inside sleeping. Will wake up for food and he will find you.

Ya RasulAllah, we are putting our love and respect to you. This is our main concept. Here it is good to know your weakness because the people they claim they are powerful, they will find some people to challenge them. If you are walking in the street and say "I am the most powerful one here", you will find some people to challenge you. This remind me also with very short and nice story. One man he was in his area the most powerful one and the most strong one and the most everybody fearing from him. Wherever he go, people said "Oh, he's very powerful. This is very strong and he has very bad character also. So we don't want to deal with him." And after a while two youngs, they came for that area and they look for this he is the strong one. He is the bad character one and everybody "it hasha, ya Sheikh, it hasha". Everybody stand beside when he come. These two guys they say "We beat this guy. It's not big deal". "Are you sure we can beat him?" "Yeah, yeah, we can." And everybody say "Don't try." "We are not scared from him." Because they come from outside. They look at him. One day they make excuse to make a fight and is a real... "I am... You don't know who am I?" "Yeah we know". What? One he catch him from his feet. He pull him. And the second one he get his head. And they beat him so badly, so badly been beaten by these two youngs. He was shocked.

And the people around, they've been shocked. And they see the aura of that man that he was putting around, it was fake actually. It was not true. So when he'd been beaten badly, then he went. After a while also he came. Now the people they have the courage that he can be beaten. "We can handle him." Then again he tried to say... And they beat him. And that guy, he say "I think I am done here." Say "I am done here. Nobody will get any respect to me. Nobody will get any respect". That's why they say if you will show yourself that you are the most strong and going around, you will find somebody and challenge you. And then if you'll be beaten or if you beat them, then you will have another one to come. And this will never end. That's why we say for ourselves: Ya Rabbi, we are weak servants.

We don't have any claiming. We are under Your mercy. We want to be under Your mercy. We don't want to be act in our acting. We are trying to do our namaz, we are trying to do our zakat. We are trying to do our fasting. We are, we are trying. Don't say we are doing. Don't say we are doing. Because when you say we are doing, that you are claiming, say "Ok, this I do. Give me my wages." This is so simple. If you are working, the second day you say "My wages please. Can you give me?" And say 'oh.' Then the one he give you the work, he will come and check for the work. He'll say "This wrong, this wrong, this wrong..." So you will be without wages. You work, work, work and no wages. But if you say "I'm doing this, this as much as I can. Ya Rabbi, whatever wages You give, I'm ok. If You don't give, I'm also ok, no problem because I do this from my heart." Because here the most important thing in the religion to do it through your heart. So many people they don't understand this. They think it is like, it should be... The perfection is for Allah Almighty. The perfection is for Allah Almighty. But as we are weak servants, as much as we can.

"La yukallifu llahu nafsan illa wus'aha" (2:286) This is every night we are saying this, every night. That Allah Almighty He never ask from His servant over his capacity. What is your capacity? My capacity is to carry 3 kilos. You are not going for Olympics to carry 150 kilos. Ya Rabbi, my capacity 5 kilo. Ya Rabbi, my capacity... But here important things by us to improve our capacity. Allah Almighty He say to you improve your capacity. And this is order to improve. Because RasulAllah (saw) he said in his hadith if you have today... if you have yesterday and today equal, you will be in loss. You have to make little bit better than today. Little bit better than yesterday. Little bit better than tomorrow. Little bit after tomorrow. So Allah Almighty He ask you to grow. He ask you to grow. But He doesn't say to you - "This is not accepted. Take it home. This is not accepted. Take it home. This is, take it home." No. Allah Almighty, He is the most merciful to make you for Him, to be in Him. This is important things for us that to learn. As much as you are simple, as much as you are able to learn more. As much as you are educated, as much as He's to give you.

Mawlana Sheikh he was all the time advising me: "My son, be like empty cup. The full cup, it's already above the capacity. But if you are empty, you still get more and more and more." So that's why, try to empty yourself for any knowledge coming to you. Anything coming to you, don't say "I have enough". No, say be empty. The capacity is so high. May Allah make us all the time with Mawlana Sheikh and make the big Awliya to have look on us and to make us happy inside. Because the nazar of Awliya, the looking of Awliya, clearing you and make you happy here and hereafter.

Wa min Allahi t-tawfiq. Bi hurmati l-Habib, bi hurmati l-Fatiha.
