As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

If these Muslims deal with themselves, they won't need anything else. AllāhNone will help them. It is said in a ḥadīth sharīf of the Holy ProphetNone "Min ḥusni islāmi l-mar’i, tarkuhu mā lā ya‘nīh." The best Muslim is the one who does not deal with things that do not concern him. A Muslim who tries to deal with himself and correct his faults, is the best Muslim, says the Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam.

They are doing wrong in everything but when it comes to others, they accuse them of doing wrong and try to warn them. They think they are warning. In fact, the others are fine but they cause them to doubt. There are many such people who serve as associates of shayṭān and appear as Muslims but chase people away from the religion. And it is enough for people to look at themselves to see their own mistakes. When others ask, only then can you point out what is right and what is wrong to them. And when you deal with people who have nothing to do with you, they will not care anymore and run away from the religion. They will not accept anything afterwards. We should treat others according to this wisdom.

There are certain words that should not be said in certain places. Even if it is true, you should not say it. If it may cause harm, better not to say it. There are some strange minds, minds that have not been trained by ṭarīqah. Those minds have not had a murshīd. They think they are doing right but they are doing wrong, in fact. They don't appreciate anyone else. Actually, they are the ones not to be appreciated. Therefore, adab is important and wisdom is important. If you want to do something by yourself, you cannot reach anywhere. You will harm both yourself and those around you. Because wherever you interfere, you will spread harm. You will muddy clean water. When you tell unnecessary things, this clean water becomes muddy. You cannot drink it. May AllāhNone protect us from the evil of these people.

They were around in old times and they are around now. They are always there. They have no value. Thousands of such people have existed. Since the time of Holy ProphetNone until now, their names are not recorded. Names of ṣaḥābah and scholars, real scholars, and murshids of ṭarīqahs, have been recorded and respected. The rest have been forgotten. And if they did not repent, they must have gone to Ākhirah carrying that responsibility. May AllāhNone protect us from their evil. Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying for people to look for themself, to not look for another which they are not, not the need to say something for other people. "Min ḥusni islāmi l-mar’i, tarkuhu mā lā ya‘nīh." Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, the who like to be good Muslim, not to look for thing he not... mā lā ya‘nīh [that] not concern him. You look for yourself. If you look for yourself, it is enough. You will be good Muslim, best Muslim. But if you not looking for yourself and trying to give advice for people – to do this, to do that – and you are not happy with what they are doing, you are not good Muslim, you are not good behaved Muslim because you don't have wisdom.

Without wisdom, if you are know whole ḥadīth, Qur’ān by heart, it not benefit for you. Without wisdom maybe you think you are doing good but you are doing bad. Because there is many true thing, good thing, you cannot say every time. There is place you must be quiet, not saying this and when you said this, then become harm for people, become bad for people. There is many thing like this. So you mustn't think every right you can say it everywhere. Right is right still, no problem for this. But when you saying in some place, maybe not making good, making very bad for you, for other also.

This is way of shayṭān and his follower. They thought they are best scholar, making people, treat[ing] them as they are ignorants and they must obey them for what they are doing. But when they are doing, not giving people anything. Even make people to run away from them, even from Islām. Make people to be away to when they see this people. Because they don't have wisdom, they don't have anything. They thought they are – when they say this everybody must obey and accept. This people they don't have any teaching from ṭarīqah, ṭarīqah people [who] they are showing the good behav[iour] and to be polite with people as Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam was treat people. Not make them to run away. But other people they are still doing so what they are doing no benefit for them, no benefit for anybody.

And this people they are not from only this time. There was since after Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam thousand, maybe ten thousands, hundred thousand people doing this. They thought they are in right. And now more than one thousand four hundred year, they are doing this but no of them any have his name or to be mentioned with good. They forgotten – they have [been] forgotten. Nobody know about them. Now also, who try to do some like other people, also they will be forgotten. Only truth, it is whole time until Qiyāmah continue, in shā’a Llāh.

AllāhNone make us to follow them, good people, to be away from this people. Even no need to answer them because they don't have only any mind to accept. Only they have like concrete in their head. You cannot crush this what they are saying. So no need to discuss with them. Leave them. Better for us to look for us, ourself, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone save Ummah from this people.
