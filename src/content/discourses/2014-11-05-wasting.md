Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

People’s economies have been set up on wasting in this age. All of their economies have been set up on wasting. You need to spend so there is an economy. Islam forbids wasting. Our Holy Prophet (SAW) says, “When you are to make ablution, even if you are by a river, even when there is so much water, do not waste water.” Wasting is not good. These ones are saying, “The more you spend the better.” There is no other way this world can go on anyway. They have set up a system and the system is a means for their own destruction and their bad end. They are digging their own graves.

It is the wisdom of Allah that is End Times now. It has to be like this since it is End Times. Everything will reach its extreme so that Mahdi Alayhis Salam comes out, and then all hell breaks loose. This is also a sign. A whole family would be able to manage with the amount of water one person spends these days. The same with the food one eats. People of old times would manage and make ends meet. However, now as we said, everything has reached the peak of wasting and the peak of unruliness. This is also a sign that Doomsday is near. Now on, Allah will send him when people have lost hope. Who will he send? Mahdi Alayhis Salam Inshallah, Allah willing. Salvation is only possible through his means.

What our Holy Prophet says in his hadith: “His name will be like my name and his father’s name will be like my father’s name.” He will only fill the world with abundance (baraka), justice, and peace. Just as there is no peace now, there is no abundance, and everything is in chaos, it can only get better when he arrives. Truly, everywhere now, nobody is happy wherever you go. Inshallah the signs point that the time is near. May Allah make us reach those days as soon as possible Inshallah.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq. Al-Fatiha.
