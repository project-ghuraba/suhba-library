As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is telling people to look after the poor and needy ones, to help them. There are some poor people who don’t show it. They don’t show it out of modesty. They don’t say, “I am poor.” Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says to pay more attention to them. People become the means (wasīlah) for what they have been given; it is not from them. They are the means. The goodness that comes through them, becomes goodness for them as well. Who understand this are people with faith. Who don’t understand are people without, or with weak faith.

Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says, in ḥadīth sharīf, whoever is rich in the wealth of the world, is not really rich. Whoever gives of his wealth is rich, says our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Because many people possess a lot, but cannot give. They have a whole lot of everything but don’t give. They say they cannot give; they themselves say that. Whatever the wisdom is, they cannot give. It is a favour from AllāhNone to be generous and to help other people. Not everyone can be like that. It is not given to everyone. Who are given this, are the chosen servants of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. They have a mind.

Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam and the ṣaḥābah are the cleverest of people. They are people of very high intellect. They saw this beautiful thing and applied it. They never showed any stinginess. They were always generous, the most generous. It is something well-known. Even the unbelievers could not deny it.

Therefore, the wealth and riches of this world last until the grave. After the grave, no one will show respect or value you for it. Some people show respect and value to such people in this world, to get something in return. But they cannot get anything. Because Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla didn’t destine it for them. HeNone didn’t grant them the benefit of this beautiful thing. AllāhNone didn’t grant it to them so they wouldn’t benefit from this beautiful reward.

Therefore, people don’t know what is good and what is bad anymore. They say they are clever. If you are clever, you must know the good and the bad. We are living in the end of times anyway. They are showing the bad as good and the good as bad. And people are completely cheated. They are making a fool out of people. Therefore, people have lost their way. They get into many different troubles. May AllāhNone protect us. We are living in the end of times.

It is important to take our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam as an example, and to take hisNone companions as an example. It is our most important duty. No need to follow the example of this and that rich man in this world. They are of no benefit to you. They do not benefit even themselves. Benefit is in our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, the ṣaḥābah and the mashāyikh who show the beauty of ākhirah. Be on their way. And stay away from the way of those shayṭāns. They want to suck people’s blood. Instead of doing goodness to you, they try to suck your blood. May AllāhNone protect all of us from their evil and from the evil of the end of times, in shā’a Llāh.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone is saying for in Qur’ān to – There is poor people, believer, poor people, you cannot tell them, tell they are poor people, because they have honour to not ask from people something for themselves. Even you thought their situation good, but they are poor. This kind of people Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone like them. HeNone love them. And telling believer, mu’min, to look for them, because they not asking, you must look and give them, help them. This is order of AllāhNone, this people, it is good people.

Who, people, there is unfortunately people, sometimes needy people, who they are asking. And they are taking more than others. And many times happen this: needy people they cannot take what the people they was giving. Other people they taking this. This people they are not good in Divine Presence. Good people, other people. AllāhNone help people who help them, look for them. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, this mercy of AllāhNone, from HisNone mercy this people also, they can take. Because AllāhNone HeNone love them, HeNone not leave them, but sometimes make it difficult for them. But when difficulty coming, they have more reward, more love of AllāhNone for them.

And there is kind of people, AllāhNone, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam say richness, not with money or land, house, shops, malls. This is – can be have this, but this is, who has this, not this is mean he is rich. The rich one, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, who give, give for people. Distribute for people. Because this people, many of them, they cannot give. They have billions but they cannot give. They, many of them said also, “We cannot give. We have but we don't love to give and we cannot give. Even if we want to give, we cannot give.”

This is what situation in this world now. Many people, they are suffering and other[s], they are, even they work for them they are not looking after them, or not giving their right. Some many times, they are make people to work and not give them their wages or salary or what they done for this. But, this people not rich people. Why not rich people? Because richness only until the grave. After the grave, they will be very poor. But the who give, he is real rich, and he will be rich forever. Other one, they will be asked, and they will be regret. They will say, wish to say if we don't have any penny, to not give this what they in grave they ask for us. What you do with this? From where? What you are – Why you didn't do this? Why you didn't do that? So, they wish when they in grave, if they don't have any penny.

This is, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam and hisNone ṣaḥābah, companion, they was most intelligent, clever people. Because they know this is in dunya, you must give to be beloved for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, and to help people. Who help people, AllāhNone loves him, AllāhNone with him. And, the idiot people, who are, they thought, if they collect this money they will be good for them. Not giving for others.

For this we must – Our best example, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam and companion, and ṣaḥābah, and mashāyikh, in shā’a Llāh. All this good attribute with them. Bad attribute, especially these days, with this other people, who only make whole world running after richness who it is false richness. Even they make real, to make people idiot, making something in air. They said, this is money and this is good. And people with, they are running after this. Not looking for real one. Who – it is forever. Not only for live, or not only for one month or two months – forever.

You look, for this, you be use your brain. If you use brain, you will find happiness. If you are not, you are not be happy here, and not... whole time anxious, all time stressed, all time sad, all time afraid. You will be this. If you are, you follow Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, nothing affect you from dunyā, from this. What AllāhNone give you, you will be happy. Not looking for something. People they don't have a limit for their ṭama‘, greed. For their greed, no limit for. You give them thousand, they want hundred thousand. Give hundred thousand, million. Million, they be want billion. But billion, in thousand years you cannot finish. But they still not thinking for this.

AllāhNone forgive us. AllāhNone make us to follow Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam; the best example for – not for only Muslims, for whole human being. HeNone is best example, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
