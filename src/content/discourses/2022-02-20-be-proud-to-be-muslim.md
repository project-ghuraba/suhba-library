As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is saying in Qur’ān: "Wa-lā tahinū wa-lā taḥzanū wa-antumu l-a‘lawna in kuntum mu’minīn" (3:139). Humility is beautiful. It is good to show humility among believers. Towards unbelievers you can keep good adab, but don't think low of yourself in front of them for the way you dress, for your religion, for anything. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is saying this. You are the one on top. "They are more advanced than us, they are more this and that." Many people list their flaws as Muslims. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is making it clear not to mix humility with what is true. You can show humility, but when it comes to the truth, never look up to them. "If only I were like this or like that. If only I were like them." Don't say this because they don't have the favour AllāhNone has given you. People who are believers never lower themselves in front of worldly things. Whoever lowers himself in front of dunyā is low. Muslims can never be low. Unbelievers are the low ones. Faithless ones are low.

It is not our word this. It is the true word of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. You are not low, you are high – "antumu l-a‘lawn". Therefore, people who are Muslim oftentimes list their flaws compared to others. Do not ever do this. That is the weakness of your īmān. You are on the right way. You speak the truth. You are with the truth. You are loved by AllāhNone. AllāhNone has chosen to give this favour to you. May AllāhNone protect us, but so many of the people you look up to don't have this favour. They have nothing in the eyes of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. They have no worth, no value. It is important to be worthy and to have value in the presence of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. This is important. Many people don't realize this point and call it humility. Humility is good. When a normal person doesn't do anything to you, it is good to treat him well. It is a good manner. But if he acts arrogantly with you and you lower yourself, don't do this. You are the right one. If he wants to be higher, this way is open. The door is open. Everyone can be high.

People nowadays attack others when they see something good. The ones attacked become scared not understanding why this happens. Don't be scared. Those who attack the truth are low. You are high. Don't be scared of anything. A few people don't have the right to control you and all the ummah. They are worthless. They are people without value. May AllāhNone let us see this and act on it. May HeNone let us understand. Therefore, do not ever do this because you are Muslim and because you are in ṭarīqah. Let them dislike it. You don't have to tell them. Just keep on your way. And make shukr to AllāhNone, for AllāhNone has put you among these servants. HeNone has put you among people who are high and have rank. May AllāhNone let it endure.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla said in Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n: "Wa-lā tahinū wa-lā taḥzanū wa-antumu l-a‘lawna in kuntum mu’minīn" (3:139). Don't be, don't feel you are low. You don't be sad. You are the highest, higher between human being if you are believer, mu’min, mu’minīn. If you are believer, you are higher. Don't think yourself... There is, of course, to be humble. Good [to be] humble but humble for believer, for mu’mīns. For other people, of course, you can be humble but there is limit for this, for humble – to be good relation with people, to be good friend or anything. Okay this is no problem. But when coming thing for belief, don't think or don't let people to think you are low. No. You are higher. The lowest people who are not believer, who are not doing good thing. This is the lowest people.

The highest in the Divine Presence – the people, believer, who AllāhNone give them this provision for to be believer. You must be proud for this. Don't shy from people – "I am Muslim, oooh I am shy to say I am Muslim." No. You can, don't afraid, don't be shy. You are the highest. Who will, must be shy and afraid? Who are not believer, who doing bad thing. Nowadays, these people they are spreading all over world. They are attacking believer, attacking good people who following good behav[iour] of human being. They are attacking, attacking them and this, who attacked by these people, they are afraid. They cannot say anything. Even now, they make law with them, with this low people. So don't be cheated with the people to think you are, they are better than you. No. The best people – who are, they are believer and this is word of AllāhNone our Creator. HeNone said, "You are the highest if you are believer."

Who not believer, not not. Of course, there is many types of them. Many of them they are not like to be with this people but they afraid to say anything against this people. This lowest people. The lowest and lowest people they are now controlling whole world and trying to spread in everywhere, every country. Who not allowed for them to spread, they are making against them problem, speaking very bad thing. But we are with Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. We not afraid from them and we are not humble against them. You must be proud. We are, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, Muslim. We have ṭarīqah. We have sharī‘ah. We have everything. AllāhNone give us this good thing. Don't... Many people, who live in non-Muslim country, they, sometime they feel they are not in the same level or same position with these people. You are better than them in Divine Presence. Don't look what for people, looking for which one it is best. You must look for AllāhNone, what HeNone, best in HisNone Divine Presence. AllāhNone make us, all of us, with this level, highest level, in shā’a Llāh.
