Salam, Fa-hyyna Rabbana bi salam. Wa adkhilna l-jannata bi Lutfika wa Karamika. Daraka daru s-Salam. Tabarakta Rabbana wa ta'alaita. Wa lakal Hamda wa ‘ala ma qadaita. Nastaghfiruka Allahumma wa natubu ilaika. O Lord, forgive us for the sake of al-Habib al-Akram!

Today is the first day of Sha'ban Mu'azzama. MashaAllah la quwwata illa biLlah. Bismillah wa biLlah. Ma'a Rasulillah, Sallallahu alaihi wa sallam.

May our burdens leave us! My we be enlightened! May we be joyful! May we not be sad and feel downcast! Rabbul Izzat, The Lord of Glory, the Owner of the seven Heavens, the Owner of the Divine Throne, our Lord, has written us as His servants. How beautiful! How nice!

O Lord, forgive us for the sake of al-Habib al-Akram! We are in the year 1434 of the Hijri calendar, the first day of Sha'ban Mu'azzama! This month is called Sha'ban Mu'azzama, the Magnificent Month. Why is that so? Janabul Mawla, who dressed Habibullah with the robe of Majesty, in this holy month, holy day, holy night when He had placed him in the womb of his most honored mother.

As-salamu alaikum, Ay yaran Shah Mardan, Help us - Madad O Shah Mardan! May we be joyful! May we be honorable! May we be dressed with the robe of Majesty also! Today is the day when our Prophet, who is the Glory of the universe, entered his mother's womb and the month he appeared therein, Sha'ban Mu'azzama. Sha'ban Mu'azzama, may you all respect it! It is Sha'ban Mu'azzama. May we ask from the Lord! Many events will happen before it is completed. The armies of the heavens are coming down to finish all oppressors.

Fatiha.
