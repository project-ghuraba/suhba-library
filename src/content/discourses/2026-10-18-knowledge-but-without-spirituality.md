As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
أَلَا إِنَّ أَوْلِيَاءَ اللَّهِ لَا خَوْفٌ عَلَيْهِمْ وَلَا هُمْ يَحْزَنُونَ
<br>“Alā inna awliyā Allāhi lā khawfun ʿalayhim wa-lā hum yaḥzanūn”<br>
“Verily on the Awliyā (Friends of Allah) there is no fear, nor shall they grieve.” (Qurʼān 10:62). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

Yesterday night, alḥamdulillāh we were in a gathering for the sake of Allah, remembering Awliyā’ullāh, all Awliyā’ullāh especially Sayyidina Shaykh ʿAbdul Qādir Al-Gaylāni. He’s one of the greatest Awliyā’ullāh. Allah gave him this honor and those who follow him also get this honor to be a beloved one for Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla.

All Awliyā’ullāh are oceans. They are oceans of mercy, oceans of knowledge, oceans of goodness. They teach people every good thing. You cannot find this special thing. He not only has knowledge but also spiritual madad from Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. They are opening the hearts of people making them to come to Islām, to be sincere, to be really good people. Maybe there are many people who are more knowledgeable than these people but Allah has given them a specialty to open the hearts of people.

Once, the son of Shaykh ʿAbdul Qādir Al-Gaylāni saw his father speaking. He said, “Maybe I know better than him. Why are these people following my father!” Shaykh ʿAbdul Qādir Al-Gaylāni knew his heart. He said, “You must give a ṣuḥbah to people.” So he observed him: one week, the next week. Every week, people were disappearing. People were running away. This was good knowledge. Everything was good. But it was knowledge without spirituality.

So, after that he asked his father, “Why is that happening? Am I speaking something wrong?” He said, “No. But you are speaking from your ego, from no spirituality.” So these people are coming like bees coming for honey, but they are finding no honey there, so they are running away. In the end, nobody was coming.

Everybody wanted Shaykh ʿAbdul Qādir Al-Gaylāni.

So this is the power of these people whom many ignorant ones do not accept. Maybe they find people around them but when they understand a little bit, there won’t be anyone left around those who don’t respect spirituality and don’t respect Awliyā’ullāh.

May Allah bless them. And make their mercy be upon us, upon all of us, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
