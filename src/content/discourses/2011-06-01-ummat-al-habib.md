Nation of the Beloved (saw) - (ar) Mawlana Shaykh Mohamad Nazim al Haqqani an Nakshibendi 29 Jumādā ath-Thāniya, 1432 A.H. Wednesday, June 1, 2011 Translated from Arabic by: http://saltanat.org/ BismillahirRahmanirRahim Sheikh Hisham Efendi, Prophet al salaatu was salaam, said, “Ad-deenu-nasiha (Religion is advice)”. Religion is based on advice. 'Nasiha' is suhbah, association with you. Just a few words for the sake of the situation: 'hawl' is fear and 'ahwal' is the situation. To speak about what is taking place and the fear of the nation about what is taking place. Prophet (sas) is an Arab Prophet, may Allah increase his honor, light and blessings. If the people of Syria had kept the praising of Prophet (sas) in all the mosques, every Friday, such hardship would never have come on them; but they did not keep this order. First of all, every Friday a group of people went around in a procession praising Prophet (sas). They would take turns standing up to praise the Prophet (sas), making dhikr, and asking the Prophet (sas) to make intercession from the Lord Almighty. But, the old scholars in Sham (Damascus) passed away, may Allah have mercy on their souls. Since they went to the Rahmet of Allah (jwa), there is no one left. Now only ignorant scholars and young ones remain. Sham is now in the hands of boys. We say nothing of the government. We are talking about what is necessary fo the people of Damascus to do. They have left what they were supposed to do. They left the footsteps of their ancestors, and grandparents. And the scholars left the way of the righteous ones. Islam is based on the honoring and glorifying of the Prophet (sas). This is the base of the religion. To love the Prophet (sas) attracts people to follow him and follow in his footsteps. Sham and its tribes have been mentioned in more than 40 traditions of the Prophet (sas). He spoke of the honor and value of Syria and its people; but unfortunately, Damascenes did not keep or treasure the honor that Allah (awj) granted to them and they became busy with this world. They became similar to the Jews in busying themselves with this world. If we speak, we will talk a long time about this point but I will mention a few things of which I must remind [you] because Allah Almighty ordered us to remind:

Fa Inna ‘dhikra tanfa’u l mu’mimeen (51:55)

[Remind.] Verily, the Reminder will benefit the believers. And nowadays, no one is left to remind the nation. Reminding makes the servants of Allah attentive. There is the one who is reminding - mudhakkir; and there is the one who is remembering - dhaakir. Praising the Prophet (sas) is the base because praising strengthens the love between a person and the Prophet of the end of time (sas) and establishes love and connection between the servant and the Prophet (sas). This love attracts the hearts of people to follow in the footsteps of the Prophet (sas).

The Condition and the Misfortunes of the Nation of the Beloved (saw) 1432 They left this; therefore they were left too. All other nations used to admire the people of Sham. But when they left these habits, such respect and glory was taken from them because they have not kept the praising of the Prophet (sas). Now they have occupied the land of Syria. The main reason the danger came is because the Syrian people, during Ottoman rule, opposed and sided against the Ottoman sultans, may Allah raise their station in paradise. Curse and trouble came on the Islamic world - 70 prophets, including the Prophet, Master of beginning and end (sas), cursed those who left the unity of one nation. The ones who left the united [ummah] were misled and deceived into adopting nationalism by the devils in Europe, instead of being one, united Muslim nation. All the curse and trouble came on the beloved’s nation because of nationalism. They started saying, “We are Arabs. Why should we follow the Ottomans who were...”. The Ottomans were Muslim, they were respecting you, they were glorifying the Arab nations and bestowing generosity on you. What harm were the Ottomans bringing on you? But for this cursed nationalism you disrespected the Ottomans. All the hatred and all the problems came because of nationalism. All the Arabs and non-Arabs (the ajam) were under the flag of the beloved Prophet (sas) and the Islamic khilafat (caliphate), the trust (emanet) that came to the Ottomans. It was granted to the Ottoman Sultan - Sultan Selim. He was awakened and ordered in his dream by Prophet (sas) to go to Egypt and to collect the holy trust, the holy relics and the flag and to move the khilafat to lstanbul. They don't know this, but the new generations were cursing the Ottomans that they took them backwards. And now look at you! You are moving forward? In your own country you are humiliated and the whole world is laughing at you and saying, “Leave the Muslims to kill each other”. You are teaching such things because of nationalism. Nationalism became a great curse on the nation of the Beloved (sas), with the Turks also saying, “We are Turks”, the Kurds saying, “We are Kurds”, and the Arabs saying, “We are Arabs”. This is making divisions among the Muslims. Allah Almighty forbade the Islamic nation to divide into groups. Oh Arabs, don’t you understand the order of Allah Almighty? What is this? Then, Allah left them to fight each other and now they are fighting among themselves. It has reached the point that we are waiting for America, Russia and China to intervene in our concerns. Allah Almighty said in the Holy Qur'an, “Isn’t Allah enough for His servants?”. Oh Arabs, why are you not understanding the Holy Qur’an? Why run to Russia or China in order to correct your situations? Isn’t Allah enough to correct the situation and for you to raise the banner of the Prophet (sas)? Isn’t it true, Hisham Efendi, that religion is advice? Now, this is advice. If they are rebellious and they do not accept this advice..., oh Arab nations. They are saying,”We are the Arab nation.” Leave this nationalism. My advice is that you say, “We are the nation of the Beloved Prophet (sas)”. And the Beloved is saying that religion is about advice. If they are rebellious, another tradition says, “Those who do not accept advice, bad tidings for them”. If you like it, you like. If you do not like, the way to hell is open. Enter it! Those on the way of nationalism are on the way to hell. This world is Jehannum now. You are already in hell in this world; and later on, hereafter, you will go to the fire of Hell. I am sorry to say this, my Arabic is little. Where are your scholars? Why are they not making clear why such hardship and problems have come on the Syrians? Why have you left praising the Prophet (sas)? Because the Wahabis and Salafis don’t accept it? Let both of them go to hell! Allah will judge them. In the near future, none of them will remain on the face of the earth. We are Rabbaniyun. We are Believers and we are Rabbaniyun, because Allah Almighty is saying in a holy verse,

wa lakin kunu rabbaniyyeen... (3:79)

[Be of the Lord... (Qaribullah)

The Condition and the Misfortunes of the Nation of the Beloved (saw) 1432

Be ye faithful servants of the Lord... (Picthal)

Be ye worshippers of Him Who is truly the Cherisher of all... (Yusuf Ali)] Be Rabbaniyun from the holy Qur'an! From where did you bring such titles and names to call yourselves? - “We are salafis; we are wahabbis; this or that”. Why? From where did you bring this salafi or wahabbi? What honor does it bring? Is it better to call yourself Salafi or Wahabi, when Allah Almighty is saying, “Be Rabbaniyun”? Why don’t they call themselves Rabbaniyun? In the Hadith ul-Qudsi, Allah Almighty says, “Oh My servant, obey me and I will make you Rabbaniyun. And if you order something to be, it will be.” Allah Almighty said the truth. And this is their punishment. The punishment came on them. If they do not return to the right way, out of 70 people, only one will remain; 69 may leave if they do not return to the right way. There was no need for this war, this trouble or this hatred. Allah Almighty sent the Holy Qur’an to bring hearts close to each other, not to move them far from each other. Oh people in Syria! They claim that praising the Prophet (sas) is bida’ (a new invention) and haram. From where are you calling this praising of the Prophet (sas) haram? Allah and His Prophet (sas) made clear what is haram. Bring your evidence that praising the Prophet (sas) and that gathering for his praise is haram. Bring it! Bring your evidence. Oh satanic people, just as the Jews were envious of the Prophet (sas), so too are you envious of the Prophet of the end of time. You are such satanic people and Allah will take His revenge on you. Astaghfirullah, astaghfirullah, astaghfirullah. The ones who do not accept advice, bad tidings for them. Oh Allah Almighty, we ask forgiveness and we are repenting, declaring our repentence, and returning to Your way. Please forgive us and grant us forgiveness. Ya tawwab tub aleyna. Ya tawwab tub aleyna. Grant us Your forgiveness. Allahu Akbar al Akbar. Tawba Ya Rabbi. Wa min Allah al Tawfeeq. Fatiha.
