As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Wa-dhkurū Llāha kathīran la‘allakum tufliḥūn" (8:45). Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is telling us to always make dhikr of AllahNone. Always remember AllāhNone. Only then will there be salvation, and your end will be good. Dhikr is the prayers and the tasbīḥāt (glorification) we make. To remember AllāhNone in every way is dhikr. It is the remembrance of AllāhNone. Therefore, shukr to AllāhNone, the daily duties and all of the Naqshbandī awrād are things that our Holy ProphetNone did, asked from us, and taught us. All of them are sunnah, from the morning prayer until tahajjud of the next day.

All the worship performed – the dhikr, awrād and nafl prayers. The farḍ prayers of course must be done. Otherwise, there is sunnah and sunnah mu’akkadah. There is sunnah mu’akkadah and wājib. They all belong to the way shown by our Holy ProphetNone, what the ṣaḥābah were ordered to do, and what we continue to follow. People who do so will be saved. Some people ask what else they should do. Keep on doing this, and don't make it less. You will be doing the most beautiful thing. And you will be giving yourself the greatest benefit. You can also deal with your business in this world. You can do them together. When you remember AllāhNone in the work of this world, it is counted as worship too, because you do it for the pleasure of AllāhNone. When you walk on the way of AllāhNone and strengthen both yourself and your family, and when you keep up your worship, everything is counted as a reward and as worship for you. You will be with AllāhNone twenty-four hours a day.

This is the benefit of ṭarīqah – to live, remembering AllāhNone, twenty-four hours a day. We should not forget HimNone for a second. Of course, mankind is forgetful. Therefore, we should make intention in the morning. "Innamā l-a‘mālu bi n-niyyāt." Actions are accepted according to their intentions. Even if you don't do anything, your intention will be accepted as if it was done. It is good to start the day intending to do everything with the remembrance of AllāhNone, and for the pleasure of AllāhNone. May AllāhNone let this state be always upon us, in shā’a Llāh. This is the beauty of ṭarīqah and the value of ṭarīqah. One doesn't live for nothing then. You do not come and go for nothing. You will spend your life with the favours, grace, blessings and mercy of AllāhNone. Without these, you will live for nothing. They say one comes and goes like weed. But the weed has usefulness, and will not be held accountable. You do have an account. It is alright if the weed lives for nothing. But if a person lives for nothing, he will be asked, "Why did you live such an empty life? You were given so many favours, and a mind. You were shown the way. But you lived in vain. Give your account," they will say. You will be in difficulty then.

May AllāhNone protect us. May we always be from those who remember and make dhikr of AllāhNone, in shā’a Llāh.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Wa-dhkurū Llāha kathīran la‘allakum tufliḥūn" (8:45). Saying Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla in Qur’ān, "All time make remember MeNone. Don't forget." For this, dhikr meaning "to remember". For this, in awrād of ṭarīqah, saying dhikr, name of AllāhNone, to be whole time with HimNone. This also, many types of remembering. Some of them, to make awrād, dhikr, mentioning HisNone name. Other thing, to every time you remember Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla: "HeNone is with me, He isNone – I am HisNone servant, I am doing this for sake of AllāhNone. I am going here for sake of AllāhNone. Coming for HisNone also. Doing this for HimNone." Every time, to remember. This is our way of ṭarīqah, to make people to be remember AllāhNone twenty-four hour.

If you are even going, making your business, your work, other thing, but your intention to be with Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla – This, your business, your work also, AllāhNone accepts as worshipping for HimNone. Because you do this, twenty-four hours you are remembering your Lord, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, and HeNone is happy with you. You are whole time with HisNone presence and HisNone blessings. Because when you do what AllāhNone orders... Our ṭarīqah – all ṭuruq, ṭarīq like this – to make people to be with AllāhNone; to put HisNone love in your heart. And your whole thing with HimNone.

We are beginning our day from fajr time, beginning to make five time praying. And with this praying we make tasbīḥ, we make ṣalawāt, we make reading āyāt, sūrah... All this coming from teaching of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. From Fajr, Ẓuhr, ‘Aṣr, Maghrib, ‘Ishā’, until next day, and tahajjud. Through this, there is farḍ and sunnah, sunnah mu’akkadah, and wājib. This all we try – our mashāyikh, they teach us, especially Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim. He was not letting any sunnah or thing to... not doing. He was doing all this, and he teach us. And this coming whole from Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam sunnah. No anything out of this thing, out of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam what heNone was doing. Is coming through ṣaḥābah, through imām of madhhab, and through this way of ṭarīqah. All this, no, you cannot find anything out of this way. And this make you twenty-four hour with presence of AllāhNone. You remember AllāhNone whole time.

Because, we see many people, they are not giving any importance for sunnah. Many of them they leave sunnah. They are not looking for sunnah. But, sunnah, it is big favour for yourself. You mustn't leave it. But we are, it is for, we give naṣīḥah, advice for people, other people to not do this. But, of course, you cannot force them. But, for naṣīḥah, advice, "Ad-dīnu naṣīḥah," Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying. You can tell them. And you can tell them, not with hard. Soft. "Oh brother, this is sunnah. You like to do? Okay. You not, you didn't do?" – don't say anything for them. Only you do this, you will be, your life, full with blessing, with reward, in shā’a Llāh. You not going life without anything, empty life. No. Your life it will be full of blessing, and full of love of AllāhNone.

Many people, they are saying, "Our life is empty." Of course empty, because you not doing, not remembering AllāhNone. Who remember AllāhNone, his life full with blessing and with good things. Who not remember, he is unlucky, because AllāhNone gave him this opportunity to – He also can, everything in front of him, he can do and fill his life, not making his life empty. Some saying of they saying: "They are like grass, coming empty, going empty." Grass can be empty, no problem. Nothing, no another life for grass. But for you, you have another life after this life. It must be you take from here. If you go empty, you will not find anything there. But if you go full, you find everything there. This is... who is empty here, he will be empty in next life. Who takes with him from here many things, he will find them there, in shā’a Llāh.

AllāhNone make us to follow our way of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam – ṭarīq meaning way – and to make our life with meaning, with full of blessing, and love of AllāhNone, love of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, and love of awliyā’u Llāh, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone bless you.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
