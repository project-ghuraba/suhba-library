Their names are sent to the offices, to the ministries, to the governorship, to district governorship, to companies. They write the names of their children at the top of list. They take one, two, three, four, five, leave 40-50 of them empty handed. So these kids are studying but our wish is that this youth not to go wasted. It is not a big deal to get a diploma in this country. If they want I can bring diploma from Sorbonne. We can bring from England, Oxford. Because they print both money and diploma. When you give money, you get whatever you want. But it has no value, no value, no value with diploma.

It is the future of these kids. These kids are suffering here. How much are they paid in this student dormitory? Do you give them monthly? Or only for accommodation? Their parents are supporting them for the moment. In the beginning they were giving 40 thousand. You remember. You can't even go out with 40 thousand lira (old Turkish currency). If you give them 50, he throws it into your face. Who saw at that time? How far we've gone in 8 years? Think about it. Now these kids need at least 1,5 million or 2 million except accommodation. Even 2 million isn't enough. They have all kinds of needs. What will they do with it? At that time 40 thousand was ok. How many times did the currency depreciate? From 2 million, fifty times our currency depreciated. Isn't it so? Huseyin I outscore you in math.

M: 50 times depreciated. It was 40 now it is 2000.

M: We have big amount of money now.

Sh.E: When they get a diploma they accept even a small salary. They accept 3-5 million salary just to have a job. Even that is low, because when you add couple of spoons of sugar in it dissolves, isn't it? When you put ten spoons, starts to stay. When you put 20 spoons - it means it is saturated. This country had enough of educated people with diploma. No need for people with diploma anymore. Unfortunately everybody is demanding a diploma. Nobody wants to be self-employed. Nobody wants to learn handcrafts. How many faculties, branches are there in a university? Say it Huseyin. Huseyin? Say.

M: I don't know in total. There are many branches.

Sh.E: Approximately?

M: Four, five.

Sh.E: Faculty. It can't be 4 or 5. Say fifteen. Fifteen kinds of expertise they teach. I hear this branch from Huseyin for the first time. They invent new branches every year in order to make people unemployed. As if they'll find a job when the name changes. If you send the deputies to your faculty. They should send the MPs first. They should pass the prep class then go in the parliament. None of them understands politics. Tell me if there is anyone, who knows politics. We pulled Fettullah Efendi's beard also. None of them has any idea. This one is a professor, professor doctor. At least he understands. Why does he collect wood from the mountains? Bring the wood into the parliament, so that they fight each other. Fifteen branches.

I saw a book in London, this big. It is written 'Murtad' on it. This big and with small fonts. That book gives information about the kinds of fields of expertise and which fields people may choose. That thick book gives information about fields of expertise. The university teaches 15 fields. It doesn't fill half of that thick book. So these kids, there are countless doctors, women doctors, dentists, pharmacists, lawyers. Countless. So what does this society need? Only 15 fields of expertise? No need except those? That book has that content. There are one thousand, maybe ten thousand fields of expertise because it is a thick book.

We don't teach our people, community, kids. This kid wants to study chemistry at the university. "You won't study chemistry, you study engineering." This one wants to be an engineer. "You go study agriculture". This one wants to be an architect. No, you'll be a lawyer. How long will this foolishness continue? A person succeeds according to his talent. A forced doctor is not a doctor. A forced lawyer isn't a lawyer. A forced pharmacist is not a pharmacist. No benefit in studying something unwillingly. Our education system has failed. But since they already started, what shall we do? You will continue until you get the diploma that you are running after.

Where is Kemal? Is he here? I told him, he went and got the diploma. After you get the diploma, make 3 copies of it. First one you put right across the entrance. Anyone coming can see that you have a diploma. Second one you put in your office or bedroom. When you wake up you see " Oh mashaAllah we have a diploma". The third copy, you wrap it and take it to the kitchen. You boil water every morning with a ceremony. You'll put it inside. Boil and drink it. This diploma has no other function. They are offended by me. What else will you do other than boil and drink its juice? I boil it at least. You hang it dry on the wall. Boiled. Brew tea with it, drink and wrap, put it there. When it melts, bring the second copy. That's all the function of the diploma.

Did these kids eat? Our crew is there. Good for you. Keep your prayers. Be careful about your prayers. Do not loosen up. Keep respect for each other. Do not bully the weak ones. Don't make jokes by hurting each other. Be gentlemen students. Should be clear that you are educated, not like ordinary people outside. Be careful! Be careful about the prayers! Keep your manners! Keep the company of good ones! You'll be harmed from bad company. You won't be harmed from good company. But bad company leads you to every badness. Adapt to good things, not bad things. There are good habits and bad habits. Why would you fall into bad habits instead of good ones? Everybody is smart here. They are all smart but bad company is evil. Evil deceives. Don't be deceived!
