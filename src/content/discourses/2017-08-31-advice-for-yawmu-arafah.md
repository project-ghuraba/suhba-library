As-salāmu ‘alaykum wa raḥmatu Llāhi wa barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīn wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

Shukr to AllāhNone, it is ‘Arafah day. Ḥujjāj (pilgrims) are standing for waqfah. May AllāhNone bless it, may AllāhNone accept it. May AllāhNone let others go there. It is a good worship. And it is the best to carry out Allāh'sNone order because AllāhNone created us and put us in this world. In this world - "Wa mā l-ḥayatu d-dunyā illā la‘ibun wa lahwun wa lad-dāru l-ākhiratu khayrun." (6:32) This world is a place of entertainment, says Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla and HeNone created us. A person who knows this wisdom lives in contentment. He tries to spend this life in a good way. The life after this one is the life which is better. The better life is that life, says Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla. This world...

People are thinking sometimes and most say, "We cannot do anything." As long as you do what AllāhNone says, you've done it. There is nothing else to do here. You should do what AllāhNone orders. That's it. And some don't even think about it. They assume they came here to live only for this life. Sometimes when someone passes away, many times they say, "See, this world is meaningless. We should enjoy ourselves because this world is empty." It is empty but there is the hereafter. They don't think about it. They do the opposite. Instead of looking at death and taking a lesson, they say, "We'll die too so we should have fun and be happy before we die." And that doesn't work. As we said, Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla put us in this world for a short term as a test. Everyone will go to ākhirah. Send good deeds to ākhirah, says Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla. And in dunyā if a person is pleased with what AllāhNone gives, he will still live happily. And he is even happier for believing in AllāhNone for the end of this way is not empty. He thinks about the real life after this life and becomes happy.

The orders of AllāhNone, as we said, today one of the biggest orders is the farḍ of ḥajj, the hour of ‘Arafah, the ‘Arafah time. It's place, ‘Arafāt, is not here but ‘Arafah day is everywhere. Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla gives its thawāb to everyone. But to become ḥajjī, you have to be present there. And they are there now. May AllāhNone add us to their prayers, may our prayers be with them, in shā’a Llāh. May it help Islām. They are oppressing Muslims everywhere. They are not seeing Muslims as human. If millions of Muslims die, it's ok. If something happens to a foot of just one non-Muslim, the world nearly ends. But there is a reward and thawāb near AllāhNone. It doesn't go for nothing. As we said, today is ‘Arafah day. And our waẓifah today, in shā’a Llāh, to recite 1,000 Ikhlāṣ until the evening, in shā’a Llāh. May AllāhNone bless it. May AllāhNone let us reach more years with the victory of Islām and Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām as our leader. Oppression is everywhere. Therefore, when Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām comes, oppressors will get what they deserve. We are not responsible for doing anything now because every action we make is in the hands of shayṭan who is playing with us as with toys. They are doing as they like. They are doing such things that there is more oppression. Therefore, it's not the time of action. When Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām comes, the whole world will become Muslim under his order with Allāh'sNone permission. Oppressors will get what they deserve, suffering ones will get their rights, in shā’a Llāh.

Today, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are now in holy days, especially afternoon before day of ‘Īd, time of ‘Arafāt. ‘Arafāt for Makkah, near Makkah, for ‘Arafah - Yawmu ‘Arafah, for all of world. It is holy and it is especial occasion. Now there is very limited people because they are not allowed for everybody to go to ḥajj. They are lucky because they are becoming ḥajjī and making order of AllāhNone. To be ḥajjī AllāhNone say for Sayyidinā Ibrāhīm ‘alayhi s-salām, "Wa adhdhin fi n-nāsi bil-ḥajji ya’tūka rijālā wa ‘alá kulli ḍāmir." (22:27) Call them and they will come for you. Who hear this calling from Sayyidinā Ibrāhīm ‘alayhi s-salām he will come and he will be lucky to be in that place and to make order of AllāhNone.

And this life it is like, "Wa mā l-ḥayātu d-dunyā illā la‘ibun wa lahwun," (6:32) - it is like a play and enjoyment. AllāhNone give example, example for, "Wa lad-dāru l-ākhiratu khayrun lil-ladhīna yattaqūn." (6:32) This for whole people it is like a playing, like a theater. Somebody enjoying, somebody not enjoying. But who are accepting next life, even they are here, also happy. They will be more happy in next life. It is better for you Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla saying. Because we are what we do - sometimes weak people they are thinking it is routine. We are this morning we wake up, we come, we pray and after we pray Ẓuhr we looking and become ‘Aṣr, after ‘Aṣr we pray ‘Aṣr, Maghrib ‘Ishā’, For believer this is, this also like routine. Every day we do this. We see one day it's finish, one week finish, every but it is same. For other people also it's same. But for us some people they asking, "it is enough what we do?" Yes it is enough. Allāh HeNone give you health and HeNone give you this belief. You are doing same thing every day it is very good, no need to be worried, no to be anxious what will do? You are in right way don't worry.

But there is people they are doing same and they are become real worried. Because after one year they become changing their outside. After five year they are also become changing they become more worried. What is this? And suddenly they hear they one of their friends he passed away, he died and becoming very sad, very anxious. After they become try to make themself - they said, "Life it is so short. We mustn't be sad. You must not be looking for this. We must more enjoy ourself, we must be drink more, we must be make try to enjoy ourself more and more." This is what they think. It is wrong thinking. Because every time life becoming, they becoming more old. People before they was handsome, they maybe they coming for them. Maybe she was beautiful they be coming for them also people looking for them. But when they are becoming older and they becoming more stressed, more worried because they no aim for their life. But our aim to be for next life what AllāhNone promised. HeNone said, this is enjoying playing life but next life it is better for who are worried and who was afraid from to do bad thing.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are now we are happy because it is blessed time, holy time and, in shā’a Llāh, for until Maghrib we must do 1000 time Ikhlāṣu sh-Sharīf, in shā’a Llāh. And we are asking to be our du‘ā’ acceptable like ḥajjī in ‘Arafāt. Some people from our people they make some this is real it was bid‘ah. What bid‘ah? Not sometimes we must say, because some bid‘ah when you do, people they thought it is from ProphetNone. After Ẓuhr they was waiting standing up and making waqfah like in ‘Arafāt. Mawlānā he wasn't, he didn't accept this. He said don't do this. So we are only making du‘ā’ to be our du‘ā’ acceptable like this people in ‘Arafāt. And AllāhNone give us, in shā’a Llāh who are not going to ḥajj to be there also. And we hope next year to be victory of Islām with Sayyidinā Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām. Because now Muslim, nowadays Muslim, they are everywhere they make them like slaughtering, killing. Everything they are doing for Muslim, oppression for Muslim. If million of Muslim they are died nobody ask for them. But for if anything happen for another people they are making too much voice.

But, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are believing when Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām coming he will be take right of Muslim and he will punish who oppression people. And we are, we don't have any order to do anything now against any people, anyone, because we are knowing everything now under control of shayṭān and his follower. Who say I'm going to fight against them, he is also with them nowadays. You cannot to move any step without their order or without their acceptable. So we are saying for whole Muslim, don't do anything and wait until Sayyidinā Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām come to be whole world it will be, in shā’a Llāh, under his command... his commander and there will be justice. Not like this shayṭān and his follower oppression, it will be not be oppression that time.

We are praying for him to come and we are saying this advice for our people because maybe you make your – to make something small and after from this what small action, they destroy thousand, millions, countries. Be careful. Be awaken. Don't be cheated with this people. This is, in shā’a Llāh, order for ‘Arafāt day we are we saying because people they are ignorant. What we want now – you must ask for guidance, you must ask for Sayyidinā Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām, Sayyidinā ‘Īsá ‘alayhi s-salām, in shā’a Llāh. Maybe, in shā’a Llāh, for next year we be with them, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq al-Fātiḥah.
