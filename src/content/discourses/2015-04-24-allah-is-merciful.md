Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi Rasulillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

“La taqnatu min rahmatillah, innallaha yaghfiruz zunuba jamia, innahu huwal ghafurur rahim.” (Zumar: 53)

Allah Azza wa Jalla is quite merciful. The Ayat Karima says never to lose hope in the mercy of Allah. He says Allah Azza wa Jalla is the Ghafur, much forgiving. Some people forgive while others do not. But Allah Azza wa Jalla forgives and pardons.

We read in the Hadith Sharif: For the sake of Friday and on this occasion, Allah (JJ) forgives 600,000 people deserving of hell every Friday. He takes them out of hell and brings them to paradise. If you look at it, very few people remain in hell. Of course, Allah forgives people who do not associate partners (shirk) with Him. Those who associate partners with him are left in hell. They are in hell forever. Those who are forgiven are Muslims, believing in Allah, and those who have said the confession of faith (Kalima-e Shahadat). Of course, they have done things deserving of hell. Everything has a punishment and everybody has something to suffer for. Every Friday, Allah (JJ) forgives 600,000 of those people deserving of hell. This shows both the value of Friday and the mercy of Allah. Some people give fear. اوﺮُ ّﻔَِﻨُﺗ َﻻوَ اوﺮُ ﺸِّ َﺑوَ اوﺮُ ﺴِّ َﻌُﺗ َﻻوَ اوﺮُ ﺴِّ َﯾ The Hadith Sharif: “Yassiru wa la tuassiru, wa bashiru wa la tunaffiru.” “Make it easy, do not make it hard, and give glad tidings, do not make people hate.” Allah’s mercy is great. Believe only in Allah, and make progress in the path He has shown. Inshallah all sins are forgiven once saying, “La ilaha illAllah, Muhammadun rasulullah”

Allah is just and forgives His own rights. Do not interfere in the rights of others, in the rights of servants. Allah (JJ) says: “This is not My right. It is the right of the servant.” You will only be forgiven if that servant forgives you. There is a punishment for whatever you do not do: prayer (namaz), supplication (dua), fasting (sawm), etc. They call it “between Allah and the servant”. Allah may forgive if He wishes, or He may throw in hell if He wishes. They are His rights. Nobody can interfere and nobody can question, “Why did you do this?” Allah Azza wa Jalla syas, “My mercy has surpassed my anger.” Nobody can have so much mercy. That is why these Fridays are beautiful days. There are hours of acceptance (of duas).

Inshallah, may Allah make us amongst servants who are forgiven. May we live in health, soundness and blessings (baraka) Inshallah. May it be happiness for us here and hereafter. For the sake of these Fridays, and with the sake and blessings of the holy night we passed Inshallah. May its blessings be upon us. The Three Months (period of Rajab, Shaban, and Ramadan) have just started. May Allah make us complete it. May He make us reach many years with Islam’s grandeur and might Inshallah. May we reach Mahdi Alayhis Salam as soon as possible.

Bi Hurmatil Fatiha.

Shaykh Muhammad Mehmet Adil

24 April 2015, Akbaba Dargah, Sabah Namaz
