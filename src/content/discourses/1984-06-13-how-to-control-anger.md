Anger is the worst characteristic of our egos and affecting on our faith and destroying our certainty. And also we are losing our peace and satisfaction inside and outside. Therefore, it is most dangerous and worst characteristic - anger. Ghaḍabu n-nafsani - that belonging to our egos. Who is getting angry by his ego, destroying everything inside and outside. And Grandsheikh was saying anger never appears in a believer till that anger damaging and destroying his faith, taking away light of faith so that the fire of anger blazing. Never blazing the fire of anger without blowing away the lights of faith. Therefore, who dies on anger dies and no iman with him. That is most dangerous and worst.

Remedy is to be patient. No one can do anything for his anger, says Grandsheikh. Only who may get up nighttimes, before fajr even one quarter of an hour, and making wudu then praying 2 rakats for shukr, wudu. And then after salam standing up towards Qibla and saying 100 times Ya Halim, Ya Halim, Ya Halim, Ya Halim... The benefit, the benefit of that holy name makes our anger to come down little by little. Even it may be a water dropping on a stone. Water is very soft but long time making a hole in a granite stone. Don't say that "I am doing and never seeing any changing from my anger". At least, you must understand that "Alhamdulillah my anger is not going away, but also it is not going to be more. If I am not saying Ya Halim, maybe should be more anger. But alhamdulillah now it is kept to be on same level or it should be more dangerous for me." Because every time anger going to be more.

Therefore, you may do that as Grandsheikh advising us. And that is the remedy, remedy for anger. And everyone in need for that remedy. In our time the most patient nation is we know cold-blood people, English people. They are also becoming too angry, too angry people; quickly coming to be angry. What about others? Mediterranean people, all of them fire. No good. It is dangerous and to be halim, calm people... Prophet, peace be upon him, was saying: halim who is not angry person, who may control his anger mostly was going to be a prophet but no [more] prophethood. Finished. But who has such a hilm (forbearance/patience) to angry, getting a control on his anger, coming to be prophet, if there was prophethood. But no prophethood, finished.

He meant to say that the rank of no-angry people going to be at the same rank of prophets. But it is so difficult. You can't find anything more difficult than keeping your anger. You may say thousand and thousand dhikr, thousand and thousand salat, salawat sharifa. You may recite Holy Qur'an. You may recite so many dhikr. Do it, it should be easy. But to keep anger, it is not easy. That is difficult. Therefore, Grandsheikh was saying who may, who is going to be angry, whole day he must say instead [of] to say Allah Allah, he must say astaghfirullah, astaghfirullah. And anger is not individual thing. But commonly whole nations and people just fall down in anger oceans. And the source of anger imansızlık, not believe, not believing in Allah Almighty. That gives anger to people. Who believes in God Almighty, always that anger coming to be less and less. Who believes that Allah Almighty above him, and His baṭsh (power) His power hand, Allah Almighty's fist striking on him, he makes himself to fear and think that "Sometimes I may be weak and some others may be powerful. Now I may feel that I am powerful with my anger but some times [will] come I must fear from my Lord. Because sometimes I may be weak and stronger people coming on me." And then he is going to leave that anger.

That is remedy for angry, for anger. And also when you are praying salatu n-najat and making sajda, you must ask that Allah Almighty takes away from you the anger of your nafs.
