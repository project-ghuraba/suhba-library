Our Grandsheikh Sultan-ul Awliya told this story: Prophet Musa was passing through some place. He stopped by a village and at the outskirts of that village he saw a flowing spring. And the village was all green with beautiful light and full of trees, fruit and houses. And its people were smiling people because when the sun of faith shines in the heart, it gives a shine to the face. When the heart is dark, the face becomes dark too.

When the faces are dark... Sometimes people are dark colour but their faces are bright. There is makeup. Don't look at makeup. Their faces are white colour but dark. There are many people with white faces who are dark. There are very dark colour people like ebony, but their faces are bright. Brightness is not because of whiteness. Being bright is dependent on faith in the heart. There are many bright faced people among coloured ones. And there are many dark faced people among white ones.

He said people with bright faces hosted the Prophet Musa. He talked to them and went on his way. Time passed and he went there once again. He went there and looked, the spring had dried up, the trees had gone bad and finished. No one was left in the houses. The beams of the houses were cracked, the country was destroyed along with its people. All of them had become dark. He said "Ya Rabbi, You know the wisdom of this. I don't know what happened to these people. All of these people were so pious in their worship. Their faces were shining with light. How did such a country of gardens and springs, such a happy place become like this all at once?"

Allah said: "It didn't happen all at once, Musa. One day after you left, a person who didn't pray passed through this place. A man who didn't pray passed through one day. He drank from that spring and washed his face and hands. His misfortune made the spring dry. As those servants let him stay there, their gardens with trees dried too when the spring dried out. I dried it because a servant without sajda to Me drank from it. And because they hosted that servant without sajda and let him pass through there. Because they accepted him, I scattered them around and dried up their gardens. I finished them because a servant who never prayed passed through."

Aman ya Rabbi. This is the situation of past nations. JanabAllah flooded thousands of people because of one man -for the rebellion of one disobedient one. For the honor of His Beloved, if one righteous man is found among thousands of disobedient people, JanabAllah covers the rebellion of thousands people. That is the Prophet of mercy, our Prophet, alaihi salatu wa salam. Otherwise, each of us would cause the sinking of a nation. Each of us has such sins that if only one of them was found among other nations, it would destroy those righteous nations like that man destroyed the village.

Aman ya Rabbi. What rebelliousness there is. Rebellions we can't imagine. Weird and strange. There are 800 prohibited things in the religion. Our Sheikh Sultan-ul Awliya used to say that when dajjal appears, there will be no bad way, no badness, no new sin left to teach people. They will have completed all of it before he appears. It is complete. When the big dajjal comes, there won't be any sin he can teach them to do. People of that time will be already doing all of them. You can't imagine it, things not fitting in your mind.

Praise to Allah who doesn't leave. If He leaves, evil is immediately born. Just like a man lies down with a woman, when ego meets shaitan, instantly some evil, sin, or rebellion is born.
