As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
قُلْ سِيرُوا۟ فِى ٱلْأَرْضِ فَٱنظُرُو
<br>“Qul sīrū fī l-arḍi fa-nẓurū”<br>
“Say: 'Travel in the land and see...” (Qur’ān 29:20).

فَٱنظُرْ إِلَىٰٓ ءَاثَٰرِ رَحْمَتِ ٱللَّهِ
<br>“Fa-nẓur ilā āthāri raḥmatillāh”<br>
“Look then at the effects (results) of Allah's Mercy...” (Qur’ān 30:50). ṢadaqAllahu l-ʿAẓīm.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla is saying, walk around, look at the creation of Allah and take example. It is the order of Allah, walking is good. It is sunnah of our Holy Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam to walk. One of the attributes of our Holy Prophet is when he walked, he walked without effort as if he would walk down. Our Holy Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam would not run. It is not actually good to run. It is not for mankind to run. It is for other creatures; they run. Mankind walks.

It is trendy now to run every day for an hour, or two hours. Why do they run? It is meaningless. There is nothing. Only to do sports, to benefit their bodies. But the opposite happens. It brings nothing but harm. It gives tiredness. It doesn't bring any benefit. People can read while they are walking. They can recite what they learnt by heart. They can make tasbīḥāt. That is beneficial. Walking is the remedy.

Shukr to Allah wherever we go, we intend to carry out this sunnah of our Holy Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. We do it everywhere, we walk around beautiful places created by Allah. Shukr to Allah it was meant to do so this morning as well. But something amazing happened. We go to the same place every day. Today, I said I wouldn't return from this way but use another way. While passing through the garden, I was thinking about its owners not to say anything. I thought they wouldn't mind. We were just walking, not eating their land.

Meanwhile, I was passing by another garden. I saw a poor African worker there. He was doing something. I went forward, there was a car. It stopped when they saw me. A man went out of the car and greeted me. A neat young man. He asked, "Are you Shaykh Nāẓim's son?" I said yes. He said, "I love Shaykh Nāẓim very much. This plot is mine." The plot was very large. I looked and saw spinach growing there. He thought a bit and said, "I couldn't sell it. As you came here, have it cropped so that we have our charity. Let it be our goodness. I was going to ride over it."

He was going to ride over it with a tractor to mix with soil. He said, "Take it if you want." I said, "May Allah be pleased with you. This is great goodness both for you and for us." It means Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla meant that for us. He took me from the way, and step by step led me to this road. While I was thinking to use other paths, He put me on this path. And inshā’Allah there is a lot of wisdom with that young man as well. Because we were thinking; may Allah forgive us, we thought there were no more Cypriots who do good and have īmān. And Allah made this man come across us.

It means they are not over with Allah's permission. It won't be over. Īmān given by Allah to Muslim places will certainly bring up good people. With spinach he gave, it is too much for dergah as well. Shukr to Allah our brothers filled their cars and distributed his charity. On this day of Ramaḍān, it is both charity and barakah. And it will be remedy for people inshā’Allah.

There is beauty in everything. By Allah's will, if we didn't pass by that place, he would cut it to plant something else. He would not crop it and distribute to people. Shukr to Allah we have many jobless brothers. We went there and couldn't crop even the half of it. We will crop them for two more days inshā’Allah. It will be both for his goodness and for goodness of our brothers. And may people who will eat it have remedy and īmān inshā’Allah.

When our intention is like this with everything, Allah will give us abundantly from His kindness, thawāb and barakah inshā’Allah. May Allah increase those who make charity. And may those who'll eat have light and īmān inshā’Allah.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
