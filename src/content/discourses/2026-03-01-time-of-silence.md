As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Our Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says, “Whoever believes should speak good or remain silent.” If a person is not going to speak good, then silence is better. Because sometimes people speak without knowing, and when that happens, instead of good, evil and fitnah arise. Therefore, silence is sometimes better. People should always be aware of what they are saying. Are they saying good or evil, bad or good? They must know that.

In this time, I believe Sayyidina Ali said,<br>
هَذَا زَمَنُ السُّكُوتِ وَمُلازَمَةُ البُيُوت
<br>“Hādhā zamānu s-sukūti wa mulāzamatu l-buyūt.”<br>
“This is a time of silence and staying at home.”

Back then, fourteen hundred years ago, it was considered a time to remain silent and stay home. Now, we need it even more than before. There’s no need to talk too much. We must say what is good. Otherwise, if you say something bad, it will harm you. If you say something good, then it will be good and beneficial.

But as we said, the way of our Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam is the good way. What he taught is good for humanity. Not just for Muslims, but for all of humanity. People must learn from our Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam what they should do. Those who want goodness and beauty in the world should follow that way. All other ways lead to loss and are never good. May Allah keep us steadfast on that way. May we not fall into fitnahs, inshā’Allah.

Not everything that is seen is true, not everything that is said is true. Therefore, it’s best not to worry or tire yourself about anything. Whatever you do, do what our Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam said. May Allah not separate us from that way. May Allah protect Islām and Muslims, and send the Sāhib.

We are living in the end of times. Certainly, the only solution to all these problems and difficulties is, as our Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam gave good tidings on, when Mahdi ʿalayhi s-salām appears, there will be no more problems, inshā’Allah.

May Allah help us, and may he come soon, inshā’Allah.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
