As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is saying, "Yassirū wa-lā tu‘assir." And our Holy ProphetNone is saying, "Make it easy. Don't make it difficult – Islām is the religion of ease. Do not push people away by complicating it," says our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam.

Whatever you do, when you do it for the pleasure of AllāhNone, AllāhNone accepts it. As we are living in the end of times, anything done for AllāhNone, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla will accept it. But there is shayṭān. "Min sharri l-waswāsi l-khannās" (114:4). As soon as you want to do something good it tries to prevent it and says: "This is not sunnah. This is wrong." You must say the word of AllāhNone. You must remember the name of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. You must do what HeNone ordered. Shayṭān comes from such a door to you and says, "This is bid‘ah. You are not doing a good thing. Do not pray. Do not make ṣalawāt. Do not gather. Do not gather with your Muslim brothers. Stay alone and wild. Don't listen to anyone. Don't follow madhhab. Don't join ṭarīqah. All of them are ḥarām, bid‘ah and shirk."

This group, who says like this, are the best friends of shayṭān. When this is done, īmān goes away. People become faithless. They are Muslim only by tongue. They have no īmān in their hearts. When it is gone, they may lose what is on their tongues as well. May AllāhNone protect us. And they will go into kufr, unbelief. Therefore, we must pay attention to this. Don't listen to those people who make waswasah, whisperings. However you do, just do. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla will accept. AllāhNone is not in need of your worship. Do it for yourself and don't let it go. Wherever there is a good gathering in remembrance of AllāhNone, attend it. Go to assemblies of ṣuḥbah. Go to assemblies of dhikr. We seek refuge in AllāhNone from shayṭān and its followers who make waswasah. "Qul a‘ūdhu bi-rabbi n-nās" (114:1). "Min sharri l-waswāsi l-khannās" (114:4). We seek refuge in AllāhNone from these people who make waswasah to others, put them in hardship and prevent them from doing goodness. Just like we seek refuge in AllāhNone from shayṭān, we seek refuge from them as well. May AllāhNone protect us from their evil.

They have repelled many people and pushed them away from the religion. There are many people who mislead. They made it so strict in Muslim countries that after losing īmān, they lose the Islām as well which was on their tongues. They turned to kufr. Therefore, easiness is the first order of our religion. Every act of our Holy ProphetNone was good. HisNone every act was with adab. HeNone was sent to people with goodness. With goodness, hearts inclined towards Islām. Without it, when they are rough and rude, people run away. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Wa-law kunta faẓẓan ghalīẓa l-qalbi la-nfaḍḍū min ḥāwlik" (3:159). Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is saying, "If you were a tough and strict person, the people around you would run away." So the character of our Holy ProphetNone is the best character. Our Holy ProphetNone treated people in the best way. Both with the young and old, heNone had a beautiful assembly with everyone. HeNone never shouted at anyone directly. When heNone didn't like something, heNone kept quiet. They would understand.

Therefore, don't listen to these rough and rude people. Everything you do with sincere intention for AllāhNone is accepted by permission of AllāhNone. Everyone must know this. And those who say they are Muslim must know this first of all.

Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying: “Yassirū wa-lā tu‘assirū, wa-bashshirū wa-lā tunaffirū.” Meaning: be easy for people, not make it difficult, make everything easy. And give them good tiding, good news, happy news for people. Don’t make them to run away. This is order of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam which whole Muslim was following this everywhere until shayṭān make a trick. He couldn’t make by himself, but by [means of] people they are claiming they are Muslim, they are knowing Islām very well, and they are saying for people: “Everything you do, everything you do [is] wrong, not right. You must do this, you mustn’t do that.” But most of this, what they teaching – to not do anything: to not pray, to not gathering, to not make dhikr for AllāhNone, to not make sunnah, to not make any nafl, or gathering for holy night, blessed night. Everything for them, it is not good, ḥarām. For them, ḥarām [but] for not Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla or Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam.

When you are praying, how it will be, it must be ḥarām? When you make mentioning name of AllāhNone, how it will be shirk? When you are gathering with people, AllāhNone saying: “Yadu Llāhi ma‘a l-jamā‘ah” – AllāhNone is happy with gathering – you said, “This is not good, don’t do gathering, don’t do... don’t pray, don’t make ṣalawāt, don’t read Qur’ān, don’t read Yā-Sīn when for in graveyard, don’t do this.” And all this... against Ahlu s-Sunnah wa-l-Jamā‘ah, against madhhab, against ṭarīqah. They are also against this. All of this, they are against, they don’t want. Why? Because, if you do this, shayṭān will be not happy. If you do this, he will be angry. So, they don’t want to make shayṭān to be angry. He is their boss, he is a friend for them. When somebody try to do good thing, many thing prevent him – "Don’t do this, don’t do that." Even it is not easy to do, and when these people coming, many people they are afraid and run away from religion, from Islām, from to be on right way. Even, because this what, when you do this, you become your īmān, your belief, stronger.

To gathering, to praying, to mentioning, make dhikr with people, with khatm – all this giving you īmān, belief, strong belief for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. But they don’t want this. For this, AllāhNone describe them, they are only they say, “By tongue they are Muslim. By heart, no. Nothing inside.” And when this finish from tongue, it's more easy to finish. For this, many people they left religion and looking for another thing. In shā’a Llāh, they will be find this way of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam and come back to īmān, they run away from this people, run away from shayṭān. AllāhNone help them, AllāhNone help us, to not be following this people, which AllāhNone describe them they are "al-waswāsi l-khannās" (114:4). They are making doubt of people. “Everything you do, you are wrong. You are not right. You are doing bad against Islām, against sharī‘ah” – this is what they claim. This is "whispering people", making people to thought they are wrong and after, run away.

AllāhNone keep us safe from this fitnah. Keep all Muslim to be safe. This is big fitnah but it is because we are living in time of the most darkness time. Fitnah it is all around the world, all around the Muslim, and even all around the human being now. But it will be, in shā’a Llāh, after this – opening and it will be light and all this darkness will go, in shā’a Llāh, with Sayyidunā Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
