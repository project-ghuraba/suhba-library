Shaykh Mehmet: As-Salamu Alaikum wa Rahmatullahu wa Barakatuhu. Madad ya Rasul Allah, Madad ya Ashab al Rasulullah, Madad ya S Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, madad ya S. Nazim al Haqqani, dastur! Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fil jamia' Our way is through association & blessings are in the gathering. May we say a few words &may Allah accept it as a sohbah insha Allah.

Today Islam is the true/Haqq religion. Allah forgives everything, except shirk (associating partners with Allah). Making shirk/associating partners with Allah is the greatest zulm/darkness. But after this, if one follows the religion & insha Allah, doesn't make shirk, Allah forgives sins. This is the simplest, most basic part. After this, the religion of Islam. In today's time the religion in the sight of Allah is Islam. When you make shahada & say "Ashadu an la ilaha illa Allah wa ashadu anna Muhammadan rasulallah", you become Muslim. But it also has some conditions, some ways. The more you do them, the more you are saved from the torment in hell.

There is Shari'ah and after Shari'ah, what is more intense is Tariqat. Tariqat means, to be with Allah. Tariqat means, to fulfil all the orders of Shari'ah. And this, we do only as much as we can. There are some things man doesn't know. He wants to do things as he sees-ponders & decides if it is right or wrong. But this is not right. There are certain things (rules). There is no such thing in religion as deciding what you think is best. It is Allah's orders & you fulfil them.

When you fulfil, already by the wisdom of Allah, with every order not only gaining spiritually, but it also has material benefits, with Allah's permission. Man doesn't see it. But even if he doesn't like it, later on it benefits his body & soul. And one of them is..Mostly our mureeds are not doing it. If you say "this person did like this, we will also do like this, we are following him". No, can't be like this. Mawlana says, do whatever your madhab orders you. We have 4 madhabs of Ahlus Sunnah wal Jama'a, you should follow one of them. And after choosing one, you will fulfil it's orders. And one of them is this salat al safar/ prayer of travelers. "We are more sufi. We don't pray 2 rakats, we pray 4 rakats". Can't be. Because madhab orders "when you go for safar, travel for a distance of 3 days, you will pray 2 rakats. If not, it is considered the same as praying 4 rakat of fardh in 2 rakats although you are not traveling.

This if you are praying alone. But if you pray with imam & he prays 4 rakats you can't leave after the 2 rakats, you will finish the 4. If they choose you as imam, you should not pray 4 rakats if you are safari/traveller. You should pray 2 rakats & people will complete the rest. And of course this also has a proper method. When we are coming from Istanbul, because here is also our home we are not considered as safari here. But all the guests coming are safari. But there is Hanafi, Shafi'i, Hanbali. We, as Hanafi, safari is 15 days. You don't leave the safari/traveller status before 15 days. There is Shafi'i, it is 3 days. After 3 days, he should pray as regular. This, if it is a 3 day travel. But for example, I came here for 3 days but I don't know how long I will stay, it can be 3 days, or 10 days.. If the duration of the travel is not known, he can be safari all the time but if his intention is for 15 days, he is safari for 15 days. After that he prays regular. And because we don't know the other madhabs..

In Shafi'i, when one is traveling he can combine zhur with asr. They can combine Maghreb with Isha. This is, for 3 days. And after 3 days when they are not safari anymore, I can't say anything about it but I think they don't do jam'/ combine anymore. This is also important. Meaning "I combined zhur with asr, maghreb with isha when traveling. I may continue like this". There is no such thing. People should not do such things according to their own thinking, it has big responsibility. When trying to do good, gain more thawab, you may sin.

People should be careful about this. The most important thing as we said, even if you are the best in the world, when they present you as imam no matter how many people pray behind you, you either lead the prayer praying 2 rakats & you tell people to complete or someone else can come & lead as 4 rakats (not safari). It is like this. But, we pray the fardh 2 rakats. There is no such thing for sunnah, you can pray it as you like. It is not forbidden. Some don't pray sunnah either, but because we are not obliged to do so we can pray sunnah as we like. But fardh, because it is Allah's order we pray it 2 rakats, as safari. And don't judge anyone saying "why this one did like this, that one like that". You are responsible for your own. You have learned this. There is no end to knowledge. One can learn in any age, we are all always learning new things.

Today we speak about the biggest zulm/oppression The biggest oppression, what is the biggest oppression? To make partner for Allah (awj). This is the biggest oppression.

S.N. Sin.

S.M. Sin... No no, oppression. Zulm for Allah. Yes, this is to make oppression. You can make oppression for everybody, but in Quran, "Inna Ash-Shirka Lazulmun `Azimun" (31:13) To make partner for Allah, the biggest one. People what they making, all this oppression here in this world it is nothing. To make this for Allah, it is the biggest one. The people, they don't know. Allah, only One. This is religion. And religion now "Inna Ad-Dina `Inda Al-Lahi Al-'Islamu" (3:19). This time, Islam. Because all other time, finish. Religion Islam, to make shahada- Ashadu an la ilaha illa Allah wa ashadu anna Muhammadan abduhu wa rasuluhu". Alhamdulillah, this is enough for everybody to survive from hell. If they don't do this, they can't survive.

So after this, Islam, what? They have rule, they have worship. Tariqat people, they like to do all this because they are all time with Allah. What Allah want, they like to do. Many people they can learn. Some time they seeing each other or seeing some people, they following them. But there is rules you must make it, obey it. One of these rules, our brother, they think they are doing better to do it. But it is not right. This is for, when travelling praying. Travelling praying when you travel 3 days,3 days but by walk. Maybe it is 120, 150 km. It depends on going up, going street, for way.. When it is like this, you must pray 2 rakats. No! If say "we are Sufi, we are following, making too much worshipping, no we must pray 4". This is.. This is, Islam you cannot weigh or you cannot measure Islam by your sense. Because you don't know what is benefit. There is in every order of Islam, benefit. But sometimes you cannot see it. But Allah, He make material benefit & spiritual benefit. Both of them, in this order we have. Allah order this, to pray in safari 2 rakat. You must obey for this.

Hadith, Prophet (sas) saying "who pray in safari 4 rakat, as he pray 2 rakat for when you are in, praying. Same if you are not in Safari, you cannot pray 2 rakat for zuhr or for asr". But when you are following imam, imam what he doing. If he is safari imam you can pray 2 rakat with him. If he is not safari, you continue with him 4 rakat, not leaving. But for sunnah praying, you can pray as much as you like. It is not order to not pray or.. But for safari, for fardh you must pray 2 rakats. This is for 4 madhab. We have in Islam, Ahlus Sunnah, 4 madhabs. But there is another Muslim also, but they are not Ahlus Sunnah. This is, I don't know how they pray. But Ahlus Sunnah, 4 madhabs. Hanbali, Maliki, Shafi'i, Hanafi-this is 4 madhab Ahlus Sunnah, they call. All Tariqat people, they following this. We must follow it because our imams, they are all from this tariqat.

So now there are many people, they don't like to follow madhab. It is, they are not from Ahlus Sunnah, from outside of Ahlus Sunnah. Hanafi, 15 days. Until 15 days you will be safari/travel.You must pray 2 rakats. In Shafi'i, 3 days. After 3 days, they can.. If they are coming & intending to stay 4 days, they must pray from third day like they are in this place, 4 rakats. But if it is 3 days, they must pray 2 rakat. And they can, Shafi'i, make together; zuhr asr together, maghreb isha together. We know about Hanafi, as we are Hanafi. Hanafi, they cannot do this. In Shafi'i they do, but only in safar/travel, when they are travelling. I don't know how they.. I don't know any more from Shafi'i, or from Hanbali, Maliki. Everybody what he following which madhab, he can follow this & make it exactly.

There is coming some people from other place. But if you have house here and house in another town or something, village when you go here, you are,this is house. I come from Istanbul here, this is my house. And now not safari, because my house. But if I go to, like Adana for more near from Istanbul, also I am safari. If it is your house, you are not safari. And if some people coming here, they are Shaykh or they have, they make praying, they must pray 2 rakats if they coming less than this time, safari time. But if they making imam, they must pray 2 rakats. If they don't like to pray 2 rakats, they can go behind & other people can pray who is here, he can pray 4 rakat for them.

This is Allah's order, we cannot measure "why this, why that". Only for fardh, Allah order only. If you like to pray, pray sunnah too much. It is not forbidden. This is Shari'ah & you.. Shari'ah obligatory to all Muslim lady & men to learn this. This is obligatory like fardh, like praying. Alhamdulillah when you sit here you make this obligatory. Allah give you thawab, rewarding you.

Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, Al Fatiha.
