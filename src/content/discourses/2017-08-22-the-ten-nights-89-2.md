As-salāmu ‘alaykum wa raḥmatu Llāhi wa barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīn wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Wa l-fajri wa layālin ‘ashrin wa sh-shaf‘i wa l-watri." (89:1-3) Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla mentions the ten days of Dhūl-Ḥijjah in the Qur’ān. These are from the holy days, very holy days, respected by our ProphetNone, respected by AllāhNone. In the Qur’ān Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla swore by them. You swear by valuable things. These days are blessed. They are a gift from AllāhNone to us. When you do good deeds during these days, AllāhNone grants more and more and gives its thawāb. HeNone gives its blessings and grants all kinds of goodness.

Who can fast nine days during these days, how nice it will be. Who cannot should fast two days on ‘Arafah day and the day before it. Or whoever wants, it is good to fast from the first till the ninth, or on the eighth and ninth day with the first. The more one can do, the more useful it is. Our ProphetNone says every fasted day is counted as one year of fasting. And same for the voluntary worship made during these days. Because these days are the holiest days of the year. There is also the day before ‘Arafah and the day of ‘Arafah. And the day of ‘Arafāt is in these nine days. It happens once a year. A man going on ḥajj must be on ‘Arafāt on the day of ‘Arafah. And that is only one day. That is the day in the first ten days of Dhūl-Ḥijjah. We can understand its blessing from this. We should respect it. Who respects it, AllāhNone will grant him from dunyā as well. HeNone will give blessings and love in the family will increase. He'll get along with the people around him and he will get great spiritual rewards and thawāb.

We should make shukr to AllāhNone for HeNone has granted us such days. Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla is generous. HeNone gives chances for a person to earn more. And to grant more favours, Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla made these days. Favours of Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla are unconditional. Because all good deeds you do have no benefit for AllāhNone. They are useful to you. And even though it is for you, AllāhNone is happy and grants you even more favours. AllāhNone gives to those who know the value and ask from AllāhNone. May AllāhNone bless it. May AllāhNone let us reach more years, in shā’a Llāh. May HeNone give us clarity and peace with the blessings of these days, in shā’a Llāh. May HeNone give us security. May we be under protection so that AllāhNone protects us from fitnah and the difficulties of the end of times, in shā’a Llāh. May HeNone not take us off the right way. May AllāhNone open all goodness, in shā’a Llāh.

Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla saying, Sūratu l-Fajr, bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Wa l-fajri wa layālin ‘ashrin." (89:1-2) HeNone mentioning this, praising this. Fajr is the best time, to wake up for first praying fajr and "layālin ‘ashr" - this is ten night, ten night beginning of Dhū l-Ḥijjah. From tonight beginning until ‘Īd is very blessed days in divinely presence of Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla and ProphetNone because it is have many occasion in this days for very important...

For first - it is night of ‘Īd but for mu’min who are they are can go to ḥajj it is also day for day ‘Arafāt. ‘Arafah only in once in the year coming and it is in this ten days for Dhū l-Ḥijjah. So it is very important, very blessed, very holy. And ProphetNone was fasting all this days, nine days from one until nine. And heNone fasting other months, three days every month, but this for Dhū l-Ḥijjah fasting whole time, from one until nine. And each day AllāhNone accept for us as one year, reward us for one year. So who can fast nine days they can. If not, maybe one day for ‘Arafah but better with Yawmu t-Tarwiyah - before one ‘Arafah also very blessed day, eighth of Dhū l-Ḥijjah. Can fast eight and nine or you can fast first of Dhū l-Ḥijjah and after eight and nine. It will be three days. So it will be also very blessed.

And who give charity, ṣadaqah, doing praying, AllāhNone reward for this not double, triple - only HeNone know what HeNone will give because very blessed day. And this is giving for spiritual but for your material also. HeNone give you health, and HeNone give you barakah. And give you HeNone will give you satisfaction and happiness also. For this who respect and make worshipping and charity, doing good thing for people, AllāhNone will reward him. And this is gift from our Lord for us. HeNone is from His generosity HeNone make occasion for us to give us from His endless mercy, give us from His reward. And it is for us not - HeNone is not in need to anything from us. But HeNone is happy when you are respect and you are doing to you like to take bless for HimNone. HeNone is more happy than us.

ProphetNone saying Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla: "My servant he is accepting and he is want from Me. I'm no fear from to be less." HeNone giving without counting. HeNone is akramu l-akramīn - the most generous. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are reach this day and we are accepting, we asking HimNone to give us every good thing, give barakah health, wealth, long life. In shā’a Llāh, give us strong īmān also, most important thing. And to be to keep us safe from fitnah, from every badne[ss], bad thing in this time. The time it is terrible time, for end of time is terrible time - but for non-believer. But we are, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we even in the middle of fire AllāhNone can make it cool and happiness for all of us. Nothing harm us, in shā’a Llāh. This is we ask HimNone, in shā’a Llāh, to be whole time in safe and to be in right way, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone make us all of many years like this reach, in shā’a Llāh, and we ask to reach Sayyidinā al-Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām also, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq al-Fātiḥah.
