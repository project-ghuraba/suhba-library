As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa raḥmatu Llāhi wa barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīn wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

A Muslim man or any person following any ṭarīqah should know the adab of Islām and the adab of ṭarīqah. The adab of Islām is a bit lighter. A person belonging to ṭarīqah should keep to the boundaries of adab and pay attention to adab. They should know everything comes from AllāhNone. They shouldn't let AllāhNone out of their heart. Dhikru Llāh means to remember AllāhNone for twenty four hours. Whatever happens, a man should say it is the will of AllāhNone and accept it, be pleased and satisfied with it – the man in ṭarīqah.

Of course, ṭarīqahs are not like before. In old times it was much harder. Now as it is the end of times, it is lighter. It is as much as a person can bear. It can be done now too. The old Mashāyikh used to tell murīds, a person who gives you gold should be the same as the person who hits you on your neck. You should think both came from AllāhNone and be neither happy, nor upset. You shouldn't have feelings for the men, you shouldn't hate them. Of course you should thank the other one. You should think that both things came from AllāhNone. People nowadays cannot carry this. People get annoyed even by words. And matters like getting hit, they cannot stand it. Yet they should remember AllāhNone. AllāhNone should always be in our hearts as much as possible. Whatever happens and you get upset, remember directly it is from AllāhNone. Your sadness will become lighter then. When you are needy, say, "There is AllāhNone. AllāhNone is testing us. This trouble will go too." Because you know it comes from somewhere bigger. It's not for nothing.

If you know that your rank and rewards will increase, it will be easier and you'll be content. Otherwise, when you don't know, you keep mumbling, "How did this happen? Where did this trouble come from? Where did this problem come from?" To know AllāhNone and to be with AllāhNone is the biggest favour, is the biggest goodness. All troubles will go away. You'll be in peace, always in peace. "AllāhNone is with me. AllāhNone is seeing me." We are always together with AllāhNone. AllāhNone is with us for twenty four hours. If you think so, you will be relaxed then. If you don't think so, you will spend your life in regret. You'll be mumbling, "If only I did this. If only I did that. If only this and that happened," and die at last with all your life spent in troubles. Therefore, a man is in peace in this world when he remembers AllāhNone and doesn't let AllāhNone out of his heart. Everything we are in this life, we must know AllāhNone He is by His will we are in this life. Many times people they are complaining, every people, but for ṭarīqah people, for Muslim and especially for ṭarīqah people to be respect and good behave – good behave it is first order, adab, good behave. Adab for ṭarīqah people you must keep respect and adab with Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla and with other people also. Everything it is from HimNone. This is adab for people to know what happen, it is coming from HimNone. And to remember this you must make remembrance of AllāhNone twenty four hour per day every day. You must put Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla in your heart. Don't go without thinking, "Our Lord is in our heart."

Dhikr, meaning to remember meaning and when you do this twenty four hour you making you like you are doing dhikr. Because you are remembering AllāhNone and HeNone – you know HeNone is with you. Everything you done it will be better. This for ṭarīqah people: it is very important good behave and to know everything coming from AllāhNone. This was a old time mashāyikh was teaching their murīd. Old time they was more strict than this day but now the end of time. It is okay like this because people even if they said in "we are in ṭarīqah" it is enough for them. Because many they are attacking reality and attacking ṭarīqah, attacking way of ProphetNone, so we are – only if you say you are in ṭarīqah it is okay also.

But in ṭarīqah you must learn adab, good behave. And good behave it is was, as we said, in old time very strict in ṭarīqah – to how to acting, how to coming, how to going, through for mosque, for Shaykh, for Murshid, and for how you must dress, what you will eat, what you will do. All this was very strict and especially for making between people also. They was saying, if somebody giving you gold coin and other one hitting you, slapping you in your neck, you mustn't be angry for this and you mustn't be happy with other. You must thanking of course but not be angry for other because both coming from AllāhNone, from our Lord. This acting this people – AllāhNone send them for you – same. This is for highest thing for murīd to if he can do this. Is very difficult to do especially this time nobody can do this. So but also just to saying how it was before and also you can take idea to be thinking about this also.

Everything coming from AllāhNone. If you think like this your life it will be more easy, more happy. Because many people complaining, "Oh from where this coming this problem for me?" And other, "From where coming this?" Anxious, this stress coming for you from where this coming? If you know this coming from your Lord, you will be quiet and you be accepted and you will be happy because you know when AllāhNone sending something for this like this HeNone make you give reward for what HeNone send for you. If when for in ākhirah, you said if you wish to have more than this difficult in your life to get more reward from AllāhNone.

So this is for believer and ṭarīqah people making our life more easier. Every time remember when you have something difficult, something you are not happy with this, you remember your Lord. You said, "AllāhNone al-ḥamdu liLlāh it is from YouNone and we are happy with what YouNone are give us. We are happy from Your mercy. We are happy from what YouNone send. Everything we are happy with this." This is happiness for believer, for mu’min. But other who are they don't have belief, whole life taking pills, going to psychiatry. Going to psychiatry they are every day they have problem, whole life problem and then nothing.

So, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are, AllāhNone make us believer but we must remember this. For this ṣuḥbah for this to remember everybody. Our Lord, in shā’a Llāh, AllāhNone give us all happiness, in shā’a Llāh, no imtiḥān (test). Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
