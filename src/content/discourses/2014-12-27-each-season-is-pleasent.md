Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Let us see what our Mawla does, for good is whatever He does (the chorus of a poem by Ibrahim Haqqi Erzurumi). Thank Allah (JJ) fall has arrived. Allah (JJ) is sending His mercy. Thank Allah. Now they will say this happened and that happened then it rained. It does not happen if Allah (JJ) does not want it.

Allah (JJ) is showing people, but people are again going their own way. You saw last year that it did not get cold all year, in winter, and it did not rain. Autumn came, winter came and it did not rain. So why did it not rain? It was winter season. If Allah (JJ) wants, He can make winter pass like summer. Allah (JJ) does it so people believe and take lesson from everything. Yet again, as long as Allah (JJ) does not guide, people are having their own way.

Allah (JJ) created everything, including the seasons, in the most perfect form. It has no flaw. Those who want to find flaw and fault should look for flaw in themselves. Allah (JJ) created everything very beautifully, He did it most perfectly. There are not thousands but millions of wisdoms behind everything Allah (JJ) did. If there is no rain during winter, there would be nothing.

In addition, nights are long. Nights are longer in winter so people can do more of their worship. Nights are not short anywhere in winter. It is the wisdom of Allah (JJ), here it is. Nights are long in winter so they may perform worship, gain knowledge, and make sohbats. Winter season is a pleasent season.

Every season is pleasent. Some people say they do not like winter or summer. When summer arrives, they say, “Summer is too hot. When is winter coming, when is fall coming, and when is spring coming?” They are all pleasent Mashallah. Whether you complain or cry, the wheels are turning without anybody looking at your crying and complaints. As we are saying, people are also missing a little intelligence, so you may tell them as much as you want and they will not listen to you. There is no such thing as continuous summer. There is, if you like, you can go to the Equator areas. It is always summer there. People do not have winter clothes either, they are always going in a summer state. But that is good for them, as they are used to it. He created them like that and they are happy with their situation.

It is much better for our country to have the four seasons. Let us be thankful to Allah (JJ), let us glorify Him, and let us be content. Wherever He has placed us, let us see the pleasentries. They are all beautiful anyway. What is not pleasent, going outside of Allah’s (JJ) orders is not good. Everything Allah (JJ) has created is beautiful.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq.

Al-Fatiha.
