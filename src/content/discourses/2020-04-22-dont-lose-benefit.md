Aū‘dhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-Shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Waṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-F’āiz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-

These days we are in a situation no one thought could happen. This situation is what Allah willed, and this is why this situation is the best—because Allah willed it! Whatever Allah does, He does the best, does in the best and most suitable way. Allah ‘azza wa jalla must not be questioned about what He does or why He does it. You may ask a man what he is doing but not Allah ‘azza wa jalla. So this situation is a fitting situation which is from Allah ‘azza wa jalla. This is why one must accept it. We were indulged too much in worldly life, so now let us give ourselves over to worship, so that from these three holy months of Rajab, Shaban and Ramadan we receive multiple rewards. Our patience in this situation will also be rewarded, and its account is with Allah.

So we are approaching the beautiful month Ramadan. May Allah reward us. We should do as much of our practices as we are capable of, including for example prayers we have missed, tasbihat, dua—whatever you do is accepted by Allah. If you do not do it, what happens? You lose in this world. They are concerned right now about their losses. This is what they think, yet when Allah wills, He bestows what is better and more perfect. What looks like a loss, turns into something good. Insha’Allah, we will face no loss either here or in Next Life. Always things can turn out to be useful and good for a mu’min. May Allah guide people, because there is no other way for salvation. Without guidance, people will always be in a state of loss. May Allah protect us and give strength to our faith. Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-F ti ah
