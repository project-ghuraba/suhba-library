As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is saying in Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n: Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Qul in kuntum tuḥibbūna Llāha fa-ttabi‘ūnī yuḥbibkumu Llāh" (3:31). "Qul aṭī‘ū Llāha wa-r-rasūla, fa-in tawallaw fa-inna Llāha lā yuḥibbu l-kāfirīn" (3:32), says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Who loves Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla should follow our Holy ProphetNone so that AllāhNone loves him too and forgives his sins. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla loves everyone. HeNone loves everyone, but those who don't obey HimNone are people who are not loved. Why are they not loved? Because they don't love themselves. If they did, AllāhNone would love them too and they would follow the right way. They would accept our Holy ProphetNone, walk on hisNone way and obey himNone so that AllāhNone loves them. When they don't do so, they oppress themselves. Who oppresses himself oppresses others. And he becomes an unloved person.

A person who is not loved in the sight of AllāhNone has no benefit. That person has nothing but harm. The beloved servants of AllāhNone, they have benefit. They are useful people. To be with them is necessary. It is salvation from hell. It is goodness here and hereafter. Otherwise, the rest brings nothing but harm. Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says a believer is like a shop which sells perfume. When you enter it, even if you don't buy anything, that fragrance gives you peace and pleasure. On the other hand, another kind of person is like a blacksmith shop. When you enter, there is a bad smell and smoke. That is the example of a kāfir. Meaning, even if you don't get burned, you feel discomfort because of the smell. Therefore, we should be with good people.

Now the hypocrites have come up with something called humanism. They say you must love humanity. You can love humanity, but how can you love a person who harms others? How can you love someone who harms himself? You don't love him. AllāhNone doesn't love him. You can't love someone whom AllāhNone doesn't love. It is not good to love him. It is better to stay away from him. You should give him advice. If he accepts your advice, it's fine. If he doesn't, no need to deal with him. No need to love him.

May AllāhNone make all of us from the people whom HeNone loves, in shā’a Llāh. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying in Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n, Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Qul in kuntum tuḥibbūna Llāha fa-ttabi‘ūnī yuḥbibkumu Llāhu wa-yaghfir lakum dhunūbakum wa-Llāhu ghafūrun raḥīm" (3:31). "Qul aṭī‘ū Llāha wa-r-rasūla, fa-in tawallaw fa-inna Llāha lā yuḥibbu l-kāfirīn" (3:32). Meaning, if you love AllāhNone, in Qur’ān this for Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, follow meNone. Follow Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. AllāhNone will love you and HeNone will forgive you. And HeNone is most merciful.

And after this, next verse saying: obey Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, obey HisNone Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. If you not accept, and go away, AllāhNone, HeNone don't like non-believer – kāfir. AllāhNone, HeNone don't love them. AllāhNone loves who follow Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam and hisNone way. This is a very important thing, to be beloved for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. The most good thing for human being. Because human being all, AllāhNone loves them, all of them. And HeNone sent them HisNone Prophets, especially the last ProphetNone, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam – Sayyidunā Muḥammad ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. And it is so big honour for human being.

So, if they follow, AllāhNone will love them. And if not following and not accepting, coming against, AllāhNone, HeNone don't love them. HeNone not love them because they are, they are not loving anything, even themselves. They do not like themselves. If they love themselves, they must accept AllāhNone and accept HisNone mercy, and this favour they give for human being. Even themselves, they are not love themselves. How Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla will love? How people, they will love them? Nobody can love them. Because they are only bad thing. Bad for themselves, and for other also. Other, no benefit from this people. Only they are harming people. They are doing bad thing for people.

Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying also for giving example for believer – he is like a perfume shop. If you go inside, if you buy, if not buy, you can smell nice smelling. And non-believer, like they are going to blacksmith. If you go there maybe something will burn your clothes from fire coming from there. If not, even from bad smelling there, you will be not happy. So, these people who go to blacksmith, they quickly left him and go away. For perfume he can [be] happy. There, he can stay. This also for non-believer, who giving people bad thing, bad idea, bad action, every bad thing. We cannot love them.

They are saying this now new thing: humanitary, something like this. They say, we must love everybody. If everybody they don't love themselves, why will we love them? And this love, AllāhNone gives from people. If HeNone loves somebody, makes people to love him. If HeNone not love them, makes people not to like him or to love him. This is from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.

AllāhNone make us from people who love them, and people love them, in shā’a Llāh. To make for good for ourselves and for others also, in shā’a Llāh. Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
