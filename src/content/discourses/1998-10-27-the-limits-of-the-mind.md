Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah. Madad yā Sulṭān ul-Awliyā’.

The Prophet (saw) was sent to people to give advice. All the prophets were sent to people to give advice. What is advice for? Advice is for people, who don't know their honor and position, who are unaware of themselves, who don't know what to do. They also don't know why they are in this world. This is ignorance of course. Not knowing why you are in this world is ignorance. Not to know how you are in this world is also ignorance. Not knowing what to do, how to do in this life is also ignorance. Isn't it? How did I come to this world? What should I do? Or what will I do? These are things that people should think about. Of course it isn't possible to find an answer by thinking. You can't find a solution by thinking. Nobody has found the answers to these matters by thinking, until today.

Lies - some don't respect the holy books. They try to explain with positive science, to answer people's questions. They think they are smart but actually they are idiots. Because these questions - Why are you here? How did you come here? What are your responsibilities? - an idiot tries to discover it with positive science. Positive science can't answer these because positive science depends upon proof and experience. It has authority where it can come to a conclusion by reason or experience. It means, it may have authority but in deciding about matters that it can't experience or prove, it is irrelevant, It is off topic, not involved. If I'm seeing - yes it is a plate. "What is it for?" "It is for coffee cup." "What is it made of?" "It is made of clay." "Who did it?" "Someone did it." "What else do you see?" "I see pattern." "Who did the pattern?" "Who did the plate also did the pattern?" No. Someone did the pattern, someone else did the plate. But the aim isn't the pattern, it isn't the shape of the plate, it is the knowledge what the plate is being used for.

The worlds were created. There are heavens and earth. They show majesty. They show a kingdom. When you look up, you see a kingdom. When you look at yourself, you are smaller, less, you are finishing. Man has a size. You can see the plane from the ground. Can't you? People in the plane can't see you. You can see the moon from the earth. Man on the moon can't see you. You can see the sun from the earth. Man on the sun can't see you. The eye sees. Isn't it? M: Yes. Pupil in the middle of the eye it is small like a lentil. When you look carefully there is a blackness inside. The white part or the circle around the pupil can't see. The 1mm of a hole can see. Ok. You can watch the universe with such a small organ. When you go up, the world gets smaller, finally disappears. What you see in this world is unseen there. You certainly can't see the world from other galaxies. Maybe the world is unseen when you look from the stars outside our solar system. You can see the solar system but the world turning inside it is unseen. But a small creature on this planet can see that star outside our solar system. The one in that star can't see you. How strange! Maybe the eye of the ox is three times bigger than your eye. Can the ox see the sky? Can it? No it can't. Maybe it has the same system but it doesn't look up to the sky. Even if it does, its eyes can't reach the sky. "What have I done? They ate the grass in front of us." It regrets and doesn't look up again. "They ate a handful of grass why should I look up? They ate my grass". It regrets. It doesn't look up again. It has big eyes. Even if it looks, can't see. "What did you see? Why did you look? You shouldn't have. Is it your duty? You lost the grass" his friend tells him. Strange. Its responsibility is to eat all the grass with its big eyes - It is to look down. Can't look up. None of the animals can look up. Those animals can't bear looking at the divine Majesty. It can't look at the divine kingdom. That's why it stands on four feet. It doesn't stand up like this. Animals stand on four feet. Because they can't look up, they don't have power to look up, they stretch their heads, collect their food from the ground.

Only humans put the food in their mouth. They stand, sit, take the food with their hands put it into their mouths. A human doesn't lean to his plate and eat. Allāh gave honor to mankind. That's why mankind is great. Allāh makes mankind to watch the universe with this 1mm pupil. Allāh makes mankind to watch the universe. Allāhu Akbar. ‘Aẓamatu Hudā rā. Al-‘aẓamatu liLlāh. Mankind. Allāh's greatness is of course beyond our comprehension. You can see, know, examine within limits. You can't go beyond that. No matter how hard you try, you can't go beyond. The eye sees but deceives. When the eye looks to the sky, the giant stars in the sky seems like spots. Kamā huwa, mankind can't bear to see as it is. That's why Allāh created the human eye in such a way that it sees the giant stars like a spot. Mankind, His holiness, mankind is the most honorable, most powerful among the creatures. In one aspect he is weak, in another aspect he represents power.

Like we said, it is like a spot. Does the human eye see on its own? It sees with Allāh's order. It sees what He orders. If He says: "don't see!" Mankind can't see what Allāh doesn't show. Even if he tries, struggles, he can't see what Allāh doesn't show. What did mankind see until now? What did he see until now? Inside the unlimited universe - it is unlimited according to us - in Allāh's presence everything is limited. Creatures aren't unlimited, they are inside this limit. Allāh is unlimited. That's why people should turn towards the inner, away from the outer. Now there is a way of thinking, a way to think about yourself. But mankind looks at something which is obvious. People look at the form of man. It isn't possible to watch the formation process of the fetus in the mother's womb a hundred percent. It is hidden. When his formation is complete in his mother's womb, he'll come to this world.

We are responsible to learn: "How did I come to this world? Why did I come to this world? What is asked from me in this world? Where is my end?" Not asking about this is ignorance. When you ask these questions to the professors at universities, they can't answer. They do, but they cross the line because the professor tells you something from here and there as if proving two times two is four. Researching the creatures in this world gives you some information from here and there. But beyond this, it is impossible for him to say something about his reality, the wisdom of creation, his duties, or his position in life. Because you can't find out about this by thinking. This is such a door, the Creator holds the key. He (jj) gives it to the Prophet (saw). He (saw) gives it to his nation. That's why this advice gives people information about themselves. That's why during the ṣuḥbah, if people object, tell their opinions or make judgements, it shows their ignorance. Because you can't argue about these issues. These issues aren't in the research field of positive science. Positive science tries to prove everything. It has to see, feel, know. But these issues that we explain cannot be calculated. If you want to touch you can't. It is a secret. It is spiritual. By using it, a person can find the meaning of his existence in this world, why he is here, his duties, what will be asked of him, what is asked of him. There is no way of thinking. The way of thinking is the wrong way. That's why the responsibility of all prophets, ‘alayhumu s-salām, was to bring realities that people can't find by thinking.

You either accept it or say "It is irrational". This isn't within the limits of the mind. Anything within the limits of the mind isn't holy. Why do you push your mind? The mind is limited. It objects and says, "This isn't my job. What you ask, isn't my job." Deniers ask with their minds. You'll answer them with good sense - "This isn't my job. Why do you ask me? It is beyond me." It is within the circle of belief - you believe it or not - up to you. "My field is here. There is no way for me beyond this. Why do you ask me?" That's why this is the point, where the 20th century people are confused. All the judgements about religion and belief, especially about Islām in the 20th century, are invalid. Whatever they say about Islām that is within the limits of the mind, or all the objections, are invalid. Because the orders that Islām brings, if they were within the limits of the mind, there wouldn't be need for the prophets, no books would be sent. The mind would solve everything. The mind can't solve everything. We see it.

People of the 20th century reaching the 21st century. Did the mind solve the problems of people? Thousands of laws, rules, regulations, decisions made by the mind, could they save humanity? No. Today humanity is in the biggest chaos. They are in such a mess, such a misery. People count on their minds. The minds reject, saying: "This isn't our job. "But don't say like that. If you don't answer us, who will control us?" You will be under the control of the reactionaries. The reactionaries will control you. You will be controlled by the orders of the believers and the orders of the unseen. You'll also be a reactionary. You'll also be reactionary. You'll also be reactionary. So that you can understand: if you aren't entering there, there is nothing for you. We came to that point. Rationality can't satisfy any of the humanity's needs. It stopped. It has come to a limit. The mind doesn't work. The minds became dull. They both became dull and were made dull. They were made dull. Human minds are constantly made dull. And as they get duller, the results are getting worse. Danger increases. It doesn't produce results. That is impossible by thinking only....

We'll solve it with the ones, coming from malakūt. You can go around the universe - if you follow the way of the Sulṭān of Prophets. The Night of Ascension was for that. To inform that he can enter. If not, let him get into a rocket, go up, turn upside down and be burned to a crisp. People. People. Positive science makes you measure the fields that oxen graze on. It tells about the mountains, seas. It explains in detail what it sees, hears but it won't let you look up. It turns you into an ox, an animal. Because animals don't look up to the sky. Animals' gaze is always on the earth. The aim of positive science is to make you look down. That grazing sheep goes like this, looking here. Look up, look up also. Would he ever look up? "What is up there? Is there a meadow up there? Why would we look up? Look down." Positive science and technology turned people into animals. Finished. If people turned into tame animals, it would be ok but technology turned people into such wild animals that they can compete with the wildest animals. Unfortunately today this is the case. Violence, Wildness.
