As-Salamu Alaikum. Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fil jamia' "Kullun muyassaru lima khuliqa lahu" Hadith.

Allah (awj) didn't cast people from one mold, didn't create them identical. They may look similar outwardly but there is no person by Allah's miracle, identical to another. They are not the same as if coming out from a car factory. Just as everyone has a different fingerprint, every person is also different. You can't find two the same among billions of men. So it is Allah's (awj) miracle with everything. How all these people are not the same? How is nothing alike? This is the power of Allah. Ok, this matter is like this. But also everyone has an... Some awliyas, when they look at people, they see them with the attribute of an animal; see them like this animal in front of them.

Shaykh is Sultan. Sultan is lion. Allah's wisdom, if you call a man "animal" he gets angry but if you call him "lion", he doesn't get angry. Even who Allah's Prophet loved the most, his uncle's son, Ali ibn Abi Talib, is called the Victorious Lion of Allah/ Asadullah al Ghalib. Meaning, his attributes & merits are different. I may say a story before we start so that people may draw a lesson from it. Mashayikhs are also lions, all of them are lions. They get more majestic as they grow older. They are not like common people.

It is told in old time books, the lion king was sitting with his entourage. And in his entourage there are animals, insects.. And there was a wolf & fox who wandered in to get some benefit. The lion said "Let's get up and hunt to eat something". So they hunted. The lion caught a wild ox. And the wolf caught a deer I think and the fox caught a rabbit. They put all of it in the middle. The lion said "How should we do this now? We will divide this. How should we do this?" The wolf thought of himself as the vizier of the lion. He said "It is easy. This ox is suitable Masha Allah for your mighty personality. Enjoy it. And this deer is enough for me & rabbit is also enough for the fox". Of course there the fox did tark al-adab left good manners. He thought he

has divided well. When he said like this the lion, with one swipe of his paw cut the wolf's head into two. His dead body fell there.

"Fox Effendi, you divide now". The fox, of course he is open-eyed and clever, said "O Master, you eat the ox in the morning & digest it from the morning till noon. At noon you eat the deer, it is smaller so will be enough for you till evening. And at night you eat the rabbit, it won't be heavy for you. Bon appetit" The lion said "Bravo, from where did you learn this good manners, good character?" He said "I learned from this (incident)". So it is like this. However the attribute of lion is he hunts & catches. He eats a little from it, the remaining his family comes & eats. They don't finish it also & after them come jackals, wolves... They can't finish also & after them come crows, vultures & they eat also. Finally even if only bones remain, the hyenas eat the bones & finish it. So they (lions) eat only one animal, they are not greedy. And also they are generous ones, not stingy. But the others' attribute, wolf &.. When a wolf attacks a herd if there are 300 animals &, if you allow it, strangles & kills all 300 of them. It destroys the herd, eats a little piece from one of it & leaves. And fox, if it attacks chickens doesn't even leave one alive. It strangles & kills all, and it also eats one & leaves. Their attribute is a bad attribute, attribute of greed. Therefore this shows that the lion has the attribute of mashayikh- It disciplines & teaches. The attributes of others, it is not to satisfy their hunger but whatever they can catch because of their greed they think that they will eat it & kills all 300. Whereas when the lion catches.. And he catches the weak one, the one which is of no use & that herd gets stronger. This is the attribute of the mashayikh.

That is why we say, those who come to mashayikhs should be under the discipline of the Shaykh. They will say & do whatever the Shaykh says. There are some incidents, you can ask "what is the wisdom in this?" You can ask what is the wisdom in it. Whatever Mawlana says, we accept it. And once you understand the wisdom in it, then you may ask is it him really who said it or not. Because as ]we said there are many wolves & foxes around. Everybody says something & they say it as if Mawlana said so.

This is no good. But once you know that Mawlana accepted it, there is no more objection to it. Because 100% there is wisdom in it & whatever Mawlana says is true. So as we said, as we explained in this story, there are some around.. it is a bit difficult for us to say but there are some who sacrifice everything for their own benefit, not for Allah but for their own benefit. They are...May Allah protect us from their evil & grant them sense.

In the end, as we said in the story, just like what happened to that animal, nothing good will come to them. They should be very careful about this because they say "Mawlana did like this, did like that" but none of it is correct unless you hear him say it. And no one heard Mawlana saying such things. This is a very important thing. Mawlana is very compassionate & soft. Whatever they say, he might not accept but doesn't say anything. Mawlana doesn't tell them anything, that is the reason why people left the good manners. And this is a great test from Mawlana.

Mawlana is not saying anything but he is not sitting there alone. That maqam is the maqam of this century's inheritor of Muhammad (sas). That maqam is not just one person. So as we said maybe nothing will appear to happen to such people but the worst comes on them. To live with such a misery, such a trouble, such a torture is worse than death for those people. Therefore they also take it as a chance since they think Mawlana is weak. However when he sits there... Something also happened yesterday. Someone brought a gift, a very big gift. Mawlana said "take this, give to people. I have nothing to do with dunya wealth anymore". So Mawlana has nothing to do with dunya so he doesn't care but don't speak without facts & don't listen to such people. Mawlana is not like before he is much more dangerous. So be careful, be very careful!

Now all who come, come with... They call now paparazzi. That doesn't work here. They should be very careful! And may Allah grant them reason.

Today we speak about character. Allah created people & He said everybody has own job or own business or what to do. Everybody has, Allah make it easy for him- way for him, easy this way. There are billions of people. You cannot find anybody look like

another one. But the way Allah put people- thousand, millions people have own way, own what to do. They can do this. This is character & what they can do, Allah make this people special for this. Some people for maybe king, some people are created for army, some people for shepherd, some people for carpenter, some people driver.. It is like this. Many time I told people, there was minibus driver in Istanbul, same, they have special character. In Damascus I see same character. In 12 000 km from Istanbul to Jakarta, the same. Allah created them like this. This is miracle of Allah.

But to find some people, nothing you cannot find, even one part from people & other like this. Everybody Allah created different. Billions of people, you cannot find two the same. And Allah also make character for people from animal also. Some awliya were looking and seeing one like horse, one like sheep, one like cat, one like lion.. Or every kind of character of animal. But the only one Allah make him... When you say to one man "you are animal" he will be angry. But there is one it is the proudness when you say this - lion. Lion, even all Sahaba are lion, but especially said for Sayyidina Ali ibn Abi Talib, Sayyidina Hamza also. And lions have special character. They are king of animals. And before the mashayikh, they are also, they calling lion. Because they are looking & protecting & they are strong. When they become more old, they become more fearful people, more fierce people.

Before beginning, there was one story about this, old time story. Lion and his people around him. It is like kingdom- he is the king & there was his near him wolf & fox. And other of course animals. Once he was sitting, he said "let's go to hunt", with this fox & wolf. They are going and the lion hunt a big ox, wild ox. And other, ghazal/deer- wolf and fox found one rabbit, wild rabbit. And they bring, putting all together. Lion said for wolf "O wolf, you make this divide, how will you divide this?" He said "Very easy, no need to be.. The ox is big, you are big &you are king. It is good for you, it is suitable for you. We cannot eat this". "Ok". "This deer for me, it is enough for me. And rabbit is for fox, it is enough for him". Lion punched him on his head. Wolf's head coming two part, because he is very strong. And he was dying there. And said for fox "O fox, you make this divide". Of course fox is very clever. He said "O Majesty, this is ox because morning, you must eat it in morning. Until noon it is enough for you. From noon you can eat this deer, until evening. For dinner, it is no need to be heavy when you sleep, rabbit is good for you." He said "Wow, you are very good. How you learn this? You are very clever Masha Allah. How you have this adab & you learn this?" He said "From this wolf I learned this".

And this is for, really the lion, they catch one ox or something to eat. And he catch the weakest one, they run. We see in the movie also, they running behind them and the strong ones run away. Who has illness or something, not good for the herd will be cleaned from that. And the lion come & eat, cannot eat all. Maybe eat 10 kilo, 500 kilo this one ox. 10 kilo, 20 kilo. Bring his children, they eat also. When they finish, coming fox, wolf. After they come eagle, other..All animal coming & eating from this, only one. And this is what is Allah sent for them, they eat. They not harm any other. Even when he is not hungry, not doing anything. This is the, he is generous. Generosity is the best character of awliya & Sahaba & all holy people. But wolf, when he coming to one place of sheep if he can go inside, not eating one 2, 3. Until morning he can kill them all from here. Fox, same. If come to chicken not eating one & going.
