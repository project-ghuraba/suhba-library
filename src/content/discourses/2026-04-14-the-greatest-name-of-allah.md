As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
‎وَلِلَّهِ ٱلْأَسْمَآءُ ٱلْحُسْنَىٰ فَٱدْعُوهُ بِهَا
<br>“Wa li-Llāhi l-asmā’u l-ḥusnā fad‘ūhu bihā’’<br>
‘‘And to Allah belong the Most Beautiful Names, so call on Him by them.” (Qur’ān 07:180). ṢadaqAllahu l-ʿAẓīm.

“Whoever recites or memorizes the names of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla will enter Paradise,” says our Prophet ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. These blessed names are the attributes of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. Some are special to Him, while others can be used by humans. Some names are unique to Him; no one else can use them.

Therefore, our Prophet ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam says, “Whoever enumerates these names will enter Paradise.” Let those who can memorize them do so. Those who cannot memorize them can certainly recite them.

Therefore, this is a beneficence that Allah has bestowed upon His servants. Of course, the names of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla are countless and infinite. Different names were given to each prophet.

Our Prophet ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam was given 99 names. One of them is Ism-ul ‘Azam, the Greatest Name. It is a secret. The du’ā of a person who attains that secret is answered; his du’ā - that is accepted. Of course, it is only granted to whomever Allah permits; it is not granted upon everyone. And those who possess it are hidden, of course; not everyone knows who possesses Ism-ul ‘Azam. That’s why it’s called “The Greatest Name.” “‘Azam” means the Greatest. And it is hidden.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla has bestowed its barakah and special qualities upon certain people. And those who possess it, of course, do not deviate from Allah’s pleasure. The person who carries the Greatest Name fulfills its right. Of course, there have been those who misused it in the past. They suffered Allah’s wrath in this world, and will suffer even more in the hereafter. It’s the famous story of the Banī Israīl (The Children of Israīl.) The shayṭān deceives them. They serve the unbelieving king. Allah sends down His curse and wrath upon them. They regret it, but their regret is of no use.

May Allah protect us from evil. May He put these names in our hearts. May Allah grant us, and indeed all of humanity, the attributes befitting us, inshā’Allah.

And for His other names, may the barakah of Allah be upon us all. May the barakah of those names special to Him be upon us all, inshā’Allah.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
