As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
فَعَّالٌ لِّمَا يُرِيدُ
<br>“Faʿʿālun limā yurīd”<br>
“He does what He wills.” (Qur’ān 85:16). ṢadaqAllahu l-ʿAẓīm.

Jalla Jalāluh. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla is the One Who does whatever He wills. What He wills is what happens. As the poet said: “Mevla’m görelim neyler, Neylerse güzel eyler”, “Let’s see what God does. Whatever He does He does it best.” He says, “Watch.” That’s the situation now. People say, “The world is collapsing,” but everything happens by the decree of Allah, by the command of Allah.

Nothing can be done by your hand, or our hand, or anyone’s hand. He does what He wants. “Faʿʿāl” means the Absolute Doer, the one who does everything, who accomplishes everything exactly and only as He wills. Nothing can stand against His will, nothing can stand in the way of His will.

Therefore, Islām is submission. You must submit to Allah. There’s no need to overthink, “What will I do?” What is meant to happen, happens. What Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla wills and says, happens. Therefore, be with Him. We should be with Allah so that He may have mercy on us and bestow His beneficence upon us.

There is no need for worry or fear. He is Arhamu r-Rāhimin, the Most Merciful of the Merciful. There is no one more merciful than Him. Even if humankind possesses everything, mercy is the will of Allah Himself. It is one of the beautiful attributes of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. The attribute of Allah Jalla Jalāluh is The Most Merciful.

Therefore, a Muslim has surrendered everything to Allah. His will, with Allah’s permission, is beneficial to the Muslim. For the non-Muslim, it is torment and punishment. May Allah protect us. May we be at rest, inshā’Allah.

For the one who surrenders, everything in life comes and goes easily. For the one who does not surrender, there is constant torment, suffering, and discomfort.

May Allah protect us. May Allah protect us all, inshā’Allah.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
