Call people by nice way and with soft, softness. Don't be tough for them. This what Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla taught ProphetNone and ProphetNone teaching his ummah. If people they know wisdom everywhere for, especially for Muslim people, it will be like paradise. But they are not knowing wisdom and they don't want to learn wisdom. Wisdom learned by way of ProphetNone and his ṣaḥābah, and after his ṣaḥābah there is follower, Tābi‘īn, and follower of follower Taba‘a t-Tābi‘īn. This follower meaning the awliyā’u Llāh.

There is ṭarīqat - way of ProphetNone. Ṭarīqat meaning 'way'. There is forty-one ṭarīqat, all going to ProphetNone. And these ṭarīqat they are teaching softness, the wisdom, and how to deal with people. If you not with them, so some people coming they teaching you opposite of this - no wisdom. They are teaching also to be tough, to not be good with anybody from your religion or other religion. They teach you to be enemy for them, to hate them. This is not good. What ProphetNone teaching [is] opposite of this. And awliyā’u Llāh also same.

Once there is famous awliyā’u Llāh from Balkh, his name Shaqīq al-Balkhī He is famous. He was trader and he was travelling and AllāhNone give him hidāyah in some way. He was good but he was following more dunyā and he, when AllāhNone give him something to show him right way, he still doing his business but with knowing it is not target, it is only way to help people, to help Muslim. Once he was with his friend. When he was young he was has many friend, going around with them. And once he saw temple for Majūsī - Majūsī meaning who are, they are worshipping fire. And he said to his friend, "let's go here and see what happen here." They went inside and he was one young man very nice looking, handsome, and he was worshipping fire. And when he see him, he tell him, "oh what are you doing?" Said, "I'm worship..." and said, "this is not real, come to Islām" for this young man he said to him. "What you doing it is not good, no benefit from this." And when happen this, young man he slapped him in his face. And he think, "Oh I deserve this because my sins too much, I cannot... not affective for this. So I'm not real, I don't have this..." But he kept his - he not getting angry. He was good and he looking for himself.

And after this he forget this, he was going with mashāyikh and he learn many thing from sharī‘ah, Islām, everything. When after many years, maybe twenty years, he also was going with friend and he said this also temple let's look what here. And he find, he going inside, seeing one old man worshipping fire. Told him: "Oh what you do it is no benefit for you, come to Islām." And he said, "ok teach me Islām" and he make shahādah become Muslim this man. And after he remember. He said "there was one here, young man, for many years. What happen for this young man?" he said. He said "this I'm. This is what young man, I'm same one." He said, "Oh you remember I was coming here with friend and I tell you and you refuse to become Muslim? Why now you accept?" He said, "that time you come you don't have any light. But when now I saw you, you are all have light and you have power from spiritual power. Quickly I surrendered and become Muslim." This is very important when you are calling somebody to good thing, to way of AllāhNone.

You must be have wisdom. You must have be patient, you must be good attribute. When you are here like this, everybody he accept, many people. But if you are just quickly, you want after two days from coming to ṭarīqat you must teaching people to come to ṭarīqat, they will run away. You must first teach yourself and to train your ego to be not up top of you, to must be under you. And after this people will be happy. And you must have also knowledge. If you don't have knowledge, if they ask something [if] you know real, you can answer, but if don't, you can say to them also "I don't know this". This is also giving good thing for people because to say something not right, make from beginning wrong thing happen. So when it is wrong thing happen, the people they cannot be benefit from you. As we said the most important to have wisdom, to have patience, and to be honest.

In shā’a Llāh, our people if they are little by little they can, in shā’a Llāh, make people to come to right way. Because way of shayṭān many people they are running there. They... because wrong way of shayṭān wrong - the people running after wrong. But if you are way of Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla need more work, need more effort, to bring people - to be careful and to teach every good thing in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone help you and help us in shā’a Llāh to be in right way.

Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq al-Fātiḥah.
