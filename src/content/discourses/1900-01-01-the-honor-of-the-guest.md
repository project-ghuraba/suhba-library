Haji Anne, Sohbat in Turkish from 1990.

Audhu Billahi min al shaytan al rajim, Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Allahumma la hawla wa la quwwata illa Billah al Aliyyil Azim. Prophet (sas) said that if a guest comes to a person’s house, an angel comes 40 days before. The angel calls, “Oh, owner of the house... Oh, host...”. For example he says, “Aminah Hanim”, she doesn’t hear this call. Or, he calls, “Fatima Hanim”. She also doesn’t hear because when the eyes of her heart do not see, her ears do not hear. There was an ancestor of ours who was a Shaykh. He said, “These eyes look like the knot holes of trees when the real eyes don’t see and when the ears don’t hear — the ears of the heart.” So Jibrail (as) came to answer, “What do you want from the owner of this house?” And that angel would have a bundle like this and Jibrail (as) would ask: “What is in this bundle?” Angel: “A guest will come to this house in 40 days, on this day, at this hour. I came to give the glad tidings of it.” Jibrail (as): “And what is in this bundle you carry?” Angel: “I bring the portion of the guest.” A house that receives many guests definitely has much barakah. So the angel brings the bundle and there is a sealed envelope inside it. Jibrail (as) asks, “What is this?” Angel: “It is the letter from the Lord of Heavens saying that the owner of this house is set free from hell and belongs to paradise. The Lord of Heavens sealed it and gave it to me for the honour of the guest.” Then Jibrail (as) starts shaking for this thing, which means he also desires it. He says, “The portion of the guest is from paradise, so the honour and value of the guest is like this: The host receives this much thawab; the one serving his guest receives paradise.” When Jibrail (as) says this, the angel asks “Should I say more?” Jibrail (as) says, “Say”. Angel: “I will stay in this house until the guest arrives and eats his qismat/share. I will worship in this house and write my worship in the host’s book.” The worship of an angel for five minutes is more than the worship of all men and jinns.... just five minutes! For 40 days, the angel will write his worship there in the host’s book. When he desires more, the angel says, “Should I say more, Oh, Jibrail?” Jibrail (as): “Say, Oh Angel!” Angel: “For each bite of food and drink this guest eats and drinks, one thawab of Hajj and one thawab of Umrah will be written for the host.” Those who perform Hajj or Umrah know with what they are dressed there. For each each bite, one Hajj and one Umrah thawab will be written. And after this, when the guest comes here, eats his share and leaves, all the sins of the owner of this house are forgiven. Whoever lives in his house — men, women, children and others, and if he has relatives (mother, father, children) in far places — their sins will also be forgiven because of that guest. What is the best of deeds? Prophet (sas) said, “It is the most difficult of them”. Maybe it is because having a guest is a heavy and difficult thing that the Lord of Heavens makes that place where guests are honored a very glorified one. All their sins are forgiven; and when that guest leaves the house, the whole family of the owner of the house is cleaned from sins like newborn babies. Once upon a time there was a husband and wife in a village. They also had children, and they had a goat. They would milk the goat, make yoghurt, get milk and make their living this way. When the Eid al-Adha came, the man said, “Everyone will sacrifice an animal tomorrow. We’ll sacrifice the goat.” The woman said, “We don’t have any other animal than this goat. Sacrifice is not obligatory for us, and the children also love this goat very much. With its milk, we can manage the children very well. It is not obligatory for us. You leave this.” So the man gave up on the idea. The Eid came. Everybody brought them meat. So they got along well. After some time passed, dervishes came to their house, lots of guests. The woman called her husband and said, “Now it is obligatory for us to slaughter the goat. We will serve these guests food and besides, we don’t have anything else. Let’s slaughter this goat and serve our guests.” The man said, “If the children see this, they will start crying. They’ll shame us in front of the guests. There is no way to explain to them. They will cry and shout saying, ‘He is slaughtering the goat!’.” The woman said, “I will keep the children busy. You go and slaughter the goat behind the old wall.” So the poor woman went to keep children busy on one side while her husband was slaughtering the goat on the other side. While he was slaughtering the goat, she saw that the goat climbed up on the old wall, bleating and crying. The woman said, “Ya Allah, my husband missed the goat”. When she went to look, she saw that he had slaughtered the goat and was about to remove its skin. But the new goat was exactly the same and belonged to no one else. It was theirs. They milked it in the same way. By the Wisdom of Allah, after that day they would have guests, serve them and send them off. And from that goat, which came from the unseen, one of its udders would give honey and the other udder butter. And just like this, whosoever came there sick, and ate and drank from the milk, was cured. If he was poor, he got rich. So people would come from many distant places to have the goat milked, to get some milk from that goat, and to benefit from it. Finally, there was Dhul-Nun al-Misri Hazretleri, who was from the great awliya. He heard this and said, “Let me go and look what is the reason for this. There must be a reason for this, that Allah granted them this goat.” All who came to visit came with a gift and did not leave empty; they found comfort. He said, “Milk this goat in front of me”. They would milk it in front of everyone so that they drink it. Really it was pure honey and cream. So Dhul-Nun al-Misri Hz. asked them how; and they explained to him. He said, “It is the barakah of the guests. Because you showed the guests respect, the Lord of Heavens granted this to you. In place of your goat, He granted such a special one from His Unseen.” He said like this and left. Ila sharafin-nabi, salla-Allahu ‘alayhi wa sallam wa alihi wa sahbihil-kiraam wa ila arwaahil a’immati’l arb ‘wa siddiqiyyun, Al-Fatihah. Allahumma Salli ala Sayyidina Muhammad.
