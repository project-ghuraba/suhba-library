Shaykh Mohammad Nazim Al-Haqqani An-Naqshbandi, Sohbat March 27, 2010.

Dastur Ya Sayyidi madad, Ya Rijal Allah, madad. Allah Allah, Allah Allah, Allah Allah, Aziz Allah Allah Allah, Allah Allah, Allah Allah, Karim Allah, Allah Allah, Allah Allah, Allah Allah, Subhan Allah, Allah Allah, Allah Allah, Allah Allah, Sultan Allah. Ya Rabbi zid Habibaka izzan wa sharafa, nuran wa surura wa ridwanan wa sultana ya anta hu sultana li'l khala'iq al jami`a ya rabbana ya rabb'ighfir w'arham anta khayrur rahimin. Madad, madad, Ya Rijal Allah, madad. Madad Ya Sahib al Zaman, madad Ya Sultan al Awliya.

Prophet (pbuh) Habibullah, what was he saying? Allahumma la takilni ila nafsi tarfata aynin. Therefore we are asking a heavenly support against our egos. Our ego is attacking us. It is asking to reach the reins to make us its donkey and putting reins to send us as it likes. Sometimes I am using for that, 'it', because to say 'that' gives honour. 'It' -for animals and for the whole world, you may use it for them that haven't any soul or living things in it. Therefore I like to make, not I like but they like to make shaytan always down. Make it down. Don't give to shaytan any honour. Don't use any honourable word for shaytan. That is important. And I am saying, audhu Billahi min as-shaytan ar-rajim.

O our Lord, we are asking humbly a protection against shaytan and its followers. That is important. Dastur Ya Sayyidi Ya Sultan al Awliya. And we are saying, O our Master, O Qutb al Zaman, O Qutb al Mutasarrif who has been granted from heavenly stations to look after this world and everything that is happening on it. Don't think this world is left as it likes to be through space. Don't think that. Everything is under control, very carefully, exactly. Control must be on everything, can't be in existence anything that its Creator s not knowing. How that can be? He is creating and He knows that an atom can't know about itself. As you Mankind, you know about yourself? Can be able to know about yourself? Who is knowing?

People are trying to know something around themselves but for themselves it is cut because our eyes are looking around ourselves only. But our eyes can't look at inner being of ourselves. If I am asking you 'Where is your liver?' So many people don't know. If you're asking ‘Where's your heart?’ "That I am not knowing". Man is ignorant. Fully ignorant about himself. And what you are saying is only for man's 'out-looking-position'.

We are saying: Audhu Billahi min as-shaytan ar-rajim because shaytan always asking to confuse. Not leaving you to know. It has been said that a person has through his physical being, 360 organs. Small or bigger and you are not knowing about yourself. How can you ask to know around yourself? Don't say that to look in space is knowledge, saying, 'That is Polestar, that is sun, that is moon, that is Seven Sisters, that is Alpha System'. It is nothing! And it gives to you nothing! It is not real knowledge about yourself that you are seeing. How you may understand about yourself, how your organs are working? You are eating and drinking and enjoying it. Where is it going inside? What is its importance? You don't know. You are only drinking and eating. And you as Man have been granted such an honour. Your honour is to know. No other creatures on this planet know about themselves because they haven't any mind or they can't think. And their levels, animal levels. But your level is on the level of angels.

Is it so? Salafi ulamas? Marhaban. What you are saying for the levels of Mankind? Where are their levels on Earth, or in Heavens? You know it? Say! Don't worry. And we are saying: Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Rahim. To open something that makes our understanding about creatures and Creation. Holy Quran, Quran-i-Karim is teaching people everything. Yes? Teaching or not? Salafi ulamas marhaban. Where are you? You must teach us. It is not enough to say 'We are Salafi ulamas'. Keeping under your desk 2-3 books, no.

Subhanallah. Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Rahim. Allah Almighty is saying: faqsusi al qasasa. You are never telling stories. If anyone asks to say a story you are saying 'Israiliyaat', hmm? Why you are saying this? Why you are not saying qissas? Stories. So many stories. From beginning up to end, so many stories. Perhaps millions of volumes of books can't carry them because they are not enough. If millions of books, a person can't be able to keep them through his mind. Yes, someone may reach that point that they may keep through their hearts stories beginning with Sayyidina Adam (as) up to today, okay?

Salafi ulamas, what is your vision about that? Now we are coming, we are coming. Don't say 'We are ulamas', but you must be seekers of knowledge. Be students!

kun `aliman, aw muta`alliman, aw mustami'an, aw muhibban, wala takun rabi'an, fa tahluk.

Yeah? Hadith. Sometimes I know hadith also. Marhaban, don't be angry. If you are getting angry, my anger will give trouble to you. Don't be angry but you must be a seeker. Ask more. Don't stop on a level and say, 'That is enough for us'. No! You must ask a second step. Second level. You are understanding Arabic?

Allah (swt) huwa yaqool jalla min qa`il:

la tarkabunna tabaqan an tabaqin (84:19)

Fi qira`a, latarkabanna, tabaqan an tabaq. That is Holy Quran or not? Why you are stopping on a level and not asking higher? You may say, 'To get up we must have some instruments or wings’. Yes, you know too much. If a person is asking to go up they must have some machines to take them up, like mighty engines of that plane? Name of that plane with mighty engines. Jet planes. No mind people all of you. Say jet planes. Jet planes may get much higher than everything and everyone. Yes, because they have jet machines that are mighty engines for taking them up. If you are not reaching there why are you denying those who are reaching up? You say, 'It is not something that is true, we are not seeing it in our books'. You can't say this. Your Salaf as-Salehi'l ulama never reached a plane and men with mighty power planes that have jet engines. Why you are staying there? Take it now because Allah Almighty is granting.

Utlubu al ilm, seek knowledge, that is the heavenly order to the Seal of Prophets (sas) to say, "O People, you must ask more and more". Our physical being’s limits are very short, but beyond our minds there is another level that belongs to our souls. You may learn 'Alif, Ba' through jet planes but what does it mean? Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Rahim Alif Laam Meem, your level can't reach the meanings including those three words and Allah Almighty making it after Surat al Fatiha, tajizan li’l khalaiq. Say! What does it mean Alif Laam Meem? You know? You don't know. How you are saying you are ulamas? What Salafi ulama said about Alif Laam Meem? You know? Alif, ocean. Laam, another ocean, Meem, another ocean. I can't open that oceans' looking-points to you because you can't carry.

Your ancestors were riding on donkey. 'Jahsh'. It used to be said that the one who rode the donkey is called the donkey's dad. We are not even having a donkey as yet. We are not yet reaching to have a donkey and to make that donkey under our commands. "O Shaykh what you are saying about jahsh and donkey? We have now a most important Mercedes car and we have Princess cars. We have so many famous -BMW". Many times people speaking (about it) and I am keeping. BMW may fall on their heads. "They are new ones and famous ones. We are not using donkeys We are using now WB.. (BMW) WC? What? What saying? Hanh, BMW, not WC. And that is better for using. Yes then, O our Masters because you are saying 'We are inheritors' from Salafi ulamas, Salafi ulamas were riding on donkeys, jahsh, but we are not using jahsh now, Mercedes or W.. '.

O People use your minds when someone is speaking, addressing to people. Yes, everyone is abu jahshin. Ayn jahshin? You may ask, "Where is my donkey?" Look in the mirror and you will see where it is. Yes, that is your donkey and that is what they are saying. "Everyone is Abu Jahshin". Our jahsh, our donkey is our ego. You know Arabic or not knowing? What the Seal of Prophets Sayyidina Muhammad (sas) was saying? What he said to the one who was asking about his 'matiyyah', his ride. What was the answer of the Seal of Prophets (sas)? nafsuka matiyatuka -yes, Arabic or Turkish I am speaking? Say what you are understanding about the two words that the Seal of Prophets (sas) was saying? To you, to me, to this one, to that one. What is our ride? Nafsuka matiyatuka and nafs jabbarrah. It is powerful, if you can be able to keep reins on your ego, it can carry you as Buraq did, it may do for everyone. If they can be able to ride on their ego it can take, masha Allah ila hayth rabbuna jalla wa aala yurid, up to that point that our Lord is asking to you to reach.

You can't reach the heavenly station as the Holy Quran is saying: la tarkabunna tabaqan an tabaqin. How they are riding, tarkabunna? With their bodies or they have a ride to say, yamaat nafsuka matiyatuka. Those who are asking to reach heavenly station must use some other creature and Holy Command comes to that one that 'Obey your Owner's command'. Why are we not speaking such things for people? To make them to take themselves from Earth up to Arsh? Why you are not saying? What you are giving your lessons about? For what? Anyone understanding what you are saying? Even you are not understanding.

O People, O People, utlub al ilm, ask Real Knowledge. From whom? From those who are granted from heavenly ones qudsi, muqaddas, they have been granted heavenly knowledge, ask them, it is coming to them, fas`al bihi khabira (25:59) There must be those who know that way. Say. Have you found it? You are saying you are Salaf as-Saleh and Salaf as-Saleh are living with ourselves? Why? Only Salaf as-Saleh are knowing and none others knowing anything? Why? That is the reason that not everyone can stand up on the minbar or kursi and speak to people. No, no. They carry a heavy responsibility because they are not understanding real meanings on our levels, that belong to our levels, no higher.

Marhaban... They know from beginning up to end, what happened and what should happen because those people are looking in mirror of the Seal of Prophets and looking there but the blind can't see anything in the mirror. Open your eyes. Go to some doctors. Who are they? Allah Almighty saying: kunu ma as-sadiqin (9:119) They may open your blindness, opening your eyes. "Insha Allah, ah, it is another view, another level, we never had been here, what is that, what is that?" You must try to know, it is not enough what you are saying.

Islam is for all nations, for all Mankind. And you may be able to give for everyone what they may be able to digest. When hearing or eating making it to digest. "We have so many millions of books" but you may go in an operation room, 100, 200 instruments. He is not using everything on a person, no, only what is necessary. Holy Quran is an endless ocean. Not whole, but even one letter, one holy letter from Holy Quran is an endless ocean. Who are you? Saying 'We are ulama'. What you are knowing? You must come to the level of 'kun taliban'. Ask and learn. Don't say 'We are followers of Salaf as-Saleh'. Ask your Salaf as-Saleh what that 'nothing-servant' is saying every night -so many things, if you have a spiritual connection to those ulamas, ask if it is true or not true.

Why you are not asking? You are asking his majesty, why asking? No, his position is something else. Allah Almighty just put everyone on their real positions. wad`u ashiya fi mawadi'ihi, Arabic. Malik he is malik, ulamas, you are ulamas. Those ones doctors, those ones.. liars? Lawyers, yes. Put everything in its real position, then you can see a good view for secret worlds through Creation. May Allah forgive me. Assalamu alaykum. That is an ocean, up to the end of the world, never finishing. We are cutting something, something, something, and reaching that point for this, our association that is granted to us from heavenly ones. Everyone of them is oceans. I don't know, they know. They know what they should say and do. Come and be a seeker. wa fawqa kulli dhi `ilmin `alim (12:76)
