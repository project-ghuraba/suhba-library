As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla says in Qur’ān ʿAẓīmu sh-Sha’n, Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ كُتِبَ عَلَيْكُمُ ٱلصِّيَامُ كَمَا كُتِبَ عَلَى ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبْلِكُمْ لَعَلَّكُمْ تَتَّقُونَ
<br>“Yā ayyuhā allaḏīna āmanū kutiba ʿalaykumu ṣ-ṣiyām kamā kutiba ʿalā allaḏīna min qablikum laʿallakum tattaqūn.”<br>
“O you who believe! Observing fasting is prescribed for you as it was prescribed for those before you, that you may become God-fearing.” (Qurʼān 2:183). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.
 
“O believers,” people who receive this addressing of Allah. What a nice and great honour it is that Allah is addressing us. Not to compare but if someone famous in this world were to address you, you would lose your mind with happiness. Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla is addressing us, all of us, “O believers.” He accepts us as believers, and then He says, “I will give you a beautiful way of worship, fasting. Fasting is given to you. It is farḍ for you as it was for nations before you. They fasted before you too. Christians did, Jews did and people before them fasted too.”

Fasting is a necessary worship for people. It is a duty. When you do it, your spirituality increases and your body is cleansed. That is necessary. Without fasting, mankind can eat and drink and do other things for 24 hours. And because of fasting, in one month they abstain for 15 hours, 18-19 hours. They eat and drink nothing. And that is something required for the body – it is a rest. 

And more importantly, Ramaḍān is a holy month, not only in this world, but also in Paradise. When Ramaḍān comes, Paradise is decorated. It is different than it was the year before. Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla gives it a different, more beautiful decoration. A nice wind from under the Throne blows in Paradise. A sound like it is impossible to hear anywhere in the world. Angels and Ḥūrīs in Paradise become very happy.

Ramaḍān is a very good month. Allah gave it to believers and addressed them. He favoured us with it, and we accepted that favour with pleasure, shukr to Allah. InshāAllah, we receive that addressing. It is a nice addressing. Just this saying of Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla is enough and even too much for us, and His favours are limitless. Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla gives more every time. When people give something, they fear they will have less if they give more. When Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla gives, He gives tenfold, hundredfold, thousandfold. The treasures of Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla have no end. May Allah bless this month and our fasting, inshāAllah.

Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla He attending in Qur’ān Karīm saying,<br>
يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ كُتِبَ عَلَيْكُمُ ٱلصِّيَامُ كَمَا كُتِبَ عَلَى ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبْلِكُمْ لَعَلَّكُمْ تَتَّقُونَ
<br>“Yā ayyuhā allaḏīna āmanū kutiba ʿalaykumu ṣ-ṣiyām kamā kutiba ʿalā allaḏīna min qablikum laʿallakum tattaqūn.”<br>
“O you who believe! Observing fasting is prescribed for you as it was prescribed for those before you, that you may become God-fearing.” (Qurʼān 2:183).

“O Believer” – Allah addressing us. It is very nice addressing. He is directly addressing for us – “O Believer” – “Yā ayyuhā l-ladhīna āmanū.” When you see, hear this it is enough for you because it is most beautiful addressing for us. Not the... Allah Almighty addressing. After this He is saying what He is addressing for us: “You must fast,” and fast it is written for you as it was written and obligatory for nations before you like Banī Isrā’il and ‘Īsa ʿalayhi s-salām, and Nūḥ ʿalayhi s-salām all this, Ibrāhīm ʿalayhi s-salām. They was fasting also.

But of course Ramaḍān, their one month, and some of them they make it for like spring time because summer very hot for them. Winter it was very cold so they was making in spring time, fasting in spring time. But is fasting also normally it must be like our fasting but Banī Isrā’il, sons of Isrā’il, there was fasting. We was also before, first of, when they were breaking fast until they sleep. When they sleep, no more, you cannot eat. Maybe 20 hours, 22 hours no eating. But Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla for us He make it more easy. Whole night we can eat until Fajr. And it is nice worshipping for our body and for our soul, our spirituality.

Of course it's most important for spirituality but it for, without forcing your body you cannot be you find spirituality. If you leave your body to, you can eat 24 hours drinking, eating. But it is obligatory to not eat anything, to not drink anything, to not smoke daytime until sunset, from sun, before sunrise. Because there is also now shayṭān people they saying you can fast from sunrise to sunset. No. Not sunrise – Fajr. Sunrise, between Fajr and sunrise some place one and a half hours, some place two hours. So not from sunrise, from imsāk it is Fajr time. And this make people to not do anything maybe 12 or until 19, 20 hours some people no drinking, no eating, no smoking, not doing anything, cannot break your fast. So it is one month you control your body, your hunger, your thirsty, thirst and anything else. So it is also you control your ego in this time. So it is very nice worshipping, very benefit worshipping.

And Ramaḍān it is month of blessed and happiness and beauty. Even in Jannah Paradise, when Ramaḍān coming they are changing from last Ramaḍān. Whole new thing they put. They make it more beautiful because Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla He is, He has endless treasure, endless beauty. Every time better and better and more beautiful and more beautiful. And when Ramaḍān coming, all this in Jannah Paradise making new decoration, a new thing. And there is, when beginning of Ramaḍān, one wind blowing from under Throne of Allah and it is giving very nice air and with this air also very nice sound. So in Jannah now there is only Ḥūri l-‘Ayn and Angels. They are very happy for this and saying, “We are waiting for our believers. When they come, Allah reward them with this Paradise.” It is very nice and who has...

Allah make it easy for him to fast. He is real be very, very happy. As Prophet saying, when ifṭār coming believer he has two happinesses. One when he is breaking fast is very nice moment, very nice time, very happy time, and this is compare, there is another one for Jannah he said. One here, one for Jannah. So, alḥamdulillāh, inshā’Allah, Allah grant us this happiness all time, inshāAllah, and give for people who are not knowing this.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
