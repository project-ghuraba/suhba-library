A‘ūdhu Bi-llāhi mina sh--shayṭāni r--rajīm Bismillahi r--Rahmani r--Rahim la hawla wala quwwata illa Billahi l-Alliyi l-Azeem

By the Name of Allah Almighty, All Merciful, Most Benificent and Most Munificent. When a person feels that he is restful, comfort then they follow everything alright. Hu. When you are going to be restful? Now, we are sitting, restful. But, rest going to be in our hearts. If in your heart you are not feeling rest and comfort, it is not something, to sit on throne, or in a comfort place. When your heart [is] feeling rest and comfort then, if you are sitting on stone, if you are sitting in prison, if you are sitting on fire, no worry, doesn't matter.

Who gives comfort to people? Their hearts. And people now, leaving their hearts. Mostly people interest-ing in their physical bodies and physical pleasures. And whole Prophets, from beginning up to end, just came to arrange our hearts. They are engineers of hearts. Not anyone else can look after hearts except prophets, and their inheritors, awliyas. I am listening what news [is] coming for today and they are going through this valley. Every day they are making with us, a tour to make us pleased.

Allah Almighty likes to be Their servants always in pleasure and to be pleased. That is important. You must be pleased with your Lord. Therefore, He is beginning His Holy Book, Quran saying:

“al-ḥamdu li-Llahi rabbi l-ʿālamīna” All praise is to Allah, Lord of the Worlds (1:2).

Yes, you must understand what He means. Awliya saying that it means, "Oh My servants, all of you, say: "Al-ḥamdu li-Llah'". He asking from us to be pleased with Him. Yes, that is important. Ghana'ima (winnings). Be pleased with your Lord! If you are going to be pleased with Him He is going to do you [make you] pleased with Him, pleased with everything. Say that, "Oh my Lord I am pleased with You". The order covering our life from beginning up to end when you are understanding yourself. You have been ordered to be pleased with your Lord.

When you have reached 15 years old, the age of maturity, you must say that you are on the age, that [is] like a new rose. You must look [at] yourself and you must say Al-ḥamdu li-Llah! From that age, up to [the] end you must say "thanks to my Lord". Praising. "Whole praisings and thanks to my Lord, Almighty. That is Holy Quran's teaching to us. And He says also, when whole our life going on and finishing and the Day of Judgement coming and then people, you believers, entering in Paradise, and you are finding everything, every desires, you may imagine or not. Allah Almighty giving you endless desires. You may ask. Endless desires, and He is going to fulfil. Giving everything, and saying: 'Ask more, and I am giving'.

waākhiru daʿwāhum ani l-ḥamdu lillahi rabbi l-ʿālamīna The first and last word from servants to their Lord: 'al-ḥamdu li-Llahi rabbi l-ʿālamīn' (10:10).

Endless thanks and most respectful praisings only to our Lord, Almighty Allah. That is Divinely Teaching. He [is] teaching us by His Prophets and by the Seal of Prophets (pbuh) and, through his inheritors, awliyas. Teaching people to say 'Al-ḥamdu li-Llah'. Thanks to our Lord, endless thanks. He [is] going to be pleased when His servants [are] saying 'Al-ḥamdu li-Llah'. No time for it, to say al-ḥamdu li-Llah and nothing else.

If you are living, from beginning up to end and saying: al-ḥamdu li-Llah al-ḥamdu li-Llah al-ḥamdu li-Llah al-ḥamdu li-Llah al-ḥamdu li-Llah and nothing else, you are reaching to your Lord. You are going to be neighbour to Prophet, Beloved Muhammed (pbuh). And you are going to be neighbour to Allah Almighty. The Day of Resurrection, whole people coming [to] Mahshar, the Place of Judgement place. It is going to be in Damascus, Prophet saying. The Plain of Damascus is the plain of Judgement Day.

Whole people from east and west, from north and south, some of them walking, some of them riding. Riders coming. Who walks [on] the Day of Resurrection? Those whom not knowing to ride on their egos. They are walking and they are carrying their egos on their shoulders. Those who [are] riding on their egos, Allah Almighty sending to them Buraq, holy horse from Paradise. And they are riding, coming to the Plain of Judgement Day. And whole people full-up that plain. So many people, from beginning up to end, it is going to be there, present In space, one foot square one thousand people, [will be] trying to put their foot there. Their feet on that place. So many.

Those people coming on their Buraq to that plain, they have private stations. Then, before beginning judgement, Allah Almighty ordering one angel to shout for all people, saying: Those whom they are always saying "Al-ḥamdu li-Llah", for every occasion, saying, "Al-ḥamdu li-Llah", for every condition that they may be in, saying "Al-ḥamdu li-Llah, endless thanks to our Lord and most respectful praising to our Lord," - saying [angels to] people, "Stand up!"

They are standing among whole people and coming to them angels, taking one from this side, one from this side, one-and-one, getting on it and going from Mahshar, Allah Almighty ordering, "Take them in My Paradise! No judgement for them, nothing for them. They were pleased with Me! Every time! And I am pleased with them. No need [for] judgement here, they may enter in My Paradise. They may enjoy themselves because they were pleased with Me". We are trying to be pleased with our Lord because as much as our egos [are] with us it is difficult to be pleased with our Lord. Because mostly, we are trying to make our egos pleased with us. Wrong way. Wrong method. And your ego, your nafs is not going to be pleased with you forever [ever]. As much as you are giving and making everything, making your best with him [it], but your nafs is not going to be satisfied and [not] saying to you, "Thank you". Never saying "Thank you". Yes, you know it? You know? Abdu n-Nur, you know this? Saying to you 'thank you?' Sometimes? Never saying! Never says 'thank you'. Very proud person. He never said to Allah Almighty, 'thank you'. How going to say to you 'thank you?'

Yes, as much as our egos [are] with us and we are listening to him [it], it is too difficult to say to Allah Almighty, "Thanks to my Lord". Therefore, it is a great fight, great war among you and your nafs. When you win that war then, he is going to say to you "O my master, thank you". And, as you like. This fasting is most important worshipping. Gives respect to Allah Almighty and same time, making our egos to know how much he is weak. And teaching him that when he is hungry and then, when he is fasting and making break fast then, he feels to say, "Al-ḥamdu li-Llah".

Teaching, yes, how you may be pleased with your Lord? Now, your ego and our nafs [is] crying, crying. "So hungry, so thirsty". Then you are saying, "No, wait until the time comes". He [is] saying "it is alright" and you are saying "2 minutes more". "Left two minutes". "Give me to drink, to eat". You are saying "No! You must wait, 2 minutes more". "One minute more". "Now!". And he, your nafs is learning to say thanks. Firstly to you, then, saying to his Lord, "Thanks. Endless thanks to my Lord, Allah Almighty" Yes. Al-ḥamdu li-Llah.

Allah Almighty helps us on our egos and you can't take Divinely Help without intention. When you are intending, then Divinely Help coming to you and supporting you. If you are not intending you can't find Divinely Help and support. Therefore, if a little boy [is] intending for fasting, he may fast. But, like Hercules, if Hercules, no intending falling down, he can't fast. Because, no Divinely Support. [He] May be Hercules but, if [there is] no Divinely Support, he may take away these columns, so powerful, but so weak for fasting.

Therefore, we are living in a very difficult time and if you look, you may decide that it is impossible to be a Muslim, or to keep the Divinely Orders in a Western country. In a circle that [is] surrounding you with devils. Everyone [is] trying to catch you out and you are weak, but you are intending and saying, "Oh my Lord, I am a weak servant," or "weakest servant to You and I am surrounded by devils and everything [is] catching me from one side to take me from īmān to kufr. From believing [to] unbelieving, from Islam to non-Muslims circles, from, obedience to be rebellion to You. From goodness to badness, from honour to dishonour, from light to darkness. from goodness to badness. And I am weak, perhaps weakest one and I am asking Your Divinely Protection and Divinely Help, Divinely Support". Then, you can find yourself protected! Fasting, praying, keeping yourself far away from devils. Devils, running away from you and your being such, as Omar. Shaytan, saying to Prophet (pbuh) when he asked "how [do] you know that one, Omar?" And shaytan saying "If I am looking [at] Omar coming from this way, I am turning to that way". Not to come face by [to] face. Yes. As much as we are feeling our weakness then, comes more, more help, more support. There is now one mu'mina with [a] basket, bringing a little boy but there is different ages, girls, boys, there - children. But, mostly his parents looking on [the] weakest one. If she is coming with three boys her eyes going to be on the weakest one.

More support for [the] weakest one. Therefore, as much as you are saying "O our Lord! I am the weakest one among those people," and mostly coming on you. Divinely Support. But if you are saying "I am doctor" "I am mureed", "I am Shaykh", "I am a scholar" "I am worshipper", then, leaving your self to your self. You have been left in the hands of your egos When you are saying that "I am enough". "I have enough knowledge", "I have enough praise". Then, you have been left in the hands of your ego, your nafs. Therefore, Prophet in his whole life [was] calling to His Lord Allah Almighty and saying "Oh my Lord, please don't leave me in the hands of my ego".

This [is a] big lesson. For everyone. Beginning from prophets, to saints, to awliyas and all believers and all people because, who has been left in the hands of his ego, ruswai oldu, resil oldu (became dishonoured). He lost his honour, here and hereafter. Making him to to be ashamed, that is meaning. Who has been left in the hands of his ego must be ashamed. And Prophet saying in his prayings, "O my Lord, please don't leave me in the hands of my ego" He is Prophet, he is protected. Divinely protected. But, he means to teach us.

We must ask from our Lord so that He keeps us and protects us and supports us in front of our egos. Because our egos is wildest or most wild, wildest creature [that] has been created. And it has been given to sons of Adam because the ranks of sons of Adam is the highest among creatures. And, as you know kings riding on gigantic horses, heroes riding on gigantic horses, but like me, on [a] donkey, riding. Such a horse I am riding throwing me. Therefore, among creatures the most respectful creatures is sons of Adam.

And then Allah Almighty giving them such a horse, as our egos. So powerful. You're riding on it, and to go in Divinely Presence from here up to Divine Presence, it is going to be in a form, you can't imagine how it should be, our egos, when arriving there. So powerful. But, we must be very careful because nafs going to cheat us and riding on us, quickly! Making us his horse. You must ask Divinely Support, that is, most lovely characteristic that Allah Almighty likes to see it on His servants. To say: ana abdu n-ajiz, "I am Your weak servant, O my Lord, I can't do anything, I am ajiz, helpless and powerless servant" and Allah Almighty [is] going to be pleased with that servant. Quickly sending His Divinely Power, Divinely Support, "Keep him". That fasting [is] teaching us. Those good manners and giving us Divinely Support when we are intending. Therefore, in Islam intention is the most important thing. And for every action, for every worshiping intention is fard most important order from Allah Almighty. Finished! He can't move even his tongue, or his hands or feet, nothing! But only, he intended in his heart "If I am going to be healthy I am going to be my Lord's obedient servant, a humble servant".

Even can say with his tongue "I accept you oh my Lord, as my Lord". But, Allah Almighty looking to his heart and looking this coming from unbelieving into believing. Without any action, but intending! Sending His Mercy Angels to take his soul with Mercy, to Mercy Oceans. Therefore, intention is the most important order of our Lord and by intention, we are going to be on the path of prophets and awliyas. Allah Almighty gives us inspirations and corrects our intentions from badness to goodness, from unbelieving into believing.
