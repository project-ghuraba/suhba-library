A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm.

Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Innamā l-mu’minūna ikhwatun fa-aṣliḥū bayna akhawaykum, wattaqū Llāha la‘allakum turḥamūn” (49:10). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l- ‘Aẓīm. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says, Muslims are brothers. Reconcile between brothers. Let there be love between brothers. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is giving this order so that there is no resentment, so that there is no fitnah. Not many people listen to this order. Shayṭān makes fitnah among many people. When there is fitnah, there is enmity. They are bad towards each other. They do not love each other. When that happens, there is no unity. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla wants unity.

Ṭarīqah advises to be in jam’iyyah, the community; be in unity. This is the advice. This is what our ṭarīqah teaches. For Muslim brothers to be united, from wherever they may be. His pain is our pain. His joy is our joy. These are the people Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla loves. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla has mercy on them. Mercy means beauty. If AllāhNone gives mercy to people, they have won. Their lives have been saved. That is mercy. If there is no mercy, the opposite would happen. And the opposite is all kinds of torment, all kinds of trouble. When trouble happens, people live their lives badly. If they do not repent, there is danger in the end. Their īmān becomes in danger.

Therefore, shukr to AllāhNone HeNone created us Muslims. HeNone helped us on these ways. We follow ṭarīqah, sharī‘ah, the right way, the way of our ProphetNone. Those who follow this, whomever they are, are our brothers. We have nothing with them; we have no problem. On the contrary, we have more joy because they are on this way. And a lot of our sorrow is for those who have gone astray. Those who go astray from the way and lead others astray are worse. They ruin themselves even worse.

Our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says, whoever teaches a good thing – teaches means he was a means of guidance for a person – he will have a reward written for him, just like that person. If one then one; two then two; three then three; twenty then twenty; and a thousand rewards are written if [he guides] a thousand people. And the reward of those people will not decrease. It will remain the same. Some people wonder, “Will it be taken away from me because he also gained [reward]?” There is no such thing. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is generous. There is no such thing as a limit for HisNone generosity. Therefore, if they led people to do evil, the same is for them. If you lead a person astray, you will have as much sin as them. If you lead two people astray, you take [the sin] of two people. If you lead a thousand people astray... now many people think they are something. They say, “Let’s do like them.” And when they do, they will be punished. And the person who taught them will be punished in the same way. So, this way, the way of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is like this. When you do a good deed, AllāhNone gives you tenfold. In Ramaḍān, eight hundred thawāb. AllāhNone gives as much as HeNone wants. But if he commits a sin, one sin is written.

The one who leads astray takes one for each person. He will also be punished. Because he is leading people astray. If he commits a sin, it is his own sin. It is written down as one. As we said, AllāhNone multiplies the thawāb. But the sin is one. Yet if you lead people astray – if you have led ten people out of the way, don’t think that one person’s sin will be written for you. The sins of those ten will be written for you. If you lead astray a thousand, you will be written a thousand. If you lead astray a million, a million punishments will be for you. Therefore, we must be careful. This religion is not a toy. Humanity is not a toy. There is an end, an account, a book. There is paradise, there is hell. Let them make their calculations accordingly.

This month is a blessed month, the month of Ramaḍān. Let them repent and ask forgiveness from AllāhNone, they will be saved. Otherwise, they will not be saved. May AllāhNone protect us.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, AllāhNone say, in Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n saying, “Innamā l-mu’minūna ikhwatun fa-aṣliḥū bayna akhawaykum, wattaqū Llāha la‘allakum turḥamūn” (49:10). Meaning Muslims, mu’min they are brothers. You must be good to be, each to other, to be in peace and to be help for each other, not to be enemy for other. Who have some problem with other, we must make them to finish this problem. So AllāhNone be happy with you, and bless you and be mercy with you. This is what Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, saying, and the mercy it is a very important thing for us. Because AllāhNone, in first thing, saying Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm – Most Merciful. Meaning, by name of AllāhNone. So, it is very important.

Maybe every day we say, we don't know what is mercy. With mercy, you find everything good in this world. So, we are happy with people who are following this way, way of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, way of awliyā’, ṣalīhīn, way of ṣaḥābah, way of good people, which showing peace and happiness and good for other, good intention also for other people. This is, when you do this, AllāhNone happy with you. HeNone is bless you, and you be satisfied and happy. Everyone you can teach them this goodness, AllāhNone reward you also.

This is what Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying. Everyone teach somebody something good, he will be reward as this man, or anyone, they are following this. And for one man, one. If you [teach] two, two. Or ten thousand... As much people following your advice and they are happy coming to right way, AllāhNone reward you. For each one, what they have from reward, when they do, coming to way of AllāhNone, you will be reward also. But nothing coming less from their reward. This gift from AllāhNone for you. Because you make people coming, save them from bad end. But opposite also, what Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying: if you teach people bad thing, each one you teach also, you will be punished for this. If you teach one, one. If ten, ten time you will be punished.

Thousand, thousand also you will punished because you teach them this bad thing, with bad idea, bad... to make people enemy, to make them harm for people. So, you will be punished. For each one, this not only one, because when you make some good thing, AllāhNone reward you from ten, and for maybe thousand, ten thousand, AllāhNone reward you for this. But if you make bad sin, only one sin they write for you. Not saying if you do one sin you will be take ten punishments. No, only one. So, for this, for when... Don't think if you, somebody you make him to do bad thing, you teach them, it will be only for one. If thousand, you will be thousand also. For this, very important for people they are not thinking about what they will be punished, if they do something and teach people. Because many people they like, like shayṭān, to teach people to do bad thing, to harm people, to destroy people, to destroy generation. All this it will be asked, and punished for him.

So, in Ramaḍān it is very important for people to look if anything they teach wrong or to say wrong, to say to people, “I was wrong in what I said, what I done, what I do, what I teach you. It was not good. Come back to Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, ask for forgiveness. I'm asking forgiveness to not be punished, to not take it to next life.” This is very important. AllāhNone accept this. But if you don't, you will be very sorry when you are close your eyes for leaving this world. AllāhNone keep us safe from our ego and bad habit. And ask AllāhNone to not make us to do anything wrong, to teach people the wrong thing.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
