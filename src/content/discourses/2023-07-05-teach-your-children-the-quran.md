As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says, "Khayrukum man ta‘allama l-Qur’ān wa-‘allamah." The best of you is the one who learns and teaches the Qur’ān, heNone says. Now that it is summertime, children go from their schools to the mosque to learn the Qur’ān. People are sending them, but not as much as they used to before. So, today or tomorrow, with Allāh’sNone permission, they will start giving Qur’ān lessons in mosques. Even one day is better than what they study the whole year. Even one hour is better than everything else they read. Because they are reading the word of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Light descends on their hearts with that word. It becomes a means for goodness.

Everything done for the sake of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is good for people. Whatever they study, they should not forget Allāh 'Azza wa-Jalla. They should study with that intention, in order for the knowledge they acquire to be a means for goodness. Because children’s schools are not good. “We pay this much, we pay that much.” People don’t know anything about it but the money they give. “Let my son study too, let my daughter get a diploma." We don't know what they will do with the diploma. People are obsessed with the diploma. They are not thinking of anything else. They do not think about the ākhirah.

Here, Qur’ān courses have started. A few children go there, May AllāhNone be pleased with them. Even if it is for about a month, still, many shayṭāns are repelled by what they have learned. Because what they learn is the way of AllāhNone. They [mosques] teach the word of AllāhNone. But they teach the way of shayṭān, the word of shayṭāns. Of course, they don't want that. But in order to be good, it is a great necessity, a great benefit for human beings, young and old, to learn the Qur’ān.

Therefore, it is not difficult. Because sixty or seventy years ago or more, they changed the alphabet of the Qur’ān. [They said] “It is very difficult. People cannot read it.” However, everyone had been reading it. They made everyone ignorant overnight, [saying] "They cannot read Latin letters." But in reality, there is no difficulty. People have been reading it for years. They learn all kinds of languages. They learn Chinese, Japanese and I don't know what. They know everything. When it comes to the word of the Qur’ān, it is very difficult to read it. However, with the permission of AllāhNone, a person can read it in a week if he makes an effort. If he can't learn in a week, he can read in a month. Here they are teaching children to read in a month. If not one month, then in two months, five months, one year, one can easily read.

When you read, AllāhNone writes thousands of rewards for each letter; gives thousands of rewards. In Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n there are all kinds of goodness, shifā’ (healing), barakah (blessing), light and faith. Every beauty is in it. It is the word of AllāhNone. It is the most precious thing. It is the best and the most beautiful of what is given to people as a gift from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. There is nothing more beautiful than it in this world. People are not aware of it. Those who are aware are only a few. May AllāhNone increase them, in shā’a Llāh. It gives clarity of mind to those who read it.

May it be means for goodness. Because they most probably start today or tomorrow. This will be a beautiful education and training, in shā’a Llāh, for people. May it be beneficial for the children, in shā’a Llāh. May they be beneficial for this country, the Ummah and Islām, in shā’a Llāh.

Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, saying: "Khayrukum man ta‘allama l-Qur’ān wa-‘allamah." They, you are, best one who learn Qur’ān and teach Qur’ān, the best one from ummah, because it is knowledge of everything. Knowledge of AllāhNone, sending for people, and we must learn. We say this because in Turkey and here, maybe other Muslim country, I don't know, they have custom in summertime, summer holiday. They open one month's course for teaching Qur’ān for children who has holiday. Every day before ẓuhr, maybe one hour – I don't think two hours, one hour I think – they are teaching for one month for children. And children going, coming. They try to make them happy, give them some present, some sweet, some ice-cream or something, to make them happy, to run to this good place. And they teach them in mosque. This is good custom, because what one hour, one day, one month in year – this is better than whole this year, what they studying.

Because now, people, they are everywhere – Before, [in] some place they was very interested to teach them, children, in school. But now, whole world like this. Only become crazy to put their children in school, in high school, in university, just to teach them. And not, they are not asking what they are learning, what they are... what this teaching them, this school. No, just send them. For this, this one hour even, from this course, better than whole year if they are not thinking anything else [but] getting diploma from school. Not thinking [about] what they teaching them – if they teaching them good, teaching them bad, teach them good behave, teach them to be good people – No, no.

But for this, they say one hour in this Qur’ān course, better hundred times than from this school. But even in school [if] they looking what they are learning, what they teach them, that time maybe AllāhNone make them to be accepted, also. But important for this: Qur’ān in school giving every good thing. Because Qur’ān, gift from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla for whole human being. It is real treasure in our hand. This is word of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Directly coming, you hold this holy book which it is from our CreatorNone. Everything, every good thing in this, every khayr in this book. From teaching whole knowledge and giving barakah for people who reading, giving also happiness, giving also shifā’, health, wealth – every good thing in this holy book from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.

Of course before – I don't know other place, but here in our country – before, they was writing in Arabic, or similar to Arabic writing. They forbidding this after Ottoman, because they put heavy condition for Turkey: "You must prevent Islām, you must take this word from this, that, that..." But it was that time maybe obligatory for government. And they said, "It is difficult to read Arabic." But whole country was reading, writing Arabic. In one night they forbid this. So all people, they become ignorant. But now, no any problem for this. And not difficult to learn Qur’ān Arabic – writing or reading. Because children or everybody, they are learning the very difficult thing. This is very easy. Who want, interest to learn, he can learn in one week to read Qur’ān. Many people they can do this. But if not one week – in one month, easily you can read Qur’ān. Maybe not one month – two months, three months, one year, you will be exactly... know... I don't think anyone, in one year he cannot read. If he cannot read, this is some very rare thing, case. But normally, it is very easy to learn Arabic... Qur’ān.

So we must learn this, in shā’a Llāh – teach our children also. Because in each word, AllāhNone give reward, which, as Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying: "One word, one letter – letter it is not only alif, like alif lām mīm – One alif, one lām, one mīm, one... this, each one they are have reward." Not only one – each letter it has a reward from AllāhNone. So when you read Qur’ān, AllāhNone send bless and light, nūr, in your heart. It is real treasure for mu’min, for Muslim.

And, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, this good occasion for children to go and learn, because not too heavy. They give them very nice teaching. No one make them angry or nobody... [They] treat them very well. AllāhNone make us to be from this Ahlu l-Qur’ān (people of Qur’ān), in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
