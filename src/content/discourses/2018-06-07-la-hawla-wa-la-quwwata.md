As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa raḥmatu Llāhi wa barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīn wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

Thanks to AllāhNone we have been created Muslim. Those who have become Muslim are also many. We need to be grateful for this. Because īmān and Islām are a great favour. People who have attained them have won and gained all that is good. There are many things in Islām that non-Muslims cannot comprehend. They look at it from the outside and move on – how these people live, do things, and so on. They do not care much because these are things that will not fit their ego. There are things they need to do and things they should not do. It is not important. What is important is that the beauties that are in Islām are countless.

Our Holy ProphetNone said to a Ṣaḥābah Kirām, "Say: Lā ḥawla wa lā quwwata illā biLlāh. Whatever amount of troubles you may have they will go away." This does not exist in another religion. It does not exist anywhere else. It is only in Islām. You do some things that are things AllāhNone wants and HeNone gives you rewards in both dunyā and Ākhirah. Our Holy ProphetNone again states: "Lā ḥawla wa lā quwwata illā biLlāh is a remedy for 100 troubles." The lightest is sadness, meaning stress. Now, the most common big problem of people is stress. If you say, "Lā ḥawla wa lā quwwata illā biLlāh," then both trouble and stress will go away. AllāhNone also gives you rewards. HeNone rewards you in the Hereafter and you will be comfortable in dunyā too. "Lā ḥawla wa lā quwwata illā biLlāh is a tree in paradise," says our Holy ProphetNone. Say it often. Let those beautiful trees and saplings of yours increase in number.

A Ṣaḥābah said that he was in distress. He said they had captured him. Let those who are imprisoned repeat this lā ḥawla wa lā quwwata illā biLlāhi l-‘alayyi l-‘aẓīm because when they say it removes that distress. The Ṣaḥābah tells us, "They had solidly tied my hands and feet and taken me prisoner. They were keeping me somewhere. I started reciting lā ḥawla wa lā quwwata illā biLlāh. My hands and feet started to loosen. Then I got out. There were camels of those who had taken me prisoner. I took them and safely reached my country.

Now there are Muslims who are in distress everywhere in the world. We hear about some of them. And some others, most of them, we do not hear about their distress. Let them, in shā’a Llāh, repeat the "Lā ḥawla" so that distress will go. Everything is in the hands of AllāhNone. AllāhNone causes a reason and the torture inflicted and the harm is removed. Those who inflicted it will get punishment, AllāhNone willing.

Because power, everything is in the hands of AllāhNone. Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla is making people repeat it so they acknowledge it. Let them constantly realise that they have nothing. Everything is from AllāhNone. Allāh'sNone help reaches when you acknowledge it. If not, if you think the karāmah is from yourself, then you are useless. AllāhNone will disgrace you. May AllāhNone protect us from giving value and credit to our ego. Power and everything else are in the hands of AllāhNone.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh we are, some of us, born Muslim, believer, mu’min. Some of them, AllāhNone also grant for them īmān, Islām and they become Muslim and we are sitting here together. Because this religion of AllāhNone, Islām, it is real very strange thing. You cannot find another thing in another religion like this because AllāhNone give for Islām barakah; give them something to do and when they are doing, AllāhNone make them more stronger, more happier, more no need for anybody else than AllāhNone. Of course there is praying, fasting, making ṣadaqah and there is also some dhikr.

ProphetNone saying for one ṣaḥābah, "Say: Lā ḥawla wa lā quwwata illā biLlāhi l-‘alayyi l-‘aẓīm. AllāhNone make your life easier. It is like small tree you can plant in Paradise." Every time you say lā ḥawla wa lā quwwata illā biLlāhi l-‘alayyi l-‘aẓīm AllāhNone make, give you one tree in your Paradise and it will be every day bigger and bigger. And it is also to say lā ḥawla wa lā quwwata illā biLlāhi l-‘alayyi l-‘aẓīm cure for hundred illness – the easiest one, and it is more, the more difficult for human being now, sadness, stress, unhappiness. And this the the smallest one ProphetNone saying. So in, when we are in sadness or stress you can say lā ḥawla wa lā quwwata illā biLlāhi l-‘alayyi l-‘aẓīm and it will go and AllāhNone He will reward you in Ākhirah. Also here in this world you will be happy and Ākhirah also you will be reward for this.

And it is opening for who are in like captured or in prison or another place, who are like this people – nowadays many people they are suffering from non-believer. They are oppressing, oppressing for Muslim. We are here very small but there is very big oppression against people and to say lā ḥawla wa lā quwwata illā biLlāh taking this. One ṣaḥābah he said, "I hear this and I was captured by enemy. My hand and feet it was tied by rope and I was lying down. And by beginning to say this lā ḥawla wa lā quwwata illā biLlāh and it is broken this rope from my hand and my feet. After this I went out and it was nighttime I find their camels. So I take all their camels and bring them to my place." This is barakah, miracle of lā ḥawla wa lā quwwata illā biLlāhi l-‘alayyi l-‘aẓīm.

Nowadays really there is people suffering. And nobody can open mouth against this people because every time people, if they say something they become more difficult for this people – they making more oppression for people, this people. So this people and, in shā’a Llāh, we also saying for them, in behind of them, lā ḥawla wa lā quwwata illā biLlāhi l-‘alayyi l-‘aẓīm. AllāhNone open for them because they cannot say. Forbidden to say lā ilāha illā Llāh Muḥammadun Rasūlu Llāh. Forbidden to marry with Islāmic. Forbidden to make janāzah. Forbidden to wash body of dead people. This is real very bad and it is for millions of people they doing this. So, in shā’a Llāh, we say lā ḥawla wa lā quwwata illā biLlāhi l-‘alayyi l-‘aẓīm.

We cannot say, we cannot have anything to do, only this and it is enough because everything from AllāhNone. And miracle – we mustn't say this is from ourself. Everything from AllāhNone. If anybody he believe miracle come from him, it is no miracle. Nothing for him. It is his empty. Only from AllāhNone. If AllāhNone want, HeNone can do everything. AllāhNone help us and help this people, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
