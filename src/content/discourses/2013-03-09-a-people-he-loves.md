Welcome O beloved ones. Beloved ones! Welcome Shaykh Adnan Effendi. You have honoured us.

S.A.: We have been honoured.

M: May Allah (swt) increase you in Heavenly Lights, happiness & welcome in this world & in the Hereafter. May the angels come to you saying "salamun aleikum for what you have been patient with." Our Lord (jj) created us, granted us our provision, honoured us & bestowed His generosity on us. Welcome to you.

Every matter that does not start with Bismillah is cut off. It is cut off. Without Bismillah it is cut off. It is cut off.... All that shaytan wants is that people forget to say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. They forgot Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim and they fell down. They have no more value nor the honour that comes to them from Heavens. Who does not say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim no honour comes on them from Heavens nor...nothing. He is left with nothing. People...Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Every matter that does not begin with Bismillah is cut off. People run in the streets screaming & shouting. They do not think of saying "Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim." So the angels come down on them.

They forgot. shaytan & his followers they stir them up. They stir them up and they shout like dogs, hasha! Like beasts. Is this the honour of Islam? "They have forgotten Allah so He has forgotten them" (9:67) Adnan Effendi. That's it. "Nasu Llaha..." They took for a Lord other than Allah (swt). Other than Allah (swt) who can claim to be a god for them? iblis, shaytan? "I will lead you to a life that you do not know." And all the teachings of shaytan people learned and taught it to their children and they left the teachings of Islam. They have no fortune, they can never have it. They cannot be saved nor have success. They will not succeed, all of them will fail.

All the world now became soldiers for iblis. They put all their efforts to follow the steps of shaytan. "And follow not the footsteps of shaitan" (2:208) Allah (swt) ordered it. People, step by step, put all their efforts into following shaytan and his followers of all kinds. "Fasawfa Ya'ti Llahu Biqawmin Yuhibbuhum Wa Yuhibbunahu "(5:54) Allahu Akbar Sadaqa Llahu l 'Azim SubhanaHu wa Ta'ala. Allah (swt) will not leave them (the tyrants), He does not want to leave them. "Allah will bring a people whom He will love and they will love Him."(5:54) The Muslim world has fallen to the ground. Allah (swt) sent from the end of the world a tribe coming from Khorasan. They came from Khorasan. Allah Allah! Their Sultan when he was bathing, he drowned. He drowned in the river Euphrates (Iraq). His son took his throne, a small tribe. He was directed by our Sultan Al Anbiya' (saws).

He did not go to East, the East side of the world, but he went to the West. Because the worst enemies of Islam are those people who claim to be the people of the Book. I asked one of them, one of their knowledgeable ones, "how is your religion?" "Our religion, we believe in the father, in the son, & in the Holy Spirit." Is it not so?

S.A.: This is what they believe in.

M: Three deities. They know also, in Egypt. How do you see? You believe that there is the father, on his right hand is his son, and on his left hand is the Holy Spirit. Yes? And then, what do you think in your faith or your way of thinking, is there one who is the Lord of the worlds? You say that there is the father, the son & the Holy Spirit. How did the order come, the father sends his son to to be crucified? He sends his son to be crucified? What do you think of this? How is God sending his son to the world to be crucified? Is this how your mind works? (spit) on your way of thinking! Which father with all his powers will send his son to his enemies so they crucify him? And Allah (swt) says "but they killed him not, nor crucified him, but it seemed to them like that."(4:157)

This religion, the religion of Christianity, this religion from its roots is false, broken. And all their efforts are in... showing, showing off, showing off their religion with buildings, astonishing buildings, saying "look this is our religion." Churches, Cathedrals... I swore on the one who founded the Vatican. For what reason? The Prophet Isa (as) came from Bayt Lahm (Bethlehem). You moved its center to the Vatican and you placed a Holy one there. Who gave him holiness? Who gave him holiness? Who made them holy? They sanctified one another. And therefore the Western people, the Christian world, from their extreme envy and stubbornness stood up against Islam until now. Until now, they do not consider Islam a religion of Allah (swt). Why?

You say that star, the North pole star, you claim there is something there. In front of you is the full moon, and you do not believe what is apparent on the full moon but you believe what is on the pole star. You have no logic, no mind nor balance. And therefore they did not spread except by their enmity to Islam. They stood up against Islam. But "Allah will protect you from people" (5:67) Allah (swt) informed His Beloved "don't fear. Jibril (as) will come." While the Prophet (saws) was in the cave of Thawr with S. Abu Bakr (as) on the Hijra, the Prophet (saws)told his friend "Don't fear Allah is with us." Rasul Al Qibriya' (saws) said this. His sword reaches from East to West.

Now shaytan taught them... Their teacher is the master shaytan. All that they call technology – meaning "knowledge inside the boat" (joke) nothing more. These are their ideas. Until now this is their idea, to remove Islam from the face of the earth. I said to them " you can only lick your hand, or under you." Tamam Shaykh al Islam! I get tired...especially the attenders.

Allah (swt) inspires wisdom on the tongue of the speakers according to the listener's capacity. And all of you are of high aspiration Especially Adnan Effendi is a hose that can swallow all the seas.... And it was thrown to my being a few words. I do not know but they are inspired to me. I say "my arabic is little" and the companions (ra) are laughing. The end of times no one is left to swear at the people, I am enough! And they laugh. Let the Shaykh swear, and I do! S. Adnan came & I am keeping the highest adab. He is a strong one. In this time it is enough for tonight, I got tired.

S.A. We were present in your company while visiting S. Rasulullah(saws) & S. Sayed Al Mu'minin Ya S.Omar Ibn Al Khattab, but instead you called him S.Omar ibn Al Hattab so he laughed & also the Prophet (saws).

M. Hattab, Khattab all the same! The letter H or Kh... Khattab! Yes They are laughing, the Shaykh is angry but they are still laughing.

Fatiha.
