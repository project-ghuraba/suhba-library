As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Shukr to Allah that He created us as Muslims. All good things come from this blessing, the blessing given by Allah. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla’s generosity is infinite, without end. He also bestowed upon us acts of worship as a further gift. These acts of worship have their designated times. In this month of Ramaḍān, the worship is fasting, of course. Fasting and Tarawīh prayers. Khatms of the Qur’ān. Zakāt can be given at any time, but what is given in this month has even greater reward. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says it is more than 700 times. “It is My month. No one can question what I do,” says Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. This is Allah’s wisdom. In this blessed month of Ramaḍān, all kinds of good deeds, charity, and worship performed, Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says, “The reckoning is with Me. It is more than seven hundred times.”

His generosity is great. We are also in need. We are in need of His generosity. The more He gives us, the more we will ask for, inshā’Allah. May He give, not diminish, but increase, inshā’Allah. May our good deeds, charity, and īmān increase. When you give, Allah gives. Therefore, there’s no need to hesitate. Some people are hesitant. Don’t hesitate. Ask from Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla, “Give me, O Allah.” As we said, let’s try to do all kinds of help and good deeds this month.

As we said, this is a blessed month. May Allah be pleased with all of you. Of course, fasting during the day, night worship, Tarawīh prayers, even iftār (breaking the fast), has its reward in the sight of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla, and there is also the reward of sahūr. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla gives us all kinds of opportunities, saying, “Win. Take. I give, you take. You are in need. Ask from Me, and take.”

May Allah keep it perpetual upon us and strengthen our īmān. May this blessed month be of goodness and barakah, inshā’Allah. May it be of benefit for people. May it be guidance for those who have gone astray bringing them back to the right way. May they also experience and taste this goodness, inshā’Allah.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
