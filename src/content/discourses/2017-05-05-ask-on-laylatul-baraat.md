As-salāmu ‘alaykum wa raḥmatu Llāhi wa barakātuh. A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

May this Jum‘ah be blessed. It is Jum‘ah of Sha‘bān, the holy month. In shā’a Llāh next week we will reach the day and night of the 15th of Sha‘bān, in shā’a Llāh. It is revealed from our ProphetNone that the 15th of Sha‘bān is holy and it is revealed in Qur’ān by Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla. HeNone says that on that night it is written what everyone will do, where they'll come and go for the upcoming year. That night is a holy night. Our ProphetNone used to pray till the morning on those nights, especially on the 15th night. Ḥaḍrati ‘Ā’ishah said she heard prayers she never heard before until the morning. And Ḥadrati ‘Ā’ishah asked our ProphetNone "Why do you pray so much on this night?" "This night is the night when everything for the coming year is written. I want to worship and stay awake praying during this night", said our ProphetNone.

We can't interfere with AllāhNone. How does this happen? Does it change or not what AllāhNone has written? He orders us to pray. The book in which there is everything written for everyone is in the presence of AllāhNone. Even if HeNone writes whatever He wishes, "Yamḥū l-lahu mā yashāu wa yuth'bit." (13:39) Even if anything HeNone wants is written, HeNone can erase and rewrite it. Or HeNone will make do with what is written. And the minds of people are capable up to a limit. Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla - the whole universe, everything we see in it, is smaller than an atom of His knowledge. All kinds of knowledges are nothing next to the greatness of AllāhNone.

Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla with all His greatness is granting us these nights. "You pray. INone will give you what you are asking for. INone will forgive you. INone will raise you up. INone will increase your rizq (provision). INone will cure you". Everything is in the hands of AllāhNone. He orders to pray till the morning. "INone will accept it. I will accept everyone's prayers and forgive everyone who is not making shirk, not drinking alcohol, not doing adultery." And if they repent for their sins, Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla will forgive them. But if they object, they are preparing their own punishment. Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla Arḥamu r-Rāḥimīn - His mercy is endless. Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla has mercy oceans. Whatever you do, AllāhNone still forgives. But if you insist on disobeying HimNone, you will have made your own punishment. Therefore, we should be careful. We have to think why we came to this world. Did we come for dunyā? If we came for dunyā, this suffering wouldn't be necessary. Just like AllāhNone created animals, we'd live and go like them. So HeNone created us for something. HeNone favoured us. He sent us the prophets so that they could show us the right way by reason. Don't leave the right way. When you are on the right way, some shayṭāns may bother you. You think you're on the right way, and even when you're on the right way, he (shaytṭān) can push you to evil. Therefore, we should be careful.

On this night, we should beg AllāhNone with sincerity, as we are weak servants, to not leave us to our egos. Don't let us act as we like. May we be on this way only for Your pleasure - we should beg for this. And the Islamic world is in a bad condition. A Muslim is a brother to other Muslims, says our ProphetNone. Muslims are like one body. If any part of the body has pain, not only does that part hurt, but whole body will feel it. Even if a finger nail is hurt, the whole body will feel that pain. And we as Muslims should pray for Muslims in this world, for this oppression to end because we can't do anything else. But praying to AllāhNone is our biggest weapon.

May AllāhNone punish those who are oppressing these people. May HeNone trouble them so that they are busy with their own troubles and leave Muslims alone. May we pray that HeNone sends us Imam Mahdi ‘alayhi s-salām. This is the biggest prayer we should make on the 15th night of Sha‘bān. The one who will show us the way of AllāhNone, the one who will raise Islām is Mahdi ‘alayhi s-salām. May we reach him. We pray that he comes as soon as possible. Then you can pray and ask whatever you want. Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla loves the persistent ones, when you ask for one thing for a hundred times. If you tell someone something several times, he'll say "Enough. You are too annoying". But Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla always wants His servant to be with HimNone, to beg HimNone. If one asks for a hundred or a thousand times, AllāhNone loves such a person. Therefore, its wisdom is to not go and beg other people. Beg AllāhNone. AllāhNone will certainly give.

But people have no patience. People are without patience. If HeNone doesn't give now, He'll give later. If HeNone doesn't give later, He'll give even later. But HeNone will certainly give. Then a person will realize it happened but he'll have had discomfort from lack of patience. Therefore, these nights are holy nights. They are a gift from Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla. We should respect them. We read Yāsīn 3 times between maghrib and ʿishāʾ. A Yāsīn will be read for health, rizq and long life. Then we will pray 100 rakaʿāt prayers. It is sunnah nafl. If you do it and make duʿāʾ, AllāhNone will grant you 70 wishes, says our ProphetNone. The least is the forgiveness of sins. Forgiving sins is not easy. People, all of us have sins. We don't feel the heaviness of sins. But when it's released, a man feels better and lighter. Even if he doesn't feel it, when sins are forgiven, he worships better and he lives better.

May AllāhNone bless these nights. May we spend them next year with Mahdi ‘alayhi s-salām in shā’a Llāh. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh we are in Sha‘bān-i mu‘aẓẓam. The month of ProphetNone, month of light of ProphetNone, month of Laylatu l-Barā’at. This is night of what will be for next year for everybody - not only whole together, everybody. AllāhNone make program for everybody from 15th Sha‘bān to next 15th Sha‘bān. In this they write, Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla write what will happen - you be married, you be travel, you be go to ‘Umrah to Ḥajj. Who will be pregnant, who will be given birth. Who will die, who will maybe something happen for him. All this from 15th Sha‘bān to another 15th Sha‘bān AllāhNone write in the Ummu l-Kitāb, the mother of the book, that book for what write for human being. This is AllāhNone greatness. Allāhu Akbaru l-Akbar.

His knowledge it is what whole this universe they have from knowledge, from think people, they are even for this not maybe one atom they are not finding even with this AllāhNone knowledge you cannot compare at all. Whole this universe not even like one dust. And HeNone is with His Mightiness saying, looking for the believer people and told them to ask what they want to ask. HeNone said The writing, what I write it must be done, but also HeNone said everything in His hand and HeNone can change "Yamḥū l-lahu mā yashāu wa yuth'bit." (13:39) HeNone can cancel and can make the new one. For this, for giving this idea for believer and saying be careful, ask what you want from me this night. It is written there is, but INone can cancel and write a new thing.

When people they ask, "How they can...?" We are praying for this and you know every Sha‘bān we do this, in 15th of Sha‘bān.

After maghrib, between maghrib, ‘ishā’ we read Yāsin sharīf three times. And after ‘ishā’ until morning you can pray hundred rak‘at nāfilat for 15 of Sha‘bān. And ProphetNone saying, who pray this AllāhNone give him seventy thing what he want he ask for this. The lowest one AllāhNone forgive your sins, all your sins.
