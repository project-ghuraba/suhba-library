La ilaha illAllah, la ilaha illAllah, la ilaha illAllah Muhammadu r-Rasulullah, alaihi s-salatullah.

We are living in paradise, still, still complaining, still not happy. O our Lord, our ego is never content, does not get satisfied or pleased. Moses used to speak with Allah, ala nabiyyina alaihi s-salatu wa s-salam, When he was speaking to the Lord Almighty on Mount Sinai he said "O my Lord, when will you be pleased with me? How will you be pleased? What should I do to make You pleased?" "O Moses! It is easy. Whenever you are pleased with Me, I'm pleased with you." Allahu akbar. Are you listening to this example? "Whenever you are pleased with Me I'm pleased with you." Is there anyone not pleased with His Lord?

Alhamdulillah our stomachs are full, we are safe, everything is fine. We are healthy, our business is good. Of course we will say that we are pleased because we have everything we want. But there are as many troubles, difficulties, as the number of people in this world. Everyone has a different kind of difficulty. When Allah Almighty is testing his servant with a trial it is important to be pleased. Otherwise, as we said, in comfort, in prosperity, in health you cannot find someone who says "I'm not pleased". Everybody is pleased. Either Muslim or non-Muslim, they are all pleased in such a condition.

But important is when you are in difficulty to praise (Hamd), praise to Allah. Look, that station, station of ridha (contentment) is the highest station. There is no other station beyond that. There are stations of contentment (ridwan) in Heavens. The ones, who are pleased with their Lord in any circumstance they will enter those stations. They are called the Ridwan Heavens. If the others try to get close, they will make them turn back, saying - "This heaven does not belong to you." Mustafa Effendi asks from which door do they enter? From which door will they enter the Heaven of Ridwan (contentment)? You will enter through the door of patience. Who is not patient, cannot find the Ridwan. If you are out of patience, you cannot enter from that door. You cannot be out of patience.

Patience is endless but of course we are weak. Even we are weak, do not think that we are carrying a heavy burden and that it is not heavy on Anbiya, Awliya. No. What comes on the Prophets - if you divide what comes on only one prophet among the whole people of this world, they cannot carry it. The burden on the Anbiya, Awliya is heavier than on anybody else. What is coming on us is according to our situation because with His Divine Justice Allah does not burden anyone beyond what they can bear. Hasha. When we say "I cannot bear it" it is a fault on our side. Because even if you cannot bear it at that moment you need to turn towards your Lord. If you go to His door and say "I can not bear it, I want more power" Divine Support reaches you that time. But if you are not determined to carry it then, you cannot receive His blessings.

A Prophet, ala Nabiyyina alaihi s-salatu wa s-salam, had a dream - be careful with this one, it is an important matter. He had a dream, their dreams are divine revelation. It is not like our dream, it is Divine revelation. In his dream, he is ordered to swallow the first thing he sees the next morning. The next day he went out and the first thing he saw was a big, black mountain. "A massive mountain came out while I was walking" he said. There is an order to him to swallow whatever comes in his way first. Ok. Without saying "This is a mountain. How shall I swallow it? How is this? " he walked towards the www.saltanat.org Page 1 mountain intending to swallow it. But the mountain was so huge, he is so small in front of it. He said "I shall swallow it" and he walked. As he came closer, it got smaller. As he walked towards it, it got smaller, smaller. When he reached it, it was like a small bite. He found one bite and he put it in his mouth. He never tasted anything sweeter. He swallowed it.

This means that determination, "fa idha 'azamta fatawakkal"(When you are determined, rely on Allah)(3:159) This is the order, divine declaration of Allah Almighty to His Prophet (sas). Consult with your companions. When you start to do something, consult, then whatever your decision is walk towards it with determination and rely upon Allah. When you are determined, the mountain can be transformed into a bite. Can a human being bring down a mountain? Ferhat drilled the mountain. There is a lesson in every story no doubt. It is a story but full of lessons. Ferhat drilled the mountain and found a water source. He reached the source of Water of Life and his goal. So there is no unreachable goal for someone who is determined. He can reach any goal. He can reach any goal.

So nobody should say "I can't bear it, I can't do it." Yes, you are alone. Maybe you can't do it on your own, the human being is weak. But when you ask support from the Lord Almighty, when support starts coming from Allah Almighty, then you are able to overcome anything. So, what we have to practice first is knowing how to ask the support of Allah Almighty. Allah Almighty's support does not come by standing there and calling. There is a castle. That castle has a door. You will enter through that door. If you can't find the door, you cannot enter. There is a way, method for everything in this dunya life. They call it 'a solution'. There is a solution for every difficulty. He gave a cure for every illness; there is a key to open every difficulty. He sent a method to solve all problems and difficulties. He gave a key to open every door. Allah Almighty has arranged it like this.

You need to know the method. Allah's support, Allah's guidance, does not come without asking. It comes if it is asked for. If we do not install those pipes here, do we have water at home? Use your brain. Allah Almighty made the Prophets as fountains of divine support. After them He made the awliyas as the fountains of life and divine support. He tells us to take from them. So, when He asks "Did you take his hand?" it means - have you found the one, who will make you reach support? Is it working with a battery? Your battery will die. A tool with a battery works until it dies. When it is finished, throw it away. But if you plug it into a real source, it will never finish, it is endless.

There are awliyas in every time period. Allah Almighty does not put them there for decoration. Because they are the most valuable ones He hides them. They are covered, hidden. They are hidden, under Allah Almighty's protection. "I'm hiding My awliyas under the domes of My heavens" He says. Why? If He should make it obvious, and people deny the awliyas, they will be deprived of all their goodness. Allah Almighty is hiding them. For this reason it is also a blessing. Whoever needs awliya, search for them. If you don't need them, you won't search.

Look there is a street in London, a district, on that street there are the most famous doctors in the world - the best specialists, doctors for all kinds of diseases. Their offices are located on that street. Their patients come from the East, the West, the South, the North, from Arabia, Turkey, India, China. They all find their doors. That means, if somebody is searching for his health, if he has ability he will go all the way there to find them. If you also need to find the awliya you'll find them. If you have nothing to do with them, why would you find them? He will say "He is an ordinary man like me, like you". What did those idiots say for the Prophets - "They eat and drink like us, go to the market, walk the streets? What prophets are they?" they said. The degree of ignorance!

www.saltanat.org Page 2 Now this is a wire and this is also a wire. They are both wires in appearance. If I say this wire has electricity, cannot be touched. This is an ordinary wire. This is also the same type, this is copper wire, but it has electricity in it. If someone says "I don't believe in such things. There is no difference in appearance. Is there any difference between this wire and the wire with electricity? They both look the same." Oh is that so? This and that are the same? Give me your hand? Touch it. He jumps from here up to there. Then he says "Now I see." Both are wires but one has a secret. People with their physical eyes thought the Prophets were like them. ─idiocy, ignorance!

Still there are people in the Muslim World, who think awliyas are ordinary people. Awliya have a secret. He reached to the secret. Do not say "he is like me and you". He eats, drinks, sleeps, lives a normal life because he has a physical body. But his secret is with Allah Almighty, with Rasulullah, sallAllahu alaihi wa sallam. He is in the assembly of anbiya and awliya. Don't see him like yourself.. When you see him like that you can't get any benefit. Leave him space. Say "This one is beyond me, he is a holy man" so that his secret flows into you. Grand Shaykh used to ask: "Does water flow upwards?" Water doesn't stay on hills, it flows into the plains. It means - if you are humble that secret of that awliya will flow into your heart. In this time the believers, let's say Muslims, they are heedless, they don't search. They think they go to the mosque, pray, go to work, again pray in the mosque, come home, listen to preaching in the mosque - they think all is well, we've done everything. They do not look for inspiration fountains. If Muslims looked for inspiration fountains they would shake these governments. There is power, spiritual power. Those awliyas with spiritual power are hidden. Now, because people don't search them, they are hidden. If they searched and asked, they would give. They are not stingy, they are ready to give if somebody asks.

May Allah keep us with them. If our hearts are with them always, we shall find them physically inshaAllah. May Allah Almighty make to us meet them.

Bi hurmati l-Habib, bi hurmati l-Fatiha.
