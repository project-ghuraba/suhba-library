Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

A’ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm:

ا ر دْ َق ءٍ ي ْ شَ ل ِ كُ لِ ُاللََّّ لَ َعجَ دْ َق ۚ هِ رِ مْ َ أ غُ لِ اَب َاللََّّ نَّ ِ إ ۚ ُهُبسْ حَ و َ هُ َف ِاللََّّ ىَلعَ لْ كَّ و َ َتَي نْ مَ و َ

(Qur’ān 65:03). Allah states in the Holy Quran: ‘And whoever relies upon Allah - then He is sufficient for him. Indeed, Allah will accomplish His purpose. Allah has already set for everything a [decreed] extent.’ When people don’t rely on Allah, they panic and wonder what to do. There’s no need to be surprised or worried. What Allah says will happen and whoever is with Allah will win. Those who are against Allah will lose. Even if the whole world was against Allah, they cannot make even a little progress or win. Not just the whole world but even if the whole universe is against Allah, they still cannot do anything. If they are with Allah, even if they cannot do anything, they are still in gain and in goodness.

Therefore, the state of the world today is ruining people’s morale. Sometimes they lose hope in Allah, but Allah says to not lose hope in Him. Allah is with you. And as long as He’s with you, you are in gain in every situation. As long as you have Iman and you trust in Allah, He will help you. People these days, Muslims and non-Muslims, are losing their morale. Whereas Muslims should have a higher morale. Allah exists so there’s no fear for those who are with Allah. Allah is The Everlasting, so whoever is with Allah is always in gain.

May Allah give us strong Iman. May Allah protect us from such states. The hopeless ones are the Kafirs, those who don’t believe in Allah. Allah told us that we’re not going to stay in this world forever. Everyone is leaving to the hereafter; that’s where people will go. May Allah have mercy on those who’ve passed away and give the rest of us Iman and safety insha’Allah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
