A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Shukr to AllāhNone after a year apart, we meet once again. There are some brothers who departed from this world, may AllāhNone bless their souls. We meet each other here now and we will meet them in Ākhirah, in shā’a Llāh. Because dunyā, as we said sometimes people cannot make it until next year, but shukr to AllāhNone. Some stupid people say they go for eternal rest when people die. It is not eternal rest, eternal is afterwards. There is eternal life not rest. And then there is good life. That is important.

People are mixing up in their heads. They say whatever they want, thinking or without thinking. It is even worse when they say like that by thinking, it is faithlessness then. Faithful people know there is Ākhirah. Who are Muslim will meet there, in shā’a Llāh. Who are not Muslim should think for themselves. They will have hard time. Therefore, may AllāhNone let us be in Ākhirah like we are in dunyā. Of course, we meet in dunyā after a year, sometimes after a month, a couple of months, in five years, sometimes longer or sooner. We meet then but what is important – in Ākhirah it happens forever. Ākhirah is without death. It is eternal.

Now they are messing with people's faith. Can it be eternal or not? It can be. Allāh's, ‘Azza wa-Jalla, promise to us is true. As a big favour, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is giving us this beautiful life. We should be thankful for it. We will do everything we can in order to earn HisNone pleasure. In fact, that is not hard. Because life is sometimes hard on people anyway, no matter if they do goodness or not. But it is hard on those who don't have faith. The believer knows everything is from AllāhNone. Whatever comes from AllāhNone is nice, say the Awliyā’. They are pleased and at peace. "It came from AllāhNone. This man did this to me. That matter went wrong like that. That problem is given as a test by AllāhNone in order to increase our rank." If you believe like this, that matter will become easy and not hard.

But a man without faith and belief just curses and swears. He swears at destiny and people and curses everything. And a man with faith, as we said, sees life as a place of test. Who pass this test are believers. Non-believers fail the test and fail the course. And it is not easy to fail this course. They will have a hard time in Ākhirah. Those who don't obsess over small things in dunyā, those who say it is from AllāhNone, will pass with Allāh'sNone help and favour. They will deserve Allāh'sNone kindness. May AllāhNone give good sustenance to all of us. We are living at the end of times now. Hard is hard. But keep in mind that everything is from AllāhNone. As our ProphetNone says, even if a thorn pricks a believer's hand, its reward won't be lost with AllāhNone. Everyone has problems. Keep in your mind that you will be rewarded for this hardship. We will be rewarded for these problems. Thawāb will be given. You will be at peace. And others will be in comfort. And teach this to your children so that they don't get depressed from hardships and feel bad.

May AllāhNone protect us. May AllāhNone not give us a test we cannot pass, in shā’a Llāh. May AllāhNone treat us with kindness as we are weak servants. No matter how much we say, when hardship comes, people forget. May AllāhNone help us.

There are such and such illnesses in this world. Don't pay attention to them. What AllāhNone says happens. It is a punishment from AllāhNone. There are those who deserve it. It comes for them. May AllāhNone protect Muslims, in shā’a Llāh. May AllāhNone protect and give us the taste of faith. May our faith be strengthened, in shā’a Llah.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
