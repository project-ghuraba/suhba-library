As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says, one of the signs of the end of times is, “I’jābu kulli dhī ra’yin bi-ra’yih.” Everyone likes their own opinions and dislikes others’. This is the time we live in. Everyone likes something, “This is what I think. It’s like this according to me.” And doesn’t like someone else’s [opinion]. He doesn’t accept it or does the opposite. This doesn’t benefit him. Because a person must seek the truth. He must accept the truth wherever it is. Not everything happens as he wishes. Everything is done as Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla destined. If you object to everything, you will have objected to the will of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla this time.

Most people sometimes say, “Why is this oppression happening in the world? Why doesn’t AllāhNone interfere?” This is another thing that must not be said. For a person to be so disrespectful to the power and majesty of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, is due to a lack of intellect; nothing else. Interfering in the work of Allāh‘Azza wa-Jalla is not the act of a person with intellect. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla does as HeNone wants. HeNone creates as HeNone wants. Let alone the works of AllāhNone, if you say to a superior person, “Why are you doing this? What are you doing?” there are many things you know nothing about. He knows them; you don’t know. If you don’t know, when you object, it doesn’t do you any good. Even most of the time, if a person can see his own shortcomings, he will see them. If he can’t see them, it’s the same: he stubbornly goes his own way. In the end, he leaves this world without any benefit.

That’s why, you must accept the truth and what is right. Accepting evil does not benefit but harms. Even if it doesn’t harm, a person would’ve done a useless and unnecessary act.

Therefore, this is one of the signs of the end of times. Everyone acts according to their own minds. They say “democracy”; they deceive the people. They disrupt the work to be done. They prohibit the good things. They accept the bad things. These are big signs that this time is the end of times. After the end times will be the Qiyāmah. So, the Day of Judgment is near. The state of this world is not getting better. Day by day, it is getting worse. Therefore, a person must submit to AllāhNone. He must accept the truth so that he can be saved from this. In order to be free from being a slave to his ego, he must accept the truth and then his ego will accept it. Then, he will become a good Muslim. May AllāhNone make us all among them, in shā’a Llāh.

Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying about this end of time, there is sign of this to be everyone, everybody to be happy with his, what he has idea, what he is thinking. He is happy with this. Only his idea, he thought perfect, good idea, so he not accept anyone who say to him good thing. Not accept. For bad thing, maybe he accept, but to be, accept another, to be happy with another idea, it is not. This is for everybody Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, which it is not good thing. They are objecting for everything, for anything. Even there is many people they're saying, “Why Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla accepting this oppression for people, for human?” This is the most ridiculous thing can anybody saying, for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Who object for this, he must be, he is not well in his mentality. He must be crazy, mad. This is because you are...

You cannot say for your boss even, "Why you do this?" and you say for the Creator of whole universe, whole existence, you, “Why HeNone doing this, why HeNone do that?” This is beginning from small, small, coming, and to this level. To not accept, only he happy. Say, “If you... Must be shayṭān cursed, shayṭān defeated. Shayṭān people, they must taken away and must be all this okay, in world.” And this is first thing for this, who thinking like this: here [is] not paradise. Here, place for test, and you must be accept reality, truth. Not: what you like, you accept, what you not like, you not accept. You must accept what AllāhNone like, what Prophet ṣallá Llāhu‘alayhi wa-sallam like and tell us. And after this, other thing, maybe you can object, maybe you not happy. But for this, you must be accept. And for other thing also, to make your ego down. Don't think everything you know, it is right. Many thing, it is wrong. Many people, whole life they are going after something. After, they discover, “This, it was wrong, it was not right. So I lose my life with running after nothing.”

So, it is important to look for what is right, what is not right, what is good, what not good. Many thing harm you if you look, saying and thinking, “This good,” and you do what you think without asking or not accepting advice for people. What you are doing, it is wrong, completely wrong. No benefit, only harm for you, for your future, for your eternal life. You must be, come back to right way, right thinking. Ask for people who are knowing what it is right, what is wrong. You must be accept. This is for yourself, for benefit yourself. It must be like this.

But this is time, all time Mawlānā was cursing this democracy. This is real democracy: to not accept anything from others. Only you happy with yourself. AllāhNone keep us safe in this time, the difficult time for whole humanity. Everything going worse and worse, making people to think good, bad and bad, good. Black, white and white, black. And everything, they're making people to think opposite, good. The truth, bad. AllāhNone give hidāyah (guidance) for people, and AllāhNone save us from this fitnah, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
