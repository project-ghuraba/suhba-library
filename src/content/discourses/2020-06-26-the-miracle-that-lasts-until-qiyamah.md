Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Allah Azza wa Jalla says and shows everything in the Qur’an ‘Azimu sh-Shan. It has the knowledge of the former and the later peoples. Our Holy Prophet says: “ ُأَبنَ هِ يِف مْ كُ دَ عْ َب امَ رُ َبخَ وَ ،مْ كُ َلبْ َق ناك امَ ”, “It contains information of what has happened before you, and news of what will happen after you.” The Qur’an ‘Azimu sh-Shan informs about the events that occurred in the past and what will happen in the future. Everything is in that holy book.

Allah Azza wa Jalla gave it to our Holy Prophet as a miracle. A miracle that lasts until Qiyāmah. The miracles of our Holy Prophet are many. There are those that last until Qiyāmah, but they are unknown; they will be known later. But the main miracle that lasts until Qiyāmah and that will even be read in heaven insha’Allah, is the Qur’an ‘Azimu sh-Shan. It is the eternal word of Allah. It is the book that was recited in eternity, that was in His own knowledge and that He sent to people as a miracle to His highest and most beloved servant, our Holy Prophet. It is unreachable in every way, no one can even approach it because it has not been changed, it is the word of Allah. That word is a cure, light, and guidance insha’Allah. May its Barakah be always upon us insha’Allah.

It is recited all over the world. There is no time that it is not recited. It is recited continuously, every second and every moment. With its light, people find guidance. May Allah make it blessed for us. By the Barakah of this Friday, may we have good days insha’Allah. Friday is our holy day, and the Qur’an ‘Azimu sh-Shan is a gift for us, a blessing. May Allah grant us to understand it. “ارً يثِ كَ هِ ِب يدِ هْ َيوَ ارً يثِ كَ هِ ِب لُّ ضِ ُي”, “By it He misleads many, and many He guides thereby.” (Qur’ān 02:26). Because just as it is guidance, it can also lead to misguidance. May Allah protect us. Those who follow the path of our Holy Prophet do not go astray by the Qur’an. The Qur’an curses those who disrespect our Holy Prophet. And what they do is useless. May Allah protect us. May Allah give us all true faith.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
