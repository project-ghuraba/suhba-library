As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Wherever our Holy Prophet, ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, pointed to, the ṣahābah considered a sign and went in that direction. They brought hisNone beautiful way to those places. After the passing of our Holy ProphetNone, the ṣaḥābah brought light, guidance, knowledge, and beauty everywhere, to all humanity by the order of our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. People were living in the time of ignorance then. There were all kinds of darkness and oppression. With the light of our Holy ProphetNone, wherever they went, they brought barakah and the means for guidance. And here as well.

Our Holy ProphetNone used to call Cyprus – Jazīratu l-Khaḍrā’. Our Holy Prophet, ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, showed miracles. Prophets have miracles. They can see everything. Not only this world, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla showed the whole universe to our Holy Prophet, ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Therefore, they called this place "Jazīratu l-Khaḍrā’ " here. It means the "Green Island". It is a miracle that heNone said it. But it is something firm for believers without any doubt. And it can be a lesson for others.

Through our Holy Prophet, ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla revealed in a dream that the ṣaḥābah would conquer this island. It is a famous dream of our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam that they would bring Islām here. Hala Sultan Hazretleri was hisNone aunt. One time heNone was sleeping with hisNone head on her knee. When heNone had this dream, heNone woke up with a smile. She asked our Holy ProphetNone, "Why are you smiling?" HeNone replied, "People from my ummah were sitting on a throne in the sea. I saw ṣaḥābah sailing in the sea." "Am I going with them?" she asked. "Yes, you are with them," said our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. HeNone slept and had the same dream again. woke up with a smile and told her about it. Hala Sultan asked if she was with them, but heNone said, "No." This was a sign of her becoming shahīd (martyr). Our Holy ProphetNone showed as a miracle by giving her that sign.

And indeed, immediately after our Holy ProphetNone, ṣaḥābah came to this island and brought the light of Islām. And this great ṣaḥābīyah, Hala Sultan, is a blessing for this holy island. She gives goodness to everyone. Therefore, whoever visits her holy tomb, even non-Muslims, get blessings and barakah. By permission of AllāhNone... You shouldn't judge this island by those who live here. There are many martyred ṣaḥābah buried here. There are many ṣaḥābah who remained here. Therefore, it is a holy place. It was shown by our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. And most importantly, Hala Sultan is here. Barakah is coming to this island through her. There will never be harm here, in shā’a Llāh. And there is guidance for those who are here. There will either be guidance, or Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla will change and bring suitable ones. May we be among the suitable ones, in shā’a Llāh.

Everywhere that ṣaḥābah went... We went even to Samarqand, Uzbekistan where the cousin of our Holy ProphetNone Qutham ibn al-‘Abbās (ra) was the means for people to reach guidance there. He became shahīd and his holy tomb is there and there is always barakah. Therefore, that is a holy place. Even though some people don't appreciate it, those who do appreciate will benefit. Those who aren't attentive will come to know. If someone wants to discard jewels and choose dirt, it means he is insane. The whole world exists thanks to these jewels, by permission of AllāhNone.

We are living in place which Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam heNone describe and as Jazīratu l-Khaḍrā’, Green Island. And for Muslim must, when Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam aim to something, they must go and... This is a sign from Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, for to there is important thing – "You must go and bring light of Islām."

This time, time of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, whole world was in darkness and oppression for people, no any right for people. Now they say "human rights". It was only with Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam heNone give value for people, for human being. So heNone is ordering, heNone is order ṣaḥābah to go East and West, everywhere, to teach people this good manner, this the highest point of civilisation which it is honour human being and give him his right. Even for one slave, if someone doing something for him, he can ask for his right from him – anybody, even from king. This is the Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, what heNone is giving for human being.

Not like these people they said they're was going by sword to kill people and to take, conquer countries. No, with this, if you do this, you cannot. Without people who living in this country they was not happy, it will be finish quickly. But it was only for give people good thing and show them good thing. Especially for when somebody some country conquer, they [are] not force people to become Muslim. Everybody can be in his religion. So this what they said, this enemy of Islām, "Islām [is] religion of blood and religion of sword" and every bad. It is not true, because Islām only giving justice and take this bad ruler, put good ruler and everybody can be in his way. No any forcing for people to change their religion or to change their language or to... No at all.

What they do, this enemy of Islām, when they occupied Quds Jerusalem? They kill eighty thousand people in this city – Muslim, Christian, Jewish, all of them. There was no difference – old, child, woman, girl. Everything they was cutting like... And they said, "We are follower of ‘Īsá ‘alayhi s-salām, Jesus." And Jesus say, ‘alayhi s-salām, he was saying, "If somebody hit you In your right face, turn to make him to this." These people what they understand, I don't know. These all hypocrite and not good people.

But important one for this island also, when Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, heNone was sleeping in his aunt Umm Ḥarām bint Milḥān, Hala Sultan we call her, in her knees and heNone was wake up with smiling. She was happy, "What you see?" she ask. HeNone said, "I saw my Ummah by throne they going through sea, going to this island, Green Island, Jazīratu l-Khaḍrā’." And she ask, "If I, am I in this with them?" HeNone said, "Yes." After, heNone also slept, wake up also with smiling. She ask another time, "And, if am I there?" HeNone said, "No." She know she will be second time, she will not: she will be shahīd, in shā’a Llāh. This what happened. And she is here since that time. She giving barakah, giving happiness for all people.

All people they are visiting her – Christian, Muslim, other people, all. Yesterday we was there maybe more than two hundred, three hundred people coming for visiting. And most of them tourist, they are not Muslim even, but she attract people. She make them, with this what we call jadhbah (attraction) to take them, bring them to her. Otherwise why they will come? Because they can go out from outside but they insist to go inside to see her. This is a miracle of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam and miracle, it is for ProphetNone, whole prophets, all prophets. Special for our ProphetNone because we know heNone not come to see in here [not come to this island himself] but heNone see everything in universe. So, to say for Cyprus "Green Island" it is not difficult.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are happy with her because she is every, until Qiyāmah, she will be for people who love her and she love them, giving barakah for our island and, in shā’a Llāh, hidāyah for people also.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
