Mawlana Shaykh Mohamad Nazim al Haqqani an Nakshibendi 23 Jumādā al-Ūlā, 1432 A.H. Tuesday, April 26, 2011 Translated from Arabic by: http://saltanat.org/

BismillahirRahmanirRahim RF: Do Jinn have Sultans? This addressing was for human beings and jinn. All jinn have kings. There are no elections in the jinn world. All jinn have kings. Shaykh Hisham: Mawlana how many kings are here? What do we need such kings for? There are some [Mawlana waves to indicate 'around here'] who are appointed and they have their duties. La Hawla wa La Quwwata illa Billah Al Aliyul Azim SH: Sayyidi you said the king of jinn is Sultan Hazzaz? Yes, Hazzaz - he is one who is very powerful. The kings of jinn are all Sultans, but there is one who is the head of the kings, the Sultan of all Sultans on earth for them. SH:Shaykh Abdullah Daghestani used to say that the Sultan of jinn would always come to visit him. Yes, seven kings were coming to ask if there was an order for them. SH: Grand Shaykh said that all Allah had given him he passed to you, Mawlana. Therefore these seven kings must be coming also to you Sayyidi? Masha'ALLAH Ya Hu … May Allah protects us. This is an astonishing and important point. It is a bombardment on all the presidents and all the nations, the nation of Islam. The Order came now also on the non-Muslim nations. May Allah punish them. SH: Mawlana, all the kings of jinn are listening to you. They may listen according to their limits. The army is ready. If an order comes, in one hour, they may throw all these presidents out. Not one of these shaytanic presidents will remain. This is according to an Order, not my job. SH: If the declaration is made, this means that the appearance of Mahdi (as) is near? Yes. If they don't listen they may send a jinn after each one who is not accepting. They may all go crazy and go to the mental hospital. [Mevlana requests a short song about Syria]. Yes. That's it. If they object to one word, they will send him one jinn to make him crazy and send them to the mental hospital. This is also a Heavenly Warning. I am a weak servant. I can't do anything, but there are those who are appointed for such a job. Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar! Look also this one is coming [Mawlana points at a cat.]. He belongs to that kind of creation. Now he came. That is the original one? RF: Yes this is the one. SH: There are many declarations today. Yes because I'm angry. That cat never leaves me. Whenever there is an association, wherever he is, he leaves and must appear here and attend. He is amazing, this cat. Maymoun come, come! He is looking, “Should I go”? SH: Mawlana he is respecting and he is keeping his distance. Look, whenever there is an association he must come and sit here. Incredible!
