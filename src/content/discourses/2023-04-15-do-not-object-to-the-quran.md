As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh. A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. What is īmān (belief)? Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Āmana r-rasūlu bi-mā unzila ilayhi min rabbihī wa-l-mu’minūn, kullun āmana bi-Llāhi wa-malā’ikatihī wa-kutubihī wa-rusulih, lā nufarriqu bayna aḥadin min rusulihī wa-qālū sami‘nā wa-aṭa‘nā ghufrānaka rabbanā wa-ilayka l-maṣīr" (2:285).

Īmān is that we believe in AllāhNone, the prophets and the books of AllāhNone. This is īmān and Islām; the belief of Ahlu s-Sunnah wa-l-Jamā‘ah is on this way. The book of AllāhNone is eternal and pre-eternal. It is HisNone word. To obey the word of AllāhNone is: we heard it, "sami‘nā". We heard it and we accepted it, to walk on HisNone way, to always walk on the way of our Holy ProphetNone. They are the same way. Our way is the same as hisNone.

Once our way is the same, we must know that everything is from AllāhNone and not object to the things HeNone says. You cannot say, ''This is right and that is wrong,'' according to your mind. Something you said was wrong ten years ago, you now say is right. What people said was wrong a hundred years ago is accepted now. So it always changes. What doesn't change are the commands and orders of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. You must follow them. It is wrong to object to them. It is not good for you.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla has given these favours to mankind. HeNone placed this whole world and all its realms under their command. HeNone has granted everything to them. But they are leaving it. They leave what is necessary for them. They object, and contemplate things that are not necessary for them. If you are a clerk, you must do your job as clerk. If you are a tailor, you must do tailoring. If you are a farmer, mind your farm. You must take care of that. And whatever you need for it, you can rule it out accordingly. But you cannot interfere in the orders of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.

Also, they say, "This is how I see it in the Qur’ān. This is how I will do it." There is also a new fashion now. They didn't accept ḥadīth, now they don't like the Qur’ān as well. "This is right. That is wrong." What do you understand? Who are you to object to the word of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla? To dislike it? Beware of making such a mistake, because that mistake will ruin you. It will destroy you. You will not do good in dunyā or in ākhirah. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla created you on this way. Make shukr for that. Do not object. The wisdom of AllāhNone and knowledge of AllāhNone are known only to HimNone. Who are we to object to what HeNone says? If we cannot do it, we may not. That is ok. But to object and say, "No, this is so and that is so," going along thinking it is like the life of this world where we can say, "I object to this and I object to that," you will not attain anything. And more than that, you will have a lot of sins. With sins you may still be saved, but you must not lose īmān. If you lose īmān, you will not attain anything at all. You will lose everything.

Therefore, these are important matters. We must be careful. Shayṭān enters through this door to Muslims: "Don't listen to scholars. Don't listen to shaykhs. Don't listen to murshids. Open and read the Qur’ān as you like. Do whatever is said in it." There are the ḥadīth. But they don't accept the ḥadīth anymore. Don't think about whether they are right or wrong. There will be no īmān then. When īmān is gone, may AllāhNone protect us, people will go to ākhirah without īmān.

We must be careful. These matters are not child's play. This is not a movie or a television series. You cannot say, "It must be like this or like that." It is not just any book. To object to the book of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is unbelief. If you say, "I don't accept it" about a single āyah, may AllāhNone protect us, you may lose your religion. Therefore, we must be careful about this. It is a dangerous thing. Say instead that you don't know. That there is wisdom to it, whatever it may be. Every letter, every word is full of wisdom. There are millions and billions of wisdoms, not just one or two.

Therefore, may AllāhNone give us from that wisdom, since, "Wa-man yu’ta l-ḥikmata fa-qad ūtiya khayran kathīra" (2:269). Whoever has wisdom has goodness. Having goodness is being saved in ākhirah. May AllāhNone make all of us a means for goodness. May HeNone show us HisNone wisdom, in shā’a Llāh.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone gave the last two verses from Sūratu l-Baqarah for Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam in hisNone Mi‘rāj when heNone went for Sidrah – AllāhNone call himNone for night journey. And these two verses was given by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla for Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, direct without waḥy (revelation), without Jibrīl ‘alayhi s-salām. First of them is showing what is – what we must believe, our belief, īmān.

This is important thing not to say anything against what AllāhNone say, especially in Qur’ān. We know, we can read, we can... We must ask, because there is special people, scholar, ‘ulamā’. All they was explaining, reading, they are very carefully looking for this. If you don’t understand, say, by yourself: "AllāhNone, what is wisdom? There is wisdom from this." Each one wisdom. Don‘t make yourself, tire yourself, because this is not your business. This is Allāh'sNone business. Don't come to place, very dangerous. Even in dunyā, when, if you are here they have electricity thing. They was putting too much wire, to not go anybody inside. If anybody go inside, quickly he will finish. Even here in dunyā this, you say this. How you can say going to dangerous? Dunyā, maybe, if you go to there, you can die, and later in ākhirah, maybe you in Jannah, or you can save yourself. But that one, if you go to this dangerous place with AllāhNone, you will be forever finished. AllāhNone keep us safe from our ego and not to make us to go to this dangerous place, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone give us wisdom from HisNone wisdom, to know everything, to accept, to say, "Sami‘nā wa-aṭa‘nā." We hear and we accept what AllāhNone saying for us.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
