As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu

Llāhi wa-barakātuh. A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīn wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa-l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

Who respects them will be respected in the Presence of AllāhNone. Their stations will be raised

When our ProphetNone came to Madīnah, the people of Madīnah were having a feast. Our ProphetNone asked what it was. They said such and such a holiday. "AllāhNone gave you better ones: ‘Īdu l-Fiṭr and ‘Īdu l-Aḍḥá." May AllāhNone bless it for all of us, in shā’a Llāh. May we reach more ‘Īds. May it be more beautiful and spread with the light of Islām all over the world, in shā’a Llāh. May the Saviour come. The real feast will be then. Because the world is full of oppressors. Even a person claiming to be the strongest cannot be saved from the oppression. In shā’a Llāh may it be different next year with Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām coming to save us and may the world reach peace, in shā’a Llāh. That is ‘Īd. It will be better, with the permission of AllāhNone.

We are thanking Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla we reach this day, feast. We call by name of Islamic name – ‘Īd, ‘Īdu l-Fiṭr, the feast of Ramaḍān, Shahru Ramaḍān, end of Ramaḍān or after all this bless days – nice day. It is only these days, bless days and can be we happy with these days.
