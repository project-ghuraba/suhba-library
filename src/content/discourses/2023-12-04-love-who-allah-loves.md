As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla has beloved servants. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Alā inna awliyā’a Llāhi lā khawfun ‘alayhim wa-lā hum yaḥzanūn. Alladhīna āmanū wa-kānū yattaqūn" (10:62-63). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘aẓīm. They are the beloved servants of AllāhNone. They have no fear, no sorrow. And there is no fear, no sorrow for those who are together with them. Because they possess loyalty. What is meant by loyalty is they never betray or do badness to other people. They are the people who care about their promises and keep their promise. Their master is our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, Sayyidunā Muḥammad ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. They follow hisNone way. That way is for people to be human. That way...

Who is a human? A human is the creature of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla who is given the opportunity to reach the highest ranks. If people follow their way, they will be from the accepted people near AllāhNone. Their way is the way of salvation, the way of happiness. And they don't get upset for dunyā, this world. They lead people to the right way and point in the direction of the right way. Certainly, those who are together with them get great benefits. These benefits are both here and hereafter.

Prophets, ṣaḥābah who were with prophets – their way is to spread light all over the world, to enlighten it with light, to take people out of darkness to brightness and to bring peace to them. This is their way. Those who serve them... They don't ask for anyone's service. What they ask for is to serve people themselves. Their service shows the right way. But people should be together with them and help them. Those who do so for the pleasure of AllāhNone will get great benefits as well. Certainly, that mercy will reach both themselves and their families, their sons and children. This is the way of awliyā’, the way of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, the way of compassion. Those who show compassion to people and who serve them will be served as well, as we said.

If someone does service, AllāhNone returns a hundred thousand times more. Its benefit is so great for those who understand it. They will certainly reach happiness. The ways of all awliyā’ are the same. "This one is different, that one is like this" – there is no such differentiation. Certainly, they are beloved servants of AllāhNone. They are beneficial for humanity. Their benefit is for all of humanity. Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says, "Bihim tumṭarūn bihim tunṣarūn – Rain comes because of their barakah. With their help, you attain victory. Provision comes through them." Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gives all these: provision, victory, rain and all kinds of goodness because of their barakah and through them. As we said, they are beneficial for humanity, for all of humanity. Because when benefit comes, it is not taken from here and given there. All of them are given by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla as they are there.

May AllāhNone make their service last. May AllāhNone be pleased with them. And when they pass away to ākhirah, their service becomes more. May AllāhNone not separate us from their doorway, in shā’a Llāh.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, there is people, HeNone love them, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. And with HisNone love for this people, AllāhNone give the best, every good thing for human being. And as we said, the first, AllāhNone who love them, they are prophets and who is near, more near to prophets, going same way of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, they are, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla love them. And when somebody love them, AllāhNone also love him.

And of course, human beings sometimes they are have, you see they need something, some help or other thing. If anybody help this beloved one for AllāhNone, this people they are very sincere and sensitive and not forgetting what they done good thing for themselves. For them or for Islām or anything, very small thing even, they are not forgetting the good thing. But they forget another, other thing. If you do bad thing for them, they are not interested to keep it, to not forget it. They forget bad thing, not forget good thing. Even very small, they are – I don't know what English, wafā’ – wafā’ meaning to keep relationship, good relationship with whole people: for people to not be betrayed them, to not harm them, to not say anything against them. This people very, of course, very few people, rare people, but they are exist. They are until Qiyāmah they will be in dunyā.

One of them, of course, Mawlānā Shaykh. He was not forgetting anything. Very small thing we are... said, "This man he do this, he done this." But he, who done some help or service for him or helped him, he never forget. They are never forgetting, Mawlānā and who is like him. And they are looking after them, this people, even from grandfather until whole grandchildren even. They have this barakah from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Who people they are happy, friend with, friend of AllāhNone, beloved to AllāhNone, AllāhNone happy with them. And they be safe here and hereafter.

This is very important, very, very benefit for human being – to be careful when they saying anything or doing anything for this beloved one for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. They must be very careful. They must be sensitive because if you friend with them, AllāhNone friend with you, AllāhNone happy with you. If you are not like this, AllāhNone not happy with you. Even you are claiming you know Qur’ān by heart, you know thousands of ḥadīth or other thing, but when you say something for beloved one for AllāhNone, AllāhNone will not be happy with you and you will be in danger for your next life. This is very important. Even without anything, this people who help, AllāhNone forgive them because they love who is loving HimNone for his sake they will be, in shā’a Llāh, safe. This is very important because many people nowadays, they are without knowing anything and they are claiming they are scholar, they are ‘ulamā’, they are know everything. But when they do something, all this going for nothing. They finish themselves.

AllāhNone help us. AllāhNone He make us to be whole time to help good people, help awliyā’u Llāh, in shā’a Llāh. Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
