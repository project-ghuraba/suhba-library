Waṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-F’āiz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-

Mankind is created weak, however he does not know his weakness. Compared to the greatness of Allah, he is nothing. He does not realize his own nothingness, and because of this ignorance, instead he challenges Allah, and the more he gathers knowledge, the more ignorant and [falsely] brave he becomes. It is said, “One who is ignorant is brave.” Those who really know are those who have fear of Allah. They ask for the Allah’s mercy. The situation of this world shows this, namely that help is from Allah. Allah made this virus pandemic a means for us, and those who learn from it will take it as such, while others will remain the same. The real calamity is going to be in the Next World. The fear, uneasiness, and discomfort of this world is as nothing compared to that in the Next World.

So all of these happenings are a reward for the mumin, a good deed for believing in Allah, while they are a torment for disbelievers, a punishment. So the one who is a believer should be at peace, and his heart should be content. Every minute of this event is a reward for him. Everything that appears to be bad has good within it, so Muslims need not be worried. On the contrary, this is from Allah. Already Allah does it in a way that none can effectively oppose. When it happens, you need to stay home, and surely you should take your precautions. You must not go here and there, must not go to unknown places. Take your precautions, trust in Allah, and submit. May Allah reward us, insha’Allah. May Allah protect us. May Allah protect our faith, so that the suffering is not in vain. Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-F ti ah
