As-Salamu Alaikum wa Rahmatullahu wa Barakatuhu. Madad ya Rasul Allah, Madad ya S Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, madad ya Shaykh Muhammad Nazim al-Haqqani, dastur! Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fil jamia'.

We may say a few words of Sohbat as tashabbuh/imitating awliya. Who you imimate, you become from them. These holy ones- the ones that we are trying to imitate, to be like them, they are "Rijalun la yashqa bihim jalisuhum- It says Rijal, Man. Now man..There are many people but very few men are left. These men are hard to find. Who sits with them, who sits in their assemblies or who tries to imitate them never goes to shaqawah. What we mean by shaqawah is bad end, being involved in bad things.

That fear of dunya, fear of poverty.. he has no fear left in him anymore- the one who follows these holy ones, who sits with them. They are like dunya's.. They are the essence of this world. This world can't remain in it's orbit even for one minute if it wasn't for them. Our Peophet (sas) said, for their honour your rizq/provision is coming. It is coming on whole world, for their honour, for their mercy- on insects, on animals, on men, Muslim, kafir, believer, unbeliever, on all of them comes rizq, rain for their honour. Tasarruf, it is called Qutb al-Mutasarrif, they are responsible for everything in this world, Allah gave this authority to them.

Therefore to be in the assembly of such holy ones is a big grant, it is a big happiness. They never suffer any sorrow, sadness- who are in their assemblies. Even so many troubles & hardships come on them like mountains, they are.. by the wisdom of Allah, just like how snow melts if it comes on hot iron. These troubles &hardships don't have any affect on those who follow these holy ones & sit in their assembly. Allah helps them, takes the pain from them & comes fadhilah/virtue on them instead.

Shukur Allah a while ago our Shaykh, our father was with us. Happiness...Like the Hadith Sharif says: "when they are near in the assembly- even if when they are far, it is nothing but when they are near it is much more happiness". One does not feel any thirst, any tiredness, no nothing. And it is from these Men- Men means a great fadhilah/virtue. "Rijalun La Tulhihim Tijaratun Wa La Bay`un" (24:37). Also in Holy Quran, what it means with Rijal are always Great Men. A great man is not for dunya but the one who looks to akhirah is considered great. Who runs after dunya is not a great man. Dunya means low, as the name implies. Who follows it & runs after it.. can who runs after low, rise? He can't rise, he also remains low. These are the Sultans of Akhirah. They are governing the world with Allah's permission. Don't be deceived by their outward appearance/situation. They can throw this world like a ball from one side to another. Mawlana said this many times: one man from this RijalAllah, Men of Allah, can throw this world to here there not like a ball, but like an orange. All is in their hand. Therefore they don't run after dunya. Dunya runs after them.

Their desire is pleasing Allah, & guiding the Nation of the Prophet (sas) to the right path. What makes them happy the most is to guide a person to the right path. They put up with all kinds of hardships & bad treatment to do this. They don't respond to meaningless words either, only for that person to be guided & his heart not to be broken. No matter what people say, they don't care at all. They are such people. If it was someone else in their place could do something bad to who says it instantly. But these are awliya-Allah - there are many who harm them, do bad things & they try to do them even more good in return.

And this, to be Muslim & to be on this path & to find the rijal Allah, men of Allah, the greatest men of Allah, this is the real joy & happiness. This is the happiness of dunya & akhirah. They show you the right way also. Now it is Ramadan Sharif. If you go out of this dargah, no one knows about Ramadan or anything else. The Hadith of the Prophet (sas) which we just read in Khutbah: if a person knowingly breaks his fast without any excuse- such as illness, traveling.. even if he fasted whole his life, he can't reach the virtue of it. He can't reach the virtue of that

one day of fast he broke in Ramadan. Of course those who sit outside, who don't come to the assembly of such men, how will they know about this? He thinks the more he breaks fast, the better. But he doesn't know what he is losing, how he will regret in akhirah, he doesn't know. Therefore they are in shaqawah/ misfortune & misery.

But who are in the assembly of awliyas & follow them like this, they don't suffer misery. What a joy for us. There is no joy in any other thing. There is no joy for dunya, no joy for nationalism. You are not like that, Allah created you like that. If you are a nation whose ancestors fought in the way of Allah, joy for you! Allah Shukur our ancestors did this, and that is by the wisdom of Allah. We will thank Allah for it, we will praise & glorify Him. Otherwise there is no happiness for a nation who worships dunya.

Today we speak about, to look like... Make sohbat and then every sohbat he said there is sohbat "Tariqatuna sohbah" Tariqat meaning sohbat, giving sohbat and good to be in together. We say this to look like our mashayekh. And to be, to try to be like them. To taqlid/imitate them, it is good. Because what you like and imitate, you will be with them inshaAllah. This who we imitate them, they are saying "Rijalun la yashqa bihim jalisuhum- They are the Men who will cause no failure to the others". This is men. There is too many, billions of people. But to call these people with "Men", it is not correct. Only very few from this people they are Men.

This word what meaning? Because Allah said Men in many place in Quran. Rijalun, this is for very high quality men meaning. And this what, they are very high quality men? They are awliya Allah. "Rijalun la yashqa bihim jalisuhum" This men who they are awliya-Allah. When you sit with them or you follow them, you will not be miserable at all. For other thing, every people they are miserable. Because they are running after dunya. This who, politic men? No, not this, not men. Even not politics, especially politics. Because they are 99% what they speak, all lying. They are liar people. This is not...

And who running after dunya, dunya meaning in Arabic, low thing. And who going after low thing, how will he be high? High, awliya-Allah. They are looking for akhirah. Dunya nothing for

them. Dunya needs them. Because Prophet (sas) saying: "With them Allah give you your food & everything, for to be survive. With them, raining. With them snowing. With them...Everything in this world, belong to one awliya. There is Qutb al-mutasarrif who looking after this. Allah give dunya for them to.. And they are not going out of order of Allah. Because if they are not in this, from this who not believer or not Muslim, or bad people, Allah not give them anything. But from Merciful of Allah this awliya-Allah, Allah give this rizq for people. And when you are with them, they protect you. Here even you have some bad thing coming on you, or on family or illness or other thing- many thing may be coming to men. This is coming on you, on this people, who with awliya, It is like snow coming on hot iron, quickly going. They not feeling.It is awliya they take, to not be miserable this people.

Alhamdulillah, this is lucky. We are Alhamdulillah, we are lucky. We are happy for this. To be lucky, you are here. You are invite with Allah, last Prophet Sayyidina Muhammad (sas) & his religion Islam. And you are in Tariqah also, & we are here. This is big luck for you. Nobody, not many people have this luck. You can be happy with this. Because now when you are in this, with this awliya Allah, to be with them.. You see, before 10 minutes Mawlana Shaykh Nazim he was here. Giving all pleasure for people, Masha Allah. Nobody feeling thirsty or tired or anything. We were very happy. And he is now, he is like here, but when he is going also, he is all time with you- with his barakah, blessings. All the time with you, because you are following him & this is, Alhamdulillah. And many who with him, do not lose anything from blessing, from gift of Allah (awj).

For Khutbah, we read one Hadith: Prophet (sas), he was saying, "who is not fasting in Ramadan without excuse.." Of course there is many excuse. For lady, another some days. For who is ill you cannot, like diabetes or other. So they cannot be fast; Ok. But for some travelling, some people when travelling they not fasting. Also this is excuse. But without excuse if for one day, you whole life fasting you can not reach this blessing for one day from Ramadan you are not fasting without excuse. For 1 day. Subhana Allah. When you be here, so you are not losing anything from blessing, from worshipping to know what is good what is bad.Because here, you see here now we in dargah. Go outside of

dargah, especially in this island, you cannot feel at all anything from Ramadan, from fasting-nobody fasting. They are not knowing. And it is real luck because Sultan al Awliya, here is here & this island, nobody know about him. Not feeling; only when he come, making salam when he passing through him, and they are not fasting, drinking and.. Alhamdulillah, we must be thankful for Allah day & night. Allah send us to this place, Alhamdulillah. When you are here, Allah make us heart staying here. But Alhamdulillah also not staying, this awliya-Allah not leaving people. Who coming to him "la yashqa bihim jalisuhum". This is meaning, for continuous. When he is coming once, he will be at the end in happiness.

Alhamdulillah. Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, Al Fatiha.
