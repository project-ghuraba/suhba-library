As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa raḥmatu Llāhi wa barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīn wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla wants goodness for us. The greatest goodness we can do is to love AllāhNone, to love the ProphetNone. This is the greatest goodness. Because only with love is a man saved. A person cannot reach anything with enmity or disrespect. Don't be an enemy to AllāhNone. Don't disrespect the ProphetNone. Shayṭān is the enemy of AllāhNone. Who doesn't love our ProphetNone and disrespects himNone is also shayṭān. Therefore, to love our ProphetNone and to love AllāhNone is farḍ. The first duty of Muslims, the greatest duty for every Muslim, is to love the ProphetNone. When you don't love himNone, whatever you do, you can think you are doing good, it's not good at all. It is evil. Certainly, at the end it will be a service to shayṭān.

Love is a great favour, it is good. You are with the one you love, says Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla. Therefore, our ProphetNone said whoever you are friends with, let him be like yourself. Let him be a good person so that its outcome is good. When you are with a bad person, you will get harm. Even if you don't do it, they'll say you're his friend. Even in dunyā, when something happens, they look to the friends of a bad man. They question those who cooperated with him. Some of them fall into difficult conditions, some are saved. Therefore, as the matters of dunyā are like this, it is even more difficult for Ākhirah. You can't say, "I didn't do it." They say, "Stay away from this person. Stay away from evil." If you can give advice against badness, our ProphetNone says, you can prevent it with your hand. If you can't, you should advise them. If they don't listen to your advice, you should say this person is wrong by heart and stay away from him.

Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla loves good ones. Some people presume Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla loves everyone HeNone created. Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla loves good people. It says even in Qur'ān, who comes against AllāhNone, Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla doesn't love them. You can't come against AllāhNone. People think they are smart and come against AllāhNone. They disobey AllāhNone. They think they are doing something. There is no escape for them. We belong to AllāhNone. The whole universe belongs to AllāhNone. Everything belongs to AllāhNone. HeNone has no partner. There is no escape. Sometimes they do something in this world and run away to other countries. Other countries protect them. But people don't have such an opportunity against Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla. Therefore, don't try your luck. Obey AllāhNone. Beg AllāhNone. Ask forgiveness from AllāhNone. AllāhNone is forgiving, HeNone forgives.

We are, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, in place where Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla love and looking for with HisNone mercy and bless because we are, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, only meeting here for HisNone love and for HisNone pleasure. And HisNone pleasure, our pleasure. Also this is what Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa sallam saying – you not be believer, completely believer, until you love what AllāhNone love and you like what AllāhNone like and to hate what AllāhNone hate. So, in shā’a Llāh, we are in place where full of love for AllāhNone, for ProphetNone, for companions, for Ahlu l-Bayt, for whole believer. With this love we can survive in next life.

The most important thing for believer, to love Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla and to love his Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa sallam. It is obligatory. But people nowadays they are not loving AllāhNone and not respecting ProphetNone. Only they thought we can practice. No need to think about AllāhNone, no need to respect Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa sallam, no need to respect companions, Ahlu l-Bayt. This is a new thing from shayṭān whispering for people to do this but all everything you done, without putting this love, in this world what you did it is not good – no any benefit from this. You only tire yourself. But with love and it is, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa sallam, only, you can survive. Because Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla HeNone love ProphetNone and HeNone love who they are loving HisNone Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa sallam.

And now the most people they are thinking they are clever. They are getting the highest knowledge and they are denying Creator and not accepting Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa sallam. This people in actually they are the most ignorant people because this universe, this whole existence, the only we have – HeNone is – everything for our Lord Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla. So you think you will run away from HimNone. It is most idiot thinking because no place to run. In this world many people they do something wrong in their country, they running to another country. Another country they are happy with them, protect them. They give them protection, money, and everything because they have something against that country. So they can survive in their life. But for Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla this life, next life, everything, universe, every existence in this. You thinking only for AllāhNone no another thing. If you run from AllāhNone don't think shayṭān he will protect you. No. He also he will say, "I'm afraid from Lord Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla. I'm not responsible for you. Why you follow me?" So no any place to run away.

Only thing, to love AllāhNone and to love ProphetNone and you will survive, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone give this for people because this days people they are not thinking anything about love of AllāhNone. Only they thinking love of women, love of cars, love of money, love of luxury. This is their thought. They... This not love. This is only ego desire. Love, real pure love – for AllāhNone, for Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa sallam. AllāhNone give us this love, in shā’a Llāh. When you get tasting this love for AllāhNone, for ProphetNone, nothing can make you happy without them. AllāhNone give that this for us, in shā’a Llāh. Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
