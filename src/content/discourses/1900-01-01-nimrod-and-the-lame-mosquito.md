Audhu Billahi min ash-shaitani rajeem Madad ya Sayyidi La hawla wa la quwwata illa Billahi l-Aliyyu l-Azeem Bismillahi tawakkaltu 'ala Allah

We are depending our Lord's Mercy. We are trusting to His Mercy that we are swimming in Mercy Oceans. Alhamdulillah, thanks to our Lord, endless thanks and endless praisings we are coming for the existence of universes. We are asking who is owner of universes? It is a question. Who can say that "I am the owner of this world?" Even for this world I am asking and no one can answer and saying that "I am the owner". When we can, where we can find a person who can say "I am the owner of this universe"?

If there was a person that claiming "I am the owner of skies", those people riding on rockets must take visa from them to go to space. "Give money that I shall give you to go". He must sit there, in the skies to take, to make stamp, yes, for it. But not finding such a person from kind of sons of Adam. No, no one saying or claiming. If anyone claiming today that "I am owner", tomorrow going away. Finish. Allah, the Owner of Universes, the Lord of Universes sending one angel, "take his soul, come, bring him to Me".

Nimrod was saying, you know Nimrod? Yes, worst... (Some people also in our time make this claim) They are foolish. Nimrod was saying that to Abraham, the father of prophets. Israel, [to] sons of Israel, how many Prophets coming, Abraham is their father, grandfather, and our Prophet's grandfather also, peace be upon all of them. When he sent by the Lord of the Worlds to call him to believe in the Lord of Universes, he refused and was saying "Oh no, I am the lord on Earth, You are Lord in the skies. I am the owner" saying Nimrod, "of this world, You are Lord in skies." Foolish. Foolish claiming.

Then he asked from Ibrahim (as) that "I am not going to obey to your Lord and I am ready for fighting, for defeating your Lord." Then Allah Almighty ordering to His Prophet Abraham, peace be upon him, saying, "Say to him to gather his armies and come out. Then I shall send from my armies, one army for fighting to him." Then Nimrod taking his armies, armoured on horses with spears. Ibrahim, famous name. You must say 'alhamdulillah' always that your name Ibrahim. Whole place, full up with armoured soldiers' army. He was waiting on his horse running like this, coming ride, like this. No patience. "Where your Lord's army? From where coming?" Then he was looking, like a black cloud appear, from the side of sun, coming. Coming so that just a cloud covering sun, becoming world dark. Then approaching. He was looking and his armies looking what is going to happen. Then begin, their noises coming. It was mosquito army.

Allah Almighty sending mosquito army, armies. Millions and billions. If one comes to you, you can't sleep. You may imagine how billion of them coming? More than jet filos (jet fleets) approaching and landing on armies of Nimrod. And beginning to biting, eating. After a short time, there was in armours only bones, nothing else. From a huge army lying on earth only armour, bones. Inside finished. Allah, the Lord of Universes saying "I am the Lord of Universe. Ana Rabbu l-'Alameen." Nimrod quickly running away, escaping. That whole army coming, finishing that huge army, finished.

One one-leg mosquito, can't reach them, it was late. Coming through later, around going, "O my Lord, no one I can find." Allah Almighty ordering, "O my slave, you may go through after Nimrod, catch him and finish him also". Then this mosquito smelling where is Nimrod, going after. Nimrod saying that "Close whole doors. No, don't leave any hole so that no mosquitos coming." They closing every place and sitting, lying on his head.

Allah leading His creatures. Allah is the Creator and He is leading every creature towards their destination. Leading that lame mosquito, finding its way through keyhole. Quickly coming and entering from his nose, going up, into his head. "You are saying 'I am Lord'? Now I am teaching you," saying to him. "I am teaching you who you are and who He is, the Lord of Universes". And biting. Eating, biting, eating. Then terrible headaches coming to him. As much as biting, terrible headaches coming to Nimrod. Then making like this, then that mosquito stopping from biting, a little. Rest for him.

Therefore, Nimrod making people to come and with their hands such a thing to beat him. When beating, that mosquito keeps quiet. Then begins. Every time one beat comes and mosquito keeps quiet. Then begins. Allah Almighty teaching who He is and who saying more than himself must be punished. We are slaves. He is our Lord. Once, a guardian too much tired from this and with this big clap, with power hit him hard and head going to be two pieces. And phrrrr... one fly flying. That small mosquito being like a sparrow, like sparrow flying away and saying, "This is the punishment [for] who claiming more than himself". Any slave claiming more than to be a slave, this is punishment to him.

This is a tradition coming through whole holy books to us that who can say we are owners for this universe? No one can say, but we know that must be an owner for whole universe. It is impossible to be a thing without owner. Look, everywhere. You may find an owner. On whole world even they are now dividing air also, saying, "This is my air" and fighting with each other saying, "You passed too much in my airspace. You must go away." Everything has an owner. [For] whole universe [there] must be an owner and our Lord saying "I am that Owner, Rabbu l-'Alameen, the Lord of the Worlds."

We are going to stop here because it is an endless ocean also. If we are beginning, too many hours must speak for it. Therefore, we may stop here. When Allah Almighty gives permission to speak on that verse, then we hope to give more details on that verse inshaAllah.

Wa min Allahi tawfeeq bi hurmati l-Habib bi hurmati l-Fatiha
