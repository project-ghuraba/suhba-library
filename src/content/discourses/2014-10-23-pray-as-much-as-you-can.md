Sheikh Bahauddin Adil Al-Haqqani sohbat of the 23rd of October, 2014.

Allahu Allahu Allahu Allah. Allahu Allahu Allahu Allah. Allahu Allahu Allahu Allah. Allahu Allahu Allahu Allah. Allahu Allahu Allahu Allah. Allahu Allahu Allahu Allah. Allahu Allahu Allahu Allah. Allahu Allahu Allahu Allah. Allahumma salli wa sallim 'ala Nabina Muhammad, alaihi salam Salatan tadumu wa tuhda ilaih mammara layali wa tula dawam. Allahumma salli wa sallim 'ala Nabina Muhammad, alaihi salam Salatan tadumu wa tuhda ilaih mammara layali wa tula dawam.

MashaAllah. It is holy night. Allah Almighty, He made every Friday night as holy night. In modern world we say Thursday night but actually it's Friday night. This is opportunity to get a blessing from Allah Almighty. Because Allah Almighty, He make Friday the blessing day. It is the most blessed day in the week. But if you calculate it as different calculation, it is the best day in Allah Almighty time zone. So Allah Almighty, He was so generous that He gives to us every week. This is the generosity of Allah Almighty. He can make it like Eid al-Adha or Eid al-Fitr once a year. No, He said "I am so generous for My servants to make it every week".

Alhamdulillah That's why, the manifestation, when it comes in Friday, it doesn't have it in any days. So the manifestation of Friday starts after maghrib until the Juma maghrib. Whatever you can get from this manifestation, it is your will. It's free to collect. Nobody asks any money. There is no tool to get money of this manifestation. This is also important announcement because everybody keeps thinking to buy something. This Allah Almighty, He gave it for free. Allah Almighty, He is offering it for free. Allah Almighty, He is offering everything for free. But we are the ones making our life difficult. We are making our worship more difficult and we are putting so many conditions. You just go and get the manifestation of Allah Almighty. Nobody is prohibiting you, nobody is telling you "Wait".

It is important to know also, as much as you can pray, you pray. Don't minus your prayers. Don't say fard is enough. Yes, fard is enough. If you are going for the central bank of Johannesburg and they tell you "Today is free to take as much as you want from money", what you will do? You say "Yes, maybe one plot is enough for me" and you go out. I don't think anybody will do this. They'll bring the lorry to take as much as they can. This is when you minus your prayer, you go for the central bank and you are taking only one paper, not even plot and say "This is enough for me". And if you tell this for people, they say "What a stupid man. He was having offer to take as much as he wants from the central bank, and he take only 1 piece of 10, what is your currency, rand. He will take 1 piece of 10 rand." Who will tell him "You are so clever and you are so genius to take 10 rand"? And they are offering you. If you bring a lorry, they will not say no.

That's why, it is important to add in your prayer. Add as much as you can. You pray sunnah. You pray another sunnah. You pray sunnat-ul wudu, you pray another sunnat-ul wudu. You pray shukr, pray much of shukr. You pray night prayer, you pray as much as you can. If Allah Almighty, He is giving you the opportunity to pray and you don't know the value of the prayers, I'm telling you the value of the prayers now. So many people, they say "We pray very slowly". It's ok, but if you are praying little bit faster but you're praying more rakat, it will be better. Because every rakat is counted. Don't forget, if you pray 2 rakat very slow, maybe you pray 10 rakat very fast, you have 10 rakat as counted. It's important to understand such things. Not that we are praying so fast or we are praying so slow. Maybe you can enjoy your prayer, I'm not saying anything. But the counting is there. Allah Almighty, He counts. "This servant, he prays for Me every day 100 rakat. And this servant, he prays for Me 2 rakat." What the angel will say? "Ya Rabbi,

this man is praying 100 rakat and this man praying 2 rakat". Which one more valuable? Of course, 100 rakat is more valuable.

Allah Almighty, He's telling you "Pray. Pray for Me as much as you can." And He says "You praise RasulAllah as much as you can. Make salatu salam for RasulAllah as much as you can. Don't be scared. Don't be scared. You make la illaha illAllah as much as you can. Don't be scared. Say Allah as much as you can. Don't be scared". This is offer from the central bank of Allah Almighty. Carry as much as you can. He says "Carry. Carry on". He says "Carry on, bring your lorry and take as much as you want". This is how Allah Almighty's so generous. This is how Allah Almighty, He is looking after us.

I wanted to say that alhamdulillah, with the baraka of Sheikh da Costa we are here and with baraka of Mawlana Sheikh, he make him the pillar in this country. And he is sacrificing by his life, by his privacy, by his family and by... to give service for all the mureeds and ummah. This is... We are people wanted to appreciate today alhamdulillah we've been in one place that our brother, he made one school, room for one school. And that I see that people, and it is coming to my heart and it was so nice to my heart, the way that they thanked us and they appreciated. And we are saying "Ya Rabbi, we are not enough appreciating You." And we're still arguing to pray 2 rakat or 4 rakat. This is what we have to appreciate Allah Almighty.

May Allah bless you and may Allah make you happy here and hereafter and make you to pray as much as you can. And may Allah give you long life, healthy life, wealthy life and no drama inshaAllah.

Wa min Allahi t-tawfiq. Bi hurmati-l Habib, bi hurmati-l Fatiha.
