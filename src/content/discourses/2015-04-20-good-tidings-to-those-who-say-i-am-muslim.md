Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi Rasulillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

We have entered auspicious months, may Allah make it blessed (mubarak). We have reached nice seasons. From now on, the month of Rajab has started today, Allah knows best (Allahu alam). Fasting Fridays, Mondays, and Thursdays in the month of Rajab is good. It is a great virtue and has great rewards. Some people can fast the Three Months, but it is still very good for those who fast Mondays and Thursdays. Every month has a prayer (dua) in the Three Months. Everybody has prayer books at home. It says it there. Reading them brings benefits and blessings (baraka) too Inshallah.

Allah (JJ) created the seasons. Spring season is also a very beautiful season. There is a spiritual beauty in these months. Flowers bloom, trees come to life, birds chirp, and beauties occur during spring months. Much greater beauties, spiritually, are in these months. So there are spiritual beauties other than the beauties that people can see through eyes. As an addition to these, Allah (JJ) also gives beauties inside of us and to our soul. Hence, good tidings to Muslims.

People who say, “I am Muslim,” are rejoiced. Good tidings to those who say, “I am Muslim.” Saying this is the most beautiful thing. Because it does not matter what you are. Allah (JJ) created you as Muslim. This is the greatest beauty. Good tidings to those who say, “I am Muslim.” Allah may create you black, He may create you white, or He may create you yellow. It is in the hands of Allah and He may create as He likes. It is not a merit or a virtue to be black or to be white. You can be as strong and as smart as you want. It is useless as long as you are not Muslim. Those are empty words.

People who think things through and are clever say, “Good tidings to those who say, „I am Muslim,‟” and say nothing else. Because as we said, everythings has occurred because Allah wanted it so. You were not created black because you wanted it, and you were not created white because you wanted it. Allah wanted and created as such. Happiness is not in being black or white but in being Muslim. Happiness here and in the hereafter is in being Muslim. It is in nothing else.

If Allah wanted, He could have created you with four legs. Would we say, “Good tidings to four legged ones?” then. We cannot say. Allah created us Muslim. Happiness and beauty is in this. This is the greatest happiness. Happiness is not in anything else. May Allah grant everybody faith (iman). This is the favor we want from these people. They claim humanity and humanism. People who have faith are happy people and good people. The rest Allah made as He liked and we cannot interfere. We only wish faith for everybody and wish for them to be in Islam. Allah knows the rest. As they like.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq.

Al-Fatiha.
