Sheikh Muhammad Nazim Al-Haqqani An-Naqshibandi, Sohbat.

How is He? No! We must... We are knowing that He is Creator and He is in existence And we are saying, we are knowing that His name is Allah. Therefore, if you are going to repeat the holy name of our Lord by His holy name Allah, if you are going to repeat it every day one hundred times - Ya Allah! Ya Allah! Ya Allah Ya Allah - after 40 days, you should find another personality with you. That holy name has such a spiritual power. A person may be Arab or non Arab - doesn't matter, but any one of sons of Adam, from every nation, if addressing to our Lord by His holy name - Ya Allah Ya Allah! Ya Allah! Ya Allah - particularly [at] night times in a silent place, in a darkness place, if you are saying 100 times you should find another personality with you after 40 days. I am not saying 15 years. I am saying only 40 days you shall find yourself exactly another personality coming on you.

Therefore, to say 'La ilaha illa Allah' also gives you such a spiritual power and quickly changing your personality. You may feel it. And, it is not a problem to be Islam or not, now. Islam only has some more practicing for our bodies. Yes. Gives discipline for our bodies. And people running away always from discipline. Everyone runs away from discipline. No one likes to be in discipline. Our egos always likes to be free, not in a discipline. That is problem for European people. They afraid from Islam - why? Islam just brought an exact discipline. Not leaving egos to do everything as they like. No! And European people, western people, they accustomed to be in absolute freedom. Only freedom. But Islam says 'No. Only discipline.' I don't understand from such a freedom but just I came to make everyone in a discipline. That is our conflict - çatışma (conflict). Yes.

But you are free. Now we are saying, you may say Allah every day 100 times and you may take a power, you may take a self control with you. That is important. You can be able to take reins, reins of your horse. You are just going to be a knight, cavalier, knight. Exactly. And some practicing also. Every practicing in Islam gives you more self control. But I am not offering to you whole practicing. No! Now it is enough for you, for every day 100 times to say 'Ya Allah, Ya Allah" If you like less from this power, you may say 'O my God, O my God, O my God! O my God guide me to you.' Or ask, 'O my God send me one of Your saints guiding me to You.' And after 40 days you must meet him, such a person. It is not going to be more than for 40 days.

Therefore, as you like you may do practicing and little by little you are going to control every (şiber) footstep in your personality, to take everything in your hands. Then you can understand what is real practicing for improvement from Earth up to Heavens. Doesn't matter if you are going to, people saying you -'You are Muslim.' No harm for us, we are looking for benefit. We are not going to be impacted, affected

from our senses, from our feelings. When our feelings going to be rulers on ourselves, we are going far away from realities. Feelings covering always. Therefore, we are not looking if our feelings may say "Why you are going to be Muslim? Yes, for what Islam? You practice in another way." What is the harm to be Muslim? Or what is the harm for me if I am practicing on a right way. I may take from Islam such a practicings. What is the harm to me from that? But, our customs, our feelings preventing us to go that side.

Q: SubhanAllah I have the feeling that my, that I am not really search for improvement, I have the feeling that my spirituality is something as an expression of my heart which I have to do. So, La ilaha illa Allah is something which I sing because I feel very happy to do this and not because I like to get better, to get cleaner.

M: Yes! Because you are now builder only, you are occupied for building. After these - whole furniture comes after building. You are not, you are only pleased with saying La ilaha illa Allah. That is all right because you are building only, now. When the building finished you may think about furniture, about decoration inside, what you are putting in it. For this furniture, we are bringing something from East - carpets. We are bringing such a things from West countries. We are using everything now, for decoration, after building. When you are building with La ilaha illa Allah then it is going to be finished. Then comes some practicing, which makes as a decoration in our inner life. Yes. That is all. Thank you. Now you may eat.
