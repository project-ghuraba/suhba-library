As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

We always start by making shukr to AllāhNone. We must start every task by making shukr to AllāhNone. Goodness and blessings increase with shukr. Blessings become lasting. Because not everything is everlasting. Everlasting is Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. With HisNone power and HisNone favour HeNone makes blessings last as long as HeNone wants. Because of HisNone attribute of being Everlasting. Therefore, we start everything by making shukr.

Shukr to AllāhNone, we have reached these holy days again. The days are passing quickly. Our shukr is for being constant on this way of truth, in shā’a Llāh. We make shukr so that we are constant and so that the blessings HeNone gives last. Shukr to AllāhNone, today is the nineteenth of Ramaḍān. Today is the nineteenth day. Tomorrow is the twentieth, in shā’a Llāh. And after the twentieth there is this beautiful worship called i‘tikāf. Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam never skipped it. But for us, heNone says whoever wants and is able, may do it. It is not farḍ, but for those who want it is a big blessing and great benefit. I‘tikāf is ten days. It can be seven days, five days, one day or one hour. Important is to make intention for it. You may enter a mosque with the intention of i‘tikāf every time you come. But the actual one is ten days.

Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam did ten days. But for the ummah, they may do as much as they can. Therefore, when we make intention for i‘tikāf every time we enter and leave the mosque, it opens thawāb (reward) for us. It opens the beautiful door of thawāb for us. As we read in the khuṭbah, our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam said, "The blessings of Ramaḍān and the rewards given in ākhirah are beyond our imagination." People yearn for rubbish in this world. They yearn for things that don't have any value. And they don't do anything for ākhirah, for the real eternal life which is in ākhirah. That is the destiny given to people by AllāhNone. Therefore, what we say to those who don't do this is the word of AllāhNone. If they don't do, it means AllāhNone didn't mean it for them. We should be sorry for them that they do not have a share from these beautiful things.

Otherwise, may AllāhNone protect us, we must beg AllāhNone not to become like them. May HeNone make us steadfast. May HeNone make us steadfast on the way of Islām, on the way of light, on the way of beauty. May HeNone help us. Because we never know how the ego is. As soon as someone loses his footing, he falls off the cliff and perishes. Nothing he did has any value then. Therefore, we must be careful. We must make shukr to AllāhNone.

It is from the grace of AllāhNone. "Hādhā min faḍli Rabbī" (27:40). If AllāhNone doesn't give you from HisNone grace, you can do whatever you want, but you won't find it. What AllāhNone wants happens. And you must not say "Why did AllāhNone do this?" Those who say like this achieve nothing. They only lose. They don't earn anything. These holy days are the days of treasure, not the treasure of this world, but the treasure of ākhirah. Take as much as you want from it. No one can say anything to you. "Take more and more," says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. "OurNone door is the door of generosity," says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. "Don't be ashamed and embarrassed. Take a lot. Take as much as you can," says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. But what are we doing? We say, "We don't want," and run away. And then they complain, "Why do we have these troubles? Why are we in such a condition?" Sure. Where will you go if you run away from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla? You will go to trouble. It is something known.

There are two ways, no third way. There is the way of AllāhNone and the way of shayṭān. You are either on the way of AllāhNone, or on the way of shayṭān. Who are on the way of AllāhNone are in profit. Who are on the way of shayṭān are in loss. They may eat or drink as much as they want. They may eat non-stop, eat day and night, they will get no profit. What they will get is a big belly and fat. That's it. There won't be another benefit. And they know these things have more harm than benefit. May AllāhNone protect us. May HeNone not separate us from the right way. Millions and billions of thanks to AllāhNone for putting us on this way. We have many examples in front of us. They are the people just like me and you but AllāhNone didn't give them this. Therefore, we must make shukr, as we said in the beginning. No need for other things. We must make continuous shukr.

May AllāhNone make us from the grateful ones, in shā’a Llāh.

But other thing also: to obey HimNone. This is also some kind of thank for AllāhNone, to worship HimNone. We are, what saying this? Because we are living in Ramaḍān, al-ḥamdu liLlāh. And nearly most of them, more than half finish. And this quickly going, these days. But we are thanking for HimNone. We are in this life until this last breath we are in danger from shayṭān because there is only two way in this life – way of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla and way of shayṭān. Way of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla – happiness, goodness, cleanness, light. Every good thing in this way. Other way, who people they are not looking for this way. Running to danger, running to fire, running to dirtiness, running to sadness, to stress, to unhappiness. This is where they are running there. It is real weird thing for human being to see this.

But for this we are saying, thanking AllāhNone to keep us in this happy way, in shā’a Llāh. We are in Ramaḍān also. After twenty or more worshipping for Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam was doing i‘tikāf – to be in mosque, masjid, for ten days until 'Īd. Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, heNone was doing this without cutting. But for ummah it is anybody can do, he can do. Big reward from this. And ten days or maybe less also, can do. One hour he can do. When you go inside mosque you say, "Nawaytu sunnata l-I‘tikāf." One hour, half day, two days, one day, three day, five day, until ten day you can do this i‘tikāf. And it is giving you more light, more reward from AllāhNone, which it is reward forever. Not like this people who running after this black badness and only for little life and after they will not find anything. We are asking Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla to keep us in HisNone way, in shā’a Llāh.
