A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina h-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad ya Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Wa-lā taj‘al fi qulūbinā ghillan li-lladhīna ‘āmanū rabbanā innaka ra’ūfun raḥīm” (59:10). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘aẓīm. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says not to keep evil and hatred in our hearts towards the believers. The believers are servants whom AllāhNone loves. HeNone says that this mustn’t be in our hearts. This du‘ā’ is both an āyah from the Qur’ān and a du‘ā’ that is recited. It is a beautiful one, a very beautiful one; all of them are beautiful. This is one of the beauties. Because a believer should not keep anything in his heart towards another believer. Of course, human beings encounter all kinds of opposition. A person is harmed or someone does something to him. Since it is a matter of this world, it must remain outside the heart. The heart is the place and abode of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. The heart of a human is a place reserved for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. That’s why those things shouldn’t enter the heart.

As we said, human conditions happen. There are fights or disputes; all kinds of things happen. But don’t let grudges and hatred stay inside you. Keep them outside. Don’t let your heart darken. Don’t let it harm your heart. The heart is a place of light. It is the place of all goodness for people and believers. That’s why, any badness done to it will harm you. Therefore, it must be kept clean. The holy awliyā’, the prophets, all the beloved servants of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla order and advise this: purification of the ego. Purification of the ego means cleansing the ego. Cleansing the ego is from the heart. The heart is the most important. Therefore, anything can happen, outside of the heart. But you shouldn’t keep it inside. If anyone reminds you of this… Our shaykh always reminds us of this. But when other people also give it as an advice, that advice is the greatest and most beautiful advice. You should accept it. No matter how righteous you feel or how angry you are, accept that advice and don’t put those things into your heart. Don’t put them in your heart. Because they are the real poison. Poison. “If the heart is corrupted, the whole body is corrupted,” says our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Therefore, keep your heart clean. Keep away from grudges, hatred and evil.

The other things can be resolved. But the heart is more difficult to correct. That’s why, let’s keep the heart clean without taking any risks, in shā’a Llāh. Let’s not place hatred or grudges towards anyone in our hearts, towards the believers. Towards a non-believer you must love for the sake of AllāhNone, and you must hate for the sake of AllāhNone. There is no need to love those who hate AllāhNone and those who do not love AllāhNone. We say, may AllāhNone improve them. May AllāhNone protect us.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying in Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n, “Wa-lā taj‘al fi qulūbinā ghillan li-lladhīna ‘āmanū rabbanā innaka ra’ūfun raḥīm” (59:10). AllāhNone, this is praying… āyah, verse from Qur’ān. We must, it is very valuable. All Qur’ān, all ḥadīth valuable. And they are giving us the best way for living. Happy, here and hereafter. What is this? What meaning? Meaning – to keep your heart clean against mu’min. Not to be, put any hatred, any anger for believer, for mu’min. “Keep your heart safe, clean, because heart, it is place for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.” Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla,

HeNone said, ḥadīth qudsī, nowhere, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone, we cannot say any place for HimNone, no place. But HeNone said, “No, no any place can fit MeNone.” HeNone is greatest, the greatest. You cannot imagine, you cannot this…for this. But HeNone is saying, “Only MyNone believer servant's heart, it is, INone can be, fit MeNone.” This, for this, you must be careful to not put any bad thing in your heart, especially to hate somebody from believer, for mu’min. Of course, there is... thing, you, make you angry, you make [may] be sad. People they can do this, they can make bad thing for you or against you or take some from, many thing from you, oppressing you. Can be mu’min also, this people. So, for this, you don't put this in your heart. Keep it away from your heart. Heart, for AllāhNone. Other, for dunyā, you can manage without putting in your heart. This is very important, because heart, it is place which it is very important and it is the most… like engine – if it is broken, everything broken.

Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, “It is one piece of meat, when it is good, all everything good, in your body and your spiritual. And when it is broken, everything broken – from your heart, for health, and from your body.” For this, we must be, keep our heart clean from hate, from anger, from… Of course, we are human being. AllāhNone create us to be angry, to be sad, to be this, but for people there is some place, keep it away from this place. This what you… you have from human being attribute or thing you do. Everything you can do. But in the end, keep your heart clean.

And whole prophets, whole awliyā’, whole scholar, ‘ulamā’, they are advising you for this. So maybe somebody, not ‘ālim, not anything, he can give you advice. Don't be angry with him. You thank him, for what he give you advice for this, and you be happy with him. No, no problem from anybody coming. What true and what is right, you must accept. Don't follow your ego, say, “This – not, he can… not ‘ālim, he not good.” No. Just, if you find something, you can follow. There is some… many, many thing, sometimes you follow, sometimes you cannot follow. You must be careful for – when you find right thing, you don't look, make it not important. If it is right, you follow, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone make us to, our heart clean and nobody, no any hatred for believer for mu’min, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
