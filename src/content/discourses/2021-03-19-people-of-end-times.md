Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Allah has assigned this holy day of Friday and gifted it to our Holy Prophet. It’s the most virtuous of days and because of this, as our Holy Prophet is the most blessed and most honorable amongst all creation, Allah has gifted it to him. Its night and day are holy. And for anything done on Friday, more rewards are written compared to other days. So we need to make use of this. We need not to miss Jumah Prayer.

We keep saying people have no idea what’s going on around the world. Truly, now the people of end times have no idea what’s going on, they only care about themselves and their ego. The whole world is only thinking about themselves and their egos. They don’t think about anything else. Also shaytan has come up with gadgets and devices that are appropriate for them, and so they just go with that and think about nothing else. If you say that they’re happy, no they’re not happy. Because no matter how much you try to make their ego happy, it won’t be happy. The Nafs (ego) takes you to a bad ending. Control your ego and obey the commands of Allah in this world so your end may be well and so everything may be good for you. If you abide by your ego, now we see strange people coming up in the news every day doing such strange and weird things. We are astonished. Can a human really do this! Let alone a Muslim, can a human do such a thing that even animals don’t do! If there were no religion, there would be nothing; neither morals nor manners would be left.

Therefore, we need to pay attention to these days and honor them. Allah has honored them and we should do the things that Allah loves and commands. We need to honor and follow the beloved ones of Allah. This is the only way for safety and salvation; we can’t be saved any other way. It is both the month of our Holy Prophet (Sha‘ban) and Friday today so it’s a day given to the Ummah (nation) of our Holy Prophet. We should honor it and know its value since it is good for us. Now there are plenty of people who are shaytans acting as if they are truthful. They claim to be doing good but they’re only doing bad. There are plenty of these people. They keep saying: “This is Bidʻah (innovation) and that is not acceptable” and they make people keep away from religion and Iman (faith). When there’s no faith left, it’s of no value. A human has no value without faith even if the whole world was theirs. As long as there’s no faith, they have no value. Anybody who has no value in the sight of Allah is worthless. May Allah give our faith more strength and increase our love for our Holy Prophet, Allah and the Awliya insha’Allah.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq. Al-Fatiha.
