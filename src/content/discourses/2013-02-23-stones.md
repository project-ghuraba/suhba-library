Marhaba O our attending brothers, our friends, our children, our brothers, the people of Sa'adah who have faith. Let us say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim; open our assembly with the Bismillah and Nur comes upon us. Joy, relief come to our hearts. Nur comes down on our faces. May strength and power come to our organs through the Divine Grants of Allah Almighty. May honour come and Allah Almighty grant us power for honourable works. What can we do? Our Grandshaykh would say like this, and it is from Shah An-Naqshbandi Hz: When a believer meets another believer, they should make a sohbat, they should make a good talk. They should not be wild. One says Marhaba. Say "As-Salam Alaikum", greet with Salam and safety comes down on you. Say Marhaba, that Marhaba is the greeting of the people of Paradise for each other. Angels also say Marhaba to the believers. When you say Marhaba, you expand, you find relief.

We say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim and a door opens. We don't know anything but with the barakah of Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim, a door opens. The Bismillah is the beginning of every goodness, and is the treasure of every goodness. Teach this. Teach this. What opens every closed door is Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. The eight doors of the Paradise open with Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Who doesn't say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim remains in dhulmat, he remains in darkness. There is no beauty in his face. There is no barakah in his doings. There is no goodness in him. His doings don't succeed. Troubles and illnesses never leave him. Whatever afflictions there are in dunya, may Allah keep them away from us, descend on the person who doesn't say the Bismillah. The Bismillah is like a shield in this way. It protects the believers. The Bismillah came for this.

Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Allah Almighty granted His Ism Al-Jalil, together with His three Ism Al-Jalils to the children of Adam. It is a Divine Grant. With the Name of Allah every act is completed. Remember the Name of Allah first. Say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. The closed doors open. Do you want a comfortable life? The key to a comfortable life is Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. There is no one who says Bismillahir- Rahmani r-Rahim and is left in hardship; there is no one who is left hungry, jobless or unmarried. Whatever intention or desire one has, it comes into existence with the Bismillah: Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Allah Almighty created all kinds of blessings for His servants. Each blessing has a different kind of benefit. When we were small ones, they showed us a kind of herb in the meadow. What did they say: "This is a bread tree". Have you ever heard of this? No. They say "This is a bread tree". It is a tree, it was a nice herb. It would grow in the meadow. Who eats it would get full. Who eats it would be comfortable. There are also such nabats. They call it "plant" in the imitated language. It is wrong. The meadows have all kind of different nabats. Each one of them has a different benefit.

When does it give you benefit? Say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim and it makes you not be in need of doctors. You won't have any stone or other thing left in your body. There is no surgery in Islam. If you will treat Muslims, say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim seven times and blow it on him. His pain goes away. His discomfort goes away.The dress of the Nur of Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim may be dressed on you. Say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim and your every organ revives. The heaviness on you may be lifted. You don't say "My head!", or "my other places". Who gets up with the Bismillah does not suffer from headache. If you don't say it, even if you take a handful of pills your troubles don't go away. You can't be saved from hardships.

Allah Almighty granted us the key of treasures. Say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim Yahu! It is the Ism al-Jalil of Who created you. Say it Yahu! Say "O My Lord! Let me remember Your Ism Al-Jalil and may You grant me: Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. May the closed doors open Ya Rabbi. May our troubles go away Ya Rabbi". "Then O servant, accept Me and remember My Ism Al-Jalil. Who acts with My Ism Al-Jalil will not be deprived". Who is the deprived one? What will open the treasures of all kinds of blessings is Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Say this, remember Me. Remember Me, so that whatever I created may give you benefit. So that they keep you revived and you may be fresh. So that you may not be idle. So that your face may be enlightened. You won't need any powder, blush or shaving. Men have plucked their moustaches. Do they have beard anyways? They have no beard and no moustache either. Nothing.

Allah Almighty dressed Nur on men, granted them as adornments. Man's moustache and beard are their adornments. Male lions have a mane. They are majestic. The female lion has no mane. Their appearances are different. Why should you not be the male lion? Allah Almighty made you as a lion against shaytan, against your ego. Why don't you stand like a lion? They are not being like lions but becoming the donkey of shaytan. "Get on us" they say to shaytan. "Make us go around". "Say what I say and I will make you go around". All the kinds of dirtiness there is on earth is in their mouths, on their faces, in their bellies, in their hearts... They have all kind of dirtiness. When you say Bismillahir-Rahmani r-Rahim you have no troubles left, all hospitals close. Doctors are left idle, they quit their professions and say "let us open a grocery shop instead." Instead of selling medicine, open a grocery shop. Collect the blessings of Allah and sell to those who can't collect them, so they eat and make shukur to Allah.

Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Ya Rabbi, may You dress on us the armour of the Bismillah so that shaytan can't command us. Would you like this? Yes, I would. Then say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim and shaytan can't command you. How easy it is. If you don't say it, shaytan won't get off your neck. If you look at his face, you get disgusted. Your face becomes ugly, becomes black. It won't go away with blushes, with powders, with cremes, with shaving...Men look like women. Yahu you are man! Can a male lion and a female lion be the same? Why do you pluck the hair in your face? If it was not necessary would Allah Almighty give man moustache and beard? He (swt) wouldn't give at all and you would be like women too. Would you like to be a woman? No. If you don't want, why do you pluck the hair from your face? Why don't you keep them like the mane of male lions and you look majestic, shaytan will be scared from you. Now you are scared from shaytan.

Say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim and your work succeeds. Say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim and you get your health back. Say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim and the door of treasures opens for you. Say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim, make sajda to Your Lord and gain dignity. Blessings will come to your home. Evil will not live in your homes. Now people in their homes see each other like enemies, they see one another like snakes. Ladies see their husbands like this and husbands see their wives like this. And now also, what will erase this is Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Before ladies would say like "Go in safety, O Effendi. Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim, here you go" and open the door for him. "Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Go in safety and come back in safety O servant of Allah. May you favor us". How beautiful was their manner. What a beautiful manner. They removed it. They made the value of women under peoples' feet. Men themselves made their value zero. Therefore they never find any goodness, they never reach any goodness.

There is a key for everything. The key to every treasure is Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Say it and walk. Don't say "I can't find a job, I am jobless". There are countless, endless jobs in dunya. "Well, we are working also". "What do you do?" "We go out for marching every night. We scream, shout. We swear at the government, we swear at anyone we come across". "What is it that you want?" "What we want is; whatever the ones before us wanted, we wanted such things too". The governments had no gold left in their hands anymore, had no silver left. And what did they do when they couldn't manage? They said "Let's print it on paper. This one is a 100, this is 1000. This is 50". This has no validity, this is clear lying. This is directly a deceit of shaytan. Remove all this, say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. So you can manage your life, you can keep good conduct with your Lord.

Say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Pray, make sajda and go out of your house saying "O Lord, I seek my provision" and your provision falls right in front of you. You can find gold in front of you, or you can find precious stones in front of you. In the old times, a street cleaner was carrying the trash at night. When he emptied up the trash, he found something in the size of an egg. He turned it around and looked. "This can be something important but what is it? Let me take it" he said. They call now garbage man. So he took it and said "Let me ask what this is". He took it to the grocery shop and showed it to its owner. He said "This is a strange thing but I don't know what it is. Take it to my neighbor's shop". And that man was a spoon seller. He looked at it and realised it is a strong thing. He said to him: "I give you a spoon and you give this to me". He said "Ok, what can I do with it anyways? It is just a stone". He gave him a spoon and he left happy saying: "We found a spoon. A spoon appeared in the trash. It was a stone but we got a spoon for it, what can we do with a stone?". And the man who took it said "Let us investigate what this is". The spoon seller took it to the jewelry shop. When he showed it to the jeweler, he said "This is a beautiful thing but you or I can't do anything with it". And then the spoon seller said "You give me a spoon and take this stone so that it will have a use. Why should I keep it for nothing?" The jeweler realised that it was a diamond with no equal or match. He said "Let me give you two spoons". "I have no spouse, one spoon is enough for me also". He said "No, I give you two, that maybe you can use it for your spouse later". So the poor one took it and left. That man saw and realised what it was. So he took it to another jeweler. That jeweler looked and said "This is a diamond that has no match or equal". So from him it reached all the way to the Pasha of the time. And when the Pasha got it he was astonished and said "We may present this to the Sultan". When he presented that diamond which was the size of an egg, the Sultan was totally surprised. They call it the spoon seller's diamond. I don't know if it still exists or was stolen. We don't know. An authority may know.

That man saw a stone but not every stone is the same. That one is a stone and this one is a stone also. That stone's worth is this much, the worth of a black stone is under your feet. But this one is a diamond. It is a diamond. We are sent such a jewel that no diamond's value can be compared to it: Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Yet we leave it like that foolish garbage man, or spoon seller? This is not reasonable. There are the jewels which are the Grants of your Creator to you: there is gold, silver, valuable diamonds, ruby, emerald, zabarjad, pearl, diamond... You leave this and continue to sweep the streets? You still continue to be a garbage man? This is a shame for mankind. What opens the door of every treasure is Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. It is a jewel that can give at least 1000 times more than the spoon seller diamond. You can't imagine what it can give at most. Now people don't know the value of the Bismillah. If they knew, the world would be at rest.

Bada'tu bi Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. O our Lord! We started our work, our day,by remembering Your Ism Al-Jalil. Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim, may You provide for me O Lord. Provide for and feed my family, my children O Lord. I make a service and my family can live comfortably. Say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Even if all the jewels in the world come together, they are zero compared to Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Who says Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim doesn't get sick. He has no troubles left, no debt left. But what can we do? Shaytan is pushing mankind, to keep them away from the servanthood of Allah. Be a servant to Allah: the Owner of the Heavens and earths, Who created you. Why don't you make sajda for Him? Why don't you keep His Order? Why don't you say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim? Say, so that the doors of every treasure may open for you.

O our Lord, may You send us the holy servants who will make us accustomed to this. May You send us the Sultans who will teach us these ways, who will make our people walk on this way O Lord. May we be in comfort. May we not be needy of any doctor or judge. Let us say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. How beautiful, how beautiful. O our Lord. Bismillahi r-Rahmanir-Rahim. Bismillahi lladhi la yadurru ma`a Ismihi Shay'un fi l-Ardi wa la fi s-Sama'i wa Huwa s-Sami`u l-`Alim. How beautiful, how beautiful. Everywhere is filled with pharmacies. But Allah Almighty created a cure for every trouble, every illness. They don't look to it and inside the capsules there are whatever Vitamins, or sulphates or vaccines... All kinds of things. Shaytan cheated people like this, made them miserable and dishonoured. And they're still running after shaytan.

Say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim and Allah will grant you everything you need. He(swt) is already granting you everything. Make sajda for the Lord. O our Lord, don't deprive us of sajda. And may You send the servants, the Sultan who will teach these people the sajda. May You send the Sultan who will order us to say the Bismillah. May You keep those who are heedless of the Bismillah away from us. We may say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. How beautiful, how beautiful if we say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim.

They make me speak with the barakah of the Bismillah when I have no strength. I am not reading you a book but we are saying what is in the book. Masha Allah to who listen and keep it. Otherwise they will run in the streets like animals that break free. Instead of screaming in the streets enter a mosque, or even if it is in the street say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim, Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim and walk up and down. Enter a mosque and your every doing succeeds. But shaytan doesn't allow this. O our Lord! Remembering Your Ism Al-Jalil is the greatest honour for us. May You not let us forget it. May You not leave us in the hands of who have no Bismillah. May You set us free from the hands of who are slaves to shaytan O Lord. May our mosques be filled with the jama'at. May our homes be full with blessings. May our pockets be full with gold. May we be in health and wellness. May You not make us be in need of doctors or judges.

Ya Rabbi Ya Rabbi Ya Karim. For the honour of Your Beloved O Lord, for the honour of the Sultan of the Prophets, may You send us the Sultan who will teach us our way O Lord. You are Subhan. You are Sultan. May You send us one who will rule us on Your behalf, who says Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. All who don't say it will vanish. May Allah protect us from the dirty ego, from the evil of shaytan. If you want barakah, say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. If you want goodness, say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. If you want a comfortable life, say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. If you want peace in your home, say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. What can we say?

O Allah, forgive us. May You send us from Your holy servants who will teach us this. Send a Sultan O Lord. Send a Sultan O Lord. May this tiredness be lifted off us. Let us say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Bismillahi lladhi la yadurru ma`a Ismihi Shay'un fi l-Ardi wa la fi s-Sama'i wa Huwa s-Sami`u l-`Alim. Heavenly Grants descend on who recites Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. His day goes well, he finds peace in his home and he finds comfort outside.
