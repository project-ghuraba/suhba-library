As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

This month is one of the honored months which are called Ashhuru l-hurum. Dhu l-Qa’da, Dhu l-Hijjah and Muḥarram - these are the months of Ḥajj. And also the month of Rajab, which comes separately. There is no permission for war in these months. Certainly, if you are attacked, you may respond to them.

This is apparently. And spiritually, there are days in them which are full of great Thawab. They are respected - Ashhuru l-hurum. Hurum means respected. And these are the months when it is forbidden to shed blood and to make war. As we said, if the enemy attacks you, it is alright to defend against them.

Of course, in this time, there is no humanity anymore. Not only considering the religion, they are not considering anything. Oppression has reached its peak. Everything has its time of ripening. And this world is ripe now. Akhirah and Qiyamah are approaching after this. 

اقْتَرَبَتِ السَّاعَةُ وَانشَقَّ الْقَمَرُ
<br>“Iqtarabati s-sāʿatu wa n-shaqqa l-qamar”<br>
“The Hour has drawn near, and the moon has been cleft asunder.” (Qurʼān 54:1).

This was said nearly 1,500 years ago in Qur’an ‘Azimu sh-Shan. It is near. Qiyamah is near. And it is closer day by day.

Therefore, we must benefit from these blessed days. It is a great benefit for people, for Muslims to do all kinds of goodness, to worship, to obey Allah and to avoid sin in these holy months. To approach Allah is the greatest benefit for mankind. And the greatest harm is to be away from Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. May Allah protect us. May Allah not separate us from the right way. May these days be blessed for Muslims, for people who show respect inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
