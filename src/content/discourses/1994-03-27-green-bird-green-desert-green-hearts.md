La ilaha illAllah. La ilaha illAllah. Muhammadan Rasulullah. Alaihi s-salatullah. La ilaha illAllah. La ilaha illAllah. La ilaha illAllah. Muhammadan Habibullah. Alaihi s-salatullah. La ilaha illAllah. La ilaha illAllah. La ilaha illAllah. Muhammadan Nabiyullah. Alaihi s-salatullah. wa salamu. Ziyadatan li sharafi n-Nabiyyi sallAllahu alaihi wa sallam Mashaykhina Hadhrati ustadhina lana mahum. wa lil-mu'mina l-Fatiha.

Hadhrat Sughuri, may Allah sanctify his soul, when two thirds of the night had passed and one third was remaining - 2-3 hours before the morning prayer, he would get up and sit in his holy room and recite La ilaha illAllah. La ilaha illAllah. La ilaha illAllah. Muhammadan Rasulullah sallAllahu alaihi wa sallam. And his town, which is approximately like Famagusta, just like when the whistle of a big train shakes all around, when Hadhrat Sughuri recited tawhid, the whole town would hear it and wake up. By his miracle, he needed neither a horn, nor anything else required for a siren. Do you understand?

When you say La ilaha illAllah, our Sheikh Sultan ul-Awliya says that Janab ul-Haqq creates a bird, that is an angel, an angel in the form of a bird. It's all green. If anyone looks at its greenness and beauty, whoever is not prepared with give up his soul. When we say la ilaha illAllah, that angel is created here and becomes a green light. La ilaha illAllah. La ilaha illAllah. It reaches immediately to the station of Jibra'il, alaihi salam, and lands there. Then it starts reciting la ilaha illAllah. La ilaha illAllah. La ilaha illAllah. Its saying of la ilaha illAllah is not like ours. The first la ilaha illAllah gives power to the second la ilaha illAllah, and it comes on a higher note. The third appears with an even higher note. The fourth is with much more power. The sound of that tawhid spreads around inside the skies and it pleases the angels. A nice smell comes with it.

That angel recites la ilaha illAllah like clockwork until Qiyama. How many millions, billions or trillions of times it will recite until then will be written in your book. You say once and Janab ul-Haqq creates that angel with His great favours, and it continues on your behalf. Therefore, no dhikr ever stops. We start it, JanabAllah makes it go on. How will you enter paradise? How can you enter by saying once? With the power of that angel and its tawhid, JanabAllah grants you its sawab/reward and you enter paradise with it. May Allah make us among those entering the paradise. If we don't enter paradise, we are in trouble. We are in big trouble if we don't enter paradise. Allah Allah. Allah Allah.

Who won't enter paradise will be in big trouble. What happens if they keep a person in an airless place? There is even no death for him there. If they don't put him in the fire and just keep him in an airless place and torture there - if he's not put in fire, how much torture he will have. May Allah keep us away from hell and keep hell away from us. Don't do the deeds of hell. Don't do the things, which will take you to hell. You have a mind. We're not innocent. We're not angels. We make mistakes. We can be ignorant and lazy. We leave required things incomplete and we run after unnecessary things. This is an attribute of our ego. It doesn't like when it is ordered to do something. Why? Because when it is ordered to do something, it comes hard on a person's ego and he wants to escape it. He doesn't like it or becomes lazy. "We will do it later. We will read it later. We will pray later. We will fast later. We will wake up later. We will do tomorrow". Ya Hu, tomorrow is not free, tomorrow is full too. When you say "I will do later", you are not free later or tomorrow or another day.

You have service tomorrow too. As we are servants, we have servanthood every day. Don't you have housework every day? "Let me do tomorrow, not today". Who will do tomorrow? Not you? "I don't want to wash these dishes today. Let me wash tomorrow." Tomorrow you will have as much. This is a fable. You will have dishes of two days. "I will worship tomorrow. We are still young. Let's dance a little more at weddings, then we will cover our heads. Then we'll sit like ash and start praying." MashaAllah to your brain. Therefore, don't leave the work of today for tomorrow. Tomorrow is not free, tomorrow there's work too. If you can't do one day, how will you do the work of two days? A man doesn't think. Not only sometimes, but always a person is in heedlessness. He is heedless. It seems easy to say "I'll do it tomorrow". Therefore, even officials get used to "come tomorrow". "Come tomorrow. Yes, sir? Come tomorrow". Bukra, never-ending tomorrow of Arabs. Where are Arabs? Tomorrow. "Ta'al bukra" - "Come tomorrow". Say bosbais, aurion. How they say tomorrow in German? Morgen. Morgen. Don't leave today's work to tomorrow.

Do your servanthood on time. Be awake. It is easy. Janab ul-Haqq loves it. Now this angel ascends there and makes dhikr for us. It doesn't skip it. It doesn't get bored or tired. The more it makes, the more love grows. One can see the change right away. And when he sees the reward for making tawhid, his desire increases. Yes. Ask for servanthood ordered by Allah. What is forbidden, people run after it. That's why, we were saying that a man's ego says "We will do later" when it comes to praying. He had a murid... Haji Hanim fell asleep already. Hadhrat Sughuri had one murid, he was a master. He would stand in front of him every day when adhan was called, and would take his apron off and didn't finish even a tiny bit of work. That man used to come in front of him and ask to release the string which was used to build a wall. You know it. The wall master Ali Effendi would say "Kemal Usta, ya Hu, finish this row so that you find it ready after you pray." That murid was telling him "the Adhan was just called. I'm going to pray." "No, finish this. Then go." How merciful you are to me. You feel for me so much. Put this apron on, finish this rock by the time I come back so that I'm relaxed".

He looked when he came back, the rock was still there. He said "May Allah throw so many rocks on your head. If you felt for me, would it be hard for you to take this rock and put it there?" "As I am going to do it anyway, there is nothing but you make me be late for my prayer. As I am the one who will do it, let me pray first. Then I'll come back and finish it." In fact... MashaAllah. In fact, the supporting angel comes to the one who offers his worship to Allah in the first place. Sheikh Effendi told me one story. This is a good one, Haji Osman. It is for those who don't pray too. Who doesn't pray should pay attention because the matter of praying is important. When you enter the grave, a dragon will come to your grave at the time of the morning prayer. At the time of the morning prayer of the first night, a dragon will come and ask why you didn't do your morning prayer and pinch you until the noon. When the noontime comes, it will ask why you didn't do your noon prayer and knead you like dough or clay pushing underground and taking up. When the afternoon comes, it will ask why you didn't pray, until the evening. When evening comes, it asks why you didn't pray, until the night. If you don't pray at night, it will repeat again until the morning.

For 24 hours that dragon will eat, bite, poison the man in the grave. It won't allow him to open an eye five times a day up until Qiyama. We seek refuge in Allah. Pray. Don't leave prayers. If you can't wake up for morning prayer, pray when you get up. No one should mess with prayers. Pay attention to praying. Praying is the right of Allah. It can't not be done. It's not your right or mine. It's an order of Allah. You must do it. If you don't do it, they'll make you pray in the hereafter. You're questioned straight after being buried.

Grandsheikh told us one story. There was a man with a carpet factory. He had several factories with workers. He produced 100 carpets every day in his factories. However, his workers... he hired only those who prayed. He didn't hire who didn't pray, saying they were trouble. He said some trouble would happen and didn't hire a man who didn't pray. Haji Effendi, don't let non-praying people in. Keep them outside your door. "Grass doesn't grow where you step, so don't come inside. Otherwise, our machines may break down." Did you understand, Haji Effendi? He accepted only with that condition and didn't want a non-praying man there. The work stopped every time it was time to pray during the workday. They took ablution. "We will pray in the mosque of this factory. We will make jamaat with the imam and perform salat. Then we'll pray and return to work". He wanted such conditions and kept this method.

To be a lesson for us, one man said "Ya Hu, let us not stop the work completely. Let us make a rule so that some people pray later and the factory doesn't take a break. They will come, pray and leave. That way there will be no gap. So there won't be a jamaat, but everybody will pray and go. Probably the time we spare for salat is too much. We should include it to working hours so that our efficiency increases." And he made them leave jamaat. And he asked them to try hard. So they did. When they counted their production in the evening, they had eighty pieces. Whereas it was hundred pieces before, it decreased to eighty. He said it is impossible. "This means you weren't careful. If so, tomorrow I will make you work the whole day without any breaks for praying in order to complete today's quota tomorrow." The poor people worked and worked and worked without a break. The man asked to make a count in the evening. They said they made sixty pieces. It had decreased to sixty pieces.

This is not a story, it is true. Our fathers' work was successful because our fathers cared about worship more. They cared about Allah's order. Their desire was not to collect the dirt of this world. It was the order of Allah. They ran towards Allah's order and didn't run away from it. Our difference from them is this. They ran after Allah's orders. We run away from Allah's orders. Turks of this time... Ottomans used to run to Allah's orders. And Allah made them respected. They ruled the world. Their swords were sharp. They are leaving Allah's order now for 70 years. They don't even allow them to go for juma. Think about it. They don't allow juma. And Cyprus is there. So many people complain to me. They put students in independent classes like gymnastics, or music, or drums. They call it a class and keep them for meetings and don't give permission. If one is an officer, his chief doesn't permit. If a soldier, his commander doesn't permit. Employer doesn't permit his employee. Before, we had permission in Cyprus during the time of unbelievers. British are gone, Greeks are gone. Neither British chiefs nor Greeks chiefs, they didn't stop the police or students, or workers or chiefs from going to juma. And these now... Therefore, they will take away from them.

The man said "O children, we made a big sin. However, as I started this as a lesson, I take it on myself. I made you pray late salat twice. But I did this, May Allah forgive us, I wanted to show it to you. Don't think that you can increase productivity by working more. No. It wasn't by working more. Janab ul-Haqq was sending supporting angels that you were producing two instead of one. It's not from you. You must understand it. See, I made you work without praying, the production dropped in half. I showed it to you so that you see how efficient you were when you kept your prayers five times and with jamaat. May Allah forgive me and you." Therefore, these people think they can earn more by working more. Because they know a lot, they are mistaken a lot. Whereas, if they keep Allah's orders first, all their work will be successful.

Now if a farmer tries hard for the grains to be good, will he be deprived of straw, Haji Osman? Because when the head of the grain is good, its straw will be accordingly good. However, if someone thinks about straw and not about grains, he will have straw and no grains. Even that straw will be little. When it doesn't give good grain, it will be just waste with little straw. But when it gives good grain, its straw will be strong too. Both grain and straw must be considered. Therefore, if a man puts akhira in first place, if he does it for Allah, he shouldn't think he will have less straw. No. This world is like straw. Never in history did our fathers complain about things being expensive. Our fathers would live counting coins. There is time when I grew up too. When

I was like this one, 12 years or 13 years old. We had 5 shillings in our country, for example. There were imams with a salary of 5 shillings, imams of villages. 5 shillings and 45 cents or 50 cents. 45 cents is 45 pence. 50 cents is half a pound. Those people were able to live on that. Before that they counted in gold. Because we called it bank notes of that time, 1 pound, 20 shillings. That was gold in fact. The Victorian gold. Victorians are with a horse on it. If they gave one of it, you'd be rich. It would cover expenses of a whole family. There is this 5 shilling coin now, 5-pence coin. I don't know if I have one in my pocket. This is five. Isn't it five? The new five is like that. However, it is a trick. It is not silver. This one is from nickel. They add some other chemical and get it. In fact, this is 5 pence. And 5 pence have no worth now. Back then, it was complete silver. It was three, three pence per day of someone, how much does it make per month? 90 pence. Right? Our Cyprus's money was 180 pence. This means an income of 10 shillings was a very good income. A person could live on it, just with this much. There was abundance and blessings. Everybody had enough. Now there is more money and more troubles, more possessions and less blessings. Why? People of that time used to run to keep the order of Allah. People of this time run away from the orders of Allah. Therefore, JanabAllah removed blessings/Barakah. People run after dunya like hounds. I ask "Did you catch something till the evening?" "No, we haven't yet, Hoja Effendi. We'll see tomorrow. We'll go tomorrow morning." And they run out of the door. They carry bags, don't they, Haji Osman? It's either a sack for common people, or samsonite for those who want to be different from common people. If one carries a samsonite, he is a registered one, interesting one. How does he exit from the door? As if he breaks the door with his speed by hitting with his samsonite. What is this hurry? He opens the door of a car with that samsonite and leaves quickly. He exists like a lightning. Did you manage, my friend? Now they go not only on foot, but by cars. Do they manage in taxis, Haji Osman? They are still tired in the evening with those samsonites. Back inside.

What a punishment? Because of that samsonite, the backs of most people lean on one side. If they carry it on other side, then on other side. And I say to carry it on both sides so that your back is relieved like Haji Cevdet Effendi's. See, he never carried a samsonite. I don't want anyone to bring me a samsonite this year. La hawla wa la quwwata illa billah. I thought of selling this one to get its money at least. People run after it. Can you hold it? You can't. Whereas in old times barbers used to carry bags. They had barber equipment inside it. They came and went with it. Now the whole world is like this. Before long, unless you keep the order of Allah, none of your work will succeed. It won't work out. As we said, a man wants straw. Look at the heads. Try to get the grains, you will have straw anyhow. There can't be grain without a stem. But if your goal is straw, you can't have grains. And a crop without grains is just a waste. It has no value.

Understanding has changed. The twentieth century put people's minds upside down. Who has mind won't be in a mess. Those who don't use their minds, their minds have shrunk. We are trying to explain such true things. We are trying,however, our work... There is Sahara Kabir in Africa. How many countries they built in Sahara Kabir now. Sahara Kabir was under French rule before. Sahara Kabir is a vast desert. You can walk for months in order to come out. There are some points in deserts from where water comes out. They call it vaha. What is't in English? Oasis. Oasis. There is water there, a couple of trees, greenery. By the wisdom of JanabAllah, there is water, a spring or a well that gives life so that the one who enters the desert doesn't lose hope, so that that hopeless poor man finds life in the water of that well and has rest, drinks from it, takes ablution and worships when he finds it. He shows it as points. However, what it can cover is just a small distance. It can't cover all Sahara.

The owner of Sahara, Allah waters it in one hour and it becomes green in 3 days. But the water of the oasis can make only a distance of five steps green. This is our condition today. There are some people now, few ones, so that people who search for truth are not deprived of it and don't lose hope. But they're not shown to all. Our door is like this. It's like an oasis. There is no strength to open for everybody. Therefore what we ask from Janab ul-Haqq ya Rabbi, You rule the clouds in the sky. Let them pass over this desert and water it. I went to Madina Munawwara for seclusion with Sheikh Effendi forty years ago. When we went for seclusion. We arrived at Madina Munawwara and passed through the desert. A desert like a river of sand. When we entered Madina Munawwara, by the wisdom of Allah, some mercy came down, everywhere became a lake. And like when snow falls in our countries, children go out to play in the snow, children went into that water pooled by the flooding rains, to refresh and play. Most of them didn't have shoes anyway. It rained for three days and three nights.

After we completed our seclusion of forty days, I was passing through the same desert, Haji Effendi. SubhanAllah, the beauty of the desert was of all colors you can imagine. Hills were covered with green grass, then flowers were everywhere of different colors, field was green. I said subhanAllah, ya Rabbi. This desert was a desert of sand. Where were the seeds of so many kinds? Not of only one kind. Where were they? In less than two months time, after forty days, a sandy desert was full of colorful flowers and green grass. I said ya Rabbi, how green can a desert become, if You want it. This is Allah's order. Can we deal with watering that desert by digging a well and pumping the water? Is there a chance?

Shortly, what we're waiting for now is such a mercy. It's the Possessor of Time who will come with complete mercy. Then our hearts which are like deserts will become green with the lights of faith and Islam. May Allah let us reach those days.

Bi hurmati l-Habib, bi hurmati l-Fatiha.
