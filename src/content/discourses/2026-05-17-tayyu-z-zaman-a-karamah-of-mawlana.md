As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says; the days of man pass quickly. Indeed, Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla, ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm, is the One who creates everything beautifully. Years pass, and man doesn’t even notice.

Yesterday, shukr to Allah, we had another trip to Bursa. We thought it had been a year or two since we last went, but it turned out to be four years. Those four years feel like they haven’t passed so long, yet four years have gone by. These days pass by without a person noticing.

But shukr to Allah, what’s important is to continue steadfastly on the beautiful way of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla, inshā’Allāh.

These days come and go; we make duʿā that they will be of barakah and that our lives will not be wasted.

Shukr to Allah, one of the karāmāt of Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim is Tayyu l-Makān (to transcend the boundaries of space). He says “Bismillāh” and travels from one place to another; he puts his foot here, and the other lands in Damascus. He says “Bismillāh” and goes to Makkah Mukarramah. He says “Bismillāh” and goes to Madīnah Munawwarah. That is Tayyu l-Makān (folding of space). That is, there’s no need for airplanes or anything like that; this is a karāmah of the awliyā.

And then there’s the second which is more unique, Tayyu z-Zamān; they expand time. They can accomplish many things within a short period. This is also a karāmah of Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim. That is, reaching everywhere and meeting with people in such a short time would not be possible in normal times. For example, Mawlānā lived for 92 years. He met with everyone as if he had lived 200-300 years; he went to everyone and met them, they came to him. They consulted with him, they received advice from him; this is also a karāmah of his.

Shukr to Allah we witnessed that. Of course, we cannot reach that level. We don’t have such karāmah, shukr to Allah. But the most important karāmah is to remain steadfast, to continue on the way. It is important to continue on the way. A person who walks steadfastly and sincerely, without getting tired, without giving up or getting bored, will always win, will always attain beautiful stations. They will attain the pleasure of Allah and the pleasure of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. 

May Allah make us all among those people. On this beautiful way, the way shown by our Shaykh, shukr to Allah, his karamāt are manifested every day. Wherever we go, we hear people saying, “We saw Shaykh Nāẓim in a dream; he told us to go there and continue on this way.” He is a means of guidance; his guidance continues. 

The tasarruf (spiritual authority) of awliyā exists during their lifetime. And after their death, their tasarruf becomes even greater. Therefore, they are not dead; they are beings who live more alive than us.

May Allah elevate and sanctify their secrets and their ranks. May we also walk steadfastly in their footsteps, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
