Audhu Billahi min ash-shaytan ar-rajim, Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Assalatu wa s-salamu ala Rasulina Muhammad, Sayyidil Awwalin wal-Akhirin. Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fi jami'a.

We may say a few words here, insha'Allah. What Shaykh Effendi has always been saying, for years - a qasida he heard from a majzub (dervish) "La tukthir li hammik, ma quddir yakun Allahu'l-muqaddir wa'l-`alam shu`un". Meaning, don't worry or think too much "What will I do, what will happen.." Allah does everything & people watch it like this. Only what Allah has destined happens, not anything else.

Like this, when I left 25 days ago I was thinking to return in 10 days. We planned things accordingly. But Allah's taqdeer (destiny) - we got ill & delayed. For 15 days, we couldn't come. We left with the intention that because of Mawlana's illness we were planning to return quickly. But Allah's destiny is different & people think and plan different things. What Allah has destined happens. And this illness is also from Allah.

Like Mawlana said before: "Wa Lillahi Junudu As-Samawati Wa Al-'Ard - And to Allah belong the armies of the Heaven & the Earth" (48:7). But Allah's armies do not only have guns, cannons & tanks. He has smaller armies as well as unseen ones. It is Allah's Destiny. Even now also, where we are sitting there are billions &trillions of these soldiers of Allah that we mentioned. If the order comes to them, not one person can survive! Allah has such a Power & Glory. And in our situation, maybe the order has come to one of them: "Make Hj Mehmet ill & make him laid up in bed a little bit". But when laid up, Alhamdulillah there were much worse situations than this..

And also, "Tafakkur sa`atin khayrun min `ibaadati alf sannah- Contemplation for 1 hour is better than 1000 years of supererogatory worship". And in this, it was a chance to think on the Destiny of Allah, on what the Shaykhs say- one can think more about these. It is the wisdom of Allah, when laid up in bed & can't move much, one's mind is much more available for reflecting. It was also was such a chance for us- like a training, discipline for us. Of course, as for Mawlana's illness it was very different- how many high maqams-ranks he has gained with his tafakkur. We did not suffer even the thousandth of what he went through. Mawlana went through such pain & suffering in his old age. In his 40 day seclusion, he experienced the biggest seclusion of all. Which means how much he was raised in rank, how much he was raised in station. Our Prophet says: "Don't ask for illness" but after it happens & cures, give thanks to Allah Almighty it has passed & gone by. May Allah grant cure for the illness. May Allah grant a good, long life to our Shaykh, insha'Allah.

Today's sohbat for, Shaykh Effendi every time speak one poem. He hear from one majzub. But he like too much, this poem. He said, Allah what He wants and it will be. Don't be very worry or you don't think too much - "What I will do what we will do, what it will happen"? Allah doing and people looking. Just they cannot do anything.

For this, when I went to Istanbul only for 10 days, I am very hurry to come here. Suddenly, as my father Shaykh Nazƒ±m Hz telling: "the army of Earth & Heavens, all for Allah". And this army not only who carrying gun or going by tanks, or this big army, no! There is a small army also, very small. Even there is one, you cannot see. Everywhere you can find. You cannot see but maybe by billions, more than billions. Here in this room also. When Allah want, nobody can survive from this. When Allah give order "attack this people, or attack that", you cannot see, nobody can survive. For this, only one small one maybe He give order. So suddenly we become ill. 15 days I can't come, for this.

So illness, everything from Allah. When Allah give order, even medicine.. Shaykh Effendi when he take medicine, he says "Bismillah, insha Allah this be shifa from Allah". Because many people they think, "I be- come good when I take this antibiotics" they say. But when we take antibiotics, even now we hear from new doctor - there is a new illness, this antibiotics not do anything for this. They are very worried about this. This Allah what He wants, it happens.

So Alhamdulillah, it was very small compared to Shaykh Effendi's illness. And this illness, when you cannot do anything, so you are thinking when you can't do. And this is also, to think 1 hour better than worshipping hundred years. Really it is. You think very much about everything. Especially about Allah's orders & mashayikh's (awliya) orders, about wisdoms. Alhamdulillah when you think this, you can not feel anything, just days passing. So Alhamdulillah, it was only little bit. Maybe even not one part from thousand part of what Shaykh Effendi suffered in this 40 days. Too big and Alhamdulillah he become better and his station & maqam, how it is going up higher and higher & higher. Alhamdulillah, he was not complaining at all.

Alhamdulillah, (Prophet says) don't want illness for yourself. But when Allah giving, this is from Allah. It is not bad. Allah don't give, insha Allah, big thing,big illness. Small is enough. What you do when Allah giving? You cannot do anything. You must be all thankful to Allah. Because Allah gives for mu'min (believer) servant every goodness. Even it is worst illness, also you must be thankful with this. Don't be complaining. Because Allah is generous. When He gives this & you be patient you cannot imagine what you won in akhirat and in your mashayikh's presence. When you not complain, they are very happy. If you complain,you will lose it. Allah don't examine (test) us, for this we cannot carry insha Allah. Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, Al Fatiha.
