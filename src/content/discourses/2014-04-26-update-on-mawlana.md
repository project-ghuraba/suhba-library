Subhanim Allah Sultanim Allah Nabim Muhammad alayhi's Salam Subhanim Allah Sultanim Allah Nabim Muhammad alayhi's Salam Subhanim Allah Sultanim Allah Nabim Muhammad alayhi's Salam

Salamu alaykum wa rahmatullahi ta'ala wa barakatuhu. Alhamdulillah, I am here to give you a good news, masha Allah, Mawlana Shaykh, he is generous to us that he start to use his miracle to cure and Alhamdulillah, by the baraka of Rasulullah, by the baraka of Grandshaykh, by baraka of Allah Almighty, Mawlana Shaykh he is well recover, masha Allah.

How I'm saying this? I'm saying this because Mawlana Shaykh situation it was so bad and now masha Allah it's climbing up, and up, and up and keep climbing but unfortunately, he is still in intensive care, he is under medication and Alhamdulillah, the treatment of medication going well. and plus of this, they reduce the medication, they reduce the medicine from him, so Alhamdulillah, that means good sign, so the heavy medicine at the moment it's not pumped to his body.

And I'm saying this, your Salawat, your Yasin Sharif, your reciting Quran on tahlil and tawhid/tamhid, and Ikhlas and sacrificing a lot of qurban to Mawlana Shaykh and feeding poor people and giving a lot of contribution for the poor people and giving sadaqa, zakat and all this kind of things is giving good tiding and good health and recovery for Mawlana Shaykh. Now we are still asking from you to keep in prayer and asking from you to keep in one heart, one soul and one love, it is Mawlana Shaykh.

This is my humble advice, today we are in the race. We are in the race. With who we are racing, this is important to know. We are racing with our Mawlana Shaykh to win. To make Mawlana Shaykh win and to win for his illness, to win for his life and to win for his good coming days, insha Allah. This is our race, this is our target.

We are not listening to anything called fitna, called things we don't want to hear. We want to hear good news, we want to hear the good things that Allah Almighty He is already granting for us that, we are not deserving it but Allah Almighty, He is the Generous One to give us such a things, to give us such a, they call it in Turkish language ‘dawlat’, that mean, the present of health. The present of power, the present of love of Mawlana Shaykh. I am asking from all of you, and first from myself, that, to keep praying for Mawlana Shaykh recovery, to keep praying for Mawlana Shaykh health, and insha Allah to keep that I will give you good news every time, every day, every moment we are willing and we are hoping to give good news for all of you, for all of us, and to make our day happy, because as I say the other day we are nothing without Mawlana Shaykh. Don't forget this. We are really nothing without Mawlana Shaykh.

Alhamdulillah, Mawlana Shaykh, as long as he is breathing, we're something. As Mawlana Shaykh he is still remaining in this life, we are something. May Allah give long life for Sultan, long life for our King, long life for our Crown, Mawlana Shaykh, qaddasallahu sirruhu al-aali.

Wa min Allahi tawfiq bi hurmati l-Habib bi hurmati l-Fatiha.
