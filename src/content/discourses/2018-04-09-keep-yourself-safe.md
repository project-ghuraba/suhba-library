As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa raḥmatu Llāhi wa barakātuh. A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīn wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

Shukr to AllāhNone, the rain of Rajab and the rain of April is blessed, they say. It has shifā’ and barakah. We have to accept it as a favour from AllāhNone and make shukr. We mentioned about this rain in order to make shukr to AllāhNone.

The main issue is that there are many ignorant people now and there are many insane people. A person with mind shouldn't deal with them. Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla is saying "Wa-idhā khāṭabahumu l-jāhilūna qālū salāman" (25:63). When the ignorant ones address you and speak, when they talk nonsense, you should say, "May AllāhNone give you peace". No need to talk to them and get into an argument. Because an ignorant one is ignorant. AllāhNone created them ignorant. You cannot correct them. There are people who can learn and people who cannot. There are people who understand the goal and those who don't. They don't get the logic. If you talk to them a little, then a quarrel starts. If you go further, as they are ignorant, they can hit you. Therefore, sometimes worse things can happen. Especially in this end of times, people have become too aggressive. You don't know what to expect.

Therefore, in order to avoid danger, don't hang out with the ignorant or insane person. You should say, "Let it be as you say," and leave in peace. Otherwise, as we said, you get into trouble for nothing. At the very least, you'll get upset. In the future, you will get into a fight. In addition, worse things can happen, may AllāhNone protect us. Therefore, it is the order of AllāhNone to not deal with ignorant ones. Don't try to explain something to them. Do it. Do your duty and leave by saying, "Be as you like."

It is dangerous times now. If you walk with strangers and something happens, say you're sorry and leave. They can't do anything. They will grumble and go away. But if you grumble, that will make it go further and get worse. Therefore, no need to throw yourself in danger. At least, no need to bother yourself. May AllāhNone help us. Because sometimes people can hardly hold themselves back. When they see some injustice or wrong, they want to correct it. It doesn't work out. Therefore, no need to get sad. It is the order of AllāhNone to us not to deal with ignorant ones.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh today raining it is April and Rajab. Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh was saying it is, April rain water it is shifā’ cure. He was collecting and was sometime drinking. But here it's clean air, no pollution in this area. You can collect. But in big city – once we try to collect and whole it was smelling like diesel because all from... So only for if you want, here you can collect but not everywhere. Because people they are listening, after going so Shaykh tell us this and we do and you cannot drink. Just we because it's raining, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are thanking AllāhNone. In raining of April for us, the old people they say also, it is like gold for earth, for human being. For everything it is very precious. We are thanking AllāhNone for sending us this rain. But our main subject not this one.

Nowadays people – there is ignorant people. The ignorant people, majority ignorant people. Before, ignorant people there was who are they don't know to read and write but nowadays the more dangerous one who are finishing university, finishing high school. This ignorant they are more worst than ignorant who they don't know to read or to write. But it is in majority and AllāhNone warning us from this people. He saying in Qur’ān, Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm: "Wa-idhā khāṭabahumu l-jāhilūna qālū salāman" (25:63). When ignorant people addressing you, don't, if you know they will, they are ignorant and they want, they will not learn anything. Only say "Peace" and don't speak to them – "Wa-idhā khāṭabahum," when they address – "Peace on you." Don't argue with them. Don't tell them to try to teach them what you know the good one.

Even you see they are doing bad thing, don't interfere because this people – you know which people they have accepting truth and who are stubborn and they don't want, only they want argue and make problem for you. Don't speak to them. Only say, "Okay what you are, you are accept, you free to accept. I not in... I will not discuss this with you." Even in street also many times people arguing with each other. After arguing, if they are ignorant it is not enough for to arguing they begin to fighting. Sometimes some from getting more angry, maybe making harming other. Sometimes more than harming, maybe they kill each other. For this Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla saying don't argue with this people and when you see ignorant...

Here this time many ignorant and they have also mental problem. And our people they don't know when – you cannot speak to somebody he has mental problem. And you speak from morning to evening he will not understand anything because he is, he has a problem, he has illness. But if you know people they will listen, and they ask for advice you can tell them what it is truth, what it is right, what is good, what is not bad. You can tell this but not to catch everybody and to try to tell them what is this. Don't do this. No need for this and order to don't speak to this people. From AllāhNone order because not everybody will be in right way. So when you are listening to this you will be safe. Even if you speak to this people, if you don't have anything by arguing, you will be angry with yourself, you will be stressed with this discussion you do with people and you... They shouting for you and you are, you cannot do anything. You will be stress and sad. So no need to for this.

Only look for people who are asking for advice, asking for good thing, looking for, searching for good people. You can be with them. But ignorant people let them away. You will be safe and they will be safe. AllāhNone make us to control ourself because many times when people seeing bad thing they cannot control themself. They interfere for this. After they become regret to, "Why we interfere? Our whole day it become bad day with this interfere with this people." Even when you are in the street and anything in traffic or in any place, don't argue with people because you don't know what will happen. Who are our, our order for us – keep away from dangerous: "wa lā tulqū bi-aydīkum ilá t-tahlukah" (2:195). This is now everywhere dangerous to speak to people, to argue with people. Don't interfere with this. Keep yourself yourself safe. You must for safety of your family, you look after family or after friend – it's enough for you.

Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
