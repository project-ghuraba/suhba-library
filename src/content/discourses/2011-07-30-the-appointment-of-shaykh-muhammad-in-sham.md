His (Shaykh Muḥammad ʿĀdil al-Rabbānī's) power keeps Anatolia, keeps the Balkans, Shām and the East. He has such a power now. Along with him, there are 40 more saints present in Shām. They are waiting for the time to stop the chaos now. When the time comes, finish, all of Shām will surrender. Allah Almighty will put fear in the hearts of Baʿthiyyin (ruling ʿAlawis). When that fear is put in their hearts, none of them can stay in there anymore. How to say? Even if you look for them to give them medical treatment you still won't be able to find even one of them. They will be gone... They will run so fast that their heels will hit their bottoms. They will not run at day time, but at night time. They won't wait the day time. Just put some fear in their hearts, no guns nor cannons needed then.

Now all the Holy Ones are ready. Let's see. They want you (Shaykh Muḥammad) too. In the apparent world there are no more Holy ones left there. Now there is power on Shaykh Muḥammad and he will be given more and he'll desire Shām. He'll say, "Ah Shām, Ah Shām..." Shām will be taken under control and will be ready for the coming of Sahib al Zamān, the Master of the Time. He will enter from Qadam (a district in Damascus, Syria). It is astonishing. It is said that he will enter from Qadam and say takbīr. The moment he says takbīr, all technology will stop. He overcomes. Now, here (in Cyprus), Shaykh Muḥammad Effendi is like in a room, not imprisoned, but it has become like khalwat (seclusion) for him and same is in Istanbul.

In Istanbul he controls the food given in the dergah. His mission/duty is not here, nor in Istanbul. It will open. We have Shām, Homs in our hand. There is also Hama and Aleppo. The people of Aleppo are now on the way of ṭarīqah, finish! Power is now all centered in one place. It is with you (Shaykh Muḥammad) now. They will start putting this power into action. Here (in Cyprus), is like a dungeon cell. Here is considered as khalwat place to keep him hidden. He still comes and goes.

The people of Shām are waiting for him now, their hearts will open. At that time, Hajji Muḥammad, we may get for you (a house), in the neighborhood of... if you like. If you don't like you may stay in the maqam of Grandshaykh. If you like, we may get something near that mosque. There are these old style residences, so many of them. They are like palaces and there is one of them, that I think Sultan ʿAzīz (lived in). You know that residence that Abdul Qadir Al-Jazayri built? Have you seen it? It is right next to Ruqiyya Sultan. It is a huge place and it is for sale. After these traitors are cleared out, we can go there. 

Shām will open and they will wait for Mahdi ʿalayhi s-salām. There are lots of people ready. Nowadays, nobody remains who has made khalwat (seclusion), baqīyyatu al-suyūf (Baʿath Party and atheists). All of them will be gone. The tasarruf becomes in your hands. What is the name of that place, what is the name of that dergah, which gives out food? 

Shaykh Muḥammad: Abu Shamat? 

Yes. All the holy ones are, in general, hidden. No one is left! There is no one left!

The Holy ones want it also (the opening of Shām). Whenever the order comes and it opens. Now, when the order comes and these Sufyāni people will be kicked out from there. Once they're gone, there'll be no need to keep Hajji Mehmet (Shaykh Muḥammad) in Istanbul. It'll be waste of time! The work in Istanbul is about giving this one dinner or that one food. Not urgent jobs. In the tekka, they're already handling the food given to poor people. But these cursed ones (in Shām), the Sufyānis in power, they are shaytans. They don't want you to open your mouth to say something. They don't want at all, but their tongues will also be cut.

"If fitnas start, then safety is in Shām."

Do you have al-Arbaʿīn, the hadith book about Shām? 

Shaykh Muḥammad: I have seen it

I think Jibril Haddad collected it? I have it as well. Al-Arbaʿīn, the hadiths about Shām. I think it is there (in Shām) - no one has touched our books inshā'Allah. In our library in Shām there are astonishing books. This man, our Kayyal, he is the first man to support you in Shām. The people of Shaghour, they are tough men anyways. When we say tough, there are low class ones, as well as holy ones among them. This (Kayyal) has been there and was sent to us through Allah's wisdom. He is the man who will handle the people of Shām. He won't be a burden on you but he carries your burden, and he has deep love inside him. You are the same age as him?

Shaykh Muḥammad: I think he is younger. 

How old is he? 40? 

Shaykh Muḥammad: I think he is about 45. 

Meaning, you are older than him, at least 5 years older. 

Shaykh Muḥammad: Maybe even more. 

Yes, it is so. You are physically strong, but your spiritual power is stronger. If you didn't have the power to go and rule in Shām, I wouldn't be given such permission.

It will open O Rukiyya. It was before you were born, maybe... Hajji Mehmet (Shaykh Muḥammad) was born at that time. We went to, I went to ʿAyn al Fijja (spring near Shām). It was astonishing. The place where the water comes out, extended from here, all the way to the end of our garden. It would come out from underneath. Like this. Now there is nothing left. Our mother Bilqīs (Queen of Sheba), the door of her palace was at that place. Such good water would come out from there. What is left now? And also, there is water flowing from up. It is Yazīd, Hajji Mehmet. The river Yazīd is the cleanest water. It was running above Muhājirīn. It has the cleanest water. But in Barada, people who were passing by would urinate in it. Poor people of Shām, they would sit here and there on Fridays, with their sheeshas, tea and samovars. They would sit and look around. They destroyed that area as well... Now, all the new, trashy things they brought recently.

Grandshaykh told me, Sultan al Awliyā, that in one night he will order. He has control over the group of jinns and he will order: Remove it! In one night, he will have all of it thrown into the sea. These buildings, these Nimrud towers, they call them skyscrapers. In reality they are Nimrud towers. They will throw them all away. They will arrange and the people of Shām will be as in the old arrangement until this fitna (tribulation) of the Dajjāl (Anti-Christ) appears and people gather there. After that, it will open again. After 40 days, none of those who gathered in Shām will remain. Everybody knows where they will go when they leave. The old Shām will be left like it was. Allahu Akbar.

Now, this means the movement is starting this year. Our shaykh, Aḥmad al Kayyal, is a preparation for it. No shaykh is left there. All of them are lazy and helpless, and the ones who are present there, they have nothing. How many years it has been since I went to Shām?

Shaykh Muḥammad: It has been 5-6 years. 

5-6 years?

Shaykh Muḥammad: I think it has been 6 years.

This man was sent with a great love and he has strong adab (good manners). Maybe his roots are Kurdi or Badawi, but they are strong. His roots are strong and he has strong adab. The people in his district are very sharp and alert and brave. Some of them are strong, they are not weak men.

The people of Shaghour fear him, respect him, and Mubarak Bahlul is there. There are so many maqams there, also Ahlul Bayt is close, Hajji Mehmet?

Shaykh Muḥammad: Yes, very close to there. 

It is very close, behind it. Also Hadrat al Shaykh Hasan. Now that I said it to Hadrat al Shaykh Hasan, he is also pleased that I said this news. Shaykh Hasan, we forgot his other name, Shaykh Hasan... What was his other name?

Shaykh Muḥammad: Are you saying Jibawi?

His masjid is in Haban? No? 

Shaykh Muḥammad: Where Grandshaykh stayed? 

Hadrat Jibawi is so pleased, what a strange thing.... He got so happy with this news. No one can pass beyond his border. If they try to enter Shām, they can't pass beyond him. Therefore they didn't allow the people of Shaghour to stand up and protest. They kept them, and said, "Stay calm. We will protect you. Don't interfere." Now, our Shaykh Aḥmad has been appointed over them. He is brave and strong and he has so much love. He has made a promise to serve Islām. Therefore, they ordered him, to make bayʿat.

The shaykh of Shām now will transfer the power to him (Shaykh Muḥammad) and he will distribute the power. Now you have authority for arranging and giving permission for for riyadah (training) and khalwat (seclusion). Dhul-Qaʿdah and Dhul-Hijjah these 2 months are the time for khalwat. Other than these times, permission will come to you and you will know it. When the permission comes, those people who had the sign for it they will come to you. You can put them in khalwat and riyada. Aleppo is under your control. Kurds love you very much. 

They made open to me an unexpected door of unveiling (futuhat) at night. I understood at that time, when they explained me, why he has come. My heart has found peace and happiness. Don't look at me, I am here there... We will see how far I have left for ākhirat, Allah knows. But you have started your service already. Beginning of this Muḥarram, the power has started opening. For Shām to open - they say, this fitna came to remove the dirt and ugliness in it.

Now Shām will be cleared out. None of those men will remain and no showy, indiscreet ladies will remain. In a night, Shām will return to its old condition. This one, she loves Shām too. Don't you love Shām O Rukiyya? 

Hajjah Rukiiya: Yes. 

There is no place like Shām. Poor ʿAdnān Effendi he can't do... He has spiritual power but his ability to move is now very restricted. Hishām Effendi can't handle it in Shām. He may handle the areas of Beirut, Lebanon. But they cannot rule over Shām. I am also looking after, in East and West, but this station is written on you (Shaykh Muḥammad). May Allah make it mubarak for you.

Shukr to Allah that this door for Shām has opened. It will open now. I may pray 2 rakats, for shukr. 

The relics of the Prophet will be delivered to him. Amān ya Rabbi. At that time it will be so crowded in Shām that there'll be no place left. As Prophet says, "Lucky is he who has, at the end of times, a place in Shām even if it's as small as a goat-shed!"

Al-Fātiḥa.
