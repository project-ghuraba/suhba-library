Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. "Wa ja‘alna min al-ma'i kulla shayin hayyin" (21:30) ('And We have made from water every living thing.') SadaqAllahu l-Azim. 'Sirru l-hayat fi l-ma'. (secret of life is in water) Janab-ul Haqq gave the secret of life to water. Janab-ul Mawla is saying: "We gave life to everything with water". Therefore, the ones who use water the most are Muslims. The more a person is in contact with water, the more he is lively and full of life.

Don't be afraid of water. Troubled people of this time are afraid of water. They are walking unclean and don't take ablution, don't wash their hands and faces. They became very dirty and run away from water. Does a man who fears water have value? Baraka is in Islam and it is ordering cleanliness. And what would these troubled people do, if they were told about it? They would raise their heads denying and say how can such a thing be? Cleanliness isn't important, washing isn't important and this and that. Whereas Islam is ordering to use water which keeps life and contains the secret of life. In every way these unclean people act against Islam. Everyone against Islam is a troubled dirty person.

Don't be afraid of water. Water gives life to soil, to plants, to animals, to people. When there's no water, there's no life. If water is gone, life will end. And when the human body loses its water, a person dies. While there's the water of life, a man doesn't die. The water of life is in the spine of a man. It is in the spine. The drop of water by which a man is created is the water of life. When it starts to dry, the spine of a person starts twisting, he starts getting older - that is the meaning of getting older. When it's getting dry, it means life is going towards death. While it's there, the body is alive. When that source starts to dry, a man becomes similar to a drying tree. Therefore, we have to take care of it. A man who can't control himself loses this source and lets it dry. He dries the source by misusing it. And when it's dry, you can't get more. It's given only once in the amount appointed by JanabAllah. If you don't manage it, it starts decreasing and becomes less. And when it's dry, it turns to a dry tree. Finished. That becomes a reason for death.

A body has water in it and it has the water of life which produces and sustains generations. And there is water by which a man is created. JanabAllah is saying "We gave life to everything with water". The beginning of life is water. What a secret it is. He sent life into water. We should thank Allah for creating us as Muslim servants. And He honored us with Islam that we became ones using water the most. We are using water in every way. A body revives in contact with water. Drink water and also wash your body with it. It brings freshness. No harm comes from water. Therefore, doctors sometimes tell us to drink a lot of water.

I met someone who was a Buddhist. He was the head representative of the Buddhist religion in Sri Lanka. I told him an advice of the old doctors - to drink hot water in the mornings to keep healthy. Old doctors are saying to drink water in the morning before eating. Drink hot boiled water. Of course, it can be tea or other plants and spices, but drink it hot, with hot water - tea or any other spices. They say to drink it. If not available, drink cold or normal water. He told me that he drinks 2-3 litres of water every morning. He drinks, drinks and drinks until his stomach is like a drum. After an hour has passed it wants to be released. Whatever is in the stomach and digestive system, it cleans out everything. He said "I am at such age and I never go to doctors. And I agree with what you say. It is a method I know and follow. Drinking a lot of water cleans all the digestive system and brings comfort to the body."

It gives liveliness inside the body. Water cleans everything, not leaving anything which prevents digestion and blocks the intestines. He said: "I do it every day. I drink 10-12 glasses of water every day". Indeed, when a man gets used to water, his condition is good and revived. The contact with water gives life to the body.

And "afdalu ṣadaqati ṣadaqatu l-ma'" among the things given in sadaqa, the most precious is the sadaqa of water. The Prophet, alaihi s-salatu wa s-salam, said there is no sadaqa as great as giving water. The most beneficial of all sadaqa is to give water to the thirsty, to give water to a thirsty man or animal or to water thirsty plants. All of them are virtuous. Our Sheikh Sultan-ul Awliya used to say this, may Allah sanctify his secret, if anyone brings water to anywhere, if anyone makes a fountain, for example, in old times, they would find water in the mountains and make a fountain there, they would collect water there for birds and insects to drink. People in old times used to build stone basins and make wells for shepherds. They made wells and put the buckets for watering animals. Also they used to make stone basins to fill with water for dogs, cats and birds to come and drink from it. They put water every day for them and take care of it. And there were many people who earned paradise by this.

And our Sheikh, may Allah sanctify his secret, says if anyone brings water to anywhere and makes a fountain, and a snake comes to drink from it, a snake is a poisonous animal, he will have 7 sawab/rewards for each drop - leave aside a worshipping man who comes, takes ablution, has a bath and drinks. How can you estimate the virtue of it? If a snake comes and drinks from it, Allah Azza wa Jalla is giving 7 sawab for each drop drunk even by a poisonous animal. It is sawab to give water to every thirsty creature. Can it be beyond a snake? It is 7 sawabs for every drop drunk by it.

Water is a big thing. It is big favour from Allah. It is under the manifestation of His honored Name Al-Hayy. And you can't hear a nicer tasbih than the tasbih of water. Everything makes tasbih of JanabAllah. But when people hear the tasbih of water, they fall in love with it. The tasbih of water is the best tasbih. It is the tasbih of the honored name Al-Hayy.

A believer should be like water. He should be clean like water. Just like there's life in water, a believer must be an elixir of life for other people. A believer is like water giving benefit to people by relieving their thirst and washing away both their hidden and obvious dirt. Thus a believer is like water. And water can fit in any vessel; be it small or wide, a vessel in different shapes, water can fit in it. Water can go in the narrowest places and it takes the shape and color of the vessel that it enters in but it doesn't lose its quality of being water. Water in the glass is like this. It took another shape in this vessel. If it is in a different vessel, it will take its shape but won't lose its quality of being water. When it is white, water seems white. When a vessel is green, it looks green but it's not. In a red vessel it looks red, but it's not red. A believer also looks like the community he is in, but he doesn't lose his characteristic of being a believer.

Therefore, being like water is an attribute of a believer. It is a sign of compatibility with everything together with his own benefit. A believer is not rigid. He is soft, smooth and easy. A believer is not difficult. A believer is not rough, he is easy like water. This is an important matter. It is enough for you now.
