Haji Anne, Sohbat in Turkish from 1990.

... “My Prophet is Muhammad Mustafa (sas). My Shaykh is this. My religion is Islam.” They make people say like this in the grave. When they say this from the outside, the dead one comes out of his bewilderment and is able to say it. There are 3, 7, 40, and 360 Holy Ones. Q: There are also 360? H.A. Yes. If the Qutb passes away they take one from the 3. And they take one from the 7 into the 3. Q. The Qutb is 2? H.A. No, 1. Qutb is 1. Then they take one from the 7 into the 3. Take one from the 40 ones into the 7. Take one from the 360 ones into the 40. And take one man from dunya into the 360 ones; they take one from dunya. These ones are alive, they are not dead ones. Q. There are some rijals, are they different? H.A. Rijal al-Ghayb, these are the living ones. They are on their feet, alive. Maybe they even have families, children. You see them but don’t know them. And they are the ones who are holding, keeping the world. If it wasn’t for them, this dunya would not remain standing. My father had a Shaykh at that really wild time, the time of Bolsheviks. They were killing, hanging people, exiling them to Siberia, doing this and that. And this Shaykh Effendi (he had no children)… He was a big Shaykh with karamat. He was the Shaykh of all our people there. If he entered a house and if there was anything haram, he wouldn’t eat there. They would call him Al Ma’ruf Shaykh Abdullah. So the time of Bolsheviks came. And he had a very beautiful house. The Bolsheviks took him out of his house. He had a hamam (bath) so they put him in this hamam with his wife. They didn’t exile them because they were old. So they stayed in the hamam. And since our hamams are big, they made a bed there and a place to make wudhu. And it also snowed one of the nights. It was between maghrib and isha. He said to his wife, “O wife, wash me and also dress me with clean clothes. They will come to take me tonight”. His wife didn’t ask who will come. She put a wash basin and washed him in it. She dried him, dressed him, put him all clean in his bed, even put on his socks. They prayed maghrib and waited for them to come. Suddenly the door knocked. His wife opened the door and saw that two young soldiers came. They asked, “Are you ready Shaykh Effendi?” He said “Yes, I am ready”. So they carried him, like how two people make one sit and hold him from both sides. These two made him sit between them and he put his arms around them. He said, “Ok, I leave you to Allah” before his wife could say “Who are you, where are you taking him?” His wife tried to catch him from his foot, but was left with his sock in her hand. Going out, they disappeared and the lady started crying. There were mureeds nearby. Those mureeds came running, asking what happened. She explained it to them, saying, “They took the Shaykh Effendi!” They started searching everywhere, but it had just started snowing. They looked for traces of an outsider, but couldn’t find any. They called the police and soldiers. He was gone just like this. They couldn’t find him. However, before he passed away, or before he got lost, he had selected a second person in his place from the mureeds saying, “This is your Shaykh”. Now that one said, “He is gone. He found his place”. He was gone like this, never found again. They took him out from the door and he disappeared like this. They took him. So this is an example for us that they can take one like this. He can go like this. Who knows how long he lived more. He joined the 40 ones because one from the 40s passed away. He joined...
