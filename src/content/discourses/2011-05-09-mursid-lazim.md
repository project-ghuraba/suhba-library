Mawlana Shaykh Mohamad Nazim al Haqqani an Nakshibendi

7 Jumādā ath-Thāniya 1432 A.H. Monday, May 9, 2011 Translation by: http://saltanat.org/

BismillahirRahmanirRahim

In Sham Sharif, Syria, under the mimbar in the big Umayyad Mosque, they showed a cushion where Sultan Abdul Hamid, may paradise be his abode, would be present and sitting for the wird during the last third of the night. From Istanbul, he would come to this place during the last third of the night. Every night, he was with the saints, and all were in the presence of Prophet Yahya (as) (John the Baptist).

The Khalifatul Muslimin (the Ottoman Sultan) had at least seven ranks of sainthood. Modern day rulers have the power of seven shaytans. These cursed ones… They think no one understands the reality; but we do understand.

Guest: They are not teaching our history properly.

Of course they don't teach. You speak as much as you know. Prophet (sas) said in his tradition: “Address the people according to the level of their understanding, according to their capacity to learn”. If you speak above or below their level, they do not understand.

[Mawlana continues with talking about a book that a popular Turkish scholar called Jubbali Ahmad is writing. It is very high level in many volumes. Mawlana found it to be above the level of the people.]

Have you seen this book Haji Mehmet [Shaykh Mawlana’s son -Shaykh Mehmet Efendi]?

Shaykh Mehmet: I have seen it; but haven't read it.

You see how many pages…? I looked at it; but there are so many items that the general public will never understand. It is only for discussion among scholars. For example, someone is asking if it breaks the wudu when being touched by a woman. There are so many pages about this topic. They are also asking whether or not it is permitted to recite Surah al Ikhlas in the mosque. They wrote about a lot of topics like this that only high level scholars can understand. But there are very few people remaining at that level.

They prepared a tafsir; but you cannot find even ten people in Turkey that can understand this book. You cannot find ten people; but they wrote a lot of volumes. This book can fill a whole library. They have made thirty volumes? or fifteen volumes?

Shaykh Mehmet: It is more. Have you seen them?

Shaykh Mehmet: I have seen a few books from the collection. It is more than twenty volumes.

You have seen one of them? There are a lot of details, explanations. There is even more to come; but people's understanding is at zero level. You must speak from the station of zero. Yes, someone like me, I must open my mouth and speak at that level. If someone can stand and speak at that zero level, then he may start swearing like me. And I would dunk the one who asks [these questions] and then pull him out and say to him, “Look at my eyes!”

These are strong writings, speaking of legislation and rules of Islam; but they cannot give light to the Muslims in Turkish Republic now. It can't enlighten them; because these writings are not showing the way for the nation. People are not on that level to understand the writing.

Recently some professors came to visit me. They are professors in Islamic Theology (or something like that). [In the modern Turkish language, the word ‘theology’ resembles the word ‘hymn-ology’.] They are teaching. I asked them if they can sing a hymn (illahi). Alhmadulillah, they were able to sing one!

With such people, for the words they are mentioning in these books, there is no one remaining in Turkey who can understand. None left! The students can surpass their teachers - if they shake them a bit. We see that they are not improving. They are becoming worse than me. At that time, what did I do? I opened my mouth and I started swearing – in my words. They laughed. They enjoyed it very much and left.

Guest: I watched that sohbet when these professors came to visit you.

Really? Where did you watch it?

Guest: They broadcasted it on the internet on Saltanat TV.

Did I say anything wrong?

Guest: You spoke very well, they came for the birth week of the Holy Prophet (sas). I saw it and said, "Now, Shaykh Efendi will rebuke them." Shaykh Jubbali Ahmad is very smart, has strong hadith knowledge, and he has read a lot about the Tradition of Prophet (sas).

Oh Haji, I am talking about a pharmacist who prepares various kinds of remedies. Can he give the same medicine to everyone who comes to the pharmacy?

Guest: No, he should not.

Your illness is different; his illness is different. Is it appropriate to give his medicine to you, and your medicine to him? No! He [Jubbali Ahmad] may know very much; but he must understand what to speak and where to say what he is saying.

Now, this is important for our time. What happened in Turkey for 100 years? What did they do to Islamic knowledge? They mixed up so many things in Islamic knowledge. So many people who may or may not know something about the religion said different things. They mixed everything up, like in the women’s public hamam [bath house]. People who don’t have knowledge got into religious affairs; and they confused people. The ones who speak without knowledge, their place in hell is ready for them. It is not a minor matter – to pretend to know, and to speak nothing.

Therefore, what can we do? In Turkey, there is no one left who knows what is good and what is bad, what is halal and what is haram. They do as they like. Every sect may pass and continue. They say "kullun mazhab yazhab" [an Arabic saying that means “Every way is continuing”]. People have become like this now.

This is my son [indicating Shaykh Mehmet], may Allah increase his spiritual power, and everyone's spiritual power. There is no one in Turkey who has read an learned what he knows. There is no such person left in Anatolia. Maybe there are some who are studying in eastern states. They cannot reply to Shaykh Mehmet Efendi. But he is keeping quiet. Around him there are so many people going with trumpets, but I told him, “Leave them be.” They drag Mahmoud Efendi [Jubbali Ahmad's Shaykh] with them. They say that he is the ‘Mujaddid – renewer of the religion - for 15th Islamic century’. This is not a simple thing, to claim such a station. It means that he is the one who revives and renews Islam in the 15th century. Is he fit for such a claim? What authority is there to give such a title and to make such a claim? Did all scholars agree on this and say it? - or the ‘reviver of 15th century’ is only called that in … What is his neighborhood?

Guest: Carsamba, Fatih [in Istanbul, where Fatih Sultan Mehmed Han is buried]

No, what is their mosque called?

Guest: Ismailiyye Mosque…

Ismailiyye Mosque's retired imam is the reviver of 15th century? Are there fifteen scholars approving this? Why do you say this? Does it have a meaning? When you say ‘Mujaddid’, he is the one who is able to make the whole world tremble from his presence! Leave external power aside. His spiritual power must be full! Imam Rabbani [qas] was Mujaddid. He shook the whole world. All shaykhs in our Golden Chain have the attribute of Mujaddid; but they didn't say anything. Now if we all gather and go to Mahmoud Efendi, what will he say to us? He has no strength, no power left. He is disabled. They take him in and out with a cart [wheel chair]. He is helpless. Therefore, when they introduce him with this title, even people around him are confused. No one is saying, "Yes, we would say he is the Mujaddid." Regarding what will someone say, "Yes, …"? He [a normal person] cannot know what Mujaddid should be, what his attribute is, what he can do, what his limit is, what his effect is, his rights, what his spiritual power is. The Mujaddid must show that he has these capabilities. Shaykh Abdullah Dagestani (qas) used to say that there must be a spiritual guide, either alive or in his maqam, to show you the way.

Grandshaykh would say this all the time… He would always say: “Nazim Efendi, You say to them” - its meaning is perfect - “for everybody there must be a spiritual guide, either alive or ‘minah’". He is saying [‘minah’] meaning: those who passed away in their maqams, in their station. It is necessary for everybody to find a live spiritual guide or take one that is in his tomb, and to follow him. This is a must. It is necessary. If you don't, you may be lost. For example, if a pilot who controls a plane doesn't understand the instrument that shows the route, there is no result in his piloting. He may wreck the plane in the forest or in the mountains. He should know his destination, his route, as well as knowing how to pilot the plane. He must be knowledgeable. [Likewise,] there is religious knowledge; but the scholars required to show people the way cannot be found anymore.

Therefore, Ibn Arabi Hazretleri [qas] said: “Everybody must find a spiritual guide, alive or from those who passed, to show you the way.” Every person must find a spiritual master for his physical and his spiritual world. If he does not find that master, he will not find the way. It is like a pilot who keeps going up and down [looking out the window], and asking, “Have we arrived yet? What country is this?”. Is he asking for a voice from above? Our situation is like that now. That pilot does not understand the map. He cannot read the compass. He only flies the plane. It can’t be done with just piloting. Where are you heading to, in which direction? Where will you land? To fly the plane is one thing, but to navigate it is something else. Landing safely is another thing. This knowledge is different than piloting a plane. Do you understand, Hajji Efendi? Now, there is no one like this left. Finished!

Guest: No more scholars like this will come anymore?

The current ones will be swept away. With the order of the coming one- Master of the Time-scholars will be revealed. It is easy. Now is like the Ottoman Interregnum [chaotic era/ no Sultan].

May Allah forgive us, Fatiha
