Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Our tariqa, thank Allah, teaches us beautiful things and forbids us from evil. As Allah Exalted and Glorious be He says in the Koran: “The good order the good, good things. People who are bad, those who follow Shaitan, order the opposite.”

Shaitan shows it as, “Do not ever pray namaz, do not ever look like a Muslim, these are not good things, people would laugh at you, and what is really good is doing all kinds of filth!” His soldiers, those who follow him, show it like this. They are enemies of Allah (JJ), enemies of people, and enemies of Muslims. A Muslim is not the enemy of people but the friend of people. He has been commanded to save people from ruin. This is what Muslims want to do. Muslims strive for people not to go to hell. And those who follow Shaitan do the contrary: they want to take everybody to hell because they are enemies of people.

Therefore we need to be careful. We need to pay attention to this in every move and beware. Is this a friend or a foe? If a foe, you try to win him, but most of the time the foe is fully in the hands of Shaitan and it is hard to save him. However, if they have been fooled, they can also come to the right way. We need to look at it without delving too deep. You advice this person if he benefits from it. If he accepts, he accepts. If he does not, what can you do? Guidance is from Allah. His kismet was hell. There is no need to strive too much.

Islam is the friend and disbelief (kufr) is the enemy. This was the case since the beginning and it will be the same way until Judgment Day. Never think befriending Shaitan will bring benefit. No, the friendship of Shaitan is taking you to hell, not paradise. The enemy of Shaitan is in paradise Allah willing. May Allah make us all enemies of Shaitan Inshallah. May we become friends of the Truth (Haqq), friends of Allah (JJ) and the Prophet (SAW) Inshallah.

Wa Minallah at-tawfeeq. Al-Fatiha.
