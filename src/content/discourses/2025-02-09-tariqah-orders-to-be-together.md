As-Salāmu ‘Alaykum wa RaḥmatuLlāhi wa Barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu BiLlāhi Minash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. BismiLlāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm.

Wa ṣ-Salātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Akhirīn.

Madad yā RasūlAllāh, Madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi RasūliLlāh, Madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

Dastūr Mawlana Sheikh Abdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistāni, Sheikh Muḥammad

Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-Suḥbah wa l-Khayru fi l-Jam‘iyyah.

Ṭarīqah is ordering to be together. This is an order for Muslims, for good people to be together because in Ākhirah they will also be together. Which in Qur’ān Aẓīmu sh-Shān He is saying, one in Jannah with his friends, in Jannah also. People will be together; those who love each other. And he was wondering, “There was one, my friend in dunyā. He was saying, ‘Do you believe that after, when you die becoming earth, dust, you will be [resurrected] again, you will come to life again?’ I wonder where he is!” And in Jannah you can also see. Allāh makes that when somebody asks something like this, He shows him that he is in hell, in between fire. And he will look at him saying, “You nearly made me with you!” When he was in dunyā, he was all the time saying to him, “You cannot believe this. This is not true; this is not so. I don’t believe. I am going my way. I am enjoying myself in dunyā. After this, I will be dust and it’s impossible to be a human being again like this.” And he was proud. He said, “You could’ve cheated me. You could’ve made me like you. You are now suffering in hell.”

This is what dunyā people do, when you are with them too much, sometimes they make doubt in your heart. For this, you must be away from people who are too much against Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, against Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Be with good people.

For this, we are saying, “Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.” Jam’iyyah means to be together, in shā’a Llāh. May Allāh make our jam’iyyah, our gathering all-time, in shā’a Llāh, forever, in shā’a Llāh. May Allāh bless you. May Allāh give you long life, in shā’a Llāh; with īmān, Islām, and happiness, you and your family also, in shā’a Llāh. This is very important, al-ḥamdu liLlāh. May Allāh give this ni’mah for us and give it for others also in shā’a Llāh.
