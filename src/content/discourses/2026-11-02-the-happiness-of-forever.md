As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

The happiness of forever makes you happy in your heart. Other happiness [ends] because everything in this life has an end. To attain the happiness of forever you must love Allah, love Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, love Awliyā uṣ-Ṣalihīn, ʿibādullah aṣ-Ṣalihīn (the righteous servants of Allah); to be with them, to ask blessings from them.

Alḥamdulillāh now we just made a celebration for Sayyidinā Aḥmad Al-Rifāi Al Kabīr. He is one of the biggest Awliyā’ullāh. More than a thousand years, he’s had big karāmahs. So all people attain from his karāmahs, and benefit from his madad. So he is barakah. His blessings reach all people who ask him for madad, recite Qur’ān, recite tasbīh, tahlīl and salawāt. They get benefit for this.

So this will, inshā’Allāh, make your heart happy, your life happy, and be a good generation inshā’Allāh. May Allah keep you safe. Because the first enemy for these people is shayṭān and his followers. They don’t like Awliyā’ullāh. They don’t like Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. For this, as much shayṭān doesn’t love somebody, you must love him.

This is a short ṣuḥbah, short naṣīḥah, advice for all of us, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
