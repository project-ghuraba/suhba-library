As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is saying, "Yā-’ayyuhā lladhīna āmanū kulū min ṭayyibāti mā razaqnākum wa-shkurū li-Llāhi in kuntum iyyāhu ta‘budūn" (2:172). Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is telling us believers to eat from the good things. Eat from what is clean and good and then make shukr to AllāhNone so that AllāhNone increases these good things.

A Muslim, a believer, should look at what he is eating and drinking, what he is giving and taking. It should be good. There is no need to buy bad [poor quality] things because he is Muslim. If he is poor, good things can still be found. He should buy accordingly. No need to buy bad [quality] things because he is poor. If he cannot buy, that is a different matter. But generally, people should eat good things to keep their bodies strong for worship and make intention to please AllāhNone. Because if it is the opposite and you feed your body without worshipping, like an animal, it is useless. You will only feed your body uselessly. Otherwise, you eat good things from good rizq given by AllāhNone and make shukr to AllāhNone and AllāhNone will increase HisNone favours.

It is like this in everything. Muslims at the present time don't know much about this. Even rich ones, they don't know what to do, how to live, or how to act. They should look and learn. Everything has its principles and adab. And Islamic life, life according to Islām, is important. First of all, people are responsible for taking care of their families. And then our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam advised about neighbours, to pay attention to neighbours. He gave so much importance to neighbours that the Ṣaḥābah were saying, "We thought he would leave them a share from hisNone inheritance." Neighbours' rights are that important. It is also important to help and care for them. And check what you are buying. Don't take without looking and checking. Also look at the clothes, if they fit you or not. That is important too.

Your body is a trust from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. If you take care of it, you will live healthy and make shukr to AllāhNone. If you don't take care of that which Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gave as a trust, you are already punished for it. Afterwards don't say, "Why did this happen to me?" It is important. Be careful in everything and choose from the best. Don't take what you don't know well. The body is important too. When someone is sick, he should take medicine. Sometimes people say they won't take it. You must take it. It is necessary. But if you are doubtful, you should consult a few doctors. With their advice, it's okay. If one of them says something different, it's okay. But you can't say, "I will take this or that," as you like. You shouldn't say that. And you should do what has been tested over the years. No one has the right to force you into taking something that hasn't been tested. You should wait. If it's useful, you take it. If it's useless, nothing will happen to you, with the permission of AllāhNone. As we said, in everything... We will come to this matter again here. We will show it as an example.

There are too many things that our Muslims don't understand. They should pay attention to what they eat and drink. They know what they drink. Let's say water, soda, or coke. They should be careful with them as well. Water is beneficial. But they say coke is very dangerous. We should be careful about it. If you want, drink but not too much. A glass per day is okay. Some people drink two to three litres a day. And then they say they are sick. Of course that happens. Too much of anything is harmful.

The most important thing is how they cheat and trick our Muslims with perfumes. They sell worthless scents as perfume for ḥajjīs and ḥājjahs. That is not the perfume of ḥajj. There is something chemical in it. If someone with an allergy buys it, he gets neither good feeling, nor lungs – nothing. I don't understand this foolishness. Muslims should be careful. You can't buy everything cheap. And if it is expensive, you should check if it is real or not. Don't buy such cheap things. They will harm you and your skin. You harm yourself and disturb others, you harm others as well. If you want it, buy a good one. If the good is expensive, don't buy it. Instead of buying the cheap one, apply diesel or kerosene. At least, the microbes will die. Don't apply the other thing. We have said it a hundred times. Nevertheless, it is direct... It is fooling people. We ask that AllāhNone gives understanding.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying: "Yā-’ayyuhā lladhīna āmanū kulū min ṭayyibāti mā razaqnākum wa-shkurū li-Llāhi in kuntum iyyāhu ta‘budūn" (2:172). Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone saying, eat and drink from the best thing, good thing and thank, give, be thankful for AllāhNone, for what HeNone... if you are worshipping HimNone, you must be eat good thing. No need to look for bad thing to eat. "I am Muslim, I humble, I will eat bad [quality] thing. No need for me to eat good thing." No. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is saying, you must eat good thing. Best thing if you can afford. Yes. If you are poor, also you must look for good thing also, for poor people also. Maybe poor people cannot eat whole meat or other thing but they can eat from vegetable or eating from ḥubūb (grains), barley or other thing who – rice or other thing. They must look for good thing, not for bad [quality] thing. Because this our body, AllāhNone give us to look after our self.

Our body. it is AllāhNone create and you can be healthy if you follow this order to find good thing to eat and to feed your family and also if you can, for neighbour. Neighbour also very important. Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam was every time saying for Ṣaḥābah for neighbour. Even once a Ṣaḥābah, he said, "I thought heNone will be give them from inheritor – part of inheritor." Every time "neighbour, neighbour" so, they thought they will... But no. Only for family – inheritance. This very important. And for your guest, for other also you must give good thing to be acceptable in Divine Presence. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying, "Wa-lā tayammamū l-khabītha minhu" (2:267). "Don't give bad thing for other." What you eat, also for you, if you have family and neighbour, you can give them also from this.

Muslim must be have good intelligent to know what is good, what not good. This is very important. Everything you must learn. You must learn for this to not be... You can be, if you... Don't be cheated by people. Many people when they saw Muslim with his dress or like this, they know he is, he is soft. Maybe if you cheat him, he will not say anything. So, they cheat him too much. But don't be cheated. And especially for, also – for giving example for our people, especially for who following Sunnah and in Sunnah Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, "AllāhNone, HeNone make meNone love three thing from your dunyā: one, good smell – perfume, second, woman, and third, praying ṣalāt."

So, first one – perfume. Every people from our, who he is Muslim practicing whole Sunnah, they thought, "We must put this," and going for this, selling perfume and they buying. But they look, this is one pound, "Eh, this cheap very good." There is fifty pound, "Oh, no this we cannot afford this. This is okay." Don't buy this. Because this is not perfume what Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam was. Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam time was no chemical factory from Holland or from Germany coming, making rose, false rose as it is real and they giving very cheap. No, it was real one. This is what Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying. If you want to follow Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam you can buy this. If you cannot [afford], don't buy this chemical. You can, organic petrol you can put by yourself. It is better. Must be knowing this. Because when you put, it make you allergic for your skin. You harm yourself and for your lungs also giving. Other people maybe they have allergy also, make harm them. This is very important to look for good thing. If you cannot afford, don't take. If you afford, okay. No need to make. You can put water – rose water or other thing, it's okay. But don't pay for chemical thing. This chemical, they destroy whole world and we are suffering now from this. We don't know what this is happening, from where coming this chemical thing.

Whole world coming for not knowing anything about what will happen. For this also, you must look for yourself also. When you ill, you can take medicine. We have normal, normal illness, we know, many people they know about medicine. They can take. There is also hopeless illness, AllāhNone make it away from us. People they don't want to make medication. But this can be because there no hope for other thing to do. They can do this. We not saying, don't do this. And also there is mind illness. People they must whole life they take this medicine, like blood pressure medicine. But this... What happen now, we don't know about what will be good, what will be not good. So, we are asking for protection from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone protect us from this every bad thing, in shā’a Llāh. From spiritual and physical, in shā’a Llāh.
