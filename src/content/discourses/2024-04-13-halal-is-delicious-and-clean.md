Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Yā-ayyuhā n-nāsu kulū mimmā fī l-arḍi ḥalālan ṭayyiban wa-lā tattabi‘ū khuṭuwāti sh-shayṭāni innahū lakum ‘aduwwun mubīn” (2:168). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘aẓīm. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says, “O you who believe, eat good things and don’t follow the footsteps of shayṭān.” Shukr to AllāhNone, AllāhNone has given every blessing. In everything, there is ḥarām and ḥalāl. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says, “Eat from what is ḥalāl.” It will be good, and a cure for you. What you eat will be good for you. Do that. Don’t follow shayṭān. Shayṭān is striving not for your good, but to harm you. That is why Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla has told us this.

So be careful what you eat. In the time we are living, people already have plenty of food. They don’t pay attention to what they eat. Most people do not pay much attention to what is ḥalāl or ḥarām, and as a result, diseases increase. This is because good things do not come to the stomach. As we said, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla has given an opposite to everything: Ḥalāl, ḥarām; Ḥalāl morsel, ḥarām morsel; Good food, bad food. There is what is ḥalāl and there is what is ḥarām. There are both.

Therefore, a person should eat what is good for him and what is ḥalāl. He should not approach what is not ḥalāl. There is no need to be greedy. Now they are doing various things to deceive people. They mix various substances into their food so that after eating this food, people will want it all the time. The substances they add are harmful. There are many harmful substances. Instead of salt, they add another substance to make it supposedly sweeter. People have no taste left in their tongue or mouth already. They eat without understanding whether it is sweet and delicious, or tasteless. When they put that in their mouths, it causes harm to both themselves and their bodies. Their spirituality is also harmed. Therefore, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says to eat what is ḥalāl, clean and good. Avoid ḥarām. There is no benefit in ḥarām. It won’t do any good to your body. It is bad and harmful. May AllāhNone protect us from this. May AllāhNone protect us from the evil and plots of people.

That is why, Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim wanted more home-cooked food. Even if what you cook at home is a small amount, it is enough. It gives strength to the body. It also gives strength to īmān. Otherwise, the body also gets sick. And it won’t do any good for your soul either. Those foods won’t do any good for the soul. You need to pay attention to that. Our body is a trust given to us, the most important thing. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla has entrusted it to us for you to take good care of it and look after it, so that you can perform your worship, so that it be with you until the end of your life, so that it doesn’t weigh you down, but carry you.

May AllāhNone protect us. May HeNone give us all a good life. And may HeNone give us good health and well-being, in what we eat, in shā’a Llāh. May AllāhNone protect us from unknown and altered foods and forgive us, in shā’a Llāh. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying: Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Yā-ayyuhā n-nāsu kulū mimmā fī l-arḍi ḥalālan ṭayyiban wa-lā tattabi‘ū khuṭuwāti sh-shayṭāni innahū lakum ‘aduwwun mubīn” (2:168).
