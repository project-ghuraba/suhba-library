A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is saying: Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Wa-lā tansawu l-faḍla baynakum inna Llāha bi-mā ta‘malūna baṣīr" (2:237). Do not forget the goodness between you, says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. AllāhNone is seeing what you are doing. HeNone is Al-Baṣīr. Nothing is hidden from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Even the smallest goodness of yours, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla sees it. He rewards you accordingly. It is the beauty of Islām, the beauty of Ṭarīqah, Mashāyikh, and Ṣaḥābah. It is the beauty of Mashāyikh. When he [the shaykh] sees the tiniest goodness, he does not forget it. Certainly, he keeps it. And he wants to do more goodness in return for it. He never discredits people. People of the present time are the opposite of this. They do not understand goodness. So much goodness is done for them but they forget all of it for a small thing. They start thinking badly. They start saying, "He did not treat us well," and complain. Whereas, it is the order of AllāhNone to not forget the goodness. When you don't forget goodness, you are always well. If you forget goodness, then you always think badly and become bad. May AllāhNone protect us.

Mashāyikh, especially Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim, [we have] many memories of him. He would always invite and favour someone. People would get astonished and wonder if that person had been around for a long time. On the contrary, they were people we didn't know. He would do many things for them just because they had invited him for tea once on some journey, some of them had invited him for coffee in their house. He favoured them so much, it seemed as if they had given the whole world to him. This is the attribute of Mashāyikh. They have these beauties. A normal person forgets everything someone did for him and at a tiny mistake falls into a situation AllāhNone doesn't like.

And there is a ḥadīth of our Holy ProphetNone which is about Mashāyikh. All people are in need of AllāhNone. They need someone to take care of them, to feed them. It is Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Everyone needs HimNone. And the best among them are the ones who serve them [HisNone creation]. The most useful are those who teach goodness, who teach beauty, and who do goodness. Certainly, they are Mashāyikh because they don't expect any benefit from people. They hope only for the pleasure of AllāhNone. And that is, of course, the biggest benefit. They never wish badness. They wish only goodness to the creations of AllāhNone. They want to follow the people and show them the right way, teach them the truth. Wherever the truth is, Mashāyikh are there.

Sometimes people see only outwardly. And even when they see the truth, they are still stuck on things on the surface that they can see outwardly. It is because Ṭarīqah shows people the truth, goodness, and beauty both apparent and hidden. Therefore, who is outside this way, outside the way of Ṭarīqah... Ṭarīqah and Sharī‘ah are one. Who does not practice them together cannot see these beauties. They cannot reach these beauties. Even if they do only partially. When there is only Sharī‘ah it gets confusing. And in Ṭarīqah, if someone does something according to his mind without following his shaykh, he makes the biggest mistake. He can make a mistake that cannot be forgiven. Ṭarīqah is together with a shaykh. And a shaykh is someone who knows Sharī‘ah and Ṭarīqah, who knows the truth. To be with them is the best thing. Their goodness cannot be forgotten.

As Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla orders, do not forget goodness. Do not forget goodness between you. And the biggest goodness to you comes from your shaykh, shukr to AllāhNone, who takes you to AllāhNone, who shows you the way of AllāhNone, the way of our Holy ProphetNone. There is no greater goodness. May AllāhNone be pleased with them.

In our khuṭbah today we say, Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Wa-lā tansawu l-faḍla baynakum" (2:237). Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla ordering people, "Don't forget what good thing between yourself, good deed somebody done for you, good thing. Don't forget this." This is order of AllāhNone. Somebody help you, somebody teach you, somebody feed you, somebody give you – all this good deed, good thing to show you good thing or to... Even very small thing you mustn't forget. This is order of AllāhNone, it is Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.

Very small thing you see, but it is real big thing for whole human being. Because people they are forget what many people – maybe help them, maybe give them very big things – but very small thing [happens and] they become forget this. All what they done this people they be forgetting from people. This is most people doing this. For this, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HisNone order, "Don't forget good deed." Don't forget your family, don't forget your friend. This is beauty of Islām. And they say, "Inna Llāha bi-mā ta‘malūna baṣīrun" (2:237). AllāhNone HeNone is seeing everything. Nothing can be hide from AllāhNone. Very small thing it is also AllāhNone know and see. And when you do something, not forget this good thing, AllāhNone will be happy with you and reward you.

But people, as we said they are, you do maybe give everything but, in very small thing, quickly they are coming against this people. It is coming against [the] order of AllāhNone. AllāhNone saying for you, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla "Don't forget the good thing." And Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam also saying, even you must don't forget your father's friend, your mother's friend. Be good with them, AllāhNone will reward you.

Al-hamdu liLlāh, Islām it is very beautiful religion and AllāhNone, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, "Al-‘ibādu ‘ayāla Llāh." The whole creature, whole human being they are – AllāhNone looking for them. They are in need for AllāhNone, all of us. And the best, the most beloved for HimNone, who help HisNone servants, HisNone who this people everything. They are most beloved one for AllāhNone who help them. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh Islām they was helping everybody, especially helping people to prevent them from bad thing, to prevent them from their bad acting, and invite them to Divine Presence of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla to become to good people.

This is the best, and this also in Ṭarīqah – Ṭarīqah and Sharī‘ah must be. Ṭarīqah without Sharī‘ah cannot be because if you only Sharī‘ah, you will see people, they are not understanding Islām. They're making, not making mercy for people. They're making bad for people. But Ṭarīqah giving understanding as Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam – you must be merciful for all people. And to be mercy – to teach them good thing, to show them the way of AllāhNone, the way of mercy. So, Mashāyikh, this is not forgetting us. We must also not forget our Mashāyikh. Even many story from thousand of thing about Mawlānā Shaykh when he was travelling or other thing. Every day we hearing new thing, new people they say. Because, of course, tens of years he was around people. Everybody he give them some story from his life to make them, show good way for people. Sometimes some people coming, Mawlānā he was make them very generous for them – "Oh come." Respect them too much and I thought, but we not see this people at all. He said, "This one, before ten years or twelve years, he was in some place he give us, invite us and give us coffee, this invite us give tea." So he was so much respect. We thought it is he give like big dinner, or staying many days in his house. [But] Only maybe ten minutes, half an hour, but he was respecting, not forgetting even from ten years, twenty years. He not forget at all this people.

This is good deed. This is what Mashāyikh they are doing, teaching us: beauty of life, beauty of Islām. Without this, only Sharī‘ah it can be very tough and not understanding. Sharī‘ah also, is Ṭarīqah, Sharī‘ah – same, no different. But this people they are making different and, without understanding Sharī‘ah, they are doing practicing – no mercy, nothing good. But Ṭarīqah also without Sharī‘ah cannot be. Of course who do this Ṭarīqah without Sharī‘ah, who has no shaykh, no murshid, he is by himself: "I am Ṣūfī. I am Ṭarīqah myself." But you don't know anything. If you with shaykh, shaykh is knowing Ṭarīqah, Sharī‘ah same. But when you take it away, it become nothing. For this we are thanking, we must thank Mawlānā Shaykh, all mashāyikh. The best thing they give us for dunyā and Ākhirah. Teaching – they are teaching, teaching of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, teaching of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.

Thank for them. AllāhNone bless them. AllāhNone make us whole time to remember their good life, good. All their whole their life good. Anything you remember you are be, in shā’a Llāh, reward for this.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
