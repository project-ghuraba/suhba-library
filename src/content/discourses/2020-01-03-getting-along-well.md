Waṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-F’āiz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-

“Get along well with people,” says the Prophet (SAW). And he says, “Those whom you should get along with best are your closest ones.” So it is necessary to get along with people. First of all, one should get along with his household. Husband and wife should get along well, brothers with brothers, children with each other. It starts from a small community and a village, and then a city, and finally all become good.

What the Prophet (SAW) and Allah want is goodness amongst people. May Allah give us goodness! Satan asks for evil. Satan asks for evil, worry, the end, uneasiness between brothers, husbands and wives, amongst people. What Allah 'azza wa jalla wants is goodness, but most often people follow their ego’s desires, and follow Satan, and then they do what Satan asks for. Of course, getting along well requires patience, and requires one to control one’s ego. When this is actually done and practiced, one earns reward (thawāb) and has a good life as well. Otherwise, all of your life goes by with difficulties, fights, and at the end there is no benefit at all.

Therefore today we find that courts are full, and there are fights between husbands and wives, and large numbers of divorce cases. In the past, it was very rare for people to get divorced. May Allah give minds to people today, for it is said that half of married couples end in divorce. May Allah protect us! May Allah give us good relations with one another, insha’Allah, because this is not a good thing. Families brake up, children suffer. Who is happy about this? Satan, only Satan would be happy. Neither Allah nor the Prophet (SAW) likes such affairs. May Allah grant us good relationships, insha’Allah. Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-F ti ah
