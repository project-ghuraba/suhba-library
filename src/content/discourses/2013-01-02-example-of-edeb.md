Fatiha.

As-Salamu Alaikum. Audhu Billahi min al shaytan al rajim, Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim.

Today Mawlana said in his fajr sohbat "teach adab to the brothers". Astaghfirullah, we are not in a position to teach adab. For almost a year now, because Mawlana was sick, the mureeds were not able to see Mawlana. So after Dhuhr prayer we were saying a few words to not leave them unattended, so that the people won't come & leave empty. But Allah Shukur now Mawlana is well, thousands millions of thanks to Allah Almighty. Mawlana's health is good, he makes sohbat. He is making sohbat everyday & we can see him- everyone is seeing him everywhere. Therefore out of adab, it is not suitable for us anymore to give sohbat here. When there is the sun, a candle can have no importance. We were already barely saying a few words & from now on there is no need for this either. May Allah give our Shaykh long life & never leave us without him. So out of adab, it is not suitable for us to speak here.

Today in the morning Mawlana Shaykh was speaking about adab- "teach people adab". Astaghfirullah we cannot teach adab, he is the teacher. And since nearly one year when he was ill, sometimes when I was here, speak little bit speech for people, to enjoy people, so that they won't be sad. So it was like this until now. But Alhamdulillah now Mawlana Shaykh everyday he is giving sohbat & he is very well. It is from adab to not speak for me here. And after this day insha Allah Allah give him long life, his speech is enough for a thousand years, hundred thousand years it is enough. We are not even like a candle compared to him- to sun. So Alhamdulillah we are happy he is speaking. No need after this to speak for me here.

Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, Al Fatiha.
