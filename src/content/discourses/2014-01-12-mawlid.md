Shaykh Muhammad Nazim Al-Haqqani An-Naqshibandi, Sohbat of of the 12th of January, 2014.

Tonight was Mawlid night, like no other in the world. May it be holy for the people of Muhammad. May Allah let us reach his intercession. May no worries remain on people. May shaitan and those who follow shaitan be defeated. May calamities stop. May mercies come down on the people of Muhammad. May relief come. May those who who want to destroy the people of Muhammad be destroyed themselves. Bi hurmati hadhihi laylat-ul Mubarak. Bi hurmati Fatiha.

I am relieved. I was upset in the beginning but now am relieved. For the holy birth-night of our Master doing as we are doing a thousand times, a hundred thousand times, is not enough. Grant us compassion and intercession, O most honored and distinguished one.

May Sham be opened as well. May the enemies of Islam disappear. Fatiha for the honor of the holy night.

A representative came. Keep it above, don't put down.
