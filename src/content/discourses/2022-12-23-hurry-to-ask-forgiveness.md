As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says in the āyah we read in the khuṭbah, "Wa-sāri’ū ilá maghfiratin min rabbikum" (3:133). Hurry to ask forgiveness from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, run to ask. Hurry to do it. Hurry to ask for forgiveness, because the forgiveness and mercy of AllāhNone are great. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla has generosity and mercy as great as the heavens and earth. Whatever you do, as long as you repent and ask forgiveness, AllāhNone will accept and forgive. AllāhNone is forgiving. HeNone is ʻAfūw – the Most Forgiving.

Not as a comparison, but when a person does something, some people can forgive but others can't forgive certain things. However, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla forgives when they ask forgiveness and they turn to the right way. Therefore, HeNone says do not miss the opportunity. You never know when someone will die. When he dies, there is no more door of forgiveness. But there is a chance while he is still alive. As soon as you pass away to the next life, there is no more chance at all. You may regret, repent and ask forgiveness as much as you want, or repent as much as you want.

Although it is not good to hurry for worldly matters but in matters of ākhirah, people should always be in a hurry. Our people used to say in the prayers long ago: " ‘Ajjilū bi-ṣ-ṣalāti qabla l-fawti, wa-‘ajjilū bi-t-tawbati qabla l-mawt." Hurry to pray before the time is up. Otherwise, it is a big loss for people. Because their prayer is not performed on time you missed out on great virtues. And its time is like life. While the time of ẓuhr is until ‘aṣr, ‘aṣr is until maghrib, maghrib is until ‘ishā’, however, time runs out quickly. Therefore, you should pray on time, keep the sunnah and adab of it, make tasbīḥāt and earn the virtues.

And you should ask forgiveness before you die. " ‘Ajjilū bi-t-tawbati qabla l-mawt." Hurry to ask forgiveness. Hurry because this chance only exists while you are still alive, while you are breathing. When your breath is cut, it will be gone. [Repentance] will have no benefit. You can repent and cry as much as you want, you can regret as much as you want, it will be gone. Therefore, the chance to make charity, to do good things exists only while you are alive. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla loves all those who help people, who forgive other people and hold their temper. "Wa-Llāhu yuḥibbu l-muḥsinīn" (3:148). "AllāhNone loves those who are kind and do goodness." And AllāhNone doesn't like those who don't do goodness but do badness. People should know this. "I don't do anything. I don't pray or anything but I do good things." Your goodness is incomplete. Complete it while you have the chance. Don't accept any excuse for yourself. Because people hurry to do worldy things so much, they run so quickly that they keep themselves in suffering. If they did one percent for the pleasure of AllāhNone of what they do for dunyā, everything would be the best for them. But they are not doing this.

You hear here and there in the news they have a sale somewhere. People don't sleep at all for the whole night. They wait in front of some shop to buy something cheap. But don't tell them to wake up to pray, to pray two raka‘āt for the pleasure of AllāhNone. Even if they wake up and you tell them to pray, they are too lazy to do it. People have become the servants of their egos. They are lazy in all things that are for the pleasure of AllāhNone. But they are hardworking and skillful in things that are for shayṭān and the ego.

Therefore, may AllāhNone protect us. This world is a world of opportunities. They say opportunities too but their opportunities are for personal and worldly benefit. Real opportunities are for the benefits of ākhirah. May AllāhNone give all of us those opportunities. May HeNone not let us obey our egos.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying: "Wa-sāri’ū ilá maghfiratin min rabbikum wa-jannatin ‘arḍuhā s-samāwātu wa-l-arḍu u‘iddat li-l-muttaqīn. alladhīna yunfiqūna fī s-sarrā’i wa-ḍ-ḍarrā’i wa-l-kāẓimīna l-ghayẓa..." (3:133-134). Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying in this Qur’ān verses: "Be hurry to ask for forgiveness from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla."

Normally, to be hurry not good for people. They must be thinking slowly when they're doing something, or they will buy, they will sell, they will do something from, anything from this world – material world things. They must be, not be so hurry. "Al-‘ajalatu mina sh-shayṭān" – meaning to make you hurry [is] from shayṭān who maybe make you hurry to make mistake, when you are hurry. But for ākhirah, to be hurry it is acceptable. Other for dunyā not acceptable.

For ākhirah you must every time you must be hurry, and what this AllāhNone order for us, to ask for forgiveness from the Lord of heaven and earth and everything – Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. HisNone mercy, whole universe, whole samāwāt, land and skies and everything, in HisNone mercy oceans. This is, HeNone calling for people to be hurry to ask forgiveness, to not lose their chance from this good thing. Because you don't know when you do something wrong, AllāhNone will be not happy with you. For this, you must be hurry. No one, one day, one hour, one minute to AllāhNone be not happy with you. All time you will remember AllāhNone and ask forgiveness. Who they're not doing good thing even, everything AllāhNone forgive. They must ask for forgiveness.

With this, is time, it is, AllāhNone give you time in this life but when you are finish your life, after this no forgiveness. For this, before dying, before not breathe, no nothing under or you are die, it's finished. As much you can do that side for asking for forgiveness, asking for your regret, nothing be benefit for you. For this you must, this life be hurry for good things, to do good things, to spend for charity, to forgive people, to help people. This all Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla – "wa-Llāhu yuḥibbu l-muḥsinīn" (3:134) – "AllāhNone love this good people," obeying people who have charity, they have helping. This is AllāhNone said, "I love them."

This is very important to AllāhNone love us. It is most important thing must be for human being, for every creature. And not for – like if some people they're love you, it is, if not for AllāhNone, it is not important, not good. Love for AllāhNone and hate for AllāhNone. Who AllāhNone hate them, we also we don't love them. Which AllāhNone loves them, we love them. How you say this you are humanity [humane]? Humanitary [humane] if they like, we are not saying we will not love them forever, if they change and they love AllāhNone, AllāhNone love them, we love them also. If not, we also not, because they are against the good things.

AllāhNone love good thing. HeNone just, in every verses in Qur’ān, saying what HeNone love, what HeNone don't love. Love good people, love charity people, love poor people. HeNone love who obeyed [obedient] people, knowledge people who know about teaching people the knowledge of AllāhNone. This is – everything good AllāhNone love. But every bad, HeNone don't love. We also [are] like HimNone. And Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying in ḥadīth also: "Be asking for forgiveness from AllāhNone and ask [to] be all time connected with AllāhNone, with obeying HimNone, and with mentioning HimNone all time. Not forget HimNone. "For this HeNone said, "Tujbarū, turzaqū, tunṣarū," heNone said. And AllāhNone when you do this, AllāhNone HeNone be your, make you satisfied and make you, your provision more. HeNone give more things for you. You have rizq barakah, and to be victory. You won on [over] bad things, in shā’a Llāh."

This is our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam for hisNone nation, hisNone advice. Everything with himNone it is good, and with himNone the end nice, good. This is very important. To many thing many time they have bad end, it's very bad. But when you are with AllāhNone, mentioning all time, you will be won and you will be safe next life. Not be regret, because you are already asking forgiveness.

AllāhNone HisNone word for us: "INone forgive. When you're asking forgiveness, INone forgive you." In shā’a Llāh, AllāhNone forgive us, all of us. And save us from bad people, from our bad ego, in shā’a Llāh. Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
