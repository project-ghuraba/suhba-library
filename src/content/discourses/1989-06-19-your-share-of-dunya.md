Ash'hadu an la iIaha ill'Allah wa ash'hadu anna Muhammadan abduhu wa rasuluh. SallAllahu Ta'ala alayhi wa sallam. Astaghfirullah, Astaghfirullah, Astaghfirullah, min kulli dhanbin wa masiyatin wa min kulli ma yukhalifu dina al-Islam Ya Arhama r-Rahimin.

Faith means cleanliness. The one who has faith is clean/pure because Janab Allah says: Astaidhu Billah: "Innama almushrikūna najasun" (9:28) Those who are mushrik, that is those without iman (faith) are dirty. They are unclean, impure. When we say unclean, perhaps you could say that unbelievers bathe, they bathe to become clean. Should they bathe themselves with the water of the seven seas, their dirt would not rub off. The kalimatu t-tayibba (good words) that will clean them are: la ilaha ill'Allah Muhammadun rasulullah, sallAllahu alaihi wa sallam. If they say these words they will become clean and pure.

The speciality that sweeps away unbelief and shirk, the power to clean them is derived from the kalimatu t-tayibba. If it purifies one of unbelief and shirk, would it not clean sins? Certainly. If the sins of all Muslims were put on one scale of a balance, and the non-believer's unbelief on the other, without doubt, unbelief would weigh heavier. Unbelief is extremely unjust. That is why the unbelief of a non-believer is heavier than the sins of all of the Prophet's ummah. That unbelief is cleansed - surely when sinful people reaffirm their faith, they purify themselves of their sins as well. This is why iman, for Muslims, is purification, as well, it is a cleaning of the unbelief of non-believers.

Hence, when we begin any act of servanthood we first say, Ya Rabbi we accept Your Oneness and we accept that Your Habib truly is the Prophet of Haqq. We testify. Perhaps on account of our heedlessness we may fall out of iman or we may fall out of iman because of our ignorance. By reaffirming our faith we enter that circle of iman once again. Alaihi s-salatu wa s-salam, our Master said: "There will come a time when my ummah will live their day until night as Muslims, but wake up as unbelievers or their night will pass as Muslims, the day after, they'll become unbelievers. That time will come for my ummah".

We can see this happening often, nowadays. Men, especially Muslims, must be prudent in their actions because Islam gives a discipline for living. Islam rejects idleness. That is why so many people go against Islam. People want to live freely because of their nature. Mankind doesn't like domination. When we say Mankind - from amongst all Creation that Janab Allah created, Mankind is the most noble, distinguished and privileged one. No one knows the creations Janabu l-Haqq created. Only Janabu l-Haqq knows. There are many kinds of creation in Janabu l-Haqq's Domains. Certainly, we see many classes and kinds of Allah's Creation living all over this world. In Allah's Domains, among these creations are the inhabitants of Earth, both human and jinn. There are humans and there are jinn. The jinn reside in this world also. However, these creatures are invisible to us. Janab Allah has hidden them because their form opposes our form. We are created from clay, they are created from fire.

Angels are the inhabitants of the Angelic Realm, which we call Malakut, the realm opposite our material world - heavenly creation. There are worlds higher up in the heavens, the seven skies. The Divine Throne belongs to Alamu l-Malakut. Its residents are the Holy Angels. We are obliged to believe in the Unseen worlds (ghayb). That means we have been asked to believe in the unseen. No one can be asked to believe in something that is already visible. "Here, I see it." - Believe in this, it exists. "I already see it!". That which is hidden - we are invited to accept belief in the Creator of the Universe.

First, we are told to believe in Allah. This invitation came through all the Prophets. Some small-minded ones, not mature enough, whose minds are not prudent say, "Show us that Allah exists!" Such query is childish and ignorant. I say to that one, leave this, we say that Allah is the Creator of this universe. I would then ask the question: Can you show me the boundries of this universe, since you want me to show you its Creator? Because in comparison to the Majesty of the Creator, the universe He created, dwindles, diminishes and disappears. It is nothing.

Alright then, leave this. If the universe is too vast, then I ask you to show me the ends of this world that we live on. That small-minded one would say, okay, in order to show you its ends we must climb in a rocket, he will say, we will travel by a satellite launched into the skies, then I can show you its boundries. Then I say, O confused one! This world is so tiny, almost nothing compared to the greatness of the universe. You would have me travel in a rocket to show me the ends of this tiny world? O mindless one! If it is necessary to travel in a rocket to see the ends of this world, then, to see Allah, you might ask: Aren't there rules and procedures to enable one to see Allah?

These eyes can't see. Allah can be seen but you must see Him with inner eyes (of the heart). One can't see with these physical eyes. We are Ahl as-Sunnah wa l-Jama'at, our belief is of the Ahl as-Sunnah. Such, that the awliya can see with inner eyes, but with physical eyes no one can see Allah in this world. That, only our Prophet could do. That honour was granted to our Master on the Night of Ascension. Seeing with physical eyes was given to him only. This rank, this honour was offered to him only. Prophet Musa had asked to see - "Ya Rabbi, You let me hear Your words". He fainted from the sweetness and pleasure of His words. Then Prophet Musa said, "O my Mawla, Owner of these words, Your words". It was not that these words were heard directly. Without means, no one may hear Divine Word in this world. In the Heavens Janabu l-Mawla will cause His faithful servants to hear on the strength of their iman. "The sweetness of Your words is enormous Ya Rabbi. Cause me to see by virtue of Divine Beauty. Fix my gaze, let my gaze be fixed on Your Beauty," he said. Allah said, "You cannot see, you cannot behold Me because your station and the ground on which you stand cannot endure it." The manifestation on Mount Tur was minute. The minutest manifestation caused Mount Tur to shatter into pieces. The Great Mountain was decreased and Prophet Musa fell down.

Allah displayed His Beauty to our Prophet on the Night Journey. The Ahl as-Sunnah hold this belief. We believe in this. Janabu l-Mawla has promised to display to His Muslim servants His Jamal (Beauty) in the heavens. May Allah grant us this favour; to be able to see His Jamal-i ba-Kamal. Try to qualify for this honour. While you have the opportunity, try to become competent in gaining this favour. O Muslims, make this your target! Your target must be to reach Jamālullah. What is your occupation? How have you benefited unless you qualify for this favour? If you should quit the world without it would this life have benefited you?

We have become oblivious, a community lacking awareness of real gain and real loss. Chasing after worldly affairs from dawn till dusk. At dusk, still running from here to there. What benefit will we gain? Are we mindful of the greatest benefit? Are we going after that? The biggest gain is to become eligible to behold Janabu l-Kibriya's Jamal-i ba-Kamal. This is the biggest profit. As you like, as you like! We have become baffled here, in assertions of 'me-you'. Leave that! Dunya is not yours, nor mine. Dunya belongs to everyone. Everyone in this World has his right/share of it. They will take their share and leave.

Don't forget your share of this world, says Janab Mawla. Astaidhu Billah: "Wala tansa nasībaka mina ad-dunya" (28:77) Don't forget your share of this world! His verses, surely the meanings of Janabu l-Mawla's holy verses are like vast oceans. We overstep our limits elaborating on the holy verses but our masters said: O heedless believers, when it is mentioned, "Don't forget your share", do not misunderstand that vineyards, orchards, gardens or buildings, apartments, gold, silver are being referred to! Allah Jalla wa Ala doesn't say not to forget naseeb. Your naseeb of this world is merely a shroud you'll be wrapped in.

Rolled up in a shroud you will go should you be fortunate enough to own one! Many are buried without a shroud. Some people depart from this world without a shroud. Your naseeb of this dunya is a roll of cotton material. In the end this is what goes with you. It is disliked (makruh) to have a fine material for a shroud. Also it is forbidden to have silk. An average shroud is 20 ells. With two garments and a cloth you will be sent to the other side. You may be a millionaire or a billionaire. However rich you are, you will be sent to the other side with it. If wrapped up with silk, the Angels meeting him will question: Why did you come with this silk shroud?

Look, who will meet you on the other side. You close the grave, shovel soil over it, but who will you meet beyond that? And how? The funeral may be ceremonious, but note this, consider who you will meet there! Will it be a dreadful meeting or will it be welcoming? Will you receive a cheerful welcome? What do you say, Haji Yashar? Consider it! What you gain from this world - your benefit O Muslim, must be that qualification: to be qualified to behold the Jamal-i ba-kamal of Janabu l-Haqq. Don't say I am this, I am that! You will not be asked! "Are you coming qualified, with a licence or unqualified, without a licence?"

People expend efforts just to get a car licence. A man is not given a driving licence easily. Ey, do you think beholding Janabu l-Haqq's Jamal-i ba-kamal will be given easily, or freely? Do you? No! Well, Janabu l-Haqq exists so that we can exist. Whatever is in existence is because Allah makes it exist. Nothing can exist by itself. Existence is only for Allah by Himself. And there can't be a measure for Janabu l-Haqq's Majestic Greatness. No way! We cannot find a measure for what He has created. Numbers are insufficient. How could it be possible to find a measure for Janab Allah's greatness and grandeur. To say to that confused one, look, and see Allah!

That confused community of Christians have saints whom we call ayyos. Those saints, we say 'hagia sophia,' whom we call ayyos, holy ones, their awliya, they have pictures of these saints. Nowadays ignorance is everywhere. When asked, no one knows Real Faith. And they want people to worship those in the pictures. They want people like this. They are not using their minds or, they don't have minds. Anyone who asks such a question has no mind! He has no knowledge at all. Allah exists. And Allah from pre-eternity to eternity is the Owner of Infinite Grandeur. In order to see something, you must be outside it, to be able to see.

The entire universe is just a dot in the Might Oceans of Janabu l-Haqq. Because His Oceans of Might are unlimited. In His Eternal, Infinite Might Oceans, the entire universe, whose size we do not know, is just a dot - swimming in His Oceans of Might. Because, as we said, in front of Him Who is abiding from pre-eternity to eternity, in His Greatness, a limited creation, no matter how big, simply dwindles. It is nothing! And this is why we are invited to believe in what is ghayb (Unseen).

O People, use your minds and in using your mind try to perceive Infinite Divine Majesty. Be on the way of adab and say "La ilaha illa Anta," O our Lord, there is no God but You! "La Ma'buda illa Anta" None other but You is worthy of worship. "La Khaliqa illa Anta," no Creator exists beside You. "La Raziqa illa Anta," no one Sustains but You. "La Mu'izza illa Anta," no other Bestower of Honours but You. Accept Allah Dhu l-Jalal with all His attributes and submit to Him in faith. The fish in the sea, the fish living in the seas, in the oceans, baby fish are asking the mother fish: the seas and oceans, show them to us! Yahu, that fish, that mother fish, how can it show them the oceans? They are inside the ocean, asking for the ocean!

Is there a place where Allah doesn't exist? Can you show me a particle that Allah doesn't encompass or His Might, Will and Knowledge don't surround? Show me a point that can stand without Allah sustaining it, O ignorant one! And when we are invited to believe in the Unseen, we accept the Existence of Janabu l-Haqq and we say: We believe in Allah! Secondly, Janabu l-Haqq invites us to believe in His Angels. Angels are invisible too. They too exist. But since they are created of light (nur) our beings cannot endure seeing them. But since our spirituality, that is to say, our souls/spirits belong to the Malakut, those in command over their spirituality, they behold with their spiritual power.

So the Anbiya Hazrat, the holy prophets, all beheld angels. They met, spoke, and saw the angels. Similarly, those whose spirituality overpowers their material being, awliya, in whom spiritual power dominates, it is possible for them to see the angels. They can see also. But those whose egos, material beings overshadow their spirituality, who are trapped under dark dirtiness, the awamu n-nas (common people), cannot see them. Who can control his ego, whose spirituality commands his ego, who is sultan of his ego, he can communicate with the Angelic Realm. All prophets were in touch with the Angelic Realm. Yes, every prophet and specially our Prophet, the Prophet of the End of Times, alaihi s-salatu wa s-salam, had contact with the Angelic Realm.

Though all were from humankind and Janab Allah had dressed them with humanity, how were they different? They were in contact with the Angelic Realm. Others have no contact with the Angelic Realm. Our Prophet is human like us in appearance but spiritually, he was in touch with the Divine Dominion (Arsh). Other prophets, according to their stations, reached the 1st level of the Angelic Realm. Or the 2nd, 3rd 4th, 5th, 6th, or 7th heaven, finally reaching Sidratu l-Muntaha where Jibril (Gabriel) was made to stop. But our Prophet's connection is to Arshu r-Rahman. Therefore, don't dare say the Prophet was a man like us. He was human in appearance like you, but, his Reality, was communing with the Arshu r-Rahman.

To where do you reach? Show what station you've reached! As nowadays, some confused people, in order to corrupt Islamic Belief, to decrease the value of our Prophet, some groups with corrupt ideas claim that our Prophet Hazrat Muhammed sallAllahu alaihi wa sallam, was a man like us. Nevertheless, it was he who reached Arshu r-Rahman! Where have you reached? Can you jump up one handspan? Can you hold yourself the width of a handspan above the floor? You are that heavy. You are heavier than this world. Not just a handspan-width but try lifting your feet a mere finger's width and see what you really are! The Prophet's reach extends to the Arshu r-Rahman.

Some, Jewish origins, are trying to decrease his value and power. Beware! Prophets are those in whom spirituality commands the material being. All of them have their miraj (ascension) according to their levels. And the awliya in their spiritual stations, all, have their ascension. Even Muslims with real faith, they too, according to their level of faith, have their ascension, have their miraj. A helicopter, flies at lower heights A plane flies higher. A jet flies even higher, it elevates to higher levels. A more powerful vehicle can rise even higher. According to the power of their motor, rockets with their motor power, as vehicles, go even further. Their engines and motors must be more powerful.

So, whatever level of faith you arrive at in this world, your soul will attain to that level when you pass away. Everyone has their own station. "Wa likullin darajātun mimmā `amilū" (6:132) Everyone will reach a degree according to their deeds, their actions. You can achieve that degree by your deeds. However, if dunya is more valuable than these degrees for you, you will waste your valuable life. Furthermore, there are some who are trapped in the world, they can't ascend. There are some who descend, below ground level. There are darajat (Higher levels) and darakat (Lowest levels). Ascending ones, descending ones.

O Muslim look at yourself, are you from ascending ones, are you from descending ones? Our days are passing, our end is approaching. Stop supporting empty claims! Don't say, 'I am this, I am that'. Whoever you are in this world, ask yourself this question, am I from the ascending or am I from the descending ones? Is it towards darajat, as our Prophet, alaihi s-salatu wa s-salam ascended to? Is my effort towards those ascending degrees or towards descending ones? O Muslim beware! Our time is the End of Times. You may hear these words again or not, but keep them in mind!

This is the summary of this sohbat! Don't say, 'I am a sheikh, I am a pasha, I am a hafiz, I am a preacher, I am a scholar, I am rich, I am an MP, I am prime minister, I am president, I am a sultan,' don't say I am this, I am that! All these will remain in this world. But you must observe yourself! Say: Our days are lessening, is it towards ascending degrees, or towards descending degrees? Some are moving up. Some when fired upward, return to smash to the ground. Beware! Because of a deficiency, it is unable to rise. Turn back. Many people are like this. Beware of this.

Ad-dinu nasihah. Religion is advice, our Prophet said, alaihi s-salatu wa s-salam. Advice is well-wishing your Muslim brother, to say, what is good for him. Insha Allah our association is such an association. May it be a blessed association, such, that there is spiritual confirmation in these words to help all of us to be from the ascending ones, not to be from the descending ones. Follow the Prophet! You will rise up! Don't go astray, you will descend! May Allah grant us to follow Habibullah and gain success in ascending in the Prophet's way. May He turns our heedlessness into awareness. May Allah correct our wrongdoings, clean away the dirtiness of our sins and honour us with His unique guidance.

Bi hurmati-l Habib, bi hurmati l-Fatiha.
