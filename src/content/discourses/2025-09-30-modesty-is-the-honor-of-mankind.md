As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Our Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says, “إِن لَمْ تَسْتَحِي فَاصْنَعْ مَا شِئْتَ”. He says, “If you are not shy, then do whatever you want.”

Without hayā (modesty), a person can do anything. Modesty is from īmān. Modesty is adab. Not everything is free. Everything has its limits and boundaries.

If everyone lives as they please - Of course, there is no unlimited freedom. Unlimited freedom would then infringe upon other’s freedom. Then, chaos would ensue.

The laws of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla are the best for humanity. There are many laws made by mankind that suit their own ego and shayṭān. Laws have been enacted that command and protect indecency and rudeness.

This is practiced in Western countries. They make people do what they want and prohibit what they want. They often prohibit what is good. If you try to do good, or say something, they condemn you.

That’s what happens when there’s no modesty. Modesty is the honour of humanity. Modesty is what distinguishes humans from animals.

Even some animals do special things. They have behaviours that make them like humans. They respect their siblings, mothers, and fathers, and so on. They don’t harm them.

Now, people are worse than animals. They have made all kinds of indecency and deeds permissible. After that, they torment those who are modest. They dislike them. Modesty is the honor of mankind. It is what makes a person human.

May Allah not deprive people of modesty. When they embrace Islām, shukr to Allah, since all kinds of beauty are in Islām, mankind reaches the highest levels in this world and the hereafter.

The level of īmān is the highest. It is the highest attribute. It is the favour of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. Who has it attains every good thing.

May Allah grant them all īmān and guide people, inshāAllah.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
