Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

A’ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm:

(Qur’ān 09:36). Allah ﷻ states in the Holy Quran: ‘Indeed, the number of months with Allah ﷻ is twelve [lunar] months.’ In the sight of Allah ﷻ, the number of months is twelve. Allah ﷻ created the months and years. The months are twelve. These months, the lunar months, are important in our lives. The month of Dhu al-Qi’dah has just started. These months are called Ashhur al-Hurum, the Sacred Months; Rajab, Dhul-Qa’dah, Dhul-Hijjah and Muharram. These last three come consecutively, and are the months of Ḥajj. “Don’t fight in those months. But if they hurt you, you can retaliate and take that precaution”, says Allah Azza wa Jalla.

These are holy months. It was going normally every year, but this year a strange state happened that they are not taking anyone for Ḥajj this year. Muslims will not be able to perform Ḥajj, only some will, because there is an illness. This year has been a strange year, Allah ﷻ knows the wisdom.

Mankind thinks it will go on like this forever. Whereas, even if your lifespan is sixty, seventy, eighty, maybe even a hundred, one hundred and ten years, it is still nothing. Even in that state, he thinks that he will never die. He thinks that everything will go on the same. Things do not go on the same. Allah Azza wa Jalla transforms this world from one state to another. Allah Azza wa Jalla is doing this with His own will. If it were up to mankind, he would do everything according to his own mind, he would control everything. But he can’t control it. Even the slightest thing such as rain destroys people, wind destroys them, and it is the same for the illness. Everything is in the hands of Allah Azza wa Jalla. He does whatever He wants at any moment. Whatever He wants happens. Allah Azza wa Jalla can do anything.

Therefore, this year is a different year. Since there was no Ḥajj either, very big things have happened, and there will be more. May Allah ﷻ help and protect Muslims. May He strengthen our faith insha’Allah. Believers and Muslims take a lesson, but those who are not become worse. May Allah ﷻ protect us, and may Allah ﷻ guide them. May these months be blessed insha’Allah. May they be with Barakah for us insha’Allah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
