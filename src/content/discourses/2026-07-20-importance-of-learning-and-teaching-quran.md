As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
إِنَّ هَٰذَا الْقُرْآنَ يَهْدِي لِلَّتِي هِيَ أَقْوَمُ
<br>“Inna hādhā l-Qur'āna yahdī lillatī hiya aqwam”<br>
“Indeed, this Qur’an guides to that which is most suitable.” (Qur’an: 17:9). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

Qur’ān ʿAẓīmu sh-Sha’n is the greatest miracle of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. There is no greater miracle than it. Because everything from awwalīn to ākhirīn, from the beginning to the end, is written in it. There is nothing missing in it.

Therefore, Qur’ān ʿAẓīmu sh-Sha’n is a great blessing for Muslims. All kinds of goodness are in it. Barakah is in it, healing is in it, guidance is in it. It also prevents man from all kinds of evil. That is, it commands good and forbids evil. Moreover, it is the word of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla that humankind can hold in his hands in this world. There’s nothing like it.

As for other books besides the Qur’ān ʿAẓīmu sh-Sha’n – the Bible, the Psalms, the Torah – all of them are the word of Allah, but nothing remains of them. They have been distorted and altered over time. People have written them as it suited them to deceive the people. But Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla’s book, without a single letter or meaning changed, remains with us until the Day of Judgment exactly as it was revealed to our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, shukr to Allah.

Therefore, learning and teaching the Qur’ān is a great reward. Even during summer holidays, they teach it to children; may Allah be pleased with them. If children learn it at a young age, it will benefit them throughout their lives. That’s what’s beneficial.

People spend all they have on unnecessary things just so their children can study. Yet, what is truly necessary is the Qur’ān. The most important thing they should learn first is the Qur’ān.

Shukr to Allah, during the summer, imāms also teach children in mosques as much as they can. They also receive the greatest reward. They are the best people; those who learn and teach the Qur’ān are the best. Of course, becoming a hāfiz is not important. What is more important than becoming a hāfiz is knowing and being able to read the Qur’ān. It is much more acceptable to read the letters exactly as Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla revealed them and as taught by our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam.

Therefore, those who say, “It’s not easy to learn,” are mistaken; in fact, it is very easy. There are those who learn this word taught by Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla in even three days. There are those who learn it in a month, and those who learn it in a year. All that matters is that they begin and continue. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla has made the reward and thawāb for this very great.

This is the word of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. There is no word more sacred than it on earth. There is no word more sacred than it in the entire universe. Therefore, it is necessary to know its value.

Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam says that for each letter of the Mus’haf we hold in our hands, that is, the Qur’ān ʿAẓīmu sh-Sha’n, which is the word of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla, there are ten rewards. Each word may have three letters, five letters, or seven letters. For each of those letters, there are ten rewards. Not only ten rewards, but also the forgiveness of ten sins and an elevation of ten degrees.

So, Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla bestows His barakah and mercy upon us, and on top of that, we earn so much reward. Therefore, may Allah grant us clarity of mind; may we learn and teach easily, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
