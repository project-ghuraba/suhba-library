Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Allah Azza wa Jalla created man forgetful. ‘ناسنإ’, ‘Man’ comes from ‘نايسن’ which means ‘Forgetfulness’. Therefore, when you do business or agree on something, He tells us to write it down. If you write it, then nobody can claim otherwise saying, “I did this, I did that...”. It will definitely be clear. Now most people say, “I told you so.” The other says, “No, you didn’t.” When you say “I did it, you did it...” there will be a conflict. After that, it is not clear who is right and who is wrong.

Allah Azza wa Jalla clearly tells us to write, ‘ُُهوُبُتكْ اَف’, ‘Write it down.’ (Qur’ān 02:282). Everyone knows how to write, thank Allah ﷻ. Hundreds of thousands of schools were built, but they are not teaching anything anyway. At least they teach how to write, yet people do not use it. They do not use things that are beneficial, but they do other things in vain. They read and such but they do not do what should be done. Allah ﷻ says “write” when something is done. Don’t say that he is a friend, he is an acquaintance. If that were the case, Allah ﷻ would’ve said not to write if he was your friend or your acquaintance.

It is Allah’s ﷻ wisdom that He gave man forgetfulness. As we said ‘ناسنإ’, ‘Man’ comes from ‘نايسن’, ‘Forgetfulness’. Forgetfulness is also a blessing from Allah ﷻ. If people did not forget, most people would not be able to survive the torments and pains they suffered in this world. They are gradually forgotten over time. As they are forgotten, the agreements and everything is forgotten. That’s why Allah Azza wa Jalla says “write” so that there is no conflict. This is an important thing, but nobody does it. They do not intend to do it saying, “So that they don’t catch us later. Let’s not write now. If we do, it will cause trouble or something bad will happen to us later.”

Therefore, this is an important issue. They should think carefully about what they want to do, and write according to it and not change it afterwards. Allah ﷻ made it easy for mankind so that there would be no conflict between them. Like in everything else, people do not follow Allah’s ﷻ orders in this too. After that, a lot of messy things happen to them. May Allah ﷻ protect us. May Allah ﷻ help us all.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
