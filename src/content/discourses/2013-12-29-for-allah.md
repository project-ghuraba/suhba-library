Give to everyone who's asking. Don't stop them. Give. Don't look at paper. Don't look even if they bring gold. Give and don't fear. Fatiha. No one is fighting his ego. Everyone is running after banknotes. No one is running after Divine pleasure, pleasure of Habibullah. What a pity! May Allah accept us for His servanthood. May we open. Fatiha. O Muslims, keep Islam, Allah will keep you. If you leave Islam, Allah will leave you on your own. You won't be able to do anything. Be for Allah. Live for Allah. Serve for Allah so that you become a lion. Fatiha.
