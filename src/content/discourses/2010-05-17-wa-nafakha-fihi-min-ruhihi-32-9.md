Mawlana Shaykh Muhammad Nazim al-Haqqani al-Qubrusi, sohbat May 17, 2010.

Madad. La ilaha illa Allah La ilaha illa Allah La ilaha illa Allah Muhammad Rasulullah alayhi salatullah wa salamuh. Dastur Ya Rijal Allah. Madad, Ya Sahib al Imdad, madad. Audhu Billahi min as-shaytani r-rajim Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim.

O Mankind, you must know what is good for you or, who is asking a good luck for you or who is asking a brilliant future for you or who is asking heavenly levels up and up for you and you must know who is against you, against your existence. That is most important point that ulama must make clear. Who is asking for our good future and for our real honour, who is asking an eternal life in countless measure and enjoyments and honours and praise from the Lord of Heavens, Allahu Akbar! Allahu Akbar wa Lillahi l-hamd. That is most important point that ulama must make clear for mankind.

Assalamu alaykum Attenders. I am a weak servant but I have responsibility from heavenly, holy ones. I am sitting here and I have been ordered to make as they like, an understanding. To make clear our positions because they are looking and seeing now, all the world’s people, all human nature they are never thinking on such points. What is their honour and praise here and in heavenly presence of the Lord of Heavens.

They are not coming here for eating and drinking. People are thinking - common people who are not thinking, they're thinking, imagining or thinking that to be in this life, their real target, because people now are thinking that their last target from beginning up to end is to eat and drink and to do their sexual desires. To fulfil everything that belongs to their material being. And that is really wrong because the Lord of Heavens never created men as animals. We have a special being among all creation. At the beginning He was saying, "I am bringing in existence a new creation, a new creature".

At the beginning He was declaring to the heavenly beings. Subhanallah. Heavenly beings are knowing about heavenly beings. Angels, their limits is through the heavens but, over the heavens there are some other levels that belong to Divine existence. Angels were knowing about heavenly panorama. They are in it but beyond that they are not knowing, therefore, the Lord of Heavens saying, declaring to them 'I am bringing a new creature'. Yes. And they should be deputies on earth. Subhanallah. Astaidh Billah,

wa idh qala Rabbuka li l-mala'ikati inni ja'ilun fi l-ardi khalifa (2:30).

And it is so full, with such secrets. Secrets like oceans. Angels may look on surface of oceans but they are not knowing what is through oceans. How deep is depth of oceans. They only looked as you are looking now to an ocean, seeing only water. But through that we are not knowing. And angels looking and seeing that is an ocean. And to say khalifa, deputy of which the angels are only knowing an understanding of that holy word. But to where reaching or where beginning up to where ending, they are not knowing. They are saying 'Oh! Khalifa'. Subhanallah, that holy verse is like an ocean, inni ja'ilun fi l-ardi khalifa. Allahu Akbar.

The Almighty needs khalifas? O ulama. What the Lord of Heavens is asking to say, you know? No one knows, only He knows. We are looking as a person is looking at an ocean, but through that ocean, who is responsible for that ocean. No one knows. "I am making a khalifa". For what? For that ocean of creation. "I am creating a new creature and honouring him and praising him also, as he belongs to Me". Allahu Akbar. Everything belongs to Him. When we are saying 'belongs to Him', you must not understand that He was needy for that new creation. No. Or you must not understand that the Lord of Heavens is needy or He was needy to create countless creations, countless levels, countless creatures. He wasn't needy. Subhanallahi l-Aliyyi l-Azim.

Therefore angels were surprised and saying, “Our Lord, You are making a deputy fi l-ardi khalifa’, not using alif-laam, that new creation, no one knowing their real position. Or, what is the Creator's will for creating khalifa on earth. That means that dunya, ard, earth, was created before. When it was created no one knows, only He knows. Who was on it, on that planet? Earth without khalifa? And that declaration was that there is no time in existence, no space in existence. And He said, "I am making," not saying, "I am creating". Saying, "ja'ilun," appointing. Appointing a new creature, those who were in "My Secret Oceans I am taking from there, putting here".

Must be, not saying ‘creating’, was in Divine Presence, who never knowing the beginning of their existence, asking to bring in existence people, to see that this earth is never going to be without an owner. And the Lord of Heavens asking to declare to heavenly beings, angel’s worlds, to know about themselves and about their creation and about their being in existence. For whom? Angels asking to know for whom they were created.

O ulama, Assalamu alaykum. We must make clear holy verses, their meaning from the treasures of wisdoms and knowledge that are granted to that one. Asking for this to be made clear or for whom this creation was prepared. It was not for angels. And the Lord of Heavens making it clear by saying, inni ja'ilun fi l-ardi khalifa. I am putting there a khalifa, deputy. Deputy can't be anyone. Allah Almighty's deputy. Because he is from pre-eternity up to eternity. He is not in need. He is not living His existence to be a khalifa after Him. He is Huwa l-Awwalu wa l-Akhir wa l-Zahiru wa l-Batin.

Allah Almighty is asking to create a new creation who were through Divine Presence to show Alam al-Malakut, heavenly people, to make known to heavenly people for whom this creation came in existence. What was aim or real target for bringing that deputy to be on this earth. That is important. O Salafi ulama, you must try to make your understandings more sharp, sharper. You must not imprison your understanding and close your understanding not to touch right hand, left hand, front and back, up and down. It is an opening, a new opening, to come a new creation in existence.

And you must know O ulama, Azhar Sharif ulama and Shi'a ulama and every kind of religious people, you must understand and you must declare that understanding according to the level of understanding of common people. We are common people, we are going running, coming down, but for what? Fro what? Ey, Saleem. Saleem. Clean understanding one. They are asking why we are here? Why we have been on this planet. What is real wisdom for ourselves to come in existence? You must make clear O ulama, that point to people. That we are creatures, created for some unexpected honour.

That is some reality to make people's conscience in sukun, to calm down, give itminan, contentment so that people may say that we are in existence just for this purpose, or that purpose then they should reach contentment. And all prophets coming, peace be upon all of them particularly Seal of Prophets, Sayyidina Muhammad, madad Ya Sultan ul-Anbiya, they are their real targets – mission. They have been loaded from heavens that our beings reaching to heavenly beings, but on this planet we have been dressed from such a material earth, water, fire, air. Our real being just powered and imprisoned through these four elements.

It is going to be like an egg. If we are putting under a hen, sitting on it, as an egg. After a while getting chickens and like this we are here now through four elements, our real being was in it but it was needy to be through this level of life on this planet. For chicken 3 weeks, for mankind on this planet, till their real beings going to appear for some years, for some months, for some more or less. And when death coming, death opening and they are seeing their reality there. And they should be on a level that is unexpected. They can't be able to expect through this life and we are in need, surrounded by 4 elements.

Wa nafakha fihi min ruhihi (32:9)

Allah Almighty saying, Subhanahu wa Ta'ala, "wa nafakha fihi min ruhihi" O ulama, where is that? You understanding Holy Quran? Where is Holy Quran, where are our levels? This is first and most simple level to make people understand about themselves. What Allah Almighty saying, "wa nafakha fihi min ruhihi". What is that? You know? For what, just blown into Man? What was that? Min ruhihi. What is that ruh? What is that unknown identity that is granted to mankind? Subhanallah. Say! Give a meaning for: wa nafakha fihi min ruhihi. If you are ulama say. What is that "min ruhihi"? Ruh - do you think ruh ul-quds? Hmm? Ruhu l-qudus or Ruh ul-Quddūs? What you are understanding? You must learn and you must teach people their heavenly positions that they have been granted from Divine Presence. And then people may run, may do their best to be qualified for that honour. And that opening when coming, endlessly, endlessly, blessings coming on them. Therefore we have been ordered from beginning up to end to do our best so that we can reach the best level, best position, best understanding, best qualification to be in our Lord's Divine Presence. That Divine Presence - don't think is one level, but countless. Try to reach, and to touch, and to enter those countless levels that now are veiled from our beings because we have been created from anasir arb'a, four elements. Allahu Akbar.

Now we are in heedlessness. Running after the most honoured creation is shaytan, to make them not to do their best but shaytanic beings are trying to urge mankind to do their worst. And prophets coming and saying O People do your best, shaytan coming and saying, “No, to do your best, no good for you. Come and follow me, I am teaching you such ways you should be so happy, so in pleasure in happiness and enjoyment”. Therefore people coming.

A few ones going and accepting holy ones who are calling and saying to people, 'O People come and do your best'. Very few. And majority are following shaytanic teachings that shaytan is trying to put through mentalities of mankind, worst things. And worst things going to be veils, veils, veils, countless veils, and can't reach to Divine Presence. May Allah forgive us. O People, come and listen. And try to understand you have been created for an understanding. If you are not understanding yourself, you can't understand who created you. May Allah forgive us.

Astaghfirullah,

dum dum dum dum.
