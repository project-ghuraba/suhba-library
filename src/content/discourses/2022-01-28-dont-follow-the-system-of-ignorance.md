As-Salāmu ‘Alaykum wa Raḥmatullāhi wa Barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu Billāhi Minash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm.

Wa ṣ-Salātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Akhirīn.

Madad yā RasūlAllāh, Madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, Madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

Dastūr Mawlana Shaykh Abdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistāni, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-

Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-Suḥbah wa l-Khayru fi l-Jam‘iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm:

(Qur’ān 39:64). ‘Qul ‘Afaghayra Allāhi Ta’murūnnī ‘A`budu ‘Ayyuhā l-Jāhilūn’, ‘Say, [O Muhammad, to the disbelievers], “Is it other than Allah that you order me to worship, O ignorant ones?”.’ It is Ayati Karimah, Sadaqa Llahu l-‘Azim. No one can be worshipped other than Allah ﷻ. This Ayati Karimah says, “You are ordering to worship someone other than Allah ﷻ, O ignorant ones!”

A person who worships someone other than Allah ﷻ is ignorant. They are ignorant ones, because the ignorant ones are the majority now. Ignorant people are always the majority in this world. Even if someone is educated, he is ignorant. Even if he is a professor, he is ignorant. Even if he is an instructor, he is ignorant. Whoever worships someone other than Allah ﷻ is ignorant. He is nothing else. Knowledge is to know Allah ﷻ. Whoever doesn’t know Allah ﷻ is ignorant. No matter how educated he is, no matter how many books he has read, [whether] hundreds or thousands, it has no benefit. No matter how many universities he has graduated from, he is ignorant in the same way.

Universities are opening now everywhere masha’Allah. This is in our countries. In order to spread ignorance, universities are opening. Universities are not for knowledge but for ignorance. Most of those who go to university, may Allah ﷻ protect us, lose their Iman if they had it. Those who pray five times a day quit praying eventually and follow the system of ignorance there.

People send [their children] for studying. They go to any lengths by selling their gardens, property and whatever they have so that their daughter or son becomes a scholar and a useful person. They don’t teach anything. They only consume their money and give a paper which says that you are a scholar, not an ignorant one. No, a complete ignorant! They don’t learn anything anyways. The only thing they learn is ignorance, lack of Adab/manners and harm. I am telling this openly.

We have fallen behind. Backward countries are doing this. The more universities are in these ignorant countries, the more ignorance there is. In Europe, we see them as advanced. In terms of Dunya, they are advanced. They don’t enroll someone who won’t study at university. And from those who will study, they ask for a fee. It’s not for free. “You are going to be jobless here. But you have to pay this money back while working”. Nothing is free. Our people acquire a system. But they neglect that system and make it worse. They turn useful people into useless ones. They turn them into harmful creatures.

Therefore, who doesn’t know Allah ‘Azza wa-Jalla is ignorant. Universities don’t benefit from the majority. Since fifty years, our people have been trying to be accepted by Europe and Africa by claiming to have many diplomas. Don’t deal with this. Enroll those who will study. Many people are needed in other fields as well. Don’t drag them. Teach Adab/good manners and discipline to people. Schools used to teach Adab and discipline. Apart from knowledge, Adab and discipline are the most important. Not ignorant people but scholars will appear then. Useful people will appear.

May Allah ﷻ give mind and understanding to all of us, because we lack understanding. Backward countries waste people on worthless interests. They are harmed themselves afterwards too. May Allah ﷻ protect the Muslim youth from this foolishness. May Allah ﷻ help us insha’Allah and give us Iman. Iman is the most important thing.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.

There are completed Qur’an Khatms, Yasin, Tabaraka, Ayat and Surahs. They’ve recited them all over the world. Tasbihat, Salawat to our Holy Prophet ﷺ, Dalail Al Khairat. We present all of them to the presence of Allah ‘Azza wa-Jalla, and we gift them to our Holy Prophet ﷺ, Ahlu l-Bayt, the Sahabah, all Awliya, Anbiya and Asfiya, to the souls of Mashayikh, and all Mu’mins and Mu’minahs, Muslims and Muslimahs. May all the good goals of those who have recited be achieved. For happiness here and hereafter.

LiLlahi Ta’ala l-Fatiha.
