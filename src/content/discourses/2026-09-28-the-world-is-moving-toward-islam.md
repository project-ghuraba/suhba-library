As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam states that one of the most virtuous deeds is,<br> 
إِدْخَالُ السُّرُورِ في قَلْبِ الْمُؤْمِنِ
<br>“Idkhālu s-surūr fī qalbi l-mu’min”—bringing happiness and joy into the heart of a believer. [al-Bayhaqi, Shu’ab al-Imān]

Shukr to Allah, this journey of ours [to Berlin] was undertaken for the sake of Allah. Our brothers there were gladdened, and their hearts were filled with joy. Inshā’Allāh, we have earned Allah’s pleasure, and they have too; for this is a joy sought for the sake of Allah, not for worldly gain. This is what Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla wants: that believers help one another, love one another, and offer naṣīḥah to one another. That was our intention, inshā’Allāh. May Allah accept the duʿās.

Of course, they are in a land of disbelief, but—shukr to Allah—even in the land of kufr, they have mosques, masjids, and dergahs. They receive help through them. They hold fast to them so as not to lose their way in that environment of disbelief. In reality, disbelief itself holds no true power, but shayṭān possesses strength; he employs every kind of trickery to lead people astray. Yet, inshā’Allāh, Allah will protect the Muslims and the believers. By remaining united and helping one another in this way, they will stand firm, inshā’Allāh.

Indeed, it is certain that the world is moving toward Islām. No matter how the state of the world may appear, in the end—inshā’Allāh with Allah’s permission—everyone will become Muslim through Mahdi ʿalayhi s-salām. May Allah hasten the arrival of those days.

May He protect us all from the evil of shayṭān, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
