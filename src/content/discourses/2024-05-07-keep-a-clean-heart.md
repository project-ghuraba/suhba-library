As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Wa-min ’ābā’ihim wa-dhurriyyātihim wa-ikhwānihim wa-jtabaynāhum wa-hadaynāhum ilá ṣirāṭin mustaqīmin. Dhālika hudá Llāhi yahdī bihī man yashā’u min ‘ibādihī wa-law ashrakū la-ḥabiṭa ‘anhum mā kānū ya‘malūn” (6:87-88). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘Aẓīm. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is saying this way is the way of the prophets. It is a favour from AllāhNone to people. Everyone should benefit from that favour. The fathers, their sons, their brothers, their descendants, all of them will be blessed by AllāhNone when they follow this way. Until when? Until they rebel against AllāhNone, disbelieve in AllāhNone, or commit shirk, they are all safe and in the grace of AllāhNone. This way is the way of our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, the prophets before himNone, and the righteous ones. That way will remain with the awliyā’ and mashāyikh of the ummah until the end. When the Day of Judgment approaches, they will pass away. After that, kufr remains. Therefore, as we are in the end of times, in order to be safe from that, we must be steadfast on this way.

Shukr to AllāhNone, today is the tenth anniversary of Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim. His himmah (support) and barakah are present and upon us. A protector, shukr to AllāhNone. Our greatest service to him is to be together – with our families, with our children, with other Muslims as brothers – to be together and to continue on the way. Of course, every now and then, shayṭān causes fitnah and disputes among people, but forgiveness... Humans are always sinners anyway. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla forgives the sin after repenting. You should not leave anything in your heart. You must never have a stained heart towards a Muslim, towards your Muslim brother. It must be clean and pure.

In any case, what ṭarīqah teaches is to crush one’s ego, to control one’s ego. To follow one’s ego causes harm both to oneself and one's spirituality. Therefore, a person’s heart must be clean. No matter what anyone else says, you will see it as good. You will not think badly. If you think badly, it will become a burden to you. Maybe the other person actually said it to you as a bad thing. But if you look at that evil as evil and it breaks your heart, it becomes a burden to you. A while later, something else happens. Your ego gets used to it, “This man did this to me. This man did that to me,” and your heart is filled with darkness, over and over again. There is no benefit in that darkness. It does harm, not benefit. Therefore, this occasion is Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim’s. Shukr to AllāhNone, Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim is with us every day. We do not consider him to have left us. We feel that we are together. There is no missing for him, shukr to AllāhNone. Because missing him means he is far away from you. Shukr to AllāhNone he is always with us, but this occasion also shows us how the days pass. Ten years ago, on this day, this place was filled with crying and lamenting. Now, shukr to AllāhNone, as he is with us, there is no such thing. Perpetual happiness, shukr to AllāhNone. The hearts of those who are with them are happy. There is never any sadness. Because he is with AllāhNone, AllāhNone is with him. AllāhNone is Ḥayy (Alive) and Dā’im (Everlasting). HisNone awliyā’ also have the attribute of Ḥayy because they are with HimNone. Shukr to AllāhNone.

Therefore, do not think that the shaykh will be pleased when there is darkness in your heart. You must clean that darkness. If you haven't, your shaykh will make du‘ā’ in the Presence of AllāhNone, to remove that darkness from your heart. He is not pleased with your state. Until this state is removed, never he will be pleased. Shukr to AllāhNone, the mashāyikh are also doing that. The power of the mashāyikh is great. May AllāhNone not separate us from their way. May AllāhNone be pleased with all of you.

Mā shā’a Llāh, with the barakah of our shaykh, there are many people who came to this way and entered it. In shā’a Llāh, the Ṣāḥib comes and we submit to him. We submit to him all together so that the way will be one and there will be no separation. In any case, that separation now is not considered a separation, but shayṭān still puts some things in between. Therefore, in shā’a Llāh, the Ṣāḥib comes. The whole Islamic world and humanity submit to him, so that the way will be more and and more beautiful for everyone.

Our request is not just for ourselves, but for all humanity. This is a very big way, a very wide way. Even if there were ten worlds like this one they would still fit inside. Even if there were a thousand or a million, they could join this way. Shukr to AllāhNone, the thought that if someone else enters this path, there won't be room for me, doesn't exist with us in Islām. May AllāhNone be pleased with you all.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying: Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm, “Wa-min ’ābā’ihim wa-dhurriyyātihim wa-ikhwānihim wa-jtabaynāhum wa-hadaynāhum ilá ṣirāṭin mustaqīmin. Dhālika hudá Llāhi yahdī bihī man yashā’u min ‘ibādihī wa-law ashrakū la-ḥabiṭa ‘anhum mā kānū ya‘malūn” (6:87-88). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘Aẓīm. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying: there is people, from their ancestor from generation, their family, their relative, their – all this. Some of them, AllāhNone choose them and praise them, and they, HeNone put them in straight way. This is from AllāhNone, blessed from AllāhNone, gift from AllāhNone. This all, they are blessed, praised, until, or any of them, they going out of way of AllāhNone, and be unbeliever, or be put another thing, worshipping something without Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. This, until that time, all this people they are blessed and AllāhNone happy with them. This is, for all prophets, like this. Their family, their... and after, what their nations who follow them, all of them they're chosen by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla and praised. And they must be, like this, to be happy, to be in good way, which it is way of AllāhNone, way of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, way of awliyā’u Llāh. All this, they are doing this. “We doing this for, we not asking,” they said, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, “In huwa illā dhikrun li-l-‘ālamīn” (81:27). “We don't want anything from you, we only want to remember [remind] you, to remember [remind] the human being, for this way. Because human being, there's nobody by himself can find way of AllāhNone. Only very rare, like Sayyidunā Ibrāhīm, ‘alayhi s-salām, he look for sun, for moon, for planet. After, he said, “This is not my Lord.” By himself, he find Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. But others, must be someone to show them the way of AllāhNone. And it is important to be connected with this holy, blessed people, which they are prophets.

So, our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, he guide for mashāyikh, for good people, for ‘ulamā’, for scholars. And we are following them, al-ḥamdu liLlāh. And this is one of them, Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh ad-Dāghistānī, Mawlānā Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. They are giant. They are, Mawlānā Shaykh was saying, sometimes, “king size,” they said. They are kings, al-ḥamdu liLlāh. Their way, way of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Who follow them with sincerity, with good intention, they are winner in dunyā and ākhirah.

And this is important: to not look for anything from human being, especially to be, not care about what they are saying. “You are here, you are there. You respect me, you not respect me.” It is not important. Important, to be in this way. To know, this is mashāyikh, they are happy with you, and you be there. What mashāyikh they like? They like their murīd. They like to make much people, as much they can bring for Islām, for way of AllāhNone, ṭarīq. They are happy and they are giving their lesson for them to be, how to behave with people, with other.

First thing, behave, good behaviour must be to do. And maybe for this also to be good thinking for people, ḥusnu ẓ-ẓānn. This is very important, because many people even they have misunderstanding. They put with misunderstanding, you must be not take care about this. Don't listen to your ego saying, “Oh this man he, maybe he is not happy with, maybe he doing this, that.” When you do this, you put one black spot in your heart. Next, second one, third, fourth, and at the end it will be, your heart, black. And this is because you [got] used to do[ing] this. If you not used [to] it, from beginning, it will not be there.

But also, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are with mashāyikh. They are taking care about their murīd. And they are looking, what they have from bad thing in their heart, or not, they cannot, do something. All this, they are complet[ing], and make, put them in Divine Presence of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla and Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, to be clean. This also one benefit of ṭarīqah. And it is better to not, to make shaykh to look your heart, your heart not clean. Better to be, he will be much happy if you find your heart clean. And you be restful, and he will be happy. But when you have darkness in your heart, it is heaviness for you before others. So you must be clean, and be thinking good for everyone. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are in tenth year of remembrance of Mawlānā. And he is, from his power – we are saying here – we are not missing him. Why? Because he is with us every minute. We not before ten years like this, we was crying, we was very unhappy. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, but after three days, he change this situation. We have satisfied and we are happy, because every minute we are thinking about him, and we are knowing he is not let us without him. This is his power. He is not ordinary awliyā’u Llāh. He is real Sulṭān.

So for this, we must be more careful, because he is more... inside us. So be careful for everything you do, for yourself or for others also. AllāhNone bless you. He is more happy, when he see, because what his mission, to make human being as human being. Take them from animal level to human being level. Human being level, the real one, it is more higher than angel even. So, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are thanking him for this. AllāhNone bless him, and make his degree higher, and to be with him whole time, in shā’a Llāh, forever, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
