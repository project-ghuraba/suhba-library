As-salāmu ‘alaykum wa raḥmatu Llāhi wa barakātuh. A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistāni, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqāni. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

Our Prophet (saw) says in a holy ḥadīth: "Some people will not be looked at on the Day of Judgement, they won't be given value". Who are those people? They are the ones, who share Allāh's dress of pride - pride is for Allāh. "al-kibriyā’u ridā’ī" says Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla. Pride, greatness, honor is with Allāh Azza wa Jalla. He doesn't give anyone His dress of proudness because we are all weak servants. A wise person should know this. If he is mindless he won't have value, he'll be punished. He'll both be punished and have no value.

Allāh doesn't share that attribute of proudness. Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla created everyone the same. Nobody was created according to their wish, to choose "I should be so, I should be beautiful, I should be rich, I should be powerful" - there is no option, we don't have a choice. There is a nice poem about our Prophet (saw), "Khuliqta kamā tashā’u" it says. "Allāh created you as you liked." He created the Prophet (saw) in the most beautiful way. It is what Allāh wants. Just as people do whatever they like, Allāh created the Prophet (saw) in the best way. Even he (saw) wasn't proud, he (saw) was humble. He could talk with a child, with someone weak, poor, rich. He (saw) could talk the same way to everyone. He (saw) wasn't proud. Pride is only with shayṭān and his followers. Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla doesn't accept this. He punishes the proud servants.

Allāh also doesn't look at people, who doubt His orders. Allāh doesn't give them value. Allāh has orders for people. You have to do them without hesitating. We have to believe, pray and fast. We have to obey all rules without having any doubt. What happens if you don't do this? You'll lose. You'll have no value. You will not be looked at. When Allāh orders, it isn't permissible to say "I doubt it". That weakens the belief. There is a strong order. Even if you can't do it, you should say "we couldn't". But if you doubt, you'll lose.

Allāh (awj), the Prophet (saws) saying He is not looking for people, not giving any value for these people who are proud people because proudness only for Allāh (awj). He is real being, He only can wear this dress. He said "dress of proudness". He is not happy to share it with anyone, nobody can share this dress of proudness. He said, "al-Kibriyā’u ridā’ī" proudness is My cloak. So nobody can share it. If trying to share it, he will be punished, and he will be in Qiyāmah, in Judgement Day no value at all for him. In life he become proud and try to make himself something. Maybe some people they are giving value but in Divine Presence in Judgement Day he has no value at all. Because real being only - the Lord Allāh (awj). The else, not only one the real being. And He is create everyone, not give them this proudness.

The worst attribute, proudness. It is from shayṭān and his followers. He said "I am, I am better." What it means? He is proud. Nobody created by himself. Everybody Allāh created us, and so no reason to make proudness. Even Prophet (saws), Allāh He created him. But He created him in the best thing, the highest attribute, highest everything. And there is poem in Arabic saying, they're saying "Allāh create you as you like to be." How many people they would like to be more powerful, more handsome, more beautiful, more long, short, everything. What people they have many things (they) imagine to they can everybody, they say "maybe if I am taller 10 cm it will be better. If I was fit it would be better." Something like this. Our Prophet (saws) Allāh He just create him in the best shape can ever be, and he is not proud.

Never Prophet (saws) he was proud. He was with everybody - small, big, old, weak, with everybody he was the same. No proudness for him because he is teaching us, and he...is the most knowledgeable in this universe. Prophet (saws), so we must look for him - if he is not proud how we can be proud? We are not one from billion, not even one drop from his ocean. You cannot be proud. Good thinking people they never be proud. Who is proud people? Idiot people they're proud only because they are cheated by shayṭān. These people, Prophet (saws) he said, "Allāh not giving them value in Judgement Day, not looking for them."

Second people who are not looking and not giving value for them, who they are looking for doubt from order of Allāh. Allāh give us one order, we must follow this order. We accept this order and follow. Some of them cannot follow - ok, also they must accept and they must repent "we cannot, we couldn't do this, but we accept this. "Not looking if it is from Lord of Allāh or not. This also people no value for them in dunyā, also there is no value for them. Allāh, He is the most generous and He wants for us to be better. For this Prophet (saws) saying what we must follow what is not good to do and bad for us to do. And the worst thing is these two things. Allāh forgive us and make us to follow right way all the time, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
