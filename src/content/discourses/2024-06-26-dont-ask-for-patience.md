As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Rabbanā wa-lā tuḥammilnā mā lā ṭāqata lanā bih" (2:286). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘aẓīm. We are begging Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla not to load us with excessive burden, not to load us with things we cannot carry – for AllāhNone to help us. This beautiful āyatu l-karīmah is an āyah gifted by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla to our Holy ProphetNone on Laylatu l-Mi‘rāj. This āyah says that Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla offered people only what they can carry. That is: to worship and to do goodness. As long as people live, they must do something with their lives. People didn't come to this world for nothing. They must worship. And Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, with HisNone wisdom, has offered tasks that people can carry out. HeNone has offered worshipping and things they can do. They must do them. No need to do things other than them.

If a person can do it, they will. But normally, doing something outside of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla's command is up to them. That means if they are going to do good, they should do as much as they can. If he cannot do, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla doesn't give a burden that people can't carry. If they have suffering to endure in this world, they will endure it to that extent. After that, if they are patient, they will receive their reward. If they do not have patience but still have faith, they will receive a reward, but of course, not like the one who is patient. Their reward will be less. If they don't believe in Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, then they will not get anything. They will suffer both here and hereafter.

Therefore, we must carry out the orders of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. May AllāhNone help us to do as much as we can. AllāhNone will forgive what we can't do, in shā’a Llāh. If we repent and ask for forgiveness from HimNone, it will be accepted. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla's forgiveness is like an ocean. HeNone doesn't look at small or big things. As long as people repent, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla forgives. Some people say they can't carry so much. But we often see that things don't happen how people want in this world. Oftentimes there is a lot of hardship.

Since the time of Ādam ‘alayhi s-salām, there have been difficulties, meaning there has never been a time of complete ease and comfort. There may be apparent difficulties, but spiritually, very great beautiful things are happening. But still, people look at it as a difficulty. Therefore, it is beneficial for you to be pleased with what Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gives you. Still, in our du‘ā’, we ask not to be burdened with more than we can bear. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is merciful. In shā’a Llāh, with that du‘ā’, we will live this worldly life in comfort, without hardship, and will live happily in ākhirah. That is an acceptable du‘ā’. There is no objection to that. However, praying to Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, asking HimNone to always grant ease, goodness, and blessings, believing that there is wisdom in everything that comes, is a nice thing and brings reward. May AllāhNone protect all of us. May HeNone not give us a burden we can't carry. May AllāhNone protect us. Because there are very tough things. May AllāhNone protect us, in shā’a Llāh.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, saying in Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n, "Rabbanā wa-lā tuḥammilnā mā lā ṭāqata lanā bih" (2:286). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘Aẓīm. This is last verse from Sūratu l-Baqarah, which it is given by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla for Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, without Jibrīl ‘alayhi s-salām. This night of Mi‘rāj, for heavenly travelling for heaven, for... from Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. In this night, many thing AllāhNone gave. And then HeNone gave, two this verse, end of Baqarah, in this night also. And it is very important to ask for, from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, to not put what we cannot carry on ourself. Many thing, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla order. And for, HeNone create us and HeNone gave everything, and sometimes difficulties, sometimes more easy. But HeNone is, HeNone put for everyone what they can carry, HeNone can give.

Some people, they have more responsibility, more, they have more difficulties. Some of them, they have many thing. For human being, AllāhNone give everything and it is test for people, but HeNone gave as much they can carry. Even you can see it is too big, but also AllāhNone gave with this, power to carry this. But we are asking from AllāhNone every time to give us easy thing, not difficult. We are not asking for difficult, because difficult, many people they cannot carry. Who carry this difficulty get reward, big reward. But if he cannot carry, also get reward, but not like who is patient. For this, we are weak. We don't... every time mashāyikh also saying, don't ask patience, don't ask anything you cannot carry. Just ask from AllāhNone to give you from HisNone kindness, no any test, to live this life easily. And to be in ākhirah also, happy. This is what we must ask because, don't think you are strong, you can carry. Every time, ask AllāhNone to keep you in strong belief to HimNone, and to not be... not happy for what HeNone gave you.

For this, we all time asking HimNone to give us easy thing, blessed thing, to make our life easy and to make ākhirah good, happy ākhirah, in shā’a Llāh. This is what we are... Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam was saying, we are listening for ProphetNone. Don't ask difficult thing. Don't ask what we cannot carry. Only you ask from AllāhNone, HisNone mercy and easiness for you. AllāhNone keep us in HisNone way. Not to make us to test, because many thing in life very difficult, very heavy, very painful, very bad thing can happen. AllāhNone keep us safe from this, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone, because our days is difficult days, and when with du‘ā’ and praying, AllāhNone keep you safe, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone help us.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
