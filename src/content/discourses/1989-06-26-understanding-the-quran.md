But Allah Almighty, may He be praised and exalted, says:

asta‘īdhu billah: wa la-qad yassarnā l-qur'āna li-dhikri fa-hal min muddakir (54:40)

We did not send the Holy Qur'an as a non-understandable text. We sent it so that it might be understood, and it is book for understanding. We said before that it contains endless oceans. In any way, everyone will take his share from the Holy Qur'an. There is no one who will not receive his share. The Holy Quran was sent down to be understood, to be learned, to be acted upon, to give benefit. It is not a book of riddles. However, it will appear to everyone according to his own capacity.

Thus: rahima-llāhu-mrā'an ‘arifa haddahu wa waqafa ‘indahu. This is a hadith of the Holy Prophet. May Allah Almighty send mercy on the person who knows his limits and accepts them. It is a good thing to know one's limits. If one goes beyond one's limits, one breaks the rules of adab. Therefore, now actually, the best adab is to know one's limits. To know one's limits is the highest adab.

Too much questioning destroyed previous nations, he says. Especially the Banu Isra'il, the sons of Isra‘il used to exhaust their prophets with their constant questioning. Always questions and more questions, and when they got an answer they still wouldn't act upon it. That is why their excessive questioning led to their downfall.

Now, having dealt everybody a few blows with the stick, with the help of Ali Ulvi Efendi, let us look at the meaning of this holy verse, let us see what it says. Our Lord, Allahu Dhū l-Jalāl wa l-Kamāl wa l-Jamāl, the Lord of Majesty, Perfection and Beauty, possesses Absolute Perfection.

wa Huwa al-awwalu wa l-ākhiru wa z-zāhiru wa l-bātin. (57:3)

He is the First and the Last. He existed before anything else came into being, nothing existed before Him, and He will be there at the very end of all things. Beyond Him there is nothing. Before Him nothing existed and after Him nothing will exist. He is the First and the Last. He is the Manifest and He is the Hidden. He is Manifest, whatever can be seen in existence, it is His. Whatever comes to be, it is He who brings it into existence. There is nothing that can come into existence by itself. Therefore, everything has its personality. Every atom, a single atom has its own character. There is nothing in existence that does not have its own personality. For things to be in existence, they all have their own character, like you, and you, and you. All beings appear in existence as manifestations of Allah Almighty. He is manifest in all things. The Names of Allah Almighty are the places of manifestation for every thing. Allah is evident in all and everything.

When those whose eyes are opened look at something, they say: Before regarding any object or looking at any created being, we behold the existence of Allah. He who does not perceive Allah's Existence is blind. He who does not see the Perfection, Majesty and Beauty of Allah Almighty, Dhū l-Jalāli wa l-Ikrām, is blind. Therefore, someone who cannot observe the existence of Allah in all beings everywhere is even more blind. In everything without fail there is a manifestation of the Majesty, Beauty and Perfection of Allah Almighty.

Therefore, wherever you may look, wherever you turn your attention, in particular if you focus your heart's attention on something, but even if you are only looking with the eyes in your head, and you do not perceive the Beauty, Perfection and Majesty of the workings of the Divine, the Divine Acts, you are blind. This is all we can say, according to the level of our faith, as much as we can understand. If he were to speak on this any further, there are no scholars left in these countries. There are no scholars left.

If you claim that there are, tell me, so that I may go and kiss his hand. No more alims. If we were to speak beyond this point to the ‘ulama' who wear the title of scholars, those possessing outward knowledge could not tolerate it. That is why they break off their speech right here. Beyond this, Allah only knows. Allah knows what is before this, and Allah knows what is beyond this. He knows all, from pre-eternity to post-eternity. We know nothing. (M: Thank you).

wa min Allahi tawfīq. al-Fātiha.
