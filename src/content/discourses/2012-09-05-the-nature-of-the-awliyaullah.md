As-Salamu Alaikum. Today we speak about those who people do not appreciate - the ones who have Tariqat, who have 'ilm/knowledge,who have irfan/awareness. We say what kind of people they are so that it will be a lesson to the so-called scholars, the ones who showed up recently to criticize them, saying "what good is Tariqat?".

When we say Tariqat, a person who became a Shaykh already has the Shari'ah knowledge- he is the one who knows it the best. He takes from the best parts of Shari'ah. Allah has ordered people with Mercy, & they- the Mashayikh give weight to the Mercy side. These awliya of Allah, the beloved servants of Allah look upon people with the eyes of Mercy. No matter who they are, even for the sinners, they look at them as "the poor people who fell in this way."

As an example of this, in old times in this place called Mawarannahr/Transoxiana, on the Khorasan side I think, there lived a servant beloved to Allah. He would spend his nights with worship, dhikr & reflection on Allah. One night he was awake when everyone else was sleeping. He was doing dhikr then he heard a click. And he saw that someone was throwing a rope, trying to climb up to the roof. Not to his roof but to the house next to him. It was a thief. When he, the awliya of Allah saw this he started shouting "Thief, there is a thief!" He shouted at the top of his lungs. All the people woke up. All people ran out with sticks in their hands. The thief got shocked when he saw people waking up & coming like this. Of course he was an expert thief so immediately he ran away. No one was able to catch him. They lost his trace. He ran away from that district.

So everybody went back to their houses. This awliya of Allah, he was merciful. He said "this man worked hard like this, put all his effort & all his efforts were gone to waste" So he got very sad. He went after this thief at that late hour of the night. Of course there is no one who could find that thief but he found him with the grant of Allah & he went to him. The man was sitting there breathing heavily & he came saying "As-Salamu Alaikum. I am an old friend of yours". He said "Really? I don't know you". He said "No, I know you for long time. You are a very brave, courageous man. There are 2 types of courage. Either when fighting, you show courage or when you see a danger you run away. And you have it both". He talked like this to him for a while & the man got surprised saying "why this man keeps praising me?"

He said "You couldn't reach your goal today but there is a house I know. The walls around it are not so high. If you put two stones you can jump. If not, you can climb onto my shoulder. There is also no one in the house. You can take everything in there. Your efforts will not go to waste." He spoke for a long time to convince this thief & finally he did. They went together. The man saw that really there was such a house. He bent & the thief jumped into the house stepping on his back. Jubbah, turban, kitchen utensils.. He collected everything in the house. Then he said to the thief "run immediately that they won't catch you". After the thief walked a few steps away he started shouting again "thief, thief" But this time the thief ran away with his goods.

The people coming saw that the house was already robbed. And the thief had thought that the owner of the house came. But the house belonged to the friend of Allah. Because he pitied him, he took him to his own house so that he wouldn't leave empty handed. He made him rob his house & load all his stuff. When he didn't leave empty handed he was happy, he was relieved.

The Awliya of Allah are like this, they pity everybody. They also pity the sinners. If they didn't pity them, the sinners can never get better & be saved anyways. There are a few different wisdoms in this story. Why did he shout & call people the first time? It was someone else's house. When he saw a thief he needed to protect it. This keeps to the Shari'ah. And for the other one, when he made him rob his own house, he considered it as sadaqa & also for this man to reach guidance- for him not to be heart broken & return empty & hoping Allah won't return him empty handed also. Awliyas are like this.

Now for Mawlana also, many times people criticize others who come to him. "The one who came here was like this, that one like that.." What this holy one did in the story, we saw much more of it in Mawlana. We experienced many such things with Mawlana. Even for a person whom people don't even like to greet, we saw many times how Mawlana showed him respect, how he praised him. And people say, criticize "why this is like this, why this is like that" Even we have witnessed some people becoming distant with Mawlana. It doesn't really matter that one is getting distant because already once he enters this way, Mawlana never leaves him. He is with him even in his last breath. So it is not important but the shame & regret they will go through in Akhirah is inestimable.

These are awliya, awliyas of Allah. They are not people who think like me, like you. As we said, what this man did, the awliya of Allah did- having his own house robbed, it is the least of things. There are so much more than this. The things Awliyas tolerate in people cannot even be imagined. A lot of people can't take it, even the ones who say "we are the closest" get astonished in some situations. At least they ask "What is the wisdom in this?" Because they know that Mawlana is an awliyaullah-The Qutb of all Awliyas in this time, inheritor of Muhammad (sas). But the things he does out of his mercy are misunderstood.

May Allah not leave any doubt about him in our hearts insha Allah. This is a pretty important matter. It is not an easy thing, wisdom. Wisdom, the people Allah granted wisdom are very rare. It says in the Holy Verse also "He, to whom Hikmah is granted, is indeed granted abundant good" (2:269) Today we speak about the friend of Allah-awliyaullah. They are different from another scholar who only looking for outside look. This awliyaullah are, know the Shari'ah. They know Tariqa. Shari'ah they know very well also. Maybe some look like they are not knowing & what doing against Shari'ah but they never doing something against Shari'ah. And what they do, they do the very merciful thing for creature, for what Allah create. Especially for people-they are looking for them as if they are sinner they are poor people. Because to be sinner, it is not a good thing. If you have some illness, bad illness, any illness you are lucky? No. To be in sins & you be sinner it is like illness for you. You cannot get rid from this. So they are looking as like this. And many people they misunderstand this.

And there was one story about this thing-little bit to give example for these good people. There was one living in something like Turkestan or Khorosan, that area, before maybe 500-600 years ago. And he was especially in night time waking up & making dhikr & making praying & thinking about Allah, about what to do good thing... Once he was sitting night time. And he hear something moving. After, he saw one rope going up to next door. So he looked, he was seeing one thief. When he saw thief, began to shout. "There is thief, O people come! Be careful, there is thief here!" Shouting very loud, many people waking up & they taking stick & running to street to catch this man. When this man saw this, he was afraid. And because he know, he is professional, he was running away. Nobody can reach him. Very quickly running away, so everybody come back to their house & sleeping.

After this, this awliyaullah was sad. He said "this man whole night not sleeping & coming to make work. And he couldn't take anything. It is pity". He feel very sorry for him. So he went, looking after him. And he find his way & reach him. He was sitting there. He said "As-Salamu Alaikum, O my friend. I know you long time ago. I am your friend. How you are courage man. And courage, there are two kinds of courage. Some to fight against enemy without fearing. But if you are in danger there is another kind of courage- to run away. Also it is courage to run away. You have two of this." And this man was astonished: "Who is this, he is making me, praising me and this & that?" After this, he said to him "I know one house. It is not like what you were doing now- it is high house. It's wall is low,only you can jump on it Or if you cannot jump, I can help you. Put my back & you come on my back & you can go to this house. Nobody in house also. You can take as much.." He was..Maybe he was suspecting. Until to make him agree, maybe half an hour speak to him. And so this man agree.

They went together to this house. And it was exactly what he said. He put his back & go. Find jubbah, turban spoon, plates, other things... What he find, he put & he come back. So he said "Go away quickly before people coming here." When he running a little bit, starting this awliya to shout "O people there is thief here!". When he heard this, the thief thought that the owner coming & making this. He ran away with what he has. So he was happy. And this house was for this awliyaullah. He take him to his house to make this man happy. Because he was sad, he couldn't take anything this night. So he was happy. Awliyaullah like this-

they have special mercy for people. Even for bad people also they have mercy. With this awliyaullah, bad people also are happy. And this is one example. What we saw many example from Mawlana Shaykh, thousands like this. Many people are not understand this. They not understand. Even I saw many people, not coming because they are not understanding, they are very ignorant, they leave this But if they leave also, the awliyaullah will help them in last breath. And they will be very shy, very regretful. Even many thing, the most near people also are not understanding. But they know there is wisdom from this. But many thing.. Of course nobody they are awliyaullah, like Mawlana. And it is difficult to understand.

But most of people thought who coming also, there was of course people crook & other & other & other.. They thought "we cheat Mawlana". Some of them think like this. Some of them thought he is not understanding. No, they are cheating themselves. Nobody can cheat him. And everything they have wisdom. If even we are not knowing about this wisdom we must be careful to not think bad for Mawlana. You must say "this is wisdom". Don't put your opinion on this. Many thing there is, the many people they are not.. doesn't concern them. There is many thing people make very bad adab. And for this they can get punishment from Allah. And this wisdom of, this is story also. This awliyaullah, when he say, saw this man going to his neighbor's house if he not shout & say to people "there is this man" he will be partner from him. Because he saw something he can change by hand or by his tongue or by heart. But when he said this, he ran away. He is making this thief to be not make- not commit sin & people not to be losing from him. He protect this neighbor & protect this man also from sin. And when he bring to his house, make sadaqa for him & make him happy. And maybe he die also after.

This is wisdom, very important thing. Allah said "Who has wisdom, Allah give him a lot of good". (2:269) And this is wisdom not everybody has it. Little (few) people have it.

Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, Al Fatiha.
