As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Wa-la-qad karramnā banī Ādam, wa-ḥamalnāhum fī l-barri wa-l-baḥr" (17:70). Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla created the children of Ādam, humans, at the highest level among all creatures. HeNone created them higher than jinn and angels. When they are as AllāhNone wants, when they are on the way of AllāhNone, their rank becomes higher than all others. Reaching that level is actually not difficult. But shayṭān leads everyone astray out of jealousy. He makes it look like the most difficult thing is to be on Allāh’sNone way. Whereas there is no difficulty in this.

As long as Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla destined it, it is easy. If Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla has destined for you to be on this way, you are a lucky person because it will be easy for you to follow this way, to go on this way. But it is difficult for a person with no īmān, for a person who was not destined to have īmān, for a person for whom AllāhNone has not decreed it. He does such hard work all day long, but when it comes to praying... This is for Muslims anyways.

It is different for people who are not Muslim, who have nothing to do with Islām. They cannot approach it at all. They are afraid. They think there is death. However, it is very easy to be on the way of AllāhNone. The way of AllāhNone is a beautiful way. That way gives you beauty in both this world and the hereafter and your real life is in the hereafter anyways. If you take HisNone way and go on that way, its end leads to eternal salvation, beauty and all kinds of goodness. But as we said, mankind follows his ego, he follows shayṭān. This has become even worse in the end of times.

In old times, there wasn’t this much evil and this much immorality. There were only a few, but those few people were ashamed. They would hide. Now there is no such thing as being ashamed. They think they are doing goodness. While knowing its harm upon him, he says it's nothing, consoling himself and consoling his ego. And he goes astray. Then, he shamelessly asks for an explanation – “Why did this happen? Why did that happen?” From whom are you asking an explanation? Some people are rebelling against Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. "What is this for?" This is the biggest form of foolishness. It is the biggest. Not knowing one’s limit and crossing over the limit is a bad thing. May AllāhNone help us all. We are living in the end of times. We live right in the middle of it. These are the days our Holy ProphetNone told us about. These are the days when if you say good things, they will not respect you and will look at you badly. If you say bad things, everyone will think you are something wonderful and run after you. They will be pleased with you. May AllāhNone help us. May AllāhNone protect us all from this bad time.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone said in Qur’ān ‘aẓīmu sh-sha’n: "Wa-la-qad karramnā banī Ādam, wa-ḥamalnāhum fī l-barri wa-l-baḥr" (17:70). Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone said, "WeNone create Banī... human being, son of Ādam ‘alayhi s-salām. WeNone make them the highest, WeNone honoured them between whole creatures, make them the highest in between this. Even than, more than jinn, more than angels." When you are way of AllāhNone, what HeNone honoured you, you be careful to keep this honour thing on you, you will be higher than angels, higher than jinns, higher than any creature.

But people they are not looking for this. They thought to be honoured [is] to do anything they like. No. If you do what AllāhNone like, you will be honoured and you will be happy. And it is not difficult to be, live. You are living life it is, you maybe, many people they thought it is very long life. No, it is very short for everybody. For believer, for non-believer, it is short. And it is easy to follow order of AllāhNone. because Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone want good for us. HeNone honoured us and we... Keep yourself in this, this honour in yourself. Don't follow your ego, follow shayṭān, you will be lose. You will not be honoured anymore when you are doing this. You will be very dishonoured.

And it is very easy, I... we said, we thought, to do what AllāhNone honour for believer, for chosen people. But for other people who are not following AllāhNone order, following their ego, they are can do very hard work, very tired work, whole day, whole weeks, whole years but if you say to them to come and pray and do this, it is so heavy for them. They cannot do this and they thought, "It is not for us. They are this for people who are not educated, not good people." But Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone calling whole people, all human being to Paradise, to peace. But ego of people, they prevent them from this.

And we are living in time, it is real tough time for believer because all we know before twenty years or for forty years, how it was the world. Maybe if you find one drunk man, people they not happy. And he is also ashamed to be appear as drunk. Or after this, before forty years ago. After, before twenty years ago, they was ashamed to be somebody tell them you are using drug or something. Now they are no, nothing at all. Nobody afraid even. As Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, we are living in end of time, which if you say something good, they, people, that not happy, "Not doing what you are doing" – [they are] saying. If you say and you do bad thing, they are very happy and they encourage you. "You are hero, you are... We like you, we love you, we follow you." We are living in this bad time. AllāhNone keep us safe. But we must know AllāhNone not like this people. AllāhNone love you and honoured you when you are in this [HisNone] way. So don't follow your ego. Don't follow bad people, be away from them. Don't be – your heart has satisfied with them. Every time you must be unhappy with this people. Not unhappy with you are, you don't have money, you don't have something you like to do. No. Don't be happy with this people. This is what make your heart happy, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone keep us safe, keep whole human being safe from this bad time, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone send Sayyidunā Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām, to finish this thing.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
