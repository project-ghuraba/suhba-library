Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi Rasulillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

The Hadith of our Holy Prophet (SAW): “The most beautiful thing a Muslim person can do is to not interfere in what does not concern oneself.” Let everybody do their own duty. Allah gave everybody a character and a specialty. Let people find that and do that. This way their whole life will be spent in achievement and success. If not, if they interfere in things that do not concern them and meddle in things that they do not understand, they cannot achieve any success. They will only bother themselves and those around themselves, spend their time in vain, and end in loss.

That is why people should check: Does this concern me? Does it benefit me? Will I be of benefit to my surroundings if I join in this? If so, let them look after that. If not, they do not have a responsibility in something that they have nothing to do with. They should only look after things they know about.

A man is passing by another street, something happens, and he goes and interferes there. A harm can inflict him too. This is End Times and everybody needs to look after their own duties. Nobody should interfere in very unnecessary things. This way they will be in safety. There is no need to stir oneself saying things like, “This was like this, we should have done such, and if it were me I would have done so...” If you were meant to be there you would habe been there anyway.

You look after yourself, fix yourself, and society would be fixed too. If everybody is fixed one by one, there is no need for anything else. Do not see yourself as grand but be a person of unpretentiousness. Know your boundaries and know yourself. This is a beautiful advice and a Hadith Sharif of our Holy Prophet (SAW). It is both an advice and a Hadith Sharif. All Hadith Sharifs are advice.

This too is a good thing for people of these times. They start talking “I think” and “according to that”. Who are you for it to be according to you?! Know your place! If you know your place, it will be beneficial to you and to the country. May Allah make us all people who know their own limit and measure. Do not interfere in other things Inshallah.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq.

Al-Fatiha.
