Sheikh Bahauddin Adil sohbat of the 14th of June, 2014.

Masha'Allah! It is nice to have such a salat wa salam to make our heart melt for the love of Rasulullah. It is important to know how valuable the love that you carry for Rasulullah. Now must understand, without love, nothing can be. Nothing! And the one that doesn't love, no value for him. You look for the animal, the mother of that animal has big love for the child. And you see the child also has big love for his parents. Subhanallah! This is how Allah Almighty He develops things and He put it in the heart of every creature that he has it.

But the love it is not easy to reach, you have to dig for it. You have to dig for it and when you reach it, nobody can take it from you. This is important also. Now, you have to work for it and you have to develop it. You find the love? It's not enough, you have to make it more, you have to build it, get the shape for it and you have to name it. This is, that we have to name love, is it belonging to Allah or to Rasulullah or to your Sheikh, or to your wife, or to your husband or to your child or to your car or to your anything. But, must put a name. Because the love is ocean. This tajalli of Sheikh Adnan is here. Ocean behind ocean, behind ocean, behind ocean. Full of oceans.

Allah Almighty He show Himself with the ocean. And when you look for our earth, you see the ocean is the most big thing on this planet. That's why the ocean is important in our Tariqa; it is the ocean, it has so many things. It has fish first that we know. Second, it might have plants, ocean, and something we don't know. There is gold in it, there is jewellery in it. There is mineral in it. There is gas in it, there is petrol in it. This is ocean. You never know what is in the ocean. And they say "we've discovered a new kind of fish." The mala'ika they are laughing at them "you haven't even discovered half of what you discover." Half percent maybe. So many hidden things. It's ocean, ocean. When you say ocean, it's really ocean. Now we come back to our ocean.

The love that Allah Almighty He gives to His servants, it is the most high love level. Allah Almighty He gives the highest rank of love to His servants. And among His servants, among His creatures, He has the highest. Because the rest of creation they have some kind of love. Maybe they love their children and it will be for short while and then "say you on your way, me in my way. You feed yourself. You look after yourself, until here." But the human know. Our test, our exams with our love, we have so many options. And we don't know which one is the true one, and this is coming the test for your humanity. And is coming test for your servanthood. When you are using your love, this is like somebody having wealth and he uses his wealth in gambling, for example. And then after a while he goes bankrupt. Yahoo! How come if you are gambling it has such an end. But if you are a clever one and you make your wealth to be in good investment, and you invest it with Allah Almighty and with His Saints, then your wealth it will not be one ocean, as S. Adnan says, ocean behind ocean, behind the ocean, behind the ocean.

This is how we have to know what we are doing. And this is our real test. I am saying Alhamdulillah that Allah Almighty He gave us drop of love and now we are coming to you Ya Mawlana Sheikh to invest in your ocean, to be part of your love of Allah Love ocean. We have a small drop, very small drop. It cannot be seen from the Love. We deserve this one, at least we deserve this one. We deserve this drop and we are coming. We are very careful to not.. cannot be seen. And we are investing with Mawlana Sheikh, Alhamdulillah. This is how it grows. This is how it makes you become bigger and bigger. The ocean also starts from a drop. Don't forget this one. The ocean also starts from a drop. The ocean never starts as an ocean - except Allah Almighty saying "Kun Fa Ya Kun" (2:117). But when it's going, it's going for drop.

We are accepting that our drop will be with you Ya Sayyidi, the ocean. That is our belief. And this is our behaviour.

Now, in old days people didn't know what to do because there was no technology, there was no communication there was no anything. So what the Sahaba they were doing they were carrying their love with their heart, and they go all around the country, all around the country to give this small love. One person, one person. They give love for nations. This is important. Because the nations they are hungry for such a thing. They are hungry. Don't worry you go. This is our duty, to go. And to get the small, small love to be showing, to be known for people. Alhamdulillah. It is Mawlana Sheikh he was saying "when I was walking on my feet and going around the country I was going, my stick, my turban and my jubba and myself. And with this Alhamdulillah Allah Almighty He never disappoint me because I am working for him." Because the order says "you are distributing My Love. You are distributing My Love." So be sure that you are distributing the love that Allah Almighty put in your heart and you put it for other heart. Alhamdulillah!

And now you see because Mawlana S. distributing the love, the world is here. Alhamdulillah! It is big miracle of Mawlana Sheikh and this is showing us how it is working. It doesn't work coincidently, it doesn't work as "Ah! somebody came!" No! It is a real work, a real job. When I was small boy, I used to say "my father is jobless." Because all my friends they have all their fathers working 8 to 5 or they have a shop or... and my father, subhan Allah, my father doesn't work. Child mind, because you know the world is only the working for dunya. And everybody says "yes, what is your father doing?" "He is a Sheikh. With mosque, with mosque." Asking "which mosque?" "Any mosque!" This, Mawlana Sheikh, he was working. The huge work, the huge scale that my minor mind cannot imagine what he is working.

Or the people what they don't know must be Sheikh, Imam in one masjid Imam in another masjid and have salary from the government. Mawlana S. says "I never take salary from anybody." "I don't need. He brings. If He sends, I am ok. Alhamdulillah!" "When He sends, I distribute. I never keep! Never!" Alhamdulillah Mawlana Sheikh, he is unique.

Now you have to know that we must be proud that we've been with Mawlana S. all these years and we know him and he knows us. And we must be appreciating Allah Almighty that He put us next to him, because Mawlana S. is such a fantastic personality that he speaks to the ant, and the professor. And he makes the ant understand what he wants, and the professor what he means. And this is how Mawlana Sheikh he was talking to all the world people. You can imagine world people. If you tell me talk to the world people I say, "he will never understand what I say. He will never understand what I mean." But Mawlana S. he makes everybody to understand the meaning of Heavenly Love that belonging to Allah Almighty, to Rasulullah and to Grand Sheikh.

And with this one we say, "may Allah keep us straight and keep us with him and keep our heart strong love to him and to Rasulullah and to Allah Almighty. And it doesn't make our thinking to go right and left and to be (to keep) the ego away from us, and the shaytan away from us.

Bi hurmati l-Habib, Bi hurmati l-Fatiha.
