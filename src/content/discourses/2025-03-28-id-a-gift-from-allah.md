As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭānir-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāhal-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says in this Ayatu l-Karīmah, “Wa-li-tukmilū l- ‘iddata wa-li-tukabbirū Llāha ‘alá mā hadākum” (2:185). This is the month of Ramaḍān; it has its time. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says, “You should complete it.” After completing it, it becomes ‘Īd. During ‘Īd, bring takbīr and tahlīl. Muslims have two ‘Īds. In this world, there are two ‘Īds. The first is the ‘Īd of Ramaḍān, and then ‘Īdu l-Aḍḥá. Other things have been invented so that people do not value these two.

There are many other holidays. Indeed, our ancestors spoke beautifully. They said, “For a fool, every day is a holiday.” When someone lacks understanding, they don’t recognise a true holiday. The holidays to be recognised as holidays are those that Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla has granted us. These are the ‘Īd of Ramaḍān and ‘Īdu l-Aḍḥá. Respecting them means earning Allāh’sNone pleasure. Not giving them importance harms a person. Even if there’s no material harm, there is a spiritual loss –not valuing what Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla has given us. Yet, what deserves value is only what Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla desires and loves. That is what should be valued. Its value is great. In this world, this ‘Īd has value, and in the hereafter, it’s worth is known even more. But today’s people no longer value anything. Nothing holds worth anymore–not money, not gold, not words, not honour, not goodness. Nothing has value to these people. They call it “inflation.” Everything has been inflated and lost. No worth remains.

Even thirty years ago, with one gold coin, a person could live comfortably for a month. Now, it doesn’t even last a week – barely a day. That’s why nothing in this world holds value anymore. The worth of the hereafter must be understood, so a person can benefit from it.

Thanks be to AllāhNone, these beautiful days have passed. Believers experienced beautiful days. For those with faith, for Muslims who worship, it was beautiful. No matter how many troubles or difficulties there were, they had no effect. Through the virtue and beauty of Ramaḍān, AllāhNone covers them all. But there is an exception. For those who are enemies of Islām, there is never comfort or goodness. Those who oppose Allah ‘Azza wa-Jalla, those who wage war against Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, cannot find happiness. In Islām, there is peace, happiness, and beauty. Every goodness is in Islām. Islām is a gift to humanity from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla –guidance and a gift. Therefore, those who follow Islām and value Islām, gain value themselves. As we said, thanks to AllāhNone Ramaḍān ends with ‘Īd. Every day felt like ‘Īd anyway, mā shā’a Llāh. Allāh’sNone blessings, manifestations, and beauties –everyone feels them in their heart. During the day, even if a person struggles at times, that struggle turns into beauty. There is relief inside, there is peace. No evil is felt, nothing bad is sensed. In the evening, as our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam said, the joy and happiness of a believer –no one else can find such a thing. Those who do not fast cannot find it.

May AllāhNone bless these days. These days have passed. May AllāhNone, in shā’a Llāh, grant us to reach it next year, and may the whole world become Muslim, with Mahdī ‘alay his-salām, in shā’a Llāh. We have hope, because the world isn’t improving –it’s worsening. The one who will save it is he whom our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam gave glad tidings of –Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām. In shā’a Llāh, we await him. May AllāhNone send him soon, in shā’a Llāh.
