May we open up, may we get beautiful. Who created us is Allah. Who keeps us alive is Allah. Who shelters us with His Divine Bounty is Allah.

O believers! May there be joy for you. Our Lord Allah Jalla Jalaluhu grants everything, creates everything. We wait for Your Order, O our Lord. May You honour us with servanthood to You. We desire to perform service for You, we desire Your servanthood. O our Lord, our Subhan, our Sultan. You are the Sultan, O Lord. The Sultan can grant anything. The Sultan can do anything. He is our Lord. Al-Hamdu Lillah.
