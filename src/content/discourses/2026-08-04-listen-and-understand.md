As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
اسْمَعُوا وَعَوْا وَإِذَا وَعَيْتُمْ فَانْتَفِعُوا
<br>“Ismaʿū wa-ʿaw wa-idhā waʿaytum fantafiʿū”<br>
“Listen and understand. When you understand, then benefit!” [Dalā'il al-Nubūwwah, al-Bayhaqī]

This isn’t a ḥadīth, but perhaps a saying of Sayyidinā Abu Bakr or Sayyidinā ʿUthmān. Because people hear but don’t understand. They only speak, but they don’t benefit from what they say.

Therefore, this blessed saying says, “Listen and understand.” If you understand, you will benefit from what you hear accordingly.

Most people come and say whatever is on their mind. They neither understand what the other person is saying nor benefit from it. Therefore, if you want advice, knowledge, or an opinion from people, listen to it carefully, understand it.

Understanding makes things easier. You must look at what is necessary, not what you want. People have now become stuck into a mold [with the same mindset], wanting the other person to say the same thing. If they say the same thing, that’s good.

However, they don’t understand what they themselves are saying; they don’t understand what they want, what they are asking, or the answer they receive. Even if it’s their own idea, they don’t know what it means.

Therefore, one must pay attention. One must understand what is being said. Understanding leads to benefit; not understanding is useless.

That’s why these lessons Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla has revealed in Qur’ān ʿAẓīmu sh-Sha’n—it is a blessed and sacred word, the word of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla—to understand it, one certainly needs a Shaykh or a teacher. You cannot understand it according to your own mind.

Nowadays, some self-proclaimed intellectuals have emerged, saying, “We can read and understand it ourselves; there’s no need for madhhab, no need for ṭarīqah; we can understand these things on our own.” You cannot understand it!

As we said, you neither understand what you are asking nor the answer you receive. You will come up with something that you claim to have understood, but that is nothing but the whispers of shayṭān. 

May Allah protect us. May Allah make us among those who truly understand. May our minds and hearts be opened. It’s not enough to understand with just the mind and intellect; we must understand with the heart as well.

May Allah open the hearts of us all, so that we may see the truth, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
