As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Wa-lā yakhāfūna lawmata lā’im” (5:54). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘Aẓīm. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says in the Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n, the believer, the one who is with Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, does not pay attention to what people say. He does not value it. What they say is not important to the person who is following the path of AllāhNone. What is important is to be on the path of AllāhNone. They do not worry about whether others will like them or not.

Their only concern is: “May Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla love us. May HeNone accept us.” This is the only purpose, concern, and thought that they have: May Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla accept us. “Someone else accepted me. That person accepted me. This person did not accept me. He did not like our appearance. He did not like the way we read. He did not like our walking,” they do not have such concerns. The person who is with Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla does not think about anyone but HimNone.

His concern is to ensure that his clothing, food, and drink align with what Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla desires. In Islām, there is no rule that anyone, whether man or woman, should like or accept it. In Islām, you are required to dress neatly and cleanly. You must keep yourself clean and stay away from impurity. After that, everything is easy. What you eat should also be pure. There should be no impurity. No dirt should be present. People who do not like us, those who do not like Islām, are filthy from top to bottom, with Janābah (impurity after intercourse).

Moreover, the food they consume is also impure. This impurity is clear. The first impurity of all is alcohol. Once it touches you, you have to wash it. You must wash it to remove that impurity. The same applies to food. Now, these ignorant non-Muslims say, “A nation ate dogs. How can they eat dogs.” You consume pork. Pigs are even more impure than dogs. What logic is this? They lack any logic. A person who does not believe in Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla has no logic at all. Nothing about them is right. That is why, they will turn you into a monkey to please them. If you are trying to please them, you will end up exposing yourself as a laughingstock, for others to ridicule you. That is when they will actually laugh at you.

A person who is on the path of AllāhNone and who follows what Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla desires, this person will have achieved the greatest success. Everything will fall into place. He will have followed the path of AllāhNone. If he acts based on the concern of, “This person will like it, that person will not,” Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla will not look upon him with favour. HeNone will not be pleased with him. May Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla’s pleasure be upon all of you, in shā’a Llāh.

Do not feel sorrowful saying, “He said this. He said that.” It does not suit you. On the contrary, if we do what those who do not like us – those who reject Islām – do not approve of, how fortunate we are. How wonderful for us. That is by doing good deeds that they dislike. So, if they will only approve of us by doing bad things, they should not approve of us at all. We do good deeds by being good. If they like us, that’s great. If they do not, it doesn’t matter.
