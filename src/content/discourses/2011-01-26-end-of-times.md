In the end of times, the place where people will be safe is the countryside, mountains. They may walk after their herds, flocks & find their rizq. The safe places are in the countryside said the Prophet (sas). It is no good to live in the cities anymore, should not live in the cities.. How many times I am saying - move to the countryside! Villages should not move to cities. Because the hit/darba on the city will be shadid/severe. A lot of people will perish. A lot of people will lose their lives, lose their children & families, lose their lives & properties. We are saying this. But people are piling up in cities more & more. And when they become piled up like this, theft, shamelessness, aggression, lack of religion & faith, dirtiness, all kind of things that are not humane take place in these places. And the people perish, are destroyed with them. It may touch whoever stands before it, whether innocent or sinful. Therefore how many times I am saying this: Leave the big cities before a (bad) state comes on you.

Who do you say this to? It means everyone has something which will come on them. "Whoever doesn't accept advice will have to regret"- words of the Prophet (sas). What a person who doesn't listen to, or accept advice faces in the end will be nadamat/regret in the end. He will regret but it will be too late. We are saying this. Our Prophet (sas) is making us avoid it, warning us, "Don't leave the countryside. Don't swarm from the country to the cities. Where there are masses, crimes & disasters take place; all kinds of killing, murder & corruption happens.

You don't gain anything by moving to the cities. Be content with the little you find in your village, breathe the clean, fresh air of the village and be busy with your ibadah/worship, servanthood of your Lord. There are no dirty places in villages like the ones in the cities. The cities have become very dirty. Because they are dirty the arrows of Heavenly Wrath have descended upon the cities. And because of this, now you see that people are rising up.

For what? Why did that many people gather in these places? They came there leaving their places; Cairo, Tunis, Algeria, Sudan, Palestine, Iraq, Turkey.. All are in a chaos. Why? They are not listening to the words of the Prophet (sas), not accepting the advice of Allah. Following shaytan, they swarm to cities. Now they will suffer because who doesn't accept advice, in the end.. Before they said, who bites one of his fingers, all the rest of them ache. Be careful, say this to people. But who are you saying to? They have things to come on them. Aman ya Rabbi, May You not make us from those who leave the right way. If we do not have feet, may we crawl to the right way. If we can't do that either, may we turn in that direction.

Tawba ya Rabbi. Fatiha. May You send us the savior who will save us, ya Rabbi. You are the one with Power, ya Sayyidi ya RasulAllah. May we reach your mercy & intercession. Fatiha. When you load 1000 tons on a ship which can take 100 tons, the ship sinks. The limit a city can take is -let's say 40-50 thousands. More than this, that city sinks. No limit in the cities today; millions of people are piled up there. They have no mind, or there is no one who guides them. Their leaders say, "They may do whatever they like. They can live in the country, or go to Cairo, or they make Cairo upside down if they like. We live nicely in our palaces, enough. Who doesn't like may not come to Cairo. Is it us who invited them? They, themselves left their places & came - so they may suffer".

Aman ya Rabbi, tawba astaghfirullah. Fatiha.

This is enough. The Prophet (sas) says that in the end of times the people will be like the animals that escape - free from bridles, free from barns, running to right, left and attacking everywhere." They are in such a state now. But this is not suitable for Islam. But the people..some deserve it some don't. It will finish. Ok, this is enough.
