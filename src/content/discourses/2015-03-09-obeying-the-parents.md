Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi Rasulillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

One of Islam’s most important orders is to be a dutiful child towards one’s parents. We need to treat them well. You need to treat them well even if they treat you bad. You need not answer them back rudely. You need to obey and manage them as long as they do not force you towards the forbidden (haram). It is not good to act in opposition to them or fight with them. These are considered amongst big sins.

There are big sins (Kaba’er) and little sins (Sagha’er). One of the big sins is disrespecting the parents and treating them badly. Islam and tariqa manners (adab) teach good morals. No wali (saint) person, Allah’s beloved servant or a servant loved by people is known to have treated his parents badly, cursed them, or beat them. There cannot be such a thing. The person who does so must be missing brains. May Allah not give anybody such brainlessness.

The new generation that came after the Ottomans always treated them badly and critisized them. They did not like what the Ottomans had done. Why? Because this is what they were taught. They cursed at our ancestors and disrespected them calling them brainless. But not even one percent of what they accomplished has been done for one hundred years. Thank Allah, we are managing with their blessing (baraka) and prayers (duas). Their prayers are accepted because they were good people. Allah accepted their prayers and nevertheless continued a dutiful progeny. Even though times are so bad, thank Allah the situation is still good. There is adab and good manners. Things that people do not like occur sometimes.

Our Holy Prophet’s (SAW) line continued with Zaynal Abidin Hazretleri. Zaynal Abidin Hazretleri is the son of our Master Hadrat Husayn. He would not eat with his mother. They asked, “You are such a good person. Why do you not eat with your mother?” Look at the manners, he says, “I fear that my mother’s eye would get stuck on a dish, she would want it, and I would act before her and eat it.” He would not eat with his mother to due his manners. He would leave his mother eat as she liked. Look at these manners and compare it with today’s people yelling and screaming or beating [their parents]. May Allah avoid it, some kill their family for money.

May Allah not leave us to our egos. May our proginy be a dutiful one too Inshallah. May they be obedient and respectful to their parents. Because there is no baraka when that does not exist. None of their work goes well either. Even if they do not get cursed prayers, nothing is of benefit for people who do so. May Allah reform people who do so. May they stay away from the orders and teachings of Shaytan, Inshallah.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq.

Al-Fatiha.
