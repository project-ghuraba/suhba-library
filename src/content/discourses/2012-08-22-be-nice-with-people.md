As-Salamu Alaikum. Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fil jamia'

Speaking nice & kind words in sohbat, you win peoples' hearts. When you treat people & speak to them with goodness, kindness you win their hearts. When you say something, to ask "do this", there is a way of saying which can be worse than punching a man, or there is another way of saying where he can't say no to you. When you speak kindly, in a soft manner, they can't say no to you, can't turn you down. But the other way, he say "Why should I bother, this man is very rude & rough." Even if what he says is a good advice, people don't accept.

There was a man. He was very gentle, soft-mannered, would speak to people kindly. He had a beautiful face also, nur flowing from it. Everybody would love him. And to top it all, he was selling honey. Wherever he went, he would sell out immediately. People would rush to his honey & he would sell well.

A man saw this, an envious man. And he was the exact opposite; a very rude, rough person. His face was as sour as vinegar. He said "I may also do this business & make a lot of money like him. This honey business is good." So he started, and he bought good quality honey, not bad. He went from morning to evening all the cities, towns, disticts.. No one bought from him. He came home at night. He told his wife about the situation. She said "The one who looks at your face can't notice the honey anyways. Even if it is the best honey they run from you."

Therefore some people, even if they give the best advice no one come near them. He wants to do the opposite because of his rude manner. Speaking, treating people kindly protects people from everything. It makes even a sharp sword not cutting. There are many examples, stories for this in books that are read in sohbats. All explained with stories.

There was a Prince in the state of Turkestan. The state was a rich state & so was he. He was surrounded with bad friends. He would drink day & night. Finally he got so wild & out of control that one day he drink, drink, drink.. He took the jug of wine in his hand & went to the mosque in that area. Of course no one was able to stand in his way. If anyone said anything, he would cut his head off. So no one can say anything. He went into the mosque & saw the Shaykh there. He said to the Shaykh "Salam Alaikum". And the Shaykh said "Wa alaikum salam. May Allah make this joy, happiness of yours to become permanent. How beautiful" Shaykh Effendi said to him. "You be happy like this on the Day of Judgement also insha Allah" he said. The people there couldn't say anything. They grumbled a bit but said to the Shaykh "what kind of a du'a you made? The man was already drunk as a skunk & come to the mosque like this. After all this shamelessness how can you make du'a for him like this?" He said..Normally mashayikh don't explain the wisdom of their acts but there he did. He said "My du'a for him was that there can't be happiness with such a disgrace. May he reach real happiness, be saved from that addiction & come to mosque, his akhirah may be good also & he be from the people of paradise. He may reach this kind of happiness. That was my du'a".

And the Prince heard these words & at that time he was awakened. He went back to the mosque & kissed the Shaykh's hand. Of course hidayah/guidance came on him. Shaykh's dua, miracle became apparent. With kind words.. If he spoke there differently, either he would cut off his head or swear at them & leave. When he heard this & reached hidayah, he turned back to his palace & saw all his bad friends. All of them were drinking, drunk speaking. He immediately ordered all jars of wine, raki, whatever there is to be broken. All got broken, all the jars of drinks. He reached guidance.”

They said, this Shaykh did. His father was also struggling with him for years, "I couldn't discipline him, make him reach guidance but with one nice word.." All the bad characteristics he had, went & he also disciplined his friends. So these things happen with one word, by one kind word. Therefore, although it is not easy for man because speaking kind words is also speaking with wisdom. "Qawlun Ma`rufun Wa Maghfiratun Khayrun Min Sadaqatin" (2:263) It is also mentioned in Quran- kind words are better than charity followed by injury.

Today we speak about nice treating to people, to be polite with people. It is from adab of Islam & adab of Tariqah- good behavior of Tariqah & good behavior of Islam, to be nice with people. Don't be tough & not good with people. Even Allah (awj), there is Ayah speaking to Prophet (sas), "if you be tough & bad speaking to people, they will run away from you" (3:159) This is also one proof to speak with people good. Because there is some people when they order, they order very good thing for people but they order like hitting people on their head with stick. So nobody like this. Even if it is the very best thing, they said but people not happy & they want to do opposite. Many story from this.

One of story; there was one very polite man, very nice speaking with people. People happy with him. He is handsome also and he is selling honey, the best. When he going to place selling honey, every people coming, taking, buying from him. Everywhere like this. And one of people he is envious & he is bad character & bad speaking. He look, he said, "this is honey making good money. This man, he selling, I can sell also". He buy, also he buy good honey, not bad honey. And went from town to town, village to village. From morning up to evening, he cannot sell anything. And coming to house, he was very angry.

He shouting for wife, "Why I am, all day I try to sell this honey, nobody buy from me". Wife, she said "Your face, like pickle, very sour so the people afraid from you. They don't like to come near you." Even he sell the best thing, the honey. It is like this, when you say very good things to people, you must say it in a way people to be happy with it.

Another story also. There was one Sultan. He has Prince, his son Prince. And Prince, he was a naughty one. Bad friend, bad behaving-drinking, doing bad things everyday. One day, he make the worst. He bring, make party in his palace and in palace, he drink drink.. And he was very drunk. He take with him his bottle of wine & come to mosque.

In mosque, there was Shaykh. The Shaykh, he see him, he was drunk like this. Saying "As-Salamu Alaikum, how are you?" Shaykh said, "Alhamdulillah, we are good" and "What you say about me?" he ask, Prince for Shaykh. Shaykh said, "You are happy. Allah make you happy like this all your life. In Mahshar (Day of Judgement) also you be happy like this." Because nobody can say anything different for this Prince. He was even bad character, he can kill people if somebody saying "why you are drunk, you coming to mosque like this?" Nobody can do this, to say anything for him. So people, they were not happy with Shaykh. And say "How you say this, for he is drunk & coming to mosque?" He said.. Normally there is wisdom from what mashayikh saying. You see, he say something. You will be surprised how he say, this is not good what he say.

He said "I will explain for you". I said to him to be happy like this. He is very happy, drunk. Now, happy. But after a little bit, he will wake up & he will be not happy. But when he become drunk with love of Allah, he will be whole life happy. And because his love of Allah, Allah, he will be happy in Day of Resurrection. And this Prince, he hear this. And from praying, du'a of this Shaykh, he also, Allah give him hidayah/guidance. And he was suprised & "What I do? I am in wrong way. This is the right way, what Shaykh said". So he went back to his palace. He break all this wine bottle & jar. And make this, his friend, to not bring anymore. Because they are afraid from him, they cannot drink any drop if he not ask for them. And his father, he was very happy. He was suprised. "How I am many years I try with many scholar, many ulama to bring him to right way, but he not coming. From one word, good polite word he become like this."

This is, most of mashayikh they do this. They have very nice & very polite & they are very tolerant with everybody. It is difficult, you cannot to, most of them we cannot do this. But it is nice to try to do it insha Allah.

Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, Al Fatiha.
