Shaykh Bahauddin: Dastur Ya Sayidi Madad! Dastur Ya Sayidi Madad! Dastur Ya Sayidi Ya Shaykh Nazim. Dastur Ya Sidi Nizamuddin...

Al Hamdulillah! Al Hamdulillah! Masha' Allah! Yesterday it was great day, Al Hamdulillah! That Mawlana Shaykh he join us for Juma prayer. Al Hamdulillah! We are lucky people that we've been with him in this Juma prayer. Allah (swt) He is so grateful on us, because He (swt) put us under the one He (swt) loves. And this is, they say "you're born in Laylatul Qadr." When you are so lucky, they say "you are born on Laylatul Qadr." The one night that is equal to 100 years, to 1000 years. Maybe more! But Allah Almighty just to make it for us to understanding. And I pretend all of us we are born on Laylatul Qadr. And we've been so lucky to be with Mawlana Shaykh. To be under his flag, to be under his feet wow!! To be under his feet it is so good! It is so good, Al Hamdulillah! It is so high.

Uwais Al Qarani. The Prophet (saws)before he passed away, he sent him his Jubba, his dress. And when they went to him, they went with S. Omar (ra) & S. Ali (ra). He was questioned "did you see the Prophet (saws)?" S.Omar (ra)was so angry "what you mean, we've been accompanying him so many years we've been with him!" S. Ali (ra) says "calm down, calm down!" He says "Yes! I see him, one time..one time!" Say "what you see?" He says " I couldn't see from here on, I only see from here from the knee. After that I couldn't see. And down also I couldn't see." "Ha!" Says "this is the true one".

So to be under the feet of the Warith Al Muhammadi (Inheritor) The inheritor of Sayidina Muhammad (saws). So it should be very good! It should be very good! See what he (Mawlana) gives us! And how he gives us without looking for us, who we are, what we are doing. And how we are acting & how we are behaving. Even we still complaining & not happy. And still we are wanting something more & we are like small kids going around. He doesn't look for all of this. He looks for the inside for the heart. Say "this is mine so I have to give! This is Allah Almighty! He (swt) dress as the one He loves, like Him 'Giver!' Allah Almighty He (swt) is the Giver He (swt) is the Generous. He (swt) is the Most Generous to give. So Al Hamdulillah He (swt) give us the most generous Shaykh, that he also doesn't look for our what we have & how we behave; and he gives, gives, gives, gives, gives never stops! Don't think you have enough, no! He is still giving even when you are in the graveyard, he is still giving. In Akhira he is still giving, in Paradise he is still giving because.. That's why I am saying it is very very, very much of giving. That generosity is only Allah Almighty, He (swt) Is Generous like this. And the people He (swt) loves,He (swt) likes them to be like Him Generous. They don't expect anything, nothing. Only they give.

Allah Almighty, He (swt) doesn't ask from you to give Him (swt) something. He (swt) is the One who giving. Al-Mu'ti His Name, The Giver. Al Hamdulillah. That's why we are so lucky & we must be so happy even anything is disturbing us, even anything we are not happy with, but we still have to have this happiness. Is very important to know that you all the time are given. So whatever bad things coming to you, say "Al Hamdulillah! I have something coming!"

This is like so many people worries in those days so many crisis in some countries. And they say "O what we shall do after this? What we shall do? How we will act?" It is so simple - Be dervish. Be dervish! Allah Almighty is saying to you "If you are dervish you are flexible to be anywhere." So this is the teaching of Tariqa, maybe Allah Almighty will give you billions but if you can behave as a dervish this is the true because you don't care what you have. Because your inside is darwish. The dervish can sleep here can, sleep outside. Can sleep in the palace it is ok. If he is in the palace it is ok it is a palace it is not excitement. If in the carpet it is ok, it doesn't disturb him. This is the dervish. If they give you one bread, with one tomato, wow! It is the most fantastic food. Maybe they give you full meal. Say "Alhamdulillah! This is maybe better but also fantastic food." So the small one doesn't make you angry and the big one doesn't make you excited.

I have experience with Mawlana Shaykh, this in the old days. We went to one of the most majestic palace in the world with Mawlana Shaykh, in the world! in the world. And the treasure and the things that I see. In those days it was worth billions. And after Mawlana Shaykh has his meeting and we are going out you know what Mawlana Shaykh ask me? "Bahauddin! Bahauddin! Bahauddin!" Did you like this palace? Say "uhmmm! It's ok, very good."M."Do you want to live in it?" S.B:"No! I don't think so. I don't think so I don't want to." M: "You like it?" S.B: "Insha'Allah it will be it will give happiness for the owner. I am not looking at it like "Wow I wish I had such a thing, wow!" I was from my inside saying "Al Hamdulillah you don't take me to the garage of that palace!" Then maybe I cannot control myself. Al Hamdulillah he put me in an area I don't like.

And the question it was a simple question. So Al Hamdulillah, Mawlana said "That's why I take you to visit these places. Because you don't look with jealousy. You don't look that "O I am jealous I want this one." But luckily he didn't take me for the garage. Al Hamdulillah this it was very important test for me. I was so young those days still young! With little bit white beard. But Mawlana Shaykh he was teaching me how to behave, and now I am sharing this experience with you. Al Hamdulillah, because Mawlana Shaykh he is, he is taking the knowledge from the highest point. And he make it for the small baby to be able to digest this knowledge. And Al Hamdulillah since these years, we've been really having this knowledge. And Mawlana Shaykh he give it in the way that everyone going out "O I got my answer! O I got my answer!.." Alhamdulillah it was particularly for the heart, for each heart is going as a line.

So on the other hand, everyone has other seeing. So you have your own corner to see. So everyone when he sees something he can explain for the others. So that's why maybe I am here to explain my my vision. But Al Hamdulillah I realize that Mawlana Shaykh he wants to give something, for you through me. It is coffee' talk. So we are very common people. And he wants to teach us how to behave when we are so high, and I don't wish that we be so low. I don't see anybody with Mawlana Shaykh becomes so low, this is impossible.I can say it. But in case you are stuck in the desert, so what you do? You sleep on the ground. Don't say "I want very luxury bed to sleep." Or don't say "I want big meal to eat." So this to make your ego... pressing your ego.

It is very important for us to be dervish. Maybe we eat one egg & no crisis affect us. It is very important, because if you go for basics nothing will affect you. The basic is solid all the time. When we are going up, it is not solid. And my mother she was saying about very funny things. Saying the luck, the good luck it has one eye only. It's funny! Say "the good luck it's taking the person when he is seeing with one eye looking see the person, O the good luck going going, taking... and going for the not seeing side so throwing it up it doesn't see. One eye see so, so when it is shifting up with the eyes that sees. But with the eyes that doesn't see throwing it. So be careful when the good luck coming to you & you're in the good side of it as you are going up, maybe you can be falling down, because there is no eyes here, one eye. One eye!

So that's why for the preparation in your life, be ready for the good one & be ready for the bad one. So don't make the good one to make you - "I am the king here, I am the creator here, I am everything." No there is no eye-side. Waiting to fall down.... So don't make the good one to make you proud & don't make the bad one to make you hopeless. So, if you can go with two balances and this is not easy! It's easy to say, but it's not easy as we are explaining. So this is very, very important for us at least to try the balance. At least to try to how to behave in the balance.

Because Al Hamdulillah I am again saying, when something is with Mawlana Shaykh, never down! Never down at all. But even so, we have to be prepared we have to be ready for anything coming good or anything coming bad. Al Hamdulillah I think today we are more than enough to speak because we didn't have any coffee' talk of again serious talk, even I put you in front of me S. Nizamuddin Alhamdulillah I like you to laugh also. Everyday you have to make therapy to laughing. You are very serious people, you know? Must laugh everyday at least half an hour, in one corner even they say to you, "you are cuckoo!" "Yes!" "What's happening?" Doesn't matter, "I am now in therapy." Al Hamdulillah you must be laughing! Laughing is good! And the good way of laughing, is to be cockoo! Then it is perfect laughing!

Al Hamdulillah, if somebody give us some minute to laugh, that person he must be in Paradise. Doesn't look for religion, doesn't look for belief, doesn't look for anything. This is Allah Almighty giving. So try first to teach yourself how to laugh and then try to go anywhere and to make somebody to laugh. Even there is hadith saying, the smile is sadaqa. Imagine you smile to somebody "good morning!" So sadaqa. So imagine, how you make somebody to laugh Zakat!! Al Hamdulillah!
