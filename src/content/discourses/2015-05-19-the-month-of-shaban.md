As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Thank Allah we were able to reach Rajab and it also ended. Now, we entered the month of our Holy Prophet, the month of Shaʿbān. These are holy months. Rajab is the month of Allah Jalla Jalaluh, and the month of Shaʿbān is our Holy Prophet’s month. Shaʿbān is the second holy month. The third is Ramaḍān.

Days are passing. Different manifestations occur during these beautiful days. Allah has different manifestations and different gifts for each month. Because the month of Shaʿbān is the month of our Holy Prophet, we need to bring forth more Salawat. Those who do not should recite at least 100. Those who have a duty and already recite Salawat everyday should recite 300 more, 500 more, 1000 more, or as much as they can. Bring forth Salawat abundantly on our Master in this month. When you bring forth Salawat on our Master, he looks at you with love in return.

The love and affection of our Holy Prophet is what Muslims and Mumins want. To love him is to obey the orders of Allah. Our holy Prophet says, “Whoever says, ‘La Ilaha Illallah,’ might be Muslim. Being Mumin is a higher station. As long as you do not love the Beloved of Allah, me (our Holy Prophet), more than everybody, you cannot reach true faith.” The person who loves our Holy Prophet does what he tells. If a person loves another person in the world, he immediately researches the loved one before that one says anything. He researches: Who is he? What is he pleased with? What does he like? He tries to do those. When we love our Holy Prophet, we need to like the things he likes and dislike the things he dislikes.

Barat celebration is also on the 15th of the holy month of Shaʿbān. It is a holy night. Everything that is to happen the following year and for the whole year is written on that night. That night is a holy night. May Allah give us good years Insha Allah. May it have its blessing (baraka). It is a holy month and a month of blessing. May Allah process and engrave (naqsh) the love of our Prophet in our hearts Insha Allah. Naqshbandis are such. It is engraved Insha Allah.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
