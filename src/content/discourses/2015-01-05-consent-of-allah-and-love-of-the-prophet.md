Assalamu Alaykum,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

May the month of Mawlid be holy. May it be a cause for good and may there be blessing Inshallah. The birth of our Holy Prophet (SAW) is a blessing for all worlds. To respect him is worship. You can respect him however you like. It is a thing Allah (JJ) loves and orders. Respect our Holy Prophet (SAW).

There are some mindless, stupid people. They disrespect our Master for a world worth twopenny: “No, respecting our Holy Prophet is a sin. No, it is not allowed.” Why are they doing this? For benefits, for worldly benefits. There is no benefit for the hereafter in what they say. They will be scolded in the hereafter and they will be degraded a level for misguiding people from the way. Allah (JJ) knows, they may even leave the world without iman in their last breath.

The man who thinks he has gained benefit by saying something against our Holy Prophet (SAW) is completely mindless. Those moneys he makes can be as much as he wants. Everything is in the hands of Allah (JJ). Once Allah (JJ) wants, He can take it all away in a minute. And the evil he has done would stay with him.

Baraka is with our Holy Prophet (SAW). Unfortunateness is with those on the side of Shaitan and with those who are disrespectful. They can have as much money as they like: money is of no benefit. The money he makes would become a poison for him. Allah (JJ) would spoil the pleasure of that money and take it out in this world for him who disrespects our Holy Prophet (SAW).

For whatever reason, they are fiddling, they are fiddling a lot. Of course, there are bought people too. There are people who follow their ego and have no mind. Whereas, if they were smart, if they love money so much, they would have thought whether this provided benefit or not. Our Holy Prophet’s (SAW) baraka is upon those who love him. Those who do not love him can have as much money as they like: it is no use. There were so many wealthy Quraishis and those moneys did not benefit them. Those who were against our Holy Prophet (SAW) were thrown in the dark well at Badr and were left rotten there. But those respecting our Holy Prophet (SAW) are all being mentioned as sultans until present. What is important is the sultanate of the hereafter. The pleasures of the world are passing. They are the sultans.

Those who do not respect our Holy Prophet (SAW) are being cursed until Judgment Day. Those who do the same in these times will also face the same thing. Allah’s (JJ) curse will be upon them until Doomsday. May Allah (JJ) protect us from their evils.

People are asking, “He said this and he said that...” Disregard them. It is not even worth talking about them, but sometimes they are fooling people. That is why it is important. The most important worship for us is to respect our Holy Prophet (SAW). Respecting our Holy Prophet (SAW) is the order of Allah (JJ). This order is in hundreds of places in the Koran Karim. To do the order is worship. May Allah (JJ) make us amongst those whom our Holy Prophet (SAW) loves. May we love him too Inshallah. Our greatest aim is the consent of Allah (JJ) and the love of the Prophet (SAW).

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq.

Al-Fatiha.
