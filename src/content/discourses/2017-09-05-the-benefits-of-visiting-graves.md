As-salāmu ‘alaykum wa raḥmatu Llāhi wa barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīn wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

"Man tashabbaha bi-qawmin fahuwa minhum." It is ḥadīth sharīf of our ProphetNone. Whoever likes a certain people, he is one of them. A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Yā ayyuha l-ladhīna āmanū lā tatawallaw qawman ghaḍiba Llāhu ‘alayhim qad ya’isū mina l-ākhirati kamā ya’isa l-kuffāru min aṣḥābi l-qubūr." (60:13) Āyati karīmah is saying don't be friends with people who don't believe in ākhirah. Don't be with them. Just like unbelievers, they don't respect the deceased. And they say of people who visit the graves that they worship the graves. Whereas our ProphetNone said you can visit the graves. HeNone said, "My shafā‘ah (intercession) is wājib (obligatory) for those who visit myNone grave." "Man zāra qabrī wajabat lahu shafā‘atī." Then heNone said who reads Yā-sīn Sharīf at the grave of his parents on Fridays, Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla will forgive all his sins. Its reward will be endless.

Shayṭān is cheating these people. Despite so many āyāt and aḥadīṭh, they are showing the wrong way to people. They don't listen to our ProphetNone. And there are people who don't even go to Madīnah. Even if they go, they especially avoid going to Masjidu n-Nabawī to visit our ProphetNone. They don't visit the graves of ṣaḥābah nor of awliyā’. Visiting these graves is a sin and shirk for them. And they are cheating people. That is a big harm to people because shafā‘ah is what we need the most, the shafā‘ah of our ProphetNone. Without it our condition is bad. No one will be able to rescue himself in ākhirah.

Shukr to AllāhNone, there was a visit to Ḥalā Sulṭān today in Cyprus. More than a thousand people went there on buses. May AllāhNone accept their ziyārah, in shā’a Llāh. May her support be with us. We went there two days ago on the third day of ‘Īd. It is a tradition from the Ottomans to visit her on the third day of ‘Īd. May AllāhNone accept our ziyārah (visit) too. She was a great lady ṣaḥābīyah (companion). She was the aunt of our ProphetNone. Therefore, we call her Ḥalā Sulṭān. She is one of the greatest ṣaḥābah When our ProphetNone gave good tidings that Cyprus would be conquered, many ṣaḥābah came here. So there are many ṣaḥābah shahīd here. But only a few are known. There are more than a thousand ṣaḥābah. May AllāhNone increase their maqām and may their support be with us, in shā’a Llāh. We are not talking about visiting awliyā’ or the prophets but who goes on ḥajj definitely should visit our Prophet'sNone grave. And there are graves of prophets in other countries too. For example, in Damascus, Aleppo, in other places in Syria, in Lebanon, in Palestine and Jordan. In Hijaz, other than our ProphetNone, there is Prophet Ṣāliḥ. Wherever the graves of the prophets are, visiting them is good. It is for our benefit. Don't listen to anyone [who says otherwise] because AllāhNone has cursed them. Allāh'sNone anger is upon them. Don't be like them. May AllāhNone protect us. May the holy ones' support be with us, in shā’a Llāh. May our ziyārah be accepted. They come to visit Mawlānā here as well. Who visits doesn't leave empty-handed, with Allāh'sNone permission.

ProphetNone he saying, "Man tashabbaha bi-qawmin fahuwa minhum," – who imitates somebody or likes somebody, he is like them, he is with them. And Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla saying, "Yā ayyuha l-ladhīna āmanū lā tatawallaw qawman ghaḍiba Llāhu ‘alayhim qad ya’isū mina l-ākhirati kamā ya’isa l-kuffāru min aṣḥābi l-qubūr." (60:13) "O believer, don't be with this people who are hopeless from next life. They are like this people who are hopeless from grave people, graveyard people." Because nowadays there is a group like this people. Like mushrik and like kāfir and who be like them he will be with them. They are not accepting to visit graveyard. Not accepting to go to any holy grave of awliyā’ or... even for ProphetNone they are not accepting. They saying don't go there and they are going there [but] not going to visit the ProphetNone, just going near the mosque and after coming, not staying at all.

And ProphetNone he say, "Man zāra qabrī wajabat lahu shafā‘atī." "Who come and visit meNone in my grave [they must receive] my shafā‘ah..." What meaning shafā‘ah? Shafā‘ah meaning to say for AllāhNone, "O my Lord this people I'm with meNone, don't put them in hell, put them in paradise." Shafā‘ah this is shafā‘ah. And too difficult for anybody to go to paradise with his deeds. What he did? Nothing. It is, it is useless and it is no any value for this. So ProphetNone also saying, "Who visit graveyard of his mother and father Friday and read Yā-sīn sharīf, AllāhNone reward him and forgive him and reward him very good, too many reward for this." Especially Friday who go. So it is ProphetNone encouraging us to visit graveyard and to visit ṣāliḥīn, prophets. And prophets... there is many place prophets in Turkey, in Syria, in Lebanon, in Jordan, in Egypt, in Palestine many, in Hijaz.

There is everywhere prophets' graveyard and there is after grave [of] prophet there is also ṣaḥābah, thousand of ṣaḥābah. Many of them you don't know where is their graveyard but some of them they we have like in Cyprus, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we have very precious, very big station. She is Auntie of ProphetNone – Hāla Ṣulṭān. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we visit in third of day of ‘Īd. It is custom for Cypriot for ‘Īdu l-Aḍḥá to visit, in third day ‘Īdu l-Aḍḥá, her and to make feast and giving food or drink or something. And today, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, also there is people who cannot go by normal. Every holy time in this year they [Greek side government] give them three days each year. So today also thousands of people they went to other side [Greek side]. AllāhNone accept their ziyārah [to Hāla Ṣulṭān] and accept our ziyārah also because it is place where AllāhNone looking for with His mercy and blest eye for this place because this is they are beloved for Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla.

Whole time HeNone is looking who is coming to MyNone beloved servant. INone am happy with them because if anybody love one and somebody love him he be love him. So Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla whole time HeNone is when HeNone looking and seeing you visiting HisNone beloved people, HeNone will be happy with you also. This is what believe, our belief. But nowadays many people they are not accepting. When you see visiting any graveyard or making du‘ā’ they are objecting. You are [they're] saying worshipping graveyard. Now they have new name "graveyard worshipper" they say. How we make worshipping? We only worship for AllāhNone and we are the making du‘ā’ for them and to accept to be like them, blessed one, beloved one for Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla. This is only what we want, not want anything asking worshipping them.

And they are cheating people and make them to be not having any blessing and this is aim of shayṭān to make people, they don't have anything, to go with him to Jahannam. Not accepting shafā‘ah, not accepting visiting any good people, so they make be proud. They making four raka‘āt without sunnah, without anything and they are, they thought they will go Jannah with this what they are doing. Astaghfīru Llāh. AllāhNone give them good thinking to come to right way, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone make us with this people, with HisNone blessed people, in shā’a Llāh.
