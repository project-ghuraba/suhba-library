Sheikh Muhammad Nazim Al-Haqqani An-Naqshibandi, Sohbat of the 10th of June, 1984.

In London, in England, in Germany, everywhere that we are living in western countries, they must be careful for their children. And it is impossible to keep our children in western countries and they are going around, out of Islam. My Grandsheikh just ordered me to tell them. It may be useful for every Muslim also. Because it is a very important thought or a very important thinking in our hearts. We are thinking about our children. We are trying to keep Islam and Iman/faith, but for our children, it is so difficult. Because conditions in western countries is too heavy, too difficult and it's taking away our children from us. And he reminded me today, Grandsheikh, to tell this, that point.

Every week, every Friday, it may be better (to make it) Friday, you must gather your wife and your children, who (ever is) living with you, small one, big one, all of them. Make them to clean themselves. Cleaning their faces, washing their hands and feet, and sitting around you and you are sitting in middle. And repeating kalimat-ush shahadah - Ash'hadu an la illaha illa Allah wa ash' hadu anna Muhammadan abduhu wa rasuluh 3 times, then also 3 times - Astaghfirullah. If more, doesn't matter. Then giving your Shahadah and Istighfar to Prophet peace be upon him, with your family. O my Prophet - "I am giving to you this Shahadah from all of us. And I am giving my family to you also, under your view, under your looking, to keep them." And Prophet, he is Ameen. Ameen is trustworthy, who keeps Amanah/trusts. Then Prophet peace be upon him, taking them under his protection, under his looking.

We hope that is going to be as a protection for new generations in western countries. Because Muslims openly asking me and complaining to me from their children. Allah Almighty, in the time of Hud (alayhi salam) peace be upon him, when they were denying Hud's (alayhi salam) prophethood and not listening, not coming to Iman, then Allah Almighty ordering to Hud (alayhi salam) to take every believer with him and making a circle around them and sitting, then Allah Almighty informing that I am destroying qawmu Ad, the Ad nation, with strong wind, storm. And Hud (alayhi salam) gathering whole believers and sitting in a place and making around them a circle.

Then, by the command of Allah Almighty, opening from that storm a hole of middle coming on them, but strongly coming then taking everything. Every person, they were like this tower of church or minaret of mosques. 60 yards, 50 yards (tall) people - giant people they were. Taking them from earth like leaves, taking up and throwing them down, making into pieces. But that circle that Hud (alayhi salam peace be upon him) was sitting with his believers coming softly, gently, gently coming that wind. Giving from paradise, like paradise wind coming. Now Grandsheikh say that in western countries and also in everywhere there is storm. Storm, storming, taking people. No one can firm himself except who makes him in a circle of protection from Allah Almighty. And this is a strong wasila - 'cause', strong 'means' to keep Ummah/the nation in western countries and everywhere.

You may do every week, once. Renew tawhid, iman. You may renew your faith, and asking forgiveness from Allah Almighty, and He makes a circle around you and your family that storm winds cannot enter, or that you are not going out to be in it (storm). That is a protection also for us or it is impossible to keep new generations. That is for Muslims, most important. But it is going to be for everyone important. Because non-Muslims, Christians, and other nations they are affected also by that stormy winds. Everyone is suffering now and no shelter for sons of Adam except that Allah Almighty is making a shelter for them. Who comes under that shelter may be in safety and peace. But who is running away, never going to save himself or anyone else. And everyone suffering now from their families, from their children.

www.saltanat.org Page 1 Nothing can be done now in our time, except to come to shelter of our Lord. And (there is) no shelter except our Lord's shelter. We are asking forgiveness during this holy month. (May) Allah makes us under his shelter, and everyone who belongs to us and everyone that we are dealing with them. And we are asking shelter for whole people living in that big city. It is a thankful duty for believers to pray for others also. To ask for them also a shelter from Allah Almighty. Because believers must ask goodness, as much as he is asking for himself he must ask for others also. We are going to be pleased with everyone (who is) going to believe in their Lord. We are going to be pleased with who is going to be his Lord's servant. And we are asking also to continue to be our Lord's sincere and humble servants. Amin.

Wa min Allahi t-tawfiq. It is enough. Bi hurmati l-Fatiha.
