As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Fa-staqim kamā umirta wa-man tāba ma‘ak" (11:112). This is an order from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla to our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. This order is a big order. Our Holy ProphetNone says, "This sūrah turned my beard white." The holy beard of our Holy ProphetNone was completely black but after this heNone had a few white hairs in his beard. That came from the heaviness of this sūrah and this order. Because being straight is a difficult thing.

Believers, people who love our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, must follow himNone and keep on the straight way. Lying and cheating are not straight. They are the opposite of the straight way. People who do these things are people who follow their egos and have weak willpower. Their willpower and their minds must be weak because things outside of the straight way bring no benefit. People think, "I have to be clever. I have to do this and that. I have to cheat people in order to gain." What they will gain is sin and regret. Because lying and cheating never bring any profit. Being straight brings profit. Even if difficult, its result is good. It is the best. Nothing is better than that.

But people choose to be on their own. They forget Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. They forget goodness. However, they think they are clever and strive to do something. As long as AllāhNone doesn't want it, they can be as clever as they want, they can think they are very clever, they will lose everything with just one thing. As we said, this is a lesson for people. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla ordered something that is 100% profit for humanity. Doing the opposite of it brings loss both to them and to everything. Therefore, be straight. Be with people who are straight.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying in Qur’ān ‘aẓīmu sh-sha’n: Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Fa-staqim kamā umirta wa-man tāba ma‘ak" (11:112). This is order for Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, from sūrat Hūd, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam said, "This sūrah make my, some beard [hairs] become white" because Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, hisNone physical [body], even [though] heNone was sixty years or more – fifty or sixty years, between this – this year heNone was physically like young. Long life but heNone is like young people. But for this it is heavy order – to be straight. Istiqāmah meaning to be straight and to follow order of AllāhNone, not to follow your idea, your self, your ego. It is for benefit of human being to be straight, honest, but people they're not following this. Even that time, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying it was heavy. But now, it is completely heavy because people they thought it is, cannot be with to be honest and to be straight in this time; you cannot live, survive in this life. But it is opposite, if you are not following this, you are not be in good position or good life for you.

Many people they are trying, and they thought they are clever, to do everything to win, to make themself higher and higher. But at the end, who following order of AllāhNone, he will be the winner. He will be the best position. The best position for human being – to be acceptable one in Divine Presence of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Other things it is not important at all.

Because people, once they like you sometimes, many times, they said, "We are with you." After, they're not honest, they leave you, because you are also like them. There... For this time, end of time, everybody they're following opposite order of AllāhNone. And after they said, "Why this happened, why that happened?" They thought they're clever, they can do everything. But when AllāhNone not want, even you have everything, you have clever, you have money, you have everything, if AllāhNone not want, you will be not be succeed for anything.

The most important every time, every minute, what you do to be honest and to be your intention for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla; to be good servant for AllāhNone and to be good for people because Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla HeNone is, what HeNone love from people – who help people and serve people, other people, for sake of AllāhNone. This is very important, not to see people like, "I will, they must serve me, they must help me, they must do everything for me." No. This what AllāhNone not like this. You must be like Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. HeNone was helping, only helping for sake of AllāhNone, hisNone ummah, even at the Maḥshar time also, Day of Resurection, heNone will be help for people, to save them from Hell, to put them in Paradise, in shā’a Llāh. You must imitate Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam and to be with himNone, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
