An incident occured. They recorded some of it anyway. Inshallah they will upload it. Our brothers were very saddened by this issue. However, Hazrat Prophet (SAW) has a hadith: There is good in what happens. Allah Azza wa Jalla says:

ُللّه او

َ

مْ ُك َّ ل رٌّ شَ و

“Wa’asa antakrahu shay’an wahuwa khayrun lakum, wa’asa antuhibbu shay’an wahuwa sharrun lakum, wallahu ya’lamu wa’antum la ta’lamoon.” (Sura Baqara:216) “You love some things and perhaps they are bad for you; you do not like some things and perhaps they are good for you.”

It is not even appropriate to talk loudly in Allah’s house. The man is out reciting the Basmala, “Bismillahir Rahmanir Rahim.” When he says “Alhamdulillah” someone screams from over there, interrupts him, and makes a commotion. People pray there, and when greeting with salam it is not returned. Giving salams in sunna, responding to it is fard. They do not do this either. We pray Duha prayer beforehand then sit with manners there. The imam is about to stand and deliver a speech when they start screaming at the imam and silence him.

This is a direct affront against Allah (JJ), it is treading on Allah’s orders. It is an insult on the sunna of our Holy Prophet (SAW) and on him. So it has nothing to do with neither Islam nor humanity. Even those people called giaour, non-Muslim people, did not do this to us. Whereas if they do it, you would not mind: they are giaour, they might do it. But these ones are acting like this as if they are people to teach us religion. You have nothing to do with the religion of Islam.

This is something that never fits the cutoms of this nation. It does not fit their manners and morals. People here are hospitable, kind people. You cannot get up and treat guests like this. Allah knows inside us, but our exterior is Islam. Those who see us respect us as, “You are Muslim”. They are saying, “Go away!” Thank Allah, along with the inspiration of our brothers, we saw that there was nothing to do there, and we decided to leave so the situation would not get worse. We fulfilled the order of Hazrat Prophet (SAW): if an argument occurs with someone, do not answer, leave it to Allah, and shut evil.

They were going to make a fuss of things. Allah kept us safe. Our intention was for Allah, so Allah kept His servants safe and we left. However, their real issue was to erupt a huge fight there and they wanted to make tasawwuf people look bad in front of the people.

They are not the folk of the country either. They came from other places, from far away places. They came for evil. If they want evil, Allah will give themselves evil. On the other hand, we want good and Allah will give good. We do not want to fight or argue with anybody. Instead of fixing us, there are hundreds of thousands of men on the streets who have no idea of religion, let them fix them. However, they are not fixing them but ruining them. Those who go to them are perished.

While we Inshallah want good, so we will find good Inshallah. Allah is with the good. He is not with those who are against Him. Inshallah what we are saying is useful and people understand what they are about. Then there are plenty of lies, yet our Holy Prophet (SAW) says, “A Muslim does not lie.” He might do everything but a lie does not befit a Muslim, it does not work. Wahhabis and Salafis never listen. Their heads are like rock. They used to say, “It is better to talk to a rock.” I have been hearing of this for thirty years and Allah showed it to us yesterday.

They are yelling inside the mosque, “Mushrik!” They are screaming, “Mushrik!” to people praying in the front lines inside the mosque who give the salam. So who knows what they say to other people then. In other words, according to their fairness, there are not even one million Muslims in the world. Even so we said, “La Ilaha Illallah, Muhammadun Rasulullah,” but again they are saying, “Mushrik!”

The key to paradise is “La Ilaha Illallah, Muhammadun Rasulullah”. Our Holy Prophet says, “Those who say it are saved.” Even that one sentence alone makes people reach eternal bliss. This is what we are saying to people. Whether here or wherever we go, we tell this to people and give this glad tiding.

Paradise is very wide, everyone would fit, and it would be enough for everybody. They are the opposite, they want to send everybody to hell. Allah is with those who want good. They want evil; we want good. Everyone wants good and they still want evil.

Therefore, there is no need to get upset. Our ikhwan, poor ones got really sad here. Meaning they got embarassed because this incident occurred. No, there is no such thing. We are used to it already, nothing much happenned, and we also have lots of crazies. There is good in this Inshallah. You will see it Inshallah. May we see that good Inshallah.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq. Al-Fatiha.
