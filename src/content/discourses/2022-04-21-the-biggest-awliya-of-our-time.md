As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Alā inna awliyā’a Llāhi lā khawfun ‘alayhim wa-lā hum yaḥzanūn" (10:62). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘aẓīm. There are many Awliyā’ of AllāhNone. There are hidden ones and known ones. Sayyidunā Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim was from among the known ones. He is from the beloved servants of AllāhNone. We say shukr to AllāhNone for favouring us and letting us follow him. Today, by the wisdom of AllāhNone, is the birthday of Mawlānā, the twenty-first of April. Exactly one hundred years ago, in 1922, he came to this world. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla created him. And he was born on this island close to Hala Sultan in the town of Larnaca.

Everyone knows what a great holy man, what a great awliyā’ he is, shukr to AllāhNone. Even when he was born, Sayyidunā Shaykh Sharafu d-Dīn gave ṣuḥbah to Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh ad-Dāghistānī and said, "A child of ours is born who will carry the burden of this ṭarīqah and who will take it from East to West. He will become the means of guidance to tens of thousands, millions of people." Shaykh Sharafu d-Dīn related this good news. He gave good tidings in such detail that, sorry to say, he even related the size of his shoes. Sayyidunā Shaykh Sharafu d-Dīn was so happy. We are lucky now to have a share in the miracle of Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim and the greatness of Sayyidunā Shaykh Sharafu d-Dīn. The man who told us this was our murīd. His father was a murīd of Shaykh Sharafu d-Dīn who heard and told about it.

So today, a hundred years have passed since then. By the wisdom of AllāhNone, though we were guessing that Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim would live for more than 100 years, he passed away physically. And he is still with us spiritually. He is guiding people. And many people are coming. Many people reach guidance through his ṣuḥbah and join this ṭarīqah. Furthermore, as his miracle thousands and tens of thousands of people see him in their dreams. And this miracle of his is continuing. They become Muslim and reach guidance. Muslims who have lost their way return to the right path. They reach guidance and follow this way. And this miracle is a sign of his being an Awliyā’ of a very high rank. He has reached all of you who have ever come to this mosque, this dergah, other dergahs. You have joined the way of AllāhNone, and reached the love of AllāhNone through his love. You reached the love of our Holy ProphetNone. With permission of AllāhNone, you will reach that in paradise as well, through him and his barakah. This door is a great door. It is the door of the friends of AllāhNone. AllāhNone doesn't let HisNone friends down. Certainly, HeNone will make those who follow him happy, here and hereafter, in shā’a Llāh.

May this day be blessed and good for all of us. May his birthday be happy not only for Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim, but for all of us. As a successor of our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam he is mercy to all people. As he followed the way of our Holy ProphetNone, he brings mercy and benefit to all mankind. Even non-Muslims used to acknowledge his ṣuḥbah, his preachings and his words. They used to accept them. Therefore, it is a holy day, a special day. It comes every year. And nothing has been done for two years because of the illness. Shukr to AllāhNone, this year with his hundredth birthday, there is no illness left with the permission of AllāhNone. It lost its effect.

The way he was in this world, shukr to AllāhNone, we know that his spirituality and support are always with us and upon us. His miracle is that he is a shield for us, as he is the beloved servant of AllāhNone. As we said, we will be together in ākhirah as well, in shā’a Llāh. We will meet physically again and will never be apart in paradise, in shā’a Llāh.

Today, we say Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, in Qur’ān saying, "Alā inna awliyā’a Llāhi lā khawfun ‘alayhim wa-lā hum yaḥzanūn" (10:62). AllāhNone praise HisNone beloved ones. "There is no any harm for them, no any sadness, nothing bad for them", HeNone said, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Awliyā’u Llāh, the biggest in our time was Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī and today, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are in twenty-one of April. On 1922, he come to this world, hundred year ago. One hundred year ago, he was born here in this island which, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam praise, "Jazīratu l-Khaḍrā’." Near, near Hala Sultan, Umm Ḥarām in Larnaca. He was one year in that place Allāhu a‘lam (AllāhNone knows) wisdom, maybe to be near her blessed maqām under her barakah. This is, I think, a wisdom because they were living in Nicosia, but one year, only, they went to Larnaca. And Mawlānā when he born, come back to Nicosia also.

So, many thing of Mawlānā karāmah from his childhood. Many thing it was around him. And one of this things, Shaykh Sharafu d-Dīn ad-Dāghistānī, he was in that time, he said to Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu-Llāh ad-Dāghistānī, "Today, one of our sons he come to this world, he born. And he will be hidāyah for people from East to West, from North to South. Thousand, millions people, they will be come to hidāyah through him." He, even he describe very carefully, very with detail, even he said his number [size] of his shoes. He was very happy that time, that day, Mawlānā Shaykh Sharafu d-Dīn ad-Dāghistānī. This is what we hear from one our old murīd, his father, he was murīd for Shaykh Sharafu d-Dīn ad-Dāghistānī and he was hearing this from him.

So, al-ḥamdu liLlāh we are thanking AllāhNone for this holy blessed day which we are celebrate. We must celebrate because, not only Mawlānā, it was for whole people of ṭarīqah. Thousands, ten thousand, millions of people, through his life, coming to Islām, accepting Islām, accept the Truth and accepting – many of people also get hidāyah, even Muslim they was out of the way, not accepting anything. All this people, Mawlānā was collecting them with his spiritual power and lectures and knowledge, especially knowledge from Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. And he was whole life for people. Going from place to place, not leaving anything. Anyone who invite him, even, we said sometimes, "No need to go there. It is not so important." He was going to reach people, even one to become to hidāyah, he was happy with this. So we are lucky for this, what we are now in his presence.

You all coming for him and he is happy with you. With who following, who cannot come – because lot of people they cannot reach here, but he is with them. He is happy with them also. He is mercy for human being because he is inheritor of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, Wārithu MuḥammadīNone. Everything he done, exactly like Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam what heNone teach Ummah. And he in pure way of teaching. Not mixing with – like other people they making religion as very bad thing. People running from this, or misunderstanding, making wrong thing. He was exactly like what Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam teaching. He put this knowledge from ProphetNone to to this time people. This, for this, still his miracle, his karāmah continue.

Many thousand of people still coming to right way, to hidāyah, through his lectures, his videos, his books. And especially, the real karāmah with his – through dream. Many of people, they accept Islām through dream. They said we see this. And many people also he tell them, "Go to dergah." People, they not know anything from religion. They are Muslim, our country, but most of them, they don't know anything. Even they are enemy for Islām. For this people also, many of them make with karāmah of Mawlānā they're coming to way of ProphetNone, way of happiness. Because happiness – way of ProphetNone way of happiness.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are lucky to follow him. And you are lucky, you are all, we are lucky we are here. We coming just for love of AllāhNone, love of ProphetNone, and of Mawlānā which he teach us this love. The most important thing for Muslim, for mu’min, for believer, to love ProphetNone, AllāhNone, and HisNone beloved one also. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh we are celebrate this day, and asking for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, to celebrate in Jannah also with him. This is big celebrate, to be from his present for us, to take us to Jannah, he show us way of Jannah, way of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. AllāhNone make his degree higher and higher, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone bless him.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
