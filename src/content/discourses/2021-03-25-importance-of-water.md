Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

A’ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm:

(Qur’ān 21:30). Allah ﷻ states in the Holy Quran: ‘And We have made from water every living thing.’ We have created every living thing from water, says Allah ﷻ. Water is a miracle of Allah ﷻ. Everything of His is a miracle. Allah ﷻ created life for these people in this world from water. Water is a great miracle. It is a great Ni'mah (favor) of Allah ﷻ. Son of man doesn’t know its value.

In Islam, there is no I’bada (worshipping) without water; it’s used from the start. Of course you can do Tayammum but ultimately you need water to do your Wuḍūʾ (ablution), so you have to use it. Our Muslims, in this regard, are the cleanest, and are the ones that do things that lead to the most righteous things by doing ablution and full-body ablution. Those who are not Muslim don’t pay attention to this anyway. The other religions or the so called religions since the only religion is Islam because the rest were changed and finished. There is ablution in the heavenly religions too but in Islam you can’t do anything without it in every aspect. Water came with Islam as cleanliness and it goes together.

Therefore, our Holy Prophet ﷺ says not to waste water and not to dirty the water. He cursed those who dirtied clean water. He said for those who break their Wuḍūʾ, meaning urinate and defecate, in clean running water that they would be cursed. Being cursed is not an easy thing so we need to honor and respect water. We need to use it carefully and not dirty it. It is one of the orders of Islam and one of the orders of our Holy Prophet ﷺ. People of today don’t give importance to this whereas now they should pay even more attention to it. Everywhere, whether [for] Muslims or not, water is decreasing and they’re talking about droughts occurring. Of course it’s going to decrease because if people don’t appreciate the Ni'mah (favor), Allah ﷻ will take that favor away from them. May Allah ﷻ protect us. May Allah ﷻ make us amongst those who appreciate it and are grateful for it. May Allah ﷻ not test us with lack of water insha’Allah.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq. Al-Fatiha.
