As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Sayyidunā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh ad-Dāghistānī gave many ṣuḥbahs to us. All of them are, mā shā’a Llāh, beautiful. And they are not lost now, but rather he transferred them to our father Shaykh Nāẓim. By the wisdom of AllāhNone, so that they didn't get into the hands of someone without understanding, HeNone put them in the best place. By the wisdom of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla and authority of the Awliyā’, he transferred that knowledge to Sayyidunā Shaykh Nāẓim who explained them to people in the best way. He told people about these beautiful things for more than 40-50 years. He spoke from the knowledge of our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, from the knowledge that Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla thinks is good for people. He told about goodness and beauty. He didn't tell about badness. He didn't tell anyone to do bad things. He didn't tell anyone to eat ḥarām. He transmitted all goodness through that knowledge to people. And people, shukr to AllāhNone, are benefitting from it day by day and coming to the way. They are entering the way loved by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.

Of course, we were present for the ṣuḥbahs but, as our age was still young, we don't remember many things. People forget when they are children. One of them... There is a part of a ṣuḥbah that we remember very well. That is because Mawlānā repeated it many times. Repeating is useful. Some people sometimes criticized Mawlānā by saying he talked about the same thing and that they had heard it all before. But hearing is necessary for people to get it into their heads and to remember. If you say it only once, many people will forget. By the wisdom of AllāhNone, "insān" speaks for itself. It comes from "nisyān" which means to forget. The actual meaning of "insān" is the one who forgets. Therefore, because he repeated it several times, even though fifty years have passed, we still remember it a little.

And that is: One day our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam told, I think, Sayyidunā ‘Alī, to call all the ṣaḥābah to gather there immediately. It was a nighttime. HeNone said, "Let everyone come here now." Of course, there were mushrikūn and war everywhere. There were people who wanted to end Islām. Sayyidunā ‘Alī shouted out, I suppose. When he shouted out, others shouted too. He shouted out for all ṣaḥābah to come there immediately. The ṣaḥābah came running, worried, ready to protect our Holy ProphetNone from anything, ready to protect Islām and the area on the way of AllāhNone. because they were called in a hurry by our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. When they all came, our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam told them, "The news came now from Jibrīl ‘alayhi s-salām. Do not talk about things that happened more than two hours ago, about worldly matters or disputes you had two hours ago."

HeNone said it as a big instruction to ṣaḥābah, because when you talk about things that happened two hours ago and say this one said this and that, that is no longer information but rumour. It will make people busy with it, cause them to fall out and create fitnah. Because people remember clearly up to two hours. Afterwards, they both forget and start adding things. One will add something, another will add another... It will be five by the third, then ten, then one hundred and then one thousand on top. It will cause people to fall out then. It will mislead them. Therefore, it is important – We should not talk about worldly matters that happened more than two hours ago. It is the order of our Holy ProphetNone not to talk. And we should obey this order as much as we can, in shā’a Llāh, because we are human. This is an advice. We must follow the advice as much as we can. It is better than not doing anything.

Therefore, it is an important matter because some people get angry about things that happened yesterday and get upset for things that happened even before that, or a week ago. Some people carry the events that took place years ago in their hearts. They tell other people and then there is resentment between people. Therefore, these issues are important. This is the wisdom of AllāhNone that even when we were kids, this was told several times and we remember it, shukr to AllāhNone. It is an important issue. It is beneficial to do this for our brothers, for all people regardless of being murīds, regardless of being Muslim.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are here again and there is ṣuḥbah from Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh, Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh ad-Dāghistānī. I was hear this. But before this Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh, every day, he was sitting maybe two, three hours making ṣuḥbah for common people. Many people coming, sitting and they put tea and they drink tea and with ṣuḥbah. So, many people coming and they're listening for ṣuḥbah. Mā shā’a Llāh, his ṣuḥbah, two hours, sometimes maybe more than two hours. Even three hours sometimes it was reach. With translator also it was. But, subḥāna Llāh, whole this ṣuḥbah, people they was listening and going. After he pass away, Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh ad-Dāghistānī, many of his ṣuḥbah was recorded. But, there is wisdom, all this disappear. You cannot find any of them.

Don't say, "pity", because he left the most perfect, he who record this, all this ṣuḥbah and can explain for benefit of people. He left Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim. So, don't say, "Pity this old ṣuḥbah not appear." Because, even they appear, people they will say, "Mawlānā say, Shaykh said this, and Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim said that." So, they make competition, something like this. So, it will be confusion between people. So only the, his successor, fully successor, coming from Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam to Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh ad-Dāghistānī, and from him to not make people say anything, so for Mawlānā Shaykh. Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim, he, more than fifty years, he was teaching his, Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh's teaching and Prophet's ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam knowledge, through his life for everybody. And hundred thousands, millions people, they benefit from this, al-ḥamdu liLlāh. So, it is big inheritor coming for Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim.

And one of this ṣuḥbah, I was child that time, Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh, he was saying ṣuḥbah. Many time he was repeating same story, or same thing what he say. It is important to hear it more than one time because people can forget. This is in Arabic name of human being – "insān". "Insān" coming from "nisyān", "nisyān" from forget. They forgetting. For this, Mawlānā Shaykh was maybe... Sometimes we can hear forty times, fifty times, same story. But, of course each time, there is a another tajallī – another manifestation. So, this is, was before Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim also, there is some idiot people when he was, before coming Cyprus, making many ṣuḥbah, and the ṣuḥbah it's very strong ṣuḥbah. But some people they said, "He repeat this many times." Repeat but if he repeat and it was benefit for you, you cannot say he repeat many times. You be have adab. You will be good behaved to say, "Yes, must be like this." But when you say, maybe he must be repeat it thousand time for you to understand. When you are not understand, he repeat and repeat and repeat.

So, this is ṣuḥbah wisdom. From each direction, from each, every side it is benefit for who listening. Even when you sit here and you listening, AllāhNone saying this place for knowledge, knowledge of AllāhNone, you all like student, ṭālibu l-‘ilm, and the wings of angel under your feet they said. For this, one of his ṣuḥbah, even I was child and then not really listening for ṣuḥbah, but this it was interesting. And I hear it many time, and so I remember some of this ṣuḥbah. The ṣuḥbah, it was very important about, he said, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. One night, heNone come out from hisNone blessed room, and calling, I think, Sayyidunā ‘Alī, "Quickly call all ṣaḥābah to come here," to masjid of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, big place to whole of them to gathering. "Something we must tell them."

And Sayyidunā ‘Alī he shout with other people also. So, whole ṣaḥābah was running quickly. Maybe something happen because it was dangerous that time because they want, non-believer they want to finish Islām. And this ṣaḥābah they come quickly for protect Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam and to what order coming from ProphetNone to do it. When all of them coming, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying to them, "Now Sayyidunā Jibrīl coming and this order he tell me to tell for people, not wait even for morning. Nighttime I must tell this for people. This is order: to not say anything you don't happy with this, happen before two hours. To forget. To not say it to anybody." Before two hours, you can say but after two hours don't say this to anybody. Because this become fitnah, become bad, bad thing, bad thinking, backbite and every bad thing coming from this. Because people, until two hour, they maybe they remember. After two hours, they put one, they put one, other put three, other put seven, other put... it become small thing – huge problem for people. For this, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam was ordering ṣaḥābah.

Ṣaḥābah they have to be obey hundred percent. But for us, we are weak, for us even it is good. This we must do it as much we can do. Because, this make people unhappy with each other. Because, "yesterday happened this", if you forget, other also will forget. After, before two weeks happen this, until two weeks and now it become more unreal thing as [if it is] real thing. Before, many people maybe they have something in their head for one year, two years, even years ago something happen, and they are unhappy with people, unhappy with what happened, and they imagine it is happen but normally it wasn't happen. For this, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam telling and ordering ṣaḥābah to not do this anymore. For this, for us, we also to obey Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. We must do as much we can do this. Don't make it bigger and bigger for making fitnah for other and for your society or for gathering. You must be careful for this. This is one, one of very rare ṣuḥbah I hardly remember from Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh. AllāhNone raise his maqām and bless you, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
