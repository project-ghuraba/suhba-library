As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says, “Min ḥusni islāmi l-mar’i, tarkuhu mā lā ya‘nīh.” What does this mean? How can a person, a Muslim, be a good Muslim? How does that happen? He should not interfere in matters that do not concern him. He mustn’t interfere. This is something that is necessary for all people, both Muslims and non-Muslims. Because when you interfere in matters that do not concern you... Even things you understand should not be interfered with. Even in the things you understand, some people will not like you interfering. Then, you will interfere and give ideas. You will confuse people’s minds. Your own mind will also be confused.

Therefore, it is good for a person not to interfere in matters that do not concern him. And it is also better for a Muslim, says our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. It is exactly the opposite in this time. Everyone will interfere in every matter. He knows everything. He knows politics, he knows economics, he knows business, he knows about studies. He knows how to sow, he knows how to reap. But he does not know anything. A person who knows everything, means he knows nothing. You have to know something so that, if they ask you about it, you can really do it. You will be useful. There are many people who jump into everything, and never do anything properly these days. It is not acceptable. It is not a good thing either. That is why the world is in chaos. People suppose they will make the world better themselves.

Some fools even want to interfere in the work of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. They reach such a level of stupidity by asking why HeNone created such people, why HeNone did things. That is why, a person, a Muslim, should mind his own business, mind his own situation, mind his own ego and train himself. It is not his duty to look at someone else’s. If he is given a duty, he must do it. If you are not given a duty, then you should mind yourself, your family, your children, your mother and father. Advise them. Show them the way. Intervene in their business; it is not a problem. But no, you will rule the world with things you have nothing to do with. Let alone the country, those claiming they’re clever are even trying to rule the world. After that, without succeeding in anything, without being able to do anything, people do not benefit from them, but rather they suffer. A useful person is a person who minds his own business, a person who does his job completely. These words of our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam are very precious jewels. He who holds on to them will be both comfortable and successful. He who does not hold on to them will be unsuccessful and, may AllāhNone protect us, will lose his īmān. May AllāhNone help us.

They have ruined the world with this thing called democracy. Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim used to say that all the time. Everything, everyone will say their own opinion. If everyone says their opinion, the valuable opinion will be lost amongst those opinions. Bad opinions will remain. And they are nothing but harm. May AllāhNone give people mind and understanding, in shā’a Llāh.

Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, “Min ḥusni islāmi l-mar’i, tarkuhu mā lā ya‘nīh.” What meaning? For to be good Muslim, to be good mu’min, believer, don't interfere with thing it is not, you not responsible for this. This Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying for mu’min to be, mu’min...

Of course for everyone, mu’min not mu’min, for believer not believer, it is good advice from Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam because heNone sent for whole universe, for whole creature. So even they are not believer, they can benefit from hisNone nice word, good word. But now, people, they are interfere in everything. What they know, what they don't know, they are objecting, they are saying, “This is wrong, this is right,” and this is not your business. You go and look for yourself. Don't make people, make you to laugh on you or to be angry for you, or to fight with you. All this, if you interfere and it will be that, no benefit from your interfering. Because nowadays people they are, everybody, they know everything. They are know everything and who know, said, “I know everything”, he don't know anything. They giving idea, they giving... Even they never do this work, but maybe they from their imagination they said, “It will be, must be like this, it will be good. And if you do that, don't, it will be very bad.” Who are you? We know you? No. Why you say this? “This, because I have, like to teach you.” But you saying wrong thing. What, from where you learn this? “From, I learn by myself.”

Once there was one story, one Bedouin, he taking goods from place to place. And he put one place, the good, this side, and other side he put sand to balance both side. And there was one man going through there and he say, “Why you do this? You need this sand because in desert?” Said, “No, just I make it to balance.” He said, “If you, so, take it out. Throw this sand, and put your thing half here, half here.” And he do like this. After he ask him, “Are you ‘ālim, scholar?” He said, “No.” “Are you teacher?” “No.” “Who are you?” “I don't... No, Nothing.”

“Why you make me, interfere in my work? Go away.” And he throw the, and put again. Because old people they were better than our people. They must... “If you are better than me, I will follow you. But if you are just giving idea, even it is right, but you are not ‘ālim or scholar or teacher, or any experience you have this thing, no need for you.” So, he put and put, he fill again sand, and he went like this. This people, many people they interfere. Even old time, not too much interfere. This time, what Mawlānā was saying, everybody, “I‘jābu kulli dhī ra’yin bi-ra’yih.” Everybody happy with his idea. “My opinion it is like this, my opinion like that.” Your opinion, leave for yourself. For this, not good to interfere with people. Just look for yourself, look for your family. You can interfere with children, with wife, with your family. Tell them, “This is good,” because you are the, in this house, you are boss in your house. For mother and father, also you can look for them, or help them or...

But for other people, if they not your business, don't say. Only for help, if they ask for help, you can say. But without this... Because many time what Mawlānā was saying also about this democracy. Nowadays, every time, one he has idea, democracy, must say his idea. For this, now world coming explosion everywhere, from this idea. Because they not let people to work. Who do something good, coming ten thousand against him. Everyone with his idea, and he cannot do anything. For this, not only for Muslim, for whole human being, good to be not interfere with other thing. Maybe you see something, you thought it is right but completely wrong thing, what you see or what you say.

AllāhNone keep us in good way, way of AllāhNone, to listen to good people, in shā‘a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
