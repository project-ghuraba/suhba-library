Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Our Holy Prophet ﷺ says in a Hadith:

“Fitnah is asleep. May Allah ﷻ curse whoever awakens it.” We are living in the end of times. There is a lot of Fitnah. Everywhere is as dark as night, because Fitnah is darkness and oppression. And whoever makes Fitnah will be cursed. Fitnah causes big harm to people. Fitnah is made in order to cause all kinds of evil to people. We are living in the end of times. They never leave people alone. If someone making Fitnah hopes to have benefit and comfort, he is cheating himself. Curse descends upon them and all their good deeds are erased. They will not have anything but the punishment of Allah ﷻ for their sins in Akhirah.

Whoever does goodness finds goodness. And whoever does evil, certainly, his end will be bad. He will end up badly. He will never have goodness. Do not believe in Fitnah. Do not be involved in Fitnah. If you are involved, you will be harmed. Do not listen to everything you are told. The world is covered with lies; lies and cheating. Fitnah is everywhere. If you believe in it and think you will have benefit, you will be at loss as well.

Before, there were newspapers where they would publish this. If there was wrong news, they would try to correct it even after years. And people would forget about it until then. But nowadays, there is not just a newspaper but thousands of bad tools of shaytan. If some villain foolish man says something, people believe it straight away as if it is the word of Allah ﷻ. And they share it everywhere. When you share it, you get no benefit but harm. There is not even one true word in a thousand words of those who speak, those who make Fitnah.

As we said, if you don’t want to be cursed, be careful regarding Fitnah. Don’t believe it and don’t share it. Their goals are the goals of shaytan. They are the soldiers of shaytan. Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla says, ‘ِ اللَّ لِ يبِ سَ نعَ كَ وُّلضِ ُي ضِ رْ َلْْا يِف نمَ رَ َثكْ َأ عْ طِ ُت نإِوَ ’, ‘And if you obey most of those upon the earth, they will mislead you from the way of Allah.’ (Qur’ān 06:116). If you believe the majority of people, they will alienate you from the way of Allah ﷻ.

Therefore, everyone is in stress and sadness at the present time. They don’t know what to do. Because they look at the tool in their hand and see so many things, so many lies. They see them as true and get upset. This is from the big Fitnahs of the end of times. Do not be involved in this Fitnah. Our advice is not to believe in it. Keep on your way and be with Allah ﷻ. As long as you are not involved with them, nothing will happen to you. But if you get involved, you will get your share of the curse. The curse is the worst thing. Allah ﷻ cursed shaytan, so he was thrown out of paradise. You may fall in the same situation, may Allah ﷻ protect us. May Allah ﷻ protect Islam and Muslims from Fitnah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
