A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Wa-man a‘raḍa ‘an dhikrī fa-inna lahu ma‘īshatan ḍanka" (20:124). Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is saying, "Whoever turns away from MeNone, his life will be hard, full of sufferings and unhappy."

What is dhikr of AllāhNone? To remember AllāhNone, to believe in AllāhNone. Because we are living in this time, it is the end of times now. All over the world people are in hardship. Governments don't know what to do. They have everything. Compared to in the past, they have a hundred thousand times more wealth, more opportunities, everything. But somehow they are not finding happiness. Why? Because they turned their faces away from AllāhNone. They don't believe in AllāhNone. And everything is in Allāh'sNone hands. HeNone gives everything so they come to their senses and return to AllāhNone. And when stubbornly they don't, they spend their lives having troubles. And they go to Ākhirah without anything in their hands. Not just empty-handed, but with their hands full of bad deeds. While it is bad for them here, it will be worse in the hereafter.

Therefore, be with AllāhNone so that you are in comfort. You will be in comfort here and hereafter. It is the promise of AllāhNone, the holy word, sacred word of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. "Who turns away and doesn't believe in MeNone, INone won't give happiness to him. INone won't give contentment." Happiness is happiness here and hereafter for those who believe in AllāhNone.

They give many promises now during the elections, "We will do this. We will do that. We will make you so happy. You will be so pleased." Nothing they promise happens. Moreover, as they don't believe in AllāhNone, AllāhNone doesn't help them. And people get cheated. They shouldn't. All of them are educated. Nearly half of them attended universities. Even the least useful job asks for a university diploma now. It means since you study that much, you have that much mind. If you understand that and don't understand to carry out the order of AllāhNone, you will be punished.

May AllāhNone give understanding to people. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying in Qur’ān: Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Wa-man a‘raḍa ‘an dhikrī fa-inna lahu ma‘īshatan ḍanka" (20:124). Meaning who is not looking for what mentioning AllāhNone or reminding [of] AllāhNone, AllāhNone will make his life terrible, unhappy, stressed, every bad thing. AllāhNone will be make "ma‘īshatan ḍanka" (20:124) – bad life, miserable life. This in Arabic translate, is – Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n have million of translate, but it is this meaning: every bad thing, every bad life what you [can] imagine in this world. Because why? Because they are denying AllāhNone and they looking for happiness what Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla HeNone promise. HeNone said, "Who will not ask from MeNone and not believe for MeNone, INone will give him miserable life."

We are living in time, the end of time, it is the worst time for human being. For humanity or human being, the worst time we are living. Why the worst time? Because old times they believe in something, they worship something. These people nowadays they not believe at anything at all. So Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla HeNone is promising and HeNone is doing. Not like politician, they promise too much and they will – "I will give this, I will make this for you, you will be happy." They promise for happiness, but no happiness. Even they not do anything for what they said. But even they do, they cannot give happiness. Happiness only Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla can give.

And old time people they have, this time people they have everything. Nothing can imagine they don't have. Every easy life they have, but AllāhNone when HeNone want something and HeNone said there is condition: for this to be happy, you must believe. If you not believe, you will be miserable life, bad life, unhappy life. Every badness you can think, you will have it. And no tablet can help you, no drug, no drink, no any this synthetic or non-synthetic drugs, nothing can make you happy. What you have... you must be happy... Who looking for happiness don't go after this [drugs], only go after AllāhNone. Look for your CreatorNone, and worship HimNone, ask for HisNone pleasure. If HeNone is pleased with you, you will be happy here and hereafter.

There is this people, nowadays people, they thought they are very clever. And we see everywhere you cannot find work if you don't have university certificate. They not accept you even for cleaning street. Now this, and they are saying, "We are very clever." It is easy. You can, must obey this. As much you are running away from AllāhNone, you are not clever. You are idiot! Because no place to run away from AllāhNone. This is, must think about this. AllāhNone give good thinking for people to find HimNone. This is what shayṭān cheat people who are proud – "We are clever, we are finish P-h, poo-h, something." What they have the highest study and they don't have any mind. AllāhNone give good thinking for people to find right way, to find way of happiness.

AllāhNone He don't want anything from you. AllāhNone want, HeNone is happy to when somebody ask forgiveness. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla very happy [with] this, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying. HeNone is not stingy, ḥāshā. AllāhNone is most generous. If everybody come to īmān, HeNone is happy, not saying, "We don't have place in Paradise." Paradise, very wide, very big place. AllāhNone make people to come to Paradise, in shā’a Llāh, to happiness, to believe in AllāhNone and Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
