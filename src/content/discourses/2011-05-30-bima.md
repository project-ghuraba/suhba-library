Mawlana Shaykh Mohamad Nazim al Haqqani an Nakshibendi 28 Jumādā ath-Thāniya, 1432 A.H. Tuesday, May 31, 2011 Translated from Arabic by: http://saltanat.org/ Audhu billahi minash shaytanir rajim BismillahirRahmanirRahim [ Meded ya Sultan Awliya Ya Sayidil awwalina wal akhirina. May Allah protect us from the deception of the devil. What you have mentioned is counted as the deception of the devil. Deception is not reality. These words they claim are false. They are not true, but corrupted. Allah Almighty, Glory to Him, said,

... Wa Wada`a Al-Mizana (Ar Rahman 55:7)

... and He placed the Balance. There is balance for the mind and a balance for the ego. There is a Divine Balance and a shaytanic balance. Allah (swt) said, "Wa Wada`a Al-Mizana" (55:7). Allah Almighty sent and placed the Balance for people to use. For what did Allah Almighty send that Balance? - for people to use it to distinguish between what is correct and what is wrong. The Balance of the Shariah was sent to the Prophet (sas); and the Prophet (sas) said: “What is permitted is made very clear and what is prohibited has been made very clear.” And, if a person is not able to distinguish between what is permitted and what is prohibited, that person is not a responsible person and is not held accountable. But how can a person who can distinguish between right and wrong, blame or load his sins on Allah Almighty? - and make Him responsible for them? Hasha (never)! This is not with the balance of Haqq. And Allah Almighty said,

Qul 'Audhu Birabbi An-Nasi, Maliki An-Nasi, Ilahi An-Nasi Min Sharri Al-Waswas Al-Khannasi

(An Naas 114:1-4)

I seek refuge in the Lord of mankind, the King of mankind, the God of mankind, from the evil of

the sneaking whisperer, who whispers in the hearts of mankind. We ask refuge in Allah Almighty from such deception. The person claiming this is talking on behalf of the devil and from it’s deception. The whisperers of shaytan speak on behalf of the devil. The person is 'free'; he may follow the way of the whisperers to use deception or he may walk on the right way, the way of truth. Is there right and wrong in this world, or not? There is sin and there is right action. Can a person distinguish this? Yes, he can distinguish. Then why is he smoking and saying, “Allah Almighty may take this desire away from me; but He is leaving me with it?” Is Allah Almighty saying, “I left my servant with the desire to smoke?” No. It is forbidden. This person is cheating, because a person is granted a partial will power, like a steering wheel in a car. If you do not use it, you can not then say, “I will not use my will power. I may leave this will power to do as I like.” That is not sound action. It is not right. These are deceitful whispers from shaytan. How can the person say this? That person may claim that he is free. If he is free, he may use his freedom as he wishes. But Allah Almighty did not leave His servant free. No! He loaded him with responsibility. The person who is not responsible is a person without a sound mind, and they are not held responsible. But a human being is responsible. Whoever claims such a thing chooses to be from the animal world. Animals do not have will power. We have a will power. If we do not have a will power, we are counted among the animals. Allah distinguished His servant with a mind and with will power. He has been granted both – a mind and will power. How can they claim such things? All of that is from shaytanic whispers. Thousands and thousands of books have been written about the deception of shaytan. Those who claim this are following the deception of the devil. They are leaving truth/Haqq and following the false/batil. If there is truth and false, and you can distinguish between them, how can you claim that Allah ordered you to do this? Allah ordered you to smoke? Or has He forbidden it for you? Did Allah make it halal to drink alcohol or did He forbid it? At the time you are saying this, you are arguing with Allah Almighty. You are going in opposition to His Divine Order. Such ideas are never accepted. How many mindless people are speaking? They are included in the Divine address in the holy verse,

Ulaika Kal anami Bal Hum Adallu (Al A'Raf 7:179)

This explanation is enough. “Go ahead and drink.” He won't drink. “Why are you not drinking? If everything is from Allah, show your submission - drink.” He won't drink the poison. “Why are you not drinking?” Finished! This may make him understand. Alhamdulillah to Allah Almighty Who made us understand. This is an important point. If I give him honey, he may drink quickly. But if I give him a glass of poison, he doesn't drink it. Why is he not drinking? Why is he not drinking and saying then, “Allah ordered me. Allah let me, and because of the Divine Will I am drinking. Oh Allah Almighty forgive us and make us understand. Oh Men of Allah, make us understand, because we can never understand. This is wrong and may Allah humiliate people with such claims. Many of the ignorant people come from such a door and they corrupt the belief of the people. May Allah humiliate them.
