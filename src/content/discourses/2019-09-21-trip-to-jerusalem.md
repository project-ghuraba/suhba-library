Waṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-F’āiz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-

İnshaAllah our murids will make this journey, and they should make their intentions (niyyah) that the journey be for the sake of Allah. With this intention, from the beginning of the trip until the end, for every minute of it, they receive reward. Where we are going is a difficult place, of course, and therefore one must be patient, you should do it all keeping in mind that this is all a test. Allah gives reward for this. Insha’Allah, we will go in ease and come back in ease. The important thing is to do this, not as a worldly trip, but as a trip for the sake of Allah.

There is a hadith of the Prophet (SAW), “A journey is made to three masjids: Kaaba, Masjid an-Nabawi, and Jerusalem (Quds). This journey is to Quds, and so if you stay patient in whatever happens, there is a reward for it. Even if you have an easy time, there there is reward for it, and it is a good deed, a provision. It is a trip that is not destined for everybody. It is a trip done only for Allah’s sake. You may advance, and it may also be a strength to your faith. May your station in the sight of Allah be raised, insha’Allah. Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-F ti ah
