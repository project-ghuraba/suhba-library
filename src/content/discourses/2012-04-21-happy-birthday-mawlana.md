One day we been in the plane with my father, 20 years ago. I was sitting next to him and he was all the time giving me his food to eat. After food finish, he was explaining the religion for me. Because I was sitting behind with one Buddhist man, and he was film producer and something like this. Yes, and from the Prophet's (saws) teaching, he was teaching.

So Subhanallah, that was when it was clear, and after that he was telling me, the religion been completed - because it was like crescent moon, and till coming to Islam, it is coming full moon. So he say it was completing with 124,000 prophets. It was like completing, completing, completing, & when it was coming to our Prophet Sayyidina Muhammad (saws), it becoming full moon. So that's why he say, when it is full moon.

Because some prophets, they been asking people, to be more spiritual. And other prophets, they been asking to be more materialistic. So when they go for spiritual side, they forgot dunya and what can be done in dunya, the material side. Subhanallah, after that, he say, our Prophet, Sayyidina Muhammad (saws), he make it full moon, so he ask us to balance between the spiritual side, and the material side. So when you are going to do that, he was telling me, 'dhu janahayn', that you have two wings. So either you can catch the spiritual side, and you can catch also, the material side.
