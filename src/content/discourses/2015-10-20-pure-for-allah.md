As-salamu alaikum wa rahmatullahi wa barakatuh. Audhu billahi min ash-shaytani r-rajim. Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. As-salatu wa s-salamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidi l-awwalina wa l-akhirin. Madad ya RasulAllah, madad ya Sadati Ashabi RasulAllah, madad ya Mashaykhina, dastur, madad ya Sheikh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheikh Muhammad Nazim al-Haqqani, madad. Tariqatuna s-sohbah, wa l-khayru fi jam'iyyah.

'ala lillahi d-dinu l-khalisu (39:3) Our religion is to worship Allah with pureness/sincerity. Our religion orders this. It shouldn't be for other benefit but purely for Allah. Your heart should be clean. There shouldn't be anything apart from orders of Allah, love of Allah and our Prophet, in your heart. Religion is this. This is what Allah orders. If you use religion, Allah, and the Prophet, for this world it is not a religion anymore. You will be doing something, which seems like it.

What Allah orders is to serve Allah, carry out Allah's commands and worship Allah with pureness without mixing it and adding any dirtiness. This is everyone's responsibility. If you lead other people, their responsibility also passes to you. Our Prophet says even if someone takes care of three people, their responsibility will pass to him. They should teach them pureness/ sincerity. They shouldn't add anything into religion to ensure that everyone has benefit in the end. If they mix it, their life will be messed up both here and hereafter. As we said, it is very important. There is a big responsibility. They should teach the truth and goodness to those who follow them. They should be cautious not to do wrong. If people let him go on with his mistakes, there will be a consequence for that too. If he shows goodness, it will be recorded for him.

It's not good to be happy to become a representative or Sheikh. They should think if they can carry this responsibility, carry the sins of these people. They shouldn't think of the benefit of this world but should think of the real benefit, which is of akhira. And that is the opposite of the benefit of dunya. If you think of receiving the benefit of this world, the real benefit, sawab of akhira will be gone. But if you want akhirah, you should leave the benefit and comfort of this world. You must think of only Allah's pleasure. 'ala lillahi d-dinu l-khalisu"(39:3) This is what Allah Azza wa Jalla says. Religion should be purely for pure Allah.

Ayat Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim 'ala lillahi d-dinu l-khalisu"(39:3) This is meaning the religion it must be pure. Pure for Allah Nothing mixing with this pureness. People, they are coming for Allah to find this they are looking for this. They are looking for who will teach them this good way. Pureness, everybody looking for pureness, not to mix with any benefit from dunya, especially from benefit from dunya. Because our ego they like dunya pleasure. Shaytan encouraging people to run after this pleasure of dunya, and forcing you. Many people, they have said 'Pray for us to not follow this dunya because it is too difficult to run away from dunya." We are running to dunya, to pleasure of dunya, but it is not good. The good thing [is] to follow Allah's order and Allah's way. This is making your heart to be pure and clean. And this is the religion. And Prophet (saws) saying for who are looking after people - if even one man he is emir for three people, he will be asked 'What [did] you do with these people?' You are make justice, you teach them good things and not showing the bad way, not following your ego?" And you will be asked this on Judgment Day, responsible for this. You will be responsible for this, even [if there are only] three people. So if more than three people, you will be asked more. More, and if you are governor, you must be asked for all of these people also.

Because what you done, not to be only to be in high position. It is [a] good thing for your ego in this world but for other world it will be difficult for you because you are responsible for all these people. If you do [with] your heart what you are doing mixing with benefits so it is bad for you. But even [if] you are in high position, or many people following you, and you are doing this only for the sake of Allah and you are not mixing what you done with any bad thing, you will be rewarded for this. So, it is not a good thing to get something and to lose many things.

The real benefit it is last, eternal benefit, eternal life. But it is opposite for benefit of this life. If you mix this benefit with your worshipping and cheating people and only for your benefit, [you are] using religion - the benefit of akhira, last day, eternal life it will be finished. But if you looking for benefit of eternal life and not looking for benefit in this life you will win. It is not easy, because many people they are also getting difficult from ego, because ego forcing people to follow easy thing. Every thing that is, to run away from this pleasure, it is difficult. But it is important, nothing you can get easily. If you get something easily, you must be careful. So, it is important to make pureness.

Especially who are looking after many people, they are more responsible, and they must be more careful to show people good things, good way. So after this, Allah He will be happy with them, Prophet will be happy with them, and Awliyaullah will be happy with them. And this is our main target. [If] they are happy with us, everything is OK. Nothing affects us. But if they are not happy with us, then you will be in trouble. Allah make us to follow [the] way of Prophet (saws) and to not be in way of shaytan.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfiq Al Fatiha
