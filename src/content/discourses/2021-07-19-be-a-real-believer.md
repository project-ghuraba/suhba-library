As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Labbayka Llāhumma labbayk. Labbayka lā sharīka laka labbayk. Inna l-ḥamda, wa n-niʻmata, laka wa-l-mulk, lā sharīka lak." This is Yawmu ‘Arafah. It is a holy day. Today millions of ḥajjīs make this takbīr and say labbayk on the mount of ‘Arafāt. They are overwhelmed with the manifestation of AllāhNone in that beautiful place until the evening in worship and obedience. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla made a day. Whoever is given that day will be present there. Whoever is not given, just like this year no one can have it. Whatever AllāhNone wishes, happens.

It is the first house of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla for people. The Ka‘bah is the first house of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. The house of the Ka‘bah was in heavens. Then it was brought down to Makkah for people. Al-Ḥajaru l-Aswad was shining like a jewel in Paradise as well. It was shining like the sun. However, when unbelievers touched it, that light disappeared. And it has got something. Whoever goes there, his record is inside it. Without modern recorders, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla records wherever HeNone likes. By the wisdom of AllāhNone, that holy stone records everyone who makes ṭawāf, comes close to it or can't, everyone who gives salām. On the Day of Judgment, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla will show it to people.

It is a holy day, Yawmu ‘Arafah. It is a very holy day both for those who are on ḥajj and those who are not. May AllāhNone give its barakah. May we be with īmān, with true īmān, in shā’a Llāh. There are many shayṭāns who try to steal īmān. Īmān is the most valuable thing people have. Thieves are always trying to steal what is precious. And it is the most precious thing. But Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla certainly helps those who obey HimNone and don't go against HimNone. We ask that our īmān increases from day to day in this world as well, until our last breath, in shā’a Llāh. Īmān is the greatest favour. One can be Muslim but there are many Muslims who don't have īmān. Whoever calls himself Muslim and doesn't believe in the miracles and the rank of our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam doesn't have īmān. To be Muslim is one thing and to have īmān is another thing. Īmān is to believe and to accept everything our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam brought to us. Who don't accept it are not believers. They can be Muslims but cannot have īmān. Therefore, īmān is the biggest gift. In shā’a Llāh with today's barakah our īmān will become much stronger. May we have more īmān, in shā’a Llāh. May it be good and blessed. May AllāhNone give better years for Muslims because there are many shayṭāns who want to cheat Muslims. But the truth will be the winner at the end.

Congratulations on this ‘Īd. May AllāhNone make our day blessed, in shā’a Llāh.

"Labbayka Llāhumma labbayk. Labbayka lā sharīka laka labbayk. Inna l-ḥamda, wa n-niʻmata, laka wa-l-mulk, lā sharīka lak." This is for what we are saying in ḥajj, especially this day, Day of ‘Arafah. It is one day for pilgrimage. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone chose one place, one day a year. This is real pilgrimage, which not like...

Many people, they said, "We are going for Rome for pilgrimage, we go to Spain, we go Argentina, we go many place." They said, "We go for pilgrimage." This is not real. Real one – only one place and one day. Not every time you can be pilgrimage. Even in Makkah, if you find this, you be in this day there, you will be Ḥajjī, pilgrimage. But if you go next day – finish. This is what happened once with Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. One of man he come to ḥajj and he said, "O Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, I missed. It was yesterday. I was thought it's today. What I can do to get this reward for ḥajj, for pilgrimage?" Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, heNone said, "Look this mountain behind you, if you give all this mountain with gold, it is not enough for, not equal for ḥajj for yesterday. Now you missed it. You cannot give anything. Only one day you can make this. For next year you can do this. But now this, if you give whole this mountain from gold, it is not acceptable like ḥajj."

So it is real. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla want people to know, to see how it is right, not like other. This heavenly order, heavenly thing. What you will do, what you will not do – all this instruction coming from Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. How to wear, how to behave – all this we have special thing, nothing in reward another thing like this. This is the real religion, real worshipping for everybody. For this we are asking, with blessed day, for our belief – īmān. It is very important to make AllāhNone our belief more stronger.

Many people they are Muslim but not believer. They don't have īmān – belief. Just saying, "Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, heNone is man like us. Ṣaḥābah – No. They are not different from us. All we are all equal." This is not belief. This Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla give everyone some state higher and some of them lower. We must respect and accept. And you respect and accept – you be believer, real believer – mu’min. If not, you okay, you are Muslim but you are not mu’min. This is – there is many difference between two of them, big difference, not a small difference. We are asking Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla to give us real belief, strong belief, in shā’a Llāh. This is biggest favour from AllāhNone for anybody.
