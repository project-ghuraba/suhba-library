As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla created everything for a reason, and there is wisdom in it. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla didn't create anything without a use. HeNone knows the wisdom. The wisdom is with HimNone. It is not like people of today. People of the present time do whatever comes into their heads. Everything has its time. It has its required time. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla didn't create anything without a meaning or wisdom. We see that man's opinions... Man's mind is very limited. He does a couple of things and wants to get rid of everything else. He doesn't keep them. He says, "Let's get rid of them. They are useless. They shouldn't take so much space for no reason. Let's throw them away. Let's replace them with things that we like." When it happens like this, the balance is disturbed.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla put everything in balance. Even though some things are bad, there is a need for them. You cannot question why they are there. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla created them. With the wisdom of AllāhNone, they are created for a reason. They might be created as goodness or as punishment. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla created both Paradise and Hell. You cannot ask why HeNone created Hell. Hell is a very bad place. May AllāhNone keep us away from it. But you cannot question why it was created. There is nothing worse than Hell but there is a need for it. AllāhNone created it accordingly. Therefore, when the time comes, we will have the benefit of everything, in shā’a Llāh. There is benefit for those who are with AllāhNone. And it will be punishment for those who are not with AllāhNone.

Everything is for the benefit of the believers, says our Holy ProphetNone. Therefore, we should be happy for good things and know there is wisdom in bad things and praise AllāhNone for both of them. May AllāhNone protect us, in shā’a Llāh.

Allah ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone create everything and everything there is wisdom to be created. You cannot say, "Why this bad thing AllāhNone create? Why they are in exist[ence] – this very bad thing, very bad stuff?" But it is AllāhNone wisdom. There is for each thing you don't like and you hate, also thousands of wisdom from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla to create him. Because we are have narrow thinking. What human being they know – very small thing. They want to make in their mind put everything in a shape and what it is out of this shape – destroy, finish, take them from exist[ence], take them out. "No need for any one of this." Even who are not following them, take them, this also command this people. No need for them.

But Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone create everything and HeNone know everything. You don't, we don't know anything. Even one – not drop, one atom from AllāhNone knowledge we don't have. Whole universe, they don't have. So it is everything we must thinking this is from our LordNone creation and HeNone is happy with this. We are happy with this. We follow HisNone order. HisNone order to kill something, to eat something, to not eat something. All this with HisNone permission. Without permission we cannot do anything. Sometimes you must do, sometimes you mustn't do. There is AllāhNone, HeNone put balance for world, for life of this planet.

But human being they nearly destroyed this balance. The everything they mixed up by their idea, by their thinking. Even they are not happy with the grass growing in field. "It is grass not good. We must put poison to finish." You can make by hand by take this grass but no, it's taking too much time. So we don't have this time, just put poison finish them. After what will happen? This poison will come to you and you will be ill because you don't understand wisdom of this grass and wisdom of creation of this grass. This is very small example but people, they are nonbeliever most of people, so they thought by themself, what they are thinking is the best. But who create this, HeNone know the best, create this for every benefit.

When you must clean this, you can clean by hand. You open for yourself, for other creature also, place. But when you do other thing, other creature also will not survive. After, coming to you your also will not – you maybe survive but with illness, with suffering from many thing. Subḥāna Llāh, we cannot say everything here. No time for say, for benefit. But Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla create all this, create whole this world for benefit of human being. But they are not respecting, are not thanking for CreatorNone of this world and what HeNone gave us. Only they thought they are knowing everything and they are destroying everything.

AllāhNone keep us safe with HimNone, in shā’a Llāh. To be thankful for HimNone, for everything HeNone create. And of course, with HisNone order, there is you can defend yourself, you can make good thing without destroying this system also. AllāhNone make us to do this because there is hypocrite from Western. They are said, I don't know – green or other – but they are all hypocrite. When just making show and their real purpose, real target for them to make people to forget Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, CreatorNone. They thought we are defending this but they are really, they are hypocrite. They are against everything because they are against CreatorNone. AllāhNone keep us safe from this people and their trick.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
