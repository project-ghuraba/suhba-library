Madad Ya Rijal Allah Audhu Billah min al shaytan al rajim Bismillah Al Rahman Al Rahim Allahu Akbar! Allahu Akbar! La Ilaha illa Allah, Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar Wa Lillahi Al Hamd.

O believers, we are servants of the Lord and we are Muslim believers, Al Hamdulillah. I am a weak servant, I ask the Sahib Al Waqt for support & safety. And we say, Audhu Billah min al shaytan al rajim Bismillah Al Rahman Al Rahim. La Ilaha illa Allah, Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar Wa Lillahi Al Hamd.

I am a weak servant. And we are reminding"Wa Dhakkir Fa'inna Adh-Dhikra Tanfa`u Al-Mu'minina"(51:55) These are the words of the Lord "Wa Dhakkir/warn". I am a weak servant. According to the request, request of the believers, we say, following the sayings of the Prophet (saws), "religion is advice" (Hadith). First of all, "take wisdom for yourself". Take wisdom yourself, because if you do you will benefit & who listens to this advice honour yourself. Allahu Akbar! Allahu Akbar Al Akbar! The Prophet (saws) says "Religion is advice." And from that we say "take wisdom". These are the words of the Prophet (saws). Take wisdom yourself & then advise others. If someone listens to you and accepts the advice. When you take wisdom yourself then you can advise people. This is the preface from a weak servant.

The tradition of the Prophet (saws) says "religion is advice." As he (saws) said, "Take wisdom yourself, then advise others." If you yourself take wisdom then advise people, otherwise that advice is useless, it is useless. The Prophet (saws) of the end of times, gave news about the past nations by order of Allah (jwa) in The Magnificent Quran. News about the coming nations, what will happen to them & he advised all humanity to advise others. As the Prophet (saws) said "Religion is advice."

There is a word that was inspired to my heart Subhana Allah! Our scholars must know it, they must know it surely. The human being cannot be a leader for another human being. Leader. Leading, lead... It is impossible for a human being to lead people by himself. It cannot be because the ego of the human being "Inna An-Nafsa La'ammaratun Bis-Su'i" (12:53). If the human being is not trained with a Heavenly training, he cannot be trusted. And the Prophet (saws) said "My Lord has given me the best of manners". This means that the human being must receive a good training about the Heavenly orders that came down from Heavens to the Prophets (as). And the Prophet (saws) of the end of times said "Addabani Rabbi Ah'sana Ta'dibi." And the human being if he asks for happiness in this world and to be honoured in the Hereafter, he must be trained by the manners of the Prophet (saws) of the end of times.

One word - the human being cannot lead other people. You are a human being & I am a human being. It is not possible for you to lead me relying on your egoistic desires. It is not possible. Unless you are granted manners from the Heavenly manners, the manners of the Prophet (saws) of the end of times. At that time then you can lead people, as did the Prophet (saws) of the end of times. And therefore the human being cannot lead any other human being. Leadership is in the hands of the Prophet (saws) of the end of times. And people must be led by the orders of the Prophet (saws) of the end of time. Once human beings left the the orders of the Prophet (saws) of the end of times, they failed and they lost. And as a result this failure makes them loose in this world & in the Hereafter.

O Egyptians, you claim that you are the most knowlegeable people in the Islamic Religion. Astonishing! If you are the ones most knowlegeable in Islamic matters, more than they were before & now, you must follow; you must know that a person cannot lead people. Leading people is from Heavens. If they lead people on earth by Heavenly directions, that is the victory. "Faqad Faza Fawzan `Aziman" (33:71) (& indeed achieved a great victory.) But they have lost & they were not successful. They turned to the worst people, the worse ones, the most ignorant ones. And therefore, O Egyptians, first thing that you must know since the time of the Prophet (saws) is that Muslim countries are lead from Heavens not from earth, but from Heavens. Leadership is granted from Heavens to the Kings that are under the orders of the Sultan of Heavens & Earths.

I see Egypt today, they do not understand anything and they think that with the elections, they will elect Allahu Akbar! Allahu Akbar Al Akbar! They left the orders of Heavens and went on another way, following their egoistic desires. And it is known that "the ones who follow their egos will perish." O Egyptians who claim to be knowlegeable scholars and who left the orders of the Lord & took as a leader someone else who depends on their desires. Therefore these people will never succeed. If 40 times they make elections, there will be no result - ever. There will be no goodness.

And pity for the people of Egypt who left the orders of Al Haqq (jwa) and took as a leader something else. And the great responsibility is on the candidate himself who claims "I am the one who can improve the situation of people more than the others." No, he is a great liar! Why did they leave the order of Allah (swt) when He says, Astaizzu Billah? O scholars, O Egyptians, Allah (jwa) says Haqq (swt). "Wa Man Lam Yahkum Bima Anzala Allahu Fa'ula'ika Humu Al-Kafiruna" (5:44) "Wa Man Lam Yahkum Bima Anzala Allahu Fa'ula'ika Humu Az-Zalimuna" (5:45) "Wa Man Lam Yahkum Bima Anzala Allahu Fa'ula'ika Humu Al-Fasiquna" (5:47) Munafiquna, Fasiquna, Zalimuna (hypocrites, trouble makers,oppressors) They are dressed (with the attributes) of these people who are running after them to elect them. Either they are non-believers, or disobedients or hypocrites.

How can a nation succeed? And they are denying the Holy verse & following other ways. Other ways, how can this be? And therefore I am informing them from now that they will not succeed in whatever they are doing because everything that they are doing is against the orders of Al Haqq, the orders of The Magnificient Quran and the Sunnah of the Prophet (saws). Let them argue, fight against each other. Because all of them who are elected are following their egoistic desires, and who follows his egoistic desires will perish. Therefore Allah (jwa) established the Sultans & appointed them and it was said in Hadith that "A Sultan is the shadow of Allah (swt) on earth." Sultans are certainly dignified & honoured. They are also backed by heavenly order. They are continuously giving priority to heavenly commands.

Those people who renounced (sultans) and followed the path of elections are the worst people, according to the Prophet (saws) who informed us that there will be a time when people will be led by the worst ones who will not succeed. Therefore, if a group of those leave, a similar type come with similar traits. So I'd like to give those people good tidings of their failure from the beginning to the end. People have become ignorant in following various different ways originating from Islam's enemies, from the ignorant Jews. And they followed them, so they will never be successful The Sultan is the shadow of Allah on Earth. He has to be one who rules people according to Allah's Command and according to Allah's Messenger's way/sunna They will prosper in this life and hereafter otherwise they will lose (their way).

This is why we are saying, men can never be ruled by a man they are at the same level, both sides are at the same level, no! For Muslims must be ruled by a Sultan, or the tyrants that Allah (swt) allowed to be used by shaytan to destroy the Tariqul Haqq/ The Way to the Truth. According to the Islamic religion, Divine Anger is on them. And we ask safety from Divine Anger.

O Muslims from East to West take a Sultan, the Sultans that act by Divine Order & Quranic Rules and by the Islamic Sharia. Then you will be successful. Otherwise you will be annihilated and cease to exist on earth & will never be successful. This is an advice, Religion is based on advice. If there is anything wrong with my words let them show it, point it out to us. The scholars of Egypt will be carrying the responsibility, a great responsibility in this world & in the Hereafter. In this world they will have no honour, never! And no Divine appreciation for them or from the side of the people. There must be a Sultan & the Prophet (saws) says "Give everyone their due rights". Give each one his rights. The rights of people can never be established from one of them. No it must come from Heavens. Allah Almighty is pointing in the Holy Quran "Ya Ma`shara Al-Jinni Wa Al-Insi Ini Astata`tum An Tanfudhu Min Aqtari As-Samawati Wa Al-'Ardi Fanfudhu La Tanfudhuna Illa Bisultanin" (55:33) If you understand, good. If not, Divine Anger is on you here & in the Hereafter. In the Hereafter you will have Divine punishment.

O Lord don't leave us to our egos, not for a blink of an eye or less than that. O our Lord forgive us & send us someone who can correct the situation of the nation otherwise humiliation & dishonour will continue till Judgement Day. We are asking forgiveness. And we are asking the Prophet (saws) to reach us with his intercession. For the sake of the one to whom was sent the Holy Quran, Fatiha.

This is drop from an ocean. More than this people cannot carry. So understand. O people of knowleadge, obey the Islamic orders and the rules of the Islamic Sharia or you will be under a heavy responsibility here & Hereafter. Astaghfirullah for me & for you. Astaghfirullah, Fatiha.

We started this talk that was inspired in me, that human beings cannot be ruled by one another, no! Human beings have an honour, therefore one person cannot rule another. Human beings, their leadership is in the hands of the Prophet (saws), the Master from beginning to end, Rasulullah (saws). Reach us O Prophet (saws). Fatiha.
