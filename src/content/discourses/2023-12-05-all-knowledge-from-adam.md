As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Wa-‘allama ādama l-asmā’a kullahā thumma ‘araḍahum ‘alá l-malā’ikah” (2:31). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘aẓīm. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla taught everything to Ādam ‘alayhi s-salām. The knowledge of human beings, the sons of Ādam ‘alayhi s-salām, until now comes from Ādam ‘alayhi s-salām. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla taught him everything. Through him comes all kinds of knowledge: spiritual knowledge, apparent knowledge and esoteric knowledge. Whatever comes to your mind of knowledge was taught by Ādam ‘alayhi s-salām: our father, the first prophet, the first human, Ādam ‘alayhi s-salām. From mathematics to the names of the stars – all the kinds of knowledge we have now.

They think that human beings learned these by chance, that they learned them by themselves. There is no such thing at all. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gave everything its appointed time. At that time, fundamental knowledge was given from Ādam ‘alayhi s-salām to people. And as time goes by, another door of knowledge opens. People learn other things. And after some time, different knowledge comes, up to our time as we see it now. This knowledge has no end, in the Presence of AllāhNone. There is no end to knowledge. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gave this knowledge for us.

To give an example, human beings burn in fire and drown in water. But there are other creatures. Jinn were created from fire; nothing happens to them. Those that live in the sea are different, and those that live in the air are different. There is no end to the number of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla's creatures. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is always creating. There is no such thing as: “HeNone is just creating us and not creating other things.” But people [ask], “Are there any creatures in space?” Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla has everything of course. There are, and when permission is given, they may appear. When there is no permission, there is a border and a limit. They cannot pass from one place to another.

That’s why here, as we said, knowledge is with AllāhNone. The source of knowledge is Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gives people some small part of it. And they think they are something important after that. Beneficial knowledge is knowledge that lets us know Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Knowledge is that which tells us about the Essence of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HisNone Power and HisNone Magnificence. This is knowledge. Others don’t know what they are doing. If they do something, they declare, “It is mine.” They make judgments, telling people, “Do this, do that.” This is not knowledge. This is something that confuses people.

Therefore, knowledge came from the prophets. All kinds of knowledge. Whatever comes to your mind: weaving, agriculture, farming, medicine. Everything came from the prophets. From the first prophet Ādam ‘alayhi s-salām, to Shīth, then Idrīs ‘alayhi s-salām, and so on. They taught people this knowledge. As we said, it is limited. It is something that opens slowly.

Because we are in the end of times, it has opened all at once. In the past, a person learned a few things in a thousand years. Nowadays, knowledge changes in a year. And that is the wisdom of AllāhNone, because it is the end of times. The end of the world is coming. This knowledge must also come out. It is reserved especially for this time. May AllāhNone, in shā’a Llāh, not divert us. May HeNone not give us knowledge that misleads us. May HeNone grant true knowledge to all of us, in shā’a Llāh.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, in Qur’ān ‘aẓīmu sh-sha’n, saying: Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Wa-‘allama ādama l-asmā’a kullahā” (2:31). Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla taught Ādam all, everything in this world. And then HeNone, because angels they was not happy with... they hear about human being, they are not like angel. Angel they are only worshipping and they are peaceful, obeying order of AllāhNone, and only doing this. But when – because they know what will happen – they said it will be with this Ādam ‘alayhi s-salām, his generation, it will be bad people. So they was not so happy.

For this, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla taught Ādam ‘alayhi s-salām knowledge and the names, everything’s name and knowledge. From knowledge, whole knowledge AllāhNone taught Ādam ‘alayhi s-salām. And HeNone then, when They ask for angel, “What is this?” They don’t know, and they said. Ādam ‘alayhi s-salām; he taught them also. They was very happy for this. So they are not anymore objecting anything for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. What Ādam ‘alayhi s-salām learn from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla? HeNone taught him everything, every knowledge we know. From smallest knowledge to biggest knowledge, he was knowing: about mathematics, about building, about calculating, about sun, about the years, about months, about small, big – everything.

Ādam ‘alayhi s-salām was knowing, and he taught his children for this. So not like what they are saying, this, who they claiming they have knowledge. Ādam ‘alayhi s-salām was knowing everything. And he teach children of Ādam ‘alayhi s-salām everything. Not: they are not knowing anything and suddenly, slowly, slowly, they learn this and that, and to walk, to run, to wear clothes, to make medicine. All this, Ādam ‘alayhi s-salām taught. After him, his sons and grandsons was also teaching people about this. And this was for benefit of people.

And everything Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla... HeNone give time. For everything, there is time. Slowly, slowly becoming, every years, become more knowledge, more thing they learn. But the important thing, the knowledge it was from prophets coming, not from ordinary people. They are giving knowledge for spiritual, and for material also. Everything. Even they taught for stars, and... everything you can imagine for knowledge, they was teaching people.

Of course that time, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, with HisNone wisdom, every time there is something – new knowledge coming, new, coming – thousands of years passing, until coming to our time. Our time we are, because we are living near Qiyāmah, AllāhNone open whole – not whole, of course. This is not one drop from ocean, this what we seeing. But for knowledge of people, they thought it is, there no more knowledge than this. No, there is. But for this time, AllāhNone open much more than this thousands of years. Maybe in twenty years it was more than ten thousand, twenty thousand year of knowledge. It was more. Why? Because we are coming to end of time.

And all this must be appear for people, to make them, testing them. Because many people, with this knowledge they are becoming unbeliever, not believer. Very few of them they are believer, because what they cheat them, shayṭān. “This knowledge, look this whole knowledge, and this religion people they don’t know anything.” They thought by their cleverness from they are knowing this. They are not accepting from AllāhNone. But who, this is big test for them and maybe they are not believe in anything. But who believe for this knowledge coming from AllāhNone, he become more believer, more afraid from AllāhNone to not testing him, to keep him in way of AllāhNone. Not coming out of way, going to destroy himself.

Who not have belief, they are destroyed people. Nothing, no any hope for them. They are destroying themselves. They are destroying their happiness, destroying every good thing in themself. Belief it is most important. And this knowledge of AllāhNone guiding people to be believer. But shayṭān cheat them and make them to be unbeliever. AllāhNone help us. Keep us in HisNone way. Give us more knowledge, in shā’a Llāh, from real knowledge.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
