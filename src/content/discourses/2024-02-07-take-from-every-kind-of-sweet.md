As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, today is the twenty-seventh of Rajab, a holy day. Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam advised us to revive its night and to fast its day. It is not farḍ (obligatory). But our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam shows us goodness and good things in order for us to benefit. HeNone shows us beneficial things. Do them, and be closer to Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Leave that [forbidden] thing. It will increase your rank [if you do]. Pay attention to your prayers. Aside from prayers, nafl (voluntary) worship is worship that makes you closer to AllāhNone. Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says to do it. Of course, even when you do it, it is important to have good manners. A generous person is better than a stingy worshipper. AllāhNone loves generous ones.

Therefore, don't say, "I am praying and doing these things. I don't need anything else." There is great benefit in generosity as well. There is charity and helping others, which bring more reward than voluntary prayers. You should help other people. Doing various good deeds is sometimes better than voluntary worship. It is more acceptable and brings more reward. But you must do both. Do not only worship and think there is no need to do good deeds. There is a need. The more goodness you do, the more you will get in the next life, ākhirah. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla has mentioned this everywhere in Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n. Whoever does goodness will get even the particles of goodness in ākhirah. And those who do evil will certainly be punished, unless they repent and ask for forgiveness.

Thanks to AllāhNone, HeNone showed us the value of these beautiful days. HeNone showed us, shukr to AllāhNone. We wouldn't know if HeNone didn't show us. Therefore, we must give thanks. Don't think we perform this worship by ourselves. Without the help and support of AllāhNone, you would do exactly what others do. And do not ever see yourselves as superior to others. Never think of yourselves as superior to others in this world.

A priest came to Sayyidunā Junayd al-Baghdādī one day. He asked him, "Are you better than me, or am I better?" Junayd al-Baghdādī replied, "Come back next week, and I will answer you." He came back the next week. But Junayd al-Baghdādī had died. He told his companion, "I had a question for him that he didn't answer. How will I get the answer now? He promised me. Let me ask once again." He asked, and Junayd al-Baghdādī replied from the hereafter, "I am better than you." "Why? You didn't say this last week." "I was alive. How would I know how my last breath would be? Would I go with faith, or would I go in unbelief? If I went in unbelief, I would be worse than you. You would be superior then, and I would be low. But thanks to AllāhNone, I passed away with faith. Therefore, I am superior." He didn't tell the priest he was superior when he was alive because he was a believer and prayed. Only when he was secure, when he breathed his last breath and passed away to the next life with faith, did he say he was superior. When he heard that, the priest became Muslim.

May AllāhNone help all of us. Don't look for superiority. May AllāhNone give guidance to others. May AllāhNone protect us from evil and from their fitnah (trouble). We must say this, too.

So... and even big awliyā’ [such as] he is, they are giving lesson for us. Don't say, I am doing this and do that and I am better than this, better than that. This is very dangerous. All time, you must thank for AllāhNone. HeNone give you, to keep you in this way and be happy with worshipping, to be happy with every good thing you do.
