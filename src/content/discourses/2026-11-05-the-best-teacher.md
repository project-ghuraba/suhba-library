As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Prophet ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam said in ḥadīth sharīf,
إِنَّمَا الْعِلْمُ بِالتَّعَلُّمِ، وَإِنَّمَا الْحِلْمُ بِالتَّحَلُّمِ
“Innamā l-ʿilmu bi-t-taʿallumi, wa-innamā l-ḥilmu bi-t-taḥallum”
“Knowledge is only by learning, and patience is only by exerting oneself to be patient.” [Bukhārī]

You cannot learn by yourself. To learn, somebody must teach you.

Prophet ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam is the best teacher for us. What he taught us is a big lesson, the best lesson for all humanity.

After 1400 years, they talk about human rights and animal rights. All this, Prophet ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam was teaching people. That time wasn’t like this time. There were people living like a stick. And it’s difficult to tell a stick, “Do this, don’t do that.” Prophet ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam was the teacher alḥamdulillāh. He taught from the knowledge of Allah.

What human beings are in need of, what’s for the happiness of human beings: Prophet ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam taught that. Now they are struggling, not following Prophet ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam and they think they can be better. They made many things, and everything they’d done didn’t succeed and even led to disasters. They made socialist, capitalist, humanist. All this is rubbish. Because they don’t have heavenly teaching.

So everything is of less and less benefit. Especially these days, they’re telling everybody that they can do what they like. If you say anything against this, they come against you. They are teaching people bad things not good things.

For this, this knowledge is not good knowledge. The knowledge of Allah is benefit. Many people study for business, for work, for job. You can study that but don’t think it is good knowledge. You must know good from bad.

Good is what Prophet ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam taught us. Bad knowledge is what he didn’t like. This is a very small ṣuḥbah that everybody must take care of, inshā’Allah.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
