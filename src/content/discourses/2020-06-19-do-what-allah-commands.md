Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

A’ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm:

(Qur’ān 02:286). ‘Allah does not charge a soul except [with that within] its capacity.’ Allah Azza wa Jalla says that He does not give anyone a burden that he cannot bear. So people can do all the worship and obedience that are assigned to them. Allah Azza wa Jalla did not command us to do anything that we cannot do.

There are some people who see themselves as Shaykh or show themselves as such. They command what Allah ﷻ does not command. People cannot do what they command, because they themselves do not even know. They come out with something and say “Let’s do this”; thinking that after they do it, everything will be alright. Do they make Wuḍūʾ(ablution)? No. Do they pray? No. Do they fast? No. They say, “We make Dua, we make Tasbihat, these will make our souls reach to Allah ﷻ.” We are saying this as an example. These things are of no use. They will be a burden on people for nothing. Because they are not doing the worships that Allah ﷻ has ordered or any other orders, but they are doing the things that are for their own benefit and that attract people. Shaytan makes them look easy and beautiful, and makes Allah’s ﷻ orders difficult.

A person adapts to such useless things easily and does it easily, thinking that he has done something. Yet he is wasting his life, wasting his dunyā, wasting his ākhirah, and would be living in vain. Therefore, may we do what Allah ﷻ commands insha’Allah. Others should also pay attention to this. They should think, “Why are we not doing what Allah ﷻ has ordered, but we are doing what we do not know and do not understand?” Anyways, the person who says these things does not understand anything, and those who follow him are also in ignorance and heresy. May Allah ﷻ protect us from such people, or our efforts will be wasted in vain.

What is easy is what Allah ﷻ commands, what is in accordance with human nature, what people can do comfortably. They say “We cannot do it”, but they do much more than what Allah ﷻ ordered, and it is useless. May Allah ﷻ not make us do things that will be in vain insha’Allah. May we be with him at all times, every minute and every moment so that our lives do not go to waste. May our dunyā not go in vain, and may we win our ākhirah insha’Allah. For the sake of the blessed time of Friday insha’Allah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
