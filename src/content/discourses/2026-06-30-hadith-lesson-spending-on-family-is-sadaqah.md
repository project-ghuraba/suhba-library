As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

1- Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Spend first on yourself giving yourself sadaqa; if anything is left over give it to your family.” To your own family. First and foremost, to yourself. And then, so that no one will be in need, to your family. “If anything is left over when they have received something, give it to your relatives; and if anything is left over when they have received something, do thus and thus.” So you start with those close to you, you start with yourself. Then the closest: your family, your relative, then your neighbor. If you have friends or relatives, you should give them so that they do not need anyone else.

2- Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Start giving first to your dependents.” So you are responsible for taking care of your family. You do not look after them, you look after people for whom you are not responsible at all. No, that’s wrong. You must see to the needs of your family first. After that you can look after others.

3- Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

Our Holy Prophet is saying “When Allah prospers any of you he should spend first on himself and his family.” When Allah gives you a blessing, to be thankful for it because it comes from Allah, you should give to yourself first, make shukr, then give your family, then your relatives, and so on.

4- Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

Our Holy Prophet is saying “When a Muslim spends on his family seeking reward for it from Allah, it counts for him as sadaqa.” If you give them even though you are responsible for them, it is also considered sadaqah. Since you give for the sake of Allah, your reward is from Allah.

5- Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

Our Holy Prophet says, “If any of you is poor, he should begin from himself.” So, this matter of Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam is very important. So that you won’t be dependent on anyone; when you have money, you should take care of your own needs first. You shouldn’t ask for anything from others. Asking (begging) is not good. Therefore, he says, “When Allah gives you, start by taking care of your own needs.” One must start with himself. “If anything is left over, give it to your family,” you must look after your family. “If anything is left over, give it to your relatives; if anything is left over (when they received something), then here and here.” So you can give it to this and that then.

6- Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “The reward of sadaqah given to relatives doubles.” So it is twice your normal sadaqah. If you take care of your relatives and family, you will attain double the reward.

7- Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “The first thing that will be put on the scale of a servant,” The first thing that will be put on the scales on the day of judgment is “what one has spent upon his family.” The person will receive the reward of what he spent in good deeds first on the Day of Judgment in the hereafter.

8- Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “The most excellent dinar,” dinar is gold of money. The money of old times was called dinar. There was dinar and dirham. Dinar was the highest in value at that time - “a man spends is one he spends on his family,” So, the most valuable and virtuous in the sight of Allah is the money spent on one’s family. “One which he spends on his animal in Allah’s path,” The expense paid on taking care of an animal that was used for jihad or travel on the way of Allah is great. “And one he spends on his companions in Allah’s path.” So this is the best money spent, says our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam.

9- Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Every expenditure you make for your family with the intention of pleasing Allah is sadaqah.” Don’t say, “It goes in vain. I am obligated to provide for my family.” You are obligated, but Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla rewards you twofold for it.

As we said, don’t forget to give for the sake of Allah. When giving, you should do it with the intention being: for the pleasure of Allah; this is my family’s rizq that Allah has given, and that is halal rizq and it will be a means for goodness.

10- Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “What you feed your wife is sadaqah is for you.” This means that if the meal is eaten at home, and your wife eats, it is considered a sadaqah. “What you feed your child is sadaqah is for you.” Feeding your child is also a sadaqah. “What you feed your servant is sadaqah for you. And what you feed yourself is sadaqah for you.” This means that even feeding yourself from your own food is considered a sadaqah for you.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla’s generosity is boundless. He gives everything, provides food and drink. And on top of that, a person earns reward. This is the goodness of a believer, the goodness of īmān. Someone without īmān: the animal earns more than him. It at least thanks Allah for what it eats.

11- Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Of a dinar you spend as a contribution in Allah’s path,” again, as we said, dinar is the most valuable currency. Dinar’s normally gold. You gave a dinar in the way of Allah for the pleasure of Allah, “Or to set free a slave,” So if you gave dinars to free a Muslim slave from slavery and freed him, this also has a great reward. Those who have been freed from slavery and have gained their freedom for the sake of Allah: this also has a great reward. “Or as sadaqa given to a poor man,” If you gave a poor man money and dinars, and took care of his family and took care of him for a long time. “Or in support of your family,” so, a dinar spent on your family. Which is the most virtuous and most rewarded, says our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam: “The one spent in support of your family produces the greatest reward.” So, the greatest of these four things, that is - you gave to the way of Allah, you saved one from slavery, you gave to the poor - the most virtuous is the money, the dinar you spend on your family, says our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam.

The Messenger of Allah says the truth in what he said or as he said.
