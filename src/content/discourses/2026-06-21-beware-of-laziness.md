As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
وَقُلِ اعْمَلُوا فَسَيَرَى اللَّهُ عَمَلَكُمْ
<br>“Wa-quli ʿmalū fa-sa-yarā llāhu ʿamalakum”<br>
“And say, 'Do!', for Allah will see your deeds.”(Qur’ān 09:105). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says, “Do not be idle.” Work, and Allah will see what you work. Laziness is an undesirable trait. The prophets, mashāyikh, and companions were constantly in service. They worked continuously to serve people and Muslims. That is, both to earn their livelihood and to serve Islām.

Therefore, there is no place for laziness. Laziness is not in their nature. The work of the prophets, companions, and awliyā is continuous service. Throughout their lives, they serve as much as they can; they want to benefit people even after they are gone.

There are many examples, many scholars. In the past, people lived for a maximum of forty or fifty years. However, the service they rendered in those forty or fifty years of life, a normal person could not accomplish in two hundred years. So how is this possible? Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla bestowed karāmah upon them and ensured that people were served through them.

Therefore, laziness is not in their nature. A lazy person benefits neither himself nor others. And when a person is idle, doing nothing, shayṭān attacks him even more. Through trickery, he says, “What will you do? Do this, do that,” and leads him into sin. If a person works, at least shayṭān doesn’t have the opportunity to do so.

Therefore, in another āyah, it is said,<br>
فَإِذَا فَرَغْتَ فَٱنصَبْ وَإِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ فَٱرْغَب
<br>“Fa-idhā faraghta fa-nṣab, wa ilā Rabbika fa-rghab”<br>
“So when you have finished (from your task), then continue striving, and to your Lord turn your attention.” (Qur’ān 94:07-08).

“When you finish one task, immediately begin another.” This means, to do this whether it is prayer or worldly affairs.

Anyways, all of them, if done for the sake of Alla , will be recorded as reward for you. If the things you strive for are for the sake of Allah, with the intention of, “I will earn my livelihood, and I will help people,” then all of these will be recorded as good deeds and rewards for you. May Allah not make us among the lazy. Because wasting life is isrāf (wasteful), and Allah does not love isrāf.

May Allah give us all strength and not make us playthings of shayṭān, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
