By the name of Allāh Almighty all Merciful, most Beneficent and most Munificent.

Now we are saying: Nawaytu l-khalwat. Nawytu l-riyāḍat. Nawaytu l-‘uzlat, nawaytu s-sulūk, nawaytu l-i‘tikāf, nawytu l-arba‘īn. Always Grand Shaykh, when he was coming to masjid, he was intending as long as he is in mosque to be in i‘tikāf. If we are collecting whole meanings it should be seclusion or to imprese. Imprese? To be, to imprison our egos our nafs for worshipping for the Lord Almighty Allāh. I‘tikāf it is same meaning. ‘Uzlat same meaning. Riyāḍat same meaning. Khalwat same meaning. Because all of them means that a person, a servant, imprisons himself for the Lord's worshipping. And here we are in seclusion. We are in... for the Lord Almighy Allāh we are intending to make a service for HimselfNone, for Allāh Almighty. We are intending to make pleased our LordNone by being through His mosques.

Mosque belongs to AllāhNone. If a person coming to mosque he is or she is visitor for Allāh Almighty. If a person asking, "I like to visit my Lord Almighty Allāh." HeNone is that one everywhere. You can't find any place without AllāhNone. HeNone is everywhere. HeNone is with everyone from creatures. And creatures, particularly man, looking after his Lord around himself or over himself or under himself or through East, through Far East or through Far West. Men they are heedless – never understanding that Allāh Almighty always with ourselves. Allāh Almighty HeNone is with His servants, as surrounding him from outside or to be inside with him.

It is important for everyone, particularly through these difficult days, must try to be for Allāh Almighty, not to be for himself. Who is going to be with his Lord Almighty Allāh, he is never going to be alone. You may sit alone but, even that one's feelings denying him, and saying you are with your Lord. But we must try to improve, through our spiritual life, for understanding that we are with our Lord Almighty Allāh. And main target from our associations, as our Grand Shaykh teaching us, and saying – to make you and to make everyone to feel that he is or she is with his Lord or with her Lord. We are practicing for feeling that we are with our Lord Almighty Allāh. If you can't do that you must try to be with your Prophet peace be upon him. You are with himNone. If you can't be able to do that also you must try to feel that your Shaykh with you. And you must try to be with him. Because they are with us. Allāh Almighty with His servants. And Sayyidinā Muḥammad peace be upon him heNone also with his ummah. And our Grand Shaykh with his murīds forever. And as much as you may be in connection from the last one from your Shaykh or someone that Shaykh making him as a permitted person, you must follow him. And it is easy to think through our hearts a Grand Shaykh. And when Mahdī comes, AllāhNone blessings him, when Mahdī coming then you should know him and he is going to know everyone. No one going to be a strange man, strange woman for Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām. Only looking to him, he should be with the Lord Almighty Allāh and he should clean you by saying your name. In one moment, Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām has a miraculous power. He is not in need to ask what is your name. And you are saying my name is Ḥasan or Ḥusayn – it is enough for him to speak your name. It is going to be a reason for cleanliness and purity. Therefore, now we are looking after Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām to come because he should give whole nations happiness and pleasure.

May AllāhNone forgive us and accepting to be His real servants and to be real followers for His beloved Muḥammad, peace be upon him, and to keep the respect of inheritors of Prophet, peace be upon him and upon them. And we are in need to look after our situation once again to make it more clear for worshipping. And Allāh Almighty protecting His servants here and hereafter Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq.
