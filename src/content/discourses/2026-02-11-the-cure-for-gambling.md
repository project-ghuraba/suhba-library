As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
وَتَعَاوَنُوا۟ عَلَى ٱلْبِرِّ وَٱلتَّقْوَىٰ ۖ وَلَا تَعَاوَنُوا۟ عَلَى ٱلْإِثْمِ وَٱلْعُدْوَٰنِ
<br>“Wa ta‘āwanū ‘ala l-birri wa t-taqwā wa la ta‘āwanū ‘ala l-ithmī wa l-‘udwān”<br>
“And cooperate in righteousness and piety, but do not cooperate in sin and aggression.” (Qur’ān 05:02). ṢadaqAllahu l-‘Aẓīm.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says, “Help in goodness. Do not help in evil.” Never help in evil! He says. What does this mean? Some people come and say, “Please make duʿā. Our brother, son, husband, etc., are gambling.” What is the cure for gambling? Unfortunately, there is no cure for gambling. May Allah protect everyone from it. Once it’s ingrained, there’s no escape. But the cure is not giving them money. They say, “They’re crying, whining, begging insistently.” You won’t give them. No matter what they do, you mustn’t give them the money. Because you are helping them commit a sin, you are losing your own money, and you are causing them to sin. This is a great evil. They have ruined the Islamic world with this gambling issue. It’s popping up everywhere. Inside the house, outside the house. Shayṭān has become bizarre in the end of times.

Therefore, as we said, the cure is not giving them money, because when you give them money, you become a partner in that sin, an accomplice. You are helping them commit that sin. Then you defile clean money. Dirty money is circulating. From one person to another, it’s becoming ḥarām and evil everywhere. This is an important matter. “Sometimes it hurts.” Don’t feel hurt. You should pity those who sin. Don’t make them sin. You mustn’t feel pity then make them sin more. You must be careful about this. It won’t work only with duʿā or so. It is such an affliction - may Allah protect us. It’s worse than anything else. People can repent from everything else, but once gambling afflicts you, it leaves you with nothing – no wealth, no possessions, no home; nothing remains.

Therefore, be careful. As we said, this is more dangerous than anything else. So, watch out. Don’t help those who do this. As we said, duʿā might work one in a billion times, but normally, there’s no cure for gambling. 

That’s the cure for it:<br>
وَلَا تُؤْتُوا۟ ٱلسُّفَهَآءَ أَمْوَٰلَكُمُ
<br>“Wa lā tu’tū s-sufahā’a amwālakum”<br>
“And do not give the foolish your wealth” (Qur’ān 04:05). Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says. ṢadaqAllahu l-‘Aẓīm.

Do not give your money to the safīh (foolish). They are safīh. Safīh means they are unreliable; you cannot vouch for anything they do! Therefore, do not disobey Allah’s command! Obey Allah’s command. Help them avoid sin, inshā’Allah.

May Allah save them. May Allah keep us away from being afflicted by it.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
