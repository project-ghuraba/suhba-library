As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

AllāhNone, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla does whatever HeNone wants. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is Most Capable of everything. Most people don't like their lives, they don't like their situations. This is a normal condition for common people, ordinary people because they don’t have much īmān, they don’t have anything, they don't have much thought. That's why they object to everything, they don't like anything. They don't like most things. There are some people who understand a little more about religion and īmān. They say, “I want to end kufr (disbelief). I will eliminate kufr.” There are many people, including Muslims, who say they will not leave anything or anyone behind. They say, “If it were up to me, I would do this.”

These issues are the will of AllāhNone. AllāhNone will certainly send someone whenever HeNone wants. HeNone does like this. This one will do it with the permission of AllāhNone. The promise of AllāhNone is true. Kufr will disappear. There will be nothing left of kufr. There will be no oppression. All this will be gone. But the current situation for the people of ṭarīqah is to be content with Allāh’sNone ruling, to accept the things AllāhNone has given, and not to object to them. We don’t accept anything as, “We judge Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla’s knowledge.” Never can the knowledge and wisdom of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla be questioned.

In old times, there were many awliyā’ and scholars. They would appear more often. Now they do not appear, they are hidden. That’s why people without īmān, Muslims but without īmān, have multiplied. These people make judgments according to their own minds, saying, “We will do this, we will do that.” In old times, as we said, there were great scholars and great murshids. One of them was Muslihuddin Efendi, a great scholar. When he completed his knowledge, his outward knowledge, he sought true knowledge. He came to a great shaykh [asking] for this knowledge. He is a great shaykh called Sümbül Efendi. A scholar living at the time of the Ottomans, he loved the Shaykh because his high knowledge and abilities were clear.

One day, the Shaykh gathered his students. “As a lesson for you today: what would you do if you were to rule this world?” This is what he asked everyone. “Think about this. Then, I will ask each of you for your opinions and thoughts. Let me see what your states are, and which of you is the best.” This is what he said he will ask them. They all thought until Maghrib. They came to the Shaykh’s presence one by one. “O Shaykh, I will remove this evil. I will erase these. I will do like this to them, I will do like that to them.” They all gave their opinions until Maghrib. Then it was Muslihuddin Efendi’s turn. He said, "What would you do? How would you fix this world? What would you do if it was yours?" He said, “If it was mine, I would leave everyone and everything in their merkez.” What he meant by ‘merkez’ is that he would leave everything in its place. “This is what Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla has willed. I cannot desire anything other than HisNone will,” he said. So his name became Merkez Efendi. People have been visiting him ever since. He has great knowledge. He has been of great guidance in the knowledge of healing and in the external and esoteric knowledge. He trained students. He raised beautiful people. People visit him until today. They also hear his wisdom more or less. Again, people study as they know; that is another matter.

This is a big lesson for Muslims and believers: that AllāhNone created everything in the most beautiful manner. Everything happens as HeNone wants. Nothing happens outside HisNone will. He told this wisdom, a very beautiful wisdom. The person who accepts it and does it, is comfortable. He has no worries, no headaches. He is pleased because this is what AllāhNone wants. May AllāhNone make us from those servants, in shā’a Llāh.

There is... What Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla HeNone want – everything in this world as HeNone like. HeNone create everything in perfect, in perfection. From Ādam, ‘alayhi s-salām, until now there is many thing happening – every time different thing, good thing, not good thing. Of course, this all with will of Allāh ’Azza wa-Jalla. Nothing out of this will. When we see something, of course if it is not good we will not be accept. But this we must know, this by will of AllāhNone happen. Nothing outside of HisNone will, can be. Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying; "If you see good thing you be happy, not good you can also be not happy. If not good you don't be happy." This is other subject but what we are, our subject it is – everything, nothing can be without will of Allāh ’Azza wa-Jalla. Whole universe, not only this small earth, in whole billions of billions of billions. We cannot count, no limit for AllāhNone Will, AllāhNone Mightiness.

So for this there was old time also one shaykh. Old time there was awliyā’u Llāh and ‘ulamā’ and scholars and other. There was people coming, they know this from awliyā’u Llāh, beloved one for AllāhNone. When somebody studying, they finish the highest study of sharī‘ah or anything else, they look for another more and more because no limit for knowledge. So knowledge of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam coming through heart, through mashāyikh. So one of this big awliyā’u Llāh living in Istanbul, his name Sümbül Efendi. This Shaykh and one of biggest scholar, he was named Muslihuddin. He living in another town and he hear about this great awliyā’ and he want to be study with him. And he study, he was, he left everything and come and he was with the Shaykh and Shaykh has many murīd also. Not only one, many murīd they was coming, taking knowledge from Shaykh, taking wisdom, taking good thing and going for other people to give this knowledge for other people. Shaykh sometimes they give them like try to explain, to know how they are, how to lead them to know themself, what they have got from knowledge. Once he said, collect all the student and tell them, "Today your duty to look until Maghrib. I will give you something. You look everyone what he think about this." What it is? "This if you are ruling this world, what you will do to be for your idea for this? How you can, what you will do? So this for you for think and I will ask each one what they want." So this people they went. They are... I think now there is election in Turkey. Everybody [saying]: "You know how I can be get better, I can make this world better than now?" Everybody, everyone he think, "I will do this, I will prevent this, I will take this, I will give this." Until Maghrib they are speaking with Shaykh. One by one they speak and the time come for this Muslihuddin Efendi. He said, "Well, now what you will do if you ruling this world?" He said, "I will leave everything in centre, merkez." Centre, merkez – 'same' meaning. Nothing change. "Because Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone is, HeNone know best." And he think what HisNone wisdom. "Same. I will leave it like this. No change, nothing. I will not change anything from this." Because he is Allāh'sNone will.

So Shaykh was very happy with him and even give, make him son-in-law also. So this is wisdom what Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, everything. We are living time, really not so nice time, but this will of AllāhNone. It must be happen. Don't say, "If this coming, if this not done this, it will be like this, or if its not like that." Don't tire yourself. Just stay quiet and look for yourself and look for what will happen, in shā’a Llāh. This is important thing for us, for ṭarīqah people, Ṣūfī people, Muslim people, Mu’min people. This is what they must – accept what AllāhNone they gave them. Keep themself, keep their belief for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. This is the most important thing. AllāhNone help us to make it, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
