Dastur Ya Sultanu l-Anbiya Madad Madad Ya Sultanu l-Awliya Madad ya Sadatina l-Kiram. Audhu billahi min ash-shaytani r-rajim Bismillahi r-rahmani r-rahim. La hawla wa la quwwata illa billahi l-'aliyyi l-'adhim.

We lay a foundation -a building is raised on a foundation. We can't live in a building without a foundation. What is the foundation? It is kalima shahadah - "Ash-hadu an la ilaha ill Allah wa sh-hadu anna Muhammadan 'abduhu wa rasuluhu Sall Allahu ta'ala alayhi wa sallam."

If we say this holy phrase 100 times, 1000 times, 100 thousand times we won't be tired or be weary. It gives more soundness to our belief. It gives strength and firmness. This world is a world of troubles. This world is a house of trouble and difficulty. They say "la rahata fi d-dunya" There is no ease in this world. Now let's see what they make us say from this source. This is an important point. There is no ease in this world. There is difficulty. There are troubles. So, how is it possible to live and succeed in this world of troubles and difficulty? Let's have a look. Don't look at who is talking, listen to the one, who makes us talk.

We are all in this world now, not in the afterlife. Is there anyone saying "There are no troubles or difficulty in this world"? Step forward. "la'anallahul kadhibin" Cursing comes on liars. Nobody can say that this world is easy. It is a world of troubles, difficulty and worries. We are living in it. We didn't choose to come. We can't choose to go. We have to complete this life here. When the appointed time comes, we leave this world. There is difficulty in everything. Nothing without difficulty.

So how can we face the turmoil of difficulty and troubles? How can we bear them without being devastated? We must know this. Like we said, when we construct a building, the foundation should be solid so that the building will be solid. Unless we build our life on a spiritual foundation, the building won't be solid. Therefore spirituality is essential for a foundation. Once we build it, we can carry all the troubles of this worldly life.

Who carries the heaviest burdens? Anbiya - prophets because the prophets stood up against the ignorant people directly. Those wild ones, who worship their egos, they targeted them. Therefore no one else can carry the burdens that the prophets carried. How did the prophets carry them? Ash-haddu l-bala 'ala l-anbiya thumma l-awliya thumma l- amthal fa l-amthal The severest troubles and difficulties of the world were laid on the prophets, and then the awliya, and then the believers (mu'minin). They carry the burdens of this world according to their degrees of belief. What our Prophet (saw) was carrying, no other prophet could tolerate. How did he (sas) carry it? Without spiritual power this body collapses. It grieves and can't bear it. Nobody can carry the burdens, difficulties that the companions carried. Within 24 hours, sometimes they found something to eat and sometimes they didn't. What they were bearing in the deserts of Hijaz? We go to Hijaz temporarily especially in hotter seasons we get weaker. We think of ending the journey, going back, taking a breath of fresh air in a cooler place. Eating and drink as we like.
