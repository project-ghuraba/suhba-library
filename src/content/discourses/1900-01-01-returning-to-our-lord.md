Audhu billahi min ash-shaitani rajeem Madad ya Sayyidi La hawla wa la quwwata illa billahi l-Aliyyu l-Azeem

Almighty, All-Merciful, the Glorious Lord of the Worlds, Lord of the Universes, Lord of sons of Adam, Most Beneficent, and Most Munificent. We are asking His forgiveness. He has endless Forgiveness Oceans. Therefore we are saying: Alhamdulillahi Rabbi l-'Alameen. We are giving our whole thanks and whole praisings to our Lord who has endless Forgiveness Oceans. We are giving our endless, unlimited thanks and praisings to our Lord who has endless Favour Oceans and we're hoping from His endless Mercy Oceans, from His endless Mercies to forgive us.

We are not teachers but our Lord, He is teaching us. We are sitting here not as a teacher teaching students in schools but all of us sitting and waiting, from Divinely Presence [for] something so that we can learn about our Lord Almighty, and may learn from Holy Quran which begins with Suratu l-Fatiha. Everything going to be open with Suratu l-Fatiha. Opening means, every closed doors may open with Fatiha, as universes from pre-eternity up to eternal opening with Suratu l-Fatiha.

The first surah that Allah Almighty recited by His Divinely Words, it is not such, our words that we are reciting Suratu l-Fatiha. No. With His Divinely reciting, He recited Suratu l-Fatiha, the Opening for everything. An opening from hidden treasures, an opening with Suratu l-Fatiha. When He open, only He knows. No any other can know. And in that opening, we were with our Lord. We were in His Power Oceans and we were in endless Divinely Mercy Oceans.

You can't imagine an ocean without fishes, without creatures inside. When oceans, where was creatures? Was there, in it. You are saying 'oceans', you are not saying fishes. This is 'fishes', you can say? No. But it is ocean. And in oceans there are so many creatures. If we can say, one thousand creatures: one on lands, nine hundred ninety-nine in oceans. So many. But we are saying only 'oceans'. Not saying that [from] so many creatures we may say, everything living in oceans, they are, let us say 'fishes'. No, it is wrong. But in oceans endless creatures living in spite of we are saying oceans.

Therefore, our Lord's Mercy Oceans, He has endless Mercy Oceans. No one can know or can imagine what kind of universes or worlds living or being in existing in our Lord's endless Divinely Mercy Oceans. No one can know. And His endless Mercy Oceans were in existence when our Lord was in existence in pre-eternity. Therefore, endless worlds, endless universes, they are swimming, they are in existence in those endless Divinely Oceans, Mercy Oceans or so many endless worlds and universes in existence in our Lord's Divinely Power Oceans.

Therefore, our Lord giving His attributes when He ordered His people for thanking and giving their whole praisings to one who, He is Allah, then going to give a description from His Essence, from His Absolute Essence. Giving something, some attributes from His existence and describing Himself saying "I am the Lord of Universes Rabbu l-'Alameen." Who can say how much His universes, as our Lord is unlimited. No limit for His existence, so for His attributes no limit. Absolute. It means no one can say any limit.

In Absolute Power Oceans of our Lord, endless universes swimming. As much as He creating. Creating means, making them to appear but in real, they are in oceans, endless oceans. When He giving them from His Light, they're appearing. If no sun, nothing may be seen on Earth. Sun shining, then everything you may see. And Allah Almighty, from His Divinely Lights giving, then they are coming to appear. When that light going away, they are going to be hidden in endless Mercy Oceans.

This is a very important thing to be known in our time. We must know something about our Glorious Lord, our Creator. He may bring everything in existence and He may take them away. When He is giving from His Divinely Lights, appearing so many universes. If taking away from them, then they are disappeared. Where they went, do you think? So many people just lived in this city, in London. Perhaps, more than 1000 years or 2000 years. Where they went? So many people disappeared.

Once they appeared and after a short time they are going to be disappear. To where they are going? They are going from where they are coming. But both sides, both sides closed. We can't see, veiled. Yes, barrier. We are in it. We may try to look what is there, what is there. He says two oceans going to beat each other. But none of them jumping to other, passing to other. Pre-eternity and post-eternity. And we are in middle now. From where coming? From pre-eternity. Where you are going? To post-eternity. It is only a spark. Finish.

Sometimes such a fish appearing from sea, there is a flying fishes coming. That we are now seeing in this life, from same ocean. Cutting a distance, short distance, then we are finding ourselves in ocean. It is very happy, that fish when flying like this but quickly finding himself in ocean. Happy and fearful also, "Who making me to fly? How I came out from my ocean?" And finding himself quickly in ocean. We also now, our souls asking His Oceans. How we finding in this life? We were in oceans, swimming. No fear, no troubles, nothing else. No burdens, pain. Enter hidden oceans. That is the meaning: "Inna li'llahi wa inna ilaihi rajiun" (2:156) Surely we come from Allah and surely to Him we shall return.

We are coming, from where coming? We are coming from Allah Almighty, from His endless Mercy Oceans and we are returning to His endless Oceans. Mostly, this is good tidings for sons of Adam to know. And this verse just sent to the Seal of Prophets, Muhammad, peace be upon him, sallAllahu alaihi wa salam. Never sent to other prophets.

If in Old Testament Allah Almighty saying also about Joseph, Prophet Joseph and his what saying 'sergüzeşt' (narrative) 'macera' (story/adventure). What happened to himself? His history. Jacob, peace be upon him, when his son Joseph has been taken away by his brothers. Too much sorrow, crying. He only said that:

"Fasabrun jameel" (12:18) Patience is beautiful.

If that verse which sent to Prophet Muhammad, peace be upon him, that 'we are from Allah coming and we are returning to our Lord', with that pleasure it should be so big pleasure to himself that never going to cry anymore. That it is mostly good tidings for sons of Adam. For every heavy burden that carrying, if you are remembering this, that we are returning to a Divinely Presence, we are returning to our Lord's Divinely Presence, no more sorry or sorrow may be in our hearts. Quickly going away.

This is so big, good tiding for everyone. When every burden comes on us we may think, "But after this, after all, we are returning". Endless, brightened future for us. "We are going, to where going?" We are going to our Lord. That is the greatest, good tidings for us. Everything may be for us in this life, but doesn't matter because finally, we are returning to our Lord. And no one can imagine what is in Divine Presence. Astaidhubillah:

"Fala t'alamu nafsun ma ukhfiya lahum min qurrati 'ayunin" (32:17) And, [as for such believers] no human being can imagine what blissful delights, as yet hidden, await them [in the life to come] as a reward for all that they did.

Saying Allah Almighty, no one can know. It is impossible to know even prophets, even saints, even angels, what they may find in Divine Presence for them from His endless favours. Therefore, be pleased with your Lord. Enjoy yourself. May be every trouble coming on people, coming on you, but don't cut your pleasure. Enjoy yourself because we are going to our Lord's Divine Presence. You can't imagine, never eyes see it, never ears heard about, or never our hearts or our minds imagined what is in Divine Presence, which kind of pleasure we may find in Divine Presence. No one can expect. Impossible. If like this, what is this? Be patient and say, "Alhamdulillahi Rabbi l-'Alameen". Therefore He is ordering firstly and endly, finally, say thanks, endless our thanks. Whole praisings to our Lord that He is going to give us from His endless favours. He is the Lord of Universes. And we are speaking to you on the existence of universes.
