As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Fa-man ya‘mal mithqāla dharratin khayran yarah. Wa-man ya‘mal mithqāla dharratin sharran yarah" (99:7-8). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘aẓīm. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says in this beautiful verse of the Qur’ān: no matter how much goodness a person does, he will see its goodness and reward, says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Even if it is just one small deed, it doesn’t end either. It will certainly reach you, says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. And the one who commits evil will find their punishment. Whether it is a little or a lot, he will surely find his punishment. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla also showed us the way to be saved from punishment. If you repent while you are alive, while you still have breath, that repentance is acceptable in the sight of AllāhNone. But after the soul leaves the body, when you can’t breathe or do anything at all, at that time you will suffer punishment.

Therefore, thinking about what you did while you are in this world, which sin you committed, or all of them... as our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam said, everyday say a hundred times, seventy times astaghfiru Llāh. “Even I say it,” says our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. HeNone has no sin. Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam has nothing [of mistakes]. Even so, heNone says seventy times astaghfiru Llāh. Even for us, if you say everyday seventy times astaghfiru Llāh, a hundred times astaghfiru Llāh or more, you will be clean from sins. You will be free from this burden. Because, as Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says in Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n, if it is left for the hereafter, even if it is as small as an atom, you will see its punishment. If it is a good deed, it is also not lost. Even if it is as small as an atom, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla will give its reward as well. HeNone won’t leave it like this.

Therefore, people are in heedlessness in this world. People are in heedlessness and misguidance. They have no mind and they engage in useless and dangerous works. Do not throw yourself in danger, says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. All people nowadays are in rebellion, as if it is ingenuity. They go against Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. They deny AllāhNone. They go against the religion. They leave the religion. As if it is a skill, they act cool, thinking they are something important. They do such things that – Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says if they do not repent, may AllāhNone protect us – they will stay in hell forever. Therefore, to leave prayer, supplication or other things, there is still an exit for that. But if these people make mistakes, enter kufr or say they left religion, AllāhNone will surely make them see the punishment for what they did. May AllāhNone protect us all, in shā’a Llāh.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying this verses Qur’ān, who make little bit good thing, even very, like atom, like very small thing, AllāhNone will reward him for it. He will see the reward for what he done in his life. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla not leaving anything without reward. In dunyā, people, they are running after benefit for dunyā. And they, when they find something better, quickly they are changing to that side. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla for this, for everything, every action you do, AllāhNone reward you, even very small. And when you do bad thing also, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone will see what you done and HeNone will punish you for very small thing even.

But for this, very easy way to save yourself from punishment. You must repent and asking for forgiveness. Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying also. Prophets they are – whole prophets – they don't have any sin. They are protected, from AllāhNone, ma‘ṣūm. Only prophets, they are protected from to do sin. After them, everybody can be doing sin: small, big. But for Prophets, there is no sin. They are very protected, very pure. Prophets, especially Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, saying, "I am every day making seventy times asking for forgiveness, saying astaghfiru Llāh, astaghfiru Llāh, astaghfiru Llāha l-‘Aẓīm. So you do also say," is order from Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Order for us, because heNone love his nation, his ummah. Every time heNone thinking about them.

So he, when they asking forgiveness, small, big, what they done, AllāhNone forgive. But if not and it is, it will be, forgiveness... Asking forgiveness, it's when you are alive, you taking breath, and you are thinking. Until end you can ask for forgiveness. AllāhNone forgive you. But when this finish, your breath finish, it is no hope. This, after this – finish. So after this, in Day of Judgment, they will look. And very small thing you will be charged. You will be, punishment you take from this, if you are not asking for forgiveness in your life.

And nowadays people, they are... They thought they are very clever people. "We are thinking, we are seeing, this: no religion. No anything what you saying. We not accept this." So, for this people, they are poor people, headless people, idiot people. They are, they will be charged and punished for what they done, because they are very proud, not asking for forgiveness from AllāhNone. So for this, even very small thing, they will ask what they done for this in their life. And it will be more punishment for them.

For this we are, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, asking whole time for forgiveness and from – Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying in Qur’ān, "Yubaddilu Llāhu sayyi’ātihim ḥasanāt" (25:70). When you ask forgiveness, your sins become reward for you. AllāhNone change it and put in your reward. Writing in reward, in you. You... Day of Judgment you will be see many thing you didn't done. But this, because you ask forgiveness, AllāhNone change your sins to reward.

AllāhNone give people good thinking because the worst thing, when somebody, idiot, thinking himself he is clever. And doing thing, and people also following him. They say, "This one clever, so he is know something." But the most clever people, the most perfect people: AllāhNone create them, they are prophets. They are completely perfect. Nowadays they saying, for perfect man, perfect woman, they asking. You must see, look for this holy people. Which now of course, no more from them. After Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, they are most perfect, more, most good people, most, every good thing in themselves. They are this: they are intelligent, cleverness, every good thing you can find them. This is good people. This is clever people. You must follow them, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone make us with them, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
