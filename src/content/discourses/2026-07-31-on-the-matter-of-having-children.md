As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
إِنَّ رَبَّكَ هُوَ الْخَلَّاقُ الْعَلِيمُ
<br>“Inna Rabbaka Huwa l-Khallāqu l-ʿAlīm”<br>
“Verily, your Lord is the All-Knowing Creator.” (Qurʼān 15:86). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla is The Creator. He is the All-Knowing. He is the One Who does what He wants; Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla.

Therefore, every human being, every living creature in this world, is created with the permission and will of Allah. Some people question, “Why did You create me?” You were created because Allah willed it. You came to this world not by your own choice, but by the will of Allah.

Some people say, “We have so many children, let’s not have any more, let’s avoid it,” and then they find themselves having children. There’s no need to get angry or upset; it means that Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla wanted that soul to come into the world. No matter how much you hide it or not want it, that person will come into the world, be born, and grow up. Whether it will be good or bad is also Allah’s decree. But what is to come will surely come; no one can prevent a soul that Allah will create. No one can prevent its coming into the world.

Because the īmān of people today has weakened considerably, to the point of being below zero. Therefore, when a situation arises, they panic, saying, “What will we do? How will we take care of this child?” However, the true Provider, Ar-Razzāq is Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. The sustenance of the one who comes into the world, when they will be born, and when they will die, all depend on the will of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. Don’t tire your mind about it. Rely on Allah.

If something is going to happen, it will happen; and if not, that too is Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla’s decree. But if something does happen, we must submit to it. We should thank Allah and make duʿā that it is good for us and not bad.

May Allah protect us. Now there are many bad people. The majority of people in the world are bad and evil people.

Therefore, we must make duʿā that the one who comes into the world will be good. That they will be a true servant of Allah, and one of those whom Allah loves; that is what we must make duʿā for.

Otherwise, there’s no need to wonder, “Will they study? What will their future be?” What’s important is that they are good people and loved by Allah; that’s what matters.

Now, people are all the same; as in one mold. They all insist on studying, they all do the same things. The three, four things they study are all the same. And even that they learn by force. If everything could be achieved simply by studying, then no one could live.

Everything is by the will of Allah; whatever sustenance a servant receives, that’s what is written in the sight of Allah. We live with that sustenance, we manage in this world that way. What’s important, as we said, is to be good people.

May Allah make us among those good people. May all our descendants and all the children of Muslims be good people, inshā’Allāh. May He deliver us all from badness, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq.

Al-Fātiḥah.

There are recited Qur’ān Khatms, ayāt, surahs, salawat, tasbihat, tahlilāt, takbirāt, khatms of Dalā’il Al-Khayrāt.

May Allah accept their good deeds. To the soul of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, for the souls of his Ahlu l-Bayt and ṣaḥābah. For the souls of all prophets, Awliyā’, asfiya, mashāyikh. For the souls of our ancestors.

For goodness to come and the evil to be warded off. For happiness here and hereafter.

Li-Llahi Ta’ala, Al-Fātiḥah.
