Because you know O Salafi ulamas, milk is clean. Just clean. But if you are putting that clean milk in a pot that is not clean quickly changing from cleanliness to dirtiness. You must throw it away. Therefore, heavenly grants are not granted to everyone. Those grants only are given to those people whose hearts are clean. How is our heart's cleanliness? Allah Subhanahu wa Ta`ala, jalla Jalajuhu jallat Azamatuhu. He is choosing the hearts of people. As you know also Salafi ulamas, but you are never speaking. I know.

The Lord of Heavens, Allah Almighty is saying: Bismillahi'r Rahmani'r Rahim, ma wasi`ani ardi wa la sama'i wa lakin wasi`ani qalbi abdi al mu'min. Allah Almighty is saying that for all creation on Earth or on heavens, it is so difficult to carry Me. They are such simple aspects that I can't be there. I am endlessly a Great One. I am The Greatest, no one can be greater than Me. Therefore, earth and heavens is not enough base or station for Me. No, but I just granted to human beings a heart, qalb. What you are calling a heart is like a fist. Outwardly you are seeing this, that is representing it but the real heart that I granted to My deputies, no one is knowing about that. Can't be.

Therefore O People, O ulamas you must try to reach levels of holy knowledge that prophets were bringing. You can't reach that sacred and secret knowledge on Earth. Show to people, teach people, don't say, bid`a, haram, kufr, shirk. Leave them. And show people where they are standing and where they have been invited to. And show the ladder, how we shall go up. All that we are saying to people about this life has no value. No value.

May Allah forgive us. We are trying, they are granting to our attenders something to taste from heavenly beings, because people now are in darkness, everywhere, there is oppression.
