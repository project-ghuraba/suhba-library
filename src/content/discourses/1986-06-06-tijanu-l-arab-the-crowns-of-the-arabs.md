So many men are not using and the men who do wear the turban have become fewer. Many are hostile to the turban and I am asking, Hoja Effendi, the men in the heavens, are their heads uncovered? Do they go with their heads uncovered or do they all wear the crown of Islam? "Al amaim tijanu l-arab," a holy hadith. The most Wahabi of the Wahabis won't read this. They put something on their heads like this or like that, that's how they do it.

It is forbidden for a man to look like a woman. What they use is what women use as headwear. That's why when the Companions wore this they wore it with a turban-cloth, they didn't wear it without a turban-cloth. They are wearing a cap and over it they are putting something called “hatta”. That's how they do it, like that, it hangs like the women's one. They are coming to the mihrab, you can't come to the mimbar like that. It is haram.

The crown of Islam, especially the Prophet (sws) directed it to the Arabs, "tijanul arab", the Crown of the Arab. "Amaim" turban cloth, called amame. Isn't it my hoja? Yes. In this country the law is forbidding it. Did you see any in Arabia with a turban cloth? Ten agents attack them from behind, just oppressing them. These are sufis, mutasawwufs, shaykhs or murids. It's none of your business if they are shaykhs or mutasawwuf!

Isn't it in the Sharia? Did the Prophet go without a turban? Did Prophet alaihi s-salatu wa s-salam come to the mihrab without a turban? They put a robe on and come to the mimbar. They are not afraid of Allah nor ashamed of the Prophet. "Tijanul arab," he specifically directed it towards the Arabs, and in Hijaz there is no turban and they frown upon the ones with a turban.

Just as they are without turban, so also with the beard. The Europeans have also a distasteful beard. It doesn't look like a beard, like a priest's or something. The command of the Prophet, his holy command, a hadith from Bukhari and Muslim: tawfiru al-liha wa ihfu ash-shawarib. Tawfiru, leave longer, don't cut and trim your beard, meaning to have a full beard. Whereas some beards are growing to their stomach, the Prophet's, alaihi s-salatu wa s-salam, in keeping with his nature his beard was a handgrip long, he did not grow it longer.

In sallallahu alaihi wa sallam's natural form his holy beard was as long as a handgrip, that's why when the Companions' beards grew longer, they were looking at the Prophet (sws) and trimming it. But now we are cutting and trimming and when you hold it from here they say this is a handgrip. Yahu! From here you can't see any part of it. What kind of handgrip is that? Yes. To grow a beard is sunnah, an important sunnah, without a doubt. Khal'a kulliha - by shaving your beard with a razor you take responsibility, as the Hanafi sholars used to respect it, but because of a public outcry they can't say anything. They are also shaving the moustache, not only the beard. That's why, may Allah help us and forgive us, (M: Hazrat Shah Naqshband,) may Allah sanctify his secret, (M: I presume he was beardless) his holy beard was huge, like this, more full. Such was the majesty of Hazrat Shah Naqshband.

As is seen now but actually from the description given by my Sheikh, his beard was so strong, because the beards of the Uzbek people are strong. Such was the majesty of Hazrat Shah Naqshband, as of course must be of those sheikhs with a keen effort for keeping the Prophet's sunnah, because he whose appearance isn't in line with it also will be wrong inwardly. May Allah forgive us.

Now in this clock, everything is complete, if the minute hand was missing, could you see what time it is? If there was a minute hand but not the hour hand, even with all being complete inside, would it have any use? That's why if something is missing outwardly from the sunnah then he has no authority. May Allah forgive us, wa l-afuw wa yafu an kathir.

A holy verse: wa y`afu an kathir (42:30) meaning, He forgives most of them. And I say that, the few also are “la hukmalah,” the few are not counted. When He (swt) forgives most of our sins, would He ask about a few, Hoja Effendi? Is it wrong? Won't Allah forgive most of them? Right. How about a few? So the few won't be asked about, but also we will be saved from the most. May Haqq deliver us. They will go to Paradise, that's how it is.

Keep the ways of Paradise open. The Hoja Effendis are too strict; closing, yahu, we can't enter then. You are narrowing too much. The Hojas are threatening, they are saying you won't pass. You also won't find your way in. Keep the door wide so that we too may pass. Isn't it? While there is a heaven would he go to hell? All to heaven. Insha Allah we all will get in line. Now I'm sorry, while the Prophet (sws) is on one side of heaven, would we go to another side?

We will line up behind. Would our Prophet get in and leave us? Hasha. Whatever we did, hasha min al huzur. We still will be walking behind the Prophet. “O My beloved (sws), I will judge your Nation, I won't show you,” says Almighty Haqq, “you won't know about their judgement, I will pass judgement on them because you also are human. If you knew what they did, you would be hurt. I am Allah,” Jalla wa Aala says, “It doesn't bother me, it is nothing”. Hayy, Hayy, Allahu Akbar. Why be afraid after you say this? “Wa yafu an kathir,” He will forgive most. The few don't count, directly to Paradise. By nuqulu l-awwalin.
