As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Fa-bi-dhālika fa-l-yafraḥū huwa khayrun mimmā yajma‘ūn" (10:58). How do people find happiness and joy? People nowadays are looking for happiness. They say, "I am unhappy. How can I become happy?" Everyone keeps thinking about it. They go to doctors, they go to hospitals and ask this and that. People should not be happy because of others. They should be happy because of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla and because of what they do.

How do people become happy? They become happy when they do something good, when they carry out an order of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, when they make ṣalawāt to our Holy ProphetNone. Happiness comes with these things, not with other things. Sometimes they say, "Take this pill. It will make you happy." You take it but it is not enough. You take two or three more, and then you become addicted to pills. Moreover, you may become addicted to even worse things later. And then you won't be happy. Especially when you continue to look for happiness, you will drown more, you will have more sadness. And sometimes their ending is so bad that they commit suicide. Because they can't find happiness, they just leave.

Whereas Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla has given jewels to people. The jewel inside you is a valuable jewel. If you discover this and get to know it, if you appreciate it, certainly you won't end up in such a condition and sadness. But people don't do this. Instead, they listen to what others say – "This and that will be good for you." They listen to this one and that one, and they don't do what is right. You need to work on the jewel inside you and not outside. You should work on the jewel inside you and do your best for it to help you. It can't happen otherwise. It can't happen through friends. You must be with good people. You must be with people on the way of AllāhNone. When you are with people beloved by AllāhNone, it brings happiness. Otherwise, being with things or people, or whatever bad things that Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla doesn't like, in order to be happy, won't bring any happiness.

As we said, when saying, "It is not enough," so getting more and more, people will get much worse. May AllāhNone protect us. This is from the troubles of the current days. People of old times didn't have so many problems. All their problems had to do with work at home and work outside. They used to get tired physically and then they forgot everything else. But nowadays, because of free time and laziness, and as there is more comfort – because of this comfort, it is more difficult for people. People ask for more. They think, "We are in comfort, we've got everything. Why are we unhappy?" However, if they paid attention to their work, they would forget about it, they would forget about the problems. They will make shukr to AllāhNone and get by. You must make more shukr now, because everything is more comfortable and easier.

But as we said, people have forgotten about Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. They have mixed up the good. They think bad is good and good is bad. Therefore, there are these conditions in the world. May AllāhNone help us. May AllāhNone protect us. The situation is very tough. May AllāhNone help us. If they listen to this advice, they will be at ease. If they don't listen, they will suffer. They can run to this one and that one. As we said, you must discover the jewel inside you. Then you won't need anything else, by permission of AllāhNone.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying: Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Fa-bi-dhālika fa-l-yafraḥū huwa khayrun mimmā yajma‘ūn" (10:58). To be happy – what HeNone said, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, in Qur’ān – "Be happy with favour of AllāhNone. This is better than what they collect" – what they are looking for they thought it is good thing. Because, our nowadays people, most of them, they are depressed, they are unhappy. They are not feeling well and they are looking for how to be happy. How to be... in good mood. But, they are looking this in wrong place. Because, this people, they are running to doctors, to psychology, other thing, and this people, they said, "Yes, you are depressed. Take this pill, you will be better." Going to other – "You must make come for me more to we speak together and you pay me money for each hour and you will be happy." This is what they thought it is good for them. It is not good.

AllāhNone create everybody with good thing, but people, they are not looking for this good thing. Because, what you need, you have it in your [in]side. AllāhNone create jewel in your, in everyone, in every human. They are all jewel. But people, they not knowing this jewel, value of this jewel, and they are looking outside to be happy from with drug, with medicine, with other thing. But this is not solution. Solution, to be happy with yourself, with Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. To be happy with yourself – "alā bi-dhikri Llāhi taṭma’innu l-qulūb" (13:28) – to satisfaction [comes] with mentioning of AllāhNone, mentioning Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. This is giving you happiness, giving you satisfaction. But other, no, it is not.

But now, even Muslim, they are also most of them not happy because they are looking for other people, imitate other people and they want more and more and more. But with this more, happiness not coming with more thing. Coming only from inside, not from outside. Outside what you give, it is not cure your inside, only cover and this cover quickly finish. For this, you must be looking for to do what Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla order you, with this you be save yourself and you'll be happy – to be good people, good man, good woman. But other thing, they make people unhappy, the most of people unhappy. Because also, old time people, they said they was more happy. This time people also they are very lazy, not doing anything. Old time people, they was working, woman or man. Woman working in house, cooking, making, sewing or other thing. Man work from morning to evening and bring thing for his house. For this, they was tired, no time to be depressed, no time to be unhappy. They are happy when they are bring something, they are happy and they are thanking their Lord, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. But nowadays, AllāhNone give us everything but people, they don't have, only they don't have happiness. This happiness, with money you cannot buy. This is proof for our days because we are very, very relaxed, very comfort – this time people. Old time people, no car – nothing. Walking, coming, going, carrying thing by their hand. But there was better than our days. They was thanking AllāhNone.

But nowadays, we have everything, but people still not happy. For when they are not happy with this, AllāhNone also... Not thanking AllāhNone. AllāhNone without thank, HeNone not give happiness. We must be thank for AllāhNone, what HeNone give us, not to be saying complaining. Everything HeNone gave us, we must be very happy. As Mawlānā Shaykh was, every time he was, many time we hear this story.

One man in Damascus, he said, when I was going to this market, bazaar, one man, he put something to sell second-hand. This maybe not second-hand – tenth- or twentieth-hand. Very old nail, lock, and other thing like old spoon or other thing. When, if you look for this maybe you can, you cannot pay five penny for this. Mawlānā was asking him, "How is business today?" He said, "Ooh," kissing his hand, put on his head, saying: "Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, very good, very good. I am very happy." And this man, people he satisfied, and he is happy with what AllāhNone give him. He thank AllāhNone, AllāhNone give him happiness. But our days’ people, what you give them, they are not happy. They want more and more and more. But, you cannot take... but they cannot take happiness.

So, we must be thankful for AllāhNone for every breath HeNone gave us. For every second we are in this life, because this life it is precious for us for other life. AllāhNone help us, AllāhNone make us to be happy with HimNone, with HisNone favour. With the biggest favour to be nation of Prophet Muḥammad ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
