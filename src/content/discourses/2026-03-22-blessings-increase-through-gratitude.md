As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Today, shukr to Allah , is the third day of ‘Īd. It passed with goodness and barakah, shukr to Allah . Many ‘Īds have passed from the time of our Prophet ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam until now. All of them were bestowed by Allah for the benefit of Muslims.

We must be grateful for what Allah has bestowed upon us. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says that blessings increase with gratitude. Nowadays, people don’t have much gratitude left. It would be accurate to say there’s no gratitude at all. When you talk to someone, they’re not grateful; they’re not content with anything. When there’s no contentment, no barakah is left, and favors don’t increase. There becomes hardship.

Blessings increase with gratitude. What is a blessing? A blessing is the gift Allah gives. There’s no limit to the blessings He has given you. Some say that there was a scholar who performs many acts of worship. He said, “I rely on my worship, I don’t need anything else. I have done everything perfectly.” He sees in his dream that the Day of Judgment has come, and he is being held accountable. They were put on the scale. He sees that the blessing of seeing with one eye is placed on one side of the scale, and all the good deeds he has done are placed on the other. But they weren’t enough.

That’s how valuable Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla’s blessings are. But people don’t appreciate them. They don’t know the value of any blessing. They don’t make shukr to Allah. Then, when something happens, they are surprised what to do. May Allah protect us.

Therefore, these blessings are the grace of Allah . They are the favor of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla for us. Shukr to Allah for all the blessings He has given us.

Therefore, our Prophet ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam said, “Every day, a person should give sadaqah for each of the 360 parts of their body that Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla has given them.” The Companions asked, “O Rasūlullah, we don’t have that much money, how can we do this?” He replied, “It’s not only with money. Your good deeds are sadaqah. Picking up a stone from the ground is sadaqah. Not throwing trash on the street is sadaqah. Smiling at your Muslim brother, showing him a cheerful face, that too is sadaqah. And if that’s not enough,” said our Prophet ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, “two rak’ahs of Duhā prayer suffice for all those as sadaqah,” says our Prophet ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam.

Therefore, we must not forget that shukr is a daily sadaqah for these blessings given to us. As we said, even those without money should show a smiling face to their brothers. People should do good deeds and help others. Even if they don’t do much else, at least performing the two rak’ahs of the Duha prayer each day is considered sadaqah for the whole day.

Sadaqah, as we said, is gratitude to Allah. Giving that sadaqah is īmān in Allah. It is even little in return for these blessings. As we said, our reward is the intercession of our Prophet. The person who follows his way will attain his intercession. If you don’t include that beautiful intention in your īmān and your actions, your actions have no value, no weight. But if you do it for the intercession of our Prophet , for his sake, for his honor, that smallest thing will be heavier than all your other deeds.

Allah has bestowed this great blessing upon us all. The greatest blessing is the blessing of īmān. And being among the Ummah of our Prophet is one of the greatest blessings.

May Allah bless us. May these holidays be a means for goodness. May He protect us from evil and our egos. May it be of barakah, inshā’Allah. May our children have īmān. Shukr to Allah , children and families came to visit. May Allah accept their visits. May they also be protected from evil.

Because evil is very bad in this time. We are living in a very bad time. One doesn’t know where evil will come from. Therefore, it is absolutely necessary not to forget this matter of sadaqah or the two rak’ahs of Duha prayer.

May Allah help us, inshā’Allah.
