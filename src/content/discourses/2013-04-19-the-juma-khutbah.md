Our assembly is the assembly of worship. It is obligatory to glorify Rabbul Izzah. To exalt, to glorify, to praise, to sanctify - it's our duty to do these. Let's mention... our Lord's Name. May our faces be white (with light) and our hearts pure and without complaints. The happy ones and the believers, this is the day that they are happy about and celebrating, a holy day and the most favorable day in this world. Yawmul Jumaa - Friday. Friday is reserved for Allah Dhul Jalal wal Jamal, it is His day for invitation.
