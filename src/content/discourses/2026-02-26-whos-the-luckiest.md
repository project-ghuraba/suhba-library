As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
مَنْ يَهْدِهِ اللَّهُ فَلَا مُضِلَّ لَهُ، وَمَنْ يُضْلِلْهُ فَلَا هَادِيَ لَهُ
<br>“Man yahdillāhu fa-lā mudilla lah, wa man yudlilhu fa-lā hādiyā lah”<br>
“Whoever Allah guides, no one can lead astray; and whoever Allah leads astray, no one can guide him.” [Ḥadīth, Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim].

Who is the lucky one? It is the person whom Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla has guided. Because it happens by the will of Allah. A person who has attained guidance is saved. If Allah does not will guidance for him, he is an unfortunate person. He may possess the whole world, everything may be his, but the Hereafter is not his. Not attaining the Hereafter is the worst thing. In this world, he indulges in all kinds of pleasures and disgrace, never refraining from any pleasure, trying everything without missing anything. That is, he is left with nothing but sin for the Hereafter. Those are the unlucky people.

The lucky one is the one who is on the way of Allah who obeys Allah. The person who performs his prayers, fasts, pays his zakāt, and does everything else, is a lucky person. Because shayṭān follows him, trying to prevent him. No matter how much you do, shayṭān will never leave you.

Therefore, those who are on this way should thank Allah praise Allah that we are on this way. We see people eating outside during Ramaḍān, and doing other things during the day. There’s no need to be angry at them. We should feel pity for them. They are unlucky people; they are not destined for goodness. In the Hereafter - May Allah protect us. Therefore, we should make shukr to Allah. And there’s no need to say, “I’m better than them.” We should say, “Praise be to Allah may He keep us steadfast.”

We hear many complaints, “My son used to pray five times a day, he wouldn’t leave a prayer, I don’t understand what happened. He’s not doing anything. He’s hanging out with bad friends. He’s stopped praying, he’s gotten involved in bad things.” Therefore, those who are on the way of Allah should always be thankful and ask Allah for help so that we remain steadfast on this way. May He not make us go astray.

This way is a beautiful way. May Allah guide others as well. May they too taste this beauty. Because the beauty of īmān is unparalleled. We see what the world has become. The pleasures and delights of the world have limits. The desires of the ego are infinite, but even those have limits. Once you reach a certain point, you can’t go beyond that point; you can’t enjoy it anymore. That’s why they want to try something else, something different. No matter what, good or bad, even the worst, the ego will still pursue it. But as we said, there is a limit, and it cannot be exceeded. That beauty can only be attained eternally in Paradise. So, one must be on the way of Allah in this world to reach that beauty.

May Allah guide people. May Allah protect us.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
