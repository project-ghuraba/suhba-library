A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

People who had no belief used to oppress and laugh at believers, pious ones and Prophets, because they didn't understand them. They used to dislike what they did. It seemed funny to them. Whereas Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says in Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n: Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Allāhu yastahzi’u bihim" (2:15). AllāhNone embarrasses them. They are the funny ones because they discard jewels and pick up dirt. They put it on their heads and try to keep it in special places. They think that dirt is something good and accept it as precious. They think precious jewels are worthless and look at those who think the opposite as being crazy and laugh at them. "Look. What are these people, what are they doing? And look at this beauty we have." What is in their hands is nothing but dirt. It is nothing but badness. AllāhNone is ridiculing them. HeNone is embarrassing them. They will fall into a ridiculous condition. They have already fallen, but they haven't realised.

Sometimes people are like this, they laugh at others, whereas they are the ones in a funny situation themselves, not the opposite. They laugh at them. Who is on the way of AllāhNone is always on the right way. The way he follows is not a way to laugh at. Rather it is the way of relief. Something that is laughed at is not good, but laughing [for happiness] is different, In the end, Muslims and believers will be the ones laughing, and the others will cry. They will regret, but regret will be useless. We must know what is good in this world. We are living in the end of times. You cannot believe but things you couldn't imagine being acceptable ten or twenty years ago, are now accepted by the same people. But they have become such that they approve all kinds of badness and dislike goodness. They are the ones who will regret. They are the ones who will be disappointed.

Therefore, you must like what everyone likes only if it is good. You must like it if it is suitable for humanity. Humanity is with Islām. On the contrary, there is no humanity with badness. There is animality with badness. Animals are not aware of anything. They just run after each other. They are not ashamed of anyone. That is their condition. The condition of humanity is good. It is Islām and īmān. If people don't have this, they fall from the rank of humanity. May AllāhNone protect us.

We live in a difficult time. People are mixing the good and the bad. When we see the bad, we must not get used to it. "This is bad. We don't accept it. We don't like it." It is the weakest side of īmān. "It is prohibited and bad. We don't approve of it and dislike it. We don't accept it." We must think like this. This is in order not to get accustomed to it. It is as it is, but you cannot do anything. Don't accept it. Reject it in your heart, in shā’a Llāh. May AllāhNone protect us from this time. May AllāhNone make us from those who see the good and not make us from those who see the bad as good, in shā’a Llāh.

We are living in time which many people they don’t know anything. We are living in most educated time, maybe from beginning. Most of people they are educated, they know to write, to read and they are not let them to, with this enough for you to write and read. No, they must put them in school. Primary school, secondary school, and high school and even university. Many people they are not giving them job without certificate. But they are teaching ignorance, not what must be people to become better. They become worse with this study because they thought they are something and they are laughing at believers who following order of AllāhNone and following Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, following religious.

They are laughing on them, they are not happy with them: "Oh this crazy people. Look how they are acting. Look how they are doing all that." But Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla HeNone said, "Allāhu yastahzi’u bihim" (2:15). AllāhNone He make them funny. Not who thought – "These people funny – believer." Reality, they are funny. They are doing something people laugh at them. Not laugh for happiness. Laugh because they are like what they do, not suitable for humanity. Humanity suits with Islām, with belief of AllāhNone. Who practice and accept this, he become in humanity maqām, place. But who not doing this, they are falling down from this to animal place. They are like animal because exactly, animal they are running after each other without shame, without anything – as normal. This people also they are doing every bad thing as normal. So becoming like animal or worse than animal.

For this, AllāhNone make them funny, not good people. But they thought they are good and they are finding believer, who practicing order of AllāhNone, funny. They laugh at them. But Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla HeNone know best. HeNone gave everyone jewel, but people who know value of jewel they keep it. Other they throw jewel and they said this not good and they catching dirt. And they put in their head, in their pocket – wearing this, going, coming with this. For this, AllāhNone make them funny. They must be laughed at them. Not believer who has jewellery of īmān and they keep themselves clean and good, but other they don’t have any cleanliness, only dirt. Going, coming, eating, drinking dirt. Wearing dirt, speaking dirt, everything they are – dirt. For this when who must be not respected, they are, not believer. Believer they are respected and they are AllāhNone love them. Other one, nobody love who eat dirt, drink dirt, doing, speaking dirt. People they will be away from them.

We are asking Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla to keep us safe from these people, from their act also, in shā’a Llāh, and to not be happy with them. Don’t be happy with them. Even people before twenty years or thirty years, they nobody was accepting what they are nowadays they are accepting. But slowly, slowly making as normal. For this, who see something wrong, not acceptable for believer, he must every time say, "This is not acceptable for us," by his heart or anything, to not make it to be normal. It is abnormal. Normal – what AllāhNone create people with good behave and good act. Other not normal. We are asking AllāhNone to keep us in way of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, with belief, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
