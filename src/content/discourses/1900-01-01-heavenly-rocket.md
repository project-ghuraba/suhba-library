Shaykh Muhammad Nazim Al-Haqqani An-Naqshibandi, Sohbat of the 1rst of January, 1991.

The servants if they are ready or prepared, no need for rockets to get up. He is going to be the Heavenly rocket. Because, this is a ramp for Heavenly rocket. Heavenly rocket it is through (within) yourselves, but there is a key. You can be able to turn much getting up. If we are not knowing or we lost the key you should be (stay) on earth, never getting up. And Awliya's associations, their life stories giving that Divinely support and Heavenly help to servants. And it is rubbed in our physical bodies spiritual rocket. Everyone has a rocket! But key, not taking yet key. And for everyone whom they are belonging to Tariqas the keys through the hands of Shaykhs, their Shaykhs. They are keeping that key for our rockets. And all just given from Allah Almighty to the Seal of Prophets, Beloved (saws) and he is giving to Tariqat Shaykhs, and they are giving to mureeds.

Everyone has that rocket, spiritual rocket taking up. And the example for it Rasulullah Prophet, he was the example through his rocket. When you are prepared. Therefore Tariqat is preparing people. If not a person not belonging to a Tariqat, for common people also there is five inheritors of Prophet - they are poles. Five poles, Qutub, Qutub Al Bilad, Qutub Al Irshad, Qutub Al Aqtab Qutub Al Mutasarrif. Five poles. They are responsible for whole common nation of Sayyidina Muhammad. They have the key for common people. And when we are going to be prepared it is going to be so easy. And, we must try to be successful when they are trying. First Allah Almighty trying His servant and Rasulullah (saws) trying his ummah and Awliyas' trying their mureeds. If he is going to be ready or not. Everyone must ask through this life for reaching to their main destination. But it is not enough to have a jet plane, if you are not taking a pilot to take you. If you have 100 planes and no driver, it is useless.

Everyone has, from mankind everyone they have a spiritual power, heavenly rocket, ramped inside. And all Prophets, their missions it is only to make that rocket to open, to make them marching towards our destination. Some of them reaching through this life, they are most precious people. If not, finally everyone going to reach to their destination, and it is going to be at the end of their lives. If not reaching they are not taking care through this life, at the end of their lives Allah Almighty obliging them and Divinely Will forcing them to reach to their destination. And perhaps going to be through seconds, maybe a long lifeline for those persons to complete that.

Prophet (saws) moved from earth from the Haram in Makka, Holy Kaaba, moved and reaching to Baitul Maqdis, Jerusalem. And whole Prophets coming and greeting him. And he was praying with them 2 rak'ats. Then he is going up for Miraj, ascension and after, after that, going to Divinely Presence also, and coming back and yet his bed not loosing his holy body's warmness. And when he was getting up, the edge of his clothes touching a water jar and falling down and water coming out, all these things happened in the Night Journey and coming back, yet this water not yet complete (not finished falling). That is Allah Almighty's endless unlimited Power Oceans. He may do as He likes. He did it! Allah Almighty for His Beloved Muhammad (saws) in such a short time.

Then, if a person not prepared himself through his life, by occupying himself with useless things, and not reaching to his destination, spiritual rocket but he worked for this dunya and wasted his life. Then when seven breaths left from his life, through that seven breaths (breathing) umhh, umhh, umhh, like this, seven breaths the Lord Almighty Allah, ordering to His poles, Qutub, to look after that one. And through that seven breaths may do 70 years more preparing that person. Then when he is prepared may move from this life to real life. And may reach to his destination. Therefore as much as a person taking care for reaching his destination here, he should be in the last moments so easy. If a person enjoying here with physical enjoyments and not taking care for his spiritual being and spiritual rocket, not arranging, then finally he should be given a test for preparing him for ascension towards Heavens. Everyone must do that preparation. And they are reaching to Heavens according to their ranks here. It is not going to be same for those whom they prepared themselves through this life, for reaching Divinely Presence. And those whom they are prepared at the end of their lives. So big distance among themselves.

Therefore we must try, and particularly in our time people they are more needy, to listens to tales, like tales of Awliyas' to be given more tasalli/enjoy not to feel the heavy burden of this life. They should be granted through their worshipping some power. That any huge event, they may face it not to come down. And whom they may keep this way, spiritual training and spiritual training centers. They should be prepared for coming huge days. Huge months and huge years. May Allah forgives us, and makes you to move towards real goals of this life, not to be occupied by useless actions. May Allah forgives us and blessing you.

Bi hurmatil Habib, Bi hurmatil Fatiha.
