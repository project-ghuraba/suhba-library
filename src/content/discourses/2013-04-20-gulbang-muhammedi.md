Welcome. Welcome. Masha Allah, la quwwata illa billah Allahumma zid wa barik. (O Allah increase and bless) Ya rabbi azhir Sahib. (O my Lord, reveal the Sahib.) Azhhir as Salatin wa-l-'Uthmaniyyin. May this gathering be for the honour of our ancestors the Ottomans, who were leading Islam for 1000 years. May this be the beginning. May our Sultans come, who will make the whole world tremble. Al-Fatiha. Ma sha' Allah. Ma sha' Allah, Ma sha' Allah. La quwwata illa billah.

O our Lord make it come true, for the nobility of your honorable Prophet (sas) Ib'athlana malikan nuqatil fi sabili Llah (2:246) (Send us a King, so we can fight in the way of Allah) Ya Rabbi. O Lord of Greatness, Might and Majesty. O my Lord bring back the honour of the Ottomans, who have carried your flag and the flag of Your Beloved for 1000 years. May the Sultan come; may the Lion who will make the world tremble come. Amin my Lord. Grant us service in those days too. Plant love for them in our hearts, O Lord. Let us live only for You, not for this world. Let us not be afraid of non-believers, but let us be afraid of Allah. Amin. May your ending be good. Please, takbir Allahu Akbar. Ma sha' Allah.

They don't get tired. They're lions, they don't get tired. Enter the Meydan (arena). Amin, amin, amin. Yes, my Lord, make it come true, O our Lord. Make it come true - by the honour of your chosen Prophet, may the army of the Ottomans conquer the East and the West. May it destroy the forces of disbelief. May it transform the nests of shaytan into dust and smoke. May there be peace and tranquility on the surface of earth again. Allahu Akbar. Allahu Akbar. Allahu Akbar. Fatiha.

Who are they? (Sh B): Germans. They will play the Kaiser march. The mehter (royal band) march. Sheikh Effendi: These ones? Sheikh B: Germans. K: The Austrian Emperor is sending you this medal of honour for your work for peace and unity. Sh Effendi: For me? I am giving to you as an honour medal from me to these people. Because this is an honour medal for our people I accepted it, and have given it to you. Keep it safe. We will come to Vienna's other side. Our wave won't stop. The Ottoman soil, the Ottoman soil on the surface of the Earth is here now. The land registration (tapu) of all of Cyprus is still with the Ottomans.

Does he understand? (Is he) a Turk? Sh B: Austrian. Sheikh Effendi: Austria - Ma sha' Allah, an Empire. Mashallah. Did he give this to me? If it's like that, I'll take, accept it. Thank you.

These are powerful. The Germans are the army of Mahdi (as) Mashallah, Allahu Akbar. Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar Allahu Akbar. Wa-l-hamdulillahi kathira, wa subhanallahi bukratan wa asila. I am asking from the Lord of Heavens that he grant us real face for Ottomans. as well as their supporters. The noble German people who will take up the holy flag of the Lord of Heavens. Insha' Allah. Fatiha. May they live long, may they live long. The Ottomans and Germans are like one apple; one is like the other half, they are generous ones. They belong to the servants who are accepted from Allah's side. They will be the first nation to bring tauhid (unity) with Allah's permission. May your ceremonies start here. May it last till the time of prophet Isa. The Ottoman flag will be raised. No one can pass in front of it. Finished. Everything has a beginning and an end. The time of this period of disbelief has come to an end. Now the victorious flags of Islam, the flags of the Ottomans, will come. There's no doubt about it. Masha'Allah la quwwata illa billahil-'aliyyil-'azim. Peace! A word from a Lord most Merciful. (36:58) Salamun qawlan min rabbin rahim (36:58) The Ottomans will come forth. Al-Fatiha.

They came in a good state, they came in a pure state; they came with blessings. Welcome. They came with awe-inspiring charisma (hayba). They came with power. They came with greatness. They came with goodness. May Allah protect you.
