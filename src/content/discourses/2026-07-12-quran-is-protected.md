As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
إِنَّا نَحْنُ نَزَّلْنَا الذِّكْرَ وَإِنَّا لَهُ لَحَافِظُونَ
<br>“Innā naḥnu nazzalnā dh-dhikra wa-innā lahu la-ḥāfiẓūn”<br>
“Indeed, it is We who sent down the Qur’ān and indeed, We will be its guardian.” (Qur’ān 15:09). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says: “We have sent down Qur’ān ʿAẓīmu sh-Sha’n and all the religious commandments. We will protect them. We are protecting them.”

The word of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla is truth. No one can change it; no one can abolish it. It will have its time. One of the signs of the Day of Judgment in the end times is that Qur’ān ʿAẓīmu sh-Sha’n will be erased. It will cease to exist. It will be erased from the minds of those who have memorized it (Hāfiz); it will no longer be in their memory. That’s in the end of times, just before the Day of Judgment. Before that, no matter what anyone does, they cannot change the Qur’ān or the religion.

Now, there are many foolish people. Many who follow shayṭān, who obey shayṭān’s commands, who are enemies of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla, enemies of Prophets, enemies of Islām.

Indeed, from the very first day, the day Allah created Ādam ʿalayhi s-salām, shayṭān has been an enemy. And he has many enemies along with him. They spend all their time trying to destroy this religion. There are many shayṭāns who strive to remove the way brought by our Holy Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. Many try to change things by saying, “This religion isn’t up to date now, this āyah applies now, that one doesn’t.” They are all doomed to failure. They will never succeed.

As we said, when Allah wishes, in the end times, He will remove Qur’ān, and the Day of Judgment will fall upon the disbelievers. Otherwise, no matter how much they try, no matter how much they sow discord, they will never succeed. Allah protects. The religion belongs to Allah, it is the religion of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla.

No sane person would harbor enmity towards Allah. They have no power. A human being cannot even have the power of an ant against Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. Not even the power of a flea.

Shukr to Allah, the religion is under protection. No one can do anything. If there are people who follow him, who have lost their minds, may Allah guide them and bring them back to their senses. May they repent and seek forgiveness for what they’ve done because of their thoughts so that Allah may have mercy on them and protect them.

Otherwise, there are many foolish people who have been deceived by the shayṭān. And there are those who show the way. We can only say, may Allah give them what they deserve; nothing else. May Allah protect us from their evil.

This is the greatest evil, playing with a person’s īmān, leading people astray. It is what shayṭān does. There are those who follow him, who do these things for worldly gain. May Allah guide them, or may Allah give them what they deserve, we can’t say anything else.

May Allah protect Islām, Muslims, and our īmān, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
