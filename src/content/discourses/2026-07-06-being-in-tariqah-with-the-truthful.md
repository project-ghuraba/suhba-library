As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

[...] be with the sādiqīn, truthful people. Truthful people have become very rare in this time. Truthful means those who don’t lie and don’t deceive people. Be with them so that Allah loves you, and Allah bestows barakah upon you.

People have been given mind and reason by Allah, but most people don’t use their mind. They do everything for the sake of dunyā; they act like clowns, they do this and that, thinking they will gain something. But they gain nothing. The gain is with the truthful people. You gain both here and hereafter. Those who are with Allah never lose. That’s what we want to say.

May Allah be pleased with you. This is the way of our mashāyikh. This way, by the permission of Allah, is the way of the Ṣiddīqīn, all the way to our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam.

Therefore, being in this ṭarīqah is the way to happiness. The way to happiness is through ṭarīqah, through the mashāyikh. Because this goes all the way to our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. The Jamā’ah is not like that. The Jamā’ah goes only so far. What’s after that is unclear.

Therefore, what’s firm is the ṭarīqah. There are forty-one ṭarīqahs. Forty of them come from Sayyidinā ʿAli. Ours comes from Sayyidinā Abu Bakr, but it still reaches Sayyidinā ʿAli. They are all perfect ṭarīqahs, but the one that no one can even utter a word against is the Naqshbandī Ṭarīqah.

Shukr to Allah, it is a great favor. Whoever attains it gains the love of Allah, and the pleasure and love of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. This is our demand here and hereafter.

إِلَهِي أَنْتَ مَقْصُوْدِي وَرِضَاكَ مَطْلُوْبِي
<br>“Ilāhī Anta maqṣūdī wa-riḍāka maṭlūbī”<br>
“O, my Lord! You are my objective and Your Pleasure is my aim.”

You are our purpose, and Your pleasure is our desire.

May Allah grant us all steadfastness on this way. May He also guide others, inshā’Allāh. May they also taste this beauty, inshā’Allāh. May they not chase after dunyā and do unnecessary things.

As we said, the righteous will win. There is no need to do anything else. Those who think they will win by chasing after shayṭān will never win. May Allah protect us. May Allah keep us all steadfast. May Allah be pleased with you.

Mashā’Allah, Murad Efendi is also here. May this be perpetual, inshā’Allāh. He constantly offers hospitality and serves those who come and go. May Allah be pleased with him. Mashā’Allah, all the murīds and grandchildren of Mawlānā Shaykh Sharafuddīn love us very much. We love them too. May Allah grant them long lives, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
