Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Sohbats are made everywhere Inshallah. And today we will be travelling by airplane to a far place. We went once with Hazrat Shaykh Mawlana. Mashallah there are quite a many brothers and sisters, many Muslims there. They invited us so they can be and remain constant on the way of Shaykh Mawlana, on the way of the Prophet (SAW). So Inshallah we are going.

There are quite a few ikhwan there. They also come and go over here. Wayfaring is the tradition of prophets. Inshallah we will also try and be of use to the ikhwan wherever we go. Hazrat Shaykh Mawlana went over there 15 years ago. He went all over the world and planted seeds. These communities have Inshallah produced leaves and are increasing. If Hazrat Shaykh Mawlana gathered at the same age as us, the congregation would be this much larger.

However, he had strength. Allah (JJ) had given him from all kinds of His favors. Hazrat Shaykh Mawlana could talk for hours. He used to guide people to the right way. Nobody was around then. Now the exact opposite has happened Mashallah: there are lots of ikhwan. Thank Allah that the sohbats Hazrat Shaykh Mawlana has left as an inheritance for us would be enough for people for a hundred years, even if we did not speak a word. This is the reason people come all the way here.

We would not be able to carry these states if their spiritual support did not exist. Even though we are not one in a million of what Hazrat Shaykh Mawlana was, thank Allah those who come go back happy Inshallah due to their blessing and with their support. They are also very glad wherever we go. Our case should have been the opposite. We should have been speaking to a little number of people. The seeds that Hazrat Shaykh Mawlana planted are reproducing. It also states in the Koran. It is plentiful when you plant the wheat grain. It produces seven spikes. Each spike produces a hundred. And this means 700 pieces of wheat. One piece is equivalent to 700 to one. This incident means the blessing of Hazrat Shaykh Mawlana. With the permission of Allah, the ikhwan will become even more Inshallah.

Even a couple of words uttered by us would become ointment for their hearts, it would become iman, and it would become continuous Inshallah. May we go and come back in safety Inshallah. Whatever good goals we have, may they come forth Inshallah. The dua of the wayfarer is accepted.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq.

Al-Fatiha.
