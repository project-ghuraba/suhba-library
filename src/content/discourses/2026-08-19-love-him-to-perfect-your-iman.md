As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Shukr to Allah, we have reached the blessed month of Rabīʿ ul-Awwal. Inshā’Allāh, we will celebrate our Holy Prophet’s birthday together in Cyprus, in the presence of Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim. Everyone sees that the barakah, compassion, and protection of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam are upon those who love him.

Those who love our Holy Prophet also love Allah. Those who do not love our Holy Prophet love shayṭān. This must be known. Because he is the human shayṭān hates the most. When he (shayṭān) was forced to appear before our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam and commanded to tell the truth, he said, “I do not love you.” He was asked many questions, finally, when asked, “Whom do you hate the most?” shayṭān said to our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, “I do not love you.”

Therefore, those who do not love our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam love shayṭān; there is no other way, no third option.

Certainly, the love of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam should be most present in our hearts. Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam said, “For your faith to be perfect, you must love me more than everyone in the world; more than yourselves, your mothers, your fathers, and your children.” If faith is not perfect, that love will not reach that level. However, it is still necessary for this love to be present in our hearts, even if only a little.

Some people who claim to be “Muslims” say, “He’s just a human being like you and me, he died and is gone.” Astaghfirullah! And they confuse other Muslims by claiming to be “Muslims”. Shayṭān leads people astray and deviates them through them. Because without the intercession of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, none of our deeds are of any use.

You can worship for a million years, but if there is no love and respect for our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam in your heart, it will be of no benefit.

May Allah bless these days. May we attain the intercession of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, inshā’Allāh. May we reside near him in Paradise, inshā’Allāh. May Allah make it blessed and of goodness, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
