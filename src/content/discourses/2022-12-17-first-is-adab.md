As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla wants people to get knowledge, to ask for knowledge. Together with knowledge, it is important to have adab in order for that knowledge to be useful. Then it will be useful. Without adab, knowledge becomes something that has no benefit. Rather than benefit, it can bring harm as well. This is because the most knowledgeable among the creatures of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla was Iblīs. He knew everything. He performed so much worship that there was no place where he didn't pray or make sajdah. However, because he didn't have adab, he was thrown away. All his ranks were thrown away, all his high stations. From a beloved one, he was thrown away as a cursed one. Otherwise, he knew every knowledge, all the knowledge. There is no knowledge that he didn't know. But he didn't have adab. Without adab, all that knowledge, everything he knew and his worship brought no benefit.

The thing that brings benefit is adab. Therefore, ṭarīqats exist in order to teach people adab. They show us the way of our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. They are for teaching hisNone way, things heNone showed and taught – for learning adab. Otherwise, ṭarīqah is not for other things. Without ṭarīqah there is no adab.

We see now there are various things everywhere around. They teach religion at universities, at schools, here and there. Most of them have no adab. How do not they have adab? They are not showing respect to our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam and they are not showing respect to ṣaḥābah, Ahlu l-Bayt, tabi‘īn and tābi‘ī t-tābi‘īn. So there is no adab. They are saying to the people, who come to them, not to respect anyone. However, the people of bid‘ah (Wahhābī) respect people who don't follow the sunnah of our Holy ProphetNone. They hold them dear. And they don't show respect, don't keep adab towards those who should actually be respected and shown adab. If you want to do something, those people say it is wrong. It is wrong. If it is wrong, you are wrong too. You lose all your value too.

The value of people, the value of a servant in the presence of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is more when they have more adab. Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam is saying, "Addabanī Rabbī fa-aḥsana ta’dībī." "AllāhNone taught meNone adab, and HeNone taught meNone the best adab," says our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Therefore, whoever loves this way, loves himNone and respects himNone will profit even without knowledge. Adab is more important than knowledge. Without adab nothing has any benefit. You tire yourself for nothing. Things are not useful for anything other than exalting your ego.

May AllāhNone protect us, in shā’a Llāh. May AllāhNone not keep us without adab, in shā’a Llāh.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, when HeNone creates people, HeNone tells them, HeNone likes them to be knowledge[able], to know, to make, taking knowledge and to look for what HisNone order, what Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam order. This is knowledge of our religion, other religion only one of course – all from beginning till end it is Islām. This is what Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla – you must learn about your religion. But with religion to be benefit, you must be have good behave, which calling "adab" in Arabic. If you don't have this, it is not useful, not useful at all. Sometimes it is curse even, sometimes. Because people with their knowledge they thought they are something, they're not respect anybody, not respecting ‘ulamā’, not respecting mashāyikh, not respecting ṣaḥābah, even they not respecting Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam nowadays.

This who they have degree, but they are like iblīs. Before becoming shayṭān, he was named iblīs. He was the most knowledgeable one between whole creatures that time. Before Ādam ‘alayhi s-salām, he know everything from knowledge of dunyā and knowledge of spiritual and material also. He was knowledgeable very good, and he was worshipping also with good but when he make bad behave, not good behave, for when Ādam ‘alayhi s-salām coming, this old everything, he lose everything. He was in highest place, he come the lowest place after this. This is showing us – knowledge not enough. Only with good behave it is benefit for who has it.

These days also there's many scholars, we not saying ‘ulamā’ even for these people, they have much very high degree, they are not respect Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam even, and not respect ṣaḥābah, Ahlu l-Bayt, or tabi‘īn, tābi‘ī t-tābi‘īn, which Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam he said, "MyNone century is the best century and after meNone until tabi‘īn, tābi‘ī t-tābi‘īn – all this the best century." And this not respecting at all. They said, "If you say heNone is, they are like us, they are human being like us, no need to respect them." But other side, you see them they respect some people who was making fitnah and they're against ḥadīth, against Qur’ān, against madhhab, against ṭarīqah. They're very valuable for them, very respectable for them, they're happy with them. But with, which one... because they are like them, they don't have manner, they don't have good behave – this, they like them. But this is not good for anybody. Only good for shayṭān, who he love to take people away from way of Paradise, to way of Hell. This is what he like and many people they are following them.

But we are saying, you must be – first thing, before knowledge, you must learn adab. Which ṣūfī ways, ṭarīqat, they are for this to learn, teach people, adab and good behave. This is the mission of ṭarīqah. First say, "Illā adab, illā adab," saying, "First adab." And which Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam also saying, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, "Addabanī Rabbī fa-aḥsana ta’dībī" – "AllāhNone He make meNone good behave, and HeNone make it the best." Al-ḥamdu liLlāh we are, in shā’a Llāh, follow Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. AllāhNone make us to be happy with us, with our adab, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
