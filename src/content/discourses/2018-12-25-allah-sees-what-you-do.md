As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīn wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa-l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla is saying, Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm, "Wa-quli ‘malū fasayará Llāhu ‘amalakum" (9:105). Do good deeds. Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla will see what you do. We came to this world not for sitting but for working and worshipping. Working is worshipping. The purpose of your work in this world is to get rizq to worship and to do good things, to have a good job. A person who sees it as worship tries to do well at work. What he does won't be bad. He says, "AllāhNone gave me this job. I have to make it right and receive a reward and a salary." Because people of the end of times don't like working. In old times, there were no days off. People would work. Moreover, there is an āyah in Sūratu l-Jumu‘ah that says we shouldn't work during Jum‘ah. No trade can be made during Jum‘ah. No sale should be done. That time is forbidden. That time is just half an hour until Jum‘ah prayer is over. It's the time between entering the mosque and finishing Jum‘ah. Then it's free.

Vacations came up later. It was one day, then it became two. Then daily working hours became less. People are not willing to work. They are trying to earn without working. Even if they do, it is not good for them. When you are jobless in this life, shayṭān becomes your friend. He tries to get you to do something. It will be bad things. shayṭān doesn't persuade you for good things. You'll be harmed both physically and spiritually. Therefore, working is not embarrassing. It is not a sin. It's the opposite, it is thawāb (good deed). When you work and earn with the intention of worshipping and earning sustenance for your family, you'll be written thawāb for it. Otherwise, when you sit lazy, no rizq comes to you. And when it doesn't, you start complaining. And at last, the complaint is about Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla, astaghfiru Llāh. "Why did YouNone create me so? Why didn't YouNone make...?" This is the biggest misbehaviour: ungratefulness.

AllāhNone has given you all types of goodness. And you are upset. Go out, don't stay at home. Go somewhere, do something good. Even if you move a rock off the road, AllāhNone will write thawāb for you. No benefit from a person who sits at home and complains. He will get nothing but harm. Therefore, people have forgotten good things that should be done. They live in worry and troubles, making themselves right all the time by what they learnt from others. A working person is a beloved servant of AllāhNone, a beloved servant of our ProphetNone. May we always work for good things, in shā’a Llāh.

Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla say in Qur’ān, "Wa-quli ‘malū fasayará Llāhu ‘amalakum" (9:105). Do work, do something and Allāh HeNone will see what you do. This is small verses but it is for whole human being advise. You are not alone, you are not coming here by chance. AllāhNone create you and send you here and HeNone order you do good thing, because Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla HeNone will ask you. And HeNone see you and what you done and you will be charge for this. To do first thing to make HisNone order to praying, to worship HimNone, and after also don't sit without doing anything, without work. You must do some work for your life also, and this work also you be reward for this, because your intention from this work to earn money to earn something to feed your family, to not ask anybody for this. And this AllāhNone will reward you for. Even you feed your wife, children all this also extra as you give ṣadaqah. When you work also you are also you are tired, AllāhNone will reward you also for this also, because everything you are thinking for to be obeyed for AllāhNone and not forget HimNone.

You will do ḥalāl work, not like people harming people, they are make people to suffer and they will be get money on them. This is not ḥalāl, but if you are doing business, you are doing job, you are doing work, all this AllāhNone will reward you for this, because HeNone is seeing you and you are sincere and only what you want to be reward from AllāhNone, and to be Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa sallam happy with you. This is but this days people they are not like to do anything. They are doing only sitting, watching computer, watching internet. This is what they are doing, because teaching of shayṭān to make people to forget purpose of their life. But Allāh HeNone is asking what HeNone want, they are not important, even they are not asking for AllāhNone. Before this, the modern people they have many vacation, but in old time there was whole time they are working. No, no holiday. Even in Qur’ān Sūrat Jumu‘ah, there is one time to work forbidden, you must go to Jum‘ah praying, to listen for khaṭīb and praying two raka‘āt. This time you cannot sell, you cannot buy. It is forbidden to do this.

Other whole year, there was working but this last century. They beginning to give one day for holiday and now end of the century they make two days, and they make day times also very hour not working and vacation every time. So people they are thought their life only for holiday, not thinking about last day, not thinking about religion. Only what they want to not do anything. And it is not good even for their body, for their spiritual to be lazy. Not good. For this AllāhNone ordering, "Wa-quli ‘malū" (9:105). You must be work, you must do something. Don't sit in your place not doing anything. And if you are helping, you will reward. If you are going for mosque, even you working also you will be reward. All everything with to be move and to be not be sitting. Laziness is not good – laziness.

AllāhNone make us from HisNone lover and Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa sallam lover. To immitate our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa sallam. HeNone was day and night working, struggling for ummah, ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa sallam. Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
