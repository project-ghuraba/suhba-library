As-Salamu Alaikum. Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fil jamia' What Allah (awj) orders you to do, are they bad things? Hasha! All the things He (swt) orders are for the good of people, for them to find peace & comfort. But the ego finds them hard, the ego doesn't want them. It doesn't want to do even the easiest one. If you ask a man to wander around in the streets for 10 hours, to go here there, he does as much as you want. But when you ask him to pray 2 rakats, he goes "I don't have time, I can't do.." And now there is a new thing, they say "they are not letting me.." Who doesn't let you? "Jinn, shaytans..." Of course shaytan doesn't let you pray, why should he?

Allah (awj), everything belongs to Him (swt). He (swt) doesn't need your little worthless worship. But if you pray, it is for your benefit. It gives you benefit, it is for your own good-the good deeds, the charity, the good works you do, the good character, all are for your own benefit. Why did we say this? Recently one of our brothers, and later on my brother in-law had a heart attack. Both of them say "We quit smoking, life is too sweet to lose". I said, good. So when you smoke, it is harmful for yourself. Meaning all that you do..And the most significant matter for man is this-when you smoke, the biggest harm is for who smokes. And it also causes harm to the ones around him. It is the same for a person who does bad deeds, bad things-the biggest harm is to himself but causes harm to the ones around him too.

Therefore there is Hadith Sharif that says "When you see someone doing such a bad action, prevent him by your hand if possible". Of course now if you try to prevent by hand it can cause big problems, fights so we skip this one. By tongue- You say "this thing that you are doing is no good, don't do it". He may not accept that either, some people get angry with this also. So then you at least have dislike against it in your heart. If you don't dislike it means you join him in it. It causes you harm also. So say "don't do" for who smokes. If he continues to do so you stay away from him. At least it doesn't cause you harm also.

Allah says "Draw a lesson from everything". So when they both said "I quit smoking" I said "Here it is, what Allah orders is for the benefit of people. This is the biggest example to it. You are saying that you quit, we say you did very well. You did good for yourself."

Today we speak about, what Allah wants from people He needs them? No. Allah give everything. Everything He give, what we will need from human being or from creature. It is impossible to get benefit, give benefit for Allah for people. Allah want you to do good thing & to not do bad thing. What He orders to do, it is all good thing for you, for community also. He not in need for anything for you. When you do this, you'll be rest & you'll be in peace with people, with yourself & you'll be happy. But people, if you say to them "Go all day around, looking for nonsense, walking running coming going, to not do any worship, they are happy to. They can do this, they are not happy of course but they do this, looking for happiness. But they cannot find happiness with this. But if some you say, many people "pray 2 rakats" "Oh, this is I cannot do.. It is too difficult & it is, I cannot do". But for other thing, they can do everything.

But Allah order for benefit of yourself. When you do good thing, you get benefit, you get good action for yourself. You be happy here, here and for last day also, it is the most happiness there. Why I said this? Because before some times, one of our brother & before one week, my brother in-law told me "O, we left to smoke, not smoke anymore". Why? They had heart attack. Yes, they said "the life is nice. We cannot...It is too difficult to be.. We afraid too much from dying" because nearly they are dying so.. And they said "we are not smoking anymore". I said "This is what you do, benefit for another people? No, first for yourself you do this good thing. This benefit, when left bad action- smoking is very bad action, especially it is first for yourself benefit. Because many times they are saying "Don't smoke, don't smoke.." "Yes, Yes, I will leave, you make dua for me to not smoke anymore". But when coming this heart attack & they nearly die, quickly finish. Because they know now the benefit, the real benefit to leave this bad habit. So if he continue, maybe not live, he will die soon. But when leaving, Allah give him long (life) & become better & more health with him.

For this, the order of Prophet (sas) "When you see some people doing bad action, you must prevent them by hand-by force." But now you cannot do this, by force. If you cannot do by force, you tell them, you can speak to them "Don't do this". Even when they are not listening for this & you will be bad, more problem with them. By your heart, you must hit this action. Because if you are not hitting this action, you be with them. Like when he is smoking, if you take..You can take from his mouth but many people they cannot do this. By tongue you say "Don't do this". Some of them, ok also not listening. But when you are near them you also become like partner & like you are smoking, same. This is good example for bad action, also same become for you. You must be away when not listening.

Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, Al Fatiha.
