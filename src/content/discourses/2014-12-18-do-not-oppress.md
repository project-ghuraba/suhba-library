Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

The religion of Islam is the religion of mercy. It does not oppress anyone and does not take the rights of anyone. It cannot be like living in the woods. Everything, every move needs to be in accordance with Islam. Meaning the moves of people who come out saying they are Muslim need to conform with sharia, with what Allah says.

We see that the men say they want to establish an Islamic state. They are butchering people like sheep. What does this have to do with Islam? They butcher and kill without discriminating children. These are things outside of Islam. Those who do these are people who have nothing to do with Islam. They are fools! Those who follow them are fools too! Becasue Allah says, “Nobody is responsible for the actions of somebody else.” A person’s father might have killed a thousand people, you cannot come and kill his child.

Therefore, even if from far away, we do not accept these things done to people. We need to tell, “They are deniers. These are things that are not in Islam.” People should know, there are no such things in Islam. It is forbidden, it is a sin. They will go to hell forever. If a Muslim kills another Muslim, his place is ready in hell. The last Islamic state was the Ottoman state. There is none left afterwards.

People who are not Islamic are certainly managing the things called the Islamic State today. And they are fooling people with little intelligence. They are making them do those things unknowingly saying, “You are Muslim!” Truth always emerges. It can never be overcome. No matter how much they are doing that is distant from Islam, they can never be successful. They are always cursed in the world. They will also end in hell in the hereafter. Have mercy on those on the face of earth so Allah may have mercy on you. In short, the more you oppress the more you will be oppressed.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq.

Al-Fatiha.
