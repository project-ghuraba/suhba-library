As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Wa-aqīmū ṣ-ṣalāta wa-ātū z-zakāh" (2:43). Zakāh is given in the month of Ramaḍān. Zakāh is given once a year. And it is more virtuous when it is given in Ramaḍān. It has much more thawāb. Normally, it is ten rewards to one. In the month of Ramaḍān, rewards of seven hundred to one are given by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. HeNone gives even more. The treasures of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla are endless. For the honour and virtue of this holy month, AllāhNone gives. When you make charity, when you perform worship, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla rewards you not only double or five-fold, but even a hundred-fold. HisNone generosity is unlimited. Everything is in HisNone hands. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla has power over everything.

Therefore, zakāh is given once a year. People can give it in other months as well, but in order not to forget and to attain more virtue, people give their zakāh in Ramaḍān. What is the amount of zakāh? The amount of zakāh is this: if someone has one hundred grams of gold, or property and trade goods equivalent to it, for the duration of a year, he should give one-fortieth part of what he has. If he has one hundred grams of gold, he must give 2.5 grams away. It can be given in money, food or drink. Anything in that amount can be given. It can be given both in gold and other things. When given with the intention of zakāh, it is counted as farḍ. It is farḍ. It is from the conditions of Islām. First condition is shahādah, second is praying, and then comes fasting, zakāh and ḥajj. All of them are farḍ.

There are many people who don't know these are farḍ. They must be done. Ḥajj can be done later. But zakāh must be given. You must give zakāh every year so that your possessions are clean. Possessions from which zakāh is not given are dirty. Don't make them dirty. The things you own are cleansed when you give zakāh. And they don't decrease but increase. AllāhNone gives barakah. Property and wealth belong to AllāhNone. When you give from your wealth, without doubt, AllāhNone gives you too. He says, "MyNone servant gave," and doesn't let it decrease. "Mā naqaṣa mālu min ṣadaqah," says our Holy ProphetNone. Assets don't become less because of ṣadaqah, says our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Therefore, even if you can't find anyone to give to in Ramaḍān, you should separate that money in Ramaḍān, and give it to the needy step by step. No need to give the money immediately. If you give more outside of Ramaḍan, you can also give that with the intention of zakāh, to people in need.

Zakāh is one of the beautiful practices of Ramaḍān. It is always remembered together with Ramaḍān. May AllāhNone make us from the giving ones. A giving hand is better than a receiving one, says our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Therefore, may AllāhNone make all our hands giving, in shā’a Llāh. May we give zakāh and ṣadaqah without our hands shaking, in shā’a Llāh.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Wa-aqīmū ṣ-ṣalāta wa-ātū z-zakāh" (2:43). Order from AllāhNone to give zakāt. Zakāt our obligatory, farḍ. One of pillar of Islām. First, to say kalimatu sh-shahādah: "Ashhadu an lā ilāha illā Llāhu wa-ashhadu anna Muḥammadan ‘abduhu wa-rasūluh." Second, ṣalāt praying five times, and then to fast Ramaḍān also farḍ. After, zakāt. Fourth one, zakāt.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla make this also to be one after each other, to show importance for this. And first three, everybody can do. Who is Muslim must say shahādah. Muslim, he must pray. If he is rich or not rich, poor – must pray. Third, fasting also, for whole Muslims. Fasting farḍ, obligatory. Fourth, zakāt. Zakāt is only for who has certain amount of money. It is equal to hundred gram from gold. If this hundred gram, you have it one year, you must pay one part from forty. Hundred meaning, two and half gram of gold you can pay it as gold or you pay it as money or you pay it as food. Anything, you can pay. No problem for this. This is first one for who not poor people.

This is very important worshipping, and we make it most of, in Ramaḍān. You can do any time of year, but in Ramaḍān you won more reward. Normally for one good thing one reward, ten reward Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla giving. But in Ramaḍān, giving from seven hundred time more, and more. Nobody ask why YouNone do this. So for this, in shā’a Llāh, AllāhNone make us to not be greedy, and to give without shaking your hand, what AllāhNone made it obligatory for you. You must be happy you have this money. And other people they don't have, so you must be happy. Give this with happiness, with thank for AllāhNone. AllāhNone make this, make me have this much money. And I have this worshipping, and AllāhNone will reward me more and more in ākhirah. This is a big thing, to have enough money to give zakāt, and to reach this reward from AllāhNone.

AllāhNone, don't make us like this, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
