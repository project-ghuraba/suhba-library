As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Mina l-mu’minīna rijālun ṣadaqū mā ‘āhadū Llāha ‘alayh” (33:23). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l- ‘aẓīm. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla praises the believers who keep their word. Keeping the promise is the attribute of the believer. It is the attribute of a believer, for a person to keep his word and to stand by his word, whenever he says something to someone. It is the specialty of the believer.

Our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu 'alayhi wa-sallam says, “When he [believer] speaks, he speaks the truth; when he promises, he keeps his promise; and when you entrust him with something, he is faithful to the trust.” This is the attribute of the believer. Believers should have this attribute. All Muslims must be like that, but believers must be more. Who are the believers? Those who follow the way of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu 'alayhi wa-sallam, those who strive to be closer to himNone. The people of ṭarīqah should have this attribute more. They should not infringe this attribute in any way. These attributes are the believer’s special and most distinctive attributes.

It is not accepted for someone who says, “I am in ṭarīqah” and lies, or “I am in ṭarīqah” and does not keep his word, or “I am in ṭarīqah” and betrays the trust. He is not in ṭarīqah. He is a person with the attribute of the hypocrite in ṭarīqah. Because the attribute of the hypocrite is, as our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says, “When he speaks, he lies; when he promises, he does not keep his promise; and if you give him a trust, he will not return that trust.” So, there are two types: you are either a hypocrite or a believer. There are two types. Therefore, keeping promises is important in every aspect. There are people who trust you and help you. You make those people look like liars. You will embarrass them too. Because they treated you as a respectable person and helped you, they tried to do you a favor.

If you break your promise, lie and commit all kinds of treachery, you are really a hypocrite, not a believer. You have the attribute of a hypocrite. Then, may AllāhNone protect you, you will become a complete hypocrite. Hypocrites are in the deepest place of hell. “Fī d-darki l-asfali mina n-nār” (4:145). HeNone says. Meaning in the deepest place of hell. There are ranks in hell. The lower you go, the deeper you go, the worse it gets. Therefore, do not lie for the sake of this world, do not promise for the sake of this world, do not betray for the sake of this world; be faithful to the trust. Especially you, O people of ṭarīqah. Because people trust those who are in ṭarīqah more. If someone trusts you and says, “This man is Muslim, he is in ṭarīqah,” they trust you and you then use the ṭarīqah, the ProphetNone and Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. to deceive people. May AllāhNone protect us from the evil of our ego. May AllāhNone protect us from falling into such situations. There are many people who do this nowadays, thinking they are being clever. But they are not clever, they are stupid. They have suffered more harm than benefit. Because in this world they don't have barakah in what they gain. In the hereafter, they will be responsible, they will be questioned, they will be punished. May AllāhNone protect us.
