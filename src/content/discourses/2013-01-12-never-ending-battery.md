We reached the morning in goodness, may we reach the evening in goodness. We reached the morning in our home, in our place, together with everything/everyone we have. Allah Almighty didn't leave us hungry or thirsty, He (swt) granted us all kinds of blessings in His Generosity. May our day be good, our doings be good. May our end be good. What can we do? This is the most beautiful dua/prayer.

We are Muslims. We may say the sign of Islam, Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Who says the Bismillah is Muslim. May Allah not make us forget the Bismillah. When you forget the Bismillah, you are of no use. There is an expression: they say "it is out of battery". When it is out of battery, what can you do with it? What is it called? When the battery is discharged, what can you do? You throw it away. You can't use it for a second time. And what we want is a battery that is never discharged, never runs out; A power, a love that will never run out. Say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim & your battery is charged. It is good for anything. Who says Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim can knock down a mountain because the battery in Bismillahir-Rahmanir-Rahim is never discharged. O son, it never discharged. There is no one who says Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim & doesn't get a result from what he does. "Kullu amrin lam yabda'a fihi bi Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim, fa huwa abtar. Prophet's (sas) word. "Kulli amrin", whichever action it is "lam yabda'a fihi bi Bismillahir-Rahmani r-Rahim", without saying the Bismillah "fa huwa abtar". Abtar means cut off. It is cut off, it has no result. Because it has ran out of battery-

The battery they use is discharged. But Allah Almighty granted Muslims such a power source that it is never ending. When you say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim your battery is full. Have no fear! Go on! Get out of your house, find a job & work too. Whatever it is that you need, you will find it. For this, in the old times, they would make it start with Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. The first thing that is taught to the students, children, is "Say O son, Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Rabbi yassir wa la tuassir, Rabbi tammim bil khair". Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. The Basmala is the never ending power source. Who says "I said Basmala & my work didn't succeed" lies. For all khair action, say the Basmala, you get its result no matter what. If you don't say, it will be like a horse whose shoes fell off- it can't run, it can't walk. Not even one who rides a horse, not even one who flies a plane, not even one who is in a rocket; all who have some kind of a ride & are looking for power-they are all zero.

If he says Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim, the rocket can't take off. The power & qudrah in Bismillah is in no any other thing. This is granted to us- the Bismillah is granted to us. If the power in Basmala al-Sharif kicked the earth it would be knocked out of its orbit. Therefore, this Basmala al-Sharif must be said alot. For whichever work it is, if one has an intention, he may say "O Lord, Bi Ismika, Bi Ismika Al 'Azim, Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. O our Lord, this work of ours is good. May You send us Support O Lord".

"Where is My support?" says Allah Almighty. Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim is the greatest source for you". If he says Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim he can take down a rocket. If he says Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim he can sink a fleet. If he says Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim the armies retreat. It is an astonishing power. There is an astonishing power in Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. They have forgotten this, shaytan made them forget. Everyone has become without Bismillah. They are without Bismillah when they are born, when they grow up. They are without Basmala wherever they go & none of their work succeeds.
