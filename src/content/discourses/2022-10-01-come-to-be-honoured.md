As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm.

“Inna l-‘izzata liLlāhi jamī‘an” (10:65), says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Dignity and honour are for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Those who are with AllāhNone attain dignity and honour. Every day during fajr prayer, in the du‘ā’ of qunūt, we recite: “Innahu lā yadhillu man wālayt wa-lā ya‘izzu man ‘ādayt.” Whoever is with YouNone will not be disgraced. And whoever is YourNone enemy will not achieve greatness. Greatness is for believers, for mu’minīn, for people who are on the way of AllāhNone. Dignity and honour are for them.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Wa-lā tahinū wa-lā taḥzanū wa-antumu l-a‘lawna in kuntum mu’minīn” (3:139). Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says, “Do not be upset. Do not be sad. You are on the highest level. You are on top.” Those who are the enemies of AllāhNone, who don’t accept the way of AllāhNone, are disgraced. Their worldly rank is nothing. They have no value. To show respect to them attracts the anger of AllāhNone. Wanting to respect someone for his worldly rank is wrong. It is not suitable for you. It is not acceptable for believers. To show respect to them and feel honoured by them, however high their worldly rank or however much they possess in this world, is not acceptable for Muslims. You can treat them like normal people. But they cannot be given the same honour as Muslims have. That dignity and honour can only be given to those who obey the order of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, who accept Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla and HisNone prophets. But even if the rest are seen as great in this world, as long as they are not on this way, as long as they are not on the way ordered by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, there is no greatness for them.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gave greatness only to our Holy ProphetNone and then to the other prophets. They are the most exalted ones. Their dignity and honour are permanent and everlasting. But as for the people who strive for this world, there is no dignity for them if they are not on the way of AllāhNone. They are not meant to have it. Those people are only great in the eyes of people who follow their egos and worldly desires. They see themselves as high ranking and important. But it is not so in the presence of AllāhNone and in the presence of the believers. They have no greatness in the presence of our Holy ProphetNone and the other prophets. They make themselves busy for a couple of days and feel important. But then they will understand their uselessness. So, Muslims and mu’minīn living in this world must not be cheated by the ranks and titles of the world. That is not greatness. The further people go away from AllāhNone, the lower they fall. They will become worthless and honourless. Therefore, AllāhNone is the greatest. For HimNone, this world is not how people claim it is.

We see some people have so much money, some people have so much wealth, and so on. And there are people who are very famous in their own countries and around the world. Sometimes people envy them, thinking they are great. They are not great. People who don't know what will happen to them tomorrow, or even the next minute, cannot be great. Those who are with Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla become great. You can admire them and show respect to them. There is no need to respect the rest. No need to respect those who are not on the way of AllāhNone. You will be misled if you respect them. You will commit a sin in some cases – in most cases.

Because such people, such types, have taken away people’s minds. These people they are respecting are not to be respected at all. They are famous. Let them be famous. Do you have to respect them? It is fine if they are on the way of AllāhNone. But people who do all kinds of shameful things and all kinds of evil cannot be respected. They are already showing what they are. No need to unmask them. Because this world has come to such a state... In the old days, they used to feel ashamed of their actions. But now they are doing their best to do more wrong, and to show it off to people. And people are even showing respect to them. Why? Because it appeals to their egos. The more it suits their egos, the more they love it. That love is wrong. It is bad love. You must love those whom AllāhNone loves and whom our Holy ProphetNone loves, in shā’a Llāh. May AllāhNone give understanding to people.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Inna l-‘izzata liLlāhi jamī‘an” (10:65). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘aẓīm. In Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n, AllāhNone saying, the most – the honour and glory and every high respect for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla; and for HisNone Messenger, for HisNone prophets; and for believers. This is what AllāhNone makes honour – for believer. Believers – they are honoured people, and not other. Who not believer, you cannot be... he cannot be honoured. Only believer, who believe and following prophets, all prophets. Most honourable one, Prophet Sayyidunā Muḥammad ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, and all prophets. The most honoured ones in crea[tion] – in this earth, and AllāhNone give them honour. And with this honour, they are honoured forever. Not like these people who we are living in this time. They giving people – cheated by people. They thought this is honourable people. No. Who not believe, he never, cannot be honourable one.

To be honourable one, there is [some]thing you must obey, you must do to be honoured one. If not, you are not honoured one. And everyday we read, for du‘ā’, for qunūt: “Innahu lā yadhillu man wālayt wa-lā ya‘izzu man ‘ādayt.” Meaning, who AllāhNone he is enemy for AllāhNone, he will never be honoured one. Not be at all honoured one. And who with AllāhNone, he be honoured one every time. This is – everyday we read this and we believe in this. So, believer, they are honoured one.

If they are not believer, they are not honoured – but we can be with them, we can deal with them, we can do everything with them. But we are not respect[ing] them, and we are not seeing ourself not honoured. We see ourself honoured one. This honour given by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, not by ourself. This is HisNone word. HeNone said also in Qur’ān, “Wa-lā tahinū wa-lā taḥzanū wa-antumu l-a‘lawna in kuntum mu’minīn” (3:139). Don't be sad. Don't be miserable, don't be afraid. If you are believer, you are higher, HeNone said. In this world, to be higher than others [is] to be a believer. Only with īmān (faith). No different between colour, no different between nations, nor between tongues or language. All this, not important. Like these crazy people, they doing racist: “I am from this race and this from that race...” This is not – no meaning, not honour for people. Honour only to be more near for AllāhNone, more accepting HisNone order, doing more good thing. To be more believer. This is for human being. Only with this. You can say... say or not say – but you must be sure, if you are believer, you are highest. This is order of AllāhNone.

Other one, if not believer... [if] becoming believer, okay – he is also highest. We are not like this time people, making racist: “You are white, you are brown, you are black, you are yellow,” to make difference. No. This is not justice. Because everybody, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla create them, cannot change. But this, you can change. If you are not believer [and] you become believer, you can be honourable, you can be higher than non-honourable, than non-believer. This is... Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone give this chance for everybody to be honourable.

Come to be honourable, and more higher stage from other people. Because everybody, they like to be higher. If you like... but you must love, like to do this with spiritual, and order of AllāhNone, and obeying AllāhNone. This is no problem with this. Problem with other. If you are white, you are make other people they are... you think you are higher than them; this is only foolishness and craziness.

So our, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we must be honour[ed], happy; even the worst, the poorest one from honourable one, he is higher than the most richest, most famous one non-believer. This is our belief, and Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone is telling us this. So, for dunyā don’t be worried, don’t be sad if you are believer. If you are not believer, you can be – you do anything, everything you can do – but we cannot respect you. We are not respect who is enemy for AllāhNone, for Prophets. And they are making invest[ment], every day bringing new thing to make people to follow them, this shayṭān people. AllāhNone protect us from their fitnah, from their harm. AllāhNone protect whole nation of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
