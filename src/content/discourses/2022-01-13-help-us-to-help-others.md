As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Alladhīna yabkhalūna wa-ya’murūna n-nāsa bi-l-bukhl wa-man yatawalla fa-’inna Llāha huwa l-ghaniyyu l-ḥamīd" (57:24). This is āyatu l-karīmah, the word of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla in Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n. Some people are stingy, and they order others to be stingy. And then they boast about it. AllāhNone doesn't love them. This group is among the groups disliked by AllāhNone. Even if they worship and pray, since they don't give in charity, they are among the disliked servants of AllāhNone. It is an illness of the ego. What the ego orders a person to do, is to not give anything away. "You gave too much. Why are you giving? Enough giving!" it orders. And many people accept that. They are stingy, but they only harm themselves, because they become from the disliked servants of AllāhNone.

The favour and the love of AllāhNone are the biggest grants to humankind. Because Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla has given us everything, when you give to others, you are only the means of giving. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla can give to someone directly. Just as HeNone has given to you, AllāhNone can give to that poor servant. However, HeNone gave it to you so you might gain thawāb (reward) and as a benefit to you. This is what Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla wants. But people say, "We already gave to this man once, ten times, fifteen times. It is enough. He is making it a habit." It is not a habit. AllāhNone is giving his provision through you, by means of you. That provision will bring you ten rewards for every act of charity; even more than ten rewards. Your beautiful charity and good deeds will be written for you. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla could give it to someone else. And that other person would benefit from it.

When you give charity, it is clear who needs it, who deserves it. When you give to them, don't put pressure on them by questioning them, or reminding them of your gift. Don't expect gratitude from them. If you give secretly, it is better. "Al-bakhīlu ‘aduwwu Llāh wa-l-karīmu ḥabību Llāh." The stingy one is the enemy of AllāhNone. And the generous one is the beloved servant of AllāhNone. May AllāhNone open people's minds so that they understand this. Because, as we said, money is something given by AllāhNone. All fortune and wealth are from AllāhNone. And poverty is from AllāhNone. When you understand that equilibrium, when you are a generous one, you will also gain. Because you will invest in ākhirah. You will invest your money in the bank of ākhirah. They invest money in banks here; eventually, they go bankrupt. Therefore, when you understand this and are able to help people, you should make shukr to AllāhNone for that opportunity. HeNone didn't leave us in need to others as they are. It is a great blessing that we should be thankful for. May AllāhNone let our good deeds be continuous, in shā’a Llāh. May HeNone not leave us to the bad characteristics of our egos.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying in Qur’ān, "Alladhīna yabkhalūna wa-ya’murūna n-nāsa bi-l-bukhl wa-man yatawalla fa-’inna Llāha huwa l-ghaniyyu l-ḥamīd" (57:24). Or I not remember exactly āyah, but saying Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla: there is people, stingy people. Stingy, and also they order to other people to be like them; to not give charity, to not help people, and also they are proud. And Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, huwa l-ghaniyyu l-ḥamīd. AllāhNone, He isNone – everything is in HisNone Hand. HeNone giving HisNone everything with HisNone Hand. HeNone the rich – Ghaniyy, rich meaning. Al-Ḥamīd – and HeNone like to be give for people. This is very important thing. When you give charity or this, you must thank AllāhNone; HeNone make you between this people and between HimNone.

Maybe you have money, you are rich. When you give people, don't be afraid it will be finish, because this coming from AllāhNone. And through you the provision of this people going to them. AllāhNone is able of course, no doubt, to give this people from HisNone provision without you also. But when you are between this, and this people, AllāhNone reward you. And their provision – don't think you are, by yourself you take this provision. AllāhNone make you, only to take benefit from this. Their provision coming from them to – from AllāhNone to you, and to them. So don't say, "This man, I give him ten times, I give him twenty times – What is this? What he is doing? He is not fed up from asking. I am fed up too much for me to this." If you have, don't say this. You are, you must be happy you are not in his position.

May AllāhNone, can be he is rich and you are poor and he – your provision come through him. So this, important thing for people, for Muslim who know, believe in AllāhNone and HisNone... for what provision, from where coming, from where going. You must be happy, AllāhNone make you, give you this chance to help people and to get reward. This is very important because many people, they thought they will collect money – not giving to anybody – and they will be happy. But what will happen, all your money it will be, stay in bank account, or in your iron safe. But for ākhirah you can send this, for real bank. In ākhirah, when you send for ākhirah, it is always safe. Nobody can steal you. Now, fashion in this government: "People, put your money in bank." After, they cannot take bank money from inside. But in, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, nothing like this. Every penny, giving ten times, hundred times reward for you and safe for you. Waiting for you in ākhirah.

But people, they thought, they by knowledge, by their ability they become rich, and they don't want to give for other people. No. This, AllāhNone make this, you "wāsiṭah" (intermediary) between people. And you must thank, be thankful for AllāhNone. Everytime you give something, you give: "Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, I can, I could give this" – without without be angry, without be unhappy. We must be happy when help people, when help poor people. Every time you help, you must be more happy, and it is real. People, they become more happy when help people. Not help their ego – which ego, only they want to not give anything, to be stingy. AllāhNone like people who give, but HeNone don't like stingy people. Even stingy people they pray and they make ‘Umrah, Ḥajj and fasting, but they are stingy. This also, HeNone is not happy with them because stingy. But maybe some people they not doing so much like them, but they are generous. Generous people, AllāhNone say, "Al-karīmu ḥabību Llāh wa-l-bakhīlu ‘aduwwu Llāh." Generous, he is friend of AllāhNone, beloved one for AllāhNone and stingy, he is enemy for AllāhNone. This is, must people know this.

And if – the people who has most fortune in the world, in the Muslim people. But Muslim people, they collect, collect, not giving anything for people. After this, unbeliever people they coming cheating them and take all of their money, and they are happy also for this. But they are idiot. They must be happy when they give charity for people, and people happy with them. Make praying for AllāhNone for them. But now people, they running after poor people to take money from them, to help themselves. This is very terrible thing, and these people they are, they don't have mind. Because from one praying for this poor people, everything they take – no benefit for them. And it will be disaster for them. AllāhNone make us to not be to like this people. AllāhNone help us to all time, to help people, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
