Let's tell a few words as it is important. today, it is Friday night. Also it is the night of ‘Īd, it is an honored night when two virtues have joined together. Tonight we have such an assembly here. Allāh Almighty has favoured and gathered us in the presence of this Sulṭān of ākhirah and those who are with him and has gathered us under the mercies coming down on them. People coming here tonight certainly have earned big delight and favours. It is not with money. It is the kindness of Allāh Almighty. If only whole Istanbul had a share from it. Certainly it is difficult for them to gather here physically.

However, it is possible for them to gather here in their hearts. If they send their souls here, they will be honored with the mercy and kindness, light and spirituality coming down here. Indeed, the alert believers ask for this in their talbiyah, takbīr, dhikr and du‘ā’ they make in waqfah "Yā Rabbi, let us be there too" starting from the day of ‘arafah when farḍ of ḥajj is done on the mount ‘Arafāt, then in Muzdalifah, then in Minā, then in Ka‘bah Mu‘aẓẓamah. "In fact, we are far away. But Yā Rabbi, You are Qādir Muqtadir. It is not hard for You to let us be in their location by the power of our spirituality or by "kun fayakūn" appointed from your world of angels, by a great angel. You create on our name who will stand there and say "Labbayk", make takbīr and be present with those praying there." When you say "I join them with my heart and soul", you'll be there too.

Likewise, it is a holy night. It is a humble, very humble dergah here. It is a humble dergah of Sulṭān of ākhirah. Of course, the 12 million Muslims of the city cannot collect their mukallafūn (accountables) here, but by heart. However, every Friday night is our holy night. Be alert in your heart and ask "Write us among Your special servants who are busy with Your dhikr, who gather with Your love, who are waiting for Your kindness, guidance and mercy, be it in Istanbul or anywhere between East and West, and particularly in Ka‘bah Mu‘aẓẓamah, Madīnah Munawwarah and Quds Sharīf which is Thālithu l-Ḥaramayni Sharifayn and other holy locations." It's not special for this night. If you ask for this on every holy night, you will have your share from those waves of blessings.

Blessings are good. Just like the water reaches the tree and gives life to it, blessings give life to our faith, it gives strength. Look for assemblies with blessings. If you can't find, turn towards Allāh in your heart and ask "Yā Rabbi, make me present in an assembly that's given Your Divine blessing, Make my heart, soul and spirituality be with them. Let me have from Divine blessing You give to them, Yā Rabbi. Increase my faith, make it abundant, Yā Rabbi" Pray so, ask for it. Yes. So it is a very holy night. Let a few words given to our heart be a short advice or ṣuḥbah.

O believers, the religion of Islām has brought all the goodness. It has brought in perfection all respect, worship and orders a servant can do and offer to his Creator. Then the goodness servants do for other servants, it's called ma‘rūf; the goodness done between the servants brings us the pleasure of Allāh Almighty. With a general saying, the attitude a servant has towards all creatures, the mercy he shows, the help and service he makes is a means to make Allāh Almighty pleased. It is a means for Allāh Almighty being pleased with us.

Hz. ‘Umar passed away. May Allāh be pleased with him. One of ṣaḥābah kirām saw him in a dream and asked him, "Yā ‘Umar, how did Allāh Almighty treat you?" Hz. ‘Umar said "My Creator stopped me and said "O ‘Umar, I showed mercy on you. I am merciful towards you. I will shower you with My mercy. I made you gain My mercy. Do you know that one day when you were walking in Madīnah Munawwarah asking about conditions of people you were khalīfah of, you passed through one district and saw a couple of children? A couple of small irresponsible children had tied a bird up from its leg with a string. They were leaving that bird so that it flew. It was trying, then they were pulling it back. The bird was trying to escape. Every time they were catching it by its foot.

When you saw it, you showed mercy to that bird I had created. You said "O children, come here. Can you give it to me? I will make you happy." "As you wish" The children didn't know you, "as long as you are making us happy, let us give it to you". You made those children happy. You took that bird I had created from them and set it free. Because you were merciful to that weak servant of Mine, I am merciful to you, O ‘Umar. Hz. ‘Umar, the best among ṣaḥābah kirām, the fortieth Muslim, the one showing Islām, Yā muẓhirū dīn al-Islām", He was called, when being greeted; "O the one who shows the religion of Islām openly, the second wazīr of our Prophet." He is the second wazīr and then he is the second khalīfah, the one who extended the light of Islām from China and the East till the Pacific ocean, the highest in worship and sincerity, love and service to the Prophet.

The highest among people after the Prophet is Hz. Abū Bakr, then comes Hz. ‘Umar. He didn't remember any of this much worship. "Only that day you showed mercy to that bird and set it free. Therefore, I am forgiving you." Allāh does what He wants. How can you make Allāh Almighty pleased? "You should be careful. Look where you least expect it." He said so to Hz. ‘Umar. "He treated me so. And He didn't count anything else." And the Prophet ṣalawātu Llāhu wa salāmu ‘alayh ordered with happiness "Irḥamū man fī l-arḍ yarḥamkum man fī s-samā’." It's ḥadīth sharīf.

Be merciful to those on earth so that Allāh whose ruling is the greatest and who is the Absolute Owner of all possessions be merciful to you. If you want Him to show mercy to you, show mercy to His creatures on earth. That's why I said there are two ways of worship of a servant and both make Allāh Almighty pleased. One is the first condition of Islām, to obey Allāh. And the second condition is to show love and mercy to His creatures. Both have reached its perfection in Islām. Then Allāh Almighty said "O My servants, be careful about it as much as you can. If you cannot do 100%, if you just do 1%, I will still be pleased. Just don't avoid what you can do." Also Islām has prohibited all types of evil. Whatever evil there is that can bring harm to humanity, don't say small or big or makrūh. All of it is prohibited.

There is no tolerance for evil in Islām. That's it. And you shouldn't tolerate evil as a Muslim. You shouldn't tolerate badness. You shouldn't tolerate oppression. This is it. No tolerance of badness for yourself and for others. We are in such condition tolerating everything. The great religion of Islām, asta‘īdhu biLlāh "Al-yawma akmaltu lakum dīnakum." (5:3) This āyah came as good tidings to the Prophet.
