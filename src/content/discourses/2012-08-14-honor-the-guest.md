As-Salamu Alaikum. Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fil jamia' Today's sohbat is about one of the noble manners of Islam, honoring the guest.

It is wajib/obligatory to honor the guest. The guest's right is 3 days, you should honor him. You should try to please him, should not ask anything from him. Meaning it can't be like "Give me money & I host you". You host him as much as you can, everybody hosts his guests as much as he can afford. A poor person who has nothing in his house does not have to borrow from people to host his guest. Allah didn't make this obligatory on people, didn't want to make trouble for them. So you offer the guest whatever you have.

The guest's right is 3 days. If he stays more than 3 days it becomes sadaqa/charity for you. For this, the old Kings who ruled with the rule of Allah, would build caravansaries for the travelers. So they would stay in them, eat & sleep & then would leave & continue their travel. And there was no payment required, they were doing this service to please Allah, to honor the guests.

Our Prophet (sas) has a Hadith which says "Let whoever believes in Allah & His Prophet honor his guest." So our ancestors considered this order and built guesthouses, caravansaries everywhere, where caravans passed by. There were guesthouses even in the cities, people would go & stay. And they had thought about every detail. The doors would be closed at night. Before the traveler leaves after fajr prayer, they would get up & eat & pray. And they would make everyone check their personal belongings before the doors open so that nothing is missing & he has no doubt inside. So that people won't take advantage of this service, because the ego can do anything. So everybody would leave pleased. Even if something happens, which usually never did, but no one could escape. Everybody there would look, check, return the trust & send them like this.

Their honoring of guests was this way. And Allah's pleasure was on them. And awliyas' dargahs, doors are open also. Guests can come & stay as long as they want. Some stay 3 days, some 5 days.. Even if they stay for months, all are built to please Allah only. They fulfill Alah's order & also perform nafila worship with this. It is sadaqa, zakat for them. All of us are guests of Allah. May Allah honor us as well insha Allah.

Today we speak about good attributes of Islam. One of them is to behave good for guest. Order of Prophet (sas) "who believe in Allah & Prophet, Last Day, must look after his guest." To look after, guest's right on the owner of house, 3 days. 3 days, he must look for him. And Allah (order), to be, give good thing for guest. And this is for everybody, from... If he is rich, he can give more, better service for him. If he is poor, Allah not give burden on people they cannot carry. If he is poor, he can give what he have. No need to take loan or to take something. What you have, give-he & his guest same. 3 days. After 3 days if he like, this is sadaqa for him-from him to guest. And this is, because very nice order, the old Muslim country, they have special place like hotel, or like fort. When people travelling from place to place, they was coming there night time for eating & drink & sleep. After fajr, they was preparing to continue their traveling. And this is, it was like fort, they were closing after maghrib or after isha. They were closing door.Not opening until after fajr, because people will go And they were thinking about everything. Even if it not happened, they were not opening door until saying for every people "are you ok? everything you have, look for your things." And when people saying "ok, everything ok" they open the door & send them where they want to go.

They was thinking for everything. Especially for safety of their life, for safety for their things, for everything. They was happy from order of Prophet (sas). And they was happy when guest coming to them & making this service. Even in, not in outside of city. Outside of city was many place where people going. And inside the city also. When guest coming from other place, they have also like this place, like inn. They can stay sometime, after they can go. And mashayekh, also they have, this is zawiya, dargah, also same. All people, they can come. They can stay 1 day, 2 days, 3 days, 1 week, 10 weeks, months.. It is ok. All they are guests. All they are Allah's guests.

When you behave good for your guests, Allah behave good for you also. This is very nice attribute. They not looking after benefit when they do this-they can find benefit from people who they are coming. Only for Allah to be happy with them. This is what they want only. Allah, insha Allah be happy with us, with to be together with guests every time insha Allah. Our Shaykh, Shaykh Nazim insha Allah give many years until Mahdi (as), all guests not be less, all time insha Allah we have guests, insha Allah. Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, Al Fatiha.
