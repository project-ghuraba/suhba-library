Allah showed something as beautiful to everyone. He showed beautiful things to everyone, but most people leave the truth and lean towards things that are not good.

Our Holy Prophet has a prayer (dua) as, “O Lord (Ya Rabbi), show us the truth (haqq) that is beautiful and grant us to follow it.” Because people might see things as truth, when in fact they are opposite turth, they are not the truth but are falsehood. They follow it, they favor it, and even sometimes give their lives in that bad way.

May Allah show us the right way. May we follow the right way and not leave it. That is a good dua. Inshallah it is granted to all of us.

Al-Fatiha. Hadrat Shaykh Muhammad Mehmet Adil (QS) 27 October 2015, Akbaba Dargah, Sabah Namaz
