A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah. Our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says, “Man ghashshanā fa-laysa minnā.” Whoever deceives us is not from us, says our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Of course, in the time we live in, everyone deceives people. They deceive them in every way. From the biggest to the smallest, there is no one who does not deceive. They trick people, they deceive. They do not do what they say. They deceive and think they have gained something. This world is a deception. That is not important. A man may have taken your money, your car, your place. Those are worldly possessions. They are not important. What matters is the hereafter. The one who deceives you regarding the hereafter – that is the danger; that is the worst one. The one who deceives people, appearing very respectable, in a very holy form before people, and then deceives them in matters of religion, and in matters of the ṭarīqah – such a person is the one our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam said is not from us. That is the essence. Because the way of our Prophet is clear. Whatever is good, he shows it; whatever is evil, he shows it. You must be as you are inside on the outside as well; not otherwise. If you appear like the people of ṭarīqah, wearing a jubbah, mā shā’a Llāh, a turban as wide as a tray, and a beard two hand-spans long, and then say, “I follow the Shaykh,” yet fail to obey any of the Shaykh’s commands or words, and not even doing what you yourself say – this is deception. They deceive people. Therefore, the one who deceives, above all, deceives himself. He cheats himself. Therefore, it is these people of whom our Prophet said: “They are not from us.” They have the attribute of hypocrisy. They have no īmān at all. If they had īmān, they would not deceive people. They deceive in matters of religion in order to gain benefit, they deceive people and profit from them. Those who deceive – they are not from us, says our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. The way of Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim is clear. After Mawlānā, many people have appeared. Therefore, not once, not twice, but again and again, we must remind our brothers and sisters from time to time. Do not think every man with a turban is a shaykh, do not think he is a walī. They say you should see everyone as Khiḍr, but do not think every man with a turban is a walī. For he deceives people with his turban. People are deceived by his beard, thinking him holy. Afterwards, they turn away from Islām. This is what they say about Muslims: “Look, this man with a turban and jubbah deceived us.” This is the worst thing. Deceiving people in matters of religion. And as we said, among the people, among the murīds, among the lovers, someone who has nothing, who says, “I am this, I am that” – the moment someone says, “I,” they have no value at all. We must be careful about this. Indeed, murīds, people are naive. Anyone can deceive them. Therefore, they must take care not to follow such people. They are everywhere, all over the world. They appear everywhere. “We are the deputies of Shaykh Effendi, Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh Hazretleri...” And there are also some people. They skip over Shaykh Baba, they skip over Shaykh Nāẓim Hazretleri, and say, “We are the followers of Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh ad-Dāghistānī Hazretleri.” Who are you that you could understand who Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh Hazretleri is? Surely in this too they are deceived even more. There are many such people. May Allāh protect us. May Allāh protect us from their evil. They have no hope of guidance, because such a shayṭān has mounted upon them that guidance does not come near them. That’s why staying away from them is goodness for a person, salvation for a person. If they come near, they confuse minds. They make waswasah (whisperings). May Allāh protect us. Some naive people think they are something. They follow them, and in vain chase after them and waste their lives. May Allāh protect us from their evil. From the evil of shayṭān, and from their evil, may Allāh protect us.
