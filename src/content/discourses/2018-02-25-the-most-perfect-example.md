As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa Raḥmatu Llāhi wa Barakātuhu.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

"Wa man yuridu Llāhi bi-khayrihi yufaqqahu fī d-dīn." It is ḥadīth sharīf of our ProphetNone. If AllāhNone wishes goodness for someone, HeNone teaches him religion. The more he studies it, the more goodness he will reach. Shukr to AllāhNone, all of us Muslims – AllāhNone has wished goodness for us. Even if little, they know the religion. They obey the religion and its orders. They say "I am Muslim" and do as much as they can. Certainly who does more, AllāhNone wishes more for them. They reach the higher stations. And the highest station is our Prophet'sNone. Then comes that of other prophets, then come ṣaḥābah and awliyā’. They are the people who have reached this favour from AllāhNone and know its value. "We reached this station."

There are some people not on the way, who say, "We reached it." There is an āyah: "Wa ‘bud Rabbaka ḥattá ya’tiyaka l-yaqīn" (15:99). Worship AllāhNone until you find the truth. And these ones say, "We found the truth, so there is no need to worship." That means AllāhNone doesn't wish goodness for them. Who is wished goodness is like our Shaykh because he knows the greatness of this favour. He did all kinds of worship, as much as he could and didn't miss anything. His farḍ was complete and he never missed sunnah. And mustaḥabb... Some things are farḍ for our ProphetNone and are not farḍ for hisNone ummah. Mashāyikh made it a duty for themselves that everything our ProphetNone did they did and never missed it. They left ḥarām and apart from ḥarām, they left ḥalāl as well.

I am thinking now, remembering Mawlānā. 35-40 years ago Mawlānā went to a dentist in Lebanon and said he had only few teeth left in his mouth and one of them was completely decayed. When a dentist wanted to pull it out, brothers asked it for themselves. But the tooth broke into three. Mawlānā became angry and said, "It is because of you. It's wrong." Mawlānā never had his half teeth filled. When they deteriorated, he had them pulled. In fact, fixing teeth is not a sin or ḥarām but the exactness of Mawlānā shows how attentive he was regarding Allāh'sNone orders. Since he was fifty-something, Mawlānā wore dentures.

He devoted himself so much on the way of AllāhNone. Even when something was doubtlessly ḥalāl, while some imāms, we mean imāms of madhhab, disputed if it was permissible, Mawlānā left them all. He never stepped out of the right way. He showed the right way to all of us. But he was very strict for himself and very merciful for people. He had alot of tolerance. Sometimes he said – one word is enough: "Say lā ilāha illāh Llāh to join Islām." But for himself, as we said, he never made an excuse.

Their methods are for their egos. Why is it so? Because the ego is an enemy. He treated it as an enemy. And he treated Muslims and believers with mercy so that they loved and accepted Islām, so that they are on this way. The things he suffered are not not one in thousand – we don't have even one in hundred thousand. If we had, we would start complaining immediately. Therefore, remembering these holy ones brings mercy to our assembly.

Shukr to AllāhNone, we are near him and there are many memories, millions of memories. Because everyone has different memory of Mawlānā. When we remember him, it becomes a means for mercy, a means for mercy for us, for our assembly. and for our family. Our love will increase. When love increases, people are with the loved ones. Therefore, this love is a big favour from AllāhNone. May AllāhNone always let us love this right way. May HeNone let us love HisNone Awliyā’ and the ProphetNone, in shā’a Llāh. May their love be our first priority. May it fill our hearts, in shā’a Llāh. The love of dunyā is useless. It has no benefit. It is nothing but darkness, nothing but burden. When in ākhirah, if you bring what you loved, it has no benefit. "I loved it and didn't want to share." But when you have the love of Awliyā’, you share it with others too. Your love towards others increases. It becomes useful for them as well. May AllāhNone give this love to all of us, in shā’a Llāh.

Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla for, ProphetNone saying: "Who want good for servant, AllāhNone make him to know about HisNone religion, to be knowledgeable in religion." How to be knowledgeable? To accept the way of AllāhNone – way of AllāhNone shown by our ProphetNone. HeNone show us and everything in AllāhNone Will. If HeNone will for us good thing HeNone make us to follow HisNone way. Other people, other people, HisNone will not to follow right way. What we will do? We object? We say "we not accept"? We cannot say anything because HeNone is our Lord. Only what you can say, not to make any discussion, to ask and accept what HeNone gives you. And when HeNone give you something, you must appreciate this.

The most one who AllāhNone give him all good thing – our ProphetNone. HeNone is the top. HeNone, AllāhNone He want for himNone the best for ProphetNone, our ProphetNone. After all prophets also because AllāhNone He want, HeNone choose them and then make HeNone make them the best between other creature. After – ṣaḥābah, disciple of ProphetNone. After them ‘ulamā’, awliyā’u Llāh – real scholar, real knowledgeable people, awliyā’u Llāh. Because they are beloved for AllāhNone and AllāhNone love them and will for them the best between people. And they are knowing this. What they, AllāhNone give them and they are happy and they are doing their best to be to thank for AllāhNone for what HeNone give them. They are giving whole, whole their life to thanking Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla, our Lord. They are doing everything HeNone want, HeNone ordered – especially following ProphetNone. ProphetNone he said something obligatory for whole nation. And there is many things obligatory for ProphetNone, not for us. We if we do, like sunnah, it is for us but for ProphetNone it is obligatory.

Awliyā’u Llāh also they are following ProphetNone. What heNone done, everything all awliyā’u Llāh they are following but for us the most perfect example – Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim. He was real following every step for ProphetNone. He do all sunnah mustaḥabb. And most of awliyā’u Llāh they was, of course they are not doing any sin. They trying to avoid sin. With any very small sin thing they don't like. They not doing but... and they are leaving the suspicious thing even they are leaving – also some who it is not forbidden, you can do it. And you can do it – like we give example for Mawlānā – he was never making fixing his teeth, [so as] not to put inside anything because some ‘ulamā’ saying this it is maybe for wūḍū’ not good. And he was, when finish his teeth, taking out, taking out. And since maybe fifty year old he was, he has dentures.

And the I remember in Lebanon once, he one teeth. It was last one. It was that time he, they tried to take it out in one dentist and he was good dentist. and he is follower, murīd. They try, try, and there was – what happen there was three other follower. Each one he said, "I want take this. I want take this." They nearly making discussion and this teeth, subḥāna Llāh, when he try it take out, broke in three part. Mawlānā was angry for this people said, "Look what you done." And this one and he no any problem to fix your teeth and here.

But Mawlānā for himself he was very tough for himself because he is seeing his ego like enemy. But for other people, he was real soft one. You cannot find softer then him. For follower, for non follower even, he speak to people – you cannot find such a man polite like him. And people when they speak to him they was happy with him. Believer or non-believer or anyone – they was attracted by him – light and his kindness. This is what awliyā’u Llāh appreciate what AllāhNone give them for their... choose them they are knowing and they are happy with this. And they try to thank HimNone. AllāhNone give them higher and higher maqām. And when we are mentioning them, our love become more and more for them. And love it is important for us because with love you survive from bad end and to be in Jannah with them also. And we are asking AllāhNone to give us this love and we are thanking HimNone for choosing us and make us to be following ProphetNone and awliyā’u Llāh and asking knowledge from them.

This is all sign of because AllāhNone He is choosing us and we must be happy and we must be thankful. Because when you thanking HimNone giving more barakah more love for us. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, because if HisNone will, you cannot say "how HeNone can make us to like this other people not like this?" This is non-believer question. Our, we cannot question our Lord Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla. We must be good behave especially for our LordNone. And thanking for HimNone, al-ḥamdu liLlāh Rabbi l-‘ālamīn.

Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
