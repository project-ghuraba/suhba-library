As-Salamu Alaikum. Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fil jamia' In this world, it is possible that anything can happen, a person can fall into any situation. What is important is to stay firm in your iman/belief.

All kinds of different situations, things that one can never imagine are happening in the world. We see it everywhere. What is important is belief, as we said. If you have tawakkul/reliance on Allah & take things accordingly they can pass by easier. A person with no iman is the one who has no hope. A person who has iman doesn't lose hope in Allah. Allah's Mercy can reach man at any time.

Luqman Hakeem Hz, there is uncertainty whether he was a Prophet or not but I think it is said that he was not Prophet. His name is also mentioned in Quran. His skin was blackish. One day a man lost his slave. And this slave who ran away looked exactly like Luqman Hakeem. In old times slaves would sometimes run away. This man caught Luqman Hakeem saying "you are my slave"&made him work a year. He tortured him. He made him build him a house.

After a year by Allah's Wisdom, the slave who ran away regretted & returned to his Master. When his Master saw him, he realised his mistake. He felt very sorry, apologized to Luqman Hakeem. He kissed his hands, feet saying "forgive me". Luqman Hakeem said "You tortured me, insulted me every way possible for a year. There is no way to forgive this with two words but still I am forgiving you for Allah. I also learned a lot of things from you in this one year. You got yourself a house for free and for me- I also had some slaves & I also was not treating them well enough. I was thinking highly of my ego.." So speaking like this despite all the torture he went through he still thanked him. Of course he went through all kinds of torturing in that year-would handle his huge house by himself.

So the great Luqman Hakeem went through all this torture & still thanked that man saying "I learned a lot from you in this time". He was like this. So people, when they fall into a bad situation they should not lose hope. It is Allah's Will. May Allah not make all this we suffered be for nothing. May He reward us for it. And may we learn a lesson, a wisdom from it, receive rewards so that we learn something with it. May it not pass by for nothing. This is also a very important thing. Because anything can come to people- any condition, anything can happen.

We have a Turkish saying, our ancestors said "Everything hangs by a (cotton) thread". What they mean with cotton thread is that, it can break any time. Although now strong threads are also available-nylon threads never break even if you pull. But cotton thread can break easily. Therefore we should thank Allah in every situation- May Allah change it for good. May we receive rewards & benefits from it inshaAllah.

Today we speak about mankind, life of people. In this world everything is possible. Everything can come to people- to man, to woman, to children who is living. They are in condition any minute something can happen for them. And this is, we must be when.. insha Allah nothing happen but when something happen you must take, that this from Allah coming. You must not be hopeless. Our hope - Allah can help us, every time help us. Even when some bad thing coming, also this- something happened & Allah reward us for this. The believer, this is good point for believer people. They have hope. But unbeliever, no hope for them at all. Believer people can take lesson from everything.

Even there was Luqman Hakeem (The Wise). Some people said he is Prophet. But most (say) he is not. He was like doctor. He know every kind of medicine. Every kind he know. Even medicine, when he go inside his room, tell him this: "I am for this illness, for that illness". He was helping people. Once, because he is dark colored, like black - One man, his slave ran away. And this man catching Luqman Hakeem. He said "Why you run away, you are my slave. Come." And he take him by force to his house, to his farm. One year keep him there. They make him (do) every difficult, everything. Very difficult time he had in this one year. And make him to build one house for him also.

After one year Allah make this slave who was running away he is coming saying "No, I must come to my lord" & he come. He said sorry. I am coming. This Lord see this, he understand his mistake. And he was very regretful & apologized from Luqman Hakeem & kissing his hand, feet. He said "Sorry, don't blame me. I am very bad man..." Luqman Hakeem said "Of course this, in one year I suffer very much. I never suffer in life more than this one year, what you do for me. It is not easy to say two word 'I am sorry' but also I forgive you." But he said, I suffered too much. "But in this one year also, you get one house free. I get many wisdom, many thing I never know about. I have also slave, I have servant. So I was not so good for them. In this one year I have time, experience. I have never had like this." Because he is famous & he is..But Allah's wisdom,He(swt) make him keep him Because people know him but Allah make him to be one year with this man. This is from Allah.

Everything from Allah coming. So everything is possible in this world. But Allah give believer people reward for this all, not coming empty. All fulling in their good work side. And everything can be in this world. In Turkish saying they say everything by a cotton thread - It is only hanging by this. Cotton is very weak, every time can cut. You don't know when what will happen. After one hour you cannot know what will happen. Now they have it of course plastic, polyester, strong but it is not polyester only cotton. Everything can happen. So don't be hopeless & don't be against Allah's Will. What come from Allah, say Alhamdulillah. Alhamdulillah, insha Allah changing to good.

Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, Al Fatiha.
