Shaykh Mehmet: Madad O Great Saints of the Naqshbandi Order, madad O S. Abdullah Fayiz Daghestani, madad Mawlana S. Muhammad Nazim al Haqqani, dastur! Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fil jamia' Our way is through association & blessings are in the gathering. May the blessings be in the gathering insha Allah.

Today we will speak about making promises. It is from the signs of a mu'min- if he makes a promise, he keeps it. And the signs of munafiqs; when they make a promise, don't keep it. They lie. They don't inform before they come/ visit. These are the signs of munafiq. And the people of this time, when they make a promise- knowingly or unknowingly, they don't give any importance to it. They don't care at all about keeping their promises. However it is the biggest distinction/difference between a mu'min & munafiq/hypocrite in Islam. Munafiq never keeps his promise. Mu'min keeps it for sure.

But like we said, people have become such that, there are very few who keep their promises. Therefore people don't give importance to this. Not even our people of this time: Arabs, Turks, Kurds, Laz people, Circassia, none of them.. There is nothing as "keeping one's promise" left in this dunya anymore. Before they said "one's word is like a warranty", but not like this anymore. Why does this happen like this? Because of lack of iman/faith & lack of manners. Who calls himself a Muslim does the same thing too. Because (of the signs) of the end times, we see many things...

Being Muslim is not only about praying or not praying. Many times we see men who have big beards & are dressed according to sunnah but not true to their word. He says, "you will work for me & get paid this day". When you go to him that day, he says "I am busy today, etc.." He has money with him but doesn't want to give. He thinks it's the trade of the day- if he gives immediately it's no good. We are saying this openly for everyone, from youngest to oldest. One time, we heard many times at that time.

Politicians, they already make many promises when it is election time. Very few of them are kept. Of course,there is no one who can please all these people other than Allah. When you make many big promises, of course you can't keep them later. At some time, 15-20 years ago, one politician came out & said something which turned out to be a complete lie. That man said"I will give you two keys". It was not a man, a female politician. "One is the car key, the other is the home key". What she meant with it was, we will give you a car&a home. Leave aside the car, didn't even give the key. At least give the key & keep your promise, but no. She didn't do that either.

It says "Rijalun Sadaqu Ma 'ahadu Al-Laha 'alayhi" (33:23) 'ahad/Oath means, it is stronger than a promise. Whom they refer to by "rijal" is, they are men. No offense but ladies, it doesn't matter if they make a promise or not anyways. In Quran it doesn't say "when ladies make a promise..", says "rijal/men" And the lady who keeps her promise is like a man. When it means with 'ahad, "Rijalun Sadaqu-They didn't lie, they kept their promises". What was that big oath? They went for jihad & sacrificed themselves. Some are martyred, some are still waiting-none went back on their promise, it says.

What they meant with "Rijal" is men, like "Rijalullah/Men of Allah". It does not even necessarily point to men here, it means who doesn't keep his promise is not man. Weigh your word before you make a promise. If you can do it, then say. You make a promise & may keep it, but if things like death, illness, etc.. happen which are out of your control it is still like you kept your promise but death prevents it. Or, you really have a big excuse all of a sudden, it is ok. But if you make a promise & don't keep it for no reason it's considered to be from the signs of munafiq &you fall into sin at that time.

Today we speak about promise. This day's people, they make promise everywhere but nobody care about promise. Before, making promise, it is like you sign on thing. But now, nobody looking for this. And promise, it is very important in Islam. It is different between believer & hypocrite. Hypocrite's signs - to not keep their promise. And they not speak true, they lying. Many things for hypocrite, but this is in every Hadith; first thing to not be liar in your promise. And nowadays people, they are...

Because nobody keep their promise. They think it is easy thing; "We can say this & it is ok. Nobody keep their promise". But this is wrong. Because Allah (awj), He said for who keep promise they are men- men of Allah (awj). Because ladies, they are weak. They are, you can't.. You know, they speak too much. Cannot catch if she speak, promise or not promise or something. But even in lady who she is keeping her promise, she is like man. Because Allah said "Rijalun Sadaqu Ma `ahadu Al-Laha `alayhi" (33:23) Not say woman, just man. And this is not for feminist or things..This is Allah. Allah, He put this rule. You can't say "we are woman, we can", no. Most of women, they are not keeping their promise. But now, this man who is not keeping promise, they are also like woman. All like woman. GrandShaykh S. Abdullah, everytime when making, after sohbat kissing his hand, he said for all this people-"bihinna bihinna bihinna- like ladies, ladies.."

It was like this. Because this promise not only who believer, or not believer, or who is carrying sunnah. We see many people with beard and Sunnah custom, all of them not keeping promise also. How is not keeping promise? Many time they have something to pay or to work or to pay for other, not paying in time. They saying "come after 10 days". This man coming, he say "no, no today we don't have this". But even he has in his pocket, but it is this times, if he give like quickly, it is shame for him. Because he is thinking it is not clever when he do. He must be, to do his promise, taking one time, three time, four time to come this man "give me, give me, give me". This is like this. Not only who not praying or not believer doing this. Even Muslim who wearing Sunnah or have beard, everything, 100-90% they doing this. And it is very important thing this, to keep promise. And after promise, there is more stronger than promise-Oath.

The Ayah "Rijalun Sadaqu Ma `ahadu Al Laha `alayhi Fa minhum Man Qada Nahbahu Wa Minhum Man Yantaziru" (33:23). This is man, people- man or woman also, they say keep their promise. And they are fighting with Rasulullah or for Islam, & they are not coming back. It says they are fighting, and some of them they become shahid, some of them waiting, and not coming back. Even they are also, they not afraid from death, or to be shahid. They are Allah lovers.

Other, they have to not keep promise, it is sin, not joke. Sin & shame for people. Now people, they are used to give promise. You look before you give promise; if you can do this promise, give. If no, nobody oblige you to give promise. But when you give promise, you must do it. Unless you have very strong, maybe you died or illness or something you can't do, this is from Allah. But to give promise and to lie, it is very bad manner. Especially in politics. Because now in these days, Shaykh Effendi - Allah give him long life - he said, not like democracy. This democracy- it is most who politics people they giving promise before election: "we will do this, we will do that, we will do that..."

Even there was one, before 15 or 20 years in Turkey, one politician said "I will give two keys." What is this? People they are saying "what is this?" And after, she is saying "One for car, one for house". This is ok you say only two keys, ok for two keys. Even you not give this two key also. She will give this two keys because no car, no house. But she didn't say, I will give car or..She said two keys. Even the two key also not giving. This is how it is funny & lie, you can see.

Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, Al Fatiha.
