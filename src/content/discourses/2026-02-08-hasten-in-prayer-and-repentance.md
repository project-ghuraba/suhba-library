As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

When the salawāt [by the mu’azzin] is recited, the salāt and salām after announcing the deceased, they say , “Hurry up to the perform prayer before the time passes, and hurry up in repentance before death comes.” [Ḥadīth] Hasten to pray. Pray before the time for prayer passes. Or if you couldn’t pray it, make up for it (Qada’). Do not leave it completely or let it go in vain. Nothing goes in vain. There will certainly be an accounting for it in the Hereafter.

Those who didn’t pray will be made to pray in the Hereafter. Each prayer is equivalent to 80 years of life. Here, 80 years is essentially the entirety of a person’s life. If they didn’t pray it here, they can pray in the Hereafter. Even if they didn’t pray for thousands of years, they will certainly be held accountable in the Hereafter.

Secondly, “Hasten to repent before death.” Repent before you die. Repent and seek forgiveness from Allah. Because there is no forgiveness after death. If you committed a sin or fault while in this world, whatever you did, there is a solution for it while you are still here. Sharīʿah clearly explains how to be forgiven from every fault. But the important thing is to repent. To repent before death.

Of course, as we said, when repenting, there are certain rights and obligations upon others to consider, and you repent accordingly. The best is, every evening, as our Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam said, “I repent and seek forgiveness seventy times a day.” When the Companions said, “You have no sins, Allah created you without sin,” he said, “I want to be a servant who is grateful to Allah.” By repenting, one would’ve made shukr to Allah.

Therefore, it is necessary to constantly repent and seek forgiveness every day. Let our sins not remain for the hereafter. So that nothing remains on our necks after we die. That’s why, repentance is important. Like prayer, repentance is very important.

These people think that nothing will happen to them. They commit all kinds of evil and filth. Then, they think they are saved. No one is saved without repentance. If you repent, then you are saved. But if you persist and continue, then your punishment will be severe. They say, “My life is ruined.” That’s the actual life that will be ruined forever. They will certainly suffer the punishment for the evil they committed and the evil they taught others. Whoever they are. There’s no distinction between big and small. Unless they repent, they will certainly suffer that punishment.

So, may Allah forgive us. We say, “Tawbah, Astaghfirullah.”

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
