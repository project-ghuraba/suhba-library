As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,
وَٱلْفَجْرِ 
وَلَيَالٍ عَشْرٍ
“Wa l-fajr. Wa layālin ʿashr.”
“By the dawn; By the ten nights.” (Qurʼān 89:1-2). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla swears by these ten days, the ten days of Dhū al-Ḥijjah. He honors these blessed days by swearing upon their name. There are certain special days and nights like this. These are days and nights that Allah ‘Azza wa-Jalla has bestowed upon our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam and his Ummah. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla has granted these days to His servants so that they may receive more blessings and earn more rewards. These days are blessed for Muslims, believers, and those who know the right way.

Shukr to Allah, today is the first day of Dhū al-Ḥijjah; these blessed days extend until the tenth day. Those who wish can fast during these days. This is a very good deed and its reward is great. Those who can, can fast until the ninth. Or, one can fast only on the eighth and ninth days; which must certainly be done. It is neither farḍ nor wājib, but it is a Sunnah, a gift from Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. It is necessary to appreciate and value it.

Some people confuse farḍ and sunnah acts. They neglect the farḍ and perform the sunnah act instead. Some don’t fast Ramaḍān but fast during Dhū al-Ḥijjah or Muḥarram. However, no sunnah can replace a farḍ.

Therefore, one must be careful. While you think you are doing good, you are actually committing a sin by abandoning a farḍ and performing a sunnah instead. Therefore, one must be careful. Allah has bestowed a gift upon you in both.

First, you must fulfill the farḍ. If you then perform the sunnah and the nāfilah, you will have gained twice. But according to their own understanding, they say, “These days are very virtuous. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla has revealed their virtue in Qur’ān Karīm, so I’ll just do this. The rest is unimportant,” and they do whatever comes to their mind. That’s how shayṭān can deceive people.

Even if you perform Sunnah throughout your whole life, they can never replace one farḍ. Not only can they not replace it, but there is also accountability for abandoning it. There is a punishment for this in the Hereafter. May Allah protect us.

May Allah make these days full of barakah and blessings for us. Most of the Ḥajjīs have gradually departed, and some are still on their way. May their Ḥajj be accepted and easy, inshā’Allāh. May the barakah of these days be upon us all. May it be upon the entire Islamic world. May Allah protect us from such deviations, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
