As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
وَمَآ أَرْسَلْنَٰكَ إِلَّا رَحْمَةًۭ لِّلْعَٰلَمِينَ
<br>“Wa-mā arsalnāka illā raḥmatal li-l-ʿālamīn”<br>
“And We have not sent you (O Muḥammad) except as a mercy for all the worlds.” (Qurʼān 21:107). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla sent our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam as a mercy to humanity, to the universe, to everything. There is no higher rank than that. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla showed mercy to people for his honor. He sent down mercies because our Holy Prophet’s foot touched the ground. He bestows all kinds of beauty upon us for his sake. 

Shukr to Allah, this gathering is also for the sake of Allah and for the love of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. This is the greatest gain. The things you gain in this world should be for the sake of Allah. Whatever you gain is given by Allah. It is necessary to know this. It is for the sake of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam that He grants us the opportunity to do good and to follow His way.

Shukr to Allah, this gathering has also come about through this means. We have brothers who have come from far away. There are scholars, there are mashāyikh. They have all gathered here for the pleasure of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla, for the love of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. That love, with Allah’s permission, is what will save us. The love of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, our love for him, the love of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla is achieved through him. 

They say there is no intermediary. They say don’t make the shaykh the intermediary, but it’s impossible without him. If it’s only about your own ego, there will neither be love nor affection. What they call love is only self-interest. And when it’s self-interest, it’s not love. That’s not love, that’s self-benefit. Love is being with the servants whom Allah loves, for the sake of Allah, and following the same way with them, the way that is the love and affection of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam.

The way of our Prophet is what the ṭarīqahs show. Some say that ṭarīqahs are not necessary. They are necessary. They are not necessary for shayṭān. They are necessary for the believer. Ṭarīqah are a great force to show the way, to be on the way, and to not stray from this way. It is necessary for a person to protect their faith. 

Shukr to Allah, ṭarīqahs will exist until the Day of Judgment, with Allah’s permission. They will exist forever. Until the time of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. Of course, the true way is shown by Mahdi ʿalayhi s-salām, the Shaykh who will be the leader of the ṭarīqah. When he comes, all the ṭarīqahs, that way, will be illuminated. Everyone will be on that way. Before that, there are many ṭarīqahs and many madhhabs. When Mahdi ʿalayhi s-salām comes, people will purely see them all, certainly as the only truth. They will follow that way. After that, it will be as it was in the time of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. Then the Day of Judgment will come. Before that, Mahdi ʿalayhi s-salām and ʿIsā ‘alayhi ssalām must come to bring people, the whole world, to the way of truth. After that, whoever wants to can do what they want. There won’t be any opportunity anyway. People, those who stray from the way, will be ruined then.

May Allah keep us all on this way. As we said, those who are on this way are fortunate people. It is a grace and favor from Allah. We must appreciate its value. We must remain steadfast on the way, inshā’a Llāh. May Allah bless it. 

This night is the main night of Mawlid al-Sharif. It begins after Maghrib. Tomorrow is Mawlid day, but of course, our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam was born at Fajr prayer. Therefore, this blessed day, the 12th of Rabīʿ ul-Awwal, begins with Maghrib prayer and continues until tomorrow at Maghrib. Today is its eve. That too is blessed. All these days are blessed. They are blessed days because they remind us of our Prophet. We must show respect to these days.
