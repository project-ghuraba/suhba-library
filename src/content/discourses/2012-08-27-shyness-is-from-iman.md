As-Salamu Alaikum. Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fil jamia' Today we will speak about haya/shyness. "Shyness is part of the faith" says the Prophet (sas), "al haya min al iman".

And what is shyness? It means ashaming from doing bad things. Haya & being ashamed is almost the same. Shyness is from the most liked character,the best attributes of Islam. Why is shyness good? Because they say for evil people "they have no haya, no shame". And no shame.. Again the Prophet (sas) said, "if you have no shame, then do as you please- Idha lam tastahi fasna' ma shi'ta" Shout, yell, swear on people, get naked, go around as you like, do all the bad, dirty things... Who has no shame does like this. Shyness prevents all this.

First of all, shyness prevents this, doing bad things. Who has shame does not drink, get drunk. He is shy from disgracing himself so he doesn't do that. He doesn't seize someone's property because he is shy from that man coming to him later on & saying "you seized my property" & disgracing him. Shyness prevents him from such things.

Of course people of this time, most of them don't see shyness as a good thing. They say "Don't be shy, ask for your right! Do this, do that". Of course that is different but still a person who is shy is better than a person who is not, who has no shame. Even if he is oppressed, it doesn't go to waste on Allah's side.

First of all, shyness is from the attributes of Allah. As of shyness, "I am shy to torment My old believing servant" Allah said. For a person who spent all his life with worship Allah (awj) said "never. I am shy doing it" Therefore shyness is from the attributes of Allah.

But one should not be ashamed of Haqq, that is something else. And in another Holy Verse, it says Allah (awj) is not ashamed to make an example of a mosquito. (2:26) So He (swt) is not ashamed, because it is not something to be ashamed of. What they call mosquito, ignorant people think of it as a small thing. It doesn't even weigh half gram. If you weigh 10 of them, it is not even half gram. However it is almost the same as an elephant, has a trunk & everything. It also has wings & flies. Allah (awj) makes an example of it. Therefore when you say the word of Haqq, don't be ashamed. Whatever you can say, whatever you can manage, say that much. No need to be ashamed if you are not able to say much. Even if you say a word in the way of Haqq, it is enough. But what should you be ashamed of? Things to be ashamed of are obvious. Be ashamed of the things that should be ashamed of. For other things, again who is shy keeps quiet, doesn't say anything. That is still good but there is permission to say in the way of Haqq.

Today we speak about haya, meaning shyness. Shyness, it is good attribute from Islam. They say "shyness, it is from belief- real belief/iman". Who has this attribute Allah give him good attribute. Because why people shying? Because when they do bad thing, people-normal people, they are shy, in Islam, of course. But who not in Islam.. If you are not shy, there is hadith "you can do what you want." Because, of course in outside Islam belief, they are making very bad thing & they are not ashaming, ashaming from this. But if Muslim, he will do to drink alcohol, he will be ashamed. If he make, steal from people, he will be ashamed; if he catch him, he afraid to be, he will be ashamed with this.

For this, shying it is good attribute in Islam. But in other culture, they have different thing. Even they are not ashamed, they do every badness & even they make regularly with rule, this country. We don't want to say what they do in this lecture because we don't want to make it so low. For this is, "al haya min al iman" Shy from iman, it is enough to say this. We don't want to say what they do, especially in western country. It is enough for Islam to this, to be know what you must ashamed from it, and you are not ashamed from it. It is, even in church they doing this thing. They make it regular. We don't want to say what this is, everybody know what I am speaking about.

And to be shy, it is even from Allah attribute also. There is Hadith, it said Allah shying to from His mu'min, believer old believer servant to say anything for him. Only He make him in jannah. After when he become old in this Islamic rule, He (swt) is ashamed. This is also from Allah's attribute.

But another also, he said in one Quran Verse, Allah not ashamed to make example, to give example from mosquito. Because that time they was laughing, this ignorant people when Prophet (sas) giving in Quran for mosquito. Because they don't know how it is, very small, maybe 10 of them it is not half gram. But even in this, it is same like elephant, even more. He has wings also. For this, to say right thing don't be ashamed. Ashamed when you do bad thing, for this, to be shy it is from Islamic attribute. It is prohibiting you or forbidding you to do bad thing. Because if you are doing bad thing, people they will be, you will be ashamed between people; "you do this, you that & that". So you ashamed, you don't do this. But who is losing to do this, they are bad people. In Turkish they say "yaramaz", not good people. They do anything; they shout, they want, they do things..They are not ashamed. This is not good people.

For this, Alhamdulillah as we said even this attribute, it is enough for whole, another culture to see, how it is Islam, high. Other now, because now, real religion now only Islam. And it is enough for whole mankind, this. Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, Al Fatiha.
