As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm.

“Alā inna awliyā’a Llāhi lā khawfun ‘alayhim wa-lā hum yaḥzanūn. Alladhīna āmanū wa-kānū yattaqūn” (10:62-63). Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla’s beloved servants, HisNone Awliyā’, have no fear and no sadness. They are with AllāhNone. They are always with AllāhNone. They are the faithful ones, says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. They have both faith in and fear of AllāhNone, and they are merciful to people. These are the Awliyā’, beloved ones of AllāhNone, whether they are alive or have died. Therefore, when you go to their tombs and visit them, do not think that it is empty there. It is the place where the beloved servants of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla rest. They always have the gaze of mercy upon them. Certainly, those who go there will share in that. It doesn’t matter how much they do.

There are those from the faithless groups who deny it, while calling themselves Muslims. They say that there is no such thing. It is something that has nothing to do with them. It is something that does not cross their minds. The value that Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla has given to these people, these blessed people, is great in HisNone sight. The people whom HeNone values will be precious in the sight of people as well. The people he doesn’t value are useless. No matter how much they think of themselves, they have no value. Even if some people show them respect, their deception will certainly appear in the end. They will be disgraced.

But Awliyā’u Llāh are not like that. For more than a thousand years, from the time of our Holy ProphetNone until now, the Ahlu l-Bāyt, the Ṣaḥābah, and others were shown respect wherever they were. Through them, goodness came to the Ummah. They have reached all kinds of Allāh’sNone bestowals. Because, as we said before, Allāh’sNone mercy is always descending on their graves, their tombs. There is a rain of mercy upon them. Without a doubt, the people who go to them to visit them, they certainly receive goodness from them during their visit. AllāhNone accepts their visit for the sake of that blessed person. In shā’a Llāh, their end will be good.

As we have said, now many kinds of people come. Praying ones and not praying ones. The ones who follow sharī‘ah and the ones who don’t follow. But it means that there is something in this person, that he respects these people and visits them. Who loves AllāhNone and HisNone beloved ones, AllāhNone will certainly grant him goodness. Therefore, to visit such places is accepted and good. Don’t listen to anyone [saying] that they are dead. Who says they are dead, is dead himself. They are people who have reached real life in the sight of AllāhNone.

May AllāhNone not deprive us of their gaze and himmah (spiritual aspirations). We love them. A person will be with whom he loves, says our Holy ProphetNone. They love the ProphetNone, and we love the ProphetNone, hisNone Ahlu l-Bāyt and Ṣaḥābah. We will be saved not by our deeds, but by hisNone love, in sha’a Llāh and we will be with them. That is certain. “Will we go to hell?” some people worry. No, you won’t enter it. Since you love our Holy ProphetNone, and you love the Ṣaḥābah, the righteous ones and the Awliyā’, you are certainly from them, in shā’a Llāh. Therefore, there is no fear. Let this be a ‘Īd present.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm, “Alā inna awliyā’a Llāhi lā khawfun ‘alayhim wa-lā hum yaḥzanūn” (10:62). Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n. AllāhNone is saying for HisNone beloved ones, “Who they are beloved for AllāhNone, there is no fear for them and no sadness.” Because, these people, they are believers and they are happy with their LordNone, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. They fear to make AllāhNone not happy with them. This is their fear. Not fear from AllāhNone, fear from make, to make not be happy... To make something to Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla not be happy. This is their fear. Because they are, these people, in English, they say “saint”. This is saint meaning: beloved one for AllāhNone. AllāhNone loves them. And they are... When they are in life, HeNone loves them. When they pass away, they also, AllāhNone [does] not cut this love for them. HisNone love [is] coming on their grave. Their maqām where they were, for this raining blessings from AllāhNone, happiness from AllāhNone for them. And this is... They are generous. For this, if you visit this grave, their grave or maqām, you will have many things from these blessings from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Because HeNone is showering [them] there.

So if you go, you will have this, from their blessing and mercy of AllāhNone. And barakah, and every good thing you will have. This is our belief. [This] belief, normal Muslim belief. Who following Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam through Ṣaḥābah, through ṭarīqah, through madhhab also, all this four madhhab they accept this. All forty-one ṭarīqah also, they accept and believe in this.

But nowadays, there is people if you say this, they will make you unbeliever, make you mushrik, make you kāfir and this. But, these people they are unlucky people. This is summary for this people, you can say, “You are unlucky people,” for this, who say this. Make people, to make them in doubt, make them in... not knowing what they are doing. They are doing everything, but they afraid. “Maybe we are going Jahannam, hell.”

If you are not following, not loving, you not making what we said – not love Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, and Ṣaḥābah, and ṭarīqah, and madhhab – of course. Because you are, only you know, you depend on your... what you doing. “I am doing this, I will go Jannah.” No, we are not like this. Who coming and believe and love, of ProphetNone and all the Ṣaḥābah, Awliyā’, and mu’minīn – this he will be in Jannah, in shā’a Llāh, no doubt for this. Other people, they have, every step, they have doubt: “If we do this...” Why are you tiring yourself like this?

You must love Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam because Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla or Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, “Al-mar’u ma‘a man aḥabb,” man with who [he] loves. We love Awliyā’u Llāh, we visit them, we go take blessing from them, and they love Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam and ProphetNone loves Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. So, no, no fear for you, you will be in Jannah, in shā’a Llāh. All in paradise. Who does this will be in Paradise. No doubt for this. So, don’t make every time, “Oh I do this. I fear I be in hell” – No. This... we believe in Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam and everything heNone said. Without this, all, if you do everything without love of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, without hisNone shafā‘ah, it will be no doubt you will be in hell. Because, first thing, or first order, for mu’min, for Muslim: to love Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. And when you don’t accept this order, you will be sometime in Jahannam. After, but if you clean yourself, you will be in Jannah. But, no need to make this, to go Jahannam, hell.

First, you love ProphetNone and you love Awliyā’u Llāh and you love Ṣaḥābah, Ahlu l-Bayt. You will be with them, in shā’a Llāh, in Jannah. AllāhNone make us with them. Their love, AllāhNone make it more and more in our hearts, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
