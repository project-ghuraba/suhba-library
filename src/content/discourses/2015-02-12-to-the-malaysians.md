Insha'Allah we say some words, first time because we are here to speak to you. Maybe 5 minutes, 10 minutes, insha'Allah, just to thank you for gathering here. Alhamdulillah, we are coming from far distance, but because we are coming for Allah, we are happy. Many people don't know why they are in this life. Alhamdulillah, mashayikh told us for what Allah created us and from what we can benefit. To be human being is big favor from Allah but most of people are not knowing this favor. Even, they are asking what we are doing here in this world?

Allah sent you, make you the highest creature in world and you are not knowing. Why you are not knowing? You are not looking for guidance. We are following Prophet, salallahu alaihi wa sallam. He is our guide. He is the best and the most honorable and most generous. He is the best for everybody. But people, they are ignorant. They are ignorant who say we are finished university, we are finished PhD, like this. I don't know so much, they know in this country these terms, I don't know too much, but only I once heard with Mawlana and somebody coming, he said my son doing PhD. Mawlana, what he said? This, no need.

They are ignorant who are not knowing Allah. They are not knowing Creator, and not knowing Prophet, salallahu alaihi wa sallam. It is a pity for them. They are trying to find whole life what there is in front of their eyes. Looking here, looking there, but it's in front of you. You look and you will be saved here and in hereafter. But no, they said, even if taking by his hand to this side, they say no, we are not finding. No, it is bad for them. Alhamdulillah, Mawlana he show us the good way and the good, clever people, who Allah gives them His favor, they find Him.

Mawlana, when first in London, he was in very small room making sohbah. And he is like ocean, not like us. Like ocean, but who coming to his sohbah, it is not more than 15, 20 people. But he is giving sohbah like ocean, giving knowledge, so he is preparing for future of ummah of Islam. And one of these meetings, Raja Ashman, rahmatullah alayhi, he was coming there. And he was real good man and clever one, so he catch Mawlana's hand and, forever never left him.

He's passed away but it is only we are not seeing him, he is seeing us. And he is happy with all these people who are continuing, growing, more and more and more by barakah of Prophet, salallahu alaihi wa sallam and the guidance who coming after him, one by one until Mawlana Sheikh. And he is the most knowledgable, because not easy what he done. He was struggle too much and he is not looking how many people there. Once we were praying, and it was only one line, he said, don't look like this, it is thousand of lines behind me from jinn. He seeing them. So he is not making, "these people, few people, why I should speak". No, he was speaking even for one man, speaking for like hundred thousand people. Still want to give them his knowledge. Alhamdulillah, he is giving and his knowledge continuing, it is enough for qiyamah. We are just sitting here for his barakah. If we speak anything, it is okay. Sohbah, it is most important thing in tariqah, so we are trying to look like him. And to make barakah for majlis.

Alhamdulillah we are happy, we are coming long distance. We are going everywhere and everywhere is the same feeling. You feel many people, we not knowing them, but still same feeling, you are happy. We are happy together. When coming, happy, when going people become sad, but what we can do? This is dunya. But Insha'Allah in akhira, we are loving Prophet, we are loving our mashayikh: al mar'u ma'a man ahhab. Hadith, Prophet salallahu alaihi wa sallam says: the man or woman, with who they are loving, they will be with him in jannah. So Insha'Allah all, we will be together forever.

Raja Ashman also, don't be worried, Allah give him his maqam here. All the time awliya is in his maqam. He is seeing and hearing and is happy more and more. And you can, if anything happens, you want from Allah, you can come here and pray 2 rak'ah and ask from Allah what you want, Allah give you. Alhamdulillah, we are happy. We are coming from very far distance, but it looks just like we are neighbor, Alhamdulillah. They said here hot and it is between here and there, 30 degrees, but Alhamdulillah I am not. Not hot for me. I am comfortable, I am happy, I am not feeling hot, Alhamdulillah.

Don't complain. If you don't complain, Allah make everything good for you. And we are following shari'ah, also. For these Malaysian people, Mawlana was loving them from 60 years or more. From first time he went to Hajj, he saw them in Hajj. He said, they are polite people. So, we are happy to be here, but some of them are misguided, they try to show Tariqah Naqshbandiyya, as if it is out of shari'ah. No, it is the heart of shari'ah, and nobody can be catching shari'ah more than Naqshbandi people.

This is what we want to say to our brothers. These are polite people, Malaysian people. Don't be misguided. And su'-i zann is not good, to be thinking bad: inna b'ada az-zanni ithmun. Don't think bad for brothers. We are with Prophet, salallahu alaihi wa sallam, and we are respecting the Four Khalifah. And we are respecting Ahlu l-Bayt, and we are respecting all Sahabah, not swearing on them. We are respecting Ahlu l-Bayt also because Prophet, salallahu alaihi wa sallam saying to be respectful. This is main pillar of Naqshibandi Tariqah.

So we are saying from here to our people, to ulama, to scholars, to professors, don't be misguided. We are like you. But we don't know, there are many misguided people, they not respecting this, what we said about Ahlu l-Bayt or Sahabah. And it is big sin and when you are in front of Prophet, salallahu alaihi wa sallam, he will not look for you. And it is the most important thing to take shafa'ah from Prophet, salallahu alaihi wa sallam. Without his shafa'ah, it will be not good for you. We are all sinner people, we must make tawbah also.

I read hadith before 2 days, Prophet, salallahu alaihi wa sallam said: to be sinner is better than being proud. Now, these people say, "we are good, we are the best, we are praying, we are doing this." They are proud. So, we are not proud, we are accepting our self. We are not like Prophet, to be no sin for us. Everybody can make sin, so we are sinner and we are wanting forgiveness from Allah. So, this is important and the people, they must know this. Very important point Prophet, salallahu alaihi wa sallam, said. You must think on this: To be sinner better than to be proud. You be the best, you do everything, you are 'alim, and you be proud, "I am very proud," so you are like satan.

May Allah make us from these people who are following Prophet salallahu alaihi wa sallam and wanting his shafa'ah. And, loving him. Insha'Allah make this country in safe from every badness. Especially from satan's badness. And Allah give barakah for Sultans. Allah likes Sultan. This is because Allah create, He is Creator. He created everybody different. So don't say, "by election I can be Sultan." No. Allah he make His election. Allah make these people Sultan. So no need for you to make election.

Some people, they have speciality to be doctors, some people, professors, some people they can be builder. Allah create them like this. So don't be cheated by satan to say, "we want to change this". This Allah put and when you change it, it makes chaos and not good for you, first. So Alhamdulillah, here Sultan and barakah for them, as-Sultan fil ardh. May Allah make them more strong and following sunnah and tariqah insha'Allah. The heart of shari'ah insha'Allah.

Wa min Allah at-tawiq. al-Fatiha.
