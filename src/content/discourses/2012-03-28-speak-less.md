Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fil jamia' Our way is through association & blessing is in the gathering. Before we left,we started speaking about the 3 attributes a mureed, a Muslim should have. Muslims should have but mureeds must have it more, we said. First is to eat less, and the 2nd is to speak less. Because all calamities coming on man come because of his speaking/tongue.

When one speaks too much, for sure he will either speak what is not correct or what is unnecessary. For this, it becomes a burden on him. There are angels on our right & left side. They are recording. When a person speaks of goodness, knowingly speaking of goodness it is immediately recorded as sawab-reward. When a person backbites, or speaks without thinking if it is right or not, the angel on his left side does not record it immediately. The same applies when a person sins. Allah (awj) orders: "Wait. This servant may repent". So it is not recorded. The angel waits 1 hour, 2 hours.. waits up to 8 hours & after this records if the person still hasn't repented. Therefore one should be careful about speaking less & think about what he speaks, what it will mean.

You should not think after you speak but before you speak. And it says in Hadith of our Prophet(S) that "Kalaam-word is in the mouth". If it does not come out, it means you own it- it is in your hands. But once it comes out of your mouth & said to people, it means it is out of your control, it rules you anymore. You can't make excuses anymore because it has come out. You should have thought beforehand. You can hold it before it comes out. But once it comes out it can destroy the world & destroy you as well. It becomes a trouble for you.

It is said "Salamat al-insan fi hifz al-lisan"- Man's well-being stems from restraining his tongue. It applies to dunya matters as well. For example, if you curse a man stronger than yourself, he will beat you up badly. This is an example from dunya matters. Being beaten is not a big deal but for your akhirat, one should be careful when preaching,knowingly or unknowingly. One should preach Allah's orders. You cannot speak of everything without knowing it just to make people happy. You will speak according to people's level, according to your own level and by weighing what comes to you.

Today, speaking about, there was three things for Muslim, or especially for Tariqat people must keep. First to eat little, speak little, sleep little. Last time we speak about eating little. Now we speak about speaking little. Because tongue it is so important. Every worshipping, everything by tongue you do. So you must be careful. When you speak too much normally so you speak from everything. You don't know what you speak: some true, some lie, some untrue. So this is when you say, there is two angels: one for good thing, one for bad thing. When you speak good thing, directly they write good, right side. But when you speak bad thing, or untrue thing or lying, this left side angel, they have order to wait- 1 hour, 2 hour, 3 hour.. Until this people make tawba or, repent. Until 8 hour. And they still, they asking "what, we still wait after 8 hour?" "No, now you can write" said. But this waiting, 8 hours until.

For this, this is meaning when you speaking little you must think what you speak about. When you speak too much, without thinking. But when you speak little, you think what will happen after this- good or bad.

And there is Hadith. Prophet (sas) he said, "this word- in your mouth, you own it". But when coming out from lips, it owns you. Because before, nobody hear what you think. Allah not write bad thing about thinking. You think, when you not coming to speak or to make with hand - this is waswas, everybody they can do. But when coming out, this is problem for you- big problem. Maybe for akhirat, or maybe for dunya. In dunya, when if you see somebody & shout on him and he is bigger than you, angry, maybe he beat you up. And this is easy, because in dunya. But for akhirat, you speak something maybe you be in hell for this thing. Because you must think.

Even when you speak to crowd people for giving lecture or something if you see huge people there waiting to listen something to make them happy and you see it is against Islam or against Shariat so you be in trouble also. You must think about akhirat also. Maybe 100 people, 1000 they will be happy. But you be jahannam one day, is not worth this. So you must think for speaking less. Or when you speak, you think what you speak. First think, after speak. Most of the people they speak, after they think what they speak. Allah make us to think about this, insha Allah. Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, Al Fatiha
