As-Salamu Alaikum. Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fil jamia' What is the biggest benefit? Is it to make haram money? Is it to gain the riches of dunya? Is the biggest benefit to get the things you like? None of them.

What you gain in dunya is no benefit at all- if you didn't do it for akhirah, it is as if you didn't get any benefit. Allah (awj) says "Whosoever you saved from sin who was able to commit that sin (but didn't), gained great benefit. I showed him Mercy" says Allah. For example because some people are poor, they can't commit sins. He feels sorry (for being poor) but it is a Mercy for that person. He can't drink, he can't do anything bad. He stays where he is, can't move anywhere. And for some people, Allah (awj) gives them an illness & that one thinks he is suffering because of that illness but in reality Allah has shown him Mercy. He (swt) made it a means for him so that he can't commit sin. He gains great benefit.

Some people can be all healthy but if Allah wants to not let them do something, He (swt) puts thousands of obstacles in their way. What we are trying to say here is that not committing sin is a great benefit. Any kind of sin - drinking, adultery, theft..And some get upset when they can't do business for the government. There is a saying "The state property is like an ocean & you are dumb to not take advantage of it". So they try to do this, try to make money through illegal ways. And they get upset when they can't achieve this. But they will see in akhirah how wrong they were to get upset. Things didn't work out & they didn't fall into sin. They didn't make illegal money & didn't sin. So they will be happy for this.

So what is the most important thing here as we said is the benefit- Allah's Mercy comes upon a person when he doesn't sin. "May Allah save us from sin" says the Holy Verse. Whoever He (swt) saves from committing sin, it means Allah showed him Mercy, Allah pitied him. He gained great benefit with this. And some say, there is also a saying about this: If I was to steal something, the sun would rise at midnight! An obstacle stands in his way so that he won't do it. So there is such a saying that most people haven't heard, meaning Allah Almighty does not let that person commit sin.

This is very important. Therefore as we said, for a person who has faith no matter what kind of a condition, situation he is in Allah gives him glad tidings every minute, continuously. He (swt) gives him hope even if he is in the worst situation, meaning these (bad) situations don't go to waste. Even if he is poor, being poor is Ok. Maybe if he was rich,he would go astray. So he may be poor, may live as a poor man all his life. Instead of committing sin, he may work in this way & reach Allah's Mercy. It is a thousand times better than being rich. Therefore this is an important matter. If you pay good attention to this no sadness or worries remain inside a person either.

Today we speak about what is the biggest benefit you can get. Money, woman, land, cars.. What is the biggest thing it is benefit for you, you will be happy with this? All this nothing, it is nothing at all. The biggest benefit is to be away from sin. Who is away from sin, he gets the biggest benefit. Because when you are away from sin, there is Ayah from Quran "Wa Qihimu As-Sayyi'ati Wa Man Taqi As-Sayyi'ati Yawma'idhin Faqad Rahimtahu". (40:9) Who you make the sin away from him, he is in Mercy of Allah. May be, how to be away from sin? Allah make between you & sin something you cannot do sin. Maybe you are very poor. You cannot go, you cannot drink, you cannot go to with woman or to go around to drink, to make bad thing, because you are poor. If you have money, many poor people when they have money the first thing they are going to be left their house with children, his wife & going to bad woman, to going to drink, to going to make sin; to sink on sin. So billion times better to be whole life he is to be poor, better from to be rich & to be doing every bad thing, every sin. Because when you do sin, you are not in benefit. He is going to be in bad condition in last day, Judgement Day.

Maybe some of them become ill, they cannot go to do sin. Also it is from Mercy of Allah. Look like it is bad situation for people. They are seeing this man or woman, like pity for them. But it is Allah, He know everything He is Most Merciful. He is giving Mercy for people. Not you, He knows. Nobody knows, only He knows. So don't say against Allah's Will. The best & the better to be ill or to be in bad condition or to make some between you & this sin something you cannot do. If you have opportunity, many people can do sin. And Allah, from His Merciful, He is putting something you cannot do sin.

When you are not doing sin, it is the biggest benefit- benefit forever. Not for very short time this, it is very short time - dunya life. So every time must make dua "Allah be Mercy with us. Don't let us to do any sin, any bad thing." Every time we must remember this. It is, every Verse, word in Quran, in life for who is believer Allah give good thing for people. For this, who is in iman, in Islam every time, everything is good for them. They are happy. They must be happy. Nothing is bad for them.

And this what was teaching from beginning of Islam until now. But in this last, maybe 50-60 year world education & look like together. All world same, so you see people they are fed up from things. They are shouting, they are objecting from what happen, "why this is for us? Why this is for me, why this..?" It is not from Islam. Islam what? If you have something, bad thing it is Ok also. This is better than to be in sin, or to.. Of course you will reward & you are satisfied. You are in satisfaction, in happiness. This is not end of life. The real life coming after this. And after this small period, all this.. Even now who suffering from his life, Allah make forget, giving to people to forget. Maybe before 2 days they were suffering & when yesterday if he is not suffering, today not suffering they will be happy. Because this is life, it is short & forgettable. Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, Al Fatiha.
