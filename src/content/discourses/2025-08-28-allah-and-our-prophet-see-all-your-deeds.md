As-Salāmu ‘Alaykum wa RaḥmatuLlāhi wa Barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu BiLlāhi Minash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. BismiLlāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm.

Wa ṣ-Salātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Akhirīn.

Madad yā RasūlAllāh, Madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi RasūliLlāh, Madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

Dastūr Mawlana Sheikh Abdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistāni, Sheikh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-

Ḥaqqānī. Madad.Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-Suḥbah wa l-Khayru fi l-Jam‘iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm:

‘Wa-quli ʿmalū fa-sa-yarā llāhu ʿamalakum wa rasūlih‘ ‘And say, Do, for Allah will see your deeds, and [so, will] His Messenger.’ (Qur’ān 09:105). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘Aẓīm.

Do good in your deeds, your charity and all the deeds you do. Do your worship because Allāh will see what you do, and our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam as well. The deeds, worship, charity and whatever you do, do it for the sake of Allāh, Allāh will not forget you. People forget, but Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla does not forget. He certainly sees your deeds. Our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam sees it. The good deeds done, the salawāt, the charity are all presented to our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam and Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.

Whatever they have done of good deeds, they will have won the pleasure of our Prophet and the pleasure of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. If one attains their pleasure, there is no need for anything else for a person. What we need is to attain the love of our Prophet and the love of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. It is the greatest benefit for mankind. It is the greatest goodness. This is the reward.

The value of this reward is inestimable. It is inestimable according to the worldly values. Because according to the worldly values, no matter how many things there are in the hands of man, it can be that he loses them or he can’t take anything with him when he dies. But the other, which lasts forever, continues without any diminishing. You will see them in the hereafter.

Therefore, it is important to go on the way of our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, to gain his pleasure, to gain his love. It is an inestimable value for mankind. May Allāh make us steadfast. Make He make us successful in doing good deeds and charity. May we not stand in front of our Prophet with bad deeds; may we not be ashamed, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa min Allāhi t-Tawfīq. Al-Fātiḥa.
