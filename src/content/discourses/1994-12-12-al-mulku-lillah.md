All mortals, all living beings die. Every new, gets old. And every awaited, comes. Our Master alaihi s-salatu wa s-salam's mother said so when she was giving her last breath. "All living beings die. Every new gets old. Every awaited comes. This is certain". The angels in Heavens, there are special angels, they call out, "Lidu li l-mawt wabnu li l-kharab", every day, "You were born to die. Construct buildings to be destroyed."

I saw a very unpleasant advertisement in Ankara, don't know what it was promoting. I looked at it carefully. "Soon I'm going to be born" was written on a pregnant lady's belly. Soon I'll be born. Children are born, everybody is born. They are born to die. Nobody is born to stay. No building is to remain. Every building will be destroyed.

Municipalities have a duty nowadays. They put a sign on worn-out buildings, "Danger of collapse". Maybe you also have this law. Building is in danger of collapsing. Actually it should be written on all of these Namrud towers, they say ‘skyscraper’. Actually they are Namrud towers. It should be written at their doors, "It will be collapsed." "Sovereignty belongs to Allah" is written in some places, I see this in Arab countries. It isn't common here. Actually it is appropriate to write this on all buildings. "It will be collapsed."

In the past, on government buildings, it was written: Al Mulku Lillah. Everybody was reminded that Allah is the Owner of everything. Nobody owns these offices. This the doctor's office, we can't interfere. You should write on the Municipality's. Is it not? The municipality is Allah Almighty's. M: If we write "Sovereignty belongs to Allah" they throw us out next day. The laws are like this in Turkey. First we have to make laws saying "Sovereignty belongs to Allah". MSN: You are "man sanna fi Islam sunnatan hasanatan fa lahu ajruha wa ajru man 'amila biha ila yawm al Qiyama." (whoever innovates a good tradition in Islam will get it's reward and to whomever the reward, apply it till Judgement Day).

You mark an era. They don't make peace with you now or in the future anyway. Therefore, write and give it, next day they come, "What is the meaning of this?" "Its meaning is: 'This building belongs to the republic', in Arabic". They don't know Arabic anyway. Sovereignty belongs to Allah. You didn't understand dear? This property is given to the republic. Be brave about things, that aren't clearly forbidden by law. Write. Is it permitted by laws to delete this? If I write "Sovereignty belongs to Allah" is there a law forbidding this? Which article in Turkish constitution forbids this? There is a secularism, that is never defined. MSN: I can define secularism also. I define it better. May Allah bless us all. Doctor we gave you headache. What shall we do? M: We are honored, delighted. MSN: We are honored also. Sultan Mawlana was kind enough to make him invite us. If he didn't give it to his heart, where are we? He reminded. After that, I said it is winter time but but the spiritual Sultan of Konya ordered, so I should go. Alhamdulillah, we were destined to come. We are honored. Our hearts are satisfied. I hope Allahumma ij'al lana fiha ghararan. As long as Allah Almighty keeps us here He, Jalla Jalauhu grants us heart contentment, so we live in peace. May He, Jalla Jalauhu send here halal sustenance. May He, Jalla Jalauhu grant us our provision. May He, Jalla Jalauhu honor us with goodness. May He, Jalla Jalauhu honor us with His good servants. May Allah protect us from evil and the evil people here. May Allah protect us.

Bi hurmati l-Habib bi hurmati l-Fatiha.
