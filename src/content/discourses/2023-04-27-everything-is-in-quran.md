As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh. A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says, “Learn and teach the Qur’ān.” The Qur’ān is the holy book sent by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla to this ummah through our Holy ProphetNone. Everything is in it, says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. There is healing and blessing in it. There are rewards. Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says there are ten rewards for every letter of Qur’ān. Not just every word, but in every letter, says our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. There is Alif-Lām-Mīm. Alif is one; ten rewards are written for it. Ten rewards are written for Lām, says our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. And same for Mīm. If a word consists of three letters, thirty thawāb are given. Not just for a word, but for every letter ten thawāb are written. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gives such favours to someone who reads Qur’ān and listens to Qur’ān.

We must certainly read it. It is one of the duties of the murīds, anyway, to read one juz’ daily as part of our daily recitation, waẓīfah, shukr to AllāhNone. We must do it. It is actually easy to learn the Qur’ān. It is rare that people can't learn. And that is by the wisdom of AllāhNone. But normally, learning Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n is not a difficult thing. Some people learn to read in three days. Some can learn in a week. Normally, on average, people can learn it in a month. As we said, some people can learn quicker than that, and some even more quickly. But others can learn in three to five months, or even in a year.

We must try our best. It opens our minds, and blessing comes. It is healing. It is blessing, barakah. All kinds of goodness are in it. It is the eternal word of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. It is Allah'sNone Word, given by HimNone to people, to believers, as miracle that we can hold in our hands. Miracles are everywhere. But this is a direct connection.

Keeping up the recitation of the Word of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is a great favour. If you stop to think about it, you realize it is such a great gift. It is such a great favour. The Word of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is always in your hands. You can read any part of it. You can touch it with your hands. We must appreciate it. It is so precious. The most precious thing is the Word of AllāhNone. You should both listen to it and read it. One of its specialities is that it doesn't matter if you understand its meaning. You don't have to understand its meaning. When you read, you attain those rewards, thawāb. May AllāhNone make all of us from the people of Qur’ān. May HeNone not keep us away from the Qur’ān. Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, about Qur’ān: “It is the best thing AllāhNone gave for human being.” Who wants to know anything, in Qur’ān – everything. And it is very blessed book, because holy, heavenly book coming from our Lord, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, for HisNone word. From word for, HeNone know from what time – no time for AllāhNone, no place. This... but HisNone – miraculously for ummat Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam – HeNone gave them this. And HeNone reward them for each letter, ten reward. Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, as if you – there is word beginning, Alif-Lām-Mīm. Alif – one letter, ten reward, heNone said, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Lām, also ten reward. Mīm, ten reward. Not one word. Each letter, ten reward. So if you read one chapter, it will be – must be – thousands of reward. Ten thousand, maybe hundred thousands of reward, when you read Qur’ān, AllāhNone reward you.

And Qur’ān ‘aẓīmu sh-sha’n, it is everything, every good thing, you find inside. From AllāhNone give you health, AllāhNone cure you, AllāhNone give barakah for you, AllāhNone make you happy. Everything you can imagine from good thing, it is in Qur’ān ‘aẓīmu sh-sha’n. And it is very good thing for, we have in our hand, in house, everywhere in shelf – Qur’ān ‘aẓīmu sh-sha’n. Who it is coming, heavenly, from heaven, word of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. The real one, not changed. Because since Ādam ‘alayhi s-salām, coming many heavenly books. But most of them, they got disappear. All which not disappeared, they was changed by people, who, long time, they cannot preserve it and they change it. Only Qur’ān ‘aẓīmu sh-sha’n was keep original as what Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying. And Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, it was coming Jibrīl ‘alayhi s-salām to himNone every year. HeNone was reading by heart to see this, no changing. And since that time, nothing changed from Qur’ān ‘aẓīmu sh-sha’n. Directly from heavenly book for human being.

For every good thing, we are using Qur’ān ‘aẓīmu sh-sha’n. We reading to get blessed. We reading to pray. Without Qur’ān, we cannot make ṣalāt, praying. And everything, right inside. Even they find knowledge for everything. Material thing also, they have knowledge. Everything in Qur’ān. “Wa-lā raṭbin wa-lā yābisin illā fī kitābin mubīn” (6:59). AllāhNone saying: “No green, no dry, nothing left without mentioning in this Qur’ān.”
