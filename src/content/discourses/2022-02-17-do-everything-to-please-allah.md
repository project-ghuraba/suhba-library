As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

AllāhNone is the OneNone who created people. Each person’s desires are different. But all of them have something in common. Most people are living in order to advance themselves, and working in order to satisfy themselves in this world. There are billions of people around the world; everyone is trying to become someone. They are trying hard day and night in order to advance themselves in their work. This is what they want. This is the nature given to people by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla has given people this disposition for doing goodness. HeNone made them like this so that they will become good ones. Not for them to build wealth, but to be valuable and beloved servants in the presence of AllāhNone. This is the reason Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gave people this nature. But they forget it, and strive to excel in things they themselves love. That is useless. Those are useless things. No matter how distinguished they are, as long as they are not the way that AllāhNone wants them to be, they are absolutely useless.

Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says unless there is the pleasure of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla in all these things they have no benefit, no importance. This is ḥadīth sharīf of our Holy ProphetNone. It is the same for all these non-Muslims, people without religion, never mind for Muslims. You should say that everything you do, you will do for the pleasure of AllāhNone. You should say to yourself every time you wake up in the morning, “In shā’a Llāh, let everything I do today be something beloved by AllāhNone. Let it be something that AllāhNone is pleased with so that I don’t spend a minute, a breath, a moment for nothing in this life.” This life is short. It should not be wasted for nothing. This thought, this intention is enough. It puts everything on track. It makes every breath valuable. This applies to everything we do... There are many crazy trends in this, that and the other. In order to be liked by others, people are dying by falling over and shooting themselves. They die at their own hand. What for? To be liked by people. People post their likes here, there, and I don’t know where. Their lives are empty. The lives of people who strive to be liked by others are empty.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, our Holy ProphetNone, and the mashāyikh should be pleased with you. You should strive for that. Strive to be liked by believers. Otherwise, if you say, “One hundred, one thousand, ten thousand people like me. One hundred thousand people like me” – then you have wasted time and your work and it is a shame. In the end, you won’t profit from it. What should be earned is the pleasure of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. That is the real gain. May AllāhNone let all of us earn that reward. May AllāhNone always be pleased with us, in shā’a Llāh. May we do our work for the pleasure of AllāhNone.

AllāhNone create human being and they are have attribute, everyone from human being he like to be special; or to be in his work, in his business, in his life, to be more higher from other. To succeed and to do many thing to be happy. This is for whole world not only... even you can see very poor man he also think like this. Don’t think he is fed up from to look up to be, think it must be something – he is also. All this human being AllāhNone HeNone created them like this. But HeNone is also order them to follow HisNone rule and to accept HisNone, what orders. But people, most of people, whole world only they are looking to be satisfy their ego, running after dunyā for to be succeed. They forget their life it is not so long and what they are doing – nonsense, nothing. Even you succeed, you have what you like but if you miss something it is nothing at all.

What this thing? Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, everything you do without intention to be your Lord Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla pleased with you it is nothing at all. When you forget this point, it is... you do everything, you tire yourself, you be happy, you be not happy. This is not important. It is at the end you are loser. You didn’t win anything. This not for non-believer, for believer even like this. Non-believer they are all, they think they are doing something, but from beginning they are loser. But for believer who and not doing thing – Everything we do, we must intention: “This it is for to make our LordNone pleased with us, happy with us, we do this.” So you are winner every second, every minute, every day you are winner. Everything you do for happiness of AllāhNone, for HisNone pleasure, you are winner.

AllāhNone happy with you because you are remember everything. You are contact. Not any second you are without thinking this. So it is this what we must think about this. Like, maybe you cannot do twenty-four hour, but from beginning of day intention. We do this from day and night for the pleasure of AllāhNone, everything we do. Also, in shā’a Llāh, AllāhNone make us to remember, to be pleased with us. You must make everything to make Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla pleased with us. This is the most important thing. Not important thing to make people happy with you or not happy. People they will not be happy. But now there is this fashion, subḥāna Llāh, every day they bring something this telephone. Many people they do very funny thing, very crazy thing to make people to... they say “like”. I don't know what is this “like”, they put something. Oh, hundred people he happy with me, he like me. Thousand people they like me. Ten thousand people, million people – ah. It is all rubbish, to make people have like you like clown and people happy with you. They will not win anything from pleasure of AllāhNone.

You must think for pleasure of AllāhNone. Not be pleasure for yourself to make you like balloon to... Everyone blowing, blowing, at the end he will be explode. So, in shā’a Llāh, AllāhNone make us to make HimNone happy with us. Not looking our ego to be happy with us. AllāhNone give us this thinking and we will be winner at the end, in shā’a Llāh. Because life it is very fast, very precious. Time very precious, not to do for nothing. For this everything you do, even you sit: “I am sitting. It is resting for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, in shā’a Llāh, happy with me.” In shā’a Llāh, AllāhNone be happy with us and to make our time all precious, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
