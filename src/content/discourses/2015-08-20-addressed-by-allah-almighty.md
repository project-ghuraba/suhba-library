Masha Allah, good practicing. We are practicing all our life. The practicing, what's mean the practicing? They say: "you practice Namaz, you practice prayer for Allah Almighty." Yes, we practice to be better. We practice to be more shining. We practice to be in the best shape or in the best looking or in the best behavior. We are as a dunya people. We are hoping to be good Akhirat people. What we are doing here, we are trying to be good Akhirat people.

Sometime, some people they have promotion and they been promote from normal to be a manager or to be general director or to be whatever rank. And you see the happiness when he get this title, he say: "I am the general manager here. Doctor, I am the general manager here." See? Everybody looking at me. I am the general manager here." Must be happy. This one reason of the happiness - to have something been given to you. Even, by word, even by talk, even by polite things. Now, why we don't want to be in better position in front of Allah Almighty? Why we not practicing to be better in front of Allah Almighty, to get higher promote, to get higher things? Because Allah Almighty He open up this door as a huge door for His servant, for His servant to come nearer to Him, to be with Him and learn from Him. This is, Allah Almighty so Generous. So Generous, He say: "Come to Me. If you walk to Me, I will run to you." He is more than you. And imagine Allah Almighty running for His servant. This is, must be big things.

One day, I was with Mawlana Sheikh and he was talking with very important people, and that person, he was saying: "Ya Sayyidi, we are hoping to have something from Allah Almighty because, maybe, we do sin without knowing. Maybe we are not good people, maybe we think that we are good people. We asking forgiveness from Allah Almighty. You are Wali-Allah and your dua it is Mustajab - accepted." And Mawlana Sheikh say: "I am saying to you about Sayyidina Abd al-Qadir al-Jilani - he say: "In Qiyamat Day, I want Allah Almighty, if He address me: "Ya Abd al-Qadir, you are guilty, you are My servant but you are guilty." He say: " I don't care about what I done, I care about one thing - that Allah Almighty, He address me - "You are My servant" and I said: "After that, if He throw me for hell or He throw me for anything, I don't care, because I been addressed by Allah Almighty to say - "you, My servant, O My servant" and, that was the main target of Awliya-Allah - to be the servant of Allah Almighty. We are wishing to be the servant of Allah Almighty, to be addressed as the servant of Allah Almighty. InshaAllah, with the Baraka of Awliya-Allah, Allah Almighty, He will look to us, because there is so many things people, those days people, they don't realize and they don't understand. Say: "Law laka law laka lama khalaqtul aflaka". This is been say to Rasul-Allah (sas). Say: "if you are not existing, I would not bother to create anything." Because of you Ya Rasul-Allah Allah Almighty He create everything, for you! For him, this is Rasul Allah (sas). Don't missed up. And we are the lover of Rasul-Allah We want to be the lover of Rasul-Allah. We want to be the lover of Awliya-Allah. We want to be the lover of who Allah Almighty give them a rank. With their Baraka, with their good faith we will be the servant of Allah Almighty.

Don't think you can be by yourself the servant of Allah Almighty. This is all the time, you have to you have to follow a Murshid, you have to follow the one that Allah Almighty He love. That's why they say: "search in your life, search for the one Allah Almighty, He love him." If you find him, catch and don't leave him. This is important, important advice. All the Awliya, they been saying: "Go all over the world and search for the good one, that one Allah Almighty He love him, and hug him and don't release because this is your only way to be addressed as the servant of Allah Almighty." This very important Nasiha/advice, this very important advice.
