As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa raḥmatu Llāhi wa barakātuhu. Thank you, mā shā’a Llāh Shaykh Rasūl, very nice.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Waṣ-ṣalātu was-salāmu ‘alá Rasūlina Muḥammadin Sayyida l-awwalīn wal-ākhirīn Madad yā Rasūlu Llāh Madad yā Ḥabību Llāh Madad yā Sayyida l-awwalīn wal-ākhirīn Dastur Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

Mawlānā Shaykh he was saying everything to be succeed, to be happy, your ikhlāṣ (sincerity). What you do, you do for to be the best, this is your intention. For dunyā thing or for ākhirah for next life, you must be sincere. Don't say this is for dunyā, we mustn't do. For intention, you do this for sake of AllāhNone, it will be reward for this what you done, even for this world, for this dunyā because you do this and you are believe Allāh HeNone is seeing you, HeNone is with you, HeNone is knowing everything. Don't say nobody see me I can do.

Of course you can do what you like, but after many people they are asking "why this happen for me? I doing everything but I am not succeed." Because you are doing only for your benefit not thinking for benefit of ākhirah. Only for this dunyā you like to do and to be succeed. You said "I doing the best" but you don't have sincerity also. You are... many people they cannot be succeed. But when you do for ākhirah, for to be AllāhNone happy with you, you are doing best even you are not succeed. You are not complaining because you have real belief, real īmān. You done this for AllāhNone, "not for myself and HeNone is knowing the best. If HeNone want, HeNone will give me, if HeNone want HeNone will cure me, if HeNone want HeNone will make me understand what I study I pass my examination." But if you are not believe, your īmān, your belief not complete, you will be every time complaining - "I done this but I couldn't succeed. I read this much from tasbīḥ, I make charity, I couldn't take benefit." This is wrong because what you done - all AllāhNone will reward you. Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla HeNone say, "ud‘ūnī astajib lakum" (40:60) "Ask Me" HeNone said Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla "INone will give you, INone will reply you, you ask MeNone." And Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla, HeNone is... when promising must be.

Many people they are in hurry saying they said "I make, ask AllāhNone, I pray for HimNone but nothing happen." This is very wrong because when He, Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla, saying if not giving here HeNone will give hereafter. Because this what you live in this world, maybe somebody can live until hundred year, but after this nearly nobody, very rare people they live. But for time of what Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla, said one day it is like thousand year, like fifty thousand years. So it is HeNone is promise, you will get it here or hereafter. But when you are get in ākhirah you will be saying, "oh I wish I haven't take anything from what I pray, what I asked in dunyā. I wish HeNone let it to ākhirah." Because what you will get there it is much, very more than what you get here.

For this, believer you must be believe more stronger, don't be hopeless. Allāh HeNone said, HeNone not like hopeless, "lā taqnaṭū min raḥmati Llāh" (39:53). "Don't be hopeless from mercy of AllāhNone." HeNone is giving, He willNone give. But HeNone like to give more but people they are not have patience especially people of this century. It is they said this is time of speed century. They want everything quickly. Before people they was going from place to place by walking, after they go by horse, by horse cart, or by train, by bus. Now they want to be very fast with fast train, with plane. This is they used to it. But for... they can, you can do this for yourself but Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla HeNone is not in hurry.

When you ask something from AllāhNone ask. HeNone said I like people to ask MeNone many times, to be insist. When somebody ask you sometime something and he insist, you are getting angry. But Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla HeNone is merciful and HeNone said "INone like people they are insisting, asking one time, ten time, hundred time, thousand time. Allāh HeNone is, why HeNone is happy with this? Because each time they asking they are remember AllāhNone and HeNone is, will reward them for every time they ask. For this they are... Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla like people to ask and we are happy with our Lord. This is what you must be especially who are following ProphetNone and following ṭarīqah. Then what coming from YouNone it is nice for us - this is they was making qaṣīdah. Many poem they are saying what come for You Lutfun da güzel azabın da güzel. "What YouNone send it is good." YouNone send for us mercy ok. YouNone are little bit make test for us, also we are happy with this.

This is what ṭarīqah, taṣawwuf people they are like. They are taking from mercy their attribute because they are loving AllāhNone and they are taking this teaching. They are soft and they are merciful people. They not like to harm anybody. If they are, they want to do something, they are thinking for other people also - "we do this it will be good for other people or they will be harm from this what we done?" For this, ṭarīqah people they are every time attacking by shayṭān and his follower. They are looking for something to attack them but, al-ḥamdu liLlāh AllāhNone, with merciful people they are coming through time of ProphetNone same until now. But they was, since ProphetNone time also, who are attacking this people with shayṭān, thousand of ways, thousand of not good ways, gathering. They was thinking they will be for qiyāmah but all of them they was finish. And it will be same for this people who are not accepting and they are saying bad thing for ṭarīqah people, they will be their end also not good and in ākhirah they will be regret for what they done. Because everything they done they thought they are sacrifice themself for to be take Jannah, they will be in Jahannam because they oppress people and doing thing against Prophet'sNone teaching, against Islām.

We are praying for AllāhNone to give hidāyah (guidance) for this people and to give them mind because they are, they don't have mind at all. All what they done, without thinking, without seeing what ProphetNone ordering, and how Ṣaḥābah was living, how Tābi‘īn was living. Until now how this coming. Any one from bad people, they must look if he is coming through ProphetNone till this time. Nobody from them. Only who are following ProphetNone they are for majority. We are feeling sorry for them, for people who are cheated by this group of people but what we do? AllāhNone know best. It is good luck to be away from them. They are like smelling bad animal. They are, you must be run away from them. AllāhNone keep them away from our people, from our youth people, and give hidāyah for who are following them.

Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq al-Fātiḥah.
