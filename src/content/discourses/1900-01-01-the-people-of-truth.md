They are recording to their body. There can be unending cassettes. Till we depart from this world, these cassettes won't be filled. They record to their bodies. We don't ask for confirmation. They ask if it's open. We pay attention so that they don't write nonsense things. We don't care about angels recordings that much. We know it. We call it garbage. They throw the garbage into dump. And they will throw our actions, which is like garbage to the dump. Actions of people like us, same as garbage. Only once in a while they investigate. "Look if a tiny thing from jewels is mixed into this garbage in the dump" says Allah Almighty to angels. "Look if these actions of this servant of ours has anything useful in it. Look into this dump". He orders this. If they find something, He makes them respect him and throw others. He throws them into dump. He throws them into hell.

Work of the great ones is different. Impure things inside their pure actions are not accepted. They are asked why they put this with their work, why they didn't pay attention. For common people, if they bring a sack of garbage for them, they'll be asked to look for useful things. But for great ones, they're asked why they let useless things in. What shall we do? Our work is so. Garbage things from a dump group. It should be known as so. When we know, there can be nothing but saying "Ya Rabbi, we don't have any deeds, which can take us to Your presence. If You forgive us for the honor of Your valuable servants, that is Your great kindness. Otherwise, we couldn't offer any pious action to reach Your presence. We are weaker than that. We come ashamed to Your door, ya Rabbi. Forgive us". Dastur ya Sayyidi, ya RasulAllah. Madad.

What shall we do? We are in shortage of great ones. Great Awliyas who are lions of Allah have pulled out and became hidden. Grandsheikh Sultan-ul Awliya used to say this. There was a Sheikh who used to be proud of himself very much. He scolded him a lot. "Do you think you became an important man if you gather a couple of Kurds? You are like, we say, black oil lamp. A man has lit a black lamp and there are insects, which come to that lamp. You consist of that. You think you're a man? You see yourself as sun?" He scolded him so. Why? Because he didn't know his place and made fitna. That person from Aleppo, from Syria was upset. Now we are like that. Our condition is similar to that. May Allah not leave us to our egos.

The mood and state of the one, who is speaking attaches to people like a vaccine. Therefore, students of a hoja, students of a teacher, listeners of a hoja, murids of a sheikh, whatever their natures are, they will be their reflection. That will spread from them. Who is with a strong one is strong. If he is with rotten sick people, their sickness will pass to him. Therefore pay attention to people, whom you meet and whom you listen to. There is an apparent apathy. We call it manginess. There's manginess too. There's apparent manginess and spiritual manginess. In our time, maybe there are just few apparently mangy ones. But spiritually mangy ones, sick with manginess, are many. We should beware of them. Where is mangy, there is rabies. There is rabies in apparent sickness. They found a cure for it. However, there is a spiritually mad group who belittle faith and religion. Beware of their biting. They will destroy you. They have spread so much.

Therefore, a student takes lessons from his teacher. Whatever teacher's attributes are, it's seen in his student. Whatever sheikh's mood and state is, it will be seen in his trained murid. A teacher injects what is inside him to his student. Therefore, now in our time Awliyas, who are lions of Allah, friends of Allah Almighty, all of them have brought the curtains down and hid themselves. And just some of people, we are walking around. May Allah forgive us. Dastur. Therefore, I say dastur. When I say dastur, those lions hear. I ask for help, I ask for support from them. They will reach us to help. So many people from ummati Muhammad have gathered here to listen to something, to learn something. We are afraid. "Man ghashshana fa laisa minna" We are afraid of this hadith. Why are we? Prophet (saw) said "Who deceives us is not from us". This is a very frightening hadith. Deceiving in dunya is a very simple thing. Deceiving in worldly matters, it binds to responsibility too. But responsibility of those, who deceive in akhira, religious and faith matters, is heavy.

We don't know whatever we say to this many ummati Muhammadiya. We ask for your help, ya Sahib-ul Waqt, ya Rijalullah, O lions of Allah, ahl-ul haqiqa, who rule the seen and unseen of this world, who rule the humans and jinns, great beings, who represent our Prophet. I am calling you for you to support me. Then when we show our weakness, as much as our weakness, we will be sent support. Wherever they call the most in the frontline and ask for help, security will send the powers there. We are in the war of ego, desires, shaitan and dunya. And we are defeated many times against them. We are remaining weak. Therefore, we ask for help from Awliya, from ahlu l-haqiqa and awliya Allah, who are representatives of the Prophet. They are not deaf. Who is deaf cannot be representative of the Prophet. Who is blind cannot be representative of the Prophet. Who is ignorant cannot be representative of the Prophet. Who is dead cannot be representative of the Prophet. Who is weak-minded cannot be representative of the Prophet. Whatever perfection the Prophet (saw) has, they inherit from that perfection. And the Prophet (saw) is given from absolute perfection of Allah Almighty.

Therefore, don't ask how they hear when we call them. This place is a place to make those present here, believe. What is the purpose and wisdom of us sitting here? To make present ones believe. What to believe in? Spirituality. You should believe in spirituality. Lesson of faith is given here. You can get general knowledge outside in schools. They are many. There are many, who can teach you that. The purpose and wisdom for our being here is to make people believe, to give a faith lesson. Therefore, if you try to taste the spoken words by your mind, you will remain where you are, your mind scale will break down, you'll be useless. These are assemblies leading to belief. That's why - pay attention! Words spoken sometimes are to annihilate you and to give you more faith. When we open our mouth here and ask for support, if the possessors of time, awliyaullah didn't hear it, they wouldn't be awliya. They'd be from dead people like you and me. They are the beings, made from the light of knowledge and skill, who are alive and who have willpower in their hands. They don't leave us. They hear and send support.

Audhu billahi min ash-shaitani r-rajim. Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. La hawla wa la quwwata illa billahi l-Aliyyu l-Adhim. Till our introduction here, it became a perfect sohba by their side. And let's say kalima shahada so that our hearts revive with blessings and light of that shahada. Ashhadu an la ilaha illAllah wa ashhadu anna Muhammadan abduhu wa Rasuluh, sallAllahu Ta'ala alaihi wa sallam. They showed us signs of the oceans now, which will be open for us there. But that will be a piece of another sohba. Now let's be satisfied with the words we heard. And inshaAllahu r-Rahman, whatever they instruct us for the forthcoming, let's look at that matter.

Wa min Allahi t-tawfiq. Bi hurmati l-Fatiha.
