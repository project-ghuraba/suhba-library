"Fa alhukmu Lillahi al-Aliyyi al-Kabiri" (40:12)

Absolute judgement belongs to Allah. We are not permanently in this world. Now living, but soon we will die, disappear. We are in existence now, in future we will vanish. Everyone will exist for some time, then vanish, disappear and be lost to the future. There was our Haji Kalpha, the brown lady and her sister, they spoke strange words. She was a sincere one. May Allah bless her soul! She wouldn't say, 'a certain person died'. 'Eh! A certain woman is not around anymore. A certain woman passed away/disappeared'. She wouldn't say 'a lady or a man died', but 'she/he is no longer around. They passed away, it's become more quiet'.

Like this some are coming continually, some are passing continually. The number of coming ones is known, the number of passing ones is known. Those who are coming in 24 hours' time, who are passing away in 24 hours' time, on this world is known. Not only mankind but all creation which is living, no matter how many billions, they too, even ants. Those passing, those coming, all is known. Could anything be forgotten? How could it be? Anything forgotten? He himself is creating, does He forget? If anything were to be forgotten, it would go into non-existence at once. It would be lost.

Everything appearing in existence is of Allah's creating. How could He forget them? They are in Oceans of Mightiness. They cannot go out. And with His attribute of Alim (The All-Knowing), He knows all Creation within the Oceans of Mightiness. He created them. All that creation lives on this huge world, these many millions, billions, trillions, quadrillions quintillions, sextillions of creation. They are asking how could Allah know them all? Strange! This thinking means to know that the Creator, Janabu l-Haqq is away and unaware of His Creation. Ey, He Himself created them. It is unreasonable to say how could He know all these? Did anyone else create them that they should misunderstand how He knows them all? Why ask such a question when there's no other creator besides Allah.

They ask how could it be, how could Allah know? Beneath and above the Earth, in the seas, in the oceans, there are so many small, big, tiny or huge creations, how could Allah know them all? Eh, this is an ignorant question! Those creations didn't create themselves, so that they remain in loneliness. He created them; what their sensations may be, what their duty may be, where they will be, what will be their provision, how long their lifetimes will be? What the wisdom/purpose of their existence will be, the Creator decides and gives all of these to His Creation. How could it be said, 'How does Allah know everything?' Ey, Ya Hu, did someone else create that that you say 'How does Allah know?' He Himself created everything. If you put something here, don't you know it?

They say all those creations inside the sea - ey, who created the sea? There are so many creations in the seas, some ask, how Allah knows everything. Did anyone else create it that you ask how could He know? He Himself created, thus He knows. You set up this house, you made its design. There are interior designers nowadays, aren't there? What is to be put in a room, and how to furnish a room, how to furnish other rooms so the house is furnished - doesn't the house-owner know what is there in each room? He himself is putting things, some here, others there. So how can you think, 'How could he know?'

That man is the owner of the house, or that lady is owner of the house. Sure he knows where he had put things. He knows why he put, why he made, what is for what, what is for what. He knows it all. This is the universe. The limitation is your mind of course, it is bound in limits. A man's mind, if you gather the minds of all people and add them together, all their perceptions are also gathered together and made to fit in a dot of one millimetre or less, that is to say, you put them all in that dot, yet, there would be more space in that dot, Haji Sevim. That dot inside is almost empty. If perceptions and minds of all creation were gathered in one place, they could fit in that dot and yet, empty space remain.

Were the number of creation doubled, Allah is capable of fitting them all inside that dot. One said, "He is Allah, Who can fit the world inside a mustard seed, without diminishing the world and without enlarging the mustard seed." Is that possible in your mind? This is impossible, that that mustard seed fit the world inside that seed without enlarging the mustard seed, without reducing the world. You can't even comprehend this. If you think on it your mind will burst. The world is not reduced, that seed is not enlarged. The world would fit inside it.

Can you comprehend what is impossible, which is not possible? Allah Almighty, the Owner of Power can make it possible. They ask, how He could know the number of stars in the sky. What is there on those stars? How many continents are there on Earth? What creations live on those continents? Flying creation, crawling creation, small ones, big ones, running ones, still ones, how could He know it all? He Himself created it all.

"Alladhi ata kulla shayin khalqahu thumma hada" (20:50)

Understand. That means that every being, their coming into existence is by the Power of Janabu l-Haqq. Each being comes into existence and can be seen when the Divine Command for them to appear in existence, comes. When "thumma hada" is stated, what things are going to happen, Riham? The story of life, a detailed description/full explanation of the lifetime, since the mother's womb. Then what happens to one after birth, what events will occur, how one will go, where one will go, what work one will do, and to make one walk towards the purpose of his being created, these are all from Janabu l-Haqq. "Alladhi ata kulla shayin khalqahu thumma hada". He has granted each creation the opportunity of coming into existence. After that nothing stands still, as frozen. Now we are all sitting here but where will we go after some time? Where shall we walk, what shall we do tonight, tomorrow, the following day, throughout our life, what shall we do in 24 hours' time, are all appointed as well, nothing is left aside. He didn't just create and left it aside, forgotten. He has given hands, feet, head, mind/brain in the head and knowledge to human beings.

Other creations have instinct, and there are ones also, Malaik-i Kiram (holy angels), to control them accordingly. Although now, I say, I will stand up, leave here, I say I am tired, I feel sleepy, I am going to make ablution. Then I will bow to Janabu l-Haqq and make sajda, as well as I can. Then I will go to bed. After that InshaAllah I will get up at late night. This is the inside schedule given to each one, what they say, program. There is a small thing, which is attached to these devices. When attached, it enables the device to work. You can't touch it. It enables the device to work by itself.

Janab Allah set up such a mechanism inside us, this is making us walk towards the purpose of our creation. A closed box. It is hidden but continually being opened, being opened. It is packed and folded up but is being opened slowly, slowly. From the time we come to live until the end, then it is over, closed. The wisdom of our coming to this world is inside it. And the state in which we will be going to the Divine Presence, Janabu l-Haqq's Diwan, is inside it. What answers will be given, what questions will be asked are all inside it. Everything concerning us is inside it.

Yours is different, theirs are different, mine is different, hers is different. Everyone's purpose for being created is completely different. You think differently, someone else thinks differently. Nazli thinks different, Fatma thinks different, Riham. If you go out of here, as many people as you see, there are billions of people in the world, all differ in their ways of thinking. Just as you place a battery in a device and it enables the device to work, if you remove it, it won't work. It is like this.

So what shall we do? May Allah Dhu l-Jalal make us the ones working in His Way. May He not make us of those who go away from Him. May He grant us to meet His awliya who are drawing near to Him. As we are with them, they take us also to where they are going. Ya Rabbi, may our end be in goodness (khair). Keep us from being disgraced here and hereafter, ya Rabbi! May You cover us with Your cover. Ya Sattar al-'Uyub, our faults are many, may You conceal them. Our deficiencies are many, may You complete them.
