Sheikh Bahauddin Adil Al-Haqqani sohbat of the 11th of December, 2014.

As-salamu alaikum. We are in shahr Rajab ul Khair - shahr Safar ul khair (the month of Safar the good). Sleeping! And we are asking from Allah Almighty to make this month passing smooth. Because this month is the heaviest month in the Arabic calendar. We are hoping that Allah Almighty will save us from the badness and make the badness for non-believers.

Ya Rabbi, we want to be in the level of believers. It is not easy to be in the level of believers. But Allah Almighty is so generous that He accepts us in the level of believers. We are weak servants. Whatever we do, we are weak servants. Maybe, we are trying our best and Allah Almighty He says "la yukallifu Llahu nafsan illa wus'aha" (2:286) Allah Almighty He will never ask more than what you can do. This is Ayat Karima. Allah Almighty He wants His servant to enjoy his ibada. He doesn't want His servant to be in stress of ibada.

So many youngsters they put themselves under the stress. And they are asking "Do you think my prayer will be accepted? Do you think my Iman is accepted? Do you think I'm good one? Do you think I am this one?" And I have only, I have only one answer for these youngsters. Your intention is the most important thing by coming to Allah Almighty. The rest is nothing. Because Allah Almighty He look at you and He says to His Mala'ika/angels "O My Mala'ika look for My youngsters. They are coming to worship for Me. I know they have the ego with them, and I know the shaytan is also making waswas/whispering for them to say - "Your prayer it was not accepted. You are not believer, you are like this, you are like this..." Allah Almighty of course He knows. Allah Almighty of course He cares. Allah Almighty of course He looks for the young that they are coming with the pure heart and pure intention to Allah Almighty.

Don't be scared. Don't be scared from your prayers. Don't be scared from your Iman. Don't be scared from your belief. Just let it go, let it go to Allah Almighty. Don't be scared. Allah Almighty never gives you the things to be afraid as a system, no. Allah Almighty He is wide. Allah Almighty... Allahu Akbar! Allahu Akbar! He is so wide, so tolerant for the youngsters. You have to keep this one in your heart and when you feel your ego it's touching you, say "I'm not listening to you my ego! I'm looking to Allah Almighty's generosity that He will accept me if I'm wrong." Because this is junction for youngsters. It is very important junction for youngsters. When they come and they make mistake and then they say "Allah Almighty will never forgive us! Lets continue in our mistake." No! Don't continue in your mistake.

Allah Almighty He has door called - Bab at-Tawba. The door of Tawba/repentence. He is waiting for you to come and ask forgiveness. Don't say "Allah Almighty will never look at my face." Allah Almighty waiting for you to come and say "Ya Rabbi, forgive me!" And Allah Almighty will say "I forgive you." Don't insist in your wrong way. that think that you are sinner and you cannot be forgiven. Allah Almighty never treat you like government - "You have to be punished!" No, Allah Almighty says - "If you have a chance to return, just turn." This is Allah Almighty. Allah Almighty's so great, Allah Almighty's so fantastic. Allah Almighty He is so beautiful, Jamal. Beautiful! We are humans, when we see beautiful one or beautiful girl, we melt at this. Allah Almighty He is creating. What about Him? What about Him?

We must go for Allah Almighty. Whatever it is, this is important for the youngsters because they feel confused about their Iman, feel confused about their ibada. They feel confused - sometimes they go wrong. Allah His Name At Tawwab. Al Tawwab means? The One He forgives you. He is waiting for you to forgive you. Allah Almighty He will be very happy to forgive you. Don't forget this one. Don't forget that Allah Almighty He will be happy to forgive you. It's important to understand. The understanding is the most important thing in life. If you don't understand you will stay in your place and you don't know what to do. If you make wrong, you stay in your wrong. But if you understand that Allah Almighty He will be forgiving you, then you will run because you understand that Allah Almighty He will forgive you. It's important for you young boys. We've been young, we know. We've been passing through so many wrongs. Yes! But when Allah Almighty He want you to be with Him, you keep your heart with Him.

We are living in such a country that everything is available. Every corner has some badness. You know, this is we cannot do anything against it, because it's darkness coming everywhere in the world. Everywhere in the world is darkness. But now it's time to put the light in the heart of the young. Make them understand, if they make wrong it doesn't matter. You come. Sayyidina Jalaluddin Rumi, his famous poem it was "Whoever you are, come to Me. Whatever you are, come to Me. Whatever you've done, come to Me." He is talking from the spring of Rasulallah (saws). This is important to understand. Don't misunderstand, no. You understand well.

We are in the time of understanding. Olden days there was not much to know. The knowledge was kept in several areas, but now everybody has telephone that he can reach the knowledge in seconds. If you want to know so many things you just click and you are there. You know! Why you don't know Allah Almighty what He is giving you, what He is offering you. Why you don't understand? Why you think wrong? Don't think wrong. Think right. Think that Allah Almighty He is the forgiver and He is the One looking for you, looking for your heart. This is Allah Almighty. This is Allah! He is not like me. He is not like you. If you are insisting in your sin, if that you have a trouble. But if you make U-turn - everybody drives car - so you know what it means U-turn. You make U-turn and come back to Allah and say "Ya Rabbi I am the sinner and I am asking for forgiveness." And you will see, forgiveness coming to you. This is important for us to understand.

Now I would like to thank you for your hospitality, for receiving us in the name of Mawlana Sheikh Nazim, RahmatuLlah alayh. That he was coming for this area and I wanted to announce that Mawlana Sheikh Nazim he is loving you so much and he is caring about you so much. That's why he sent Sheikh Muhammad here to be with you and to say to you we are continuing our spiritual journey. And Sheikh Muhammad he said "I am not changing anything in Tariqa. I am not changing what Mawlana Sheikh, he began. I am not making any revolution in Tariqa." He said "Because Mawlana Sheikh he put very solid foundation that is continuing this Tariqa forever." Alhamdulillah! And you are the pearls of Mawlana Sheikh in Tariqa. You are the jewelry of Mawlana Sheikh in Tariqa. All of you, you have a different shining and you have different beauty that only the spiritual people they can see. This is Awliya'Allah the one they can see what you have in your heart.

Don't... the physical things it is going. It doesn't last, but the mineral and the jewelry inside you it will remain. This important. And my advice for you to keep, continue with Mawlana Sheikh and to keep your sincerity with what you began. And to get on and on and on in your spiritual journey. Because everybody has his own spiritual journey. Everybody has his own spiritual role. Everybody has his own spiritual way. And all will reach for destiny that Allah Almighty He appoint. And what we want? We want when we reach our destiny, we want to face Rasulallah (saws) by saying "Alhamdulillah Ya Sayyidi Ya Rasulallah, by the way that you give for us, we reach for you."

The dunya it is empty. If all the dunya belonging to you, it's worthless. It's rubbish! Why? Because at the end of the day, you have to leave everything and you have to go. They say somebody he was rich. They say this place it was for him, that place it was for him, that road it was all belonging to him, and he had a lot of money in the bank. And he have a lot employees, he has lot of companies, he has a lot of cars, planes etc. He passed away. Immediately they remove his name. They remove his name from title deeds. No, no, no it's that way, finish! He is under the ground. Now they say the richest dead man is Steve Jobs. So what? He is billionaire but he cannot spend. We are not saying anything about that gentleman, but we are giving as example for you to understand what meaning this dunya that is nothing.

Now you have to catch the real treasure that is belonging to Rasulallah (saws). Because all of us we will face the grave one day. May Allah give you long life, healthy life, but there is no way to run. We will face it. They say 3 things must happen. One is the one everybody born, must die. Definitely. Every new, must come old. Definitely, Especially people they like brand new car, after 1 year it's old. What old? And every expected, must come. And to expect the death is one of the expectations. Mahdi (as) one of the expectations. Qiyama one of the expectations. It is everything being expected, must come. These 3 rules never change.

And when somebody die, so he has to have good receiving by Azra'il (as). Because that moment also it is very important how he takes the soul - Azra'il (as). How he takes the soul. Does he take it gently? Does he take it by anger? Naudhu Billah. That moment it is one of the most critical moments in the life of the person. Very critical. There is one story about this Waliullah. He was lying in the street. His house it was so humble house, in the street. Doesn't have house. And his house only covers his feet. That's it! Nothing else, and he is waiting for Malak ul mawt (the angel of death). And Sayyidina Azra'il (as) he came to him as a man, very...he changed himself. And that Waliyullah he was telling him "O Azra'il (as) why you're hiding yourself? Show yourself." And he was telling him "I am shy to take your soul as Azra'il (as)." And he was telling him, "We are Awliya' Allah we are also shy to show you our face for you."

Say, he changed himself just to be polite taking his soul. Politely, we want to be politely giving our soul. We want to be received in mazar/grave in good receiving by Munkar wa Nakir (the questioning angels). Not to hit us on our head. "Who is your Allah? Who is your Rasulallah? What is your religion?" Hitting, hitting... Yes! All this journey it is not an easy journey. That's why the spirituality must be high with you. Must be sincerity high to have good receiving in such places. Because we don't end up here. Here is the easiest. Here Allah Almighty He give us the time for exam. We are in exam now. Whatever we do doesn't count. But the other side it will start counting from Azra'il (as). Say "O you make wrong, you make wrong.." Zero, zero, zero.... Ya Latif. Ya Latif.

We want Awliya'Allah to save us because we are weak servants. We want Murshidi Kamil (perfect guide) to save us because we are weak servants. We want them to look after us. We only have our sincerity and love for them, we don't have anything else. The rest we are doing as they want. We want to be good Muslims, we want to be good Mu'min. We want to be good murid, we want to be good in spiritual way, under the feet of Murshid, under the feet of Awliya' Allah. Because we only know that. We don't know Rasulallah if they dont' tell us. We don't know Allah if they don't tell us. Because they are explaining to us the real way, the real love, and the real sincerity.

May Allah make you sincere for them. Make us sincere for them and make us under their feet. And make us to be saved here and hereafter. May Allah bless you and thank you very much for hosting us and insha'Allah we hope to see you again, next year insha'Allah. May Allah make next year for you good year, and make it full of good things, goodness, health, wealth, and good children, and good grandchildren. And many many sadaqa, many many zakat, many many goodness for the poor people insha'Allah. May Allah bless you.

Wa min Allahi t-tawfiq, bi hurmati l-Habib, bi hurmati l-Fatiha.
