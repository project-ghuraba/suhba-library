A human being to his Lord, Janāb-i Ḥaqq. How can he achieve nearness? In this world what does a human do, for what must he strive? For your own ego you collect your own food, even more than you need. You busy yourself for more but all of that will remain in the world. What you should learn in this world is how to be near to Allāh Almighty. To Janāb-i Ḥaqq, with what means can we be near? Janāb-i Ḥaqq is nearer to us than all other things but when we say let us be near, we mean as people love those to whom they are closely related. People love and care for close relations. If someone gets nearness to Janāb-i Ḥaqq, Janāb-i Ḥaqq favours that servant both, in the world and in the Hereafter. If you want to be favoured try to be close to AllāhNone. Otherwise do as you like.

Those who don't ask to be favoured find no goodness in the world or in the Hereafter. How can one be close to AllahNone? One who remembers is one who calls on AllāhNone much, he comes near to AllāhNone. One who calls on AllāhNone often will become beloved to AllāhNone. One who remembers AllāhNone much, AllāhNone will give him from HisNone Divine Love. Then his heart is filled with Allāh'sNone Love.

Dunyā cannot fill a human heart. If you throw the whole world into his heart it will be like throwing a sesame seed into the enormous world. A human heart can only be filled with the Divine Love of AllāhNone. To whom is Allāh'sNone Divine Love permissible (ḥalāl)? It is ḥalāl to whomever calls on HimNone the most. To reach this favour try to recite the Name of AllāhNone. Say "AllāhNone". Say "Mawlá". Say "Mawlá" so that Janāb-i Ḥaqq may enliven your heart with HisNone Divine Love. Whose heart is enlivened, his body is alive (ḥayy), his body is a living being. That servant whose heart is enlivened by AllāhNone, will not rot in his grave. He will rise up just as he was buried. A human life has both outward appearance and reality. The life of humans in appearance is the same as animals. Real life is in remembering AllāhNone. Those who are remembering AllāhNone, Janāb-i Ḥaqq gives them Real Life. Their lives are not like the lives of animals. Those who say AllāhNone, their lives become sweet.

Those who don't say AllāhNone their lives are poisonous, full of difficulties. The world crushes them. The world has a mill. It throws them into this mill and crushes, grinds, grinds and shows them no relief. Since they don't believe in AllāhNone and the Hereafter, day by day envy, jealousy, will crush, grind them. They get various kinds of illnesses, miseries. They become full of troubles and grieve about these troubles in this world. Fire awaits them in their grave, the fire they send. For this reason, those who keep reciting AllāhNone will be saved. Those who don't say AllāhNone take responsibility for themselves. He takes on this responsibility. Those with minds will say AllāhNone.
