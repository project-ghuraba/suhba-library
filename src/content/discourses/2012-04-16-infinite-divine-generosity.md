Audhu Billahi min al shaytan al rajim, Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Assalatu wa s-salamu ala Rasulina Muhammad Sayyidi l Awwalin wa l-Akhirin. Madad ya Rasul Allah, Madad ya S Abdullah Faiz Daghestani madad ya S.Muhammad Nazim al Haqqani, dastur! Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fil jamia' Our way is through association & blessings are in the gathering.

Insha Allah we are intending to say a few words, with the permission of our Shaykh. May Allah help us & our Shaykh support us. May he send something to our heart, insha Allah. When we were sitting here this morning, a friend of ours made some paintings for Mawlana & put some nice writings on them. It says: "You are welcome in our darga- Come to the darga of Allah, the oceans of Allah. There is more than enough Mercy-Rahma for everyone, more than enough Forgiveness-Maghfirah, love-muhabbah. There is everything. Allah is calling the people: "Wa Allahu Yad`u 'Ila Dari As-Salam" (10:25) Allah calls man to peace, Allah calls man to beauty-"Leave the dunya-earthly things. Leave the fight & discussions".

Now let's say they give out something for dunya, for example gold. Leave aside the gold, even if they say "we give out wheat, barley" here people break each other to take it. But they are the goods of dunya, of course Allah's blessing but people so rush into it that if it is a poor neighborhood they may even kill, or hurt each other. Allah's (awj) grants can't even be counted for those whom He calls to peace. And people are heedless of it. For this Mawlana (Rumi) says: "Come, whoever you are, idolater, worshipper of fire, sinner.." Our people call Rumi a "humanist". What they mean by 'humanism' is kindness. And kindness is in Islam. One who is not Muslim has no mercy, nor has he any affection or love. All are animalistic ones. Therefore Mawlana Shaykh Nazim (has all these)..

All this in Tariqat, shows the beauty of Islam, It shows the mercy of Allah. Therefore who enters into Tariqat must leave everything, anything other than Allah & look towards Allah. He doesn't need anything else. Allah has opened. Allah is Generous, Owner of Bounty. He is the Most Generous of all because He has endless oceans. There is no end to them, nothing called "limits" there. Man's intelligence can reach only up to a certain point. His mind has a limit as well. But there is no limit for Allah. Man's mind can't reach there anyways.

He (jwa) says to everyone "Come & take. Don't fight". Man says: "No, I am Wahhabi. Beyond this, it is shirq. I can't go". If you can't go take this much, take a handful & go. Another says "I am Salafi, this, that..." He wants to limit Allah's power, Mercy as he likes. If you want, take. If you don't want, leave.

There are Tariqats which really are not Tariqat. For them, one can take as much as his naseeb. Meaning it is not as if when this one takes, other's share will be lessened. Therefore this is, for man's ego-nafs to be satisfied, says Mawlana. There is no end to the greed & shamelessness of man's ego. But Allah grants so that he is satisfied, no need for anyone else. This is how our religion is. And also, there is a Hadith of the Prophet (sas) about this. Now when you read Hadith in Arabic, you need to recite exactly as it is, even without deducting a letter. Because it is Hadith, it cannot be changed. Quran is not like this. In Quran, even if you deduct a letter, it is protected-'Inna Nahnu Nazzalna Adh Dhikra Wa 'Inna Lahu Lahafizuna" (15:9) But Hadith is not like this. Therefore it is very dangerous, when you read it in Arabic. You have to read it the same as the original. When you say it in Turkish, it turns to it's meaning anymore, so no harm.

There were recorders, meaning when revelations descended there were recorders with the Prophet (sas). One of them was Hanzala ibn al-Rabee' (ra). One day he was walking on the road and saw S. Abu Bakr (ra). S. Abu Bakr said "How are you" to Hz Hanzala. And he said "Hanzala became munafiq". "How come you became munafiq? You are recording revelation, how can you be munafiq (hypocrite)? He said: "It is true. I am recording but when I am sitting with the Prophet (sas) when he speaks to us, when he makes sohbat to us, when he (sas) tells us the Hadiths, we can feel the paradise & hell there. We are forgetting dunya. We don't want to do anything else. But when we leave, we spend time with our family, go to work and forget most of it. For this reason, I am becoming munafiq".

S. Abu Bakr & Hanzala (ra) went to the Prophet (sas) like this. Our Prophet asked, how are you? S. Abu Bakr said, "Hanzala says that he became munafiq. The situation is such and such. When he is in your presence he does everything but when he leaves heforgets. He feels himself as munafiq/hypocrite". Prophet (sas) said If it was not as you said, angels would come and greet you as you walk. They would be by your side when you sleep. So it is like this 1 hour, like that in 1 hour". He repeated this 3x.

What you are hearing here, these sohbats become barakah. Even if very little comes to man's heart it is still enough. Our prophet gave this good tidings as well because mankind is really different in sohbats & different when they leave it- Of course, getting busy with dunya, family, work etc..they forget.

Today we speak about this brother- He, this morning was giving one picture & he wrote something very nice on it. It is Mawlana Shaykh Nazim's picture & he put some word from him: "Come, take. Don't be afraid to take. Take as much as you can take from the Mercy Oceans of Allah. Allah is Generous & His Generosity is unlimited. Not like people- even people's mind also limited. But Allah's Generosity, like every attribute of His, unlimited. Even when you say unlimited, this also is not enough to describe Allah; how His Greatness from everything. So when this, Mawlana Jalaluddin Rumi also saying: "Come, whatever you are- even you worshipping fire or you are unbeliever, just come to our darga and Allah will give you".

Because you know they said..Jalaluddin Rumi now they make new fashion- they said he is humanist, or something like this. Some new word, to effect people to read him or to make Islam. Because he is greatest poet, he is a big poet and it is really very great when somebody reads it. So they want to make this difference between Islam & this Jalaluddin Rumi. But Jalaluddin Rumi said, "We take this from Islam". And Islam the greatest one. Without Islam; no humanist, no mercy, no blessing, nothing. Because Islam is the religion of Mercy or knowledge. Without this, only pretending. They pretending, even people without Islam, all they pretending. Just outlooking, not inside. Inside, inner world in Islam. Islam giving this, other only empty thing. Just to see from outside, very nice thing. But when go inside, nothing at all. So for this Sufism or Mawlana Jalaluddin Rumi say "come". When they come, who unbeliever, they know this is the right thing and coming inside.

And Allah is Generous, giving for everybody. Take as much as you can take. No limit. If you give for, sometimes they give some food or something for poor people. Sometimes maybe they kill each other to take this. This is nothing comparing to what Allah giving people. They not looking for this but looking for dunya. And Allah give for everybody he want, take mercy he can take. Somebody they don't want, like Wahhabi: "No, this is shirk. You can't do." So you can take as you like, you take little bit. If you are happy with this, ok. Salafi also like this. Tariqat people, as they want they can take. It is open- Generosity, unlimited Generosity. But people, nafs. Because always hungry, only with this.. Allah gives this to finish the hunger of ego. Because ego hungry for dunya things. But when you give this, only dunya you can not satisfy the ego. As much as you take, eat, you can't finish. But when they take from what Allah giving from Mercy Oceans, from good grants from Allah, only with this nafs-ego can be satisfied.

And there is one Hadith from Prophet (sas). He said about people- there was a sahaba. His name was Hanzala ibn al-Rabee'. He was walking and meeting S. Abu Bakr Siddiq (ra). Abu Bakr Siddiq (ra) asked him, How are you? He said, "O Abu Bakr, I became hypocrite." S. Abu Bakr, he said "What? How, you are writing Quran when Gabriel (as) coming. And Prophet (sas) made you to write one of those who writing Quran. How you say this? What did you do?" He said: "When we are in Prophet's majlis/gathering, when Prophet (sas) speaking, we feel ourselves in jannah, we all forget dunya. We forget everything. But when we go out, we going with work, or with family, with children, market, we forget many things. Many, many things we forget. So for this, I feel I become hypocrite". S. Abu Bakr (ra) didn't say anything. They went to Prophet (sas) and he (sas) asked "How are you?" He said, "Hanzala said I become hypocrite" And told him why this. And Rasulullah (sas) said, "No, you are not like this. Because if you do all what you hear, or you not forget this, you become like angel. And angel come & shake your hand, sleeping in your room, you see them. But this is 1 hour like this, 1 hour like this. He said this 3 times.

So people forget too much. But even some thing become remember, it is enough for them. Wa min Allah at-tawfiq. This is insha'Allah be barakah for beginning. Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, Al Fatiha.
