Sheikh Bahauddin Adil Al-Haqqani sobhat of the 23rd of November, 2014.

Ya Salam! It is nicer to know than to not know. Allah Almighty He prepared His servant to know. He didn't make him like an animal, He put a program in his head. Say, "you're born, you'll eat, then get children and die?" No. This is they have very limited program. Every creature has a very limited program, except Allah Almighty's servants. They have full program! Even they have so many extras but they don't know how to use it. They say the genius mind can use 5% of his mind, maybe less. 4.5 and 95.5 it is empty. You see you have 95.5 option if you can use, if you can use. This is, I don't think anybody can use. They are trying hard. Allah Almighty says "This is the limit and with this limit see what you do." And this is Ayat in Quran - hal yastawi lladhina ya'lamuna wa lladhina la ya'lamun(39:9) Allah Almighty He mentions in Holy Quran saying: "The ones they know and the ones they don't know, are they equal?" Never! Never equal.

Alhamdulillah, Allah Almighty when He gives permission for somebody to know, He opens us the door. When I look for you and I look for me I say "masha'Allah what a lucky people that Allah Almighty He gave you the pure knowledge that put you in Iman/faith." Yes! This must be appreciated from Allah Almighty. We must look at it as big wisdom. We cannot say it is a small wisdom. No, it is a big wisdom. Why? Why this wisdom? Why? Because Allah Almighty He is the one guarding His religion. He doesn't want army or He doesn't want intelligence or He doesn't want so many I.T. people, or I don't know which kind of people, to guard His religion. Allah Almighty He makes....ping, ping. You believe in e-mail, why you don't believe in Allah Almighty's e-mail? Why you don't believe in Allah Almighty's e-mail? He sends you e-mail and you accept saying, "Ya Rabbi, I am for you!" Who will take the Iman from your heart? Who? Nobody. No way! Because you've been sent e-mail, heavenly e-mail, to your heart and this you say "I accept!" The acceptance is very important. You didn't ignore it. And when you accept, everything will be open.

And you realize this world it is very empty, it is nothing even you have everything, it is not belonging to you. Only the Iman/faith is belonging to you. I'm not saying Islam, Iman. Iman is different, Islam is different. There are so many Muslims, but how? How? This is a big question. Question mark! Muslim? How you are Muslim? La hawla wala quwwata illa Billahi l-'Aliyyi l-'Azim. Say, La hawla wa la quwwata illa Billah, Ya Rabbi! Send e-mail for these Muslims also. Not only the others. Only for Muslims they need Iman e-mail. They need it, they really need it. Ya Rabbi we need this e-mail to come as soon as possible, asap! ASAP! As soon as possible! Whatever! Ya Rabbi we need this one, we need it very urgently. Because we are weak servants, we are weak in our Iman. We are weak in our Iman/faith. Maybe we are Muslims, it's ok. But this is inherited. We inherited, we are very relaxed. But we need the true, we need the soul of Islam and Iman.

This is important for us. We are not the people that we are strong with our Islam. Islam is our shell, but the Iman is our soul. This is what we want. And we are saying Ya Rabbi we want the true Iman, the true one that leads us for Your love, leads us for Rasulullah's love, it leads us for Awliya's love, for sahaba, for Ahlu l-bayt. This is a big thing to understand. When you love you are mu'min. When you hate, even if you are the best Muslim, you are nothing! There is no value! They make big huge castle but nobody living inside. No value. That will die. Definitely it will die. But when the people coming in, living in, then it's alive. Even a small cave with people, it's alive. This is what Allah Almighty He show you what is the Iman and what is Islam. We have to understand this because we are weak servants. We need guidance.

Nobody can be without guidance. Because we have shaytan, who is the first to guide you. And then the big cooperation is with your ego. And your ego cooperates with shaytan and gets you funny funny funny, ideas. This is it! But if you have a murshid - and everybody objecting for murshid - Why? Not everybody objecting for murshid. Shaytan and ego objecting for murshid, saying "we are your murshid, why you need a murshid? We are, we can handle it don't worry. We can give you good good things, don't worry!" But no, the murshid will stand up and say, "You are not right! You are not leading for the safe way. You are leading for yourself, shaytan leading for him and ego leading for himself. Even you pray, even you praise, even you do anything but it's... finally you are doing it for yourself. Because even when you pray with your egoistic behaviour, than you are not praying to Allah Almighty. You are praying for yourself, saying - "Look my prayer is so perfect! My neighbour is not so perfect, maybe my prayer gets more reward than him."

Ego doesn't order you to be bad. No! Egos can go any shape, no problem. For ego it is easy going. The ego it is very easy going. Can be anywhere! Can be mu'min, can be Muslim, can be Christian, can be Jewish, can be that, can be Buddhist. Can be everything, whatever. "What you want? Just name it!" The ego says, "what you want? Ok! I am there. What you want? I am there...." This is the ego. It is the most dangerous instrument been created, the most dangerous. That's why when Rasulullah (saws), the sahaba they came from, I think the battle of Uhud, and they've been very tired, like finished! Jihad-ul asghar - the small Jihad you made. The say "Ya Rasulullah how come we are almost half of us gone and we are very difficult we win and you tell us this?" He says, "Yes, this is nothing comparing for Jihad-ul Akbar." They say, "what is Jihad-ul Akbar Ya Rasulullah? What is it, let us know." He said "You have to fight your ego." This is the one that made them sahaba. This is the one making them shining, this is the thing that made them huge.

124.000 sahaba of Rasulullah, all of them they know how to deal with their ego and all of them they know how to be with Rasulullah. This is Rasulullah - he was Murshid 'Azam - The Great Murshid/Guide. This is, he is Rasulullah? Yes! But in the same time he is the Murshid 'Azam. The one he is giving the big irshad/guidance this is giving how to deal with your ego, how to deal with your life, and how to deal with Allah Almighty, nearer to Allah Almighty. He is the sun, he is the shining, he is the murshid. Everybody thinks Rasulullah's coming, they give him the Quran in his hand, saying "this is the Quran ok, and I have maybe 50 Hadith take it ok, finished!" Yahu! What is this? We are nothing and we speak when you put the speaking in words, you see billions of words. Not millions - billions of words during your... born until you die - I don't know how many billions of words you speak. Nobody realized this.

And Rasulullah only spoke 5 Hadith or 50 Hadith or 100 Hadith? What is this? Don't mis-estimate Rasulullah. Don't mis-estimate him. This is Rasulullah "wa ma arsalnaka illa rahmatan lil 'alamin" (21:107) This is "I am sending you for the benefit of humanity, for mercy for them." And imagine what a huge murshid is Rasulullah. This is, his irshad, that means his knowledge and his guidance, it can be not enough for our planet - it will be enough for whole universe. This is Rasulullah don't mis-estimate. This makes the Muslims very weak! This is the point how the Muslims they become very weak. How these big Muslims they become as a shell? How these Muslims they become unwanted? Because they mis-estimate Rasulullah. They disrespect Rasulullah. They make Rasulullah as ordinary person. And Allah Almighty He makes them down.

This is how Allah Almighty...You are fighting with Allah Almighty. You are not fighting with anything else. Because you are fighting the beloved of Rasulullah, S.Muhammad (saws). You are mis-estimating him and Allah Almighty He makes you to be dhallin/humiliated. This is you have to wake up. You have to understand who is Rasulullah and you have to not mis-estimate Rasulullah. This is not a joke. Rasulullah is not a joke. Rasulullah is the Murshid 'Azam. What you think, he is sleeping in his grave? What you think why we are here, because he is sleeping there? What a foolish... today ignorant salafi wahabi, the stupid Muslims! I am sorry to say, I am very sorry to say about because you have to be the leading people to not mis-estimate Rasulullah (saws). This is Rasulullah, this is not a joke. You look for his history, you look how he begin, and how he ended, and how his people now, his ummati Muhammad (saws). How it became? Why?

Why you don't love Rasulullah because you are jealous from him. Because your ego is jealous from him, shaytan is leading you for wrong way. This is not a joke. Allah Almighty, when you play with Allah Almighty, Allah Almighty He press you! He sent the non-Muslims the non- believers to press you because you are enemy of Rasulullah (saws). You are enemies of Awliya' Allah. Allah Almighty is not sleeping. Everything in His hands. Must wake up! I am saying Muslims must wake up. Muslims must be mu'min. Take your shell off your Islam and put the Iman in your heart and believe in Murshid 'Azam, Sayyidina Muhammad (saws) Murshid 'Azam. Because we are his, we owe him. In old days they say, if somebody gives you a cup of coffee, you have to remember this cup of coffee 40 years. And you have to say, "This man he give me a cup of coffee and he was respecting me and he was offering me a cup of coffee." Rasulullah he gave you everything!

And you say "Ah! He brought the Quran. If they give me the Quran, I also distribute it." Why you don't distribute it today? And you have everything. How you can make somebody to be Muslim how can you make somebody to be mu'min? It's easy? No, it's not. It's the most hard thing to bring somebody from some place to the other place. Especially the belief, especially the belief. It is not easy at all if there is no love. The love makes you to believe in everything. Rasulullah first he gave the love, he gave the respect, he make the human the humanity. He didn't make discrimination, you are black you are white you are this...no, no, no. I am looking for your heart. I'm looking for your heart. I want your heart. There is no color of the heart. If you open everybody's heart it's same. Same shape, same color, the blood same color everything is same color. That means one that means one, we are one Alhamdulillah. Allah Almighty He gave us the oneness as humankind. But He likes to make it as a flower - some yellow, some green, some white, some dark, some... Allah Almighty He loves it this way. And when you look at it this way you see how beautiful it is - the creation of Allah Almighty. How it's beautiful, Subhan Allah.

That's it is very important in our side of belief and in our side of love, to be together. And Allah Almighty He didn't want this to be as discrimination, He wanted it to be as one. And now we are saying, may Allah accept our dua and make the true Iman and true Islam to come, to come over for all the hearts. We are not saying for them - you are bad, but we want them to be good. We want them to be good. We don't have any power. We don't have anything. We only have power of dua, we only have power of dua. We say, Ya Rabbi we are with You. Ya Rasulullah we are with you. We hope that you love us as we love you. This is our hope, we are very weak servants we don't have anything. But we have to say the Haqq. And this Haqq must be said.

May Allah bless you and may Allah make it easy on us, in our spiritual journey that it is very hard journey after Mawlana Sheikh. Now we realize how hard work he was doing and how he was so active in his work. And now we are trying to do maybe 1% of his work and we see how it was hard, and how it was difficult. May Allah bless you and may Allah make it easy on us and make it easy on you.
