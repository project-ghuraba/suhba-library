Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

A’ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm:

(Qur’ān 39:09). Allah states in the Holy Qur’an: ‘Say, “Are those who know equal to those who do not know?”’ Are those who know equal to those who do not know? Will they be at the same level? Never. What do they know? Knowledge. What is knowledge? Knowledge is to know Allah, to believe in Allah. This doesn’t happen by reading a lot. There are plenty of those who do that and deny Him even more. This is not knowledge but ignorance, nothing else. Even for a small matter, they can read for years, take lessons, do this and that, but they deny Allah Azza wa Jalla all at once. They also make children deny Him.

This is not knowledge, this is ignorance. Allah Azza wa Jalla describes their state in two words; are those who know equal to those who do not know? No. Those who know are the chosen ones, the most acceptable ones and the highest ones, firstly in the sight of Allah and then among people. Those who do not know are ignorant. Those who teach ignorance are the lowest, the inferior and the unacceptable ones.

The more you acknowledge Allah, the higher you rise. The more you deny Him, the lower you become, the lower you fall and the more worthless you become. Allah is great. Be with Allah. Do not be with those who think they are great. Those people are deceiving themselves, nothing else. They deceive unfortunate people like them. Those who follow them will be of low level like them, and will not be accepted.

Let them say that they are such and such as much as they want. When they have no value in the sight of Allah, it is the same in the sight of people. Sometimes there may be people who claim that they are such and such on the outside, but it has no benefit. In the sight of people who see the truth, people who know the truth, they have no value and no worth. The value is to be with those who know and recognize Allah. May Allah make us one of them insha’Allah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
