They said there is Hasirpush Dede in some place in Karacaahmet (a cemetery outside of Istanbul). "Go to Hasirpush Dede. Let that person make a prayer and stay there." It's not appropriate for our nature. Saints are on a spiritual journey. They have reached a certain station. Who has reached that, acts with adab - he does no harm. Therefore, they send there.

The number of great awliyā’ never decreases. There are as many big awliyā’ as there have ever been after our Prophet. However, adab yā Hū. There is no permission to show their will. Not time yet, now at some places, Kadir bey, near road construction, in order to show the way, they put temporary traffic lights. They put them for a reason. It makes people stop before reaching the actual lights. They give way there. Now saints - if people with spiritual powers wanted to make a temporary barricade, they could stop it. However, they are afraid from others too. If they look at them, they will start trembling too. And if saints say "Don't touch", it's finished.

There are many who can stop from majādhīb (fools of God). They are bothered but if I look like this, they will tremble and shake, out of adab. Otherwise, there are many saints who can stop them [secularism in Turkey]. Technology cannot do it. If saints turn the power switch off, it will become dark. Technology will be finished, soon. But just a space of three to four minutes is left until the actual destination. In these three to four minutes, those who have waited patiently for so many years, their waiting caused them to not put their will into it.

Because when they enquired about the dethronement of Sulṭān Ḥamīd, may paradise be his abode, in the Ottoman assembly of deputies, you know that matter, the Ders Vekili (Commissioner of Medrese Education) or Muftī gave an advice about dethroning the Padishah.

KM: Fetva Emini (head mufti of fatwa department) Hacı Nuri Efendi, he said "The dethroning is ominous. Don't do it."

SN: "It has dire consequences. Don't dethrone the Pādishāh". He informed them about its dire consequences. They knew it. Those Hojas (scholars) weren't empty. They weren't smoking hojas, hojas of the Republic time. What shall we do? We said Republic hojas - I am also shaykh of republic time [joking].

It's ominous. Because when you put your will in it, it becomes bad. Every time they dethroned a Pādishāh in the history of the Ottomans, they suffered from its ominous consequences for years. They paid much for it. That man [Hacı Nuri Efendi] was an alert one. He had warned about not going in this direction. But it was the Divine will. Dawlat-i ‘Āliyyah (exalted empire). Soon that matter became a big matter. It was a reason for the fall of the huge empire, because they inserted their will into it.

According to Allāh's rule, Allāh has decreed. If He wants to remove, He will. If we interfere in it, it will make a mess. Bad luck and disasters will come. And it did. And now in this century Awliyā’ don't want to interfere with their will. Up until the end their [secular system] motors have burnt out, their fuel is over and tyres are broken. Landing is over and their work is over. Nothing has remained. No need for putting your will into it and ordering destruction. They finished themselves. They finished themselves in 70 years. They have nothing in their achievements... None. They didn't accept any of the surviving real ‘ulamā. And those who grew up in modern times had no dignity. When a few people with no dignity joined the assembly, he [Sultan Abdul Hamid] couldn't do anything among so many enemies of religion.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh there was no activity done by consulting or asking us. All responsibility and sin are upon them. They did it themselves by entering it. And it fell down on their heads.

Allāh Allāh, Allāh Allāh, Allāh Allāh, Azīz Allāh. Allāh Allāh, Allāh Allāh, Allāh Allāh, Karīm Allāh. Allāh Allāh, Allāh Allāh, Allāh Allāh, Subḥān Allāh. Allāh Allāh, Allāh Allāh, Allāh Allāh, Sulṭān Allāh.

You are Sulṭān. Send us the Sulṭān of the world. Send us the Sulṭān of the religion. Send us the Sulṭān of Islām. Send us the Sulṭān of the Ottomans. And let us be with the Sulṭāns. It's not an easy matter to be with the Sulṭān. His fire burns our sins.

Fātiḥah.
