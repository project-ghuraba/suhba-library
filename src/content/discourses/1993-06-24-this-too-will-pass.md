Tarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah. Shāh-i Naqshband is the īmām of the Naqshbandī ṭarīqah so he is a great one. "Our way is based on association, sohbet" he said. Now the nights are short not long, so let's say a few words.

Allāh Almighty (jj) allowed us to make dhikr. If Allāh (jj) didn't love you, He wouldn't gather you here. The ones He (jj) doesn't love don't come here. Who are coming are the ones Allāh (jj) loves. They come to mention His great and holy names. He (jj) allows us to mention them and sends us here. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh. Gratefulness is to Allāh (jj). He (jj) sends His servants whenever He (jj) wants, from wherever He (jj) wants. This too shall pass Yā Hū.

Another Friday night is passing. We don't know if there will be a next Friday. You can't control what is gone. The past has already passed. You should be careful about the moment you are in. They say the past is history, the future is an unborn lamb - they used to say like that. Now it has passed, you can't control it. You can't control the future because you don't know what the future holds. Life doesn't go according to your programme. We also have our own programme - they call it a small bite of a programme. Here you are. Yā Rabbī shukr. You can't say that you own the moment you are in. It isn't like that, so quickly is it passing. When going fast in a car, from left and right, everything is looks like it is moving backwards. We are going forward. The closest thing passes the quickest. The farthest seems as if it is fixed but then it also passes.

Another Friday night is passing but this Friday night is the first Friday of the Hijrī year, our calendar. Yes we are Muslims. We have everything, Islām has a unique personality. We have our own calendar. We have our own days and nights, so we don't need to follow the European or the Christian world's calendar. If we didn't have a calendar we would need a beginning for a calendar. The Christian world asserts that their calendar starts with the birth of Jesus (as).
