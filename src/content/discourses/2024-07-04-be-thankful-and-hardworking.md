As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Wa-idhā an‘amnā ‘alá l-insāni a‘raḍa wa-na’ā bi-jānibihī, wa-idhā massahu sh-sharru kāna ya’ūsa" (17:83). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘aẓīm. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is saying, when people receive good things, they become ungrateful. They resent the people who did something good for them and avoid those people. They don't thank them. And on the other hand, when they are harmed, they become helpless and hopeless. The reason for their hopelessness is their ungratefulness towards those who helped them, so that help doesn't last. When they are ungrateful, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla knows everything. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla sees everything. Therefore, unless they repent, they will be punished. Their punishment will come both here and hereafter.

But people who are not with AllāhNone in this world are always in a hopeless state. For the slightest thing, they lose all their hope, and their dreams are gone. Their thoughts become bad because they did not do any good. This is true for all of us. As much as you strive to please your ego, don't expect any gratitude from it. Therefore, you must always keep it busy on the way of AllāhNone, on the way of earning a livelihood, by studying or worshipping. It needs to be always engaged in something. People who are idle will be under the command of shayṭān and their ego. When there is nothing for them to do, the shayṭāns quickly fill up their time. The whole world is in this situation.

In the past, people used to say that they had a lot of time. Nowadays, even if there is free time, people are enslaved by these devices (phones, etc.). They are busy with meaningless things. They are not on the way of AllāhNone. These are the things disliked by AllāhNone and we are filling our time with them. We must fill our time with remembrance of AllāhNone, by contemplating and worshipping AllāhNone. Even if you don't worship, you must make shukr, give thanks, for the things AllāhNone gave you and thank AllāhNone. Whatever AllāhNone has given, it is our duty to thank and praise HimNone. When the ego is free, it causes harm. You must not leave it free. You must always keep it busy by doing acts of goodness and by teaching goodness. You must go to work and deal with work. We are working now. They say to work is worldly. No, it is not worldly. If you do it for the pleasure of AllāhNone, AllāhNone will reward you for it. It will be as if you perform worship. Because you spend your time earning ḥalāl rizq (permitted provision) for livelihood. It has benefit in all its aspects. It has both physical and spiritual benefits, with the latter being more important. Therefore, being free and lazy – indolence and laziness are attributes of shayṭān. Not the attributes of shayṭān, but rather the attributes of those who follow shayṭān. He advises them not to work because others will benefit from it. "Why are you working? Why are you tiring yourself? Relax. Go for a vacation. Go here and there. Make sure to entertain yourself. That is your profit." They say, "How many times will we come to this world?" If you came once, you will come for the second time. They can't comprehend that, or they don't accept it. But that is true. When that happens, those who wasted their time will regret it.

May AllāhNone protect us. As we said, laziness is not the attribute of the prophets, ṣaḥābah, awliyā’. Not at all. They were never lazy. Prophets always used to strive nonstop to give guidance to their people. They prayed, met with people, and helped people. Therefore, may AllāhNone give us from their attributes, in shā’a Llāh. May laziness be lifted from us. May indolence be lifted from us, in shā’a Llāh.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying, "Wa-idhā an‘amnā ‘alá l-insāni a‘raḍa wa-na’ā bi-jānibihī, wa-idhā massahu sh-sharru kāna ya’ūsa" (17:83). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘aẓīm. This āyah describing human being situation. Nearly all human being like this: if AllāhNone give them everything, they will be thought this coming from themself and not accepting it is from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, not thanking HimNone. They, nothing at all about this. They are happy. "We have everything, we have money, we have land, we have wife, children, everything." This is from AllāhNone and a ni‘mah provision from AllāhNone and AllāhNone give them. "If WeNone gave this," HeNone said, "for this people," Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying, "what they will do? They will be, not be thankful."

This the most attribute of human being – to not be giving any value for other people what they do, not be happy with them. And said, "No." And especially for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, they will be saying, "We are not caring about this. We only we are happy, we enjoying ourself." But after this, if anything happen and some wrong thing happen for them, they becoming hopeless. No hope for them and they will be sad, miserable. They will be unhappy people. Why? Because they are not appreciate what AllāhNone gave them and thanking HimNone for what HeNone gave. This is attribute of most of human being and for this too, our attribute – following our ego and shayṭān.

What ego and shayṭān saying? To not be thankful for anything you get from people or from other, especially from AllāhNone. They not thinking at all for AllāhNone, for their CreatorNone. Maybe they think for people, sometimes they thank for them. But for especially, which most important thing, to thank for AllāhNone, they are not thanking HimNone and not appreciate what HeNone gave them. For this, when they are become, something happen to them, no hope for them. They will be finish. They will be not happy at all. They looking for to something to make them happy. But there, the biggest thing there in front of them, they are not looking for this. They going another small thing to be happy. But no benefit.

For this, our, what ProphetNone saying, teaching us, to train, to not follow your ego. Don't follow shayṭān. If you follow them, you will be at the end hopeless and no benefit for anything what you done. For this, special thing for human being to not be lazy. Laziness not from attribute of prophets and holy people. They all hard worker. You cannot find one lazy from them. Their whole life they say to, for AllāhNone, "Wa-maḥyāya wa-mamātī" (6:162) – "All our life for AllāhNone, for sake of AllāhNone, for pleasure of AllāhNone. No any [other] thing for us." This is the hard worker to save human being. But human being, they don't care about them. They not care about anything. Only they care about their ego, their pleasure. And that pleasure become, [in] the end, no benefit, no any happiness for them. AllāhNone keep us with this holy people in their way to be hard worker, to not be lazy, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
