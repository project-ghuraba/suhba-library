Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar La Ilaha illa Allah, wa Allahu Akbar Allahu Akbar wa Lillahi Al Hamd. Allahumma Salli 'ala Sayidina Muhammad Habibika wa Rasulika, wa Nabiyuka. Shafi' Al Mudhnibin Sahib Al Maqam Al Mahmoud Sayidina Muhammad (saws), Zid'hu Ya Rabbi 'Izzan wa Sharafan Nuran wa Surura wa Ridwana wa Sultanan Subhana Allah! We say audhu Billah min al shaytan al rajim Bismillah Al Rahman Al Rahim.

We start to speak with Your Name O our Lord and with salams & salat on Your Beloved Al Mustafa (saws) to glorify him. Everything is dressed with greatness if Allah (swt) grants it greatness. Everything becomes glorified & great. This is enough. Religion teaches how Allah (swt) dressed His Prophets (as) with greatness. And first duty for us is to learn to glorify the ones Allah (jwa) glorified. Who does not glorify will be brought down and once he is down he is under the feet of people. And who appreciates what Allah (swt) appreciates, he is glorified. If you glorify what Allah (swt) glorifies with such greatness, you will find that glorification in Malakut/Heavens. Because the glory in this world has no value, no value. But we have been ordered to put all our effort to gain glory in Heavens.

Who are the most foolish ones? The ones who run after people to glorify them in this world. That man has no mind and no value in this world or in the Hereafter. And therefore O people, O Muslims and believers, the first order is to follow whom Allah (swt) glorified and glorify him (saws). You will find this glorification in Heavens. And the glorification of Heavens is the target. This world what is its value? This world is a carcass. Truly this world is a carcass & those who want it want wordly pleasure & ranks and richness and high ranks in this world. Who wants this world is asking for this world. The honourable Hadith of the Prophet (saws): "This world is a carcass & dogs ask for it." Enough! And unfortunately in our days now we say Audhu Billah min al shaytan al rajim, Bismillah Al Rahman Al Rahim.

What is religion? To glorify what Allah (swt) glorified. And what is disbelief? To value and ask for what Allah (swt) has dishonoured. O people, O believers, do you understand Arabic? You don't? Translate it so you understand. "This world is a carcass & dogs ask for it." Who wants something from this world & expends all their efforts to reach it, his attributes are like the attributes of dogs, filthy, filthy with no value. Dogs run after carcasses. And who wants this world & runs after it his attributes are like the dogs who look for carcass, carcasses.

Unfortunately in our times now the majority of people are asking for carcasses, young people & old ones, educated ones & ignorant ones, all of them are running after carcasses. Especially if it is a carcass thrown in the desert. People start plotting to get the carcass so it is all for them. Take it! O leaders of nations spend all your efforts to get it so you reach it. What did the first tyrant in Egypt gain? What did the second tyrant reach with his presidency? What did the third one from Egypt reach? And he was running after a carcass. All of them put all their efforts to take as much as possible from the carcass, carcasses. And they spent all their efforts for this goal. Which one of us can get more chance, more chance from the carcass...

Especially Egypt is a fat carcass. After the Kings the carcasses left were fat, and people put all their efforts to take it. Some of them were more eager than others and they did what they must to get the carcass, the Egyptian carcass. What happened to them? They didn't get it. First one, second one, third one what happened to him, Allah (swt) knows. O people, Egyptian people think, pay attention. If you do not take consideration of what happened to them, it may happen to you too. What was waiting for them, will await you also. At the end where you will be buried Allah (swt) knows. And after burial in their grave how they will be treated Allah (swt) knows.

O people religion is based on advice Take the advice. So what happened to them in their life in this world... and not knowing what happens to them in Barzak. So consider this, O scholars of Azhar Al Sharif. Teach people. Let them learn what is for them & what is needed for them in this life in the world. As the Prophet (saws) of the end of times said "contentment is a never ending treasure." The opposite of it is greed, which has no benefit for you. The Pharaohs hundreds of them were greedy & what happened to them? Go and look in the museums what their end was like.

Take wisdom people of sound minds. The straight way and as the Prophet (saws) of the end of times said "Religion is advice". This advice is for today and it is enough for everyone. If they don't learn their end will be like the end of the past Pharaohs or the new ones. Naudhu Billah from following our egos and running after the carcass of this world.

May Allah (swt) forgive us & save us & pardon us & have mercy on us and grant us goodness, and guide us. Your guidance is the best. O people ask for what is best. Don't run after what is filthy & dirty. You will win & you will be happy & you will be raised in your station and your affairs in this world & in the Hereafter. Look at the end of the tyrants, what happened to them & what will happen to the armies of tyrants.

May Allah (swt) keep us to follow the way of righteousness O Egyptians, and all the Muslims. May Allah (swt) forgive us & save us & forgive us & have mercy on us & grant us guidance & correct our situation & of all Muslims & grant us victory over the tyrant people.
