As-Salāmu ‘Alaykum wa RaḥmatuLlāhi wa Barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu BiLlāhi Minash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. BismiLlāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm.

Wa ṣ-Salātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Akhirīn.

Madad yā RasūlAllāh, Madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi RasūliLlāh, Madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

Dastūr Mawlana Sheikh Abdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistāni, Sheikh Muḥammad

Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad.

1- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Protect yourselves from hellfire by giving sadaqah, even if with one half of a date.” So, sadaqah protects a person both physically from evil, illness, and calamity in this world, and also from hellfire. Don’t say it’s a little or a lot; just give. What’s half a date? It’s nothing. Even that can protect you from hellfire.

2- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Protect yourselves from Hellfire by giving sadaqah, even if with one half of a date. If you cannot find even that, then protect yourselves from Hellfire by (saying) a kind, pleasant word.” In the past, they couldn’t even find that; they couldn’t find anything to eat. Not even half a date, let alone a full date. Instead, they sought to be kind winning the hearts of those who desired it with kind words.

3- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says: Our Holy Prophet is saying “Even if it’s only half a date, put a veil between yourself and Hell.” So, this sadaqah will act as a veil (barrier) between you and Hell; you won’t fall into the fire.

4- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet is saying “Give as much as you can afford. Do not hoard your wealth, for Allāh will withhold His blessings from you.” Give as much as you can afford. It’s not that you should give more than you can afford, but give as much as you are able, says our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. When you hoard the excess of your wealth, Allāh will withhold His blessings from you. Whatever you give as sadaqah, you will surely receive the reward for it; it will not cease.

5- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet is saying “O Asma,” our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam addressed Asma, the daughter of Sayyidina Abu Bakr. “Do not hoard your wealth, lest Allāh withhold His bounty from you.” The stingier you are, the less you will receive; if you give, more will come to you, says our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam.

6- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Know that there is no one among you for whom the wealth of his heirs is not dearer than his own wealth.” So, a person loves what will be left for the heir from wealth and property more than his. Yet it belongs to his heirs. But he loves it, doesn’t give it away, hoards it and keeps it. “Your true wealth is what you send forth for the Hereafter. And the wealth of your heirs is that which you have left behind in this world.” So, the true wealth is what you send forth; what’s yours is the wealth you send to the Hereafter. What’s here remains for the heir, and it is of no benefit to you.

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Surely, Allāh will admit three people to Paradise because of a morsel of bread, a handful of dried dates, or something similar given to the poor.” That is, if something is given as charity from a house— dates, provisions, meat, water, whatever it may be—three people from that house will enter Paradise because of it, says our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. “One is the head of the household, the house owner and who gives the orders in it, who ordered the charity to be given. Another is the housewife who prepared the meal. And the third is the helper (servant) who delivered the charity to the poor.” These are the virtues of sadaqah.

8- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Allah Almighty accepts sadaqah.” This means that when you give charity, Allāh accepts it. “He receives it with His right Hand, meaning with good acceptance.” Of course, Allāh is free from such descriptions such as a hand or so, but metaphorically, it means He accepts it. “Allāh nourishes this sadaqah for its giver, like a small ration, like feeding a newborn horse.” This sadaqah, in the sight of Allah, becomes something that is nourished for the giver. Like feeding a newborn horse. “So much so that the reward of a single morsel of sadaqah nourishes until it grows as large as Mount Uhud.” Even though it’s a small thing, Allāh counts it as large as Mount Uhud, which as you know is a huge mountain in Madinah Munawwarah. That’s how great it becomes in the sight of Allāh.

9- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Giving charity extinguishes the wrath of the Lord and prevents a bad death.” Charity is very important. It extinguishes the wrath and anger of Allāh. No matter what sins you have committed, with that charity, Allāh will forgive those sins. It also protects you from a bad death. May Allāh protect us.

10- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Surely charity extinguishes the fire of the grave.” That is, even in the grave, which, may Allāh forbid, might be a pit of hell, charity extinguishes that fire. “Surely the believer will be shaded by the shade of his sadaqah on the Day of Judgment.” On the Day of Judgment, there will be no trees or anything else. But when you’ve given sadaqah, sadaqah has its shade. The believer will be shaded under its shade.

The Messenger of Allāh says the truth in what he said or as he said.
