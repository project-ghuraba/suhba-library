In this 24 hours' time that which we call valuable time is when Janab Mawla and Rasul Kibriya gaze on servants. These are precious times. The time between asr prayer and maghrib prayer is precious. Janabu l-Haqq gazes on His servants' worship. Rasulullah also gazes on his ummah at this time, between asr (late afternoon) and maghrib (evening). The worshippers come for the asr prayer, whatever their qismet, they get it until asr time. When asr time comes, they close up shop for that day. Today's earning has been collected. "So I served Mawla and now go home," saying like this, they keep themselves busy with worldly affairs, earning their living until asr.

People would keep the service of Janabu l-Haqq in olden times. Someone going to market, bazaar early was not acceptable. Someone going latest was acceptable, it is said. Then, someone coming back early was acceptable, one who was late to return from market was not acceptable. Nowadays as we are wholly after dunya, we lost satisfaction with moderate amounts. We are eating more than we need, we are buying what we will not use. We are continually collecting and piling up. And it is useless, there will be questioning for them on Day of Qiyamat.

When you increase possessions you will be asked whether you used them well or not, for what you ran after them? Why did you collect? Why did you pile up? Janab Allah will ask. Use only the amount you need for yourselves. You accumulated the excess, why did you? Was your intention to buy rizq/provision when the doors of rizq were closed? The doors of rizq are open, heavenly blessings are descending, so also, our food and drink for this world are allotted. Should the sky be clamped with iron and steel, its surface covered by rocks, a believer mustn't wonder or doubt from where will I eat and get my food.

One who is a true believer does not have doubt about his rizq. Whoever has doubt about his rizq it means he has doubts about Al-Razzaq. Al-Razzaq is Janabu l-Haqq. He said: I will give, I am the Guarantor, I will give your rizq and you cannot leave this world before using up your rizq. When one's rizq is used up then he will leave. Rizq too is appointed. Your breaths that you inhale are also of a limited number. Your breaths will not be less than that number, or more than that number.

When one reaches the state of coma, the time of death, when he is near leaving this world, four angels come, it is said. That is the time when one's eyes are fixed and his eyes no longer see this world. As he is not looking at this world, that one begins to look at akhirat. One after one, four angels come to him. One comes and says "as-salamu alaika ya 'abdAllah! O servant of Allah I salute you, I was the one appointed for your food, in order for the food to reach you, from wherever, east to west, I was appointed to bring it to your presence because on every morsel is written the name of one who is going to eat it. No one else but you can eat that morsel. His name is on it.

The one who is from Ahlullah can see on the fruits of a tree, the names of those who are going to eat those fruits. No disorder. It is neat and systematic. Everyone's food is reserved. That angel says, "I travelled this world from east to west and there is no morsel left on which your name is written," and then it leaves. This happens in a moment. A second angel comes, he too salutes and says, "I am appointed for supplying the water you will drink in this world. No water is left to pass your throat any more. That is why when water is given he can't swallow, the water spills out. Only with cotton his lips are made wet, water can't pass through. He says “there is no water left to pass through your throat and leaves”.

Then a third angel comes, he salutes, and says, "O ‘abdAllah, O servant of Allah, I am the angel appointed to supply you with breath. There is no breath left for you to inhale". Three of them. Unless that angel conveys it you can't breathe, with that angel breath comes in. Nowadays, they try to give oxygen by pumps connected to mouth or nose. His soul is leaving his body, oxygen is of no use. Will you make him breathe by force? He is dead. When oxygen is given, it means he is near death. It has no use, that breath is not real breath. The angel says the breaths for you to inhale are finished. He leaves and the fourth angel comes.

When the fourth one comes he salutes and says, "O ‘abdAllah I am the angel responsible for your time of death. As for your life, your time is over, beyond this you have no life to live, now is the last limit of your life,” he says. The Angel of Death salutes and takes one's life. A believer, if one is a real believer, his soul talks to Azrail alaihi s-salam. Azrail alaihi s-salam says, come and surrender. The soul says, "I didn't enter this body by your order, so I will not come out by your order." Azrail alaihi s-salam says, “I came here by Divine order of Janabu l-Haqq.” The soul says, “show your court order and I will submit.”

Then Azrail alaihi s-salam stands in The Presence, making sajda to Janab Rabbu l-'Izzat and says “Ya Rabbu l-'Izzat your servant is not surrendering his soul. He is asking to see an order. Then in Azrail alaihi s-salam's palm are written majestic words of light. As he shows this to the believer, the soul perches on it at once. The soul falls in love with the Light of Janabu l-Haqq's name and leaves the body at once. That is why a believer doesn't notice how he dies. Do you notice when you fall asleep? How did you fall asleep? In a moment you wake up, in a moment you fall asleep. There is nothing in between.

A dying believer’s soul when passing to Akhirat, comes out in a moment. One doesn't notice he is dead until his soul is taken by angels and brought to The Presence, to the heavens. In the heavens Janab Allah praises that believing servant. He says, "My servant," then He says, "O My servant, now an angel will come to your body in the grave for its questioning". So that one gets ready to answer that angel. When he returns he enters his body and awakens. Like waking up from sleep.

The dead one awakens in his grave at that moment, and in that pitch blackness calls out to his son, daughter, his wife, "Turn on the lights, is there a power cut? Why is everywhere in darkness?" Then as he moves his hands and looks around, he finds black soil and then there is said to be a deep silence with no sound at all. When he feels the soil then does he realize I have passed away and entered the grave. Then does he say I am dead. At that time he knows he is among one of the dead in the graveyard. And just then angels come.

At that moment two questioning angels come. They make him sit and ask him their first question. Their first question is “Who is your Lord? Whom did you keep as Lord? Did you know your Lord? Who is your Lord, O servant,” and as the angels ask, if you can answer "my Lord is Allah," you will be in good condition. If you can't say this, shaytan speaks. When it finds someone to carry it, it won't climb off his neck. Shaytan has made so many its donkey. I say in Turkish (eshek/ass), lets not say, merkep (donkey). Not only do we say donkey, we emphasize and say dooonkeeey!

Awliyaullah nowadays see most people from east to west as they appear in attributes of a donkey. Why? Because shaytan is on their neck. Examine yourself, look at yourself! If it is on your necks also. How many times it comes? How many times it rides? Bayazid Bastami Hazrat was in Madina Munawwara and as he was coming out of Rawda Sharif from the door at the northeast, he saw shaytan waiting outside the door. It was holding many bridles, its hands and shoulders full of bridles for animals.

Bayazid Bastami looked at it, asked what it was doing there because awliyaullah, - "Don't sleep Hajji Ahmet Efendi," awliya can see shaytan. As they can recognise shaytan, they also recognise people surrounded with shaytanic attributes. Awliya know both shaytanic servants and shaytanic jinn also. Awliyaullah knows whether one is rahmani or shaytani. He saw it at the door with bridles in its hands. He asked why it was waiting there. It said, "I am waiting for my men, my rides, in order to bridle and ride them".

Even at the Rawda Sharif in Madina Munawwara. Do you know any place without shaytan, Hodja Efendi? He says no. Is there a place without shaytan in your village? If there is we may go there. Is there in Istanbul? Or in Ankara there are more? Is there any place that shaytan is not? Bursa? Erzurum? It is everywhere. Nowhere is shaytan not present. Bayazid Bastami asked what it was doing with them? "I hold these bridles to fit them to my men, - tired of waiting for them to come out so I can bridle them." He asked if it waited only at that door. It said, "no, I go to the doors of mosques, I pick more on jamaats of mosques. I follow Muslims more. I am strictly following Muslims because others anyhow, are already mine. We are joined. I bridle them and let go or I bind them to an iron stake. They can't escape, they keep grazing. Now I look for Muslims going to mosques. I will bridle them," it says. "I send my helpers to the doors of the mosques, as people are coming out they quickly tie them up them with tobacco. They say, 'be quick, come and smoke.' " They don't understand when it's said tobacco. Cigarettes. Marlboro. Ya! Its orders are such.

The one outside says, "enough! Hodja is keeping it long, we are fed up! The khutba is long, he keeps the namaz long." It calls from outside, "come quickly," it says, "put incense on me because I love its incense very much". Isn't it so? May Allah protect us from the control of our nafs over our beings. May we control our nafs! May we keep servanthood to You ya Rabbi! May we not be left to our evil egos!

Zid Habibaka 'izzan wa sharafa wa nooran wa surura wa ridwanan wa sultana bi hurmati l-Habib, bi hurmati-l Fatiha.
