As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Today (tonight) is the 27th of Ramaḍān according to our calendar, the 28th according to some, and different dates for others. May Allah bless it and accept from us, inshā’Allah. Every year, people consider the 27th to be Laylatu l-Qadr; which is tonight, (today) the 26th, and tomorrow is the 27th [An Islamic day is from sunset to sunset]. Since Laylatu l-Qadr mostly falls on this date, Muslims strive to observe it with worship.

These are beautiful occasions, of course. There needs to be an occasion for people to perform their worship with more enthusiasm. Therefore, Allah will surely grant them according to their intentions. It will be a means for goodness, inshā’Allah.

Laylatu l-Qadr, as we said, is better than a thousand nights [months]. That is, one night is better than a lifetime – a thousand nights [months] is probably equivalent to 80 years. 80 years is roughly the average lifespan of a person, sometimes less, sometimes more, around that time.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says in Qur’ān ʿAẓīmu sh-Sha’n that nothing is meaningless. It has thousands, millions of meanings. And that meaning is that on Laylatu l-Qadr, a person can earn the reward of all their worship throughout their life in a single night, with the power of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla.

Therefore, this is our intention. May Allah grant us to revive many more Laylatu l- Qadr. May He grant us the reward and thawāb according to our intention.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says, “Ask, and I will give.” So don’t be afraid. If Allah gives the reward of a lifetime in one night, there’s nothing as He won’t give it again. He will certainly give it. He will give a thousand Laylatu l-Qadr, with Allah’s permission. A person will earn the reward of a thousand lifetimes. A person with īmān accepts this, knows it, and doesn’t object. Those who don’t know object to everything. You pray, and they object, “You prayed too much.” You do good deeds, they say, “You did too much.” You recite salawāt, they say, “Don’t.” You fast, they say, “You mustn’t fast, it’s not allowed. Enough! Don’t do too much.” There are many who strive to prevent goodness.

Therefore, a person with a ṭarīqah murshid will not go astray. Those without a murshid will go astray even on the straight way. That’s why many who don’t have a murshid are deceived by those people. They abandon their religion, their īmān, and even Islām because of them. “It’s such a difficult thing” – they make it so difficult that people abandon it and run away. They cause harm, not benefit. Therefore, always speak of beauty, speak of goodness. How generous Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla is! All blessings are in His Hand. He gives as much beneficence as He wills. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla has boundless and immeasurable blessings.

May Allah bless these nights. May they be beneficial and of barakah for humanity. Dunyā, since its creation, has never been a place of comfort. It’s the same now. People ask, “What will happen?” Many people ask, “What will become of this situation?” Things are happening exactly as Allah has decreed. Don’t worry about anything. Don’t be sad, don’t be anxious. Your worries and anxieties won’t do you any good. What Allah has decreed will happen.

Therefore, be with Allah and you will win. May Allah grant us all the reward and blessings of these nights, inshā’Allah. May Allah bless it.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
