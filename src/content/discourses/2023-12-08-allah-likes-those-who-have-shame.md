As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla always tells about the situation of the untruthful ones in this world, in Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n. In today’s āyah also, it tells about their situation: that they speak with their mouths things that are not in their hearts. Their hearts are one way. And what they tell you is another. This is the situation of most people. But they don’t know that Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla knows what is hidden in their hearts. Nothing is hidden from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla knows everything. These people think it is beneficial for them to hide some things. They hide the truth, or what they know, and think it is beneficial to say other things. They think it will be in their best interest. Everyone is trying to cheat each other. But no-one can cheat Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla knows whatever is inside people. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is the one who knows best the reason why they do things, whether for the pleasure of AllāhNone, or for their own benefit. Nothing is hidden from AllāhNone. If people knew this, this world would be like Paradise. But certainly, as this world is dunyā, everyone must see this. Whoever is truthful will be in profit. Whoever is not truthful will be disgraced in the next life and this one.

Because when people think someone is righteous and then realize he is a liar, it will be bad for him. He will be embarrassed, if he has shame. But in the present time, may AllāhNone protect us, there is no embarrassment anymore. Whatever people are doing, they show it off in front of others as if it is a skill. They have no shame. They think what they do is quite normal and do it without shame in front of people. However, lack of embarrassment and shame is not good. “Al-ḥayā’u mina l-īmān,” says our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. To feel embarrassed is from īmān, faith. People must be ashamed for things they do and not boast about them. If they are ashamed, they must ask for forgiveness from AllāhNone. And AllāhNone will hide [their mistakes]. HeNone will not embarrass them. Otherwise, if they are not ashamed, they will be punished in Allāh’sNone Presence. There is punishment they must face in return.

May AllāhNone protect us from this. May both our tongues and our hearts be the same, in shā’a Llāh. May AllāhNone protect us from hypocrites and bad people. This is a big badness, badness towards people to have a difference between heart and tongue. Lying and badness don’t bring any benefit. May AllāhNone protect us from their harm. May such people stay away from us. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone said in... We read: “Yaqūlūna bi-afwāhihim mā laysa fī qulūbihim, wa-Llāhu a‘lamu bimā yaktumūn” (3:167). HeNone know everything, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Human being – there are many from beginning, but now this days it’s more and more – they say something, but their intention, what they are in their heart, different thing. They are cheating people, but they cannot cheat Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.

Said Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone know what is in your heart, what is in your tongue, so nothing can be hide from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. AllāhNone know hide, and open, and dark, and everytime Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, nothing can be... cannot see. HeNone is Samī‘u l-Baṣīr. This is HisNone attribute, which it is whole time for HisNone attribute. Nothing can be hide from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. So people, they are doing something, saying something. It is not true. They are promising, they not doing. They are telling people many thing, in everywhere, every time cheating people. But there is no benefit from what they do. If what they do it is shame, normally they must be ashamed.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla like people who ashamed. Ashamed meaning – ḥayā’, ḥayā’u mina l-īmān. He ashamed to be, not be happy with bad thing you do. Something, many thing normally in, before – we say not hundred years ago, maybe for twenty years ago or thirty years ago – many, many thing, even non-believer they were ashaming to do. Now, nothing ashamed. They not ashamed from anything, because this is kufr (unbelief). But ashaming it is very acceptable attribute. Even somebody coming say, “Oh my daughter, she ashamed not go speaking with people.” Even Muslim, they are from this whole global culture. What they said, they are thinking bad thing it is good.

So what it is, for this: they are not afraid, not ashamed from AllāhNone, not ashamed from people. If they, HeNone know, or people know, they cheating people, they stolen people, they doing bad for people, they are not care at all. Not ashamed at all. But Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, who ashamed, AllāhNone, if they asking for forgiveness, HeNone forgive them. But other, they will be, they will know, when Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla asking for Judgment Day, they will know everything they done without shame. They will be ashamed that time. In that position they will be very ashamed, but finish, that time. But they must [be] ashamed here, to not do.

But now, as we said, to be [a] not good one, popular now, in this time. Ashaming, it is not good, for people. People, they not like ashamed people. They want people without shame, to do everything against will of AllāhNone, against order of AllāhNone, against religion, against human being even. What AllāhNone give human being attribute they can do, they doing outside of this. AllāhNone keep us safe from HisNone... AllāhNone keep us in HisNone way, to be with Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam and with good people, in shā’a Llāh, not be with this not ashamed people, in shā’a Llāh. Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
