Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

A’ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm:

(Qur’ān 61:08). Allah says: ‘Allah will perfect His light, even though the disbelievers dislike it.’ Allah will perfect His light insha’Allah. And even if unbelievers dislike and hate it, they have no authority at all. They don’t have power against Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla. We shouldn’t be hopeless. With the permission of Allah, He will perfect His light. The light of Allah will cover the whole world. The light of Allah is Islam. Hearts will be enlightened with Islam. This light will be everywhere.

When Islam enters someone’s heart, his face illuminates with this light. When people don’t have this light, they may struggle to get light as much as they want, it will be useless. Apart from being bright, this light is something special. Only when it enters one’s heart does it show. Otherwise, people don’t realize it. And many people have darkness on their faces, not light. The more they alienate from Allah, the more darkness appears on their faces. Their faces cause people to turn away from them. People with light have familiarity and love. This is a favor from Allah which is not given to everyone. But insha’Allah it is a promise of Allah that this light will be spread all over the world. And then people will go astray again, and resurrection will take place. It cannot be otherwise.

There are various events, as we are living in the end of times. After distress there will be relief, and the promise of Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla will manifest. We look and see that it is impossible not to be sad at the condition of Muslims. But these must certainly happen in order for this light to come out. This light is a beauty from Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla. People who are given it are lucky. May Allah make this light last upon us. And may this light come to this world very soon.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
