Shaykh Muhammad Nazim Al-Haqqani An-Naqshibandi, Sohbat of the 31st of March, 2014.

Marhaba, O lovers of Shah Mardan, Marhaba. We are weak. With what is our weakness removed? Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. This is the holy great Name on which earths and heavens stand. Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim.

Go ahead, O lovers of Shah Mardan. Friends of Shah Mardan, go ahead. Welcome. Whatever you wish, you can have it. Every beauty is for you. Let us start, our child. Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Let's start. How beautiful. How beautiful. The holy word on which earths and heavens stand. Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Aman ya Rabbi. The great Word. Wise people tremble from this Word - Bismillah.

Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Marhaba, O lovers of Shah Mardan. I ask for strength. We ask for strength but we don't have the ability to carry it. According to our selves, O lovers of Shah Mardan, that is what is given to us. What is an ant given? An ant has its provision. It takes it. We have our share too. What is our share? An amount as much as an ant takes, from the Possessor of heavens to His Beloved, from His Beloved to us is as much as is given to an ant or even less. Al-hamdu liLlah wa shukru liLlah. We praise Janab-ul Mawla and I thank Him. He has favoured us according to our levels, according to our reasoning, my son. Our everything is according to our reasoning. An ant carries a stick. It gets relief. It goes. That is its share.

O man, O man. Allahu Akbar. O man. O dear nations of the Habib. Go ahead, O Shah ar-Rasul, Sultan of prophets, go ahead. Tell your nations to say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Allahu Akbar. May they not look at rocks and mountains. Mountains and rocks are empty things. They have no value. O lovers, friends of Shah Mardan, those who love him, those who want to receive spiritual enlightenment from him, come and listen, even if it is only two words. Even if it's two words, it's better for you than the whole world. What is the whole world? Here it is. Here it is thrown. Here are the mountains and rivers of the whole world. What did it give you? What do rivers give? What do mountains and rocks give? Nothing. Here they are piled up. Ask for jewels, O lovers of Shah Mardan. Ask for jewels from Shah Mardan. Ask for jewels. Yes.

If you ask for mountains and rocks, take them. They are piled up there. What is their use? You can't put them in your pocket. You can't carry them on your back. You can't sell them and take something in return. This is the world with its mountains and rocks. The world with its everything; with animals on it. What is there? What did it give you? Can you carry it? You cannot. Can you sell it? You can't. Who will you sell it to? Here they are piled up. Piled mountains and rocks. Who will you sell it to, mindless man? You look for a person to sell to. Who will you sell to?

If you say to an ant: "Come and take it", an ant says: "What will I do with it? No. My nest is enough for me." Allah Allah. Being content is something that gives value to a creature. If there is contentment, that person is valuable. If he has no contentment, he is rubbish. An ant says: "Let's go into our nests. The sons of Adam will eat you." What are the nests of the poor ones? Their shelter is a place assigned to them. What do they do? They say: "My Lord is Allah". Aman ya Rabbi. They say "My Lord is Allah. My Lord who created me is Subhan". A tiny ant. The mind and understanding of an ant comprehends this. A huge man doesn't comprehend it. It's a shame. It's a pity. Aman ya Rabbi. He's unaware.

O our Lord, many repentences. They say, offer repentance to the Possessor of Time. There is a Possessor of Time so that you offer repentance to Him. Many tawba. There's someone ruling the sun, someone ruling the earth. There are those ruling the moon and stars in the sky. Does the huge sun move by its own will? If the sun is not told to rise, if the archangels assigned for this tell it to stop in its place, it will. If it's told to rise, it will rise. Yes. Know Allah Jalla Jalaluhu Jallat Azamatuhu wa la ilaha ghayruhu.

May we open, may we open. May we run after servanthood to our Lord. Don't run after rubbish. O man, you are honored. You are created with honor. You are created with honor. Is what you fall for honored? What is the value of the mountains and rocks? Sheikh Mehmet Effendi my son, what honor does it have? You follow the honored ones. Who is honored? Who is given the highest honor? "O My Beloved Muhammad Mustafa (saw)" "O My Beloved". He came with an addressing of greatness. "O My Beloved Muhammad Mustafa, step, step on My Throne. Listen to my speech so that you understand who you are, so that these people understand why they came here. I didn't create these people for them to run after mountains and rocks - Isn't it so? - after the worlds, continents and mountains. So many signs and an addressing from heavens. "I didn't create mankind to run after mountains and rocks. It's a pity that they run and are not aware of who they were created for."

You are Subhan, O our Lord. You are Subhan, You are Subbuh, You are Quddus, ya Rabbina. Forgive us. "For the honor of My Beloved, I will forgive you. For the honor of My Beloved, I will give you paradise. For the honor of My Beloved who looked at My Greatness, I created you so that the majesty of that look and its beauty remains on you." Our Allah, our Lord. Our Subhan. Our Sultan. He says: "I am Sultan". O our Lord. "Say therefore, My servant, Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. This word makes Me pleased. This word makes Me pleased. Say it. Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim." My Allah. May Allah Dhul Jalal not deprive us of saying this holy word. May we always say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. You will have neither troubles, nor sadness, which upsets you. Nothing will remain. Pure and clean.

You are Subhan, my Lord. We are weaker than an ant. Janab-ul Mawla makes His Beloved speak. His Beloved teaches us. His Beloved. O glorious Prophet. Forgive us. Send us the Sahib, O glorious Prophet. Send us the Sultan, O glorious Prophet. Send us Sultans who will revere you and your holy stations, O glorious Prophet. May we not run after dunya. Don't strive for its rubbish, pearls and beads. Don't strive. O our Lord, our Subhan. Forgive us, Allah. For the right of the Habib-ul Kibriya, may no heaviness remain on us. He says: "I will take it. If you're loaded with whole world, it won't be like a stick on an ant. I am Rabbul Alameen." Aman ya Rabbi. Aman ya Rabbi. Aman ya Rabbi. Tawba ya Rabbi. Tawba astaghfirullah. "Make sajda to Me. Stand in qiyam to Me. Do servanthood to Me. I am your Lord" - says Jalla Jalaluhu Jallat Azamatuhu wa la ilaha ghayruhu.

What teachings there are. What are people running after? Aman ya Rabbi. If we are given power, I wish to shake this world. Power is not given like an ant. If we're given - it takes a lot to shake the world. You are Subhan. You are Sultan. You gave being sultan to Your Beloved. Our Subhan, You gave Your love to Your Beloved. You granted us to be of the nation of Your Beloved, ya Rabbi. May we walk step by step after him. They put hats on their heads, and say "we are his nation". They are liars. They fight with each other. They are liars. They destroy each other. They are liars. They are soldiers of shaitan.

O our Lord, forgive. For the honor of the Habib-ul Kibriya, give us strength to shake it(the world). "O My Beloved. O My Beloved. If you address an ant, if you speak to an ant, an ant can shake the world. Al 'azamatu liLlah wa l-izzatu liLlah wa l-jabarut liLlah. If you tell an ant to shake the world, it will shake it. What is the strength you have? Don't you feel shame? Aren't you embarrassed in front of the Creator? Why don't you make sajda to Him? Why don't you revere His command? Aman ya Rabbi. Aman ya Rabbi. Shukr to Allah. Shukr that He sent His Beloved to us. O glorious Prophet, I ask for strength. He says: "O my nation, you ask for strength. If I give strength to an ant", the prophet is saying this, "If I speak to an ant, the ant will shake the world. What are these toys in your hands? Yes. Know Allah! He is my Lord."

O my Lord. Aman ya Rabbi. Tawba ya Rabbi. Tawba ya Rabbi. Tawba ya Rabbi. That is why, I ask for such a Sultan. I wait for such a Sultan. Forgive, Allah. Janab-ul Haqq doesn't allow us to be oppressed with troubles. Praise and glory. Praise and glory. Let us praise and glorify our Allah. Let's say alhamduliLlah, let's not be afraid.

Fatiha.
