As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh. A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Qul in kuntum tuḥibbūna Llāha fa-ttabi‘ūnī yuḥbibkumu Llāhu wa-yaghfir lakum dhunūbakum" (3:31). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘aẓīm. Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says, "If you love Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, follow my way and AllāhNone will love you." The love of AllāhNone is our wish. Our goal is the love of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. It is also the reason why we have gathered here. Mā shā’a Llāh, people have come from all over the world. May AllāhNone be pleased. For the pleasure of AllāhNone, for the love of AllāhNone, out of affection for AllāhNone, we are walking on the way of our Holy ProphetNone, for this way is the greatest favour to us. It is the greatest virtue AllāhNone has given to us. We must make shukr to AllāhNone, as many people are not aware of it. Even if they know, they don't care if it is today or any other day. Other days are sometimes more important for people.

But the main, important thing is to follow the way of our Holy ProphetNone. To remember himNone at every chance and occasion, to ask for help from himNone, to ask for strength from himNone are necessary things for believers. Other things are not important. When they are necessary, HeNone gives them. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gives to people whom HeNone loves. As we said, let AllāhNone love you. It is not important if others love you or not. Some people say, this man said this, that man came or didn't come, and did or didn't do something. No need for that. People should mind themselves. They must look at themselves so that they realize what kind of favours they have. If they look at others, they will never be happy. And they won't attain the pleasure of AllāhNone, because when you don't look at what was given by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, and look at this world instead, you will be inclined to this world. And if you turn away from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla will turn away from you. It will be useless.

Therefore, may AllāhNone be pleased with you, as you came for the pleasure of AllāhNone from so far away. We have all gathered here together. You also made ziyārah of Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim. His barakah comes from the barakah of our Holy ProphetNone and from the barakah of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Therefore, those who came here will surely not leave empty-handed. Considering those who say this and that – that this is accepted and that is not accepted – as we said, don't listen to what they say. Don't deal with them. Thanks to AllāhNone, our way is the beautiful way, the way of light, the way of our Holy ProphetNone, the way of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, the way of Paradise and happiness, with permission of AllāhNone. May AllāhNone make it blessed for everyone.

May AllāhNone be pleased, because our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says, when someone goes to visit his Muslim brother for the pleasure of AllāhNone, a reward is written, and a sin forgiven, for every step that Muslim takes. And he is raised up one rank. Shukr to AllāhNone, you made more than one step; there are many people who came from as far as millions of steps away. Therefore, they will take great profit back with them, with permission of AllāhNone. May AllāhNone make it last. May HeNone show this way to those who don't know it, in shā'a Llāh. May they know it too, and benefit from it. This is the benefit.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying in Qur’ān: A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Qul in kuntum tuḥibbūna Llāha fa-ttabi‘ūnī yuḥbibkumu Llāhu wa-yaghfir lakum" (3:31). Say, if you love meNone, and you love Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla... If you love Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, you must follow meNone. In Qur’ān, saying this for Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. If you love Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, you must follow Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, he is our guide for AllāhNone, for love of AllāhNone. For every good thing, heNone guide us. And everything heNone done, it is for us. Big favour, big benefit for believer who following Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam.

Because our aim in this world, the main aim for our life – purpose of life – love of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Because this is what make us different from animal, from other thing. Because our – also AllāhNone create us all, like same kind system – for animal, for human being, for everything. But what they are making different, HeNone gave us this honour, to be follow HisNone order. If you follow HisNone order, you will be honourable one. If you not follow this, so, animal it's better from you. Because they eat, they drink, they go same thing human being, also. Eating, drinking and doing everything. But difference: we have order to be good people, to worship HimNone, to love HimNone to give us everything we need. This is what Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying. If you love AllāhNone, so you must follow Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, not follow other people. But unfortunately, the most people, they are not caring about this.

We are here, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, thanking for all of you. AllāhNone bless you, because you are coming here for, only for sake of AllāhNone, for love of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. We are in this holy month, month of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. You leave everything and coming just here, our island. Nothing here for benefit for dunyā. Small island, maybe somebody can come for holiday but, even it is so hot. Even I run away from, when it's so hot. I don't know how this people they come summertime, to holiday. But for even this, our people they are coming not for holiday, just coming for enjoying themselves with blessed place, with love of ProphetNone and love of Awliyā’u Llāh, to be in maqām of Mawlānā Shaykh. And his presence very clear, al-ḥamdu liLlāh. For this, he attracting people and bring them here. This is very important. You are, of course, lucky people, because many of people they are not interesting about this occasion. Many of them also they are not knowing this, even. Many of them, also objecting for this, also. But we are, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are not interesting for people. Just you be interested for what AllāhNone love, what Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam love. Don't care anybody what they said, what they are happy, what they are not happy. We are happy, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, it's enough for us. Let people to say what they can say. Just be happy, because this gathering for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, for love of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. It's pure for this, al-ḥamdu liLlāh. This is what we see.

And Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, when some mu’min coming to meet his brother mu’min in any place, for just for sake of AllāhNone, AllāhNone reward him. For each step, one reward. And forgive one sin and make him, raise him one level more. Each step this, you are the nearest one maybe coming here. It is maybe ten thousand step or hundred thousand step, like this, so, even from here. So, many people coming from very far, maybe millions of steps. AllāhNone give. HeNone give, without fearing it will be too much for me to give. Ḥāshā, this is impossible for AllāhNone to be afraid from anything. AllāhNone reward you. AllāhNone accept your ziyārah. AllāhNone, in shā'a Llāh, HeNone give you from barakah for dunyā, for ākhirah, and for happiness for whole life, in shā'a Llāh. Keep your family also to be, your country also, happy with ProphetNone, and give hidāyah for all of them, in shā'a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
