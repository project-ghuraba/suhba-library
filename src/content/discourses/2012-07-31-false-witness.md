As-Salamu Alaikum. Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fil jamia' The purpose of today's sohbat is to warn about a very bad illness, insha Allah. People think it is easy but it is a big thing with Allah. Some don't consider these things important but they are one of the biggest sins with Allah. And what is it? To bear false witness.

And this, some people do it as a professional job in courts. For a little money he bears false witness, bears witness for something he didn't see. They bring a man, saying "you will say that you saw this, you saw that". They bring a few men for the same thing. And of course when there is witness whole trial can change. A person who is right, can end up being wrong. Haqqani, being righteous is gone at that time. Therefore Mawlana is Haqqani for everybody. "You shall give his right" he says. This is a big, very important matter. Beware. This can happen in the court, bearing false witness.

Of course not everybody does this-going there & bearing witness for money. There are some who do but... But normally in daily life also, in the course of our everyday lives this can happen. For example "Did you see it?" "No, I didn't" "But you just saw it".. Even encouraging the man, making him doubt this time. "Did I see or did I not see?" No, if you saw it, say "Yes, I saw it." If you don't remember, say "I don't know, I don't remember. I can't bear false witness for you &get into big sin" For a little.. This is not even for money. I can't bear witness just for the sake of conversation. Therefore we should say 100x, 500x, 1000x Astaghfirullah everyday that if we have fallen into such sins knowingly or unknowingly, to be saved from their consequences.

Of course there is no such a big harm in daily life. Actually the one who bears false witness in court can cause people real harm. Sometimes it happens such that a person's life can be subject in these trials. Therefore one should not think of it as a game. A person, when he speaks should know what he is saying. Today we speak one illness, it is very bad illness. Maybe some people can fall in this. This is to be liar witness. In this, this our countries here- Middle East, or other country, many of them, because they are poor people, they give them money when going to court. Saying you must say, "this is what I saw. I see this, I see that". To bring some people, for everybody give him his money and they make witness.

And this is from big sins in Islam, like sins or like killing man even, like very big sins this. But people because they believe they are weak now, they not listening. Only they want to get money. From where he get money, he can get. For this, Mawlana Haqqani (Haqqani meaning to give right to everybody) when they make this witness in front of judge so he cannot do anything, only what they said he will do. And this right for people, going out. They become miserable and they don't take their right. It is very big subject. Even some people, maybe they die from this when they not get their right. And they change, coming on them court, not with them. They have many, many darar/harm- By money, by themselves, maybe for going to jail. For this, Allah make it big sin. And this for court.

But from sometimes in life also, people, it is, saying "you see? When you were looking for me, you can tell this people" If you know, ok. You see this & it is right, you can tell. But if not right, don't tell. Tell him "I don't know, maybe I see but was not looking well or I don't know". No need to be going for sin. But this is of course not like court, to take money and making witness. Just it is small one, but this is also teaching people to make haram. Even who telling this, he making people, encourage them to make bad thing. "You see this?" Maybe sometimes from shy from that man, "Yes, yes, I saw." You can say this. But no, no need for to say this. You must be pay attention for this. You didn't see anything, don't say "I see". Because now in last day, no justice, no nothing going well. So don't be, you also, you put yourself under heavy weight of sin. Even you feel it also on yourself. If you saying something lie & you not seeing but you were saying "I see this". It is heavy for people also. So be careful from this. InshaAllah today for Ramadan, it's nasihat/advice enough.

Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, Al Fatiha.
