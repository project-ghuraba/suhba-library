As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
أَلَا إِنَّ أَوْلِيَاءَ اللَّهِ لَا خَوْفٌ عَلَيْهِمْ وَلَا هُمْ يَحْزَنُونَ
<br>“Alā inna awliyā Allāhi lā khawfun ʿalayhim wa-lā hum yaḥzanūn”<br>
“Verily on the friends of Allah there is no fear, nor shall they grieve.” (Qurʼān 10:62). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

The awliyā of Allah—a Wali is a person who obeys Allah and whom Allah loves. Anyone can become a Wali; it is not unattainable. Anyone who obeys Allah and follows His commands is a servant beloved by Him; indeed, awliyā means Allah’s beloved servants.

Some people mistakenly believe that there are no Awliyā left. Yet, they do exist; by Allah’s permission, all who obey Him become His beloved servants. These blessed ones do no harm to anyone. They inflict no suffering on anyone. They walk the way of Allah in peace and safety. However, they have many enemies—the ego (nafs), shayṭān, desires (hawa), and the dunyā.

Once they are established on that way, they find true peace. It is shayṭān who is truly disturbed; he is troubled by them. He strives to lead them astray and incites enemies to attack them. Yet, because they are with Allah, they harbor no fear. Allah Himself protects them. One must strive to be like them.

People ask, “How does one become a Wali?” This is what an awliyā is. They do not engage with anyone. They do not answer those who attack them. Allah protects them. Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam states that Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says, “I declare war against anyone who harms or speaks ill of My friend (Wali).”

Therefore, as we have said, the Awliyā have no need to engage with anyone. No matter what others do, their Protector is Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. Anyone who wages war against them or harbors hostility toward them will inevitably and always suffer loss. One can never prevail against Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. These are the blessed words of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam.

There are times when people speak recklessly or utter nonsense. May Allah grant them guidance so that they do not see any harm, for by doing so, they are effectively waging war against Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. Allah will never grant them victory; their ultimate end is always loss.

One must be very careful in this regard, for we are living in times of fitnah. There are great fitnahs. All manner of ruses are employed to lead even well-intentioned people astray. People succumb to ill-founded suspicions without knowing. By making sū’ zann (bad presumptions) based on such suspicions, they are dragged into sin with every word they utter. In doing so, they set themselves against Allah. May Allah protect us. It is essential to be mindful of this.

One should keep the company of righteous people. You must be with those whom Allah loves, inshā’Allāh. 

May Allah make us among them. May Allah protect us. May we not fall into fitnah, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
