As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Raddi l-ḥūqūq ilá aṣḥābihā." The order of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is to give others their rights, to whomever it is owed. A person's right is a difficult thing. It is easy to make things right in this world. But if you leave it for ākhirah, it will be very difficult for you. May AllāhNone protect us. Therefore, if you took someone's rights in this world, you should be careful.

Mu’mins, Muslims and especially people of ṭarīqah should pay more attention to this issue. Each person's right belongs to himself. It is ḥalāl for him and ḥarām for you. That right will not be lost. There are many people in the world who take others' rights. They think they earn a profit from that, whereas there is no profit from it in this world, and it is even worse in ākhirah. Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam suffered from a heavy illness a week before hisNone passing away. HeNone was going up for khuṭbah every now and then. HeNone would tell ṣaḥābah during khuṭbah, "Whoever has a right over me should ask for it. Whoever has given me something should ask me to return it. If I have done someone wrong with or without knowing..." HeNone never did anything on purpose, of course. No one had a right over himNone.

Our Holy ProphetNone was the most just person in the world. HeNone didn't take anyone's rights. Yet our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam used to say that. As a lesson to us, in order to teach us, heNone said, "Whoever has a right on me, may he not leave it for ākhirah." It is difficult to give one's right in ākhirah. Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam was told by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, "All your past and future sins are forgiven." HeNone was forgiven. So it is in order for us to learn. Indeed, it is the biggest burden for people to take others' rights.

May AllāhNone protect us from committing that sin, from obeying our egos. The ego is obeyed. There are many people who obey their egos. Some of them take away others' rights directly. They see it as making a profit, or earning a trophy. They see it as watchfulness and wisdom. Whereas, it is nothing. All of it is stupidity and madness. It is nothing else. People who take others' rights and who think they have made a profit have little mind. They have no intelligence. They are people who don't think about ākhirah. Unfortunately, in this time we are living in, all people, or at least, we can no longer now say a few, but most or many people have this mentality. Even though they say they wouldn't do this, may AllāhNone protect us, if something comes up, they might. Therefore, this matter of rights is important.

Give others their rights. You may think you earn three pence in this world, but you won't have earned anything in dunyā. And you will be devastated in ākhirah. Its account, questioning and punishment will be overwhelming. Therefore, in this world everything has a solution. There is a way for everything in this world. Solve all your problems in dunyā. And don't leave anything for ākhirah. Don't leave unpaid rights. The other things, the rights of AllāhNone, AllāhNone will forgive. But the rights of others... AllāhNone is the owner of justice. HeNone won't interfere in the rights of others. HeNone gives a person's rights to the owner of those rights.

May AllāhNone protect all of us from this thing.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Raddi l-ḥūqūq ilá aṣḥābihā." Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying, give rights to people who have this right. We must give them what they have. This is very important for Muslim, for believer, especially for ṭarīqah people who are following the heart of belief or īmān. Of who this heart? Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. We [are] following himNone, we must be doing what heNone is doing, what heNone shows us, what heNone teaches us.

Many people in this time, they are, can take everything from other people, not give back. And they thought they are clever, they are... they can do what others cannot do. But their reality [is] they are not clever at all. They are idiot, they are stupid. Because when you thought; "I take this and nobody catch me, nobody touch me. So, I am clever, I am doing this, I am safe. Nobody, no judgement, no court." But they are forgetting the most important court; court for next life. Which it is really difficult for this people because they don't have anything. That time...

In this dunyā, very easy to be finished, this problem in this dunyā. You have money, you have something, you take from people, you give them back. They will be happy, they will accept. But when it is for next life, in Judgement Day, there is no – nothing, no money, no gold, no credit card, no this new kind of money which it is air in the sky. Nothing at all in the other side. What you will give? They will take from your good deeds. If don't have good deeds, they will put their sin on you. You become more sinner. For this, more easy for people to finish a problem in this dunyā. It is clever who do this. Not thinking it will be safe for him to go with this, what he take from people.

Even Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam; heNone is the biggest teacher for us. HisNone last day on this dunyā life, heNone was very ill from, heNone has fever and heNone has headache and very, very ill. And heNone was coming, telling. Ṣaḥābah was very sad, unhappy. So, heNone ask for Sayyidunā ‘Alī and the hisNone uncle son to take himNone to mosque and to put him on minbar. HeNone make hisNone speech and during this speech first heNone ask, "O, ṣaḥābah – O, companion. INone am leaving this world. But the most important thing here, INone am coming to ask for you if anybody he has – INone take from him something. He can ask meNone. Maybe INone forget, or INone don't know. If anything like this, immediately ask, because INone will give this. And if any INone make harm, anybody with word or speaking not good for them; also if INone hit anybody – everything you must ask meNone. You must ask this for to finish to this problem. You say, "Okay, we are happy with you I give this," INone will be very happy. If anybody has something like this, don't [be] ashamed, don't be afraid, don't worry if INone will be not happy with him. INone will be very happy with him, if anything like this you have."

So, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, of course Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam heNone is teaching us because heNone is most perfect, most polite human being. So, nothing can happen from this but heNone teach us. HeNone said, "Because INone don't want to go to next life with anything. You ask for me, INone want to be very happy, very clean to go for presence of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla." This is a very big teaching for people, for Muslim, for mu’min. Especially for believers and ṭarīqah people, who are the most love, they said, for Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Who love Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, he must be like himNone, to follow himNone what heNone done, what heNone do. Now, many time we see people after they left their country and other they coming, taking from their land. And they are claiming they are Muslim, they are that and like this... It can be, but this ignorant people. They must know first thing, most difficult thing for ākhirah: Rights of other people. Because right of AllāhNone, AllāhNone HeNone is most merciful and HeNone forgives us. No problem with AllāhNone, we say, "Astaghfiru Llāh wa-atūbu ilayh." Whole this your sin, going and changing to good deed. But for people, AllāhNone not interfere because HeNone said, you must ask. This is justice if you do. [If] INone will forgive you for other people's rights, it will be not justice, it will be mess. For this, very important for people to know this. And if anything, they must look; wrong, they must change it to good, in shā’a Llāh.

AllāhNone don't make us to follow our ego, because ego ordering for bad thing. AllāhNone not keep us for hand of our ego, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
