As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Wa-tilka l-ayyāmu nudāwiluhā bayna n-nās" (3:140). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘aẓīm. This world is a series of days and then everyone will be gone. Everyone owns something until the time comes and then others take their place. They use it for a period of time and then another comes. This is how Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla made it in this world – everyone will have it for a while but then the days will pass and be gone. There is nothing eternal here. Eternal is ākhirah. We should prepare ourselves for that. We should work and strive for that. That must be our goal. Otherwise, the time of this world is limited. It is restricted.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gave it to everyone. And HeNone gave mind to everyone so that they can evaluate that precious time. But people, especially people of the end of times, think there is a lot of time. They see it even as too much. And they waste it on useless things. What is a useless thing? What they do is just to make their egos happy, to make their egos satisfied. If you remember Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, you take your ego out of the way and attain the pleasure of AllāhNone. But if it is only to satisfy your ego, as most people do, if it is only to make your ego happy and have fun, then it is wasted. Days will be wasted too. And then it will be the turn of those who come after you. May AllāhNone let us do good things, in shā’a Llāh. That is the important thing.

Days are passing by. Mawlid is over. We came here and today we are going back again. We will come again, in shā’a Llāh. We will return. It is important to spend these days with such blessed people for the pleasure of AllāhNone. It is important to be with good people. The intention to attain the pleasure of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is important. Shukr to AllāhNone. When you make shukr to AllāhNone, HeNone increases it. If you don't make shukr, it will be decreased. Therefore, we are making shukr. It is a great favour for all of us to be together with good people who gather for the pleasure of AllāhNone everywhere we go. It is a great happiness. These days didn't pass for nothing, by permission of AllāhNone.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, saying in Qur’ān: Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Wa-tilka l-ayyāmu nudāwiluhā bayna n-nās" (3:140). This days, this meaning we make it by one-by-one, it is going around, people – around human being. This days, all time moving, not staying. You thought it is nothing moving. Everything moving. Especially time and thing, they are not same. Nothing same from one second to second second, not same. It is different. For this, the important thing here – to be awake for this. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla make us... every time warning us about this.

Be awake. You are not here for to enjoy yourself, to looking only for time how it's going. Special this, last days people, more and more, they have everything and they're only thinking to enjoy themself. Only aim for themself to be happy, to enjoy themselves, to be get more and more enjoy, they thought. But, you can enjoy yourself, you can do good thing. What you do for... Don't forget AllāhNone. This is important. Different between people who... Most of people they forget their Lord Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, their Creator and just running after enjoy themself and nothing else. And they making everything to enjoy themself. Very bad, very ugly, very terrible thing they are doing to enjoy themself. But this is no benefit because this days, it will go, and you will go, and they, after you coming another people. They're not for this, for you, forever.

Forever, only who be with AllāhNone even when he is happy to enjoy themself to do what AllāhNone happy with this. Eating, drinking, go and come, but don't forget your Creator, your Lord, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. This is very important. When you do this, you reward and you will be forever happy. But other, they only trying to be happy here but, quickly days running, coming for other people's turn. It is the turn coming from one to one, so, it will be.

We see many people, especially, they thought they said who make in seventies or sixties, they was make revolution with, like, for student revolution or other, hippie or other thing. This people, especial this, they are but they are... just, we give example for this. They thought still, they're living in that time. Even their clothes or their type of dressing, shaving, like this – they thought it is still there in that time. But this, many sixty year, fifty year going after you and you still there. No, don't be like this. Wake up and catch the time, which it is very valuable, very important. AllāhNone make us to...

We are happy, al-ḥamdu liLlāh. We are coming here, going there, with good people, and we good place, al-ḥamdu liLlāh. This is important to be happy with this. AllāhNone make us to all time, everywhere we go, with people they are gathering for sake of AllāhNone, for love of AllāhNone. We are, when this gathering happening, this giving our soul happiness. Giving light, giving nūr, in shā’a Llāh. Giving more īmān, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone make it for whole life, in shā’a Llāh, forever. Because we thanking AllāhNone for this. Because when you thank AllāhNone, AllāhNone give you more. "La-’in shakartum la-azīdannakum" (14:7). AllāhNone, shukran liLlāh.
