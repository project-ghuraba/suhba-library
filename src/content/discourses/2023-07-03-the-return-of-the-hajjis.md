As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Today is a beautiful day shukr to AllāhNone. With the barakah and himmah of Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim, the Ḥajjīs came today thanks to AllāhNone. Those who go to Ḥajj will have barakah (blessing), and whoever is around them will also have from that barakah. Now, of course, the old customs are gone, the good old customs. When the ḥajjī came from Ḥajj, they would give him a big welcome. They would come to visit him. They would drink from Zamzam and eat from the dates he brought. They would kiss his hand. These are beautiful customs. Those customs no longer remain. But those customs bring blessings. This means the people will also be granted from the barakah that comes to him.

That is why, Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim paid great attention to these matters. He even went to the airport to bid us farewell every time when we were going to Ḥajj. And when coming back, he would come to welcome us every time. So it is barakāh for them and for those who make Ḥajj. Ḥajj is a great virtue. It is a great favour of AllāhNone to mankind. If one was destined and favoured to go, it is a great grace. It is grace from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Every sin of the ḥajjī is forgiven. His du‘ā’s are also accepted. The du‘ā’s of the ḥajjīs are accepted for forty days after their arrival. So you must ask them for good du‘ā’. In shā’a Llāh, it will be good for him, the ḥajjī, and us, in shā’a Llāh.

May it be a victory for Islām. May AllāhNone protect us from the evil of evil people, in shā’a Llāh. This is a beautiful thing, shukr to AllāhNone.

Our brother Shaykh Bahauddin Efendi had a very nice Ḥajj with his sons, mā shā’a Llāh. May AllāhNone give such good children to everyone, in shā’a Llāh. These du‘ā’, the most important du‘ā’, the most important du‘ā’ now for Muslims is for their children to be good, to be protected from these shayṭāns. Wherever we go, shayṭān is mostly attacking – he is attacking the elders but the youngsters even more. Because they didn’t... Therefore, our du‘ā’ must always be to have good children. Good children so that they will benefit you in both this world and the hereafter, in shā’a Llāh; to not be in need of anyone. May they all live with barakah, in shā’a Llāh. Today we are – happy day for us, al-ḥamdu liLlāh. The happiest day when pilgrims coming back to their house, ḥajjīs. It is like feast for people. Old people, they was more looking for this. They more respected, more careful for this – when ḥajjī will come. We, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we remember when we was child, when Mawlānā coming, we make decoration in house also for his coming. Everybody, when in our neighbour or other, they make decoration. Even they, many of them, they write in their door – "Ḥajj mabrūr wa-sa‘ī mashkūr." And when coming ḥajjī, coming with big crowd, coming to meet them, to greet them.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, this custom, good custom for Muslim people because old people they know with ḥajjī, who went to Ḥajj, that AllāhNone send with him HisNone bless for people around him also. Because ḥajjī going to Ḥajj, his, AllāhNone forgive him and bring him with many gifts – for him, for his family, for his neighbour, for his country even, in shā’a Llāh. This is, Mawlānā was very careful for this. Many times when we went to Ḥajj, he coming to airport to send us. When we come back also, he come to welcome us.

It is not easy for journey. This very, most blessed journey for human being, for Muslim – the most important thing, more important than meeting and what others think they are – people running after this for journey, for holiday, for anything, important meeting, for... Some no-mind people they are going for TV star. They... If any place there is, they invite them, uff... They will be die for to go there and this is not good. The good, they must... people for Ḥajj, they must be very happy for, if they have this. From Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla the biggest gift for Muslim to be in Ḥajj and... Because everyone cannot be in Ḥajj. Only what AllāhNone, they, they choose them. And when you go there, HeNone is... you are going to Sulṭān of everything, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, AllāhNone Sulṭān Allāh, subḥāna Llāh. And with HisNone, you are HisNone guest, HeNone give you from endless reward, bless, mercy, every good thing. And you will not let [leave] them there, you carrying them, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, to your country, to your children, to your family. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, this is big luck for people.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are greeting Shaykh Bahauddin Efendi. He was very lucky. He went with his sons, al-ḥamdu liLlāh. The lions, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, they are. The most important thing for us, now everywhere we going, children, good children, khayr, blessed one, we must. Everywhere we going, they are asking for du‘ā’ for their children. Everybody afraid now from this time, from... Especially for youngster, for children, with situation of world it is very difficult. And shayṭān, he is waiting to put this people in his trap, trap and take them to away from their family. They're... for this we are saying, most important praying for us, every time we ask for ḥajjī also. Because Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, when ḥajjī coming – his praying, du‘ā’, accepted for forty days – to make du‘ā’ for youngster of Ummat Muḥammad – to not be lose. Because they are real lost. They are not knowing good from bad. Not knowing sin from reward. They are mixing, completely mixing. They are not caring if anyone coming from Ḥajj [or] coming from from other place. Just looking for their pleasure. And pleasure not whole time for them. Little bit they make, put honey on their mouth. After, they put poison. This is, for this we are, all time we are saying to advise people to look after their children and to ask protection from AllāhNone from, for this, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone protect Ummat Muḥammad from shayṭān, in shā’a Llāh. Youngs and olds also. So sometimes, they become crazy. They not knowing. They just running after their desire, after their ego. Becoming – not looking for children, not looking for wife, not looking for anything AllāhNone help us. AllāhNone keep us in HisNone way, in shā’a Llāh, way of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
