Salam alaikum wa rahmatullahi Ta'ala wa barakatuh. You look fasting... Everyone is fasting here, Shaykh?... And you're saying, why this man coming here now? He irons our head. We've been with M. Shaykh and he said "Go and make your duty". I say, I understand that I have permission to iron your head. And you are looking very, very, very fasting. And now I am trying to get little bit warm up and insha'Allah and make all of us more strong to continue fasting until one year.

We used to have one man in Damascus, he was sa'im ud-dar'(forever fasting). I was small kid, I was looking at him...Allah, saying, how he's making all his life fasting? He only breaks his fast twice a year one in Eid al Fitr, and the other one in Eid al Adha. Just 2 days in the year he breaks his fast, the rest he's fasting all the way. And I was saying, "I don't want to be like him"... Small kid looking, "No, I don't want to be like him". I want to be like my father, but I cannot. This one also, I cannot. Because Mawlana Shaykh is really beyond. There is competition between the father and the children, it's all the time happening. Who is the best example for the kids? It's the father. He looks at his father and says, "You know when I grow up I will be better than you". This chance, I don't have. Even if I am sitting in his chair, I don't have this chance. Sitting in chair is different & to be like him is different.

Al Hamdulillah, yesterday we have the doctor visiting him, Al Hamdulillah, he is okay, & good & well, masha'Allah. But because of his himma, everyday he is giving 45 minutes sohba, and standing up and sitting, standing up and sitting, by respect to Allah Almighty and Rasulullah (saws). That's why he was a little bit tired. But I am thinking Subhana Allah, with this age, with this himmah, nobody can do it. Al Hamdulillah, Allah Almighty gave us such a shaykh, such a master. He is the best example for all of us. Just making it on me that I cannot catch him, but, nobody can catch him, masha'Allah.

One day we've been in the boat,in Malaysia, and it was a navy boat, and there is one place the river and sea come together. And you don't realize which is the river, which is the sea. This is the mouth of the river, huge, huge... And Mawlana Shaykh is telling me, very quietly "See, you see the mouth of the river". I said 'Yes'. This is when it's coming to Grandshaykh's heart, the knowledge of all the Grandshayks, from Sayyid. Muhammad (saws) to the chest of Sayid. Abu Bakr (ra), to the chest of Sayid. Salman (ra) & all, & all... Now it's coming to G.Shaykh". He doesn't say to himself. "Look, this is the mouth of the river, joining the sea, joining the ocean. Sea, this is it". Al Hamdulillah, so no need to compete. Don't try, just enjoy the ride of the boat. By saying, enjoy the ride of the boat.. -Al Hamdulillah Allah Almighty gave us a very nice lifeboat. And some people say, "This boat is boring", and some people say "We enjoy it". Some say "It was choppy". Some people say "It was smooth". Some say, "It was what we expected". Some people say, "We've been disappointed". But the boat is the boat. So, I was the other day, in Istanbul, looking at this big huge boat. They are getting the tourists, and I said, wow, these guys put you in the boat, and they put all the entertainment, and so many people say "We feel bored". So many people comment, "We've been bored on this boat. It was a terrible trip". Ya Hu. Imagine that company, what they are bringing. They are bringing food, beds, servers, bringing this, everything, and at the end of the day you are still complaining saying, "I couldn't enjoy it".

I was thinking, Allah Almighty made for us also a similar boat, but as life -because life's destiny is going, so you cannot interfere with the way, where to go, because Allah Almighty put the main line. He gave you all the entertainment in the boat, food, drink, sleep, whatever, but, if you say, "I am still bored", Allah Almighty will not be happy with you. He wants us to be happy so, when you are not satisfied with this one, you'll not be satisfied with Allah Almighty. Maybe, our life will be smooth. Maybe our life will be choppy. Maybe our life will be miserable. Maybe we will be the servant in this world. Maybe we will be king. But, even if you are the king, or you are the servant you have to be careful about shukr. Shukr is very important. When you are in shukr everything will come in place. And everything even if you say it is choppy, okay, let me be used to "Shukr Ya Rabbi. It is nice to be with 'shukr Ya Rabbi'.

One very nice story for Mulla Nasruddin. Mulla Nasruddin was looking for one bag, this bag was carrying the rubbish. It was very famous, small bag, carrying the rubbish having one rolling, rubbish. Actually dirt. He saw this bag...and he said, "Why, what is your purpose? Why you've been created? Why?" He was saying, "No need for you". After a while he became very ill. Very, very ill, poor Mulla Nasruddin, almost dying. And one doctor said, "I have medicine to cure you, but, I don't know if you can accept it or not". He said, "Please anything, anything, I am dying". "There is a bag, it is carrying the dirt". Said, "yes?" "That dirt is the medicine for your illness". He said, "Please, please leave it". So he got bit of this dirt, and he was cured.

Then after a while, he went on the boat trip, so the boat was in a storm, and everyone panicking. "What we will do?" Mulla Nasruddin sitting. Saying, "Ya Mulla Nasruddin, why you are not panicking? We are almost sunk. He said, "No, I will not interfere with Allah Almighty's business. I will not interfere with Allah Almighty's things at all. I interfered once, he made me to eat dirt." So no, it is dangerous place to enter. So that's why when you make shukr, it is very important, because you'll not be like Mulla Nasruddin. Because so many people interfering with Allah Almighty's matters. Don't interfere. What you got is the best. If you deserve better than this, Allah definitely will give you. But, this is your deserving, this is your 'qismat'. This is what Allah Almighty said is good for you. If you know to be satisfied with what is good for you you will be the most happy man in the world, the most happy. The most happy; cannot be more happy than this.

I give you another story, it is very nice story. When Mawlana Shaykh Abdullah, Grandshaykh & Mawlana Shaykh they were all the time going to Damascus from Jabl Qasiyun, and they go around and come, patrolling. This is Sunnah. One day, they were passing from the old bazaar, and there was one man there. He had a small towel, but very old and very dirty. It was white colour, but not anymore. And he had on the top of it rusty nails, rusty kind of metals, rusty, I don't know, but it's like, if you say somebody will give you for free, you will not take it. And M. Shaykh & Grandshaykh come, said, "Salam alaykum". He immediately made his feet and hands clean, -never clean - runs and kisses his Grandshaykh's hands. "Salamu alaykum Sayid". W'salam, saying.. "How is the business?" He was asking him. "Al Hamdulillah! Shukr, very good business. Al Hamdulillah, masha'Allah, very good business. A lot of customers" - People running, coming up and down from the bazaar, but he say, "This is my customer. Al Hamdulillah Ya Sayidi, very good business". Mawlana Shaykh say, "I was looking, but there's nothing to buy". "Al Hamdulillah, shukr Ya Rabbi, very good business. Too much clients, too much customers." So this is the most happy man. He doesn't care. He sees all the customers are for him. Big business, if they buy or don't buy, no problem.

That's why we have to try to be the most happy people, by saying shukr Ya Rabbi, shukr Al Hamdulillah, like my brother and to be tawba Ya Rabbi, tawba astaghfirullah, like me. So you'll be the most happy people, insha'Allah here & hereafter.

Wa min Allah al Tawfiq, Bi Hurmat Al Habib, Bi Hurmat Al Fatiha.
