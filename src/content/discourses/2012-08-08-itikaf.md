As-Salamu Alaikum. Audhu Billahi min al shaytan al rajim, Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fil jamia'

Today is the 19th of Ramadan, tomorrow will be 20th. The last 10 days of Ramadan, the Prophet (sas) would do even more worship. He would do more khayr/goodness & charity in the last 10 days. Because there is the holy night of Qadr in these days, it mostly falls in the last 10 days of Ramadan. There is a big ibadah/worship in this last 10 days, it is Itikaf. The Prophet(sas) would do it every year. When it is the last 10 days of Ramadan he would put something like a curtain in the mosque. He would bring his bed & put it there. He would do the last 10 days there. He would do Itikaf. This is Sunnah, as the Prophet (sas) did.

When he was traveling one year & couldn't do it that year, he did in his last year 20 days Itikaf. And it was a sign that he will change his world - pass to akhirah. There are many Hadiths for Itikaf. And one of them, a very beautiful one is from Hz Hussein (ra), the grandson of our Prophet (sas). The ones he (sas) loved most, he & his brother Hz. Hassan. This Hadith is narrated from Hz Hussein that the Prophet (sas) said: "Whoever does Itikaf in the last 10 days of Ramadan gets thawab of 2 hajj & 2 umra". And Itikaf should be done in the masjid. It should be a masjid where 5x adhan is recited & prayer performed, that Itikaf is done there. To sit there on the side & not go out unless there is a need. One can only leave for ablution & to eat, and should again return to the masjid immediately. And for ibadah/worship, he can do as he likes. There is no special ibadah, only to say "I make niyyah/intention for Itikaf" when starting.

And this can be tonight or tomorrow after fajrprayer- the Prophet (sas) would do like this; "I make niyyah for Itikaf." This is for the Itikaf of Ramadan. There can be other Itikafs also, can be between maghrib & isha. Prophet's (sas) Hadith says "whoever does Itikaf like this; stays in masjid & worships, he moves away from hell such that it is 7 times greater than the distance between heaven & earth". Therefore when we enter mosques, & in many masjids there is such a writing, if you say "I make niyyah for Itikaf" it is also ok. Each time you enter a mosque with the intention of Itikaf you still reach it's virtues.

Itikaf can be any time but this Itikaf for Ramadan must be 10 days, in the last 10 days. According to some who related, it is fard al-kifaya. What fard kifaya means is that one person in a place should do it, even if others don't do it they won't be held responsible. When they remember that Sunnah & do it, it brings Rahma/Mercy upon them, upon the whole place. If no one does it, they are not aware of what is happening in the world.. Well, it is not that they are not aware of the world but not aware of akhirah, then they would be responsible & sin.

As we said, Allah is not in need of anyone, is not in need of anyone's worship. For those who do, He opens such doors of reasons from His Bounty & Generosity. He gives them chances so that they worship more & gain more- To gain more thawab & for it to be a Mercy upon them.

Today we speak about Ramadan, it is very holy month. Now 19th of Ramadan, we are today. Tomorrow 20. 20, there is still 10 days to finish Ramadan. Prophet (sas), he was worshipping more in this last 10 days. Especially he was making Itikaf. Itikaf meaning, to stay in mosque, not going anywhere else. Only 10 days- when last 10 days, he was ordering to put curtain in some corner in mosque, his holy mosque. And bring pillow & his bed, his bed also bring. His bed not like our bed, only small one.

So, 10 days he was worshipping & he was speaking very little in this last 10 days. Because in this last 10 days also, very holy night in this- Most of time, this 10 days coming. Even for ladies also, they also can do, Itikaf. Because there is Hadith, many Hadith from Prophet (sas). The most famous from his grandson, beloved Sayyidina Hussein (ra). He said, Prophet (sas) saying, "who make Itikaf last 10 days for Ramadan, Allah give him 2 Hajj, 2 Umra, like he do this". Write for him, who doing Itikaf. So it is very big honour, very big kasib/winner. Trading for akhirah, you win too much insha Allah. And Itikaf, this for Ramadan.

For other times also there is Itikaf. You can make Itikaf between maghrib and isha. This is also Prophet (sas) one Hadith, he said "who make this and between him and between hell 7 times like between earth and sky, be away from hell." Other times when you go to mosque, you can say "I intend for Itikaf". When you are in mosque, you write like you have, making Itikaf in mosque. And Itikaf, only to stay in mosque this last 10 days. Not going out from mosque. Only for necessary thing, like wudhu or to eat, & come back to mosque.

What you can do for worshipping? You can do everything. Not, there is no special thing to do. The most important, to intend to be Itikaf in mosque. And this is beginning from either this night or tomorrow. This fajr, also you can do. And Itikaf is Sunnah. Because Prophet (sas), he was doing every year. Once before last year, his last year, he was in traveling, he couldn't do. So last year, he do it 20 days. He was in Itikaf 20 days, from 10th of Ramadan until end of Ramadan because one year he couldn't do. For this some scholars, they say this is obligatory but if somebody do it in one city other people, it is free. But if you not doing, so there is blame for them. Because they forget Sunnah, they forget Allah, they forget akhirah. Only they know about dunya, about not worshipping, playing. This is also important, to even some one he must do it in some city, one city. And this is occasion for people, Allah He make. Because it is from His Generosity to give people. He make special occasion. This also for one of this occasion. Because Allah, He is not in need of your worshipping

or your praying or your fasting, all you doing. Only from His Generosity, He like to give for you. If you like to take, He is giving without counting. You can do, take as much as you can take. Insha Allah, Allah make us with this people, with our niyah insha Allah. And many year for Mawlana, with Mawlana also. Mawlana making too many times, make khalwat even, Itikaf many times. Allah accept it for us & for him. Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, Al Fatiha.
