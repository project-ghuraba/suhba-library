As-Salamu Alaikum, our young visitors. You are like our grandchildren. May Allah guide you to His right path. May everything you do be for Allah. That way Allah makes you happy & in pleasure in here & hereafter. And He also grants you the eternal life. Welcome to you. You came with goodness. I would like that, my wish was that I, myself welcome you downstairs & make a sohbat with you. But because my situation can't tolerate that much, please you listen to the words that our daughter is recording.

Know that dunya is temporary & akhirat is eternal. No matter how long we stay in dunya, eventually we will leave it & go. We have our eternal life in Akhirat, with the pleasure of Allah Almighty. Therefore, it is good to not be deceived too much by dunya. Who are deceived, carry it's weight. Can't carry, they perish & go.

To gain nearness to Allah Almighty when you have the power of your youth make His servanthood daily at least by glorifying Him with sajda. You take a shower everyday. Right after it, if you pray 2 rakats wherever you are Allah Almighty accepts your sajda. Your dunya & akhirat be happy & prosperous. And your hearts are filled with nur. Your faces become beautiful. People who have no ibadah, their faces are ugly. All who have ibadah, their faces are beautiful. There are all kinds of beauty appliances & equipments. People can't be beautiful with them. Beauty is a natural gift from Allah Almighty to His servant. Who is nearer to Allah becomes more beautiful.

Be careful about this, o son. Dunya destroyed many people. And made those who run after it in a terrible situation.Finally those people, they collected goods of dunya, left them & passed away.

Therefore, you are like the children of my grandchildren. I make dua for you & I also accepted this visit of yours. Insha Allah you visit BaytAllah also & visit our Prophet (sas) as well, the Sultan of both worlds. May your dunya & akhirat be happy & prosperous. Be careful. Try to stop the night life. Get married & you become happy. Who don't get married, they wander around like a street rag; they can't hold on to anything & they become miserable. Don't be deceived by shaytan. Keep the way of the Beloved Prophet (sas) of Allah Almighty. Be in possession of youself, this is enough for you. Our advice to whole mankind is also this. I kiss & entrust all of you to Allah. Insha Allah in Akhirat you are granted to be in paradise. Allah also protect us from His Wrath & make us to be from the people of paradise, insha Allah. Salamullahu Alaikum. Send my greetings to them. This is enough for them.

H.A. I may take it to them &they listen.

M. They are relieved now.

H.A. Yes, they are.
