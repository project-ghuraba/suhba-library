Shaykh Muhammad Nazim Al-Haqqani An-Naqshibandi, Sohbat from January, 1991.

By the name of Allah Almighty, Beneficent and Most Munificent. Allah Almighty grants to you and to me good manners to be good servants, to be good servants. Allah grants to you to be beloved ones, not to be bad servants. Bad servants - they are devils, who really they are following devils. Anyone who's following devils, he is also a devil.

There is a saying from Prophet, sallAllahu alaihi wa sallam, "Anyone who makes himself like someone, he's going to be from that someone." Therefore, a believer must look after what he is doing. If he is looking and seeing that he's acting, it is actings of devils, he must not. Everyone asking an honor for this life, and believers, they are asking more than others - honor or to be honored here and hereafter. And who's asking honor for this life and after this life, he must take care for his actings and doings. And a believer, particularly a Muslim, must look which things giving to him honor, and which ones taking away from them honor. And most important, the honor in Divine Presence.

Here for this life so many devils, they have been honored by another devil. That is not honor. Honor, it is which going to be in Divine Presence. And if Allah Almighty giving honor to His servant, he's going also to be honorable here also. All saints, they have been honored for hereafter, but also they are honored here also. And their remembrance continues forever; continues with honor by everyone. Yes, that - it is real honor.

I was passing through Switzerland and I was visiting a holy man through Switzerland. According to traditions, he was a very simple person, never belonging to emperors or kings. Simple and poor person, living through forest, far away from people, running away from people, running to his Lord's service. Running to be able for his Lord's servanthood better. He was asking servanthood for his Lord. Perhaps, 300 years or more, he passed away. But yet, he has been respected and visited. Because his Lord dressed him honor from His Divine Presence. And it is someone that belongs the nation of Christians. But he was a true believer in his Lord and in His, Almighty's prophets and His Beloved Prophet, the Seal of Prophets, Sayyidina Muhammad, peace be upon him.

Now that I am speaking for him, he's sending to me to say that "I was follower the Seal of Prophets also. Don't think that, that honor given to me only to be follower Jesus Christ." That was the reason he was saying "that I was escaping from people away. And blessings that coming with me, it was a protection for this country. And so many years, through centuries, I was a guarantee for the peace, to be peace in Switzerland."

And thousands and hundred thousands and millions ones through the nation of Beloved Muhammad, peace be upon him, they are specially honored. Because whole nation of Beloved Muhammad, peace be upon him, just, they have been honored and especially millions through billions especially honored. Because they asked for honor through their servanthood to their Lord. And they are taking care, full care not to be taken from them that honor. That was they are saying now that every believer must take their full care for looking after their honor, which granted by their Lord to them.

Shaitan and devils calling people to free actions. "Free actions" means to live without discipline. And Allah Almighty calling His servants to accept His heavenly discipline. And mostly unbelievers, they are running out from discipline and joining devils. Therefore, mercy taken away from people. Justice taken away from them and love taken away from them and respect taken away from them. Then it is going to be no value communities. The value and honor of man is according to his mercy and justice and respect and love. If people losing them, no value for them.

We are trying to establish once again good communities with good members, whom, they should be against the devils. Because devils, their intention - now just it is going to be so clear - devils asking to destroy mankind. Devils, they are asking to destroy this world. And they are asking to bring sufferings to people. And all sufferings, all miseries are because the people, they are following devils, not following prophets, good ones. They are asking divinely help. If no heavenly help coming, it is so difficult. But people, because they are following devils, they must be punished. They are not accepting now what happening on earth from both sides.

Both sides, they are following devils. And they are not catching, and controlling themselves, they are going to destroy each other. Allah Almighty not ordering to fight. Allah Almighty ordering mankind to fight against devils only. Fighting both only against devils. They are like scorpions, they are like dragons. If Prophet, peace be upon him, fought, he fought only against dragons. And who he is not fighting against dragons, he is going to belong, to be belonged, to devils.

May Allah forgive us and support good servants. Wa min Allahi t-tawfiq. This is enough.
