As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
إِنَّ اللَّهَ مَعَ الَّذِينَ اتَّقَوا وَّالَّذِينَ هُم مُّحْسِنُونَ
<br>“Inna Allāha maʿalladhīna t-taqaw walladhīna hum muḥsinūn”<br>
“Indeed, Allah is with those who fear Him and those who are doers of good.” (Qurʼān 16:128). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says, “Allah is with those who fear Him and those who do good.” If you wish to be with Allah, know that a person who fears Allah definitely does not do evil. A person who is ashamed before Allah does not do evil. A person who fears Allah does good. “Muḥsinūn” means those who do good. Allah loves them. The people He loves are good people. They are the ones who have won; true humanity is in them. Do not look for humanity outside of them.

The more faithless a person is, the more harm they cause to others. The more faith a person has and the more they fear Allah, then they are not to be feared; only all kinds of goodness can be expected from them.

Therefore, the servants whom Allah loves are His accepted servants. A person should always think about doing good. One should only harbor evil, envy, or hostility towards something for the sake of Allah’s pleasure.

That is, a person should love for the sake of Allah’s pleasure and hate for the sake of Allah’s pleasure. How to hate? It means disapproving of the evil deeds done. One should make duʿā for the person, saying, “May Allah improve them,” but never approve of the evil deeds done. One should strive to stay away from evil deeds and not follow his ego.

The ego always inclines towards evil, towards all harmful and bad things. It is necessary to guide that inclination towards good. There is no other way; you must incline it. It is not possible to say to the ego, “Come, I will do everything you want, and then you will follow me.” The more you do what the ego wants, the worse and more evil will come; there is no end to it. There is no end to the desires and greed of the ego.

Therefore, you must fear Allah. One must fear straying from the way of Allah. May Allah protect us. Because that is the most thing to fear.

They ask, “Why fear Allah?” A person who fears Allah, as we said, can do all kinds of good. Those who do not fear Allah can do all kinds of evil. When a person does not fear Allah, even the worst creature cannot be as bad as him. Because those who do not fear Allah are those who do not value human beings, who disregard them. A person who fears Allah fears even harming an ant. May Allah make us among them.

May He make us among those who fear Allah, inshā’Allāh. May He make us among those who are on the right way, upon istiqāmah, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
