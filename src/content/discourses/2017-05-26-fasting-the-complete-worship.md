As-salāmu ‘alaykum wa raḥmatu Llāhi wa barakātuh. A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Shahru Ramaḍāna alladhī unzila fīhi l-Qur’ānu hudan lil-nāsi wa bayyinātin mina l-hudā wal-furqān." (2:185) Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla is praising the month of Ramaḍān for us. What a beautiful month it is. Qur’ān was descended in it. And guidance descended in this month. The month of Ramaḍān is the holy month since as long as the Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n was sent down to people as a guidance and light. Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla made this beautiful month a gift for ummati (nation of) Muḥammad. It wasn't meant for other nations. Rajab is the month of AllāhNone. Sha‘bān belongs to our ProphetNone. And Ramaḍān is a gift for the ummah so that their faith is strengthened and becomes a favour for ummati Muḥammad.

Who is ummati Muḥmmad? Who accept ProphetNone is from hisNone nation. Who doesn't, knows it for himself. But Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla didn't give this month to other prophets. To be more correct, no other month was given to other prophets or other people. Some days, for example, Friday is the best day. It is the day of ummati Muḥammad and our ProphetNone. It is holy as a day. Saturday is for Mūsá ‘alayhi s-salām. Sunday is for ‘Īsá ‘alayhi s-salām. Each prophet has his day. But as a month, only ummati Muḥammad was granted Ramaḍān by Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla. It is a great favour. How lucky is the one who has his share from it. How lucky and how fortunate is the one who knows the value of the month of Ramaḍān.

Some people say "When we are going out we see Ramaḍān only here in the dargāh". It is not clear if it is Ramaḍān in other places. And how unlucky and ill-fated those people are. People who don't know Ramaḍān, don't get benefit and don't take blessings out of it. They lose so many things. They don't realize they are pitiable. We are the people who know this favour, appreciate it and feel happy that it is a favour from AllāhNone. AllāhNone is giving to whom HeNone wishes. AllāhNone forbid, AllāhNone could have created us like other unaware people rambling in the streets. Therefore, it is a big favour that HeNone made us from those who appreciate it a little. Certainly, mankind is able to thank Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla only little. "Wa-ammā bi-ni‘mati rabbika faḥaddith." (93:11) It is a big favour. It should be said. It is not for boasting, but if AllāhNone granted us something, we should tell everyone, says Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla. We shouldn't hide it. This grant, this big favour is from Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla that we are trying a little and imitating to do as HeNone likes. At least, this fasting - you fast from morning till evening and it doesn't have any flaws. Because the aim of fasting is not to eat and drink from sunrise till the evening. There are some things that break it. As long as you don't do them, you are doing this worship purely. You are doing this worship. When a person is praying, did he make ablution correctly? Is what we read correct or not? It is possible that most people can't do it 100%. But the wisdom of AllāhNone, when you don't eat and drink, you are fasting. Awliyā’ did the same thing. Prophets did the same thing. Ṣaḥābahs did the same thing. And its wisdom is this, the wisdom of giving it so many virtues - because you are doing this worship 100%. You are doing it completely. People don't do other worships completely. No matter how much they try, they cannot. But fasting is not like this. And it is a favour of AllāhNone. It is a favour of AllāhNone to this ummah.

How lucky we are, shukr to AllāhNone, we reached Ramaḍān again. We will pray tarāwīḥ this night, in shā’a Llāh. Then we wake up for suḥūr. Suḥūr is also important. Our ProphetNone is ordering to make suḥūr. He is telling to make it late. People nowadays make suḥūr at 1 am or 12 pm and go to bed. That is not suḥūr. You should sleep and wake up. And it must be late. Suḥūr is made until imsāk. It is more virtuous to make it late.

Our ProphetNone is saying "Ṣūmū taṣiḥḥu" for fasting. He says we get health when we fast. It will be cure. And doctors say the opposite. They say to some sick people not to fast. But there are sick people and sick people. Some sick people are really badly sick. They can't fast. But those who are on foot all the time and do everything - when it comes to fasting, doctors say don't fast. Don't listen to them. Fast. It will be cure, with permission of AllāhNone. May AllāhNone bless it.

Next year may we be with Mahdi ‘alayhi s-salām in shā’a Llāh.
