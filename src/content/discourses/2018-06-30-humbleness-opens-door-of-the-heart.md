As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa raḥmatu Llāhi wa barakātuhu.

In shā’a Llāh we make ṣuḥbah, little ṣuḥbah, after make dhikr because, in shā’a Llāh, you coming for AllāhNone you must make something. In shā’a Llāh AllāhNone accept our ziyārah, our deed what we are doing.

Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.
