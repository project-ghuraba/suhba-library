Sheikh Bahauddin Adil, Sohbet of the 17th of April, 2016.

Allahu Allahu Allahu Allah. Allahu Allahu Allahu Allah. Allahu Allahu Allahu Allah. Allahu Allahu Allahu Allah. Allahumma salli wa salim 'ala Nabina Muhammad alaihi salam. Salatan tadumu wa tuhda ilaih mammara layali wa tula dawam. Allahumma salli wa salim 'ala Nabina Muhammad alaihi salam. Salatan tadumu wa tuhda ilaih mammara layali wa tula dawam. Allahumma salli wa salim 'ala Nabina Muhammad alaihi salam. Salatan tadumu wa tuhda ilaih mammara layali wa tula dawam.

Ya salam. May Allah keep us forever with the love of RasulAllah (saw). Today the fashion among the people is the jealousy. The first man to be jealous [of] for today it's Sayyidina Muhammad (saw). Everybody in this planet jealousing from the love of RasulAllah (saw). Countries, religions, people, all jealous from RasulAllah (saw). And as long as they are jealous, as long as we love RasulAllah (saw). This must be known.

Today we have strange movement, weird movement going on in the name of Islam called ISIS. Why they kill people? What they want to prove? What they want to change? What is the system they will put? It's very simple questions everybody must ask. We are simple people. Let's go like this. We are very simple people. We are very basic people. We are only knowing, ash-hadu 'an la ilaha illAllah wa ash-hadu anna Muhammadan 'abduhu wa rasuluh. And we are doing all what Allah Almighty order us to do as a prayer, as fasting, as zakat, as hajj. What else you can require from us as Muslim? You require from me to put a bomb in me and go and kill so many innocent people? Then I will become true Muslim? Or I will become true mu'min? Or I will become shahid? Or I will become this or I will become that?

We ask to get some explanation. What you are understanding from your behaviour and our behaviour? What is wrong in our behavior and what is right in your behavior? If we are wrong, tell us what is our wrong. If you are right, tell us what is your right. So simple. Yes. We are right al-hamdulillah. Because we are loving RasulAllah (saw). And we are distributing the love of RasulAllah (saw) among everybody. This is very important to understand. Our mission is to love RasulAllah (saw). That's why, we love RasulAllah (saw). Also I will answer this. When you are loving RasulAllah (saw), you will be full of love. Then you can be able to give love for others. If you hate RasulAllah (saw), as these people, then you are able to kill everybody and to hate everybody. This is so simple answer and questions. So simple results. If you make one plus one, if you give it for small child, he will tell you two. Never say you 3.5, 10.5, 1.500.000. No, he'll say one plus one is two. This is we are basic people.

Allah Almighty, when He sent RasulAllah (saw) as the greatest Prophet, His Beloved one, He said "You just tell them to believe in Me." He didn't bring the fast. He didn't bring the prayer. He didn't bring the rule and the regulation and everything. He said "Let them believe in Me and say la ilaha illAllah. Accept Me as their only God, only Allah." From where you bring all this kind of things and rubbish? Yes, if I go for very professional lawyer that he's very good in his job, he will write for me thousands of things to say I am right. This is what they are doing now. They say "According to this, you are kafir. According to this, you are mulhid. According to this, you are mushrik. According to this, you are this and according to this you are that. And..." Wow. I didn't know. I didn't know. I didn't know that I'm kafir. I didn't know. Because you write this one and you tell me I become kafir. But this is lawyer, he put all this kind of things according to this one, you are this and according.. And you look for result, you are kafir. La hawla wa la quwwata...

How can I be kafir? I say ash-hadu 'an la ilaha illAllah wa ash-hadu anna Muhammadun RasulAllah. How can I be kafir? I pray five times al-hamdulillah. How can I be kafir? I fast Ramadan al-hamdulillah. How can I be kafir? I pay zakat. How can I be kafir? I go for hajj. Explain. There is no explanation. This is all law talking. But when you are in front of the judge and the judge is Allah, the judge is Allah. Fear from Allah. We fear from normal judge. Everybody go for court, everybody says "Oh this is judge. No joke." must be...put our... yup, yup... Wear the tie and you must be quiet. Why? Fear from the judge. Because that judge, he can put you in jail in a minute. If you make noise he'll say "Take him one week for the jail." If you make more noise, one month. If you make more, one year. And it's judgment done. You cannot say "I am not accepting your judgment." You'll be in the prison anyhow. But now the judge is Allah Almighty. You must fear.

These people, they don't have fear of Allah Almighty by the way. Because they don't have the love of RasulAllah (saw) they don't have anything to lose. We have so many things to lose. We will not make Allah Almighty to not be happy with us. We're afraid to lose this. Because we are respecting Allah Almighty, we are loving Allah Almighty and we try to be in good shape in front of Allah Almighty. And we're afraid to lose the love of RasulAllah (saw), to not make misbehavior for RasulAllah (saw). These people, they don't care. They don't have anything to lose. They don't have anything precious to lose. We have so many thing that we're afraid to lose in front of Allah Almighty. We like to have our faith in Allah Almighty in good way. We want to be good one. We want to be the best one that reaching to Allah Almighty. This is our main target and this is our main job. Al-hamdulillah. And we are proud to be like this. And we are not scared from anybody because we're only scared from Allah Almighty. The one He give us the soul and whenever He want, He can take the soul. No problem. We are relaxed al-hamdulillah.

May Allah forgive us and make us all the time with the love of RasulAllah (saw) that carry on in our heart. And then it will carry on for other people's heart. This is our main mission. We don't have anything else. It is not time to do things that Allah Almighty, He doesn't like. It is the tyrants' time. Our time is to keep the love of RasulAllah (saw) in our heart, to keep the love of Allah Almighty in our heart, and to keep the respect and love for our murshid that we are continuing receiving all these precious things and light from them. May Allah forgive me and forgive us and make us all the time in the straight way to Allah Almighty.

Wa min Allahi t-tawfiq. Bi hurmati l-Habib, bi hurmati l-Fatiha.
