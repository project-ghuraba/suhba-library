Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alá Rasūli Llāh wa ālihi wa aṣḥābihi ajma‘īn.

I have been visited by a scientist of the universe, Sayyid Sa‘d. Sa‘d. We are honoured to meet you, mā shā’a Llāh! He has a lot of knowledge about universes. He came to me with an atlas. I cannot say that this atlas can give real benefits concerning the greatness of the universe. It is not possible that this atlas, through it, if it was a billion atlases it cannot give knowledge from the universe except an atom.

And we are human and Allāh Almighty honoured us with mind and also with will power, and has given us honour that was not given to anyone else from all Allāh'sNone creations. We are on the face of the earth with a size which compared to dunyā we are nothing, an atom. But Allāh Subḥānahu wa-ta‘ālá has given us a secret. Considering our outer appearance our creation is nothing, nothing! Maybe, maybe, it is part of an atom but it is not the honour of the human being. It is not related to the size of the physical being, the honour that Allāh Almighty has given us, has given not to the physical being, our visible, physical being - our physical being is useless - but Allāh Subḥānahu wa-ta‘ālá has given us a secret. This secret cannot be weighed. It cannot be valued with measurements, by any of the human scales, that grant we have from Him Almighty. It is not of material or non material. It's not of physical matter or of matter. No! AllāhNone has given us, Allāh Almighty has granted to the children of Adam a secret. Subḥāna Llāh!

We have the capacity to look to unbelievable distances. If you a hundred zeros in front of one it is of no value. If you put in front of it billion, billion, or trillion, or quatrillion, or quantillion - as much as you can put. If you say we were granted, that our physical being was granted value which does not compare to the value of the secret that has been given to us from our Lord, the Creator of Heavens and the universes, the Creator of everything. Now if through this atlas that was brought by our brother Sa‘d? Sa‘duddīn, if through it billions or trillions or quatrillion bigger maps, it cannot be of any value next to not even one of the dots that is being shown by this atlas, it cannot give from that point billions quatrillions pentillions times atlas it cannot give any value even to one dot. But Allāh Subḥānahu wa-ta‘ālá has given us an honour with something, a secret from Him Subḥānahu wa-ta‘ālá for us to understand. But understanding what we need now is for understanding creation. Subḥāna Llāh!

Creation is running it is not only within limits, it is running, running, running because HeNone is the Creator of pre-eternal and is the Creator of eternal. HisNone Creations are eternal. What do you want? Understand? It's continuous - like waterfalls, like the ones in America, Niagara falls. It is running, running and no body knows. He is Allāh Subḥānahu wa-ta‘ālá the Creator, the All Capable, the Creator of heavens and earth, most transcendent. Subḥānahu wa-ta‘ālá.

The ambassador who visited us, the ambassador, the one he worked on something that could not be figured out. There is an opening for us a door with this ṣuḥbah. The ones who know, the one who knows what it is that can be within limit or without limit and everything is within limits by HimNone. That limit is not for HimNone, it is for creation, for creation. To HimNone, everything by AllāhNone is within measure. What is given to creation, the people of earth and of heavens and above the skies. It is said that our LordNone is in darkness, and, you know darkness? Darkness? The extreme darkness, the extreme blackness cannot be known, unless HeNone sometimes gives something like a shooting star. The creation can see things like that.

They have no value in comparison to the creations of AllāhNone and this gentleman is coming here to tell me that he would like to write something from the realities of the universe. The universe cannot be known without the knowledge of the Creator. AllāhNone is the Creator of everything, everything! "Kull" (all), when Allāh Almighty says "kull" it cannot be known. What can be known is the particle that is indivisible of an atom. We are still in the indivisible particle of an atom. All the universes, millions, trillions quadrillions universes, all of them are the particle that cannot be divided. There's a limit to the dividing of an atom. Once it drops from the indivisible level, nothing remains but darkness, total darkness.

May AllāhNone forgive us. May AllāhNone forgive us, wa liLlāh AllāhNone our Lord Almighty said, "AllāhNone will not burden us with more than what we can carry." We are not responsible on this matter. There are people who are specialized in this matter and have been given a secret from the secrets of the particles that are indivisible. We are, we leave this matter to our Lord Almighty. May AllāhNone forgive us, forgive us and give us understanding. If we understand according our capabilities, this is it. AllāhNone bless you yā Shaykh
