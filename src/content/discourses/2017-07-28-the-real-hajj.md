As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa raḥmatu Llāhi wa barakātuhu.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīn wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh. Madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr. Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistāni, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla said in Qur’ān: A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Wa-liLlāhi ‘alá n-nāsi ḥijju l-bayti mani-staṭā‘a ilayhi sabīla" (3:97) Al-ḥamdu liLlāh we are now in Dhū l-Qa‘dah fourth here?

K: Fourth. Urdu number....

Sh: Four Dhū l-Qa‘dah. Now ḥujjāj who pilgrimage they beginning to go, for ḥajj Makkah. Now most of them who go this time they are going first to Madinah. After Makkah – because so early now but they are beginning because million of people they are going for ḥajj. Ḥajj it is very important and it is one of Islām who can afford by money, by health to – he obligatory to go for ḥajj. Ḥajj, ProphetNone saying cleaning you like you are from every sin like you are newborn baby from mother – how he is innocent. You will be no any sin left for you. But nowadays of course many here, maybe from Europe or UK or country of non-Muslim country, who live Muslim in this country, it is easy for him to go if he has enough money. If he want directly he can go. But other Muslim country unfortunately they are not allowed. Only certain number of people this they can go. And people they are writing their name – maybe I know people since ten years they are writing – and this year they said, "It will maybe my name coming, I can go for ḥajj." They still waiting. For this people it is, AllāhNone reward them as [if] they are going for ḥajj, in shā’a Llāh, every year. HeNone reward them because they are really waiting.

But for who are not allowing people to go there they will be responsible for this people because they cutting what AllāhNone saying. Call Sayyidinā Ibrāhīm ‘alayhi s-salām when he make Ka‘bah Mu‘aẓẓamah saying, call people, "Wa adhdhin fi n-nāsi bil-ḥajj" (22:27), call people for ḥajj. Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla ordering Sayyidinā Ibrāhīm and Sayyidinā Ibrāhīm he is, of course he is Khalīlu Llāh, he is not say objecting. He call people to ḥajj. No need to say pilgrimage we know everybody ḥajj. Ḥajj it is known. Especially for pilgrimage when you say pilgrimage, there is every kind of religion they said for everything pilgrimage. But our ḥajj only once a year and it is real pilgrimage, ḥajj. Not like that everybody by him what they like to do. They say the pilgrimage here, pilgrimage there. That's not good. So Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla when HeNone said for Sayyidinā Ibrāhīm, "Call people for ḥajj," he call and who has luck for to be come answer this calling, their name writing there and they will go and this is what we said real ḥajj. Because for other what this people they are claiming, no Sayyidinā Ibrāhīm. Only for this ḥajj, for Ka‘bah Musharrafah, and only once a year calling and who are lucky and they are went there. This Sayyidinā Ibrāhīm ‘alayhi s-salām, they by his barakah they was there, their name written there in that day. Who will go to ḥajj since that time, since Sayyidinā Ibrāhīm ‘alayhi s-salām, maybe four thousand year or so, four thousand year something like this. It is from that time AllāhNone write name who are will be in ḥajj. Of course as we said, there is AllāhNone make some reason – some of them maybe ill, some people not have enough money, some people government prevent them to go, some of them other going – this all from AllāhNone. But important, if you have enough money and health, you must try to go because it is not joke. It is farḍ obligatory, you must do this. As we said there is condition.

And when you go this is big blessing. All your sin going and other thing. There is in Ramaḍān also, when people fasting in Ramaḍān also going but after Ramaḍān, if you not going to ḥajj, also coming to you. You have enough money, you have everything, you don't go. This also coming for you, you are not obeying order of AllāhNone. You are not doing farḍ, obligatory. You also in sin until you make ḥajj. And ḥajj it is ‘ibādah worshipping. It is "al-ḥajju mashaqqah" meaning it is difficult. It is not easy and this is what ProphetNone, as Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla HeNone is saying for ḥajj, it is mashaqqah, it is not holiday. You will be tired, you will be suffering, you will be angry. So you must, you are going there for making worshipping, you must be very careful. You are not going for holiday village. You will be angry with people who are taking you. What is this? Leaving worshipping and looking for something it is not important. And even as much you suffer, as much you get blessing, you get reward from AllāhNone.

But many people they have learn from this rubbish school around whole world same. They must make protest and asking for their right. In Ka‘bah you are guest of Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla, guest of ProphetNone, it is shame and this is not good adab to say anything. You must be happy. "You must be you are accept us. We are coming here we are do to our the biggest worshipping who once in a life we will do." We must be patient for this. Mawlānā Shaykh every time they was asking Mawlānā, "Give advice for us. We are going to ḥajj." He said, "Don't be angry." This is first thing he said but they said asking another. This is enough if you don't angry. You can't angry there, you not do object for anything, you will be rewarded and AllāhNone will be pleased with you because you are pleased with HimNone. Allāh HeNone is seeing you. HeNone... you are HisNone guest. Maybe you are getting tired or angry but don't make it. Say for your ego, "it is nothing for me, you deserve more than this. This is you are make me to not happy with my Lord so you are deserve more than this. Don't speak I'm accept this, I'm happy with this."

So Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla HeNone accept your ḥajj and reward you, in shā’a Llāh. And HeNone take your whole sins and changing to reward and HeNone give you from barakah, from light, from imān with you to go to your country. But after ḥajj, when coming people from ḥajj, many people they are not acting like ḥajjī. They wasting what AllāhNone give them quickly. Why they are doing this? Because shayṭān he is angry with them, he is jealous. "What I done all this for now it is gone so I must attack them more and more." For this we are seeing, unfortunately many people. We see many cases they was before ḥajj they was better, not hundred times, thousand time better from after ḥajj. Subḥāna Llāh, this is from people who are looking after them their ego and walking after ego. You must be careful. Don't say "I'm ḥajjī now and I can do anything and I'm, I don't have sins maybe I can do anything." But it is, subḥāna Llāh, ya‘nī, many people they said this but it is real. Be careful. Keep your ḥajj and keep your Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla what HeNone grant you because what we said, people they are waiting years and they are not getting to go to ḥajj. Ḥajjī must be better with people, good behave with people, with family, with everything. But if he is behave against, he will be loser.

Don't follow shayṭān be careful of shayṭān. What as we said, if shayṭān, one shayṭān attacking before going to ḥajj, after ḥajj hundred shayṭān attacking. Mawlānā once he was saying one story for Bāyazīd al-Bisṭāmī Imāmu l-‘Ārifīn. He was in ḥajj and he see shayṭān there. He [shayṭān] has in his hand many ropes. You say if it will be shayṭān in ḥajj in Makkah Mukarramah? Oh the biggest one there because there the most enemy place for them there so they are every time to make ḥajjī to finish their ḥajj before going back to their country. And he [Bāyazīd] said to him, "O mal‘ūn what are you doing here?" He say, "I'm riding ḥajjī with this rope. I put rope them in mouth and ride them like donkey." And Bāyazīd al-Bisṭāmī he was say his coming for him funny little bit thought he now ḥajjī. He said, "For me also?" He said, "For you, I ride you without rope." Because that minute he make fault, Bāyazīd al-Bisṭāmī, to think he is better than other. So AllāhNone make him to for shayṭān to say this for him.

After there was after little while there was raining and was one old man stopping and rain coming water under – he couldn't pass to other side. Bāyazīd al-Bisṭāmī he said to him, "O uncle come for I take to other side by my, come on my back I take you." And he take him and when he was in the middle of water he say, "Oh I told you I can ride you without rope." So he was shayṭān. This is what, ya‘nī shayṭān he is really dangerous for everybody but especially for who are in right way. Be careful, be careful, be careful of shayṭān. Don't follow your ego. Don't be angry with people and destroy your good deeds. Be careful. AllāhNone save us from shayṭān. AllāhNone open for us, in shā’a Llāh, way of light and we, for who like to go to ḥajj, AllāhNone open way for them, all of them, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq al-Fātiḥah.
