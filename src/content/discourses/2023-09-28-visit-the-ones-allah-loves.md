As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Going to visit Awliyā’ brings benefit to everyone. Their barakah comes over those who visit. First of all it is good to visit the maqām of our Holy ProphetNone. Certainly, the greatest virtue is in visiting himNone. But of course, it is not easy for people to go there and visit himNone every day. Visiting Ṣaḥābah, Mashāyikh, and Awliyā’ wherever they live brings virtue and reward, great thawāb as well. Their doors are the door of AllāhNone. Whoever comes for the pleasure of AllāhNone, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gives him whatever he needs. Whatever their portion, certainly it is given to those who enter those doors. They come for the pleasure of AllāhNone. Whatever intention they have, AllāhNone gives their reward. It is always so.

Awliyā’ were sometimes not known to the people but their maqām is famous. They become more known after they pass away. Some Awliyā’ are known while alive as well. Therefore, for every person, how much it is needed for them, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla certainly gives reward to them based on their level. There was Somunju Baba who lived in Bursa. He lived in the time of Ḥaḍrat Yıldırım Bayezit. He used to make bread and sell to people. But people didn't see he was awliyā’. They knew him as derwīsh and a blessed man. They used to visit him and buy bread from him. They asked for a blessing and his du‘ā’. The son-in-law of Ḥaḍrat Sulṭān Bayezit was Emir Sulṭān then – the son of Amīr Kulāl who came from Bukhara. He was the Sulṭān's son-in-law. The Sulṭān built Ulu Camii (The Great Mosque) in Bursa. He decided to have the most knowledgeable man read the khuṭbah. So he opened it on Friday and asked that person to read khuṭbah. He told his son-in-law, Sayyid Amīr Sulṭān, "You can do this." He said, "No. There is a greater, a superior person, there is a shaykh here. He will do it."

The Sulṭān did not object, of course. Obedient to the order, Somunju Baba went up to give the khuṭbah. He explained al-Fātiḥah Sharīf in his khuṭbah. And he explained it in seven different ways. Firstly, he explained it in the way that the simplest people could understand. And then he explained it to people who have just started learning. And then in the way that hodjas could understand, and then in the way scholars could understand, and then in the way the Sulṭān could understand, and then in the way mashāyikh could understand. The seventh was the explanation no one could understand. It means whoever comes to this door, everyone takes his share... No one goes back empty-handed. Everyone benefits from that favour. The explanation of it in the way people can understand is that when you make a visit to any awliyā’, you will certainly profit. It will be given based on your level. Everyone will be happy. No one will be upset and say, "I took a very little." Because it is the gift of AllāhNone. They don't realize what they got. But it is certainly written as the portion of people.

And then this Somunju Baba went out of the mosque after shaking hands with everyone.. He greeted everyone and shook hands. The mosque had four doors. Everyone exited through one of those four doors. When they went out, everyone said, "I shook hands with him." Everyone who exiting through the doors said that. The miracle is that the same person was seen at all four doors. The miracles of awliyā’ are real. After showing that miracle and after becoming recognized, this holy being left Bursa and moved to Aksaray. People who come to holy ones leave with big gifts. They return with big favours. Therefore, visiting these holy ones is disliked by shayṭān and those together with shayṭān because they know and don't wish any goodness to people and believers out of envy and jealousy. May AllāhNone protect us. May AllāhNone not separate us from these doors. May their help and barakah be upon us, in shā'a Llāh.

This place is with awliyā’u Llāh. Everybody coming visiting them, sure he will be get blessed and get good thing with him. Anybody coming here for intention to visit, to see this place or other place like this with holy people, their graveyard – it is big favour for who visit this. And the first, the biggest one, of course, our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, Masjid an-Nabawī. With visiting himNone, with hisNone companion, with other – this is the most perfect one, most rewarded one. But of course we are to go there or to stay there or to... It is not easy. It is very difficult. Of course sometimes if anybody can visit, he must visit to get this blessed from Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam.

But other place also, everywhere, there is this blessed graveyard, blessed maqām. First for Ahlu l-Bayt some places, some places with companion, ṣaḥābah of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. They are respected and they are known. When in each country, their people, they from generation[s], they keep and they said, "This ṣaḥābah in this place, that in that place." So to visit them also good, very good. After this, mashāyikh or tābi‘īn, tābi‘ī t-tābi‘īn, awliyā’u Llāh. To visit them, this you get blessed because this place they are under light of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. It's raining bless[ings] for this maqām. Each one coming there, they can, they take as much their maqām, they are in need. Because maybe some people they coming there, they are not knowing anything for, from religion or from to what is this. Just to get blessed. We have this many people, they are not praying, not doing anything, but they coming for sake of AllāhNone to visit this holy people, holy place. So AllāhNone give them each one for how much he, his afford.

For this, there was in old time, maybe for four hundred year or some, around this, in Bursa. It's more than this. It is six hundred year, before Istanbul there was capital of Ottoman, Bursa. And there was this Sulṭān Bayezid, Yıldırım Bayezid. His son-in-law, Amīr Sulṭān, he is son of Sayyid Amīr Kulāl from our silsilah, Naqshbandī. His father in Bukhara, he was in... Sulṭān make him son-in-law because he was, he know the value of ‘ulamā’ and awliyā’. And he intend, Sulṭān, to build big mosque. Now it's still there, Ulu Camii calling. And big mosque meaning, huge mosque. It's very nice, it's still there. So he built and finish and he said to his son-in-law, "I intend to make the most knowledgeable one to make khuṭbah. begin opening, it will be day Friday, Jum‘ah. And it will be khuṭbah, Jum‘ah khuṭbah with you. You are the most I know, knowledgeable and I want you to make khuṭbah there." Sayyid Amīr Sulṭān he said, "No. There is one he is more knowledgeable than me. I will, in shā'a Llāh, he make this khuṭbah."

Of course Sulṭān they have adab, not arguing like this, "Who? Why? Not this, what this?" He said okay and this was one baker making bread and his people coming and taking, buying from his bread. They know he is blessed one but they don't know he is ‘ālim or scholar or awliyā’. Just they are very happy with him. They know he is blessed one and so he said for him, "You will make this khuṭbah." And he make khuṭbah this.

They call him Sumunju Baba meaning bread father, something like this. So he went through khuṭbah and explain Sūratu l-Fātiḥah, in seven different understanding for people. First one he speak and the children or normal people who not knowing anything, they were listening, they understanding and they were very happy. They explain what they can understand and all this understanding. After this second khuṭbah he make second explanation, explain for Fātiḥah for who little bit knowing to reading and Qur’ān and just not ‘ālim. Third for imāms for this not so very knowledgeable one. After he make for ‘ulamā’ khuṭbah. This people... But what they... Every time he making, other people not understanding. Only what this stage understanding. After he make for mashāyikh and after for Sulṭān and after for he make one, nobody understand. Seven different thing and what people they was astonishing – "This is our derwīsh Baba. He is, we buy from him bread and he make bread and how he is like this?" They know he is awliyā'u Llāh.

And when, for this example, for how when you coming some place, everybody he will take blessed. AllāhNone He give blessed as his level and everybody will be happy. For this, to come to visit awliyā’u Llāh, to visit maqām, it is order and good for believer. Don't believe for shayṭān and his follower because they are jealous for people to get blessed without tiring. They thought it is not good. You must follow them to make something difficult. But no, you can with coming to take blessed from this place for your intention this. AllāhNone give you because you are not coming worshipping a graveyard, not coming to worship awliyā’u Llāh. No, you are coming here because this beloved for AllāhNone. Because they are beloved one for AllāhNone, for sake of AllāhNone we are coming and AllāhNone reward you for your intention, your niyyah. This is everybody coming even they don't know anything, they not praying, not doing, also they are believer. They have something in their heart so this it will be growing more with coming to place like this.

And after this, after khuṭbah, this awliyā’u Llāh he, they are in this big mosque. Because very big, there is four entrance for mosque. Everybody going from there. They want to kiss his hand and he was in front of door. Each one passing, kissing and greeting Shaykh and they going out. After this they said, everybody from other side going, "We greet Shaykh and we kiss his hand," they said, from four of door of this. Even with this he make karāmah to show people and they was all happy. They know he is awliyā’. But what happen? He is left this Bursa. He went to another city in Türkiye because he is, he don't want to be, they don't like to be famous. They like to hide.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh this is, this awliyā’u Llāh we... They are beloved one for AllāhNone and we love them. And they are giving – as much you love them, they love you and give you from bless and from reward, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone keep us with them all time, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
