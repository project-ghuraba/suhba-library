As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, madad yā Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Inna mā tū‘adūna la-’āt" (6:134). It is the Qur’ān of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla: The promise of AllāhNone is true. It will happen. It will certainly happen. There are things foretold by our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam in this world. All of them happened and we are approaching qiyāmah. Some big events that will happen, like the coming of Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām and the descent of ‘Īsá ‘alayhi s-salām, are big signs. They are very near too. That has been promised. That is the word of AllāhNone. That will happen.

‘Īsá ‘alayhi s-salām is in heaven. He will descend to earth and join Sayyidunā Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām to destroy the dajjāl and kufr. Some people are saying now that Mahdī (as) has appeared and ‘Īsá (as) has returned spiritually. That is not so. There will be no kufr when Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām comes for real. There are two types; several types of Mahdī to be more correct. The real one hasn't appeared yet.

Fake ones have come and keep appearing. And there are many crazy people who claim they are Mahdī. People just laugh at them. But there are some fake ones who are followed by some foolish people. They are not foolish, but cheated. Because our holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam promised that Mahdī (as) will come. They don't know his true reality, for when Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām comes, he will not raise money and won't ask for help. He will help everyone instead. He will gather everyone to him. If he collected people one by one on television and newspapers, he wouldn't make the world Muslim in a million years. But during his time everything will take place miraculously.

About what they say... From time to time, we laugh about it. However, who says he is Mahdī, as we said, should either check his sanity, or people should be warned about him, as he may be trying to harm people. There are large groups of people who even claim their Hodjas are Mahdī. If he is Mahdī, why did oppression not end? Tyrants are everywhere. Nothing has changed. Islām hasn't spread all over the world. When Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām appears, everyone will become Muslim. The whole world will become Muslim. It is the word of AllāhNone: the promise that the whole world will be in Islām. It is what AllāhNone has promised. It will happen with Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām and ‘Īsá ‘alayhi s-salām. ‘Īsá ‘alayhi s-salām is in the second heaven. He will certainly come. There is no, "He came physically, he came spiritually." No. He will be by our side and will lead us. After Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām, he will rule. Islām will rule. He will lead as someone from the Ummah of our Holy ProphetNone. These things will happen. No doubt. This is the belief of ṭarīqah of Ahlu s-Sunnah wa-l-Jamā‘ah. This is right. Everything apart from this is not right.

May AllāhNone let us reach that time. May we be with him in shā’a Llāh. These beautiful days will come. The second century of happiness after our Holy ProphetNone will be in their time. It is near in shā’a Llāh. This world is in an awful condition. It was awful in terms of religion before. But now it is awful from all aspects.

In old times, when people were told someone had died, they would have goose bumps. Hundreds of thousands of people die now – millions die. And no one cares. The world is in such a state now. It cannot stand more in shā’a Llāh. May it be close in shā’a Llāh. May we not wait longer in this darkness in shā’a Llāh.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla HeNone said in Qur’ān: "Inna mā tū‘adūna la-’āt" (6:134). What HeNone promised, it will come. AllāhNone promised for HisNone Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, for Muslims for to be, whole world Islām. It will be, it must be. Who will do this? Sayyidunā Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām. Sayyidunā Muḥammad al-Mahdī and Sayyidunā ‘Īsá ‘alayhi s-salām. This is, we are waiting for this. And it will be happen.

But, there is now, or before also... every time people they saying, this happened – where you are? Sleeping? Our Mahdī came, and he died and passed away, and ‘Īsá ‘alayhi s-salām he is only by spiritual he will come. And he come also. There is many Muslims they are saying this. Because they don't have knowledge or belief, good belief. They saying this, and there is, for this, there is a real Mahdī (as). There is, I said, there is three kinds of Mahdī. One real one, he didn't appear yet. And there is false [ones], many of them appear. And they have big group following them. And still believe they are Mahdī. And you... How they believe for this? Even himself, he didn't say for himself [I am] Mahdī. But his followers they are saying [he is] Mahdī. And there is third kind. He has mental problem: many of these. Every day you can see many of them you meet – "I am Mahdī, I am Mahdī." These crazy ones from mental house. These are only make you to laugh.

But false one, it is problem because many people following them. Why they are following them? Because they don't have knowledge. Only what they have knowledge, they believe Mahdī (as) will come. And they don't know how Mahdī (as) he will be. Mahdī (as) not be, going to television, giving speeches and giving for people, discussing with people – "I am Mahdī... I am like this, I am like that." And collecting money to buy Mercedes or to – other things. There was one, he passed away, that Mahdī was collecting money, in England, and he passed away. [There are] many of these people, but people must know Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām when he come, he will change everything. As Mawlānā said, he will change from A to Z. To be Alif, to be Lām Alif at the end. All completely will change - all this world system. Everything. And no need for help from anybody for him, to collect money, to ask for money. The money it will be like mountain on him. No value for money for him. Only value – to save humanity from this situation which it has become worse and worse. What will...

Before, it was worse only by religion people, they are, cannot to... many people not following religion. But now, religion and even for oppression. Before was saying, if you hear somebody they killed, you was very anxious, very not happy. But now, thousands of people every day, they are killing there, burning there, oppressing the people. Going out from their country, nobody care about them. Millions of people they are suffering. Before not, not like this. Now, it is time for some help from heaven. And this heaven, will, in shā’a Llāh, help with Sayyidunā Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām.

He must come. Because, now, time – there was jabābirah, there was, Mawlānā said, time of jabābirah – tyrants. Now, more, with hormone tyrants. They are more worse now in this time. In shā’a Llāh, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla - There is limit for everything. When this limit coming, in shā’a Llāh Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām come. And ‘Īsá ‘alayhi s-salām he will come also. In shā’a Llāh, we hope to be very near, and to be with him, in shā’a Llāh. This century of happiness, like Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam century of happiness – first one. This, in shā’a Llāh, second one. In shā’a Llāh, we will be with him, in shā’a Llāh. We ask for this from AllāhNone. Because real, we are people needy for this. People they don't have any hope. They don't have anything, except to wait for Mahdī (as). And these people, some people they use this and to – for their benefit, saying, "I am Mahdī... I am like this, I am like that." So, in shā’a Llāh, when real one coming all this will disappear. And we be with real one, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
