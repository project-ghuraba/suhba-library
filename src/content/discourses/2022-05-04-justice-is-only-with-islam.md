As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Dastūr. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Justice is the foundation of Islām. When there is no justice in Islām, it is not real Islām. Justice was done by the Islamic states, by the caliphs. After the end of the Ottomans, there was no such thing as justice. Neither in Turkey, nor in Arabia. Neither in Europe, nor in Africa. They say they are doing justice. But what they say is not true, especially in western countries. They pretend to do it. But in reality, it is not applied. Only Islām does justice.

Our Holy ProphetNone had said about Sayyidatinā Fāṭimah, "Even if myNone daughter does something, I will certainly punish her." Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam said so. So when someone goes to the court now, it takes years. In the end, he will be proven right, or accused, even if he is right. But even when he is proven to be right at last, late justice is not justice. There were qāḍī during Ottoman times, who are called judges now. They used to come to court and close the case in one session. The right one would take his right. And the wrong one was given punishment if he had punishment, or made to return the other's right if it was due. They would be satisfied and left without any objection. But now they bother the court for years with useless things. And in the meantime, the things they are right about get lost.

This is injustice. It is wrong. AllāhNone is not pleased with this. The claim is transferred to ākhirah then. And in ākhirah, it will be more difficult. There will not be a delay that time. Whoever takes someone's rights, immediately receives punishment. AllāhNone is that just. HeNone is the possessor of justice. HeNone asks for it in everything. When there is no justice in a country, there is no peace, no goodness. That is why it is important. Everyone sees it, but cannot change it. Why? Because there is no real caliph of our Holy ProphetNone. And they are taking other people's rights as they like. They are working together for injustice.

May AllāhNone send us the Ṣāḥib who will bring justice, in shā’a Llāh.

We say for justice, it is the main thing Islām was doing and ordering. AllāhNone ordering: "A‘ṭi kulli dhī ḥaqqin ḥaqqah." Justice, this is. Nowadays, no any place in this world they make justice. All court... Especially in countries like our countries, they are making like very long procedure for one very small thing. They make it very long, long, long, and at the end, many times they not give right justice, they give wrong decision. But maybe this, like Europe or America, other countries, they claim they making justice also. But they are also not justice at all.

Big example, for our doctor from our in-law, America, he said, he must... justice is very long, so they make "special", they said. There is special place they – government, accept a "special justice court". This finish – one, in six months. And they take three years, and they take all his money, finish his money, and not giving decision also. This is, Mawlānā Shaykh was many times telling the story of this. Story from old book called Kalīlah wa-Dimnah, from old Sanskrit book I think, about two cats and one monkey. Monkey, he is justice – judge. They come, these two cats. They bring one piece of cheese. And they said, we we stole this and we made it in half, but he take bigger than me. So, you must give us our rights. "Okay," he said. He bit one, put in scale. "Ah, no. This, it is too much for this." He bit another one, and at the end very small thing have left there. They said, cats, "Okay, okay. Oh, highness judge. We accept this." "You accept, but justice not accept this." So, he ate the two. Now just finish.

Our justice like this – whole world. No justice. Only justice with Islām. And this was finished when Khilāfah (the Caliphate) finished. After this, all coming oppressor... corruption – everywhere corruption. No, you cannot say in any place in world there is no corruption; everywhere corruption. Only Islām and khalīfah (caliphs), they was doing this. And they was, in one sitting they finish, this qāḍī, judge. Qāḍī, name of Muslim judge. He know about religion, he know about psychiatry. Everything he know; he is very clever man. They put him there. And they choose these people to judge for people. And in one time, they was finished. Everybody taking his right, and they accept, they not making any objection. And they was happy. But after this, this world became mess, all this, what happened in this our time. All this – last century, from this injustice.

AllāhNone keep us safe from this fitnah, and send somebody to make again justice, in shā’a Llāh. Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
