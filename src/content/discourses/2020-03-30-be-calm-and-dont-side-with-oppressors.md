Waṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-F’āiz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-

A game has been organized, and now they say, “The world will not be the same again.” As they see it, they threaten other people for their own interest’s sake. They did things so as to govern the world. It is done by some people, and they have plans, etc. What they have been planing [though] is not for the benefit of mankind but for their own. They do this in order to rule over the world. There have always been Nimrods and Pharaohs, and they continue to try to manage the world [to place it] under their rulership. Allah will let their plans go ahead, however, [only] until an appointed time, and then Allah will turn their plans upside down.

So there is no need to be hopeless. Relax your hearts. Muslims, those with faith, have their strength in their faith. Allah is with those who are with Him. Stay on the side of Allah, and not against Him. No matter how strong they seem to be, they have no power. Allah will cancel all their dirty work suddenly, and then they will not be able to manage anything. So trust in Allah, and turn towards Him. Those who are with Allah are the victorious ones, and they gain.

This [message] is for Muslims, because Muslims are naive, and they easily believe whatever they hear. They see the outward, [but] not the inward. What it is or is not, they do not know, and so they should be with Allah, and thus be saved here and in the Next World. Do not take the side of the oppressors! Stay on the side of Allah. Allah is not an oppressor. Oppressors are those who obey Satan and pharaohs. May Allah protect us and guide people. May Allah protect everyone. Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-F ti ah
