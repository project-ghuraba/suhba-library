As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says, whoever is to say something, let him say good things. If he won't say good things, he must be quiet. It is better to keep quiet. Speak about good things. To speak good things is carrying out the order of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. And believers, Muslims, those who love our Holy ProphetNone, people of ṭarīqah, people of Ahlu s-Sunnah wa-l-Jamā‘ah and people on the right way carry out this order, shukr to AllāhNone. It is always carried out.

But these days, it is the month of Rabī‘u l-Awwal, the month of our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. We always remember the birth and arrival of our Holy ProphetNone in this month and take this beautiful manifestation upon us. We take our share of the mercy, gaze and reward of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla for ourselves. This is a good thing to say. It is not good to keep quiet about it. You must not. You must say it because you are saying good things. Who are those who don't say good things? Those who object to this. Those who say when you read Mawlid that you don't do good, that you commit a sin. And they are people who think they are Muslim. How can that be? Which one is saying something good, and which one is saying something bad? The people who are saying something good are those who honour our Holy ProphetNone, who remember hisNone birth, life and character. Our duty is to remember all the actions of our Holy ProphetNone, those things that are wājib and those that are farḍ. It is good that we do them. As our Holy ProphetNone said, we must speak. But when we speak, we must speak well. If you are going to say bad things, keep quiet. Those who object to it should keep quiet. They must keep quiet. It is bad for them to speak. It is not good. Therefore, they should know this themselves.

This is just a short advice. We don't make it long for them to think about. May AllāhNone give understanding to all of us. May people not be confused about Mawlid being bid‘ah or Mawlid not being good. Besides, we are not celebrating the birthday of a singer for it to be bad. Ḥāshā mina l-ḥuḍūr, lā tashbīh. It tells us about the power and the miracles of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, and tells us about our Holy ProphetNone who is an example for us – all about himNone and about hisNone miracles. What can it be apart from goodness? May AllāhNone let all of us attain hisNone intercession, in shā’a Llāh. Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying: “Who wants to speak must speak good, khayr, who will not, he will not speak.” “Fa-lyaṣmut,” ProphetNone saying – to keep quiet, don't say anything.

Now our days, al-ḥamdu liLlāh we are in month of birth of of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. And every time we make reading hisNone mawlid in very many languages. They have this: how Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam... from hisNone... before birth. And how hisNone mother, miracle happen for her or for others. And when heNone honour this world, how it was miracle happen. How light coming, how angel was happy, whole exist[ence] they was happy. This is what Mawlid – this book we read every time telling us about this. This is order from AllāhNone to praise. And we are praising, and with – making ṣalawāt, and asking for shafā‘at from ProphetNone. What is wrong with this? This, you can speak. You can tell this. This is about Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam – this is khayr. Khayr for us, before other. This is benefit for us, before other.

But now there is people, they claim they are Muslim. And they understand, they Arabic. They understand, they, maybe we don’t have – cannot have one percent from their knowledge from everything. But they are... they have only knowledge from outside. But inside, they are not understanding. They have knowledge without understanding. They say, “This bid‘ah, this is ḥarām. This is…” Why? We are making order of ProphetNone. We are saying good thing. We say khayr. We are not adding anything from ourself. Only with people happy to hear how our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, how heNone is great, how heNone is blessed, how heNone is mercy for us. This is what in Mawlid, they are telling. So this is we must speak. But who must shut up, not speak? These people. Because they are not saying good. They are saying bad. They are saying sharr: “Don’t praise, don’t do this. Don’t do good thing, do bad thing only.” This is what they are telling people.

And this today, short ṣuḥbah but they must listen for this and understand; because they if have anything to object – they cannot object for this. This is ḥadīth of ProphetNone, order of ProphetNone: “Say good thing.” And we are people who are reading mawlid, gathering for mawlid. They are doing good thing. Not back-biting for people, and they are not gathering for birthday of a pop-star or something like this. We are, only for AllāhNone, for sake of AllāhNone and love of ProphetNone, we are gathering. AllāhNone make these people, give them hidāyah. And AllāhNone make our ProphetNone to be happy with us, to be give hisNone shafā‘ah for us, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
