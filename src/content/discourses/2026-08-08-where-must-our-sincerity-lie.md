As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا اتَّقُوا اللَّهَ وَكُونُوا مَعَ الصَّادِقِينَ
<br>“Yā ayyuhā lladhīna āmanū t-taqūllāha wa kūnū maʿa ṣ-ṣādiqīn”<br>
“O you who have believed, fear Allah and be with the truthful.” (Qurʼān 9:119). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

“O believers, fear Allah and be with the righteous,” says Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. He says, “Be sincere so that your life may be good and beautiful.” But people, because their egos don’t desire it, associate not with the good, but with the bad or with unnecessary things. They become attached with sincerity to things that are completely useless.

People become so attached sincerely to bad things, or rather, to unnecessary things. And the most useless thing they become attached to – and I’ve been thinking about it for days – is football. Football is useless. Okay, you play a little, run around, have fun, we understand; but what we mean is these football clubs.

People become so attached to those clubs with such sincerity, such devotion, that it’s impossible to change them to another club. Even if you gave them millions, they wouldn’t change their club. But when it comes to the right path, they’ll be extremely fickle; a thousand times over. They won’t keep their word. You can’t rely on them. But in the other matter, there is neither fickleness, nor breaking of word, nor reneging. He continues with that commitment until the end of his life.

These are unnecessary things; what is truly necessary is to remain steadfast on the way of Allah. It is to find a true murshid and follow him. This is not a game. Life is precious. Time is precious.

You get angry, fight, curse and insult others over unnecessary things. Okay, we understand you love it, but there’s no need to do these unnecessary things. A person should think a little. Okay, you love it, we understand that. But the sincerity, loyalty, and keeping of word shown towards that club must be directed towards the way of Allah. If one does that, he would become one of the great awliyā.

Therefore, as we said, shayṭān distracts people with either unnecessary things or sins. May Allah protect us. This is a strange disease and one of the biggest problems of the end of times. As we said, a person can love that, but that sincerity must be directed towards Allah.

May Allah help us. May He not keep us preoccupied much with unnecessary things, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
