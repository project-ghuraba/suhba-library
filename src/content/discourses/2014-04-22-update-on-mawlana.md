Subhanim Allah Sultanim Allah Nabim Muhammad Alayhi's Salam Subhanim Allah Sultanim Allah Nabim Muhammad Alayhi's Salam

Salamu alaikum all brothers and sisters. Alhamdulillah, I like to make some update about Mawlana Shaykh health. As you know, Mawlana Shaykh at the moment he is in intensive care and he is under the medication and he is under the pressure of medication and this kind of things. Everyone knows how it is, but I like to say about Mawlana Shaykh, good things.

Alhamdulillah today, I am able to say good things about Mawlana Shaykh He is much better, he is much improving and he is going to be better and better insha Allah with your du'a and with your sincerity and with your donation, qurban for poor people, and I know that been all around the world been feeded for the poor people. So many food, charities, in the name of Mawlana Shaykh.

And we are believer Alhamdulillah, and we are the people that we believe in sadaqa, we believe in charity and as-sadaqa tul, tazidu l-umr wa tadfa' ul bala; the sadaqa is taking the bala out and make the life longer. And insha Allah with this one, will give long life to Mawlana Shaykh. We are happy from you, Mawlana Shaykh is happy from you, and Mawlana Shaykh, insha Allah, insha Allah, we are asking from Allah Almighty to give him health because he need this one.

And insha Allah, Allah Almighty will give him power to look after himself to do all the things by himself, and to guide us. His presence is one reason and the big reason for us to be guided, insha Allah. And I am very happy that I am giving the good news and it is good news for me first, and for all of you, and for all of us. And I am saying this to make you happy and I am very happy, Alhamdulillah. I am waiting from Allah Almighty to accept our fully du'a about Mawlana Shaykh, fully recoverment.

Wa min Allahi tawfiq bi hurmati l-Habib bi hurmati l-Fatiha Ameen.
