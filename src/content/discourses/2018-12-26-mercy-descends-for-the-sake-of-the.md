Awliyā’

As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīn wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

Shukr to AllāhNone we came here again and met you. It has been more than a year. But as it is Cyprus and Lefke is close... Still we have to see it. We have to visit the saints here as well. We have to get from their virtues. We meet our brothers and visit mashāyikh here. They are the spiritual power of this place. Awliyā’ and mashāyikh here bring mercy of AllāhNone to people in such darkness. Because people have forgotten ākhirah completely. They are after dunyā. They are after dunyā too much. Therefore, even when Allāh'sNone anger is necessary, AllāhNone is sending HisNone mercy for the honour of these saints and giving barakah. They are the guards of this place, spiritual guards. No matter how much oppression there is and how much people are sinning, AllāhNone will finish them later. They will be replaced with people who will serve Islām and fill these mosques. This is the promise of AllāhNone.

Because these people have given their blood and souls here. Was it for its wealth? No. Not for wealth. They could have been wealthier in other richer places if they wanted. But they discovered, conquered and left this place for the pleasure of AllāhNone. And then mostly people who follow Shayṭān came afterwards. But AllāhNone is changing it. Mankind is mostly defeated by their egos now. What happens when defeated? Does it harm AllāhNone? No. No harm comes to AllāhNone. It only harms themselves. During the time of Mūsá ‘alayhi s-salām Banī Isrā’il suffered a lot from Pharaoh. Mūsá ‘alayhi s-salām saved them. And though Pharaoh was destroyed, they started a rebellion. They said, "Give us God to worship." They asked from Mūsá ‘alayhi s-salām various kinds of oppression and wishes Pharaoh had never done to them. Despite being given the best every time, they were ungrateful. They went against AllāhNone.

At last, AllāhNone forgave them again for the honour of Mūsá ‘alayhi s-salām. HeNone said, "INone am giving you this city." There is a big city of Eriha (Jericho) in Palestine. It is an abundant rich place. Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla said, "Enter it." HeNone said it through Mūsá ‘alayhi s-salām. "You should say ḥiṭṭah (2:58)." Ḥiṭṭah is probably something like wheat. Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla said, "Say it and enter upon that nation." If they did, they would be fine, they would live well. But they rebelled so much that even they saw many miracles from Mūsá ‘alayhi s-salām, they didn't accept it, making it a punishment. They had reached the last point of rebellion. They ordered Mūsá ‘alayhi s-salām to go away as their rebellion and all types of ungratefulness. "Fight with your Lord. After you fight and defeat them, call us there. We'll come then."

After so much misbehaviour and lack of adab, they deserved their punishment. And AllāhNone gave them some punishment. HeNone said, "Go and get lost in a desert. You won't be able to leave this desert." "Why won't we? There is no wire, no wall, nothing. We will escape from it. Nobody can stop us." They woke up in the morning and decided to leave in order to escape from Mūsá ‘alayhi s-salām and not to see him. Mūsá ‘alayhi s-salām was upset with them. He had left them and cut his contact. They started their way in the morning and walked till the evening. When it was evening they realized they had returned to where they started. They thought they walked the wrong way. So they decided to walk in the opposite direction on the second day. They walked till evening again and got very tired. When they woke up in the morning, they were at the same place again. It happened so for 40 years. Within 40 years, all of those who rebelled there died. Their children survived and left the place. Even Mūsá ‘alayhi s-salām couldn't enter Palestine. He passed away outside Palestine away from those people. He never talked to those people anymore. Because they deserved Allāh'sNone punishment, he didn't pay attention to them.

So no one can interfere in Allāh'sNone order. Mūsá ‘alayhi s-salām passed away outside it. Hārūn ‘alayhi s-salām as well. Then Yūsha‘ ‘alayhi s-salām entered Palestine. Why am I telling this? This is the condition of the country [Cyprus]. It is a Muslim country. Islām conquered this place. No secular people or others conquered it. Those who conquered it were Muslims. It started during the time of our ProphetNone with ṣaḥābīyah Ḥalā Sulṭān. Up until the conquest by the Ottomans it was with Muslims. They didn't look for anything else here. They didn't come for money. They wouldn't sacrifice so many souls for money. They wouldn't give so many martyrs.

Therefore, with Allāh'sNone permission no need to lose hope and be sad by looking at this condition. AllāhNone willing mosques will fill up here, dargāhs will be full, in shā’a Llāh. May AllāhNone be pleased with all of you. As we said, the rest who go against AllāhNone will be finished. They're not going to stay forever. Because I am seeing for the second time now wildness has reached its limit. AllāhNone can remove them one by one or as HeNone likes. Everything is under Allāh'sNone command. No need for our interference.

We pray to AllāhNone that HeNone gives guidance so that we see those beautiful days, in shā’a Llāh. Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām is near AllāhNone willing. Mawlānā once said about here when he returned from Shām: "I brought Shām here." Barakah of Shām has moved to Cyprus with Allāh'sNone permission. Shayṭān can try as much as he wants. Everything he does is void. No benefit from it. Only actions of the one who says "Allāh" are more valuable than the world. Our ProphetNone says, "When you go to bed saying, 'Lā ilāha illā Llāhu waḥdahu lā sharīka lah. Lahu l-mulk wa-lahu l-ḥamd yuḥyi wa-yumīt wa-huwa ‘alá kulli shay’in qadīr,' INone forgive all your sins." Therefore, we don't worry AllāhNone willing. We are safe. Allāh'sNone favours are great. May AllāhNone guide these people. May they be forgiven their sins, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq. Al-Fātiḥah.
