As-Salāmu ‘Alaykum wa Raḥmatullāhi wa Barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu Billāhi Minash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm.

Wa ṣ-Salātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Akhirīn.

Madad yā RasūlAllāh, Madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, Madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

Dastūr Mawlana Shaykh Abdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistāni, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-

Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-Suḥbah wa l-Khayru fi l-Jam‘iyyah.

May there always be Shukr to Allah. No matter how much we make Shukr, it is not enough. Shukr to Allah, we had a trip for the pleasure of Allah; we went safely and came back safely. Insha’Allah with the Barakah and presence of Mawlana Shaykh Nazim, these things are going on. His Barakah and Himmah are everlasting.

The love of our brothers there and they themselves are increasing. It is the same everywhere. It has been two years since we couldn’t go for a trip to Europe. Masha’Allah, they have become 3 or 5 times more. They’ve already had lots of love, and it has increased. As they love this way of Haqq/truth, they keep on the way of Mawlana Shaykh Nazim who showed them this love. They keep on the way of our Holy Prophet. And whoever is granted this and loves him, attains the kindness of Allah. Not everyone wants to leave their egos and the desires of their egos and follow this way. The ego predominates more. People who come to this way attain the kindness of Allah. Allah has granted this way to them, this beautiful way.

Most of the people are after Dunya, their ego and their desires. They don’t pay any attention to this. And those who go on that path are unfortunate people. They fall into all kinds of troubles. They don’t know where they come from. If they do, they will come to the right way so that their sufferings turn into benefit for them. But now there is no benefit. They put blame on this and that. Their minds are capable of that only. Whereas if they knew that ىلَاَعَت ِاللَّه نَ مِ هِ ر ِشَ وَ هِ رِيْ خَ, both goodness and badness are from Allah, they wouldn’t be so upset. And all their sufferings would be of benefit for them. Our Holy Prophet said, “I am amazed by the condition of believers; everything always turns out well for them”. Whatever looks like evil is good and the good is good for them. Nothing turns out bad. Otherwise, people who are with shaytan are always in loss and suffering.

Shukr to Allah, He put us on this way. Insha’Allah may there be means for guidance for people. People are in need. They don’t know what they need. But the real need is spirituality, Iman in Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla and love of our Holy Prophet. There are people who can show them this way. But many people obey their egos. May Allah protect us. May Allah make us all steadfast on this way insha’Allah. Because nevertheless, we have to be careful; may we not suddenly slip and perish.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
