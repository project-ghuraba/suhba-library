As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm.

The greatest oppression is to associate partners with AllāhNone. “Inna sh-shirka la-ẓulmun ‘aẓīm” (31:13). Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is one. There is no one like HimNone. As it is said in Sūratu l-Ikhlāṣ, “Qul huwa Llāhu Aḥad” (112:1); AllāhNone is one. HeNone has no partner. HeNone was not begotten. HeNone has no offspring. Nothing is equal to HimNone. Ḥāshā.

But people make shirk as their egos like and according to their understanding. Non-Muslims are all in shirk. Christians say there is not one, but a trinity. It means three. We know about them; they are mushrik from the start. But people are calling Ahlu s-Sunnah mushrik.

What is the real shirk? Shirk is to put AllāhNone, to put Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, in limits. Shirk is to associate HimNone with something. People who call Muslims mushrik are so themselves, according to their beliefs. They say Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla will sit on the Throne. When HeNone sits, there will remain a four-finger space. HeNone will let our Holy ProphetNone sit there. Is there a greater shirk than this? To embody HimNone, that is to imagine HimNone as an object, is shirk according to the belief of Ahlu s-Sunnah wa-l-Jamā‘ah, and according to the belief of our Holy ProphetNone. Otherwise, nothing of Ahlu s-Sunnah is shirk. What they call shirk is the waswasah (whispering) of their shayṭāns. It is the trick of shayṭān to mislead people.

Therefore, shirk is a sin. It is a great oppression against Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, the greatest oppression against Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. HeNone gave us everything. HeNone created us. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is giving everything. And when you say there is another one next to HimNone, it is shamelessness. It is a lack of adab. It is shamelessness, a lack of adab, and it is oppression. If you told someone who gave you everything that it was not him but someone else who gave it, he wouldn’t stand for it. Therefore, we have to be careful. There must be adab for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. The biggest adab and respect is for HimNone, and for our Holy ProphetNone. Do not pay attention to what others say, but follow this way. If you don’t follow this way, you will go astray. May AllāhNone protect us.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying, “Inna sh-shirka la-ẓulmun ‘aẓīm” (31:13). To be put partner for AllāhNone, to make “shirk” meaning to put partner for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.
