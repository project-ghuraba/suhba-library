Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

A’ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm:

(Qur’ān 22:38). Allah ﷻ states in the Holy Quran: ‘Truly, Allah defends those who believe.’ Allah ﷻdefends the Mu’mins (believers). He protects and preserves them, says Allah ﷻ in the Holy Quran. Because Mu’mins don’t think of anything but goodness for others, even though they sometimes make a mistake, Allah ﷻ protects them. Shaytan and his helpers are continuously attacking Mu’mins and want to get rid of their faith and their leader through various types of trickery. Sometimes directly as war and when it’s not war it’s through Fitnahs (dissensions/ discords). They’re acting as if they’re doing good to people yet they try to destroy Islam. However, they do not know, shaytan knows, but those who go after him do not know that nobody has any luck in being victorious against Allah ﷻ. Here is the Head of Muslims, Sheikh Nazim ق used to say. It was the Ottoman Empire; the whole world is looking here. That’s why the enemies of Islam are attacking here. They think: ‘If we finish this place then Islam will be finished.’ They don’t know that no matter what they do, if Allah ﷻ doesn’t will it they cannot [do it].

Allah ﷻ says: ‘ُ هرُ صُ نهي نمه ُ اللّ ن ره صُ نهيلهوه ’, ‘And Allah will surely help those who help Him’, (Qur’ān 22:40). Whoever helps Allah ﷻ, Allah ﷻ will help him. Victory is for Allah ﷻ. In the end, victory is for Islam. All the things that they do will be in vain and of no use. All their trickeries will be going to garbage. Muslims have put their trust in Allah ﷻ. We don’t know how to make agreements, we’re naive. And they mix in trickeries with the intention to harm Islam but insha’Allah it will not happen. They can claim to be as strong and powerful as they want. They can claim that they know all the knowledge and that they’re teaching it to others but essentially it all happens through Allah’s ﷻpermission. That permission is given and that’s how that door opens. As long as Allah ﷻ doesn’t want, nothing can move from its place.

Therefore, we need to be thankful to Allah ﷻ and pray that all harm may stay away from us, the harms that we don’t know, the evil that Mu’mins don’t know of. Without the believers’ knowledge, they’re trying to do so many things but Allah ﷻ is stopping it and getting rid of it through his beloved and chosen servants. He gives them power and without listening to them, Allah ﷻ willing evil is destroyed and stopped. May Allah ﷻ give us more strength and protect us. May He also send the Sahib (owner) as soon as possible so he may clean the whole world because there’s no other way for humanity to be saved. Anyway it is end times and the time is near insha’Allah. May Allah ﷻ make us reach Sahib uz-Zaman as soon as possible. May Allah ﷻ make us reach his beautiful days that are full of Barakah (blessings), when the whole world will be Muslim. It is the promise of Allah ﷻ and insha’Allah it will happen; it will definitely happen insha’Allah.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq. Al-Fatiha.
