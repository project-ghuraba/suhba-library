Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Our door is open to everyone. We continue upon the method of Shaykh Mawlana (QS). Whoever comes, welcome to them. Our door is open. Those who come come with their own ideas. Those who want to benefit will benefit, and those who do not, it is up to them.

Our way is clear. It is the way of Allah (JJ) and the way told, ordered, and shown by our Holy Prophet (SAW). People come with various reasons. They say about some: “They neither like you nor dislike you.” May Allah be pleased with those who like and do not like. It is not important, but there is an important thing everybody should know. We are not changing according to how everyone who comes or likes says or wants us to be. We are responsible for ourselves.

There is compulsion in religion. These days, everybody does as they like or as they want. Their doing contrary things does not bind us. Our way is clear. The way of Allah (JJ), the way of our Holy Prophet (SAW), and what the sharia says. But whoever comes, welcome to them. If they want to get their share, they know for themselves. Let them not say, “This one is coming and that one is coming.” It is good for him to come. Let them know the Truth and the right way. Our duty is to teach and explain this. There is no such thing as whatever anyone who comes and says will be absolutely done or that we will follow them. This is the way shown by Shaykh Mawlana (QS). Hadrat Shaykh Mawlana (QS) never showed anything contrary to Allah, the sharia, the Koran, our Holy Prophet’s (SAW) sunnats. We are continuing the way he showed Inshallah.

People’s intelligence and their approach vary widely. There is no such rule as it will definitly happen. The guest that comes is Allah’s guest. They come, see, and stay if they like. Yet again, thank Allah they do something with the himmat of Shaykh Mawlana (QS) and the way shown by him. As long as there is affection when they come, it would benefit them even if by an atom. This is the wisdom of this tariqa.

Hadrat Rumi also said, “Come.” Once they come there, Allah’s gaze and grace is upon those people. Nobody should critisize anyone who come or goes. Everybody should look at their own ego. The ego is the worst thing. This is true for all of us. There is no need for people who look at themselves to look at anything else. May Allah be pleased.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq.

Al-Fatiha.
