As-Salāmu ‘Alaykum wa RaḥmatuLlāhi wa Barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu BiLlāhi Minash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. BismiLlāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm.

Wa ṣ-Salātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Akhirīn.

Madad yā RasūlAllāh, Madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi RasūliLlāh, Madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

Dastūr Mawlana Sheikh Abdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistāni, Sheikh Muḥammad

Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad.Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-Suḥbah wa l-Khayru fi l-Jam‘iyyah.

“Al-ḥubbu fi Llāh wa l-bughdu fi Llāh.” [Hadith]. This means love for the sake of Allāh and also do not love for the sake of Allāh. You cannot love all your life. It cannot be. Sometimes you love something or somebody, and sometimes you don’t love.

But the Muslim must not hate the Muslim; only for the sake of Allāh. If he sees something not good, he must not agree with it. If you agree, you are not with Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. You must be with Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. What He loves, you must love. What He doesn’t love, you don’t love. What does Allāh love? He loves the good thing. What does He not love? The bad thing, the dirty thing, every bad thing. There are thousands or more. Before, it was 800. Now, what Allāh doesn’t like became more and more.

For this, our way is the way to ask for the love of Allāh. And the love of Allāh is like this: what He loves, you must love. What He hates, you must hate. We hate the bad thing, we hate shayṭān, we hate the oppressor, we hate what is making people suffer. This is what Allāh doesn’t like. He doesn’t hate what is good; never. Never is Allāh Azza wa Jalla like this. It is not from His attribute. Allāh Azza wa Jalla’s attribute is only the good thing. The bad attribute is for human beings, for shayṭān. We do not agree with this and we hate this. We don’t have hate the personality of a human being or other. No, we hate the bad attribute they have. This is what Allāh orders us. Some people say, “You are Muslim,” they hate everything. No. We hate only the bad thing. May Allāh show us the bad, and to hate it. And may He show us good, and to love it, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa min Allāhi t-Tawfīq. Al-Fātiḥa.
