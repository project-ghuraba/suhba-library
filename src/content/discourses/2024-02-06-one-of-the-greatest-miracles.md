As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam has thousands of miracles. Mu‘jizah (miracle) means an extraordinary thing. It’s not something a normal person can do. Miracles happen to prophets. The miracles of prophets are called mu‘jizah, but not those of others. Of course, the miracles that happened to the ṣaḥābah (companions), or the awliyā’ and the mashāyikh are called karāmāt. People sometimes confuse mu‘jizah and and karāmāt.

One of the greatest miracles of our Holy ProphetNone is Isrā’ wa-l-Mi‘rāj, going from Mecca to Jerusalem and ascending to heaven from there. No one has ever been granted this blessing before our Holy ProphetNone. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla granted this miracle, honouring himNone, as a gift to people. What our Holy ProphetNone did is a miracle, a great miracle. It is the gift of AllāhNone. It is not just for show. Great favors and blessings came with it. This miracle, this gift from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla for the Ummah continues until Qiyāmah.

People today are still the same, thinking according to their own minds. The believers among people of that time and people of this time are the same. And the faithless ones, the unbelievers are the same. They say, “We don’t believe; such a thing cannot happen.” Of course, the unbeliever can say that. But shayṭān makes people who claim they are Muslims fall into doubt and suspicion, saying, “This is wrong. No, it was a dream. No, I don’t know what it was.” And they lose its blessing and honour.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gave miracles to the prophets. And the awliyā’, among people, are the same. Just as people shouldn’t say, “How could that be?” about our Holy ProphetNone, awliyā’ also have ṭayyu l-makān (folding of space). He can be wherever he wants, whenever he wants. And this is the barakah of our Holy Prophet’sNone Isrā’ wa-l-Mi‘rāj. What was given to our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam was also given to hisNone Ummah. That’s why, through the knowledge given by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, everything happens. Nothing is impossible. Unbelievers work hard at it, saying, “We will go here. We will go there.” This has a limit. Everything has its limit. Whatever Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla wants happens, whenever HeNone wants. That miracle was given to all the prophets and awliyā’. It was even given to Sulaymān ‘alayhi s-salām, before our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Of course, he has miracles too. There were jinn with him. There were also scholars. He asked, “Who will bring the throne of Bilqīs here?” One of the jinn said, “I will bring it here before you rise from your place, your council.” The [ruling] council used to take them one to two hours. Before they brought it, one of the awliyā’, servants of AllāhNone, said, “I will bring it here to you within the twinkling of an eye.” And he instantly placed it right in front of him. So, this is how the knowledge given by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla occurs.

The one with the highest and greatest rank among the prophets is our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. HeNone was shown the entire universe: the world, the hereafter, Paradise, hell, and the seven levels of the heavens. Then, the believers were granted gifts, one of which we read every night: the last two verses of Sūratu l-Baqarah. They were directly given to himNone when heNone was in HisNone Presence. Its blessing was given to the Ummah.

Therefore, tonight is a beautiful night. It is an accepted night. Tomorrow is the twenty-seventh of Rajab. It is also a blessed day. You can spend the night in du‘ā’ and worship. And fast its day. You will receive the blessing and honour of this night. Some people – some knowingly and some unknowingly – say, “There is nothing such as this. There is such a ḥadīth. There is no such ḥadīth.” On this basis, there is evidence of the virtues of this night and this day from all the awliyā’ and the ḥadīth of our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam.

Whenever a person tries to do some small, good deed, a hundred shayṭāns will come against him. They might come with the attribute of a scholar. They might also come in the figure of a teacher, hodja: “Don’t do this. You are not doing a good deed. There is no such thing.” Whereas of course, our grandfathers and ancestors did it for hundreds of years. Now, when these stupid people say that there is no such thing, they deprive people of the blessings. They deprive them of the rewards. When they do so, a sin is written for them as well.

May AllāhNone make these nights blessed for us all. May their blessings be upon us. We believed in this. The people of ṭarīqah are the people of belief, īmān. There is no belief without ṭarīqah. Being a Muslim and having faith are not the same thing. In Islām, everyone is Muslim. Those who believe in the miracles of our Holy ProphetNone and what heNone said are believers. The others are Muslims. Therefore, may AllāhNone make us among the believers, in shā’a Llāh, and not lead us astray.

Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, heNone has thousands of miracles. Miracle – for in English there is only one word for miracle. In Arabic, there is two word – mu‘jizah and karāmah. Mu‘jizah: miracle for prophets. You cannot say for anybody else. But in English, it’s okay because it is no any other name for [it]. Miracle for awliyā’u Llāh, for ṣaḥābah or for other: we say karāmat, this. But for prophets, mu‘jizah. Miracle – every prophet, he has miracle. Thousands of miracles. But the last prophet, our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, heNone is the most near for people, and people, they see this miracle. And believer and not believer, also they see. But they have very few, what we know about them. But for Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, heNone, every step, ṣaḥābah, they was keeping in their mind, and then they write.

There is sūrah, Isrā’ wa-l-Mi‘rāj. And there is also one, Najm [sūrah 53], also for this night, describing this night. This night it is miracle night, which it is in time and in distance. Making everything very narrow time, narrow distance, you can feel.

But believer, they know this is nothing, compared for – No, you cannot compare at all. Astaghfiru Llāh, we cannot say compare, what AllāhNone want. But in this, all this happen in one night. And very, for our time, short time. This is the biggest, one of biggest miracle of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Only direct from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, for Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, gave gift for this night. And also praying and other thing. All this, present from AllāhNone for people. Who are practicing, they are getting the bless of AllāhNone from this night. And we are believer, mu’min, we said. We are believer. Who? They who believe. And believer, they are Ṣūfī people. Because this, who are not following ṭarīqah, most of them they said, “This is true, this is not true. No any... no need for worshipping. No need for fasting.
