Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīn wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa-l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla is the greatest. Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla, Allāhu Akbar. No doubt in the greatness of AllāhNone. Mankind stands against Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla with their tiny bodies. They are mindless. AllāhNone does what HeNone likes. As you see, mā shā’a Llāh it is raining heavily. If AllāhNone wants, HeNone can drown the whole world in rain. If HeNone wants, HeNone can make it not rain at all. "Fa-lā mumsika lahā wamā yumsik falā mursila lahu" (35:2). No one can handle what AllāhNone gives. And no one can give what HeNone doesn't give. No one can do it but AllāhNone.

Mankind says nature. Nature is the servant of AllāhNone. AllāhNone created it. HeNone can make it rain or stop whenever HeNone likes. HeNone can make it freeze or burn whenever Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla wants. Therefore, we should take lesson from everything. We should remember the greatness of AllāhNone and praise HimNone. We should say, "Subḥāna Llāha l-‘Aẓīm," and bear witness for the greatness of AllāhNone. Virtues and blessings will come at that moment from the Presence of AllāhNone. May AllāhNone give barakah in all our work, in shā’a Llāh. May HeNone give us goodness. May HisNone anger be away from us, in shā’a Llāh. We believe in HimNone. We believe in and know HisNone greatness. And we know HisNone mercy. May AllāhNone give all of us from HisNone mercy, in shā’a Llāh.

Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla, The Greatest. HeNone is Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla our Lord. HeNone is – do can do anything. Nobody can do such a thing like HimNone. HeNone can in five minute flood all this earth with water and even HeNone not can stop not giving one drop. Everything in our Lord Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla hand. We are believer this. The crazy people saying nature, nature it is also from AllāhNone creation and it is obeying AllāhNone. When AllāhNone say rain it rain, when say stop it is stop.

Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla saying in Qur’ān: "Fa-lā mumsika lahā wamā yumsik falā mursila lahu" (35:2). who AllāhNone send nobody can prevent and one HeNone don't want to send nobody can send. Whole, not whole world, whole universe if they are coming together try to do something against will of AllāhNone they cannot do. Everything in HisNone hand and we are believe in this, and we are accept HisNone greatness. And we are glory HimNone for this and when you do this saying "subḥāna Llāh, mā shā’a Llāh" this from our Lord, AllāhNone reward you also. And we are believe and we are asking to send us every good thing and to keep every bad thing away from us. Because all everything in HisNone hands, on HisNone will. When something will be, HeNone can. without HisNone will cannot be. We must believe this. This is believe not to listen to this foolish people who are, they are one very small. Even whole world it is nothing comparing to universe, and still they are challenging saying we are like this, we are doing this, this is from nature. This is from here, from there, from this thing coming, from here. When this wind coming from here it will be like this, when coming from other side it will be like this. All this AllāhNone will. Without HisNone will nothing, you cannot breathe even.

AllāhNone give us all best, in shā’a Llāh from HisNone glory and HisNone mercy, and to keep every bad thing away from us, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone give this, make barakah for this rain, in shā’a Llāh. Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
