As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

‘Īdu l-Fiṭr Mubārak. May it be full of barakah. This ‘Īd is a gift from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla to us, the Nation of Muḥammad. It is a spiritual feast, and precious because of this. With ifṭār on the last day of Ramaḍān, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla exonerates those who fast. HeNone gives confirmation to Muslims regarding the acceptance of their fasting and worship. To those who perform this beautiful worship and complete the month, it is a feast. This is the real feast. It is amnesty granted to you by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla for your ākhirah; it is a certificate. It is something that has the greatest benefit to people. ‘Īd becomes ‘Īd because of that. If there is no spirituality in dunyā, the feast has no use. It becomes nothing but badness.

People have made various holidays as they like. They made this and that. And they present them to people, saying, "It is our national holiday. It is our tradition. It is our thing." None of them have any value. Because they have no spiritual worth. Nothing specific is given to them by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Something special is given to ‘Īdu l-Fiṭr and ‘Īdu l-Aḍḥá. Certainly, ‘Īdu l-Aḍḥá has different favours and grants. ‘Īdu l-Fiṭr has different ones. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gives opportunities to people so that they benefit from these favours.

And to visit each other on these feasts, to share joy, brings rewards. When believers go to visit each other, a reward is given for every step, and a sin is forgiven. Thawāb (rewards) are written, and their ranks increase. Because of these occasions, even though some people don't worship, many people still visit each other. That brings benefit to people too. As we said, there is no benefit in holidays fabricated by people. Apart from benefit, there is a lot of harm.

Therefore, we must be on the way of AllāhNone. Who is on the way of AllāhNone wins. He is at peace, he has barakah, and he wins his ākhirah. Some people didn't pray and didn't go to Jum‘ah in old times as well. There were people who prayed only on ‘Īd before. I don't know about now. Probably, they don't do even that now. But may AllāhNone give guidance. That is beneficial for people as well. May AllāhNone give guidance to people. May they taste this beauty. May they have a share from it.

Believers wish people to have goodness. Believers don't envy others. Those who envy are not believers, mu’mins; they don't have faith in their hearts. People who don't want others to benefit, who don't wish goodness to them are not mu’mins. Their faith is not perfect. Therefore, we wish goodness to all humanity, in shā’a Llāh. We wish the goodness that Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla and our Holy ProphetNone wish, in shā’a Llāh. May our ‘Īd be good, in shā’a Llāh. May next ‘Īd be even better, in shā’a Llāh. May all people be in Islām, so that they benefit from these beautiful things.

‘Īd Mubārak, in shā’a Llāh, for you ‘Īd, Ramaḍān. Holy months. At the end we have holy, blessed day, feast – the feast Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gave for nation of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. And it is feast because AllāhNone gave for believer who fast whole month of Ramaḍān, and praying and making everything what AllāhNone want to do. HeNone is happy with them. HeNone said, "INone give certificate for these people who doing this from... INone am happy with them. INone, in shā’a Llāh, will put them in Paradise." This is certificate.

This is the important thing for feast, this spiritual side. With this spiritual side, becoming real feast. Because our life, it is today one day, today, tomorrow. After tomorrow, it will finish and we will forget. But Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone keep for us this happiness, and HeNone said, the two happiness we have – for fasting people: One, when he open his fast maghrib time. Second, when he in Paradise. So, we have first one here. Second one, AllāhNone keep it, HeNone keep this for us in Paradise, in shā’a Llāh. For this feast, it is, without spiritual, nothing.

Now they make many day, like to celebrate. We celebrate feast, Ramaḍān and Aḍḥá. Both of them have different spiritual thing. Only two we have, for believer: two feasts, two days for celebrate. For common, we pray and we call it ‘Īd. Of course there is another days for holy, blessed days but this, not celebrate like this, to go with children big and small, to go around and to enjoy. Other, with only for get bless, but these two for make people happy. Make them pleased to visit each other, to visit relative, to visit friend.

And it is big reward for this also. Because each step you go to visit some mu’min, Muslim, AllāhNone reward you and give you, forgive you from one sin and make you higher one degree, more level. Each step, this what Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla give. For this ‘Īd, it is very important for believer. And other days which it is for common people, the government or another group, they claim, "This is also, we must celebrate. This is good days." Every day, every month, they put one day. This is what they say – Ḥajjī Osman – "rubbish". Nothing at all. Only for to take money from people. And to make people to forget about spiritual. Only to focus on material, to look what they... what we can get for enjoying our ego. This is only... This is no benefit. Benefit, we celebrate what AllāhNone gave you, to be happy with this. Even you are happy with ‘Īd, AllāhNone reward you also. AllāhNone make us, whole time under HisNone order, what HeNone said. And AllāhNone keep us, give us barakah and many ‘Īd.

And we wish for, who are not celebrate this, AllāhNone give them hidāyah, to make celebrate also. Because we wish, mu’min, believer, they wish good for other people. They are not envy: "If I have, I don't want other to have." No. It's our way: "If I have, I wish good for other also to be, also to have this good thing." AllāhNone give hidāyah for whole world. And we wish to be, in next ‘Īd, all world celebrate ‘Īd, in shā’a Llāh. Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
