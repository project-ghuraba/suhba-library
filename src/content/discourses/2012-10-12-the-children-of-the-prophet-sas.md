Dastur Ya Sayidi, Alf wa alf Salat, alf wa alf Salam 'ala Habibi Sayidina Muhammad (saws) zid'hu Ya Rabbi Izzan wa Sharafan, Nuran wa Sururan, Ridwanan wa Sultanan. And also to his (saws) household.

Although I am weak, insha'Allah with your baraka I will get stronger. And I would like to speak to the Arab people especially. Audhu Billah min al shaytan al rajim, Bismillah Al Rahman Al Rahim. O Arabs! O Arab nations since you are from the nation of the Prophet (saws) I have wanted for a few days it was inspired to my heart & I wanted to speak a few words especially to the Arab people.

Audhu Billah min al shaytan al rajim. Naudhu Billah min al shaytan al rajim! Bismillah Al Rahman Al Rahim, Bismillah Al Rahman Al Rahim. Bismillah Al Rahman Al Rahim! O men of Allah (swt) support my tongue so I can speak a few words that are especially... especially for the Arab people. The Prophet (saws), Sayyidina Muhammad (saws)... O Arabs! O Arab Muslims, the Prophet (saws) said " Akrimu karima Qawm" honour the most noble people. This is an important matter for the Arab people. Karima Qawmin/The most noble people. O Arab Muslims! You understand what the Prophet (saws) says. "Honour the most noble people." (Hadith) Who are they now? Now who are the noble of the nation? The ones who are related to the Prophet (saws)? His children, and his grandchildren and those who are related to the Prophet (saws).

Wake up! The Prophet (saws) says "honour the most noble people." Who are they now? And forever they are the household of the Prophet (saws). It is an order to you O Arabs! "Honour the most noble people!" Who are they? Not the ones whom you are electing, this president & that one, no they have no value. Value & respect are for those who are descendents of the Holder of the absolute Message (saws), who is praised, honoured & glorified in the Divine Presence. To whom it was told... His Lord Allah (swt) told him, "if not for you, I would not have created creation! O My Beloved Ya Muhammad Ya Mustafa!" Our Lord (jwa) appreciates him (saws) with highest appreciation, greatest appreciation O Arabs!

"Honour the most noble people. "Who are they now? The family of the Prophet(saws), they are here, honour them, bestow honour on them. With what right, O Arabs do you run after those who have titles with no value? No! Not one of them is noble. And especially the noble people among Arabs, they are the family of the Prophet(saws) honour them, bestow honour on them. Allah (swt) will honour you. Honour your Prophet (saws) and his descendents & those related to the Prophet (saws), Ahl Al Bait. I want this answer from the Arab world - Who from the nation of the Prophet (saws) is suitable to be honoured and respected & to have homour bestowed on him? That's it! Descendents, children of the Prophet (saws). Their line is noble. These people, they are in the land...the land of Jordan.

They took, the Arab leaders from the Arab countries, all that are rich with minerals, especially petrol. They value petrol more than the children of the Prophet (saws). Hasha! No way! They were not supposed to leave the descendents of the Prophet (saws), the children of the Prophet (saws), in the middle of the desert. This is what they did and the Prophet (saws) says "honour the most noble people." Honour the most noble people, O Arabs! You left them in the middle in the desert, with no water or anything with them. Is this how you understand of the teaching of the Prophet (saws) "honour the most noble people"? Who is more honourable than the descendents of the Prophet (saws)?

Woe to you! Woe to you. All the fitna/corruption is from this because you left the banner of the Prophet (saws) and his (saws) children in the middle of the desert & then you are proud of this Prince & that King and this Imam. Where is that honour? "Verily, the most honourable of you to Allah, are the pious ones." (49:13) Is it not like this! Subhana Allah. You must honour him (saws) because the beginning is with him (saws) & the end of Religion is with them. It ends with them also. The first banner, the banner of the Prophet (saws), was raised and then they changed, and changed it, and now the banner of the Prophet (saws) must be raised so Allah (swt) may put in the hearts of the non-believers the fear of Islam, the glory of Islam. Masha'Allah!

Another Hadith, you understand it, I don't know… or have you forgotten Arabic like me? "Put people in their rightful place." This also is a Hadith. Why they do not honour the family of the Prophet (saws)? O Arab people, "honour the most noble people." They are the ones who are connected to the Noble Messenger (saws) who is to Allah (swt) at the greatest station, as an honour for him (saws). Until you understand and leave the falsehood that is of shaytans & jinn and the rest of the religions you will not be successful. You will not be successful otherwise. O Arabs this addressing is for you. "Put people in their right places." (Hadith) Honour them & Allah (jwa) Subhanahu wa Ta'ala will honour you. He (swt) Will honour you O Arabs! There is no victory except with them. With them you will be victorious. With them you will be proided for, with them you will be honoured.

It was inspired to my heart to speak to his Highness the Prince. His face is like the sun. Honour them, Allah (swt) honours you. They are His (swt) guests. The guest of Allah (swt) on earth. They are related to the Prophet (saws). O honourable King do not fear, you are on truth. If you move, the support of Al Haqq is with you. No fear for you & no sorrow.

The ones who honoured the family of the Prophet (saws) the most were the Ottomans. Ottoman Sultans in the times of feasts/Eid they would first greet the Naqib Al Ashraf/head of the family of the Prophet (saws). He used to come to the Sultan who would kiss his hand. This is honoring. The Sultan honored him and Allah (swt)gave him power & respect. Subhana Allah. The last Sultan was Sultan Abdul Hamid May Allah (swt) grant him a place in the Paradise of Firdaws with the Master of beginning and end (sas). He used to honor the Prophet (saws) & the land of Hijaz. The land of Hijaz honored him. O you Arabs you don't have this honoring & glorifying, and so you came into a very bad condition. And if you don't give to everyone their due rights - it is an order to give to everyone their due rights - O Arabs! O Arabs, give...give the family of the Prophet (saws) their due rights. Honor them, bestow honor on them, praise them and Allah (swt) will honor you. Otherwise you will eat one another and not only will you be unsuccessful, but you will never be successful!

And so, I apologize to the Sultan... Sultan Abdullah, they say King Abdullah, but it should be Sultan AbduAllah. He is a Sultan. And no matter where he places his foot, he will be victorious. Until the Arabs unite under the banner of Sultan King Abdullah, son of Hussein, son of Abdullah, son of Sharif Hussein. These words are important Masha Allah. Light is coming from their faces. Its a duty on me to tell the inspirations coming to my heart from our Masters (saws).

A strong one among the Awliya is Sayyidina AbdulQadir Al Jilani the one with powerful language. Nobody goes to him to say, "O Sultan, put an end to these problems." Nobody in Egypt goes to Sayyidina Al Hussein (as) and says "Save us from the problems." He (as) will advise you to go to the most noble of people, the ones you left in the middle of the desert in Jordan. Pity, pity on them. I think the days will turn around and their turn will come. And they are the servants of Mahdi (as). And Sultan, His Highness King Abdullah, has three banners. One banner for Prophet (asws), a second banner for Mahdi (as), and a third banner for him, as he is the King of the time. Let him walk forward without fear. We don't fear, not from Syrians, nor from Iraqis, nor from Turks, nor from the peopls of the West, nor China, nor Russians. We have power. Power... Finished! Masha Allah! The time has come.

I have declared what I was told. And now, you know best O Arabs, honor the most noble of people, those who are related to the Prophet (saws). Their lineage is honored. Honor them so Allah (swt) honors you. Their station is high. It is a duty to praise them, honour their station, insha'Allah. I would like to give Bayat to the King, and then Mahdi (as) - Insha Allah he is from the ministers, owner of the sword. No fear for them nor shall they be sad.

Fatiha.
