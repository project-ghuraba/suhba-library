As-salamu alaikum wa rahmatullahi wa barakatuh. Audhu billahi min ash-shaytani r-rajim. Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. As-salatu wa s-salamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidi l-awwalina wa l-akhirin. Madad ya RasulAllah, madad ya Sadati Ashabi RasulAllah, madad ya Mashaykhina, dastur, madad ya Sheikh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheikh Muhammad Nazim al-Haqqani, madad. Tariqatuna as-sohbah, wa l-khayru fi jam'iyyah. There are many sunnahs of our Prophet. And important sunnah is to fast for six days of Shawwal after the Eid of Ramadan. Our Prophet used to fast then. Most people don't know about this sunnah. However, when fasting these days it is as if you have fasted one year. Every day is accepted as 10 days. Ramadan's 30 days are 300 days. With six days like this, 360 days of hijri year. 360 days make up a hijri year. The other one is 365 days or more. They call saimu d-dahr (everlasting fast) those who fast then. It brings the reward of complete fasting with no breaks. Also you will have done the sunnah of our Prophet. There is time until the end of Shawwal.

Who wants can do like Sheikh Effendi beginning the second day of Eid. People get confused about Eid, they say you can't fast on Eid. You don't fast on the 1st day of Eid ul-Fitr. The other days you can fast. However, fasting is forbidden on the 4 days of Eid al-Adha. It's not acceptable. But our Prophet used to start from the 2nd day of this Eid. He would fast for 6 days and the 7th day was assumed a holiday. He called it Eid ul-Abrar. Who does it has followed the sunnah of our Prophet. Who can should do it. Who can't... Grandsheikh Abdullah Daghestani used to say who can't fast for 6 days should fast for 1 day, the 6th day - it's the 7th day of Shawwal. Fasting on the 7th of Shawwal can be for intention of 6 days. They will deserve its reward. At least they will have made sunnah with 1 day. Who can do it should do so.

Tomorrow it's possible. Tomorrow Thursday is the 7th day of Shawwal and the 6th day of six day-fasting. But who can't fast tomorrow too, if they fast 1 day before the end of the month, they will have made the sunnah of our Prophet. They will have its benefit. It can be done with the intention of making up missed fasting. The days ladies couldn't fast are like this. Therefore, there is easiness. Fasting in Shawwal and completing 6 days is good. If those who can't do it, fast for only a day, with the baraka of Grandsheikh Abdullah, its reward will go both to them and to Grandsheikh for his encouraging them to fast for even one day. He encouraged us to do so.

We are following the Prophet (saw), doing what he did. We like to follow. We cannot follow all. But there is one sunnah also from him to fast 6 days in Shawwal after Ramadan. It is every day 10 days Allah giving reward, For Ramadan 30 - 300, 6 - 360 days acceptable fasting. When somebody fasts like this, Allah make him like fasting whole year. He reward him like fasting whole year. And Prophet was encourage people to fast. And he fasting 6 days, but he's normally beginning 2nd day of Shawwal. Because first day only you cannot fast in Shawwal, on Eid. But on second Eid, Eid al-Adha the first 4 days not allowed to fast.

Only 5 days a year, you cannot fast, not allowed to fast. Others you can fast. For this Prophet (saw) he was beginning to fast from 2nd of Shawwal until 7th. And 8th he said it is Eid al-Abrar, second Eid. Second feast for the very obedient people for Allah. Abrar meaning they are very obedient for Allah. This we have second Eid also on Friday inshaAllah. You can fast until end of Shawwal 6 days. Together or one by one you can fast.

But the best to fast like Prophet (saw). But maybe some people they have work, something to do. They cannot fast all together. It's allowed until end of Shawwal. Even who cannot fast like this, Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Daghestani, he was making for people who are not fasting at all, he said to get baraka for them also, they can fast 7th of Shawwal. It Allah accept like they fast 6 days of Shawwal. And he encourages to make people fast. And Allah He said who makes somebody to make good thing, Allah reward him. So inshaAllah who cannot fast - one day even in Shawwal, Allah He reward him and reward Mawlana Sheikh also from this until Qiyama. Because he showing good way for people.

It is sunnah of Prophet (saw) but it is forgotten sunnah. Very few people they know about it. And it is also these days, 6 days if you don't have, some days you didn't fast, you can fast these days. For ladies also, they have some excuse. They fast six days in Shawwal, also acceptable. Allah make it easy for people to follow sunnah of Prophet, to not forget sunnah. Allah give us baraka of and mercy of Prophet (saw) to follow his sunnah and we loving him.

Wa min Allahi t-tawfiq al-Fatiha.
