As-Salāmu ‘Alaykum wa RaḥmatuLlāhi wa Barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu BiLlāhi Minash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. BismiLlāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm.

Wa ṣ-Salātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Akhirīn.

Madad yā RasūlAllāh, Madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi RasūliLlāh, Madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

Dastūr Mawlana Sheikh Abdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistāni, Sheikh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-

Ḥaqqānī. Madad.Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-Suḥbah wa l-Khayru fi l-Jam‘iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm:

(Qur’ān 06:88). ‘Dhālika Hudá Allāhi Yahdī BihiMan Yashā'u Min `Ibādihi ◌ۚ Wa Law 'Ashrakū Laĥabiţa `Anhum Mā Kānū Ya`malūn’, ‘That is the guidance of Allah by which He guides whomever He wills of His servants. But if they had associated others with Allah, then worthless for them would be whatever they were doing.’ Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘Aẓīm.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says, He grants guidance to whomever He wills. He destines guidance upon whomever He wants. This destiny is not granted to everyone. Whoever attains it wins a great, eternal gain. If others disbelieve in Allāh, if they associate partners with Allāh, all their deeds are worthless. No matter how much they possess in the whole world, all things, even if everything is in their hands, the worldly possessions are of no use. One will only pass into the Hereafter through īmān. Those without īmān will suffer their punishment.

Therefore, this matter of guidance is a grace and favor from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Shukr to Allāh, and those who are a means in guiding others will also receive this reward. Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim al-Haqqāni became a means in guiding so many people. All their descendants also attained that fortune through Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim, and the reward continues to go to him.

Shukr to Allāh, we are on his way. His way is the way of our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, the way of haqq (truth). It is a beautiful way followed without deviation. Because there are many people who try to corrupt this way, knowingly or unknowingly. But the true way, the pure way, is the Naqshbandi ṭarīqah on which Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim guided us, which continues exactly as it was transmitted from our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, shukr to Allāh. And it will continue forever. May Allāh bless it. May He keep those who come on this way steadfast and protect them from tests, in shā’a Llāh..

Wa min Allāhi t-Tawfīq. Al-Fātiḥa.
