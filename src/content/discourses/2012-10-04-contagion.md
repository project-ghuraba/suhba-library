As-Salamu Alaikum. The sohba of today, Mawlana never said or spoke of it. Why did he not say it? Because of his good manners, his mercy, his kindness he never gave such a sohbah. Because we don't have much of these, we need to say & the people here should know about it. And what is this? "A Muslim should love his Muslim brother as he loves himself" says the Hadith of the Prophet (sas).

So now if, Allah forbid, you are carrying a contagious disease, if you do unknowingly it is ok but if you knowingly go & make hijama saying "nothing happens" or if you go to dentist..Or there are some other diseases related to blood. So if you do this, you commit big sin. And if the other person dies because of this illness, Allah forbid, you will fall under a big responsibility. Therefore this, when a Muslim does something and it causes harm to himself, it is not a big deal. You are responsible for your own self. But if it causes harm to someone else, it is a big sin & responsibility.

This, we said for little extreme cases but normally when you get sick a little, even when you get flu, cold, be careful about the people around. We are not saying - don't go out - but when you are sneezing, coughing cover your mouth. Of course our community here gathered from every part of the world. All kinds of different things come from everywhere. And the most important thing here.. Somewhere else, even if all get sick, get flu or cold & rest for 3-5 days, it is not a big deal but the reason why we are saying this is that we should be careful for Mawlana.

For example last year, because of one thing we don't know from where it came, Mawlana went through a very serious illness. He suffered too much. He lived many khalwas, it felt like many khalwas to Mawlana. Therefore here... Mawlana would never say this. He says "It came from Allah". Really everything is from Allah but still we are saying this because the ones coming here should also be careful. Otherwise Mawlana never opens his mouth about the guests no matter what they do. I am saying this, that Mawlana would never make this sohbat. He never says "Don't get close when you are sick". He doesn't say anything. But for us, we are obliged to say this.

When Sayyidina Umar came to Quds (Jerusalem), when he came close to Sham it was just conquered. When he was about to enter they said there is plague in there, they call it taun. He couldn't decide whether he should enter or not. Then, when he didn't enter they said to him "Are you running away from the decree of Allah? He replied "Yes, I am running from the decree of Allah to the decree of Allah". Meaning, there is nothing like going and dying there. There is Hadith Sharif saying "if there is a disease in one place, people should not leave it, not spread it to other places". These things are sensitive points, it is also in Tariqa- it is the highest adab. If one may get infected, he can stay a bit far. So today the sohbat came like this. It is Allah's destiny. It could be that if S. Umar had gone there instead of being martyred, he could have died there from plague but Allah's decree was like this, without going there Allah made his destiny another way. So S. Umar stated it in a nice way.

Today we speak about one sohba Mawlana will never say this. Why? From his politeness, from his merciful, from his generosity. But I don't have this, so I can say. What is this? This is, Prophet (sas) saying "The believer must love his brother as his self, himself." So you want anything to be happen for you? No. But if you have something, you have something wrong, don't give this for your brothers. Especially these days we speak about many illnesses, many diseases. Some of them can go from one to other. Especially in making hijama or in making dentist or something with blood..There is many blood diseases. If it is coming one to other people, they finish. Even suffer whole life from illness, or even sometimes they can die. This is big responsibility. And it is big sin. You cannot imagine how it is. How you.. It is if you know this.. If you don't know, of course, of course you are not responsible. But if you know you have some disease like this & you don't tell, to coming to other people, it is big sin for you. And maybe if this man not forgive you, Allah not forgive. Because this is not Allah's right, the right of this man who you make ill. This is one.

Other one, small one like influenza, or like something like this. Coming from one to other, like here. When you have this, if you will cough you can put... You can be more careful not spread for people. Especially for here we are saying this. Because coming from everywhere from world. Some illness coming, you don't know, maybe taking too long. And what happened last year for Mawlana? Because he was too much mixing & it was autumn. It is the worst. So maybe from.. We don't know how it come but he suffered really very bad, many khalwat like this. Maybe more worse than suffering from khalwat what he suffered. And for us it is not important, for inluenza or for flu. Maybe you can sleep 5-6 days and you are strong & it can go. But this is for Mawlana, special for this we say. But from him also, for people they must be careful. If he has something like this, he can be more careful to not spread. Allah give reward also for this, because you do this for Allah to not harm people, Allah give you reward. Nothing going empty.

And one, they saying "how you are going.. you are not believing Allah, He give?" We believe but like Sayyidina Umar (ra) when he was coming to look for Damascus in Palestine it was conquered. And when he coming here Sahaba coming telling him "Don't go inside, there is plague. Don't go". And so he was coming back. Some of the people ashaming him, "What you saying? You are going away from Allah's order, destiny?" He said "I am going from one destiny to another destiny". Yes, and there is Hadith also. If there is something in some city, some disease, don't go out from this city or don't come in also. This is order also. So it is not shame to do this. But from first what I said, Mawlana never said this. Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, Al Fatiha.
