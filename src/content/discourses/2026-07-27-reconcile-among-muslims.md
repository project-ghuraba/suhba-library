As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
إِنَّمَا الْمُؤْمِنُونَ إِخْوَةٌ فَأَصْلِحُوا بَيْنَ أَخَوَيْكُمْ ۚ وَاتَّقُوا اللَّهَ لَعَلَّكُمْ تُرْحَمُونَ
<br>“Innamā l-mu'minūna ikhwatun fa-aṣliḥū bayna akhawaykum wa t-taqūllaha laʿallakum turḥamūn”<br>
“The believers are but brothers, so make reconciliation between your brothers, and fear Allah, that you may receive mercy.” (Qurʼān 49:10). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says, “Believers are brothers.” If there is disagreement among these brothers, reconcile them and make peace with each other so that Allah may treat you with His mercy.

Now, some people, even though they are Muslims, do not accept others. They say, “We are the Muslims, not the others.” However, everyone who says “La ilāha illa Allah Muḥammadun Rasulullah” is a Muslim. They may be sinners, they may be all sorts of things, but ultimately they are considered Muslims. It is necessary to reform them as well.

Whatever disagreements they have, they must be resolved. But these disagreements will, of course, only completely disappear when Mahdi ʿalayhi s-salām appears. There will be no problems left. By then, the Day of Judgment will be very near.

Therefore, whatever the situation, reconciling Muslims is a command from Allah; we must mend their relationships so that Allah’s mercy may be upon us. These people may be sinners or astray. But since they say “La ilāha illa Allah Muḥammadun Rasulullah”, they should be considered Muslims. May Allah protect us.

Now, some ignorant people think they are doing something. They say, “We have left the religion,” and they do certain things. May Allah protect us, they are actually losing a lot. They are gaining nothing. They are satisfying their egos. They are being crushed under their egos. They are becoming slaves of shayṭān. Instead of being servants of Allah, they are becoming slaves of shayṭān. One becomes a “Servant” to Allah, but a “slave” to shayṭān.

Therefore, one must be careful. As we said, a person should at least recite the Kalimat t-Tawḥīd. After accepting it, no matter how many sins they have committed, the rest is easy. Because that person will eventually attain Allah’s mercy. But if they have left the religion, then woe to them! May Allah protect us.

There are many ignorant people among youngsters. The more they study, the more ignorant they become. They think they are accomplishing something. They follow this philosopher, that thinker, and go astray. The more they study, the more they think they are something special. They accuse their parents and society of ignorance. However, they are the truly ignorant ones.

The person who does not know Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla is the truly ignorant one. That is the educated ignorant one. The more they study, the more ignorant they become, the more unknowledgeable they become, and the more they go astray. May Allah protect us.

وَزَيَّنَ لَهُمُ الشَّيْطَانُ أَعْمَالَهُمْ
<br>“Wa zayyana lahumu sh-shayṭānu aʿmālahum”<br>
“And shayṭān has made their deeds pleasing to them.” (Qur’ān 27:24).

The shayṭān makes things appear tempting (decorative) to them. He makes wrong things seem more appealing to them. However, it is all baseless. Therefore, after at least reciting the Shahāda, the rest, with Allah’s permission, falls under His forgiveness. Allah bestows all kinds of goodness upon their hearts.

Those who do not know Allah find no goodness. May Allah protect us. Their faces darken, their hearts darken. They become unloved creatures. May Allah guide the ignorant and lead those who have gone astray to the right way, inshā’Allāh. May they be guided.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
