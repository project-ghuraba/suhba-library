Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habe Rasulallah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Our Holy Prophet would teach good manners and say, “I came to discipline you, and teach you good habits and good morals.” Allah gives you the reward if you are well mannered. You will be of benefit to yourself and to others. In response, you get rewarded and are not left empty handed.

It is good to do kindness. The person who does goodness is a person who is liked. The person who continuously helps and does goodness is liked. The order is such too. Allah Azza wa Jalla states in the Ayah Kareema, “Help each other and do good.” To give charity is good. They told our Holy Prophet, “We do not have money to give charity.” So our Holy Prophet said, “It i also charity to remove rock from the road, and to remove trash away. The kind word is charity.” Meaning good works count as charity. Our Holy Prophet teaches so people do goodness.

Doing good is firstly beneficial for the person who does it. It benefits the person who does it. You wil harm yourself if you say, “I will be vigilant and avoid work.” Because you will get used to not helping at all and not doing any kindness. And if you do this, everybody grows cold towards you and is not pleased with you. They will call you vigilant. Those kinds of people are not liked wherever they go. The person who does goodness should continue doing so. It does not matter whether they know or not. Allah knows anyway. We have a proverb, “Do goodness and throw it into the sea. If fish do not know it, the Creator does.” Allah knows. Nothing is in vain if you do kindness. As we say though, a person gets used to everything by repetition. If little by little you try to do goodness, try to help, and try to do as much as you can, it becomes a habit in you and you will continuously do kindness to everybody. That kindness is also firstly beneficial for the person. On the other hand, if you think, “I will be vigilant, and avoid helping and working,” everybody will be cold towards you and hate you. At the end, you will suffer the loss yourself again. Nobody will like you in this world, and you cannot gain a benefit or a reward in the hereafter.

People are used to laziness, and used to their own comfort. There, this is selfishness. Nobody likes the selfish person either. We are trying to teach Islam’s orders and beautiful manners with Tariqa. We are working to teach people our Holy Prophet’s instructions. Inshallah, may everybody follow our Holy Prophet with good manners, and apply his orders. It is not hard. It is not difficult, but it feels hard when people are not used to it. May Allah make it easy Inshallah.

Al-Fatiha
