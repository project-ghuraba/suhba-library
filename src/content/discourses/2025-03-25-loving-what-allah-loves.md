As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna -l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam: “Al-ḥubbu fī Llāh wa-l-bughḍu fī Llāh.” To love for AllāhNone and to hate for AllāhNone are the things Muslims should do. Whom should they love? For AllāhNone, they must love everything for the pleasure of AllāhNone. If they love what Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla loves, they love it for AllāhNone. If there is something disliked by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, disliking it is to attain the pleasure of AllāhNone. Certainly, it is the worldly condition that mankind loves many things. But not all are acceptable because they love them.

Love is a good thing. But it is accepted when there is the pleasure of AllāhNone and it is the way AllāhNone wants. If it is the way AllāhNone doesn’t like, it is wrong. That is not love, but trouble, then. That is suffering, badness and even worse. Therefore, when people want to do something, they must look from all sides if it is good or not, if the person is good to be friends with, if he is worthy of love or not. It will be clear from that. If he takes you away from the way of AllāhNone, that means he should not be loved. That is the person disliked by AllāhNone And you must not love him, too. Why? Because he won’t bring anything to you except badness and troubles. If he is a good person and you love him, you’ll have goodness. He will help you, and you will help him. You will attain the pleasure of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Both you and him.

You must look at the person you want to marry. “I love this “herif” (guy) so much. I love this woman so much.” Okay. But what kind of people are they? Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim used to say, “If you call him “herif,” that means he’s not accepted. If you call him a man, that’s fine.” Saying “herif” means it is not good. Stay away from him. Even if your ego wants, stay away. How is this woman? She is not the one. You can’t even come close to her. She recognizes neither AllāhNone, nor ProphetNone. She has neither īmān, nor religion. And she has no family, nothing. Stay away from her. “I am dying from my love.” Die then. It is better. Rather than being with her, it is thousands times better to stay away from her. Even if you die...

Who dies from love, our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says, he becomes a martyr. Therefore, staying away from her for the pleasure of AllāhNone,and suffering, all kinds of suffering will be a rank and elevation for you. Therefore, the measurement is the pleasure of AllāhNone. You must look if one is a beloved servant of AllāhNone or not. Certainly, everyone is the servant of AllāhNone. They are servants of AllāhNone, but AllāhNone doesn’t love everyone. AllāhNone doesn’t love those who do badness, and kāfirs; HeNone doesn't love faithless ones. Looking at the people of this time, everyone is like this. May they come to the way of AllāhNone, and may we love them and welcome them. But no. They are enemies to AllāhNone, enemies to religion, enemies to people. Do we have to love them? No. Therefore, this is the measure. We must love people who love Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. We must stay away from those who don’t love HimNone. It is not the issue to love them or not. Just stay away. That’s it.

May AllāhNone protect us. May HeNone give guidance to people so that they are from the beloved people of AllāhNone, from people beloved by Muslims, in shā’a Llāh.

Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying: “Al-ḥubbu fī Llāh wa-l-bughḍu fī Llāh.” Love for AllāhNone, and hate for AllāhNone.But this is our way, way of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam: to be love what AllāhNone love him, or what AllāhNone HeNone love, we must love. And HeNone, when HeNone not love, HeNone hate, we also hate. We not love him. We are not obligatory to love everybody. Only we are, we love what Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla want, him, happy with him. This is scale for our religion life. Because when you love somebody and AllāhNone not love him, meaning this man or woman, she is not good. Because they out of way of AllāhNone and AllāhNone, [they] making harming people, teaching people bad thing, not respecting anybody, not respecting ProphetNone, not respecting believer, or family, or other. We are not obligatory to love them. We must be, not happy with them. Must be away for them. “This is my friend.” Okay, he is your friend, but he is not good one. Don't be with him. Or if you are, you have some, working same place, you can only look not to come very friendly, very close to him, until he become good one. This is order of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam for good believer.

And if also, you see some friend, how you can act for him? If he is way of AllāhNone, you must be, love him. And anything, anything you do, not only friend or other thing, what AllāhNone like, you must like. What HeNone is not like, you mustn't like. What Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla not like? Not like to be harm people, not like to harm yourself. How you harm yourself? You take some bad thing, drink, or eat, or make in your body some not good thing. All this, AllāhNone not like. What HeNone like? HeNone like good thing, to be eat and drink, and to look for after yourself, your body good. This is why it is not bad, it is good.

So, if anybody has good mind, he must love, like good thing, hate bad thing. This is simple, and AllāhNone order us very simple thing. For our nature HeNone order us to do this. Maybe also, for to look for friend, if you, for marriage or something, also maybe you love somebody, but this one he is not good. So you must be away from him, or she also not good. Also, you don't involve too much, don't follow your ego, running after this. “I love.” This love, it is not all time good thing. Some love, Arab saying, there is, “Wa-mina l-ḥubbi mā qatal.” There, from love, you can kill, some love. This is also killing, by real killing, and by destroy yourself, destroy your family, destroy people who love you, if you follow this. And at the end, you also be regret, when you see what it is not. But unfortunately, this love, very few people they are be safe from. If they are fall in love, they follow. Even the hundred people saying, thousand people saying, this not good, this is bad. And you see by yourself also, they are following this.

So Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, make also this test for people, because some love can, even somebody die from his love. Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, “Who die from love, his love, he is shahīd.” And maybe love somebody and he couldn't reach this love for them, and from his sadness, he die. Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, shahīd.

So you must keep yourself, if you, this love making problem for you, for your family, destroying around, and all people they are not happy with this. You, even you are real lover and people, it will be bad thing for people, you must sacrifice yourself in this love. And don't look, go away from there. AllāhNone help you, in shā’a Llāh.

This is, AllāhNone give us good thinking, for love what HeNone love. Make us to love what HeNone love, and to be away from what HeNone don't love, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
