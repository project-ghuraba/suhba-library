As-salāmu ‘alaykum wa raḥmatu Llāhi wa barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīn wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

May this holy 'Īd be blessed for you, in shā’a Llāh. Today is two feasts. Two feasts are ‘Īdu l-Aḍḥá and Jum‘ah - the feast of Muslims. Therefore, some say you can't make nikāḥ between two feasts. Its reason is today. You make the prayer of ‘Īdu l-Aḍḥá in the morning, then when you make Jum‘ah prayer the time between these two feasts becomes short. But people assume it to be between ‘Īdu l-Aḍḥá and ‘Īdu l-Fiṭr.

May your feast be blessed and beautiful. They call ‘Īdu l-Aḍḥá the feast of ḥajj. May AllāhNone bless it because all of them became ḥajjī. May AllāhNone give it to everyone. And who can, should slaughter qurbān. It is wājib. Muslims don't know anything now but they think they know everything. They hear something and tell each other everyone should do it. In Islām, in sharī‘ah, there are four madhāhib which go on the way of our ProphetNone - as Ahlu s-Sunnah wa l-Jamāʻah. There are Ḥanafī, Shāfi‘ī, Mālikī, Ḥanbalī maḍhhab. If you belong to any of these madhāhib, you should follow whoever is imām of our madhhab. Because there were 124,000 ṣaḥābah. Each of them was imām. But you can't follow all of them, your life won't be enough. Therefore, you should follow one madhhab. You should follow one imām. Whatever he says, you should do.

Sometimes if there is need, you can imitate the other three madhāhib. Only once for that particular time you can copy them and do as they are doing. But you can't make it up from your head that you shouldn't cut your hair, nails and beard during the 10 days before ‘Īd. Ḥanafī madhhab doesn't have it. Other madhāhib have it. Some have it. Some say it's up to you if you do it or not. But this year I see that everyone is talking about it. Then for qurbān it is wājib for Ḥanafī madhhab. Why wājib? Because other madhāhib don't have wājib. They have sunnah. Our Prophet, ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa sallam, said, "Three things are farḍ for me. These should be done by myNone ummah." - "Al-witr wa n-naḥar, sunnatu l-fajr." Praying witr was farḍ for our ProphetNone. For Ḥanafī madhhab it is wājib. For other madhāhib it is sunnah. And when it is sunnah, they don't give importance to sunnah. Whereas, there is sunnah mu’akkadah - this they should do. Same with witr. It is wājib. The sunnah of the morning prayer is sunnah mu’akkadah. You can make it up until noon. If you didn't wake up for fajr and got up after ishrāq, you have to pray those two sunnah raka‘āt. But you can't leave it to later in the afternoon. Before the noon. People can't do whatever they like. Sharī‘ah has its rules, its methods and ādāb. To be out of madhhab is not good. Who doesn't follow a madhhab does as he likes. He follows his ego. And his ego always confuses him, takes him out of the way. These are holy days. We should pay attention to them. As we said, when there is need, it can be imitated. But not whenever you feel like, 50 times a day. You don't know what to imitate anyway. To sit and learn those things, even a clever person needs at least five years, a person with high intelligence. And a person with middle intelligence needs at least 20 years to learn ḥalāl, ḥarām, sunnah, mustaḥabb. Therefore, keep the way you know as you've been taught.

Giving fatwá for something you don't know is not good. In old times, those who gave fatwá from their minds, who were happy that it worked, were punished. You give fatwá and you don't know, that's wrong. You should ask. Then you should say whether we have it or not. Most people, since they are in Islām since childhood, know their madhhab and what is what. There are some things they don't know. They shouldn't tell everyone without asking. This has its consequences. May AllāhNone protect us. Don't reap its consequences. These things we are saying look like goodness but they are means to destroy madhāhib. When madhāhib are broken, Islām is destroyed. That is the matter. It looks like goodness, but the biggest harm actually comes from them. Respect ‘ulamā’, imāms of madhāhib, imāms of ṭarīqah and ask support from them so that AllāhNone helps you, in shā’a Llāh. May the virtue of these days be upon us, in shā’a Llāh.

Today, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are thanking AllāhNone when we are saying to you ‘Īd mubārak. Today ‘Īdu l-Aḍḥá And it is today we have two ‘Īd, two feasts, one for ‘Īdu l-Aḍḥá, one for Friday. Friday it is day for feast for Muslim people. AllāhNone give this day Friday for our ProphetNone. It is the best day in week. AllāhNone give it for our ProphetNone. And, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are reaching this day. Today we make two ‘Īd, two feasts. And it is some people saying you cannot make wedding or you cannot marry between two ‘Īd. It is this what they say because too short time between praying of ‘Īd praying and the Jum‘ah praying. Too, but some people they make it as between ‘Īdu l-Fiṭr and ‘Īdu l-Aḍḥá, more than two months. No it is only this because no time, it is cannot be reach. It is not ḥarām or anything but it is no time for to do.

So, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are today make two ‘Īd and, in shā’a Llāh, AllāhNone give you happy life for this. And today, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, ḥajjī also, pilgrimage people, they finish. They become ḥajjī because yesterday they have been in ‘Arafāt and now they finish all this what they must do. And people now it is day we are seeing many people they what hear they hear they are like to do. But without knowing you cannot do because we are following madhhab. We have four madhhab they are acceptable now. All Muslim they accept these madhhab - Ḥanafī, Shāfi‘ī, Mālikī, Ḥanbalī. Four madhhab, each one they have little bit different thing because many of what they are saying, doing, ProphetNone was doing. So some madhhab take little bit from here, other they take this. Because you cannot, people they cannot do everything. It is difficult. So AllāhNone make it mercy for ummah, make them four choice, which one it is easy for people they can follow. Like African people they can follow Ḥanbalī because Ḥanbalī - in Ḥanafī you must wear until here, until under your knees. But in Ḥanbalī they can wear until here. This for men. For ladies also because in Africa there very hot they - it is open for them. It is given permission for them also. But in Ḥanafī they cannot do this.

So each one AllāhNone make mercy for people. Which one they like to follow, they can follow. But you cannot follow all of them to as much you like. If there is any necessary thing you can follow. Only short time for them. Maybe you have your blood - bleeding in Ḥanafī your wuḍū’ it will finish. But there, there is permission. And it's not stopping so you can imitate other madhhab. For this now before ‘Īd and ‘Īd they, we hear many thing from people and they are insisting saying, "You must do this because this is I hear this from Azhar, I hear this from... I read this in some place, must follow this." Yes what you hear it, is right, correct. But we have our madhhab it is not necessary, not impo... no any zaruret, no obligatory for us. Nothing so we must follow our madhhab. Not what you say everybody can say maybe you can say this for Shāfi‘ī, you can say this for Ḥanbalī or Mālikī they can follow. But also they are not obligatory to follow our madhhab. If not necessary.

So this is some people without knowing but other people with knowing to destroy madhhab. They this is movement maybe since seventy year or sixty year or fifty year. They are because it is strong wall against kufr - madhhab and ṭarīqah. So first they are was attacking ṭarīqah. Many people they are went out from ṭariqah, they not coming near ṭarīqah. After they are making this for sharī‘ah, for madhhab, so it will be destroy also this. After they will destroy Muslim. This is their real intention. So we are, we must follow first - madhhab. And what other madhhab doing they are ḥaqq, they are in right also. We are not criticising, saying anything against them. Four madhhab they can, each madhhab you follow continue this. If any necessary for short while you can follow. In madhhab, our madhhab Ḥanafī there is wājib. It is not strong like farḍ but it is stronger than sunnah.

Other three madhhab they don't have wājib, only they have sunnah. But there are sunnah mu’akkadah, sunnah ghayru mu’akkadah. And to cut sheep it is sunnah mu’akkadah for them. But for Ḥanafī - wājib, because it is for ProphetNone heNone said, "There is three thing it is farḍ for me." Really obligatory for ProphetNone and it is wājib for Ḥanafī and for other madhhab - sunnah mu’akkadah - they must do. One witr, this is after ‘Ishā’ we pray. And naḥar, cutting sheep in ‘Īdu l-Aḍḥá. And to pray sunnatu l-fajr, for Ḥanafī also mu’akkadah, sunnah mu’akkadah not wājib. But this is three thing it was for ProphetNone farḍ, obligatory. Even when somebody not waking up for fajr in time - after sunrise also he must pray two raka‘āt sunnah fajr. Until Ẓuhr, after Ẓuhr no need.
