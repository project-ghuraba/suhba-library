As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

The Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam is saying: "Al-arwāḥu junūdun mujannadatun famā ta‘ārafa minhā i’talaf, wa-mā tanākara minhā ikhtalaf." Ṣadaqa Rasūla Llāh fī-mā qāl aw kamā qāl, (the Messenger of AllāhNone has said the truth in what he said, or as he said). Our souls are like soldiers. When Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla created us in preeternity on the day of Qālū Balá, there were souls. We were not there. We were not there physically. In the beginning, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla created souls. Some of the souls there liked each other and got along very well. It is the same when they come to this world. In this world, you like some people and dislike other people. Some people love each other, some of them hate each other. This is their condition.

By the wisdom of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, when souls were created, a promise was taken from all of them regarding who would be a willing servant of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Everyone recognized Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla and said they accepted HimNone. But when they came to this world, they said they didn't know HimNone. They broke their promise. There cannot be familiarity with people who don't keep their promise. There cannot be admiration for them. Therefore, this beautiful word of our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says sometimes someone doesn't get along with another person. He doesn't know why he doesn't get along well, and says it doesn't come from his heart. This means they met in the spiritual world and didn't like each other, or hated each other then. When they are physically in this world, such souls cannot get along well, either. They cannot stand each other. They become enemies. It is the same for all people in this world. Both Muslims and non-Muslims have such situations.

Therefore, may AllāhNone make us love good people, in shā’a Llāh, because it is a great favour. It is the wisdom of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. We cannot interfere in that wisdom. Asking why and how is not from adab. It is neither from adab, nor from any protocol, to ask this question. Some people think they can ask everything. Some things pertain to Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla and things HeNone knows Himself. They say HisNone wisdom can't be questioned. It is a beautiful saying. This is our condition. It is against adab to question one's condition. And it weakens people's īmān. May AllāhNone protect us, they may lose their īmān. In shā’a Llāh, be with good people. Good people are beloved servants of AllāhNone. Protect them and be together with them. Don't be against them. There can be familiarity with people on the way of our Holy ProphetNone, and the way of AllāhNone. And we ask that AllāhNone gives guidance to the rest, in shā’a Llāh.

Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam was saying for this ḥadīth, for our soul, AllāhNone... "Al-arwāḥu junūdun mujannadatun famā ta‘ārafa minhā i’talaf, wa-mā tanākara minhā ikhtalaf." Aw kamā qāl. Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, ṣadaqa Rasūla Llāh ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, heNone saying this ḥadīth for our souls. In time what Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone create whole soul, in, thousands years ago. Before creating our body, HeNone create our soul. And this soul also... Many people, they are saying about soul, how it is, what it is, Like this, like that. This only we know for soul. AllāhNone, HeNone create them before, some, maybe, fifty thousand year ago or more. Fifty thousand year of our time or Allāh'sNone time, we don't know. But they create our soul long time ago. But for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, no time, no place. So, when... For us maybe billions years; for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, nothing at all. HeNone is same, nothing change, nothing can be changed.

This is the greatness of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. When HeNone create our soul – and HeNone create. HeNone is Creator. For this also, we saying for this narrow mind people, what they are thinking, soul is, maybe one billion soul you have. So every soul, it must be go from place to place. From man to mouse, mice, from mice to cat, from cat to cow, from cow to fish. They are, this, very narrow mind. They not knowing the greatness of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. AllāhNone create billions of souls for human beings. And HeNone is still creating for other thing also. But for our subject, HeNone create this. And this soul in that time. And this also, we say, prohibited, for soul, to ask for, "How is soul, how is..." Is only for AllāhNone, know this. But what we know, as we said: HeNone create them. And HeNone take a promise from them. "Am INone your Lord?" They say, "Yes, we, YouNone are our LordNone. YouNone are our CreatorNone. YouNone are everything." But when they come to body, their body they change them to not be loyal for their promise. They deny this.

This is not [the] problem. We are, our subject what Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying for ḥadīth. HeNone said, when this soul, that time also, they was seeing each other, soul. Some of them, they are happy with each other. They friend with, together. Some of them, they are enemy. Some of them, they are not so happy from people. Some of them, running from people. It was like this also in soul... place where they was. And when this soul coming to dunyā, to earth, with body, coming to, with, from baby or from mother womb. So after this, when coming to life, some people, they are not like other people. Some people, they are happy with other people. Some people running from people. Some people, different. All this was, in that time, before thousands of year, also the soul was running away from some people. Some of them running together with, to people. Some of them happy with people.

So this, what, some little bit secret from what happen in this earth for human being. Some people, they hate other people. There are people, hate other people. This is wisdom of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Just, we are hoping to be happy with good people, with people who AllāhNone love them, beloved one for AllāhNone. AllāhNone, we hope to be happy with them. Because many people they cannot be in such a place. They cannot be such... Very dirty place, they are happy, very happy there. But when they are coming to nice place, good place, they will be not happy. As Mawlānā Shaykh, many times he was saying about rat in sewage. He is happy, very happy there. Where, he going up and jumping on, swimming in sewage. He is very happy there. But when you put them outside, not happy. Nothing for him. For this we are, we hope AllāhNone make us to be whole time, with good people, happy with them, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
