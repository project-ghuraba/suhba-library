As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says: Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Rabbanā wa-lā tuḥammilnā mā lā ṭāqata lanā bih" (2:286). "Do not give us a burden that we cannot carry, that is beyond our ability to bear." These two āyāt from Sūratu l-Baqarah were gifted to our Holy ProphetNone directly by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla on Laylatu l-Mi‘rāj. Not through Jibrīl ‘alayhi s-salām or by revelation, but by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Someone who recites them every night will attain great favours.

It is said in them, "Don't make us responsible for things we are not capable of. Do not give us situations we cannot handle. Do not even let them come close to us." That is a favour from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Certainly, people have troubles in this world. But everyone's troubles are different. Different people go through different hardships. Even in comfort, some people say they are in trouble. Why? Ḥāshā. Some people even say, "Why did AllāhNone create me?" even though they have everything, Some people have disasters coming on them like rain. Yet, they are patient. They trust in Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Their faith in AllāhNone is strong. And others must surely learn about faith. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla didn't create this life and create you by asking you. HeNone didn't ask, "How do you want it? How shall INone create you? Do you want to be created or not?" Some people have logic. But there are many people who don't have understanding or logic.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla created us, for once. How dare you ask why HeNone created you? If it is up to you, if you don't understand, you may ask as much as you want. There is no benefit in it. May AllāhNone protect us, there is only harm. AllāhNone created everyone. HeNone created in order to test us. And HeNone created by HisNone wisdom. For once, you have come here. It is a great favor for you. If you don't realize this favour, you deserve a great punishment. Therefore, go on.

May AllāhNone protect us, there are all different kinds of afflictions that people have. There are diseases, the rebellion of children, diseases of the children, and many other problems of children. There are many afflictions in this world. Therefore, we must always make du‘ā’ that AllāhNone doesn't give us a burden we cannot carry. "We are weak servants. As we are weak servants, don't burden us. Treat us with YourNone Generosity and Mercy. Don't give us any tests at all. Grant us. We don't ask for tests. We ask for grants. YouNone are generous. We are YourNone poor servants. Give us from YourNone generosity, O AllāhNone." And may HeNone give us the biggest thing, the strength of īmān. When HeNone gives the strength of īmān, people can carry it. Certainly, not everyone can carry...

Therefore, we make du‘ā’ asking that HeNone grants us without giving any tests. May AllāhNone give us from HisNone favours, not from HisNone tests, in shā’a Llāh. May AllāhNone help those who are tested. May their tests be lifted and be easy. May AllāhNone give patience to them, in shā’a Llāh.

In Qur’ān ‘aẓīmu sh-sha’n Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying, "Rabbanā wa-lā tuḥammilnā mā lā ṭāqata lanā bih" (2:286). "O our LordNone, don't put something we cannot carry on us. We are weak. We cannot carry." And this āyah, two āyah from end of Sūratu l-Baqarah. There was Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla one of gift for Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam when heNone was in mi‘rāj. HeNone directly giving to Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Because whole Qur’ān sent by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla for Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam through Jibrīl ‘alayhi s-salām, Jibrā’īl ‘alayhi s-salām. So but this two āyah, as when for Sulṭān, going some people [to visit him] must take them present, AllāhNone give many presents for Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, endless. But the famous one also this āyah, two āyah and of course five times praying also from this [time]. So it is very precious and very... very much, very important thing inside.

One of them – to not put something we cannot carry. As nation before, they was cannot carry and AllāhNone curse them. So for this AllāhNone make this, to read this every day, every night before sleeping, make you safe from this badness, in shā’a Llāh. But it is important here for us. We are living in dunyā, in this world many thing happened for everyone. It is not easy for some people. Some people they have big test, big thing they are carrying. Other people they cannot carry. But we are... AllāhNone He know better for everything. Because you see some people, they have many problem, many thing difficult in life and they are patient. And also we see some people they have everything and they don't be happy or even not happy to be... Don't say, "I am not happy." Just complaining.

Once, many time, I hear also some people, "Why AllāhNone create me?" This question they have I think bad – meaning as they are angry from AllāhNone. Why HeNone create you? AllāhNone, subḥāna Llāh, this people they who has no mind, they have no good thinking. AllāhNone He will ask you, "What, O my servant, how you like to be? You like to be exist or not exist?" As much this crazy people to ask this. AllāhNone create you by HisNone wisdom and you cannot say, ask why or not. Just you must know you create[d] by the LordNone, and you must be obey and be in good behave. For, especially for AllāhNone, "Addabanī Rabbī fa-aḥsana ta’dībī", you be in good adab. If you are saying this for normal man, HeNone will be angry with you. How you are saying for Lord, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, your Creator?

So this people they have everything but they are, I don't know why, still not satisfied, not happy, not putting their head [bowing] in front of the Divinely Presence of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. This people they have worse thing for them. For this we are asking Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, "We are knowing, YouNone are our Creator and we are happy for everything YouNone gave us. But also we ask to not put too much for us from weight, heavy weight on us. We are weak, YouNone are generous. Only little bit it's enough to not be complain for anything." AllāhNone make us to be obedient, good behave servant for HimNone, in shā’a Llāh. This is what we are, we like to do, to say.

And we see many people also they are carrying something you cannot carry – not one day. Maybe one hour you cannot carry this. All they are carrying this whole life or many years. AllāhNone give everyone as power to carry but we don't ask for this. Don't ask for ṣabr. Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam to be patient. For patient, AllāhNone give him more test for to be tested for this patience. For this we are asking AllāhNone to keep us safe from everything we are not, we cannot carry. Especially for children, for young or for old, for all of them, we are living time. Every time you hear something, you become real sad for this, especially the new generation. AllāhNone keep them and keep us safe from this bad thing to, to do this. AllāhNone help us to be away from this thing, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
