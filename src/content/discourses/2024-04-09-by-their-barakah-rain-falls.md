As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says, “Bihim tumṭarūn bihim turzaqūn bihim tunṣarūn.” These Abdāl (forty saints) are among the greatest of the great awliyā’. HeNone says that through them, AllāhNone gives you rizq, rain and victory. Shukr to AllāhNone, it means that it happened today by their pleasure. It’s been a while; there hasn’t been much rain here this year. There was a strange drought. But Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, with the barakah of Ramaḍān and the du‘ā’s of the believers here, this rain has occurred as an acceptance of our Ramaḍān, with the permission of AllāhNone. All the worship and good deeds were accepted by AllāhNone. AllāhNone will give us their reward both in this world and the hereafter. Just as HeNone gives this beauty in dunyā now, HeNone will certainly give more in ākhirah. What’s given here is nothing compared to ākhirah. The things given in dunyā are not even as much as dust compared to ākhirah. Ākhirah is what is important. This rain in this time is good for the body and good for spirituality. For a spiritual person who does what Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says and commands, it also has great benefit for his spirituality. If someone doesn’t know HimNone, he knows for himself. Whoever wants – “Fa-man shā’a fa-l-yu’min wa-man shā’a fa-l-yakfur” (18:29), says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Ṣadaqa Llāhu l- ‘aẓīm. “Whoever wants, let him believe; and whoever wants, let him not.” Whoever wants can be a believer, whoever wants can be an unbeliever, whoever wants can be an atheist, whoever wants – whatever they want, they know for themselves.

Therefore, some people ask: “Why these favours are also given to unbelievers?” Because it has no value. This world has no value. Not only does it not have the value of a drop of water, but the whole world doesn’t have the value even of a mosquito. What is the value of a mosquito’s wing? Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla created the mosquito as a test for people. Even killing it has a reward. Because it is a pest that causes harm. But the point here is that this world has no value. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla does not give anything valuable to the non-believer. The believer benefits in both. The believer benefits. The non-believer does not benefit himself. He wants to harm himself with his own hands. May AllāhNone bless this month of Ramaḍān. AllāhNone accepted it, in shā’a Llāh. May it be blessed upon us all. May you have many happy years, in shā’a Llāh. May the next year be more beautiful. How can it be more beautiful? With Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām. This world becomes more beautiful when the whole world fasts. This world becomes beautiful when the whole world worships. In shā’a Llāh, we are hoping. The promise of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is true. HisNone word is true. It will happen, in shā’a Llāh. We hope and make du‘ā’ that it will be soon, in shā’a Llāh. May AllāhNone bless you. Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying for awliyā’u Llāh, when [whom] they called Abdāl, “Bihim tumṭarūn bihim turzaqūn bihim tunṣarūn.” By their barakah you get rain. By AllāhNone for...because AllāhNone bless them. Through them you get rizq, provision. And with them you get victory, this awliyā’u Llāh. So, this is saying of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh we are thanking AllāhNone. HeNone gave this rain for our island, because since nearly two months, even it is winter, it was no rain. It was very dry. And now this is the last day of Ramaḍān. AllāhNone, this sign, AllāhNone accept our Ramaḍān, al-ḥamdu liLlāh. This awliyā’u Llāh they are happy that through them they give permission for rain and it was rain, good rain, al-ḥamdu liLlāh. Watering every mountain, valley, garden, field, all full of rain, al-ḥamdu liLlāh. This is, and this rain in this time of year, it is blessed rain. It is good for health, for spiritual and for normal physical body also. For each drop giving life. For whom? For whole, everybody in this world. People sometimes saying, even non-believer, because it's no value for earth or for this in Divine Presence of AllāhNone. For this, HeNone gave everybody. Not cut anything.

Because whole world it is not worth one wing of mosquito. Mosquito: what is this? What value for mosquito wings? Nothing. Even mosquito, if you kill mosquito, you can get reward because it is harm, harmful insect. When AllāhNone make it only for some reason, some people to get ill, some people to get not happy. Also, this, there is, but to be, me, giving this examp[le] – AllāhNone give example, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, to see, show people how it is, the whole world. Not value anything, in Divinely Presence. For this, HeNone give everybody in this world, their food, their water, their everything, richness, giving the land, children or anything. It is no problem. But here there is two thing. AllāhNone give all this. Some people they get benefit from this, some people not getting. People who get benefit, who are believer and they are happy with AllāhNone, they know from where coming this. Who not get benefit, other kind of people, who not accept Creator, not accept anything from religion or other. This people they will be, they are free. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying, "Fa-man shā’a fa-l-yu’min wa-man shā’a fa-l-yakfur" (18:29). This is, they say, democracy. AllāhNone said anybody can be believer, anybody can be not believer also. You are free. But there is two option only: one fire, one Paradise. So, any one you choose, you will go there. AllāhNone keep us in HisNone way.

We are happy with this good tiding today, what AllāhNone gave us. We are, wanting to be many years like this and we like for next year to be better than this. How to be better than this? To be with Sayyidunā Mahdī (as), to be whole world fasting, praying tarāwīḥ, praying all this and doing good thing. This is our wishes which AllāhNone, in shā’a Llāh, reward us for this and to make it happen, in shā’a Llāh, very soon. Because AllāhNone promise this and it will be, in shā’a Llāh. We hope very soon it will be, in shā’a Llāh.
