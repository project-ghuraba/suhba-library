As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh. A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Wa-‘lamū anna fīkum Rasūla Llāh” (49:7). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘Aẓīm. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says, “The Prophet is among you.” He is among the ummah. Our Holy Prophet, his ummah cannot be without him. He is always with us. Shukr to Allāh, these blessed days are his blessed days. He is forever with us, together with Muslims, with believers, with those who love him. Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam is with them. He himself says, “Al-mar’u ma‘a man aḥabb.” A person is with whom he loves. So many millions of people love our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. They always remember him. When they remember him, he is present. We are always in his presence, shukr to Allāh. People of ṭarīqah give more respect to him and believe in him. Rābiṭah – this rābiṭah of ours is from the principles of ṭarīqah. Many people ask what is rābiṭah. Rābiṭah is to connect your heart to your shaykh. And the shaykh connects it to our Holy Prophet. How do we do it? We have some methods. Some are difficult, some are easier. Ours is the easiest. You should ask these. And through him, ask for the support of our Holy Prophet. This is rābiṭah. However it is done in other ṭarīqahs, you are in this ṭarīqah. Ḥaqqānī, the Mujaddidī of our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam on the Khālidī path – this is the path you are on. This path is the easy path. It is established upon ease for people. There is ‘azīmah (strictness) and there is rukhṣah (permission). As it is now the end of times, ours is with rukhṣah. Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim always said, “We are doing rukhṣah, because if we applied ‘azīmah, no one could take a step into ṭarīqah.” Therefore, in our ṭarīqah, shukr to Allāh, with this ease we make rābiṭah, connecting to our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. That is rābiṭah. The shaykh is the means. Through him, rābiṭah is to reach our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Many people keep asking about rābiṭah, because everyone says something different, how should you do it, how should you practise it. But ours does not even take a minute. Remember your shaykh, connect to him, and ask for his support. That is enough. Then rābiṭah is complete. After that, it is in their hands, not in ours. You may strive as much as you want, but unless they open it, nothing happens. When they open it, they see your state. Based on your intention and sincerity, Allāh will accept it then. May thanks be to Allāh who gave this path. Of course, “Wa-qalīlun min ‘ibādiya sh-shakūr” (34:13). Few are grateful. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says few have faith. Once you enter this, you must give thanks to Allāh. On this blessed day today, on this blessed beautiful day of our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, there has been this explanation about our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Today already, all over the world, various celebrations and gatherings take place for the birthday of our Holy Prophet. In earlier times, people showed more care. They used to celebrate more, with more joy. People nowadays have become like machines. Nothing matters to them. They are unaware of anything. Nothing but the phone or the computer in their hands; they look at that and nothing else. However, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla did not create you to look at that. Allāh created you to be on His path, to be with Allāh, to be from the ummah of our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. And today is that day. It is the blessed day of our Holy Prophet. There are shayṭāns who say it is wrong to celebrate this day, and there are people deceived by shayṭāns. About them, the Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n makes a comparison. It says “merkep.” Old Cypriot people used to say “merkep.” Merkep means donkey. It is written in the Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n. A donkey carries books, valuable books, on its back, but it knows nothing of what is inside. Such are those who do not honour our Holy Prophet, who do not know the worth of our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Because our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam used to fast every Monday. They asked what was the wisdom of it. He said, “I was born on this day.” Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam was born on a Monday. He remembered it every Monday, and reminded his ummah as well. So why should it be bad to celebrate the day of his birth? Why should it not be allowed? That should be asked to those four-legged donkeys. May Allāh give understanding, because they also drag others into misguidance. Everyone thinks they are something and follows them. These ones say, “This one said it, he understands better than us,” and so they stop doing it. But the one with sense, the one who has intellect, turns back to Ḥaqq. May Allāh, for the honour of this blessed day, make them among those who turn to Ḥaqq. May they know its value. May we know its value, in shā’a Llāh. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, saying: Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Wa-‘lamū anna fīkum Rasūla Llāh” (49:7). Meaning Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, he is with you, in you, every time. Not only that time. Every time, because Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n, meaning, continuously. So, we are believe for Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, he is with his ummah, with his nation. He is with us. This is our belief, belief of Ahlu s-Sunnah wa-l-Jamā‘ah and belief of mu’min, believer. Maybe, Muslim, there is many Muslims they not believe this. But believer, mu’min, they believe this. And, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, ṭarīqah, the main thing for ṭarīqah: to make rābiṭah. Rābiṭah, what meaning? Many people they asking for rābiṭah. Rābiṭah mean to connection with Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. So, it is from ṭarīqah order, to make rābiṭah. And rābiṭah, you connect with your shaykh, and from your shaykh, in heart, to Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, he connect you. This is rābiṭah.
