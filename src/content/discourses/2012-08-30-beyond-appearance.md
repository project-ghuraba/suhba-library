As-Salamu Alaikum Dastur. Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fil jamia'. "Wa Ma Al-Hayaatu Ad-Dunya 'Illa Mata`u Al-Ghururi" (3:185) "The life of dunya is like a temporary enjoyment, playing." says Allah (awj) in His Holy Verse. And what is this indicating? It means that what is important is in the akhirah, don't give too much importance to dunya. Earn your money in a halal way& eat & drink from halal. Don't waste. Don't put yourself in hardship.

The blessings have become plentiful & and with plenty, waste has increased. In the Holy Verse it says, if you waste too much later you see that you have nothing left. And you regret, "I wish I had not wasted this much." And what are people spending on today? Spending on unnecessary things. They eat & drink.. this is not the thing-it is the rizq of the family. When he eats with Basmala, it becomes Nur.

It is mostly clothing & household items. It is unnecessary, meaning if it is good enough to wear it is ok but they say: it is out of fashion, we should change it. This one is not a brand name.. And now there are brand names also, everybody has to wear.. There are certain brand names and they are all big companies- Now all people work for spending on European companies. Their clothes have a small logo somewhere, if it doesn't have that.. Meaning more than 60% of the people don't wear it if it doesn't have a logo. They want to wear something which should definitely have a name, a logo.

These are useless things. And people are also deceived by it- they are deceived by one's clothes & they value him accordingly. Who value people this way have no mind anyways: "What this one is wearing, that one wearing" It has nothing to do with this. There are some people who don't care too much about their outer appearance. He may be a doctor, an engineer, or a professor & when you look at him he is not very stylish to draw your attention but if you were to talk to him, no one can stand on his way!

This thing existed even in old times. In old times also they would value people according to the their clothes, their styles. Of course people were poor at that time, there was poverty. And one day there was a meeting of scholars. The most powerful one of that area, the Qadi/Chief Judge he gathered all the scholars. They wanted to discuss a matter. He also gathered the common people, scholars being seated in higher seats. And on the floor the common people sat & listened to take benefit from it. There was a good scholar but as we said poverty was more common at that time. He sat in the row of scholars with his old jubba, with patches all over it. They were not on chairs but on, like high cushions. The servant of the house saw that this man is sitting on these cushions among the scholars. He looked, looked & came to scold him "How can you come & sit here with all these scholars with your ragged clothes? Do you have no shame? How dare you sit there? Quickly come &sit downstairs!" There is no discussion with ignorant people by scholars. So immediately he went down & sat on the floor.

And the scholars came & started speaking- from fiqh, from here there.. Then they came to a matter that they had great difficulty solving. There were almost about to fight, to yell, to shout.. When it came to this, the host of the meeting- the Grand Qadi asked is there anyone who knows about this? He said, "if you allow me, I can speak." He explained it in a very beautiful way that no other one could think of. They all got surprised. They said: "How did we make you sit like this on the floor?", they kept apologising.

Like this, it is not with the clothes one has on but the jewel Allah granted him is inside. You buy the dress yourself & adorn yourself with it. But Allah granted the adornment inside man-knowledge, softness, taqwa, understanding... This beauty is what Allah grants, no one else can give. Meaning, adorn yourself as much as you can, it is of no use. You can't learn a line more. Even if you put 10 turbans on your head, still doesn't do anything. There is a lot to say on this matter.

GrandShaykh would also speak about it. There was a man who was wearing a very beautiful jubba & turban. He came to the gathering & sat by the side. Sh. Sharafuddin Hz said..He was sitting quietly. When he is quiet, no problem but when he speaksit is no good. And some people also speak without facts & disgrace themselves. He was sitting nicely in one side. Now of course when there is a scholar in the gathering mashayikh want to size him up. He waited, waited & finally when they were casually conversing that man started. They offered him tea & he said "What a great wisdom there is in this. Without this sugar, this tea has no taste". So Sh Sharafuddin said "Ok, you are finished too. Your understanding is this much. Very well, go ahead & drink your tea & we can talk as we like. We thought you were a big scholar that we can speak from a high level."

And Mashayikhs show this as an example that it is not with fine clothing. So there is this, and also the people who wear the clothes of Ahlus Sunnah they should also be careful. Because people come to them for Allah. One should not betray the things that they entrust him with. For example we had a deceased partner in business. He was a very pure, naive person. One man gave him $2000 to distribute to people as zakat. He said "While I was distributing this $2000, I put another $2000 on it to make sure that it is all just & correct". So he was a man like this. And he didn't know more than Fatiha & Ikhlas. Yet he was such a naive person. But you go to some people he has the clothes of Ahlus Sunnah- jubba, beard, turban. You can't entrust him even a cent, not being sure if you can take it back. He is responsible for this sin.

So one must be very careful about this. Meaning, before wearing Sunnah clothes your inside should be pure. Wear it if you can carry it. If you can't carry don't wear, don't deceive people. Appear as you are. This is a very important thing.

Today we speak about, to life style these days, nowaday. Most people, they wasting money for eating, for drink. Especially for clothing or houseware. For eating & drinking, it is ok. For we do this for Allah. But especially for these days, for wearing clothes the people wasting too much money for this also. Especially they wasting for trademark. They have now this years - before it was also trademark but these days it is more, become more people they like certain labels. And maybe 60% of people maybe they have 10 labels or 12 labels. They not wearing at all anything other than this. Only one mark there. So it is like, it must be this. If this not, no. And they are taking many.

They wasting money for this. And this is nonsense. Not giving any benefit for who is wearing it. Even for dunya not giving any benefit. If you wear this, if you wear another thing what will be different? If you feel cold you can put another thing on you. If you are hot, also many thing you can wear. But people cheated by Western company & they are like sheep, they milk them. They have billions of people. So coming money for them like rain. But for other people who is poor, or even if not poor they like to collect money & buy this. And this not giving any benefit for akhirah, for dunya even not giving benefit. How for akhirah? Nothing at all. Only wasting your time, your money. And with clothes.. To wear clothes..

From old time also, special people looking for appearance of people. Old time also looking who is wearing good- scholar, they listening for him, other people they are listening for him also. So once the head of judge in one city, he collect all scholars in his house. And they were meeting-meeting between scholars & common people also. But for scholar they put high place to sit there Where they are sitting, scholar, there was one-he is real scholar. But he is not looking at all for his appearance & he was poor also. His jubba making some holes & putting something. Because he is a scholar, they said scholar must sit in up, common people down. Scholar, they will discuss some subjects. So he was sitting there, the servant of house coming looking for people & looking for this man. "What, what are you doing there? Are you not looking for yourself? How you are appearance, like beggar! Come down." Because he is a scholar, he not discuss with ignorant people. Come & sitting down with people.

After this, beginning meeting. And this, beginning discussing many thing. So coming for one subject, it was very difficult. Nobody can settle this, solve it. There was shouting, too much shouting. And after this the judge said, "any of this people also, he has some idea for this?" He said, "I listening this. I can say something for this matter." So he was explain very good. They were very surprised "how this man, he is doing this?" And they were ashaming by themselves and apologized from him. "Come sit upstairs & sit with us." And so he was speaking many things.

They were very happy with him. But when finish, he take permission & he went. They were suprised "who is this man, who is this?"

So you must not look for appearance of people. Appearance not giving one letter more from wisdom for people. The good looking- ziynah/adornment, meaning the nice thing, Allah give-wisdom from Allah. This is nice. Knowledge also from Allah. Inside, from inside this beauty, beautification. Mercy, all from this inside. You cannot..If you wear hundred jacket, nothing will happen for you. You see this people in everywhere in the world. Especially they were saying mafia people, they are very very good suits & ties. And what? This is also example because this not giving beauty for people, to wear.

And the are other things, also when you wear sunnah- beard & jubbah, turban..You must be also give respect for this dress. Because people, they trust. And they give you everything for this appearance- Your appearance like holy people, like Prophet (sas), mashayikh, like awliya. They are opening all defense, no defense at all because they are for Allah, they giving. When you, after this you doing bad thing it will be very bad for you. Be careful for this also.

Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, Al Fatiha.
