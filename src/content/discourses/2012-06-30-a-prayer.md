He has made us from the nations of His Beloved (sas). O our Lord! May You show us beautiful days in dunya and in akhirat, to be collected under the liwa-ul Hamd/Prophet's banner (sas). I also desire to be there, with my children, and grand children..

O our Lord, Laka Aslamna Wa Bika Amanna Wa 'Alayka Tawakalna Wa Ilayka Anabna. Tub'na wa raja'na ilayk ya Rabbana.Tub'na wa raja'na ilayk Tub'na wa raja'na ilayk.

O Allah, O Allah, O my beautiful Allah.. O Allah, O Allah, O my beautiful Allah.. It is Your Beloved (sas) who represents Your Beauty. What can we do.. What can we do..May You grant us from that Nur also.. O Allah, O Allah. You are Subhan, You are Sultan. O our Lord, what a great honour it is, O our Lord, to be Your servants. How beautiful is Your worshipping. What an endless virtue it is- Your pleasure with us.

O our Lord, our situation is like this, as You know. O Allah, O Allah, O my beautiful Allah.. I desire beauty, nur to our hearts. Nur to our hearts and may You grant surur/joy to our sirr/secret inner beings. A weak servant, I am a weak servant of Yours, O Lord. Hamd to You, Shukur to You that You make us walk, You make us eat, You make us drink. You make us talk. You make us love. Shukur ya Rabbi.

Our pre-etermal Lord, Our eternal Lord. Azaliyyun Abadiyyun, eternal life! Eternal life! Eternal happiness, eternal joy, O our Lord. There is no limit to Your Grants, O Lord. And we keep being pressed (by troubles) in this dunya. We wish to reach Your Divine Presence, wish to reach Your endless grants, to be honoured by Your endless honour, and wish to love Your beloved (sas) & to gather under his banner. What have we found in dunya all these years? Shukur, Shukur to Mawla. Fatiha.

He is the one who makes you laugh, who makes you cry. He is our Lord! We cry for Him, we laugh for Him. Our joy is with our Lord. He created us, submerged us in His grants & Generosity. There is nothing in this world but there is an eternal gaze for us in akhirat of His beauties, tajallis changing in every moment. It says in Arsh ar-Rahman/ Throne of the All-Merciful, 70 thousand colors in every moment descends upon it, Nur. Then that one is removed & a new one descends. No two of them are alike. Allahu Akbar! Nur descends upon Arsh ar-Rahman in 70 thousand colors. They change in a moment, with another 70 thousand that no new one resembles the past one. Fatiha.
