“Ya ayuhallathina aamanu la tattakhithoo ‘aduwy wa’aduwakum awliya tulquna ilayhim bilmawadda.” (Sura Mumtahina:60) Allah says: “Do not be friends with my enemy.” “I know what is hidden before it is made apparent.” Allah Azza wa Jalla is the knower of all.

Be with the friends of Allah. Love each other for the sake of Allah. This is the order of Allah. Allah created us. He knows best what benefits us and what harms us. Do not say, “I know!” Follow whatever Allah has shown. People find peace when they are with the friends of Allah. Otherwise, they do not know where the blow will come from. You can see and talk to the servants of Allah who are on the right path as long as it does not harm you, to show the right way. Do not befriend those who are enemies of Allah. You can insistently give advice to people who are being an enemy of Allah. If they do not accept, do not get near them.

“Wala tarkanu ilallathina thalamu fatamassakum annar.” (Sura Huud:113) “They are tyrants, people who are an enemy of Allah are tyrants. Do not get close to them for the fire would rub on you too.” There are many other unseen fires other than the hellfire in the hereafter. Various kinds of people are not in peace: they are burning. Those people are burning where they are standing. Do not get close to them for you would burn too. Do not run towards the nar but towards the nur. What is meant by nar is fire and nur is light. Light is in the beloved servants of Allah, with those who love for the sake of Allah. Let us get near to them and let us be with them.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq.

Al-Fatiha.
