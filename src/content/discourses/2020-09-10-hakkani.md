Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

A’ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm:

(Qur’ān 17:81). Allah ﷻ states in the Holy Qur’an: ‘And say, Truth has come, and falsehood has perished. Indeed is falsehood ever bound to perish.’ When the truth comes, false does not remain, it ends. The truth is the word of Allah ﷻ and the word of our Holy Prophet ﷺ. Eventually, the truth will appear, even after years. What matters is the end. People are deceived by what is not right. They follow it and they consent to injustice. The truth is the word of Allah ﷻ, injustice is the work of shaytan.

Therefore, be with the truth and do not stray from the right way. The truth is beautiful. When everyone gets their right, everyone is comfortable and satisfied. But if you do not accept the truth, there will be no peace, no comfort and no blessing. Mankind is deceived. No matter how much he knows and reads, he can still be deceived. Actually, he is deceived more. When there is more arrogance, people get away from the truth.

Truth is the word of Allah ﷻ. When it comes, others do not matter. Therefore, be with the truth so that even if the whole world is against you, it will not have any effect. But if you are with the unjust, you will not end well. Allah ﷻ Azza wa Jalla says it clearly, but human beings follow their ego, shaytan and their own desire, which seem beautiful to them. May Allah ﷻ make us among those who are with the truth. Mawlana’s title was Hakkani, it means the defender of the truth. Insha’Allah we are Hakkani until the end and we are with the truth.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
