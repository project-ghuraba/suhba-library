What is a nebula? You know about these star clouds in the Milky Way? The star clouds like the ones in our Milky Way are called nebula. Their numbers are countless. They brought me a map, of the known universe. A professor brought, have you not seen him?

H.M. I have.

M. They can't observe what is beyond it. It is all black. Black brings magnificence and majesty. They call it "space", they don't know its beginning or end. They observed only this much, can't observe or reach what is beyond it. And this, what they reached also is imitated. Allahu Akbarul Akbar.

Here, I ask a question and no one answers. What was that question, Mehmet Nazim? I ask "Do the clouds come down from up or come up from the earth?" Who can answer this? They can't say "it comes down from up" but say "it forms through the earth" Say "It comes down from up.” No, it doesn't make sense to them. Donkey-headed ones! This comes down from upstairs. They say "it forms rising from the earth, through the oceans". Then how does it come out from the oceans? Do they pump it? How do they bring it out? And then, is the cloud a (solid) mass? Or is it liquid, or gas? Is it a solid thing? Or is it liquid or gas? What are these clouds? And then, millions of tons of water are carried in these clouds. Why does it not fall down? And it falls like this, drop by drop.

They don't teach these things, these treacherous men. Vapor rises from the oceans and forms clouds, they say. Shameless, ignorant men! They are ashamed to say Allah, shameless men! All of them are donkeys who have the same mentality as Darwin. They say "nature". What is nature? Say! Is it an individual? Is it a power? Is it a dream or is it a reality? If it is nature, Who made the earth as a globe? Who makes the earth spin? Who makes the sun rise and set? There is no answer. Nature. May they bury these pimps in the filth of nature. They teach this in the maktabs. But theirs are school, not maktabs...
