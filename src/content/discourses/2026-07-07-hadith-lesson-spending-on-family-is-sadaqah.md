As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

1 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Charity given by a relative to another relative is both charity and maintaining kinship ties.” Maintaining kinship ties is the most important thing for barakah. Therefore, charity given to relatives is doubly rewarding. It’s both charity and maintaining kinship ties. One earns reward twice. Sometimes, even if you can’t visit, sending charity serves both as charity and as if you’ve visited.

2 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Giving alms to the poor is sadaqah.” One will have earned a reward. “Giving alms to relatives is two alms: sadaqah and kinship.” So, it is considered as alms to the poor, but if you have a relative, if you give alms to them, it is both a sadaqah and a relationship with the relatives. It is also more important than sadaqah. Maintaining kinship ties is both barakah and a means for īmān and all kinds of goodness.

3 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Every good deed is a sadaqah.” So even if you do good, it will be considered as sadaqah. Again, if you help others, remove stones from the road, or help people, that is also considered charity. It’s not only with money, but after making the intention to give sadaqah, all of it becomes sadaqah. 

“When a Muslim person spends money on himself and his family, it is recorded as sadaqah.” So, whatever is given to his family is recorded as charity for him. 

“It is the alms given to protect one’s honor.” So, if he gives money and does something to protect his honor, it is also charity. 

“The alms given by a Muslim, its return belongs to Allah.” The charity he gives belongs to Allah; Allah will reward him. 

“Alms other than those spent on construction and maintenance are under the guarantee of Allah.” If one builds a building for himself, it does not count as charity, of course. The expenses incurred in sinful acts are not considered charity either. If someone gives money to sin, do something evil, or do something haram, it will not be considered charity. Apart from all of those, it is charity. They are under the protection of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla.

4 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Everything a person gives to his spouse is charity.” So the alms you give to the house are not in vain.

5 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

Our Holy Prophet says, “Whatever a person spends on his home, family, children and servants is charity for himself.” So when a person is a Muslim, he is rewarded for everything by Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla.

6 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “If one of you is going to give charity in the way of Allah as a voluntary act of charity, and they are Muslims, let him give it in the name of his parents.” If they have passed away, let him give it as their charity. Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam advises that a person should include his parents in the intention when giving charity. Thus, the reward of the charity goes to his parents, and he also receives an equal reward without any reduction. 

So, when charity is given with this intention, they also earn the same reward. Nothing is diminished from the person’s own reward. So, always keep this rule in mind: If a person dedicates all his good deeds, sadaqah, prayers, and worship to his parents and ancestors, the reward reaches them all. The same reward and thawab is also yours. Nothing is diminished from you. So, there is nothing as your reward is reduced.

7 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “The expenditure made by one’s family is charity.” So, all kinds of expenses, food, drink, clothing, their marriage, all their needs - that is, the things he spends on his family, the expenses, are charity.

8 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam addresses a lady companion, “Take your need of your husband’s wealth to provide for you and your children.”

9 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “If a woman spends money from her husband’s house, she will be rewarded for what she spends.” So, a woman who pays for her husband’s household needs, assistance, and family expenses has the reward of what she spends. Reward is written for her and her husband too. 

“There is also a reward for her husband because he earns the money.” So, because this wealth belongs to the husband and he earned it, the man earns the same reward as the wife. 

“At the same time, there is such a reward for the treasurer that protects and preserves goods.” So, there is a reward given to protecting the trust. When they take it from there, a reward is also written for it. 

“The reward of one of them does not reduce anything from the reward of the other.” So, they earn the same reward as the reward given; at the same time, it does not decrease. Allah gives that reward to each of them individually.

10 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “When a woman spends from her husband’s household without his order or permission, she receives half of the reward for her charitable act.” So, even if her husband doesn’t specifically give permission or is unaware of it, she will still receive half of the reward; her good deed will not go in vain.

The Messenger of Allah says the truth in what he said or as he said.
