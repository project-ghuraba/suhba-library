Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

May this holy month be good for everyone insha’Allah. May the month when our Holy Prophet was born and came to this world be blessed and good insha’Allah. It is a month with Barakah insha’Allah. Mawlid Sharif is written in every language in order to honor the birth of our Holy Prophet. There are so many different books on Mawlid of our Holy Prophet which explain the events that had taken place. And the best is Mawlid Sharif written by Süleyman Çelebi. No other version could reach up to his; neither Arabic, nor Persian nor any other. His writing is certainly given by inspiration from our Holy Prophet. It occurred by his virtue. Therefore, wherever it is recited, Barakah comes there.

Some foolish so-called ‘Alims, “ءوس ملاع”, “Bad scholar” say that to read Mawlid is Bid’ah. Why is it Bid’ah? People come together, those who pray and don’t pray, those who are religious and know nothing about it. They gather to listen and understand the beauty of our Holy Prophet because it is written with sincerity. As it is written with the Himmah of our Holy Prophet and with the approval of our Holy Prophet, it is read everywhere and it is heard everywhere. Peace and beauty enter the hearts of people through it. As we said, everyone listens to it without feeling any doubt, even people who know nothing about the religion.

There are people with good voices who recite Mawlid. People invite them to recite and they listen to them. The Mercy and Barakah of Allah descend upon that assembly then. But shaytan wants to stop it. With whose hands? With the hands of the rascals who call themselves Hojjahs and ‘Alims. They are not men but rascals. Who doesn’t show respect to our Holy Prophet will not be shown respect. Who doesn’t love our Holy Prophet will not be loved. If someone has a tiny bit of love to our Holy Prophet in his heart, he will save himself. He will be saved in Akhirah. But shaytan doesn’t want that. Shaytan has many tricks; he says Bid’ah and other things.

People gather for an hour or half an hour, they do not backbite and do not say bad words. They say “Allah” and make Salawat, they read Qur’an and praise our Holy Prophet. What is bad about it? Those rascals will be punished. None of their deeds will save them. Only the intercession of our Holy Prophet will save us. May Allah increase his love in our hearts insha’Allah. May this month be blessed for all of us insha’Allah. May it start with mercy and protect us from the troubles going on in this world insha’Allah. We can only be saved by our Holy Prophet insha’Allah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
