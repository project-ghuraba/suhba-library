As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

As we have said, our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam is the one who teaches the most adab (manners). He is the one who teaches all adab and morals. Whoever follows his way and adheres to his Sunnah becomes one of the servants beloved by Allah.

Every aspect of our Holy Prophet’s life is beautiful, everything he does is beautiful. He is beneficial to people. Nothing he does is in vain.

The Qur’ān says,<br>
وَمَا يَنطِقُ عَنِ الْهَوَىٰ
<br>“Wa mā yanṭiqu ʿani l-hawā”<br>
“Nor does he speak of (his own) desire.” (Qurʼān 53:3). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

He does not speak from his own whims and desires. Every aspect of his life, every action, is Sunnah; it is a lesson for people.

Today, there are those who want to emulate him, and those who don’t. Those who don’t want to emulate him are the people of shayṭān. They do not accept the Sunnah. They make “Sunnahs” according to their own minds, and when they see the true Sunnah, they dislike it.

Therefore, if you want to be a servant beloved by Allah, strive to do everything that our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam did. He has both forgotten and remembered Sunnah practices. 

Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam said,<br>
من احيا سنتي عند فساد أمتي فله أجر مئة شهيد
<br>“Man aḥyā sunnatī ʿinda fasādi ummatī falahu ajru mi'ati shahīd.”<br>
“Whoever revives my Sunnah when my Ummah is corrupted, Allah will reward him with the reward of one hundred martyrs.” [al-Bayhaqi]

There are many forgotten Sunnahs that most people don’t even know exist. Whoever revives these Sunnahs and tells them to people will earn the reward of one hundred martyrs.

Therefore, being with our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam is the greatest blessing. Showing him respect and reverence is the greatest blessing for us. Because there are many people who claim to be Muslim but do not value him at all. Many follow those who cannot see the greatness of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, who do not respect him, and who speak ill of him.

Therefore, those who are on this righteous way are the servants whom Allah loves. Allah will reward them Himself. Their way is light; their hearts are filled with light, faith, and happiness. Those who are not on his way are in distress; they cause distress to themselves and those around them. They never find peace; their hearts and faces are darkened. Everything they do is evil. May Allah protect us from their evil.

On the occasion of this blessed month of Mawlid, those who honor our beautiful and blessed Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam and recite Mawlid are on the way that Allah wants. Those who do not honor him, no matter how much they know of the Qur’ān and so, only know what they know and strayed from the righteous way. 

May Allah protect us. May Allah grant us all the intercession of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. May we never separate from his way, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
