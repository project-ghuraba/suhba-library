Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

May this Rajab and The Three Months be Mubarak (blessed) insha’Allah. The month of Rajab is the month of Allah, therefore they say: “بجعلا ىرت بجر يف”, “In the month of Rajab you see wonders; strange things may happen.” You see Allah’s power in the month of Rajab. Different states occur during this month. Around this time last year, the world went upside down; again, it was almost around this month that it started. Different things that people couldn’t even imagine happened and will continue to happen. They’re thinking that they’re the ones who are doing it, but it’s the will of Allah that is happening. Allah has assigned a specific time for everything.

This world and these people are in end times now. These are the end times that the Holy Prophet spoke about. In these times, people are saying that they are controlling everything and everything is under their command. They’re getting wilder. They’re putting themselves in place of Allah saying: “We’re going to do such and such”, let us see what they can do. Nothing can stand in the face of Allah or against Him. Allah puts His own program, and He changes it as He wills. Therefore, this month of Rajab is an important month, let us see what will happen. Insha’Allah it will happen just as Shaykh Baba, Shaykh Nazimق used to say: “Its goodness is for us and its evil is for them.” Who are with good? Those who are with Allah. Those who are with evil are always at loss and won’t win anything. Their efforts are useless and in vain because they are with shaytan; it is nothing but harm for them.

Islam says do not harm, do not harm anybody and you won’t be harmed either. They use their whole time and efforts to do evil and to spread evil, yet Allah has set a boundary and they cannot pass that boundary. They are going to consume, thinking that everything is in their hands but they’re going to see that nothing is in their hands or in their control. Therefore because of these states of affairs and because Muslims have forgotten Allah, they’re in hardship. Allah exists. We have no worries as long as we have Allah. Allah is with us so do not be sad. Mu’mins should not be saddened but should be patient instead, and the end of patience will be salvation and safety insha’Allah. Allah will send the Sahib (owner) and he will cleanse the world. What will he cleanse it from? From unbelief. What is unbelief? It is filth and dirt. We see this evil and badness all over the world. We see how these disgusting people are battling Allah. People should see better. Muslims especially, should not be deceived by these soldiers of shaytan which are enemies of Allah. You are either going to be a friend of Allah or an enemy of Allah, there’s nothing else. May Allah make us amongst the friends of Allah. May our faith strengthen insha’Allah.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq. Al-Fatiha.
