As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,
وَٱللَّهُ يُحِبُّ ٱلْمُحْسِنِينَ
“Wa Llāhu yuḥibbu l-muḥsinīn”
“And Allah loves the doers of good.”(Qur’ān 03:134).

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla is the Possessor of Beneficence. He loves those who do beneficence; do beneficence means those who give.

We also desire that Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla treat us with beneficence. In our duʿās, we say to Him, “Give us from your beneficence.” There are two types: beneficence and tests. Our Prophet ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam says, “Do not ask for tests,” because they are difficult. Not everyone can bear them. A person who, trusting in themselves, says, “I can do it,” and asks for a test, would’ve made a mistake.

Only very great Awliyā’ can do this. They do it so that they endure and bear on behalf of the ummah and lift the burden from the ummah. Even they do not ask for tests, but if tests come, they say, “Welcome.” But for an ordinary person, it is always necessary to say, “Treat us with Your beneficence.” In our duʿās, we must ask Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla for beneficence – “O Lord, do not test us! We cannot bear trials; they are not for us. Treat us not with tests, but with Your beneficence.” Beneficence is easy, it is good; and Allah loves those who bestow beneficence.

Therefore, to be among His beloved servants, we always ask for His beneficence. Beneficence means all kinds of ease, all kinds of beauty, barakah, and shifā (healing). All kinds of goodness are in beneficence. In return, you must thank Him When He bestows beneficence upon us, we will thank Allah.

As for tests – may Allah protect us, inshā’Allah, may we not be tested. We must also praise Him for tests. Gratitude (shukr) is for beneficence, and praise (ḥamd) is for tests. Therefore, may Allah treat us all with His beneficence. May He not test us. There are many tests, more than one’s mind can imagine. May we not be tested, inshā’Allah. May Allah protect us. We don’t even want to mention the different types of tests, so as not to stay in people’s minds. We always seek His beneficence. May Allah grant us, all of us, from His beneficence, inshā’Allah.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq

Al-Fātiḥa.

There are Qur’ān Khatms. They’ve sent them to us from all over the world. These khatms, verses, surahs, Yasin, salawat, Dalail al-Khayrat, and all kinds of good deeds done; we gift them the soul of our Prophet ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, his family and companions, all the prophets, Awliyā’, asfiya people, and mashāyikh; for the souls of all our ancestors; for the arrival of good and the warding off of evil; for the strengthening of our faith; for protection and preservation; for happiness here and hereafter.

Lillāhi Taʿāla

Al-Fātiḥa
