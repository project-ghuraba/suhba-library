Allāhumma ṣalli wa sallim ‘alá nabiyyinā Muḥammad ‘alayhi s-salām, ṣalātan tadūmu wa tuhdá ’ilayh, māmarra llayāli wa ṭūla d-dawām. Yā salām.

Allāh He grant us the ṣalātu salām. This is gift from Allāh Almighty. Everybody thinks everything is happening coincidentally. No. If something meant to be to you, that will come to you. If it's not for you, will never pass even next to you. So simple. If something is belonging to you, even you are in the moon, it will come to you. But if something, it is not belonging to you, you just look at it and you cannot reach it. So simple. This Allāh. Allāh Almighty He make everything according to Him, not according to you. This is if we understand this one, we will be all of us mu‘min and good believers. But the ego it is all the time doing the job that Allāh Almighty He give for the ego also to do.

We have to analyze everything. Somebody go for doctor, say "I have stomach ache". Doctor say "Ok, we need some analysis". I don't want to mention other meaning. But have to do. Somebody has to analyze. And he has to go for most, most things that people they will be... But somebody has to analyze. And some people, special people they are analyzing. The awliyā’ they are also analyzing. When you analyze, you can see where is the illness and where is the way out also. Mawlānā Shaykh he used to say, so many people used to say to Mawlānā Shaykh "Yā Mawlānā, you don't have good murid. All of them they are having mental illness, this illness." He'd say "Ya Hu, we are hospital. Did you see somebody in good form going to hospital?" Never. Must be ill to go for hospital. If you are all the time good, in shā’a Llāh everybody will be good, you never know the address of hospital. No need. Mawlānā Shaykh he was the hero that he take the worst case. He is not accepting half mental situation. Should be full mental situation. I am number one. Yes.

"Man 'arifa nafsah 'arifa Rabbah" This is the wisdom behind this one. Who knows himself, he knows his Lord, his Allāh Almighty. Very important. Because if you are something and you become acting as something else, this is wrong. This is wrong. But it not necessary to show your illness but you have to know your illness. Mawlānā Shaykh he was having, this is his favorite joke: he says somebody gone to one city or village and they were famous in this village to be crazy people. And he come and he look. And he said "I heard that this village has a lot of crazy people." "Yes, we all of us crazy here but except one person. He is the most decent one." "Oh very good. Can I see him?" "No, you cannot see him." "Why?" "He is not around." "Where he is?" "We roped him in the tree tightly because he has some times coming to him this crazy moments. And then he starts to hurt everybody so we keep him tight to not hurt anybody." This important to know. When this crazy moment coming and then to not harm anybody.

Now so many people from where to where coming to this crazy situation or crazy talking? Yā Hū, if you don't analyze, you will not get the result. Say "Doctor, what is my result?" "I cannot know. You look ok. But I may look for your brain. But I cannot see. Maybe there is brain, maybe not. If there is brain, maybe it's working, maybe it's not also." It is important for us to know how to go on. That's why the murshid in our life it's very important. Who is the murshid? Not the murshid like the one been roped in the tree, can make harm for your spiritual way. That's why Allāh Almighty He spread all over the world saints. It is true saints. That's why Mawlānā Shaykh he was all the time telling me "My son, I will give you golden advice. And the golden advice is to know after under any head can be Khiḍr ‘alayhi s-salām." And don't mess up with anybody. You have to fully respect for somebody you see. You have to fully respect because you don't know who he is. Could be Khiḍr ‘alayhi s-salām. You never know.

This is for the saint around the globe because every place has. Allāh Almighty, He plant His servant to serve His servants. Very important. Because Allāh Almighty He didn't create this world and say "Do what you like". No, it is not like this. Everybody thinks "What's going on? What's going on? What's going on? The world become crazy." No, no, no. Everything is under control. Everything is 100% under control. Mawlānā Shaykh used to say: you think you're walking by your own. No way. No way to walk by your own. Allāh Almighty He is putting everything in the program. And we are following the program. Maybe Allāh Almighty He give us the will power but still we are inside the program. Mawlānā Shaykh he used to say: "we are in the boat." The boat it has all program to go on. But the passengers they can go up and down in the boat. Plus there is something also in the boat. There is some areas you cannot enter, like the machine room, like the captain room, like some private room. You cannot enter. But you will be able to go all around the ship. So you have will power to go around the ship but certain areas you are not allowed. This is how we are. Allāh Almighty He put big program. And with this big program the boat is going, going, going...

Now we all know that we are in ākhir ẓaman - End of time. When Rasūlu Llāh (saw) ṣaḥābah asking him "Yā Rasūlu Llāh, is Qiyāmah so close?" He said "It's like this" - he put his fingers [together] - so close. Two fingers like this. It's 2000 year now. Mā shā’a Llāh, yā Sayyidi. But that means we're still so close. That means we're still so prepared. That means we have to be prepared. Today it is fitnah time. It is not the time that everybody have to be relaxed and to be in holiday. No. Now we have to be prepared for Allāh Almighty what is coming next. Because when you look around the geographic areas, it is boiling. And my advice, as Mawlānā Shaykh Naẓim he was advising, those days at least you have to keep something in your house for food-wise and to make a lot of astaghfiru Llāhu l-‘aẓīm wa 'atūbu 'ilayh. Because this is when you make...

This is ayah "wa mā kāna Llāhu mu‘adhdhibahum wa hum yastaghfirūn" (8:33) - āyah karīmah that say Allāh Almighty He will never punish them when you [Prophet sas] are with them. And Allāh Almighty will never punish them when they are keeping saying astaghfiru Llāhu l-‘aẓīm wa 'atūbu 'ilayh. That's why we are saying we are with you, yā Rasūlu Llāh (saw) because we are loving you so much. This is the secret - to be with Rasūlu Llāh (saw) that you have to convey your love to him. Very important. Because when you are conveying your love to somebody, then he will say "Yes, you are with me." But when you are looking with suspicion, say "Ah, maybe I'm not with you." Yes, that's why very important to say we are loving you, yā Rasūla Llāh. Don't be shy by loving Rasūla Llāh. Be extremely loving Rasūla Llāh, extremely as much as you can. As much as you can. Don't be hesitating. Don't put a border.

Young man, bachelor man, he find his love, do you think he will be hesitated with convey his love for his woman or for his girl? No way. You cannot tie him like that crazy man to say "No." This is very important. Why we don't love Rasūla Llāh (saw) as much as we can? We have to say it. We have to declare it and we have to be proud of it, to not be shy by loving Rasūla Llāh (saw). Yā Ḥabībi, yā Rasūla Llāh. Because they say loving Rasūla Llāh is shirk. No, it is not. It is not. If Allāh Almighty He love him, why we shouldn't love him? What is the reason? But we have to, we have to be extremely loving him, extremely. And this is coming for this, Allāh Almighty will never punish us when we love Rasūla Llāh (saw). He never punish us when we are with Rasūla Llāh. And the istighfār, that means keeping saying astaghfiru Llāhu l-‘aẓīm.

Maybe the bad days will come, maybe. It's expected by the way. It's not far away. If some armies putting arms and weapons as a mountain, they will not put it to look at it. They will use it. Definitely they will use it. Nobody make mountain of weapons only to look for it. They say "We have to use it." They will use it. This is definitely. And this is shows the prophecy of Rasūla Llāh (saw). It's ḥaqq. This also it is ḥaqq. It's coming. That's why we have to warn ourself and to be prepared. Why? We have to prepare spiritually because we are not interfering in anything. We don't need to interfere with anything. We are only watching. Now they put big screen, say "Today it will be football match with this very famous team and with that very famous team." And millions watching. Millions watching. So say "What?" Nobody can interfere. Both of them playing. This is our job. Today our job is to be watching, looking. But we wish that Allāh Almighty to not bring these things to us and make us safe because it is not easy.

Allāh Almighty He never punish people without reason. If you see something happen around, should be a reason. Should be a big reason. It is not easy that... This is Allāh Almighty, never do things without reason. This Allāh. It is not according to you. Maybe this.. No, no, no. He is the one deciding. I think if we are only saying "Yā Rabbi, we are following Your order. We are powerless and we are just for You and we are keeping loving Rasūla Llāh (saw). And we are only for You", then I think we will be safe, in shā’a Llāh.

May Allāh forgive me and forgive us and make us in the safe time, in the safe place, and with awliyā’uLlah because they are our light, they are our love. And they are the one showing us the way to Allāh Almighty.
