As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam orders us to do good things. HeNone tells us about the things that are useful for us. And heNone tells us about the useless things that harm us, as well. HeNone warns us so that people will live well, in comfort and peace in this world; and so that their place is good in ākhirah and they will be in paradise. Those nowadays... Among the people of our time, these qualities are turned upside down.

Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam told us about many of these useful and important virtues. Ṣilatu r-raḥīm is one of them. It means not to forget relatives, to be in close relationship with them. Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam advised us to pay attention to this. When relatives are close with each other, there is barakah. Their rizq increases. They attain the pleasure of AllāhNone. AllāhNone loves them and our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam loves them; for when something happens to a relative and he asks for help, he receives the help he seeks. Family members look after one another. This is what ṣilatu r-raḥīm is. People of old times kept to this more carefully. Their relatives and kin were truly important to them. People of the present time run away from each other. Relatives, friends, neighbours; no one wants anyone in their way. They don't want to deal with anyone. If they have a group of friends they have chosen themselves, they spend more time with them. And AllāhNone knows if they are on the right way or not. That is another matter.

As people today are only thinking of their material lives, they are trying to move away from that good characteristic. They are running away, to be more correct. No one cares about relatives. People of the present time don't care about them at all. Therefore, the order of our Holy ProphetNone, and hisNone advice, is a good thing for us. To ask after our relatives, to give them advice, to help them, and to work to do good together before AllahNone, with the pleasure of AllahNone, is something forgotten by Muslims. We must do this. Many people pray and do other things. There are people who don't do this also. Those who do pray also, as we said, people are following their egos now. They are following the customs of this time, and don't want to deal with anyone. They don't ask after anyone. Thanks to AllāhNone, we are Muslim. That brings beauty and goodness to us. Every word of our Holy ProphetNone is good for people from all sides, materially and spirituality. Our Holy Prophet's ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam advice, teachings, and instructions, have uncountable benefits, both physically and spiritually; benefits that last forever. The more we can follow them, the more we will gain.

May AllāhNone not let ṣilatu r-raḥīm, the love of our own people, be lacking in us, in shā’a Llāh. Indeed, when you think about the people of this time... But as Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim used to say, we too have grown old. Not just half of our life, but two-thirds of it is over, probably. We can remember old times, how close people were with their relatives. And we are surprised when we see what people are like now. May AllahNone help people. May AllāhNone give guidance to them as well, in shā’a Llāh.

Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam every time heNone teach us good thing. Very good thing for, not only for Muslim – for human being. Because many people they are saying okay, if they are... like to look for Islām or for this, they are fair people, they said – they not say Prophet, they say Muḥammad ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam – He say heNone teaching very good thing. HeNone is really, for human being, whole human being, when, not looking for Islām or other thing also... Who, they said that heNone is first one teacher good thing in history. Whole history from Ādam ‘alayhi s-salām until now. They accept this, and it is real like this. And heNone teach us the best thing for Muslim, for non-Muslim, for humanity all.

What heNone... many thing heNone teach, but Muslim even they are not giving any importance. Not listening and obeying as much we must do. Many thing heNone saying, ordering and – is for our benefit first. One of them, to be good with relative, with your first... ṣilatu r-raḥīm, calling. This meaning who they are relative, to not cut relationship between you and your relative. This it is giving barakah, giving rizq, and giving health also, giving īmān also. This is what benefit. If not doing this, your barakah going, your rizq, and AllāhNone not happy with you and not look for you. For this, we are many people now in this days, we are living – they was saying "modern time", now more more modern time. Every time going more modern, becoming less relationship between relative. Even with any people. Nowadays people they like to be only when they like, to be with people. And sometimes after, they left no friendship, no relative to visit them or to contact them or to look for them. This time like this, but it is not good. Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, hisNone order continue until Qiyāmah.

We are now in time no need for relative, no need for other. Maybe old time of course relative it was very important for protect yourself even, old time. But now they said no need for this. We are government, there are police, there is any. So no need to look for these people to burden on us. It is not they are burden. They are even opposite. They are barakah for who continue to be with them, and to look after them. And to not cut this relation between that people. So nowadays people as we said, they are everything they are thinking with material thing, not with spiritual. They thought if material coming, no need. They forget not thinking even for spiritual. And the spiritual, they give you happiness, give you satisfied and give you barakah. Give you every good thing. There is millions of example for rich people, they are not happy. They have million, billion, but they are not happy. But when without anything, and your relationship between family and relative, AllāhNone make, give this. It is... AllāhNone have secrets, many secret, and this one of them. So we must not forget this. Old time it was real different. As we said, I am now as Mawlānā Shaykh, we are becoming old. Our life maybe more than two third or more than this is finish. Before, fifty or sixty year ago, it was very different relationship between family and relative. It was very strong. After, slowly slowly, we see everywhere, everybody forgetting, not looking. But it is order of Islām. Muslim they must be look after family, after their relative after their, even neighbour. Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam was extremely ordering for look after neighbour. As one of ṣaḥābah he said, "I thought heNone will give them inheritance even for ṣaḥābah."

So it is very important to be with people, good relationship with every people, and make you happy. Make AllāhNone happy with you. This is very important. Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam heNone is happy with us, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone help us. AllāhNone make shayṭān away from us, not to obey him – to obey Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
