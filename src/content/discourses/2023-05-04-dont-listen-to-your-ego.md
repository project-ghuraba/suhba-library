A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Wa-la-qad khalaqnā l-insāna wa-na‘lamu mā tuwaswisu bihī nafsuhū wa-naḥnu aqrabu ilayhi min ḥabli l-warīd" (50:16). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘aẓīm.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla creates man. And Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says, "We know what his ego whispers to him." Nothing is hidden. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla knows these whisperings (waswasah) and what a person’s ego tells him. "We are closer to you than your jugular vein," says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Everything is swimming in the ocean of Allāh’sNone Mightiness: people, creatures, the whole universe. We are all swimming in the ocean of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla’s Mightiness, Qudrat. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla knows everything. Our ego is constantly making waswasah or striving to get us to follow it. Most people follow their ego.

Now, especially in this time which we call the end of times, there are very few people who do not always follow their ego. Others have completely surrendered to the desires of their egos. They are doing everything. They also make laws. Now, people say, "Do not go against your ego. Do whatever your ego says. Don't listen to anything else: to religion, to faith, to Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, to the ProphetNone. Do not listen to anything. Just listen to your ego. Follow your ego, and enjoy yourself." Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says, "INone know what your ego is telling you. INone am closer to you. Therefore, don't follow your ego," says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Don’t obey your ego. Our Prophet ṣallá Llahu 'alayhi wa-sallam also says, "Don’t follow your ego. Don't let your ego ride you. You ride your ego. Don’t let it ride you," heNone says. Whereas the people of this time take care of whatever their ego desires. "Be like a donkey. Let it ride on your back. Let it make you run to wherever it wants. Let it whip you and hit you. Let it disgrace you. You will be satisfied. The more evil you do, the more satisfied you will be," these people say.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says otherwise. HeNone says, "Beware of disgracing yourself like that. Don't put yourself in these bad situations." Most of today’s people, as we said, have already given up the struggle against their ego. There are some people who ask, "How will we train our ego? How can we kill our ego?" The ego can not die. You can control it. If you claim that it is dead, it will get to you. It rises up suddenly and swallows you. Therefore, while a person is alive, his ego does not die. The ego dies only when you die. Of course, the great awliyā’ who make the greatest jihād (struggle) against their egos, still never leave their egos alone. They don't arrogantly say, "I killed my ego." They do not stop trying to fight the ego. They always, until the end of their lives, strive to control it. And they do so. They know that if you loosen up a little bit, it will come out and control you right away. May AllāhNone protect us from the evils of our egos, in shā’a Llāh.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying: Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Wa-la-qad khalaqnā l-insāna wa-na‘lamu mā tuwaswisu bihī nafsuhū wa-naḥnu aqrabu ilayhi min ḥabli l-warīd" (50:16). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘aẓīm.

AllāhNone, HeNone said, "WeNone create human being and WeNone know what his ego telling him, whispering him. And WeNone are very close. Even WeNone are closer than his artery," – his blood artery, big artery in heart or in everywhere – because we are living in ocean of HisNone Mightiness, HisNone Qudrat, HisNone... Mightiness, of course. Everything swimming in this ocean. Everything AllāhNone HeNone is, ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone, closer. Nothing can be without HisNone will. So HeNone is very near for us, not like these crazy people they said: worshipping idol, worshipping stone, worshipping wood. And saying, this god there. God, if he is there he cannot see you? HeNone is, God, in everywhere in universe. So we are – HeNone is knowing what our ego telling us, what ego want. For this, HeNone is making us to be careful about ego. Don’t make, think, ego doing thing without Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla knowing. Only, even in your heart if whispering, AllāhNone knowing this. But of course when whispering, different. To do what ego want – what whispering – it’s different. If this waswas, whispering – everbody has it – but not make it for action. When you make it action, you punished for this.

But, we are living, of course, time which ego must be... In most of people, ego controlling people. And in very, very few people, their ego, they not control them. And plus this: the time we live in, everything – this people who is claiming they are human being – they are making ego, first thing. What ego want, you must do. Don’t let anything your ego want, to not do it. Everything ego – with very bad thing, very ugly thing, very disgusting thing – ego want, quickly you must do it. Even they have now laws. If anybody coming against this ego – what ego want – and saying this wrong, they charge you. They can put you in jail or take your children from you, or do thing. This is, we are living. This is what we saying, "like" human being. This is not human being. Just, they are just donkey for ego. Egos, they riding on them, and they are very happy.

"Oh our ego is, you are happy?" "Yes." "What we can do?" "You do worse than this." "What we can do worse this?" "You can do very bad thing, I will be very happy." "Okay, your order on our head, but you be happy with us."

This is, our time, people doing. Just like this. But Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, saying don’t let this to happen to you, to come like very low people. You become higher. Don’t listen to your ego. You must put your ego under you, you must ride ego. Ego must be your donkey, and you order what she must do. Not she is ordering you, no. You must order. You must be human being, not donkey.

This is, AllāhNone and Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, showing us the best manner, best thing for your honour of human being. But these people, still not listening for this. Only, they are trying – for east and west, everywhere in this earth – to spread this bad manner, unnoble thing, character, for human being. Even not good for animal even. They still, they think, making people, forcing people to do this. And people, of course, they are already suffering from ego. It – for maybe, many people – they are, become easy, them to follow this. But, they don’t know, they will be regret for what for they do. AllāhNone keep us safe from our ego, from these people. In shā’a Llāh, AllāhNone save us, protect us from them, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
