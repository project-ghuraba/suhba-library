Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Our Holy Prophet describes the world in this time, the end of times and says: “There comes a time when people pass through graves and say, ‘How fortunate they are that they did not live in this time, they died before this time, the time of Fitnah’.” That means that they envy those in the grave because they know that those in the grave are in a better state. Because in the end of times, Fitnah is increasing day by day. People think that they can make the world a better place, but they are moving away from Allah and it is getting worse.

Therefore, this is the time of Fitnah. People should hold on to the rope of Allah and run to the mercy of Allah. ‘ِ اللَّ ىَلِإ اورُّ فِ َف’, ‘So flee to Allah.’ (Qur’ān 51:50). Where are we going to run to? Run to Allah. There is no salvation other than with Allah. It is not clear what these people are doing. The more evil these evil people do, the more evil will come upon them. They think that they are in gain but they are harming people and in the first place they are harming themselves.

In end of times, the time we live in, it is by the will of Allah that it is a time of great reward. Be patient. It is a great blessing to be on your own and to live by trusting Allah. Allah Azza wa Jalla protects and preserves those who seek refuge in Him. Mawlana used to say: “Every new day that comes is worse than the one that passes.” Therefore, seek refuge in Allah, flee to Allah; this is the cure. Everything has a cure. Everything has a salvation. The greatest salvation is to seek refuge in Allah, to run to Allah. Whether it was a disease, a virus, war, bombs, earthquakes, etc., it all happens by the will of Allah Azza wa Jalla and by a means. And by this means, the believers win and the unbelievers lose; nothing else. May we not be from those who lose but from those who win insha’Allah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
