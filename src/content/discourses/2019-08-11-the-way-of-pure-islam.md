As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Happy ‘Īdu l-Aḍḥá to all our brothers wherever in the world they are. May AllāhNone bless this holy day. May it become a means for good things, in shā’a Llāh. May HisNone gifts be everlasting, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone gives physical and spiritual gifts to people on this holiday. HeNone distributes them. We should be happy with HisNone gifts. In old times, there was poverty among people. If they bought a new dress, they would wear it from holiday to holiday. They used to buy it then. They would wear it clean and buy clothes for that year, clothes, accessories, and shoes. Therefore, people were happy. People were happy when ‘Īd came for they would wear new clothes. Therefore, it says this is not what should make you happy. He should be happy who fears AllāhNone. Because when he fears AllāhNone, his end will be good. He won't do wrong things. He won't do evil. His end will be peaceful.

It is important that our end be good. People now wear something once, sometimes three times. They wear it five times and buy new clothes. ‘Īd has nothing to do with clothes anymore. The gifts HeNone gives are important. And HisNone forgiveness is important. All sins are forgiven. The reward that is given is known only to Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. May it be blessed for all of us. May it be a means for goodness, in shā’a Llāh. May it give strength to our īmān, in shā’a Llāh. May AllāhNone protect Muslims, especially the Muslim children.
