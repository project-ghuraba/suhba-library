As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Shukr to Allah we were granted to visit Iraq: Baghdad, Karbala, Najaf. May Allah be pleased with them. May their ranks be exalted, and may their himmah be upon us. These are the great figures of Islām, the great personalities of the Ummah. They invited and called millions to the way of truth and served them. Their himmah is eternal. Their support is continuous. There is no such thing as “it’s over when they die.” When they die, they become even stronger. Their spiritual strength is greater. Because they no longer have any worldly ties. Their duty continues, never ceasing, to prepare those in this world for the Hereafter. Their records are open. As our Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam said, as long as there are people who pray for you and benefit from your knowledge, your records are open.

Therefore, their books of deeds are open. And they are generous. They make duʿā and strive with himmah for others to come to the right way. The rain of mercy never ceases upon their tombs and blessed places. Those who go there do so for the sake of Allah. They seek mercy, forgiveness, and goodness from Allah. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla does not turn them away empty-handed. Those who go there do not go for this world, but for the hereafter, so that their hereafter may be prosperous; and they can ask for anything.

Shukr to Allah this journey was a beautiful one. It was a journey undertaken for the pleasure of Allah Shukr to Allah we went and returned safely. We returned with mercy. For their sake, we did not return empty-handed, but we returned with Allah’s gifts, inshā’Allah. May this, inshā’Allah, strengthen our īmān, and may it strengthen the īmān of others, inshā’Allah.

These blessed ones, from the time of our Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam and later too, Ahlu l-Bayt, scholars, saints, and great Awliyā’ have been present there in great numbers. Because it is the city of the Caliphate, because it is one of the cities of Islām, people have come from all sides seeking himmah (support), knowledge and wisdom. That place has been filled with their blessed knowledge, grace, and barakah. And it continues in the same way, shukr to Allah The outward appearances are not important. What is important are the personalities resting there. Their himmah overshadows worldly matters. It is not for dunyā, but for Allah’s pleasure, inshā’Allah. May Allah make it a means of goodness. May their himmah be present, inshā’Allah.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq

Al-Fātiḥa.

They said that 100,000 Quran Khatms were read. We gift them firstly for the noble soul of our Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, for the souls of all the Prophets, Awliyā’, Asfiya, and mashāyikh. For the souls of all those who have passed away. For the souls of believing men and women, Muslim men and women. May those who read this attain whatever good intentions they may have, inshā’Allah. May we all have happiness here and hereafter.

Lillahi Ta’āla

Al-Fātiḥah.
