The ego-nafs is jabbar-tyrannical. May Allah not give our ego a chance! Aman ya Rabbi. Fatiha. "Talabana, wajadana" They asked for it & He gave them. The tyrants asked & Allah gave them. And, in the end, look at their tyranny! You are only one person. How much can you collect? In how many seats can you sit? On how many thrones can you sit? What can you collect? What can you put in your pocket? Nothing. You can't put anything in your pocket.

Do good works, charity. If you did charity, people would not be in such a situation. Give! Give! "Spend O Bilal, and don't ever have any fear of any decrease from the Master of the Arsh - Divine Throne". It is Hadith. Bilal - there are two meanings in this. They call this Al-Mutaradif. He (sas) says, give without saying, "I don't have enough in my hand". Or, he (sas) specially addresses Bilal al Habashi (a companion): Give. "Don't ever have any fear of any decrease from the Master of the Arsh". There is Azamat-grandeur in this! "Don't ever have any fear of any decrease from the Master of the Arsh!" The scholars may repeat this address to this king. Are you afraid that your wealth will decrease? Who can take it with them (to hereafter)?

People are crushed with poverty, they are tired & fed up with them. All who come to power want to collect. Give! Give! Give without saying "there is not enough". Don't fear that Allah(jwa),the Master of the Arshu'r-Rahman-Beneficent Throne will decrease (your wealth). Aman ya Rabbi. Tawba ya Rabbi. Tawba ya Rabbi. Tawba ya Rabbi. For infaq - spending for charity, (Allah) grants very high ranks. Spending in charity gives a very high rank for people. Allah Almighty loves it. But..They call it "shuhhan muta'an"? "Shuhhan muta'an - stinginess". What does it mean? S.N. You know better. M. Is it not Arabic? "..When you see stinginess being obeyed, desires being followed & each person being deluded by his own opinions, then leave the common people & take care of yourself" (Hadith). Allahu Akbar! Our Prophet (sas) informed fully about everything that will happen in the future. Everybody likes their own ideas, no one likes our mind.

They asked Mulla Nasreddin: "How many kinds of people are there?" He did not answer... They said: "Ok, you couldn't answer this. Then, how many kinds of donkeys are there?" He said, "For donkeys.. There are donkeys that look like men, & donkeys that look like donkeys. He says, some men are donkeys & some donkeys are like men. When it becomes like a man, he also becomes a donkey already. He says, there is the original donkey & the imitated one. The original donkeys are ahead of the imitated ones. When the last days come, the original donkeys will outrun the imitated ones. The donkeys know themselves - that they are donkeys. But men, the imitated donkeys, they don't know themselves. May Allah forgive us. Why did Greece go bankrupt? Everyone getting out from work and goes to restaurants to eat. They don't cook at home. Of course they go bankrupt this way. No one cooks at home anymore. Thanks to Allah, hundred thousand times. You put 5 kg rice in it? 10 kg? H.R. Not 10 kg. 5 kg. All these people ate & got full. Alhamdulillah. It is Allah who feeds people.
