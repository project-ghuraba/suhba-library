As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Kullu nafsin dhā’iqatu l-mawt, thumma ilaynā turja‘ūn" (29:57). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘aẓīm. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says each person, all of mankind, will certainly go to the next life (ākhirah). No one will remain here. Therefore, to people with īmān, death is not a scary thing. Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim used to say that the awliyā’ say, the death of people, the death of believers, is as easy as passing from one door to another. Dying, for them, is not scary. But for people without īmān, may AllāhNone protect us, their death and the release of their souls are very bad. It is nothing but torture. But it is not a scary thing for believers. It is the most frightening thing for people. Some people say they have the fear of death. The fear of death. Why are you afraid? [Your life] is as long as AllāhNone has appointed for you. Being scared will not benefit you. It is better if you're not scared.

If you are scared, it is not that bad for you. It makes you do good deeds, in order not to be scared. Repent for the bad things you've done. Ask forgiveness for your sins. Make things right with people, if you have harmed them. Put an end to their harm. Whatever you did to them, do as much as you can to make things right and for them to forgive you. Because the right of a servant is more difficult. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla forgives everything. HeNone is forgiving. But HeNone wants the right of a servant to be forgiven by that servant himself. Therefore, giving other people their rights is harder for those who are gone. There is nowhere to run, there. You can be saved in this world by talking, with money or making things right. But there, you have to give up your pious deeds for them to forgive you.

Therefore, this world is not eternal. Everyone will go from here. However long AllāhNone has appointed for this life, it will be that much. Therefore, believers must know this and be content with it. As we said, we should not be afraid. There is nothing to be scared of. As long as we are on the way of AllāhNone, no need to be scared. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla will certainly treat us with HisNone kind favours. Those who are on the way of our Holy ProphetNone will certainly be with himNone. The love and intercession of our Holy ProphetNone will be upon us. This is true.

They are frightening people with the fear of death now. "It will happen so and so." Panic attacks and the fear of death have occurred. This is because of the ignorance of these people. Who is ignorant? Professors who graduated from universities are very ignorant. Most ignorant ones are from them, and from learned ones. Because the ignorant are not those who don't know how to read and write, but those who don't know Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Those who don't know what they do and how, where they come and where they go, are ignorant. People on the way of AllāhNone are not ignorant. May AllāhNone protect us. May AllāhNone make our ending good, in shā’a Llāh.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying in Qur’ān: Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Kullu nafsin dhā’iqatu l-mawt, thumma ilaynā turja‘ūn" (29:57). Everyone, he will taste this, death. Everyone who breathe, he will come some day. For each one different day, what AllāhNone write for them, they will be, taste this death. Death it is, sometimes, it is very bitter, very bad, very ugly. But for mu’min it is nothing, very sweet, very easy. So many people in this world. Everybody know, sometimes they thinking, "I will die also." But most of them, they're not thinking about this. But for mu’min, every time he thinking about this. And it will be, for him, easy. What, this, Mawlānā was saying, death like coming from one room to other room. You open door, and go to other side. It is just very easy, like this, for believer, for mu’min, for who obey Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.

But don't think if you are making some bad thing for yourself or for other thing, you will be, it will be easy like this. No. Easy, for who are accepting and following order of AllāhNone, following way of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. It will be easy for them. For other, not easy. Sometimes they said, death, for some people, like very painful, like thousand cutting with sword. Some people it will be like this. But for believer, it is not like this at all. It is very easy. For this, what you suffering, what you are doing in this world, don't be unhappy, especially believer people. So it will be for you to... Your travelling to next life, it will be very easy. But if you are following your ego, doing bad thing for yourself, for other people, it will be difficult. And Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, for people, for human being, HeNone is accepting their forgiveness before death. After death, that's finished. So before, what they done with bad thing, they can ask for forgiveness from AllāhNone. AllāhNone forgive everything. Don't worry. But for what, if you done something wrong for people, cheat people or harm people or what you do, you must ask for forgiveness from people also, to be, go clean for ākhirah.

This is very important. And for whole believer, it is like this. For, very, very easy. But nowadays there are many people, even believer, following. I don't know what happen. Every people they are saying, I have fear from death. Coming fear from death for me. And they say "panic attack". This panic attack it's fashion now for whole people, Muslim, non-Muslim, believer, non-believer. They are saying, what happen? I sometimes coming, I am very afraid I will die. You will die. When your time coming, you will die. Before, you cannot die before this. If you, before your time coming, you cannot. Even you want to die, you will be wait what AllāhNone put for you, not by yourself. And if you are fearing or not fearing, no benefit. If you are, "I am very fear, I am afraid from death." And what [about] death? He will be run away from [you] when you are afraid? No. It is only, you harm yourself. You put pressure on yourself. So for this, believer they must be very comfort, relax. When AllāhNone send this angel of death, they must be ready for this. They said, "Isṭā‘ad lil-mawt, qabla l-mawt." Be ready for death, before death. When you are ready: "Oh, come. We are going? Oh, yallā (come on)." We go from door to other – from good door to best, better door. For this don't worry. Don't afraid from this. But don't harm yourself, also. This maybe people they think they can suicide or do this. It's not acceptable. Who suicide or doing bad for himself, whole he will suffer until Qiyāmah, same pain for what he done for himself. All time, he will die, he will come again. He will... He cut himself, he will cut whole time for...

So no, you cannot run from AllāhNone. AllāhNone, everywhere. No, no place you can run. Maybe you can run from government, from state to other state, you can run. But here, if you run for end of universe, you cannot run from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. For this, be relaxed. Think about this, and obey AllāhNone. Go in way of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. AllāhNone make us to be real[ly] happy with our way, for what AllāhNone gave us. We are happy. AllāhNone be happy with us, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
