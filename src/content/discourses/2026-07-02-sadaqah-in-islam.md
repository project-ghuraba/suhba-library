As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
وَأَنفِقُوا مِن مَّا رَزَقْنَاكُم
<br>“Wa anfiqū mim-mā razaqnākum”<br>
“And spend from that which We have provided for you.” (Qurʼān 63:10). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says, “Spend from what We have provided for you.” Spend; that is, give for good, spend for yourselves.

As stated in the ḥadīths of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, if a person seeks the pleasure of Allah, what he spends for himself is as if it were given in the way of Allah. Everything he spends on himself, his family, his relatives, and his neighbors becomes goodness and rewards for him. This is a great benefit for a Muslim.

Of course, when a person becomes a Muslim, all kinds of goodness come to him. Allah accepts what he does and will do, and gives him barakah. But if he does not know Allah, then no matter how much he helps, no matter what he gives to whom, it benefits neither himself, nor his family, nor his neighbors. Such people don’t even give their neighbors anyways. They don’t do their help for the sake of Allah. If they help, it’s only in exchange for some benefit.

Therefore, our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam says that when giving for the sake of Allah, you should start with yourself. If there is anything left over, give it to your family, then to your neighbors, relatives, and wherever there is someone in need. Distribute what is left over from your sustenance to them, give to them, so that Allah may give to you; so that goodness comes from your hands.

As we said, being a Muslim is a great blessing, a great favor from Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. Everything has value and benefit. Every minute of your life, everything you do, has a benefit; nothing goes in vain.

Without īmān and adherence to Islām, a person lives an empty life. No matter how full they think their life is, the disgraceful and evil deeds they commit will not benefit them or bring them any profit. But if they repent and enter to the way of Allah, then they will have succeeded.

يُبَدِّلُ اللَّهُ سَيِّئَاتِهِمْ حَسَنَاتٍ
<br>“Yubaddilullāhu sayyiātihim ḥasanāt”<br>
“For them Allah will replace their evil deeds with good.” (Qur’an 25:70).

That is, Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla will transform their evil deeds and sins into good deeds. Therefore, this is very important.

Allah invites people to Paradise so that they may come to the right way. He says, “Do not go to Hell.” But people strive, insisting “We want to go to Hell.” May Allah protect us and not separate us from the right way, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
