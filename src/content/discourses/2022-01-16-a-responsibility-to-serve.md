As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn.

Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam is saying, "Kullukum rā‘in, wa-kullukum mas’ūlun ‘an ra‘īyatih." All of you are responsible for those who follow you. Responsibility for them is on your shoulders. At home, the man is in charge. He is responsible. A mayor is responsible for his city. Responsibility is not an easy thing. Responsibility at home is for a family of four or five people. You can carry that responsibility. You can help them, and work for them. It is fine. However, people who ask for a big responsibility should understand its heaviness and what they are asking for. It is not an easy thing.

Why are we saying this now? We have a small place here, Cyprus. There will be elections soon. People from every corner want to participate in these elections and take on responsibility. They would go to parliament, and become a member; and then they have to serve this country. They shouldn't go to parliament for honour and reputation. If you take on a responsibility, you will be questioned for it. You can't just say, "I persuaded people and joined the parliament now." Many people don't realize what kind of spiritual responsibility they take. They should try to understand it, so that their service will be accepted, and they will fulfill that responsibility.

Their responsibility is to go there and to protect people's rights, to serve and to help people in different ways, physically and spiritually. Who is elected, takes a salary, and says, "When they are right, I won't run away. When they are not right, I will keep quiet. I will get my salary and carry on. I am famous enough to get away with it." He will be majorly in loss. He puts himself in big trouble. Because it is the biggest thing.

On the day of judgment, people who will have the hardest time are the rulers. The rulers are those who are in parliament. In old times, there was one man. We don't want to talk about which one is better and which one is worse. There was just one man and he would reign. If he was right, he would be right. If he was wrong, he would be wrong, and that was his responsibility. On the day of judgment, this person has the most difficult position. And now it is all these people. You make up a new law and oppress others. People put you there to end oppression. You were elected in order to be useful to people. But you don't do it. It is not an honour for you. It is not nice. It is a burden for you. And AllāhNone forbid, you will suffer from this burden in ākhirah. Good people speak the truth. They are not afraid of anyone. Truth is truth. And you will have a clean conscience in front of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Because of your actions, AllāhNone will reward you and write thawāb. And you will live with honour in this world. They won't say that you are capricious. If you act otherwise, you won't be useful in dunyā and you won't have any thawāb in ākhirah. You will leave with sins. May AllāhNone protect.

Everyone is talking about democracy now. Everyone thinks, "I can become a member of parliament. It is just an election. They formed new parties. They will take me in. I will do so and so." People are living in their imaginary world. Therefore, the important thing is to serve, to be right and to be with the truth. If you are not with the truth, even if you become the ruler of whole world, it won't be worth a penny in ākhirah. But when you are with the truth even if you have nothing, you will profit.

May AllāhNone help us, in shā’a Llāh. In shā’a Llāh may these people be with the truth as we said. Its benefit will be to themselves first of all. Because the truth brings barakah to every part of this country. When there is no truth, there is no barakah. They can strive as much as they want. May AllāhNone help them. May AllāhNone give them awareness. May they be with the truth, in shā’a Llāh.

Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, "Kullukum rā‘in, wa-kullukum mas’ūlun ‘an ra‘īyatih." Everybody he is like shepherd and he has a flock. and he – everybody responsible, who is on top of this. For his family, beginning from family, house – after, coming for like city or country. Who governor of this, they are responsible for their nation; under their – who are they are under them, their responsibility to look for them. And now we are living time, they say "democracy"; we are not discuss now democracy or other good or not good, but what happen, democracy, they give election every time. Every four year, five year, six year, they make election.

Now in our – this small country, where we live, they have also election. Election – is like a model for other whole countries. People – this for party people – they are going from place to place, to meet people, to tell people, "We are good, we are doing this, we will do that." This what they are going around and telling people. But they don't say, "We are asking for to be truth." No. Just say, we will do this, we will do that. And they are, don't know this is big responsibility. The biggest responsibility for who he is – he want to be MP, to be president, to be minister. It is big responsibility.

On Judgement Day, the most the people they have difficult – this people. The governor or kings or president or other, they have big responsibility and AllāhNone will ask them for what they done. After, the judge and other. But first one – this people. For this they must know, this people, first thing to do if they are want to do: To be with the truth. Because people, they trust them and they send them to on behalf of them to government place to defend their right, and to give them good thing, and to make good laws for country, and to help people with everything. But this, many of them, they are going there, and when something happen they run away. Even they are MP – nothing can touch them, nobody can harm them. They have protection – legal protection. And when something happen, some law it must be come and to be defend of people, they afraid and they run away from there.

This people, they don't have responsibility. Most of them, they don't have responsibility and they are not doing what they promised. And this is the most dangerous thing for them, first. Because they will be responsible, and they will be not good look between people because AllāhNone, HeNone don't like the one they not help people, serve people. Because, "Sayyidu l-qawmi khādimuhum," Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying. Ya‘nī, the master of people, who he is serve them. This is mentality of Islām, and they must look for this. If they have this intention, AllāhNone will reward them. And what they are doing, it will be good for them. This responsibility come out from them. But if not doing, they will be responsible and they will punished also in Yawmu l-Qiyāmah. And there will be no barakah for them or for their country. This is what happen in whole world.

With this, what we will do? They don't know. Only they like to be in special position; MP, minister, president – this is what they like. They forget other. You can be MP, you can be other also but if you are brave and with right, everybody will be respect you, even your enemy. But if you are not like this, nobody respect you and you be like clown for enemy.

AllāhNone help us, AllāhNone make this people to understand this. Everywhere – not only here, everywhere in world. This is advice for them, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
