As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
إِنَّ الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا وَعَمِلُوا الصَّالِحَاتِ كَانَتْ لَهُمْ جَنَّاتُ الْفِرْدَوْسِ نُزُلًا
<br>“Inna l-ladhīna āmanū wa ʿamilū ṣ-ṣāliḥāti kānat lahum jannātu l-firdawsi nuzulā”<br>
“Indeed, those who have believed and done righteous deeds - they will have the Gardens of Paradise as a lodging.” (Qurʼān 18:107). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

We are now in the last Fajr of this trip [Malaysia]. Inshā’Allāh, we will be coming again. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla is giving believers good tidings that they will be together forever. This is our aim, our happiness. We are now going back. Next time will maybe be next year or after two years; we don’t know. When are we forever together? Inshā’Allāh, in Jannah we will be together forever; no separation, no sadness.

Alḥamdulillāh we are happy. But of course, there’s a little bit sadness because will be away from you. But inshā’Allāh - maybe the body is far but the heart is alḥamdulillāh together. This is the important thing. For the heart to be with Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla, with Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, with mashāyikh, with believers, inshā’Allāh. This is the important thing. Because you cannot be the whole time by body with anyone. But by heart, you can be together in their heart.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says,<br>
ما وَسِعَني أرضي ولا سَمائي، ولكنْ وَسِعَني قَلبُ عبدي المُؤمِنِ
<br>“Mā wasiʿanī arḍī wa-lā samā'ī, wa-lākin wasiʿanī qalbu ʿabdī al-mu'min”<br>
“Indeed my earth and my heavens could not contain Me but the heart of my believing servant can.” [al-Ghazālī, Iḥyā ʿulūm al-dīn]

Of course, we cannot say there’s a place for Allah. But He said, “Nothing, no place can contain Me. Only one place: the heart of the mu’min, believer.”

So this is very important. Alḥamdulillāh we must be all the time with Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla in our heart and on His way. If you love Allah and our Prophet, you love the believer, mu’min. If you don’t love Allah, nothing can be good for you. You are like a stone. May Allah open our hearts. Inshā’Allāh we see you many times again and again.

Inshā’Allāh may we meet with Mahdi ʿalayhi s-salām soon; we be together, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
