As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Huwa lladhī ja‘ala sh-shamsa ḍiyā’an wa-l-qamara nūran wa-qaddarahū manāzila li-ta‘lam ‘adada s-sinīna wa-l-ḥisāb” (10:5). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l- ‘aẓīm. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla created the moon and the sun for us to know the months and years, and to know your calculation. Every day, it begins and ends as a new day. After that, the week begins and ends. The month begins and ends. The year begins and ends. Thanks to AllahNone, those who worship must do so for the pleasure of AllahNone, so that it does not go to waste. And today is the last day of this year, in shā’a Llāh. It is the end of Dhū l-Ḥijjah and the Hijrī year.

Tomorrow is the first of Muḥarram. What we call tomorrow starts with the evening prayer. When the sun sets, the day is over. According to the Hijrī calendar, a new day begins in the evening. Therefore, our new year begins this evening with the call to prayer, marking the start of the new Hijrī year. May it be full of barakah and blessed, in shā’a Llāh. Because it has been nearly one thousand four hundred fifty years since the Hijrah of our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam.

Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says, “The distance between meNone and Qiyāmah is very near.” Therefore, this world will not last forever. Most of the time, people do things thinking they will last forever. What will last are your good deeds, your charity, your obedience to AllāhNone, and your acts of worship. Those will last forever. It will last till eternity. People do things in this world as they like. It is of no importance. Everyone says what he likes and does as he likes in this world. He will give his account in the Hereafter. It will not be unquestioned.

We should seek refuge in AllāhNone and not do bad things. We should do good things. May our lives pass in goodness and worship for the pleasure of AllāhNone, in shā’a Llāh. Otherwise, we will have a hard time. Qiyāmah is near. That is certain. It is the promise of Allah ‘Azza wa-Jalla in Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n. “Iqtarabati s-sā‘atu” (54:1). HeNone says, “Qiyāmah is near.” It is even nearer now. It is approaching day by day. And how should one prepare himself for Qiyāmah? He can prepare by being on the path of AllāhNone, by not doing evil, and by performing acts of worship. Worship brings benefit to him. It brings no benefit to Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. “I did my worship for AllāhNone, not for myself.” It is not like this. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla doesn't need it. You are in need of that worship. Therefore, as we said, Qiyāmah is near. Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam had spoken even more clearly and beautifully. “The Qiyāmah of every person is his death.” Because when they die, they will open their eyes in the Hereafter. They may live for a thousand years or have died ten thousand years ago; it does not matter. They will turn into dust.

On Judgment Day, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla will create bones, flesh, blood, veins, nerves, and everything from that earth, and you will rise on the Day of Resurrection just as you are now. It will not matter then if you stayed for ten years, a hundred years, or a thousand years. What will matter there is if you have done good deeds. Nevertheless, there is an end for them in this world so that they can understand. Everything has its time. And the time is near. Only big signs have remained. All small signs have occurred. May AllāhNone protect us all.

May HeNone protect us from all evil and the evil of our egos above all. Because the biggest enemy of people is, as our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says, “A‘dá ‘aduwwaka nafsuka llatī bayna janbayk – Your biggest enemy is your ego.” Even if you do good, it strives to ruin it. When you do something good and think to yourself, “What a good deed I have done. No one else can do this, but I did,” repent and ask for forgiveness immediately. And if you say it out loud, you will be boasting about it, and that has no value.

May AllāhNone protect us. May AllāhNone grant good years to the Islamic world, in shā’a Llāh. May it be good for humanity, in shā’a Llāh. Because humanity is not rising lately, but is instead going down and becoming lower. It is going towards disgrace, not just down. The lower they are, the more accepted they are by people now. Therefore, may AllāhNone protect us during this time. May AllāhNone send the saviour, in shā’a Llāh. May this year be the year of goodness and the year of salvation for humanity, in shā’a Llāh.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla say, “Wa-sakhkhara lakumu sh-shamsa wa-l-qamara dā’ibayn” (14:33). “Li-ta‘lamū ‘adada s-sinīna wa-l-ḥisāb” (10:5). Meaning to, AllāhNone create sun and moon for to know time and collection, how to collect, count, counting. All this with wisdom of AllāhNone. The people who know this knowledge, they know better than us. But it is meaning – these two creations of AllāhNone, moon and sun – they have time, years, different from each other. But all this for benefit of human being. We can use both of them. But now today, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are using this day, last day of year of – one thousand four hundred forty-six, it will be. Forty-five finish this, and forty-six beginning time of moon calendar from Maghrib time, not from midnight or twelve o'clock. Twelve o'clock for Islāmic calendar – it is Maghrib time. Every time, old system of timing. Still, they say there is timing in calendar for this.

Beginning from twelve o'clock. Twelve o'clock, when sun go, sunset. On sunset beginning new day. So now today, until after maybe six hour, it will be new year for us, for Islāmic calendar, which it is for our religion thing very important. Also, other we can use because also AllāhNone said you can use sun calendar and moon calendar. But moon calendar it is the most important for Muslim. And Muslim, they are following this for, as we said yesterday, for fasting, for ‘Īd, for ḥajj, for zakāh, for everything. You following this.

But nowadays, there is also some fitnah people, they try to change this to make it in sun calendar. But it is not, cannot be. It's impossible. They do this only to – AllāhNone make them to appear, the hypocrite and non-believer, what they are doing. But for us important, this year. We are hoping good with this year. When you enter some new thing, new house, you wear something new, you make du‘ā’ to be good, blessed.

So, we are also for – we are coming to new year, we ask from AllāhNone bless for us, for Islām, for whole human being. Because Muslim, they want good for others. Who are following Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, whole time they trying to give humanity good thing, not bad thing.

In Islām Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, “Lā ḍarara wa-lā ḍirār – No harm and no to harm anybody.” Don’t make something to harm. This is the rule of Islām. But for other, the whole time trying to find something to harm people because they thought if this people there, it will be less thing for them. When this people less, they will be more happy. They will be more better. For this whole time, they are envious and they are trying to harm people. For this our religion, which it is Islām, whole Prophets'None religion, calling for peace, real peace. Not like this.

Nowadays people they saying peace but they are not meaning this. They making opposite, they make oppression, they making every bad thing for humanity. We are asking Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla from this new year to be good for us because it is time for end of time. It is near, very near. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla also in Qur’ān saying, “Iqtarabati s-sā‘ah” (54:1). Meaning: time is approach, coming nearer, coming near. And we must be careful. We must do as much we can do good thing because Qiyāmah for everybody – Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, “If near or not near, for everybody when he close his eyes, he die – it is his Qiyāmah.” Meaning: Day of Resurrection. Because when they close his eyes, if he stay in grave ten years, hundred years, thousand years, he will be dust.

When time coming for, to come again for life and to give –Day of Resurrection, it will be everybody appear in this day and coming for answering what they done, what they do. For this, it is important for people to be ready for Qiyāmah from now. If it is near or not near, for everybody it is near. No, no problem. But time, what Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, it is nearly everything ready for the end of time. Because there is time for everything, for this world also. Time is nearly finish. AllāhNone help us, keep us safe from every badness, from every, especially from our ego badness, the most worst one, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
