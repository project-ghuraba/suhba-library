As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

May these two feasts, the feast of Jumʿah and ʿĪd al-Aḍḥā be blessed. May Allah make them perpetual. May we always have beautiful days, inshā’Allāh. 

Our Holy Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam says in ḥadīth sharīf that we read in khuṭbah,<br>
المؤمنُ القويُّ خيرٌ وأحبُّ إلى اللهِ من المؤمنِ الضعيفِ
<br>“Al-mu’minu l-qawiyyu khayrun wa aḥabbu ilā Llāhi mina l-mu’mini ḍ-ḍaʿīf.”<br>
“A believer who is strong is better and dearer to Allah than the weak believer.” Aw kamā qāl. 

The one who believes, the one who is strong and good is the one who is beneficial. Who is beneficial to other people is a more beloved person in the sight of Allah. He is a better person. Because when one is strong and helps people, and removes badness from them, he must do that. Not everyone can do that. There is virtue to those who do so, says Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla.

Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam says they are the people beloved by Allah. Therefore, we should strive. If you can’t do it physically, if you can’t support with your body, do as much as you can. Helping the weak, helping the poor, there are many types of help. It is not only one kind of help.

Therefore, people should improve themselves as much as they can. If there are opportunities for them, they must evaluate them. The Ḥadīth goes on with, “They should ask for goodness.” Our Holy Prophet says to ask for more goodness. Therefore, a strong believer should evaluate when opportunities arise to become stronger, says our Holy Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam.

And then when you do something, don’t say “If only,” says our Holy Prophet. What Allah wanted has happened. We can’t change anything with “If only.” It is over now. You must leave it, and look ahead. No need to look back with sorrow and get upset. That’s happened. What Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla wanted happened. That is the destiny of Allah. No need to be upset about it. You should take lesson from that. If not, you’ll be sorry and longing; saying “If only.”

Our Holy Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam says “If only” is the door of shayṭān. Shayṭān puts it upon people to say “If only I did so and so. If only I took that. If only I went there. If only I came here. If only I did this.” Every word of our Holy Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam is precious and shows the way to people. And it is a great thing among people. Everyone says “If only.” Sometimes they go to extremes with it.

Therefore, for people not to be upset and not to lose their balance of mind, he says not to do it. Shayṭān doesn’t wish you goodness. He wishes you bad. And that is one of shayṭān’s doors. Therefore, beware of doing so.

Āyati karīmah says,<br>
فَتَوَكَّلْ عَلَى اللَّهِ ۖ إِنَّكَ عَلَى الْحَقِّ الْمُبِينِ
<br>“Fa-tawakkal ʿalā Llāhi innaka ʿalā l-ḥaqqi l-mubīn”<br>
“So rely upon Allah ; indeed, you are upon the clear truth.” (Qur’ān 27:79)

Rely upon Allah, says in āyati karīmah. You are on the right way, on the clearly true way. Don’t be upset about other things. Listen to these beautiful words of our Holy Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. Obey them. You will be successful and happy. You will be happy here and hereafter.

May Allah give it to all of us, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
