May the support of the saints reach us, our shaykh's support reach us. May their support be with us. A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. We need their support to expel shayṭān. May their support be present. Don't forget to ask for support.

Possessors of support can move mountains. There is power. Power isn't absent. There is power. Ask. They will reach you according to what you intend. Don't try to do something that is out of reach. Ask according to your power limits. Ask from the saints. Say: "I intend this service, give support. I want support." They'll look. What is it that you want to do? If it is for your ego, they will leave you. If it is for Allāh (jj), if it is for Allāh (jj), they are ordered to help you. If you want to do something for Allāh (jj) and ask help from Allāh's saints, all saints are obliged to help. If you intend something for the Sulṭān, governor of that city, district governor, administrator, judge, pasha, all will help because you intend something in the name of the sulṭān. When you do something for Allāh (jj), when you ask help from the saints, saints are obliged to help you. They are responsible.

If we sit here to advertise, promote ourselves, they won't help. When we ask for weapons - to imprison our egos, to use against enemies, they give it. When we ask for arms, they give it. If we ask which way leads to the enemies, they show us the way. This is important Haji Efendi. Do for Allāh (jj). Ask for help. When Allāh (jj) hears, the Prophet (saw) also hears. The Prophet (saw) asks: "Who does this servant belong to from the saints?" "He belongs to this person." "Serve this one because he intends to serve for Allāh (jj). Help him." Allāh (jj) won't leave His servant without support or a supporter when he is serving for Allāh (jj).

Shayṭān approaches those, who don't serve for Allāh. "Come with me. Come to our assembly. There are no conditions. Everything is free. Everything is free here." Shayṭān deceives like that. Everything is free in shayṭān's way. Discipline isn't needed in shayṭān's way. People, who come to us, entering from our door, who accept us, our door is open day and night. And there is support. What kind of support do you want? You offer something. Shayṭān says; "Don't look at this offer. You won't benefit from this. But I'll show you the way so you'll get a lot of benefit." Shayṭān has so many skills. It learned perfectly to confuse Ādam's sons. To ruin the sons of Ādam (as), to put them in hell, it has thousands of tricks and traps.

It says: "Come to me brother, I'll train you." If you are deceived and follow it - from one trouble to the next trouble it will carry you. May Allāh (jj) protect us. If you give your nail, it will catch your finger. If you give your finger, it will catch your wrist. If you give your wrist, it will catch your arm. Once it holds your arm, it will catch you by the neck. If it grabs your neck, it immediately jumps on you. Don't let it jump on you! Don't be shayṭān's donkey. This is Turkish. Burro. Burri. Burro means donkey in Spanish. Don't be shayṭān's donkey! See. Once it jumps on the donkey, it whips and takes you directly to a minefield. What is a minefield? It takes you to the field, where it is free to commit sins. May Allāh (jj) protect us.

It says "Enjoy as you like, play, have fun." He says; "I don't know what to do, how to do." "Come my donkey. I'll ride you. I'll make you run in such a way you'll be happy and who sees you will be amazed." It rides and goes around. A minefield is the field of sins. "Eat this, drink this, go there, do this, say this" - it gives directions. Once it captures you, you have to do what it says. If it captures you, you'll do whatever it tells you. 20th century people are captured by shayṭān, shayṭān captured them. They say this and that.

Don't be deceived - "20th century people have freedom". It would be better if they were cart horses. "They are free" - It means they left the order of Allāh and Rasūlu Llāh (saw). They left the discipline of Islām and belief. They became "free, liberated". Then shayṭān says: "I got rid of them, come let us be together. Let's take our freedom. Nobody cares, bothers us. We'll do what we like. Nobodys asks." There are people, who ally themselves with shayṭān. That's why, be careful.
