As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Today is, in shā’a Llāh, the twenty-seventh night. The Islamic world and all Muslims celebrate Laylatu l-Qadr tonight. As they don't know which date it is, they celebrate it on the twenty-seventh. They ask for its barakah. AllāhNone, in shā’a Llāh, gives its barakah. Laylatu l-Qadr is one of the holiest nights. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla has granted it to the ummah.

Laylatu l-Qadr has many characteristics. One of them is that Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n was sent down in this night. Of course, Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n did not come all at once. In the presence of AllahNone, the secret of Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n is astonishing. People cannot grasp it fully. It is the word of AllāhNone. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla sent it to humankind, and related it specifically to our Holy ProphetNone, as the last revelation. And HeNone has put everything that is useful for Islām as a whole, and for humankind, in Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n. It is very valuable. It was sent down on this night, Laylatu l-Qadr. And it was completed step by step over twenty-three years, as a gift to the Ummah.

Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n is the ancient Word. The Word of AllāhNone. People who philosophise about this are just arguing semantics, and want to weaken people's īmān. That won't happen unless Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla wants it. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Innā naḥnu nazzalnā dh-dhikra wa-innā lahū la-ḥāfiẓūn" (15:9). "WeNone sent Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n. And WeNone will protect it," Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla has said. Therefore, by the leave of AllahNone, all these shayṭāns and everything around cannot do a thing. Reading Qur’ān is healing. It is a means for all kinds of goodness. Our Holy ProphetNone says Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n is Shāfi‘un Mushaffa‘. It is a cure. It intercedes for us, and becomes an intercession for others. And it is healing. All goodness is in Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n, in the Word of AllāhNone. Reading it during these nights, reading it any time, is a means to goodness for people. It is a solution for all problems, in shā’a Llāh.

May AllāhNone bless this night for all of us. Even when someone listens to Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n, it is muqābalah. All people present attain its merit. When someone reads and the rest listen, they reach the same virtue. This is from the barakah of the Qur’ān. May AllāhNone bless these nights and days, for all of us, in shā’a Llāh. Today twenty-sixth, tomorrow twenty-seventh. In this night, usually, Islamic world, Muslim people, they celebrate as Laylatu l-Qadr. Which, exactly we don't know when it is. But because they don't know, they cannot... They just put this night of twenty-seventh of Ramaḍān to celebrate. This is a custom for whole Muslims. And it is a good custom, not bad, because they are hoping to... and respecting this night. It is good.

But normally, we don't know when. But if you don't know, you don't – you mustn't ignore it. You must remember. This for people to remember this. They, ‘ulamā’ and old people, they was celebrating twenty-seventh, most of them. And this night, it is a very blessed night for Muslim people. There are many things in this night. One of them also, AllāhNone, HeNone gave Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n for Ummat Muḥammad ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, in this night.

Qur’ān, it is Word of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. It is not created. It is only... we don't... many people they misunderstand. They thought it is coming like this. No. What we read, it is Qur’ān. But reality, it is Word of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Not created. AllāhNone not like us. For this, this Qur’ān AllāhNone ‘Azza wa-Jalla sent in night of Qadr. And in twenty-three years, slowly, slowly completed. So it is pure, from the Divine Presence of AllāhNone. This for human being. Whole human being must respect and to take from knowledge. Because in Qur’ān, there is knowledge from beginning to end. And knowledge was – people they don't know, even now. They will not know, also, this knowledge. But they say, trying to learn and learn and learn. It is nothing compared what knowledge in Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n.

People... some people even they are not believer, they make researching for Qur’ān, what they have knowledge. And they – everybody can take, no problem you are believer or not believer. If you look carefully for Qur’ān, you can have benefit. Many benefit for human being. For whole human being, not only for Muslim. It is sent for whole human being, you can take as much you can take. Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying for Qur’ān, praising Qur’ān, this for ākhirah also, Shāfi‘un Mushaffa‘. And for everything good – for health, for richness, for everything, Qur’ān help you. Help to be safe from hell, to save from bad thing, in shā’a Llāh also. It is very valuable.

The people, most of people not thinking only reading Qur’ān it is for Muslim people. For non-Muslim also, can benefit. AllāhNone when HeNone sends something – for whole human being. Like provision, for whole human being, for rain for all of human. Everything. But who take this and respect and accept, he has more benefit. Who only looking up from what they will find something good for life, they can, for this life. For other life also, who want to have benefit, both side. Some of them one side they have benefit. Some of them, for here and hereafter. AllāhNone make us to have benefit whole time – to respect, to make us to read and understand reality. And AllāhNone give us barakah and strong īmān with Qur’ān, in shā’a Llāh.

Mubārak in shā’a Llāh. This night also AllāhNone give this barakah, in shā’a Llāh, real barakah of Laylatu l-Qadr, for us this night, in shā’a Llāh. Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
