Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

“Wala tahinu wala tahzanu wa’antumul a’lawna in kuntum mu’mineen.” (Sura Aal Imran:139) Allah Azza wa Jalla says: “Do not grieve!” Meaning do not distress yourselves. “You are superior if you are true believers.” Allah (JJ) was addressing our Holy Prophet (SAW) but the Koran is for everybody. The greatest good tiding of our Holy Prophet (SAW) is the Koran, great in glory. The Koran is Allah’s word. It applies to everybody until the Day of Judgment. The Koran, azimush sha’n, includes advice for everybody and all benefits are in it. It is both advice and healing.

You are here as a minority. There are other nations in this land too. Do not feel oppressed or inferior. Allah Azza wa Jalla says: “A Mu’min is always superior.” We need to cast off this disease from inside us. You do not need to imitate anybody. We have millions of scholars, saints, and heros. This is advice for the people here.

Another advice: there is no need whatsoever for you to go and appreciate the Wahhabi just because he is Arab. You are a Mu’min. Your scholar is not Arab or Wahhabi. They came out of your nation. You are a Mu’min so never see yourself as inferior. Be thankful for Allah’s blessings. You have received the greatest blessing. There is no need to imitate the Arab or the Ajam and go after them.

This is a Jewish game - I am saying it directly - nothing else. A Mu’min does not let others tread on oneself. They are causing discord amongst Muslims. They cannot stomach faithful people being on top. Iman is topmost.

Bismillahir Rahmanir Rahim.

“Qaalatil a’rabu amanna, qullam tu’minu walaakin qulu aslamna,” said Allah Azza wa Jalla in the Koran (Sura Hujurat:14). When there is Islam everybody is Muslim. But iman is different. Our Holy Prophet (SAW) taught it and showed the way. Following him the companions, and following them the scholars and saints showed those ways. Millions of awliya, ulama, and sultans came. They would all cringe of the awliya. They all reached high stations in the sight of Allah (JJ). Those who went astray are forgotten and gone.

Therefore, let the people in this country not be fooled. One is not superior because one is Arab. If they do not respect Hazrat Prophet (SAW) they are never superior. The way of tariqa is the way of the Prophet (SAW). Hazrat Prophet (SAW), the awliya, and the mother and father are respected on the way of tariqa. The mother and father are obeyed, and the children and the wife are treated with tenderness.

There is a hadith of our Holy Prophet (SAW). He told the sahabis to pay so much attention to the neighbors that some of them thought he was going to give them inheritance rights. What these ones are teaching is only denigrating Islam and nothing else. May Allah guide those who are to be guided. For those who are not to be guided, Allah will account them Himself. However, our purpose is to show the right path to our children and kinfolk. The person who is not on the right path would fall for them and be ruined.

It is said, “Shaitan is the shaykh of those who do not have a shaykh.” Here, this is what is going on. This is why they have animosity towards shaykhs, since they are obstructing them. Allah Azza wa Jalla sent hundreds of thousands of scholars and shaykhs. He will send more until Judgment Day. Therefore, they can try as much as they like. This nation will not be fooled by them. They have a vindication to this. Once in a while some people are fooled, but that is a different matter. Essentially though, as a nation you are a respectful people. You are people who love Allah, the Prophet, and the awliya.

May Allah keep you safe from Shaitan’s fitna. May He make our children to become good children Inshallah. May they receive Allah’s, Hazrat Prophet’s, and the awliya’s duas Inshallah.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq.

Al-Fatiha.
