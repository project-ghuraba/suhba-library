As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is saying: “Fa-‘tabirū yā-ulī l-abṣār” (59:2). “Fa-‘tabirū yā-ulī l-albāb.” HeNone says to take lessons. Learn lessons from what you see. People with hearts who understand take a lesson. Our life is a lesson. And the lives of people before are also lessons, for mankind to correct themselves, to be on the right way, to be from good people and to be with good people in ākhirah.

Because in the stories of all prophets, since Ādam ‘alayhi s-salām until this time, there have always been good and bad ones. The good are those who are on the way of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, who are with the prophets and pious people. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is praising them. Who are with them will be saved. Who love them will be saved as well. And opposing them are people who are with shayṭān, who are enemies of AllāhNone. They are decrying and cursing. Those who are with them lose during their lives. They lose in their real life, in ākhirah. So when they enter hell, they will swear at those who put them in such a condition. They will beg for them to be given double the punishment, and double the suffering. But the latter will say, “You shouldn’t have followed us.” This is a lesson. This lesson has been the same for thousands of years. People should take a lesson. Clever people learn the lesson. They live their lives in obedience to AllāhNone, with people beloved by AllāhNone. Those who don’t take a lesson are always with people who are cursed, who manifest the punishment, wrath, and condemnation of AllāhNone. Who go along with them are always in loss. And they make others lose.

Therefore, taking a lesson is important. People can take lessons from their own lives, from how life passes by instantly. How acquaintances and other people, people who shake the ground, cannot control themselves in the end. They themselves shake and fall into a pitiful condition. People should take a lesson from this as well. Taking lessons and examples...

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla tells mankind the goal is to take a lesson and to come to the right way. But in the end they say, “He was like this. That is how it happened.” He is not like this. That is how it was. You could become just like them. This world will not remain to anyone. May AllāhNone protect us. May we take a lesson, in shā’a Llāh. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying: Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Fa-‘tabirū yā-ulī l-abṣār” (59:2). “Fa-‘tabirū yā-ulī l-albāb.” Many place in the Qur’ān, AllāhNone mentioning these verses, to take lesson, to from this what you see, what you hear, what you understand, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying for human being. We – not only for Muslim, because Muslim maybe not majority in the world – but other also, they must take lesson from life, from history. From what they see, what they hear, about the life of the human being. About life of other nations, their nations. All this, AllāhNone give lesson for people.

Why? To be with good people. Because, this since thousands of years, from beginning of Ādam ‘alayhi s-salām until now, every time, we see good people and bad people. Good people, who are the best, the most of good people. This is prophets and who are following them. This is good people. AllāhNone praise them, AllāhNone love them. HeNone said, who will be with this people, AllāhNone love them also and praise them, and encourage people every time, “O ayyuhā n-nās, O people, all... you be with good people, don’t be with bad people.”

The bad people, other side, who AllāhNone curse them, AllāhNone not love them, not like them. All time they are fighting against AllāhNone and against good people. Because, they are... Many of them, they claim they are god. They are... “Worship me,” they said. Some of them, they killing people who not worshipping them. But this people, they was cursed and everywhere – in Qur’ān or in Tawrāh or Bible – everywhere they are cursed. And not praised at all. Nobody praise for bad people. Maybe bad people they are praise themselves, but normally for good people, for human being, for humanity, they are not good people. Because they are oppressor and only they have for their ego, egoist, from top of the egoist, to claim they are god.

Now there is many people like this, but they cannot say, “Worship me.” – “Love me, all people must love me.” This is what they said. Because now, fashion to be atheist. They say, “No God.” So, if they was like old time, many of them, I think, they will claim they are god. “Worship me,” they will say. But, this only want people to be – show, to be famous, to be everybody following them, what they said, what they giving bad instructions, bad teaching for people. They want this people to follow them, and to be obligatory to follow them. This people not praised one. This people, cursed one.

They must take lesson from old life, old people life, and even from our life also. We are, we was young, we was child, we was baby, we was youngster, teenager, quickly this finish. And there is people, they were very strong people. You see them in your life, giving everybody fright from them. After little while, you surprised. “What happened to this man?” He was shaking this world and now he shakes. Nobody can help him. This is all example or lesson for who has mind. “Ūlī l-albāb” meaning, who has mind, who has good thinking. You must think about this, not to follow this bad people. We must be correct ourselves, not to be happy with our ego, ourselves. If we are with, all time, try to be with Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. With people who AllāhNone love them. Because other people, when what they will do, who follow other people, they will be in hell. And in hell, AllāhNone describe this in Qur’ān, they will say, “Oh our Lord, give this people double punishment. Because, they are take us to this place. We follow them and they bring them to hell, give them double punishment.” But the other said, “Why you follow me? Why you saying this? You complaining.” This is very bad situation. AllāhNone save us from this end, and to be with good people, in shā’a Llāh, with prophets and awliyā’u Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
