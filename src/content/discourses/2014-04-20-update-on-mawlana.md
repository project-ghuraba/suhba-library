Subhanim Allah Sultanim Allah Nabim Muhammad Alayhi's-Salam Subhanim Allah Sultanim Allah Nabim Muhammad Alayhi's-Salam Subhanim Allah Sultanim Allah Nabim Muhammad Alayhi's-Salam Alayhi's-Salam Alayhi's-Salam Alayhi's-Salam Alayhi's-Salam.

Salamu alaykum wa rahmatullahi ta'ala wa barakatuhu. This is salam from our beloved Mawlana Shaykh Muhammad Nazim al-Haqqani for all the murids in the world, for all the lover of Mawlana Shaykh in the world. For the one been seen and one not been seen. We are doing insha Allah good announcement about Mawlana Shaykh health and what is going on in this moment.

First of all I like to start with Mawlana Shaykh personality. Mawlana Shaykh he is 92 years old in one day time. Tomorrow his birthday and we are hoping that Mawlana Shaykh he will have the second spring in his life, insha Allah. This is, we are saying, why we are saying this? We are saying this because the prayers coming from all of you to Mawlana Shaykh start working and your love to Mawlana Shaykh is make him to come back to us. This is, we have to be knowing this and we have to understand this. It is important for us that Mawlana Shaykh he love us, so as much as we pray for him, this is as much as we can keep him here.

Now I like to get some briefing about the situation of Mawlana Shaykh. Mawlana Shaykh, normally if he was ordinary person, I am not wishing to say it, but, we all of us, we been very sad for today and we been losing Mawlana Shaykh from this world, but Alhamdulillah that Mawlana Shaykh he love us so much and he is caring about us so much, he fighting to be here and he is insisting to be with his murid and he is our, how to say, he is responding to our du'a. That our du'a is nothing, but all of us, I believe that he is not only my father, he is the father for all of us.

There is no discrimination and I know we are sharing Mawlana Shaykh beauty and we are sharing Mawlana Shaykh life. Mawlana Shaykh life, it is for us life, Mawlana Shaykh breathing, is for us breathing, Mawlana Shaykh happiness, is our happiness, Mawlana Shaykh illness, it's our illness. And we are asking from Allah Almighty, Ya Rabbi, remain our Shaykh for us, maybe we can give up from so many things but we cannot give up from him. This is very important in our life to not give up our Shaykh.

And I know the people they are worried and I know everybody worried about Mawlana Shaykh and I know everybody heart is here. Maybe you don't have the possibility to come, the possibility to get the correct news, you don't get the possibility to get things that it is very complicated things. So now I am telling you the pure news from Mawlana Shaykh official side, insha Allah from his mouth to not worry about him. Maybe he is up and down, by now he is in ICU and he is improving, Alhamdulillah, and he is much co-operating with the medication and he is like, if somebody else will never co-operate, but Alhamdulillah, Mawlana Shaykh he is giving us good tiding, and he is giving us the way that, to be with us.

This you have to understand and also you have to be clear by saying Ya Rabbi, we are looking for Mawlana Shaykh to be here as we are not having any other interest. We want Mawlana Shaykh with the pure intention, we want Mawlana Shaykh to be with us with the pure intention that he is our Shaykh, he is our crown, he is our everything! And we don't want to lose our crown. Maybe, there is some people they can get on but they cannot be Mawlana Shaykh, because Mawlana Shaykh he is the only one that he can carry our burden and he is the only one that Allah Almighty He give him such a power that he love his murid more than anything.

And you have to know that Mawlana Shaykh he is the one loving us, and he is the one caring about us and he sacrifice from his maqam to be with us in this miserable dunya-life. I call it miserable dunya-life because there is no taste in it, and there is no enjoyment here, and there is only suffering this is the area for suffering. It is the testing area and Mawlana Shaykh keep remaining because of his murid, because of du'a of his murid.

Now I want to thank you for your understanding and I want to thank you for your du'a and I want to thank you about what Allah Almighty He grant us and I want you to appreciate what Allah Almighty He give us, and I want you also to know that Mawlana Shaykh he is alive, Alhamdulillah, and he is alive and he is remaining alive for us, Alhamdulillah. And I wish to keep giving you good news today, tomorrow, after tomorrow and maybe 100 years insha'Allah about Mawlana Shaykh.

With this one I say Ya Rabbi, make us to be his servant make us to be in his feet and make us to be under his feet that to serve him by our love, by our sincere and I am believing with the sincerity of all of you. It is important. Today is the day of sincerity. Please be sincere to Mawlana Shaykh. Please don't listen for so many rumours going around. Don't listen to anything, just concentrate your heart for the sincerity of Mawlana Shaykh, for the sake of Mawlana Shaykh. Salamu alaykum.

Wa min Allahi tawfiq bi hurmati l-Habib, bi hurmati l-Fatiha.
