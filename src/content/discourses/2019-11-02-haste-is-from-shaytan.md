Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Allah Azza wa Jalla says in the Holy Qur’an: ‘اوُنيَّ َبَتَف’, ‘Investigate.’ (Qur’ān 49:06), meaning be sure. When you are going to do a work, when you are going to do something, when you are going to work with a person, do your research. Or when you say to someone, “This person told me this. That one told me that”, one word changes everything.

Most people get fooled. They are fooled with two words, and then they are regretful. That’s why Allah Azza wa Jalla says, Research, look, don’t be fooled, don’t be deceived and don’t be harmed. In all respects, in the affairs of both this world and the hereafter. Of course, the hereafter is more important. Be sure to research and don’t say, “This man tricked me.” This man tricked you, but why were you in a hurry? Why did you get tricked without researching, looking and asking? Research. Haste is from shaytan.

One of the things they say is that he snatched it away. He will rush you, then he will trick you and run away. If you are deceived by him and you follow him, then it is your fault. A Muslim should not be deceived. He should research everything he does, because human beings follow their egos. You shouldn’t trust your ego for a minute, not even for a second. Therefore, be careful and don’t be fooled. Don’t be fooled, because to be fooled means to be fooled by a bad thing. One does not get fooled by good things; he knows. May Allah ﷻ protect us. May Allah ﷻ protect us from the shaytans of people and the shaytans of the Jinn. May they not deceive us insha’Allah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
