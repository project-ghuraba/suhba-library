Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīn wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

One of the holy ones once said, "Anta turīd wa ana urīd waLlāhu yaf‘alu mā yurīd." "You ask for something, I ask too, and AllāhNone does as HeNone likes." It is not according to your or my wish. Whatever AllāhNone wants, it will be so. Who follows the will of AllāhNone is in comfort, peaceful and happy. They say "I wanted this and I couldn't do it." A man suffers and says, "If only I could do it". It can't be so. What AllāhNone says will happen. But the saying of our ProphetNone, "Lā yum’inu aḥadukum ḥattá yakūna hawāhu taba‘an li-hawā’ī." Aw kamā qāl. Our ProphetNone is saying: "A man doesn't have true īmān unless he loves what INone love. He can't have real īmān". And this, related to the will of AllāhNone is a good advice for people so that they are not sad.

What AllāhNone wants happens. And we have to try to do things our ProphetNone loved and wanted in order to have īmān. Who has īmān submits to AllāhNone. He surrenders to the wish of AllāhNone and accepts it. He won't do the opposite of what AllāhNone says. If there is something he doesn't like, he won't persist and push his opinion. A man can push his opinion as much as he likes, it has no benefit. He doesn't earn thawāb either. He becomes unhappy. He becomes sad. AllāhNone has shown us the good way. HeNone has shown the easy way. When what AllāhNone wants happens, you should say "How nice! What AllāhNone wants happened. And we wanted this". This is it in short.

There is big awliyā’ saying: AllāhNone "Anta turīd wa ana urīd waLlāhu yaf‘alu mā yurīd." I want something, you also want something but Allāh HeNone do what HeNone want. So this is for everybody. It is golden advice to not be unhappy, to not be sad, to not be worried what I done, it is not happen. What happen - what Allāh HeNone want to be happen it happen. You must be happy with this. If you are not accept this you will be not happy. You cannot do anything against this also because it is happen also. What happened is happened. So you must be satisfied with AllāhNone.

And ProphetNone saying "Lā yum’inu aḥadukum ḥattá yakūna hawāhu taba‘an li-hawā’ī." Aw kamā qāl. As said, nobody can be real believer until his will, what he like, what INone like - he must be like what I like, what I happy with this. If you are not happy with what ProphetNone bring you, nothing good for you. If you are happy, you will be real believer and accept what AllāhNone done, this is coming from Allāh. This is the real belief, this is what we accept. If you are not like this you say, I wish it was be like this, I wish like be this. So you're, you be your envious. Your this thought make you burn from inside, destroy you and destroy your life. And even in ākhirah also you will be not in high position because you are not believer and the real believer they are most highest station. They are the higher station, they are near ProphetNone. With Anbiyā’u Llāh, with Awliyā’, with Ṣaḥābah they will be because ṣaḥābah all they was like this. They are accepting and obeying what ProphetNone bring and they was happy. This is "happy century" - they are saying for century of ProphetNone.

Why they are happy? There was whole hungry and no clothes, nothing. They very poor but they was very happy. This is whole, from Adām ‘alayhi s-salām until qiyāmah, the most happiest century with ProphetNone because they are near ProphetNone, they are near the highest belief. ProphetNone give them and they accept everything. This is what we, in our days people, not accepting anything. If not accept, you are free, you can be miserable. If you accept you be happy, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq al-Fātiḥah.
