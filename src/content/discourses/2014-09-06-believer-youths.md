Assalamu Alaykum,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

May Allah make us meet good people. May we always be with good ones Inshallah. Humankind are like wild animals. People certainly need a guide (murshid) so the person disciplines oneself. Otherwise, the person would be beaten by his ego. Humankind go after their ego. The ego likes comfort. It inclines towards whatever evil there may be.

There are many people who follow their ego these days. Because their ego wants that evil. And when these people get what they want, the nation runs after them. Of course, these things are harder when a person is young. Most people are defeated by their ego. Young believers (mumins) who obey Allah and go after the murshids have much higher stations. They are people whom Allah loves.

We are not to stay young all the time. This is why we need to know the value of our youth. Make use of your time by doing your spiritual duties. Do not spend your days with games and entertainment. Take pleasure from good, beautiful things. When a person obeys Allah, Allah gives that person peace and beauty, and He gives satisfaction from little things. Without going to a bad place, they would be a thousand times more at peace, pleased, and satisfied than people who are on a bad way.

We see that devils (shaitans) are everywhere in these days of ours. Games and entertainment are not even enough for children to be happy anymore. When these are not enough, the person resorts to drinking and drugs. We see these incidents every day. Why is this happening? Because they are defeated by their ego and go after their ego. The end of these people is bad too. They need to go after good people. Let it be a tight and good discipline. This is available in tariqas. Otherwise, they fall in bad ways and are ruined.

We hear these incidents every day and are saddened. That is why we are saying here. The whole country is in danger. May Allah keep us safe from the evils of bad people. May Allah give guidance to these people. Giving advice is fard (obligatory). Religion consists of advice. Those who accept advice are saved, and those who do not regret it.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq. Al-Fatiha.
