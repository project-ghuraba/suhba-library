Assalamu Alaikum wa Rahmatullah. Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fil jamia' Madad Ya Rasul Allah, madad Ya Shaykh Nazim Haqqani. Dastur. Insha Allah with the permission of Mawlana, we may give a short sohbat.

There is the Hadith of the Prophet (sas): ‚ÄúAd-dinu nasiha‚Äù - Religion is advice, to command good. When you see something (wrong), change it with your hand, if you can. If you cannot, advise people to guide them to the right way. If not, show dislike by heart". Now, someone who preaches to mankind- if he knows what Allah commands & hides it knowingly Prophet (sas) has Hadith about this, it is as if he hides Islam from people- such a big sin, it deserves hell fire. Therefore, when one gives sohbat- we mentioned about this point before as well: One should not hide what he has to declare, thinking that some people in the gathering may be bothered or unhappy with it.

Even the newspapers now, they publish the worst news. Even if there is a small cut, they get angry, saying it is censored. However they are in the way of shaytan. If they, who are on the wrong path, do not accept it then how about us- we sit here to make a sohbat & not speak because this man may resent or get offended? Then we would hide what Allah says & fall into sin. Therefore, when you hear in a sohbat or preaching- whether it is a waaz-sermon in a mosque or sermons from shaykhs, when you are in any kind of sermon you can understand anyways if it is reality or not. Who is in such a sohbat-sermon, he can't hide knowingly. But who speaks there, if he speaks as he likes, it is also a big sin for him.

We were already not making sohbat here before. 5-10 years ago - or it has not been 10 years, less than 10 years- we would never make sohbat. These sohbats are happening with the barakah & support of our Shaykh. We are saying openly- we don't have anything, everything is with him, with Allah's permission. Insha Allah with his himmat, what he says are given to our heart & you benefit from it. Otherwise, we don't have anything to make sohbat. Insha Allah, Allah does not make us make a mistake (in these sohbats). Then people say "you said like this, like that".

Take the parts you like, you can leave what you don't like. Even if you can get 1% of a sohbat- it is more than enough. If you take 1%, then you get 100% with 100 sohbat. What matters is for people to get some benefit from it. We speak about sohbat, many Hadith of Prophet (sas) to "give advice". ‚Ad-dinu nasiha. Religion of Islam meaning advice- giving advice to people, to go to right way. And, many Hadith. Another Hadith also saying, if who giving lecture- scholar or shaykh or other people giving religious lecture. Giving lecture but hiding something they know-they afraid from people, to not be angry for them. This is as, hiding religion of Islam from people. It needs big punishment for this.

Of course when you speak to Muslim people, not for common people or who not understand this. When some people coming & want to hear advice & you hide this advice- maybe their ego not happy with this- this is big punishment can be, for who do this. Before this also, we say. When making lecture before 10 years, or less than this we were..I don't want to say "I" but I wasn't doing any sohbat. I cannot do any sohbat before this. Now this is not from me, barakah of Mawlana Shaykh- to make me to make sohbat. And sohbat, insha Allah our intention to be for benefit of our brother, or who listen for this sohbat.

So when you do sohbat, you cannot say this- who listening here I must do sohbat to be they are happy. You cannot.. People, only Allah can make them happy. You cannot make everybody happy. So we are speaking, insha Allah inspiration of Mawlana. And who is happy, he can take from sohbat. Who are not happy, he is not obligated. But when you stay here&listen to sohbat also this is ilm-knowledge place. Angel put wing under this place. There Allah very happy, angel wing under our feet. So if not 100% if we are not happy, even 10%, even 1% if we are happy it is enough, more than enough. You can take 1% from this sohbat. Next time 1%, next time 1%.. Maybe 20 sohbat, 50 sohbat, 100 sohbat.. So everyone slowly slowly, you will be happy. Don't take for yourself this.

Everybody has ego-I have ego, you have ego, that has ego.. Even this sohbat coming to ego, it is better. Must be until finish this ego, our ego insha Allah. There is many Hadith also- if you can change by.. When you see something wrong, you change by hand, if not by hand by speaking, giving advice. But if they don't accept this, by your heart also. For this, insha Allah first to our ego. Allah make us accept right way. And we see many people, doing something. They are happy, maybe not object against them. All they, calling people to Allah. But they are intending good, only for Allah, to intend to bring people to right way. So they can do anything. But not here. We speak- who accept, accept. If not accepting- not saying to people by name or to make people ashame..No. Insha Allah this is..Even Mawlana when he speak against Wahhabi or something, not saying "this man, this man".. Just like this "Wahabi, Salafi" he said. So who has this bad character, he take this bad character from him. Insha Allah, Allah make us in right way, don't let us to our ego.

Bi hurmati Sayyidina Muhammad (sas). Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, Al Fatiha.
