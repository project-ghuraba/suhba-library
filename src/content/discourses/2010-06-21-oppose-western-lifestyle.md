Even, no [other] creature may be able to reach an atom's existence. When beginning, from where beginning, to where going, up to where ending.

What about Man? Man always unknown one. Unknown one. Yes. The Creator giving to elected or selected or chosen ones. Giving something. Something never going to be one. Assalamu alaykum Salafi ulamas, how are you today? Don't say 'we are Salafi ulama'. Ulama - plural of saying 'alim'. You are alim? You can say that 'I am alim'? If you can say 'I am alim,' that means you know everything. Once upon a time Hisham Effendi, one Salafi alim sitting on a chair and teaching people and he was saying,

"O my listeners look, you can ask everything and I can answer to you". "Yah, what you are saying ask, I may answer, and I may give through my answer a light that you can understand." One person stood up and saying, "O Salafi alim you are claiming that you are knowing through east and west, can you say to me between east and west, how many arm-lengths, dhira - metres for example?" "You are asking such a question that is a very important question and I never tried to find out length of distance between east and west. So big question you are asking, ask another simple one". Another one stood up, "O Salafi alim, you are saying 'we are alim, we are knowing everything, we may teach people everything.' Yes, and you are saying 'ask, and I shall answer'. Please O Salafi alim, can you tell me how many hairs in your beard?" "What is with such a question?" "You said, 'I know everything' and we are asking you." "Sit down! Don't ask such a question." "Oh, you said to me that 'ask everything and I will say an answer' ". "Leave that, anyone else? These two foolish ones asking foolish questions. Anyone else? So that I may do it clear for you". Another bedouin stood up, "O Salafi alim, you said you are knowing everything and giving a permission for asking therefore, I am asking, long time I am surprised." "For what?" "For what? We are surprised, I am surprised, every time I am looking on my tents, [looking] around so many ants, I am taking one of them, it is a big ant."

Salafi alim was listening very carefully. He is not knowing where bedouin carrying him to [make him] fall down. Allah Almighty never likes His servants to be proud with anything. Allah Almighty likes that everyone should be humble servants and obedient servants. He created man not to claim that he is something. "I never created mankind to be proud in anything. I created mankind for My obedience. Therefore everything that signifies pride, I don't like." Therefore that bedouin was saying,

"Oh, my master, every time I am looking around our tent running, I am looking, I am taking one of them and looking that it is a big ant and same manifestation for all ants also". "What is that?" "Their bodies consist of two parts, head-side, back-side." "What is that? What is that? Such a question!" "Please wait because you are Salafi ulamas, you are saying we know everything and I am asking this, what about, coming to me to ask someone like you, alim, I am surprised, this ant, from head-side, eating, and coming in a point that so weak, and second, back-side I am surprising that ant's intestine, what do you think, intestine head-side or back-side?" "Sit down! What!" "You know everything, I am surprised, you must make me to be satisfied, because this point so fine, I am surprised, how this is coming this side and this side also like head-side". Now, alim, you, become a student. It comes to you through humbleness - heavenly knowledge comes to make you satisfied. Therefore everyone must have a teacher teaching what is necessary. Such questions may be, but they are not necessary. There are some questions that are necessary, you must learn. You are coming to this life for learning, not dancing, not playing. Why you are not teaching people O Salafi ulamas, that they have been created for an important mission. Why you are not saying?

Look, people around you are thinking they have been created for dunya or to make themselves in pleasure with ni'mat dunya, favours of this life. People running after this and they are looking that Western countries have so many ways or means for tasting and enjoying themselves through this life. And it is forbidden. Allah Almighty is not creating men for enjoying themselves or to reach a pleasure through this life, but the Lord of Heavens created - kings, sultans, sheikhs, rich ones, because they have possibility to live to make pleasure for themselves and you are seeing and looking, that they are running after their egoistic pleasure and enjoyment.

Why you are not stopping them? Look at their dress. Look their eatings and drinkings, look their houses, their homes, look their cars, look their offices, look their ornaments that they are using. They are making nighttime to be in pleasure and to enjoy their selves. And Allah Almighty saying, O People, we are making day for your living program to look after 'rizq', provisions. You may work. And nighttime, making night for resting. Look at your countries' people, what they are doing? Particularly, rich people. How are their lives?

They are sleeping daytime because they found so many sources of richness. They are not in need to work daytime, but sleeping and waiting, when night coming, to get out for enjoying. And the Seal of Prophets saying, "O People, O my ummah, protect your children". Keep your youngsters not to go out after Maghrib". Finish. When darkness coming, 'ahfazhu', that means, don't leave them to get out. Don't leave them because that is the time intashar ush-shayatin - that shaytan is spreading [with] its followers, afarit. Why you are not making such a rule through kingdom? Why you are not preventing people through your kingdoms, daytime and nighttime. Leaving people to do everything against heavenly orders. How you are [expecting] a heavenly support here and hereafter. O People, O our attenders, Assalamu alaykum. Assalamu alaykum. It is so fine and difficult point that doctors of Sharia never saying or Salafi ulamas never speaking [about]. Step-by-step the Seal of Prophets bringing heavenly rules. Twenty-three years for teaching his Nation. Why you are not keeping this?

Why you are leaving youngsters outside nighttime? Why you are not taking care for youngsters not to come outside after Maghrib? It is not Sharia, doctors? And shaykhs of Azhar sharif and other places? Allah will ask you, ballaghtum, did you declare My Holy Orders? I am asking all ulamas, including Salafi ulamas. What I am saying or what heavenly powers are making me to speak, to address to you, if it is wrong or right? If you are saying 'wrong', heavenly cursing comes on your head. If you are saying 'it is true,' please come with me to prevent people from Western lifestyles.

That is important, or your final situation going to be like Iraqians, or Iranians, or Turkians or Yemenians, or Egyptians, or everywhere. That much.
