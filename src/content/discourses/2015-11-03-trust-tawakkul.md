“Wa Alallahi falyatawakkal al-Muminoon.” (Tawba:51, Tabaghun:13, Aali İmran:160)

Allah Azza wa Jalla says in the Koran, “Let the Believers (Mumins) trust in Allah.” You do not loose when you trust (tawakkul) in Allah. There is danger everywhere when you live in the world. When Allah wants it happens, and when He does not want it does not happen. Everything is as mush as Allah wants.

You could not even live for a minute if Allah does not want. Allah has such ability and grandness that there are 35 different killer substances on a fly. Allah placed them on it. Flys whiz around everywhere. If Allah activates one fly that you see as little, everybody would die.

There is no need to fear people ever. Trust in Allah. Those who trust in Allah have always won, let them not fear. Thank Allah Mumins are naive. They trust in Allah and go on. Otherwise, as we said, if there are so many things on one fly, the grudge, hatred, and nurtured resentment of those who are against Allah would not let us live for one minute. We have neither preparedness nor have as much wealth, power, and weapons as they do. But Allah protects.

Thank Allah good people are leading us. This is a favor (lutuf) of Allah on us. We should not forget this and be thankful to Allah. Do not fear from such and such becasue this and that happened. Nothing would happen as long as Allah does not want. Allah protects.

May Allah be content with (Jazallah) those who serve this nation. Let them serve for the sake of Allah since here is the head of the Nation of Muhammad (Ummat Muhammad). The whole Nation is looking here because they have inflicted devastation everywhere. This place has remained under protection with the favor and kindness (karam) of Allah. There is a reason behind it and that reason will appear Inshallah. The time is near.

Mahdi Alayhis Salam will go out from here, rule all Muslims and the whole world with justice, and beautiful things will happen. Whatever darkness, gloom, and cruelty there is will lift Inshallah. We are waiting for him Inshallah. We are trusting in Allah. May Allah protect the Ummat Muhammad. May Allah be our helper.

Al-Fatiha.
