Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

“Alhamdulillah,” the Glorious Koran starts with this beautiful verse.

“Alhamdu Lillahi Rabbil alamin.” (Sura Fatiha:2)

“Alhamdu Lillahillathee khalaqas samawati wal’arda waja’alath thulumati wannoor.” “All praise is due to Allah, who created the heavens and the earth and made the darkness and the light.” (Sura An’am:1) This is a sign for us to praise Allah (JJ) and be thankful to Him. We need to always say “Alhamdulillah wash-Shukrulillah” to be thankful for this great benediction.

Alhamdulillah we are counted as having worshipped all night, because we had made the intention to go to sleep and continue after waking up. That time it would be like worshipping all night. We glorify and thank Allah for reaching this blessing. This is a beautiful thing. This is not everyone’s kismet.

He says, “Wa qalilum min ‘ibadiyash shakoor.” “Those who are grateful are very few.” (Sura Saba:13) Thankfulness is a great duty. It is something Allah loves. Allah loves those who are thankful and does not like those who disbelief.

“Fainnallaha laa yuhibbul kafireen.” “Indeed Allah does not like the disbelievers.” (Sura Aali Imran:32) The reverse, meaning opposite of gratefulness is unbelief. This is why we need to be careful. We need to thank Allah and we need to be thankful to Allah. Thanking people is thanking Allah. You should also thank people when they do you a favor. If not, if you do not, Allah forbid your faith would weaken and you could lose your faith. Therefore, being thankful for these blessings is a duty upon everybody.

What happens if you do not? If you do not you are at loss. Nobody can give you what Allah gives. Neither Europe, nor America, nor other people can give. Beware of trusting in humans but trust in Allah. Be thankful to Allah for granting us these days and these nights. Know their virtue, respect them, know their value, and do not idle away. This is thanking Allah.

As has been said, very few people are thankful. Allah Azza wa Jalla says, “Few are thankful.” Let us Inshallah always be from those who are thankful. Allah Azza wa Jalla says, “Ibadiyash shakoor,” meaning, “My servants.” “Very few are are the thankful.” May we be amongst those servants Inshallah. May we always be in gratitude and praise Inshallah.

There is nothing bad Allah willing. Allah created everything good. The bad person sees everything bad. The good one, the one who believes in Allah and has a strong faith, is thankful and praises for everything. May we be from them Inshallah. May we not conform to our ego and not conform to Shaitan. May we not lend an ear to what they say Inshallah. May our hearts always be full of faith so there is no place for Shaitan Inshallah.

Wa Minallah at-tawfeeq. Al-Fatiha.
