Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

A’ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm:

(Qur’ān 06:61). Allah ﷻ states in the Holy Qur’an: ‘And He sends over you guardian-angels.’ There are angels to protect you. There are certain things that are for goodness and some are only to allow people to stay alive. The duties of all angels are for the service of mankind. By the order of Allah ﷻ, their duties are both to worship and to perform the duties given by Allah ﷻ; not mere worship. For example, when it snows, when it rains, an angel descends for every drop, then comes back. When it snows, these angels stay on the ground and become a blessing. This is an example. Every angel has a duty. Therefore, this universe is not empty.

Unbelieving people want to instill in Muslims and Muslim children that this happens by chance. There is no such thing. BismiLlahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim: “ُّر ادَ قْ مِ ِبُُّهدَ نعِ ُُّّء يْ شَ ُُّّل كُ وَ ”, “And everything with Him is by due measure.” (Qur’ān 13:08). Nothing is in vain, nothing was created in vain. Everything has been created with the knowledge of Allah ﷻ. When Allah ﷻ says be, it happens. And when He does not will it, it doesn’t happen. Let them try as much as they want. Let them do as much as they want. They instill this in children since they are little. And then as they grow older, more bad and faithless people are brought up. When a person is faithless, he can do all kinds of bad things. There is nothing to stop him. On the contrary, they cause Fitnah and corruption along with shaytan.

There are some things that people do unknowingly, then they regret it. People and leaders think that we are doing good, but they are doing evil. The first thing to be taught is the love of Allah ﷻ and the love of our Holy Prophet ﷺ, honoring them and glorifying them. They are the ones that should be glorified: Allah Azza wa Jalla and our Holy Prophet ‘alayhi s-salām. If normal people are following their way, they are also great people. If anyone goes against them, he can do as much as he wants for this world, it is of no value. If it’s done for the sake of Allah ﷻ, then it’s valuable and great. If he did it for this world, then what he did is selfish, doubtful and of no value. That’s why you need to be careful.

While raising these people when they are young, it is necessary to raise them as good people so that they can form a good society. Thank Allah ﷻ, despite such attacks, this country is still standing with the blessings of our ancestors, and good people are being raised. No matter how hard they try to take our faith and leave us faithless, it brings benefit to both here and the whole Islamic world with the blessings of our ancestors. May Allah ﷻ protect us. May Allah ﷻ give strength to our faith.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
