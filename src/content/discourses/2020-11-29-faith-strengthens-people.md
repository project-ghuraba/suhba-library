Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

A’ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm:

(Qur’ān 04:28). Allah ﷻ says: ‘And mankind was created weak.’ Allah ﷻ says that mankind is created weak. Eventhough he has been created weak, he is the creature that has the most control in the world. It is the wisdom of Allah ﷻ that He created man weak. But if He created him as other creatures, nothing of this world will remain. Humans may be weak, but most of the time they are oppressive. Because they follow their ego, when they find an opportunity they destroy and do not listen to anything else. Those who do not obey their ego and put it under control are the acceptable ones. They are not weak. The weak one is the one who surrenders to his ego. Allah ﷻ created him weak but he can be strengthened by following and obeying Allah ﷻ.

Weakness is not acceptable. Our Holy Prophet ﷺ says, “ نِ مِ ؤْ مُ لا نَ مِ رٌ يْ خَ ي وِ َقلا نُ مِ ؤْ مُ لْا فِ يعِ ضَّ لا”, “A strong believer is better than a weak believer.” Of course, we are all weak before Allah ﷻ, but a person gains strength from faith. If he has no faith, he is defeated and weak. Faith strengthens people. Faith elevates people. Otherwise you are just a useless and harmful person. As we see those who follow their minds and whatever their ego wants, they become slaves to their ego. Thus, a society that does not believe in Allah ﷻ is formed. They want everyone to do all the filth that their ego wants. They want everyone to be just like them. These are weak people who have been misled by their egos. We must not be like them. We must oppose them.

They say that there is nothing left, neither freedom nor humanity nor democracy. And when you are not with them, when you oppose them, you become an enemy to all of them. Everything they say is lies, hypocrisy and nothing but filth. They are the soldiers and followers of shaytan. Therefore, a person becomes stronger through faith. May Allah ﷻ give us all true and strong faith. May we be strong servants against unbelief and oppression. May Allah’s ﷻ help be with us insha’Allah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
