As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Fa-l-yafraḥū huwa khayrum mimmā yajma‘ūn" (10:58). "Qul bi-faḍli Llāhi wa-bi-raḥmatihī fa-bi-dhālika fa-l-yafraḥū huwa khayrum mimmā yajma‘ūn" (10:58). Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is saying, whoever attains the mercy of AllāhNone and HisNone excellent kindness should rejoice and feel relief. The best thing has happened to him. There are things that happen in this world, and people aren't aware of them. They don't know what to be happy for, what to do and how to comfort their hearts. They are running after useless things which don't bring any benefit.

It is the last day of holy Ramaḍān. Shukr to AllāhNone because, as much as we could, we did what AllāhNone ordered this Ramaḍān. Shukr to AllāhNone, our souls are at peace. AllāhNone has given peace, for we acted according to HisNone will, and HeNone gave out of HisNone generosity. We make shukr to AllāhNone, we thank AllāhNone for having fasted, prayed and walked on this way as much as we could during this Ramaḍān. AllāhNone intended these things for us. HeNone granted us these beauties. HeNone bestowed these beauties on us. We make shukr to HimNone. We are happy to have done this. HeNone permitted us do it, to be correct.

Many people don't want to do it, they do not do it or cannot do it. And it is not meant for them. Once people have missed the goodness, charity, thawāb, light and barakah given by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, even if they try for the rest of their lives, they cannot reach that again. They cannot attain the reward and the excellence of that day. We must make shukr for it. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says to be happy for it. "Fa-bi-dhālika fa-l-yafraḥū huwa khayrum mimmā yajma‘ūn" (10:58). To be happy with this is much better than what you collect in dunyā. You should not be happy with the money and wealth you collect in this world, but feel relief when you earn thawāb and are given such things as AllāhNone grants to you. Otherwise, mankind loses such big things, and does not attain to great things then.

As we said, its merit comes from AllāhNone giving us from HisNone endless treasures. The thanks we give is little; we give such little thanks to Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Therefore, we reached these very beautiful days. Shukr to AllāhNone, there was no illness. Anything could have happened. But there was no obstacle. We completed it, and finished it, shukr to AllāhNone. May AllāhNone let us reach many more years, many more Ramaḍāns, and better days. Better days will come, in shā’a Llāh. The end of darkness is light. Certainly, those days will come, in shā’a Llāh. We are waiting for those days, in shā’a Llāh. Until those days, may AllāhNone grant us these beautiful things, and not deprive us of them, in shā’a Llāh.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says in Qur’ān: "Qul bi-faḍli Llāhi wa-bi-raḥmatihī fa-bi-dhālika fa-l-yafraḥū huwa khayrum mimmā yajma‘ūn" (10:58). For this, HisNone generous and HisNone Mercy of AllāhNone, AllāhNone give us this good thing. You, HeNone said you must be happy with this. To be very happy with this. This is better than anything else from dunyā.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are saying with this blessed month of Ramaḍān, with HisNone favour, with HisNone blessing, we al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we finish this. Now last day, and even last hours. By HisNone favour for us, HeNone make us to fast all this month, and pray and do every good thing. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, more than any other month, we are living for ākhirah, for sake of AllāhNone, for sake of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. We are happy whole time from twenty-four hours. It was very blessed days. And this from HisNone favour for us, because many people they are not doing this. And they thought they are happy, and they are good. "We not doing this. People they are hungry, they are thirsty," and maybe they think we are unhappy. But these people, they are wrong. Because what AllāhNone gives us, if they try to get one hour from what AllāhNone gave us, from HisNone blessing and favour and reward – whole life if they try to make it again, they cannot reach this. And they are cheating themselves, and losing very big thing which it is not going away. But what they thought they are getting from this world – material thing, money or jewellery or land or other thing – it is nothing. Because it will not be forever.

This what AllāhNone gives us. We are not from ourself. We also thanking AllāhNone, and HeNone said you must be happy because this from HisNone bless and HisNone gift for us. We be happy with this. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are all happy we can finish this month with whole this blessed thing, and we reach the end. We are happy with this. AllāhNone make us happy all time like this; to not lose anything from HisNone blessings, from this. We are doing for HisNone sake and for love of HimNone and love of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. This is very important, and al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are this last hours. We hope, in shā’a Llāh, to be many Ramaḍāns, and better than this world, in shā’a Llāh. Better than world, world with peace. With peace is Islām. Nothing other – they are not peace at all. They are only cheating each other, killing each other, and they said, "We are making peace." They are hypocrites. They are oppressors.

Only Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HisNone light, with Islām, can be peace with this world, in shā’a Llāh. We hope, in shā’a Llāh, next Ramaḍān to be better, in shā’a Llāh. For us it is good, no problem. Because we are – al-ḥamdu liLlāh HeNone make us in this way, and HeNone make us to do this thing. But for other people, must be to change themselves, in shā’a Llāh, and change world for to be – get what they lose before. What they lose, they lose, they cannot reach it. But for other, they can begin again, in shā’a Llāh.
