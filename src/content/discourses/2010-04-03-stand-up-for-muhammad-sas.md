Assalamu alaikum wa rahmatullahi wa barakatuhu.

O our Attenders, our attenders for a most important target. When a hunter is asking to hunt a hunt, he takes all his attention for it, nothing else does he ask to be occupied with. For what? For a small bird. Giving so much effort and so much attention for hunting that and it is only one ounce, maybe or not. Allahumma, la hawla wa la quwwata.. And Mankind are not taking any care beyond their existence through this life. This is an existence for everyone. Yes? But people are not taking any attention or care beyond this life. And this life is such a small bird. Beyond it, Allahu Akbar. What Allah Almighty is granting, no one knows. No one knows.

And then, salamu alaykum our attenders. Don't be heedless, don't be heedless. Don't think that we're through this life continuously. Yeah. One day is coming when it stops. And we are not taking any attention for our being on this planet. For what we are here? And we are saying also: Assalamu alayk O the one who is responsible for all that is happening on this planet among mankind first and then for everyone that is living on this planet. Their responsibility is on one appointed and dressed with such a heavenly power that he is able to control from east to west, from north to south, oceans from top point up to deepest point.

Subhanallah, most Glory for the Lord of Creation. How it can be? Do you think that sometimes we are looking and seeing on this handkerchief, a small ant running, and I am saying "Oh that small one of creatures, I am asking, who is creating you?" And it is answering, "He Who created you has created me." Allahu Akbar. Why are people not thinking? Because people are drunk, therefore not thinking on it. And this planet is nothing. Such a small one. Perhaps through greatness of this space it can't be seen, can't be known, so small but we are on it, we are on it and we are making so many efforts, we are running, we are working we are asking, we are trying. For what we are trying O Mankind? For what?

When Noah, peace be upon him, was asked "O eldest one among prophets, no one has lived as long as you lived, what is your intiba' impression through those thousand years? What are you saying as you are the second father of mankind on Earth, because the Flood has taken them away. What is your impression? What you are going to say to your descendents? He was saying, "O my sons and my grandsons, now I am looking and seeing a vision". "What is your vision?" "I am seeing that a caravan has reached an inn, a caravanserai, it has entered and is passing through and now they are leaving. I am looking and seeing dunya as a person that just entered the inn, on which is written 'In'. I am getting in and now it is written, 'Out' and I am going out. Nothing else. A thousand years.

What about Salafi ulamas? If that is true or not, we are not going to discuss it, but it is for realising an unchangeable reality for mankind, yes? Yes. Billions among mankind have entered from one entrance and they passed through. They were sitting either a little longer or shorter time and then standing up and getting out. That is my impression about the life of mankind since I was one of them, the longest one living on this planet and now I am passing out". "What about other nations? Other nations, other prophets' people?" "After me their lives are going to become shorter shorter, shorter, shorter and it is going to be shortest life for mankind for the Seal of Prophets Sayyidina Muhammad (sas)". And he stood up, he was lying, Noah (as) and then when it was said 'Sayyidina Muhammad' he was standing up from his bed giving the highest respect to the Seal of Prophets, Sayyidina Muhammad (sas).

Our civil service ulamas are not accepting to stand up when his holy name is mentioned. You must learn. Salafi ulamas you must learn to give your highest honour and respect to the Seal of All Prophets (sas) and most beloved one, most glorified one, most honoured one, most majestic one among Creation. You must learn when his name is mentioned to stand up. If standing up, the Lord Allah Almighty is not getting angry but He is getting angry (if not standing) for His most beloved, most honoured and most glorified one. His absolute deputy from pre-Eternal up to Eternal. Don't think that another Muhammad (sas) is coming. No, one is enough. No need for anyone else. When his name is mentioned, you must stand up.

Don't say that is bid`a, that is haram, that is shirk, no. That is respect, greatest respect to that one who had been respected in Divine Presence. Endlessly representing, respected one. Don't think that Allah Almighty is going to penalize you, mu`aqabah. That means to give to you punishment because you are standing up. Because Allah Almighty respected that one and for his honour created everything. No one knows beginning, no one knows ending. And our religion, Islam just came on such strong pillars and most strong one of pillars, aati kulli li haqqin haqqa. You are Salafi ulamas that is what they are making me (say), our teachers teaching and sending to me. That is Hadith or not?

Everything that has an honour belongs to the Seal of Prophets (sas) and Allah Almighty granted this to His absolute deputy from pre-Eternal up to Eternal. Allah Almighty not changing not changing His deputy. No. He is One and His deputy is one. Mutlaq, absolute. Yes. You must give your highest respect to that one respected in Divine Presence. And our religion is based on respect and glorifying and honouring, yes? Just built on it. If you are not respecting some that are respected through heavens you are not on a right way but you are shaytan's followers because shaytan is never happy for Real Deputy of Allah Almighty to be respected. Do you know that O Salafi ulamas? That shaytan, why he was angry and not making sajdah? And it was so mujtahid, hardworking one to be obedient to his Lord and he was thinking that honour would be granted to him, but no.

Allah Almighty was not bringing the Seal of Prophets (sas) in that mashhad, that scene. Not bringing Sayyidina Muhammad (sas) No. No, because that mashhadun azim, that scene couldn’t carry (his light) therefore sending Adam (as) and putting on Adam's forehead very, very, very, very, very, small spot from the lights to be deputy. Granted to Adam (as). And whole world coming to be in lights that you can't imagine. Not bringing there Sayyid Rusul Kiram, no, but coming Sayyidina Adam (as). Allahu Akbar, yafal ma yurid wa yahkum man yasha, Rabbuna Jalla wa Aala. Allah Almighty's deputies’ divinely lights can't appear or carry that. The whole Creation would be burned and disappear.

Where is your knowledge O Salafi ulamas? What we are saying do you think are fairy stories? Fairy tales? They are opening a little bit from that knowledge that you may say is a spot through an ocean. No more. No more. Whole Creation can't carry more than that spot. Allahu Akbar! Where is your knowledge? Why you are not looking at Islamic books? But you are making someone's knowledge as, 'This is not true, that is not good' and making it like this, like that. No. You can't do that.

Islam is so high, Iman so much higher and love of the Lord of Heavens is something else. You can't imagine, you can't imagine. But, you are not asking for that, you are taking your books always like this and coming. What is this.. What do you think? Millions books have been written through Holy Quran's holy knowledge and it is only first level. They are not going up to 2nd level, 3rd level, 4th, 5th level, no. We can't even finish first level, how we are going to finish, to reach 2nd stage, or 2nd, 3rd level. When reaching Heavenly levels, those limits are according to ourselves. Everything that belongs to Allah Almighty is unlimited. Unlimited. Where we are? O people take care. Don't say we are ulamas or Salafi ulamas. Ask what was their opinion or their knowledge about heavens? About 1st level, 2nd, 3rd, 4th level, 5th, 6th, 7th levels. You can't say because you are not as yet entering in it, you are only looking at map. Prophet's (sas) holy tomb, here. Do you think that I am such a person as he is inside? I am like him? No. Therefore 7 levels of heavens, yet, we are looking at it as a picture. And more than picture we are looking only about their names, the pictures even, we are not looking at.

And how, when you are reaching and seeing those oceans and where you are coming into that ocean and diving in it. O People, kun taliban. Ask more, ask more. And the Lord of Heavens saying, wa ladayna mazid. As long as you are asking, He may grant to you endlessly. May Allah forgive us. We are trying to wake up sleeping minds or frozen minds for understanding something that is la yudhkar, not to be mentioned, yes. We are on a small planet, we can't reach everywhere, up and down. What about Heavens? Heavens, do you think are a handful of oceans? Oceans are having small drops in them because our ocean is in limits. Heavens and heavenly oceans are unlimited. Another secret knowledge. O People, we are leaving now Salafi ulamas to discuss among themselves which of it that we are saying is true, which you are not understanding or which you are not accepting. But if you are not accepting we may ask, we may say to you, qul: hatoo burhanakum. If you are going to refuse what has been said now, bring over this knowledge another level of knowledge for which you can bring a real proof, if not, useless. O People try to learn. Therefore the Lord of Heavens saying to his most beloved one "Say to your Nation 'utlub al `ilm. Kun taliban', utlub al-ilman min al-mahd ila'l lahad.

O our Lord, we know nothing. We only know to eat and drink and enjoy ourselves with halal or with haram. Hafazan Allah, Allah protect us. This is a sohbah, association with our Grandshaykh as they are sending something. It is not from my thinking, coming and I'm writing, no. It is waridat, inspirations coming from heavens. Be respectful. If you can't refuse it with adillatun qawwiyah, if you are not bringing real proof through your real objections then it is something without reality. Don't say 'I finished it'. No. Don't say 'I became a doctor'. If you become a doctor, come, I shall look for your pulse. There are some ones who are looking here, not only physicians, some ones that have been authorized for looking at your understanding levels, your learning level, your knowledge level, then sending you there.

May Allah forgive us for the honour of the most honoured one in His Divine Presence, Sayyidina Muhammad (sas).
