A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah. Khallāqu l-‘Aẓīm, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla... The Creator is Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. The mind is astonished at Him; minds are astonished at His dominion. The mind cannot grasp its boundaries. Even if the entire universe joined together, it could not grasp even a single point of His wisdom. We have such a majestic Lord – Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Mankind learns two things, and begins to think he is something, trying to show superiority over others. Allāh has written everything in Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n. There is one beautiful āyah – all are beautiful, but this one says: Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Minhā khalaqnākum wa-fīhā nu‘īdukum wa-minhā nukhrijukum tāratan ukhrá” (20:55). Allāh created mankind from here – from the Earth, from this globe. The soil of Ādam ‘alayhi s-salām was taken from here, and He placed him in paradise. Then, he returned here again. Thereafter, he was buried in the earth. After burial, mankind will be created anew a second time. We were all created from the Earth, from this globe. He says, “We will bring you forth from it.” Then we will again become earth, we will become soil. Just as Allah ‘Azza wa-Jalla first created us, He will recreate us a second time – from this earth, with our flesh, bones, and all that we were. Then, the Earth’s duty will be over. At that time, a person will either go to paradise or hell. The Earth will no longer be necessary for the children of Ādam. The creation of Allāh continues. Allāh is the Creator. There is no rule that He will only create us and not create anything else. He is Al-Khallāq, which means The Creator. That creation will continue. Our place is here. We were created from here. Mankind will go from here to ākhirah. Now there are foolish people deceiving others, and I don’t know why. “No, we will go there. We will go to the moon.” They forgot about the moon; the moon is very close. “We will go to other planets. We will go to Mars. We will go to that planet. We will settle there.” People don’t even want to go to a slightly difficult place on Earth. You don’t have to spend that much money. It’s not a smart thing to do, but people are cheated. People are so easily deceived that they don’t know what will happen. What will happen? Exactly this: We were created from this Earth, and to this Earth we will return. Then, the second time, is different. There is no space shuttle. There is no space rocket. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla will bring them all to their places in the most beautiful way. Those who belong to paradise will go to paradise. Those who belong to hell will go to hell. Those left in the middle will stand for a while, then Allāh will hold them accountable as He wills. So this isn’t something people can say, “We know.” Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gives people something – He gives them knowledge so they may see their helplessness. Some don’t see their helplessness; they put themselves forward as if to say, “We know better.” As many precious mines and jewels as there are in the world, there are millions or billions more in the sky. That’s something a person cannot reach. They say, “How will we get it? What should we do?” This shows exactly that. This ends here. Come, try to take it if you can. That means Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gives us this as an example so that you may know your helplessness. You may turn to Allāh. You may return to Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. You may ask Allāh for mercy and compassion. He is showing you this so that you seek forgiveness from Allāh. But mankind is very easily deceived by shayṭān. There are some people who say they are smart. The minds of such people are the most deceived. May Allāh protect us. May Allāh not separate us from the right way. Because Allah ‘Azza wa-Jalla created man in a way that the person who strives to make you better makes you worse. He turns you into a useless creature in every way. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla created us in the most beautiful way. The other, the shayṭān, is striving to turn us into the worst form. May Allāh protect us. May Allāh not take our minds, in shā’a Llāh.
