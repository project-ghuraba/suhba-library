As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh. A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Our Holy Prophet salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says that richness is not an abundance of wealth and possessions. This is not richness. Richness is in controlling the ego. The person who controls his ego is the rich one.

Richness – a man has wealth as much as this world and he still wants more on top of it. He wants for himself without giving anything to anyone else, even to the poor. On the other hand, a person whose soul is rich, even with little, does not look down on anything. Whatever he finds is enough. This is the rich one. He doesn't need anyone. Otherwise, he will be all the time not satisfied with what he has, and not happy with what he has. He chases after dunyā greedily all the time for more things. He chases after dunyā. And while chasing after dunyā, he does not recognise the rights of others. He recognises neither the rights of servants, nor the rights of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. He attacks all kinds of things like "a hungry wolf" they say. Otherwise, even if he has a little, he doesn't need anyone. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gives him what is enough for him from provision, gives him peace, and is pleased with him because of his beautiful state.

Otherwise, a person who goes after dunyā, AllāhNone is not pleased with him because he forgets AllāhNone. What is important is that our richness is the pleasure of AllāhNone. When AllāhNone is pleased with us, this is our greatest richness. It is an eternal richness. Dunyā does not remain with anyone. The person who chases after this dunyā and ruins and destroys himself for dunyā, will eventually also leave it and go. At that time, those who have rights on him will follow him to ākhirah. If he did not ask for forgiveness from AllāhNone, he will also have to account for it. This means there is neither comfort in dunyā nor comfort in ākhirah. Therefore, let your soul be rich. Don't be in need of anyone. Don't disgrace yourself to anyone. Don't run after dunyā. Whatever your provision is in dunyā, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla sends it. The person who knows this, is a rich one.

May AllāhNone make us from them, in shā’a Llāh.

Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, to be richness... We read this in hisNone ḥadīth, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, richness not with lot of things, lot of money, lot of car, lot of house, lot of land, anything. This is not richness. The richness – to be control your ego. Who control his ego, he is rich one. We see people, rich people, everywhere we see. Don't think they are rich. Not controlling their ego, not controlling themselves, they are not rich. Because, whole time, if they have a lot of things, they still running after material, after dunyā. They want this. They want that. They want more and they want more and more and more and they are not looking from where they are getting this, from where, from whom [they're] taking. Not looking for right of AllāhNone, right of people. They are only running after dunyā for themselves. They forget ākhirah, not remembering ākhirah.

For this, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, this not richness. This is only tiring themselves and they're forgetting, not enjoying from anything, just enjoying to run after dunyā. But the real richness is [with] what AllāhNone gave them, they are happy with AllāhNone what gave them. Not running after dunyā. If AllāhNone send them, open for them some way to for rizq (provision), they are happy, if a lot or small. It is, for them, happiness. It is coming from AllāhNone. They are remembering this is not from ourselves, [but] from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla and they are happy with this. This is real happiness, real richness which most of people, not looking for this. They thought by their running after dunyā, they can be more happy. No. If you are only for dunyā, running after dunyā, you will never be happy. But if you are working and AllāhNone open for you and [you] doing good thing, you will be happy here and hereafter.

Because, who running, not looking for, not giving right, not giving zakāt or what AllāhNone order, for this, in ākhirah, there will be nothing for them. They will leave everything here and going to ākhirah. Only what they will do? They must give what you done here. You will [be] asked there, "What you do with this?" This only they get. Not getting their thing, their money, their gold with them. Only the accounting – "What you done with this, what you do with that?" And you, "I left them there." But you left them there, you take them from, not giving their what you must give for them. Just taking like this and you thought you will be happy. Now, you will be double not happy, double miserable for you. Because, you left this and on top of this, you have debt for AllāhNone, for poor people.

But who is with AllāhNone, he is not looking for dunyā, only for ākhirah, what they ask from him, he will be ready for this and AllāhNone will be happy with him. This is most important thing – to be happy with AllāhNone and AllāhNone happy with you. This is the real richness. Not... You can have, no problem, millions billions, but if AllāhNone happy with you, you will be also happy. But, if you have billions and AllāhNone not happy with you, no any benefit for this.

AllāhNone help us and to not forget every minute Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Everything we do, asking from HimNone to help us, to be win on our ego, to make our ego on way of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.
