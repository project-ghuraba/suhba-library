As-Salamu Alaikum. Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fil jamia' Today insha Allah, again the dargah's manner- it is the best of Islam's good manners & also dargah's good manners.

What is taught in dargah? Humbleness. Humbleness is like earth. The earth is humble but everything, all good comes out from it. Children of Adam are created from earth. All goodness comes from earth. The fire rises, thinking of itself as a big deal. And most of the time badness comes out from it. Shaytan is created from fire. Therefore being humble makes a person rise. Meaning a person who is trying to rise himself loses. Allah makes man rise; the more humble he is, Allah makes him rise & as he becomes arrogant Allah makes him lower. They teach this in dargah. Humbleness is from the first of teachings. Saying "I worshipped this much, that much" & being arrogant about it is no good.

A man lived in the time of Isa (as). He was a sinner. He committed all kind of sins possible. Even shaytan was fed up with him, that bad. When he came to him, he was saying "this one is already doing everything, so no need to give him waswasa & tell him 'do this, do that'." Even no need for shaytan to do anything! Whatever sin, dirtiness, evil, shamelessness there is, this man would commit all. He was such a sinner. One couldn't look at his face. Because of sins, it was all dark. People would fear to be close to him. Wherever he was, they would stay far. The pages angels record; on the right side there was no record. And the one on the left almost filled it all &finished. He spent most of his life like this, yet never repented. He never asked forgiveness. He didn't think about it, didn't think there is akhirah. He continued with evil.

And Isa (as) one day.. Of course he was traveling to places. When he was returning from the desert, there was an abid/worshipper, an old man. To the contrary he devoted his life to worshipping. To not be busy with anything other than Allah, he isolated himself from people. He would stay in his room & would not see anyone. Isa (as) wanted to stop by this abid man saying "he is such a holy one". When the abid heard that the Prophet is coming to him, he showed respect. He ran out from his room & fell on his knees before Isa (as).

As they were chatting, by the Wisdom of Allah, that evil man looked from afar; one is a Prophet, a great Prophet and the other an abid who worshipped for years. He watched from afar like this. He admired them. He said "I wasted my life all these years in dirtiness, in evil. How did I fall into such a mistake? I don't even have a single good deed. I don't have anything other than evil. How beautiful are these people;a Prophet & abid who spent his life in worship." He regretted so much upon this scene, that he cried there. He said "I wish Allah would forgive me, I wish He could forgive me & I will correct myself from now on".

And at that moment, that abid man saw him also there, from afar. And this time the abid got mad at him. "O you evil man, what are you doing here? There is a Prophet here & an abid. What business do you have in our presence? Do you have no shame that you came here with all your evil? Allah didn't make you die that we be saved from you". He mouthed off for a long while and finally he said: "May Allah not gather me on Yawm al Qiyamah where this man is."

At that moment Allah (awj) sent revelation to Isa (as). He (swt) said, "This man made such a tawba that I accepted his tawba". Because no one returns empty handed from His door. He made tawba for all his sins, & he did it sincerely. And when the other one got proud with his worship & said "I am like this, I am like that" Allah accepted both their dua; He forgave that one & other will not be gathered with him. He will be in hell" He (swt) said. Because he made dua in such a way.

So this matter is like this. Meaning with his worship...No harm touches Allah with this one's sins & no benefit to Allah with this one's worship. Therefore when you worship be happy for yourself that Allah has opened for you such ways. Or else Allah could have made you like this man, or you could be from hell people in the last minute even if you worshipped.

And another story, this is also nice. And this one shows the manner of dargah. A darvish traveled from one place to another by ship. He got off from the ship there. And his face is all beautiful, he is in good state. He has Nur. It is obvious that he is a beloved servant to Allah. He asked where dargah is & they showed him. The people in dargah liked him so much. They hosted him for 3 days. The guest's right is 3 days so they didn't ask anything from him. On the 4th day, they said to him "clean this dargah. Being a guest is for 3 days, you can serve now". And they looked, the man had disappeared! "Ya Allah, how is this? And we thought he was a good man" they said. "When it came to work, he immediately ran away just because we ordered one thing". The Shaykh there & mureeds grumbled about this for a while.

After a long time, one of the mureeds found him in one place. He said to him "Yahu, are you not ashamed of this, what you did? Shaykh Effendi ordered you to do a small work & you just escaped". He started crying, that holy one. He said "I looked in the dargah didn't see anything dirty, didn't see anything unclean. I saw only myself as unclean. So I cleaned, meaning I left it". He has such a humbleness & this is from the manners of dargah.

Today we speak about humbleness. Humbleness is the most good behavior in Islam; especially in dargah, in zawiya. The best thing they teach in dargah, in tariqah; to be humbleness. There is two kind: Shari'ah,who studying in madrasa without looking for.. Only reading, they be proud. They are not humble. But after, they must come & put their foot in dargah & put their head on earth in dargah. They will be real good people. Because without this humbleness, you are not

going up, you are going down. Humbleness is coming from earth. Earth, every good thing coming from earth. Even our body also coming from earth, Allah create Adam (as) from earth. Fire going up. And fire, the most of fire, it is not good. Especially Iblis, shaytan, Allah created him from fire. For this, when you are angry or when you are proud, you are like fire. But when you are humble, you are like earth. And as much as you be humble Allah make you up. And as much as you be proud Allah make you down. Because Allah Who make people up or down.

And there was one story about humbleness from Isa (as) time. There was one very bad man. He is doing every bad thing. Even shaytan, he is not coming near him anymore. Because he is doing, even shaytan cannot do & no need to order to him to do: "Do this or do that." People, they were running away from him. When his name is said in some place, people were trembling. From his sins & bad behaving & every badness on him, Allah make his face dark, give him darkness. And this is important point also; when people doing bad thing light come off from them, darkness coming on them. From his darkness, even animal was afraid. In his right side, angel was not writing anything. It was empty. Left angel- they were nearly finished, this books from his badness. He was like this, very bad. He was like this until, maybe coming old, maybe 50-60 or more years. People were very fed up from him.

Isa (as) was traveling. And once when he traveling, coming to his city. And in this city also, there was one like monk-worshipping Allah. He is very old, maybe more than 100 years old. And he is worshipping only, to not his heart to be busy, sitting in one room, not meeting any people. And Isa (as), when he coming, he said "I will visit this worshipper." He come to him. When he is, this worshipper hearing Prophet Isa (as) coming to him, he was very happy, coming out from his room & going kissing his feet. And he was speaking to him, and they were happy. It was very nice view. Prophet (as), Holy Prophet Isa (as) & this worshipper, like light all around.

And this man, he was around that area & he was looking from far away from them. And he was looking this is, something in his heart broken. He was looking, looking, looking.. And after, beginning to cry. He said "What I done all this my life? It is rubbish. Very bad thing I do. Why I lost all my life in this bad action? And this is Prophet & this is worship man. How they are nice, how it is, why I do this.." He was very regretted. And crying, crying, crying.. And asking from Allah: "I will never do anything more at all after this. I will.. I don't want to do anything, only Allah.." After this, he repented.

And from far also, this worship, he saw him, this man. When he saw him, he become angry: "You are here? You are not ashamed to be around here, you are in front of Prophet & holy man worshipping whole his life. You are; all people fed up from you. Allah not make you to die & to we get rid of you. You are very bad, you are this, you are that.." And he was shouting on him & speaking against, angry with him. The last word he said, making dua "Allah not make me to be with you in Qiyamah, in same place". That second, Allah send Jibril to Isa (as) & tell him: "Allah accept this two men's dua/praying, what they want from Allah. Allah forgive for this man, who real he was repentant. Allah not let anybody to go empty from His door. What he want & they

are repentant & asking, Allah give. And for other one, Allah also accept his dua & he will be, not be with him in paradise. He will be another place, in jahannam.

Because Allah not harming from this man, what he do & Allah not need for this worshipping, worship. Only Allah accept for, to be humble. When you are not humble, you say "I am doing this, that" you are like this Allah not happy with this. Allah happy with humbleness.

And another story from dargah. Because in dargah, I said, to be learned humbleness. There was one very nice young man coming from traveling from sea & coming to some place. Asking for dargah. He want, they take him to dargah/zawiya. He was very good, lightening & good adab. They were happy with him. 3 days, as we said yesterday for guest, it is we must honour him & not ask him to do anything or to work, or to help. If he like, he can do. After 3 days, we can ask. After 3 days, the Shaykh of dargah ask him to clean dargah. When he said this, after this nobody see him-disappeared. This people, they were unhappy with him. They were speaking "What is this? We thought he is good adab, with good behave, very good, we were wrong with this man."

Many months after this, they forget this. But one of the mureed of the Shaykh saw this man in some place. When seeing, he become too angry & shouting "Why you do this? You behave bad behaving. You not clean & you run away from when order some work for you." This man, he began to cry. He said: "I looked for dargah". He was very humble. "And saw dargah very clean. Nothing dirty in this dargah, at all. It was like very very clean dargah. But one thing, it was dirty. And it was me. So I take, I go away from dargah, the dargah will be clean". From this, dargah teaching to know yourself you are dirty, other to be clean.

Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, Al Fatiha.
