Ya Salam!

Masha Allah I will start with: The Purpose of Living. Everyone is living but he doesn't know why. That is why people become old, they become elderly and they end in 'elderly house', and then will be thrown away. This is not the purpose of life. So when you see such a personality having this kind of ending, that means that the poor man or poor woman was not having any purpose of living. Just were born, lived, died. This is very bad, it is extremely bad. Alhamdulillah, that Allah Almighty gave us Iman. And, this is the real purpose of living, but we don't know how to use it.

This is another thing. Allah Almighty grants you well but you spend it for a very bad investment. The wealth in two days is finished. But Allah Almighty gave you this one. You can't blame Allah Almighty, "Ya Rabbi, you didn't give me." Allah Almighty gives you generously but you didn't know how to use it. And you didn't know how to put it in good investment. Everyone knows that bad investment is losing. But good investment is trebling, 10 times more, 100 times more, 1000 times more. And with Allah Almighty, million times. Countless, Subhanallah. It is the purpose of living.

If you know why Allah Almighty grants you the favour of living and it is very important to know it, then Allah Almighty is already giving you a big wealth. But how to put it in good investment, or bad investment. Alhamdulillah, now we have Mawlana Shaykh. He is our big investment, the biggest that Allah Almighty put in our life. So that is why when I look to Mawlana Shaykh, masha Allah, he is an old man but he is not elderly. He is young. Young old man! Masha Allah, he knows that the purpose of living is Allah Almighty. The purpose of living is, ‘to look after my murids'. The purpose of living is ‘to give the power of spirituality to my murids, my children, my sons, my daughters'.

This is Mawlana Shaykh. That is why he is really powerful. He is all the time in good shape. Even if he is passing through as a human body. We can be ill, we can be powerful, we can be wet, we can be hungry, we can be everything. It is human nature. Nobody can discuss it. Mawlana Shaykh is going through his age. But Alhamdulillah, when you look at his mind, at his heart -perfect condition. Perfect condition. Because he is knowing why Allah Almighty created him and he is knowing what is the purpose of living and he is really with the Master of the Seas. Catching it. He is really putting all his power and effort. This will give the second thing, the sincerity. Shaykh Jamaluddin, the sincerity. A dog is 'najis'. What is najis? Unclean. Let us say it is unclean. In Arabic, the word najis means you cannot touch him and go and pray, like the cat is okay but the dog has some kind of things. The sincerity makes the dog become a Paradise animal. When The Cave People were running for their Iman from the king, the dog was following them. And they said, “This dog will show us to these kafir people, to kafir king, who will kill us”. And they started to hit the dog with stones to make him run away from them. That dog never did, even though he was hurt but still running after them. And when they entered the cave he was outside and was sitting like this, waiting and guarding them with sincerity.

With sincerity, imagine. Dog is sincere to these people. Allah Almighty gave this love and sincerity. And when they woke up after 300 years they went outside and the first thing they see, the dog is a skeleton. It was as they left him. As they left him, he was like this. He didn't move even one step. He didn't make any movement. Just sitting there and waiting for them to go out. His life was not enough. But the sincerity… How you are sincere? So this dog because of his sincerity became the Paradise animal. He had been honoured to be the Paradise animal. There are seven animals that will go to Paradise from this dunya and one of them is the dog.

Alhamdulillah, I am saying first to myself, "Ya Rabbi, don't take away my sincerity for my Shaykh". And don't take my sincerity for Rasulullah (s) and Allah Almighty. Because the Shaykh is the first step to Prophet (sas), to Allah Almighty. He is the one showing you the door. Every house must have a door and the Shaykh is the door for the Big House. So many people misunderstand and say, "Oh the Shaykh make.." No! Shaykh is showing you the real door to Allah, the Prophet (s) and for Iman because we don't know. We listen, we hear and then we put in our mind minor thinking and say, "Maybe this is right, maybe this is wrong". Actually it is not. You have to have a murshid, you have to have guidance, you have to know. This is not simple.

Allah Almighty made it two ways. The simple way is to say, Ashhadu an la ilaha illa Allah wa ashhadu anna Muhammadan Rasulullah, you are in. Okay, this is the simplest way. Anybody says it by joking, or by mistake, Allah Almighty will accept it even if you don't realise what it is. Allah accepts it, 'This is done, you are on this side. Come, come for the safe side'. But after that it is the way of living, the way of going on, because life is not only Ashhadu an La ilaha illa Allah wa ashhadu anna Muhammadan Rasulullah. Actually it is, but to continue in living, we have other things. How to process in praying, how to process in fasting, how to process in our family, community, social life, How to marry, how to divorce, how, how, how. So many hows. How-how.

So the important thing is that the Shaykh is the guidance to put you on the Real Way. And the Shaykh has so many helpers. As we say, the Shaykh is human, so he is all the time appointing people. But the dangerous part there, like me, now I am sitting here, and I am thinking, ‘I am a shaykh’, but I am not. But, because I have so many people to listen to me, so our egoistic, second part will come and say "Umm hmm, I am the one. Yes, aha, ha". These people will never listen to you, if not for power of the Shaykh. This is very known. Everyone knows it. And, we ask Allah Almighty to give our Shaykh long life & be in our head until Mahdi's (as) approach. This will be our biggest reward from Allah Almighty, that we see Mahdi (as) with our Shaykh and this day is very nearby. The world cannot carry more than this. In olden days the Judgement was so quick. Any mistake happening in ancient time, Allah Almighty, "Boom!", immediately. But now is the tajalli of As-Saboor. This is the last name of Allah, As-Saboor -The Patient. The One Who tolerates. And I don't know how much He will tolerate, but at the end of the day there will be some ending and some new beginning. With this one we say:

wa min Allahi tawfiq bi hurmat al Habib, bi hurmat al Fatiha
