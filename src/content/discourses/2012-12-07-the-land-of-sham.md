Safety is in the land of Sham at the end of times. The land of Sham. My Master & Mawlay used to say that there is the heart of Sham & there are the limits of Sham. The heart of Sham, once you go up the minaret in the Umawi Mosque in the four directions, the distance of 6 days walking, this is the heart of Sham. The heart of Sham also has a heart. Once the muazzin goes up the minaret his vision as far as it reaches - this is the heart of Sham. But the borders of Sham are 6 days walking distance, either East, West, North & South.

The heart of Sham. The limits are clear when the muazzin goes up the Umawi Mosque & as far as his vision reaches in the four directions, this is the heart of Sham. While the borders of Sham are 6 days distance in any of the four directions, on a fast walking camel, a very fast camel. Or as he said. Thanks to Allah (swt).

This is a secret. Nobody knows it except the people of Sirr/secrets. It is not for just any person to know. These secrets of Sham are not revealed except to the people of secrets. "Sham is the land of resurrection & the Day of Judgment." (Hadith) This is the holy Hadith "Ard al manshar wal manashar". Sham.... Allah (swt) & His Prophet (saws) know best. The people of knowledge, from the Saints... Allahu Akbar-ul Akbar! Allahu Akbar-ul Akbar! The secret is for the people of secrets. The secret of secrets is for the people of the secret of secrets. This you may enter it, and you may not appear again, vanishing..Allah! Aman Ya Rabbi. Tawba Ya Rabbi.

Mawlana Shaykh (said) that the limits of Sham reach Uskudar. My father is buried there. From the honourable ones Al Ghumuqi's blessing reach to there. Up to there reach the borders of Sham, not beyond. Allah Allah! The land of Sham. Sayyid Jamaluddin Al Ghumuqi, this is the limit. Up to there it is protected. On that side there are Saints but his speciality (Al Gumuqi) reaches to the limits of Damascus and it also reaches from the sea to the Green Island/Cyprus, to our Sayyida Umm Hiram Sultan.

This indication, what I am speaking about, is from my Master & Mawlay. He taught me, Sultan Al Awliya Shaykh Abdullah Al Daghastani, may Allah (swt) always raise his station. He took from them these secrets. May Allah (swt) always raise his station.
