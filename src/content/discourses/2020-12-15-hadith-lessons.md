Madad Ya Rasulullah, Mada Ya Ashab-e Rasulullah, Madad Ya Mashayikihina. Dastur. ------------------------------------------------------------

What is ihsan, which is the higher level after iman? “Ihsan is to worship Allah ﷻ as if you see Him”. Certainly, a man cannot see Allah ﷻ but he should pray as if he does. You cannot do it. However, He is seeing you. You should do your worship so that it is sincere and pure. Thus, you will reach the level of ihsan. ------------------------------------------------------------

Our Prophet ﷺ is saying, “There are three signs of a hypocrite. When he speaks, he lies”. First sign of a hypocrite is that he lies. He always tells lies and does not regret it.

“When he gives a promise, he does not keep it”. To keep a promise is a sign of manhood. It is the quality of men. Otherwise, people are worthless.

“When he is given amanah, he betrays your trust”. He doesn’t respect your trust. Whatever you trust him, be it a word or an item, he betrays it and does not show respect. These three signs are the signs of a hypocrite. ------------------------------------------------------------

Our Prophet ﷺ is saying, “The difference between us and hypocrites is that they don’t pray isha’ and fajr prayers in jama’ah”. Nowadays, people are not praying at all. There are many hypocrites now. However, during the time of our Prophet ﷺ, hypocrites would come to prayers. And to differ them, our Prophet ﷺ said they didn’t come to isha’ and faj prayers. These times are hard for them. They cannot force themselves to attend prayers at these times. Hypocrites of present time do not pray at all. ------------------------------------------------------------

Our Prophet ﷺ is saying, “Beware of people with yellow faces”. Our Prophet ﷺ tells to beware of people with pale faces.

“If this condition is not from a disease or sleeplessness due to tahajjud prayer, certainly, it is from the enmity they feel against Muslims”. No need to beware of people whose faces became pale because of tiredness. If it’s not, their faces are yellow like poison. Some people recognize them. Our Prophet ﷺ tells to beware of them. This sign is on the faces of people who are enemies to Islam. Differ those who are tired or sick. Some have yellow faces because of the poison in their hearts. Beware of them, our Prophet ﷺ warns. ------------------------------------------------------------

All of these hadith of our Prophet ﷺ show the nature of people. Our Prophet ﷺ is saying, “When someone’s sinfulness is complete, he gets control of his eyes and cries whenever he wants to”. He makes it seem like he cries because of fear from Allah ﷻ. For this, our Prophet ﷺ used to say, “Tears of a hypocrite are at the tip of his eyes”. He’s got so much evil and so many sins that when they are full, he cries whenever he wants and deceives people. It is not from iman or from fear from Allah ﷻ, it is from the evil. May Allah ﷻ protect. We see so many people crying on the TV. So what from that? ------------------------------------------------------------

“Whoever calls a hypocrite his master makes the Creator angry”. You should not say “My master” to a hypocrite. If you have to deal with him, don’t be close. Call him by his name when you have to. They cannot be called masters. If you call him your master, you will make Allah ﷻ angry. ------------------------------------------------------------

“When you see someone’s face yellow not because of a disease or tiredness, it is because of his heart cheating on Islam”. As we said, someone’s face can be yellow because of a disease or tiredness, and that will pass. But this yellowness is from the poison against Islam. Allah ﷻ didn’t give light to them but gave paleness. He gave bad color to the faces of these people. May Allah ﷻ protect. ------------------------------------------------------------

“Whoever has four attributes is an absolute hypocrite”. They say four out of four. When a man has all four of these attributes, he is a complete hypocrite.

“Whoever has one or two of these attributes is a hypocrite until he gets rid of them”. One or two attributes of a hypocrite make a man who has them half hypocrite.

“One is he lies when he speaks. Another is he doesn’t keep his promise. He cannot be trusted. And he exaggerates when he fights”. He doesn’t listen to advice, and when you make an agreement, he breaks it and hits you on your back. When he fights, you cannot escape from his hands, as he goes too far. These are the signs of a hypocrite. Who is not a hypocrite doesn’t go too far, he feels shame. ------------------------------------------------------------

Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla is saying, “I ﷻ created such people that their tongues are sweeter than honey”. It is hadith qudsi. When they speak, everyone becomes happy. They have such sweet tongues.

“But their hearts are more bitter than aloe”. The aloe plant has a very bitter taste. Hypocrites’ tongues are sweet but hearts are bitter. ------------------------------------------------------------

“I ﷻ swear on My glory, I ﷻ will put them in such fitnah and test that even clever ones among them will be confused”. They think they are cheating people and Allah ﷻ and getting benefit from it. Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla is swearing that He will put them in fitnah and test, and they won’t know what to do.

“Are they deceived by My mercy or daring against Me?” Certainly, these people are daring against Allah ﷻ. ------------------------------------------------------------

Our Prophet ﷺ is saying, “There are three categories, only hypocrites laugh at them”. There are three categories of people in Islam and whoever laughs at them is a hypocrite.

“They are people whose hair become white on the way of Islam, people with religious knowledge and fair rulers”. Whoever laughs at these people is a hypocrite. ------------------------------------------------------------

Our Prophet ﷺ is saying, “A mumin cries from his heart. A hypocrite cries from his head”. A hypocrite can cry instantly and cheats people with his tears. Believers are not so. Mumins are graceful, they cry from their hearts. ------------------------------------------------------------

Our Prophet ﷺ is saying, “There are three things that whoever does them is a hypocrite even though he prays, fasts and goes to hajj or umrah. They are lying while speaking, not keeping the promise and betraying one’s trust”. Praying and fasting don’t save you from hypocrisy. If you do these three things, you are a hypocrite until you stop them. ------------------------------------------------------------

Our Prophet ﷺ is saying, “Whoever shows himself as more fearing from Allah ﷻ than he really is, he is a hypocrite”. If a man says, “I’m a good servant, I am so and so” to show off, he is a hypocrite. ------------------------------------------------------------

Our Prophet ﷺ is saying, “A hypocrite has three attributes. When he speaks, he lies. When he gives a word, he doesn’t keep it. When you trust him something, he betrays your trust”. Our Prophet ﷺ is telling this over and over so that we get rid of these qualities, not just listen to them. If we have even one of them, we have to get rid of it. ------------------------------------------------------------

Our Prophet ﷺ is saying, “A hypocrite does not pray duha prayer and doesn’t read Surah al-Qafirun”. ------------------------------------------------------------

Our Prophet ﷺ is saying, “A hypocrite is in control of his eyes. He cries as he likes whenever he likes”. He is such a person that when he cries people get desperate too. And he betrays Muslims on the other side. When a hypocrite cries, he cries out of benefit, not for something else. ------------------------------------------------------------

Our Prophet ﷺ is saying, “There are twelve hypocrites among my sahabahs”. Their condition is worse than ours. Because they were with our Prophet ﷺ, saw so many things and yet became hypocrites. May Allah ﷻ protect us from hypocrisy. They thought our Prophet ﷺ didn’t know. But he ﷺ said “There are twelve hypocrites. Eight of them will not enter paradise until a camel passes through a needle hole”. Among those twelve, four sahabahs were fine, but eight are going to hell.

Sadaqa RasuluLlah fima qal aw kama qal.
