As-Salāmu ‘Alaykum wa RaḥmatuLlāhi wa Barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu BiLlāhi Minash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. BismiLlāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm.

Wa ṣ-Salātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Akhirīn.

Madad yā RasūlAllāh, Madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi RasūliLlāh, Madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

Dastūr Mawlana Sheikh Abdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistāni, Sheikh Muḥammad

Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad.Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-Suḥbah wa l-Khayru fi l-Jam‘iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm:

(Qur’ān 06:152). ‘Wa Lā Taqrabū Māla Al-Yatīmi ‘Illā Bi-Atī Hiya ‘Aĥsanu Ĥattá Yablugha ‘Ashuddahu, Wa ‘AwfūAl-Kayla Wa Al-Mīzāna Bil-Qisţi’, ‘And do not approach the orphan's property except in a way that is best until he reaches maturity. And give full measure and weight in justice.’ Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘Aẓīm.

Allah ‘Azza wa-Jalla says to respect the rights. Don’t take anyone’s right. Allah forgives everything. Allah is forgiving. But for the right of a servant, you must only ask for his forgiveness, or return his right so that his right is not on your shoulders. If that right remains to Qiyamah, you will have a hard time. Allah ‘Azza wa-Jalla will take from your Thawab and give to him. If you don’t have any more Thawab, He will load his sins onto you.

Therefore, everyone must return others’ rights in this world. No one should think “I’ve cheated him, I will have profit from that.” That is not profit but loss. That loss can be gone in this world. If you return the right while you’re still alive, it may be gone. But if he doesn’t forgive you, you have a hard time then. You should be careful not to take anyone’s rights while you’re alive. If you’ve taken others’ rights, you should go and ask for their forgiveness and return their rights.

Many people live by as Muslims. But they take others’ rights. It can be spiritually as well, not only physically. They close their eyes on what is right and put them in harm. That means taking the right. You are given a chance to be with the right. If you do the opposite, you commit a very big sin. Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says the worst is the false testimony. Our Holy Prophet said in one of his Hadiths, “The biggest sin is the false testimony.” Then he said again, “It is the false testimony. It is the false testimony.” They said, “If only our Holy Prophet stopped saying so.” He had repeated it so much, as the worst way of taking someone’s right is by making false testimony. So in order not to make false testimony, we must pay attention to the right spiritually. Not only materially, but also spiritually. May Allah protect us. May Allah not let us obey our egos. Our egos show the bad as good. Therefore, may Allah protect us.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.

There are recited Khatms for the honor of the holy night. There are Khatms of Yasin, Surahs, Ayat, Tasbihat, Tahlilat, Khatms of Dala’il al-Khayrat and Salawat. We gift all of them for the pleasure of Allah to our Holy Prophet Sayyidina Muhammad ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam wa Alihi wa Asahabihi l-kiram, wa ila arwahijamee’i al Anbiya’i wa l-Mursalin wa Khudama’i sharai’ihim wa ila arwahi al ‘Aimmati al arba’a, wa ilaarwahi Mashayyikhina fi t-Tariqati n-Naqshbandiyyati l-Aliyyah, khassatan Imamu t-Tariqah wa Ghawthu l-Khaliqah Khwaja Bahauddin Muhammad al-Uwaisiyi l-Bukhari, Sayyidina Abdul Khaliq al Ghujduwani, Mawlana Shaykh Sharafuddin al Daghestani, Mawlana Shaykh Abdullah al Faiz al Daghestani, Mawlana Shaykh Muhammad Nazim Adil al Haqqani, wa sai’ri Sadatina was-Siddiqiyun, Wa ila ruhi ‘aba’ina w ummahatina, ajdadina wa jaddatina, wa ila jamee’i al muslimin wal muslimat, wal mu’minin wal mu’minat al ‘ah’ya’i minhum wal amwat. ‘Ala niyyat al shifa’ wa daf’i al bala’, wal futuh wal faraj a’n al muslimin. For goodness to come and the evil to be gone. May the good intentions of those who recited be reached. May they be happy here and hereafter. Li-Llahi Ta’ala l-Fatiha.
