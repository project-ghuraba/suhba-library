O believers, Allāh Almighty honored us with the honor of faith. Allāh Almighty created mankind with honor. And He ordered that the honor should be guarded. Mankind is honored. And their life must be honored. They should reach the presence of Allāh Almighty with honor. This is the matter we are responsible for. Allāh Almighty is ordering "O My servants, I created you with honor. You must appreciate that honor and try to guard it."

There are people working for the government now. There are those serving in military. These are on the government's side. And there is their record. That record has an impact on their future. The record is important for the ranks they receive. Therefore, appointed people who serve for the government and those who serve for the military are very cautious about having a negative record in their books. Aren't they? All of them are. All of us understand it. And is there not a record of Allāh regarding us? He is without faith, who says there is not. "Wa-inna ‘alaykum la ḥāfiẓīn" (82:10) "Kirāman kātibīna ya‘lamūna mā taf‘alūn" (82:11-12) There are two recording angels above you who are respected in the presence of Allāh. They are appointed to keep your records. They are always with you, never leaving. When you sleep, one is by your head and the other is by your feet. If you move right and left, they write even this. They write if you say "ah" or "uf". "Why did My servant say "uf"?" That is written too.

Allāh doesn't want His servant to say "uf". "O My servant, I gave you so much. Why are you sighing? How are you saying "uf"? How do you deny My favour?" "Fabi’ayyi ālā’i rabbikumā tukadhdhibān" (55:13) O people and jinn, which of the favours of your Creator do you deny and show ungratefulness? They write our sighing as well. It doesn't suit a believer to sigh. O yā Rabbī, shukr. O yā Rabbī, laka l-ḥamd. When you say "oh", it gives relief. When you say "uf", distress comes. Yes. "Wa-inna ‘alaykum laḥāfiẓīn" (82:10) The angel of protection, "ya‘lamūna mā taf‘alūn" (82:12) sees everything you do as complete. Maybe you don't weigh what you say, you don't weigh what you do. You're not aware if the angel of the right or left is writing it. Those angels know how to write your every word. Our record is kept. We'll reach Qiyāmah with 70 records.

In old times, before these tools were invented, we were still reading these ḥadīth sharīf and news. In those news, it said every servant will go to maḥshar with 70 records. We could never understand how these films recorded whole life stories and could extend so long till the horizon. However, Allāh made these tools to be invented. They capture our words. There are other tools which record our every action both words and gestures. They are the video cassettes, they record everything. The ones on our right and left are more perfect. These are just inventions of the servants. The record by the order of Allāh is not like this. It is so perfect and based on perfection. They will make you watch it. And you'll be informed about your 70 records in a moment. They won't make you wait there to show page by page. You will be shown all your life in one look. "Let's see, O My servant, look at your record. Isn't it nice? If it is nice, mā shā’a Llāh. If not, have a look. Did I teach you to live like this? Did you like this lifestyle? If you record the actions of a drunk man and show it afterwards, he'll be embarrassed. He'll say "How come?" You misbehaved while you were drunk. Do you like it? Now look when you're sober." "An-nāsu niyāmun fa’idhā mātu intabahū" People are in the sleep of heedlessness. They are drunk. They will wake up after they die. What is its use? What's the use of waking up then? The record is made already. Don't make or let your record be dark. It should be clean.

What can we do? We are not innocent. We know it. Mankind is sinful. "Al-insānu murakkabun mina n-nisyān" We forget and make mistakes. But if Allāh Almighty didn't give water, no one would be able to clean. Yes. A man gets dirty. But the water makes everything clean. "Ahlaktu banī Ādama bi dh-dhunūb fa ahlakūnī bi l-istighfār". Shayṭān said "I will finish the sons of Ādam with sin. But they finish me when they say "tawbah yā Rabbī astaghfiru Llāh" and make istighfār. I run after them. They follow me and I make them do every evil. Then one day will come, tawbah yā Rabbī, they wake up and make istighfār. And Allāh Almighty will forgive them. Then they will finish me with istighfār". Yes.

Allāh Almighty knows that we commit sin. He is not unknowing. However, He is showing us the way of cleaning up from sins and giving us a chance. "Make tawbah. O My servants, make tawbah." As soon as you know that your action is bad and dishonoring, leave it. Let me say in authentic Turkish. Does the one who fell into the toilet feel himself in comfort? Doesn't he want to come out of it immediately? If a sinful person knows the evil of his sin, will he keep it? A sin is evil. Coming against Allāh is dirtiness and shame. Don't fall into it. If anyone comes close to fire, what strength does he show to step back? Or as we said, if he falls into dirt, how much will he resist not to fall? When shayṭān pushes you towards sin and rebelliousness and your ego obeys, resist it. Oppose it. Then Allāh Almighty will order to release you from those black spots of darkness in your heart and in your records.

Therefore, when you wake up in the morning, O believers, say "Yā Rabbī, I reached the morning as a believer. I woke up with my faith in You. Protect me from every action that will damage my faith today. Protect me from people who will harm my faith. Keep me away from such deeds as well." And in the evening, "yā Rabbī, I made many mistakes but I repent. Yā Rabbī, forgive me". Ask this and make shahādah, repent. Maybe you fall asleep and when you're asleep, your soul won't return from where it goes and you die. There are many who slept and didn't wake up. Allāh Almighty knows the reason for our death. If it's not to wake up after sleep, let your last words be shahādah. The one who passes away with shahādah and repentance will certainly reach the forgiveness of Allāh Almighty.

O believers, life with honor is for us. Let us be cautious. This month is the last month of the year, which is the custom of the whole world and by us also. It's the twelfth month. And we have entered the last week of the twelfth month. The whole world is in a strange condition. Amazing drunkenness is covering the whole world when the last week comes. It was not like this in old times. Now we are trying not to fall behind them pleading modernity. I don't know, when you think in your conscience, will we reach Allāh Almighty with our these actions or will we get further away? Pay attention to that as well. The identity of our nation is not the same as the identity of unbelievers. We have customs and traditions. We have feasts and holidays. We have everything. We don't have to follow those who are not from us. No. We are not an orphan nation and we are not a nation without roots. We existed in the beginning of history. In shā’a Llāhu r-Raḥmān, we'll exist till its end. We are not orphans to celebrate holidays of this and that, to feast on their feasts. "But the world is celebrating." The world is different. Your national identity is different. Your religious identity is different. This Islamic world, and now our community, cannot appreciate it most of the time. They cannot pay attention to it. They should.

Therefore - they have Christmas this week. Before it was only the night of the 31st of December because January is the beginning of every Gregorian year. They would celebrate that night. Now a couple of Christian missionaries and Jewish are getting prepared for Christmas. They are accustomed to it being on the 25th. The Noel feast of the Christians. We have the feast too. We call it Ayvasili feast in Cyprus. Blind musicians playing violin and so go out on that day, begging and calling for him. That is Ayvasili feast. That was the day only known like this. And we didn't know anything more about this day and night. And now they are trying to make everything like others spending a lot. This comes beyond ignorance. It is rejecting our individuality. It's not our way. It is a communal shame. It is a sin in the presence of Allāh. Yes. We have to be cautious about it. "Everything passes. No harm", your ego is upset with you. Shayṭān will be upset too that you don't do it. This will pass too. They won't get up with honor that day or that night. Honor is for us.

May Allāh make us from the servants who hear the word of truth and follow it.
