As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam is saying that whoever makes something up that is not in the religion and spreads it, punishment for its mistakes, and sins committed by everyone because of it will be on him. If there is one person, one punishment comes. If two persons follow it, punishment for these two persons will come to him. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla said so. Because bid‘ah means spreading bad things which are not in Islām for personal benefit or to make fitnah. There are many of such dajjāl type people who want to mislead people from the right way. There will always be small dajjāls and cheaters before the big dajjāl appears. They will do things for their own benefit. And the people who believe in them will disappear. May AllāhNone protect us. Therefore, our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says to be careful. Don't follow such people; don't follow things that are not in the religion.

Shukr to AllāhNone, there are millions of saints and scholars brought up in the Islamic world. There are beautiful books and beautiful knowledge from all of them. But people of the present time... There were such people in old times too. There were people with bad intentions. But none of them have any importance. Those who followed them went astray. Some of them left Islām and some of them left the way. That is not so important. But they are a lot in the present time. Those who follow them will disappear. Some of them are fake scholars. Just like fake scholars, there are fake Shaykhs. There are people who make ḥalāl things ḥarām and ḥarām things ḥalāl as they like. There are such people. Some of them are quickly forgotten. But some of them leave bad works. People who follow those works are in loss, but it goes on like that. Those who follow them...

AllāhNone has given understanding. The way is clear, and the truth is clear. Those who follow them do so because their egos like it. You cannot go after something your ego likes. That is not the true way. The true way is clear. May AllāhNone grant protection from such people. Because people go to a Shaykh and he says, "I am a Khalīfah. I am this. I am above that." Many people claim so. Those who have weak willpower and strong egos follow them. And then they will vanish. May AllāhNone protect from their evil. Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, "Who make something not in Islām, and saying, 'This is from Islām, follow this' – who follow this one, he will be punished." He has punishment for one, for one who follow him. Second, if there is second also, he will be punished for two people. For hundred people, hundred time he will punished. Thousand, also thousand time. This is for, to prevent people to make divide in Islām, or to make people to go away from reality and following them.

This kind of people, all time they was in... since even end of time of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, many liar "prophets", they said they was going around this Arabia, and they whole finish. They destroyed, but they make big fitnah. This is what Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying [calling] bid‘ah. Bid‘ah – bad thing to do, what is not in Islām. Not like this people who saying bid‘ah – accusing real mu’min, real Muslim they are bid‘ah. This is also – they are real bid‘ah people. But, who follow Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, and his companions, ṣaḥābah and scholar, ‘ulamā’, awliyā’, beloved ones for AllāhNone; there is million of them. They have good book and follow way of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam This people, they still same, not changing.

But there is fitnah every time. And especially in the end of the time, many people they are claiming they are scholar, they are understanding; but they are not understanding anything and they make fitnah. And they will be punished for this, what they are taking people from right way and claiming they are awliyā’, they are shaykh, they are scholar. There many of this people; you find everywhere. But for small period and they will disappear. But when they are disappear, maybe some people will be going with them. Why this people they are going with them? Because, they are happy – their ego strong, and their willpower, weak. Not strong. They like what this people offering them as ṭarīqah, as sharī‘ah, as religion, they are happy with this. But reality is openly appear for everybody. They mustn't look for this, like this people. Because this people they want this for their benefit – to benefit from people. And they are not caring about what will happen for this people at the end. And they are – because they are not believer, only appearing like believer. They are only like some insect, going from to suck blood from people. Like this, these people. And they're worse. Maybe this animals better than them. Because AllāhNone create them to be like this but this people they not create for to cheat people, and to make people who are in right way to go out of the way.

AllāhNone save us from this. AllāhNone make our ego weak. And our willpower strong, in shā’a Llāh. To not follow to this people. And they are liar, they are – what they are doing, they are everywhere. Now especially they have this internet. You can find thousand of them. But they don't have any value. AllāhNone give hidāyah for people and to make people to know who they are following, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
