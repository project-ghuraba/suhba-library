Aū‘dhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-Shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Waṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-F’āiz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-A’ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm: “Wa qul Rabbi zidnī ‘ilmā” (Qur’ān 20:114). It is a holy verse where Allah advises us to ask Him to increase our knowledge. It is Allah’s order, a task for us. It is Allah’s command to seek knowledge. For a Muslim to learn is an obligation until death.

Whether you go to a madrassa or you learn something new everyday, a Muslim should learn knowledge for the sake of Allah. Knowledge that is not for the sake of Allah is not knowledge but ignorance. It has no benefit. Our Holy Prophet (SAW) says, “ I seek refuge from useless knowledge.” Some people learn and read, but they do the opposite of what they learn. So seeking knowledge is an order of Allah, and let us do it just as Allah wants and wills, insha’Allah.

Thanks be to Allah that at this [our] madrassah people no longer have to come physically, but it comes all the way to them now. Slowly but surely, however much they can do, they should do. Nobody is pushing them, but they themselves will benefit from it. This will be a benefit too, insha’Allah.

This is a beautiful thing, because unlike in the past when people had to go to another place for knowledge, and stay away from their families for years, and be cut off from the world, now there are more opportunities and means. But [still] it is difficult. More people can join in nowadays by using these means, but there is no trust. There are those who, instead of being useful, are harmful most of the time, and as we have seen their knowledge to be, their teachings have confused the Muslim world. People go all the way to certain countries, and then they come back to their countries not with knowledge but with fitna. So insha’Allah what we are doing is for the sake of Allah, and therefore it maybe useful for everybody and for Muslims, insha’Allah. This is the intention, and may Allah give us according to our intention. May He accept it and what we are trying to do, because we are trying hard, and there is fitna, a very serious fitna that makes it unclear what is good and what is bad.

So insha’Allah, these things that are done with sincerity are being done five days a week for the sake of Allah, and the teaching will be based on the most trustworthy authors and books. Of course, the first year will be a preparatory year, and after that students can start fully [into the subjects during] the second year. We took a step. May Allah be our helper. May Allah make it for guidance, insha’Allah. Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-F ti ah
