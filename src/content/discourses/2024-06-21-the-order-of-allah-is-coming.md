As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla sent our Holy ProphetNone as the last prophet. And among hisNone signs of prophethood, a specialty of the Prophet is to foretell and foresee the future. That specialty is given to prophets from the knowledge of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Prophethood is nubuwwah; it is to foresee and to tell the future. Also, in Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n, there are many āyāt that tell about and show the future.

Sometimes people understand them, sometimes they don't. But this āyah we read in the khuṭbah is showing clearly where we are heading. So, take precautions accordingly, says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Mankind says, “I rule over the world. Everything is in my hands. I have control over everything. Nothing will affect us, no one will, when we are here.” They don't recognise AllāhNone or God anymore. They say this, “We control everything.’’ Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says: “WeNone will make it flat in a moment either in daytime, or at night. We will make as if there was nothing here the day before,” says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. “The order has come.” When the order of AllāhNone comes, the order of AllāhNone will come. Therefore, don't think we control every situation, everything in this world, and don't forget Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Everything is in the hands of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.

Everything will be gone in an instant. Don't be gone with everything going. At least, you must have something to return to AllāhNone. You must have good deeds to save you. Otherwise, if you are with those who are against Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla you will perish. Nothing will remain in this world anyway. And then there will also be a disaster in ākhirah. These are the days of the end of times. We see everywhere that strange events are taking place. Things we couldn't imagine before are happening. People who see them are cheated and start following them. Who follow them will perish. Who don't walk on the way of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla but believe other people …Clever ones don't do that. Because other people are human too. If you follow them, in the end, neither you, nor they will stay.

Therefore, be with Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla who is eternal. Don't be with those who will die and disappear. People who are against Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla will disappear. They will vanish. “Don't vanish together with them,” says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. “WeNone are giving you an advice, WeNone are advising you,” says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. But who listens to advice? No one. Because people are like it is said in the ḥadīth sharīf of our Holy ProphetNone: “They are worse day by day.” With each coming day, they are not better but worse and worse. They are worse in terms of īmān (faith). They are worse in terms of everything. People were better in old times. They are getting worse now. Yet, people don't take lessons from this. They continue, whereas if they knew this and sought refuge in AllāhNone, they would profit. When they don't trust HimNone, they lose on both sides. There is no benefit for people in it. The way is the way of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. It is the way of salvation. Because the world is reaching its end. No matter if the end comes or not, people’s lives are ending.

Some people say there is no Judgement Day and that there is still a lot of time. Do you think you are going to live until Judgement Day? If those people use their minds, they will profit. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla has given us mind. But only those who believe in Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla use their minds. Who don't believe in AllāhNone don't use their minds, unfortunately. We ask that AllāhNone gives understanding. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, send HisNone Prophets, all prophets and prophecy, meaning – to tell the future. Nabīy, prophet, the meaning. Of course, English, you know. This prophecy until whole prophets, they have this. Qur’ān saying, “When earth become very …” Very good as you say. Everything can be easy for human being – building, road, planes, rocket. Everything become as they are …They thought they control world. When this people, they thought they control everything in this world and they will be sure for this, suddenly it will be when AllāhNone, order of AllāhNone coming. Day or night it can be. All this going like taking with broom as it is nothing, [although] it was before yesterday here. When AllāhNone want, order coming, it will be like this. Nobody can prevent this. You will be more weak because Sayyidunā ‘Umar, he said, “When you are the most strong one, the weakest one, if he [is] oppressed, he is the most strong one. I will be with him. No. No power, nothing for Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla.

Everything will be finish, in shā’a Llāh. We are living in time as Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, saying, “Every day it is become worse than other day. Every day coming worse than other day.” So don't think – if you go with this people, you will be more stronger. No. When you be with AllāhNone, you will be more stronger, you will be safe, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone save us from this bad people and we be away from them, in shā’a Llāh.
