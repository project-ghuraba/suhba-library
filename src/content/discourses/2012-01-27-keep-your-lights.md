Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Rahim Allahu Akbar Allahu Akbar la Ilaha illa Allah w'Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar wa Lillahi Al Hamd alfu salam alfu salat ala Sayyid al Awwalin wal Akhirin Sayyidina Muhammad (sas), ishfa'lana Ya Rasulullah. Audhu Billahi min al shaytan al-rajim Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Rahim

“Wa Ma Arsalnaka Illa Rahmatan Lil Alamina We sent you not, except as a Mercy for all Creation” (21:107)

Assalamu alaykum o servants of Allah (swt) Assalamu alaykum O believers men & ladies May our Lord accept from me and from you, accept our best. Try to be good servants, it is a holy month, Mawlid an-Nabi, Milad an-Nabi, (sas) Increase your praisings, make much more praising and blessing upon Sayid al Awwalin wal Akhirin We are saying, audhu Billahi min al shaytan al-rajim Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Rahim We are happy that we have reached most holy month, Rabi'ul Awwal in which Allah Almighty is sending His most beloved servant Sayidina Muhammad (sas) to be a Mercy for all nations. But, I am sorry to say that even the Muslim world are not following advices of the Seal of Prophets (sas). And so many, so many heavy punishments coming on mankind. O people, come and say, Astaghfirullah, Astaghfirullah. We repent and turn to you, O our Lord.

Ooohh... O people, everywhere for Muslim world according to High Command of Allah almighty, saying: Dhakkir - remind. Remind people that Last Day, approaching. Everyone is going to die, everyone is going to pass away. Where are they going? Everyone is asking and going to Divine Presence of Allah almighty. Try to make your face in Lights of Islam. Keep yourself, keep your lights, keep the 'Nur' that Allah almighty granted to you. O people, don't fight each other, Allah prohibits killing His servants. And now, tyrants killing innocent people.

O people, ask from Allah almighty, Libyan people, Egyptian people, Turkish people, Syrian people, Arab people, Hind people, Ajam people, asking from Allah almighty to send to you one of His... He knows, one of His... Sultans, to collect you under the holy flag of the Seal of Prophets (sas). O people come and listen, if you are not listening, heavenly punishment, day by day approaching. Ask O Muslim World, Khalifah, Khilafat al Islamiyah. Allah almighty says “Wa A`tasimu Bihabli Allahi Jami`an Wa La Tafarraqu And hold fast, all of you together to the Rope of Allah and be not divided"(3:103)

Being divided - bringing so many heavy punishments, O people. "and be not divided" (3:103) O Arab World, come and ask for a Deputy of Allah on Earth, and give your oath to him and sit down, and follow Orders of Heavens. There is no other way for Muslim world. O people, a very difficult time, we are in it. And, Allah almighty's punishment little by little, coming on people. But they say 'It is from nature'. Not nature but (from) Allah almighty Who created everything and nature also, giving to you a caution,

'O people take your caution,' because the last Day is approaching, and everyone is going to pass away quickly, up to the end of 15th Century of Islam. May Allah forgive us, O people. I am weak, I am asking from Allah almighty and His most beloved servant, (asking) for his honour, to reach good things with Mahdi (as) and Isa (as), but I don't know, I don't know, I don't know.

And all Awliya are asking, to do something, but, everything must reach to their last point, from which to move. Reaching there, everything is going to be "Kun Fayakunu Be!" and it is!" (36:82)

Saying 'Be!' And everything is going to be according to Heavenly program. Don't run after dunya, but run to reach your Lord's pleasure. Nothing else! If He is pleased with you, you are pleased forever. If He is not pleased with you, up to infinity, that person is going to be in troubles, and he is losing his chance for paradises. O people, pray for me, pray for everyone so that, they are able to reach those brilliant days with Mahdi (as) and with Jesus Christ (pbuh). Therefore, Seal of Prophets (sas) I am asking forgiveness, from Allah almighty, after forgiveness, coming blessings.

May Allah bless you, O Mankind. O Mankind, keep your ways,...keep your ways to reach the Seal of Prophets (sas), you will be happy here and Hereafter. May Allah forgive me and forgive you, forgive everyone who is on wrong way, and sends Mahdi (as) to take us on Right Way May Allah give His Mercy on me, on you, on mankind, and make shaytan down, down, down. May Allah send us His Mercy one drop is enough to clean...Ya Rabbi! Assalamu alaykum Fatiha..Baya'
