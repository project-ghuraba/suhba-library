As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
وَلِلَّهِ عَلَى النَّاسِ حِجُّ الْبَيْتِ مَنِ اسْتَطَاعَ إِلَيْهِ سَبِيلًا
<br>“Wa li-Llāhi ʿalā n-nāsi ḥijju l-Bayti mani staṭāʿa ilayhi sabīla”<br>
“And [due] to Allah from the people is a pilgrimage to the House - for whoever is able to find thereto a way.” (Qurʼān 3:97). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

To carry out the orders of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla everyone may do as he likes. What is the order of Allah? It is prayer, Ḥajj, Zakah. They must do all of them and what is Fard as much as they can. For Ḥajj, sometimes it requires good health and money. Same with Zakah. They are like that. Also fasting; who has good health may fast. Who doesn’t may give money instead. But there is no skipping of prayer. It is Fard to do prayer. Not praying is a sin. Unless they make it up as Qada’, it remains to Akhirah. And they will do it with more hardship in Akhirah. But in this world, as we said, you must do what should be done. There is nothing like you must do or not do it.

Sometimes they are forbidding it. The sin is on those who forbid it. People must still do what is Fard as much as they can. If they don’t, the sin and punishment are on those who prevent it. The punishment is for those who don’t open the way and don’t let people do it. But praying as we said is Fard even in bed by moving eyes. No one can prevent it. No one can stop it. If you couldn’t pray during the day, you can pray at night. If you are sick, you can make Tayammum and perform your prayer, because it is from wherever you are. But the matter of Ḥajj, there may be no opportunity if the ways are closed. 

Therefore, Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says,<br>
مَنِ اسْتَطَاعَ إِلَيْهِ سَبِيلًا
<br>“Mani staṭāʿa ilayhi sabīla”<br>
“For whoever is able to find thereto a way.” (Qurʼān 3:97). 

It is Fard to those who can reach.

The situation in this world is as such that for every Muslim Ḥajj is always difficult. And the hardship of the present time is the limitation of it, that very few people go there. That is an obstacle. That is an evil obstacle. Who cannot do it is not responsible for it. Responsible are those who prevent it.

Inshā’Allāh there is a month until Ḥajj. Ḥajjis will probably start going now. May Allah bless it for those who are able to go. And may He let those who haven’t done it do this beautiful worship inshā’Allāh. Because Ḥajj is not only going there and making Tawaf, it is the means for all committed sins to be forgiven.

Our Holy Prophet ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam says people who perform Ḥajj must not leave unresolved rights around them. There is this as well. They must ask for people’s forgiveness if they oppressed anyone and had something against someone, they must make amends. Those who do this come absolutely clean as if just born. They come without a sin. May Allah make them successful. May He open the way for those who haven’t gone yet. May He let them become Ḥajji.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq

Al-Fātiḥah

There are recited Qur’an Khatms, Yasins, Ayat and Surahs, Tasbihat, Tahlilat, Salawat, Khatms of Dala’il al-Khayrat. Whatever else is recited by our brothers from all over the world by the morning of this beautiful day, may Allah accept them. We gift them firstly to our Holy Prophet Muḥammad ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, his Ahlu l-Bayt, Sahabahs, souls of all Ambiya, Awliyā, Asfiya and mashāyikh, souls of all Mu’mins and Muslims. For goodness to come and evil to be gone. May all good goals of those who recited be reached.

Li-Llahi Ta’ala l-Fātiḥah
