Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Thank Allah ﷻ, He created us like that, He created us beautifully. He is ‘ نُ سَ حْ َأ نَ يقِلِاخَ لْا’, ‘The Best of Creators’. Allah Azza wa Jalla created everything beautifully. Therefore, we should thank Him. Being thankful is also done with worship, with obedience and with Taqwā (fear of Allah). Allah ﷻ has given everything to these people, everything is for them. He has put everything at the service of humankind. And humankind is being disobedient, not believing and thinking he is something. They read a few things and become beyond proud.

Pride is an attribute that Allah ﷻ dislikes. Why doesn’t He like it? Because pride belongs to Himself. No one can have pride except for Allah Azza wa Jalla. ‘ يِف ءُ ايَ رِبْ كِ لْا ُهَلوَ ضِ رْ َلْْاوَ تِ اوَ امَ سَّ لا’, ‘And to Him belongs [all] grandeur within the heavens and the earth.’ (Qur’ān 45:37). His pride is in the heavens and the earth, because Haqq belongs to Allah ﷻ. He does not want partners with Himself. Pride is for Allah ﷻ. Allah ﷻ does not like a person who is proud and our Holy Prophet ﷺ doesn’t like him either. His account will be big. Many people perished in misery and went to hell because of pride. Some of them went to hell for eternity. And some even if not for eternity, they have still entered hell because of pride.

Therefore, pride is not a good thing, humility is a good thing. It is good to be humble towards Allah Azza wa Jalla and to obey Allah ﷻ. We thank Allah ﷻ that He created us in this state. May Allah ﷻ protect us from the evil of our ego. It can be all kinds of pride. Even people of worship can also be proud. If they mix it up with worship, then shaytan ruins it too. We need to be careful of this. If we worship, we do it with the help of Allah ﷻ, and that’s why we should thank Allah ﷻ. There is no need to be proud about it either. “I pray that much, I do that, I went to Ḥajj ten times, I went to ʿUmrah twenty-five times.” It is not good to mention them. These should be hidden. If you are using them as a means to be proud, then they are useless. May Allah ﷻ protect us. May Allah ﷻ not deviate us from the right path, and not make us follow our ego.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
