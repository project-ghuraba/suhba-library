As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
وَلَا تَقْرَبُوا الْفَوَاحِشَ
<br>“Wa lā taqrabū al-fawāhisha”<br>
“And do not approach shameful deeds.” (Qur’ān 06:151). ṢadaqAllahu l-‘Aẓīm.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla commands us to stay away from sins as much as possible. Don’t even approach them. This is because if you say, “I can save myself,” you might fall into more sin. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla makes it a test for people by His command. Those who stay away from it become among Allah’s beloved servants. If those who fall into sins don’t seek forgiveness, they will fall into sin even more, it will become a habit, and they won’t be able to give it up.

Now, because we are in the end of times, these evils have increased greatly. It has become very bad. In the past, this was very common among non-Muslims. All kinds of evil were present among them. Now, everywhere in the world, Muslims and non-Muslims consider sins normal and commit them. And as they do them, they sink deeper. Just as a person cannot escape from the swamp, it is the same. Therefore, you must flee from the swamp without even approaching it. If you fall into it, if your foot sinks, immediately pull yourself out, repent, and seek forgiveness. Then, Allah will forgive you. No, if you make it worse, the more you get involved, the deeper you sink. In the end, it will be difficult to save yourself.

Therefore, even if a person constantly commits sins – mankind commits sins – Allah created humans so that they would sin and He would forgive them. But if people continue doing those things without even thinking about forgiveness, thinking, “I won’t be forgiven,” then they will destroy themselves, their ākhirah will be destroyed, and they will remain in a bad place, in hell, forever. May Allah protect us.

Repentance and seeking forgiveness is a great niʿmah (favour) for us. One of the greatest favours given by Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla is: repentance and seeking forgiveness. The Door of Repentance is open to us until the sun rises from the west. His mercy is everlasting. Therefore, may Allah forgive us. May Allah protect us from sins and keep us away from them. May He grant us His mercy.

In Qur’ān ʿAẓīmu sh-Sha’n,<br>
‘وَقِهِمُ السَّيِّئَاتِ ۚ وَمَن تَقِ السَّيِّئَاتِ يَوْمَئِذٍ فَقَدْ رَحِمْتَهُ
<br>“Wa qihimu as-sayyi’āti, wa man taqī as-sayyi’āti yawma’idhin faqad raḥimtah”<br>
“And protect them from the evil consequences [of their deeds]. And he whom You protect from evil consequences that Day - You will have given him mercy.” (Qur’ān 40:09).

May Allah protect us from sins. A person protected from sins attains Allah’s mercy. That mercy keeps one away from sins. If one commits a sin, and repents, Allah forgives him. May Allah make us among all the people He forgives, inshā’Allah.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
