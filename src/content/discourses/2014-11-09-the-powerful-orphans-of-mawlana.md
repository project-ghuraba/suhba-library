Sheikh Bahauddin Adil Al-Haqqani sohbat of the 9th of November, 2014.

Ya salam! This is we still in heavenly music. Always insha'Allah. They say the music it is the medicine of souls. I think it is wrong. The music is the medicine of ego. Zikr is the medicine of the soul. So if you mix the ego and the soul together, you will have this kind of music.

Alhamdulillah now we are in modern way, in modern time. Maybe before 50 years you cannot get such a thing. Those days the people they have other style of living. And it is very hard to move people from their living style for new living style. What happened? The happening it was so simple - the world become global. Because we have the network that connecting everybody to each other. And from that concept, as we are human so we have to take little bit, little bit from each flavor of the nations and make it simple to be digested for everybody. So they call it a global things among the people. If you are going outside on the street you see the people same face, same haircut, same dress, same fashion. If you have something new, you can find it in another country. You can find it in another place. So, basically the world become as a one. It didn't get so many things to be seen unless you go for the jungle in Africa, so you see the tribes that they're still having their old traditions, old things.

Now, for this matter as we are Naqshbandi people, we have our old used and our all use it was Mawlana Sheikh. It is important for murid to understand this. Now we've been moved out from station called Mawlana Sheikh Nazim to station called, Mawlana Sheikh Muhammad. We have to adapt ourselves for the new station. Our old station it is still alive in our heart. So this is the real seed of our being - our love to Mawlana Sheikh. And this nobody is telling you to get rid of it. No need to get rid of it. You just have to adapt to the new situation.

What is our new situation? Our new situation it is like this: Mawlana Sheikh he spoiled us very much. This is we have to know, we've been spoiled by Mawlana Sheikh. Because, the way that he treated us, he treated us, so tolerant in everything. He was so tolerant in himself also. It is the real meaning of full-time Sheikh. That he is giving from himself for his murid, for his children. I never say, Mawlana Sheikh murid, I all the time say Mawlana Sheikh children. And I mean it, because Mawlana Sheikh he was treating all his murid in the way that he loved them like his children. There is no discrimination among his children. And now we have new things coming. The new things, now we have to be adapted to our new station as we will be orphaned - in orphan age and waiting for somebody to give us this love again. This is how we are, and unfortunately we've been waiting for somebody to be mercy on us, but we have been trying to be hitted badly and to be bite badly. And this is, it is hurt. It really hurts.

We are orphaned, to know. Alhamdulillah, maybe we are orphan but we are strong orphans, we are not weak. We are the orphan that can say "yes! We still have our brother and sister with us. And we want them to be with us, we are one family. We are one with Mawlana Sheikh, we are still under his wings because he is still alive in our heart. He is still alive in our being." Why we are putting the turban? We are putting the turban to know that we are the children of Sheikh Nazim. This is the main reason, to know that this is why this turban. This turban it is just to show that we are still his children. Even me I am part-time turban wearing but still I love the turban. I love Mawlana Sheikh and I love all my brother and sister. This is the main things in our life.

Because if we are looking for the normal behaviour in our life, there is nothing much. Because you work, you gain money, you lose money. You grow up your children. You marry your children. You see them grow grandchildren. This is classic life. So this is everybody having this life. Everybody has this life. It is not something new. They call it monotone. Or they call it daily things or they call it every sun rising and sun setting - same thing. Except when you are with Mawlana Sheikh you have everyday upside down day. No day look like the others. No way to be same day as the others. And this is speciality of Mawlana Sheikh. This is how Mawlana Sheikh he was going on and on and on with our life. That's why they say "hey you are, what happened to you? What's wrong with you? You're never having real deep sadness, you're never having real deep problem, you never have deep things in your life." We say Alhamdulillah, because Mawlana Sheikh he didn't make every day the same he is every day asking "did you get something more in your life? Did you get something better in your life? Did you do something it doesn't make you to feel yesterday is better than tomorrow? Mawlana Sheikh he was all the time chasing us to work. Not to look for things that, mala ya'ni (useless things).

Mawlana Sheikh he never cared about giving ranks because all of us for him we are high rank; because we are his children; because we are the ones with him. This is how Mawlana Sheikh he was treated us. Imagine the king he has 50 children. Maybe the youngest, do you think he is lower than number one? All children, his children, you cannot make level in the children, this is not right. Never right. One mother she has 40 children, one of them missing, she remains with 39. She will not see the 39 because is belonging to her, she will look for the one missing. Because she says "my child is missing" and she has all her life praying for that child. Imagine 40 children and 1 missing. He is missing, she will not care about the 39 because they are with her. But the 1 missing she will look after. This is how Mawlana Sheikh not saying to anybody "O no you are not belonging to me." No, Mawlana Sheikh he was looking for all his murid around the world, his children. So this is, then there is no don't go for leveling. It's wrong. It's wrong to be in leveling. Just enjoy your position. The son of the king maybe the last one, maybe the king even he didn't see him. But when he goes for society he says "I am the son of the king!" And everybody will respect him. And this is how it will be respect among the Awliya'.

They say these are the children of Sheikh Nazim. These are the children of him, how they will be respected. This is not only world matter. We have spiritual matter that Mawlana Sheikh he is there. And we know, and we see and we understand. This is how Mawlana Sheikh he was and he is still remaining. So now we have to go for the new station that we are all orphan. And we having, been in orphan age. But we are strong, Alhamdulillah. We are very strong because our father is strong. This you have to understand. Our father is really strong. Because our father is the one that he didn't left us. He didn't left us, we still feel him in our heart. We still having the behaviour that he is making us to be as we used to be with him. We continue, this is never ending. Don't think it will end. Don't think it will be ending. Everybody of us has something from Mawlana Sheikh, this the spiritual touch. I was going to say, magic touch, but no it is spiritual touch in every heart, in each. This is how Mawlana Sheikh, this is how great he is.

Mawlana Sheikh never been interested in positions. Never been interested in money wise. Never been interested in anything. This is Mawlana Sheikh. I will tell you this story of Mawlana Sheikh that it make me understand more. One day, Mawlana Sheikh he call: "come, come." And he put his hand in his pocket and he take 10 Lira. And he says "this 10 lira, been given by this old lady." And he tell me, "you see this old lady?", - yes - she have retirement money, very little, very little. But every month she goes and get her retirement money she comes and she gives something for Sheikh. Say, "do you know, I am as a Sheikh I have to accept her money. I cannot ignore her and tell her - you need this money more than me, keep it. - Say, No, it is not good manner." He say, how I make her to go about her intention. Maybe if she gives 1 lira also it doesn't matter, this is her intention to serve the Sheikh because she understands that this is her father that she has to serve him and even she has nothing to give, to give her love also, must be accepted. This it was a big lesson to me, it was most amazing lesson to me. And then he was saying to me, "my son our duty is not to keep this one. We have to find somebody else to give it away."

That's why Mawlana Sheikh he was like this! Never keeping anything, never want to give keep anything. And he was telling me "zibil, zibil! This all rubbish, this money rubbish. Send it away, send it away." He never, never keep. This is Mawlana Sheikh. This reminds me with another story, the one they are living in Alaska. And this Yakutistan, a country called Yakutistan. They are finding gold, finding gold and they are hunting leather and things. And they go for the capital and they trade with them and they give them money, paper money. And when they see the paper money they panic. And immediately they go and they buy material; they buy something useful for their use. Say, "Alhamdulillah we got rid of this paper." And Mawlana Sheikh he says "zibil, zibil, this is zibil this is rubbish. Must get rid of it." It is nobody can be like Mawlana Sheikh, nobody.

It is very hard to be like Mawlana Sheikh. And nobody can even come near to Mawlana Sheikh. It is impossible. It is impossible. Mawlana Sheikh was man coming every 1000 year - This kind of man. There is so many men, but only 1 come every 1000 years. Alhamdulillah I'm saying shukr Ya Rabbi Alhamdulillah at least we have the honour to be in his table, to be in his presence; at least to see his face, kiss his hand. This is very important thing for us. May Allah make us under his feet. And may Allah make him happy with us. And may Allah make us to be in ikhlas to him. And not make us ikhlas to our ego and our egoistic desires. This really hurts. Because you spend 10.000 years with your Sheikh and finally you find out yourself outside of your Sheikh. This is really hurt. It is not hurt us, it hurt them.

This is important to know where you are stepping and where you are being, and where you'll be. Important where you will be. Ne idim, ne oldum, ne olacam - (what I was, what I have been, what I will be) in Turkish they say. What I was, what I am now and I will be. The most important is what I will be. This is a huge lesson there. Because the past is past. The present maybe we are strong now, but after a while we don't know what we will do. This is also important to know that we are not remaining forever here. And we will not be in powerful forever here and we will not be safe by sitting in our seat. A few months ago there was a chair, now there is no chair. I am sitting in the ground. What shall I do? I'm still sitting, and I'm still talking. But I don't know what will happen later. Nas'al Allah.

So, beware about what is coming. Don't be proud that you are sitting in Mawlana's chair. No, they will take it. Say "No! Finished!" And may Allah forgive us and forgive you and make us from the lovers of Mawlana Sheikh. Make us from the one having sincerity for Mawlana Sheikh. And make us from the one that we want to be with Mawlana Sheikh, whatever the price is.

Wa min Allahi t-tawfiq, bi hurmati l-Habib bi hurmati l-Fatiha.
