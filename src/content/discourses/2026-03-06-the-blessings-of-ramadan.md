As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Ramaḍān is a blessed month, a very beautiful month. In it are many beautiful days and nights. Shukr to Allah many beautiful things happened to our Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam in this month.

One of these beautiful things was the birthday of Sayyidinā Husayn, which was on the 15th of Ramaḍān. He was one of our Holy Prophet’s most beloved people. When Sayyidinā Hasan and Sayyidinā Husayn would arrive, our Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam would come down from the pulpit, laugh with them, and carry them on his back up the pulpit. So, beautiful people were born in this beautiful month.

And then there are the battles; this is the month in which our Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam went to war and fought. The great Battle of Badr. Our Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam did not leave Madīnah in order to fight a great war. He left solely to seize the possessions of Quraysh, because they had seized the possessions of the Muslims in Makkah. They wanted to do so in return. But it was Allah’s decree, Allah’s will, that this war would be a Ghazwa. They asked if they should return. Our Holy Prophet did not want them to return. The Muhajirīn also did not want to return. But the people of Madīnah, the Ansār told our Holy Prophet, “We will come with you.” And so it became a Ghazwah. By Allah’s permission, by Allah’s protection and help, they eliminated the greatest infidels, the greatest enemies one by one.

Now they say, “They have been neutralized” (War terminology). How did they neutralize them? They have become corpses, incapable of causing harm. That’s what happened to them. Those seventy of the greatest kāfirs, the infidels of Quraysh, were erased in that battle. That is, they were purged from the face of the earth. Because their presence would give strength to others. It would strengthen disbelief. It would incite disbelief; it would lead them astray.

Therefore, the power of disbelief was broken. Muslims gradually began to spread more. Because when our Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam was in Makkah, they migrated to Abyssinia and so. But it never fully spread. This means that it would spread and there will be a conquest after a battle. The battle is for the sake of Allah not for anything else. It is to save people. Unlike those who now say they will save people through democracy, that they will enforce democracy. But in doing so, they have made the state of people a thousand times worse.

Wherever our Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam went, his soldiers brought enlightenment, light, īmān, beauty, and humanity. They brought all kinds of goodness. But the helpers of shayṭān portray them oppositely. In fact, it is they themselves who are wrong. They are the oppressors. They are the ones who cause unrest. They are the ones who commit all kinds of filth. Therefore, wherever Islām goes, it arrives with mercy. There is no oppression. Wherever Islām is, wherever true Islām is, there is no oppression. There are Muslim countries, but they are not true Muslims, that’s why it is like that. Whereas true Islām is the way of our Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, and all those who came after him up to the last caliphs. They all acted according to the rulings, commands and principles of Islām, according to what our Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam said.

Therefore, wherever a true Muslim goes, there is barakah, peace and beauty. May Allah be pleased with them. May Allah grant us those beautiful days.

Inshā’Allah, when Mahdi ʿalayhi s-salām comes, these days will come. Otherwise, there is neither peace nor beauty in the world. Day by day, it’s getting worse. Ugliness and filth are sweeping across the land, both apparently and esoterically.

May Allah save us and send us the Sāhib, inshā’Allah.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
