Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Those who are with Allah ﷻ do not lose. Those who are with Allah ﷻ always win. Sometimes it seems like a test, but keep your intentions solid and there is no fear Allah ﷻ willing. A person who believes and trusts in Allah ﷻ is always at peace, always at ease. Because you cannot trust anyone else. One day he is with you, the other day he is against you. Even if the whole world is against you, there will be no sadness or trouble as long as you are with Allah ﷻ.

Be with Allah ﷻ, have pure intentions and fulfill Allah’s ﷻ orders. Do not follow your ego. Do not deviate from the path by following your ego. Once you deviate from the path, then it’s your fault, you bear the responsibility. You no longer have the trust that Allah ﷻ gave you. Allah ﷻ is with pure people. “Why did this happen to us? What is left?” When you say this, then the deficiency is in you. Look for the deficiency. If there is a deficiency, then do not complain. When you complain, you complain against Allah ﷻ. As long as you are on the path of Allah ﷻ, you have no fear. If you have made a mistake knowingly or unknowingly, you will be punished in this world and in the hereafter. A believer repents in this world and saves himself. But those who are not believers, and those who are against Allah ﷻ will suffer both in this world and in the hereafter. After that, they oppose Allah ﷻ without any morals or Adab.

Allah ﷻ tells you to be straight. BismiLlahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim: “ْتَ رْ مِ ُأْامَ كَ ْْمْ قِ َتسْ اَف”, “So remain on a right course as you have been commanded.” (Qur’ān 11:112). Be straight. Allah ﷻ is with you as long as you are straight. If you deviate from the path, Allah ﷻ will not be with you, you will go astray. Allah’s ﷻ path is straight. Aṣ-Ṣirāṭ al-mustaqīm, The straight path. If you go outside of it, it is your fault. Do not look for fault in Allah ﷻ. Repent, seek forgiveness and turn from your mistakes.

May Allah ﷻ protect us from the evil of our ego. Our ego is strong. If we give it what it wants, we will not be saved. Some foolish ones say: “We will feed our ego until it is satisfied”, but it will be worse. The ego is never satisfied. The more you give the ego, the more it wants. You try so much, saying: “Let my ego be full, then I will turn to Allah ﷻ” but it won’t work that way. The ego always wants more and more. The more you give it, the more it wants. Therefore, it is necessary to train it so that you are on the right path and you are with Allah ﷻ. May Allah ﷻ help us all, and not leave us to the hands of our ego. Don’t look down at others, we also have the same ego. It is not necessary to condemn that ego, but what is necessary is to take a lesson from the situation in which they fell in; it is necessary to take lessons. We should not give the ego what it wants even for an hour, a minute or a second; it should be trained. May Allah ﷻ help us.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
