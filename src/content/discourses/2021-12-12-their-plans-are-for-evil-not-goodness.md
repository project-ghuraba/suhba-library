Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

A’ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm:

(Qur’ān 03:54). Allah says, ‘Wa Makarū Wa Makara Allāhu Wa Allāhu Khayru Al-Mākirīn’, ‘And the disbelievers planned, but Allah planned. And Allah is the best of planners.’ Allah ‘Azza wa-Jalla says that they devised a plan; but their plan doesn’t work against those who are with Allah. Eventually, they will fall into their own trap, says Allah ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Allah will destroy their plans.

During the present time and before it, shaytan and those who are with him have always been busy making plans against people and wanted to harm them. Why? It is because they are together with shaytan. When shaytan was thrown away from paradise; Allah ‘Azza wa-Jalla told him, “You will go to hell”. So he said, “I am going to hell because of ‘Adam”. He didn’t say Sayyidina ‘Adam. He said, “I will take the sons of ‘Adam together with me”. So Allah ‘Azza wa-Jalla said, “Those who are cheated by you will go together with you”. Therefore, all the time, because of this reason shaytan wants to cheat people with his tricks and take them to hell.

This is evil. To take to hell is evil. Goodness is to go to paradise. Therefore, all these plans against mankind and enmity to Islam are evil. It is to destroy mankind. It is to do badness to people, nothing else. Every time they make up new inventions. But all of them are used for evil purposes, not for goodness. Whatever goodness, whatever beauty there is, they want to remove it and replace it with evil, oppression, darkness, Kufr and rebellion. Wherever there is corruption, they are there. And people run to help them across the world in order to do evil. In order to do evil and not to do goodness, they create chaos between countries. They create chaos between people. They leave no conscience, no goodness and no beauty. This is what they do.

We see now in the end of times; they’ve made up this illness through their plan. It is not enough, they make up another one. That is not enough too, they make people hurt each other. They make people adopt bad habits. They make people do things that are not in their nature and habits. And they are showing this as normal. However, all of them are meaningless. They will bring no benefit even though they think that they will benefit from this. Can benefit come from evil? No, it cannot. With goodness, Allah ‘Azza wa-Jalla will turn their plans against them eventually. They will remain beneath it and go to hell. May Allah protect us. May Allah protect Muslims. Those who are with them will go with them. Whoever they are – Arab, Turkish, Kurdish, black or white; they will go to hell together with them. And if they are not with them but together with Allah, then Allah ‘Azza wa-Jalla will put them in paradise.

May Allah protect us from their plans and evil. There are many people who are caught in their plans and evil. We see them everywhere we go. Youngsters especially are cheated; they think that they are clever and don’t like Islam. They don’t like anything. They are cheated unknowingly. They are cheated by assuming ugliness to be beauty, and then they are destroyed.

In Islam, a person is kept responsible when he reaches maturity. Before that, he is not kept responsible, but he is kept responsible after that. People of old times used to own a home, own a land and were real men at the age of 15. Now even at the age of 30-40, they say that they are young. And when it comes to doing something [bad], they come forward to do it. May Allah protect us. May Allah help us. May He make us steadfast and give strength to our Iman.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
