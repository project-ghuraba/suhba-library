Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

A’ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm:

(Qur’ān 04:128). ‘Wa ṣ-ṣulhu Kḥayr, Wa Uh’dirati-l Anfusu sh-shuh’, ‘And peace is best. And present in [human] souls is greed.’ Egos are always ready for badness. Allah ‘Azza wa-Jalla says that it is good to make peace. Islam means peace, safety and goodness to people. Kufr is completely the opposite. It is harm and badness to people. Therefore, peace is good, says Allah ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Let there always be peace and beauty in everything between people.

Peace between people is the best. Resentment, discord and turmoil are not good; because they come from the ego. Disagreement occurs from the badness of the ego. In order to train the ego, make peace and get along well.

Islam is the only thing that will provide peace in the world. There is no other thing. The shaytan is always making up other things, and they are not for peace. He is making them up in order to make it worse. He is showing the real which is good as bad, because he doesn’t want it. If it spreads, there will be no place for badness. And when Islam spreads all over the world there will be peace and goodness.

This will happen insha’Allah. It is the order of Allah ‘Azza wa-Jalla. It is His good tidings. Islam will spread all over the world. When Mahdi ‘alayhi s-salam appears... People are arguing with each other now by saying, “There is a border. Our property is stuck in the border. That side is better, this side is worse”. When Mahdi ‘alayhi s-salam appears, there will be neither borders, nor obstacles. The whole world will be in peace under the beautiful ruling of Islam.

This will last for certain time, because it is Dunya. Dunya must be like this. And then the doomsday will come. The world will come to its end then. May Allah make us steadfast and let us reach those beautiful days insha’Allah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
