Kuftaro with GrandShaykh

Mawlana Shaykh Mohamad Nazim al Haqqani an Nakshibendi

26 Jumādā al-Ūlā 1432 A.H. Saturday, April 30, 2011 Translated from Arabic by: http://saltanat.org/

BismillahirRahmanirRahim

[Several guests have brought a number of books to Mawlana Shaykh Nazim Hazretleri.]

"Nahu Sarf" is a book from Imam Bigoui, a book "Nahu Sarf" about Arabic grammar, written by Imam Bigoui. That is... Imam Malik is the one who wrote it. This is a very difficult book. It is Arabic grammar derivatives. [Mawlana recites grammar exercises for some Arabic words.] This is a lesson in Arabic grammar. Finished.

Seventy years ago I read this book. I was twenty years old at that time. We were also taught the Fiqh of Imam Shafi. Maulana said, I studied the Imam Hanafi. But you are Shafi.

Jalala´in. For what reason he is called Jalala´in? Because it was written by two Imams. One started the book and one completed it. Both of them were Jalal, so Jalala´in. Imam Suyuti and this are the easier interpretation. For the student, this is the easiest, and for those who are seeking knowledge, it is the best and easiest to read.

Glory to Allah Almighty. And now, no one remains who can understand. Not the book by Jalala´in, not the interpretation by Khasin. That is a very sweet interpretation.

[Mawlana looks through another book.] Abdur Rahman Ghazi al Ghumuqi. This is a copy.

Guest: This is your book - "Mercy Oceans", translated into Russian. [Mawlana shows the photo of Shaykh Abdullah Daghestani (qas) on the first page. He kisses it and touches to the top of his head.] Also we are translating other books.

I´m going to look at this, open it. [Mawlana looks through a copy of Kuran-i Kerim.] The letter M, Mim. Look. [Mawlana is pointing at the text]. The Wahabis, they are like dogs. They removed all the signs. Look. [Mawlana takes one of his own versions of the Kuran, to compare Suratal Fatiha with the new version.] Here [in the older version] it says, "La", here [new version] nothing. They removed the sign. They are traitors.

La, the letter Alif Lam, it means don´t stop. It is not permitted to stop. Here [new version] they removed it because when they are reading they stop after every verse. This is not according to the original recitation of Suratal Fatiha. This is not permitted, to stop after every verse. They removed the marks that indicate "no stopping". [Recites beginning of Suratal Fatiha.] Yes. This is how you should recite it. But here, “AlhamduillahiRabbil´AlaminarRahmaniRahim”, they stop. This is not permitted. You should read "Bismillahi Rahmani Rahim, Alhamdulillahi Rabbil ´Alamin, Ar Rahmani Rahim, Malikiyaumiddin", stop.

For what reason did they place the signs for stopping? Mim is signing that you should stop. But the Lam-Alif, La means "do not stop". It is not permitted to stop after “Alhamdulillahi rabbil ´Alamin”. You should go until "Bismillahi RahmaniRahim, Alhamdulillahi Rabbal ´Alamin, Ar Rahmani Rahim, Malikiyaumiddin". Then you can stop. You understand?

But these devils, the Wahabis, they canceled -removed- all the signs. They removed the signs permitting one to stop, and they removed the signs that do not allow a stop. This version is printed in Damascus, but it is also done there.

[Mawlana takes another copy of Kuran-i Kerim from the shelf.] Hafiz Osman. [Mawlana examines Suratal Fatiha.] In the Ottoman printing, look, here are the signs saying that stopping is not permitted. Even here [in another Surah]. Recite up to here, then you can stop. Here you cannot stop. It is not permitted. You should continue reading, joining the verses.

This is a trick that they did. Why? For what did they place such... Why did they put it here? And why did they remove it? They removed all the signs for stopping. There are no more signs. They removed them. And everyone is heedless about this point.

If this is not accepted, why are they printing it then? For what reason are they not using the signs for stopping and non-stopping? They removed it because their sect is based on falsehood. They are from the groups that went astray. They may go to hell.

[Mawlana examines another new version.] There are no signs. There is none. They left the numbers for the verse; but they removed the signs. This one is from the mufti of Syria, Shaykh Ahmad Kuftaro. He passed away. He knows me. Since 1944, he knew me - Shaykh Ahmad Kuftaro.

Every time I went to Friday prayer, he would stop and greet me while he was preaching to people. And after giving Nasiha (this is all before the Friday prayer), he used to inform the people that Shaykh Nazim is here, and he would ask me to speak a few words for the attenders. He was insisting that I speak to the attenders. I used to say, "Shaykh, you are our Shaykh. You are enough. You have offered the attenders a stuffed lamb, what can I offer them? I am only going to offer them bread and olives." He said, "It does not matter. Sometimes people may need bread and olives." And then I would speak.

You knew that he was coming to visit Shaykh Abdullah Daghestani (qas)? He used to come and visit, yes -Shaykh Ahmad Kuftaro. He used to come after Fajr prayer to visit Shaykh Abdullah Daghestani. He lived in an area called Ruqnuddin, in Damascus. The Muhyiddin neighborhood was on one side [of Shaykh Abdullah's neighborhood], and the end of the street used to be called Ruqnuddin area. He had a mosque and a school. What was its name? Abu Noor. The school was called Abu Noor. He was visiting Shaykh Abdullah Daghestani after the Fajr prayer. He used to come in his car; and he was driving.

Guest: He was driving the car?

Yes, alhamdulillah I never drove. He used to come by himself after Fajr prayer. Shaykh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani Hazretleri ki ko ni. Abil Qabl. Abil Qabl Abdullah. Abil, the son of Abil. Shaykh Efendi was an orphan. He was an orphan. Shaykh Abdullah Daghestani was an orphan. Shaykh Ahmad Kuftaro used to come alone to Shaykh Abdullah's association. He came alone. He didn´t bring anyone. My Shaykh, Sayiddi ya Maulaya, my Shaykh and your Shaykh -Shaykh Abdullah Daghestani - told me (when he was ninety-three years old) that he used to come and listen to him. Shaykh Kuftaro used to come to listen to Shaykh Abdullah Daghestani, ki ko ni.

And Shaykh Abdullah Daghestani told me, "Nazim Efendi, I saw the Prophet (sas) and he told me, 'Oh Shaykh Abdullah, it is not the time to open for Shaykh Ahmad Kuftaro. Make him search a little bit, because now is not the time for opening for him.'”

That morning Shaykh Ahmad Kuftaro came as he was accustomed to. He sat, and he listened to the words of Shaykh Abdullah Daghestani, according to the principles of Tariqat. At the end, Shaykh Abdullah Daghestani asked him, "Oh Shaykh Ahmad, who is your Shaykh?" "Oh my master, my Shaykh is my father. Shaykh Amin al Kuftaro was his name."

He then asked him, "Who permitted you or authorized you to be a Shaykh? From whom are you receiving, now that you give advice and preach to the people?" He said, "My father. As you know, he passed away, Allah bless his soul.”

“And now, when you are sitting and preaching to the people and giving advice, is there a connection from your heart to his heart? Can you receive from the knowledge of your father? Or are you speaking from yourself?" He said, "Oh my Shaykh, ya Sayiddi, I preach to the people from the books, from many books. I am looking and I am giving to the listeners."

"Oh my son, an authorized one is the one who, when he sits for preaching, he is in connection with his Shaykh. You are disconnected. You are not connected. You need someone. Because you are disconnected from your Shaykh, the line is disconnected. You need someone who can join you to your Shaykh. Don´t claim that you are a Shaykh, no! You still have a way in front of you, before you may become from al Wasilin." This refers to people who are living, but they are connected to Ahlil Barsah - those who passed away to the other life.

Then, he told me, "Once I told him, 'You are in need of a Shaykh to connect you to your father', he didn´t come back again.” However, he was still respecting Shaykh Abdullah, and he was still coming sometimes. And Shaykh Abdullah used to visit him sometimes; and I would accompany him. You understand?

[Mawlana reads from the first page.] This is about Shaykh Kuftaro: “Shaykh Ahmad Kuftaro. Samahatal Muftil´an, the general mufti of Syria, Shaykh Ahmad Kuftaro. La ilaha ilAllah.”

The one who cheats us is not counted to be amongst us. This [removing marks for reciting Kuran-i Kerim] is cheating. They are cheating. They are not counted with us.

[Mawlana makes dua for the guests.] Oh Allah Almighty. Treat our hearts with the knowledge of knowing You. Make their traveling blessed and easy for them, and accept apologies. And please make us to be with Imam Sahibul Zaman, for the service of the Prophet of the end of time, and all thanks are for You, and the highest praising on the Master of beginning and end, Hadratil Rasul, (sas), Sayidinna Muhammad (sas). And gather us under his flag, here and hereafter, with Hazreti Mehdi (as). And make us reach to be in the service of the Shariat. For the sake of the one that You sent. Suratal Fatiha.
