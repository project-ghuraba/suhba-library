As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Today is the last Wednesday of the blessed month of Ṣafar. MashaAllah, this month of Ṣafar has passed. Years pass. It is the decree of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla; nothing remains still. Even mountains move and pass.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla states in Qur’ān ʿAẓīmu sh-Sha’n that nothing remains the same; everything changes. Therefore, this month of Ṣafar is a heavy month. It is Allah’s wisdom; there is wisdom in everything. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla created this month of Ṣafar as a heavy one. But shukr to Allah, it has passed, and may Allah grant us to spend it with His blessings, inshā’Allāh.

Today is the last Wednesday, so it is the heaviest day. With Allah’s permission, we will pass it by giving ṣadaqah, reciting tasbih, and repenting and seeking forgiveness. With Allah’s permission, this too will pass safely.

Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam said to his companions, “Whoever brings me the glad tidings of the end of the month of Ṣafar, shall also receive glad tidings.”

Shukr to Allah - today is not the last day; tomorrow is the last day, but today is the heaviest day. Therefore, we must certainly fulfill our duties, and most importantly, give ṣadaqah. Ṣadaqah wards off all calamities and afflictions, prolongs life, and is beneficial in everything. Give it today so that it passes easily. By the grace of Allah, it will pass easily.

As we said, they say, “This too shall pass.” Nothing stays put. You can’t hold on to it. You can’t hold on to days, months, or years. It is Allah’s command; the good and bad, everything will pass. But may Allah grant us to spend these days, years, months, and weeks in ways that please Him. May we live them all as He loves and wants, inshā’Allāh. May we not deviate from His pleasure. May Allah protect us.

Inshā’Allāh, the next month will be Rabīʿ ul-Awwal, the month of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. inshā’Allāh, it will be the blessed days of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam’s birth, the Mawlid. May their grace and barakah alleviate the burden of this month of Ṣafar.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla has created everything, and He has created it beautifully. He has also created good; everything is good, but only if mankind choose good does it become good. If they do not choose it... may Allah protect us and may we reach Rabīʿ ul-Awwal safely inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
