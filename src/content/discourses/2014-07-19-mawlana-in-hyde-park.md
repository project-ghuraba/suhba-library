This is Allah Almighty. This is the beauty, that Allah Almighty so beautiful so lovely, so full of love that Allah Almighty you cannot imagine the real love that Allah Almighty put in sainthood. You cannot imagine how that concentrated and put in such a small thing, His big love, this is Allah Almighty. Ajeeb, this is how Allah Almighty is. This is very important gift of Lailatu l-Qadr.
