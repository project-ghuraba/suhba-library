Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

We are continuously grateful to Allah that He created us Muslim. For a person being created a Muslim is the greatest honor and the greatest virtue. A believer (mumin) person is better than the whole world in the sight of Allah.

People need to know the value as people who know the value are acceptable. Ungrateful people are not acceptable. This is the last nation prior to the Day of Judgment, it is the Nation (Ummah) of our Holy Prophet (SAW). Allah Exalted and Glorious be He informed our Holy Prophet (SAW): “This is the best.” The best nation is the nation that follows our Holy Prophet (SAW).

There were many prophets who desired, “I wish I were not a prophet but just a member of the Hazrat Prophet’s (SAW) Ummah.” It is such a virtuous nation but people do not know. Those who follow our Holy Prophet (SAW) are his Nation, and those who do not follow him are not considered from his Nation.

As we said, people do not know the value of the honor and bounty given to them. That is why they go to other way and do not accept this honor. They are at a loss if they do not accept it. What is important is for us to know its value. We need to be happy for this and not for something else. Allah Azza wa Jalla says, “Be happy.”

“Fabithalika falyafrahoo.” “In that let them rejoice.” (Sura Yunus:58) “Let them be happy with this and be at rest, since this path is the path of Allah and the Prophet. Let those who follow it rejoice.” Otherwise, there is nothing else to rejoice about. They are all empty things. Things assigned for this world, such as rank and position, none have any value whatsoever. It is useless as long as one does not recognize Allah and the Prophet. We need to be careful of this.

What Allah has treated us with - Islam, the Koran, and our Holy Prophet (SAW) – are relief, joy, healing, and light for us. May Allah make us amongst those who know its value. May we follow them and may affection for them always be in our hearts Inshallah.

Wa Minallah at-tawfeeq. Al-Fatiha.
