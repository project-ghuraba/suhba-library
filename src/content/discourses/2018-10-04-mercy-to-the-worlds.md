Waṣ-ṣalātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīn wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā

Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad.

Allah Almighty said to our Prophet (SAW). Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm.

“Wa mā arsalnāka illa raḥmatan li-lālamīn,” (Qur’ān 21:107) Allah Almighty said for our Prophet (SAW), “And We did not send you if not as a Mercy to the worlds.” Through his mercy and saving ability, we are going to the saved here and in the Next Life.

If not for our Prophet’s (SAW) intercession, our work would not only be difficult, it would be very difficult! Even if a man is a great scholar, he also obeys his nafs. People make mistakes every hour, and commit sin. Only the intercession of our Noble Prophet (SAW) can save them. Therefore, as Allah ‘azza wa jalla says, “raḥmatan li-lālamīn”, mercy for all worlds, his mercy is enough for everyone. After one wants it, our Prophet (SAW) saves him.

But a part of humanity has ruined the whole world of Islam. They sucked hypocrisy into them. They also weakened others’ faith. They did this under the name of Islam. In fact, they speak Arabic well. Even better than us. They know it all well, however, they accepted their desires as the higher thing. They submitted themselves to their desires. They showed arrogance. Though it has been 1400 years, their arrogance has not gone from these men. There is a hadith from the Prophet (SAW), “One who has arrogance in the heart cannot enter paradise.” That one will go hell first, so that the arrogance will burn out and [then] he will be let into Paradise. Or he shall ask our Prophet (SAW) for intercession. Because of their arrogance, they do not ask for this, [and] they influence ignorant people and cause them to become the people of hell.

Without the intercession of our Prophet (SAW), surely they will go to hell to be cleansed. Why go to hell when there is a short, easy way? Let us ask for our Prophet’s intercession. Let us submit to the words of Allah. Our Prophet (SAW) is sent to the world as mercy. May Allah have us all receive his intercession, insha’Allah. Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah
