As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla tells us to be sincere. "Mukhliṣīna lahu d-dīn" (98:5). HeNone is praising sincere people, sincere Muslims. Who worships AllāhNone sincerely, loves for the sake of AllāhNone, does everything for the pleasure of AllāhNone, doesn't show off and asks only for the pleasure of AllāhNone is the sincere one. People attain it by worshipping. And AllāhNone gives it to their hearts. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says, "It is one of MyNone secrets. INone give it to the heart of whomever INone want." Whoever worships AllāhNone sincerely, this secret enters his heart. It remains with him.

We should do everything as AllāhNone orders, as our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam told us. We should strive to do so, and may AllāhNone forgive what we cannot do. There is no need to add other things to this in order to show off. You should follow the religion purely. Whatever you were ordered, follow that. The religion has a method, its own way. You cannot do as you like. As ṭarīqah, we should follow first of all our Holy ProphetNone, hisNone ṣaḥābah, then the scholars, and the imāms of the madhāhib. Whatever the imām of the madhhab says, you must follow it. There are orders like – this is for you, this one is for ladies and that one is for men. You must follow them. When you go out of them, you become a bad example. It becomes bid‘ah. That is bid‘ah. It is fine if it is good. But when you do something opposite the religion or madhhab, and madhhab explains the religion, you make bid‘ah. That bid‘ah will take you out of the way. You will think you are doing something good but you will be out of the way.

Of course, here in ṭarīqah, there is tolerance for everyone to come. But people understand tolerance in a bad way at the end. They think it is from the religion. There is nothing like this. Especially, ladies should not be loud. When ladies are loud, it is wrong. Ladies can be loud among themselves. Their voices are a private thing. Their saying aloud, especially while making takbīr in a prayer, while saying āmīn... Normally, you must be quiet when saying āmīn in Ḥanafī madhhab. It is ok for Shāfi‘ī. But when they are together, men are mixed, then the ladies must not make that takbīr. Shāfi‘ī or Ḥanafī, they should not say it loud. We have been showing tolerance. But we see that it is getting worse. Our ṭarīqah pays attention to madhāhib and is obedient to them. You can't do as you like. If you do so, your sincerity will be gone. You will be showing off and pretending. You will be doing things to suit yourself. And we don't give any permission for that. Whoever does it does on his own, but he is an example to others as well. Therefore, we are warning about it. They should observe that. You have come until here. You have come in order to earn this virtue and thawāb. Don't commit a sin while trying to earn thawāb. Therefore, we have to be careful. Other things are not so important. But there are some things, things that our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam forbade, things that Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla doesn't want. Don't mix them and say it is the way of ṭarīqah. Ḥāshā. Ṭarīqah doesn't go even a tiny bit outside of sharī‘ah and madhhab.

When you do by yourself, it is a different issue. But you can't mix others in it. Don't be a bad example. Therefore, if there were no examples, we wouldn't pay much attention. But everyone who comes sees it and says, "We have to do like this." You go to other places and hear ladies’ voices while praying. "How are you doing it?" [they ask]. "It is like this in our ṭarīqah. Shaykh Efendi gives permission. We do it this way." What happens when they say like this? Our actions go outside of sharī‘ah then. We go outside of Ahlu s-Sunnah. Ḥāshā. Therefore, we should be careful. This is sincerity. You must pay attention to your worship.

May AllāhNone help us, in shā’a Llāh.

Sincerity, very important thing. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying in āyah, "Mukhliṣīna lahu d-dīn" (98:5). You must be sincere in your religion, following Islām, following Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. You must be sincere, you must be careful not to be... There is sincerity and there is show. Many people they make sincere. They claim as they're sincere people and they make show. But this is not sincerity and Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla in ḥadīth qudsī saying, "Sincerity one of MyNone secrets. INone put in heart of whom INone love, INone love him."

This is very important and to get sincerity we try our best especially you following ṭarīqah. Ṭarīqah meaning to showing people the best thing for to reach to AllāhNone, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla and Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. This is important thing. How can we can be sincere? To practice with what AllāhNone order, what Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam show us how to be do everything. They show us and only follow what Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam doing. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh we are, our ṭarīqah, our Mashāyikh they are following this step-by-step and doing everything Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla and Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam... what Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla order and Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam doing with sunnah, with this. Everything, we are following, not going out of one millimetre out this.

But sometimes, [we] meet people they like to make show and they mix between their ego – what like – and showing people as "this is from religion". Our religion and our ṭarīqah, Ahlu s-Sunnah wa-l-Jamā‘ah, which it's Prophet's ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam way. Who going out from this way, he from bid‘ah, way of not good, out of ṭarīqah meaning, out of way. And this, must be careful for our people and we are like to say for our people – you, what you do you must be careful. You must be very sensitive for this thing – what man can do, what woman can do. We are living in time they said, "No difference between this and difference with that." No. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone create them. They have special thing. We have special thing, men. So, you cannot say, "We will do same with what men doing. Women we can do also this." This is meaning you are out of way, out of ṭarīqah, out of sharī‘ah, out of madhhab. You're not more anymore from Ahlu s-Sunnah wa-l-Jamā‘ah. So for this, you must be very careful.

Maybe we are tolerate something. Okay, we cannot do hundred percent, but there is something which it is showing as it is from ‘ibādah, from praying, from this. What is this? We are meaning to voice of woman. When men and women, you must, women not make their voice so high. Especially in takbīr when saying, "āmīn", even in Ḥanafī madhhab, men also only with voice of "āmīn" [quiet] you can say, not "āmīn" [loud]. "Āmīn" [loud] for this, Shāfi‘ī or other madhhab they can do. But in... for women even no one any madhhab you can say this with high voice. Because this we see before we was tolerate, tolerate, it become real too much. Because, one coming, others saying, "Oh this is coming from ṭarīqah adab. We must do this – āmīn [loud]." When it's their voice come more than men voice. This is you cannot say, accept this.

We, okay, we tolerate a little bit but after, coming more and more and more. But this is what happen with this. Many, many group coming after Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, slowly, slowly, slowly going out. This we cannot tolerate this. So, we must be careful for this especially when you are with other, you must... If they cannot do it you can – okay – you don't do but this is right, this is not right. We cannot make hundred percent our this only but not put new thing in praying, in ṭarīqah, in madhhab. You must be careful for this.

Don't make this because you come for to get reward, don't make you go with sin. This very important. We cannot accept this. Because after, many people sometimes they going to other place, they doing something not acceptable, "What, why you do this?" "Oh, we are coming from dergah, from ṭarīqah of Shaykh Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. He do this and he accept this." And this people they will say, "What is this? They are from bid‘ah people and they make separate between Muslim even. They make enemy to be each other." So, you must be careful you come here, in shā’a Llāh. This naṣīḥah for all of you also. This enough for you for deserve what you come here. In shā’a Llāh, you come with good and go with, in shā’a Llāh, present full of light and full of barakah, in shā’a Llāh. For all of you.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
