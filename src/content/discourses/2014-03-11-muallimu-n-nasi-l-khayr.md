As-salamu ‘alaikum, O attendees. A new day with a new manifestation is coming. Welcome, O beloved brethren of Shah Mardan, how beautiful. Welcome, O beloved brethren, lovers of Shah Mardan, too, welcome to them. May expansion come, may happiness come, may strength come, may honor come.

My venerable Guardian, the Most High, did not leave man in want of anything, Shaykh Mehmet Efendi. He is the Creator! {Hal min khaliqin ghayru Allah?} {Is there a Creator except Allah?} (35:3) Man can't create anything, man can't originate anything. Al-Khaliq, the Creator, is Allah Almighty. I ask your support, O men of Allah, support, O Shah Mardan! Listen, listen and understand: Who created the world? Did you create it? All those blessings on earth, did Allah Almighty create them, or did you? "We will invent a medicine", they say. What do you know about medicines? Your medicines are all fake, they're false. Astaghfiru-Llah, forgiveness, O my Lord, forgiveness!

Welcome to you, may joy be yours, O beloved brethren, Shah Mardan's lovers, welcome to all of you! May happiness envelop you! May you find joy, may your gathering be a gathering full of joy! Welcome, here we go, a new day with new provision. Allahu akbar. You are exalted, O Lord, You are the Sultan, O Lord. He didn't – Shaykh Mehmet Efendi – leave anything for humans to make up or invent, Allah Almighty. In whatever He created is a remedy, is of benefit. He didn't leave man all to himself, Allah Almighty. But man, {innahu kana zalūman jahūla} (33:72), man is an oppressor as well as being ignorant.

Who created this world? Where did this world come from? There are so many kinds of creatures in this world, where did they all come from? Particularly man, where did he come from? Did he come down from heavens to earth in a basket? Allah. "Who?" is the question you must ask! Welcome, O you beautiful, intelligent and well-mannered servants, who are the brethren of Shah Mardan, welcome to you, O you honorable servants. O beloved brethren of Shah Mardan. Welcome, our assembly is an enlightened assembly, he says, a beautiful assembly in which they speak about the secrets of the honor of man. There is a secret of the humanness of man. Who speaks about these secrets? It is Shah Mardan, he speaks about them. They comprehend, those possessing understanding comprehend, too.

This enormous world, this huge world, with its oceans and seas, its mountains and rocks, with all the different kinds of creatures living on it, who put it all in its place? Who made all this? Ask this question! Here you go, O our brethren. O our brethren, Allah sent us the Sultan of the Prophets, He sent us His Beloved, and His Beloved informs us according to the capacity of our understanding and there are oceans and seas of what we can't understand. Allah Allah. One horse bean, one chickpea, can it come into existence by itself? Does it have a choice, whether it wants to be a chickpea or a horse bean or a haricot bean? Where? There is a secret, nothing is without a secret. There is a secret. And someone carries a secret, but the secret which he carries his son doesn't carry. Allahu akbar Allahu akbar wa li-Llahi l-hamd.

Welcome, O beloved brethren of Shah Mardan. Allah, the Possessor of absolute Majesty has made man's place on this earth. He gave him his place and prepared for him whatever he may need in this place for the sons of man. Purslane or mallow, for example. Purslane is a herb that grows wild by itself. It grows by itself but who is the one making it grow? Purslane has certain properties, which other plants don't possess. They belong exclusively to this herb. Who is this? Who is the one giving purslane its unique properties? Who is the one giving spinach its unique properties which make it spinach?

O beloved brethren of Shah Mardan, here you go. Everything has its secret. There is its outward appearance, and there is its secret. There's a secret. If there was no secret, that unique honor wouldn't exist, it would not be there. Welcome, O beloved brethren of Shah Mardan. How many things there are that people need to learn Shaykh Mehmet Efendi, so many things. Allahu akbar, Allahu akbar, Allahu akbar wa li-Llahi l-hamd. Our Lord, exalted is He in His Majesty, Allah Allah. If Allah Almighty wished He could make all species of fish live inside one lake. There would be no need for huge oceans. But He Most High made the oceans for each one of these creatures, carrying its own unique secret, its own characteristics, its own benefit, to take its place therein. There is no "We know it all, we researched it, we found out!" What is your "research"? What are you searching for?

Welcome, O beloved brethren of Shah Mardan, how beautiful are Shah Mardan narrations, his teachings, his explanations. Who is man? That is the question. What is the wisdom behind his existence? That is the question? Who is woman? Who is man? That is the question. There must be a Creator making humans women, making humans men, One possessing that Power to create. If He didn't exist, there would be no will, and will is an essential thing, if there was no Possessor of Will, the human being would not exist. If there wasn't the will of that Possessor of Will, not even a tree would exist. If there wasn't the will of that Possessor of Will, no animal would exist. Would this world be empty? It wouldn't. With what would it be filled? If it wasn't empty, with what would this world be filled?

The One making this world spin in space like a ball, He is that Possessor of Power. There is beauty and there is ugliness. The beautiful ones take the beauty, the ugly ones take on ugliness. The one who is just, represents justice in this world, he takes justice. The oppressor represents the oppression of this world, he takes it and he goes to hell.

Welcome, O beloved brethren of Shah Mardan. These are the sayings of Shah Mardan, and Shah Mardan was taught by the Messenger of the Almighty. You must look at these teachings! Who is the One giving a cat its unique characteristics, so that it meows, crying "meow, meow"? Who taught it to do this? Where did it get this from? A dog barks "bow-wow", it does not meow. And a wolf growls "ghur ghur". A bear behaves bearish, a wolf behaves wolfish, a lion behaves the way lions do, so humans will behave the way humans do. Because the unique characteristics which man was given were not granted to any other creature by Allah Most High. "I dressed them with My attributes", He says, "so they might know Me." Saying: "Not knowing Me, if they don't possess something from My attributes, they can't get to know Me." The Lord of Might and Glory. Yes. O Lord of Might and Glory.

Welcome, O beloved brethren of Shah Mardan. He is the Creator, the Maker, He knew and He created. But when He created us, He didn't order us to devour each other, crush one another, kill each other, that was not His order. Every created being, whatever it might be, embodies some kind of wisdom from its Creator. An ant has a special function, a mission, Shaykh Mehmet Efendi. Even an ant has a mission. If it had no mission it would not have been created. O you, know your mission! The moment you understand your mission, you will find the way to your Creator. If not, you can't find it, you become a thing like mountain rocks. They are of no use if one doesn't understand they're mountain rocks. Their benefit lies in knowing who created these mountain rocks.

What is the wisdom underlying your creation. To teach us what we should do is the wisdom behind sending us a teacher [mu‘allim]. Because if there is no teacher, the students don't learn anything. That's why Allah, Most High, Majestic and Exalted, brought the creatures into being and gave teachings to His Beloved. {‘Allama l-insana ma lam ya‘lam.} (96:5) The true and perfect human being is the master of all creation (Muhammad sallaLlahu ‘alayhi wa sallam). He came to teach human beings what they did not know. He taught, and who learned profited. Who did not learn became rubbish and went. O our Lord, this huge divine sign of a world. This huge world has become full of creatures. But Allah Most High did not order us to kill one another. {Wa la taqtulu anfusakum} (4:29) {Don't kill each other}, He says. This is an order, it's an order, a divine order. This is an order coming from heavens: {Wa la taqtulu anfusakum!} Don't kill each other! The heavenly command reads: "Don't kill!"

Everything should be for the benefit of man, there should be honor, strength and wisdom, there should be knowledge and a befitting decorum. A tiny flower blossoms after coming out of the earth. Having grown it stands in its full size. Facing whom? Facing the One who created it. That is also what man has to learn first of all to stand in front of the One who created him standing upright in His presence in veneration, presenting himself upright in appreciation of His absolute Greatness. O beloved brethren of Shah Mardan, these are the things the children of man have to learn. {‘Allama l-insana ma lam ya‘lam} (96:5), teaching man what he didn't know.

Another personality shows in the arena, a strange wild, brutish personality appears, coming from my gracious Lord. Allahu akbaru l-akbar. Allahu akbaru l-akbar. refusing to learn. To learn from him He sent the prophet as a teacher, {Ar-Rahman, ‘allama l-Qur'an, khalaqa l-insan, ‘allamahu l-bayan} (55:1-4) How beautiful, what immense greatness! Ar-Rahman – Exalted is He, my gracious Guardian! O our Lord! He sent us His Beloved to teach us, a teacher of goodness to people. That's the attribute of our master. Teach people goodness, don't teach them evil. Man is capable of doing good and doing evil, but you must teach goodness, don't teach evil. Because what is Shaytan's purpose? He came to teach people evil, to teach them badness. "I came out of paradise", he said, "but now, what will I teach them? I'll teach them satanic teachings." What became of them? People turned into devils, All over the world today people are carrying Shaytan's satanic attributes. They do whatever Shaytan tells them. They don't listen to the Beloved sent by the All-Merciful, the Lord of all the Worlds. They don't listen and they don't do what he tells them to do. Allah Allah. Who is profiting? Shaytan has become the profiteer. Who is in loss? Man is the loser. The Lord of Might and Glory, Exalted in His Majesty, whose favours encompass all, did not send man into this world to be in loss, He sent him here to gain benefit. "Find out what benefit is to be gained in this world", He said when sending him there.

O Lord of Might and Glory, You are exalted, You are the Sultan. {Wa ja’a min aqsa l-madinati rajulun yas'a} (36:20) Ha? "Someone came running", He says, "from the far end of a town", {min aqsa l-madinati} From a far end of this world He send someone to make them think, so they may learn, know proper manners, to be informed why they are in existence. {Wa ja’a min aqsa l-madinati rajulun yas'a qala ya qawmi t-tabi‘u l-mursalin} (36:20) Those who follow the messengers are men of intelligence. If not, we can't be considered intelligent. As what then? {Wa idha l-wuhushu hushirat} (when the wild beasts are herded together) (81:5) Isn't it like this, Hajji Mehmet? "Wuhush" (wild beasts). We can't be counted among the intelligent. Why? Because we are what is called wild beasts, a herd of wild beasts, being with them, He says, if we don't want an intelligent man. Well, if you don't want an intelligent man, what do you want? You want a punishment to come down on your head. May Allah forbid it.

All in all everything has been made clear to man. Who is his enemy and who is his friend has been clarified, too. What they are struggling for has also been explained. You will be a separate being all on your own, you are a creature made to become sultan. Not to be, sorry to use this expression, one of Shaytan's dogs. Shaytan's dogs are like all other dogs running after a bone. And Shaytan makes people run after the bone of this dirty world, too. That is yours, this is mine. But in reality no one can pack the continents on his shoulders and carry them away. All there are, are names. There's something called Crimea, there's something called China, called Russia, called Turkey. Can you put it in your pocket? No one can. What are you struggling for? What's the benefit?

But contemplating one hour is better than the worship of seventy years. When he contemplates, man benefits, if he doesn't think, he doesn't benefit but he becomes miserable and wicked. Allah Most High has granted us intelligence, it is an adornment. So use your intelligence, learn and comprehend! By learning you earn honor, in the presence of Shah Mardan. Later they take you to the high assembly. O our Lord, forgive us! Send us in your gracious kindness a teacher who teaches us! "I've sent him", He says, "I've sent him. Just listen!" The attribute of our master is "mu’allimu n-nasi l-khayr" (teacher of goodness to people). Our master came to teach us goodness, he didn't come to teach evil. Learn from this world what is good. Don't learn what's evil. That is what Allah Most High proclaimed to people as His command. This is the order to the teacher of goodness to people: Teach people goodness. O my Lord, send us powerful servants of Yours, so that they may teach us. Here you are, Shah Mardan, and all success is from Allah. Fatiha.

Every plant contains a unique healing power, Shaykh Mehmet Efendi, but we leave them and say, "let's produce our remedies ourselves; let us invent a medicine." Well, what can your medicine avail? Who are you to invent a cure? The cure is coming down from heaven, it's not coming from earth. Allahu akbar wa li-Llahi l-hamd. Welcome, You've brought honor to our assembly, O beloved brethren of Shah Mardan. May Allah's mercy rain down onto your being. May your face shine with beauty. May your heart be the heart of a lion. May you be worthy of the magnificent presence of your Lord. Don't run after this world! To behold the beauty of your Lord let us look at His Beloved. From him you will learn! Fatiha.

These are but a few sayings from the assembly of Shaykh Mehmet Efendi, our heir apparent to Shaykh-hood. We can't delve deeper into it – he understands. Welcome, Shaykh Mehmet Efendi, my son, come here! Come here, I long to see you. May Allah be pleased with you. Purslane, peppers, onions, tomatoes, what a nice salad they make, Mehmet, don't they? He likes it. He likes this kind of thing. Pray for me that I may last, too. May Allah grant you long life as well, so that you see al-Mahdi. You are from Sham, you will conquer Sham! Sh. Mehmet: Allah willing together with you! Mawlana: By Allah's permission, may your wish come true, Mehmet!
