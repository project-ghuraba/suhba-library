As-salāmu ‘alaykum wa raḥmatu Llāhi wa barakātuh. A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

Shāh Naqshband said this. Ḥaḍrat Shāh Naqshband is the head of the Naqshbandi ṭarīqah. After his name, Naqshbandi way was written down. Coming from our ProphetNone it is called the Naqshbandi way, says Ḥaḍrat Shāh Naqshband. He said "I prayed to AllāhNone that it lasts until Qiyāmah and AllāhNone accepted my prayer". Awliyā’s sometimes when they pray, they know if their prayers are accepted or not. By this prayer, Shāh Naqshband didn't ask for wealth. Because this way is a beautiful way, he asked that it lasts until Qiyāmah as it's the way shown by our ProphetNone. He prayed that it isn't forgotten.

What is the Naqshbandi way? It is the way of knowledge. There is no ignorance. It is to follow the way shown by AllāhNone. It is not the way of obeying your ego. It is the way of educating your ego. It is the way of worship and obedience. The Naqshbandi way is the way of submission. Nor do they run after dunyā, but dunyā runs after them. It runs if it runs. If not, they don't go after it. Who doubts, doubts from his weakness. If a man uses this way for dunyā, it is from his own weakness. No Mashāyikh, not one Shaykh, struggled for this world. AllāhNone gave them. It happens as AllāhNone wants. That is for use in dunyā because they spend it on the way of AllāhNone. What a great favour it is. That is a beautiful favour.

Like those people who run after dunyā, you can say how bad dunyā is. It is not dunyā, but those who follow it or see it as a means or a purpose, it is their fault. The Ḥadīth of our ProphetNone says there is no blame in having dunyā. The blame is for those who give it importance, who think only about it and don't stop and then waste it. It is like the one who has his hands full of jewels but dies of hunger without spending them. You have everything. You have all the possibilities in your hands and you are not using them. You are carrying them as a burden. Mashāyikh are not like that. People on the way of the true Mashāyikh are the same. They value it - They spend it for the benefit of people and Muslims.

As we said, the Naqshbandi way is the beautiful way. AllāhNone rewards those who follow it several times more as it's the way of our ProphetNone. In this end of times there are thousands of ways. Each considers itself as something and misleads people from the way. Therefore, keep tight to this way. No need to deal with other things. It is a big favour. For how many hundreds of years, since our ProphetNone, since Shāh Naqshband till now, it is more than 700 years. This way will go till Qiyāmah with the permission of AllāhNone. It is a holy and good way, shukr to AllāhNone.

Ḥaḍrat Shāh-i Naqshbandī he was saying I pray for AllāhNone to make Naqshbandī way, Sufi Naqshbandi, way until Qiyāmah. And HeNone accepted my praying, my du‘ā’, and al-ḥamdu liLlāh we are happy. AllāhNone grant us to be in this nice way, high station way. Naqshbandī way it is the most perfect way after ProphetNone, following ProphetNone and his order, respecting whole companions, respecting awliyā’u Llāh, respecting mu’mins. And it is merciful for everybody and it is light. In the darkest time in history of Muslim or from old or new history Naqshbandī way it was like light - cannot anybody turn it off. Quickly when opening again coming. People going around this ṭarīqat and they have blessed and they have real Islām. Very pure Islām because this coming from ProphetNone and the praying of awliyā’u Llāh, mashāyikh, make this very pure.

Because many thousands of people they claiming they are something. Maybe after while they go very wrong way, even they are going out of Islām. But this ṭarīqat, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, very pure as it is time of ṣaḥābah, time of ProphetNone. Respecting, accepting, and helping people. Helping with knowledge, with charity with every kind of help you can find, spiritual and physical, in shā’a Llāh. But it is sometimes people thinking why this, why benefit of this? It is Allāh'sNone wish, Allāh'sNone will for to be something to hold for ummah. And this is main thing to hold and to be for everybody safe to come. Even not following but it is there until Qiyāmah, meaning without this Qiyāmah it will be[when this is gone it will be Qiyama].

So al-ḥamdu liLlāh we are happy and thankful for AllāhNone to HeNone make us in this way. Allāh bless you and bless us, in shā’a Llāh, and give knowledge for all of us. Knowledge very important in our ṭarīqat also. And knowledge and adab, good behaviour and to know limit. If you know something - if some people ask you, you can tell them. If you don't know don't be ashamed, say '' I don't know, sorry.'' This is limit for what we say. Don't say maybe this be I will be ashamed some, I must say something. Don't say this. If you know say, don't know. Don't say. And be respectful for other also, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq al-Fātiḥah.
