As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīn wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa-l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

Our ProphetNone is saying, "Isma‘ū wa‘ū, wa-idha wa-‘aytum fāntafi‘ū." Listen and understand. Then use what you understand. These ṣuḥbahs are assemblies of knowledge. To gather and ask for knowledge is farḍ for Muslims. It is farḍ for every Muslim to get knowledge. Therefore, these assemblies are good for doing that farḍ. We listen to advice. We should try to understand what they say. When a person understands, he uses it. There is spiritual benefit too. Because you learn the word of AllāhNone and what our ProphetNone taught in these assemblies. You benefit from it physically as well in terms of health and everything. In these gatherings, Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla makes angels spread their wings on the ground. And seekers of knowledge walk on them. Therefore, our ṭarīqah is the way to get pure knowledge of AllāhNone and our ProphetNone through these gatherings and ṣuḥbahs. And it is a great benefit and a big favour.

People don't realize and don't follow any of it. They go to other places and learn useless things. They spend lots of money. They send their children here and there for studying. That is not knowledge. That's ignorance. Because he who doesn't know AllāhNone is ignorant. That knowledge is not for knowing AllāhNone but to make away with HimNone. May AllāhNone awaken them so that they give one percent of what they give for the other knowledge and get big benefit. They don't give even one percent. Therefore, these people should wake up. Unless they do, as we said, there is no benefit from what they learn. Neither physical, nor spiritual benefit. May AllāhNone always give us from the true knowledge, in shā’a Llāh. May we be in these beautiful gatherings until our last breath, in shā’a Llāh.
