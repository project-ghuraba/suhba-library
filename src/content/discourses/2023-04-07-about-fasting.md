Dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘AbduLlāh al-Fā’iz Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

We can never say enough about the virtues of this holy month. Fasting has been given to every nation. It was required of every Muslim since Ādam ‘alayhi s-salam, of every prophet on the way of AllāhNone. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says in āyatu l-karīmah that it is required of us to fast as well. In fact, even some people without religion, or in perverse religions, fast in order to train their egos. But what they are doing is in vain, a useless thing. Unless it leads to Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, whatever you do is useless. It has no benefit. You must be on the way of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla in order to receive benefits both apparent and hidden. Material and spiritual benefits are for people who are with AllāhNone.

How lucky are those who are given this. There are many people who describe themselves as Muslims. Most of them neither fast, nor pray, nor know anything about the religion. Although they say they are Muslim, this good fortune didn't happen to them. May AllāhNone give guidance to them. May HeNone let them taste this beautiful taste. This taste cannot be found elsewhere. This beautiful taste is not found in disobedience and sins. It comes only with faith. People taste it in everything they do then. Otherwise, what they do is useless. There is nothing but harm, because you are feeding your body. Feeding the body is for animals. It is peculiar to them. We feed their bodies to make them fat and to be able to benefit from them. But the feeding of your body is harmful to you. The larger your body gets, the more harmful it is. They know this, and everyone knows this. The larger you are, the heavier your weight is, the more harmful it is. Therefore, they always warn you not to gain weight. It is obvious anyway.

Therefore, you must feed your soul, not just your body. And the soul is fed by worshiping, by praying, fasting, and giving charity, by obedience to Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, by loving our Holy ProphetNone and people whom heNone has showed us to love, by loving the believers. All these things are for your benefit, to feed your soul. They are food for the soul. If you do them, even if your body is small then, it doesn't matter. But otherwise, if you don't care about these things, ḥāshā mina l-ḥuḍūr (heaven forbid), you become similar to an animal one doesn't even want to name. They are the fattest ones. May AllāhNone protect us.

In Ramaḍān, fasting has its adab and rules. And people must break their fast according to its rules. Our Holy ProphetNone says, "Make ifṭār with dates." Dates have all the nutrients in them, enough for the body. If a person eats five to six dates a day, or ten dates, he may skip eating other things. The body will have taken its nutrition. But of course, there is no need to do that. Break your fast with dates and then eat other things. If not, break it with water, says our Holy ProphetNone. Sometimes you cannot find dates. Break your fast with water. Of course, after that come prayers, tarāwīḥ and tahajjud.

Also, our Holy ProphetNone told us to wake up for saḥūr. Certainly, there is benefit in the appointed time of saḥūr. Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says saḥūr is barakah. Making saḥūr brings barakah to people. Get up in order to attain that barakah. "Wa-law bi-sharbatin min mā’." Even if you drink a sip of water with the intention of making saḥūr, it is valid. Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam tells us to do that. Don't go without saḥūr. Also, what is eaten in saḥūr is not questioned, we read in hadīth sharīf just now. AllāhNone won't take its account. Neither the food of saḥūr, nor of ifṭār, nor that eaten in brotherhood will be questioned. Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says saḥūr is what distinguishes us from other nations. Other nations used to eat just once, only at ifṭār. They ate in the evening. And then they didn't eat for twenty-four hours. But for it to be barakah for us, for our health, and to reward us, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gave saḥūr to us, to Ummati Muḥammad. May AllāhNone bless it.

And may AllāhNone give guidance to those who don't fast so that they do it. Some people boast and act arrogant about breaking their fast in front of people. May AllāhNone give guidance to them as well. We have nothing to say to them. They are people to feel sorry for. They must not boast. And we should not be angry at them. We should feel sorry for them that Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla didn't give it to them. Don't boast, "You and I are fasting, but he is not." May AllāhNone protect us. AllāhNone didn't give it to him. But HeNone gave it to you. And this is important. May AllāhNone make this beautiful state remain with us. May HeNone make us steadfast, in shā’a Llāh.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are again, we speak about Ramaḍān, fasting. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, in holy Qur’ān saying, "WeNone make fasting, Ramaḍān, for you obligatory as WeNone make for other nations before you." So, fasting, it was from Ādam ‘alayhi s-salām until now, whole nations, prophets, they was ordering for their nation to do it. Of course there was different shape for this. But normally, when you are fasting, you cannot eat, you cannot drink – and no smoke also. Some people who they want to make fitnah in Islām, "Smoking it is not, you can, [does] not spoil fasting." No, when you, by you know, but, if you are going around and there is smoke, it is no problem. But, if you take, and smoke, it is – you finish your fasting. So many kind, but the main thing, it is this. But now, even these people, they make fast as they like. They only: you cannot eat meat, you cannot eat this. But, normally, fasting, it must be without eating, without drinking anything.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are, from Allāh'sNone mercy for us, HeNone gave us this month, because before also there was more than one month fasting. And also, fasting from twenty-four hours. Only once, you break your fast. They eat after maghrib, and when finish [what] they eat, cannot eat anything more until next day. For this, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying for saḥūr, it is very important for Muslim. Because saḥūr [is] barakah, heNone said, and it is giving you power, to be more easy for you. And this is what AllāhNone give you. You must do it. Because to sleep and wake up it is difficult for people, many people they said we eat before sleeping, we can make saḥūr. No, this is not saḥūr. This is late dinner. So, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying for saḥūr even one drop of water, if you wake up and drink, this is saḥūr. And saḥūr make you different from other nations. Other nations, they don't have saḥūr – only once. You, AllāhNone give you this chance and you must be happy with this, and you drink and eat if you can. If not, you can, only one drink, and sleep. But, you must [make] intention. After, AllāhNone reward you, in shā’a Llāh.

Everything Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam show us. And for breaking fast, heNone said, break fast with date. Date, it is also blessed. AllāhNone create this tree from Ādam ‘alayhi s-salām, same earth. HeNone create Ādam ‘alayhi s-salām and create date tree. For this, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, "Date is you are auntie," for tree. And so, in this fruit, it is, everything AllāhNone put in this fruit. Even if you, in day time, with some five or ten one, whole day you can, it's enough for you to live. No problem, if you not eat anything [else] even, it is no problem. Everything in this. For this, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying to make ifṭār, break fast, with date. Maybe, when you cannot find date, heNone said with water also you can break fast.

Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, heNone is our guide. You must, everything what heNone said, there is many benefit for us. Many good things for us. Because, fasting giving you for your spiritual power – spiritual food. Because, human being, AllāhNone create them for... not for only for eating, drinking. Without this, if you are doing only eating, drinking, without belief and accept and obey AllāhNone and Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, you are like this animal. Like animal, only they have, their purpose of their life to grow, to grow them, make them big, and later you can use them for eating. But, if any human, he become bigger, fatter, nobody likes this. No, you only you think about not doing any good thing, just enjoying yourself, eating, eating, eating, even doctor, they are not happy with this. Your body not happy with this. But, if you are following and obeying, that time it's different. Maybe you can, you are fat or you are slim or you are – this is not a problem. Problem, to be obeying AllāhNone. If you not obeying, so you are second type of human being. One type for ākhirah, one type for dunyā. For dunyā what they are doing? Animal, what they are doing. Animal – the same thing who following dunyā desires, this. If not, it will be worse than this animal.

AllāhNone – we are lucky AllāhNone guide us in this way and to know what we must do, what we must not do. This is also very important, not what they're saying. Now, they have law, everything coming against humanity, against human being, and they are happy. People slowly, slowly accepting. But AllāhNone not accept this. AllāhNone call you to good thing. These people calling you to bad thing. AllāhNone help us. AllāhNone keep us safe from these bad people, in shā’a Llāh. For sake of AllāhNone, for sake of ProphetNone and for sake this holy month, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
