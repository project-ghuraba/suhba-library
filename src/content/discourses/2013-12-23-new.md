Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Yawmun jadeed, sha'nun jadeed. (New day, new glory). It says renew every day. Let's say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. May our each day be a new glory. And this is being asked today. Yawmun jadeed. Sha'nun jadeed. Our Mawla is Subhan. He created us for action, for His servanthood. He wants the world to be happy. "May My servants be happy with the traces (athar) of My Omnipotence (Qudrat). May they find relief. May My servants not be oppressed. May they not stand still, may they open, may they sit within My Dominions. May they increase My Dominions and find relief in them for themselves and their children." May illnesses and troubles be away from us. May we say Allah Allah Allah wa Lillahi l-hamd. Fatiha.
