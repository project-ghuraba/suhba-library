As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa raḥmatu Llāhi wa barakātuhu.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Wa ṣ-ṣalātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlina Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīn wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh madad yā Mashāyikhinā dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistāni, Mawlānā Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī, madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

We are thanking Mr Mawlá, we call him Ibrāhīm. Thank you for your welcoming your... Long, we are, long time we lost contact but it was from heart still but from appearance we not hearing anything. But the soul it was there is contact to bring us here again. Thank you because Mawlānā when he come some place there is wisdom and there is maqām for him. So he was coming here many time this place. So this bring us again here.

And it is for him it was place bring many people to way where there are people looking, searching for reality. And this place many people they come to reality, to find what they are looking for. Because we are seeing here Switzerland very rich country very beautiful country. Our Lord Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla, HeNone created very beautiful, very nice place and HeNone give everything for this country. Give beauty, give water, give health, work, business, and many money – rich country. And this people they are not so happy because they don't have the real value, valuable thing.

Valuable thing as ProphetNone – once heNone was sitting in like this Jum‘a praying and near himNone was one ṣaḥābah, his companion. Ask him, 'Look around you. Which one here the most high position, the the highest one? Look around you," [he] said. And he was looking and he said, "This in my right side that little further." He was wearing very good and he look like rich man. And heNone said for him also, "Look for the lowest one in this place." And he was looking, looking and so he one was from far little bit, this he said he couldn't find poorest than him. He said, "This one." And ProphetNone say, "You, you say right. This one the highest one you see and that one real it is he is the lowest one. But you know this one what you said he is highest one, if whole world like him it is not comparing [to] this one what you see the lowest one."

So it is not important to be rich or to be in strong and your country is good or not good. The most important to find the reality. Reality it is important because now we are here like shadow. As we said, after two hour, three hour, nobody here was here. After Mawlānā was saying all this example every time. Before hundred year nobody here [was here]. After hundred year also nobody here [will be here]. Only our reality it will be continue, continuing and to next life. We have next life. This is what people, shayṭān as shadow what you said, they not let people to see this reality. Only seeing material and running after this, not running after reality. So ProphetNone he showing us – okay you can be enjoy in this beauty. AllāhNone give you this. If you enjoy and you are believe and you have this belief for reality of our LordNone you will be safe and you will be happy. If you don't have this, if whole world for you it is not important.

And this is enough for this majlis. Thank you for your listening, mā shā’a Llāh.

Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
