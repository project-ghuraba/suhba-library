Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya

Allah Azza wa Jalla says He forgives sins; He forgives all sins. Repent and ask for forgiveness. When we repent and ask for forgiveness for whatever mistakes we have made, Allah Exalted and Glorious be He certainly forgives them. Of course, Shaitan is the enemy of humans, he does not want good for them, and wants them to sin. However, Allah Azza wa Jalla makes Shaitan’s efforts come to nothing due to His mercy. Allah wants people to repent and get cleaned from the sins they have committed. And Shaitan wants them to fall into more sin and send them to hell.

People of this day and age are ignorant. They are more ignorant than people of old times. People of old times had a stronger belief, but those of today are not like that. We should make good dua for them, not bad dua. May Allah give them guidance and may Allah grant them faith as well. May they also enter the mercy of Allah. Allah says He forgives everything, all sins, once they repent and ask for forgiveness from the sins they have committed.

“Innallaha yaghfiruz-zunuba jamee’a.” (Sura Zumar:53) Allah Azza wa Jalla forgives all sins once you repent. When you truly repent, He also turns those sins into good deeds. They would be gaining from this. If the sins they have committed are like mountains, they would be good deeds like mountains in the sight of Allah. It is in the hadiths of our Holy Prophet (SAW) and what Allah Azza wa Jalla says in the verses of the Koran. Allah Azza wa Jalla said so.

Therefore, the door of repentance is open. May Allah give these people and us guidance and show us the right path Inshallah. May Allah not deviate us from the right path. May Shaitan’s efforts come to nothing Inshallah.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq. Fatiha.
