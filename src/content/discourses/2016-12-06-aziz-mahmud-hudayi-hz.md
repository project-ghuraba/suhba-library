Allāhu Allāhu Allāhu Allāh. Allāhu Allāhu Allāhu Allāh. Allāhu Allāhu Allāhu Allāh. Allāhu Allāhu Allāhu Allāh. Allāhu Allāhu Allāhu Allāh. Allāhu Allāhu Allāhu Allāh. Allāhu Allāhu Allāhu Allāh. Allāhumma ṣalli wa sallim ‘alá nabiyyinā Muḥammad ‘alayhi s-salām, ṣalātan tadūmu wa tuhdá ’ilayh, māmarra l-layāli wa ṭūla d-dawām. Allāhumma ṣalli wa sallim ‘alá nabiyyinā Muḥammad ‘alayhi s-salām, ṣalātan tadūmu wa tuhdá ’ilayh, māmarra l-layāli wa ṭūla d-dawām.

Mā shā’a Llāh. Opera. Turkish opera. International opera. Universal opera. Yā Rabbi, we want to be opera for Your sake, yā Rabbi. Yā Rabbi, we want to be for You, yā Rabbi. Yā Rabbi, we are asking to accept. We are asking You to accept us to be for You because our asking is not having any value without your acceptance. If you know this, then you realize everything. Everything is according to acceptance of His majestic and His magnificent and His the most beautiful acceptance must come from Him. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh. Because we are nothing. You have to realize that you are nothing. You have to understand that you are nothing. And you have to know that you cannot claim nothing. Even the claiming it should be according to His permission. Nobody can claim.

The ignorant people in the history when the Prophets they've been coming, they looked for that Prophet. He look very derwish. He look like German derwish, not you either. You are not German derwish. I have my German derwish there. And he is homeless, no money, no wife, no children, no nothing and say "I am Rasūla Llāh." And in olden days they looked at him. "How come you can be Rasūla Llāh? You can be Prophet of Allāh?" This is for before Sayyidina Muḥammad (saw). Say "Yes, I've been sent for you because you are wrong people. You are doing wrong things. You're behaving wrong. That's why, I've been sent to you." And then that people they say "But you never look like a Prophet. You are only German derwish. Who will believe you?" Say "He sent me and He accept me to be His Prophet and carrying the prophecy for you." Then they say "But you have to bring some wealth with you. You have to have very good income. And at least you will have some angel with you that to prove your prophecy." How the people, they were looking? But Allāh Almighty He never get it as a show-off.

That's why we say, if somebody asking something and asking some kind of wealth from his murīd or from his followers or from his the one they believe in him and says, "I need your money. I need your gold. I need your precious stones, or I need anything from you", this is not true Shaykh, not true prophecy. The true prophecy or the true sainthood, it was never by asking, it was by giving. It was by giving. Because the sainthood, it is not come to depend on people, or the prophecy was not depending on.. depending on the Lord Allāh Almighty. If Allāh Almighty give you permission and accept you to be in the prophecy or in the sainthood, that means you are over everything. There is no material can buy you. Material cannot buy you. No. You look for Mawlana Shaykh, not any material was buying him. When it's coming for ḥaqq, he say "It is ḥaqq - if you like it or you don't like it." From where this power coming? Do you think from where this power coming? Coming from the ḥaqq, from Him because he say, "I separated you from the materialstic I am the one looking after you, you German derwish." And he is the most happy one. No tax to pay. If you give him food, he is happy. If he doesn't give him food, he'll wait for the second meal, doesn't matter. If you give him something, he will be happy, it is ok.

This is the heavenly satisfaction. This is the way of sainthood. This is the way how you can be a saint. To collect money from people, it is not sainthood. To ask money from people, it is not sainthood. To ask something from people, sorry to say, we call it beggar. If I ask money from you, say "Oh, what kind of..." By the way, I'm part-time sainthood also. It is how can be? How you can accept this? "Give me this, give me this... I like this in you, give me this. I like... You take this one, I sell you this one." This is not a trade or commercial area. This is spiritual area. In the spirituality, the money it doesn't have any value because here it is not for money trade. We don't accept. Some people they have something in their heart and they are willing to give. I don't have any issue with this one because also we have order to give ṣadaqah, to give zakāh, to give help. This is order. But in Ottoman time, there was in the city some places, some people, they went there and put money. And some people they are in need for money, they go and they take what they are in need. It should be full of gold. But he say "My need is one gold". He take it and he was not getting all the gold. Say "It's mine." No, even that one was have adab. He was have good manner. Good manner, it was taught by ahlu t-taṣawwuf, by ahlu ṭ-ṭarīqa - because when you are entering, say adab yā Hū. What means adab yā Hū. Means be in good manner. Try to learn the good manner. Whatever you need, it's for you. Things that you're doing don't need - don't touch it. Very important.

This is how the teaching of the spirituality, how the teaching how to be saint. Allāh Almighty He make the door of sainthood to be open for everybody. He didn't say "This is for you, for you... And not for you, and not for you". No. Allāh Almighty so generous. He opened it for everybody. He open it for everybody. And He say it is open for you to move on. This is very important. The prophecy is different. Prophecy has been given for some people. That's why, the mashāyikh ʿiẓām, the Grandshaykh, they were all the time saying "If Allāh Almighty He give me some maqām, I want to carry all my murīd also with me. I want to take all these murīd with me. I want them to be in that maqām." That's why, all the people they come and glue for the Shaykh. That's why these people they come and love the Shaykh. These people, the true Shaykh all the time been having special kind of love. True Shaykh. There are so many fake Shaykhs also. That's why I'm saying to myself I am part-time Shaykh. Could be fake, could be not. No claiming. Don't think to claim because the claiming is the most dangerous area. Because they say immediately will tell you - prove it. If you don't claim, derwish, you will be free because no claiming. This is the good things about derwish - there is nothing to claim. If you are in the first level, so nobody will say, claim you are derwish. I am derwish. What to claim? I don't have money, I don't have house, I don't have this, I don't have that. I am with the Shaykh. Wherever he take me, will go. He feed me, I'm happy. He doesn't feed me, also ok. Waiting. This is derwish. Derwish doesn't have to claim anything. That's why, derwish it is things that - you take off all your dunyā things and then he will be something.

Now Shaykh Meḥmed (qs) he say one story about derwish. And it is historical story. It is happened with Aziz Hudayi hz. - this is big awliyā’ in Istanbul. Many of us been visiting his maqām. He was qāḍī l-quḍā. That means the judge of the judges. And he was... The story was like this. This is his titles and his position. One man, he was a poor man. And this poor man he wanted to go for ḥajj. Every time he try, he doesn't have money to prepare himself to go for ḥajj. Those days the ḥajj it was need 6 months by travelling. And this man one day he was so angry that he missed ḥajj and he say "I will be divorcing my wife in three divorce if this year I'm not going for ḥajj." That make as a promising for himself and also punishing for himself if he cannot make it this year. Then he was so trying hard. But when you are poor, you are always poor. Astaghfiru Llāh.

The ḥajj time coming and only few days left and he couldn't go to ḥajj. Like six months to go. He didn't find the money and only very short time for ḥajj time. And he start to look around how to do, how to go. And he's swearing also. What he was saying, it's come. And his wife say to him "You are making big promising and you didn't make it. And I think I am already divorced from you. Out of the house! You out! Out of the house! Not ḥajji? No, finished. You cannot make it? Yalla." She was angry with him. Of course. How you make such crazy things? Man crying, crying, crying. And he went right, he went left and he saw one derwish. And he say for this derwish "You know I make such a mistake that even I'm not going for ḥajj, I'm losing my wife also." And you know in those days how to marry wife? It is so expensive also. He say "I have my Shaykh. Let's go to him and we say how we do? We look for some solution. Maybe he can find a solution for you." And it was Hasirci Baba, his name in Bursa.

And this story it was happening in Bursa city. He went to him. And he said "I have such a thing. And I'm very poor man. Please help me." And he said "You see this derwish?" Say "Yes." "This derwish he is able to take you for Makkah and to be in 'Arafah and he make your hajj and to bring you back." And say "With your permission, O my master?" "Yes, I give you permission. Take him for ḥajj." And he make like this for him. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm -woosh - They were in 'Arafah. He give him iḥrām and he make the ḥajj, ṭawāf. And then he see some people from the city that they are coming for ḥajj from Bursa. And he buy few things and he say "Please, maybe I cannot join you with travelling. Please take this one with you and bring it to me." "Yes, yes" They know him very well. He give them the gift. And after finishing ḥajj duty, they say Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm - woosh - They come and he was going to the qāḍī. And the qāḍī was with Hudayi hz., Aziz Hudayi hz. He say to him "I was having such a divorce from my wife but al-ḥamdu liLlāh I managed to make ḥajj. And I come back". And he say "It is impossible. When you go?" He say "I go this day." "And when you come?" "I came this week." Say "In two days you make this hajj and you come? This is no way."

And say "But you have witness?" "Yes, I have witness." "So what is your witness?" Say, "The caravan, I give some gift for them. And they see me." And he say "I have to wait for them to make this decision that you divorce your wife or not." Everybody say this is dreaming. The caravan arrive after few months and he go for these people. And these people they brought gift to him. "Look, you give us this. And we brought". "Ok, you come with me for the qāḍī." And they went to qāḍī. And he say, "See these people?" "Yes, we see him. He give us this gift. And he make ḥajj with us. And after that we didn't see him. Maybe he take another caravan. We don't know." And say "Ok, with the witness I accept your marriage is valid" Because the wife also was not believing him and sent him away "You go, you go. You are dreaming. You cannot come." Then decision been made. This the part of...

First part of the story. Now we make another....

He say "You, I want you to know how you go". He say "This derwish he take me for ḥajj." He went to him and say "How you take him to ḥajj?" He say "It is not my secret. It's my master's secret." And he say "Who is your master?" He say "Hasirci Baba." "Where can I find him?" Because his heart start to trembling. And he say "It is in that place." Ok, he have very expensive jubbah called qafṭān. This qafṭān only the king or high position people can wear it. It is like very precious, you know, like maybe you can buy few houses with this one - so high. He put his qafṭān, ride his horse, going for the address of Hasirci Baba. When he come nearby, he was in the rock. And the rock catch the leg of the horse and the horse cannot go and cannot come. He look and say "I have to walk for him." And he start walking and then the earth it was coming very muddy that he cannot walk in two feet. So he was walking like down with the hand and feet. And he reach to that Shaykh place and he see like our German derwish - Shaykh working in the garden. And he have another derwish working with him, very messy dressed like doesn't look like a real Shaykh. No big turban, no big beard, no big dresses, no big cars. Only very humble, beyond humble person, even he doesn't have turban. He was having only hat and working. And he was never looking for him. He was not intending to look at him.

And say he come, was everything coming mud all this qafṭān was dirty. He say "You didn't know to come for Shaykh, to meet master, to come in the horse proudly? Do you think if this Shaykh want, cannot change so many things? We want you to come to us with four, with your hand and with your feet, not to come riding your horse. We don't care what is your position. We don't care who you are. We are not interested with dunyā matters. It is not our concern. It is not our interest. What is our interest? It is Allāh. We don't think anything else." Say "I come here to learn from you." "What you want to learn? Before you learn, we have to give you duty. Then we teach you." He say "Any duty I will do." "Ok, if you accept the duty, we can teach you." He say "I'm ready." "Ok, turn back and go for the slaughter house." "Yes." And he say "You will buy the liver, this is the inside, intestines. And you take a wood here in 7 years. And you sell it in Bursa. And every evening bring one for us. Also derwish need to eat." And he was with the qafṭān and he take it off, putting, selling. This meat all blood on him up and down for 7 years. For 7 years he was selling. And everybody say this qāḍī, the biggest judge, he become crazy, selling intestines for people. 7 years he was selling. 7 years.

And in the end of 7 years, he come one day, he is coming bringing also for derwish to eat. Derwish were having good times those days. And say "Now your duty finished." He was happy and say "Now I give you new duty." Say "What is it?" He say "You go for the mosque, big mosque, and clean the toilet." And he was cleaning for another 3 years. At the end of 3 years he say "Now we give you your secret, we give you your power." And he become the most biggest 'ālim and the most biggest awliyā’ in Istanbul. And until today his power is going on and on and on. You say how it's happened? Because with these 7 years and 3 years, he finish his ego. Nothing left. Then Allāh Almighty He put him high. This is Allāh Almighty, it doesn't look for material things. You cannot say to Allāh "Yā Rabbi, I am this materialistic, this rich this powerful." You are talking to Allāh Almighty, you are not talking to me. So I won't be impressed. Maybe you make me impressed. But Allāh Almighty nobody can make Him impressed. This is Allāh. It's very important to understand this key point. This is... Then He give him, He give him, He give him, He give him... Then how He give him? Even universe for him, it doesn't worth nothing. Even they say this universe belonging to you, for him it doesn't make any interest because he have more higher things that it's Allāh.

This is important for us to understand what we are doing and to understand the material issue. We don't have any issue with it. We don't care. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh Allāh Almighty he give us dry bread, also ok. And if we have derwish like Aziz Maḥmud Hudayi hz, he bring us some meat, will be ok also. This is the way to get things. This is the way to understand things that it belonging to Allāh Almighty. That whatever Allāh Almighty He give us, it will be very acceptable and very nice and to make us to be satisfied. May Allāh make us from the people that they've been satisfied from their positions and they are satisfied from their Lord Allāh Almighty. "Raḍī Allāhu 'anhum wa raḍī 'anh" This is big maqām. And in shā’a Llāh we're trying to be in that object. We're trying. I'm trying very hard by the way. But we're still trying in shā’a Llāh.
