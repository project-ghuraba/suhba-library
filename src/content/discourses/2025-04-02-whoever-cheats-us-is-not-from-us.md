As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Yā-ayyuhā lladhīna āmanū in jā’akum.fāsiqun bi-naba’in fa-tabayyanū an tuṣībū qawman bi-jahālatin fa-tuṣbiḥū ‘alá mā fa‘altum nādimīn” (49:6). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l- ‘Aẓīm. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says when you hear some news, when you hear about something, investigate it thoroughly. Ensure it is not wrong and you are not given false news. If you do something then, you may regret it. Therefore, we must surely investigate everything before acting. Your business, others’ business, business of jamā‘ah, business of ṭarīqah; you must be completely sure and only then tell people how this is and how that is. You will not be responsible then.

The matter of ṭarīqah is that people come to ṭarīqah for the pleasure of AllāhNone. They find a door to stay away from dunyā. They come in order to hold onto that door. And then, because those who come are pure, because they have come with pure intentions, they sometimes come across ignorant people inside. They think they are important ones and follow them, whereas they must ask and learn about them. It is not their fault, because they came for the door of AllāhNone. They came to the dergah, they came to the madrasah, they came to a mosque. That is something for the pleasure of AllāhNone. They don’t expect it there. But it is everywhere. Even in the least expected place, the holiest place: Ka‘bah, Masjidu l-Ḥarām. Every good deed earns a hundred thousand rewards, and sins there are a hundred thousand times more. They commit theft at Ḥajaru l-Aswad. They steal people’s money. That happens too. It happens in dergahs as well. Therefore, when people come to the dergah, they shouldn’t ignore it. People must ensure their safety. “Who is this person? What is he doing there? Can you trust his words, or not?” It must be investigated so that one may benefit.

We are standing here for the pleasure of AllāhNone, for the benefit of everyone. We are standing here by command. When such people come, we get upset. We get upset when people come with bad intentions, or without good intentions, or with sick minds, and people believe them, then go another way. Therefore, even though we warn them, they don’t listen. So we have to put their names out. And that is to expose them so that people are protected. That is for their protection. There is taqdīr (gentle guidance) and takdīr (stern reprimand). One who does not understand through taqdīr, then takdīr comes into effect. Taqdīr is explaining with good words. If they do not understand, then you must explain with harsh words. You must punish him so that the people who come are not harmed. Everything happens for the benefit of people. There is punishment and reward in sharī‘ah. Therefore, outside there are names and photos of people here and there from time to time. That is not something we like to do. We do it because we must, so that this man is not harmed, so that he does not fall into sin by harming others. These things are done to ensure that people are not harmed either. Therefore, one must be cautious. When you enter the dergah, you must be cautious and check who the person is, so that you do not end up in loss while hoping for gain. This is important.

Our Holy ProphetNone, in his last days... Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla did this for our Holy ProphetNone. They were not in Makkah because it was difficult there. When heNone came to Madīnah, there were many hypocrites. If they were not Muslims, they would be in trouble. They were the ones who looked like Muslims but were enemies of Islām. These people exist everywhere, at all times. Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam knew them. And most of the Ṣaḥābah knew them too. Sometimes, they would mention their names, sometimes they wouldn’t. Their condition is also mentioned in the Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n. For this reason, the Ṣaḥābah were also informed to be careful of them. That is why we must be cautious in the dergah as well. There may be hypocrites. There may be those lacking wisdom.

There may be fools and those with bad intentions. Therefore, you must ask and inquire about those who are here. You must stay away from such people. If he were not harmful, we would not speak of it today. But he has done many things that harmed many people. And he does not understand from advice. He doesn’t understand anything. So be careful.

May AllāhNone protect us. May AllāhNone improve these people. It is ṭarīqah here. People come to improve. Some people also come to cause corruption. May AllāhNone help us. We will speak the truth. We will say the truth. We do not take offence, nor do we fear anyone. Therefore, may AllāhNone help us. AllāhNone is with the righteous.

Those with bad intentions... “Wa-lā yaḥīqu l-makru s-sayi’u illā bi-ahlih” (35:43), says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. The harm of those with evil intentions returns upon them. May AllāhNone improve them, so that this harm does not come to them. May they repent and seek forgiveness, in shā’a Llāh.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Yā-ayyuhā lladhīna āmanū in jā’akum fāsiqun bi-naba’in fa-tabayyanū an tuṣībū qawman bi-jahālatin fa-tuṣbiḥū ‘alá mā fa‘altum nādimīn” (49:6). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l- ‘Aẓīm. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, saying in this verses, “O believer, be sure when you do something.” When you think about some people, something – to not be regret what you say, what you done – first, you must be very sure about everything, anything you do. This is important in Islām. Not without searching, without doing, looking here, there. “This man, he said for us, “This, good.” We must follow this.” So, everybody running, following. This man said this thing, making you to win money, too much money: “You put this one, you take ten, you take thousand. You go, you put your money, or your money it will be disappear.” Many thing, like this. So, you must be sure about what it is right, what it is not right, what is good, what is not good. This is very important order from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla for believer: “Don't be make people to cheat you.” "Man ghashshanā fa-laysa minnā.” Who cheats us is not from us. “Don't be cheated by this people,” Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying.

We are living, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, in time, it is difficult. And people, they are, most of them, they like to come for spirituality. They, for, to look for happiness in places like, very rare place, and they are coming. And after this what happen? Also in this place, maybe, some bad intention people or idiot people or crazy people, they catch this people and they teach them what it is not correct. For this, we are warning people about this, we are... But, we are first, as we, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, we search too much, and warning too much this people, to not do this. Don't do this. Don't make people going crazy, going unhappy from here. But still, there is people, they are doing this. Because, this all time like this, since Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, and until Qiyāmah, maybe, it will be this people. Where is the holiest place? Ka‘bah. So not to be surprised to be, in people in dergah, they come to dergah and they are cheated, it can be. No... In Ka‘bah, the most holiest place in the world which, everything, hundred thousand time reward, hundred thousand punishment. The people they are stealing people, in this ṭawāf. So, it is not weird to see in dergah something like this.

For this, we don't like to say thing for people, but we are obligatory. Because, they are coming for AllāhNone. And, but this people still not learning, from naṣīḥah (advice) or warning them or anything. Just still continue. And people going, because they are coming by pure heart. They surround themselves for dergah, for this place, for people to be clean, to be good. And when this people doing this, they are, become no benefit. Harm, for them. For this, we are warning people. Especially, we put something here. We not like to put name or to put picture, but we obligatory. Because we are sure, since many years, he harm people. They make people to run away, or to be harmed from this. Many thing, bad thing happen with this people. So, we are obligatory to say this. To make them, openly showing this. Because people, as much you aim, you can't, without saying name or without telling people about this one, they are still not understanding. For this we are, AllāhNone forgive us, but it is, sometime must be firm about this.

And this people, they are, they not bring people. They are coming middle of dergah or murīd of Mawlānā, take them. If they find people from street or other place, okay but, no problem, they can do. But they are, they don't have any ashamed, they don't have any, thing, ḥayā’. They are just harming people. For this, we are not happy and by, without happiness we put this. We must be to take responsibility out of us. We, in shā’a Llāh, we put this. AllāhNone give hidāyah for these people, not to harm people. Because, everything they harm people, AllāhNone will ask from them, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone keep us safe from these people. But what we will do? Human being like this, not... AllāhNone create them for test for other, test for everybody. So be sure, when coming to dergah or another place, asking, if somebody saying something, ask about him. He is good or not good? Shaykh agree with him, or not agree with him? Because, also many place, people, they are claiming they are wakīl, they are this, they are that. But it is for benefit, benefit for themself. But this is no benefit at all.

AllāhNone make them to be good people, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone forgive us.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
