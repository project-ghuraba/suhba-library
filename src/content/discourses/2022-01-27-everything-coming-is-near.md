As-Salāmu ‘Alaykum wa Raḥmatullāhi wa Barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu Billāhi Minash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm.

Wa ṣ-Salātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Akhirīn.

Madad yā RasūlAllāh, Madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, Madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

Dastūr Mawlana Shaykh Abdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistāni, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-

Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-Suḥbah wa l-Khayru fi l-Jam‘iyyah.

Our Holy Prophet says:

“Everything coming is near”, says our Holy Prophet salla Llahu aa’layhi wa sallam. Also, Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm:

(Qur’ān 35:05). ‘Inna waa’da Llahi haqq’, ‘Indeed the promise of Allah is truth.’ The promise of Allah is true. Allah ‘Azza wa-Jalla has told about the things that will happen or come through our Holy Prophet. The things that are going to happen are near, they are not far away. Because as they are going to come, they are close.

What is going to come? Qiyamah will come; people will be gathered for judgment and go to paradise. It seems far away for people. However, it is close. The end of the world has come near. Our Holy Prophet had said, “I and Qiyamah are as close as these two fingers”. 1,400 years have passed since then, and we have approached the end. Therefore, there’s no need to worry about this world. What Allah ‘Azza wa-Jalla had said will happen. “The government did this and that. Will there be war, or not?” It is the order of Allah.

Our Holy Prophet has told everything that will happen. Awliya know it too. Some know and some don’t know. Those who don’t know don’t want to know. Some Awliya don’t care about Dunya at all; what will go and what will stay. Their hearts are only with Allah. If the world is set on fire or not, they don’t care about it. And it is the duty of some Awliya to protect this world until that time, to be together with Mahdi ‘alayhi s-salam. Therefore, all of this is in our belief as Ahlu l-Sunnah wa l-Jama’ah. Mahdi ‘alayhi s-salam will come, and ‘Isa ‘alayhi s-salam will come down.

There are some groups who say that it will be a spiritual coming. It is not spiritual. He will come physically, in his body. He will come from his abode in heavens. And he is on the second heaven. When the time comes, he will come down from there. He will kill the pig, remove the cross, and no religion will be left except Islam. People won’t follow other religions, because they won’t be able to say that they are with ‘Isa but they are not Muslim. Sayyidina ‘Isa will follow Islam too. The whole world will be Muslim then.

This is the promise of Allah ‘Azza wa-Jalla. And it has been told by our Holy Prophet that it is near and it will come insha’Allah. How close is it? It is close and it will come. It is not important. What’s important is that it will happen with the permission of Allah. Whatever is not going to happen is not near, says our Holy Prophet. And whatever is going to happen is near. Whether it takes ten years or twenty years, what’s important is that it will happen.

A year passes instantly. Days and months are flying. Therefore, everything is near. Accordingly, Muslims should rejoice with these good tidings and not fear about the condition of the world. As long as you are with Allah, you are safe. May Allah give this Iman and peace to all of us insha’Allah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
