Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm, La hawla wa la quwatta ila billahi l­’Aliyu l'Adhim. 

Everyone is suitable or created to be suitable [to] bring wisdoms from Divinely Presence. Not looking and saying from books that wisdoms - you are only going to be like cassettes. Yes. Cassettes keeping and saying. Does not mean that this cassette knows wisdoms. No. If it can say by itself, it is going, but when you are putting in it, writing on it, recording on it - they are not wise ones, all these tapes. No! Therefore, you must try to reach wisdom sources through your heart. And the main condition for that it is to be sincere. Sincerity.

Sincerity, first condition which taking you to certainty. And when you are coming to certainty you should find sources of wisdoms through your heart. But sincerity it is not easy to reach to that sincerity. [There is] No market [where] you can ask and you can buy it. But only you can find it through sincere people of the Lord Almighty Allah. And sincere people, they are Holy men. Holy men, they are wise men. You may find sincerity through themselves and, when you are going to be with them, that sincerity coming from that one on you also. Or you can't learn it through reading books. No.

But from one to the second one - running that good attributes. Therefore Allah Almighty not sending only books but sending books through His servants, through His Prophets. Because Prophets they are [the] most sincere ones. And when they are giving from Holy Books to their companions, they are giving with their sincerity. Therefore, you must look after [for] sincere people. And you must be time by time with association with them and it's giving to you sincerity and then you may reach to certainty. Certainty is most important thing for believers.

And Allah Almighty ordering to believers to reach to certainty in their faith. Asta'idhu billah,

يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓا۟ ءَامِنُوا۟ بِٱللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِ
“Yā ayyuhā l-ladhīna āmanū āminū bi-Llāhi wa-rasūlihi”
“O you who believe! Believe in Allah, and His Messenger (Muhammad ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam) (Qurʼān 4:136)

You must, you must reach to certainty. Allah Almighty saying - O! Believers, whom they are believing only by saying, you must reach to certainty in your beliefs. When you are saying something, you must know that it is 100% that is all right - no doubt through certainty. And there you should find wisdoms and that wisdoms may take you to divinely presence. That is the means that Prophets and Awliyās using for approaching from [to] Divinely Presence.

You can't approach from [to] Divinely Presence by material means, by flights, as Nimrod was expecting that he may reach with some means to the skies to reach to the Lord of Universes for fighting. Don't be such a Nimrod. No! We can't approach with material means to Allah Almighty's divinely presence. We may use wisdoms. Wisdoms making you to approach from [to] Divinely Presence. And we are in need for that wisdoms. And in our time, people they are in need for that wisdoms only. Knowledge ­- we have enough knowledge. But we must practice certainty so that we may reach to wisdoms that Allah Almighty gives us.

And that is going, as angels they have wings, mankind's wings - that is wisdoms. And according to your wisdoms, you may approach from [to] Divinely Presence. Some small motorcycles, there is like hair, petrol runs and makes it to move. But there is 24 tires big lorries - can't, can't move with such a things. And also that planes flies - according to their size and power you must give them that petrol for moving, to make it to move. And you are looking and seeing rockets, how much it needs for power for moving to cut [the] atmosphere and get out from the gravity of this world. Must have strong ones. According to their power, they are going up, approaching.

And according to your worshipping and asking wisdoms. According to your wisdoms you may move towards Divinely Presence. And you are free to ask more because Allah Almighty gives you as much as you can ask. He is giving, He is not greedy. No. Giving. "Ask!" He says, "And I am giving." Allah gives us from His endless blessings, endless favours and understanding so that we should be able to move towards our destinations in Divinely Presence.

Wa l-hamdulillahi rabbi l-'alamin

Al Fātiḥah
