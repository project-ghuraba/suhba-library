Shaykh Muhammad Nazim Al-Haqqani An-Naqsbandi, Sohbat of the 21st of March, 2014.

He has power in his eyes. They (ahlil-Bayt) have so much power they can destroy (their enemy). Let him pray for me. I am speaking to our sisters. Every Juma they are coming here to hear something. He is Ali Riza Effendi! He is Ali Riza Efendi! Hoshamadi (welcome in Persian) Your father? His father, he looks younger than him. This is the mother? Welcome! Insha'Allah with Mahdi aleihi as-salam. With Mahdi aleihi as-salam.

O my monkey........................

I like him too much. Astaghfirullah, astaghfirullah. Always he serves me. Turn me to the other side. Insha'Allah soon Libya and the surrounding countries will be under the governance of Kings. Sanussi. Rabbul 'Izza Subhana we Ta'ala will take the rule will take the wealth from those people. The rule is for you (the Libyan prince). MashaAllah! Tell him (the prince) when he is on the throne, he shouldn't look down on me and say, "who is this one!"

P: Astaghfirullah!

M: Our Malik, our king! Alhamdulillah, Alhamdulillah with the blessings insha'Allah, insha'Allah everything will go back to its original state. Sovereignty is for Allah. He gives it to whom He wills, Allah! Jalla Jalalu. Ali Effendi (a guest) masha'Allah! I love him very much. Ali Effendi. Insha'Allah His Majesty will be with Mahdi aleihi as-salam. You (to the prince) the Sanussi's are Amiru-l Mu'minin. Allah Jalla wa 'Ala gave it to you, He gave you a station. He gave saintliness. He you gave light to your faces. Masha'Allah, Allah! My Allah!

This one doesn't leave me ever, my monkey. Tawba, tawba.

Asakir Mansura Muhammadiyya/ The victorious warriors of Muhammad. Everything will return to its original state. Welcome to you, welcome to you. Welcome. What are these things? N: Nothing! Only a pillow! M: I thought this is cherries for us to serve you. Insha'Allah the rule of Libya will be given to the kings, to Sanussi Hz. Kiss his hand! Welcome to you, welcome. May they make dua/pray for me. You are all royalty, and it is not suitable for your station to visit a weak servant like me. May Allah raise your station higher. Sultan, may Allah raise his station higher and higher, masha'Allah. He has light on his face. MashaAllah!

The ladies here are my daughters. Our mothers, our wives, ladies are our sultans.

H.R.: After Juma prayer he comes here & talks to the ladies. He is saying my daughters they're our mothers, they're our sultans our ladies.

M: He has light/nur on his face, The visit of these holy ones is a great honour for us. They are the holy grandchildren of Rasulul Akram (sas), our Master. Kiss his hand! What a beautiful face he has! How beautiful! They have the light of the Prophet on their faces!

P: It's your lights.

M: It is the light of the Prophet(sas). These honourable people are humble enough to visit us. These ladies are all my daughters. This one is our sayyid/decendant of the Prophet. Our lion, our sultan. Kiss your father's hand!

Ah! If there were no ladies, all men would have gone to hell. Kiss your father's hand! He is the man of the Mahdi. Turn me to the other side. He is the man of the Sultan. Welcome to all of them (all the ladies). All will enter through our Fatimatu-z Zahra Mother's door. By that way ladies will not go to hell.

What's up, how are you.

Hawa: Alhamdulillah.

M: Serve proper food to the guests. This seat is not proper for him, not for His Highness. Do not make him sit there. It is not proper to look into his eyes. It is adab/manners.It is not proper to look into your eyes.

G: Astaghfirullah.

M: May His Highness glance at me with the glance of Ahlul Bayt. May my illnesses go away! May we reach to Imam Sanussi Hazrat! May we reach Mahdi aleihi as-salam, Fatiha.

Women all are ladies, don't say just women. Say like this! All those whose name is Fatma will enter into heaven.

You also welcome! Welcome. Give me some water! Let him drink first and then give me. May Ahlul Bayt come ya Rabbi, Ahlul Bayt! May Ahlul Bayt come, al-Habib's beloved grandchildren may they come! They come to us, to a poor and weak servant like me. They came to visit us, they are royal, aman Ya Rabbi. I wish I could be the sand that our Mother Fatimatu z-Zahra steps on.

InshaAllah, sahib-ul sayf, sahib-ul sayf. Mubarak Ahl al Bayt. Welcome. They will come with Mahdi alayhi s-salam. They will. They are lions.

Welcome ladies, Shukr Allah, welcome! Salamun aleikum. There are potatoes, there are tomatoes also, peppers also. When I get angry I am like pepper with the himmat of Sultans.

I wish, I will come to Khorasan. From Khorasan 70,000 lions will come, with Mahdi aleihi s-salam. With them there is a Sultan. This one is Sultan.

G: Ali Riza's father says, the best day of my life is here with you.

M: Let him give his hand and I kiss. A very beautiful man. This one will be vizier (minister). As-salam aleikum. As-salam aleikum. As-salam aleikum. Welcome! Hoshamadi, welcome. Aleikum as-salam.

May they all be people of Paradise! Allah! May Allah raise their ranks! May they be neighbours to our Mother Fatimatu z-Zahra! Amin. May their ends be good!

As-salamun aleikum. Aleikum as-salam.
