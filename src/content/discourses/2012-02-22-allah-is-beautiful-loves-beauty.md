Our Lord loves the beautiful ones. He, Himself, is Beautiful. Absolute Beauty belongs to Him. He loves the beautiful ones. "O our Lord, make us from the beautiful ones. Don't make us ugly ones, O our Lord". "O My servant, you are the one who makes yourself ugly. I don't make you ugly, you are the one who makes yourself ugly". Such an address is coming. Be beautiful ones, O people. Run to beauty, O people. This is what our Lord wants: "I am beautiful & I love beautiful ones. I don't love the ugly ones."

He says. Who are the ugly ones? They are the bad ones, the ones who do bad things, who run away from good things, zulmat/darkness comes on their faces. An ugly person is not acceptable. Allah (jj) does not love them. The ones He loves are the beautiful ones. What makes a person beautiful? It is not powder, or blush, etc.. The beautiful ones are the clean-pure servants who walk on the path Allah ordered. The ones whom Allah loves & to whom Allah gives beauty- they are dressed in a new beauty, wellness in every hour. Each rebellious act towards Allah, or following one's ego makes a person ugly. Neither powder, nor blush.. nothing can help it- cannot make one look beautiful anymore.

Beauty is from Allah Almighty, O people. Be beautiful, don't be ugly ones! Tawba Ya Rabbi, tawba Ya Rabbi, Tawba Astaghfirullah. Salam Allahu Alaikum O people of Islam, O people of knowledge, O servants of Allah! Ya man Huwa yuhibbul Muhsinin - Allah loves Al-Muhsinun (the good ones). Make us from the ones who have Ihsan- goodness. Who are those who have Ihsan? They are the beautiful people. There is no goodness in ugly people. What makes a person ugly is dirty acts, sins, to not have faith, iman. He dresses light on those who believe, who doesn't believe remain in darkness. Tawba Ya Rabbi. Send us Helpers. Fatiha.

This is the first blessing-barakah & the first address of the holy month Rabi-ul Thani of the Hijri year 1433... The hijri year of 1434 to all mankind: "Be beautiful, don't be ugly. Be just, don't be oppressive ones. Be good, don't be bad ones". This is what Allah wants- He says: Be beautiful. Be beautiful, there is no other command. This is what the Lord wants. "I am beautiful & love the beautiful ones" says Allah. This is all it says in the Bible, Torah, Psalms, Holy Quran.

Masha Allah, O our Lord send us helpers. Grant us wisdom & understanding, so that we can think.. So that we can think. Therefore our Prophet (sas) says: "Thinking for one hour is better than 70 years of worship." It is this, this is the thinking. How beautiful. Endless salams to the Sultan of the Prophets (sas). The glory of the glorious Prophet (sas) in the sight of Allah, no one from us can even imagine it. Endless salat & salam to the Beloved (sas), O people! This is enough, Fatiha.

The first tamdih-praising is the barakah of today. It will come down and Allah's Heavenly Wrath-Qahr will wipe out the ugly ones. Only beautiful ones will remain on earth. "Don't kill but bring back to life. Don't cheat one of his right but give his right. Help & gain the sawab- reward of it. Run away from ugliness. Be beautiful"- This is the summary of all Holy Books. The order has come to me to declare this to the people from my sickbed, so I am informing today. The whole world may hear it, all people may hear it & say "it is true". If there is someone who finds it wrong- what I am declaring from my sickbed, what comes to my heart, may he not survive till tomorrow.

Tawba ya Rabbi, tawba O our Lord. Bring us together with the beautiful ones,take us away from the ugly ones. Who is the ugly one? shaytan. Who is the beautiful one? The Sultan of the Prophets (sas). It is enough, Fatiha.
