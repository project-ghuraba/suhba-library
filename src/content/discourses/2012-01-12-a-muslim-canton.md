"Ad-dinu nasiha" - Religion is advice, said our Prophet (sas). He is the pride of creation. The Master of the whole of creation - of all creatures, of all men created, is our Prophet (sas). We may stand up, to show respect for him. And, he (sas) is the Beloved of Allah. Shaytan & those who are with shaytan do not like him (sas). Who are with shaytan want unbelief. They have become the vehicle of their egos. People's egos are putting them in the place of a donkey & riding on them. And, people now do what their egos tell them. Meaning, their ego made people lose their humanity. Egoistic desires are animalistic desires. Now all people, to satisfy their animalistic desires & enjoy themselves, they say to their egos: "Come & ride on our back. Whichever direction you want to go, we go that way. We don't accept anyone". "If you don't accept, then in that case, we accept you. You may become one of us".

And now, most of the people have become evil-shaytani, with shaytan attributes. They don't do anything good. They don't appreciate beauty. Whatever shaytan puts in front of them- even if it is filth, they still eat it. Even if it puts urine, they drink it. Why? They say "cheers for shaytan"! They raise their glasses to shaytan. What is in it? Shaytan's urine! Therefore they have become a group who has lost their honour of humanity & became evil. Whatever one tells them, they don't accept. If they don't accept, then they may leave us - may not rule over us.

We are Muslims. We believe in Allah (jj). We believe in His prophets, in His last prophet (sas). We believe in the Holy Book & want the Divine Law Allah sent. If they don't want these, they may leave us - give us a separate land. They may live in a different canton, we may live in a different canton. We may not intefere with them, they not with us. We may look at how the situation will be in these two cantons after 40 days. The Muslim canton will be all clean, pure. There is no lying in the Muslim canton, no slavery of one to his ego, no becoming donkey for shaytan. There is servanthood to Allah, being a nation for our Prophet (sas), respecting elders, thinking about the akhirat & making ibadah (worship) for Allah. None of the haram-forbidden things to exist there. Everyone will know their place. Whoever wants to live with us will live like this, clean & pure. No debtor will be left here. Paper money will be removed in our canton. Gold & silver will come to the markets. The military service will not be compulsory but voluntary. Soldiers will receive a monthly payment & be called to service when necessary. Poor & needy will be protected. There can't be any hungry, unemployed, idle ones on our side. We will send them to their side. They may pay them. In our side will live the people who work by the sweat of their brow. Otherwise they may go to the other side. There is plenty of free, paper money there. The studying here is not for leaving the youth heedless. In our canton, studying will be for the purpose of "teaching them". The young ones who are studying will learn the knowledge they need. There will be no books in many volumes, needless courses & works here. Holy Quran will be taught them first. Then handcrafts will be taught. There will be no one here who is a burden for the government. The farmers, shepherds, tradesman.. all will do their own work & we won't bring-import anything from outside.

The blessings Allah granted us are not given to any other country. We have all the kinds here. Here rightminded, Muslim people will live. There is no enmity here. Who come against, disobey our laws here will be deported, dismissed from this canton. People cannot live as they wish here. Everyone will do whatever is incumbent upon them according to Islamic Laws and they will make their livings this way. We have no fight against anyone. We will be busy with ourselves, with ibadah-worship. Who will worship Allah may come here. Who will be a servant to shaytan may go to that side. It is like this. This is what really all mankind needs - not only a piece of land for us as a Muslim canton, and for them a canton according to their desires.

No! In reality this is what the whole world needs: all of them can recover with the Islamic Shariat in 40 days. But their made-up, false laws can not do anything- not in 40 days, but even in 40 years they can't do anything. All of them will collapse. All of them will go bankrupt & all will come to us. They will beg from the Muslims. Now, non-Muslims are all asking help from them - all of them who are with democracy are asking help from unbelievers: "Please, give us money. Please, help us". No! Our helper is Allah (jj) & His Prophet (sas). Don't look for anything beyond this. Whoever likes may come, whoever likes may stay as they are. What will save us is an Islamic State based on religion. This is what we want. Because the governments, states, republics that they have formed could not do anything till now. People went down, not up. Everybody is in debt. What they eat & drink is not clean. Their doings are not good. They do everything (dirty) & Allah Almighty does not let them advance.

We will advance. If not today, tomorrow. If not tomorrow the next day, Islam's sun will rise. And, no cloud that can block this sun will come. This is what we are thinking. If they like, they may come. If they don't like, we didn't like them either. When a wife & husband mutually don't like each other, they get divorced. Just like this, if they don't like us we left them. They may leave us as well. They may leave us & say: "Do if you can manage to". We can! When we bring the order of Islam, the whole world will run to this order. Because salvation is through Islam. Other than this, no order can advance. They may go ahead & try! Go on! Give me 40 days & I may show them, how we will achieve it in 40 days. 40 days is enough. May Allah send us His good, rightminded servants that we may find an opening & recover, find some relief. People coming here are constantly complaining- complaining here, complaining outside. Thanks to Allah, since we lead an Islamic life, we have no complaint. We have enough to eat, drink & wear- which are halal-permissible. We don't eat, drink nor do we wear something that is haram-forbidden. But they are taking, doing everything without looking if it is halal or haram. They may do. One day it will all collapse on their heads. If an earthquake happens, it will destroy them but will not touch our side. May Allah forgive us. We thank you for your visit.

We are relieved, seeing that our youth is slowly awakening. Because our youth could not find what they had hoped in them. Their real face, real direction has been revealed. Now the hearts of the youth are turning towards this side, to Allah's way. They say: "We will not be deceived again". "A true believer is not bitten by a snake twice from the same hole"(Hadith). When he puts his hand into a hole, if there is a scorpion in it & it stings he takes out his hand. Would he do it again for a second time? No! Just like this now, our people here say: when we get the chance would we fall into this trap again? We have been misled. They cheated us. Who cheated us may find their punishment from Allah. And they will. Wa min Allahu tawfiq. O our Lord forgive us. May You send us the ones who will discipline us. We are pleading at Your door, O Our Lord. May You not make us fall to the door of shaytan. Fatiha.
