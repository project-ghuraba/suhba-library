Masha Allah. Don't sleep. Maybe I sleep but don't sleep. We are here on behalf of Mawlana Sheikh Nazim. We are here with Mawlana Sheikh Muhammad, we are here to say to you Assalamu alaikum wa rahmatullahi ta'ala wa barakatuhu. We wanted to be in Durban because Durban is the place of awliya.

You say such a country, why the awliya come here? Must be something for it. And when Mawlana Sheikh come here he was welcoming the awliya to be here. And this mosque, it have this manifestation on these awliya. In every prayer definitely you will have one awliya joining your prayer, definitely. Alhamdulillah, this is big blessing for you. It's big blessing to be here.

We are here, coming for you humbly. We are not asking for power, we are not asking for money, we are not asking for vote also, because you cannot elect us. We are here for Allah Almighty. We are here to give the love of Allah Almighty, to give the love of Rasulullah, and to give the love of awliya. Because without their love we are nothing. We must love, we must love something and Allah Almighty He put the awliya for your love.

Maybe we are loving the dunya too much, it's ok. Maybe we love our business too much, maybe we love money too much, maybe we love our wives, our husbands too much, maybe we love our children too much but this is not real love. This is just temporary love. It's coming and going. This is rule. Everybody knows this. But we have to love the awliya. Love the awliya because the love of awliya will bring you to Allah Almighty, it will bring you to Rasulullah, will bring you for another level.

That level it is not existing in our world. This is beyond. When the Grandsheikh, Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah and Mawlana Sheikh Nazim they were doing tawaf in Makkah, Olden days, and the people they pushing each other, pushing, pushing, pushing. And it was so crowd and it was Hajj-e-Akbar and Mawlana Sheikh he was trying to keep his Sheikh from this pushing. Just he wanted him to not be hurt from this crowd. And he was telling him "Nazim Effendi," and he put his hand in his shoulder and he say, "Look now for these people". And he look and all the people been different kind of animal because this is their egoistic shape. And then he tell him "Nazim Effendi, look upstairs". And he had his head and he see people walking on the people, very elegant and very nicely and full of design and they were one by one making tawaf and this tawaf is going on and on and on and on, you cannot see, so high. This is awliyaullah.

This is another level that people don't know. We want to take these people for that level. He say Allah Almighty He give us this duty to take the people from this egoistic level to the human level. That's awliya level. He say that's why, he was saying 'no need to complain if something happens, say Alhamdulillah shukr, this is maybe Allah Almighty'. He was not weak. Maybe this one will be small for something coming bigger. That when you say 'Shukr Ya Rabbi, Alhamdulillah,' Allah Almighty He make it like this then that manifestation will change and will come for khayr, will come for goodness. This is important to know.

Now I am looking, masha Allah you are all full of nur masha Allah. Alhamdulillah you're all believer in Allah Almighty, believe in Rasulullah and believe in awliyaullah. It's important. In those days there's other people I don't want to mention their name but they're not believing in awliya, not believing in Rasulullah, they say only Quran is more than enough for everything. We say Quran, Quran is here but nobody can explain Quran except awliyaullah. They are the one knowing how to explain.

Grandsheikh he was saying "The Quran it is so great, one letter of Quran it's have 24,000 meaning. So how you can understand? Don't look for Quran, small book, it's not book, it is Umm ul-Kitab, it's the Mother of Everything. Every knowledge is coming from Quran, Alhamdulillah. That's why the Murshid is very important in our life. If we are having murshid, we will be riding plane, no need to fly the plane. Because we don't know how to fly the plane. And we don't need the fahrist (index) to see how to fly the plane.

If they give you the book how to fly the plane, are you able to make this plane to fly? No. If you are saying you have the 'Book of Doctor' and you look and make operation, are you able to make operation? This is the job of murshid, he is the specialist. You say "This doctor is a specialist, we trust him, we give him our self. The murshid is like this. The murshid is very important in our life. When you find the true murshid this is heavenly gift.

We are asking Ya Rabbi, don't take this Murshid from our life. Make the Murshid in our life, in every part of our life let's have a murshid. And we are insha Allah trying to have a Murshid for us that he guide us for good way, and he guide us for Rasulullah, and guide us for Allah Almighty. May Allah bless you, may Allah give wealth, health and happiness. And, to be with Murshid all your life and to feel comfortable in your life, because the Murshid is gift from Allah Almighty, for his ummah to make them very comfortable and to be confidence with Allah almighty, with Rasulullah. wa min Allahi tawfiq bi hurmati l-Habib bi hurmati l-Fatiha.
