As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

There is a holy saying. I don't know exactly if it is ḥadīth or a courteous saying of pious people. Because we must mention from whom ḥadīth sharīf is narrated. "Kullun muyassarun limā khuliqa lah". It is a holy saying: "There is wisdom in everything Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla creates." And everything has its duty accordingly. They must carry out and complete their duties. Certainly, that duty is by the will of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Therefore, most saints don't ask about its wisdom - "How is this? Why does it happen this way? Is it good? Or is it bad?" They know it is from AllāhNone and try to look at it from the perspective of wisdom. If they don't know the wisdom, they say, "AllāhNone knows its wisdom," and don't interfere.

The current situation in the world is that there is evil everywhere. There are many people who are not good. And more than not good, there are bad people. Everything is created with wisdom, as we said. The wisdom is near AllāhNone. Both the good and the bad are given as a test. There are some things that people don't approve, because they are the things done against AllāhNone. When badness is done towards people of faith, against Muslims, or against any person or animal, Muslims don't approve that, of course. They don't want that. But there is wisdom in some things for the creatures. And there are things without wisdom. AllāhNone knows all the wisdom of that. We can't interfere in it. It is the condition of this world. You must accept good things. And you may change what is not good if you are capable of it. If you are not, you must at least say in your heart that you don't accept it.

This is important for common people and for people of ṭarīqah. It is important for people who say they are Muslim. Because if they know this, they will be from the accepted people near AllāhNone. If they don't know and reject this, if they object by saying "What is this?" they will be at loss then. They will lose their reward, because there is a lot of bad things in this world for people. Speaking about them without knowing their wisdom is sometimes good and sometimes not good. Most of the time it is not good. Therefore, what mankind sees as good is sometimes bad. And what they see as bad is good. That is also the wisdom of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. AllāhNone knows the wisdom. People who are believers must strive to either abstain from doing that, or stay away from these things, this fitnah, this worldly situation and badness. They must not be involved with them. As Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim used to say, in the end of times people who stay at home are safe, in shā’a Llāh. May AllāhNone protect us. Wisdom, as we said, there are various things, you must not say why AllāhNone created this or that. AllāhNone created with wisdom. You can't interfere in the business of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Everything has its balance. Everything must happen. Some things must happen. The bad must happen. And the good must happen. At some times, the bad happens more. It will be over and then goodness will come. This is the balance of AllāhNone. If you don't interfere in it, that is better for you. As we said, regarding most things people think the bad is good without realizing, and see the good as bad.

May AllāhNone protect us. May AllāhNone change our situation for the better, in shā’a Llāh.

There is a saying, I don't know if it is Ḥadīth, saying of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, or from awliyā’ or scholars. They are saying: "Kullun muyassarun limā khuliqa lah." Everything Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, they create everything and everything they have some role, something to do. When they're in exist[ence], AllāhNone make them to exist, to appear. Also they gave them something to do, something they must do. And of course, will of AllāhNone will be the guiding this people, this thing – not only people, even animal, even insect, even rock, wheat. Anything it is with wisdom AllāhNone create. And this, they give them way to go and they are going this way. This is from AllāhNone.

Our believer must be knowing this. Everything [is] from AllāhNone and AllāhNone create this. HeNone create good. HeNone create bad. HeNone create ugly. HeNone create everything. AllāhNone create them and give them this what they will do and they are doing what AllāhNone put them in this shape, in this place. They will be making this role what Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, creating. They are suitable to do this thing and easy for them – muyassar meaning, it is easy for this thing or that human being or that animal or other. Everything it is easy for them to do it. Like sheep cannot eat meat. AllāhNone create them only to eat wheat or barley or other thing because if they will eat meat they cannot chew it. But for wolf or dog or cat, they're easily they ate meat. Sometimes maybe they eat grass for benefit for them but not whole time. Maybe some little bit to cure themself. But normally AllāhNone make them, easy for them to eat meat.

For human being also. Some people they are easy for them to be in way of AllāhNone. But many of them, not easy for them. They are... You can maybe fifteen hours running from here to here but they cannot do two raka‘āt praying because this is not for them. AllāhNone create them only to be looking after dunyā, running after their desire, after this, to torturing people, to kill people, to do this, do that, that. But they're easy for them to do that. But for to pray two raka‘āt it is, cannot. It is, you cannot imagine how it is difficult. They cannot do this because AllāhNone HeNone...

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, for who praying, we must be thankful for AllāhNone because HeNone make it easy for us. For other very difficult. This is wisdom from AllāhNone. HeNone know best for us. We must be careful for everything. You not object – "What is this? What happening, what?" All this with will of AllāhNone and this will be as we are born baby, after become big, and after we pass away. This world also same thing. All this must be happen and we are not objecting. Just we are surrendering for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. What HeNone is giving us, we are happy and we must be thankful for HimNone for make us to be in HisNone way, way of... and His Prophet, ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, way. This is very big luck for us. We are very lucky to be in this way because HeNone make it easy, make it not difficult.

Of course, there is many thing in this world. Even believer they said: "What is this insect? It is dirty, other it is this, not good." All this – dirty, good, poison, or making problem for people – also from... not coming from... not where... AllāhNone create them. So we are not... Don't make too much objecting for this, for that. When you do this, you become used to be angry for everything. You be careful, be calm. This also from AllāhNone and AllāhNone when HeNone want, HeNone take them. Because everything... We don't know everything. Who know everything? Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. We must be just relax and accept what HeNone gave us, in shā’a Llāh.

AllāhNone protect us from bad thing, to not be object anything for from what HeNone create, in shā’a Llāh. Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
