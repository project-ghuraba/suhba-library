As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Rijālun lā tulhīhim tijāratun wa-lā bay‘un ‘an dhikri Llāhi wa-iqāmi ṣ-ṣalāh" (24:37). There are people whom, neither the business of this world, nor anything at all, can distract them from the dhikr of AllāhNone; from the love of AllāhNone. When people engage in trade, while thinking about what to do and how to do it, certainly most of them forget. Neither dhikr nor praying crosses their mind. It slips their mind. They only remember their business and position in this world. Most people are like this.

But a few people are beloved ones of AllāhNone. They don't care about these matters, the matters of dunyā. They say that even if the world collapsed, they wouldn't care about it. Why? Because this world will come to an end one day. It is important to them to remember Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, who has no end. Their goal is to lie down and to get up with HimNone; to be with HimNone every moment. This is their purpose and what they gain. People are running after dunyā. While chasing dunyā, if they can at least remember AllāhNone, it will benefit them. But people of the present time don't remember at all. They don't even believe, let alone remember. May AllahNone prevent that from happening. They bear the responsibility of that.

Our job is to be with AllāhNone. Trading, dunyā, shopping and money; don't put them first. Don't put them in your heart. Put Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla in your heart. AllāhNone loves such people. People like that are loved by HimNone. Do you love someone who doesn't remember you? You do not. It is Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla who has given us everything we have; every blessing is from HimNone. We should remember HimNone. Other things should not prevent you from worshipping and obeying HimNone. Be with AllāhNone, in shā’a Llāh.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla say in Qur’ān, Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Rijālun lā tulhīhim tijāratun wa-lā bay‘un ‘an dhikri Llāhi wa-iqāmi ṣ-ṣalāti wa-ītā’i z-zakāh" (24:37). There is people, nothing important for them – like business, or work, or job, or trading – make them to forget Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, and to forget to pray and to do what AllāhNone order. Because most of people, they are in... busy, they are busy and they are forget Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Only their focus on business and money, and woman, land, job. What they will do next year, what they will be for holiday, for other thing. This is for people, they they put this first. And after, many of them not remembering AllāhNone or to do worshipping. Only what they remember – what they will do, how it will be their business, their money. This is what people, most of people in world these days, this thinking.

But AllāhNone like other kind, who they, nothing can make them to forget AllāhNone and to forget worshipping. First number in their life, to love Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla and obey HisNone – After, other thing not important at all. If whole world burning, they are not care. They don't care for this because dunyā, world, it will be finished. Here, here this and that but there are important thing; this is not important. Important, to be with AllāhNone. If you are with AllāhNone, you must be lucky. Because not many people can do this. Very, very few people they are like this: that only what they care, how to love AllāhNone, how to love ProphetNone and to obey their order. And this is people lucky, and clever also.

Because when people thinking about this life, it is only short, and it is like theatre or like playing. From this time to that time, and after, finish. Other one coming. From this time to that time, finish. Who if you have all business, work, trading for you, what benefit? No benefit. Benefit with other – who put, first thing in his life: love of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, and to not forget HimNone at all. And to obey HisNone order. Other one, first thing for them: to how to enjoy themselves and their ego. And how to make this holiday, or to make this business, next year, summer; what we'll do with studying, with business, how – which university we must send our children, what school they will be, which country we will go. This is only they thinking, and they thought they are doing good.

Especially nowadays, they become worse. They become unbeliever also. Not accepting Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, how to remember HimNone. This is people, unfortunately, they are unlucky. Lucky one who is with Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, and he remember HimNone. AllāhNone make us all time like this. AllāhNone making strong īmān, in shā’a Llāh, for all of us, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
