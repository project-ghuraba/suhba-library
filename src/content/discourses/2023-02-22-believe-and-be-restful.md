As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Lā ‘āṣima l-yawma min amri Llāhi illā man raḥim" (11:43). Only Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saves whomever HeNone wants. AllāhNone shows mercy and saves whomever HeNone wants to show mercy to, says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.

The time we are living in, which we always say is difficult, is the end of times. Mankind is living in a difficult time. But what can he do? There is AllāhNone. AllāhNone created us in such a time. HeNone created us in this place, in this time. There is nothing we can do. People should believe in this so that they feel at rest. There is wisdom in everything that AllāhNone does. There are things that HeNone knows and that people don't know.

The things that Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gave to people, HisNone favours, are endless. And the greatest favour is the favour of īmān, faith. A person who attains the favour of īmān – the rest, like disasters and calamities, are as nothing for him. They will be written as thawāb and profit for him.

With the permission of AllāhNone, we entered the month of Sha‘bān. Among the three holy months, Sha‘bān is the month of our Holy ProphetNone. It is the month of mercy. They are scaring people now by saying this and that will happen. In shā’a Llāh, for the honour of our Holy ProphetNone, all possible hardships will be easy and without harm, in shā’a Llāh. What we should do in this month, in shā’a Llāh, is to honour our Holy ProphetNone, to make ṣalawāt to himNone and to ask forgiveness from AllāhNone. What we should do is to perform the worship ordered by AllāhNone and to trust in AllāhNone. Make du‘ā’ that HeNone shows mercy and compassion to us for the honour of our Holy Prophet'sNone face. Du‘ā’ is the weapon of the believer. With it, if AllāhNone wants, HeNone can take away disaster.

However, the most important thing is that now people are scared. They are scared of what will happen. There is nothing to be scared of. The Will of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla will happen. Nothing outside HisNone Will ever happens. Therefore, you must submit yourself to HisNone Will and pray that HeNone protects us. With the permission of AllāhNone, for the honour of our Holy Prophet'sNone face, AllāhNone will show mercy and we will be safe, in shā’a Llāh. These people are very anxious. And the remedy for it is to trust in AllāhNone. May AllāhNone help all of us. There is no one in whom to seek refuge except AllāhNone. "Lā ‘āṣima l-yawma min amri Llāhi illā man raḥim" (11:43). Whoever seeks refuge in AllāhNone will be saved. May AllāhNone help us.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying, "Lā ‘āṣima l-yawma min amri Llāhi illā man raḥim" (11:43). AllāhNone, only who want, HeNone can save him. You cannot save yourself. But Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone save you, if HeNone want, you will be safe. And, AllāhNone – everything happening here by HisNone Will, by HisNone Wisdom. Everything happening in this universe, all which we seeing or not seeing – everything, nothing can do [it] by himself. This is our belief, Ahlu s-Sunnah wa-l-Jamā‘ah belief. So, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla with wisdom everything happen. Don't be not accept this. You must accept. If you accept, you will be restful. If not accept, you will be afraid: you don't know what you do, where you go, where you, to whom listening, to who saying right, who saying not lying. You will be like toy between this people who making, liking to disturb people.

There are people they are like shayṭān also, disturbing people. They make them to forget we are servant of AllāhNone and HeNone do what HeNone want to do. But, if you accept, you be reward. But if not accept, you be no reward and you be not restful. To peace in your heart coming with belief for AllāhNone Wisdom, surround [surrender] for AllāhNone, HeNone do what HeNone like. We are HisNone servants, we cannot do anything against HisNone Will. We are under HisNone Order. This is what we must do and we accept.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, also, we are, now we come to month of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, Sha‘bān. This month, in shā’a Llāh, it will be mercy for ummah and be, in shā’a Llāh, nothing harm more people, in shā’a Llāh. Because now, making people to afraid, many people very afraid, "it will be like this, it will be that." Only AllāhNone know what it will be. And when HeNone want, it will be. So, for this, you must make du‘ā' and praying for AllāhNone, asking for forgiveness, and make ṣalawāt for Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam by hisNone mercy, AllāhNone, in shā’a Llāh, be mercy for us and we are safe, in shā’a Llāh, from what they are saying, what they are telling people. In shā’a Llāh, we will safe with Will of AllāhNone. But, for we must be believe in HimNone, this is first thing. Who believer, they are rest. Who not believer, they are anxious all time. AllāhNone help us and make our belief more stronger. As much more stronger, you will be more safe, more restful, more happy, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
