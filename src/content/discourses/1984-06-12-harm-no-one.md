Sheikh Muhammad Nazim Al-Haqqani An-Naqshibandi, Sohbat of the 12th of June, 1984.

Which thing makes a person, no value for him? We are valuable; sons of Adam, all of them valuable, too much valuable. Everyone is more valuable from whole universe, no including men. But something making us no value. What is that? We must know it. Now I am translating from our headquarters, from main central, spiritual central, from Grandsheikh. We must know this so that we may leave it. There is something makes us more valuable. But some others making us to be no value. Which makes us to be no value for us? Taking away from value?

Anyone who not respecting others, he is losing his value. Who harming others losing his value, by your speaking or by your hand. If you are harming anyone, even one, he is going to lose his value. Shaitan, he was worshipper - no one worshipping like him in his time. And he was most learned one among angels. But he harmed one, among who... and he fell down. No value for him now. He harmed Adam alaihi salam, peace be upon him. No respecting. Whole universe giving respect to Adam, peace be upon him, only shaitan not giving his respect and harming Adam alaihi salam. And he fell down. No value now for him from that time.

Who harming a person, even by look or by saying, or by his hand, he is on the same level of shaitan, and losing his value. That is an important point that we must know it and we must practice it also. It is not enough to know only. But most important, it is practicing. Don't give trouble to anyone. Don't harm anyone. Then you are Muslim. You are valuable servant. You are beloved one in Divine Presence.

Allah Almighty trying His servants. No one is going to be without trying. Everyone examined, or should be examined during his life and everyday. Everyday you may be examined from our Lord. And He is looking to our attributes with His servants. Who gives harm to others, then Allah Almighty leaving him and heavenly beings, angels, also leaving him. If Allah Almighty leaving a person, falling on him every suffering and troubles, falling on him, raining on him. He never going to be in peace or happiness in his life. And Allah Almighty, our Lord asking for us to be in a happy life and peaceful life. But we are changing peaceful life into unpeaceful and into a suffering full of troubles. We are doing that.

If we are listening, Adam, our grandfather, the first man, he was advising to his sons and ordering them to advise their sons. And every prophet repeated it, Adam's advises, to their nations. And his advises also coming, reaching to our time also. He was advising to his sons "O my sons, look and learn from my example. Everything and every happiness, it was for me. But I lost it because I left my Lord's pleasure. I made Him to be angry with me. And I lost all happiness and I take all sufferings in this life. You must take an example from me. Don't do your Lord angry with you. If you are making your Lord angry with you, you are losing every happiness and peace, and taking every suffering during your life."

Therefore, all prophets, they are advising to their nations - "Don't do any action that makes your Lord angry with you." It costs you too much, too expensive. Because Allah Almighty asking for you a peaceful and happiness life. Keep it. But if you are making angry your Lord with you, you are losing everything. Now in our time we are not asking our Lord's pleasure or anger. We are saying "I don't mind (if) He is pleased with me or angry." Because mostly they are not accepting to be in existence a God looking after them. They are denying to be a God and Creator to be in existence. That making Allah Almighty to be angry with them. From every sin that is the biggest one they are saying "No God". That is bringing a huge, a very terrible, terrible punishment coming on men now because they are saying "No God". That carrying a terrible punishment for all nations. www.saltanat.org Page 1 Only whom that Allah Almighty sheltering them under His divinely shelter, who entering may be in safety, and others going to be punished. Allah Almighty, because He sent His last Prophet to be mercy for all nations, when they are refusing him, then Allah Almighty making people to be into two camps, and making among themselves enmity so that one side will fight second one, second one will fight first one. And from one to others, from others to others, giving His punishment. Now whole world working only for that point. They are preparing everything for that punishment. Allah Almighty should give punishment east from west and to west from east. No one can escape. No way for people except to go under divinely shelter. But who is saying "No God", they are not sheltered. Their Lord is angry with them. Who may be with his Lord pleased, and his Lord pleased with him, should be sheltered.

Therefore, my Grandsheikh was saying - "His terrible punishment soonerly coming on earth, approaching." Awliyas looking as you are looking when black clouds appearing, you know that rain coming. They are looking that divine punishment soonerly coming on whole earth, covering. And Grandsheikh was saying "Who should be under divinely shelter, who accepting his Lord, saying that there is in existence a Lord, the Lord of universes, and (they say) I accept Him and I am His servant". Who saying this must be sheltered. Who says "O my Lord, forgive me and I am Your servant", he must be sheltered. And secondly, who never intending, more than (much less) harming, not harming and never intending in his heart any bad intention to anyone from people.

If anyone keeping a bad intention in his heart to anyone, that intention should shoot him. As a bullet should shoot him. Who thinking evil and badness for anyone, that coming around and shooting him. If he may take a shelter under seven underground, that bullet coming around and shooting him. Therefore, who is going to be brave for badness, intending for anyone badness and evil, even that intention should take him away from this life. Because this is going to be greatest war and the last war on earth taking so many people. You can't imagine. From seven taking six, leaving one. Divinely anger.
