A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah. Wherever the family and exalted ṣaḥābah of our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam go, light appears there. Its barakah increases. Its light increases. Shukr to Allāh, our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam called this place, the island of Cyprus, Jazīratu l-Khaḍrā’, the Green Island. A group of ṣaḥābah set out to conquer it, and our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam said, “This is how I saw it in my dream.” Our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam was lying in the lap of Hala Sultan. Hala Sultan asked, “Am I among them?” “Yes, you are among them.” Again, our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam dozed off. Again he saw it, again he woke up smiling and laughing, and once again he said that the ṣaḥābah had mounted upon thrones to conquer Jazīratu l-Khaḍrā’. Again she asked, “Am I among them?” This time, the Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam said nothing. She understood that she would be with those who went first. Hala Sultan went there at the age of eighty, not on foot, but riding an animal. I think it was a horse. The horse stumbled, and Hala Sultan fell and became a martyr. Martyrdom is a great rank. It is the rank that all ṣaḥābah wanted. Martyrdom is not a bad thing. Sayyidunā Ḥamzah was a martyr. Sayyidunā Ḥusayn was a martyr. Many from Ahlu l-Bayt are martyrs. Their ranks are rising. They are already higher, and in addition, the rank of martyrdom was granted to them. Therefore, for martyrs, even here, when their children become martyrs, people do not wish to weep. They say, “Our son has become a martyr for the pleasure of Allāh.” Therefore, martyrdom is a high rank. It is not good to see it as bad. What is bad is like what Sayyidunā Khālid said, “There is no inch of my body that has not had a wound from a sword, or a dagger, or an arrow, or a spear. There is no place on my body without a wound.” And that is no small matter. He was saying, “But I am dying here on a mat like an animal, dying without martyrdom.” Sayyidunā Khālid, may Allāh be pleased with him, was deeply sorrowful. Therefore, Muslims must have wisdom. He must look to his shaykh, his murshid – what he says, what he does, what the situation is. He must look to what is good for us. He must obey him and attain that virtue. This is the virtue to be attained. One must know that whatever Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla bestows upon someone is a great blessing for them. May Allāh exalt their ranks. The leader of the martyrs, Sayyidunā Ḥusayn ‘alayhi s-salām, and his companions – they drank martyrdom like a sweet drink. Many others died lying in their beds. There are many who did not reach this rank. May Allāh grant us all their virtue, in shā’a Llāh. May Allāh exalt their ranks, in shā’a Llāh. Today is ‘Āshūrā’ – a blessed day. Now, after the ṣuḥbah, in shā’a Llāh, there is a four-raka‘āt ‘Āshūrā’ prayer. We will pray it together, in shā’a Llāh. After that, those able should recite Sūratu l-Ikhlāṣ one thousand times – it will be written in their book. As we said, take a shower. Those who cannot shower can use the sea. The weather is hot anyway. Subḥāna Llāh, these days are like the day of Sayyidunā Ḥusayn’s passing. It is very hot. May Allāh accept all our worship, obedience, and good deeds, in shā’a Llāh. May Allāh grant victory to Islām through their barakah. By Allāh’s permission, the whole world will attain safety through Islām. Through Islām, the world will find peace. It cannot find it by any other means. It will not find it through the tools of shayṭān. There will be neither peace, nor goodness, nor beauty. With Islām, everything finds peace. All beauty is in Islām. May Allāh grant it to everyone. May Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām come soon. When Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām comes, he says there are secrets – many secrets will be revealed. When people learn these secrets, they will see how right Ahlu s-Sunnah and the people of ṭarīqah are. Everyone will see this, in shā’a Llāh. Certainly, as we said at the beginning, there is Hala Sultan; her miracles, her spiritual grace and barakah are continuous. Her influence is also continuous. They do not lie there dead. Whatever is needed – according to one’s needs, and for those who ask – they give. If one does not ask, they do not give. Therefore, may Allāh let their himmah be upon us. May their barakah be upon us. May their protection be upon us, in shā’a Llāh. May Allāh bless these holy days for all of us, in shā’a Llāh.
