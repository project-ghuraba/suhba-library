Vesselatu Vesselamu ala Resulina Muhammedin Seyyidul evvelin vel ahirin.

Medet Ya Resulullah, Medet ya Sadat-ı Ashab-ı Resulullah, Medet ya Meşayihina,

Şeyh Abdullah Dağıstani, Şeyh Muhammed Nazım el Hakkani, destur.

Times it does not rain, rain is demanded in the honour of the Holy Prophet. Then it rains. As it was one of the holy prophet’s miracles, at the time he was a young child, there was a draught during which time he was at his Foster mother’s place. When it was asked in the honour of this child it rained lot. Later in many other places, in Macca too, Allah Azza wa Jalla caused the rain for the sake of the Holy Prophet. This today is a sign also insha’Allah this month, month of the Prophet, the other night was the beginning of Shaban, we entered this month with Takbeer and salawat recitations. With these resitations protection was asked for. For the sake of the Prophet with Allah’s will protection will be provided. This is from the miracles of the Prophet. There are miracles which continue till the day of resurrection. There were miracles happened during his time and miracles to happen after him too. İnshaallah these calamities will be dismissed for his sake. It was dreary but now it is removed. Allah Azza wa Jalla removed it for the sake of the Holy Prophet inshaallah. He says “Give glad tidings”. İnshaallah this will be a glad tiding. İnshaallah it will be a cause for people to acknowledge the greatness of the Holy prophet. There are a lot of confused Muslims. Number of people who do not venerate the holy prophet is great. Lately a lot of them say he is just a human being like us. Therefore let this be a lesson to all of them so that they realise the Holy prophet’s greatness. Let them know how valuable he is in yhe sight of Allah. İnshaallah shortly soon this calamity will be dismissed. It will not be waisted. This will be a lesson for people. People (Muslims) do not respect the holy Prophet as they should. They do not remember him. This shall be a lesson for them all, let it be beneficial. Allah attached wisdom and goodness in everything. there is good in bad. Therefore no need to worry oneself. May Allah protect us all. May Allah increase our love for the holy Prophet. May it be a lot may our faith increase. Ve Min Allahu Tevfik El Fatiha
