As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
إِنَّهُ بِكُلِّ شَيْءٍ بَصِيرٌ
<br>“Innahu bi-kulli shay'im baṣīr”<br>
“Verily, He is the All-Seer of everything.” (Qur’ān 67:19). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla sees everything; nothing is hidden from Him. When a believer, a Muslim, knows this, all his affairs go well, and he stays away from bad things. He always strives to do what Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla loves.

Of course, a person will do as much as he can. There is the ego, the shayṭān, desires; there are many things that try to prevent mankind. No one can achieve this 100%, but a person must control his ego as much as he can. Because Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla sees everything. But still, we are weak servants; we make mistakes and commit sins.

Repenting and seeking forgiveness is for our benefit so that Allah may forgive us. Because Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla transforms the sins and mistakes committed by the person who repents and seeks forgiveness into good deeds. As for those who do not repent, that is their own business.

Now, people have followed their egos; they consider themselves very great, they do whatever they want, and moreover, they defy Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. They think highly of themselves. However, in reality, they are only harming themselves, not doing themselves any good.

A person who acts arrogantly before Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla is actually humiliating themselves; because they have become a slave to their own ego, a mount for their ego. Their ego is riding them, making them run; and they think of themselves as something important. May Allah protect us from the state of people in this time. May Allah improve them.

But of course, since it is the end of times, shayṭāns and evils are deceiving people, pushing them towards evil things. May Allah protect us from the evil of our ego.

As we said, even if it’s a small thing, resisting the ego is always beneficial for us. As you resist it, Allah will gradually give you strength, and your spiritual strength will increase. By resisting your ego and not following it, you will become stronger both spiritually and outwardly, with the permission of Allah. May Allah protect us from our ego.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
وَمَن جَاهَدَ فَإِنَّمَا يُجَاهِدُ لِنَفْسِهِ
<br>“Wa-man jāhada fa-innamā yujāhidu li-nafsih”<br>
“And whoever strives only strives for [the benefit of] himself.” (Qur’ān 29:06).

Jihād means opposing the ego. We will wage jihād as our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam said: The greatest jihād is the jihād you wage against your ego, the war against your ego. If you do this, you will already be considered a mujāhid in the presence of Allah and you will earn its reward.

May Allah help us, and may we overcome our egos, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
