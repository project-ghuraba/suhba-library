As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Inna d-dīna ‘inda Llāhi l-Islām" (3:19). Religion, in the presence of AllāhNone, is the religion of Islām. All prophets were sent to people with this religion. This is what was the basis of religion in their times: to worship AllāhNone, to be on the way of AllāhNone, to order goodness and to prevent from evil. This is the way of the religion, the base of it. But there are specific details. Every prophet has a different sharī‘ah, which is law – Islamic law, or law made for people on how to live, from the perspective of the religion and social order. Sharī‘ah in this case means law. It was different. Every place changed, from ummah to ummah throughout time, until our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam came. Our Holy ProphetNone said in hisNone last khuṭbah, the farewell khuṭbah on ‘Arafāt, "Our religion is complete today." The religion of humanity is complete. There is no more change, because there is no one after our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. People will follow this religion and sharī‘ah until Judgement Day.

Other religions and other sharī‘ahs were slightly different. Many of them. We went some place on our last trip to Europe. Monks had their rooms there, where it was forbidden to talk. They can come out to another room and talk for an hour. We went to another place. There was also a monastery for monks. There was a group of people with children who were talking. A priest came at one point. He didn't say much. Then we understood they had two hours of silence as worship. Our people made some noise, of course. But the man was with adab. He didn't say anything but then he gave a sign. Sometimes they have things like this in their sharī‘ah.

Sayyidunā Zakarīyā ‘alayhi s-salām was [ordered] by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla to give a sign to his people and say, "I won't speak, by the order of AllāhNone. You keep on worshipping." But all of these were removed. They were removed which means this worship is not in Islām – to be silent, not to speak as worship. You keep quiet in khalwah, seclusion, and make your dhikr of AllāhNone. You speak only when there is need and avoid worldly conversation. But such order is lifted for Muslims. There is not such order anymore. The order is to follow the way of our Holy ProphetNone. In the time of our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, three men came. One said, "I will never sleep. I will worship all the time and not sleep." Another one said, "I will always fast. I will not break my fast." The third said, "I will not get married and have a family." When our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam heard this, heNone called them and told them, "INone sleep and INone get up. INone fast, and there are times when INone don't fast. And INone get married. Who follow my sunnah are from my people." Those who don't comply with the sunnah are not accepted.

Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam is showing this as a lesson to the Ummah, that when people do some things as they like, thinking it is good, they don't do goodness. They satisfy their egos and think, "I did this. Even ProphetNone didn't do this." Astaghfiru Llāha l-‘Aẓīm. Therefore, instead of gaining a big reward, they commit a sin. May AllāhNone protect us.

Our religion is easy. It is for all of humanity. We are upon this religion until Judgement Day. And there are various details, there is ijtihād. There are madhhabs (schools of thought). People nowadays don't even accept ḥadīth. They say you must check Qur’ān. You must do how it is written in Qur’ān. You don't understand when you read a newspaper. How can you understand Qur’ān? That is another plot of shayṭān. May AllāhNone protect us.

Therefore, you must follow madhhab and ṭarīqah, and do as much as you can. But sharī‘ah is sharī‘ah. We cannot deny it. If you can't do something... But, may AllāhNone protect us, if you deny sharī‘ah, then you make a big mistake and sin. May AllāhNone not separate any of us from the right way, in shā’a Llāh.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Inna d-dīna ‘inda Llāhi l-Islām" (3:19). The religion, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla religion, one. From Ādam ‘alayhi s-salām, until Yawmu l-Qiyāmah, whole prophets, they are in Islām. Religion, Islām. "Inna d-dīna ‘inda Llāhi l-Islām" (3:19). Religion, it is Islām. No Christianity, no Judaism, no... other thing. Many prophets, they coming, whole prophets they were Muslim, in Islām. "Aslamtu wajhiya li-Llāh" (3:20). This, all prophets, in Qur’ān mentioning, they are Muslim. But they are, of course, this religion coming from Ādam ‘alayhi s-salām, many thousands of years. Every time for this people or human, there was law – sharī‘ah, we call it. Law, meaning: for life of people must follow law. Law of Islām for religion, and for your normal life also. Everything, there is rule and who follow this rule, he will be safe at the end. Who not follow, it is not, he is not safe. But who... this rule was changing. Not the main pillar Islām not changing at all. But the rule, it was changing from prophet to prophet, from place to place.

They was changing until when Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, before heNone is changing this world to next world, [before] four months heNone was in ḥajj, ḥajju l-akbar. HeNone was make khuṭbah. In this khuṭbah, heNone said, "Al-yawma akmaltu lakum dīnakum wa-atmamtu ‘alaykum ni‘matī" (5:3). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘aẓīm. "Today, I complete for you the religion." So it was from Ādam ‘alayhi s-salām, until ProphetNone, it was coming by – slowly slowly, and with Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, this was completed. And it is no changing from this until Yawmu l-Qiyāmah. We must follow this as much we can do. But not... main, main thing, you cannot change, main thing. Islām religion, okay. But for sharī‘ah there was many thing in old law, sharī‘ah of Mūsá ‘alayhi s-salām or ‘Īsá ‘alayhi s-salām or even until now they are, some of them following this law or way.

So this, we was in Europe before twenty days and we visiting one monastery. There was one good guy. He welcomed us and he explain how they was doing, what are they doing in this monastery. There was room for who priest there. But he said something, it was strange for us. He said this: they was no, not permission to speak. Silent, only silent in this. Twenty three hours, silent. And there was big room in this area. They said, one hour they was coming here. Only allowed in this room to speak. Not to, you cannot speak other way, at other time or other place, in this. And they was living – not one month, two month, one year – whole life they was living this. Maybe fifty, sixty or eighty or hundred years, they was not speaking. Only once a day.

And we went after this, also this monastery, another one. There was also there, other side. Like island. And we go with our people. They have children, they have something to eat and so was, they was speaking loud. And after, one priest coming for us, welcoming us. He was polite, but after, we understand, there was two hours for them, silent time. He didn't say. But our people, they was with children, other, laughing, making joke. So, he didn't. But it is still continue like this in their sharī‘ah.

And it also in Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla describe Sayyidunā Zakarīyā. When AllāhNone, HeNone gave good tiding for his, to be, "You will be have son." He was happy and from his happiness AllāhNone make him inspiration for to make three days, not speaking. Only give sign for people, "I am, three days I will not speak." But in our sharī‘ah, this no more. Many thing like this, in old sharī‘ah, they was doing. We are not responsible, and not for us now. Now the complete one, what Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, doing.

And there was three men in Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam time. And they was speaking to each other. Going to mosque, they said, one said, "I will not sleep at all. I will, whole, will make, worshipping, ‘ibādah, whole time. No sleeping for me." Other one, he said, "I will be fast whole time. I will not break fast at all." Other one, he said, "I will be never married. I will be making ‘ibādah. Not looking for family, for wife or something." When Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam hearing this, heNone said, "INone am sleeping and wake up and make our, my daily worshipping, ‘ibādah and looking for people. And INone am fasting and also break fast," also this. And for marriage also: "INone am also marrying and not be without marrying. Who follow meNone, he must follow my sunnah."

And this is sunnah of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, showing us the easy way for life for whole people. Can marry, can sleep, can eat, and do anything with permission, in border of what allowed for, in sharī‘ah, Islām. Other thing, you cannot do. Many of people now they are trying, saying, we are following sharī‘ah. We must look for Qur’ān. No, nothing else of Qur’ān. But Qur’ān without ḥadīth, we cannot understand anything. So for this, we are ordering to follow madhhab. Because madhhab giving you detail for life. As much you can do, you can do. But main thing, the belief of Ahlu s-Sunnah wa-l-Jamā‘ah, this is important. You cannot change this. And you must accept sharī‘ah. Don't say, "I like this, I don't like this." You must be accept this. As much you can do, you can do. Don't... Say, "Oh I am accept this, but I am, only can do little bit. AllāhNone, we asking from AllāhNone forgiveness." Because we are, we know it is, we cannot follow whole. AllāhNone, HeNone is most forgiveness. HeNone will forgive us. We must do this. When you do this, you are accept and you are not proud. "No, I don't like this, I don't want this" – this is make you twice to be punished. Once for you are saying this, and once for because you again, coming against AllāhNone saying. But if you say, "Okay, all this we can do but we are sinner, we are not strong people. Only we can this do. And we believe AllāhNone, HeNone will forgive us." AllāhNone forgive you, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone help us to follow what HeNone is order, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
