As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
إِنَّ عِدَّةَ الشُّهُورِ عِندَ اللَّهِ اثْنَا عَشَرَ شَهْرًا فِي كِتَابِ اللَّهِ يَوْمَ خَلَقَ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضَ
<br>“Inna ʿiddata sh-shuhūri ʿindAllāhi thnā ʿashara shahran fī kitābillāhi yawma khalaqa s-samāwāti wa l-arḍ”<br>
“Indeed, the number of months with Allah is twelve [lunar] months in the register of Allah [from] the day He created the heavens and the earth.” (Qurʼān 9:36). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

In the sight of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla, the year consists of twelve months. Everything that Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla creates comes into being through the command, “كن فيكون”, “Kun fa-yakūn”, “Be, and it is.” When He says “Be,” it is; when He says, “Do not be,” it ceases; as it is said in the [Turkish] Mawlid, “Everything ends.” As Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla has decreed, the secrets of the entire universe are in Qur’ān ʿAẓīmu sh-Sha’n; everything is contained there, in Qur’ān ʿAẓīmu sh-Sha’n.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla has determined the year as twelve months and created everything based on calculation. Nothing happens according to its own will. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla has made it accordingly. Each of these months has different manifestations.

Shukr to Allah, we have entered the month of Ṣafar. They say the month of Ṣafar is “a heavy month,” so they refer to it as “Ṣafar ul-Khayr” for it to be of goodness. May it bring goodness, and may evil stay away from us, inshā’Allāh.

Of course, nowadays people are unaware of anything in this world. They don’t know what is and what’s not; they only claim to have an understanding of education.

In the past, children were only educated for a few years, that was all. Now, they say, “They will go to university, they will study this, they will study that,” but they don’t benefit from anything they study. They only know what is shown to them; they either don’t accept or don’t know anything beyond that.

Yet these months are very important; people must know this. They must know how Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla created man, and how man should be. They need to know which months are blessed and which are a little more difficult.

Because everything has a wisdom, and every month has a duty. This month is a bit more heavy, so there are different duties that need to be fulfilled. These must be done, and Allah will protect us, turning them into good; with Allah’s permission, evil will be transformed into good.

This month’s duty is written down; what we say is written and shared everywhere. Certainly, the most important is sadaqah.

Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam placed great importance on sadaqah. He even said, “Give sadaqah, even if it is half a date, and protect yourselves from the Fire.” In that time, people, even our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, could survive for two days without eating anything, surviving on just one date. Now, half a date is not enough for anyone; even half a kilo wouldn’t be enough for people today.

Therefore, no matter how small the amount, be sure to give sadaqah. Don’t consider what you give to be little. Whatever you have, whether it’s money or something else, be sure to give sadaqah. Because sadaqah prevents accidents and calamities, and prolongs life. Especially since this month we are in is a heavy one, do not neglect giving sadaqah.

Repentance and seeking forgiveness are necessary; this also keeps calamities away from us. Repentance and seeking forgiveness are beneficial in all things. Turning to Allah and asking for help from Allah is very important. Do not say, “We have committed many sins, Allah will not forgive us anymore.” Allah forgives; Allah is the All-Forgiver.

Increase your repentance and istighfār even more during this month. If others have rights upon you, you will be relieved of this heavy burden by returning those rights to their owners.

The matter of rights is very difficult; the rights of Allah are easy, but the rights of servants are difficult. Allah forgives His rights; However, unless the servant forgives, Allah will not forgive that wrong. A person must absolutely return the right to the wronged party. May Allah protect us from violating anyone’s rights.

This is also a beautiful month; everything is beautiful once a person is with Allah. As the blessed Yunus Emre said, “Everything that comes from You is beautiful.” Everything is inherently beautiful because it comes from Allah. This month is beautiful, other months are beautiful, and our upcoming months will also be beautiful, inshā’Allāh.

Indeed, after this month comes the month of Rabīʿ ul-Awwal, the month of our Prophet. May Allah make all our days beautiful, and may our lives be filled with īmān. After living with īmān, everything is beautiful, with the permission of Allah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq.

Al-Fātiḥah.

In honor of this blessed day, may Allah accept the Khatms of Qur’ān ʿAẓīmu sh-Sha’n, including the surahs, verses, Yasin, Tabarakah; and all the Quran Khatms, takbirs, tahlils, salawats, and the recitations of Dalail Al-Khayrāt, as well as all the good deeds and charity entrusted to us by our brothers.

We dedicate this to our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam; with him, everything will be accepted. For the souls of our Prophet, of his Ahlu l-Bayt and ṣaḥābah. For the souls of all prophets, Awliyā’, asfiya, mashāyikh.

For the souls of our ancestors. For the souls of believing men and women, Muslim men and women. For goodness to come and evil to be gone. For strong īmān.

Li-Llahi Ta’āla, Al- Fātiḥah
