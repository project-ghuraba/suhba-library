As-salāmu ‘alaykum wa raḥmatu Llāhi wa barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla is saying in Qur’ān A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Innā ja‘alnā mā ‘alá l-arḍi zīnatan lahā linabluwahum ayyuhum aḥsanu ‘amalan wa-innā lajā‘ilūna mā ‘alayhā ṣa‘īdan juruzan." (18:7-8) AllāhNone created this world in perfect form. "Fatabāraka Llāhu aḥsanu l-khāliqīn." (23:14) HeNone created everything perfect and made it beautiful. HeNone gave it as a home, a temporary home for people, for testing. HeNone created beautiful things. People without minds assume it to be a permanent place, that there is no other place but this and they forgot about ākhirah. And for Muslims, AllāhNone made all these permitted things ḥalāl. They can use the products of dunyā for the pleasure of AllāhNone. They can use on the way of AllāhNone. It is useful. But if they use this dunyā as if they will never die and they do nothing for others, don't recognize AllāhNone, don't do goodness, then they are in complete loss. Our ProphetNone has said, "ad-dunyā dāru man lā dāra lahu". Dunyā can become home for those who don't think about ākhirah. But for people who will have palaces and castles in ākhirah there is nothing to look at in dunyā. It's not good to put it in your heart.

And at last, "Wa-innā lajā‘ilūna mā ‘alayhā ṣa‘īdan juruzan." (18:8) All these beauties you see will become dry on the day of resurrection. Neither grass, nor water will remain. It will be like a dry desert. It won't be useful then, if they understand it or not. Therefore, we should know that dunyā is a world of tests and we should use the favours given by AllāhNone and be thankful. With shukr, favours increase and last. And we will gain the pleasure of AllāhNone.

Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla saying about this world "Innā ja‘alnā mā ‘alá l-arḍi zīnatan lahā linabluwahum ayyuhum aḥsanu ‘amalan wa-innā lajā‘ilūna mā ‘alayhā ṣa‘īdan juruzan." (18:7-8) "WeNone create this world we create all universe". Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla HeNone create but especially for human being it is important what AllāhNone create us. "Tabāraka Llāhu aḥsanu l-khāliqīn" (23:14) HeNone is the most perfect Creator, Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla. HeNone create world with very good, very beautiful, everything - enough for everybody, for every creature. But people they for themself or for their selfishness they finish, they destroy, they make it very bad. Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla even HeNone said this for people like, for test for people. "WeNone will look who is happy, he is thankful for his Lord." And one for people who only they think it is they come by themself to this world. And they are think no more, any life after this. No, this is only for test.

Dunyā, AllāhNone give us. When people not knowing AllāhNone they will be not happy here. "Ad-dunyā dāru man lā dāra lahu." This, only in this world they can get some house because it is - in ākhirah, next life they will not find because they didn't believe; they not ask for better from this. But believer they can be use what AllāhNone give them from favours, from good ni‘matu Llāh. We can with ḥalāl - you can be enjoy yourself, you can from every favour from Allāh HeNone give you. Allāh HeNone is happy also. You can enjoy if you have opportunity, you can enjoy and you can don't forget AllāhNone. All time make thank for AllāhNone. With thanking you be, AllāhNone give you more and continuously giving. "Wa bi sh-shukru tadūmu n-ni‘am." Because to, if you have some good thing it is difficult to keep it. This is if you find some good place, some good thing, you can take. Don't ask, continue take and you will be blessed. And this is with thanking AllāhNone for what HeNone give you, it will be continue until end of your life.

But if you be become lazy and used to it - it is normal for us. No. Every day you must thanking your Lord for everything for HeNone give you. Specially for ni‘mati l-īmān (the favour of belief). And the end of this world also Allāh HeNone said, "INone will make it dry like desert." To see, show people it is only for this life. In Judgement day you cannot see any green, any water - only flat like desert. No shadow, shadow [shade] for believer only. You must be thankful for AllāhNone for what HeNone give us to be get happiness. Happiness you can only with thanking AllāhNone HeNone give you. If you are against HimNone you will never be happy.

AllāhNone keep us in His way, in shā’a Llāh. And give us from His favour. We are asking. Don't be shy from asking from Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla. Ask what you want. No need to ask for people. Ask from AllāhNone, "Give me, give me, give me." It's ok. If you ask from somebody he will fed up from you. He run away from you if you say this.

Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq al-Fātiḥah.
