We came to learn about the eternal life. "We study at universities." What are you studying? "The way of the mortals." Is there a man who learns from the way of the immortals? Do we have a school, madrasah? Do we have a great school? Are there mosques, dargāh, tekkes left? We have nothing left. That is the true culture, the way of the immortal ones. Hundreds of thousands of books which tell about the way of the immortals became dusty (unread, untouched). People who are looking, look at their faces and don't understand anything. Each one is a different treasure. All of them had struggled to show the way of the immortals.

Why was Qur’ān Karīm sent? Why did Qur’ān Karīm come? It came in order to revive the souls. It came to give the real life. Qur’ān Karīm came to take away the life of animal level and give you the life of the immortal. If there is another meaning, may the one who knows object it. What had the Prophet ṣalawātu Llāhu wa salāmu ‘alayh taught for 23 years? Brothers and sisters, mu’mins and mu’minahs, what did the ProphetNone teach us? Did heNone teach us to eat and drink well? To dress well and enjoy this dunyā? What had heNone taught for 23 years? What's the summary? It is the way of the immortals, says Sulṭān of this place. It is his table and favour here.

I don't make this ṣuḥbah by thinking and writing before it. It is from the spiritual way; they are the immortals. Allāh AlmightyNone dressed them from HisNone attribute of Ḥayy. Therefore, their tables are not folded. Their tables were set during their lives and their spiritual tables are always set. Physical tables may be folded. The spiritual tables of Awliyā’ never fold. But you have to ask. They are rejecting Awliyā’. They deny Awliyā’, they deny Qur’ān, they deny Prophets. Don't do so! Awliyā’ are the top on the way of the immortals. They have reached the secret of eternity. They have reached the true life.

They used to call them reached ones, where did they reach? to Immortality. How did they become so? Hū. They followed the way of the immortals and became from the immortals. Had they followed the mortals, they'd be mortal too. Therefore, that is what we are looking for. That is what we will look for. That is the way to follow. This is what should be asked for. Dunyā runs after the immortals. But they don't flatter. Why would they flatter? They have learnt the reality of dunyā.

A man who doesn't know the reality of dunyā runs after dunyā. And dunyā runs away from him. People run after it and get tired. They depart from this world tired. A man who knows the reality of dunyā uses dunyā to send to his ākhirah. Because the more you send to your ākhirah, the more you have there. If so, let us put your chechbook in your kafan so that they write it down on the other side. Let him take from that side. Dunyā can't be transferred to ākhirah with a chechbook. You should send it while here.

You can't transfer anything by writing millions in your checkbook and putting it in your kafan. It is those who say AllāhNone. The target of the immortals is AllāhNone and what is for AllāhNone, it is the ProphetNone and what is for the ProphetNone. What is for the ProphetNone and for AllāhNone, it is immortal. That is the way of the immortals. Of course, it has been 1,500 years since the Prophet ‘alayhi ṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salām came to this dunyā. Even if it was 15,000 years, this religion wouldn't become old, this way wouldn't change. The way of the immortals doesn't change. And the way of the mortals is clear.

During the time of the Prophet ‘alayhi ṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salām those who accepted our ProphetNone followed the way of the immortals. They became from the immortals. They earned the eternal life here. All honorable ṣaḥābah are Sulṭāns of ākhirah, they never die. Heedless people put a tin plate on Ayyūb Sulṭān which says "No help from the dead". Who wrote that is dead himself and is a stinky man. That can't be written in his presence. That is a shame. It is showing no adab towards ṣaḥābahs. If a dead person lied there, millions of people wouldn't come there. Because dry soil is dead soil. There is nothing in dead soil. So there must be someone alive lying there with a true life that his attraction and his life brings millions of people there. Let them put that person when he dies and see how many people will come to visit him.
