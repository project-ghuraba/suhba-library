The Holy Hadith about Damascus: The Prophet (saws) said "If fitna spreads in the world, then go to Damascus." The Prophet's (saws) words are Truth. The last order is that safety is in Sham. From East to West, safety is only in the land of Sham. Sham has the best houses for the Muslims. Hadith says it is safe for the Muslims.

In the days of Armageddon there is a land called Ghuta and there is a city called Damascus. It is the place safe for the Muslims in the last days as the Prophet (saws)said.

O people of Damascus be careful to be with Allah (swt). Don't be with the rest of the tyrant people. Be with the people of Allah (swt) and you will be saved in this world & in the Hereafter. May Allah (swt) grant you all goodness.

Welcome the nation of the Beloved (saws) because the land of Sham...The land of Sham, masha'Allah! The houses in Damascus in the end of times are the best houses. Gather in Damascus & give Bayat to the Sultan, the one who will rule in Sham very soon. And he is from Ahl Al Bayt (household of the Prophet,saws). He is honorable and now he is present & has the characteristics to rule in Sham - His Royal Highness Abdullah, may Allah (swt) support him in this world & in the Hereafter. May Allah (swt) dress him with the Majesty & Glory of Islam & Power.

Give him your allegiance & come under his regiment that will carry the regiment of the Prophet (saws), the regiment of Sayidina Muhammad (saws), and the regiment of Sahib Al Zaman Mahdi (as). And the third regiment is his that he will be king in Sham.

May Allah (swt) support him, give him glory & bestow on him Divine Generosity. May Allah (swt) grant him power & especially because he is going to be the one with greatness & glory among the nations of the world.

May Allah (swt) support him. I have given him Bayat openly - the first Bayat. I am asking the believers to give him Bayat & to support him in all possible ways.

Wa Min Allah Al Tawfiq. Fatiha
