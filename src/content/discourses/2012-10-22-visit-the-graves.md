As-Salamu Alaikum. Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fil jamia'

This month of Dhu-i Hijjah, ash-sh'huru l-hurum, is a respected month on the part of Allah. In these 9 days, of course all of them are holy but you can fast from the 1st till the 9th. And what is performed in these days from good deeds & charity are also more virtuous. Meaning, it is again a season of worship. It is a wasila/ means for the people here as well as the people who go for Hajj-many people go to fulfill a big fardh, the Hajj fardh.

And the ones here, if they wake up for night prayer & get busy with worship more during the day time it is more virtuous compared to the other days, Allah's gifts are more. And the most is on the 8-9, the day of tarwiyah.The day of tarwiyah is the 8th. It is the day that the pilgrims climb Arafat. So to fast in these days, the 8th & the 9th, who can't fast the whole 9 days should at least fast these. Who can't fast it should fast the day of Arafat. He reaches the grants of Allah. And also 1000 Surat al Ikhlas on the day of Arafat is the advice of mashayekh. And of course they say this because of the knowledge they receive from the Prophet (sas). They should definitely do 1000 Surat al Ikhlas on the Arafat day.

And there is also the sacrifice which is obligatory. It's obligatory for those who can afford it, not obligatory for who can't afford. And also for who can afford it, if he is in his home country, not travelling, it is obligatory. He can still slaughter even if he is travelling but who is sitting without travelling in his home country & who can afford it, it is obligatory on him. He has to slaughter. If he doesn't of course again the responsibility is on him.

Allah (awj), as we said, when He orders worship for people it is not to make difficulty but to make a benefit for them. So if a person slaughters only for himself as he can afford & even if he doesn't have any more money, he can eat that meat even without giving anyone. He gains it's reward & also he can keep that meat for 3-5 months & eat it. He doesn't necessarily need to give it to people. But if it is obligatory on him, he is responsible for doing it. If he doesn't, he will be asked "Why did you not do this?"

So to not regret in akhirah, one should do these things in dunya & he also reaches big material & spiritual benefits. And of course there are many sunnahs & mustahabbs. And one of them is the visiting of the graves. Now, fortunately we don't have many of those people but they come out & speak & confuse people. There is the Salafi group. They say "people shouldn't visit the graves". There are hundreds of Hadiths "visit the graves, you may draw lesson from them & they get benefit from the Quran you read". There are clear Hadiths like this. Bukhari also narrated these & others. So there are a lot of such Hadiths narrated. Meaning, no one should be confused that it may not be allowed.

And people, they listen to it without investigating. "One man appeared on TV & said it is a sin to visit the dead ones, one should not visit their graves.." However it is the order of Prophet (sas) "Visit them, they are your friends. Draw a lesson from them & they also benefit from the Quran you read, they receive your gifts." There are many Hadiths like this. And to visit, to see the relatives, close ones, friends on Eid is also thawab. It also strengthens the love Muslims have for each other. And another thing, Allah (awj) orders beautiful things. Shaytan orders the exact opposite. And man's ego wants the bad things. May Allah protect all of us from it.

Today we speak about Dhu-l Hijjah. Now it is months; there are four months, holy months-hurum..It is speacial months. Dhu-l Qidah, Dhul-Hijjah, Muharram & Rajab this is ash-sh'huru l-hurum. Allah make them, Muslims not allowed to fight in this months. But if somebody attack them, so it is, they can fight. And they are holy months, especially this; from beginning of Dhu-l Hijjah until 9th, mostly must be fasting. But if not fasting, you can fast first of Dhu-l Hijjah & 8th & 9th. Because on 8th, the pilgrimage, Hajjis are going to Arafat to complete their Hajj. So it is very holy day. So if not fasting from 1 to 9, you can fast 8 and 9.

And Allah give His gifts in these months, special gift. So what you can do? Charity. You can make worship & sitting with, listening to mashayekh or to be in zawiya or mosque. They have special gift from Allah, more & more. Because this is Dhu-l Hijjah, one of.. Hajj- Islam, when you become Muslim, must do 5 things, one of them is Hajj. If he is able to do it, by health, when by financially. If he is poor, no need for him. But if he has enough money, he must go. But there is now, they making not everybody can go because not allowing people. Only from 1000 each, only one they allowed. Because many people going to Makkah, they said we cannot take all this people. So if he has ability, he must go. And Allah, for this worshipping for all Muslim giving this barakah.

So we must fast 8-9. And who has enough money he must slaughter sheep for each he has money. If he has not, no need. Because Allah, what He order, order for benefit of human being, for people. Even he has money, he can slaughter one and Allah give him, rewarding him. Even he can eat this sheep by himself- 3 months, 4 months it is enough for him. Also this is easy but Allah want to do what He order. And mostly he must be not in traveling. If he is traveling & he is not in this Eid days in his country, this is no need for him. But if he do, it is ok also. But if he is sitting & not traveling, he has enough money & not slaughtering so it will be problem for him in Akhirah. Allah rewarding by material & spiritual also, benefit for people.

Alhamdulillah in Eid also, you meet friend, you meet relative, you meet brothers. So this is also good for people. And many thing Sunnah & something like Sunnah- it is Allah love this thing, Prophet love it; specially to visit graveyard. We say this because now there is very bad people. They are saying this is haram to visit graveyards & it is not allowed in religion. It is lie. Because there is hundreds of Hadiths from Prophet (sas), he ordering "Go & visit graveyard. And they are your, to take lesson from this people. And they are your brothers. This people, look for them & give them gift, read for them Quran & send charity for them. This is benefit for them". This is, hundred of Hadith like this. Because people now, they are not listening. Just one man said to them "This is not Sunnah, not halal. It is forbidden in Islam" they are listening. They are not asking if there is Hadith or no Hadith" No. They say it is haram to go to graveyard, to visit this people". And this is very wrong. And they are now, even they are blowing up graveyard of awliya. Even to.. There is many Hadith, Prophet (sas) said "the bone of Muslim you must be also careful & respect this bone even."
