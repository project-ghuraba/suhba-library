As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Wa-quli ‘malū fa-sa-yará Llāhu ‘amalakum” (9:105), says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Do what is ordered. Whatever you do, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla will see it. Don’t be idle in this world. You must do something. You should know that Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla sees you and do good things accordingly. You must strive to do good things. We did not come to this world to have leisure time. If Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla had sent us to this world to while away the time, HeNone would have made us from the four-legged creatures or other things, things that are of this world, created for the benefit of mankind. This is their duty. This is their work. But the work of mankind is to do goodness, to obey AllāhNone, and to do pious deeds. Those who don’t do this waste their lives. They spend them in loss and don’t gain anything.

People of the present time, most people, strive to do like they see others doing. And there are very few people who want to be like the people who do good deeds, or who try to do so. Many more people want to imitate the lifestyle of those who are busy with fruitless, unnecessary and harmful things. They want to be like them.

Whereas in reality, there is nothing there to imitate. From the outside, it looks a certain way. When you look inside, you see something very different. They are in complete loss. There is nothing there. And even if you say you didn’t do anything harmful, spending your time uselessly will be a loss for yourself. Time is precious for you. People should always be busy with good deeds in order not to waste time. Today many people speak without knowing. People used to look for wisdom in old times. Now they look for entertainment. Wherever they see wisdom, they don’t like it. They look for whatever will entertain them. Therefore, there are many people in this day and age. But very few of them like goodness.

May AllāhNone always let us be in goodness and do the things that HeNone likes. May we realize that HeNone sees us, and at least make intention to do all kinds of goodness, in shā’a Llāh. May AllāhNone let us succeed in it as much as we can. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Wa-quli ‘malū fa-sa-yará Llāhu ‘amalakum” (9:105). AllāhNone saying in Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n, You do... What you do, AllāhNone, HeNone will be... HeNone is seeing us, but for our future HeNone will see, said Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla in Qur’ān. Everything we are doing, AllāhNone knows it and HeNone is saying HeNone will tell us what we did in our life. This is meaning of this āyah. So, this means be careful. Don’t do what AllāhNone HeNone don’t like you to do. Do as much as possible, what AllāhNone HeNone is happy with this.

Because, we are living in a time, whole world they are not interested in this āyah. They not believe. And they like to do what their ego likes to do. Just enjoying, enjoying, enjoying. This is what their philosophy of this life. “Life, it is short. You must enjoy yourself,” they say. If you enjoy yourself here, you will be not enjoying, in forever. For this, you must know, and we must not imitate these people who [are] saying this nonsense thing. Whole world they are following these people. Even they are becoming worse and worse. For, every time coming something it is worse than others, quickly people they try to imitate – do it. Not imitate – They are doing the same what the others are doing.

And they, “We are proud, we are doing this.” This is... Cannot be anyone proud with this bad action, bad thing you done, you are doing. And they are putting in... for other people to see it also. This is craziness. But when somebody following shayṭān and ego, he become crazy. Not become good one, become bad one. But, these people, they are cheated by [it]. And they are thinking this good. Looking from outside. They thought it is: “This is life, this is what you must do.” But when you going inside – only outside making you think it is good. But inside only bad, and nothing good. Only dirt and misery and poorness and every bad thing you imagine, it is in this. But people they are not looking for this when they are going, and they thought it is like this. But in the end, they will regret. But that time regret finished, you cannot do anything. But we are – don’t look for this.

This is, many people from Muslim people also. They are thought it is life, this is life, they will be happy if they done like these unbeliever people doing, these who not following rule of humanity, rule of AllāhNone. They thought they are – they have freedom, and freedom good for them. They have freedom only to do bad thing. When you come to do good thing, many things prevent you: “Don’t do this. Do bad thing, don’t do good thing,” they say.

AllāhNone keep us and not be cheated with this people. Because really, they are very excellent to cheat people. To show the black, white. And good bad, and bad good. They are very, very... masters. They are masters for this. They cheated the whole world. And now they are controlling, and they are proud with very bad people. Good people, they are... They don’t have any respect. They don’t have any value. Only if you are bad. Even they are putting very bad action, very bad word, and they said, “This is the best for human. Must be do what they like, human. Give freedom for your ego. Do what you like. You are the best,” they said. No, this is not possible. AllāhNone keep them away from us and make us to awake. Not to follow these bad people, bad thing. AllāhNone – Because, we must know AllāhNone HeNone whole time seeing us. And HeNone will show us what you done in this life, and we will regret. In shā’a Llāh, AllāhNone not make us to regret in front of HimNone, in shā’a Llāh. And save us from this people.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
