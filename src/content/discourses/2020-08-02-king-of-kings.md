As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Today is the third day of the holy days of ‘Īd. Of these holy days Yawmu ‘Arafah is the most blessed, and after that, the first day of ‘Īd, second day of ‘Īd. All of these four days are valuable in the presence of AllāhNone. They are made as a feast for us. It is both a physical feast in that people congratulate each other, visit others, and give presents, take care of the poor and the elderly. And it is spiritual, there are other than material presents, there are more valuable spiritual presents. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla prepares such presents for us that we have no way to evaluate them.

Dunyā is like this that we were waiting for ‘Īd, and now it is almost over. It's the third day and soon it will be over too. These are all transitory, not permanent things. It is here and after a moment it's over. Summer comes and goes, winter also. Life goes too. What remains, is what was made ready for life in Ākhirah. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla promised the mu’min servants all goodness. HeNone promised this to all mankind. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Ayyuha n-nās, "tūbū ilá Llāhi jamī‘a" (24:31). "O people, repent to AllāhNone all of you." White or yellow or unbeliever, HeNone doesn't distinguish between them. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is addressing all mankind and saying to repent. Repent for the mistakes you have made. Who is not Muslim does not know how to make istighfār but they know tawbah. When they make tawbah, they repent and come to Allāh'sNone way. Unbelievers are held accountable from that moment. Nothing from before remains valid. Our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam said, "Al-Islām yajubbu mā qablah." Īmān, belief erases everything that came before. A man starts again from a blank page.

Therefore, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla doesn't oppress people. People oppress each other. AllāhNone forgives all our sins, wrong actions, everything you can think of. "Laysa ba‘da l-kufri dhanb." There is no bigger sin than kufr. The biggest sin is kufr. kufr is bigger than all sins. If you leave kufr and turn to AllāhNone, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla will not question you about your past. You will start from zero. Therefore, Allāh'sNone mercy is great. A man does wrong to himself and he does right to himself. May AllāhNone let us do the right. May we deserve the rewards of these valuable days, in shā’a Llāh. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are living in blessed, holy days, especially from Yawmu ‘Arafah. It is the highest, blessed day. After [that] first day of ‘Īd – tenth of Dhū l-Ḥijjah. After yesterday, Yawmu n-Naḥr – first day Yawmu n-Naḥr – to slaughter. Second day calling Yawmu l-Qarr it is everybody finished from qurbān, finished from... Who in ḥajj finished from ‘Arafāt, ramiy jamarāt, slaughter, and he come out from iḥrām. It is settle day meaning, Yawmu l-Qarr. It is also blessed after first day of ‘Īd, second day as well. After [that] third and fourth also blessed. But the most blessed [are the ones] before this days.

But Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla also, HeNone is giving us this feast. AllāhNone, HeNone put this feast for Muslim. And Muslim, they are happy with ‘Īd. They celebrate. Everybody celebrate. Who practice and not practice, all they are celebrate. And also there is spiritual celebration for ‘Īd. AllāhNone give gift, gift, precious gift not like dunyā or this life gift. This life gift – it is very short. We was waiting for ‘Īd, for Dhū l-Ḥijjah, for Dhū l-Qa‘dah, Dhū l-Ḥijjah, after Muḥarram. Quickly coming. Now third day of ‘Īd – it will be finished. Summer, it will be finished. Days like this. Life it will be finished. But what is not finished, what AllāhNone gave us from HisNone gifts, forever it will be, in shā’a Llāh. And this in special day,

HeNone is King of kings – Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, whole universe for HimNone. HeNone is giving for who... Sending angels [HeNone is] asking: "Who asking something, INone will give him. What they want, INone will give them. Look, go and look for this people." So this also, very blessed days. You must be ask from AllāhNone every good thing, in shā’a Llāh. And it is for, especially must ask for forgiveness. And AllāhNone give this for whole human being, not only for Muslim. HeNone said to them: "O human being, ayyuha n-nās, "tūbū ilá Llāhi jamī‘a" (24:31), ask for repent from AllāhNone to forgive you to, what you done before. If you ask for forgiveness, AllāhNone will forgive you, and give you – changing, what you do bad to good. And who they don't have any belief, HeNone accept them, even they are ninety year or eighty years, whole life who is non-believer but when they asking – doing every bad thing – but when they asking from AllāhNone forgiveness and accepting HimNone and become mu’min – Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying: "What they done before it is finished. AllāhNone not asking." Beginning from zero from that time.

Even they die after five minute, also they will be pure one. This is mercy of AllāhNone. HeNone encourage people to accept HimNone, to believe [in order] to reward them. HeNone don't want to punish them. People, human being they are oppress themselves. AllāhNone not oppressing anybody. And Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying in ḥadīth: "To Islām – removing what they before, what they done, they are not asked." They will not ask whatever you done. You will be beginning from first, from one step – continue. In dunyā they don't have this law. If anybody they... But Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, because HeNone is knowing what in heart, HeNone accept who HeNone know who is sincere or not sincere – doing this for AllāhNone or not doing this for AllāhNone. If he is sincere and accepting not [doing it] only to cheat people, it will be safe. This is our LordNone. HeNone is most merciful. We must be happy, because HeNone not looking for small thing. HeNone giving us chance to repent, to ask for forgiveness, to forgive us. And when forgive us, HeNone changing bad action for reward for us. This is also from HisNone generos[ity].

AllāhNone accept our ‘ibādah, our ‘Īd, in shā’a Llāh. And we wait for reward, here and hereafter, in shā’a Llāh. Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah
