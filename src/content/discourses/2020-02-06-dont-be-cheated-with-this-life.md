As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh. A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Fa-lā taghurrannakumu l-ḥayātu d-dunyā, wa-lā yaghurrannakum biLlāhi l-gharūr" (35:5). Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is telling this āyah in Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n: don't be cheated by the life of this world. The life of this world cheats people and makes them forget AllāhNone. When they forget AllāhNone, they lose in the end and depart from this world. They have wasted their lives. They have wasted their hopes. What they thought to be good turns out to be bad. They will have lived completely useless lives. There are many cheated people.

People are cheated by the appearances, by talk or by actions. They are lost in dunyā. And if dunyā distances them from AllāhNone, their work becomes completely harmful. You should not trust everyone. Because Muslims are cheated by other Muslims as well. They are Muslim but what they order is showing Islām as bad. From the time of our ProphetNone till now, for fifty years or sixty to seventy years, this Islām became different. So people are distancing themselves from the way of our ProphetNone every day. They say they are Muslim and keep Islām but what they are doing is separating themselves from the way of our ProphetNone. This is the life of this world. They are cheated by following untruthful knowledge and unwise people.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla didn't order for this way. HeNone sent our ProphetNone. Everything heNone did, everything heNone said shows us the way. If you don't follow himNone and instead follow these people who have appeared in recent years, you are separating yourself as much as they have. You are going further away. It is so useless and so harmful. You do something and end up harmed. You get tired without seeing any benefit. This is it. Don't be cheated. Don't look at appearances and lose the way. Beware. Don't be cheated. Our ProphetNone says Muslims are not cheated. "Lā yuldaghu l-mu’minu min juḥrin wāḥidin marratayn," says our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. You may put your foot once without knowing and get bitten by a snake. Will you put your foot again? No. Same with cheating. You should stay away from these people. They are looking for people to cheat. Who is foolish gets cheated. Who is not foolish isn't cheated. Because our grandfathers, our fathers followed the way of our ProphetNone. They followed himNone and were not cheated. In shā’a Llāh, may AllāhNone protect us and may the evil of these people turn back on themselves. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying: Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Fa-lā taghurrannakumu l-ḥayātu d-dunyā, wa-lā yaghurrannakum biLlāhi l-gharūr" (35:5). Don't be cheated with this life. And don't be cheated to be away from AllāhNone with life, running after, forgetting Ākhirah, forgetting anything, only running after dunyā, after people who are they are cheater, to make you to forget Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. You must be awake, you must be thinking. Don't be cheated with appearance. Appearance of people or appearance of this life, or luxury or anything can be everything can be cheat people because everyone they have capacity – some of them very small thing can cheat them, some of them more and more. So everybody can be cheated.

For this cheated maybe you can, many people they cheat people, they take from them what they want. But this is for dunyā. But for Ākhirah it is most important to not be cheated with people they are claiming they are Muslim and they are saying for people to, "This is way of Islām. Other not way of Islām." Because many people cheated with this people, especially who they thought they are very clever, they are understanding everything. They are most of them following these people. And these people they are coming, appear not so long, maybe fifty or seventy years. Everyday becoming more and more and more. And they are first beginning, they was very small poison they put. Now they put more and more and people following them.

This people they are cheated with this people. This, they are cheating people against Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, not respecting ProphetNone, not following Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. They make sunnah, sunan, yāhū sunnahs from themself and they said, "This is Islām." And they cheat people. Many people they are cheated everyday they invent new thing and they said not make invent thing – "This is bid‘ah." But real, they are bid‘ah because they are only a new thing happen for Islāmic world, for human. It is not good for anyone. Good only for shayṭān and for non-believer, [who] they are using them to divide Muslims. But it is the most important thing here for Muslim he must be awake.

Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying: "Lā yuldaghu l-mu’minu min juḥrin wāḥidin marratayn." Mu'min believer he couldn't, he not will be bitten or not cheated, like snake coming from hole. Same hole cannot be two, twice, snake bite him. Maybe once can bite, but other time will be clever, careful to not be this. But unfortunately this is so. This people they are not mu'min, not believer. They don't have anything from belief, anything from īmān, only they are Muslim. And this people, because they don't have belief, easily can be cheated. So mu'min not... he is straight in way of ProphetNone and way of Ṣaḥābah and Ahlu l-Bayt and Awliyā’u Llāh. This is not, they cannot be cheated these people. And they are, everything they do, they are winning.

Other, everything they are doing only they tire themself and only satisfy their ego. They say, "Okay today I was shouting for people, today I was fighting with people, with this believer mu'min because they are doing what we don't do and we are not happy. I am happy. Today I was very happy to shout people, to harm people they are doing this." This is they be nighttime they are happy with what they done. But this they cheat themselves only. Don't be cheated by these people. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is the most Merciful and most Generous. HeNone not looking what they are wasping [whispering] for themself. They are like shayṭān. They are wasping for themselves even they are not happy with themselves but for bad thing. For good thing they are not seeing at all. AllāhNone keep us safe from these people and give awakeness for Muslim to see this and to not be cheated with this people.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
