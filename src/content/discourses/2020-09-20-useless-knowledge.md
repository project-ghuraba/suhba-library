Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

In the sight of Allah, everything is known and people are told what to do. It is by the wisdom of Allah that people think that by reading a few books they know everything and they understand everything. They believe so. They think so. They do not accept anything from others. The knowledge they read that does not recognize Allah is not knowledge, it is not solid knowledge. Everything they read and teach becomes imagination. They say, “We know this.” Allah has given these people this knowledge, it is pertaining to them. The knowledge of other creatures He created is different, ours is different.

Allah’s knowledge is endless. That’s why we have to turn to Allah. Scholars and educated people should turn to Allah so that they can be saved, and the knowledge they study becomes beneficial. They say that we are being educated. But the more educated they get, the worse they become; they do not get better. They become harmful instead of useful, because what they read is what shaytan teaches. They teach it gradually. Now it is totally clear because it is the end of times. It is not: ‘ركنملا نع يهنلاو فورعملاب رملأا’, ‘enjoining good and forbidding evil’, but it is: ‘فورعملا نع يهنلاو ركنملاب رملأا’, ‘enjoining evil and forbidding good.” They enjoin evil and forbid good. In short, this is what they teach. They don’t teach goodness.

They say now everything is under our control. It is under the control of shaytan. Now you see in Europe, by the wisdom of Allah, they closed everywhere because of this disease but they always insist on opening schools. Why? Because they don’t want to lose control. They will pollute and dirty the child’s brain when he is younger. What they do is not to wash but to dirty. That’s why, while there are so many prohibitions on this and that, they insist on schooling them so that they can teach them the disgrace and filth of shaytan. They have programs to get kids into this filth, starting in kindergarten.

By the wisdom of Allah, this thing came before they even started [the educational year]. They closed schools last year. You cannot go to war against Allah. The end of those who go to war against Allah is loss. Those who are with Allah are saved. May Allah help Islam, because they are doing what Europe and the West are doing. They are sinking with them. They have no thought of their own. Allah gave you mind, and He says: “Think”. It is said: “An hour of contemplation is better than a hundred years of worship.” May Allah give us all minds and intellect insha’Allah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
