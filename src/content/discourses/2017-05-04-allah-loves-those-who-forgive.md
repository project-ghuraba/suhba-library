As-salāmu ‘alaykum wa raḥmatu Llāhi wa barakātuh. A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah. Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla is saying A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm.

"Wal-kāẓimīna l-ghayẓa wal-ʿāfīna ʿani n-nāsi wa Llāhu yuḥibbu l-muḥsinīn." (3:134) Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla created mankind. HeNone created them beautiful. And for their behaviour, HeNone sent the prophets to people to clean their wild character, to make them good people. A good person is a person who believes in AllāhNone. A person who follows AllāhNone and the ProphetNone is the good one. He is the one who keeps Allāh'sNone orders and prohibitions. And these orders are to get along well with people, to treat people well. The true Muslim people love for the sake of AllāhNone and dislike for the sake of AllāhNone. Of course, you should treat every one well. Everybody is the servant of AllāhNone. However, a person who says "I don't love AllāhNone", a person who denies AllāhNone - of course we mustn't beat and swear at them - but we should dislike them. At least, we shouldn't love them. You should say "AllāhNone created them like this, may AllāhNone protect us". You should say "Shukr to AllāhNone He didn't create us like them". But Muslims and those who are on the path of AllāhNone, you should treat them well.

As all Muslims believe in AllāhNone and are on the right path, they should be treated well. But those who enter Islām and mislead people from their way, they can't be given love. As they are not on this way, we should pray to AllahNone that they are guided and come to the right way. But between Muslims, there should be love and respect. As long as a man is on the way of AllāhNone and the ProphetNone, people can make mistakes. There is an ego. Sometimes the ego wins. Mistakes are made. We should not turn those mistakes into a grudge. Grudge-bearing is not good. It doesn't suit Muslims. The one on the way of our ProphetNone cannot hold grudges. A grudge is not a dislike. A grudge is different. To dislike means to not show admiration for what they do. To have a grudge is to have some enmity against them, it is a shayṭānic thing. If someone lets it inside, it won't go away. Therefore, when people, to be more accurate, people on the right way see a mistake or something not nice, they should love for AllāhNone and dislike for AllāhNone. If they come to the right way, that dislike goes away. No aversion remains. It turns into love. But the grudge doesn't leave. Even if a the other person is Awliyā’, that grudge remains inside him. Therefore, we should be careful about it. People who are on the way of our ProphetNone never keep a grudge, not dislike.

There are many groups in the Islamic world. And there is a middle group which goes on the way of our ProphetNone. And there are two more groups, both are built on grudge-bearing. They are harming Muslims. Nothing can harm Islām. One group is those who don't love Ahlu l-Bayt of our ProphetNone. They have a grudge against them. They have grudge.
