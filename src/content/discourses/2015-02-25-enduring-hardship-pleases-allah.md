Salam alaikum wa rahmatullahi wa barakatuh Audhu billahi min ash-shaitani rajeem. Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. As-salatu wa s-salamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidi l-awwalina wa l-akhirin. Madad ya RasulAllah, madad ya Sadati Ashabi RasulAllah, madad ya Mashaykhina, dastur ya Mawlana Sheyh Abdullah Faiz Dagestani, Mawlana Sheikh Muhammad Nazim Haqqani, dastur. Tariqatuna as-sohba wa l-khairu fi l-jamiyah.

Association it is one pillar of tariqa. Insha’Allah short association, sohba to be benefit for you. For me also insha’Allah. Once one haji was going by himself, I think. He lost his way in desert. And nearly he died. So one of Awliyaullah come for him and tell him "I will serve you but you must do what I tell you". Of course, he was happy, very happy. He said to him "When you get back to your country, you must go to this man and slap him in front of people." So he said "Ok. Just that?" When he came back to his place, he went. And this man, he was Sultan of this area. He came near him and he slapped him.

Sultan, he looked for him. He tell him, "Tell this man who sending you here, it is easy to go around desert and where no people and it is difficult to be like me, around people. He must take this slap for him. He much more in need for this, for him." So Sultan, he was also Awliya and he know who sent this man to him. Just saying to be away from people, it is more easy. To be with people, to carry their matter, their problem, their burden, what they have, it is more difficult to be around people, with people.

Once also, we were with Mawlana Sheikh in Istanbul. There is big Awliya, his name Abu l-Wafa. We visit his grave, maqam, and there near maqam, there was small room they say cilehane for seclusion and to be difficult for who is in this room. It will be like jail or something. Mawlana, he also was saying this is more easy to be around people, with people it's more difficult to be alone, away from people. So to be with people it is really more difficult. And when something more difficult, more acceptable in Divine Presence of Allah.

So you are here, it is not easy for our people to be around non-Muslim and be very few people. It is acceptable for you, so you must be happy. Don't worry, Allah keep you. When you are with Allah, He not forget you. You don't forget Allah. People are forgetting Allah, all time looking short distance. Even if you say for 1 year you must do this, it become for them like forever. No, everything, when counting, will be finished one day. So no need to waste your time, waste your life in bad place, with bad people, to do bad actions. No. Allah open these places for people who are looking for Allah. No need to go to desert, no need to go to high mountains to be away from people. No. Better to be here in this place, to be together, to remember Allah. When you know the wisdom of creation, you'll be happy, you'll be satisfied. But if you are searching out of Allah way, never finding this. And shaitan cheating you and make you the worst creature. To be animal many times better than this creature because animals, they have their life, they are not doing against their nature. You cannot find any sheep eating meat. You cannot find any lion eating grass. But people, they are doing everything. They are trying everything. Why they are trying everything? They have reason. They are looking and searching for happiness, for presence. They are looking, looking, but they are destroying themselves.

Only who coming to Allah, many people we saw, they regretted and came. Left this ugly, dirty life, coming to right path, way of Allah and they are becoming really good Muslim. And they are helping sometimes, if they want, their old friends. There is saying in Turkish, sometimes wine changing to vinegar. Sometimes changing to vinegar because they put... It is so sharp. When vinegar is sharp, it is the best vinegar. So these people also. When they are coming from wrong way to good way, they are becoming very good Muslims. Because they were trying everything, and they couldn't find anything from good. What they are finding, only in Islam they find. So they are happy and they are trying to help their friends or human beings.

This city, it is big city, and many people, they are lost. So the best thing is to make them to come to Allah, to follow tariqa also because tariqa giving people love and mercy. Other ways also. Those who following sometimes Salafi or other, they're only giving hatred and anger. And it is also not good. So you must look for people who are in need and to make them come to this place, insha’Allah. They're our brothers, they are doing this for Allah. And their intention to do this, it is good intention. Allah help them and make their action, to make their maqam and their situation higher and higher in presence of Allah Azza wa Jalla. Because every time you save one man, Allah give you.

Allah Azza wa Jalla said when you save a man, it is better for you from even all this world and what is inside it also. Just for saving one man. This is what Awliyaullah, Sultan is saying, to be in city around people, it is not easy but when you help people, it also make you higher and higher, insha’Allah. Allah help you, Allah be with you. It is most important to not forget Allah and to accept what He give you and to be happy and to be thankful to Him for everything. Allah make everyone from you to bring thousands, insha’Allah. Wa min Allahi tawfeeq. Al-Fatiha.
