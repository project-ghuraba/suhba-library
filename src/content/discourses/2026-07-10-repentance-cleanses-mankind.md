As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
إِنَّ اللَّهَ يُحِبُّ التَّوَّابِينَ وَيُحِبُّ الْمُتَطَهِّرِينَ
<br>“InnAllāha yuḥibbu t-tawwābīna wa-yuḥibbu l-mutaṭahhirīn.”<br>
“Truly, Allah loves those who turn unto Him in repentance and loves those who purify themselves.” (Qurʼān 2:222). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla loves those who repent and purify themselves. Therefore, the rank of those who repent so that Allah may forgive their sins is great. 

Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam says,<br>
كُلُّ ابْنِ آدَمَ خَطَّاءٌ وَخَيْرُ الْخَطَّائِينَ التَّوَّابُونَ
<br>“Kullu-bni Ādama khaṭṭā'un wa-khayru l-khaṭṭā'īna at-tawwābūn.”<br>
“Every child of Ādam sins, and the best of the sinners are the repentant.” (Sunan at-Tirmīdhi)

This means that mankind make many mistakes and commit many sins.

Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam said, “Allah loves those who repent.” People make mistakes and commit sins from morning till night, knowingly or unknowingly. There is certainly not a single hour a person spends without sin. Therefore, repentance cleanses those sins, leaving nothing behind. As long as you haven’t violated the rights of others, Allah will surely forgive the sins and mistakes you have made.

Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam said, “I also say ‘Astaghfirullah’ seventy times.” The Companions said, “You have no sins.” Prophets are infallible, they never commit sins. Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam said, “I do it so that I may be among those who are grateful. Should I not be a grateful servant?” Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam did this throughout his life and actions so that it would be a lesson and an example for us.

Therefore, repenting and seeking forgiveness removes the burden of sin from a person, bringing them relief. We should repent in the morning, at noon, and in the evening; repenting and seeking forgiveness at all times is very important. That is why, after the five daily prayers, right after Fard, we repent and seek forgiveness three times. After that, that burden on us is lifted, and Allah willing, we continue on our way again.

The angels wait to record the sin immediately. If repentance is made before they record it, Allah willing, that sin is erased. Sins are a burden, a darkness; they cause all kinds of harm to a person.

Therefore, Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says,<br>
إِنَّ اللَّهَ يُحِبُّ التَّوَّابِينَ وَيُحِبُّ الْمُتَطَهِّرِينَ
<br>“InnAllāha yuḥibbu t-tawwābīna wa-yuḥibbu l-mutaṭahhirīn.”<br>
“Truly, Allah loves those who turn unto Him in repentance and loves those who purify themselves.” (Qurʼān 2:222).

Those who repent and those who purify themselves. Purity is both physical cleansing, like ablution and ghusl, and spiritual cleansing. Therefore, when one repents, spiritual cleansing is achieved. Thus, we are cleansed of the sins we have committed. We become among the people whom Allah loves, from His beloved servants. Allah loving a servant is the greatest blessing.

Shukr to Allah for putting us on this way. May Allah perpetuate this blessing and grant guidance to others, inshā’Allāh. May Allah protect us all from the evil of shayṭān.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. 

Al-Fātiḥah.

There are recited Qur’ān Khatms, Yasin, tahlilāt, tasbihāt, salawat, and Dala’il Al-Khayrāt.

Our brothers from all over the world have read them. May Allah accept whatever good deeds they have done. We gift them all to the soul of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, for the souls of his Ahlu l-Bayt and ṣaḥābah. For the souls of all prophets, Awliyā’, asfiya, mashāyikh.

For the souls of our ancestors. For the souls of believing men and women, Muslim men and women. For goodness to come and evil to be gone.

For happiness here and hereafter, inshā’Allāh. For all of us to remain steadfast, inshā’Allāh.

May we be from Allah’s beloved servants, inshā’Allāh. Amīn. Amīn.

Wa alḥamdulillāhi Rabb il-Ālamīn. 

Al-Fātiḥah.
