As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
فَلِلَّهِ الْحُجَّةُ الْبَالِغَةُ
<br>“Fa lillāhi l-ḥujjatu l-bāligha”<br>
“With Allah is the perfect proof and argument.” (Qur’ān 06:149). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

The Ḥujjah (proof) is something that is absolute and definitive. The proof is that Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla is above everything, that He is the existence of everything, the reality of everything; there is no greater truth than that.

An Arab poet said,<br>
كل ما عدا الله باطل
<br>“Kullu mā ʿadā Allah bātil.”

Everything other than Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla is false, void. Everyone’s existence is considered void compared to the existence of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla.

There are some people today who consider themselves smart, but the more they study, the more ignorant they become. They deny Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. In fact, if they deny Him, it means nothing exists. Humans and everything else came into existence through the existence of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla.

Therefore, what they say is ignorance, utter (dark) ignorance. Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam called it the second age of ignorance. The first age of ignorance was at that time. Ignorance means not knowing anything. Even then, it was better than this time. They were creating something to worship. But they were confused about what to do regarding belief; they worshipped something non-existent.

But people today, the more they get “educated”, the more ignorant and worse they become. They deny everything. Then, they even deny their own existence. But unfortunately, what they are teaching is the shayṭān’s game. The shayṭān is showing them the way to become worse. The shayṭān never wants what is good for man.

إِنَّ الشَّيْطَانَ لِلْإِنسَانِ عَدُوٌّ مُّبِينٌ
<br>“Inna sh-shayṭāna li-l-insāni ʿaduwwun mubīn.”<br>
“Verily! Shaytan is to man an open enemy.” (Qur’ān 12:05). 

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says that shayṭān is the greatest enemy. You cannot befriend shayṭān, your enemy. Even if what he says seems good, it is certainly harm for you.

Therefore, the greatest Ḥujjah, proof, is the existence of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla; there is nothing else. True existence is only the existence of Allah. We are merely shadows. The entire universe is the creation of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. Everything created is by the infinite power and wisdom of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. His power and wisdom transcend human comprehension. Because human mind has a limit. But no limit or boundary can be conceived for Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. This is an absolute and certain truth: Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla is the creator of every moment, the world, the hereafter, time and space, everything.

Everything that has been created is nothing compared to the power of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. But looking at the people today, they are bewildered. They are striving to destroy themselves. That is, they are ruining themselves. Because if a person who has entered this deviant way does not repent and come to their senses, they are doomed and destroyed forever.

May Allah protect us. May Allah guide all people, inshā’Allāh. Because this ignorance is such a vile thing that it is worse than scabies; it spreads rapidly from one person to another. It constantly spreads from one to another, and from one to another. Scabies has a cure; but for ignorance, if the person does not repent, it has no cure.

May Allah protect us; may He protect our children and all of us from this ignorance and the traps of shayṭān, inshā’Allāh. May Allah protect us.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq

Al-Fātiḥah.

There are Qur’ān Khatms, Yasin, ayāt, surahs, tasbihāt, tahlilāt, salawāt, khatms of Dalail al-Khayrāt, read by our brothers and sent from all over the world.

We gift them first to the soul of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, his family and companions, all the prophets, awliyā, asfiya, and mashāyikh.

We gift them to the souls of all our ancestors.

May it be a means for happiness here and hereafter. May it be a means for the strength of our īmān, inshā’Allāh. May whatever good goals there is be attained.

Li-Llahi Ta’āla, Al-Fātiḥah.
