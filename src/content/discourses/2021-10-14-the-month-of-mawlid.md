Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

With the Barakah of Rabi’ al-Awwal, the month of birth of our Holy Prophet, may there be cure to the people and sick ones insha’Allah. We hope that this oppression is lifted off the nation and the sun of Islam rises insha’Allah. We hope so. Shukr to Allah, Allah created us from this beautiful nation of our Holy Prophet. Allah created us as the Ummah of our Holy Prophet. That is a great favor. Whoever knows its value is favored both here and hereafter. And whoever doesn’t know it is unlucky. There is benefit neither for his Dunya, nor for Akhirah; only harm for both. Whoever is with our Holy Prophet deserves the biggest favors and virtues.

Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla says, ‘ ءُ ﺎﺸَ َﯾ ﻦﻣَ ﮫِ ﯿ ِﺗﺆْ ُﯾ ِ�ﱠ ﻞُ ﻀْ َﻓ ﻚَ ﻟِ َذ’, ‘Dhalika fa luLlāhi yū’tīhi man yashā’u’, ‘That is the bounty of Allah, which He gives to whom He wills.’ (Qur’ān 62:04). He gives this virtuous favor to whomever He likes. When you deserveḍ this virtue, you are from the lucky servants of Allah, you are from beloved people of Allah. You cannot go against His ruling. You cannot ask “Why?” and “What for?” People should not ask so. Muslims know this Adab. But non-Muslims try to mislead others like themselves. They show bad ways to people: “Follow your ego and see how much freedom there is”. Their freedom is only in bad deeds. There is unlimited freedom in bad deeds. And they prevent doing good deeds. They can prevent as much as they want, they have no chance. If they had a chance, they would be on this way, on the way of Allah, on the beautiful way of our Holy Prophet.

We are happy that mercy came to us and our worlds during this month of Mawlid. We celebrate the day of Mawlid of our Holy Prophet. This day is such a holy day that Sayyidina Süleyman Çelebi said that it is as valuable as Laylat al-Qadr. It is at the same level. And for those who follow him, it is the day of joy. Shukr to Allah, for the honor of this holy day, insha’Allah Himmah will come to us and there will be protection for the Ummah from the evil of shaytan. Allah will send the Sahib for the honor of this day insha’Allah. May Allah give cure to the sick ones. May He protect us from all possible evil and badness. May He treat us with goodness and give us goodness insha’Allah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
