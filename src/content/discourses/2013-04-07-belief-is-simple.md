Shaykh Bahauddin, sohbat April 7, 2013.

Allhumma salli wa sallim ala Nabina Muhammad alayhi salam Salatan tadumu wa tuhda ilayh Mammarra layali wa tul ad dawam Allhumma salli wa sallim ala Nabina Muhammad alayhi salam Salatan tadumu wa tuhda ilayh Mammarra layali wa tul ad dawam Allhumma salli wa sallim ala Nabina Muhammad alayhi salam Salatan tadumu wa tuhda ilayh Mammarra layali wa tul ad dawam.

It's like opera. Italian people like opera, I make for them. Pavarotti. Masha Allah as long as people coming in, I will make more opera. Alhamdulillah, that the people make people happy. And at same time the people make people sad. So everything built on people. Allah Almighty, He built this planet for people. He didn't build the moon for people or the sun, we will be burning there. But, He built us such a nice place to live in, and this is the sign to move for better as paradise. We can make here, there's so many people they say, the hell and the paradise is here. Actually they are right in one point, they can turn their life to hell or they can turn their life to heaven. But that doesn't mean it is here.

Allah Almighty give you the option to put it in your hand. He give you this to be in your hand. But so many people think, 'No,no,no, there is no akhirah, no Eternity, no nothing'. And they say, 'Do you know? Who told you, anybody gone and come back and tell there is another life there?' And we very simply answer, 'We are Believers'. Very simple. You don't have to make it complicated, you don't have to make argue, nothing, no need. Alhamdulillah, we are Believers.

Sayyidina Ali, in Baghdad, was every Friday meeting people in the market, in the bazar. So many people coming and asking so many questions to him, and he was answering because Sayyidina Ali was the door of knowledge-city. That's why everyone coming asking, there was 'Eyy Ali!' There's no respect Ya Amir al Mu'minin, this is the King of Believers, and he was so humble, he was having small tent, and small sajjadah, sitting in it and everyone coming and making argument with him. And he was saying 'My mission to bring people to Iman, to Islam'.

He say, there's 40 priests coming to him, and this, they been saying to Sayyidina Ali (as), 'We heard about your miracle, we heard about your knowledge, we are hearing about your experience and how you are treating people, and we have a few questions to ask and then we will decide our belief'. And, the first test was, they brought one bread, big bread and they take this bread and throw it in the water. They break it and throw it in the water. And after that the bread becoming not-bread anymore. And they been telling him, 'Which part is which part of this bread? Can your Lord know what is every part belonging? He say "Its very simple, say, 'Bismillahi'r Rahmani'r Rahim', and he put his hand in the water, take the bread all these pieces and make it one piece again and give it to them. 'This is the bread, any piece you want, is here' ". They say, 'Maybe it's magic. Doesn't matter, I'm coming for the second question'. And the second question it was more as knowledge. It was not to show miracle or thing like this. They say, 'We believe that Paradise and Hell is in this planet, after we die there is nothing. If we are rich people and having good life that means we are in Paradise. But if we are poor people and have miserable life and don't know what to do and these people appointed to be in Hell. How you explain this?'

Sayyidina Ali, very simple. He say, 'Did you look at the dead body? Say, Yes of course, we are burying people. This is our job'. Good job. Say, 'You are burying a lot of dead bodies'? Say 'Yes'. He say, 'Did you have any difference between the one he was in Hell and the one he was in Heaven according to your belief?' ‘All look same’. So he say, 'That means the result is same. When you make the result it's equal, no change'. Say 'Yes. Ahh.. now I believe you'. He say, Sayyidina Ali, so simple, 'I believe, that is what you believe but if my belief come true, what you will do? And, it is true'. ash'hadu an la ilaha illa Allah wa ash'hadu anna Muhammadan abduhu wa rasuluhu 'Today I am believer, I believe what you believe, because I know how is the ending of these dead bodies, nothing, but the second stage I don't know'. He say 'You have to be careful about what you know and what you don't know'.

We know Allah is haqq and the Prophecy is haqq, the Books are haqq, the Malaikah are haqq. Everything been sent from Allah Almighty is haqq. We know this is haqq. Then 40 of his friends also accept shahadah and they been one of the greatest distributor for Iman and Islam. Masha Allah it was very big job, they done very great job. So that's why it's good to have connection with so many people. We don't have to be rude, we don't have to be insisting in our things. You have to be knowing that if somebody happy with what he got, try to go from his side of his happiness. Don't go from your side, maybe it will be hitting each other. So he run away.

Mawlana Shaykh was since all history going all around the world. He can sit here and be waiting for people, he never do this. He was going to every door, knocking every door, not to say, 'Ya Rabbi, one day I was not making my job properly'. He went everywhere, he speak with the youngs, he speak with the children, with the elderly, with the seniors, with his age people, with women, children, with men. He speak with everybody. Why? Say this is my job, I have to do it.

Until now masha Allah he is still continuing. He say 'If I have power in my body I'll still continue in my mission'. And we are asking from Allah Almighty to give him more power and more power and more power that he will be in our head. I am sitting here like artificial, I'm not the true one. He is the true one, he is the true one. I am only part-time shaykh. I been paid also part-time, never full-paid. But it's good salary. Everyone is happy to have good salary. Even when I was kid, Grandshaykh used to give me every morning, salary. So one day I come to Grandshaykh and say I kiss his hand and Grandshaykh asking me 'You kiss my hand for money or for Allah?' My mother she was beside, say 'for Allah, for Allah.' Small boy, very proud of course I am guaranteeing I'll get money. Very sure, listening to my mother, say, 'For Allah'. 'For Allah.' Grandshaykh say, 'Go, Allah give you'. I went out, come back again, kiss his hand, very sure again about my answer, and Shaykh also again asking, 'You kiss my hand for Allah or for money?' Very sure about my answer now, my mother also, 'For Allah, for Allah'. 'For money'. Ok, taking.. unh huh, it was only 10 cents. 'Now I got the correct answer. This is your money.' I take my money, very proud of taking my money.

I told my father this story. He was laughing Mawlana Shaykh and he said, 'Alhamdulillah, you been taking two side, from dunya and you take the akhirah side'. So now I'm trying to teach you how to deal between dunya and akhirah so there is no harm to deal with your ordinary works and be stronger on financial side and don't forget the spiritual side that is also in need to be more powerful and as long as you are dervish in your inner life, you can be strong financially in outside life, there is no harm. Allah Almighty like His servant to be in very good places as long as he is working and going on. But he doesn't like him to be trapped in dunya.

That's why say, 'you have to be dervish inside'. If they say to me you sit on the ground maybe I'll object little bit but finally sit on the ground. I'll leave this chair, but, it's good to have the ability to go up and down without any hard feeling. And I was on the first, first tip with Mawlana Shaykh, like two months journey in Far East. And we been visiting Sultan of Brunei, it was extreme riches and in a few days we went to Sri Lanka the extreme, ground. And Mawlana Shaykh asked me one question. 'How you been thinking? What do you think about this gap?' 'For me this richness doesn't make any excitement. And about this poor side also doesn't make me sad'. 'Hmm. So you don't feel anything about 2 of them?' ‘Actually not feeling, really feeling, but I asked Allah Almighty to protect His Majesty and to be more mercy for His poor servants. But both of them they are heavy with what they got. For me what I got make me happy’. Mawlana Shaykh, Alhamdulillah give me good nod, say, 'Perfect'.

This is only to be sense of humour, to make you understand what you got is the best. Cannot be better than this. And this, it make us thank Allah Almighty from real sincere, thanking Allah Almighty with the sincere, because you say, 'Ya Rabbi what I got now is the best. Alhamdulillah, is the best what I got'. If You give me more it will be nicer. But if you keep me here also very good, but please don't let me down. And if you keep thanking Allah Almighty will keep giving you. If you keep satisfied with what you have Allah Almighty will be satisfied with you and you will be in maqam radi'allahu anhum wa radu anh. That means Allah Almighty was very pleased with them and they be very pleased with Allah Almighty.

That's very important sentence, radi'allahu anhum wa radu anh. This is very big maqam. It is very easy to reach that maqam, only be satisfied. And say, Alhamdulillah shukr Ya Rabbi shukr, shukr Ya Rabbi shukr, you can add like me tawba Ya Rabbi tauba, Astaghfirullah. I'm tawba one and that's why it is very important to be satisfied with what Allah Almighty give you and be shakireen wa min Allahi tawfiq bi hurmat al Habib bi hurmat al Fatiha.

Subhanim Allah Sultnaim Allah Nabim Muhammad Alayhi salam Subhanim Allah Sultanim Allah Nabim Muhammad Alayhi salam Subhanim Allah Sultanim Allah Nabim Muhammad Alayhi salam
