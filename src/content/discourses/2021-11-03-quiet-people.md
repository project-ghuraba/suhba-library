Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

A person in Islam has a comfortable life. He arranges his actions according to it. Otherwise, he runs after whatever his ego wants. No matter how much you give to your ego, it is never full. It plays with you like a toy. A Muslim person is good, Shukr to Allah ﷻ. The more he is closer to Allah ﷻ, the better and more beautiful it is for him. May Allah ﷻ be pleased with all of you.

Our father-in-law passed away. He was a simple man. He was practicing Islam as much as he could. He hadn’t studied and hadn’t learnt it. But he was an Anatolian man who was given this by his family and the community. He performed hajj and used to pray. And he would pay Zakah by asking us to calculate for him, and he would give it as well. Shukr to Allah ﷻ, there are millions of people like him who make Dua to Allah ﷻ so that Islam goes on in this country. Because of such quiet people like him, Allah ﷻ protects these people and the Muslim generation goes on. There are many noisy shaytans opposite to them who raise hell for the smallest thing. But people like him are patient people who stay quiet, and they trust in Allah ﷻ.

Therefore, Shukr to Allah ﷻ, this country is the head of Islam and the power of Islam insha’Allah. Islam will rise here again. And it will become hope for all of the oppressed ones with the permission of Allah ﷻ. It is their hope. When Mahdi ‘alayhi s-salam appears insha’Allah, all the oppression will come to an end. Oppression will not last with the permission of Allah ﷻ. Allah ﷻ is with the truth. As we said, people opposite to them are not millions but a minority. However, they are trying to mislead everyone. They are shaytans. And whoever is with shaytan will lose. Who is with Allah ﷻ will win.

Therefore, such people will be more insha’Allah. Their Duas will raise and keep this country and the Islamic world insha’Allah. They are quiet people. When they stay quiet, you think that everything is over. No, with the permission of Allah ﷻ, Allah ﷻ is with the truth. When the time comes, the truth will come out insha’Allah. May we see those days insha’Allah. May Allah ﷻ give strength to our iman. May He give us safety. May Allah ﷻ protect us from the evil, Fitnah and all kinds of troubles of this world insha’Allah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
