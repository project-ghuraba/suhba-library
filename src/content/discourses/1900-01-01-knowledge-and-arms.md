Mawlana Shaykh Nazim al Haqqani an-Naqshbandi sohbat

Inshallah this talk will provide a spiritual support for these young gentlemen, our children. Those who are present in this assembly will experience a tajalli/manifestation that will strengthen their faith. Allah Almighty is the Absolute Master of Islam. The Absolute Master of this religion is Allah, and true guidance is from Allah Almighty. However, some servants were chosen as a means to guide other servants. That is why our advice to these young ones is to have the attribute "walood". Walood means those who teach and rear children. Walood means to take people from a level of unbelief to a level of faith.

To take people from unbelief to iman/faith, even if it's only one person, that person will have the highest rank in Allah's presence. And you are young ones with a high level of education. You have studied some positive knowledge. And all knowledge informs us about Allah. Any information that does not inform about Allah is not knowledge. Use the different branches of your knowledge along with your spirituality to seek the ways to, and from Allah.

They are saying "researcher". Researcher. Instead of researcher it would be better to say "explorer", because man must search and explore to find, not just research. He must research and explore. You have studied the different branches of knowledge by searching and exploring. You must find the ways that reach Allah Almighty in order to stop falsehood. Weapons and physical strength are for those who have no minds.

Our Master, alayhi s-salatu wa s-salam, used reason for 13 years to overcome the disbelievers. That was the order. For 13 years he invited them to faith using the springs of knowledge. For 13 years. After 13 years, those who didn't surrender to knowledge and logic and to the contrary, used brutal force against logic and reason, at that time, it was said: Take the sword O My Habib. The order was given. However, for 13 years there was no permission to use the sword.

It was when the disbelievers and supporters of falsehood opposed all the ways of knowledge and logic, when they wanted to oppose logic and reason using brutal strength and force, Allah Almighty gave the order: O My Habib, now you can take the sword because they do not accept the ways of reason and logic and will not surrender to Haqq/Truth through them. Perhaps they are advancing to overcome Haqq with brutal strength. Now, you should oppose and defeat them to avoid their harm. Break their wings and arms. Remove their poisonous teeth and claws so that they cannot harm the people of Haqq. Take your sword. And so, our Prophet alaihi s-salatu wa s-salam, took it. Now, the powers in this time against Islam are fake powers, corrupt powers. They want to remove Islam and Muslims by using brutal strength. And now, in order to repel them, the time has not yet come for us to use physical strength because we need the one who will give that order. You or me, we cannot give that order. The one who will give that order will lead all the Muslims. Authority is for the one who holds full power. Power/authority cannot be shared. That is the rule in Islam. The one in authority cannot have a partner. They say "associates in power", that is nonsense.

Therefore, our Master, alayhi s-salatu wa s-salam in Madina al-Munawwara, he held physical and spiritual power to destroy falsehood. He declared war against falsehood and the people of falsehood. He drew the Holy Banner. The jihad we must make now is a jihad by the way of knowledge. We must use the method that the Prophet used in Mecca. If we go to Madina, which means that if there is a place only you can rule, then that is a different situation. In Madina, there weren't any polytheist disbelievers. They did not rule or have any power. Since the people and the sultanate was in the hands of the Muslims, divine permission was granted to use force against the brutal force of the unbelievers.

We must perform our jihad by way of knowledge. We will disgrace unbelief with knowledge. We shall make them miserable and wretched so that they are not able to show their faces to anyone, so that they cannot live amongst the Muslims, so they will get out and leave. You will defeat them without using weapons, only using the weapon of knowledge. Your sincerity will defeat them.

Very well, when you have a valuable teacher like this one, he will help you through spiritual ways and the physical and spiritual help of many more people will reach you. You must try to achieve that high level of spirituality which will overcome and disgrace unbelievers. Success is from Allah.

Bi hurmati l-Habib bi hurmati l-Fatiha.
