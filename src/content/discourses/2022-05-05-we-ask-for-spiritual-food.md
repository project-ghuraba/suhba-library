As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

The apostles told Sayyidunā ‘Īsá, "Ask AllāhNone to send a table for us. Let us eat and make our hearts content. Let us trust your words and believe more." They said this to Sayyidunā ‘Īsá ‘alayhi s-salām. These people were the apostles of Sayyidunā ‘Īsá. They were his ṣaḥābah, the closest people to him and they said this. They asked for rizq (provision) from AllāhNone to strengthen their belief. Therefore, our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says, "The ‘ulamā’ of myNone ummah are like the prophets of the Banī Isrā’īl." Of course, they can't reach the rank of prophets but they are like them. Because ṣaḥābah believed, they didn't ask for anything. They didn't ask for proof. But despite being near Sayyidunā ‘Īsá for so long, the apostles still had this request. It means their īmān was far behind. Only when they are prophets is their īmān complete. Then comes level of īmān of their apostles which was very weak.

Sayyidunā ‘Īsá ‘alayhi s-salām always showed miracles. He had not thousands but tens of thousands, hundreds of thousands of miracles. Yet they asked for this thing. Shukr to AllāhNone. We make shukr to AllāhNone for being from the ummah of MuḥammadNone. There is a table. Here is the table of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. May there be a table for our īmān, not for eating and drinking like them. May there be food for our īmān, food for our souls. This is what we ask for. Shukr to AllāhNone, people are running here, because it [the table] is here. You came here, so may AllāhNone be pleased. This is the table of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, in shā’a Llāh. It will be food for our souls. Those who came here won't go empty-handed, in shā’a Llāh. They will go back with īmān, Islām, and with their good wishes coming true, in shā’a Llāh, because they came for the pleasure of AllāhNone, not for eating and drinking, not for anything else. They came just for AllāhNone, the ProphetNone, and for the love of Awliyā’ and Shaykh. May AllāhNone be pleased with you. Welcome.

This the disciples of Sayyidunā ‘Īsá ‘alayhi s-salām, they were asking him for dinner – food to... They ask if their Lord, "Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, can send you food for us to eat, make table, dinner. We need," they said, "for to be, to make our belief stronger, to be sure." This is what they ask. And for this, they're disciple, meaning like ṣaḥābah for Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. And you can compare between them and between ṣaḥābah how their belief it is very weak, very, very weak.

Because everything they, AllāhNone give them. In this world, what you eat, what you drink – from AllāhNone. So, they was but asking also, "If they bring food for us to eat and to be our heart satisfied and belief – we believe you." For this, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, " ‘Ulamā’u ummatī ka-anbiyā’i Banī Isrā’īl." "MyNone nation's – ummat, scholar – ‘ālim, awliyā’, believer, they like... look like prophets of Banī Isrā’īl." Because only prophets in Banī Isrā’īl, they was has hundred percent īmān – belief. Other, they don't have. They only... they want to proof and this disciple of prophet ‘Īsá ‘alayhi s-salām, they see thousand, ten-thousand, hundred-thousand of miracles of Sayyidunā ‘Īsá ‘alayhi s-salām. And after this, they will "to make our belief more sure" they said, "We need to eat to believe." This is what they said.

For this, ya‘nī, Sayyidunā ‘Īsá he was really, or all prophets of Banī Isrā’īl, they was suffering. But what they do? Only they accept and make du‘ā’ for them. If any want any miracle they want, they make du‘ā’. Even for this dinner they make du‘ā’ – Sayyidunā ‘Īsá ‘alayhi s-salām. And ten thousands of people they was eating from this dinner. And after this, also they was not sure again. For this, Sayyidunā ‘Īsá said: "AllāhNone will send this for you. if you not believe after this, HeNone will be punish you."

But, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, for our belief, we are thanking AllāhNone we are from nation of Prophet Muḥammad ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. We are believe only we need food for our soul, for our belief. This is what we want. Not asking for to send us table with food – with bulgur, chicken, fish, like this. No. We are only asking to give our soul food from spiritual food. And this is our main aim, not for this. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, this is from HisNone favour "Hādhā min faḍli Rabbī" (27:40). Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. And this is what today you are here. Many of coming of you from everywhere, from around world, to visit this place. You are not coming for food, not coming for drink, not coming for enjoy. Only all your aim – to come and to be get spiritual food for your spiritual to be more stronger. And AllāhNone accept your ziyārah. AllāhNone give you strong īmān, strong belief, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone give you barakah also.

AllāhNone, when you ask for food, spiritual food, AllāhNone give you everything. Nothing... It is the most... most precious thing. If you have strong belief, in this world, everything it is nothing – no difficult, no sadness, no anything if they have this, people. This is very important. With food only maybe you can be more fat, but with this, everything it will be easy for you. In shā’a Llāh, AllāhNone give us with your barakah, barakah of Mawlānā, accept your ziyārah. AllāhNone help us, in shā’a Llāh, all time to be in right way all time, in shā’a Llāh.
