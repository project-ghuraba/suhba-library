As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Mawlānā Shaykh was giving an order to begin the day with ṣuḥbah, nasīḥah, advice; for the sake of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. People of old times didn’t have many things to make them busy.

Now everybody has a television in their home. And we now have what’s worse than the television.

In old times, when we were small, alḥamdulillāh we didn’t have a television in our house. But later there was a television that closed at eleven o’clock, before midnight. Now they have this device, internet and computers and they are staying up until Fajr. After that - many don’t pray of course - but those who pray come to us and say, “O Shaykh, pray for us! We cannot wake up for Fajr.” Of course, you cannot wake up. If you’re sleeping at 12, 1 or 2 o’clock, how will you wake up for Fajr! You cannot.

For this, you must put a limit for everything. Freedom is not good. You must know this. Don’t give everything to your ego, nafs.

If you want to wake up for Fajr, sleep early. Mawlānā was asked what’s the best time of sleep. He said between 11 and 3 o’clock. In this time, many people are awake till after 12 or 1 a.m. Some are staying awake until 3. They sleep after 3. This sleep is not enough for our body. We need to rest our body and our mind also.

Allah made this prayer for you at that time for taḥajjud, for Fajr, but you are sleeping during that time. For this, you must put a limit for everything. Don’t give freedom to what your nafs, your ego wants.

Of course, you cannot prevent a hundred percent; it’s difficult. But don’t surrender yourself to everything your nafs, your ego wants, and give it. If you do this, your body, your soul will be happy. And when they’re happy, you’ll have power to control yourself and your body will be healthy, inshā’Allāh.

May Allah make us do this, and may it be easy for everyone, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
