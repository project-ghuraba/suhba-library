As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa raḥmatu Llāhi wa barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīn wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

Mashāyikh sometimes ask "What did you do for AllāhNone today?" Sometimes they write it down in some places. They ask, "What did you do for AllāhNone today?" You should have done some goodness and remember it. Think whether you did something good or something for the sake of AllāhNone. We should do so. But even better is, as our ProphetNone said: Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm, "Qul inna ṣalātī wa nusukī wa maḥyāya wa mamātī liLlāhi rabbi l-‘ālamīna lā sharīka lahu wa bidhālika umirtu wa-ana awwalu l-muslimīn" (6:162-163). "MyNone prayers and worshipping, myNone life and death, everything is for AllāhNone. I am ordered so," says our ProphetNone. HeNone says, "I am the first Muslim." And like our ProphetNone did, Muslims shouldn't ask what they did during the day but make intention from the morning when they wake up and head to work to do everything for the pleasure of AllāhNone.

In shā’a Llāh, as AllāhNone likes and wants it. HeNone says, "We intend to do everything for the pleasure of AllāhNone." Then everything we do will be accepted. Whatever you do – when you go after provision, when you go after knowledge, when you go for a visit, when you go for a trip, when you talk to people – everything you do will be for AllāhNone. When you ask, "What did I do today?", better to be able to say, "I made intention in the morning to do everything for AllāhNone, to obey HimNone." This is the shortcut. We shouldn't forget it every day so that when we do wrong during the day, we realize we won't deserve the pleasure of AllāhNone and be embarrassed from Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla. Then we remember not to do it. But apart from that, whatever you do, eat, drink, go somewhere, or come, as we said, AllāhNone will accept it. "We did everything for AllāhNone, not for our ego.

AllāhNone sent us to this world not to be a servant for our ego, but to be a servant for AllāhNone. We intend to do servanthood for AllāhNone. For HisNone pleasure, we intend our position to be like this from morning till evening, in shā’a Llāh." Everything will be for the pleasure of AllāhNone then. AllāhNone will give according to our intentions. "Innamā l-a‘mālu bi n-niyyāt." Actions are according to intentions. People make intentions. Most people can't accomplish them but as long as there is intention, that intention is accepted with AllāhNone. Because HeNone knows our intentions. Someone decides to do something, he promises to do it, and when he doesn't, he says he was going to. Others ask, "Why didn't you do?" "I was intending to. It didn't work out." They say, "I don't benefit from intentions." But Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla is not this way. Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla accepts our intentions. If you do it – ten rewards. If you couldn't do it, HeNone still gives one reward.

Therefore, we make intention, in shā’a Llāh, to live all our lives for AllāhNone – until the end. And every day, in shā’a Llāh, when we wake up in the morning, we intend it for Allāh's pleasure. May we be able to achieve our intentions, in shā’a Llāh.

We intend so, in shā’a Llāh. They ask there is a word – "What you done today for AllāhNone for your Lord?" This is many place they write. Even in this wall. In sometimes in book and some place – "what you done good thing today?" What you normally for Muslim, "What you done for your Lord for AllāhNone?" "What you done for AllāhNone?" You give charity, you pray, you fast, you do this and that – this all you must do. So we can say, "We can do, we do this." But there is more important than this. What āyah for ProphetNone saying, Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm, "Qul inna ṣalātī wa nusukī wa maḥyāya wa mamātī liLlāhi rabbi l-‘ālamīn, lā sharīka lahu wa bi-dhālika umirtu wa ana awwalu l-muslimīn" (6:162-163). "My praying, my fasting, my doing good thing, charity, my life and my death – all this for Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla. For no any partner for HimNone and this is what I was ordered and I'm the first of believer."

This is for ProphetNone teaching us. So no need to say, "Today I done this, I did this." You must intention to be whole your day for Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla, for your Lord. "We intention this morning we wake up for fajr, our intention to be all my days according for Allāh'sNone order and to do good thing until next day. This is my intention." You can, so it is like whole thing what you done in your day, for Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla and HeNone accept because it is intention. Intention very important. This is what you done in daytime: go wake up, eating, drinking, praying, and meeting people, speaking, working. teaching people, you learn something from people – everything AllāhNone reward you as you done for HimNone. This is what ProphetNone teach us. Our whole life for Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla. Who do this, intention for whole day for AllāhNone, AllāhNone reward him. Not to for our ego, no. This day for AllāhNone.

Even ego trying to do not make you to do something, also AllāhNone reward you by for your intention. Because intention very important in divinely, in presence of Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla. HeNone said if you have good intention, and your intention to do something I couldn't do, it will be reward you one reward. If you done it, you will reward ten times, hundred times, or more. But for people, human being, you intention something to do for people, and tell them, "I was intention to do this but I couldn't do this. Sorry." They will become angry for you. "Why you say this and you didn't do and we are waiting for you and come you didn't come, you didn't..." They get very angry. They not even ask for something from them. "What? Why we will give you this? You didn't done anything." "But my intention good to, I'm was intention to do this for you." But nothing hear. But for Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla not like this. If you even you didn't do anything, HeNone reward you but because HeNone not in need for you. People in need for you.

So it is good tiding from ProphetNone. Whole days, whole life, in shā’a Llāh, for our Lord. We intention to be in HisNone service, to be HisNone servant, and to be our whole life for HimNone, only for HimNone not for our ego. AllāhNone help us to to do this and reward us, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
