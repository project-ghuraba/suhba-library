As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

They said, "Fi t-ta’annī as-salāmah, fi l-‘ajalah an-nadāmah." It is a saying of our grandfathers. I don't know if it is ḥadīth or not. "Haste causes regret." Things done in a hurry mostly end up in regret. Some things happen that can't be done again. Ta’annī means when you do something with careful thinking, it will be safe. You won't feel regret. They say it is better to be late and more sure so that it will not harm anyone. This is applicable to people individually and to everything in Ṭarīqah, Sharī‘ah and all dunyā matters.

Approach advice with care so that you don't say later, "If only I had done this... " Some things can be corrected but some things can be done just once, you cannot do it over. Therefore, we should be careful about things we are doing and will do. It should be done with care and not in a hurry. "Al-'ajalatu mina sh-shayṭān." Haste is from shayṭān. Doing things in a hurry, not quickly, sometimes confuses people. They do something and regret afterwards. You should hurry only for good deeds. Saying: "Good deeds I will delay until tomorrow. I will do it later. I will do it tomorrow. I will pray later. I will do this worship afterwards." It is better to hurry in doing these things. But in matters of dunyā, no need to hurry. You can hurry as much as you can, it remains where it is. You will go, dunyā will stay. But the more you hurry for your Ākhirah, the more benefit you get.

May AllāhNone protect us. May we have a chance to do good deeds, in shā’a Llāh.

There is a saying, or maybe Ḥadīth, or saying of wisdom: "Fi t-ta’annī as-salāmah, fi l-‘ajalah an-nadāmah." Meaning to be thinking, making slow, don't be very hurry, it is, you be safe. If you are hurry, you're doing fast, quickly without thinking, you'll be regret. So everything you must be careful. Don't be hurry to take decision or to make acting to do this, do that, without thinking. You must think. After, you do what you will do. Not what when you, you want, you coming idea for you, quickly to do. No. You must ask: how it is, it will be good for me or benefit for me, or we can take good thing after this what we do? If you do fast, maybe you lose something. Maybe you do something you cannot, only once you can do, and you do it and finish. After this, because you do this, you will be regret. So for to be not regret, think. Don't be hurry for this dunyā or material thing. But for job, for work, for anything in this your life, be careful. Hurry with good thing. Don't say, "When I'm getting old I will go Ḥajj," or "Later I can pray," or "Later I fast, later I give charity." This don't be wait. Be hurry with this. Other thing, it's for material thing, don't be hurry because as much you are hurry, it is still there, not going anywhere. But for Ākhirah, for charity, for good thing, for worshipping, you will be, they are not waiting for you. Dunyā waiting, still you go, millions of people they're going. Billions of people since thousands of years they're going. Dunyā still there. But what they take with them, what they hurry to collect from reward, from charity, from worship for themself – this is very important. But for dunyā it is not. You must be careful to not be cheated with people, to not be cheated with shayṭān, to not be cheated with your ego. Be careful, don't be no-mind. Be clever and look for this.

AllāhNone save us from shayṭān and his follower and our ego, in shā’a Llāh. Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
