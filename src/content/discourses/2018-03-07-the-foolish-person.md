Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya

Who is the clever person? The clever person is the one who believes in Allah (JJ). Those who do not believe can claim to be as clever as they like. They are foolish, blind, and deaf. Because everything proves His existence. It is a trend now for people to say, “I beleive like this and not like that.” They say so for people to respect them more. They do not use their minds to gain people’s respect and they submit to them, they submit to Shaitan. They can be as highly educated as they want with as many professorships, associate professorships, or doctorates, but none of them is beneficial. A person who has never been educated, who only knows a couple of words, and only knows two verses is a thousand times, a hundred million times better than them. Because his end would be good. Those who are arrogant and do not recognize Allah are of no benefit, and their end would be bad.

Because it is End Times these are times when disbelief (kufr) is the darkest. The whole world has declared Muslimism as the enemy and has waged war on it. They are saying, “We are going to finish it.” They also have their own men who present themselves as Muslims to people, whereas they are people who have nothing to do with being Muslim. They are people who try to harm Muslimism but they cannot harm it. Islam is under the protection of Allah. They will all end in loss and they will be defeated Inshallah. All their efforts will come to nothing.

May Allah grant us true faith. Iman is the most important thing. There is nothing more important. Even if the whole world were yours, it would have no value as long as you have no faith. It would be enough if you have nothing but faith. This world is not lasting so they call it zawal (perishing). It will end and it will nothing will be left of it. The true life is the eternal life. Those who are prepared for it are the winners. Otherwise, as we said, they are foolish. It changes by the blink of an eye. Could people who value it and waste themselves be considered clever? They are fools.

May Allah give us all good thought and contemplation. It says an hour of contemplation is better than 60 years of worship. Therefore, Inshallah we become from the clever so we obey what Allah commands, love those whom He loves, and dislike those whom He dislikes Inshallah.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq. Al-Fatiha.
