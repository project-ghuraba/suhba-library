As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh. A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Wa ṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Bi-qawmin yuḥibbuhum wa-yuḥibbūnahū" (5:54). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘aẓīm. There are some people, men who love AllāhNone and whom AllāhNone loves. This is the important thing. Shukr to AllāhNone, this is our Shaykh’s way – to make us love AllāhNone and for AllāhNone to love us too. This beautiful thing makes us men. That's it. May AllāhNone raise his rank, throughout his whole life, he wanted to teach this to people. To make them love AllāhNone. When they love AllāhNone, AllāhNone loves them. The person who loves AllāhNone wins. He wins everything. This world is not important. What is important for them is the love of AllāhNone, the love of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. That love is the most precious thing.

People can learn it from anywhere, but you need a person, a man in front of you to show you this way. Shukr to AllāhNone, our Shaykh Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim taught this to the whole world. Whoever learned this has won. Thousands, tens of thousands, hundreds of thousands, millions of people are on this way. Their rewards also go to our Shaykh, Shukr to AllāhNone. People, who are guided by means of him, are written in his records. Therefore, this is a big door, the door of our Holy ProphetNone. People who came to this way were chosen by AllāhNone. It is by the will of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. HeNone opened their hearts and put them on this way. If not for the will of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, you would not be on this way. Some people waste their lives and leave without ever going on these ways. And some hold onto a way just because that way will benefit them in something. That way becomes a broken way and it becomes worse. Everything they do becomes unnecessary and their deeds go in vain. Therefore, people who are on this way are the people chosen by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. There are millions of people, billions of people. Among them, the chosen people are few. They have been granted that. It has not been granted to others.

As we said, this way is the way of love of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, the way of love and affection. “Al-ḥubbu fī Llāh, wa-l-bughḍu fī Llāh.” It is to love for the sake of AllāhNone and to hate for the sake of AllāhNone. Whom do we hate? We hate shayṭān and those who are with shayṭān. If they leave the shayṭān, there are some who do so. Just because you were with shayṭān, the Door of Repentance is not closed, it is open. If you repent, you will attain the love and affection of AllāhNone. That love will save you too. When Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim was sick, he said that if shayṭān, himself, says this, he also will be saved. “Subḥānim AllāhNone, Sultānim AllāhNone, Nabīm Muḥammad ‘alayhi s-salām.” Shaykh Efendi said that if shayṭān says this, he will be saved also.

This means believing in AllāhNone and loving the ProphetNone is a means to salvation. So this door has not yet been closed. It is open for everyone. It also shows the greatness of Allāh'sNone mercy. The door is not closed to anyone. There is no such thing as, “You are in Hell. You are in Paradise.” What is important is that after repenting and coming to the right way, everyone will be saved from Hell with Allāh’sNone permission and will enter Paradise with Allāh’sNone permission. May AllāhNone be pleased with him and may his rank be high. Shukr to AllāhNone his himmah (spiritual aspiration) is continuous. His karāmāt (miracles) are continuous. It has not been cut and will not be cut, in shā’a Llāh. This way is a continuous way. The way that is cut is not good. This is not a way that ends. It is a never-ending way, in shā’a Llāh.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying in Qur’ān: "Bi-qawmin yuḥibbuhum wa-yuḥibbūnahū” (5:54). Meaning, ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘aẓīm, āyah: "There is men, who they love AllāhNone and AllāhNone love them." This is real men. Real one, these people. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are in his place, in his dergah. Which, one of them is Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. He give us this lesson to love AllāhNone. Love of AllāhNone, it is the most important thing. Mawlānā Shaykh, he was teaching this all his life. And al-ḥamdu liLlāh, he has thousand, tens of hundred thousands, millions of people who honoured by this teaching, what he teach us. And they won the biggest prize. It is not a prize you can win and after you forget. This is forever.

To love AllāhNone. It is very important. To not give any value for material thing – this is important also. You can have everything, but you must have your heart full of love of AllāhNone, not love for dunyā, anything from dunyā. This is what Mawlānā was teaching. Because he is, since young age until end of his life, they was teaching this, not giving any value for this material thing. Because he was, everybody know his life, biography. He was when he learning in university there was even no primary school in many of cities in world. And he was the highest. Still, he left this for sake of AllāhNone, left everything and run after love of AllāhNone. And AllāhNone, HeNone love him and HeNone give him everything, mā shā’a Llāh.

This is his mission, who make people awake from their sleeping. They thought it is only this dunyā and we must run after this, not looking for ākhirah. Yes, we can, we must look for both. But not in heart. In heart, only for one, for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla and for love of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. In other, you can do anything to help your family, your country, your people, your human beings. But it is important, your heart to, must be open only for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. This is what whole life Mawlānā was teaching. Not giving any value for dunyā, for material thing. And he become, mā shā’a Llāh, in highest maqām. And he become, everybody after, even now, people continue to become for hidāyah (guidance) by him. Miracle, karāmah. And growing and growing, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are happy.

We are, we love him. We, in shā’a Llāh, who love him, he will be with him. In shā’a Llāh, we love him, we love Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. In shā’a Llāh, we will be in jannah with him, in shā’a Llāh. Don't think, it is, you don't have anything. No. You cannot do what he done. HisNone mercy, you can be, in shā’a Llāh, with him in jannah also. AllāhNone bless you. AllāhNone accept your ziyārah (visit). AllāhNone give you and other people who are remembering Mawlānā, the highest station. And keep them safe from this time of fitnah, until we reach Sayyidunā l-Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
