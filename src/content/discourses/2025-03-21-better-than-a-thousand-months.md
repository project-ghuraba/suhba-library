As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Shukr to AllāhNone, this occasion of Ramaḍān is a means to all kinds of goodness. In this holy month Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n was revealed. AllāhNone revealed the Qur’ān to our Holy ProphetNone in this month in Ramaḍān. Therefore, it is the night of Laylatu l-Qadr – the Night of Power. This night is better than a thousand months, says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.

A thousand months is the longest people can live. If you subtract childhood years, people attain all virtues of Laylatu l-Qadr is even better than that, says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Therefore, everyone looks for Laylatu l-Qadr. If it manifests to someone, it becomes the means for all kinds of goodness.

Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam relates in the ḥadīth sharīf. In the ḥadīth sharīf that we recited, a holy ṣaḥābah asked our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, “Which night is Laylatu l-Qadr?” Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam replied that Laylatu l-Qadr can be found throughout the year, but usually it is in Ramaḍān. And it often falls within the last ten days of Ramaḍān. Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam said, “If I tell you which day it is, then people will quit their prayers and just wait for that night. They won't do anything else.” Therefore, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla hid Laylatu l-Qadr so that people are always in worship and come across that night. And they surely catch it. Even if they don’t know it, people who keep their prayers surely catch it. And its benefit goes up to ākhirah. People will be surprised then and say, “I’ve caught Laylatu l-Qadr several times. But I didn’t know. I’m glad I didn’t know. So in order for me not to ask for foolish things, AllāhNone saved its virtue to ākhirah.” As its abundant rewards are in ākhirah, there will be a great beauty for people. This month of Ramaḍān has all kinds of beauties.

Therefore, people feel it as the best and most virtuous month of the year. They feel it, as one of its secrets is Laylatu l-Qadr. Fasting and saḥūr; all of them will be rewarded.

For everything you do, it’s not ten to one; it is a hundred to one, seven hundred to one, thousands to one. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla said, “INone will reward as much. INone will give the reward to those who fast,” says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. HeNone gives unlimitedly. Therefore, how lucky are Muslims. Not otherwise. How lucky are humans and Muslims. Because humans are created by AllāhNone. Belonging to a nation is something else, and it matters too, but following Allāh’sNone path is up to the individual. If he wants, he can do it. Therefore, controlling the ego and walking on the path of AllāhNone is the best thing for people. It is a blessing and benefit. We must make continuous shukr to AllāhNone day and night for being given this blessing. We must not go astray. May AllāhNone help us. May it be blessed. In shā’a Llāh,

Ramaḍān is not over yet. We’ve caught it. In shā’a Llāh, if we catch it, we will. For sure. As we said, people caught many nights without knowing. Because there are certain things that Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla created. If people catch them, their wishes are fulfilled. Even worldly ones. Things of ākhirah are fulfilled too.

Therefore, if we catch it, Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says the best du‘ā’ you can make is, “Allāhumma innī as’aluka l-‘afwa wa-l-‘āfiyata wa-l-mu‘āfāta d-dā’imah fī d-dīni wa-d-dunyā wa-l-ākhirah.” Ask for forgiveness from AllāhNone. Ask for forgiveness, health and well-being. May our well-being be steady and lasting. Well-being is one of the greatest favours. Health and well-being are great favours. Not to be a burden to anyone, to serve on the path of AllāhNone, for your worship to last, that is important. We must always ask so. People’s minds ask for money, land, cars and other things. You may ask for them. But the most important is this du‘ā’. Who makes this du‘ā’ certainly catches it. And he spends it with health and wellness. And AllāhNone will forgive us, n shā’a Llāh.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are again saying, we are in holy blessed month, the most blessed month, Shahr Ramaḍān, the month of Ramaḍān, which AllāhNone give for Ummah of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. And there is many thing in this month but the most important one: the holy Qur’ān was sent in this month for Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. And it is sent by, in night of Qadr, Laylatu l-Qadr. For this, this night it is better, more blessed, than thousand months, which Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying. It is one whole life for any human being, for... Maybe some people cannot be reach even, this eighty years.

So, it is one night equal for whole life. And it is in Ramaḍān, AllāhNone send it in Ramaḍān. Of course, there is also, whole year you can find this, but mostly in Ramaḍān, Laylatu l-Qadr, in Ramaḍān. For this many people, they asking, “Which night?” They are wondering [if] it's twenty-seven or twenty-one or twenty-eight, twenty-five, or maybe before, maybe later. For this, even in time for Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, one ṣaḥābah was asking, in khuṭbah, ḥadīth was about this. Asking Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, “Which night, O my beloved, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, this night, holy night?” Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, answering, “If INone say you, people, they will leave to pray. Only they pray in this night.” For this, it is hidden. To make people to pray whole year and to ask for blessing. To be not be lazy, because if you in one night, whole life it is enough for you. But when you not knowing, so you continue to pray and to ask from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, this. But mostly it is in, as we said, in Ramaḍān, this night. And Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, saying for this ṣaḥābah that time, maybe twenty-three. But every time, because not same time, maybe that time it was in twenty-three. But now or after also, every time changing. Not in one special day, special date. It changing. So, we are happy. What we will do if you, this night, maybe people, the whole night they trying to pray and ask for AllāhNone. Most of people, they do this in twenty-seven. But this is what important for us: when you, as Mawlānā Shaykh first he met his shaykh, they say for him, “Shaykh say for people around there, not showing. Mawlānā, but, “You must know each night as Laylatu l-Qadr.” Meaning, each night you must pray you must make worshipping and you must also to see everyone as Khiḍr ‘alayhi s-salām. Khiḍr also hidden between people and he is coming for people, I say, “You know who is Khiḍr? What is most... most sign for Sayyidunā Khiḍr?” Ask, I ask people, they don't know. So, I tell them, “It is very easy. He is very teasy, teasing people, and people angry for him. And after he come, he go. This is Khiḍr,” I say. Qadr also, Laylatu l-Qadr, it is hidden between night. And so every night, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, all of you making ‘ibādah, worshipping night.

So many time, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we have Laylatu l-Qadr without knowing. Without knowing, but what happen it is good, because you worshipping, you asking for AllāhNone, not asking anything ridiculous. So, it is hidden and you will find in ākhirah. When you wake up in day of, you see: “Oh I have, many time we have Laylatu l-Qadr,” and this reward, it come for you in ākhirah. So, reward [in] ākhirah most perfect, most precious thing. So, you will find it, in shā’a Llāh. So, for this, don't worry.

Every night you do as it is Laylatu l-Qadr, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone bless us with this night and accept our du‘ā’. And most important for us to ask, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying [to] ask this du‘ā’: “Allāhumma innī as’aluka l-‘afwa wa-l-‘āfiyata wa-l-mu‘āfāta d-dā’imah fī d-dīni wa-d-dunyā wa-l-ākhirah.” This du‘ā’, meaning asking for forgiveness from AllāhNone and asking for health for dunyā and ākhirah. Health, it is very important also. The most precious reward, gift from AllāhNone, ni‘mah this. As to be healthy one, it is very important. And we, for this Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, saying, “Everytime ask this, whole time.” Ramaḍān, after Ramaḍān, but in, most for Laylatu l-Qadr, heNone said for this. So, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, to not be burden for anyone, to be whole life strong and healthy, to be help for other, for yourself, for Islām, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone give us this.

AllāhNone forgive us and give us health, in shā’a Llāh, with strong īmān, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
