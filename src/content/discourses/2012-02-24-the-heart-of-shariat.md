Audhu Billahi min al shaytan al rajim. Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. as-Salamu Alaikum wa Rahmatu Allahi wa Barakatuhu.

Alhamdulillah, Mawlana is getting better, thanks to Allah. He sends salams to all of you. This hal-state coming on him was really something from Allah.. He also showed us as a miracle. Mawlana really went back & forth to akhirat (between life & death). It was something like this. But thanks to Allah, he has come back. After a few weeks we will see him with us, insha Allah.

Now, he says that instructions are coming to him that he wants to deliver. The last time I saw him, he said there was an instruction. He says, "Mureeds/followers may be careful about keeping Shariat". Naqshbandi way is already the essence of Shariat. Therefore Mawlana warned about this point. Now, there are four True Madhabs-School of Thought. They may follow Shariat according to whichever school they belong to. If there is an absolute necessity, one may copy from-imitate the other. But when there is no necessity, then there is no need. Sometimes there can be absolute necessities & if there is no other way, one may copy from Hanbali, Shafi'i, Maliki.. Mawlana himself is on Hanafi school. He also mentioned another point.

He says "be careful about men & women sitting separately. Men & women may not mix too much." Also, men may not go too much upstairs to his place".

He also mentioned something else. We haven't heard of it too much before. Musafaha-handshaking, it is not very common among Turks but Arabs do Musafaha more. Men kiss each other on cheeks when they meet - Mawlana says "it is makrouh-disliked, haram-forbidden". He doesn't want it (to be done). He warned about this.

Alhamdulillah, we already gave the glad tidings that he is getting well. There is no need to keep it longer. Alhamdulillah Mawlana is getting better. This is, Subhan Allah, a miracle. He went to akhirat & came back. This is real. Even doctors, when they came, they said: "it is finished". But Alhamdulillah, this is very nice. Allah was Merciful to leave him with us, and us with him, insha Allah till Mahdi (as). And this is also khalwat-seclusion for him. And he gave some instructions for people, what to do.

He said, to keep Shariat. This is very important. And there is, when they say Sufi, there are many kinds of Sufi. Sufi without Islam cannot be Sufi. But people they do this, they say Sufi. But no, without Islam cannot be Sufi. Especially with the Naqshbandi Way - it is heart of Shariat, Naqshbandi. So he said, you must keep your Madhab. We have 4 Haqq Madhabs. It was before too much but, 4 is enough for people. Without this, it is not our way, Naqshbandi way, only 4 Madhabs. So everybody must keep his Madhab. This, which are Imam Abu Hanifa, Shafi'i, Hanbali, Maliki- this is 4. Which way you are following, you must continue with. But there is sometimes very important thing that you can't find in your Madhab, then you can take another Madhab only for this reason. And there was one special order to him, when you make musafaha men do not kiss each other. He doesn't like it, "it is makrouh or haram" he said. Only musafaha one makes, this is his order.

There is instruction coming, we tell everyday insha Allah, what is coming. Allah keep him with us, and keep us with him also until Imam Mahdi (as). This is, Alhamdulillah we are happy today. Every Juma, we become more happy. Insha Allah, after 3-4 Jumas he will be with us insha Allah.

Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, Al Fatiha.
