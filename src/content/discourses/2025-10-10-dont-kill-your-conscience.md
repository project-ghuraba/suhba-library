As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

When Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla created humankind, He created him above other creatures, that is, at a higher level. And He placed all kinds of goodness within him. Also, there’s the ego. He put the ego inside him. As we constantly say, the ego desires evil.

Allah also placed within us that which does not desire evil: the conscience. Everyone has a conscience, Muslims and non-believers. Everyone has conscience, which Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla placed in humankind. He placed it so that they can reason and not act unjustly. He also put compassion.

But to do these things, one must overcome his ego. Because when one has a conscience, he does not oppress anyone, he does not harm anyone, he does not usurp anyone’s wealth or deceive anyone. After that, his īmān gradually increases. Ultimately, he attains guidance, most of the time.

But without a conscience, one does not do good deeds, even if one is a Muslim. Even if he is a Muslim, a conscienceless person knows neither rights nor laws, nor what is ḥalāl or ḥarām. They say, “I am a Muslim,” perform the five daily prayers, and sometimes even go to Ḥajj. But when there’s no conscience, they follow their egos and go after their egos.

Therefore, this is the wisdom of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. His wisdom is incomprehensible. Mankind’s mind cannot fathom it.

Allah says, “I created man in the most perfect form,”<br>
وَلَقَدْ كَرَّمْنَا بَنِي آدَمَ
<br>“Wa laqad karramnā Banī Ādam”<br>
“And We have certainly honoured the children of Adam.” (Qur’ān 17:70). 

“I created humankind at the highest level, with the most beautiful qualities. We honoured them on land, at sea, and everywhere,” says Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla.

How does humanity come about? Humanity comes with conscience. Without conscience, humanity is lost. A person does everything for their egos’ sake.

Therefore, you often see a non-Muslim, yet they have a conscience. They do things that Muslims don’t. “Where does this come from?” They ask. It comes from their conscience. The conscience that Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla has placed within mankind.

You see a Muslim committing all kinds of oppression, all kinds of fraud, and evil. Why? Because they have no conscience left; they have killed their conscience.

Of course, if you kill your conscience, you can hardly revive it. But if you preserve it, it will benefit you. What you do will be great. And the best thing is to be accepted in the presence of Allah and our Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam.

That conscientious, compassionate person is the one loved by AllahʿAzza wa Jalla, our Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, Awliyā’ and believers.

That is what matters. Otherwise, this property and wealth you seized from people through fraud, robbery, and deception are of no use. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla doesn’t need them. You do.

People must restore that conscience, so that they can find peace. People say, “A clear conscience is a clear heart.” If your conscience is clear, your heart will be at ease.

May Allah not make us among the unscrupulous, inshāAllah. May Allah also guide people so that they don’t kill this beautiful thing, inshāAllah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq

Al-Fātiḥah

There are recited Quran Khatms, tasbihat, Surahs, ayāt, and Dala’il al-Khayrat Khatms. Whatever good deeds and charity there is, we gift them firstly to our Prophet, his Ahlu l-Bayt and Ṣaḥābah, all Anbiya, Awliyā, Asfiya. To the souls of our mashāyikh. May they reach them, inshāAllah.

May whatever good goals those who’ve recited have be reached. For happiness here and hereafter. For shifa. May it be a means for goodness. inshāAllah, may it benefit people and be a means for guidance. May Allah protect us from the evil of people and the evil of shayṭān. May Allah elevate us to the highest ranks. May He send his owner inshāAllah.

Li-Llahi Ta’ala l-Fātiḥah.
