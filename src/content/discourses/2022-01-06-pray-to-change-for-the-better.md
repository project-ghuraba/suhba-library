As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Yā muḥawwila l-ḥawli wa-l-aḥwāl ḥawwil ḥālanā ilá aḥsani l-ḥāl." This du‘ā’... Mankind changes from one state to another. No matter if they want it or not, people change. They are different as babies, different as children and different as adolescents. People change all the time. We ask that our changes are for the better side. May we not become worse. We beg AllāhNone that HeNone changes us for the better. Because everything is in HisNone hands.

Thoughts and actions of young people change when they get older. Some people get worse. Others say, "This man was like an angel. He used to help his family and everyone. He used to pray and worship. He was such a good man. What happened to him?" You look, he has changed so much. He treats his family badly. He doesn't take care of them. No more praying. And he does all kinds of bad things. The state of this person has changed. That good man, angel-like person is gone. And shayṭān has come instead. This happens. Therefore, we always pray that AllāhNone leaves us in good condition, in a good state. And if HeNone wants to change us, may HeNone change us for the better, in shā’a Llāh.

This is important. People should be careful about these things. If they follow their egos, their condition will get worse. And if they look at themselves and say, "We used to pray. We used to treat people well. Our condition is getting worse;" if they repent and get rid of these things, they will change for the better. They will save themselves from falling off the cliff. And it will be a good thing for the people around. They will earn their ākhirah as well. Otherwise, if they say, "I have prayed for so many years. I did so much goodness. It is enough. Let me do other things instead. Let me skip this," their state will get worse. And that will bring loss both to themselves and to others.

May AllāhNone protect us from these bad conditions.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. There is a du‘ā’, prayer, saying... "Yā muḥawwila l-ḥawli wa-l-aḥwāl ḥawwil ḥālanā ilá aḥsani l-ḥāl." Our living, when we are living, from childhood or from baby, every time, human being changing. From childhood, baby, he become childhood, after he become young and become middle age. This is AllāhNone creation, so thinking also can change. Everything can change. When you change like this, you must ask for to be change for better. Who change... Make our change to better not to worse. This is du‘ā’ praying.

Because people without knowing, sometimes changing to worse. For this we must be careful to not be change to worse. We must be careful, we must be stable in good way. Many people you see them like angel. They're praying. They're good with family, with wife or with husband and with children, with neighbour. Praying, fasting, making charity, helping people. After a while, you hear they become, they left this and they change. They're not praying, not fasting, not believing. And they beside this, they are harming their family, wife, or children – not looking for them. And they're going to bad way, bad companion. And you... surprise you. "What happened for this?" This what happen because every time changing.

They must, we must pray to not make us, AllāhNone to change us. And but also we have to see ourselves. If we are not going well, you must quickly come back to Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla and ask for forgiveness and to ask for help for yourself to not be in this bad way. Because this way, way of shayṭān. And shayṭān never want good for people. All what – they – he want, to make people misery and poor, and ill, everything. In Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n saying, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Ash-shayṭānu ya‘idukumu l-faqr, wa-ya’murukum bi-l-faḥshā’ " (2:268). "And he promise you to be poor and to be faḥshā’ bad habit, bad action, everything he order you for this. When you do this, you will be poor. You will be no value for you.

AllāhNone keep us safe from this changing, AllāhNone. Because many people they're saying, "Oh, we are making praying too much, long time. I fed up from this. Maybe to leave this and go and with companion drink and go making bad thing without any harm, anything to prevent yourself from ḥarām." Many people they can do this. But at the end, they will be lose everything and to harm around themself.

AllāhNone keep us from this because we are living in time there is many thing can cheat people to take them from good way to bad way. So, in shā’a Llāh, AllāhNone keep us in stable and if HeNone will change, AllāhNone make us to change to better and better, in shā’a Llāh. Not to be cheated with shayṭān and his followers.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
