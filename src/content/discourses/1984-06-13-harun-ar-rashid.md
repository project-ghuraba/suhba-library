Harun Al-Rashid, the famous Khalif of Islam. Now, I am not going to make a long lecture, but it must be short one. But I am asking from our Lord to make it effective in our hearts. This is a event but we are saying as a tale, but it is a real event. And, showing us what is the value of whole world and its treasures. We are speaking on the value of sons of Adam. Now, we are looking at the value of this world and it's treasure, whole treasures.

Once, Harun ur-Rashid was sitting on his throne in the Royal Palace for him. Then suddenly, a Shaykh entering and looking, like this, like that around at the walls, and roof, and everything. We are not going to be a maturity man, always going to be like a lazy boy, then… Never can look around in the presence of a huge Khalif, such as Harun ur-Rashid. Looking around? If you are going to find such a palaces in akhirat also, you are alright. If you are finding, even such a palace".

Awliya's words coming from heart and going in hearts because Allah Almighty dressing them 'hayba'. One Sultan was saying, Abu l-Fateh, one famous Emperor of Ottomans who conquered Constantinople, which the Prophet (pbuh) praising him. Before conquering Constantinople Rasulullah (sas) giving good tidings that that big city must be conquered. And after nine centuries just captured and conquered. He was 21 years old when he captured that. But he had Grandshaykh. Without Grandshaykh no one can do such a big events, victorious.

He was saying, "Even Kings coming to me for visiting, they are trembling," but he said "When I am going to visit my Grandshaykh I am trembling" because, this is sultan for this life they are sultan, here and hereafter. That Shaykh also, Almighty dressing him from His Greatness-Power. Harun Rashid trembling, asking, "O my Shaykh, give me 'nasiha', please give me one advice".

In such a Khalif's presence it is no good to make a long advice. Therefore, he was going to give a short advice to him. And asking, "O Harun! Imagine that you have been lost in a desert. You lost your way in a desert and going to be thirsty and going to be dying from thirsty. What are you going to give if anyone gives you one cup of water?" He said "Just I was giving half of my treasures and my territory. I am ready to give half of it that everything I have". Because this should give him life. He said "Half of my treasures and territory, I am giving". "Good," said Shaykh, "if that water going in and not coming out". You understand, there is a illness - not coming out. And that is a kind of a punishment that should be given to people in their graves. "If imprisoned inside, not coming out, that water what you are going to give?" And he was saying, "The second part also, I was giving for that water that I drink, to come out". "What is remaining for you? Only from once drinking, once going to toilet, you gave whole treasures! Look!" This signing what is the value of the whole world, if you have, you are ready to give for one drinking, for one going to toilet. This is the first, number one. Number two?

You must consider every time that you are finding something to drink and sometimes that you may go to toilet to be rest, that every time, you are a man that has been given whole treasures of this world.
