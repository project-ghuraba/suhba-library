Assalamu Alaykum,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Allah Azza wa Jalla says, “Go after your sustenance on the face of earth. Go after the awliya.” So instead of waiting, more needs to be requested. Ask, look, look for the good, and look for the better. It points to this. Meaning laziness is not good in anything.

Effort, himma, and hard work are needed in Islam. Allah gives to those who work. He does not leave it lacking. Many examples were also given in this matter. For example, the ant, it works nonstop. Allah Azza wa Jalla shows people. It works, works, and works. Its name is even mentioned in the Koran Karim. In fact, it will also enter paradise. Those mentioned in the Koran Karim will enter paradise. Allah will also place the bee in paradise. Allah will place a couple of animals in paradise. The ant and the bee will be amongst them. They have been shown himma. They are hard working and beneficial insects. They have been shown superior to other things by Allah Azza wa Jalla so people may take lesson from them.

Worldly life is short anyway. Allah created us all not to lie down but to work and bustle about. Do not bustle about in vain. Let your bustling about be for Allah. Let your intention be so too. Allah gives according to your intentions.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq.

Al-Fatiha.
