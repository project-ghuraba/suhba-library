As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Jumʿah Mubarak, inshā’Allah. We are gathering for the sake of Allah. May Allah bless us all, inshā’Allah. Jumʿah is the day of every good thing. Allah gave it to His most beloved one Sayyidinā Muḥammad ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam and his ummah, his nation. Of course, every Prophet has a blessed day. Each of the Uli l-ʿAzam, the seven Prophets has a blessed day. Allah gave them one day. And He gave Prophet ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam the best day, Jumʿah.

He created the first man, our grandfather, the father of all humankind, Sayyidinā Adam ʿalayhi s-salām on Jumʿah; and he passed away on the day of Jumʿah. Also, Yawmu l-Qiyāmah will happen on the day of Jumʿah. And the opening, the resurrection of all humankind will be on Jumʿah also. Prophet ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam also says that in this day, Allah has given many gifts for us. One of them is that there’s a time in this day when Allah accepts the duʿā you make.

Alḥamdulillāh, we are thanking Allah that He created us in this time, the time of more reward for the ummah who’s following Prophet ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. Of course, this is the most difficult time for the believer, for the Muslim, for any believer. Because to keep your religion or your belief, Prophet ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam says, it’s as if you’re holding fire in your hand.

Of course, it’s not by our choice that we came to life in this time. Allah created us and He does what He likes. We cannot object to this. And this is the difference between Muslim believers and the other nations of prophets. All Muslims of course, all prophets and other prophets’ followers, believers, are in Islām. But we are happy that Allah made us like this and created us in this time. We cannot object to this. It’s forbidden for the believer to say this.

This is the difference between us and all the followers of prophets before Prophet ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. Even the companions of Uli l-ʿAzam, like Sayyidinā ʿIsā and Sayyidinā Musa. All these companions were making problems for them and asking things that cannot be asked by anyone who has adab with Allah and with a prophet.

Sayyidinā Musa ʿalayhi s-salām saved his followers from the oppression of Pharaoh. All these miracles came to him, and he saved them and took them to a safe place. They took their women and used them. They oppressed them and their situation was very bad. And as soon as they went to Sina, to a safe place, when Sayyidinā Musa left them to go a few days to a special place to address Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla, they quickly made a statue and were worshipping this cow. There are thousands of examples on how their belief was very weak.

Sayyidinā ʿIsā ʿalayhi s-salām also. Now there are those who say they follow him, but in reality, they’re not even following one percent of his teachings. Our Prophet ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam says, the most patient people and the most oppressed people are the prophets. And Sayyidinā Muḥammad ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam was the most patient among them all.

For this, we were saying about Sayyidinā ʿIsā ʿalayhi s-salām that he was patient. Every time, every day a miracle. Hundreds or thousands of people were waiting daily to get blessed, to get shifā’ from him in everything; the blind one’s eyes opened and he could see, he cured skin diseases. He cured thousands of people with incurable diseases. Here, we say to those saying that Islām is not a religion and do not accept Prophet ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam: these people [with weak belief] were the (most) closest disciples to Sayyidinā ʿIsā, the nearest ones to him and who are considered the head of the followers of Sayyidinā ʿIsā. And they were asking him, “If your Lord, your God Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla can send us a table of food to eat and to be happy.”

Qur’ān is a miracle of Allah. The only real heavenly book now from Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla is Qur’ān ʿAẓīmu sh-Sha’n. When you read a word, you understand it and understand how these people were, how they used to think, their character, their situation. They said, “If your Lord can do this.” If you say this now, your belief is considered finished.

وَاللَّهُ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ
<br>“Wa-Allahu ʿalā kulli shay'in qadīr”<br>
“And Allah is over all things powerful.” (Qur’an 03:189).

Allah does everything. He does, He can do, He has done. He only says “Kun”, “Be,” and it will be – “Fa-yakūn.” But Sayyidinā ʿIsā was really patient. He wasn’t shouting at them. And he even asked Allah and Allah sent them this table.

For this, we see many non-Muslims, and sometimes Muslims also who are following them saying, “Why did this happen? Why did this happen to me?” Many are objecting; Christians or non-Muslims, all. But maybe these Hindu or others are patient because next time they will be a chicken or a mouse or something. They are patient not making problems, “The other time we can be better.” But Christians or others are all the time like this.

Who had belief, now doesn’t have belief also, they don’t believe in God. They are objecting to Allah. Sometimes I see people who say, “I left prayer. I am not praying for Allah.” In old times when we were children, when we weren’t happy with someone, we don’t speak to him, we don’t go near him. These people are doing this with Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. They don’t know that Allah is not in need of what they do from ‘ibādah; nothing affects Allah.

But shayṭān is cheating them saying, “Look, Allah is giving others like this and you are suffering from this, from that. So, you must listen to Him! Don’t do this!” This favor you do for yourself, not for Allah. If you are sick, you are poor or other, and you are patient Allah will reward you. And they make philosophy: like this, like that. In religion, there’s no philosophy. Only follow. Our philosophy is to follow. If you find the right way, run to this way; don’t go out of this way.

For this, in the Khutbah we said the ḥadīth of Prophet ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam which says, in order to attain the sweetness of īmān, belief, you must love Allah and Prophet ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam more than anything. This sweetness of īmān is the best thing for the believer. And to love for the sake of Allah, and to not love for the sake of Allah. And the third condition, which was our subject now, is to hate becoming an unbeliever as much as you hate being thrown in fire.

This is very important to not fall into a wrong thing thinking you’ve done something good: you are not happy with what happened or has happened. Allah has willed this. You must think that there are people who are a thousand times, a million times worse than your situation in many things. Today, I was looking at the messages on the telephone. One of our murīds in Dagestan has two sons fighting in Ukraine. One of them became shahīd (martyr). He was just asking us for duʿā for him. His belief is very strong. This one, Allah rewards him and is happy with him.

إِنَّمَا يُوَفَّى الصَّابِرُونَ أَجْرَهُم بِغَيْرِ حِسَابٍ
<br>“Innamā yuwaffa ṣ-ṣābirūna ajrahum bi-ghayri ḥisāb”<br>
“Indeed, the patient will be given their reward without account.” (Qur’ān 39:10).

Those who are patient, Allah will reward them without account. For this, we are saying alḥamdulillāh from the beginning of the ṣuḥbah, we are happy with what Allah granted us, to be in this time and in this place also, and every good place. It is a big favor from Him. And we are asking Allah also to not give us any test, inshā’Allah. This is also an important thing. Our ‎Prophet, Awliyā’ullāh and ṣaḥābah tell people not to ask for what they cannot carry.

May Allah keep us safe, inshā’Allah, until Sayyidina al-Mahdi ʿalayhi s-salām comes and saves all humanity, inshā’Allah. May Allah keep you and your children, and the children of all Muslims and all mankind safe from shayṭān and his followers, inshā’Allah.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
