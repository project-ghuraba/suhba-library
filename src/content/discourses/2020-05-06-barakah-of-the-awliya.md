Aū‘dhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-Shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Waṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-F’āiz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-

Thanks be to Allah for providing us today—every second, every minute of it; and thanks be to Allah that He created us in the religion of Islam and made us amongst the people of the Holy Prophet (SAW). We are trying to be mureeds of the tariqah—thanks be for that, too! It is the greatest thing, and also a great happiness, to be with the shaykhs! There are a lot of new mureeds who did not meet Mawlana, and yet even after his passing, they continue to be initiated into the tariqah day after day. Most did not meet him but they too are counted as his. Mawlana’s station is high, both in the seen and in the unseen. Whoever has submitted to him, by the will of Allah, is an acceptable and sincere servant of Allah on the path of the Holy Prophet (SAW).

Mawlana is one of the rare people. Someone like him arrives only once every hundred years or maybe not even that often. But barakah from him continues many years, and if the End Times were not near, it would have continued for many more centuries! But, insha’Allah, there is not that long left before the End.

We are in the End Days. The world, Allah knows best, cannot continue any longer. For this reason, those who gave bayah to Mawlana [directly], and those who gave it later, are in contentment and are safe. Unlike when people do things sometimes and find out later that their actions were in vain. The acts of those who have submitted to these people are not in vain, because as in shariah, Ahl us-Sunnah wa-l-Jama‘ah through tariqah continues the same way with their barakah, and they give benefit to those who are in tariqah. Sometimes a person who has been doing something for a long time may at the end [change and] go [even] in the opposite direction. He then will have to go start over, but in many cases there is not enough time to go back to the starting point. So [being able to] submit oneself to these people is a favor and something fortunate. May Allah make their station the highest, and may their help come upon us, insha’Allah. They are not dead. The one who is dead—there are a lot of “dead” walking around now. They say they are alive, but their hearts are dead, and so [in reality] they are dead. Awliya’uLlah, the Companions of the Holy Prophet (SAW), and the Prophets (AS) do not die. They continue perpetually. Their spirits are stronger, and that is why with their barakah, insha’Allah there is no difficulty. There are no difficult days with their help, and by will of Allah things become easy. May Allah make our faith stronger through their help, insha’Allah. Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-F ti ah
