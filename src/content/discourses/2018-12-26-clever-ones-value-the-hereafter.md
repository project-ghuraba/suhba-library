As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīn wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa-l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

In shā’a Llāh as our ṭarīqah requires, we will give ṣuḥbah. It is the main thing in our ṭarīqah. Ṣuḥbah is important. The gathering of brothers, these are the pillars. It's been long since we didn't meet. We shall speak a little, in shā’a Llāh. May it be beneficial for you and for us, in shā’a Llāh.

If you ask who is a smart man, most people say, "I am the smartest." Nowadays, a smart person is an alert one. "The more you benefit from this world, the smarter you are," everybody says. They assume collecting the goods of this world as smart. They assume having a rank as smart. They think it is useful. To bring people together, to become a professor or a doctor, they think all of it as smart. Now, if all the things we mentioned are the goal, to be in a high position, to be rich, to be a leader, to have properties, in reality, they are not smart but stupid. Because it has an end. To aim for something that has an end is mindless. Because we have two options in life. One is to go after eternal life, to make it the main goal. And the other is to have the trash of this world as the goal. How many people before you came and left? Those who said they were smart got no benefit. They got nothing but harm.

Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla tells us a lot about Banī Isrā’il in the Holy Qur‘ān. Banī Isrā’il are the Jewish people. They were... AllāhNone created everyone. Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla created people as HeNone liked. And HeNone says, "We chose this for them. We chose them for knowledge." Knowledge means to have both mind and intelligence. They tell people about themselves, "We are the chosen nation of AllāhNone." I don't know if they say they are servants. They say, "We are the chosen tribe of AllāhNone." We can't question the wisdom of AllāhNone. Many stories tell about them in the Qur’ān. Why are they told? Because as we said, they have mind and intelligence. And however they use them, in order to show them as a lesson to people, Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla mentioned them. And they were sent many prophets. Prophets were sent. And what did they do? They are given intelligence but not given mind then. At some period, they killed a thousand prophet in one day.

So being intelligent doesn't mean being smart. Being smart means using your intelligence and asking, "Why did I come to this world? If I run after this garbage, it will disappear in a couple of years. I will have nothing in my hands but my shroud. Did I come for that? Or did I come for eternal life? Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla sent those prophets to us as a favour. HeNone chose us and gave us so much. HeNone favoured us in it." Is killing them smart, instead of treating them well? We shouldn't blame anyone for this. Because if you follow your ego, you lose. Even if the whole world is yours, if you don't do anything for your hereafter, if you don't believe in AllāhNone, you are in eternal trouble. You are eternally in a bad place, in hell. Even if you don't have anything, when you are on the way of AllāhNone and obey AllāhNone, you win. You will be eternally happy.

Therefore, when people in this world see Muslims or those who fear AllāhNone, they call them stupid, mindless and crazy. Why are they crazy? They are not doing wrong or cheating someone. Even when they can, they are not stealing. They fear AllāhNone. And they call them crazy, mindless, and stupid. Let them say. No harm comes from people's words. Besides, benefit comes. Therefore, they used to say in old times, "You can't be a walī unless you're crazy." Crazy is the one who doesn't value dunyā as they say. They call him mindless. And that is a good attribute. We don't value dunyā at all.

We value AllāhNone. We value the ProphetNone. We value the orders of AllāhNone. As long as the whole world is not destroyed, a smart person won't take even a bit. But another one runs after anything that brings money. He embarrasses himself and passes away leaving everything at last. These are the differences between the smart and mindless. And this is what taṣawwuf is. Because as we said, it is not only for deniers. It is the same for hodjas as well. There are many among them who do what they know as right, the way others want in order to save their salary or have a vacation. Taṣawwuf teaches adab. It teaches the right way. And the rest teach how to mislead from the way. May AllāhNone protect [us].

It is the end of times. They are not showing respect to hodjas and awliyā’, to people who fear AllāhNone. Moreover, they swear at them. And then they lose. They think of others as unintelligent, they are mindless themselves. We should be careful about it. They should respect those who were respected by our forefathers. They should remember them as good. Where we are now, Quṭb ‘Uthmān Efendi was also one of these awliyā’. There are books about him. And it's known how he lived. It brings benefit to follow their way. These new trends and fashion are shayṭān's work. And there is new trend not to respect those who are in ṭarīqah. And they are swearing at madhāhib and their imāms, showing them as bad. It happens by reading the books of people who are shayṭān's agents. Unfortunately, present imāms and highly educated figures have always been under their control. More than half of them tend towards that. Their brains are so polluted that when you say something, they think about that one, and you are thought of as ignorant next to them. He places a veil over your speech. You can shout as much as you want, they won't hear. They don't try to understand. Their ears are shut. As Nūḥ ‘alayhi s-salām said, they shut their ears with their fingers. They don't want advice from you. They don't listen to the truth you say. Unfortunately, they are under control.

But we are saying, as it is the end of times, it is normal. Ṣāhib of this religion will come. He will bring everyone out. In shā’a Llāh, Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām will separate right and wrong. Therefore, the worse the situation is, the closer the coming of Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām with permission of AllāhNone. Truth will show itself like the rising sun. He will defeat those false liars who are on the way of evil, in shā’a Llāh. May AllāhNone send him soon. May we reach him, in shā’a Llāh. May we see the good days, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq. Al-Fātiḥah.

Some friends are suggesting we should ban these TV series. We know what TV series are. Worse than TV series, these organizations that spread this poison, these types of people who head our religious organizations; may AllāhNone protect us, we should be cautious from them. Series are going on like they did a century ago. Nothing comes from them. Our belief is being harmed. They have squeezed in like worms into trees. Otherwise, those series are like wind which breaks a couple of branches. The main thing is this. We should be careful about it. The situation is worse than you think. But as we said, thank AllāhNone we don't fear anyone. Our ProphetNone said this would happen. This is necessary for Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām to come. In shā’a Llāh, his coming is near.
