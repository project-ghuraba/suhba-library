As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh. A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

We have a duty to protect the rights of people. People's rights and the law are the most investigated things in Islām. They are part of the foundation of Islām. Giving rights to the ones to whom they belong is a pillar of Islām. This is one of the most important orders in Islām. Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim was called based on this name, Ḥaqqānī, which means the one who protects rights. It means a person on the way of truth. It is easy to talk about protecting the right but most people cannot do it. Many people act according to the whispering of shayṭān and the orders of shayṭān. They want to acquire the right for themselves rather than giving it to others. And if the truth is against them, they don't accept it. This is the nature of people. Because people need to be trimmed like a wild tree. They need to be pruned to become good. That is in the character of people. Even if it is not their right, when it is in their personal interest, they see themselves as right. This is something not good. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla and our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam are not happy with it. Whoever has the right, you must give it.

Many times, wrongful people used to come to Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim and say, "We ask this from you." Even though they were wrong, Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim gave it to them so that they have their rights in any case. Therefore, this is an important matter. People of the present time, they have no rights at all in all aspects. There is no right and law in this time. It is the end of times. People don't get their rights in court. There is no law either. There is nothing anywhere in this world because there is no country which is based on Islām. No one keeps the law of Islām. All of them are different. Even if someone is right and wants to get his right, even if he is able to get his right in a court, it takes ten years, five years. No one gets it in five years. It takes longer than ten years. A man may die by then and there is no use in this law. There is no right and no law in this time.

Don't say, "Why would I keep the right and law?" Everyone is responsible for himself. Whoever does injustice, the right one will get his right in front of AllāhNone, says our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Moreover, if a horned sheep attacks a hornless sheep, on the Day of Judgment it will be given horns to attack back. It will get its right back. It is so thorough. If Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla maintains the rights of animals, HeNone maintains them for people too. Everyone is doing badness in this world. Don't say, "I will also do badness." Be on the right way, on the way of truth. You will attain the mercy of ḤaqqNone. Otherwise, you will have struggled for nothing. What you earn is nothing. What you think you earn, is in fact you taking someone's right. That will turn bad for you. It won't be good for you. It will bring suffering and badness. May AllāhNone protect us from taking anyone's right. May AllāhNone protect us all. We have a good tradition in which people say, "Forgive your right with me." Some people forgive their rights. But some cannot forgive their rights to people who have taken them. Therefore, we must be careful with rights. We must ask for forgiveness. When they say they don't forgive, you should ask, "What is your right?" "Such and such." "Let me return it and then you forgive it." We must say like this. May AllāhNone protect us. May HeNone let us go to ākhirah without taking anyone's right.

We are, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone put for human being, system and thing, the best thing for human beings. And human being, they are like wild tree. You must trim them. You must make them, craft them, to make good thing. And it is the most important thing to be, keep right of people. Ḥaqq meaning right of people. AllāhNone ḤaqqNone AllāhNone True one, ḤaqqNone one. So, Mawlānā Shaykh also he was very sensitive for this. For this, his, they give him title Ḥaqqānī from ḥaqq, coming from right, from keeping right. It was very important for him. He never oppressed anybody, never cheat anybody, never saying something not good. Even many times people coming to him and they are not right. And they asking something from him or they said, "This is, it was us." Or sometime they take without permission. He was not angry with them. "Okay, take." Don't be saying, "Shaykh take this from us or other." Many time he left many good thing for people, they are claiming this for them.

"To be," he said, "to be oppressed, we be oppressed, better [than] to be oppressing people." To be maẓlūm – maẓlūm [means] oppressed – not to be ẓālim – ẓālim who oppress people. This is very important because we are living in time, very bad time. For nobody, no law, no right, no justice in this world. No any place, in this world now they have justice. They claim the Europe, they have justice. This is the biggest liar and bigger hypocrite. They show themself as they are justice but don't have any justice, even one drop of justice. All world now, all people, their right they don't have. All people, they have their rights taken from them. Not real, giving them. What they in need, they not given. Justice, many time who have right he become, he is person who not have rights. Other who oppress him, he become, he has right for him. We are living in this time. But, so people saying: "Why we must be keep other people rights? Everybody they are eating from other right, other thing. So, why we are only we be keep justice? We keep ḥaqq, we keep right of people, no need to do this." No, it is, if whole world doing this and you also with them, you will be charged from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla and punished. Because we have Day of Judgement, which everything, it will [be] asked. But Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, even for animal. If one sheep has horn and beat other without horn, AllāhNone they will create them again and give, put horn for this not horn and hit other sheep. And this right, it will be taken. And after, they become dust again. So, even for animal, it will be like this. You think, you will be safe from, if you do... take right of other people? In Qiyāmah, each one, they will take his right, their right. But there is no money that time. That time, they will make, take from your good deeds. If you don't have good deed, they will put your sin on them. They will be more sinner, more punishment for them. This is, so... Don't think, when everybody doing something, it is you can do also [and] it is, AllāhNone accept this. AllāhNone never accept bad thing. And we are asking AllāhNone to keep us from to make anything bad for other people. Or to take their right from them or anything from them, without knowing or with knowing. Because many people they are now also – [those] who they are claiming they are, even they are Muslim and they are claiming they are most pure Muslim, even they separate from [those] who they call it kāfir. India, Pakistan, they have many cases. We have every time, they have no right, nothing. They can take everything from other people. They take, not giving daughter, not giving who are outside of this country. So, it is not acceptable. Even you are going to Ḥajj, you got your, maybe accept Ḥajj but you will be charged for what you take from other people.

And so, in Turkish, they say every time, "Hakkını helel et." Meaning you make your ḥaqq, forgive us for, if you have anything, forgive us. This good saying, but if they have rights, some people they not accept. They [say], "No, I don't give you, I cannot give you ḥalāl for what you done for me." So, you must ask him, "What's your right? What you want from me, to make, to forgive me?" If they accept and they give, so it will be in this dunyā. They have their, finish this problem. No need to take it to ākhirah. If they not, they are, can be little bit, maybe here. Even they are not happy with what they are taking from other people. But in ākhirah, they will be charged it. AllāhNone keep us safe from to do anything bad for people and to be, which harm with this. AllāhNone make us to control our ego, because this also from ego. They want everything and they not satisfied with [this] thing. AllāhNone keep us safe, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
