As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Alḥamdulillāh we are ṭarīqah. There is Naqshbandī, Qādiri, Rifāi, etc. All ṭarīqahs are the same. There is ṭarīqah but there must be knowledge also of sharīʿah. Sharīʿah has madhhab. There is no ṭarīqah without madhhab. There must be madhhab with ṭarīqah. There are four madhhabs: Hanafi, Shāfiʿi, Māliki, Hanbali. Whichever you follow of these four madhhabs, you’ll be on this road.

We must say this. People here sometimes think that when the imām doesn’t say “Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm” loudly, that this is out of Islām, out of sharīʿah. So you must know.

When you see something like this, you must ask, “What is this? What is that?” Not directly judging. If you judge without knowing, it’s a great wrong; it’s not good. It is a sin also because if you’re considering that act out of sharīʿah and judging without knowledge, you’ll be punished for that. Because you make fitnah and without knowing it, you make people have doubt.

There are four madhhabs. You ask each, “What is this? What do you do in this case? What’s the rule for this? What’s the judgement on that?” After that, you’ll know.

For this, you must be careful. Even our people, when they see something, they quickly become angry. Don’t do that. Just ask. Because half of knowledge is in asking. No need to get angry without knowing. Because there are many people - especially those out of the four madhhabs - who say, “There’s no need for madhhab.” And of course about ṭarīqah, they say, “This is bidʿah. This is shirk.” But even about madhhabs, they say, “There’s no need for madhhab. You can act by yourself.” This is not true at all. Many people go out of īmān and out of Islām when following that.

So, don’t hurry [in judgment] about anything. Just ask if it’s really out of Islām. Then, you know, “This what we do is not good.” If you just had doubts and your doubts were wrong, you say, “Alḥamdulillāh we didn’t say anything, so we won’t be asked [from Allah] for what we said.” May Allah keep us on His way. May Allah fill our hearts with īmān, nūr of īmān, light of īmān, inshā’Allāh.

Allah makes the mu’min see with the light of Allah put in his heart. Inshā’Allāh, may Allah make all of us be like this, inshā’Allāh.

We are not talking about everybody but there are people who do this. Once you see something like this, say you don’t know and ask about it. May Allah keep us, all of us, safe from shayṭān and his followers.

May we be a guide for others also, guidance for this beautiful country. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla made Ādam ʿalayhi s-salām take his first step on this land, on this island. May Allah bless you. May we be together forever inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
