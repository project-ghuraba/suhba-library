Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Thank Allah for this journey. We travelled and returned with their Himmah (support). Insha’Allah we came back with Barakah. People there have Barakah too, because they were both giving and serving. May Allah be pleased with all of them. The last time we went to these holy places with my father Mawlana Shaykh Nazim was twenty years ago. There are more followers now. There were many Murids back then as well. Everyone who saw Mawlana Shaykh Nazim knew and recognized him. Everyone showed respect. They had so much love and faith.

The Himmah of Mawlana and the Himmah of the Mashayikh there is always present. You can really see their Barakah on them. They will reach happiness here and hereafter with their patience, Adab and beautiful manners. There are thousands, millions of holy people there. All of them were people who walked on the way of Allah, people who followed the true way of our Holy Prophet and people who served Islam. Their Himmah is always present insha’Allah.

It was a great honor for us to have been there. It has been a while. Despite this distressed time, thank Allah, it was very nice and you don’t feel this worldwide trouble there. Here, they say to do this and that. And there, they trust in Allah, so Allah is helping them. This is important. There is big difference between people who are cheated by Dunya and forgot Allah, and people who trust in Allah. It is difficult to explain this to people. Whoever understands, understands. Whoever doesn’t understand, what can we do? They will live in distress and in difficulty. And their distress will increase day by day. Those who trust in Allah will be saved by Allah. Allah will help them. This is so with the permission of Allah.

May Allah let us all trust in Him. Those who trust in Him will be saved. Otherwise, if someone trusts in others, he will never get any benefit. And if someone trusts in Kafirs and expects help from them will not be saved in the end. Whoever wants to imitate them will be like them. “َّح لاََّفََّّمِاركِ لاِبَّهّبشَ تلاََّّن ِإ”, Imitating and resembling the good ones leads to salvation. We have to be like these holy ones, not the Kafirs and oppressors. May Allah make it blessed and let us go once again insha’Allah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
