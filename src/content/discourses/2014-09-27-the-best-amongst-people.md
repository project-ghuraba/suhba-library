Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

If Allah (JJ) gives a person a long life and the person’s works are good, then their life also becomes good. It says in the hadith sharif:

“The best amongst people is the one whose life is long and works are good.” The best amongst you are those who live long and do good deeds - none but those who obey Allah’s commands and are occupied with good works.

The old imam of Yahya Efendi Dargah came yesterday. Mashallah he is 105 years old. He gave beautiful advice. Mashallah the hodja efendi also speaks very well. These people are Allah’s beloved servants. The younger the no-good person dies, the better it is for themselves and for others, because their sins increase over time. If they do not intend on repenting either, then it is better for them not to live at all. However, if they live and truly repent, Allah converts their sins to rewards. It would also be a great benefit for the person. We need to repent and seek forgiveness from our sins everyday.

Allah Exalted and Glorious be He changes sins to rewards and good deeds. He is the most generous of the generous. Allah just makes a means for people so He may treat (ikram) them. Allah gives from His endless treasures. Allah’s treasures do not deplete. Allah gives when He gives and He never fears. No matter how rich a human may be, he still gives thinking. Hazrat Allah Azza wa Jalla is not like that. The whole world is nothing next to Hazrat Allah Azza wa Jalla. Allah’s treats are constant so people may do good and recognize Allah. They are endless and they do not deplete. May Allah make us all amongst those people who are granted from His treats Inshallah. May we not deviate from the right path.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq. Al-Fatiha.
