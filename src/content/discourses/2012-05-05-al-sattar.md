Shaykh Mehmet:As-Salamu Alaikum wa Rahmatullahi wa Barakatuhu. Audhu Billahi min al shaytan al rajim, Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Assalatu wa s-salamu ala Rasulina Muhammad Sayyidi l Awwalin wa l-Akhirin. Madad Ya Rasulullah, madad Ya Mashayikhina, Madad Ya Shaykh Abdullah Fayiz al Daghestani, madad Ya Muhammad Nazim al Haqqani, dastur! Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fil jamia' Our way is through association & blessings are in the gathering.

Today's sohbat- as a nasihat/advice, we will speak about the tajalli/ manifestation of the Holy Name "Al-Sattar", insha Allah. The Hadith says "When you are afflicted with a sin, hide that sin". So it means sin is a bala/affliction. So if you are afflicted with it, don't do it openly. Even if you did, don't say it to anybody. Allah al-Sattar meaning, He is the coverer. If you don't say it to anyone, Allah also keeps it & forgives it. But if you boast of your sins, proudly tell of it to people "I did like this, I did like that", then at that time you make them as shahid/witnesses for yourself. And you suffer the punishment for it.

Some people say, "Since Allah knows, why should I hide it from His servant?" This is also a big word which may even go up as far as kufr/unbelief. Allah (awj) knows everything. You raise the servant to Allah's level? When it is like this, these servants also become witnesses for you, therefore the sin is getting bigger & bigger. The person definitely suffers its punishment. And also, Islam is not like any other religion. It is the True religion. Ibrahim (as), Adam (as) all of the same religion. Musa (as), Isa (as) are all the same. There is no difference but the people, themselves changed their religion.

A Christian sins & tells of his blame to a priest. In reality there is no such a thing in Christianity. But later they invented so that they can make money out of it. When one tells the priest, there is no (privacy) anymore. It doesn't matter anymore, they already (changed it). Religion is only Islam. We don't want to be like them either. When you sin, when you are afflicted with sin. Hide it, don't say it to anyone & Allah forgives.

Ok, this is about sins but there is also different types of sins. If a person sins about Allah, Allah forgives them. But there are some sins that, Allah is the Owner of Justice. If you oppress someone or seize a person's property or you killed someone, for example.. When you violate the right of a servant, it is not something that can be covered anymore. There are sins related to the "Rights of Allah", they are different. If you used to drink before or didn't fast, didn't pray..If you don't tell of them, they may be forgiven insha Allah. They are the "rights of Allah", most things are the rights of Allah. But when it is the right of a servant; you seize, steal someone's property, you beat him etc..That, Allah granted it to that servant. Only if he forgives you, then Allah forgives you as well. These are the ones.

The people of this time are also keen of show off. When they do something, they like to show it off. Even their sins, they like to do by showing it off that they enjoy more. A person who is afflicted with sin, he may keep in mind that he doesn't tell anyone, repent to Allah. Allah forgives,by the permisson of Allah.

Today we speak about one name of Allah, "Sattar". Meaning, He is covering- Covering sins, covering 'ayb-bad actions. When this, Allah (awj) saying; If you do something wrong, don't tell anybody. This is 'bala/curse. If you have been cursed, you must..don't tell anybody for this. Because when you tell anybody, it is like advertising for sins. Other people also seeing you doing this, they will be also do same thing. But if you do this bad thing & you don't tell anybody & you will be in Akhirat, Allah insha Allah will forget, forgive this.

And in Islam, all religions same, from Adam (as) until Islam. But in Christianity, they have to come to priest & they tell him: "we do this, we do that". This is not true. This is just for, to earn more money for people. Because When you do something, you musn't tell anybody. Allah forgive this. This is for between you & between Allah. Like maybe you not fasting. Maybe sometimes, all time you do wrong things; drinking or smoking or doing this, who not harming people.

But when it harm people, it is not between you & Allah but between you & this people- Allah give this right for people. If you kill somebody, if you take his money, take his land, take his.. meaning many thing you can do for people. Between you & people, Allah give this right for this people; to forgive you or not forgive you. But what is between you & Allah, if you not tell anybody He (swt) forgives.

This is from the Hadith of Prophet (sas). This is good for people. Allah give opportunity to forgive people. But there is rules. When you do something and not listening & insist for this, Allah not forgive this. And this, nowadays people, when they do something they like to show everybody; "We doing". They are very proud to do this bad thing or to behave like this. It is, many people forget Islam. Even in Muslim countries, they doing this. Allah, insha Allah forgive us, all of us. This for today, it is enough.

Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, Al Fatiha.
