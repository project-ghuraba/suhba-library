It is first order of Allāh Almighty to be clean, to be pure because this life so dirtiness, so much dirtiness in this life. You must be clean from every dirtiness from this life. All Prophets calling people to Paradise. No one of them calling people to this life. No!

This life is going on, never stopping. If you say high life or low life, they are both of them same. As they are ending going to be same. And for high life functions and low life functions are same. We are living in the same atmosphere. Everyone - rich one, poor one, young or old one, educated or non-educated, citizens or peasants - we are living in the same atmosphere. Don't be cheated by devils. No! You can't get out from this atmosphere. Yes! Therefore, satan cheating so many people by saying that "if you are going to be rich or you're going to be high rank person, or to be such a person you should be in another level." No! Another level only for awliyā’ and for real believers, not for others.

Everyone living on same atmosphere. But Allāh Almighty making a different world for His awliyā’ and for His real believers. No affection (not affecting) on them this life's functions. No. They are in their private or special worlds, living. They are living with their Lord but others they are living with satan, they are living with devils. Yes.

Now, people have been invited by their Lord. And inviters, after Prophets, they are Saints, they are awliyā’. Their signs in their assemblies or in their associations you may listen about Last Day and about Qiyāmah, Resurection Day and about Paradise and about eternal life and about your Lord, Allāh Almighty. If you are listening in a person's association [to] such a matters, speaking and inviting you to Paradise, to eternal life, and giving you eternal life's pleasure, 'anzil' - stay with him. Don't leave him, keep him firmly because your happiness with him. Be careful from those people that in their assemblies, in their meetings, nothing they may give for Allāh or for eternal life. Escape from them. That is a good sign for every real seeker, who is asking real, reality. You must look after such a people.

Therefore, two kinds of scholars, every time. One of them for this world, for this life. The second one, they are ‘ulamā’u l-ākhirah, for eternal life's scholars. This must be well known by everyone because they are our heart's doctors, physicians making us happy and giving us more love in our Lord. And, we have been ordered to increase everyday in the love of our Lord Almighty. Everyday. Not for this life, for Allāh. Because we are going to Him Almighty. Be pleased with your Lord for everything. Then He keeps you pleased with Him. And He should be pleased with you. That is endless honor and endless happiness and endless pleasures for us, for son of Ādam.
