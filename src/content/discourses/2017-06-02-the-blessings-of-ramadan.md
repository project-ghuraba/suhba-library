As-salāmu ‘alaykum wa raḥmatu Llāhi wa barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

Shukr to AllāhNone, we reached the first Jum‘ah of Ramaḍān. Days are passing by quickly. It's been seven days of Ramaḍān. Last Friday it was not Ramaḍān but the last day of Sha‘bān. And today it is holy Ramaḍān. Our ProphetNone is saying who fast during Ramaḍān, Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla watches them from the first night. HeNone looks with mercy. Every day angels make tawbah for these people who fast. During Ramaḍān, shayṭāns, the bigger shayṭāns, are chained up and thrown into the oceans so that they don't harm the fasting of Muslims. And when Ramaḍān is over, Allāh 'Azza wa Jalla forgives those who have fasted. They will have reached HisNone forgiveness and will be pure. The evil, dirtiness and burdens will be removed from us. And we will reach ‘Īd light and without sins.

This is the virtuous kindness of AllāhNone. It is the reward HeNone gives in dunyā. And the reward in ākhirah is much higher and much more. To those who are fasting, AllāhNone gives more peace in dunyā compared to those who don't fast. They get the taste of what they eat. Who don't fast can't even imagine such freshness and peace. And they can't get that taste. Therefore when people are eating, if they digest it for the pleasure of AllāhNone, that food is ḥalāl for them. It will have benefit and it will give strength. AllāhNone created us in order for us to know HimNone, to obey HimNone and to worship HimNone. Whatever you do with this awareness, it is accepted in the presence of AllāhNone. But if someone doesn't know it and says "What's this fasting? Why fast in such heat?", that means he came to this world for eating and drinking and that is not an acceptable thing. He won't be an accepted creature. Because there are creatures which came for eating and drinking. They are without speech and have no responsibility. Neither did AllāhNone offer them to pray, nor did HeNone offer them to fast. But they are offered only one thing and they know it very well. They know AllāhNone. They remember AllāhNone - the animals, the four-legged animals. But if the two-legged creature sees no responsibility for himself, then he becomes even worse because he doesn't know AllāhNone. He doesn't know the Creator. He doesn't remember and never thinks of AllāhNone. But animals remember HimNone every moment. They make their dhikr. They eat and drink and wander until their end reaches them. They have no responsibility or accountability.

But mankind is responsible. For the things they don't do AllāhNone will punish them in ākhirah if they are believers. If they believe in AllāhNone and Islām, they will be punished in ākhirah and then be sent to jannah. And if they are even worse and lived without belief in AllāhNone, they will be punished forever. They will neither enter paradise, nor smell it, says our ProphetNone. We ask that AllāhNone protect us and guide them. And don't curse. Cursing is not good. Always ask for goodness. For us, a person coming to guidance is thousand times better than his punishment. It is more favourable which is what we want. A Muslim should wish so. If you see, you should ask that AllāhNone guides and corrects him. And we ask that they take their share of these beauties, in shā’a Llāh.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh today we arrived first Jum‘ah from Ramaḍān, Friday. It is our very blessed day for ummati Muḥammad. AllāhNone grant us this month and these days and months also. And we are, from first day of Ramaḍān Allāh HeNone is looking for believer who are accepting and knowing the Ramaḍān and Ramaḍān worshipping, what you do inside [it], AllāhNone look for them by His Merciful eyes from first day. So al-ḥamdu liLlāh who are in that position what Allāh is happy with this, he will be happy forever. Because AllāhNone when HeNone is giving His blessing for somebody HeNone is not taking anymore, HeNone is generous.

So this is good tiding for believer and in Ramaḍān also every day there are angels they are asking forgiveness for fasting people from Allāh. Every day until end of Ramaḍān. And end of Ramaḍān Allāh HeNone forgive us all what we have from bad thing and become clean for ‘Īd, for feast, ‘Īdu l-Fiṭr. We are, we will be, in shā’a Llāh, happy because this dirtiness coming from bad thing we do. but we know or without knowing it is not good and heaviness and darkness and dirtiness. In shā’a Llāh when Ramaḍān finish AllāhNone take all of this from us.

This happiness AllāhNone grant us, believer. Because many people they are not fasting and they are thinking they are happy. No they are with, every time they are not doing what AllāhNone ordering, one more darkness coming on them, one more heaviness coming for them. Every badness coming for them. And this is for human being order from AllāhNone. And there is many kind of people. Some of them al-ḥamdu liLlāh they are obeying AllāhNone and doing what HeNone said, what HeNone is order, what ProphetNone said. They will be reward in this world and other world also. The next world in Jannah it will be real reward. But even in this world also AllāhNone give them happiness. Give them something nobody can have it, reach it, from people who are not fasting, who are not praying. This taste even material taste also. When somebody fasting and break fast nobody can reach this from anybody from who are not fasting.

For other thing also and other thing we are eating, you can eat, you can drink as much you like but, and do other thing also. But there is thing you must be know - we are doing this not for dunyā, not for happiness of life. No, you must know this. We are eating, drinking going coming for āhkirah, for Allāh. We doing this not for our pleasure, for pleasure of AllāhNone. For Allāh HeNone give pleasure here also and after also. But there is people, there is creature, they are coming for this dunyā for only for eating and drinking. This animal, insect, everything AllāhNone create them for to do this. They are coming, they are quiet, they are not asking anything, not shouting, arguing. Why you are here? What, why you don't give us? Why you are taking? We are not taking. No, they are good. They are only eating drinking and they are dying. But they have no for any responsibility for them. AllāhNone not order them to pray, not order them to fast, not order them to make zakāt or ḥajj. Only they have one order and they make it perfectly they are making, poor creatures. They are knowing AllāhNone and they are mentioning HisNone name, dhikr. They have own dhikr, own mention name of AllāhNone by their think, whole time they are doing this. This order they are doing. But for human being Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla HeNone give orders and who are not doing and they are saying we are also not responsible. There is many kind of people, one of them they are doing, other they are believe in AllāhNone and but not practicing. This people they will be ask in ākhirah and AllāhNone will make them to do what they didn't do here. But it take for them long time. Before that and after when they finish this they come to paradise, jannah. But there is one kind they are not accepting even Creator Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla. And this people they will be for hell forever because they are not accepting.

And, but we are all time asking from Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla to guide this people to right way. We are not cursing them because to curse it's easy but [it is] better for you to get one for right way, to give them good guidance, better than this world. So every time we are asking AllāhNone give this people hidāyah, give them to know right way. AllāhNone make us whole time with people to come to hidāyah, in shā’a Llāh. And AllāhNone bless us for Ramaḍān and many Ramaḍāns, in shā’a Llāh and we are asking also in Jum‘ah time there is time for acceptable duā’ praying. Duā’ accept, in shā’a Llāh, every good thing we are asking especially Mawlānā's saying - ask for Mahdi ‘alayhi s-salām. We are asking, in shā’a Llāh, to come soon to make whole this world clean after this dirtiness, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq el-Fātiḥah.
