Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajim. Bismillahir Rahmanir Rahim.

“Wa ith ta’athana rabbukum la’in shakartum la’azidannakum, wa la’in kafartum inna athaabi lashadid.” (Sura Ibrahim:7)

Allah Azza wa Jalla states: “Allah increases if you are thankful.” For years, thank Allah, there was plentitude, blessing (baraka), and everything. People were not thankful. They were not thankful and they forgot Allah. When people forget Allah, in order for people to remember Him, Allah gives distress and no safety is left for people. All kinds of distress occur. Why? Becasue they have forgotten Allah. All of them, everybody, all believers and unbelievers never saw the blessing (nima), were not thankful to Allah, and wanted more. So Allah is warning us a little now.

Turn to Allah and do not forget Allah. Everything is in the hands of Allah. If you do not forget Allah, the world can burn and nothing would happen to you. However, even if you are in the best place in the world, it would burn you from the inside. Even if you look good from the outside and you have everything, once Allah wants you would burn up inside. But people are satisfied when they do not forget Allah. Allah does not punish them, does not give them distress, and they would not care if the world burnt.

Regarding current events people are asking, “Why is it happening?” One of our ears is being pulled now (we are being admonished). This is a cautioning: Do not go too wild, do not forget Allah, and pay attention to haram (forbidden) and halal (allowed). Eat of the halal, drink of the halal, and go after the halal. Inshallah people get on the right way so these calamities and these tribulations get off of us.

The majortiy is what matters. For the minority, the tribulations Allah Azza wa Jalla will give them is enough for them. Everything is in the hands of Allah. If He wills it happens, and if He does not will it does not happen. Protection is from Allah. Let us seek refuge in Allah.

There are so many troubled lives all over the world. It is ruggedly all over, but they have still protected this country of ours with the blessing of our ancestors. However, they say “Comfort stings”. The more comfortable people are, the more they want, they have greed, and they forget. People forget the good done. People are ungrateful. So do not forget. Let us not be ungrateful towards Allah and let us seek refuge in Allah.

May Allah protect our country and the Nation of Muhammad (Ummat Muhammad). Because this is the capital city of the Ottomans. The whole Islamic world is looking here. There are attacks from all over. There are attacks from the Kufr world. They are saying, “This is the head. Let us remove the head and leave it headless!” They can easily do everything as they want when there is no head. Let us not be a tool then and not let them do what they want Inshallah. Let us cling on to Islam, go in the footsteps of our Prophet, and follow the orders of Allah, Inshallah.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq.

Al-Fatiha.
