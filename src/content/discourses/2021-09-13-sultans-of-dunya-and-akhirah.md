Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Insha’Allah today we intend to make Ziyarah to our holy Mashayikh in their holy places. Insha’Allah their Barakah will be upon us and they will accept our Ziyarah. May Allah increase their stations. They are Sultans both for Dunyā and Ākhirah. Islam has spread everywhere with their Barakah. Their efforts in order to spread Islam and in order for Islam to be served, and the power of Awliya’ led so many people to guidance. Not only did they give guidance, but they also made scholars from each of them by teaching them the knowledge of Dunyā and Ākhirah. They sent their followers and Murids here and everywhere. Thus, they became means for spreading Islam. They wanted this for the pleasure of Allah. They never asked for worldly fame. Allah granted them according to their intentions. Their names and deeds will go on until Qiyamāh.

Since the beginning of the religion of Islam with our Holy Prophet, they have tried to destroy it. And as it is the religion of truth, those who tried to destroy it, were destroyed themselves. They are done, and they’ve become history. It is the time of Fitnah. There is always a war against Islam. Allah is always Al Ghalib, The Victor. And everyone with Him has been victorious too.

Therefore, the holy people who brought this way to us have great Himmah and efforts. They were never lazy. They begged and worshipped Allah day and night; thus, became a means for the guidance of people. With the wisdom of Allah, they brought brightness and light to people. They brought beauty and goodness. They taught all kinds of goodness to people and never taught badness and evil. They are examples to humanity. Millions of people reached guidance through them and are still reaching. Many non-Muslims are getting benefit from their beautiful manners and their beautiful works, and coming to the way of truth. They are people who are on the way of truth.

May Allah increase their stations. Insha’Allah they will accept our Ziyarah and give us from their spirituality. May we not return without spiritual gifts. We are going empty, so may we return full. May Allah make our trip easy, may it pass smoothly insha’Allah. May we go safely and return safely insha’Allah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
