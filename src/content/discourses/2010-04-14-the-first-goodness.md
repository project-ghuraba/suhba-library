Dastur Ya Sayyidi, madad. Madad Ya Rijal Allah. La ilaha illa Allah La ilaha illa Allah La ilaha illa Allah Sayyidina wa Nabiyuna Muhammadun Habibullah wa Rasulullah Nur arshillah, zidhu Ya Rabbi izzan wa sharafa nuran wa surura ridwana wa sultana.

Assalamu alaykum our Attenders. And we are beginning, saying: Audhu Billahi min as shaytani'r rajim Bismillahi'r Rahmani'r Rahim. O our Lord grant us from Your heavenly support to fight against shaytan and its followers. Yes, all ulamas through east and west including Christian learned people, including Jewish learned people, what is our mission? must know it. Dastur Ya Sultan al Awliya, madad. A question. What you are teaching people, Christian or Jewish people and Islamic ulamas, including Salafi ulamas? A question. What you are teaching people?

And, the Seal of Prophets Sayyidina Muhammad (pbuh) his mission was Mu'allim un-Nas al Khayr (sas). What was mission of Jesus Christ? His followers were saying "Our teacher". People were running to Prophets, for what? For learning. Learning and then knowing and then understanding and then standing up about what their mission is they were trying to fulfill their missions.

This addressing is from east to west, from north to south. I am a weak person, I am asking in front of all nations to holy people from Christianity and Judaism and as well I am asking Muslim authorities for teaching people. I am asking what was the teaching of Prophets to people? Just making it clear the Seal of Prophets Sayyidina Muhammad (sas), saying one word, that he had been sent to teach people 'khayr'. Al khayr. This one word included all teachings from all Prophets. Allah Almighty sending 124,000 prophets. All of them were calling people and saying to them "O my Lord's servants, come to me, and listen to me and try to understand me. O people I am coming to you to teach you al khayr, goodness”.

What is opposite of goodness? Badness. That means on this world people are learning and practicing good things. Some other people are not trying to learn and they are doing their worst badness and asking to follow badness. What is goodness, al khayr? Every kind from goodness man must know because man is born and not knowing anything. And his parents teach something first and, O people, our attenders look to me and try to keep it through your minds. It is our old adab, good manners. When a new child is growing up a little bit and beginning, asking to say something our old manner and good manner is to say to that baby, say "Allah, Allah, Allah". That is the top point of khayr is to teach that new baby to open his mouth and to speak first and to learn to say "Allah". And through our traditional knowledge if a person, how they are beginning must go on it. When you are teaching small one to say "Allah, Allah", in the last moment that that one passing away then his tongue is going to say "Allah, Allah, Allah". Opening and finishing going to be "Allah, Allah". From Allah to Allaaah.. Inna Lillahi wa inna ilayhi raji'un. Salafi ulamas, say batil? Ad-deenu nasiha, this is nasiha that is making me to sit down here and to speak to people. To make advice to people. If anything is wrong, say to me that 'You are a wrong one'. I am sitting here empty, then heavenly madad, heavenly support making me to be full of addressing.

O Salafi ulamas! There are some new instruments, like a finger, there is a part and they are saying to me that this small finger-like part may contain perhaps 20 hours of speaking or more. Much more, yes. Subhanallah, Subhan al Mu'allim. Mu'allim un-Nas al Khayr, Rasulullah (sas). Very important point they are giving sometimes. That is just granted to common people and there is an important reality. What is that? I am asking to ulamas.

The Day of Promises Allah Almighty (swt) Jalla wa Aala, Jalla Azamatuhu wa Qudratuhu amma Nawaluhu, Subhan Allah, Sultan Allah. O People, the Day of Promises when Allah Almighty saying, addressing to our Real Beings, not this body, Real Beings, it is beyond our 'Ruh', souls. A dharra/atom and beyond it for an atom there is another reality that such a knowledge you can only find through heavenly people. Yes, Allah Almighty addressing: Alastu bi Rabbikum, O My Creatures, from Mankind, are you accepting Me as your Creator? Rabbukum, your Lord, hanh, say! Your Lord. Accepting Me as your Lord and Creator? They said "Yes, O our Lord, You are our Lord and You are our Creator". Qalu: Bala.

This part of holy verse we are understanding, 3-4 words we are understanding. But the Lord's addressing, the Lord's kalamullah, His speaking is Azilyun-Abadiyun. From pre-Eternal up to Eternal. And through that question believe that countless knowledges, countless sirri/secret Knowledge Oceans, you can't imagine. Allah Almighty is saying Alastu bi Rabbikum? Qalu: Bala. They are admitting His Lordship and that they are His servants. Yes. Through that addressing are oceans of knowledge. Therefore anbiya, prophets are taking knowledge from that Divine addressing from which oceans come, through asking: Alastu bi Rabbikum.

Oceans, you can't find a shore, you can't find a deepness for it. Therefore when he was addressing don't think that that addressing should be for everyone same addressing. Zahir, appearance is the same but don't think that His addressing to Prophets is the same as their followers. There is a general addressing for all, but everyone is taking from that ocean what is for them, privately. Knowledge, endless oceans and through that we are coming to say Mua'llim un-Nas al Khayr, that holy word that the Seal of Prophets declared to nations. He knows the meaning of khayr, goodness. Goodness he knows, it is such an ocean. And he is saying: I am coming to teach people goodness. And, what is goodness? 1,2,3,4,5,6,7. How many goodnesses? Five hundred goodnesses that the Seal of Prophets (sas) was saying to his Nation. Teaching them every kind of goodness. Sayyidina Muhammad (sas) just taught them. Sallallahu alayhi wa sallam. Taught them and others every kind that has been mentioned and they have been taught to his Nation. And the first of them, what is first of the beginning of khayr? To say: Allahu Rabbi Allahu Rabbi, Allahu Rabbi, khalaqani, razaqani, ahyani, amatani, thumma yuhyeeni. First one of khayr, goodness is to know who created him, her. They must learn. O ulamas what you are saying every Juma on minbars? Why you are not saying such things, hmm? So many people also they are using to speak on political aspects, it is haram.

Ulamas can't speak on political subjects. You are only authorised to teach people al khayr, goodness. That is your mission, if you are leaving that direction and you are going like this, left or right, you are getting out from Sirat al Mustaqeem. Therefore Allah Almighty, 40 rakats, every day ordering us to ask from His majestic and most glorious existence. "O our Lord, ihdina as-sirat al mustaqim. Who is going out of that sirat, just direct way they are falling into traps of shaytan. Therefore O People, O ulamas, from Muslim ulamas or Christian World ulamas and Jewish Nation ulamas you must say what we are saying now. It is a heavenly announcement.

Announce for everyone among mankind that they must know what is rightness. What is goodness, top point of goodness. Top point of rightness, that people must learn this then they may walk. Or they are coming down, can't move, can't do anything that people may say to them, "Capital to you". Or when they're passing to Judgement Day in Divine Presence for Divine judgement, all of them will be thrown away. O holy people from Christianity, holy people from Judaism and our ulamas you must say from early morning up to evening as the command of the Lord of Heavens: Wa dhakkir!

Don't say everyday we are saying. You are in need to say: fa inna adh-dhikra tanfa'ul mu'mineen because when you are repeating, it gives much more power, much more lights, much more understanding to Believers and they may keep their ways in safety here and Hereafter and their faces coming in Divine Presence and shining. O People, I am addressing to learned people tonight according to my inspirations and coming to me from heavenly ones I am addressing to you. Keep it. And run after goodness, to do goodness and try to make badness less, less, less, till you are leaving it. When you are leaving badness that means you are leaving shaytan. When you are trying to complete goodness that means that you are approaching the Seal of Prophets (sas). May Allah forgive us for the honour of that one that every creation is created for his honour, Sayyidina Muhammad (sas).

Al Fatiha.
