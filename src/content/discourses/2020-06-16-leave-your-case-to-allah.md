Aū‘dhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-Shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Waṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-F’āiz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-

A’ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Fa-satadhkurūna mā aqūlu lakum, wa-ufawwiḍu amrī ilā Llāh, inna Llāha baṣīrun bi-l-‘ibād” (“And you will remember what I [now] say to you, and I entrust my affair to Allah. Indeed, Allah is Seeing of [His] servants (Qur’ān 40:44). Trust in Allah is the start. It is the prayer of Yaqub (AS). When they said that Yusuf (AS) had died, this blessed prophet of course knew that his son was alive, but because of the pain caused by this separation, he cried so much that his eyes went blind. The sons told him,“ You do not know that Yusuf has died.” At that time Yaqub (AS) made this prayer, “Wa-ufawwiḍu amrī ilā Llāh, inna Llāha baṣīrun bi-l-‘ibād.” “I leave my case to Allah. Allah Almighty sees His servants, what they are going through.” So when there is a matter even in which everything seems against you, you leave it to Allah. Allah knows everything. He knows the truth of the matter. As long as you are innocent before Allah, there is nothing to fear.

What is more important is that you keep your relationship with Allah clean, good. You might be able to fool everyone [else], but not Allah. So we need to know this truth, that Allah is with us every second and at every breath. Do not do evil. Remain with good ones and do good, so that Allah is with you. When you do that, you will not be harmed by anyone. Leave your case with Allah. Know that this world will always and at all times throw at you different kinds of slanders and ugly words—especially in these End Times, which are the times of fitna. To leave it to Allah is the best option, and then Allah will help you. And when He helps you, there is no need for anyone else.“Ḥasbunā Llāhu wa-ni‘ma l-wakīl, ni‘ma l-mawlā, wa-ni‘ma n-naṣīr” (dua). There is none other than Him, no one better than Him or more powerful to help. Even if the whole world is against you, as long as Allah is with you there is no problem, no worry or issue, no fear. May Allah be with us, insha’Allah. May our hearts be pure. May we be amongst who are with Him. May we be amongst the true and clean ones. Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-F ti ah
