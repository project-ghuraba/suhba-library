A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says, “Aṣ-ṣadaqah taraddu l-balā’ wa-tazīdu l‘umur.” Aw kamā qāl. Ṣadaqah is important. it is very important. Mankind has 360 body parts – moving parts, fingers, feet, neck. There are 360 bones all over the body. You should give ṣadaqah for each of them, says our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam.

The Ṣaḥābah asked, “What if we, as poor people, cannot do this?” HeNone said, “Instead, pick up dirt, filth, garbage, or stones from the ground. If you remove stones from the road, it is also ṣadaqah.” It doesn't necessarily have to be money to count as ṣadaqah. Every kind of goodness takes the place of ṣadaqah. A person must give ṣadaqah each day, as gratitude to AllāhNone, for every part of their body. It can be done like this.

Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says, “Give ṣadaqah, even if it is only half a date, not even a whole date.” There was poverty at that time. “If you eat half, give the other half as ṣadaqah. That will protect you from the fire of hell,” heNone said. This is important. People don’t want to give ṣadaqah. Their egos don’t want it. They think it is difficult to give ṣadaqah. It is a very important and beautiful advice for mankind, given by our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Because ṣadaqah is not farḍ for mankind. It is not compulsory, but it is important. They say, “Why should I give ṣadaqah? I give zakāh.” But it's uncertain if they give zakāh. They don't want to give ṣadaqah at all. But they spend tons of money on useless things. Ṣadaqah is a very important act. Ṣadaqah prevents accidents and disasters and protects people from harm. If people understood its benefits, they would give half of their wealth in ṣadaqah every day. It is so important. This is the advice of our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam to hisNone ummah. Don't be stingy. Stinginess is not nice. AllāhNone doesn't like it.
