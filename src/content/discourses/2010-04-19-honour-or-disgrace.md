Madad ya Sultan

La ilaha illa AllahLa ilaha illa AllahLa ilaha illa AllahMuhammadun Rasulullah (sas)

For the honour of Your most honoured and glorified deputy, we are asking heavenly support. And we are saying Assalam alaykum Ya Ibadallahi 's Saliheen, Assalamu alaykum Ya Rijal Allah, Assalam alayka Ya Sahib az-Zaman, Assalam alayka Ya Qutb al-Aqtab, Assalamu alaykum Ibadallahi's Saliheen. Amidduna bi madadikum.

We are seeking your heavenly support because we are most needy ones through our times. And we are saying: Audhu Billahi min as shaytani'r rajim as shaytan is thinking no one can be more powerful than itself. And I am saying 'itself' as that is the lowest level of creatures. No one can be under shaytan's level. Deepest one. But it is thinking 'I am powerful'. And we are running to Lord of Heavens as He ordered to say: Audhu Billahi min as shaytani'r rajim. And we have been granted heavenly support through heavenly sword: Bismillahi'r Rahmani'r Rahim. Bismillahi'r Rahmani'r Rahim is protecting ourselves here, through this life, through our graveyard, through the Day of Resurrection, through Judgement Day.Protecting us, Bismillahi'r Rahmani'r Rahim, Allahu Akbar.

O our Attenders, now we are coming to you. Assalamu alaykum, how are you today? Collecting too much money? Hmm. Say, true? Yes, everyone now is asking to collect more, and more, and more from nonsense treasures of life on this planet. La ilaha illa Allah, La ilaha illa Allah, La ilaha illa Allah. O our Lord, for the honour of Your most honoured one Sayyidina Muhammad (pbuh), if there was a value for treasures of this world Allah Almighty would make those treasures to fall on His most beloved one as rains. He is able to send from heavens pearls, diamonds, rubies, emeralds. Yes? He is able?

O People. Eh, our Salafi ulamas, marhaban, kayf ahwalikum? You like dunya? I think that you may find only a handful people who are hating. Very few people you will find that are not going to prefer rains of pearls, rains of diamonds, rains of rubies, rains of emeralds, rains of so many kinds of precious stones. If raining, I don't think there would be on that place, those who are not running to take something from it. Very, very, very few ones. Try yourself, Salafi ulamas. Don't say to anyone else but you do it, you try yourself, say 'Ehh.. Ohhh.. shaykh, shaykh.. how you are going to be if raining one day from heavens emeralds and pearls and diamonds? What do you think? Say righteously. Say that 'I will not run to take even one'. You can say this? Say, for the honour of the most honoured one, you are not thinking to run and to take a pearl, a diamond, what do you think? Yes, our egos saying to us, 'No, if raining jewels how I can stand here? I must carry as much as possible I must collect, even not bringing a camel and two sacks to fill it but eh, at least yes sir, I am not looking too much but, so beautiful ones, maybe my wife going to be very happy I must put in my pocket some of these pearls or my wife coming on me with a stick. There was raining on that valley jewels but I was ashamed to carry so much then I am remembering, oh my darling you like such diamonds therefore I take, because I am big alim, big scholar, therefore I am only taking and putting to make you happy'. 'Oh, oh, big shaytan. You are shaytan, why not carrying to me even one handful and coming to me and saying you are 'zahid'. What is that zahid? Why not bringing to me?'

Salafi ulamas, Allah Almighty, Qadir wa Muqtadir may do anything and everything. Perhaps through deserts instead of rainwater raining He was raining (jewels). What do you think you are going to do? Allahu Akbar. O People, particularly doctors of Shariah you must hear what Allah Almighty saying: Bismilllahi'r Rahmani'r Rahim,

wa la nabluwannakum (2:155)

Yeah? You know that ayatu'l karima? wa la nabluwwanakum bishayin Hanh? Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar. Allah Almighty saying, 'I am never leaving My servants without tajrubah, I am testing. I am testing them, all those who are responsible adults wa la nabluwannakum. Allah Almighty saying I am not leaving you O My servants, I am going to try everyone, on every level, with so many things, with different tests I am going to test you. You understanding or not, Salafi ulamas? Why you are not saying to people? And all ulamas also that are saying 'We are ulamas, learned ones of Ahl as-Sunnah wa'l Jama`a', or those who are saying, 'We are Shi'a' and that addressing of Allah coming to them also.

The Lord of Heavens looking to His servants and trying and testing them, making some of them to sit on high saying, 'Stay here, but don't touch, that is amanah trust, don't touch it'. When the Lord of Heavens saying, 'Don't touch it', shaytan coming around and saying, 'What are you doing here? I am waiter or guardian of this honey, hive, you taste some of it?' 'It is forbidden for me to taste'. 'Why? Yes, if not eating but taste a little bit, look, I am tasting'. 'Don't touch, shouting to shaytan, don't touch, this is amanah, trust'. 'You, a little bit like this, put'. 'I can't'. Shaytan never leaving till cheating him or putting a trap for him and using its countless trips, saying, 'A little bit, here look, anything happened to me? No'. Never coming to his memory that maybe that is shaytan. And when looking, taking and nothing happening he also is taking.

O People, O Ulamas, Salafi ulamas, I never heard from Salafi ulamas or other doctors of Shariah or ulamas from Azhar to say through their speeches or addressing to people, I never heard one of them warning people and saying, 'O People, do you know that? Beware, beware Generation of Paradise. Yes, they don't know because you're not saying such things. Salafi ulamas you're saying this? Bring your addressing and declaration to people. Ever you said that, 'O Mankind, beware, Generation of Paradise'. It is true or not? It is just true.

And, what happened? We were Generation of Paradise, how it happened that we were thrown out from Paradise? Why you are not saying? What is the reason? Why you are not making clear about Adam and Eve, what happened to them? Why you are not speaking about the beginning of their creation, astaidhu Billah,Rabbuna Jalla wa A`ala said:

wa idh qala rabbuka li'lmalaikati inni ja'ilun filardi khalifah (2:30)

Hmm? That honour is from Allah Almighty asking to come into existence. And rank of deputy highest rank of all Creation not any other only, but to be deputies. This is an ocean of knowledge. What is coming to me to say about such things, it is really an ocean of knowledge. Opening Adam. Allah Almighty, He created by His divine hands O Salafi ulamas! Allah Almighty, Allah Jalla Jalaluhu giving hands to His deputies. What do you think, He hasn't hands? Without hand? And giving us hand and He is without hand? What is that? Yes, say, answer. That there is hand for Allah Jalla Jalaluhu but you can't be able to know real being of the hands of the Lord of Heavens.

Who is giving from His endless treasures of everything. Who is granting? Who is sending from skies, rains? And giving countless pleasures to Earth? And you, Mankind are eating and getting pleasure and through endless contentment. Without a hand, sending? You know such things? I don't know but now coming, and I am saying. I am not claiming that I am knowing something, nothing! But, the Lord of Heavens according to His Divine Will sending to His beloved one and that beloved one sending to some from his ummah o one is knowing them. Only that most, most well-knowing one, he knows as much as the Lord is giving to him, the Seal of Prophets (sas).

How is He giving? Salafi ulamas say? Say! Say realities because people, some of them are asking, people, from beginning up to end, it is their grant now. And they are asking such questions. Yes, Adam and Eve were in Paradise because their ranks were so high and it was impossible with that honour to be sent to Earth as Earth is full of troubles. Everything is giving trouble to people, to mankind but the Lord of Heavens was asking there to be for that new Creation a place according to their maqam/station.

According to their honours therefore, Paradise was brought into existence. And Allah created Adam there and bringing him up. Then what happened? Because men, they were tested, to be under imtihan, yukramul mar'u aw yuhaan Allah Almighty wished to give much more to Adam, to give to him higher paradises-stations, levels, but in first testing he fell down. He fell down. Saying, 'No, you must get out'. What was the reason? O Salafi ulamas you know that: wala taqraba hadhihi 'sh-shajarata fatakuna min az-zalimina (2:35) ayatu'l karima? Subhanahu wa Ta`ala. Allah Almighty ordering, 'Ya Adam, wala taqraba hadhihi 'sh-shajarata fatakuna min az-zalimina.

Amr; Holy Order of the Lord of Heavens, 'I am giving everything but I am prohibiting you from that tree and I am Lord, you are My servant. I have My right to test you'. And every test is going to be a reason for a person either to get up or to fall down. Yes, they are making me to speak on highest of creatures, shaytan coming and saying 'a little bit' as it came entering Paradise and saying, 'Don't eat, but taste,taste, I am happy, I am coming to you to give much more honour and to know much more from secret knowledges that is a tree full of such things that no one knows, only the Lord of Heavens. But they are hearing, listening and following shaytanic suggestion. And coming, 'A little bit, don't eat but taste'. Putting it here and what was raining from divine existence, what was coming on them, was cut off and they are standing as naked ones.

O People, say such wisdoms. Holy Quran is not (just) a book of stories but you must take hikmat, wisdoms. Say to people don't run after dunya but, try to run after heavenly grants. Don't (run after) lowest ones, no and try Allah Almighty. But our ulamas are not saying such things. I am nothing but making me address all people through east and west, from Book-given people also. Those who are granted Old Testament, granted New Testament those who are granted Gospels, Injeel Sharif. To all of them I am bombarding shaytan's aspects. That is my mission. And this is very, very, very simple bombarding. Heavy bombarding coming after me. O People, look, listen, accept or heavy bombarding taking you away.

May Allah forgive us and bless you for the honour of the most honoured one Sayyidina Muhammad (sas). Fatiha.
