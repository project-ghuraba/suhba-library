As-salamu alaikum wa rahmatullahi wa barakatuh.

Audhu billahi min as-shaytani r-rajim. Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. As-salatu wa s-salamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidi l-awwalina wa l-akhirin. Madad ya RasulAllah, madad ya Sadati Ashabi RasulAllah, madad ya Mashaykhina, dastur ya Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Dagestani, Sheikh Muhammad Nazim al-Haqqani dastur. Tariqatuan as-sohbah, wa l-khayru fi jam'iyyah.

Shukr to Allah, with himmah (support) and baraka of Sheikh Efendi, we visited a lot of brothers, we went to many countries. Brothers and mureeds over there, masha'Allah, respected us as they respect Sheikh Efendi and were happy. This shows the highness of a person's faith. The more the spiritual level of a person increases, the more he gets pleasure in akhira. These people were so happy, it felt as if they had been given the whole world. They were happy for their Sheikh to come; the successor of Sheikh Efendi, for the honor of our Sheikh Efendi.

Wherever we go, his support always reaches us, his spirituality is present. Otherwise, we have nothing. We went on this journey by his order to not neglect people to whom he taught goodness to do the things he had done. We went to many places this year. It was necessary. Traveling is sunnah of our Prophet. This is an order to them. It is an order to them so that people come to guidance. They order not to sit still. Because if you sit still, generally, laziness develops and people of the area stop caring and stop valuing. Therefore, Allah Azza wa Jalla always ordered the prophets to travel.

An example of this, Sultan Mahmud Ghaznawi was taking a trip. In his way, he noticed a dergah with smoke coming out of its chimney. So he decided to visit a holy man, Sheikh Hasan al-Kharqani. Sultan Mahmud Ghaznawi went inside. He was from ahl al-sunnah wa l-jama'a. A very strong man who brought Islam to India. When he came inside, he entered as a Sultan. He looked, nobody paying attention. He became upset and asked Sheikh Hasan al-Kharqani "What kind of man was your Sheikh, Sheikh Bayazid Bistami?" He replied "My Sheikh was such, whoever looked upon him became a believer and accepted Islam".

Then the Sultan said to him "How come? Is your Sheikh greater than our Prophet? Because most people then didn't become Muslim. There was Abu Jahl, Abu Lahab, Walid and others. All of them, they looked at our Prophet but didn't become Muslim. How are you saying this? One looking at our Prophet doesn't accept Islam, and one looking at your Sheikh accepts it?" "They didn't look at him as a Prophet. They looked at him as an orphan of Abu Talib. They thought Abu Talib, his uncle is taking care of him and he's an orphan. They didn't value him as a Prophet. But when people looked at Bayazid Bistami, be they Zoroastrians or fire-worshippers, when they knew it was Bayazid Bistami, the longing reached them."

As he said this, Sultan said, "You say correctly". Sultan was clever and a man of understanding, and accepted the truth. After this, he was treated well, and when he was taken to the door, he said to Hz. Hasan al-Kharqani "You didn't look at me when I entered, I won't bother you, I'll go." He said "No. You entered with pride when you came. Now you're going with a surrendered ego, that's why we're honouring you." Thus do the awliya teach adab and manners.

Sheikh Efendi also, as we said, wherever he went in the world, he has left at least one murid or dergah or a point. They were good while he was in this world, insha'Allah now, they will prosper even more with his help. Just like a machine producing a lot with one button, insha'Allah, so too they will operate. All of this will happen with the support and baraka of Sheikh Efendi and by the grace of our Prophet, with the permission of Allah. Our strength is not enough, we are weak people. But they didn't go there for nothing. They had prepared those places. With permission of Allah, Islam, true ahl al-sunnah wa l-jama'a tariqa, lovers of our Prophet will come out of dergahs of our Sheikh Efendi.

We can't do it in a thousand years with our own strength and effort. Sheikh Efendi did it with Allah's permission. They will become light, places of guidance, places of baraka for the area. Goodness, beauty, baraka and light will be spread with Allah's permission. We saw these everywhere we went. Also Sheikh Efendi is almost a year physically separated from us, but he is reaching out to everyone, even more now. He's reaching easily. Everywhere we go people tell us how he helps them, how he comes in their dreams. Therefore, we are on the right path, shukr to Allah. This is a big favour. Everyone should know the value of this favour. May Allah increase good ones. May Allah gather all in good place insha'Allah.

Alhamdulillah, we have come back from long journey to very far place. But where we go everybody you feel you're like in your home. Because they were taught by Mawlana Sheikh. Everything they were learning from him they are continuing to do this. And they are happy, very happy to see us around them as (when) Mawlana came. Because Mawlana now nearly 1 year, his body is not around us but spiritually he's more powerful, everywhere. Because here maybe by body you can see but that far place, they weren't seeing by his body, physically. But spiritually, now he is more powerful and he is supporting his mureeds and all like his children.

Many people, everywhere we go, are saying about his supporting, spiritually. Especially when they are seeing dreams about him, he told some of them what to do, what will happen. So they are Alhamdulillah, very strong in spiritual. And Mawlana, he is in every corner in this world. He has followers and dergah/zawiya. He prepared them and they are waiting to take off. And insha'Allah it will be beginning to spread more and more, the real Islam. Islam it is religion of love. Love of Allah, love of Prophet salallahu alaihi wa sallam. Not love for shaitan or his followers. No. We are loving Allah, we love Prophet salallahu alaihi wa sallam. And Prophet salallahu alaihi wa sallam, he was ordered to go around. Every prophet alaihum us salam, around people, to teach people. Not only sit in his place. Because everybody knows no prophet, his nation happy with him. Every prophet, his nation they was more enemy for him. Other people they believe in him, but every prophet, they are not believing. And specially for our Prophet Muhammad, salallahu alaihi wa sallam.

There is one story about this. There was, you know a Sultan, Mahmud Ghaznawi. He is the conqueror of India. He was very good Sultan, and powerful, and he respect mashsha'ikh and awliya. When he was going to some place with his army he find one cottage. It was smoke coming from its chimney. He know there is zawiya there. He went to see and to make respect for Sheikh. When he entered there, there was Sheikh Hasan Khraqani, from our Golden Chain, big mashsha'ikh, awliya.

They were not caring about Sultan, just sitting, not saying anything. Sultan come and ask him because he was little bit not happy, nobody looking for him, not respecting him. Like to do, to ask something, to make maybe to understand for what this? He said "How is your Sheikh?" Sheikh Hasan Kharqani's Sheikh was Bayazid Bistami. He said, "My Sheikh, who looking for him, even non-Muslim, become Muslim, become believer". Sultan, he said, "O Sheikh, how you say this? Prophet salallahu alaihi wa sallam even was not like this. Many people like Abu Jahl, like Abu Lahab, they are not becoming Muslim. Your Sheikh higher than Prophet?"

"No," he said, "these people they are not looking for Prophet salallahu alaihi wa sallam as a prophet, they are looking as poor man, orphan of Abu Talib. But others, who looking for Bayazid Bistami, they say 'this is Sheikh Bayazid Bistami'. When they are looking this, Allah make them to become Muslim. But other they are proud and not happy and just looking by hate for Prophet salallahu alaihi wa sallam so Allah, He not showing them this Prophecy. This is what was situation in Prophet salallahu alaihi wa sallam's time. Nobody from his nation or tribe accept him, very few people. But others when they are seeing Sheikh Bayazid Bistami, Allah make them to accept Islam, to become Muslim. The power of his light".

This is what happened he said, for him. So Sultan, he was clever one, and he accept the truth. He said, "Yes, Ya Sheikh Hasan, you said truth and we are happy". And after this Sultan take permission to go. Sheikh Hasan Kharaqani take him until door and he respect him. Sultan he said, "No need for this, when I come you didn't do this. No need". He said "No, you come you are proud, 'I am Sultan, I am like this,' but now you are going humble and you accept us so Allah make you higher and higher". This is teaching of our mashsha'ikh. And all around the world, this is Mawlana’s teaching and insha'Allah it will be light for every point of our followers of Mawlana. It will be spread - light and baraka and love for Allah. We believe on this and we are sure it will be spread between East and West, because Mawlana he never was resting. All time was traveling to spread this, and it has wisdom. We are believing in this, Allah not make his tiredness to go for nothing, because he is really from biggest of mashsha'ikh. His baraka, it's enough for us until Qiyama. We are here just to make people happy. We are continuing his way insha'Allah. Thank you to everybody.

Wa l-hamdu li'Llahi Rabbi l-Alamin, al Fatiha.
