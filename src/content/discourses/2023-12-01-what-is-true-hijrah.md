As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

As we said yesterday, Islām has its orders. There are orders, as we said, that change or are dependent on the situation. They have conditions. One of these orders is the order to migrate, that came during the time of our Prophet, ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. The order came for them to migrate from Makkah to Madīnah. They were all supposed to follow that order. But of course, the polytheists of Makkah didn’t let them go. They did not allow them to migrate. Those who were able to migrate either left in secret, or were one of the strong ones to whom they couldn’t do anything. Those were able to leave. The poor or weak ones were forced to stay there. But this was the order of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. This order stayed until Makkah was conquered. When Makkah al-Mukarramah was conquered, that order was lifted. So migration was not farḍ, obligatory, anymore.

When is migration allowed? When people are forced to migrate – the enemy comes and removes them, sending them away – or when a person migrates for his religion. His migration is allowed. This migration will be for the next life, not for this world; not away from the troubles of this world, but to avoid endangering their hereafter. That person is migrating to protect his religion, language and honour. There is reward for them too. AllāhNone rewards them. Otherwise, as we said, if they are forcing them to change their religion... If there is an opening, they may leave by that way. Otherwise, if he can preserve his religion as long as he can, it is allowed [to stay]. But most of the time people become so wild. They don’t give any value to mankind. They have neither compassion nor mercy.

Therefore, migration is appropriate in some places. But as we said, the true migration was the migration in the time of our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. It was an order. They all had to leave. Thanks to AllāhNone, ten years later, they came back to their place again. In shā’a Llāh, this is... Now they are constricting and pressuring Muslims everywhere. No matter how much they constrict, eventually AllāhNone is the Victor. Shayṭān and those with him are always in loss. As Mawlānā Shaykh says, shayṭān and those with shayṭān may think they won. But in the end, they will see that they didn’t win anything. They lost everything. They have no share in any goodness. They have no share in the goodness of this world or the hereafter. Oppressors will have their punishment. Oppression does not come from AllāhNone – only punishment comes. People oppress themselves. Nobody else oppresses them. A person does it to himself. When he oppresses others, AllāhNone punishes him. He suffers the outcome of his actions. May AllāhNone be with us, in shā’a Llāh. May AllāhNone protect the Muslims.

As we see, the world everywhere exists on oppression. Now is the end of times. The situation is that there is nothing but oppression. There is not even a bit of justice. In the past, they used to deceive people. Now they don't even deceive people anymore. They don’t care. “Whether they fall for it or not, we will oppress. We will continue with our oppression. Let people do what they can.” Everything is in the Hand of AllāhNone. HeNone will certainly punish them.

There was, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are... There is, like yesterday I was saying about, some order in Islām. There was... there is time or condition. There was, sometimes you can do, sometimes not. But what we said yesterday: some things, you cannot change this order. All time you must do. But there is also order, like to, for hijrah. Hijrah, meaning emigrate. This was order for Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam and hisNone companions, from Makkah to Madīnah, to make hijrah. To emigrate from Makkah to Madīnah, because it was not possible anymore to be with this oppression people, who was making every torturing, giving bad time. Even they was beginning to kill Muslims in Makkah. So Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla ordered believer, mu’min, who is in Makkah to emigrate to Madīnah.

This was order. Everybody must do it. But what happen, only strong one or who has money or other, they can – they could emigrate from Makkah to Madīnah. But poor people, weak people, they cannot, because they keep them like prisoner in Makkah. Other, they went to Madīnah. And who went to Madīnah, ordered – reached Madīnah, AllāhNone give them, reward them, because this, they make this order. And this order, hijrah order, was until ten years, until conquer of Makkah again, from Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam and hisNone companions. When heNone come and conquer Makkah, this order finish. Emigrate order. It was until now [that point], and there was big reward for who emigrate, because they emigrate, they left everything. Only by their clothes or by their horse or other, any... only nothing. They left everything what they got from fortune, from gold, house, land, goods. Everything they left and they – for sake of AllāhNone, order of AllāhNone, they have been in Madīnah with Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam.

And of course, these ten years with Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, the most precious time in history of human being. Of course, there was nothing to eat, to drink and to wear and there was fighting all time, but it was the most precious time for them. But this is important: when they conquered Makkah, this order finish. The big reward of AllāhNone for who emigrate, finish. So other time... After this, many thing happen since that time until now. Many time, people they emigrate, they run away from people, oppressor people, from... many things happen. This, who emigrate to be safe from enemy from who they are not allowed him to pray, not allowed him to practice Islām, also AllāhNone reward them. But many people they emigrate just for dunyā. This is not real emigrate. Emigrate, real emigrate, for to keep yourself and your family, your honour, your religion, to keep them with you. Because many place, they are, first thing they was – like Spain or other place, Spain, Russia, China, everywhere – they was... first thing you, they must, you must leave your religion, or you will die. We will kill you. So this you can, if you find a way, run away it was okay. But to accept and to stay there, this is, it was not good. So it is, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla testing people, many people. Many people they cannot do this.

But AllāhNone, HeNone said you must emigrate for sake of AllāhNone, to keep your – not this life, other life – safe. This life it is... if you save your life now, at the end you will die. You will not be forever here. After, you will be suffering in ākhirah, because you are not listening for order of AllāhNone. But for people who are running after dunyā, no any benefit. First thing to, you must – you must think about your religion, your family, your your neighbour, your... everything. You must be careful for sake of AllāhNone, not for sake of dunyā. But people, of course maybe they can say, "Okay, we can save ourself." But if you save yourself, but your son or grandson or other son, it is in danger. AllāhNone keep them safe, in shā’a Llāh. We are what, we cannot do this. Because everywhere now full of dangers, and full of enemy of religion. Any religion they are – especially for Islām, the most enemy for this. Whole world coming against. But it is, no mind, never mind. AllāhNone with us, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone save us, keep us safe, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
