As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

By the will of AllāhNone everyone will depart from this world one day. We ask that AllāhNone gives us health and does not separate us from īmān. One of our brothers, a representative in Switzerland, had an accident and passed away today. And we should be happy that he left with īmān and found Islām in a place like Switzerland. And he found Ṭarīqah.

His belief was firm. Over 30 years ago he met Mawlānā, they say by chance. It is not by chance. AllāhNone appointed that time for him to meet Mawlānā and be honoured with Islām. He was meant to meet Mawlānā on that day, in that place, to choose to become Muslim. And he joined the Ṭarīqah. For 30 years, maybe more than 30 years, he has been doing service. He would come a couple of times throughout the year.

We are sad that he passed away in an accident. However, in shā’a Llāh our sadness... AllāhNone is Arḥamu r-Rāḥimīn. There is wisdom in his passing away like that. He left with the rank of shahīd. He was unique anyway. Despite everything, he was trying to teach Islām on his own. He would wear Islamic clothing, and get along with everyone. He was a good example. Always smiling, he was never seen fighting with anyone. Therefore, may he have a high station. He is gone to the eternal world, the true abode.

This world is an illusion. It will pass in some way. Important is to pass away with īmān. People of Ṭarīqah leave with īmān within ṭarīqah and reach Mawlānā's presence, Muslims, and people of Ṭarīqah. A person who leaves Muslim, meaning with īmān, his soul hangs in Paradise like a bird. Who goes to Hell remains in Hell. They say they call spirits. Spirits don't come. What comes is a silly thing. As we said, important is his service and creed. These are the things that will reach him following after. He will be with his loved ones, with his Shaykh and our ProphetNone, with Allāh'sNone permission. May AllāhNone increase his station.

Of course, they have their own ceremonies to be done. We will pray ṣalātu l-ghā’ib after he is washed, in shā’a Llāh. They don't bury immediately in Europe. They make a lot of things. In shā’a Llāh it will be good if brothers living closer to his area go to his janāzah. AllāhNone will reward them for their every step. Our life of course some day it will be end. We are not living in this world forever. Eternal life – after death. So, today, last night, yesterday we hear one news, sad, about our brother ‘Abdu sh-Shakūr from Switzerland, he has accident and he pass away. Pass away when he has accident with īmān in hand, is you counted as shahīd, martyr. Martyr.

He was real very sincere, very nice murīd, all time laughing. Since first time I meet him in Basel, he was laughing and I was, "Why this man laughing too much." So he was like this. And he invite us for to one and half year to Switzerland. He was really very good, make his best to bring people to Islām, to bring murīd together, to help people, to show them right way. And this, since maybe 30 years or more we know him. And he said, was telling us how he become Muslim. You say accidently, but nothing accidently. It was AllāhNone prepare this time and place for him to meet Mawlānā. And when he meet Mawlānā he become Muslim. You say where it was? He said, "I was in petrol station, selling, help for working in petrol station and saw Mawlānā Shaykh there," and immediately he become Muslim, and he become real sincere one. Since more than, I think more than 30 years. So I know him since '85. He was before maybe two years – maybe 35 years he is Muslim.

And he with accident, AllāhNone also want this. We are sad for him, but maybe he is happy with this because become martyr and his, he is higher and higher, in shā’a Llāh, in Divine Presence. And he will be, in shā’a Llāh, with who love – he loved Mawlānā, he loved Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. We are sure he is not going to bad place. Even we are sad for his – we will not see him in this world – but, in shā’a Llāh, also we will meet again, not forever this separat[ion]. It is short time. Life it is so fast. So, in shā’a Llāh, for Jannah we meet him, in shā’a Llāh, there.

And in Europe they make too much time to make preparation for janāzah but when he will be prepared, we will pray for him ghā’ib janāzah namāz. But who as near this area from our murīd, we ask them who can go to attend his funeral, he must go there, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone reward these people who is going also there. AllāhNone raise his maqām and give hidāyah for his family to be, he will be more happy with this, in shā’a Llāh.

AllāhNone save you, AllāhNone give hidāyah for other people, because there is many good people in Europe and, but they are all cheated. They are very... they have like... pollution of knowledge – showing Islām as bad. But if they know, all they will be, most of them will be Muslim. But there is real attacking for Islām, and they are making many thing, many theatre, but bloody theatre they make, and they are saying, "This is Islām, this is... Don't go there."

Because before, in time of Mawlānā, when he first going to England, there was many English, German, coming by hundred, by thousand people to Islām. And they make this theatre – first one to Iranian revolution. They say, "Look, Islām this is." Second they make with... Afghanistan and other. All this, this all not from Islām. This is theatre, bloody theatre from enemy of Islām, to not let people to come to Islām. Even they kill their own people, they are not, no harm for them to do this. So, real one like Shaykh ‘Abdu sh-Shakūr, there is million like him, but they are cheated to not be come to Islām. AllāhNone give hidāyah for all of them, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
