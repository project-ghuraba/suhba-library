As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh. A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Fa-lā taghurrannakumu l-ḥayātu d-dunyā wa-lā yaghurrannakum bi-Llāhi l-gharūr" (31:33). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘aẓīm.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says: "Don't be cheated by the life of this world and don't be proud." "I am better than these people, I have more wealth and property, my rank is higher." A person who is proud of his life in this world is an idiot. Because this world does not belong to anyone and it won't belong to you. Moreover, it is unclear what will happen to it in the end. Everything can happen in this world. Therefore, it is important to be on the way of AllāhNone. And do not go against Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.

Your pride can take you to such destruction that you won't recognize Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. You won't want HimNone or accept HimNone. Pride is something that comes from arrogance. During the last years, it has increased a lot in this world. They think they are important and don't accept AllāhNone. They don't accept because of their pride and arrogance. You will be the loser if you think you are important in this life of two days. This is not harmful to others. This badness that is done will turn back on yourselves. It will bring loss to you.
