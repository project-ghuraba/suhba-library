As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
إِنَّ نَاشِئَةَ اللَّيْلِ هِيَ أَشَدُّ وَطْئًا وَأَقْوَمُ قِيلًا
<br>“Inna nāshi'ata l-layli hiya ashaddu waṭ'an wa-aqwamu qīlā”<br>
“Verily, the rising by night (for Tahajjud prayer) is very hard and most potent and good for governing (the soul), and most suitable for (understanding) the Word (of Allah).” (Qurʼān 73:6). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says that nighttime is more powerful and effective for worship. Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam said that worship performed at night is far more acceptable than worship performed during the day. Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam said, “Whoever prays two rak’ahs at night will earn the reward of praying a thousand rak’ahs during the day.”

Waking up just before Fajr prayer and performing even two rak’ahs of Taḥajjud prayer brings that great reward. Of course, one can perform more; such as the prayer of ablution, the prayer of shukr, Taḥajjud, and then the prayer of najāt (salvation). Shukr to Allah that these prayers are a means for us to draw closer to Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says in a ḥadīth Qudsi, “My servant draws near to Me through nāfilah worship.” Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says, “The closer one draws to Me, the more I will become his tongue with which he talks, and his sense of sight with which he sees, and his hand with which he grips.” Of course, Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla’s wisdom becomes in that person. The more difficult these acts of worship are for your ego, the more reward Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla gives you. Along with this, spiritual strength is also gained.

Therefore, performing Taḥajjud and Nāfilah prayers at night while people are sleeping is a great blessing for those who are able to do so. Those who can do this should thank Allah and praise Allah. They should not look at others and say, “I do it, they don’t,” and degrade them; this is also important. It should not be forgotten that Allah has granted this to you, and you are performing it; He has not granted it to them. If you say, “I did it, I performed it,” all your worship will be in vain.

Therefore, if you are able to perform your acts of worship, thank and praise Allah and say, “May it not decrease, but increase. May Allah protect me. May Allah keep me steadfast and help me.”

Many people say, “We cannot get up for Taḥajjud prayer.” This is due both to going to bed late at night and to the shorter nights in the summer. They think, “I’ll just sleep a little and then wake up,” and before they know it, it’s morning. Even if it’s morning, if there is time, they can still get up before sunrise and perform those acts of worship. If time is running out, they should perform the Fajr prayer directly without missing the time.

As we said, even those who never pray should gradually start praying whenever they can. Prayer is very important; for prayer is the pillar of religion. Also, as we said, our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam recommended night prayers as a favor to us.

Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam often rose for worship at night. Even before the five daily prayers were made obligatory, our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam would rise for night prayers and worship. May Allah help us and keep these good deeds perpetual upon us, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
