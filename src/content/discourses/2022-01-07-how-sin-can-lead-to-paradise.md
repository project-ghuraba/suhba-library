As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Our religion is beautiful. AllāhNone is beautiful. The holy ProphetNone is beautiful. Everything is beautiful. And a person who follows this religion is beautiful too. This is the religion of mercy. The mercy of AllāhNone will be upon those who follow this religion. All blessings and beauties will be upon them. People make mistakes and commit sins. They can do everything but Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla forgives all of it when they repent. HeNone even changes sins into goodness.

Our holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam is saying in some of his ḥadīth, there was a man who didn't earn so much reward. He wondered how he attained them. He was told, "You committed so many sins but when you repented and asked forgiveness from AllāhNone, when you regretted, your sins were erased and reward was written instead." Our religion is so beautiful. Yet people do without knowing... Who are clever follow this religion and believe in AllāhNone. They follow our holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam and love himNone. To love himNone is salvation for us. It is good for us. Otherwise, the more enmity you have, the more loss you get. Nothing will happen to them, you are nothing in front of them. Who stand against Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla have no mind. They are stupid.

People of the present time are cheated. It is because of their young age as well. They want to look tough and act so. But they do something and regret it. When they regret and repent for it, AllāhNone accepts their repentance. "Who asks forgiveness will have MyNone forgiveness," says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Our holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says in his ḥadīth sharīf, "Sometimes a man commits a sin and that sin becomes a means for him to enter paradise." Our holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam said so. Ṣaḥābah were surprised, "How is that, yā Rasūlu Llāh? A sinner will go to paradise with his sins?" A sin became the means for this man to enter paradise. Ṣaḥābah were astonished. Our holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam said, "He committed a sin but he regretted it and he repented. Therefore, because of his repentance, he went to paradise. If he hadn't committed that sin, it is possible that he would have gone the wrong way and ended up in hell. However, due to that sin, he repented and AllāhNone looked at him with mercy and put him in paradise." It is a beautiful religion. There is no difficulty. Those who make it difficult are people who don't know this religion, who don't know about ṭarīqah and sharī‘ah. People who know about ṭarīqah and sharī‘ah know how tolerant and how easy it is. Otherwise, some people have ideas that can take all people to hell. But they have no standing. They have nothing. They have no foundation. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla always says, "INone am al-Ghaffār. INone am forgiving. Repent and INone will accept your repentance."

Shukr to AllāhNone, we are from this ummah. We are going on the way of our holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Ṭarīqah is hisNone way. We are going on hisNone way, shukr to AllāhNone. May AllāhNone make all of us steadfast. And may AllāhNone show this beautiful way to those who are not on it. May HeNone give guidance, in shā’a Llāh.

Our religion, our GodNone, our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam – beautiful all of them, very beautiful. You will be beautiful if you're following them. If you don't follow them, no beauty for you at all, only ugliness. But beauty for who follow this beautiful way, way of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam who take you to highest place and to be in paradise, in shā’a Llāh. This is our way. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone is merciful. HeNone most merciful for people, for human being. And HeNone call them to taste this nice taste which [is] īmān. Who taste this belief, īmān taste, he will be not forget it and not taking any taste from other thing. Because, other thing, it is only colour, only like plastic, no taste for them. Maybe you can see them like good, beautiful, but it is only ugly. When you taste, you will feel not happy with them. But who taste this belief, īmān, he will be happy for whole time.

And Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, He'sNone open HisNone, we are open HisNone mercy door for everybody. Because human being, AllāhNone create them to make fault, to make wrong thing, to make bad thing. But HisNone forgiveness door, it is open. What you done, if you regret and ask for forgiveness, AllāhNone saying, "INone will forgive him. INone am the most forgiving." For no [one] more than Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla can carry this. Because they do bad and HeNone forgive them. They do bad again, even hundred time, HeNone said, "INone will forgive them if they ask for forgiveness." So for this, we are lucky. Human being, whole human being lucky but they are not understanding this and not accepting. Who has brain, he has intelligence, he will be accept this. Who they are not accepting this, they are not clever, they are not understanding. Because, this thing is openly for everybody who think and look and listen carefully. He must accept this reality, this truth of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.

And Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying, "Who accept, INone will be forgive him and he will be happy." Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam also saying this. And this ḥadīth we read, heNone said, "Some sin..." Sayyidunā Muḥammad ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, "Some sin man can do, take him to paradise." Ṣaḥābah was wondering how it can be somebody do sin and go to paradise? "Must be we don't understand how this." They ask Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam and Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, "Somebody can do sin, and after this he will be regret and asking for forgiveness. And it will be... he put his sin on his front of him – every time remember this he regret and ask for forgiveness. AllāhNone will forgive him and put him in paradise." This is our religion's beauty. If in dunyā you do something, they will not forgive you. What you done you must take punishment. But Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone forgive. Not looking for [fault]. When you ask forgiveness, HeNone will forgive you and reward you. And sometimes, people coming for Day of Resurrection, and they saw they have too much reward. Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, they're wondering from where this coming. "We didn't do good deed." Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, "They are, when they regret and ask for forgiveness for their sins, AllāhNone forgive them and change this sins to reward." And this coming this.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh we are happy to be in nation of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam and we ask Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla to make us stable and to make other people to come to this nice religion, nice way of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, who we are created for this, not for dunyā. We [are] create[d] for ākhirah. In shā’a Llāh, AllāhNone help us and make people to touch this good thing, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
