Waṣ-ṣalātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīn wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā

Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad.

Today, in the End Time, people have left spirituality, [and] are following material life. They have forgotten the spiritual life completely. The idea of a Next Life does not come into their minds. They say that the population of the world has increased so much, [and that] we are going to live on another planet, but they do not realize that the end is very near.

There is a designated time for you—only until that year will you survive. And other people also have a designated date. Since the time of Adam (AS) until now, every tribe has its place. We are at the end—after now there is no time left. They say, “I will go here, there…” You are not able to live in many places [on this planet], but [then] say that you will get up and go to another place. They do not know that they cannot do this, but they are saying it to keep people away from spiritual life.

Allah gave intelligence to people but they do not use it. They do not use it on the way of righteousness, then they get upset. They think we are going to be here on Earth forever. You will stay only a little time, after that there is the Next Life. Make your next life thriving. Insha’Allah, may all of you have a thriving next life, [and] may you all be successful. Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah
