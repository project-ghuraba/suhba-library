Dastur ya Sayyidi, dastur Ya Sultan, madad.

Allah Allah Allah Allah Allah Allah Aziz Allah Allah Allah Allah Allah Allah Allah Subhan Allah Allah Allah Allah Allah Allah Allah Karim Allah Allah Allah Allah Allah Allah Allah Sultan Allah Ya Rabbi salli wa sallim ala Sayyidina Muhammad wa Alihi wa Sahbihi wa sallim.

Madad, Ya Sahib al Waqt, madad. We are saying Assalamu alaikum our Attenders. Those who are attending are Ahl us-Sa`ad, happy people, beloved people and honoured people. Those who are not listening, escaping, no honour and no pleasure for them here or Hereafter because they are making themselves slaves for shaytan.

O our Lord, protect us! Protect us. We like to be your humble servants and Your grants, never-ending grants, You are giving. O Ya Rabbi Subhan sen, Sultan sen. We are saying: Audhu Billahi min as-shaytan ar-rajim O our Lord! Protect us, Your weak servants from shaytan and its trips and traps. He fell down and is asking to make all Mankind to fall into darkness territories. No life there. They are not living and they are not dying. Such a terrible situation for those who are following shaytan. No hope for them, no lights for them no pleasure for them. They are not living and they are not dying. They will ask to die. They will say, "We prefer to die".

When Allah Almighty is ordering creatures that are on this planet, "Be earth! Be dust! You are created from earth, go back to earth". That finished. Nothing. Those people who have been granted to be honoured ones, as deputies of the Lord of Heavens, when they look and see that all creatures are going to be dust and animals' world going to disappear, coming back to be dust, coming from earth and going to be once again earth, they will ask but they never become dust. They are not living and they are not dying. Allah Allah, Aman Ya Rabb.

People are thinking that this life is a joke. They are denying the beginning as well as the ending. They are saying this is such a dream it just began and just finished. Their earthly life, it is true they are not knowing its beginning but mostly, they are knowing their end, therefore they will say, "Oh, if we can be unknown ones, if we can be as animals' world that are going back to be earth". But never! They will be punished. That is our knowledge, reaching to that point.

And we are saying: Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Rahim. O our Lord protect us protect and grant to Your weak creatures weak servants, your divine protection. The one who says 'La ilaha illa Allah' through his life you may think that even once to say 'La ilaha illa Allah' giving something, that is something in our sights but what is happening when a person says: La ilaha illa Allah? That is an unknown treasure. As Prophet (pbuh) was saying, "When a person saying once, 'La ilaha illa Allah', we are not going to give our judgement for that one". He whose tongue even once said: La ilah illa Allah, no one is knowing what is going to be for that one, how he would be dealt with in Divine Presence. That is so big, great grant from Allah Almighty. Allahu Akbar.

O People, come and say La ilaha illa Allah, La ilaha illa Allah, La ilaha illa Allah, Sayyidina Muhammadun Rasulullah alayhi salatullah. And we are saying: Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Rahim He knows. But now people are thinking that this life is a joke, no. This is not a joke, this is a Reality. There is a Reality for this planet and everything in existence. There is a Reality which belongs to that one. Even for an atom there is a Reality. An atom that is coming in existence.. I heard from our Grandshaykh he was saying that every atom, dharra, has a Reality. There can't be anything in existence without a Reality. And if coming in existence Allah (swt) Allah Almighty is saying:

Wain minshayin illa yusabbihu bihamdihi walakinla tafqahoona tasbeehahum There is nothing but celebrates His praise, and yet you understand not how they declare His glory (17:44).

Everything in existence must have a tasbeeh, glorification for its Creator. What about Mankind? What is going to be for Mankind? How many atoms do we have? And everyone is glorifying, but the honour that should be granted to everyone for their glorifying is something else.

The Lord of Heavens has sent His Prophets, His distinguished ones to say to them, “Your whole body and all atoms through your physical being are glorifying their Creator. You have been granted also a mind for understanding and you are following shaytan and not saying (La ilaha illa Allah). That is a blame on you. On the Day of Resurrection when his physical being is glorifying his Creator, that person will be trembling and ashamed, "How is everything in my body glorifying, why I am not glorifying? Why was I not one of glorifying ones through my life?" That shame perhaps is going to be a greater punishment for them than to be thrown in Fire. They will blame themselves in the Fire through Hell, "How my physical being was glorifying its Creator and I am not using my good will to glorify my Lord?"

They lost their level or the best condition from Allah Almighty's grants. They lost it, but down below, they will have something and no one knows what is going to be for them. That is Hell's Fire. Really, it is their fire burning them. If you could give to them everything from Paradise, its favours and grants, they would say, "It is not for me, I am ashamed to eat from this because I did my worst and I cannot eat." If eating, they are not going to be in pleasure or enjoying themselves. That is cut down. Otherwise our physical beings' glorifying would continue, but they will say that they are giving their punishment themselves. If maybe, put in front of them all of Paradises' pleasures they would say, “We are making this haram for us because we didn't glorify our Lord (even) for a short time and we are receiving this punishment”. Therefore it will be said to them "You give judgement for yourself now". What we are saying now a like a drop from an ocean (in comparison to) what is going to appear:

Yawma tubla alssarairu The Day all secrets will be revealed (86:9).

O Salafi ulamas, marhaban! Marhaban, marhaban! If it is true or not -what you are hearing from a weak servant? Ask! Ask someone from your trustworthy people, if it is true or wrong? Ask them what that servant is saying, if it is true or not? What they should say to you, your Salafi ulamas. They should say that it is wrong or it is right because to be heedless bringing to people every badness here and every blame through next life. They are never going to finish blaming themselves up to Eternity. It will be said to them, "O People, eat! Eat, this is from Paradise". They will say, "We are not eating, it is haram for us because we did something that was such bad action, bad thinking, blame on us. We are making haram, prohibiting our egos from eating up to Eternity".

Faalhukmu lillahi alAAaliyyi alkabeeri The judgement is with Allah, Most High, Most Great (40:12).

Final judgement is for Allah Almighty. There are so many unknown points mentioned through the Holy Quran but not everyone can understand them. Quran Karim is not like an ordinary book. You are reading it and understanding one understanding. Holy Quran is our Lord's glorified book, granted to the Seal of Prophets (sas). Because you are not giving your understanding to know something therefore you are going to be punished. As a punishment, you may give punishment for yourselves.

And we are saying: Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Rahim. O our Lord, don't leave us to be heedless. Don't leave us to be occupied by earthly, material enjoyments. Material enjoyments punish people forever. They will be ashamed up to Eternity. Also, there is not one kind of eternity. Countless eternities for people. Perhaps for everyone there is an eternal direction through his eternity. He should be happy and enjoying and pleased and with pleasure. Everyone's eternity is something else. That is a new understanding from Holy Quran also.

O People, come and listen before last moment coming to you and making you to fall into an endless loss of your chance. Therefore they can't ask to come back to change their eternity to another level as it will be said to them, “No. You are on same Eternity. If we were taking you from end up to beginning you are going to remain same because you lost that which you were through, you wasted your lives through dunya, therefore your eternity is never going to be changed. If we are bringing you to the beginning, your beginning will go up the same way. You will find the same one. Your only chance was your life on this planet, your life in dunya. You chose with your will this direction that is Eternal. You chose this and you were following it. If we bring you back, you will use the same, to go up like before. Therefore no”. They will ask, "O our Lord, let us to go once again to do our best (instead of) what we are doing now, worst. It will be said to them "No way, because you chose this direction through this life. This was your eternity and if thousand times we are bringing it back, you will continue to be on same direction”.

O People, believe in Allah, believe in your Creator. Believe what He is promising to you here and Hereafter. That is important. Choose here, Paradise or Hell. But you are thinking that Hell is never going to happen and you are asking to use the way of your egos’ pleasures. And everyone is going in that direction. Millions or billions of people should have the same way to Eternity. Therefore no need to come and be good. Allah knows what they chose. Thousand times you are going on same eternity-line and saying, "Oh, if we could get back". No, no benefit to bring you once again. Subhanallah. Subhan al Muqtadir.

Our Lord did everything. He sent us Holy Books making clear our futures. Use it! Use it! O People, choose your Eternity, as some of them are best for you and some worst for you. If you are choosing best ones you will reach to a position so that every moment or second, or less than second, a changing is coming because you are believing in Eternity, Eternal Life, as its pleasures are changing, not as a photocopy, no.

This is for people who reached to that point, tooba lahum, good tidings for them, but those who are not thinking and following an eternity that their egos are happy with, if at the ending they are asking to come back, same going, coming. And there is this hayatu'd-dunya, life on this planet, it is so short, 3 days. As 3 days. Be patient for 3 days not to listen to your egoistic pleasures, but follow the Way of your Lord, to make Him happy and you should be happy. May Allah forgive us.

Dunya is deceiving people and people are going in the wrong direction for their eternities and eternity is for everyone. There is good eternity, there is bad eternity. You are free, run after cars, run after nightclubs, run after enjoyment on this planet; run after every materialist aspect. Run after dunya, run after its treasures, run after its diamonds, you are free. But it is not going to be changed on the Day of Resurrection when you may ask to come back. No, now you must choose good way or bad eternity.

All prophets coming and calling people to good directions and good eternity, full with pleasures that every moment, you can't imagine. Also second ones should be repenting that they were running after their egoistic pleasures. Take, same ones going with you. Never-ending, never changing this way, like this. May Allah forgive us.

O doctors of Shariah, or doctors in Christianity, or doctors in Judaism, you must speak about such Realities as mentioned through Holy Books and finally through Holy Quran. Good tidings, endless good tidings for those who are trying to understand some good tidings from Allah Almighty. Tooba lahum, this is biggest pleasure for them. O People, don't be cheated through dunya and its nonsense pleasures. Give through 24 hours even one hour. If you are not giving 24 hours for your Lord, give less, less, less. At least try to give for your Lord time, to say, "Glory to my Lord, Who created me, and directed me through His Prophets to direction of Paradise, Eternal Life". And refuse shaytan's ideas and followers. If not, you are going with those people who are never reaching any real pleasure here or Hereafter.

O People, we have been created and granted from our Lord such a perfect creation for understanding but you are not using your best understanding and you are not understanding what has been said, what has been sent through Holy Books. All prophets came to show real ways to Allah Almighty's pleasures. May Allah forgive us and send us someone who has tasted real taste of our lives, not like those people who are asking a pleasure, full pleasure through their egoistic desires.

May Allah forgive me and you not to be changed and we know that we are in need of someone who can send us on right way, on right direction, on right understanding because people mostly are on bad or wrong understanding. Try to come for a new understanding, the best understanding. You will be happy here and Hereafter.

Allah Allah Allah Allah, Allah Allah Karim Allah, Allah Allah Allah Allah, Allah Allah Allah Allah, Allah Allah Allah Allah, Allah Allah Karim Allah.

Fatiha
