Salamu alaykum all my brothers and sisters.

First of all I like to thank everybody, everybody, that you give your best by giving your du'a, by supporting, by reciting Yasin, reciting kalima tawhid, reciting Qurani Karim and reciting dhikrullah and reciting all the necessary things for curing of Mawlana Shaykh.

It is the most important things in our lives is Mawlana Shaykh. So that's why please keep continuing by your prayer, keep continuing by asking Allah Almighty to give shifa for Mawlana Shaykh, to give him good health and remaining him until Mahdi alayhi’s-salam time.

And insha Allah we are believing and hoping that Allah Almighty will give him the curing in few days time. And also I like to thank the people, they are donating their blood in the hospital. This is very important mission and masha Allah been doing well and Alhamdulillah, Mawlana Shaykh he take so many blood from so many murid all over the world.

Subhanallah, some people they coming from South Africa they have match, their blood match with Mawlana Shaykh, some people coming from England their blood match with Mawlana Shaykh. So Mawlana Shaykh he was taking so many units of blood that it is been from only murids and this is Subhanallah, this is not coincidence, and it is not things that you think about it and it's happened. This is Subhanallah, so you have to know that this is the miracle of Mawlana Shaykh. You have to know, this is the miracle of Mawlana Shaykh. And you have to know this is the love of Mawlana Shaykh for his murids that he accept they can contribute of their blood to Mawlana Shaykh. Alhamdulillah and this is very, very good sign. Very, very good sign of Mawlana Shaykh that he is willing to remain and he is willing to be with us and he is willing to be insha Allah until Mahdi alayhi’s-salam time.

Now we know it is difficult days, now we know it is not easy days and now we know it is days as test for us because we have to learn how to behave in such difficult days. This is, I am only say this one first, for myself and then I'm sharing it with my brothers and sisters to let you know how to behave when you have difficult time coming in our lives.

You have to understand, this is important point. It is important point, I am repeating also this is important time, an important minute, an important zone of difficulty. And now we have to learn how to behave, and how to keep our love to Mawlana Shaykh, how to keep our love to Tariqa, keep our love to Rasulullah, how to give our love and sincerity for Mawlana Shaykh. I am also pressing in this point very hardly.

I am asking the sincerity you have, to keep your sincerity for Mawlana Shaykh, it is very important. You have to know who is your Shaykh and please, please, please, don't waste your sincerity to Mawlana Shaykh. Mawlana Shaykh is still alive, he is still with us, and he is still breathing and he is still willing to come back, he is willing to come back and he is fighting for it.

Today the doctor he say, "I see so many miracle from Mawlana Shaykh". He is the doctor, he is a very nice guy, masha Allah, and the doctor himself he say, "Our Shaykh, he is improving". The doctor he say 'our Shaykh', he doesn't say 'your Shaykh'. That means he also must have some kind of goodness of Shaykh for him and that he make his tongue to say 'our Shaykh'. And you have to understand the main situation by saying somebody doesn't know Mawlana Shaykh, only few days and he start to say 'our Shaykh'.

This is giving us big lessons to keep our sincerity to Mawlana Shaykh. Please don't waste your sincerity to Mawlana Shaykh. When the King is alive, everybody follow the Sultan, follow the King. I wanted to say this one because we are in need for some guidance and Mawlana Shaykh, he appoint me as a part-time shaykh to guide you by how to behave for Mawlana Shaykh.

I am trying my best to keep my sincerity to Mawlana Shaykh and I'm asking you also to keep your sincerity to Mawlana Shaykh, it is important point. The fitna it is going all around, the rumour is going all around. I don't know what is going all around. This is very, very big test for all of us and please you are my lovers, you are my sisters, you are my brothers, you are one part of Mawlana Shaykh family, and I wanted to warn first myself and then I warn you to keep your sincerity clean, and to keep your love clean, and to keep your heart clean to Mawlana Shaykh. Don't let the shaytan go on in you, don't your ego to go on on you, don't make the fitna go on on you. Say ash'hadu an la ilaha illa Allah wa ash'hadu anna Muhammadan Abduhu wa Rasuluh and, I am accepting Mawlana Shaykh, here and hereafter to be my Shaykh, and I am keeping my sincerity for my Shaykh.
