Shaykh Mehmet: As-Salamu Alaikum wa Rahmatullahi wa Barakatuhu. Madad ya Rasul Allah, Madad ya Ashab al Rasulullah, madad ya S Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, madad ya S.Muhammad Nazim al Haqqani, dastur! Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fil jamia' We may say a few words insha Alah, making niyah for sohbah. The greatest trouble of today's people is the concern for their livelihood. Hadith Sharif says, "Whoever falls into such a hardship (in livelihood), if he forgets Allah & go to people to overcome his hardship he never succeeds, never recovers from that hardship". When people do something..The people of this time have no patience, no faith, no nothing anyways... When there is a hardship, he immediately goes to someone either to take loan, or to take loan & do business with it to resolve the hardship. He thinks that immediately it will.. Everything needs patience, nothing happens in one day. If he goes to people 1-2 times & they help him, he also gets used to it and doesn't do anything because of that help. So his hardship persists. They take money from banks to do business and it makes people even more, totally miserable. What is the cure for this? In the second part of the Hadith, it says, "Whoever turns to & asks from Allah, Allah for sure resolves his hardship".

Resolves financially/physically, or sometimes one has such a hardship that he goes to akhirah, goes with iman & is saved in both sides. It says, don't rely on people. People say now "how is this? Either his hardship is resolved, he gets rich & finds comfort. Or he goes to akhirah?" If you go to akhirah with iman, it is also comfort. Meaning, death is not something to be feared. Death becomes khayr-good for some. Who puts his trust/tawakkal in Allah & dies, he has gained. Or if you have kismat/destined to (live longer), who has put his trust in Allah with real iman, for sure Allah.... The relief & happiness he has in him is the important thing.. The people who live 100 years restless, not happily or who live 100 years without iman, it has no benefit for them. He has lived in vain & becomes a burden in akhirah.He has more to suffer.

Therefore one must remember Allah all the time, in every minute knowing that all comes from Allah & be patient with it. If he shows patience, if he puts his trust in Allah when he starts something it's end will be khayr/good. But if he is not needy of anyone, it is the best. Today we speak about most people they complain from this, from how to, they have narrow life, stress or they cannot, they don't have money or they have no work, nothing. And they become very stressful. Hadith Sharif, Prophet (sas) saying this people going to ask from people, not ask from Allah they will be not successful at all. Whole life going like this. You cannot find something to make you happy. All time you will ask from people. And sadaqa Rasulullah saying, and most true thing Prophet (sas). Because when you ask for help, people maybe help you 1 time, 2 times. But when you take this help, because you are not tired to own it, you will be lazy. And 1, 2, 3 times.. People, they will not give you. Because people also, they don't have patience. You do some work, even if you are doctor or you have shop, or something like this people they thought from first day, people they will come, they buy, they come to see doctor.. No, it needs many, not months, years. Maybe 10 years, 15 years until people, they look and see; this is good doctor, this is nice shop, this is nice carpenter or.. They slowly slowly coming. But people, when they get help then quickly, if people not coming, this is not good job, they will change to other job. Until end like this. For this who want help from people, he will be not success at all. But other part, if you remember Allah & ask from Allah Allah will make you rich or if you are.. There is two way. To be rich, if you are very difficulty in your life. They will be two kinds. Many time make you rich or enough money. Or if not like this, take you to akhirah. This people, they say "what is this? If you go to Allah, they make you rich or to go to akhirah, how is this?" This is good because you are believe in Allah & when you are believe in Allah, Allah happy with you.

Even they give you here, or take you to akhirah with iman it is the best thing people can get. For this, don't forget Allah & don't be burden on people. Only you ask from Allah. Allah most Generous and perfect, He doing everything perfect. Especially for mu'min people, believer people. If you are still in life, ok. Not still, some people to go with iman to akhirah, better billion times from, to be whole this world for them without iman. Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, Al Fatiha.
