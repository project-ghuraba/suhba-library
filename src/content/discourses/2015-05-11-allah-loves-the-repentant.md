As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ تُوبُوٓا۟ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ تَوْبَةًۭ نَّصُوحًا
<br>“Yā ayyuhā l-ladhīna āmanū tūbū ilā Llāhi tawbatan naṣūḥan”<br>
“O you who believe! Turn to Allah with sincere repentance!” (Qurʼān 66:8)

Allah Azza wa Jalla is ordering to repent. 

إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُحِبُّ ٱلتَّوَّٰبِينَ وَيُحِبُّ ٱلْمُتَطَهِّرِينَ
<br>“Inna Llāha yuḥibbu t-tawwābīna wa yuḥibbu l-mutaṭahhirīn”<br>
“Truly, Allah loves those who turn unto Him in repentance and loves those who purify themselves” (Qurʼān 2:222). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

Allah loves people who repent and those who are clean. The door of repentance is open till the sun rises from the place of sunset (West) and sets in the place of sunrise (East). Allah has left this door of mercy open for people. Whatever sin you do, you must repent.

There are kinds of repentance. Many people say they have repented and return to their previous states. And some people think about other things while repenting. These are repenting too but the best is tawbah naṣūḥ, to repent with sincerity, not to return to the sin ever again and never do it. That is the best. This is the highest level of it, the best of repentance. Allah loves those who repent. Whatever one does, if he knows he sinned and repents of it, he will be the recipient of Allah's love and his sins will be forgiven. But if he sins again or does wrong, he then breaks his own repentance.

Allah still forgives however much one may break it. If everyday one breaks it and asks again for forgiveness, Allah forgives, Allah accepts his repentance. As we said, Allah gives according to everyone's rank. If an ʿālim sins and breaks his repentance, that man falls down to the level of ordinary people. His repentance is accepted as of a lower level.

However, repentance in general is what Allah loves. Allah loves who repents. May Allah make all of us among the repentant ones inshāʾAllah so that we don't return to our sins. Of course, there are no people without sins. But there are big sins - we really mustn't do them. Because some of them have punishments, they must have. These are not given when one repents. And repentance should be made so as not to repeat these sins again. 

[In an] Āyat karīmah, Allah saying: "Repent, Repent."

Āyat saying,<br>
يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ تُوبُوٓا۟ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ تَوْبَةًۭ نَّصُوحًا
<br>“Yā ayyuhā l-ladhīna āmanū tūbū ilā Llāhi tawbatan naṣūḥan”<br>
“O you who believe! Turn to Allah with sincere repentance!” (Qurʼān 66:8)

"O Believers! Be Repentant for what you do from wrong things to Allah." 

Another āyat,<br>
إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُحِبُّ ٱلتَّوَّٰبِينَ وَيُحِبُّ ٱلْمُتَطَهِّرِينَ
<br>“Inna Llāha yuḥibbu t-tawwābīna wa yuḥibbu l-mutaṭahhirīn”<br>
“Truly, Allah loves those who turn unto Him in repentance and loves those who purify themselves” (Qurʼān 2:222). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

Allah loves those people who repent, and the clean people; to be in cleanliness. So, what we are doing, what we do wrong Allah opens [the] door of repentance until [the] sun rises. From sunset to sunrise. From East to West against what there now, so it is. It is open and it is mercy from Allah. What you do from wrong, everyday you must pray, say 'Tawbah Astaghfirullāh' 'We are repenting what we done this day' And Allah, He is happy with you because He loves people.

But there is big sins; so you must really be sincere to not do it. When if you by wrong, you do something [of the] big sins, so you must repent to not do it any more. Because small wrong things what we [do] from borning [birth] until death - people, we are not innocent like Prophet. We [are] doing wrong things. Small [and] big. But the small ones every people they are [doing], until end of the life we are doing this. But this is what Allah said; He is loving who, people [who] repenting. Maybe he make it to [have] Mercy for us also. To make more rewards, to get more rewards. So we must every day repeat: 'We are repenting, we are repenting for what we [have] done, with knowing or without knowing.'

But, with big sins like killing people, like stealing [from] people, like doing drinking, other things, there is many big things wrong things, sins you must not do it. Because normally in Islam there is punishment for this, what you do. But now, nowadays, Allah make it like this; if you, by yourself you are repenting and to not do [it] any more [then] it is between you and Allah. And these people what you do wrong for them, if they forgive you. But for Allah other things, Allah forgives. So, don't be, don't be hopeless, don't be hopeless. Allah He is everything for Him [and] by His hand and He is showing us the good things.

We are in this world, like in [an] exam; [a] test for us. Everything [is] a test for people. But if you are in life, in school, you cannot take copy. How I cannot be, I can be success with this test. In life, in school you cannot do this. But, in Allah ʿAzza wa-jalla in this test of life he is giving us [a] copy saying how we can be happy and we will be success for ākhirah. So, it is for this; the biggest one [is] this door - Repentance door. So, Inshā'Allah Allah keep us in the right way and forgive us.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
