As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

All praise and gratitude belong to Allah. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla is the only one to whom all praise is due. That’s why, at the beginning of the Qur’ān, it says, “Al-ḥamdu lillāhi Rabbil ʿĀlamīn” (Praise be to Allah, Lord of the Worlds). All praise is due to Allah. Praise is due to Allah, but thanks can be to everyone. You can thank everyone. But praise is only due to Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. It says, “Al-ḥamdu lillāhi Rabbil ʿĀlamīn.” Therefore, praise is due to Allah, to Allah the Creator. Praise is due to Allah, the Lord of everything.

We must give thanks. We must thank everyone. But there are some things that are exclusively due to Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla: praise is due to Him.

Therefore, among the names of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, are names like Muḥammad, Aḥmad, Ḥāmid, and Maḥmūd which are used to praise him as his rank is high. The name of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam is also put alongside His, praising him and glorifying him : La ilāha illa Allah Muḥammadun Rasulullah.

Therefore, thanks be to Allah for bestowing these blessings upon us. And you should praise Him in everything; for goodness and badness. When one gives thanks, one shows gratitude to Allah. One shows obedience to Allah. Through this, blessings multiply, and one is rewarded even more abundantly.

That is why one must praise Him for everything.

ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ ٱلَّذِي لَا يُحْمَدُ عَلَىٰ مَكْرُوهٍ سِوَاهُ
<br>“Al-ḥamdu lillāhi alladhī lā yuḥmadu ʿalā makrūhin siwāh”<br>
“Praise be to Allah, who alone is praised even in the face of adversity.”

It is said: You should praise Allah for whatever He has given you, for goodness and even for what you perceive as bad.

Whatever befalls you, whether good, or bad according to your perception, to praise Allah will benefit you. If it is good, it is good. If you endure even what you perceive as bad and praise Allah, Allah will raise your rank and give you your reward. That hardship will disappear, inshā’Allāh.

May Allah make us among those who praise and give thanks. May He not make us among those who complain and do not accept Him. This must absolutely be considered. It must come to mankind’s mind. People should praise Allah whatever happens.

May Allah make us among those who praise Him, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq

Al-Fātiḥah.

There are complete recitations of the Qur’ān, tasbihs, tahlilāt, salawāt, and all kinds of good deeds, our brothers have sent them from all over the world.

Firstly, for the sake of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, for the souls of his Ahlu l-Bayt and ṣaḥābah. For the souls of all prophets, Awliyā’, asfiya, mashāyikh.

For the souls of our ancestors. For the souls of believing men and women, Muslim men and women. For goodness to come and evil to be gone. For the strength of our īmān. For happiness here and hereafter.

Li-Llahi Ta’ala, Al-Fātiḥah.
