As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh. A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Wa-quli l-ḥaqqu min rabbikum fa-man shā’a fa-l-yu’min wa-man shā’a fa-l-yakfur innā a‘tadnā li-ẓ-ẓālimīna nāran aḥāṭa bihim surādiquhā” (18:29). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘Aẓīm. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says in Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n. This āyah is in the very middle of the Qur’ān. It says: Speak the truth – the ḥaqq. Say the true word that came to you from Allāh. Ḥaqq is ḥaqq everywhere. Wherever it may be, there is nothing against it. No one can say anything to it. Whoever wishes may accept it. Let him believe. He may accept the truth and believe. Whoever does not wish may not believe, but remain in kufr. That is, by kufr it means: let him remain in kufr, remain in denial. But He says: Speak the truth. Your duty is to say that. Whoever wishes will accept the truth and be on the way of Allāh. Whoever does not wish will remain in kufr. And in the end, he will enter a dungeon of Hell. If they ask for water, they will be given boiling, foul water. In this world, everyone talks about democracy. This is the real democracy. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla has left people free. But He speaks the truth and shows it. Believe in it and accept it, so that you may be at ease and so that others may be at ease. If you do not want it, then you will have a punishment to bear. Here it says: for oppressors and for disbelievers. The disbeliever is an oppressor, and whoever commits kufr is an oppressor. Those who do not accept the word of truth are oppressors. Oppressors to whom? First of all, they are oppressors to themselves, oppressors to those around them, oppressors to Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Because the greatest oppression is to commit kufr against Allāh. That is the greatest oppression. Therefore, it certainly carries a burden and a punishment. For this reason, in this world, speaking the truth is often hard. Sometimes it can even be dangerous. At other times, the least of it is that people who do not accept the truth take offence at you, become upset with you, act superior towards you, and oppose you. That is the least of it. Most people today accept only what they themselves know as true. They do not accept the truth. Whatever the nafs (ego) commands, they take that as truth and follow it. But Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla commands you to speak the truth. Do not be ashamed of anyone, do not fear, do not hold back. Truth is truth. I have said it. Did it touch you? Did it offend you? I said it openly. This is not something to be told to each person individually. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla has said it for everyone. Whoever wants the truth turns back from the bad path and accepts the truth. Allāh will transform his sins and the wrongs he has done – according to how much he did – into reward and good deeds. He will be saved. May Allāh grant people reason and understanding so that they may accept the truth. Otherwise, they will have entered a path of no benefit. They will have entered a dangerous path. May Allāh protect people.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, saying in this Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n: Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Wa-quli l-ḥaqqu min rabbikum fa-man shā’a fa-l-yu’min wa-man shā’a fa-l-yakfur innā a‘tadnā li-ẓ-ẓālimīna nāran aḥāṭa bihim surādiquhā, wa-in yastaghīthū yughāthū bi-mā’in ka-l-muhli yashwī l-wujūh, bi’sa sh-sharābu wa-sā’at murtafaqā” (18:29). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘Aẓīm. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying in these verses of Qur’ān which it is the middle of Qur’ān. This āyah, Sūratu l-Kahf, middle, I think, even just in middle. What is this? This summary, saying heart of Qur’ān, “You must say the ḥaqq.” Ḥaqq mean right. What it is not lie. Advice, giving people, what Allāh order, this is ḥaqq. Ḥaqq, meaning every good thing for human being – justice, happiness, every good thing, which must everybody accept this. “You must say this,” saying, for Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, and for other also who reading this. And after this – they say democracy. Here democracy: He said, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, “Who like to believe, believe it is up to him – fa-man shā’a fa-l-yu’min wa-man shā’a fa-l-yakfur” (18:29). Other, what if they not believe, to be unbeliever, also can be for them. But what is different between this? They continue of verses: If you not believe and not accept this, Allāh prepare for oppressor. Oppressor, oppressor way, with full of all whole side with fire. And if they want water, they give them the worst dirty boiling water. And it is, the continue of verses saying: it is the worst place and the worst friend there also. This is what, if they are not accept. Because, this not saying only non-believer, for oppressor. Oppressor meaning, even maybe some Muslim they are following their ego and not listening for right. Also for them, also this. If they not repent and come back for Allāh, they will be in this bad end. And this is very important verses. Every time it is like this, but this time it is more difficult, to even normal one. They are not accepting the truth, the right thing, what you say of right. They are not accepting. “You are wrong. This is not true, this. We are not accept this,” they saying. And sometimes, it is dangerous to say truth in some place. Even the easiest one, if you say truth between people, some people getting angry for you, from you. And they saying, “Not happy, you are saying this for us. We are not accept this. We are truth, you are not saying truth. This is not acceptable.” But it is, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying, you must, if you ask and somebody want truth, you must say without any shy, without any difficult, to not afraid from people. You must say this, “This is what is must be. What this is not, cannot be.” So, this is order of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. And, but this time people, they are not listening for order of Allāh. Only what they truth: their ego. If their ego happy with something, “This is the truth. This is right, You must, we must follow. Other it is not important.” Prophet Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, or awliyā’, or ṣaḥābah, or other, it is not any important for them. Only their must, master for them their ego, and only they accept this. But if they are accept the right, and come back for the right way, accept truth, Allāh will forgive them, and change what bad deed they do for reward for them. As much it was, even they like mountain, all this it will be reward for them, if they accept truth and come back to way of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Allāh keep us in His way, in shā’a Llāh, and give hidāyah for other also. Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
