As-Salāmu ‘Alaykum wa Raḥmatullāhi wa Barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu Billāhi Minash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm.

Wa ṣ-Salātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Akhirīn.

Madad yā RasūlAllāh, Madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, Madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

Dastūr Mawlana Shaykh Abdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistāni, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-

Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-Suḥbah wa l-Khayru fi l-Jam‘iyyah.

Sayyidina Imam Al-Busayri is one of the great Awliya and he wrote Qaṣīdat al-Burda. There is a verse in it which Grandshaykh Mawlana Shaykh AbduLlah ad-Daghestani used to repeat.

“Good tidings for you in Islam, for you always have an unbreakable support and grace from Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla. He will save you here and hereafter”. That is the good tidings. The news is that you will be saved. There is always an unbreakable support behind you. The grace, kindness and favors of Allah are together with Muslims.

This is a great favor that Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla gives to someone who believes in Him. It is a great good tidings for people who follow the way. It doesn’t break down. Everything destructs, but it doesn’t. All of the Kufr and those who believe that they control the world, none of them are useful. They are not even as a wing of a mosquito next to the Power of Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla. Therefore, this verse from the Qasidah of Awliyau’Llah is very beautiful.

Allah ‘Azza wa-Jalla has given the best things to Muslims in this world as well. But unfortunately, Muslims don’t appreciate it. They think that the things others possess are better than theirs. Nothing and no one is better than what we have. And even in this world, in countries and lands where Muslims live, Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla gave Barakah there. He gave all kinds of beauty. As they don’t appreciate it, they are in such a condition.

Therefore, we have to make Shukr to Allah. And we have to appreciate the favors He gave us. We shouldn’t waste them. May Allah help Muslims insha’Allah. May they understand how great their favors are insha’Allah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
