As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is saying, "Inna Llāha yumsiku s-samāwāti wa-l-arḍa an tazūlā" (35:41). Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is capable of everything. The skies, the earth, the universe, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is holding them. No one other than HeNone can hold them. If someone tries, and if Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla lets go a bit, nothing will remain of them. Everything will be gone. AllāhNone is the possessor of power. AllāhNone is the possessor of sovereignty. AllāhNone does whatever HeNone wants. Therefore, believing people, Muslim people should believe in HimNone.

It is a big thing. It is the word of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Everything is in HisNone Hands. It is not in the hands of people, not in the hands of this or that country. Everyone comes out and claims to own the world. As long as AllāhNone doesn't want it, nothing can happen. There is the will of AllāhNone. Everything has its time and hour. When the time comes, whatever has to happen will happen. And afterwards, its consequences will appear. Therefore, no need to be scared too much and to feel sad about the matters of this world. There are things we are waiting for. They will occur. And the things that lead to them are arranged. Let's see how it goes.

The Ṣāḥib will come. The Ṣāḥib, who will put this world and everything in order, is Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām. When he comes, for the first time since the world was created, there will be peace, safety, and harmony. This will happen under the command of Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām. There will be barakah and peace then. There will be no kufr, no oppression, nothing. And more importantly, none of these disputes of people over land and borders will remain. There will be no customs, no visa regulations, nothing. Everything will be clean. But before that, there will be hardship.

Allāhu ‘ālim when it happens, when its time is. It is in the hands of Allāh ’Azza wa-Jalla. Therefore, when something happens, no need to be afraid and worried. You are a believer. Our Holy ProphetNone foretold these things. It is called nubuwwah. Nubuwwah means to foretell the future events. And our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam foretold all of them and through ṣaḥābah and awliyā’ they were passed on to us. This is the end of times. The time is near. AllāhNone knows the exact hour. Therefore, no need to get upset about the events occurring here and there. May AllāhNone let us meet the Ṣāḥibu z-Zamān, Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām, in shā’a Llāh. May HeNone also let us reach these beautiful times, in shā’a Llāh.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla say in Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n, "Inna Llāha yumsiku s-samāwāti wa-l-arḍa an tazūlā" (35:41). This is āyat from Sūrat Fāṭir meaning whole this world, heaven, universe, by, hold by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Nobody can hold it. If anybody try to hold it, cannot be. And one second, nothing left if Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla not holding this. Everything by HisNone Hand. AllāhNone Hand holding whole universe. Heaven, earth, planet, everything hold by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. This what AllāhNone tell us in HisNone Holy Book, Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n. And mu’min, believer, must be happy with this because this is clear thing. And Hand of AllāhNone nothing, nobody hold it.

Now, many country they claim, "We are control whole world. We are control this, we control that." And even there is people with this playing electronic, they thought they are, they claim they are like Fir‘awn or Nimrud, they're gods. This is what they claim and they trying to do same thing. But, nothing, nobody, they are very ignorant, very small. They thought they are giant, they are controlling. If they control this world, what is this world? It is not even one sand. Sand it's too big for this our planet compared to universe. And they are claiming this and they are trying to do something they thought they will do. Don't worry. This is whole in HisNone, HisNone Power, HisNone Hand. AllāhNone power controlling everything. Nothing can be. So, for this, we are waiting.

Now people worry about what will happen in this world. This country attacking other, other attacking, there's war, there is that... Don't worry! This is, you must be happy because we are living in end of time. And Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, prophecy, meaning... You know in Arabic maybe 'nabīy', they can, many people Arab not they knowing what meaning 'nabīy'. They thought maybe he is a prophet but what prophet [means?] – prophet – prophecy meaning, who tell what will happen for next generation, hundred years, centuries after. This Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam was telling us what will happen. It will happen.

There will be peace when Sayyidunā l-Mahdī come and clean all this world from kufr, non-belief, to make all believers. When this happen, the real peace, it will be that time. In that time, it will be barakah. No difficult. No hungry. No hunger in world. Nothing. No oppression. No attacking or that. And the most important thing here, it will be no border. Now, every country, they claim this country take our land and they put border. "This border we must take." Other country, they said same. Other also. Some country, people running from their country. They are not be patient, no ṣabr. They leave everything with his family or other. They try to go to Europe. They thought Europe it is paradise and they most of them they die before they arrive there. But, when Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām coming, [there] is no border. Everybody can go from East to West, from North to South. No border, no visa, no custom, no police, no... Nothing at all. No need for this because, whole world it will be under commander of Sayyidunā l-Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām in peace and happiness. And no kufr, no non-believer – all be Islām, in shā’a Llāh. Islām meaning peace. All it will be in peace.

So for this, what happen these days, don't be unhappy. Don't be anxious, don't be worry about what will happen. Just wait, in shā’a Llāh, AllāhNone make us to reach to Sayyidunā l-Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām. This is nice, good, beautiful days, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
