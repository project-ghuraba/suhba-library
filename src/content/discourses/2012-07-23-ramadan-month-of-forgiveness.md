As-Salamu Alaikum Madad ya Rasul Allah, Madad ya S Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, madad ya S.Muhammad Nazim al Haqqani, dastur! Audhu Billahi min al shaytan al rajim, Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fil jamia'

Insha Allah we may make a sohbat today with the barakah of Ramadan Sharif, and another name for it is the Month of Tawba. Tawba means to ask forgiveness, repent of your sin. When one sins, angels wait for him to make tawba. "Don't record immediately" orders the angel on the right side to the one on the left. "Don't you ever record immediately. If he makes tawba, we will delete it". They wait for about 8 hours, then finally they record only if he doesn't make tawba. Then it is recorded as a sin.

But Allah has kept the door of Tawba open until the sun rises from west, meaning until it rises from the opposite direction, the door of Tawba is open. And Allah loves who makes tawba. It says in Hadith Sharif; "'Inna Al-Laha Yuhibbu At-Tawwabina Wa Yuhibbu Al-Mutatahhirina" (2:222) Actually this is the Holy Verse. Allah loves who makes tawba and loves who is pure, Allah (awj). And in Hadith Sharif "Attaaib Habibullah minath zambi kaman la zamba lahu" Meaning if he doesn't make tawba in that 8 hours & remembers to do so later, whether it is 1 month, 1 year, no matter when it is. If he intends to make tawba, he makes tawba & Allah accepts his tawba.

The best time to remind about this in Ramadan, because it is the month of Tawba. In another Hadith of the Prophet (sas), he explains with an example how much Allah (awj) feels happy when a person makes tawba after he commits a sin. In old times, of course there... When they were in the desert, the situation was much different. For example it says, one man put his water, his food, his load, everything on his camel. And he set off to a journey with his camel. And when he got tired, immediately he fell asleep in a shady spot. When he woke up he could find neither his camel..Of course when he can't find his camel everything is on it. He was left with nothing in the middle of the desert- no water, no bread, no nothing. He ran here, there but couldn't find the camel. Finally he lost his hope & said "I may lay down & wait for death". He was waiting like this. When he woke up, he saw that his camel was over his head. How much this man was happy.

Allah (awj) gets even happier than him, that "My servant made tawba to Me". Even it says in the end of that Hadith of Prophet (sas) that the man got so happy that, filled with amazement instead of saying "You are my Lord", he said "I am Your Lord, You are my servant". He did not know what he was saying out of his happiness. This is, Allah (awj) is that Merciful & wants that much for His servant to make tawba. Therefore, this month of Ramadan is also the month of tawba so may we make tawba for all our sins insha Allah.

Today we speak about, to tawba, meaning forgiveness, to want forgiveness from Allah. Tawbah, it is in Arabic. When some servant, some people making wrong thing there is two angels: one right, one left. Right, they write good thing. Left, writing bad thing. When he do something bad, he must- left one take permission to write from right one. Right angel, telling left angel "Wait,maybe after one hour later he will want forgiveness". 2 hours, 3 hours, until 8 hours. After, if he is not making forgiveness, writing bad on left side for him. This is for, when everyday. But even tawba/forgiveness door, it is open until the big sign of Day of Qiyamah approach. This is one of them, to sun coming from sunset (West) wrong, to other side. Until that, the door of forgiveness, it is open.

Maybe same day you cannot want forgiveness. But even later, when you want forgiveness Allah give this forgiveness for you. Allah like to forgive His servant. And there is Ayah also, Allah like who want forgiveness & Allah loves who they are clean people. The Prophet (sas) in Hadith; "attaib Habibullah", who want for you this, he is lover of Allah. And who want forgive from his sin, he is as he has no sin. And Allah (awj), He is happy with, very happy.

Prophet (sas) giving example from Hadith. Saying as one man in desert, he traveling from place to place. And he has camel. And everything on his camel; food, drink, everything what he want to travel, on camel. And he was tired. And he sleep. When wake up, find no camel; no nothing at all. Because he is not, cannot carry by himself. And he was searching, looking, running this side, that side everywhere. And then getting tired & he say "no hope for me, I will die here. So I am very tired, I will sleep". When he sleep, wake up he find his camel over his head, waiting for him. How he is happy, how much he is, very, very, very happy he must be, like this. Even he mix between to say thank for Allah, I am Your servant, He said "Thank for You, I am Allah You are My servant".

Even Hadith like this, he is from happy. Allah more happy from people to be want forgiveness from Him. And He quickly giving forgiveness. And Ramadan, it is month of forgiveness also. Insha'Allah forgive us also, what we do with to know (knowingly) what we do or without knowing, we make many mistakes. Allah, He is forgiveness. And we want forgiveness from Him.

Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, Al Fatiha.
