Allāhumma ṣalli wa sallim ‘alá nabiyyinā Muḥammad ‘alayhi s-salām ṣalātan tadūmu wa tuhdá ’ilayh, māmarra llayāli wa ṭūla d-dawām.

Ṭūla d-dawām. In shā’a Llāh we will be the lover of Rasūla Llāh (saw) until the end. Yesterday we have one of Mawlānā Shaykh murīd, she was a lady that she was cooking for Mawlānā Shaykh with love. And she was one of that ladies, what time Mawlānā Shaykh knocked their door, it was ready to host Mawlānā Shaykh, subḥāna Llāh. And yesterday, she passed away. Innā liLlāhi wa innā ilayhi rāji‘ūn (2:156). It was my duty, subḥāna Llāh, to be there representing Mawlānā Shaykh and to pray Janāzah prayer. And I have a few things I like to share also with you about simple burial. It is been simple burial subḥāna Llāh. When we come to her grave, I have a rose in my head and I have some Mawlānā Shaykh give to my heart and say "this is from my garden, put it in her grave." And I take the rose and I give to the grandson and say this is from Mawlānā Shaykh garden. And, by the way, that rose was one week in my turban. Normally it will come very dry. But subḥāna Llāh it was fresh. I was saying, 'Surprise, how can be fresh?' For one week no water, no nothing, just.. must be a reason. Must be reason. And it been given as a fresh rose. When we look at it as normal looking we say "cannot be". But Mawlana Shaykh wanted it to be. When it is meant to be it must be there.

And then al-ḥamdu liLlāh then we make the burial. And normally when you see the burying you will have some fear about what's going on. And some kind of satisfaction coming to the heart, it says "No, its relaxing area, don't worry." That's why I wanted to share this one with you because it was very important things. I all the time we went to get some burials and all the time have some fear. But with this one I see it is coming very calm and coming very nicely. And then Mawlānā Shaykh he sent to my heart again the reason why. Very important to understand such a things. "My son, if you are loyal and you are in the service of Allāh and Rasūla Llāh and your Shaykh and your murshid you'll have such an end." Nicely, peacefully and restfully. No panic, no nothing. In two minutes it was buried and finished. So simple and so nice and so easy subḥāna Llāh. I say this is the reason - this she lady was all the time ready. If I come to their house and knocked the door, they will not say "O Shaykh you come again? Oh what I will cook, what I will give, what I will serve?" No. Going to my house, I'm going to my family house. And that's why it was big picture in front of me I wanted to share with you.

Subḥāna Llāh it was fantastic things for us to learn. Fantastic things to know what we are doing. Fantastic things that we are in the right track. Very important because normally, after a while you will be saying "why I'm here, what I'm doing? Why?" This is ego. That is saying to me so much but saying "Shut up, shut up". But it is all the time puting the screw with the fire and with the salt to make it painful. This is ego job. Eee..eee.eee... You have to be used to it. When Mawlānā Shaykh when we go extreme in our thought or in our thinking or in our behaviour, because first it's coming thinking, then it's coming in your tongue, and then you come in physical acting. Before going to physical acting you have to finish it here. Because when you also in your tongue, you will be responsible also. When you are in a physical action that you are out. And this is that we don't have to be there. Very important.

That's I why say tarbiyatu n-nufūs. What's meaning tarbiyatu n-nufūs? Train your ego. They say everything has his training. "What you are training?" "I train to make tasbīḥ" for example. Oh, Oh. Very important to train to make tasbīḥ. You think it's simple by training tasbīḥ? It is just making - but to make your ego to accept this tasbīḥ it take ages. Say what is this, why this, why need this? You start to train Train slowly, slowly, slowly, slowly, slowly, slowly and then you make it as big muscles. Everything it's need for train, by the way. If we are not able to train our ego and make tarbiyatu n-nufūs - training the ego is the most important things in ṭarīqah, in tasawwuf. That's Mawlānā Shaykh, he was all the time saying, "I'm here to train your ego, I'm here to help you to train you ego. I'm here to help you to get rid of your ego." Never happened to get rid of ego but at least, at least we put in the frame.

The human they don't like to tell them no. Which the best things you like to do, is to cross in the red light. We love it. Red light say "Oh we hate red light" especially in big cities. Red light it is the most hate point. Why? Because it is stopping you. Forbidden to pass. The ego it hates red lights. If you are willing to train we have to have red light in our life. Mawlānā Shaykh he used to give us very mild training, very mild. The training was Mawlānā Shaykh give us it is like kindergarten children. Don't this, do this. Don't this, do this. Don't this, do this. Why? Because we are really weak. We are really not qualified people. We are really in need to guidance. We are really in the position that must somebody take our hand and take us right or left or to take us out or in. And Mawlānā Shaykh he was so huge heart, that he looked for us as a father. That's why I don't say Mawlānā Shaykh is my father. No I say Mawlānā Shaykh is our father. Mawlānā Shaykh he was true father. He was the real father that caring. There is too many fathers they don't care. But there is a father that he care. There was a story about father, we say this one also. One of the ṣaḥābah he had a proposal for marriage but he didn't see his wife. And in the day of marriage when he enter for the wedding room he see his wife and she was black lady. Maybe she was not pretty and say "I'm in shock, I didn't expect such a lady" and he say I don't want to marry you - after nikāḥ. And he say I don't want to touch you. And I'm going so you be free I'll be free. Then she said 'It's not fair to make like this.' And she was convincing him that to continue the marriage. And he say ok, we will be married. And then he continued his marriage and he say, but I cannot stand you. It is, you are not my type, you are not my what I was expecting. And then she say maybe the badness that you see in me will be so much goodness. And the man he say, no I cannot stand and he left. And he was away from Madina for twenty years. Left.

Then after twenty years he come to Madina and he went to Masjid an-Nabawī and he was one imām making ṣuḥbah after the prayer. And it was so nice ṣuḥbah. So sweet. And he say, "What a lovely man, what a lovely imām you are. Who is your father?" He say, "My father, he left my mother twenty years ago and I don't know who, I didn't see him." And he say, "Will you take me to your house?" And he take him for that man house and he know that boy is his son. And he say, "will you go and tell your mother the badness can bring very goodness, too much goodness." And he entered and he say, "Mum, there is one man he is sending you this message. What you think?" Say "This is your father bring him in." And that person was Anas ibnu Mālik that he carries so many ḥadīth of Rasūla Llāh (saw). And that lady she was ṣaḥābah also and she was saying "Look it is not the physical seeing that you see on me that can bring bring the best goodness."

That's why the father is very important in our life. And Mawlānā Shaykh he was the true father for all of us that he is all the time trying to bring the goodness to you for Allāh Almighty for Rasūla Llāh (saw) and that's why we have to be very proud about what Mawlānā Shaykh he make as a service for all of us. And we have to be also thankful to Mawlānā Shaykh that he accepting us as his children. And he doesn't make discrimination you are like this, you are like that, or you are this, or you.. He doesn't. He say my door is open for everybody. Why? Because he know the jewelry in your heart that's can be very very beautiful and very very nice. This Mawlānā Shaykh, he is so beautiful, so kind, that he discover all the beauty inside us and make us to be with him. And he make us to be with Rasūla Llāh (saw) and he make us to be with Allāh Almighty. May Allāh forgive us and make us that people deserve such a things. And to behave as what we are deserving.
