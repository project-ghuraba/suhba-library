As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam says, “A believer neither harms nor reciprocates harm.” He does not cause harm to anyone. The nature and characteristic of a believer is to do good. Doing evil is not among the characteristics of a believer.

A Muslim is always obligated to do good. He stays away from doing evil and does not harm anyone. Whether it be a person, an animal, or a tree: he does not harm anything. He desires everything to be as Allah wills. This is the characteristic of a believer.

A non-believer, on the other hand, tends and intends to do evil out of envy, jealousy, or hostility. Therefore, there are limits set by Allah. Whoever transgresses those limits incurs Allah’s wrath. A person who incurs Allah’s wrath becomes someone whom Allah does not love.

In ṭarīqah, our entire purpose is to be a person whom Allah and our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam love. As we have said, this is achieved by always doing good without harming anyone. This is also an act of worship.

Every state and action of a believer, as long as it is done for the sake of Allah, is considered an act of worship and earns him reward. Reward is recorded for every step he takes, every breath he takes.

Therefore, may Allah not deprive us of the ability to do good. Some people are deprived of doing good and occupy themselves with doing evil. May Allah protect us from such people.

May we not follow our egos and not exceed our limits, inshā’Allāh. May Allah protect Ummati Muḥammad.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
