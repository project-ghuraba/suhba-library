Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi Rasulillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

“If Allah (Azza wa Jalla) wills harm or something that is not good for you, only Allah (Azza wa Jalla) can lift that thing from you.” This is a holy verse (ayat karima). Nobody can lift it. As long as Allah does not wish goodness for you, nobody can lift it even if the whole world got together. If the whole world helped you, it cannot benefit you. He is capable of everything if you ask for goodness. Allah Azza wa Jalla says if the whole world stood in front of you, again they cannot do anything. That would become good for you, and nobody would be able to interfere.

This shows us to Turst Allah and ask Allah for help. Ask Allah for kindness. Do not approach anybody for benefit. It would be better for yourselves and you would benefit. He says not to approach a rich, wealthy, or powerful person for advantages. Never approach people for worldly gains and do not disgrace yourselves. Ask from Allah.

Allah is capable of everything. “Wa huwa „ala kulli shay‟in qadeer.” He is not capable of doing some things or others, Allah Azza wa Jalla is capable of everything. Humankind is not, no human is, capable of doing everything. He is capable of doing very little, and this partially so, let alone everything. The richer the person, the more scared he becomes of giving and finishing. Whereas rich people such as that have so much wealth that it would suffice not seven but 70 of his generation. Regardless, Allah makes their hands shiver when they try to do something.

Allah (JJ) gave Mumins and Muslims honor. Yet again, nobody can do anything. Only Allah Azza wa Jalla can. Ask from Allah and do not get close to anybody for benefits. Ask Allah both physically and spiritually. You can ask Allah Azza wa Jalla for everything. May Allah not make us in need of anybody.

May Allah (JJ) help these Muslims. If the world were to come against them, they cannot do anything to them. So do not be afraid of the world, do not refrain from anybody, and fear Allah. Ask from Allah. Ask for goodness from Allah.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq.

Al-Fatiha.
