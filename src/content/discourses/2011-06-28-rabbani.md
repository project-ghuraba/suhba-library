Pray 2 rakaat and ask Allah from His favours. I am shy to ask anything without having prayed 2 rakaat. Otherwise it would be from the ego. In this case pray two rakaat for Allah's sake, for Allah's might & the Prophet's (saws) honour, then seek support from Allah's faithful servants. The 2 rakaats are so Allah and His Prophet (saws) may give support. Then immediately the role is given to the saints whose spiritual power is sufficient. Their power comes, inshaAllah for these 2 rakaats. Allahu Allah, Allahu Allah Allahu Rabbi, Allahu Hasbi, La Ilaha illa Allah La Ilaha illa Allah Sayidina Muhammad Rasulullah Madad Ya Rijal Allah.

Be a student. Allah, Allah. It is good manners for us to say, Rabbuna our Lord Almighty - then we revert to the presence of the men of Allah - we transfer our request to the men of Allah. We are not qualified to request directly from our Lord Almighty. However, out of good manners we ask (Him) but we must lower ourselves to the lowest level. Then... we call "O good servants of Allah!" reach to us; reach to us for Allah's grace and for the grace of His Beloved, peace & blessings upon him. Hisham Effendi welcome to you. Our people arrived two days ago and delighted us. Because the guest removes the burden from the people he is visiting, he opens their chest, increases their rizq and increases their energy. In fact, who welcomes a guest, verily, he must be granted a favour. Welcome to you, mashaAllah. What were you saying at the beginning of the meeting? What was it?

S.H: Mawlana, now everywhere they are holding conferences, but there is a big conference held in Indonesia for the whole world. The President will give the opening & it is about the role of Sufism in solving the present global problems.

M: Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Rahim. First of all, The expression "sufism" was not existing in the first era, at the time of the Sahaba. It is new/recent.

S.H: The word "sufism" is new?

M: This word, its reality. What we need people to follow - the path of sufism, first of all, it is an expression. Because Allah Almighty has not left anything without showing it to His (swt) servants. Therefore there is no need to invent a new word, no, there is no need. Allah (awj-jwa) SubhanaHu wa taa' la, Sultansin Ya Rabb! He, Rabbuna (jwa), did not leave anything without showing it in the Holy Qur'an. And therefore there is no need to invent a word like "Sufism". I haven't heard, or came across the word sufism, among the words of the Prophet (saws), I don't know. Did anyone invent the word "Sufism"? Allah Almighty (swt) wants His servants to be "Sufis"? Is this written anywhere? "Be Sufis"? Did he (saws) say this?

Why they brought this? Did you hear Sayyidina Muhyiuddin (Ibn Arabi) speak about "Sufism"? Or have our honourable Naqshbandi Mawlanas, spoke about "Sufism"? Or our honourable Mawlana Grand-Shaykh did he speak about "Sufism"? Let go of this. Sayidina Adam (as) did he advise his children to "Be Sufis"? Or did Sayyidina Ibrahim (as) the father of Prophets,or S. Nuh(as) did they say to people "become Sufis"? In the Psalms, and in the Bible & in the book of Dawud (as), the Zabur, is it mentioned there "O people be Sufis"? If sufism would raise the servant to the highest level, the highest level, then that level it would be mentioned, "That man is a sufi". This 'Sufism' in our time now, has became a fashion. Fashion, fashion... Fashion, for what?

Why didn't the Prophet (saws) say "O people become Sufis"? Why did the Prophet (saws) not say this? "O people be Wahabis"? Why did Haqq (swt) not say to people "Be Salafyin"? From where did they bring this? These people don't read the Holy Qur'an? The reality is that Allah (swt) said in the Holy Qur'an to people, this is a holy verse, I want to mention, pay attention. Allah (swt) said

"Wa Lakin Kunu Rabbaniyin" (3:79).

And there is no one who says "Rabbani." This is the Truth's demand –

"but be lordly" (3:79)

Be Lordly, there is not a higher rank for the servant than this! A lordly person is connected to the Lord. He is one who represents & follows the orders of the Lord Almighty. Right from the beginning of the Holy Quran

Bismi Allāhi Ar-Raĥmāni Ar-Raĥīmi (1:1)

"Al-Ĥamdu Lillāh Rabbi Al-`Ālamīna"(1:2)

Right? The Lord of Creation "Praise be to Allah, Lord of Creations. (1:2)

This indicates that the Lord wants a lordly person connected to Him. He is the Lord of creations and we are lordly ones, connected to Him.

One day I was in noble Sham - may Allah save Sham from the tyrants - I was in my house when a person knocked at the door. I opened and I saw a young man. This young man, maybe he was around 30 years old, maybe a little more or less. I welcomed him. He came for a visit, (A visit is) a sunnah of the Prophet, so I sat with him. "O Sayyidi, I have graduated from University, the Islamic University of Sham." SubhanAllah. It came to my heart to ask him, I told him, "you have studied 5 years at university. Tell me one word or a sentence that you have learned. Tell me." SubhanAllah, I don't know from where this man came. Did you understand? He is not like what I learnt. He graduated from an Islamic university. He did not fall in his studies. Then I asked him "tell me what you have learnt from the knowledges, you`ve studied in the past years, tell me something." And with no any pretense he said O shaykh, "Al Haqq (swt) says in a Hadith Qudsi while addressing His servant, He says: "O My servant be Rabbani".

Allah likes His servant to be Rabbani. Connected to the Lord. Is there a higher rank? What is that of a Sufi? You understand? Is this clear? Allah Almighty says "O My servant, be Rabbaniyan!" He did not say be Muslim, believer or any other human characteristic. What honours him, Allah's (swt) hadith Qudsi says kunu Rabbaniyan/be lordly. He did not say be Mosesian, Jesuit, Ibrahimian, Mohammadian (saws). Muhammadi (follower of the Prophet) is Rabbani/lordly. Allah likes His servant to be connected to Him only. "O my servant, I am your Lord and you be Rabbani/lordly. "O My servant, be Rabbaniyan/lordly." This is a Hadith Qudsi.

The Holy Quran states: "But, be lordly" (3:79) The Holy Quran says "Wa Lakin Kūnū Rabbānīyīn" (3:79)

verily it does not say "mutasawwif"/sufis. He (swt) likes for His servant to be connected to Him (swt). A Rabbani person connected to the Lord. "O My servant, be Rabbaniyan". Ya abdi ati'ni aj'aluka rabbaniyan. Yes, this is it. O My, servant be lordly. S.H. "Obey Me I make you lordly, you say to something be and it is." Ya 'abdi kun Rabbaniyan. Takul lil shay'i kun fayakun." Allah Akbar, what a power to be Rabbani. If this lordly person wished to kick like football players kicks a ball. If one of them swore by Allah and he kicked with his foot this world, from here it would leave its orbit and reach the sun. This is the power he possesses.

"Be lordly, you say to something be and it is."

Why don't you write this? What is a sufi? Sufism must guide people to be lordly. Otherwise the word sufi is for one who wears wool, and so he is a sufi. What is that? They wear wool and so they become sufis or become servants to the Lord by wearing wool?

S.H:with their asceticism. Ibrahim Ibn Adham was ascetic.

M: So the instructions for us, that have reached me are to instruct the servants, is to become lordly. None amongst the Muslims, followers of Jesus, Moses, can object to these words. Unite people to this. (become rabbani) Don't say I am this or that. No! Have you exerted all your efforts to become rabbani? This is a very high rank that cannot be estimated. Even Prophets cannot measure it. He says: Be lordly. "Obey Me, O My servant I make you Rabbani connected to Me, you say to the thing be and it is." This is a hadith Qudsi, it is supported by a Quranic verse, for mindless people. The Almighty says,

"We have honoured the children of Adam." (17:70)

For 40 years Mawlana Shaykh spoke about Allah honouring of His servant. The value of the human being cannot be estimated, only his Lord can assess it. He (swt) knows his value. Shame on all the people who don't appreciate the servants of Allah whom He has honoured. On the contrary, the devil tries to demean the servants of Allah. Allah Almighty says:

"Wa Laqad Karramnā Banī 'Ādama" (17:70)

Glory be to Him. This is an important issue. From this point you must speak if you are attending any meeting.

S.H: Masha Allah. This is above all knowledges, sayyidi.

M:"and above every knower there is a more knowlegeable one."(12:76)

S.H. No one has spoken about sufism in this manner. No one took the rabbani/lordly angle when speaking about its meaning.

M: Yes, there is no one. No one could see it. It has not been opened to them.

S.H: Even Imam Malik, Sayidi he spoke about sufism & shari'a. He said if there is no sufism and only Shari'a, then the human being would be a hypocrite. He said Sharia & Sufism combined but he did not mention Rabbani.

M: "Wa Fawqa Kulli Dhī `Ilmin `Alīmun"(12:76) He was the imam of his time. There are also imams for every period of time. Some are granted something that wasn't opened to previous ones. And success comes from Allah.
