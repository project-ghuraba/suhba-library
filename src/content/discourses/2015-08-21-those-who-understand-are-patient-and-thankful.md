Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Allah Azza wa Jalla gave us all of His favors, thank Allah. Those who understand are thankful and those who do not understand revile (deny) it. There is nothing missing in what Allah (JJ) has created. Allah has created most perfectly. Allah is treating people with His mercy, He is not giving them what they deserve, and is leaving them for the hereafter.

The holy verse says: “We can also make the house and roof of unbelievers out of silver or gold so they would go wilder.” The riches given to them, this and that, have no benefit. In the hereafter, they will answer for it and for the orders they did not obey. Do not believe these people, people who rebel against Allah, and do not go after them. Even Shaitan on the Day of Judgment says, “I fear Allah.” They are worse than Shaitan. They say “Neither fearing Allah nor ashamed of the servant”. These people are like this. Do not follow them. If you chase after them you would regret it in the world and be ruined, and you would regret it in the hereafter.

We did not come to the world for the worldly. Let us be thankful for the blessings Allah has given and not rebel against Allah. Allah Azza wa Jalla states: “Obey Allah (JJ), obey the Prophet (SAW), and obey those of authority (i.e. the sultan).” As long as the sultan is on the way of Allah, revolting against him is blasphemy. Muslims should know this too. Let them not be fooled: let them not ruin their world nor waste their hereafter.

May Allah give us all an understanding that is good and that we can understand. May Allah keep Muslims safe from the fitna of the times. Those who trust in Allah have already won. Those who are patient will find their reward. However, the others who when thrown in hell on Judgment Day, Allah Azza wa Jalla says, “Whether you are patient or not,” to those tyrants, to those who revolt against Allah. It does not matter whether you are patient, whether you shriek, yell, or scream, patience has no benefit anymore on Judgment Day. Patience is here. You will be a little patient, you will be thankful to Allah, and you will get your reward. Otherwise, if you follow the kafir, you will become miserable. They have nothing else.

When Shaitan enters hell he will say, “I fear Allah.”

“But you brought us here.”

“You should not have followed me,” he will say.

Allah gave us a mind and He has shown us the good way. Do not fall for the infidels, do not follow them, do not follow Shaitan, and do not follow your ego. Follow Allah’s orders.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq.

Al-Fatiha.
