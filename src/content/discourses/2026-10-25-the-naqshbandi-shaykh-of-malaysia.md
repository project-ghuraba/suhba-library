As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Thank you for you hospitable and for the family, aunty and all of them. Māshā'Allah. And thank you for Malaysian people. Allah bless you, bless them.

Last night we say about this what many people they didn't hear and they didn't know. So we repeat this. After, now 14 years nearly, Shaykh Raja Ashman pass away and no shaykh, his sister is here, but Shaykh needs a shaykh here for Malaysia, and inspiration come for my heart, we attend (appoint) Shaykh ʿAbdul Latif to be Shaykh for whole Malaysia, inshāAllah. Who not accept him is not from our ṭariqa, he can find another Shaykh; but our ṭariqa, he represent (Shaykh ʿAbdul Latif) Mawlana Shaykh Nazim, Shaykh ʿAbdullah Daghestani, until Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam.

People are free, if they don't want, they are not from our tariqa, from other way, they can choose one. But this way which it is coming through Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam.

Allah bless you, Allah make it, alḥamdulillāh, perfect for everything, nobody can say anything. For Sharia, for ṭarīqah, for everything, he is alḥamdulillāh, he cooked long time, now it's alḥamdulillāh perfect. 14 years after Raja Ashman.

InshāAllah Allah make this open for people, many people. They are lose their way. In sha Allah this, our tariqa way, Sufi way, good, mercy of Islām, mercy of Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. Many young people, many people coming again to way of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. Because with this difficult people not giving people something, they are even running not from tariqa, running from Islām also. InshāAllah, now this beginning to come back to Islām inshāAllah. InshāAllah Allah make it bless way, baraka for this country, for whole Islām world inshāAllah.

Fātiḥah
