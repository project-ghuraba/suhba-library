As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa raḥmatu Llāhi wa barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Wa ṣ-ṣalātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlina Muḥammadin. Sayyidi l-awwalīn wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr. Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī, madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Mina l-mu’minīna rijālun ṣadaqū mā ‘āhadū Llāha ‘alayhi fa-minhum man qaḍá naḥbahu wa-minhum man yantaẓiru wa-mā baddalū tabdīlan" (33:23). This āyah, verse from Qur’ān, saying there is real man they are promising and they are doing what they giving promise. Some of them completely they said, "We are going back to our old religion." Some of them they said for Sayyidinā Abū Bakr, "You must take this. If you don't make this, we are not following you, we are going back to our old." This is real manhood for human being to make this. To be honest, to be honorable you must give your promise – what you promise you must do. And he was promising and not afraid from all this tribe, from all this enemy and he fighted them and make them coming to way what they run away from this. And the end, before maybe thirty year ago, AllāhNone grant him through awliyā’u Llāh, he said through ProphetNone, be his name to be Ḥaqqānī.
