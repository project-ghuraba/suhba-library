Shaykh Bahauddin sohbat of the 19th of May, 2014.

Masha'Allah, this is very big gift - to swim in the ocean of Allah. This maybe it looks like simple words but it is so huge ocean.

There is a story about Sultan Suleyman. But which Sultan Suleyman? Nabi (Prophet) Suleyman, the one he was the highest rank in sultanate, ruling in this dunya. And Mawlana Shaykh used to say, there are 7 ranks of governing that have been given for humanity. And the top level is rank 7. And this has been given to Sultan Nabi Suleyman. This is Nabi Suleyman he was governing the wind, he was governing the clouds, he was governing the jinn. He was governing everything in this world, including the creatures, animals, ants. Everything was under his comand. Whatever he ordered they were obeying. It is, if you go for ant colony and you stand up there and you say "I am your lord." Who will look at you from the ants. You are bigger than them and say. They do their own job, even they don't bother to look at you. But S.Suleyman was saying "you are under my command." and they were obeying. SubhanAllah.

One day, Nabi Suleyman he was sitting on his balcony and he was looking and he saw 2 people walking and speaking. And it was coming interesting for him what they were speaking and he ordered the wind to bring their talking to his ears. And that talking coming to him. And the topic was; one of the friends telling the other "SubhanAllah, how Allah Almighty He gave this power to Nabi Suleyman." And the other one was saying, "Masha'Allah Subhana Allah! So great Allah Almighty." and that topic coming to the ear of Sayyidina Suleyman. Immediately he order jinn to bring them for him. They came in front of Nabi Suleyman and he was so haibat (majestic), so powerful! Everybody shaking to come in his presence. Say, "yes, cannot look." He was saying "I heard what you speak." Say, "we don't have any jelousy we don't have any hasad for you."They were shaking. He says "No, no don't worry. I would like to tell you the words that you said what is the value of it." And they were saying, "what were we saying? We forgot. "From fear they forgot And say "you tell to your friend Subhan Allah, what Allah give to S. Suleyman." Say "This SubhanAllah, I can give you everything that I have! For this "SubhanAllah"- Everything! Even my power, my ruling my palace, my richness my everything, for Subhana Allah!"

This is Mawlana Shaykh gift for us. Subhanim Allah. This means we are in your ocean Ya Allah. Sultanim Allah we are under Your Command Ya Rabbi. And Nabi Muhammad, making us to accept Sayyidina Muhammad as our Prophet that our to be proud that we have such a Prophet, that he is our Prophet. Alhamdulillah, and Allah Almighty He is hiding the ocean secrets in 2 words. Such a huge ocean He makes it to be reachable you know what it means reachable? Reachable it is something like this - you see a plane, for example and you don't have money to buy the ticket to enter that plane. For example if you don't have money to buy a ticket, you will never ever be in that plane. It is forbidden for you to pass the custom control, the immigration control, and everything. We say "no, you don't have a ticket! So we don't allow you to go for checking desk. We are not allowing you to go for the custom desk. We are not allowing you to go for the immigration desk." Say, "I want to go for the plane!" "No!

So Allah Almighty He made it so simple and easy. He gave you the ticket of the plane. Made you to have access for the check in, making access to get to the customs. Making access to get to the police. And everybody telling you "you can go for the plane now." This is Allah Almighty how He make it easy for us. Subhana Allah and you are in the plane. SubhanAllah and you are in the Ocean of Allah Almighty. This is how Allah Almighty He made it so easy. And so reachable. Maybe you will not own that plane but you will be in that plane. It is important. It is not important to own. Here it is important to enter. And this is our problem, that we want to own. Don't try to own you'll be stupid! Take the ticket and be in the plane. That's it! The operational side leave it for Awliya'. This is what the salafi they don't understand. This is what the wahabi they never understand. They say "No! We want to own!" It is not for you, you are stupid. You are only passenger. Try to get the ticket and be in the plane. This is it! Let the Awliya' to operate. Allah Almighty He is the owner of everything. That's why they say, "Your prayer is not good. Your this is not good. Your reading is not good. Sharia is this!" What's going on? If you are the owner, ok! You can tell whatever you like. This is a very important thing, journey to Allah Almighty. When we are in the plane, we start the journey to Allah Almighty. It is important to know how to enter the plane. And when you enter that Heavenly train, Heavenly Plane, train is in the road. We must fly. We are flying. Even the fly can fly. What Allah Almighty He put us in His Heavenly Plane. Alhamdulillah!

This it will be good for us, to have a comfortable journey. Alhamdulillah the Awliya' are promising us to get a comfortable journey. Because we are travelling in the time zone. Time zone is very important. We are all time thinking about the time zone it is "O we are bored, what time we will....you know." Time makes you bored. But actually it's the most precious thing in our life, time. And Allah Almighty He can replace everything to you except the time. He will never replace it. The time it is going on, and on and on. Even nobody can stop it. Say somebody "time up!" Go out. "Time up!" Go out! "Time up!" Go out. Yahoo! Me, you go also. No out, finished." No mercy. The time doesn't have mercy.

And our journey it is important to have a good captain. The captain is important in your journey. So If there is no captain, this plane will have dangerous to be crash; to not continue; to be not going for runway also. I am not saying already flying, still on runway. And nobody knows how to make it take off. Mawlana Shaykh he appointed for us even me, I would sleep also. Mawlana Shaykh he is giving us very good tidings about the captain that he appoint for us. Alhamdulillah Mawlana Shaykh he gave us the best option that we never dream about it. And Mawlana Shaykh he put us in safe hands, that is Mawlana Shaykh he is the one that he didn't hesitate with this Tariqa. And he didn't put it for chance to say this Tariqa can be with anybody. No! He made it perfectly handle to the perfect captain to continue running it. Alhamdulillah. It is important for us that we take bayat from S. Mehmet and that he gives us his bayat and he renew our bayat for acceptance, that he is the new captain.

And Malwana Shaykh he didn't leave any inheritor because he didn't have anything in his name and he didn't have nothing in his pocket. He distribute everything for the poor people. I am not sad because I didn't have any inheritance, but I am happy that Mawlana Shaykh he is the one that show us the dunya as empty, as nothing. And this remind me a very nice story about one Awliya'. He was in the death bed and he was very rich, very wealthy. And immediately he distribute all his wealth. Nothing left except his bed. And everybody comes and says "Ya Shaykh how you dare to distribute everything without thinking about your family, your children, without thinking about. And the Shaykh he was saying "Allah He is the One, Al Mu'ti,(The Giver) that He give. He give me. Al Mughni,(The Enricher) that He made me rich.

And he was having all this wealth coming for him and now I am distributing this wealth for the poor people and I believe, because if I leave something for my children maybe they will do haram with my wealth that Allah Almighty He give me. And they will be having sin because of my wealth. But now I am Alhamdulillah free like a bird. And I believe that Allah Almighty how He give me as Al Mu'ti. And how He make me rich, Al Mughni. Then, His manifestation can go on in my family in my children, in my.. So he say, no fear this is Allah! How you can be Tawakkul (reliance) that you put your back for Allah Almighty? Big richness and you've been tawakkul on Him. And you are appointing all your things from Him. And you believe that Allah Almighty gives everything without if you deserve or you don't deserve Allah Almighty doesn't look. You deserve or you don't deserve He doesn't count like this. When He give He give. And when He takes, He takes.

We are saying Ya Rabbi we believe that You are giving and we believe that You are taking but we are more for Your giving. You are taking, we are not ignoring but we don't want to know. We don't want to know. We know that You can take, but Ya Rabbi we are not in that point. We are more in the giving point.

Alhamdulillah. You are the jewelry of Mawlana Shaykh. You are the children of Mawlana Shaykh and you are the ones that Mawlana Shaykh he care about you and he is in love with all of you. It is important to know that your value is so high in his heart, and you've been treated as high value people Alhamdulillah.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfiq, Bi Hurmat al Habib, Bi Hurmat al Fatiha.
