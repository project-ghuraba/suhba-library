Aū‘dhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-Shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Waṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-F’āiz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-

Allah is everlasting, of course, and good works are everlasting. A man goes to the Next World either with good or bad deeds. What we need is good works that are everlasting. An exemplary of such good works are what we observed in the life of Mawlana Shaykh Nazim. He spent his entire life in good works and service. It was not an easy, but rather a difficult, life. However all that remains in the past now. What remains are his good deeds. The good of these deeds, and that from however many people he guided, is written in his Book of Deeds as a reward for him.

If there is anything remaining of Islam in Cyprus, this is for the sake of Mawlana Shaykh Nazim and because his effort and struggle. He was around 23 or 25 when he first arrived there. The pharaohs of that time were trying to wipe out the religion from its roots, and they almost succeeded. No one could do or say anything. Only Mawlana Shaykh Nazim stood against it. He caused the people to be guided, and for them not to fear others but only Allah. Against him those heathens who were trying to get rid of the religion of Islam could not succeed.

In Cyprus, there are shuhada’ from amongst the Companions of the Holy Prophet (SAW). (Those others who are [ruling] over this earth are [only] temporary.) So Allah sends some people there. It was Mawlana Shaykh Nazim’s kismet to be given this role. He received the rewards of his doings. From there he caused many from all around the world to be guided. Thousands, a hundred thousands of people were honored by Islam. There were many who were Muslim by name but who lived like non-Muslims, and they too were guided.

All of these are eternal deeds, good and accepted. These are what is important. Those other ones who oppress, who are against Islam, are disgraced and left. In the Next Life what they will face is worse. They did not leave this place with good deeds but bad deeds. It was evil what they did to people whom they diverted from the path. They did bad to all, and will receive their punishment. For us, what is important is to leave this place with good, eternal deeds. We are not here for this world but for the Next. Allah provides [for us] here and in the Next World. There is no hindrance. May Allah protect us. May Allah have us stay on the path of Mawlana Shaykh Nazim, which is the path of the Holy Prophet (SAW). May Allah have our steps fixed on it, insha’Allah. May it be strength to our iman. Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-F ti ah
