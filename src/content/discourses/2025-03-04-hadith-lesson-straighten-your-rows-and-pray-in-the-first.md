As-Salāmu ‘Alaykum wa RaḥmatuLlāhi wa Barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu BiLlāhi Minash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. BismiLlāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm.

Wa ṣ-Salātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Akhirīn.

Madad yā RasūlAllāh, Madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi RasūliLlāh, Madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

Dastūr Mawlana Sheikh Abdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistāni, Sheikh Muḥammad

Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad.

1- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

ﻢْ ﻜُ ھِ ﻮﺟُ وُ ﻦَ ﯿْ َﺑ ُ)ﱠ ﻦﱠ َﻔﻟِ ﺎﺨَ ُﯿَﻟ وْ َ أ ﻢْ ﻜُ َﻓﻮُﻔﺻُ نﱠ ﻮﱡ ﺴَ ُﺘَﻟ ،ِ)ﱠ ُدﺎﱠﺒﻋَ

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “O servants of Allāh, either you straighten your rows or Allāh will make your faces and hearts oppose each other.” If those rows are not straight, hearts will be hostile towards each other; there will be no goodness. If the row is straight, their deeds will definitely be good.

2- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

يراﻮﺴﻟا ﻦﯿﺑ ﻒﱠ ﺼﱠ ﻟاو

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Stand in the first row and on the right side.” The right side of the prayer rows. Even if it is the first row on the right side. Pay attention to that, says Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. “And beware of having a pole that separates between the rows.” So, if there are poles, beware of them, our Prophet says, be separate from the pole. Because it cuts the row causing a gap.

3- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “The first row has superiority over the other rows in prayer.” The first is more superior.

4- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

ً ﺔﻋﺮﻗ ﻻإ ﺖﻧﺎﻛ ﺎﻣ لِ وﱠ ﻷا ﻒِّ ﺼﱠ ﻟا ﻲﻓ ﺎﻣ نَ ﻮﻤَﻠﻌﺗ ﻮﻟ

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “If you knew what the rewards were in the first row, you would definitely draw lots to pray in that row.” It goes as, “I won the draw, it is my place.” The other didn’t win so he prays back. There are that much rewards in the first row.

5- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

ةﻼﺼﻟا ﻲﻓ ﻢْ ﮭِ ﺑِ اوُﺪَﺘﻘْ َﯿﻟِ رِ ﺎﺼَ ﻧَْ ﻷاو

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “The Bedouins should stand behind the Muhajirin and the Ansar, so that they may follow them in prayer.” People who know more of prayer should stand before others, in the first rows, says our Holy Prophet. The Muhajireen are those who migrated from Makkah, and the Ansar are the ṣaḥābah who were in Madinah. Let them be in the first rows and those who do not know stand behind, says Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Because they are Bedouins, they came from the desert, so they still don’t know the prayer, the adāb, etc. So they should stay behind until they learn, says our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu alayhi wa sallam.

6- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says: اﻮُﻔﻠَِﺘﺨْ َﺗ ﻻَ و

َ

ﻢْ ﮭُ َﻧﻮ ُ ﻠَﯾ ﻦَ ﯾﺬِ ّ ﻟا ﻢﱠ ﺛ ﻢْ ﮭُ َﻧﻮ ُ ﻠَﯾ ﻦَ ﯾﺬِ ّ ﻟا ﻢﱠ ﺛ ﻰﮭ

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Let those of you who are mature and prudent be nearer to me,” Scholars and those who know Qur’an and prayer well should stand behind me in the first row, says Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. “And then those who are next to them.” Those with lower knowledge stand in the next row behind them. And after that, those lower than them in this regard. The rows should go to the back in that order. And of course, the rows should be filled. If it’s not filled, you can stand in the front. “Do not stand crooked in the rows, so that your hearts do not fall into discord.” The rows should be straight, so that their hearts and love may be pure. “And beware of tumult such as found in market.” So don’t talk, shouting and calling like in a market, says our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam.

7- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Those who can learn something from me and convey it to the people should stand behind me.” Because every action of our Prophet, every word, every saying is necessary for the ummah, those who stand behind him must learn those things and teach them to others, says Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. He said, “Let them stand behind me in prayer.”

8- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

ﻒٍّ ﺻَ

Our Holy Prophet is saying,“The feet that get dusty while walking is not more beloved to Allāh than filling the gaps in the rows of prayer.” There is no more dust now but Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam is praising those who fill the gaps in the rows. He declares that Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla loves them.

9- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “The straightening of the rows is amongst those things which make your prayer a correct and perfect one.” So, when all of the rows in prayer are straight, the prayer becomes more beautiful.

10- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

ﺮِ ﺟْ َ ﻷا ﻦَ ﻣِ ﻦ

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Whoever frequents the left side of the mosque, and stands in a row on the left side, Allāh will write for him two Kifl (fold) of reward.” So, when one side is empty, and all of them are on the other side, if one goes to the left, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla will give him two-fold of rewards.

11- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

ناﺮَ ﺟْ َ أ ُﮫَﻠَﻓ ﮫِ ﻠِ ھْ َ أ ﺔِ ﱠ ﻠﻘِ ﻟِ ﺮﺴَ ﯾ َ ﻷا ﺪِ ﺠﺴْ ﻤَ ﻟ ْ ا ﺐَ ﻧﺎﺟَ ﺮَ ﻤﱠ ﻋَ ﻦْ ﻣَ

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Whoever fills the left side of the mosque because the congregation are few,” if he completes it, “he will be given double the reward.” Because when all of them stand on the right side because there is reward, and the other side is empty – if a person fills this side, he gets double the rewards.

12- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

ُ)ﱠ ُﮫَﻌﻄَ َﻗ ﺎuﻔﺻَ ﻊ

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Whoever completes a row, and fills the gaps in the rows, Allāh bestows His mercy upon him.” Meaning Allāh loves him, and is generous towards him. “And whoever cuts a row and leaves a gap, Allāh will also cut His mercy off him.” Rows are important; there should be no gaps.

13- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “He forbade that children take place in the first row.” Because it is not clear what a child will do among the adults. Therefore, children should be in the back rows.

14- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Have the imam in the center of the front row and fill up the gaps.” So, it is wrong for the imam is to stand on the right and not in the middle. He must stand in the middle, and then if there are gaps, fill those gaps, says Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam.

The Messenger of Allāh says the truth in what he said or as he said.
