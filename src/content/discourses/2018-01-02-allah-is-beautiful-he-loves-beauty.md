Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya

“Innallaha jameelun yuhibbul jamal.” “Allah is beautiful; He loves beauty.” Allah created everything beautifully, but people are ruining it and making it ugly. Whatever Allah created He created beautiful. It is not alright to change what He created. People do not like what Allah created and are even trying to change themselves with surguries and this and that. Whereas, even if they properly pay attention to their food, to what they eat, what Allah created is most beautiful.

Allah created everything beautifully. The world was also created beautiful, but people ruined it and finished it. You cannot even drink water anymore as this is dirty and that is bad. People have mixed filth and dirt in the water, in the sea, and in the air. What Allah creates is clean and beautiful. Our Holy Prophet’s (SAW) order is the same way: he cursed the one who mixes filth in water. Our Holy Prophet (SAW) does not curse, but he cursed because this is so important. We need to be careful.

So your home, where you live, also needs to be beautiful. It needs to be a place where you can live. It is End Times now, they are demolishing big houses and making it one fourth the size, like a box. They are not thinking how people will live. “Here, we built you a home. Live in it!” But you cannot live in it! Then diseases come out and what not. Here, this is the reason.

People should not complain. Allah gave the most beautiful, but son of man ruined and destroyed everything. Then they keep complaining. Allah showed son of man both the material path and the spiritual path. Those who abide by them are in felicity in this world and become the people of felicity in the hereafter. May Allah grant us all beauty. May we do beautiful things Inshallah.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq. Fatiha.
