Shaykh Mehmet: Madad ya S.Muhammad Nazim al Haqqani, dastur! Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fil jamia'

The sohbat of today is insha Allah for the things done in haste. Things that are done in haste bring regret. It is said, "Al `ajalatu mina 'l-shaytan wa-'l ta'anni’ mina 'l-Rahman" What is done in haste is from shaytan. And what is done deliberately is from Rahman, from Allah. But the people of this time are always in a hurry, they want everything to happen immediately. Whatever they do, all fast. For this, it is called 'the time of speed'- they called these centuries, the century of speed. There are cars, planes, phones.. Man tries to do everything fast now, speedily. Whatever comes to his mind, he does immediately, tries to do immediately. And if he does, mostly he regrets. Because as we said, shaytan meddles in the things done in haste;it deceives. Because of this he regrets afterwards.

This is for everything, not just one thing. He hears from someone or something comes to his mind - "I have to do this immediately or someone else will do it & make more profit. Or I do it before another person does". But if he was to think that, everybody has the same sight & way of thinking. Everybody has the same opportunities. All of them do & later regret, saying "why did we do this?"

And the worst of this is... Some people say"how did you wait in your mother's womb for 9 months patiently?" if he is an impatient, hasty one. Now the baby shows patience but his parents don't, his relatives & neighbors don't. They say "look, this one is a girl or a boy?" Meaning the ones outside can't be patient for 9 months. The one inside has to be patient regardless. But the ones outside they interfere in Allah's work. This is exactly shaytan's work. Many people...It has become such a normal thing, become a custom. "My wife is pregnant". "Pregnant, what is it?" they ask. "What do you mean 'what'? It is human" "No no, is it a girl or boy?" It has become such that...

Men has no patience anymore. They want everything in a hurry. Want to be rich, want it immediately. He rushes into something and then loses all he has & regrets at that time. All these things in haste are from shaytan. But for haste.. When will you make haste? Make haste towards doing khayr/good deeds. If you will do khayr, they said "Ajjilu bi-salati qablal faut; ajjilu bi-taubati qablat maut". "Hasten to offer your prayer before it's time lapses - pray it in it's prime time and make tawba immediately. Death can come to you at any time." Man doesn't know when he will die. Therefore this, making tawba in front of people, etc..Some say make tawba in front of murshid, make tawbah in front of Hoja.. There is no such thing. You make tawba immediately. You should rush to give your zakat. You hasten, immediately to give your zakat.Don't say, I will give later. If it comes to your mind to give sadaqa, give it also immediately, rush. You should go to Hajj. You have money for it, you are healthy, you have all.. Don't say let me wait a bit more & go later.It's fardh for you,go immediately.

For everything like this, for khayr work you can hasten. But for the dunya work, definitely take it slow. Think it through. Because at the end of it, there can be regret. Even for the person you will marry, you will do business with, buying selling, if you have something to do with him/her think well if it will be good or bad. And then do it.

Like we said, the religion of Islam is the most perfect religion. Because he (sas) is the last Prophet it has nothing missing in it. It shows the way to every beauty, every goodness. Who follows & applies it does not regret. And this is one of it's advices - Don't make haste for dunya work. For the work of akhira do it immediately Don't spend any time, make it very fast at that time.

Today we speak about hurry, to be hurried. To be hurry, it is from shaytan. And to be, to wait & look around, and after do your work, that is from Rahman, from Allah. This two century, they said 'speed century' because all people they have car, they have train, they have plane, going coming quickly for everywhere. People they forget to be waiting for doing. They, what do by try to do it well. Now people also, they don't have any patience now. All hurry.

Even when some ladies pregnant - before there was a saying about somebody very hurry, he is not have patience, "how you stay in your mother womb 9 months?" Now, this baby they wait. But parents not waiting. They must look, scan it if he is boy or girl. And even it is normal now. So many time we find people asking somebody what it is.. I say, human being. "No no" he said, "girl or boy?" It is normal. And this is worse to.. Because Allah make covering to people to wait, not to look for this. This is not their business to see, if he is boy or girl. But people, from hurry they not realise they making harm in themselves. When they do this,they said "many people they look & they find, nothing happen". Ok, many..If everybody harming this, with scan nobody they will be doing. But it is, I think between 5% or 2% it must be harming this. But from 100, they said "no, this is not..." But still they taking risk. For what? Because they are hurry. To be hurry, it is all time people losing. Even to do some work or to go somewhere to.. they be hurry to "before another people they can take this from me. Nobody know this". No, everybody has ear & everybody has eyes. Many people like you also, they know this. But only who is hurry, he can be go to trap.

To be hurry for dunya, it is very bad. But why, for what we must be hurry? To be hurry to make good thing, to do worship. First to be hurry to make praying before time finish. Other, to make tawba before death coming. So tawba, you must not wait for, to come to Shaykh or imam to do or to do your tawba in front of him. Direct, quickly immediately you must do this. Zakat also, you must give zakat. Not wait many month after, 1 year. In one year, you must be, every.. you must give like in Ramadan.. You can give..After also you can give but if you give same time, it is more better. You be hurry for this. And to make sadaqa, if you like to do sadaqa quickly also do before your ego says "No, it is too much. Don't give this, don't give that.." And for Hajj also. Many people they are young, they have money, they have everything. They can go to hajj. Meaning first hajj because it is fardh, obligatory. You must quickly make this. This is what you do for hurry.

Because dunya, you cannot, you don't know what is this. Like Mawlana Jalaluddin Rumi say, "3 days; yesterday, it is passed. You cannot do anything then.. If you give whole your money you cannot take yesterday to come today. But today, you have today. Today you can do what you cannot, you didn't do yesterday. Or you couldn't do it. Tomorrow, you don't know if you will be alive or not alive". For this, to be hurry with good thing.

Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, Al Fatiha.
