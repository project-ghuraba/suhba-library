A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Wa ṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīn wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh madad yā Mashāyikhinā dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistāni, Mawlānā Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī madad dastūr. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

Our ṭarīqah - Naqshbandī ṭarīqah. Ṭarīqah Naqshbandīyyat al-‘āliyyah meaning the highest ṭarīqah. There is many way. For our ṭarīqah Naqshbandī in Indonesia also. There is Naqshbandī but I think other ṭarīqah they are more from Naqshbandī ṭarīqah. All they are for helping people to take them to heavenly station, to make their maqām higher.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh Indonesian people they are humble people. They are happy for everybody coming from where they come for helping Islām. They are welcoming them, they are happy with them. Why we are coming to this Muslim country and everything - they have ṭarīqah, they have everything? What is reason for us to come here? Of course we are happy with all of you. We are happy with your generous hosting us, making everything very nice. But it is not reason for us to come here. Our reason for this because in day of promises yawmu l-‘ahdi wa l-mīthāq you all written to be Naqshbandī and AllāhNone was giving this date for each of you to meet you and to give you bay‘ah, to make you Naqshbandī. Mā shā’a Llāh here there is hundred thousand of mashāyikh, ‘ulamā’, awliyā’. All other ṭarīqah they are here but your way it is for Naqshbandī. So we must come AllāhNone be make us to help us to come here. Mawlānā Shaykh coming here to make this, give you this what we promise in Promise day to meet you and to give you Naqshbandī ṭarīqah. And this is what happen when we in going four days out of Jakarta many people they was coming and insisting to take bay‘ah. For us here end of the world for us - so far. And we are going until middle of this big land and in small town they are coming asking for bay‘ah.

This is our duty. We are happy when we see. We are not looking - he is or she is rich or she is poor or beautiful, ugly, handsome or not handsome. Only for our duty to give bay‘ah not to look for outside appearing. Mawlānā Shaykh Sharafu d-Dīn ad-Dāghistānī, our grand Shaykh, Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh ad-Dāghistāni's Shaykh, our Shaykh also. He was saying if I'm going from valley and there is one old lady in top of mountain asking for bay‘ah, I'm going to give her. This is our duty. In shā’a Llāh as much AllāhNone give us power and life we like to come and to visit you and give bay‘ah for who are want this bay‘ah. From other ṭarīqah also people there is bay‘ah two kind. One to be following our way Naqshbandī Ḥaqqānī way, Naqshbandī ‘āliyyah, ṭarīqah Ḥaqqāniyyah ‘āliyyah, and one only for tabarruk - just for taking barakah. This for who make bay‘ah for his intention, what the intention AllāhNone accept in shā’a Llāh. And we are happy with this. No feeling any tiredness, any sadness anything. No. Only we are happy who AllāhNone make us to be wasīlah between you and ProphetNone. And we are not looking for people from another ṭarīqah to catch them and bring them here. No. Mawlānā was saying we are we are not like that. But who has from day of Promise he will be with us, his heart he will come to us and he will come. But we are not running to other ṭarīqah - "oh come to our ṭarīqah, you must be this better from you or..." It is not good, not necessary for our. It is gift from AllāhNone as much, as like whole everybody has something his be better from like profession, like his attribute, what he like, what he not like. Also for ṭarīqah not everybody can follow. Everybody can follow many way and one of this our ṭarīqah Naqshbandīyyah ṭarīqah.

Some people they knowing to write. Somebody knowing to AllāhNone give him good song voice, he can song or he will read qaṣīdah. Somebody can make some craft. Everybody has a special and this for ṭarīqah also. Everybody he AllāhNone make his heart to follow one ṭarīqah. A very important thing to respect other ṭarīqah also. And to respect mashāyikh of ṭarīqah. All of this ṭarīqah who make ṭarīqah they are great awliyā’u Llāh. And not to argue with other ṭarīqah people because maybe other ṭarīqah even they say making you to be angry. Be calm and only say you are also our brothers, we are happy you are following ṭarīqah..

Because this time end of time, it is for fitnah time. Everything in between Muslim they make reason for fitnah. Even you are from that ṭarīqah - I'm here. I'm better from you, you are not better from me. They make hatred between ṭarīqah people. And we are happy when hearing there is other ṭarīqah they have five thousand people gathering, ten thousand people gathering, fifty thousand people gathering here. Even I hear hundred thousand people, ṭarīqah people they gathering and following their Shaykh and they listening for him. Mā shā’a Llāh this only in your country. Nobody in other world country something like this. Mā shā’a Llāh. We are very happy for this. And for ourself it is important for our people to be gathering, to make we make for each place wakīl for us. After we, when we leave here, he make attending dhikr, ṣuḥbah, giving bay‘ah. Everywhere in shā’a Llāh we have this. With this your connection going on and you take barakah and you get bless.

Every week, every week once khatm and ṣuḥbah, ṣuḥbah even five minute, ten minute, from maybe ḥadīth, maybe from book Mawlānā's book you can say and to give bay‘ah also you must do this. If not in once a week maybe two weeks once they can, they must come. But if he leave it three weeks he will be not good for follower of ṭarīqah. Because we are coming maybe every year once. And there is, we must... also Mā shā’a Llāh here you have many good murīd. They must be head for other because to bring water from other make to make water with carrying water you cannot do anything you must to from yourself to help each other. And who is looking for this people he must be patient, he must be knowledge for religious thing. It is not easy to do this. First you must control your ego. And ego it is not easy to control. You must until end of your life. Don't be trust - "I finished my ego so I can do anything I want." And the heaviest thing for man and this what for wakīl khalīfah who are in area, if anything wrong maybe, maybe with knowing or without knowing he do something. So who are in this area they can tell him. Ask him what is this, it is wrong or not wrong? You must ask him. But not in front of people beside. It is too heavy for our ego. And people many times they are saying something, it is not no reason for complain.

So also you must be patient if you before answering you thinking good and if they are right, you say "ok maybe I not knowing, I will look also." After this if you are right, you can tell them. If no, say "you are right. I can do myself better." Once Mawlānā Jalālu d-dīn Rūmī's son Bahā’u d-dīn Walad - his name. He is also great awliyā’. He was successor after Mawlānā, but there was Ḥusāmu d-dīn Chalabī after he was - after him he come Bahā’u d-dīn walad. He was asking his father Jalālu d-dīn Rūmī, Mawlānā Jalālu d-dīn Rūmī, "O my father" he said to him, "I'm wondering you said my ego when I was five maybe six year old, my ego following my spirit, spiritual my... my rūḥ. And every time you are speaking in your Mathnawī and everything against ego. You still speaking about this how it is?" Because Mawlānā Jalālu d-dīn Rūmī his father also very big awliyā’ Sulṭānu l-‘ulamā’ Bahā’u d-dīn, Shaykh Bahā’u d-dīn and he was teaching him, training him so he is six year old, he is finish his ego. Ego following him, not following surrounded. Shaykh Jalālu d-dīn Rūmī answering him and he say to him "O my son, this ego the most idiot thing in the existence. Why? Because only want bad thing for himself. Nothing good they want, ego. For this I'm afraid if he leave him, he will come again because he is stupid. Ego he will come again. So until end my life, I'm fighting with him, making it under control."

And once also another story for this. Mawlānā Jalālu d-dīn Rūmī he was going to his khalīfah place, Ḥusāmu d-dīn Chalabī. He and his murīd and he taking his son riding mule. Mawlānā Jalālu d-dīn Rūmī walking with his murīd and his son on mule following them. He was after his father and Mawlānā Jalālu d-dīn Rūmī when he was walking Bahā’u d-dīn Walad, Shaykh Bahā’u d-dīn Walad he said he see light coming from his father and like sun going everywhere. And he was thinking, "Oh why this who are criticising Mawlānā Jalālu d-dīn Rūmī many some who are not happy with him, not accepting him how this people they can deny this big awliyā’. How they are in and I'm seeing this whole light coming from him." He was thinking like this. And suddenly Mawlānā Jalālu d-dīn Rūmī turn back to him. Said to him, "O my son, leave this people. You look for yourself. Be careful from your ego. Because this ego can from everywhere make you to go down and you are maybe you be proud. You are even now, you are riding up one meter up from us. You can your ego make you proud and this proudness make you to lose everything." This what he was teaching him and very even not one point leave him to be with himself, with his ego quickly. Making him to make it right.

This is Sulṭān of ākhirah. Jalālu d-dīn Rūmī, Sulṭān he was time of great Sulṭān Alā’ ad-dīn Kayqubād this is Seljuq Sulṭān - like Ottoman, strong very strong. Alā’ ad-dīn Kayqubād he was finishing this crusader. Fighting agains Mughul. So he was very strong and he was loving too much Sayyidinā Jalālu d-dīn Rūmī - loving, loving. And he come to Mawlānā Jalālu d-dīn Rūmī when he was little bit ill, he come to him and tell him, "O my Shaykh, in shā’a Llāh, when you are be better I will give you my sultanate, you be king here, for here you be Sulṭān." And of course he said, "We are not looking for sultanate in this world. And our time finish. so after little while we will be pass away. In shā’a Llāh in ākhirah you will be follow me after while also in shā’a Llāh forever we together" he said to him. And really it was like this. And after three days in Jum‘ah day, Mawlānā he was passing away and Sulṭān very sad. He was accepting condolence, not...condolence in earth not in throne.

After this khalīfah he was Ḥusāmu d-dīn Chalabī after Mawlānā. And from in even in his life he was attending khalīfah Mawlānā Jalālu d-dīn Rūmī, and when pass away Jalālu d-dīn, Ḥusāmu d-dīn Chalabī he come to Shaykh Bahā’u d-dīn Walad and tell him, "Oh you are son for our Mawlānā, our Master, our beloved one. So you continue this ṭarīqah. You are, Mawlānā was loving you too much and he, you are with him every time. You must continue this ṭarīqah." Shaykh Bahā’u d-dīn Walad he said, "no it is order from Mawlānā and you attend as khalīfah. You must be and we must follow order of Mawlānā." And he was continue until his death. After him he take Shaykh Bahā’u d-dīn Walad.

So this is how real awliyā’u Llāh, real ṭarīqah people, they was doing and they was able to take everything but they have no reality. For this when we appointing them somebody, we are they many people they are able to do this. And we al-ḥamdu liLlāh we know they are good but also we awaring them from ego, from shayṭān. Because they are doing big job and AllāhNone reward them for each people coming for ṭarīqah, for hidāyah, like this world six times. Maybe can be cheat people. Somebody but his life is short, even if he live hundred year also it will finish and he will ask what he done. But if he has good intention and helping people and making people to come hidāyah, he will be real reward and he will be blessed and he will be have all barakah in this world also. This is what Khulafā’u r-Rāshidūn they was saying first when they are going for khuṭbah, when they after ProphetNone. Sayyidīnā Abū Bakr, ‘Umar, ‘Uthmān, ‘Alī - all when they are going to khuṭbah asking people, "If we do you see wrong thing, you warn us, tell us what is wrong. Don't be ashamed even with sword you can make us to come to right way." This is what they saying, this is our way from Sayyidina MuḥammadNone, Abū Bakr raḍī Allāhu ‘anhu. Our way like this.

So who are looking after ṭarīqah, after people he must be real patient and real look good intention. But al-ḥamdu liLlāh there is now something you can see from far away also. So in shā’a Llāh we asking madad from Mawlānā and Rasūla LlāhNone to make, give power for you and for wakīl khalīfah who are there here to continue this way the best way. We who are following ṭarīqah, he must know when he is following Shaykh and his ṭarīqah to know it is the best. No need to say for other people who are not from our ṭarīqah but when you are together you must say, thank for AllāhNone, our ṭarīqah the best ṭarīqah. This is what Sayyidinā Khālid Baghdādī saying - murīd must know and accept his Shaykh and ṭarīqah as best, the best one. Maybe your ṭarīqah ten people, maybe twenty people, maybe fifty people. Other ṭarīqah maybe five million people. You musn't be saying "this better than us." Make for yourself you say, "ok I'm nothing" but for your Shaykh and your ṭarīqah you must be proud and saying, "oh al-ḥamdu liLlāh we are following the most stronger." Because Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla where HeNone put secret it is important. Because everybody HeNone create from earth and it has same body, same everything, but some of them they have special secret - they are chosen from people. It is faḍlun min AllāhNone, favour from AllāhNone. AllāhNone what HeNone want, HeNone can do. For whom HeNone can give, HeNone give. You cannot interfere with this. And we must be thankful for, we must be thankful for HimNone. Because million of people that not knowing anything about this. Thank you for you who are listening and you are tired.
