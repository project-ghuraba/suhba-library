As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh. A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says, "Ulā’ika lladhīna hadá Llāhu fa-bi-hudāhumu qtadih" (6:90), in this āyatu l-karīmah.

The most beloved people for AllāhNone are the prophets. After that, the ones close to them, the companions (ṣaḥābah) and the friends of AllāhNone (awliyā’u Llāh). AllāhNone loves those who follow their way. HeNone says to follow their way. AllāhNone has granted them guidance. No one can prevent what AllāhNone wants. Therefore, this is Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla’s command. If you follow these blessed people, you will be guided. If you do not accept them, nothing will be good for you then. There will be no goodness for you at all. May AllāhNone be pleased with you.

Today so many people, with the barakah of Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim, have been honoured with Islām. Non-Muslims have joined the way. By means of him, people who had gone astray were guided and found their way. In the ḥadīth sharīf, it is said that Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says, if AllāhNone loves a servant, HeNone orders Jibrīl ‘alayhi s-salām to love him. And HeNone says the same to the angels, “Love this servant of Mine.” When the angels love that servant, everyone loves them. Their love is from the love of AllāhNone.

Therefore, to respect and love the awliyā’ and to love the good ones is the most beautiful thing. What we mean by beautiful is: the most beneficial thing. Not everyone can have this beauty. The person whom AllāhNone loves attains this beauty and this virtue. AllāhNone makes HisNone servants love him, the believers from HisNone servants. HeNone can make everyone love him, whether believers or not. Respect and familiarity come, because he is loved by AllāhNone. That is why these blessed people are a light for us. They are the light of our life. They are the light of our hearts. With their light, people find happiness.

People are at ease when it is light out. When it is dark – most people are afraid of the dark, afraid of darkness. But when there is light, it is good and there is no fear. There is no fear of the dark in the daytime. Everyone, most people, have uneasiness even if nothing happens. But when there is light – it is like this even if for the light of this world. Inner light is more important. That is what is important. Because when there is darkness inside, even if the light outside is bright, still that uneasiness remains – but of course not as much as at night. People attain that inner light only with the love of AllāhNone. These people of light illuminate others with their light. The universe is already illuminated with the light of our Holy ProphetNone. It was created from hisNone light.

May AllāhNone accept your visit. HisNone Mercy is great, HisNone Compassion is great. Both those who came and those who could not come – because of course it is very difficult for everyone to come. Even if they came, mā shā’a Llāh, Cyprus could not fit all of them. So may AllāhNone be pleased with them also. May AllāhNone grant them all, grant them from HisNone light. May AllāhNone be pleased with all of you.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying in Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n, we read: "Ulā’ika lladhīna hadá Llāhu fa-bi-hudāhumu qtadih" (6:90). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘aẓīm. You are coming here for sake of Mawlānā, which AllāhNone praise them, these people. This in Qur’ān, these people, AllāhNone gave them hidāyah (guidance). The most beloved one for AllāhNone, these people. Of course there are prophets. AllāhNone gave them hidāyah. From their hidāyah, other people continuing this order from AllāhNone. AllāhNone give them hidāyah. You follow them. You follow their hidāyah, their good way, right way. You follow them, you will be beloved for AllāhNone. Because these people, beloved for AllāhNone.

And ḥadīth of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, "Idhā aḥabba Llāh ‘abdan amara Jibrīl wa-l-malā’ika uḥibbahu." If AllāhNone love somebody, ask, order Jibrīl ‘alayhi s-salām and angels to love him. And he will be, when they love him, he... they become beloved one in Earth. Everybody love him. This is awliyā’u Llāh, which AllāhNone love them, and from HisNone order people love them. Because they are in way of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. HeNone, if they are not in way, AllāhNone will not love them, not order Jibrīl to love them. So, and people, and with order this, they are loving awliyā’u Llāh. They are coming where awliyā’u Llāh. They are going, visiting even very far distance. They are going everywhere to get blessed. To get nūr, light, from these people. These people who are giving, distributing nūr, light.

Light, it is very important for all creatures. Without light, nothing happen. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla create whole this universe from light of, nūr of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. And this light coming for awliyā’u Llāh, for who love Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. They give for their heart. Of course in even light, in daylight or night, it, you can see even by yourself. Many people, they afraid from darkness. When night coming, maybe people they are afraid to go here, there. Which no light, with light maybe little bit there, but when light going they are afraid. So light giving happiness, giving good feeling for people. Even, this for material world, but most [more] important from this: light of heart, which Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, hisNone light came – coming for heart of people. And this coming from awliyā’u Llāh also. They bring people to them to take light. Everybody coming from outside.

Of course here, now, many of people coming now, al-ḥamdu liLlāh for ‘Urs of Mawlānā with their love – love, and it is very, mā shā’a Llāh, too many people. But who cannot come, it is more, very more than [those who] they come. AllāhNone give them also light, because they have intention to come – but it is not easy to come and to be here. But for intention, for their love, AllāhNone give them also, because they love, who AllāhNone love him. It is, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are following this people which AllāhNone give them hidāyah and order us to follow them. Our intention to follow them, because we know they are hidāyah for human being. And al-ḥamdu liLlāh, here many of people, whole of people nearly, they become to hidāyah, to Islām, or to ṭarīqah, or to leave their bad character and follow good – changing themself to good people – with Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, you are following him, you will be, in shā’a Llāh, with him in Jannah (Paradise) also.

AllāhNone give you and give these people who are following from very far distance, and all give us light in our heart. This is more important. Because light of heart make you happy. If you don't have – you have darkness inside and outside is light – nothing good for you. The most important, to be your heart, light. Nūr, in shā’a Llāh, in our heart. AllāhNone make this for us with barakah of whom HeNone love them, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
