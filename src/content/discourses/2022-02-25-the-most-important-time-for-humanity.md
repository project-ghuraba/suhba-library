As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A’ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

May AllāhNone make us from those who know the value of these days. We are reaching the end of holy Rajab. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla said about this month, “It is MyNone month.” In it, there are two holy nights. Every Friday is a holy day, just like them. There are certain nights which are valued very much in the presence of AllāhNone. Of all days, Fridays are valued very much, and come every week, shukr to AllāhNone. HeNone made us from those who know the value of this day, in shā’a Llāh, because the honour of the day of Friday is to go to Jum’ah prayer at the time of Jum’ah. There is an order. For those who are able to carry out this order, but do not, it is a great loss. Apart from being a sin, it is also a loss. It is not a profit, but a loss for them. People who skip this holy time for dunyā and deal with useless things are like people who leave jewels and gold to deal with garbage.

Therefore, this holy month has come again, and in it there is Laylatu l-Isrā’ wa-l-Mi’rāj, when our Holy ProphetNone went up to the presence of AllāhNone and reached the highest level. No one from among humankind has ever reached that level. Our Holy ProphetNone reached it on this night, and it is a holy night. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla presented gifts, both to our Holy ProphetNone and to hisNone ummah on that night. Actually, showing respect to that night is a big duty for Muslims. People of the present time, who are Muslim but who don’t have īmān, don’t accept this. They don’t even think about it. They are not aware of it. Many of them are educated. They know Qur’ān and ḥadīth but don’t know the value of these things. There are many people like this. If they do so on purpose, they disgrace themselves. If they do it without knowing, or because they are not accustomed to it, they are still in loss. People go to the corners of the world to earn profit, for material gain and interests. But when it comes to ākhirah, they are lazy. They don’t care about it.

These nights are the times we should pay attention to, because we are not only living to eat, drink and to have fun. There are creatures who only eat and drink. Their duties are different. And mankind should not be like them. AllāhNone has given them duties appropriate to them. In the end, it has been given as a benefit to people. Mankind was created not to eat and drink but to perform worship in this world. Worship is the most important thing. Who doesn’t know about it says his life is eating, drinking and having fun. That is not life. Life, real life is this: to be busy with worship and to get the most usefulness from it. What we mean by worship, is everything. Not only praying and fasting, but also giving advice, helping others, and doing charitable deeds are worship. They are not useless. They are worship. They are the heart of worship too. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Lan tanālū l-birra ḥattá tunfiqū mimmā tuḥibbūn” (3:92). You cannot attain goodness until you spend and help others. That is worship as well.

Therefore, people should be busy with these things every minute. Some people only think, “We are eating and drinking. We eat and drink and sit. We eat and drink today.” Some people eat at breakfast, lunch and dinner. Some have breakfast and dinner. Some eat once a day on a diet. And the same repeats on the second and third day. People who think so have forgotten about their ākhirah.

We eat, but we eat with Basmalah and make shukr to AllāhNone. Then we eat again and make shukr to AllāhNone again. And we make du’ā’ so that it gives strength to our bodies and we have īmān. We pray. We make our waẓīfah. And then it is same the next day. It is so. AllāhNone created people in order to do this. They can do whatever they want then. But when you do this, you have to make shukr. Don’t think you are doing the same thing. This is life. It will go on like this. But you have to pass it with taqwá and worship. Otherwise, you will live for nothing. Four-legged animals are better than you then, because they won’t be questioned in akhirah, but you will be.

May AllāhNone let everyone live as HeNone wants. May we be as AllāhNone loves us to be, and as our Holy ProphetNone wants, in shā’a Llāh.

We are, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are in very good day and good time. We are living in this month of Rajab – Month of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. HeNone said, “Rajab MyNone month.” So, we are, nearly we finish, but it is holy and blessed month. And there is two night, very blessed night. First, first Thursday, to Friday night: this is Laylatu l-Raghā’ib. And second, it is after two days, in shā’a Llāh, maybe Sunday to Monday, in shā’a Llāh. But maybe other country I don’t know how, but here like this – and it is blessed night. AllāhNone give more bless in this night. In year maybe six or seven night very blessed like this. But every week also, AllāhNone al-ḥamdu liLlāh, with HisNone mercy give us Friday also. Friday night, and day also blessed one.

So, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are in this days, and this days it is very important for believer, for mu’min. Because they was, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, hisNone blessed – heNone blessed them. But many people they not remember this. In some country they don’t know at all about this. Muslim country, not this non-Muslim country. Muslim country, many country they not giving any importance for this night. Nobody know – no ‘ālim no imām, no shaykh no believer. Just when reading Qur’ān, they know there is sūrah for Isrā’, for Mi’rāj, but they don’t know when this happen.

When this happen, it is... it was the most important time for whole human being. Because the highest stage Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam reach from human being or from other creature even, near to Divine Presence of AllāhNone. And HeNone bless himNone, and HeNone generous himNone, and give present for himNone and for hisNone ummah – in this night! And how it will be not important for believer? So, we must give importance – to know this, how it is very important for us. And we celebrate. No, they celebrate for... Now we see this rubbish everything for... Pumpkins they put, pumpkins holiday, pumpkins Halloween. And this rubbish, before some days they make red thing for people. They celebrate every kufr day, but when you celebrate Muslim thing, they said, “You are shirk, you are kāfir”. What is this?

So, no, don’t listen to this people. Who not knowing, maybe responsibilty for ‘ālim, for ‘ulamā’ in that country of Muslim. Not only this, what they said Saudi, Wahhābī or… other, all, many maybe. Most of Muslim country they don’t celebrate. They don’t know this. And if somebody doing this, quickly coming against him. So it is, unfortunately, they not get any benefit. Who notice this and ask from AllāhNone, and Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone said, “Look for who asking something in this holy night, for angel. Give him what he want. Bless him, give him everything.” So, we are looking for rubbish, and not looking for jewelry. This is real, and they are real jewelry. It is for us in this life, important.

Because we are living, everyday... we, many people just eating, drinking, sleeping. After, next day, same; morning, noon, evening. Somebody eat five time, six time. This is... But next time, next day, same. Next day, same. So people, they are saying, “What is life?” This is life for animal, also living like this. Everyday they eating, sleeping, eating, getting more bigger. But they have benefit for – AllāhNone make them to benefit for human being. But when human being doing this, there is not benefit for them even.

Benefit if you are... Okay, you eat, with “Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm”, and say, “This for to be strong for AllāhNone, for HisNone way.” And for after, next, second time eating, third time eating, sleeping also; all with remembering. You are living for every day you get more benefit, spiritual benefit. This is benefit. But if you don’t have this, next day same, next day same. Whole life like same type. After, nothing – no benefit. But if you are living with... Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HisNone order, and you remembering HimNone, every day you get more benefit, you get more light, you get more reward. We are living, we eating, drinking and praying and making good thing for other people also. All this, like worship. Not only fasting and praying worship. Also to help other people, to give charity, to give advice, or anything you do; all this, benefit for other also, for believer, for human being. It is for your benefit. Because many people, they said, “We are fed up from – what is this life? Only eating, drinking, sleeping. Next day, again same. Next day same.”

You must be thankful for AllāhNone, you are living and you get everyday more reward. You must think about this. Because many people now, from their attracting from other culture, they are not remembering ākhirah. They just remembering this day, nothing else. So, we – every second, every minute, we must thank AllāhNone. We are, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, in īmān, believe; and in good health, and we remember HisNone favour for us. This is very important. AllāhNone make us to remember HimNone and to... If you remember HimNone you not be fed up, you not be any kind of stress or any kind of unhappiness. AllāhNone make us with HimNone whole time, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
