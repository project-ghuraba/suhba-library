As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
وَلِلَّهِ عَلَى ٱلنَّاسِ حِجُّ ٱلْبَيْتِ مَنِ ٱسْتَطَاعَ إِلَيْهِ سَبِيلًا ۚ وَمَن كَفَرَ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَنِىٌّ عَنِ ٱلْعَـٰلَمِينَ
<br>“Wa lillāhi ʿalā n-nāsi ḥijju l-Bayti mani s-taṭāʿa ilayhi sabīlā. Wa man kafara fa-inna Llāha Ghanīyun ʿani l-ʿālamīn.”<br>
“And Ḥajj to the House (Kaʿbah) is a duty that mankind owes to Allah, those who can afford it; and whoever disbelieves, then Allah stands not in need of any of the ʿālamīn (mankind, jinn, and all creation).” (Qurʼān 3:97). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla has commanded people to perform Ḥajj, saying that whoever is able should perform it; it is Fard (obligatory). He says people should perform it as long as there isn’t an impediment. Shukr to Allah, today is the Day of ʿArafāt. We are honored by this blessed day of ʿArafāt.

The divine manifestation that is present on Mount ʿArafāt is felt here, shukr to Allah. We feel it as if we were there, shukr to Allah. This blessed manifestation, this beautiful manifestation, is coming upon us. This is a beautiful favor from Allah, a grace from Allah; it doesn’t happen every year, but it is a gift once a year.

May Allah be pleased with our Shaykh, may his station be exalted, through his himmah, all the brothers are attaining that manifestation. Even if they don’t go there, the barakah, the manifestation, the reward, inshā’Allāh, has also come upon us, shukr to Allah.

They ask, “What should we do today?” We need to perform as many acts of worship as we can—reciting tasbihāt, tahlilāt, salawāt, and a thousand ikhlās—until evening. That is a gain. We must not lose it. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says this is a gift for people. 

Those who do not want—<br>
وَمَن كَفَرَ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَنِىٌّ عَنِ ٱلْعَـٰلَمِينَ 
<br>“Wa man kafara fa-inna Llāha Ghanīyun ʿani l-ʿālamīn.”<br>
“And whoever disbelieves, then Allah stands not in need of any of the ʿālamīn (mankind, jinn, and all creation).” (Qurʼān 3:97).

Those who disbelieve, who do not recognize Allah, who do not want Him — Allah is not in need of anyone! Allah’s favor is for the believers. He has no need of anything. He doesn’t ask anything from anyone. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla bestows these blessings so that they may earn reward and attain His mercy. Allah is not in need of anyone, nor does He need anyone’s worship or charity. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla is not in need of anything. He only gives these beautiful things so that they may receive them. But people don’t take them. They disbelieve. Those who disbelieve know best.

There are people who think they are something because of their possessions, money, weapons, and guns. They have no value whatsoever. If Allah didn’t give them a breath, they would perish. They would become corpses; nothing else.

Therefore, thanks be to Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla for this blessing He has bestowed. May Allah increase and perpetuate His goodness. Of course, as we said, the things to be done on this day are clear. Acts of charity and good deeds are performed. Qur’ān is recited, salawāt, Dalail Al-Khayrāt, and duʿās are offered. Do as much as you can.

Now they are standing in ʿArafāt, performing the Waqf, and making duʿā. Sometimes people think that – there was a certain majzub who told people, “At this time, get up and stand with the people in ʿArafāt, stand and make duʿā.” We did it a few times. Then, shukr to Allah, our Shaykh said not to do it. It’s not necessary. Because some things are done, and then you think you’re doing a good thing. Either knowingly or unknowingly, it causes fitnah (discord). There’s no need to cause discord. Just be on the right way, and that’s enough.

In our ṭarīqah, everything done is in accordance with the Sunnah, the sharīʿah and ṭarīqah; nothing else. Therefore, don’t look too much at things you don’t know. Do what your Shaykh, your murshid, does. There’s no need for anything else. May Allah forgive us all. May it be blessed. May Allah make it blessed and beautiful for us all.

Many of our brothers, beloved ones and relatives went this year. May those who couldn’t go also have the opportunity this year, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
