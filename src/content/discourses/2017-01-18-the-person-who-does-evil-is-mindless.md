Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Our Holy Prophet (SAW) states: “When Allah (JJ) wills something for someone, He takes away one’s mind.” That person does something, then Allah Azza wa Jalla gives his mind back. Then the person gets up thinking, “Subhanallah! What have I done?! Why have I done this?” So something bad has been done, because Allah Azza wa Jalla would not take one’s mind if a normal thing or a good thing were to be done.

A person who does evil is certainly mindless. They have done something outside of intelligence. A mistake means a wrong. A wrong means something that does not fit intelligence, mindlessness. Good things are not mistakes. A mistake, a wrong, are bad things. Therefore, we need to think before doing something, trust in Allah, and ask Allah for help. We need to seek refuge in Allah saying, “I am doing this but may He change my way if it is wrong.” If there are those who know, he needs to ask and consult them and then start the job.

You need to trust in Allah, use your head, and consult. In another hadith our Holy Prophet (SAW) said to the bedouin:

“I’qal wa tawakkal.” The companion had thought, “I trust in Allah,” he let his camel loose and the camel ran away. “I’qal” means tie it and use your head, then trust in Allah. So you have to investigate the reasons and perform the action accordingly.

Most people do things then regret it saying, “How did I do such a thing?” So they are making a mistake. May Allah keep us away from mistakes Inshallah. Mistakes occur in this world. People are able to fool others. In that case, you need to research, think long and hard, and then take action if it fits intelligence. Otherwise, it is not good to jump at things just because you want to do them. You could lose what is in your hands and regret it later.

May Allah keep us far from mistakes and may we always be in our right mind Inshallah. It is bad for worldly work if you make a mistake, but it is much worse for the hereafter. If you make a mistake regarding the hereafter, especially if you infringe on the rights of others, that time your situation is bad altogether. If you make a mistake Allah forgives that sin of yours if you repent, but never infringe on the rights of others! We need to always caution about this. It is not enough even if we say the same thing every day. The rights of others are very important. Pay attention to it.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq. Al-Fatiha.
