Audhu Billahi min al shaytan al rajim, Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Assalatu wa al salamu ala Rasulina Muhammad Sayidi al Awwalin wa al Akhirin. Madad ya Rasul Allah, Madad ya S Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, madad ya S.Muhammad Nazim al Haqqani, dastur! Tariqatuna sohbah wa al khayri fil jamia' Our way is through association & blessings are in the gathering.

We may say a few words & may it be good for you & for us insha’Allah. Man's nature tends more towards badness. Therefore Islam is a means to discipline him, to guide him to the right path but this is still not enough for man. When he is left to his own he is imprisoned more by his ego, shaytan & people around him. When one follows tariqat & a guide, the discipline is more perfect. The guide-murshid - makes up for his deficiencies. He always reminds him what his deficiencies are by sohbats & wird.

Today insha Allah (we speak about) this- Don't forget about goodness. This is the best character of the great saints. One man did him a favor 40 years ago, he never forgets it, saying "this man did us a favor". However he did many bad things after this but Mawlana doesn't care at all. He considers the favor as a debt for himself. And many people don't have this. Even if you gave one whole world, if he sees even a small (bad) thing from you, he starts talking about your badness; saying "he was like this, like that.." He forgets the goodness & reminds the badness.

This is not true only for people to each other, even to Allah they are like this. We read a Hadith today, which describes this very well. Our Prophet (sas) says in the Hadith, that there were 3 men in the time of children of Israel.

One of them had a disease called abrash (vitiligo). It is a disease where one has white spots on him, people get disgusted when they see such people. And also, there was a bald one. Before there were not many bald people so people would get disgusted with them as well. And, there was a blind man. These 3 lived in the time of children of Israel. Allah (awj) sent an angel to them, to test them.

The angel came to the man who had the vitiligo disease & asked: "What do you want?". He said: "I have only one wish. I want to be be healed from this bad disease. People are disgusted with me, they run away from me. I want my skin color to be like before & my skin to be beautiful. Enough for me". The angel said "Ok" & prayed for it. He also asked "which animal do you like?" Of course before there were no cars. Only there were horses, donkeys, camels. He said "I like camel". The angels said: "Take this camel. May Allah bless it for you".

Then the angel went to the second man, the bald one & asked the same. He too said, "People are disgusted with me, don't want to come close to me. I want hair to grow on my head". The angel said ok & made dua. His hair grew again. The angel asked: "Which animal do you want?" He said, "I like cattle". He gave him a pregnant cow & made dua for him too, "May Allah make it blessed for you".

Then he came to the blind one. The angel asked him, "What do you want?" "I want my eyes to open. I may see the people, the world. Enough, I don't want anything else". He prayed for him too & his eyes opened. "Which animal you like?" He said, sheep. "Take this sheep, may it be blessed for you".

Really, because it was the Wisdom of Allah those miracles appeared on them. They asked for a single thing only. None of them asked for sheep or cow, only to be saved from their conditions. It was worth the whole world for them. With Allah's Wisdom, from that one camel whole valley was filled with camels. The other one had a valley full of cattle. And the third one's sheeps had so much baraka that they filled all the valley. Allah's Wisdom, to make it a lesson for people, He shows these as examples.

This time the angel came as an ugly one, like the first man was before- came as someone who had the vitiligo disease, even more disgusting looking. Came to the first one saying "I am sick, poor, have no one to take care of me. Give me a camel so that I can continue my travel". He said "No, I have debt to pay, people have rights on these camels. I can't give you." The angel said: "Your situation was like me before, I know you, you were like this too. And Allah granted you all this" He said: "No, there is no such a thing. I inherited all this from my father". Look at man's ego. Before it was enough for him only if his skin was healed. Now when he became like this, he forgot everything. The angel said: "If you are lying, you return to your old situation insha’Allah"

He came to the bald one, he said the same "I inherited from my father" and turned him down. The third one.. When he goes to each one of them, he goes as how they were before: to bald as bald, to blind as blind. When he went to the blind one, he said: "I am a poor one, have nothing. Give me a few sheep that I sell them". He said, "Not just a few, you can take as many as you like & leave the rest. I was a blind one like you before. Allah healed me, opened my eyes & granted me all these blessings". At that time Allah sent His Heavenly Wrath on the first two, and was pleased with this last one.

Like this, our Prophet's (sas) Hadith shows that most people they forget the goodness/favor. He doesn't remember his old situation either, because of his foolishness. And when he does like this, he loses the barakah-blessings, lose what he has in his hand & gets to an even worse situation It means Allah doesn't do zulm- injustice to anyone. Most people are in this situation. There are many Verses in Quran about this too.

Some people, when they are about to be drowned in the sea they call for Allah. And when saved, they forget. So the one in tariqat, always says, "Allahu nadhiri, Allahu hadhiri"- meaning Allah is always with me, Allah is seeing me & we are always in His presence. This is the important point in tariqat- never forget the favor/goodness, but hide the badness. Even if you see something bad, it is better if you hide it. Today I will speak about, to look for good thing. In Islam, they are.. Islam making people to good behave, to make adab. But many times, it is not enough for people to be only Islam. So to be more higher position, to get rid of ego & this bad character, they must be in tariqat. And tariqat, teaching people what is good character.

Today we speak about to not forget good action some people do for you. Many people they forget good action, who people making. But awliya, especially mashayikh not forget. Sometimes you see Shaykh Effendi say: "This man did good thing for me". But it was maybe 20 year ago, or 30 year ago. He not forget at all. He forget if somebody making bad thing for him but not forget good thing. But normally people, even before one day they make good thing for him. When they make bad thing, they remember bad thing, not good thing. For this, today I read one Hadith of Prophet (sas). It was, just what people like this.

He says (sas), In Banu Israel time, maybe 3000 years ago. All people character the same. There were 3 people, ill people. One had skin illness. Maybe white or something like this. And people,they were disgusted from him. Other one bald, no hair in his head. Also, maybe at that time it was not many people bald. People also not coming to him, disgusted from him. And there was one blind man. So Allah (awj) sent one angel for them. First coming to this, skin illness he has. Asking, "what you want". He said, "I want my skin to be good, nice. And I don't want anything else". He said "ok", made dua & he become clean, cure. And he asking, "what you like from animal?" He said "camel". And give him one camel & make dua to be barakah. Barakah meaning, sometimes if you take something, with barakah growing. But without barakah nothing happen, going less.

And he went to other, bald one. He said "what you want". He said, "I want nice hair, and all this is what I want". So he said "ok". He make dua, and he become very nice hair. He said "what you like from animal?" He said "I like cows". Give him one cow.

And go to third one, blind one. He asking, "what you want?" He said, "I want to see this world, I want to see people because I am not seeing anything. I don't want anything else". And he make dua, and his eyes open. He see everything. And he isasking "what you like from animal?" He said "sheep". He give him one sheep. And went, make dua for barakah. Each one, one valley of camel for first one. For second one, one valley of cows. Third one, one valley of sheep- meaning many sheep, thousand thousand. Everyone they were happy. After this maybe, of course until become like this, need maybe 5-6 years or something like this. With barakah in 5 year, it will be like this. Because one cow,until coming like this.. When with barakah, quickly coming. In 5 year, these people forget.

So, now coming test time. Angel coming by, now this time coming by shape of one man has skin disease-ugly, disgusting. Coming to this first, who was in this position & asking "I am poor man, I am ill man. I want to go some place, I don't have anything. Can you give me one camel to ride & go?" That man, he said. He is lying also. He said "many people they have (I owe them) debt or something they want from me, I can't give you anything. Angel, he said to him: "Look, I remember you, you were same like me. So this is from Allah, you must give me". Lying more, this man. "No, no, this is from my father, grandfather. This is not from, can't be like this in 5 or 6 years. This is not from (Allah), go away". And the angel said to him, "If you are lying,Allah make you worse than before". And he went to the other one. Other one, coming also same like him before becoming rich, becoming with bald head. So said to him: "I want to go.." Same like first one. Other one also saying same: "This is from my father, from my grandfather. Nobody give me this". And same thing, tell him: "if you are lying you be worse than before".

And then coming to the blind one. And tell him: "I want to go, I don't have anything. If you give me..." This one, he said to him: Go & take what you want, and leave what you don't want. This is from Allah, gift from Allah. I was like you, blind. And nothing, Allah opened my eyes & gave me this barakah". So this, he make dua for 2 of who not remembering goodness. Allah make them worse than before. Because everything is in Allah's hand. And third one, Allah was happy with him. This is very good example. It is really like this. People, when they find something they are making everything bad. Not remembering at all, when nafs- they become ego riding on them, they forget goodness. They remember badness only.

For this tariqat, to train you, to control your ego, and to remember goodness, to forget badness. This is big irada- willpower. Who have weak willpower, he cannot do this. Willpower it is important here. Because you must be strong with willpower. If not strong, your ego stronger than you, so you follow ego. Allah don't make us like this. Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, Al Fatiha.
