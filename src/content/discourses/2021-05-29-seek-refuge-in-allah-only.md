Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Allah always shows the uselessness of everything people do. They struggle to do something. But because what they do is in order to satisfy their ego and to serve their ego, it has no benefit. In the end, they fizzle out. The truth shows itself. The truth is victorious. Falsehood never wins. They think that they won. However, they are eventually destroyed. Therefore, people in this world think that they own it, but they eventually break down by the will of Allah. They can be strong as much as they want. They can presume themselves to be important and come against Allah as much as they want. There have been lots of Pharaohs and Nimrods. None of them remained in this world. They thought that they owned the world and claimed to be gods. People are claiming worse things in the present time. They have no value at all.

People have become hopeless and don’t know what to do. What you should do is seek refuge in Allah. Whoever seeks refuge in Allah wins and is protected. Otherwise, whoever seeks refuge in those people will be broken down like them, as there is no one to protect you. If you rely on a person, there will be no person left. There is a saying, if you rely on a mountain, even the mountain will be gone. Nothing can protect you in this world. Seek refuge in Allah who will protect you in times of trouble and despair. Don’t lose hope in Allah. ‘ِااللّ ااةِ مَ حْ ار انمِ ااوطُ َنقَْتاالَ ’, ‘Do not despair of the mercy of Allah’. (Qur’ān 39:53) Do not lose hope in Allah’s mercy. This is good tidings. If people know this, they will not have troubles and they won’t be afraid. Be with Allah and don’t be afraid. Everything is by the order of Allah. If they ask you: “When will you die? When will you do this?”, you should say: “When Allah wants it”. There is nothing as: “You did this and that. You will die.” Nobody knows the unseen except Allah.

Therefore, people make mistakes and say things with or without knowing. Whoever knows, does not say: “When did you do this? You will die at this time.” It is the will of Allah. Allah gave a lifetime for everyone. He causes some means and in spite of you protecting yourself, when your time comes, you cannot stay more than a second. This world will not remain to anyone. Be with Allah so that Allah saves you in Ākhirah. Allah is The Everlasting. Whoever is with Allah here and hereafter is saved forever. When you rely on someone in this world, there is no one for you when he is gone. Therefore, whoever is with Allah always wins. May Allah protect us. The Fitnah of the end of times is big. Whoever seeks refuge in Allah is saved insha’Allah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
