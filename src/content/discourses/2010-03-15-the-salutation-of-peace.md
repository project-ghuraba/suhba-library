Madad Ya Sayyidi.

La ilaha illa Allah La ilaha illa Allah La ilaha illa Allah Sayyidina Muhammadun Rasulullah, Habibullah Nur Arshillah alayhi alfu alfu salat, alfu alfu salam Ya Rasulullah ishf`Alana. Madad ya Sultan al Awliya.

We know nothing. We are trying to know what is best. Teach us O our Grandshaykh. Teach us O Sultan of this World. Grant us what you have been granted. Heavenly Sultan, we are asking such a grant that may support our Real Being so that we are not going to be tired. Tiring is a sign of vanishing and finishing. When a person is getting tired fully that means he is leaving this life. He just left it. Therefore if a person is leaving this life for the next life, when he was 100 coming down, down, down and going to be 90, 80, 70, 60, 50, 40, 30, 20, 10 and zero, finishing.

Say, "Assalamu alaykum", O People. Salam brings salamat, peace to you. Therefore as much as possible say, “Assalamu alaykum”, as more heavenly grants come to you and you get much more energetic. Those who are leaving to say ‘Assalamu alaykum’, are coming down, down, down, to the point of being nothing, nothing, nothing. Reaching to finishing, finishing, finishing. As-salam grants to Mankind heavenly power, peace and lights and endless hopes and endless pleasure. Assalamu alaykum! Say it. Everything that the prophets brought from heavens was for our benefit. Not Arabic and Turkish, I'm speaking English. Eloquent speech. My English is more than Shakespeare's speech. Shakespeare was speaking and using such beautiful speech to people but not continuously. You may feel all of these pleasures when you are leaving on the last breath, then that pleasure comes on you as a dress.

If you collect everyone’s pleasures in your time, you can't reach such a sweetness, peace, pleasure and contentment as when your soul is getting out. It is covered with such a pleasure. If it were to be divided among all nations, all Mankind, their miseries and sufferings would leave them suddenly. More than ‘suddenly’? But lost in English also. Instantly, yes. Open your mouth! Why I'm making you sit next to me? That is pleasure.

O People! Come and say 'Salamu alaykum'. What happiness, what pleasure, what contentment. What endless pleasure for you in the last moment. All that is closed now, will open at that time. The Prophet (sas) was saying that for a Mu'min the most contentment and pleasure is the Day when he is reaching his Lord's Divine Presence. O People, therefore, listen. You never can digest that word 'Assalamu alayka'. Why saying 'alayka'? And I am saying because he is there. Allah Almighty is saying for Shuhuda, martyrs:

bal ahyaun `inda rabbihim yurzaquna They are alive with their Lord, well provided for (3:169).

If Allah going to be witness for the martyrs’ lives and say that they are living are you saying the Seal of Prophets (sas) is dead? Can't be. What is that foolishness from you, Wahabi people? Yes? And you know that the present tense is indicated in always, dawam. ‘Always’, without stopping. Hmm? They should say that, “That One Who makes anything to speak is making us, ordering us to speak and we are speaking now. It is not through our will. No! His Will is commanding, ‘Say the Truth!’ ”

Therefore don't say, "Who are you and your speech?" You must say at least what organs are going to say on the Day of Resurrection, antaqana allahu alladhi antaqa kulla shayyin. You may say, "That is a weak servant, an old servant and the Lord of Heavens is making him to speak to address or to remind us what is coming on the Day of Resurrection, therefore to think on it".

I know nothing! But this caution is important particularly for Wahabi people. Leave fanaticism and come to the big crowd of Muslims because the Seal of Prophets (sas) was saying, 'alaykum bi's sawad al-azam'. "O my Nation, if you see my nation separate into 2 parts and you are coming on them don't be surprised about which one I must go to. Where there is the greater mass O my Nation, go with them". The Wahabi people may tell me how many of them are among Ahl as-Sunnah wa'l Jama`a? Million? 2 million, 10 million, 50 million, 100 million. Hmm? "Alayka salam". Hmm?

I am asking a question. Making me ask a question. Allah Almighty saying:

wa`alamu anna feekum rasul allah And be aware that it is Allah’s Messenger who is among you (49:7).
