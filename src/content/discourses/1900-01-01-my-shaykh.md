You may say, "My Shaykh is from one of the seven, walī." You may say, "My Shaykh is the Ghawth." You may say such a.. But you may say, only. You may think that. You never know it - what is the real position of your Shaykh until Shaykh says it to you. It is your imagination. And your respect for your Shaykh - You are asking to be top. May be top. May be another one over that station. But you can't see. You can see only the top point for your Shaykh. Not seeing above him. It is true, what you are saying: "My Shaykh on top level." According to your vision but it does not mean that it is the end of whole ranks. No. According to you, that is the final. But in sight of Allāh Almighty it is endless stations and levels. Yes. And as long as your heart is with your Shaykh, it is all right.
