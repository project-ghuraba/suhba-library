As-salāmu alaykum wa raḥmatu Llāhi wa barakātuh. A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alá Rasūlina Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūl Allāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh ad-Dāghistāni, madad ya Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Haqqāni. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

Today is the 20th of Ramaḍān. In the last 10 days the Prophet (saw) used to do i‘tikāf. He made a special section in his masjid, and moved there. 10 days he did his worship there and didn't talk to people much. He prayed with the jamā‘ah. It was the Prophet's (saw) duty, he did it every year. One year he couldn't do it, he did double the next year.

I‘tikāf is a nice duty, it is an obligation on the Ummah but if one person does it, the rest are free of it. If one person doesn't do everyone will be accountable for it. "I‘tikāf is an obligation. Why didn't you do it?" Allāh Almighty will address them like this. Thanks to Allāh, now everywhere there are people, who love i‘tikāf. In every city, every country, even in villages there are people, who go into i‘tikāf. They are granted huge blessings, good deeds, prosperity. For them, also for the city they live in it is a blessing, prosperity. They keep the sunnah of the Prophet (saw). May Allāh be pleased with them, they do it instead of us. We don't find the opportunity, never could do it but they are doing so when we ask their du‘ā’, in shā’ Allāh, a bit of their goodness comes on us.

Ramaḍān is ending, 10 days left. In it there is this i‘tikāf. Laylatu l-Qadr is in these 10 days, in shā’ Allāh most probably. If we can catch it, how lucky we are. In shā’ Allāh, we perceive the reality of Laylatu l-Qadr.

Yesterday there were a lot of people, doing i‘tikāf. May Allāh accept it. May Allāh make it easy everywhere. Many more years, in shā’ Allāh - next year they do it with Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām. Our prayer is always happiness and finding the true path, only then can you be happy, you can't be happy with anything else. Saying "Let's do this, that" they do it their way, in the end it is useless. Whatever they do, it comes to nothing. Important is to find the true path, then there is happiness.

Today, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, it's the 20th of Ramaḍān beginning I‘tikāf. I‘tikāf what it means? It is to stay in mosque for men, 10 days. Last of Ramaḍān's 10 days you stay in mosque worshipping there 24 hrs. Only for wuḍū’ you go wuḍū’. You eat everything there, praying, worshipping. It is sunnah of Prophet (saws). When Ramaḍān coming Prophet (saws) was making more worshipping and when last 10 days he was preparing, order people to prepare place in the mosque and he bring everything - he moved to the mosque. 10 days worshipping. What was he doing? He was worshipping more than speaking to people. He was liking I‘tikāf, meaning to be away from people. He was away 10 days. Only he want to be with his Lord, worshipping 24 hours. He didn't want to be with people.

And it is obligatory for ummah but when somebody doing, other it is ok no need for them to do. But if they do, ok also they can do. But if not [nobody] doing, everybody will be blamed, accountable because nobody do this. But if only one do it, it's ok it's enough. But who is doing it is big blessing, big barakah, Allāh reward him because he is doing sunnah of Prophet (swas). Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, nowadays many people everywhere they like to do i‘tikāf. I‘tikāf better to stay alone from people. But for eating, drinking like... fasting people, Not like khalwah - a special thing to eat or to drink, it is free they can eat everything. It is barakah for [those] around also people.

Allāh give barakah and give hidāyah this is what we want. Hidāyah means, to show them good way, because human being they are looking from time to time to change their style, they are not happy with what they [have]. Because they are doing by their mind. If they follow order of Allāh, they will be happy, no need to try every time new, new one. Sometime after, finish. They become sad, they become miserable, they become hopeless. But Allāh, He put His rule, you can do until end of the world. No need to look for another thing [else]. And this is making you happy here and hereafter.

Allāh make us happy all the time, in shā’ Allāh, with hidāyah and barakah, in shā’ Allāh, we ask barakah of these 10 days of who are making i‘tikāf, Allāh gives barakah for us also, in shā’ Allāh.

Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq al-Fātiḥah.
