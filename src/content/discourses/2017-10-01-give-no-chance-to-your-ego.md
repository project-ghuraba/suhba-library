As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa raḥmatu Llāhi wa barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-Ṣalātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīn wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

Our biggest enemy is our ego, says our ProphetNone. "A‘dá ‘aduwwika nafsuka llatī bayna janibayk." Your worst enemy is your ego, says our ProphetNone. Your ego takes any shape. It is turning good things into bad, useless, and worthless things. You worship and if you are happy with yourself, that worship becomes worthless. Sayyidinā Abū Ḥanīfah once went to the Ka‘bah with his father. They woke up for tahajjud. They got up for tahajjud. Everyone in old times was different from now. Now it's different, people can't sleep. Then most of the people slept. Sayyidinā Abū Ḥanīfah said to his father: "Look how they are sleeping." He meant they didn't get up for tahajjud. And his father replied in a wise way: "You too, my son, go and sleep because after these words of yours your tahajjud became worthless."

Don't see your ego as high. If AllāhNone has favoured you to do goodness, to get thawāb and to worship, make shukr to AllāhNone. Don't use it for your ego. Don't look down on others. Let your worship be pure for the sake of AllāhNone. That's the important thing. The ego can't handle the tiniest praise. Praise is for AllāhNone. AllāhNone loves those who praise HimNone. And HeNone likes to be praised. It suits HimNone. We don't deserve it. Don't praise yourself even for the tiniest thing. Don't spoil your ego. No need to look at people and say they did this and that. Your ego can make you worse in a moment. Therefore, control your ego. Always keep your ego under control. Always educate it. Don't leave its halter even for a moment. If it runs away once, it can take you out of the right way and throw you down the cliff. May AllāhNone protect us.

Tarīqah order of to follow order of ProphetNone. And heNone said, "You know who is the most dangerous enemy for you? It is your ego." Your ego it is the worst enemy for you because it is showing you to bad thing and encouraging you to do bad thing, not love to do good thing. To do any good thing, even small thing, is difficult for the ego to do this. So this ṭarīqah is all the time training the ego to make it down. To make it good behave you must make this. Don't give any chance for your ego. Don't even very small thing even it is ego can take from anything to make against you, you are doing good thing. Your worshipping even with this can change it to bad thing. Once Sayyidinā Abū Ḥanīfah - he is the Imām al-A‘ẓam, the biggest imām for madhhab - was in ḥajj with his father and they was waking up for tahajjud. Old time not like this - now if people waking tahajjud... they are sleeping still but there is not like before. They was whole people was sleeping that time. No any voice it is good. So was sleeping and he and his father was waking up for tahajjud and he was looking around all people was sleeping. He said to his father, "O my father look this people whole sleeping." His father told him, "O my son, you go sleep better for you, better from your tahajjud, because you say something you finish your good thing. No benefit anymore after this what you said." Because he say, maybe see himself better than other all sleeping and he is waking for tahajjud.

So when this, his father teaching him and taught him and even Sayyidinā Abū Ḥanīfah he said after, when end of his life said, "I'm was praying with wuḍū’ of ‘Ishā’ I pray fajr." Was never sleeping after this. Whole night was forty year he never sleep because if he sleep he must make renew wuḍū’. So he was with this, so how he is teaching him his father he get good result and giving for this millions of people following his madhhab.

It is very important to not give any chance for your ego. Whole time you must tie it. If little bit you loose your tie, it will run away and take you to bad place, maybe to dangerous place you cannot go back again. For this ṭarīqah you must be careful from your ego. Don't say, "I'm shaykh, I'm imām. I'm praying five time. This people not praying." You must be thankful for Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla HeNone help us we are in this way and ask for other people hidāyah, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
