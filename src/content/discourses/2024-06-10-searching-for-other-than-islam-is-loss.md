As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Aūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
وَمَن يَبْتَغِ غَيْرَ الْإِسْلَامِ دِينًا فَلَن يُقْبَلَ مِنْهُ وَهُوَ فِي الْآخِرَةِ مِنَ الْخَاسِرِينَ
<br>“Wa man yabtaghi ghayra al-Islāmi dīnāan falan yuqbala minhu wa huwa fī al-ākhirati min al-khāsirīn”<br>
“And whoever desires other than Islam as religion - never will it be accepted from him, and he, in the Hereafter, will be among the losers.” (Qur’an 03:85). ṢadaqAllahu l-‘Aẓīm.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says, whoever looks or searches for a religion other than Islam, it is not accepted from him. What’s accepted is to be upon the religion of Islam. Shukr to Allah, we have travelled through Europe for 12-13 days. Shukr to Allah, there are Muslims, and the old ones are many. People in these countries are searching for something. As what they’re searching for is other than Islam, it is not acceptable in the sight of Allah. Everything they’re doing according to their own minds has no value at all, and they will be in loss in akhirah. This is what Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla said.

We have seen very strange things; what they’ve worshipped, what they’ve done. But it is clear that it’s of no benefit. What’s beneficial is the religion ordered by Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla, the religion of Islam. The light is in it. Barakah, benefit and every good thing is in it. Those who follow it and go on this way have won, even if they are not rich in this world.

“The richest country in the world,” they say. That has no value at all. Because they can’t take anything of it to akhirah. They leave it in dunya and go. Moreover, they burn people here even before going to akhirah, and throw their ashes. This has no benefit. The end of this way they follow is loss.

Therefore, they have minds. They see, most of the times. But the ego, shaytan, and human shaytans which are worse; as soon as they take two steps on this way, this beautiful way, they immediately take them out of that way, throw them into the abyss and they perish.

May Allah protect us. May Allah guide them. May they see the truth. May they see the true way. May they not suffer in vain. May Allah give guidance to people inshā’Allāh. May He send the Sahib. May people not obey shaytan inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
