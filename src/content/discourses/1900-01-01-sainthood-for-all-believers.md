Ka‘bah is Ka‘bah. Awliyā’ is Awliyā’. Ka‘bah is Baytu Llāh. Awliyā’ is Walīu Llāh. Baytu Llāh is from stone. Walīu Llāh are khalīfah of Allāh. Who was Ka‘bah made for? Awliyā’ are those who carry the reality of Ka‘bah, not the stones. Do stones carry it? Of course, Awliyā’ carry it. Of course, Awliyā’ are greater. The one who dies with the sword of sharī‘ah in the apparent battle becomes shahīd. Awliyā’u Llāh are those who kill their egos with the sword of truth. Certainly their ranks and levels are very high.

The ranks of Walī and Prophets are higher than of Awliyā’. Among Awliyā’ and Anbiyā’, 124,000 are Anbiyā’ and 313 are Mursal. There is Nabīy Rasūl. And there is just Nabīy. Nabīy Rasūl are higher than simple Nabīy. And all of them are Walī. Among 313 Nabīy, 5 are Ūlū l-‘Azm, grand Prophets; Nūh, Ibrāhīm, Mūsā, ʿĪsā and Muḥammad ‘alayhi ṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salām. Khātamu n-Nabīyīn is higher than all. All from all are just like a spot in the ocean next to the greatness of our Prophet. And what is given to our Prophet, all of it is as much as what is left on the tip of a needle after it's put into an ocean and taken out next to the greatness of Allāh.

The greatness of our Prophet is as much. And the greatness of our Prophet; what is given to all creatures is as much as what's left on the tip of a needle after it's taken out of the sea next to the greatness of our Prophet. And our Prophet is just as that tip next to the greatness of Allāh. Walī, Walīyyun.

There is sainthood for all believers. But they cannot have it completely because they obey their egos. If they don't obey their ego, all of them have sainthood. M: Will they have it one day? If they work hard, they will. If they are as lazy as you, they won't even have a school diploma. It is so. Go and ask for it. How will someone who doesn't work take it? He will remain like that. Be organized.

If they put a ḥarām rock in between the structure of a building, that rock will be a reason for its destruction. And there are bad ones among believers who try to get inbetween. Don't let them do so. Don't leave them to spoil the relationship. If you leave, they will make each other sick. Just like a rotten grape, they will spoil everyone around. Don't be rotten. Don't be with the rotten. Don't be with those who have no adab. Be with those who have adab.

Fall and stand up with faithful ones. Fall and stand up with ones who have adab. Don't stand next to mangy people. Otherwise, you will scratch yourself here and there. There will no place remain you do not scratch. There are mangy people. Don't come close to them. There are many mangy people now. And there are rabid people apart from mangy ones. There are many rabid ones also. If they bite you, they infect you. May Allāh protect. Don't come close to them. Don't go into their community. If you find him alone, you can leave him ineffective. But if you go into their community, each of them will run onto you and tear you into pieces. May Allāh protect.
