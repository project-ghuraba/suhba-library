As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
اقْتَرَبَ لِلنَّاسِ حِسَابُهُمْ وَهُمْ فِي غَفْلَةٍ مُّعْرِضُونَ
<br>“Iqtaraba li-n-nāsi ḥisābuhum wa hum fī ghaflatin muʿriḍūn”<br>
“[The time] draws near for mankind their reckoning, while they turn away in heedlessness.” (Qurʼān 21:1). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says that the Day of Reckoning is approaching. They are in a state of heedlessness; their minds don’t even consider it. They want to stay away from it, to continue on their own way. Reckoning is not easy. It’s easy because Allah is forgiving.

The government, especially tax offices in some places, have very strong accounting systems. There’s no such thing as forgiveness. They say, you kill someone and they forgive you; but if you mess up your accounts in Europe or America, they won’t forgive you. They’ll imprison you. You don’t know when you’ll get out. It’s not like that with Allah.

There is an accounting with Allah. Before the reckoning comes, while you still have breath, while you are in this world, if you ask Allah for forgiveness, Allah will forgive you. Your reckoning will be over. That is, if you repent, seek forgiveness, and ask for forgiveness for your sins, Allah will forgive you.

Therefore, Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says it is approaching, and He tells people not to miss this day. Don’t miss the day of forgiveness. Travel the world and go while you are still healthy and able, but do not stray from the way of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. Do not stay away from the mercy of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. Allah’s mercy is abundant.

Therefore, it is not certain when a person will close their eyes or when they will breathe their last. No one can guarantee whether they will live an hour or a day later; there is no guarantee.

Therefore, Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla always says, “It has approached, it is near.” The Hereafter is near, not far. Some say the Day of Judgment is very far away. Even if the Day of Judgment is far away, your qiyāmah is when you close your eyes then open them.

On the Day of Resurrection, you emerge from your grave and head towards the Day of Resurrection. “What happened? Were we here this many years? It passed so quickly.” In the blink of an eye, says Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla, and people will go towards the Day of Resurrection in a state of shock. Those who do good will thank and praise Allah for having done good. Those who are far from Allah in dunyā will regret it in the hereafter. That is the place where even regret for what they have done will be of no use.

As we said, everyone should seek forgiveness from Allah while in this world. Allah forgives the sins they commit.

Therefore, when people mention entertainment or something far from religion now, you see thousands of people gathering. They rush there thinking, “There’s entertainment there, let’s go and have fun, let’s rejoice, let’s be happy.” But regarding the way of Allah , they stay away thinking, “Is there really such a thing?”

May Allah grant people wisdom and understanding. May He grant them guidance, inshā’Allāh. May Allah not lead them astray from the right way.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
