As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says, “Khayrukum man ṭāla ‘umruhu wa-ḥasana ‘amaluhu.” The best among you is the person who has lived a long life doing good deeds. That is a good person. And the worst among you is the person who lives a long life harming people. A harmful person is from those disliked by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.

Certainly, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla has given a lifetime to everyone. And AllāhNone knows what it is. How long they will live, how much they will breathe – that is appointed by AllāhNone. Nothing takes place outside HisNone will. Children and babies may live for one day or ten days. People may live for ten or one hundred years. That is by the will of AllāhNone. But it is stupidity to waste and miss this life. It is wise to know that this life is a favour and to live it as AllāhNone wants. Otherwise, people will pass away anyway, leaving everything behind. They will pass to ākhirah with nothing in their hands. People who cause harm will find nothing in ākhirah. And good people will reach great ranks. The highest wish of people is to be in Paradise. They will be given this as long as they have the pleasure of AllāhNone and as long as they are good. Otherwise, they are useless.

People nowadays not only don't know this, they don't believe it. They don't want to believe. You may or may not believe. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla takes no benefit from you. And your badness has no effect on Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. You do it to yourself. Whatever people do, they do it to themselves. Others may struggle as much as they want. Unless they put effort and work, no one can help them.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla created people of all kinds. There have various characteristics. As they have various characteristics, they have various thoughts, and their capacities are different. There is a type of person that whatever you tell them, they find an excuse, they find a pretext. Everyone is to blame but them, and they are innocent. They cannot do anything.

“Do this.” “– I can't.” “Do that, there is this job.” “– No, that job is like that.” “There is such a duty.” “– That duty is bad because of this.” “Do this.” “– No, it's not suitable for me.” “There is another job.” “– No, they treated me badly.” There is such a type of person. They get by in this world as they like, for themselves.

But there is another type which doesn’t accept or like anything. They don’t accept the orders of AllāhNone for akhirah. They don’t accept the orders of Islām. Their condition is worse, because where they end up eventually is not a good place. They think people will remember them when they die and say, “Our country had such a person.” They say, “Throw me in a hole, don’t come to me after I die.” They think they will be saved when they die. But the real life starts afterwards. You will be punished then. You will see then if the reality of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is true or not. What is true is true, with permission of AllāhNone. We are all going towards the truth. Everyone will see: those who believe and those who don’t. May AllāhNone protect us, in shā’a Llāh.

Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, heNone is saying, “Khayrukum man ṭāla ‘umruhu wa-ḥasana ‘amaluhu.” AllāhNone, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying to the best from ummah who they have long life, and they do good thing, good deed. Good thing they do. This is the best people. Best one which Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla love them. And the worst one, who has long life and he all time doing bad thing. This also opposite for other one. Other one he is one who will win his real life. Other one, only, in this dunyā even he is not good; in ākhirah he will be never happy. Worse and worse.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone gave, HeNone create us. And HeNone gave everyone time for live in this. After birth from his mother, some people maybe half an hour, some people one hour, some one day. Maybe one month, ten year, twenty year or until hundred year, maybe more than hundred year. All of this from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. So who doing whole his life from beginning, he is lucky, because he beloved one for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.

But this time people, they are not – never believe. Most of them they are running from religion, from belief. And they are thinking they are clever. They are doing this with making, looking for [at] other people as they are idiot, they are stupid. But they are... Reality, who think like this, they are. Because they must think from many thing Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gave us. They are... This coming from miracle. Everything in this world, in this life – miracle. Even your birth, also miracle. So who deny this, they are not clever people.

For this we are happy with what AllāhNone gave us. And we ask AllāhNone to give hidāyah for other also. We are not happy to see people running away from religion. And they are thinking they are doing, thing. Even in their life – of course, if they deny all religion – but even in their life they putting in their body something. Later become illness for them. They regret for them. They cannot get rid of this. Even this, they are not able to think about good thing or bad thing. When they see someone doing thing, they are imitating him, and running after him. And this, from beginning of world, shayṭān make people to run after bad people, who – as Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying for this bad people – they have long life, but they have bad thing. AllāhNone not love them. AllāhNone not love people [who] follow this people. We are sorry in this, we are living, time. Before, when old people they saying, “In our time...” but it was, their time, not too much different between their time and our young time. But now it’s completely different. Completely becoming very bad. Going from bad to more, worse. AllāhNone help us, because we are living in end of time. Everything mess up. Everywhere people they are, as Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, “Harj wa-marj.” Meaning, everything upside down. People they don’t know what they are doing – killing, war. Worse than war, this, what they are putting in mind of people. AllāhNone help us. AllāhNone give us, in shā'a Llāh, strong īmān. And to protect our Muslims and children of Muslimīn, because they are, they are most target for this bad people. AllāhNone keep them safe, in shā'a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
