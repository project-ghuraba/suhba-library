A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Shukr to AllāhNone, our old traditions are the traditions of love for our Holy ProphetNone, love of Islām. All traditions of the old people are like this. Everything was for the pleasure of AllāhNone. Therefore, it is our duty to keep what they did. We shouldn't let them be forgotten. Who lets them be forgotten has gone astray.

Shukr to AllāhNone, may AllāhNone bless it, it is the third day of ‘Īdu l-Aḍḥá today. An old tradition in Cyprus, that Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim used to follow, is going to Sulṭān here on the third day of ‘Īd. Who is Sulṭān? Hala Sultan. Hala Sultan who is the Sulṭān of ākhirah. They used to visit her on the third day. Her blessing is taken and ‘Īd is then complete.

Therefore, shukr to AllāhNone for AllāhNone is giving us this chance to follow this blessed tradition. We haven't been able to go for years. It was closed. We did not go. But nowadays this chance is given to visit her, to visit awliyā’, ṣaḥābah and prophets. Barakah and mercy descend on their graves and mercy comes to those who visit them. Do not listen to new people. They say visiting graves is wrong. Our Holy ProphetNone used to visit graves. And heNone advised this. There are ḥadīth. It is something that was always done. And it is the work of shayṭān so that people don't attain the rewards. He whispers in the ears of people and says it is wrong to visit graves and this and that. Where were you? Things done 1,400 years and 100 years ago are on this way. There have been deviants just like always. They don't show respect. There were people who didn't respect our Holy ProphetNone, hisNone ahlu l-bayt and ṣaḥābah. But they were shaken every time and got scattered like dust. However they got scattered, they got scattered over Islām.

Therefore, wherever there is ziyārah and a maqām, they should be visited to get their blessing. Qur’ān should be read, and all kinds of favours are given. Their virtue and blessing will come to you, in shā’a Llāh. We do it for the pleasure of AllāhNone. We don't worship them. We go to the beloved servants of AllāhNone. Mercy that comes down to them will come to you as well, with the permission of AllāhNone.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are thanking AllāhNone for these days also. We are in ‘Īdu l-Aḍḥá, third day. There is old custom, old people they was doing. They have many customs, many traditions they were doing. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are all our tradition according to Sunnah and Qur’ān and Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, good people. What they was doing since old time, this tradition coming until now. We are, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, especially for Cyprus island, they were visiting Sulṭān.

Who is Sulṭān? Sulṭān, Hala Sultan. She is Sulṭān of our island. The third day of ‘Īdu l-Aḍḥá, they was going there making food, eating, and reading Qur’ān, and get blessing from this holy Khālah, auntie. We say Hala meaning auntie, our Sulṭān auntie.

You can, they was doing this and they get blessed. And no problem to visit grave, graveyard. It is blessed because this area where they are buried, the shower of mercy, shower of blessing, AllāhNone showering them. So you are going there, you get this also. Don't listen to this people who are not knowing anything and they said this is not – ProphetNone forbid this. Never forbidden. Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam heNone was encouraging and heNone was visiting graveyard, reading. All ṣaḥābah they were doing this. But now, this shayṭān, he's not happy for people to get blessed from AllāhNone. He said make people to be not sure for this. But, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, who are following ṭarīqah, following real Islām, they know this is some chance, some luck for people who they have this people in their land.

So, we are also in this island, we are visiting her. In shā’a Llāh, we will visit now after ṣuḥbah. For this we are making short ṣuḥbah but it's important for people to know this. Everywhere where they have graveyard of prophets or ṣaḥābah or ahlu l-bayt or awliyā’u Llāh, they may visit to get blessing because we are not worshipping graveyard. We are worshipping Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla and Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla love these people and shower this for people. For this we going, reading Qur’ān, reading, praying. AllāhNone, for the sake of these people, accept our prayer. AllāhNone make them higher and higher, in shā’a Llāh, to we love them, to make us with them, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
