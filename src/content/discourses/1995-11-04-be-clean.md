Allah likes his servants, who are clean, who are careful about being clean. It is mentioned in the holy Quran. He wants them to be extremely clean both physically and spiritually clean. There is no point in being physically clean but dirty inside. If you are clean inside, your outside should also be clean of course. Physical cleaning is to clean the dirtiness from our body; to clean the dirtiness of your body, your dress, your worshipping place, your home, your street, your country. This is an order. Allah wants that. Allah loves that.

Our ancestors were very careful about cleaning. I heard people saying many times - "May Allah take our lives, when we are clean." Because there are people, who die while they are in dirtiness. Some people die in dirtiness. Some people die in their own vomit. So it is a nice prayer. We heard it from our mothers and grandmothers. May Allah take our lives when we are clean. Clean.

Tariqatuna s-sohba wa l-khayrun fi l-jam'iyya. Our way is the way of association. Association is for cleaning the attendants. If they have an outward or spiritual dirtiness, the speaker aims to clean that dirt. Whoever comes to an association, should make intention to clean their hearts. They look clean physically - we dress clean and take ablution and come here. Our aim in coming here is to clean our hearts. What is important is cleanliness of the heart. But it is not easy to achieve that. Many ignorant people claim that their hearts are clean. Most of those claiming that their hearts are clean, start stinking in their beds before they leave this world.

O our Lord! Pureness of heart is not from words. You can't clean it with soap. What do Turkish people say? Detergents. We can't clean by putting detergent inside and wiping. That is a different skill. The Prophets (as) came to clean the hearts. The Prophets, alaihimu s-salam, are the masters of this job. After them came the people, who were educated, authorized by the Prophets. The Seal of Prophets (sas) came to clean the hearts of all people. First to clean the hearts from unbelief, from the dirtiness of unbelief and polytheism. "Polytheists are filthy" (9:28) Mushrik (polytheist) means someone continuing in unbelief. Unbelievers are included. When we say mushrik (polytheist) unbelievers aren't excluded. A Musrik (polytheist) is also a kafir (non-believer). But every kafir (unbeliever) can be mushrik (polytheist).

Christians made three gods. They attributed a son to Allah Almighty. They fell into shirk (polytheism). They became kafir (non-believer). They became kafir because they didn't accept our Master (sas). They became kafir a third time because they denied Quran, which was revealed to our Prophet (sas). Jews aren't musrik (polytheist) because they say "La ilaha illAllah". But they are non-believers, they are kafir because they don't accept Isa (as). They are kafir because they don't accept the Holy Bible, given to Isa (as). Also because they didn't believe in our Master (sas), they don't accept RasulAllah (sas). Although they say 'La ilaha illAllah Musa KalimAllah', they are kafir because they don't say 'Muhammadun RasulAllah'. Because if they really testified to 'La ilaha illAllah Musa KalimAllah' - inside the Torah that was sent to Musa KalimAllah, our Prophet's (sas) name is mentioned in 70.000 places. Torah isn't such a small book. It is a big book. If they really accepted Musa (as) they would have accepted our Master (sas) also.

A Jew saw our Prophet's (sas) name while reading the Torah, in the time of our Prophet (sas). He erased it, then he saw in 2 places. He erased, then he saw in 4 places. He erased, then he saw in 8 places. He was surprised. It became visible, his holy name became visible even inside the books sent down from heavens. If they really believed in Musa KalimAllah and what he brought, they wouldn't be kafir. Because Hz. Musa testified to our Master 'ala Nabiyyina alaihi s-salatu wa s-salam. He believed in our Master (sas). Musa (as) was not a prophet until he testified to our Master's prophethood. None of the prophets took prophethood until they accepted and believed in our Prophet's (sas) prophethood. But those Jews didn't accept our Prophet (sas) because of jealousy and envy. They are kafir (non-believer). It is a lie that they believe in Musa and his prophethood. When they say they believe in the Torah, it is not true. Whoever doesn't accept the Prophet (sas) is a kafir. They are non-believers in 4 books - Bible, Torah, Zabur and in Quran. Yes they say "La ilaha illAllah" - it isn't shirk. Not shirk (polytheism) but they are kafir.

The Prophet of the end of times, the Master of the universe, the Pride of the universe, our source of pride, our Master (sas), he came to clean the hearts of people. The starting point for cleaning is - "La ilaha illAllah Muhammadun RasulAllah". He asked people to say this. Cleaning the hearts from disbelief, is understood by this testimony. This is the duty of our Master. The dirtiest, filthiest thing is disbelief and being a non-believer. Our Master, alahi s-salatu wa s-salam, came to clean that away. 23 years he fulfilled prophethood and kept the order of Allah Almighty. He informed people whatever Allah ordered him. The ones, who surrendered to him were cleaned. Our Prophet (sas) was born into a nation of ignorance. Because of their ignorance maybe they were the farthest people from having good manners. What did the Prophet (sas) do to them? Prophet (sas) cleaned the ones, who surrendered; took them from the lowest to the highest station. He made them clean. They became noor (light); were enlightened inside and out. Every atom of their bodies was submerged in light.

The companions became very clean. They conveyed our Master's eternal prophethood to the whole of humanity. They traveled, didn't stay in Makkah and Madina. They knew their duty. "Our duty is not waiting by the Baitullah in Makkah Mukarrama or to wait in the presence of Rasul in Madina Munawwara." They knew that and they went to the East and West to clean the sons of Adam. Whoever surrendered to them or obeyed them they were cleaned. They cleaned them from disbelief, then cleaned them from egoistical bad manners. They raised them from being outwardly human to being really human. They took them from human to the station of holy mankind. Our Master, alaihi s-salatu wa s-salam, wants people to reach that stage of being holy mankind. It means that it is obligatory to clean yourself until you reach that title. Allah Almighty says 'I like My servants clean. I love My clean servants.' There are hundreds of egotistical bad manners. But awliyaullah, whose hearts are awake, they inform us about 60 kinds of egoistical aspects inside the heart. It is a hidden section inside the heart but it eats people. It prevents you from being a perfect human being. A perfect human being is the holy mankind. Hajji Mestan you are a doctor can you cure yourself when you are ill? No. You call the doctor. Ya hu you are a doctor. Can't you cure yourself? No, call the doctor. You can't cure yourself. If we were able to cure ourselves, if people could be doctors by reading books, everyone would read the books and be a doctor. If people were able to be clean just by reading, Allah Almighty would send the Books from heavens to people. Everyone would read and act accordingly. However Allah sent down Books and Prophets. So you can't take a book and act on it alone.

If you take a Hadith shariff book and say "I'm reading this. I don't need a teacher, a shaykh, an imam" - you are ignorant. There are some who lead to the denial of prophethood. Prophet, alaihi s-salatu wa s-salam, those ignorant people say that he takes from Allah and informs people - that's all. Like a postman, he brought, gave and finished. He trained his companions one by one. He trained them, healed them, perfected and refined them. Every companion had a kind of perfection. That's why no one can put the companions on a scale. Whatever the Prophet (sas) said about their stations we believe it and that's all we know. Whoever believes in something else, has gone astray. He is gone astray. Every companion had a perfection.

Our Master, alaihi s-salatu wa s-salam, trained each companion according to their stations. One of them came at the end and looked into his eyes (sas), in his presence and said "I accept you Ya Muhammad you really are the messenger of Allah". When he looked, he raised the last one also to his station. He was in his (sas) association only once, his destiny was to meet the Prophet (sas) only once. He knew. In one moment he raised him to a station of perfection. Companions they are all perfect human beings according to their situations, according to their capacity, according to their capabilities. There are small diamonds as well as big diamonds. There are small and big diamonds. There are small rubies, middle size rubies, big rubies. Of course the value of a big ruby and a small ruby isn't the same. It is a ruby by its nature. It is a ruby in its own class. Its value is according to that. A big ruby is in its own class, has a greater value.

So our Master, alaihi s-salatu wa s-salam, made the companions clean. When he looks with a Prophet's look and grace, he cleans. It is his special attribute. Now there are awliya who are authorized to do that. After the companions, there are inheritors of the Prophet (sas) among the nation, who have that power, the light of sainthood. When they look with that light, the surrendering ones are cleaned. They clean people. You can't clean yourself. It needs training as well. For this reason O Muslims! Know that Allah loves the servants who care about cleanliness. Most companions had a piece of cloth covering their body to the knee. White cloth. But Grand Shaykh said that it was white dress. Their white dresses didn't look like white but they smelled like musk. Smell like musk. It wasn't dirty. Musk smell. As if they were inside the musk. Someone came to the Grand Shaykh. When Grand Shaykh kissed his beard - that man was 147 years old - he told me that his beard smelled like musk. He said "La ilaha illAllah" nonstop, a prayer bead in his hand. After completing that inner cleaning his breath was clean. Clean, smelled like musk. No need to put on perfume. Companions' dresses smelled like that - musk smell. With the tawhid (la ilaha illAllah) dhikr they made, their breath was purified. Wherever you touch their bodies, you smell musk. They clean inside and outside like this.

O you Muslims! All of us, we go to Allah's presence with the smell with which we exited this world. Beware! Leave this world clean. When they transferred a saint's tomb in Damascus after 500 years, they took the soil from his holy grave and let us smell. It was smelling like rose. His holy body touched the soil and it smelled like rose. When they took him out of his tomb, that huge crowd was shocked by the rose smell. Be clean. Do not bear hostility, don't be jealous. Leave love of this world. Don't fight. Don't search for the faults of others. Mind your own business. Ask Allah Almighty - "Ya Rabbi keep us with the clean ones. Let us be clean outside and inside." So your grave shall be clean. When you are resurrected on the Day of Judgment Allah Almighty will resurrect you with the clean ones.

We are showing you a target. This is an association. What did you learn? When they ask you "What did you learn? What did he tell you?" Tell them Mawlana asked us to be clean. I'm working for that". Know your target. Living clean and leaving the world clean is our main target because we'll go to the divine presence. You can't go dirty. The angels will kick you out. Angels kicks out the ones, who have the devil's attributes. They don't stop the clean ones. When a clean soul reaches heaven - "Who is this?" "It is this person." If he is clean, doors are open and he is invited. He goes there with a nice smell. Whoever leaves the world dirty, when he reaches there, the doors aren't open for him. They kick him back.

Beware! There is a countdown for all of us. It is not one, two, three, four, five. Nine, eight, seven, six, five, four, three, two - we count until one. Since we are born, there is a countdown. Not forward. Counted breaths are decreasing. We are counting down. Since it is unknown when we will reach our last breath, we keep our intention strong as much as possible and try to be clean. Our words make us dirty most of the time. Keep your tongue. It is our eyes that make us dirty most of the time. Keep your eyes. It is suspicions in our hearts that make us dirty mostly, bad suspicions that come to your hearts. Keep your hearts. It is our ears that make us dirty, the things you hear.

Allah Allah Allah Allah Subhan Allah. Allah Allah Allah Allah Allah Allah Sultan Allah.

You are the Sultan Ya Rabbi. Send the Sultan of Shariah. Send us the Shariah of Muhammad O our Lord. We ask forgiveness for our mistakes, we repent, we turn to You, Ya Rabbi. Send us Your divine beneficence.

Amin amin wa salamun 'ala l-mursalin hususan 'ala sayyida l-mursalin wa salamatun 'ala l-hadhirin wa l-ghaibin wa l-muminin wal hamdu lillahi rabbu l-'alamin al-Fatiha.
