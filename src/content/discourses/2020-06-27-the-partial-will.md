Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Allah Azza wa Jalla gives people the most precious things. Allah Azza wa Jalla gives everything, and tells us to take what we want. He gave us what they call the partial will to do so. Therefore, you can’t say, “How come Allah ﷻ does this?” Allah ﷻ does what he wants, and no one can ask Him. People of today want to ask everything, and they think they are smart. The person asking this question is stupid.

Allah Azza wa Jalla gave the will to people. And with that will, they can take what is good or what is bad. Allah Azza wa Jalla knows its wisdom, it is known in His sight. But it is a favor of Allah ﷻ that everyone does things by choice. A person who follows his ego does not choose the good, he chooses the bad. The ego never wants the good. It is Allah’s ﷻ wisdom. Allah ﷻ created man’s ego that way. The ego chooses the bad. You should train it with your will so that it can take the good and leave the bad. Whatever is bad, such as bad manners, bad actions and evils, are all from the ego and from the shaytan.

Therefore, thinking whether Allah ﷻ willed it that way - Allah Azza wa Jalla willed it but you cannot interfere with Allah’s ﷻ work. Allah ﷻ showed us the way, showed us the good and showed us the bad. And everything is from people. Because people can choose the good or the bad. May Allah ﷻ grant us to choose good things and help us. We ask for help because help is from Allah ﷻ. If you ask for help, Allah ﷻ will help you. But if you forget Allah ﷻ and stay away from Allah ﷻ, then you will go wherever your ego takes you and you will do bad things. May Allah ﷻ protect us. May Allah ﷻ grant us success in doing good works insha’Allah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
