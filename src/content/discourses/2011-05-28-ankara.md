Mawlana Shaykh Mohamad Nazim al Haqqani an Nakshibendi 25 Jumādā ath-Thāniya, 1432 A.H. Saturday, May 28, 2011 - after Fajr Translated from Turkish by: http://saltanat.org/ BismillahirRahmanirRahim [A big group of bothers and sisters from Ankara came to visit Mawlana. Because of the big number, more than 90 murids, they were called in Mawlana's presence in two groups: the first at night and the second the following morning. Their Imam, Ramadan Effendi, was accompanying them. Both sessions are documented in the video clip and this document.] They are coming from Ankara?

Ramazan Efendi: All of them are from Ankara. Are they your brothers, following you?

RE: Yes, all of them are your followers. Masha Allah!

RE: All of them are from Ankara, leaving tomorrow morning. Have they stayed for 3 days?

RE: 2 days No problem. We may charge their batteries. I may charge and Shaykh Mohamad Efendi (Mawlana's son) may seal it. It will work like this, don't worry.

RE: Insha Allah, one week after we leave, Shaykh Mehmet Efendi will be our guest.

Pray for us that we may serve him well. Now, he is like a dragon. His attraction and spiritual pull is strong. I am in zero level but he... But he is in his full strength. It is a dangerous time but his spiritual power is very high and very strong. That is why holy ones appointed him to Istanbul. He is a blessed one. May Allah bring out his light, give abundance to our lives, and to his life. May Allah dress him and you with grandeur, Fatiha. Huu

RE: May Allah keep us under your vision. Your existence gives us power, happiness,

and honor. Your existence makes us refreshed, makes the whole world refreshed. There are so many saints like dragons. With as many as there are, they may finish the whole world. When a saint looks [with his power], he may finish something off. There are so many saints like this. Now, there are 124,000 saints, representing 124,000 prophets. They are dragons. Don't say: “These unbelievers...”. If one of these saints looks at them, he can destroy all of them. Every day, seven Jinns would come to the presence of Sultan al Awliya [GrandShaykh Abdullah qas] in Sham [Damascus]. Seven kings from the Jinn would come and ask: “Do you have any orders for us?”. GrandShaykh would say: “Our orders are according to what comes from Up [Heavens]. If an order comes from up, then we may order you.” One of these kings from the Jinn can wipe out all that the unbelievers have established. But Shaykh Abdullah is waiting for that order that is to come from the Preserved Tablet. They are waiting for the order. There is power, but before it's time even the train doesn't move. The train cannot go without an order. They are waiting for the whistle before starting to move. [Mawlana takes a whistle out of his pocket.] Be careful! If I blow the whistle now, a jinn may come to us. They keep looking and waiting, but I don't pay attention. [Mawlana gestures as if toward a jinn]: “OK, go away!”. We also have Jinn here. We have a flag raised here which belongs to Grandshaykh. Because of that flag, they know the place and we have a detachment from the Jinns here, to protect this area. They can see the banner of Grandshaykh.

RE: And you are the commander … Who am I to command...? They are waiting as guards. May Allah also accept their service, make their ranks high. The same for you as well. Now instructions are coming for Ramazan Efendi. Three are appointed to control the situation in Ankara with our brothers in Ankara. No one will touch you or cause you any harm. When those three come to your area, they will bring as many as they want. May Allah brings out your lights. [Mawlana stands.] Ashadu An La ilaha il Allah, il Allah, il Allah. Wa ashadu anna Sayyidina Muhammadan abduhu wa Habibuhu wa Rasuluh, sallallahu alayhi wa sallam. Ya Rijal Allah (Men of ALLAH). Ya Sultan Al Awliya. Ya Mawlana, accept us. Oh our Lord, don't take us out from your protection. These brothers have been left out poor and confused. They have no one to protect themselves. We are asking your protection. Fatiha. Our mission is not the old days, for us to give an order and adjust the world. There is such a movement, in Rajab, Month of Allah. The coming Thursday, is the first day of Rajab. According to this, whoever wants may fast three months. Or, they may fast Monday and Thursdays. Or they may fast in the beginning, in the middle and at the end of the month. Power and light is coming down strong now. There are so many changes (to be done) in this year. Allah will have His order to be executed. We are weak servants and our wish is to become servants for our Lord, to be in the service of His Beloved (sas), and to continue keeping his tradition - nothing else. Even if you live a thousand years, million years, live billion years, or live longer and longer … The days that are numbered will come to an end. What is not numbered is here after (Akhira). Allahu Akbar! Allahu Akbar! Allah Hu! Allah Hu! Allah Hu! Allah Hu! Allah Hu! Allah Hu Haq! Allah Hu Allah Hu Allah Hu Haq! Allah Hu Allah Hu Allah Hu Haq! Hasbinallahu wa ni'mal wakil la hawla wa la quwwata Ya Rabbana, vaghfer lana Oh Allah Almighty, grant us from Your Mercy and give us from Your guidance. Grant us for the sake of Your Beloved Prophet (sas) for whose sake you created us. Fatiha. [Mawlana distributes bags of candy.] Give out these to the children. Give candy for the children. Very good. May Allah brings out your lights, may He open this Nakşibendi way for all people. May your physical and spiritual power increase. May your end be in a good way. May you gather under the flag of the Beloved (sas) and drink from the fountain of Kawthar. With the eternal, divine life may we reach constancy in the presence of Allah Almighty, pre-eternal up to eternal, may our end be in a good way. Fatiha. Masha Allah. I kiss the threshold of Haji Bayram-ı Veli's maqam (in Ankara). Insha Allah, he may look at you with a heavenly look. You may also give him my high respect, on behalf of me. Also, is the maqam of Imam Samarkandi (qs) close to you?

RE: Yes, it is close. I may also kiss the threshold of his tomb, we seek his support. We are here in a "strange, poor" situation. We are trying to do as much as possible with our murids coming here. Allah will grant Islam from east to west soon inshaAllah. Fatiha. Ok, now I give you permission (to leave). [Meeting with the second group from Ankara, Mevlana is telling a joke.]

… Three months after they got married, she brought a baby. She brought

a baby, and her husband got surprised.

He said: “It has been 3 months that we got married, how come we have a

baby?”

She said: “How many months since I took you? - 3 months. Add another 3

months that you took me - 6 months. Then add Rajab, Shaban,

Ramadhan. It is 9 months!"

I never heard such a calculation, really.

Now, this Ramazan (the leader of the murids) came before the Month of

Rajab. We never heard of Ramazan coming before Rajab. Welcome! [Mawlana stands up.] Allah Almighty, Subhanahu wa Taala, high respect to our Lord. La ilaha illa Allah, Sayydina Muhammad Rasul Allah. Sayyid al Awwalin wal Akhirin (sas) (Master of pre-eternal until eternity). Ya Rasul Allah, grant us from Your mercy and intercession. Ya Rabbi, send us those ones from Your servants who can bring power to Islam. May the ones who are attacking our religion be dismissed, kicked out. May we raise the flags of Islam. Oh our Lord, weak servants, we are weak servants, weak... “O My Lord, I am a weak servant. You make me stronger so that I can be in service. Ridaka [Allah's pleasure with His servant]." (Prophet's dua). We seek refuge in Allah. We are weak, but for the sake of being in Your service, don't leave us in this weakness. May you give power and strength to us, our bodies, so that we may always be at your service, to serve you day and night, for Your pleasure. May we walk in the way of Your Beloved (sas), Fatiha. OK, Now I may give bayat for everyone. Put hands on shoulders. Divine Hand is over their hand and Allah will grant blessings to the One who keeps his oath. Allah Hu Allah Hu Allah Hu Haq! Allah Hu Allah Hu Allah Hu Haq! May Allah keeps us firm and fix our feet on the true way for the sake of the one for whose sake You sent us here. Fatiha.

RE: We are asking for your dua. You are working for whose sake?

RE: We work for Allah. If you say this, what will I say? If you employ people (and run a business), do you pay them or leave the payment to me? Whoever works for the sake Allah, Allah will reward them. Whoever works for His Prophet (sas), He will reward them. Whoever works for saints, He will reward them. Restore and protect the tombs of the saints. Raise their flags. Light their candles. The ignorant ones who appeared lately don't accept the maqam of the saints or visiting their tombs. But, they have no mind. Now that I am viewing this [news]paper, I am getting angry. They ask: “Why do they light candles and lanterns in the mosques?” At least, when you get close to the maqams, you feel the fear of Allah, respect for the Prophet, respect for the saints. Mercy falls upon you. They don't say that, but they say this is "forbidden/superstition/bidah". The one who is appointed and in charge of Anatolia is my son, Shaykh Mehmet Efendi Hazretleri. [Certain] ones with spiritual power are spread throughout Anatolia. There are five great saints like this; he is one of them. He doesn't show it, but his power... His power is full. Masha Allah. He will collect all people. Follow him! Follow him! Keep his service. Don't be heedless. Don't be without ablution, if possible. If death comes to a person who has ablution, he passes with his iman/faith. May Allah grant you good manners. Be careful with your children. Treat your wives with generosity. Good character, morals is not to offend/hurt anyone. We must keep ourselves always in the balance. Every night, we should judge ourselves, our actions, what we have done in the last 24 hours. There are two tablets. On one of them, the names of people who will have paradise are written. On the second one, the names of people who will go to hell are written. Every night, think! Is my name firm and fixed on the list of people who will go to paradise? Is my name written on that list? Or, did I lose it? Pay attention! Think about these things. Good people are amongst the ones who will get paradise. Bad ones are from the ones who will go to hell. That is the measure. "He is a good one" [people say for the dead at the funeral]. There is no value to what people say in front of the coffin. If an answer comes from heavens, "That one is a good one", then this dead person may go to paradise. Otherwise, don't look at how people down here are saying, "He is a good one". Angels look to see if his name is written on the list of paradise people, or people of hell. If that list says: “We know him as a good one”, then angels take him to paradise. It doesn't matter if the people down here are saying: "We know him as a good one!" Allah Almighty will ask: “You say, 'we know him as a good one'; but did he pray? Did he fast? Would he recite Holy Qu'ran? Would he do good deeds? This person, would he smoke tobacco? This person, would he drink? This person, would he go out, wander out in the streets? These people would go to beach?”. He asks these questions. The man gets treated according to the answers. When people down here are saying, “We know him as a good one”, ALLAH Almighty may ask: “Then my servant, tell me what goodness he had? Would he pray? No! Then how do you say 'good' for this person?” An address comes from above: “How did you say 'good' for this person who doesn't pray? How did you say 'good' for the one who doesn't keep My order? This man drank, gambled, went out with bad women. He took My orders under his feet. How did you say here: 'We know this man as good”? This is an address from above, you understand? Be careful about this. Instead of saying: “I know him as a good one”, say: “May Allah forgive his sins”. When asked at funeral: “How do you know this person?”, say, “Oh our Lord, we are all sinners, forgive his sins as well. You know about his situation. We cannot witness for the situation of this person. We cannot be witness on Your Shariat for Your orders. All we want is for You to forgive him and grant us forgiveness!” What can we do? People who are in control, their heads may go down! They didn't guide us to the right path. They filled everywhere with bars, brothels, etc.. Oh our Lord, take these people from us! Have them leave, oh our Lord! They closed our mosques, closed our tombs. They removed Holy Qu'ran. Remove their bodies, oh our Lord! Don't be afraid. Wa min Allahu tawfiq, Fatiha. May Allah give you safety in your travel and security. May your way be open, and your visit be blessed.
