Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Thanks be to Allah that The Three Months are approaching in a few days. They start on Saturday insha’Allah which is also the first of Rajab. Rajab is a holy month, it’s the month of Allah. It is both the beginning of The Three Months and one of the Sacred Months (al-Ashhur al-Hurum), the respectable months in the sight of Allah. The Three Months are: Rajab, Shaʻban and Ramadan. Then there are the Sacred Months (al-Ashhur al-Hurum) which are Rajab, Dhul-Qa’dah, Dhul-Hijjah and Muharram. These are the Hajj months. Rajab is not one of the Hajj months but again it is a sacred month and it’s one of The Three Months. Because of its majesty and glory, it has been granted respect twice.

This month is a holy month. In the past when The Three Months started, people would increase worship and respect these months. They would be careful not to commit sins. Those who were addicted to sinning would stop what they were doing for the honor of these Three Months. They would say to themselves: “Let’s be patient for these Three Months and then we’ll continue afterwards.” For the honor of their respect for these months, Allah would make it a means for them to be guided.

Those who respect what Allah respects [of which are the Three Months], Allah will also respect them and help them. What they do would be acceptable. Allah states in the Holy Quran: ‘ م ظ ِّ َعُي نمَ وَ بِّ وُلُقل ا ىوَ ق َت نمِّ اهَ َنِّإَف ِّ اللَّ رَ ِّئاَعشَ ’, ‘And whoever honors the symbols of Allah - indeed, it is from the piety of hearts.’ (Qur’ān 22:32).

To respect and honor time, months, places and people. What we mean by people is those honored people whom Allah has honored. Beginning with our Holy Prophet, then the Prophets, the Companions, the Saints (Awliya) and the True Scholars. And since they are honored people whom Allah has honored, if we honor them, Allah would give us huge rewards and He would strengthen our faith. May it be Mubarak (blessed) insha’Allah. There are certain duties in Rajab that I will mention later insha’Allah, may Allah make us reach it. May Allah make us reach the holy days.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq. Al-Fatiha.
