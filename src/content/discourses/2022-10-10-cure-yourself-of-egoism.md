A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Egoism is one of the worst attributes. It is a disease of the ego. Egoism means selfishness; to say, “I am so and so. I am this and that,” so that everyone loves them and praises them. There are many people who think like that. It is a disliked characteristic from the diseases of the ego. Usually, such a person is not liked by people. But in the recent times, this characteristic has spread among people. It has become much more common. Because everyone wants to praise himself. To be the most famous one, to be listened to and heard – this characteristic is their favourite.

In old times, if someone said, “I am this, I am that,” it might have reached a couple of people in the household. But now there is this tool with which people show their skills everywhere, all over the world, so that other people see them as someone very important and flatter them. This is the concern of all people now: “I want everyone to like me. I want them to imitate me. I want to be famous. I want to be so and so.” What is the benefit of their love to you? If they don’t love you for the sake of AllāhNone, there is benefit neither to you, nor to them. There is nothing but loss. What people think has no benefit, if they are not on the way of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Their thoughts have no value. Whole world may like you, if you want. But as long as AllāhNone doesn’t like you, your work is not good, your way is not good.

You should change yourself so that Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla loves you. If you are on the way of AllāhNone, HeNone will like you. Otherwise, you can somersault as much as you want. They are doing all kinds of foolery and shame. They are showing ridiculuous things so that people like them. What they are doing is useless, unnecessary. It is a waste of their time. If they do something bad, they lead both themselves and the people who follow them and imitate them into sin and harm. These problems... People of old times were better people. They used to live with adab. They would not show what they ate, thinking about the poor ones. Now they want to show everything – what they eat and drink, where they come and go. There are all kinds of shame.

May AllāhNone correct them. What is done is contrary to the religion. Even if it is not contradictory, it doesn’t suit it. The religion is adab and discipline. These things that are done are at odds with it. Therefore, it is not nice to do something because someone else did it. If he does something, let him do it. We should imitate good people, not him. If you imitate a good person, you will have benefit. If you deal with bad ones and useless things, you will have wasted your time and life. Even if there is no sin, you will have wasted your life without earning anything. May AllāhNone protect us. May AllāhNone give understanding to us and these people, for who have a mind don’t do such things.

To be egoist, it is not a good attribute. People also, they don’t like egoist one. Because, they thought everybody must be happy with him, everybody must accept him, everyone must like him, everybody must... praise him and everything going around himself. Whole world in himself – this is egoistic, which, it is not good attribute in Islām. And it is one of illness of your ego which must, to not do it. To must, illness, you must cure yourself from this illness, which it is, nowadays, it is more than before. Before, there was people, they have different thinking, different good behaviour they have from before. Now, everything going upside down – changing. Which it is a shame to do something in old time – old time, what we do, we not [did] for a thousand years. Maybe for thirty years or twenty years now it is become fashion to do this. So, to be following this fashion, it is no benefit for you.

Especially now, they have this machine – everybody in his pocket it is – everywhere looking for people who doing something they thought funny or interesting, people, to make them to happy with them. This is to make their ego satisfied. It is not many of them – most of this thing, no benefit. Maybe there is harm also from this, what they do. But people, all world, before it was maybe some part of world, now everybody he has this in his pocket, in his hand. And looking around the whole world, making people to be... their most important thing for them, to make people to like them. Not to be happy with them – of course you can’t do – doing such nonsense thing, and they thought they are doing good. Other people also, looking. “We must imitate this people, they have... People like them. We must also do this.” This is not a good way for humans.

Before, old time people, they was, have good behave. Not, they have good behave, but now, this all good behave they are forgotten, and it is not important for people. But, for believer, for muslim, it is very important to not waste your time with nonsense. Even it is not a sin or not harming, even with this, you can finish your life without doing anything, and you kill your time, and your life how going with nothing. Our life is very precious, our time is very precious. Don’t waste with this nonsense thing. Every time, remember Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, remember Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam and awliyā’u Llāh, for what you done, what you will do for them.

This is important thing, not other thing. There is many kind of thing shayṭān can make people, to cheat people, to forget the time. After when they looking time, “Ooh, it is nearly midnight.” Some of them, after midnight sleeping. Some of them, even for morning with this nonsense thing, looking, looking, looking and after, they not praying even. And they saying, “We must be like this.” What? This is not good thing. You must imitate good thing. Which is good thing? Must imitate Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam and ṣaḥābah and awliyā’u Llāh. And this is benefit for us. Other thing, no benefit, no any good thing. AllāhNone give us, all of us, good thinking to not follow this. To follow good thing in shā’a Llāh. Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
