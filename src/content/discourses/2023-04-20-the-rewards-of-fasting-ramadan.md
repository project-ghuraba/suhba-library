As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh. A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh for these favours. Thanks and praise to AllāhNone for the favours that HeNone gives. We have reached the last day of the holy month of Ramaḍān. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla will give great rewards tonight to those believers who spent this Ramaḍān fasting, making i‘tikāf, giving zakāh and performing their prayers. HeNone says, "I know the amount. Only INone know. No one else knows how much INone will give." Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is generous. What HeNone gives is countless and unlimited. May it be a blessing and great good tidings for people and believers who appreciate this beautiful month, who spend it as AllāhNone wants and as AllāhNone loves.

Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam has said and promised great rewards at the end of this month. It is the word of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. HisNone word is true. Therefore, may it be blessed for everyone. Because it is such a holy month that a person who doesn't fast on purpose loses a lot of things. People will regret it later. They will want to make it up. It is okay to make it up, but when they make it up, because they purposely didn't fast, they have to fast sixty days kaffārah and after that they can start fasting their missed days.

For all missed days, people can make them up slowly. But there is a “but” here as well. They cannot attain the same virtue. Even if they make kaffārah and fast their missed days, our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says they cannot attain the same virtue. Even if they fast all their lives, if they break it only on ‘Īd and continue to fast on the other days for years, they cannot attain the virtue of that one day [they missed]. It is so valuable. People are missing it. They are wasting it without caring. But if AllāhNone shows them guidance, it is better that they make it up but they will not get the precious things and rewards they missed. May AllāhNone protect us all. May AllāhNone help us protect the obligations (farḍ) ordered by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Farḍ is the most important thing. Certainly, you cannot have it when you miss sunnah as well. But farḍ is most precious. Therefore, we must not miss our prayers. We shouldn't delay making them up. This is an important matter. People must certainly be careful about them.

Shukr to AllāhNone, tonight is the eve of ‘Īd. It is one of the virtuous nights. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gave us Ramaḍān, and HeNone gave us the last day, the eve of ‘Īd. It is among the holy nights like Laylatu l-Qadr, Laylatu l-Mi‘rāj, Laylatu l-Barā’ah. Just like those nights, the eve of ‘Īd is also blessed and Jum‘ah is blessed. This time they have come together. There will be more barakah, in shā’a Llāh. May AllāhNone give you barakah. May AllāhNone make all of us steadfast on the way of truth. May others have the means of guidance. May AllāhNone give guidance to them so that they do not miss these virtues, in shā’a Llāh May it be blessed for us all, in shā’a Llāh.

Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam say, "AllāhNone, HeNone giving good tiding for ummah who are, they are make this month of Ramaḍān and doing what AllāhNone, HeNone like to do, as fasting day time, praying nighttime and doing charity and doing i‘tikāf or what all this." AllāhNone will reward this night with HisNone countless blessing. HeNone said, INone, ‘‘Aṣ-ṣawmu lī, wa-anā ajzī bih.” "Fasting [is] for MeNone and INone will give what INone want." And HeNone is generous, HeNone will be, in shā’a Llāh, without counting. You will be surprised when you see what you get in for this. AllāhNone reward you in this month. In ākhirah you will be really pleased, shocked. Unbelievable what AllāhNone give you, for mu’min, for Muslim who know value of this month. And al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are, even without seeing anything, we feel very good, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, from this blessing month which it is for ummah of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, it given. And really it is very blessed thing.

Many people they don’t know, most of people and what, they – people who giving value for this month, very few. But other, they are not giving value, they are going... AllāhNone give hidāyah for them also we saying, because they miss so many thing. Even many people, before, they can be not give value for this month, but after they are giving value and beginning to fast. But when you, if you miss one day from Ramaḍān, you must make it again, after. You must fast one day for each day you missed. But, if you are knowing and insisting, but without knowing it is maybe AllāhNone ‘afuww. But when, if you know, first you must fast sixty days for as before making this fast what you missed. One day, five days, ten days one month, ten months, twenty months. Each day, not for each day sixty days. Only first one, sixty days. After, anytime, slowly slowly you must make it, because this is obligatory for you.

You have to give it back from your side for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. But it is also good for who can do, AllāhNone give him hidāyah and give, become, making to finish what he not fast, to fast them. But also Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, they cannot reach the value of what he who fast in its time of Ramaḍān. Every time, the time of fasting, even for one day Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying: “If you fast whole your life without cutting, years and years, it is not value for one day blessed, this what you missed.” So, but also, if they even they know they cannot reach this value, but must make it again. Because, it is in ākhirah more difficult to make it. AllāhNone give hidāyah for other people also and give you from HisNone endless treasure, in shā’a Llāh.

And the end of this month also, this night of ‘Īd, the first of Shawwāl, it is also blessed night. Like Laylatu l-Qadr, like Isrā’ wa-l-Mi‘rāj, this night also blessed night. AllāhNone accept du‘ā’ and reward you more and more, in shā’a Llāh. And night of Friday also, same like this. This year coming all – ‘Īd and Friday together. It will be more blessed, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone bless you and AllāhNone make us to be with Sayyidunā l-Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām, in shā’a Llāh. We hope, in shā’a Llāh. Every time we are in need for him. Today, before tomorrow. Ummah it is in need for him, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone make him to come quickly, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
