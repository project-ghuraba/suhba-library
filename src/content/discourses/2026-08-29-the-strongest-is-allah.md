As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Mankind desires to be powerful. They want to be the most powerful. If there is a powerful state, mankind seeks help from it. This is human nature. They think that if they are powerful, they will protect themselves. They don’t consider the Most Powerful. The Most Powerful is Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. Because power is with Him. What He wills happens, and what He does not will does not happen.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says,<br>
وَمَا ذَٰلِكَ عَلَى اللَّهِ بِعَزِيزٍ
<br>“Wa mā dhālika ʿalā Llāhi bi-ʿazīz”<br>
“And that is not difficult for Allāh.” (Qurʼān 14:20). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

Nothing is difficult for Allah, nothing can stand against Him. Even if the whole world, governments, people, and all of creation were to oppose Him, it would be nothing; it’s not even air in comparison to the majesty and power of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla.

Therefore, if you, a person of intellect, wish to be on the side of strength, be on the side of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. Do not oppose Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. He shows us things every day. Those who oppose Allah were destroyed in the past, and they will be destroyed again. They will never win against Allah. It is impossible.

A person who deceives himself by clinging to someone else and thinks he will be saved will not be saved. You must be with Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla to be saved. There is no other way to be saved. This applies in this world and in the hereafter; there is nowhere to run. Where will you run? No matter what hole you hide in, you will not escape Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. No matter how high you climb a mountain, you will not escape. No matter how many weapons you hold, you will not escape Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. This is salvation. Salvation is fearing Allah and being with Allah; nothing else.

Because shayṭān deceives people and nations, they think, “We’ve made so many tools and machines, we’ve done this and that,” but let alone that, they’ve made machines more intelligent than us.

Therefore, those who think, “Nothing will happen to us,” are making the biggest mistake. They are making the biggest wrong. Because those tools will neither give you life, nor soul, nor save you. When it comes to the will of Allah, it sweeps them all away. It leaves nothing behind. Neither the intelligent, nor the insane, nor the rebellious, nor the oppressor, nor the oppressed, none of them have any value in the face of the Power of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla.

Therefore, we say to all people: Turn to Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. It is Allah who creates from nothing. It is Allah who creates, not your father or mother. Some are very foolish people. If someone gets angry at their parents they say, “Why did you bring me into this world?” then, God forbid, they’re like a donkey, and by donkey we mean those four-legged creatures we know.

So, you came into this world by the will of Allah. Your soul, your body, everything came into being by the command of Allah. To utter such senseless words only brings more sorrow and distress upon yourself.

Therefore, it’s necessary to understand this. We are all with Allah. We must pray that Allah will place us in His loving servants. May He not make us among those who oppose Him, may He keep us away from them. To even make a comparison with them is a sin, an act of disrespect against the majesty of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla.

A few people openly do this wrong act. They oppose Allah, acting tough, saying, “I’m an atheist, I’m this, I’m that.” At the slightest thing, if they feel death, they defecate themselves out of fear. There’s such stupid, so many unbalanced, mindless people. May Allah not make us like them, and may He guide them. May they not disobey Allah. Allah’s wrath is great. Nothing can stand against it. No force can stand against it. May Allah protect us. May we always be with Him, inshā’Allāh.

May He give us strength, spiritual strength, and peace, along with strength from Him, and may we be a means of guidance for people, inshā’Allāh.
