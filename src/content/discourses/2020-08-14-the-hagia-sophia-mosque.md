Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh, Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem, Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin, Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina, Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad. Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya. Thank Allah ﷻ, we visited the Hagia Sophia Mosque yesterday and prayed the Dhuhr prayer there. May Allah ﷻ bless the president for this great service. He did such a service that Allah ﷻ gave him courage. May Allah ﷻ protect him, and may the goodness continue. Thank Allah ﷻ there are many services, but this was one of the biggest and most beautiful ones. These services are also a great goodness to the Islamic world, because the world of disbelief had set the Islamic world as an abasement. This humiliation has been lifted insha’Allah. Those who say something against this are ungrateful. Thank Allah ﷻ, the whole Islamic world knows this, but people even show goodness as evil for their own benefit. Therefore, may Allah ﷻ not make us from the ungrateful ones, but from the good ones insha’Allah. May Allah ﷻ grant Islam many of such victories and save us from humiliation insha’Allah. May Allah ﷻ bless our Friday. Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
