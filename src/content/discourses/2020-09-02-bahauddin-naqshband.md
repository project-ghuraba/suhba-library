Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

A’ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm:

(Qur’ān 10:62). Allah ﷻ states in the Holy Qur’an: ‘Verily on the friends of Allah there is no fear, nor shall they grieve.’ The Awliya of Allah ﷻ are the beloved ones of Allah ﷻ, the servants He loves. They are people who carry the light of our Holy Prophet ﷺ to people. They carry it in a live way, not soulless like the scholars who read from the books. When you become a scholar, there must be both spirituality and knowledge, otherwise it will not be beneficial. Thank Allah ﷻ, yesterday was the birthday of Shaykh Bahauddin Naqshband ق, one of our great Mashayikh in the Tariqa. It is the 14th day of Muharram, after Ashura. It is a means to remember them, to reach them and to take from their blessings. When we remember them, they are there and their support reaches us insha’Allah.

Shaykh Bahauddin Naqshband ق is a great saint and the Imam of our Tariqa. He was born on this day. It has been nearly eight hundred years, and his name continues to be mentioned. Millions and billions of people follow his beautiful Tariqa. Indeed, it is the pure Tariqa of Ahl as-Sunnah wal Jama’ah. It shows the way of our Holy Prophet ﷺ. It even shows the smallest Sunnah of our Holy Prophet ﷺ. He is a holy person. May Allah ﷻ sanctify his secret. May Allah ﷻ have mercy on us for his blessing insha’Allah.

These days when you say Tariqa, people don’t understand. The way of Shaykh Bahauddin Naqshband ق is a pure way. Whoever objects it is foolish and faithless. That way is the way of light. It is the way that leads to Allah ﷻ. The way that leads you to Allah ﷻ is a beautiful way. May Allah ﷻ bless it. May Allah ﷻ grant us from his Himmah and blessings. May it be a blessed day for us. Al-Fatiha to his soul, to the soul of all Mashayikh and to the soul of our Holy Prophet ﷺ.
