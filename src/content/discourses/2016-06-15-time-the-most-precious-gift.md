A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alá Rasūlina Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūl Allāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh ad-Dāghistāni, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqāni madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

You should know the value of Allāh's blessings. The biggest blessing is the time we live in. We have to appreciate it. Now this moment passed we can't take it back. It passed, nothing can be done. But Allāh granted to Muslims, although you can't go back and change what you did, but you can repent for your sins and they will be erased. Instead it will be written as a good deed. This is also a big grant. Because past is gone, the present moment is important. We have to appreciate it. We have to think about our past sins. We have to repent and use this present moment in a good way.

When we say good, it is not to forget Allāh. "Fa-udhkurūnī adhkurkum" (2:152) - remembering Allāh, praising Allāh. Dhikr, of course lafẓ al-jalālah (Allāh) is also dhikr but in fact remembering is dhikr. Keep Allāh always on your mind, you'll be in constant dhikr. Tomorrow is the third. Yesterday, today, tomorrow they say this world is three days. Allāh knows what will happen tomorrow. Nobody has a guarantee. Nobody has a guarantee to see tomorrow but it is important to use the present moment in a good way, useful way.

Shayṭān makes people forget. Let's spend time. People are bored. They say; "We are bored, don't know what to do." They aren't aware that it passes quickly. Time passes, they aren't aware of it. They want it to pass even faster. We shouldn't use this blessing that Allāh gave us in a bad way. Don't say "I'm bored." Allāh gave you a big favour. If you say "I'm bored." You aren't thankful for Allāh's blessing. You are being ungrateful. Beware. Time isn't for getting bored. It is for obeying Allāh. Your ego might be bored. Even if the ego is bored say to it: "you get bored, I'm not bored. I'm remembering Allāh."

A little bit of time is our treasure. We should value it and put it to good use. If you aren't using it for bad deeds, every minute, every second is written as a good deed. So let's not forget Allāh. Let's be with Allāh. Let's Thank Allāh.

Thanks to Allāh it is a beautiful time. It is Ramaḍān. Allāh (awj), created us and He gives us many favors. But people they are not knowing, not feeling, not knowing what is this favor. It is big favor for us. We are living in this time we must appreciate this. Because time, three days - yesterday, today, tomorrow. What you've done yesterday you cannot go back and change it. Don't look for yesterday. You must look for this time, what you are living. Tomorrow it is unknown. We don't know what will happen tomorrow. We know what happened yesterday and what we can do now in this time. But we've done yesterday, if we've done wrong thing we can... Allāh give us a chance to change it. You may repent and Allāh forgive you and gives you a good thing, rewarding you for what you repent and you make tawbah but what you've done for wrong, for dunyā of course you cannot change it.

But now time, you must be happy and to do what Allāh saying. Remember Allāh. Allāh gives you the most precious thing - time. Time it is life. Many people they are saying, "I'm bored I don't know what to do." This is ignorant saying. It is your life running, you are not feeling it is running. Some people (say) "I want to kill my time to go these days quickly." It is not good idea. It is idiot idea. You have certain years, certain time in this life. And this, it is Allāh's gift. Because He give for each people, certain breath, certain food to eat, to drink. When finish this, maybe 10 years, you can die. 20 years you can die, 70, 40, 60, something like this, you can die but it is....You cannot do anything, because it is from Allāh.

So when you are in life, every time you must be happy. Allāh make me, give me one more day to live to live and to remember Him, and to obey for Him, to be good believer and to try to be His beloved one. This is, when people thinking for this, they cannot say "I worry, I'm fed up, I don't know what to do with this life." The people who are saying this at the end most of them they can suicide themselves, or to do wrong thing because not remembering Creator, only remembering themselves, their ego. The main aim for them is to be make their ego happy and ego never be happy, never be satisfied.

You must... you must be thankful for Allāh we are this, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, this time also we reached to Ramaḍān and we are in place, good place. Allāh make us happy here and hereafter. Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah
