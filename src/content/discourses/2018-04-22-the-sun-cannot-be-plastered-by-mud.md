Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Thank Allah ﷻ that we are on this path. Such beautiful paths are the best gift for those who are believers. Not everyone can follow them. It is not the destiny of everyone. This is the gift of Allah ﷻ. We say thank Allah ﷻ, because in gratefulness there is plentitude in favors. The greatest favor is faith, the favor of Islam, being on the path of our Holy Prophet ﷺ, and [with] the Mashayikh being one’s Murshid (guide).

Thank Allah ﷻ that our Murshid showed us these beautiful paths and made us love them. Yesterday was Mawlana Shaykh Nazim’s birthday, and his remembrance also. It would not be wrong to say that there are thousands, tens of thousands, hundreds of thousands of people who have been guided through him. He sacrificed his life to make others believe in Allah ﷻ, and make Him known [to them]. Until his last breath, he showed the path of Allah ﷻ to people. This is a beautiful memory. Many, whether they followed him or not, loved him and talked about him.

Of course, the worst enemy of our Holy Prophet ﷺ is shaytan, and those who follow him ﷺ also become the shaytan’s enemies. That’s why shaytan would want to deface them, and make them look bad. They say that the sun cannot be plastered by mud. It is exactly like that. They are the sun, light. Others can bark as much as they like, it has no effect. They harm themselves. They [the Prophet’s ﷺ followers] are those whom Allah ﷻ loves. In addition to the Qur’an, Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla says in the Hadith Qudsi: “ بِ رْ حَ لْاِب ُهتنَْذآ دقْ فَ اًّيلِوَ يلِ ىدَ اعَ نْ مَ ”, “Whosoever shows enmity to a Wali of Mine, then I have declared war against him.”

These stupid people do not show respect to our Holy Prophet ﷺ and to those Awliya who are on his ﷺ path. They talk lies behind their backs and slander them, thinking they would gain something. Their calculations are wrong. They are not doing the right thing. They are not doing good to themselves at first. Then, those who listen to these disgraced people and those who believe them will also suffer.

They have Allah ﷻ to protect them. They are not in need of anyone’s protection. With the permission of Allah ﷻ, they will always remain as light until the Day of Judgment. Neither those disgraceful ones nor any other ones can defame them. This thing returns to them; they regret it, but this regret does not help them.

We should show respect to these people, because their purpose is not worldly. If they had wanted this world, they could have been its leaders. But they did not value this world, rather they valued Allah ﷻ, and Allah ﷻ valued them. Whoever values Allah ﷻ, Allah ﷻ values him. Those who value this world have no regard. May Allah ﷻ let us all know their value insha’Allah. We live by means of their blessing. By their blessing, their mercy and their intercession, we enter paradise insha’Allah. May we be together with them insha’Allah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
