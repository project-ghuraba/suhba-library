As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

May this ‘Īd be blessed for all of us. May AllāhNone accept our worship. May we all attain to the pleasure of AllāhNone, in shā’a Llāh. ‘Īd Mubārak. May AllāhNone protect the Muslim world. May they be on the right way, in shā’a Llāh.

This is a ḥadīth sharīf of our Holy ProphetNone. We read it before. This is the most virtuous day of the year in the presence of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. "Yawmu n-Naḥr wa-Yawmu l-Qarr." "Yawmu n-Naḥr" means today is the day of qurbān (sacrifice). The tenth of Dhū l-Ḥijjah, the first day of ‘Īdu l-Aḍḥá is the best day in the presence of AllāhNone. When it is the best day in the presence of AllāhNone, it is the best day of our Holy ProphetNone, Muslims, and all believers. Certainly, there are good nights. But regarding the days, our Holy ProphetNone is saying that the tenth of Dhū l-Ḥijjah is the most virtuous day. Because many things both farḍ and wājib are to be done on this day. Because of these, it is the holiest day in terms of time and space.

What does a holy day mean? It is happiness of the soul. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gives happiness to people's souls, and this peace continues until ākhirah and lasts forever. There are people who think it is for this world. They are doing everything. In order to make themselves happy, people do many things. They do things in order to be happy and think it will make them happy. That is instant happiness – for a minute or an hour. It feels like some happiness or some joy but it doesn't last. You cannot find that happiness after an hour or after a day. If you try to find it, you will repeat the same things and it will disappear in the same manner. It will not last. You may do everything that makes you happy in this world, or do things that satisfy your ego. After an hour or a day, after a month, you will say, "I did so many things. There is no benefit, and it didn't last." It is done in that hour and remains there. That is something that has no benefit for the future.

Things that don't bring and don't include the pleasure of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla bring hardship afterwards and things become worse. Even if they try to do it again, it is something that won't last. Therefore, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is saying, "Fa-bi-dhālika fa-l-yafraḥū huwa khayrun mimmā yajma‘ūn" (10:58). "Let them rejoice with the good deeds they do." If they do goodness and carry out the orders of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, the mercy of AllāhNone will be upon them. And they will be happy for it. This happiness is not temporary. It is eternal. When you die, it will go with you. It will be with you on Judgment Day. It will be with you in paradise. That happiness will be with you forever. As we said, the happiness of this world...

Even Qārūn who was so rich and wealthy, who had everything, but it didn't bring him any profit. This is a big example. A smaller example is that people nowadays say they want to 'live'. "We want to live." What they mean by 'living' is that they want to satisfy their egos – not to pray, not to fast, not to do anything. When they don't do these things, they think that is 'living'. They think they are 'living' when they do all kinds of stupid things. But as we said, it has no benefit for them. That kind of peace, joy, and happiness disappears after an hour. Therefore, for people who want happiness – you get happy today, on these holy days. When you are happy, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gives more happiness and joy. May AllāhNone bless these days. May we distinguish the good from the bad, in shā’a Llāh. What is good is permanent. What is not good is temporary and like a mirage. Like people who see a mirage in the desert, people get happy with their worldly life in a similar way. It has no benefit. And the end is disappointing.

May AllāhNone bless these days. May AllāhNone give peace to all of us. May we have barakah. May HeNone protect us from all kinds of badness, in shā’a Llāh. May HeNone help Islām. There are Muslims in hardship. May AllāhNone increase their ranks. We say that is their fate. Because they are patient people. Even in such distress, they praise AllahNone, saying it came from AllahNone. They are content. May AllāhNone help them, in shā’a Llāh.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are, first of all, make ‘Īd Mubārak for all of you. ‘Īdu l-Aḍḥá, this feast of ten of Dhū l-Ḥijjah, it is Yawmu n-Naḥr. Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam praise this day and heNone is saying it is the best day for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla give for ummah: "Yawmu n-Naḥr thumma Yawmu l-Qarr." Yawmu l-Qarr meaning day after – eleven of Dhū l-Ḥijjah. In Islām, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, subḥāna Llāh, HeNone choose the language of Arabic language and, even they are living in desert, nothing around them, but everything they have name. This from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Miracle from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla to see, show people every day they have name. Every hour in day also, twenty-four hour, each hour they have a special name. So for this time, day – Yawmu n-Naḥr and Yawmu l-Qarr and after all every day until... they have also name for this.

So best day for AllāhNone, in Divine Presence of AllāhNone, this day. Of course, there is holy nights but for day, they, HeNone said for this Dhū l-Ḥijjah, ten of Dhū l-Ḥijjah, this is best day. Because in this day many thing changing in life of people or many thing they do only in this days. First ḥajj, of course, we say this. And second to sacrifice sheep or cow or anything for sake of AllāhNone and to win from reward of AllāhNone for each thing. And Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, when cut sheep, from first blood, drop of blood, AllāhNone forgive you. And for each hair of sheep or goat or cow, AllāhNone reward you – for, ten reward for each one. You can imagine, maybe millions of hair they have, for each then... It is big reward, big pleasure for our spiritual life. Because life, we have now people they have, they in this time... Before they was trying to survive to some, find something to do for looking for family, for themself. They was more active, more busy, not lazy. They was hard working. So that time people, their ego no time to be ask for other thing. But now day by day, day by day, everything become more luxury. More people, they not used to do work. They only lazy and with this laziness, what they said, "We like to live, live in this life – not to be sitting." To 'live' for them, meaning – to get enjoyment, to be happy, to be, to do something they thought they will be happy for this. And what they ask for happiness? They have everything and they are enjoying themselves. So they said, "We are happy, we are 'living', we have enjoyment, everything." But this enjoyment, it is not continuous, just in that time. You enjoy yourself, you finish this. After ten minutes, nothing. After half an hour, you forget. After one day... So they said, "We can, we must do this [again]." Whole life, if they are doing, enjoying themselves, at the end there is nothing in their hand. No, only air, empty. Nothing. No continuous. What is this enjoyment? Whole life you running after your ego, you doing what ego like and you are not happy at the end. "We, I do all this. I have car, I have woman, I have... everything I have." "Eh, okay but now you are happy?" No. Because it is not continuous, finish. Not continue.

The real enjoyment [is] with Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. HeNone said for like these days, to be happy in these days – this happiness going forever, not only for that time. This enjoyment for until you die, after you die, after you AllāhNone make you in paradise still continue forever, not cutting. Not like other, what the shayṭān showing people it is good, but it is now continuous, only every time you must do what your ego like. After, also finish. Doing, after finish. No any happiness, no any enjoyment with what you do if you not continue, if... And it is impossible to continue whole time like this. Maybe whole life you continue this, time by time, but no continuous, no benefit, only bad end.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, for this we are happy with what AllāhNone gave us and AllāhNone order us. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥim. "Fa-bi-dhālika fa-l-yafraḥū huwa khayrun mimmā yajma‘ūn" (10:58). Meaning, ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘aẓīm. AllāhNone in Qur'ān: "You must be happy, be happy with this what AllāhNone gave you and it is better than what they collect." What they thinking it is good for them – it is much better [than that], billion times better from what they are happy. You are, to be in way of AllāhNone, AllāhNone happy with you. And we are asking AllāhNone to make us happy, real happiness, this happiness to be with us also. For us, for whole Muslim, especially for who are suffering whole around the world. Muslim, many Muslim, they are suffering from fighting, war, and oppression. They are suffering but, subḥāna Llāh, their reward it is more than us, billions times. Because Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, saying, to be waiting in your place when you have oppression, AllāhNone reward you as you are in one, one hour or one day, I exactly not knowing... You not fighting, just to be there and to carrying this with AllāhNone Will. AllāhNone will reward you as you are seventy year worshipping in Ka‘bah. You making like ḥajj it will be like this for this people. For this, AllāhNone give them patience and they are patient and they are more dependent on AllāhNone from ourself even, from many, more than many people, or Muslim people. For this, what AllāhNone He want, don't be asking – "Why this? What? How this?" All this, AllāhNone give their reward and give them HisNone pleasure. AllāhNone help us and help them. Send for us Ṣāḥib to protect us, in shā’a Llāh, from every badness.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
