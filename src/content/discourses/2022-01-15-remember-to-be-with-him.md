As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is saying, A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Alā bi-dhikri Llāhi taṭma’innu l-qulūb" (13:28). Hearts find peace and contentment only by remembering AllāhNone, says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. People are trying to find contentment and peace in this world. They are trying to make their hearts content. That only comes with the remembrance of AllāhNone. Hearts are content with the remembrance of AllāhNone.

People try every imaginable thing in order to make their hearts content and at peace. They are trying so many different things. But nothing helps. They gain nothing from these things. They practice meditation and yoga. There are thousands of different sorts of nonsense things in this world. "If you do this, you will find peace." Who has tried it and found contentment? If you ask, you don't get a straight answer. They claim what they have done works. It doesn't work. It absolutely doesn't. They claim that because, in trying out this practice, they paid so much money and they suffered so much. So they lie to themselves that their hearts are content and at peace.

It is not your heart. Only your ego is at peace. It has nothing to do with the heart. The heart is different. The heart is the spirit. And the ego has no relation to spirituality. It should be trained. It is trained through the dhikr of AllāhNone, with the remembrance of AllāhNone. Dhikr means to remember AllāhNone. By saying "Allāh Allāh", people remember AllāhNone. At first, they remember only with the tongue – by saying it. And then this dhikr goes into the heart. Otherwise, if you stand in front of a statue and try to imitate him, you cannot attain anything. It only brings more discomfort and disturbance to you. Many people have ruined their lives by imitating the idols in front of them. They have nothing to offer.

People, especially Muslims, should remember AllāhNone. They should make dhikr of AllāhNone. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, our Creator, is telling us how our hearts work, and what HisNone creation needs to function. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, who created you, is telling you what HisNone creation needs. If you are a car, you need fuel. If your car uses diesel, you put diesel in it. If you put water in it instead, it won't do any good. Therefore, the dhikr of AllāhNone is of inestimable benefit to us. It is a great beauty. Even when you make the daily tasbīḥ, when you remember AllāhNone in that way, it is dhikr. When you make "Allāh Allāh" by heart, it brings contentment to your heart, and your work goes well. You will be at peace. AllāhNone is with you then.

There is a ḥadīth qudsī: "Ana jalīsu man dhakaranī." "INone am with the one who remembers MeNone. INone am his friend, and INone sit with him," says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Certainly, HeNone is not bound by the limits of space. It only means that AllāhNone is close to those who remember HimNone. HeNone is their greatest friend. And it is the most beautiful thing when AllāhNone is with you, and not against you.

May AllāhNone make HisNone dhikr continuous in our hearts and in every atom of our being, in shā’a Llāh.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying, Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Alā bi-dhikri Llāhi taṭma’innu l-qulūb" (13:28). Heart, only with mentioning or remembering AllāhNone, become satisfied. No other thing. Because people, they are looking for many thing to satisfy themself. They cheating themself, "If I do this, it will be – I will be happy. I be satisfied, my heart it will be soft and happy. Inside me it will be peace, and it will be like this, like that." They are just cheating people.

Sitting in front of statue, doing meditation, doing yoga, doing many thing they have now. This crook people, they invent many thing and they call people for this; and people, they are cheated. And when you ask them, if you – they have satisfied? "Yes, yes." Why they say this? Because they pay much money for this crook people and they cannot say, it is we cheated. No, it is real like this. When some crook people giving something, it is nice look. "Yes, yes, very nice." So this people, like this. Not no for heart anything can get satisfied, or peace for heart. Only remembering of AllāhNone and to mention HimNone.

This is what they can – your heart, because heart, place of spiritual. But what they are happy – not heart, not spiritual, only they make satisfy their ego. We do this, hours we stay in front of this statue, worshipping them and try to do hours without moving, to only make meditation. It is all for ego – not for spiritual, not for heart. They mixing between heart and between their ego. Even ego never be satisfied if they do what they do. It will be not benefit for them. Only benefit for people, for Muslim, non-Muslim, to mention their Lord Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. To remember HimNone, everything, every time remembering HimNone. And make – when you make dhikr by your heart, "AllāhNone, AllāhNone" Later it come to – coming inside your heart. And you feel every time, every minute, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.

Because Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying, "Ana jalīsu man dhakaranī." "INone am sitting with who is mentioning MeNone." Ya‘nī, like friend, like who sitting with people; but of course Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, no place, no time for HimNone. This another thing – but it is HeNone... HeNone is with love this people, and HeNone will be with them. And to be with AllāhNone, it is biggest benefit for us: AllāhNone with us, AllāhNone not against us. This is our main aim in our life. You can do everything – but this aim, we must not forget. Everything for HimNone. What we are eating, drinking, going, coming; no problem, because HeNone create us to live in this world. But the important thing: to put HimNone in your heart, not forget HimNone any minute, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone make us to... mentioning, to remember to be with HimNone, every second, every minute, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
