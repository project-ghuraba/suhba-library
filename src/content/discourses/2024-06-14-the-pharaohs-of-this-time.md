As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Today is such a blessed day. AllāhNone calls it Yawmu t-Tarwiyah. Not tarbiyah, but Tarwiyah – the day before Yawmu ‘Arafah. These are holy days, shukr to AllāhNone. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gave them to Ummati Muḥammad for us to earn more rewards. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gave us these days in order to give us more gifts. We must appreciate these days.

Tomorrow is Yawmu ‘Arafah. Certainly, ḥajjīs have started going up the mountain of ‘Arafāt today. And tomorrow, people present there will carry out their duty of ḥajj. They will carry out this order which was made obligatory, farḍ, by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla – those who can. Many people cannot, of course. For those who can, this burden will be lifted.

Because farḍ is a big debt. It is a big burden for people. Whoever can, should do it immediately. They must not delay it for later, because it will be more difficult to be deprived of its barakah due to the fault or mistake of not doing it. Therefore, who can perform this farḍ should do it as much as he can without delay.

Certainly, it is not done easily nowadays. You can go everywhere in the world [today], but when it comes to ḥajj, by the wisdom of AllāhNone, there is an obstacle or something. In old times, the distance was too far. There were people who blocked the way. Ḥajjīs were robbed. There were diseases and all. Therefore, by the wisdom of AllāhNone, there is always some hardship. People who are able to go, who can overcome those hardships, must certainly go. They must not skip it. "I will go next year." We cannot determine even the next second, let alone next year. Therefore, we must make intention. And Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla will grant your intention. When people intend to go to ḥajj next year, they will do it if they can. If they cannot, AllāhNone will reward them for their intention. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is the most merciful.

Therefore, this matter is important. And for those who couldn't go there, it brings great thawāb to them to be busy with worship on Yawmu ‘Arafah. Their thawāb is tenfold, seventy fold, seven hundred fold, seven thousand fold, in the presence of AllāhNone. AllāhNone knows how much HeNone will give. People must do as much as they can. At least, they must fast. If they have missed prayers, they should make them up. They should make ṣalawāt and recite a thousand times Ikhlāṣu sh-Sharīf until the evening. Rewards for all of them are in the presence of AllāhNone.

Also, ‘Īdu l-Aḍḥá is coming. ‘Īdu l-Aḍḥá is the day of of holy worship. The sacrifice (qurbān) is made after the ‘Īd prayer, not before. But it is wājib to make qurbān after the prayer according to the Ḥanafī madhhab. According to other madhhabs, it is sunnah mu’akkadah. As they don't have wājib, they call it sunnah mu’akkadah. They make qurbān if they want to, or don't make it if they don't want. But those in Ḥanafī madhhab who have money may make qurbān. It is wājib to do so. They earn the reward of wājib, and also may keep all the meat to themselves, if they can't afford too much. They may keep it or eat it. It is not like a vow sacrifice. People confuse them a lot here. You cannot eat from the vow sacrifice but you can eat from ‘aqīqah. People mix these as well. There are deranged scholars – not scholars, but hodjas – who have no idea about fiqh or religion. They tell people that ‘aqīqah and qurbān cannot be eaten. That is not valid. That is not right. The right thing is that it can be eaten. The only sacrificial animal that can't be eaten is the vow sacrifice. The rest can all be eaten. As we said, those who can't afford it may keep all of it to themselves. They will earn thawāb and keep their provision for a year, or as they like. And they may eat it.

Also, there will be takbīr made starting from tomorrow morning. Every day until ‘aṣr of the third day of ‘Īd, we will make takbīr, starting after fajr prayer tomorrow. It is also wājib for us to make it after the farḍ of each prayer. Many people forget to do it. If they don't forget, they must certainly do it. They can't make it up. Congratulating each other on these days of ‘Īd, celebrating and coming together also become a means of reward. People get to see each other, pray for each other and keep each other company. ‘Īd is spent nicely then. It is also nice to make children happy on ‘Īd. In old times, new clothes were bought only on ‘Īds. They would also take children out, and it was a real feast. Nowadays, people don't do so anymore. They have added a hundred other holidays close to the ‘Īds. Such and such a holiday, pickle holiday, chicken holiday, I don't know what holiday. There are many nonsense things, only for people to spend money on them. That is a plot of shayṭān for people to forget the value of the ‘Īds. May AllāhNone give guidance to people.
