As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh. A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says: When We took you out of your home and sent you for hijrah, the hearts of some believers were filled with sorrow. However, when the time for that hijrah came, the command of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla had to be fulfilled. Everything has its time, its appointed moment. If the time hasn’t come yet, no matter how much you strive, nothing will happen. But of course, some people fall into despair, saying things like, “It didn’t happen.” They lose hope and say, “Our affairs aren’t going well. What shall we do? We are praying, but our prayers are not accepted.” Indeed, everything is in the hands of Allāh, in the absolute power of Allāh. Whenever He wills, it happens. And whatever He does is done with wisdom for human beings. There is wisdom in every moment that a person lives. But the greatest possessor of wisdom is our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, who was endowed with it. His every movement, every word, every sign and instruction is full of wisdom. Therefore, no matter how much mankind does, if they do not understand the wisdom, some objection may arise within them, or they may ask what the wisdom is. That is why the hijrah of our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam took place by the command of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. He stayed for years in Makkah Mukarramah. When the instruction from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla came down to him, his stay there had reached its completion, and the time for an opening had come, he made hijrah to Madīnah Munawwarah. It was not out of fear. The path of our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam could have been travelled without any obstacles. But in order to teach people, so they may take lessons, and so they may witness miracles, he travelled that path and came to Madīnah Munawwarah. There, too, the sun shone brighter. The whole world saw that light. Those who were destined for it received their share. Those who were not, did not. At that time, the hijrah of our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam from Makkah Mukarramah to Madīnah Munawwarah was a command. Hijrah was obligatory for people then. Because making hijrah is not easy. Abandoning everything, relocating to another place, and starting anew is a difficult thing. Yet the ṣaḥābah kirām, without hesitation, abandoned all they had to make hijrah following our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. After that hijrah, when Makkah Mukarramah was conquered, our Holy Prophet declared, “There is no more hijrah.” So, the command for hijrah had ended. So when is there a command for hijrah? If people face oppression and great hardship, then there is hijrah. But it is not a command. As a command, it occurred only in the time of our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Otherwise, people become refugees, or whatever else. That is another matter. In some lands, remaining steadfast is better than making hijrah. Rather than abandoning your homeland to disbelievers, if you have the strength to support yourself where you are, then staying there is better for you and for Islām. Because those lands belong to Muslims. If you leave, and others leave, and more follow – who will remain? It will be left to the disbelievers. Therefore, Muslims must be careful about this. They must know what they are doing. Those who say, “Our Holy Prophet made hijrah, so we should make hijrah too,” often don’t follow our Holy Prophet. Instead, they do things that are not necessary. May Allāh protect us. It is hard. As we live in the end times, people leave their homelands for various reasons. They leave Muslim lands and go elsewhere in pursuit of wealth and provision. But it is better to remain in your homeland and earn ḥalāl provision. Living there is better, in shā’a Llāh. May Allāh protect us all.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying in Qur’ān, sometimes people not happy because they, for Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, people they are not happy. You leave your house, your home and going away from this. But this is what Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam make hijrah. It was necessary not for afraid from enemy or from kāfir or non-believer. No, because this must be time to complete what he staying in Makkah Mukarramah, the holiest place in the world. And all this coming for him. Even Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, Isrā’ wa-l-Mi‘rāj also, from Makkah he was, this journey he was, from there. So when time finish, no need, no more anything to get from Makkah. So he, full of power, to distribute this power, he must go to Madīnah Munawwarah. For this, he left Makkah Mukarramah, and he went. Makkah Mukarramah, his home. So some mu’min, they was not happy. They said – maybe they was waiting to make all Islām from Makkah, growing from Makkah. But Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, He has, He is Creator, and His wisdom, He know. We are not knowing. For this, when time coming, order coming for Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam to move towards Madīnah. And for to go from Madīnah, it was very easy for Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, because whole thing, everything in his order: jinn, angel, stone, animal, all, everything. They will be going with him, and nobody can touch him. But he prefer to show people, to his companion, he is like them. He is going through difficulties. And through this journey also, it was many wisdoms, and so many miracles also. Miracle, real miracle, every step miracle from Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Everything he doing, he miracle, advice, teaching for us. Every movement, every word, every sentence he said, teaching for us. So for this, he was going through this difficult journey little bit, but it was also full of miracle. And this, many of them mentioned in Qur’ān, and mentioned in ḥadīth, and mentioned in stories with ṣaḥābah who say. So this is very important journey for human being, for whole human being. Because when he arrive, next station after Makkah, Madīnah Munawwarah. From there his power go through East and West, and his light full off [filled] this earth. And people coming with millions to enter Islām and serve for Islām and serve for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. This is very good place, which it is, it is difficult to live. But Allāh, from also miracle, to show people how can be the most precious place in very difficult place. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, this is for mu’min, for human being, it is blessed from Allāh. There is many thing in this place. One of them also, one piece from Paradise. Ḥajaru l-Aswad coming from Paradise, from Jannah. Many things. So, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, that time to be emigrate, it was obligatory for who live in Makkah or other places where it is difficult to live with non-believer. They was torturing them, killing them, make them, difficult life for them. So everybody slowly, slowly coming to Madīnah. Many of them they was very rich, and they catch them. And said, one of them he said, “You have all this treasure, all this money. Either you, we cannot leave you to go to Madīnah Munawwarah, or you give us all this.” He, without thinking, not bargaining, not one word more. “Yes,” he said, “Okay, take all of this. Leave me.” And he went to Madīnah. All, they was like this. That time is order, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, order from Allāh to emigrate. After when, until emigrate it was order for believer to emigrate from there to Madīnah Munawwarah, but when Makkah Mukarramah conquered, it is finish, no more order for emigrate. It is only for maybe some very difficult, people where they live, they can be difficult for them, to be not safe. So they can emigrate. But other thing no need to emigrate from place to place. Allāh, He is giving rizq, and everywhere you can find. And sometimes, whole country, some people they are not good thinking people. They leave their country, millions of people, emigrate to place, another place. This is not order. This is against Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam order, against Islām order. You leave all this thing, which it is Muslim country. They conquered, by people, they sacrifice themselves to spread Islām in this country. And after, you leave country for this people without anything, only for to think you will be get better kind of life. This is not good. This is not emigrate. This is against will of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam and Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. So who the, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, who stay in place against non-believer, to not let them to take their place, one night guarding this, it is more than seventy year for ‘ibādah, whole night and day fasting. One night, it is more valuable. For this, but people, even this ‘ulamā’ they make this fatwá: “You must leave this place because this is not, we are making for you better.” This is also some kind of trick of shayṭān. So who has place, he must be patient, he must be looking for himself. No need to look for other country. In your country it is more – you are honourable. Other country, when you go to foreign country, you will be – even you are the best one – you will be second class. So better not to put your, this thinking on your mind, because this is growing, growing and it is make you not happy whole life. You be accept what Allāh give you and special you must be thinking, make intention, “I am staying here to keep my land against non-believer. I will be here, I will, in shā’a Llāh, spread belief with my citizens and my neighbours, my friends. So we can be in our country, not to leave Muslim country to non-believer. And Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla He give most valuable place for Muslim. Allāh help us not to be cheated with shayṭān and his followers, in shā’a Llāh. Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
