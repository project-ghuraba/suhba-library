As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

The religion of Islām – “An-naẓāfatu mina l-īmān.” Islām is founded upon cleanliness. Cleanliness is the essence of Islām. If there is no cleanliness, no worship will be accepted. Therefore, in the book of fiqh, the first lesson is Kitābu ṭ-Ṭahārah (The Book of Cleanliness). It is based upon ṭahārah cleanliness. The first thing shown is that the water must be clean. It explains what makes water clean, and how many types of water there are. Everything is made from water. And water is necessary for cleanliness. It’s wrong if it isn’t clean. When obliged, one can make tayammum. That’s another matter, but the main thing – there will definitely be water later to cleanse oneself. This is outer cleanliness.

Inner cleanliness is also needed; for the inside to be clean. How is inner cleanliness achieved? Inner cleanliness is achieved with sincerity and by following the way of our Holy ProphetNone. HisNone way is clear and open. There are those who show hisNone way. There is sharī‘ah. There is ṭarīqah. One must follow himNone. Those who follow hisNone way continue their lives clean. After outer cleansing, inner cleansing also happens in the same way. Otherwise, if you don’t do that, your water mixes with dirt. Just as it’s not permissible to perform ablution with dirty water, you mix other impurities with your path. These things you mix with will make you lose your īmān. May AllāhNone protect us.

Shayṭān and his helpers have been working hard on these issues, since after the time of our Holy ProphetNone, to corrupt the religion. They come up with new things all the time. But thanks to AllāhNone, the imāms of madhhabs and the mashāyikh of ṭarīqah clean all their dirt. They purify people. People don’t respect them. But lately, unfortunately, these shayṭāns are causing more and more trouble. He tells you something, and it all seems good and beautiful. But in the end, he mixes some dirt into it. It dirties you inside. The deeds you do become useless. Those deeds won’t benefit you, but harm you. Therefore, mankind must certainly follow the way shown by our Holy ProphetNone, so that their hereafter is prosperous, so that their hereafter is complete.

Otherwise, there’s a new fashion now: “There’s no need for madhhab. There’s no need for ṭarīqah.” They are the things that are needed first. Ṭarīqah, madhhab, sharī‘ah, and ṭarīqah are the same; they are not different. Some people don’t understand that. That’s why they are deceived. Even when deceived, the benefit from their deeds is very little. It is Islām; it is all complete. But they also have sins then. How is that? They harbour hatred. They hate some of the companions. They hate Ahlu l-Bayt. “They are like us. They have no importance whatsoever,” they say about them; sometimes they express even lesser things. Those who are more deceptive from within gradually and slowly make people fall into doubt and destroy their īmān. Not everyone has īmān. There is Islām, but the rank of those with īmān is higher.

A Muslim has a rank, but those with īmān have a higher rank. They have much more importance. They are Allāh’sNone beloved servants. May AllāhNone protect us. May AllāhNone protect us all from badness, evil, and impurity, in shā’a Llāh.

Our religion, religion of Islām. It is the most important thing for our religion: clean, to be clean, to be pure. Because, “An-naẓāfatu mina l-īmān,” Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, saying. Cleanliness from belief. Believer, he must be clean. Without clean, nothing accept from worshipping, ‘ibādah. The most important thing, you must first to be clean yourself with washing, after, to make ablution. Without this – no, you cannot make any worshipping. All this, you must be, ablution. And the first lesson from book of teaching Islām, about book of cleanness, cleanliness, Kitābu ṭ-Ṭahārah. This is, and this is explaining how to be. And the first thing from this – water. They explain about water, many kind of water. Which one can be use for clean, to clean yourself, to wash yourself, to make ablution. Which one it is, maybe, not okay for drinking, but you can also, you can use for washing. And there is other water you cannot use for ablution, and not acceptable, because … Why? It is dirty. This is our religion, the most important thing, most pure thing for human being, to show, everything showing this religion, from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. And Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, teach us how to worship, to clean yourself. This is cleanliness from outside. It is very important.

But also, there is cleanliness, to clean your inside. Your inside. How we can clean our inside? With worshipping, with ‘ibādah, and following what Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, how heNone showing us what to do from ‘ibādah, exactly like himNone. And to be respect and love for Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. This is very important. And other thing, to follow himNone. How we can follow himNone?

We are... Even after Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, there is thousands of shayṭān people coming, showing people, cheating people, to go, to do something Prophet ṣallá Llāhu‘alayhi wa-sallam, not doing. Or to accept something, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam not accept. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, there is, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying: two things you must follow – Qur’ān and love of Ahlu l-Bayt, and hisNone ḥadīth. This is what we can follow. And, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, this‘ulamā’ and mashāyikh, they are making, showing with their knowledge, thousand of books and student, teaching people. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are happy with this, but there is people who all time, all time they are trying to make people go away from this way. Who is this people? This is shayṭān follower. They are not happy so they all time making something, not accepting what real way of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. And then not accepting madhhab, not accepting ṭarīqah. Because this is the most powerful defender for this way, way of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, to help people, to serve them, to show them the way. Because without somebody know way, you cannot go anywhere. You will be lost and you will be whole time without any benefit for you. For this, our thing: to be our inside clean, you must follow madhhab and ṭarīqah. This is very important. They say, “Ṭarīqah not important.” No, it's very important. Without this, they can make you, play with you and make you to be doubt about everything. With ṭarīqah, ṭarīqah give you more belief, more īmān and, in shā’a Llāh, you be safe. This is very important. Our life, it is not playing. We are here for serious thing, which it is, forever it will be important for us. For this, you must follow ṭarīqah, in shā’a Llāh. And sharī‘ah or ṭarīqah, they are same, but they make people to afraid. When you say ṭarīqah, they are afraid. They are not happy. But this is shayṭān playing.

AllāhNone keep us safe from these people. AllāhNone give more belief, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
