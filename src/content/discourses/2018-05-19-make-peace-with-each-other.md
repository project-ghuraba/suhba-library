As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa raḥmatu Llāhi wa barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīn wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Wal-kāẓimīna l-ghayẓa wal-‘āfīna ‘ani n-nāsi wa-Llāhu yuḥibbu l-muḥsinīn" (3:134). Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla does not like those who are angry. For those who are angry with each other, Ramaḍān is the month for them to make up. And it is a great deed. So for those who are angry to make up is also a beautiful form of worship. Its rewards are great. Worship which involves waking up for night prayer, or fasting – there is nothing hard in this, nothing tiresome. However, doing it appears harder to people.

According to the commands of Islām, being angry for more than three days is not good. So if someone insists on being angry, then let them be. Do not be angry with anyone. We will answer the Salām if they give the Salām. If they come, we will accept them. So there is not much to do. Because AllāhNone says HeNone loves those who forgive. Our Holy ProphetNone says to forgive. HeNone says, "AllāhNone loves those who forgive."

Our Holy ProphetNone has a hadith: he asked whether he should tell us about the bad ones among us? They asked him to tell them. A few of them are: those who eat alone, the person who does not feel affection towards anybody. He said the bad ones among us are the evil ones among us. Secondly, our Holy ProphetNone asked whether heNone should tell who is even worse – the person who does not like people is one who is also not liked. Thirdly, heNone asked whether heNone should tell who is even worse – the one from whom you expect no good. He does not do good. Even if he did good in the past, he did it by mistake and he goes and says, "Oh, I had done this to my surprise. Let me erase it immediately. Let me change my mind from doing this good." He would do this if he could. The one from whom you cannot expect good is a person from whom you expect evil. Our Holy ProphetNone says, there is a worse, fourth level person. "Should I tell you of a more evil, worse one?" heNone asked. "Please tell us," they said to our Holy ProphetNone. The one who does not accept the excuse of anybody, the one who also does not forgive. He says, "The one who never forgives is the worst among you."

These are not good things. What is good... Well of course, not everybody can get along with everyone. But even if one cannot get along, there is no need to be angry. The Salām belongs to AllāhNone. It should be answered. You should not be around each other so much that there arises too much rancour and spite. This is one of the beautiful traditions of Ramaḍān. AllāhNone says HeNone forgives those who forgive. "They will be resurrected and be together under MyNone Throne." Our Holy ProphetNone says, "Those who forgive, go to Paradise." Therefore, making up is a worship that needs to be done without feeling tired, in this month. Getting along well with people and not staying angry with anyone. As we said, whatever a Muslim might have done, you still cannot be angry with a Muslim. Even if there is not much friendship, Allāh's Salām should be answered.

Things like that happen. There is no giving Salām or he pretends not to hear it, or even if he hears it, he does not return the Salām. The giving of Salām is sunnah, and the answering it is farḍ. So he would be committing a sin then. Therefore, the good advice of this beautiful month is the need to accept what our Holy ProphetNone and what Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla say. It is the wisdom of AllāhNone that it happens a lot between people. It happens between brothers and between relatives too, friends also, neighbors also. Being angry is the work of shayṭān. It is a strange thing. AllāhNone wants people to act, not according to their customs, but according to the orders of Islām and the beautiful advice of our Holy ProphetNone. Let us do these things in this world. Now every nation has its own customs. There is obstinacy and there is that. Leave all that aside and do what AllāhNone says: "This is what AllāhNone says. We will follow this." As we said, there is no need to be intimate either. So also do not exaggerate too much. But there should be no animosity or anger says our Holy ProphetNone.

Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla saying in Qur’ān: A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm "Wal-kāẓimīna l-ghayẓa wal-‘āfīna ʿani n-nāsi wa-Llāhu yuḥibbu l-muḥsinīn" (3:134). AllāhNone saying who this people who are can control their anger and forgive people and make charity for – AllāhNone loves who makes charity. Now it is month of Ramaḍān this is blessed month and it is reward for everything – for worshipping. We are, al-ḥamdu liLlāh we make, we worship all together in shā’a Llāh – not for us only – people more than other months they fasting, they making saḥūr, ifṭār, and pray tahajjud, and they make tarāwīh. Reading Qur’ān, everything they are doing.

This is good but there is one thing very important for this month, speciality for this month. Now the people they are not care about this too much. This is what AllāhNone for saying for benefit of us, for believer, and for benefit of Ummah whole together also. So it is important. This not easy and its reward more than ‘ibādah even, worshipping even. And ProphetNone saying who are ‘afuww yu‘azzikumu Llāh – if you are, you forgive, AllāhNone make you ‘azīz meaning very high and HeNone put you in HisNone paradise. This is ḥadīth of ProphetNone to be forgiving for people.

And one ḥadīth other, ProphetNone saying, "Do you like to tell you who are the bad people from you? They said, "Yes." You must make ourself, to train ourself to be against – not this – opposite of this what ProphetNone – if you do the opposite you be the best for ProphetNone. And it is good for whole human being not only for Muslim but of course human being they are they have another teaching – teaching of shayṭān – and it is not like this. To be make fitnah, to make to be divide people, to make people to fight to each other. But Islām and ProphetNone like people to make peace between themself first, after for other people also. AllāhNone help us and to make this, to make it easy for us. As we said, you can at least you can say Salāmu ‘alaykum for who are not happy with you. If he said, "Wa ‘alaykum salām," okay. if not you are be safe from, to be, you be rewarded and not be responsible for this what happen. Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
