As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
وَسَخَّرَ لَكُمُ الشَّمْسَ وَالْقَمَرَ دَائِبَيْنِ ۖ وَسَخَّرَ لَكُمُ اللَّيْلَ وَالنَّهَارَ
<br>“Wa sakhkhara lakumu sh-shamsa wa l-qamara dā'ibayn. Wa sakhkhara lakumu l-layla wa n-nahāra.”<br>
“And He has made the sun and the moon, both constantly pursuing their courses, to be of service to you; and He has made the night and the day, to be of service to you.” (Qurʼān 14:33). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla has made the sun, moon, and stars subservient to humankind. This means they have an influence and wisdom on human lives. Nothing is created in vain. Allah created this universe for humankind. Everything that happens is by the will and pleasure of Allah.

Yesterday, we didn’t see it, but there was a solar eclipse elsewhere. A solar eclipse is a divine decree, a sign from Allah. Of course, it has an effect; scientifically, they know it has an effect. It is known that such natural events affect the world and people. These things happen by the command of Allah; nothing happens by coincidence.

Sometimes people say, “A stone will fall from the sky, it will hit us, it just grazed us.” They don’t know, because they are those who lack faith. A person of faith knows that. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla has described the signs of the Day of Judgment in Qur’ān ʿAẓīmu sh-Sha’n, and our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam has described them in his ḥadīths. Everything will unfold accordingly.

However, to avoid such events and unfortunate situations, there are instructions and teachings from Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla and our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. Prayers are performed during those times. Long surahs are recited in these prayers. But only in places where the eclipse is visible. There is no need to pray in places where it is not visible. In places where the eclipse is visible, prayers are performed, tasbihāt and duʿās are recited until the sun or moon reappears. However, there is no need for this in places where it is not visible. Because that event is a sign for other places. They have states upon them.

These prayers and recitations of Surahs and so are for worshipping Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla and seeking His grace and mercy to alleviate those states. Undoubtedly, these acts of worship will alleviate them. But if it is not recited, may Allah protect us, it becomes much more difficult.

Such situations are signs of the wisdom and majesty of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. Everything that happens in the universe—in the moon, the stars, the sky, and the earth—every minute, every second, happens by the will of Allah. They have their own wisdom.

People should not think that “We were created from nothing and that everything that happens is ordinary.” Certainly, nothing happens outside the will of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla.

Therefore, all of these are actually warnings for people. Allah does what He wills. When He wants, He makes the middle of the day as dark as night. He makes it cold in the middle of summer and hot in the middle of winter. All of this happens by the will and decree of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla.

Therefore, people of īmān must believe in this. Of course, there is also a scientific aspect to it, which is known through calculation and accounting. However, this knowledge is only found in those whom Allah has given knowledge to and taught. Otherwise, not everyone knows how these things happen, how they come and go.

Therefore, may Allah protect us and grant us what is best for us, inshā’Allāh. May it be good, inshā’Allāh, and may it be a means of good, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
