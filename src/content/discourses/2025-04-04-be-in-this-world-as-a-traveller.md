As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh. A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is saying, “Don’t forget that everyone will return to where they came from: to Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.” They will be held accountable there for what they did in this world. Whatever a person does, if it’s good, he will receive its reward. If he did evil, he will answer for that evil. “Why did you do evil? Why did you cause suffering to people? Why did you wrong yourself?” These questions will be put to him. That is where he’ll face accountability, before Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Therefore, this world is not eternal.

Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says in a ḥadīth sharīf, “Live in this world like a stranger, like a person who owns nothing.” Be like a passing traveller. Because this dunyā is not the homeland. Our true homeland is the ākhirah. This world is just a place to prepare for the affairs of the ākhirah. It is the place to gather them. The good deeds and virtues you collect for the hereafter are in this world. When you enter ākhirah, nothing will remain with you. Unless you leave behind righteous children and good deeds, unless you leave ṣadaqah jāriyah (ongoing charity), nothing will follow you. Therefore, think about everything, think about ākhirah while in this dunyā, and set your affairs for the hereafter on the right path accordingly. Matters of this world must be for the hereafter. When worldly matters are only for this dunyā, you will leave them behind. But when they are for the hereafter, you will find them in ākhirah. You will have their benefit. But those who work only for this dunyā will gain nothing in ākhirah. Especially those who follow the path of disbelief will face regret far worse than hell. Burning in hell will be nothing compared to that regret.

Our goal must be the hereafter. “Consider yourself as if dead,” says our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Indeed, people live by the power of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. They move by it. The moment Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla reclaims that trust, a person can do nothing. Even if the whole world belongs to someone, he cannot buy a single breath with it. It will be over. Therefore, Muslims should always think about the hereafter and act accordingly in this world. You may work for this world. There is no prohibition. But your intention must be the pleasure of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. It must be HisNone way. It must carry HisNone barakah. This is the truth for Muslims. This is its benefit... either you find benefit or you face harm. The benefit comes from being on the path of AllāhNone. The path of loss is the way of shayṭān. Abandoning the way of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla or not valuing it also brings error and regret. May AllāhNone protect us. May AllāhNone not separate us from the straight path.

This is the beautiful path. There are many deceivers in the time we live in. Human devils have become worse than shayṭān. People are no longer deceived by shayṭān. They are deceived by other people. People deceive one another. They are worse than shayṭān. They present falsehood as truth. And they deny the truth. May AllāhNone protect people from their evil.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone say for us, be careful for day you will be come back to HimNone, to Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. And that day, everyone, he will take what he done in dunyā. “Be careful,” HeNone said, “Ittaqū yawman turja‘ūna fīh” (2:281). And everything, you will be asked for this. What you done in dunyā, you will [be] asked. Not, be finished, nobody seeing you. No, nothing. Everything writing, with angel, and they are, you will be see every single thing you done. For this, you must be careful for this day, to not do bad thing. If you do bad thing, to ask forgiveness from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. If you ask, and not doing again, Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla, also HeNone forgive you. Every time you do something, you ask for forgiveness, AllāhNone forgive you. Because dunyā, it is place of work.

And Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying this: “Be in dunyā, world, as you are stranger, or who, like who going, passenger.” Stranger and passenger, you are going from place to place. Stranger, you are nobody with you. Be like this. You must be careful to take, to make your travel easy, and to go, to aim what to reach to your target, safely. Don't follow other people. They will be take you to another place. Especially these days people, they are cheating people. They are showing good thing as it is bad. And if anybody, he is in right way, they make him, attacking him, take him from his way, to take out of way. This is time, for everything, it is not real. Real, only Islām, and the way of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam.

But now they make it, every day, they bring another way. They said, “This is, we are modern, and we are knowing best for human. So, we do this. You must follow this.” If you not follow, they not accept you as good one. We are hoping to be good one in Divine Presence of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. This is important, not other. Our way, it is long way. So, must be careful to not go out of this way. This is very important for human being, whole human being. They must think about what they are here, why they are created.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone create many creatures. Very few of them, they are responsible. Who appear in world you can see: human being. There is also jinn; also, they are responsible to worship as we are worshipping. Angels, they are all time worshipping without any sin, nothing for them. But for jinn and human being, they have [to] think. So, who are human being like us, he must think how, we, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, why HeNone give us this shape, this mind, this thinking to... Can be, we can be like others, like animal, like animal or small thing. Many thing, they are AllāhNone create, but only for us HeNone gave this. So, it is easy for who thinking right to accept Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, and follow HisNone order. This is, because there is end for this, in ākhirah, not in dunyā. There is end, and we will [be] asked for what we done. AllāhNone keep us in HisNone way, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
