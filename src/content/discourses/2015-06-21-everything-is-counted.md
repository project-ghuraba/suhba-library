Tariqatuna s-sohbah, wa l-khayru fi jam'iyyah. Audhu billahi min ash-shaytani r-rajim. Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. "Wa kullu shayin indahu bimiqdarin" (13:8) Allah Azza wa Jalla is saying in Qur'an that everything has its measure and scale.

Allah Azza wa Jalla has put a measure for everything. He indicated what things to do and He has said what things should not be done. Things to be done are goodness - worship and all good things. He orders to do good to everyone. Do good to everyone who deserves it because goodness cannot be done to some people. You can't help someone who is an enemy to Islam and Muslims. You can give him advice but only as a help. Otherwise, it can't be. It's not good to treat your ego well as well. Ego is something, which always needs to be crushed. If you do good to it, it will ride you immediately. If you help an oppressor, you will oppress the oppressed even more.

However, our Prophet said to help your brother even if he's an oppressor or oppressed. Sahabas asked "How shall we help an oppressor?" He said "Give him advice so that he gives up what he does. If he stops his oppression, you will have helped him. You will have done the biggest help." Because if it is left until Qiyama and the right of that person [of his victim] remains on him, he will be in a bad situation.

Everything is shown. Allah Azza wa Jalla said and showed everything, halal and haram. And there are things in between some people have doubts about. Those are not important. For the things not accounted Allah will give according to the intentions of people. But don't be with people who are against Muslims. What they'll have is Allah's punishment. Allah's punishment will come to you too. "Wala tarkanu ila lladhina dhalamu fatamassakumu n-naru"(11:113) (And incline not toward those who do wrong, lest the Fire touch you) Beware not to be with them. Otherwise, you will burn too, badness will come to you too. And may Allah protect, whatever your intention is, if being against Muslims - if it's clear that there is a movement against Islam and Muslims and you support it and you are with them, then you will suffer both in dunya and have punishment in akhirah. Pay attention, don't be with oppressors. Be with Muslims, with Ahl as-sunnah wa l-jama'ah.

Ahl as-sunnah wa l-jama'ah is clear. We are saying here, imams of four madhhabs are Ahl as-sunnah wa l-jama'ah. There are imams of belief, Imam Ash'ari and Imam Maturidi Hz. Who follow them are Ahl as-sunnah wa l-jama'ah. Others are not ahl as-sunnah wa l-jama'ah. Don't be with them because they are oppressors. They are shedding the blood of Muslims. They're not saying Allah created. They are such people. Their belief is wrong, actions are wrong. They are wrong themselves. And they have all dirt. Beware not to be with them. Ramadan Sharif is the month of advice. And we are advising so from here. There are many people who are out of right ways. They are deceiving people and showing Ahl as-sunnah wa l-jama'ah as mushrik. Whereas, we are following the way shown by our Prophet. We are walking on the beautiful path of our fathers and grandfathers which came from our Prophet. The rest just [recently] appeared. They brought up new things every time. They are the people of bida't and depravity/misguided. Who follow them will be vanquished. There are newly brought up [immature] children who don't know about religion and faith. Their grandfather is shaitan. Sheikh Effendi used to call the television a shaitan box. Now this internet has become shaitan multiplied, shaitan's grandfather. Someone shows up from there and misguides children. They become rebellious against their parents. Then they lose their lives, property and akhirah. Who are next to them? - those people who don't like sahabas are the same.

Ahl as-sunnah wa l-jama'ah is now in between, being hit from both sides. Allah is with us. We won't be harmed, we are patient with them and we will be rewarded by Allah. Nothing goes uncounted in akhirah. What the others have done is not uncounted also, they will be accountable for what they do.

Allah Azza wa Jalla, He put. Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim "wa kullu shay'in 'indahu bimiqdarin" (13:8). He said in Qur'an, everything it is counting, counted. What you do, what you must do, what you must not do, what they are good, what they're bad, what is worshipping - all it is shown by Allah Azza wa Jalla and by His Order, and shown by Prophet (sas) and Sahaba, and tariqa Imam Mashaykh and Imam of Mazahib. We have four Mazhab who are following Ahl as-sunnah wa jama'ah and to believe leader, Imam - Who are following this, they are following right way. Who are going out from this way, they are not from Prophet (saws). They're party of who, said Prophet(sas) [about them that] they're going to hell.

It is shown what, for whom you must do good thing, for who you must be merciful. You must be merciful for your Muslim brothers. If you see him in right way, you must be happy. If you are see him in wrong you must tell him, advise him and you be done for him the best. Because Prophet (sas) said you must help your Muslim brother, if he is oppressor or oppressed. Sahaba said: "How if is oppressing, how we can help him?" You can help to tell him, you are in, doing against Islam and you must be stop this what you are doing. When he is stopping, you make for him the best thing he can take, get from this dunya. Because, you make him to be in right way again and to not be, take sin for Last Day.
