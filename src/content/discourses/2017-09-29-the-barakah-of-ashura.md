As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa raḥmatu Llāhi wa barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-Ṣalātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīn wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

Shukr to AllāhNone, today is the 9th of holy Muḥarram. These days are holy. The month of Muḥarram as a whole is liberation from needs, it is days of liberation. Especially the 10th of Muḥarram, Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla made it special and unique so that until Qiyāmah it is a holy day. The worship made on that day as our ProphetNone said, "Who fasts on that day receives the thawāb (reward) of a thousand ḥajj’s and a thousand ‘umrahs. And he gets thawāb of a thousand shahīds," says our ProphetNone. It is such a valuable day.

ʻĀshūrā’ means the 10th day. The 10th day of Muḥarram is ʻĀshūrā’. People should not miss their opportunity on that day. Our ProphetNone said to fast on the 9th and 10th - not just one day. We fast on some days during this year. Most of the year we can fast. Some days we can't fast like ‘Īd. Five days a year we can't fast. And we can't fast the 10th of Muḥarram alone. You should fast either the 9th and 10th or the 10th and 11th. It is a fast that can't be done in one day. That is the order of our ProphetNone.

All awliyā’ and Prophets reached their stations on this day. AllāhNone created the throne, the preserved tablet and the pen on this day. Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla created mountains and oceans on ʻĀshūrā’. Therefore, it has its specialty. And on this day Sayyidinā Ḥusayn reached the station of shahīd. In order to confuse people, shaytān is using this day too. If ʻĀshūrā’ is not blessed, why did Sayyidinā Ḥusayn become shahīd? It is a blessed very good day. It is the most virtuous day. His station was high and Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla gave him even higher stations. Therefore, we should be more careful. We have to live this day for AllāhNone. We shouldn't give it to our nafs. The nafs asks for evil. It uses even this holy day as a means for enmity between people. That is the work of shayṭān.

Whereas, all Ummati Muḥammad celebrates ʻĀshūrā’. Everyone loves that day and respects it. Of course, as we said, these are people on the way of our ProphetNone. But people cheated by shayṭān use even this day for enmity. We accept all of them - we accept all Prophets. We don't differentiate between any Prophet. And we respect the family, Ahlu l-Bayt of our ProphetNone and his ṣaḥābah. It is the order of ProphetNone. Don't be cheated by fitnah. As we are seeing now, they are making fitnah in all of the Islamic world. And shayṭān with his followers laugh from the sidelines. They accept they made fitnah. Whereas ʻĀshūrā’ is not a fitnah day. It is a day of blessings, a day of great virtues. There is nice worshipping to be made. Make them.

First of all, fast. Fast for sure. It was the fast of our ProphetNone, sunnah mu’akkadah and earlier it was farḍ. Before Ramaḍān they used to fast in Muḥarram. When it was changed to Ramaḍān, it became sunnah mu’akkadah. It means, as our ProphetNone said, it brings thawāb of a thousand ḥajj’s, a thousand ‘umrahs and a thousand shahīds. It is not a small thing. Even if you go on ḥajj every year, you can't make it. As long as AllāhNone wants, Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla gives. And on that day we have to recite 1,000 Ikhlās. Also on that day Mawlānā used to pray four raka‘āt after ẓuhr. Recite 11 Ikhlās in each rak‘ah, then 70 Ḥasbunā Llāhu wa ni‘ma l-wakīl. Then there is its du‘ā’. We should make that du‘ā’ and complete our prayer.

And there is the night of ʻĀshūrā’. It is tonight. It has big thawāb and virtues. Du‘ā’ get answered tonight too. For all worship, AllāhNone will reward you from HisNone presence. And on the day of ʻĀshūrā’ to make ghusl is - this is ḥadīth of our ProphetNone - you won't have serious illness that year with permission of AllāhNone. Who put kohl on their eyes won't have eye problems with permission of AllāhNone. And who please their children, bring home provisions, make their families happy will have a blessed abundant year. Everyone who can, should do it. They should do as much as they can so that they don't complain with troubles with permission of AllāhNone. There will be no trouble that year. It is the true word of our ProphetNone. Who does it will certainly reach what is promised. That year will be easy.

People are suffering from troubles. Therefore, pray a lot tonight and make du‘ā’ continuously. Don't get bored and tired. AllāhNone loves who prays. Acceptance will reach, in shā’a Llāh, and your du‘ā’ will be accepted. In shā’a Llāh, for the honor of this year may the head come for Islām. Muslims are suffering everywhere now. They are oppressed all over the world. They are oppressed both by those who call themselves Muslim and by non-Muslims. There are people who look like Muslims but who help shayṭān. May AllāhNone protect us from them. May we all be secure. In shā’a Llāh, may our government not decline too. May AllāhNone help it because it is standing against whole world, against this shayṭān. They are human. They make mistakes. May AllāhNone forgive.

May AllāhNone show us the right way and not separate us from the right way. May this duty go on until Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām, in shā’a Llāh. We hope that Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām comes very soon, in shā’a Llāh.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are today in ninth of month of Muḥarram, New year. Muḥarram beginning with our new year - it is beginning nine days ago. It is blessed month this Muḥarram also. ProphetNone saying, if you like to fast, fast in this month. And it is many things happen in this from beginning of creation until whole ProphetNone. In this day AllāhNone create the Lawḥ wa l-Qalam, Preserved Tablet. And HeNone brought mountain, everything in this month, especially in day of tenth of Muḥarram, we call it ‘Āshūrā’. It is very holy very blessed, blessed day. Many Prophet, many awliyā’u Llāh they take their survive [salvation] and they AllāhNone good tiding them in this day. Give them highest stage, prophecy and wilāyah awliyā’ and many things in this days. Even Sayyidinā ‘Īsá ‘alayhi s-salām he born in this day. ProphetNone he was married with Khadījatu l-Kubrá, Ummunā Khadījatu l-Kubrá raḍī Allāhu ‘anhā this day also.

Many thing happen in this day. It is blessed and AllāhNone happy to give this day for Ummati Muḥammad. Before Ummati Muḥammad also Banī Isrā’īl they was also fasting in this day. And this day also Sayyidinā Ḥusayn raḍī Allāhu ‘anhu, the grandson of ProphetNone was become shahīd. They make him shahīd so what happen? There is ‘Āshūrā’ day, it is for whole Muslim important day. But some of them with making shayṭān make this fitnah to make this day also not blessed, to make it wrong. But ProphetNone saying this is very blessed day and you must respect and who fast one day in this, tenth of Muḥarram, ‘Āshūrā’, AllāhNone reward him as thousand time pilgrimage, ḥajj and ‘umrah and thousand of reward of shahīd. Martyr? Martyr. And AllāhNone forgive him for fifty year what from his sins. So we must be happy with this day. Because what AllāhNone want to Sayyidinā Ḥusayn to making him this day, to make him come to highest stage, stage of martyrs. It's not easy but Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla HeNone want this and it is for people who done this it is very bad but AllāhNone HeNone want, so they are unlucky. The lucky one Sayyidinā Ḥusayn raḍī Allāhu ‘anhu karram Allāhu wajhu and so we are respecting. And all Muslim respect ‘Āshūrā’ and it is day for blessed day.

ProphetNone saying in this day you must - who make ghusl washing, taking shower, this year he will be not seriously ill. And who put in his eye some kohl, his eyes it will be no any illness. And to give something in house to make people happy and bring food and many thing to house make this house barakah for one year. It is, it will be no anything less - getting barakah it more and more, in shā’a Llāh.. So this is what this day having from barakah, mercy, everything in this day. Who make people also to break their fast AllāhNone reward him also as fifty martyrs, ProphetNone saying. So we must be careful for this day not to make any wrong. Every day, every from tonight until tomorrow night it is ‘Āshūrā’. This night also holy night.

People they are complaining, they are for everything. So it is blessed day and AllāhNone accept du‘ā’ more than other days. Don't be without making du‘ā’, praying for AllāhNone to ask what you want to ask, ask from HimNone. In shā’a Llāh, HeNone will give you, in shā’a Llāh. And tomorrow also another thing ProphetNone was saying for us - to pray four raka‘āt. Each rak‘ah we pray eleven Ikhlāṣ, "Qul huwa Llāhu aḥad," (112:1). After making seventy times, "Ḥasbuna Llāhu wa ni‘ma l-wakīl," (3:173). After there is also du‘ā’ special for this that day. So, in shā’a Llāh, don't forget this and make thousand Ikhlāṣu sharīf also.

So, in shā’a Llāh, AllāhNone make this day barakah and victory for Muslim because they are now in everywhere they are attacking them. We ask from AllāhNone to be protect us from shayāṭīn, whole world now against Muslim. Especially there is people they claim they are Muslim they are doing worse than other also. Also they are army of shayṭān. AllāhNone make them to fight with each other to forget Muslim, in shā’a Llāh. And we are asking for protection for Muslim who are in right way and keep them safe until Sayyidinā l-Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām. And we ask, in shā’a Llāh, for Sayyidinā l-Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām to come this year, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
