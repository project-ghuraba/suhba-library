As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh. A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah. As our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says – what we recited in khuṭbah – “O son,” our Prophet is addressing Anas Efendi, the companion who was serving him, “If you do not have any deceit in your heart towards anyone, then do so morning and night if you can. It is my sunnah,” said our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. “Whoever does my sunnah loves me,” says our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. “And whoever loves me will be with me in paradise.” This is the summary of all Islām. A person who is Muslim should strive to do this. He should not deceive anyone. He should not harm anyone. Whether close or distant, he should think goodness for everyone. He should not think of badness for his own benefit, so that he follows the sunnah of the Prophet. The sunnah of our Prophet is important. Ṭarīqah is already based on love of our Prophet. It is to follow his way. And doing his sunnah is the main adab. Ṭarīqah stands upon adab. And that adab is good character. Good character means doing goodness, wishing goodness, not letting badness enter your heart, not falling into badness, not wishing it. You must always think good of people so that goodness comes to you, so that you may be with our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam in paradise. This is our greatest aim. There may be harm but no benefit. You may follow someone not on the way of Allāh and gain something, but most of the time there is no benefit. On the contrary, there is harm. Therefore, we must be on the way of Allāh. We must be on the way of our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. We must respect the sunnah of our Prophet, which is important.
