Mawlana Shaykh Mohamad Nazim al Haqqani an Nakshibendi

21 Rajab, 1432 A.H. Thursday, June 23, 2011 Translated from Arabic by: http://saltanat.org/

BismillahirRahmanirRahim

Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar, la Ilaha illa Allah, wa Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar wa Lillahi Al Hamd. Allahumma Salli wa Sallim Abgharqana Sayidil Awwalin wa al Akhirin Habibak Sayidil Awwalin wa al Akhirin. Praise the Master of beginning and end, Your beloved Prophet and place us under his holy flag, oh Lord of the worlds. Thumma assalat wa al salam 'alà sai'ri Al Anbi'yà wa al mursalin wa Khudam'ì shara'ihim wa 'alà ay-matuna wa Salat wa salam 'alà Awli'yà Allah (jwa). And praises on the rest of the Prophets and praises on our great Masters and praises on all Friends (Saints) of Allah Almighty. Meded ya Rijal Allah. We are asking support, oh men of Allah. Destur ya Sultan Al Awliyà. We are asking permission from the Sultan of Saints. Meded! Meded, meded. Meded Ya Rijal Allah. We are asking support from the Men of Allah. Allah Al-lah, Allah Allah.

We are asking to wake up all the servants of the Lord Almighty. To awaken them must come from the Malakut (heavenly) domain. The kind of waking up that belongs to people and this world, has no value. To truly be awakened has to come from the heavens. Otherwise it is not possible for a man, who has no relationship at all with the heavens, to wake people up. No! Why were the Prophets (as) taking their instructions from the heavens (the Divine domain), not from the people on earth and men connected to dunya (the world)? No! Worldly men do not know anything. They are blind, blind. And a blind person doesn't know where to place his foot, where to step. When he puts his foot on the ground, he may step in a hole he doesn't know, and he may fall and kill himself. We are asking for the children of Adam (as), all human beings. We need someone who can see and can hear from the Heavens.

And we are now saying: Audhu Billah min al shaytan al rajim, Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Rahim. Ya Rabb support us. Ya Rasul Allah Shafaàt, we are asking your intercession. Our way is in association [with the Shaykh]. We are not claiming that we know something. But when the order comes from the Heavens, it is sahih (true, right). 'Sahih' means it is correct, and that which is sahih corrects what is wrong. If mankind do not hear it from the Heavens, they cannot distinguish what is wrong and what is right. Therefore, human beings need a man who can hear from the heavens and who can distinguish between what is right and what is wrong. This is not an easy task. We are living at the end of times. There is no doubt about this. This age that we are in is end of times. Haqq (swt) said, Faqad Ja'a 'Ashratuha (47:18) … its signs have already come.

Oh Shaykh of Al Azhar, don't you read the Holy Qur'an? Oh you who claim to be scholars or doctors in the sciences of Shariat, have you not come across what Allah (swt) said, “Faqad Ja'a 'Ashratuha” (some signs of Judgment Day have already come)? Between us now and that statement there are 1500 years. Where are you? Say, what are the signs to which Allah (swt) is pointing in this verse, "Faqad Ja'a 'Ashratuha"? Do you think that these signs have not yet appeared? Are you claiming that signs of Judgment Day have not shown up yet? This would mean that you are denying the Heavenly news. You have no right to deny the Heavenly news. In the holy verse, 'Faqad Ja'at' is formed in Arab-ic in a way that removes any doubt. There is no doubt at all that signs of Judgment Day have ap-peared. "Faqad Ja'a 'Ashratuha" (some of its signs have already come). From the time of the Prophet (saws) up to the present time, the signs have been coming. They came. Say it, oh you who claim to be scholars. Tell me! What are the signs that have already come? The signs that we are experiencing, the troubles that we are in today, what do you think? Are these from the signs of Judgment Day or not? Tell me. All these problems that the nation of the Beloved Prophet (sas) are experiencing now, what are they? What do you think? Why aren't you saying? Why don't you speak? Don't you know this? If you don't know, you must ask [as mentioned in the holy verse], "Fas'al Bihi Khabiran" (25:59) Ask the one who has the knowledge.

This holy verse is ordering that you must ask an expert, someone who knows, someone who has the news from the hereafter and who has been informed. For all these troubles, there must be an expert (khabir), an informed one who will tell you. Ask him. Who is that one – the Khabir? Ask him about the signs of the Last Hour. This is important. It is important. Why did Allah (awj) mention it in the Holy Qur'an? He (swt) said, "Faqad Ja'a 'Ashratuha". Who is speaking? Allah (swt) is speaking. A person cannot understand this by himself. The [meaning of these] words in this verse is not obvious; it is con-cealed. It is not clear for the general public to understand. If you do not understand the meaning of what Allah Almighty says, "Faqad Ja'a 'Ashratuha", you must ask for a Khabir, an expert, a learned in-formed person who knows and who may convey heavenly information (from certainty) about this mat-ter [of the signs and troubles in the end times].

Why doesn't the Shaykh of Azhar ask, “Who is the expert about the problems of the world that the ummah has fallen into?” We are not asking for words (grumbling) from his stomach, no! Those words are not to be considered as certain. We are asking from those who have news of ahkirat (al-khabar al-yaqin). Why does the Prophet (saws) say, "Juhayna has al-khabar al-yaqin"?. Who is Juhayna? Oh director of Azhar Al Sharif, who is Juhayna? Why did the Prophet (saws) say this? [He said it] to make clear and to correct the understanding about who the expert is in "Fas'al Bihi Khabiran". 'Khabir' is indicating 'Juhayna'. Who is Juhayna? Are you Juhayna? Who is this Juhayna who reports Certain News? Who is this one who the Prophet (saws) is pointing to? He is saying that the nations and the nation of the Beloved must always look for Juhayna. The Prophet did not speak from his whims or ego. No! Then why did he say that al-khabar al-yaqin is with Juhayna? This means that at all times there must be among the ummah someone who has the characteristics of Juhayna, someone of whom you can ask and who has the al-khabar al-yaqin.

* “al-khabar al-yaqin” may be translated as Certain News, meaning infallible, indisputable information that may be reported by someone who has certainty regarding this information. Oh scholars, oh you who claim to be scholars, why don't you ask about Juhayna? Do you think that the news that the Juhayna carries about the end times is only about the destruction of Madina Al Mun-awwara? Does Juhayna only know this? No! From the beginning to the end of time, Juhayna is present. It must be - Juhayna. Why don't you ask about Juhayna so that he may deliver to you news from the Akhirat? He is the expert who can give certain, indisputable information. "Fas'al Bihi Kh-abiran" (ask the expert). Who is the expert? It could be Juhayna because he was pointed out in the tradition and blessed words of Holy Prophet (sas).

Ask, oh Shaykh of Az'har! [Al Az'har means 'apparent'.] What has appeared from you? What became apparent from you, 'Al Az'har'? (Al Az'har, meaning apparent, is an Arabic word in a form that indicates exaggeration of an attribute.) Do you claim (or do you think) that the knowledge which appears in your school or institution is specialized and not apparent to others? Is it especially for you? Well then say, oh scholars of Azhar and the director of the scholars, say, who is Juhayna? Have you asked about and searched for Juhayna so that you may know who he is, to be certain about where he is, and to under-stand the al-khabar al-yaqin he carries? What is this? Do you think that the news the Prophet of end times (sas) indicated that Juhayna would give in the end times was only about the beasts that had entered Madinatul Munawwara? What is this? Who would ask him about this? Of what benefit is this information for us now? If the beasts enter Madinatul Munawwara, what benefit is it for him to talk about this from all the true knowledge he has? So what do we do with this news? - go to Madina and fight the beasts? La hawla wa la quwwata illa Billah … La hawla wa la quwwata illa Billah...

Who is that Juhayna? We are asking, we are asking his highness, the Director of the Azhar University. 'University'. He didn't like using the word 'madressa' (school); so they chose the word 'jamià'. They call it 'University of Azhar'. Not one time did the Prophet (saws) say 'jamià' (university). Why did the Proph-et (saws) give us news about Juhayna? This is an important point, a very important point. Allah Almighty said, “Faqad Ja'a 'Ashratuha”. Sadaq Allahul Adhim (Allah Almighty has spoken Truth) that signs of Judgment Day have already come. And the people may ask, "What are these signs?". People may ask. Their answer is in the Holy Qur'an: “Fas'al Bihi Khabiran” (ask the expert). Is there any in-dication who that expert is who can give us an answer? Yes there is. If you would like to know about the reality of the matter that you are asking about, go to Juhayna to ask. He has the Certain News “al-khabar al-yaqin”. Go to him!

Did Juhayna pass away? Did he live? Is he alive? Or is he hiding? Where? If he is hidden, the Prophet (saws) would not have said that the Certain News is with Juhayna, pointing to Juhayna. Prophet (sas) said, "Ask where Juhayna is so that he may give you the al-khabar al-yaqin, the Certain News". You don't have the al-khabar al-yaqin. Oh Azhar scholars, you don't have the expertise. But the expert knowledge is with Juhayna. Juhayna has the al-khabar al-yaqin, the Certain News. Ask where he is. Who is Juhayna? In every period of time, there must be one Juhayna. If you like, ask about him. Who is he? Ask about him. Who is Juhayna? Where does he live? From whom did he learn? How did the Prophet of the end of times (sas) speak about him? From his Prophecy he said, "Juhayna possesses the al-khabar al-yaqin." What is that al-khabar al-yaqin, the Certain News? Is it about the entrance of beasts into Madina? What is the benefit of this information for us? These words are general to the whole nation. At every period of time there are beasts who come among the ummah and enter into the city of the Prophet (sas)! Ask about Juhayna. Who is he in reality? Why you are not saying anything about this, oh Shaykh of Azhar? Why is he not declaring who is Juhayna and what is meant by that Hadith? Is he claiming to be Juhayna? If you are not claiming it, then where is Juhayna? The words of the Prophet (sas) words are for the whole nation. Why is Shaykh of Azhar not asking? Why is he not showing us who Juhayna is in our current times? Why doesn't he declare and explain?

What is halal (permitted) is clear and what is haram (prohibited) is clear. Everything has been made clear in some way or another, like in the powerful Surat in the Holy Qur’an called Al Bayyinah (98). Is it there? Yes. This is an ocean. Surat Al Bayyinah (98) is an ocean. It makes everything clear. If you don't know anything, then enter into such an ocean and you may find everything there. Surat Al Bayyi-nah. It has been made clear (tabayyinah). What is wise has been made clear and separated from what is false. Allahu Akbar! Wisdom is separated clearly from falsehood. Surat Al Bayyinah. Why did they call it "Bayyinat"? Say! Let's hear the Head of the University of Azhar (Shaykh Ahmad Al Tayyeb) say! Who is that one, who has such a clarity and ability to discriminate (bayyinah) that you can go to him and learn from him? Learn from him! Go!

Why are you collecting the worst of the people, the lowest level of people, the worst people who do not understand the Orders? They don't think. They do not understand and they are not trying to learn the Heavenly Orders from the Divine Domains. Why are you collecting such people and consulting with them? The consultant must be somebody trustworthy, someone who you can depend upon. Which one of the people you collected is trustworthy, oh Shaykh Al Azhar? From every sect of society you have collected one person. What do they have to offer that makes you ask for consultation? What do they possess? They have nothing! How is it that you have gathered those who have no wisdom, and no understanding; and you are consulting with them? Ask, oh Shaykh of Azhar! Ask Juhayna because the al-khabar al-yaqin is with him. Ask, ask from him! Where is he? Where is he, oh Sheikh of Az'har? He will not come to you and tell you, "I am Juhayna", no!

Did you read the Holy Qur’an, oh Sheikh of Al Azhar? I hear that you are reading and reciting the Holy Qur’an. Have you read the story of Prophet Moses (as) with Khidr (as)? Did you read it? What do you think? How do you see? Did Sayidina Al Khidr (as) go to Prophet Moses (as) and tell him, "I am Khidr"? How much the Prophet Moses (as) tired himself, exhausted himself, until he reached Khidr (as). [It is not a matter of just] sitting in your chair and waiting for Juhayna. No! You will not be granted [meeting Juhayna]! You will not be granted. No! You must learn. Do not say, "I am the Sheikh of Azhar. I know everything". If it was true, then the Prophet (saws) would not have said, "Seek knowledge from the cradle to the coffin". Ask! Seek knowledge! Are you, Shaykh Al Azhar, seeking knowledge? Are you a scholar? In seeking knowledge, how is your situation? Are you now learning, or not? Arrogance (qubriya) has overtaken the the scholars of this world. There are scholars who have knowledge and there are scholars of the market place. The scholars of Akhirat (hereafter) are one type, and the schol-ars of dunya (this world) are something else. The scholars of this world do not know these Truths. They wake up ignorant and they go to sleep ignorant. Allahu Akbar!

I heard that the head of the scholars of Azhar Al Sharif collected people from every group that have no mind and no sound opinion. He gathered them to consult with them. There are people who can be consulted in such matters – consultants! Allah Almighty ordered His Beloved Prophet (saws). wa- shaawir -hum fe al- 'amr (3:159) … and consult with them upon the conduct of affairs.

Consult with them in your affairs. The Prophet (sas) did not go to the people in the marketplace to con-sult them. No! But, "Ask the expert" for a consultation. Who are these groups of every sect that you collected and that you are consulting with and they are saying something and you are saying some-thing else? And you know, oh head of Azhar al Sharif, that what is prohibited is made clear and what is permitted has been made clear. Let them judge what is Halal and what is Haram, those people that you have gathered from every caliber of sect. Consult with them. The consultant, the person that can be consulted, must be an expert. They must possess expertise. You should not gather people from every sect and consult with them when none of them qualifies as a consultant. Why are you collecting them? Allah will judge you. Allah will give His judgment on you. Allah (jwa) will question you, oh Shaykh of Al Azhar! For the purpose of consulting and to take a consultant, you must search to find the trusted and worthy ones who may be consulted among the ummat. [Search] who is possible for you to consult or you will be punished. You will be punished, and your punishment will be heavier than that of the rest of the people.

I regret having to say such words. My Arabic is limited. You must understand. You can arrange my words in such a way that you can gain an understanding. If you find that I made a mistake, say, "Oh Shaykh, you made a mistake in this point and in that point". Then bring your proof and evidence that I have spoken wrong or without authority. Be firm on proof. If I have made a mistake in your eyes, I will ask forgiveness from my Lord. But that has not been proven. This is my station. You are claiming that your stations are close to the level of the Divine Throne. So, bring your proof. Bring your evidence. Show us your station. Our station is visible (zahir) to us and clear. If we discuss the matters of Islam and the nation, do we bring men from all sectors of the society to consult with them? This indicates our station, our level. The perfection of a human being is made clear by his words! If you are speaking words of wisdom, how can you select people from all sects when not one of them accepts the other? All of them are far astray from the Powerful Shariat, the Shariat of the Beloved (saws) of Allah! Wake up! Wake yourself up! And awaken the people. Then you will be saved. If not, you will burn yourselves out. Say astaghfirullah. Say astaghfirullah!

We say astaghfirullah. We repent and turn to Allah. Ya Rabbi. Put our affairs in order. Send us one who may put our affairs in order, who can put order to rule our lives in accordance with the powerful Shariat. We do not accept anything except the powerful Shariat. Never! We do not accept anything that is opposing the Shariat. All the scholars should submit and follow the Shariat of Allah. We do not want anything except the Shariat of Allah. We do not ask opinions from the different levels of society. No! We have in front of us the Holy Qur’an. Ya waylana (woe) to you, oh poor one who leaves the Haqq (True) way and who follows the ways of shaytan. You will be burnt out here and hereafter. I say this to you and astaghfirullah for me and you. Good news for those who ask forgiveness and say as-taghfirullah, astaghfirullah. Fatiha.

Shukr Lillah, Allahu Akbar....
