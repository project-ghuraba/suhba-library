Aū‘dhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-Shay"āni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Ra!māni r-Ra!īm.

Wa#-#alātu wa s-salāmu ‘alā Rasūlinā Mu!ammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti A#!ābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā

Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-F’āiz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Mu!ammad Nā$im al-%aqqānī. Madad.

All people are the servants of Allah and are His creatures. Islam sees them with mercy. Islam treats them with mercy. In Islam, Muslims are commanded to treat them with mercy. Islam exists not to harm anybody, but to do good.

The religion of Islam is the last and final of all the heavenly religions, completed and perfect. As the Holy Prophet (SAW) said in his last sermon, the religion has been completed, which means the religion from the Prophet Adam (AS) to the last prophet Muhammed (SAW). And that is for the benefit of humanity, not in order to harm anybody, but to do good, and for their own good. People who go out of this path are capable of doing anything. After all, when one is an infidel, nothing according to them is wrong. They torture everybody, they demolish, burn and kill, and murder for their benefits and their egos.

There is no such thing in Islam. Islam is to help everybody, and to do good for everyone. There are battles and wars, so that oppression might be put to an end. Their [Islamic] wars were to benefit humanity, so that they might be saved from those evil situations, from the evil doings of those people, so that they might teach good, and become the means of goodness. Now we see throughout the world that there are evil people doing bad. Because of what they do, people are fighting against illnesses and deaths. This was not caused by Islam, but by these disbelievers, by those who are the army of Shaytan. They do it all together with Shaytan. [But] no matter what these do, by the will of Allah, those who are with Allah are going to have the final victory. What the others do becomes vain.

So Muslims should not worry. Our end will be good, insha’Allah. The end of those who do evil is not good, but bad. May Allah protect us. May Allah help us. Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-F ti ah
