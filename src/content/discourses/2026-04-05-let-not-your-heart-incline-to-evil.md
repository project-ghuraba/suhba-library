As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُدَٰفِعُ عَنِ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓا۟
<br>“Inna Allaha yudāfiʿu ʿani l-ladhīna āmanū”<br>
“Truly, Allah defends those who believe.”(Qur’ān 22:38). ṢadaqAllahu l-ʿAẓīm.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says, “He defends and protects those who believe.” This is His true promise. Believers, those who possess true īmān, are under the protection and guardianship of Allah. They have no other occupation than the remembrance (dhikr) of Allah and worship of Allah. Whatever others strive to do, Allah will protect them. They are under the protection of Allah, they are with Allah.

Therefore, whoever is with Allah is always saved. They are always victorious, triumphant. They are the possessor of all good. Whoever is not with Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla will face all kinds of difficulties, big and small.

Therefore, being on the way of Allah is a great blessing, a great virtue. Because Allah guides whomever He wills and withholds from whomever He wills. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla guides whomever He wills.

Today, people boast about the evil they do. They think they are doing something good. However, what they do only harms themselves. Of course, it also harms others. When it harms others, the responsibility for it falls upon them. And they will certainly receive their punishment. Therefore, whoever is with Allah is always in goodness, beauty, and all kinds of beneficence.

Therefore, one’s heart mustn’t incline towards evil. Because shayṭān makes evil appear good and good appear bad to people. May Allah protect us. May He protect us from the traps and evils of shayṭān. May Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla not separate us from His protection and from that īmān. May He strengthen our īmān.

We are in the end times; a thousand things can put doubts into a person’s mind, planting false thoughts in their head. Therefore, it is necessary to always remain on the way of truth. If you have chosen the way, hold on to it firmly. 

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says,<br>
وَٱعْتَصِمُوا۟ بِحَبْلِ ٱللَّهِ جَمِيعًا وَلَا تَفَرَّقُوا۟ ۚ
<br>“Wa-ʿtaṣimū bi-ḥablillāhi jamīʿan wa lā tafarraqū”<br>
“And hold firmly to the rope of Allah all together and do not become divided.’’ (Qur’ān 03:103). Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says, “Hold fast to the rope of Allah, to His way, and do not be divided.”

May Allah protect us. May we always remain steadfast on this way, inshā’Allah.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
