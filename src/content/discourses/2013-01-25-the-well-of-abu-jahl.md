As-Salamu Alaikum Ya Hadhirun. Salams on those who are present. Salam/peace descends from Heavens. May we be granted from that Salam also. Allah Almighty greets His servants with Salam. Allahu Akbar! Allahu Akbar wa Lillah al Hamd. Shukur our Lord. Allahu Akbar. Today is Rabi-ul Awwal, the month of the birth of our Prophet (sas), the night of his holy birth- we celebrated it. With it came joy on us. Glad tidings came to us. Power came to us. There is no one who opens his hands & returns empty handed. There is no one who goes to the door of the Sultan & returns empty handed. O Lord, may this ignorance leave us. It has become "Ignorance covered the world. If you ask they don't know faith." We ask Your help & guidance which will save us from ignorance for the honour of Your Beloved. We ask & expect from the Beloved (sas). Our Umm Hiram Sultan also sends us support. May the honour of the honorable Companions get more & more.

As-Salamu Alaikum Ayyuhal hadhirun. O who are present, salams to you. Dastur Ya Rijal Allah! O mukhlas/sincere servants of Allah, O who love Allah, O holy ones who live on the way of Allah's love! We ask your support. May you send us support. We may also begin, our way is the way of sohbat. What is sohbat? Sohbat is the assembly which injects every person with the strength he needs. When you run out of gas in your car, you go to a gas station. You go, fill up your car at the station & go on driving. The secret is in that station, it fills up something. When it runs out, you are finished & left. You start searching for gas. "My car ran out of gas. It ran out of gas & it is of no use now". With each sohbat, a new power is granted to our spirituality.

Our physical being will perish & become earth. Our reality does not die. Our physical being will die. It will be buried. It will become earth. They say "it came from earth & returns to earth". Therfore everyday a new.. Yawm al-jadid, rizq al-jadid. You understand? Don't look here, there. Focus your attention here. Yawm al-jadid, rizq al-jadid. New day, new provision. When the children of Adam woke up everyday, the master of the house takes his bag, takes his basket. "Where are you going to, Yahu?" "I am going to buy our needs. I am going to the market". "What is there in the market?" "They have everything in the market. I may buy & come back. I may buy what is granted as our share &come back" This is for our physical being. Yawm al-jadid, rizq al-jadid. Rizq- there is the material provision for our physical being & there is the spiritual provision for our spirituality. Therefore man seeks provision for his exterior body and also for his spirituality he seeks provision. It is this provision that keeps us alive. It does not crush us & does not let us be crushed. It is a dragon! Our spirituality is a dragon. "Our sermon is sohbat" said the great ones. A huge plane flies & goes up in the sky. With what? Huge planes fly in the sky when they take the power they need. Astonishing. If you throw a stone, it falls back on the ground. I heard that there are planes that carry 1500 people, 1000 people. Don't corrupt your language. Say "tayyara", the old word for plane. Tayyara/plane, don't use the imitated word for it. We have honour. Say tayyara. Plane/tayyara is a vehicle. And us also, to make a short sohbat here for whosoever is present here- one person, two people.. whoever is present here, they may listen and "Innallaha yurkil hikmata ala lisan al waidhin bi qadari mustameen".

I don't know anything but they are making me speak. If there are donkeys in the assembly, givem them barley. If they are human, give food. If he is a great person, give something accordingly. Provision is of two kinds: for our physical being & for our spirituality. One whose animal side dominates, constantly looks for food on earth for his physical being, for his stomach. One whose spirituality is strong seeks from the Heavens, wants to rise to the Heavens. Subhan Allah. O believer, listen! We made our words a little bit scattered but really we are saying them to present the Bismillah. They call it dibaja, preface in the new imitated language. Is it not so? We made a preface so that we start the sohbat, tell about the importance of sohbat. What is the heading, preface of our sohbat?

I don't know anything. I don't claim to know. But the Mercy of the Bismillah rains on me. There are clouds in the sky. The cloud says "I am nothing" but it makes the mercy of rain. Without receiving the order "O cloud, water this earth" it does not rain even a drop. Allahu Akbar. He (swt) makes thousands, millions of tons of water wander in the sky & makes it fall as rain, as hail or snow over wherever He (swt) likes.

O foolish ones, why don't you ask "Who is He Who does this?" You don't ask because who asks is reasonable, he is human. Who doesn't ask, who doesn't seek but only asks for barley is not human, he is an animal. Aman Ya Rabbi, tawba Ya Rabbi. Allah Almighty in His Own Being is Hamid al-Majid. You should teach this. You should teach What & Who is the One Who created you. That you will have some familiarity, acquaintance with Him. The people of this time have no acquaintance with Allah, not at all! They are acquainted with donkeys, with the foolish like themselves but there is no one left who is acquainted with His Lord.

I am going to see doctors" "What benefit do you get from it?" "Well, they give me pills & injections. They make surgery. They inject me with new life". "It is not with the doctors. Because animals don't enter mosques. This is a new appearance.

"Have you ever seen an animal entering a mosque?" "No, they don't". "Who enters?" "Man does". "Congratulations! Do you enter?" "No, we don't". "Allahu Akbar, our army is always victorious". How beautiful. Tariqatuna as-sohbah. Therefore our way is sohbat. We are explaining. If they say "you say wrong" may they fall in the wrong well. You should take the hand, they would say like this before. You should take a hand, meaning you should find beautiful servants who know the Lord of Heavens & Earth & who keep His Orders so that you take their hand: "O the servant who is honoured with his Lord's service, save us too" This was the meaning of taking a hand. Abu Jahl wanted to make our Prophet (sas) leave the world, meaning "let him die and we will be saved from him". Abu Jahl had a well dug in front of his door. He kept himself, imprisoned himself at home saying "I am sick". He rested in his bed & had a well dug in front of his door. He covered the top of it, its surface so that whoever comes will fall in it. He thought of this with his little mind, Abu Jahl. Then? "Then what happened Shaykh Effendi? Tell us its end" "O foolish one, listen to see what is its result, so that you draw a lesson from every story". He put his wooden bedstead..Let us not say bed, before they would say bedstead. So he had his bedstead put in his room, right next to the window & he spread the news. He said "Tell people that Abu Jahl is sick". Why did he do this? Because he would say for the Prophet (s) "he is an orphan". He is the Master of the Universe, how can you call him orphan? But his little mind was only this much. He said "I got sick. And that orphan goes to visit sick people. I had a well dug in front of my door & covered its top lightly. The moment he steps on it, he will fall into the well & we will finish him.

He was laying like this in front of the window. Really our Prophet would visit the sick people. Why? "Maybe his heart is softened. Maybe he accepts faith from fear of death". So our Prophet went to visit him also, saying "maybe he will come to faith". Right when he reached this well, our son Mehmet Effendi, he was about to take one more step on it when Jibril (as) came & said "Return O Muhammad! (sas)" And our Prophet (sas) returned without stepping on it. Seeing this, Abu Jahl got surprised. Saying "Let me run after him & call him back" he, himself fell into that well! He started crying for help. "What happened to our master?" Mushriks came saying "what happened to our Master? His voice comes but his self is not seen". They searched & searched & finally they saw that he is calling from inside the well. And the well was not that tall. Only the height of one or two men.

He said "take my hand & get me out of this" And they stretched out their arms. He was in the reach of their arms. They said "Give us your hand" He said "I am reaching out". Those ones are stretching out their arms from up, then? "Your hands are not reaching. Bring a rope or something". So they brought rope & stretched it out. Abu Jahl is crying "It is not reaching. I am going to be drowned here!" They brought rope & stretched it out. "It is not reaching!" What else? "Find another rope". So they found another rope & threw it down to him. He said"It is not reaching. It is not reaching. I am dying here. Look around in the neighborhood. Let them bring a rope & save me". They brought all the ropes in 7 neighborhoods & threw them down to him. Crazy Abu Jahl cries "Yahu it is not reaching.." They stretched out all the ropes they had but nothing. Finally he said "O my soldiers, look. Even if you bring me all the ropes in the world, they won't reach But him, bring him to me". He would call him "the orphan of Abu Talib". Astaghfirullah. Our Prophet, Sultan al-Anbiya (sas). O my beautiful Prophet.. They called him, our Prophet Sultan al-Anbiya, Habibullah.. They called him & he said "let me go & see what he will say".
