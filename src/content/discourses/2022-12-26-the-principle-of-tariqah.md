As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Our ṭarīqah is the way of our Holy ProphetNone by permission of AllāhNone. It is the way to himNone, going on hisNone way. The du‘ā’ of our Holy ProphetNone tells about the way that Muslims must follow. To make it always... It is the principle of our ṭarīqah, in shā’a Llāh.

"Allāhumma a‘inná ‘alá dhikrika wa-shukrika wa-ḥusni ‘ibādatika wa-lā taj‘alnā mina l-ghāfilīn." This is du‘ā’ of our Holy ProphetNone. May AllāhNone help us. Help us in what? To remember, always to remember AllāhNone by making dhikr and to make shukr. Being thankful (shukr) to AllāhNone is a great blessing. With shukr AllāhNone increases goodness, favours, and barakah. The more you make shukr to AllāhNone, the more benefit you get. And then "ḥusni ‘ibādatik" – "to worship in the best way." To worship in the best way is to worship on time with sunnah and with mustaḥabb (virtuous acts). Whatever sunnah there is, to perform them and to present them in worship in the presence of AllāhNone is a great gift. Not everyone can do it.

And then, the du'ā' says, "Don't make us among the heedless ones." There are many heedless people. People who spend their lives in heedlessness are in the majority. They are not to say many but the majority. Few people are not heedless. Many people are heedless. Heedlessness is not a good thing. They waste their lives. Not that it is the same, but some people get on the bus and sometimes they fall asleep. They miss their stop. They go all the way until the last stop and wake up in confusion. "O my God, where am I?" Heedlessness is like this. It is something similar to this. A couple of minutes they lose, even if they lose five to ten minutes or an hour, it is nothing. But people in heedlessness lose all their lives. They waste them uselessly.

Therefore, this du‘ā’ is the basis of our ṭarīqah. It says, "May AllāhNone help us." "Allāhumma a‘inná ‘alá dhikrika." "A‘inná" means, "May AllāhNone help us," so that we are not defeated by our egos. With YourNone help, with YourNone support, with the willpower YouNone give, we will do everything easily with love and enjoyment, in shā’a Llāh. These are the most important things for people in ṭarīqah. To always remember AllāhNone, to make dhikr of AllāhNone, to make shukr to AllāhNone, and to perform the worship of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla in the best way. We must accept such worship not as a burden but as a gift. When people pray, it is not to just do it as a duty and as if it is a burden. It is great happiness to wait for it and to be in the presence of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. You should pay attention to it and understand it like that. Many people pray quickly and hastily. No. It is good too. Certainly, it is good to perform farḍ prayers. But praying better, praying in a more accepted and virtuous way has more virtues.

May AllāhNone make all of us successful in applyng this du‘ā’ of our Holy ProphetNone, in shā’a Llāh.

In our ṭarīqah, all ṭarīqah, but Naqshbandī ṭarīqah, we are in this ṭarīqah with most distinguished Tarīqah, way of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, way of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, way of happiness, way of cleanliness, way of every good thing. And there is for praying from Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, we all the time repeat it, but we must, this is our ṭarīqah principle: Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Allāhumma a‘inná ‘alá dhikrika wa-shukrika wa-ḥusni ‘ibādatika wa-lā taj‘alnā mina l-ghāfilīn."

This is, "O our Lord help us to be mentioning YouNone." Make dhikr, dhikr meaning mentioning, remembering Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. And when you remember AllāhNone, you must be doing everything with good, to not make bad thing, and to be nashkurak, to give our thanks for AllāhNone. It is, to thanking AllāhNone, it is big benefit for you, for spiritual and for material also. Because when you thank AllāhNone – shukran liLlāh – making, AllāhNone make it, what HeNone give you from good thing, increase; make give you more and more and more, every time more. Every time you say thank you for AllāhNone, AllāhNone HeNone is Generous, HeNone give you more and more, from dunyā and from ākhirah, in shā’a Llāh.

And for "ḥusni ‘ibādatik" to be make good worshipping for YouNone. AllāhNone orders us to worship HimNone. There is of course, in twenty-four hours, many things but the most important – five time praying, to be make it nicely, acceptable what AllāhNone like it, as much HeNone... You must know you are the nearest time for you, to be in praying with sajdah, in rukū‘. All this thing, you must be happy HeNone accept you, HeNone not refuse you. If you go to some people who in government or rich people, you must, you want to make meeting. Because you are not important, no any benefit for them, they will not accept you. They will, even they not saying any excuse. Just say, "Send him away. No need. I don't like to meet this man," he said. But Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, whole this universe whole this, HeNone is owner for all this, and HeNone accept you five times and you are running away. This is, we must think about this. And "ḥusni ‘ibādatik" (make it nicely) [means] to make it with sunnah, with tasbīḥ, with mustaḥabb. As much as you can do it – better, better for you.

And don't be "wa-lā taj‘alnā mina l-ghāfilīn". Don't be, make us forgetting or heedless. Do not make heedless, because heedless people, they are majority in the world. Very few people they are knowing and they are doing good thing for themself. For other also, they try to do something good, but the most people, heedless. Heedless people they spend whole life in nonsense things, and no benefit. At the end, they will go from this world without any benefit, with big losing. They lose their life. They lose this life and other life also.

This is our ṭarīqah. We try to do this, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone help us. This is because the most important, saying ProphetNone, "Allāhumma" – "O our Lord, help us to do this. We know this, we are, we cannot do. Do not make us cheated by our ego, by shayṭān, by dunyā." AllāhNone help us to be in this way, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
