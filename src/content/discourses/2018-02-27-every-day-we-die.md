As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa Raḥmatu Llāhi wa Barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīn wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla is taking and giving back people's lives every day in order for them to take a lesson. They depart from here, they leave their bodies. When they wake up in the morning, their souls return to their bodies. They travel all around and when it is night again, souls go to the spiritual worlds again. If AllāhNone wants, HeNone puts it back. If not, they pray janāzah (funeral prayer) in the morning. But we have to make shukr to AllāhNone for living in Islām. We make shukr for these favours. Who makes shukr is thankful. Who doesn't, should be.

It is in The Qur’ān also. When Qiyāmah comes, mankind will be as if they just slept and woke up. A hundred years, five hundred years, a thousand years – no matter how long they'll be asleep, on Qiyāmah they will open their eyes. And in this world, for people to be an example, AllāhNone showed Aṣḥābu l-Kahf. They ran away and sought refuge in the cave. 300 years had passed. They spent 309 years there. When they woke up – they felt weakness in their bodies because they were very tired – they wondered how long they were asleep and said, "We must have slept at least for a day. Maybe a couple of days out of tiredness from running away from the people." And then when they went down to the city to get some food, they found out that 309 years ago they lost these young men. They had been asleep for 309 years. Aṣḥābu l-Kahf. They slept in the cave. They assumed they had slept for a day or two to three days.

Also ‘Uzayr ‘alayhi s-salām, he contemplated and wondered how this may happen. He wondered how a man can die and be returned to life. Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla caught his soul immediately. And he had a donkey, it died too. After a hundred years AllāhNone returned his ['Uzayr's] soul to make an example and he woke up. He looked around and didn't understand anything. Because he was a prophet, revelation came to him – "WeNone captured your soul for a hundred years and you remained lifeless here. You only had your body. And to show you an example, look how decayed your donkey is. It is only bones." Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla slowly gave it flesh, blood vessels, and nerves and revived it in front of the Prophet ‘Uzayr (as). It is a big example for people. Qiyāmah is not far away. You close your eyes and open them on Qiyāmah. Your sleep won't give you any benefit. You should prepare before you sleep. You should do your good deeds and be on the right way before you die. For when you close your eyes, you will open them on Qiyāmah. Then you will be questioned. This is the wisdom of AllāhNone. When someone falls asleep, he is not aware of anything. Some people cannot sleep but when they do, same things happen. AllāhNone is making us experience this event every day. HeNone is making us die and reviving us. "Yuḥyī wa yumīt" (3:156). May AllāhNone give our last breath with īmān. May we open our eyes with īmān on Qiyāmah, together with our ProphetNone, in shā’a Llāh.

Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla HeNone give us example – every day HeNone make us to die and after you come again to life. Nighttime when you are closing your eyes, your soul AllāhNone take it and going around spiritual world. All night we going seeing that and seeing this. Whole night you see dreams, you are free from your body. When it's coming morning, AllāhNone HeNone send it back – your soul to your body – and you open your eyes and you are continue life, this life. This is for these people who are not accepting life after death – every day AllāhNone make us to die and HeNone make us to make a new life. Each day "yawmun jadīd, rizqun jadīd" Mawlānā was saying – a new day, a new rizq, a new life. And there is many example for and this is what, when... And if HeNone want, nighttime if HeNone take your soul, if HeNone not send it back to your body, this morning make janāzah for this body. No where is his soul, not coming back. It's gone and not coming back. Allāh HeNone don't want, so –

But al-ḥamdu liLlāh every day HeNone send for who must live more to think and to thank AllāhNone for every day. And there is many example for this, especially for this Aṣḥābu l-Kahf. There is famous Seven Sleepers. They was young boys and they know finding truth, finding to worship Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla. But king he was angry with them and they run away. And very far they running and they going to one cave to rest. And after they said, "We will continue to run away from this." But they was so tired, AllāhNone make them to sleep 309 years. They sleep in this cave and when they wake up – because they was very tired when they are running away – asking, "Oh we are too much, what, what happen? How many days we, how many time we sleep here?" asking each other. They said, "Maybe one day at least we are sleeping, maybe more than one day because we are very tired and our body, we from not feeling how many day we are." And after they send somebody to town to bring food. In this town they know they sleep 309 years but they [the Sleepers] only they thought one day.

And there was an other story also of this what happen with Prophet ‘Uzayr (as). What is ‘Uzayr in English? ‘Uzayr (Ezra). Ha same. He also he is prophet and he was going with his donkey, traveling from town to town to teach people. And he was wondering how AllāhNone in Day of Resurrection how HeNone will make us to come again from earth, from dust, to create us in that time. Allāh HeNone take his soul from his body. He stay there hundred year. Donkey was – because he is prophet nothing happen for him – but donkey they finish. All dust and little bit bone from his donkey. When he wake up and said, "What happen here?" and he saw. He AllāhNone send him message, "You slept here WeNone make you to sleep here hundred year." Hundred year – but he thought he only one day. And HeNone said, "Look around. You look for your donkey what happen. Look for this area how it is change." And HeNone said, AllāhNone order him, "Look to your donkey," and donkey become slowly, slowly bone, after coming vein and coming meat flesh and coming meat and coming skin on his. When he was looking for this donkey and after donkey come same as he was before in hundred year.

This is, not for them, for whole human being to accept reality and to prepare themself for real life, life after death. Because when you are, as we said, when you close your eyes even thousand of year passing, you are not feeling only you feeling when Day of Resurrection, the trumpet blowing, you hear and you wake up. "What happen now? We are..." "Ha you are here now, come give your answer." For this we are, we must be take lesson from everything. AllāhNone He didn't create anything without reason, without purpose and this is every day, every night we are taking this lesson but nobody care about this. Not only he said this is normal – "We can sleep, we can open, we will wake up." No maybe you cannot sleep, maybe you cannot wake up. So be careful and do what AllāhNone order you because the Day of Resurrection not far. When you close your eyes, when you open it will be there.

AllāhNone save us and to be, when you are wake up, to be with ProphetNone, with awliyā’u Llāh. In that day, horrible day, we be safe with them, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
