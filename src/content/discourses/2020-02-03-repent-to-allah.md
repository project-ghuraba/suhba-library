As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh. A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is saying, "Wa-tūbū ilá Llāhi jamī‘a" (24:31). All of you repent to AllāhNone. Mankind is sinful. AllāhNone created them to make mistakes and to forgive them. HeNone promised to accept their repentance. If you make a sin, do good after it, says our ProphetNone. "Wa-atbi‘ s-sayyi’ata l-ḥasanata tamḥuhā." When you do goodness after your sin, it will clear your wrong. No sin will remain. You will have repented and be as if you didn't sin. This is addressed to all people, not only to Muslims but also to unbelievers. They should repent and return to AllāhNone so that their wrongdoing is erased. AllāhNone forgives when asked for forgiveness. But if you don't ask for forgiveness from AllāhNone, AllāhNone will give you all kinds of hardship and troubles. HeNone makes people taste this in dunyā before Ākhirah. Evil brings nothing but evil. And goodness brings goodness.

All nations in this world, by the wisdom of AllāhNone, not just one place but all these billions of people, are afraid of this illness – wondering where it came from. From AllāhNone. Everything comes from AllāhNone. AllāhNone didn't give it to you as a reward. HeNone gave it because of your wildness, in order for you to remember HimNone. When you are punished, what do you do? You repent so that AllāhNone forgives you. May AllāhNone take this hardship off our shoulders. We always ask forgiveness.

May AllāhNone forgive us. May AllāhNone protect us. May it not be a punishment for us.

When you repent and ask forgiveness, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla turns sins into thawāb. Therefore, we are lucky to be Muslims, to be believing ones. Mankind doesn't know this. They think lucky people are those who live in Europe and who live in America. They are not lucky. Lucky are those who know AllāhNone, lucky are Muslims who believe in AllāhNone. The rest is nothing. Even if they live for fifty or a hundred years, people pass away from a little thing. A man can live in the biggest house in the world, in the richest country in the world, it has no benefit. Benefit is in faith. Lucky is the believing servant, a Muslim servant.

Don't bother yourself with other illnesses and sicknesses. Depend on AllāhNone and you won't care. AllāhNone is with those who are with HimNone. HeNone gives to those who ask for a cure. "Pray, and INone will accept your prayer." AllāhNone protects. May AllāhNone protect all of us. May AllāhNone protect Muslim servants. May nothing happen to any of them, in shā’a Llāh. Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.

As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying: Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Wa-tūbū ilá Llāhi jamī‘a" (24:31). Repent for AllāhNone all of you. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying because human being, AllāhNone create them to do wrong, to do sin, to do everything and, but to remember AllāhNone and to ask for forgiveness from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying, "If I... all of you, you be obedient people, INone will create people who making sin making to forgive them. INone like to forgive people."

So all this people, human being, not saying for only Muslim, for whole human being, "Repent, ask for forgiveness from MeNone. If you ask forgiveness, INone will give, INone am happy to forgive you." But people they are not asking. They thought they are right everything and not remembering even there is a CreatorNone, we must look for HimNone. Only what they are looking – for their, themself, how their bad desire it must be first thing before order of AllāhNone. You cannot prevent people to do bad thing but they prevent you to do good thing. This is our day's problem, and it is the most darkness days since beginning of humanity. No any time it was like this. The most... they are against everything, against nature of human being they are doing. And they are saying, "This is nature and we are doing what we, our ego like. This is freedom. You cannot say anything against this."

And Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla HeNone is punishing people. Now we see whole human being, billions, they are afraid. From what? From very small thing, from illness. Muslim, not afraid. He must be not afraid. Because he is lucky, he, AllāhNone create him Muslim. The most lucky one – who is believer and he is Muslim. But Muslim people also they not seeing themselves lucky. They think the lucky one who live in Europe, in America or in this world rich countries. Who live in poor country, they are not happy but they are also no mind. It is not lucky to live in Europe or to live in America or the rich, most richest country in the world. It is not lucky because they are also like you. They eating what you eat, maybe eating more, but they cannot eat [like] ten people. But people the lucky one who is believer, because after this life he will be happy. Other no, even in dunya also they don't have any rest, any happiness. Only by drinking, by using drugs or other thing, they are trying to make themself happy.

And now this coming, this illness, make them more unhappy. Muslim, AllāhNone prevent [protect] him. If you ask for forgiveness – because this is not mercy from AllāhNone, this punishment for human being. So who repent and coming asking for forgiveness from AllāhNone, AllāhNone protect him and, in shā’a Llāh, he be safe. But who are still going, looking for medicine from here, there, not remembering their CreatorNone, it will be for them difficult. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh we are, for this we are saying we are lucky. We are depend, tawakkul ‘alá Llāh, "Tawakkaltu ‘alá Llāh" (11:56). This is our saying, slogan. Our slogan, "Tawakkaltu ‘alá Llāh" (11:56). Tawakkal ‘alá Llāh. Depend on AllāhNone. If you depend on AllāhNone nobody can make anything for you. If you depend on your power or your money or your anything from this world, all this can be finished. But Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla He is One. HeNone is no changing for HimNone.

So you Muslim people, our murīd in East and West, don't be worry. Only be depend on AllāhNone and make ṣadaqah and say everyday, "Astaghfiru Llāha l-‘Aẓīm" at least 100 times. In shā’a Llāh we'll be safe all of us and this will affect only non-believer, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
