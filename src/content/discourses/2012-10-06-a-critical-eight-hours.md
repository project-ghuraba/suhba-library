Ya Salam!

Alhamdulillah, Alhamdulillah, Shukr Ya Rabbi, Shukr Alhamdulillah

We must be sure we are from and we must be sure to be shakireen. We have to work for it, to be shakireen. This one also needs work. To be from shakireen. We need to work for it because in these days nobody appreciates what he got. And nobody appreciates what Allah Almighty grants him. And, nobody appreciates what he has been taking or what he is spending or what he is doing. For walking itself, it is not enough to make shukr all your life, only for walking, to use your feet. What about the rest? What is Allah Almighty granting? What about Allah Almighty’s giving?

Alhamdulillah, we are humble people. We have only this amount of shukr. Ya Rabbi, don't look at us as adult, or big people, or grown up people. This is what we have. And there is a saying,

idha aradta an tuta' fa'mur bi'l mustata'

That means, if you want somebody to obey you, ask him that which he can do and what is possible (for him) to do. That is very important. So many people they tell you,

“You take one ton on your shoulder”. Yahu, nobody can do this. “Okay, 500 kilo”. Also, impossible. "Okay, 300 kilo". Yahu! 300 kilo also is too much. “So what you can carry?” “I am able to carry in my age and power, 50 kilo. But not for so long, also”. Okay, we give you 50 kilo and put it on your shoulder all the time.

He cannot. That is why Allah Almighty separated the day into 3 chapters. One chapter is the sleeping time, very important. Doctor, it is very important, the sleeping time, eight hours. you are allowed to sleep eight hours. This is normal. Allah Almighty make it three pieces. And the second one, say okay purpose for living you need to work. So He gave you eight hours to work. This is known. And you have eight hours for worshipping Allah Almighty. Hanh -this is the critical eight hours. Some people say with these eight hours we can put our head in sajdah for Allah Almighty. Eight hours for Allah Almighty. We work and we sleep. Some people say that these eight hours are for our entertainment, so we use it as we like. So we entertain ourselves with these eight hours. As we like, it is ours. So this will be like extreme in this side and extreme in that side. Allah Almighty gave you these eight hours, belonging to Him. This is very sensitive eight hours. It is very critical eight hours.

They say with this eight hours you look after your family, you look after your parents, you make 'worshipping for Me', and all that is necessary things, that is keeping the social life in your life. And this eight hours actually, is the meaning of living. Allah Almighty didn't interfere with your eight hours work, He didin't interfere with the eight hour sleep. He give you this. He gives you 2/3 and He said, "I want 1/3. It is for Me". And this is the most important 1/3. To be useful for your community, useful for Allah Almighty things, be useful for your family, look to your children, this or that. He didn't include it in your work and rest. He said, "I am giving you".

So, if you steal from that eight hours, let us say, you work ten hours so you stealing from Allah Almighty's hours. So that is why Mawlana Shaykh was saying for people working so long, "After eight hours whatever you gain is for Allah Almighty, not yours". Allah Almighty will never forgive you for this. You steal these hours. So this actually is the basis of the family life. It is the basis of social life, it is the basis for distributing Iman, Islam, Tariqa with these eight hours. So when you are knowing that you have a free eight hours, it is actually belonging to Allah Almighty. So it should be for Allah Almighty's purposes.

With this one we start, idha aradta an tuta' fa`amur bi'l mustata’. We come from there because so many people are all the time burdening themselves with nonsense, with unnecessary weight. If you want your body to serve you, you have to give it a rest. And if you want your family to obey you, you have to give them the time. And if you want your work to be successful, you have to be on time. So this is one of dunya advices. We are dunya people. They are all the time telling me, "Yahu, you are dunya one". I say, Alhamdulillah, we are dunya one, we are living in dunya, we are not living in the other world. No, as long as Allah Almighty let us live here, we have to obey the rule that Allah Almighty put. That is very important.

The most important thing is to understand how to deal with dunya, how to deal with Akhirah. I don't have any knowledge, I am not the one having knowledge I am not the one searching in the book, not one looking up but I am only looking from the point that makes the life easier, and it makes it easy-going. Also, because people in this time are very hard. They have everything, they become harder, become more arrogant. They have everything, they become more proud. And when you are more proud, you are more hard and when you are more arrogant, you are more hard and when you are pushing, your push will be for nothing. So our advice, I am humbly sitting in this chair, I am not actually that humble, but humbly sitting in this chair. Why? Because Alhamdulillah I am not Shaykh. Shukr Ya Rabbi, this is very good thing. I am only here to fill the empty space with some entertainment, plus with this one maybe I have some things that Mawlana Shaykh wanted to send through me. So I am here just as you, as me. Me, me, me. But the importance here to look for dunya things also and also the concept, to enjoy what Allah Almighty gives.

wa amma bi n`imati rabbika fa haddith (93:11)

This is very famous ayah that means, if Allah Almighty grants you things you have to be happy with it, and you have to also push the people to do the same. It is very important, don't keep it for yourself. Say, "look I've done, you can do. I succeeded, you can succeed. I do this, you can do this". And, so many people look with envy. No need for hasad, yahu, no need. No need to be in envy. This planet is full with everything. Alhamdulillah these days nobody hungry, nobody who wants something, and he cannot buy it. Maybe he doesn't buy real things but can buy the imitation one. But at the end of the day he finds something similar. Very important.

So, if you cannot get real one, get the imitation one. Allah Almighty even gives you this opportunity. So try to get things that Allah Almighty grants everyone and don't be envious of somebody having something. You can have it. There is no need for Rolls Royce, could be Renault for example. Two of them, cars, and makes your things to happen. But if somebody has what Allah Almighty gave him more than you, say, "Alhamdulillah, look at him, I am proud that Allah shows His favour on him".

This is very important and then you will see that Allah Almighty will give you. But if you look in envy, "He deserve this one" "No, I don't think he deserves it". Allah Almighty gave him. How you can say this one? Just say “Whatever He gives somebody, Allah Almighty says this one deserves it”. This is definitely. Why? Because he is already in that favour. He has already been given so that means Allah Almighty granted, He gave him this favour, so you can say “Ya Rabbi, I am also asking”. Ask! The asking door is open. Allah Almighty didn't tell you to not ask. He says, "Ask, whatever you want, I am for you". But, if you don't deserve it, He doesn't give you.

This is Allah Almighty, you cannot interfere in His job. This is Allah Almighty, yahu. Careful. This is sensitive area. You are crossing the red line. You are in immigration, they say, "Don't cross the line". But it is very important to know where to stop. Ya Allah! I am stopping before the red line. I don't want to mess up. This Allah Almighty likes, truly, Allah Almighty likes when you are behaving in good adab with Allah Almighty. He likes it. But if you are without manners and without adab, Allah Almighty hates it. I mean it, He hates it. It is not that Allah Almighty doesn't like it, no, He hates it. So that is why when you see the red line don't cross it. This is ‘Immigration’, for Allah Almighty. So that is why we all the time keep saying: Astaghfirullah al Azim wa atubu ilayh. Very important even after the prayer, say:

astaghfirullah al azim wa atubu ilayh min kulli dhanbin wa m’asiyatin wa ma yukhalif al deen al Islam ya arhamar rahimin.

This is istaghfar, all the time pushing you from the red line. You understand 'hanh okay!' Because, we don't know. You see small children in immigration, crossing coming. And the police are fed up from them and say, "Keep your child, keep you child". So going, coming, we are coming we say: Astaghfirullah al Azim Ya Rabbi. They call me, ‘Astaghfirullah al Azim Ya Rabbi’. And that is why the istaghfar also is important in our lives. If we are able to do istaghfar just do it, without any reason, Astaghfirullah al Azim and make your tongue used to it. And with this one we say:

wa min Allahi tawfiq bi hurmat al Habib bi hurmat al Fatiha.
