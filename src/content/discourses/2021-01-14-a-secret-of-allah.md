Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Our Holy Prophet ﷺ states:

ُفَ َلَتخْ اُاهَ نْ مِ ُُرَ كَ اَنَتُامَ و

“The souls are troops collected together and those who had a mutual familiarity amongst themselves in the store of prenatal existence would have affinity amongst them, (in this world also) and those who opposed one of them, would be at variance with one another.” When the souls where first created on the day of ‘؟مكبربُتسلأ’ ‘Am I not your Lord?’ (Alastu bi Rabbikum), Allah ﷻ made the souls to get to know each other. Those who had a mutual familiarity amongst themselves then, have affinity amongst them now in this world, and those amongst them who opposed each other then, would also be divergent and have animosity towards each other here. That was the case on the day of the creation of the souls. This is a secret of Allah ﷻ. So whether souls have friendship or animosity towards each other, it was placed by Allah ﷻ on that day of creation. Sometimes you look and you wonder how some people get along very well with each other and others don’t, they were created by Allah ﷻ so they may get to know and meet each other and be together.

Thank Allah ﷻ that he created us as muslims and especially as those who are on the path of the Prophet ﷺ and the path of Tariqa. This is why we love this way; it is a favor of Allah ﷻ. Those who love each other here, loved each other on the day of creation of the souls, on the day of ‘؟مكبربُتسلأ’ ‘Am I not your Lord?’, and so this love continues in this life when we meet each other here. It’s a great favor and blessing because such people don’t like bad and ugly things such as treachery, non-belief, faithlessness and shamelessness. They’re such people that aren’t fond of these things and they try to stay away from people that have these attributes. And in the same way, these people [with bad attributes] don’t like good people and don’t take care of them or help them, but also try to finish such people as much as possible. They can’t finish these good people, but Allah ﷻ has placed such a state in this world that it just continues in this state.

In the hereafter, everybody will take what’s destined for them, whatever their Nasib (destiny) is. May Allah ﷻ keep our love continuous, and may we be with the good ones. May Allah ﷻ give guidance to those with bad attributes because guidance is from Allah ﷻ and nothing is hard for Him. Therefore, may Allah ﷻ help us and may we meet with good people always, may He send us good people insha’Allah.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq. Al-Fatiha.
