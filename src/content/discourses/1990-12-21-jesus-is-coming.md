Sheikh Mohammad Nazim Al – Haqqani An – Naqshibandi, Sohbat in English from 21 December 1990.

By the name of Allah Almighty All Merciful most Beneficient Allah! The Greatest! And you the smallest. Yes! Allah The Greatest... Not any limit for His Greatness. And no limit for His servants’ smallness. (Shakespeare English... Yes?) No limits for His Greatness, Allah The Greatest! Allah The Great. Say! Greatest! The Great...Absolute Greatness. No limits to His Greatness. We smallest. No limits for our smallness. One divided to infinity, what coming equal? Zero, our smallness zero, nothing else. La Hawla wa La Quwwata illa Billah Al ‘Alyi’ Al Azim. Allah Almighty opening onto you, on mankind, from His Divinely Lights, and we are coming into existence. Lights are coming on spots and [the spots] coming into existence. Through darkness, Divinely Lights coming on stars and stars going to be in existence. Taking [away] that Light, no more stars. The Divinely Lights on those spots making them to be seen as the stars through that dark darkness. And you are so small. Zero small! And Divinely Lights showing you and you are going to be seen in existence. When that light is taken from you, you are finished, disappear. Huuu Rabbi La Yazal, Habib Rasulullah (saws). Peace be upon him Beloved Muhammad (saws). The representative of that – The Great – can’t be small. May the wahabis’ hearts burst! Who is representing Haqq can’t be small! It is impossible. Must be great! If he (saws) is not great – I mean to say Sayidina Muhammad (saws) – if he is not great, never [would be] written his name on same line as the name of his Lord Almighty Allah. La Ilaha illa Allah Muhammad Rasulullah. Let them die of their spite – Wahabiyun. Yes! Therefore Muhammad (saws) – he is the greatest! Allah The Great and Muhammad (saws) the greatest. (Yes! It is true English? My English is true!) And that is honour. Therefore he (saws) is the pride of all universes. Therefore Prophet Sayyidina Muhammad (pbuh), he is the honour of whole (all) universes – not only one universe – whole universes from pre-Eternal up to Eternal which coming in existence. Don‘t think only one universe that Allah Almighty creating – universes, through every moment, through every second from pre-Eternal up to Eternal. Yes... Sayyidina Muhammad (pbuh) – he is the greatest. He is the honour of whole universes from pre-Eternal up to Eternal – what universes coming in existence, full with creatures, full with angels, full with mankinds. And Sayyidina Muhammad (pbuh) – he is honoured for whole universes. Hazir Rasulullah (saws). Therefore he is Muhammad (saws) the greatest. No measure for his greatness also. No one knows. Allah (swt) knows! Allah (swt) The Great and His Beloved one the greatest. The greatest – after Allah Almighty – he (saws) is the greatest among creatures, among creation. Therefore Sayyidina Isa (as) Jesus Christ (pbuh) was asking to be from his (saws) nation. To reach to that honour, being one of his (saws) ummah – Rasulullah (saws). And Allah Almighty granting this to him (as). Therefore he (as) is coming soonerly, landing from Heavens on Earth. I don’t know... Perhaps war coming, the biggest war coming, the armageddon coming, and after that Mahdi (as) coming, and after that dajjal coming, and after that Jesus Christ (as) landing on earth. Once again coming to earth to be with Mahdi (as), to be honoured being one of the nation of Muhammad (pbuh). We are lucky ones. We must say thanks, endless thanks, endless praisings to Allah Almighty that He (swt) chose ourselves to be in His Beloved one’s nation. Sayyid coming and asking from me dua for lottery – for lottery to reach the biggest prize. It is a chance to reach the biggest prize from lottery, but we can’t... We can’t imagine or we are never thinking on that point that the biggest chance that we have been given, we have been granted by Allah Almighty to be from the nation of Beloved Muhammad (saws). That is the biggest chance for ourselves. People are asking lottery prize. We have been granted without asking, Allah Almighty just chose ourselves to be from the nation of His (swt) Beloved Muhammad (saws), beloved servant, Sayyidina Muhammad (saws). We are not really giving the value for that grant from Allah Almighty to ourselves. Whole material things, if you have been given, it is nothing in front of that choice position from Allah Almighty to be His Beloved servant’s nation. Nothing can be equal to the value that we have been chosen to be the Seal of Prophet’s (saws) nations. And billions yet, they are not thinking on that point, to say so. Yet they are claiming to be Jesus Christ’s (as) nation or Moses’ (as) nation, with their ummah. And their value is just finished. The value, never ending value, is for those who they are from the nation of Beloved Muhammad (saws). Millions yet are not declaring the value of being from the nation of Beloved Muhammad (saws). And that is the main source of troubles on earth. Until they are declaring that value, they will fight to each other. They are never going to reach peace. When they are declaring and saying Muhammad (pbuh) is the greatest and his (saws) nation the most honoured nation, then peace coming. Finished! Write this with golden letters to put in East and West, North to South, everywhere. That is never going to be changed. I am not someone speaking from my stomach or I am not someone who is speaking through his mind. But I am someone speaking through my heart. Wa Min Allah Al Tawfiq, Bi Hurmat Al Fatiha.
