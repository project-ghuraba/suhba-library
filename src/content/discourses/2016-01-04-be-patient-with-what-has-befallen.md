Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Auzu Billahi Minashaitanir Rajim. Bismillahir Rahmanir Rahim.

“Qul lan yusibana illa ma kataballahu lana.” (Sura Tawba:51) Anything can happen while mankind is alive. What Allah (JJ) says is happening. When something happens it has happened already, Allah (JJ) has written it. To be patient with it is to show consent to Allah’s (JJ) destiny. Being patient with it is a cause for you to receive a great reward.

We need to be thankful to Allah (JJ) for everything that happens since something worse, something harder could have happened. Something worse can happen in everything. However, it is also a means for Mumins to rise to a station and gain rewards.

To bow to Allah’s (JJ) order is a great virtue and blessing for people, for Muslims, and for those who accept. There is no reason to say, “Why did we do this? How did it happen?” It happend with the predestination of Allah (JJ), and the time that passed cannot be changed.

If people think, for them to submit, thank Allah (JJ), and sit is a cause for their own comfort and, as we said, for their rewards to increase. Even if a mosquito bites, our Holy Prophet (SAW) says there is certainly a reward for that person. Accordingly, however much of a state has come upon that person, that much more Allah (JJ) gives its rewards and good deeds to that person.

That is why may Allah (JJ) protect us. If something befalls someone, Inshallah they are patient and firm. May Allah (JJ) protect from what is worse. Of course there is much worse than worse. We see what is happening and what is left in the world everyday. We are still to say we are fortunately thankful for our situation.

May Allah (JJ) protect and not test us. Once we are tested, Inshallah there is no rebellion but there is consent. May Allah (JJ) protect us all Inshallah. May you have a good day and good ages, years, and Mawlids Inshallah.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq.

Al-Fatiha.
