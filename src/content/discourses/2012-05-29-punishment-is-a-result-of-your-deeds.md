Shaykh Mehmet: As-Salamu Alaikum. Audhu Billahi min al shaytan al rajim, Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Assalatu wa s-salamu ala Rasulina Muhammad Sayyidi l Awwalin wa l-Akhirin. Madad ya Rasul Allah, Madad ya Ashabu Rasulullah, madad ya S Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, madad ya S.Muhammad Nazim al Haqqani, dastur! Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fil jamia' We may do a short sohbat.

May Allah grant all of us inspiration to do khayr/good deeds insha Allah. "'Innama Tujzawna Ma Kuntum Ta`maluna" (52:16). Insha Allah we didn't say the Holy Verse wrong. Most people have worries. Everybody has a trouble, has a problem. Some complain more, some less. How is it happening? Allah (awj) says, "For everything, you are finding whatever you did". This is the same for dunya, and same for akhirat as well. If you prayed, etc for akhirat, you find them there. If you did bad things, you also find it there. But this, Allah (awj) gives it to man in dunya, too. If one does good deeds in dunya, he feels peaceful. Even if he is poor or no matter how troubled he looks, he is happy inside. But when one does bad things, even if he owned the whole world, even if he bought 10 worlds like this he can't find the peace & happiness that poor one has in him- the one who does good deeds, not doing bad things. He always feels stress/sorrow inside. This is for big things.

But like us, everybody also has this thing. "Wa Ma 'Ubarri'u Nafsi 'Inna An-Nafsa La'ammaratun Bis-Su'i" (12:53) Because everybody has ego, he does things, knowingly or unknowingly. And definitely it brings some trouble/problems to people. Meaning, Allah (awj).. For dunya, everybody understands this as for akhirat. It is true for akhirat but the same applies to dunya as well.

A lot of people say "I don't pray but my heart is pure"..If you don't pray you already fall into sin. When you drink..Ok, you do everything and then you say "my heart is pure" but you are distressed, in troubles. You put here, look there..He says "I don't take other's property" but Allah knows what he does. This is different but even the people who pray, still many troubles, sorrows because they follow their egos.. If we try to give examples for this one by one it doesn't finish till the end of the world. It means, for sure not a goodness, not a good state was seen in that person that, that thing comes on him. Whether small or big, Allah gives everything's equivalent first in dunya, before Akhirat. Our Cypriots say, there is no hereafter, all is here-paradise is here, hell is here. It is here but there is paradise & hell in akhirat as well. Meaning, there is akhirat as well & the same is in dunya also. Allah makes you suffer here and also in akhirat. The real one is in akhirat but here is the small suffering. Most people say "I didn't do anything (bad)". This is because of the evil of ego. It doesn't accept any fault. People never accept their faults, imperfections. No one accepts. But definitely everyone has some thing, even they are little, that these things come on them. This is already..

What the Prophets, Awliyas suffer, they are not this way.. When they go through a suffering, they already feel as if they are in paradise. Even if you see it like a suffering from outside they do it only for showing, the Prophets or the..(awliyas). They don't have any sorrow inside, with the permission of Allah. You may ask "How come, do they have sins like us that they are distressed too?" No, they don't but they suffer for the pleasing Allah, for endurance.

But the common people like us.. Not to show you as an example but this weak & humble one in front of you experienced this many times. Even for the smallest things, you see that it's answer comes immediately. You say "ok, so this is the equivalence of that.." If man was to think about his mistakes, faults, everybody for sure will find. But most people don't see. Therefore may Allah forgive our sins, faults. So it is necessary to make always tawba, ask for forgiveness. Don't say "We don't have any mistakes". We are all sinners. So always to say "Astaghfirullah, Tawba Astaghfirullah". Always must keep on with Tawba & Istighfar that something (bad) doesn't come on us. Because there are many things we do knowingly & unknowingly. And the biggest cure is Tawba Istighfar.

Today we speak about, most of people they have difficulty in their life. Sometimes illness, sometimes accidents, sometimes.. many things happen for people. For people, they are asking "why this? why me?" Allah (awj), He say in Ayat al Quran "what happen to you, it is from you". Allah reward you what you do. This is, most of people they thought this for akhirat. Of course this for Akhirat. But for dunya also. Dunya also akhirat, small practising for akhirat.

What you do wrong, quickly you find wrong. You find bad thing in yourself, in, astaghfirullah, in family, in land, everything. You cannot give example for this because if we list this, end of year we cannot finish. People, most people in our, here Cyprus, they are not religious at all. And they say, "our heart is clean, it is enough". If it is enough, ok. But you after this, don't come & complain "why this happen for me? Why this I couldn't do, bad thing happen, why, why, why?" Don't ask if your heart is clean, it is enough for you. But it is enough for this, you get all this badness. When some good servant of Allah not doing bad thing, even he is most poor one in world he has in his heart satisfaction, you cannot find in the most richest of world. It was all this world for him, but he not doing good thing it is eating him from inside, fire. Even ten worlds like this, if he has he cannot find 1% from what this poor man has. So this is important. Allah giving people here, before Akhirat. And there was also Cypriot people, they are not believer most of them. They are saying "Hell & paradise in here, in dunya". Of course here & hereafter also. We accept this, not only here. Because one man here, he was before 25 years working in mosque garden. A neighbor coming & making a speech like this. And after, before one year he passed away. I think he saw there is paradise, or no.

So, many people because ego not accept wrong in herself, or in people, their ego they not accept wrong. All they say "we are right, we are right". But you cannot. You make many mistakes without knowing, or if you know you say ok. But after, this coming. And I am not give example for you. Just example from me, I.. many times if you do wrong thing, quickly I saw it is coming like warning, something happen like this. But when you are in right path, you will be in your heart satisfaction. You not fear from anybody. Many people maybe they fear, ashame from something they do. But when you are not doing wrong thing, you will be not fear from anything. Allah protect you.

This is most of what people, they difficulty coming.. Many difficulty, like maybe not married or difficulty with wife, difficulty with children,difficulty with job, other things.. We cannot give, millions of examples from this. Must be, there is something wrong. Without doing anything, you cannot.. When you didn't touch this button, not working. You must be by wrong, by without knowing or something, when playing you touch one button and this coming for you. After, don't ask where this coming. But for this, we must be regretful and make Tawba Asthagfirullah.

For this, to say Astaghfirullah, it is very important. We say everyday maybe with praying, with other nearly 1000 times we saying. And when second time you say this, you must say it what we do without knowing we make astaghfirullah for this also. Allah forgive us & take this what happen from us, or not come to us. We are not accept, we are not accept, and not insist on this wrong thing. We say Astaghfirullah.

Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, Al Fatiha.
