As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh. A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Innamā amwālukum wa-awlādukum fitnah” (64:15). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘Aẓīm. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says: Your wealth, your family, your children, and this world are a test for you. In order for AllāhNone to make them steadfast on HisNone way, for them to remain on HisNone way, you must pray to AllāhNone. You must raise them so that they become a means for goodness. Good children are the most valuable treasure for a person. Unless they have good children, even if they own the whole world, in the end, their children will neither say a Fātiḥah for them nor do any good deeds. They will waste everything you saved in bad places, and it will be gone. And you’ll be left with nothing. Therefore, raising good children is the best thing a Muslim can do. How do you raise them? Good children are raised with ḥalāl earnings. From the beginning, you must instil in them your religion, your faith, the love of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla and the love of our Holy ProphetNone. Our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says: The first thing to teach a child when they begin to speak is to say “Allāh”. Let the first word from their mouth be “Allāh”, so that their final word in life will also be that, and they may meet AllāhNone with that word, and be among HisNone beloved servants. When they become beloved servants of AllāhNone, then you’ve succeeded. They will bring goodness to you in this world, and goodness in the Hereafter. Because when people try to raise their children, they don’t know what to do. They think raising a child means giving them whatever they want, sending them to the best schools, and putting all the worldly things in front of them, thinking that will make them good children. But most of them don’t become good children that way. Very few turn out well. And even that is rare. They are not good because the ego never gets satisfied; it always wants more. So, when raising children, as we said, giving them everything is not good. You should give only what is necessary. You must teach them to be careful with money. You must teach them generosity. And they must know what is generosity and what is being careful with money. There are many things that Islām shows, and many things that our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam showed. You should not be harsh, nor should you be overly soft. There is a limit. You are allowed up to here. Beyond that, for your own good, you will have it later. If a child does not behave today, say, “I will give you what you want after a week, or after a month.” Do something good, and you will get it.” If they get it for free, it won’t be worth much. It has no value. What has value requires waiting, patience, and desiring it. What is given without desire has no value at all. It will be of no benefit to them, nor to you. Therefore, Islamic discipline is important. Today, they are far from Islamic discipline. And then they wonder, “Why are our children like this? Why are our children like that?” But some people are too harsh. Far too harsh. That’s also not suitable for the people of this time. If you are harsh, and the person of this time breaks, you won’t be able to reach them again. May AllāhNone grant them goodness. “My daughter took off her covering. She was a ḥāfiẓ. She used to wear ḥijāb, even the full covering (chador).” Because you forced her to wear the chador, once she found the chance, she dropped it immediately. So, don’t go that far either. Keep a middle way. “Ummatan wasaṭan,” says our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. When you follow the middle way, you can manage and carry on. They will know both the good and the bad, and they will know the value of things. May AllāhNone protect us. The time we live in is truly one of the worst times in the world. It is the end of times. So children must be raised well, and they must know why they were created. Some of them say, just to blame their parents: “Why did I come into this world?” It is neither your mother’s fault, nor your father’s, nor the world’s. Even if the whole world came together, you would still have come. Because it is the will of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla: your soul was destined to come to your body. That was decreed in pre-eternity. So don’t blame anyone. We are speaking to children and youth: they should not treat their parents badly. It is not their fault. It is no one’s fault. You came into this world by the will of AllāhNone. You must thank them, so that AllāhNone gives you what you want – so that you become a good Muslim, one of the beloved servants of AllāhNone. May AllāhNone protect all of us. Shayṭān targets them the most everywhere, in the east and in the west. All over the world, youth have become like toys in shayṭān’s hands. May AllāhNone help them, in shā’a Llāh. May AllāhNone protect and preserve them all. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Innamā amwālukum wa-awlādukum fitnah, fa-ttaqū Llāha mā staṭa‘tum” (64:15-16).
