As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Wa-ṭ-ṭayyibūna li-ṭ-ṭayyibāt” (24:26). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘Aẓīm. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says that good men are for good women. And bad ones are for the bad, says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Good people are beneficial for the world and for humanity. Their presence here, showing us the way, is the greatest favour. Those who follow them and walk on that way, those who meet them, attain many things. Even if they don’t meet, thousands of such good, righteous and beautiful people have come and gone.

All awliyā’ and prophets are ṭayyibūn. It means they are good ones. And ṭayyibāt refers to good women. Those who are on the path of AllāhNone, who serve humanity, who support their husbands, and who make that path easier and show it to others are also among them. Such people are rare. And their way is the way of light. People are asking for that. They ask for such people.

Shukr to AllāhNone, for most of us – I don’t know, probably there are not many, but few, who are left that met Ḥājjah Anne – but her service is enormous. That service has surely reached all of you. And it will reach until Qiyāmah. She was the helper of Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim. She was in his service. She served humanity.

Of course, she changed worlds twenty years ago. She wouldn’t want to see the condition of the world today. It was very good for her. Her passing twenty years ago was better for her than witnessing these conditions. Because of her compassion, she would not have been able to endure these days. Therefore, as she is from the arba‘īn (the forty saints), they took her away immediately. And she is still helping us in ākhirah.

A day before she passed, she had severe pain. When we say severe, it wasn’t extremely severe – more like a bad flu. One of our brothers had insisted that we go to talk to Ḥājjah Anne in the evening. We said she was sick and that we would rather not, but we did. And that morning, while we were resting, news came from there. They said Ḥājjah Anne had passed away. After a while, our brother Metin came and said that Ḥājjah Anne was gone. But before she passed, she went through Akbaba like a storm and left without looking back. May AllāhNone increase her maqām. Fātiḥah.
