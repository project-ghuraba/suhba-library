Sheikh Muhammad Nazim Al-Haqqani An-Naqshibandi, Sohbat of of the 22nd of September, 1993.

Audhu Billah min as-shaytan ar-rajim Bismillahi r-Rahman r-Rahim "Salamun qawlan min rabbin rahim"(36:58) Razaqana Allah. La hawla wa la quwwata illa Billahi l-Aliyyi l-Azim.

We must ask from Allah Almighty every time forgiveness. Every breathing you are in need to ask pardon from Allah Almighty. And every breathing we must try to give our praisings and thanks to our Lord Almighty Allah. It is important for everyone from mankind to praise his Lord, and to give his high respect for His commands and for His orders. That is real praising, to keep and respect, respect His commands. Allah Almighty's commands all of them they are respectful commands.

And man-made commands, man-made orders, man-made rules, they never carrying any respect among people. Therefore, whom they were practicing Holy commands of Allah Almighty as their rules - if rulers they were using heavenly rules, people, particularly believers, they were taking their high respect for that rules. Because they were believing that they are holy commands from Heavens and we must respect. And that brings peace to the hearts of people, to the minds of people. But, man-made rules that our rulers everywhere they are using rules, man-made rules. Man thinking and producing through their minds, through their views, through their intentions, secretly or clearly. And they are putting rules and never going to carry man-made rules any respect. Because they never carry any holiness through that rules. Never belongs to Heavens. Therefore people saying "why am I going to respect? I may think better than that. It is not that belongs to my belief, to respect, no!"

Therefore every day rulers bringing another rules, daily. If you may say, "you may full, you may fill White House 10 times with rules that, since America discovered has been discovered, perhaps 10 times on White House, maybe full with laws, with rules, that rulers are making, making, making and now in US no one happy with that rules. Every time they're electing another president and they hope that this one should bring something else. Ford went. Carter coming, Nixon going, Ford coming. Clinton not yet. Carter coming Reagan after coming. After Reagan, Bush coming, Bush going, Clinton coming. People looking "what is happening? We are thinking that should be Clinton better, as he was promising us before. But now we are looking - same routine going on, never changed." Yes. What you are hoping? Because rulers they are taking rules from people, from congress. Saying this, putting that, taking this, getting out to that, and same routine.

Therefore people never finding what they are hoping through their presidents. And presidents also before sitting on that chair, they were thinking that they may be able to change something, before. When they are sitting on that chair, they're looking that their hands are tied, their feet tied, their tongues also - there is what is this zipper. Not any time they can speak, only for occasions. Must open, speak then close it. Therefore nothing changing, presidents changing. I was with Roosevelt and before Roosevelt, after Roosevelt coming so many presidents. Perhaps I am reaching from US 15 presidents. But, yet even not keeping [even] 7 [of them] same level, but there is coming down. Therefore people must think on it. That what is the reason? And the reason is what I am saying. Because rules that rulers are using, they are putting that rules - man-made rules. And people they are in need of Heavenly rules. Heavenly rules may bring peace, because Heavenly rules are respected. But man-made rules never respected.

In Turkey 70 years ago they were using Heavenly rules through 1000 years and Islamic world also. But now, since from first war, first World War not second, Turks they refused to use holy rules from Heavens and they followed their first president, whom they filled everywhere with his statues like Buddha. In Buddhist countries you may find everywhere with every occasion they are making Buddha statues and new Turks also everywhere filled with statues of first president for them, whom taking away Heavenly rules, Sharia' and bringing man-made rules from Switzerland or from western countries. And people they were living in peace through the time of Empire. And now people eating some of them some others. Instead peace just came wars, miseries, fightings, quarrelling, even through their parliament they are fighting each other. And we are thinking that they are much more clever ones. And we are looking that those people if they are the most clever ones, what about others? If they are fighting with each other and swearing and cursing and making so many nonsense, silly things, how they can be able to rule countries?

Therefore now in Turkey no peace, finish! Because people they are not respecting man-made rules. And, in other Islamic territories also running after same way, they are taking away Sharia, holy rules from Heavens and bringing man-made systems. Making parliaments, and no parliament in Islam. No parliament for Heavenly Religions because they have Heavenly rules and parliaments only to make mind production rules. Man-made rules, parliaments, MPs they are doing rules. And Muslims they are not in need to do that. And I am surprising that Iran claiming that he is Islamic republic and they have a parliament, and I am asking for what their parliament? If you are saying Islamic territory, Islamic laws, Heavenly rules is ready. For what you are bringing this? And this is not Islamic system you are only cheating people to say "Islamic Republic". And no republic in Islam, but must be Kingdom. Because Heavenly rules gives authority to one person to practice whole Sharia.

And without Sultan, no Sharia and whole Muslim world they are in need one Sultan, not 100, 1000 people for Muslim territory to be under their rules, no. Therefore they are using man-made rules that gives them authority to be so many presidents, so many heads. But Heavenly rules if they are asking to use it, Heavenly rules saying to them "you must have one Sultan." Maybe others - wazir, ministers - but Sultan must be one. Ministers as much as it is necessary may be. And on behalf of Sultan may be, through different countries on behalf of Sultan, ruling with Heavenly rules - Rulers may be, but Sultan must be one. That is Sharia that is Heavenly rule saying also through Tawrat, through Holy Bible, old Testament, new Testament and last Testament. All of them saying that must be one ruler, one Sultan. If it is going to be two, one taken away, leave one. And what is the reason? It is well known that whole world just fall in troubles and miseries because whole rulers trying to rule people with mankind rules. They are saying for Heavenly rules "Get away! We are not in need of Heavenly rules, we may do, we may make rules." And they are doing rules and everything going to be down.

People must understand that point! They're understanding today, before tomorrow they should be winning, winners. They should be happy. If they are delaying, tomorrow they should carry much burden on their shoulders. But they must understand, if not understanding tomorrow, next week they must understand or next month they must understand, or next year they must understand. They should understand, no any way because their direction that whole world going on it, at the beginning of it, it was written, "Dead End". Dead End. I saw in America for some places, 'Dead End' [road sign] and I am not understanding what is dead end. That means as much as you are continuing, but finally you must come back, you can't find a way further - Dead End. They must come back. It is not enough, they are looking that this [there is a] sign like T. It is not enough for some crazy ones. Therefore putting by red writing, "Dead line, Dead End." They are saying: O... that color come now.

And sometimes written also - 'Wrong Way' [road sign]. Wrong Way I am seeing also in America. American people very clever ones, intelligent ones. And I am looking what is "Wrong Way"? It is like others. Then I looked that, when we, if you are continuing on that way, I saw that a lorry coming on you, Ah! That maybe an accident. Yes! The Lord of Heavens just mentioning us "Wrong Way!" But people, countries, territories, presidents, kings, everyone insisting on wrong way. Insisting. But one day must leave that direction, they must come back. They must come back, yes.

Once upon a time two kings they were fighting and years, years, fighting two nations, two kings. And finally people they were tired and asking, "For what we are fighting?" And they are saying that, once our kings, that late kings, they were sitting and they brought [them] eggs, eggs. And one of them he said that "We must break egg from this point." Second one saying "No, from down." "No, from this side." Second one saying "No from this.." Taking swords beginning war. You understand? Or sleeping? What am I saying? This war just began on that point. And people when they are asking "yahu! What is we are finishing from this war. What is the reason?" They are understand that it's so nonsense reason. One day should come to these people to ask "why we are going day by day to much more difficult conditions? Why?" One day they must ask, they must say to their rulers, "O our rulers with which rules you are ruling us? Before, our grandfathers and ancestors they were happier than ourselves. What is the reason?" They may ask and they may find the reason and they must come back to right direction.

No any hope from these rules that rulers using now, to save mankind. Only respected rules from Heavens, if they are using it back, whole nations should reach a peace, through themselves and through others. Or these troubles and fighting should continue. May Allah gives us good understanding. Good understanding, we are in need for that. To look and to know what is the reason. And that is the reason, it is so simple: Because people just left Heavenly rules and divinely punishment leaving them to punish men each other. Allah Almighty not coming from Heavens to give punishment to people but making someones for some others to make punishment for them.

May Allah forgives us and blesses you. Allah Allah, Allah Allah, Allah Allah, Aziz Allah Allah Allah, Allah Allah, Allah Allah, Karim Allah. Allah Allah, Allah Allah, Allah Allah, Subhana Allah, Allah Allah, Allah Allah, Allah Allah, Sultan Allah.

You are Sultan we are slaves. You are King, we are servants. O our Lord, send Sultan al Sharia, send Padishah for Islam.

Bi Hurmat Al Habib. Ziyadat li sharaf an-Nabi (saws) wa alihi wa sahbihi wa Mashaikhina, hadrat ustazina wa lana wal mu'minin Fatiha. Allahumma salli 'ala Muhammad Bismillahi r-Rahman r-Rahim al-hamdu lillahi Rabbi l-alamin Ar-Rahmani r-Rahimi, Maliki yawmi d-dini iyaka na'budu Wa iyaka nasta'inu ihdina s-sirata l-mustaqima.... Amin.
