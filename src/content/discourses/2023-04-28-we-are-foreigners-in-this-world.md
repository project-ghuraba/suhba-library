As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh. A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Every blessed word of our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam is a jewel. Every word heNone says is a light that shows people the way. They are words that illuminate the way. People who follow those words reach safety. Those who don't follow them will be destroyed. A holy word of our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says you pass from this world just like you pass on a road. You are like a visitor or a traveller in this world. So be like one, says our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, "Ka-annak ‘ābiru sabīl." Be like a person passing on a road. Pay attention because here is not our place to stay. Even if you stop, the way doesn't stop. It takes you onward. It takes you towards your death. Therefore, make sure to take benefit. The more goodness you can do, the more worship you can perform, do so, says our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam.

It won't wait until you feel like doing it and start worshipping. No. Those who wait and don't worship are still travellers on the way. They are travellers towards ākhirah. The destination is ākhirah. No one will remain in this dunyā. Everything except Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla will be tested with death. Everything, from stones to iron, everything has its end. And people are also going towards their end. Stones are going, animals are going. This is the way. Know this way well so that the end of the way is good. Know and recognize your destination. Don't wander without knowing. Ignorance is hard. Not knowing is hard. If you go somewhere without knowing, you ask, "Where are we going? What are we doing?" And if no one knows, people get worried. Whereas Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla has shown the way. HeNone has shown the target and where we must go. HeNone has shown everything very clearly. Yet, mankind does not believe in that way. They don't accept it. They may accept it or not. Eventually, they will reach ākhirah, the grave, and qiyāmah.

Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says that qiyāmah is near but people see it as far. Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam has ḥadīth that the death of people is their qiyāmah. Because when you are put in your grave, is when qiyāmah takes place: it will seem like the Hour for you. Even if thousands of years pass, that won't matter. After death comes qiyāmah. Therefore, be careful. Know your way. Know your way and take precautions accordingly. And precaution is to walk on the way of AllāhNone, to obey the orders of AllāhNone. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is Beneficent. HeNone didn't order things that mankind cannot do. All of them are easy things. Performing worship is easier than most things you do. But shayṭān is cheating and showing it as difficult. May AllāhNone protect us and enlighten our way, in shā’a Llāh.

The word of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. It is everything heNone say, it is very nice, very beautiful word what heNone said. Each one it is like jewel. The Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, what coming from hisNone blessed tongue giving wisdom, giving bless, giving every good thing for human being. So, one of hisNone thing we read in khuṭbah for Jum‘ah khuṭbah: “Kun fī d-dunyā ka-annak ‘ābiru sabīl.” "Be in dunyā, in this world, as you are traveller, or passing through, not staying. "Or as foreigner - aw ka-annak gharībun.” You are don’t think it is your, this world, your home. Staying place? No. You are, even you have house, you have palace, you have everything, still you are travelling. Where you are travelling? You are sitting, you are not travelling. No. You are travelling towards your end, towards next life.

This life it is only world, place to passing through, not staying. Don’t think you will be here forever. Only little bit after, you will be in like place which you will be forever. So this way for next life. We all traveller, we all foreigner in this world. Because before us also, millions, billions people they come and they went, they come and they went, they come and they went. And for everything also there is end, even for this world, for dunyā, for stone, for mountain, for river. For everything they also they have end. But important for us, our end.

So, you must be careful. You must be take your things when you are travelling. Take your goods with you. Don’t take, don’t think you are going without anything to next life, you will be good there. But many people they don’t believe. They don’t understanding this. They thought it is difficult for this people. Non-believer people, they have life, very bad life because they don’t know their destination. They don’t know why this, where, and what will be happen. It is so difficult to be without knowing anything, you travelling some place and you don’t know anything. You will be like anxious. What will happen? What will be for us? So, it is better and for believer, it is AllāhNone give this for them. They are happy, they know what will be happen. Because Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying and Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla telling in Qur’ān, in HisNone Holy book. So, it is we must be prepare ourself for this journey.

This is the biggest journey, the life journey for us. If you are staying or you are walking, you are not sleeping, you also, you are still travelling. Don’t say "I am not going." You by... If you like to go, if you not like to go, it is still this journey going. Be careful and be prepared yourself. In shā’a Llāh, who prepare and he knowing where he is going, he will be safe, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone make us from these people. And make us to more believe, to be more believe on this. And every time we must think about this – don’t give value for dunyā. Here okay, you can make dunyā, you can use dunyā for your good thing. But for bad thing be careful because you will be asked for this.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
