Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Our Master’s (SAW) dua is manifesting itself. Hazrat Allah Azza wa Jalla created people and He is testing them. Our Master (SAW) states in his hadith: “The difficult becomes easy for those who are with Allah.”

People are always challenged. You look at some people: they are challenged with a small matter. You look at others and they say, “This is very easy. Why are you grumbling so much? It is not hard.” When you are with Allah, there is nothing hard in the sight of Allah. Our Master (SAW) prays to Allah as, “Make the difficult easy for us.” Our Holy Prophet (SAW) was exposed to cruelty all by himself in the middle of so many people. No matter how hard it might have been, it was not hard for him when he was with Allah. Never see anything as hard.

Nothing is hard next to Allah (JJ). Whatever difficulties there may be, may Allah give us all ease Inshallah. Do not succumb to the ego, do not succumb to hopelessness. Nothing is hard as long as you are with Allah (JJ). Everything is hard and bad if you are not with Allah. The end is well when you are with Allah. With the permission of Allah, do not call the things you do hard. Difficulties are overcome with the help of Allah. You cannot overcome them any other way. May everything be easy and may there be no difficulties Inshallah.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq.

Al-Fatiha.
