Aū‘dhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-Shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Waṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-F’āiz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-

Allah shows mercy to the Muslim, thanks be to Allah. May we be one of those servants to whom Allah shows mercy. Thanks be to Allah for all the provisions He has bestowed upon us. The greatest provision, of course, is Islam, wellbeing and health. During days such as these, people acknowledge this a little. Don’t count and don’t compute. A’ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bi-smi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Wa in ta‘uddū ni‘mata Llahi lā tuḥṣūhā inna Llaha la-ghafūru r-raḥīm” Qur’ān 16:18). “And if you should count the favors of Allah, you could not enumerate them. Indeed, Allah is Forgiving and Merciful.” We think of it as a few provisions, but according to the ayat there are millions of them. You cannot possibly count them. Allah ‘azza wa jalla offers these to all.

Those destined to receive them benefit from what they receive, and along with receiving rewards, their station also is elevated. Those who are not destined, although they do receive the provisions, yet they do not understand where they [i.e., the provisions] come from, or might not even want to know. They claim that these provisions comes coincidently by means of this or that. Such a man is unfortunate. Because he is not thankful for what he is provided, it comes and goes without benefit to him. Benefit is for those who are thankful, for otherwise even if one possesses the whole world, there is no benefit in it. For a Muslim there are great benefits even in little things.

These days we are in a blessed month. It is a little difficult, but Allah has shown mercy on us by having this occur during the blessed months. I am not sure whether others are aware of this, but what has happened is a miracle of Allah. Allah is showing us this miracle. Whether this or that one caused it is not the point, what is important is by the blessings of these months this calamity will be lifted off. It is already being lifted off. Doctors are surprised at how it is happening, and are taking precautions, but with precaution there is also prayer. Without prayer, not much can happen by the precautions [alone]. So thanks be to Allah that, in the name of this holy month, Allah is slowly lifting the distress. Insha’Allah, at the end of it we will be safe, and our suffering will bring benefit to those who remained in a thankful state to Allah, insha’Allah. As for the others, may Allah guide them. They should not be ungrateful. It is not a good thing, and it is harmful to them. So thanks be to Allah. May Allah lift this calamity off from us as soon as possible. May He keep us on the straight path, and give strength to our faith. Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-F ti ah
