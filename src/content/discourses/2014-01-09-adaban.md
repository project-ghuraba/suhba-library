Shaykh Muhammad Nazim Al-Haqqani An-Naqshibandi, Sohbat of the 9th of January, 2014.

May mercies come descend for you. InshaAllah it will open. It will open. May Allah be pleased. Blessings to you and your children. Blessings to all who come and go. May mercy be upon your father. Fatiha.

Welcome. Sheikh Mehmet Effendi, how are you, hay faqir? Hay faqir, live long. Live long. May it be holy. This holy month has entered with blessings. More of its blessings will come. Allahu Akbar. Allahu Akbar. Allahu Akbar.

Her Maqam is there for 1,500 years. It's not easy. She is Sultan, our Hala Sultan. She is Sultan. I am afraid to enter it even now. I'm at this age and still afraid. I haven't looked inside. For adab, and there's fear. Allah Allah. May Allah Dhul-Jalal make her level high. May He accept us for her sake.

Don't be hungry for dunya. Look at what is in the Presence of Allah. My son, look at Allah and His great favours. Let's say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Go ahead. All your work will succeed. Feed. Did you understand? Feed. A holy month has come. Feed. Don't look.

May relief come to our hearts. May relief come for the honor of our Master. May relief come to us as well. May knowledge, understanding and adab come to you. May you be dressed in majesty, my son. Fatiha.

Prophet of people and jinn, our Master Sultan-ul Anbiya; May his honored shoes be on our heads. Oh, our Prophet. Our beautiful Prophet. I am relieved. I am relieved. Sometimes worry troubles me.

If an ant, Mehmet, says "This mountain is mine", what is its benefit? What will it do with a mountain? If these ants say "It's mine" to a mountain, what will they do?

Your attitude to people is good. Allah will give. Give to whoever asks, don't fear, my son. Allah Allah, Allah Allah, Allah Allah. Fatiha.

I am tired. InshaAllah may tiredness be for the honor of our Master. May it for the honor of our Ummuhan Sultan. Fatiha.

Our Ummuhan Sultan makes us comfortable.
