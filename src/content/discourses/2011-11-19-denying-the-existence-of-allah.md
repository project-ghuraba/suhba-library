Which one is it that flows through the vessels? G: The red ones, Sayyidi. They go one by one, consecutively. M. Meaning, Inside these vessels which are thinner than a hair, these red & white blood cells move? Gson. No Sayyidi, only the red ones. M. The white ones cannot flow? Gson. No, it can't pass, too large. They are very large. M. La Ilaha illa Allah.. Gson. He says, their length is the distance from the moon to the earth. This is from a single cell? G. Yes, Sayyidi. And also the red blood cells are very elastic, they are not hard. By squeezing themselves, they can flow through the capillaries.
