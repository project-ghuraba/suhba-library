As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh. A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla has gifted these blessed months to Muslims. Muḥarram is the last of them. There are three sacred months: Dhū l-Qa‘dah, Dhū l-Ḥijjah, and Muḥarram. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla made these months sacred to protect ḥajj, so that no fighting would happen during them. If war comes upon you, you may defend yourself, says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. But it is not necessary to start a war. It does not happen in these months. Because these months are the months of ḥajj, so that people can go and return from ḥajj. So that they can travel with ease. And there is the month of Rajab. That is also one of the sacred months, on its own. These have been sacred since the day Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla created the earth and the heavens. This world of ours, what we live in – these four months are sacred months. Everything has its wisdom in the sight of Allāh. So that people are not always in a state of war, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla granted it. Jihād is necessary. Jihād is also required, but of course, not everyone can do jihād as they like. A person who wants to perform jihād must be under a commander, under an amīr. If he performs jihād together with them, that is how it should be done. But if you set out on your own, saying, “I will wage jihād,” and start leading people, and those who rise up, but their identities aren’t clear, not only do you disobey the command, but you oppose it. You must obey Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. His command is not to act alone. You must have an amīr over you, so that you act according to that command. Of course, in today’s world, nothing is clear. Nothing is as it appears. People think others are sincere and follow them, but they are using them to destroy and ruin them, only to earn a few pennies. Therefore, it is not good to act by yourself or with people you don’t know. You should follow the known way and act with known people. So, at this time, our jihād, as our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam said, is against our nafs: the greatest jihād. Especially now, as we are in the end times, we must do jihād against our nafs. Whatever the nafs tells you, you must resist it – but of course, can you even resist one percent, two percent, or ten? What matters is that the greatest war is against the nafs. Because the other battles are not under your command – you will be under theirs. Shukr be to Allāh. The state of this world is unclear. What you think is good turns out bad, and what you think is bad turns out good. That’s why we must be careful. There is no need to put yourself in danger. It is the month of Muḥarram, the month of ‘Āshūrā’. In shā’a Llāh, this month will be a means for good. Many Prophets and Awliyā’ – all of them attained their ranks on this day of ‘Āshūrā’. So we must pay attention to it. We should not follow everyone. Because treachery is widespread, and sincere people are few. Those with sincerity are very few. And then you cannot trust anyone’s word – neither women’s nor men’s. “I’ll do this, I’ll do that.” Be careful. Be careful with everything you have – especially your religion. Those who have emerged in the last century, and spread everywhere like a cancer – this group known as Salafīs – they declare you kāfir. But there is no sincerity in them, nothing. They deceive people. Let’s see how long people will keep falling for it – and when disaster will strike them. May Allāh protect us. May Allāh correct them. May Allāh protect us all, in shā’a Llāh, for the sake of these blessed months.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are living in first month of year, which it is Muḥarram. It is the last month from month, three months, four months. It is gift from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, to human being, to Muslim. It’s, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, He create, He said, years and months and days and create twelve months since He create this earth. And twelve months: four of them, no fighting, no war in this month. Don’t, you cannot declare war in this month. At least, if they attack you, you can attack them. But for normally, it is forbidden. And these four months, one of them Rajab, it is three months between them to Dhū l-Qa‘dah, Dhū l-Ḥijjah, Muḥarram. Dhū l-Qa‘dah, Dhū l-Ḥijjah, we have passed. Now, month of Muḥarram, which it is also one month of end [of] pilgrimage, ḥajj. From, because three months they need to reach Ka‘bah, to reach this holy place. And to come back also need maybe forty days or forty-five days, the nearest thing. So for this, Allāh make it to be safe for people, to not, no fighting in this month. And this giving them to be rest. Because in Islām, there is order to follow your country, your amīr, to defend your country against this who want to destroy it. This is order for Muslim – jihād. This is jihād meaning: to defend Islām, defend yourself, defend your family. Because, non-believers they don’t have mercy. So, you must be strong to defend, to make them to not come to you. But also, Allāh give Muslim to be rest four months, to be not going for fighting, only staying. But now, of course, no jihād. But there is some people who cheating people and tell them, “Come, we must make jihād, we must fight, we must...” But all of this ninety, ninety-nine percent, they are not good people. Not good people, just to make problem for Muslim, for people who are following them. And this is head of them, I think, he is shayṭān. You cannot make jihād by yourself. You must follow your commander, your amīr, your president who order you. That time, you can do. But this time, no, nothing like this. We have bigger than this fighting jihād. Jihādu l-Akbar, meaning biggest fight, we have every day: to fight your ego, to fight your desire, what it is not acceptable for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, for Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. To make them, to fight, to keep them away from you. Of course, we are, most of time we not win, we lose this fighting. But still, you must fight your ego. Every day you will be in war, jihād with your ego, and Allāh reward you for this. This is our thing. Because there is many people. And many people they are cheated with this people. And they are happy, shouting, doing this, doing that. And they thought they are doing something. They don’t know they are... This shayṭān playing with them. When the something happen, they will be, no support for them, no any benefit for, no for dunyā, no for ākhirah. Because they don’t have any, any knowledge from Islām. Which, Islām teaching people first thing, to be good behave, to respect from Prophet to Ahlu l-Bayt, for ṣaḥābah, for tābi‘īn, for tābi‘ī t-tābi‘īn, mashāyikh, awliyā’, all this respect. But when this people, who are claiming they are real Muslim and they want to fight and make jihād, nothing from this. When you don’t have this, you don’t have anything. Just you fight for yourself and you will be loser. Nothing you can win. Allāh with who are respect Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. respect ṣaḥābah, respect Ahlu l-Bayt. This people, they are going so far, they even not respect Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. How they can respect ṣaḥābah or Ahlu l-Bayt or ‘ulamā’ or mashāyikh? They don’t have this. For this, our first now days to fight our ego, to not be cheated with this people. We hear there is many people they like them. They like to be with them. But when it is real time, you cannot find anyone. And just, this people, they will be without any support, without any benefit. Allāh keep us away from these people, to not be cheated with them. Allāh give strong īmān for us, in shā’a Llāh. Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
