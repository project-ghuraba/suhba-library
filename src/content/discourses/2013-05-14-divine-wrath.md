Na'uthu biLlahi min ghadhabi-j Jabbar - We seek refuge in Allah from the Anger of Al Jabbar (The Compeller). Na'uthu biLlahi min ghadhabi-j Jabbar. Aman Ya Rabbi. Heavens and Earth tremble when the Divine Anger is mentioned. May Allah (swt) protect us from His Divine Anger. What can we do? We have become weak. O Changer of States, change our state to the best of states - Ya muhawwilal hawli wal ahwal hawwil haalana ila ahsanil hal.

Let's fulfill our servanthood. Let's fulfill our servanthood. We should know our identity. We should know our identity. These people don't know their identity. And that is why they are suffering. The whole world is suffering. What is the reason? Because they do not know their identity? Who are we? If they say - We are Allah's (swt) servants, the One who created them won't leave them on their own. And He won't leave them in the hands of satan either. Everything will go smoothly, their honor will be great.

Who are we? "We are Muslims". If they say that, they will be saved.

Fatiha.

Na'uthu biLlahi min ghadhabi-j Jabbar Aman Ya Rabbi. Na'uthu biLlahi min ghadhabi-j Jabbar This... at least 7x a day. 70x, 100x they can say it.

Fatiha.
