Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi Rasulillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Eid Mubarak (May your Feast be blessed). May Allah (JJ) destine for us to reach many more years Inshallah. Again, holy days that are limited in number have come and gone. Today is the fourth day. There are two more times left for the Takbirs. Inshallah those also continue in goodness.

May Allah grant guidance to Muslims Inshallah. Because shaitans are attacking from all sides. Wherever there are true and pure Muslims, they try to misguide them from the path or pull them to wrong ways. But Allah Azza wa Jalla protects His religion until Judgment Day. This religion is the religion of Islam. There is no other religion. Those who do not follow this religion are prone to Allah’s wrath.

Allah gave people every kind of beauty but people do not know. Allah Azza wa Jalla did the best of everything for people. And People leave that and jump on the worst and try to do the worst. May Allah guide them. We are not saying anything else. There are people who think what they are doing is good, even though they are doing it in vain.

These days are holy days. Allah gives the good like a sea. Allah shows everything to people, but people do not want to see. Mashallah – blessed – it is raining ever since morning, ever since last night. Every time it is Eid, surely this mercy descends. Some say it is raining for the blood to be washed. It is not because of that, but Allah is granting His mercy. Allah is content because of the worships done and He is bringing down rain. This is mercy. What a beautiful sign, what a beautiful blessing (baraka). People are comforted when they see it and are thankful becasue they have witnessed Allah’s favor (lutuf).

May Allah (JJ) grant us to do all the worships, as doing them is beneficial for us. It is beneficial in all aspects, both externally and internally. Meaning it has tangible uses. It is useful for our body, our health, family, and country. On the other hand, it has spiritual benefits for the hereafter as they are much greater. Allah Azza wa Jalla is showing these to people to know that, “You are on the true path. Do not fear. You have Our offering (ikram), blessing, and things others do not have.”

Allah may treat a whole country because of one person. He treats all people because of one person. That is why there is no need to question its reason. Allah gives with His favor. May Allah make it lasting Inshallah. May Allah protect us from bad ones. May our Feast – the Feat – be lasting Inshallah. May its blessing be lasting Inshallah. Inshallah it ends being good for our country. May good people always be leading us.

Wal hamdu Lillahi Rabbil alamin.

Al-Fatiha.
