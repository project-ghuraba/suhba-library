M. Mubarak! G. Allah bless you. G. We came to ask for your himma-support. M. If we had himmat, we would make it upside down, but we have nothing. May Allah forgive us.. Ashadu an la Ilaha illa Allah wa ashadu anna Muhammedan 'abduhu wa rasuluh. Astagfirullah, Astagfirullah.. O our Lord, forgive us. May You make this year mubarak for us. May it be the year to be acknowledged! May You make it a means-wasila for the conquest by Islam. And, May the blessings of this Muharram Shariff be upon us. May we be enlightened with it's light. May Allah not leave His mu'min servants in difficult positions. Keep Allah and Allah will not leave you. Who leaves Allah & follows shaytan can have neither this world nor the akhirat. Among these ladies, is there anyone who works outside? G. There is none who works outside. There is only her but she goes to people's houses to work. M. No harm. We give no permission to ladies to work at places where there are men. And also, do not go out of your houses unnecessarily. Open your door with Bismillah & close with Bismillah. Don't be without ablution in your homes. No one who does not pray should be in your homes either! Don't open the door to anyone who is coming. If you hear noises around your home or hear anything happening take ablution, spread your prayer rug & sit with your tasbih in your hand after praying 2 rakats. Allah assigns angels to protect you. Keep yourselves from the tall, large buildings as much as you can, if it is possible to do so. If there are brothers & sisters coming from Anatolia, Istanbul is not a very secure place. Go to small villages. And in small villages, reside in humble, adobe or wooden houses.

Save, economise as much as you can. Don't waste. Keep the blessings & the blessings keep you. And, keep your neighbours as well. Keep in your homes food & supplies which can last at least for 10 days, if you can. Spend sparingly. Cook simple meals. Be careful with the bread. Don't throw bread in the garbage. If it is plenty, give your neighbour. Don't follow the fashion. Whoever follows the fashion, sets foot on the way to hell. No good comes from her. Be careful with your men. They may not smoke. Who smokes will not be able to find anything to eat later on, will starve. There are people who drink other poisonous things along with tobacco. Not a good end awaits them either. Keep away from what Allah prohibited. Don't speak to any men. Don't you go to market to buy things. Your husband may go & bring. If there are people selling things in your street, you can buy from them. Our pants would wear out in the knees so our mother would patch them. When the back and bottom of our clothes were.. worn out, they would again repair with patches. We would wear them patched. And our shoes, when they were worn out they would be resoled every year. We would wear like this. When our socks wore out and got old our mothers would sew patches on them from other socks. Be careful. Keep this advice. Don't speak to, don't look to, don't meet with men you don't know! You will find comfort this way. Even if whole world shakes, pray 2 rakats, either on a sheepskin rug.. Sheepskin rug is good. Buy sheepskin rugs & put in your homes. Or, you can make patchwork rugs on your own. Be careful about your homes, the cleanness of your homes. Don't do anything without Bismillah. Allah makes your doors protected with angels. Even if whole world becomes upside down, nothing will happen to you. Ok..I may rest. I am getting tired. Therefore... Hold your hand.. Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Allahu Allahu Allahu Haqq. Allahu Allahu Allahu Haqq. Allahu Allahu Allahu Haqq. Hasbuna Allah wa Ni'mal Wakil La Hawla Wa La Quwwata illa Billah Al Aliy' Al Azim. Ya Aliy' Al Ya Azim! Ya Aliy' Al Ya Azim! Ya Aliy' Al Ya Azim! Hasbuna Allah! Allahumma Thabbit'hum 'ala Al Haqq, Allahumma Thabbit'hum 'ala Al Haqq, Allahumma Thabbit'hum 'ala Al Haqq, bi Jahi Man Anzalta aleyhi Surat-ul Fatiha.
