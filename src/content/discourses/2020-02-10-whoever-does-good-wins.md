Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Our Holy Prophet says:

“Protect yourself from the evil of the person you do good to.” Man’s nature demands training. Man’s ego needs to be trained so that he can get rid of these habits. Not knowing the goodness of those who do good and doing evil are among the attributes of the ego. Allah Azza wa Jalla is the One who does us the greatest favor. People do the most evil to Him, do not accept Him and do not follow His orders. Now, most of the people do not even accept the Creator. It doesn’t matter whether he does or not, this is a bad habit. Allah has created man’s ego, and there are bad habits in his ego. Man rises when he trains his ego. The one who obeys his ego and does evil to the one who does good to him loses, then goodness has no value. But the person who does good wins.

Our Holy Prophet does not say not to do good deeds. Now a person does good, and sees evil, what should he do? You say that you shouldn’t do good, but it’s not like that. “Do good,” says our Holy Prophet. Don’t do less goodness, but know and protect yourself. Protect yourself and don’t say, “I did this person a favor, no harm will come from it.” Humans have egos inside, they are not angels. Not some but most of them obey their egos. That’s why we should protect ourselves. Do not say, “He is human, he doesn’t do anything.” Protect yourself, then you won’t be harmed. You know you have protection, you have protected yourself.

“ِق َّتا”, “Ittaqi” means protecting yourself, you will protect yourself. This is also an advice and order of our Holy Prophet. Don’t say Muslims do or don’t. Everyone has an ego, everyone can do it. Therefore, may Allah not make us from those who follow their ego. May we not do evil to those who do good to us insha’Allah. May we do better favors for those who do good to us, so that this goodness will be of benefit and reward insha’Allah. May Allah protect us from the evil of our egos.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
