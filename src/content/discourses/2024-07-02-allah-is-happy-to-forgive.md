A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Yā-ayyuhā lladhīna ’āmanū tūbū ilá Llāhi tawbatan naṣūḥa" (66:8). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘aẓīm. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is the OneNone who accepts repentance. Repent for the sins you have committed, says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. The repentance of those who repent is accepted. What they have done is forgiven in the presence of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. HisNone door of forgiveness is open. It is open until just before Judgement Day. When that door is closed on Judgement Day, it is too late. But now, it is always open.

Mankind, by asking for forgiveness from AllāhNone for their mistakes and sins, get rid of them. They get rid of them and feel lighter. The burden of sins is heavy. A person who commits sins says there is no heaviness, but he doesn't feel it. He doesn't realize he carries a heavy spiritual burden. The burden of a sin is very great.

When Banī Isrā’il arrived at Mount Sinai, Mūsá ‘alayhi s-salām was addressed by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla on Mount Ṭūr and stayed there in conversation. When he returned, his people had started worshipping a calf because of a misguided man. He had made a golden calf, and they were worshipping it. They asked for forgiveness for that. It was not as easy for the children of Isrā’il to be forgiven as it is for us. Someone had to die to be forgiven. They had to kill each other. Who committed a sin had to sacrifice himself in order to be forgiven. He would place his head on that thing to be cut off. There was no forgiveness otherwise. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla said HeNone would forgive them on that condition only. Even though they were in such a state, they accepted it because there was a prophet. They admitted their sins and said, "If we are to be forgiven, then let them kill us, and we will be forgiven." It was not as easy as now, to say "astaghfiru Llāh" and be forgiven. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gave us the easy way, for the sake of our Holy ProphetNone.

So on that day, they started killing those who had committed the sin. Cut down one by one, seventy thousand people were killed from the children of Isrā’il that day. And then they were forgiven. Then Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla told Mūsá ‘alayhi s-salām to come to Mount Ṭūr with seventy people, and that the judgement was not yet over. When they went there, the mountain rose over them to crush them. But Mūsá ‘alayhi s-salām begged Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla and said, "It is not our fault. We will be crushed for the sins of these people. This is not our sin." Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla then forgave them. The weight of that mountain is the weight of our sins. It is so heavy. People don't feel it. They don't feel so much regret for their sins, as there is not so much punishment. We are easily forgiven. Even though we are easily forgiven, people don't want to do it. They don't do it. "What have we done? What is our sin?" You followed your ego, you followed shayṭān. Ask for forgiveness from AllāhNone so that HeNone will forgive you again. Don't oppose Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Don't go against Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. If not only you, but the whole world, the entire universe oppose HimNone, no one can do anything against Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.

Therefore, bow your head to Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Ask forgiveness from HimNone in order to be saved from this heavy burden. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is so merciful that HeNone changes them to good deeds. When we ask forgiveness for sins we have committed, we are saved from sin, and good deeds are written for us. Is there something better than this? But mankind is ungrateful. They don't appreciate anything. They don't know goodness. They like those who do evil and keep away from those who do good. May AllāhNone protect us and not let us obey our egos. May AllāhNone not separate us from the right way, in shā’a Llāh. May AllāhNone forgive all of us.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying, "Inna Llāha yaghfiru dh-dhunūba jamī‘a" (39:53). Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone is forgive everything what you do, what you done. When you ask for forgiveness, HeNone forgive you. Just, you ask for forgiveness. Because forgiveness, AllāhNone happy to forgive us for sake of ProphetNone. And we are very lucky nation to be from nation of Prophet Muḥammad ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, Ummati MuḥammadNone. Everybody can be from hisNone nation. If he follow himNone, if he accept himNone, accept Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, he will be lucky. Because heNone, AllāhNone grant himNone many thing which, what it was before, not for other nations. Many of them they were suffering for small thing. AllāhNone punished them. AllāhNone send some punishment for them, and after this, sometimes HeNone forgave, sometimes not forgive.

As was Sayyidunā Mūsá ‘alayhi s-salām, he left, with his Banī Isrā’il, from Egypt to Sinai. Immediately, Sayyidunā Mūsá, he went to Ṭūr – mountain – Ṭūr Sinai mountain, which AllāhNone addressing him there. And he was there for forty days. In this forty days there was one man, he make people, the Banī Isrā’il people, to worship idol as it is from gold, cow from gold. They make him to make some voice and they were cheated by him. And they was worshipping this idol. When Mūsá ‘alayhi s-salām coming from there and he see this people, he was very angry, and he ask for forgiveness, to AllāhNone to forgive them. And it was that time to, if doing anything, they must cut, kill, to AllāhNone forgive them. And when this people, once this knowing they are make sin and wrong, they ask from Sayyidunā Mūsá ‘alayhi s-salām to ask Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla to forgive them. And Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla say they must, everyone, to be forgiven, to must killed, each one who done this. And they was accept.

To, because they know to be forgiven, it will be forever for them, good. If not forgiven, they will be forever bad for themself. So the beginning, who not worship this idol, to kill this people with sword, cutting, cutting, cutting, cutting until become seventy, more than seventy thousand people was killed from Banī Isrā’il. And that time Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla say, "Okay. Now INone forgive them." So it was like this before.

But nowadays, only ask forgiveness from sake of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, HeNone forgive you. No need to make it... Not difficult, like that time. But people they are not interested for asking for forgiveness. "Why – we are doing this?" They don't know there is heaviness, spiritual heaviness on them, like mountain. But they not feeling this, not knowing this. Why this?

Also, after this one finish, with this seventy thousand people killed, and Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla told Sayyidunā Mūsá, "Come to Ṭūr Sinai with seventy good people from your nation. Come there." And when they arrive there, this Mountain of Sinai, with huge mountain, coming, flying on top of them. It will be coming, and smash them in the earth. Sayyidunā Mūsá ‘alayhi s-salām pray for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, "O, our Lord, we are, we only worshipping YouNone. We are not doing any sin. Our people they done this, we are not from them. Forgive us." AllāhNone accept this, and they take this. HeNone didn't kill them there. But showing how it is heavy, this, to be have sin in your, with you.

For this, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla and Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying to people to ask forgiveness, to take this mountain of heaviness, spiritual heaviness from you. And you will be more happy without this heaviness. People, they thought... They doing bad thing. They doing everything bad. And they thought, nothing. But reality not like this. Who see with eye of reality, he see many thing in this world, very bad thing. AllāhNone keep us safe from our ego. And we are asking for forgiveness for everything we done, and for other, what they done, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
