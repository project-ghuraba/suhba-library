As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

There is always a teacher for people to teach them. They need a person to show them the way. Mankind learns day by day. Instead of learning on their own, they learn much faster with a teacher or someone who teaches them. They attain more knowledge then. This is the acceptable method in everything. You must learn everything from the competent ones. Whatever task you need to do, you must learn from a master, someone who teaches that well. By learning from him, you will become an excellent artist, an excellent teacher, or an excellent hodja. This is the law of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Those who learn on their own are the Prophets. But their teacher is Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. You can say they don't learn on their own. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla teaches them. HeNone shows them the way so that they can teach their ummah. Those who come after them, their companions or who were together with them show the way of the prophets. The way of our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam is the same.

There are ṣaḥābah, and after the ṣaḥābah, ‘ālims show that way. But they must be connected to our Holy ProphetNone. Connected ‘ālims... all ṣaḥābah were connected to our Holy ProphetNone. They went on hisNone way and showed hisNone way. “Whomever you follow, you will reach guidance,” says our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. If those who come after them walk on their way, it is perfect. But when they don't like them and go to other ways, they go astray. Therefore, the awliyā’ and mashāyikh who have the right connection to our Holy ProphetNone continued on this way after the ṣaḥābah. It has reached this time, but there is a lot of fitnah nowadays. They say there is no need for it. They say there is not such a thing. Therefore, people are confused and running after things out of the religion, or avoid sunnah and wājib. There are many people who listen to them, whereas the way of our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam is everlasting. It lasts until Judgment Day. That are ṭarīqahs. Because ṭarīqahs are connected directly to our Holy ProphetNone. From Shaykh to Shaykh, they go up to our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam.

There are forty-one ṭarīqahs. All of them are connected to our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Their way is never wrong. But of course, there are mutamashyikh (shaykh impersonator). Numerous people with no relation to ṭarīqah claim to be shaykh. They are more than the true ones. And people are cheated by them, because they don't know. What can you say? People favoured by AllāhNone find the true one. When it is not meant to be, they find those, those who are not real. Who are the true ones? Their attributes are clear. Firstly, they follow the way of our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam and show the highest respect to himNone. And then they love hisNone four khalīfahs, the ṣaḥābah, Ahlu l-Bayt, and all of them. People say they are in ṭarīqah now, but they don't accept the ṣaḥābah. They accept some of them and don't accept others. They are the ones who are not true. They want to conform to people's desires. They think if they do so, they will gather more followers. But when they join their way, they have no connection. There will be no connection at all. People bring more harm than profit then.

May AllāhNone protect us. May AllāhNone not separate us from the right way. May HeNone let all of us meet the right people, in shā’a Llāh.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla when send somebody from... Everybody coming to this world, they have somebody to teach them. Because human being, he become not knowing anything. With teaching he learn many thing from baby – mother. After mother, teacher or master or who looking after some good work, teaching them. This is life of human being – to learn. You must learn. And because Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone like. HeNone order us to read, to learn. And everybody he has capacity for this. Not, no problem with this. As much they can do. But in life, also, you must look to somebody, some teacher, to learn from him. Human being can be – they have ability to learn by themself. Make experience every time; experience many time – you make fault, wrong thing. But maybe, you at the end, you be, you learn something.

But more easier from this to follow teacher, to learn from him – what you will do? How you can do? It is more easy, what if you learn by yourself in ten years, in with teacher maybe you learn in one month or fifteen days. And you be successful in what you learn. Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, saying, “My companion, they are like star. Each one you follow, you be guided to right way.” For this we are, many of people, they are guided by ṣaḥābah. Many of them like this. But who following Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, especially who following “ṭarīqah” people, forty-one ṭarīqah, all of them.

If you follow any one of them, you follow Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam in right way, which pure sharī‘ah. Pure Islām with ṭarīqah people. After himNone, four Khalīfah – Sayyidunā Abū Bakr, ‘Umar, ‘Uthmān, ‘Alī, raḍiya Llāhu ‘anhum. And Ahlu l-Bayt and ṣaḥābah, whole ṣaḥābah. Not swearing, not saying bad thing for ṣaḥābah for this.
