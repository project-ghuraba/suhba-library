Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

The blessed Eid of Ramadan (Eid al-Fitr) has passed. May it be with Barakah and goodness insha’Allah. Eids are the blessings of Islam, they are the gift of Allah. They are holidays for us both physically and spiritually. The real holidays are these Eids that Allah gave us. Of course, it passed, but you still need to know its value. How do we know how useful and beautiful it is? Because Allah has given it to us as a gift, and made it special twice a year. These are the real holidays. Physically, people get closer to each other, visit each other and be blessed. Spiritually, believers will definitely see its benefits in both the hereafter and this world.

Mankind does not know what to do. That’s why, when he follows what Allah says, his life gains meaning and benefits. Otherwise, it is of no use, it has no meaning and it is a dry life. They don’t get a taste of the Athan, nor the fasting, nor the blessed days. It would be of no use for them, and they wouldn’t know of it either, it just passes.

A Muslim already prays to Allah five times a day from morning to evening, begs Allah and remembers Allah. It is the same with days, whether it is Friday, Monday, or Thursday; they are blessed days. And it is also the same with months. They are all full of meaning and benefit. Otherwise, it would be a dry and useless life. We should be thankful that Allah gave us this life. We should know its value. May Allah make us reach many more years, and may it be blessed insha’Allah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
