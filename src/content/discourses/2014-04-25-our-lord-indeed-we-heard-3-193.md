Salamu alaikum wa rahmatullahi ta'ala wa barakatuhu. We are trying to do the sunnah of Mawlana Shaykh by giving small talk. Subhanim Allah Sultanim Allah Nabim Muhammad alayhi 's-Salam. This, Mawlana Shaykh was saying when it was difficult time and now insha Allah, we repeat together, Subhanim Allah Sultanim Allah Nabim Muhammad alayhi's-Salam Subhanim Allah Sultanim Allah Nabim Muhammad alayhi's-Salam Subhanim Allah Sultanim Allah Nabim Muhammad alayhi's-Salam.

This is, we are bargaining for Allah Almighty we are beggar-ing Allah Almighty, Ya Rabbi, give long life, healthy life for Mawlana Shaykh. Ameen! Audhu Billahi min ash-shaytani r-rajim Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim,

Rabbana innana sami'ana munadiyan yunadi lil-imani an aminu birabbikum fa amanna (3:193)

This is Ayati Karima. It says, Allah Almighty He send somebody to tell you, 'to believe in Me'. And we are saying, Ya Rabbi we believe on You because of him, because we don't know nothing. We don't know nothing, we don't understand nothing, and we are nothing! We are something with him, we are believer with him, we are something with him. And this, we must understand! This we have to understand, that Mawlana Shaykh, he is everything! And we are nothing! We are nothing, nothing, nothing!

If you are today, you know something, you have something, and you claim something, this because of Mawlana Shaykh! And Mawlana Shaykh, he is claiming by Allah Almighty! He never claim by his ego! We all of us claiming by our ego! All of us, we are claiming with our ego! We have to think logical, we have to understand logical, and we have to know for our future. If we are going with our ego, that ego will take us for very bad situation.

If you are winning something today, keep it for tomorrow and keep your faith for Allah Almighty Presence, for Rasulullah divine presence, for Awliya divine presence. It is important to know, to not have this said, "What a donkey you are, I give you so many thing and you lost it by your small desire of your ego, by following your ego". This is, I wanted to warn you, first myself, and then toward everybody. I want you to know this. Mawlana Shaykh, Alhamdulillah, he is good, he is doing well and insha Allah, he is willing to be with us, but are we, are we these people that deserving him? This is question. This is big question. It is nothing wrong with him, the wrong with us. It is my humble advice to leave all your calculation, hidden agenda, known agenda, whatever agenda it is and put your sincerity to Mawlana Shaykh and ask by sincere heart. Sincere heart. Mawlana Shaykh he doesn't ask anything from us, nothing! Except our sincere heart.

He didn't look by our benefit, he didn't look for our money, for our rank, he didn't look for anything from us, he just say, "You give me your sincere heart, I want your sincere heart". I am asking from Allah Almighty that, to not test us with our ikhlas (sincerity), the dunya is running, the life is running Everybody have end, everybody have Qiyamat, everybody have place to stop. I want to stop with ikhlas. I am asking you to stop with ikhlas. Nobody asking too much things.

Wa min Allahi tawfiq bi hurmati l-Habib bi hurmati l-Fatiha.
