As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Mina l-mu’minīna rijālun ṣadaqū mā ‘āhadū Llāha ‘alayh” (33:23). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘aẓīm. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says, “There are men steadfast on this way and who keep their word.” They are “rijāl” – the men. Rijāl is the highest rank. They are the people who don't break their promise. Some of them are alive, and some of them are gone. But there is no difference. This way is the way of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, the way of our Holy ProphetNone. This way is the way of mashāyikh, our shaykhs who come from the silsilah (chain). “When someone is gone, another person replaces him. And the way goes on. It is not tied to only one.” Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim said this. His words are true and beautiful. People sometimes follow someone. When he passes away, they fall apart. That way is wrong. As the way is the way of AllāhNone, it must go on. Mashāyikh don’t claim anything for themselves. The way of mashāyikh is the way of AllāhNone. It is the sunnah of AllāhNone for people. And it is important that those who follow it are steadfast on this way.

Shukr to AllāhNone, tomorrow is the tenth ‘urs of Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim, ten years since he passed away physically. Most people present here came within the past ten years and have not met Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim. But as this way is a steady way, with his support it is going on. This is the most important thing. Not to go astray, not to switch, not to go out of the way, is the greatest miracle and greatest favour of ṭarīqah. For people, for believers, for people of ṭarīqah, being steadfast on this way is to continue with this way. This must be followed by everyone. Because this way is the meaning of our lives. What our existence needs is to find the right thing and hold onto it. Who holds on to it will be saved. Who doesn't hold on to it, AllāhNone will still treat him with mercy, in shā’a Llāh. May AllāhNone increase the rank of our shaykh. May we not go astray from his way, in shā’a Llāh.

What way is that way? It is the way of our Holy ProphetNone, Ahlu l-Bayt, the four khalīfahs, the twelve imāms, the imāms of the madhhabs, the imāms of belief, the way of mashāyikh, the way of the forty-one ṭarīqahs. We will not go out of it in order to look good to someone. We can't compromise on this. If you make a compromise… There was a beautiful word always said by Grandshaykh ‘Abdu Llāh ad-Dāghistānī: “Man shadhdha shudhdha fi n-nāri abadā. – Whoever leaves the way, leaves it and goes to hell.” He will be in hell forever. Because that is out of the way. Our way is steady and beautiful. It is the way of salvation and profit. May AllāhNone make us steadfast on this way. May the support of Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim always be upon us. It certainly is. It is much higher than it was while he was here, with the permission of AllāhNone. It can be seen. Everyone knows it, in shā’a Llāh.
