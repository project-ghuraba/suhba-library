Aū‘dhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-Shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Waṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-F’āiz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-

A’ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Alā inna awliyā’a Llāhi lā khawfun ‘alayhim walā hum yaḥzanūn” Qur’ān 10:62). “Is it not certain that for the Friends of Allah there is no fear upon them nor shall they grieve?” There is no fear, no worry for the Friends of Allah. They fear Allah. Their faith is in Allah, and they fear him. Today is another anniversary of Mawlana's passing. Their station is high. May Allah be pleased with him. He served people and humanity a great deal. He saved many people from a bad end. He gave them a true life by the permission of Allah. He caused them to be guided so that they would live contentedly for eternity, and so may Allah be pleased with him. We always say this. It is necessary to say it always. A man is fortunate if he finds a guide (mursheed). Thanks be to Allah that a true guide was given.

Mawlana Shaykh Nazim Kibrisi was one of the true guides—the true, the faithful, the most trustworthy. We live in a world now where people consider the outer form and start following, then they find out it was all in vain. Thanks be to Allah, Mawlana was a true presenter of the Holy Prophet (SAW). He was a completed man on his (SAW) path. He was an example to many of today’s people. Thousands of people, even millions, benefited from him. Allah has a station for everyone, and bestowed one to him too, and raised it high. So he attained great virtue, and became a means to eternal bliss for us along with himself, on this path which is the path of the Holy Prophet (SAW). It was a destiny to be on this path. Thanks be to Allah. May Allah be pleased with him. May Allah grant that more people be guided. May Allah protect us from the evil of our ego self and from Satan. May Allah protect us from any type of evil, insha’Allah. May He bring us peace, bestow on us eternal bliss in the Next World too, insha’Allah. And may Allah also strengthen our faith. Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-F ti ah
