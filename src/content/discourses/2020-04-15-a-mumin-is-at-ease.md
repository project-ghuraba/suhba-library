Waṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-F’āiz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-

Allah ‘azza wa jalla has bestowed on us every kind of goodness. Allah does not oppress, mankind oppresses himself. The path Allah shows us is the right path. People have sped far off this path, and are still doing so. This all happens because these are the End Times. During these times Allah lets this situation occur because of what people have caused for themselves. These situations are normal, and not even a big of penalty. What He sent upon people is a curse. All of this is their doing, their evil. Allah does not will evil, Satan does. Most people submit to Satan and to their ego. They are doing however much evil is possible evil. Then after all their doings, they complain. A fire is put out by water, but if you add more wood, petrol, or fuel, it will not stop. It is you who will put it out. Only by repenting and prayers will the fire be put out by the will of Allah.

Through Allah’s mercy and help, it will stop. This cannot be observed physically with one’s eyes, but Allah’s miracles can be perceived, yet not by those who are blind. Such people deny, but it does not matter what they think. Everything is clear. Everything in the universe is calculated as Allah says. All is written down in the sight of Allah. Nothing happens by itself, so let us plead with Allah. Be with Allah, and then the rest is easy. There is then no difficulty but rather relief, insha’Allah. A mu’min’s heart is content, relaxed. Calamity is for the one who is not a mu’mim, for those who are not believers. May Allah be our helper and give strength to our faith. This is something I have been saying continually these days, because one who possesses faith is relaxed. For him, whether things seem easy or difficult, they are experienced as easiness, because he has faith in Allah, and whatever happens is from Allah. Thanks be to Allah that all of these are maqams for us, degrees. May Allah protect us, [and protect] Islam and Muslims, too. May it be a guidance for others, also. Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-F ti ah
