Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Religion is based on advice but people do not accept advice. Advice is to counsel, to show the right way, and to help people. People are glad when they are given advice to “Do this and do that” in books. But they are not glad when it is done to them. Or it is heavy on their ego. Whereas accepting the truth and the advice is good for them selves. It shows the right way, and it is the most important thing to find the right way. However, people fall into troubles when they do not accept advice.

They say “A single trouble is better than a thousand advice”. You can tell the man to “Do it like this and do it like that” a thousand times, give a thousand different advice, and they would not accept it. However, in the very end something bad befalls him, then he says this is good. He does not forget it then. We should not do this. Whereas that advice came about with experience.

The advice given by anybody out of the blue is not considered advice either. Advice is that which shows the right way. That which misguides to the wrong way is not advice. Advice is to show the way of Allah or the useful way. Our Holy Prophet (SAW) has a dua as “They listen to advice and do the best.” Inshallah we all become like that.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq.

Al-Fatiha.
