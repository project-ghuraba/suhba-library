As-salāmu ‘alaykum wa raḥmatu Llāhi wa barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

Ramaḍān is almost over. This is the last Friday. Holy Ramaḍān is ending. Our ProphetNone is saying that between the prayers, from Jum‘ah to Jum‘ah, between two Ramaḍāns, HeNone makes all sins be forgiven. If there is no big sin and no right of someone, AllāhNone forgives. If a person from the noon till the afternoon... There is no person without mistakes. He makes mistakes. AllāhNone forgives those mistakes. Then AllāhNone forgives mistakes, faults and sins made between Jum‘ahs. And HeNone forgives sins made from a year to a year, from Ramaḍān to Ramaḍān. So if a man prays Ẓuhr and prays ‘Aṣr, he is forgiven. But if you don't pray Ẓuhr, don't pray ‘Aṣr or Jum‘ah, don't fast in Ramaḍān, they will pile up on you. You will answer for them on Qiyāmah. If you don't repent and ask forgiveness from AllāhNone in dunyā, you will carry them. And even this is by the mercy of Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla and our ProphetNone.

If you don't pray Ẓuhr and ‘Aṣr, don't pray Jum‘ah, then repent and start, everything between Ramaḍāns will be forgiven. As we said, it will be a big sin. What we mean by big sin, if there is no redemption for it, it will be forgiven. AllāhNone is treating people with mercy but people are not accepting. They say they don't want it. If you don't want, you know for yourself. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Yurīdu Llāhu bikumu l-yusra wa-lā yurīdu bikumu l-‘usra." (2:185) Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla wishes only good for you.

We finished this Ramaḍān too. Shukr to AllāhNone, we are reaching ‘Īd. And on ‘Īd "wa litukabbiru Llāha ‘alá mā hadākum" you should make Takbīr. Up until the ‘Īd prayer is over, before and during it, make Takbīr after khuṭbah, then Takbīr is finished. These Takbīrs are Allāhu Akbar Allāhu Akbar Lā ilāha illā Llāhu Allāhu Akbar Allāhu Akbar wa liLlāhi l-ḥamd. There are many revelations we heard from our mashāyikh, imāms of madhāhib. There are other ones as well. But if you do this, as it is our tradition to do this... When done otherwise, people get confused. Therefore, when we make Takbīr, we are making shukr to AllāhNone for the favours He'sNone given to us. The biggest favour is, may AllahNone protect it, may AllāhNone protect our īmān. We see that many people don't know these things. Or they say they don't believe. They don't do anything. If AllāhNone wanted, He'dNone make us like them. May AllāhNone protect us. We have to be thankful for this. Your biggest shukr is staying on the right way, being guided and obeying Allāh'sNone orders. It's not easy to do it. Because a man can do various things. And when it comes to worship, he can do only a little of it. And that is a favour of AllāhNone. We have to thank AllāhNone. We passed this whole holy month. May AllāhNone let us reach even better ones. May we reach the days when Islām rules over the world with Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām and ‘Īsá ‘alayhi s-salām. This holy month is over again. In shā’a Llāh, we will reach another one. We will reach a better one, in shā’a Llāh. May it be blessed, in shā’a Llāh. ‘Īd Mubarak to you! May AllāhNone improve, guide and give understanding to ummati Muḥammad.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are, we come to one month nearly we are finished. Tomorrow or after tomorrow, the last day for Ramaḍān. In Ramaḍān, ProphetNone saying between two Ramaḍāns AllāhNone forgive what you done. Between Jum‘ah and Jum‘ah also AllāhNone forgive what you, your fault, your wrong thing, your sin you done. And between two time of praying AllāhNone also forgive you. But it is, there is one thing what HeNone can forgive for everybody but HeNone said to people who are knowing - if we pray Ẓuhr and between this everybody human being they are weak and they can do wrong every time. Until ‘Aṣr what he done, when he pray ‘Aṣr, AllāhNone He was finish this, forgive you. Between one Jum‘ah until next week Jum‘ah, also what you done HeNone forgive you. But not there is some big sin, this need another thing to also AllāhNone forgive but there is another condition but for normal, every time we can do wrong, between if anything left between one Ramaḍān to another Ramaḍān, also whole year HeNone forgive.

It is big good thing for human being. But human being they are not accepting. You are even Muslim many of them they are not knowing that not practicing. And it is, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are with this two days we will be finish and Allāh HeNone said "yurīdu Llāha bikumu l-yusra wa-lā yurīdu bikumu l-‘usra wa-litukmilū l-‘iddata wa-litukabbirū Llāha ‘alá mā hadākum" (2:185) Allāh HeNone want for you the best, the easy thing. Easiest, but you are, and HeNone don't want hard thing for you. But HeNone give us what HeNone order us. Everybody can do if they are try. But most of people they are not trying.

And HeNone said until you finish this fasting you must after make Takbīr. Takbīr for thank for AllāhNone. What thanking? We are thanking Him to HeNone help us to be in this good way. Because many people they cannot do, even it is easy, but they cannot do. They can do thousand times more hard thing in every day, but for two minute they cannot pray, they cannot fast. Coming too difficult for them. For this we are thanking AllāhNone [that] HeNone help us and HeNone give us this big favour to be in way of ProphetNone and way what HeNone love. AllāhNone He love believer and HeNone said HeNone don't love non believer. "Inna Llāha lā yuḥibbu l-kāfirīn" (3:32) HeNone is knowing which people what they are doing; knowing everything. For this we are thanking HimNone. And we are make Takbīr after we finish Ramaḍān - from Fajr, after Fajr until Ṣalātu l-‘Īd. This also thanking for HimNone. There is many kind of Takbīr but we normally say Allāhu Akbar, Allāhu Akbar lā ilāha illā Llāhu Allāh Akbar Allāhu Akbar Wa liLlāhi l-Ḥamd. This is ṣaḥābi Ibn Mas‘ūd he saying this ProphetNone was saying. But there another kind of Takbīr also - we know this we must continue to not mix. And tomorrow, in shā’a Llāh in our country they are saying after tomorrow ‘Īd. Tomorrow night also very holy night. Night of ‘Īd it is very holy night, acceptable for praying, for du‘ā’, for charity. So we, in shā’a Llāh AllāhNone, give us from this blessed night also. And we hope to next Ramaḍān to be more better for ummah. AllāhNone send us Sayyidinā Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām. AllāhNone give ummah, ummah of Sayyidinā MuḥammadNone, to make them to understand right and wrong thing because most of them they are going wrong and they thought they are going right. No. AllāhNone give us Imām for to show us the right way, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq al-Fātiḥah.
