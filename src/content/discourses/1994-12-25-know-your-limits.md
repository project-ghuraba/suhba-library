Madad yā Sulṭānu l-Anbiyā’, madad yā Sulṭānu l-Awliyā’ madad yā Rijālu Llāh. They had a saying in old times: "He goes to the barbershop from ‘Īd to ‘Īd, doesn't like the barber." Now people are like that, they are ignorant, they don't appreciate anyone. Lā ḥawla wa lā quwwata illā biLlāhi l-‘alayyi l-‘aẓīm.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm.

May Allāh (jj) not make us from the unlucky ones, who are the followers of their ego. Most of today's people are followers of their egos. People, especially educated ones, are followers of their egos. Many Muslims are also followers of their egos. Everybody thinks he is so high, they think they know. The saying of the Prophet (saw); "Raḥima Llāhu-mra’an ‘arifa ḥaddahu wa waqafa ‘indahu." "May Allāh (jj) bless that one, who knows and keeps his limit." He doesn't go too far - "This is my limit. It is up until here, nothing beyond this." May Allāh bless the one, who knows his limit. This is the Prophet's (saw) prayer and saying.

Nothing is better than knowing your limit. If he doesn't know his limit, he gets whipped all the time. Whoever doesn't keep his limit, passes his limit, is worthy of being whipped. And in this time there are few people left, who know their limits. Yes. There are few, who know their limit. The ones, who don't know have increased. The chaos in the Islamic world is caused by this. People, who know their limit have become fewer among Muslims, no matter what their rank. Especially Muslims are deceived by this world. They are proud with worldly things. With the pride the world gives them, they no longer know their limit. With pride, they no longer know their limits, they cross the limits.

The matter of this world is different, the matter of the hereafter is different. That's why in this time what we can do and what we try to establish is especially the way of ṭarīqah: "bal ṭarīqatu kulluhā adabun" it is ordered - Ṭarīqah is all good manners. Ṭarīqah, is the way of good manners. In fact ṭarīqah is good manners, from beginning to end. It teaches the good manners of Islām. With the new expression, it trains the Muslims because Muslims are in need of training. They are in need of training. They are responsible for practicing what they learn. Knowing is something, practicing what you know is something else. Ṭarīqah informs the believers how to behave within sharī‘ah. So that it lets us know what our limits are, teaches us our limits. Ṭarīqah is all about good manners. If you don't enter ṭarīqah, you can't practice the sharī‘ah of Islām. Because the sharī‘ah of Islām orders you to be connected to someone. It orders you to follow someone, follow his way. This is sharī‘ah.

Following whom? Following the good ones. Follow them. This is an order: Follow the good ones. Follow the ones with good manners - meaning, walk in their way. Is there anything else in Islām? Does it say to follow the bad ones? Who is the good one? The one, who knows his limit. Whoever doesn't know his limit, isn't a good one. When we say his limit, firstly, someone, who doesn't acknowledge Allāh (jj) is someone, who doesn't know his limit. Of course someone, who steps over Allāh's orders, doesn't know his limits. If he knew his limits, how could he step over Allāh's orders? If I tell you, ḥāshā, "Put Qur’ān on the floor", you would refuse to put it saying "This is where I walk." Good manners is not to put it lower than the belly. Our old tradition is to keep Qur’ān at the level of the belly, not to put it below that level. You can't put Qur’ān at your feet - that is leaving good manners. Ok. What if I tell you to put it under your feet? Ḥāshā. Could anyone dare? A believer wouldn't dare. Should be a non-believer? All right, we respect Qur’ān this way. How is it we put Allāh's order under our feet?

Putting Allāh's order under your feet means putting Qur’ān under your feet. Why do we do it? Whoever doesn't know his limits, steps over Allāh's (jj) orders. He isn't a good person. You can't follow him. If you do it is a disaster. It is a disaster for the ones, who follow. That's why we said we should know our history. We have to know our history. Our history shows us. History shows us the days when we used to follow the good ones and how we suffered when we followed the bad ones. No need for us to say. Open and read. If we follow those, who know their limits we will also know our limits. Divine mercy will reach us also. When we don't know our limits, divine mercy goes away. Trouble comes on those people. Like we said, nowadays people, who follow the good ones, have become less. People, who follow the bad ones, have become more. That's why the troubles, difficulties coming down have increased. There is no peace. There is no blessing. The doors of goodness are closed, can't be opened. The doors of badness are wide open. Everybody's sunk in evil.

We are talking about knowing your limits, especially you need to know your limits about the holy Aḥādīth and about Islām and the Qur’ān. What is being a Muslim and what it isn't? How should the manners of believers be towards the Qur’ān? How should our manners be towards the holy Aḥādīth of the Prophet (saw)? We need to learn and practice this. First, the believer should insist, meaning insist to learn. Before Qur’ān and holy Ḥadīth, he has to know what Islām ordered and obligated. Qur’ān is the word of Allāh (jj). Holy Aḥādīth are the words, holy sayings of our Prophet ‘alayhi ṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salām. It isn't an easy and a cheap thing to understand the words of Allāh and the Prophet (saw). Old scholars, they were sincere and effective people. When they read Qur’ān they said: "We shouldn't relate the words incorrectly" and they trembled with fear interpreting its meaning. They feared Allāh and were ashamed before Him (jj). They used to relate the words of the Prophet (saw) and the companions, relying on the interpretations of the big saints. They never read verses or holy Aḥādīth without having ablution.

‘Abdu l-Khāliq al-Ghujdawānī built a private building out of his respect for the Qur’ān, just to read the Qur’ān. He built that building, spending a big amount of money for that time. He put a lectern in that building and put the Qur’ān on it. Before he read the Qur’ān he took a bath. He took full ablution (ghusl), wore his newest, cleanest clothes, put on beautiful perfume, and went inside that area on his tiptoes. The Qur’ān was somewhere high. He went inside as if entering the sulṭān's presence. He stood. He was ready to recite the Qur’ān, standing up. The words of Allāh (jj) - as much as you show respect, you will be respected that much in Allāh's (jj) presence. It is so. This is the word of Allāh (jj). It is worth it. If there's anyone, who can do more, Allāh's word is worth it also.

I went into a church, a monastery. The monk invited us to the monastery. It was a long time ago, 40 years ago. As he showed us around the monastery, he took out his bibles. The covers of those bibles were decorated with rubies, emeralds, chrysotile, and gems like that. Such a perfect, big one, two, three, four copies. He took out four copies. They were all bejeweled like this. Then I criticized myself, I blamed myself. They weren't Muslims, they were Christians. They kept their holy book with such respect. They actually ruined their book, even so, they keep their holy book in such a way in the church. We have to respect the Qur’ān on this level - even show more respect. That's why al-Ghujdawānī built such a building. He spent seven thousand gold coins. He made everything perfect, and made a building like a palace. He put only the Qur’ān inside. It was hand written, perfect. Standing up, not sitting down, by praising Allāh Almighty, as if he entered the sulṭān's presence, with good manners.

Our Shaykh Sulṭānu l-Awliyā’ told me: "Nāẓim Efendi when al-Ghujdawānī went inside like that for the first time an addressing came: "Don't read me without having full ablution." He was puzzled because he had full ablution. He came on his tiptoes, with new clothes, wearing perfume. "If you don't have full ablution don't touch me". Asta‘īdhu biLlāh. "Lā yamassuhu illā l-muṭahharūn." (56:79) Muṭahhar means someone, who is extremely clean. The deeper meaning is - "Someone, who is clean inside and outside may touch us, may read us, can read the Qur’ān." Full ablution means inside and outside. "Say shahādah", suddenly the addressing came. Take the shahādah, which will break the ego's rebellion; renew and show your belief in Our presence - Ashhadu an lā ilāha illā Llāh wa ashhadu anna Muḥammadan ‘abduhu wa rasūluhu (saw). After saying it three times - now there is no trace of disbelief. Now you entered the circle of belief. Repent. We aren't worthy of this presence with our egos. "With repentance, 100 times, stand in our presence" - this is the addressing. A hundred times "astaghfiru Llāh, astaghfiru Llāh, astaghfiru Llāh." We repent from all the sins, big and small, mistakes, rebellion, forgetfulness. We repent from all this O Lord! We ask Your forgiveness. Our bodies are clean with water that cleans us from sins, with shahādah that cleans our secrets, with repentance that cleans our spirituality. We reach an external perfect cleanliness. Then he ordered to read. "Now read, read My words."

The more you respect the words of Allāh, the more you are respected. Reading the Qur’ān is like this. How can we say the meaning? Where are we, where is the Qur’ān to say its meaning? So, not knowing your limit starts here. Many people today, even if they don't know the language of the Qur’ān, they cross their limits and talk about the Qur’ān. And they talk about the meaning of the Qur’ān. They are so ignorant, they don't even know the Arabic letters. The Muslims crossed their limits in this way. The Qur’ān is the word of Allāh. That's why we fear from saying the verse directly and give a meaning for it. The manner of all the holy ones is to give a ṣuḥbah, give an advice. They keep good manners with Allāh (jj). They say "what we say is from these meanings that this verse refers to." There is a sign in this verse about what we say, and they keep their manners. Mā shā’a Llāh our scholars always read verses first, then they read ḥadīth. This is the meaning of the verse. This is the meaning of the ḥadīth.

Mawlānā Jalāluddīn Rūmī said six volumes of Mathnawī Sharīf, may Allāh sanctify his secret. Last week there was Shab-i ‘Arus. They put on us a conical hat also. Haven't you seen it? That holy person, may Allāh sanctify his secret, he is a honourable servant of Allāh, one of the Prophet's, ‘alayhi ṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salām, beloved nation. Mawlānā is our master also. Yes. He made six volumes of Mathnawī Sharīf. Take a look at that - Mathnawī Sharīf. Does he read a verse and say - this is the meaning? Or he says and then refers to the verse? We went that far. They don't know their limits. People are clueless about what they say, there is no authorised person. When he isn't authorised, how will he know his limits? Now the highest religious rank make them read khuṭbah in that way. They let you interpret the meaning. "We interpret the meaning so people understand". If people understood one word from what you read, there would have been many scholars. By reading, understanding, many scholars would result. They don't have a clue.

Imām al-Ghazālī, Muḥyī d-dīn ibn ‘Arabī, Shāh-i Naqshband, Imām Rabbānī - all these big saints said: "It is referring to this from the oceans of meanings. We take from there. These words are from this ocean." So that's why educated people have increased but scholars have decreased. This is a sign of the end of times said the Prophet (saw). Yaqillu l-‘ulamā’ wa yakthiru l-khuṭabā - "Scholars decrease, preachers increase." There are so many people to read khuṭbah. They say "I interpret this verse. This is the verse and its meaning. This is the ḥadīth and its meaning." O people understand, get used to it. Aşık Ali. And when educated people don't know the good manners of ṭarīqah, they fail to respect the Qur’ān. They fail to respect. They fail to respect the Prophet (saw) and his ḥadīth. Who are we to interpret the Qur’ān, interpret the ḥadīth? Try to take from the ocean of Qur’ān from those who are giving. The Prophet (saw) was authorised to give, ‘alayhi ṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salām. He gave from the meanings of the Qur’ān for 23 years. He took and related.

Today's ignorant people have reduced the number of the Prophet's ḥadīth to a handful, you can count with the fingers that you use for dhikr. They reduced it to that because of their heedlessness - unauthorized people, who don't know their limits. Respect and praise the Qur’ān. That's why I'm scared to read a verse and say something, because it isn't our limit. We can only relate to you what the holy ones give to our heart. Listen a bit and make known one's limit, this is the purpose. Respect the Qur’ān. Respect the ḥadīth of the Prophet (saw). Until you understand the Qur’ān, until you understand the holy ḥadīth, give service so that you attain support. Whoever gives service to Allāh and His messenger - drops from the meaning-oceans are given to the ones, who serve their way. Whoever drinks from those drops, finds real life, becomes from the immortals. Whoever wants immortality, eternal life, should drink from the Qur’ān, from the ḥadīth. They are the eternal life springs. Eternal life is the real life. Allāh says; "Drink. Drink. I made it permissible for you". But we don't know how. We can't drink. When we can't drink, we stay dead in this world. On the Day of Judgement they go to Allāh's presence without having taken that real life - rotten, in a state of decay. They stay rotten in the world and the hereafter. But the Qur’ān came to enliven people, to revive people. There is Yūnus Emre also, he has many couplets. Does he ever give the meaning of verses? Does he give the meaning of ḥadīth? They pour like a river from the verses and ḥadīth. Whoever drinks, they enliven. It is important to make people drink. There's no one left. I'm travelling - not to one or two countries. I'm going around the Islamic world as well as the Western world. Maybe there are hidden ones there. No. There's nobody to enliven these people. There are many loudmouth people. The number of khaṭīb (preachers) will increase. The ones reading khuṭbah will increase but there won't be scholars. We find so many of them but there isn't anyone, who will enliven people by giving from the wisdom oceans, belief, and love oceans.

Shortly and precisely - may Allāh (jj) make it easy for us to have good manners and know our limits. May Allāh make us keep good manners for the Qur’ān and ḥadīth without directly saying: "from Qur’ān or ḥadīth." The Qur’ān was interpreted by our Prophet (saw) in 23 years. It was completed. 23 years is not an easy matter. The Qur’ān was completed in 23 years. It was completed externally. Also its meaning was complete. Allāh Jalla wa ‘Alā, poured its meaning into the Prophet's (saw) pure heart. He opened the fountains of life to all people, who will come until the Day of Judgement. Saying "Come and drink, bathe in it, find life ". May Allāh (jj) make us from the nations respecting him (saw). There are many corrupt people, who don't keep good manners to the Prophet (saw). May Allāh (jj) protect us from their mischief.

Biḥurmati l-Ḥabīb, biḥurmati l-Fātiḥah.
