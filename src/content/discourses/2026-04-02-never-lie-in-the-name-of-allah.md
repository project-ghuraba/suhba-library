As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
وَأَنَّا ظَنَنَّآ أَن لَّن تَقُولَ ٱلْإِنسُ وَٱلْجِنُّ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ كَذِبًا
<br>“Wa annā ẓanannā al lan taqūla l-insu wa l-jinnu ʿalAllahi kaḏhibā”<br>
“And verily, we thought that men and jinn would not utter a lie against Allah.” (Qur’ān 72:05). ṢadaqAllahu l-ʿAẓīm.

In Qur’ān ʿAẓīmu sh-Sha’n, our Prophet ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam says about the matter of Jinn: they heard the Qur’ān and realised that jinn, like humans, could act according to their own minds and lie in the name of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla saying, “Do this, do that.”

However, this way is the right way. Therefore, those who lie in the name of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla and say things that Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla did not say or want, will incur Allah’s wrath and punishment.

Therefore, one must be careful about these things. There are many people who sell their ākhirah for the sake of this dunyā. Some claim to be scholars, saying and doing whatever they want for their own benefit. When they tell people that this is Allah’s command, they face punishment themselves, and those they led astray also face punishment. The punishment for every person they led astray falls upon them. They bear the same burden of sin. As much people they lead astray, as much punishment they will suffer.

Dunyā is temporary. A person who sells his ākhirah, his eternal life, for the sake of dunyā is foolish and has no mind. Because this life is temporary and short. To value dunyā more than the eternal life is not at all rational. That is both foolishness and faithlessness. Faith, believing in the unseen, believing in ākhirah, is obligatory for a believer, a Muslim. Believing in them is fard. Those who believe in them are mindful. They do not sell their ākhirah for this dunyā. Dunyā passes in an instant; even if one lives for a thousand years, it will still come and go. But the ākhirah is eternal. 'Abad' means eternity. There is no death after that.

Therefore, may Allah protect us. May Allah protect us from people who lie in the name of Allah and say things that Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla did not say. May Allah protect us also from saying that, knowingly or unknowingly. And if we were to say so, our Prophet ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam would say, “Even I would say ‘Astaghfirullah, Tawbah Astaghfirullah’ seventy times every day.”

Therefore, we repent and seek forgiveness every time for the things we do, knowingly or unknowingly.

May Allah forgive us all, inshā’Allah.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
