There isn't anyone who doesn't have his shayṭān. Everyone has a shayṭān. There is shayṭān who cheats some people saying "My brother, my hips are thin. I am weak. And you are my friend. You run like a horse mā shā’a Llāh. I can't catch up with you. So why don't you give me a ride? If you let me ride you, I will wander around. I will show you many places and will take you anywhere without effort so that you get pleasure." Because he gets pleasure when you let him ride you. "And if you become my donkey, you will get pleasure. Actually, you should become my horse. You will get so much pleasure." And many people let him ride them. Thus, they put shayṭān on their shoulders and shayṭān makes them run.

Where does he make them run to? Of course, where they run is not a good place. He makes them run to a bad place. He doesn't let it be a good place. He leads them to run to a bad place. Some say "Walk by me. I am your friend but I can't let you ride me. I can't carry your load. But if you want to come with me, come. I won't be against it." And shayṭān goes along. Shaytan says "If so, I will come with you. I know the way very well." What are the ways he knows well? It means he knows all the sewage ways, which are lovely to people's egos, the sewage channels. He says "I've been to all the dirtiest sewage channels many times. But what a pleasure, what a taste in those sewage channels! You can dive in and come up. So much pleasure!" A channel of dirt. "My brother, that's not dirt. It's rose water. Just come with me. Try drinking from it, you'll see Chania and Konya. Let me cheat you. Come with me. I know all kinds of sewage channels. Because there are different colors. They have different odors, different types. There are liquid ones and solid ones. I will show you all kinds. If you taste it once, you won't be able to leave it. But how nice it is!"
