As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, madad yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī,

Sayyidunā Quṭb ‘Uthmān madad. Our ṭarīqah exists with ṣuḥbah. Our goal is to be with good people in good gatherings. Mawlānā used to come to Famagusta since he was young. Famagusta is a blessed place, mā shā’a Llāh. At times there were hundreds of brothers coming. Something happened and then the people became less or more. It is a place Mawlānā paid attention to, Famagusta. Because many shaḥīds, martyrs gave their lives to make it a place of Islām.

Since the time of our ProphetNone Ṣaḥābah came to this island of Cyprus. After Ṣaḥābah, their followers came. At a certain point, for hundreds of years it was dominated and oppressed by the Christians. After that a fatwá was issued to take back this place, as it is an important place. If it was not taken, there would be danger coming to Islām and Muslims. So then by the order of the Ottoman Sultan, from Anatolia shukr to AllāhNone this country was taken not by a hundred, but by a thousand martyrs. There are martyrs everywhere, everywhere Awliyā’ and Ṣaḥābah. Even though there is an inappropriate situation now, it is not important. It is temporary. It is neither important, nor worthy. They can make everything with gold. It has no value. Value comes from the spiritual owners of this place. And one of them is Sayyidunā Quṭb ‘Uthmān.

There is Canbolat Pasha, Hala Sultan at the top. Apart from them, there are hundreds of Ṣaḥābah. Some of their places are known. The rest are not known. Their respect is high in the presence of AllāhNone. Who helped Islām is Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. For HisNone honour, shukr to AllāhNone we are given this place. May AllāhNone increase their stations. May their support be with us and may AllāhNone show the right way to those who live here now. May they turn back to right way. May they be good servants.

A good servant is a good man. Who is not a good servant, is not a good person. They say they are good. You cannot be a witness to yourself because people always say they are good. People never call themselves bad. Even the worst person claims to be good. Let others say good of you in order for it to be true. So we wish for these people to be good. Because everything is by Allāh'sNone command. Hearts are in Allāh'sNone hands. HeNone can turn hearts into good and bad. We have no power in it. We can only pray. And a prayer is a big weapon. People with biggest weapons in this world can remain weak in some things – not in some, but in most things. With prayers, AllāhNone conquers them with this weapon. Therefore, prayers are important.

May AllāhNone help our people see the right way and the truth. Because the truth is all clear but people are blind and their ears don't hear. "Wa-lahum a‘yunun lā yubṣirūna bihā wa-lahum ādhānun lā yasmaʿūna bihā" (7:179), says the āyah. They have eyes that don't see and ears that don't hear. What is it that they don't see and don't hear? The truth. They see what it is not true. They hear what it is not true. They believe this to be true. They are unlucky people. People who assume as true what is not true but false, they are unlucky. Why are they unlucky? People have everything. They have cars, houses, and money. Why would they be unlucky? They are unlucky because all of those things are imagination and temporary. They can be rich up to seven generations, it has no value. Valuable is to see the truth and obey the truth. And truth is one. Truth is AllāhNone. The rest is imagination. All of it is temporary. Most people don't see this one truth. Muslims also don't see it. Non-Muslims don't see it anyway. And Muslims are cheated. Therefore, may AllāhNone let all of us see and obey the truth, let us follow the truth. May our people see that truth because if they see it, they will have no troubles, with Allāh'sNone permission.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq.
