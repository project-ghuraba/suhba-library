As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh ‘alá n-ni‘mah, wa-shukru liLlāh. Praises and shukr to AllāhNone we realised ‘Īd today. It is the means for goodness, in shā’a Llāh. May AllāhNone accept our worship. Ḥajjīs became ḥajjī now. They returned by doing their duty of ḥajj. Who couldn't return, who passed away, has stayed there. This is a nice thing.

And qurbān (animal sacrifice) is made here. Some people did in their place. Those who couldn't, sent to poor places. There are people who eat meat once a year or even once in several years. It went to them and was very much accepted by them. Their du‘ā’ is accepted in the presence of AllāhNone. In shā’a Llāh, it will be the means for goodness. Our end will be good with their du‘ā’ and barakah. Certainly, qurbān...

Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam used to select an animal every year himself – nice big ones. Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam used to slaughter them, eat, and distribute. Also it is our tradition, thanks to AllāhNone. Muslims in many places follow this. In some places, they do it less – according to their madhhab. In some places, they do more. But shukr to AllāhNone, people, Muslims can benefit from the reward and from the meat as well. The best is to both eat and distribute it. But this opportunity is more difficult now. People can't do it. Instead, they can send its price to poor countries, have it slaughtered there and get more du‘ā’ from them.

Now some people are obstinate and insist on doing exactly what is written in ḥadīth. Without understanding the meaning of the ḥadīth, they say, "This is how it is," and follow it. If it were like that, nothing else would be necessary, not ṣaḥābah nor anything else. Where will you find out how it was done or performed? How will you know how to do it? You won't know, of course. But people nowadays think they are clever and claim that the ḥadīth says this: "You must slaughter and eat here." That is okay. In the time of our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam there was great poverty. Therefore, that was appropriate. Afterwards, whatever is appropriate is okay. Important is to make qurbān, which is wājib in our madhhab, wherever you perform it. As long as you give your name and do it in your name, that wājib is done. With its first drop of blood, you will be given the good tidings of paradise. With every hair, however many hairs there are on that animal, ten rewards are written for each one. That is alright.

Otherwise, you get so much du‘ā’ from the people who eat it. Therefore, it is acceptable for those who want to do it that way. Who can do the slaughtering themselves may do so. They can eat from the qurbān meat and share with neighbours. But people are eating meat every day now. They say it is expensive. They complain by saying this and that because they eat nothing but meat every day. Therefore, it's better when people, who can't afford meat, can eat it. Nevertheless, who wants to eat qurbān meat at home, may eat it. Many people go on vacation for ’Īd anyway. They go here and there. They don't have the opportunity. Who has the opportunity can give it to someone trustworthy who makes sure the qurbān is done. That is necessary. They might give to some place but it is unclear if it was done. It is unclear if qurbān was made or not. They must look into it. They must give it to people they can trust, and then it becomes the responsibility of people you trust. You are not responsible anymore. They will be responsible. AllāhNone gives you thawāb and reward according to your intention. May AllāhNone bless it.

Today is the last day of ‘Īd. It has passed, shukr to AllāhNone. Our life is passing. We can't stop it anyway. But it is nice when it passes in worship. In shā’a Llāh, this ‘Īd has passed nicely with brothers who come and go. They come for ziyārah (visiting) for the pleasure of AllāhNone, to visit the maqām of Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim. May AllāhNone accept it from all of them and bless it. May their good du‘ā’ be accepted, in shā’a Llāh. May AllāhNone let us reach more years, in shā’a Llāh.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh we are in living, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, this fourth day of ‘Īdu l-Aḍḥá. We are thanking AllāhNone for what HeNone gave us from HisNone favour – to celebrate this days which it is most blessed day in the year, which with happiness, with ‘ibādah. And many of people they was in ḥajj, in pilgrimage, they finish their ḥajj obligatory [obligation]. And they become ḥajjī – we call them. They are now finish; their everything complete there what they must do. So it is, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, AllāhNone bless them, AllāhNone accept their ‘ibādah. Many of them they are nearly return. But also some of them, subḥāna Llāh, AllāhNone make them, bless them, grant them to pass away in this holy land. And HeNone accept them in there, HisNone Divine Presence, and near ProphetNone or in Ka‘bah, in Makkah, also big luck. Very lucky people who pass away in that blessed area, most holy area. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, AllāhNone bless them. AllāhNone give us from their reward also. AllāhNone accept their ziyārāh.

And we are here also, everywhere in Muslim place, they was doing their ‘ibādah and doing ‘Īd feast, cutting sheep for sake of AllāhNone, for by order of AllāhNone. And they eating and feeding people also. This is important also for to make whole Muslim be happy with what AllāhNone grant them. It is not farḍ, but in our madhhab [it is] wājib. Other madhhab, they are, it is sunnah. ProphetNone, heNone was doing this whole time and heNone was choosing very strong sheep and heNone cut by himself. This is sunnah to cut in your house with by yourself. But now, nowadays, people it is difficult for them to do this. But sunnah to do this. But nowadays, as we said, it is not easy to do this. And many people they give this for trustful [trustworthy] people to cut them, behind of them in other country, poor country which they are cannot eat meat maybe once a year. I hear once we sending some place, one man he is nearly forty year, first time he eat meat. He is not vegetarian but he is not finding meat to eat. So it is real reward from AllāhNone, I think this.

Many people, nowadays people, they are just looking in ḥadīth or Qur’ān and they said, "This is not acceptable. This is not sunnah. With sunnah, you must cut by yourself and you eat." But sunnah of course, but this ignorant people they know, they must know this ḥadīth. You cannot by yourself to do what ḥadīth saying without learning meaning of this ḥadīth. With this ḥadīth, of course, time of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, also there was very few food and people was not easy to find something to eat. But nowadays, every day, people they not eating – only meat. Not eating anything else. So no need for us maybe, one day not eat meat. Give this people once to eat in once a year. But also of course for people, if they can cut, it is good to eat from their what they cut from sheep. But it is not important this. Important for to give for good trustful people and by your intention, they cut by for your name this. And it is reward coming again same what Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, saying: from first of drop of blood from this sheep, AllāhNone open door of paradise for you. And for each hair of sheep, AllāhNone reward you ten ḥasanāt [as for ten good deeds]. And there is millions of hair in sheep or goat or other. So AllāhNone, it will be also you will be rewarded with this.

Many people they are saying this. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, Ḥanafī madhhab, they, it is wājib for us. But for other they make it, they not giving any important for sunnah. This which it is sunnah, it is reward from Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam for you. Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, heNone is not in need for you. AllāhNone not in need for you but HeNone, from HisNone mercy, HeNone gave you this chance to make something for your next life. Not just keep your money here and after they will finish this money, your children or other. HeNone give you chance and it's not so much to, to pay for this. But AllāhNone give everybody something but they are not appreciate this. They thought it is like heavy burden for them but it is, no. Every time you do something you will be more happy, you will be more stronger with belief, with inspiration and good thing.

But, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are, for this we are saying we are happy with this days. All this days now passed away. ‘Īd finish. "Oh coming from our life this, gone this days." We are not sad. We are happy, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone make us to whole life to be in this like this, to be in obedient for AllāhNone, doing good, doing charity. And no, it is, our life not important. Important, if you are happy or not happy, you must be happy if you are in way of AllāhNone – this is happiness. But if you are not in this way, you must be regret and ask for forgiveness. AllāhNone will change for bad thing for good thing, in shā’a Llāh. Sins for reward. AllāhNone forgive us, in shā’a Llāh. Mubārak, in shā’a Llāh, this days. AllāhNone make us to be whole time like together, in shā’a Llāh.
