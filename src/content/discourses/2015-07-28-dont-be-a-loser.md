As-salamu alaikum wa rahmatullahi wa barakatuh. Audhu billahi min ash-shaytani r-rajim. Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. As-salatu wa s-salamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidi l-awwalina wa l-akhirin. Madad ya RasulAllah, madad ya Sadati Ashabi RasulAllah, madad ya Mashaykhina, dastur, Sheikh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheikh Muhammad Nazim al-Haqqani, madad. Tariqatuna s-sohbah, wa l-khayru fi jam'iyyah.

Allah Azza wa Jalla - there are ayats and surahs in the Qur'an. But mankind... It is probably the shortest surah of Qur'an, surat-ul Asr. "Wa l-asr" (103:1) Allah Azimu sh-Shan is swearing by His glory/vowing that people are losers. It seems like no one is losing, however, they are only missing one part. How people live gives them no benefit. The properties they have, beauty, fame, whatever they have no benefit. All of it is against them, says Allah Azza wa Jalla.

And there is one part - "illa lladhina amanu"(103:3) - Those who believe and invite others to the way of Haqq, and the patient ones who believe and are on the way of truth and are patient. But first, they should believe. Being patient or being on the right way without belief is not important. Important is to believe in Allah, then accept the truth. You must give everyone their rights and you must be patient in worldly matters. These are who are the winners. The rest are people who lose everything and are losers. All mankind is like this. No need to look at someone and say "He is so rich, this one's so beautiful, that one's so strong". No need to be happy about it. Allah Azza wa Jalla has said. He said it by swearing/vowing.

People, all of people... Those with faith are always in minority. People who follow their egos are the most. There are many people who obey shaitan. All of them put themselves in loss. Allah says: "Wa la tattabi'u khutuwati sh-shaytani innahu lakum 'aduwwun mubin"(2:208) Don't go in shaitan's steps. He is your big enemy. He is a very big enemy. He will take you over the cliff, ruin and kill you. Killing is nothing. More importantly, he'll destroy your akhira and take you to hell. Don't follow or obey him. Allah is showing the way. Who has a mind will follow Him. Because Allah Azza wa Jalla says that by swearing/vowing Allah is swearing/proclaiming in the ayat, there are many surahs, many ayahs in Quran, but there is, the shortest one Surat ul-Asr, it is shortest surah in Quran but it is giving all wisdom, all that human being should follow.

Allah swearing and He said all human beings, they are losers. All human beings losers. Only who they are believer, they are not loser. Wa l-asri inna l-insana lafi khusr, illa lladhina amanu(103:1-3) who believe and they saying to each other for to be following right, and be patient - these people, they are not losers. They are winners. But most of people they are losers because they are following their ego, they are following easy thing. Look like easy, but not easy, but they are thinking it is easy, following shaitan. And shaitan - Allah also saying wala tattabi'u khutuwati sh-shaytani innahu lakum aduwwun mubin (2:208)

Allah saying don't follow shaitan, he is the biggest enemy for you. He is huge enemy, he only... Don't follow his step. Because people following step of shaitan also, what he doing, step by step they following him. Don't follow him, he is big enemy for you. Follow what Allah saying. It is shortest surah and it is giving summary of advice for whole human being. Because whole human being they are losers. No benefit for beauty, no benefit for strong, no benefit for richness, no benefit for health. No benefit for anything from this for human being. Only benefit to be winners- to be believer and to be accepting right and to be patient. This is 3 thing, who following this he save himself, herself from hell, from bad end. Others losers.

Why they are loser? It's real loser, because they will be, they lose all next life, eternal life. Not like this small period. For small period, you lose whole your future. Now, yesterday some, our crazy one, he is saying his wife she is saying she is looking for future and the future not good for her and she is coming from Syria. Whole Syrian people no future for them and she is still thinking for future and future what it is? Maybe 10 year, 20 year, 30 year here. Real future is eternal life. The people must thinking about this. This is permanent. Permanent life this not temporary. This is people, they are mixing future for eternal life. Don't be lose that life. This life it is like this, like that, rich, happy/not happy, thinking/not thinking, it is coming and going, but if you are not believer and not accept right, you will be the loser. Allah make us to understand this, inshaAllah.

Wa min Allahi t-tawfiq, al-Fatiha.
