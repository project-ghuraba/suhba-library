Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

People want to leave something behind in this world. Most people try to do something so that they are remembered and their names are not forgotten. Those who can’t do it are sad because they couldn’t do it. Leaving an important legacy is better if it is for the sake of Allah. If it is for your own ego, then it is of no benefit whether you leave it or not. “We do it so they remember us when we leave.” If you say so, then it is for your own ego. As our Holy Prophet said, “If a person leaves three righteous things behind when he dies, that is the legacy. Either a righteous child, a good knowledge, or Sadaqah Jariyah (ongoing charity).” These are beneficial. If you leave unnecessary things behind, they will not benefit you.

Therefore, people cannot know for themselves what is useful and what is not useful. You have to follow the right path so that you know what Allah’s orders are. They are means to goodness and good deeds. You left a statue behind, you left this and that, you left knowledge that denies Allah… The more you lead people astray, the more your punishment will be in the hereafter. Good things must be left behind. If you can’t, you must at least do good for yourself, and be on Allah’s way for your benefit so that you leave having won the hereafter. What did you leave behind? “I left good offspring for the sake of Allah.” If you say so, you will gain rewards for their worship. “I left this, I left that, I left no faith.” If you say so, the more sins your children commit, the more they will come to you. May Allah protect us and grant us to leave good works insha’Allah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
