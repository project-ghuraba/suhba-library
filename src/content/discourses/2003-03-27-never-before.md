I will speak in English also, to you that, no mind people now - Europeans and other people - and reminding [them of] something that [n]ever happened on earth. To make war, one country with another country, and capturing a town or city and not giving trouble to inhabitants there? In spite, bringing so many things, food and everything then giving to them? This happened through whole historical time? period of mankind? Say.

sn: Never, never.

And for the sake of Allah, ever happened? Allah saying through His prophet's time "O People give your mercy and pity on My creatures on Earth, I am going to give to you My Mercy, divinely Mercy to you!" That Americans, when entering Basra, Najaf, Karbala and other countries in Iraq and bringing, after they are getting in, and bringing hundreds of this big trailers - every kind of food, aids to people. This happened?

sn: Never.

How? How that newspapers or broadcasting, or TV centers, blaming the America that they are invaders and they are doing their worst to Saddam even he did anything such a thing when he was rushing and overtaking Kuwait? sn: Never. Ah! What is that? Syrians also. They fight inside, in Aleppo, in Homs, in Hamma, they bring [food] or they bring only fire? Palestinians also. Egyptians also. What is it? What is it? Therefore, they are going to gain the sword of divinely revenge, sword just granted to US U.S. (United States) to use it against every tyrants. When they are finishing that, they are coming and to give finally that sword to Mahdi (as). Finish. Everyone must know this. If they are insisting on their wrong ideas and attacking that they should be killed also. They should be taken away also. Bitti (finished).

What we did for ourselves as Turks? Iranians, what they did through themselves, to each other? What did Russians to Germans [in the] second war? What he did? You know? Germans or no? Huh? You knowing or you're forgetting?

g: They took everything.

Then Germans cursing Americans, why he... what? Russians just destroyed - what is the name? - Chechenya. Chechenyan city? Grozny. I went there, I never see any building - only two lines of stones. All finished. No one speaking, no one saying from Turks, from Arabs, from Muslims. In Bosnia they make everything down, destroyed, and thousands of people they have been killed. No Islamic league, Arab league, shaytan league? Not saying anything. Now when America coming to save people from that, from that jabbar tyrant, whole small tyrants getting up. Should be killed. should be killed, should be killed - that one and whom [is] with him. Shari'a says if 1,000 people attacking on one person to kill him, and only [the] closer ones killing him but 1,000 people taking their swords or their pistols - all of them Shari'a ordering to be killed. Where you are Arabs? Turk where you are? Hadith ash-Sharif. Iktub

Arab league - which country making a war and capturing a place and bringing food and aids to people? Not killing them, or not leaving them to to die by themselves. Has [it] happened?

sn: Never.

Ever happened?

sn: Never.

Why they are taking? And they are against America they are running with that tyrant. Huh? I was, I was asking if there was a long period for qiyama, I was asking that Allah Almighty to send [to] all of them, over them tyrants like the tyrant in Baghdad. But no time. Therefore, they should be killed - without coming a tyrant they should be taken away also. Bitti. (Finished)

'Merhamet dir?' (it is mercy?) How you can say there is mercy through that person in Baghdad if he is using for his nation these chemical weapons and another worse weapons and he killed hundred thousand of people? No one speaking on it and they are saying fifteen people - bombing America [a] market. What a big, big crime, biggest crime, biggest crime for America - bombing a market and killing 15 people. You are tyrant! One million people killed in his country and out of his country. No one say this? It is forgotten? Huh? What? And Germans they so quickly forgetting what Russia did for them? And they are coming with Russians now? Leftist? Hunh? Blame to German nation. Turks, leave Turks.

I am asking from Allah Almighty from divinely presence, through holy presence of Rasulullah (saws), Ya Rasulullah, I am humbly asking to give me much more time and perhaps I am that one only knowing such a secret things about nations, to finish I am beginning - I am asking to finish it. Insha'Allah should be acceptable. May Allah forgive me and bless - other people whom their hearts not reaching a satisfaction they may look something to come on their heads. I am running to Prophet (saws) and I am trusting on their protection dhahiran wa batina (outwardly and inwardly), from every direction and to save good ones. And bad ones must be taken away.

For the honour of most honoured one Sayyidina RasulAllah (saws) Fatiha
