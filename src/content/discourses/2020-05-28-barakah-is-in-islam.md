Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Alhamdulillah, Allah ﷻ created us as Muslims, and let us live as Muslims. May we leave with faith insha’Allah. Whoever is honored with Islam attains a great virtue and attains a great favor. Every goodness is in Islam, every ease is in Islam and every beauty is in Islam. There are different kinds of blessings. ‘اهَ وصُ حْ ُت لََ ِاللَّه َ ةمَ عْ ِن اودُّ ُعَت نِإوَ ’, ‘And if you would count the favors of Allah, never could you be able to count them.’ (Qur’ān 16:18). If you count them, you cannot finish. It does not end with counting.

One of them is Barakah (abundance), which is only found in Islam, not in anything else. That’s why, billions of people, Muslims live in Barakah. And the rest get a little bit of this Barakah and Rizq (sustenance). Since our Holy Prophet ﷺ said about the Awliya, “نورصنت مهبو نوقزرت مهب”, “For their honor, you get Rizq. For their honor, you are aided.” Without them, your Rizq will not be given. Allah ﷻ does not give a drop of water to the disbeliever, but they also benefit from their Barakah. The most important thing is that the Barakah is in Muslims. There is no such thing as Barakah in anyone except in Islam. For the honor of their faces, all humanity gets Barakah and Rizq. This Barakah, even if it is a little, you can be content with a little. When He gives Barakah to someone, it becomes like mountains, it is limitless, it does not end.

People of this time have forgotten that. They are running after money and after property, not after Barakah. They have forgotten Barakah. We should make Dua to Allah ﷻ that there be Barakah in ourselves, our time, our progeny, our children, our property, our money and everything else. Then, the Barakah will not end. When there is no Barakah, even if there are mountains, it does not last, it ends.

We see here, with the Barakah of the Ottomans and the Barakah of the Muslims, even the poor are getting along. These Kafirs have neither Barakah nor mercy, and they have taken control of this world, robbed this world and have not finished yet. They can’t even manage themselves. May Allah ﷻ protect us. May Allah ﷻ give strength to our faith so that the Barakah is with us Allah ﷻ willing.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
