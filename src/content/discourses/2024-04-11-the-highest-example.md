As-Salāmu ‘alaykum.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Wa-innaka la-‘alá khuluqin ‘aẓīm” (68:4). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘aẓīm. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is telling about the attribute of our Holy ProphetNone. HeNone says, “HeNone is the most perfect person. The most perfect person is the one who has the best adab (manners): our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam.” HisNone way is for all humanity. AllāhNone sent himNone not only for Muslims, but for all humanity. HisNone way is the way of light. Those who follow hisNone way will attain all kinds of goodness.

Following hisNone way is happiness. It is the means to all kinds of goodness. The greatest goodness is to drink water from hisNone Pool of Kawthar in ākhirah, to drink from the water of Al-Kawthar. There will be no evil left in the person who drinks from that water. There will be no more waswasah, no more [devilish] thoughts. He will not be afraid of anyone. He will not hold a grudge against anyone. He will not be hostile to anyone. In Paradise, there aren’t such things as there are in this world. To cleanse oneself of them, one drinks from this Pool of Kawthar and enters. You cannot find these attributes and bad deeds in Paradise. Of course, they exist in this world as a test. But they don't exist in Paradise, in ākhirah. And there are so many of them in hell.

Therefore, the way of our Holy ProphetNone is the way. It is the beautiful way. It is a gift from AllāhNone to people. It is a blessing from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla to our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. And shukr to AllāhNone, we are from hisNone Ummah. It is a greater blessing for us. These holidays were given to the Ummah for hisNone sake. With these holidays come both apparent and spiritual gifts. We thank AllāhNone for that. May our holidays be blessed, in shā’a Llāh.

HeNone says: “The ‘Īd of the fasting person is both when breaking his fast at ifṭār, and again in Paradise.” We attained the first now. In shā’a Llāh, the second one is to drink from the Pool of Kawthar and attain that great celebration. In shā’a Llāh, we all attain that. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying in Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n: Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Wa-innaka la-‘alá khuluqin ‘aẓīm” (68:4). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘aẓīm. For Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam: “YouNone are perfect in everything, perfection, especially from good behave, youNone have. YouNone are the highest one.” The example for human being to follow, this is very nice example for whole human being, how they must be. They must – they are writing law since thousands of years, but law of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, what Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam showing us. If you follow this, you will be happy, no any problem in this world. So that all world will be with peace and happiness, with adab of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, good behave. This is most important. When you have good behaviour, you not be oppress anybody, you don't make problem for anybody.

Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam was showing way of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, the best way. This way take you to Paradise, to drink from hisNone Kawthar water, which it is – when you enter and you drink this – all this bad character, bad thing you have, it will be clean. No more. In Jannah, you don't have jealousy, you don't have enmity, you don't have hatred, you don't have lying, anything from dunyā you have. All, in Jannah, it will be outside. When you drink this, all this clean. you never again to be like this. You have bad character – all good character. And this is way of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, especially for Ramaḍān time. HeNone said: “When mu’min, be happy twice: when he break his fast Maghrib time and when he drink water, be in Paradise.” Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we get first one, in shā’a Llāh, second one also. We sure, in shā’a Llāh, we be, in shā’a Llāh, with Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. AllāhNone give us this happiness also, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
