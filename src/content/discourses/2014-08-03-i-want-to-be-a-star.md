Ya Salam! Allah Jalla Jalaluh, He put so many nice things in our life. Allah Almighty He give us very beautiful things in our life. Allah Almighty He was so generous to us in our life. Allah Almighty He give everybody individual beauty. And Allah Almighty He give everybody individual enjoyment. But what we are doing? We are taking this individual beauty and make it so ugly. And we are taking this individual happiness and make it so sad. And we are taking our beautiful life and make it miserable. This is what we are doing.

Yahu, Allah Almighty never give you sadness. Never give you bad things, never give you miserable life. This we have to know. If we are realising this point everything will be changed upside down and will go for goodness. Only matter of looking. There is difference between look, and look. Somebody tell one old man that you are becoming younger and that old man answer 'I think you need glasses'. He say, 'I think you need glasses'.

Actually it shows how the old man he is looking and the other man, how he is looking. The old man he is looking from the miserable side of it, 'No, I am not young anymore. You need glasses'. And the young man he is looking from the beautiful side of it and he say, 'You become younger, maybe you become nicer and he is looking from the nice concept and the beautiful concept. So this is the difference between two lookings. It is a very simple example but it is giving how we have to look. It is giving you idea how we have to look.

Allah Almighty, He put everything in the stage. We are in big stage in life. It is life-stage, everybody have role, everybody come and he make his role and he go. And this role will never be repeated. One role only. If you are clever to make your role as becoming the star of the stage and it is very rare to be star of the stage, like Maulana Sheikh, he is the star of the stage, and we are his followers. So Maulana Sheikh, he show us how to be star of the stage. He show us. He didn't keep this secret for himself and say 'no, I have to be the shining one and the rest must be follow.' No, he say 'no, you can be the star, that is available for you also'. You have to follow me and everybody will be star.

From where we can understand this? When the Sahaba asking Rasulullah, 'Ya Rasulullah, what will happen after you leave?' He say 'As'habika n-nujum, fabi ayyihim iqtadaytum ihtadaytum'. Say, you will be as a star and anybody he is following you, he will be in hidaya, he will be in the right way, he will be in the good way. This is so simple. Rasulullah, who is Rasulullah? Rasulullah is our biggest example. No doubt, he is the biggest. And who is following his footprint? The Sahaba, the tabi'een, then taba tabi'een.

Then it is going like this because this association it was not finished. This is what the enemy of Islam, they think, this association we can finish it with fitna, we can finish it with these stupid extremers, we can finish it with Salafis, with Wahabis. We can finish it with other methods, we can finish it with killing. It is brains factories to finish this association and Allah Almighty say this religion is belonging to Me, inna d-deena indallahi l-islam (3:19). This, it is belonging to Me. It these brains, the factories of the brains of fasaad and trying to make this big, shining star to finish, they are wrong.

This, my advice for this, how to look. The beginning, said how to look. If they are looking from the beautiful concept these brains will be working for Islam, they will be working for Islam to be shining. They will be working to make Islam more and more and more because it's the most beautiful religion, it's the most tolerant religion, it is the most elite religion, it is high class. If they have such things, you know what they will do? They will tell you that you have to have a card that says you are elite person to enter Islam. How it has so high level, this Islam.

So high that Allah Almighty, He make it available for everybody. If it is in their hand, they will say no, you have to have certain conditions to enter Islam. Allah Almighty say: ash-hadu an la ilaha ill'Allah wa ash-hadu anna Muhammadan Rasulullah, you are in Islam. You are in Islam, we don't need anything else. You are Muslim, finish. You say by mistake, finish. Also you are, it's counted. Allah Almighty He is looking for any excuse to put you in Islam.

This is how Allah Almighty is asking His servant to look, not like the old man, he say you need glasses, no, I'm looking from the beautiful concept of seeing you and putting this and I am insisting to tell you 'yes, you become younger, you become nicer, you become this. Say, 'no, no, no, you put glasses'. This is Allah Almighty, He is asking us to look from the beautiful concept and He is asking us to look for this Islam, that huge association, you are not able to make it down with blowing, phh, phh. I think in Siberia there is one hole the other day I see it, it is God’s natural hole, and it is flaming. And they say we don't know when this will go. And you are with that big flaming and like children, say, ok, now it's birthday party, just blow up and make it too good, phh, phh, phh, and big fire, flaming. You cannot come near it. And this is Islam. This is how Islam is great, how Islam is flaming. I am not saying Islam as a sun because sun is so far away and you cannot stand in the heat but this one okay, just to make it logical for the people.

These no-mind-factories people for them to think they are able to finish Islam and think by destroying the maqam or by destroying the mosque, people they will not love that maqam, and they will not rebuild that maqam and that mosque for the honour of the people sleeping under it, for the honour of Allah Almighty, to say Ya Rabbi, we are the one for You, we are the one sincere for You. Sincerity is the most precious thing in the heart of Muslim. Sincerity is the biggest weapon, nobody can take it.

I have a nice example. I used to have one friend, he was Kung-fu master and he was telling me they can take anything from you, your weapon, your anything, but they cannot take your Kung-fu knowledge because it's in you. And now I'm saying they can take everything, they can destroy everything but they cannot take the ikhlas (sincerity), it is in you. We have to work for ikhlas, we have to know the ikhlas. We have to be in ikhlas and we have to melt in ikhlas. This is very important.

What this is, is the fight between your ego and yourself, the ikhlas. This is the biggest fight. How to be in ikhlas to Allah Almighty, to Rasulullah and to your awliyaullah, to your Master, to your Sheikh. It is very important to keep this ikhlas. And we are hoping that Allah Almighty, He keep this ikhlas in us and make it stronger and stronger. And we are not going away unless we see Sayyidina al Mahdi and unless we be in his presence and unless we are intending to be with him.
