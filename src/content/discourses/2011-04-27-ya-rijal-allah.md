Mawlana Shaykh Mohamad Nazim al Haqqani an Nakshibendi 24 Jumādā al-Ūlā, 1432 A.H. Wednesday, April 27, 2011 Translated from Arabic by: http://saltanat.org/ Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Rahim Allah Almighty sent His Prophet (saws) as Bashiran and Naziran to bring good news and to warn. As Bashiran, he brought good news to those who believed what the Prophet (saws) brought from the Lord of the Worlds, The Almighty (swt). Allah! Jalla Jalalahu … Stand up and don't hesitate for even a split second! If we are sitting down, the least we can do is to stand up when His Name Almighty is mentioned. This is nothing; but,

Wa Khuliqa Al Insanu Daifan (4:28)

And man was created weak We are weak. However, a person is rewarded not for his actions, but for his intentions. Therefore our intention is that we would like to stand up in every second without sitting at all, just standing in the Divine Presence, Al Haqq (jwa). Allah Almighty sent His Prophet (saws) and dressed him with the attributes of Bashiran and Naziran, bringer of good news and bringer of warnings. Is it not like this? Mubashiran means ‘bringer of good news’, and Munziran means ‘warner’. Allah Almighty clearly showed everything that can improve and correct the situation of human beings in their life on earth. Therefore they were warned about what is harmful for them in their life on earth and in the hereafter. It is clear. Religion is clear and its target is very clear. Religion addresses Ulil Al-Abab (‘those who have a good understanding’). Isn't it like this? “Ya Ulil al-Abab” there is a Holy verse:

Inna fee khalqi alssamawati waalardi waikhtilafi allayli waalnnahari laayatin liolee alalbabi

(3:190)

Behold! in the creation of the heavens and the earth, and the alternation of night and day, there

are indeed Signs for men of understanding. But they don't believe. Human beings, individually and generally, are not accepting, or believe, or thinking. They do not think so as to believe. If they became believers, they would be in peace here in their worldly life with what is written for them. Allah promised the Truth; but people run away and prefer to follow imaginary things, unreal things, delusions. Everything that people imagine or think about this worldly life is coming from the deception of shaytan. shaytan is not visible, but it deceives. Its target is to deceive all human beings from the beginning of time until the end. The cause of all problems in the world and our lives is that human beings follow the deception of shaytans. This is the reason. The Holy Qur'an, with all its clarity, gives the human being the program for life: how to have a good and happy life in the world and in the eternal life of Akhirat (the here-after). But they run away. When they run away, a whip comes on them and threatens them. Allah Almighty threatens them to warn them. He warns them, right? Because He sent His Beloved (saws) as Bashiran wa Naziran, bringing good tidings and warning. When I was reading these two words in the Holy Qur'an, it came to my heart,

Man 'Arsalna Alayhi Hasiban (29:40)

And we send them a (hurricane, tornado, deadly storm wind, violent storm of sand and stones) ‘Hasiban’ … ‘Hasiban’ means what? - that which is coming from above onto them. The things that happened to Japan did not come from the heavens, they came from the earth. In the Holy Qur'an I says, “From above their heads.” Sometimes it happened over and over again, in other times and other worlds. What happened? - floods and earthquakes. May Allah protect us. This happened “from under their feet”. They were taken away and destroyed. From among earlier nations, Allah Almighty made many nations perish. Allah Almighty! I cannot say, “Their Lord destroyed them”. It would serve to give them honor if we say, “their Lord”. But we say Allah Almighty made them perish “from under their feet”. They did not have good manners; and they did not make any effort to repent. So then “We sent them hasiban” from the heavens. A few days ago, some news came from America. There was a hurricane in the ocean, coming from the skies. Then came a tornado. This is ‘hasiban’ - from up in the skies to the earth, not from the ocean. From the sea, we may send on human beings, the generation of Adam (as), a tornado. Yursila Aleihim … On human beings will be sent a tornado. These things are examples, very small examples. They are just warnings for them, just as the rain begins with a few drops and then it comes into full downpour. The few drops are a sign. These are also just signs, these tornadoes on earth. They started here and there as a declaration to them: “Oh servants of Allah, be on the straight way. Keep straight.”

Kunu Qawwamina Bi al Qist (4:135)

Stand out firmly for justice. Oh servants of the Lord, keep the way of Prophets (as) who were sent from Heavens, from the angelic realm of the heavens to you. Keep their ways. Otherwise when it begins, We may send things that are not like the weapons you invented. No! You became impressed with atomic reactors. You became deeply awed. 'How can all these atomic centers be displaced?' you asked yourselves. You are confused now because you saw one tornado which in a few seconds can take your atomic centers from their foundations and throw them in the air. Oh people! Be careful. Oh people take your precaution. You turned out to be tyrants, to have the attribute of tyrants. It is not suitable for the servant to be magnificent. Jabbar (magnificence, might) is for Allah Almighty. You seek (within your capacity) to be mighty (jabbar) on earth. But the holy verse says,

“Wa ahlakna al jabbarin”

We destroyed the tyrants. In one second We may throw them away. With one tornado We may take them from the earth, from the cities where they are with everything in them, and throw them into the sea. There is enough power. La Hawla wa La Quwwata illa Billah Al Aliyul Azim. “Then We sent to them Hasiban”. ‘Hasiban’ is the sign of the tornado. Oh, Allah Almighty, keep us protected; and we ask Your forgiveness. Allahumma Ij'alna fi Huz'fika, wa Huf'zika wa Amanika. Tawba Ya Rabbi, tawba Ya Rabbi, tawba Ya Rabbi astaghfirullah. Allah Almighty will ask. He asks the common people [from the age of ignorance]... (Subhana Allah! The Holy Qur'an explains itself clearly, with some verses making other verses clearer):

Wa Idha Al Maw'udatu Su'ilat (81:8)

When the female infant who was buried alive is asked

Bi'ayyi Dhanbin Qutilat (81:9)

For what sin was she killed? Allah Almighty will question these are tyrants, tyrants. He will ask them: “For what sin did you take lives, and more lives, and more lives? Isn't it like this Hisham Efendi? SH: This is a new interpretation. It is important; it is the truth. We thought that it was only during the first period of ignorance for those who used to bury their daughters alive; but the holy verse is in a present, continuous verb form. “Wa 'Idha Al-Maw'udatu…” – “When the female infant...” Allah created His servant and placed in him or her the secret of life for that person to live and survive. But those tyrants killed “Bi'ayyi Dhanbin Qutilat”. For what sin did they kill? Nowadays those tyrants are committing the same on the children of Adam, the nation of the Beloved (saws), who don't have weapons and who are screaming, “We don't want these presidents! They mistreated us. They tortured us. We don't want these people.” This is the tyrants’ fault. The people do not have weapons and you bring tanks and shoot on them with missiles. These tanks are supposed to protect people and their belief, their homes, and their dignity, to protect and secure them so they are not attacked by enemies. Why are you using these weapons on people who have no weapons at all? You bring tanks and kill them. For what sin are you killing them? You use snipers why? For what reason are you killing these people? Did they come to you, to fight you? No! You used the snipers from above them. Do you know if they have harmed anyone? Or do they have weapons to fight you back? For what are you killing them? They are also considered to be like the newly born girls who were buried. And that verse now covers today's killing in the same way.

Wa Idha Al Maw'udatu Su'ilat (81:8)

When the female infant who was buried alive is asked

Bi'ayyi Dhanbin Qutilat (81:9)

For what sin was she killed? Oh Allah almighty, take them. Oh Saints of Allah … We shouldn't call to Allah. We call the men of Allah (Rijal Allah). It is not good manners. La Ilaha illa Allah. Aleyka bihim Ya Rijal Allah … Allah… When they are given permission the men of Allah are enough. Enough... Ya Rijal Allah, support us with Divine support. [Mawlana asks for a short recitation.] Rijal Allah Ain unnah Bi awunillah Wa kunu awnan lana asalah la bifadhlilla And after this these wahabis, these shaytans, would also say these words are shirk. They only want to bring Allah down [for help]. Other than that, they do not accept. Tawba astaghfirullah. This is the point. We want also to call the servants of Allah. Oh Men of Allah, servants of Allah, support us with the support of Allah and be for us a support so that we may reach the favor of Allah. This is the Shariat; but they accuse us of committing shirk. How could this be? Where is the shirk? And we are saying there is no god but Allah; there is no partner with Allah. Astaghfirullah, astaghfirullah, Tawba Ya Rabbi, tawba... Link to video:
