As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Inshā’Allāh we will be going to a gathering but we will say two words for the sake of Allah. Inshā’Allāh, it will be of benefit for me and for all of us, inshā’Allāh.

Today alḥamdulillāh we made a big tour for the sake of Allah. Of course, human beings get tired. But when for the sake of Allah, it is a big reward from Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. For this, we are happy. As much as Allah gives us health, it is easy.

My mother was once worried about Mawlānā Shaykh. They were also on a travel and tired. He said, “This tire is nothing.” It is only a day-tire, not a health-tire. Health-tire is very bad. When you travel or go around, you get tired a little bit. But when you get with good people, it goes away most of the time. And when you rest, sleep, alḥamdulillāh the next day you become healthy and strong again.

For this, we are thanking Allah. Allah gave us health and, inshā’Allāh good people and good places to go. We are not going to gamble or to night clubs or this or that. We are going to see our people who miss us and whom we miss. We didn’t see them for more than two years or so.

Alḥamdulillāh now we saw them. They are happy and their happiness reaches us also. May Allah bless you all. Thank you to everyone coming, seeing us and being with us. May Allah reward them and give them also health and strong īmān, long life, happy life, inshā’Allāh, here and hereafter, inshā’Allāh.

As-Salāmu ʿalaykum.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
