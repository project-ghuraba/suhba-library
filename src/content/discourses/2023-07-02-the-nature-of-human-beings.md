As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

The most important thing in this world is to be on the way of AllāhNone. When you are on the way of AllāhNone, you have work to do until you pass away from this world. While in this world, you must do what AllāhNone orders all the time. Islām is the religion of nature. It is the religion that suits the nature of people. Therefore, praying five times a day – just like eating and drinking, just like breathing – is necessary nourishment for people. It is spiritual food. People are not really people without it. A person's rank increases with worship. When they quit worshipping, their rank decreases. They join the category of four-legged animals. And there are some who fall even lower. People living in the present time do not accept the favour and high rank given to them by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. They say, "No, we don't want it. We will do whatever we want." When they do so, they become damaged. They become more damaged day by day.

Therefore, we start teaching children how to pray when they are seven years old. More when they are ten. When they are mature, it becomes farḍ, obligatory. They become accountable for it, or Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla will make them settle the balance on the Day of Judgment. Their account is in the Presence of AllāhNone. Doing this while in this world brings great benefit to people. They say no one wrongs people more than they wrong themselves. This is it. You don't do it while you are free and have the opportunity. And when it is postponed to ākhirah, they will make you do it in a much more difficult way.

Therefore, spirituality is more important than material life. People have indulged themselves one hundred percent in material matters. They don't give even one percent for the spiritual life. And then they complain, "Why is this so? Why is that so?" If you tell them their faults, they won't accept it. Therefore, it is not good to say much in this time, because if you tell them, they may commit more sins. So we stay patient and say we don't accept it in our heart. When we don't accept it, we have the weakest level of faith. But there is nothing else we can do. As we are living in the end of times, this is the situation. You may say, "Most people are like this. Look at other people. Everyone is doing it, so I will too." But those people will give their account, and so will you. If you separate yourself from them, you will be saved. But if you go their way, you will be pulled into it along with them. May AllāhNone not allow it, there is a way leading to hell. May AllāhNone protect us. Therefore, as we said, we must carry out the orders of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla throughout our lives. It is more important than eating, drinking and sleeping. It is something more necessary. It is above material concerns. Because material concerns only take you so far. The rest is up to spirituality. If you don't have spirituality, then you are in trouble. May AllāhNone protect us. May AllāhNone not separate us from the right way. May HeNone not give us weariness.

Sometimes, some people say, "When is the prayer?" Morning, afternoon and night. "Tomorrow?" Tomorrow, too. "After tomorrow?" After tomorrow, too. "Until when?" Until you die. "Isn't it too much?" People eat every day. They drink every day. They sleep every day. It is the same. There is no difference in worship. It is no different than eating, drinking and sleeping. It is the same. We are even more in need of this. May AllāhNone help us. May HeNone not let us obey our egos, in shā’a Llāh.

We are living in time, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, for HisNone favour, for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, we are happy. Because AllāhNone gave us this way which it is important for human being. To be in this way of AllāhNone, very important thing. Because we are, AllāhNone create us from meat and blood and bone. We need to eat, to drink. To survive, ourself, we must do this. This is important for everybody. Without this, they cannot live. But most important, also this: there is other side, with this, to accept, obey Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla order.

What HisNone order? To do, like same, like when you eat, when drink, when sleep. Same thing, for what AllāhNone ordered you, you must do this. Because our religion, it is for nature of human being. Islām, fiṭrat. In whole human being, born in fiṭrat, you are. Meaning, nature of human being, from beginning, to be in religion of Islām. Whole prophets in Islām. And everybody coming, to first born baby, they was whole Muslim. They are in religion of Islām, because no sin for them, still small.

And this nature, how you eat, you drink, you sleep – also nature to be, make your worshipping. No problem, nothing difficult. Everything easy for you can do. And you are in need for this spiritual side. Material side, first, to eat and drink and sleep. And must be in this other side also, to be balance[d], spiritual side. To obey AllāhNone, to pray five times, to make zakāt and other thing, fasting – all this giving your body strong. Not only strong for your body. Strong for your character, for yourself also. To be more acceptable in Divine Presence of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.

Without this, only without the spiritual thing, like animal. Animal also, they don't have any obligatory to pray. No obligatory to go ḥajj, no obligatory to be fasting. They are just... They are, AllāhNone create them to eat and to drink, and for benefit of human being. AllāhNone create them, and they are happy with this. But when you are making yourself like them, you become worse than them. Because AllāhNone give you this big chance to be... You have spiritual side, and to improve yourself, you go higher and higher. When you leave this, you go lower and lower. And with this, you be not acceptable one. You be, AllāhNone not, HeNone is not, like, “Inna Llāha lā yuḥibbu l-kāfirīn” (3:32). AllāhNone not love... HeNone doesn't love unbeliever. This is what HeNone said. Because HeNone gave everything. And HeNone gave you proof about what AllāhNone create, but they are not accepting. AllāhNone gave them every good thing and they are trying to give every bad thing for themself first. Because nobody more harm for himself than himself. The worst enemy for himself your ego, yourself.

For this, religion make you to be free from this bad situation. But you are still only drinking, eating and doing bad thing. This is not what purpose of your creation. Your creation, AllāhNone purpose of your creation: to be more honourable, more... better. But people, they are not looking for this. They seeing, "Oh, billion of people, they running after this, after that. And not doing, not praying, not..." But if you are following them, you will be like them. And you will be, like going direct with them, to hell. Said, "No I... I was following these people, it is not save you from hell. You must know, you... you must know AllāhNone give you thinking. And HeNone sending HisNone prophets, HisNone books, to make you in right way. Then why you following these bad people, follower of shayṭān, follower of bad thing? You be, yourself, deserve this.

But Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone is merciful. HisNone door of forgiveness is open all time. For this saying, don't be hopeless. HisNone door is open. Come back to HisNone door. HeNone will be, accept you, forgive you from everything what you done. AllāhNone keep us safe from our ego. And make us to be happy. Easy for us to do this.

Because sometimes shayṭān, for people they said, "You pray today five times? Tomorrow?" Of course. "After tomorrow?" Yes. "After one month still praying, after one year, after, until when this?" Until last breath it is. And how it is too much? Everyday you eating, you drinking, you sleeping, this is same. No different at all from what you are doing. No problem for you to do, praying whole your life. It is your nature. When you know this, it will be easy for you. And it is not difficult for nature of people. AllāhNone make it easy, as drinking, as smelling, as breathing, as eating. Same this, to pray, to worship HimNone.

AllāhNone make us to be, to do this without any weakness, any tired. To make us do it with happiness, in shā’a Llāh. And it is giv[ing] happiness for you. This is important. Don't be cheated with shayṭān. He said, make you, to make you lazy to do this. AllāhNone accept, in shā’a Llāh, what we are doing without refusing, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
