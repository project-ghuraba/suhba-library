As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
وَجَعَلْنَا مِنَ ٱلْمَآءِ كُلَّ شَىْءٍ حَىٍّ
<br>“Wa-jaʿalnā mina l-māʾi kulla shayʾin ḥayy”<br>
“And We have made from water every living thing.” (Qur’ān 21:30). ṢadaqAllahu l-‘Aẓīm.

وَأَنزَلْنَا مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءً طَهُورًا
<br>“Wa anzalnā mina s-samāʾi māan ṭahūran”<br>
“And We send down from the sky pure water.” (Qur’ān 25:48).

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says in these āyāt, “We created everything from water. We gave life to everything with water.” And “What We send down from the sky is clean water.” Cleanliness is the foundation of Islām. It is for ablution and ghusl. We cannot do without it. There is both physical and spiritual life with it. In physical life, certainly, nothing can be without water.

Nothing can grow where there is no water. There cannot be life. Therefore, rain falls down with the power of Allah. Certainly, He makes it rain when He wants. And He doesn’t, if He doesn’t want. “It happens so and so.” That is another matter. If Allah wants, as He said, He can evaporize it and it becomes a cloud not coming down to you. He can make it rain in the middle of sea and not above you. What can you do then? Let’s see.

Therefore, we must make shukr to Allah for these blessings. We must know that everything is a blessing from Allah. We must appreciate that blessing. We must show respect to that blessing. We must make shukr to Allah.

Our Holy Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam in his holy ḥadīth sharīf cursed those who urinated in flowing water, in rivers and so. Cursing is not an easy thing. They were directly cursed. Therefore, it is a great blessing. Shukr to Allah. For the barakah of our Shaykh, the barakah of this month and these days, Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla gave us this blessing.

And the spiritual side of it is that we use it in our prayers and ablution, in various things; standing clean in the presence of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla is done with this blessed water. We can’t do without it. You cannot wash with petrol or diesel. You cannot wash with alcohol. They don’t make you clean. They are not clean. You can’t use them. But with this blessed water, both your ablution and ghusl is made. You receive spiritual rizq then.

Without ablution, without ghusl, spirituality doesn’t come. “I didn’t take ablution. I am clean. I do it in my heart.” Do you pray? “No, I don’t.” Why don’t you pray? “I cannot do it.” They don’t take ablution. They don’t make ghusl. Many people are like this now. Without it, nothing good happens to them.

Our Holy Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam said not to waste water. Our Holy Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam used to make ablution and ghusl with a small thing. Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim would do the same. He could make ablution with just a glass of water. And he would make ghusl not with a bucket but with a small bottle. He would show so much respect to it.

Therefore, people nowadays are not aware of it. They are wasting and polluting it. And then there is no barakah.

May Allah give us barakah. Shukr to Allah for these blessings.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
