As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Innama l-mushrikūna najas" (9:28). Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says in Qur’ān that people without belief, mushrik, kāfir, and nonMuslims, are all dirty and filthy. They are dirty. Therefore, "Falā yaqrabū l-masjida l-ḥarāma ba‘da ‘āmihim hādhā" (9:28). Those dirty people cannot enter Makkah and Madīnah. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says not to let them in there. Don't let those people in there.

What kind of people are they? They take showers, cleanse themselves, bathing with soap and everything. The cannot be clean that way. Cleanliness is spiritual and shirk (associating others with AllāhNone) is dirt. A man like this is saved from dirtiness only by accepting Islām. Otherwise, it is impossible. Even if all the water of the world washes over him, he is still dirty and impure. Just one word can bring him to cleanliness. Cleanliness comes after this: Lā ilāha illā Llāh Muḥammadur Rasūlu Llāh. As long as they don't say it, they can be as clean as they like according to themselves but they are not clean at all. To be clean is so easy. But they make it difficult and don't want it. Our ProphetNone is saying if they smelled the spiritual stench, no one would survive. It is much worse than toilet dirt.

Therefore, the matter comes to this illness again. And this illness requires more cleanliness. It requires spiritual cleanliness. It needs a spiritual remedy. Muslims should pay attention to this too. We should keep our prayers, pay attention to our worship, and ṣadaqah, and make a vow. When our ProphetNone visited a sick person, heNone would tell him to keep his word when that sick person recovered. Because every sick person says, "I will do this and that when I recover," and then they usually forget about it. That is why our ProphetNone said people vow to either give flour or a sheep or a chicken, or give money and then they forget it. Our ProphetNone said, "Ūfi bi-‘ahdak." "Keep your word" to those who were ill and recovered.

Therefore, there is this big thing now. But with the permission of AllāhNone it won't come close to Muslims and believers, in shā’a Llāh. Because it looks more like a punishment. It does not look like a test but like a punishment because they have oppressed the Muslims too much so it is coming to the oppressors. Therefore, nonetheless when it is over, everyone should make a vow according to their ability, "I will give 3 kg of sugar when this disaster is gone. If nothing happens to me with Allāh'sNone permission, I will give 5 kg of flour. I will give dates, this and that. I will slaughter a sheep." This inspiration came today.

We should do it for Muslims – it will be over with Allāh'sNone permission. Because when you don't oppress anyone, nothing will happen to you, in shā’a Llāh. This is an important lesson. It is to remind people of the hereafter. And it is punishment for the oppressors. May AllāhNone protect us. May AllāhNone help us, in shā’a Llāh. Therefore, there is no fear for Muslims.

Because the governments are too worried. We can't comment on it because it is not right to say something in such a situation. We will obey them and wait. Therefore, we will turn to AllāhNone and pray, in shā’a Llāh. It will finish by Allāh'sNone permission. No fear for Muslims and Islām with Allāh'sNone permission.
