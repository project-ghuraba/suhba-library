As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says in ḥadīth sharīf, "Al-ḥayā’u mina l-īmān – Modesty is from faith." A person having modesty, feeling ashamed before AllāhNone and people, is from faith. They asked the Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, “Will Jibra'il ‘alayhi s-salām descend after youNone? Will the revelation stop?” “He will descend several times,” said the Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. “He will descend to take away some things, not to bring down revelation. He will come down to take away modesty and shame from people. He will take away those good things." People will be immodest shameless, they will throw away shame. HeNone says there will be no shame left.

We are exactly in that time. They do not appreciate the modest person. “He is shy, he doesn’t come out, he can’t talk.” Especially about the girls, “They are shy, they don’t meet with anyone.” It doesn’t seem like an acceptable attribute among people. Why? Because the virtue of modesty has been taken away from people. They now see it as a bad thing. Therefore, “If you are a shy or a modest person, go and see a doctor immediately. Let him give you medicine so that you can get better. What a bad thing this is, you have shame. Get rid of that shame.” This is how it is in this time.

They do what’s worse and all sorts of dirty things. Instead of feeling ashamed of it, they take pride in it. They proudly declare, “I have no adab. I am immodest. I have no shame.” You can’t interfere with them. If you ask, “What are you doing? Aren’t you ashamed?” They say: “He is attacking us. He is against shayṭān’s order. Let’s disgrace him right away. Let our shayṭāns attack him. Let’s disgrace him. If it were in other countries, we would put him in prison. Let’s do something and embarrass him in front of people. This person is a shy, modest man. He talks bad about shameless people like us and people who set bad examples like us. Let’s cut out his tongue, let's silence him.” This is what they say.

In this time, we are in a situation that would shock people – not just the people of old times, but in our time also. If it were in the old times, they wouldn’t believe that it could be like this. No one would believe that we could be in such a situation. If a person did such bad things, he would be ashamed. He wouldn’t want anyone to hear about it. Now he wants to make the whole world hear about what he does. He wants to tell everyone. And everyone strives to be like him. These are things that AllāhNone does not approve of or like. The things that AllāhNone dislikes are not good things. The things that AllāhNone loves bring goodness to people. They bring all kinds of beauty. What AllāhNone dislikes brings all kinds of evil, all kinds of ugliness and disgust.

May AllāhNone protect us. May AllāhNone protect people, Muslims and all people, from these situations. Because these situations do not suit humanity. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla created man beautiful. HeNone created him the most perfect human being. HeNone created him to elevate him. People today are racing to get lower. “The lower I am, the more satisfied I will be,” they say. Exactly as Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim would say – sewer rats. Those animals, by the wisdom of AllāhNone – of course, there is a wisdom in everything – serve as examples. They wander around in the sewers. Everyone knows it. There is no one who does not know. They are even shown in some movies. People see them in the sewer while walking. You see there are rats jumping into that dirt. It swims there and comes out the other side. It doesn’t care at all. These people, these kind of people, are like this. They are pleased when they are in the dirt. They are not happy outside. They get very happy when they are in dirty and disgusting places. They are pleased. They want the same thing for others. Well, this can’t be for others. A clean cat, rabbit, or lamb will not get up and jump into this thing. It doesn’t jump into that dirtiness because they are clean creatures. The unclean ones can live in that place. The clean ones stay away from such filth.

May AllāhNone keep us away and protect us, in shā’a Llāh.

Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, saying, "Al-ḥayā’u mina l-īmān." Ṣadaqa Rasūla LlāhNone. Meaning – to be, have shame and to be ashamed to do bad thing, it is from īmān, from belief, strong belief. One of good attribute of mu’min to be, not to be... he must be ashamed from to do bad thing. This is ḥayā’, meaning this. And this is from belief. Because if you are believe and you have shame, you cannot make something AllāhNone not happy with you. Or people saying, "You doing this," you ashamed if they – with this, you cannot do.

But nowadays, people make this attribute as [if] it is bad, bad manner, bad attribute for human being. If anybody, he is shy, they are not happy – "What is wrong with this man? With this girl? He is very shy. Not, not coming like other people [who] they don't have any shy, don't have any ashamed. They must be like this all people. Go bring him to doctor to see him what is wrong with this and give him some medicine, because he is or she is ashamed. Not speaking to people or to foreigner or to..." So, they... But normally, this is what AllāhNone like from this attribute for people that HeNone give this and HeNone is happy with this.

Sayyidunā ‘Uthmān, he was shy too much. For Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, saying, "The angel, shy from Sayyidunā ‘Uthmān, because he is shy." Because, they are also shy from him to say anything about him. So it was very good attribute but nowadays, it is not like this. It is very bad thing for... in society of, or through this world. Why this? Because, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, saying, ṣaḥābah ask himNone, "If Jibrā’īl ‘alayhi s-salām, he come for, after youNone, for giving some verses or anything?" HeNone said, "Yes, he will come, but not for to give anything. [Rather] for to take something. One of them – to take this shy[ness] from ummah, from people after meNone. And at the end of time, he will come and take this shy[ness]. And with people, they will be – no ashamed, no any afraid from people to be seeing them doing bad thing. It's no. Coming opposite, this."

They like to do many thing which it is not normal for human being to be happy. Normally, if they do them, something like this, they are hiding themselves. They are shying, too ashamed to somebody to see them doing this bad thing. But nowadays, it is fashion to do every bad thing, everything not acceptable for human being, for nature of human being. And they are happy, very happy with this. And they doing more and more and they encourage other people to do something, like the something, same thing what they are doing. If you do, you are not happy with them, they become angry for you. They are swearing, they are shouting. If they can do – put you in prison. They can punish you. They can do this, because they are doing the worst thing as human being can do. But you cannot say this because, time it is what Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, saying, this ashamed, shy coming out, no more shy.

They are, as much they become lowest, they are happy. Because, Mawlānā Shaykh was giving example for this: the rat, they are living in sewage. And everybody, you see in movies and everything. When somebody going through this, sometimes you see rat jumping, as they jumping for swimming pool in this dirt and this disgusting place. And come jumping from this side, going to other side. Very happy, very much happy, because they are the lowest. AllāhNone create them of course but there is wisdom for them, for everything. But their nature, to be the lowest, to be in dirt. To show people, if they are going to in, through sins and bad thing, this is [what it] look like. But you cannot find cat or you cannot find rabbit or other jumping in this dirty water. They are clean creature. Clean creature, they are more benefit, more blessed for people.

For this Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, create many thing to see, for human being to see what is good, what is not good. But still they are happy to be the in most dirtiest. Even they are more dirtiest than this rat. More dirtier than this sewage, what, where they are with sin. spiritual sin of... The dirt of sin, it is more dirtiest this from sewage but not, cannot see. But Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, saying, "If you smell, one sin smelling, maybe whole world, it will be die from the bad smelling." So, it is more dirty than this... So, AllāhNone keep us away and keep our shy[ness] from HimNone. It is more important thing to shy from AllāhNone, from Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, to do bad thing. AllāhNone keep us safe, in shā’a Llāh, from this.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
