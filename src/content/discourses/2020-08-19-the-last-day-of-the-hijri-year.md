Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Our Holy Prophet did not consider anything to be unlucky. He interpreted everything as good luck. Today is the last day of the year, the last day of the Hijri Year. This year has been difficult but as our Holy Prophet said, we do not consider it unlucky. Very big things have happened, but insha’Allah they all happened for the good of Islam, for the good of Muslims. Today is the last day of the year. Insha’Allah the next year will be auspicious and full of blessings. May it be a means for Islam and Mahdi ‘alayhi s-salām to appear insha’Allah. May we be together with Mahdi ‘alayhi s-salām insha’Allah.

This year, we repent to Allah for whatever mistakes and sins we made in the last year. May all the efforts of shaytan be in vain. May Allah forgive us and turn our sins into good deeds. When you repent and seek forgiveness, Allah Azza wa Jalla will turn sins into good deeds. Allah says: ‘ُت اَنسَ حَ ُُم هِّ ِّتاَئ ِّيسَ ُُُاللَّ ُُلُ د ِّ َبُي’, ‘Allah will replace their evil deeds with good deeds.’ (Qur’ān 25:70). He turns sins into good deeds. Therefore, whatever mistakes and sins we have committed this year, we repent to Allah and ask Allah for forgiveness. Insha’Allah He turns them into good deeds. May the efforts and works of shaytan and his associates to deviate people from the way all year long be in vain insha’Allah. May this next year be better insha’Allah. May we be with Islam and Iman, and with Mahdi ‘alayhi s-salām for many years.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
