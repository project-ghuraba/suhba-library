As-salamu alaikum wa rahmatullahi wa barakatuh. Audhu billahi min ash-shaytani r-rajim. Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. As-salatu wa s-salamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidi l-awwalina wa l-akhirin. Madad ya RasulAllah, madad ya Sadati Ashabi Rasulillah, madad ya Mashayihina dastur, ya Shaykh Abdullah Faizi d-Daghistani, madad ya Mawlana Sheikh Muhammad Nazim al-Haqqani. Tariqatuna as-sohbah, wa l-khayru fi jam'iyyah.

Holiest days of the year are the first 10 days of Dhul Hijjah. Allah Azza wa Jalla swears on these holy days. "wa l-fajr wa layalin 'ashr" (89:1-2) (By the dawn and by ten nights). These are the very holy days of the year. These are Allah's grants to people, especially for Muslims. Because if you are not a Muslim, you do not get benefit but for sure they are the days that Allah loves, there is blessings and blessing comes to the world. When you do good deeds during these days Allah gives much more rewards compared to ordinary days.

Fasting these 9 days is the sunnah of our Prophet (sas). If you do not fast all, you can fast on the 8th and 9th day, they call it 'yawmu tarwiya' They are the most excellence of these days for fasting. You get great benefit and blessings when you fast those days. It is Allah Azza wa Jalla's grant. Of course the same also for the night prayer, when you wake up at night and pray. Your prayers are accepted and you get blessings. You get a big treasure for the hereafter. As our Prophet mentioned in the hadith to his companions, when you fast in the first 7 days or when you do night prayer, for each day it is as if you freed 100 slaves. Even freeing one slave is a huge thing, imagine freeing 100 slaves. Allah knows how much He is going to give reward.

But if you fast the 8th and 9th day for each day - on the 8th day it is as if you freed 1000 slaves. On the 9th day it is as if you freed 2000 slaves. Allah gives that much reward. As we said, for night prayer perform ablution, pray 2 rakaats before you go to sleep. Then you wake up before the night ends, before fajr, you pray 4 rakaat, 8 rakaat or 12 rakaat. Pray as much as you can. It is as if you prayed all night. Some people stay awake praying all night, who can't do that but Allah Azza wa Jalla made it easy, if you go to sleep making ablution, you pray 2 rakaats, and you wake up before fajr, it is as if you prayed all night. Allah Azza wa Jalla gives such a reward.

On the 9th of Dhul Hijjah we must read 1000 Ikhlas, that is our duty (wazifa), do not forget it. I'm not saying if you can, do it. You should definitely do it. Mawlana did it all his life. He told everybody to do it. This is for Arafa, day of Eid ul Adha, you have to read 1000 Ikhlas. And of course for the Eid the wealthy ones should sacrifice an animal. It is obligatory. You are not going to sacrifice and throw it, you sacrifice it and you are rewarded. If you need to you can eat it all. You can keep it for 3, 4 months and eat it slowly. You both keep Allah's order and get benefit. Allah rewards you for each hair of the animal. With the first drops of blood all your sins are forgiven.

These are such holy days. Ten days, they can fast 9 days if they are able to. If not, like we said, they can fast on 8th and 9th day. If they cannot do that either, they can only fast on Arafa day. It is also ok. But do not miss that, it is a big opportunity. These are beautiful gifts from Allah. You see the situation of the world. There is corruption, oppression everywhere. No peace, no tranquility. So, do not be occupied with worldly things. Keep Allah's orders. If you keep Allah's orders you get more benefit from His grants. Do not be busy with this world. Everything goes as Allah wants. Either you are sad or not, there is a decree. Whatever Allah decrees, it will happen.

So, be content with Allah's decree. Mind your own business. We should see these beauties. The world is already full of dirtiness, corruption and trouble. We should not look at that. We keep worshipping, we submit to Allah's orders and Allah's will. Do not be sad for this world. May Allah not keep anyone busy with this world. We are not created for this world, we are created for akhira (hereafter). If you do something for this world, whatever you do, do it for Allah. When you do like that, you feel peaceful.

Because our ancestors conquered not for this world, but for Allah. Everything they did was successful. People make research - "How did this empire last hundreds of years?" Because they feared Allah, they did everything for Allah. They tried to do whatever which is good. They ran away from badness, never oppressed anyone. Now oppression spread to whole world. So many people work for this world. May Allah protect us. We are in very holy days in divine presence of Allah, not in people.

It is the 10 days of Dhul Hijjah. It is mentioned in Qur'an and Allah swore on these holy days. It is a very precious gift from Allah for us. We must appreciate this and take as much as we can take from this gift. Especially nine days, who can fast, he can fast from one until the ninth of Dhul Hijjah. If it cannot be, may be eighth [and] ninth. Because [on the] eighth, yawmu tarwiya calling, before arafat. Hajji's they are going to arafat and the 'yawmu arafa' is the most holiest because on this day [those] who are in pilgrimage they will be in arafat and it is only one day in the year. If you go before these days and go to pilgrimage, nothing can be. If you go after also - only one day. So it is a very precious day. So, who are not in Hajj, in pilgrimage he must fast it.

It is not obligatory, but it is what Prophet (saws) was doing, and giving this present for his ummah, from Allah. He said if you fast nine days, the first seven days, every day it is like if you free 100 slaves from capture. And to free one slave it is [a] very big thing in Islam. And Allah reward you and only He knows what He give. But if you fast [the first] seven; every day, 100 slaves you free. On eighth [it is] as you free 1000 slaves. On ninth, 'yawmu Arafa' [it is] as you free 2000 slaves Allah reward you. It is real, precious, and it is precious in the divine presence. And people they are not knowing or not appreciating, they are not interested about this. Only interested in rubbish, and they are interested in this dunya - how it will be, and what, it is too much difficult now, and it is how this it will be everywhere fire, everywhere war, everywhere no, crisis. Only, this is what they are knowing.

Allah, it is not important in His divine presence this. All what is important for you, it is not important for Allah. Important for Allah; what He give us. And it is real. We must be happy with this gift, with His present that He gives us from precious things. And there is on ninth of Dhul Hijjah also we must make 1000 times Ikhlas as-shareef "qul huwa Allahu Ahad" (112:1) This is what Maulana whole life he was doing and he make us to do it. And on Eid we will slaughter if anybody he has enough money. Not poor people but who has enough money they must slaughter one sheep or one goat or a cow. This is order also. And if he is not so, not so rich, he can eat this also. Even rich people, also they can eat [from it]. And for each hair of goat or for sheep Allah rewards you and when cutting and first drop of blood Allah forgives all your sins. It is really very precious. We must understand this.

We are, today we are speaking about this because all people they are very worried, very afraid what happened, what is going on. It is our duty what Allah tell us to do. We must first look for this, and after this you will be satisfied because everything what Allah, [is] under His control. Nothing [is] out of control. So only you take what Allah gives you from good things and don't think for anything else.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfiq al-Fatiha
