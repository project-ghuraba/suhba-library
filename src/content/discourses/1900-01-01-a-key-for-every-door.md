This is a new month, and it has a new appearance, divinely appearance, Yeni ay yeni tecelli. Manifestation.

Dastūr yā Sayyidi yā Sulṭānu l-Awliyā’ al-ḥamdu liLlāh al-ḥamdu liLlāh wa sh-shukru liLlāh. Ziyādatan li-sharafi n-Nabī ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa sallam, mashāyikhina l-kirām ḥaẓrati ustādinā wa ‘alá (?) lil-mu’minīn, al-Fatiḥa.

It is our real life that passing through for our Lord's service and we are sitting here for our Lord's sake and in His love and in His service and in His respects and in His glorification. We are not sitting for our egos but Allāh Almighty making the benefit for all His glorifying and all His respecting or all His praisings – whole benefits returning to ourselves. When we are giving everything to our Lord then our Lord giving everything that we gave, more than that we gave, turning to ourselves. Therefore give everything to your Lord. Don't keep nothing with you, even give your body for His service. Give your whole life for His services. Then HeNone gives you everything that you can imagine it or not.

Now our brother is standing on, was asking an important question. We shall listen and we shall look what comes from our spiritual center, what comes as an answer that may be for him and for us and for everyone can be lesson benefits. Yes.

G: I try to ask the question in a simple way. Sometimes a person has so much difficulties in life, feels very... sometimes a person seems so unlucky, everything he tries to do he can't do it. Wants to become a doctor, they throw him out of doctor school. Wants to become a lawyer they throw him out. Not because, not because he cannot, because he is not smart enough. Not because he is not intelligent but because not suitable. Throw him out. Wants to become a psychologist. They throw him out. Gets a job as a, in an office working as a clerk – ordinary job. They throw him out. Everything he tries to do he cannot succeed.

SN: He is intelligent? G: Intelligent.

S: But he not suitable for any...

G: Everything he becomes so unhappy person. He say, "Hello how are you?" Goes, doesn't want to talk. Always unhappy more and more and more unhappy, constricted closed person. What can, how to help such a person?

SN: Easy, in shā’a Llāh. By the name of Allāh All Almighty, All Merciful. It is easy the most Beneficent, most most Munificent – it is easy to help such a person. Only he must believe. When he is believing it is enough and so easy to give him. A help – divinely help, comes to him. For everything there is a cure. For every illness there is a cure. That is a kind of illness. Not everyone going to be ill. Some people they are ill – mostly people running. A few people, a lot of people in hospitals but majority they are healthy. And that kind people that Mr Lowen indicating it, there are also a lot of people they are ill people and they have special cures for them.

Now they are saying his answer. And we say as we have been sent as an answer to that question. For every closed door, it has a key. You can't open that door with every key. No. There is some kind keys – Yale. That factory never doing same two keys same exactly, must be a difference. If so many you may put it inside you can't turn it. You are very happy when it is going inside – going to open. But for every closed door must be a key for it. For every problem, must be a solution. If there is a problem, there is a solution. It is impossible to be a problem without solution.

So many problems you are studying in schools without solution. Yes? But not, not everyone knows to to make a solution for that problems – physical problems, chemical problems, arithmetical problems. Yes too much problems giving. So many people bringing. They can't do one of three make – no this way. Coming down. When you are going to solve it, you are only happy. Therefore it is an locked up problem, locked up door and must be a key for it. For every solution, lying under it a kind of knowledge. Without that knowledge you can't solve it that problem. Not everyone can solve it. Even they may have a basic knowledge but so many people they can't know to solve it. Therefore for every solution must be a basic knowledge. Then secondly important you must know how you can use that knowledge. That is a kind of illness and we have some basically knowledge but no treatment, special treatment for that. Not everyone knowing it is important.

Once upon a time just came to our Grand Shaykh some European people – they are peoples, young fellows. He called me as a translator to those people young fellows. And he was saying, "First knowledge that may be a basic knowledge for sons of Ādam, they must know about themselves. And every knowledge that you are taking without knowing about yourself, it is something based on air – no foundation." Therefore every knowledge that we must learn first, firstly it is the basic knowledge for ourselves. You must know firstly about yourself. Basic knowledge is important for the Children of Ādam and every education system not based firstly on knowledge about Sons of Ādam...
