As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Lā tay’asū min rawḥi Llāh" (12:87). Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says in Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n, when the son of Sayyidunā Ya‘qūb ‘alayhi s-salām, Yūsuf ‘alayhi s-salām, was lost, they said he was gone. Therefore, he [Ya'qūb] says, "Do not lose hope." This is the wisdom of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. People in this world, who are believers, should not lose their hope.

Of course, every person makes mistakes and does wrong things. There are many kinds of mistakes. Some are big mistakes which means committing sins. That is the biggest mistake. If you do them knowingly, that is even bigger. There is small sin and big sin. They are of different kinds too. If you commit a big sin, you make a big mistake. If you commit a small sin, you still make a mistake. And there are mistakes in worldly matters. People start a job, but that job doesn't last because of their own mistakes or someone else's mistakes. They get stuck and cannot solve it. They worry and suffer because of it. There are other mistakes also. There are mistakes in studies, mistakes at work. Because mankind is not perfect, he makes all kinds of mistakes. Therefore, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says to not lose hope.

As long as people are alive, they can compensate for these mistakes until they die. After they die, it is impossible to compensate for them, especially sins. When you commit a sin in your life, realise your mistake and repent by quitting it – "yubaddilu Llāhu sayyi’ātihim ḥasanāt" (25:70) – Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla will turn those sins you committed into rewards. Because you left them and repented, as a favour from AllāhNone it will be written for you as a reward in return for what you did when you repented and came to the right way. It is the same in worldly matters. Sometimes mankind thinks he does something well but it doesn't turn out well. He either doesn't know how to plan it, or even if he knows, there is the will of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Whatever one's share, one will take it. Therefore, mankind should not be hopeless for that. Their rizq will come.

Sometimes people benefit from their mistakes. They make a small mistake and don't repeat it. They become more careful and more cautious. And that is a benefit for them. But if you make a mistake and after are aware of it, and then persist in it, it is wrong. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla has given mind to people. They should think a little bit. No matter what, when it is a worldly matter, there is no danger. They may lose their wealth, rizq or so on. Those are worldly issues. If their īmān is fine, the other is not important. Important are mistakes that cannot be compensated for. Those sins are: not believing in AllāhNone, not thinking about AllāhNone. But the rest of the mistakes are for this world.

Mankind goes from one state to another. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gives what is beneficial for them. As long as they have īmān, everything is good for them. Therefore, they shouldn't be hopeless. AllāhNone is ar-Razzāq. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla supports believers. Otherwise, people get depressed by saying "If only I did this, it would be like this..." all the time. May AllāhNone protect us from that, in shā’a Llāh.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying, "Lā tay’asū min rawḥi Llāh" (12:87). This was Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, in Qur’ān, telling us about Sayyidunā Yūsuf and his brothers. When they come to their father, they said, "Our brothers, they not coming." Because, before. he was losing Sayyidunā Yūsuf, he was sad. And then he said like this. He said, "Don't be hopeless from AllāhNone. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. HeNone give everything and we are believer so don't be hopeless. Go and look again." We are living in our life, there is many thing happening. We do, because we are human being, we are not angel, we are doing many mistake. But don't be hopeless. This is what Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying in Holy Qur’ān addressing for, not only for Muslim, for whole human being. If they listening, they will be not sad, no sadness for them.

And then, we do many mistake. There is many kind of mistake. Maybe thousands, hundreds, ten thousand mistake. There many we are doing. But, even mistake also, there is kind, some kind of mistake it is terrible mistake. What is this? When you are, doing sin, sins. This is mistake for human being to do. What AllāhNone not want and what AllāhNone HeNone don't want, this is calling sin. There is sin also many kind of sin, some of them big and some of them small they said. Big sins, many of them, for people nowadays, they are doing most of them. And this mistake, as much as you are in life, you can change them. If you are repent and ask for forgiveness from AllāhNone, AllāhNone, HeNone forgive you and even HeNone change this wrong, sins to reward. Because, you are sincerely asking for forgiveness and not doing any more this, AllāhNone reward you what you done. "Yubaddilu Llāhu sayyi’ātihim ḥasanāt" (25:70). This is in Qur’ān also. HeNone changing the sins to reward.

This is big tidings for human being because, if you do something in world and you ask forgiveness from government, they never forgive you. But Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone forgive. As much you are in life, you have this chance to finish your mistake and to be clean and to continue your life with safe and peace. This is for Ākhirah. This is very important part for mistake, what we doing, to be... because until you are in life, you can change this but when you die, you cannot change this. You will ask and you will be punished. AllāhNone what HeNone is doing HeNone is, HeNone knowing. Other also, in life we have also many, we do many mistake and many kind of mistake. Some people, they're studying maybe ten, twenty year, and after they find this it was no benefit for this. This big mistake. Some of them marry not good people, also this mistake. They also, they suffering. Some people doing business. They're also with wrong people, or with themself, they make wrong calculation or something, also, they are have problem. Other, ya‘nī, this mistake, not one kind – million kind there is mistake. But all of them, as much you are in life, don't be hopeless. This is what Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying. When you are in life, AllāhNone, will [be] fine, if you are trust in AllāhNone, you will, HeNone will help you and you will be, in shā’a Llāh, succeed again. This is what Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying.

So, human being must be patient. They are not patient. When something happen, they want it to be very quickly. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone is know. HeNone is not looking for what you want. What HeNone want, you must look for HimNone for this. For this, you must be depend on AllāhNone, This is the best thing for believer, and to ask for forgiveness for everything you done. Sometimes, of course, there is some very difficult thing happening, you cannot go out from this. Also Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone, HeNone can change everything, in shā’a Llāh.

There is one, sometimes, there is in some business, some people doing business they put money and they put everything. They have every time losing, and they want to go out but they stuck in this. Even with everything they want to go out but they cannot. And this is very difficult but AllāhNone, also for everything no problem, when you in life, not finish. This finish, in shā’a Llāh, also. Once, one Turkish saying, Once, one thief coming to house, and father tell his son, "Go and look what something there." And he, his son he went there and show, see the thief and say and thief... He said his father, "O my father, I find thief here." He said, "Bring him here!" He said, "No, he not coming. I cannot bring him here." He said, "So leave him to go." He said, "He is not going also." Sometimes, problem like this happening.

So, AllāhNone not make us to be in like a trap, in trap and he will... But from AllāhNone, in shā’a Llāh, everything, HeNone said, "Don't be hopeless." In shā’a Llāh, AllāhNone help us, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
