As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīn wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

One of the attributes of our ProphetNone among others is trust. What we mean by trust is he never betrays anyone and speaks right. He says what is right. The attribute of our ProphetNone; before the prophecy came to himNone, he was known as Muḥammadu l-Amīn among people of Makkah. No one was given that name by the wisdom of AllāhNone. That name given to our ProphetNone is a beautiful honoured name. A person who is trustworthy. People couldn't find anyone to trust their belongings before. And as Muḥammadu l-Amīn, everyone would leave everything to our ProphetNone. When they returned from somewhere after some time, they asked it back and took away with them. Even during the time of hijrah those trusts remained with himNone. HeNone told Sayyidunā ‘Alī to return the goods to their owners. Such a beautiful manner, such a beautiful attribute that our ProphetNone has, this feature.

People are following sunnah now but they cannot follow this manner. They are not trustworthy. They don't keep the trust. They betray the trust. That is bringing harm to people. It is the most important attribute of our ProphetNone. A person's everything, appearance and looks are according to Islām, but when he doesn't respect trust, it doesn't work. It becomes worse. They can't correct their egos. Important thing here is to correct the ego, to keep the trust. Whatever you do, whether you're a cobbler or a farmer, a doctor or a hodja or a teacher, you have to do it as best as you can. Only then you keep the trust. And trust has thousands of types. One type is these things we said. In everything a person does, he shouldn't cut it half and say that's enough. He gets paid. He works and it's his job. He should stay there till it's time. He should do his work. That is trust too. You are not sitting there for free. You are not sitting to serve neither. You get paid. So you have to match that payment. Whatever service you do there, you should do it completely. It is important. At the end of times now no one is keeping the trust. There are many traitors. May AllāhNone protect [us]. We shouldn't look at them. Everyone should look at himself. May we keep the trust, in shā’a Llāh.
