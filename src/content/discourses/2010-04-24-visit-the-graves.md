Bismillahi'r Rahmani'r Rahim. Dastur Ya Sayyidi,Ya Rijal Allah.

Allah Allah, Allah Allah Allah Allah, Aziz Allah Allah Allah, Allah Allah Allah Allah Karim Allah Allah Allah, Allah Allah Allah Allah Subhan Allah Allah Allah, Allah Allah Allah Allah Sultan Allah

Ey Rabbimiz anta Sultan ighfirlana, w'arhamna, wa tub alayna zid Habibika Sayyid al Awwalin wal Akhirin Sayyidna Muhammad (sas) zidhu Ya Rabbi, izzan wa sharafa nuran wa surura, ridwanan wa sultana

Despite shaytan and those who follow it, madad Ya Sahib az-Zaman, madad ya Qutb al Mutasarrif. Madad Ya Sultan al Awliya, we are weak servants and asking support, heavenly support for our weakness. Give us O our Shaykh, support us, look after ourselves and look after the attenders, here and everywhere. Assalamu alaykum Ya Attenders for this humble association. And we are saying: Audhu Billahi min as sahytani'r rajim Bismillahi'r Rahmani'r Rahim, is the holy power that Allah Almighty is granting everything on this planet; it is coming through Bismillahi'r Rahmani'r Rahim.

Therefore, first of all ulamas must try to teach the honour of Bismillahi'r Rahmani'r Rahim as no one can be able to make a limit for the honour of Bismillahi'r Rahmani'r Rahim. And also, whoever is asking heavenly support that power is put in Bismillahi'r Rahmani'r Rahim. Every kind of work that you are asking to do is going to be complete with Bismillahi'r Rahmani'r Rahim. If not getting it something is missing, like a tree that has no roots. Only sometimes it is going to be seen and then you must throw it away. But if you are saying Bismillahi'r Rahmani'r Rahim that amal, that action, as well as that good thing that you are asking to appear and you are asking to reach, that heavenly honour, heavenly support, heavenly power you may take from Bismillahi'r Rahmani'r Rahim.

That's why the Seal of Prophets Sayyidina Muhammad (sas) was saying if anyone is not saying Bismillahi'r Rahmani'r Rahim for any action or any effort it is going to be like a tree that you are planting and its roots are cut off. You can't reach anything. Therefore it is the power station for everyone who is asking to do something. They must come to that power station.

As a car, if you are asking it to go, to make it move, to run, you must come to petrol station. If not taking from petrol station, one cup, one litre, 2 litres, then you must call people 'O People come and help me, push a little bit'. To where we are? Full way, a bus stopping. On way, no petrol station and the driver saying, 'O People, our power just finished and now we must use something else’. People asking 'How it was running before? Now stopping. 'Hanh, hanh, hanh, before we were using a liquid putting in its tank. Now what happened! Just finished. What we can do? Come down and.. hiya maula, hiya laysa.. Come down, everyone must push'. They are pushing and then coming a little hill and they are saying, 'We can't do it, it is a big bus. How can we move it on this hill? What happened? Yes.

An arabi was travelling and in the way coming a place for resting till dawn opening. Arabi bedouin was sleeping, he was so tired. When dawn was getting clear he was standing up for travelling. Looking that his camel was putting its head down and stretching its legs. Calling it. That camel never understanding. No answer, no answer. Turning around arabi 'Oh my camel, what happened to you'? What happened to you? You were running like the wind. Now what happened to you that you are lying down, you can't move, you can't see, you can't hear?

Subhan Allahi'l Alliyi'l Azim fi'l amthal, hiya ibrat (through the moral comes wisdom) Yes. Teaching people something. Another bedouin reaching there and looking at that bedouin crying and shouting and speaking to camel and camel just laying down. ‘What happened? Why you are crying’, and he was saying kayfu kayf, like this happened and ‘I am looking what happened? It was just perfect. Nothing from its body left it but what happened?’ And that one like philosopher-people saying, 'Oh, my brother, this camel has passed away’. ‘To where? I may run after it to catch it?’ ‘O akhi'l arab, my brother this, what is leaving it is not such a thing that you can touch, you can see, you can hear. That is a secret power granted to every living one, it is not from Earth, from heavens’.

I am saying to people, all people, O People through east and west, from north to south, you are running, running, running and then you are collecting, collecting, making heaps of gold, heaps of jewels and one day you are falling down, you are finishing. You are finishing. That is something from heavens granted to every living creature to make it to move up to their last moment. Now people never thinking on it. People saying we are eating, drinking. If we are feeling no good, taking medicines, so many things and then after a while we are going to be as we were. Never going to be as before. No one ever can be as before. Ya Shafi, Ya Kafi.

Therefore, two kinds of powers. Heavenly power and earthly power. Earthly power is going to be useless, if heavenly power is cut off. And now people are never thinking on such things. Particularly we are in need of heavenly support for every moment and second. If it not reaching us you can do nothing. You are finishing and going away. Till our life is reaching to its limits through that line you are in need heavenly support to do as much as possible. You may do. But when you are reaching your life limit, no way. Finish when our last moment is reaching. Don't ask support from material aspects. No, ask spiritual support. Spiritual power is filled from heavens. Every holy word that you are using gives to you a heavenly power to move, to do, to act, to reach your efforts and if they are not doing that they are going to perish, be dead people. The power of the body can't carry over its capacity but if you are using and therefore Allah Almighty is sending Bismillahi'r Rahmani'r Rahim. Say it, don't worry, Bismillahi'r Rahmani'r Rahim is from pre-Eternal up to Eternal. Endless power oceans are in it.

O our Salafi ulamas. We are leaving you, tonight also we are turning our batteries of bombing cannons on the Wahabi teachers' batil, false aims to which they are calling people, to follow them. How many people following them? Anyone through their conscience accepting their ways? Never! Why they are insisting? What they are asking to do? Say clearly. You are bringing another Shariat? Or you are claiming that you are new prophets? Why you are insisting on wrong way? Come to true way. Yes, you may ask for the true ways? Yes, we are following true ways. I am asking, as Holy Quran saying that: Bismillahi'r Rahmani'r Rahim

ya ayyuha alladhina amanu ittaqu allaha wa kunu ma as-sadiqin (9:119)

If you are claiming that you are trying to teach people what is true, you can do but from where you are reaching to truth? That is a question in front of the whole ummah that we are asking. From which true one you are learning the ways of Wahabi rules? Say. Who is that one? You are thinking only Muhammad Abdul Wahab only he was sadiq, true one? And still he is only true one? How you are claiming? What he brought for Mankind? What was wrong from the Nation of the Seal of Prophets so that he was coming and saying 'I am correcting wrong beliefs'. What he did?

What he was saying were wrong things, destroying graves, never leaving anything. This is new Shariat? The Seal of Prophets (sas) was saying udhkuru'l mawt, udhkuru'l mawta. Yes, so that when you are remembering so many people passed away you should remember death and after death. You made Jannatu’l Mu`alla not leaving anything, you made it as a field. You made same thing of Jannatu’l Baq`ee. What honour coming to ummah, which benefit reached ummah when you are making field of the graveyard. As a simple field, what we gained? To where we reached?

And you are knowing O Wahabi teachers what the Seal of Prophets Sayyidina Muhammad (sas) was saying when he was asked by his Companions about saints. What was his answer? The real sign, if you are asking for a real wali, saints/awliya, he said, idha ru'u dhukirallah (in seeing them, you remember Allah).Yes? The beloved one through Divine Presence, the Prophet (sas) was saying, there are signs when he was looking at them, seeing the hair standing up, and khashiyat, trembling coming and quickly you are remembering the Lord of Heavens. That is sign of wali. Yes, it is true. You can't say 'no'. Prophet (pbuh) was saying: zuroo maqabir al muslimeen (visit the graves of Muslims). Make visiting to them. If you are going to your graveyard and it is only a sandy desert, sandy place, what are you going to think? If anyone going to a field, he is remembering death? No. And their graveyards, Jannat ul Mu`alla and Jannat ul Baq`ee, both of them they made it as a field-yard. People think that they are ploughing and planting on it. What is that? That is your shariat?

If anyone coming before for ziyarah, visiting graveyard, coming and looking that is a domed place of Sayyiditana Khadijat al Kubra. Coming a trembling from her spirituality, running on you and you are coming to remember the time of Prophet (pbuh) and remembering death. 'Once upon a time those people were like us going around now they are under ground. One day we're also going under earth', so that it reminds them of the Lord of Heavens, Who created everything. Why they destroyed? They are saying it is munkar. Up until Muhammad Abdul Wahab it was not munkar and then becoming munkar? What is that foolishness? Who giving such a fatwa to you, O Wahabi teachers? You are on wrong way. If you are not changing wrong way for true ways something will come on your head.

May Allah forgive us. O People, we must try to give to people a true aqeedah, belief, not a wrong one. May Allah forgive us and make our ways true ways. And we are asking that Allah Almighty give His guidance to them who are cheated by Wahabi teachers, to make them on the right way. May Allah forgive us for the honour of the most honoured one here and hereafter. Fatiha. We hope to hear heavenly singing when we are passing from this life to eternal life. Fatiha.
