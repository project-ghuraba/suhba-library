As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, almost two-thirds of Ramaḍān is over today. One-third is left, and there is worship for that. Everyone performs worship. We should follow the worship of our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. It is important to emulate himNone. In shā’a Llāh some of our brothers and people want to perform it. May AllāhNone bless it for them. What is that? That’s the sunnah of i‘tikāf. I‘tikāf means spending the last ten days of Ramaḍān in a mosque.

The sunnah of our Holy ProphetNone is that when the last ten days arrived, our Holy ProphetNone took his bedding to the mosque. Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam didn’t have worldly possessions. HeNone had a mat and a cover for himself. HeNone would bring them and perform more worship in the last ten days without engaging in worldly conversations. Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu‘alayhi wa-sallam didn’t engage in worldly conversations anyway. In order to provide a lesson to people, our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam shows us these things, beautiful things. Doing that is a great virtue and a great beauty. There is much beauty in Ramaḍān. And there is great virtue for some people, for those who do it.

Certainly, there are conditions for it. You must always be in a mosque. You must perform worship in the mosque, break your fast in the evening, perform regular prayers. You can perform extra worship. You must also have saḥūr. Some people ask if they should eat only lentils. No. This is not khalwah seclusion. I‘tikāf is different, and khalwah is different.

Everyone may perform i‘tikāf. But there must be special permission for khalwah. Its requirements are different. And it is not for everyone. It is done by people who need it. Sometimes, some people make everyone go into khalwah. hat is not our concern. Our concern is that our Holy ProphetNone said to perform i‘tikāf. If every time you enter a mosque you say, “Nawaytu l-i‘tikāf,” that earns reward. I‘tikāf is for ten days. But it can be less. People should do as much as they can. Every time they enter a mosque, if they make an intention for i‘tikāf, they attain its reward. Some of our brothers say, “We can’t perform it in the mosque. There is no mosque.” When there is no mosque... Actually, women perform it at home. Women cannot perform it in the mosque. Women must perform i‘tikāf at home. If they have a prayer room, they should perform i‘tikāf for ten days there and perform their worship. Men must do it in mosques. It must be in a place where five daily prayers are performed. If one person, does it in every region, other people share in its blessing too. If no one does it, they will be deprived of that blessing. Shukr to AllāhNone, it is done everywhere now. Everywhere all over the world there are people who perform i‘tikāf.

Therefore, we must give shukr to AllāhNone. We must give shukr for being in this beautiful religion. A lady asked for advice from us to share with people a while ago. We said our advice is to fast. In order to attain the virtue of Ramaḍān, keep your fast. Because many people don’t know what fasting is. They say they will fast, but think AllāhNone will benefit from their fasting. Ḥāshā. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla doesn’t need anything. Even if all people don’t fast, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla won’t lack anything. HeNone won’t gain extra, if they fast. Therefore, fasting, and all worship, is for your own benefit. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gives that benefit to you. HeNone doesn’t need it HimselfNone. You must appreciate this. You must give shukr to AllāhNone for every worship you perform.

We must give shukr so that beyond our shukr, our worship, obedience and servanthood to Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla may become more beautiful, in shā’a Llāh.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, today we are nearly make nineteen days of Ramaḍān. Two-thirds of Ramaḍān passed. And it was quickly, because these days, counted days, coming morning, evening, morning, evening, morning, evening; one day, one day, one week, one day, week, week; and coming months, quickly passing. So, we must be careful to not follow our ego. Follow order of AllāhNone. Who follow order of AllāhNone, he will be happy.

Now, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, this very, most happiest month in year, we are celebrate every day. This, morning, evening, every time, we are celebrate. But now we also, there is one worshipping, what Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, doing: to make i‘tikāf. I‘tikāf, meaning to be last ten days in mosque, whole time in mosque you must be. You can do ‘ibādah, you can read Qur’ān, dhikr, sleeping, waking up, all in mosque. This ten days, which Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam never left it, and for himNone, like obligatory. But for us, it is not obligatory. Only who like to do it, he can do it. And in these days, also you can when make ṣaḥūr, make ifṭār, normal. Some people they asking, “We must eat lentils?” No, no need. If you can, you eat. You can eat this, you can eat anything else. No, not important this. And it is not like khalwah. Khalwah, different. This i‘tikāf for everybody. Everybody, anyone he like to do it, he can do it.

But khalwah, which meaning arba‘īn, forty days in seclusion, it is different. This is special. With special order you can do it. You cannot do it by yourself. If you do it by yourself, you will be hurt. Don't try and don't follow this people. Maybe for other ṭarīqah you can, but in our ṭarīqah, without permission, no seclusion, completely seclusion. I‘tikāf for anybody he like, he can do. Because sunnah mu’akkadah for Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. For us, if anyone, only one can do it, it is enough for whole city. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, in here, other place, many people they doing. Even we was in Jerusalem, there was also the people they was doing there. They said, coming from abroad, make i‘tikāf. And i‘tikāf, ten days. But if you cannot do, you can do – every time coming to mosque, intention for i‘tikāf, “Nawaytu l-i‘tikāf.” If you can do one day, two day, three days, it is... But original, ten days, and last ten days of Ramaḍān. So for this, every time go, coming to mosque, say, “Nawaytu sunnata l-i‘tikāf.” They in mosque, normally also they write in door of mosque: nawaytu l-i‘tikāf, nawaytu sunnata l-i‘tikāf. You get this reward from AllāhNone.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, our thing, we are Muslim, everything AllāhNone give us for us. HeNone want you to be happy. This before was one lady, make interview and asking. After she, mā shā'a Llāh, she was with Mawlānā, she is speaking good. And this last thing, “What you advise for people?” she said. I said, “Advice, to fast.” To fast, because fast meaning: giving you health and happiness. And power, spiritual power you get with fasting. And fasting it is for you. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla give us, give this chance for human being to get reward, to get the most precious thing HeNone gave us. It's precious – all worshipping. Especially fasting, giving you happiness.

Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, saying, “Who fast, he has good, two happiness. To [be] very happy when he break his fast in maghrib time. And second, in ākhirah.” So, this is combine in same time and you feel very happy, this. So, this, for us to be worshipping, not for AllāhNone. HeNone is not in need for our worshipping. HeNone not in need for our fasting, our praying, our anything we do for worshipping. It is not for HimNone. If whole world or whole universe not fasting, not praying, nothing less for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. If all of them fasting, nothing more, plus, for AllāhNone, nothing at all, because HeNone is giving this. HeNone not in need for us, but HeNone, from HisNone mercy, HeNone gave us this good thing to collect for yourself, to be good servant for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. And this is, AllāhNone we must appreciate this. If you are fasting, other not fasting, for this we give advice for them – “You must, don't lose this chance.” The biggest thing happiness. Everybody looking for happiness. Happiness in worshipping, in praying, fasting, doing good thing. AllāhNone make this, more and more people to be like this, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
