As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam states, “There are 360 joints in the human body.” Of course, Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla informed our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam of everything—every single atom—of the human being He created; he knows what doctors do not know.

Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam says that a charity must be given for each of these joints; ṣadaqah is required. Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam says, “Charity can also be given in the form of money.” He also says, “Removing harmful objects from the way—such as stones, thorns, paper, and the like—is also an act of ṣadaqah.” After all, not everyone may have the means to give 360 monetary ṣadaqah a day.

Therefore, our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam explains that performing these other acts serves substitutes for that ṣadaqah.

What suffices for all of them is the two-rakʿah ḍuḥā prayer (mid-morning prayer). The ḍuḥā prayer can be performed twenty minutes, half an hour, or an hour after sunrise. That initial two-rakʿah prayer performed shortly after sunrise is the ishrāq prayer (sunrise prayer).

The ḍuḥā prayer can be performed in sets of two ranging from two to twelve rakʿahs. Performing at least two rakʿahs serves as the ṣadaqah required for all the joints in the body. If you begin your day with taḥajjud or the morning prayer, spend the day occupied with deeds that Allah loves and that benefit you, and conclude the day with prayer as well, then you will have spent your entire life on the way of Allah. This benefits you and brings health to your body, for ṣadaqah is health, while also elevating your spirituality.

This is a truly beautiful thing. Through it, you become a beloved servant of Allah and a beloved servant of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. Your love for him will increase and become deeper, for practicing his Sunnah means striving to be like him and emulating him. This is the very aim of our ṭarīqah and of Islām.

To follow the Prophet, to walk his way, and to strive to resemble him as best we can—this is the greatest virtue and the highest beauty for a human being, for a believer. Honoring him is likewise a supreme merit.

May Allah protect us from those who do not respect him; their harm is immense, for they would keep you away from this beautiful reward and this beautiful life. If you were to become like them, neither your words nor your deeds would be of any benefit to you.

Therefore, may Allah protect us all. May Allah grant us the grace to always walk his way until our very last breath, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
