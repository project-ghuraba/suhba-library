As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla has given intellect to mankind so that they use that intellect. A person who uses his mind certainly finds the right way. If he doesn't use his mind and listens to words of others... "Wa-in tuṭi‘ akthara man fi l-arḍi yuḍillūka ‘an sabīli Llāh" (6:116). If you listen to others, in most cases people will mislead you and separate you from the way of AllāhNone. You will be destroyed. AllāhNone gave mind to people in order for them to be saved, in order to do their work in dunyā and, more important than dunyā, to do things for ākhirah, to know Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla who gives their rizq, their life and souls.

These people, most people, use it. They think they use it in the right way. However, it is wrong. They are going in the wrong direction. While the way is here, you are going in the wrong direction and saying, "I am going where I will reach." There are many things. There is philosophy, there are other crooked religions. People even don't accept any religion and say, "We appeared by ourselves from nothing." There are many people like this. They don't use their minds. They are not clever people. Clever people are aware of goodness. They know from where and from whom goodness is coming. Who is helping us? Who brought us to this world? Who put us here? Who is that? People must know this because Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla showed it, and prophets showed it. Clever people know that every hour Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is the OneNone who does everything, HeNone is the CreatorNone who gives and takes. Who knows this, is clever. He uses his mind correctly. Who doesn't know may have as many diplomas as he likes. He may have graduated from as many universities, written as many books, done this and that; he is a person who didn't use his mind. May AllāhNone protect us.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says, "a-fa-lā ta‘qilūn" (37:138), in Qur’ān. "Don't you have mind? Are you not clever? When there is so much proof and evidence, you are saying, 'No.' Where is your mind?" Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla asks like this. "Are you stupid? Don't you have a mind?" If you have a mind, you wouldn't do such things. You wouldn't do things that will ruin you. Who does such things didn't use his mind. He has mind but he doesn't use it. He used it for other things. He used it wrongly. Thinking he did right by himself, he actually did wrong to himself. May AllāhNone protect all of us, in shā’a Llāh. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla HeNone give human beings thinking, brain and from brain or from heart also. To think not only from brain. Maybe people they said: "Human being they have big brain therefore this, they are more intelligent from other creatures, from animals, from bird, from anything. They thought because other brains small, they thought it is from when brain big it will be more clever. No. It's between...

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla also in Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n saying: "Lahum qulūbun lā yafqahūna bihā" (7:179). For heart also, connecting with brain. Not only brain giving you good thinking, cleverness or to be intelligent. No. It's from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HisNone wisdom, they give everybody this and must be you know your, with this thinking, with this intelligence AllāhNone gave you, if you are real clever and intelligent, you must know what you must do. When you are doing thing harming you, you are not using your clever intelligence. You are, just you think you are clever, you are intelligent. But normally you are not. You are, because you harming yourself and you think you doing good. It is not good. The good thing – to know your CreatorNone and who is your, what is your mission in this world.

And Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla HeNone told you, with HisNone Messengers and heavenly Books, why you are here, who you must do, what you must do, and who must you follow. Don't follow shayṭān. Follow Prophets. This, if you are clever, you do this. If not, you follow other way, wrong direction. Going whole... Maybe sometimes people, in old times there was not this machine, they was looking going wrong direction. "Oh we come fifty kilometers wrong direction. We must go back all this way." Sometimes, maybe going a thousand kilometers after they, "Oh this wrong. Why we come until here?" But some people still going, not stopping – wrong direction, wrong way. This is in life happening from going from beginning to wrong direction. Somebody tell them the right direction, they're not listening. "Oh no, don't, you are idiot. We are clever. Don't tell us. We are knowing what this." Following wrong religion, following wrong philosophy, following wrong idea, many idea.

Because Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying in Qur’ān who, if you listen to most of people, you will be wrong way because most of people they are following their desire, following their ego and they are not happy to be right way. So most of people, if you are following them, you will be, go wrong way. You must follow special people who AllāhNone send them. If you follow them, you will be safe and you use your intelligence, your cleverness, you are clever. But now you are not, even you are finish ten universities, you write a hundred books, many people following you. It's all not a sign for you are clever. If you are clever, you must think about your end, your life here and hereafter. You must be careful. For this Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla in Qur’ān saying: "A-fa-lā ta‘qilūn" (2:44) – meaning you don't understand, you are not using your brain, not using your intelligence. This is Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla warning people in Qur’ān: Use your thinking. Go after right thinking, don't go after wrong thing. And HeNone is clearly have send Messengers, send heavenly Books. Everything it is not hidden. Everything openly for human beings to follow this. They gave instruction. When you follow this, you will be safe. Many people they have new machine they look for instructions and after this they're using. If not looking for this, maybe from beginning they will finish, burn this machine and they will not be, not good for them. AllāhNone help us to use what HeNone order for us, what is useful for us, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
