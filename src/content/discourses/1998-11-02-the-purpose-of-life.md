A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Lā ḥawla wa lā quwwata illā biLlāhi l-‘alayyi l-‘aẓīm. Don't be idle. Don't be idle. If you are idle, you lose.

Dastūr yā Sayyidī, yā Sulṭānu l-Anbiyā’ ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayka yā Sayyidī, yā Rasūla Llāh. Dastūr yā Sayyidī, yā Sulṭānu l-Awliyā’. Madad yā Rijālu Llāh. Astaghfiru Llāh. Astaghfiru Llāh, astaghfiru Llāh.

Allāh Almighty (jj) loves people, who repent. This is a ṣuḥbah from our grand shaykh Sulṭānu l-Awliyā’. It is transferred to our hearts. Each time there is different manifestation. We need to speak according to that manifestation. You can tell a story a thousand times but each one is different. The way you tell a story is according to the manifestation on that day. And with that manifestation, on that day, if you accept and listen to it, you'll find closeness to Allāh Allmighty (jj). Our shaykh Sulṭānu l-Awliyā’ said: "Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah". He obeys Shāh-i Naqshband's words and legacy and always keeps his spiritual connection with us. Let's listen to him. Let's see what kind of manifestation there is. If he says, tells something, a hundred times, each time there is a different attitude. There is a different way of showing, there is a different benefit. Yes.

Saints are the friends of Allāh (jj) and the Prophet ‘alayhi ṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salām. And how did they become saints? They listened to Allāh (jj) and the Prophet (sas) and became saints. The way to sainthood is open, easy. If you want to be a saint follow the way that Allāh (jj) showed. You'll be a beloved servant. To be the Prophet's (saw) beloved nation you follow the way that the Prophet (saw) showed. The Prophet (saw) will write your name among his friends. How happy are those that their names are written among the friends of Allāh (jj) or the friends of the Prophet (saw), while they are alive. That's your gain. If you won Allāh's (jj) or His Prophet's (saw) love that is the biggest gain. If you don't win that, what's the use if you win the whole world? That person turns into Qārūn. Qārūn had the whole world. He had the treasures of this world yet he couldn't be beloved to Allāh (jj) or the Prophet (saw). He had all that wealth. With all that wealth, he couldn't make Allāh (jj) love him, Moses (as) love him.

Try to make yourself beloved to Allāh (jj) and the Prophet (saw) on all occasions. If He (jj) gives poverty, you can make yourself beloved to Allāh and His Messenger. If He (jj) gives wealth, you can make yourself beloved to Allāh (jj) and His Messenger. So with everything you can make yourself beloved to Allāh (jj) and His Messenger. At the same time you can distance yourself from Allāh and His Messenger with any of those things. May Allāh (jj) protect us. You may get closer to shayṭān. Every state may get you closer to Allāh (jj) or shayṭān. You can get closer to Allāh or shayṭān with wealth. You can get closer to Allāh and the Prophet (saw) with a weapon. So you can get closer to shayṭān just like you get closer to Allāh and the Prophet (saw). Whatever there is in this world - you can get closer to Allāh (jj) or shayṭān with everything. You have to distinguish between them.

Even the sheep when they graze, there are two angels warning them: "This grass is poisonous. This one is good for you. This is good, this is bad for you" say the angels, who are with the sheep. Allāh (jj) protects the sheep. You are a human being, won't Allāh (jj) give you sign? When you do something, will you get closer to Allāh (jj) or shayṭān? But there are a lot of parasites in the hearts. You can't listen. Either good or bad, you take it anyway. When you take it, if you can't tell the difference, you are distanced from Allāh, you get closer to shayṭān. Your duty in this world, what is asked from you, is to be close to Allāh (jj). Allāh (jj) wants this from you. "Seek closeness to Me. Don't take the road, that distances you from Me. So many things distance you from Me. Beware. Because when you are distant from Me, you are distant from everything, from My blessing. There is no limit to that distance. You can't find the limit, you'll be lost. Instead of being close to Me, on the contrary you'll be distanced each day from Me."

Seek closeness to Allāh (jj) in every possible way. That's why Allāh Almighty (jj) ṣalawātu Llāhu wa salāmu ‘alayh showed 500 mā jā’a bihi n-nabī - He (jj) showed 800 forbidden actions. 500 mā jā’a bihi n-nabī means the farḍs, wājibs, sunnahs, mustaḥab, mandhūb, mubāḥ they are 500 in total. There are deeds done with wealth, with the body, with the tongue. There are 500 kinds of services that get you closer to Allāh (jj). There are 800 forbidden actions that distance you from Allāh. That is also written. That's why a believer should have a scale. What kind of a scale? The scale of sharī‘ah. The scale of sharī‘ah weighs your every deed. Weighs, gives you the balance. It tells you if it is balanced or not, the sharī‘ah. They talk against sharī‘ah - heedless idiots. They think it is a game. Bees will sting their tongues. They'll finish them by cutting their tongues. Sharī‘ah belongs to Allāh (jj). You must have ablution to speak about sharī‘ah. You'll talk after you take ablution. But al-jāhilu jasūr, because of ignorance they are brave. They talk against sharī‘ah. Don't talk against sharī‘ah. They'll cut your tongue. They won't cut it once. When there is a bee they'll be finished by being cut. Allāhumma ‘āfinā.

Sharī‘ah belongs to Allāh (jj). Sharī‘ah informs you about the value and scale of your deed. The scale shows you if it gets you closer to Allāh (jj) or shayṭān. Now there are 500 services from worshipping, obedience, reading, writing, actions; there are 500 actions done with wealth or body. Some are farḍ, some wājib, some sunnah. Because sharī‘ah judges the value of people's deeds, actions. There is no system other than sharī‘ah that informs us about the value of deeds. Which system has it? Which system shows the value of people's actions? Is there anything? Which system? Do they have it in a secular system? Is there anything that weighs people's deeds and judges their value? None of them has it. Only Allāh's sharī‘ah shows the value of people's deeds. It puts them in the scale. Sharī‘ah is very refined and makes you live in an order and a system. Sharī‘ah prevents your feet from slipping. Because if you slip, you'll fall into shayṭān's arms. You'll get close to shayṭān. Sharī‘ah shows you. There are thousands, ten thousands of rubbish books - can they show a system that can weigh people's deeds and judge their values? If there is a regimen or a government, let them give advice. Impossible.

There is a parliament here. How does it judge? Is there anyone, who can determine this except sharī‘ah? No. Except sharī‘ah, the judgement they'll make are meetings here, in the coffee shops, at the restaurants, at the night clubs, at the bars, at the cinemas, at the theatres. Wherever it is, they are all gathering. They can't say this gathering has a different meaning. Because sharī‘ah brought such a system that people's deeds are according to their intentions. Their value is according to their intentions. Your deed is different by your intention. These people's meeting here and there in a cafe - democracy says this is also a meeting, that also. What is its value? "They are also meetings, people are gathering." They talked, drank and went. They came to a cafe, gathered around tables. It finished at midnight. Sharī‘ah says no.

This meeting here is for Allāh (jj). People come here for a reason and intention. Ask them their purpose. What is the purpose of the meeting in the dargah? For what purpose they gathered here? People, who gathered in the dargah their purpose is to seek closeness to Allāh (jj). They search for Allāh (jj). They want to find Allāh (jj). In a cafe? Ask, is there anyone, who comes to a cafe to find Allāh (jj)? Do they come to find Allāh? Allāh Allāh. Who is in the cafe? A cafe is a place, of which Allāh doesn't approve. People there didn't come to search for Allāh. They came to feed their egos. They came to spend time. They came to waste time. They gather for nonsense intentions. What is the value of people in the cafe? Their intentions are zero, they are also zero. They are vulgar. But people, who gather in the dargah they have a purpose. People's value increases according to the greatness, holiness, value of their purposes and intentions. That's why sharī‘ah is a discipline.

Animals in the jungle don't like discipline. To live without discipline is the nature of the animals in the jungle. Whoever has a certain character, they'll be with them on the Day of Judgement. That's why on the Day of Judgement people will come in the shape of wolves, in the shape of bears, in the shape of foxes in the shape of snakes. He is a human but will appear as a snake. He is a human but will appear as a bear. Sometimes in this shayṭān's box (TV) they show ads, something on the outside is different in the inside. Now they sit with people, but will appear as a bear, he will sit like a bear among people. It will be known that he had bear attributes. He'll come in the shape of a snake. The snake is like this, a person will be inside. "Who is this?" "Didn't you recognize him, why didn't you recognize?" "Let me look, Allāh Allāh." "He had a very high position in the world?" "How? No. Under that position he was a snake in secret. He was a bear in secret, he was a fox in secret." Someone will appear inside the fox. He wasn't a fox, he was a human. No. Fox means he was a man like a fox. People, who obey the sharī‘ah regulations will come in the form of humans. Whoever goes out of sharī‘ah regulations will come in the form of animals. He may appear as a scorpion. He'll come sitting inside a big scorpion. He'll come in a wolf's form, a fox's form, a hyena form, a snake form, in a scorpion form - which means that person will be seen in that animal. It will be known that this man had those attributes. He harmed people like that animal. So, try to make your reality a human being. They say that the form of a baby appears on the 40th day in the mother's womb. It takes the shape of a human, the size of a finger. It is easy for it to take its form. It takes the shape of a human but being a human doesn't take 40 days, it may take 40 years to make his reality a human. That's why he says "fight with the ego." Our jihād today is to turn our ego into a human, to turn our form into a human in the real sense. We fight for this. Otherwise, the other type of jihād isn't present today. There is no need for it anyway. If everybody trained their egos, made their reality human as well as their form, the world would come up roses. Nobody would interfere, nobody would fight with anyone. Everyone would be content. Nobody would oppress anyone, nobody would be unfair. Whoever cheats somebody from his rights, his reality isn't human. Whoever harms someone, he is a human in shape but inhuman in reality. That's why a Muslim is someone, who doesn't harm with his hand or his tongue. There is a saying "Bites from front, kicks from behind". If you bite from front, kick from behind, you aren't a human, you are an animal. Human in form but harms people - your reality is an animal.

Why do you make your reality a human? What is it for? You try to make your reality human. Only sharī‘ah can make that; the measures of sharī‘ah. There are no other measures except these. With sharī‘ah measures, you become a perfect human. A perfect human is close to Allāh (jj). Otherwise if you aren't a perfect human you are close to shayṭān. You get closer to shayṭān. So, O people! Yā ayyuha l-insān, "O people!" says Allāh Almighty (jj) O people if you make your reality a human being, you'll get closer to Allāh (jj). O people, who have human form but can't make their reality human, you get closer to shayṭān. Shayṭān is close to the fire. Whoever is close to shayṭān, will fall into the fire. Closeness to Allāh (jj) and His Messenger (saw) is something that concerns all people, maybe it is something, that shows our life's purpose. People should know what to do. When you think of what to do in this life - "I should do this to be close to Allāh (jj). I shouldn't do that to be close to shayṭān." Do what gets you closer to Allāh (jj). Leave what gets you closer to shayṭān.

This is the reality that 124.000 prophets, 104 holy books, brought and informed. O people! Hold tight to the service that gets you close to Allāh (jj). Give up actions that get you closer to shayṭān. Leave. Withdraw. There is nothing else. If anyone says there's something else, let them speak. What is in Islamic sharī‘ah? This is in Islamic sharī‘ah - Islamic sharī‘ah is a way that gets you close to Allāh. Other than that you get closer to shayṭān. It's up to you. Nobody can be saved or find goodness by getting close to shayṭān. Getting close to shayṭān may make you inhuman. People, who follow shayṭān turn into monsters. Whoever follows the Prophet of the end of times and the Prophets, are the saints. They are saints, beloved to Allāh (jj), beloved to the Prophet (saw). Be careful because death walks behind us, won't leave us. One day death will catch us too. Everybody is followed by their own death. When it ends, you'll fall down. Don't forget that death follows people. There is no rule such as "I'm young, it happens to the old. I'm healthy, it happens to the unhealthy. I'm rich, it happens to the poor. I'm strong, it happens to the weak." No. That's why this is about the present moment. Who am I with, at this moment? Look at that. Know who you are with in the present moment. Are you with Allāh (jj) or shayṭān? If you are with Allāh (jj), relax. If you are with shayṭān you are anxious. You have worries in your heart. It won't be satisfied. There is fear, anxiety inside. It isn't peaceful. Because if a fish is out of the water would it be peaceful? It wants the water. If you are with Allāh (jj) you are like a fish in the sea. If you aren't with Allāh, you are like a bird in a cage. A bird in a cage tries to escape and flutters. A bird in a cage isn't at peace. Even if you put it in a golden cage, a bird in a cage isn't at peace. If you make a big cage for the bird, still it isn't at peace. It isn't at peace. It leaves the empty space and comes to the fences, the bars that surround it. It clings there, wants to open it and go out. There is a big empty space in the cage. No. It doesn't want that space. It clings to the bars, stays there, wants to go out. People are also like that. Your peace is with Allāh (jj). If you are with Allāh (jj), you are like the fish in the sea - doesn't want to go out of the water, the sea. But if you aren't with Allāh (jj) it makes the whole world a cage, you'll cling to the bars of the cage. The whole world. Fly in every direction. No. He'll come, to cling and to stay on these bars, wanting to go out.

That's why may Allāh (jj) not make us from the ones, who are imprisoned by their egos. The ego imprisons itself and leaves. It deprives you of peace and comfort. Don't be with your ego. Be with Allāh (jj), you'll be at peace even if you are in prison. Yūsuf (as) was in prison, but because he was with his Lord, he wasn't grieving. But pharaoh was sitting on his throne and, because he wasn't with his Lord, he felt imprisoned on his throne. He was complaining. He wanted to be entertained. That's why today people can't stay at home, they rush outside. Why? They want to be entertained because they aren't peaceful. 20th century people have lost peace. Because of that they say: "How do I find peace, how do I entertain myself?" You can't find it outside. Wherever you go, you feel imprisoned. You sway from side to side. Games, entertainment. On the way back home, your world is black. All those colorful lights of thousands, millions of watts shining, cannot lighten you inside. When he goes out of that atmosphere it is black on the way back home. Those ones, who wear glamorous dresses, ball gowns, look at their faces on the way back. Leave their faces, look at their hearts. Their world is black.

Whoever is with Allāh, has peace. Whoever is with shayṭān, has no peace. Sharī‘ah shows you the ways to be with Allāh (jj) the ways to be closer to Allāh (jj). Once you leave, you'll be with shayṭān. Shayṭān's way is dark. It is scary and dangerous. Finished.
