Madad! Dastur ya Shah Mardan. Present us your holy words which are treasures. May we listen! Let's liven up! Let's get refreshed! May we be honourable! O people! Seek honour in this world! Honour begins with: Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. MashaAllah, mā shā’ Allāhu kāna, wa mā lam yashā’ lam yakun. Our Allah. Please go on Shah Mardan! Your friends admire you. They want to listen! Madad, O Shah Mardan, madad. May he cleanse us from the dirtiness of this world. Here is Shah Mardan's instruction: Prophet sallAllahu wa sallam said, "This world is a carcass". This world is a carcass! Don't run after it! Allahu Akbar.

Janab Allah created Man honourable. A carcass is not the ration of an honourable human being. He didn't say, run after the carcass, hunt a carcass, or eat the carcass. Please go on Shah Mardan! A human being is honourable. Men are bestowed grants according to their honour by their Creator. Madad, O Shah Mardan. Come on, O our friends who are listening! Don't run after the carcass of dunya! "At-tayyibūna li-ttayyibāti" (24:26). Beautiful things are for pure ones, only if you are pure/clean! Tayyibat, li-ttayyibin. Pure things, for pure ones.

Please go on Shah Mardan! Let's listen. We are not created for this world. Ta'allamu, learn! You are not created for this world! Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar wa Lillahi l-hamd. Thanks to Ghani Padishah (Allah), He created us beautiful and exceptional! Dastur Ya Shah Mardan. Your friends admire you! "Ta'allamu," he says to learn! What to learn? Study yourselves! The human race has filled this world with effluence. Is this our occupation? Eating, drinking, then filling the WC. Are we created for this? Astaghfirullah.

Please go on Shah Mardan! Your friends admire you! Let's say, Allah Allah! Let's say: hasbunā-llāhu wa ni’ma l-wakīl. Janab Rabb ul-Izzat dressed us with a robe of light (nur). He dressed us with an honoured, robe of light. The angels said, "dress that robe of honour on us too," requesting to be dressed in it! He said, "No! No!" "They are beautiful and exceptional". "They have a speciality". What is that? They represent Me! Allahu Akbar. This means that Janab ul-Haqq created us beautiful, He wants us to be beautiful. "I gave beauty to every creation," but the beauty given to humankind is another. That honour is exceptional. The Children of Adam are dressed with that honour. Our Allah, Our Subhan! Ad-dīnu n-nasiha. Religion is advice. We must know our way and our practice; we must know the way we should act. We didn't come to this world, to fight, to quarrel, to fight each other. Not created to make wars. We are dressed with such a robe of honour that when a sultan meets another sultan, he stands up. He greets the other sultan. The giving of salam, from one king to another, from one sultan to another, is different. So, the honour given to Man is not the same as the honour given to angels. The angels were ordered to prostrate to Adam. Adam was not ordered to prostrate to the angels. Come to your senses!

Allah, Allahu Dhū l-Jalāl dressed the Children of Adam, with the honour that He dressed them in, and then He said to the angels: O Angels, I dressed you with robes of honour, but what I dressed on Adam, I did not dress on you. "Make sajda!" O Children of Adam! Leave fighting! Leave fighting! Allah Allah, what an honour! Angels asked for that honour, it was denied to them. "That honour is for Adam! Make sajda in honour of his majesty". He is Adam. They are the Children of Adam. Allah!

O ignorant people! Ignorant people who don't know themselves! Those ignorant ones who can't appreciate their honour. Please go on Shah Mardan! O people! The most honourable creation of Janab ul-Haqq, O Children of Adam! I made angels prostrate to you! In order that your respect and honour of each other be seen when the angels prostrated to you, I ordered that you propstrate (to Allah). How do you respect each other? By killing each other? By destroying and burning? Say, O spiritual ones! Say what the honour of mankind is and inform people.

O our Lord, our Subhan! "Wamā khalaqtu ‘l jinna wa l-insa illa liy’abudūn" (51:56). I created man and jinn, in order to serve Me. Ey, "li y'arifūn". By the means of that robe of honour I dressed on My servants from My Majesty, let them glance into the Mirror of Mightiness and they shall see themselves. You are Subhan, Allah, You are Subhan, our Lord! Such honour has been given to Man! Please go on Shah Mardan! Having been given this honour, it doesn't suit Man to act dishonourably. He has dressed on you the robe of honour, the robe of sultanate, Ey, in order to enable you to be sultan.

O Children of Adam! Janab ul-Haqq made you sultan! What is your occupation? Eating, drinking, then filling the WC? Be ashamed O Mankind! Is it your work to fill the WC? Your work and your degree have been appointed by Janab Allah! You are supreme, you are exalted! You must observe both, your sublimity, and also exalt the ones Allah exalted. Madad, Ya Rijal Allah! Madad, O Shah Mardan. This is our mission, to exalt the ones Allah exalted. This is what Rabb ul-Izzat asks from you! "Wa rafa`na laka dhikrak" (94:4). "O My Habib, We have exalted your dhikr!" "All the angels in the heavens are made to prostrate to you so that you may know Me! Never leave making sajda to Me!" Sajda gives you honour! The ones without sajda, have no honour! O our Lord! Mā shā’ Allāhu kān. Don't fight O people! Please go on Shah Mardan! Let's say: Bismillahi r-Rahman, Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Thanks to our Lord, let's wear the honourable robe of Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Let's make qiyam for our Lord! Let's make ruku to our Lord! Let's make sajda to our Lord! Wa min Allah at-tawfiq.

Fatiha.
