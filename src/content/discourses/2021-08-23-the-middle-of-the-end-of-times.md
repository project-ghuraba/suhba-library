Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

BismiLlahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim: “هيلإ لاّ إ ه الل نم ىجنم لاو أجلم لا”, “There is no refuge and no place of safety from Allah except in Him”. It is said about the condition of these days. We have reached the middle of the end of times. People cannot figure out what to do. There is a trouble that won’t go away. They try one thing, it doesn’t work. They try another thing, it doesn’t work either. It is not only in our country but all over the world. There is a game of shaytan everywhere, but it certainly doesn’t work without the permission of Allah ﷻ.

Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla gave its remedy, and they are running away from this remedy. BismiLlahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim: ‘ نٌ يهبمُّ رٌ يذه ن ُهنْ مّه مكُ ل يهّنإه ۖ هاللَّه ى لهإ اورُّ فه ف’, ‘So flee to Allah. Indeed, I am to you from Him a clear warner.’ (Qur’ān 51:50). “Seek refuge in Allah ﷻ. Who resorts to Allah ﷻ will be saved. Otherwise, I am warning you. And I am a great warner”, He says. If you don’t do so, you won’t be able to escape the troubles.

The whole world doesn’t know what to do. They think they will be saved if they do this, or it will be good if they do that. And they are waiting to say, “I did this” when something works out. You cannot do anything without the permission of Allah ﷻ. You cannot escape troubles and you cannot escape disasters. As soon as people get a little strength, they get wild. They don’t accept anything and say, “I did this”. These Kafirs don’t even accept the existence of Allah ﷻ. So Allah ﷻ sent this trouble for them to deal with it. Whereas, we should seek refuge in Allah ﷻ. We should beg Allah ﷻ, and the trouble will be lifted.

There were always troubles coming on Pharaoh. When Sayyidina Mūsā ‘alayhi s-salam said “Return to Allah ﷻ. Believe in Allah ﷻ. Trust in Allah ﷻ”, they asked him to show them something. And then frogs started appearing from everywhere. They said, “Fine, we will believe. Just make Dua for this to end”. That trouble was gone. But again, they didn’t believe. So fleas covered everywhere and bit everyone. People said, “Make Dua to your God so that fleas are gone. We will believe”. One trouble after another, water turned into blood, but they didn’t change.

It is the same in our time. An illness has occurred. They called Adhans and Salawat in the beginning, and then forgot about them. And when it became lighter, they stopped completely. It is exactly the same. Various troubles are coming one after another. When they think they are saved from one thing, another appears. But instead of improving, people are only going wilder. We should repent to Allah ﷻ and beg Allah ﷻ to save us from these troubles. Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla is our only destination. There is nowhere else to go, not to Kafirs. Do not expect mercy from Kafirs. Kafirs are merciless. The Merciful is Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla. Kafirs give you poison instead of water, may Allah ﷻ protect us. May we repent to Allah ﷻ so that He helps us and protects Islam and Muslims insha’Allah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
