Shaykh Muhammad Nazim Al-Haqqani An-Naqshibandi, Sohbat from 2005.

Dastur ya Sayyidi. Send us, O our master, inspirations from words of Haqq/truth that are necessary for us and our followers, that will wake us up at a level that we can carry. We don't know. We want to know. What is the sign of someone who knows? He does what he knows.

This is a sohbah/association. A morning sohbah when the day is beginning. First of all - let's mention Allah's Name first; it is a must for all before every action. How nice. The person who wrote the Mawlid, may Allah increase his rank. Sulaiman Chalabi. Chalabi means - someone who gives himself for Allah. They also used to say Chalab in old Turkish language, which means someone who gave up himself for Allah. Someone who lives for Allah. How nice. How beautiful the one who lives for Allah. How nice. The holy (Sul.Chalabi) said like that - We must mention Allah's Name first of all, it is a must for all before every action. It says to mention Allah Almighty's Name before all. Let's do that - Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. O Allah. Start with Basmala. An action that doesn't start with Basmala does not succeed. Any action without Basmala will be ruined, finished. Don't look at disbelievers. Their situation is different. They were given dunya. Disbelievers are given heaven on earth but they don't get the eternal life & paradise like believers. That is why they don't use Basmala.

I am always seeing our followers, Haji Sevim, Haji Neshe, Haji Nurten, who are present for our morning associations. Allah gave us honor through these associations. Allah Almighty taught us this through our elders. What a great honor that we were present together with our elders. What a great honor. It is a great honor to enter the presence of our great ones. Allah is the greatest one. Allahu Akbar. Ya Rabbi. To stand in Your presence and glorify you endlessly will still be nothing. Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar. How nice. How Great He is. Our Almighty Mawla gave us such greatness. To be in the presence of holy ones gives us greatness. When a great one accepts you in his presence, isn't it a big honor? People become very proud when a VIP of dunya invites them. Why become so proud? "The president invited us for a gala night... or pala night... or fighting night." They are inviting them. They say - we came by your invitation. Come to our meeting with the president at so and so place to get honor. What we mean is - being with great ones gives us honor. We gain honor.

If you sit with lowly ones, what honor is there? Does the one sitting in the coffee house have honor? What honor can he have? What honor does the one sitting in the tea house have? Or the one sitting in clubs? What honor does the one who goes to clubs have? But the one who goes to the mosque has honor. The one who goes to the dergah/zawiya has honor. The one who goes to the masjid has honor. The one who goes to Mecca has honor. The one who goes to Madina Munawwara has honor. The one who goes to the holy city of Sham has honor. They take honor. We are saying - we are sitting with great ones. Allah Almighty granted us to sit with His beloved servants. He gave us honor. Honor is by being with them. Those who don't sit with great ones but sit with useless ones become worthless. Who is great one? The one who sits with Allah. Can we sit with Allah? "Ana Jaleesu min man dhakarani." I am sitting with My servant who remembers/mentions Me. Jalees means - you who are sitting in our assembly. It means those who are present at the association. Allah Almighty says - I am with My servant who mentions Me. What a great honor. It has no limits.

How foolish we are that we leave dhikr. Allah says He is with the servant who makes dhikr. That servant remembers Me. What great honor for the servant who remembers Allah Almighty. This is what we saw and heard from our holy ones. And we are proud. We are grateful. It doesn't mean we are boasting like people of dunya. People of dunya boast and become proud and arrogant and say - we were invited by the president last night. Allah Allah. So, what happened? Are you such a great person? "We are important so they invited us". People are drunk. They have no idea who is great, who is small, who is here, who is not. They say that in order to be proud and arrogant. They think they are something and they were invited because they are great men. They are arrogant. To be in the assembly of great holy ones, we are grateful that Allah Almighty allowed us to be in the assembly of Beloved saints. We are not worthy to be here, but Allah allowed us.

Let's recite Allah's Name first. How nicely said by Sulaiman Chalabi. Some foolish ones say don't recite Mawlid. The mawlid is teaching us. The explanation of mawlid would fill 10 books. 100 books would be filled. It is full of knowledge. The ignorant teachers of this time, they will be punished. They are wearing ties. They are not aware of anything. They oppose mawlid recitation. What right do they have? They are wrong. Which part of the mawlid recitation is wrong? It is teaching us - O nation of Muhammad, recite Allah's Name first of all. Mention Allah's Name before anything else. Say - Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. That is our duty. It is the duty of all servants before every action. It is our duty to receite Basmala before every action. If you forget, it has no blessings. It will not succeed, it will finish, it will be rubbish. Don't take disbelievers as an example. You are believers. You forgot Basmala. Allah Almighty forgets those who forget Him - I will leave you by yourself, since you don't ask help from Me.

When you mention My Glorious Name, it means you want My help. But you ask America for help. Your removed My Name from your parliaments and forbade it. In the past, it was written - Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. When the republic started, they removed Basmala Sharifa from the parliaments. What right or authority do you have? Will you succeed when you do that? For 80 years did they succeed? Every day they sink lower. Every day there are more accidents and calamities. Allah's wrath is upon them but they don't ask why. If a car breaks down, the driver gets down to see why it stopped. He opens the engine and looks here and there, he opens this and looks at that. He fixes something and tries to start the car again. Either the car starts or he doesn't know how to fix it. He doesn't know about fixing the car so he calls the professionals to fix it.

For 80 years we didn't gain one speck of land. They wasted all the inheritance left by the Ottomans. They ruined everything and lived off the remaining crumbs. Why don't you ask? Why did this country become like this? Why did these people become so immoral? Why are our youngsters on the wrong way? Why can't the government succeed? What is the reason? Why don't you ask? They are never asking. How can that be? How can someone who doesn't go to a doctor, find a cure? They are not asking. Allah helped those ones - It was written in their assemblies - Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. When they became victorious and won everything - now we will keep people far away from Islam. We will make reform. The first reform is to remove the Book that Allah sent? Hasha. Who has the right? We will remove Shari'ah/Divine law and bring European laws and govern the people.

How nice they have governed so far. Is this the way to govern? "Our parliaments are not mosques. Religious writings are for mosques only. We don't need Basmala, bring it down. Put up a portrait instead. We'll get support from this portrait." How can that be? The portrait is bigger than them and they are sitting under it. They brought down the Basmala and everything they do is unsuccessful. They are sitting in the assembly of great ones. What does it say - let's mention Allah's Name first of all. If someone tries to say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim, everyone in the parliament will turn the building upside down. "Why did he say Basmala? Why say Basmala here? We build such a big building here. His head is covered. How can you enter this parliament?"

Allah Allah. What is this? What happened to us? Are you angels? Are you angels or satans? People were like angels, but they turned them into satans. May Allah protect us from their evil. This is enough for today. This bombardment won't finish until noon. This is important. People are not aware. And these stupid, ignorant religious teachers are all dressed so fancy, and they claim to be scholars. What are you wearing? "I am the head preacher at the mosque." I thought you were a customs porter. A chef has a uniform - a chef hat and a white coat. And you are the important men who are serving Islam and you don't have anything? Don't you have an official religious dress? "Religious dress is forbidden." Where are we? Did you ask the people if you should forbid religious dress? You did this according to your own mind and now people are suffering because of that. The religious teachers are like that. They must wear ties. They must wear ironed trousers. They must shave their faces and look like women - no beard, no moustache. "I prepared you" "I prepared your destination well" Soon you will understand where you will end up.

Let's mention Allah's Name first. "It's not allowed, we don't say it." They are teaching you Allah's Name. When you mention Allah's Name, which is our duty, it is a sign that you are working for Allah. It's a sign that you recognize Allah. Belief in Allah strengthens your faith and gives you honor. Who are you to not allow mawlid recitation? You say - don't recite, you won't get any deeds. What shame and ignorance. They are following satan, not Islam. Their way is not the way of Allah and the Prophet. People should not pray behind these foolish ones. We seek refuge in Allah. This bombardment is for them. They will be questioned. Even if I don't question them, those coming after me will judge them. Allah won't leave them without questioning. Allah will ask them about their insults to Islam. There is more bombardment coming to them.

We seek refuge in Allah. Ya Rabbi tawba ya Rabbi. Ya Rabbi, send us the Sultan of Islam who will pull us to the right path. Islam is without a leader. There is a leader but he not appeared yet, that's why we can't do anything. They do not see us as worthy, ya Rabbi. They don't listen to us, nor do they accept what we do, nor do they accept us. Ya Rabbi, we leave them to You. Those who fight Your religion, those without respect to Your Prophet, those who don't accept Your Book, we leave them to You, ya Rabbi. Khudhhum akhdhi azizun Muqtadirun ya Jabbaru ya Qahharu. We ask for Your Mercy for innocent children who can't defend themselves, for those who prostrate and bow to You, for the elderly. ya Rabbi ya Allah. Ya Rabbi, send the one, the Sultan who will destroy satan's sultanate and punish tyrants and raise the flag of Islam. Those who are against him are under my feet.

Fatiha.
