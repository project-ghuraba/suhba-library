Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi Rasulillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Allah Azza wa Jalla’s names are plenty but He has 99 that are known most. His last name is As-Sabur, the Patient. The manifestation of these times is this name.

End Times have arrived. May Allah give us patience. Wildness, rebellion towards Allah, spilling blood, treading on Allah’s orders, and unbelief in what Allah says is everywhere. These are everywhere in the world now.

Why is Allah not punishing these? Because Allah’s As-Sabur name is being manifest. And when it is over, they will get what they deserve. Now, during these times, Allah (JJ) is treating people with the manifestation of His name As-Sabur.

We should not deplete it either. Do not oppress anybody and do not be unfair towards anybody. Do not think there is no hereafter because Allah has patience. If you do not get punished in this world, you will get punished in the hereafter. Fear Allah and obey Allah’s orders. May Allah be content with you.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq.

Al-Fatiha.
