As-Salamu Alaikum wa Rahmatullahu wa Barakatuhu. Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fil jamia'.

They say "every future is near". Days are passing by quickly. Because you have numbered days all of a sudden many years pass by- 1 year, 10 year, 20 year. You see that it has passed by all of a sudden. Therefore who did bad things in this life is mistaken. He has deceived himself, he has fallen into a big mistake. Because how long can he live? Man can live 100 years max. In the blink of an eye, what he gained from people by cheating them, oppressing them, by not showing them mercy is not considered as benefit, all is loss. Because real trade is the one for akhirah as we said. Dunya is temporary. Dunya passes by in the blink of an eye.

Prophet (sas) says in his Hadith. He (sas) asked "Who is the muflis (bankrupt)?" And the Companions said "who fell into dept & went bankrupt. He has no money left, he can't pay his debt so it means he is bankrupt". Prophet (sas) said, "No, it is not like this. Who did bad things in dunya- who stole, cheated people & worse than this, who killed people, this is the real bankrupt." Because in akhirah if he has thawabs/rewards.. Even if he has little reward he will give here there...Those he oppressed will take from his rewards. And how many rewards can such a man have? When he runs out of rewards, then he loads up the sins of those he oppressed. He will go further down in the depths of hell. This is the real bankruptcy.

Money is no good in akhirah. Gold is not valid, silver is not valid, nothing is valid. They are only valid in dunya. If you spend it in a good way, in the way of Allah, it is very much valid but once you die & get buried under the ground it has no benefit for man anymore. Therefore when you leave your wealth after you die, they run after legal fights for it's right. Then when they take it, again they spend it all. Meaning, nothing is really spent & sent for you to akhirah. That is only if you, yourself send it when you are alive, then it is sent. This is an important thing.

Dunya is like this...They said "summer has arrived". This long summer time has passed by now. We are entering the Fall. In a little while-in 10 days, starts the Hajj season/ashar al-hurum, 3 months. And you will see that in a little while comes Ramadan again. It is said that night & day is like a saw, black & white. You see it cutting. What is the saw cutting? It is cutting your life. Cutting, cutting.. Finally it reaches a point & it is finished there. It breaks the two & you deliver your soul. Therefore don't be cheated by dunya. How to say? Do for Allah, do everything for Allah. Who does for Allah.. Even if he is a laborer, if he says "I do for pleasing Allah", then all that work he does is for Allah. It is that he is earning the rizq for his family, it is for Allah. But the one who goes around saying "I am Hoja" & sell his religion for money he is losing. It means he is not doing those things for Allah but for money. If he says or does something for his benefit, even if it is 1 cent definitely he is at loss. He is under big responsibility. They should know these things better. They studied, read more than us, know better than us but it is Allah's wisdom. May Allah not make anyone stray from the right path, not be left to his ego. It is really a difficult thing to deal with the ego.

Today we speak about life. It is, if somebody live long, maybe 100 year. Very few people live 100 year. Most of them die before. In this life the people, they thought "we are living forever". They collecting. They try to make not looking what is good, what is bad. They doing every- thing, they not thinking for this. But life going very fast- Day & night, day & night and after, 1 year, 10 year, 50 year going like this. At the end they will look & they have nothing.

Prophet (sas) saying "who is bankrupt?" Sahaba, Companion said; The bankrupt one has debt & he finish his money. He cannot pay anymore. This is bankrupt". He said "No, not this. Bankrupt is who has many sins in his world life. He cheat people, he steal from people, he make wrong for people- he take money or sometimes maybe he kill people. This is the real bankrupt." Because this life is very short. For next life is long & they will give everything he do. He will ask for this-what he do. And when he doing something, maybe he has little bit good thing. But in akhirah, next world, they will ask "this man, you cheat him, give him this good thing for this. Other one, give little bit. Already he has nothing, good thing he do. So when he has nothing they will put since this people, he was cheating them or he make bad thing for them. He will carry this, all their sins on his shoulders. Because he has nothing to do good thing. So this people their sin, Allah take him from them & give for him. So he is real bankrupt because forever this life, going to hell.

So you must be careful. Everything, every breath is counted. You must not lose it in bad ways. First of all you must make intention to do everything for Allah. Even you are ignorant or you are not doing anything, but if you know.. You are simple worker, also you are better than who is calling himself a scholar. Because this, he is eating from his hand work. But who is claiming he is scholar or he is knowing everything, sometimes for money they are doing everything. They can say anything. They can go to everything. They say "we are doing this for Islam" but no, they are doing this for their pocket. And this is coming more bankrupt for them in next life. Because they know better than me and then 100x, because they are scholar. They know, they are reading, they have thought before.

So no need to be scholar & following your ego. It is better to be ignorant, to know nothing- only pray & make good thing, intending for Allah. You live for Allah it is 100x better than to be scholar or to be, it is big responsibility. When you are, people following you or you can give lecture in TV or in conference or something like this, so you must be especially careful in your private life also. Don't follow your ego. It is real difficult thing, it is not easy. Allah make it easy for us insha Allah.

Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, Al Fatiha.
