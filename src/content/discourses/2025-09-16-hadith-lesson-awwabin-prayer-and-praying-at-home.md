As-Salāmu ‘Alaykum wa RaḥmatuLlāhi wa Barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu BiLlāhi Minash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. BismiLlāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm.

Wa ṣ-Salātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Akhirīn.

Madad yā RasūlAllāh, Madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi RasūliLlāh, Madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

Dastūr Mawlana Sheikh Abdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistāni, Sheikh Muḥammad

Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad.

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “If anyone prays six rak'as after the Maghrib prayer, not saying anything evil during them, they will be treated for him as equivalent to twelve years’ worship.” After completing the Sunnah prayer of Maghrib, one prays the Awwābīn prayer, six rak’ahs. These six rak’ahs are equivalent to the reward of twelve years. The reward is so great. It is a virtuous prayer.

2- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Whoever prays the prayers between Maghrib and Isha, then these are undoubtedly the prayers of the Awwābīn, those who turn to Allāh.” They are considered among their rank. The rank of Awwābīn is a high rank among Muslims. Those six rak’ahs between Maghrib and Isha, whether more or less, however much you can pray, are considered the Awwābīn prayer.

3- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

ﺔ ﻨﳉا ﰱ ﺎﺘ   ﷲا ﲎﺑ ،ﺔﻌﻛر ﻦﻳﴩﻋ ءﺎﺸاو بﺮﻐا ﲔﺑ ﲆﺻ 

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Whoever prays twenty Rak’ah between Maghrib and ‘Isha’, Allāh will build for him a house in Paradise.” During Khalwah (seclusion) we pray twenty rak’ahs. If one wants, they can also pray the 20 rak’ahs as the Awwabin prayer in the Partial Khalwah.

4- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says: ﺔ  ﲔﺴﲬ بذ ﺎﲠ  ﻏ ﻜﻳ نأ ﻞ بﺮﻐا ﺪ تﺎﻌﻛر ﺖﺳ ﲆﺻ 

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Whoever prays six rak’ahs of voluntary prayer after Maghrib prayer before speaking to anyone, their sins of 50 years will be forgiven,” says our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Our sins are many. Therefore, these prayers are necessary for every Muslim in every respect. No one is sinless. Therefore, one should pray these six rak’ahs as much as they can.

5- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

ر ﺎا  ةءاﺮﺑ  ﺐﺘﻛ ﺔﻜﺋﻼاو ﷲا ﻻإ هاﺮﻳ ﻻ ءﻼﺧ ﰱ ﻦﻌﻛر ﲆﺻ 

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Whoever prays two rak’ahs of voluntary prayer in a place where no one sees him except Allāh and His angels will be written salvation from Hell.” If he prays in a secluded place saying, “Only Allāh and His angels see me”, he will be saved from Hell by the permission of Allāh.

6- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

ُ: ُ Q َ ﱢ َ ُ َ

ﺲ ﻤا ﻊﻠ ﺎﻣ ﺪ ﲈﻠﺼﻓ ا ﱴﻌﻛر ﻞﺼﻳ  

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Whoever did not pray the two Rak’ah before Fajr (the sunnah) then let him pray them after the sun has risen.” If he didn’t pray them before sunrise, then he should certainly pray them after the sun has risen.

7- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Do not abandon the two rak’ahs of Fajr prayer even if you are driven away by the horses.” Pray them, he says. Fajr prayer is one of the most important prayers. It is also mentioned in Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n. He says, “Do not abandon it.”

8- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

 ا ﲈﻓ نﺈﻓ ا ةﻼﺻ ﻞ ﲔا ﻦﻌﻛا اﺪﺗ ﻻ

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Do not leave the two rak’ahs before Fajr prayer, for there is much reward in them.” The sunnah of Fajr prayer is a very sunnah mu’akkadah. Do not neglect it. Certainly perform it, says our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam.

9- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Only those who seek refuge and turn to Allah most, often continue to perform the two rak’ahs sunnah of Fajr prayer.” Shukr to Allāh, Muslims, the people of ṭarīqah don’t leave any Sunnah prayers. Especially this Fajr Sunnah, of course, everyone performs it. But of course, what other people do is up to them. But this is a prayer that our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam emphasized and explained. May no one never abandon it, in shā’a Llāh.

10- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

ﻦ ﻤ َ ﻳ ْ ﻷ َ ْ ا َ ﻪﺒ ْ َ  َ َ ﻊ ْ ﺠ َ  ْ Q  َ ْ ﻓ َ,  Q ا ةﻼ َ ﺻ َ ﻞ َ  ْ َ ﻦ ْ َ ﻌ َ ْ ﻛ ﱠ ا َ  ْ ُ  ُ َ أ َ ﲆ ﱠ ﺻ َ اذ َ إ

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “If any of you prays the two Rak’at before Fajr prayer, i.e the sunnah, he should lie on his right side.” We do this; it is a Sunnah. Many people get surprised when they see us. During Fajr prayer, when in mosques, etc., they ask, “What are you doing? What are they doing?” They look at us strangely, asking, “What are these?” This is a Sunnah. A Sunnah of our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Most people have either never heard of this Sunnah or forgotten it.

11- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Perform some of your prayer in your houses, and do not turn them into graves.” If one doesn’t pray at home, it becomes like a cemetery, like a grave. So pray at home as well. Not the Fard prayers. One should pray the Fard prayers in the mosque if he can. But perform the Sunnah prayers and the Nafl prayers at home, the Shukr prayer, the Duha prayer, the Ishraq prayer, whatever it is. One should definitely perform a prayer at home. Our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says, “So that your house doesn’t become like a grave.”

12- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says: ﷲ َ ﱠ ا ن ﱠ ﺈﻓ ،ﻪ ِ ﺗ ِ ﻼﺻ َ ِ ﺎﺒ ً ﯿ ِ  َ Q ﻪ ِ ﺘ ِ ﯿ ْ  َ ِ ﻞ ْ ﻌ َ ﺠ ْ  َ ْ ﻓ َ ،ه ِ  ِ ِ  ْ  َ ﰱ ة َ ﻼ ﱠ ا  ُ  ُ َ أ ﴣ َ  َ: اذإ

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “When one of you performs the prayer in the mosque he should leave a portion of his prayer for his house, for Allāh Almighty blesses his house because of his prayer.” The Nafl prayers performed at home bring goodness to the house, in shā’a Llāh.

13- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “The most excellent and virtuous prayer of a person is (that which he prays) in his house except that which is prescribed (the five congregational prayers).” Meaning the tahajjud prayers, Awwābīn prayer, ishraq prayer, duha prayer, shukr prayer, and the tasābih prayer, are better to be performed at home. Except the fard prayer. The fard prayer is to be performed in the mosque, not at home; it will be a prayer with 27 times more reward.

The Messenger of Allāh says the truth in what he said or as he said.
