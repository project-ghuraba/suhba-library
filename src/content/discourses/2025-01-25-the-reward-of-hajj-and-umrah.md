A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla has holy places, and HeNone has holy times. HeNone granted them to the Ummah of MuḥammadNone and to mankind so that they may benefit from them. HeNone has given these beautiful things for both the body and the soul. It may be a hardship for the body, but it is relief and beauty for the soul. And it is beneficial for the body as well. These acts of worship: prayer, fasting, ḥajj. These are ḥajj and ‘umrah. The time for ḥajj is, of course, once a year. In the past, not everyone could go to ḥajj. It was difficult. Its difficulty was different, and the difficulty of this time is different. So not everyone is destined to go.

There may be an obstacle for various reasons. It’s the wisdom of AllāhNone. We cannot question it. In the past, people didn’t go just for ‘umrah. They would go for ḥajj, and then perform ‘umrah after ḥajj. They would only do it once in their lives. But now they can. They can just go. It has become easier to go for ‘umrah at any time. Anyone who wants can go. But for ḥajj, what is important now is to perform ḥajj before ‘umrah. They say, “We’re waiting for our turn. It’s not coming. Let’s do ‘umrah until our turn comes.” If you spend the money you saved for ḥajj on ‘umrah and then your turn for ḥajj comes and you cannot go, this is wrong. But if you have set aside a sufficient amount of money for ḥajj, then you can perform ‘umrah.

Later, whenever ḥajj is granted to you, that intention is already in place. Every year, with the permission of AllāhNone, that reward is written for you. Because every year – we know of people who have waited for fourteen years for ḥajj. They get hopeful every year, “We’ll go this year.” They make the intention with that hope. If they do not go, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla does not leave them empty-handed. They attain that reward. The second year is the same. Until they are granted ḥajj. As we said, without wasting that money, set it aside. After that, if one has the means, they can go for ‘umrah so that they can see. They will have visited our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam and seen the Ka‘bah. Because a person has never seen them in their life.

So, it is good to see them, to take from their virtues. Because every deed you do near the Ka‘bah has the reward of a hundred thousand. Each rak‘ah has the reward of a hundred thousand raka‘āt; each prayer has the reward of a hundred thousand prayers. A grant. All of them will be beneficial to those people. But if someone goes for ‘umrah now, and then the following year or later, ḥajj is granted but they couldn’t go, at that time, they lose a great reward. So today, thanks to AllāhNone, our ḥajjis are going for ‘umrah. Our sister RukiyeHanim – your Ḥājjah Anne, or Sultan Anne – Ḥajjī Mehmet and Shaykh Bahauddin Efendi, they were destined to go for ‘umrah, thanks to AllāhNone. When AllāhNone destines it, it’s great. Places like these are where people benefit. The real benefit is in going there, nowhere else. “I went to London, I went to Paris,” I don’t know where, “I went here and there.” They have no benefit. When done for dunyā, it is of no use. These beautiful places, for ākhirah, are the beneficial places.

May AllāhNone grant us this. May AllāhNone accept it. In shā’a Llāh, may their blessings and rewards be written for us as well, for those here and for those listening, in shā’a Llāh. May AllāhNone grant ḥajj many times to those who haven’t been to ḥajj, in shā’a Llāh. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone gave us place and time, this blessed time, blessed place for human being, not only for Muslim, for all of them. Everybody can take, nobody prevent them to do this and to get blessed. And there is many thing AllāhNone gave, some, this for, sometimes difficult for body but good for spiritual life, and spiritual life is more important. But if you are, you don't happy with the spiritual, your body will never be happy, never be good for anything. But also, for body also, give happiness. If even it is difficult …

Many people they said to pray five time it is difficult. To fast it is difficult. But all this giving benefit. Especially for ḥajj it's more difficult. But people when they finish, they are very tired, but they are very good in spirituality because AllāhNone give them from HisNone Divine Presence, present for them, for their soul, for their spirituality. They will be, their maqām higher and they will be more stronger. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, this is gift from AllāhNone. This is not dry, our religion. All alive, giving life for everything. And now …

Olden time, for ḥajj there was difficulty. Now also, there is difficulty. But what happen, that time, difficulty to go: it's too dangerous or very expensive. Very few people they can afford. This time, different difficulty. You cannot find visa, cannot, some, many thing. This is from wisdom of AllāhNone. So, people asking for, if we are not going to ḥajj, we cannot go ḥajj, no permission from anything. Can we go to ‘umrah? For ‘umrah, we can go, but if you make ḥajj, okay, go for ‘umrah. If you waiting for ḥajj, you must keep your money for ḥajj beside. This money for ḥajj, not for ‘umrah, only for ‘umrah, because... But if you have extra money for ḥajj you put and after you can find money for ‘umrah, you can go. Because many people they are never been there, not visiting Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, not seeing Ka‘bah, which it is, everything in Ka‘bah you do, from praying, from taṣaddaq (giving ṣadaqah), from dhikr, Qur’ān, hundred thousand time for each one, what you do.

If you do in your country you have one, there is hundred thousand in Ka‘bah. And Madīnah also, thousand time. So, it is a must, if can afford this, they can go. But the most important to be for ḥajj. But nowadays they have many people they write for, queuing for hajj, but every year not coming for them. They looking, no, “Our name not here. Oh, we are sorry.” Don't be sorry, because each one, because you have intention, every year Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, for intention, HeNone make you, giving you reward as ḥajj, in shā’a Llāh. This is sure, and at the end, in shā’a Llāh, we know some people, ten years waiting. Some of them twelve, some thirteen. I know this year, there was one waiting fourteen years and this year he get this ḥajj, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone bless them. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, today Ḥajjī, Shaykh Bahā’u d-dīn, Ḥājjah Sulṭān Ruqayyah and Ḥajjī Mehmet Nāẓim, and their family, they are going, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, for ‘umrah, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone make for each one, anyone he like to go, AllāhNone make it easy for him and help him too, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
