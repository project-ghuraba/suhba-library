As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh. A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah. A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Mā yaftaḥi Llāhu li-n-nāsi min raḥmatin fa-lā mumsika lahā wa-mā yumsik fa-lā mursila lah” (35:2). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘Aẓīm. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says, when Allāh sends His mercy, no one can prevent it or withhold it. Mercy is all the mercy we see. Rain is also called mercy. This is the mercy of Allāh to people, to earth, to every place. It has not rained for months; not just here but everywhere. There’s no rain. Make it rain, let’s see. You say you’ve developed all this technology and that you know so much. Make it rain; you never will. When He withholds it, no one can give His mercy. Nobody can give that mercy. Allāh says, and our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam also states in a ḥadīth, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla created this world. He provided for all its needs. With Allāh’s wisdom, it is not according to the very knowledgeable people, but Allāh created it and provided for its needs. He provided for everything our planet earth needs. Our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says that during every twenty-four hours, there is certainly rain without stop somewhere on earth. It rains wherever Allāh has destined it, wherever Allāh wills. As we said, these people sometimes think they’re smart: “It evaporated, and then it rains.” It’s true. It evaporates, turns into clouds, and then it rains. But it happens wherever Allāh wants, as He wills. Dunyā has its kismet also. Rain occurs somewhere on earth, in every twenty-four hours. But it doesn’t fall where you want it to. Some places become bone dry. Other places sink. They submerge with the flood and the rain. This demonstrates the power of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Those with īmān believe. Those who don’t have īmān say, “There was this, there was that.” Yet, what should we do to attain the mercy of Allāh? We must obey Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. We must ask for the mercy of Allāh. You must make du‘ā’ so that Allāh will send His mercy upon you. And how is the du‘ā’ accepted? Every du‘ā’, as our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says, every du‘ā’ is either accepted or not. But if you recite ṣalawāt upon our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, then it is accepted. You should recite ṣalawāt at the beginning of your du‘ā’ and at the end of your du‘ā’, sending ṣalawāt upon our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Since ṣalawāt is accepted, what is between it will be accepted too. Ṣalawāt is acceptable in the sight of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Then, your du‘ā’ becomes an accepted du‘ā’. Now you see they make du‘ā’s for rain. There are those who recite ṣalawāt also. But in some places, they do not realise the greatness and honour of our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam in the sight of Allāh. They say, “He is like us.” That is why they perform rain prayers and rain du‘ā’s without reciting ṣalawāt. After that, they say, “We prayed many times, it didn’t rain.” Of course, it won’t. If you don’t say, “For his honoured face,” it won’t happen. Even when there was a drought, when our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam was a child, when they said, “For the sake of his face,” the whole desert became green from the rain. But when you do not say so, when you do not believe in him, then take your drought. Allāh makes it rain in the middle of the sea. You stand there with your mouth open and wait. No rain. He makes it rain in one place until it sinks. On the other side, nothing. This is the power of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, the majesty of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. He does whatever He wills, and no one can make what He does not will. Neither technology nor anything else makes it rain. Therefore, when this mercy comes, it is Allāh’s favour and grace. We should rejoice in it. We must give shukr to Allāh so that He makes it lasting. Because when we are grateful, blessings increase and become lasting. Without shukr – now most people are not grateful at all. No one gives shukr; many complain. They do not like anything. They do not value the blessings they are given. Then they ask for something. Are you going to defy Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla? If you want to, then defy Him as much as you want. No one suffers any harm but you. May Allāh protect us. May Allāh make His blessings lasting. Indeed, for the past year or two, our situation has been very bad; people have been bad, even spiritually. That is why this mercy is delayed. Therefore we must repent and seek forgiveness. We must beg Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla to increase these blessings and make them lasting, in shā’a Llāh. This is not easy. Because, “Wa-ja‘alnā mina l-mā’i kulla shay’in ḥayy” (21:30). Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says, “We created every living creature from water.” All these creatures cannot be without water. Water is life. Life is a blessing given by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Therefore let us give shukr to Allāh. May Allāh increase it, in shā’a Llāh. May He forgive us. We are all sinners. In shā’a Llāh, let us repent and seek forgiveness, and ask Allāh for mercy, in shā’a Llāh. May He send His mercy, in shā’a Llāh. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, saying in Qur’ān: Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Mā yaftaḥi Llāhu li-n-nāsi min raḥmatin fa-lā mumsika lahā wa-mā yumsik fa-lā mursila lah” (35:2). Meaning, Allāh, when He send His bless, His mercy, nobody can stop it. Mercy and bless coming from Allāh. We call, we... in Turkish they said for rain, “rahmet”, old people. Now, people they don’t know this. But old people, Mawlānā was saying, “O rahmet yağdı,” meaning rain falling down. And this is from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. When He send, nobody can stop it. Nobody. When rain coming, you cannot stop. Because this coming from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, and this is mercy and bless from Allāh. And of course, we are, since long time, maybe two years, it is, rain it is very few, everywhere drought, everywhere dry. So, this is mercy and bless, raḥmah from Allāh. And when He send, nobody can stop it. And if He – “Wa-mā yumsik fa-lā mursila lah” (35:2). If He stop it, nobody can send it. He said, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Of course, they try to make rain, to make this, to make that. It is... they cannot do. They have everything, technology, they have many thing. But for, to make rain coming and raining, cannot be without order of Allāh. So this is very good example for human being, for, for atheist, for who not be, they are not believe in religion, not believe in mightiness of Allāh. And also for this people who are, they are not believe – they are Muslim, but they are not respect as much they must respect for Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Because, with bless of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, Allāh send His rain, His bless, His good thing for human being. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, He create our earth, this planet. And He put everything it is in need in this: water, air, oxygen, or anything they want, they are in need in this planet, He put in this planet. So he is, continue, as Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla order for them. So Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying in twenty-four hour, it must be rain in this planet. Continuously, twenty-four hour, there is rain. As where in some place, some other place, but this rain coming for... And this... Of course, people, the scientist people, they are saying rain, how it’s coming. Come from sea, from water. It is become cloud and raining. It is okay, no problem for this. This is also... But this also in, from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla to make this circulation, He make this. But important here, to know it is also twenty-four hour raining anywhere in planet. But where it will be rain, when it will be rain, this is what Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying. “You rain this country. You rain this city. You rain this city.” Many time we see cloud coming over us. No, not raining. Just passing through and going, raining maybe sea, middle of sea. Middle of sea. After people there is drought, there is dry, there is this, there is that. So this is from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla’s wisdom. Because, people, they forget Allāh. Before they was, when its not raining, going whole city outside and praying, asking for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. And make ṣalawāt for Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Because every du‘ā’, every praying they said, not accept. Only ṣalāt ‘alá n-Nabiyy, “Allāhumma ṣalli ‘alá Sayyidinā Muḥammad” – this is accept. So with this first make ṣalawāt, and after make du‘ā’ and after again make ṣalawāt to be accepted, in shā’a Llāh. This is what people, nowa[days] people, new people, they are claiming they are Muslim. They make Islām as monster. It will be kill everybody. Only they will be safe. For this, Allāh also curse them, not giving rain. Because we hear, this people last year, also they went twice making, praying, asking for rain. But of course without respecting Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, nothing, and nothing happen with this. It is very important to ask for his holy face, of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. “Yustasqá l-ghamāmu bi-wajhihi l-karīm,” they said. From his childhood, his uncle, when was a draught, they take him and ask, make du‘ā’ and ask Allāh, “For this boy, blessed boy, Allāh send us rain.” And it was raining, whole desert it was green, that time. Yes, very important, water. Everything Allāh create from water. So this, we must thank Allāh for this, because with thanking, with shukr, it is coming more and continue. “Bi-sh-shukri tadūmu n-ni‘am.” When you are thanking for Allāh, your, what you have from good thing, it continue and growing more and more. If not, you don’t have shukr, you don’t have thank for Allāh, it is going less and less. And nowadays, people, they are very few people they are thankful for Allāh. All time complaining, not happy with anything in this life. Allāh make us to be happy, and to be thankful all time, in shā’a Llāh. Allāh bless you. Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
