As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
قُل لَّن يُصِيبَنَآ إِلَّا مَا كَتَبَ ٱللَّهُ لَنَا هُوَ مَوْلَىٰنَا
<br>“Qul lan yuṣībanā illā mā katabAllahu lanā”<br>
“Say: 'Nothing shall ever happen to us except what Allah has ordained for us.'” (Qur’ān 9:51). ṢadaqAllahu l-ʿAẓīm.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla is saying, "Nothing will happen to us except what is written by Allah, what is predestined to happen to us." Therefore, current situation when people are worried, "What will happen? What will be?" Everything will be as Allah likes. It doesn't happen otherwise. Nothing happens that Allah doesn't want. Therefore, you must trust in Allah.

There is nowhere to run.

فَفِرُّوٓا۟ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ
<br>“Fa-firrū ilAllah”<br>
“So flee to Allah.” (Qur’ān 51:50).

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says what you should do is run towards Allah, seek refuge in Allah. There is nowhere else to find shelter.

The situation in this world is all blurry. It is unclear what may happen. People are worried not knowing what to do. Don't worry. Who is with Allah doesn't worry. We came from Allah, to Allah we'll return. Nothing else. Others don't know where they came from. They don't know where they'll go. Let them worry. We don't worry with Allah's permission.

As much rizq as Allah gives us, wherever it is, that's with His permission. Our duty is to trust Him and to keep on serving in our work, our worship, our family, our environment and people. Things we do to them should be for Allah's pleasure so that they don't go in vain. Neither suffering, nor goodness goes in vain then. Everything is beautiful for believers near Allah says our Holy Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. It's profitable when it's good. And it's profitable when it's tough. There is nothing that goes in vain or to the worse.

The most important thing is to seek refuge in Allah, to rely on Allah. What we must do is trust in Him. It is one of the conditions of īmān. "Khayruhu wa-sharruhu min Allahu Ta‘ālā". Everything is from Allah. We must believe that both goodness and badness is from Allah. We will be rewarded then.

Otherwise, people have no īmān now. As if they are doing something good, they say "I don't believe." Suffer if you don't. You will suffer here, and you will suffer more in ākhirah. New models appear every time. Children believe their friends. But they don't believe their fathers, grandfathers, ḥojjas. And then they don't know what to do. Therefore, don't go for a bad way. Don't think badly.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla has appointed everyone's rizq, their everything, time they will live. He has said that. So it will happen. Therefore, don't obey your ego. And guard your ego. Guard yourself from badness, from ḥarām, from bad places and everything. Trust in Allah.

World's situation is clear. There is nothing like this world will recover. It's getting worse day by day. Our Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says, "The best time is my time. Then comes the time of ṣaḥābahs and so on. 2-3 years after me, it will get worse day by day," says our Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. And now it is even faster, even worse every day.

Therefore, don't hope to have comfort in this world. Your hope should be ākhirah. Then Allah will give you comfort here and hereafter. May Allah protect all of us inshā’Allah. Inshā’Allah good days will come too. Days of Mahdī ʿalayhi s-salām announced by our Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam will be a short time like Asr-i Sa'adah. And then it will be over too. Nothing is permanent in this world. Permanence is in ākhirah. Constancy is in ākhirah. Everything is interim in dunyā. It is temporary.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
