As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

1 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

There are different types of ṣadaqah. Ṣadaqah is not just with money but with different other things. Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam says, “A smile in the face of your Muslim brother is a charity for you.” So smiling in your Muslim brother’s face, looking at him well and making him happy is charity. 

“It is a charity for you to enjoin what is good and forbid what is evil.” So showing what’s good, saying, “This one is good, that one is bad. Give up this bad habit. Give up your bad deeds” is also charity. Reward of ṣadaqah will be written to you. 

“To direct someone who has lost his way; helping and assisting is a charity for you.” So when people ask for some street address, “Where is this? Which road is it? Where does this road go? I want this address.” Showing the right path is charity for you. Some foolish people show those people a different path instead of the right path as a joke, but it is not a joke. Therefore, if you know the street they’re asking about, show them the way. If you don’t know, say, “I don’t know, ask someone else.” 

“Removing things such as stones, thorns and bones from the road that may prevent people from passing by is a charity for you.” Keeping the road clean is charity for you. Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam says that removing a stone or a bone or thorn aside is considered charity. 

“Pouring water from your bucket into your Muslim brother’s who’s in need is a charity for you.” So, if your brother’s bucket is empty and you bring water, you help him and give him water it is charity for you.

2 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Making a deaf person,” that is, one hard of hearing, “hear, is a charity.” If you speak loud enough for him to hear, and you keep saying it until he hears it, it’s charity. But if he is completely deaf, trying to make him understand as much as you can with signs is considered as ṣadaqah.

3 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

Our Holy Prophet is saying “On every Muslim there is enjoined ṣadaqah.” So he has to give ṣadaqah daily. Because mankind has 360 joints, a Muslim has to give ṣadaqah upon each. There mustn’t be something as a Muslim who doesn’t give ṣadaqah. 

“If he doesn’t find anything, he works with his hand.” So one must set aside money for charity. If he doesn’t have money or is poor, he should work with his hands, it becomes charity for him. 

“It will be beneficial for him and he will give charity.” So, he works, gets paid, and it becomes a charity for him. 

“If he can’t find a job or work anything, he should help someone in need.” So if he can’t find any job or earn money, he should help someone. It is also considered charity. 

“If he can’t do this, he should enjoin what is good.” Command people to do whatever is good and guide people by saying “This is good, if you do this, it’s good.” 

“If he cannot do this, then he should refrain from doing evil, which is a charity for him.” So, if you couldn’t do them all, at least don’t do evil, it will be considered a charity for you.

4 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

Our Holy Prophet says, “For an unchaste woman,” of course, may Allah help us, may Allah protect us from falling into such states. Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam says, “Forgiveness was granted to an unchaste woman who coming upon a dog panting and almost dead with thirst at the mouth of a well, took off her shoe, tied it with her head-covering, and drew some water for it. On that account she was forgiven.” So this bad thing she did: adultery is one of the major sins. But Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla has forgiven her by this means, by making a charity for an animal.

5 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

Our Holy Prophet says, “A human being has three hundred and sixty joints,” There are 360 joints in the human body: in the fingers, on the back, on the neck, everywhere. They call it a joint. Each one separately. 

“For each of which he must give alms daily.” So a person has to give 360 ṣadaqahs. 

“It may be mucus in the mosque which you bury, and something which you remove from the road; these are ṣadaqah.” Of course, in the past, the mosque was all soil, and since they did not have much knowledge of adab and good behavior at that time, they sometimes spit and expelled phlegm. Covering it is charity. Removing the stones, soil, thorns, wood and garbage from the road is also considered charity. These are always counted among those 360 ṣadaqahs. 

“But if you do not find such, two rak’ahs in the forenoon (Duha prayer) will suffice you,” that is, for the of 360 ṣadaqahs. So, if you don’t do any of these, performing two rakats of Duha prayer will replace these 360 ṣadaqahs. You should definitely perform it.

6 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “There is a reward for serving any animate. Watering a thirsty animal is charity for you.” So watering the thirsty liver (living creature) is charity.

7 - Qala Rasulullah ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam [The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) says]:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “A reward is given in connection with every living creature. Watering every thirsty living creature is a charity for you.” So, even if a snake comes to the water, it is not okay to kill it. So it also has liver and is thirsty. If you see it outside, it’s okay, but if it’s in the water, or if it comes to the water, or if it comes to drink water, you mustn’t do anything (kill it).

The Messenger of Allah says the truth in what he said or as he said.

Ziyadatan ila sharafi’ ‘n-Nabi, sallaLlahu `alayhi wa sallam wa Alihi wa Asahabihi l-kiram, wa ila arwahi jamee’i al Anbiya’i wa l-Mursalin wa Khudama’i sharai’ihim wa ila arwahi al ‘Aimmati al arba’a, wa ila arwahi Mashayyikhina fi t-Tariqati n-Naqshbandiyyati l-Aliyyah, khassatan Imamu t-ṭarīqah wa Ghawthu l-Khaliqah Khwaja Bahauddin Muḥammad al-Uwaisiyi l-Bukhari, Sayyidina Abdul Khaliq al Ghujduwani, Mawlana Shaykh Sharafuddin al Daghestani, Mawlana Shaykh Abdullah al Faiz al Daghestani, Mawlana Shaykh Muḥammad Nazim Adil al Haqqani, wa sai’ri Sadatina wa s-Siddiqiyun, wa man nahnu fi hadratihim wa jiwarihim, for the souls of all our past ones, for the pleasure of Allah, for the souls of all Shaheeds. Li-Llahi Ta’ala, Al-Fātiḥah.
