As-Salamu Alaikum. Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fil jamia' This, what Shaykh Effendi is saying all the time- be happy, don't be sad. Already everything was khayr/good for us but from now on, goodness is for us, badness for them. Mawlana spoke of this matter how many years ago, and really since then always goodness is coming on Muslims, with Allah's permission. No evil is touching those who are on Allah's Way, on His right way. They are under protection.

And there is also another meaning to this which is, no matter what happens even a person gets ill, or was martyred, all is good for him. There is no evil/badness for who is on the right way. Especially those who are on Tariqat way, following Shaykhs, all khayr come on them. Many people, as the Prophet(sas) said, are deceived by two things: good health & free time. When he is in good health, he doesn't care about his health much, thinking it will continue like this forever. However he has to be careful about it.

And free time also.. Some people say "how to pass my time" & some from the common people say even worse, they want to "kill their time". So that it passes by. However they will regret very much later on for this time spent in vain.

But when one is in Tariqat, of course he remembers Allah all the time, intending to please Allah all the time. For this, it also becomes khayr for him. At that time, he is not idle doing nothing. When he wakes up in the morning, makes niyah/intention "I will spend today for pleasing Allah" or as Mawlana says "I make niyah to destroy kufr/unbelief". By this intention, khayr comes on him, & he gets thawab.

He should be careful about his health, should stay away from things that may be harmful for his health. And if we were to speak about these things here, time would not suffice- like smoking, tiredness.. Meaning a person knows when he harms his health. He should be careful about this also. Allah entrusted us with our bodies. He also granted us good health, the biggest blessing. If you let this good health slip through your hands, the only harm is for yourself. Don't complain after this "it happened like this, like that.."

Khayr for us. Bad for who don't believe in Allah, who are on kufr/unbelief. He can have all the wealth in dunya, all the income, it is no benefit for him. It is not for his good, but for his bad. Goodness is for us, with Allah's permission. Always keep this in your mind & thank Allah. Today we speak; Mawlana Shaykh, every time he say khayr, meaning good for us the bad for unbeliever. Before was, sometimes good for unbeliever also, but now all for believer, good/khayr. From Allah, gift from Allah. Good, how good? Sometimes people, they are miserable. Who is following Allah's order, Prophet's (as) order & Tariqat, he is in safe. Even something happen to him, never mind. It is also gift from Allah. When Allah making some wrong.. Not wrong Astaghfirullah, you cannot say wrong. Some thing happen to, if he die by accident or something happen to him, even this also, big khayr, big gift from Allah. Allah, He will reward him. He will be happy with this.

For this awliya; they not speak, only truth they speak. What they say, they are Haqq, real, what they said. For this every time he is saying, "good for us, bad & worse for unbeliever." Especially these days. Even this unbeliever, he has everything- cars, house, income.. What he imagine he has, also not good for him. When getting this, getting more miserable for him, getting more worse for him.

This is good, good tiding from awliya, for us. Everytime saying this & Alhamdulillah real also. Muslim become more understanding about why they are here, living & why they are doing things, especially Tariqat people. Other people, never mind, they will be... Because they are not believing in Mercy of Allah & Prophet Mercy, they only, they said "we praying & we doing fasting, it is enough for us. No need for Prophet, no need for awliya.." So they can. Allah, He will charge them with this & they will look if it is enough for them or not.

This is very important. Alhamdulillah, we must be thankful for this. Every time he remind us to be happy, don't be sad for what happened. You are in Tariqat especially. There is two thing people, Prophet (sas) he said, cheated by this, most of people; by time & by health. They thought their health, it will be like this every time. And not looking after themselves. They are making every badness for their body. After one day they become ill, they are becoming sad & complaining. Other thing, time. Time very important.

In Tariqat, from morning when you wake up he said, make intention, intend to "whole day for Allah we do this". Especially Mawlana Shaykh, "we intend to destroy unbelief. We want to make people to believe in Allah". This is his intention, all world it must be like this. This is intend, so time, when sometime make people who not in Tariqat, not in religious, they said: we are killing time, we are boring, so must do something to kill the time. Really they are killing time; this is good, what they said for this.

So Alhamdulillah, Mawlana, we are with him. He is showing us right way, and giving good tiding. You are everyday getting more benefit, Alhamdulillah.
