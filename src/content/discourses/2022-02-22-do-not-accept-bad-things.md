As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A’ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says when you see any oppression or a bad thing, if you dislike it and do not accept it, it is as if you are away from it. But even when away, if you approve of that bad thing, that oppression... if you accept it, you will be partners in that sin with them, says our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. They are ordering all kinds of evil in the world today. There is a condition that people don’t care about anymore, acting as if it is normal. They say, “It doesn’t concern me,” and go on with their lives. It is wrong. Even if you are far away, you should not accept it. If you accept it even from afar, that sin will pass on to you. And you will take your share from it.

Therefore, wherever you see bad things, from far away or up close, if there is a rebellion against AllāhNone, if it opposes AllāhNone, you should not accept it. These perverse ones are all over the world now. They are striving to make people accept it by force. If people accept it, they will be partners in that sin. They will get the punishment of AllāhNone, the anger of AllāhNone, ma’ādha Llāh. That is not an easy thing.

Lūṭ ‘alayhi s-salām said to his people, “The indecent and disgraceful acts you are doing cannot be done. No one has done it before you. You are both blocking the right way and showing perversion.” He admonished them. But it didn’t affect them. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla told Lūṭ ‘alayhi s-salām to leave them. HeNone said, “WeNone will destroy these people. All of you, go. You go. But your wife will stay.” Why? Because even if she wasn’t doing anything, she approved of what they had done. Because she said, “It doesn’t concern me. Let them do it, I am not doing it” she got that punishment as well. The fire came down from the sky onto them. Such diseases and all kinds of awful punishment came down onto them that they sank into the ground. And before sinking, they suffered from the great anger and punishment that came down to them. They died and disappeared.

We cannot accept because someone says so and so. We accept the words of AllāhNone. We don’t accept those who are against Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla and against human nature, whoever that is. It is not a normal thing. Not a human right, not some other right. There is the right of AllāhNone here, not human rights. The right of AllāhNone comes first. The rights of the perverse ones don’t come first. May AllāhNone help us, because they are slowly showing this as normal to people and letting their egos do what they want. May AllāhNone help us. May AllāhNone protect us. May Muslims be in the circle of protection – their families, children, and everyone, in shā’a Llāh.

Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying if you are near some doing bad thing and you are not accept, you will be not responsible for this. But if you far – not same place, maybe far, but they do this bad thing... they are, you say, “Okay I am not care, it is not for me,” you be part of this people and AllāhNone will punish, not happy with you.” This for these days because many thing happen in this world. Don’t say, “It is far, okay, never mind, we are safe from this.” No, everytime you must be awake and you remember not accept this bad thing what happened. Even it is very far from you, you must be saying, “No, this is against Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla and we are not accept this. This is a bad thing – for human being, for everybody.”

Good thing, what Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone order. Because nowadays, there this people who are not normal people, they are bad people. They try to control whole world. Like shayṭān, they are going from making pressure for every government, for people, “Give money” and I don’t know how. But now people, they are afraid from this people. You must afraid from AllāhNone, don’t afraid from them. If you are afraid from them, AllāhNone will punish you.

This what happen for Lūṭ ‘alayhi s-salām. His nation was not listening to him. Only he and his family was in right way. All other city, they was, they doing bad thing, not normal thing, sapık – perverted, perverted. All they was like this. They are not accept, and even angel coming by... like human being, they coming to make also bad thing with him. But Sayyidunā Lūṭ (as), he was afraid what will be, this our guests, “I will be ashamed from this people.” “Don’t worry, Sayyidunā Lūṭ (as),” angel telling Sayyidunā Lūṭ (as). And when they come there, they was first, they – their eye blow up and they was blind. And after this, AllāhNone order Sayyidunā Lūṭ (as), “Go out with your family, only leave your wife.” Why? Because she wasn’t do bad thing, but she was accept what they are doing. So, she also punished with this. And after when they went out, AllāhNone sent for them the heavy punishment. First, from sky something coming on them burning everything. They make them black. After, going, their city going, sank down in the earth, and nobody survive from them. It was terrible punishment, for – never happened like this before.

So Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, when HeNone is punishing, don’t be with this punished one. If you are not accept, you will be safe, in shā’a Llāh. Nowadays, everybody they doing this, and they thought they are strong. They are putting pressure on all people, nobody can say anything for them. But Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla not happy with them. And who is happy with them, he will be with them. For this we are not happy with them, we not accept them. Anybody can say anything but we are only accept what Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying. For this, don’t look, even when you are living any place, like normal for people doing this. We are, in shā’a Llāh make us to not accept. Protect us from this bad thing; our families and Muslim family all, in shā’a Llāh, from this bad people. AllāhNone take them away from us.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
