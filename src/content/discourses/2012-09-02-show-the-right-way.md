As-Salamu Alaikum.Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fil jamia’.

The Prophet (sas) says that whosoever shows people the right way - he is a means for a person to reach guidance, or teaches him something he doesn't know, or brings him from the wrong way to the right, whatever good deeds that person does the one who became a means for his guidance is given in the same rewards & thawabs. He does 1 good deed, then the one who led him to guidance gets 1 also. 2, 2. If he led 1000 people, then the thawabs of that 1000 are written for him. But Allah does not take the thawabs from that 1000 people & give that one. He (swt) grants the same thawabs but separately. Meaning, there is no need to take from him & give the other.

Everything is in the hand of Allah(awj), He is al Qadir al Muqtadir. He is the most Generous One of all. He (swt) does not say "I take the thawab from this one & give to that one". No. But for people, if they do business & hire a man if he sells & makes profit they give him from the share. If two men do the sale, then they divide that share by 2. If three people do this job, then they share the profit. But there is no such thing with Allah(awj). He grants everyone the same-without anyone's thawab lessening, the one who led to guidance gets also. This is for the people who show the beautiful way, who show the goodness, who forbid the evil.

There is also the opposite of this. Whosoever leads people to the bad way, that man gets the sin of each person whom he led to the bad way. This also is important. Because if man intends to do something good but doesn't do it, one thawab is written for him. If he does then 10, 100 is written for him in Allah's side. He grants as He likes. He intends to do something bad, of course only intention. When he doesn't do it, thawab is written for him. If he does, one sin is written for him. As we said if he intends but doesn't do bad, it is recorded as good deed for him but this thing is different.

If you lead a man to evil, show him the bad way, order him for evil then every evil he commits is written for yourself as well. If it is 1 person, sins of that one, if it is 10 people then the sins of that 10 are written for you. You will be judged for it on the Day of Judgement. Why do we say like this? Because there is "Amr bil Ma'ruf wa Nahyan al Munkar". It was like this in the time of the Prophet (sas),"but will be the opposite in the end of times", says he (sas).They forbid the good &command the evil. They command for the evil. That is why one gets that much sins. For 1-2, as many as he misled he doesn't get any less sins. Meaning, "doesn't matter how many men I mislead, I still get the same sin "No, it is more. Per man, for how many men sinned in the way you ordered them, or showed them, it comes to him, & to you-comes in multiples. If the misled one gets only one, you get as many sins as the number of people you misled.

You have to be very careful about this. Because the people of this time don't know what is good, what is bad. As we said, they made everything messed up. People think what is bad is good & what is good is bad. Therefore if you don't know, it is ok, just don't speak. We are saying, even the alcoholic beverages. In the old communist countries Turkish or Muslim people were drinking with Basmala, thinking it is thawab. They say there is no covering/hijab in Islam. They say "do this, don't do that" So everything is all messed up. Of course these ruling systems that imitate the Europeans are also very important. Drinking, if you don't drink they make you suffer in many ways. The real big sin is that one. The more people you make drink the more sins you get. Now they are being beaten with maces in hell.

But it is too late anymore. You have to be careful about this. Beware of not causing even one man to go to wrong way. You also get as much sin as he does. When you do good things, when you make people get used to good their thawabs come to you as Allah's grant.

Today we speak about, Prophet (sas) said "Who show people right way, every man he become to right way by your advice, you also take same reward from Allah. If you bring one man from bad way to right way one reward for you, one reward for him. If you bring second, you have two also-two reward. If you bring 10, 10 reward. 1000, 1000 reward for you. And this reward, Allah not taking from this man & give you. Give him alone, same for you. No need for Allah to bring, to take from people. He is very Generous & everything under His order. So He can give you as much, you cannot imagine what He can give.

Because in dunya, this world when they make business, maybe they-people take commission. Maybe one man taking if he bring something for commission he take maybe 10%. But maybe they are 2 partner. So everybody take 5%. Maybe 4 or 6, like this they divide this. But Allah (awj) not like this. He give without asking. Only you try to bring people to right way. And it is the best trading for people. Trading forever, eternity. But there is another side of this. Who show people bad way, teach them bad manners, bad acting they also-Allah punishing him for each one, same he get punish for one, He get for him. If he teach another one, also twice he will be punished. If he bring 10 people, He will punish10x. 1000, 1000 time punishing. Millions also, millions for each. And this is punishment will not be less.

Because there is, who make good thing-for intending..If he is not doing what he intending for good thing, Allah reward him one reward. But if he do, 10x, 100xAllah know how will be reward. But if he intend to do bad thing if he not do, Allah reward him one also. If he do, Allah punish him one only. But this is to take people to bad way, it is different. He give for each man, as what he dofrom teaching from this man forever He will punish him for one man,10 man, as much he make people to go out from right way and going to bad ways not good ways, Allah punishing him.

Why this? Because before, in Prophet's (sas) time there was ordering for good thing. They are forbidding to do bad thing, not good thing. Prophet (sas) said, when last time approaching it will be opposite. They will order for bad thing. They will be forbidding to do good thing. So it is like this. For this, who ordering this, Allah punishing him for each man he take away from right way. So we must be careful to look what we do. We do bad thing & people learning from us so we must tell them this is not good way. And this is, every time I say it, first for ourselves, our ego we say this for everybody he must look for himself to not do what is not right. And this is from adab of Tariqat-to control yourself, to be look for what you do, if you are making good or bad. Maybe if, without intending, ok. But if you remember after you must say for this man "I do this wrong, don't do this". This is not easy but it is from teaching of Tariqat-to not be, make yourself high. You must put yourself under your feet.

Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, Al Fatiha.
