As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
إِنَّ اللَّهَ مَعَ الَّذِينَ اتَّقَوا وَّالَّذِينَ هُم مُّحْسِنُونَ
<br>“InnAllaha ma‘a lladhīna t-taqaw wa lladhīna hum muḥsinūn”<br>
“Indeed, Allah is with those who fear Him and those who are doers of good.” (Qur’ān 16:128). SadaqAllahu l-‘Aẓīm.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says, indeed, if you want Allah to be with us, follow the way He has shown and fear Allah. Fearing Allah means if you’ve committed something wrong, you appear before Allah in shame.

What you must fear is committing something wrong and then not repenting. This is what’s bad. If you want Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla to be with you, and if you want Him to help you, fear Allah. Fearing Allah means doing good to people. It means avoiding harming people.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla loves those who do good. Muhsinīn means those who help people. He loves them. Ṭarīqah, Islām and sharīʿah all command this.

Those who deviate from this act according to their own whims. They say, “I am a Muslim,” but torment Muslims. They say, “I am a Muslim,” but do badness to others. They say, “I am a Muslim,” but engage in all sorts of deception.

The greatest deception is to deceive people, leading them astray from the way of true Muslims, and making them like themselves.

Therefore, being with the righteous ones is being with Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. Being away from them is disliked by Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla, and is not being with Allah. Being with Allah means first and foremost respecting and honouring our Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam.

Respecting all the Companions, the Ahlu l-Bayt. Respecting the Awliyā and Mashāyikh is the way that Allah loves and wants. Those outside of it satisfy their own egos and do what their egos dictate.

Therefore, we must be careful. People must not be deceived. Now, every day, people say, “He deceived this man, he deceived that man, he stole his money, he took the money and ran away.”

That’s not important; taking the money and running away isn’t important; taking your īmān and running away is what matters. Don’t let them deceive you or swindle you. Worldly wealth comes, but the Hereafter has no compromise.

May Allah protect us and keep us safe from their evil. May Allah make us among those whom Allah loves, inshāAllah.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
