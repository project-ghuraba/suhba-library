Most Beneficent, most Munificent, the Lord of worlds, the Lord of universes, the Lord of the sons of Ādam, give us endless favours. Give us, O our Lord, from Your endless mercy oceans. Support us and give us more power to stand up for Your worshipping, for Your sake and for the sake of Your Beloved Muḥammad, peace be upon him. We are asking, O our Lord, to be Your obedient servants. And Allāh Almighty, the Lord of universes, teaching us how we can be obedient servants by saying - the first step: Say al-ḥamdu liLlāhi Rabbi l-‘ālamīn. That is the beginning to be obedient servants. We must say whole thanks and whole praisings to our Lord, the Creator, Glorious Allāh. Who He is? He is the Creator and He is the Lord of universes, Rabbu l-‘ālamīn. Who is Allāh? He describing Himself: "I am your Lord, the Lord of worlds." Then, the Lord of universes, just He described for Himself and saying to everyone from mankind: the Day of Promises - "Alastu bi-Rabbikum?" (7:172) Asking all people, the sons of Ādam, "Am I your Lord? Am I not your Lord?" And taking our replies. We are saying: "Yes. You are our Lord and the Lord of the universes." Who accepts His Lordship, it is the beginning of our worshipping. And when we are accepting that He is our Lord, no more we are saying that we are Lord also. Because our egos refusing the lordship for anyone except for himself (ego).
