Allāhumma ṣalli wa sallim ‘alá nabiyyinā Muḥammad ‘alayhi s-salām, ṣalātan tadūmu wa tuhdá ’ilayh, māmarra l-layāli wa ṭūla d-dawām. Allāhumma ṣalli wa sallim ‘alá nabiyyinā Muḥammad ‘alayhi s-salām, ṣalātan tadūmu wa tuhdá ’ilayh, māmarra l-layāli wa ṭūla d-dawām.

Yā salām. May Allāh make us to be always with the good one. To make us always with the awliyā’ullah. To make us always to realise where we are and how to behave. We are a weak servant. We are so weak servant that sometimes we don't know and we cannot figure for what we are doing and where our action will be going. If somebody is... they say if you are in situation that pressing to be humble, and acting as a humble, don't think that person he is really humble. No. We all of us pretending to be a humble, pretending to be a good servant, pretending to be a good murīd. Don't say "I'm humble, I'm better than you." No, this is never happen. Don't say "I'm more prayer than you. I'm better than you." Don't say this one also. Azazīl he was the most praying one. Nobody catch [up to] his prayer. But in one sentence he become ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. One things, one small things, one order that he say Allāh Almighty say "you make sajda to Ādam" then he say, "No I'm proud to make sajda to him. You create him from mud, You create me from fire. So I will not make sajda for him." Then become shayṭān.

Don't trust your next moment. Try to be all the time careful about your situation. This is first for me and for everybody. It is very important to realise because we are not in the time that we are playing. No. The playing time is over. We are in the serious time. You have to behave as serious one. And you have to realise who is who and what is everybody's mission. You don't have right to break hearts. You don't have right to say "it is my right to break hearts." No. Don't make discrimination. If you make discrimination you don't have a place here. Because discrimination is the last things what Mawlānā Shaykh was teaching. We have to be understand. It is also for me and also for everybody. Because here it is the place that you have to understand your value and you have to understand why you are here and why Shaykh Effendi is keeping you here. If you are responsible you have to know. If you are animal it's ok, you don't have to know. This is teaching. Mawlāna Shaykh one day all the murīds they was arguing and Mawlāna Shaykh say, "If I'm acting as a Shaykh none of you will be in my door. I will be kicking you out, all of you." You have to be careful. Mawlānā Shaykh is not sleeping. Mawlānā Shaykh is not sleeping. He is fully aware about everything. Don't feel free. If you feel free you will have something coming on you. Allāhumma innī qad ballagh - I'm only warning. It is not my duty to warn but Mawlānā Shaykh he is the one warning. This one is making group, that one is making another group. That one is say your group is better than my group. You like this. What is this? We are acting as social media here? This is ṭarīqat. This is not social media to say I'm, my group is better than your group. What is this? It is not accepted. We have to know that we are darwish. We have to know that we are acting for Allāh Almighty. Maybe we have facility as social media but doesn't matter, this is not for you to fight among themself. Is not allowed. This is big fitnah. And Mawlānā Shaykh Muḥammad he is the first thing is a point that don't make fight in the social media. This is also important for us to understand.

Yes, we are living in modern world. Yeah we are living in modern world but don't forget your beginning. Don't forget your root. Don't forget your kindness. Don't be rude, be kind. We are here to learn how to be kind. We are here to learn how to be hosting people. We are here to learn how to behave for people. Mawlānā Shaykh he was opening his door twenty four hours. He was not saying, "now my door is closed." No. Mawlānā Shaykh he opened this door and open it for twenty four hours. We are servant, we try to serve as much as we can. It is not our right to say no for anybody coming. We used to open door after midnight, three o clock, after midnight. Our door was knocked and we open and host people. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh we live all this way. We live all this years. We been knowing all this. Now to make a new rule for rule of Mawlāna Shaykh it is not accepted. It is not accepted. Mawlāna Shaykh will not accept it. Mawlāna Shaykh was shouting on me if somebody say "Bring some soup for this person", I say "eeh". "Bring some soup, quickly!!" and shouting on me to bring soup for that person. We don't have any chance to say no. If Mawlāna Shaykh want - immediately. And if you say no, you will be taking it.

This is how ṭarīqah, this is how how Mawlāna Shaykh behave. We have to behave as Mawlāna Shaykh. We cannot behave as our own behaviour. No. He say, Allāh Almighty when HeNone say to Sayyidina Mūsā "fa'ikhla‘ na‘layk innaka bil-wādi l-muqaddasi ṭ-ṭuwá" (20:12) That's mean "yā Mūsā take your shoes off and come for the valley of Ṭuwá." What was the shoes? What was... it was ordinary shoes? No. Say, "leave everything that you know, leave every behaviour that you have. Leave it outside and come naked without nothing." This is how Allāh Almighty order Mūsā (as). "You leave everything and come without nothing." That to make us to understand say "leave your shoes out". What is your shoes? It is your manner, your behaviour, you good behaviour, bad beh... "everything leave it outside. What you know, leave it outside and then come to Me free. Then I will load you." This is, this is very important Dr. Munir. If we are a murīd to Mawlāna Shaykh Nazim, we have to leave our all behaviour and then to come to Mawlāna Shaykh. "Yes, Mawlānā we leave everything and we are coming", they say. That's why when say you take Bay‘ah from Shaykh that you leave everything and "I'm belonging to you yā Shaykh. Whatever you like, you do for me." Then when you start to say, "Oh, Shaykh this is wrong and this is right and maybe you do like this and maybe you do like that..." Then it is wrong. Then it is wrong. We are a servant and we try to be a servant. If we are volunteer for this road then we have to be behaving as this road condition. We cannot put our own condition. If you will put your own condition, don't come here. So simple. Nobody forcing you to come. Nobody forcing you to be here. Nobody forcing, no. If you are here then you have to behave as your Shaykh. Yes. As your Shaykh. If you say, this is my Shaykh I have to be in his color. The Shaykh cannot be in my color. No way. We have to follow the color of our Shaykh. We cannot make the Shaykh to follow our color. So simple.

May Allāh forgive me and forgive us and make us to be in good behaviour. And we need guidance yā Mawlānā we need your guidance. We lost, we lost. We need your guidance. Is seriously we need your guidance. May Allāh forgive us and make us to behave as Mawlānā Shaykh he is satisfied with us. And make us to be in his way, to not make our things wasted as shayṭān. May Allāh forgive me and forgive us.
