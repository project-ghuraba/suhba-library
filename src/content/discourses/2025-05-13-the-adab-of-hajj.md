As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh. A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Wa-liLlāhi ‘alá n-nāsi ḥijju l-bayti mani staṭā‘a ilayhi sabīla” (3:97). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘Aẓīm. Ḥajj journeys have begun. It happens for whomever AllāhNone grants it. Because going to ḥajj has always been difficult. And it is difficult again in these years. To go there, to be able to enter, to reach there. In the past, they used to go with such things – with camels, or on foot. That was difficult. Now, even though everything is easier, this time the difficulty is in entering. It is the wisdom of AllāhNone. Everyone should intend for ḥajj. Those who can should make the intention so that the responsibility of the farḍ is lifted from them. Those who go should also observe the adab, the adab of ḥajj its sunnahs, mustaḥabb, wājib, and farḍ, as much as they can. Because that has become another problem. They go to ḥajj, but people are already unable to do anything from the sunnah. As for the wājib, it is either done or not done. That’s why, when someone has the opportunity to go to ḥajj, they need to be careful about this. What is wājib must be done. If they fail to do it because of their own fault, there is punishment for that. But if it’s because someone else didn’t allow them to do it, then they must ask forgiveness from AllāhNone. They should repent and say, “I wanted to do it, but they didn’t let me.” In shā’a Llāh, they will not be held accountable for it. And then ḥajj is performed. Ṭawāf is performed. The most important thing after ḥajj is over is to not remain idle. Whenever the prayer time comes, they should perform all the prayers at the Ka‘bah. Because that prayer is in Masjidu l-Ḥarām. Masjidu l-Ḥarām means the Ka‘bah, its surroundings, its courtyard, its mosque. Now even those who are there are exaggerating this. They say Masjidu l-Ḥarām means all of Makkah. It is not so. Makkah is something else. And Masjidu l-Ḥarām is different. There are many mosques and masjids in Makkah. But Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says, “Masjidī hādhā” – HeNone calls it MyNone mosque. HeNone does not say, “Baladi hādhā” – HeNone does not say this city. HeNone says masjid. Therefore, a prayer performed in that masjid is equal to one hundred thousand prayers. If you pray five times, each worth a hundred thousand, you will have prayed five hundred thousand prayers. If you pray for ten days, that becomes five million. You cannot pray five million prayers in your whole lifetime. Therefore, we must pay attention to it. We must not miss it, because the opportunity doesn’t often come. It is difficult to go there and return – it is not an easy thing. So, we must be careful about it. Secondly, the mosque of our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam is there too. It is easier there – being present for the daily prayers and praying there is easy. It has been built very large and spacious. One can pray there too. That is equal to a thousand prayers. So, it is as if one performs five thousand prayers in a day, and fifty thousand in ten days. The worship done in ten days equals what one could perform in many years. They never allow people to stay there for ten days, but whatever one can manage – nāfilah, ṣadaqah, all of them – is a thousand times more virtuous than in other places. They are both more virtuous and bring a thousand times more reward. There, instead of worldly talk, doing more tasbīḥāt, reading Qur’ān, helping others, or asking questions – all of that brings knowledge. To sit in the presence of our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam – every minute, one is washed in mercy and barakah in that blessed place. To sit near the oneNone most beloved to Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla – our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam – is uninterrupted mercy, a means to mercy. May AllāhNone grant it to everyone. And may HeNone help those who cannot go, so they too may go, in shā’a Llāh. May the door open, and may you enter, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone saying in Qur’ān. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm: “Wa-liLlāhi ‘alá n-nāsi ḥijju l-bayti mani staṭā‘a ilayhi sabīla” (3:97). AllāhNone ordering who can go for ḥajj to make this obligatory worshipping. One of five pillars of Islām – ḥajj. Who can afford by money or by health and to be safe to go to ḥajj, he must go. This is order from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, and it is not easy. Before it was, they going by camel, by feet, walking to there. Was difficult. Not many people can reach in this, to be healthy or to be safe in this way or... many difficulty it was before. But still nowadays, very easy to reach there if there is opening. But if not opening, difficult. This is some kind from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying, it is not easy worshipping. For this, you make intention as, if you can go, to go. Because it is not allowed. You cannot go if you say, “I want to go ḥajj.” You cannot. You must apply. You must wait, you must maybe... We hear there is some people they are... Before, I hear, it was ten year they waiting. Now, I hear one of them, fifteen year they are waiting queue for ḥajj to reach them, not reach them. For this we, AllāhNone make it difficult. But for people who make intention: “I will go,” AllāhNone accept this. And when you reach there, there is also many thing you must remember and you must do. First of all this: when you must do what, in ḥajj, pilgrimage duty, obligatory and farḍ and wājib and sunnah, mustaḥabb. They was before doing this. Now, mustaḥabb, long time ago they take it out. Sunnah also, they take it out. Now wājib, sometimes they doing, sometimes you cannot do. Only farḍ, what you do. For this, as much you can do this, what... If you can do, it will be best. But if you cannot do and they not let you do, so you must ask for forgiveness for AllāhNone. “I couldn’t do.” But if, by yourself you didn’t do, there is some kind of thing you must do, to AllāhNone accept your ḥajj, in shā’a Llāh. Many thing there. First, to be also, not to harm people, not to fight with people. This Qur’ān, because Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla make test for people. In Qur’ān also, remembering people: Don’t shout, don’t fight, don’t argue with each other. This is what Qur’ān saying. So in ḥajj, most important thing after ḥajj, to can be praying five time in Masjidu l-Ḥarām, in Ka‘bah, which it is, each praying, equal to hundred thousand praying. So in one day, you can pray five hundred thousand reward, as you pray five hundred thousand ṣalāh. Ten days, it’s five million. This five million, whole your life, you cannot pray this. You cannot pray even half of this. So it is very good occasion to be there, and to wait for praying. Also difficult to go inside, to go outside. It is not easy now. In Masjidu l-[Ḥarām]... But they are also, they saying for people, “Whole Makkah it is Masjidu l-Ḥarām.” No, Masjidu l-Ḥarām, one. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying, “Masjidī hādhā” – AllāhNone, “MyNone house.” Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone mention Ka‘bah. It is Masjidu l-Ḥarām. This is, who, for this people, they cheated. They stay in hotel, praying in hotel, other side. They said from all around Ka‘bah, Makkah, all in this system. Even driver, when we arrive there, he quickly giving fatwá. He says, “Look from here, from fifty kilometre before Ka‘bah. From here, beginning as, anywhere you pray, you get reward hundred thousand.” How you can take hundred thousand and Prophet’s, ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, masjid only thousand reward? This is not, not true. True: one must be near in Masjidu l-Ḥarām, or if you follow in outside also, it’s okay, following imām still in Masjidu l-Ḥarām. But other thing, no. So for this, you must be careful not to waste your time, waste this chance to take this whole reward. Don’t look for shops, don’t look for restaurant. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, nowadays there are, before it was better, all shops, restaurant. Now it’s better to not eat, to not look for anything. You have everything in your country, so only go for intention, for pleasure of AllāhNone, to get this reward from AllāhNone. Not... Your trade for ākhirah, not for dunyā. And, in shā’a Llāh, after this for Madīnah Munawwarah, also you must be in Prophet’s ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, divine presence, in hisNone mosque. After, you pray. There, is more easier than Makkah. So, it’s easy to pray five time in mosque, Masjidu n-Nabawī, ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, and you sit there. No need to go shops, no need to go outside, because there, showering of mercy, of pleasure, from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Whole, no one second cutting. So you are near the most beloved one for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. You are in divine presence of Prophet, ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. For this, you must be also, as much you can be there, you must be there, in shā’a Llāh. And, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, AllāhNone make for everybody to visit this and take this rewards, in shā’a Llāh. But there is the most important thing for ḥajjī (pilgrims) also: to take telephone and make video call with them. This all, this is very important for ḥajjī. This is, AllāhNone... This is what you see. Before, people they have thinking. Now, everything, AllāhNone take our mind, we all become crazy. Going to ḥajj, not looking for yourself. You looking for family. Before, ḥajj, they going to ḥajj, six months, one year not looking for family. This, coming, phoning to neighbour, neighbour, neighbour, or friend of a friend of friend, or other. All just like this. They disturbing, really disturbing Muslims there. This is not good. With adab, who can, everybody can see in television they put. They can see this good place, blessed place. So, no need to disturb. You only go by adab. Look, you are going there to be in front of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Not to, speaking with family, with wife, with children, with grandchildren, granddaughter. This is ridiculous. So this is also, in shā’a Llāh, some people be not doing this. AllāhNone give us good thinking, in shā’a Llāh. Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
