Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Auzu Billahi Minashaitanir Rajim. Bismillahir Rahmanir Rahim.

“Wa ‘alallahi fal yatawakkalil mu’minoon”.

“Mumins trust in Allah in everything, they are content with whatever happens.”

He says in the Koran Karim: some things that you do not like could happen, they are good for you. There are some things that you like, you ask that they happen. And that is not good but bad for you. That is why when something happens, Mumins should trust in Allah (JJ). Allah (JJ) gives the best.

Every work of a Mumin is good. Our Holy Prophet says, “Ajibtu.” Meaning, “I was surprised.” The Hadith Sharif: “The work of a Mumin is a wonder, his works are completely good.” There is nothing bad for a Mumin. If something difficult happens or Allah’s (JJ) test occurs, its reward is great too. If something good comes, it is also a good deed. That is also good because they have faith in Allah (JJ) and go on the way of Allah (JJ). It is a great good.

That is why when something hard befalls people, they should not forget themselves, should not forget Allah (JJ), and should say, “There is good in this too.” Surely, something that you know to be good most of the time can turn bad. That time people are sad. Whereas Allah (JJ) showed a beautiful way, He showed what is good. Let them follow this and say, “Allah knows what is best, this is good for us.” Let them be thankful and grateful and trust in Allah (JJ). Let them wait to see how it will end.

Allah (JJ) showed people the good way. If they follow that road, there would be no distress or depression. Let them say, “Everything is from Allah. May Allah give us what is best.” Our Holy Prophet said, “Everything is good for a Mumin.” And what he says is truth.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq.

Al-Fatiha.
