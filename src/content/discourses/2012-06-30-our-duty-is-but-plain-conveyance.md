Oppression never lasts. Oppression never lasts, if it lasts it destroys. "We have said what we should say"(hadith). Finish! The final destination is good for the pious ones. Is there something

If they are pious then it is for You. If they are not pious then their final destination will be in the hands of the pious ones. And the final destination is for the pious. The final destination is for the good ones.

We have indicated the Khalifa. If they like it good. If they don't let them be. This is the law of Haqq, the Khalifa has to be from the family of the Prophet (saws). Fatiha.

And all we have to do is to inform & declare. The station/Maqam of the Khalifa is protected according to how it is mentioned in the old books of traditions. If they follow it, they follow, but they are not even following the Great Quran, how would they follow these books. Let them be, they are... All we have to do is to inform & declare.

Allahu Akbar Al Akbar! Fatiha.
