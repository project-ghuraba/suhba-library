Madad Ya Rasulullah, Mada Ya Ashab-e Rasulullah, Madad Ya Mashayikihina. Dastur. ------------------------------------------------------------

Our Prophet ﷺ is saying, “A believer is like a person who feels the pain of his head or the eye in the whole body”. Believers are not alone, they think about other believers. They feel each other’s pain. Believers are like one person. ------------------------------------------------------------

Our Prophet ﷺ is saying, “There are two attributes which are not present together in a believer. They are stinginess and lying”. These are bad characteristics. A believer cannot have both of them. One can be present but never both. ------------------------------------------------------------

“A believer is not bit twice from one (snake) hole”. If cheated once, he is not cheated once again. He should be careful. A believer should be alert. If he puts his hand in a snake hole and the snake bites him, will he put his hand again? No one will. It is same for everything. We should pay attention to this. We should not make same mistakes again, or have the absence of mind. A believer should be very careful. He may say, “I trusted and made a mistake”. You trust once and be cautious next time. You should not make same mistake next time. ------------------------------------------------------------

Our Prophet ﷺ is saying, “A believer can have all qualities except of betraying and lying”. He can be a coward, a shy person or whatsoever. However, there is no betraying and lying in his nature. ------------------------------------------------------------

Our Prophet ﷺ is saying, “Allah ﷻ protects His mumin servants like a caring shepherd protects his herd”. When you have true belief in Allah ﷻ, you are under Allah’s ﷻ protection. Just like a shepherd protects his herd from wild animals and from falling down the cliffs, Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla protects His believing servants. We should believe in Him. Allah ﷻ is with us. People have no iman now. They fear they will catch a disease. If Allah ﷻ doesn’t want it, nothing will happen. ------------------------------------------------------------

Our Prophet ﷺ is saying, “Just like you protect someone sick from forbidden foods with a diet, Allah ﷻ protects His servants from worldly goods”. Some people may live in poverty without certain goods. This happens by the will of Allah ﷻ. If they have worldly comfort, they will lose their iman. Therefore, Allah ﷻ is keeping them away from dunya, or dunya away from them. Everything has its wisdom and benefit. No need to rebel and object Allah ﷻ. Allah ﷻ gives so much provisions. If we have more rizq, we will forget Allah ﷻ and dive into dunya. ------------------------------------------------------------

Our Prophet ﷺ is saying, “Verily, Allah ﷻ protects hundred surrounding houses because of one pious person”. If a pious person lives in a neighborhood or a village, Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla protects hundred houses in his area for his honor. ------------------------------------------------------------

Our Prophet ﷺ is saying, “Allah ﷻ has such servants that He protects them from death and extends their lives through good deeds”. There are beloved servants of Allah ﷻ. He favours them so much that He protects them from death, and their lives are long and deeds are good. They have good provisions. They are not in need of anyone and have good life and deeds. Allah ﷻ is kind to them and they live healthy. With Allah’s ﷻ kindness, whole life of believers is good. They die happily and reach the rank of shahid. ------------------------------------------------------------

Our Prophet ﷺ is saying, “Verily, Allah ﷻ has chosen servants on earth who live in health. When they die, they go to paradise. Fitnah passes over them like dark parts of the night”. The end of times is like these dark nights. There is so much fitnah and darkness. But these blessed people are never effected. ------------------------------------------------------------

Our Prophet ﷺ is saying, “Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla said, “My servants are more beloved than some of My angels”.” A believing servant is more beloved in the presence of Allah ﷻ. ------------------------------------------------------------

Our Prophet ﷺ is saying, “There is no one more valuable in the presence of Allah ﷻ than a believer”. A mumin servant is valuable in the presence of Allah ﷻ. He can have the whole world, if he’s not a believer, it has no value. It has no benefit neither to him, nor to others. He is not accepted in the presence of Allah ﷻ. Accepted is the believer in Allah’s ﷻ presence. ------------------------------------------------------------

Our Prophet ﷺ is saying, “A believer is more valuable than some angels in the presence of Allah ﷻ”. ------------------------------------------------------------

Our Prophet ﷺ is saying, “We don’t know anyone of his kind who is thousand times more valuable than a believer”. No one is more valuable than a believer among his kind. A believer is thousand times better and worthy. ------------------------------------------------------------

Our Prophet ﷺ is saying, “Everything has its truth”. The truth is what a believer looks for. When a man knows the truth, he understands everything.

Our Prophet ﷺ says, “A believer cannot reach true iman unless he knows that whatever is coming has to come”. How can a person reach true iman? They say, when something happens to them, “If I had left earlier, this would not happen”. No, it’s not like that. You should say, “No matter how much I try to escape, it will happen to me”. If Allah ﷻ wants it, weapons of the whole world will be shot towards you and nothing will touch you. That is by the will of Allah ﷻ. To know and believe in it is the true iman.

People now are telling this illness came from this one or that one. There is the will of Allah ﷻ. These things will happen. Therefore, people with iman should not be effected with such things. If it happened, be patient and pray to Allah ﷻ. InshaAllah you will recover. If it doesn’t happen, make shukr to Allah ﷻ for protecting you. And know that it is not written for you. If people know this, they will be at peace and won’t panic so much. ------------------------------------------------------------

Our Prophet ﷺ is saying, “Certainly, iman in the heart of someone among you wears out like a dress does”. Sometimes people say they were more pious and had more iman before. Iman wears out like a dress. Therefore, we have to pray to Allah ﷻ that he renews iman in our hearts. Our iman weakens and gets old. If you ask from Allah ﷻ, Allah ﷻ will give it. May iman of all of us be renewed inshaAllah. ------------------------------------------------------------

Our Prophet ﷺ is saying, “Renew your iman by reciting la ilaha illAllah many times”. If it wears out again, recite la ilaha illAllah again. May Allah ﷻ give true iman to all of us inshaAllah.

Sadaqa RasuluLlah fima qal aw kama qal.
