Waṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-F’āiz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-

Allah ‘azza wa jalla says in the Quran, “Wa-tarjūna mina ’Llahi mā lā yarjūn” (Qur’ān 4:104). One who is a Muslim begs Allah, asks from Allah. One without faith would not know what to do. He would wonder [about it and be] troubled. [It would be] a wonder without hope. Even if he seems fine on the outside, inwardly he is bothered. Inwardly there is gloom and darkness in him, because there is a lack of light. Light only exists with faith. One with faith has light on his face and heart. Thanks be to Allah that He given this best provision, for this provision is not destined to everyone.

“Wa-’in tuṭi‘ athara man fī-l-arḍi yuḍillūka ‘an sabīli-’Llah” (Qur’ān 6:116). If you listen to people most will divert you from your path. Which means that the people with faith are few. Most are following their egoic self. They are without faith. If they say to you let’s do this or that, it is [something that will] put you off of your path.

Today, people are going through a test. They [i.e., these others] are trying to make it something not useful. Satan wants this suffering to be in vain, and is trying to make sure people do not benefit from it. Allah ‘azza wa jalla, however, is the one who gave this test. He is the One who does it all, so it is not good to be hopeless. “Wa-tarjūna … mā lā yarjūn” (Qur’ān 4:104). You are not hopeless. Allah says,“ There is a door for you that others do not have.” That is why tonight is a holy, blessed night.

In this blessed night, pray that Allah 'azza wa jalla will not test us with things that we cannot bear. May He elevate our reward. Already it is a blessed month, and Allah ‘azza wa jalla accepts people’s prayers and orders His angels to bestow what people have asked for. Tonight insha’Allah our prayers... May Allah be our helper, give us relief, give peace to the world of Islam, insha’Allah. May it be good, and may evil be dismissed. May Allah protect the children and the people of Muhammed (SAW). May there be healing tonight, insha’Allah. It is a blessed night tonight. Allah changes or does not change whatever He desires. Everything is in His hands. May tonight be a way towards goodness, and may suffering end. May our faith become stronger. Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-F ti ah
