As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Wa-in ta‘uddū ni‘mata Llāhi lā tuḥṣūhā" (14:34). Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says, you can't count the favours given by AllāhNone. Not only on your ten fingers, but also on the computer it is impossible to count Allāh's favours. They are so many. AllāhNone has favoured us so much. HeNone gives to everyone, not only to Muslims. HisNone favours are given to all mankind. Mankind does not know the value. They are ungrateful. They say it happens or occurs by itself. They object and rebel. It is not good to object. The more you are ungrateful, the lower your rank falls. You become worthless. The value of a person is to know AllāhNone. By knowing AllāhNone a man becomes worthy, his rank increases. He becomes a beloved person in the presence of AllāhNone.

AllāhNone says, "Inna Llāha lā yuḥibbu l-kāfirīn" (3:32). AllāhNone created mankind great and respected. But people who rebel and make kufr, AllāhNone doesn't love them. Whom does AllāhNone love? AllāhNone loves those who appreciate HisNone favours, Muslims and mu’min. You should make shukr for favours. Nothing can be given in return for HisNone favours. No one can pay it back. The worship we do does not resemble anything. Therefore, these favours are important. We should always make shukr. When we make shukr, the favours increase. But otherwise, people who rebel and show ungratefulness by saying, "We did everything ourselves. We did it," and so on, their station decreases. They become the lowest of creatures. People rank each other as they like: "This is a very great man, this is a philosopher, this one is a professor, that one is a doctor, this one wrote so many books." All of this has no value so long as they don't believe in AllāhNone. The most ignorant person, a completely illiterate person who believes in AllāhNone is better than all of them in the sight AllāhNone.

Taqwá, (God-fearing) a person with taqwá has a high rank. Regardless of color and race, regardless of his knowledge, what is important is his strong faith, strong taqwá. Therefore, shukr to AllāhNone for HisNone favours, especially for the favour of īmān (belief). Shukr to AllāhNone. May AllāhNone strengthen our faith. Today is a holy day, a day when prayers are accepted. It is one day in a year for all Muslims. Those who went on ḥajj, today millions of people are becoming ḥujjāj. May they have Allāh'sNone pleasure by completing their duty, in shā’a Llāh.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying, bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm, "Wa-in ta‘uddū ni‘mata Llāhi lā tuḥṣūhā" (14:34). If you count the favour what AllāhNone give us for, not only for Muslim, for whole human being, you cannot count what HeNone is, HisNone favour for all this humanity. Counting, not by finger, by computer even you cannot count Allāh's ‘Azza wa-Jalla favours, what HeNone give us. We are just going around not seeing anything from HisNone favours. But Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone is knowing and HeNone is happy with people who appreciate this favour and thank HimNone. And this people, they are believer people, who appreciate and accept and try to do something for this favour, what AllāhNone give us. But we cannot give anything for our Lord. HeNone is not in need to anything but HeNone is happy when you are thank, you thanking: "O my Lord, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, thank YouNone for what YouNone give us from this million, billion of favours. We are thankful, very grateful for YouNone," for what HeNone give us. And HeNone is happy.

"Inna Llāha yuḥibbu sh-shākirīn." AllāhNone, HeNone is in love the people thanking for HimNone. For other people, AllāhNone not love them. This is... here, AllāhNone give favour for whole human being. But when some people, many of people, not accepting this from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, our Lord, and not thanking for what HeNone give us, AllāhNone, HeNone said, "Inna Llāha lā yuḥibbu l-kāfirīn" (3:32). This is kāfir, kāfir meaning he not accept Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. "We are coming from nothing," they said. "From coming from nature, no Creator, nothing." So AllāhNone HeNone said, "Inna Llāha lā yuḥibbu l-kāfirīn" (3:32). AllāhNone He not, HeNone don't love, HeNone doesn't love non-believer. Kāfir meaning, who not accept, he is against. How AllāhNone HeNone give you everything and you are not accept and you ask to be beloved for AllāhNone? No. You are not beloved to AllāhNone.

The beloved one they are coming higher and higher in Divine Presence of AllāhNone. And who are not believer, non-believer, coming against, they are going lower and lower and lower and they becoming lowest creature among creatures. Among this all thing, from human being, from animal, from insect, from everything, they are becoming the lowest one when they are not accepting. Because everything they are accept. Even they cannot speak, they cannot think, but they knowing their Creator and whole time they making mentioning HisNone name by their way – what AllāhNone they give them way to take. Even insect – look he is all time making this voice. But there is also wind, there is tree, stone even. Even not hearing what they are saying, but all praising AllāhNone, mentioning HisNone name whole time. But only one not mentioning and coming against, the, who calling themselves "human being". After also they are making philosopher. They are saying "humanistic". So, no any value. Even whole world following him, or he write books, or he write, giving a doctrine, this rubbish things. All no any value in Divine Presence. The one shepherd, he not knowing anything but only he is believer, he is better from all of these people in Divine Presence of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. The belief it is very important, the biggest favour for us. We are thanking AllāhNone for this favour and also we are today day, special day for believer, for whole human being. AllāhNone calling people to come to HisNone House, to HisNone, where most blessed place in this world, to clean from their sins and to become a new one. It is no difference between anyone, if when you are living you have breath and you ask for forgiveness and come there, AllāhNone accepts. But people they are making bad for themselves, not Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla making this for themself.

AllāhNone accept, in shā’a Llāh, ḥajj of these people, millions of people. Today they will become ḥajjī and they become acceptable one, in shā’a Llāh, newborn from clean from sins. AllāhNone reward them and reward us and asking for shifā’ for whole people and giving strong īmān for us, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
