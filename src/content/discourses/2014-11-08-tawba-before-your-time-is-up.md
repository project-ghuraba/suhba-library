Sheikh Bahauddin Adil Al-Haqqani sohbat of the 8th of November, 2014.

Ya salam! Salam aleikum. Masha'Allah it is a wonderful day to be in Lefke. I am inviting everybody to be in Lefke because to know that Mawlana Sheikh he is still alive; to understand this feeling and to get the big hug of Mawlana Sheikh. He is Haqq, Haqqani! Haqqani! Haqqani is Haqq! We have to know that Mawlana Sheikh is Haqq! We have to know that Mawlana Sheikh is Haqqani! This is Mawlana Sheikh. When he claimed Haqq and he claimed Haqqani either for himself he was saying, "Yes! It is Haqq for you, come and take it."

If you know it is your haqq, Mawlana Sheikh he didn't close his door. Mawlana Sheikh, he keep his door open. Mawlana Sheikh he keep his heart open. Mawlana Sheikh, he keep himself open. Mawlana Sheikh never hide himself behind the wall. Never hide himself behind the words, never hide himself behind anything. He was so clear, he was so open. And he was for all. He was never making discrimination between the poor ones and the rich one. He never make discrimination between the old one or the new one. He was all the time open for everybody. This is Mawlana Sheikh how he was acting. And still acting, because he is alive in our heart. This must be known.

When I come to Lefke after a long trip, I feel him in my heart more and more and more. Alhamdulillah, this has been some message coming from him for people thinking that Lefke is closed. They think Lefke it is not valid anymore. They think the secret is not here anymore. The main source of the secret is here. If you don't understand this one, you don't understand nothing. It is waste for your life, it is waste for your behaviour, it is waste for your service. It is waste for everything that you've done if you don't understand this small point. Maybe, you are driving Rolls Royce, but you don't have the key, so this Rolls Royce it doesn't work. Look for the key! Mawlana Sheikh he is all the time saying "Look for the key! Don't look for the big issue."

There are so many big things you cannot enter without the key. Mawlana Sheikh he was the huge key, not small key. He was huge key, and he was known as the key. If you can understand that Mawlana Sheikh how he was a key for us and you are still ignoring Mawlana Sheikh as a key and you try to go by yourself for things, no way! We are, trying to explain so many things since a long time. And Alhamdulillah we believe that we succeed to make so many people to understand what we mean and what Mawlana Sheikh meant. Because Mawlana Sheikh he is..

They say we are going, travelling.. you have a problem with the check-in desk and somebody will not be in the plane and say, "I cannot help you. Go for supervisor. Go for supervisor. He will help you, if he wants he will help you." Mawlana Sheikh he is the supervisor. If you don't believe me go for my supervisor, this is what I will tell you. If you have access for supervisor then you go direct for supervisor, don't ask me. But I am here in the check-in desk, trying to make service for the ones coming. And I am trying my best, I don't have any issues. I don't have anything. I am only the part-time man.

I think everybody is part-time. Even we are part-time alive because one third of our life is being sleeping and this means we are not alive. Maybe we are breathing but we are not alive. We are out of order. They say, "why he is out of order?" What does it mean out of order? He cannot drink, he cannot eat, he cannot speak he cannot act - just... resting. But this is half dead. So that means even our life we are part-time. This, don't forget about this. It is very important to understand what it means part-time. So you go for work, what you work? One third of your life you are working, still part-time. And you have another third

of your life, one third of your life you don't know what to do with it. You feel bored, you feel this, you feel that. So, if you put your lifestyle, it will be part-time lifestyle.

And if you are claiming that you are full-time Sheikh, also it is wrong. You cannot be full-time Sheikh because you'll say "I am tired I cannot be with people all the time. People kissing my hand, I must go and wash my hand. And I don't know if people they have disease or viruses so what shall I do?" You try to save yourself from people, so this is also part of your part-time Sheikh. If you are with people too much, say "O the people brought all their problems for me. It's so heavy on me I cannot carry it, I have my own problems." And this one also cannot be as full-time Sheikh. Masha'Allah I see only one full-time Sheikh. One! Not sleep, not be afraid to be among people. He give his hand, he give his feet, he give his face to be kissed by murid, by anybody. And scared not either from disease or viruses or whatever it is. And this is Mawlana Sheikh. He was with the people, in the middle of people, in the middle of crowds, in the huge...

And I never see him say "I am tired today I don't want to see anybody." Even in his worse case, no he grant us, he grant us so many years of his life and we've been complaining - The Sheikh doesn't see people, the Sheikh is ignoring people. The Sheikh he was going just in weakest time for him, just he was going out in Asr time, patrolling. Just the people to look at him, and to see him. He was so generous. He was not a young man, he was a 90 years old man. And even though he was trying his best I didn't see any 90 year old man can be with a crowd. Did you see any? Did you see any? Any man who is 90 years old and he was with the crowd and he was trying to polite the crowd. Not the crowd do polite him, no! He was affording to polite the crowd. And then you go and make say - "they don't allow the Sheikh to see people. They're doing this."

You have to be smart. You have to understand where you step and what you behave. It is very important, because you are dealing with Allah Almighty directly. This is very tough place. Maybe you can deal with me. Maybe you can deal with others. It doesn't matter we can be easy going no problem. But when you are dealing with Allah Almighty then if you have been slapped heavily for your bad manner against your Sheikh. And you dare to speak about your Sheikh like this. Then it will be the real hitting. This is how Allah Almighty will never forgive you to be bad behaviour for your Sheikh; will never forgive you, don't forget this. He will never forgive you because Allah Almighty He knows the reality. He knows the truth. Because Allah Almighty He is not sleeping.

Allah Almighty think like you or like me? Allah Almighty He has His own rule. He is the majestic and magnificent. He is the One that getting when He will put the rule. You have to obey. And when you are hurting the one He loves then you have big, big things to face. "Allah Yumhil wa la Yuh'mil." Allah Almighty He gives you time but He never forgets what you've done. That's why He is all the time saying "I am waiting for you to give the forgiveness to say tawba Ya Rabbi tawba astaghfirullah." What do you think I am saying all the time - tawba Ya Rabbi, tawba Ya Rabbi astaghfirullah?" Maybe I've done something bad, maybe I've done something not good. Maybe I've done something it does not suit Allah Almighty. So I am trying to be one of the tawba people, saying "Ya Rabbi I understand my mistake and I am trying to be tawba."

But I am saying to you, if you don't understand your mistake and continue in your road, then you have to face the real judgment of Allah Almighty. Allah Almighty He gives you time to make tawba and to understand your situation and turn back. Allah Almighty He is not tyrant. He is still giving you time. Say "Yumhil" what does it mean yumhil? He gives you time. He gives you time, but be careful the time might be up! And they call it time-up! When it's time-up, then you are in trouble. When you are in time-up, then you are in trouble. And nobody will save you, because you say "I am the one saver." No, you are not the one saver, you are nothing. You have to understand that you are nothing. If you understand that you are nothing, then you start to be something. I am not saying you'll be something. you start to be something because Allah Almighty He doesn't like the servant to say "I am something." He doesn't like it. Allah Almighty He says "in My manifestation there is none other, nothing!" Everything is nothing when Allah Almighty He makes His manifestation. And don't try to play fool in that level. This is important also to understand. This is coming from Mawlana Sheikh. Mawlana Sheikh he is not sleeping in his Maqam. He is not resting in his Maqam, he is still active. This is our main belief. It is not sub-belief, it is our main belief. If we don't believe in this we don't have to be Naqshbandi, we don't have to be in this road, we don't have to be Sufi, we don't have to be in this place. Go! Do what you like.

But this is our main, main, belief. And this is how we were brought up, and this how distributing Naqshbandi, we understand that all the Naqshbandi Sheikhs they're alive and they are active. And all their secrets with the Khalifa of them and now today the Khalifa of them Mawlana Sheikh Muhammad (qs). He is the one having all this power in him. Maybe he is very quiet but he has this power. You travel with him and see how he has this power. Alhamdulillah we've been travelling with him 15 days and Mawlana Sheikh he showed me; he showed me the realistic, he show me everything. Alhamdulillah I see it and I understand what Mawlana Sheikh he gave to Sheikh Muhammad (qs). I see Alhamdulillah. He show me. And I am saying this for all murid because all murid have to know who is their Sheikh. There is nothing called spring there, spring there, no! We have one huge ocean called Sheikh Muhammad (qs). This must be known. And this I am saying for murid to understand.

Maybe you have small spring doesn't matter. But which foolish one he leaves the ocean and he goes for small spring? Which foolish one? I don't think in this time people if somebody give them, for example, one billion, and they say "no, no we have 1note for 100 it will be enough for us." I don't think anybody in this world will say this one, even in the dark Africa. They will say "no we know the billion what it means." Or in the Amazon, they will say "we know what a billion means." 100 note, who will do? Even a child he will say "I prefer the billion." So my advise for the clever ones to understand what is the difference between the two.

It is very important to understand. Allah Almighty He gave His secret for which servant He likes. He doesn't ask you. And He doesn't say "you've been in My service 1000 years but I will not give it to you and you have to obey." This is your test. Why you don't take it as your test? Take it as your test. If you take it as your test you'll be upgraded. But if you take it and say "No! I am objecting!" So you will be going down, down, down. Down! And then you'll be losing your reputation. You will be losing your hair. You'll be losing your turban. You'll be losing your wealth. Your children, you'll be losing, losing...Because Allah Almighty He has all the power, say tac, tac... take this off, take this off, take this off... If you lose your health, if you have the world for you what will it mean for you? Nothing. One thing, Allah Almighty takes your health and I will see what you will do with all your power, with your connections, with all your wealth, with everything. You are in bed, lying and having the machine inside you pip pip pip, pip. And then you are the most powerful one.

Nas'alu Allah al lutf. I just say "Ya Rabbi don't put us in that situation, and we know that Your tawba door is open all the time & You are giving us time to make tawba and to understand our mistakes. Maybe our egoistic desires it will be ahead of our tawba, but we try to make Ya Rabbi, to go ahead of our egoistic desires and we say "Tawba Ya Rabbi. Tawba astaghfirullah." May Allah forgives us and may Allah forgives you and may Allah makes us return, to be return to our real road with Mawlana Sheikh and to not put our ego in front of Mawlana Sheikh and to say "we are the one making Mawlana Sheikh to be this or we are the one making Mawlana Sheikh to be that and we are the one making Mawlana Sheikh to be famous and we are the one." No nobody making Mawlana Sheikh famous. Mawlana Sheikh he's been dressed these things. Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Daghastani he's been hidden and Mawlana Sheikh Nazim he made that treasure to be open up for all the world. Mawlana Sheikh Nazim he is the one making to be open for all the world. Mawlana Sheikh he was in Jabal Qasiyun in very humble area that nobody knows where he is. Even in those days you google it you cannot find it. But this is to know that he was a hidden treasure and this hidden treasure has been shown to the whole of the world by Mawlana Sheikh Nazim (qas) al 'aliy.

And may Allah bless you and may Allah make us to be in their service not they are in our service; to make us under their feet, under their service and to make us, Ya Rabbi not for our ego to be proud about what we are doing or proud about what we are giving as a service. No we want their blessings. Ya Rabbi make us in that level that we are under their feet Ya Rabbi. And this is our wish and this is our dua, and I am wishing this for all the one who've been thinking they are important - To not think they are important and let them think they are in need of Allah Almighty, in need to Prophet and in need of Grand Sheikhs. Otherwise we'll be slapped so badly that they will not even say what's going on, because there is no time to say this.

May Allah bless you. May Allah make you in the service of Mawlana Sheikh and please, please, please say "tawba Ya Rabbi tawba astaghfirullah - for your sake and for your safety." May Allah bless you.

Wa min Allahi t-tawfiq, bi hurmati l-Habib bi hurmati l-Fatiha.
