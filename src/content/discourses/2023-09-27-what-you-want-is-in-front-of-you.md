As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh. A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Yā-ayyuhā l-insānu mā gharraka bi-rabbika l-karīm. Alladhī khalaqaka fa-sawwāka fa-‘adalakk" (82:6-7). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘aẓīm. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is saying, "O mankind, how are you cheated? How are they cheating you that you don't recognize Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla?" Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla created you. And HeNone created you in the best shape. HeNone gave you the best things. HeNone made you with both a high rank and a position from which you can do everything. And HeNone made everything under your command. But you are cheated by others. You have ruined yourself. This is good and momentous advice for all people.

People think they can earn with their minds, that they will have fun and entertain themselves and be saved without doing anything. They have no knowledge of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, or going to HisNone presence. This time we are living in is the worst time in the history of mankind, because people of olden times knew there was a creator. But nowadays they mostly deny HimNone. They say, "Do whatever you want. Enjoy yourselves." But they cannot. It doesn't work. Not everyone can enjoy himself. And for those who can, this enjoyment doesn't bring them anything, after some time. They ask for something else. They ask for more. They search. People are searching. What they search for is in front of their eyes. But they don't see. Because of the badness they do, they don't see the huge thing in front of them. They see everything aside from it. This is mankind's kufr, rejection of AllāhNone. Kufr is not good. Īmān, to believe in AllāhNone, is good.

AllāhNone created us. HeNone created us in the best shape. HeNone granted us the best things. HeNone gave us arms and legs, eyes and mind. HeNone told us and showed us everything mankind should be, and is, able to do, from the beginning until the end of their lives. There is no need for other things. If mankind goes on this way, they will not need anything. And their end will be good. Most importantly, they will attain the hereafter. They will attain the hereafter – ākhirah. Otherwise, they run around this world in vain. In the end, they won't have accomplished anything. They will lose everything. May AllāhNone protect us.

May AllāhNone put HisNone love in our hearts, for the honour of our Holy ProphetNone, in shā'a Llāh. The greatest profit for Muslims is the love of our Holy ProphetNone. And to show respect to himNone is our most accepted worship in the presence of AllāhNone. May AllāhNone not let people be misled. There are many confused people around Muslims. There are many people among them who say the good is bad and the bad is good. May AllāhNone protect us.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying in Qur’ān: Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Yā-ayyuhā l-insānu mā gharraka bi-rabbika l-karīm, alladhī khalaqaka fa-sawwāka fa-‘adalak, fī ayyi ṣūratin mā shā’a rakkabak" (82:6-8). Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying, "O son of Ādam, who make you to cheat you? And make you to not believe in your Lord, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, whoNone is create you, make you good shape, who give every good thing. And the best creation, HeNone create you, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. And you still not believing in CreatorNone.

AllāhNone, HeNone, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, HeNone create us in best shape. Maybe some people they say, "If we have four legs, we can be running more than this." Now people, they are running also. Or, "If we have big ear, we can hear more better." Or, "Big eyes, we can..." Everything, this, become ugly if you do this. Even they... Some people they said, wing. If they put wing, it will be also not... They make, stupid people, picture – but even in picture, if they put wing, they put small one. For to carry fifty, sixty or eighty kg [kilogram] man, you need one wing, it is, not even this room enough for this. Even in the picture, they are not useable. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone create us in the best shape, and best position. And HeNone honoured us.

But people they not believe. Why they not believe? Because they are cheated with other people. They said this, "No creator. There is nothing. Only we live in this life, and after this, nothing. But we, when we die, we will be only earth." And this, even they don't want earth, they burn now. They – only little bit ashes, and they said, finished. You, you can think this finish. Who brought you to this life, HeNone not ask you – what for you. And when HeNone will recreate you, also will not ask you. You, so you will be regret and you will be, feel, real[ly that] you have been very badly cheated. Very badly, they cheat you, for your life finish forever, with this.

But people now they are saying – They are thinking they are very clever, and only they are looking after happiness here, and they said, "Okay, we are happy now." But here happiness not... for they, people, they got bored. Even they have everything, they looking for another thing. For another thing, going this side. No, this also, I will fed up. We go to other side, also fed up. But they are, what they are looking, they said — many people, human being, they are in looking, researching for something, for good thing for themself. And Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone just put in front of them this, what they want, and calling them, "Come here to be happy." But they are not even seeing. There, like mountain in front of eye, they are not looking. Looking for other side. Looking for other side until they will die. And after, they will see this reality.

AllāhNone help people to come to hidāyah. This is what we are – human being – in need. They said human being they need this, they need that. They need hidāyah, they need belief, and ask for forgiveness from their Creator, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. AllāhNone making us in this way, way of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, which it is the way of what AllāhNone love. When you go in this way, you will be happy. Not be regret at all. This is guarantee from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla and Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, AllāhNone give hidāyah for all people, we are asking. We are not happy, or jealous for people. Maybe too much people coming to Islām, coming to hidāyah, and it will be too much? No. AllāhNone... As a mu’min, he like good thing for other. Never like bad thing. AllāhNone... for this, the best thing for everybody: to believe, īmān, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone give us, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
