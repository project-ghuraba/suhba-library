A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is the one who gives everything to people. No one can prevent what HeNone gives. And no one can give what HeNone withholds. Mankind forgets Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla and is grateful to others, or expects and asks for help from them. They place importance on them, whereas everything is in the hands of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. We must make shukr to AllāhNone. We must praise AllāhNone.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla made two feasts. There are two feasts. But people made up other holidays in order to forget those of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. And every time they make up something different. There are two feasts.

Today is Workers' Day. We all have to make shukr to AllāhNone, for HeNone gave us skill and talent. HeNone gave us work to do. If AllāhNone didn't give you work, where would you find it? Where would you find bread? Where would you find food? We must make shukr for this. We must make shukr to Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. As we said, no one can prevent what AllāhNone gives. And no one can give what HeNone doesn't give. No one can stop what HeNone gives. And no one can provide what HeNone doesn't give.

Some people place importance on these days. It is okay when it is like a normal holiday. But giving it importance and making it special is stupid. It is completely useless. Sometimes people commit sins for nothing. Because they think they are important on that day and say, "How cool I am to do so much work." If you did it, AllāhNone meant it for you to do. There are many people who can't work. There are many jobless people who can't find work. And there are many hungry ones. They wish to have any job. And those who work say, "We work too much." If you do, you are doing it for yourself. And if you are doing it for AllāhNone, the work you do counts as worship. You will eat from ḥalāl food. Otherwise, people work for their egos. They say, "Others are taking my rights. They are using me."

The most important thing in Islām is the right of workers. In Islām, it is said you must pay a worker before his sweat is dry. Of course, if that is not applicable nowadays, you must certainly pay their salaries. You must pay them everything they are owed. The right of workers is the most important thing in Islām. Our Holy ProphetNone strongly emphasised this point. HeNone placed importance on it. There are many people who take workers' rights. And then they make a holiday for them or I don't know what. These are useless things. But what is important are their goals and intentions. The goals and intentions for this thing began in opposition to AllāhNone. And then it became a tradition. AllāhNone gives according to the intentions of people. So they must make their intention to get some rest for the pleasure of AllāhNone We are getting our rights, shukr to AllāhNone. AllāhNone gave us work and strength. They must make shukr to AllāhNone. And they shouldn't go into other matters and dangerous things. They mustn't be with things and people that AllāhNone dislikes. They must not imitate them. "We are not like them. May AllāhNone protect us. We had a day off today because it is a holiday. But we don't accept it as a feast." They must say so. There are two feasts. There is no holiday apart from them. They call it Workers' Day and I don't know what. It is a completely useless thing. Usually there are all kinds of bad things that happen on this day. You will see on the news tonight that they destroyed here and there.

Therefore people, Muslims, must pay attention to this. They must make their intention to gain the pleasure of AllāhNone in everything they have to do. We do whatever the pleasure of AllāhNone is. We make shukr to AllāhNone. HeNone gave us work, food and a spouse. Shukr to AllāhNone for these. With shukr, favours increase. Otherwise, there will be neither blessings, nor favours. May AllāhNone protect us.

We are... al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are thanking AllāhNone for what HeNone gave us from HisNone good thing, gift for everything. And we are knowing everything, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone gave us. What HeNone gave, nobody can prevent from you. What HeNone will not give you, nobody can bring for you. Everything AllāhNone, HeNone is able to do. Nothing else. Without HisNone knowing, nothing happens. And HeNone gave us two days for feast.

But nowadays people, they are trying to forget this. Everytime they bring something new. Something good, they thought. Because they make people to think it is good. For people, for... Like this day of first of May. They say this is Workers' Day feast – They say "feast". Feast only one – or two. ‘Īdu l-Fiṭr, ‘Īdu l-Aḍḥá. Other, they make. In Western country, okay. They don't have anything. They can do. But Muslim country, people they must be awake for this. Not to be cheated with this people because they gave them one day holiday to make it feast. No, it is not feast. Because feast for people, who have job, he has work, he has business to do – AllāhNone gave them this. Everyday he must be thankful for AllāhNone what HeNone gave. Not for one day making feast and other days not remembering what will be what...

Because in Islām, the most important thing, to keep right of worker. Who work for you, you musn't take from his, what you must give him daily. "You must give him before his tears [sweat] dry." This is Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying. Of course, this old time, every day they was paying. And so, somebody, maybe, they make it late. For this, heNone said, the best thing – to give him every day before his tears [sweat]... Of course, now it is not like this. But many people, they not giving right of worker. Especially, when it is foreigner worker, they work for them without permission – work permission. So, this many people we hear, they give them working. And they cannot complain for anything. So, they doing this. But in Islām, it is very important to keep workers' rights. Because they are working for their life, for their ḥalāl food, for... by their... So, it is very important. So, before... before the tear [sweat] [be]coming dry, they must give.

And it is, also for worker nowadays, many of them, they are not happy with what they have. But they must be happy, because AllāhNone give them work give them job, give them something to do to get their money for family, to keep their family to be safe from poorness. When you thank AllāhNone, say Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam and AllāhNone in Qur’ān say, "La-’in shakartum la-azīdannakum" (14:7). "You must be thankful, AllāhNone make it more, giving more." But if you are angry, you are not happy and you not happy with what they give you, AllāhNone will make it less – no barakah. For this nowadays, people, whole world, they are angry. Everytime going in the street, demonstration, doing this, destroying here and there. Because they are not thinking about ākhirah, about what Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla like, what Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam like. And only thinking their provision coming from work, from these people. So, they must demonstrate and take their right by this.

But in Islām, AllāhNone saying, "When you are accept and thankful for this, it will be enough for you and for your family and for your country, also." Because when government giving for you more money they will take more tax, or they will make it other place. To bring money, they must do something. You take and they take. you take, they... And it will be, at the end, no benefit for you, no benefit for the other, because barakah went and nothing come for you. AllāhNone help us this. But now we are living in time, this system of kufr, non-believer. They are not believe in god, not believe in AllāhNone. They thought they will manage to do everything by themselves. And coming worse and worse and worse. And nobody happy, nobody satisfied. No worker, no trader, no staff.

AllāhNone help us. AllāhNone make us to reach time of to this whole, this rubbish system going. Coming justice system Sayyidunā l-Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām, in shā’a Llāh. That time it will be a feast for us, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
