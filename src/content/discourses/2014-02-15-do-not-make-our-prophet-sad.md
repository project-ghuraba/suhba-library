Shaykh Muhammad Nazim Al-Haqqani An-Naqshibandi, Sohbat of the 15th of February, 2014.

Do not be lazy. Have the attributes of a lion. Be like a lion in front of the door of the Sultan. Be honorable. What came from heaven is Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Al-hamdu lillahi rabbil 'alamin. Our Allah, our Subhan, our Sultan. Thanks to Allah, thanks. What we are asking from Allah is faith, religion and faith, salvation, Islam, light. Our Master is the Sultan. The lights in which He dressed our master He may give us also. May it be for the honor of Ummuhan Sultan (Hala Sultan). May our honor be great. We should not be lazy. What we eat and drink shall be turned into light. May they give us strength for worshipping our Mawla. Allahu Allah Allahu Rabbi Ma li siwahu illa Hu.

Greetings. May our day bring goodness. O lovers of Shah Mardan, we shall laugh and be cheerful. Let us be cheerful. We are asking for servanthood to our Lord; to have love and desire for the Most Beloved. O Glorious Prophet. O Glorious Prophet. Who is connected to you and who is at your door won't waste away. Can it be? To waste away at the Sultan's door. You cannot be left a snotty kid at the door of the Sultan. Man finds honor at the door of the Sultan. Allahu Allah Allahu Rabbi ma li siwahu. illa Hu. O our Lord we are weak servants. Do not be weak. Do not be weak at the Sultan's door. The Sultan's door. Be cheerful. Be honorable. May we be dressed by the honor of the Blessed one. May our day be with goodness, my son. May it be blessed, may it be enlightened. May we be full of love for our Lord and His Most Beloved. Don't be weak. Don't be weak at the Sultan's door. Allahu Allah Allahu Allah Allahu Rabbi ma li siwahu.

Go ahead please O Shah Mardan. Such beauty, such honor. Welcome O lovers of Shah Mardan. Ask from the Sultan. Don't be lazy at the Sultan's door. Be a lion at the door of the Sultan. O our Lord, our Subhan, our Sultan. May we be dressed with the cloak of faith. May we be dressed with the cloak of Islam. May we be dressed with the cloak of wellness. May we be dressed with the cloak of honor, O our Lord. Allahu Allah.

Welcome O lovers of Shah Mardan. Allah Allah. How beautiful they are, how beautiful are the holy ones. My son Haji Mehmed, this attribute comes from heaven. The attribute that descends from heaven gives Man honor, gives Man miracles, gives Man bravery, gives Man beauty. Man can't become mature all of a sudden in the Sultan's presence. They will load you with love. They will load you with health. They will load you with faith. There can't be any trash at their door. Thanks to Allah, praises and glory. May Ummuhan Sultan cast her glance over us. It is refreshing to stay at the door of the great ones. Nothing can touch you. Everything will be at your command. Kun fa yakun (2:117) (Be and it will be) Allahu Akbar, so beautiful. This is their attribute. They are their Lords servants. When they say 'Be' it happens.

You are Subhan my Lord. You are Sultan my Lord. Nobody can be despised at your door. Nobody can be wretched in the presence of the Glorious Beloved. O our Lord, we are living by Your grace. Fill us with the love and affection of Your most Beloved. May we get enough. That is heaven. That is heaven. Ask for heaven. My Lord I am asking for heaven. My son Mehmed effendi; the people want to run after this material world's tastes, pleasures and enjoyment. O Shah Mardan. MashaAllah. How nice. Do not go after this world's dirt. Go after Shah Mardan's. Be a lion, be a lion. He has well-being and wellness. What comes to us with well-being and wellness? Faith comes. Faith fills us. Faith gives every beauty to Man. Allahu Allah Allahu Rabbi ma li siwahu.

May we get well, may wellness come to our bodies. Every beauty comes from the heavens. Do not be ugly! Be beautiful. Do not be lazy! Be well. Our Lord, our Subhan, our Sultan. May He give us strength, may we get strength. May we get strength for Allah's way. My son Mehmed effendi, may we receive for Allah. For what other reason would you receive? The world has gone mad. They are following something and they are saying, 'I want this'. The people are out on the streets without knowing what they want. So know! Don't ask for this world. Do not ask for this world. The world makes you ugly. The hereafter makes you honorable.

O our Lord, to be servant for You. May the health and joy of the Glorious Most Beloved come on us. Grant us from his honor, O my Lord. May we have grace, may we have grace. Do not be a wolf. A Man is different than a wolf. Have the attributes of Man. Don't take on the attributes of a wolf. For heaven's sake! You created us as Man. We are getting far from humanity. Do not get far, run to Humanity. Run to Humanity. Allahu Allah Allahu Rabbi ma li siwahu illa Hu. What would you do if the whole world was given to you? What would you do with it? What can you do?

Say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Be a Man. The Almighty Lord will accept those who call His great Name. You should also try to get accepted into the presence. Don't be trash. Don't be ugly. Don't be a wolf. Look for what is coming from heaven. Don't go after this world's carcass, it will run after you. The people have gone mad. The people have gone mad. No, don't be mad. Why should you be mad? Your Lord's grants and gifts for you are unlimited. Give thanks for it. Say: Thank you for Your grants, O Lord. May He make it more, may the Almighty Haqq make it more. It won't decrease, Allah won't make it less. He will make it more. "Wa ladayna mazid" (50:35) "In Our presence, it is getting more."- In Our favor, in Our kindness, in Our prosperity there is honor. There is comfort. Run after Him. O our Lord. O our Lord. Make us of the loved nation of Your Beloved Habib.

Do not upset your Prophet. Who upsets the Pride of the Universe is not human. He will be a carcass, worthless. Do not upset our Master! Do not upset the Sultan of the Sultans. Do not grieve him. The people - his nation is upsetting our Master. What a shame! It is not all right to upset our Master. What a shame! O Glorious Prophet. We are far from upsetting you. As you are our Almighty Mawla's Most Beloved, to upset you is not within a Man's ability. It is beyond humanity. Beauties are given to our Master. Go after him, you will be beautiful. Those who love and respect him are beautiful. They can't be ugly, can't be ugly. They will be in comfort, not uncomfortable. They will be honorable, not dishonored. O Glorious Prophet those who are connected to you will also be glorious and beautiful. Our Ummuhan Sultan is glorious, famous, beautiful. According to our Master's holy sayings - One word from him can't be two.

What beauty Almighty Allah gave to the Prophet. Let us be beautiful Mehmed. Let us be beautiful Mehmed, we should not be ugly. What is suitable for Man? Being beautiful. It is suitable for Man to be beautiful. It is not suitable for Man to be ugly. The ugly ones have been expelled. The head of the ugly ones is satan. Don't go after it or you will also be ugly. O our Lord, for the sake of Your Most Beloved, You granted us beauty. You let us love Your Most Beloved. O Glorious Prophet. He is the Glorious Prophet. Allah should not make us devoid of his love. Be in love! O Shah Mardan. O Allah. You won't get sick, won't feel sorrow. You won't suffer, you won't live the hardship of this world. Fill up with the love of the Glorious Prophet. He has everything. Fatiha.

Well done Mehmed, Way fakir. Way fakir. You are not poor you are rich, rich. Also the Prophet loves my grandson and his father too. He also loves the ones connected to him. May they also love us. Fatiha.

These few words belong to today. May our love increase our striving increase. May our strength, our faith increase. May our faith, our Islam increase. May our service for our Lord increase. May we have more love for the Most Beloved. May boredom go from us, it shall go to the mountains and stones. Fatiha.

If you would be given a mountain full of gold everyday, what would you gain? Look for what comes to your heart, look to your heart. Be with the Sultans of hearts. Let's say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Our worries will go, our sorrow will go. May our Lord forgive us. Forgive us O our Lord for the sake of Your Most Beloved, For the sake of Ummuhan Sultan, for the sake of saints and prophets.

Fatiha.
