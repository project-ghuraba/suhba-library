There is all kind of dirtiness there now. Before it was a clean city because it was a coastal city, etc.. All the dirty people of Europe coming there have made our people dirty, too. They should go the straightway, should stop the prostitution. In the event that they don't stop, this is the first warning, this flood.. They may be grateful that he didn't shake. This is the news reaching me. The people of Antalya may make shukur & make towba (repent). "Otherwise, I will shake that place" said the responsible one of that area. When he shakes like this, half of the people perish. They may run away from there now. Those hotels, that do these dirty things have made that place dirty. They may leave Antalya immediately & escape from there. Otherwise, it will fall down on their heads. (G.) You told us to visit the tomb of Abdal Musa Hadrat (in Antalya). You introduced him to us. ( M. )Neither Abdal Musa nor smart Musa can stop this (joke). What can we do?...When the flood news came, other news also reached us. They may be grateful that He didn't shake it because of their sins.

Great Antalya has become the center of prostitution. There are all kinds of dirtiness there. All that you can & can't imagine! Women men, men women. But tasteless! They are doing things that Allah does not accept. This is a warning! These people should leave this city. The Europeans coming there should leave the city, it will fall on their heads. This time he will shake. Antalya will be ruined, will go down. It will sink into the sea. This is the warning. Insha- Allah, I may say this news to the Turkish people here.

Istanbul has also become tasteless. Izmir has become tasteless.. All the coastal cities have become dirty. They polluted the sea as well. This is what the one they worship wanted. He made people like this & left. His grave is not known.( G.) I am following from the internet. You said, they didn't even perform his janaza prayer. (M.) You watched it? (G.) I am following every day. I am following Saltanat TV every day. (M.) Masha Allah, Allah blesses you! He left (died) on the 8th (of November). He left on the 8th. (G.) I am following everyday Mawlana. (Religious Affairs Department in Turkey) I forgot his name. ( G.) Did they perform his janaza prayer? The head of Diyanat at that time performed his prayer. What was his name?
