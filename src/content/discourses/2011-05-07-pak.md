Hearts Pure

Mawlana Shaykh Mohamad Nazim al Haqqani an Nakshibendi

5 Jumādā ath-Thāniya 1432 A.H. Saturday, May 7, 2011 Translation by: http://saltanat.org/

BismillahirRahmanirRahim

May Allah make your final destination on the good way. May you live with dignity and honor; with faith and deen. May you go to Allah's Presence with your face clean and enlightened. Pray like this: Oh our Lord, enlighten our faces and purify our hearts. Say it!

Guest: Enlighten our faces and purify our hearts.

This is it! This is a sharp dua. It is what has been inspired to me. “Oh our Lord, enlighten our faces and purify our hearts.” This is an accepted dua (maqboul); and for whoever says it, the heavenly light in his/her face increases. If there is trouble in his heart, it goes away, and the person finds honour in the Divine Presence.

Madad ya Sultan al Awliya. Ask madad (support) from saints. Don't do anything without Bismillah. Start reading with BismillahirRahmanirRahim. They [visiting Turkish women, to whom Mawlana is speaking] should not run after studying [attending college]. They should not lose their naseeb.

Guest: They do not go to universities. These ones are not studying, they are all housewives.

Advise your children, always to come to me. "Oh Shaykh Efendi, our children are going to university. That is why we came here. We heard about you and wanted to visit you." I say "Welcome". Whether or not they go to school to learn, there is no work left for those who study. They cannot be employed by the government because it is saturated [no more jobs]. If you put 1 teaspoon of sugar into this [glass of water], it dissolves. You may also put 2 or 3 spoons; they melt. 5 or 6 spoons, it still may be dissolved. If you put 7 spoons, there will be some sugar left on the bottom. 8 spoons do not dissolve. 9 spoons do not dissolve. When you put 10 spoons, it does not dissolve anymore. This means that this water can handle 3 spoons [of sugar]. After this, the sugar starts settling. This is called the saturation state, where the water is saturated. The water cannot dissolve sugar anymore. The government is also saturated. It cannot employ any more [university graduates]. Do you understand?

That’s why the ones who send their girls to university will not be saved on Judgment Day. And the ones who educate their daughters with men [in mixed schools] - their punishment on the Day of Judgment will be doubled. You cannot compare it with the punishment here, which is nothing. On the Day of Judgment, Allah Almighty will ask: “This girl was sitting with this boy? Was this halal or haram for her? Why did you send these girls amongst the boys? Where is your chastity, where is your virtue? Did I order you like this? Did I say: you may sit men and women together? Or did I order you to watch your virtue, dignity?” There is no escape for them. They will suffer the punishment, no matter what. When I speak, it is like speaking to the walls. They listen to what shaytan says but not to Allah Almighty's orders. Do they do good? Is there anyone who does good? They all have sorrows, troubles. Their hearts are broken, their bodies are twisted.

Guest: There are so many ill people.

There are so many illnesses. Why? Because they are against Allah's order. If they keep the Order and become housewives, Allah Almighty gives heavenly light to their faces and health to their bodies. He gives abundance and blesses their husbands. That will be a blessing for their children, in their homes.

There is no one who listens, finished! If I speak to the wall, it may listen; but these people do not listen. La hawla wa la quwwata … Good! They will suffer.

Ladies come here to complain: “Oh Shaykh Efendi, is my daughter's chance to get married blocked for some reason?” I say: “Why blocked?” She says: “She is not getting married, Shaykh Efendi.” I ask: “How old is she?” She says: “Well, 28 years old.” She is actually about 30 - or older; but she is ashamed to say this. So she says 28. Girls can get married at the age of 14. Look, this one… [Mawlana gestures toward his daughter – Hajjah Nezihe.]

Hj. Nezihe: My father gave me in marriage when I was 15.

Now she has become a grandmother.

I asked: “Why didn’t you have your daughter get married?” She said: “She was going to go to college to complete her education.” I said: “You did well! She completed her education but missed her naseeb [chance for husband].”

A girl's naseeb comes to her door. They say: “Your daughter has a suitor.” He waits, waits... When she doesn’t take her chance, he walks away; and it is very hard for it to come back. That chance does not come back. At that time, you should accept the man you like. Give her in marriage. After that you have to take the man you don't like. This is my advice to all the girls -who are like my daughters and granddaughters. I give them this advice. Grandshaykh told me like this; but no one listens. I may read on them; but they go to college. I ask: “Why did you come here?” "My girl is studying, my granddaughter is studying here." Even your son didn't study here! How can you send your daughter here? Is she studying anything? It is like a hadra. It is so crowded, full of girls and boys together. Is this an educational system? Girls and boys study together! How can this happen?!

And they remain like this – without marriage. The order in society has become corrupt because the suitable time for marriage has passed for this girl. [Later] their marriages start ill-tempered, with aggressiveness; and end with divorce. Their children go to one side; they go to the other side. They have ruined the generation for the last 100 years. The ones who make these laws [for obligatory education] may not have 100 days left. I am making a bad dua on them. Allah knows. Allah knows.

Be careful, do not go outside at night. I don't like ladies or girls outside at night time. It is dangerous. There is danger. Men should not leave the ladies alone at home at night. The men should not walk around in streets. They must watch and protect their homes. Women look after their children and the husband should be with them in the home. If someone comes to the door and knocks, he must know there is a man at home. If he wants to do anything, there is a man inside to protect the home. If he does not find a man...There are so many evil, wicked people. The man should be there to defend the home and household. He should be armed to defend himself. It is a right from Allah Almighty to be able to overcome an attacker.

Guest: Is this an indication that a time like this will come?

We are already in that time. What are you waiting for? Close your door after dark. Turn off your lights after Isha. Don’t make too much noise in the house. Everybody should take their rest. And recite Ayat al Kursi four times in the four directions. Read and send it up and down. And also read 7 Ayatul Kursi on yourself.

And recite Al Hafiz Anta Al Hafiz 100 times. (You protect us, oh our Lord.) Say: We entrust our deen, our faith, our honour and our virtue, our children, old and young, to You. Take ablution before going to bed. Recite at least 2 pages from Quran; and then sleep. Then they watch over you. After that, guards come to you.

Allah Allah Allah. We ask forgiveness.

Guest: You want us to leave Istanbul. [The ladies] ask: How can we leave Istanbul? Where can we go?

You can go to the countryside. For those who live within the old borders of Istanbul, there is safety. There are a lot of mosques. Beyond those borders, all of the new areas they built are dangerous.

Guest: What do I do? How is my neighborhood?

You may dance. She asks me, “What do I do?”

Guest: I ask for our neighborhood. May we continue living there?

You may take a bell and dance.

Guest: We have such a Shaykh [Mawlana], we don't need to go anywhere.

OK. What are they asking? [Mawlana asks the leader.]

Guest: The Cekmece residents are asking what they should do.

Listen to what I am saying. Correct your situation and remain in your homes. Special angels are appointed to protect you. You should not fear! They are building a lot of new concrete apartments. [Mawlana gestures to indicate high buildings.] All of them are in danger. When it does like this [Mawlana demonstrates a building falling over, as happens in earthquakes] – finished! No one survives. People who came from Anatolia should return to their homes; or the Black Sea may swallow them. The Sea of Marmara as well … Marmara is not safe. It is a sea that boils from a volcano below. If it erupts, it will be dangerous. It ruined Golcuk (in 1999). We seek refuge in Allah. May Allah protect us.

If you follow our way say, “Ya Hafiz" [Oh our Protector] 100 times everyday. Nothing will happen to you.

Guest: Also there are youngsters called to the army. They are going to Shirnak [a south-eastern area with frequent battles]. They sent this gift for you. They ask for your dua. They should say: Hasbuna Allah Rabbuna Allah and then they should not fear.

[The group renews their bayat.] Our Lord is Allah Jalla Jalaluhu. Our Prophet is the Last Prophet, Muhammad Mustafa (sas). We accept this; and we accept Sultan Al Awliya as our shaykh - Shaykh Abdullah Daghestani. Allah Almighty accept us to Your Divinely Presence with an enlightened face. Oh our Lord, enlighten our faces and purify our hearts.

Don't fear. Say: Rabbuna Allah Hasbuna Allah and don’t fear. Fatiha.

Everybody, should keep enough food in your homes for 40 days, at least for 40 days - such as: rice, salt, butter, soap, etc. Store essential things like this.

Guest: Yes, we watched from internet. We did this, all of us.

You should do this. When you store enough for 40 days, it may last for 3x forty days [120 days].

Don't fear. Also pray for me. May Allah not tie us to our bed.
