As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa raḥmatu Llāhi wa barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-Ṣalātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīn wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

Our ProphetNone is saying, "At the end of times nothing will remain of Islām except its name. You will be many but without any value," says our ProphetNone. These days are those days, the days of the end of times, the time described by our ProphetNone. One third of the world is Muslim but they are worthless, not valued by anyone. They have nothing, no benefit because we've become a toy in the hands of shayṭān. Muslims of this time are like this. They've left the right way. Whatever wrong ways there are, they've chosen them. They've lost their way.

There is a saying in Turkish: "A man angry with a priest breaks his fast." That is what the priest wants, for you to break your fast, for you to lose your way. This is what shayṭān wants, for you to not be on the way. Why are they angry? They are swearing at ṣaḥābah. They get angry with each other and swear at Ahlu l-Bayt. The situation is like this, just as the soldiers of shayṭān want. Without them even doing anything they are upsetting each other.

These are the days mentioned by our ProphetNone. And these days will have an end. Our ProphetNone gave good tidings about it. Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām will come and make this disorder right. He will make everyone know their place. He will put them on the right way. Otherwise, it's not working. Muslims are brothers. Muslims should love other Muslims. The blood, property and chastity of Muslims are ḥarām for each other, says our ProphetNone.

They are not doing what heNone said in Khuṭbatu l-Wadā‘. They are doing the opposite. They are saying Muslims blood is ḥalāl, property is ḥalāl, chastity is ḥalāl. They are calling ṣaḥābah kāfir. If we call saḥābah kāfir, what shall we be then? We should be infidel then. If ṣaḥābah are kāfir, we are just infidel. People became so confused that it cannot be worse. Therefore, we get upset from one side and we don't approve of it but from other side, we are hoping that the days mentioned by our ProphetNone are near. And we are praying to meet him (as), in shā’a Llāh.

These days are bad days indeed. These are difficult days because billions of people who are Muslim fight each other, upset each other, and are enemy for each other. May AllāhNone send Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām very soon. There is no other du‘ā’ to make. These days, as ProphetNone saying, the last days for this world. Judgement Day it is near. ProphetNone saying, "You will be for Muslim too much but you don't have any value, you are like tide of sea – quickly going you cannot – nothing – like bubble. No any value, no any power for you. You are too many but no value for you.

It is nearly Muslim people in this world, maybe one third of the world they are Muslim but not have any power from... Economic power it's nothing. The most important to not play with you. We don't have any value. We don't have any power. They are like toy in hand of non-believer and hand of shayṭān and his army. They are not fighting us, only they make us to fight to each other, hate each other, making trick for each other, dividing us. This is what happen this days.

And there was Turkish saying is saying, somebody from we have, fasting Muslim once he getting angry for priest and he break his fast. When you are angry with him and he is you make break your fast he will be much happy because make you to make sin. Like shayṭān also if you are angry for shayṭān and you are say, "I will break my fast," he is be angry or he will be happy? He will be happy. This is what happen now. There are people they are angry with each other, they are swearing for ṣaḥābah one side, other side they are swearing for Ahlu l-Bayt – our ProphetNone generation, his family. And what happen? It's both of them same. One of them said for ṣaḥābah, "They are kāfir." This is what we see. And if they said this for ṣaḥābah – they are kāfir non-believer – we must be no any belief for us because they was the source of belief, they are real believer. You cannot say anything for them. But this is what this our days Muslim they are doing for each other.

And we are sad for this situation but from other side we are we have good tiding from ProphetNone. He said, "When time like this, no hope from anything it will come, HeNone will Allāh, HeNone will send for you one to save you. He is from my generation and he will be full of justice and barakah for this world – Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām." This is our belief and it is this days I think because really no more, no days like this it was for believer, for Muslim people – the worst days for believer. They are everywhere they are cutting, killing each other without any mercy, without any fear. And ProphetNone saying for every time and especially in speech of Wadā‘, this is last one he was making in ḥajj, heNone saying, "Your blood it is ḥarām to blood of Muslim to other cannot. His property, his women with everything it is not ḥalāl for Muslim." But they are now doing wrong, exactly opposite what ProphetNone saying. So don't be worry, in shā’a Llāh, time it is very near for Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām. We are hoping in shā’a Llāh. Mawlānā was saying morning and or evening he will come, in shā’a Llāh. We hope like this, In shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone make us to be with him, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
