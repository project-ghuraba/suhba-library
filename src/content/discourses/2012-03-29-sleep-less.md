Audhu Billahi min al shaytan al rajim, Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Assalatu wa s-salamu ala Rasulina Muhammad Sayyidi l Awwalin wa l-Akhirin. Dastur ya Sayyidi. Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fil jamia'. This is the last one of the sohbat series: things that a mureed should be careful with most: to eat less, speak less & also to sleep less.

When we say "sleep less", to sleep 8 hours maximum- which is more than enough for people. This is the limit. For a mureed it should be less - can be up to 5-6 hours. Because sleeping..Allah granted everyone an istid'ad-capability- some can survive on 3-4 hours of sleep or some with no sleep at all. Whereas some cannot survive on even 7 hours of sleep. But even if they can't they should definitely not sleep more than 8 hours. Allah divided the day, 24 hour, into 3 parts: 8 hours for sleeping-rest, 8 hours for worshipping and 8 hours for the dunya work.

Of course when you do things for pleasing Allah, even the dunya work if you say "I do for pleasing Allah" it is considered as worshipping as well. Sleeping is like this too but everything has an adab- proper way. Mashayikh izam-great saints divided the night again into 3 parts: they wake up for tahajjud when two thirds of the night has passed. They spend this time with worshipping.

How do they do this? They go to bed early. For example, if one goes to bed by 10 pm, or 11 pm latest, 1-2 hours after Isha & wakes up at 4 am.. Allah's Wisdom, Allah granted for man the best of everything. The doctors also say, the restorative & healtiest sleep for body is between 11 pm - 3am. In this 4 hours, the physical body gets the most rest. But what do the people do now? They sometimes stay awake till 1 am, or 2 am, being busy with unnecessary things. Before there were TVs. Now there are computers which are worse than TV. They get busy with them. Then of course, leave tahajjud. They can't even wake up for fajr prayer. They pray fajr when they wake up. When one sleeps at 2 am, he already can't get enough sleep. If he is sleeping 8 hours, will wake up by 10 am. So they wake up by 10, 11 in the morning, and can't get enough sleep.

Therefore Islam makes people do things that their ego doesn't like. But what their ego doesn't like is good for both their soul & body. In the early times that Prophet (sas) first started conveying Islam he (sas) was on his feet all night long praying, that his feet would swell. After this, the Holy Verse came that "you are up all night long, no need for this much, it is enough if you are up half of the night, or one third of the night". Such a Holy Verse has come. Allah (awj) ordained how much man can carry & said accordingly. 8 hours is quite enough for man. Don't sleep even 5 minutes more than this 8 hours because even an extra 5 minutes harms your body. It harms physically. Spiritually, it harms even more. Of course this is for ordinary people. For sick people it is rest, different. For old people, again it is different. They need rest. But for normal people like you & me, it is not good to stay up till midnight and then sleep 9-10 hours. Meaning, it harms the person physically & spiritually. Therefore like we said, the orders are to eat less: when a person eats little, it keeps his body robust & active. When he eats more, it is reversed, it harms. When one speaks less it is good for him, everybody listens to what he says. But when one speaks too much, they say "this man talks too much". Sleeping is the same way. When you sleep less, it is enough. When you sleep too much, it harms physically & spiritually.

Today we speak about 3rd thing for Muslim, good to do. First it was for less eating & less speaking. Now, for less sleeping. This is important thing. For Allah divided day, the 24 hours, into three parts. Each part, 8 hour- 8 for sleeping, 8 for ibadah-worshipping, 8 for working. Even it is, when you do this for Allah all of this, Allah He is Generous, He writing as worshipping. Even sleeping, even working.. When you do niyah for Allah all Alhamdulillah hasanat-good deeds. But for sleeping, many people they have different body. Some of them maybe 5 hour is enough for them, 6 hour enough for them, 7.. But nobody need more than 8 hours. And night time also, awliya and ulama-scholars, they make it 3 parts. 3 part: after maghreb-sunset until tahajjud, 2 parts- 2 thirds. After the third third, it is day sleep. After Isha, maybe 1 hour, 2 hour, and wake up for tahajjud. When they do this, this is better and new modern people also, they say the best time to sleep from 11 o'clock until 3 o'clock. 4 hour this is the best. When you sleep this, it is maybe as you sleep 8 hours. Body taking all rest.

But now people, nowadays people everything shaytan making good for them, to not sleep. Even before it was TV, they were watching TV until 1 or 2 o'clock. Now there is computer, maybe now for 4 o'clock not sleeping. When become tahajjud, they sleeping. And this is, when they sleep 4 o'clock, or 3, or 2 o'clock, they can't wake up before 12 o'clock. Because not resting, body. And Allah, what order, good for people. But for nafs-ego, not good. Ego like like a small boy, they want everything- good, bad, all thing- ego like this.

But Allah, what He order as you eat less, your body will be good. If you eat more, body will be ill. Speaking much, people they don't like you. Speaking too much-they run away. When you sleep more, body also become ill. More than 8 hour this is become illness for body- Spiritual and physical also. So limit until 8 hours, sleeping. Maybe somebody, 7 hours, 6 hour, he can sleep. Before, first of times of Islam, Prophet (sas), whole night they were not sleeping, worshipping. Even their feet coming like this, from standing up. So Allah (awj) making Ayat-Verse in Quran. He said: We know all night you are worshipping. But maybe half of this is enough, even one third of this is enough. So for this awliya, they take the third of the night. The end third of the night, they come up, not sleeping until after ishraq.

So this modern time,it is really light everywhere and TV & computer. Before people, no light, nothing. When after Isha they can close it, and sleeping. Waking very early. To wake early, not sleep after fajr to go work after fajr, the most barakah time this is. Barakah, even barakah by time also. When you do this, you feel like day not finishing. For rizq-provision also. This is best time, to not sleep after fajr. Insha Allah, Allah help us to do this. Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, Al Fatiha.
