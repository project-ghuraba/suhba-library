Allah Allah. Allah Allah. Allah Allah. hasbunAllah. HasbunAllah wa n'imal wakil n'imal Mawla wa n'iman naseer ghufranaka Rabbana ilaika l-maseer.

O my Lord, increase Your beloved one's glory and honour, light and gladness, and contentedness and majesty, and whoever follows him from the prophets and saints wa s-saliheen, ya Rabb al-alameen. Hu Rabbana, qawwi da'afi fi ridak. O our Lord, we are weak servants. Give us, O our Lord, our Creator, much more strength for standing up for heavenly obligatories. We know we are weak ones. As You Almighty saying:

"Wa khuliqa-l-insanu da'ifa" And man is created weak (4:28)

Irham da'afana (Mercy on our weakness). As you said, O our Lord, that man created weak. You grant us from heavenly support and sufficient, to be stand up for Your holy commands; to keep them and to follow Your heavenly commands. O our Lord, please write our names under the table of good ones, good servants. If our names just written under bad servants, we are asking forgiveness and Your blessings. We are saying our salam, salute for our world's commander, Qutb al-Mutassarif who looking after everything on behalf our Lord's Command. May Allah forgives us.

Ask forgiveness and you should find your Lord forgiving and granting from the endless Mercy Oceans, from His endless Blessing Oceans. Look yourself, O mankind, and don't claim these useless means that you are strong ones or you are victorious ones or you are powerful ones because you have so many aspects from this life. Don't think that material aspects giving to you power. No. Don't believe. Don't trust on people and don't believe that material aspects supporting you.

Don't trust people who is heedless one, or ignorant one. Whom they are trusting mankind they are heedless ones. And also those no-mind people, they are those whom they are trusting for material aspects and they are getting proud with big amounts of treasures. Those people also unlucky people, no-mind people and losing their chance in Divinely Presence. O people, our attenders, don't trust your imitated titles. Leave that. Don't trust that on material aspects. If trusting on material aspects, they are so foolish ones, no-mind ones and their levels under the level of animals. Even those people going to be cheated. Cheated with their dressings, cheated with their nonsense titles. Yes. Now even you may go to an office from government offices and when you are entering for interview or to speak for something, you are looking and seeing a person sitting behind that table, on a chair. And in front there is a writing: 'chief commissioner', Mr. Foolish One. Or written on it: 'director of this big building,' Mr. Monkey. Or you are entering and looking, a very fat person sitting behind a table, very good decorated. And his chair maybe enough for 3 ones to sit on it but it is only for himself. And in front of it, not to him, to who coming inside, written: Mr. Chairman 'Limited Company's Chairman': Mr. Elephant.

Getting in it and you are finding a person sitting, never smiling, very, very very, very proud one and if anyone entering inside, showing to him that he is so proud and never saying "Sit down," but saying "Look where you are, here, in front of which one?" It is written on that: Mr. Chimpanzee I, through monkey monkey territory. And another one sitting on behind or on left hand of that one And it is written on it table also: 'The Second Chairman for the Monkey's Territories, Prime Minister' Mr. Orangutan. Or entering to another office for asking something, and you are entering and looking, very angry person sitting and its chair maybe for three ones.

Sitting on it and putting his eyeglasses like this and looking "Who are you? Whom?" "Your Excellency I am..." "You are a servant. What do you want?" "I want to reach a Monkey's Area in Southern Africa to be there Governor of Chimpanzees' goverment's chairman. Yes sir." You, you my servant, please write for that person that he is one of Deputy, asking to be a Deputy through the Jungles of India, to be there that his order must go on. write a writing and put my symbolic (stamp on it) and send him away." You're understanding what I'm saying? Yes, that is a reality. Whom they have some power, some power in their hands, they are looking people downstairs and looking themselves upstairs.

Therefore looking, "Who is that one?" And he is saying "I am Mr. Cock". "What do you want?" "I am coming for a complaint, for complaining about..." "About whom?" "About foxen." "Foxen? What they are doing?" "Always running after cocks and hunting them and eating them." "And we are asking justice for that, please write for that person our order that I am the King of Jungles ordering to wolves. They must be very, very keenly looking for jungles [public security] - to keep, to keep everyone their levels. That is my order to wolves, if they are meeting foxen running after a cock, you must reach and catching that fox and throw it away. If dying, dying. If not dying, never coming back and making trouble for cocks and hens also".

Ok? You understand? Now all countries you may find such people, they are thinking themselves that they're special ones through jungle of this world and they can do everything. And they can do trouble for everyone but they are never happy to be in trouble by anyone. Even from ants. Yes. Why we are saying this, O our attenders? Because you are not understanding heavenly knowledge. They are making me to speak on your level, level of animals, level of jungle animals. I can't speak up because our attenders, they never taking any care. Therefore I'm saying their real personalities. Personalities that it is under the level of animals also. And their personalities belonging some of them to Australian deserts or some of them Indian jungles, some of them African jungles, and some of them through deserts people level I must speak to you because you are not understanding heavenly declaration. You are never giving any value. I am making theatre for you today. Yes. You are so proud mankind. Shaitan blowing through everyone from heedless mankind that you are important VIP one, you must not be happy to be an ordinary person. You must be always VIP level.

If they are saying this VIP position, we are coming down and showing their egoistic levels through living ones on this planet. The Lord of Heavens calling people, calling you to His Divinely Presence. But you are insisting to be on this planet to be VIP ones. Take your VIP ranks. And who asking to be VIP level on this planet, they must, their names through heavens just appointed from heavens as a wolf, as a fox, as a snake, as a bear, as a tiger, as a wild animal. People now insisting to be on the level of animals.

Very few people asking to reach VIP people through heavens. Their interest only for this life. Take it. But it is going to be written on you, "That was representative of wolves." "That is representative of all foxen". "That is representative of donkeys". Or they are so-and-so representative. Why you are not trying to be heavenly representative on earth? And always you are going to be angry with me because I am saying to you "Look up". Ask heavenly ranks, heavenly honor. And all of you running imitated honors through imitated titles. No.

Therefore we are saying this today. They are making me to say to you in such a way. Whom they are following their egoistic demands, they are on the level of animals, on level of jungle animals. If you are asking honor, honor that should be for you from pre-eternal up to eternal, you may ask heavenly honors. And heavenly honors never reaching to you till you are following one heavenly personality, heavenly being. And they are messengers of the Lord of Heavens and their real followers, holy ones. If you are asking real honor, follow them. If you are asking honor for this world, then your stations with that jungle people stage and levels.

O salafi ulamas, don't be angry. Welcome, how are you today? You are happy what we are saying now? Do you think that we are speaking wrong? You can't say this. Whole Prophets coming for what? For what? They are saying "We are ulamas". The Lord of Heavens, for what created mankind? Say? Say to people to learn and to know. Beginning from sultans or presidents or prime ministers, or this one or that one whom they are carrying imitated ranks, that it is only up to graveyard. Not forward that graveyard.

Why you are not reminding people? "O people, you are not created to run after imitated titles or to run after nonsense treasures. Why they are nonsense? Because you are going to leave them. When you are leaving this life, you are leaving your huge treasures and you are going only with your white kafans. Why you are not saying this matter also every Juma, every Friday? It is not written through Holy Qur'an? How you are reciting Holy Qur'an? How you are reciting the story of Qarun? Why you are not saying to muluk, to presidents, to kings about the Qarun's treasures? And about himself that his name mentioned through Holy Qur'an?

Why you are not making people to remind? Why? Every Juma you must say. Even daily you must say to people:

"Wa dhakkir" And remind (51:55)

Holy command of the Lord of Heavens. You must remind people what it is good for them and what it is cheating them and worse for them. That is another goal. And daily they are shooting to you some mermi (projectile), not bullet, but big ones, that artillery throwing. Artillery shells. Everyday coming to you. Why you are not saying, daily declarations that you are giving to people? Say, if not saying, be fear from the Lord of Heavens that He should ask you why you are not saying to My servants to know what is reality and which thing their Lord asking from them?

O ulamas, doctors of sharia and ulamas Azhari sharif and salaf us-salih. You are making dunya on your heads and salaf us-salih, they are putting earth under their feet. How you are claiming that "we are salafi ulamas"? May Allah forgive us. I am sorry to say such things. But it is heavenly orders. I must say. And I am addressing to my ego first and anyone who interesting may accept it, may follow it, he should take benefit here and hereafter. May Allah forgive us Ameen. Fatiha. Their names written on heavens, whom they are attending and whom they are not attending. Attenders, their stations just VIP stations. Others in stations, stations of animal-stations. They may choose. Doesn't matter. Fatiha.
