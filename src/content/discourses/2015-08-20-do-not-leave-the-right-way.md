Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi Rasulillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

May Allah not, as He need not, help anybody do works that are not right. There are some things people put in their minds, and think they are doing good but they are not doing good. This applies to everything, not only to worldly matters. In wordly matters, it might still be acceptable if the person is praying (namaz) and supplicating (dua). They might think something is right, but that thing they think is right might harm them.

However, for the hereafter... What is important is the hereafter. People chose a path while going through great suffering in the world. That way can also be the wrong way. They put all their efforts and all their strength for a wrong path. In the end, they realize they are doing something that has no value in the hereafter. But it is too late at that moment.

When people put something in their mind, most of the time they think it is right. That there becomes the predestination of Allah. He puts them in that path. That is why we say: May Allah show us the good, may he show us good things and good people. May Allah show us all the good, may He put them in our heart, and may we go in that way. May we go after the right person and do the right things.

He says, “Allahumma alhimna rushdana.” Meaning show us good things and beautiful things. Do not make us put useless things in our mind and succeed in doing useless things. Do not even put them in us O Lord (Ya Rabbi).

Even if all the people were on the right path, it has no benefit for Allah. And if they were on the wrong path, it does not harm Allah. Its benefit and harm are for people’s selves. Allah showed the right way. May we not leave the right way Inshallah.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq.

Al-Fatiha.
