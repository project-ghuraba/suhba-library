As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
أَلَا فِى ٱلْفِتْنَةِ سَقَطُوا۟ ۗ وَإِنَّ جَهَنَّمَ لَمُحِيطَةٌۢ بِٱلْكَـٰفِرِينَ
<br>“Alā fī l-fitnati saqaṭū wa inna jahannama la-muḥīṭatun bi l-kāfirīn”<br>
“Surely, they have fallen into trial. And verily, Hell is surrounding the disbelievers.” (Qur’ān 9:49). ṢadaqAllahu l-ʿAẓīm.

Those who fall into fitnah (discord), He says, they deserve hell. Fitnah means something that harms people. Fitnah is from shayṭān. The shayṭān doesn’t want people to rest. He wants to lead them all to hell. And the way to hell passes through fitnah. The way of fitnah leads to hell. Fitnah is something that harms. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla has cursed it. Our Prophet ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam said there is a curse upon those who instigate fitnah.

Therefore, one must be careful. Some people think they are doing good but instigate fitnah. Because of fitnah, they harm people. First and foremost, they harm themselves.

Fitnah has been a constant source of trouble since the time of our Prophet ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. Even since the time of Adam ʿalayhi s-salām. But most recently, during the time of our Prophet ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, there were great fitnahs. These fitnahs caused harm to those who caused them. And those who were subjected to the fitnah, even if it caused them harm in this world, their reward in the hereafter is great.

Therefore, you mustn’t fall into fitnah. You must be vigilant. The shayṭān is luring you into a trap. The shayṭān’s helpers are luring you into a trap so that all the good deeds, thawāb and rewards you have will be taken away. And on top of that, they will burden you with a great deal of sin so that you are guaranteed to go to hell. May Allah protect us.

We are living in the end times. Our Prophet ṣallAllahu ʿalayhi wa-sallam said that the fitnahs of the end times are like the darkness of night. Therefore, there is no need to dwell too much on what is done. What Allah says will happen. Whatever happens, whether you like it or not, you will wait for when Allah will send the savior to save you.

You cannot save yourself. If you want to save yourself, simply don’t fall into fitnah. Don’t get involved with anyone, don’t follow anyone, and you will find salvation from shayṭān and his followers. That’s why, Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim used to say, when there is fitnah, when there is great fitnah, do not leave the house and do not listen to anyone.

May Allah protect us from fitnah. May Allah protect us all from the fitnah of shayṭān and from the fitnah of the end times, and grant us all vigilance, inshā’Allah.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
