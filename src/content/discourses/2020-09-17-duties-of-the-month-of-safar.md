Aū‘dhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-Shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Waṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-F’āiz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Today is the last day of muharrem a month has passed in our hijri calendar. last year also passed quickly 1441 passed and now we’re in 1442 last year strange things happened nobody was able to go to haj either those who were able to go don’t count the haj was shut off for people this year we shall see inshallah how it’s going to become how Allah wills it So, I hope this Safar month will pass comfortably. There is also a vazifeh for it, and the most important thing is sadaqah. Sadaqah is important, after that three Shahadahs and seventy Astaghfirullahs are to be recited, because it is necessary to ask forgiveness of Allah for what we knowingly or unknowingly used to do. God forgives and erases our sins, and when we wipe them away, we will not be punished. There is also seven times Surat ul-Fil. It should be read seven times a day. Sadaqah should be given, and as we already mentioned, three Shahadahs and seventy Astaghfirullahs. After that, there is no need to go here and there unnecessarily. This is important. These are easy things. Allah has shown ease in everything for us, and it will be easy, insha’Allah. I hope the month of Safar is beneficial to us and to His Ummah Muhammadiyah.

May Allah protect us and His Ummah Muhammadiyah from all kinds of misfortunes, evil, unseen accidents, jinns, magic and spells. And may He send us the Owner. As we said, last year passed with such and such, and we approach closer to the time of Sayyiduna al-Mahdi (AS) and Akhirah. Since we are living in the End Times, these issues are getting closer and closer. Things that have happened are things that seem so rare in human history. Allah considers the situations we live in. Allah assigned them to us, and insha’Allah, He will reward us, and He will reward the patient. Nothing goes to waste for believers, but those who oppose Allah suffer and will not benefit. May Allah guide us and give strength to our faith. Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-F ti ah
