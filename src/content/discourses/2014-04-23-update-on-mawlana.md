Subhanim Allah Sultanim Allah Nabim Muhammad Alayhi's-Salam Subhanim Allah Sultanim Allah Nabim Muhammad Alayhi's-Salam

Salamu alaykum wa rahmatullahi ta'ala wa barakatuhu I would like to thank Allah Almighty for accepting our prayer, and accepting our sadaqa, and accepting our good intention. Alhamdulillah, that Allah Almighty He have His own viewing on us, not our behaving, so we are hoping that Allah Almighty will look for our good side, will look for our sincerity, He will look to our love to Mawlana Shaykh.

I like to say first of all, Alhamdulillah, Mawlana Shaykh he's improving. As you know Mawlana Shaykh he was in very, very, very, very, very critical now he is only very critical. Not too much very, but he still in intensive care, and he's still under medication treatment and he's still responding and even Subhanallah, the doctor, they say he is willing to be with us, and this is good tiding for all of us. I am saying keep praying, keep loving your shaykh, and keep your heart with your Shaykh.

It is important for us to know that our Shaykh is alive. Today, my nephew he was telling me, Lefke, it is empty without my grandfather. I say, no, the world will be empty without your grandfather. This is, you have to know, it's Haqq. Alhamdulillah, he is filling up our world, he is filling up our soul, he is filling up our body, and he filling up everything in us. Alhamdulillah, we have the taste of the life with him, we have the taste of living with him, we have the taste of the love with him.

And I'm only saying Ya Rabbi, don't make us to be yateem (orphaned) from all this beauty things. Don't make us to be not deserved this things. We know we don't deserve but Ya Rabbi You're knowing better we are weak servant and we are bargaining you, Ya Rabbi we say we are beggar, Ya Rabbi we are beggar for our Mawlana Shaykh life, for our Mawlana Shaykh health, and for our Mawlana Shaykh to be with us.

We are beggar Ya Rabbi, we can say. We are beggar Ya Rabbi and we are not shy to say we are (?)alone and begger, to say Ya Rabbi, we want Mawlana Shaykh to be healthy, to be in good shape and to be coming to Allah Almighty with better shape, and to come to Mahdi alyhi's-salam time with better shape and to guide us because without Mawlana Shaykh we are lost and we don't want to be lost after we are finding the Mercy Ocean of him and we don't want to be yateem, we don't be orphaned. We don't want to be orphaned, we are so weak. And Ya Rabbi, I'm asking from all the murid, from all the lover to Mawlana Shaykh that we are asking sincerely for his remaining with us, because we are such a people that we don't care about what happening, we don't care about fitna, we don't care about nothing, it is not our things, I know all the heart, it's coming together at the moment. Everybody, he leave his personal interest beside, we are one heart, one hand, and one, we are coming oneness in Mawlana Shaykh, Alhamdulillah, I'm feeling this, I'm seeing this, that's why I wanted to make updating if possible everyday about Mawlana Shaykh condition.

Alhamdulillah, I can say very clearly, very restfully that we are with Mawlana Shaykh and I can say that Mawlana Shaykh also with us. He is taking step to us but I like you to know that Mawlana Shaykh today's step, it is like baby step. His curing is very slow, but he is in the processing to be cured. Alhamdulillah, yesterday I see him and I can tell you I see him better than day before and the day before when I see him, I can tell you I see him better than day before. That's why it is good tiding when you see somebody improving, day-by-day, day-by-day, day-by-day.

And we are asking from Allah Almighty, Ya Rabbi keep our Shaykh for us. We are nothing without our Shaykh, we are breathing with our Shaykh, and we are living with our Shaykh. And I'm asking to continue du'a, to continue sadaqa for poor people, and continue for salatu l-hajat. This is the prayer for in-need. This is 2 rakat salatu l-hajat for in-need. Everybody must pray after Isha prayer, before he sleep, 2 rakat of salatu l-hajat, this is for the sake of in-need, that we in-need of Mawlana Shaykh and Ya Rabbi with 2 rakat we are doing, it is, maybe doesn't effect and doesn't make big effect, but do it.

We do it to show our intention, how we love Mawlana Shaykh and how we are caring about Mawlana Shaykh, 2 rakat salatu l-hajat. You take Allahu Akbar, you read Fatiha and then you read 3 Ikhlas (112) and you make ruku, sajdah, and then you come for the 2nd, in the 2nd one you read Fatiha and then idha ja-a nasrullahi wal-fath (110) then read, qul audhu bi rabbi l-falaq (113) and qul audhu bi rabbin-nas (114).

And then when you finish you make du'a specially, purposely for Mawlana Shaykh saying, Ya Rabbi, we want our shaykh to be back in good health we want our Shaykh to be in good shape, we want our Shaykh to come and beat us also. And to making joke with us and at least if only we see him going out and going in, this is more than enough, we're not asking more than this. To see him breathing's more than enough for us. And, wa min Allahi tawfiq bi hurmati l-Habib bi hurmati l-Fatiha.
