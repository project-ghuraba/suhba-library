Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

The state of this world is sometimes very difficult and sometimes comfortable for everyone. In both, we should be thankful to Allah ﷻ, we should praise Allah ﷻ. Allah ﷻ is with the person who is on the right way. If people say that they can do it themselves, they are wrong. They should certainly ask Allah ﷻ for help. They should beg Allah ﷻ so that they can spend this life in the best way possible. The best way is to serve Allah ﷻ. The most beautiful thing is not gold, silver or money. All of them are temporary, the permanent are the good deeds.

Therefore, the person who spends his life in dunyā in the best way is the one who fears Allah ﷻ, obeys Allah ﷻ, loves our Holy Prophet ﷺ and is on the right way. If people go according to their pleasure, it will never be of benefit. You should go on the way that Allah ﷻ loves so that it will be beneficial. When you are with Allah ﷻ, Allah ﷻ will definitely help you, your end will be good and the hardships in this world will be easy. With the help of Allah ﷻ, the truth becomes apparent, falsehood is shown as false and no harm will come to people.

As long as you are with the truth, never be afraid. In the end you are the winner. Even if it seems like they lost, the winners are those who are with Allah ﷻ. Because such things happen where there is oppression or the truth seems as false, then Allah Azza wa Jalla brings out the truth and the truth becomes apparent. Then, the honor and dignity of those who are with the truth will be restored. May our honor and dignity be high in the sight of Allah ﷻ; it is not important in the sight of people.

People are looking for shameful things, “How can I defame this person?” They’re looking for something. They do not seek goodness, they seek evil. Our Holy Prophet ﷺ says, “ُمْ كُ اَتوْ مَ ُنَ سِ احَ مَ ُاوُرُ كُ ذْ ا”, “Mention the virtues of your dead.” Our Holy Prophet ﷺ says, don’t say bad words even about the dead. If you know something good, say it. Wherever there is evil, people are trying to show it. May Allah ﷻ protect us. May we be with Allah ﷻ insha’Allah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
