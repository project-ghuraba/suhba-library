Sheikh Bahauddin Adil Al-Haqqani sohbat of the 23rd of September, 2014.

I am the second son of Sheikh Effendi, and the third child among the children. I am number three. Let me say that we are four siblings. Everybody knows that. For those who don't know, Sheikh Effendi has four children; two daughters, two sons. One of our sisters is in America, my brother in Istanbul, I and my sister are in Cyprus. We didn't leave Cyprus. Now being children of Sheikh Effendi for us is a feeling like walking on the edge of a blade. Because we have big responsibilities both worldly and in terms of our association.

Of course, it's not only in Cyprus, for example, there are many tariqas or sheikhs dealing with sufism throughout whole world. However, they either address their own nations, groups, or communities. But our Sheikh Baba, Allah bless him, as he was someone addressing the world directly, we have to learn the nature of every nation. We have to know what every nation likes and dislikes. We have to know what they are busy with, what they prefer, what they don't. Sometimes you can say a wrong word to someone, and it will be a reason for disappointing or breaking his heart. Therefore, we have to be more careful. We have this feeling of walking on the edge of a blade by being children of Sheikh Effendi.

Now Sheikh Baba actually was a very broad man. In such manner, people coming to Sheikh Baba got the benefit of his fatherhood. Therefore, in old times, they used to say a 'fatherly man', and Sheikh Baba is a person who comes in top of the fatherly men list. Now this is a very important question. Everyone's asking this. Actually, Sheikh Baba filled us so much that he didn't let us feel his absence. Moreover, we should accept it as a miracle that even his grave is at home. Therefore, he didn't give us that emptiness at all. For example, politicians complain about it mostly, they say they couldn't take care of their children well because of serving to people. That's why, Sheikh Baba was very competent in this matter.

Sheikh Baba was very humorous and his jokes were in the first place within the family or and private life. Like every Cypriot, he liked to tease also. Sheikh Baba didn't compromise being a Cypriot. Everyone should know this. One day, I was probably seven or eight years old, and Sheikh Baba used to tease the daughter of our friend. He used to say to her "(Your) Dad is mine. Dad is mine". The girl would cry saying "No, dad is mine". She was around my age. Then her father came and said "I will tease your son now. How dare you tease my daughter?" And he said "Bahauddin, (your) dad is mine". I looked at his face and started laughing saying, "You can't take my dad away from me". That's a nice memory. The man kept on saying he can't deal with us and that our family connection is very tight. We don't fear.

G: Did he have angry attitudes beside his humorous side?

SB: Of course he did.

G: What was he angry with most?

S.B. Injustice. He couldn't tolerate injustice. Because one of his names is called Haqqani, the one who shows justice. Therefore, politics sometimes appeared wrong to him, for example, our politics or the global politics. He used to say people should develop their humane sides and believed that they should preserve justice and law right. And he was very angry about it. If there was any injustice, Sheikh Baba couldn't tolerate it. He truly stepped firmly in this. No one could compromise Sheikh Baba in that.

Of course, Sheikh Baba, as we said, he was a Cypriot beside everything. And he used to tell the reaction of Cypriots because he thought like a Cypriot. And he lived in this country, and he loved this country. Because he always used to say "Allah looked at the face of Cypriots. He brought a great government after 1974 and rescued from Greek slavery" And Sheikh Baba said "Ya Hu, Allah helped you so much, at least thank Allah". Sheikh Baba thus... If we look into our Cypriots deeply, we will find either a mulla, or a haji, either a jamaa, or imam, or alim. However, the new generation separated from them and went in a different direction. What Sheikh Baba tried to explain is he wants us to come back to our origin.

But as a second question, what I think about Turkish Cypriots is that Turkish Cypriots are searching today. We live in society and see it. What is the society going towards? They go towards drinking. Why are they drinking? Now we have to ask a question- "Why does a man drink?" He should have a certain problem that he wants to forget. Whereas, our problem is a spiritual problem. People can't reach the material side of Turkish Cypriots today. It is a blessing of Allah. We say shukr. We have to say shukr. We have to say shukr for some things. If you ask me, as Turkish Cypriots are searching, they will find spirituality. Because it is important that you are searching. If a society starts to look for something, it means everyone will start going to an address. If one goes, he'll tell to his friend, brother, son, mother and father. He will lead them for sure.

Is spirituality bad now? No, it's not bad. At least we can stand on our feet with our spiritual power. When a nation doesn't have spirituality, suicides and depressions increase in that nation. They don't know what they want. Now in spirituality, a man at least knows what he wants. I can sum up Turkish Cypriots as unconscious. Not because they know but they follow fashion. One says he's a communist, and he drives the best Mercedes or the best car and lives in the best villa and says he's a communist. What communist are you? Now is this consciousness raising right? If you ask me, this consciousness is wrong. It certainly should come from the inside. This belief comes from the inside for all cases. When it doesn't come from the inside, that belief is not a belief. They say artificial, in English.

I truly appreciate the support of Turkey, the embassy and the ministry of religious affairs. But the issue here is this, they couldn't come to an agreement with Cyprus. They can't get into it. It is like you are you, I am I.

G: How can this be overcome?

SB: I will leave it to them. We have to leave everything to whom it belongs.

G: What are they doing wrong?

SB: Nobody's asking us, of course. That is wrong. Nobody's causing us to advance. That's another wrong thing. We are doing our service according to ourselves. We don't expect any salary from anyone. We don't expect any vote, we don't expect any support. We have activities we do for Allah's pleasure which are very active. At least we have a very good Cypriot association.

Shukr to Allah, society looks at Sheikh Baba with sympathy. And the new generation compared to the old one is far more understanding. Because for the old generation, nationalism, communism, materialism and dictatorship were dominating. But at the present time everyone can think what they want, they get what they want. In other words, people of this time are more alert. Therefore if the society is sympathizing it means it's on the right path.

Sheikh Baba in 2012 went through an illness. He broke off completely after that illness. Let me say it so, before 2012, he used to walk around in the derga and our last trip with Sheikh Baba was in 2005. After that, between 2005 and 2012, he was in Cyprus. What happened? After 2012, Sheikh Baba used to go out for a drive in the afternoon. People could see him only then. That's why, Sheikh Baba made us get used to the current situation. And that's one of the beautiful sides of Sheikh Baba - he showed us that beauty. Apart from this, as Sheikh Baba always lives in our hearts, we are getting used to not feeling his absence. You said it in your first question already; how is Sheikh Baba close to us, how are we together. Now we will go on with that feeling. But I have to express it like this: 1 million men cannot do what Sheikh Baba did. I have a nice memory.

May Allah bless him, Dr. Ertugrul Hasipoglu. We went to visit him in the Parliament. He was the head MP. We went with my brother-in-law. They were friends with my father-in-law. And he said "I surpassed my father". Dr. Ertugrul said "I surpassed my father. My father was not a doctor. I became a doctor. My father wasn't a politician. I became a politician. He wasn't an MP. I became a deputy. My father wasn't a minister. I became a minister. My father wasn't the head MP. I became the head MP. I surpassed my father". He said to my brother-in-law "You can do it as well because you achieved quite high level in your position" He then turned to me and said "Bahauddin, you have no chance to surpass your father". This is a very good summary. This is a good anwer for a question about replacing Sheikh Baba's position.

I remember one journalist lady asking Sheikh Baba "What role are you taking in introducing the Cyprus problem?" He said "Ya Hu, what is my address?" He asked the woman "What is my address?" She said "Lefke". "Where is Lefke?" "In North Cyprus". "And what am I doing here?" "So your living here and having this address is your full individual attempt to introduce the country, North Cyprus." Whereas Sheikh Baba, all those people were coming from all corners of the world and we know very well how difficult it was to come especially to North Cyprus. Just think, a man comes from Argentina and flies for 30 hours. In 30 hours a man comes to North Cyprus to visit his Sheikh. When he lands at the airport, they give him 3-5 days. A man flew for 30 hours. He paid a big amount of money in order to come to your country. They give him 3 days, 5 days. Before they wouldn't give at all.

Sheikh Baba never had any expectations like "Let me boast for doing this. I brought tourists, I brought 10 people, I brought 5 people." He used to say "I should do my duty at least". And Sheikh Baba always did his duty. And he never expected anyone's appreciation for that. But now we like dramatizing very much. Whenever we have a good excuse, we try to push it forward. Now Sheikh Baba transferred sheikhhood to my brother. He is the Sheikh of tariqa now. He is responsible for everything, for us. Normally he resides in Istanbul but in order to continue the mission of Sheikh Baba, he decided to stay in Cyprus and said "Our headquarters is here".

We have centers throughout the world and one of the biggest centers in London, it is a huge place. He didn't make it even there. He said "The headquarters is where Sheikh Baba is", and he spends approximately 15 days a month in Cyprus so that people are not told to go to Istanbul, the center is there, or to go to London, the center is there. He is trying to continue the mission of Sheikh Baba. New people are coming. People we never met are coming. The spiritual attraction of Sheikh Baba is bringing people including Cypriots - bus after bus for visiting. I want to add this as well: We made this place for Cypriots as well, it's a place to visit. At least he's a true man of Cyprus, he's Awliyaullah. It's his maqam. We want it to be known as spiritual maqam. And we want them to come and ask spiritually for their wishes, their prayers and whatever needs they have. Our doors are open for that as well. And welcome to everyone. Nobody should hesitate. It belongs to Cypriots.
