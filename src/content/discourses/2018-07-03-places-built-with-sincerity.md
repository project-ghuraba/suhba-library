Thank AllāhNone these kinds of places have been granted by the himmah and efforts of Muslims. These kinds of places are a means to guidance for people here. These types of places are on the way of our shaykhs. It is from the good deeds done by people on the path of the shaykhs. These things were done with sincerity. They founded these places with their own lawful money. These are good places for people. Those who come here benefit, AllāhNone willing. Because there are some places, some mosques that were built with dirty money and have caused dissension. They have brought trouble for people.

Because the way of our ancestors is the way of beauty for people. It shows itself first by example. The shaykhs raise people, train them, and train their egos. Ḥaḍrat Aḥmad Yasawī trained more than 10,000 murīds and sent them west. He sent them to Anatolia. They conquered it spiritually, they prepared it spiritually. They showed people the good, wherever they went. People who saw the good they did, most of them became Muslim from their example. Here, this place is an example for us. Then there were those who came later with that which was nowhere to be found in Islām, those who came oppressing people by force. They could not last long and disappeared. Therefore, for this way of the shaykhs at first you need to be a person loved by others. You cannot do everything all at once. You cannot make one Muslim by force, telling him to become Muslim.

Mawlānā Shaykh's way made thousands and tens of thousands of people Muslim, thanks to AllāhNone. Some people criticize: "No sir, he prayed ṣalāh like this and like that," whereas they need to grab on to a hook. Once they are hooked then they come. Because people are distant, meaning once they are caught and they see this beauty, they will not leave it, AllāhNone willing. These types of places, thanks to AllāhNone, these places made with sincerity have been a means for this, AllāhNone willing. We should fix ourselves and not be too hostile with people. Be together with them. If there are things you do not like, still you need to be patient. Everything is possible with patience. The patient person surely comes out winning. When one is not patient, people do not stay, they run away from that one.

The way of Mawlānā Shaykh and the shaykhs is beautiful. That is why if it were not for them these kinds of places would not exist. People would melt away from here. That is why they are bringing out the other group, so people would run away from Muslims, and so Muslims look bad. People who are not with shaykhs can only do evil. Islām is the religion of mercy. There is no faith in the one without mercy. Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla says: Irḥam, turḥam – Be merciful so that AllāhNone may have mercy on you. The merciless, as we said, are not from us says our Holy ProphetNone. We are in need of Allāh'sNone mercy. We need to treat everybody with mercy, in shā’a Llāh. May AllāhNone be pleased with everybody. Mawlānā Shaykh came here last more than 20 years ago I think. May his station also be high, in shā’a Llāh and may his himmah be present.

Fātiḥah.
