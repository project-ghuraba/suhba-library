As-Salāmu ‘Alaykum wa RaḥmatuLlāhi wa Barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu BiLlāhi Minash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. BismiLlāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm.

Wa ṣ-Salātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Akhirīn.

Madad yā RasūlAllāh, Madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi RasūliLlāh, Madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

Dastūr Mawlana Shaykh Abdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistāni, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-

Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-Suḥbah wa l-Khayru fi l-Jam‘iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm:

(Qur’ān 03:160). ‘In yanṣurkumu Llāhu falā ghāliba lakum’, ‘If Allah helps you, none can overcome you.’ Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla is the Victor. And those who are with Him ﷻ win. Falsehood doesn’t win, but truth wins. The promise of Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla is Haqq/truth here and hereafter. Nothing will remain in the hands of oppressors in this world. They won’t have any benefit. Whatever they do; the more they do, the more they will get punished.

Therefore, be together with Allah ﷻ. Don’t be scared. No one can stand against Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla. A person who fights against Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla is stupid. He is mindless. Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla is the one who created everything. And everything is in His ﷻ Hand. Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla is the one who does whatever He ﷻ wants whenever He ﷻ wants. He ﷻ conducts as He ﷻ wants and He ﷻ does as He ﷻ wants. Therefore, always be together with Allah ﷻ. You will never have any hardship. The result of hardship is goodness. There is benefit in hardship too. There are rewards and Thawab in it.

Whoever is not together with Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla, his every work is a failure. And he doesn’t get any benefit. Even if the whole world is with him, he will not have any benefit because of the things he does. And the suffering he goes through will bring no benefit but harm.

Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla is Powerful over everything. With few people He ﷻ rules over the world. Don’t look at the majority. Don’t say “The majority is going on this way. We should follow them”. Their way is not right. The right way is the way of Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla. Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla always gives victory to those who are with Him ﷻ at last. Certainly, it is so. The promise of Allah ﷻ is true. No one can stand against the will of Allah ‘Azza wa Jalla.

May Allah ﷻ let us be with Him ﷻ, always remember Him ﷻ and not let Him ﷻ out of our hearts. Our profit is big. Don’t get upset by looking at the enjoyments of this world. Don’t be sad for the condition of this world. If you are with Allah ﷻ, be happy. If you are not, then be sad. You can be sad for everything then. As we see, the condition of the world is such that everyone is upset, because they are not with Allah ﷻ. They are against Allah ﷻ and rebelling against Allah ﷻ. May Allah ﷻ protect us.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
