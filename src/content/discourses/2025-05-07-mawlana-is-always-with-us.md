As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh. A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah. Thanks to AllāhNone, we gather here once again for the pleasure of AllāhNone. To continue together on this way we came together with beloved servants of AllāhNone today. We always come together. The great awliyā’u Llāh, Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim, our father had passed away around this time. He passed away, but thanks to AllāhNone, he did not leave us. He does not leave us. He supports all the brothers and grants spiritual strength to them. Some murīds ask, “How do I make rābiṭah? How will I hear a sound in rābiṭah?” Your rābiṭah (spiritual bond) is to have affection for our Holy ProphetNone, to have affection for the mashāyikh, and to love them. That is our rābiṭah. Rābiṭah is not “Should I do this, or not do this?” That’s fine too. But no need to wait for “Do this or that, my son or daughter,” when you make rābiṭah. Those who ask for that don’t know the way, or its adab. When you join ṭarīqah, you must submit yourself. You must submit to your murshid. When you find a true murshid, you must give thanks to AllāhNone, not a thousand, but a million times. When you submit yourself to him, you reach peace. But may AllāhNone protect us, if you get involved with someone who pretends to be a murshid for the wrong reasons, may AllāhNone help you. Because that serves their ego. Because that way is for the ego, it brings no benefit. But when you have followed the way of the murshid, thanks to AllāhNone, life passes as usual; but it won’t be in vain. It will be full. It will be filled with rewards, barakah and beauty. If you’re just going to wander around aimlessly, asking, “What should we do today? How can we have fun? How can we make money? How can we make people like us?” – that’s what we call empty business. That is what we mean by useless things. It’s a nice saying. In Turkish, they call it “empty work.” Living for dunyā, for personal gain, and for your ego is an empty thing. If you want your life to be full, follow the way of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla and the path of the mashāyikh; submit to them. Don’t think that the path is empty. It is full, with the permission of AllāhNone. As we said, those who are empty are the ones who think about how much of the world they can earn. Their lives pass in vain. May AllāhNone protect us. Apart from being empty, their lives are also filled with evil and sins. May AllāhNone protect us. Therefore, seeking support and help from the mashāyikh is beneficial. Being on that way with them is protection for you. It gives value to your life. Your life becomes meaningful. “This man lives like a weed. He goes from home to work and back. He doesn’t take loans, he doesn’t curse the government, he doesn’t get angry at this or that.” He doesn’t live like a weed. It is others who live like harmful weeds. People who think too highly of themselves, who get angry at everyone and expect something from everyone, who engage in all kinds of dirtiness and forbidden things to satisfy their egos, they are the empty ones. Let us be like weeds. Weeds are good. There are harmful weeds, and there are harmless weeds. When we say weed, everyone knows its meaning. Ours, in shā’a Llāh, is with light and īmān. May we be among the beloved servants of AllāhNone, not like the weed they smoke. Let us be like weeds, not smoked weeds, in shā’a Llāh. May AllāhNone protect us. Shayṭān has already taken over the world. He doesn’t want to let go of anyone. The ones who are saved are those who hold onto the rope of AllāhNone. The others may think they are saved, or that they’ve succeeded – but there is no such thing at all. Therefore, being with the mashāyikh and following them is the greatest gain for a person. Not being on their path is a great danger. May AllāhNone protect us. At any moment, shayṭān may cause you to slip – you think you’ve become something, and you end up falling into his trap. May AllāhNone protect us. May the support of our Shaykh be present. As we said, it has now been eleven years since Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim passed away. But he is watching over us every moment. We are under his spiritual support. Whoever seeks his support, he surely comes to their aid and does not leave them. There are people who don’t believe. And there’s a group who don’t believe in the awliyā’, in their miracles or wonders. Yet they say, “We are Muslims.” But the condition of Islām is to believe in the miracles of the prophets, and to believe in the miracles of the awliyā’ – this is one of the essential principles of the Muslim faith. Therefore, shukr to AllāhNone, you are here. May AllāhNone be pleased with those who have come. There are many who could not come, but their hearts are united. Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim will reach them. He will reach everyone with the permission of AllāhNone. May AllāhNone be pleased. May AllāhNone change the state of this world. May HeNone send the Ṣāḥib. As Mawlānā used to say, may Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām appear, in shā’a Llāh. Wherever you look in this world, it is in the hands of shayṭān – completely in his hands. Being saved from shayṭān can only happen through Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām. There is no other way, no matter what they say. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh we are here again. So, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, he is with us all time. This is we know and we feel, and we are happy for this Because he is acting, he is in action for ummah. He just leave us like a picture, but he is, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, every time supporting each one. Everyone, he is supporting. We are very happy for this. If he is not supporting, we cannot be here. Many people they was, they didn’t meet him, they wasn’t know him. But, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, he choose you. He choose the people who like. So, you beloved for him, and AllāhNone beloved for him also.
