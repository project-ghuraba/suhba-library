As-Salamu Alaikum. Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fil jamia' Allah (awj) says"Wa Quli A`mala Fasayara Al-Lahu `Amalakum Wa Rasuluhu. (9:105) And say "Do deeds! Allah will see your deeds & (so will) His Messenger"

Do good deeds, work so that you make goodness, charity or any other work, any other deed. Do it for the reason that Allah & His Prophet (sas) will see you doing it. And our Prophet (sas) says, Musawwif/Procrastinators have perished. Who is the Musawwif? Who postpones his work saying "I will do it later. I will do this later. I will do that later, at that time..." It is he who always postpones his work although he has the means. Because when he postpones, it becomes laziness. He postpones each time & when that time comes he postpones again & again.. So never does any goodness, good work and a life passes like this.

And our Prophet (sas) says, man is deceived by 2 things: good health & free time. He thinks that he will always be in good health but of course he can't be. Allah forbid, an illness comes & he can't do what he was going to do. Life also, it passes so fast. All of a sudden again he is not able to do anything. Therefore don't be lazy. Whatever work you have in your hands, do immediately when you have the chance, says the Prophet (sas). Allah (awj) also says, don't be idle. Do good works, good deeds. Even if you do dunya work, if you are doing it to please Allah that also is considered to be good deed. Don't just sit being lazy & saying "I will do good works, charity". The Prophet (sas) says who didn't do although they had the chance, they have perished". You should definitely hurry when doing good works. Not for other things; if it is dunya work then take your time to think, to consider but for akhirah there is no need to think it through, do that work immediately.

And lately a lot of people are asking about this matter of going to Hajj. If you have the means to go to Hajj- if you have money, you are healthy & you haven't yet gone to Hajj, then you should go. It is obligatory for you. If the World War starts, then it is different. But in this situation now you can make niyah for it & go, with the permission of Allah. If something happens when you are there, say "what Allah orders happens". But if you don't go, you fail to fulfill one of the obligations. It is that you miss one of the orders of Islam & fall into big sin. There are certain situations where you are not able to go but other than that you should go.

Therefore we have explained this matter here. So now who haven't gone to Hajj if he has the means..Of course in our Muslim countries there is a waiting list for Hajj. So if one is waiting for his turn & can't go than it is different, it is not in his hand. But if he has money & his turn has come or, in foreign countries they can go anytime they want. Who hasn't gone to Hajj, he has to go. It is obligatory on him. No need to keep asking about this. But who has already gone to Hajj once he doesn't need to go for a 2nd time unless there is a necessity. Necessity meaning, to take the Hajjis or to be mahram to accompany his mother or sisters but no need to go alone for a 2nd time. This is. Mawlana also explains this all the time but still people keep asking..

Today we speak about, to do. Allah ordering "Wa Quli A`malu Fasayara Al-Lahu", you make good thing- work or do thing & Allah will see you, Prophet (sas) also will see you. So you must be careful what are you doing.

Other thing, you must not be lazy. Because Allah ordering "A'malu", meaning do work, do good thing. Don't be lazy, this is order. Other thing also, Prophet (sas) said "Who say we will, we will.." they are in danger. Halaka- they are destroyed people- who said 'we will do'". So because this, when somebody is used to say 'I will do, we will do' like this, this meaning they used to laziness. They all time throwing this matter to future. 'In the future maybe we can do'. Next time when coming this "now it is too early, we can do later.' And there is 2 things Prophet (sas) said people cheated by it: The health & the life.

Because health, they think whole time they will be healthy. But some people cannot be healthy. They cannot do good thing after. For life also, life is going quickly. Quickly they become old, they cannot do good thing. So it is good for ego because ego like to be lazy, rest, not doing anything. But it is order from Prophet (sas), to work- for Allah, for Islam, for our brothers, help people. All this for Allah. For dunya ok, you can think about what to do, what not to do. Not important to be hurry. But for akhirah, there is order also to be hurry for this. For dunya, of course you make dunya work also for akhirah. It is also good thing. But for akhirah, when you have chance to do something.

Many people they have, but they say from.. They have power, they have capacity, they have everything. But when some laziness coming-illness this they say "we will do, we will think, we will look.." So it is not good. For akhirah, they must be..Like many people, they ask for Hajj also. Who has enough money for to go to Hajj & come & he has good health and it is not like in our country. Islamic country, they have like to not every people can go. They choosing people. There is maybe more than 1 million people in Turkey. They waiting for queing. They taking by chance. So they cannot do, they cannot go. But in Europe or other place it is more easy to go from there. And who has enough money & he has good health & he can go. First time, he must go. Because it is one obligatory for Islam. "Wa Lillahi `Ala An-Nasi Hijju Al-Bayti Mani Astata`a 'Ilayhi Sabilaan" (3:97) Must go.

Every time they ask, if no whole world war, they can go. Or if not war in Saudi Arabia, they also can go. But for 2. time, no need for them. Every time they ask Mawlana for this. And he said, no need for to go 2. time. But maybe 2. time if he will take his mother, his sister, his wife, it is also obligatory to be with man, these ladies. So he can go. It is also, must do it if he has all condition.

If not doing, he is He will blame & He may be punishment for him because he not doing this big obligatory for Islam. And this is very important.

Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, Al Fatiha.
