As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
وَأَن لَّوِ اسْتَقَامُوا عَلَى الطَّرِيقَةِ لَأَسْقَيْنَاهُم مَّاءً غَدَقًا 
لِّنَفْتِنَهُمْ فِيهِ ۚ وَمَن يُعْرِضْ عَن ذِكْرِ رَبِّهِ يَسْلُكْهُعَذَابًا صَعَدًا
<br>“Wa allawi astaqāmū ‘alā aţ-ţarīqati l'asqaynāhum mā an ghadaqāan. Li-naftinahum fīhi; wa man yu’riđ ‘an dhikri Rabbihi yasluk-hu ‘adhāban şa’adāan”<br>
“If they had believed in Allah and went on the Right Way (i.e. Islam) We should surely have bestowed on them water (rain) in abundance. That We might try them thereby. And whosoever turns away from the Reminder of his Lord, He will cause him to enter in a severe torment (i.e. Hell).” (Qur’ān 72:16). SadaqAllahu l-‘Aẓīm.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla protects and preserves those on the right way. Those who are not on the right way cause fitnah (discord) for others. They think their fitnah is good for them; that they are doing good. Whereas, shayṭān destroys their īmān.

There is īmān and Islām. The essence of Islām is īmān. Believing in Allah, believing in the ghayb (unseen), believing in the angels, and believing in the afterlife. These are all īmān. Only those who follow the ṭarīqah truly do this.

Others always - a group of people call themselves Jamā’ah (community) or I don’t know what else. Their īmān is based on nothing, yet they appear to be truly Muslim. But without īmān, nothing remains. This is a ploy of shayṭān. Many people have been deceived by this and followed that way. It has been a means of their disappointment and loss.

Therefore, it is necessary to connect with a Murshid (guide) and ṭarīqah, which is connected directly to our Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Jamā’at, on the other hand, have no such connection. Because they lack that bond, they can quickly gather people, only to then finish their īmān and divide Muslims against one another.

They will also be deprived of intercession in the hereafter. Because they have no connection with our Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Without it, there is no īmān. Without him, without the means of our Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, there is no īmān. There is Islām, but no īmān. They are not believers (mu’min); they are Muslims.

You must pay attention to that. Muslims who want to perfect their īmān connect with a Murshid so that they can reach our Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. May Allah grant people this beautiful way. May Allah help them. May He protect them from shayṭān, inshāAllah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq

Al-Fātiḥah

There are recited Qur’an Khatms, Dalail Al-Khayrāt, Salawat, Tasbihāt, Yasin. We gift everything our brothers read from all over the world sfirstly to the blessed soul of our Prophet ṣallAllahu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, his Ahlu l-Bayt and ṣaḥābah, souls of all Anbiya, Awliyā, Asfiya.

To the souls of our mashāyikh. To the souls of all Muslims and Mu’mins. May those who recited reach whatever good goals they have. For our strength of īmān. For happiness here and hereafter.

Li-Llahi Ta’ala l-Fātiḥah.
