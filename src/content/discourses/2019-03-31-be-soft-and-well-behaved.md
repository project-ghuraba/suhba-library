As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīn wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa-l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Inna Ibrāhīma la-awwāhun ḥalīm" (9:114). Āyatu l-karīmah. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is describing Ibrāhīm, ‘alayhi s-salām, in the Holy Qur’ān ‘aẓīmu sh-sha’n. He was very merciful to mankind. And he was soft and kind, with a good character. He was soft to people and helpful. Whatever good manner there is, he has it, says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla in praising Ibrāhīm ‘alayhi s-salām in Qur’ān ‘aẓīmu sh-sha’n. Our ProphetNone says in an ḥadīth, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla told Ibrāhīm ‘alayhi s-salām. "Ḥassin khuluqaka ḥattá ma‘a l-kuffāri takūna mana l-abrār." "Be soft even to unbelievers. Don't treat them badly." To whomever, Ibrāhīm ‘alayhi s-salām was ordered to be like this.

All prophets were like this too. However, Ibrāhīm ‘alayhi s-salām is the father of prophets and the order of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is for all of us, not only him; it is for all mankind. Don't treat other people badly. Treat them with goodness and kindness. Good people, our ProphetNone is saying, are people with good manners and adab who get along well with people. And our ProphetNone is saying, "How do you reach this high rank? It is as if you pray all night, standing up day and night and fasting during the day." By being a man with good manners, it is the easy way for people to reach that rank. Just be well behaved. Unlike others who fight with people, even though they worship day and night, who prays but doesn't get along with people; Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla writes more thawāb for the first than HeNone does for such a man. It is the advice of our ProphetNone to us.

Muslims nowadays are in great need of it because when they see someone, they wait for a chance to expose their faults. And they look intently to find something. In fact, everyone has some fault. This way is not from good manners. Good manner is to get along with people and to cover their faults. This is the advice of our ProphetNone. It is easy. You can earn these thawāb without tiring your body or yourself, says our ProphetNone. It is what we need in our time. Because people are not learning these things. "You must fight, you must get your rights back, you must do this and that" – all they teach is this. And then people run away from Islām thinking this is how Islām is. Instead of showing Islām's beauty, they show its bad side. It doesn't have a bad side. There is no bad Islām but people who think they are Muslim do like this.

May AllāhNone grant adab to people. One of the noble ṣaḥābah named Adshaḥ, our ProphetNone told him, "Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla loves you because of your good manners and doing things with knowledge." Our ProphetNone said so to the holy ṣaḥābīy. And the ṣaḥābīy replied, "Is it from me?" Look at his adab. "Is it from me, or is it the favour of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla?" the ṣaḥābīy, raḍī Allāhu ‘anhu, asked our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam said, "This is a favour Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gave you." So he said, "I thank AllāhNone for giving me this good character. Endless shukr to HimNone." May AllāhNone give all of us such good manners and character, in shā’a Llāh.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla praising Sayyidunā Ibrahim ‘alayhi s-salām in Qur’ān. HeNone is saying. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Inna Ibrāhīma la-awwāhun ḥalīm" (9:114) meaning Ibrāhīm (as) he is very soft and very kind for people. HeNone praising him in Qur’ān many places. HeNone praises him and and he is also in this āyah. This is āyah. I remember there is one calligraphy in our house in Damascus this was writing there. Mawlānā he bring from Cyprus. And every time I was seeing but, of course we not knowing meaning. After we knowing. It is the biggest honour for Prophets, to praise them in Qur’ān. It is forever this. And because he was soft for people, for everything he was merciful.

And other – Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, there is ḥadīth saying AllāhNone inspiration for Sayyidunā Ibrāhīm ‘alayhi s-salām – awḥá: "Yā Ibrāhīm, be soft!" "Ḥassin khuluqaka ḥattá ma‘a l-kuffāri takūna mana l-abrār." "You be good behaved even with non-believer, kāfir – who said – non believer – AllāhNone make you with good people." So it is not only for believer or Muslim you must be good behaving – for whole human being, for human being, or for even for animal also. For everything you must be good behaved.

Good behaved it is the most acceptable attribute in Divinely Presence. And Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam he said, "The nearest me for in Qiyāmah, who the best be good behaved and the far from me – the tough people and not good behaved people. They are will be very far from me." Good people they will be... Good behaved people they are be near Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. And good behave make your this balance scale very heavy for good deeds. So it is. But it is nowadays people they are not looking for this, even not thinking for this. Only they are, they don't have good behave. They are aggressive. They are angry for everything, not looking to win hearts of people, only they are criticising them and looking for their fault, for their sin, to make to be angry for them.

But it is, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla even for Sayyidunā Ibrāhīm saying: "Don't be tough for this people. Be good behaved for them. Maybe later from this they can come and they be good people." In ṭarīqah they are teaching this. But nowadays they are making ṭarīqah not acceptable and only they following people, teaching people everywhere to be tough with people. With this toughness, even believer they are afraid to come to mosque or to follow people. Only few people they making big noise and they are serving shayṭān not serving Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla HeNone want us to be soft and good behaved. AllāhNone help us to be like this.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
