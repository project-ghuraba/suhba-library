Dastur Ya Sayyidi, madad Ya Sultan La ilaha illa Allah La ilaha illa Allah La ilaha illa Allah Muhammadun Rasulullah alayhi salatullah wa salamuh Assalamu alaykum Ya Ibadallahi s-Salihin

Say, audhu Billahi min ash-shaytani r-rajim Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. O our attenders from men and Jinn, from both living ones, on this planet who are carrying responsibility for worshipping. Countless kinds of creatures, living on this planet, dunya, but only Human Nature and Jinn are responsible. They are carrying responsibility for being servants of Allah Almighty, Subhanhu wa Ta'ala. Anta rabbuna anta hasbuna anta wali'una, anta ni'mal Mawla wa ni'ma n-nasir Ya Rabbuna!

Asking Your forgiveness for the honour of most honoured, most glorified one through Your Divine Presence, Sayyidina Muhammad, sallallahu alayhi wa sallam. O our attenders, those, you are claiming you know something and you are learned ones, but what you learned? What you learned, it can't be more than an atom’s bi-nisbah, according to the greatness of this planet, dunya. But you are claiming, 'we know, we learned'. What you learned, say to me? Your height - how much? They are saying 'we don't know'. You are not knowing about your height and you are saying, 'we know, we are learned ones'.

What did you learn? Subhan Allahi'l Aliyyi'l Azim! Man is most proud one through creation. The seed of pride just put through their egoistic being. Ego. They are claiming, for what? They are claiming and saying, 'we are proud ones because we are masters of creation'. [Even] if we are living, not 70 or 100 years, or 1000 years, or million years, or billion years you can't reach anything. That thing you learned about, [which is] making you to be proud, it is not enough. Because everything, Shaykh Hisham that is in creation, each one, just granted to that one a sirr, (secret). Secret, but secret is not enough, wisdom.

For everything, it carries a wisdom from huge Wisdom Oceans that are so big, no creature may know for it a beginning or an ending. Just put in it something. And men when created just put in it a seed of hidden wisdom. Leave what is around yourself but you look into yourself. Men, they are using mighty microscope to see something. Why you are not making a mighty microscope for yourself? But they are making research with mighty microscope to know about smallest creation, and to know about their selves, it is impossible. It is not enough to use mighty microscope and see that smallest creation. You may see perhaps but you can't understand the wisdom that [was] put in it and that one coming in existence with that secret purpose. And Allah Almighty saying in Holy Quran: rabbana ma khalaqta hadha batilan Our Lord, You did not create this aimlessly (3:191)

Allahu Akbar. You are Arabs. You are saying we are learned people. You're understanding what is heavenly wisdom, when the Lord of Heavens making and bringing countless kinds of beings into existence. But no one knowing real purpose about that creation. Therefore they are only saying: Our Lord, You did not create this aimlessly; exalted are You, then protect us from the punishment of the Fire. (3:191). Hadha, this You created, O our Creator. You created. It is not without a purpose. 'Hadha', that Arabs knowing more than other nations.

What does it mean 'hadha'? - isharatun ila mawjud. Signing/indicating a point in existence. Saying, finally, our understanding is going to disappear, to finish and then we are saying, 'rabbana ma khalaqta hadha batilan'. But we don't know, we don't know for what You created. Must be some purpose for the creation of that one. We are not going so deeply, but we may speak about humanity because sometimes people may say that that servant of our Lord is speaking such a point about Creation, we are not able to follow him. Yes, it is true. It is true, but Insan, Man, Human Nature, Allah Azza wa Jall Subhanahu wa Ta'ala, He is granting for Man who is master of this creation. Astaidhu Billah, Allah Subhanahu wa Ta'ala yaqul, Jalla min qa'il:

wa laqad karramna bani adam (17:70) And We have certainly honoured the children of Adam

He Almighty, Creator, The Creator saying and praising among whole creation, Man, and saying - He is giving His Divine Oath and saying, “just We created Man and We granted to them takrim, heavenly honour”. That honour [was] never granted to any other creation. Therefore we are speaking on something [about] smallest ones, but our target from that smallest representative of generation, is to reach Man. If that smallest piece of creature so deeply, includes/contains secret wisdoms, what about for Man?

And particularly, the Lord of Heavens saying that, giving His Oath, “And We have certainly honoured” – “karramna, We granted to them such an honour [that] no one knows”. No one knows real honour that is granted to Man. Allahumma, only, only that “We granted” some of them. According to their levels of understanding, “We are granting”. But no one can reach that honour that granted by our Creator to ourselves.

O People, Salafi ulamas, Marhaban. Marhaban. You ever opening and speaking on that? A holy verse that Allah Subhanahu wa Ta'ala He is sending Archangel Gabriel [with], that holy verse through Holy Quran to His most beloved one and most learned one, because, your teachers [are] from yourselves but the teacher of Nabiyyun ummi, “I am That One. I have granted to My most beloved one, I taught him!” Don't make discussion on his knowledge or his speeches or his declarations. Don't use scale on it. It is not for you. You must say only: Rabbana amanna bima anzalta wattab'ana arrasula faktubna ma'ash-shahidin Our Lord, we have believed in what You revealed and have followed the Messenger, so register us among the witnesses (3:53)

O People, for all Human Nature [who are] responsible for being servants of the Lord of Heavens, they must say finally, “Our Lord, You did not create this aimlessly; exalted are You, then protect us from the punishment of the Fire” (3:191). “And We have certainly honoured the children of Adam” (17:70). Oceans, through oceans, through oceans, through mighty, majestic Dominions that belong to Lord's Divine Presence. You must say, “Our Lord, we have believed in what You revealed and have followed the messenger, so register us among the witnesses” (3:53).

“And have followed the messenger" - you must, you must follow the Seal of Prophets, but you are using, Salafi ulamas and Azhar Sharif ulamas, doctors of Sharia, you are using always new, fashionable dressing. You are saying ‘we must show ourselves that we are such a people following tajaddud (modernity), we are following modern lifestyle so that people may come and listen to our speeches. We are not using that [which was] before our time, people they were never taking any care for modernising. But we are [modern], new fashion, fashionable dressing and fashionable, modernised lifestyle’. Hanh, masha'Allah.

Those modernised styles of life that you are calling ‘westernised’ fashions. You are saying this, we are modern or learned people we are modern ulamas. No you can't do, you can't do that. You must try to dress [according to] what Allah Almighty is saying, O Salafu s-Saleh,

wa libasu t-taqwa huwa khayr The dress of righteousness is best (7:26)

Where is your dress of righteousness? Where? Say! I am asking for whole muluk also, and all doctors, Salafi ulamas that Allah Almighty saying: But the dress of righteousness - that is best (7:26). Which one is dressing now in dress of righteousness? Send to me, send to me. O Salafi ulamas or doctors and that dress of righteousness, where it is? In the museum or through your books you are looking and seeing their pictures? Keeping there. Why you are not using? And the Lord of Heavens just forbade [it]. Forbidden through holy shariatallah to use raqiq, very thin clothes. Where they are? They never using libasu t-taqwa.

Libasu t-taqwa is not modernised dressing. No, you must keep because as much as you are dressing in modernised clothes you are going to your graveyard and those clothes [will be] taken from you. And you are going to be put in a coffin. You are going. You can't take that fashionable dress to your graveyard, no. Say that you are not following the ways of prophets. No! Why?

fattabi'anee ahdika siratan sawiyyan So follow me; I will guide you to an even path (19:43) “Ittiba” - to follow someone who is knowing the value of this life and this dunya. Follow such a people. But you are following your egoistic desires. And so no one taking any care what you are saying. You and an [audio] casette, it is same. It is same but people asking to look [for] a person who is carrying secret power of Prophet, peace be upon him. They like it. They don't like whom they are dressing new, fashionable, modern dressings. “No modernising in Islam”, say through Juma khutbah. Say, why you are not saying?

Yes, follow, follow the ways of those people as Allah Almighty saying: wattabi'a sabila man anaba ilayya. Suratu Luqman, yes? And follow the way of those who turn back to Me (31:15). What does it mean? Follow those people who are accepting - (MSH: those who are coming to Me), wattabi'a sabila man anaba ilayya, that holy verse, a deep ocean. You are understanding or not? Those people, O ulama-u Azhar sharif those people only, they have been living in past-times? Or yet they are in existence, in our days also?

Follow, follow, Allah Almighty is ordering, “the steps of those people whose real target is My Pleasure. They are trying only to make Me pleased with them. And, they are asking only pleasure from Me, not anything from material world”. Where are you? You find one? If you are saying they were once upon a time, and now they are finished, can't be. In our days they have [them] also. They are in existence but you are not asking [for them]. You are fearing from your ladies because ladies, fashionable ones. They like to be most fashionable lady among their community. You are fearing, therefore you can't use such a dress that Awliyaullah [wear].

Awliyaullah, who are beloved ones in Divine Presence, you can't follow them because your wives never asking their husbands to be in such a way and not to use Cadillac. Cadillac cars, how I know it? I heard from someone. There is another car, WC. WC car. Why I am saying Shaykh Effendi? When they are asking to do something in that car, there is also W.. Therefore it is famous car, WC car. Sleeping car, in WC. They are following hawa an-nafs, selfish desires. That worst desire, coming from our egos.

May Allah forgive us, O People, try to follow such a one who is saying only, ill'Allah, ill'Allah, ill'Allah! But people preventing to be said through mosque 'la ilaha illa Allah'. Because they are saying only ‘ill'ad-dunya, ill'ad-dunya, ill'ad-dunya’! Instead of saying "la ilaha illa Allah", they are saying ‘la ilaha ill'ad-dunya’. Audhubillah, Audhubillah, la ilaha illa Allah, la ilaha illa Allah. Afdalu ma qaal an-nabiyuna min qabli la ilaha illa Allah, la ilaha illa Allah. Hadith or not hadith? Why you are preventing people when they are coming and sitting, and saying "la ilaha illa Allah", - bida? From where bringing such a foolish order!

And the Seal of Prophet's saying: the most preferable saying among the prophets before me, wa ana minhum, la ilaha illa Allah. Who preventing? Saying, ‘why sitting and saying la ilaha ill Allah?’ Bida! Sharr, sharr ulama is saying. Salafi us-Saleh - Is this the rank you reached? Woe to the Arabs - are they from the ignorant? We are non-Arabs (Aj'am) but Allah Almighty saying, the Day of Resurrection for all people, astaidhu Billah: waqifuhum innahu mas'ulun And stop them; indeed, they are to be questioned (37:24)

O woe to the Arabs! He should be asked from beginning up to end. We are A'jam/non-Arabs, “What do they know?" We [say], “la ilaha illa Allah, Muhammadun Rasulullah”. “Y’Allah Paradise”. For Arab, “And stop them; indeed, they are to be questioned”. Good tidings for us Human community, good tidings for us, Salafu s-salehin. “And stop them!” We will say, "O our Lord they are in front of us, steering [the] wheel - we are sitting but steering wheel in Arab [hands]”. "Where's your ID”? Arab? Arab, you are warned, these papers (?) Take them away!” Arab.

O People come, and try to be as our Lord asking to be of His servants. O our Lord forgive us. Allahumma ighfirlana wa'rhamna, w'atub alayna Ya Rabbana bi ja'hi man anzalta alayhim surat al-Fatiha.

Fatiha.
