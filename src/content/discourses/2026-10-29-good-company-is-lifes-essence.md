As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Alḥamdulillāh we are here; all are ṭarīqah people. This is the word of Shah Naqshband, the Pīr of Ṭarīqah Nashbandiyyah. There are things that must be in ṭarīqah—Khatm ul-Khwājagān and ṣuḥbah, inshā’Allāh. To be together. Alḥamdulillāh we are now gathering by the order of our Pīr Shaykh Bahā'uddin Naqshbandī, inshā’Allāh; with his nazar, his sight upon us. This opens that Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla’s sight to be upon us, to be happy with us. Every time, I pray for Allah to send good people to us, to be together with good people. This is our prayer, our duʿā every time.

To be with good people is the best thing to do, because Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam ordered this. He gave example on this. If your friend is good, he’s like a perfume seller. If you go to his shop, even if you don’t buy, if you just go there, you smell nice smells and that will make you happy. Even if you don’t buy anything, if you just go inside, you will be happy because there’s a good smell, a clean place and good people.

And the bad friend is just like the blacksmith. If you go to his workshop - it’s his work of course, there’s no problem for him. He pumps air into fire to make it strong and to melt iron a little bit. So when he blows air on the iron, this small fire coming out will either burn your clothes or make you smell like smoke. Besides this, to temper the iron and make it stronger, they sometimes quench it in foul water-even using horse urine-which constantly gives off a terrible smell. For this, this is an example here.

Of course, there’s no need to go near the blacksmith; from far away, if you have any request, you can buy or ask. But nobody goes to the blacksmith’s shop and enjoys his time there.

So we must find good people to be friends with; with good people, inshā’Allāh. Good people are those who love Allah, love Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, love the ṣaḥābah, love Ahlu l-Bayt and mashāyikh. These are the good people, the best people. This is the meaning of life of human beings. This is how it must be.

They are looking and researching “Why are we here?” You haven’t been invited to come to this world. Allah created you. You cannot ask Him, “Why am I here?” 

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says,<br>
أَفَحَسِبْتُمْ أَنَّمَا خَلَقْنَاكُمْ عَبَثًا وَأَنَّكُمْ إِلَيْنَا لَا تُرْجَعُونَ
<br>“Afa-ḥasibtum annamā khalaqnākum ʿabathan wa annakum ilaynā lā turjaʿūn”<br>
“Did you think that We had created you in play (without any purpose), and that you would not be brought back to Us?” (Qurʼān 23:115). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

Allah says, “Do you think We created you just to enjoy yourself and that you are not coming back to us?”

This is to wake people up. But if they are not reading this and not listening, they are all the time suffering. They thought they are only coming here by means of their father and mother. They are not happy with them, saying, “Why did you bring us to this world!” This is the real ignorance, Jāhiliyyah. Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam says, the second Jāhiliyyah, the second time of ignorance is worse than the first one. So if they know what the meaning of life is, they will be satisfied, they will be happy, no stress, no anxiousness, no sadness. 

This is the meaning of a good friend, of good people. To be with good people: this is the meaning of life.

Alḥamdulillāh here we see many people alḥamdulillāh coming for ṭarīqah. There are many zāwiyahs here and everywhere, inshā’Allāh, in Indonesia. Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim, our father, used to say that, inshā’Allāh, there will be 40,000 dergahs, zāwiyahs across the world. 

Allah created everybody a little bit different from the other. Someone might like to be with his friend whom he’s more happy with. They can be together with them, no problem. There’s no problem as long as they are following Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla and have love towards each other. There are 124,000 prophets. There were some whose entire ummah, nation, was only one person. For one prophet, one nation. For this, if you are a wakīl (representative) or you have a dergah, there’s no need to be worried if people are coming or not. Of course, you must welcome anybody coming and be together. If not coming, okay also.

Alḥamdulillāh we are Naqshbandī; and this applies towards other ṭarīqahs also. They are all our brothers. We love each other. We traveled last year to Irāq. We visited all mashāyikh: Shaykh ʿAbdul Qādir Al-Gaylāni, Abu Hanifa, Sayyidina Aḥmad Rifāi, Hassan Al-Basri. We visited all these Awliyā. All are upon our head. We are happy with them. And we are happy with those in our time also—many great ʿulamā, Awliyā and salihīn (righteous).

Alḥamdulillāh, we meet with them; they visit us and we visit them. They are beloved ones for Allah. So no need to make a problem. As much as you meet with good people you must be happier.

Alḥamdulillāh our Imām was reading a very nice Sūrah, Sūrat uz-Zumar. The meaning of the āyah is that those who believe in Allah, and remain conscious of their deeds to not feel ashamed in front of Allah, will be together. The Sūrah says, they will go in troops; party by party. “Zumaran” means troops together. They will go like that to paradise. When they are in dunyā together, in ākhirah also, they will be going to paradise together. The angels will greet them, “Salāmun ʿalaykum.” The malā’ika (angels) will say, “Allah is happy with you, come to paradise.”

May Allah make more and more people be together to go to Jannah with millions of people, inshā’Allāh. May Allah reward you because you’ve been waiting here since Maghrib; many of you. They are not tired, they’re waiting for the love of Allah. They were waiting here for us and they are not tired from waiting. Because they are not waiting for a party, for singing or playing. They are waiting with the intention being just to hear something to open their hearts and for Allah to bless them. 

May Allah bless you, inshā’Allāh. May we be together forever, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
