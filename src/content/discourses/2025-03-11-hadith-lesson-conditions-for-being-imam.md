As-Salāmu ‘Alaykum wa RaḥmatuLlāhi wa Barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu BiLlāhi Minash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. BismiLlāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm.

Wa ṣ-Salātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Akhirīn.

Madad yā RasūlAllāh, Madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi RasūliLlāh, Madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

Dastūr Mawlana Sheikh Abdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistāni, Sheikh Muḥammad

Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad.

1- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

ﻢﻜﺑر ﻦﯿﺑو ﻢﻜﻨﯿﺑ ﺎﻤﯿﻓ ﻢﻛُﺪﻓْ و

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “You should make the best among yourselves your imāms of prayer. Because they are your messengers between you and your Lord.” So imāms of prayer used to have attributes in old times. The most learned, the most knowledgeable person, the most Hafiz person would be the imām. Therefore, they are the best, said our Holy Prophet, make him the imām. Imām is not the unknowledgeable, he is the one who is both pious and knowledgeable, and he is more acceptable.

2- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “When the iqāmah is made for Fard prayer, you cannot perform any other prayer except the Fard prayer.” So, when the iqāmah is recited, the one who performs the Sunnah prayer continues. Who didn’t reach the Sunnah prayer, he should directly follow the Fard prayer.

3- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

،نَ ﻮﺸﻤﺗ ﻢْ ُﺘﻧأو ﺎھﻮﺘﺋا ﻦﻜِ َﻟو ،نَ ﻮﻌﺴَﺗ ﻢْ ُﺘﻧأو ﺎھﻮﺗﺄَﺗ ﻼﻓ ،ُةﻼﺼﱠ ﻟا ﺖِ ﻤَ ﯿﻗأ اذإ

ﻮﻤﱡ ﺗﺄﻓ ﻢﻜُ َﺗﺎﻓ ﺎﻣو ،اﻮ ﱡ ﻠﺼﻓ ﻢْ ُﺘﻛْ رَ دأ ﺎﻤﻓ َﺔﻨﯿﻜﺴﱠ ﻟا ﻢُ ﻜُ ﯿﻠﻋو

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “When the Iqamah is called for Fard prayer in congregation, do not come to it rushing.” Some people run to catch up. Our Prophet says don’t do that. “Rather come to it walking, and while you have tranquility. What you catch of it then pray it,” if you reached the first rak’ah, it’s good. If you reached the second rak’ah, you can catch up; no problem. “And what you missed of it, then complete it yourself after the imām gives salam.” Complete what you missed.

4- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “When the iqāma for prayer is pronounced, do not get up till you see me.” First the imām will stand, then the congregation will stand, when the iqāma is recited.

5- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

اَذو

َ

ﻒَ ﯿﻌِ ﻀﱠ ﻟاو

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “When one of you leads the congregation in prayer, he should be brief,” He should keep the prayer short and light; not lengthen it. “For among them are the young and the old, the weak, the sick and those who have needs to attend to.” When there are such cases, if they stand in prayer for a long time, some of them will break their ablution. Therefore, pray light and quick when you are an imām; don’t make it long. “But when one of you prays by himself, he may be as long as he likes.”

6- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

ﺖَ ﯿْ ﱠ ﻠﺻَ ﺪْ َﻗ ﺖَ ﻨْ ﻛُ نْ إ ِ و َ سِ ﺎﱠﻨﻟا ﻊ َ ﻣَ ﻞّ ِ ﺼَ َﻓ اًﺪﺠِ ﺴْ ﻣَ ﺖﻠﺧد اَذإ ِ

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “When you you enter a masjid, pray along with the congregation even if you have already prayed.” If there is an imām, follow him. Pray following him if you have a qada’ prayer, or if you don’t have qada’, pray nāfilah.

People ask about that a lot. Our brothers do that a lot. When the time of prayer enters, they would be waiting for us in front of the mosque, while they are praying inside. You pray too. It would be better if you pray a second time when we come. Everyone already has a qada’ prayer. They should definitely pray it. It can be prayed a second time; it’s no problem.

7- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

ّ ّ

،ﻢﮭﻌﻣ ﻲﻠﺼﯿﻠﻓ ،نﻮﻠﺼﯾ مﻮﻘﻟاو ﺪﺠﺴﻤﻟا ﻞﺧد ﻢﺛ ﮫﺘﯿﺑ ﻲﻓ ﻢﻛﺪﺣأ ﻰﻠﺻ اذإ

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “If one of you prays in his house and then enters the mosque while the congregation are praying, then let him pray again with them.” A man who prayed at home then went to the mosque and saw that there was a congregation, our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam said, let him pray with them. “Because this prayer he will pray will be a nāfilah prayer for him.” Of course, at that time, the ṣaḥābah had no qada’ prayers. So it would be a nāfilah for them. But for most of us, it won’t be a nāfilah but directly a qada’ prayer. If there is no qada’, then it replaces the nāfilah prayer.

8- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet is saying,“When the Imām recites aloud in Maghrib, Isha and Fajr prayer, then listen attentively.” While the imām is reciting, according to our Hanafi Madhhab, you don’t have to read following with the imām. According to Shafi’i, the imām makes a pause after Al-Fatiha so that they recite Al-Fatihah. Then, the imām continues.

9- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

ﻢھؤُ ﺮَ ﻗ َ أ ﺔِ ﻣﺎﻣﻹﺎﺑ ﻢﮭﱡﻘﺣَ َ أو ،ﻢھُﺪﺣَ َ أ ﻢﮭﻣﱠ ﺆُ َﯿﻠ ْ ﻓ ٌ ﺔﺛﻼﺛ نﺎﻛ اذإ

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “When three people pray, one of them should lead them in prayer as an imām.” So if there are three people, one of them should be the imām, says our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. “The one who is most worthy of being an imām is the one who knows and reads the Qur’an best.”

10- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

ءً اﻮﺳ ةِ ءاﺮﻘﻟا ﻲﻓ اﻮﻧﺎﻛ نﺈﻓ ِﷲ بِ ﺎﺘﻜﻟ ﻢھؤُ ﺮﻗأ ﻢﮭﻣﱠ ﺆُ َﯿﻠﻓ ﺔﺛﻼﺛ اﻮﻧﺎﻛ اذإ

ﺎﮭً ﺟو ﻢﮭﻨﺴﺣﺄﻓ ءً اﻮﺳ ﻦﱠ ﺴّ ﻟا ﻲﻓ اﻮﻧﺎﻛ نﺈﻓ ،ﻦﺴّ ﻟﺎﺑ ﻢھﺮﺒﻛﺄﻓ

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “When three people come together for prayer, let the one who recites Qur’an best be the imām.” The one who recites the best among the three becomes the imām. “But if they are all the same in reading Qur’an, if they were equal, the oldest should be the imām. However, if they are equal in age then the most beautiful in terms of facial beauty should be the imām,” says our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. So to be the imām, one must be the best. And our Holy Prophet tells us all the conditions one by one.

11- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says: وْ َ أ رٍ ﺎﻤَ ﺣِ سَ ْ أرَ ُﮫﺳَ ْ أرَ ُwﱠ ﻞﻌﺠﯾ نْ َ أ مﺎﻣَ ﻹ

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Does he who raises his head before the imām not fear that Allah may change his head into a donkey’s head or his face into a donkey’s face.” Who raises his head before the imām, may Allāh protect us, his face becomes a donkey’s face, says our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Even if his face doesn’t change, he receives the manifestation of that. That’s why, you must be careful. Sometimes most people try to get up before the imām. If it happens by mistake, it’s not a problem. But there are those who are constantly do it making it a habit. Therefore, we must be careful about it.

12- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

هﺮﺼﺑ ﮫﯿﻟإ ﻊﺟﺮﯾ ﻻأ ةﻼﺼﻟا ﻲﻓ ُﮫﺳَ ْ أرَ ﻊ

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “When one of you raises his head in prayer, does he not fear that his eyes will not return to him?” So, while praying, one should not raise his head to look here and there. There is danger upon his eyes, says our Prophet. Be careful. Look at the place of prostration in prayer. Even when you get up, you mustn’t look here and there.

13- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says: ﻲﻓِ زُ ﻮﱠ ﺠَ َﺗ َ ﺄَﻓ

ِ

ﻲ

ّ

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “When I begin the prayer. I intend to make it long,” the most beloved thing to our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam is prayer. So he says, “I intend to make it long, but I hear a boy crying and shorten my prayer, being aware of his mother’s emotion for she cannot bear her child’s crying.” May Allāh grant us all to follow him, in shā’a Llāh. He shows us and teaches us the best of everything. May He grant us to follow that way.

The Messenger of Allāh says the truth in what he said or as he said.
