As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

There are some people, most people, who say they are distressed. Why does distress occur? People feel distressed because they are too busy with this life, dunyā. The more they think about dunyā, the more they feel distressed. They feel much worse. They say, “I will do this. I will do that,” or they want to be promoted at their work by working harder and more. When they sit down to talk, they talk about worldly issues. The more they talk about it, the more distress they have. Even Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim used to say not to listen to these things. It brings more distress.

If you want to get rid of distress, be with people who are after the next life, ākhirah. Attend their gatherings. When there is a talk about dunyā, there is heaviness, darkness and sadness. When the matter of ākhirah is on the agenda, people’s souls get happy. People’s spirits are pleased and happy. Otherwise, people are never happy with the talk of dunyā, because either others talk about themselves, or you talk about your problems. And people who cannot stand those problems feel distressed and sad too. Things that are not nice happen.

Therefore, if you want to be at peace, listen to the words of ākhirah. Keep on the way of AllāhNone. Keep on the way of our Holy ProphetNone. You may read about the people of old times, their lives and what they did. You may listen to them. Besides, when pious people are remembered, AllāhNone sends HisNone mercy down. HeNone sends barakah. People get comfort. But if you look at worldly people, how much someone earned, how someone worked, what someone did and things like this, you will be depressed and feel distressed.

Therefore, you don’t find such good people among any religion as you do among Muslims, shukr to AllāhNone. The rest want [more] people not for guidance but to use them to convert the whole world to their religions. It has been this way since old times. The religion of Islām is not cumpulsory. It is to save people. It is to lead people to happiness and peace, both here and hereafter. Therefore, wherever this religion comes, it makes it prosperous. It makes both their dunyā and ākhirah prosperous. In this world, they have put all people into the same mold. They have turned their faces towards dunyā. They don’t look at ākhirah. Therefore, whole world is distressed and sad. May AllāhNone give guidance to people, so that they see this beauty, in shā’a Llāh.

The people, human being now, they are, most of them, running after dunyā, after material. Whole, they are thinking about this. They thought happiness with this, it will be with... if they going after dunyā, running after dunyā, after material benefit, it will be happiness for them. No. It is: as much you running after dunyā, after material, you will be tired. And you will not catch it. And you will be not happy, even you have. And today of course, many people they have, they want more and more and more, every time they have. So they not find happiness, because they are only for material.

We are, have spiritual also. When you look for spiritual, you can get happiness. Without spiritual, no happiness, no any good thing. Only sadness, stress and unhappiness, darkness. When you sit with people, they are only speaking for their business, for dunyā, how they will be take this, how will be running people after them, how they will be succeed, how be – this. All this, make who sit with this people, or listening for them, he will be not happy.

But most of world now just looking for this. Not looking anything for spiritual, for... be, happiness in this way, the real happiness in spiritual way. This is giving you, your heart, light and happiness. Without this, no, impossible to get happiness. But as we said, nowadays whole world they are looking only to this side, to material thing, and not looking for spiritual. Not sitting, thinking about how this... al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we have in Islām thousand, millions of story and example from prophets and from ṣaḥābah, from awliyā’u Llāh, from... This, all this, you can look and see this whole people in Muslim, they was looking happiness with serving people, to teach them more better. Not to use them for their benefit. No. Only they was looking for to make them better, to give them happiness here and hereafter.

But other – we are hearing about the not real Christian. They was going East and West. Everywhere they going, they torturing people, they oppressing people, make them to... killing millions of people. And after they said, ‘Īsá ‘alayhi s-salām, he was for peace. They was real. For this, no happiness any place in their... what they are doing. Only sadness, only misery. Happiness with, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, with Islām, with this good people who are in Islām, al-ḥamdu liLlāh. They are, was from beginning until end, until now they are serving people, good thing, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone make us to look for spiritual, not...

Because now all people they thought if most of people going somewhere, way, it is good way. But sometimes, many people, they are like sheep. Sheep, when someone of the front of them running from high place, without knowing he is fall down from there. Whole, maybe, thousand sheep running after them. All of them die from jumping from high. Now, nowadays people they are like this. Don’t look for this people. AllāhNone, if HeNone give you enough for to live, be happy with this. And look for ākhirah also, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone help us to be going in this way, in shā’a Llāh. Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
