Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

A’ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm:

لَه ِإ ِاللَّه رِ مْ َ أ نْ مِ م

‘[The son] said, “I will take refuge on a mountain to protect me from the water.” [Noah] said, “There is no protector today from the decree of Allah, except for whom He gives mercy”.’ (Qur’ān 11:43). Noah ‘alayhi s-salām said to his son, “Come, board the ship of salvation and escape. The whole world will sink, and those who board this ship will be saved.” The flood had started, it was raining. His son said: “There are high mountains, I will not wait to drown below, I will go up to the mountain, I will be saved.” Noah ‘alayhi s-salām said: “There is no escape from Allah’s ﷻ wrath, from what He promised. Only people who have attained His mercy are saved.” Even at that moment, he did good. But after that, the wave came and took him away, and his son drowned and died.

It is the same now in the end of times, the things that Allah ﷻ promised are happening. Those who will be saved are people who take refuge in Allah ﷻ. Precautionary measures or arrangements are of no use. Whoever enters the mercy and protection of Allah ﷻ will be saved. Otherwise, let them protect themselves as much as they want, it will be of no effect. Nothing can stand in the way of Allah’s ﷻ Greatness. He says, return to Allah ﷻ, neither the mountain will save you nor anything else. It is Allah Azza wa Jalla who will save you.

The protection of Allah Azza wa Jalla is the greatest protection. Everything is in Allah’s ﷻ hands. He says: ‘ نُ وكُ َيَف نكُ ’, ‘He says to it, “Be”, and it is.’ (Qur’ān 36:82). When He ﷻ says “Be”, it happens. When He ﷻ says “Don’t be”, it doesn’t happen. When He ﷻ says “Be”, no one can stand in front of Him. And when He ﷻ says “Don’t be”, no one can do anything. That’s why people should turn to Allah ﷻ. They put masks or this and that thing on, but these are of no benefit. What is of benefit is to take refuge in Allah ﷻ. Put your trust in Allah ﷻ and turn to Allah ﷻ so that Allah ﷻ may have mercy on you and protect you.

People have rebelled, extremely rebelled. Since the world was founded, people have not been so rebellious. They do great evil and commit great sins. The places are empty, yet they pretend that there is nothing and act as they please. But Allah ﷻ also teaches them a lesson and punishes them. The punishment here is nothing, the greater punishment is in the hereafter. Therefore, turn to Allah ﷻ. Don’t let the shaytan and his associates deceive you. May Allah ﷻ protect us. May Allah ﷻ protect us all insha’Allah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
