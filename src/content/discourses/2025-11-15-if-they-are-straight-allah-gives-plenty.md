As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh. A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Wa-allawi staqāmū ‘alá ṭ-ṭarīqati la-asqaynāhum mā’an ghadaqā” (72:16). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘Aẓīm. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says, if they are straight upon istiqāmah – this applies to everyone, all mankind – if they are straight on the right way, there will be enough of everything for them, and even more, says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. After being straight upon istiqāmah, Allāh has provided everyone with his rizq. But mankind does not keep upon istiqāmah. What is meant by istiqāmah is being straight. If everyone were to be straight, without deceiving anyone, without harming anyone, minding their own business, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla has provided for each what is enough for them. But because people follow the shayṭāns – ego, desires, shayṭān and dunyā: the four enemies of mankind – they cannot keep upon istiqāmah at all. They cannot remain straight on the right way. They cannot stay upon justice; they do not practise justice. Therefore, dunyā has become torture for everyone. And those who do not keep upon istiqāmah are tortured even more. The more they go on crooked ways, lie, and do what is wrong, the more they stray from the way, and it does not benefit them in any way. Materially also, it becomes a life without barakah. It becomes a lifetime of no barakah for them. Now, the world’s state is always in that direction. People, schools, madrasahs, universities. What do they teach? They supposedly teach the right thing. And while teaching what is right, they end up teaching even more of what is wrong. They direct people as they like, saying, “If you do this, you will gain that. If you do that, you will gain more.” And then they gain nothing. They obtain nothing but harm. What they obtain is nothing but evil. Because istiqāmah must also be upon the command of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Without that, that istiqāmah will certainly, in the end, become a means to stray from the right way. Therefore, istiqāmah is important. Once there is istiqāmah – they say the world is eight billion now. It is enough for eighty billion. But in this state, it is not enough for them. It is not even enough for them. So, they are devouring each other. They devour each other, thinking, “If this person eats, I will not eat.” They do not trust each other. They stray from the right way. They also claim and show what they do as if it were a skill. They also lead others astray. The person who leads others astray, his punishment – however many people stray through him, their sins are also loaded upon him. May Allāh protect us. In many places in Qur’ān ‘Aẓīmu sh-Sha’n, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla mentions this matter of istiqāmah, of being straight. Muslims should be the first to do this. Unfortunately, Muslims are the most distant from istiqāmah. May Allāh guide them all. May Allāh protect us from the evils of our egos and the evil of shayṭān.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, saying: Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Wa-allawi staqāmū ‘alá ṭ-ṭarīqati la-asqaynāhum mā’an ghadaqā” (72:16). Meaning, if they are going straight, Allāh will give them plenty of barakah. Qur’ān saying, “mā’an ghadaqa”, meaning, water, plenty of water. Water, meaning the best thing for human being. Without water, they cannot live. For this, He give this. Of course, Qur’ān have millions of meanings. But this also for, to show people to be in straight way. Not going of the outside of way. What is this straight way? It is... Humanity, Allāh create human like this: to be straight, to be merciful, to be helpful for each other. And to live in this earth together, and to help each other. But what people they are doing? No one listening for this. Very rare people who are in straight. All of them, outside of way, outside of nature of human being. They do something, it is become, make people like wild animal. [They] become like crazy, become like... very ugly thing they are doing. For this, it is, they don’t have, Allāh not help them, not give them happiness, not give them satisfaction. Only miseries, they had. But Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, His promise... We have now eight billion people in earth. If they are in straight way, it is enough for eighty billion people. But even for eight billion, it is not enough for them. They are fighting each other, to get from each other as much they can grab, as much they steal, as much they cheat people to take from them. This is not straight. It is outside of way of Allāh. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, He promise if they are in way... And we are seeing also, most of, unfortunately, this who going outside of way – Muslim, more than others even. For this we seeing everywhere, whole Muslim country, they have no value, no richness. Allāh give them every richness but they don’t... Poor, people poor because their governor... Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, saying, “Kamā takūnū, yuwallá ‘alaykum.” As you are, Allāh put one from you, in top of you, it is still same. Why you are complaining for governor or for who ruling you? And he is coming, not come from moon, not come from Neptune, not coming from this. He is from your same country, same people. One of you. He is your president, your minister. If you also same, you will be same. Why? Because you don’t have straight, istiqāmah. You don’t have this istiqāmah. For this, the most important thing, Allāh saying, but people not listening for this at all. All, they thinking for small thing, not thinking for big reward, and big, what Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla will give you next life, if you are in istiqāmah. Not only here, in next life also. In this life also, who has, he is honest and he is in straight way, he is every time winner. Don’t think if you are crook, you will be more winner, you will be more, you have more money. No. If you are only just honest, and do with... be not crook, not cheating people, you will be also, in this life also. Allāh help you, Allāh happy with you and He will give you. So don’t think about this people. Many people now, not looking for themselves, looking for others. “One, he is rich because he done this. Other, he done that, he is rich. So we must be like them.” No. Don’t cheat yourself. Don’t destroy your life, destroy your ākhirah. Allāh keep us in way of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, way of ṣaḥābah, with way of barakah, in shā’a Llāh. Allāh help us to not follow shayṭān, our ego, in shā’a Llāh. Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
