Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistāni, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Fa-man yuridi Llāhu an yahdiyahu yashraḥ ṣadrahu lil-Islām wa-man yurid an yuḍillahu yaj‘al ṣadrahu ḍayyiqan ḥarajan ka-annamā yaṣṣa‘‘adu fī s-samā’ " (6:125). Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla saying: who AllāhNone want for him good, khayr, HeNone open his heart for Islām and he is happy, he has satisfaction with Islām. But who are – AllāhNone HeNone don't want him to be good for him, HeNone make his heart narrow and it like very tight as if it is something pulling him, squeezing him up. So, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are, in shā’a Llāh, we are happy with this majlis, with Islām, with ṭarīqah, with mashāyikh. We are not happy with other thing.

Maybe we are can go everywhere but most important thing to be in this way. AllāhNone open for us this dunyā "kulū wa-shrabū wa lā tusrifū" (7:31) – you can enjoy yourself, you can do everything but in limit of Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla what HeNone put for us. And it is more than enough for human being but of course they want more and more and more and more enjoyment. They thought it is enjoyment but no it is just like fire. You put more need more, you put more it need more. For our ego you cannot make it satisfied. All they, they need more. But, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, what we are get from Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla HeNone put us in way of ProphetNone, way of ṣaḥābah and mashāyikh and mu’minīn. This is important thing. This thing, many people they don't know this but AllāhNone make some reason, somebody to show them way of happiness, way of Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla, ProphetNone. There is some people like this because millions of people they are going without knowing anything.

But Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla when HeNone want something HeNone make somebody to be like key to open the heart and Mawlānā Shaykh he was opener for heart of people and take them to hidāyah. And one of them or the very precious one Mawlānā he bring him and he was key to open this heart of this area. Because this area it was no any awliyā’u Llāh like Mawlānā can open. So, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, AllāhNone send Mawlānā and touch marḥūm Shaykh Raja Ashman and from one man he is – I'm thinking looking around because the most of people he didn't bring them from mosque. He didn't bring them from madrasah. Most of them they are forgetting Islām, only looking for Western. They educated in Western because fashion in this area to be educate. Who want to more educate, to be educate in Western university. I ask people why they are going to Australia? They going, they here this area they go Australia. Our place they going more to Europe. They said better. You have university not enough? He said no better to go there. So the who are studying that in this university, most of them they are forgetting about Islām, about īmān, about anything from this area. But AllāhNone when HeNone want something, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, through Shaykh Raja whole this people they are he catch them, who are maybe never putting sajdah for never. All of them they become, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, strong mu’min. Not only Muslim, mu’min – for Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla saying for our "don't say you are mu’min, you are Muslim. Īmān, you don't have īmān." Īmān you need Shaykh to give it connection to put īmān in your heart. So, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, with through Mawlānā and through Raja Ashman HeNone make this people to follow the best way in Islām. Way of ProphetNone pure looking for heart of Islām, to repair and prepare heart to be good, to be real heart.

Not for cover, you have nice cover – sometimes we have walnut, very good cover [shell] but when you crush it only black, nothing inside, finish. Nothing, only but cover is strong. You must crush by stone but when you are open, no benefit. Even ant they cannot eat because nothing inside, only like dust, black thing inside. And our aim to make this people from their heart to be have this sweetness, this power, this light. Light you cannot put from for you heart from any place except following and obeying mashāyikh and mashāyikh they are not looking for dunyā. They are looking only for ākhirah. Real life not this life, they are looking for life but life, real life not this short life. But there is now also to make people to use their life with short life for their eternal life, for eternity. They teach them, they test them, they train them.

Many of them, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, with, I saw here also with this area, mā shā’a Llāh, many Naqshbandī people. Most of them coming in 2000. 2000 year, the year of millenium, Mawlānā was making test for whole murīd around the world. They said in 2000 everything it can be finish, so you must be have place outside of big city. In many place they try to do something. They are looking to go out. Many of them they have this place. Other place in the world also they do this. The most of after this normally – we don't know spiritually – but from outside of what we see, nothing happen. There was of course some objecting from new Muslim. They are normal. Normal for them because they are mu’allafa qulūb – we cannot say anything. But still, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, there also nothing happen for them. Our, this area people okay. Turkey okay. Everywhere okay. Only one place it was real disappointed and many of them run away from ṭarīqah – this country they are famous with trader – Lebanon. They was writing against Mawlānā in newspapers accusing him many thing and so from this many of this, who are was following Mawlānā, run away from ṭarīqah.

But other place no, al-ḥamdu liLlāh. But what funny thing for this to be there is many wisdom also from this. In Lebanon this people they thought it's finished. Everybody running to bank taking credit, buying a new house, new car so after 2000 nothing, no one can take this back from us. So they do this and after they making angry with Mawlānā. "We take all this..." and Mawlānā he didn't... He said to you, "It is, it will be, in shā’a Llāh, finish this world. Why you taking new car? Why you take money? Why?" Ya‘nī there is many wisdom but, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, the only this place they was not passing test – very few people they left there. But other place, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, and this is also wisdom from Mawlānā, to show people to not follow; how from small thing they can be leave all this bless, all this favour; forgetting everything. You must not looking for dunyā. You must look for Mawlānā Shaykh say. Only you continue. Not happen ok. What is no problem we are with Mawlānā, with our Shaykh. We are in paradise now in world. In shā’a Llāh, in ākhirah also we will be with them in paradise. This is the important thing and no matter for... but need people to learn wisdom.

And of course when you are in university, you in school, even small school, every two month, three month they make test. They make looking for your level. You can pass this to second class or you will be in same class or you will be run away – but fired no fired. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, even what they done when somebody coming to ṭarīqah, they leave, they keep him but maybe in kindergarten every year – not going up, going down. This is what happen and, al-ḥamdu liLlāh. But what I'm happy, going all around the world.

Last year we was also in Argentina also they have, they make big and they buy big land and they was maybe four, five family but now they have maybe thousand in that area. Family living with children with... it was good for them to go away from city and it is they have barakah from Mawlānā. Mawlānā was happy because... Awliyā’u Llāh, somebody saying awliyā’u Llāh they are knowing everything. They know but what Allāh HeNone want them to know. They are not 24 hour looking what you do, what you done. It is not important for them. Because you are human being, you can do. But what is important, they, Allāh HeNone let them to know. This is you can know, you can say for them this but no need for 24 hour. This is what AllāhNone saying, "Wa lā tajassasū," (49:12) – "Don't spy," – this meaning. So it is for awliyā’u Llāh more important. For this what it is you must know.

If anything AllāhNone want them to know – you be careful. No need for them to be 24 hour looking for you. But when you are in need you say "madad", they arrive. Read three times Ihklāṣ: "Qul huwa Llāhu Aḥad," madad ya Mawlānā Shaykh Nāzim. He will be helping. Madad ya Shaykh Raja Ashman also he is helping. He is also awliyā’u Llāh, mā shā’a Llāh. And Mawlānā he make him and give him his wilāyah and he serving. After passing away it is more powerful for awliyā’u Llāh – seven times. Their spiritual power becoming more seven times more. So when anything you are in need, you are in difficulty, you are in bad situation, you can ask madad, help from them, and by idhni Llāh, AllāhNone, make them to help you. It is no problem from this.

Many story from awliyā’u Llāh they was helping people when in difficulty in desert or in mountain or in sea. When something happen and they are calling for them they are helping. Not little story, there is thousands of this story from awliyā’u Llāh. But, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are in barakah of Shaykh Raja Ashman and don't be worry, in shā’a Llāh, every time it will be with you. One of – there was one Shaykh everybody they was very happy with him. Awliyā’u Llāh he was good but they was thinking one thing what is this man? He has many sons in his life, one after other they pass away but he was as nothing happen for him. He is smiling as much now his son still alive. No any crying, no anything. People they was saying, this man is very good, this awliyā’u Llāh and he is... but why he didn't, he not have mercy? He not have love for his son? And at the end they ask him. He said, "You are, your sons all pass away why you still like this?" He said, "Pass away but I still with them. I'm seeing them in my heart. They are not in eye seeing, I see them by heart. When I see them by heart no need to be sad to not be any sadness. I'm always with them so I'm not sad. Who are not seeing, they can be sad, but I all time I'm with them."

So, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, Shaykh Raja also all time with us. In shā’a Llāh, by with heart all time we are also with him. We feeling him everywhere so, in shā’a Llāh, our life also will be one time it will finish but, in shā’a Llāh, in ākhirah we will be forever with him, with Mawlānā in Jannah, in shā’a Llāh, all of you. AllāhNone make us to be all time together, all time in this way, way of ProphetNone, way of happiness. Real happiness this is.

Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
