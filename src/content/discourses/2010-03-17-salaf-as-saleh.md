Dastur Ya Sayyidi, Ya Sultan al Awliya madad.

La ilaha illa Allah La ilaha illa Allah La ilaha illa Allah Sayyidina Nabiyyina wa Mawlana Muhammad Rasulullah Habibullah, Nur Arshillah.

Assalamu alaykum Ya Ibadallah, O our Lord's servants. Servanthood is highest honour for all Mankind. Angels’ levels are different and their honours are different also. The honour of Mankind is different one hundrend percent and we are knowing that the highest level of honour for Mankind is servanthood. Salamu alayka Ya Sayyidi, Ya Sultan al Awliya on this planet, on this world. We are asking your heavenly support. We can't speak or understand anything here if your heavenly support is not reaching us.

We are saying: Audhu Billahi min as-shaytan ar-rajim. Shaytan is always asking to make people to be in wrong understanding, wrong effort, wrong action and wrong life. And we are running to the representative of the Lord of Heavens to grant us from heavenly support so that we may be able to face wrong people, wrong creation, wrong ideas, wrong beliefs and everything that is wrong. For coming days, we are running to that one who is responsible for every action that Mankind is doing now

O People we are saying: Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Rahim. Take heavenly sword against shaytanic centers, shaytanic ideas, shaytanic understandings, shaytanic wrong beliefs. Take through your hand Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Rahim. That is our power, the heavenly sword, against people who are always on wrong way and wrong ideas. Now we are saying: Audhu Billahi min as-shaytan ar-rajim.

Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Rahim gives to you power. Through Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Rahim heavenly power reaches every point of our bodies. That is a heavenly grant. When saying Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Rahim no one can understand the light through the 19 letters of those holy words. Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Rahim, I think it is 19 letters, not completing to 20. No one knows to make it two-zero, leaving one and nine. And that is just full with secret knowledge, with secret powers. That may bring a dead body into life. Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Rahim. Once there was Hasan al Basri. You know about that...Salafi ulamas? You heard about Hasan al Basri? I think he was Tabi`een. He reached to the Sahaba, companions of the Seal of Prophets (sas). To reach one of them gives so much honour to people because Sahaba, the companions of the Seal of Prophets (sas) are like fountains; springs of the subjects of heavenly life. Therefore those who were reaching them reached a pleasure of the life that reaches to Sahaba. If you are saying you are belonging to Salaf as-Saleh are you adding Hasan al Basri as one of them?

You must know about the Salaf as-Saleh, the time of the Sahaba, companions of the Seal of Prophets (sas). Then coming followers of Sahaba, then coming the followers of those ones. What you are saying through Islamic terms Rasul salawatullah wa salam alayh giving from his secret knowledge to his companions and those most strong ones, the Sahaba, the companions because they were on the base of the building of Islam. They must be most powerful. If a person building a high building he puts under it such a strong foundation to carry it. Therefore Sahaba, companions are most powerful ones to carry Islam and its heavy responsibilities for coming generations up to the Day of Resurrection.

Who are your Salaf as-Saleh? You may say some names. I am asking those people who are claiming we are following Salaf al Saleh. How many from those ones do you know? And how many of them whose names you may say? Their situations, their positions, their identities, their real beings? The Seal of Prophets (sas) when he reached his Lord's Divine Presence, left behind him on Earth 124,000 Companions, Sahaba. Your Salaf as-Saleh, you are adding those ones as Salaf as-Saleh or you are leaving them off? Why are you saying 'We are followers of Salaf as-Saleh?' Why are you not saying, 'We are followers of Sahaba'? Why you are making that distinction? Why you are saying 'We are followers of Salaf as-Saleh'? What do you mean when you are saying 'Salaf as-Saleh'? Sahaba were not Salaf as-Saleh?

The Salaf as-Saleh, from where are they taking heavenly knowledge? Heavenly knowledge you can't find on Earth. That is important. That is teaching all nations, showing them their ways to heavens. Why you are saying 'We are Salaf al Saleh followers'? Who is that? Who are they? How many? Say? 124,000 Sahaba were Salaf as-Saleh or not? And the Seal of Prophets (sas) was saying:

ashabi kal-nujum bi ayyihim iqtadaytum ihtadaytum

Please say those Salaf as-Saleh's names. Write through your books, ‘We are maqsudana -our intention, our goal, maybe, in saying Salaf as-Saleh we are thinking or we are asking to make clear for people someone’. Don't say Salaf as-Saleh. And you are saying, Ibn al Qayyim, Ibn Taymiyya (Abdul Wahab) three of them only? And you are taking away 124,000 Sahaba? Wahabi People! Say!? The Companions of the Seal of Prophets (sas), were they not Salaf as-Saleh? What do you mean?! Every time saying, 'Salafi ulama'. Who are they? Companions of the Seal of Prophets, (sas) if they are not Saliheen that means Islam is just finished. Do you think that 3 common ones can carry the building of islam? Hmm? What is that foolishness? You must know, fa man shazza shuzza bi'n-nar abadan -that to make yourself apart from majority you are leaving sawad al azam, the Greater Majority and you are a handful people. But always you are throwing on Ahl al Sunnah wal Jama`a you are not saying 'We are Ahl al Sunnah wal Jama`a', you are saying, 'We are Salaf us-Saleh and our followers are on right way'. No you and your followers are on wrong way.

You must say 'We are with the Greater Majority, we are with them'. Why you are making this shuzuz, to be different? Why? What is the reason for what you are doing? To where reaching your opinions? To where reaching your beliefs? Those are all wrong beliefs. La hawla wa la quwwata illa Billahi'l Aliyy'il Azim. My companions are like the stars on the sky and whichever one you are following, you are following Right Way and reaching to Paradise. Those are the ways reaching to Paradise.

Therefore it is a terrible responsibility now for a handful people to say, "We are Salafis, you must follow us". We are not following you. We are following Sahaba, the Seal of Prophets' (sas) companions. And after them, Tabi`een. and after them Tabi tabi`een. What you are saying, Ibn al Qayyim, Ibn Tayyim and Muhammad Abdul Wahhab? They are not counted from Sahaba, or Tabi`een or Taba tabi`een. You may be making bid`a. You are taking yourself out from big mass of Muslims and you are shooting Ahl al Sunnah people. Why? It is not for you. You can't do this. You can't do this, you must tell us who are Salaf as-Saleh? What do you mean? Give their names.

Therefore we are saying Hasan al Basri is a follower of Sahaba, a Tabi`een. Following the companions of Sayyidina Rasulullah (sas) and their honours are high also because they are taking from the first level of those who were with the Seal of Prophets (sas). Tabi`een, those who followed Sahaba, companions of the Seal of Prophets (sas). Then Hasan al Basri was Tabi`een. He reached to the companions of the Seal of Prophets (sas). You are accepting Hasan al Basri as Salaf as Saleh? Or so many thousands of followers? The companion, Sufyan al Thawri, Tabi`een, you are accepting him? And those people were they destroying tombs? How could you destroy? Whom are you following?

Hasan al Basri and thousands of them, perhaps millions. One day he was coming to pass from one side to another side of Dijlah. He was waiting and coming upon Habib al Ajami from among the Saliheen. If he wasn't from the Saliheen, his name not reaching to today and we are speaking about him. Habib al Ajami wasn't original Arab. Perhaps from Ajam people. Coming on same place, seeing Hasan al Basri, sitting there and asking "O Shaykh, O Imam, why you are sitting here?" Saying, "Ya Habib, I am waiting for raft". People standing on it, and one making like this, like this, passing across there. And he was saying “I am coming here to cross to the next bank”. “Ya Imam, you are Imam and waiting for that raft? Waiting here for that? Why you are not passing how I am passing? Look”. And, Habib al Ajami saying: Bismillah-ir-Rahman-ir-Rahim, Bismillah-ir-Rahman-ir-Rahim, Bismillah-ir-Rahman-ir-Rahim. There was lightning coming from his body when he was saying Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Rahim and passing, walking on water and going there. "Why you are not saying O Hasan al Basri, Ya Imam, say Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Rahim and you may pass". And crying, Hasan al Basri and saying, "Yet we are not reaching that level of yaqeen, certainty. You reached certainty, you may walk on it". And he passed by.

Power of Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Rahim is never-ending. But we are only saying with our tongues, not saying full, with our beings. If we can say, we pass. Therefore Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Rahim is full with heavenly power. If one of them may say on a dead body Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Rahim "Get up! Stand up!" It must stand up. Why you are not teaching to people that power Ya Salafi ulamas? You ever say such things? I think you don't like 'hikayat'. Stories of Saliheen. There are Saliheen. Saliheen -all those people who have yaqeen, certainty. Certainty. O People, try to reach to that point.

There are some instruments in our meeting-hall for example and we are hearing that these instruments taking this speech addressed to people from East to West. Yes, it is real but I can't arrange this instrument to do what it is going to do. Must be such an expert, a specialist. I can't do anything but these, our brothers know how to use it. And they are sending now from east to west, from a small place. That power also is secret power through Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Rahim but you are not ready to use it therefore you can't use Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Rahim as a wali, a saint is using it. They may use, but not us, we can't use.

Therefore, Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Rahim is full of that power but if you are not knowing how to use it, you can't reach anything. And they are not learning to use these instruments at home, they are not learning from their mums and dads. They learned from expert. Expert. Learning from them and this working. And Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Rahim is a heavenly power that not everyone can touch. There must be authorised one to do that. Hasan al Basri wasn't authorised. Habib al Ajami, whose language wasn't Arabic, was saying Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Rahim and walking on water.

O people, so many things that you must try to learn about Islam's ruhaniyyah, (spirituality). Don't think that Islam is only some actions you are doing and finishing. No! The actions like praying, like fasting, like going to Hijaz, like giving your charity to people. No, there is secret power through praying, through giving sadaqah, charity, to make special actions for your Real Being or special practicing for learning how you can use your spiritual power. All of us have spiritual power. But we are not knowing how we are using. Like here, these three or four instruments we have but if we are not knowing to use it, no benefit.

And for that purpose I must ask: Fas`al Bihi Khabira 25:59

You must ask the one who is knowing the secret power and authority through these instruments which is granted from heavens for the use of Mankind. If not, it is useless also. May Allah forgive us, O People, O People, we must try to learn. And, you can't go for learning Medicine by going to grocery shop. Or you can't go for a building to a doctor to show you how it is going to be built. No. Fas`al bihi khabira, the one who is knowing the secret power and ways it reaches you, you may take that power from them.

All Sahaba were taking their power from the Seal of Prophets (sas) and they are becoming shining stars in heavens. O People, try to understand. My English is not so powerful, but if you are hearing, may Allah grant us a good understanding and to keep the ways of the chosen ones through Mankind as they are experts and specialists for everything on Earth and in heavens.

Allah Allah, Allah Allah, Allah Allah Karim Allah Allah Allah Allah Allah Allah Allah Subhan Allah Allah Allah Allah Allah Allah Allah Sultan Allah Allahumma salli wa sallim wa barik ala Sayyidina Muhammad (sas) wa Alihi wa Sahbihi wa sallim
