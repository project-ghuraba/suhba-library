As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Thank Allah we did the duty of khalwat (seclusion). They made us do it thank Allah: Forty days with the support of our mashāyikh. Shaykhs would do what was much harder in the past. We did it as a similitude. May Allah Jalla Jalaluh accept it as genuine InshaAllah.

Khalwat is a different worship. You are secluded from the world for forty days. News of the world needs to be nonexistent for things not to come to mind. You struggle with ego and Shaitan for forty days. Of course, these things are hard if there is no himmat (support). Thank Allah they are being supportive.

However, it does not work if you say, “We did khalwat and beat our ego.” The ego is with you until the last breath. Therefore, there is no such thing as, “I did it and it is over.” “Continuous greater jihad,” said our Holy Prophet about jihad (struggle) with the ego. Let us continue with it InshaAllah. If people trust their ego and get proud saying they beat their ego, they will have lost. What is important is to continue this drawn inspiration (fayd) InshaAllah. InshaAllah our aim is for the consent of Allah Jalla Jalaluh.

It was necessary to do khalwat, training, after Shaykh Mawlana’s passing. InshaAllah more power comes, more inspiration is drawn, and people benefit InshaAllah. Allah sends more good people and it becomes a means for guidance InshaAllah. May this power reach other people, people who are not in guidance InshaAllah, and may it be a means for their coming to the right path. For them and for all of us InshaAllah. May Allah be our helper and may He not let us abide by our ego.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
