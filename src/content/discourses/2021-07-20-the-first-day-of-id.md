As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Today is ‘Īd. Congratulations on ‘Īdu l-Aḍḥá. May AllāhNone make these virtues last for us. This ‘Īd is a favour of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla for us. Certainly, HeNone gives barakah to those who accept this favour. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is al-Karīm, the Generous. May HeNone give happiness to Muslims, in shā’a Llāh. May there be salvation.

There are more troubles for people and Muslims from day to day. Troubles are increasing. As it is the end of times nowadays, the world is messed up, says our Holy ProphetNone. It is the time when people oppress and kill each other; they don't show mercy to each other. But to seek refuge at the door of AllāhNone is the best thing. AllāhNone won't deny anyone who seeks refuge in HimNone. HeNone will help him. Who seeks refuge in AllāhNone becomes a winner in the end. Because there is hardship in the beginning and then comes ease. A chance is given to oppressors in the beginning and then that chance is taken away from them and they get the punishment they deserve.

Therefore, these feasts are always a means to mercy for people, for Muslims. May your ‘Īd be blessed.

People couldn't go to ḥajj this year. It is the feast of qurbān (sacrifice) and ḥajj as well. Many people made qurbān, shukr to AllāhNone. Our Holy Prophet's ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam order says whoever can make qurbān, but doesn't make it, shouldn't say he prays. Because when a person can afford it but doesn't make it, he doesn't carry out the order of our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. He upsets our Holy ProphetNone. Shukr to AllāhNone, many people who could afford it, made it or had it done for them. Because it is no longer like it was before. In older times, everyone would slaughter their qurbān with their own hands as the sunnah. And it would be a nice thing on that day. And nowadays, even though they do it, it has become more difficult.

Many people are sending to needy people so that qurbān is slaughtered wherever there are more poor people. This is acceptable too. May AllāhNone accept it. May AllāhNone let us celebrate it better and in a better condition next year. May HeNone send aṣ-Ṣāḥib (as) so that he saves people, in shā’a Llāh.

Today, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, first day of ‘Īd feast, ‘Īdu l-Aḍḥá. This blessed, very blessed day also from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. AllāhNone make us to be under HisNone mercy and HisNone generosity, from give us from HisNone generosity from everything. There is billions of things AllāhNone gives us. We are thanking HimNone. But this also they give ‘Īd different. Four days ‘Īd blessed days. HeNone said, "You must be, in shā’a Llāh, accept this present from MeNone be barakah for you, for your family, for human being."

Because nowadays we are living in some difficult time but this is what AllāhNone want because now time of dunyā it is finish slowly slowly. So everything coming together. Because before it was, something happen need many long time, but now everything coming on top of each other and we must be under protection from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla to protect us, to be safe from every bad thing, from every bad people, who attacking human being. They are attacking Islām. They are not listening ‘Īd or not ‘Īd or blessed day or holy day. They are not looking, just they are trying to finish Islām.

But, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, Islām will be not be finish because with difficulty – AllāhNone after difficulty HeNone give easy, easiness. When you in difficult first, you be later easy for you. But if it is easy, after become difficult. Now, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, difficult for Muslim but it will be easy days, nice days, in shā’a Llāh, after this. It will be, in shā’a Llāh, real ‘Īd feast for Muslim, for mu’min when this all oppression people finish and AllāhNone take them away from us. This our praying for Ummah – to be protected and to be, in shā’a Llāh, in right way, way of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. And it is this, we pray ‘Īd and many people they are slaughter sheep or cow or camel. This is order of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam for those people they have enough money to slaughter.

It is better for people to slaughter by themself. And it was first day of ‘Īdu l-Aḍḥá many people it was nice thing to slaughter and to be together, clean and not let anything from this qurbān to throw away. But now they are throwing more of them and many people cannot do by themself because no place, difficult to do. But also they have another thing to send for poor people who are not finding, maybe once a year they eat only, meat. Some of them more than one year. They send this for them and they will be take bless[ing] from AllāhNone for this ṣadaqah they do for these people.

AllāhNone accept our qurbān and, in shā’a Llāh, make us to be for next year better and better and better, to send us Sayyidunā Mahdī (as) to save us from this oppression people.
