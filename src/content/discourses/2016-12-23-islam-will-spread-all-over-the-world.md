Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Our Holy Prophet (SAW) states:

“Alfitnatu na’imatun la’anallahu man ayqathaha.” It is End Times now and dissension is plenty. It is not hard to cause fitna. It is easy. Our Holy Prophet (SAW) says, “Cursed be the one who causes fitna.”

Whoever causes dissension may that one be cursed. We acknowledge the words of our Holy Prophet (SAW). It could be a hodja, it could be a scholar, a man or a woman, it does not matter whoever it may be. Those who cause fitna are cursed. “Cursed be the one,” says our Holy Prophet (SAW). We are also saying to them, “May you be cursed!”

It says fitna is worse than killing a man. It could happen that a man fights with someone and kills him, but when it is a dissension it harms all Muslims. This is what is meant by fitna. So fitna is what Shaitan wants and the path shown by Shaitan. Never did any prophet order for dissension to occur. Neither did they allow it.

It is End Times, it is times of fitna. Let us be careful accordingly. Muslims need to be awake. They need to be on the path told by our Holy Prophet (SAW). The path he has clearly shown is the most beautiful way for people, the bravest way. There is no backstabbing and there is no evil. However, a group of people, I do not know how they do it, show it as if it was in Islam and cause dissension. Whereas they would be obeying Shaitan, and helping Shaitan’s soldiers and Shaitan’s friends.

It is End Times, as we said, and Shaitan is attacking Islam with all his might. They want to root out Islam. They do this through all means. They cannot do it openly, but they are trying to do it by making Muslims fight each other. And they have been pretty successful. Their being successful means fitna is out and spreading. Allah Azza wa Jalla will certainly make His promise come true. Islam will spread all over the world and besides true Islam, clean Islam. There cannot be backstabbing Islam. Those who are backstabbing and causing fitna are crews, groups. Islam is clean. It is brave.

Nobody wants harm to befall someone you took under your protection in your country. When an agreement was made with the Polytheists during our Holy Prophet’s (SAW) time, when someone sought refuge with them they would take the person under protection and nobody could touch that one.

People of today have really gone nuts. As we said, this shows people have gone astray. Most people do not care for the words of our Holy Prophet (SAW). It is something openly said and seen, and it is known by everybody. As we said, even Mushriks would keep their word in the past during our Holy Prophet’s (SAW) time.

Now, they say they are Muslim and kill Muslims. They say they are Muslim and hit people living in safety in the country. They are under the protection of Islam living in an Islamic country. Their hair should not be harmed. This has been so throughout history. Non-Muslims would live as Thimmis in Muslim countries, in the Ottoman [State], and the ones before. Everybody had their temples, chapels, churches, and synagogues. Everybody would live their religion in peace and security. Those who wanted would become Muslim and those who wanted could keep their religion.

There is no evil in this and there is no contradiction. This is Islam’s tolerance. In response to this, you could not find one Muslim in Europe. Was it possible to build a mosque until now? We went to Spain last year. Mosques have been turned into churches. When they saw us, those men did so much to get us out of there. They would oppress Muslims saying, “You will either die or become Christian!” So it was as such and it was not possible otherwise.

The religion of Islam is the religion of tolerance, the religion of beauty, not the religion of bullying. Those who are Islam have no fear of anybody. If there is something bad, people fear so others do not to see it and do it. They continuously tyrannized people so they would not lift their heads. However, the religion of Islam is not so. It is open for everybody, it can be seen, and it can show its beauties. But Shaitan wants to show the exact opposite with dissension. He is trying as much as possible with all his might to reach this purpose and to reach this goal. Let him try, but in the end they will be at loss again.

Unbelief never rises. People who are deceived by the unbeliever and expect help from the kafir against the Muslim become despicable, become vile, become disreputable and always looks like a traitor. Traitors never have esteem. Nobody gives them regard or listens to them. Allah will surely question them for what they did, and their real regret will be in the hereafter.

Now they comply with their ego and try with all their might saying, “These people did things to us. Let us harm them,” but it is useless. Its harm is on themselves. What Allah says happens. Certainly the whole world will become Muslim as Allah promises. They will all give an account. Those who become Muslim become Muslim, and those who do not can fit themselves. But the promise of Allah is truth and this will certainly happen.

The punishments they will receive in the end of the public and hidden attacks made against Islam will be accepted by everyone. These people cannot stand us where we are and cannot stand Islam. They especially cannot stand those who are Ahl-e Sunnat wal Jamaat, those who are on the path of our Holy Prophet (SAW).

They are helping people with all kinds of perverted mentalities and thoughts. Whoever it may be: racist, sectarian, this and that. They are helping them all. They are helping them secretly and publicly. They are also attacking Islam and attacking our Prophet (SAW) with all their might. What does this mean? This means when Allah (JJ) gives them their punishment they cannot say, “Allah did us wrong.” They will have deserved that punishment.

Allah knows how much longer it will go now on. Five months, ten months, one year, or five years. It will go on for a certain time but they say, “Kullu aatin qareeb.” It is a hadith meaning “every arriving is near.” We were here these times last year too and the year was ending. The end of this year has also arrived and one year has passed since. So it passes instantly. There is no need for sorrow. We will rely on Allah. All the people who deserve punishment will suffer their punishment. May Allah keep all of us safe and not mislead us from the right path Inshallah.

Inshallah Mahdi Alayhis Salam arrives and we become his soldiers. Not one unbeliever will be left then. All of them will have been questioned that time, they would receive their punishment, and the world would be cleansed. Because unbelief is filth, kufr is immorality, and kufr is tyranny. Whatever evil there is it is in disbelief and it is with the unbeliever. Never believe they think well. We say, “May Allah guide them,” but Allah knows. Whoever comes to guidance comes from now on. The door of repentance is open, and those who do not repent suffer their punishment.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq. Al-Fatiha.
