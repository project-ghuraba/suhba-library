As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

About the ocean, I remember Mawlānā Shaykh in his last days was going every time to the seaside and saying “Madad Ya Sayyidinā Ilyās”. There are two Prophets, Sayyidinā Al-Khidr and Sayyidinā Ilyās, whom Allah gave life that they will be the last people to die in this world. Sayyidinā Al-Khidr ʿalayhi s-salām is for land. And Sayyidinā Ilyās ʿalayhi s-salām is for the ocean, for the sea.

When someone is in trouble, they help him. Ilyās ʿalayhi s-salām helps people in the sea, ocean. And Sayyidinā Al-Khidr ʿalayhi s-salām helps on land. They have a mission. They have a duty. This is their job. So I just wanted to say this, to give this ṣuḥbah from Mawlānā.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
