As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh. A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah. Our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says: Bi-mā ma‘ná l-ḥadīth. “There is a group from my ummah that is upon the truth.” This group – what we mean by group is jamā‘ah, a community. He says, “Their way is my way.” It is the way of truth. That true way continues until the Day of Judgement. The true way, shukr to Allāh, is the way of the ṭarīqahs. Other things – those that are not ṭarīqahs – many thousands appeared, and many thousands disappeared. Not one of them was upon the way of our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. They continued on whatever way suited themselves. They all came to an end, but another takes their place, then that one ends, and yet another appears. So they keep following the same pattern – but of course, it is not the same. They all have different things. Their goal is not the path shown by our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, but rather the path that their egos and selves desire. By following the way their ego desires, they leave the right path. The way of our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam – Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm, “Ijtanibū kathīran mina ẓ-ẓanni inna ba‘ḍa ẓ-ẓanni ithmun wa-lā tajassasū” (49:12). “Do not make bad assumptions,” says our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. He says, stay away from bad assumptions. Then he says, don’t spy either. When you avoid these things, you will have fulfilled our Prophet’s order. Now we are in the days of ‘Āshūrā’. We are approaching the day of ‘Āshūrā’ in Muḥarram. The day of ‘Āshūrā’ is a day that our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam loved. He said to value it and fast. Fast and perform worship. There are several different pieces of advice given and actions shown by our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam for that day. Do them; that is enough. Do not give much importance to other things. “What happened on that day, what remained?” These are things based on assumptions, intended to make people suspicious of one another. What truly matters is the test for each person. We must look at whether a person listens to the words of our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam or not. Our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says, don’t make bad assumptions. Mind your own business. As for what else has happened or remains, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla will judge it. He is the Judge. Nothing is lost. What is lost is a person’s peace and their īmān. If they fail the test, that is the outcome. May Allāh protect us. The path of Islām is difficult to hold on to. You must certainly remain on the right path, because if you let go of it from one side, you will surely gain nothing from any side. While trying to hold on one side, it slips away from the other. But if you are on the right path, and walk steadfastly on the way of our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, have no fear – safety is yours. Your end will be safety. Because you will be among the company of our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, among the group upon the true path, among those on the true way. They are the victorious. They are also a means of guidance for others. No matter how many people may stray, ultimately they are saved by their barakah. So the path of Islām, shukr to Allāh, continues together with those upon this path. That beautiful way is the way of our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. May Allāh grant us all the ability to remain on this path. Otherwise, as we said, if you say, “Let’s step out of this path a little,” yes, you may step out – but whether you can return, that is uncertain. Therefore, may Allāh protect us. May we be upon this right path – the path praised by our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, in shā’a Llāh.
