ﺎﻣﻮﻗ اﻮﺒKﺼ۪ ﺗ نا اﻮﻨﻴFﺘﻓ ﺎ “Yā ayyuhā l-ladhīna āmanū in jā’akum fāsiqun bi-nabā’in fatabayyanū an tu#ībū qawman bi-jahālatin fa-tu#bi!ū ‘alā mā fa‘altum nādimīn” (Qur’ān 49:6) “O believers, if someone morally astray brings you any news, verify [it],” so that you do not harm people unknowingly, becoming regretful for what you have done.” Allah ‘azza wa jalla says when you hear news, research it. Find out whether it is true or not, and act accordingly, for otherwise you might be sorry. So that you may not do things that you will regret later, don’t believe any news that you hear: do not consider it true at first. Research it to find out whether it is true or not. When you have some task or job to do, verify the facts, otherwise you might be sorry later.

And this applies to everything, not just against those hostile, but also to work, to travel. It applies to everything. You should not believe everything you hear. People are deceived by such, so Allah is warning us. Especially during these days now with us having computers and Internet not [just] thousands, [but] hundreds of thousands, millions, of unnecessary news reports and lies are everywhere. Don’t follow it, and don’t get upset, sad, or feel uneasy. Sick people enjoy lies, and like to make people uncomfortable. We should not heed them or such news. We receive weird, strange news, and I mean, it's unbelievable, but there are a lot of people who believe it, as if it were the word of Allah, [although] they don’t believe the [actual] Quran. Whatever is written there [in these report] is used to make people anxious and uneasy. It would be better not even to look at that stuff at all, because even if you look and read it without believing, it still might make you uneasy and anxious.

Allah ‘azza wa jalla is merciful. He warns people so that they will not be restless, and so they may not be deceived. Allah ‘azza wa jalla created people, the nafs, and [also created] Shaytan. All end up doing evil. Allah ‘azza wa jalla knows, [and informs us] so that we might not be fooled. First of all, there are many of these [sources] who claim to be Muslim and tell us to do such and such, to do this and that, and [then also] there are those who are not Muslims [also saying such]. There is all kinds of devilishness. Allah tells us to be careful so we might not be regretful later. May Allah not make us sorry in the end. May He strengthen our faith. Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-F ti ah
