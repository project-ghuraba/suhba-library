Salamu Alaikum. Ahlan wa Sahlan/Welcome. Welcome to you. Allah Almighty gives you much more honour.

That is really, really the day of, birthday of one- He is only Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin. It is only for Hz Muhammad Mustafa (sas). Birthday/Mawlid is celebrated only for him, can't be for anyone else. But they have made it a custom. Alhamdulillah it was just yesterday night, the birthday of Seal of the Prophets (sas). Just it is going to be suitable for His servant's birthday also. And we are coming here for a celebration. Really I am celebrating birthday of the Seal of Prophets (sas).

Welcome to you. Next year insha Allah, I hope that it should be a big ceremony that Mahdi (as) comes & the flags of Islam going to be up everywhere! And shaytan's sultanate just going to finish, and Islam's sultanate coming over all the world.

May Allah forgive us, forgive you, forgives me. I am weak one. I am, with your prayings. I am happy that I am coming for a celebration for the Seal of Prophets, just yesterday night. May Allah bless you. O people, O people! My sons, my daughters, my brothers, my sisters...

O mankind, you must be happy by celebrating of the Seal of Prophets, Sayidina Muhammed (sas). Pray for me please, that this heaviness leaves me & I am going to be with you everywhere insha'Allah (swt). As-salamu alaikum wa Rahmatullahu wa Barakatuhu.

Happy birthday - only for one and his happiness going be through his whole nation, Ummatu l Muhammad (sas). Miladul Nabi may be mubarak/blessing upon you, O brothers, sisters, friends. Allahumma thabbitna `ala al-Haqq, Allahumma thabbitna `ala al-Haqq, Allahumma thabbitna `ala al-Haqq, bi jahi man anzalta alayhi Surat Al Fatiha. To be with you insha Allah soonerly. May Allah bless you, for the honour of the Seal of Prophets Sayidina Muhammad (sas).
