Shaykh Mohammad Nazim Al-Haqqani An-Naqshbandi, Sohbat June 13, 2010.

Dastur Ya Sayyidi, madad Ya Sultan al awliya Allah Allah, Allah Allah Allah Allah, Aziz Allah Allah Allah, Allah Allah Allah Allah, Subhan Allah Allah Allah, Allah Allah Allah Allah, Sultan Allah. Your blessings O our Lord, give Your most glorious, most praised, most majesty for Your most beloved one, who was created for every creation. The real reason for heavenly and Majestic demands of the Lord of Heavens, is the Seal of Prophets, Sayyidina Muhammad, sallallahu alayhi wa sallam. And we are asking for all holy ones that are belonging to heavenly stations through this life that Allah may grant them much more honour and power.

O People, our attenders, Assalamu alaykum wa rahmtullahi wa barakatuhu, masha Allah. First, coming from heavenly levels are praisings and honour on those people who are knowing they are servants. And they are humble ones and they are knowing for what they have been created and they are taking much more care for their real missions. The mission of mankind is only to be servants for their Creator, the Lord of Heavens. Allahu Akbar.

Salamu alaykum our attenders. O our attenders, don't leave to be among yourselves spying people, because shaytan sends its spies for spying to look at ‘what that one is speaking, and what kind of wrong things he may say, and we may take that wrong one and make a show through east and west’. And I am saying, Who is making that servant to be here for addressing, is giving His heavenly protection to that weak one, not leaving him through your hands, so that your eyes are not going to see, and your ears not to hear, or your tongues not to speak against heavenly declarations and may Allah Almighty grant us His divine protection.

We are saying: audhu Billahi min ash-shaytani r-rajim. O our Lord make us far away from shaytan and its followers. As long as they are not asking to hear truth, they are deaf ones. As long as they are not asking to see what is true position, they are not looking because they are blind ones. Their tongues can't speak because they are not able to understand, to prevent real declarations for reaching mankind. We say audhu Billahi min ash-shaytani r-rajim, that is the cover that surrounds us.

When we are saying audhu Billahi min ash-shaytani r-rajim, they can't see, they can't hear, they can't speak, they can't understand. And, their positions are under the level of animals. They have eyes, they can't see. They have ears, they can't hear. They have tongues but they are not able to stand up and be shahid, witness for truth. And we are coming and Allah Almighty, Subhanahu wa Ta'ala asks His servants to be true ones, to be true witnesses for Truth, Eternal Truth. Yes, they have only been created for seeing Truth, or hearing Truth, or witnessing Truth and for understanding heavenly Truth. We have been offered, that is a heavenly offer for mankind. And we are saying: Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim, O our Lord, You are our Lord, make us to understand and to see and to hear and to be witness for Eternal Truth. Even animals, O People, all of them on their levels are witnessing the Truth and also, every atom, they too are witnessing – fa fi kulli shayin lahu ayatun tadullu annahu wahidun. That is heavenly declaration fafi kulli shayin lahu ayah tadullu annahu wahidun. That is true, correct.

If you are accepting your prophet, peace be upon him, and his inheritors, they are not leaving you with mistakes. No, they are correcting them. Therefore, sometimes, coming some inspirations and when they are saying in another way they are making it more clear because we are surrendering to them, and they are responsible. When they are putting such a weak servant for addressing all nations, they have a responsibility for that weak servant not to be on mistakes. Correcting, correcting till they understand.

It doesn't matter, if you put milk in a big pot, or in a small pot, or through a glass, or put it in a cup. Doesn't matter, out-looking (outer appearance) is not important. Important, muhtawa, what is really in it. That is important. It is important to make people understand. Make like this, make like that. We are attenders and we are like students for preliminary school, small ones. The teacher is correcting. And therefore sometimes we may say something, maybe its out-looking is different but what is its content, what it is containing is the same.

You may drink water through your hand, you may drink water from a golden cup, you may drink warm water from an ordinary pot, you may drink from a silver cup. It out-looking is not important, but what is in it. That is important. Therefore we are correcting, doesn't matter. I know nothing, but they know and they are muraqabah, observing what we are speaking. Correcting it, till people understand. But if people who are attending such a meeting or association, coming, sitting, but renting their understanding somewhere, to someone, what are they going to understand? Nothing.

Therefore be with your position in every moment, be what you have been asked to be. Here are so many instruments, if those people do not take care about it, it will not work. But if taking care, it is not important if their models are different models, no, important to understand. Therefore there, our guides are looking at our positions if we are coming here and giving wholly, in such a way.

People are coming and as they said, some of them put their understanding on rent, that means their hearts - because the centre of understanding is not here, here, through their hearts and their hearts through east and west, going, coming, making business, and they are sitting here, how can it be? What he can take? Nothing. If you are making like this, may put in it. If making like that, who is coming like this, is taking nothing. But if making like this, something must come. Make it more wide. Men, their capacity, their physical being is so unimportant. Our physical being is so small but our understanding capacity, is reaching from east to west, from north to south, from earth to heavens. Therefore we have been honoured by the Lord of Heavens, Allah Almighty with such a qualification that no other creation is granted. That is one meaning, when Allah Almighty saying:

wa idh qala rabbuka li l-mala'ikati inni ja'ilun fil ardi khalifah (2:30).

That is khalifah, to be deputy is the last point a creature can reach. Beyond that no one is able to understand what mankind is understanding. Our ability, no other creation is reaching, or our capacity is never granted to any other creature. Azamat, Allahu Akbar wa lillahi l-hamd.

Therefore O People, try to learn. We must try to learn. But yet, we are closed. Yet, we are closed. No opening. If an opening should come, countless oceans would open through countless territories, all of them a sign of endless majesty, most high majesty, most high praising, most high mightiness that belong to the Lord of Mankind, the Lord of All Creation. Our capacity, so big. So big. We are such small ones, but our capacity is covering the whole universe. If there were such universes, billions or trillions, or quadrillions, or pentillions, like this universe, bigger, bigger, bigger, we have such a power, we have been granted it, to reach to them. To cover them. Allahu Akbar. That is granted to us. Allahu Akbar, Subhanallah, Sultanallah, Karim Allah.

Men were created knowing nothing. When we are born, we are knowing nothing. Knowing, but it is not open. We have been granted such a power for understanding. But, if we are looking at our physical being we are saying it is impossible. The Lord of Heavens, Who is He? He is That One, making impossible, possible. Allahu Akbar. We have been granted such a power. And also through power, not only power, we have been granted authority also on power, to use that power also.

But O People, hear and try to be under this heavenly appearance. Coming here, try to be with your spirituality. If you are trying to use your physical capacity you can't reach it, but Allah Almighty is sending His holy ones and the Seal of Prophets, Sayyidina Muhammad, sallallahu alayhi wa sallam to make a way, or to make ways for mankind. There are openings coming. Who is coming and asking, prophets may open to them. And prophets, the level they are granting to people - what they are asking is going to be according to the level of people who are asking.

And such realities you can't learn by reading books. No. No. You can reach to those oceans through heavenly ones. Therefore the out-looking of all prophets is belonging to mankind but their real beings are from heavens. Try to understand. And they are coming O People with keys for everyone's treasures. Because everyone is granted such a grant from heavenly treasures, must have the holy key. Not ordinary key, but, holy key. And keys are going to be on such different levels. Don't think that one key is opening everything, no. For everything there are countless keys that are going to open to deputies from heavenly and Divine Presence Oceans to show them something from the Heavenly Greatness of the Lord of Heavens and beyond that you are going to stop. And there is someone, only one for reaching the Divine Presence and his position is just 100% different. He is only One. The Oneness of the Lord of All Creation never accepts two. One to one.

May Allah forgive us and grant us something for understanding. And they are saying such a speech perhaps, it is difficult to find through books but through every holy book it is mentioned but to come to it is mushkil, a problem. When they are coming up to the entrance they are using different keys, so it is not opened. There are special keys for every heavenly treasure which is just granted to the deputies and that deputy may use it with an angel. With an angel, they are not alone, but, they may use the key with an angel, or 2 angels, or 3 angels, or 7 angels, or 70 angels or, or, or, according to their powers that power comes for an opening.

We are knowing nothing. We are knowing nothing. Heavenly knowledge is never-ending. And they are granting now because the Last Day is just approaching, to taste something about ilahiyyat, divinity, and under it are heavenly levels, to make them prepared for that level which they could never conceive. They are thinking that this creation is such, tasadum accident.

May Allah forgive us and give us such an understanding that we may reach to the levels of heavens from where we may move, we may continue, continue, continue and when the servant will ask 'hal min mazeed, if any more', and the angels will call, 'yes, go on and find, and go on and take, go on and taste, and go on and understand, go and enjoy'. May Allah forgive us for the honour of the Seal of Prophets Sayyidina Muhammad, sallallahu alayhi wa sallam. We are asking forgiveness and a power to our souls for understanding. Fatiha.

Fatiha
