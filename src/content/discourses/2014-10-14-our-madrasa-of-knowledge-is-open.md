Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya

May Allah continue these gatherings of knowledge Inshallah. Today is Wednesday. Hazrat Shaykh Mawlana (QS) stated, “It is good to start on Wednesdays at places like madrasas to attain knowledge.” We have a Koran school. And today, let us start teaching true and clean knowledge saying, “Bismillahir Rahmanir Rahim.” Thank God. Let us start with the basmala so it becomes good Inshallah.

These types of madrasas are needed. May Allah be content, there are many madrasas and schools. They are good but need ijaza (permission). When there is no ijaza there is no sincerity. What is more important than knowledge is the sincerity there. What is important is fear of Allah. What is important is not abiding by your ego. This madrasa too Inshallah stays on that path. Hazrat Shaykh Mawlana used to say, “The scholars of Sham are god-fearing (taqwa).” We also studied there thank Allah. The person we brought to head the madrasa now is the son of our shaykh there. Inshallah we are starting from zero. We are trusting in Allah and starting. The rest will come Inshallah. Sham is a very sacred place. Istanbul is also a sacred place. Nobody can go there anymore. Inshallah it continues here. Of course, it is needed there too.

It does not end with one or two madrasas. There is a need for many scholars. There is need for sincere, pious, and Allah-fearing scholars. We do not want scholars who give fatwas for their own ego. We do not want to name any here, but they are giving fatwa without fearing Allah. Without knowing people end up in wrong ways and commit sin.

May Allah make our madrasa succeed. May his service be accepted Inshallah. May it reach everywhere in the world. Everyone can attend our madrasa. Young or old, anyone can study in this madrasa. Allah has protected the ink of the scholar and blood of the martyr. The ink is heavier. This is how important the matter is. May Allah bless it (mubarak). May Allah make it everlasting Inshallah. May we be successful.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq. Al-Fatiha.
