As-Salāmu ‘Alaykum wa RaḥmatuLlāhi wa Barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu BiLlāhi Minash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. BismiLlāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm.

Wa ṣ-Salātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Akhirīn.

Madad yā RasūlAllāh, Madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi RasūliLlāh, Madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

Dastūr Mawlana Sheikh Abdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistāni, Sheikh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-

Ḥaqqānī. Madad.Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-Suḥbah wa l-Khayru fi l-Jam‘iyyah.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm:

(Qur’ān 21:107). ‘Wa-mā arsalnāka illā raḥmatan li-l-‘ālamīn, ‘And We have not sent you, [O Muhammad], except as a mercy to the worlds.’ Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘Aẓīm. May the barakah of this blessed month be upon us; his blessed birth month of Rabih Al-Awwal. Shukr to Allāh, for granting us these barakahs. We are thanking Allāh again.

Yesterday blessed people came to visit. They are from Pakistan, from the people of ṭarīqah, from the people of Ahlu s-Sunnah wal Jama’ah. They are doing good deeds and are carrying out many good activities against those who do not respect our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. They said something good; we don’t remember it exactly now.

This year, this Mawlid month, marks the fifteen hundredth anniversary of our Prophet’s birthday. Fifteen hundred years ago, our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam honored this world with his presence. His light was already present from the beginning, and it will be fifteen hundred years this year that he honored this world physically.

In shā’a Llāh, this is beautiful sign that may be a means for goodness and for this oppression and evil to be removed. It will not be removed otherwise. The good tidings our Prophet gave to Muslims was that a person from his lineage will come and he will save the world. Dunyā is filled with oppression, injustice, evil, all kinds of evil things and sin. Dunyā is filled with injustice and oppression. Only he can cleanse it. He will purify this world, the entire world, once again. In shā’a Llāh, this is a sign that this is near, in shā’a Llāh.

Of course, we don’t know how near. But considering the state of dunyā, there has never been a time as bad as this time. Since ‘Adam ‘alayhi s-salām there has been much evil, but not this much. They have oppressed a lot, killed many people and so. But they still believed in something. People today no longer believe in anything. They do what their own ego tells them, what shayṭān tells them.

Therefore, there has never been a time as bad as this time. In shā’a Llāh, everything has its peak. After the peak there’s the termination. This is the time of the highest oppression and evil. n shā’a Llāh, there will be its termination after this. Allāh will send Mahdi ‘alayhi s-salām. He will save humanity. All of humanity will be saved not just Muslims. There is no other way for salvation. May Allāh send him soon and save humanity, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa min Allāhi t-Tawfīq. Al-Fātiḥa.

There are recited Qur’an Khatms, Surahs, Tasbihāt, Tahlilāt and good deeds. We gift everything firstly to the blessed soul of our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, his Ahlu l-Bayt and ṣaḥābah, souls of all Anbiya, Awliya, Asfiya. To the souls of our Mashayikh. To the souls of all our passed ones. To the souls of all Muslims and Mu’mins. For goodness to come and evil to be gone. May those who recited reach whatever good goals they have. For happiness here and hereafter for us all, in shā’a Llāh.

Li-Llahi Ta’āla, Al-Fatiha.
