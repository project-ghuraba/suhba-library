Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Wherever Allah Azza wa Jalla sent Islam, thank Allah light appeared there. It became a refuge for people there. Islam provided everything people needed. Not only externally, but it also provided internally. Because humans are not only created from material. Humans also have a soul. Beneficial things were done for both their body and their soul.

The huge continent of India was a very dark place before Islam. People were treated like animals or lower. When Islam arrived, it became a guidance for those people. Their country was built up and they won their hereafter. Of course, in the mean time Shaitan did not sit still. He will neither sit still until Judgment Day. When Shaitan sees something good, he is immediately against it. When he sees the beauty of Islam, he tries to use it, do the opposite, and ruin it.

When people in the West hear of India, they immediately run to go there. There they go after silly ideas that fit their ego. If they let people free in India, more than half of them would become Muslim. They place a lot of pressure on people. They beat and curse people who choose Islam, and force them to go back to their old religion. However, there is no such forcing in Islam. I asked so many times whether Muslims or other people in India would want to embrace this religion (Hinduism). They said nobody wants to go to them, except for those who come from outside. The Hindu religion looks decorative and nice to Westerners. It is a religion that is nice to their ego. They work on this religion and think they made progress. They think they can find what they want, to complete what is missing from their soul. They try and in the end are disappointed. They are disappointed both in the world and in the hereafter.

Do not go astray from the way of Allah. The religion is Islam. A religion that is made according to people’s own heads and desires is not the religion. Anyway, they also say this is not a religion. How come so many temples were built and their surroundings were filled with statues and idols if it is not a religion? What is it? They do not know what it is either.

That Shaitan, as they say, he puts people in ridiculous situations. Really, he is completely making fun of people. They do all kinds of vileness, and then say they are educated and the most advanced people in the world. Whereas that education is not knowledge but ignorance. It is what makes people go astray and leads to destruction. Let us say, May Allah give common sense. Thank Allah, Islam is light. Those who are bound to it, Allah willing, are profitable both in the world and in the hereafter.

Wa Minallah at-Tawfeeq.

Al-Fatiha.

Hadrat Shaykh Muhammad Mehmet Adil (QS)

14 November 2015, Akbaba Dargah, Sabah Namaz
