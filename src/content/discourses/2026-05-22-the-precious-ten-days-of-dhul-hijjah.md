As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says in Sūrat Al- Fajr, Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
وَالْفَجْرِ . وَلَيَالٍ عَشْرٍ . وَالشَّفْعِ وَالْوَتْرِ . وَاللَّيْلِ إِذَا يَسْرِ . هَلْ فِي ذَٰلِكَ قَسَمٌ لِّذِي حِجْرٍ
<br>“Wa l-fajr. Wa layālin ʿashr. Wa sh-shafʿi wa l-watr. Wa l-layli idhā yasr. Hal fī dhālika qasamun li-dhī ḥijr”<br>
“By the dawn. By the ten nights (i.e. the first ten days of the month of Dhul-Hījjah). And by the even and the odd. And by the night when it departs. There is indeed in them sufficient proofs for men of understanding” (Qur’ān 89:1-5).

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla swears in this holy Sūrah. In its end,<br>
فَادْخُلِي فِي عِبَادِي . وَادْخُلِي جَنَّتِي
<br>“Fa-d khulī fī ʿibādī. Wa-d khulī jannatī”<br>
“Enter you, then, among My honoured servants. And enter you My Paradise!” (Qur’ān 89:29-30). 

“Enter My servitude, enter My Paradise,” says Allah ‘Azza wa-Jalla. This sūrah begins with this oath and concludes with good tidings.

Therefore, these nights, the first ten nights of Dhul-Ḥijjah, are sworn upon and their virtue is immense. Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam says in a ḥadīth that fasting for one day earns a year’s reward. He mentions those nine days. He says that fasting on any of those days has a year’s reward.

The Day of Arafah, the ninth day, is the most acceptable day. Because on that day, the Ḥajjīs are in Arafāt, the standing of the Day of Arafāt. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla has designated that day for our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam's Ummah. On that day the Ḥajj is accepted, and the acts of worship performed are more virtuous and generously rewarded. Because when the reward of the Ḥajjīs and their reward are combined, it will be multiplied. It will be many times greater.

These days are blessed days; these are the days we are in now. Today is the fifth day. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla has bestowed this favor upon us for the sake of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. We thank and praise Him , shukr to Allah. We have both witnessed these days and been granted the grace and favor of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla.

Being from the ummah of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam is the greatest honor. We must know its value. Its value is priceless. It cannot be measured by gold, silver, jewels, or anything else. But those who don’t know, don’t know. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla hasn’t granted it to them. They don’t know its value. They squander it, they boast about it. They leave nothing in their hands. On top of that, they show ingratitude. The commit an even worse sin. May Allah protect us.

These days are a beneficence from Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. There is beneficence, and there is trial. He has given beneficence to the Ummah of Muḥammad. We must appreciate that. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla’s oath, “Qasam” means oath. This shows their value.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says that whatever good deeds you do, worship, or if you get up for Qiyāmu l-Layl it is considered as if you have prayed for a whole year. Some people think that Qiyāmu l-Layl means staying awake until morning. But our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam said that whoever prays two rak’ahs at night and then goes to sleep, and then wakes up for Taḥajjud, is considered as if he has prayed for the whole night.

Therefore, the night prayer is not difficult. So Qiyāmu l-Layl doesn't mean staying up all night. Allah has forgiven us. Allah even considers that sleep as an act of worship because you go to sleep with ablution, you wake up and perform ablution again, and you pray. Even your sleep is considered an act of worship.

Thousands of thanks be to Allah. For the sake of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, may Allah grant us all his intercession, inshā’Allāh. May He not make our love diminish for them and for this way, but increase their love in our hearts. Because shayṭān deceives people in every way.

Most people don’t fully honor our Prophet with true respect. The greatest act of worship is for us to know and appreciate the honor and value of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. That is a blessing and favor from Allah and we must not diminish it. The love for our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam doesn’t require digging and breaking stones. Having this love in your heart is enough. Respect is enough. He will be the one to intercede for you, his intercession will be wājib for you and reach you. Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam says, “I will intercede.”

May Allah not deprive us all of his intercession.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
