Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Tariqa is for Sohbah and for gathering good people. Tariqas are the ways that lead to our Holy Prophet ﷺ. Tariqa means the way. It is for goodness and to guide people. It is a way disliked by shaytan. It is the way that Allah ﷻ and our Holy Prophet ﷺ love. That’s why, our Holy Prophet ﷺ says that in the end of times it will not be ‘ركنملا نع يهنلاو فورعملاب رملأا’, ‘enjoining M’aruf (good) and forbidding Munkar (evil)’, but the opposite will be happening.

This is the sign of the end times. They will order you not to do good but to do evil and commit sin. That’s why shaytan takes the opportunity. They attack Islam and the Tariqa by drooling like rabid dogs. They are trying to stop and ban Islam and Tariqa. Why are they banning them? They are forbidding good. It is one of the miracles of our Holy Prophet ﷺ that in the end of times they will order evil and forbid good.

It is the same all over the world. The shaytan and his followers are using all kinds of means, all kinds of things to attack Islam, to attack Muslims. They are attacking the biggest forts of Islam, which are the Tariqas. Shari’ah and Tariqa go together, they do not go alone. Can a single winged bird fly alone? It cannot fly, it falls. That’s what they want too. They are trying to separate the Shari’ah from the Tariqa and then do as they wish. This is a blessing Allah ﷻ has given us. Allah ﷻ will protect it insha’Allah, there is no need to worry. There is a goodness in everything insha’Allah. The more they attack, the more Allah ﷻ will protect us.

Our aim is to worship Allah ﷻ sincerely, to serve Allah’s ﷻ servants and to serve Islam. This is our intention. Allah ﷻ gives man according to his intentions. If his intention is bad, his end will be bad. Therefore, the intention is very important. Most of the time, some people come and say; “We will do this, we will do that.”. We say, keep your intention in your heart clean. As long as it is clean, the rest is not important. But if your face is different, your heart is different and your intention is different, then we will not interfere. You will be slapped and you will be disgraced. May Allah ﷻ protect us from the evil of our ego. The Tariqa is actually to train the ego, not to pump the ego. The more the ego goes up, the more you should crush it. May Allah ﷻ protect us. May we always be in service insha’Allah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
