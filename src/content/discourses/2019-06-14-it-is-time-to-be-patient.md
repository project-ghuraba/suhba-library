Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa-l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

May Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla bless our Jum‘ah. May our servanthood be as HeNone wants, not as we want. Our servanthood... We are servants of everything. We don't do proper servanthood to Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Because when you ask for something else, when you want another thing, you work for it and serve it. Therefore, may Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla accept our half servanthood as a true one. Because we admit we can't do it. And we say that we can't. We ask forgiveness from AllāhNone. When people cannot do it, AllāhNone doesn't look at mistakes. But when you say you can do it and then don't do it well and you don't ask forgiveness from AllāhNone, then you have a hard time. Therefore, we should make shukr and ask forgiveness from AllāhNone.

And as our ProphetNone said, as we said in the khuṭbah, īmān is two halves: one half is patience, the other half is shukr. That is the level of īmān. Everyone can be Muslim but those who have īmān are few. And the condition for īmān is to make shukr for what AllāhNone gives. We should always make shukr. Whatever HeNone gives, you must say shukr. Everything is in HisNone hands. HeNone gives us from HisNone kindness. We should be happy and thank HimNone. May we be grateful, in shā’a Llāh.

And another part is patience. Patience is something hard to find in the people of this time. They want everything instantly. Everything must come straight away. Because they have made such a thing... In old times, if you had to go from one place to another, you had to walk. Now everything works from where you are. You don't even have to go shopping. You press a button and order things to be brought to you wherever you are. It is the condition of the world. But not everything works this way. Patience is required. Impatience is something taught by shayṭān: "Al-‘ajalatu mina sh-shayṭān," they say. Impatience and hurry are from shayṭān.

"I want it immediately." Wait and see if this thing works or not. When you do something, there is no going back. Therefore, the result of patience in everything is security. Who is not patient will regret it. It is the same with everything. There are hundreds of sayings, ḥadīth and āyah. All of them advise people to be patient. But people don't listen. Even if you say it to their face, they will look and not understand. Patience is... When you say to be patient, waiting for two hours or a week is not patience. Sometimes you have to be patient for years to reach your wish. They say, "Wa-laman ṣabara ẓafar." Who is patient reaches victory. As we said, nearly half of the proverbs are about patience. They say patience is bitter but its result is sweet. Therefore, when people don't have patience, they lose hope. May AllāhNone not let this be. Most of the impatient people will be destroyed in the end. They even commit suicide because they stay away from the teachings of Islām, teachings of AllāhNone and our ProphetNone and want to do everything immediately. Great countries have collapsed due to impatience, great people vanished – all from impatience. Therefore, patience is the quality we are most in need of now. Patience is the biggest remedy for this time. However, no one wants this remedy. May AllāhNone help us, in shā’a Llāh. We are still patient with such situations. How are we patient? They don't obey the words of AllāhNone. It is the time of patience now. Impatience leads to a bad outcome.

When you decide to do something to save the world, that is impatience too. It will turn out worse. We can only pray, begging AllāhNone to send us a saviour who will save us from this situation. And we are waiting for him, Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām, in shā’a Llāh. The world has come to such a state now – oppressors have taken over everywhere. Only Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām can stop their oppression. May AllāhNone let him appear as soon as possible, in shā’a Llāh.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh we are thanking Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla for HisNone, what HeNone give us. HeNone gave us from HisNone endless treasure. The best thing [HeNone] give us – īmān and Islām and we are asking, we must ask forgiveness from HimNone for what we cannot do. As much HeNone is, we must do for HimNone, we are, we do everything wrong. And we know we are doing this wrong. We ask for forgiveness from HimNone. And when you say this, HeNone is Merciful and HeNone not looking for what we are doing, for our faults, our wrong things. HeNone forgive because HeNone said, "This people they are knowing and they are asking mercy from MeNone." So HeNone give HisNone Mercy for us. HeNone forgive us.

But who claim he is knowing everything, perfect sharī‘ah and Qur’ān, whole Qur’ān he know and he can say everything from Qur’ān without... Now there is a new fashion. Before they was attacking ṭarīqah, madhhab, and after slowly, slowly, attack now for ḥadīth also. "Only Qur’ān" they said now. He said, "I am knowing this Qur’ān. I am good Muslim. No need for to ask forgiveness from AllāhNone." This will be very bad because he deny everything and even deny Mercy of AllāhNone and he will be very regret. He will [be] punished. But for this, we are knowing our limit. And we are very weak. We don't, we are nothing. Only asking for HisNone forgiveness. And this is, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying about believe, believer whom... Muslim – there are Muslim and there is mu’min. Mu’min who are believe. They know they are nothing and only asking forgiveness from AllāhNone. This is believer. Other maybe we don't know about them, other people who not accepting ProphetNone, not accepting hisNone ḥadīth, or anything. But we are also thanking Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam for hisNone ḥadīth.

And there is – we read in ḥadīth in minbar: "Al-īmānu niṣfān: niṣfun shukr, wa-niṣfun ṣabr." Meaning – two īmān, two parts – half thanking for AllāhNone and half to be patient. We are thanking AllāhNone for everything because we cannot have anything without HisNone gift. We know this. We believe this. This is, for this they say, this is belief, real belief. And for ṣabr, to be patient, it is very important thing for whole human being from beginning to end. But this time we are more in need for this and unfortunately it is now nobody like to be patient, no patience. This is the most thing it is away from this century people. Because they saying this is fast century? " ‘Aṣru s-sur‘ah" – century of speed. So speed, no, no any patience. Quickly everything. You must press button, must be here. You must, you want something? You are not going from here to there to walk. You make ask, order and they bring for you. Everything like this. This is something you can be like this but not everything. This is life. Very important thing to be patient, to know there is time for everything. AllāhNone, when HeNone want to give something, there is time and so you must wait for this time. If you don't wait, you cannot get.

And there is many ḥadīth from Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, to be patient, to patient. When ask somebody for advice from Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam – "Be patient," heNone said. And there is many āyah also, many saying from people about patience. When you are patient, you will be reach your, what you want. Without patience you cannot take anything and you only be angry and nothing coming for you. But if you are patient, you will be at the end, you will take what you want. I see people they don't have patience, as we are said, you tell them about patience they not understanding. Some of them looking – "What is this patient?" They not understand. Some of them said, "Yes. I was patient since two hours. Nothing happened." Maybe two days, nothing. Patient not like this. It may maybe years going you be patient. At the end you will be, in shā’a Llāh, you reach. The Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, “Wa-laman ṣabara ẓafar" – who patient, he will be get victory, get what he want.

In shā’a Llāh we must be patient because this time. who people they wasn't patient, they destroy their countries, they destroy their families, they destroy everything. After, they saying, "Oh what we done? If we was patient, it was million times it's better for us to be patient. Everything now nothing like before. Nothing it can be. And we are in bad place, bad situation." But if they was patient, everything it was good. It was... but shayṭān... Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, "Al-'ajalat mina sh-shayṭān." – to be not patient from shayṭān. He make people hurry. "Don't be patient. Don't be patient. Fight. Go take your right. You must do this. You must do that. Don't wait this people." So if everybody no patient, it will be mess. And that's what happen. And for this we are not, we must be patient. Even everything you look against us or against what you are doing, you be patient, be cool. Because Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam also was waiting whole time in Makkah without anything. HeNone was patient. And they was doing the worst for himNone and for hisNone Companions.

So after time, come victory, in shā’a Llāh. And we are waiting for victory with... who has this... AllāhNone will send for us Sayyidunā Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām. And we must be patient, not doing stupid thing, not doing foolish thing, and you say, "I am doing this for AllāhNone." No. This you are doing against, because AllāhNone and Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying – patience. We be patient, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone help us and send us soon Sayyidunā Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām Because everything in this world now coming worse and worse and it is time to be patient.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
