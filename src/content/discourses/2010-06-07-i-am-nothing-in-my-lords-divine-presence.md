Dastur Ya Sayyidi, madad. Allah Allah Allah Allah Allah Allah Subhan Allah Allah Allah Allah Allah Allah Allah Sultan Allah Allah Allah Allah Allah Allah Allah Karim Allah tabarak Allah tabarak Allah tabarak Allah Subhanallah nahnu abiduk, irham ibadak ad-duafa narju rahmatak wa nakhsha adhabak

Ya Subhan, Ya Sultan, forgive us for the honour of the most honoured one through Your Divine Presence, and give endless blessings on him and endless glory for him and endless majesty for him, from Your Most Majestic, Most Glorified Presence. And we are saying for all Prophets and holy ones, saints, Assalamu alaykum. Greetings from the heavens, that give us pleasure and satisfaction and enjoyment and, and, and endless grants from the heavens. Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar wa Lillahi l-hamd.

O People stand up and say Allahu Akbar Rabbuna wa Rabbu l-Mala'ikati wa r-Ruh! Listen, O People, we are hearing and representing someone. This humble servant is nothing! I am trying to be nothing. Nothing. I am trying to be only for His most glorified servant's ocean, a small atom. O People we must come and we must learn. We are saying Assalamu alaikum. As-Salam is coming from heavens. Tuba thumma tuba, good tidings over good tidings. Good tidings for those who ask to be with us, to reach countless honour, countless enjoyment, countless blessing, countless lights. Assalamu alaykum alayna wa ala ibadallahi s-salihin.

O ulama we must follow the Seal of Prophets. The Seal of Prophets, the most beloved one, most honoured one, most praised one, most glorified one, the one most-granted from Divine Majesty. We must be very happy and say audhu Billahi min as-shaytani r-rajim. Shaytan is asking to make mankind to be thrown away from heavenly grants. Therefore we must say audhu Billahi min ash-shaytani r-rajim. O Holy Ones protect us from falling into the trips of shaytan and into its traps. Subhanallah, Subhanallah.

Come and hear. We are not making a show here. For what we are going to make a show? For what? There's no meaning for a servant to make a show, Say!! Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. O heedless mass of heedless mankind. For what are we going to make a show? Do you think that a servant dressed in a dress of servanthood will ask to make a show? Such foolishness, such a low, low level. They must be such no-meaning, no-mind people who ask to make a show in front of their Lord, in front of His Majesty, the King. That one has reached the last point of heedlessness and the last point of being ignorant. He who asks to make a show, his level must be the lowest level. O People leave that desire that shaytan is putting through man's real being and blowing into, and saying, "You must make a show". That is the last point of being ignorant and heedless and the lowest level of being in existence. It has no meaning, no possibility. For whom are we going to make a show? I am making a show for you? You are like me. To him? He is like me. To that one? He is like me. They have been dressed on the same level. For what are we going to make show?

But now, 21st Century's mentality is on that point. Everywhere, everyone, is asking to make a show. For whom? Other people, all of them, are on the same level as yours. For what are you making a show? With your dress? With your nonsensical, imitated titles? If you are asking to make a show, he may do it also. We are servants. Servants can't make a show. That should be known well among all holy people. Holy ones must teach their followers that making a show is forbidden and a show makes [that] shaytan fall down because shaytan was trying to make a show above all of Creation.

When the Lord of Heavens created Man and dressed on him a dress that was a Divine grant, it was saying, "I have been trying from the beginning until today to make a show of myself in front of all Creation. Now my Lord has created one and is giving him such honour, dressing him with it and I have lost it, I am not accepting this". Finish. The main reason for shaytan to fall down is clear through all holy books but, people are imprisoned through their mentalities, they are thinking that a show must be for some who have the right to make a show.

O People, O People, come and learn. Come and learn that that is making mankind fall into endless darkness, endless evil, endless trouble, endless problems, endless miseries, endless suffering. That is only Iblis, because everyone wants to make a show. Yahu show, for what? And the Seal of Prophets, peace be upon him – the ulama are saying, 'We are someone, we are Salafi ulama'. You are making a show, you like making a show? Hmm, you like to make a show, you are trying to make an exception for yourselves. "We are not ordinary ulama but we are Salafi ulama. Yes, we are Salafi ulama".

Hmm? You are Salafi ulama? "Look at us, O People, look we are making a show with our big belly and dressing in abaya, putting on eye-glasses", putting something on your hair and taking a car that must be a new fashion, the latest model, "so that we may do a show for those people who are not understanding us". Who are you? I may do theatre also, happily, to make you to understand, yes! You are doing this, you like to make show. Muluk also, presidents also. Everyone asking to make a show, your show under my feet!! What was the Seal of Prophets saying:

ajlisu kama yajlisu al-abd wa akulu kama yakulu al-abd. I sit like the servant sits and eat like the servant eats. Enough! This is enough for teaching all people. No need for thousands of hadith sharif, only this will show you your way, the right way and the wrong way. Aja'ib, aja'ib, Allahu Akbar. Why are you saying, Allahu Akbar, if you are making show? You are leaving that and saying "I am akbar, I am akbar". How are you saying "We are akbar"? Allahu Akbar wa Lillahi l-hamd.

O our Lord, we are ashamed to address You, but as an order we are addressing or, we are Your representative through the Divine Presence as no one can be there, no one can put his foot on that level. He is teaching us that that one Hadith is enough. That means, "I am coming to you O People, to teach you that you are servants". But he is saying, ajlisu kama yajlisu al abd, is enough, without speaking of any other hadith sharif. The Seal of Prophets is enough for all nations or for all mankind on sirat al-mustaqim.

wa’anna hadha siratee mustaqeeman Verily, this is My way, leading straight (6:153)

You may say that sheikh is 'kharfan'. Kharfan? Not kharfan, he is speaking what is necessary to be [said]. Ta`allum is something, tafaqquh is something else. So many people may learn but they don’t understand Fiqh. Faqīh ` la min al `alim. The level of fuqaha (Islamic jurists) is higher than the level of ulama. Fuqaha and ulama. It is enough to learn this, that that hadith an-nabawi sharif must be written with golden letters and first it must be put in front of kings and emirs and presidents and those who are saying 'I am this one, I am that one'. It must be written in golden letters ajlisu kama yajlisu l-abd wa akul kama yakulu l-abd. Put this in sight of your eyes, put it there.

That teaches all nations and gives peace to all nations. And it gives peace also to their outwardly actions or the efforts they are making and also gives peace through their hearts so that they may live in Peace Oceans. O People are you understanding Arabic? My Arabic is so bad but you must understand. Ey marhaban Salafi ulama! Marhaban, doctors of Sharia, for what are you becoming doctors? Do you know what is the illness afflicting the whole ummah, as they are falling down, not getting up?

If we are doctors or ulama of Azhar Sharif are you understanding Arabic or is your Arabic like my Arabic? Qal l, arabiyat qal l. My Arabic is so limited and your Arabic is so much. Therefore I am saying, that you understand that hadith sharif, is it not enough? The Seal of Prophets, peace be upon him, was he saying nothing when he was saying, ajlisu kama yajlisu al-abd wa akul kama yakul al-abd? Was it enough or not? The meaning of four holy books is coming through those eight words, coming or not? If nothing [else] had come that would be enough to make people know about their positions and the mission of their creation.

Yes, yes. Don't say that Jesus Christ, peace be upon him, his mission was to be crucified. No. But he was teaching that his real mission was to show people "I am nothing in my Lord's Divine Presence". If you are saying 'crucified', I am nothing and no nail gave me trouble. I am nothing. Trouble comes to those who are claiming they are someone. Our mission was to be servants. Man is created to be a servant of the Lord of Heavens.

Therefore, the first lesson was just an order. The Lord of Heavens says that He created Adam and dressed him. Adam, peace be upon him, was not coming naked. No, dressed. What was his dress? His dress was from a glorious dress and his position was a majestic position. The Lord of Heaven dressed him from His Glory and His Majesty Oceans. And Adam, peace be upon him, was not doing a show for angels. He was dressed from heavenly glory and majesty.

And the Lord of Heavens who dressed Adam from heavenly glory and majesty ordered, "I dressed that one from My Divine dress of Glory and Majesty to represent to you Who I am. Bow to Adam!" "O my Lord". Subhana man khalaqa Adam min adamin fa adamahu! (Glory to Him, Who created Adam from non-existence and returned him back to non-existence). Allahu Akbar Sayyidi Muhiyuddin, Allah bless him, was saying: Alhamdu lillahi lladhi khalaqa Adam min adamin fa adamahu. You are ulama, try to learn what he means. Angels bow because they look and see the Divine Glory and the Divine Majesty.

Mightiness is something else. Mighty is another thing. Allah only dressed him with a heavenly dress of glory, heavenly dress of majesty. 'Mighty', no one can enter that. No one can be mighty. Man may be glorified and majestic but 'Mighty', to be 'Mighty', no one can be mighty. Allah is All-Mighty. Allahu Akbar Allahu Akbar Allahu Akbar O our Lord, O our Lord, O our Lord, tawba Ya Rabbi. And shaytan is making people claim 'you are mighty ones' and on that point, it makes people fall down because it claimed to be 'Mighty', astaghfirullah. Mighty, Almighty is for Allah only.

No one can be His partner. No one can be a partner to His Divine Glory. No one can be of His Majestic Mightiness. No one can be. Impossible. Impossible. Impossible. Allahu Akbaru l-Akbar. O our Lord forgive us for the honour of your honoured one, Sayyidina Muhammad's honour. Fatiha. Aja'ib hadha Sultan Allah Subhan Allah. We say Fatiha, dum dum dum dum. The King never plays an instrument but His servants may do this, dum dum dum dum - for His honour. Allahu Akbaru l-Akbar, Fatiha.
