As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm.Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim, may AllāhNone elevate his station...This qaṣīdah, these qaṣīdahs, these poems, there are many beautiful words spoken by the great ones of Islām. There are thousands of them. They describe the magnificence of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla and the exalted station of our Holy ProphetNone. There are many qaṣīdahs telling of hisNone manifestations.

Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim would sometimes recite them according to people’s conditions. There was one saying: “Lā tukthir li-hammik, mā quddir yakūn, fa-Llāhu l-Muqaddir, wa-l- ‘ālam shu’ūn.” It says, don’t worry too much. Don’t worry or be distressed. For that reason, do not worry much. Accept everything and move on. Especially for the world, don’t tire your mind at all. Because Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla does what HeNone wills. Whether you tire your mind, give yourself a headache, or exhaust yourself completely, it’s of no use. This qaṣīdah, this poem, expresses it beautifully. Don’t worry. AllāhNone is there. Everything is done by AllāhNone. Don't tire yourself. Surrender yourself to AllāhNone. Submit to Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, and everything becomes comfortable. With this attitude, the world won’t change. By giving yourself a headache or overthinking, nothing will change. What changes is that you’ll have a headache. Sometimes it might even weaken your faith. May AllāhNone protect us.

Some people even lose their faith due to worldly matters, yet it’s not worth it. Since Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla created this world, it has continued the same way. There’s no time free of hardship. Of course, there were beautiful times, the most beautiful times. And with whom? With our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. But that time was also the most challenging. Yet because they surrendered to Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, it was the most beautiful time. They went hungry, tying stones to their stomachs. They couldn’t find a morsel of bread to eat. Still, it was the most beautiful time. In this world, everything becomes beautiful when you surrender to AllāhNone. The world becomes a paradise when you’re with the righteous.

If you’re with the wicked, even if the whole world were yours, you wouldn’t be content, you wouldn’t find peace. You wouldn’t feel at ease. Therefore, these beautiful things, these counsels – Mawlānā Rumi has many – like the Qaṣīdah al-Burdah There are beautiful words spoken by thousands of awliyā’. All of them are for relying on AllāhNone, for leaning on AllāhNone. They composed those beautiful words to convey this to people with kind words and in a beautiful manner. Some people understand, some do not; sometimes they just brush it off. But what’s important is this: things happen in the world, whether like this or like that. What does it matter to you? It has its Lord. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is there. What HeNone wills happens, what HeNone doesn’t will doesn’t. Therefore, there is no need to worry too much about this or that happening. Just be on the path of AllāhNone.

May AllāhNone keep us all on this path. May it go and not bother us with other things, in shā’a Llāh.

Mawlānā Shaykh was sometime saying poem, from Islāmic poem, and it is very nice. Many of awliyā’u Llāh or ‘ulamā’, scholar, ṣaḥābah, even they, many of them, they was saying poem. And this poem make you happy. Make you very happy, because it is not for material thing. Not for this dunyā. Only for love of AllāhNone. To make people, to give them for AllāhNone, to leave everything for HimNone. One of this, Mawlānā was many time saying this, “Lā tukthir li-hammik, mā quddir yakūn, fa-Llāhu l-Muqaddir, wa-l- ‘ālam shu’ūn.” Meaning – don't be worry. Don't be sad. Don't be gloomy. Don't be anxious. Be happy. Give yourself for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Everything by HisNone hand. HeNone is doing everything. What HeNone will do, it will done. Not, what, if you are not happy, you are thinking, “What will happen? This world, very bad now situation.”

There is this people, they are not... [In] every time [period], they have a special difficulty. Who is the most restful in whole this history of human being? If you see, maybe the most happiest time with Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. HisNone time. But what it was? It was misery for people, hurting them, fighting. They give them hunger, no food for them. Even, they was putting stone in their stomach, and tied stone, to not feel hungry. Nobody know this, now this time, this method – to put something to not feel hungry. It was like this, but was, there was most happiest time in history. Not only Islāmic history, whole history from Ādam, ‘alayhi s-salām. Because they are not looking for dunyā, not thinking about, “What it will happen? What it is? What going on? No food, no work, nothing. How we will survive? How we will be?” No, they are not looking at all, even they know baby coming, or baby was dying from hunger. But all of them, was the most happiest time ever.

Everybody, if ask Muslim, they will say they are most. And Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, saying, the most best time, hisNone time. After, time of Khulafā’u r-Rāshidūn, of course, after. But the best time, it was hisNone time, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Because, they was not looking at all for material thing. Only for Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, what heNone will say, what heNone will do. And accepting everything from AllāhNone, and, “We are coming from AllāhNone, we will go there.” So, no problem. This is what Mawlānā Shaykh was saying, all time, for this poem: “Don't be unhappy. Don't thinking for future, what you will, what will be, what you will do. No.” All this, when you are surrounding yourself for AllāhNone, you will be no care and don't care about anything from dunyā. Because, you know – your time limited. Your time, it is known by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. So you must be, also, don't worry about anything. Many people now they have this funny illness, attack, panic attack. Panic attack, what is this? “I am afraid I am from death...” saying, “I will die” or “I afraid from dying.” If you afraid or not afraid, no benefit. When your time will coming, it will go. You will go, she will go, everybody will go. If you afraid or not afraid, no any benefit. For this, you must leave yourself for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. HeNone create you, and when your time coming, you will be go. When you, AllāhNone send you something to eat, you will eat. Maybe you cannot, you, come food in front of you, it's not for you. It will go for other. Drink, you can maybe not drink here, drink other place. All this Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, knowing.

So, don't be thinking, “What will happen?” Leave this for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. If you do this, you will be happy, and AllāhNone happy with you. Because, you give yourself for HimNone. AllāhNone bless us, in shā’a Llāh, to make, to be like this, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
