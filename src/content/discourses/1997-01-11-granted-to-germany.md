No one can be like mankind. You are great from all universe - not with your physical being. Your physical being [is] nothing. But, we are representing the Lord of Heavens and you have been honoured to be His servants. Therefore you must try first, to give your whole respect first of all to your Lord. And He is accepting that from you. And looking after - bagislamiyor [not forgiving].

He is not leaving it, saying 'It doesn't matter' - [He] is asking His rights. [There is] no tolerance [on] that point. He is asking: 'Where is my respect, O my servant? I am asking! I am not giving up and not to ask, No! I am asking. That is My divinely rights on you! You must keep it! You must do it! You must be for Me only! I am very jealous. I am asking only to be for Me, My servants. Not to be for anyone else.' And people running after shaytan And saying [to] shaytan, they are best friends. Going with their egos and granting everything for their egos. And saying to their egos 'As you like sir!' And they are saying to shaytan 'O our master, as you like!'

Allah Almighty looking and never forgiving who is leaving their high respect highest respect for His Divinely Presence. Therefore, first, first of all you must know and you must try to give highest respect to His Divinely Presence. If not, He is calling you and giving His judgment on you with your shaytan, with your best friend, that making you away from giving your high respect to your Lord. That is holy month, teaching us that we must give our highest respect, full respect, full honour, absolute servanthood to our Lord Almighty Allah. You must try to learn, and you must keep it. Try to keep it after Ramadan also. Not to lose.

One day, that pil [battery] through our hearts finishing and we finish. Everyone looking for their pils, batteries. Everyone looking one moment that it is going to stop, to finish. Don't think that it is an endless life. Don't think that what you are running after it from this life, it is going to be for you. Running to take keys from that dead person and he should be so happy when died, I have everything. And never thinking that one day someones should do what he is doing now. Yes. Try to do your best for your self. The meaning to your best for yourself - what is the best for you. Best for you? To grant, to offer your high respects and worshipping to your Lord's divinely presence. That is for you, best. Not to make through Deutschland Bank, Commercial bank to make their millions. Yes, if you must try to send as much as possible from your, from your own property that it should be your charity, to send to Day of Resurrection to come to you. You may put here and you may take there. Who is giving, making his charity here, finding that Day. If you are not putting, you never finding there anything.
