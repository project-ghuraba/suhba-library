The subject that I am going to speak about is for the people of Egypt. In this evening of Juma of the month Subhana Allah about The Prophet (saws) said, "Ask favors from people with enlightened and good faces" (Hadith). Sadaka Rasulullah. I ask from those of you who understand. Who has good understanding to bring a photo of King Faruk. (King of Egypt) The one, whom they went against him, the tyrants and they threw him out from his Kingdom. Bring his photo and put it in the middle. Then put the photo of the face of Abdul Nasir (first president of Egypt) then put the photo of A.Sadat. (second president of Egypt) Then put the photo of H. Mubarak. (third president of Egypt)

And I ask the people of Egypt to judge who from them has an enlightened face. The person who reached the King never left empty handed. But these other ones. These tyrants they sucked... the blood of people, until they rebelled against them. This is enough for us. I want a photo up of the King, who passed away Faruk, may Allah (swt) bless him. And he (the King) is innocent from all of what they accused him of. The people of Truth, they are witness that what they accused him of, was a false accusation. It was false & evil. The people of Egypt must know who is good for them; good for the people of Egypt. These people who made the parliament are from among them (tyrants). The Kingdom is what Allah (swt) Wants. The rest of the systems want shaytan. Those who support shaytan, are tyrants. They destroy people (nations).

Kings! In any country that there is a King the people are happy and satisfied, while the rest are not. This inspiration came the evening of Juma, of Rabiy ul Akhir. The last Juma. Let them print the photos & distribute them among the people. Enogh! Those who oppose, Allah (awj) Will take revenge from them. Those who are taking side with a King are satisfied, happy in their life in this world and in the Hereafter. We ask from Allah (swt) to restore the thoughts of the Muslims so they may understand the Holy Hadith of the Prophet (saws) "Ask favors from people with enlightened and good faces".

Wa min Allah Al Tawfiq. It is enough for us, from a weak servant to narrate. This is enough to shake Egypt, insha'Allah. This is enough.
