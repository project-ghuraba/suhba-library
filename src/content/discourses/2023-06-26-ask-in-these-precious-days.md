As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Shukr to AllāhNone for favours HeNone gives. Today is one of the most blessed days. The ten days of Dhū l-Ḥijjah, or its thirteen days, are all blessed. The virtue of these days starts at the beginning of the month. Today is the day before Yawmu ‘Arafah. It is called Yawmu t-Tarwiyah. Tarwiyah means to be given water. It means drinking water from the pool of Kawthar of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla and our Holy ProphetNone, in shā’a Llāh. Today is a blessed day. Whoever can, should fast on these nine days, or on the eighth and ninth. Fasting on Yawmu t-Tarwiyah and Yawmu ‘Arafah, the day of ‘Arafah, is counted as fasting for a year. Today is from such virtuous days. The value of these days is very high. They say, it is "something money cannot buy". Even if the whole world is yours, without belief it has no value. Who attains the bounty of today attains something much more valuable than all of this.

These days we see, may AllāhNone protect us, people who have riches, wealth and all kinds of ranks and positions in their lives. But there comes a day – may AllāhNone not show that day to anyone, to any believer – that they lose everything. Does anything remain from those days they enjoyed so much? Nothing remains at all. They say what remains is a bitter memory. A bitter memory of what they have lost; but that is not something that lasts. Or else a day comes and people leave all of that and pass away. They bother people every year, saying there are so many rich people, people with so much wealth and possessions. Are such people able to take anything with them to the next life? They are not.

You are the ones who will take it with you. These favours are eternal – these beauties granted to you by AllāhNone, this worship and ṣadaqah, charity, destined for you by AllāhNone. All thanks to AllāhNone, the ḥajjīs are starting to move towards ‘Arafāt today. Even though these beauties are difficult, they are permanent. You won't leave them behind with permission of AllāhNone. They will stay with you. They are with you in the grave and on the Maḥshar. And they will be with you forever in Paradise with permission of AllāhNone. The rest... they list many names now. There is a new millionaire or billionaire every day. People look up to them and wish they had the same. So what if you have it? So what if they have it? It won't remain with them. And it won't remain with you. What will remain is AllāhNone. What is permanent is obedience to AllāhNone. Therefore, know the virtue of these days. Appreciate these days. Benefit from them. May we benefit from them and may AllāhNone make us from HisNone beloved servants, in shā’a Llāh.

May these days be blessed. Shukr to AllāhNone, we were meant to be here in the presence of Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim. We are with him. May AllāhNone be pleased with our ḥajjīs. May AllāhNone accept their ḥajj. They are on ḥajj. Our heart is with them, in shā’a Llāh. May we have from the rewards given to them, in shā’a Llāh. May AllāhNone bless these days, in shā’a Llāh. How lucky are we, how lucky are the Muslims. We have to appreciate this. How lucky are those on the way of AllāhNone. How lucky are those who win ākhirah. Other things cannot make you happy. There are many meaningless words. Don't listen to them. Be with AllāhNone. Don't be afraid. Don't be afraid in dunyā and ākhirah with permission of AllāhNone.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are reaching today the most blessed days of [the] year, which AllāhNone gave for believers. For mu’min, Muslim people, the most precious thing, this. Real one. Real jewel, real diamond, not like glass. They give stone, glass, and mix with diamond. The real one: this, which it is the best thing for human being, the most valuable thing. Everybody running after money, running after many thing. But this thing, the valuable [things] of this world, not forever.

Even, many people, they become rich, they become high position, they become successful in life – and suddenly something happen. They are losing everything. Nothing left for them. What benefit? They will be happy, what the nice day, good, happy thing[s] they happen for them? If they lose this, they will be happy with the memory of what they had? No, it will be bitter memory for them. Because they are suffering now. Nothing in their hand, nothing left for them. Just they're kicked out from their position, or they bankrupt, or they become ill, or become, this.

The important thing not what you get, you left. But you will be taken. To be whole time like happy – and even [when] everyone is happy, nothing happen for them – no bankrupt, they are still in that position – but some day it will come, and they will leave everything. And not taking anything, and they will be throwing in a hole. Or now, [there is a] new fashion for this people. They don't like to leave anything, even for them. They thought they will be safe. They burn themselves and throw their ashes in [the] air. This is not a solution.

The real precious thing, what believers they have. Everything they have, this not will be left behind of them, when they are leaving this world. They passing away, all these things they will come with them, especially in grave. It is graveyard, in grave it is the most, first difficult place. In this place, you find your good deed, what you do good thing, you will be find[ing] first in grave. And when you find this in grave, other you are safe. In grave, after grave, in Day of Resurrection and in Paradise. Forever it will be this with you. Not left you. Like this people, who have, if their whole, now they are try[ing] to be new... They say for themselves, "I am your lord, I am your god," like old people. This ego, they have this. Everyone, [he] can say for himself. But, after this, there will be nothing left for them. Earth, money, everything, they will leave here, and they will go without anything. The most important thing, the precious thing, this is. And we are living in days, very precious day. Today, all this... from Dhū l-Ḥijjah, until ten. And after ten also, ‘Īd day, also very precious. And today also, Yawmu t-Tarwiyah, meaning which is giving water. This water of Ḥawḍu l-Kawthar, from Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, in shā’a Llāh. Tomorrow, Yawmu ‘Arafah, ‘Arafah day, which Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam encouraging people to fast this day. Because when you fast day of ‘Arafah, AllāhNone forgive you for one year and more – what you have before, also. And you make praying, ṣalawāt, tasbīḥat, everything you give – AllāhNone giving more and more from HisNone endless treasures, in shā’a Llāh.

Today, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, ḥajjī, pilgrammage people, ḥajjī people, they are moving to ‘Arafāt today, slowly, slowly. Tomorrow, they will stay there. And al-ḥamdu liLlāh, our brother and nephew, our murīd brothers, many people they are in ḥajj. AllāhNone accept their ḥajj, and reward for them, and give us. Because we are also, we like to be in ḥajj with them. AllāhNone, HeNone is generous. HeNone gave us as they have from reward, in shā’a Llāh. This is a gift from AllāhNone. We are poor, we are asking from the Most Generous OneNone, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. And HeNone is generous, HeNone not make people who ask, without giving.

We ask HimNone. You ask. Don't worry. If you ask anybody from people, they can give for some limit. But, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, no limit for HimNone. You can ask everything. Ask for, first, for strong belief, for love of AllāhNone, love of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Ask for rizq also, ask for health, for shifā’. For everything you ask, don't [be] ashamed. HeNone is your Lord. HeNone knows you. And no need to [be] ashamed from AllāhNone when you ask HimNone. Ask HimNone, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone give us every good thing, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
