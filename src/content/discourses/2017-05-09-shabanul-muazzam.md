Shaykh Mehmet Adil Al-Haqqani, Sohbet of the 9th of May, 2017.

As-salāmu ‘alaykum wa raḥmatu Llāhi wa barakātuh. A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

Shukr to AllāhNone we are in the month of Sha‘bān. Sha‘bān is the month of our ProphetNone. It is a holy month. Our ProphetNone used to fast the most in this month. He fasted in other months too of course. They said our ProphetNone sometimes didn't fast, they thought he never would. And sometimes he fasted so much, they thought he'd never stop, say ṣaḥābahs. But each one's value is much greater than of other days. And the fasting our ProphetNone did the most was in Sha‘bān Mu‘aẓẓam.

Sha‘bān is a holy month. Worshipping made in this month is accepted. Especially, tomorrow night, in shā’a Llāh. We will reach, in shā’a Llāh in peace Laylatu l-Barā’at. Laylatu l-Barā’at is the night when what will happen in the year is written for everyone in the presence of AllāhNone. Therefore, there can be a change for believers as mercy. Everything is possible in the presence of AllāhNone. That's why that night, tomorrow night, there are 3 Yāsīn between maghrib and ʿishāʾ. These should be recited with that intention. Who can, should read and gather. Shukr to AllāhNone in our mosques they show respect to these holy nights. Even if people are not religious, they still remember these days and value them. And as they show value, AllāhNone will value them too. People who don't value these holy virtuous nights, whoever they are they may have the whole world, but they have no value. Who values is valued. If you don't value, you have no value. You can be very educated, you can have money and ranks. Some group appeared that is against these valued nights. Shayṭān worked it out well. They say it is sin and don't want these nights. Don't listen to them. May AllāhNone help us against these mindless people. Because so many people have mind but they cannot really use it especially these Muslims. They are running after useless people. May AllāhNone make us stronger this year and next year with stronger Islām, in shā’a Llāh and reach Mahdi ‘alayhi s-salām in shā’a Llāh.
