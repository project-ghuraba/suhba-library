As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

[…] One after one they are leaving dunyā and others are taking their place. Like this house, it’s maybe a hundred years old less or more. So, many generations, many people lived here and they left. They cannot take it with them. They were here. But what’s important is to leave a good deed, a good thing for people. They will either praise them or curse them. Many things happen here. Many people cursed [those ones after their passing.] 

Alḥamdulillāh Muslims have been always praised. Because they are not oppressing anyone. Oppression is forbidden for the Muslim. He mustn’t harm. “اﻟﻤﺆﻣﻦ ﻻ ﯾَﻈﻠﻢ وﻻ ﯾُﻈﻠﻢ”, “Mu’min doesn’t harm or get harmed.” The Mu’min cannot oppress anybody. Even dead people, you cannot touch the body of a killed human being. Even in war, you cannot do anything to him; you cannot cut, throw or burn the corpse. It is forbidden. But for non-believers, they can do everything.

For this we are saying, the good ones, praised by people and by Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla are the Muslims alḥamdulillāh. There are many maqāms here, many ziyārah places are all around Ceylon, Sri Lanka. There are thousands of awliyās maqāms, most of them of course, are not known. But Allah made them known and apparent. He showed them to people to come and take blessings from them. Because when Awliyā’ullāh pass away, their spirituality becomes at least seven times more powerful from when he’s alive. 

For this, alḥamdulillāh today also we intend to visit one near the fort. They said they don’t know whom he is, but by miracle he appeared for them. They made him a grave and people visit him. And his miracle is that he has sweet water, just a few meters from the sea. Sweet water cannot be found near the sea side; it is impossible. But by the miracle, karāmāh of Awliyā’ullāh, Allah made it there. Many people come and visit. This is for the sake of Allah, he is a beloved one for Allah, for this we are visiting and respecting. Because Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says in a ḥadīth qudsi, “I love my good people. And who becomes an enemy for my beloved ones, I declare war against him.” 

Now many people think some groups are Muslim and good. They are the ones who say, “These people are worshipping them. They are worshipping graves.” No, not worshiping the grave. We are worshiping Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. And Allah loves these people, we love them also. Ask by their sake from Allah. Not ask from them. They also don’t want you to ask from them. They say, “Ask from Allah. Don’t ask from me, ask for Allah.” But when you make them to ask for you, it’s better. Because they are more acceptable in the Divine Presence of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. Allah doesn’t refuse Awliyā’ullāh. We are sinners, we don’t know how to make duʿā, we ask them to ask Allah for every good thing we want; especially strong īmān and to be in the way of Allah and to be forever with Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. This is our aim.

The Muslims in Sri Lanka are sincere people, but unfortunately, this illness is coming like covid, we’ll say. Because cancer is terrible, but it doesn’t spread (not contagious). Cancer, of course, is a very dangerous illness, but it does not spread to others. Contagion is like covid or a cold or anything that spreads all around the people. Very few people can be saved. So, it spreads because people are cheated. 

The human being likes the one who cheats him and harms him. This is what they say in a Turkish proverb, “The camel likes thorns.” What the camel eats, you cannot even touch. If you touch it, it will scratch your hands; it has thorns. The camel likes this very much. And the human being likes the one who cheats him. This is a Turkish saying. So it is true. When they find an honest person, they don’t believe him. When they find a cheater, they all come like sheep, running after him. 

Unfortunately, this here is due to the ignorance of the people. They don’t know the way of Allah. Just because somebody is shouting, saying this and that, they run after him, “Oh, this is a big ʿālim (scholar), he’s shouting so much.” Like last Jummah, the khatīb (who gives the sermon) was shouting so much that we had to close our ears. When someone shouts like that, it pierces right into your brain. We closed our ears so we wouldn’t hear anything.

So these people, unfortunately, are like this; they do not appreciate the value of those who are beloved to Allah. When you respect them, Allah is happy with you. Allah says, “I am happy with you; because you are good to the one I love, I am happy with you.” Because if you like somebody, you like everyone around him, his family and others; you respect them and love them. If you do not respect them, then you too will not be respected or loved by Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. Allah will be angry with you, not happy with you. This is what we are trying to do, and what all Prophets tried to teach people: you must love Allah and love what Allah loves.

الْحُبُّ فِي اللَّهِ وَالْبُغْضُ فِي اللَّهِ
<br>“Al-ḥubbu fillāh wa-l-bughḍu fillāh”<br>
“Love for the sake of God, and hate for the sake of God.” [Abī Dāwūd]

Meaning love for the sake of Allah, and hate for the sake of Allah. If someone does not love the Prophet and does not love Allah, we are not obligated to love him. Somebody might say this is against humanity. We don’t care about this; we care about Allah. If they don’t like, of course, we don’t love that. But if you say something hateful, we will not be happy with you because Allah is not happy with you. May Allah keep us safe and keep you safe, inshā’Allāh, from this illness and from these people. 

We are not saying anything against them, only asking Allah to give them hidāyah (guidance). Because the mu’mins here are a minority, they must stay united and also be good with others. Ṭarīqah people are good to everybody: to believers, non-believers, Buddhists, Hindus, Christians, and Jews; no problem with any of them. They live together, because we are not obligated to force anybody. You cannot force everybody to be Muslim; if they like to be, okay, welcome. If not, you cannot force them. This is not acceptable because you cannot act on your own accord. If you do anything like this, you will be questioned and punished in ākhirah by Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. May Allah keep us safe and keep you safe, inshā’Allāh.

Alḥamdulillāh, we are happy to be here again. Many of our people who were here last time are no longer here this time. May Allah bless them and make their maqām higher and higher, because they are beloved ones to Allah. They served Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla, they loved Mashāyikh, they loved Awliyā’ullāh. For those who have this love, Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam says,<br>
الْمَرْءُ مَعَ مَنْ أَحَبَّ
<br>“Al-mar'u maʿa man aḥabb”<br>
“A person will be with whom he loves.” [Bukhārī, Muslim]. 

A person will be with the one he loves. Because Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam asked one of the ṣaḥābah who was crying, “Why are you crying?” He said, “O Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, in Jannah you will be in the highest station, and we cannot be on the same level with you; we will not see you in Jannah.” Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam says, “الْمَرْءُ مَعَ مَنْ أَحَبَّ”, “Al-mar'u maʿa man aḥabb”, “A person will be with whom he loves.” 

This is Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam and he is the most truthful in speech. So alḥamdulillāh, those who were with us last time and passed away will be with Mawlānā Shaykh and with the Awliyā in Jannah, inshā’Allāh. There is no doubt about this. 

Alḥamdulillāh, this is also the beauty of īmān, the beauty of ṭarīqah and sharīʿah: it gives people good things, good guidance, and good news. It does not make them despair, saying, “Oh, all of us are going to Jahannam.” No, alḥamdulillāh, we are not going to Jahannam. Whoever wants to go to Jahannam can go; we don’t want to go to Jahannam. 

Alḥamdulillāh, Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says in a Ḥadīth Qudsī,<br>
أَنَا عِنْدَ ظَنِّ عَبْدِي بِي
<br>“Anā ʿinda ẓanni ʿabdī bī”<br>
“I am as My servant thinks of Me.” [Bukhārī, Muslim]. 

So we think well of Him, as He says He is the Most Merciful, and He says we will be in Jannah. This is our thought towards Allah; inshā’Allāh, there is no doubt about this. 

May Allah bless you and keep us safe and together, inshā’Allāh, and give hidayah to others as well, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
