Today we have very sad happening in our community as the Sheikh's children. And so many people see, some happenings happened. Yes, we are very sad and it is our big loss. What's happening?"

Mawlana Sheikh he was so merciful on us that to put a murshid: the new murshid to redesign our data according to our new murshid and to be under his wings. It is important for us. For him is ok, he has already he has his secret, he has his maqam and now we are the one to have redesign our behavior for the new murshid. Alhamdulillah we are happy that Mawlana Sheikh he appointed such a personality to get us in unity after the atomic bomb exploded our being.

And now Mawlana Sheikh he appoint Sheikh Muhammad(qas) as a murshid. He is our murshid. Mawlana Sheikh he was previous murshid, now Mawlana Sheikh Muhammad he is the new murshid. And we are

giving bay'at for him and we accept him to be our murshid from now on. May Allah give him long life. May Allah make him in our head and may Allah make his personality strong to keep us with him.

Now this reminds me with a very nice story about what's happening here. It was the story there was - everyone knows this story, but to remind. it is important Mawlana Sheikh was saying "the reminder it should be there all the time. Because if you remember you don't make mistakes. If you don't remember than the mistake will happen." And the story was: one Sheikh he was having his old murids since long time and one day one murid, new murid, appear and he comes for his majlis and that Sheikh calls him "come, come" and he puts him next to him. And he was all the time next to the Sheikh. And all the murids they start to blablablah... "I've been with the Sheikh too many years but the Sheikh you know he takes this man & he puts him next to him. You know I was serving the Sheikh and you know he is supposed to put me next to him, but he put that man."

You know this and they start to speak, to speak to speak. And the Sheikh, he says "I would like to give you some order." The Sheikh he knows the feelings of the people. The other day I was visiting some people and the owner of the house he says "I have this cat and that cat." And he says "that cat, she is very stupid cat, you know!" I say "why?" "She" he says "she give birth and her children. She was taking children from place to place to place. And now she lost the children and she look free. And, look she's stupid." Two minutes, that cat came with the baby kittens and showing us that "I am not stupid and I put my cats in front of you I didn't lose them." And imagine the Sheikh great Sheikh that he doesn't know what is going on and how the heart is acting and how the heart is moving.

So Alhamdulillah he was so beyond us to put the correct person to be our murshid and to be for us; to be mercy for us. Because he didn't change anything of the Sheikh's will or he didn't change his way to say "no, no, I want to change this one, I want to change that one & I want to make this one like rolling, & I want... no!" As Mawlana Sheikh was behaving, he said "I will continue this and I will preserve it as Mawlana Sheikh wanted it to be." And this is our luck. If I am being your murshid, you'd be having problems. I've been your murshid, you will have a problem definitely. But Alhamdulillah Mawlana Sheikh he didn't put me as your murshid. I am part-time murshid. I was part-time Sheikh, now I'm part time murshid. Just joking. It is important for you to know that Mawlana Sheikh he was so merciful on us to make it a nice behavior and a nice way.

So the story was, the Sheikh he says "I want you to slaughter an animal according to your budget." So in one place nobody can see you. So everybody the rich one they get the cow, the bull and below that, they cut the goat and chicken it was for the new murid, poor man. And he got everybody got his animal and in the evening they all come with the meat. They put it in front of the Sheikh, showing. One of them he was like me, he slaughtered a bull. And only that one, the murid he come, the new one, he has the chicken with him. Now you understand, because you've been so angry tell me I am chicken, no I am not the murid is having the chicken. Don't be angry with me because I am explaining. And...

Then everybody like me also putting the bull there, and Dr. Munir putting the goat, except that one he is with the chicken. Doesn't know what do with it, and he was so embarrassed and the Sheikh telling him "come, come, sit next to me." And he has to take the chicken otherwise it will run away. He put the chicken, he put the chicken next to him. And the chicken keeps on (bak, bak, bak) and everybody says "Ya Sheikh, we put the order, we...we.. obeyed the order and what about this one?" I was the one to laugh first because I have the bull. Huge! The Sheikh he says "No, no, no. This one he makes well." "What about us?"

He says "Didn't you listen for the order?" We say "yes". "It should be in a place nobody see you." And I say "Ya Sayyidi this bull gave me a hard time, I was alone to slaughter this one. You don't know. And I was alone nobody see me. And having difficult time even this bull I break my feet until I slaughtered him." Dr. Munir says, "I was alone & I don't know how to slaughter this animal - it made it very difficult." And the other ones so many complaints. He says "stop! You speak." He said "I didn't find any place without nobody seeing me, and especially you my Sheikh. You've been looking at me spiritually all the time. And I know that Allah Almighty and Rasul looking. For this order, so I didn't find a place to slaughter this my chicken and I had to bring it here and make the noise." And he says "Now you understand what is the difference between you and him."

That's why Sheikh Muhammad he became murshid that's why he realized this point. Me, no need because I have other job to do. Because you have other things to do. Don't be sorry for not having the title to be murshid for ummah and to get the great secret of the Golden Chain. It is not important. To make the egoistic side of you saying "Why he take it and I didn't take it. And I've been serving for 50 years, for 100 years for 1000 years and I didn't get it." Your job is different, his job is different. That's Allah Almighty. He take this to put it in the hand of anybody. He doesn't have to ask you. He doesn't have to appreciate your service or he doesn't have to say "you've been serving me so much and you owe me to put this maqam." No. As Allah Almighty He is free to do whatever He likes. Sheikh also free to do whatever he likes. This is up to them. If you are not surrendering for them you will never have nothing, nothing!

This is important to know because we are follower of Sufism. What Sufism means you have to understand. First you have to kill your ego first and then to have thinking for maqam. If you are coming thinking for maqam, this is not the place. This is not the place for maqam. If they wish to give you some maqam, they will do. If they don't wish to give you some maqam, they'll never do. And there is no pressure on them, there nothing. Everything is belonging to them. Even our breathing is belonging to them. If we are Sufi. If we are not Sufi ok you can claim whatever you like you can be whatever you want and you can behave as you want. Nobody will charge and nobody will say why you are doing this or what you are doing or what I am doing. Alhamdulillah. I am happy to have my new maqam here. I was having chair before, but now no chair. Now sitting as maqam, same as you but bit better.

Alhamdulillah we are comfortable and we are hoping that Allah Almighty will make us comfortable, innerly comfortable. Very important, inner comfort that it is important for our spiritual behaviour. And may Allah make us easy to go there.
