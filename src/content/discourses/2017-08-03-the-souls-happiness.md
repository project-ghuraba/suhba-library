As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa raḥmatu Llāhi wa barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Wa ṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīn wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistāni, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, AllāhNone make us to meet again. We are thanking AllāhNone for everything. HeNone is our Lord. HeNone is everything. We are belong to HimNone. Our way, way of ProphetNone, way of ṣaḥābah, awliyā’u Llāh. We are, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, gathering for remembering AllāhNone, mentioning His name, mentioning ProphetNone, his companions, and awliyā’u Llāh. This is the way of good people who AllāhNone love and HeNone want them to do this. HeNone said, "Afaḥasibtum annamā khalaqnākum ‘abathan wa-annakum ilaynā lā turja‘ūn." (23:115) "Do you think We create you to only for playing and making what your ego like and love what your desire like?" No. This is you must train yourself to come to way of Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla. ProphetNone and whole prophets they are coming for this. If you are on their way you will be happy and a winner here and hereafter.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are trying and we go whole this travel, traveling from place to place. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we meet people they are just for Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla coming, gathering, asking pleasure of Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla. They are not coming for anything else. This is way what Mawlānā Shaykh was doing and we are feeling Mawlānā Shaykh – "Alā inna awliyā’a Llāhi lā khawfun ‘alayhim wa-lā hum yaḥzanūn." (10:62). They have light, real light and they are supporting everywhere where we go, in shā’a Llāh. With support of Mawlānā, people they are feeling, not seeing, even Mawlānā but they are feeling very good thing, very they are happy, very happy people. Happiness coming from your soul. If your soul happy, you are be happy. If not, nothing can make you happy. To make the food for your soul – love of ProphetNone, love of awliyā’u Llāh.

In shā’a Llāh, Mawlānā is looking because we are meeting thousands of people, many of them they are saying Mawlānā is, I saw him in dream. This, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, he is even when he was in this life also many people seeing but now more and more. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are feeling his support and feeling sometimes, many times, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, he is. Without knowing, people coming – he send people even as what he was doing in this world. Also from ākhirah also he is really giving more support than this, al-ḥamdu liLlāh. Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we meet again, in shā’a Llāh. This small ṣuḥbah because from morning we traveling and meeting people. This is for barakah, in shā’a Llāh. Jum‘ah barakah, ṣuḥbah barakah and make dhikr – Khatmu l-Khwajagān. In shā’a Llāh, now we pray ‘Ishā’.

Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq al-Fātiḥah.
