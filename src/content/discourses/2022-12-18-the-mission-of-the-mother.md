As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Innamā amwālukum wa-awlādukum fitnah” (64:15), says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. HeNone says, “Your wealth and children are a test for you.” HeNone says to be careful with them. How? The rights of children on their parents are to be given good names and to be brought up well. These are the rights children have on their fathers and mothers. Meaning, starting when they are little children, our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam says, first thing you should teach them when they start to talk, is to say AllāhNone. They should say AllāhNone first. The first thing they should say is the holy name of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, says our Holy ProphetNone. We do this in order for children to grow up with the pleasure of AllāhNone. First, we need to teach the name of AllāhNone. A child raised with AllāhNone, with the name of AllāhNone, will be a good child and a good person.

Without doubt, educating children, teaching them good things, is important as well. If you leave them alone without teaching anything, the first thing they will learn then will be... People are not teaching good things. As they don't teach them good things, they will leave, and separate from you later. They will become alienated from you and see you as a stranger. That's why bringing up children is the greatest mission of a mother. The task of motherhood is great, and its reward is as great as well. One blessed ṣaḥābīy asked our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam for advice about his will. Our Holy ProphetNone said, “Your mother.” Again he asked himNone about his will, and heNone said, “Your mother.” HeNone told him to take care of and respect his mother more than ten times. Because the greatest duty is the duty of a mother.

They say, “Mothers must work. Women must work. They must have freedom. They shouldn't stay at home jobless.” They are not jobless. Their bringing up one good person is better than a thousand of your executive positions. It is better than a thousand jobs. Bringing up a good person is thousand times better for the country, for the nation and for humanity. They are implicating housewives in uselessness and say, “You are useless, You cannot do anything.” People are obliged to work now. Everyone is working. But the most important thing is to bring up children as good people at home. That is the most important duty. It has the highest value. Even if someone is president, his contribution is not as much as bringing up a good person. As we are living in the end of times, they are bringing up children as they like. They make them love what they love. They make them love useless people. They make them love things that are not good. They make them love various things that shayṭān loves. We must pay attention to this. The whole world, rich and poor, are complaining about their children. You bring them up carelessly, and then they follow various bad ways. They do various things in front of you. And you can't say anything. There is such a situation now. And then they dare praise themselves. How did these people become like this, if you are good? They didn't come from some other place. These are the people you brought up and educated.

May AllahNone protect us. We live in a tough time. Nevertheless, people who believe must show importance to this. They must show importance and value to discipline at home. The first school is at home. “Knowledge is from the home.” I think it is the word of our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. AllāhNone knows best. “Al-‘ilm mina l...” Probably not, but there is a saying, “Mina l-mahd ilá l-laḥd.” Mahd, meaning, learning knowledge starts in infancy and lasts until the grave. And that, as we said, starts at home with the mother. The mother teaches. She hands over good people. They also raise good people in turn. May AllāhNone protect our children, the children of Ummat MuḥammadNone. May they not be bad ones, in shā’a Llāh.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla say: Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Innamā amwālukum wa-awlādukum fitnatun” (64:15). AllāhNone saying, your children, your money, your... what you own, it is like fitnah, like, meaning test for you. For this, be careful how you are looking after this. To make it not fitnah, to make it good thing. For you – for other also. Most important, to grow children with good, to be good people. And to grow it, it is most important one for this mission: the parents, especially, mother. Mother, for this Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam was praising mother every time, for ṣaḥābah. Each one asking about, “What I must look?” HeNone said, “Look for your mother.” Many time this ḥadīth, many time repeat. One repeat maybe five or ten times, who he want to hear, “To your father,” to say Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam – heNone didn't say. Only say “mother” and “mother”. Because, she is very important. She is first teacher for baby, for new one for this world.

Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying you must first thing to teach your children, “AllāhNone.” You say it. When say, many time children they cannot say something, but “AllāhNone”, they can more easy to say it. Say, “AllāhNone, AllāhNone,” you say it. With this, giving barakah for whole life. And the mission of mother, it is very important. More important for anything else.

Now, modern time people they said, “No, woman she must be independent. She must have job, she must have career. She must have this and that.” But, they are not knowing the most precious one: [a] mother who raise[s] good people. Maybe one lady raise good one, she is better than to be president for country. Because, this man can serve more better than other. To be honest, to be clean, to be believer, to help people, to teach people, to – Right thing, right way, this is cannot learn by himself. By himself, maybe learn from school to be crook, to be proud, to be looking for himself, to be... This is what they teach. So, before this, they must, in house, mother, she must look for children. Teach them to be good manner[ed], honourable, mannerable and good people. Not lying, not doing bad thing. This is from beginning coming in their head and mind and heart. They... nothing can later affect them. This is very important because, after...

Now, this shayṭān people... Before, it was, our time, seven years you must go to school. Okay, until seven years, many times mother can teach children. But now, two years, three years – leave it for four years it will be too late for this. Quickly, they must catch this baby, children, to teach them teaching of shayṭān. Which, whole world now suffering from this teaching. Because, children they not learn from family, from mother, learn from people not responsible, not honourable. Many of them just for... they are looking for money. Not looking for, to give good example, or to be teach people good thing. And, children they are first learning bad word in school. This what we are knowing. And when they go, they said, this for kindergarten. They thought it is, they are pure. But first thing they learn there from teaching of shayṭān, and it is continue like this until growing. And when growing, they are against their family, you cannot tell them anything. You cannot do anything.

AllāhNone keep us safe from this thing. But we must try to teach them as much as we can do, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone save Muslim people, mu’min people and their children because we are in very dangerous time. AllāhNone keep safe, all of them safe, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
