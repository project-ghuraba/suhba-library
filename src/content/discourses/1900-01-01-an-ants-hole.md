Shaykh Muhammad Nazim Al-Haqqani An-Naqshibandi, Sohbat from January, 1991.

Now, we are looking for our European & American brothers and sisters. Audhu biLlah min al shaytan al rajim. By the Name of Allah All Mighty, all Merciful. Try to keep yourselves. Try to protect yourself by the Name of Allah All Mighty all Merciful, all Benificent, most Munificent.

Be with Allah then you should be protected. If you are going to be with shaytan no protection. It is so easy, no need for concrete. Concrete shelters can't protect you. Allah protects you. Be with Allah, you should be protected. Don't be with shaytan, it is impossible for a protection to those who are with shaytan. Now in our days everyone asking "where I can go? Where I can go? What can I do?" And I am saying don't go, don't run. If you are running a bullet may reach so quickly or quicker. It is useless to move from one place to another place. And to say "maybe this one is more protected. No! Only protection - you should be with Allah.

Where is Allah? To be with Him, everywhere is for Allah. He is not in need to be "where", But all wheres are for Allah. Everywhere for Allah. And everything is for Allah and you are for Allah. You can be able to declare and to say "O my Lord, I am for You. Finished!" It is for me, look, I am putting in my pocket (a watch). Not to be lost. Yes! It's cheap. That is a cheap thing but it belongs to me. I am keeping it more, more carefully, if keeping it here. Yes there is a chain to tie it also. Which things belong to me I am keeping, protecting, looking after it. If it is going to be golden, Ohh! Too much taking care. You know this is a stick, I am taking care, astaghfirullah! Because it belongs to me.

We are saying "O my Lord, I am belonging to You. Please look after me, I am for You." Nothing can touch you. Everywhere may be under fire, but who belongs to Allah Almighty, finished. He is. We are looking what is for you. What is for you we are looking after it. How about Allah Almighty? Do you think that He is not looking after those whom they belong to Him Almighty. Simple, declare that "O my Lord I am for You!" I am asking shelter, but it is so difficult to say this declaration. Astaizzu Billah "Inna Allaha shtara mina l-mu'minina Anfusahum Wa 'Amwalahum bi 'anna lahumu l-jannat (9:111) (God has bought of the believers, their selves and their possessions, promising them paradise.) Finish. Some will know that finished. Allah Almighty just bought Mu'mins everything. Spiritually & physically Allah Almighty He bought them. Give to Him what He bought. Why you are quarreling & fighting?

We are quarreling & fighting about ourselves. And all, whole physically and spiritually we are for Allah. He bought us. But yet we are quarreling and fighting, not surrendering. No need for concrete shelters. Don't enter. Say "O my Lord, I am for You. Would You like to take me to You?" "Yes!" "I am ready, You are leaving me here, I am here. Taking me to You, I am coming, as You like my Lord." Say and be at rest, don't ask to go there to come here, no! Go to Him. Where you should find Him? He is everywhere. And everywhere in Him. Everything for Him, and everywhere for Him. Anywhere should be a shelter for you.

When He is asking to shelter you, should be ant's hole shelter for you. Ant, you know ant? Ant's hole should be a shelter for you. Should not be difficult for your Lord to shelter you through an ant hole. He puts you easily through that hole - without making the hole big & without making you small. Allah! He can do everything. Without making you small or making the hole bigger He puts you, your body, through the hole of a small ant's hole. A big ant's hole, He may put in it an elephant. You know elephant? Without making the elephant smaller and without making ant's hole bigger may put that huge elephant through that small ant's hole. An elephant may run down, up and down through that hole. Good news! You may

find one thousand elephants running now up and down through small ant's hole. Allahu Akbar! Allahu Akbar! Allahu Akbar!

He can do everything. He has endless ability, capability endless Qudrat/power for Him to do this. You can't imagine but Allah Almighty beyond your imagination. Therefore we are asking shelter. Ant's hole enough, for what you run under concrete? when this...(siren sound) this siren. Their siren you need it under concrete shelter. Look one ant one run in it "O my Lord I am Yours put me in it!" Believe in your Lord and His endless power. Endless ability, Qudrat. Everywhere we have shelters, therefore there's no need to go under concrete shelters.

Be with Allah and Allah protects & shelters you even through an ant's hole. Ants bring to you a chair also and another four ants carrying a bed also for you. And others put bricks covering the entrance and no more you should fear this war. Rest, believe in Allah Almighty and His protection and it is enough.

Wa min Allah At-tawfiq.
