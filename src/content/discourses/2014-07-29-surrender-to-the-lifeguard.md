Sheikh Bahauddin Adil al-Haqqani sohbat of the 29th of July, 2014.

Masha'Allah! Ya Rasulallah. Alhamdulillah we reached Eid Mubarak, Eid al fitr as-sa'id. We reached the Beyram. We reached the beautiful days that Allah Almighty He presents for us. Allah Almighty He is so kind and so Latif (Gentle). And He is Al Wadud (the Loving). He thinks about the details that make you very happy. Allah Almighty has never been rough. He is looking for the most nicest spirit coming for His servant. And He wants His servant to be like Him: very nicely, smoothly, and gentle/Latif. When you see somebody he is in good behaviour, you say "this man is a gentleman!" But when you see somebody rough, you say "what a man, he is not polite at all" And you start to put so many words for him. But Allah Almighty He loves us to be gentle, to be gentlemen, to be lady. Not to be ignorant doesn't know nothing and sending everybody to hell. (sneeze) This is sign of my uncle, saying "This is truth."

Alhamdulillah! Now today is Eid day, today is happy day, and today is one of the days that Allah Almighty He grant for us. And we are accepting these days from Him, because normally we don't have any choice! If Allah Almighty says "It must be!" It must be! No choice. Don't think about choice. The choice that you have to think about it you have to obey. You have to surrender to Allah Almighty's Will. But this surrendering will take you to the safe side. They are saying - if somebody sunk in the sea and somebody wants to save him, this is very simple rule, they tell him "don't do anything, just surrender for me, I will save you." But if he moves, he will make the one coming to save also to sink.

This is the rule. Say, "just leave yourself surrender to Me and I will save you." Allah Almighty when He says Muslim, Islam, Taslim - this is all about surrendering, surrendering for your Lord, surrendering for your Creator. Because when you are surrendering to Him you know that you are going for the safe area, for the safety. He is giving you all the necessary information how to surrender. It is not coincidently surrendering like the one in the sea. He is surrendering to someone saving him. But he doesn't know if this man can save him or not, that's why he tries to save himself. But if you know this man, he is one who can save you, you will surrender. You know that you will be in the safe side. That's why Allah Almighty He sent Rasulullah (saws), Khatim Al Anbiya', to say "you surrender to me, because I am the one leading you to safety. I am the one taking you for Heavens. I am taking you... I am the one taking you even more than Heaven."

Some Awliya they say "The Heavens is nothing, it is nothing according to what Allah Almighty He will give for His servants that love Him and surrender to Him - True surrendering. Heaven is nothing!" they say. The Heavens is nothing, can you imagine? We are working all our life to be in Heavens. And we cannot be in Heaven and maybe we are in Heaven. But Allah Almighty He says "If you are truly surrendering to Me, then the Heavens is nothing!" This is how is the difference between surrendering surrendering....surrendering...Not surrendering! It is levels. We have to know the level. Allah Almighty says "Hal yastawi l-ladhina ya'lamuna wa l-ladhina la ya'lamuna" (39:9) Allah Almighty He is addressing "do you think the ones who know equal the ones who don't know?" Never been equal. Never been equal! Even any simple thing, they say "this man he knows."

For cooking for example. This man knows cooking, but this man he doesn't know. He doesn't know how to cook. So which one do you go to? Say "no, no I go for this one. Maybe this one he is shape is not so well. But this one's shape is very nice, still look. "Ah! You look very nice, but this one's a better cook!" Even you understand -Hal yastawi l-ladhina ya'lamuna wal-ladhina la ya'lamuna(39:9) This is very important Ayat. There is very huge meaning in it. It's explaining everything. Allah Almighty He explained everything in 2 words. He doesn't need the 3rd one. But Allah Almighty, He is so generous He put everything in the Quran, everything in the Hadith, everything in the chest of Awliya', everything that it is necessary for us to be in the safe side and necessary for us to surrender to Allah Almighty.

Normally as a servant we are so far away from Allah Almighty because we don't have any level to be with Allah Almighty. But Allah Almighty in the same time, He is very near to us. Because if Allah Almighty He is not near us, we will not be existing and we would not be alive. So these extreme 2 sides it is approached on us. You can see it on us. That's why Allah Almighty is so far away from you, at the same time so near to you. Actually Allah Almighty He is not far, we are far away from Allah Almighty. We are the ones far away from Allah Almighty. Allah Almighty is inside us, with us. Otherwise we would never be alive, we would never be existing. Because everything existing for Allah, from Allah. This is high knowledge, like S. Adnan knowledge masha'Allah. He is ocean of knowledge.

Today is Eid, that's why Allah Almighty He was generous with His knowledge for us. To put this knowledge among us. And to put this love for Him, to love Him more. We should love Allah Almighty more. How to love Allah Almighty more? Allah Almighty He created Mala'ika (Angels). He created Anbiya' (prophets), He created Awliya' (saints) and all this a way to Allah Almighty. What we want? We want to love our Murshid (guide). If you love your Murshid you get the surrendering point that takes you for the real surrendering ocean that belongs to Allah Almighty.

And with this one we say, Ya Rabbi don't take us away from Mawlana Sheikh. Ya Rabbi put us in his way and Alhamdulillah we are happy that You put S. Muhammad that he continue this saving mission for us and surrendering mission for Rabb al 'Alamin, for Prophet - that we know we still have the address to get on and on...and with this one we say Wa min Allahi t-Tawfiq bi hurmati l-Habib, bi hurmati l-Fatiha.
