Madad. Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar la ilaha ill'Allah Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar wa Lillahi l-hamd Allahumma zid habibaka izzan wa sharafa nuran wa surura, ridwana wa sultana. Ya Rabb.

And we are saying Assalam alayka Ya Sahib al-Waqt who was granted a full control on this planet, dunya. Asking also his permission for addressing all nations as a warning from heavens to all mankind as they have been ordered to be from human beings. We are saying Assalamu alaykum Ya Ibadallahi s-Salihin. We are giving our salute to all those ones who are following holy orders of the Lord of Heavens, our Creator, Allah Jalla Jalaluhu.

And we are saying Assalamu alaykum our attenders. You may attend or not. If you are attending and coming to attend/pay attention to this humble declaration you will be happy. Just going to be taken from you, (Mawlana was distracted) - what are you doing? If someone is making useless things through our meeting it makes the heavenly connection to be cut down. Give yourselves and don't do actions that make heavenly connection to be cut down.

O People, we must keep a discipline. Discipline was sent from heavens to mankind. We are saying: audhu Billahi min ash-shaytani r-rajim. That is from heavenly discipline. And then to say: Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. That also is heavenly discipline that we have been ordered to keep because mankind through their creation are proud ones. Proud ones means, they are not leaving their pride easily to follow someone.

The most difficult thing for mankind is to follow someone or to be on the second stage. That is a most difficult thing for our egos. And we are saying now audhu Billahi min ash-shaytani r-rajim, that is heavenly discipline for all mankind. Beginning, even from prophets, up to everyone through this creation. They must keep that discipline. They must learn because servants can't do anything that they like to do. No.

A servant is that one who follows his owner. Or, servants are under the commands of their Lord, Lord of All Creation. He is Allah Almighty Subhanahu wa Ta'ala. And Allah Almighty, He has Absolute Lordship. It is only for Him, Almighty. Real Lordship is only for the Lord of Heavens. Allah Almighty, Subhanahu wa Ta'ala! O People, the Lord of Heavens is the only One who may grant to some among His creatures to represent His Lordship-ness, as He likes. No one can be Lord or can have Lordship by themselves or through themselves. That is mentioned through all holy books. And it is also mentioned in the last Message from heavens to mankind through the last messenger Sayyidina Muhammad sallAllahu alayhi wa sallam who had been granted the Holy Quran. And the Holy Quran was reaching as a last message to teach people about their positions because shaytanic touch is coming on men through their creation. Shaytan is trying to make everyone from mankind say, 'Ana' - I am this one. And the meaning of saying 'I am' means, 'I am the lord'. Impossible.

It is the worst characteristic of mankind to say 'I am here'. But shaytan is insisting on making people say, 'I am this one, I am that one'. That means 'I am also a lord', 'I am a lord' also they are saying, claiming. Shaytan is teaching mankind to say 'I am this one, I am that one'. And they haven't such a right, to say 'I am this one or I am that one'. But they are claiming this because shaytanic teaching is only on that point - to make people say 'I am this one'. Shaytanic teaching is only to make people claim, 'I am someone'.

For what? Because I am a king. For what? Because I am a president. Or to say, because I am a commander-in-chief or because I am chief of all armies. 'I am saying I am that one, that I am a lord'. You are not a lord. Every teaching now on Earth is on that point, to make people to say 'I am also a lord'. And Lordship, Absolute Lordship is only for Allah Almighty, Subhanahu wa Ta'ala and we are slaves. Until you say, 'I am a slave', you are going to be with shaytan, sadanas and his follower. Or going to be supporter of sadanas, shaytan.

That point is most important O ulama. Are you teaching your listeners or students or followers that point or not? If you are not teaching them you are going to be first to be punished on the Day of Resurrection. Learned people, ulama, they have first responsibility. That responsibility is given to them to teach people, 'O People your egos are asking to claim that you are a lord.’ You are claiming that you are a lord. Do you think that lord is getting into the WC? How it can be?

O People, think on it, everyone, 'I am this one, I am that one'. Claiming to be some VIP one. If you are VIP one and coming from plane going for rest, all of the VIP people that are asking 'please where is WC?' You are VIP person and asking WC? Hmm? What is that? You are so important that means, 'I am a lord'. Hanh, if you are lord what are you going to do in WC? Hmm?’ Ulama-u Islam, ulama Salaf us-Saleh, teach them in such simple words to make them understand to leave to be proud ones, that Allah Almighty is never happy for His servants to be proud! And anyone who has pride can't enter Paradise. Yes? Yes.

We must say something that is important for learned people to know about themselves and to know about their Creator, Allah Subhanahu wa Ta'ala. Every teaching now is only to make youngsters to claim that we have a certificate from Oxford, or graduated from Nelson, or we are belonging to Cambridge University. That means you are claiming 'we are VIP ones' because shaytan is making them blow up through themselves and their bodies with shaytanic blowing becoming bigger, bigger, bigger, bigger and they are thinking they are something. O Muslim ulama, ulama who are claiming 'we are Salafi ulama', why you are not making clear men's positions? Do you think holy books coming to give mankind an artificial greatness? Why you are not making clear to people, 'O People who are claiming greatness, it is artificial, real one belongs to heavens'. As long as you are on Earth, shaytan is pumping through you that you are someone, you are such big ones, you are such important ones, or you are Lords on Earth also. And real reality is to say the only Lord is Allah Almighty. No one else.

Shaytanic claims are artificial, not real. You must try to break that egoistic greatness. You must break it down. You must take it away from people. For that purpose Allah Almighty sent His holy ones from heavens; to break down imitated greatness and imitated titles of mankind and to bring them on their true levels and that is to say, 'O Lord, we are your servants'. Allah Amighty, Allah Almighty is saying through Holy Quran about the grandfather of prophets, Sayyidina Ibrahim, peace be upon him and the works that he did, or what he was going to do or what was done.

He was saying to his nation, nation of Nimrud. Nimrud and his nation were keeping idols that were representing their egoistic pride. And he was saying, 'O my nation I am going to break down your idols'. Do you think that O learned people, doctors of shariah, ulama of Azhar sharif, that Allah Almighty making Holy Quran story, or fairytale book? No. Take what He is asking to understand, from his servants. He was saying through Sayyidina Ibrahim's story what happened. He was saying I am going to break down all idols, those imitated idols through which you are claiming 'We are lords'.

And there is no lord, only one Lord, Allah Almighty Subhanahu wa Ta'ala. Allah Almighty did not send holy books as a fairytale or story books. No, it was for teaching. That means, O People, till you are not breaking your idols because everyone has idols they keep though their selves and look after them carefully. No one to touch their idols. They were fighting, killing if anyone was breaking their idol. And the Lord of Heavens is teaching people that you must break down your idols, your ego's idol. If not, you are not My servants and you are slaves of shaytan, go away'.

O People, Absolute Lordship is for Allah Almighty. Allah Almighty never created His servants to claim 'I also am a Lord'. No! No, no, you can't be. Therefore the Seal of Prophets was saying when coming with his companions from a battle, he was saying to his companions, 'O my companions, we have now finished war with an army who are belonging to the wrong way and we have been granted from Allah Almighty, victory, we are coming victorious now, but we are beginning a new fight with an enemy that is the worst enemy among enemies. 'Ya Rasulullah, O our Beloved Prophet, who is that?' And the Seal of Prophets saying, 'Nafs. Jihad un-nafs.' You are doing that jihad un-nafs. O those ones who are claiming 'we are belonging to Salafi ulama' or those who are claiming 'We are doctors of Shariah', or those who are saying, 'We are ulama Azhar sharif'. You never tried yourselves to fight with your egos? I think no one. And that is making people here to be disrespected ones through heavenly beings and on the Day of Resurrection they are going to be thrown into fire for their bad actions and intentions. They will be thrown into fire.

O People, try to understand something. Leave to claim for your ego this or that, no. Don't think that the clothes that you are wearing give you a right to say, 'I am this one, I am that one', Leave that and dress the dress of servants. Then your Lord Almighty Allah Jalla Jalaluhu will dress you the dress of servanthood. That, He likes it. He never likes any other dress. May Allah forgive us. Assalamu alaykum. Fatiha
