As-Salamu Alaikum. Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fil jamia'. May this sohbat in the day of Ramadan be blessed. May it be a barakah.

Now these Muslim trade & businesses mostly turn into the trickery of the Easterners. Meaning if two, or three people start a business, for sure they separate, fight in the end. "I did, I earned, this one earned, that one took more, this one got less" they make such claims. And this happens among the Muslims more. Shaytan must be haunting the Muslims more that there are no long partnerships in these countries- no 30-40 years, 50 years, 100 year partnerships. The business ends in 3-5 years. Everyone separates & runs away from each other, saying "you did like this, I did like this". But when they first start the business, all of them are really well intentioned. Most of the time such people come here, we say "try to reach an agreement."

Allah (awj) says like this, when you do business, write it down- "this one takes this much, that one takes that much, we accept this way. It will continue like this whether the business is good or not". But mostly they don't do this either. Even if they did, probably they wouldn't take it seriously. And the secret of this is when a group of people start a business & when that business goes well, don't be greedy. Greed is not a good thing. When you are greedy & say "I did, he didn't do" you make the business go down & the barakah of that business is gone.

It's secret lies in that barakah. Instead of making 100 pound, make 50 pound, it is continuous, this is barakah. You kick your partner, your friend out of the business, saying "I take all that 100 pound",you take it. And then you ruin the business without taking anything. You regret later but it of no use at that time. And also, Allah does not give this opportunity every time. Fill your bucket while the tap is running. Don't say like "I close it so that it doesn't go away, I take all, no one else can take." You fill, others fill their bucket also.

And also, sometimes people do good business but again greed mixes in it. As we said he insists on making 100 pound with that business. And his business going well, but before it is 3-5 years you see that he went bankrupt. Why? Because he changes the ingredients trying to make more money, He says "I make 100 instead of 40". And how does he do this? He uses low quality ingredients. People can fall for it for 1-2 years but after that they see that there are better quality ones. And you go down.

There are others who are honest companies. Very few in these countries where these men continued their business without mixing any trick. There are companies that are on the market for 100 years. But this happens mostly in the non-Muslim world. They also, don't change even the smallest thing in what they are doing. Like the largest beverage companies of the world, their budget is bigger than Turkey's budget. Turkey is a big country yet they can't earn that much. They keep their secret formula very carefully, don't even change a bit. There are many who imitate them.

You should also be like this if you are a smart one, you don't try to cheat people in dunya also. And the Prophet (sas) says "who cheats is not from us". And when you cheat, you are the first one to receive harm. It is the same in akhirah matters as well as in dunya matters.

There were two brothers at the time of the Prophet (sas), again Hadith Sharif. They worked together. One came to our Prophet (sas), his assembly, sohbats and the other one works. That one complained to the Prophet (sas) saying "He doesn't come to work. I do the work". Prophet (sas) said: "Allah knows but probably you make money with his barakah. Your rizq comes through him". And this is a big thing, a sign from the Prophet (sas) that he gave a secret, "not everything comes from working".

Millions, billions of people do the work you are doing but whichever one Allah gives barakah, it grows. Everything is in Allah's hand. "It is about my intelligence & hard work.." No, it is not like this. As we said, when you form a partnership business don't disturb it & your heart by saying "I do work, he doesn't, I know the business". Don't ever do this.

If the business is going well, continue the same way without changing anything. Thank Allah, with shukur blessings increase.

Today we speak about business and to be partner, to do business in Islamic society. They are, in Islamic or this Mediterranean, Middle East- this area where is most of people Muslim, you cannot find partnership going long. Maybe going 5 year, 10 year, 20 year, after it is finished. Why this happen? It is maybe because shaytan is more attacking this Muslim people, to be away from right, right way & to make more sin. How to make more sin? If you are partner, you must be you must take your part, & other part you must give for your partner. But when you are beginning to say "I am working more, I know this business more than him & I must take this, this is my right. And he is not doing anything". So becoming, even sometimes maybe they take, this become sin & shaytan, he will be happy with this.

Muslim, many time we see, and they are not following what Allah say in Quran even. In Quran Allah said, "when you do partnership or to do some work you write between you & put some witness to, after not denying." Many time people speaking "we will do this". And "Ok, ok" They beginning. After he said "I said this", "No you said this". He said "No, you are wrong I said this" Because nothing right registered. So everybody he can say & he can imagine even & he thought he is in right. But when you write this, many time.. But very few people, they write. And when they write even, also shaytan.. There are very few people who can continue with this. Because

Allah give barakah when He put jamaa, many people to do work, Allah give barakah. And but when some of them making hiyanat/betrayal, not right, so barakah gone. And Allah (awj) when maybe put, you can win 100 pound- give 50 for partner, 50 for you. No, he said "I must take all" & after little while he send this partner & he become alone. When he become alone, even he have 50 pound, he will lose this also. Because barakah, it was gone.

And there is Hadith from Prophet (sas), "who are making trick for us, they are not from us". In nonMuslim world, you find many companies, many partnerships, maybe from more than 100 year. And why they like this? Because they are, even they are richer than Turkey government. Because they have something to do & they've not changing anything from this. But in Muslim, we see this very much everywhere; They want to earn more money, do not put.. they will take maybe 50 pound. No, they want to have 1000 or 100 pound. And they becoming to be cheat. They put less good thing in what they produce. Many we see this, they are become famous & people they have, they are very happy to buy this thing. But after sometime they find this is changed. And when they change, nobody take from them. Maybe who not know their name they are knowing. But it is not going long, 2 year, 4 year, 5 year. After they become who making better in good quality. And this also,maybe 5 year. After 5 year he say "everybody coming to me. I can do more money to cheat also".

This is really what happen in Muslim country. But they are foolish. Because what we said, there is many trademark in worldwide. They are not changing even one atom from what they have. And they are not telling any people what they are doing, what they have. They must, even if not understanding from Quran, they must took for this people also.

And one Hadith, they was 2 brothers living in time of Prophet (sas),they are Sahaba of course. They have business. One of brother complain to Prophet (sas). He said "My brother, he is not coming & he is going all time in front of Prophet (sas) to learn Hadith, Quran & about this thing." And he said "I am working, he is coming, not working". Prophet (sas) said, "Maybe Allah, He give your rizq, your (provision) what you want from shop, by his barakah, by the blessing of him (the brother)." This is secret also Prophet (sas) telling. Because maybe you know business. There is, like your business thousands or millions but Allah not give for everybody this barakah. Maybe with your friend, he have barakah & you are partner. He not know but you know. With barakah, Allah open for you this. This is, to not be tamah- greedy. This is very important.
