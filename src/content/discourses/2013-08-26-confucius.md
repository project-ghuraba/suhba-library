Come in, let us start. "May we remember the Name of Allah first, it is obligatory for every servant in all their doings". Allahu Akbar! Dastur O RijalAllah, dastur O Shahi Mardan. How beautifully he said it. He who was in love with Shahi Mardan said it(Sulaiman Chalabi/Mawlid Sharif). May we start with the Name of Allah - Jalla Jalaluhu, Jallat Azamatuhu. Allah Jalla Jalaluhu, Jallat Azamatuhu, Jallat Kibriya'uhu. O Rabbu-l Izzati wa-l Azamati wa-l Jabarut! Allahu Akbar Allahu Akbar Allahu Akbar wa Lillahi-l Hamd. Zidna taqatan. Increase our strength. O Sahib Maydan Shahi Mardan. May our strength increase. "Then say", he says, "Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim". When we say it, the power & strength of who remembers His Majestic Name can destroy mountains.

Marhaba, O yaran Shahi Mardan. Marhaba - marhaba to you in dunya, marhaba to you in akhirah and marhaba to you in paradises also. How beautiful. Learn (to greet with) the marhaba. Our Allah, Our Subhan. He (swt) sent us Shahi Mardan (so that) his yaran love him and take Nur from him. "What is your give and take, O master?" "Well, here we came to the market & bought tomatoes, cucumbers, broad beans & black-eyed beans". So you went to collect these for this body of yours, which will decay? And where is your give & take for your spirituality today, what happened to it? Hal min mazeed, hal min mazeed. Go ahead O Shahi Mardan. They are not the ones who provide for our dirty bodies. Every person is busy with providing every day. For what? They are striving for this body, which will decay, which will suffer what will come to it inside the grave. Why should you strive for it? Let us say Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. This Basmala revives you and you don't decay in your grave. Your dunya is ma'mur/joyous too. What is the joy of dunya with? It is with worship & obedience.

Consider this. Everyday the foolish Man - every single day - takes a basket, takes a bag in his hand and where does he run to? He runs to the market, buys whatever his share/naseeb is and returns to his home happily. He buys broad beans, black-eyed beans. He buys meat, buys milk, buys things like this. Whatever provision is needed, he buys and comes to his home. Is this his job? Is this what Man's job is? Is it to feed his dirty ego? Or is it to magnify himself with imitated titles? What do the people of this time think? They think that they have grown great with imitated titles, they think they've become great. Actually they have gotten smaller but they don't know it. Go ahead O Shahi Mardan. Our way is the true/haqq way.

O Shahi Mardan, go ahead. How beautifully does he say it - We are not created to take a basket in our hand and provide from the market. Do you think ants have a market? He says "Why should an ant need a market? It finds a little piece and takes it. It finds a little trash and takes it. It raises its two little hands. Pay attention to this - An ant makes shukur to its Lord, saying "You granted me this little piece of straw O my Lord Who created me, and I make sajda for You". There is no any other creature that neglects the sajda; that is heedless of making sajda other than the heedless men. While making sajda and glorifying his Lord is for Man the most important, he is who is the most heedless of it. He assigned his every hour for meaningless duties according to his situation and can't get out beyond it.

What does he say? "If I do this business, I will grow big". "What will you become?" "I will be a big trader". "What will you become?" "I will be a big commander or official". "What will you become?" "I will be a big trader". Ok, and what benefit did it give you? If you have a house titled under your name, you live in it. If you have three houses, you get happy. If you have businesses, you start to claim greatness. You bring imitated titles to show yourself great? You use them to create an image of greatness, to show yourself as great, to say that "I am great?". Ok, you have grown great but your real rank falls down. Greatness is for - al Kibriya'u Lillah, Greatness belongs to Allahu Dhu-l Jalal. You won't be great by living in a palace. O Kings, listen! You also are a servant of Allah. You also feel sleepy and go to your bed when it is nighttime. Your soul is taken and a sleeping corpse remains there. Don't think that you are great. Greatness is for - al Kibriya'u Lillah. Greatness belongs to Allah Almighty. Who are you?

There is no knowledge greater than knowing yourself, he says. The people of today don't know themselves. Shaytan - may it get what it deserves - made a mirror, an imitated mirror. Sometimes it shows people in their own height/size. Then it shows another mirror. He asks "Who is this?" "This is you as you will be. You will be in this size. This is what your honour is". "Ok, what should we do (for it)?" "I will whip you now, I will make you run, until you possess a crown & a throne. You sit on that throne and say "I am a mighty/aziz person who rules over this world". Hasha, Izzat/mightiness belongs to Allah. O yaran of Shahi Mardan. Erase your ego from the way, remove it and look at the reality. Go ahead O Shahi Mardan. Masha'Allah. Your speech is the cure for your yaran. May your words, your sohba be Nur for the people.

Shaytan turns people into all kinds of shapes and it has no value. The greatness shaytan will give you is imitated, by making small talk (laghw) over you. What is its useless talk? "He is a sergeant". "What is his rank?" "He is a Bey". What is his rank? "He is a Pasha/Admiral". Well, what about the Sultan? Sultan is only one. Let us listen, let us understand. May they dress us the rank from Heavens. Go to Al-Kaba Al-Muazzama and they may dress you with it. May you dress such a glorious dress of rank & you know it. You should know it. People like to show off too much. Why should you show off? "If we are going to show off something we want to show ourselves". "With what will you show off?" "Have you not seen the dress I wear? How is it? Does this dress look good on me?" How beautiful does it look! "It is the latest fashion" he says. Well, what happens when we strip you of it? Your shape which is like an ape is left showing. So the beauty was not with you but was with the dress you wore, or with the underpants you wear. These don't give you beauty. Beauty comes down on Man from Heavens. The beauty that comes down from Heavens is the real beauty. The beauty that you wear & show with dresses is trash. It is of no benefit. Let the beauty be within yourself. You be beautiful, not your dress. How is this speech? People are heedless - they don't look at their own beauty but want to show the beauty of what they wear. O foolish ones! Is it you who is beautiful or is it the dresses you wear? "This dress we wear shows our rank". And when you take it off? You are left like an ape! What is his value when he takes off his clothes? People say "Oh! What an ugly man he really was". There is an ugliness in you. What ugliness? There is the ugliness of "seeing yourself". You say "I am beautiful, I am handsome". With what? With the dress I wear. "I have it made with the best tailor and wear it. How does it look?" "It looks very nice". O foolish mankind! Don't see your humanity in the dress, don't say "it looks good on me". You won't be beautiful, you won't be handsome, you won't be pretty unless you fulfill what being a human requires. You won't be beautiful. What will you be? The laughing stock of shaytan! Especially women! They have lost their minds with fashion already. They seek the beauty in fashion.

The beauty should in your own being, not in the dress you wear. Your greatness should be in your own being, not in the dress you wear. Get used to this, learn and teach this. He says "I am Putin". He says "I am the president of Americans". He says "I am the great one of China". With what do you prove your greatness? "With my army". Did He Who created you tell you to collect soldiers and make mischief in dunya? Or did He (swt) create you to be the Imam/leader, the Sultan of the people whom He (swt) handed over to you; so that you honour them, you serve them? Allahu Akbar! Allahu Akbar wa takabbara wa tajabbar. 'Izzi Jabarut belongs to the Possessor of the earths & Heavens. Look at yourselves. May an honour dress be dressed on you. May Nur be given to your face. May a salamat/safety come to your body. May your rank be Sultan. To teach these...

Go ahead O Shahi Mardan. Go ahead, your yaran admire you. What are the people worthy of and what are they running after? Men are the beautiful and exceptional (hasna & mustasna) ones Allah created for them to know their humanity and then to be dressed by that honour. Prostrate/make sajda, bow your head/make ruku and magnify/takbir Allah Almighty. Give what is suitable to who deserves it. Don't hang a jewel around a donkey's neck. An animal is an animal. Many people wear pearls, diamonds, gold around their necks. Does it change them? Even if you put pearls on an animal's neck, an animal is still an animal. It doesn't find value because it carries pearls. Subhan Allah Sultan Allah. Go ahead O Shahi Mardan. Masha'Allah.

May our day be a good day. May our honour be high. May our servanthood for our Lord be forever. May our honour come down from Heavens. Abandon what people give on earth. It is imitated. "I burn, I destroy"... What happens if Allah Almighty sends you the Angel of Death before you burn & destroy? They will carry your coffin with a ceremony. What have you become? You have become a dirty/najis body. Allahu Dhu-l Jalal said: "Innamal Mushrikuna Najasun"(9:28). Who asserts his ego - everything he does for his own ego, everything he does to magnify his own ego is kufr, is shirk and that person becomes the laughing stock of shaytan. Leave your claim of greatness. O Man, listen! O yaran of Shahi Mardan, let us listen. Let us see the beauties. May the beauties be dressed on us from Heavens. Run away from artificiality. Don't be the artificially great, don't make artificial beauty. Don't do artificial work. Look for cleanliness at work.

O Man! Who did you work for today? "Today we went to our work. We worked at our work". Who did you work for? "Well, we worked for the government". Who is there above the government? There is the government of Allahu Dhu-l Jalal. Did you work for Him? You didn't. Then take it all over your face! You foolish man! You the animal of shaytan! What is your work? To build weapons, cannons. Is that so? For what do you build these weapons? To kill the people. Is the honoured Man created to be a murderer? Does being a murderer give Man an honour? Think O tyrant/zalim! Think O traitor/kha'in! Think O ignorant one/jahil! There is no thinking left in people now.

Go ahead O Shahi Mardan. Your yaran admire you. Listen, learn and you become a Man. If you don't listen, don't learn you become shaytan. May Allah not leave us to shaytan. Who is in the hand of shaytan becomes shaytan. Return to your Lord. Return to your Lord. Who is your Lord? Return to the Sultan, Allahu Dhu-l Jalal Who is the Owner of earths and Heavens. O our Lord! Shahi Mardan is who explains to us this. How beautiful. O yaran of Shahi Mardan. Correct yourself a little bit in every assembly. Smooth, correct your situation. Reveal your identity. Your identity is not the imitated identity in here, may you find your identity in Heavens. O Lord, You know. It (shaytan) is cracking up laughing now- it is making mankind scream, shout everywhere. How happy is it! "I made the people crazy" it says. "They can't know what they want. They are killing each other. How nice is my sultanate" says shaytan.

Say to the Christians, O Pope! Say O the ones who say "we should rule the religions"! O Man, what have you become? What did Confucius say? He said whatever he said. Anyone who passes beyond the border of humanity is an animal, he said. "Come to me". They did not accept and expelled that man- Confucius. Chinese, Japanese, Indian all know him. Yes. They know but they cannot comprehend what he says. They say "keep your humanity". "Keep your humanity" he says. All Prophets say like this. Janab-i Rabbu-l Izzat sent those men from among themselves to that ignorant nation so they explain them in their own language. And He (swt) sent the Prophet of the End of Times for all mankind. "Innama bu'istu litammi makarima l-akhlak". "I came to clean the people from the bad characteristics of their dirty ego - "makarima l-akhlak"- to teach about the adab that will make them go up to the Presence of the Sultan" says the Prophet of the End of Times. Listen, Understand, Do. Dunya will be saved from those troubles.

Allah Almighty made certain things forbidden. There is allowed & forbidden in every religion. There is good, there is beautiful. But the people of this time don't understand either allowed or forbidden. They do all dirty things and then call in the streets: "We are this, we are that". Go and ask Diogenes what you are. Who they call Diogenes was a philosopher of the old Greeks, a dervish person. He would sit inside a barrel. When Alexander the Great was passing by he saw him & asked: "O Diogenes, why do you sit in here?" He said "And where do you sit?" "I sit in a palace". "And my palace is here" he said. "I find comfort here". May you keep the way of Prophets, may you keep the order of Malakut. May you be respected and loved, may you not be cursed. Subhan Allah Sultan Allah. O our Lord! May He (swt) send us the holy ones like Shahi Mardan. O Shahi Mardan! Your yaran admire you. Our power & strength is gone. May the ones who will instill in us the strength and power of Heavens come to us. O yaran Shahi Mardan!

Fatiha.
