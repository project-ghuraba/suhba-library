As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Wa-la-qad karramnā banī Ādam" (17:70). Ṣadaqa Llāhu l-‘aẓīm. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla created human beings as the highest creatures. HeNone has favoured them and in order for them to do all kinds of goodness, HeNone sent them prophets. And HeNone told them what to do.

When Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla creates mankind HeNone gives them a soul. No one knows what the soul is except for AllāhNone. What is the soul? It is the business of AllāhNone. "Quli r-rūḥu min amri rabbī" (17:85). "I know about soul. A soul is like this and like that." Don't listen to people who say like this. Don't go near them. It means they will confuse you and weaken your īmān. Therefore, don't go near people who talk about these matters. People who talk about these matters... There are a multitude of things, this and that, you may talk about. But do not approach someone who goes beyond that. Run away from him.

Also, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla created the ego inside people. AllāhNone created conscience and compassion to oppose the ego. People with a conscience don't obey their egos. Certainly, ego can go on any way. It can do good as well as bad. But conscience is the good side of people. It shows the good things and makes you do good things. People who believe in Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla have true conscience and compassion. These are found in people with īmān (faith). Sometimes other people have them too. Having a conscience is mostly because of the fear of AllāhNone; to have a conscience means to be a human. A conscientious person fears AllāhNone. "Is what I have done good or bad? Is what I have done to this person in accordance with my conscience? What will happen if I oppress him?" Conscience stops them and says, "Beware of being without shame. Don't be without mercy. Don't cheat this person. Don't harm him. Don't slander him. Don't be cruel to these people or animals, whoever or whatever they are. Help them. If you can't help, don't harm them."

Conscience prevents people from doing badness. As we said, when there is conscience sometimes the ego gets used to being compassionate, step by step on the right way. And conscience is the aspect of people that safeguards the commands of ṭarīqah and Islām. Therefore, sometimes there are rare people with no īmān, who – because, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla created everyone – by following their conscience, eventually reach guidance. Many people with conscience see the truth and accept the truth. They certainly reach guidance in the end, and join the way of AllāhNone. Therefore, to be conscientious is a great goodness for people. It is a great virtue.

Conscientious people are also beloved by the people around them. They are shown respect. Otherwise, they say this man is merciless, shameless, and rude. People run away from him. They run away from that man as he follows his ego and shayṭān. Therefore, beautiful things raise the person; they raise the reputation of that person. He becomes beloved near AllāhNone. When Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla loves him, everyone will honour and love him. On the contrary, no one likes the person who is disliked by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Of course, they may love him for personal benefit, or they appear to. Otherwise, no one would respect him. There are many people like this in this world. Many people think other people love them. They are wealthy and rich. But actually, people respect their wealth, not them. They don't respect them. If they didn't have money, no one would respect them. No one would even look at them.

May AllāhNone protect us. May AllāhNone put us with good ones on a good way and make us from the beloved servants, in shā’a Llāh.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla HeNone saying in Qur’ān, Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Wa-la-qad karramnā banī Ādam" (17:70). Meaning, "WeNone make the human being the highest creature and WeNone give them the highest respect between creature." And HeNone gave us many thing, especially we are, when we arrive with our mother, first sending soul for us. With this soul you will live and it is highest for creature. What is soul? This is thing HeNone know, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. As we said before, there is many billions of secret we cannot know, we will not know. One of them this – soul.

With soul when coming to baby, become growing, growing until end of life. And when soul going, this only body left here and they will put him underground. And to ask what is soul, it is this forbidden for Muslim, for believer to ask for this. It is not allowed to speak about this. Who speak about this, don't listen to him, run away from him. Because Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam also not saying anything. HeNone said, "We not speak about this." Whole awliyā’, or ṣaḥābah also, they were saying this. And so nowadays many people... I don't know what their purpose for them to say this. Maybe to be famous or to be people interesting for them – "What is this man he saying? He know, he knowledgeable." No, not knowledgeable. He is ignorance. Ignorant because whole this great people not speaking about soul and you will speak? It is not acceptable, not... But our subject not this.

Our subject to be other thing in our life with soul, we have ego. This everybody know. You can speak until end of life about ego, no problem. You can speak. Because ego for everybody they have. And ego it is can be good, can be bad. But with ego we have also one thing which it is, ḍamīr, conscience. Conscience meaning to say good thing, to make you to do good thing. If you do bad thing, this conscience say for you, "You make wrong, you are not right, you are doing bad."

And conscience mostly in believer, especially in Muslim because this our order for our religion. All religion they have order for conscience but it is they forgetting, they change it. And Islām, this is very important because this Islām is mercy. Who is Muslim without mercy, he is not, he don't have real teaching of Islām. And mostly in Muslim this conscience and mercy but because this order from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, from Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Especially for ṭarīqah people, it is very important to be conscience [conscientious] – to not harm anybody, to not make bad thing, to not harm people, animal, anything. It is very important.

But for who not believer, sometimes you can find this but mostly, because they give freedom for themselves from everything, nothing can prevent them to do something, most of them you can't find, you cannot find this. When they say, sometimes they try to appear like this but most of time they are not like this. But for, for Muslim it is very important and it is for, as we said, to if you do anything wrong – "Don't cheat this man, don't cheat this, don't harm this one, don't make people fighting. Be helpful for people, with sincerity, not only little bit to cheat people [and] after, you harm them." But for also, for non-believer also who has this good manner, it is very good manner for humanity, human being. And most of them, AllāhNone, HeNone happy with them. In the end they find the right way. Who has this conscientiousness from human being, anyone, he will find and he will be... Because this is like nūr, light for heart. With this he find his way to Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Because we are not harming any people because and this ego can be trained with this.

With good people your ego will be good. With bad people it will be bad. For this you must be very careful for this. Don't cheat anybody. It is very important. Don't harm anybody. Don't... But our nowadays, whole people they are doing this. They said, "No problem." You are not [do this], you are lucky. If you are lucky, you don't do like them. You listen for your conscience, for your, order of AllāhNone and ProphetNone and good people. To find good people it is not easy. To find jewel, it is not easy also. You can find tons of stone, billions of stone, but to find jewel it is not easy. Don't say all this [is] jewel. No. Jewel only one, little bit. So follow the jewel people, and don't listen for other. Don't look for bad example.

Unfortunately nowadays whole you can very rare to find good example from anyone from East to West. Whole they are going after their ego. Nobody looking for this conscience. They said, "Go away. Don't look for it. Don't... You are not good. You are... We are following this ego. He is taking us higher." But they don't know, he take them down. AllāhNone keep us safe from our ego, not to follow bad one, in shā’a Llāh.
