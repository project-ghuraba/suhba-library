As-Salāmu ‘Alaykum wa Raḥmatullāhi wa Barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu Billāhi Minash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm.

Wa ṣ-Salātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Akhirīn.

Madad yā RasūlAllāh, Madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, Madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

Dastūr Mawlana Shaykh Abdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistāni, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-

Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-Suḥbah wa l-Khayru fi l-Jam‘iyyah.

Shukr to Allah ‘Azza wa-Jalla, we are spending these holy months in safety. It is a big favor that Allah ‘Azza wa-Jalla has given to us. Since the world was created, people have been causing suffering to each other and cannot tolerate each other. Allah ‘Azza wa-Jalla has given Rizq/provision to everyone. Allah ‘Azza wa-Jalla says, “If you obey Me, you will be in comfort.”

People are in such a condition that they think, “He will be a partner for my Rizq and wealth”. That is why all these fights and war are happening. It is because of this. Whereas, Allah ‘Azza wa-Jalla is the Guarantor of His ﷻ creatures’ Rizq. When Rizq is finished, a person will no longer live. The life of this world is with Rizq. How much is written, you take from it. And when it is finished, a person passes away to Akhirah. Mankind doesn’t think about it. They think about someone taking their Rizq and property. So they want to attack him and finish him.

Islam protects everyone’s rights. Those who are out of Islam don’t recognize any right, any law, or anything else. Whoever that is, he is like that. Islam is the religion of mercy. It makes you think about giving an account for everything and act accordingly. We have to act accordingly. Muslim states have always acted upon this manner. And those who had left the way and left Shari’ah have oppressed, destroyed and wronged people.

Therefore, the way of truth is the way of our Holy Prophet ﷺ. It is the way of mercy, the way of justice. Whoever goes out of this way goes astray. “ادً َبَأْْرِاْ نلاْيفِ ْ ْذشُ ْ ْذشَ ْْن مَ َف” - Whoever leaves the way will go to hell, says our Holy Prophet ﷺ. May Allah ﷻ protect us.

True Muslim states have never oppressed. Even if they were oppressed, they didn’t oppress in return. They showed goodness in return. The last Muslim state that was as such was the Ottoman Empire. In spite of having so much troubles and betrayal, it didn’t oppress anyone. And it is accepted in the presence of Allah ﷻ. Therefore, oppressing people is not good. Oppression won’t last long. May Allah ﷻ protect us from it. May Allah ﷻ protect us. Shukr to Allah ﷻ and praise be to Allah ﷻ for these days insha’Allah. May Allah ﷻ send the Sahib to end the oppression in the world insha’Allah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
