As-Salamu Alaikum, O Muslims! May your day, month and year be blessed/mubarak. Pray/make dua for each other and you will find khayr/goodness. May you not treat each other as enemies, you will not find goodness. Those who divide people into two they don't find any comfort in dunya nor do they get saved in akhirat. Try to be a good one.

We are Muslims.And, our people are coming from Ertugrul Gazi- may paradise be his abode. Then, the factor that kept the Ottoman Empire up & standing was our people, under the title "Ottoman". The children, descendants of Ertugrul Bey,had been guided to Islam for 700 years. Therefore we had been a noble nation who raised the flag of the Prophet (sas) for 700 years-Nation of Islam, Ottomans. May Allah bless our ancestor's soul. Their fame & glory spread all over the world. They made the whole world respect Islam & they, themselves also respected Islam. They kept Islam high & up, and Allah Almighty kept them high & up. We built a victorious Sublime State/Dawlatul Aliya, everlastingly victorious! May Allah make it's glory high & may He (swt) not separate you & us from it's flag.

As-Salamu Alaikum, O servants of Allah! Our address to you is enough for today. Honour it & you shall be honoured also. Show respect to Islam & Allah makes you respected also. If you despise Islam, then you shall be despised & dishonoured also. Listen & keep the right path! We are Ottomans & followers, citizens of the Caliphs who raised the flag of Islam- may paradise be their abode.

Be good ones. Wa Al Hamdulillahi Rabbi Al Alamin. Fatiha.
