As-Salamu Alaikum. Audhu Billahi min al shaytan al rajim, Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Madad ya Rasul Allah, Madad ya S Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, madad ya S.Muhammad Nazim al Haqqani, dastur! Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fil jamia' May this sohbat be a blessed one insha Allah.

The Prophet (sas) said, "Khayrukum man ta'allamal Qur'ana wa'allamah‚Äù. The best of you are those who learn the Quran & teach it to others. The Holy Quran is the al-Kalam al-Qadim (eternal word) of Allah Almighty. It is such a miracle..The Prophet (sas) had many other miracles also but even if there had been no miracle other than Quran, it would have been enough. The Holy Quran has everything in it. "Wa La Ratbin Wa La Yabisin 'Illa Fi Kitabin Mubinin" (6:59). This means, everything in this world from the past, present &future is in it. They may not underestimate, thinking that it is an (ordinary) book. Man can't produce even one ayat of it. Therefore they should be careful about learning Quran.

And their kids are already starting summer break. Kids can learn faster. Their mind is sharp. But Allah's Wisdom, even 50-60 year old people can also learn. When they strive for it, they learn. We see that many people in these ages- at the age of 30, 40, 50 or sometimes even older, they are learning how to read. Yes, but the one who teaches should be a qualified person. Masha Allah, now there are many people like this, who know the proper way & can teach well. A person who worked hard can read in 1 or 2 weeks. And we have seen, there are many who can learn in a week. If not one week, in 15 days or in 1 month. He can easily learn in 2 months. Even someone who doesn't know at all or who is not a fast learner can easily learn in 2 months.

Quran is shifa, barakah. "Fi Shifa wa Rahma" (17:82) There is healing & Mercy on who recite. Even for being imam, our Prophet (sas) says in Hadith: "The one who knows the most Quran should be the Imam. If all know the same, then the one who learned Quran first should be imam". Meaning if everybody knows the same, the one who learned Quran 1 month earlier, even that one month makes him more virtuous. And when you learn, don't stop reading saying "I already learned".

Read at least 1 page everyday. Normally the daily awrad is 1 juz but it is difficult for the people who are recently learning. Meaning they already read 1 page in 1 juz's time. They should try & read that 1 page. It becomes easier, opens as they read. Opens as they read more. Their reciting opens & their hearts open as well. Today we speak about the Hadith, Prophet (sas) said: "the Best of people are those who learn Quran &teach Quran". Quran, very Holy Book. If no another miracle for Prophet... Thousand of miracle for Prophet (sas). But if it was no miracle, Quran it was enough. Because until Qiyamat it's Mercy & blessing going on. And nobody can imitate Quran. Even one word, you cannot change. From other Holy Books, maybe you cannot find anything left- all changing. But Quran, since 1400..more than that, 1432 years still same. And if thousand or million years, also not changing.

Quran, when you read Quran become on you blessing & pureness also and opening your heart. Now summer time coming. Who has children in school, they finish school. After school, children most of them summer time they learning Quran. And it is easy for them. But for elder people also easy. Allah make it easy. There is who know to teach people Quran, there is special people, teacher. In one week you can learn. We see many people like this- one week they learn Quran, to read Quran. Sometimes maybe 15 days. If it is 1 month or 2 months, everybody in 2 months they can read Quran. They can learn to read Quran.

And to read in Arabic letters, it is better. Who they don't know Arabic, there is Latin letters also. But you must learn in Arabic letter. And when you learn this, you must everyday, at least you read 1 page. You may read 1 page, will be easy for you. Everyday more easy, more easy & opening your mind also.

For children, somebody ask Mawlana in Singapore. He was minister of education. He asked, in Singapore the Chinese they looking for children, when small to... Because clever, to how to make them clever. This minister asking Mawlana "what we do?" He said "teach them Quran". And really one who learn Quran from children, they become more clever. They become more clever & they become more good person. Because that age, they put in kinder- school or school for children. This school, the boy coming not knowing anything. If normal school, they teach them all badness. You cannot imagine how it is bad, this small school. But there is school for kindergarden only to teach Quran, praying & other thing. This is good. But other, don't send your children. Even who make imam, he must be more learning Quran from other. If all learning same, who learn before he can become imam.

Quran, everything in Quran. It's miracle is still - until Qiyamat. This is very important. Allah make us Ahlul Quran.

Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, Al Fatiha.
