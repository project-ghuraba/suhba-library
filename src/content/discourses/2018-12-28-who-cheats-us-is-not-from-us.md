As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu biLlāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīn wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa-l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

Our ProphetNone is saying, what we said in the khuṭbah, Man ghashshanā fa-laysa minnā, "He who cheats us is not from us," says our ProphetNone. This matter is in everything. In matters of dunyā, and in matters of ākhirah. You should be honest and not cheat in ṭarīqah and religion. What if you cheat? "You're not from us," says our ProphetNone. You are either with us...

There are some things. There are some politicians. They are with everyone and they cheat everyone. But they can't cheat AllāhNone. They can't cheat the ProphetNone. "I took bay‘ah and then..." There are different types of bay‘ah. It can be for giving barakah. You can take bay‘ah to get barakah. Or you can take bay‘ah sincerely and follow your shaykh. That bay‘ah reaches our ProphetNone. If Shaykh is gone, that bay‘ah is not lost. Because no one will stay in this world. Everyone will certainly pass away. And then that way keeps going on. You take bay‘ah and then you say, "I have found someone more powerful. He attracted me more." Then your bay‘ah was for barakah since the beginning. And that bay‘ah, the mashāyikh know it. "You can't cheat us," says our ProphetNone. AllāhNone is with us. Therefore, bay‘ah, taking bay‘ah has its benefits. It has great benefits in dunyā and in ākhirah for the one who gives real bay‘ah. "I gave oath, let's see how it goes." You get what you intend for.

There is one bay‘ah that when you give it, with Allāh'sNone permission you will be saved in your last breath and saved in ākhirah. And there are some bay‘ah that you give by chance and then say you don't like it and leave. You decide if you don't like it. It means you're not from us. As our ProphetNone says, you can't cheat. You can't cheat by saying you gave bay‘ah. Bay‘ah of ṭarīqah is a great favour and a great honour. In old times, they wouldn't accept people in ṭarīqah immediately. They tried them first and also istikhārah was made for them. But now we take bay‘ah from everyone for barakah. Those who want to be firm appear by themselves. Those who don't want are free. There is no forcing. This way is the way of heart. No one can interfere in the way of heart. If it's meant for them to be here, they will be here. If not, then somewhere else.

But if you say you were in another ṭarīqah and came here, that wasn't you anyway. You were not there from the start. You are susceptible then. Wherever the wind blows, there you go. And it's all the same for us. If you're meant to, you come here. If AllāhNone wishes, you come and get from the virtue. If not, there are hundreds of million people. None of them have anything to do with ṭarīqah. We wish for them all to come. It will be good for us. Because our wish is to bring people to the good way. But if they don't want, then no forcing. If AllāhNone favours them, they'll come. If not, they won't. Therefore, we should explain this matter of bay‘ah. Because some people think they give bay‘ah and that's it. No. If it's true bay‘ah, then you're not afraid. But this bay‘ah for barakah and experience, it comes and goes. "Nasū Llāha fanasiyahum" (9:67). "You can't cheat us," says our ProphetNone, you're not from us. In shā’a Llāh, if you save your belief, that's enough.

We in khuṭbah we say, mention ḥadīth. Bismi Llāh... Rasūla Llāh, ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, heNone said, "Who don't make people, trick people, don't cheat people, who cheat people he is – he who cheat us he is not from us," heNone said, Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. This for everything when you are in this world. This for everything for daily work, or buying, selling, speaking. What you are doing for this also, it is to not cheat people. And for religion also to, if you say something you must say true, not cheat people, saying I'm like this, I'm that one. I can do this, I can do that. And people believe on you and running after you, and you are not doing anything, only cheat them. This also not good, because Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, "If you – you must be from us or from – you are not from this people, you be with us or not with us."

There is two kind of people so for everything, for religion, for specially for ṭarīqah also. Because many people they are making, taking bay‘ah and after you see they are not in way, or they find another one in bay‘ah. Because there is many kind of bay‘ah: Bay‘ah for tabarruk, you are happy, maybe you can do with any Shaykh for tabarruk. But main bay‘ah, you must be with your Shaykh, but and there is other one also for trying, and for there is real bay‘ah. Real bay‘ah, who is keep this bay‘ah it is bless for him, and it will be safe him in this world and in another world. Even in... when going to out of this world, it will be help him to be in īmān, in shā’a Llāh. And to be in ākhirah, in Jannah also. This is who are keep their promise, bay‘ah for Shaykh and for ProphetNone and for Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla.

Our bay‘ah it is first accepting we are in bay‘ah. We say we are accepting Islām and we are accepting our Lord Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla, and HisNone Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, as messenger and Prophet. And we accept our Shaykh also to be guidance for us to straight way. This is bay‘ah, nothing else. So we are, knew you keep this. It is normally your believe you will be in the last breath with īmān, you go for ākhirah. This is what mashāyikh, murshid like to do for follower, for who are keeping their promise. They will be, in shā’a Llāh, safe. But who are, there is many people, there is many sometimes, many years you see them as they are real, sincere murīd. After you find they left and they said we are we find better than this, and we can do better from this. This people they are from – their bay‘ah from beginning it is not real bay‘ah.

And here, there is this world, it is free people, to do what they like to do. Not obligatory if they can be in ṭarīqah, and accept and keep their promise, it will be good for them. But if they not keep their promise, this not responsible for murshid or for Shaykh, to keep them. Because their bay‘ah from beginning, it's not well. This is what, for who real bay‘ah they have, they will be safe. But who are from beginning cheating, they will be out and they are free. And it is not obligatory for, because there is million of people they are not in ṭarīqah. But if they, ṭarīqah door is open for all of them. They like to come, we will happy. If not, they are not coming, also this is Allāh'sNone will. HeNone is not giving this for everybody. For special people they keep this. So also okay but if they can come, we are be happy to save them from dunyā, from shayṭān, from ego. But if they not, what we will do? We are obeying AllāhNone and not object for HimNone. Only we try. When they come, our door open and it will be good for them to keep their promise. And they will be happy here and here after, in shā’a Llāh.

AllāhNone keep all of us in this way, way of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
