Sheikh Bahauddin Adil, Sohbat of the 6th of April, 2016.

As-salamu alaikum. Allahu Allahu Allahu Allah. Allahu Allahu Allahu Allah. Allahu Allahu Allahu Allah. Allahu Allahu Allahu Allah. Allahumma salli wa salim 'ala Nabiyina Muhammad alaihi salam. Salatan tadumu wa tuhda ilaih mammara layali wa tula dawam. Allahumma salli wa salim 'ala Nabiyina Muhammad alaihi salam. Salatan tadumu wa tuhda ilaih mammara layali wa tula dawam. MashaAllah.

Alhamdulillah that we reached the three holy months. This is Allah Almighty's month is Rajabu sh-shahru Llah. This is Allah Almighty saying "I appoint this month for Myself." And then Sha'ban. Sha'ban is shahr [month] that RasulAllah (saw) saying "It is my month. And Ramadan for my ummah. It is gift for everybody." All the important spiritual actions was having in these three months. That's why, the seclusion will start from the beginning of Rajab - that I'm not attending. And it'd been only for holy people and I am not the holy one. Only for holy people. Of course, all the people, they want to be holy, like Sheikh Mahmud, German Sheikh.

And Mawlana Sheikh Muhammad, he is our Sultan. He decided to go for seclusion after Mawlana Sheikh. Because when GrandSheikh Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah ad-Daghestani (qs), he passed away and he was in his maqam, immediately Mawlana Sheikh Nazim, he make seclusion to dress the power of the previous Sheikh. But unfortunately, last year Mawlana Sheikh, he was so busy by putting his authority among the murids. And to not make any doubt about him. He was going all over just to make everybody satisfied with his Sheikhhood. Now he says alhamdulillah all the hearts been settled and everybody saying Mawlana Sheikh Muhammad, he is our Sheikh. And we are happy to be like this and to be with him as Mawlana Sheikh order that he say "Follow Sheikh Muhammad. If you want to be my follower, follow Sheikh Muhammad." Alhamdulillah. This is important to understand why Sheikh Muhammad, he was not last year in seclusion. This is what he done. He done the right way.

And now alhamdulillah we are following Sheikh Muhammad by going for seclusion. He open the door for seclusion for everybody. People can do for forty days, he also give permission. Or people they cannot do forty days, he give two kinds of permission. Just to make it known for the murids. It is because so many questions coming - what to do with this seclusion? Are we going to seclusion? How to go to seclusion? Why we are not going to seclusion? Who going to seclusion? When is going to seclusion? So many questions coming. So now we will make it more clear. Mahmud? Sheikh Mahmud, now we will make it more clear.

Sheikh Mehmet, he says you can do intention for seclusion from Fajr prayer. You wake up before Fajr and you start with night prayer. And then after make some wazifa. And then after that you make Fajr prayer

and then until Shuruq. Until sun rising, this will be your seclusion time. It will take about 2 hours maximum or 2,5 hours. If people able to do this one, also being accepted. If people, they cannot do this, and mainly the house ladies, they are preferring this - from 'Asr, they start from 'Asr until salatu l-'Isha. After praying 'Asr will go until 'Isha. And 'Isha will be finished. So this also seclusion. But I don't have the details for dhikr, what kind of dhikr they will do. This you have to ask from the administration or from who has been responsible, maybe Doctor Munir can answer this question. I don't think so.

Anyhow, Alhamdulillah now today we are here to send also my son. We call him Sheikh Mehmet Nazim. He was assisting his grandfather for 10 years. And he was day and night with his grandfather that his grandfather, he kept him for himself. And even he didn't allow him to marry also. I was, when Mawlana Sheikh, he want me to marry, he start to search for me when I was 17. When I say to Mehmet Nazim we want to... "No, no. No need. I marry in 32". It's ok, Mawlana Sheikh said. Why for me, you want me to burn very quickly? "You are different. He is different." Alhamdulillah now he is going for the seclusion. And inshaAllah we're hoping after his completing his seclusion successfully. And all your dua being needed for him because it is hard, hard duty. And I remember Mawlana Sheikh, he was telling me "I ask my ego - 'Do you want to be fasting all your life or you go for one for seclusion?' And my ego said 'No, no. I prefer to fast all my life.'" So difficult. So if Mawlana Sheikh saying like this, it's so difficult. That's why, it is not easy. So all the murid that they love Sheikh Mehmet Nazim, they've been required also to make dua for him. Otherwise, you have to go for administration. This is coming sohba as announcement.

And the manifestation of the seclusion, it is really heavy. Because all the eyes of Rasulallah (saw) and the Awliya and the saints, it is there looking. Because you are under heavy test. All of us we know when we go for test, examination, oh it will be sweating, we'll be like... At any movement we'll be... "What happened?" Very nervous. And this is really nervous things. Because you are trying to keep your ego and you are trying to go ahead with your wazifa, with your duty. And also you are trying to sleep less. And then your stomach will be your first enemy. Because you are not feeding it as much well. And doesn't feed your stomach that what stomach wants. You're only getting fed by little bit lentils. Lentils. Little bit. That's it. Finished. And this is the most enemy after the ego in our body, it's our stomach. It goes "You don't feed me, I will show you hell." That's why they say the fat people, they are very relaxed people. They don't have a trouble with their stomach. But the thin people, they are very nervous all the time, having problem with the stomach. Because stomach never being fed as they want. And this is important also to understand from where coming your enemy. It's very important.

(Don't look for the watch. I'm the one taking for the airport. Yes. So without me, you cannot go.)

These people they want something to be hear. Very important. Yes. No. What is Mawlana Sheikh sending this is important. It is not the plane. Mawlana Sheikh was stopping the plane. Very serious matter. Because the ummah have also right to know. It is... We cannot be selfish. This is important. Mawlana Sheikh he was sitting because he had some message to take. He has to know what he is doing. You have to know why he is going. It is not picnic. This is not picnic. It is very serious matter. The seclusion is very serious matter. Because you will be getting some responsibility, having responsibility that it open other views in your life. It is like opening. It is not, we are closing in the room and we don't know what's going on. Maybe I'm not going for seclusion but doesn't mean that I don't have vision also. Important to understand. Very important. But their duty is different. It is important for us, for ummah also to know how to continue. Because if you are not having your stock of spiritual power, you cannot continue these 40 days. Very difficult.

When you have these 40 days and you know what is next, you can understand and you can deal with it. Because here in the seclusion, how to deal with it. I like to give you a story about Mawlana Sheikh seclusion. Very short side. Don't worry, I will not be long. When Mawlana Sheikh, he go for seclusion with Sheikh Adnan after Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah passed away, my mother that night she see a dream. She see Grandsheikh Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah telling her "Tell Sheikh Nazim to be careful about Sheikh Adnan, to be careful about him, to not leave him alone." This is... And my mother, she was giving the service for them. She was cooking the lentils and giving them the service in their seclusion. And my mother, she tell Mawlana Sheikh "Last night I see Grandsheikh Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah and he is saying warning about Sheikh Adnan, to be careful about him, to look after him well." And Mawlana Sheikh smiling. Say "Sheikh Adnan last night he freak out. And he wanted to run away. He wanted to run away from seclusion." And he said "Adnan Efendi!" -like this, Mawlana Sheikh was all jalali - "Adnan Efendi, sit!" "No, I'm going. I'm going." "Ok, go". He open the door and he see in front of him another Adnan Efendi. And say "If you go out, I will kill you." He said "I don't care". He went and he see a lion in front of him. And say "Now I'm scared, I'm going to sit at my place".

This is how the seclusion is. It is not only sitting in the room making tasbih. No, it has other things. So many things that we don't know. I don't know because I'm not there. But this is how it is. That's why, it is important for us to let them know what they will face and to let them know at least to be ready for such things. When you are ready for something, you can deal with it more easy than you don't know. It's important. May Allah forgive us and make us all the time in the straight way to Allah Almighty and to make our intention good as we begin.

Wa min Allahi t-tawfiq. Bi hurmati l-Habib, bi hurmati l-Fatiha.
