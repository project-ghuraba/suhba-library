Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya Sadat As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Dastur Mawlana Sheikh Abdullah Faiz Daghestani, Sheikh Nazim al-Haqqani. Madad.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Allah Azza wa Jalla has created a higher degree of everything. The highest rank of people is our Holy Prophet ﷺ. He gave him the best things, one of which is Friday. Friday is special for our Holy Prophet ﷺ. It is a gift to his Ummah.

Every Friday is a feast. It is a day when the rewards are plenty. “There is the hour of response on that day”, says our Holy Prophet ﷺ. Whoever makes Dua at that hour, his Dua will be accepted. Therefore, it is necessary to pay attention to Friday. You can also make Dua while on your way to a place. There is no rule that says to make Dua while sitting. Dua can be done at any time.

The specialty of Friday is that it is a gift from Allah ﷻ to the Ummah of our Holy Prophet ﷺ as a feast. An hour is hidden in it, and its wisdom is for people to remember Allah ﷻ all the time and make Dua for what they want. If there is a certain hour, then they will all leave the whole day and just make Dua on that hour and go. But Allah Azza wa Jalla hid that hour so that they remember Allah ﷻ and ask Allah ﷻ all day on Friday. May Allah ﷻ give us goodness insha’Allah. May Allah ﷻ protect us and the Ummah of Muhammad ﷺ from the evil of shaytan, the evil of the jinn, and all evils and diseases.

What is more important than protection from disease is protection from the evil of shaytan. So much is happening, but again people go after shaytan. They do not remember Allah ﷻ. Even though there are so many things happening and people are afraid, but those who follow shaytan and those who follow their ego do not come to the right way. May Allah ﷻ grant us all true faith and goodness for the sake of this day insha’Allah.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. Al-Fatiha.
