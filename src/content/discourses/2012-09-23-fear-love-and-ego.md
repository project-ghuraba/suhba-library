Ya Salam.

They put me in this chair very early. I don't know why, but, this is order by Nabil, ‘must come and sit because we have to fix the camera’. By sitting here I was thinking what to say also, but I just had small conversation with Mawlana Shaykh and I was telling him, ‘I have people waiting to listen for this coffee talk’. And he said "Really?" I said 'yes'. He says that he doesn't know. He knows. He says, "What you talk?" I say, ‘whatever you send’. He says, "Yes, we have a lot of things to send you, so it will come to your heart and then it will go to their heart". Masha Allah this is good one, just in the morning. And today, I was fishing just now, sitting, earlier in my chair. Now this is my chair. I will not give it to anybody. Now it is mine.

It was the topic of 'fear'. The fear. What kind of fear we have. How many kinds of fear we have. And how much we are having fear. What is the size of our fear. From whom we are frightened, or afraid of. The first thing that is coming is that we are afraid from our enemy. Maybe we are living in peace. Alhamdulillah this is big, Allah Almighty gives it also that there is no real enemy to fear, as physical. So what we say? Okay, we are fearing our parents for example, but what kind of fear we have?

There are so many people fearing that the parents might hit them, or might shout at them, or might give them some punishment. So this is one kind of fear. But this fear is a very simple fear. The real fear is if you have good relations with your parents and you are fearing to not upset them. And this is the real fear. That is why when you have something in your heart for your shaykh be aware to not upset your shaykh. This is the real fear. Maybe the shaykh will shout on you, he will throw you from the window, throw you from the door, but as long as it is shouting it is okay. But if he is upset then this is the fear. Because if he is upset with you that means the Prophet (sas) will be upset with you and Allah Almighty will be upset with you.

That is why the shaykh, the teacher is very important. The teaching is very important. The perfect student is not afraid from his teacher. He is afraid for his teacher to not be upset with him. This is one of the most important fears we have to be careful about. Sometimes we think the teacher is giving us hard times. The master is giving us ‘not fair’. He treated us as ‘not fair’. "He is not fair with me, he is fair with that one. He is fair with Hussein", for example. Then what will happen? "Yahu, why he is like this?" Everyone has a way to be in 'tarbiyah'; trained, because Allah Almighty created us individual. Everyone has his ID and way to be trained.

So the perfect shaykh trains everybody according to his character, according to his ID, according to his 'in need' also. Now I'm really becoming very serious. I don't know why, it is matter that 'Ya Shaykh, how we became serious?'. We come from coffee talk to very serious talk. This is not my character at all but if Mawlana Shaykh is sending something to the heart to be distributed you will be like a loud speaker saying whatever he wants. It does not matter that you are sitting in his chair. This is his chair and you become he. Yes, you become he. Why? Because he is making you him.

This is very important thing to understand. Delicate point, because when Waliullah has people obeying him and following him, they will become he. This is definitely because his colour must be in you. His colour must be in you or you will be dressed as him. That is why Fana-u-Fillah. This is very high rank, to be in Allah Almighty, Fana-u-Fillah. You cannot go for that level without having your master because first you have to be as him, and then he will take you from station, to station, to station. So he is the one lifting you up, up, up, up. But what is the important thing here? To obey your shaykh, be with your shaykh. So you allow his colour to be in you.

The other day we had been saying about the ego that we have to make the ego, slowly, slowly, to understand and to accept the shaykh, to obey the shaykh. You know to make the ways to accept the shaykh because the ego is very important instrument again in that, if the ego blocks you, you will not allow the colour of the shaykh to be in you. Never. It will be big blockage and you cannot pass it. So that is why Sufism, the main topic in Sufism is to concentrate on your ego. Sufism doesn't tell you to pray, to fast, to do all this because it is Shariat. It is already known. Everyone knows to pray, how to fast, how to keep zakat, how to be good Muslim. This is okay. This is general.

But tassawuf is telling you to "Beware of your ego, beware of your ego. Ego, ego, ego. EGO. To wake up people. That is why tasawwuf is building up how to train your ego. The ego, everyone thinks it is simple. Everyone is taking the ego as, "Aah, we can handle it". No, you cannot handle it. You cannot. Nobody can handle his ego by himself. No way! You need a trainer. You need a shaykh. You need a Waliullah. Not anybody, not any trainer not anyone coming to you. No, first you have to train your ego. To train my ego. You have to be passing from that training that you can be able to 'train my ego'. But I have to be helpful for you to, 'train my ego'. So that is why it's very important that you treat your ego slowly, slowly, okay we have to accept this, so many things will come against your ego, hitting your ego. And ego saying 'No. No'. And you say 'Yes. Yes. Let me accept some of it'. So you just start to eat from your ego's decision. You don't make your ego to say 100% 'no'. 99 % the ego says, 'no', & you say OK I'm able to accept 1 % of it. So that is when you start to bite and start to make the light of the shaykh enter through this 1 %. So when it's entering the green will be on your side so that will help to remove the ego from the bad side to the good side.

I remember one day our very famous Sayyid Ali, Yemeni Prince, he is one of the oldest murids of Mawlana Shaykh and he say, I remember we been in London he went to the mihrab & make khutbah and he was being disturbed by some people but, he said, "I was with Mawlana Shaykh, 20 years with him, and everytime he gives sohbah, he talk about ego". And as classic Arab, objecting all the time, saying, "What is this Shaykh? Doesn't have anything else other than ego? Let him talk something else & he keeps saying 'Beware of your ego, 20 years I 'm with him and every sohbah must have something for ego". And he was in khutbah, saying, "Wallahi'l Azim!" Arab. "Wallahi'l Azim Ya Shaykh! You are right. Even if you say 100 years about this ego is not enough. 100 years you say about this ego is not enough. Now I accept, Yahu".

This is Mawlana Shaykh. He is taking us according to our mind, according to our level, according to our understanding that is why we're very easy-going normally we're very easy-going. I'm talking first of me, I am very easy-going. Mawlana Shaykh is treating me very easy-going. Because one day he said, "I am ruling kindergarten. I am not ruling murids, if I will put the rule of how to be murids then I don't think anybody will be here, I will be alone. I will be a lonely shaykh because nobody can carry". It's not easy to carry, to be murids for Mawlana Shaykh or for the real masters.

So from his mercy point Alhamdulillah that we're coming, to his mercy point that he is accepting us, and he allow us to put turban, to put beard and wear Naqshbandi. He allows us. This is like fun when you have small kids specially the girls, they wear their mothers high-heeled shoes so you see them very funny. So we are like that we are wearing things that actually do not belong to us but we are getting this to be as: man tashshabbah bi qawmin fa huwa minhum. If you imitate somebody you like, you will be from them. So this is very generous of Mawlana Shaykh.

So that is why when he sees some- body wearing sunnah he's very happy, says, "Alhamdulillah, somebody wearing sunnah, even to see it, it makes Allah happy with us, even if our inside is empty. Nothing. But it is okay we're imitating, this is our intention. To be with the road of those that Allah Almighty He loves them. And this is the love factor coming in. To fill, fulfil the empty side of our insides. After the fear because the love also is very important. Nobody will be without love. You cannot sit here without love. Must be love, so the love is very important. Why? Because if you love you will be forgiven. Even if you have so many suspicious or nonsense thoughts and ideas and whatever, because the love will clean everything. "Hey you are black eyes but normally if you are blue eyed you'd be more beautiful or more handsome, but it's okay, I love you". So you don't care about what is the shape or what is coming because you love. So when you look for the Shaykh and you love the Shaykh, you make the empty side of what you have full of love.

So the love that Allah Almighty put in our heart, Allah Almighty's finger is in it. Allah Almighty is putting the love, because the love is coming from Him. The love is coming from Allah Almighty because you cannot create love. Allah Almighty, He put the love. So if you love you don't see. You become blind. When somebody loves somebody he is blind because the love will take over everything. It doesn't see the fault, it sees nothing. It just sees the love. That is why fill the pot with love then insha Allah it will be completed. And what Allah Almighty gives for us, what we are fearing to not upset (make it like school lesson) and to be with Allah Almighty as a servant to be trained for our ego, to make the greenery of the Shaykh coming to you and then fulfil the empty side of our things by the love of Allah Almighty.

Wa min Allahi tawfiq bi hurmat al Habib, bi hurmat al Fatiha.
