As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is saying, Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Tu’tī l-mulka man tashā’u wa-tanzi‘u l-mulka mimman tashā’u wa-tu‘izzu man tashā’u wa-tudhillu man tashā’u bi-yadika l-khayru innaka ‘alá kulli shay’in qadīr" (3:26). says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gives property and rule to whomever HeNone wants. HeNone raises and lowers whomever HeNone wants. Everything is in the hands of AllāhNone. When things AllāhNone gives are good, you should be grateful. When they are not good - astaghfiru Llāh. AllāhNone gives people what they deserve – you should be patient and thankful.

Because Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gives people whatever they deserve. If they don't want the good, AllāhNone gives according to what they want and then they suffer for it and they get no benefit. If AllāhNone gives you a good person [as ruler], you should make shukr and say, "Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla favoured and showed kindness to us. We have a worshipping and believing person over us." Since a hundred years, after the Ottomans, in return for the great goodness of the Ottomans, they showed ungratefulness and destroyed them. And they have never stopped. They have become worse and worse. What shall we do? We should be patient and praise AllāhNone. We should ask forgiveness for our ancestors who rebelled and came against the Sulṭān, the Khalīfah. And now we have this trouble. Forgive us, yā Rabbi. Remove this disaster from us. You should pray like this. Because when you don't say like this, it only gets worse. You think you can do something but something worse will happen.

Shukr to AllāhNone, thanks to the service of our ancestors, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla favoured us and they are still standing at the head of Islām and the Muslims. Because the khilāfah is still with the Ottomans. Until it is passed to its owner, the trust remains with them. With their barakah, shukr to AllāhNone, they are helping all Muslims and giving them hope. However, there are many people who are Muslim but don't understand. As we said, you may not like what is happening but you should be thankful that AllāhNone showed kindness to us and everything is open now, everything is manageable. Some people say they are barely surviving, nevertheless, they can afford to smoke every day and have smartphones. Even the poorest cannot do without them here. They say it is necessary. Even the poorest have them. And then they keep complaining. They have lost Islāmic adab. They have learned what unbelievers taught them, this democratic system. There is a rule [in that system] that says that the demands of even the least intelligent people should be fulfilled. And everyone is allowed to speak up. They don't like anything. When everyone looks at each other and chooses the best one by guessing, they deserve what AllāhNone gives them. May AllāhNone protect us.

Everything has its time. With the permission of AllāhNone, this time is the time of Islām. There cannot be anything else, with the permission of AllāhNone. It will be the time of Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām, in shā’a Llāh. Relief will come with him after these troubles, not anything else. They have tried everything. They have tried communism, capitalism, a lie called democracy. They have tried all kinds of things. They saw their uselessness yet they are persisting in it. Their stubbornness will be removed by the Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām. May AllāhNone send him as soon as possible. May he arrive, in shā’a Llāh.

Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla in Qur’ān HeNone is saying: Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Tu’tī l-mulka man tashā’u wa-tanzi‘u l-mulka mimman tashā’u wa-tu‘izzu man tashā’u wa-tudhillu man tashā’u bi-yadika l-khayru innaka ‘alá kulli shay’in qadīr" (3:26). Meaning: AllāhNone gives power for who want to rule people and take power from people also from who was ruling people, and make people to be high in society or around with human being, to be high position and some people to be lowest. Make them too low, to be no value for them. All this in AllāhNone will. AllāhNone what HeNone want everything it is become like this.

We are living in time everything mixed like a soup. So, people they thought they can do something to save themselves. They're looking for, they said, "We must look for a new president, for a new government. We want early election. This not good, we'll find better." This is ridiculous thing from people, because Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla HeNone gave what they deserve. If you are deserve good people, AllāhNone HeNone must give you good people to rule you. If you are deserve bad people, also AllāhNone will give. If HeNone give you good people, you must be thankful, shukran liLlāh. If not good – everything from AllāhNone good – and this also [must be] good. To send not good people to rule people also good because people they deserve this.

People they are not knowing, not thanking for people who doing good thing for them. When people doing bad thing, they are happy and they like them to rule them. But when they rule them, they will be very unhappy. Very bad. But for Muslim, he must be patient, not doing anything against this, only to be – we make al-ḥamdu liLlāh for everything. Al-ḥamdu liLlāhi ‘alá kulli ḥāl – meaning al-ḥamdu liLlāh for everything AllāhNone give us: "This also we deserve, so HeNone give us. We are also accept this. We are not objecting for Allāh'sNone will." Because this time people really they're becoming very against every good thing. Every good thing is from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.

Especially after Ottoman Empire, nobody – from this more than forty country become; they divide for more than forty country. Each one, since more than hundred years, they are suffering and every time going worse and worse and worse. Why? Because they are deserve. They are coming against Khalīfah of Islām, Khalīfah of Rasūlu Llāh ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, the highest one in Islām world, who all, everybody, they must accept his what he said, and listen to him, not coming against him. And he was really giving everything for his nation, for human being even. But this shayṭān people they destroy all this empire and make it small, small countries with such a bad rule, ruler and everything. They make it especially to be day by day, worse and worse and worse. And it is also punishment from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.

So we must ask for forgiveness for us from our LordNone. Our grandfathers that time, maybe great grandfather, they was coming against Khalīfah, against... Coming against Khalīfah meaning coming against Islām. Because he is rule, he is Khalīfah of Rasūlu Llāh ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Coming against Khalīfah, against Rasūlu Llāh ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. This it was really very bad, and we must ask for forgiveness and not coming against any ruler in country even they are worst oppressor one. Also we must be patient because we are deserve this. What happened everything it is not coming without reason. There is reason for this. For this we are not want to do anything against this. We are waiting for forgiveness from AllāhNone and to send us HisNone Khalīfah, Sayyidunā Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām. He is only can be survive for human being. Because they finish everything. Everything they finish from capitalist, communist, socialism. Everything they try, becoming worse and worse and they have now, they try the worst thing also. They try to make human being to finish with bad, worse, bad for human being, for human nature.

So, there it must be, this is end for them, in shā’a Llāh, and beginning to be Islām whole world, in shā’a Llāh. We are waiting for this. We are not hopeless. We are happy for what AllāhNone give us. What HeNone want, it will be, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone keep us in HisNone way and make people to come to this way to not be in danger.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
