Allahumma salli wa sallim ala Nabina Muhammad alaihi salam salatan tadumu wa tuhda ilaih mamarr al-layali wa tul ad-dawam Allahumma salli wa sallim ala Nabina Muhammad alaihi salam salatan tadumu wa tuhda ilaih mamarr al-layali wa tul ad-dawam Allahumma salli wa sallim ala Nabina Muhammad alaihi salam salatan tadumu wa tuhda ilaih mamarr al-layali wa tul ad-dawam

Ya Salam. Alhamdulillah, we reach safely Rabi ul-Awwal, the month of Rasulullah (sas). Safar ul-khair was so heavy this year, having so many signs, so many fitna, so many things going on and on and this month of Safar was like never know how to end. So hard, this month it was so long. We didn't even believe that this month will be over. Everything was upside down. Everything was messed up. Everything was as going inside the typhoon. What is this world? What's happening? Typhoon coming, coming, coming.

Alhamdulillah, we reached shahru-rasul Sayyidina Muhammad (sas) mawlid ar-rasul, shahru Rabi ul-Awwal. The 1st of Rabi ul-Awwal, Alhamdulillah. First I give my congratulation for all of the Muslim world, for all the believer, for all the beloved of Rasulullah (sas) and for all the one they love Rasulullah (sas). We have to know our life without the love it doesn't have any meaning. It doesn't carry any value and, it is like dry desert.

Mawlana Sheikh he went to Madina i-munawwarah for seclusion. And he say "I was passing from the desert to reach Madina i-munawwarah, and it was only yellow desert, all sand, nothing, dry". He say "When I go and make my seclusion, in the way back there was a rain coming in the desert" and he say, "I passed from the same desert, I see flower, colourful, so many kinds of roses, so many kind of plantation that you never seen when it was dry". This is how the love it's like the rain, bringing all the flowers and making some meaning for your life.

Alhamdulillah, we are proud to be the lover of Rasulullah (sas). We are pretending to be the lover of Rasulullah (sas), and we are putting our life for the love of Rasulullah (sas). This is, we have to claim this one and we shouldn't be afraid because so many people they say the love of Rasulullah is shirk. Yahu, how can be? To not love, that Allah Almighty He love Rasulullah (sas) and you don't love Rasulullah (sas). How can be? If you put it in the mathematical way it will never match the true answer. This is like 1+ 1 = 2, everybody knows this one. And you say no, it is 14 1/2. 6. From where coming this point and 6 and the 1/2 and 14? How?

This is how they are understanding. This is how they are professor. No, Allah Almighty He make the love so simple, it's like 1 + 1 = 2. So if you say to child, 3 years old, he will understand 1 + 1 = 2. This important. This important that Allah Almighty, He make the love available for every heart. He didn't keep it for Himself, he can say, "The love is for Me, I am not giving it for My servant". Allah Almighty He can do. No, He say, "The love I put it in every servant's heart".

Every servant's heart, he's have a love. You look for animal world, you see how the mother love the kids. Any animal, the mother will feed the kids first and then what left, she will eat. And this is, if there is no love that wild animal will eat first, and then will not let the children to eat. Allah Almighty how great He is, He show you what the love can do. Yahu, in our situation we are believer, Alhamdulillah.

If you are not in love with what you believe, you are not believer. So simple. If you are not having love in your belief you will never be in that point. No way. You look for Sahaba i-kiram. When Sayyidina Muhammad (sas) he say, "This is khutbat ul-wada, the last khutbah I do after that I am going". He say, "I'm going and I'm giving you, I give you everything". So many Sahaba when they are going out from the mosque of Rasulullah (sas), they didn't go home, just said "We are in duty and we have to distribute this love all over the world".

This religion is not for the Hijaz, it is not for Makkah, Madina, it is for whole the world. Do you believe these Sahaba if they don't carry the love of Rasulullah (sas) they can distribute this religion around the world? So simple. We have to look for today Muslim, yeah. Big question mark. Very big question mark, today Muslim. La haula wa la quwwata illa billahi l-alliyyi l-azim. Violence, violence, violence in the name of Islam. We feel more bad when we see all this. We feel very terrible when we see all this. This really hurts, because Islam never been violent. Never ever.

Islam is justice. It is putting everything straight in the justice. When you look, honesty in Islam, justice in Islam, love in Islam. Respect in Islam, every good manner in Islam. Everything in Islam, Alhamdulillah, we are proud to be Muslim, by the way. We are very proud to be Muslim, Alhamdulillah because we know we are in the right way, because we have things that called 'love'. We can open up our house, no problem. We can open up our heart, no problem. We can open up everything, no problem, because we are having the inner peace.

So many people they are looking for inner peace. And they go right, left, up, down. Wherever they go they cannot find this inner peace. But Alhamdulillah, Allah Almighty He put it inside. This is Allah Almighty, He put the inner peace inside us. Because when say: ash-hadu an la ilaha illallah wa ash-hadu anna muhammadan abduhu wa rasuluhu you got the key for inner peace. That's the first step. This is the key. Some people they say, "Where is the key?" If I say: ash-hadu an la ilaha illallah wa ash-hadu anna muhammadan abduhu wa rasuluhu, you got the key. Yahu, I didn't see the key: ash-hadu an la ilaha illallah wa ash-hadu anna muhammadan abduhu wa rasuluhu, you got the key. Finish. You got your inner peace. This is No.1. Allah Almighty He make it like this. This is the treasury of you.

This remind me of my mother. She was saying to us small story when we are kids. There was one man, he was travelling in very cold area, and that night was really heavy snow and really heavy wind. And he see one small village so he say, "I'm safe". It was almost sun-setting. And he go and knock one door. The person he opened the door, say, "Will you take me home, I will be die freezing". Say "Sorry I cannot take you". Boom. Close the door. And he knocked the other door also, "You are not welcome, we don't care". He went for other door. "No, no, no.

Finally he come for last door, "I have to otherwise I will die from the snow". And the man he tell him, "Look, our tradition we don't accept guest. We don't care, but there is a house there, it's empty house you can stay there. But in this house nobody will accept you. Even me". He say "To who belong that house?" "We don't know, but this is empty house, this is ghost house. Everybody goes there, in the morning, we take his body out". Say, "Choose either to stay in the snow or to be in the ghost house". And the man said, "Yes, let me go for the ghost, I don't want to die in the snow. At least I have place and I die in the house".

He went there, open door, it was like horrible house. Very horrible, really scary but no choice. What to do? He entered and he find some corner and he make himself to be little bit hot. And he take on big stick, say, "In case somebody will come I will hit it". And he was falling to sleep because he was so tired and then the house start to be shaking. "Woo, ooo..." It's a ghost house. And then big noise coming. Big, big noise coming. My mother she was saying in Turkish "geleyim mi?" like this. We been scare also. What's going on here?

Say "Do you want me to come?" Was very, very loud voice. And he was wake up and he was so scared. He doesn't know what to do and again that voice saying "geleyim mi?" What to do and more scared. Almost his heart stopping. And finally he find courage in himself and he pick up his stick. And he say, "geleyim mi? " Also, "want me to come". And he take the stick and say, "If you want to come, come!" And say "geleceksen gel" in Turkish. "If you want to come, come". And he was taking the stick and ready to hit.

The wall was breaking and one treasure start to come, zhuzhuzhuzhu... Was golden jewelleries and it was going, going, going and it was one room full of jewellery. They say it was the secret word for this jewellery to come, "Come". But so many people when they hear this voice they have heart attack and die. But this man he was so in courage to say, "Come". This is Allah Almighty He give him this jewel, He give him this treasure. Now we are in the way that Allah Almighty He give us the treasury of inner peace. When you say this one you are already having this. So many people they are scared. Don't be scared. Allah Almighty He is saying to you this is the key, for that treasury it was the key to say "Come". For inner peace the key is: ash-hadu an la ilaha illallah wa ash-hadu anna muhammadan abduhu wa rasuluhu. This is because all the people in the world they are looking for inner peace. Whatever you do you can gain but there is something you cannot gain, it is inner peace. This is gift. It's a gift from Allah Almighty. We want to have this gift, Alhamdulillah.

Alhamdulillah, that's why I'm saying we are lucky to be in the way of Rasulullah, to be in the love of Rasulullah and to distribute this for people. This is our main job. We want to distribute, we want to give everybody. We are willing to give everybody. There's something you don't like to give, you don't like to share. So many people don't like to share their food, don't like to share their wealth. But these people they are lonely. Maybe you have billions but you are lonely because you are separating yourself from the people. You are making status for yourself and you say these people they are very below of me, I don't have to speak to them. But this is not good because you will be alone, you will be lonely. You will not have communication with people. Allah Almighty He make the good taste to have communication with people.

"waja'alnakum shu'uban waqaba'ila lita'arafu" (49:13)

This Allah Almighty saying in Quran. We create you as tribe, create you as nation to come along together and to understand each other. To get more enjoyment in your life. This Allah Almighty how He make it so nice and simple. He never tell you if you have your status then you will be better with people. No you will be worse because you are separating yourself. And this is not good because Allah Almighty He make us to be together. When we are in a good way together, this is even better and better.

This is what we are willing to have, this is what we are willing to distribute and this is what we are giving because Mawlana Sheikh is the one teaching us how to give because to give also you need teacher. You cannot give in your own. Your teacher will tell you if you give this side is ok, if you give this side, is not ok. Is very important. May Allah forgive us and make us from the people that they love Rasulullah (sas) and are tightly loving Rasulullah (sas). And, may Allah make us happy here and hereafter, with the inner peace that He grant us.
