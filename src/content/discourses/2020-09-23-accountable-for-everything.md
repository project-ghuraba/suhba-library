Aū‘dhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-Shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Waṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-F’āiz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Allah ‘azza wa jalla says in the Holy Quran, bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm:

Everyone is responsible for what he gets and does, and he will be held accountable for it. Everything is recorded. Everything remains in the sight of Allah, so that no one will be able to deny even the smallest things, whether we forget them or not. We night forget, but Allah does not. All is recorded and kept, so that we will give account for it. We will answer for it. Everybody will get credit for whatever good he does, and will receive reward for it, and his end will be good. But if he does wrong, he will suffer the consequences, and suffer the punishment for it.

The most important thing is to believe in Allah. This is true to such an extent that the Holy Prophet (SAW) said in a hadith that although good and bad might be done, nevertheless what is most important is to say, “La ilaha illa ’Llah”, whereas to associate others with Allah, there is nothing worse than that, for the result will be hell forever. Our Prophet (SAW) in a hadith also says that if someone commits adultery, or steals, or does evil, if he has said,“ La ilaha illa ’Llah”, he will enter Paradise after he has received his punishment. But if he is insists to deny, (and such stubbornness is what Shaytan wants, [for] Shaytan tries to get people to enter Hell,) then because of that, there will be no salvation for him. But the other will answer for his good and evil deeds, and after he has believed, after he has believed in Allah, and after he has not ascribed partners to Him, he will eventually be in Paradise, insha’Allah.

For him, that is the important thing. But more important is to pay attention to what we do. When we do evil, don't say that no one has seen us. And if you do, repent and ask forgiveness. And if you have done anything against people, ask them for forgiveness, or repay them in this world, so that you may not be held responsible for it in the Hereafter, and so that you may be pure in the hereafter.

Entering the Next Life with burdens and sins is the hardest and worst thing. It is for a man’s benefit to be pure in the Hereafter. What else do you earn in the world? No matter how much you gain, don’t be happy that fooled such and such a person and that you gained some money from it. Be careful! We all commit sins, people commit sins, but if you ask for forgiveness and repent, Allah will forgive. May Allah forgive us. Allah is all-forgiving. May He treat us with mercy. May He forgive those who ask for forgiveness. But if they don’t, they themselves will suffer the punishment. Allah forbid! May Allah keep us safe from sins, inshallah Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-F ti ah
