Assalamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakatuh,

Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi RasuLillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

Tariqatunas sohba, wal khayru fil jamiyya.

Let us apply the order of our tariqa and make a sohba. We will make a sohba here and there is a good gathering here. We have not come to this holy place in a while. We did not come last year because we were travelling, and we went here and there. We did not get the opportunity to come. Thank Allah we came this year. Our ikhwan (brothers and sisters) are close by anyway and we see them all. However, still visiting these holy places are a benefit for them and for us, and it is a blessing (baraka) for everybody Inshallah.

Lots of things took place in the world, in this area, and in this country since two years ago until now. Our Holy Prophet (SAW) informed and told the believers (mumins) since the beginning that End Times dissensions (fitnas) will occur. The world was never a bed of roses anyway. People should not think they are in heaven in the world. The world is a place of hardship and a place of trials. People think this world will last forever, meaning with no end. May Allah protect him, when our Hussain Chichek hodja makes dua he says, “Ila nihaya.” “Ila nihaya” means something with an end. Nihaya means end. If you say, “Ila mala nihaya” it would mean for eternity.

No, this world will not last forever. It has a time and a date, and when the time comes this world will end. It has its signs. What our Holy Prophet (SAW) said is true, it is right, and everything he said ocurred. A nabi (prophet) means someone who gives news of the future. Our Holy Prophet (SAW) told about the things that will occur all the way from his time to the Day of Judgment.

These times we are living are End Times. Our holy Prophet (SAW) would call it shambles. What does shambles mean? It means total disorder. A time when those killing will not know why they are killing, and the killed will not know why they are being killed. What our Holy Prophet (SAW) said is the truth. We see that new cases and new incidents occur everyday. Of course, people are sad about it. They cannot do without being sad. However, people should not fall into despair and hopelessness, and their morale should not be down. Because these need to happen so the things our Holy Prophet (SAW) said can occur.

What our Holy Prophet (SAW) said was: “The world will fall into darkness and tyranny. There will be injustice and evil. There will be all kinds of filth and all kinds of brutality. Only a person coming from my progeny will be able to stop it. When he appears, all tyranny, evil, and injustice will end. Only he will be able to forbid it all. He will be victorious over all, and Islam will appear. There will be justice, there will be mercy, and there will be goodness.” We see now that there is oppression everywhere, and mostly against Islam. It is more against people who are on the path of our Holy Prophet (SAW), true Muslims.

The whole world is restless. They think they will end being Muslim and have rest. This is Shaitan’s trickery. Shaitan has fooled them. He shows them this way, “You will be at rest and you will be in heaven if you finish the Muslims, and scrape off and throw away Islam!” This is a complete lie and a trick. However, people who do not believe in Allah (JJ) and in the Prophet (SAW) accept this as the truth. That is why they are attacking Islam with all they have got. They attack Islam and they try to finish Islam.

There is a nice point here too. The whole world, all perverted ideas, perverted religions, perverted people, and brutal people are attacking Islam. Actually, it should have been finished by now. Because they are attacking with all the world’s weapons, soldiers, hidden and apparent, and with all they have got. People would come to faith and come to the truth if they knew this point.

Well then, why is this Islam not finished? Because it has an owner. If it did not have an owner, if it were a false religion, and if it were a false belief they would have finished it long ago. If the people who are not on the right path, those who are attacking Islam, had comprehension they would know this is the religion of Allah. Allah is helping us. Allah is the owner of Islam. Allah is the one protecting Muslims. If it were another baseless thing it would have been finished long ago. They would have finished it from the beginning before it came this far. But this religion has an owner and they cannot defeat Him. It is something that is not possible to defeat. Those who oppose Allah Exalted and Glorious be He are always in frustration, have always lost, and are defeated.

People are thinking, “These are three to five dervishes, jokers (bahluls), and incapable people. We can finish them off!” They do not know those who look like bahluls and dervishes are people who have found the truth and are on the path of Allah. They are under protection in this world and will be winners in the hereafter Allah willing. There is peace in the hearts of those of true faith. As we said earlier, the world is not paradise, but everywhere is a pradise for the person who is with Allah and for the person whose heart is full of faith. Like Hazrat Ibrahim Alayhis Salam, he thinks of himself in paradise even in the fire. They threw him in the fire and Allah Azza wa Jalla turned the fire into a garden of paradise for him.

This applies to everybody. Allah Azza wa Jalla did it for Ibrahim Alayhis Salam and He would do it for every mumin. Therefore, it is not good to fall into despair due to this situation and these fitnas. As we said, you might have pity and feel sorry, but this does not mean the world is ruined and gone. That is why the future is ours. The future belongs to those who are with Allah. Never fear, even if the world crumbles. Do not be sad and do not be hopeless. Allah is able and capable (qabil wa muqtadir) of protecting us all.

Shaykh Mawlana had advice and he would tell them before these events took place. Do not go to overly crowded places if you have no business. Stay at home, make more zikr of Allah, and do not forget charity. You should definitely give sadaqa before leaving the house everyday. And read seven Ayatul Kursis according to the method taught by Hajja Mother (Haji Anne) and Shaykh Mawlana. You will blow the first one on the right side and the second on the left side. Blow towards the front, the back, above, below, and the seventh all over. This will be like a shield for you. No devil, no jinn, no magic, and no external trouble will touch you that day Allah willing. They stay away from you.

These days, as we said, are days of fitna. We also need to tell about the advice of Shaykh Mawlana because he would say them insistently. There is no need to join crowds and to roam around unnecessarily when you do not have much business, meaning when there is no necessity. May Allah help us Inshallah.

Inshallah we see the good days. They are near Inshallah. The good person and the bad person will be clear when Mahdi Alayhis Salam appears. Because there is no injustice during his time, nobody’s rights will be intruded upon as an oppressed and as a suspect. We see in these days that people have become such devils that even though they themselves are the traitors, they show innocent people as traitors. Oppressed people occur this way.

Their intentions are devilry in every way. The men have absolute Shaitanic methods. They are both hiding themselves and are making a huge mass get oppressed. They are making people become enemies of each other both materially and spiritually. Because the sighs of the oppressed are a big thing, if a person gets opressed and begs Allah saying, “I am oppressed. These people have incriminated me even though I did not do anything,” then may Allah protect from that person’s curse.

However, it is impossible for such things to happen during Mahdi Alayhis Salam’s time. All kinds of evil exist now since we live in a normal world. May Allah keep us all safe from their evils. Inshallah when we overcome these days, not only Turkey but the whole world will become Muslim. This is the promise of Allah. The word that Allah (JJ) gives us through the tongue of our Holy Prophet (SAW) is a promise. The whole world will become Muslim.

It will not be the same as now when Mahdi Alayhis Salam appears. Different karamats (spiritual gifts) will occur that time. The whole world, from top to bottom, will become Muslim, and those who do not become Muslim will go to the other side. There is no other alternative. That time there will not be these bombs and what not. The technology of those times will be different. There are not 1001 but millions of kinds of karamats and miracles next to Allah. Wondrous (khariqul’ada), extra ordinary things will occur. Those times are beautiful times Allah willing.

“Wa yashfi sudoora qawmin mumineen,” He says (Sura Tawba:14). What does it mean? He will pour water in the hearts of mumins. Now we see and it aches us inside. Our insides are burning from anger and sadness. You see all the traitors, and the whole world is coming upon us. We are only trying to protect ourselves. You will give a sigh when the whole world becomes Muslim. People’s hearts will be sprinkled with water. May Allah bring those times Inshallah as soon as possible. May we also have the kismet to see it and live through it Inshallah.

Wa Minallah at-tawfeeq. Al-Fatiha.
