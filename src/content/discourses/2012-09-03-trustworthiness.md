As-Salamu Alaikum. Tariqatuna sohbah wa l-khayri fil jamia' Our way is through association & blessings are in the gathering insha Allah. May there always be blessed gatherings.

Today's sohbat is insha Allah about Amanah (trust). The nickname of the Prophet (sas) before the time of Islam was Muhammad Al-Amin (sas) which means "the trustworthy". People could trust in him for everything, entrust him with everything. It was like this also in the time of the Companions (ra). It is said that after the Companions amanah/trust will become less. Getting less & less, it reached today. It is very rare now to find a trustworthy person. And this is a result of the lack of iman/belief.

As people become more distant from the Prophet (sas), their iman is getting less & less. Dunya makes people forget iman, forget the good things. And there is also this matter of amanah/trust. They say "never betray a trust". But now no one can trust anyone because there is no trust/amanah anymore. They prepare vouchers, they write checks. They make paper documents, contracts but still because there is no amanah they do all these in vain. There are many lawsuits & 90% of these lawsuits are already because of this, lack of trust, because of this khiyanah-betrayal of trust. And it will be even worse in akhirah.

It is a very high station for man- to be trustworthy. Now no one is able to trust another by looking at his outer appearance &this is because man is suffering the punishment of what they did with their own hands. What to say, you become like who you imitate. Therefore this matter of trust is very important. Mashayikh, Prophets, Sahabas were all trustworthy, reliable people. May we also resemble them insha Allah. Try so that you become like them. Khiyanah, stabbing in the back, to deny something which was trusted to you- all of this is khiyanah, not amanah. Be very careful about these. Don't follow your ego. Discipline your ego that people know you as a trustworthy man because such people are very rare.

Today we speak about trustworthy. Prophet (sas), before Islam even, they were calling him "Muhammad al-Amin" meaning "Muhammad the trustworthy" (sas) who you can trust him. You can give everything & he give back everything for you or what he said you can believe on what he said. This attribute was, in old times more. Especially in Islam time, Prophet (sas) & his Companion. It was normal for them to be trustworthy. But Prophet (sas) said "after me, it will be taken from people". And it was like this. How much you coming, how many years we are more far from Prophet (sas) people become more untrustworthy. They become, you cannot trust. Very rare people, you can trust for them. And this is coming from less iman, less belief. How people are going for more for business, for looking, playing, enjoying themselves they forget to be believer. And how many they are coming not believer, become more untrustworthy. You cannot trust. And nowadays nobody trust for each other. Even they make when they make trade or they can give check, they can give paper, they can give contract but still people can cheat, cheat for this. So it is from Allah punishment for people, between themselves if you are not trustworthy, making bad for themselves also. This also punishment from Allah.

It is not good attribute this- to be not trustworthy. Trustworthy is the attribute of Prophet (sas) & for his Companions, for awliya of Allah-all they are trustworthy. Now you cannot trust for people even from how they wear, how they are looking you cannot trust for them. This is very bad attribute. So what we will do? You must train first your ego to be trustworthy really. Because many people from their ego many of them if you give them something even they deny. They say "No, you didn't give me this" or not giving back.

But old time, Sahaba/Companion they even when they, if they die in war or far away, they leave tell their children: This people will come,they will take this-what they have, you must give them" & it was like this. And people were trusting for this people. Even they know, if they die they will take their wealth. And now it is impossible to be like this. But we must be trustworthy. It is the best attribute from Prophet (sas). Because he is trustworthy, we trust him for everything. This is the meaning. All our religion trusting him. So if you like him (sas) you must try to be trustworthy also. It is really not difficult but people make it difficult. If you be trustworthy, many people they laughing for you, yes. So don't listen to people, to your ego. Only do what is right.

Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, Al Fatiha.
