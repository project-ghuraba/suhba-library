Nothing is worse than people envying each other because of something worthless. People envy eachother for this world. Actually this world isn't as valuable as the wing of a mosquito in the presence of AllāhNone. "If it was, I wouldn't even give drinking water to the unbelievers. If this world's worth was like the wing of a mosquito in My presence" says AllāhNone.

People envy each other for this worthless world. This is the reason of envy. How do we get rid of envy? By knowing the world's value. When you know the world is worthless, what is the value of this world? Less valuable than the wing of a mosquito. Without knowing that this world is worthless you can't get rid of envy.

If you value the world you envy what is given to people. When the world is worthless for you, you can't have envy. You can't get rid of envy if the world is valuable for you. You can't have real faith if the world isn't worthless for you. The whole world should be worthless for you. Then you can get rid of the harm of envy.

Whatever other people are given you should say; "For me whole world is worthless, so what is given to others is like rubbish, For me all is worthless." AllāhNone satisfies the eyes and the heart of the believer. You can't get rid of envy if your eyes and heart are full of desire of this world. You drown in darkness of envy. You live with the worst illness. You can't get the taste of life. You burn in darkness of envy in ākhirah.

Nothing can expel envy from mankind. First you have to take out the love of this world from your eyes and heart. There is also the way of praying. We are obliged to pray. "O Lord save me from the harm of envy. Save the person, whom I envy from the harm of my envy." You have to continue this prayer. Instead of sleeping at nights, before fajr, at least before dawn if you can wake up 15 or 30 minutes before and pray two raka‘āt, and then make sajdah, during prostration say "Oh Lord save me from being envy, this is the worst characteristics, if you pray during prostration, "Oh Lord save the people, whom I envy, from the harm of my envy".

If you pray like this for forty days that envy in your heart starts melting. Once it starts melting AllāhNone deals with the rest. Even if you can't get rid of the harm of envy completely at least until your last breath you can get rid of it and leave this world with the presence of the ProphetNone. If you aren't completely clean from envy you can't see the ProphetNone in your last breath in the presence of himNone you can't leave this world without gaining the faith that heNone taught hisNone nation. You will sure see the ProphetNone.

Once your envy melts and finishes, in shā’a Llāh, you'll see the ProphetNone in last breath you'll leave the world seeing himNone. That's why you have to keep this prayer. If you can continue, that would be very good. If you continue for 40 nights you can get that rank. Even if you can't completely get rid of that envy heNone cleans that in your last breath. You'll leave this world under hisNone gaze. You'll find perfect faith then.

The honourable companions asked about these spiritual sicknesses then they learned the methods of keeping the faith. Many people are addicted to this envy, that we are informed about. Some people say "I don't have it". But it is hidden somewhere. When time comes, it appears. That's why if you don't try to get rid of it you will suffer. If you want to be prophet'sNone neighbour, if you want to be closer to AllāhNone, if you want to be in the assembly of the saints fight against envy.

Now the holy three months started. You know which bad characteristics you have. At least within these three months, fight against three of them, get rid of them. When you get rid of three of them that is real ‘Īd, real salvation, you'll reach ranks of real faith. This is an opportunity. Now you know your bad characters, that you don't like. Remember that. Focus on that. If you get rid of one in one month, if not, if you can get rid of one in three months that's a huge honor. Especially be careful about this envy issue.

As long as you can keep going AllāhNone helps you beyond that. HeNone helps you from the start. On one condition, your intention should be good and you should try to fight. May AllāhNone protect us from the harm of that envy. May AllāhNone make us from HisNone clean servants.

Biḥurmati l-Ḥabīb, biḥurmati l-Fātiḥah.
