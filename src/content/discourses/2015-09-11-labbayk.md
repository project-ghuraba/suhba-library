Auzu Billahi Minashaytanir Rajeem. Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem,

Wassalatu Wassalamu ala Rasulina Muhammadin Sayyidul Awwalin wal Akhirin,

Madad Ya Rasulallah, Madad Ya As’habi Rasulillah, Madad Ya Mashayikhina,

Sheykh Abdullah Daghestani, Sheykh Nazim al-Haqqani. Dastur.

It says in the Koran that Allah Azza wa Jalla said to Ibrahim (Alayhis Salam), “Call the azan. They will come from all over the world once you build the Kaba.” It is time for Hajj and Mashallah Hajis are going from all over. Our brothers and sisters (ikhwan) also came all the way from Argentina. Today, in a little while, they are going on the Hajj trip, Mashallah. May Allah make it blessed (mubarak). May their Hajj be accepted Inshallah.

Those who have the ability should go on Hajj. Of course it is not easy now: you register for Hajj and go when the lottery hits you. However, sometimes people do not even do that. Even though Hajj is one of the five pillars of Islam. They take it very lightly. Even though those who have money and are able need to go on Hajj. They need to go on Hajj to complete their religion and to complete Allah’s orders.

Inshallah their way opens and everybody goes. Millions of people are waiting in line. May it be granted for them too Inshallah. Of course, for the turn to come to all amongst so many people, many people will pass away. But as long as the intention is made, a person will have been recorded. This is in the Hadith Sharif. If they made the intention and registered, then they are written as if they have started the trip. They are not cut from that virtue. The responsibility has fallen from them. May Allah grant it to us all. Now, let us make Talbiya all together for these brothers of ours.

Labbayk Allahumma labbayk. Labbayka la sharika laka labbayk. Innal hamda, wanni’mata, lakawal mulk. La sharika lak.

May those who are against Islam be lost. May Allah correct them all. May we all be under safety and security. May we live in abundance and plenty, and not be in need of anybody.

Al-Fatiha.
