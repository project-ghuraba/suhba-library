As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh. A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Annamā l-ḥayātu d-dunyā la‘ibun wa-lahwun wa-zīnatun wa-tafākhurun baynakum wa-takāthurun fī l-amwāli wa-l-awlād" (57:20). Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says the life of dunyā is play and amusement, multiplying, and boasting. “I am better than you. You are lower than me.” “Put on airs,” they say in Turkish. And most people know dunyā as entertainment and amusement. On top of that, they think they are something, and superior to others. This is not an acceptable attribute because AllāhNone knows. AllāhNone is All-Seeing, AllāhNone is All-Knowing.

Whatever you have done, whatever you are doing, nothing is hidden from HimNone. That’s why you don’t gain anything from bragging and thinking you are something. Mankind must know his limits because when he exceeds his limits, he harms himself. He stays under his own burden and is crushed eventually because everything is in the Hands of AllāhNone, AllāhNone will show everything to people at the Time of Reckoning. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla will bring everyone to account. Therefore, there are many ignorant people in dunyā. Don’t be like them. Don’t act like them. Be from the beloved people of AllāhNone.

The beloved people of AllāhNone know that Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is with them every second and all the time. AllāhNone will give them rewards for their deeds. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla will give them. It is of no benefit for the people to admire, love, or value you. What is beneficial is value from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. It is AllāhNone who gives you value. It is HeNone who gives it. If you do as HeNone wants, you will have value. Value in the sight of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is what is important. Value in dunyā is nothing.

Do not trust anyone. They value you and love you one day – they like you one day and the next day they humiliate you, insult and curse you. Theirs is not value. Value is if Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla loves you, your value will exist forever in the sight of AllāhNone. You become as HeNone wants. May AllāhNone make us all do as HeNone wants, not as people want, not as our egos want. May HeNone protect us from their liking us. What most people like is not the good thing anyway. May AllāhNone protect us. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Annamā l-ḥayātu d-dunyā la‘ibun wa-lahwun wa-zīnatun wa-tafākhurun baynakum wa-takāthurun fī l-amwāli wa-l-awlād" (57:20). Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying this āyah from Qur’ān: "This life like playing, enjoying and be try to be more higher than others, and to be collect money, collect thing for dunyā." This is most of people after this. But, they said, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla [says], "This is like green wheat coming, like this. So people they are happy. After – dry, it will be dry and it will be thrown by wind."

So no any value for this. What people they are thinking it is valuable, it is not valuable in divine presence of AllāhNone. This is not good. The good thing, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla is saying, the good deed which it is not going with wind. Going with wind, dust and straw and this dry wheat, it will go like whole life you are thinking you are doing something, but this is nothing, no benefit at all for you. The benefit thing – love of AllāhNone. If AllāhNone respect you, AllāhNone love you, you are valuable one. But not people – they are human being. They said, "This man – the people, they love him." For what they love him? For dunyā. This is not, no value for this. Because, human being's love, it is changing quickly, not stable at all. Don't think trust to this people and you make something. You think you are doing good because this people they're happy with you. No. They are happy one minute, second day they are against you, they're shouting on you, they're swearing on you, they are spitting on you.

This is, if you are depend on love of people, on human being, you will be like this. But if you depend on love of AllāhNone and prophets and good people, holy people, you will be succeed. You will be have valuable, value in your life. Your life it will be valuable. Without this – nothing. It is just like straw – straw or like dry thing. It going with wind. Small wind it will be take all of this. And you thought, "Whole life I was doing this. How it is going like this?" Because no value. Other valuable thing, it will be stay for you.

AllāhNone give us from HisNone, this good manner and good thinking about HimNone. And we must keep our promise for AllāhNone, what to do as HeNone wants and you will be valuable. But, if you forget, you will be also forgotten. and no value for you. AllāhNone help us, in shā’a Llāh, to do this, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
