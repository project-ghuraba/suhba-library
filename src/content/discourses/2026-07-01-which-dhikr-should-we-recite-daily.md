As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
وَلِلَّهِ ٱلْأَسْمَآءُ ٱلْحُسْنَىٰ فَٱدْعُوهُ بِهَا
<br>“Wa li-Llāhi l-asmā'u l-ḥusnā fa-dʿūhu bihā”<br>
“And to Allah belong the Most Beautiful Names, so call on Him by them.” (Qurʼān 7:180). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla has ninety-nine names. Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam says, “Recite them.” He also said, “Whoever memorizes or recites them will enter Paradise.” Of course, Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla has many names, but ninety-nine names have been revealed to the Ummah of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam. It’s very good if we recited them every day. Thus, we will attain the good tidings of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam for Paradise, inshā’Allāh. “You will enter Paradise,” says our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam.

Of course, people sometimes wonder, “Which name should we recite?” In ṭarīqah, there is the duty of reciting “Ya Wadūd” three hundred times daily. Reciting this is good. It increases love among people. Evil will not come from people. The name “Wadūd” has meanings like familiarity and love. Of course, it has many meanings; the names of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla have infinite meanings. Shukr to Allah, Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim recommended this to us. Those in ṭarīqah should recite it. 

Of course, there are other names as well. For those who ask, “Which one should we recite?” you should recite and make dhikr of all the ninety-nine names once a day. Barakah come from the blessed and beautiful names of Allah, and all kinds of goodness reach people. A person who is with Allah, all kinds of goodness will manifest upon him, he attains goodness, and stays away from evil.

May Allah keep His dhikr constant in our hearts, inshā’Allāh. May Allah grant it to those who do not recite it. Because today, people know neither religion, nor īmān, nor Prophet, nor Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla. They live empty lives. Besides themselves, they drag others into this emptiness by saying, “There is nothing.” Yet they are unaware of the immense benefits a person receives from merely mentioning one of Allah’s Names. Light fills their hearts and faces, their lives become beautiful and of barakah. 

May Allah not deprive us of this blessing, and may He grant it to those who do not yet recite it, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allahi t-Tawfīq.

Al-Fātiḥa.
