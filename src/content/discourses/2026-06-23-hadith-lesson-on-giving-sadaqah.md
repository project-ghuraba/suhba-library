As-Salāmu ‘Alaykum wa RaḥmatuLlāhi wa Barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu BiLlāhi Minash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. BismiLlāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm.

Wa ṣ-Salātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Akhirīn.

Madad yā RasūlAllāh, Madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi RasūliLlāh, Madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

Dastūr Mawlana Sheikh Abdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistāni, Sheikh Muḥammad

Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad.

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Doing good closes the doors to evil. Just as unhappy in this world will give charity to become happy. They will take good care of their parents. They will not sever ties with their relatives. This will transform their unhappiness into happiness, prolong their life, and protect them from a bad death, says our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam.

2- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says: Our Holy Prophet is saying “Charity given in the morning eliminate disasters (afflictions).” So it prevents whatever happens to a person from disability or so. That’s why, we often tell people to give sadaqah; which is Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim’s will and advice to people. “We just give, we give a lot.” When do you give? “I give once a week.” You must give every day. Put a box aside and put in it every morning. Put three, put five, put ten in it; as much as you can. That charity is the charity of the day. You collect them all later and give them away. As we said, give it once a week, once a month, whenever you want. But don’t leave that daily sadaqah. Sadaqah protects you and guards you from all kinds of evil.

3- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet is saying “Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla says, “Spend (O man), and I shall spend on you.” If you don’t give, He won’t give you.

4- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet is saying “The handfuls of dates given to the poor are the dowries of the houris.” Of course, in old times, there was not as much abundance as there is now. If you were going to throw a loaf of bread like that; nothing would be found like that. The one who found two or three dates for the day was happy and thanked Allāh. Therefore, even if you give a few dates as sadaqah, they will be the dowry of the houris in heaven.

5- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet says, “Everybody will be shaded by his Sadaqah (on the Day of Judgment) until it has been judged between the people.” So, there will be no shadow on the Day of Judgment. However, the one who gives sadaqah stays under the shadow until his turn comes.

6- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “There are many beautiful-eyed ladies whose dowry is only a handful of wheat and the same in amount of dates.” So even if you pay a million in the world, you cannot find it; a handful of wheat and a handful of dates are their dowers.

7- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Let one of you protect his face from the Fire, even if with part of a date.” So, charity saves you both here and hereafter; it saves you from Hell, and saves you from affliction in this world.

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “It is better for a man to give a dirham as sadaqa during his lifetime than to give a hundred at the time of his death.” So, if he gives just when he dies or after he dies a hundred dirhams of this and that; one dirham given before his death is better than a hundred after. That’s why, when a person’s healthy - late Hajji Yashar used to say, “Whatever you give with your hand, goes with you.” So this is it. Give while you are healthy.

9- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says: ﺮ  ئﺪﺘﺒا ﺮﺟأ ﻞ ﺮﺟﻷا  ﻢﳍ نﺎﻜﻟ ،ﺔﺋﺎﻣ يﺪﻳ  ﺔﻗﺪا ت 

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Even if a charity passes through the hands of a hundred people, each one of them will have the same reward as the reward of the first without any deficiency.” So, someone gave sadaqah, and he gave it to the other person, who then passed it on to 100 people. All those people will receive that reward. When there is isqāt, each person gets the reward. One gives it to another saying, “I took it, I accepted it, I gave it away, I donated it.” They will attain rewards and the one who passed away will be free of the sin of not doing that deed (worship).

11- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

ٌ  ً ُ ٌ €

It never decreases with charity. “No one forgives another except that Allāh increases his honor.” So one was treated unfairly. There are many people who are being treated unfairly at this time. They are cursing them, making bad du’ā’s. This is not necessary. Allāh gives him glory. It is more honorable for him to be better. To receive the honor that Allah has given him is better than to curse people. “And no one humbles himself for the sake of Allāh except that Allāh raises his status.” The one who humbles himself for the sake of Allāh, that person’s rank rises in the sight of Allāh and among people. Humility means that people should be humble towards Muslims, that is, not be arrogant.

12- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

ً ْ َ َ ْ َ ْ َ ْ ﱠ ُ َ َ ﱠ ً َ ٌ ُ َ ُ ْ ُ

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “A man does not give anything in charity except that he frees it from the jaws of seventy devils.” So, when one wants to give sadaqah, shayṭān clamps its jaw and clings to this person from all sides in order to prevent him. So when you give sadaqah, just like a dog biting something, you are opening the mouth of the shayṭān and giving sadaqah.

13- Qala RasuluLlah salla Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam

The Messenger of Allāh (peace be upon him) says:

Our Holy Prophet is saying, “Giving sadaqah to the poor prevents evil death.” If one gives charity to the poor - may Allah protect us, bad deaths are happening a lot everywhere, may Allah protect us all.

The Messenger of Allāh says the truth in what he said or as he said.
