As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Shukr to AllāhNone, our ṭarīqah, Naqshbandī ṭarīqah, is the way that follows the way of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. It shows HisNone way and guidance. Shukr to AllāhNone, whoever is meant to be on this way reaches happiness. His life becomes better. Both in his worldly life and the life in ākhirah becomes good and blessed. They changed in a way that is pleasing to AllāhNone. All ṭarīqahs are ways that take you to AllāhNone. They are the ways that lead to the same path. And the Naqshbandiyyah is much powerful, in shā’a Llāh. This way, with the permission of AllāhNone, is the way that leads to all kinds of goodness. It comes from our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam.

It is the way of all Ahlu l-Bayt, ṣaḥābah, awliyā’ and ‘ālims. This way is the way of light for humanity. It is the way that shows the right thing. Shukr to AllāhNone, it is the unchanging way, without any changes, with the permission of AllāhNone. The Naqshbandī ṭarīqah is the path that shows the way until qiyāmah. Shukr to AllāhNone, those who join this way are lucky people. There are many people out there who are unaware of Islām. There are many people who know it but who sometimes don't carry out the orders and wishes of AllāhNone. We cannot say anything to them. We must make shukr for our own condition. Our condition is the one to be grateful for, because only some people are chosen to be part of such gatherings. And these gatherings are the assemblies that please Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla and the angels are longing for.

We have gathered all together for the pleasure of AllāhNone on this day of Ramaḍān. The virtue, barakah, and mercy of AllāhNone are showering on us. This is the greatest happiness. It is the greatest profit. It is the greatest joy. However, there are not many such gatherings. Compared to gatherings disliked by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, they are very few. Many people want to create various troubles. Because Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla looks at HisNone gatherings with pleasure, these are the best gatherings, the most beneficial, and the most profitable gatherings, shukr to AllāhNone. May the barakah of our Mashāyikh be upon us. The eyes of ṣaḥābah, Ahlu l-Bayt, our Holy ProphetNone and angels are on us. Their barakah is on us. Therefore, we thank AllāhNone. May these favours be everlasting.

As we said, people sometimes say that others are not fasting. No one should listen to them. We must be thankful for our own condition thousands and millions of times because AllāhNone gave it to us and HeNone didn't give it to them. They know. AllāhNone knows. Our job is ourselves, our egos, to train our egos, in shā’a Llāh. May AllāhNone not leave us to obey our egos. May we put our egos under our feet. May we keep on this way by putting our egos under our feet, in shā’a Llāh. May we not give a single chance to our egos, in shā’a Llāh.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh we are happy with what AllāhNone gave us from this ṭarīqah, Naqshbandiyyah ‘Āliyyah, which it is most distinguished between other way, ṭuruq – the most highest, in shā’a Llāh. There is many way, ṭarīqah in Muslim. All this taking you to Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam and showing you good way. One of them Naqshbandī, our ṭarīqah, which it is praised between [among] whole ṭarīqah. It is not make you, too difficult for you, most easiest but it is most highest, in shā’a Llāh. If you continue with your adab and good behave and to train yourself, your ego, to not be following her, to follow you, it will be the most happiness, the most good way for anybody. Who it is open for anybody but people they don't know, many of them they don't know about it. Many of them they are thinking not good for this. But Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, who are chosen to be in this ṭarīqah, they are most lucky people because there is not thousand, millions of people they are not following this. Very few people they are following and you are one of them.

So, we must be thankful for Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla to keep us in this way, way of holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, holy ṣaḥābah, holy Ahlu l-Bayt, holy ‘ulamā', mashāyikh, all good people coming through Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, not changing from one thousand four hundred and fifty year maybe. Since that, before also of course. But it is same, showing the right way of Islām, right way of taṣawwuf, right way of way of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. So, they are coming, thousands, millions of some people, they like to change. But, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, this way not changing – straight, no any changing until qiyāmah, in shā’a Llāh. For this we are thanking AllāhNone for what HeNone gave us and we are looking for ourself. Of course, some people saying, ''Nobody fasting, nobody doing this, doing any good thing, no praying, nothing.'' We are not care about them. We are care about ourself, to control ourself, to know what to do, what to say, to be safe from this bad thing, bad end.

We are, first thing, thanking for AllāhNone. HeNone gave us this and for other. AllāhNone give him hidāyah as HeNone like. But for ourself we are asking, thanking for HimNone and to keep us in this good way, not changing us until our last breath, in shā’a Llāh. AllāhNone make it happy, more happy here and hereafter for all of us, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
