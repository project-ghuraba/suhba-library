That is the second of holy month's Jum‘ah, second Jum‘ah. So quickly running, and that is holiest month during all year. It is holiest because we have been created to be for Allāh Almighty – servants. And we have been honoured to be offered by divenely service. Not nothing else. Not for nothing else. We have been created to be HisNone servants and to keep HisNone divinely service daily, whole our lives.

But it is going to be difficult for majority of mankind to understand and to keep that honour, that most honourable service. It is service, divinely service of Allāh Almighty. You can't do, you can't keep it through other months as you are keeping through this holy month. Now whole day, we are for divinely service, we are fasting. And whole night also, except we are resting, all night also we are for divinely service, we are praying. So that mankind getting much more honour through this holy month. And it is most valuable month among twelve months for Allāh Almighty. Keep it! And ask more favours and more blessings from Allāh Almighty.

Ten days it is going to be blessings from AllāhNone to HisNone servants. And ten days from holy Ramaḍān going to be for HisNone forgiveness to HisNone Servants and giving them that perfection. And the last ten days of holy Ramaḍān going to be granted from Allāh Almighty from HisNone endless mercy oceans that we should reach to HisNone pleasure, HisNone pleasement. And who is going to reach pleasement of Allāh Almighty, he has a right to show enjoyment and happiness through ‘Īd Mubārak. Keep your Lord'sNone orders. The Lord of Heavens, Allāh Almighty, the Lord of whole creation, keep HisNone pleasure. Try to make HimNone pleased with you.

If you are reaching to make Allāh Almighty pleased with you, every doors from blessings going to be open for you here and hereafter. If Allāh Almighty is not pleased with HisNone servant, everything blocked for him; no one making him to stand up – must go down. And just we reached from holy Ramaḍān – ten days, so quickly, as we are looking from Allāh Almighty, from HisNone endless blessings to change everything. Till second Ramaḍān or coming Ramaḍān reaching, we are asking a change, holy changing from Allāh Almighty. HeNone only may change everything from bad to good, from darkness to lights. And we are in need lights, because whole people getting in darkness and no peace, no happiness, and servants suffering.

May AllāhNone bring HisNone beloved servants to change everything, and to destroy the heritage of satan, sultanate of satan. please look after yourselves don't be from supporters of satanic heritage. Even if one smoking, you may give a support to satanic heritage. Don't smoke, and don't do bad things, because it is supporting for devil and evil. And you should be responsible for that action in divinely presence.

May AllāhNone forgive and bless you and your generations, your descendants. And give from HisNone endless mercy oceans to your ancestors.
