Aū‘dhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-Shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Waṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ‘alā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-Awwalīna wa l-Ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā,

dastūr yā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-F’āiz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-

A’ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “…Atuhlikunā bi-mā fa‘ala s-sufahā’ minnā?…” Qur’ān 7:155). What low, misbehaving people do has nothing to do with us. They are not from us. We are not able to interfere with them. They themselves are responsible for what they do, and will be treated separately. We shall be praying, even though the number of bad people increases.

The worst thing about evil is that evil people want to make things and everyone else the way they like. These are Satan’s men. They want all to belong to Satan. They do not desire good, and instead their desire is that all be evil. What they wish would be an oppression. They do not see what has come upon us now. They do not see it! They do not become wiser. A Muslim believes in Allah and repents. Most people did repent. These, however, possess the stubbornness of a disbeliever. This type of stubbornness is like Satan’s. He said he would not prostrate to Adam (AS). These are the same. No matter how much Allah shows them the truth, they do not want to see it. They try to do what they believe is right. They try to have people on their path. What they do is not commanding the good and forbidding the evil, rather the opposite, they command evil and forbid people do good. What they plant is foul, dirty, filthy. No benefit comes from it, only harm, even to themselves. There is also harm to those they cause to be put off of the path. However many people one causes to be out of the path [of Allah], by that much is one committing sin, and is burnt in hell longer.

May Allah grant protection. May Allah have us amongst those He protects, in this and also in other Muslim countries. They are bothersome. Shaytan says that he will take them all to hell, and he does not want to let anyone enter paradise. So may Allah have all of Shaytan’s doings come to nought. May Allah protect us. May Allah give strength to our faith. Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-F ti ah
