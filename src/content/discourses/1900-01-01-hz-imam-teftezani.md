Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Satan is the sheikh of one who has no sheikh. Satan can't approach him who has a sheikh. If it does, on account of the sheikh's spirituality reaching one, it runs away, it is burned. Hz. Allame-i Taftazani, this 'allame' is a title for a scholar who teaches other scholars what the belief of Islam is, all that Muslims believe, beliefs as taught by our Prophet. A great scholar, he wrote books on this. He was living in the times of Hz. Ibrahim Gulsheni.

Hz. Ibrahim Gulsheni, the imam of the Gulsheni Tariqat. It is one among 41 tariqats, he was its imam. He was inviting him to the tariqat but Hz. Taftazani was saying, "Our knowledge is enough for us, we are not in need of finding a sheikh, following him and entering tariqat." He was not interested, the Sheikh accepted this but when that one was lying on his deathbed, getting ready to face Akhirat, satan came in front of Hz. Allame-i Taftazani, in order to steal his faith. We take refuge in Allah! He was having such difficulty.

Hz. Ibrahim Gulsheni was sitting with his mureeds in an assembly, "O our brothers, Hz. Allame-i Taftazani is in a very difficult passage, let's go and visit him. He is on a bridge getting ready to leave this world," he said. "He is in struggle with satan now, let's go and visit him," he said. Immediately he stood up, leaving the assembly, his mureeds following him. Not with lady mureeds following him, like mine.

He walked with all his mureeds. He came to Hz. Allame-i Taftazani's door. He stood waiting. While at the door the voice of Hz. Taftazani was heard. It was heard to say, "Al hamdu Lillah". He went close to Hz. Taftazani, he saluted him and as he was inquiring after his health, Taftazani took him by the hand: "O holy one, we didn't appreciate your worth while in this world. We were negligent regarding this matter of tariqat. We thought that we could save ourself with our knowledge. (We thought) there is no need for tariqat. We felt no need.

Today, had you not reached us, in this moment, I was afraid satan would have stolen my faith," he said. "Before you approached, it came here in front of me, wanting me to say 'there is no Allah'. While I was stating the proof, the evidence for the existence of Allah, which it kept rejecting, it began asserting other things to trick me. Responding to it, I finally managed to invalidate its claims, silencing it. Another came, with yet another query, another question, saying: 'Say, Isa is the son of Allah.' As I was refuting that, it started compelling me to say that Allah is a third of three (the trinity) " because, it has much knowledge. {Maulana digresses - tell those women satan has zahir (apparent/material) knowledge, which could be learned from it, tell them to find satan to teach you its apparent knowledge!}

Continually, question after question as it was asking him, he was suffering on his deathbed close to submitting his soul, close to passing away, close to surrender. "Finally, I was near surrendering to what it was saying when your light appeared at the door." The light of sainthood (wilayat), awliya have the light of wilayat. "The moment it saw that light, baffled where it should run, leaving me, it fled. Now I surrender to you." "So, I do accept you". Then he pointed out that Azrail (as) took his soul.

Now then, the light of wilayat surely is given to Allah's walis (saints). Without that light a wali can't show the way. If a car has no lamp, if all its parts are complete, but no lamps, it cannot be driven. How can it move? A car moves. But it has no lamp! Could it be driven? Every human being has a light, but like a candle. If you don't light it, it is an unlit candle. When you light it, it gets lit. Everyone has a light. It is hidden. A candle has a light inside but it is hidden. Isn't it? If you light it with a match the light gets to be seen. Now Anbiya, the prophets came with light. Had they come without light, prophets couldn't open peoples' hearts, they couldn't have lit the hearts. They couldn't have given light. Faith is light. When they give light to the heart of a believer, then that one becomes illuminated.

That is why Prophets have light. And coming from prophets, all awliya, inheritors of the prophets, have light also. When you draw near, you get illuminated. If not, could a thousand candles light each other? How could they? It is hidden, but all have a light inside waiting for a match. As you light one, as the glowing candle comes, gives its light, one by one, like this, all get lit up. That is why prophets were sent - for this purpose. Awliya who came after them, are all sent for this purpose. If unable to find awliya, you have a candle but no light. Your light remains hidden. Many of those, though their lights are still hidden, become 'sheikh' by name. In any case, they didn't reach the one who could light their candle. That is why they are like you, like me.

Whoever has the light, Janabu l-Haqq, astaizu Billah: "Faltamisu Nura" (57:13) Janab Allah says: seek the Light. Search for the Light. That Light, is not a light alone by itself. Light doesn't exist without a means. It is inside a candle. But it needs something to light it; the light coming from the Heavens can light it, that Light belongs to the Anbiya and Awliya. If you go after the awliya, the awliya can awaken the hidden light inside you. He can awaken the hidden light inside him/her. He can awaken the hidden light inside everyone.

When I was a child, I would see, there were Armenians in our street, some came from Kayseri, some came from Adana. So many Armenians came from those places. They had holy days at times, according to their traditions or they had wedding ceremonies. When coming out of that church, all would pass by, with glowing candles lighted in the church in their hands. I used to watch them as they were passing by. If a candle of any one of them went out, he/she would hurry to the glowing candles of others, reaching the light of a glowing candle, would light his/her candle again and keep walking. Like this, Janab Allah is saying to people to seek the Light on Earth. Look for the possessors of Light who will awaken the hidden light inside you. (G: We have found that Light). Unless you find it, you are lightless.

You have light but hidden. It is not awakened. It must be awakened. This inheritance is coming since the Prophet, through the men of the Golden Chain, through all those awliya, then by Grandsheikh it was given to us. Were it not given, what would I give to all these people? That man came by plane from a distance of fifty-six hours. Why did he come? Did I send him money? Leave that Filiz, in those countries we went to, ten thousands, ten thousands - had those policemen not been around they would have squashed me. The amount of crowd, the onslaught. They hadn't seen me (before). They hadn't seen me physically but when their spirituality sees me they are attracted to us.

Yahu, how could it be? Where did all these people see me? They hadn't, but they came. Young people like you, like him, of her age, many people. Ten thousands. Subhanallah. Without that power - if not, their hearts opened by the power of attraction (jadhba). How could all those people have come? Some men (who reject sheikhs) are mindless! Yes! Everyone has a lamp, a candle which is hidden. If you light it, its glow appears. There is a need for the one who can light it. He is looking, no matches for lighting. If there is no match for lighting, it wouldn't matter if there were a thousand candles.

A glowing candle can light a hundred thousand candles without itself dwindling. Does a glowing candle dwindle? Does its power diminish when it has lit a thousand candles? No, it remains the same. Allah. Holy ones, on this line. Our oath in the presence of Janabu l-Haqq, Hazrat Jalla wa Ala, since this service was assigned to us, as we are standing in the line of this chain, Sheikh Effendi ordered me to stand at its door. We are obliged to stand. Not an obligation but his wish is our command. Not an obligation, our heart is given to him/this service.

So whenever someone attacks, I strike back severely. They disturb/annoy me. (G: InshaAllah will we be together in Akhirat, Sheikh Baba? - They asked!) A Jew asked a rabbi, "Will Jews go to Paradise?" "No doubt they will," he answered. Now this one is asking whether we are going to be together? A Jew asked a Rabbi, will Jews go to Paradise? "No doubt they will". Will Muslims go to Paradise? he asked. "So-so", he answered. Will Armenians go? He asked. What is this, do you think is it a lunapark/fair ground? He didn't accept them. He didn't accept Armenians at all. He is asking whether it was a fair ground! What would Armenians do there?! No need! Wa min Allahu tawfeeq.
