A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. For the blessed face of our Holy Prophet, “Wayustasqá l-ghamāmu biwajhihi l-karīm.” For the honour of his face, Allāh sends rain. It is one of his miracles. When he was still a child, his uncle took him, and there was a drought, like the days we live in now. May Allāh protect us now. There is drought all over the world. It does not rain. And when it does, it ruins people. Therefore, the uncle of our Holy Prophet took him and made du‘ā’, “For the sake of this child, O Allāh, send us rain.” At that time abundant rain fell. People and animals were saved. That is a famous event. Therefore, for the benefit of people, it is sunnah to ask for any goodness for the honour of his face. Those who believe in this will surely receive that benefit. It brings all kinds of healing, barakah, and good children. Ask through the honour of our Holy Prophet. Because if we ask for ourselves, Allāh never rejects du‘ā’, though He may delay it. For the honour of our Holy Prophet, however, He does not delay it for people. May Allāh help us. People nowadays live in all kinds of hardship. It is the Mawlid day and the Mawlid month of our Holy Prophet. Very few people remember it. No one remembers. And even if people do, some people appear and say, “This is not allowed; you are committing a sin,” and they try to forbid it as well. When they do so, people are deprived of thawāb. They are deprived of virtues. They are deprived of shafā‘ah. In shā’a Llāh, tonight we will have tasbīḥāt for rain. This is the Mawlid day, the Mawlid of our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. No one can deny what took place on his Mawlid. Even the harshest ones who are against our Holy Prophet cannot deny it. That night the thousand-year-old fire of the Zoroastrians was extinguished. The palace of Kisrá collapsed. Many events took place on that night. It was a sign to people that the night of Mawlid was blessed from the very moment of his birth. Mu’mins must know this. There are Mawlid books that describe what happened, how it happened, and what remained. They don’t let people read them, calling them bid‘ah, and they get angry with those who read. They do not wish to teach people how blessed is the birth of our Holy Prophet, how blessed is the day of his birth, and how blessed is the night of his birth. They say, “We are Muslims. This is not needed. It is bid‘ah, it is wrong. Our Holy Prophet did not do this.” Our Holy Prophet is himself sacred. Being with him each day was the most virtuous of days in the world. We did not live in those days. To honour his memory, whatever memory there is, we strive to do as much as we can. So tonight, with Allāh’s permission, we ask goodness through the honour of our Holy Prophet. We ask victory for Islām. May Ṣāḥib come, and teach their limits to those who do not know themselves. May he awaken Muslims. May he save humanity. That is what we ask, in shā’a Llāh. And of course, on this occasion, shukr to Allāh, the jamā‘ah is here, with mu’mins, murīds, and lovers. This place where we are was established in the time of Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim. Back then, too, the jamā‘ah was large. Now also, mā shā’a Llāh, it grows through his himmah and barakah. On this occasion, today, for our new masjid, there tonight, in shā’a Llāh, we will hold its opening, in shā’a Llāh. And we will make this tasbīḥāt. May Allāh send barakah, both outward and inward. May those blessings be upon us, in shā’a Llāh. May Allāh bless us. May there be abundant mercy and rain. May He drown us in His mercy. Yā Allāh. May Allāh give guidance to people, to those who have gone astray, those who are bewildered, those who are deceived. May Allāh give guidance to them all, in shā’a Llāh.

We are also in day of the Mawlid of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. And it is one of his attributes: “Wayustasqá l-ghamāmu biwajhihi l-karīm.” Allāh give us the rain with his blessed face, of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. One of his attribute this, since his childhood. His uncle was knowing about this. His grandfather also knowing about it. And once, there was very drought in, around Makkah and around this Ḥijāz. And their people, they was praying, asking for Allāh for rain, but nothing happen. So, his uncle, he know about Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. And he take him, and raising him, and asking from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla: “For sake of this blessed boy, give us rain.” And was immediately raining, raining, raining, until they are full of rain, full of water. Happy year it was. For this we are also, everything you can ask from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, for sake of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. You must ask goodness, happiness, health, wealth, strong īmān, good children, good generation, good life. This, everything, you may ask for sake of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Some people say, ‘No need. You are making shirk. You must ask Allāh saying: “Ud‘ūnī astajib lakum” (40:60). You pray for you and I will give you.’ Okay, you can pray. But you are not valuable in Divine Presence of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. When you put blessing of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, Allāh not reject this for you. For this, the whole people knowing, loving Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, they are winner. Other, they are cheated by some people. As anyone can make thing, quickly shayṭān send his people to prevent him: “Don’t do this, do that. It is: you are also man, and Prophet he was man. No need to ask for him. You can only ask for yourself.” This is, people cheated by shayṭān, not anything else. So Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla not happy with this people. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla happy with Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Because Qur’ān say: “Fa-ttabi‘ūnī yuḥbibkumu Llāh” (3:31). Follow me, Allāh will love you. He love you. If you are following yourself, Allāh not love you. For this, we are asking from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, all good thing. And, in shā’a Llāh, this night the night of blessed, very blessed night, which Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam honoured this earth with his presence and his birth. And this night it was very extraordinary night in whole world. Many miracle happen, many thing happen. And even this people who are not respect Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, they know about this. What it is fire of fire worshipper, thousand year never stopped. And that night, finish. The castle of Kisrá, the king of Iran was destroyed. Many thing happen in this, and this mentioned, and was everybody knowing about this in book of birth of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, which we calling, Mawlid Sharīf. This very nice book describing the Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam his life, from his childhood, before his birth until the end of his life. And it was around all Islāmic world, but when this people coming, they are prevent this. They said, “This is bid‘ah, this cannot be. You cannot follow this.” And special one, Mawlānā was, he is very happy with this, with Turkish one, Süleyman Çelebi. He was real describing... He was big awliyā’, and he describing very clear the birth of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, and Mi‘rāj, and other, all other thing. All this, was people listening and enjoying. Allāh send barakah, raḥmah for them They were happy. But now they, nobody looking for this. Because they afraid: “If you, we are doing wrong. This people they are ‘ulamā’, they know Qur’ān, they know ḥadīth. So, they are saying something. They must be, they are right.” But what the experience for people, for everybody? These people, the most liar people. The most liar people, lying for Allāh, and Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, and ‘ulamā’ and awliyā’. All what they say about them, they are lying. For believer, they are lying also. And Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam saying, mu’min cannot be liar. They can do anything, but he cannot be liar. For this, these people not respectful and not trustful people. Allāh keep us safe from them, in shā’a Llāh. And, in shā’a Llāh, with this occasion of this night, in shā’a Llāh, we make, Mawlānā was making tasbīḥ, seventy thousand time. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. “Wa-huwa lladhī yunazzilu l-ghaytha min ba‘di mā qanaṭū wa-yanshuru raḥmatahū wa-huwa l-waliyyu l-ḥamīd” (42:28). We collect stone, and, in shā’a Llāh, we read this after ‘Ishā’ tonight. And this we must put in water, and this for rain. Many times Mawlānā was doing this and it was raining after this. In shā’a Llāh, this, we are beginning from beginning of season to not be lazy. Last two years we didn’t do this. We was lazy little bit. So, in shā’a Llāh, today many people, and it will be easy for us to do this also. And, in shā’a Llāh, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are here also in dergah of Mawlānā, which it was original like what he is in life. And we keep this as it is, and we make one bigger now. Because people, jamā‘ah, murīd, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, with barakah of Mawlānā, he sending people for ṭarīqah, for to follow. They are coming more and more. We make this new one and not complete, but this, for occasion of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam for barakah, in shā’a Llāh, this night we pray there, in shā’a Llāh, we make tasbīḥ there, in shā’a Llāh. Allāh bless you. Allāh send us Sayyidunā l-Mahdī, ‘alayhi s-salām. We are waiting for him, in shā’a Llāh. Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
