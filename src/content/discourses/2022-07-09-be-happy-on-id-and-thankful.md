As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. "Fa-bi-dhālika fa-l-yafraḥū huwa khayrun mimmā yajma‘ūn" (10:58). AllāhNone tells us to be happy for what HeNone gives. What HeNone gives are things to be happy for. They are better than the goods of this world. What people collect are the goods of this world. Things given by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla are much better. If you are happy, be happy with these things, not for earning money and wealth, but be happy if you have īmān, says Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. That is better than everything. Things given by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla are things to be happy with.

So today we have this ‘Īd. To be happy and joyful on ‘Īd is a type of worship. It is to carry out Allāh'sNone order. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla loves people who make shukr and rejoice for what HeNone gives. If they don't make shukr and are not happy, AllāhNone won't be pleased. Because it is HisNone gift. When someone gives you a present but you don't look at him and push it away, he gets upset. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gave everything to us. And above all, HeNone gave these ‘Īds to us. These are beautiful things. These are things to be happy for, not anything else. In old times, people used to value holy days more. Because there was less idleness then. Better things were done during the holy days. They used to put on new clothes and food had more meat and sweets. The taste of ‘Īd was more noticeable because of the poverty back then. Even if it is not so noticeable now, people should be happy as it is something given by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla.

And when asked how you are, you should say, "Al-ḥamdu liLlāh. I am happy, shukr to AllāhNone." People are getting used to saying "I am unhappy". People have a habit of saying "I am not happy at all". They think it is cool to say this. Shukr to AllāhNone, we are eating and drinking. AllāhNone gave us provision. And if Muslims perform their worship, there is nothing better than that. They should make shukr to AllāhNone. This matter...

The Īmān of the people of the present time is not just at zero, it is below zero. In old times, even people with sins used to remember Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla on these occasions, and they used to pray to HimNone even if only once or twice a year. There is a famous story. Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim and Ḥājjah Anne used to tell it. People had asked someone who never prayed, "Do you ever pray?" He said, "I do." "When do you pray?" He said, "From ‘Īd to ‘Īd." He said this as if he prayed every day. They asked, "Do you drink alcohol?" He said, "From evening to evening," as if it was very rare. Even though it is a joke, it is a funny story, it means even in a drunken state, people used to come for prayers on ‘Īd. There is not such a thing now. As I said, people are at zero. We have gone below zero. It is the condition of the world. The Īmān of people in this world has gone below zero. Therefore, may Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla help us. People with īmān should be happy now for not going below zero. We are near zero, around zero. They should make shukr even for that.

May AllāhNone give strength to our īmān. May AllāhNone make ‘Īd a real feast for us, in shā’a Llāh. These feasts will turn into real feasts because of our thawāb in the presence of AllāhNone and our shukr, in shā’a Llāh. May AllāhNone also give guidance to people. May AllāhNone increase people's īmān, in shā’a Llāh.

Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm, "Fa-bi-dhālika fa-l-yafraḥū huwa khayrun mimmā yajma‘ūn" (10:58). This āyah from Qur’ān, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla saying, "For this, they must be happy. This is better from what they collect." What is this? "Qul bi-faḍli Llāhi wa-bi-raḥmatihī" (10:58). There for generous of AllāhNone and HisNone mercy, we must be happy. Human being must be happy. This is order from AllāhNone. For who they are believer, they must be happy because they have this mercy and AllāhNone give them this īmān and it is the most valuable thing. Better than if you collect whole world. If all this island, all this country, all this world for you, it is nothing compared [to] what AllāhNone give or will give. If you are not believer it is nothing. The better than this, to be mu’min and you don't have anything – it is better than this. This is Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HisNone order and this is very precious thing you get, you have, if you are believer and you are mu’min.

It is from order from AllāhNone to be happy. And we must be happy also because HeNone gave us this īmān and HeNone also gave us many thing to be happy what HeNone give. You must be thankful for HimNone. You must be happy for what HeNone give. Especially this, HeNone gave us this ‘Īd – feast of Ramaḍān and feast of this ‘Īdu l-Aḍḥá, ‘Īd. This gift from AllāhNone. You must be happy. This days, don't say, "I am not happy, I am miserable." Maybe people who live in tent, refugee, sometimes they're more happy than people they have everything but they don't say thank for AllāhNone. This is not good. Good thing, to be say when they said... Everything, it is YourNone favour. It is for everywhere."

So, HeNone will maybe not give you more. Because if anybody give you some present and you are not looking and you throw it like this, he will be never give you any more and will not be happy with you. But, if you precious and thank for him, he will be happy you like his present and you thank for him, he will be happy. AllāhNone, HeNone is most one we have to be happy with what HeNone gave us because HeNone gave everything. If HeNone not give, HeNone will, nothing can be with you. This is most people not understanding this and they are going away from AllāhNone. AllāhNone happy with people they are thankful for HimNone and remembering HimNone.

But this days, belief, they make, it was zero, now below zero, minus zero. Many there countries like this and, unfortunately, Islamic country also become, because they're taking, looking for other and they thought it is good thing to do this, to be against belief, against AllāhNone. Old time people they was, even they have sin – they're not praying, they drink – they was, when some occasion, holy blessed occasion, they was respect and they was doing. Because... One story or tale from Mawlānā and Ḥājjah Anne, they was saying this too much for like to make people laugh but it is very good for example for old people how was their belief. Even who they have sin, but they have belief. Not zero, they was below... up. Now, not zero, below, minus zero. One, they ask one man, he is not praying five times, who not even Jum‘ah not praying. They ask him, "Do you pray?" He says, "Every ‘Īd, every ‘Īd, every ‘Īd". As you said like, "every day, every day", but between ‘Īd and ‘Īd nearly ten months. And they ask him, "Are you drink?" But even they have, they put their head in sajdah once or twice a year, they have still belief.

But, this days, this also they don't have. Before, it was ‘Īd time, all mosque, it was full, everybody going. Even not praying, only once in year or twice, they was going. Now, it is unfortunately it is they lose this. Very few people they are going. AllāhNone help us. So, this, in shā’a Llāh, who have belief, he must be thankful to AllāhNone to make it more and to make in way of AllāhNone, to make AllāhNone happy with us. If you are happy with AllāhNone, HeNone will give you more from everything – from īmān, from health, from wealth, from happiness also. AllāhNone give us all this, for this sake of ‘Īd, for sake of Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
