By the Name of Allah, All Mighty, All Merciful, Most Beneficent, Most Munificent. Listen! We are in need, divinely help, commonly and individually. Allah Almighty saying, just I created Man weak. May be prophets, may be saints, may be believers, may be unbelievers. Whole mankind, just created weak. Everyone, they are in need divinely support. Prophets can't do anything because they are weak also. They are in need of miraculous powers. If those divinely, miraculous powers not reaching to support prophets, they can't be able to face even one person.

And awliyas, they are also weak ones, they are also in need of divinely support with miraculous, divinely power. If that support not coming to awliyas, they can't face people also. Believers also, they are weak, and if no divinely and heavenly supports, believers can't be able to face devils and unbelievers. They are weak. Therefore they are preparing so many arms, every kind of arms and you are looking now, there is perhaps 6 months or more that a super-state bringing, preparing much more power and yet, she is feeling that is weak. 'I must put much more power'. [Still] no confidence through that super-power for fighting. [As] yet, hesitating.

That means without arms they are feeling weakness through themselves. Without arms man feeling that he is weak even in front of cat, or in front of dog. If carrying a stick, he should be brave in front of a dog. Without stick, feeling weakness in himself. Allah Almighty spoke the Truth saying:

wa khuliqa'l insanu da'ifa And mankind was created weak (4:28)

Man is just created weak. No doubt [that] heavenly support is always over weapons and arms support. The first war that happened among believers, mu'mins, in the time of Prophet, beloved Muhammad, sallallahu alayhi wa sallam and unbelievers, mushriks, Badr, war of Badr. The companions with the Seal of Prophets, peace be upon him, 313. They had only 2 swords, others, small knives. Two horses, two camels and others only with sticks or carrying the bones of camels and [placed] beside everyone, stones, preparing to throw [them]. And that was preparation of Companions of Rasulullah, sallallahu alayhi wa sallam.

And coming from Mecca, more than thousand people dressed in armours, on horses and camels. The Companions, they were weak, nothing from weapons with them. And the second party, coming with fully armoured army. They were trusting on their weapons, they were feeling that now we are powerful army, [that] their weapons were going to support them. They were thinking that. Believers, they were saying, "O our Lord, we are weak servants, please send us Your divinely support". And divinely support reached to them. They were winners. They were victorious. That huge army just finished.

Therefore through whole Islamic history you may find hundreds and thousands such examples, that, when Allah Almighty supporting, His weak servants are going to be much more powerful from those who have strong armies and strong weapons [and] they are going to disappear. Yes. Anbiya, prophets and awliya, saints, all of them supported by heavenly supports; supporting them. And our faith, that is from heavenly support. Iman (faith) supporting weak servants to carry their responsibilities. With no Iman, you can't carry your responsibility through this life.

Responsibility of servanthood, it is a heavy burden. Through Iman, Allah Almighty supporting His servant. Also our life, it is heavy burden and [Faith] makes easy carrying for Believers. And you are seeing that unbelievers quickly coming down and, individually and nation-wide, unbelievers coming down now because material support gives to man, nothing. Never taking [away] their weakness. And men need for taking away their weakness, the support of their Iman. Support, spiritual support. Now time coming for mankind living on Earth to step [into] spiritual life.

Wa min Allah at-tawfiq, bi hurmati 'l-Fatiha.
