This dunya (world) is so crowded. Manure and trash. Full of carcasses. Here, this is dunya. Marhaba. O our yaran (friends), our children. May our Lord give us strength. May He go ahead. May our power increase. May we not fall. Marhaba. Marhaba. Bismillahi r-Rahmani r-Rahim. Welcome. O yaran.

Allah Dhul Jalal wal Kamal wal Jalal. Allah Almighty His attributes have no boundary, Sheikh Mehmed Effendi. Al Azamatu Lillah, wal Izzatu Lillah, wal Jalalu Lillah. Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar. We are so weak. Many thanks that you are coming. They are making me speak a couple of words. We ask. We have need. Sheikh Mehmed Effendi, our son. We desire from the news of heavens. Sheikh Mehmed Effendi, our son. News of heavens. That of the earth is trash. The work of earth is like that of wolves struggling with each other. Saving your presence (if you will excuse the expression) it is like dogs fighting with one another. This is not our job. This is not our job.

No fault is left with the one who makes Takbir Mehmed Effendi, our son. He does not leave the one who makes Takbir in loss. Takbir. Honor is only with being able to make Takbir. Sheikh Mehmed Effendi. Allahu Akbar, Allah Akbar Allahu Akbar wa Lillahi l-hamd. What an honor it is to be able to say this. What an honor it is.

With such an honor did Allah Almighty create us. Being able to say Allahu Akbar once goes further than the worth of the whole universe. To say, "Allahu Akbar," to our Lord is not an easy matter. Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar wa Lillahi l-hamd. O our Lord, there is no end to Your Lutuf (Kindness) to us, our Allah. May our sins not be counted. For the sake of that Habibi Kibriya (Most Beloved), for the sake of that Habibi Kibriya, forgive us O my Lord. Give us strength. Give power O Lord. Let us thank You. Let us praise You. Let us glorify You. How can you compare?

Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar Allahu Akbarul Akbar wa Lillahi l-hamd. Thanks be to Allah. Even these couple of words, if we say it in every gathering of ours, is worth this universe. It does not leave heaviness on us. It does not leave. We get comfortable. We get comfortable, Sheikh Mehmed Effendi. Let us say, "Subhan Allah, wa l-hamdu Lillah, wa la Ilaha illAllah, w'Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar wa Lillahi l-hamd." Saying this is more than enough. What does it mean to praise, to thank and to glorify Allah Almighty, Subhanahu wa Ta'ala? Allahu Akbarul Akbar. Subhan Allah. O our Lord, what an honor Allah Almighty gave us. What an honor Mawla Almighty gave us. What a beautiful form He gave as well. What were we going to do if He gave us the form of monkey, Mehmed? Huh, like a monkey. Do you look like a monkey? Leave it. There is no person that looks like a monkey. "Laqad khalaqna-l insana fee ahsani taqweem."

Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar wa Lillahi l-hamd. This blessing, is from pre-eternity to eternity, Sheikh Mehmed Effendi. It is not temporary, Mehmed. O our Lord, our Subhan and our Sultan. Neither is its size clear, nor its distance clear, nor is its power clear, nor is its essence clear. The sun. "Wa sh-shamsu wa l-qamaru." Is it so? Allahu Akbar. How did it happen? It happened with "Kun fayakun". How many shams, suns? He makes no calculation. "Idha arada shayan an yaqula lahu kun fayakun" (36:82) To make a billion suns - When He says, "Be" it happens immediately. Allah Almighty's Greatness. This is a very, very, very small representation. It comes out of there. For whom? "Walaqad karramna bani adam" (17:70).

Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar wa Lillahi l-hamd. May Allah open our understanding to know about Allah Almighty's Greatness. May it open. May it open. May it open. To represent Allah's Greatness - He can create a hundred billion suns in an instant. Allahu Akbar. O our Lord forgive us. So much did He give us. What beauties He gave. What understanding He gave, Sheikh Mehmed Effendi.

O yaran, Shah Mardan's beloved friends, listen, think and understand. Perform your servanthood, and perform your duties. Rabbul Izzati wal Jalal gave you everything. Fatiha.

Sheikh Mehmed Effendi. This is my lion. He is a lion, but he is hidden and covered. Yes, did you read? May Allah be pleased with you. May Allah increase your grandeur.
