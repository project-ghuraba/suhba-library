Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīn wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā ṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jam‘iyyah.

Mā shā’a Llāh, we're back in this country after quite some time. Mā shā’a Llāh, we have been meeting with a fine congregation. Days are passing by quickly. Our Holy ProphetNone says in the End Times, days will pass quickly. He talks about time passing quickly. Weeks will be like a day, a month like a week, and a year like a month says our Holy ProphetNone. We are living in the End Times. So it has been four years. There are those amongst our brothers who come to visit but of course the majority cannot make it. People have come in crowds today, it's like a feast here, mā shā’a Llāh. It's festive and blessed thanks to AllāhNone. They did not come here to watch a film or theatre. They all came for AllāhNone. They only came for the love of AllāhNone. They also brought their children with them. People like to give good things to their children.

Thanks to AllāhNone they came with this intention since good things are being done here. If you were to look at reality, truly the world's most beautiful and most valuable thing is here: the love of AllāhNone. The love of the world is of no use. The love of the world is not our goal. The world should help to promote love, the love of AllāhNone. The love of the world is ephemeral. As you can see, days pass quickly. We are nearing the hereafter, the eternal life. The love of our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa sallam is older than 1400 years. It is in everyone's heart. That love for him will last forever, it will be everlasting, that affection. Ever since the time of our Holy Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa sallam, the world has seen so many millions and billions of people who walked on the same spot, and used the same field, garden, and water. None of them lasted forever. They did not take anything with them. You keep using it and it's gone, but love is everlasting. That lasts forever.

So nobody stopped loving AllāhNone because their love was not going to diminish. It went with them. However, all of the world's wealth and property stayed. The one coming after used it. Then the one after that one used it. Then like this, millions and billions of people used the earth. If everyone had taken something, nothing would be left. Therefore, what is everlasting is what is valuable. That is why we said the most valuable thing here for people, who brought their children, is to show this, to show the love of AllāhNone, the affection for AllāhNone, to show faith and to teach them. That is why they brought their children. It's a beautiful thing. It's the favor of AllāhNone. It's a great favor of AllāhNone. Because many people have gone astray. Always make your children get used to good things. When you're doing this, a person also takes control of his own self. I'm teaching these children what's good. A person can fix his self too: "What mistake am I making? Why am I doing this?" As Imām Būsīrī says in his Qaṣīdah al-Burdah: "The ego is like a child." If you don't train it well, it will take the easy route throughout life. It will go to things it likes, yet the things the ego likes aren't good things.

Now, ordering good is the order of our religion. Goodness is when you see something bad, you change it. If you can't change it then give advice. If you can't give advice, as some don't accept advice, say in your heart that you don't like it. It's contrary, it's contrary to Allāh'sNone commands. It tells us to at least say in your heart that you don't want it. So people don't get used to what is bad. So we don't get used to seeing what is bad as normal. This is certainly why it is the order of our Holy ProphetNone. At least have an ill feeling inside towards these bad things. Thanks to AllāhNone so many people came here now. They came to visit for Allāh'sNone sake, and AllāhNone does not send empty-handed those who come to HisNone door.

Now, Mawlānā Shaykh used to say, "When you have distress, go to holy people if they are around. If you don't know any, if there are graves of holy people in the city or village you're it and you visit them, then that heaviness, that distress will be lifted off you," he used to say. Now, there aren't many such places to visit in Europe. Most of you head to your hometown on vacation. There are graves of companions and prophets in some towns. There are saints, and virtuous ones. You should certainly visit them. Their spirituality will be present. Various people have come out today, and they claim you cannot visit graves. They themselves shouldn't be visited because they themselves are wrong. Our Holy ProphetNone has ḥadīth that tell to visit the parents. Our Holy ProphetNone says, "Visit me." HeNone himself also visited his uncles, and he would visit the companions. HeNone would go to Jannatu l-Baqī‘ often. Jannatu l-Baqī‘ is the graveyard immediately beside Medina that has been there from the time of our Holy ProphetNone till now. Our Holy ProphetNone would visit them all.

They're misleading people, posing as scholars. When they say this, people think, "I wonder if it's true? Am I committing sin by visiting graves?" No. Visiting is acceptable. Reciting the Qur’ān, the gift of ṣalawāts, kalimatu t-tawḥīd, and all are beneficial to the dead one and even to the dead who are in the vicinity. It will be a gift for them all. Those around would get happy.

May AllāhNone give you all a good life. May we live with this faith, in shā’a Llāh. May your visit be accepted. You are all asking for du‘ā’ here. May AllāhNone answer your prayers. May AllāhNone make whatever good intentions you may have, come true. May your children become good people. May they live with faith, blessings, in comfort, and in happiness, in shā’a Llāh. They sent plenty of papers. What everyone wants is du‘ā’. May AllāhNone accept them. May their distress be gone. May their sick get healthy, in shā’a Llāh. May the troubled ones be cured. May AllāhNone give useful knowledge to those studying. Knowledge that is good is more important, excluding a clear mind. There is some knowledge – the man says he's studying theology and he comes out an atheist. May they not be from those, in shā’a Llāh. May they be from those who study knowledge that is good, in shā’a Llāh. May they have good destinies. May they meet good people and have good children, in shā’a Llāh. Whatever good purposes those, who have come here may have, may they come true. Because if I were to meet everybody one by one, I think we would be here till morning.

That is why, AllāhNone knows everyone's heart, everyone's prayers and wishes. AllāhNone would not turn HisNone servants away empty-handed. May AllāhNone make us all especially reach to Mahdī ‘alayhi s-salām and the beautiful days, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah.
