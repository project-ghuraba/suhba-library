As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh. A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah. Today is a blessed day. It is the first day of our new year. It is the year 1447 of the Hijrī calendar. So many years have passed since the time of our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Those with īmān know its value. Those without īmān, according to their own ideas, welcome the new year with all kinds of things that serve no benefit. Some do a little, some do a lot, some go completely overboard. But on the next day, there is nothing. They don’t even understand why they celebrated, why they rejoiced. They wonder, “We were so happy – what happened today?” They look around and see everything is the same; nothing has changed. It is people who change. They become one year older. But the Hijrī year, shukr to Allāh, is a year in which one performs the worships of the past year and begins anew in the new year with fresh duties. And this, gradually, as its time comes, is spent worshipping Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, giving thanks, praising, making du‘ā’. And Allāh gives them their reward and thawāb. They will have won. They won’t lose. Therefore, this is our year – one that begins not with evil, but with goodness. This year is entered by making du‘ā’ for people, for yourself, your family, your children, your homeland, your Ummah. You enter it asking for goodness, seeking true goodness – that the īmān of the believers may increase, and that others too may be guided – that guidance may come to them as goodness. You enter praying that they too may taste these beautiful days, these beautiful times – that they too may be granted this. No harm is wished upon anyone. We wish for oppressors to cease their oppression. As for the oppressed – the reward Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla grants them is endless and boundless. The reward for the patience of the oppressed is: “Innamā yuwaffā ṣ-ṣābirūna ajrahum bi-ghayri ḥisāb” (39:10). Those who are patient will be given their reward without account. Not ten, or a hundred, or a thousand rewards – but uncounted, beyond measure. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla gives from His own treasures. Therefore, this blessed day is the first of Muḥarram. May Allāh make this blessed year of ours a means for goodness. For a person with īmān, everything is good. What has changed is that another year has passed. The final act of worship of the past year – shukr to Allāh – was ḥajj for those who were able to perform it. The duty of ḥajj remains for those who couldn’t go. Some were able to go – may Allāh accept it from them. And many could not go. There were even many who reached as far as the border of Makkah, yet could not enter. They went all the way there with both their intention and action. Allāh will surely accept their ḥajj as well. Because the same happened to our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam: He was prevented from performing ḥajj – our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam was prevented. He came out of iḥrām and sacrificed a qurbān. And this year, those who faced the same situation were more than those who actually performed ḥajj. They entered the borders of ḥajj, they visited the Ka‘bah, but were not able to reach ‘Arafāt. Yet Allāh will surely grant them their reward and thawāb. “Innamā l-a‘mālu bi n-niyyāt.” Actions are according to intention. They both intended and went there, seeking a way to enter. But it was not their destiny – yet it was granted to them in essence, in shā’a Llāh. May Allāh accept it. The last worship of the past year was the duty of ḥajj. And now, we begin again – a new beginning. These blessed days are from the month of Muḥarram, which is also one of the sacred months. In earlier times, fasts were observed in this month. Later, Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla transferred it to Ramaḍān. Fasting became obligatory in Ramaḍān. This one was not obligatory, but was still fasted. Now, fasting from the first to the tenth is also virtuous. But the most important day begins – the day of ‘Āshūrā’. The day of ‘Āshūrā’ is one of the first virtuous days. ‘Āshūrā’ means the tenth of Muḥarram. There is fasting on that day, and there are duties to be fulfilled. Whoever fulfils them receives their reward. As we said, either the ninth and tenth, or the tenth and eleventh whichever suits. One may also fast for three days, or from the first to the tenth. One may do as they wish. These are gifts from Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla to the believers, the Muslims. After that, of course, is the blessed month of Rabī‘u l-Awwal, the month of the birth of our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Rabī‘u l-Awwal too – in it is a blessed and great day. Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla sent our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Some people today, ignorant ones, say it is bid‘ah. But our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam used to fast every Monday. When our Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam was asked why, he said, “I was born on this day.” So our Prophet celebrated his birth day every week. Because his birthday was on a Monday. Is it too much if we celebrated it once a year? Then, as we said, come Rajab, Sha‘bān, Ramaḍān, ‘Īdu l-Aḍḥá, followed by Laylatu l-Qadr – all of these occur within this year. From one year to the next. This is not an empty year. For a Muslim, neither life nor time is empty. It is all full – there is nothing empty. Nothing in creation is empty. How could Islām be empty? It is never empty. The empty one is he whose life is empty, who has no faith. He is the empty one. Let him think he is something as much as he wants. He is an empty person. He runs after empty people. The people he thought were full are empty. There have nothing. The full ones, by Allāh’s permission, are the believers. Every minute of theirs is precious. Every minute is filled with Allāh’s bounty and reward, in shā’a Llāh. May Allāh bless these days of ours, in shā’a Llāh. May He send the Ṣāḥib in this new year, in shā’a Llāh. This is what we are waiting for. It is drawing nearer day by day. In shā’a Llāh, may it be this year. Let us hope, in shā’a Llāh.

Al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are here now first of year, the year of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla. Year, for Islām, a new year. We are celebrating a new year, which it is fourteen forty-seven Hijrī, from what Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam, he come to emigrate from Makkah to Madīnah. This was, before, fourteen forty-seven year ago. Ya‘nī, one hundred... one thousand four hundred forty-seven year ago. But He, Allāh ‘Azza wa Jalla, He like to make him more, higher and higher, and to show the people how he is great one. Which, many miracle happen until he arrived to Madīnah, and from there. Nowadays, you see whole, maybe more two billions, two billions maybe Muslims. All celebrating with this occasion, which it is very important occasion for believer, and they celebrate. How we celebrate? We... with kindness, with happiness. There is too much different between what they celebrate for a new year which it is nothing. Of course, nothing changing because you are doing for nothing. You are doing for your ego. What is ego? But, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, our new year which we celebrating today, first day, it is by happiness coming, by goodness coming. Wishing for people goodness, for whole humanity. For mu’min, we wish more love for Allāh, for Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. And this give them happiness, give them good feeling. And for non-Muslim, for non-believer also, we are wishing for them good thing. But we, Allāh give them hidāyah to taste this good thing, how it is important for humanity to celebrate, and to follow way of Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, not follow way of shayṭān. So, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we are, our life full with meaning, with blessing, with reward. It is not empty. We are, our year beginning from good, and ending. Now, just before twenty days, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, this year was in ḥajj. Allāh without asking, Allāh sending this for us and, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, naṣīb, we been in ḥajj. So, in ḥajj, it is last duty for the year. For Hijrī, for Islāmic calendar year, it is, it is many worshipping inside. The last one: the ḥajj, which it is in ninth and tenth of Dhū l-Ḥijjah. So we are, al-ḥamdu liLlāh, we have been there. and many people, they was our people, other people, their intention to come to ḥajj and they was really, they was in that area. But also, Allāh want them to be imitate Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam. Which, he was intending to ḥajj, and he arrive to Makkah. But this people, mushrik people, they not let him inside. And Prophet ṣallá Llāhu ‘alayhi wa-sallam: “Innamā l-a‘mālu bi n-niyyāt”; what we do, with intention. Your intention, to be good. All this people are, in shā’a Llāh, Allāh accept them at ḥajj.
