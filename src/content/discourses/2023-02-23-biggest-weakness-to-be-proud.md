As-Salāmu ‘alaykum wa-raḥmatu Llāhi wa-barakātuh.

A‘ūdhu bi-Llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismi Llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-Salāmu ‘alá Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa-l-ākhirīn. Madad yā Rasūla Llāh, madad yā Sādātī Aṣḥābi Rasūli Llāh, madad yā Mashāyikhanā, dastūr Mawlānā Shaykh ‘Abdu Llāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatuna ṣ-ṣuḥbah, wa-l-khayru fi l-jam’iyyah.

It is not good for mankind to be arrogant, to favour oneself. It ruins the things you do. The more people favour themselves and see themselves above other people, the more they are defeated by shayṭān. Because arrogance, boasting, and favouring yourself are the attributes of shayṭān. He wants everyone to be so, and strives so that they are like him. People's egos like this, and say, "I am the best. I know the best." Therefore, this attribute is disliked by Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla and our Holy ProphetNone. They talk about vanity. It is one of the diseases of the ego. The diseases of the ego are not in the body, but in spirituality and behaviour. And its harm is not small. Its harm to mankind is great. Therefore, we have to be careful.

Sayyidunā ‘Abdu l-Qādir Gīlānī was Quṭbu l-‘Aẓam and Sulṭānu l-Awliyā’. He is from the greatest Awliyā’ and Quṭb. Sayyidunā ‘Abdu l-Qādir Gīlānī said a light appeared one night. All his surroundings were filled with light. And someone appeared out of it. When this great light came forward, it said, "I am your Lord, ‘Abdu l-Qādir. I make everything permissible for you. Forbidden things are permitted for you now." Sayyidunā ‘Abdu l-Qādir Gīlānī said, "Shut up, you cursed shayṭān." He said this to him.

Shayṭān asked Sayyidunā ‘Abdu l-Qādir Gīlānī, "How did you know?" "It happened by the grace of AllāhNone," he said. He didn't favour himself. And he didn't put forth his ego. He said, "By the grace of AllāhNone, I wasn't cheated." Sayyidunā ‘Abdu l-Qādir Gīlānī said this to shayṭān. Then he said, "How did this happen?" "Through the grace AllāhNone bestowed on me, and with the worship and du‘ā’ HeNone granted me, I wasn't cheated." Then he said, "I have cheated seventy knowledgable scholars like you." He had misled them. And there was no worship for them; they did nothing. And he made them do ḥarām things. They were cheated, but they were people who liked themselves. It was easier for him to cheat them.

A person who is humble and is aware of greatness of AllāhNone, never sees anything as coming from himself. Everything is from AllāhNone. It is from the bountiful gifts of AllāhNone. He knows this, so he acts accordingly. And he follows what is given, told and ordered by AllāhNone. He is not cheated by anyone. Many people are cheated by saying, "I am this and that. I don't need to do this." Be as scholarly as you want, be awliyā' – the orders of AllāhNone are for everyone. If you don't do what is ordered, everything you have earned will be gone. Therefore, may AllāhNone protect us. May we capture our egos. May we not obey our egos, in shā’a Llāh.

To make order of AllāhNone, it is very important for Muslim. One of this order: to not see yourself very knowledgeable, very believer, more believer than others. You see yourself higher than others, this is also... to not do this [is] order from AllāhNone. Because who see himself, he is perfect and others not good, they become like – he has found himself, the best one. This is illness of ego, also. One of biggest illness, to be proud with himself. To be, see himself like nobody can be like him. This is very dangerous thing for human being, but especially for Muslim. This [is] bad attribute. It is not good for Muslim or non Muslim, but we are speaking for Muslim to not be cheated. I have big turban, I have big beard, and I am worshipping, I am doing this and that. You can easily go [in] trap of shayṭān, and you lose everything. This is very important: to follow order of AllāhNone.

Once Shaykh, Quṭb, Ḥaḍratu l-Bāz, Sayyidunā ‘Abdu l-Qādir Gīlānī, he was saying, "Once appeared for me big light, big nūr, full of horizon, full of nūr, full of light. And appeared one, like something you can see. And after, they said, this, what appeared, “O ‘Abdu l-Qādir, I am your Lord. I am AllāhNone, and I am happy with you. I make every sin, free for you. You can do everything. We make every sin ḥalāl. Ḥarām, we make ḥalāl for you he said this light coming when. Shaykh ‘Abdu l-Qādir said: “Shut up! You are cursed one.” And suddenly this picture finished, coming like smoke and light becoming darkness. And shayṭān said, “Huh? How you know me?” He said, "This is from favour of AllāhNone for me."

So, Shaykh ‘Abdu l-Qādir Gīlānī even not saying, "This because I am worshipping, I am shaykh, I am this, this." He said, “It's AllāhNone, favour from AllāhNone, to know you, who are you, you are ordering this.” And he said, shayṭān, asking: How you get this knowledge? He said: “From following order of AllāhNone, doing what HeNone has ordered, and not doing what HeNone has not ordered. This is, how I save." And this shayṭān said, “Seventy, I have cheated seventy big ‘ālim, big shaykh, in this way. All of them because they say proud[ly], 'O Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla, HeNone speak with us, and HisNone order to do anything, [and] we are making this ḥalāl for you.' " They were cheated by shayṭān and they were finished. Because [what] Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla giving us, not changing, they cannot do this. But, from your, he [shayṭān] catch these people from their weakest thing: to their proudness, to be happy with themself, to see themself higher than others. From this, he catch them and destroy them.

So, it is very important for us to follow order of AllāhNone. Not to follow anyone who says, "I am shaykh, I am this, I am that," and not following what AllāhNone ordered. What Allāh ‘Azza wa-Jalla not accept, making to not do this. You must follow this and you will be safe. But other way, you will be, not safe. AllāhNone help us, because our ego very weak, can be cheated.

So, in every, in dunyā also, how they can cheat people, these people? They will make them like balloon. They praise them, praise them, praise them. After, they can take anything from them. But if coming and saying truth, many people they cannot accept. But if they lie and say something, cheat people, they are happy, people, with this. Because this make their ego happy, and easily can be cheated. AllāhNone save us from every badness, in shā’a Llāh.

Wa-min Allāhi t-tawfīq, al-Fātiḥah
