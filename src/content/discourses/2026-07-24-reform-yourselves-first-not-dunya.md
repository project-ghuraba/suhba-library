As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh.

Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni r-rajīm. Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm. Aṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā Rasūlinā Muḥammadin Sayyidi l-awwalīna wa l-ākhirīn. Madad yā RasūlAllāh, madad yā Sādāti Aṣḥābi Rasūlillāh, madad yā Mashāyikhinā, dastūr yā Shaykh ʿAbdullāh al-Fā’iz ad-Dāghistānī, Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī. Madad. Ṭarīqatunā aṣ-ṣuḥbah wa l-khayru fi l-jamʿiyyah.

Bismillāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm,<br>
وَلَا تُسْرِفُوا ۚ إِنَّهُ لَا يُحِبُّ الْمُسْرِفِينَ
<br>“Wa lā tusrifū innahu lā yuḥibbu l-musrifīn”<br>
“But waste not by extravagance, certainly He (Allah) likes not Al-Musrifīn (those who waste by extravagance).” (Qurʼān 07:31). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla does not love wasting by extravagance. He also does not love those who waste.

Now, people have built an economy based on wasting. This economy is a system that Allah does not like at all. That is why they are consuming and depleting the world. You might say, “The world is inexhaustible, the sea is inexhaustible,” but they are depleting it. Because people are disrupting the order and regularity established by Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says,<br>
ظَهَرَ الْفَسَادُ فِي الْبَرِّ وَالْبَحْرِ
<br>“Ẓahara l-fasādu fī l-barri wa-l-baḥr”<br>
“Corruption has appeared throughout the land and sea.” (Qurʼān 30:41). ṢadaqAllāhu l-ʿAẓīm.

Evil has appeared on land and sea, corruption has spread. Everything has become corrupt.

Therefore, the world’s term has expired. Because its term has expired, we are living its final days with the permission of Allah. No matter how much people strive, saying, “We will fix it, we will cleanse it,” they are actually making it worse.

Therefore, only Mahdi ʿalayhi s-salām, whom Allah promised of and whom our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam foretold about, can cleanse this corruption. Everything will be put back in its place. But even after that, there will not be much time left in the world.

Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla says,<br>
اقْتَرَبَتِ السَّاعَةُ
<br>“Iqtarabati s-sāʿat”<br>
“The Hour has come near.”(Qur’ān 54:01). 

“Qiyāmah is near.” Now it has drawn even closer.

Our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam informs us that the time between us and the Day of Judgment is as short as the time between Asr and Maghrib. We are now living through the end of this time.

Therefore, instead of asking, “How will we reform the world?” you must first reform yourselves. And you must wait. The fulfillment of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla’s promise will, inshā’Allāh, not take too long. Because in the sight of Allah, one day is like a thousand years. In the knowledge of Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla, one day is like fifty thousand years. Inshā’Allāh, those days are near. Because the world has completely gone mad.

Nothing is done properly anymore. Mistake after mistake, evil after evil is committed. Despite this, they still think they are doing good. May Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla protect us.

For Muslims and believers, this situation is a trade: the more they hold onto the religion and turn to Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla, the more they will profit. Allah will protect them both physically and spiritually. He will give them peace into their hearts. He will give barakah to their families. Allah ʿAzza wa Jalla will protect them amidst this spreading evil.

Do not say, “Everyone is doing it, so I will too.” Following them will not save you. Look to your own salvation, because everyone can only save themselves. With Allah’s permission, once you are on the right way, no matter how much evil there is, Allah will protect you. May Allah protect us all.

May He make us reach those clean, beautiful, and good days, inshā’Allāh.

Wa min Allah at-Tawfiq. 

Al-Fātiḥah.

There are recited Qur’ān Khatms, Yasin, tahlilāt, tasbihāt, salawat, and Dala’il Al-Khayrāt. May Allah accept whatever good deeds they have done.

We gift them all to the soul of our Prophet ṣallAllāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, for the souls of his Ahlu l-Bayt and ṣaḥābah. For the souls of all prophets, Awliyā’, asfiya, mashāyikh.

Especially our Shaykh, Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim, Hala Sultān, Ḥajjah Anne Sultān. For the souls of our ancestors. For the souls of believing men and women, Muslim men and women. For goodness and charity to be accepted.

To be a means for the happiness here and hereafter, inshā’Allāh.

Al-Fātiḥah.
