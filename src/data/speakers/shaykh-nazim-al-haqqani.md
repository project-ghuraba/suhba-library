Mawlānā Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim ʿĀdil al-Ḥaqqānī ar-Rabbānī (1922–2014) was one of the most influential Muslim spiritual masters of the modern era and the 40th Grand Shaykh of the Most Distinguished Naqshbandi Ṭarīqah. Revered by millions across the world as Sulṭān al-Awliyā, he dedicated his life to reviving the spiritual sciences of Islām and guiding seekers through the path of remembrance, humility, love, and service.

Born in the city of Larnaca on 21 April 1922, Shaykh Nāẓim descended from the family of the Prophet Muḥammad ﷺ through both his maternal and paternal lineages. Through his noble ancestry he was connected to the spiritual legacy of ʿAbd al-Qādir al-Jīlānī and Jalāl ad-Dīn Rūmī, inheriting a rich tradition of scholarship, spirituality, and devotion.

From an early age, Shaykh Nāẓim displayed a deep inclination toward worship and sacred knowledge. He pursued studies in both the traditional Islāmic sciences and modern disciplines, later travelling to Istanbul where he continued his education. His spiritual journey reached its defining moment when he was directed to the great Naqshbandī master Shaykh ʿAbdullāh Fāʾiz ad-Dāghistānī in Damascus. Under Grandshaykh ʿAbdullāh’s guidance, he underwent rigorous spiritual training and became his foremost khalīfah and spiritual successor in the Naqshbandī Golden Chain.

For more than half a century, Shaykh Nāẓim travelled extensively across the Middle East, Europe, Asia, Africa, and the Americas, calling people to the beauty of Islām and the purification of the heart. His teachings emphasised mercy, sincerity, tolerance, good character, and the remembrance of Allāh. Through his warmth, wisdom, humour, and profound spirituality, he attracted followers from every background, helping establish Naqshbandī communities and centres throughout the world.

Known for his powerful and deeply personal ṣuḥbas, Shaykh Nāẓim spoke not only to scholars and students of religion, but also to ordinary people searching for meaning and spiritual direction in the modern age. His message consistently called humanity back to faith, compassion, humility, and Divine love.

In the years before his passing, Shaykh Nāẓim publicly appointed his eldest son, Mawlānā Shaykh Muḥammad ʿĀdil ar-Rabbānī, as his khalīfah and successor in the Naqshbandī Golden Chain. Shaykh Nāẓim returned to his Lord on 7 May 2014 in Lefke, leaving behind a vast spiritual legacy that continues to inspire millions around the world.

Today, his teachings, recorded ṣuḥbahs, books, and worldwide communities continue to serve as a source of guidance and inspiration for seekers from every nation and walk of life.
