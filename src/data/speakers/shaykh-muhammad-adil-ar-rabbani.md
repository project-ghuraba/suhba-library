Mawlānā Shaykh Muḥammad ʿĀdil ar-Rabbānī — also widely known as Shaykh Mehmet ʿĀdil — is the 41st Grand Shaykh of the Most Distinguished Naqshbandi Ṭarīqah and the spiritual successor to his late father, Sulṭān ul-Awliyā Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim ʿĀdil al-Ḥaqqānī.

Born on 29 March 1957 in Damascus, Shaykh Muḥammad ʿĀdil is the eldest son of Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim ʿĀdil al-Ḥaqqānī and Ḥājjah Amīnah ʿĀdil (Sulṭān Ḥānim). Raised in an environment deeply rooted in Islāmic scholarship and spirituality, he spent his youth in Damascus under the guidance of both his father and the renowned Naqshbandī master Shaykh ʿAbdullāh Fāʾiz ad-Dāghistānī. Alongside his spiritual training in the Naqshbandī path, he studied the traditional Islāmic sciences with leading Ahl as-Sunnah scholars of Damascus.

From both his paternal and maternal lineages, Shaykh Muḥammad ʿĀdil descends from the family of the Prophet Muḥammad ﷺ. Through his father’s noble lineage, he is connected to two of the great spiritual traditions of Islām: the Qādiriyyah ṭarīqah through Sayyidinā ʿAbd al-Qādir al-Jīlānī and the Mawlawiyyah tradition through Sayyidinā Jalāl ad-Dīn Rūmī. His mother, Ḥājjah Amīnah Sulṭān, also descends from the Prophetic lineage and came from a distinguished Tatar family that migrated to the Levant through Anatolia during the era of anti-religious Communist persecution.

For many years, Shaykh Muḥammad ʿĀdil served quietly and tirelessly alongside his father, helping establish and support Naqshbandī centres and communities, particularly in Istanbul. Known for his humility, simplicity, and unwavering dedication to khidmah (service), he became beloved by murīds and visitors from around the world. Despite his spiritual rank and global influence, he remains recognised for his modest lifestyle — whether working in the fields, hosting guests, delivering ṣuḥbas, or guiding seekers with wisdom, gentleness, and compassion.

Before his passing in 2014, Shaykh Nāẓim al-Ḥaqqānī publicly appointed Shaykh Muḥammad ʿĀdil as his khalīfah and successor. Following this appointment, Shaykh Muḥammad ʿĀdil assumed leadership of the Naqshbandi Ṭarīqah as the 41st Shaykh in the Golden Chain of spiritual transmission, continuing a sacred lineage that traces back through Sayyidinā Abū Bakr aṣ-Ṣiddīq (raḍiy Allāhu ʿanhu) to the Prophet Muḥammad ﷺ.

Today, Shaykh Muḥammad ʿĀdil continues to guide students and communities across the world while residing between Istanbul and Lefke. Through his teachings, ṣuḥbas, and personal example, he continues the tradition of service, humility, and spiritual guidance that has characterised the Naqshbandī path for generations.
