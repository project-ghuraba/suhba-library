Mawlānā Shaykh ʿAdnān Kabbānī was a distinguished scholar, spiritual guide, and senior representative of the Naqshbandi Ṭarīqah, widely respected for his lifelong service to traditional Islām, spiritual education, and the propagation of moderation, mercy, and tolerance. A descendant of the Ahl al-Bayt and belonging to the 40th generation from the family of the Prophet Muḥammad ﷺ, he came from one of the most respected scholarly families in Tripoli and the wider Levant.

The Kabbānī family has long been associated with religious scholarship and leadership in Lebanon, including prominent jurists, scholars, and public religious figures. Among his relatives are notable members of the Lebanese religious establishment, including figures connected to the Association of Muslim Scholars of Lebanon and the office of the Grand Muftī of Lebanon. Raised in an environment deeply rooted in Islamic learning and spiritual discipline, Shaykh ʿAdnān inherited both a scholarly and spiritual legacy from an early age.

Alongside his traditional religious education, Shaykh ʿAdnān also pursued modern academic studies, earning a degree in Engineering before further specialising in Islamic Divine Law and the classical Islāmic sciences. This combination of traditional scholarship and contemporary academic training enabled him to engage effectively with both Eastern and Western audiences, particularly in addressing the spiritual and ethical challenges facing modern Muslim communities.

His spiritual formation took place under the guidance of Grandshaykh Shaykh ʿAbdullāh Fāʾiz ad-Dāghistānī and later under Sulṭān al-Awliyāʾ Shaykh Muḥammad Nāẓim ʿĀdil al-Ḥaqqānī. Through years of close companionship, service, and spiritual discipline, he was granted ijāzah (authorization) to teach, guide, and counsel students in Islamic spirituality and the Naqshbandī path. He later became one of the senior khulafāʾ and trusted representatives of both Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim and, subsequently, Mawlānā Shaykh Muḥammad ʿĀdil ar-Rabbānī.

Shaykh ʿAdnān devoted his life to spreading the traditional teachings of Ahl as-Sunnah wa’l-Jamāʿah and emphasizing the spiritual heart of Islām. His teachings consistently promoted peace, humility, remembrance of Allāh (dhikr), good character, and opposition to extremism in all its forms. He was particularly known for his emphasis on spiritual etiquette (adab), sincerity, and maintaining strong bonds of brotherhood and service within Muslim communities.

Over the years, he travelled extensively, participating in conferences, retreats, spiritual gatherings, and educational programs throughout the Middle East, Europe, Africa, Asia, and North America. Through his lectures and counsel, he became a beloved teacher for many murīds and seekers connected to the Naqshbandī-Ḥaqqānī tradition.

Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim held Shaykh ʿAdnān in exceptionally high regard and once remarked concerning him: “He is not only an ocean, he is the hose that can swallow all oceans,” reflecting the esteem in which he was held within the ṭarīqah.

Shaykh ʿAdnān Kabbānī passed from this world on 17 Rabīʿ al-Awwal, leaving behind a profound legacy of scholarship, spiritual guidance, and service. His passing was deeply mourned across the Naqshbandī world, particularly by the household and murīds of Mawlānā Shaykh Nāẓim. He is remembered as one of the most honoured murīds and senior khulafāʾ of the Naqshbandī path in the modern era.
